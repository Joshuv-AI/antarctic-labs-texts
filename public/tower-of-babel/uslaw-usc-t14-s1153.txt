14 U.S.C. § 1153 — Prohibition on overhaul, repair, and maintenance of Coast Guard vessels in foreign shipyards
Citation: us:usc:14:1153
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section1153
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 11 (Acquisitions); Subchapter III (Procurement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A Coast Guard vessel the home port of which is in the United States or Guam may not be overhauled, repaired, or maintained in a shipyard outside the United States or Guam, other than in the case of voyage repairs.
