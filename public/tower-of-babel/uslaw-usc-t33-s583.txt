33 U.S.C. § 583 — Payment of allowances, etc., incident to change of station of Engineer officers from appropriation for improvements
Citation: us:usc:33:583
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section583
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 12 (River And Harbor Improvements Generally); Subchapter II (Pay And Allowances; Traveling Expenses And Subsistence). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When in the opinion of the Secretary of the Army the changes of a station of an officer of the Corps of Engineers is primarily in the interest of river and harbor improvement, the mileage and other allowances to which he may be entitled incident to such change of station may be paid from appropriations for such improvements.
