38 U.S.C. § 904 — Equipment and weapons
Citation: us:usc:38:904
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section904
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part I (General Provisions); Chapter 9 (Security And Law Enforcement On Property Under The Jurisdiction Of The Department). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall furnish Department police officers with such weapons and related equipment as the Secretary determines to be necessary and appropriate.
