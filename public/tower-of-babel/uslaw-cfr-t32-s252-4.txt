32 C.F.R. § 252.4 — Policy.
Citation: us:cfr:32:252.4
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-252.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 252 (Professional U.S. Scouting Organization Operations At U.S. Military Installations Overseas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is DoD policy to cooperate with and assist qualified scouting organizations in establishing and providing facilities and services, within available resources, at locations outside the United States to support DoD personnel and their families in accordance with 10 U.S.C. 2606, 2554, and 2555 and Executive Order 12715, “Support of Overseas Scouting Activities for Military Dependents”.
