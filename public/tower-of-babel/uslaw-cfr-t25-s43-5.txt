25 C.F.R. § 43.5 — Access to records.
Citation: us:cfr:25:43.5
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-43.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 43 (Maintenance And Control Of Student Records In Bureau Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Educational institutions shall give parents of students or eligible students, who are or have been in attendance at the institutions, access to student records, except as stated in § 43.6.
