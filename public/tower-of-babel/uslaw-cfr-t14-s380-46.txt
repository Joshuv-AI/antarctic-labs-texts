14 C.F.R. § 380.46 — Charter trip reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:14:380.46
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-380.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 380 (Public Charters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The direct air carrier shall promptly notify the Office of International Aviation, U.S. Air Carrier Licensing/Special Authorities Division, regarding any charters covered by a prospectus filed under § 380.28 that are later canceled.
