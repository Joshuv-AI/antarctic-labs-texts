39 C.F.R. § 3022.40 — Policy on settlement.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3022.40
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3022.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3022 (Rules For Complaints). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It shall be the general policy and practice of the Commission to encourage alternative dispute resolution and settlement of complaints by informal procedures, such as correspondence, conferences between the parties, and the conduct of proceedings off the record with the consent of the parties.
