13 C.F.R. § 106.201 — Who may be a Cosponsor?
Citation: us:cfr:13:106.201
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-106.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 106 (Cosponsorships, Fee And Non-Fee Based Sba-Sponsored Activities And Gifts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as specified in paragraph (b) of this section, SBA may enter into a Cosponsorship Agreement with an Eligible Entity as defined in § 106.101(e).
(b) SBA may not enter into a Cosponsorship Agreement with an Eligible Entity if the Administrator (or designee), after consultation with the General Counsel (or designee), determines that such agreement would create a conflict of interest.
