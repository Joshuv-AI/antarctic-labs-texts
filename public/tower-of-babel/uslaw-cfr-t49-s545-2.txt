49 C.F.R. § 545.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:545.2
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-545.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 545 (Federal Motor Vehicle Theft Prevention Standard Phase-In And Small-Volume Line Reporting Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of these requirements is to assist the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration in determining whether a manufacturer has complied with the requirements of 49 CFR 541.5.
