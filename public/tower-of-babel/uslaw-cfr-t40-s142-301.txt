40 C.F.R. § 142.301 — What is a small system variance?
Citation: us:cfr:40:142.301
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-142.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 142 (National Primary Drinking Water Regulations Implementation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 1415(e) of the Act authorizes the issuance of variances from the requirement to comply with a maximum contaminant level or treatment technique to systems serving fewer than 10,000 persons. The purpose of this subpart is to provide the procedures and criteria for obtaining these variances. The regulations in this subpart shall take effect on September 14, 1998.
