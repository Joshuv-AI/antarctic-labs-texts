7 C.F.R. § 52.14 — Order of inspection service.
Citation: us:cfr:7:52.14
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-52.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 52 (Processed Fruits And Vegetables, Processed Products Thereof, And Certain Other Processed Food Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inspection service shall be performed, insofar as practicable, in the order in which applications therefor are made except that precedence may be given to any such applications which are made by the United States (including, but not being limited to, any instrumentality or agency thereof) and to any application for an appeal inspection.
