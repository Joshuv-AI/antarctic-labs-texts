40 C.F.R. § 1051.335 — How do I ask EPA to reinstate my suspended certificate?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1051.335
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1051.335
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1051 (Control Of Emissions From Recreational Engines And Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Send us a written report asking us to reinstate your suspended certificate. In your report, identify the reason for noncompliance, propose a remedy for the engine family, and commit to a date for carrying it out. In your proposed remedy include any quality control measures you propose to keep the problem from happening again.
(b) Give us data from production-line testing that shows the remedied engine family complies with all the emission standards that apply.
