45 C.F.R. § 1613.2 — Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1613.2
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1613.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1613 (Restrictions On Legal Assistance With Respect To Criminal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Criminal proceeding means the adversary judicial process prosecuted by a public officer and initiated by a formal complaint, information, or indictment charging a person with an offense denominated “criminal” by applicable law and punishable by death, imprisonment, or a jail sentence.
