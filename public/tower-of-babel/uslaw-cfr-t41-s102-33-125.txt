41 C.F.R. § 102-33.125 — -33.125 Training requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.125
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must establish or require training standards, including initial and recurring instruction for flight personnel on roles, responsibilities, and operational skills.
