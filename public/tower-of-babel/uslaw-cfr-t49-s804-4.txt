49 C.F.R. § 804.4 — Open meetings requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:49:804.4
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-804.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 804 (Rules Implementing The Government In The Sunshine Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Members shall not jointly conduct or dispose of agency business other than in accordance with this part. Except as provided in § 804.5, every portion of every meeting of the Board shall be open to public observation.
