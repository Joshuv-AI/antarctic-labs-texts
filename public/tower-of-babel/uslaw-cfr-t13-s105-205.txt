13 C.F.R. § 105.205 — Duty to report irregularities.
Citation: us:cfr:13:105.205
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-105.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 105 (Standards Of Conduct And Employee Restrictions And Responsibilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every employee shall immediately report to the SBA Inspector General any acts of malfeasance or misfeasance or other irregularities, either actual or suspected, arising in connection with the performance by SBA of any of its official functions.
