48 C.F.R. § 811.107-70 — 811.107-70 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:811.107-70
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-811.107-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 811 (Describing Agency Needs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 852.211-70, Equipment Operation and Maintenance Manuals, in solicitations and contracts for technical medical equipment, and other technical and mechanical equipment and devices where the requiring activity determines manuals are a necessary requirement for operation and maintenance of the equipment.
