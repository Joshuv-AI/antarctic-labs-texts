10 U.S.C. § 9686 — Obsolete ordnance: gift to State homes for soldiers and sailors
Citation: us:usc:10:9686
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9686
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 973 (Disposal Of Obsolete Or Surplus Material). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to regulations under section 121 of title 40, the Secretary of the Air Force may give not more than two obsolete bronze or iron cannons suitable for firing salutes to any home for soldiers or sailors established and maintained under State authority.
