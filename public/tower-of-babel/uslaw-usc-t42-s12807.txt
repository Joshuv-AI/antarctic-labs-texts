42 U.S.C. § 12807 — Low-income housing conservation and efficiency grant programs

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12807
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 130 (National Affordable Housing); Subchapter II (Investment In Affordable Housing); Part D (Specified Model Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary shall make available a model program to provide safe, energy-efficient affordable housing for low-income persons.
(b) Activities
The model program shall provide for—
(1)
identification of housing that is—
(A)
owned and occupied by low-income families who have received, are currently receiving, or are scheduled to receive assistance under the weatherization assistance for low-income persons program under part A of title IV of the Energy Conservation and Production Act [42 U.S.C. 6861 et seq.] (or a comparable Federal or State program);
(B)
in danger of becoming uninhabitable within a 5-year period because of structural weaknesses or problems; and
(C)
not sufficiently sound to permit energy conservation improvements without other repair or rehabilitation measures to protect such energy investments;
(2)
repairs that will significantly prolong the habitability of units identified under paragraph (1), including roofing, electrical, plumbing, furnace, and foundation repairs or replacement that will prolong the use of the unit as a safe and energy-efficient residence for low-income persons; and
(3)
reasonable steps to ensure that any units so repaired will remain occupied by persons or families eligible for assistance under this subchapter.
