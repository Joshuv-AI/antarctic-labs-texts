23 C.F.R. § 661.45 — What happens when TTFBP funds cannot be obligated by the end of the fiscal year?
Citation: us:cfr:23:661.45
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-661.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 661 (Tribal Transportation Facility Bridge Program (Ttfbp)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The TTFBP funds from the Highway Trust Fund (HTF) provided to a project that cannot be obligated by the end of the fiscal year are to be returned to FHWA during August redistribution. The returned funds will be re-allocated to the BIA the following fiscal year after FHWA receives and accepts a formal request for the funds from BIA, which includes a justification for the amounts requested and the reason for the failure of the prior year obligation.
