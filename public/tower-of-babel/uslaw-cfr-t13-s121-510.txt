13 C.F.R. § 121.510 — What is the size standard for leasing of Government land for uranium mining?
Citation: us:cfr:13:121.510
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-121.510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 121 (Small Business Size Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A concern is small for this purpose if it, together with its affiliates, does not have more than 100 employees.
