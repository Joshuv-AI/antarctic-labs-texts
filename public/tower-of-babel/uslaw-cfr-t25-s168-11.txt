25 C.F.R. § 168.11 — Range improvements; ownership; new construction.
Citation: us:cfr:25:168.11
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-168.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 168 (Grazing Regulations For The Hopi Partitioned Lands Area). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided by the Relocation Act, range improvements placed on the permitted land shall be considered affixed to the land unless specifically excepted therefrom under the permit terms. Written permission to construct or remove improvements must be obtained from the Hopi Tribe.
