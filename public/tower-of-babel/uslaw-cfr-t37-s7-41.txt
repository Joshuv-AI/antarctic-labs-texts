37 C.F.R. § 7.41 — Renewal of international registration and extension of protection.
Citation: us:cfr:37:7.41
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-7.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 7 (Rules Of Practice In Filings Pursuant To The Protocol Relating To The Madrid Agreement Concerning The International Registration Of Marks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any request to renew an international registration and its extension of protection to the United States must be made at the International Bureau in accordance with Article 7 of the Madrid Protocol.
(b) A request to renew an international registration or extension of protection to the United States submitted through the Office will not be processed.
