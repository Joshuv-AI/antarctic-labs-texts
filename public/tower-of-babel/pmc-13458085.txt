Author Correction: Natural photosynthetic system for restoring homeostasis of animal organelle interaction network
Authors: Chen Xia, Zhanqiu Dai, Yongcheng Wang, Jiachen Yu, Zijie Wang, Zhenxiang Huang, Kaifeng Pan, Ke Yang, Yiyu Chen, Peiyu Zhong, Chenhui Gu, Shunwu Fan, Xianfeng Lin, Pengfei Chen
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-026-69825-y, published online 31 March 2026

In the version of this article initially published, in Figs. 2m, 5i, 9d and associated Source Data, statistical data regarding the transmission electron microscopy and light intensity was incorrect. The figures and Source Data are now revised in the HTML and PDF versions of the article. For comparison, the original and revised figure panels are available as Supplementary Information accompanying this article.
