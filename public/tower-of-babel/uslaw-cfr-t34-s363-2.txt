34 C.F.R. § 363.2 — Who is eligible for an award?
Citation: us:cfr:34:363.2
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-363.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 363 (The State Supported Employment Services Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any State that submits the documentation required by § 363.10, as part of the vocational rehabilitation services portion of the Unified or Combined State Plan under 34 CFR part 361, is eligible for an award under this part.
