14 C.F.R. § 11.85 — Does FAA invite public comment on petitions for exemption?
Citation: us:cfr:14:11.85
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-11.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 11 (General Rulemaking Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, FAA publishes information about petitions for exemption in the Federal Register. The information includes—
(a) The docket number of the petition;
(b) The citation to the rule or rules from which the petitioner requested relief;
(c) The name of the petitioner;
(d) The petitioner's summary of the action requested and the reasons for requesting it; and
(e) A request for comments to assist FAA in evaluating the petition.
