12 C.F.R. § 302.2 — Implementation of the Statement Clarifying the Role of Supervisory Guidance.
Citation: us:cfr:12:302.2
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-302.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 302 (Regulations Governing Bank Supervision). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Statement describes the official policy of the FDIC with respect to the use of supervisory guidance in the supervisory process. The Statement is binding on the FDIC.
