Bethe solutions in the two down-spin sector of the spin-1/2 massive XXZ spin chain
Takashi Imoto (TI this color), Jun Sato, Tetsuo Deguchi

Abstract
Every solution of the Bethe ansatz equations(BAE) is characterized by a set of quantum numbers. In the massive-XXZ chain in the two down-spin sector, all Bethe quantum numbers for all complex solutions is known but those of a real solution is not known. We derive the exact Bethe quantum numbers corresponding to all the solutions of the massive XXZ chain in the two down-spin sector analytically. In this sector, we obtain every solution of the BAE from the Bethe quantum number numerically. There are several results: ( i \rm{i} )We obtain the number of the one-string solutions which depend on the site number N N and the anisotropic parameter ζ \zeta . ( ii \rm{i\hskip-1.00006pti\hskip-1.00006pt} )We derive all infinite-valued solution of the Bethe ansatz equations in the XXX spin chain in the two down-spin sector. ( iii \rm{i\hskip-1.00006pti\hskip-1.00006pti} )We show the completeness of the Bethe ansatz for the massive XXZ spin chain in the two down-spin sector. ( iv \rm{i\hskip-1.00006ptv} )We propose a new method to derive a solution of the Bethe ansatz equation in the two down spin sector from the Bethe quantum number. From the above, we show how to get the solution for the Bethe ansatz equation in this sector numerically.

Introduction

The Heisenberg spin chain (also called the spin-1/2 XXX spin chain) and the spin-1/2 XXZ spin chain are fundamental integrable models in both condensed matter physics and mathematical physics.
The Bethe ansatz is a powerful method for solving quantum integrable models

In the method the Bethe ansatz equations play a central role. From a solution of the Bethe ansatz equations, we derive the corresponding eigenvalue and eigenvector of the quantum Hamiltonian.
However, it is not trivial to obtain all the solutions of the Bethe ansatz equations numerically even in a sector.
We focus on the XXZ spin chain in the present paper.

The Hamiltonian of the spin-1/2 anisotropic quantum Heisenberg spin chain, i.e., the spin-1/2 XXZ spin chain under the periodic boundary conditions is given by
