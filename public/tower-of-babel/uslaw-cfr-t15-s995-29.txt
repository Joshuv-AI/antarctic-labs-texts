15 C.F.R. § 995.29 — Limitation on endorsements.
Citation: us:cfr:15:995.29
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-995.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 995 (Certification Requirements For Distributors Of Noaa Hydrographic Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

By certifying compliance with this part, NOAA does not automatically, directly, or indirectly endorse any product or service provided, or to be provided, by distributor or value added distributor or its successors, assignees, or licensees. The distributor or value added distributor shall not in any way imply that this certification is an endorsement of any such product or service without separate, written permission from NOAA and the Department of Commerce.
