45 C.F.R. § 506.19 — Members of the Armed Forces of the United States precluded from receiving award of compensation.
Citation: us:cfr:45:506.19
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-506.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 506 (Eligibility Requirements For Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any member of the Armed Forces of the United States, who at any time, voluntarily, knowingly, and without duress gave aid to or collaborated with, or in any manner served any force hostile to the United States, is precluded from receiving an award of compensation based on that member's capture and internment.
