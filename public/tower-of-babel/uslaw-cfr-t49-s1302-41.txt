49 C.F.R. § 1302.41 — Suspension of statute.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1302.41
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1302.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1302 (Export And Import Shipments; Railroads). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of section 28 of the Merchant Marine Act, 1920, are hereby further suspended from and including the first day of January, 1921, until further order of this Board.
