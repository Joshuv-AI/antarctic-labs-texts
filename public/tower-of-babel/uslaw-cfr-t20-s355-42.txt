20 C.F.R. § 355.42 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:20:355.42
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-355.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 355 (Regulations Under The Administrative False Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The statute at 31 U.S.C. 3805 authorizes judicial review by an appropriate U.S. District Court of a final decision of the presiding officer imposing penalties or assessments under this part and specifies the procedures for such review.
