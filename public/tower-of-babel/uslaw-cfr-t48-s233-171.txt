48 C.F.R. § 233.171 — 233.171 Reporting requirement for protests of solicitations or awards.
Citation: us:cfr:48:233.171
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-233.171
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 233 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Follow the procedures at PGI 233.171 for reporting information on protests involving the same contract award or proposed award that have been filed at both the GAO and the United States Court of Federal Claims.
