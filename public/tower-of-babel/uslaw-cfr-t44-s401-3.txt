44 C.F.R. § 401.3 — Reports.
Citation: us:cfr:44:401.3
Creator: Department Of Commerce And Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-401.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 401 (Shipping Restrictions (T-1)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons subject to this order shall submit such reports to the Assistant Secretary for Domestic and International Business as he shall require, subject to the terms of the Federal Reports Act.
