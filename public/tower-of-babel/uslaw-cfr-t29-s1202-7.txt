29 C.F.R. § 1202.7 — Who may participate in elections.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1202.7
Creator: National Mediation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1202.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1202 (Rules Of Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As mentioned in § 1202.3, when disputes arise between parties to a representation dispute, the National Mediation Board is authorized by the Act to determine who may participate in the selection of employees representatives.
