47 C.F.R. § 22.867 — Effective radiated power limits.
Citation: us:cfr:47:22.867
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-22.867
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 22 (Public Mobile Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The effective radiated power (ERP) of ground and airborne stations operating on the frequency ranges listed in § 22.857 must not exceed the limits in this section.
(a) The peak ERP of airborne mobile station transmitters must not exceed 12 Watts.
(b) The peak ERP of ground station transmitters must not exceed 500 Watts.
