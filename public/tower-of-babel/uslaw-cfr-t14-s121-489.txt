14 C.F.R. § 121.489 — Flight time limitations: Other commercial flying.
Citation: us:cfr:14:121.489
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-121.489
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 121 (Operating Requirements: Domestic, Flag, And Supplemental Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No pilot that is employed as a pilot by a certificate holder conducting flag operations may do any other commercial flying if that commercial flying plus his flying in air transportation will exceed any flight time limitation in this part.
