34 C.F.R. § 650.10 — How does an individual apply for a fellowship?
Citation: us:cfr:34:650.10
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-650.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 650 (Jacob K. Javits Fellowship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual shall apply to the Secretary for a fellowship award in response to an application notice published by the Secretary in the Federal Register.
