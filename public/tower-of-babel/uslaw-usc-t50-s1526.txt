50 U.S.C. § 1526 — Effective use of resources for nonproliferation programs
Citation: us:usc:50:1526
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section1526
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 32 (Chemical And Biological Warfare Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Prohibition
Except as provided in subsection (b), no assistance may be provided by the United States Government to any person who is involved in the research, development, design, testing, or evaluation of chemical or biological weapons for offensive purposes.
(b) Exception
The prohibition contained in subsection (a) shall not apply to any activity conducted pursuant to title V of the National Security Act of 1947 [50 U.S.C. 3091 et seq.].
