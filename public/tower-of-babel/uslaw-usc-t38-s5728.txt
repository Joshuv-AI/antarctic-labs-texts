38 U.S.C. § 5728 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:38:5728
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section5728
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part IV (General Administrative Provisions); Chapter 57 (Records And Investigations); Subchapter III (Information Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this subchapter such sums as may be necessary for each fiscal year.
