Abstract
In a recent reformulation of top-quark condensation for the Brout-Englert-Higgs boson, we introduced an extended internal wave-function, ϕ ⁡ ( r ) \phi(r) . We show how this leads to a manifestly Lorentz invariant formalism, where the absence of “relative time” is a gauge invariance of the bilocal field theory. This dictates a novel and nontrivial Lorentz invariant vacuum structure for the BEH boson, the relativistic generalization of a condensed matter state such as a BCS or Bose-Einstein condensate.
