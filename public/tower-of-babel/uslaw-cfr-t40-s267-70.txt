40 C.F.R. § 267.70 — Does this subpart apply to me?
Citation: us:cfr:40:267.70
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-267.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 267 (Standards For Owners And Operators Of Hazardous Waste Facilities Operating Under A Standardized Permit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to you if you own or operate a facility that stores or non-thermally treats a hazardous waste under a 40 CFR part 270, subpart J standardized permit, except as provided in § 267.1(b). In addition, you must comply with the manifest requirements of 40 CFR part 262 whenever a shipment of hazardous waste is initiated from your facility.
