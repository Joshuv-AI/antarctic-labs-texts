49 C.F.R. § 218.11 — Filing, testing, and instruction.
Citation: us:cfr:49:218.11
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-218.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 218 (Railroad Operating Practices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The operating rules prescribed in this part, and any additional or more stringent requirements issued by a railroad in relation to the operating rules prescribed in this part, shall be subject to the provisions of part 217 of this chapter, Railroad Operating Rules: Filing, Testing, and Instruction.
