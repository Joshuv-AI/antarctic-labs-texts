33 U.S.C. § 853o–1 — Credit of service as deck officer or junior engineer and certain other active service for retirement and retirement pay
Citation: us:usc:33:853o–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section853o–1
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 17 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Active service in the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration as a deck officer or junior engineer and active service counted on June 30, 1922, for longevity pay, shall be credited to commissioned officers as active commissioned service for purposes of retirement and retirement pay.
