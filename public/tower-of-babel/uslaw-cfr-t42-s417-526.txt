42 C.F.R. § 417.526 — Payment for covered services.
Citation: us:cfr:42:417.526
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-417.526
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 417 (Health Maintenance Organizations, Competitive Medical Plans, And Health Care Prepayment Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subpart O of this part set forth the principles that CMS follows in determining Medicare payment to an HMO or CMP that has a reasonable cost contract. Subpart P of this part describes the per capita method of Medicare payment to HMOs or CMPs that contract on a risk basis.
