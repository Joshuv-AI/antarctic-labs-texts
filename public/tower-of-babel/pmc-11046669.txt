The Compassionate Leadership Programme
Authors: Claire King, Imogen Bidwell
Published: 2024

Background

The Compassionate Leadership Programme is a novel scheme started at the Royal Free London NHS Foundation Trust. We have a vision of creating a community between in-training registrars and middle-grade doctors across all of our trust sites. We seek to address a culture of alienation between specialties; we want to redirect the culture towards creating community and mutual understanding.

Our aim was to do this by creating a safe learning space, addressing the universal needs of registrars and to develop a cohort of future compassionate leaders in local healthcare.

Method

Having consulted the NHS Leadership Academy and The King's Fund work on compassionate leadership, we invited registrar-level doctors to attend an initial session to shape a curriculum of local leadership training.

These lunchtime fortnightly hour-long lectures and seminars are hosted online at Barnet and the Royal Free Hospitals. Session topics include 'Serious incident investigation’, 'Preparing for coroner's inquest’, 'Challenging unprofessional behaviour’, 'Building psychological safety’ and 'Developing into an educational supervisor’. The speakers are both internal and external invited experts and include those from the GMC and NHSI.

There has been iterative development of the programme since its inception in January 2022; with continued development and ongoing roll-out.

Results and conclusion

Having gained feedback both live during the programme sessions as well as with structured post-session questionnaires, our programme attendees have felt ‘connected’ and have valued the sessions. And for those who have attended psychologist-facilitated sessions, they were able to ‘digest any trauma or emotional load of the work’.
