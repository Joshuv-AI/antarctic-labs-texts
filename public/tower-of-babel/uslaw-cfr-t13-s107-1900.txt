13 C.F.R. § 107.1900 — Surrender of license.
Citation: us:cfr:13:107.1900
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-107.1900
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 107 (Small Business Investment Companies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may not surrender your license without SBA's prior written approval. Your request for approval must be accompanied by an offer of immediate repayment of all of your outstanding Leverage (including any prepayment penalties thereon), or by a plan satisfactory to SBA for the orderly liquidation of the Licensee.
