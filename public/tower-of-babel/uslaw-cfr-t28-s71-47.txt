28 C.F.R. § 71.47 — Limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:28:71.47
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-71.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 71 (Implementation Of The Provisions Of The Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Of 1986). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The notice of hearing with respect to a claim or statement must be served in the manner specified in § 71.8 within 6 years after the date on which such claim or statement is made.
(b) If the defendant fails to file a timely answer, service of a notice under § 71.10(b) shall be deemed a notice of hearing for purposes of this section.
(c) The statute of limitations may be extended by written agreement of the parties.
