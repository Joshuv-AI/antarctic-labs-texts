48 C.F.R. § 536.511 — 536.511 Use and possession prior to completion.
Citation: us:cfr:48:536.511
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-536.511
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 536 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insert the clause at 552.236-11, Use and Possession Prior to Completion, in solicitations and contracts if construction, dismantling, demolition, or removal of improvements is contemplated.
