19 C.F.R. § 123.62 — Baggage in possession of traveler.
Citation: us:cfr:19:123.62
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-123.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 123 (Cbp Relations With Canada And Mexico). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For baggage arriving in the actual possession of a traveler, his declaration shall be accepted in lieu of an inward foreign manifest. (See § 123.3.)
