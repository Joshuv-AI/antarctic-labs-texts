37 U.S.C. § 421 — Allowances: no increase while dependent is entitled to basic pay
Citation: us:usc:37:421
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section421
Context: United States Code, Title 37 (Pay And Allowances Of The Uniformed Services); Chapter 7 (Allowances Other Than Travel And Transportation Allowances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A member of a uniformed service may not be paid an increased allowance under this chapter, on account of a dependent, for any period during which that dependent is entitled to basic pay under section 204 of this title.
