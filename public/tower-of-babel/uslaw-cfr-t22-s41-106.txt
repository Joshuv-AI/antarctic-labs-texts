22 C.F.R. § 41.106 — Processing.
Citation: us:cfr:22:41.106
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-41.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 41 (Visas: Documentation Of Nonimmigrants Under The Immigration And Nationality Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consular officers must ensure that the Form DS-160 or, alternatively, Form DS-156 is properly and promptly processed in accordance with the applicable regulations and instructions.
