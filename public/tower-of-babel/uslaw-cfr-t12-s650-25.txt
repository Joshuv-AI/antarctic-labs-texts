12 C.F.R. § 650.25 — Report to Congress.
Citation: us:cfr:12:650.25
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-650.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 650 (Federal Agricultural Mortgage Corporation General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On a determination by the receiver that there are insufficient assets of the receivership to pay all valid claims against the receivership, the receiver shall submit to the Secretary of the Treasury and Congress a report on the financial condition of the receivership.
