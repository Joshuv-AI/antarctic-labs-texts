40 C.F.R. § 105.12 — How is the awards review committee selected?
Citation: us:cfr:40:105.12
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-105.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 105 (Recognition Awards Under The Clean Water Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

EPA review committee members are selected by the EPA and in some cases, State or Tribal water pollution control agencies. The number of participants in a nominations review process is based on staff availability, and may be one person.
