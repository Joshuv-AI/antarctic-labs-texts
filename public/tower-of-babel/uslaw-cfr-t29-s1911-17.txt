29 C.F.R. § 1911.17 — Certification of the record of a hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1911.17
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1911.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1911 (Rules Of Procedure For Promulgating, Modifying, Or Revoking Occupational Safety Or Health Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon completion of the oral presentations, the transcript thereof, together with written submissions on the proposed rule, exhibits filed during the hearing, and all posthearing comments, recommendations, and supporting reasons shall be certified by the officer presiding at the hearing to the Assistant Secretary.
