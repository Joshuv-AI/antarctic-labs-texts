49 C.F.R. § 536.2 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:49:536.2
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-536.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 536 (Transfer And Trading Of Fuel Economy Credits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to all credits earned (and transferable and tradable) for exceeding applicable average fuel economy standards in a given model year for domestically manufactured passenger cars, imported passenger cars, and light trucks.
