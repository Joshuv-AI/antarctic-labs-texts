Murmurations and Sato-Tate Conjectures for High Rank Zetas of Elliptic Curves
Zhan SHI, Lin WENG

Abstract
For elliptic curves over rationals, there are a well-known conjecture of Sato-Tate and a new computational guided murmuration phenomenon, for which the abelian Hasse-Weil zeta functions are used. In this paper, we show that both the murmurations and the Sato-Tate conjecture stand equally well for non-abelian high rank zeta functions of the p p -reductions of elliptic curves over rationals. We establish our results by carefully examining asymptotic behaviors of the p p -reduction invariants a E / 𝔽 p , n ​ ( n ≥ 1 ) a_{E/\mathbb{F}_{p},n}\ (n\geq 1) , the rank n n analogous of the rank one a a -invariant a E / 𝔽 p = 1 + p − N E / 𝔽 p a_{E/\mathbb{F}_{p}}=1+p-N_{E/\mathbb{F}_{p}} of elliptic curve E / 𝔽 p E/\mathbb{F}_{p} . Such asymptotic results are based on the counting miracle of the so-called α E / 𝔽 q , n \alpha_{E/\mathbb{F}_{q},n} - and β E / 𝔽 q , n \beta_{E/\mathbb{F}_{q},n} -invariants of E / 𝔽 q E/\mathbb{F}_{q} in rank n n , and a remarkable recursive relation on the β E / 𝔽 q , n \beta_{E/\mathbb{F}_{q},n} -invariants, both established by Weng-Zagier in [ 19 ] .

\alpha_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}(d)=(q^{d}-1)\beta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}(d),\quad\beta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}(mn)=\beta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}(0),\forall d=mn,\ m\in{\mathbb{N}}.

(8)

Here in the second relation, we have used the fact that each rank

of degree

is obtained from a uniquely determind rank
n
semi-stable vector bundle on

). Accordingly, by the well-known Duality, a standard procedure in zeta function discussion, (

\zeta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}(s)=\alpha_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}(0)+\beta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}(0)\cdot\frac{(Q_{n}-1)T_{n}}{(1-T_{n})(1-Q_{n}T_{n})}.

With

{\mathbb{F}}_{q}

\alpha_{n}:=\alpha_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}(0),\quad\beta_{n}:=\beta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}(0)\qquad{\rm(and}\qquad Q=Q_{n},\quad T=T_{n},)

so that the rank

zeta function for elliptic curve

and its associated polynomial

can be simply expressed as:

where

as to be seen by a direct calculation from (

Theorem 5

(
\alpha
- and
\beta

For an elliptic curve

(1)

(Counting Miracle) For all

\alpha_{n+1}=\beta_{n}.

(11)

(2)

(Structural Recursion Formula) For all

, the
\beta_{n}

(12)

with initial values

Remark 1
.

The counting miracle was first conjectured by the senior author for elliptic curves based on some detailed calculations in lower ranks, with the help of the so-called Atiyah bundles. As indicated above, the so-called Counting Miracle Conjectured was first proved in (the first version of)
[

by Weng-Zagier for elliptic curves
E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
. Later, this was generalized for general (integral regular) projective curves

of genus

by Sugahara (
[

) and independently by Mozgovoy-Reineke (
[

) in the form:

\alpha_{X/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n+1}(0)=q^{n(g-1)}\beta_{X/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}(0).

Since the counting miracle offers us an intrinsic relation between the
\alpha
- and
\beta
-invariants, all the higher rank zeta functions for elliptic curves (over finite fields) are totally determined by the sequence
\{{\beta}_{n}\}_{n\geq-1}
of the beta invariants. That is to say, for
n\geq 1
,
the rank
n
zeta function of elliptic curve

is given by

\displaystyle\zeta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}(s)=

\displaystyle\beta_{n-1}+\beta_{n}\cdot\frac{(Q-1)T}{(1-T)(1-QT)}.

(13)

Consequently, the polynomial
P_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}(T)
simply takes the form

\frac{1}{{\beta}_{n-1}}P_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}(T)=1-\left((Q+1)-(Q-1)\frac{\beta_{n}}{\beta_{n-1}}\right)T+QT^{2},

(14)

and the associated
a
-invariant in rank

is given by

a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}=(Q+1)-(Q-1)\frac{\beta_{n}}{\beta_{n-1}}.

With this point settled, next we turn to the simple 2-step recursion formula on the
{\beta}_{n}
’s, from which the
{\beta}_{n}
in rank

can be determined completely from its initial values

and

.

Example 1
.

When

, we have

\displaystyle(q^{1}-1)\beta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}=

\displaystyle(q^{1}+q^{1-1}-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1})\beta_{1-1}-(q^{1-1}-q)\beta_{1-2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle q+1-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}=\#E({\mathbb{F}}_{q}).

Accordingly, the rank one zeta function of
E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
becomes simply

\zeta_{E,1}(s)=\beta_{0}+\beta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}\cdot\frac{(q^{1}-1)t^{1}}{(1-t^{1})(1-q^{1}t^{1})}=\frac{1-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}t+qt^{2}}{(1-t)(1-qt)}

which is nothing but the classical Hasse-Weil zeta function
\zeta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q}}(s)
of
E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
.

Similarly, when
n=2
, we have

\displaystyle(q^{2}-1)\beta_{2}=

\displaystyle(q^{2}+q^{2-1}-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1})\beta_{2-1}-(q^{2-1}-q)\beta_{2-2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{(q^{2}+q-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1})(q+1-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1})}{q-1}.

Accordingly, the rank two zeta function of
E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
becomes

\displaystyle\zeta_{E,2}(s)=

\displaystyle\beta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}+\beta_{2}\cdot\frac{(q^{2}-1)t^{2}}{(1-t^{2})(1-q^{2}t^{2})}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{q+1-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}}{q-1}\times\frac{1-(a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}-q+1)T+QT^{2}}{(1-T)(1-QT)}

Obviously,
\alpha_{2}=(q+1-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1})/(q-1)={\beta}_{1}
is a constant depending merely on the elliptic curve
E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
and, in particular,

a_{E,1}=a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}=q+1-\#E({\mathbb{F}}_{q})\qquad\mathrm{and}\qquad a_{E,2}=a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}-q+1.\hskip 28.45274pt\square

Back to the general discussion,
by the recursion relation, namely Theorem 4(2), we have, for
n>0
,

\frac{\beta_{n}}{\beta_{n-1}}=\frac{q^{n}+q^{n-1}-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}}{q^{n}-1}-\frac{q^{n-1}-q}{q^{n}-1}\times\frac{\beta_{n-2}}{\beta_{n-1}}.

(15)

To simplify our notations further, for our own convenience, set now

\gamma_{n}:=\frac{\beta_{n}}{\beta_{n-1}},\quad A_{n}:=\frac{q^{n}+q^{n-1}-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}}{q^{n}-1},\quad\mathrm{and}\quad B_{n}:=\frac{q^{n-1}-q}{q^{n}-1},

(16)

we have, from (

\gamma_{n}=A_{n}-B_{n}\gamma_{n-1}^{-1}.

(17)

Consequently, by definition,

\displaystyle a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}

\displaystyle=(q^{n}+1)-(q^{n}-1)\gamma_{n}

(18)

\displaystyle=(q^{n}+1)-(q^{n}-1)\Big(A_{n}-B_{n}\times\frac{q^{n-1}-1}{q^{n-1}+1-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n-1}}\Big).

where in the last equality we have used the level
(n-1)
definition

a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n-1}=(q^{n-1}+1)-(q^{n-1}-1)\gamma_{n-1}.

(19)

Now we are ready to introduce our main technical result to prove Theorem
4
on the murmurations and the Sato-Tate conjecture for rank
n
zeta functions of elliptic curves:

Theorem 6
.

[Asymptotic behavior of
a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}
]
We have

a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}=a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q}},\qquad a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},2}=1+a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}-q,

(20)

and

a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}=(5-n)+(n-1)a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}-(n-1)q+O\Big(\frac{1}{\sqrt{q}}\Big)\quad(n\geq 3)

(21)

In particular, for
n\geq 3

a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}\sim(5-n)+(n-1)a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}-(n-1)q\qquad(q\rightarrow\infty).

(22)

From this theorem, up to a well-understood normalization if necessary, the asymptotic behavior of
a
-invariant
a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}
in rank
n
is reduced to that of
a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}
. This indicates that the murmuration and the Sato-Tate conjecture for
a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q}}
associated to the classical Hasse-Weil zetas of elliptic curves work in exactly the same way for all high rank zetas as well.

Proof.

To prove this theorem, we use an induction on
n
, based on (
18
) and the standard Hasse-Weil bound for elliptic curves, i.e.
a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q}}=O(\sqrt{q})
.

Indeed, by (
18
), we have

\displaystyle a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}=

\displaystyle(q^{n}+1)-(q^{n}-1)\frac{q^{n}+q^{n-1}-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}}{q^{n}-1}

(23)

\displaystyle+(q^{n}-1)\frac{q^{n-1}-q}{q^{n}-1}\times\frac{q^{n-1}-1}{q^{n-1}+1-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n-1}}

\displaystyle=1+a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}+\frac{-q^{n}-2q^{n-1}+a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n-1}q^{n-1}+q}{q^{n-1}+1-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n-1}}.

We start with
n=3
. From the recursion relation (
23
) above, we have

\displaystyle a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},3}

\displaystyle=1+a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}+\frac{-q^{3}-2q^{2}+a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},2}q^{2}+q}{q^{2}+1-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},2}}

(24)

\displaystyle=1+a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}+\frac{-2q^{3}+(a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}-1)q^{2}+q}{q^{2}+q-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}}

\displaystyle({\rm using}\ a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},2}=1+a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}-q)

\displaystyle=1+a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}+\frac{-2q(q^{2}+q-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}-1)}{q^{2}+q-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}}

\displaystyle+\frac{(2+a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}-1)q^{2}+O(a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}\cdot q)}{q^{2}+q-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}}

\displaystyle({\rm make\ an\ oriented\ seperation})

\displaystyle=1+a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}-2q+1+a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}+\frac{O(a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}\cdot q)}{q^{2}+q-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}}

\displaystyle=2+2a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}-2q+O\Big(\frac{1}{\sqrt{q}}\Big)

as wanted. Here in the last three equalities, we have used the Hasse bound
a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}=O\Big(\sqrt{q}\Big)
.

Assume now inductively that the assertion holds for level
n\geq 3
, that is,

a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}=(5-n)+(n-1)a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}-(n-1)q+O\Big(\frac{1}{\sqrt{q}}\Big)\qquad n\geq 3.

To understand
a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n+1}
, using the recursion relation (
23
), we have, as

,

\displaystyle a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n+1}

\displaystyle=1-q^{n}+a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}+\frac{(q^{n}-q)(q^{n}-1)}{q^{n}+1-a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},n}}

\displaystyle=1-q^{n}+a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}+\frac{(q^{n}-q)(q^{n}-1)}{q^{n}+(n-1)q-(n-1)a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}-(5-n)+O\Big(\frac{1}{\sqrt{q}}\Big)}

\displaystyle=1+a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}+\frac{-nq^{n+1}+((4-n)+(n-1)a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}+O\Big(\frac{1}{\sqrt{q}}\Big))q^{n}}{q^{n}+(n-1)q-(n-1)a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}-(5-n)+O\Big(\frac{1}{\sqrt{q}}\Big)}

\displaystyle=(5-(n+1))+na_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q},1}-nq+O\Big(\frac{1}{\sqrt{q}}\Big).

This is exact what we are looking after.
∎

Proof of Theorem

Theorem 7

(Sato-Tate Conjecture
[

[

]

[

0\leq\alpha<\beta\leq\pi
, we have:

\lim_{N\rightarrow\infty}\frac{\#\{p\leq N:p\ {\rm prime},\ \cos\alpha\geq\Delta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},n}\geq\cos\beta\}}{\#\{p\leq N:p\ {\rm prime}\}}=\frac{2}{\pi}\int_{\alpha}^{\beta}\sin^{2}\theta d\theta,

(34)

where
\Delta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},n}
is defined as:

\Delta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},n}:=\left\{\begin{array}[]{lcl}\sqrt{p}\cos{\theta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},2}}+\frac{1}{2}\Big(\sqrt{p}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{p}}\Big)&\mbox{for}&n=2\\
\frac{\sqrt{p^{n-1}}}{n-1}\Big(\frac{\pi}{2}-\theta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},n}\Big)+\frac{1}{2}\Big(\sqrt{p}+\frac{n-5}{(n-1)\sqrt{p}}\Big)&\mbox{for}&n\geq 3\end{array}\right.

(35)

In the sequel,
we only consider when
n\geq 3
since the case for
n=2
is trivial.
We start with the following

Lemma 8

(Small
\delta
distribution)
.

With the same notation as above, the
\theta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},n}
’s converge to
\frac{\pi}{2}
as
p\to\infty

Proof.

From the asymptotic behaviors of
a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},n}
’s in Theorem
6
and the classical Hasse theorem for Hasse-Weil zeta function of
{\mathbb{E}}/{\mathbb{F}}_{p}
,
a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},n}=O(p)
. This, together with the rank
n
Riemann hypothesis for
E/{\mathbb{F}}_{q}
, namely, Theorem
3
implies that
\theta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},n}
has a limit at
\frac{\pi}{2}
as
p\to\infty
, which yields the small Dirac delta distribution
\delta_{\pi/2}
. Similarly, we have
\alpha_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},n}/2\sqrt{p^{n}}
goes to
0
when
p
approaches to
\infty
.
∎

By definition, (with the lemma,) we have

\displaystyle\Delta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},n}

\displaystyle=\frac{\sqrt{p^{n-1}}}{n-1}\Big(\frac{\pi}{2}-\arccos\frac{a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},n}}{2\sqrt{p^{n}}}\Big)+\frac{1}{2}\Big(\sqrt{p}+\frac{n-5}{(n-1)\sqrt{p}}\Big)

(36)

\displaystyle=\frac{\sqrt{p^{n-1}}}{n-1}\Big(\arcsin\frac{a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},n}}{2\sqrt{p^{n}}}\Big)+\frac{1}{2}\Big(\sqrt{p}+\frac{n-5}{(n-1)\sqrt{p}}\Big).

Consequently, for sufficiently large
p
, from the lemma above, we have

\Delta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},n}=\frac{\sqrt{p^{n-1}}}{n-1}\times\frac{a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},n}}{2\sqrt{p^{n}}}+\frac{1}{2}\Big(\sqrt{p}+\frac{n-5}{(n-1)\sqrt{p}}\Big)

Therefore, by Theorem
6
, we conclude that
\Delta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},n}
is asymptotically given by

\frac{\sqrt{p^{n-1}}}{n-1}\times\frac{(5-n)+(n-1)a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},1}-(n-1)p}{2\sqrt{p^{n}}}+\frac{1}{2}\Big(\sqrt{p}+\frac{n-5}{(n-1)\sqrt{p}}\Big)

which is nothing but
\displaystyle{\frac{a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},1}}{2\sqrt{p}}}
, by a routine but direct simplification.
That is to say, we have proved the following:

Theorem 9

(Structure of Big
\Delta
in the rank
n
)
.

With the same notation as above, we have for a fixed
n
, when
p
becomes sufficiently large, we have asymptotically

\Delta_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},n}=\frac{a_{E/{\mathbb{F}}_{p},1}}{2\sqrt{p}}.

With this, it is now crystal clear that the rank
n
Sato-Tate conjecture is a direct consequence of the classical Sato-Tate, namely, Theorem
9
.
