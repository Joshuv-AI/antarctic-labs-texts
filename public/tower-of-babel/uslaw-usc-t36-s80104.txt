36 U.S.C. § 80104 — Property
Citation: us:usc:36:80104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section80104
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 801 (General Federation Of Women’S Clubs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property as necessary to carry out the purposes of the corporation; and
(2)
issue instruments of indebtedness in relation to its real property.
