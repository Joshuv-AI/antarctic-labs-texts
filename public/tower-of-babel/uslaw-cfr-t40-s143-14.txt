40 C.F.R. § 143.14 — State enforcement of use prohibitions.
Citation: us:cfr:40:143.14
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-143.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 143 (Other Safe Drinking Water Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As a condition of receiving a full allotment of Public Water System Supervision grants under section 1443(a) of the Safe Drinking Water Act, States must enforce the requirements of section 1417(a)(1) of the Safe Drinking Water Act and § 143.13 through State or local plumbing codes, or such other means of enforcement as the State may determine to be appropriate.
