24 C.F.R. § 5.151 — Affirmatively Furthering Fair Housing: AFFH Certifications.
Citation: us:cfr:24:5.151
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-5.151
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 5 (General Hud Program Requirements; Waivers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A HUD program participant's certification that it will affirmatively further fair housing is sufficient if the participant takes, in the relevant period, any action that is rationally related to promoting one or more attributes of fair housing as defined in section 5.150(a). Nothing in this paragraph relieves jurisdictions of their other obligations under civil rights and fair housing statutes and regulations.
