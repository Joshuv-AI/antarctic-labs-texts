7 C.F.R. § 3405.16 — Access to peer review information.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3405.16
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3405.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3405 (Higher Education Challenge Grants Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After final decisions have been announced, NIFA will, upon request, inform the project director of the reasons for its decision on a proposal. Verbatim copies of summary reviews, not including the identity of the peer reviewers, will be made available to respective project directors upon specific request.
