41 C.F.R. § 302-4.600 — -4.600 Advance of funds for lodgings-plus per diem and mileage allowances for PCS travel.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-4.600
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-4.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-4 (4—Allowances For Subsistence And Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees may request an advance of funds for lodgings-plus per diem and mileage allowances for PCS travel, except for overseas tour renewal agreement travel.
