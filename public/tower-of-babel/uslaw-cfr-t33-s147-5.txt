33 C.F.R. § 147.5 — Delegation of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:33:147.5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-147.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 147 (Safety Zones). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority to establish safety zones and to issue and enforce safety zone regulations in accordance with the provisions of this part is delegated to District Commanders.
