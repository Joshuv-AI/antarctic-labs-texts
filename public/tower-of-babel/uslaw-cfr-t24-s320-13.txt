24 C.F.R. § 320.13 — Guaranty.
Citation: us:cfr:24:320.13
Creator: Government National Mortgage Association, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-320.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 320 (Guaranty Of Mortgage-Backed Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Association guarantees the timely payment, whether or not collected, of the interest on the outstanding balance and the specified principal installments on securities that are registered on Ginnie Mae's central registry. The Association's guaranty is backed by the full faith and credit of the United States.
