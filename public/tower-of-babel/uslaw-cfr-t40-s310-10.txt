40 C.F.R. § 310.10 — What are temporary emergency measures?
Citation: us:cfr:40:310.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-310.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 310 (Reimbursement To Local Governments For Emergency Response To Hazardous Substance Releases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Temporary emergency measures are actions taken to control or eliminate immediate threats to human health and the environment.
(b) Examples of temporary emergency measures are:
(1) Site security;
(2) Controlling the source of contamination;
(3) Containing the release to prevent spreading;
(4) Neutralizing or treating pollutants released; and
(5) Controlling contaminated runoff.
