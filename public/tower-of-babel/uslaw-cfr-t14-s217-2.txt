14 C.F.R. § 217.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:217.2
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-217.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 217 (Reporting Traffic Statistics By Foreign Air Carriers In Civilian Scheduled, Charter, And Nonscheduled Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to foreign air carriers that are authorized by the Department to provide civilian passenger and/or cargo service to or from the United States, whether performed pursuant to a permit or exemption authority.
