50 C.F.R. § 85.46 — Survey and plan standards.
Citation: us:cfr:50:85.46
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-85.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 85 (Clean Vessel Act Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Survey standards. (1) Surveys should be conducted according to the technical guidelines as published in the Federal Register.
(2) Surveys may be conducted Statewide, if necessary, to obtain information on boats using the coastal zone.
(b) Plan standards. Plans should be developed according to the technical guidelines as published in the Federal Register.
