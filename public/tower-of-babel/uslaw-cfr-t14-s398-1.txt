14 C.F.R. § 398.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:14:398.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-398.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 398 (Guidelines For Individual Determinations Of Basic Essential Air Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish general guidelines for the determination of basic essential air service for each eligible place under 49 U.S.C. 41731 and 41732. Procedures for the determination of the essential air service level for a place are contained in part 325 of this chapter.
