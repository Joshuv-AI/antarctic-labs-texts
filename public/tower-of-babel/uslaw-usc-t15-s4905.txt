15 U.S.C. § 4905 — Cooperation among executive agencies
Citation: us:usc:15:4905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section4905
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 75 (National Trade Data Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each executive agency shall furnish to the Secretary such information for inclusion in the National Trade Data Bank as the Secretary, in consultation with the Advisory Committee, considers necessary to the operation of the Data Bank.
