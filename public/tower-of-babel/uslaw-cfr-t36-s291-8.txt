36 C.F.R. § 291.8 — Area closures.
Citation: us:cfr:36:291.8
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-291.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 291 (Paleontological Resources Preservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In order to protect paleontological or other resources or to provide for public safety, the Authorized Officer may restrict access to or close areas to the collection of paleontological resources.
(b) The regulations in this part do not preclude the use of other authorities that provide for area closures.
