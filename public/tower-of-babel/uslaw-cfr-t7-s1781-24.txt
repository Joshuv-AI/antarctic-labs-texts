7 C.F.R. § 1781.24 — State supplements and availability of bulletins, instructions, forms, and memorandums.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1781.24
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1781.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1781 (Resource Conservation And Development (Rcd) Loans And Watershed (Ws) Loans And Advances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) State supplements will be issued as needed in accordance with applicable provisions of part 1780 of this chapter.
(b) Bulletins, instructions, forms and memorandums are available from any USDA/Rural Development office or the Rural Utilities Service, United States Department of Agriculture, Washington, DC. 20250-1500.
