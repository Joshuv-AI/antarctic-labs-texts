28 U.S.C. § 88 — District of Columbia

Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part I (Organization Of Courts); Chapter 5 (District Courts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The District of Columbia constitutes one judicial district.
Court shall be held at Washington.
