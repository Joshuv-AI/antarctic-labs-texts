10 C.F.R. § 1708.106 — Limitation on participation.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1708.106
Creator: Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1708.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1708 (Procedures For Safety Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A safety investigation under this rule is not a judicial or adjudicatory proceeding.
(b) No person or entity has standing to intervene or participate as a matter of right in any safety investigation under this regulation.
