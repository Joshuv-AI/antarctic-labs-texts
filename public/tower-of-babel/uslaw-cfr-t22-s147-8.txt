22 C.F.R. § 147.8 — Final agency action.
Citation: us:cfr:22:147.8
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-147.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 147 (Information And Communication Technology). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Either a decision by the Secretary on the merits of a complaint, or no notification in writing from the Secretary within 180 days of filing the complaint, will a constitute a final agency action and exhaustion of the complainant's administrative remedies for purposes of 5 U.S.C. 701, et seq.
