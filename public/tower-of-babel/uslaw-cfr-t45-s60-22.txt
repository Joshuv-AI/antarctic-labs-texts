45 C.F.R. § 60.22 — Immunity.
Citation: us:cfr:45:60.22
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-60.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 60 (National Practitioner Data Bank). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Individuals, entities or their authorized agents, and the NPDB shall not be held liable in any civil action filed by the subject of a report unless the individual, entity, or authorized agent submitting the report has actual knowledge of the falsity of the information contained in the report.
