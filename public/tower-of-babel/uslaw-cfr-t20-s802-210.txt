20 C.F.R. § 802.210 — Acknowledgment of notice of appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:20:802.210
Creator: Benefits Review Board, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-802.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 802 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt by the Board of a notice of appeal, the Clerk of the Board shall as expeditiously as possible notify the petitioner and all other parties and the Solicitor of Labor, in writing, that a notice of appeal has been filed.
