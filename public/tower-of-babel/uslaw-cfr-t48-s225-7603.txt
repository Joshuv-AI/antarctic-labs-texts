48 C.F.R. § 225.7603 — 225.7603 Exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:48:225.7603
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-225.7603
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 225 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This restriction does not apply to—
(a) Purchases at or below the simplified acquisition threshold;
(b) Contracts for consumable supplies, provisions, or services for the support of United States forces or of allied forces in a foreign country; or
(c) Contracts pertaining to the use of any equipment, technology, data, or services for intelligence or classified purposes, or to the acquisition or lease thereof, in the interest of national security.
