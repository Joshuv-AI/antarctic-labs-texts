47 C.F.R. § 61.73 — Duplication of rates or regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:47:61.73
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-61.73
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 61 (Tariffs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A carrier concurring in schedules of another carrier must not publish conflicting or duplicative rates or regulations.
