19 U.S.C. § 2079 — Payments in foreign countries; claims for reimbursement
Citation: us:usc:19:2079
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2079
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 10 (Customs Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this Act shall not affect payments made for the United States Customs Service in foreign countries, nor the right of any customs officer or employee to claim reimbursement for personal funds expended in connection with the enforcement of the customs laws.
