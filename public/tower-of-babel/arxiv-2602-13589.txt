Painlevé XXXIV asymptotics for the defocusing nonlinear Schrödinger equation with a finite-genus algebro-geometric background
Engui Fan 1 , Gaozhan Li 2 , Yiling Yang 3 and Lun Zhang 4

Abstract
. In this paper, we consider the Cauchy problem for the defocusing nonlinear Schr o ¨ \ddot{\text{o}} dinger equation with a finite genus algebro-geometric background. Long-time asymptotics of the solution are derived in four space-time regions. It comes out that the leading-order term in the expansion is, up to a constant, given by the background solution with a shift of the parameter. The subleading term, however, decays at different rates for different regions. We particularly highlight that in the two transition regions, they are of order 𝒪 ( t − 1 / 3 ) \mathcal{O}(t^{-1/3}) and the coefficients involve an integral of the Painlevé XXXIV transcendent. We establish our results by applying a nonlinear steepest descent analysis to the associated Riemann-Hilbert problems. Keywords: defocusing NLS equation, finite-genus algebro-geometric background, Riemann-Hilbert problems, Painlevé XXXIV transcendent, long-time asymptotics. MSC: 35Q55; 35P25; 35Q15; 35C20; 35G25.

1.
Introduction

The one-dimensional, cubic, defocusing nonlinear Schr
\ddot{\text{o}}
dinger (NLS) equation

(1.1)

iq_{t}+q_{xx}-2|q|^{2}q=0,\qquad x\in\mathbb{R},

is one of the most fundamental integrable equations in mathematical physics. Since its connection with the theory of superconductivity
[
25
]
found in the 1950s, the NLS equation (
1.1
) is widely viewed as an important model in describing a variety of physical phenomena, which include water waves
[
35
]
, surface gravity waves
[
38
]
, nonlinear optics
[
2
,
10
]
, plasmas
[
40
]
and Bose-Einstein condensates
[
11
]
.

Due to the integrable structure behind, the NLS equation (
1.1
) exhibits rich mathematical properties as well. It has recently been proved that equation (
1.1
) is locally and globally well-posed in
H^{s}(\mathbb{R})

[
26
]
and admits a
H^{s}(\mathbb{R})
conserved energy
[
33
]
for
s>-1/2
. Zakharov and Shabat derived a Lax pair for (
1.1
) and performed the inverse scattering transform for the initial value problem in a seminal work
[
50
]
; see also
[
1
,
51
]
. Of particular interests are the long-time asymptotics for the NLS equation under different backgrounds. For Schwartz initial data, Zakharov and Manakov first established the long-time asymptotics of the solution for the associated Cauchy problem by using the inverse scattering transform method
[
49
]
; see also the work of Its based on the stationary phase method
[
29
]
. A rigorous derivation of the leading and high-order long-time asymptotics was given by Deift and Zhou
[
17
,
18
]
with the aid of powerful nonlinear steepest descent method introduced in
[
16
]
. By reducing the regularity of initial data to the weighted Sobolev space
H^{1,1}(\mathbb{R})
, Deift and Zhou further showed that the same leading asymptotics holds but with an estimate
\mathcal{O}(t^{-1/2-\kappa})
of the error term for
0<\kappa<1/4
, in
[
19
]
. An improved and sharp error estimate was later reported by Dieng and McLaughlin via
\bar{\partial}
methods
[
20
]
; see also
[
21
]
. Quite recently, Lee and Lenells
[
36
]
considered the NLS equation on the half line with a Robin boundary condition at
x=0
and with initial data in the weighted Sobolev space
H^{1,1}(\mathbb{R}^{+})
. For finite density initial data, Vartanian computed both the leading and first correction terms of the long-time asymptotic expansion in a series of papers
[
41
,
42
,
43
]
. Cuccagna and Jenkins
[
12
]
confirmed the soliton resolution conjecture by establishing the leading order approximation to the solution of (
1.1
) in the solitonic region
|x/(2t)|<1
. The long-time asymptotic results in the solitonless region
|x/(2t)|>1
and two transition regions
|x/(2t)|\approx 1
were later obtained by Wang and Fan in
[
44
]
and
[
45
]
, respectively. We also refer to
[
23
,
32
,
46
]
and references therein for the studies of the NLS equation with step-like initial data.

In this paper, we consider Cauchy problem for the NLS equation (
1.1
) with a finite-genus algebro-geometric background, that is,

(1.2)

\displaystyle q(x,0)=q_{0}(x)\sim q^{(AG)}(x,t)\big|_{t=0},\qquad x\to\pm\infty,

where
q^{(AG)}(x,t)=q^{(AG)}(x,t;\boldsymbol{E},\hat{\boldsymbol{E}},\boldsymbol{\phi})
is an algebro-geometric solution of (
1.1
) depending on some real vectors
\boldsymbol{E}=(E_{0},\ldots,E_{n}),\hat{\boldsymbol{E}}=(\hat{E}_{0},\ldots,\hat{E}_{n}),\boldsymbol{\phi}=(\phi_{1},\ldots,\phi_{n})
with
n\in\mathbb{N}_{0}:=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}
. Following
[
4
,
24
]
, the construction of
q^{(AG)}
is standard, which makes use of the Baker-Akhiezer function on the Riemann surface of genus
n
defined by the equation

(1.3)

w(z)^{2}:=\prod_{j=0}^{n}(z-E_{j})(z-\hat{E}_{j})

with

(1.4)

\displaystyle E_{0}<\hat{E}_{0}<E_{1}<\hat{E}_{1}<\cdots<E_{n}<\hat{E}_{n}.

We refer to Section
3.1
below for the precise definition of
q^{(AG)}
. For completeness of the Cauchy problem, we supplement the following boundary condition:

(1.5)

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{R}}|q(x,t)-q^{(AG)}(x,t)|\mathop{}\!\mathrm{d}x<\infty,\quad t\geq 0,

which is a natural extension of (
1.2
) to
t>0
; see also
[
7
]
for similar conditions for the focusing NLS with step-like oscillating background.

It is the aim of the present work to establish long-time asymptotic behavior of the Cauchy problem (
1.1
), (
1.2
) and (
1.5
) for the NLS equation by implementing the nonlinear steepest descent analysis of the associated Riemann-Hilbert (RH) problem. As far as we know, there is currently no literature available to study such problem for the NLS equation (
1.1
); see however
[
37
]
for the invetigation in the context of KdV equation. It comes out that
q(x,t)
exhibits qualitatively different behaviors in four different regions of the
(x,t)
-plane. We particularly highlight the Painlevé XXXIV asymptotics of the solution in two transition regions. Our results are stated in the next section.

The Painlevé transcendents and their higher order analogues play important roles in the studies of transient asymptotics of integrable PDEs. In the literature, the transient asymptotics was first understood in the case of the Korteweg-de Vries (KdV) equation by Segur and Ablowitz
[
39
]
and of the modified Korteweg-de Vries (mKdV) equation by Deift and Zhou
[
16
]
, respectively, which are all given in terms of the Painlevé II transcendents. The appearances of Painlevé II equation in asymptotics of other integrable systems can be found in

[
6
,
9
,
27
,
5
,
45
,
48
]
. In addition, Boutet de Monvel, Lenells and Shepelsky found the asymptotics of focusing NLS equation with step-like oscillating background in a transition zone between two genus-3 sectors is related to a model RH problem associated with the Painlevé IV equation
[
8
]
. Bilman, Ling and Miller established a connection between fundamental rogue wave solutions of the focusing NLS equation in the limit of large order and certain members of the Painlevé III hierarchy
[
5
]
.
Huang and Zhang obtained Painlevé II hierarchy asymptotics for the whole mKdV hierarchy
[
28
]
.
It has been known that the Painlevé XXXIV equation ever appears in the investigations of critical asymptotics of orthogonal polynomials and random matrix theory
[
14
,
30
,
47
]
.
However, to the best of our knowledge, there exist no results prior to this work that report Painlevé XXXIV asymptotics for integrable PDEs.

Outline of the paper

The rest of this paper is organized as follows. In Section

to the associated RH problems in Sections
