48 C.F.R. § 919.7005 — 919.7005 Eligibility to be a Mentor.
Citation: us:cfr:48:919.7005
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-919.7005
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 919 (Small Business Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be eligible for recognition by DOE as a Mentor, an entity must be performing at least one contract for DOE.
