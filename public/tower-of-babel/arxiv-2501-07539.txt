Uniform large-scale ε \varepsilon -regularity for entropic optimal transport
Rishabh S. Gvalani, Lukas Koch

Abstract
. We study the regularity properties of the minimisers of entropic optimal transport providing a natural analogue of the ε \varepsilon -regularity theory of quadratic optimal transport in the entropic setting. More precisely, we show that if the minimiser of the entropic problem satisfies a gradient BMO-type estimate at some scale, the same estimate holds all the way down to the natural length-scale associated to the entropic regularisation. Our result follows from a more general ε \varepsilon -regularity theory for optimal transport costs which can be viewed as perturbations of quadratic optimal transport. We consider such a perturbed cost and require that, under a certain class of admissible affine rescalings, the minimiser remains a local quasi-minimiser of the quadratic problem (in an appropriate sense) and that the cost of “long trajectories” of minimisers (and their rescalings) is small. Under these assumptions, we show that the minimiser satisfies an appropriate C 2 , α C^{2,\alpha} Morrey–Campanato-type estimate which is valid up to the scale of quasi-minimality.

\pi\in\Pi\left(\left(\frac{\tilde{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}{\bar{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}-1\right)\bar{\lambda},\Pi_{y}\pi_{\varepsilon}\big|_{A})\right)
must be supported on
B_{\Lambda R}\times B_{R}
from which it follows that

(171)

OT\left(\left(\frac{\tilde{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}{\bar{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}-1\right)\bar{\lambda},\Pi_{y}\pi_{\varepsilon}\big|_{A})\right)\lesssim R^{2}(\tilde{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{d})-\bar{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{d}))\lesssim\delta\int_{\#_{2R}}|x-y|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}\,.

It then follows that we have the estimate

(172)

\displaystyle\,\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})OT(\bar{\lambda},\bar{\mu})

(173)

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\,OT(\tilde{\lambda},\tilde{\mu})+\delta\int_{\#_{2R}}|x-y|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}+\frac{\delta}{R^{2}}\int_{\#_{2R}}|x-y|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}OT\left(\frac{\tilde{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}{\bar{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\bar{\lambda},\frac{\tilde{\mu}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}{\bar{\mu}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\bar{\mu}\right)

(174)

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\,OT(\tilde{\lambda},\tilde{\mu})+\delta\int_{\#_{2R}}|x-y|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}\,,

where in the last step we have used the fact that
\bar{\lambda},\bar{\mu}
are supported on
B_{\Lambda R}
and so

(175)

OT\left(\frac{\tilde{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}{\bar{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\bar{\lambda},\frac{\tilde{\mu}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}{\bar{\mu}(\mathbb{R}^{d})}\bar{\mu}\right)\lesssim R^{2}(\tilde{\lambda}(\mathbb{R}^{d})+\tilde{\mu}(\mathbb{R}^{d}))\leq R^{2}\,.

Moreover, for
R\leq R_{0}
,
\pi_{\varepsilon}(\#_{\Lambda R})\lesssim\pi_{\varepsilon}(\#_{R})
. Thus, (
158
) can be reduced to the form of Assumption (i). We now provide the proof of the result.

Proof.

As before, we prove the result only for the trivial scaling
\mathsf{s}=(\mathrm{Id},0,1,1)
, since the proof in the general case is similar. Let
(\bar{\lambda},\bar{\mu})
denote the normalised marginals of
{\left.\kern-1.2pt\pi_{\varepsilon}\vphantom{|}\right|_{P_{R}}}
, i.e.

(176)

\bar{\lambda}(A)=\frac{{\left.\kern-1.2pt\pi_{\varepsilon}\vphantom{|}\right|_{P_{R}}}(A\times\mathbb{R}^{d})}{\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})}\,,

and analogously for the other marginal. Let
\bar{\pi}=\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})\textup{argmin}\,OT_{\varepsilon}(\bar{\lambda},\bar{\mu})
. Note that
\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})\bar{\lambda}\leq\lambda
,
\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})\bar{\mu}\leq\mu
. Consider
\hat{\pi}={\left.\kern-1.2pt\pi_{\varepsilon}\vphantom{|}\right|_{P_{R}^{c}}}+\bar{\pi}
. Note that

(177)

\displaystyle\Pi_{x}(\hat{\pi})=\Pi_{x}{\left.\kern-1.2pt\pi_{\varepsilon}\vphantom{|}\right|_{P_{R}^{c}}}+\bar{\lambda}=\lambda,

and similarly
\Pi_{y}(\hat{\pi})=\mu
. Thus,
\hat{\pi}
is valid competitor for
\pi_{\varepsilon}
and we find

(178)

\displaystyle\int\lvert x-y\rvert^{2}\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}+\varepsilon^{2}H(\pi_{\varepsilon}|\lambda\otimes\mu)\leq\int\lvert x-y\rvert^{2}\mathrm{d}\hat{\pi}+\varepsilon^{2}H(\hat{\pi}|\lambda\otimes\mu).

Set
A=\mathrm{supp}({\left.\kern-1.2pt\pi_{\varepsilon}\vphantom{|}\right|_{P_{R}^{c}}})\cap\mathrm{supp}(\bar{\pi})
. Using convexity of the function
x\mapsto x\log x
, we obtain the following estimate:

(179)

\displaystyle\int_{A}\log\left(\frac{\mathrm{d}(\pi_{\varepsilon}+\bar{\pi})}{\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\right)\mathrm{d}(\pi_{\varepsilon}+\bar{\pi})

(180)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}\int_{A}\log\left(\frac{2\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}}{\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\right)2\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}+\frac{1}{2}\int_{A}\log\left(\frac{2\,\mathrm{d}\bar{\pi}}{\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\right)2\,\mathrm{d}\bar{\pi}

(181)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\int_{A}\log\left(\frac{\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}}{\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\right)\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}+\int_{A}\log\left(\frac{\,\mathrm{d}\bar{\pi}}{\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\right)\,\mathrm{d}\bar{\pi}+\int_{A}\log(2)\mathrm{d}(\pi_{\varepsilon}+\bar{\pi})

(182)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int_{A}\log\left(\frac{\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}}{\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\right)\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}+\int_{A}\log\left(\frac{\,\mathrm{d}\bar{\pi}}{\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\right)\,\mathrm{d}\bar{\pi}+\log(2)\left(\pi_{\varepsilon}(A)+\bar{\pi}(A)\right)\,.

Combining the above estimate with (
178
), we arrive at:

\displaystyle\int_{\#_{R}}\lvert x-y\rvert^{2}\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}+\varepsilon^{2}\int_{P_{R}}\log\left(\frac{\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}}{\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\right)\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\int\lvert x-y\rvert^{2}\mathrm{d}\bar{\pi}+\varepsilon^{2}\int\log\left(\frac{\,\mathrm{d}\bar{\pi}}{\mathrm{d}(\bar{\lambda}\otimes\bar{\mu})}\frac{\mathrm{d}(\bar{\lambda}\otimes\bar{\mu})}{\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\right)\,\mathrm{d}\bar{\pi}+\log(2)\varepsilon^{2}\left(\pi(A)+\bar{\pi}(A)\right)

(183)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})OT_{\varepsilon}(\bar{\lambda},\bar{\mu})+2\log(2)\varepsilon^{2}\pi_{\varepsilon}(\#_{\Lambda R})-2\varepsilon^{2}\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})\log\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})

(184)

\displaystyle\,+\int_{\#_{R}\setminus P_{R}}\lvert x-y\rvert^{2}\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}\,,

where we have used the fact that
\bar{\pi}(\mathbb{R}^{d}\times\mathbb{R}^{d})\leq\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})
and that
\pi_{\varepsilon}(A)\leq\pi_{\varepsilon}(B_{\Lambda R}\times B_{\Lambda R})\leq\pi_{\varepsilon}(\#_{\Lambda R})
. Now, comparing entropic optimal transport to quadratic transport, we obtain for some constant
C_{\mathrm{T}}<\infty

(185)

\displaystyle OT_{\varepsilon}(\bar{\lambda},\bar{\mu})\leq OT(\bar{\lambda},\bar{\mu})+\frac{d}{2}\varepsilon^{2}\log(\varepsilon^{-2})+C_{\mathrm{T}}\varepsilon^{2},

which leaves us with

(186)

\displaystyle\int_{\#_{R}}\lvert x-y\rvert^{2}\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}+\varepsilon^{2}\int_{P_{R}}\log\left(\frac{\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}}{\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\right)\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}

(187)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\,\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})OT(\bar{\lambda},\bar{\mu})+\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})\frac{d}{2}\varepsilon^{2}\log(\varepsilon^{-2})

(188)

\displaystyle\,+C_{\mathrm{T}}\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})\varepsilon^{2}

(189)

\displaystyle\,+2\log(2)\varepsilon^{2}\pi_{\varepsilon}(\#_{\Lambda R})-2\varepsilon^{2}\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})\log\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})

(190)

\displaystyle\,+\int_{\#_{R}\setminus P_{R}}\lvert x-y\rvert^{2}\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}\,.

If we choose
\Lambda=11/4
, the last term on the right hand side can be controlled using Proposition
7
by
C_{2}\delta\int_{\#_{2R}}|x-y|^{2}\,\,\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}
by choosing
\varepsilon/R\leq\delta
. We will now derive a careful lower bound on the entropic contribution of
\pi_{\varepsilon}
on
P_{R}
. Using the convexity of
x\mapsto x\log x
, we have that for any
\pi\ll\lambda\otimes\mu
, the following bound holds true

(191)

\displaystyle\int_{P_{R}}\log\left(\frac{\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}}{\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\right)\frac{\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}}{\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)\geq\int_{P_{R}}\left(1+\log\left(\frac{\mathrm{d}\pi}{\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\right)\right)\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}-\pi(P_{R})\,.

We now make the following choice:

(192)

\pi=\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})\frac{\mathbf{1}_{B_{R}\times B_{R}}e^{-\frac{\delta\lvert y-x\rvert^{2}}{(1+\delta)\varepsilon^{2}}}\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}{\int_{B_{R}\times B_{R}}e^{-\frac{\delta\lvert y-x\rvert^{2}}{(1+\delta)\varepsilon^{2}}}\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\,.

We can then obtain

(193)

\displaystyle\varepsilon^{2}\int_{P_{R}}\log\left(\frac{\mathrm{d}\pi}{\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\right)\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}

(194)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\,-\frac{\delta}{1+\delta}\int_{B_{R}\times B_{R}}\lvert y-x\rvert^{2}\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}+\varepsilon^{2}\log\left(\frac{1}{\int_{B_{R}\times B_{R}}e^{-\frac{\delta\lvert y-x\rvert^{2}}{(1+\delta)\varepsilon^{2}}}\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\right)\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})

(195)

\displaystyle\,+\varepsilon^{2}\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})\log(\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R}))\,.

For the second term on the right hand side, we estimate the integral in the argument of the
\log
as follows

(196)

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\times B_{R}}e^{-\frac{\delta\lvert y-x)\rvert^{2}}{(1+\delta)\varepsilon^{2}}}\,\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)

(197)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\,\frac{\lambda(B_{R})\mu(B_{R})}{|B_{R}|^{2}}\int_{B_{R}\times B_{R}}e^{-\frac{\delta\lvert y-x)\rvert^{2}}{(1+\delta)\varepsilon^{2}}}\,\mathrm{d}(x\otimes y)

(198)

\displaystyle\,+\int_{B_{R}\times B_{R}}e^{-\frac{\delta\lvert y-x)\rvert^{2}}{(1+\delta)\varepsilon^{2}}}\,\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)-\frac{\lambda(B_{R})\mu(B_{R})}{|B_{R}|^{2}}\mathrm{d}(x\otimes y)

(199)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\,\frac{\lambda(B_{R})\mu(B_{R})}{|B_{R}|^{2}}\int_{B_{R}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}}e^{-\frac{\delta|y|^{2}}{(1+\delta)\varepsilon^{2}}}\,\mathrm{d}(x\otimes y)+R^{\alpha}[\lambda]_{\alpha,R}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}\times B_{R}}e^{-\frac{\delta\lvert y-x)\rvert^{2}}{(1+\delta)\varepsilon^{2}}}\,\mathrm{d}(x\otimes\mu(y))

(200)

\displaystyle+\,R^{\alpha}[\mu]_{\alpha,R}\frac{\lambda(B_{R})}{|B_{R}|}\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}\times B_{R}}e^{-\frac{\delta\lvert y-x)\rvert^{2}}{(1+\delta)\varepsilon^{2}}}\,\mathrm{d}(x\otimes y)\,.

Rescaling and simplifying the integrals, we obtain the bound

(201)

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}\times B_{R}}e^{-\frac{\delta\lvert y-x)\rvert^{2}}{(1+\delta)\varepsilon^{2}}}\,\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)

(202)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\,\left(\frac{\varepsilon^{2}(1+\delta)}{\delta}\right)^{\frac{d}{2}}\left(\frac{\lambda(B_{R})\mu(B_{R})}{|B_{R}|}+\lambda(B_{R})R^{\alpha}[\mu]_{\alpha,R}+\mu(B_{R})R^{\alpha}[\lambda]_{\alpha,R}\right)\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}e^{-\lvert x\rvert^{2}}\,\mathrm{d}x

(203)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\left(\frac{\pi\varepsilon^{2}(1+\delta)}{\delta}\right)^{\frac{d}{2}}\left(2\pi^{2}\lambda(0)\mu(0)+2\pi\lambda(0)+2\pi\mu(0)\right)R^{d}.

This leaves us with the lower bound

(204)

\displaystyle\varepsilon^{2}\log\left(\frac{1}{\int_{B_{R}\times B_{R}}e^{-\frac{\delta\lvert y-x\rvert^{2}}{(1+\delta)\varepsilon^{2}}}\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\right)\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\,-\varepsilon^{2}\log(M)\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})+\frac{d}{2}\varepsilon^{2}\log(\varepsilon^{-2})\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})\,,

where the constant

(206)

Note that
M

R
.
Combining (

195

(207)

\displaystyle\int_{\#_{R}}\log\left(\frac{\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}}{\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\right)\frac{\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}}{\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)}\mathrm{d}(\lambda\otimes\mu)

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\,-\varepsilon^{2}\log(M)\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})+\frac{d}{2}\varepsilon^{2}\log(\varepsilon^{-2})\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})+\varepsilon^{2}\pi_{\varepsilon}(\#_{R})\log(\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R}))

\displaystyle\,-\frac{\delta}{1+\delta}\int_{B_{R}\times B_{R}}|y-x|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}\,,

which together with (

(210)

\displaystyle\int_{\#_{R}}\lvert x-y\rvert^{2}\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}

(211)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\,\pi_{\varepsilon}(P_{R})OT(\bar{\lambda},\bar{\mu})+\pi_{\varepsilon}(\#_{\Lambda R})C_{1}\varepsilon^{2}+\delta\int_{\#_{2R}}|x-y|^{2}\,\mathrm{d}\pi_{\varepsilon}\,,
