45 C.F.R. § 2525.41 — When must an application for an extension be submitted?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2525.41
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2525.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2525 (National Service Trust). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application for an extension must be submitted to AmeriCorps before the award use period ends, or the individual must have been unavoidably prevented from timely submitting the extension application.
