20 U.S.C. § 1098g — Exemption from State disclosure requirements
Citation: us:usc:20:1098g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1098g
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter IV (Student Assistance); Part G (General Provisions Relating To Student Assistance Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Loans made, insured, or guaranteed pursuant to a program authorized by title IV of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (20 U.S.C. 1070 et seq.) shall not be subject to any disclosure requirements of any State law.
