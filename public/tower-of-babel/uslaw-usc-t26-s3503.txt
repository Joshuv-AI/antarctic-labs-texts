26 U.S.C. § 3503 — Erroneous payments
Citation: us:usc:26:3503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section3503
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 25 (General Provisions Relating To Employment Taxes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any tax paid under chapter 21 or 22 by a taxpayer with respect to any period with respect to which he is not liable to tax under such chapter shall be credited against the tax, if any, imposed by such other chapter upon the taxpayer, and the balance, if any, shall be refunded.
