7 C.F.R. § 4284.901 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4284.901
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4284.901
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4284 (Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements the Value-Added Agricultural Product Market Development grant program (Value-Added Producer Grants (VAPG)) that provides grants to support Agricultural Producers' for-profit businesses that produce and market Value-Added Agricultural Products.
