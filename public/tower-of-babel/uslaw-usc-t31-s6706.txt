31 U.S.C. § 6706 — Income gap multiplier
Citation: us:usc:31:6706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section6706
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 67 (Federal Payments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of determining the income gap of a unit of general local government under section 6705(b)(4)(A),1 the number which applies is—
(1)
1.6, with respect to ½ of any amount allocated under section 6704 to the State in which the unit is located; and
(2)
1.2, with respect to the remainder of such amount.
