46 C.F.R. § 401.432 — Certification of support services.
Citation: us:cfr:46:401.432
Creator: Coast Guard (Great Lakes Pilotage), Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-401.432
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 401 (Great Lakes Pilotage Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each association holding a Certificate of Authorization shall certify each year whether any support service entity is directly or indirectly related by beneficial ownership to that association or to a United States registered pilot who is also a member of that association.
