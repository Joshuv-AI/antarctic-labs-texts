11 U.S.C. § 929 — Municipal leases
Citation: us:usc:11:929
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section929
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 9 (Adjustment Of Debts Of A Municipality); Subchapter II (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A lease to a municipality shall not be treated as an executory contract or unexpired lease for the purposes of section 365 or 502(b)(6) of this title solely by reason of its being subject to termination in the event the debtor fails to appropriate rent.
