14 C.F.R. § 303.46 — Decision by the Assistant Secretary.
Citation: us:cfr:14:303.46
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-303.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 303 (Review Of Air Carrier Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Secretary shall decide, on the basis of the record and in accordance with the procedures prescribed in part 302 of this chapter, whether to grant or deny, in whole or in part, the application. A copy of the Assistant Secretary's final decision shall be served on all parties.
