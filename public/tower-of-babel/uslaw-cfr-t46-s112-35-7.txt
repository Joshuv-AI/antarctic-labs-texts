46 C.F.R. § 112.35-7 — -7 Activating means.
Citation: us:cfr:46:112.35-7
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-112.35-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 112 (Emergency Lighting And Power Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The activating means must be in the navigating bridge or in a location where the means can be controlled by the chief engineer.
