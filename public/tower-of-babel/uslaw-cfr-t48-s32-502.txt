48 C.F.R. § 32.502 — 32.502 Preaward matters.
Citation: us:cfr:48:32.502
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-32.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 32 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This section covers matters that generally are relevant only before contract award. This does not preclude taking actions discussed here after award, if appropriate; e.g., postaward addition of a Progress Payments clause for consideration.
