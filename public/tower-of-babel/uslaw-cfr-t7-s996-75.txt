7 C.F.R. § 996.75 — Effective time.
Citation: us:cfr:7:996.75
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-996.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 996 (Minimum Quality And Handling Standards For Domestic And Imported Peanuts Marketed In The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part, as well as any amendments, shall apply to current crop year peanuts, subsequent crop year peanuts, and prior crop year peanuts not yet inspected, or failing peanut lots that have not met disposition standards, and shall continue in force and effect until modified, suspended, or terminated.
