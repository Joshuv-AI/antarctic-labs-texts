49 U.S.C. § 41506 — Price division filing requirements for foreign air transportation
Citation: us:usc:49:41506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section41506
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart ii (Economic Regulation); Chapter 415 (Pricing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every air carrier and foreign air carrier shall keep currently on file with the Secretary of Transportation, if the Secretary requires, the established divisions of all joint prices for foreign air transportation in which the carrier participates.
