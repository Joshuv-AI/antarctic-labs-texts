33 U.S.C. § 426d — Payment of expenses
Citation: us:usc:33:426d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section426d
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 9 (Protection Of Navigable Waters And Of Harbor And River Improvements Generally); Subchapter I (In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any expenses incident and necessary in the undertaking of the general investigations authorized by sections 426a to 426d of this title may be paid from funds appropriated prior to or after July 31, 1945, for examinations, surveys, and contingencies for rivers and harbors.
