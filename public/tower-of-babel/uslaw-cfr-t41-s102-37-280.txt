41 C.F.R. § 102-37.280 — -37.280 Eligibility determinations.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.280
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.280
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For most public and nonprofit activities, the SASP determines if an applicant is eligible to receive property as a public agency, a nonprofit educational or public health institution, or for a program for older individuals. A SASP may request GSA assistance or guidance in making such determinations.
(b) For applicants that offer courses of instruction devoted to the military arts and sciences, DoD will determine eligibility to receive surplus property through the SASP as an SEA.
