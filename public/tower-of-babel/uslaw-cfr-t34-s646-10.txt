34 C.F.R. § 646.10 — How many applications may an eligible applicant submit and for what different populations may an eligible application be submitted?
Citation: us:cfr:34:646.10
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-646.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 646 (Student Support Services Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An eligible applicant may submit more than one application as long as each application describes a project that serves a different campus or a designated different population.
(b) For each grant competition, the Secretary designates, in the Federal Register notice inviting applications and other published application materials for the competition, the different populations for which an eligible entity may submit a separate application.
