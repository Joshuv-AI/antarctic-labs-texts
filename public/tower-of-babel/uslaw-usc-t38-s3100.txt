38 U.S.C. § 3100 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:38:3100
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3100
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 31 (Training And Rehabilitation For Veterans With Service-Connected Disabilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of this chapter are to provide for all services and assistance necessary to enable veterans with service-connected disabilities to achieve maximum independence in daily living and, to the maximum extent feasible, to become employable and to obtain and maintain suitable employment.
