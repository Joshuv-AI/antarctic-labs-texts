28 C.F.R. § 36.507 — Effect of unavailability of technical assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:28:36.507
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-36.507
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 36 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability By Public Accommodations And In Commercial Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A public accommodation or other private entity shall not be excused from compliance with the requirements of this part because of any failure to receive technical assistance, including any failure in the development or dissemination of any technical assistance manual authorized by the Act.
