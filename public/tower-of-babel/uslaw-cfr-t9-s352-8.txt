9 C.F.R. § 352.8 — Time of inspection in the field and in an official exotic animal establishment.
Citation: us:cfr:9:352.8
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-352.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 352 (Exotic Animals; Voluntary Inspection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The official exotic animal establishment on behalf of the applicant shall notify the Regional Director or designee, in advance, of the hours when such inspection is desired. Inspection personnel shall have access at all times to every part of any field ante-mortem inspection area and/or official exotic animal establishment to which they are assigned.
