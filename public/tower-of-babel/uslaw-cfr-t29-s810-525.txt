29 C.F.R. § 810.525 — Notice provided to CBP regarding the Administrator's findings.
Citation: us:cfr:29:810.525
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-810.525
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 810 (High-Wage Components Of The Labor Value Content Requirements Under The United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement Implementation Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator will provide verification findings and analysis to CBP, which retains the authority to make the final determination of LVC compliance, based in part on the Administrator's verification findings.
