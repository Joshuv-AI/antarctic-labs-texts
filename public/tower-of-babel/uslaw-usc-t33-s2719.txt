33 U.S.C. § 2719 — State financial responsibility
Citation: us:usc:33:2719
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2719
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 40 (Oil Pollution); Subchapter I (Oil Pollution Liability And Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A State may enforce, on the navigable waters of the State, the requirements for evidence of financial responsibility under section 2716 of this title.
