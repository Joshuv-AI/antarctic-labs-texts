42 C.F.R. § 435.326 — Individuals who would be ineligible if they were not enrolled in an MCO or PCCM.
Citation: us:cfr:42:435.326
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-435.326
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 435 (Eligibility In The States, District Of Columbia, The Northern Mariana Islands, And American Samoa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the agency provides Medicaid to the categorically needy under § 435.212, it may provide it under the same rules to medically needy beneficiaries who are enrolled in MCOs or PCCMs.
