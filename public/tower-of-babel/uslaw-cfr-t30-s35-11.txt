30 C.F.R. § 35.11 — Material required for record.
Citation: us:cfr:30:35.11
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-35.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 35 (Fire-Resistant Hydraulic Fluids). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

MSHA may retain for record all or part of the material submitted for testing. Any material that MSHA does not require will be returned to the applicant at his expense upon receipt of his written request and shipping instructions not more than 6 months after the termination or completion of the tests. Thereafter MSHA will dispose of such surplus material as it deems appropriate.
