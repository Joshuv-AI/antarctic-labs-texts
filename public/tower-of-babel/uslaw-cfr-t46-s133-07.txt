46 C.F.R. § 133.07 — Additional equipment and requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:133.07
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-133.07
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 133 (Lifesaving Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The OCMI may require an OSV to carry specialized or additional lifesaving equipment other than as required in this part if the OCMI determines that the conditions of a voyage present uniquely hazardous circumstances which are not adequately addressed by existing requirements.
