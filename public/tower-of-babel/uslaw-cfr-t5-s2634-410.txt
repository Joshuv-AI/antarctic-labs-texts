5 C.F.R. § 2634.410 — Dissolution.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2634.410
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2634.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2634 (Executive Branch Financial Disclosure, Qualified Trusts, And Certificates Of Divestiture). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 30 days of dissolution of a qualified trust, the interested party must file a report of the dissolution with the Director and a list of assets of the trust at the time of the dissolution, categorized as to value in accordance with § 2634.301(d).
