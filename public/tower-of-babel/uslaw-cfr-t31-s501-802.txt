31 C.F.R. § 501.802 — Decisions.
Citation: us:cfr:31:501.802
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-501.802
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 501 (Reporting, Procedures And Penalties Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Foreign Assets Control will advise each applicant of the decision respecting filed applications. The decision of the Office of Foreign Assets Control acting on behalf of the Secretary of the Treasury with respect to an application shall constitute final agency action.
