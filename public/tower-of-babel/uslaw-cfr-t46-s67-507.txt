46 C.F.R. § 67.507 — Application for replacement of lost or mutilated Certificate of Documentation.
Citation: us:cfr:46:67.507
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-67.507
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 67 (Documentation Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application fee is charged for replacement of a lost or mutilated Certificate of Documentation in accordance with subpart K of this part.
