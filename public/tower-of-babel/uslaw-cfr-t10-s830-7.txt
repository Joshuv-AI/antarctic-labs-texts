10 C.F.R. § 830.7 — Graded approach.
Citation: us:cfr:10:830.7
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-830.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 830 (Nuclear Safety Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where appropriate, a contractor must use a graded approach to implement the requirements of this part, document the basis of the graded approach used, and submit that documentation to DOE. The graded approach may not be used in implementing the unreviewed safety question (USQ) process or in implementing technical safety requirements.
