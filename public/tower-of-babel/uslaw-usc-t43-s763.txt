43 U.S.C. § 763 — Deposits in Louisiana applicable to resurveys
Citation: us:usc:43:763
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section763
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 18 (Survey Of Public Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Such sums as have been or may be deposited for surveys in Louisiana by actual settlers, under sections 759, 760, and 762 of this title, may be, in whole or in part, employed in making such resurveys as may be necessary in the discretion of the Secretary of the Interior or such officer as he may designate.
