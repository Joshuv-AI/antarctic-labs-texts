12 C.F.R. § 1240.61 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1240.61
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1240.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1240 (Capital Adequacy Of Enterprises). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 1240.61 through 1240.63 of this subpart establish public disclosure requirements related to the capital requirements and buffers described in subpart B and subpart G.
