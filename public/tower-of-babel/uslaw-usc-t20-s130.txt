20 U.S.C. § 130 — Purchases through the General Services Administration
Citation: us:usc:20:130
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section130
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 8 (Howard University); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after September 8, 1978, Howard University is authorized to make purchases through the General Services Administration.
