40 C.F.R. § 74.4 — Designated representative.
Citation: us:cfr:40:74.4
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-74.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 74 (Sulfur Dioxide Opt-Ins). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The provisions of subpart B of part 72 of this chapter shall apply to the designated representative of an opt-in source.
(b) If a combustion or process source is located at the same source as one or more affected units, the combustion or process source shall have the same designated representative as the other affected units at the source.
