16 C.F.R. § 1507.6 — Burnout and blowout.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1507.6
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1507.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1507 (Fireworks Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The pyrotechnic chamber in fireworks devices shall be constructed in a manner to allow functioning in a normal manner without burnout or blowout.
