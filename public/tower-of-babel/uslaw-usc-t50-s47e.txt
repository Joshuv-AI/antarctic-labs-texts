50 U.S.C. § 47e — Certification of award; approval; payment
Citation: us:usc:50:47e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section47e
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 4C (Atomic Weapons And Special Nuclear Materials Information Rewards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any awards granted under section 47b of this title shall be certified by the Attorney General and, together with the approval of the President in those cases where such approval is required, transmitted to the Director of Central Intelligence for payment out of funds appropriated or available for the administration of the National Security Act of 1947, as amended.
