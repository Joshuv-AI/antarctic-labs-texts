42 C.F.R. § 137.34 — May a funding agreement be executed without negotiating a compact?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.34
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, a compact is a separate document from a funding agreement, and the compact must be executed before or at the same time as a funding agreement.
