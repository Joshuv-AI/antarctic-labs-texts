14 U.S.C. § 2902 — Surveys of Coast Guard families
Citation: us:usc:14:2902
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2902
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 29 (Coast Guard Family Support, Child Care, And Housing); Subchapter I (Coast Guard Families). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Authority.—
The Commandant, in order to determine the effectiveness of Federal policies, programs, and activities related to the families of Coast Guard members, may survey—
(1)
any Coast Guard member;
(2)
any retired Coast Guard member;
(3)
the immediate family of any Coast Guard member or retired Coast Guard member; and
(4)
any survivor of a deceased Coast Guard member.
(b) Voluntary Participation.—
Participation in any survey conducted under subsection (a) shall be voluntary.
(c) Federal Recordkeeping.—
Each individual surveyed under subsection (a) shall be considered an employee of the United States for purposes of section 3502(3)(A)(i) of title 44.
