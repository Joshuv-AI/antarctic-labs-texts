9 C.F.R. § 311.11 — Neoplasms.
Citation: us:cfr:9:311.11
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-311.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 311 (Disposal Of Diseased Or Otherwise Adulterated Carcasses And Parts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An individual organ or other part of a carcass affected with a neoplasm shall be condemned. If there is evidence of metastasis or that the general condition of the animal has been adversely affected by the size, position, or nature of the neoplasm, the entire carcass shall be condemned.
(b) Carcasses affected with malignant lymphoma shall be condemned.
