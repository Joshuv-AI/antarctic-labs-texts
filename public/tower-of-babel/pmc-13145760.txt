ABSTRACT NUMBER: ESOC2026A164 THORACOSCOPIC EPICARDIAL APPENDAGE EXCLUSION WITHOUT ANTITHROMBOTIC THERAPY FOR STROKE AND BLEEDING PREVENTION IN PATIENTS WITH ATRIAL FIBRILLATION AND POOR TOLERANCE TO ANTICOAGUALTION: LONG TERM OUTCOMES
Authors: Stefano Branzoli, Domenico Catanzariti, Massimiliano Marini, Andrea La Licata, Ginevra Tecilla, Martina Venturini, Francesco Corsini, Cecilia Pravadelli, Bart Maesen, Mark la Meir
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackground and aimsIn 2024 ESC/EACTS guidelines for Atrial Fibrillation left appendage occlusion has a class indication IIb for stroke prevention in patients with contraindication to anticoagulation. With DOACS having same bleeding risk of aspirine the role of appendage occlusion is defined “unclear” on the assumption that post procedure antiplatelet therapy is mandatory. Endovascular closure mandate antiplatelet therapy, epicardial closure do not. Investigating safety and efficacy of thoracoscopic epicardial appendage exclusion without antiplatelet therapy might shed light on this topic with clinical implications.MethodsBetween 2016-2025, 425 patients with AF and poor tolerance to anticoagulants (CHA2DS2VA4.1±1.5, HASBLED3.2±1.1) underwent multidisciplinary evaluation for thoracoscopic epicardial appendage exclusion. No antithrombotic therapy was prescribed for entire follow up unless required for persistent left atrial thrombosis, or acute cardioembolic event on anticoagulants. Follow up was at 3,6,12 months and yearly thereafter,EQ-5D-5L was used for quality of life pre procedure and at 12 monthsResults385 patients underwent thoracoscopic appendage exclusion, 40 continued anticoagulation. In thoracoscopy group at follow up 92% of patients received no antithrombotic therapy, 5.9% were on anticoagulants.In the treated groups overall incidence of stroke, composite outcomes, bleeding events were 0.46(95%CI:0.12-1.14), 0.79(0.32-1.62), 0.47(0.12-1.15) respectively.Cause specific survival was 100% vs 89% at 1 year and 98% vs 83% at 4years (p<0.001). EQ5D5L scores increased from median 50(IQR 40-60) to 74(IQR 70-78)(p<0.001)at 12 months.ConclusionsThoracoscopic epicardial left appendage exclusion without antiplatelet therapy is safe and effective in patient wiht AF poor tolerant to anticoagulants.Conflict of interestnothing to disclose
