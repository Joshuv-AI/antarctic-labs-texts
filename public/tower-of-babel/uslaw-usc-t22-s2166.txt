22 U.S.C. § 2166 — Regional development in Africa
Citation: us:usc:22:2166
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2166
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter I (International Development); Part II (Other Programs); Subpart i (Multilateral And Regional Development Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is requested to seek and to take appropriate action, in cooperation and consultation with African and other interested nations and with international development organizations, to further and assist in the advancement of African regional development institutions, including the African Development Bank, with the view toward promoting African economic development.
