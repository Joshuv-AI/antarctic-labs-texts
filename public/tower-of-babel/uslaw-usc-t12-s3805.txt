12 U.S.C. § 3805 — Applicability of consumer protection provisions
Citation: us:usc:12:3805
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section3805
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 39 (Alternative Mortgage Transactions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Section 501(c)(1) of the Depository Institutions Deregulation and Monetary Control Act of 1980 shall not apply to transactions which are subject to this chapter.
