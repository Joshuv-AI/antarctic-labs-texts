36 C.F.R. § 51.50 — Does the existence of a preferred offeror limit the authority of the Director to establish the terms of a concession contract?
Citation: us:cfr:36:51.50
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-51.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 51 (Concession Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The existence of a preferred offeror does not limit the authority of the Director to establish, in accordance with this part, the terms and conditions of a new concession contract, including, but not limited to, terms and conditions that modify the terms and conditions of a prior concession contract.
