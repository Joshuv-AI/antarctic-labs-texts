49 C.F.R. § 1019.6 — Disciplinary and other remedial action.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1019.6
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1019.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1019 (Regulations Governing Conduct Of Surface Transportation Board Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any violation of the regulations in this part by an employee shall be cause for appropriate disciplinary or other remedial action as provided in the STB's Manual of Administration 22-751, which may be in addition to any penalty prescribed by law. The manual is available from the Section of Personnel Services, Surface Transportation Board, Washington, DC 20423.
