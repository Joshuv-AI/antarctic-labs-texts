40 U.S.C. § 11701 — Identification of excess and surplus computer equipment
Citation: us:usc:40:11701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section11701
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 117 (Additional Information Resources Management Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In accordance with chapter 5 of this title, the head of an executive agency shall maintain an inventory of all computer equipment under the control of that official that is excess or surplus property.
