7 U.S.C. § 138g — Public disclosure
Citation: us:usc:7:138g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section138g
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 6A (National Laboratory Accreditation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The results of the evaluations of laboratories conducted by the Secretary under this chapter shall be made available to the Secretary of Health and Human Services and to the public on request.
