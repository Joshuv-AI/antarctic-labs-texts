10 U.S.C. § 8716 — Naval Observatory: exchange of information with foreign offices
Citation: us:usc:10:8716
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8716
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 867 (United States Naval Observatory). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary of the Navy may arrange to exchange data with foreign almanac offices to reduce the duplication of work in preparing the different national nautical and astronomical almanacs and make available for publication a larger amount of data useful to navigators and astronomers. Each such arrangement shall be made terminable on one year’s notice.
(b)
The work of the Nautical Almanac Office shall be so conducted that in an emergency the part of the work intended for the use of navigators may be computed by the force of the office without foreign cooperation.
