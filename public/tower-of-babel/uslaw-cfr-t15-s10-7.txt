15 C.F.R. § 10.7 — Procedure when a recommended standard is not supported by a consensus.
Citation: us:cfr:15:10.7
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-10.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 10 (Procedures For The Development Of Voluntary Product Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Department determines that a recommended standard is not supported by a consensus, the Department may:
(a) Return the recommended standard to the Standard Review Committee for further action, with or without suggestions;
(b) Terminate the development of the recommended standard under these procedures; or
(c) Take such other action as it may deem necessary or appropriate under the circumstances.
