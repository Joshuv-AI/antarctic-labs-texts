21 C.F.R. § 812.42 — FDA and IRB approval.
Citation: us:cfr:21:812.42
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-812.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 812 (Investigational Device Exemptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A sponsor shall not begin an investigation or part of an investigation until an IRB and FDA have both approved the application or supplemental application relating to the investigation or part of an investigation.
