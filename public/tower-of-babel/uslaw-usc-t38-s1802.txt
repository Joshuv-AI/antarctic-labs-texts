38 U.S.C. § 1802 — Spina bifida conditions covered
Citation: us:usc:38:1802
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1802
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 18 (Benefits For Children Of Vietnam Veterans And Certain Other Veterans); Subchapter I (Children Of Vietnam Veterans Born With Spina Bifida). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter applies with respect to all forms and manifestations of spina bifida except spina bifida occulta.
