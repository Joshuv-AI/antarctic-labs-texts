21 C.F.R. § 369.2 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:21:369.2
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-369.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 369 (Interpretative Statements Re Warnings On Drugs And Devices For Over-The-Counter Sale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) As used in this part, the term act means the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act.
(b) The terms drugs and devices are defined in section 201(g) and (k) of the act.
(c) Official compendia are defined in section 201(j) of the act.
