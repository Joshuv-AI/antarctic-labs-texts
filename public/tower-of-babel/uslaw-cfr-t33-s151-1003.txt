33 C.F.R. § 151.1003 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:33:151.1003
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-151.1003
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 151 (Vessels Carrying Oil, Noxious Liquid Substances, Garbage, Municipal Or Commercial Waste, And Ballast Water). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided by paragraph (b) of this section, this subpart applies to each vessel whose purpose is the transportation of municipal or commercial waste in coastal waters.
(b) This subpart does not apply to public vessels.
