8 C.F.R. § 103.17 — Biometric services fee.
Citation: us:cfr:8:103.17
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-103.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 103 (Immigration Benefit Requests; Uscis Filing Requirements; Biometric Requirements; Availability Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DHS may charge a fee to collect biometric information, to provide biometric collection services, to conduct required national security and criminal history background checks, to verify an individual's identity, and to store and maintain this biometric information for reuse to support other benefit requests. When a biometric services fee is required, USCIS may reject a benefit request submitted without the correct biometric services fee.
