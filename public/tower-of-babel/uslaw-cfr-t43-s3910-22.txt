43 C.F.R. § 3910.22 — Lands managed by agencies other than the BLM.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3910.22
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3910.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3910 (Oil Shale Exploration Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The consent and consultation procedures required by § 3900.61 of this chapter also apply to exploration license applications.
(b) If exploration activities could affect the adjacent lands under the surface management of a Federal agency other than the BLM, the BLM will consult with that agency before issuing an exploration license.
