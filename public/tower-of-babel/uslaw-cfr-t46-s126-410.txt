46 C.F.R. § 126.410 — Prerequisite to reissuance of Certificate of Inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:46:126.410
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-126.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 126 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An inspection for certification is a prerequisite to the reissuance of a Certificate of Inspection.
