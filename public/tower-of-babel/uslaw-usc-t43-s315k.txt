43 U.S.C. § 315k — Cooperation with governmental departments; coordination of range administration
Citation: us:usc:43:315k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section315k
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 8A (Grazing Lands); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized to cooperate with any department of the Government in carrying out the purposes of this subchapter and in the coordination of range administration, particularly where the same stock grazes part time in a grazing district and part time in a national forest or other reservation.
