34 C.F.R. § 86.3 — What actions shall an IHE take to comply with the requirements of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:34:86.3
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-86.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 86 (Drug And Alcohol Abuse Prevention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An IHE shall adopt and implement a drug prevention program as described in § 86.100 to prevent the unlawful possession, use, or distribution of illicit drugs and alcohol by all students and employees on school premises or as part of any of its activities.
(b) An IHE shall provide a written certification that it has adopted and implemented the drug prevention program described in § 86.100.
