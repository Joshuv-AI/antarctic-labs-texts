46 C.F.R. § 174.075 — Compartments assumed flooded: general.
Citation: us:cfr:46:174.075
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-174.075
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 174 (Special Rules Pertaining To Specific Vessel Types). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The individual flooding of each of the compartments described in §§ 174.080 and 174.085 must be assumed for the purpose of determining compliance with § 174.065 (a). Simultaneous flooding of more than one compartment must be assumed only when indicated in §§ 174.080 and 174.085.
