48 C.F.R. § 1306.303-70 — 1306.303-70 Documentation and legal review of justifications.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1306.303-70
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1306.303-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1306 (Competition Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The justification for providing for other than full and open competition in accordance with FAR 6.303-2 shall contain sufficient facts and rationale to justify the use of the authority cited and include all of the content set forth in FAR 6.303-2 as appropriate for the cited authority. If the estimated value of the procurement is over legal review thresholds, review by the Procurement Counsel is required.
