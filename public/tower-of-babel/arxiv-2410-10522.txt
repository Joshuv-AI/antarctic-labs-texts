Comment on “A try for dark energy in quantum field theory: The vacuum energy of neutrino field”
James M. Cline

Abstract
It was recently suggested ( https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.06604 ) that the small value of the dark energy of the universe could be explained in terms of the scale of neutrino masses through a simple quantum field theoretic mechanism. I clarify that the quantity computed is only a threshold correction to the vacuum energy at the low m ν m_{\nu} scale, and therefore cannot explain the magnitude of the vacuum energy.
