34 C.F.R. § 222.143 — What terms and conditions apply to the transfer of minimum school facilities?
Citation: us:cfr:34:222.143
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-222.143
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 222 (Impact Aid Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the Secretary transfers to an LEA or other appropriate entity (transferee) facilities that have been used to carry out the purposes of section 10 of Pub. L. 81-815 or section 8008, the Secretary establishes appropriate terms and conditions for the transfer including that it be—
(a) Without charge; and
(b) Consented to by the transferee.
