11 C.F.R. § 100.71 — Scope.

Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-100.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 100 (Scope And Definitions (52 U.S.C. 30101)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The term contribution does not include payments, services or other things of value described in this subpart.
(b) For the purpose of this subpart, a contribution or payment made by an individual shall not be attributed to any other individual, unless otherwise specified by that other individual in accordance with 11 CFR 110.1(k).
