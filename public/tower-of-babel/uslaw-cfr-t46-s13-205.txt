46 C.F.R. § 13.205 — Proof of service for Tank Vessel-PIC endorsement.
Citation: us:cfr:46:13.205
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-13.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 13 (Certification Of Tank Vessel Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Provide evidence in a method prescribed by the Coast Guard of proof of service from the owner, operator, or Master of the vessel on which the applicant obtained the service. The evidence must contain the information described in § 13.127(a).
