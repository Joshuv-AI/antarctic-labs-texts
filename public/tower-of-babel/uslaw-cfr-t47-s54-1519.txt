47 C.F.R. § 54.1519 — Geographic area eligible for transitional mobile support.
Citation: us:cfr:47:54.1519
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-54.1519
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 54 (Universal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Uniendo a Puerto Rico Fund and Connect USVI Fund transitional mobile support may be used for all geographic areas of Puerto Rico or of the U.S. Virgin Islands, respectively, within a recipient's designated eligible telecommunications carrier service area.
