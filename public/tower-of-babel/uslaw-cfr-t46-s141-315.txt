46 C.F.R. § 141.315 — Marking of survival craft and stowage locations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:141.315
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-141.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 141 (Lifesaving). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Survival craft and stowage locations must be marked in accordance with the requirements of §§ 199.176 and 199.178 of this chapter.
