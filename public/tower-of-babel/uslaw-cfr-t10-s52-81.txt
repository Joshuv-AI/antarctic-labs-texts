10 C.F.R. § 52.81 — Standards for review of applications.
Citation: us:cfr:10:52.81
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-52.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 52 (Licenses, Certifications, And Approvals For Nuclear Power Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applications filed under this subpart will be reviewed according to the standards set out in 10 CFR parts 20, 50, 51, 54, 55, 73, 100, and 140.
