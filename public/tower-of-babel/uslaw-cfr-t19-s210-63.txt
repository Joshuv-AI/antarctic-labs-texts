19 C.F.R. § 210.63 — Briefs.
Citation: us:cfr:19:210.63
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-210.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 210 (Adjudication And Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The administrative law judge shall determine whether and, if so, to what extent the parties shall be permitted to file briefs under § 210.40 concerning the issues involved in adjudication of the motion for temporary relief.
