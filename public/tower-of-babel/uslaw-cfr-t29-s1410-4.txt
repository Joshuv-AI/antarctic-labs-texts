29 C.F.R. § 1410.4 — Requirements for identification of individuals making requests.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1410.4
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1410.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1410 (Privacy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Satisfactory identification (i.e., employ identification number, current address, and verification of signature) must be provided to FMCS prior to review of the record. The requestor will be provided the opportunity to review the records during normal business hours.
