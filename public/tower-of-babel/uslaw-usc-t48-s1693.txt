48 U.S.C. § 1693 — Audit of government; access to books, records, etc.
Citation: us:usc:48:1693
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1693
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 14 (Trust Territory Of The Pacific Islands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Comptroller General of the United States, or any of his duly authorized representatives, shall have access, for the purpose of audit and examination, to any relevant books, documents, papers, or records of the government of the Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands.
