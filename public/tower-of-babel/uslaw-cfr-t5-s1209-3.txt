5 C.F.R. § 1209.3 — Application of 5 CFR part 1201.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1209.3
Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1209.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1209 (Practices And Procedures For Appeals And Stay Requests Of Personnel Actions Allegedly Based On Whistleblowing Or Other Protected Activity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as expressly provided in this part, the Board will apply subparts A, B, C, E, F, and G of 5 CFR part 1201 to appeals and stay requests governed by this part. The Board will apply the provisions of subpart H of part 1201 regarding awards of attorney fees, compensatory damages, and consequential damages under 5 U.S.C. 1221(g) to appeals governed by this part.
