42 C.F.R. § 457.370 — Alignment with Exchange initial open enrollment period.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 457 (Allotments And Grants To States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The terms of § 435.1205 apply equally to the State in administering a separate CHIP, except that the State shall make available and accept the application described in § 457.330, shall accept electronic accounts as described in § 457.348, and furnish coverage in accordance with § 457.340.
