25 C.F.R. § 20.305 — What is redetermination?
Citation: us:cfr:25:20.305
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-20.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 20 (Financial Assistance And Social Services Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Redetermination is an evaluation by a social services worker to assess the need for continued financial assistance as outlined in § 20.304. It includes:
(a) A home visit;
(b) An estimate of income, living circumstances, household composition for the month(s) for which financial assistance is to be provided; and
(c) Appropriate revisions to the case plan and the ISP.
