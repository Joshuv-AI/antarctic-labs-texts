25 C.F.R. § 43.13 — Right of appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:25:43.13
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-43.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 43 (Maintenance And Control Of Student Records In Bureau Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any parent or eligible student is adversely affected by the decision of the head of the institution, that party shall have appeal rights as given in 25 CFR part 2. However, each official decision shall be issued within 30 days from receipt of the appeal.
