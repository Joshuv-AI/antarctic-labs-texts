46 C.F.R. § 122.320 — Steering gear, controls, and communication system tests.
Citation: us:cfr:46:122.320
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-122.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 122 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The master of a vessel shall have examined and tested the steering gear, signaling whistle, propulsion controls, and communication systems of the vessel prior to getting underway for a voyage, except that such examination and testing need not be conducted more than once in any 24 hour period.
