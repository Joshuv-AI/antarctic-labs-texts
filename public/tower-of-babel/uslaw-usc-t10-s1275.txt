10 U.S.C. § 1275 — Computation of retired pay: law applicable
Citation: us:usc:10:1275
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1275
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 63 (Retirement For Age). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A member of the armed forces retired under this chapter is entitled to retired pay computed under chapter 71 of this title.
