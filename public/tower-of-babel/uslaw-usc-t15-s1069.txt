15 U.S.C. § 1069 — Application of equitable principles in inter partes proceedings
Citation: us:usc:15:1069
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1069
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 22 (Trademarks); Subchapter I (The Principal Register). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In all inter partes proceedings equitable principles of laches, estoppel, and acquiescence, where applicable may be considered and applied.
