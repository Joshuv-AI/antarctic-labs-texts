14 C.F.R. § 374.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:14:374.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-374.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 374 (Implementation Of The Consumer Credit Protection Act With Respect To Air Carriers And Foreign Air Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to state the Department of Transportation's responsibility to enforce air carrier and foreign air carrier compliance with Subchapters I, III, IV, V and VI of the Consumer Credit Protection Act and Regulations B and Z of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System.
