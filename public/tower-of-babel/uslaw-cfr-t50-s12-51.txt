50 C.F.R. § 12.51 — May I simply abandon my interest in the property?
Citation: us:cfr:50:12.51
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-12.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 12 (Seizure And Forfeiture Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may voluntarily abandon your interest in property to the United States by signing Service Form 3-2096, Fish and Wildlife Abandonment Form, or equivalent Federal, State, Tribal, or local form, or by signed letter to the Service or the Solicitor containing substantially the same information as Form 3-2096.
