10 U.S.C. § 8463 — Midshipmen: discharge for deficiency
Citation: us:usc:10:8463
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8463
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Education And Training); Chapter 853 (United States Naval Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Midshipmen found deficient at any examination shall, unless the Academic Board recommends otherwise, be discharged from the Naval Academy and from the naval service.
