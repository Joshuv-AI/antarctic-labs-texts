41 C.F.R. § 301-10.200 — -10.200 Types of Government vehicles.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-10.200
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-10.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-10 (10—Transportation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees may be authorized to use a Government-furnished automobile, a Government aircraft in accordance with §§ 301-10.260 through 301-10.265, and other types of Government vehicles in accordance with Government-issued rules governing their use.
