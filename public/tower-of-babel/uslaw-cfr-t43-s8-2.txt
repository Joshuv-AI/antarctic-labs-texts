43 C.F.R. § 8.2 — Additional lands for correlative purposes.
Citation: us:cfr:43:8.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-8.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 8 (Joint Policies Of The Departments Of The Interior And Of The Army Relative To Reservoir Project Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The fee title will be acquired for the following:
(a) Such lands as are needed to meet present and future requirements for fish and wildlife as determined pursuant to the Fish and Wildlife Coordination Act.
(b) Such lands as are needed to meet present and future public requirements for outdoor recreation, as may be authorized by Congress.
