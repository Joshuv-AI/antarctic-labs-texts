28 C.F.R. § 551.102 — Commitment prior to arraignment.
Citation: us:cfr:28:551.102
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-551.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 551 (Miscellaneous). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On receipt of a U.S. Marshal remand, the Bureau of Prisons shall accept an individual who has not been arraigned for commitment as a pretrial inmate, provided that the institution has appropriate detention facilities available for that individual.
