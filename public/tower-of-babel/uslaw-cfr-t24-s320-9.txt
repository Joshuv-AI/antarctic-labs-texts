24 C.F.R. § 320.9 — Pool administration.
Citation: us:cfr:24:320.9
Creator: Government National Mortgage Association, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-320.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 320 (Guaranty Of Mortgage-Backed Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Association will only guarantee securities if the issuer executes a guaranty agreement or contractual agreement in the form prescribed by the Association. Pool administration requirements are set forth in such agreements or the applicable MBS Guide.
