41 C.F.R. § 109-1.5000 — -1.5000 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-1.5000
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-1.5000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart supplements the FPMR/FMR, states DOE personal property management policy and program objectives, and prescribes authorities and responsibilities for the conduct of an efficient personal property management program in DOE.
