Best Constant and extremal functions for a class HARDY-SOBOLEV-MAZ’YA INEQUALITIES
Daowen Lin, Xi-Nan Ma

Abstract
. We derive an integral identity for a class p p -Laplace equation, and then classify all positive finite energy cylindrically symmetric solutions of the equation ( 1.2 ) for 3 ≤ k ≤ n − 1 , 3\leq k\leq n-1, with the help of some a prior estimates. Combining this with the result of Secchi-Smets-Willem [ 22 ] , as a consequence, we obtain the best constant and extremal functions for the related Hardy-Sobolev-Maz’ya inequalities.

Introduction

In this paper, we study the positive extremals for the following Hardy-Sobolev-Maz’ya inequalities when
s=1,k\geq 3

Theorem 1.1
.

[
4

\displaystyle\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\frac{|u|^{\frac{p(n-s)}{n-p}}}{|y|^{s}}dx\right)^{\frac{n-p}{p(n-s)}}\leq S_{p,s}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|\nabla u|^{p}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}},

where for

as the closure of

(1.2)

\displaystyle-\Delta_{p}u

\displaystyle=\frac{u^{p^{*}(1)-1}}{|y|}\,\,\textrm{in}\,\,\mathbb{R}^{n},

\displaystyle u

\displaystyle>0,

\displaystyle u

\displaystyle\in D^{1,p}(\mathbb{R}^{n}),

where

,
