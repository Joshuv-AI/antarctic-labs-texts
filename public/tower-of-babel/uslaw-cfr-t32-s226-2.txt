32 C.F.R. § 226.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:32:226.2
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-226.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 226 (Shelter For The Homeless). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to the Office of the Secretary of Defense (OSD), the Military Departments (including their National Guard and Reserve components), the Unified and Specified Commands, the Defense Agencies, and Department of Defense Field Activities (hereafter referred to collectively as “Department of Defense Components”).
