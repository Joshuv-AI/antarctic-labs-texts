15 U.S.C. § 68j — Exceptions from subchapter
Citation: us:usc:15:68j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section68j
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2 (Federal Trade Commission; Promotion Of Export Trade And Prevention Of Unfair Methods Of Competition); Subchapter III (Labeling Of Wool Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

None of the provisions of this subchapter shall be construed to apply to the manufacture, delivery for shipment, shipment, sale, or offering for sale any carpets, rugs, mats, or upholsteries, nor to any person manufacturing, delivering for shipment, shipping, selling, or offering for sale any carpets, rugs, mats, or upholsteries.
