10 C.F.R. § 605.6 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:10:605.6
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-605.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 605 (The Office Of Science Financial Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any university or other institution of higher education or other non-profit or for-profit organization, non-Federal agency, or entity is eligible for a grant or cooperative agreement. An unaffiliated individual also is eligible for a grant or cooperative agreement.
