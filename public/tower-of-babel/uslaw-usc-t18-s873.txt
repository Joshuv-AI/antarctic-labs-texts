18 U.S.C. § 873 — Blackmail
Citation: us:usc:18:873
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section873
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 41 (Extortion And Threats). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, under a threat of informing, or as a consideration for not informing, against any violation of any law of the United States, demands or receives any money or other valuable thing, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
