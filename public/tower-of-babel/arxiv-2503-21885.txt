U U -spin conjugate C ​ P CP violation relations in bottom baryon decays
Bo-nan Zhang 1, Di Wang 2

Abstract
Motivated by the first observation of C ​ P CP violation in baryon decays, we study the relations for C ​ P CP asymmetries of bottom baryon decays. It is found that all the C ​ P CP violation ratios of two U U -spin conjugate decay channels satisfy a simple equation in the U U -spin limit. As examples, we present the U U -spin conjugate channels in the four-body decays of bottom baryons. The C ​ P CP violation ratio between them might provide predictions for unobserved channels and be tested in future experiments.



I
Introduction

Very recently, the experimental advancements by the LHCb Collaboration have discovered
CP
violation in bottom baryon decays with the result of
LHCb:2025ray

\displaystyle A_{CP}(\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to pK^{-}\pi^{+}\pi^{-})=(2.45\pm 0.46\pm 0.10)\%.

(1)

This discovery constitutes a milestone of particle physics since the
CP
asymmetries are well established in meson systems
LHCb:2019hro
;
Belle:2001zzw
;
Christenson:1964fg
;
BaBar:2001ags
, while the
CP
violation in baryon decays had not been observed, except for a sole evidence in the three-body mode
\Lambda_{b}\to\Lambda K^{+}K^{-}

LHCb:2024yzj
, until now.

CP
violation is a crucial element in interpreting the matter-antimatter asymmetry in the Universe
Sakharov:1967dj
and serves as a window in searching for new physics.
Within the Standard Model, CP violation arises naturally through the Kobayashi-Maskawa (KM) mechanism
Cabibbo:1963yz
;
Kobayashi:1973fv
, the study of which plays an important role in testing the Standard Model, and helps us to deepen our understanding the strong and electroweak interactions.

Flavor symmetry, including the
U-
,
V
-spin and isospin symmetry, provides a model-independent tool to study heavy meson and baryon decays.
The
U
-spin symmetry, reflecting the symmetry between the two down-type
d
and
s
quarks, is the most powerful compared to the isospin and
V
-spin symmetry, since it connects the weak phase and
CP
violation in weak decays.
The
U
-spin analysis for the
CP
violation of bottom and charm hadron decays has been performed in literature such as
Wang:2019dls
;
Wang:2024rwf
;
Schacht:2022kuj
;
Gavrilova:2022hbx
;
Dery:2021mll
;
Grossman:2018ptn
;
Bhattacharya:2015uua
;
Gronau:2013mda
;
Gronau:2000zy
.
The
U
-spin conjugation is defined by the following changes:

\displaystyle s\leftrightarrow d,\qquad\overline{s}\leftrightarrow-\overline{d}.

(2)

Two decay channels are
U
-spin conjugate if they have
U
-spin conjugate initial and final states.
For example, the
\Lambda_{b}^{0}\to pK^{-}
and
\Xi_{b}^{0}\to\Sigma^{+}\pi^{-}
decays are two
U
-spin conjugate channels.
In Ref.
Wang:2024rwf
, the authors proposed some relations for the
CP
violation ratios of the three-body bottom baryon decays.
In fact, these relations can be explained by a universal rule for two
U
-spin conjugate channels.
In this work, we illustrate this universal rule, and particularly focus on the four-body decays of bottom baryons, in which the
U
-spin conjugate channels are proved to satisfy this rule.

The rest of this paper is organized as follows. In Sec.
II
, we prove that the
CP
violation ratio between two
U
-spin conjugate channels satisfy a universal law.
In Sec.
III
, we present the
U
-spin conjugate channels in the four-body decays of bottom baryons.
Sec.
IV
presents a brief summary of this work.

II
Universal rule for
U
-spin conjugate channels

In this section, we prove the universal rule about
CP
asymmetries of two
U
-spin conjugate decay channels.
The effective Hamiltonian of
b\to u\overline{u}q
transition is given by
Buchalla:1995vs

\displaystyle\mathcal{H}_{\rm eff}=

\displaystyle{\frac{G_{F}}{\sqrt{2}}}\sum_{q=d,s}\left[V_{ub}^{*}V_{uq}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{2}C_{i}^{u}(\mu)O_{i}^{u}(\mu)\right)+V_{cb}^{*}V_{cq}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{2}C_{i}^{c}(\mu)O_{i}^{c}(\mu)\right)\right]

\displaystyle-{\frac{G_{F}}{\sqrt{2}}}\sum_{q=d,s}\left[V_{tb}V_{tq}^{*}\left(\sum_{i=3}^{10}C_{i}(\mu)O_{i}(\mu)+C_{7\gamma}(\mu)O_{7\gamma}(\mu)+C_{8g}(\mu)O_{8g}(\mu)\right)\right]+h.c..

(3)

In the
SU(3)
picture, the weak Hamiltonian of bottom decay can be written as
Wang:2024ztg
;
Wang:2020gmn

\mathcal{H}_{\rm eff}=\sum_{i,j,k=1}^{3}\{H^{(u)k}_{ij}\mathcal{O}_{ij}^{(u)k}+H^{(c)}_{i}\mathcal{O}_{i}^{(c)}+H^{(p)k}_{ij}\mathcal{O}_{ij}^{(p)k}\},

(4)

where
\mathcal{O}_{ij}^{(u)k}
,
\mathcal{O}_{i}^{(c)}
and
\mathcal{O}_{ij}^{(p)k}
denote the four-quark operator including the Fermi coupling constant
G_{F}
and Wilson coefficients.
Superscripts
u
,
c
and
p
are used to distinguish the tree operators
\mathcal{O}_{1,2}^{(u)}
,
\mathcal{O}_{1,2}^{(c)}
and penguin operators
\mathcal{O}_{3-10}
.
Indices
i
,
j
and
k
are flavor indices.
The matrix
H^{(u,c,p)}
is the coefficient matrix.
Similar to the effective Hamiltonian, the initial and final states, for example light meson
M
, can be written as

\displaystyle|M^{\alpha}\rangle=(M^{\alpha})^{i}_{j}|M^{i}_{j}\rangle,

(5)

where
|M^{i}_{j}\rangle
is the quark composition of meson state,
|M^{i}_{j}\rangle=|q_{i}\bar{q}_{j}\rangle
, and
(M^{\alpha})
is the coefficient matrix.
The decay amplitude, taking the
\mathcal{B}^{\gamma}_{b\overline{3}}\to\mathcal{B}^{\alpha}_{8}M^{\beta}
mode as example, is constructed to be

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(\mathcal{B}^{\gamma}_{b\overline{3}}\to\mathcal{B}_{8}^{\alpha}M^{\beta})

\displaystyle=\langle\mathcal{B}_{8}^{\alpha}M^{\beta}|\mathcal{H}_{\rm eff}|\mathcal{B}^{\gamma}_{b\overline{3}}\rangle

\displaystyle~=\sum_{\omega}\,(\mathcal{B}_{8}^{\alpha})^{ijk}\langle\mathcal{B}_{8}^{ijk}|(M^{\beta})^{l}_{m}\langle M^{l}_{m}||H_{np}^{q}\mathcal{O}_{np}^{q}||(\mathcal{B}^{\gamma}_{b\overline{3}})_{rs}|[\mathcal{B}_{b\overline{3}}]_{rs}\rangle

\displaystyle~~=\sum_{\omega}\,\langle\mathcal{B}_{8}^{ijk}M^{l}_{m}|\mathcal{O}_{np}^{q}|[\mathcal{B}_{b\overline{3}}]_{rs}\rangle\times(\mathcal{B}_{8}^{\alpha})^{ijk}(M^{\beta})^{l}_{m}H_{np}^{q}(\mathcal{B}^{\gamma}_{b\overline{3}})_{rs}

\displaystyle~~~~=\sum_{\omega}X_{\omega}(C_{\omega})_{\alpha\beta\gamma}.

(6)

In above formula,
\sum_{\omega}
represents summing over all possible full contractions, i.e., the invariant tensor
X_{\omega}
, and
(C_{\omega})_{\alpha\beta\gamma}
is the Clebsch-Gordan (CG) coefficient.

If a
U
-spin conjugation transformation is performed to the initial and final states in Eq. (
II
), the indices of operator
\mathcal{O}_{np}^{q}
must be interchanged as
d\leftrightarrow s
to keep all covariant and contravariant indices contracted.
Then the coefficient matrix
H_{np}^{q}
must also have
d
and
s
interchanged.
Similar analysis can be performed to other modes, such as multi-body decays.
According to the effective Hamiltonian of
b\to u\overline{u}q
decays, the non-zero CKM coefficients include:

\displaystyle H^{(u)1}_{21}

\displaystyle=V_{ub}V_{ud}^{*},\qquad H^{(u)1}_{31}=V_{ub}V_{us}^{*},\qquad H^{(c)}_{2}=V_{cb}V_{cd}^{*},\qquad H^{(c)}_{3}=V_{cb}V_{cs}^{*}

\displaystyle H^{(p)1}_{12}

\displaystyle=H^{(p)2}_{22}=H^{(p)3}_{32}=-V_{tb}V_{td}^{*},\qquad H^{(p)1}_{13}=H^{(p)2}_{23}=H^{(p)3}_{33}=-V_{tb}V_{ts}^{*}.

(7)

Under
SU(3)
symmetry,
X_{\omega}
is invariant under the
U
-spin conjugation transformation according to the Wigner-
Eckhart theorem
Eckart30
;
Wigner59
.
The
d
and
s
interchange is a
2\leftrightarrow 3
interchange in Eq. (
II
).
Then we have

\displaystyle V_{ub}V_{ud}^{*}\leftrightarrow V_{ub}V_{us}^{*},\qquad V_{cb}V_{cd}^{*}\leftrightarrow V_{cb}V_{cs}^{*},\qquad V_{tb}V_{td}^{*}\leftrightarrow V_{tb}V_{ts}^{*}

(8)

under the
U
-spin conjugation transformation.

If the decay amplitude of a
\Delta S=0
mode
i\to f
is written as

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(i\to f)=V_{ub}V_{ud}^{*}\mathcal{A}^{u}+V_{cb}V_{cd}^{*}\mathcal{A}^{c}+V_{tb}V_{td}^{*}\mathcal{A}^{t},

(9)

the
U
-spin conjugate mode is a
\Delta S=-1
mode
i^{\prime}\to f^{\prime}

with decay amplitude of

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(i^{\prime}\to f^{\prime})=V_{ub}V_{us}^{*}\mathcal{A}^{u}+V_{cb}V_{cs}^{*}\mathcal{A}^{c}+V_{tb}V_{ts}^{*}\mathcal{A}^{t}.

(10)

The unitarity of the CKM matrix indicates that

\displaystyle V_{ub}V_{us}^{*}+V_{cb}V_{cs}^{*}+V_{tb}V_{ts}^{*}=0.

(11)

Then the decay amplitude of
i\to f
mode is

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(i\to f)

\displaystyle=V_{ub}V_{ud}^{*}\mathcal{A}^{u}+V_{cb}V_{cd}^{*}\mathcal{A}^{c}-(V_{ub}V_{ud}^{*}+V_{cb}V_{cd}^{*})\mathcal{A}^{t}

\displaystyle~~=V_{ub}V_{ud}^{*}(\mathcal{A}^{u}-\mathcal{A}^{t})+V_{cb}V_{cd}^{*}(\mathcal{A}^{c}-\mathcal{A}^{t})=V_{ub}V_{ud}^{*}\mathcal{A}^{\prime u}+V_{cb}V_{cd}^{*}\mathcal{A}^{\prime c}.

(12)

Similarly, the decay amplitude of
i^{\prime}\to f^{\prime}
mode is

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}(i^{\prime}\to f^{\prime})=V_{ub}V_{us}^{*}\mathcal{A}^{\prime u}+V_{cb}V_{cs}^{*}\mathcal{A}^{\prime c}.

(13)

The direct
CP
asymmetry of
i\to f
decay is defined as

\displaystyle A_{CP}^{\rm dir}(i\to f)=\frac{|\mathcal{A}(i\to f)|^{2}-\mathcal{A}(\overline{i}\to\overline{f})|^{2}}{|\mathcal{A}(i\to f)|^{2}+\mathcal{A}(\overline{i}\to\overline{f})|^{2}},

(14)

where
\mathcal{A}(\overline{i}\to\overline{f})=V^{*}_{ub}V_{ud}\mathcal{A}^{\prime u}+V^{*}_{cb}V_{cd}\mathcal{A}^{\prime c}
.
Following by Ref.
Wang:2024rwf
, the relation between
CP
asymmetries of two
U
-spin conjugate modes is derived to be

\displaystyle A_{CP}^{\rm dir}(i^{\prime}\to f^{\prime})\simeq-A_{CP}^{\rm dir}(i\to f)\cdot\frac{\mathcal{B}r(i\to f)\cdot\tau(i^{\prime})}{\mathcal{B}r(i^{\prime}\to f^{\prime})\cdot\tau(i)},

(15)

in which the CKM unitarity relation
\mathcal{I}m(V_{ub}V_{us}^{*}V_{cb}V_{cs}^{*})=\mathcal{I}m(V_{ub}V_{ud}^{*}V_{cb}V_{cd}^{*})
is used.

\displaystyle\Lambda^{0}_{b}\leftrightarrow\Xi^{0}_{b},\quad\Xi^{-}_{b}\leftrightarrow\Xi^{-}_{b},

(16)

Similar to the bottom hadron decays, the
CP

U
-spin conjugate charmed hadron decay channels are connected through a simple rule.
Due to the approximation of the CKM matrix elements
V^{*}_{cd}V_{ud}\simeq-V^{*}_{cs}V_{us}

U

For example,

\displaystyle A_{CP}^{\rm dir}(\Lambda^{+}_{c}\to\Sigma^{+}\pi^{-}K^{+})=-A_{CP}^{\rm dir}(\Xi^{+}_{c}\to pK^{-}\pi^{+}).

(20)

Considering the

Since the

15

However, it indicates that the
CP

U

\displaystyle\Delta A_{CP}

The
CP

IV
Summary

In this work, we studied the relations for
CP
asymmetries of bottom baryon decays.
It is found that there is a universal rule for the
CP
violation ratio between two
U
-spin conjugate modes in the
U
-spin limit.
As examples,
U
-spin conjugate channels in charmless four-body decays are presented to satisfy the universal rule.
The
CP
violation relations between them could provide predictions for unobserved channels and enable testing of
U
-spin symmetry in future measurements.
