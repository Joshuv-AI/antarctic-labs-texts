40 C.F.R. § 420.80 — Applicability; description of the salt bath descaling subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:420.80
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-420.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 420 (Iron And Steel Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges and to the introduction of pollutants into publicly owned treatment works resulting from oxidizing and reducing salt bath descaling operations.
