14 C.F.R. § 1215.110 — User cancellation of all services.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1215.110
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1215.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1215 (Tracking And Data Relay Satellite System (Tdrss)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The user has the right to terminate its service contract with NASA at any time. A user who exercises this right after contracting for service shall pay the charge agreed upon for services previously rendered, and the cost incurred by the Government for support of pre-launch activities, services, and mission documentation not included in that charge. The user will remain responsible for the charges for any services actually provided.
