46 C.F.R. § 130.340 — Compass.
Citation: us:cfr:46:130.340
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-130.340
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 130 (Vessel Control, And Miscellaneous Equipment And Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel must be fitted with a compass suitable for the intended service of the vessel. Except aboard a vessel limited to daytime operation, the compass must be illuminated.
