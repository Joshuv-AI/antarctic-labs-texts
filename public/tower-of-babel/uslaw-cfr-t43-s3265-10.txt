43 C.F.R. § 3265.10 — What part of my drilling operations may BLM inspect?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3265.10
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3265.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) BLM may inspect all of your Federal drilling operations regardless of surface ownership. We will inspect your operations for compliance with the requirements of § 3200.4.
(b) BLM may inspect all of your maps, well logs, surveys, records, books, and accounts related to your Federal drilling operations.
