15 C.F.R. § 231.111 — Predominately serves the market.
Citation: us:cfr:15:231.111
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-231.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 231 (Clawbacks Of Chips Funding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Predominately serves the market means that at least 85 percent of the output of the semiconductor manufacturing facility (e.g., wafers, semiconductor devices, or packages) by value is incorporated into final products (i.e., not an intermediate product that is used as factor inputs for producing other goods) that are used or consumed in that market.
