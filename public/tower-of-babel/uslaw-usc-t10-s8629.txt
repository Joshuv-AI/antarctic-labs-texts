10 U.S.C. § 8629 — Purchase of fuel
Citation: us:usc:10:8629
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8629
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 861 (Secretary Of The Navy: Miscellaneous Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In buying fuel, the Secretary of the Navy may, in any manner he considers proper, buy the kind of fuel that is best adapted to the purpose for which it is to be used.
