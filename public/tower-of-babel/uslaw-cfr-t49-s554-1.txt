49 C.F.R. § 554.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:554.1
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-554.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 554 (Standards Enforcement And Defects Investigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes procedures for enforcing Federal motor vehicle safety standards and associated regulations, investigating possible safety-related defects, and making non-compliance and defect determinations.
