54 U.S.C. § 302906 — Grants and loans to Indian tribes and nonprofit organizations representing ethnic or minority groups
Citation: us:usc:54:302906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section302906
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3029 (Grants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may, in consultation with the appropriate State Historic Preservation Officer, make grants or loans or both under this subdivision to Indian tribes and to nonprofit organizations representing ethnic or minority groups for the preservation of their cultural heritage.
