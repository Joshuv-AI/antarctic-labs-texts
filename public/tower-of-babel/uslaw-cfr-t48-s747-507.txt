48 C.F.R. § 747.507 — 747.507 Contract clauses.
Citation: us:cfr:48:747.507
Creator: Chapter 7—Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-747.507
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 747 (Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officer must insert the clause at 752.247-70 in solicitations and contracts solely for ocean transportation services, and in solicitations and contracts for goods and ocean transportation services when the ocean transportation will be fixed at the time the contract is awarded. Contracting officer must use (48 CFR) FAR 52.247-64 as prescribed in (48 CFR) FAR 47.507(a) in other situations.
