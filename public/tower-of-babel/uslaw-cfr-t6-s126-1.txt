6 C.F.R. § 126.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:6:126.1
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-126.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 126 (Transportation Security Oversight Board Review Panel Process And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes the procedures by which a Transportation Security Oversight Board (TSOB) Review Panel reviews and acts to resolve an appeal from an Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) decision regarding a Determination of Security Threat made by the Administrator of the Transportation Security Administration (TSA).
