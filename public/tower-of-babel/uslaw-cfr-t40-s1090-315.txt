40 C.F.R. § 1090.315 — Heating oil, kerosene, ECA marine fuel, and jet fuel provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1090.315
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1090.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1090 (Regulation Of Fuels, Fuel Additives, And Regulated Blendstocks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Heating oil, kerosene, ECA marine fuel, or jet fuel must not be sold for use in motor vehicles or nonroad equipment and are not subject to the ULSD standards in § 1090.305 unless it is also designated as ULSD under § 1090.1015(a).
