17 C.F.R. § 302.107 — Qualified Financial Contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:17:302.107
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-302.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 302 (Orderly Liquidation Of Covered Brokers Or Dealers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rights and obligations of any party to a qualified financial contract to which a covered broker or dealer is a party shall be governed exclusively by 12 U.S.C. 5390, including the limitations and restrictions contained in 12 U.S.C. 5390(c)(10)(B), and any regulations promulgated thereunder.
