2 U.S.C. § 195 — Fees of witnesses in District of Columbia
Citation: us:usc:2:195
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section195
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 6 (Congressional And Committee Procedure; Investigations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Witnesses residing in the District of Columbia and not in the service of the government of said District or of the United States, who shall be summoned to give testimony before any committee of the House of Representatives, shall not be allowed exceeding $2 for each day’s attendance before said committee.
