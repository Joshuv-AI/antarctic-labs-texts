40 C.F.R. § 82.100 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:40:82.100
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-82.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 82 (Protection Of Stratospheric Ozone). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to require warning statements on containers of, and products containing or manufactured with, certain ozone-depleting substances, pursuant to section 611 of the Clean Air Act, as amended.
