Agency Information Collection Activities: Proposed Request

    The Social Security Administration (SSA) publishes a list of 
information collection packages requiring clearance by the Office of 
Management and Budget (OMB) in compliance with Public Law 104-13, the 
Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995, effective October 1, 1995. This notice 
includes revisions of OMB-approved information collections.

estimate; the need for the information; its practical utility; ways to

    1. Response to Notice of Revised Determination--20 CFR 404.913-
404.914, 404.992(b), 416.1413-416.1414, and 416.1492(d)--0960-0347. In 
certain circumstances, SSA may reopen and revise a final determination. 
When a determination that an individual is disabled is revised based on 
medical factors, advance notice of the revised determination is given. 
In response to this notice, the affected individuals may: (1) request a 
disability hearing to contest SSA's decision; and (2) submit additional 
information or evidence for SSA to consider. Affected individuals and 
their representatives use Form SSA-765 to accomplish these two actions. 
If respondents request the first option, SSA's Disability Hearings Unit 
uses the form to schedule a hearing; ensure an interpreter is present, 
if required; and ensure the disability recipients or claimants, and 
their representatives, receive a notice about the place and time of the 
hearing. If respondents choose the second option, SSA uses Form SSA-765 
and other evidence to reevaluate the individual's case, and to 
determine if the new information or evidence will change SSA's proposed 
revision. The respondents are current disability recipients or their 
representatives.
    Type of Request: Revision of an OMB-approved information 
collection.

    2. Redetermination of Eligibility for Help with Medicare 
Prescription Drug Plan Costs--20 CFR 418.3125--0960-0723. As mandated 
under the Medicare Modernization Act of 2003, SSA conducts low-income 
subsidy (Medicare Part D) eligibility redeterminations for Medicare 
beneficiaries who have limited income and resources and who thus 
currently receive the Medicare Part D subsidy to help with Medicare 
prescription drug plan costs, including premiums, deductibles, and 
copays. SSA asks Medicare Part D subsidy recipients to complete Form 
SSA-1026 (1) for periodic, standard cyclical redeterminations and (2) 
when recipients report a potentially subsidy-changing event. For the 
cyclical, periodic redetermination process, SSA's system automatically 
selects annual respondents based on the following profile and selection 
criteria: (1) individuals who became entitled to the Medicare Part D 
subsidy during the past 12 months; (2) individuals who were eligible 
for the Part D subsidy for more than 12 months; or (3) individuals who 
reported a change in income, resources, or household size. For the 
second category of redeterminations, report of the following life-
changing events would trigger a redetermination and would require 
Medicare Part D beneficiaries to complete the form: (1) marriage; (2) 
spousal separation; (3) divorce; (4) annulment of a marriage; (5) 
spousal death; or (6) moving back in with one's spouse following a 
separation.
    SSA mails a cover letter explaining the specific need for the 
redetermination; a Resource and Income Summary form listing the 
subsidy-eligible individual's income and resource types and amounts, 
and the individual's current household size on record; and the 
appropriate version of Form SSA-1026 (based on the category of 
redetermination) for the beneficiary to complete and return to SSA's 
Wilkes-Barre Direct Operations Center. Once we receive the form, SSA 
technicians scan the information it into the Medicare Application 
Processing System to start the redetermination process. If a 
beneficiary finds the paper process difficult, they may call or visit 
their local field office, and a technician completes the form with them 
through a personal interview.
    The respondents are current recipients of Medicare Part D low-
income subsidy who will undergo an eligibility redetermination for one 
of the reasons mentioned above.
    Type of Request: Revision of an OMB-approved information 
collection.
