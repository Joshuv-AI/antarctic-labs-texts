36 C.F.R. § 72.48 — Federal coordination.
Citation: us:cfr:36:72.48
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-72.48
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 72 (Urban Park And Recreation Recovery Act Of 1978). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants requesting UPARR assistance under one of the three grant categories shall investigate the possibilities of administrative and/or funding coordination with other Federal programs. Higher priority is given to proposals which relate to a comprehensive neighborhood revitalization strategy, including, but not limited to programs such as the Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) Neighborhood Self-Help program.
