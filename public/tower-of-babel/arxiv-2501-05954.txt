Derived Models in PFA
Derek Levinson, Nam Trang

Abstract
We discuss a conjecture of Wilson that under the Proper Forcing Axiom, Θ 0 \Theta_{0} of the derived model at κ \kappa is below κ + \kappa^{+} . We prove the conjecture holds for the old derived model. Assuming mouse capturing in the new derived model, the conjecture holds there as well. We also show Θ < κ + \Theta<\kappa^{+} in the case of the old derived model, and under additional hypotheses for the new derived model.



1
Introduction

In this paper, we prove several results which represent progress on a conjecture of Trevor Wilson relating large cardinals, derived models, and the Proper Forcing Axiom (PFA).

Derived models were invented as a source of models of the axiom of determinacy (AD). Suppose
\kappa
is a limit of Woodin cardinals and
G
is
Col(\omega,<\kappa)
-generic. A relatively simple model of
AD
is

, where

represents the reals in the symmetric collapse of

induced by

in

. The “old derived model” at
\kappa
includes
\mathbb{R}^{*}_{G}
as well as a large collection of set of reals

not contained in

and, like

, can be shown to satisfy
AD^{+}
. The “new derived model” includes even more set of reals and is roughly the largest model of
AD^{+}
contained in

.

Derived models have been extensively studied in the case that

is a mouse (see
[
Ste08
]
). Much less is known about the derived model if

lacks the fine-structural properties of mice. In the opposite direction, we are interested in the derived model in the case where

Theorem 1.1

(Wilson)
.

.

In other words, the theorem says functions from

into

which are ordinal definable in the derived model are bounded in

. On the other hand,
V[G]\models|\mathbb{R}^{*}_{G}|=\kappa
. So the theorem implies the ordinal definable part of the derived model is not close to being all of

.

In a sequel to this paper we will show the hypotheses of Theorem
1.1
imply
\Theta

. For now, we are interested in a different generalization of this theorem. Wilson conjectured the assumption of countable cofinality in Theorem

We also prove the conjecture for the new derived model assuming mouse capturing:
