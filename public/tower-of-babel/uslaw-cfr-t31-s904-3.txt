31 C.F.R. § 904.3 — Preservation of evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:31:904.3
Creator: Federal Claims Collection Standards (Department Of The Treasury—Department Of Justice)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-904.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 904 (Referrals To The Department Of Justice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Referring agencies must take care to preserve all files and records that may be needed by the Department of Justice to prove their claims in court. Agencies ordinarily should include certified copies of the documents that form the basis for the claim in the packages referring their claims to the Department of Justice for litigation. Agencies shall provide originals of such documents immediately upon request by the Department of Justice.
