42 C.F.R. § 419.90 — Basis and scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:42:419.90
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-419.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 419 (Prospective Payment Systems For Hospital Outpatient Department Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Basis. This subpart implements sections 1861(kkk) and 1834(x) of the Act, which establish the rural emergency hospital Medicare provider type and the payment requirements applying to such entities.
(b) Scope. This subpart describes the methodologies used to determine payment for REH services and the monthly facility payment amount paid to REHs.
