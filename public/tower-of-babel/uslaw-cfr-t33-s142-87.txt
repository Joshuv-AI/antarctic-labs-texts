33 C.F.R. § 142.87 — Guarding of deck openings.
Citation: us:cfr:33:142.87
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-142.87
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 142 (Workplace Safety And Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Openings in decks accessible to personnel must be covered, guarded, or otherwise made inaccessible when not in use. The manner of blockage shall prevent a person's foot or body from inadvertently passing through the opening.
