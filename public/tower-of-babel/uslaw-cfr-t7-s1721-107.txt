7 C.F.R. § 1721.107 — Agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1721.107
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1721.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1721 (Post-Loan Policies And Procedures For Insured Electric Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After approval of the Borrower's request for a deferment of principal and interest, an extension agreement, containing the terms of the extension, together with associated materials, will be prepared and forwarded to the Borrower by RUS. The extension agreement will then be executed and returned to RUS by the Borrower.
