34 C.F.R. § 108.9 — Procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:34:108.9
Creator: Office For Civil Rights, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-108.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 108 (Equal Access To Public School Facilities For The Boy Scouts Of America And Other Designated Youth Groups). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedural provisions applicable to title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, which are found in 34 CFR 100.6 through 100.11 and 34 CFR part 101, apply to this part, except that, notwithstanding these provisions and any other provision of law, no funds made available through the Department shall be provided to any school, agency, or school served by an agency that fails to comply with the Act or this part.
