46 U.S.C. § 104 — Citizen of the United States
Citation: us:usc:46:104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section104
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 1 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this title, the term “citizen of the United States”, when used in reference to a natural person, means an individual who is a national of the United States as defined in section 101(a)(22) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(22)).
