16 U.S.C. § 1220b — Obsolete ships available; number; equitable administration
Citation: us:usc:16:1220b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1220b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 25B (Reefs For Marine Life Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A State may apply for more than one obsolete ship under this chapter. The Secretary shall, however, taking into account the number of obsolete ships which may be or become available for transfer under this chapter, administer this chapter in an equitable manner with respect to the various States.
