36 C.F.R. § 1011.3 — Do these regulations adopt the Federal Claims Collections Standards?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1011.3
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1011.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1011 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part adopts and incorporates all provisions of the FCCS. This part also supplements the FCCS by prescribing procedures consistent with the FCCS, as necessary and appropriate for Presidio Trust operations.
