12 U.S.C. § 3418 — Injunctive relief
Citation: us:usc:12:3418
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section3418
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 35 (Right To Financial Privacy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to any other remedy contained in this chapter, injunctive relief shall be available to require that the procedures of this chapter are complied with. In the event of any successful action, costs together with reasonable attorney’s fees as determined by the court may be recovered.
