29 C.F.R. § 779.117 — Salesmen and sales clerks.
Citation: us:cfr:29:779.117
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-779.117
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 779 (The Fair Labor Standards Act As Applied To Retailers Of Goods Or Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A salesman or a sales clerk who regularly and recurrently takes orders for, or sells, or selects merchandise for delivery to points outside the State or which are to be shipped or delivered to a customer from a point outside the State, i.e. drop shipments; or who wraps, packs, addresses or otherwise prepares goods for out-of-State shipments is performing covered activities.
