42 U.S.C. § 2000b–1 — Liability of United States for costs and attorney’s fee
Citation: us:usc:42:2000b–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2000b–1
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 21 (Civil Rights); Subchapter III (Public Facilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In any action or proceeding under this subchapter the United States shall be liable for costs, including a reasonable attorney’s fee, the same as a private person.
