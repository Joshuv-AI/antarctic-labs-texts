33 C.F.R. § 110.6a — a Fore River, Portland Harbor, Portland, Maine.
Citation: us:cfr:33:110.6a
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-110.6a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 110 (Anchorage Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The water area beginning at a point on the shoreline near the Coast Guard Base in Position 43-38 43″ N and 070-14 49″ W; thence 319 to position 43-38 55″ N, 070-15 03″ W; thence 50 to position 43-39 06″ N: 070-14 43″ W: thence 161 to mainland; and thence southwesterly along the shore to the point of beginning.
