12 C.F.R. § 157.20 — What records should I maintain on deposit activities?
Citation: us:cfr:12:157.20
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-157.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 157 (Deposits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You should establish and maintain deposit documentation practices and records that demonstrate that you appropriately administer and monitor deposit-related activities. Your records should adequately evidence ownership, balances, and all transactions involving each account. You may maintain records on deposit activities in any format that is consistent with standard business practices.
