21 C.F.R. § 182.99 — Adjuvants for pesticide chemicals.
Citation: us:cfr:21:182.99
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-182.99
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 182 (Substances Generally Recognized As Safe). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Adjuvants, identified and used in accordance with 40 CFR 180.910 and 40 CFR 180.920, which are added to pesticide use dilutions by a grower or applicator prior to application to the raw agricultural commodity, are exempt from the requirement of tolerances under section 409 of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (21 U.S.C. 348).
