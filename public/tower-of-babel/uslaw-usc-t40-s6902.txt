40 U.S.C. § 6902 — Assignment of right, title, and interest in the Union Station complex to the Secretary of Transportation
Citation: us:usc:40:6902
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section6902
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part C (Federal Building Complexes); Chapter 69 (Union Station Redevelopment); Subchapter I (Union Station Complex). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation has the right, title, and interest in and to the Union Station complex, including all agreements and leases made under sections 101–110 of the National Visitors Center Facilities Act of 1968 (Public Law 90–264, 82 Stat. 43). To the extent the Secretary of Transportation and the Secretary of the Interior agree, the Secretary of the Interior may lease space for visitor services.
