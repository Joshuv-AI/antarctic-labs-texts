29 C.F.R. § 4043.66 — Application for minimum funding waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4043.66
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4043.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4043 (Reportable Events And Certain Other Notification Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Reportable event. Advance notice is required for an application for a minimum funding waiver, as described in § 4043.33.
(b) Extension. The notice date is extended until 10 days after the reportable event has occurred.
