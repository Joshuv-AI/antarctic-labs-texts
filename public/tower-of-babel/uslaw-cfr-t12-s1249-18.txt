12 C.F.R. § 1249.18 — Liability of Enterprises and Federal Reserve Banks.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1249.18
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1249.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1249 (Book-Entry Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An Enterprise and the Federal Reserve Banks may rely on the information provided in a Transfer Message, and are not required to verify the information. An Enterprise and the Federal Reserve Banks shall not be liable for any action taken in accordance with the information set out in a Transfer Message, or evidence submitted in support thereof.
