45 C.F.R. § 1618.2 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1618.2
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1618.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1618 (Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

LSC requirements means the provisions of the LSC Act, the Corporation's appropriations act or other law applicable to LSC funds, a Corporation rule, regulation, guideline or instruction, or the terms or conditions of the recipient's grant or contract with the Corporation.
Violation means a violation by the recipient of the LSC requirements.
