10 U.S.C. § 10213 — Reserve components: dual membership prohibited
Citation: us:usc:10:10213
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10213
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1007 (Administration Of Reserve Components). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in this title, no person may be a member of more than one reserve component at the same time.
