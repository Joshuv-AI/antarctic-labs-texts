10 U.S.C. § 3742 — Adjustment of threshold amount of covered contract
Citation: us:usc:10:3742
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3742
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart D (General Contracting Provisions); Chapter 273 (Allowable Costs); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective on October 1 of each year that is divisible by five, the amount set forth in section 3741(2) of this title shall be adjusted in accordance with section 1908 of title 41.
