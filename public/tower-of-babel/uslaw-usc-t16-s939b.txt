16 U.S.C. § 939b — State laws and regulations
Citation: us:usc:16:939b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section939b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 15A (Great Lakes Fisheries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as preventing any of the Great Lakes States from making or enforcing laws or regulations within their respective jurisdictions so far as such laws or regulations do not conflict with the Convention or this chapter.
