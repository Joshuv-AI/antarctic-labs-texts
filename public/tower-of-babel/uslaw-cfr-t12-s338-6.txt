12 C.F.R. § 338.6 — Definitions applicable to this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:12:338.6
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-338.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 338 (Fair Housing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this subpart—
(a) Bank means an insured State nonmember bank as defined in section 3 of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act, 12 U.S.C. 1813.
(b) Controlled entity means a corporation, partnership, association, or other business entity with respect to which a bank possesses, directly or indirectly, the power to direct or cause the direction of management and policies, whether through the ownership of voting securities, by contract, or otherwise.
(c) FDIC-supervised institution means either a bank or a State savings association.
(d) State savings association has the same meaning as in section 3(b)(3) of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act, 12 U.S.C. 1813(b)(3).
