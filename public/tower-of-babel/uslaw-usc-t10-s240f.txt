10 U.S.C. § 240f — Audits: selection of service providers for audit services
Citation: us:usc:10:240f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section240f
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 9A (Audit). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Department of Defense shall select service providers for auditing services based on the best value to the Department, as determined by the resource sponsor for an auditing contract, rather than based on the lowest price technically acceptable service provider.
