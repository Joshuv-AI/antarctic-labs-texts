25 C.F.R. § 39.140 — How does a school qualify for a Small School Adjustment?
Citation: us:cfr:25:39.140
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-39.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 39 (The Indian School Equalization Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A school will receive a small school adjustment if either:
(a) Its average daily membership (ADM) is less than 100 students; or
(b) It serves lower grades and has a diploma-awarding high school component with an average instructional daily membership of less than 100 students.
