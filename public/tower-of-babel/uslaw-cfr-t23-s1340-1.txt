23 C.F.R. § 1340.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:1340.1
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-1340.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1340 (Uniform Criteria For State Observational Surveys Of Seat Belt Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes uniform criteria for State surveys of seat belt use conducted under 23 U.S.C. 402, procedures for NHTSA approval of survey designs, and administrative requirements relating to State seat belt surveys.
