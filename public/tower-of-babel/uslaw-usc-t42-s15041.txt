42 U.S.C. § 15041 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:42:15041
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section15041
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 144 (Developmental Disabilities Assistance And Bill Of Rights); Subchapter I (Programs For Individuals With Developmental Disabilities); Part C (Protection And Advocacy Of Individual Rights). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this part is to provide for allotments to support a protection and advocacy system (referred to in this part as a “system”) in each State to protect the legal and human rights of individuals with developmental disabilities in accordance with this part.
