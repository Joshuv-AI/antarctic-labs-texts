29 C.F.R. § 1922.5 — Notice of investigational hearings.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1922.5
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1922.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1922 (Investigational Hearings Under Section 41 Of The Longshoremen'S And Harbor Workers' Compensation Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chairman of the Board shall give reasonable notice of the time and place of any investigational hearing to any person whose conduct is or may be pertinent to the subjects of investigation; to any prospective witnesses; and to any Federal or State agency engaged in similar investigative work.
