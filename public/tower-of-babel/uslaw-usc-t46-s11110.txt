46 U.S.C. § 11110 — Seamen’s clothing
Citation: us:usc:46:11110
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section11110
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 111 (Protection And Relief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The clothing of a seaman is exempt from attachments and liens. A person detaining a seaman’s clothing shall be fined not more than $500, imprisoned for not more than 6 months, or both.
