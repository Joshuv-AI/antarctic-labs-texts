52 U.S.C. § 10311 — Impairment of voting rights of persons holding current registration
Citation: us:usc:52:10311
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section10311
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 103 (Enforcement Of Voting Rights). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in chapters 103 to 107 of this title shall be construed to deny, impair, or otherwise adversely affect the right to vote of any person registered to vote under the law of any State or political subdivision.
