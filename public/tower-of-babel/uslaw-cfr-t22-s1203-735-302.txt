22 C.F.R. § 1203.735-302 — -302 Use of Government employment.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1203.735-302
Creator: United States International Development Cooperation Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1203.735-302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1203 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A special Government employee shall not use Government employment for a purpose that is, or gives the appearance of being, motivated by the desire for private gain for the employee or another person, particularly one with whom the employee has family, business, or financial ties.
