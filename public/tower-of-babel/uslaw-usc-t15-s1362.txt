15 U.S.C. § 1362 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:15:1362
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1362
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 37 (State Technical Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to establish such policies, standards, criteria, and procedures and to prescribe such rules and regulations as he may deem necessary or appropriate for the administration of this chapter.
