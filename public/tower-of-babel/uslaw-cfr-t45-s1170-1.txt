45 C.F.R. § 1170.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1170.1
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1170.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1170 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Federally Assisted Programs Or Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to implement section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, which is designed to eliminate discrimination on the basis of handicap in any program or activity receiving Federal financial assistance.
