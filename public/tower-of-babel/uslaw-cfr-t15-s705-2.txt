15 C.F.R. § 705.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:15:705.2
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-705.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 705 (Effect Of Imported Articles On The National Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations set forth the procedures by which the Department shall commence and conduct an investigation to determine the effect on the national security of the imports of any article. Based on this investigation, the Secretary shall make a report and recommendation to the President for action or inaction regarding an adjustment of the imports of the article.
