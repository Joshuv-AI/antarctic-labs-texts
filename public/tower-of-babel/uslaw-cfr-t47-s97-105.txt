47 C.F.R. § 97.105 — Control operator duties.
Citation: us:cfr:47:97.105
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-97.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 97 (Amateur Radio Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The control operator must ensure the immediate proper operation of the station, regardless of the type of control.
(b) A station may only be operated in the manner and to the extent permitted by the privileges authorized for the class of operator license held by the control operator.
