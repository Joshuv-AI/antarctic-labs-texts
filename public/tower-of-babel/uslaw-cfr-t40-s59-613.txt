40 C.F.R. § 59.613 — What operation and maintenance instructions must I give to buyers?
Citation: us:cfr:40:59.613
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-59.613
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 59 (National Volatile Organic Compound Emission Standards For Consumer And Commercial Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must provide the ultimate purchaser of the new portable fuel container written instructions for properly maintaining and using the emission-control system.
