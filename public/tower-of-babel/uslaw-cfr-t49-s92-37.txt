49 C.F.R. § 92.37 — Non-waiver of rights by payment.
Citation: us:cfr:49:92.37
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-92.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 92 (Recovering Debts To The United States By Salary Offset). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee's payment or agreement to pay, whether voluntary or involuntary, of all or any portion of an alleged debt being collected pursuant to these procedures shall not be construed as a waiver of any rights which the employee may have under this part to the extent of such payment or agreement.
