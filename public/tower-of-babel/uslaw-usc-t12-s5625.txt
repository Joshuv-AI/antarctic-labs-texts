12 U.S.C. § 5625 — Procedural provisions
Citation: us:usc:12:5625
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section5625
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 53 (Wall Street Reform And Consumer Protection); Subchapter VII (Improving Access To Mainstream Financial Institutions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An eligible entity desiring to participate in a program or obtain a grant under this subchapter shall submit an application to the Secretary, in such form and containing such information as the Secretary may require.
