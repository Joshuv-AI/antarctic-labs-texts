45 C.F.R. § 2522.300 — What are the application requirements for AmeriCorps program grants?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2522.300
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2522.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2522 (Americorps Participants, Programs, And Applicants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All eligible applicants seeking AmeriCorps program grants must—
(a) Provide a description of the specific program(s) being proposed, including the type of program and of how it meets the minimum program requirements described in § 2522.100; and
(b) Comply with any additional requirements as specified by AmeriCorps in the application package.
