Covariant dynamics from static spherically symmetric geometries
Cong Zhang, Zhoujian Cao

Abstract
We show that any one-parameter family of static, spherically symmetric spacetime metrics can be embedded as the unique vacuum solution set of a generally covariant theory of pure gravity. These theories, involving at most second-order derivatives of metrics, are uniquely specified up to two arbitrary functions that leave the metric unchanged, by a recently derived covariance condition in the Hamiltonian framework. This result solves the inverse problem of Birkhoff’s theorem in full generality and establishes a systematic approach for reconstructing covariant dynamics from a given geometry. The framework applies broadly to quantum-gravity-inspired metrics, including regular black hole models, and provides a new tool for probing their dynamical and observational foundations.

Beyond observational implications, exploring metrics that deviate from GR is theoretically motivated by addressing fundamental issues like spacetime singularities.

and

VI
Conclusion and discussion

This work establishes a foundational result: any one-parameter family of static, spherically symmetric metric can be associated with a family of underlying generally covariant theories for which the metrics emerges as the solutions. Remarkably, within each such theory, the given one-parameter family of metrics forms the unique solution space of the theory, demonstrating that a generalized version of Birkhoff’s theorem remains valid. The freedom in constructing these theories is characterized by arbitrary functions
\Xi
and
\mathcal{R}
.

As demonstrated in
Zhang et al. 2025
;
Zhang et al. 2024
, Eq. (
1
) follows from three fundamental assumptions: 1) the diffeomorphism constraint remains its classical form; 2) the constraint algebra retains its geometric interpretation as generating hypersurface deformations; 3) the effective Hamiltonian constraint depends only on the same basic scalar combinations
s_{a}
as in the classical theory. Therefore, given a static metric, the underlying generally covariant theory is uniquely fixed, up to the choice of the functions
\Xi
and
\mathcal{R}
, as long as these physically motivated assumptions are imposed. Moreover, the condition 3) ensures that each of such theory contains at most second order derivatives of metrics.
This complete and robust reconstruction framework resolves the covariant origin of spherically symmetric and static spacetimes, providing a solid foundation for exploring quantum-gravity-inspired and regular BH geometries.
