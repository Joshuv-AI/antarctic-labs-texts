19 C.F.R. § 207.42 — Investigation continued upon request.
Citation: us:cfr:19:207.42
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-207.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 207 (Investigations Of Whether Injury To Domestic Industries Results From Imports Sold At Less Than Fair Value Or From Subsidized Exports To The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of advice from the administering authority that it has received a request for the continuation of a suspended investigation pursuant to section 704(g) or 734(g) of the Act, the Commission shall continue the investigation. The procedures set forth in subparts B and C of this part, including applicable time limitations, shall apply to all continued investigations within this rule.
