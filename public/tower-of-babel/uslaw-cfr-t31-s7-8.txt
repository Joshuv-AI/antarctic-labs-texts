31 C.F.R. § 7.8 — Delegation.
Citation: us:cfr:31:7.8
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-7.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 7 (Employee Inventions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The heads of bureaus or offices and the General Counsel may delegate, as appropriate, the performance of the responsibilities assigned to them under this part.
