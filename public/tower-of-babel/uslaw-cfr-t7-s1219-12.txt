7 C.F.R. § 1219.12 — Hass avocado.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1219.12
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1219.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1219 (Hass Avocado Promotion, Research, And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Hass avocado means the fruit grown in or imported into the United States of the species Persea americana Mill., or other type of avocados that, in the determination of the Board, with approval of the Secretary, is so similar to the Hass variety avocado as to be indistinguishable to consumers in fresh form. The term shall include all fruit in fresh, frozen, or any other processed form.
