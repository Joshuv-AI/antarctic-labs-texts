16 U.S.C. § 718t — Termination of State participation
Citation: us:usc:16:718t
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section718t
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 7 (Protection Of Migratory Game And Insectivorous Birds); Subchapter IV–A (Permanent Electronic Duck Stamp). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authority of a State to issue electronic stamps under this subchapter may be terminated—
(1)
by the Secretary, if the Secretary—
(A)
finds that the State has violated any of the terms of the application of the State approved by the Secretary under section 718q of this title; and
(B)
provides to the State written notice of the termination by not later than the date that is 30 days before the date of termination; or
(2)
by the State, by providing written notice to the Secretary by not later than the date that is 30 days before the termination date.
