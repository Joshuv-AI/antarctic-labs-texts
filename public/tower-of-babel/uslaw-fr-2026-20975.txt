    Title of Collection: IHE Considerations Around Program Closure: How 
External Pressures and Internal Policies Shape Decision Making.
    OMB Control Number: 1850-NEW.
    Type of Review: A new ICR.
    Respondents/Affected Public: State, Local, and Tribal Governments.
    Total Estimated Number of Annual Responses: 2,160.
    Total Estimated Number of Annual Burden Hours: 384.
    Abstract: The Institute of Education Sciences (IES) within the U.S. 
Department of Education (ED) requests public comment in advance of a 
request for clearance from the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) to 
conduct new data collection activities for the IHE Considerations 
Around Program Closure: How External Pressures and Internal Policies 
Shape Decision Making study. This is the first and only request for the 
collection of data for this study.
    The purpose of the planned information collection is to understand 
the factors linked to program closure and suspension in U.S. 
institutions of higher education (IHEs), including how IHE policies and 
procedures shape these program decisions. The study will combine extant 
data with new data collection, including a national survey of IHEs that 
appear to have closed or suspended programs since 2022 and structured 
interviews with institutional leaders. This mixed-methods approach will 
reveal patterns of closure, explain decision-making processes, and 
explore how IHE governance and human

[[Page 65201]]

resources (HR) policies play a role in decision making.
