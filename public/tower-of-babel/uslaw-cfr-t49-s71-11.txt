49 C.F.R. § 71.11 — Alaska zone.
Citation: us:cfr:49:71.11
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-71.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 71 (Standard Time Zone Boundaries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The sixth zone, the Alaska standard time zone, includes the entire State of Alaska, except as provided in § 71.12 of this title.
