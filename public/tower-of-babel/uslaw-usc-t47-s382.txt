47 U.S.C. § 382 — Vessels excepted from radiotelephone requirement
Citation: us:usc:47:382
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section382
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 5 (Wire Or Radio Communication); Subchapter III (Special Provisions Relating To Radio); Part III (Radio Installations On Vessels Carrying Passengers For Hire). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this part shall not apply to—
(1)
vessels which are equipped with a radio installation in accordance with the provisions of part II of this subchapter, or in accordance with the radio requirements of the Safety Convention; and
(2)
vessels of the United States belonging to and operated by the Government, and
(3)
vessels navigating on the Great Lakes.
