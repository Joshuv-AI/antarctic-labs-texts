36 C.F.R. § 72.40 — Historic properties.
Citation: us:cfr:36:72.40
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-72.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 72 (Urban Park And Recreation Recovery Act Of 1978). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Properties listed in or determined eligible for listing in the National Register of Historic Places must be treated in accordance with the Advisory Council on Historic Preservation procedures described in 36 CFR 800, “Protection of Historic and Cultural Properties.” Applicants must identify such properties in the preapplication if they are situated at a UPARR grant site.
