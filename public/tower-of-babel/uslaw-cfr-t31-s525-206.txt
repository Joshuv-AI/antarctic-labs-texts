31 C.F.R. § 525.206 — Evasions; attempts; causing violations; conspiracies.
Citation: us:cfr:31:525.206
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-525.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 525 (Burma Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any transaction on or after the effective date that evades or avoids, has the purpose of evading or avoiding, causes a violation of, or attempts to violate any of the prohibitions set forth in this part is prohibited.
(b) Any conspiracy formed to violate the prohibitions set forth in this part is prohibited.
