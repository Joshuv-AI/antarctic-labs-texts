33 C.F.R. § 105.550 — Alternative screening.
Citation: us:cfr:33:105.550
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-105.550
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 105 (Maritime Security: Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the owner or operator of a U.S. cruise ship terminal chooses to screen using equipment or methods other than those described in § 105.545, the equipment and methods must be described in detail in an approved TSP.
