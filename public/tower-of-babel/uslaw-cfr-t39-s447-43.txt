39 C.F.R. § 447.43 — Investigation and enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:39:447.43
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-447.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 447 (Rules Of Conduct For Postal Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of the Special Counsel and the Merit Systems Protection Board investigate and adjudicate allegations of political activity in violation of the regulations of the Office of Personnel Management by Postal Service employees. For jurisdiction in such a case, see 5 CFR 734.102 and part 1201.
