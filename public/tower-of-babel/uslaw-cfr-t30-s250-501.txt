30 C.F.R. § 250.501 — Definition.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 250 (Oil And Gas And Sulphur Operations In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When used in this subpart, the following term shall have the meaning given below:
Well-completion operations means the work conducted to establish the production of a well after the production-casing string has been set, cemented, and pressure-tested.
