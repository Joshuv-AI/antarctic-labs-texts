42 C.F.R. § 600.315 — Certified application counselors.
Citation: us:cfr:42:600.315

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 600 (Administration, Eligibility, Essential Health Benefits, Performance Standards, Service Delivery Requirements, Premium And Cost Sharing, Allotments, And Reconcilation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State may have a program to certify application counselors to assist individuals to apply for enrollment in the BHP and other insurance affordability programs. If the State chooses this option, the State must follow the procedures and standards for such a program set forth in the regulations at either 45 CFR 155.225 or 42 CFR 435.908.
