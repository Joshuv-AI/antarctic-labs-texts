48 C.F.R. § 49.402-5 — 49.402-5 Memorandum by the contracting officer.
Citation: us:cfr:48:49.402-5
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-49.402-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 49 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a contract is terminated for default or a procedure authorized by 49.402-4 is followed, the contracting officer shall prepare a memorandum for the contract file explaining the reasons for the action taken.
