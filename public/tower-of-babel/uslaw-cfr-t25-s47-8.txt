25 C.F.R. § 47.8 — Who develops the local educational financial plans?
Citation: us:cfr:25:47.8
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-47.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 47 (Uniform Direct Funding And Support For Bureau-Operated Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The local Bureau-operated school supervisor develops the local educational financial plan in active consultation with the local school board, based on the tentative allotment received.
