21 C.F.R. § 112.81 — How do the requirements of this subpart apply to areas where covered activities take place?
Citation: us:cfr:21:112.81
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-112.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 112 (Standards For The Growing, Harvesting, Packing, And Holding Of Produce For Human Consumption). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The requirements of this subpart apply when a covered activity takes place in an outdoor area or a partially-enclosed building and when, under the circumstances, there is a reasonable probability that animals will contaminate covered produce.
(b) The requirements of this subpart do not apply:
(1) When a covered activity takes place in a fully-enclosed building; or
(2) To fish used in aquaculture operations.
