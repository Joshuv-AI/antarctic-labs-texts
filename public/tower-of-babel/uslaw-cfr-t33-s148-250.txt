33 C.F.R. § 148.250 — Who must be served before a document is filed?
Citation: us:cfr:33:148.250
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-148.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 148 (Deepwater Ports: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before a document may be filed by any party, it first must be served upon:
(a) All other parties; and
(b) The Commandant (CG-5P).
