10 C.F.R. § 835.602 — Controlled areas.

Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-835.602
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 835 (Occupational Radiation Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each access point to a controlled area (as defined at § 835.2) shall be posted whenever radiological areas or radioactive material areas exist in the area. Individuals who enter only controlled areas without entering radiological areas or radioactive material areas are not expected to receive a total effective dose of more than 0.1 rem (0.001 sievert) in a year.
(b) Signs used for this purpose may be selected by the contractor to avoid conflict with local security requirements.
