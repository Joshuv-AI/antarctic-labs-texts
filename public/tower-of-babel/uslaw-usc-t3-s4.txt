3 U.S.C. § 4 — Vacancies in electoral college
Citation: us:usc:3:4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title3-section4
Context: United States Code, Title 3 (The President); Chapter 1 (Presidential Elections And Vacancies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each State may, by law enacted prior to election day, provide for the filling of any vacancies which may occur in its college of electors when such college meets to give its electoral vote.
