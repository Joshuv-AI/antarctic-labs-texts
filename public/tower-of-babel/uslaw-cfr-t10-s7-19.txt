10 C.F.R. § 7.19 — Advisory committee members with disabilities.
Citation: us:cfr:10:7.19
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-7.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 7 (Advisory Committees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

While performing advisory committee duties, an advisory committee member with disabilities may be provided the same services by a personal assistant as those that may be provided to employees per 5 U.S.C. 3102.
