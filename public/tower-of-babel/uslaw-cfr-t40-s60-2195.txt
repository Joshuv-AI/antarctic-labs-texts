40 C.F.R. § 60.2195 — What information must I submit prior to initial startup?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.2195
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.2195
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must submit the information specified in paragraphs (a) through (e) of this section prior to initial startup:
(a) The type(s) of waste to be burned;
(b) The maximum design waste burning capacity;
(c) The anticipated maximum charge rate;
(d) If applicable, the petition for site-specific operating limits under § 60.2115; and
(e) The anticipated date of initial startup.
