Duke Energy Progress, LLC, Duke Energy Carolinas, LLC; Notice of 
Application of Transfer of License and Soliciting Comments, Motions To 
Intervene, and Protests

    On February 4, 2026, Duke Energy Progress, LLC (transferor) and 
Duke Energy Carolinas, LLC (transferee) filed an application for a 
transfer of license for the 108.6-megawatt Walters Hydroelectric 
Project No. 432. The project is located on the Pigeon River in Haywood 
County, North Carolina.
    Pursuant to 16 U.S.C. 801, the applicants seek Commission approval 
to transfer the license for the project from Duke Energy Progress, LLC 
to Duke Energy Carolinas, LLC. The transferee will be required by the 
Commission to comply with all the requirements of the license as though 
it were the original licensee.
