10 U.S.C. § 715 — Attending Physician to the Congress: grade
Citation: us:usc:10:715
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section715
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 41 (Special Appointments, Assignments, Details, And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A general officer serving as Attending Physician to the Congress, while so serving, holds the grade of major general. A flag officer serving as Attending Physician to the Congress, while so serving, holds the grade of rear admiral (upper half).
