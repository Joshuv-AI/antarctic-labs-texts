16 U.S.C. § 4412 — Limitation on assessments against Migratory Bird Conservation Fund
Citation: us:usc:16:4412
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4412
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 64 (North American Wetlands Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, only those personnel and administrative costs directly related to acquisition of real property shall be levied against the Migratory Bird Conservation Account.1
