45 C.F.R. § 1326.104 — Evidentiary purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1326.104
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1326.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1326 (Developmental Disabilities Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The hearing is directed to receiving factual evidence and expert opinion testimony related to the issues in the proceeding. Argument will not be received in evidence; rather, it must be presented in statements, memoranda, or briefs, as directed by the presiding officer. Brief opening statements, which shall be limited to a statement of the party's position and what it intends to prove, may be made at hearings.
