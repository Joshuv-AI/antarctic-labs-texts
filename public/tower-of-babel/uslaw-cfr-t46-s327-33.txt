46 C.F.R. § 327.33 — Claim denial presumption.
Citation: us:cfr:46:327.33
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-327.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 327 (Seamen'S Claims; Administrative Action And Litigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If MARAD fails to give written notice of acceptance or denial of a claim in accordance with § 327.30 within six months following the date of receipt of such a claim by the proper person designated in § 327.24(b), such claim will be presumed to have been denied by MARAD.
