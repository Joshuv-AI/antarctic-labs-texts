20 U.S.C. § 10011 — Prohibition on provision of certain assistance
Citation: us:usc:20:10011
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section10011
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 80 (State Fiscal Stabilization Fund). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No recipient of funds under this chapter shall use such funds to provide financial assistance to students to attend private elementary or secondary schools, unless such funds are used to provide special education and related services to children with disabilities, as authorized by the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (20 U.S.C. 1400 et seq.).
