15 U.S.C. § 2671 — Additional authorizations
Citation: us:usc:15:2671
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2671
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 53 (Toxic Substances Control); Subchapter III (Indoor Radon Abatement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amounts authorized to be appropriated in this subchapter for purposes of carrying out the provisions of this subchapter are in addition to amounts authorized to be appropriated under other provisions of law for radon-related activities.
