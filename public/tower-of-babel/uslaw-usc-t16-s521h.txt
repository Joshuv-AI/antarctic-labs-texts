16 U.S.C. § 521h — Regulations; contents
Citation: us:usc:16:521h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section521h
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 2 (National Forests); Subchapter I (Establishment And Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall issue regulations to carry out the provisions of sections 521c to 521i of this title, including specification of—
(1)
criteria which shall be used in making the determination as to what constitutes the public interest;
(2)
the definition of and the procedure for determining “approximately equal value”; and
(3)
factors relating to location or size which shall be considered in connection with determining the lands to be sold, exchanged, or interchanged under clause (1) of section 521e of this title.
