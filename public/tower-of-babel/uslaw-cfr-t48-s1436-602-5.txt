48 C.F.R. § 1436.602-5 — 1436.602-5 Short selection processes for contracts not to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1436.602-5
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1436.602-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1436 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At each occurrence, CO approval shall be obtained prior to the utilization of either of the short selection processes used for architect-engineer contracts not expected to exceed the simplified acquisition threshold.
