33 C.F.R. § 25.401 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:33:25.401
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-25.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 25 (Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the requirements for the administrative settlement of claims against the United States arising out of the activities of the Coast Guard under the Military Claims Act.
