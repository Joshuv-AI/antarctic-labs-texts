29 C.F.R. § 793.8 — “News editor.”
Citation: us:cfr:29:793.8
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-793.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 793 (EXEMPTION OF CERTAIN RADIO AND TELEVISION STATION EMPLOYEES FROM OVERTIME PAY REQUIREMENTS UNDER SECTION 13(b)(9) OF THE FAIR LABOR STANDARDS ACT). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A news editor is an employee who gathers, edits and rewrites the news. He may also select and prepare news items for broadcast and present the news on the air. An employee who is primarily engaged in the above duties and in activities which are an integral part thereof will be considered to be employed as a news editor within the meaning of the exemption in section 13(b)(9).
