36 C.F.R. § 1250.24 — Does NARA accept electronic FOIA requests?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1250.24
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1250.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1250 (Nara Records Subject To Foia). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. You may submit and track requests for NARA operational records through the FOIAonline program, accessible at https://foiaonline.regulations.gov, or by sending an email to FOIA@nara.gov. The body of the message must contain all of the information listed in 36 CFR 1250.20. You may also file a FOIA request by emailing your request to the offices listed in the table at 36 CFR 1250.22.
