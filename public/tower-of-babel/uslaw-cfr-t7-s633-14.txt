7 C.F.R. § 633.14 — Debt collection.
Citation: us:cfr:7:633.14
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-633.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 633 (Water Bank Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any debts arising under this program are governed with respect to their collection by the Federal Claims Collection Act of 1966 (31 U.S.C. 3701) and the regulations found in 4 CFR chapter II.
