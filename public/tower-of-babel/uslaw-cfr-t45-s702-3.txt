45 C.F.R. § 702.3 — Notice of hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:45:702.3
Creator: Commission On Civil Rights
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-702.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 702 (Rules On Hearings, Reports, And Meetings Of The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At least 30 days prior to the commencement of any hearing, the Commission shall publish in the Federal Register notice of the date on which such hearing is to commence, the location at which it is to be held, and the subject of the hearing.
