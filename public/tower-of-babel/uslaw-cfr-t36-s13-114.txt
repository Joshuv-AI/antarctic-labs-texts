36 C.F.R. § 13.114 — Appeal procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:36:13.114
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-13.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 13 (National Park System Units In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedures set forth in § 13.55(b) govern appeals of a permit denial, a denial of a permit renewal, a permit revocation and a superintendent's final decision on a permit revocation issued pursuant to § 13.112(a).
