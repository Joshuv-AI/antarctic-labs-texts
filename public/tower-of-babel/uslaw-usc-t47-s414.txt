47 U.S.C. § 414 — Exclusiveness of chapter
Citation: us:usc:47:414
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section414
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 5 (Wire Or Radio Communication); Subchapter IV (Procedural And Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter contained shall in any way abridge or alter the remedies now existing at common law or by statute, but the provisions of this chapter are in addition to such remedies.
