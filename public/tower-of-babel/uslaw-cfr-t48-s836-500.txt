48 C.F.R. § 836.500 — 836.500 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:836.500
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-836.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 836 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The clauses and provisions prescribed in this subpart are set forth for use in fixed-price construction contracts in addition to those prescribed in FAR subpart 36.5.
