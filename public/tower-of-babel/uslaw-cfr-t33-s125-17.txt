33 C.F.R. § 125.17 — Persons eligible for Coast Guard Port Security Cards.
Citation: us:cfr:33:125.17
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-125.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 125 (Identification Credentials For Persons Requiring Access To Waterfront Facilities Or Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Only the following persons may be issued Coast Guard Port Security Cards:
(1) Persons regularly employed on vessels or on waterfront facilities.
(2) Persons having regular public or private business connected with the operation, maintenance, or administration of vessels, their cargoes, or waterfront facilities.
(b) A holder of a Merchant Mariner's Document shall not be issued a Port Security Card, unless his Merchant Mariner's Document is surrendered to the Coast Guard. In this connection, see § 125.09.
