29 C.F.R. § 4043.24 — Termination or partial termination.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4043.24
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4043.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4043 (Reportable Events And Certain Other Notification Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Reportable event. A reportable event occurs when the Secretary of the Treasury determines that there has been a termination or partial termination of a plan within the meaning of section 411(d)(3) of the Code.
(b) Waiver. Notice is waived for this event.
