29 C.F.R. § 548.502 — Other payments.
Citation: us:cfr:29:548.502
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 548 (Authorization Of Established Basic Rates For Computing Overtime Pay). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Extra overtime compensation must be separately computed and paid on payments such as bonuses or shift differentials which are not included in the computation of the established basic rate and which would have been included in the regular rate of pay. 26
