5 C.F.R. § 315.908 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:5:315.908
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-315.908
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 315 (Career And Career-Conditional Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An employee who, in accordance with the provisions of this subpart, is assigned to a nonmanagerial or nonsupervisory position, has no appeal right.
(b) An employee who alleges that an agency action under this subpart was based on partisan political reasons or marital status may appeal to the Office of Personnel Management using the procedures in 5 CFR part 751.
