20 C.F.R. § 323.6 — Treatment of benefit payments under a nongovernmental plan for purposes of contributions.
Citation: us:cfr:20:323.6
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-323.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 323 (Nongovernmental Plans For Unemployment Or Sickness Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Benefit payments under nongovernmental plans approved by the Board under this part are not compensation as defined in section 1(i) of the Railroad Unemployment Insurance Act, and therefore they are not subject to contribution under part 345 of this chapter.
