48 U.S.C. § 2152 — Intervention in litigation
Citation: us:usc:48:2152
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section2152
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 20 (Puerto Rico Oversight, Management, And Economic Stability); Subchapter II (Responsibilities Of Oversight Board). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Intervention
The Oversight Board may intervene in any litigation filed against the territorial government.
(b) Injunctive relief
(1) In general
If the Oversight Board intervenes in a litigation under subsection (a), the Oversight Board may seek injunctive relief, including a stay of litigation.
(2) No independent basis for relief
This section does not create an independent basis on which injunctive relief, including a stay of litigation, may be granted.
