36 U.S.C. § 80510 — Service of process
Citation: us:usc:36:80510
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section80510
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 805 (Gold Star Wives Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall comply with the law on service of process of each State in which it is incorporated and each State in which it carries on activities.
