22 C.F.R. § 504.12 — Procedure when a decision is not made prior to the time a response is required.
Citation: us:cfr:22:504.12
Creator: United States Agency For Global Media
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-504.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 504 (Testimony By Bbg Employees, Production Of Official Records, And Disclosure Of Official Information In Legal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a response to a demand or request is required before the General Counsel can make the determination referred to in § 504.9, the General Counsel, when necessary, will provide the court or other competent authority with a copy of this part, inform the court or other competent authority that the request is being reviewed, provide an estimate as to when a decision will be made, and seek a stay of the demand or request pending a final determination.
