7 C.F.R. § 800.150 — Maintenance and retention of records on fee schedules.
Citation: us:cfr:7:800.150
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Federal Grain Inspection Service, Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-800.150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 800 (General Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies, contractors, and approved scale testing organizations shall maintain complete records on fee schedules. These records consist of (a) a copy of the current fee schedule; (b) in the case of an agency, data showing how the fees in the schedule were developed; (c) superseded fee schedules; and (d) related information required by the Service. These records shall be maintained for 5 years.
