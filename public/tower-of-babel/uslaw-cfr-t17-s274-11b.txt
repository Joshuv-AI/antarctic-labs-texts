17 C.F.R. § 274.11b — b Form N-3, registration statement of separate accounts organized as management investment companies.
Citation: us:cfr:17:274.11b
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-274.11b
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 274 (Forms Prescribed Under The Investment Company Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Form N-3 shall be used as the registration statement to be filed pursuant to section 8(b) of the Investment Company Act of 1940 by separate accounts that offer variable annuity contracts to register as management investment companies. This form shall also be used for registration under the Securities Act of 1933 of the securities of such separate accounts (§ 239.17a of this chapter).
