14 U.S.C. § 2707 — Protection of uniform
Citation: us:usc:14:2707
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2707
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 27 (Pay, Allowances, Awards, And Other Rights And Benefits); Subchapter I (Personnel Rights And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of law relating to the protection of the uniform of the United States Army, Navy, or Marine Corps shall apply to the protection of the uniform of the Coast Guard, in the same manner, to the same extent, and under the same conditions.
