20 C.F.R. § 725.480 — Modification of decisions and orders.
Citation: us:cfr:20:725.480
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-725.480
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 725 (Claims For Benefits Under Part C Of Title Iv Of The Federal Mine Safety And Health Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A party who is dissatisfied with a decision and order which has become final in accordance with § 725.479 may request a modification of the decision and order if the conditions set forth in § 725.310 are met.
