15 C.F.R. § 970.509 — Notice of issuance or transfer.
Citation: us:cfr:15:970.509
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-970.509
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 970 (Deep Seabed Mining Regulations For Exploration Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Administrator finds that the requirements of this part have been met, he will issue or transfer the license along with the appropriate terms, conditions and restrictions. Notification thereof will be made in writing to the applicant and in the Federal Register.
