17 C.F.R. § 5.24 — Applicability of other parts of this chapter
Citation: us:cfr:17:5.24
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-5.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 5 (Off-Exchange Foreign Currency Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insofar as it is consistent with the requirements of this part, all other provisions of this chapter that apply to a person shall apply to such person as though such provisions were expressly set forth in this part.
