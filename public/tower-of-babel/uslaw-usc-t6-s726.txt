6 U.S.C. § 726 — Basic life supporting first aid and education
Citation: us:usc:6:726
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section726
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 2 (National Emergency Management); Subchapter I (Personnel Provisions); Part B (Emergency Management Capabilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall enter into agreements with organizations to provide funds to emergency response providers to provide education and training in life supporting first aid to children.
