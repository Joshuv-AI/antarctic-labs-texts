44 C.F.R. § 206.66 — Limitation on expenditures.
Citation: us:cfr:44:206.66
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-206.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 206 (Federal Disaster Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Total assistance provided in any given emergency declaration may not exceed $5,000,000, except when it is determined by the Administrator that:
(a) Continued emergency assistance is immediately required;
(b) There is a continuing and immediate risk to lives, property, public health and safety; and
(c) Necessary assistance will not otherwise be provided on a timely basis.
