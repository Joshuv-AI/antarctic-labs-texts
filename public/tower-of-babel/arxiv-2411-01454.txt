Note on surface-link of trivial components
Akio Kawauchi

Abstract
As a previous result, it has shown that every sphere-link consisting of trivial components is a ribbon sphere-link. In this note, it is shown that for every closed oriented disconnected surface 𝐅 {\mathbf{F}} with just one non-sphere component, every 𝐅 {\mathbf{F}} -link consisting of trivial components is a ribbon surface-link. Further, it is shown that for every closed oriented disconnected surface 𝐅 {\mathbf{F}} containing at least two non-sphere components, there exist a pair of a ribbon 𝐅 {\mathbf{F}} -link and a non-ribbon 𝐅 {\mathbf{F}} -link that consist of trivial components and have meridian-preservingly isomorphic fundamental groups. Keywords : Ribbon, Non-ribbon, Surface-link. Mathematics Subject Classification 2010 : Primary 57Q45; Secondary 57N13
