10 U.S.C. § 8384 — Promotion of retired members to higher enlisted grades: retention of grade upon release from active duty
Citation: us:usc:10:8384
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8384
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 845 (Recall To Active Duty). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When on active duty, retired enlisted members of the Navy or the Marine Corps are eligible for promotion to higher enlisted grades or ratings. When released from active duty, they shall, unless entitled to a higher grade under another provision of law, retain the grades or ratings they hold at the time of their release.
