46 U.S.C. § 80502 — Application of Convention
Citation: us:usc:46:80502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section80502
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 805 (Safe Containers For International Cargo). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Convention applies to an owner of a container used in international transport if the owner is domiciled or has its principal office in the United States.
