10 U.S.C. § 940 — Art. 140. Delegation by the President
Citation: us:usc:10:940
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section940
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter XI (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may delegate any authority vested in him under this chapter, and provide for the subdelegation of any such authority.
