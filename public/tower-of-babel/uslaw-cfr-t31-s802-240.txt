31 C.F.R. § 802.240 — Urbanized area.
Citation: us:cfr:31:802.240
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-802.240
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 802 (Regulations Pertaining To Certain Transactions By Foreign Persons Involving Real Estate In The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term urbanized area means a statistical geographic area as identified in the most recent U.S. Census consisting of a densely settled core created from census tracts or blocks and contiguous qualifying territory that together have a minimum population of at least 50,000 individuals.
