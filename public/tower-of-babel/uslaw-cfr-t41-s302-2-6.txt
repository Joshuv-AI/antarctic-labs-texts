41 C.F.R. § 302-2.6 — -2.6 Effect of having multiple service agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-2.6
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-2.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-2 (2—Employee Eligibility Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the employee has multiple service agreements, they cannot be grouped together and must be adhered to separately. Each agreement is in effect for the period specified in the agreement.
