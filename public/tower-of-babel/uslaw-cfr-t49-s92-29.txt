49 C.F.R. § 92.29 — Limitation on amount of deductions.
Citation: us:cfr:49:92.29
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-92.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 92 (Recovering Debts To The United States By Salary Offset). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The size and frequency of installment deductions shall bear a reasonable relation to the size of the debt and the employee's ability to pay. However, the amount deducted for any period may not exceed 15 percent of the disposable pay from which the deduction is made, unless the employee has agreed in writing to the deduction of a greater amount.
