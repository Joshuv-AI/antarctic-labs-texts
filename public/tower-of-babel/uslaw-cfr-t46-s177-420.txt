46 C.F.R. § 177.420 — Vessels complying with SOLAS structural fire protection requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:177.420
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-177.420
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 177 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Vessels meeting the structural fire protection requirements of SOLAS, Chapter II-2, Regulations 5, 6, 8, 9, and 11 (incorporated by reference, see § 175.600 of this chapter) may be considered equivalent to the provisions of this subpart.
