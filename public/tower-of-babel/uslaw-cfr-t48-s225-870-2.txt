48 C.F.R. § 225.870-2 — 225.870-2 Solicitation of Canadian contractors.
Citation: us:cfr:48:225.870-2
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-225.870-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 225 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If requested, furnish a solicitation to the Canadian Commercial Corporation even if no Canadian firm is solicited.
(b) Handle acquisitions at or below the simplified acquisition threshold directly with Canadian firms and not through the Canadian Commercial Corporation.
