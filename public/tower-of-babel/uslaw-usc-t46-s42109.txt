46 U.S.C. § 42109 — Consultation with other agencies
Citation: us:usc:46:42109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section42109
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Actions To Address Foreign Practices); Chapter 421 (Regulations Affecting Shipping In Foreign Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Federal Maritime Commission may consult with, seek the cooperation of, or make recommendations to other appropriate agencies of the United States Government prior to taking any action under this chapter.
