49 C.F.R. § 392.67 — Heater, flame-producing; on commercial motor vehicle in motion.
Citation: us:cfr:49:392.67
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-392.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 392 (Driving Of Commercial Motor Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No open flame heater used in the loading or unloading of the commodity transported shall be in operation while the commercial motor vehicle is in motion.
