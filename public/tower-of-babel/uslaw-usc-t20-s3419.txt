20 U.S.C. § 3419 — Institute of Education Sciences
Citation: us:usc:20:3419
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section3419
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 48 (Department Of Education); Subchapter II (Establishment Of The Department). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be in the Department of Education the Institute of Education Sciences, which shall be administered in accordance with the Education Sciences Reform Act of 2002 [20 U.S.C. 9501 et seq.] by the Director appointed under section 114(a) of that Act [20 U.S.C. 9514(a)].
