47 C.F.R. § 61.135 — Concurrences for other purposes.
Citation: us:cfr:47:61.135
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-61.135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 61 (Tariffs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an issuing carrier permits another carrier to concur in its tariff, the issuing carrier's tariff must state the concurring carrier's rates and points of service.
