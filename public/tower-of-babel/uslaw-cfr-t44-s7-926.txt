44 C.F.R. § 7.926 — Age distinctions contained in FEMA regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:44:7.926
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-7.926
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 7 (Nondiscrimination In Federally-Assisted Programs (Fema Reg. 5)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any age distinctions contained in a rule or regulation issued by FEMA shall be presumed to be necessary to the achievement of a statutory objective of the program or activity to which the rule or regulation applies, notwithstanding the provisions of § 7.921.
