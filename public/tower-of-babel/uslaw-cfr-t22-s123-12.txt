22 C.F.R. § 123.12 — Shipments between U.S. possessions.
Citation: us:cfr:22:123.12
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-123.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 123 (Licenses For The Export And Temporary Import Of Defense Articles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An export license is not required for the shipment of defense articles between the United States, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, and U.S. possessions. A license is required, however, for the export of defense articles from these areas to foreign countries.
