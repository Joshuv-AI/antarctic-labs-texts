7 C.F.R. § 621.43 — Interstate compacts and commissions.
Citation: us:cfr:7:621.43
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-621.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 621 (River Basin Investigations And Surveys). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As assigned, an NRCS State Conservationist is the USDA point of contact for governing bodies of interstate compacts and commissions concerned with the conservation, development, and proper use of water, soil, and related resources.
