44 C.F.R. § 296.36 — Access to records.
Citation: us:cfr:44:296.36
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-296.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 296 (Hermit'S Peak/Calf Canyon Fire Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purpose of audit and investigation, a claimant will grant the DHS Office of the Inspector General and the Comptroller General of the United States access to any property that is the subject of a claim and to any and all books, documents, papers, and records (including any relevant tax records) maintained by a claimant or under the claimant's control pertaining or relevant to the claim.
