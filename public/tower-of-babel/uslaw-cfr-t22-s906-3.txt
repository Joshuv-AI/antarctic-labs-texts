22 C.F.R. § 906.3 — Notification.
Citation: us:cfr:22:906.3
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-906.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 906 (Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the Board orders a hearing, the executive secretary shall so notify the parties in writing. The parties shall be given reasonable notice of the date and place selected by the Board for the hearing.
