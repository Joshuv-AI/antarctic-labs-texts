In the original publication [1], there was a mistake in the column titles of Table 4. The units of all energy parameters should be kcal/mol. In the discussion, correct values are quoted. The corrected Table 4 appears below. The authors state that the scientific conclusions are unaffected. This correction was approved by the Academic Editor. The original publication has also been updated.
