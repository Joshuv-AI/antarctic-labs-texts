22 U.S.C. § 4414 — Requirements relating to the Endowment and its grantees
Citation: us:usc:22:4414
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4414
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 54 (Private Organization Assistance); Subchapter II (National Endowment For Democracy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Partisan politics
(1)
Funds may not be expended, either by the Endowment or by any of its grantees, to finance the campaigns of candidates for public office.
(2)
No funds granted by the Endowment may be used to finance activities of the Republican National Committee or the Democratic National Committee.
(3)
No grants may be made to any institute, foundation, or organization engaged in partisan activities on behalf of the Republican or Democratic National Committee, on behalf of any candidate for public office, or on behalf of any political party in the United States.
(b) Consultation with Department of State
The Endowment shall consult with the Department of State on any overseas program funded by the Endowment prior to the commencement of the activities of that program.
