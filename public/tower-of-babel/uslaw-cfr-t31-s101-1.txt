31 C.F.R. § 101.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:31:101.1
Creator: Monetary Offices, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-101.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 101 (Mitigation Of Forfeiture Of Counterfeit Gold Coins). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish a policy whereby certain purchasers or holders of gold coins who have forfeited them to the United States because they were counterfeit may, in the discretion of the Secretary of the Treasury, recover the gold bullion from the coins. This part sets forth the procedures to be followed in implementing this policy.
