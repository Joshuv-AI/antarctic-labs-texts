48 U.S.C. § 1489 — Loss of title of United States to lands in territories through adverse possession or prescription forbidden
Citation: us:usc:48:1489
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1489
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 10 (Territorial Provisions Of A General Nature). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after March 27, 1934, no prescription or statute of limitations shall run, or continue to run, against the title of the United States to lands in any territory or possession or place or territory under the jurisdiction or control of the United States; and no title to any such lands of the United States or any right therein shall be acquired by adverse possession or prescription, or otherwise than by conveyance from the United States.
