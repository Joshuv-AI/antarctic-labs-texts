45 C.F.R. § 1803.2 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1803.2
Creator: Harry S. Truman Scholarship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1803.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1803 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to all programs or activities conducted by the Foundation, except for programs or activities conducted outside the United States that do not involve individual(s) with handicaps in the United States.
