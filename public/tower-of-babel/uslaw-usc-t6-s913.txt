6 U.S.C. § 913 — Facility exercise requirements
Citation: us:usc:6:913
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section913
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 3 (Security And Accountability For Every Port); Subchapter I (Security Of United States Seaports); Part A (Port Security Grants; Training And Exercise Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Department in which the Coast Guard is operating shall require each high risk facility to conduct live or full-scale exercises described in section 105.220(c) of title 33, Code of Federal Regulations, not less frequently than once every 2 years, in accordance with the facility security plan required under section 70103(c) of title 46.
