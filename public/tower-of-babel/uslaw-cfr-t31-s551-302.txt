31 C.F.R. § 551.302 — Arms or any related materiel.
Citation: us:cfr:31:551.302
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-551.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 551 (Somalia Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term arms or any related materiel means arms or related materiel of all types, including military aircraft and equipment, transferred in violation of the United Nations arms embargo on Somalia.
