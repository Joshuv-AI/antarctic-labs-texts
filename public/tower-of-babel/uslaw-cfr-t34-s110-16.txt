34 C.F.R. § 110.16 — Special benefits for children and the elderly.
Citation: us:cfr:34:110.16
Creator: Office For Civil Rights, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-110.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 110 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a recipient operating a program or activity provides special benefits to the elderly or to children, the use of age distinctions is presumed to be necessary to the normal operation of the program or activity, notwithstanding the provisions of § 110.12.
