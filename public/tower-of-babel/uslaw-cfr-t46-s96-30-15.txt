46 C.F.R. § 96.30-15 — -15 Self-contained breathing apparatus.
Citation: us:cfr:46:96.30-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-96.30-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 96 (Vessel Control And Miscellaneous Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each vessel must have a self-contained breathing apparatus for use as protection against gas leaking from a refrigeration unit.
(b) The self-contained breathing apparatus required by paragraph (a) of this section may be one of those required by § 96.35-10.
