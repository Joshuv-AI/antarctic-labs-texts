49 C.F.R. § 228.329 — Housekeeping.
Citation: us:cfr:49:228.329
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-228.329
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 228 (Passenger Train Employee Hours Of Service; Recordkeeping And Reporting; Sleeping Quarters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A camp car must be kept clean to the extent allowed by the nature of the work performed by the occupants of the camp car.
(b) To facilitate cleaning, every floor, working place, and passageway must be kept free from protruding nails, splinters, loose boards, and unnecessary holes and openings.
