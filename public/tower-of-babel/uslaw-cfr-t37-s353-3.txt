37 C.F.R. § 353.3 — Procedure on rehearing.
Citation: us:cfr:37:353.3
Creator: Copyright Royalty Board, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-353.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 353 (Rehearing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of a motion for rehearing, the Copyright Royalty Judges will issue an appropriate order. No participant shall file a response to a rehearing motion, unless such response is allowed by order of the Copyright Royalty Judges.
