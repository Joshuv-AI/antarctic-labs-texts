40 C.F.R. § 413.21 — Specialized definitions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 413 (Electroplating Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of this subpart:
(a) The term sq m (“sq ft”) shall mean the area plated expressed in square meters (square feet).
(b) The term operation shall mean any step in the electroplating process in which a metal is electrodeposited on a basis material and which is followed by a rinse: This includes the related operations of alkaline cleaning, acid pickle, stripping, and coloring when each operation is followed by a rinse.
