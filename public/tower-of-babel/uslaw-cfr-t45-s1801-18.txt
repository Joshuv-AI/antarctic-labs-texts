45 C.F.R. § 1801.18 — Limitations on nominations.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1801.18
Creator: Harry S. Truman Scholarship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1801.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1801 (Harry S. Truman Scholarship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A candidate nominated by an institution and not selected as a Truman Scholar may not be renominated the following year.
