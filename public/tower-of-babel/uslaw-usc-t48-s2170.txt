48 U.S.C. § 2170 — Applicable rules of procedure
Citation: us:usc:48:2170
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section2170
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 20 (Puerto Rico Oversight, Management, And Economic Stability); Subchapter III (Adjustments Of Debts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Federal Rules of Bankruptcy Procedure shall apply to a case under this subchapter and to all civil proceedings arising in or related to cases under this subchapter.
