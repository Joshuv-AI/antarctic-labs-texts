28 U.S.C. § 3011 — Assessment of surcharge on a debt
Citation: us:usc:28:3011
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section3011
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 176 (Federal Debt Collection Procedure); Subchapter A (Definitions And General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Surcharge Authorized.—
In an action or proceeding under subchapter B or C, and subject to subsection (b), the United States is entitled to recover a surcharge of 10 percent of the amount of the debt in connection with the recovery of the debt, to cover the cost of processing and handling the litigation and enforcement under this chapter of the claim for such debt.
(b) Limitation.—
Subsection (a) shall not apply if—
(1)
the United States receives an attorney’s fee in connection with the enforcement of the claim; or
(2)
the law pursuant to which the action on the claim is based provides any other amount to cover such costs.
