29 C.F.R. § 1635.6 — Causing a covered entity to discriminate.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1635.6
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1635.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1635 (Genetic Information Nondiscrimination Act Of 2008). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A covered entity may not cause or attempt to cause another covered entity, or its agent, to discriminate against an individual in violation of this part, including with respect to the individual's participation in an apprenticeship or other training or retraining program, or with respect to a member's participation in a labor organization.
