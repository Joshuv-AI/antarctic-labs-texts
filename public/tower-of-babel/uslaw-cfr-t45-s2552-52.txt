45 C.F.R. § 2552.52 — What factors are considered in determining a Foster Grandparent's service schedule?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2552.52
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2552.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2552 (Foster Grandparent Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Travel time between the Foster Grandparent's home and place of assignment is not part of the service schedule and is not stipended.
(b) Travel time between individual assignments is a part of the service schedule and is stipended.
(c) Meal time may be part of the service schedule and is stipended.
