36 C.F.R. § 14.9 — Resource impact considerations.
Citation: us:cfr:36:14.9
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-14.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 14 (Rights-Of-Way). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The NPS may direct the use and disposition of resources disturbed under a right-of-way permit. The permittee may be required to mitigate or compensate for permitted impacts to NPS resources and lost uses.
