33 C.F.R. § 90.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:33:90.1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-90.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 90 (Inland Rules: Interpretative Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part contains the interpretative rules for the Inland Rules. These interpretative rules are intended as a guide to assist the public and promote compliance with the Inland Rules.
