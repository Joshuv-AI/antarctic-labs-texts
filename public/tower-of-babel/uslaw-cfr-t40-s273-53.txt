40 C.F.R. § 273.53 — Storage time limits.
Citation: us:cfr:40:273.53
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-273.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 273 (Standards For Universal Waste Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A universal waste transporter may only store the universal waste at a universal waste transfer facility for ten days or less.
(b) If a universal waste transporter stores universal waste for more than ten days, the transporter becomes a universal waste handler and must comply with the applicable requirements of subparts B or C of this part while storing the universal waste.
