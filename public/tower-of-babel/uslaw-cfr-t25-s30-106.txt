25 C.F.R. § 30.106 — How will the Secretary provide for the inclusion of all students in assessments?
Citation: us:cfr:25:30.106
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-30.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 30 (Standards, Assessments, And Accountability System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary will provide assessment instruments that allow for:
(a) The participation of all students, generally;
(b) The participation of students with disabilities, as detailed in §§ 30.107 and 30.108; and
(c) The participation of English learners, as detailed in § 30.109.
