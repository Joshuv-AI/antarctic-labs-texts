41 C.F.R. § 301-11.4 — -11.4 Determining the applicable per diem reimbursement rate.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-11.4
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-11.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-11 (11—Subsistence Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Generally, the temporary duty (TDY) location determines the per diem reimbursement rate. However, if lodging is obtained outside the TDY location, the agency may authorize or approve the per diem rate for an alternate location if it is advantageous to the government. If arriving at the lodging facility after 12 midnight, an employee may claim the lodging cost for the preceding calendar day.
