7 C.F.R. § 3555.207 — Special requirements for Planned Unit Developments (PUDs).
Citation: us:cfr:7:3555.207
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3555.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3555 (Guaranteed Rural Housing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Loans may be guaranteed for PUDs that meet all of the requirements of this part, as well as the criteria for PUDs established by HUD, VA, Fannie Mae, or Freddie Mac.
