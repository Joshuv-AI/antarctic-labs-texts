28 U.S.C. § 1501 — Pensions
Citation: us:usc:28:1501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1501
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part IV (Jurisdiction And Venue); Chapter 91 (United States Court Of Federal Claims). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Court of Federal Claims shall not have jurisdiction of any claim for a pension.
