49 C.F.R. § 1110.5 — Consideration of comments received.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1110.5
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1110.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1110 (Procedures Governing Informal Rulemaking Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All timely comments will be considered before final action is taken on a rulemaking proposal. Comments which are filed late will be considered so far as possible without incurring undue expense, delay, or prejudice to other parties.
