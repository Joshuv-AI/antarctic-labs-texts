10 U.S.C. § 18233b — Authority to carry out small projects with operation and maintenance funds
Citation: us:usc:10:18233b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section18233b
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 1803 (Facilities For Reserve Components). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under such regulations as the Secretary of Defense may prescribe, the Secretary may expend, from appropriations available for operation and maintenance, amounts necessary to carry out any project authorized under section 18233(a) of this title that costs not more than the amount specified in section 2805(c) of this title.
