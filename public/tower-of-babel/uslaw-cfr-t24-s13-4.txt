24 C.F.R. § 13.4 — Reports.
Citation: us:cfr:24:13.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-13.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 13 (Use Of Penalty Mail In The Location And Recovery Of Missing Children). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HUD shall compile and submit to Office of Juvenile Justice and Deliquency Prevention (OJJDP), by June 30, 1987, a consolidated report on its experience in implementing S. 1195 Official Mail Use in the Location and Recovery of Missing Children along with recommendations for future Departmental action.
