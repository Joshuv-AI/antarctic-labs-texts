25 U.S.C. § 5356 — Supplemental assistance to funds provided to local educational agencies
Citation: us:usc:25:5356
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section5356
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 46 (Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance); Subchapter III (Indian Education Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The assistance provided in this chapter for the education of Indians in the public schools of any State is in addition and supplemental to assistance provided under title VI of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 [20 U.S.C. 7401 et seq.].
