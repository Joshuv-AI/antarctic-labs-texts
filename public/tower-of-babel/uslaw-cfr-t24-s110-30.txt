24 C.F.R. § 110.30 — Effect of failure to display poster.
Citation: us:cfr:24:110.30
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-110.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 110 (Fair Housing Poster). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who claims to have been injured by a discriminatory housing practice may file a complaint with the Secretary pursuant to part 105 of this chapter. A failure to display the fair housing poster as required by this part shall be deemed prima facie evidence of a discriminatory housing practice.
