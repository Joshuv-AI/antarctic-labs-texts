45 C.F.R. § 287.140 — With whom should the Tribe coordinate in the operation of its work activities and services?
Citation: us:cfr:45:287.140
Creator: Office Of Family Assistance (Assistance Programs), Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-287.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 287 (The Native Employment Works (New) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The administration of work activities and services provided under the NEW Program must ensure that appropriate coordination and cooperation is maintained with the following entities operating in the same service areas as the Tribe's NEW Program:
(a) State, local and Tribal TANF agencies, and agencies operating employment and training programs;
(b) Any other agency whose programs impact the service population of the NEW Program, including employment, training, placement, education, child care, and social programs.
