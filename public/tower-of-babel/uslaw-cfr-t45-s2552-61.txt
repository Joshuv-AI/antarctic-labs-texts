45 C.F.R. § 2552.61 — May a sponsor serve as a volunteer station?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2552.61
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2552.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2552 (Foster Grandparent Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. A sponsor may serve as a volunteer station, if the activities are part of a work plan in the approved project application.
