15 U.S.C. § 4011 — Export trade promotion duties of Secretary of Commerce
Citation: us:usc:15:4011
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section4011
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 66 (Promotion Of Export Trade); Subchapter II (Export Trade Certificates Of Review). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To promote and encourage export trade, the Secretary may issue certificates of review and advise and assist any person with respect to applying for certificates of review.
