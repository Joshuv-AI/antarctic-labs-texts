19 C.F.R. § 151.74 — Retest at Center director's request.
Citation: us:cfr:19:151.74
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-151.74
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 151 (Examination, Sampling, And Testing Of Merchandise). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Center director is not satisfied with the results of any test provided for in § 151.71 or § 151.73, he may, within 14 calendar days after receiving the report of the results of such test, proceed to have another test made upon a suitable sample of the wool or hair at the expense of the Government. When the Center director is proceeding to have another test made, he shall, within the 14-day period specified in this paragraph, notify the importer by mail of that fact.
