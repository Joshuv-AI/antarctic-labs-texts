FEDERAL MINE SAFETY AND HEALTH REVIEW COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meetings

TIME AND DATE: 10 a.m., Thursday, March 26, 2026.

PLACE: The meeting will be held via remote means and/or in the Richard 
V. Backley Hearing Room, Room 511, 1331 Pennsylvania Avenue NW, Suite 
504 North, Washington, DC 20004.

STATUS: Closed.

MATTERS TO BE CONSIDERED:  The Commission will conduct a meeting closed 
to the public to consider: GMS Mine Repair & Maintenance, Inc., Docket 
No. VA 2023-0021 (Issues include: (1) whether the Secretary of Labor is 
collaterally estopped from enforcing a safeguard notice; and (2) 
whether the Judge erred in concluding that the operator violated a 
safeguard notice).
    Commissioners will attend the meeting. Commission staff members who 
provide technological support and other Commission staff may also be 
present as necessary. The Commissioners have unanimously voted to close 
the meeting. This meeting is closed to the public pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 
552b(c)(10) on the basis of the Commission's consideration or 
disposition of a ``particular case of formal agency adjudication.''
