9 C.F.R. § 77.8 — Interstate movement from accredited-free States and zones.
Citation: us:cfr:9:77.8
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-77.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 77 (Tuberculosis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cattle or bison that originate in an accredited-free State or zone may be moved interstate in accordance with 9 CFR part 86 without further restriction under this part.
