44 U.S.C. § 3313 — Moneys from sale of records payable into the Treasury
Citation: us:usc:44:3313
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section3313
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 33 (Disposal Of Records). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Moneys derived by agencies of the Government from the sale of records disposed of under this chapter shall be paid into the Treasury of the United States unless otherwise required by law.
