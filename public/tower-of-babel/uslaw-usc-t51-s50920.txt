51 U.S.C. § 50920 — User fees
Citation: us:usc:51:50920
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section50920
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 509 (Commercial Space Launch Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation may collect a user fee for a regulatory or other service conducted under this chapter only if specifically authorized by this chapter.
