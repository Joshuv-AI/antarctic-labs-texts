52 U.S.C. § 21043 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:52:21043
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section21043
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 209 (Election Administration Improvement); Subchapter II (Commission); Part D (Election Assistance); Subpart 3 (Grants For Research On Voting Technology Improvements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There are authorized to be appropriated for grants under this subpart $20,000,000 for fiscal year 2003.
(b) Availability of funds
Amounts appropriated pursuant to the authorization under this section shall remain available, without fiscal year limitation, until expended.
