22 U.S.C. § 2584 — Dual compensation exemption
Citation: us:usc:22:2584
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2584
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 35 (Arms Control And Disarmament); Subchapter IV (Additional General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Members of advisory boards and consultants may serve as such without regard to any Federal law limiting the reemployment of retired officers or employees or governing the simultaneous receipt of compensation and retired pay or annuities, subject to section 5532 1 of title 5. This section shall apply only to individuals carrying out activities related to arms control, nonproliferation, and disarmament.
