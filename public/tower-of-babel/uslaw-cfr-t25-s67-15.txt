25 C.F.R. § 67.15 — Special instructions.
Citation: us:cfr:25:67.15
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-67.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 67 (Preparation Of A Roll Of Independent Seminole Indians Of Florida). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To facilitate the work of the Superintendent and Area Director, the Assistant Secretary may issue special instructions not inconsistent with the regulations in this part.
