5 C.F.R. § 351.603 — Actions subsequent to release from competitive level.
Citation: us:cfr:5:351.603
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-351.603
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 351 (Reduction In Force). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee reached for release from a competitive level shall be offered assignment to another position in accordance with subpart G of this part. If the employee accepts, the employee shall be assigned to the position offered. If the employee has no assignment right or does not accept an offer under subpart G, the employee shall be furloughed or separated.
