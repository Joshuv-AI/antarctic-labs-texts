32 C.F.R. § 1657.5 — Place of induction.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1657.5
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1657.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1657 (Overseas Registrant Processing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may order an overseas registrant to any place in the world for induction.
