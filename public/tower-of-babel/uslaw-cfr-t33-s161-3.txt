33 C.F.R. § 161.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:33:161.3
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-161.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 161 (Vessel Traffic Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart shall apply to each VTS User and may also apply to any vessel while underway or at anchor on the navigable waters of the United States within a VTS area, to the extent the VTS considers necessary.
