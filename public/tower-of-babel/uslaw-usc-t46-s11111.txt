46 U.S.C. § 11111 — Limit on amount recoverable on voyage
Citation: us:usc:46:11111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section11111
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 111 (Protection And Relief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When a seaman is on a voyage on which a written agreement is required under this part, not more than $1 is recoverable from the seaman by a person for a debt incurred by the seaman during the voyage for which the seaman is signed on until the voyage is ended.
