27 C.F.R. § 19.293 — Receipt of materials.
Citation: us:cfr:27:19.293
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-19.293
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 19 (Distilled Spirits Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a proprietor receives certain materials on bonded premises, the proprietor must determine the quantity received and record those quantities in the records prescribed by subpart V of this part. This requirement applies to:
(a) Fermenting materials;
(b) Distilling materials (including nonpotable chemical mixtures containing spirits); and
(c) Spirits, denatured spirits, articles, and spirits residue for redistillation.
