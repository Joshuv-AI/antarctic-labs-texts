16 U.S.C. § 973m — Fisheries-related assistance
Citation: us:usc:16:973m
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section973m
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 16C (South Pacific Tuna Fishing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary and the Secretary of State may provide assistance to a Pacific Island Party to benefit such Pacific Island Party from the development of fisheries resources and the operation of fishing vessels that are licensed pursuant to the Treaty, including—
(1)
technical assistance;
(2)
training and capacity building opportunities;
(3)
facilitation of the implementation of private sector activities or partnerships; and
(4)
other activities as determined appropriate by the Secretary and the Secretary of State.
