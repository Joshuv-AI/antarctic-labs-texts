29 C.F.R. § 1990.122 — Response to petitions.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1990.122
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1990.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1990 (Identification, Classification, And Regulation Of Potential Occupational Carcinogens). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever the Secretary receives any information submitted in writing by any interested person concerning the inclusion or omission of any substance from the Candidate List, the Secretary shall briefly review the information and any other available data, as defined in § 1990.121(a). The results of the Secretary's review shall be transmitted to the petitioner, together with a short statement of the Secretary's reasons therefor, and made public upon request.
