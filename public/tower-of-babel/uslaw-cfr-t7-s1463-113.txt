7 C.F.R. § 1463.113 — Issuance of payments in event of death.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1463.113
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1463.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1463 (2005-2014 Tobacco Transition Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a quota holder or tobacco producer who is eligible to receive a payment under this subpart dies, the right to receive payments shall be transferred to the estate of the quota holder or tobacco producer unless such person is survived by a spouse or one or more dependents, in which case the right to receive the payments shall be transferred to the surviving spouse.
