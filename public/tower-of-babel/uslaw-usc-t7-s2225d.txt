7 U.S.C. § 2225d — Availability of Department of Agriculture funds for temporary employment
Citation: us:usc:7:2225d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2225d
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 55 (Department Of Agriculture). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after November 10, 2005, funds appropriated by this or any other Appropriations Act to the Department of Agriculture (excluding the Forest Service) shall be available for employment pursuant to the second sentence of section 2225 of this title and section 3109 of title 5.
