27 C.F.R. § 45.33 — Return of shipment to factory.
Citation: us:cfr:27:45.33
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-45.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 45 (Removal Of Tobacco Products And Cigarette Papers And Tubes, Without Payment Of Tax, For Use Of The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tobacco products, and cigarette papers and tubes which have been removed, under this part, may be returned to the factory without internal revenue supervision.
