4 U.S.C. § 72 — Public offices; at seat of Government
Citation: us:usc:4:72
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title4-section72
Context: United States Code, Title 4 (Flag And Seal, Seat Of Government, And The States); Chapter 3 (Seat Of The Government). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All offices attached to the seat of government shall be exercised in the District of Columbia, and not elsewhere, except as otherwise expressly provided by law.
