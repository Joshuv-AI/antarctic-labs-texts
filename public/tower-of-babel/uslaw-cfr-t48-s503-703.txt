48 C.F.R. § 503.703 — 503.703 Authority.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 503 (Improper Business Practices And Personal Conflicts Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).
