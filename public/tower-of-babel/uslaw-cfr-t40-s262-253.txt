40 C.F.R. § 262.253 — Testing and maintenance of equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:40:262.253
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-262.253
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 262 (Standards Applicable To Generators Of Hazardous Waste). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All communications or alarm systems, fire protection equipment, spill control equipment, and decontamination equipment, where required, must be tested and maintained as necessary to assure its proper operation in time of emergency.
