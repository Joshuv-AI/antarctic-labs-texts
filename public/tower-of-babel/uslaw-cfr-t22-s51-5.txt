22 C.F.R. § 51.5 — Adjudication and issuance of passports.
Citation: us:cfr:22:51.5
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-51.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 51 (Passports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A passport authorizing officer may adjudicate applications and authorize the issuance of passports.
(b) A passport authorizing officer will examine the passport application and all documents, photographs and statements submitted in support of the application in accordance with guidance issued by the Department.
