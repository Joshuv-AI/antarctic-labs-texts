26 U.S.C. § 4291 — Cases where persons receiving payment must collect tax
Citation: us:usc:26:4291
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section4291
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 33 (Facilities And Services); Subchapter E (Special Provisions Applicable To Services And Facilities Taxes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in section 4263(a), every person receiving any payment for facilities or services on which a tax is imposed upon the payor thereof under this chapter shall collect the amount of the tax from the person making such payment.
