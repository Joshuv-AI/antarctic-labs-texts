15 U.S.C. § 2404 — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 51 (National Productivity And Quality Of Working Life); Subchapter I (Findings, Purpose, And Policy; Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this chapter—
(1)
the term “Center” means the National Center for Productivity and Quality of Working Life;
(2)
the term “Board” means the Board of Directors of the Center;
(3)
the terms “productivity growth” and “improved productivity” shall be interpreted to include, but not be limited to, improvements in technology, management techniques, and the quality of working life; and
(4)
the term “quality of working life” shall be interpreted to mean the conditions of work relating to the role of the worker in the production process.
