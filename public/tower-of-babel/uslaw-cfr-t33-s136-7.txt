33 C.F.R. § 136.7 — Foreign claimants.
Citation: us:cfr:33:136.7
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-136.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 136 (Oil Spill Liability Trust Fund; Claims Procedures; Designation Of Source; And Advertisement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to other applicable limitations on presenting claims to the Fund, claims by foreign claimants to recover removal costs or damages may be presented only when the requirements of section 1007 of the Act (33 U.S.C. 2707) are met.
