10 C.F.R. § 835.203 — Combining internal and external equivalent doses.
Citation: us:cfr:10:835.203
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-835.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 835 (Occupational Radiation Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The total effective dose during a year shall be determined by summing the effective dose from external exposures and the committed effective dose from intakes during the year.
(b) Determinations of the effective dose shall be made using the radiation and tissue weighting factor values provided in § 835.2.
