16 C.F.R. § 301.18 — Passing off domestic furs as imported furs prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:16:301.18
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-301.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 301 (Rules And Regulations Under Fur Products Labeling Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No domestic furs nor fur products shall be labeled, invoiced or advertised in such a manner as to represent directly or by implication that they have been imported.
