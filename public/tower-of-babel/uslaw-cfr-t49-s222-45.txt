49 C.F.R. § 222.45 — When is a railroad required to cease routine sounding of locomotive horns at crossings?
Citation: us:cfr:49:222.45
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-222.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 222 (Use Of Locomotive Horns At Public Highway-Rail Grade Crossings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On the date specified in a Notice of Quiet Zone Continuation or Notice of Quiet Zone Establishment that complies with the requirements set forth in § 222.43 of this part, a railroad shall refrain from, or cease, routine sounding of the locomotive horn at all public, private and pedestrian grade crossings identified in the Notice.
