5 C.F.R. § 532.105 — Pay-fixing authority.
Citation: us:cfr:5:532.105
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-532.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 532 (Prevailing Rate Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The head of each agency shall authorize application of the rates established by the lead agency or the Office of Personnel Management (OPM) to prevailing rate employees within the appropriate wage area, in accordance with the provisions of this part.
