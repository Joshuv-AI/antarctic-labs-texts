40 C.F.R. § 704.9 — Where to send reports.
Citation: us:cfr:40:704.9
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-704.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 704 (Reporting And Recordkeeping Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Reports must be submitted by certified mail to the Document Control Office (DCO) (7407M), Office of Pollution Prevention and Toxics (OPPT), Environmental Protection Agency, 1200 Pennsylvania Ave., NW., Washington, DC 20460-0001, ATTN: 8(a) Reporting.
