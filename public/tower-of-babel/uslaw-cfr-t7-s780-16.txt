7 C.F.R. § 780.16 — Implementation of final agency decisions.
Citation: us:cfr:7:780.16
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-780.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 780 (Appeal Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent practicable, no later than 30 calendar days after an agency decision becomes a final administrative decision of USDA, FSA will implement the decision.
