15 U.S.C. § 8007 — CPSC report
Citation: us:usc:15:8007
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section8007
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 106 (Pool And Spa Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 1 year after the last day of each fiscal year for which grants are made under section 8004 of this title, the Commission shall submit to Congress a report evaluating the implementation of the grant program authorized by that section.
