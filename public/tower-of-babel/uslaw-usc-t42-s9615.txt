42 U.S.C. § 9615 — Presidential delegation and assignment of duties or powers and promulgation of regulations
Citation: us:usc:42:9615
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9615
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 103 (Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, And Liability); Subchapter I (Hazardous Substances Releases, Liability, Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is authorized to delegate and assign any duties or powers imposed upon or assigned to him and to promulgate any regulations necessary to carry out the provisions of this subchapter.
