22 U.S.C. § 3791 — Prescription of measurement rules and rates of tolls
Citation: us:usc:22:3791
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3791
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 51 (Panama Canal); Subchapter I (Administration And Regulations); Part 6 (Tolls For Use Of Panama Canal). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission may, subject to the provisions of this chapter, prescribe and from time to time change—
(1)
the rules for the measurement of vessels for the Panama Canal; and
(2)
the tolls that shall be levied for use of the Panama Canal.
