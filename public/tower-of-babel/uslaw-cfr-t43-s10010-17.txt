43 C.F.R. § 10010.17 — Public involvement.
Citation: us:cfr:43:10010.17
Creator: Utah Reclamation Mitigation And Conservation Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-10010.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 10010 (Policies And Procedures For Implementing The National Environmental Policy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The public may be involved in the EA process when appropriate. Public notification will be made of the availability of an EA document (40 CFR 1506.6).
(b) The scoping process may be applied to an EA (40 CFR 1501.7).
