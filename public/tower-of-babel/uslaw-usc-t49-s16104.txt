49 U.S.C. § 16104 — Disobedience to subpenas
Citation: us:usc:49:16104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section16104
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Pipeline Carriers); Chapter 161 (Civil And Criminal Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever does not obey a subpena or requirement of the Board to appear and testify or produce records shall be fined under title 18 or imprisoned not more than 1 year, or both.
