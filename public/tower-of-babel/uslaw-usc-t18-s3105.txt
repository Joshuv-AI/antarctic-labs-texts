18 U.S.C. § 3105 — Persons authorized to serve search warrant
Citation: us:usc:18:3105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3105
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 205 (Searches And Seizures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A search warrant may in all cases be served by any of the officers mentioned in its direction or by an officer authorized by law to serve such warrant, but by no other person, except in aid of the officer on his requiring it, he being present and acting in its execution.
