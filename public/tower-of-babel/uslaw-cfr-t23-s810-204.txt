23 C.F.R. § 810.204 — Application by mass transit authority.
Citation: us:cfr:23:810.204
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-810.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 810 (Mass Transit And Special Use Highway Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A publicly-owned mass transit authority desiring to utilize land existing within the publicly acquired right-of-way of any Federal-aid highway for a rail or other nonhighway public mass transit facility may submit an application therefor to the State highway agency.
