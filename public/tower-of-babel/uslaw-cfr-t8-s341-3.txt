8 C.F.R. § 341.3 — Depositions.
Citation: us:cfr:8:341.3
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-341.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 341 (Certificates Of Citizenship). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If satisfied that a witness whose testimony is essential is not available for examination in the United States, the assigned officer may authorize the taking of a deposition abroad by written interrogatories before a DHS or Department of State official.
