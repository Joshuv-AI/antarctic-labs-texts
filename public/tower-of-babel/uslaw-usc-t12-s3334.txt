12 U.S.C. § 3334 — Officers and staff
Citation: us:usc:12:3334
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section3334
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 34A (Appraisal Subcommittee Of Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Chairperson of the Appraisal Subcommittee shall appoint such officers and staff as may be necessary to carry out the functions of this chapter consistent with the appointment and compensation practices of the Council.
