25 C.F.R. § 162.316 — How will BIA enforce removal requirements in a residential lease?
Citation: us:cfr:25:162.316
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-162.316
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 162 (Leases And Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We may take appropriate enforcement action to ensure removal of the permanent improvements and restoration of the premises at the lessee's expense:
(a) In consultation with the tribe for tribal land or, where feasible, with Indian landowners for individually owned Indian land; and
(b) Before or after expiration, termination, or cancellation of the lease.
