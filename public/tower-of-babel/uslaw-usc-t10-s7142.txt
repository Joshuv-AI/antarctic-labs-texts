10 U.S.C. § 7142 — Army: percentage of high-school graduates
Citation: us:usc:10:7142
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7142
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 713 (Enlistments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding section 520(b) of this title, of the males with no prior military service who are enlisted or inducted into the Army during any fiscal year, the number who are not high-school graduates may not exceed, as of the end of the fiscal year, 35 percent of all such persons.
