33 C.F.R. § 173.19 — Other numbers prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:33:173.19
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-173.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 173 (Vessel Numbering And Casualty And Accident Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may operate a vessel to which this part applies that has any number that is not issued by an issuing authority for that vessel on its forward half.
