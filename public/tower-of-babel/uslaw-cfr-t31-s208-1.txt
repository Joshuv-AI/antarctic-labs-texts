31 C.F.R. § 208.1 — Scope and application.
Citation: us:cfr:31:208.1
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-208.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 208 (Management Of Federal Agency Disbursements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to all Federal payments made by an agency. Except as specified in § 208.4, this part requires payments, other than payments made under the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, to be made by electronic funds transfer.
