23 C.F.R. § 658.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:658.1
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-658.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 658 (Truck Size And Weight, Route Designations—Length, Width And Weight Limitations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to identify a National Network of highways available to vehicles authorized by provisions of the Surface Transportation Assistance Act of 1982 (STAA) as amended, and to prescribe national policies that govern truck and bus size and weight.
