DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Advisory Committee on Cemeteries and Memorials, Notice of Meeting 
Cancellation

    The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) gives notice under the 
Federal Advisory Committee Act, 5 U.S.C. Ch. 10, that the Advisory 
Committee on Cemeteries and Memorials, previously scheduled to be held 
on October 28 and October 29, 2025, has been cancelled due to a lapse 
in appropriations.
    For more information, please contact Ms. Faith Hopkins, Designated 
Federal Officer, at 202-603-4499 or via email at [email protected].

    Dated: October 15, 2025.
Taylor N. Mattson,
Alternate Federal Register Liaison Officer, Department of Veterans 
Affairs.
