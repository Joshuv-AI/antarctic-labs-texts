16 C.F.R. § 323.3 — Applicability to mail order advertising.
Citation: us:cfr:16:323.3
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-323.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 323 (Made In Usa Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent that any mail order catalog or mail order promotional material includes a seal, mark, tag, or stamp labeling a product Made in the United States, such label must comply with § 323.2.
