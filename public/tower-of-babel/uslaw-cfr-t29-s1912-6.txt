29 C.F.R. § 1912.6 — Conflict of interest.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1912.6
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1912.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1912 (Advisory Committees On Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No members of any advisory committee other than members representing employers or employees shall have an economic interest in any proposed rule.
