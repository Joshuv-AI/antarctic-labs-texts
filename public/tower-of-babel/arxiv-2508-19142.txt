Exotic non-orientable four-manifolds with prescribed fundamental group.
Rafael Torres

Abstract




2020 Mathematics Subject Classification
Primary 57R55; Secondary 57K40, 57M05

Abstract
: We show that any finitely presented group with an index two subgroup is realized as the fundamental group of a closed smooth non-orientable four-manifold that admits an exotic smooth structure, which is obtained by performing a Gluck twist. The orientation 2-covers of these four-manifolds are diffeomorphic. These two smooth structures remain inequivalent after adding arbitrarily many copies of the product of a pair of 2-spheres and stabilize after adding a single copy of the complex projective plane.

1.
Introduction.

Kreck showed
[
15
, Theorem 1]
that for any finitely presented group
\pi
with a non-trivial element
w_{1}\in H^{1}(\pi;\mathbb{Z}/2)
, there is a closed smooth four-manifold
M_{\pi}
with fundamental group
\pi
and orientation-character

such that the connected sums
M_{\pi}\mathbin{\#}K3
and
M_{\pi}\mathbin{\#}11(S^{2}\times S^{2})
are homeomorphic, but not diffeomorphic (see
[
13
, Section 5.5]
). The K3-surface is denoted by
K3

[
10
, Section 1.3]
. The purpose of this brief note is to strengthen and extend Kreck’s result as follows.

Theorem A
.

Let
G
be a finitely presented group that has a subgroup of index two and denote its deficiency by
\Def(G)
. There are closed smooth non-orientable four-manifolds
M_{G}
and
N_{G}
with fundamental group
G
and that satisfy the following properties.

•

The Euler characteristic of
M_{G}
is
\chi(M_{G})=4-2\Def(G)
.

•

There is a homeomorphism

M_{G}\rightarrow N_{G}.

•

There is no diffeomorphism

M_{G}\mathbin{\#}(n-1)(S^{2}\times S^{2})\rightarrow N_{G}\mathbin{\#}(n-1)(S^{2}\times S^{2})

for any
n\in\mathbb{N}
.

•

There is a diffeomorphism

\widehat{M}_{G}\rightarrow\widehat{N}_{G}

between their orientation 2-covers
\widehat{M}_{G}\rightarrow M_{G}
and
\widehat{N}_{G}\rightarrow N_{G}
, which admit a
\Sp

•

The four-manifold
N_{G}
is obtained by performing a Gluck twist along a smoothly embedded 2-sphere
S\hookrightarrow M_{G}
.

•

There is a diffeomorphism

M_{G}\mathbin{\#}\mathbb{CP}^{2}\rightarrow N_{G}\mathbin{\#}\mathbb{CP}^{2}.

The first and last three items of Theorem
A
do not appear in Kreck’s work. An advantage of the construction used in the proof of Theorem
A
over the examples of Kreck is that the Euler characteristic of the four-manifolds considered here is considerably smaller. For the sake of completeness, we show in Section
3.7
that the four-manifolds
M_{\pi}\mathbin{\#}K3
and
M_{\pi}\mathbin{\#}11(S^{2}\times S^{2})
also satisfy the properties listed in the fourth and sixth items of the theorem. Cappell-Shaneson
[
4
]
showed that if
X
is a closed smooth four-manifold such that its fundamental group contains an orientation-reversing element of order two, then there is a closed smooth non-orientable four-manifold
Q
and a simple homotopy equivalence
f:Q\rightarrow X
whose smooth normal invariant is the nontrivial element in the kernel of
[X;G/O]\rightarrow[X,G/TOP]
. The four-manifold
Q
, however, might well be diffeomorphic to
X
as shown by Akbulut in the case
X=S^{2}\times{\mathbb{R}\mkern-0.5mu\mathrm{P}}^{2}

[
1
]
. We use the
\eta
-invariant of the twisted Dirac operator to show that the four-manifolds of Theorem
A
are not diffeomorphic through work of Stolz; see Section
2
. The construction of the exotic smooth structure

of Theorem
A
uses an exotic smooth structure

due to Akbulut
[
1
,
2
]
that arises from performing a Gluck twist to the connected sum
(S^{3}\widetilde{\times}S^{1})\mathbin{\#}(S^{2}\times S^{2})
of the non-orientable 3-sphere bundle over the circle
S^{3}\widetilde{\times}S^{1}
with a copy of
S^{2}\times S^{2}
; see Section
3.1
. A result of Gompf
[
9
]
implies that the orientation 2-cover of
A
is diffeomorphic to
(S^{1}\times S^{3})\mathbin{\#}2(S^{2}\times S^{2})
as pointed out by Torres in
[

, Proposition 9]
. Hence, Theorem

provides a multitude of new examples of exotic orientation-reversing free involutions on four-manifolds
[
4
,
5
,

,

]

Kreck’s examples and the four-manifolds of Theorem

satisfy the identity
w_{1}^{2}=w_{2}
, where

This section contains a brief discussion on
\Pin^{+}
-structures and on the
\eta
-invariant of a closed smooth four-manifold that is equipped with such a structure. The reader is directed towards

\Pin^{+}

[

[

, p. 190, Theorem 5.2]

[

Theorem 1
.

Stolz
[
20
]
. Let

be a closed smooth
\Pin^{+}
- four-manifold.

\bullet
The invariant

\Pin^{+}
-bordism invariant.

\bullet

Stolz used these properties along with the modified surgery theory methods developed by Kreck
[

Theorem 2

Let
M_{1}

\Pin^{+}

(2.1)

\eta(M_{1},\phi_{M_{1}})\neq\eta(M_{2},\phi_{M_{2}})\mod 2\mathbb{Z},

(2.2)

M_{1}\mathbin{\#}(n-1)(S^{2}\times S^{2})\rightarrow M_{2}\mathbin{\#}(n-1)(S^{2}\times S^{2})

n\in\mathbb{N}

[
Proof]Recall that a normal 1-type
\xi:B\rightarrow BO

\xi

[

. A choice of normal 1-smoothing determines a class in the group
\Omega^{\xi}_{4}
. Different normal 1-smoothings need not define the same class in
\Omega^{\xi}_{4}
. The main ingredient in the proof of Theorem

.

Kreck
[
17

M_{1}

M_{2}

such that the connected sums

We now argue that Theorem

3.7.
Properties of Kreck’s examples

As the referee kindly pointed out, weaker versions of the fourth and sixth items of Theorem
A
immediately follow from coupling Kreck’s examples
[
15
, Theorem 1]
with a result of Gompf
[

[
8
, Corollary 2]
. This establishes the property in the fourth item of our main result for these examples. An argument using Kirby calculus shows the existence of a diffeomorphism
K3\mathbin{\#}\mathbb{CP}^{2}\rightarrow 4\mathbb{CP}^{2}\mathbin{\#}19\overline{\mathbb{CP}^{2}}

[
10
, Exercise 8.3.4(d)]
(see also

,

,

]

and

.
\square
