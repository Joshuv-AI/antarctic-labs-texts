In the present paper we have determined the time-dependent survival
probability function

These walkers are characterized by the restart mechanism

probability

discrete time step (and a complementary probability

It has been proved that the Sisyphus random walkers, like standard
biased random walkers, are characterized by exponentially decaying
late-time survival probabilities [see Eq. (

have explicitly shown that these two groups of biased random walkers

standard random walkers (who have no restart mechanism).

Finally, it is interesting to point out that, based on the fact that
