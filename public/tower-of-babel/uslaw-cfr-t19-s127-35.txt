19 C.F.R. § 127.35 — Presentation of accounts.
Citation: us:cfr:19:127.35
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-127.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 127 (General Order, Unclaimed, And Abandoned Merchandise). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Accounts for the auctioneer's charges and all other expenses of sale which may be properly chargeable on the merchandise shall be presented to the port director for payment within 10 days from the date of sale. Such expenses shall be apportioned pro rata on the amounts received for different lots sold.
