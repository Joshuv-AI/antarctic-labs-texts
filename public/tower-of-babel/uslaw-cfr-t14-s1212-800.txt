14 C.F.R. § 1212.800 — Civil remedies.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1212 (Privacy Act—Nasa Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure to comply with the requirements of the Privacy Act and this part could subject NASA to civil suit under the provisions of 5 U.S.C. 552a(g).
