15 U.S.C. § 1676 — Enforcement by Secretary of Labor
Citation: us:usc:15:1676
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1676
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 41 (Consumer Credit Protection); Subchapter II (Restrictions On Garnishment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Labor, acting through the Wage and Hour Division of the Department of Labor, shall enforce the provisions of this subchapter.
