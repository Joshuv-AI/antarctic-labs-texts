21 C.F.R. § 1314.10 — Effect on State laws.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1314.10
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1314.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1314 (Retail Sale Of Scheduled Listed Chemical Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part preempts State law on the same subject matter unless there is a positive conflict between this part and a State law so that the two cannot consistently stand together.
