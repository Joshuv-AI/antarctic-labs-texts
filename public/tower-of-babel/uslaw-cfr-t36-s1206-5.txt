36 C.F.R. § 1206.5 — Who serves on the Commission?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1206.5
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1206.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1206 (National Historical Publications And Records Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Established by Congress in 1934, the Commission is a 15-member body, chaired by the Archivist of the United States and comprised of representatives of the three branches of the Federal Government and of professional associations of archivists, historians, documentary editors, and records administrators.
