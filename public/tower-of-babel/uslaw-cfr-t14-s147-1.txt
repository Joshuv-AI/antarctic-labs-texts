14 C.F.R. § 147.1 — Applicability.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 147 (Aviation Maintenance Technician Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes the requirements for issuing aviation maintenance technician school certificates and associated ratings and the general operating rules for the holders of those certificates and ratings.
