7 C.F.R. § 1407.1 — Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1407 (Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part specifies the policies that CCC will follow in taking action to debar or suspend individuals or firms from participation in Federal nonprocurement and procurement activities.
