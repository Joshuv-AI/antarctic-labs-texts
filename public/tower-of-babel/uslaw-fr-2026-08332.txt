POSTAL SERVICE


International Product Change--Priority Mail Express 
International, Priority Mail International & First-Class Package 
International Service Agreements

AGENCY: Postal Service.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Postal Service gives notice of filing requests with the 
Postal Regulatory Commission to add certain Priority Mail Express 
International, Priority Mail International & First-Class Package 
International Service contracts to the list of Negotiated Service 
Agreements in the Competitive Product List in the Mail Classification 
Schedule.

DATES: Date of notice: April 29, 2026.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Christopher C. Meyerson, (202) 268-
7820.
