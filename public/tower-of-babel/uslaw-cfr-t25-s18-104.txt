25 C.F.R. § 18.104 — May a tribe include provisions in its tribal probate code regarding the distribution and descent of trust personalty?
Citation: us:cfr:25:18.104
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-18.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 18 (Tribal Probate Codes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. All trust personalty will be distributed in accordance with the American Indian Probate Reform Act of 2004, as amended.
