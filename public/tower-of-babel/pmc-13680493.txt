Author Correction: Twinned-magnesium metal negative electrodes enable reversible magnesium electrodeposition
Authors: Guodong Zou, Jingli Ren, Jinming Wang, Yong Sun, Weihao Yang, Tingting Niu, Jinyu Li, Carlos Fernandez, Jean-Jacques Gaumet, Qiuming Peng
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-77543-8, published online 4 September 2026

In the version of this article initially published, there were typographical errors, where in the Fig. 2d legend, “LCSM” originally appeared as “CLSM” and in the Fig. 5b caption, “MgCl2, MgO, and Mg0” originally appeared as “MgCl2, MgO, and MgO.” The text is amended in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
