42 U.S.C. § 10143 — Title to material
Citation: us:usc:42:10143
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10143
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 108 (Nuclear Waste Policy); Subchapter I (Disposal And Storage Of High-Level Radioactive Waste, Spent Nuclear Fuel, And Low-Level Radioactive Waste); Part A (Repositories For Disposal Of High-Level Radioactive Waste And Spent Nu­Clear Fuel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Delivery, and acceptance by the Secretary, of any high-level radioactive waste or spent nuclear fuel for a repository constructed under this part shall constitute a transfer to the Secretary of title to such waste or spent fuel.
