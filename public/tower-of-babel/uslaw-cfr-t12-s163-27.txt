12 C.F.R. § 163.27 — Advertising.
Citation: us:cfr:12:163.27
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-163.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 163 (Savings Associations—Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No Federal savings association shall use advertising (which includes print or broadcast media, displays or signs, stationery, and all other promotional materials), or make any representation which is inaccurate in any particular or which in any way misrepresents its services, contracts, investments, or financial condition.
