40 C.F.R. § 165.92 — What if I need both a containment pad and a secondary containment unit?
Citation: us:cfr:40:165.92
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-165.92
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 165 (Pesticide Management And Disposal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may combine containment pads and secondary containment units as an integrated system provided the requirements set out in this subpart for containment pads and secondary containment units in §§ 165.85(a) and (b), 165.87(a) and (b) and § 165.90, and as applicable, §§ 165.85(c)-(f) and 165.87(c)-(f) are satisfied separately.
