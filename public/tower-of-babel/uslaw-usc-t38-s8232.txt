38 U.S.C. § 8232 — Definition

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part VI (Acquisition And Disposition Of Property); Chapter 82 (Assistance In Establishing New State Medical Schools; Grants To Affiliated Medical Schools; Assistance To Health Manpower Training Institutions); Subchapter III (Assistance To Public And Nonprofit Institutions Of Higher Learning, Hospitals And Other Health Manpower Institutions Affiliated With The Department To Increase The Production Of Professional And Other Health Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of this subchapter, the term “eligible institution” means any nonprofit educational facility or other public or nonprofit institution, including universities, colleges, junior colleges, community colleges, schools of allied health professions, State and local systems of education, hospitals, and other nonprofit health manpower institutions for the training or education of allied health or other health personnel affiliated with the Department for the conduct of or the providing of guidance for education and training programs for health manpower.
