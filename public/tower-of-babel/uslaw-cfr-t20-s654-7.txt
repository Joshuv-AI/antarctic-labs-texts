20 C.F.R. § 654.7 — Publication of area classifications.
Citation: us:cfr:20:654.7
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-654.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 654 (Special Responsibilities Of The Employment Service System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Secretary shall publish annually a list of labor surplus areas together with geographic descriptions thereof. The Assistant Secretary periodically may cause these lists to be published in the Federal Register.
