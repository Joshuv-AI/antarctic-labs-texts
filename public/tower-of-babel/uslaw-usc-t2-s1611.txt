2 U.S.C. § 1611 — Exempt organizations
Citation: us:usc:2:1611
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1611
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 26 (Disclosure Of Lobbying Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An organization described in section 501(c)(4) of title 26 which engages in lobbying activities shall not be eligible for the receipt of Federal funds constituting an award, grant, or loan.
