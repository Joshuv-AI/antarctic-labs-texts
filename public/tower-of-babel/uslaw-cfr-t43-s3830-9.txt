43 C.F.R. § 3830.9 — What will happen if I file a document with BLM that I know contains false, erroneous, or fictitious information or statements?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3830.9
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3830.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3830 (Administration Of Mining Claims And Sites; General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you file a document that you know contains false, erroneous, or fictitious information or statements, you may be subject to criminal penalties under 18 U.S.C. 1001 and 43 U.S.C. 1212. The maximum penalty is 5 years in prison and/or a fine of $250,000.
