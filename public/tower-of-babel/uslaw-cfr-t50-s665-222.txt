50 C.F.R. § 665.222 — Management area.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-665.222
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 665 (Fisheries In The Western Pacific). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Hawaii coral reef ecosystem management area is as follows:
(a) The U.S. EEZ around the Hawaiian Archipelago lying to the east of 160°50′ W. long.
(b) The inner boundary of the management area is the seaward boundary of the State of Hawaii.
(c) The outer boundary of the management area is the outer boundary of the U.S. EEZ.
