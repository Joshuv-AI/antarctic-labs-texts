42 U.S.C. § 12644 — Drug-free workplace requirements
Citation: us:usc:42:12644
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12644
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 129 (National And Community Service); Subchapter I (National And Community Service State Grant Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All programs receiving grants under this subchapter shall be subject to the Drug-Free Workplace Requirements for Federal Grant Recipients under sections 8101 and 8103 to 8106 of title 41.
