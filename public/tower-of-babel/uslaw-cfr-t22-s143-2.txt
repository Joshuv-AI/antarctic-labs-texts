22 C.F.R. § 143.2 — To what programs or activities do these regulations apply?
Citation: us:cfr:22:143.2
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-143.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 143 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations apply to each foreign affairs agency recipient and to each program or activity in the United States operated by the recipient which receives or benefits from federal financial assistance provided by any of these agencies.
