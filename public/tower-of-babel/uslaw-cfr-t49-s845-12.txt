49 C.F.R. § 845.12 — Evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:49:845.12
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-845.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 845 (Rules Of Practice In Transportation: Investigative Hearings; Meetings, Reports, And Petitions For Reconsideration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with § 845.2, the chairman of the board of inquiry shall receive all testimony and evidence that may be of aid in determining the probable cause of the transportation accident or incident. He or she may exclude any testimony or exhibits that are not pertinent to the investigation or are merely cumulative.
