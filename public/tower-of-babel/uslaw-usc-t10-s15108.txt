10 U.S.C. § 15108 — Continuation on the Reserve Active-Status List
Citation: us:usc:10:15108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section15108
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Promotion And Retention Of Officers On The Reserve Active-Status List); Chapter 1413 (Alternative Promotion Authority For Officers In Designated Competitive Categories). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Sections 14701 through 14703 of this title shall apply in continuation or retention on a reserve active-status list of officers designated for purposes of this chapter.
