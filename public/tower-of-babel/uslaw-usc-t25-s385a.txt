25 U.S.C. § 385a — Irrigation projects; deposit of assessments as trust fund; disposition of fund
Citation: us:usc:25:385a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section385a
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 11 (Irrigation Of Allotted Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective August 7, 1946, collections made from water users on each Indian irrigation project on account of assessments levied to meet the cost of operating and maintaining such project shall be deposited into the Treasury for credit to a trust-fund account pursuant to section 1321 of title 31, and shall be available for expenditure in carrying out the purposes for which collected.
