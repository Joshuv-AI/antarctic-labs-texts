7 C.F.R. § 1724.9 — Environmental review requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1724.9
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1724.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1724 (Electric Engineering, Architectural Services And Design Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Borrowers must comply with the environmental review requirements in accordance with 7 CFR part 1970. or any successor regulations that implement the provisions of the National Environmental Policy Act.
