48 C.F.R. § 416.603-2 — 416.603-2 Application.
Citation: us:cfr:48:416.603-2
Creator: Chapter 4—Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-416.603-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 416 (Types Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The HCA is authorized to extend the period for defining a letter contract required by FAR 16.603-2(c) in extreme cases where it is determined in writing that such action is in the best interest of the Government.
