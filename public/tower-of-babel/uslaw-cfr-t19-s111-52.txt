19 C.F.R. § 111.52 — Voluntary suspension of license or permit.
Citation: us:cfr:19:111.52
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-111.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 111 (Customs Brokers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The appropriate Executive Director, Office of Trade, may accept a broker's written voluntary offer of suspension of the broker's license or permit for a specific period of time under any terms and conditions to which the parties may agree.
