34 C.F.R. § 101.114 — Expeditious treatment.
Citation: us:cfr:34:101.114
Creator: Office For Civil Rights, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-101.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 101 (Practice And Procedure For Hearings Under Part 100 Of This Title). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests for expeditious treatment of matters pending before the responsible Department official or the presiding officer are deemed communications on the merits, and are improper except when forwarded from parties to a proceeding and served upon all other parties thereto. Such communications should be in the form of a motion.
