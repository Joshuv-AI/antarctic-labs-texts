7 C.F.R. § 25.5 — Waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:7:25.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-25.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 25 (Rural Empowerment Zones And Enterprise Communities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may waive any provision of this part in any particular case for good cause, where it is determined that application of the requirement would produce a result adverse to the purpose and objectives of this part.
