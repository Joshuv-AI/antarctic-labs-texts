32 C.F.R. § 1803.4 — Suggestions and complaints.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1803.4
Creator: National Counterintelligence Center
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1803.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1803 (Public Requests For Mandatory Declassification Review Of Classified Information Pursuant To Section 3.6 Of Executive Order 12958). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NACIC welcomes suggestions or complaints with regard to its administration of the mandatory declassification review program established under Executive Order 12958. Letters of suggestion or complaint should identify the specific purpose and the issues for consideration. NACIC will respond to all substantive communications and take such actions as determined feasible and appropriate.
