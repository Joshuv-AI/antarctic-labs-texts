49 C.F.R. § 391.55 — LCV Driver-Instructor qualification files.
Citation: us:cfr:49:391.55
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-391.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 391 (Qualifications Of Drivers And Longer Combination Vehicle (Lcv) Driver Instructors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each motor carrier must maintain a qualification file for each LCV driver-instructor it employs or uses. The LCV driver-instructor qualification file may be combined with his/her personnel file.
(b) The LCV driver-instructor qualification file must include the information in paragraphs (b)(1) and (b)(2) of this section for a skills instructor or the information in paragraph (b)(1) of this section for a classroom instructor, as follows:
(1) Evidence that the instructor has met the requirements of 49 CFR 380.301 or 380.303;
(2) A copy of the individual's currently valid CDL with the appropriate endorsements.
