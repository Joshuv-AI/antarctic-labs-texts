15 U.S.C. § 1335 — Unlawful advertisements on medium of electronic communication
Citation: us:usc:15:1335
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1335
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 36 (Cigarette Labeling And Advertising). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After January 1, 1971, it shall be unlawful to advertise cigarettes and little cigars on any medium of electronic communication subject to the jurisdiction of the Federal Communications Commission.
