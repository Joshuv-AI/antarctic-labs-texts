14 C.F.R. § 133.13 — Duration of certificate.
Citation: us:cfr:14:133.13
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-133.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 133 (Rotorcraft External-Load Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless sooner surrendered, suspended, or revoked, a Rotorcraft External-Load Operator Certificate expires at the end of the twenty-fourth month after the month in which it is issued or renewed.
