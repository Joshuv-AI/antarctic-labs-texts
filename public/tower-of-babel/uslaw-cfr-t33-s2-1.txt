33 C.F.R. § 2.1 — Purpose.

Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 2 (Jurisdiction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The purpose of this part is to define terms the Coast Guard uses in regulations, policies, and procedures, to determine whether it has jurisdiction on certain waters in cases where specific jurisdictional definitions are not otherwise provided.
