42 U.S.C. § 704b — Nonavailability of allotments after close of fiscal year
Citation: us:usc:42:704b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section704b
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter V (Maternal And Child Health Services Block Grant). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No allotment for this or any succeeding fiscal year under such title V shall be available after the close of such fiscal year except as may be necessary to liquidate obligations incurred during such year.
