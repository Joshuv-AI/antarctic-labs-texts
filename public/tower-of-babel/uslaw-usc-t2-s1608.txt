2 U.S.C. § 1608 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:2:1608
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1608
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 26 (Disclosure Of Lobbying Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter, or the application thereof, is held invalid, the validity of the remainder of this chapter and the application of such provision to other persons and circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
