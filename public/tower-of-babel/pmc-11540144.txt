Correction to: Insertions and Deletions: Computational Methods, Evolutionary Dynamics, and Biological Applications
Published: 2024

This is a correction to:

Benjamin D Redelings, Ian Holmes, Gerton Lunter, Tal Pupko, Maria Anisimova, Insertions and Deletions: Computational Methods, Evolutionary Dynamics, and Biological Applications, Molecular Biology and Evolution, Volume 41, Issue 9, September 2024, https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msae177

In the originally published version of the manuscript, here was a reference missing from Table 2. In row for “PhastSIM”, column 3: “https://github.com/NicolaDM/phastSim” should read: “(De Maio et al. 2022) https://github.com/NicolaDM/phastSim”. The full reference was added into the References list: “De Maio N, Boulton W, Weilguny L, Walker CR, Turakhia Y, Corbett-Detig R, et al. (2022) phastSim: Efficient simulation of sequence evolution for pandemic-scale datasets. PLoS Comput Biol 18(4): e1010056. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010056.”

The emendations have been made to the article.
