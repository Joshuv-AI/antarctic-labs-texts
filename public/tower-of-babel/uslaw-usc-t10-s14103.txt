10 U.S.C. § 14103 — Oath of members
Citation: us:usc:10:14103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section14103
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Promotion And Retention Of Officers On The Reserve Active-Status List); Chapter 1403 (Selection Boards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each member of a selection board convened under section 14101 of this title shall take an oath to perform the duties of a member of the board without prejudice or partiality, having in view both the special fitness of officers and the efficiency of the member’s armed force.
