48 C.F.R. § 8.406-7 — 8.406-7 Contractor Performance Evaluation.
Citation: us:cfr:48:8.406-7
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-8.406-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 8 (Required Sources Of Supplies And Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Ordering activities must prepare at least annually and at the time the work under the order is completed, an evaluation of contractor performance for each order that exceeds the simplified acquisition threshold in accordance with 42.1502(c).
