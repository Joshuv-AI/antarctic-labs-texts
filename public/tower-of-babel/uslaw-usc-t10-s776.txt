10 U.S.C. § 776 — Applicability of chapter
Citation: us:usc:10:776
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section776
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 45 (The Uniform). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter applies in—
(1)
the United States;
(2)
the territories, commonwealths, and possessions of the United States; and
(3)
all other places under the jurisdiction of the United States.
