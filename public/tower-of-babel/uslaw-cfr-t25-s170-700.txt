25 C.F.R. § 170.700 — What is the TTP national business plan?
Citation: us:cfr:25:170.700
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-170.700
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 170 (Tribal Transportation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The TTP national business plan delineates the respective roles and responsibilities of BIA and FHWA in the administration of the TTP and the process used for fulfilling those roles and responsibilities.
