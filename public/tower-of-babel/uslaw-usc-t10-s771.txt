10 U.S.C. § 771 — Unauthorized wearing prohibited
Citation: us:usc:10:771
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section771
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 45 (The Uniform). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided by law, no person except a member of the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, or Space Force, as the case may be, may wear—
(1)
the uniform, or a distinctive part of the uniform, of the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, or Space Force; or
(2)
a uniform any part of which is similar to a distinctive part of the uniform of the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, or Space Force.
