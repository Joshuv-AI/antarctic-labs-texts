41 C.F.R. § 105-74.505 — -74.505 How are violations of this part determined for recipients who are individuals?
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-74.505
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-74.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-74 (74—Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual recipient is in violation of the requirements of this part if the Administrator of General Services determines, in writing, that—
(a) The recipient has violated the requirements of subpart C of this part; or
(b) The recipient is convicted of a criminal drug offense resulting from a violation occurring during the conduct of any award activity.
