25 C.F.R. § 166.316 — Can a permittee construct improvements on permitted Indian land?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.316
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.316
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Improvements may be constructed on permitted Indian land if the permit contains a provision allowing improvements.
