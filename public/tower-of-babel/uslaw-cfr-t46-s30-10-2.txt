46 C.F.R. § 30.10-2 — -2 Accommodation space—TB/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:30.10-2
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-30.10-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 30 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term accommodation space means any public space such as a hall, dining room, mess room, lounge, corridor, lavatory, cabin, office, hospital, cinema, game and hobby room, pantry that contains no cooking appliances, and a similar space open to the passengers and crew.
