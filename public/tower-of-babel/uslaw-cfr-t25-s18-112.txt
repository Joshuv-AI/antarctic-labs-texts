25 C.F.R. § 18.112 — May a tribe appeal the approval or disapproval of a probate code?
Citation: us:cfr:25:18.112
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-18.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 18 (Tribal Probate Codes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. There is no right of appeal within the Department from a decision to approve or disapprove a tribal probate code.
