38 U.S.C. § 1317 — Restriction on payments under this chapter
Citation: us:usc:38:1317
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1317
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 13 (Dependency And Indemnity Compensation For Service-Connected Deaths); Subchapter II (Dependency And Indemnity Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Except as provided in subsection (b), no person eligible for dependency and indemnity compensation by reason of any death occurring after December 31, 1956, shall be eligible by reason of such death for any payments under (1) provisions of law administered by the Secretary providing for the payment of death compensation or death pension, or (2) subchapter I of chapter 81 of title 5.
(b)
A surviving spouse who is eligible for dependency and indemnity compensation may elect to receive death pension instead of such compensation.
