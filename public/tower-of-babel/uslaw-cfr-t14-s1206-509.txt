14 C.F.R. § 1206.509 — Form of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1206.509
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1206.509
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1206 (Procedures For Disclosure Of Records Under The Freedom Of Information Act (Foia)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Payment shall be made by check or money order payable to the “Treasury of the United States,” or by credit card per instructions in the initial determination or billing invoice and sent to NASA.
