49 C.F.R. § 387.405 — Limits of liability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:387.405
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-387.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 387 (Minimum Levels Of Financial Responsibility For Motor Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The minimum amounts for cargo and public liability security are identical to those prescribed for motor carriers at 49 CFR 387.303. The minimum amount for the surety bond or trust fund is identical to that prescribed for brokers at 49 CFR 387.307.
