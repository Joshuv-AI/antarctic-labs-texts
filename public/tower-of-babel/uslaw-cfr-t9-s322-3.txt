9 C.F.R. § 322.3 — Transferring products for export.
Citation: us:cfr:9:322.3
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-322.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 322 (Exports 1). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When inspected and passed products for export are transferred from tank cars to other containers on vessels, such transfer shall be done in accordance with the provisions of part 350 of subchapter B of this chapter.
