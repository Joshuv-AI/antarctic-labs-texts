10 U.S.C. § 1404 — Applicability of section 8301 of title 5
Citation: us:usc:10:1404
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1404
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 71 (Computation Of Retired Pay). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The retirement provisions of this title are subject to section 8301 of title 5.
