41 C.F.R. § 102-71.230 — -71.230 Waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-71.230
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-71.230
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-71 (71—Real Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of HUD may waive any requirement of this subpart (over which the Secretary of HUD has jurisdiction) that is not required by law, whenever it is determined that undue hardship would result from applying the requirement, or where application of the requirement would adversely affect the purposes of the program. Each waiver will be in writing and will be supported by documentation of the pertinent facts and grounds. The Secretary periodically will publish notices of granted waivers on the HUD website.
