30 U.S.C. § 960 — Limitation on issuance of temporary restraining order or preliminary injunction
Citation: us:usc:30:960
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section960
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 22 (Mine Safety And Health); Subchapter V (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In any proceeding in which the validity of any interim mandatory health or safety standard set forth in subchapters II and III of this chapter is in issue, no justice, judge, or court of the United States shall issue any temporary restraining order or preliminary injunction restraining the enforcement of such standard pending a determination of such issue on its merits.
