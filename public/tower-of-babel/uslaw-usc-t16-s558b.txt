16 U.S.C. § 558b — Definition of Secretaries
Citation: us:usc:16:558b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section558b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3 (Forests; Forest Service; Reforestation; Management); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this Act, the term “Secretaries” means each of—
(1)
the Secretary of Agriculture, acting through the Chief of the Forest Service; and
(2)
the Secretary of the Interior, acting through the Director of the Bureau of Land Management.
