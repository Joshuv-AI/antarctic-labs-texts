34 C.F.R. § 303.340 — Individualized family service plan—general.
Citation: us:cfr:34:303.340
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-303.340
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 303 (Early Intervention Program For Infants And Toddlers With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For each infant or toddler with a disability, the lead agency must ensure the development, review, and implementation of an individualized family service plan or IFSP developed by a multidisciplinary team, which includes the parent, that—
(a) Is consistent with the definition of that term in § 303.20; and
(b) Meets the requirements in §§ 303.342 through 303.346 of this subpart.
