14 U.S.C. § 3756 — Grade on entry upon active duty
Citation: us:usc:14:3756
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section3756
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 37 (Coast Guard Reserve); Subchapter II (Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A Reserve officer ordered to active duty or active duty for training shall be ordered in the grade held; except that the Secretary may authorize a higher grade.
