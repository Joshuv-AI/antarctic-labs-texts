45 C.F.R. § 1616.2 — Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1616.2
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1616.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1616 (Attorney Hiring). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Community, as used in this part, means the geographical area most closely corresponding to the area served by a recipient.
