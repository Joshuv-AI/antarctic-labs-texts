48 C.F.R. § 425.603 — 425.603 Exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:48:425.603
Creator: Chapter 4—Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-425.603
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 425 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary, without power of redelegation, has the authority to make the necessary determination(s) and authorize award(s) of contract(s) in accordance with FAR 25.603(b).
