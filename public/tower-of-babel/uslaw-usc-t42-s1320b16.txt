42 U.S.C. § 1320b–16 — Public disclosure of certain information on hospital financial interest and referral patterns
Citation: us:usc:42:1320b–16
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1320b–16
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter XI (General Provisions, Peer Review, And Administrative Simplification); Part A (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall make available to the public, in a form and manner specified by the Secretary, information disclosed to the Secretary pursuant to section 1395cc(a)(1)(S) of this title.
