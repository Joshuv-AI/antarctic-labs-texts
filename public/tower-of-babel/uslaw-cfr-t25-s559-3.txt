25 C.F.R. § 559.3 — When must a tribe submit a copy of a newly issued or renewed facility license to the Chair?
Citation: us:cfr:25:559.3
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-559.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 559 (Facility License Notifications And Submissions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A tribe must submit to the Chair a copy of each newly issued or renewed facility license within 30 days of issuance.
