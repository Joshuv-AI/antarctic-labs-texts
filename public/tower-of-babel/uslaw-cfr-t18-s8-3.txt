18 C.F.R. § 8.3 — Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:18:8.3
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-8.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 8 (Recreational Opportunities And Development At Licensed Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every licensee maintaining recreation facilities for the use of the public at a licensed project, or employing or permitting any other person to maintain such facilities, shall permit, or require such other person to permit, equal and unobstructed use of such facilities to all members of the public without regard to race, color, religious creed or national origin.
