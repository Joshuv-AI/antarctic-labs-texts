43 C.F.R. § 3000.10 — Nondiscrimination.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3000.10
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3000.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3000 (Minerals Management: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person acquiring a lease under this chapter must comply fully with the equal opportunity provisions of Executive Order 11246 dated September 24, 1965, as amended, and the rules, regulations and relevant orders of the Secretary of Labor (41 CFR part 60 and 43 CFR part 17).
