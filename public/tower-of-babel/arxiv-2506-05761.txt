The D + → K S 0 ​ π 0 ​ μ + ​ ν μ D^{+}\to K_{S}^{0}\pi^{0}\mu^{+}\nu_{\mu} decay is observed for the first time, with a branching fraction of ( 0.896 ± 0.017 stat ± 0.008 syst ) % (0.896\pm 0.017_{\rm stat}\pm 0.008_{\rm syst})\% , and the branching fraction of D + → K S 0 ​ π 0 ​ e + ​ ν e D^{+}\to K_{S}^{0}\pi^{0}e^{+}\nu_{e} is determined with the improved precision as ( 0.943 ± 0.012 stat ± 0.010 syst ) % (0.943\pm 0.012_{\rm stat}\pm 0.010_{\rm syst})\% . From the analysis of the dynamics, we observe that the dominant K ¯ ∗ ​ ( 892 ) 0 \bar{K}^{\ast}(892)^{0} component is accompanied by an S S -wave contribution, which accounts for ( 7.10 ± 0.68 stat ± 0.41 syst ) % (7.10\pm 0.68_{\rm stat}\pm 0.41_{\rm syst})\% of the total decay rate of the μ + \mu^{+} channel and ( 6.39 ± 0.17 stat ± 0.14 syst ) % (6.39\pm 0.17_{\rm stat}\pm 0.14_{\rm syst})\% of the e + e^{+} channel. Assuming a single-pole dominance parameterization, the hadronic form factor ratios are extracted to be r V = V ⁡ ( 0 ) / A 1 ​ ( 0 ) = 1.42 ± 0.03 stat ± 0.02 syst r_{V}=V(0)/A_{1}(0)=1.42\pm\,0.03_{\rm stat}\pm\,0.02_{\rm syst} and r 2 = A 2 ​ ( 0 ) / A 1 ​ ( 0 ) = 0.75 ± 0.03 stat ± 0.01 syst r_{2}=A_{2}(0)/A_{1}(0)=0.75\pm\,0.03_{\rm stat}\pm\,0.01_{\rm syst} . Based on the first comprehensive angular and the decay-rate C ​ P CP asymmetry analysis, the full set of averaged angular and C ​ P CP asymmetry observables are measured as a function of the momentum-transfer squared; they are consistent with expectations from the Standard Model. No evidence for violation of μ − e \mu-e lepton-flavor universality is observed in either the full range or the five chosen bins of momentum-transfer squared.

The angular coefficients can be expanded in terms of four form factors
\mathcal{F}_{1,2,3,4}
as

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{1}=

\displaystyle\frac{\beta_{\ell}}{4}\left[\left(1+\frac{m^{2}_{\ell}}{q^{2}}\right)|\mathcal{F}_{1}|^{2}+\frac{3}{2}\left(1+\frac{m^{2}_{\ell}}{3q^{2}}\right)\sin^{2}\theta_{K}\left(|\mathcal{F}_{2}|^{2}+|\mathcal{F}_{3}|^{2}\right)+\frac{2m^{2}_{\ell}}{q^{2}}|\mathcal{F}_{4}|^{2}\right],

(13)

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{2}=

\displaystyle-\frac{\beta_{\ell}^{2}}{4}\left[|\mathcal{F}_{1}|^{2}-\frac{1}{2}\sin^{2}\theta_{K}\left(|\mathcal{F}_{2}|^{2}+|\mathcal{F}_{3}|^{2}\right)\right],

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{3}=

\displaystyle-\frac{\beta_{\ell}^{2}}{4}\left[\left(|\mathcal{F}_{2}|^{2}-|\mathcal{F}_{3}|^{2}\right)\right]\sin^{2}\theta_{K},

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{4}=

\displaystyle\frac{\beta_{\ell}^{2}}{2}\operatorname{Re}\left(\mathcal{F}_{1}\mathcal{F}_{2}^{*}\right)\sin\theta_{K},

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{5}=

\displaystyle\beta_{\ell}\operatorname{Re}\left[\mathcal{F}_{1}\mathcal{F}_{3}^{*}+\frac{m^{2}_{\ell}}{q^{2}}\mathcal{F}_{4}\mathcal{F}_{2}^{*}\right]\sin\theta_{K},

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{6}=

\displaystyle\beta_{\ell}\operatorname{Re}\left[\mathcal{F}_{2}\mathcal{F}_{3}^{*}\sin^{2}\theta_{K}-\frac{m^{2}_{\ell}}{q^{2}}\mathcal{F}_{1}\mathcal{F}_{4}^{*}\right],

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{7}=

\displaystyle\beta_{\ell}\operatorname{Im}\left[\left(\mathcal{F}_{1}\mathcal{F}_{2}^{*}\right)+\frac{m^{2}_{\ell}}{q^{2}}\mathcal{F}_{4}\mathcal{F}_{3}^{*}\right]\sin\theta_{K},

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{8}=

\displaystyle\frac{\beta_{\ell}^{2}}{2}\operatorname{Im}\left(\mathcal{F}_{1}\mathcal{F}_{3}^{*}\right)\sin\theta_{K},

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{9}=

\displaystyle-\frac{\beta_{\ell}^{2}}{2}\operatorname{Im}\left(\mathcal{F}_{2}\mathcal{F}_{3}^{*}\right)\sin^{2}\theta_{K}.

The form factors
\mathcal{F}_{1,2,3,4}
can be expanded into partial waves to show their explicit dependence on
\theta_{K}
. If only

S
- and
P
-waves are kept, one obtains:

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}_{1}=\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\mathcal{F}_{1,l}\,P_{l}\left(\cos\theta_{K}\right)=\mathcal{F}_{10}+\mathcal{F}_{11}\cos\theta_{K},

(14)

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}_{2}=\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\sqrt{l(l+1)}}\,\mathcal{F}_{2,l}\,\frac{d}{d\cos\theta_{K}}\,P_{l}\left(\cos\theta_{K}\right)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\,\mathcal{F}_{21},

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}_{3}=\sum_{l=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{\sqrt{l(l+1)}}\,\mathcal{F}_{3,l}\,\frac{d}{d\cos\theta_{K}}\,P_{l}\left(\cos\theta_{K}\right)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\,\mathcal{F}_{31},

\displaystyle\mathcal{F}_{4}=\sum_{l=0}^{\infty}\mathcal{F}_{4,l}\,P_{l}\left(\cos\theta_{K}\right)=\mathcal{F}_{40}+\mathcal{F}_{41}\cos\theta_{K}.

By comparing expressions given in Refs.

;
