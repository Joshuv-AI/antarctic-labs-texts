40 U.S.C. § 3102 — Naming or designating buildings
Citation: us:usc:40:3102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section3102
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part A (General); Chapter 31 (General); Subchapter I (Oversight And Regulation Of Public Buildings). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator of General Services may name or otherwise designate any building under the custody and control of the General Services Administration, regardless of whether it was previously named by statute.
