7 C.F.R. § 3430.203 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3430.203
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3430.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3430 (Competitive And Noncompetitive Non-Formula Federal Assistance Programs—General Award Administrative Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Eligible applicants for the grant program implemented under this subpart include: Federal agencies, national laboratories; colleges and universities (offering associate's or higher degrees); research institutions and organizations; private organizations or corporations; State agricultural experiment stations; individuals; and groups consisting of 2 or more entities identified in this sentence.
