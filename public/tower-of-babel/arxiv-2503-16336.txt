A parallel algorithm for the odd two-face shortest k k -disjoint path problem
Srijan Chakraborty, Samir Datta

Abstract
The shortest Disjoint Path problem ( 𝖲𝖣𝖯𝖯 {\mathsf{SDPP}} ) requires us to find pairwise vertex disjoint paths between k k designated pairs of terminal vertices such that the sum of the path lengths is minimum. The focus here is on 𝖲𝖣𝖯𝖯 {\mathsf{SDPP}} restricted to planar graphs where all terminals are arbitrarily partitioned over two distinct faces with the additional restriction that each face is required to contain an odd number of terminals. We call this problem the Odd two-face planar 𝖲𝖣𝖯𝖯 {\mathsf{SDPP}} . It is shown that this problem is solvable in randomized polynomial time and even in 𝖱𝖭𝖢 {\mathsf{RNC}} . This is the first parallel (or even polynomial time) solution for the problem. Our algorithm combines ideas from the randomized solution for 2 2 - 𝖲𝖣𝖯𝖯 {\mathsf{SDPP}} by Björklund and Huslfeldt with its parallelization by Datta and Jaiswal along with the deterministic algorithm for One-face planar 𝖲𝖣𝖯𝖯 {\mathsf{SDPP}} by Datta, Iyer, Kulkarni and Mukherjee. The proof uses a combination of two involutions to reduce a system of linear equations modulo a power of 2 2 to a system of triangular form that is, therefore, invertible. This, in turn, is proved by showing that the matrix of the equations, can be interpreted as (the adjacency matrix of) a directed acyclic graph (DAG). While our algorithm is primarily algebraic the proof remains combinatorial. We also give a parallel algorithm for the ( A + B ) (A+B) - 𝖲𝖣𝖯𝖯 {\mathsf{SDPP}} introduced by Hirai and Namba.
