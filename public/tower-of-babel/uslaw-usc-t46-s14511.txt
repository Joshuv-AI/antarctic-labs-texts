46 U.S.C. § 14511 — Application
Citation: us:usc:46:14511
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section14511
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part J (Measurement Of Vessels); Chapter 145 (Regulatory Measurement); Subchapter II (Formal Systems). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter applies to a vessel described in section 14501 of this title if—
(1)
the owner requests; or
(2)
the vessel is—
(A)
self-propelled;
(B)
at least 24 meters (79 feet) overall in length; and
(C)
not operated only for pleasure.
