15 C.F.R. § 19.15 — How will Commerce entities refer Commerce debts to private collection agencies?
Citation: us:cfr:15:19.15
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-19.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 19 (Commerce Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Commerce entities will transfer delinquent Commerce debts to the Bureau of the Fiscal Service to obtain debt collection services provided by private collection agencies. See § 19.9 of this part.
