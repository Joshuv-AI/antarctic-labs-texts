25 U.S.C. § 123b — Tribal funds for traveling and other expenses
Citation: us:usc:25:123b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section123b
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 4 (Performance By United States Of Obligations To Indians); Subchapter II (Disbursement Of Moneys And Supplies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after May 9, 1938, tribal funds shall be available for appropriation by Congress for traveling and other expenses, including supplies and equipment, of members of tribal councils, business committees, or other tribal organizations, when engaged on business of the tribes.
