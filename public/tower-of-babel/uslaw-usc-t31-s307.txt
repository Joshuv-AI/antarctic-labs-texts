31 U.S.C. § 307 — Office of the Comptroller of the Currency
Citation: us:usc:31:307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section307
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 3 (Department Of The Treasury); Subchapter I (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, established under section 324 of the Revised Statutes (12 U.S.C. 1), is an office in the Department of the Treasury.
