25 C.F.R. § 1000.1015 — Is it mandatory to go through the information phase before initiating the negotiation phase?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.1015
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.1015

No, a Tribe/Consortium may go directly to the negotiation phase.
