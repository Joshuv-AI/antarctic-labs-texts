48 U.S.C. § 1425e — Additional powers
Citation: us:usc:48:1425e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1425e
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 8A (Guam); Subchapter V (Public Housing And Urban Renewal). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Powers granted herein shall be in addition to, and not in derogation of, any powers granted by other law to, or for the benefit or assistance of, any public corporate authority.
