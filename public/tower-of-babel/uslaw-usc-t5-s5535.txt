5 U.S.C. § 5535 — Extra pay for details prohibited
Citation: us:usc:5:5535
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section5535
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart D (Pay And Allowances); Chapter 55 (Pay Administration); Subchapter IV (Dual Pay And Dual Employment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
An officer may not receive pay in addition to the pay for his regular office for performing the duties of a vacant office as authorized by sections 3345–3347 of this title.
(b)
An employee may not receive—
(1)
additional pay or allowances for performing the duties of another employee; or
(2)
pay in addition to the regular pay received for employment held before his appointment or designation as acting for or instead of an occupant of another position or employment.
This subsection does not prevent a regular and permanent appointment by promotion from a lower to a higher grade of employment.
