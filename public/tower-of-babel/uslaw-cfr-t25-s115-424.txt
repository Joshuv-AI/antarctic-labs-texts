25 C.F.R. § 115.424 — Who receives a copy of the BIA-approved distribution plan and any amendments to the plan?
Citation: us:cfr:25:115.424
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-115.424
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 115 (Trust Funds For Tribes And Individual Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The BIA-approved distribution plan will be provided to:
(a) The custodial parent; or
(b) A legal guardian; or
(c) At the Secretary's discretion, in unusual circumstances, to a family member who has been recognized as having control and custody of the minor; or
(d) An emancipated minor; and
(e) OTFM.
