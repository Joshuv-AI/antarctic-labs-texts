19 U.S.C. § 1313a — Appropriations for refunds, drawbacks, bounties, etc.
Citation: us:usc:19:1313a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1313a
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part I (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are appropriated such amounts as hereafter may be necessary for refund or payment of custom collections or receipts, and payment of debentures or drawbacks, bounties, and allowances, as authorized by law.
