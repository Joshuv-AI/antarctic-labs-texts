40 C.F.R. § 799.11 — Availability of test guidelines.
Citation: us:cfr:40:799.11
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-799.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 799 (Identification Of Specific Chemical Substance And Mixture Testing Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The TSCA and FIFRA guidelines for the various study plans are available from the National Technical Information Service (NTIS). Address and telephone number: National Technical Information Service, 5285 Port Royal Road, Springfield, VA 22161 (703-487-4650).
(b) The OECD guidelines for the various study plans are available from the following address: OECD Publication and Information Center, 1750 Pennsylvania Ave., NW., Washington, DC 20006 (202-724-1857).
