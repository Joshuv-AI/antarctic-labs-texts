5 C.F.R. § 837.804 — Finality of elections under this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:5:837.804
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-837.804
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 837 (Reemployment Of Annuitants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided by this subpart, an election of coverage under, or annuity from, another retirement system, in lieu of CSRS or FERS coverage or annuity, or the election between simultaneous entitlements under CSRS or FERS, is final and conclusive for the period of simultaneous entitlement to coverage or annuity.
