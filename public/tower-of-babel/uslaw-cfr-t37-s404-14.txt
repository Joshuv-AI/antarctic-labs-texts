37 C.F.R. § 404.14 — Confidentiality of information.
Citation: us:cfr:37:404.14
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-404.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 404 (Licensing Of Government-Owned Inventions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

35 U.S.C. 209(f) requires that any plan submitted pursuant to § 404.8(a)(8) and any report required by 35 U.S.C. 209(d)(2) shall be treated as commercial or financial information obtained from a person and privileged and confidential and not subject to disclosure under 5 U.S.C. 552.
