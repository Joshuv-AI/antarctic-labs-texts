21 C.F.R. § 1309.54 — Burden of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1309.54
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1309.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1309 (Registration Of Manufacturers, Distributors, Importers And Exporters Of List I Chemicals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) At any hearing for the denial of a registration, the Administration shall have the burden of proving that the requirements for such registration pursuant to section 303 of the Act (21 U.S.C. 823) are not satisfied.
(b) At any hearing for the revocation or suspension of a registration, the Administration shall have the burden of proving that the requirements for such revocation or suspension pursuant to section 304(a) of the Act (21 U.S.C. 824(a)) are satisfied.
