Results of Fractional Rough Burgers equation in H s H^{s} space and its application
Shuolin Zhang, Zhaonan Luo, Zhaoyang Yin

Abstract
In this paper, we study the well-posedness of Fractional Rough Burgers equation driven by space-time noise in H s ​ ( 𝕋 ) H^{s}(\mathbb{T}) space. For the higher dissipation γ ∈ ( 4 3 , 2 ] \gamma\in(\frac{4}{3},2] , we establish local well-posedness. Global well-posedness is further obtained when γ \gamma is restricted to the interval ( 3 2 , 2 ] (\frac{3}{2},2] . For the lower dissipation γ ∈ ( 5 4 , 4 3 ] \gamma\in(\frac{5}{4},\frac{4}{3}] , we use the regularity analysis derivation the para-controlled solution. Keywords: Rough PDEs; Paracontrolled solution; Well-posedness; Shallow water.

\displaystyle\|f\prec\!\!\!\prec g\|_{\mathcal{W}_{2}^{\alpha+\rho}(T)}\lesssim T^{1-\frac{\rho}{\gamma}}\|f\|_{\mathcal{W}_{\infty}^{\epsilon}(T)}(\|g\|_{C_{T}H^{\alpha+\gamma-\epsilon}}+\|\mathcal{L}g\|_{C_{T}H^{\alpha}}).

In this little section, we introduce some stochastic tools we need in this paper. The first tools is the Kolmogorov’s continuity criterion

Lemma 2.6

(
[

]
)

Let

Then, there is a modification

of

\alpha

such that, for every

\displaystyle d(\tilde{X}(s,\omega),\tilde{X}(t,\omega))\leq C_{\alpha}(\omega)|t-s|^{\alpha},

where

Now we give a basic analysis for white noise
\xi
. For the linear evolution
X(t):=\int_{0}^{t}e^{(t-s)\Lambda^{\gamma}}\xi(s)\mathrm{d}s
, given a decomposition

X(t)=\int_{-\infty}^{t}e^{(t-s)\Lambda^{\gamma}}\xi(s)\mathrm{d}s-e^{t\Lambda^{\gamma}}\int_{-\infty}^{0}e^{-s\Lambda^{\gamma}}\xi(s)\mathrm{d}s.

Its not difficult to show that the second term is smooth, which means all the irregularity of
X(t)
is described by the first term of the right hand side of (

). Hence we define
Y(t):=\int_{-\infty}^{t}e^{(t-s)\Lambda^{\gamma}}\xi(s)\mathrm{d}s
. For
X(t)
and

, we have following lemma.

Lemma 2.7
.

The spatial Fourier transform
Y_{k}=\mathcal{F}_{x}Y(k)
is a Gaussian process with zero-mean and satisfy

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}[Y_{k}(t)Y_{k^{\prime}}(t^{\prime})]=e^{-|t-t^{\prime}||k|^{\gamma}}\frac{|\mathbb{T}|}{2|k|^{\gamma}}\textbf{I}_{k+k^{\prime}=0}(k,k^{\prime}).

where
|\mathbb{T}|=\int_{\mathbb{T}}1\mathrm{d}x
and
\textbf{I}_{k+k^{\prime}=0}(k,k^{\prime})
is the indicator function. Moreover, for any
T>0
and
\beta<\frac{\gamma(1-2\kappa)}{2}-\frac{1}{2}
, we have

, for
\kappa<\frac{1}{2}
.

Proof.

It’s easy to check the first result by noting that

\mathbb{E}[\mathcal{F}\xi(t,k){\mathcal{F}\xi(s,k^{\prime})}]=\delta(t-s)\textbf{I}_{k+k^{\prime}=0}(k,k^{\prime}).

(2.4)

Considering the second result, we can rewrite
Y(t)
as

\displaystyle Y(t)=\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}Y_{k}(t)e^{ikx}.

Calculate
\mathbb{E}|\Delta_{j}(Y(t)-Y(t^{\prime}))|^{2}
and
\mathbb{E}\|\Delta_{j}(Y(t)-Y(t^{\prime}))\|_{L^{2}}^{2p}
as follows

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}|\Delta_{j}(Y(t)-Y(t^{\prime}))|^{2}=

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\sum_{k,k^{\prime}\in\mathbb{Z}}\rho_{j}(k)\rho_{j}(k^{\prime})[Y_{k}(t)-Y_{k}(t^{\prime})][Y_{-k^{\prime}}(t)-Y_{-k^{\prime}}(t^{\prime})]e^{i(k-k^{\prime})x}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{k,k^{\prime}\in\mathbb{Z}}\rho_{j}(k)\rho_{j}(k^{\prime})|\mathbb{T}|\frac{2-2e^{-|t-t^{\prime}||k|^{\gamma}}}{2|k|^{\gamma}}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\sum_{k\in\mathrm{supp}(\rho_{j})}k^{-\gamma(1-\delta)}|t-t^{\prime}|^{\delta}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle 2^{j(1-\gamma(1-\delta))}|t-t^{\prime}|^{\delta},

for some
\delta\in[0,1]
. By Gaussian hypercontractivity
[
Jan97
]
, we have

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}|\Delta_{j}(Y(t,x_{i})-Y(t^{\prime},x_{i}))|^{2p}\lesssim(\mathbb{E}|\Delta_{j}(Y(t,x_{i})-Y(t^{\prime},x_{i}))|^{2})^{p}.

It implies that

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\|\Delta_{j}(Y(t)-Y(t^{\prime}))\|_{L^{2}}^{2p}=

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}(\int_{\mathbb{T}}|\Delta_{j}(Y(t)-Y(t^{\prime}))|^{2}\mathrm{d}x)^{p}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{T}^{\otimes p}}\mathbb{E}\prod_{i=1}^{p}|\Delta_{j}(Y(t,x_{i})-Y(t^{\prime},x_{i}))|^{2}\mathrm{d}x_{i}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{T}^{\otimes p}}\prod_{i=1}^{p}(\mathbb{E}|\Delta_{j}(Y(t,x_{i})-Y(t^{\prime},x_{i}))|^{2p})^{\frac{1}{p}}\mathrm{d}x_{i}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{T}^{\otimes p}}\prod_{i=1}^{p}(\mathbb{E}|\Delta_{j}(Y(t,x_{i})-Y(t^{\prime},x_{i}))|^{2})\mathrm{d}x_{i}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle 2^{pj(1-\gamma(1-\delta))}|t-t^{\prime}|^{\delta p}.

Then we can estimate
\mathbb{E}\|Y(t)-Y(t^{\prime})\|_{H^{\alpha}}^{2p}
as

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\|Y(t)-Y(t^{\prime})\|_{B_{2,2}^{\beta}}^{2p}\lesssim

\displaystyle(\sum_{j\geq-1}2^{2j\beta}\mathbb{(}E\|\Delta_{j}(Y(t)-Y(t^{\prime}))\|_{L^{2}}^{2p})^{\frac{1}{p}})^{p}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle(\sum_{j\geq-1}2^{j(2\beta+1-\gamma(1-\delta))})^{p}|t-t^{\prime}|^{\delta p}<\infty,

for
\beta<\frac{\gamma(1-\delta)-1}{2}
. By Lemma
2.6
, we have
Y(t)\in C^{\frac{\delta}{2}-\frac{1}{2p}}_{T}H^{\frac{\gamma(1-\delta)-1}{2}}
. Choose

large enough, we can prove the case of

. For Hölder space, we can also have the results by similar argument

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}\|Y(t)-Y(t^{\prime})\|_{B_{2p,2p}^{\beta}}^{2p}=

\displaystyle\sum_{j\geq-1}2^{2j\beta p}\mathbb{E}\|\Delta_{j}(Y(t)-Y(t^{\prime}))\|_{L^{2p}}^{2p}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\sum_{j\geq-1}2^{2j\beta p}\|\mathbb{E}|\Delta_{j}(Y(t)-Y(s))|^{2}\|_{L^{p}}^{p}

\displaystyle\lesssim

\displaystyle\sum_{j\geq-1}2^{jp(2\beta+1-\gamma(1-\delta))}|t-t^{\prime}|^{\delta p}.

Next we introduce a generalized contraction mapping theorem which compared with Lemma 5.5(

Lemma 2.8
.

Let

in

\tilde{x}=a+L(\tilde{x})+\mathcal{B}(\tilde{x},\tilde{x}).

Proof.

The proof of first result can refer to
[
BCD11
]
. For second, we involved an application of iterative scheme defined by

\tilde{x}_{0}=a\quad\tilde{x}_{n+1}=a+L(\tilde{x}_{n})+\mathcal{B}(\tilde{x}_{n},\tilde{x}_{n}).

By induction argument, we show that
\|\tilde{x}_{n+1}\|\leq 2r
when
\|\tilde{x}_{n}\|\leq 2r
. In fact, by our assumption, we get

\displaystyle\|\tilde{x}_{n+1}\|_{E}\leq r+\frac{r}{4}+4\|\mathcal{B}\|r^{2}\leq 2r.

\displaystyle\|L(\tilde{x}_{n}-\tilde{x}_{n-1})\|_{E}+4r\|\mathcal{B}\|\|\tilde{x}_{n}-\tilde{x}_{n-1}\|_{E}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{3}{4}\|\tilde{x}_{n}-\tilde{x}_{n-1}\|_{E}.

Then we get the limit of

is a unique fixed point of
\tilde{x}\mapsto a+L(\tilde{x})+\mathcal{B}(\tilde{x},\tilde{x})
. This means that the lemma is proved.
