42 C.F.R. § 65a.3 — a.3 Who is eligible to apply for a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:42:65a.3
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-65a.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 65a (PART 65a—NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH SCIENCES HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES BASIC RESEARCH AND TRAINING GRANTS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as otherwise prohibited by law, any public or private nonprofit institution of higher education may apply for an award under this part.
(b) Awardee institutions may carry out portions of the research or training components of an award through contracts with appropriate organizations, including:
(1) Generators of hazardous wastes;
(2) Persons involved in the detection, assessment, evaluation, and treatment of hazardous substances;
(3) Owners and operators of facilities at which hazardous substances are located; and
(4) State and local governments.
