25 C.F.R. § 12.3 — Who supervises Bureau of Indian Affairs criminal investigators?
Citation: us:cfr:25:12.3
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-12.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 12 (Indian Country Law Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All BIA criminal investigators are supervised by other criminal investigators within the Office of Law Enforcement Services.
