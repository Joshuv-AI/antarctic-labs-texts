12 C.F.R. § 7.1020 — Purchase of open accounts by a national bank.
Citation: us:cfr:12:7.1020
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-7.1020
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 7 (Activities And Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General. The purchase of open accounts is a part of the business of banking and within the power of a national bank.
(b) Export transactions. A national bank may purchase open accounts in connection with export transactions; the accounts should be protected by insurance such as that provided by the Foreign Credit Insurance Association and the Export-Import Bank.
