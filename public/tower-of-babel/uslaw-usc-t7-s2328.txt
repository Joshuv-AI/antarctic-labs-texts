7 U.S.C. § 2328 — Library
Citation: us:usc:7:2328
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2328
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 57 (Plant Variety Protection); Subchapter I (Plant Variety Protection Office); Part A (Organization And Publications). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall maintain a library of scientific and other works and periodicals, both foreign and domestic, in the Plant Variety Protection Office to aid the examiners in the discharge of their duties.
