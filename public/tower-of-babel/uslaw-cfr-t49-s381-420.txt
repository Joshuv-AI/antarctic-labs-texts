49 C.F.R. § 381.420 — What will the FMCSA do after the agency receives my suggestion for a pilot program?
Citation: us:cfr:49:381.420
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-381.420
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 381 (Waivers, Exemptions, And Pilot Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration will review your suggestion for a pilot program and make a recommendation to the Administrator. The final decision whether to propose the development of a pilot program based upon your recommendation will be made by the Administrator.
(b) You will be sent a copy of the Administrator's decision. If the pilot program is approved, the agency will follow the administrative procedures contained in subpart E of this part.
