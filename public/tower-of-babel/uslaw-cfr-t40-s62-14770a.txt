40 C.F.R. § 62.14770a — a Am I required to apply for and obtain a title V operating permit for my unit?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.14770a
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.14770a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Each CISWI and ACI subject to standards under this subpart must operate pursuant to a permit issued under section 129(e) and title V of the Clean Air Act.
