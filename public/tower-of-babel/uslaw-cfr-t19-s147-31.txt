19 C.F.R. § 147.31 — Articles to be kept separate.
Citation: us:cfr:19:147.31
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-147.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 147 (Trade Fairs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Articles for exhibit at a fair shall be segregated from domestic articles and from imported articles entered under the provisions of the general Customs laws and released from Customs custody.
