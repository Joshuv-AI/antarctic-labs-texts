22 U.S.C. § 1381 — Retention by United States of title to real and personal property
Citation: us:usc:22:1381
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1381
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 15 (The Republic Of The Philippines); Subchapter V (Property Retained By The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall remain vested in the Government of the United States or its agencies or instrumentalities all the right, title, and interest of the said Government or its agencies or instrumentalities to all real and personal property within the Philippine Islands as may now be vested in, or later be acquired by the Government of the United States or any of its agencies or instrumentalities.
