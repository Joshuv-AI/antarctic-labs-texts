10 C.F.R. § 820.72 — Referral to the Attorney General.
Citation: us:cfr:10:820.72
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-820.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 820 (Procedural Rules For Doe Nuclear Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If there is reason to believe a criminal violation of the Act or the DOE Nuclear Safety Requirements has occurred, DOE may refer the matter to the Attorney General of the United States for investigation or prosecution.
