10 U.S.C. § 8213 — Enlisted grades and ratings: authority to establish
Citation: us:usc:10:8213
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8213
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 825 (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Navy may establish such enlisted grades and ratings as are necessary for the proper administration of the Navy and the Marine Corps.
