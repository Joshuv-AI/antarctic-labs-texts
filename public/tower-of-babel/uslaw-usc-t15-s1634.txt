15 U.S.C. § 1634 — Effect of subsequent occurrence
Citation: us:usc:15:1634
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1634
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 41 (Consumer Credit Protection); Subchapter I (Consumer Credit Cost Disclosure); Part B (Credit Transactions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If information disclosed in accordance with this part is subsequently rendered inaccurate as the result of any act, occurrence, or agreement subsequent to the delivery of the required disclosures, the inaccuracy resulting therefrom does not constitute a violation of this part.
