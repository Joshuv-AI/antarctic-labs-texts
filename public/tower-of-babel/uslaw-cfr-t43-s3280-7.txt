43 C.F.R. § 3280.7 — Can BLM require an owner or lessee of lands not under Federal administration to unitize their lands or leases?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3280.7
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3280.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3280 (Geothermal Resources Unit Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM cannot require the commitment of lands or leases not under Federal administration or jurisdiction to a Federal unit.
