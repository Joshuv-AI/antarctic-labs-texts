31 C.F.R. § 561.326 — Privately owned foreign financial institution.
Citation: us:cfr:31:561.326
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-561.326
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 561 (Iranian Financial Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The phrase privately owned foreign financial institution means any foreign financial institution that is not owned or controlled by the government of a foreign country.
