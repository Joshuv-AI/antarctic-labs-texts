15 C.F.R. § 2006.10 — Waiver of requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:15:2006.10
Creator: Office Of The United States Trade Representative
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-2006.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 2006 (Procedures For Filing Petitions For Action Under Section 301 Of The Trade Act Of 1974, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent consistent with the requirements of the Trade Act, the requirements of §§ 2006.0 through 2006.3 and 2006.8 may be waived by the Trade Representative or the Chairman of the Section 301 Committee upon a showing of good cause and for reasons of equity and the public interest.
