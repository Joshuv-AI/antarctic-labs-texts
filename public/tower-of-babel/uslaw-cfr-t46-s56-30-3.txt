46 C.F.R. § 56.30-3 — -3 Piping joints (reproduces 110).
Citation: us:cfr:46:56.30-3
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-56.30-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 56 (Piping Systems And Appurtenances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The type of piping joint used must be suitable for the design conditions and must be selected with consideration of joint tightness, mechanical strength and the nature of the fluid handled.
