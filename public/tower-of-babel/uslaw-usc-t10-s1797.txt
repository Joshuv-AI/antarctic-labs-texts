10 U.S.C. § 1797 — Early childhood education program
Citation: us:usc:10:1797
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1797
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 88 (Military Family Programs And Military Child Care); Subchapter II (Military Child Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense shall require that all military child development centers meet standards of operation necessary for accreditation by an appropriate national early childhood programs accrediting body.
