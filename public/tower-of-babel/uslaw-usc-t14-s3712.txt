14 U.S.C. § 3712 — Exclusiveness of service
Citation: us:usc:14:3712
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section3712
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 37 (Coast Guard Reserve); Subchapter I (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No member of the Reserve, other than a temporary member, may be a member of another military organization. A temporary member of the Reserve who is a member of another military component shall, if ordered to active duty therein, be disenrolled as a temporary member of the Reserve.
