45 C.F.R. § 1174.6 — Prerequisites for issuing a complaint.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1174.6
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1174.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1174 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority may issue a complaint only if:
(a) The Attorney General (or designee) approves the reviewing official's referral of the allegations for adjudication; and
(b) In a case of submission of false claims, if the amount of money or the value of property or services that a false claim (or a group of related claims submitted at the same time) demanded or requested does not exceed $150,000.
