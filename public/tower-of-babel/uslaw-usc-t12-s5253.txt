12 U.S.C. § 5253 — Emergency treatment
Citation: us:usc:12:5253
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section5253
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 52 (Emergency Economic Stabilization); Subchapter II (Budget-Related Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All provisions of this chapter are designated as an emergency requirement and necessary to meet emergency needs pursuant to section 204(a) of S. Con. Res 1 21 (110th Congress), the concurrent resolution on the budget for fiscal year 2008 and rescissions of any amounts provided in this chapter shall not be counted for purposes of budget enforcement.
