25 C.F.R. § 212.6 — Authority and responsibility of the Minerals Management Service (MMS).
Citation: us:cfr:25:212.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-212.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 212 (Leasing Of Allotted Lands For Mineral Development). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The functions of the MMS for reporting, accounting, and auditing are found in 30 CFR chapter II, subchapters A and C, which apply to leases approved under this part. To the extent the parties to a lease or permit are able to provide reasonable provisions satisfactorily addressing the functions governed by MMS regulations, the Secretary may approve alternate provisions in a lease or permit.
