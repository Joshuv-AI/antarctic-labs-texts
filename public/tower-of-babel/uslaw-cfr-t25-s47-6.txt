25 C.F.R. § 47.6 — Who has access to local education financial records?
Citation: us:cfr:25:47.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-47.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 47 (Uniform Direct Funding And Support For Bureau-Operated Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Comptroller General, the Assistant Secretary, the Director, or any of their duly authorized representatives have access for audit and explanation purposes to any of the local school's accounts, documents, papers, and records which are related to the Bureau-operated schools' operation.
