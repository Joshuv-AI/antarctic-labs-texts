46 C.F.R. § 120.392 — Radiotelephone installations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:120.392
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-120.392
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 120 (Electrical Installation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A separate circuit, with overcurrent protection at the main distribution panel, must be provided for each radiotelephone installation.
