46 C.F.R. § 143.510 — Verification of compliance with design standards.
Citation: us:cfr:46:143.510
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-143.510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 143 (Machinery And Electrical Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Verification of compliance with the machinery and electrical design standards in this subpart is obtained by following the provisions in §§ 144.135 through 144.145 of this subchapter.
