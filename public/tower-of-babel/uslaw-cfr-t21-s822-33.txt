21 C.F.R. § 822.33 — How long must we keep the records?
Citation: us:cfr:21:822.33
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-822.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 822 (Postmarket Surveillance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You, the designated person, and your investigators must keep all records for a period of 2 years after we have accepted your final report, unless we specify otherwise.
