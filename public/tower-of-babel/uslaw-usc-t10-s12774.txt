10 U.S.C. § 12774 — Retired lists
Citation: us:usc:10:12774
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12774
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1225 (Retired Grade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary concerned, there shall be maintained retired lists containing the names of the Reserves of the armed forces under the Secretary’s jurisdiction who are in the Retired Reserve.
(b)
The Secretary of the Navy shall maintain a United States Naval Reserve Retired List containing the names of members of the Navy Reserve and the Marine Corps Reserve entitled to retired pay.
