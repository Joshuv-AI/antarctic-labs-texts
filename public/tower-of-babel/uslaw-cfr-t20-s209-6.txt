20 C.F.R. § 209.6 — Employers' notice of death of employees.
Citation: us:cfr:20:209.6
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-209.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 209 (Railroad Employers' Reports And Responsibilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each employer shall notify the Board immediately of the death of an employee who, prior to the employee's death, performed compensated service which has not been reported to the Board.
