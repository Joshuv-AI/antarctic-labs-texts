46 C.F.R. § 34.05-10 — -10 Portable and semiportable extinguishers—TB/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:34.05-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-34.05-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 34 (Fire Fighting Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All portable and semiportable extinguishers on board tank vessels shall be of an approved type.
(b) The type, size, location and arrangement of portable and semiportable extinguishers shall be as set forth in subpart 34.50.
