28 C.F.R. § 505.2 — Annual determination of average cost of incarceration.
Citation: us:cfr:28:505.2
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-505.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 505 (Cost Of Incarceration Fee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to 28 CFR 0.96c, the Bureau of Prisons staff is responsible for calculating the annual average cost of incarceration. This calculation is reviewed annually and the revised figure is published as a notice in the Federal Register.
