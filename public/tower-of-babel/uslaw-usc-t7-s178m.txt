7 U.S.C. § 178m — Report to President and Congress
Citation: us:usc:7:178m
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section178m
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 8A (Rubber And Other Critical Agricultural Materials); Subchapter II (Critical Agricultural Materials). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretaries shall submit to the President and the Congress, no later than December 31, 1980, and each year thereafter through 1987, a report on the status of the research, development, and other work underway under this subchapter. Such report shall (1) recommend specific directions for further research, development and other work, and (2) recommend funding levels for various elements of the overall project.
