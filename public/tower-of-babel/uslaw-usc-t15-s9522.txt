15 U.S.C. § 9522 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:15:9522
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9522
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 120 (Minority Business Development); Subchapter I (Existing Initiatives); Part B (Minority Business Development Agency Business Center Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of the MBDA Business Center Program shall be to create a national network of public-private partnerships that—
(1)
assist minority business enterprises in—
(A)
accessing capital, contracts, and grants; and
(B)
creating and maintaining jobs;
(2)
provide counseling and mentoring to minority business enterprises; and
(3)
facilitate the growth of minority business enterprises by promoting trade.
