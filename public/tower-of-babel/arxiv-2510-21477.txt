\{x^{2k_{i_{\beta_{1}}}+1}e_{r+\beta_{1}},\ x^{2k_{i_{\beta_{1}}}}e_{r+\beta_{1}},\ \ldots\ ,\ xe_{r+\beta_{1}},\ e_{r+\beta_{1}}\}

forms a super Jordan block of size
2k_{i_{\beta_{1}}}+2
, which is even.

Therefore, we conclude that

\{J_{1},\ldots,J_{t}\}=\{J_{i_{1}},\ldots,J_{i_{r_{1}}}\}\;\sqcup\;\{J_{j_{1}},\ldots,J_{j_{r_{2}}}\}.

This completes the proof of Lemma
4.7
and, consequently, that of Theorem
4.2
.
