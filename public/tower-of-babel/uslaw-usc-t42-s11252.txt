42 U.S.C. § 11252 — Dissemination
Citation: us:usc:42:11252
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11252
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 118 (Alzheimer’S Disease And Related Dementias Research); Subchapter IV (Research Relating To Services For Individuals With Alzheimer’S Disease And Related Dementias And Their Families); Part 1 (Responsibilities Of National Institute Of Mental Health). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the National Institute of Mental Health shall disseminate the results of research conducted under this part to appropriate professional entities and to the public.
