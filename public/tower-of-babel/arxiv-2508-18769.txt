The monodromy representation of a hypergeometric system in m m variables of rank p m p^{m}
Kaneko Jyoichi, Matsumoto Keiji, Ohara Katsuyoshi, Terasoma Tomohide

Abstract
. We study the monodromy representation of the hypergeometric system ℱ C p , m ​ ( a , B ) \mathcal{F}_{C}^{p,m}(a,B) in m m variables of rank p m p^{m} with parameters a a and B B . This system can be regarded as a multi-variable model of the generalized hypergeometric equation of rank p p . We construct m + 1 m+1 loops which generate the fundamental group of the complement of the singular locus of ℱ C p , m ​ ( a , B ) \mathcal{F}_{C}^{p,m}(a,B) , and we show that they satisfy certain relations as elements of the fundamental group. We produce circuit matrices along these loops with respect to a fundamental system of solutions to ℱ C p , m ​ ( a , B ) \mathcal{F}_{C}^{p,m}(a,B) under certain non-integrality conditions on parameters a a and B B .

Proposition 4.8

The amplified vanishing cycle space
{\bf U}

Proof.

For

E_{j}({\bf v}_{j})M_{0}=E_{j}({\bf v}_{j})+\gamma{\bf v}=E_{j}({\bf v}_{j})+\gamma(E_{1}({\bf v}_{1})+\cdots+E_{p}({\bf v}_{p}))

\gamma\in K

E_{j}({\bf v}_{j})M_{0}\in{\bf U}

∎

We set

We choose a basis

{\bf U}

Since
{\bf U}

We define the trace

It is easy to see that

Proposition 4.9

Proof.

(1)
