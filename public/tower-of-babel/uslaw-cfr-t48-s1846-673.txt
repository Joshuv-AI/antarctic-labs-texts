48 C.F.R. § 1846.673 — 1846.673 Distribution of DD Forms 250 and 250c.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1846.673
Creator: Chapter 18—National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1846.673
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1846 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) DD Forms 250 and 250c shall be distributed in accordance with installation procedures.
(b) The contractor is responsible for distributing DD Forms 250 and 250c in accordance with the provisions of the contract or instructions of the contracting officer.
