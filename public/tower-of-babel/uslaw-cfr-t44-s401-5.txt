44 C.F.R. § 401.5 — Defense against claims for damages.
Citation: us:cfr:44:401.5
Creator: Department Of Commerce And Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-401.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 401 (Shipping Restrictions (T-1)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall be held liable for damages or penalties for any default under any contract or order which shall result directly or indirectly from compliance with this order or any provision thereof, notwithstanding that this order or such provision shall thereafter be declared by judicial or other competent authority to be invalid.
