33 C.F.R. § 6.19-1 — -1 Primary responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:33:6.19-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-6.19-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 6 (Protection And Security Of Vessels, Harbors, And Waterfront Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing contained in this part shall be construed as relieving the masters, owners, operators, and agents of vessels or other waterfront facilities from their primary responsibility for the protection and security of such vessels or waterfront facilities, including any data, information, network, program, system, or other digital infrastructure thereon or therein.
