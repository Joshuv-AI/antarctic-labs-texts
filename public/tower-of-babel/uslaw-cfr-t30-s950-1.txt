30 C.F.R. § 950.1 — Scope.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 950 (Wyoming). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part contains all rules applicable only within the State of Wyoming which have been adopted under the Surface Mining Control and Reclamation Act of 1977.
