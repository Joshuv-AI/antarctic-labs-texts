1 C.F.R. § 21.41 — Agency responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:1:21.41
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-21.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 21 (Preparation Of Documents Subject To Codification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each issuing agency is responsible for the accuracy and integrity of the citations of authority in the documents it issues.
(b) Each issuing agency shall formally amend the citations of authority in its codified material to reflect any changes therein.
