17 C.F.R. § 37.901 — General requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:17:37.901
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-37.901
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 37 (Swap Execution Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

With respect to swaps traded on or through a swap execution facility, each swap execution facility shall:
(a) Report specified swap data as provided under part 43 and part 45 of this chapter; and
(b) Meet the requirements of part 16 of this chapter.
