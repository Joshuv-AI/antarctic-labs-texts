40 C.F.R. § 57.815 — State notification.
Citation: us:cfr:40:57.815
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-57.815
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 57 (Primary Nonferrous Smelter Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator shall give notice of the final decision in writing to the air pollution control agency of the State in which the smelter is located.
