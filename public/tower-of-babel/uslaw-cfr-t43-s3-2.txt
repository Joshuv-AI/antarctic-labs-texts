43 C.F.R. § 3.2 — Limitation on permits granted.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3 (Preservation Of American Antiquities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No permit for the removal of any ancient monument or structure which can be permanently preserved under the control of the United States in situ, and remain an object of interest, shall be granted.
