46 U.S.C. § 57533 — Vessel chartering authority
Citation: us:usc:46:57533
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section57533
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Government-Owned Merchant Vessels); Chapter 575 (Construction, Charter, And Sale Of Vessels); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation may enter into contracts or other agreements on behalf of the United States to purchase, charter, operate, or otherwise acquire the use of any vessels documented under chapter 121 of this title and any other related real or personal property. The Secretary is authorized to use this authority as the Secretary deems appropriate.
