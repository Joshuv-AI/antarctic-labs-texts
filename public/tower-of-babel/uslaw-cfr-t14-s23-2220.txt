14 C.F.R. § 23.2220 — Ground and water load conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:23.2220
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-23.2220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 23 (Airworthiness Standards: Normal Category Airplanes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant must determine the structural design loads resulting from taxi, takeoff, landing, and handling conditions on the applicable surface in normal and adverse attitudes and configurations.
