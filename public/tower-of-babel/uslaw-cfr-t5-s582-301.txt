5 C.F.R. § 582.301 — Suspension of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:5:582.301
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-582.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 582 (Commercial Garnishment Of Federal Employees' Pay). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon proper service of legal process as specified in §§ 582.202 and 582.203, the agency shall suspend, i.e., withhold, payment of such moneys for the amount necessary to permit compliance with the legal process in accordance with this part.
