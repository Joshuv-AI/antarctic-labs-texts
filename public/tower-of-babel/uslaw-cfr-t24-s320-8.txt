24 C.F.R. § 320.8 — Excess Yield Securities.
Citation: us:cfr:24:320.8
Creator: Government National Mortgage Association, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-320.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 320 (Guaranty Of Mortgage-Backed Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Definition. Excess Yield Securities are securities backed by the excess servicing income relating to mortgages underlying previously issued Ginnie Mae mortgage-backed securities.
(b) GNMA guaranty. The Association guarantees the timely payment of interest as provided by the terms of the security.
