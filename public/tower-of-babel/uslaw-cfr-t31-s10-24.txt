31 C.F.R. § 10.24 — Assistance from or to disbarred or suspended persons and former Internal Revenue Service employees.
Citation: us:cfr:31:10.24
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-10.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 10 (Practice Before The Internal Revenue Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A practitioner may not, knowingly and directly or indirectly:
(a) Accept assistance from or assist any person who is under disbarment or suspension from practice before the Internal Revenue Service if the assistance relates to a matter or matters constituting practice before the Internal Revenue Service.
(b) Accept assistance from any former government employee where the provisions of § 10.25 or any Federal law would be violated.
