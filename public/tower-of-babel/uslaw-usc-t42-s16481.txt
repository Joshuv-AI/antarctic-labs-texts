42 U.S.C. § 16481 — Commission defined

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter XII (Electricity); Part F (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter, the term “Commission” means the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission.
