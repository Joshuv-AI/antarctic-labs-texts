49 C.F.R. § 665.25 — Transportation of vehicle.
Citation: us:cfr:49:665.25
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-665.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 665 (Bus Testing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A manufacturer shall be responsible for transporting its vehicle to and from the Bus Testing Facility at the beginning and completion of the testing at the manufacturer's own risk and expense.
