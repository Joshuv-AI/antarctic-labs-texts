16 U.S.C. § 3163 — Effect on other laws
Citation: us:usc:16:3163
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3163
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 51 (Alaska National Interest Lands Conservation); Subchapter IV (Transportation And Utility Systems In And Across, And Access Into, Conservation System Units). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as specifically provided for in this subchapter, applicable law shall apply with respect to the authorization and administration of transportation or utility systems.
