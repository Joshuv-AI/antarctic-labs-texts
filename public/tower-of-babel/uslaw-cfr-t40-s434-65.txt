40 C.F.R. § 434.65 — Modification of NPDES permits for new sources.
Citation: us:cfr:40:434.65
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-434.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 434 (Coal Mining Point Source Category Bpt, Bat, Bct Limitations And New Source Performance Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any coal mine or coal preparation plant which was considered a new source under previous EPA regulations may, notwithstanding § 122.62 of this chapter, apply to have its NPDES permit modified to incorporate the revised new source performance standards.
