46 C.F.R. § 532.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:46:532.1
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-532.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 532 (Nvocc Negotiated Rate Arrangements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part, pursuant to the Commission's statutory authority, is to exempt non-vessel-operating common carriers (NVOCCs) from the tariff rate publication and adherence requirements of the Shipping Act of 1984, as enumerated herein.
