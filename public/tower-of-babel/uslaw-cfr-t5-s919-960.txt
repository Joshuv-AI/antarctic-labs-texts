5 C.F.R. § 919.960 — Ineligible or ineligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:5:919.960
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-919.960
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 919 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Ineligible or ineligibility means that a person or commodity is prohibited from covered transactions because of an exclusion or disqualification.
