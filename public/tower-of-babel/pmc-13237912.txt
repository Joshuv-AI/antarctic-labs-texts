Authors: Wei Wu, Hongzhe Zhao, Shize Hao, Guiwei Li, Xihuai Xue, Jiaxuan Li, Wen Cui, Yanping Jiang, Yingying Ma, Xinyuan Qiao
Published: 2026

Following the publication of the original article [1], the authors reported that an unexpected Chinese text appeared in the published version of this article. However, there is no Chinese part in the uploaded original manuscript, nor in the calibration, which is an oversight in the final proofreading stage.

Location of error: [The Chinese text appeared after the subsection header “Prevalence of S. aureus strains” in page 4, after the sentence “The results showed that S. aureus was more prevalent in mastitis samples than in fecal samples” and after the subsection header “Staphylococcal protein A typing” in page 5, etc.]

The original article has been corrected.
