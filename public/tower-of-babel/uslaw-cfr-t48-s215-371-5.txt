48 C.F.R. § 215.371-5 — 215.371-5 Waiver.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 215 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The head of the contracting activity is authorized to waive the requirement at 215.371-2 to resolicit for an additional period of at least 30 days.
(b) This waiver authority cannot be delegated below one level above the contracting officer.
