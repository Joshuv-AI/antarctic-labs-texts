40 C.F.R. § 127.25 — Time for states, tribes, and territories to revise existing programs.
Citation: us:cfr:40:127.25
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-127.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 127 (Npdes Electronic Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A state, tribe, or territory that has received authorization from EPA to implement the NPDES program must make program revisions in compliance with 40 CFR 123.62(e).
