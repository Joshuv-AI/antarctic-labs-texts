20 U.S.C. § 1131d — Report
Citation: us:usc:20:1131d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1131d
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter VI (International Education Programs); Part C (Institute For International Public Policy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Institute shall prepare a report once every two years on the activities of the Institute and shall submit such report to the Secretary of Education and the Secretary of State.
