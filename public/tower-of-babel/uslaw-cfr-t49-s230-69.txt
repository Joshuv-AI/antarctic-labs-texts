49 C.F.R. § 230.69 — Ash pans.
Citation: us:cfr:49:230.69
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-230.69
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 230 (Steam Locomotive Inspection And Maintenance Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Ash pans shall be securely supported from mud-rings or frames with no part less than 2 1/2 inches above the rail. Their operating mechanism shall be so arranged that they may be safely operated and securely closed.
