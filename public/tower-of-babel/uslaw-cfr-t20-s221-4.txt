20 C.F.R. § 221.4 — When a jurisdiction decision may be reversed.
Citation: us:cfr:20:221.4
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-221.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 221 (Jurisdiction Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may reverse a jurisdiction decision whenever evidence is received by the Board indicating that the original decision was incorrect.
