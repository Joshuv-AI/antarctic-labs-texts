DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Notice of Supplemental Award; Maternal Health Research 
Collaborative for Minority-Serving Institutions Coordinating

AGENCY: Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA), Department 
of Health and Human Services

ACTION: Notice of supplemental award.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: HRSA is announcing a supplemental award to Morgan State 
University (MSU), a current HRSA awardee for the Maternal Health 
Research Collaborative for Minority Serving Institutions (MH-RC-MSI) 
Coordinating Center (CC). HRSA administers this grant using funding 
made available by the Department of Health and Human Services' 
Assistant Secretary for Planning and Evaluation's Patient-Centered 
Outcomes Research Trust Fund. The Assistant Secretary for Planning and 
Evaluation's Patient-Centered Outcomes Research Trust Fund supports 
this multi-agency data linkage project to build further data capacity. 
The recipient of the MH-RC-MSI CC, MSU, does not need to submit special 
documentation to establish eligibility. If this exception is not 
granted, MSU will not have the funds to test the data linkage.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Evva Assing-Murray, Senior Health 
Scientist, Division of Research, Office of Epidemiology and Research, 
Maternal and Child Health Bureau, HRSA, at [email protected] and 
301-594-4113.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 
    Intended Recipient of the Award: Morgan State University.
    Amount of Non-Competitive Award: One award for $400,000.
    Project Period: September 30, 2023, to September 29, 2028.
    Assistance Listing Number: 93.110.
    Award Instrument: Non-competitive single-source supplement.
    Authority: 26 U.S.C. 9511(d)(2)(C).

    Justification: The project, Linking the Boston Birth Cohort and 
Pregnancy to Early Life Longitudinal Data System and Community-Level 
Health Databases: A Longitudinal Cohort of Mother-Child Dyads, will 
create a dataset with a broad array of data on biological, socio-
economic, home and neighborhood environment, behavioral risk and 
protective factors, health services and expenses across the 
developmental and life stages. In addition, this project intends to 
make this novel dataset shareable to other researchers.
    This supplement will support testing the dataset, its user-
friendliness, and the sharing process.
    The MH-RC-MSI CC is uniquely qualified to test and validate the 
data linkage and sharing process through use cases and research studies 
because:
     They have the current infrastructure and connections with 
16 HRSA funded MH-RC-MSI Research Centers to ensure coordinated testing 
and validation;
     They support numerous early-stage investigators through a 
mentorship program who can use the dataset to conduct research projects 
on maternal health; and
     They have a record of successfully managing HRSA's 
Maternal and Child Health Bureau-funded projects that examine a broad 
range of adverse maternal health outcomes since 2023 and have 
consistently met goals and objectives.
    These data linkage activities run from September 2025 to July 2027, 
during years 3 through 4 of the 5-year Minority-Serving Institutions 
project period. Not making the award would preclude testing of the data 
linkage as described in the project proposal.
