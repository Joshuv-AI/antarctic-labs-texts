14 C.F.R. § 291.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:291.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-291.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 291 (Cargo Operations In Interstate Air Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to cargo operations in interstate air transportation by air carriers certificated under section 41102 or 41103 of 49 U.S.C. Subtitle VII. It also applies to applicants for an all-cargo air transportation certificate under section 41103 of 49 U.S.C. Subtitle VII.
