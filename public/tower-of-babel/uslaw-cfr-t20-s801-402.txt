20 C.F.R. § 801.402 — Representation of Board in court proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:20:801.402
Creator: Benefits Review Board, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-801.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 801 (Establishment And Operation Of The Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except in proceedings in the Supreme Court of the United States, any representation of the Benefits Review Board in court proceedings shall be by attorneys designated by the Solicitor of Labor.
