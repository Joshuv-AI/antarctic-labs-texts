25 C.F.R. § 81.63 — May the same petition be used for more than one Secretarial election?
Citation: us:cfr:25:81.63
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-81.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 81 (Secretarial Election Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. A petition may not be used for more than one Secretarial election. Each request for a Secretarial election requires a new petition.
