49 C.F.R. § 386.49 — Form of written evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:49:386.49
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-386.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 386 (Rules Of Practice For Fmcsa Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All written evidence should be submitted in the following forms:
(a) A written statement of a person having personal knowledge of the facts alleged, or
(b) Documentary evidence in the form of exhibits attached to a written statement identifying the exhibit and giving its source.
