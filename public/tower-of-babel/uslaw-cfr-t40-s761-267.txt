40 C.F.R. § 761.267 — Sampling non-porous surfaces.
Citation: us:cfr:40:761.267
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-761.267
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 761 (POLYCHLORINATED BIPHENYLS (PCBs) MANUFACTURING, PROCESSING, DISTRIBUTION IN COMMERCE, AND USE PROHIBITIONS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Sample large, nearly flat, non-porous surfaces by dividing the surface into roughly square portions approximately 2 meters on each side. Follow the procedures in § 761.302(a) with the exception of the sampling grid size.
(b) It is not necessary to sample small or irregularly shaped surfaces.
