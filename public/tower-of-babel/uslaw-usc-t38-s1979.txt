38 U.S.C. § 1979 — Incontestability
Citation: us:usc:38:1979
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1979
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 19 (Insurance); Subchapter III (Servicemembers’ Group Life Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to the provision of section 1973 of this title, insurance coverage granted under this subchapter shall be incontestable from the date of issue, reinstatement, or conversion except for fraud or nonpayment of premium.
