29 C.F.R. § 1918.82 — Building drafts.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1918.82
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1918.82
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1918 (Safety And Health Regulations For Longshoring). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Drafts shall be built or means shall be taken to prevent cargo from falling from them.
(b) Buckets and tubs used in handling bulk or frozen cargo shall not be loaded above their rims.
