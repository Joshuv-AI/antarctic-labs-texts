48 C.F.R. § 1449.106 — 1449.106 Fraud or other criminal conduct.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1449.106
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1449.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1449 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When fraud or other criminal conduct is suspected, the CO will submit a report documenting the incident to the BPC for transmittal to the OIG. Informational copies will be forwarded to the HCA and the Director, PAM.
