47 C.F.R. § 2.404 — Resumption of operation after distress.
Citation: us:cfr:47:2.404
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-2.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 2 (Frequency Allocations And Radio Treaty Matters; General Rules And Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No station having been notified to cease operation shall resume operation on frequency or frequencies which may cause interference until notified by the station issuing the original notice that the station involved will not interfere with distress traffic as it is then being routed or until the receipt of a general notice that the need for handling distress traffic no longer exists.
