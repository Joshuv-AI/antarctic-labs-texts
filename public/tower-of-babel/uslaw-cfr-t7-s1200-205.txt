7 C.F.R. § 1200.205 — Termination of proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1200.205
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1200.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1200 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure Governing Proceedings Under Research, Promotion, And Information Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If at any time during development of a new program the Administrator concludes, based on public comments, referendum votes, or other available information, that an order will not tend to effectuate the declared policy of the Act, the Administrator shall terminate the proceedings and collect reimbursements from the bond or other collateral posted pursuant to § 1200.204 for any expenses incurred in development of the proposed program.
