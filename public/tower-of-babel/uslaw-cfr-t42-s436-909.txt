42 C.F.R. § 436.909 — Automatic entitlement to Medicaid following a determination of eligibility under other programs.
Citation: us:cfr:42:436.909
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-436.909
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 436 (Eligibility In Guam, Puerto Rico, And The Virgin Islands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency may not require a separate application for Medicaid from an individual if the individual receives cash assistance under a State plan for OAA, AFDC, AB, APTD, or AABD.
