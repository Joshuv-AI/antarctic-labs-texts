42 C.F.R. § 61.36 — Selection and appointment of service fellows.
Citation: us:cfr:42:61.36
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-61.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 61 (Fellowships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary shall:
(a) Prescribe a suitable professional and personal fitness review and an examination of the applicant's qualifications;
(b) Designate in writing persons to receive service fellowships; and
(c) Establish procedures for the appointment of service fellows.
