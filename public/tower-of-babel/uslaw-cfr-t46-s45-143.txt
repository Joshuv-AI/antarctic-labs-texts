46 C.F.R. § 45.143 — Hull openings above freeboard deck.
Citation: us:cfr:46:45.143
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-45.143
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 45 (Great Lakes Load Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Closures for openings above the freeboard deck must be as strong as the structure to which they are attached and must be weathertight.
