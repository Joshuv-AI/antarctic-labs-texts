48 C.F.R. § 538.201 — 538.201 Coordination requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:48:538.201
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-538.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 538 (Federal Supply Schedule Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

GSA will coordinate with other agencies who administer FSS contracts specific to their delegated authority (i.e., the Department of Veterans Affairs). Coordination will ensure adherence to policies and procedures at the program level, such as providing guidance on approved exceptions (see 538.001).
