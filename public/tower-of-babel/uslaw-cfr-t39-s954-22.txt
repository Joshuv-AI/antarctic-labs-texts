39 C.F.R. § 954.22 — Continuances.
Citation: us:cfr:39:954.22
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-954.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 954 (Denial, Suspension, Or Revocaton Of Periodicals Mail Privileges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For good cause shown, continuances or extensions may be granted by the presiding officer. Similar action may be taken by the Judicial Officer when the proceeding is on appeal.
