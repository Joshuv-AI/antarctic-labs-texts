40 C.F.R. § 52.1478 — Extensions.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 52 (Approval And Promulgation Of Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator, by the authority delegated under section 186(a)(4) of the Clean Air Act as amended in 1990, hereby extends for one year, until December 31, 1996, the attainment date for the Clark County (Las Vegas Valley), Nevada carbon monoxide nonattainment area.
