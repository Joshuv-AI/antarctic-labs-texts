42 U.S.C. § 5163 — Limitation on use of sliding scales
Citation: us:usc:42:5163
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5163
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 68 (Disaster Relief); Subchapter III (Major Disaster And Emergency Assistance Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No geographic area shall be precluded from receiving assistance under this chapter solely by virtue of an arithmetic formula or sliding scale based on income or population.
