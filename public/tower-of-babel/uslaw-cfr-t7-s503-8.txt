7 C.F.R. § 503.8 — Intoxicating beverages and narcotics.
Citation: us:cfr:7:503.8
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-503.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 503 (Conduct On Plum Island Animal Disease Center). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Entering the PIADC or operating a motor vehicle thereon by a person under the influence of intoxicating beverages or narcotic drugs, or the consumption of such beverages or the use of such drugs in or on the PIADC, is prohibited.
