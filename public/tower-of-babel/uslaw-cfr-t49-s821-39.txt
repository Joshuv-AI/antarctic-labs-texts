49 C.F.R. § 821.39 — Argument and submissions.
Citation: us:cfr:49:821.39
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-821.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 821 (Rules Of Practice In Air Safety Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At the hearing, the law judge shall give the parties adequate opportunity for the presentation of arguments in support of, or in opposition to, motions, objections and proposed rulings. Prior to the issuance of the initial decision, the parties shall be afforded a reasonable opportunity to submit for consideration proposed findings and conclusions, and supporting reasons therefor.
