5 U.S.C. § 8150 — Effect on other statutes
Citation: us:usc:5:8150
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section8150
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart G (Insurance And Annuities); Chapter 81 (Compensation For Work Injuries); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
This subchapter does not affect the maritime rights and remedies of a master or member of the crew of a vessel.
(b)
Section 8141 of this title and section 9491 of title 10 do not confer military or veteran status on any individual.
