2 C.F.R. § 423.1 — What does this part do?
Citation: us:cfr:2:423.1
Creator: Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-423.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 423 (Geospatial Data Management And Standards For Federal Financial Assistance Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements the requirements at 43 U.S.C. 2808(b)(3) which pertain to the disclosure of USDA Federal financial assistance that involves geospatial data, as supplemented by this part.
