16 C.F.R. § 1611.34 — Only uncovered or exposed parts of wearing apparel to be tested.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1611.34
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1611.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1611 (Standard For The Flammability Of Vinyl Plastic Film). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In determining whether an article of wearing apparel is so highly flammable as to be dangerous when worn by individuals, only the uncovered or exposed part of such article of wearing apparel shall be tested according to the applicable procedures set forth in section 4(a) of the act.
