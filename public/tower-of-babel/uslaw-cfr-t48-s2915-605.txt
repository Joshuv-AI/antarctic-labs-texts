48 C.F.R. § 2915.605 — 2915.605 Content of unsolicited proposals.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2915.605
Creator: Chapter 29—Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2915.605
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2915 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the contents required by FAR 15.605, unsolicited proposals for research should contain a commitment by the offeror to include cost-sharing or should represent a significant cost savings to DOL.
