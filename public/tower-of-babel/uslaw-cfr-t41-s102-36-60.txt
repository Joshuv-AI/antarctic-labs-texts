41 C.F.R. § 102-36.60 — -36.60 Excess personal property removal.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-36.60
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-36.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-36 (36—Disposition Of Excess Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Normally, you have 10 days from the date the transfer order is completely approved to pick up the excess personal property for transfer. You are responsible for scheduling and coordinating the property removal with the holding agency and requesting additional time, if needed.
