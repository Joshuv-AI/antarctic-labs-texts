43 C.F.R. § 3162.2-13 — -13 If I acquire an interest in a lease that is being drained, will the Department assess me for compensatory royalty?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3162.2-13
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3162.2-13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3160 (Onshore Oil And Gas Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you acquire an interest in a Federal or Indian lease through an assignment of record title or transfer of operating rights under this part, you are liable for all drainage obligations accruing on and after the date we approve the assignment or transfer.
