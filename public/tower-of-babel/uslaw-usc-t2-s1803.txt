2 U.S.C. § 1803 — Delegation of authority
Citation: us:usc:2:1803
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1803
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 28 (Architect Of The Capitol); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Architect of the Capitol may delegate the duties and authorities of the Architect to officers and employees of the Office of the Architect of the Capitol, as the Architect determines appropriate.
