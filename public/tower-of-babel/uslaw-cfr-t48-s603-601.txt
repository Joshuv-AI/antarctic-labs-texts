48 C.F.R. § 603.601 — 603.601 Policy.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 603 (Improper Business Practices And Personal Conflicts Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) It is Department policy not to award contracts to Federal employees, or businesses substantially owned or controlled by Federal employees. This policy also applies to individuals hired under personal services agreements and personal services contracts.
