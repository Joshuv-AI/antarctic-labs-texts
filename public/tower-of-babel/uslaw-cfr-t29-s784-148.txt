29 C.F.R. § 784.148 — General scope of processing, freezing, and curing activities.
Citation: us:cfr:29:784.148
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-784.148
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 784 (Provisions Of The Fair Labor Standards Act Applicable To Fishing And Operations On Aquatic Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Processing, freezing, and curing embrace a variety of operations that change the form of the “aquatic forms of animal and vegetable life.” They include such operations as filleting, cutting, scaling, salting, smoking, drying, pickling, curing, freezing, extracting oil, manufacturing meal or fertilizer, drying seaweed preparatory to the manufacture of agar, drying and cleaning sponges (Feming v. Hawkeye Pearl Button Co., 113 F. 2d 52).
