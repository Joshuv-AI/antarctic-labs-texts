2 U.S.C. § 4722 — Mandatory Senate ethics training for Members and staff
Citation: us:usc:2:4722
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4722
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 47 (Congressional Ethics); Subchapter III (Senate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Training program
The Select Committee on Ethics shall conduct ongoing ethics training and awareness programs for Members of the Senate and Senate staff.
(b) Requirements
The ethics training program conducted by the Select Committee on Ethics shall be completed by—
(1)
new Senators or staff not later than 60 days after commencing service or employment; and
(2)
Senators and Senate staff serving or employed on September 14, 2007, not later than 165 days after September 14, 2007.
