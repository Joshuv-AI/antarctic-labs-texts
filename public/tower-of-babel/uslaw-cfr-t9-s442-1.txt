9 C.F.R. § 442.1 — Quantity of contents labeling.
Citation: us:cfr:9:442.1
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-442.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 442 (Quantity Of Contents Labeling And Procedures And Requirements For Accurate Weights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes the procedures to be followed for determining net weight compliance and prescribes the reasonable variations allowed from the declared net weight on the labels of immediate containers of products in accordance with 9 CFR 317.2(c)(4), 317.2(h), and 381.121.
