25 C.F.R. § 152.6 — Issuance of patents in fee to non-Indians and Indians with whom a special relationship does not exist.
Citation: us:cfr:25:152.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-152.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 152 (Issuance Of Patents In Fee, Certificates Of Competency, Removal Of Restrictions, And Sale Of Certain Indian Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever the Secretary determines that trust land, or any interest therein, has been acquired through inheritance or devise by a non-Indian, or by a person of Indian descent to whom the United States owes no trust responsibility, the Secretary may issue a patent in fee for the land or interest therein to such person without application.
