15 U.S.C. § 384 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:15:384
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section384
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 10B (State Taxation Of Income From Interstate Commerce); Subchapter I (Net Income Taxes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application of such provision to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of this chapter or the application of such provision to persons or circumstances other than those to which it is held invalid, shall not be affected thereby.
