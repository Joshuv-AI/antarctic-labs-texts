Abstract
. This paper introduces and explores functions defined on H ∗ \displaystyle H^{*} -normal spaces through the framework of H ∗ \displaystyle H^{*} -open sets. We extend the concept of H ∗ \displaystyle H^{*} -normality and investigate its connections with g \displaystyle g -normal and classical normal spaces. A major focus of ongoing research has been the characterization of normality via combinations of weaker separation and covering properties. \displaystyle H^{*}

\displaystyle H^{*}
