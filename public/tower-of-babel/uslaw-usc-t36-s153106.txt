36 U.S.C. § 153106 — Deposit of historical material in Smithsonian Institution
Citation: us:usc:36:153106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section153106
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1531 (The National Society Of The Daughters Of The American Revolution). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Regents of the Smithsonian Institution may permit the corporation to deposit its collections, manuscripts, books, pamphlets, and other material for history in the Smithsonian Institution or in the National Museum, on conditions and under rules they prescribe.
