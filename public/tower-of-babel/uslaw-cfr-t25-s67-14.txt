25 C.F.R. § 67.14 — Preparation of a per capita payment roll.
Citation: us:cfr:25:67.14
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-67.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 67 (Preparation Of A Roll Of Independent Seminole Indians Of Florida). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Superintendent shall, based on the roll approved under § 67.12(c), prepare a per capita payment roll. The payment roll shall be comprised of those persons whose names appear on the approved roll and who have elected to share in the per capita payment.
(b) The per capita payment roll shall contain for each person a roll number or identification number, name, and address.
(c) The Area Director shall authorize the distribution of the judgment funds to those persons named on the per capita payment roll.
