32 C.F.R. § 1630.11 — Class 1-A-0: Conscientious objector available for noncombatant military service only.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1630.11
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1630.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1630 (Classification Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accord with part 1636 of this chapter any registrant shall be palced in Class 1-A-0 who has been found, by reason of religious, ethical, or moral belief, to be conscientiously opposed to participation in combatant military tranining and service in the Armed Forces.
