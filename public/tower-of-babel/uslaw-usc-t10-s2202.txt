10 U.S.C. § 2202 — Regulations on production, warehousing, and supply distribution functions
Citation: us:usc:10:2202
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2202
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 131 (Planning And Coordination). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense shall prescribe regulations governing the performance within the Department of Defense of the production, warehousing, and supply distribution functions, and related functions, of the Department of Defense.
