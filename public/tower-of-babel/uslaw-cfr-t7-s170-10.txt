7 C.F.R. § 170.10 — Must a participant in the market have insurance?
Citation: us:cfr:7:170.10
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-170.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 170 (Usda Farmers Market). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There is no requirement for a participant to have insurance; however, USDA asks that participants with insurance provide insurance information for our records.
