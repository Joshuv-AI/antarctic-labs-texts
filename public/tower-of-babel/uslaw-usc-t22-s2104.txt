22 U.S.C. § 2104 — Authority of Federal officers and agencies unaffected
Citation: us:usc:22:2104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2104
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 30 (International Cooperation In Health And Medical Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to repeal or restrict authority vested in the President, the Secretary of State, the Secretary of Health, Education, and Welfare, the Surgeon General of the Public Health Service, or any other officer or agency of the United States by any other provision of law.
