40 U.S.C. § 15731 — Southeast Crescent Regional Commission
Citation: us:usc:40:15731
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section15731
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 157 (Administrative Provisions); Subchapter II (Designation Of Regions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The region of the Southeast Crescent Regional Commission shall consist of all counties of the States of Virginia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Georgia, Alabama, Mississippi, and Florida not already served by the Appalachian Regional Commission or the Delta Regional Authority.
