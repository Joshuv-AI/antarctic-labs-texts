39 C.F.R. § 959.29 — Official record.
Citation: us:cfr:39:959.29
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-959.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 959 (Private Express Statutes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The transcript of testimony together with all pleadings, orders, exhibits, briefs and other documents filed in the proceedings shall constitute the official record of the proceeding.
