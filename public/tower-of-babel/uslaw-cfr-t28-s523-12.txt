28 C.F.R. § 523.12 — Work/study release good time.
Citation: us:cfr:28:523.12
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-523.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 523 (Computation Of Sentence). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Extra good time for an inmate in work or study release programs is awarded automatically, beginning on the date the inmate is assigned to the program and continuing without further approval as long as the inmate is participating in the program, unless the award is disallowed.
