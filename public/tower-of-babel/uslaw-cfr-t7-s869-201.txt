7 C.F.R. § 869.201 — Agricultural product certificates; format.
Citation: us:cfr:7:869.201
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Federal Grain Inspection Service, Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-869.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 869 (Regulations For The United States Warehouse Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each inspection, grade, class, weight or combination certificate issued under the Act by a licensee to perform such services must be:
(a) In a format prescribed by AMS;
(b) Issued and maintained in a consecutive order; and
(c) As prescribed in the applicable licensing or provider agreement and authorized by AMS.
