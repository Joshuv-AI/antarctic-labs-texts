22 C.F.R. § 133.610 — Controlled substance.
Citation: us:cfr:22:133.610
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-133.610
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 133 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Controlled substance means a controlled substance in schedules I through V of the Controlled Substances Act (21 U.S.C. 812), and as further defined by regulation at 21 CFR 1308.11 through 1308.15.
