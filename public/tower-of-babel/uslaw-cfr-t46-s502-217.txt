46 C.F.R. § 502.217 — Record of decision.
Citation: us:cfr:46:502.217
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-502.217
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 502 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The transcript of testimony and exhibits, together with all filings and motions filed in the proceeding, will constitute the exclusive record for decision. [Rule 217.]
