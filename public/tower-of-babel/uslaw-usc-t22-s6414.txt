22 U.S.C. § 6414 — High-level contacts with nongovernmental organizations
Citation: us:usc:22:6414
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6414
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 73 (International Religious Freedom); Subchapter I (Department Of State Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

United States chiefs of mission shall seek out and contact religious nongovernmental organizations to provide high-level meetings with religious nongovernmental organizations where appropriate and beneficial. United States chiefs of mission and Foreign Service officers abroad shall seek to meet with imprisoned religious leaders where appropriate and beneficial.
