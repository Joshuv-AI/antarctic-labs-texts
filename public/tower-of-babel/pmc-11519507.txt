The edge artifact extraction method for Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling element microcracks characterization
Authors: Tao Chen, Xin Xia, Jianbin Zhou, Changfu Fang, Jianbo Le, Nanxing Wu
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Aiming at the problem that the edge artifacts of Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling element microcracks have low contrast, contain noise, and easily merge with the background, making it difficult to segment. A method based on 2D discrete wavelet transform and Otsu threshold segmentation is designed to achieve the extraction of microcrack edge artifact features. Wavelet decomposition is used to remove noise, while wavelet reconstruction features are used to restore lost details. Creation of 2D discrete wavelet transform functional equations combining wavelet reconstruction and wavelet decomposition to improve contrast and eliminate noise in images featuring edge artifacts. For the problem of feature edge artifacts that are difficult to remove, the threshold segmentation function equation is designed to maximize the interclass variance, and the optimal threshold value is selected to remove the edge artifacts. The experimental results show that the average PSNR of the Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling body point, line, and surface microcrack edge artifact feature images enhanced by the method in this paper is close to 62.69 dB, and the average SSIM is about 0.77. The method in this paper improves the contrast of microcrack edge artifact features of Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling bodies and makes the feature extraction effect of point, line, and surface microcrack edge artifacts more complete.

Introduction

Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling element has excellent heat resistance, corrosion resistance high hardness properties, and so on1,2. As a new type of ceramic bearing rolling element, it is widely used in aerospace, chemical machinery, high-speed machine tools, and other fields requiring high strength and precision3. However, Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling elements have a low feature coefficient and a complicated manufacturing process. Various features and damage are prone to occur during the production process4. These flaws will impair the operation of the equipment, resulting in shorter fatigue life, and decrease the performance of the Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling element5. Commonly used feature detection methods include ultrasonic non-destructive testing, radiographic non-destructive testing, eddy current non-destructive testing, etc6–8. Precise, qualitative, and quantitative detection of roller features in Si3N4 bearings is difficult due to the shape of the features. Non-destructive testing methods based on computer vision can effectively identify image features and improve the accuracy and efficiency of detection9–11. At the same time, computer vision nondestructive testing has excellent image detection efficiency and is capable of qualitative and quantitative detection.

Edge artifacts in the rolling elements of Si3N4 ceramic bearings are not well characterized, and there is noise in the background region, which makes the details and structure of the edge feature region unclear. The use of non-destructive visual inspection methods for images is limited due to the difficulty of extracting features using traditional methods. The image complexity problem of dermoscopic images due to artifacts was studied by Ramya et al.12. Algorithms such as discrete wavelet transform-assisted histogram thresholding are proposed to overcome the complexity of the image and the difficulty of segmentation and to eliminate artifacts effectively. The issue of how Gaussian noise degrades image quality and impacts post-processing is the focus of Wang et al.13. The goal of reducing image noise and enhancing image clarity is accomplished by the wavelet denoising technique with a shrinkage factor. The effect of complicated image backgrounds on acquisition tasks was combined by Yang et al.14. Adopt the Otsu threshold segmentation method with high efficiency, fast speed, and easy implementation on hardware. Apply threshold segmentation to leaf images to segment the image’s background. HIS are vulnerable to different types of noise pollution, which is an issue that Cao et al.15 Attempted to address. An enhanced E 3DDWT method is proposed to achieve the effects of reducing noise and extracting features. The aforementioned researchers enhanced the quality of their images by removing noise and artifacts using wavelet transform. There is some theoretical guidance involved in using threshold segmentation to carry out background segmentation on photos to extract features. However, the microcrack edge artifact characteristics of Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling element are studied. The image can only be slightly improved by employing wavelet transform alone to identify edge artifact features, and achieving the desired edge artifact feature extraction effect is challenging.

To enhance feature extraction rigor and accuracy of surface, line, and point microcrack edge artifacts of Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling element. Build an image acquisition platform for Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling element microcracks. Acquire an image database of Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling element microcrack edge artifact features. Otsu threshold segmentation feature extraction method and two-dimensional discrete wavelet transform for images in the image database. By the properties of wavelet reconstruction to restore image details and wavelet decomposition in the two-dimensional discrete wavelet transform to eliminate noise. Create a discrete wavelet transform function equation in two dimensions to enhance the contrast of images featuring roller edge artifacts in Si3N4 bearings. Aiming at the problem that artifacts are difficult to remove at the edge of features, a threshold segmentation function equation with the maximum inter-class variance as the core idea is designed. Finish choosing the ideal threshold to accomplish the goal of eliminating edge artifacts. This technique aims to address the issues that make edge artifact removal of Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling elements challenging, such as low contrast, unclear details, and background noise. It offers some theoretical guidance value for enhancing the recognition and accuracy of edge artifact removal.

Wavelet decomposition

When analyzing images of Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling element, it has been discovered that the image contains more or less partial noise for a variety of reasons. The presence of these noises makes it extremely difficult to extract edges from images. A wavelet is used to decompose the image in order to solve these issues. Decompose the signal into sub-signals of different frequencies. Because the noise is usually distributed in the high-frequency part. Wavelet decomposition eliminates high-frequency noise components, which effectively reduces the impact of noise on feature images.(2)Wavelet reconstruction

Result analysis and discussion

Two-dimensional discrete wavelet transform feature enhancement analysis

To verify the experiment’s dependability and compare the results before and after the Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling element feature, the image was enhanced. PSNR, MSE, SSIM are used as evaluation indices in the analysis of enhanced Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling element feature images16. The PSNR is used to compare the target signal strength with the background noise strength. There is less image distortion when the PSNR is higher. The MSE is mainly used to measure the similarity of two images. The gap between the original and enhanced images is smaller when the MSE is smaller, which suggests that the processed images have higher quality. SSIM is a measure of visual similarity between two images. The image similarity was measured by brightness, contrast, and structure. The better the image quality, the smaller the difference between the original and output images, and the larger the SSIM value.

Conclusion

A proposed technique utilizes two-dimensional discrete wavelet transform and Otsu threshold segmentation to extract edge artifact features from Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling element microcracks. Aiming at the problem that the edge artifacts of Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling element microcracks have low contrast and the background contains noise. Easily merges with the background and makes it difficult to remove. Combined with wavelet decomposition. Decompose the signal into sub-signals of different frequencies to remove noise. Wavelet reconstruction makes the signal accurate and complete by restoring lost details. Construct a two-dimensional discrete wavelet transform containing wavelet decomposition and wavelet reconstruction. Achieve image contrast improvement and noise removal of edge artifact features. Solve the threshold segmentation function expression with the core idea of maximizing the inter-class variance through design. Complete Select the optimal threshold to effectively isolate edge artifact characteristics from the backdrop to eliminate artifacts. The average PSNR of the point microcrack, line microcrack, and surface microcrack images enhanced by a two-dimensional discrete wavelet transform is close to 71 dB, 55 dB, and 63 dB, respectively. The SSIM is about 0.98, 0.42, and 0.93, respectively. Increases the contrast of Si3N4 ceramic bearing rolling element microcrack edge artifact features in an efficient manner. Avoid being mistaken for background and ignored.

A mode of image enhancement that creates wavelet decomposition and reconstruction using adaptive filtering, two-dimensional discrete wavelet transforms, and other algorithms. Favorable to enhancing image quality in domains like medical image processing and computer vision. To create better visual effects, it can also be applied to super-resolution networks that are already in place.
