5 C.F.R. § 511.201 — Coverage of and exclusions from the General Schedule.
Citation: us:cfr:5:511.201
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-511.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 511 (Classification Under The General Schedule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part and chapter 51 of the title 5, United States Code, apply to all positions in the agencies except those specifically excluded by section 5102 of title 5, United States Code. (5 U.S.C. 5102)
