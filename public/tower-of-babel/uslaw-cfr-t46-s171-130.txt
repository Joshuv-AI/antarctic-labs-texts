46 C.F.R. § 171.130 — Specific applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:171.130
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-171.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 171 (Special Rules Pertaining To Vessels Carrying Passengers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Section 171.135 applies to each vessel that is 100 gross tons or more.
(b) Sections 171.140, 171.145, 171.150, and 171.155 apply to each vessel under 100 gross tons.
