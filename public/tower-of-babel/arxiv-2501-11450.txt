Tiling H H in dense graphs
Nannan Chen, Xizhi Liu, Lin Sun, Guanghui Wang

Abstract
We determine asymptotically the two extremal constructions for the tiling problem of the H H -shaped tree. In particular, the first extremal construction is close to the complement of two cliques, in contrast to previously studied bipartite graphs, where the first extremal construction is close to the complement of a single clique. This result refutes one of Lang’s conjectures, which seeks to generalize the Erdős Matching Conjecture. Keywords: tiling problem, average degree, trees, Erdős Matching Conjecture

Concluding remarks

A straightforward modification of the proof of Theorem
1.3
yields the following theorem, which provide infinitely many counterexamples to Conjecture
1.1
.

Theorem 6.1
.

Let
t\geq 1
be an integer and
\beta\in\left[0,\frac{1}{6t}\right]
be a real number.
Then

\displaystyle\mathrm{ex}(n,\beta n\cdot H[t])\leq\left(\max\left\{3t\beta(1-3t\beta),~18t^{2}\beta^{2}\right\}+o(1)\right)n^{2}.

For an
r
-graph
F
and an integer
i\in[r]
, the
i
-covering number

\tau_{i}(F)
of
F
is defined as

\displaystyle\tau_{i}(F)\coloneqq\min\left\{|S|\colon S\subseteq V(F)\ \text{and}\ |S\cap e|\geq i\ \text{for all}\ e\in F\right\}.

Note that
\tau_{1}(F)
is simply the covering number of
F
, and
\tau_{r}(F)=v(F)
.

We call an
r
-graph
F

rigid
if there exist integers
1\leq s_{1}\leq\cdots\leq s_{r}
satisfying
v(F)=s_{1}+\cdots+s_{r}
such that
F\subseteq K_{s_{1},\ldots,s_{r}}^{r}
and
\tau_{i}(F)=s_{1}+\cdots+s_{i}
for every
i\in[r-1]
.
In particular,
K_{s_{1},\ldots,s_{r}}^{r}
is rigid for all integers
1\leq s_{1}\leq\cdots\leq s_{r}
.
Observe that
K_{s_{1},\ldots,s_{r}}^{r}
in (
1
) can be replaced by any rigid spanning subgraph of
K_{s_{1},\ldots,s_{r}}^{r}
.
Thus, we propose the following revised version of Conjecture
1.1
.

Conjecture 6.2
.

Let
r\geq 3
and
1\leq s_{1}\leq\cdots\leq s_{r}
be integers. Let
m\coloneqq s_{1}+\cdots+s_{r}
. Suppose that
F
is a rigid spanning subgraph of
K_{s_{1},\ldots,s_{r}}^{r}
. Then for every real number
\beta\in\left(0,\frac{1}{m}\right)
,

\displaystyle\mathrm{ex}(n,\beta n\cdot F)=\max\left\{|G_{n,i,\beta}(s_{1},\ldots,s_{r})|\colon i\in[r]\right\}+o(n^{r}).

Remark.

Note that the case
r=2
follows from Theorem
1.2
.

The asymptotic behavior of
\mathrm{ex}(n,\beta n\cdot F)
remains unclear to us when
F
is nonrigid, as there are numerous parameters of
F
that could be leveraged to construct lower bounds for
\mathrm{ex}(n,\beta n\cdot F)
(see e.g. the concluding remarks in
[
HHL+23b
,
GLMP24
]
for more details).
Thus we propose the following question concerning nonrigid
r
-partite
r
-graphs, which appears to be plausible.

Problem 6.3
.

Let
r\geq 2
and
1\leq s_{1}\leq\cdots\leq s_{r}
be integers. Let
m\coloneqq s_{1}+\cdots+s_{r}
. Suppose that
F
is a nonrigid spanning subgraph of
K_{s_{1},\ldots,s_{r}}^{r}
.

\beta\in\left(0,\frac{1}{m}\right)
and

\displaystyle\mathrm{ex}(n,\beta n\cdot F)\leq\max\left\{|G_{n,i,\beta}(s_{1},\ldots,s_{r})|\colon i\in[r]\right\}-\varepsilon n^{r}.
