20 U.S.C. § 1226a–1 — Payments; installments, advances or reimbursement, and adjustments
Citation: us:usc:20:1226a–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1226a–1
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 31 (General Provisions Concerning Education); Subchapter II (Appropriations And Evaluations); Part 1 (Appropriations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Payments pursuant to grants or contracts under any applicable program may be made in installments, and in advance or by way of reimbursement, with necessary adjustments on account of overpayments or underpayments, as the Secretary may determine.
