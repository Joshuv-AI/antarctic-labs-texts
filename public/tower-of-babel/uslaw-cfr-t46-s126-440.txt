46 C.F.R. § 126.440 — Lifesaving equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:46:126.440
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-126.440
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 126 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At each inspection for certification, the tests and inspections specified by § 91.25-15 of this chapter must occur in the presence of a marine inspector, or as otherwise directed by the cognizant OCMI.
