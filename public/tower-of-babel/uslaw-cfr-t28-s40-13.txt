28 C.F.R. § 40.13 — Review by the Attorney General.
Citation: us:cfr:28:40.13
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-40.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 40 (Standards For Inmate Grievance Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Attorney General shall review and respond to each application as promptly as the circumstances, including the need for independent investigation and consideration of the comments of agencies, and interested groups and persons, permit.
