36 U.S.C. § 153103 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:153103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section153103
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1531 (The National Society Of The Daughters Of The American Revolution). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
adopt a constitution and bylaws;
(2)
adopt a seal; and
(3)
acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property as necessary or convenient to carry out its purposes.
