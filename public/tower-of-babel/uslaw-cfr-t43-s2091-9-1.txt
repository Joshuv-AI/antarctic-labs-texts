43 C.F.R. § 2091.9-1 — -1 Alaska Native selections.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2091.9-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2091.9-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2090 (Special Laws And Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The segregation and opening of lands authorized for selection and selected by Alaska Natives under the Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act, as amended (43 U.S.C. 1601 et seq.), are covered by part 2650 of this title.
