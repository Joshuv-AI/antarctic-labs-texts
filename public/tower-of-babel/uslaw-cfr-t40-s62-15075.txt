40 C.F.R. § 62.15075 — How do I comply with the increment of progress for initiating onsite construction?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.15075
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.15075
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must initiate onsite construction and installation of emission control equipment and initiate the process changes outlined in the final control plan.
