45 C.F.R. § 2520.45 — How much time may an AmeriCorps member spend fundraising?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2520.45
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2520.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2520 (General Provisions: Americorps Subtitle C Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An AmeriCorps member may spend no more than ten percent of his or her originally agreed-upon term of service, as reflected in the member enrollment in the National Service Trust, performing fundraising activities, as described in § 2520.40.
