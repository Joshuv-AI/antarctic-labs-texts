49 C.F.R. § 1550.1 — Applicability of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1550.1
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1550.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1550 (Aircraft Security Under General Operating And Flight Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to the operation of aircraft for which there are no security requirements in other parts of this subchapter.
