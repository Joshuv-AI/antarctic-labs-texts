45 C.F.R. § 180.40 — General requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:45:180.40
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-180.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 180 (Hospital Price Transparency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A hospital must make public the following:
(a) A machine-readable file containing a list of all standard charges for all items and services as provided in § 180.50.
(b) A consumer-friendly list of standard charges for a limited set of shoppable services as provided in § 180.60.
