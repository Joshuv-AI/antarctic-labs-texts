19 C.F.R. § 191.43 — Unused merchandise claim.
Citation: us:cfr:19:191.43
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-191.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 191 (Drawback). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Rejected merchandise may be the subject of an unused merchandise drawback claim under 19 U.S.C. 1313(j)(1), in accordance with subpart C of this part, to the extent that the merchandise qualifies therefor.
