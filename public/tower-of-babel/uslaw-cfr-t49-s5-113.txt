49 C.F.R. § 5.113 — Small Business Regulatory Enforcement Fairness Act (SBREFA).
Citation: us:cfr:49:5.113
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-5.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 5 (Administrative Rulemaking, Guidance, And Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department shall comply with the terms of SBREFA when conducting administrative inspections and adjudications, including section 223 of SBREFA (reduction or waivers of civil penalties, where appropriate). The Department will also cooperate with the Small Business Administration (SBA) when a small business files a comment or complaint related to DOT's inspection authority and when requested to answer SBREFA compliance requests.
