12 C.F.R. § 263.21 — Failure to appear.
Citation: us:cfr:12:263.21
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-263.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 263 (Rules Of Practice For Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Failure of a respondent to appear in person at the hearing or by a duly authorized counsel constitutes a waiver of respondent's right to a hearing and is deemed an admission of the facts as alleged and consent to the relief sought in the notice. Without further proceedings or notice to the respondent, the ALJ will file with the Board a recommended decision containing the findings and the relief sought in the notice.
