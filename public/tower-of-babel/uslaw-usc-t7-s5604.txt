7 U.S.C. § 5604 — Preservation of traditional markets
Citation: us:usc:7:5604
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section5604
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 87 (Export Promotion); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall, in implementing programs of the Department of Agriculture intended to encourage or assist exports of agricultural commodities, seek to preserve traditional markets for United States agricultural commodities.
