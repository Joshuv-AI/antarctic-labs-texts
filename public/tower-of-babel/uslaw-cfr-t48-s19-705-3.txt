48 C.F.R. § 19.705-3 — 19.705-3 Preparing the solicitation.
Citation: us:cfr:48:19.705-3
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-19.705-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 19 (Small Business Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall provide the Small Business Administration's (SBA's) procurement center representative (or, if a procurement center representative is not assigned, see 19.402(a)) a reasonable period of time to review any solicitation requiring submission of a subcontracting plan and to submit advisory findings before the solicitation is issued.
