16 C.F.R. § 1115.28 — Multiple products or models.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1115.28
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1115.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1115 (Substantial Product Hazard Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For each product or model covered by a recall notice, the notice must meet the requirements of this subpart.
