10 U.S.C. § 8215 — Citizenship of officers of vessels
Citation: us:usc:10:8215
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8215
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 825 (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The officers of vessels of the United States shall in all cases by citizens of the United States.
