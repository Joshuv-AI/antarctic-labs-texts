15 U.S.C. § 690b — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:15:690b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section690b
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 14B (Small Business Investment Program); Subchapter III (Investment Division Programs); Part C (Renewable Fuel Capital Investment Pilot Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall establish a Renewable Fuel Capital Investment Program, under which the Administrator may—
(1)
enter into participation agreements for the purposes described in section 690a of this title; and
(2)
guarantee the debentures issued by Renewable Fuel Capital Investment companies as provided in section 690d of this title.
