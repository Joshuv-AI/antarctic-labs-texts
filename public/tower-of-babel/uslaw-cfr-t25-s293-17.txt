25 C.F.R. § 293.17 — May a compact or amendment include provisions addressing the allocation of criminal and civil jurisdiction between the Tribe and the State?
Citation: us:cfr:25:293.17
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-293.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 293 (Class Iii Tribal-State Gaming Compacts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. A compact or amendment may include provisions allocating criminal and civil jurisdiction between the Tribe and the State necessary for the enforcement of the laws and regulations described in § 293.16.
