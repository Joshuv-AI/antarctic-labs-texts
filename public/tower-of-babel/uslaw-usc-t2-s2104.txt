2 U.S.C. § 2104 — Publication of list of works of art, historical objects, and exhibits
Citation: us:usc:2:2104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section2104
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 30 (Operation And Maintenance Of Capitol Complex); Subchapter V (Historical Preservation And Fine Arts); Part B (Senate Commission On Art). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission shall, from time to time, but at least once every ten years, publish as a Senate document a list of all works of art, historical objects, and exhibits currently within the Senate wing of the Capitol and the Senate Office Buildings, together with their description, location, and with such notes as may be pertinent to their history.
