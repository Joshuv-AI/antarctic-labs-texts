48 C.F.R. § 31.205-23 — 31.205-23 Losses on other contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:48:31.205-23
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-31.205-23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 31 (Contract Cost Principles And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An excess of costs over income under any other contract (including the contractor's contributed portion under cost-sharing contracts) is unallowable.
