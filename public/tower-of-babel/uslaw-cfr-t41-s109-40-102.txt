41 C.F.R. § 109-40.102 — -40.102 Representation before regulatory bodies.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-40.102
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-40.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-40 (40—Transportation And Traffic Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Participation in proceedings related to carrier applications to regulatory bodies for temporary or permanent authority to operate in specified geographical locations shall be confined to statements or testimony in support of a need for service and shall not extend to support of individual carriers or groups of carriers.
