42 C.F.R. § 416.178 — Limitations on administrative and judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:42:416.178
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-416.178
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 416 (Ambulatory Surgical Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There is no administrative or judicial review under section 1869 of the Act, section 1878 of the Act, or otherwise of the following:
(a) The classification system;
(b) Relative weights;
(c) Payment amounts; and
(d) Geographic adjustment factors.
