Published: 2025

V. K. Jordan, B. Fregeau, X. Ge, et al., “Genotype–phenotype correlations in individuals with pathogenic RERE variants,” Human Mutation 2018, no. 39, 666–675, https://doi.org/10.1002/humu.23400.
