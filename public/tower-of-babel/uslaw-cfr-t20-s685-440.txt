20 C.F.R. § 685.440 — How are costs classified under the National Farmworker Jobs Program?
Citation: us:cfr:20:685.440
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-685.440
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 685 (National Farmworker Jobs Program Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Costs are classified as follows:
(1) Administrative costs, as defined in § 683.215 of this chapter; and
(2) Program costs, which are all other costs not defined as administrative.
(b) Program costs must be classified and reported in the following categories:
(1) Related assistance (including emergency assistance);
(2) Supportive services; and
(3) All other program services.
