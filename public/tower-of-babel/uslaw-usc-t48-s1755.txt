48 U.S.C. § 1755 — Compensation, privileges, and immunities
Citation: us:usc:48:1755
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1755
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 16 (Delegates To Congress); Subchapter III (Northern Mariana Islands Delegate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Until the Rules of the House of Representatives are amended to provide otherwise, the Delegate from the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands shall receive the same compensation, allowances, and benefits as a Member of the House of Representatives, and shall be entitled to whatever privileges and immunities are, or hereinafter may be, granted to any other nonvoting Delegate to the House of Representatives.
