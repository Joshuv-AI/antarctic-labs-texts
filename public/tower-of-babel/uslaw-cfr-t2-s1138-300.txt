2 C.F.R. § 1138.300 — Purpose of SUB Article III.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1138.300
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1138.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1138 (Requirements Related To Subawards: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

SUB Article III specifies information that recipients must include in subawards they make under DoD grants and cooperative agreements. It thereby implements OMB guidance in 2 CFR 200.331(a)(1).
