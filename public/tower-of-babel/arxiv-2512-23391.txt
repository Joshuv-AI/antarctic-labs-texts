Abstract
. We consider sequences counting integer partitions in two colors (red and blue) in which the even parts occur only in blue color. We focus on subsequences defined by constraints on the parity and color of the summands. We establish formulas for our sequences and deduce identities of integer partitions.

\begin{split}(a)\ &H_{0}(n)=\begin{cases}\frac{\overline{p}_{o}(n)}{2}+1&\text{if $n$ is a square,}\\

Recall that

Now, by separating the cases according to whether the greatest part is even–blue, odd–blue, or odd–red,

\displaystyle+\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{2n+1}}{(q^{2};q^{2})_{n}(q;q^{2})_{n+1}^{2}}\quad(\text{greatest part odd-blue})

(5.1)

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}F(n)q^{n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{2n+2}}{(q^{2n+2},q^{2n+3},q^{2n+3};q^{2})_{\infty}}\quad(\text{smallest part even})

\displaystyle+\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{2n+1}}{(q^{2n+2},q^{2n+1},q^{2n+3};q^{2})_{\infty}}\quad(\text{smallest part odd-blue})

\displaystyle+\sum_{n\geq 0}\frac{q^{2n+1}}{(q^{2n+2},q^{2n+1},q^{2n+1};q^{2})_{\infty}}\quad(\text{smallest part odd-red}).

(5.2)
