14 C.F.R. § 1250.103-5 — -5 Special benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1250.103-5
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1250 (Nondiscrimination In Federally-Assisted Programs Of Nasa—Effectuation Of Title Vi Of The Civil Rights Act Of 1964). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual shall not be deemed subjected to discrimination by reason of his exclusion from the benefits limited by Federal law to individuals of a particular race, color, or national origin different from his.
