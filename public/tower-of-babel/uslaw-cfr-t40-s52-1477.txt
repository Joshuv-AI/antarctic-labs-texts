40 C.F.R. § 52.1477 — Nevada air pollution emergency plan.

Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-52.1477
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 52 (Approval And Promulgation Of Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 6.1.5 of the Emergency Episode Plan submitted on December 29, 1978 is disapproved since termination of the episode is left to the discretion of the Control Officer and not specified criteria and it does not meet the requirements of 40 CFR 51.16 and Appendix L. The old rule 6.1.5 submitted on January 28, 1972 is retained.
