14 C.F.R. § 323.8 — Exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:323.8
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-323.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 323 (Terminations, Suspensions, And Reductions Of Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Carriers are exempted from paragraphs (a)(1), (a)(3), and (a)(5) of § 323.3 to the extent that those provisions require them to file a notice when terminating or suspending the domestic leg of an international flight (fill-up service).
