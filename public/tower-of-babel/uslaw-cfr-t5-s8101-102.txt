5 C.F.R. § 8101.102 — Prohibitions applicable to Commissioners.
Citation: us:cfr:5:8101.102
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-8101.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 8101 (Supplemental Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The Consumer Product Safety Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commissioners of the Consumer Product Safety Commission are subject to section 4(c) of the Consumer Product Safety Act, 15 U.S.C. 2053(c). That statutory provision provides that a Commissioner may not engage in any other business, vocation, or employment.
