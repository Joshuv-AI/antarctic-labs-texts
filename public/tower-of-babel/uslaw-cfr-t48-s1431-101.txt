48 C.F.R. § 1431.101 — 1431.101 Objectives.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1431 (Contract Cost Principles And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Individual deviations concerning cost principles and procedures shall require the approval of the cognizant Assistant Secretary, with further redelegation authorized. Redelegation is limited to the BPC.
