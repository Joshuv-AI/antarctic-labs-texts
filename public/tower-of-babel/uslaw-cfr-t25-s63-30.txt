25 C.F.R. § 63.30 — What is the purpose of the Indian child protection and family violence prevention program?
Citation: us:cfr:25:63.30
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-63.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 63 (Indian Child Protection And Family Violence Prevention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this program is to develop tribally-operated programs to protect Indian children and reduce the incidence of family violence on Indian reservations.
