54 U.S.C. § 307105 — Attorney’s fees and costs to prevailing parties in civil actions
Citation: us:usc:54:307105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section307105
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3071 (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In any civil action brought in any United States district court by any interested person to enforce this division, if the person substantially prevails in the action, the court may award attorney’s fees, expert witness fees, and other costs of participating in the civil action, as the court considers reasonable.
