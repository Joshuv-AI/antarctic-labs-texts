43 C.F.R. § 9185.1-3 — -3 Mining claims.
Citation: us:cfr:43:9185.1-3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-9185.1-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 9180 (Cadastral Survey). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Application for survey. Application for the survey of a mining claim should be filed with the State Director for the State in which the claim is situated.
(b) Mineral surveyors. See § 3861.5-1 for the appointment of mineral surveyors pursuant to section 2334 of the Revised Statutes (30 U.S.C. 39).
