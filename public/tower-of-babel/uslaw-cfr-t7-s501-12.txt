7 C.F.R. § 501.12 — Weapons and explosives.
Citation: us:cfr:7:501.12
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-501.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 501 (Conduct On U.S. Meat Animal Research Center, Clay Center, Nebraska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person while in or on Research Center property shall carry firearms, bows and arrows, darts, other dangerous or deadly weapons, or explosives, either openly or concealed, except as officially authorized, for official purposes.
