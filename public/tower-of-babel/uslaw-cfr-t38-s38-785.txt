38 C.F.R. § 38.785 — Record retention and access.
Citation: us:cfr:38:38.785
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-38.785
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 38 (National Cemeteries Of The Department Of Veterans Affairs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Recipients must ensure that records are maintained and accessible in accordance with 2 CFR 200.334-200.338. Recipients must produce such records at VA's request.
