49 C.F.R. § 5.89 — Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR).
Citation: us:cfr:49:5.89
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-5.89
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 5 (Administrative Rulemaking, Guidance, And Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The OAs and the components of OST with enforcement authority are encouraged to use ADR to resolve enforcement cases where appropriate. The Department's ADR policy describes a variety of problem-solving processes that can be used in lieu of litigation or other adversarial proceedings to resolve disputes over compliance.
