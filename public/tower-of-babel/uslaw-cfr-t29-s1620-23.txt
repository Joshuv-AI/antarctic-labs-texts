29 C.F.R. § 1620.23 — Collective bargaining agreements not a defense.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1620.23
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1620.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1620 (The Equal Pay Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The establishment by collective bargaining or inclusion in a collective bargaining agreement of unequal rates of pay does not constitute a defense available to either an employer or to a labor organization. Any and all provisions in a collective bargaining agreement which provide unequal rates of pay in conflict with the requirements of the EPA are null and void and of no effect.
