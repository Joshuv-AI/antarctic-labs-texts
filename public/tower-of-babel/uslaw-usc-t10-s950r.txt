10 U.S.C. § 950r — Accessory after the fact
Citation: us:usc:10:950r
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section950r
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47A (Military Commissions); Subchapter VIII (Punitive Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person subject to this chapter who, knowing that an offense punishable by this chapter has been committed, receives, comforts, or assists the offender in order to hinder or prevent his apprehension, trial, or punishment shall be punished as a military commission under this chapter may direct.
