10 C.F.R. § 81.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:10:81.1
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-81.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 81 (Standard Specifications For The Granting Of Patent Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations of this part establish the standard specifications for the issuance of licenses to rights in inventions covered by patents or patent applications vested in the United States of America, as represented by or in the custody of the Commission and other patents in which the Commission has the right to accord or require the grant of licenses.
