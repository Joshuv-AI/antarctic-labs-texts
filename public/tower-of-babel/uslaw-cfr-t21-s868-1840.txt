21 C.F.R. § 868.1840 — Diagnostic spirometer.
Citation: us:cfr:21:868.1840
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-868.1840
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 868 (Anesthesiology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A diagnostic spirometer is a device used in pulmonary function testing to measure the volume of gas moving in or out of a patient's lungs.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
