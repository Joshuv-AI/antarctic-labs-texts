5 C.F.R. § 724.105 — Compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:5:724.105
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-724.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 724 (Implementation Of Title Ii Of The Notification And Federal Employee Antidiscrimination And Retaliation Act Of 2002). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agency's failure to reimburse the Judgment Fund, to contact FMS within 45 business days after receipt of an FMS notice for reimbursement under § 724.104 will be recorded on an annual basis and posted on the FMS Web site. After an agency meets the requirements of § 724.104, the recording will be eliminated no later than the next annual posting process.
