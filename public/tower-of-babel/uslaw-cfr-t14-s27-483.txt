14 C.F.R. § 27.483 — One-wheel landing conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:27.483
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-27.483
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 27 (Airworthiness Standards: Normal Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the one-wheel landing condition, the rotorcraft is assumed to be in the level attitude and to contact the ground on one aft wheel. In this attitude—
(a) The vertical load must be the same as that obtained on that side under § 27.479(b)(1); and
(b) The unbalanced external loads must be reacted by rotorcraft inertia.
