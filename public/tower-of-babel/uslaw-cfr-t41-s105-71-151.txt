41 C.F.R. § 105-71.151 — -71.151 Later disallowances and adjustments.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-71.151
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-71.151
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-71 (71—Uniform Administrative Requirements For Grants And Cooperative Agreements With State And Local Governments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The closeout of a grant does not affect:
(a) The Federal agency's right to disallow costs and recover funds on the basis of a later audit or other review;
(b) The grantee's obligation to return any funds due as a result of later refunds, corrections, or other transactions;
(c) Records retention as required in § 105-71.142;
(d) Property management requirements in §§ 105-71.131 and 105-71.132; and
(e) Audit requirements in § 105-71.126.
