12 C.F.R. § 1277.8 — Reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1277.8
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1277.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1277 (Federal Home Loan Bank Capital Requirements, Capital Stock And Capital Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each Bank shall report information related to capital and other matters addressed by this part in accordance with instructions provided in the Data Reporting Manual issued by FHFA, as amended from time to time.
