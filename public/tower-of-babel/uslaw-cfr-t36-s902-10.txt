36 C.F.R. § 902.10 — Delegation of administration of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:36:902.10
Creator: Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-902.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 902 (Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in subpart H of this part, authority to administer this part is delegated to the Administrative Officer, who shall act upon all requests for access to records which are received by the Corporation from any person citing the Act.
