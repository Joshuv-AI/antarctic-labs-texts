5 C.F.R. § 845.202 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:5:845.202
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-845.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 845 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart covers the collection of debts due the Fund, with the exception of the collection of court-imposed judgments, amounts referred to the Department of Justice because of fraud, and amounts collected from back pay awards in accordance with § 550.805(e)(2) of this chapter.
