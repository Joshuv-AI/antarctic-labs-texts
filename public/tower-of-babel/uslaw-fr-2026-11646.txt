DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY

United States Mint


Pricing for the FIFA World Cup 2026 \TM\ Commemorative Coin 
Program and the 2026 Precious Metals Grid

AGENCY: United States Mint, Department of the Treasury.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

    The United States Mint is announcing pricing for the FIFA World Cup 
2026TM Commemorative Coin Program as follows:

------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                           Introductory
                  Coin                         price       Regular price
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Silver Proof............................         $174.00         $179.00
Silver Uncirculated.....................          169.00          174.00
Clad Proof..............................           64.00           69.00
Clad Uncirculated.......................           61.00           66.00
------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Products containing gold coins will be priced according to the 
Pricing of Numismatic and Commemorative Gold and Platinum Products Grid 
posted at www.usmint.gov.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Rosa Matos, Program Manager for Sales 
and Marketing; United States Mint; 801 9th Street NW; Washington, DC 
20220; or call 202-354-7500.
