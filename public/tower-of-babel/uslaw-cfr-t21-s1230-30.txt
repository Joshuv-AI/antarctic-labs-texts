21 C.F.R. § 1230.30 — Collection of samples.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1230.30
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1230.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1230 (Regulations Under The Federal Caustic Poison Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Samples for examination by or under the direction and supervision of the Food and Drug Administration shall be collected by:
(a) An authorized agent in the employ of the Department of Health and Human Services;
(b) Any officer of any State, Territory, or possession, or of the District of Columbia, authorized by the Secretary of Health and Human Services.
