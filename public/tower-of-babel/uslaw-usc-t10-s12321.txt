10 U.S.C. § 12321 — Reserve Officer Training Corps units: limitation on number of Reserves assigned
Citation: us:usc:10:12321
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12321
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1209 (Active Duty). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The number of members of the reserve components serving on active duty or full-time National Guard duty for the purpose of organizing, administering, recruiting, instructing, or training the reserve components who are assigned to duty with a unit of the Reserve Officer Training Corps program may not exceed 275.
