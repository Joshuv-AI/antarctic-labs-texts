18 U.S.C. § 1368 — Harming animals used in law enforcement
Citation: us:usc:18:1368
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1368
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 65 (Malicious Mischief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Whoever willfully and maliciously harms any police animal, or attempts or conspires to do so, shall be fined under this title and imprisoned not more than 1 year. If the offense permanently disables or disfigures the animal, or causes serious bodily injury to or the death of the animal, the maximum term of imprisonment shall be 10 years.
(b)
In this section, the term “police animal” means a dog or horse employed by a Federal agency (whether in the executive, legislative, or judicial branch) for the principal purpose of aiding in the detection of criminal activity, enforcement of laws, or apprehension of criminal offenders.
