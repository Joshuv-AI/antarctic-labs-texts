41 C.F.R. § 109-40.104 — -40.104 Use of Government-owned transportation equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-40.104
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-40.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-40 (40—Transportation And Traffic Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The preferred method of transporting property for the Government is through use of the facilities and services of commercial carriers. However, Government vehicles may be used when they are available to meet emergencies and accomplish program objectives which cannot be attained through use of commercial carriers.
