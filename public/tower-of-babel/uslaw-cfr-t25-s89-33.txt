25 C.F.R. § 89.33 — Notice from attorney.
Citation: us:cfr:25:89.33
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-89.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 89 (Attorney Contracts With Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Attorneys desiring to execute contracts with any one of the Five Civilized Tribes shall be required to give written notice to the Area Director through the Superintendent having jurisdiction over said tribe.
