5 C.F.R. § 894.201 — What types of enrollments are available under FEDVIP?
Citation: us:cfr:5:894.201
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-894.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 894 (Federal Employees Dental And Vision Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FEDVIP has three types of enrollment:
(a) Self only, which covers only the enrollee;
(b) Self plus one, which covers the enrollee plus one family member; and
(c) Self and family, which covers the enrollee and all family members.
