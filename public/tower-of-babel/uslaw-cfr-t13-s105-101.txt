13 C.F.R. § 105.101 — Cross-reference to employee ethical conduct standards and financial disclosure regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:13:105.101
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-105.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 105 (Standards Of Conduct And Employee Restrictions And Responsibilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to this part, Small Business Administration (SBA) employees should refer to the Standards of Ethical Conduct for Employees of the Executive Branch at 5 CFR part 2635 and the regulations at 5 CFR part 2634 entitled, Executive Branch Financial Disclosure, Qualified Trusts and Certificates of Divestiture.
