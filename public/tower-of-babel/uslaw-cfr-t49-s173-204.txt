49 C.F.R. § 173.204 — Non-bulk, non-specification packagings for certain hazardous materials.
Citation: us:cfr:49:173.204
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-173.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 173 (Shippers—General Requirements For Shipments And Packagings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When § 172.101 of this subchapter specifies that a liquid or solid hazardous material be packaged under this section, any appropriate non-bulk packaging which conforms to the general packaging requirements of subpart B of part 173 may be used for its transportation. Packagings need not conform to the requirements of part 178 of this subchapter.
