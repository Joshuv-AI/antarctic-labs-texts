34 C.F.R. § 669.22 — What priorities may the Secretary establish?
Citation: us:cfr:34:669.22
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-669.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 669 (Language Resource Centers Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary may each year select funding priorities from among the following:
(1) Categories of allowable activities described in § 669.3.
(2) Specific foreign languages for study or materials development.
(3) Levels of education, for example, elementary, secondary, postsecondary, or teacher education.
(b) The Secretary announces any priorities in the application notice published in the Federal Register.
