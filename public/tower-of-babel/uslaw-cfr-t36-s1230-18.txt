36 C.F.R. § 1230.18 — What assistance is available to agencies to recover unlawfully removed records?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1230.18
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1230.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1230 (Unlawful Or Accidental Removal, Defacing, Alteration, Or Destruction Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NARA will assist the head of the agency in the recovery of any unlawfully removed records, including contacting the Attorney General, if appropriate.
