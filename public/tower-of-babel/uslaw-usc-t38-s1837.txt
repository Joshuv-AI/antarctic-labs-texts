38 U.S.C. § 1837 — Duration of health care and benefits provided
Citation: us:usc:38:1837
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1837
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 18 (Benefits For Children Of Vietnam Veterans And Certain Other Veterans); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall provide a covered child with health care and benefits under this chapter—
(1)
for the duration of the life of the child; and
(2)
notwithstanding any death of a parent of the child that precedes the death of the child.
