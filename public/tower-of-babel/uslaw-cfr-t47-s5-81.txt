47 C.F.R. § 5.81 — Discontinuance of station operation.
Citation: us:cfr:47:5.81
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-5.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 5 (Experimental Radio Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In case of permanent discontinuance of operation of a station in the Experimental Radio Service prior to the license expiration date, the licensee shall notify the Commission. Licensees who willfully fail to do so may be subject to disciplinary action, including monetary fines, by the Commission.
