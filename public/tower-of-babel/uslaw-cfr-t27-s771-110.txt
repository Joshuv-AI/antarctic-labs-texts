27 C.F.R. § 771.110 — Revocation or denial of renewal.
Citation: us:cfr:27:771.110
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-771.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 771 (Rules Of Practice In Explosive License And Permit Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to § 771.109(a), when the Director of Industry Operations issues an order revoking or denying the renewal of a license or permit, he shall furnish a copy of the order and of the recommended decision on which it is based to the Director. Should such order be subsequently set aside on review by the courts, the Director of Industry Operations will so advise the Director.
