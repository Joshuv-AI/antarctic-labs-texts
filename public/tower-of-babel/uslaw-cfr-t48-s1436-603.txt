48 C.F.R. § 1436.603 — 1436.603 Collecting data on and appraising firms' qualifications.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1436.603
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1436.603
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1436 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HCAs who require architect-engineer services shall use their established procedures to collect data on and appraising firms' qualifications.
