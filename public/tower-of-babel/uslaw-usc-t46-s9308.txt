46 U.S.C. § 9308 — Penalties
Citation: us:usc:46:9308
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section9308
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Manning Of Vessels); Chapter 93 (Great Lakes Pilotage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
An owner, charterer, managing operator, agent, master, or individual in charge of a vessel knowingly allowing the vessel to be operated in violation of section 9302 of this title is liable to the United States Government for a civil penalty of no more than $10,000 for each day during which the vessel is in violation. The vessel also is liable in rem for the penalty.
(b)
An individual who directs the navigation of a vessel in violation of section 9302 of this title is liable to the Government for a civil penalty of no more than $10,000 for each day during which the violation occurs.
(c)
A person violating a regulation prescribed under section 9303 of this title is liable to the Government for a civil penalty of no more than $10,000.
