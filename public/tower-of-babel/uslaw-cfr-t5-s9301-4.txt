5 C.F.R. § 9301.4 — Availability of records.
Citation: us:cfr:5:9301.4
Creator: Special Inspector General For Afghanistan Reconstruction
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9301.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9301 (Disclosure Of Records And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

SIGAR provides records to individual requesters in response to FOIA requests. Records that are required by the FOIA to be made available for public inspection and copying are accessible on SIGAR's Web site, http://www.sigar.mil. SIGAR will also identify records of interest to the public that are appropriate for public disclosure, and then post these records.
