54 U.S.C. § 300304 — Cultural park
Citation: us:usc:54:300304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section300304
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3003 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this division, the term “cultural park” means a definable area that—
(A)
is distinguished by historic property, prehistoric property, and land related to that property; and
(B)
constitutes an interpretive, educational, and recreational resource for the public at large.
