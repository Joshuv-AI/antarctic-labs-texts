18 U.S.C. § 1710 — Theft of newspapers
Citation: us:usc:18:1710
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1710
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 83 (Postal Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, being a Postal Service officer or employee, takes or steals any newspaper or package of newspapers from any post office or from any person having custody thereof, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
