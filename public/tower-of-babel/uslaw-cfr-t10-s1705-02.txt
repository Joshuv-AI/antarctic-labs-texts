10 C.F.R. § 1705.02 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1705.02
Creator: Defense Nuclear Facilities Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1705.02
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1705 (Privacy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following terms used in these regulations are defined in the Privacy Act, 5 U.S.C. 552a(a): agency, individual, maintain, record, system of records, statistical record, and routine use. The Board's use of these terms conforms with the statutory definitions. References in this part to “the Act” refer to the Privacy Act of 1974.
