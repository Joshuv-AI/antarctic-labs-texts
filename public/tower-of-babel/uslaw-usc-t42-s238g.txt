42 U.S.C. § 238g — Wearing of uniforms
Citation: us:usc:42:238g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section238g
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter I (Administration And Miscellaneous Provisions); Part B (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as may be authorized by regulations of the President, the insignia and uniform of commissioned officers of the Service, or any distinctive part of such insignia or uniform, or any insignia or uniform any part of which is similar to a distinctive part thereof, shall not be worn, after the promulgation of such regulations, by any person other than a commissioned officer of the Service.
