42 U.S.C. § 8145 — Coordination and development of program with other Federal and non-Federal programs
Citation: us:usc:42:8145
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8145
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 90 (Neighborhood And City Reinvestment, Self-Help And Revitalization); Subchapter III (Livable Cities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall coordinate the administration of the provisions of this subchapter in cooperation with other Federal agencies and assure that projects assisted under this subchapter are coordinated with efforts undertaken by State and local public and private entities, including arts organizations.
