22 C.F.R. § 903.3 — Rulings on materials.
Citation: us:cfr:22:903.3
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-903.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 903 (Initiation And Documentation Of Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may at any stage of the proceedings exclude materials from the record of proceedings at the request of a party or on its own initiative, on the grounds that such materials are irrelevant, immaterial or unduly repetitive.
