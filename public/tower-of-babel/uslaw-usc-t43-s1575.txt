43 U.S.C. § 1575 — Contract authority
Citation: us:usc:43:1575
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1575
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 32A (Colorado River Basin Salinity Control); Subchapter I (Programs Downstream From Imperial Dam). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to enter into contracts that he deems necessary to carry out the provisions of this subchapter in advance of the appropriation of funds therefor.
