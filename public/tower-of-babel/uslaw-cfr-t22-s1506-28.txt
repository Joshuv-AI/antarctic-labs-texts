22 C.F.R. § 1506.28 — Will I receive a refund if the claim against me is found to be without merit?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1506.28
Creator: African Development Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1506.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1506 (Collection Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

USADF will promptly refund to the employee any amounts paid or deducted pursuant to this section that are subsequently waived or found not owing to the United States Government. Refunds do not bear interest unless specifically authorized by law.
