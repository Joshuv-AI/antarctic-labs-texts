16 U.S.C. § 1010 — Data
Citation: us:usc:16:1010
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1010
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 18 (Watershed Protection And Flood Prevention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall collect and maintain data on a national and State by State basis concerning—
(1)
expenditures for the individual flood control and conservation measures for which assistance is provided under this chapter; and
(2)
the expected flood control or environmental (including soil erosion) benefits that will result from the implementation of such measures.
