31 C.F.R. § 315.77 — Exchanges by fiduciaries.
Citation: us:cfr:31:315.77
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-315.77
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 315 (Regulations Governing U.S. Savings Bonds, Series A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, J, And K, And U.S. Savings Notes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fiduciaries are authorized to request an exchange of bonds of one series for those of another, pursuant to any applicable Department of the Treasury offering. A living coowner or beneficiary named on the bonds submitted in exchange may be retained in the same capacity on the new bonds.
