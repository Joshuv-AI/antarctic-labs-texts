30 C.F.R. § 33.23 — Mechanical positioning of parts.
Citation: us:cfr:30:33.23
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-33.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 33 (Dust Collectors For Use In Connection With Rock Drilling In Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All parts of a unit that are essential to the dust-collection feature shall be provided with suitable mechanical means for positioning and maintaining such parts properly in relation to the stratum being drilled.
