43 C.F.R. § 3283.5 — When will BLM periodically review unit agreements?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3283.5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3283.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3280 (Geothermal Resources Unit Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM will periodically review all unit agreements to determine compliance with § 3283.6 in accordance with the following schedule:
(a) Not later than 5 years after the approval of each unit agreement; and
(b) At least every 5 years following the initial unit review.
