45 C.F.R. § 1171.13 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1171.13
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1171.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1171 (Public Access To Neh Records Under The Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part will be construed to entitle any person, as of right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the FOIA.
