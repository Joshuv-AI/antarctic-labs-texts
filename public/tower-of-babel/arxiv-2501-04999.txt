Abstract
. Let ξ ∈ 𝔽 q m \xi\in\mathbb{F}_{q^{m}} be an r r -primitive k k -normal element over 𝔽 q \mathbb{F}_{q} , where q q is a prime power and m m is a positive integer. The minimal polynomial of ξ \xi is referred to be the r r -primitive k k -normal polynomial of ξ \xi over 𝔽 q \mathbb{F}_{q} . In this article, we study the existence of an r r -primitive k k -normal polynomial over 𝔽 q \mathbb{F}_{q} such that the last two coefficients are prescribed. In this context, first, we prove a sufficient condition which guarantees the existence of such a polynomial. Further, we compute all possible exceptional pairs ( q , m ) (q,m) in case of 3 3 -primitive 1 1 -normal polynomials for m ≥ 7 m\geq 7 .

. This gives
\xi^{3(q-1)}=1
, that is, ord
(\xi)\leq 3(q-1)<\frac{q^{3}-1}{3}
for
q>2
, a contradiction.

Case 2.
If Ord
{}_{q}(\xi)=(x-1)(x-c)
, then we have
(\xi^{q}-c\xi)^{q-1}=1
, that is,
\xi^{q}-c\xi\in\mathbb{F}_{q}
. Let
\xi^{q}=c\xi+f
, for some
f\in\mathbb{F}_{q}
. Now,
\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathbb{F}_{q^{3}}/\mathbb{F}_{q}}(\xi^{-1})=0
gives us the minimal polynomial of
\xi\in\mathbb{F}_{q^{3}}
is of the form
x^{3}+dx^{2}+e
for some
d,e\in\mathbb{F}_{q}
. Then,
\xi^{3}=d_{1}{\xi}^{2}+e_{1}
for some
d_{1},e_{1}\in\mathbb{F}_{q}
, which implies that
\xi^{3q}=d_{1}{\xi}^{2q}+e_{1}
, that is,
(c\xi+f)^{3}=d_{1}(c\xi+f)^{2}+e_{1}
. Further, by substituting
\xi^{3}=d_{1}{\xi}^{2}+e_{1}
, we get a polynomial
P
of degree
\leq 2
over
\mathbb{F}_{q}
such that
P(\xi)=0
. Then
\xi\in\mathbb{F}_{q^{2}}
, that is, ord
(\xi)\leq q^{2}-1<\frac{q^{3}-1}{3}
for
q>3
, a contradiction.∎

Lemma 4.12
.

If there exists a
3
-primitive
1
-normal element
\xi\in\mathbb{F}_{q^{4}}
over
\mathbb{F}_{q}
such that
\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathbb{F}_{q^{4}}/\mathbb{F}_{q}}(\xi^{-1})=ab^{-1}
and
\mathrm{N}_{\mathbb{F}_{q^{4}}/\mathbb{F}_{q}}(\xi)=b
, for some
a\in\mathbb{F}_{q}
,
b\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*}
, then
3|q^{4}-1
and
q\equiv 1(mod4)
.

Proof.

Let
\xi\in\mathbb{F}_{q^{4}}
an
3
-primitive
1
-normal element over
\mathbb{F}_{q}
such that
\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathbb{F}_{q^{4}}/\mathbb{F}_{q}}(\xi^{-1})=ab^{-1}
and
\mathrm{N}_{\mathbb{F}_{q^{4}}/\mathbb{F}_{q}}(\xi)=b
, for some
a\in\mathbb{F}_{q}
,
b\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{*}
. If
q\not\equiv 1(mod4)
, then
x^{4}-1=(x-1)^{4}
or
(x-1)(x+1)(x^{2}+1)
. Since
\xi
is
1
-normal, we must have deg(Ord
{}_{q}(\xi))=3
. We now consider the following possible cases:

Case
1
.
If Ord
{}_{q}(\xi)=x^{3}+x^{2}+x+1
, then we have
\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathbb{F}_{q^{4}}/\mathbb{F}_{q}}(\xi)=0
. In addition to this,
\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathbb{F}_{q^{4}}/\mathbb{F}_{q}}(\xi^{-1})=0
gives us the minimal polynomial of
\xi
is of the form
x^{4}=cx^{2}+d
, for some
c,d\in\mathbb{F}_{q}
. Then
{\xi}^{4}=c{\xi}^{2}+d
, which implies that
{\xi}^{4q}=c{\xi}^{2q}+d
, which further implies that
\xi^{4q}-\xi^{4}=c\cdot(\xi^{2q}-\xi^{2})
. Since
\xi^{2q}\neq\xi^{2}
, it follows that
\xi^{2q}+\xi^{2}=c
, which gives
\xi^{2q^{2}}+\xi^{2q}=c
. Thus
\xi^{2(q^{2}-1)}=1
, that is, ord
(\xi)\leq 2(q^{2}-1)<\frac{q^{4}-1}{3}
for
q>2
.

Case
2
.
If Ord
{}_{q}(\xi)=(x-1)(x^{2}+1)
, then we get that
(\xi^{q^{2}}+\xi)^{q-1}=1
, that is,
\xi^{q^{2}}+\xi=g
for some
g\in\mathbb{F}_{q}
. Since
\xi
is
3
-primitive, we must have
g\neq 0
. Further,
\mathrm{Tr}_{\mathbb{F}_{q^{4}}/\mathbb{F}_{q}}(\xi^{-1})=0
gives us the minimal polynomial of
\xi
is of the form
x^{4}-dx^{3}-ex^{2}-f
for some
d,e,f\in\mathbb{F}_{q}
. Then, we have
{\xi}^{4}=d{\xi}^{3}+e{\xi}^{2}+f
, which gives that
{\xi}^{4q^{2}}=d{\xi}^{3q^{2}}+e{\xi}^{2q^{2}}+f
. Thus
({\xi}^{4q^{2}}-{\xi}^{4})=d({\xi}^{3q^{2}}-{\xi}^{3})+e({\xi}^{2q^{2}}-{\xi}^{2})
, which implies that
(\xi^{q^{2}}+\xi)(\xi^{2q^{2}}+\xi^{2})=d(\xi^{2q^{2}}+\xi^{q^{2}+1}+\xi^{2})+e(\xi^{q^{2}}+\xi)
. Further, by substituting
\xi^{q^{2}}=g-\xi
, we get a polynomial
R
of degree
2
over
\mathbb{F}_{q}
such that
R(\xi)=0
. Hence ord
(\xi)\leq q^{2}-1<\frac{q^{4}-1}{3}
for
q\geq 2
, a contradiction.

Thus, a
3
-primitive
1
-normal element
\xi\in\mathbb{F}_{q^{4}}
with the properties as mentioned above may exist, only if
q\equiv 1(mod~4)
and
3|q^{4}-1
.
∎
