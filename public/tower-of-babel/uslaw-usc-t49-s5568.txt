49 U.S.C. § 5568 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:49:5568
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section5568
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 55 (Intermodal Transportation); Subchapter II (Terminals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) General.—
The following amounts may be appropriated to the Secretary of Transportation:
(1)
not more than $15,000,000 to carry out section 5562(a)(1) and (3) of this title.
(2)
not more than $2,500,000 to carry out section 5562(a)(2) of this title.
(3)
not more than $2,500,000 to carry out section 5562(a)(4) of this title.
(b) Availability of Amounts.—
Amounts appropriated to carry out this chapter remain available until expended.
