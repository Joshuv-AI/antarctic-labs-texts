46 C.F.R. § 535.803 — Ocean freight forwarder compensation.
Citation: us:cfr:46:535.803
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-535.803
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 535 (Ocean Common Carrier And Marine Terminal Operator Agreements Subject To The Shipping Act Of 1984). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No conference or group of two or more ocean common carriers may:
(a) Deny to any member of such conference or group the right, upon notice of not more than 5 calendar days, to take independent action on any level of compensation paid to an ocean freight forwarder; or
(b) Agree to limit the payment of compensation to an ocean freight forwarder to less than 1.25 percent of the aggregate of all rates and charges applicable under the tariff assessed against the cargo on which the forwarding services are provided.
