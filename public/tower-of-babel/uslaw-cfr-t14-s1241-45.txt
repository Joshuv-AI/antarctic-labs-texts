14 C.F.R. § 1241.45 — Collaboration with other agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1241.45
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1241.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1241 (To Research, Evaluate, Assess, And Treat (Treat) Astronauts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Copies of records generated from medical monitoring, diagnosis, and treatment collected by primary payer facilities and/or relevant health care providers will be acquired by NASA. NASA will collaborate with the Department of Defense Military Health System, Department of Veterans Affairs, and Department of Labor Office of Workers' Compensation and other entities for acquisition of copies of these medical records as allowed by law.
