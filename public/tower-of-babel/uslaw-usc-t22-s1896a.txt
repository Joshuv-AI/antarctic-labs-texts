22 U.S.C. § 1896a — Restriction on commitments for technical assistance
Citation: us:usc:22:1896a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1896a
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 24 (Mutual Security Program); Subchapter III (Economic Assistance); Part C (Technical Cooperation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No commitment for the calendar year 1955 or thereafter, with respect to contributions to the United Nations expanded program of technical assistance, shall be pledged on behalf of the United States until the Congress appropriates for said purpose.
