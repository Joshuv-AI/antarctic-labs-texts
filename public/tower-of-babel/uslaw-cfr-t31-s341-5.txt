31 C.F.R. § 341.5 — Limitation on holdings.
Citation: us:cfr:31:341.5
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-341.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 341 (Regulations Governing United States Retirement Plan Bonds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The limit on the amount of any Retirement Plan Bonds issued during 1974, or in any one calendar year thereafter, that may be purchased in the name of any one person as registered owner is $10,000 (face value).
