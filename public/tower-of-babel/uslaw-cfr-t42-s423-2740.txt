42 C.F.R. § 423.2740 — Negative invoice payment process for Part D sponsors.
Citation: us:cfr:42:423.2740
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-423.2740
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 423 (Voluntary Medicare Prescription Drug Benefit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) CMS will invoice negative amounts to Part D sponsors when a PDE(s) which had been previously invoiced is deleted or adjusted such that the reported Manufacturer Discount Program discount amount is less than originally invoiced.
(b) Part D sponsors are required to pay such negative invoice amounts in the manner specified by CMS within 38 calendar days of receipt of the invoice.
