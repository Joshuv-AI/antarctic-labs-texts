49 C.F.R. § 383.151 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:49:383.151
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-383.151
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 383 (Commercial Driver'S License Standards; Requirements And Penalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The CDL must be a document that is easy to recognize as a CDL.
(b) The CLP must be a separate document from the CDL or non-CDL.
(c) At a minimum, the CDL and the CLP must contain the information specified in § 383.153.
