48 C.F.R. § 1816.404 — 1816.404 Fixed-price contracts with award fees.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1816.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1816 (Types Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 1816.405-2 applies to the use of FPAF contracts as if they were CPAF contracts. However, neither base fee (see 1816.405-271) nor evaluation of cost control (see 1816.405-274) apply to FPAF contracts.
