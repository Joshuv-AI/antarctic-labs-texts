29 C.F.R. § 2200.69 — Examination of witnesses.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2200.69
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2200.69
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2200 (Rules Of Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Witnesses shall be examined orally under oath or affirmation. Opposing parties have the right to cross-examine any witness whose testimony is introduced by an adverse party. All parties shall have the right to cross-examine any witness called by the Judge pursuant to § 2200.67(j).
