46 C.F.R. § 57.05-3 — -3 Limited space qualifications.
Citation: us:cfr:46:57.05-3
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-57.05-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 57 (Welding And Brazing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a welder is to be qualified for welding or torch brazing of piping on board ship in a limited or restricted space, the space restrictions shown in connection with figures 1 or 2 to § 57.05-3 must be used when welding and brazing the test joint.
