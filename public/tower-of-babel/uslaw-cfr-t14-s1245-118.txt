14 C.F.R. § 1245.118 — Record of decisions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1245.118
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1245.118
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1245 (Patents And Other Intellectual Property Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The findings of fact and recommendations made to the Administrator by the Board with respect to each petition for waiver shall be recorded by the Board and be available to the public.
