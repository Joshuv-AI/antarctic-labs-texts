27 C.F.R. § 22.52 — Rules of practice in permit proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:27:22.52
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-22.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 22 (Distribution And Use Of Tax-Free Alcohol). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations of part 71 of this chapter apply to the procedure and practice in connection with the disapproval of any application for a permit and in connection with suspension or revocation of a permit.
