5 U.S.C. § 8130 — Assignment of claim
Citation: us:usc:5:8130
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section8130
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart G (Insurance And Annuities); Chapter 81 (Compensation For Work Injuries); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An assignment of a claim for compensation under this subchapter is void. Compensation and claims for compensation are exempt from claims of creditors.
