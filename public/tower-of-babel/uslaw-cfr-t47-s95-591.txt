47 C.F.R. § 95.591 — Sales of FRS combination radios prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:47:95.591
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-95.591
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 95 (Personal Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Effective September 30, 2019, no person shall sell or offer for sale hand-held portable radio equipment capable of operating under this subpart (FRS) and under any other licensed or licensed-by-rule radio services in this chapter (devices may be authorized under this subpart with part 15 unlicensed equipment authorizations).
