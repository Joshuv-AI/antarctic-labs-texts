40 C.F.R. § 355.43 — When must I submit the information?
Citation: us:cfr:40:355.43
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-355.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 355 (Emergency Planning And Notification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must provide the required emergency release notification information described under § 355.40(a), immediately.
(b) You must provide the written follow-up emergency notice (or notices, as more information becomes available) described under § 355.40(b), as soon as practicable after the release.
