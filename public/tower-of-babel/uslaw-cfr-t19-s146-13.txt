19 C.F.R. § 146.13 — Customs forms and procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:19:146.13
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-146.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 146 (Foreign Trade Zones). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where a Customs form or other document is required in this part, the number of copies of the form or document required to be presented and their manner of distribution and processing shall be determined by the port director, except as otherwise specified in this part.
