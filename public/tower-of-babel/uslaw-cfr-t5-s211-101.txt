5 C.F.R. § 211.101 — Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 211 (Veteran Preference). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).
