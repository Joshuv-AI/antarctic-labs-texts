37 C.F.R. § 2.194 — Identification of trademark application or registration.
Citation: us:cfr:37:2.194
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-2.194
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 2 (Rules Of Practice In Trademark Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No correspondence relating to a trademark application should be filed prior to receipt of the application serial number.
(b) (1) A letter about a trademark application should identify the serial number, the name of the applicant, and the mark.
(2) A letter about a registered trademark should identify the registration number, the name of the registrant, and the mark.
