43 C.F.R. § 3102.10 — Who may hold leases.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3102.10
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3102.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3100 (Oil And Gas Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Leases or interests therein may be acquired and held only by citizens of the United States; associations (including partnerships and trusts) of such citizens; corporations organized under the laws of the United States or of any State or Territory thereof; and municipalities.
