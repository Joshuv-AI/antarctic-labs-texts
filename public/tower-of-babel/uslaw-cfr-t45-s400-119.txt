45 C.F.R. § 400.119 — Interstate movement.
Citation: us:cfr:45:400.119
Creator: Office Of Refugee Resettlement, Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-400.119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 400 (Refugee Resettlement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After the initial placement of an unaccompanied minor, the same procedures that govern the movement of nonrefugee foster cases to other States apply to the movement of unaccompanied minors to other States.
