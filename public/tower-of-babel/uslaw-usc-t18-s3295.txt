18 U.S.C. § 3295 — Arson offenses
Citation: us:usc:18:3295
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3295
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 213 (Limitations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No person shall be prosecuted, tried, or punished for any non-capital offense under section 81 or subsection (f), (h), or (i) of section 844 unless the indictment is found or the information is instituted not later than 10 years after the date on which the offense was committed.
