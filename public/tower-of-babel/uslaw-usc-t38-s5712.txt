38 U.S.C. § 5712 — Validity of affidavits
Citation: us:usc:38:5712
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section5712
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part IV (General Administrative Provisions); Chapter 57 (Records And Investigations); Subchapter II (Investigations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any such oath, affirmation, affidavit, or examination, when certified under the hand of any such employee by whom it was administered or taken and authenticated by the seal of the Department, may be offered or used in any court of the United States and without further proof of the identity or authority of such employee shall have like force and effect as if administered or taken before a clerk of such court.
