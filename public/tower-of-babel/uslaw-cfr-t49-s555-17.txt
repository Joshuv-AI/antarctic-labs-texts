49 C.F.R. § 555.17 — Termination of temporary exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:49:555.17
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-555.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 555 (Temporary Exemption From Motor Vehicle Safety And Bumper Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may terminate or modify a temporary exemption if (s)he determines that:
(a) The temporary exemption was granted on the basis of false, fraudulent, or misleading representations or information; or
(b) The temporary exemption is no longer consistent with the public interest and the objectives of the Act.
