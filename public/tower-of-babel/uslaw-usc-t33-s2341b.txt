33 U.S.C. § 2341b — Prioritization of certain projects
Citation: us:usc:33:2341b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2341b
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 36 (Water Resources Development); Subchapter V (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall give priority to a project for flood risk management if—
(1)
there is an executed project partnership agreement for the project; and
(2)
the project is located in an area—
(A)
with respect to which—
(i)
there has been a loss of life due to flood events; and
(ii)
the President has declared that a major disaster or emergency exists under section 5170 of title 42; or
(B)
that is at significant risk for catastrophic flooding.
