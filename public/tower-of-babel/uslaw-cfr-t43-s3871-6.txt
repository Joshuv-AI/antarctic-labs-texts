43 C.F.R. § 3871.6 — Certificate required when no suit commenced.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3871.6
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3871.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3870 (Adverse Claims, Protests And Conflicts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where an adverse claim has been filed but no suit commenced against the applicant for patent within the statutory period, a certificate to that effect by the clerk of the State court having jurisdiction in the case, and also by the clerk of the district court of the United States for the district in which the claim is situated, will be required.
