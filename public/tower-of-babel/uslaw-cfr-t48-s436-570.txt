48 C.F.R. § 436.570 — 436.570 Emergency response, fire suppression and liability.
Citation: us:cfr:48:436.570
Creator: Chapter 4—Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-436.570
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 436 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at AGAR 452.236-70, Emergency Response, Fire Suppression and Liability, in Integrated Resource Service Contracts (IRSCs) awarded for the Forest Service. The clause AGAR 452.236-70, Emergency Response, Fire Suppression and Liability, is optional for non-IRSCs.
