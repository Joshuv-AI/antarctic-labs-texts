18 C.F.R. § 154.207 — Notice requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:18:154.207
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-154.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 154 (Rate Schedules And Tariffs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All proposed changes in tariffs, contracts, or any parts thereof must be filed with the Commission and posted not less than 30 days nor more than 60 days prior to the proposed effective date thereof, unless a waiver of the time periods is granted by the Commission.
