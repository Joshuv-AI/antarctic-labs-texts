40 C.F.R. § 166.34 — EPA review of information obtained in connection with emergency exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:40:166.34
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-166.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 166 (Exemption Of Federal And State Agencies For Use Of Pesticides Under Emergency Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

EPA shall review information submitted in connection with emergency exemptions and, when applicable, use it in connection with other regulatory decisions under the Act.
