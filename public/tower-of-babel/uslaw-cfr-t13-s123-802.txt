13 C.F.R. § 123.802 — Scope and applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:13:123.802
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-123.802
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 123 (Disaster Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to Disaster-Related Activities and all SBA Disaster Loan borrowers, including any contractors, subcontractors, and agents of such borrowers conducting Disaster-Related Activities.
