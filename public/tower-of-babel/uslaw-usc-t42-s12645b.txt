42 U.S.C. § 12645b — Generation of volunteers
Citation: us:usc:42:12645b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12645b
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 129 (National And Community Service); Subchapter I (National And Community Service State Grant Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In making decisions on applications for assistance or approved national service positions under the national service laws, the Corporation shall take into consideration the extent to which the applicant’s proposal will increase the involvement of volunteers in meeting community needs. In reviewing the application for this purpose, the Corporation may take into account the mission of the applicant.
