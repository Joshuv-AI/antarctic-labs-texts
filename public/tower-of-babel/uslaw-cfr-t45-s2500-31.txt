45 C.F.R. § 2500.31 — Retirement of logos.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2500.31
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2500.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2500 (Americorps Statement Of Organization, Agency Operating Name, And Logos). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency officially retired the day-to-day use of all pre-existing logos, emblems, and other insignia, except the Days of Service logos, but does not relinquish the legal rights to any retired logos.
