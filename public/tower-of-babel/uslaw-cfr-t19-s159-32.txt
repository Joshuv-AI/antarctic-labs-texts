19 C.F.R. § 159.32 — Date of exportation.
Citation: us:cfr:19:159.32
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-159.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 159 (Liquidation Of Duties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The date of exportation for currency conversion shall be fixed in accordance with § 152.1(c) of this chapter.
