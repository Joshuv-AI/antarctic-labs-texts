21 C.F.R. § 205.8 — Violations and penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:21:205.8
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-205.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 205 (Guidelines For State Licensing Of Wholesale Prescription Drug Distributors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) State licensing laws shall provide for the suspension or revocation of licenses upon conviction of violations of Federal, State, or local drug laws or regulations, and may provide for fines, imprisonment, or civil penalties.
(b) State licensing laws shall provide for suspension or revocation of licenses, where appropriate, for violations of its provisions.
