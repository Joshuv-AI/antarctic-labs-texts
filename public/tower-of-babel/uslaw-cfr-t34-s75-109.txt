34 C.F.R. § 75.109 — Changes to applications.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.109
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant may make changes to its application on or before the deadline date for submitting the application under the program.
