4 C.F.R. § 25.2 — Inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:4:25.2
Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-4/section-25.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 25 (Conduct In The Government Accountability Office Building And On Its Grounds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Packages, briefcases, and other containers as well as vehicles and their contents are subject to inspection while in or when being brought into, or when being removed from the GAO Building. A full search of a person may accompany an arrest or apprehension.
