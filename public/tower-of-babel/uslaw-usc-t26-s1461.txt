26 U.S.C. § 1461 — Liability for withheld tax
Citation: us:usc:26:1461
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section1461
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 3 (Withholding Of Tax On Nonresident Aliens And Foreign Corporations); Subchapter B (Application Of Withholding Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every person required to deduct and withhold any tax under this chapter is hereby made liable for such tax and is hereby indemnified against the claims and demands of any person for the amount of any payments made in accordance with the provisions of this chapter.
