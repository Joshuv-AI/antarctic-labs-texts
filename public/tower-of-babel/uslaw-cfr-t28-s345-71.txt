28 C.F.R. § 345.71 — Official commendations.
Citation: us:cfr:28:345.71
Creator: Federal Prison Industries, Inc., Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-345.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 345 (Federal Prison Industries (Fpi) Inmate Work Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An inmate worker may receive an official written commendation for any suggestion or invention adopted by FPI, or for any special achievement, as determined by the SOI, related to the inmate's industrial work assignment. A copy of the commendation is to be placed in the inmate's central file.
