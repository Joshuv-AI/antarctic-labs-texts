21 U.S.C. § 379k — Information system
Citation: us:usc:21:379k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section379k
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 9 (Federal Food, Drug, And Cosmetic Act); Subchapter VII (General Authority); Part D (Information And Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall establish and maintain an information system to track the status and progress of each application or submission (including a petition, notification, or other similar form of request) submitted to the Food and Drug Administration requesting agency action.
