36 C.F.R. § 1008.18 — Amendment of records.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1008.18
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1008.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1008 (Requests Under The Privacy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Privacy Act permits individuals to request amendment of records pertaining to them contained in a system of records if they believe the records are not accurate, relevant, timely or complete. 5 U.S.C. 552a(d)(2). A request for amendment of a record shall be submitted in accordance with the procedures in this part.
