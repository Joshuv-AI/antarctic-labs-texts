36 U.S.C. § 150706 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:150706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section150706
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1507 (National Conference On Citizenship). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
adopt and amend bylaws and regulations for the management of its property and the regulation of its affairs, including the establishment and maintenance of local and State conferences on citizenship;
(2)
adopt and alter a corporate seal;
(3)
adopt emblems and badges;
(4)
choose officers, managers, employees, and agents as the activities of the corporation require;
(5)
make contracts;
(6)
acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property as necessary to carry out the purposes of the corporation;
(7)
borrow money, issue instruments of indebtedness, and secure its obligations by granting security interests in its property;
(8)
use corporate funds to give prizes or awards to citizens for outstanding contributions toward the achievement of the purposes of the corporation;
(9)
publish a magazine and other publications consistent with the purposes of the corporation;
(10)
sue and be sued; and
(11)
do any other act necessary and proper to carry out the purposes of the corporation.
