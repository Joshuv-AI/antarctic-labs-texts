45 C.F.R. § 1356.10 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1356.10
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1356.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1356 (Requirements Applicable To Title Iv-E). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to title IV-E agency programs for foster care maintenance payments, adoption assistance payments, related foster care and adoption administrative and training expenditures, and the independent living services program under title IV-E of the Act.
