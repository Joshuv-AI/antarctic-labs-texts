30 C.F.R. § 845.2 — Objective.
Citation: us:cfr:30:845.2
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-845.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 845 (Civil Penalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Civil penalties are assessed under section 518 of the Act and this part to deter violations and to ensure maximum compliance with the terms and purposes of the Act on the part of the coal mining industry.
