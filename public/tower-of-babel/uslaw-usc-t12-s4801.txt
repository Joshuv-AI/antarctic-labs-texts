12 U.S.C. § 4801 — Incorporated definitions

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4801
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 48 (Financial Institutions Regulatory Improvement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unless otherwise specifically provided in this chapter, for purposes of this chapter—
(1)
the terms “appropriate Federal banking agency”, “Federal banking agencies”, “insured depository institution”, and “State bank supervisor” have the same meanings as in section 1813 of this title; and
(2)
the term “insured credit union” has the same meaning as in section 1752 of this title.
