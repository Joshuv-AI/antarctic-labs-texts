The goal of this paper is an extension of the Weierstrass inequality in a different way, as follows.

By substituting in inequality (

\prod_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}\geq\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}-(n-1),\ with\ x_{i}\in(0,1],\ n\geq 2.

(3)

To simplify our calculations, we consider an inequality equivalent to (

x+y-1\leq xy,\ with\ x,y\in(0,1].

Let us note that

inequality\ (\ref{W})\ also\ holds\ for\ x,y\in[1,\infty)\ (\mbox{see}\ \cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{}{Finol-Wojt}{}{}, \rm{Theorem \ 5}]}).
