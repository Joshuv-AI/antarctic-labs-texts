49 C.F.R. § 198.57 — What is the process PHMSA will use to notify a State that its damage prevention enforcement program appears to be inadequate?
Citation: us:cfr:49:198.57
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-198.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 198 (Regulations For Grants To Aid State Pipeline Safety Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

PHMSA will issue a notice of inadequacy to the State in accordance with 49 CFR 190.5. The notice will state the basis for PHMSA's determination that the State's damage prevention enforcement program appears inadequate for purposes of this subpart and set forth the State's response options.
