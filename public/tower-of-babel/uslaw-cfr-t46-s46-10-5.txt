46 C.F.R. § 46.10-5 — -5 Load line requirements for subdivision.
Citation: us:cfr:46:46.10-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-46.10-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 46 (Subdivision Load Lines For Passenger Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The load line requirements of parts 42, 44, 45 of this subchapter as applicable to the passenger vessel and her service, shall be complied with before a passenger vessel will be marked with and certificated as to subdivision load lines.
