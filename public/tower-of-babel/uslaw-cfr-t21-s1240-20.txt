21 C.F.R. § 1240.20 — Issuance and posting of certificates following inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1240.20
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1240.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1240 (Control Of Communicable Diseases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commissioner of Food and Drugs may issue certificates based upon inspections provided for in this part and part 1250. Such certificates shall be prominently posted on conveyances.
