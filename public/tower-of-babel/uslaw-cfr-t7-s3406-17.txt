7 C.F.R. § 3406.17 — Program application materials—research.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3406.17
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3406.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3406 (1890 Institution Capacity Building Grants Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Program application materials in an application package will be made available to eligible institutions upon request. These materials include the program announcement, the administrative provisions for the program, and the forms needed to prepare and submit research grant applications under the program.
