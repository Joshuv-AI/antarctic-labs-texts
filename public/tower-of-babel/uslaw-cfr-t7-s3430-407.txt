7 C.F.R. § 3430.407 — Program requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3430.407
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3430.407
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3430 (Competitive And Noncompetitive Non-Formula Federal Assistance Programs—General Award Administrative Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Following the completion of a peer review process for grant proposals received under this subpart, the Director may provide a priority for those proposals, found in the peer review process to be scientifically meritorious, that involve the cooperation of multiple entities.
