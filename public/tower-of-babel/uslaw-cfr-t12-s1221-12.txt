12 C.F.R. § 1221.12 — Capital.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1221.12
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1221.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1221 (Margin And Capital Requirements For Covered Swap Entities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A covered swap entity shall comply with the capital levels or such other amounts applicable to it as required by the Director of FHFA pursuant to 12 U.S.C. 4611.
