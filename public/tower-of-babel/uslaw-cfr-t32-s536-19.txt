32 C.F.R. § 536.19 — Disaster claims planning.
Citation: us:cfr:32:536.19
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-536.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 536 (Claims Against The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All ACOs will prepare a disaster claims plan and furnish a copy to USARCS. See DA Pam 27-162, paragraph 1-21 for specific requirements related to disaster claims planning.
