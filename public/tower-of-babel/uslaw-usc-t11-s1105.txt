11 U.S.C. § 1105 — Termination of trustee’s appointment
Citation: us:usc:11:1105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1105
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 11 (Reorganization); Subchapter I (Officers And Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

At any time before confirmation of a plan, on request of a party in interest or the United States trustee, and after notice and a hearing, the court may terminate the trustee’s appointment and restore the debtor to possession and management of the property of the estate and of the operation of the debtor’s business.
