29 C.F.R. § 102.108 — Response to petition; service of response.
Citation: us:cfr:29:102.108
Creator: National Labor Relations Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-102.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 102 (Rules And Regulations, Series 8). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party to the representation or unfair labor practice case may, within 14 days after service, respond to the petition, admitting or denying its allegations. The response must be filed with the Board in Washington, DC. The response must be served on the General Counsel and all other parties, and a statement of service must be filed as provided by § 102.5(h).
