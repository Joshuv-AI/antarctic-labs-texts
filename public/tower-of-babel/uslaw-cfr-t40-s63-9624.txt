40 C.F.R. § 63.9624 — How do I demonstrate initial compliance with the work practice standards that apply to me?
Citation: us:cfr:40:63.9624
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-63.9624
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 63 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants For Source Categories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must demonstrate initial compliance with the work practice standards by meeting the requirements in paragraphs (a) through (c) of this section.
(a) You must prepare a fugitive dust emissions control plan in accordance with the requirements in § 63.9591.
(b) You must submit to the Administrator the fugitive dust emissions control plan in accordance with the requirements in § 63.9591.
(c) You must implement each control practice according to the procedures specified in your fugitive dust emissions control plan.
