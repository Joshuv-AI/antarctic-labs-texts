25 U.S.C. § 410 — Moneys from lease or sale of trust lands not liable for certain debts
Citation: us:usc:25:410
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section410
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 12 (Lease, Sale, Or Surrender Of Allotted Or Unallotted Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No money accruing from any lease or sale of lands held in trust by the United States for any Indian shall become liable for the payment of any debt of, or claim against, such Indian contracted or arising during such trust period, or, in case of a minor, during his minority, except with the approval and consent of the Secretary of the Interior.
