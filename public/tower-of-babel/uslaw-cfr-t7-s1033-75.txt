7 C.F.R. § 1033.75 — Plant location adjustments for producer milk and nonpool milk.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1033.75
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1033.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1033 (Milk In The Mideast Marketing Area). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of making payments for producer milk and nonpool milk, a plant location adjustment shall be determined by subtracting the Class I price specified in § 1033.51 from the Class I price at the plant's location. The difference, plus or minus as the case may be, shall be used to adjust the payments required pursuant to §§ 1033.73 and 1000.76.
