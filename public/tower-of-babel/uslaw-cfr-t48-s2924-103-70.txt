48 C.F.R. § 2924.103-70 — 2924.103-70 Contract clause.

Creator: Chapter 29—Department Of Labor
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2924 (Protection Of Privacy And Freedom Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officers shall insert the clause at DOLAR 2952.224-70, Privacy Breach Notification Requirements, in all solicitations and contracts except solicitations and contracts that are solely for the acquisition of commercially available off-the-shelf items.
