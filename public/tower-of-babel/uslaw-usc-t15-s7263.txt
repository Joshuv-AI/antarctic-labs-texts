15 U.S.C. § 7263 — Exemption
Citation: us:usc:15:7263
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section7263
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 98 (Public Company Accounting Reform And Corporate Responsibility); Subchapter IV (Enhanced Financial Disclosures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in section 401, 402, or 404, the amendments made by those sections, or the rules of the Commission under those sections shall apply to any investment company registered under section 80a–8 of this title.
