12 C.F.R. § 192.320 — Order of priority to purchase conversion shares.
Citation: us:cfr:12:192.320
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-192.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 192 (Conversions From Mutual To Stock Form). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A savings association must offer to sell its shares in the following order:
(a) Eligible account holders.
(b) Tax-qualified employee stock ownership plans.
(c) Supplemental eligible account holders.
(d) Other voting members who have subscription rights.
(e) The savings association's community, its community and the general public, or the general public.
