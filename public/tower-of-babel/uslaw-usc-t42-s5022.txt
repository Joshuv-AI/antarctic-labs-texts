42 U.S.C. § 5022 — Payments; adjustments; advances or reimbursement; installments; conditions
Citation: us:usc:42:5022
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5022
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 66 (Domestic Volunteer Services); Subchapter II (National Senior Service Corps); Part D (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Payments under this subchapter pursuant to a grant or contract may be made (after necessary adjustment, in the case of grants, on account of previously made overpayments or underpayments) in advance or by way of reimbursement, in such installments and on such conditions, as the Director may determine.
