49 C.F.R. § 557.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:557.2
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-557.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 557 (Petitions For Hearings On Notification And Remedy Of Defects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to enable the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration to identify and respond on a timely basis to petitions for hearings on whether a manufacturer has reasonably met his obligation to notify or remedy, and to establish the procedures for such hearings.
