Why Trees and Cycles?

To motivate our focus on trees and cycles, we briefly survey some key results on the

If

In 1961

Berge noted that for any

Berge conjectured that a graph is perfect precisely if this property holds. This celebrated conjecture, known as the Strong Perfect Graph Conjecture, was proven in 2002 by Chudnovsky, Robertson, Seymour, and Thomas

Motivated by the Strong Perfect Graph Conjecture, in 1987 Gyárfás wrote a paper entitled

[

He recalled a result of Erdős, which states that for every
