15 C.F.R. § 905.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:15:905.1
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-905.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 905 (Use In Enforcement Proceedings Of Information Collected By Voluntary Fishery Data Collectors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to the use, in enforcement proceedings conducted pursuant to the Magnuson Act, the MMPA, and the ESA, of information collected by voluntary fishery data collectors.
