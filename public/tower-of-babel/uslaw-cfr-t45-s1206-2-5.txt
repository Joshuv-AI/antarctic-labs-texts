45 C.F.R. § 1206.2-5 — -5 Right to counsel.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1206.2-5
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1206.2-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1206 (Grants And Contracts—Suspension And Termination And Denial Of Application For Refunding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In all formal or informal proceedings under this subpart, the recipient and the Corporation shall have the right to be represented by counsel or other authorized representatives, at their own expense.
