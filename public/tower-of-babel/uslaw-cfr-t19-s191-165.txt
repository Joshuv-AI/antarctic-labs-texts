19 C.F.R. § 191.165 — No exportation by mail.
Citation: us:cfr:19:191.165
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-191.165
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 191 (Drawback). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Merchandise covered by this subpart shall not be exported by mail.
