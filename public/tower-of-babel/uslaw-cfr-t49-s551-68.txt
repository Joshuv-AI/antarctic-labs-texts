49 C.F.R. § 551.68 — What if an agent cannot be served?
Citation: us:cfr:49:551.68
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-551.68
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 551 (Procedural Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an agent cannot be served because the agent cannot be located, has ceased to exist or does not receive correctly addressed mail, service may be made by posting the notice or process in the Office of the Secretary of Transportation.
