5 C.F.R. § 875.410 — May I continue my coverage when I leave Federal or military service?
Citation: us:cfr:5:875.410
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-875.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 875 (Federal Long Term Care Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are an active workforce member, your coverage will automatically continue when you leave active service, as long as the Carrier continues to receive the required premium when due.
