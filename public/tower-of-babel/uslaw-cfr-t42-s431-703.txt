42 C.F.R. § 431.703 — Licensing requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:42:431.703
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-431.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 431 (State Organization And General Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State licensing program must provide that only nursing homes supervised by an administrator licensed in accordance with the requirements of this subpart may operate in the State.
