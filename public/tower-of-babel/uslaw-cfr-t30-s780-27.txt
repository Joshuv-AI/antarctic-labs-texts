30 C.F.R. § 780.27 — Reclamation plan: Surface mining near underground mining.
Citation: us:cfr:30:780.27
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-780.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 780 (Surface Mining Permit Applications—Minimum Requirement For Reclamation And Operation Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For surface mining activities within the proposed permit area to be conducted within 500 feet of an underground mine, the application shall describe the measures to be used to comply with 30 CFR 816.79.
