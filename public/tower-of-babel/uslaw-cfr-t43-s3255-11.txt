43 C.F.R. § 3255.11 — When I submit confidential, proprietary information, how can I help ensure it is not available to the public?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3255.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3255.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When you submit data and information that you believe to be exempt from disclosure by 43 CFR part 2, you must clearly mark each page that you believe contains confidential information. BLM will keep all data and information confidential to the extent allowed by 43 CFR 2.13(c).
