48 C.F.R. § 1204.103 — 1204.103 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1204.103
Creator: Chapter 12—Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1204.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1204 (Administrative And Information Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at FAR 52.204-1, Approval of Contract, filled in as appropriate, in solicitations and contracts when approval to award the resulting contract must be obtained from an official at a level above the contracting officer.
