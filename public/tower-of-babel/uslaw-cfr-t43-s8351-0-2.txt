43 C.F.R. § 8351.0-2 — -2 Objective.
Citation: us:cfr:43:8351.0-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-8351.0-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 8350 (Management Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To assure that all public lands administered under provisions of the Wild and Scenic Rivers Act and the National Trails System Act are managed in a manner consistent with the purposes of these Acts.
