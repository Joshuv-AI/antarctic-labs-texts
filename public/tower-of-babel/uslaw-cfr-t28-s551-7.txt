28 C.F.R. § 551.7 — Bathing and clothing.
Citation: us:cfr:28:551.7
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-551.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 551 (Miscellaneous). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each inmate must observe the standards concerning bathing and clothing that exist in the institution as required by standards of § 551.1.
