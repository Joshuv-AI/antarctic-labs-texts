43 C.F.R. § 34.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:43:34.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-34.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 34 (Requirements For Equal Opportunity During Construction And Operation Of The Alaska Natural Gas Transportation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations apply to all activities including, but not limited to, contracting for goods and services, employment, and any other benefits that flow from activities conducted under permits, rights-of-way, public land orders, and other Federal authorizations granted or issued pursuant to ANGTA, by recipients of those authorizations, their agents, contractors, and subcontractors, including labor unions or other persons.
