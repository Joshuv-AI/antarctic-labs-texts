7 C.F.R. § 3570.69 — Environmental review requirements, intergovernmental review, and public notification.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3570.69
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3570.69
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3570 (Community Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Grants awarded under this subpart, including grant-only awards, must be in compliance with the environmental review requirements in accordance with 7 CFR part 1970, to the intergovernmental review requirements of 7 CFR 3015, subpart V and RD Instruction 1970-I, “Intergovernmental Review,” and the public information process in 7 CFR 1942.17(j)(9).
