31 C.F.R. § 566.320 — U.S. financial institution.
Citation: us:cfr:31:566.320
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-566.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 566 (Hizballah Financial Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term U.S. financial institution means a financial institution located in or organized under the laws of the United States or any jurisdiction within the United States.
