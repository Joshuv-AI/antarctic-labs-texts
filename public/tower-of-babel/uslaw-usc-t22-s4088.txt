22 U.S.C. § 4088 — Education allowance
Citation: us:usc:22:4088
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4088
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter IX (Travel, Leave, And Other Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A Department or United States Agency for International Development employee who is on leave to perform service in the uniformed services (as defined in section 4303(13) of title 38) may receive an education allowance if the employee would, if not for such service, be eligible to receive the education allowance.
