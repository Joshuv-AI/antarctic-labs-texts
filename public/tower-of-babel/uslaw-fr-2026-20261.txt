DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-22-2026]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 153; Authorization of Limited Production 
Activity; CMS Circuits, Inc.; (Electronic Manufacturing Services in 
Aerospace, Industrial, and Medical Applications); Murrieta, California

    On February 23, 2026, the City of San Diego, grantee of FTZ 153, 
submitted a notification of proposed production activity to the FTZ 
Board on behalf of CMS Circuits, Inc., within FTZ 153, in Murrieta, 
California.
    The notification was processed in accordance with the regulations 
of the FTZ Board (15 CFR part 400), including notice in the Federal 
Register inviting public comment (91 FR 10053, March 2, 2026). On 
September 29, 2026, the applicant was notified of the FTZ Board's 
decision that further review of part of the proposed activity is 
warranted. The FTZ Board authorized the production activity described 
in the notification on a limited basis, subject to the FTZ Act and the 
Board's regulations, including section 400.14, and further subject to a 
two-year time limit for admitting fiber optic bundles to the zone under 
foreign status.

    Dated: September 29, 2026.
Colin Agostisi,
Executive Secretary.
