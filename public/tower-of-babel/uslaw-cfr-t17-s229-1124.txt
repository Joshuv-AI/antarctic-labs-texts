17 C.F.R. § 229.1124 — (Item 1124) Sponsor interest in the securities.
Citation: us:cfr:17:229.1124
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-229.1124
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 229 (Standard Instructions For Filing Forms Under Securities Act Of 1933, Securities Exchange Act Of 1934 And Energy Policy And Conservation Act Of 1975—Regulation S-K). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Provide information about any material change in the sponsor's, or an affiliate's, interest in the securities resulting from the purchase, sale or other acquisition or disposition of the securities by the sponsor, or an affiliate, during the period covered by the report. Describe the change, including the amount of change and the sponsor's, or the affiliate's, resulting interest in the transaction after the change.
