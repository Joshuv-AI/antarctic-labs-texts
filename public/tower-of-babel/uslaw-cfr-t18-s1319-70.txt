18 C.F.R. § 1319.70 — General and class reviews.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1319 (Floodplains And Wetlands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).
