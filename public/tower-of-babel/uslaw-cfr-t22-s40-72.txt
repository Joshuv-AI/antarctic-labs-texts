22 C.F.R. § 40.72 — Documentation requirements for nonimmigrants.
Citation: us:cfr:22:40.72
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-40.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 40 (Regulations Pertaining To Both Nonimmigrants And Immigrants Under The Immigration And Nationality Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A passport which is valid indefinitely for the return of the bearer to the country whose government issued such passport shall be deemed to have the required minimum period of validity as specified in INA 212(a)(7)(B).
