43 C.F.R. § 4300.44 — Are there any major restrictions on my grazing permit that I might otherwise think are allowed?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4300.44
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4300.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4300 (Grazing Administration; Alaska; Reindeer; General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. You must not:
(a) Enclose roads, trails and highways as to disturb public travel there;
(b) Interfere with existing communication lines or other improvements;
(c) Prevent legal hunting, fishing or trapping on the land;
(d) Prevent access by persons, such as miners and mineral prospectors, entitled to lawfully enter; or
(e) Graze reindeer without complying with applicable State and Federal laws on livestock quarantine and sanitation.
