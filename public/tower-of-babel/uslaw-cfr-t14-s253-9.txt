14 C.F.R. § 253.9 — Retroactive changes to contracts of carriage.
Citation: us:cfr:14:253.9
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-253.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 253 (Notice Of Terms Of Contract Of Carriage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An air carrier may not retroactively apply to persons who have already bought a ticket any material amendment to its contract of carriage that has significant negative implications for consumers.
