48 C.F.R. § 536.7104 — 536.7104 Construction contract award.
Citation: us:cfr:48:536.7104
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-536.7104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 536 (Construction And Architect-Engineer Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with FAR 4.1001, the contracting officer shall use the SF 1442 to identify the services or supplies to be acquired as separately identified line items on a unit price or lump sum basis including the design phase services, the construction work GMP option(s), and any other work not included in the previously identified items.
