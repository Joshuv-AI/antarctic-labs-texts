12 C.F.R. § 602.16 — Combining requests.
Citation: us:cfr:12:602.16
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-602.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 602 (Releasing Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may not avoid paying fees by filing multiple requests at the same time. When FCA reasonably believes that you, alone or with others, are breaking down one request into a series of requests to avoid fees, we will combine the requests and charge accordingly.
