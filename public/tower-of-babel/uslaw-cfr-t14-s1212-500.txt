14 C.F.R. § 1212.500 — Exemptions under 5 U.S.C. 552a (j) and (k).
Citation: us:cfr:14:1212.500
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1212.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1212 (Privacy Act—Nasa Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) These provisions authorize the Administrator of NASA to exempt certain NASA Privacy Act systems of records from portions of the requirements of this regulation.
(b) The Administrator has delegated this authority to the Associate Administrator (See § 1212.701).
(c) For those NASA systems of records that are determined to be exempt, the system notice shall describe the exemption and the reasons.
