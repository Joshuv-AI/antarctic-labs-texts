26 C.F.R. § 1.662(a)-1 — (a)-1 Inclusion of amounts in gross income of beneficiaries of estates and complex trusts; general.
Citation: us:cfr:26:1.662(a)-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.662(a)-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 1 (Income Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There is included in the gross income of a beneficiary of an estate or complex trust the sum of:
(a) Amounts of income required to be distributed currently to him, and
(b) All other amounts properly paid, credited, or required to be distributed to him
