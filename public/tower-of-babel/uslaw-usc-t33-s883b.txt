33 U.S.C. § 883b — Dissemination of data; further activities
Citation: us:usc:33:883b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section883b
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 17 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration); Subchapter II (Surveys). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In order that full public benefit may be derived from the operations of the National Ocean Survey by the dissemination of data resulting from the activities herein authorized and of related data from other sources, the Secretary of Commerce is authorized to conduct the following activities:
(1)
Analysis and prediction of tide and current data;
(2)
Processing and publication of data, information, compilations, and reports;
(3)
Compilation and printing of nautical charts;
(4)
Distribution of nautical charts and related navigational publications.
