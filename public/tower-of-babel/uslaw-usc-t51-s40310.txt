51 U.S.C. § 40310 — Designation or award to be on competitive basis
Citation: us:usc:51:40310
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section40310
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 403 (National Space Grant College And Fellowship Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall not under this chapter designate any space grant college or space grant regional consortium or award any fellowship, grant, or contract unless such designation or award is made in accordance with the competitive, merit-based review process employed by the Administration on October 30, 1987.
