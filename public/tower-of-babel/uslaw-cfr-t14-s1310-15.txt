14 C.F.R. § 1310.15 — Amendment or Waiver of a term or condition of a guaranteed loan.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1310.15
Creator: Air Transportation System Stabilization
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1310.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1310 (Air Carrier Guarantee Loan Program Administrative Regulations And Amendment Or Waiver Of A Term Or Condition Of Guaranteed Loan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may, in its discretion, charge the borrower a fee, in an amount and payable as determined by the Board, for each amendment to, or waiver of, any term or condition of any guaranteed loan document or related instrument approved by the Board.
