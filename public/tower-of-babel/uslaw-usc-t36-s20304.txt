36 U.S.C. § 20304 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:20304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section20304
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 203 (American Academy Of Arts And Letters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
adopt bylaws and regulations;
(2)
fill vacancies;
(3)
provide for the election of foreign, domestic, or honorary associate members, and the division of those members into classes;
(4)
receive bequests and donations of property, hold the property in trust, and invest the property to carry out the purpose of the corporation; and
(5)
do any other act necessary or usual for such a corporation.
