28 U.S.C. § 567 — Collection of fees; accounting
Citation: us:usc:28:567
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section567
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 37 (United States Marshals Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Each United States marshal shall collect, as far as possible, his lawful fees and account for the same as public moneys.
(b)
The marshal’s accounts of fees and costs paid to a witness or juror on certificate of attendance issued as provided by sections 1825 and 1871 of this title may not be reexamined to charge him for an erroneous payment of the fees or costs.
