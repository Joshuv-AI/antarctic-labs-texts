46 U.S.C. § 31103 — Applicable procedure
Citation: us:usc:46:31103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section31103
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 311 (Suits Involving Public Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A civil action under this chapter is subject to the provisions of chapter 309 of this title except to the extent inconsistent with this chapter.
