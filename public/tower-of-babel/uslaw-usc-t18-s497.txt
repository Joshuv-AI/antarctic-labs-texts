18 U.S.C. § 497 — Letters patent
Citation: us:usc:18:497
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section497
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 25 (Counterfeiting And Forgery). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever falsely makes, forges, counterfeits, or alters any letters patent granted or purporting to have been granted by the President of the United States; or
Whoever passes, utters, or publishes, or attempts to pass, utter, or publish as genuine, any such letters patent, knowing the same to be forged, counterfeited or falsely altered—
Shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than ten years, or both.
