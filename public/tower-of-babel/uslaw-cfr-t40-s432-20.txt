40 C.F.R. § 432.20 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:432.20
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-432.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 432 (Meat And Poultry Products Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to discharges of process wastewater associated with the production of meat carcasses, in whole or in part, by complex slaughterhouses. Process wastewater includes water from animal holding areas at these facilities.
