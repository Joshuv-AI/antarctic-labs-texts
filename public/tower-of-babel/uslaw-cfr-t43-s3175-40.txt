43 C.F.R. § 3175.40 — Measurement equipment approved by standard or make and model.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3175.40
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3175.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3170 (Onshore Oil And Gas Production). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The measurement equipment described in §§ 3175.41 through 3175.49 is approved for use at FMPs under the conditions and circumstances stated in those sections, provided it meets or exceeds the minimum standards prescribed in this subpart.
