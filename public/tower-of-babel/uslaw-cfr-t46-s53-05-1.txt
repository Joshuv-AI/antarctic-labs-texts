46 C.F.R. § 53.05-1 — -1 Safety valve requirements for steam boilers (modifies HG-400 and HG-401).
Citation: us:cfr:46:53.05-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-53.05-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 53 (Heating Boilers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The pressure relief valve requirements and the safety valve requirements for steam boilers must be as indicated in HG-400 and HG-401 of Section IV of the ASME BPVC (incorporated by reference; see § 53.01-1) except as noted otherwise in this section.
(b) Each steam boiler must have at least one safety valve.
