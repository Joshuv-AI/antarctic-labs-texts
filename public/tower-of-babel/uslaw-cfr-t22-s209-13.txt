22 C.F.R. § 209.13 — Delegation of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:22:209.13
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-209.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 209 (Non-Discrimination In Federally-Assisted Programs Of The Agency For International Development—Effectuation Of Title Vi Of The Civil Rights Act Of 1964). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Responsibility for administration and enforcement of this part, with respect to Federal financial assistance administered by another Federal department or agency pursuant to delegation, transfer interagency service agreement, or other arrangement is vested in the head of such department or agency, or his delegate, and subject to such delegations or redelegations as he may make or authorize.
