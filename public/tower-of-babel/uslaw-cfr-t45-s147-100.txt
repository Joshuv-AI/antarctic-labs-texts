45 C.F.R. § 147.100 — Basis and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:45:147.100
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-147.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 147 (Health Insurance Reform Requirements For The Group And Individual Health Insurance Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 147 of this subchapter implements the requirements of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act that apply to group health plans and health insurance issuers in the Group and Individual markets.
