31 C.F.R. § 5.6 — When will Treasury entities allow a debtor to pay a Treasury debt in installments instead of one lump sum?
Citation: us:cfr:31:5.6
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-5.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 5 (Treasury Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a debtor is financially unable to pay the debt in one lump sum, a Treasury entity may accept payment of a Treasury debt in regular installments, in accordance with the provisions of 31 CFR 901.8 and the Treasury entity's policies and procedures.
