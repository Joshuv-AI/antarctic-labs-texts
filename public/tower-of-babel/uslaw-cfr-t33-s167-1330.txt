33 C.F.R. § 167.1330 — In Haro Strait, Boundary Pass, and the Strait of Georgia: General.
Citation: us:cfr:33:167.1330
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-167.1330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 167 (Offshore Traffic Separation Schemes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The traffic separation scheme in Haro Strait, Boundary Pass, and the Strait of Georgia consists of a series of traffic separation schemes, two-way routes, and five precautionary areas. These parts are described in §§ 167.1331 and 167.1332. The geographic coordinates in §§ 167.1331 and 167.1332 are defined using North American Datum (NAD 83).
