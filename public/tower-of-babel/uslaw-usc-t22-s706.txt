22 U.S.C. § 706 — Operative effect dependent upon Presidential finding
Citation: us:usc:22:706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section706
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 13 (Service Courts Of Friendly Foreign Forces). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall be operative with respect to the military, naval, or air forces of any foreign state only after a finding and declaration by the President that the powers and privileges provided herein are necessary for the maintenance of discipline. The President may at any time revoke such finding and declaration.
