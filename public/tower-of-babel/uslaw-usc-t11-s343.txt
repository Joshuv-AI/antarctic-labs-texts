11 U.S.C. § 343 — Examination of the debtor
Citation: us:usc:11:343
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section343
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 3 (Case Administration); Subchapter III (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The debtor shall appear and submit to examination under oath at the meeting of creditors under section 341(a) of this title. Creditors, any indenture trustee, any trustee or examiner in the case, or the United States trustee may examine the debtor. The United States trustee may administer the oath required under this section.
