28 U.S.C. § 582 — Assistant United States trustees
Citation: us:usc:28:582
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section582
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 39 (United States Trustees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Attorney General may appoint one or more assistant United States trustees in any region when the public interest so requires.
(b)
Each assistant United States trustee is subject to removal by the Attorney General.
