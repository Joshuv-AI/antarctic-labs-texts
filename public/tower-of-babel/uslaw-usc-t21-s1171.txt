21 U.S.C. § 1171 — Drug abuse prevention function appropriations
Citation: us:usc:21:1171
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section1171
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 16 (Drug Abuse Prevention, Treatment, And Rehabilitation); Subchapter IV (Other Federal Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any request for appropriations by a department or agency of the Government submitted after March 21, 1972, shall specify (1) on a line item basis, that part of the appropriations which the department or agency is requesting to carry out its drug abuse prevention functions, and (2) the authorization of the appropriations requested to carry out each of its drug abuse prevention functions.
