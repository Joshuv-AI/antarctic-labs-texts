37 C.F.R. § 11.305 — Impartiality and decorum of the tribunal.
Citation: us:cfr:37:11.305
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-11.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 11 (Representation Of Others Before The United States Patent And Trademark Office). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A practitioner shall not:
(a) Seek to influence a judge, hearing officer, administrative law judge, administrative patent judge, administrative trademark judge, juror, prospective juror, employee or officer of the Office, or other official by means prohibited by law;
(b) Communicate ex parte with such a person during the proceeding unless authorized to do so by law, rule or court order; or
(c) [Reserved]
(d) Engage in conduct intended to disrupt any proceeding before a tribunal.
