28 C.F.R. § 97.16 — Clothing requirements for transported violent prisoners.
Citation: us:cfr:28:97.16
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-97.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 97 (Standards For Private Entities Providing Prisoner Or Detainee Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Companies covered under this part must ensure that all violent prisoners they transport are clothed in brightly colored clothing that clearly identifies them as violent prisoners, unless security or other specific considerations make such a requirement inappropriate.
