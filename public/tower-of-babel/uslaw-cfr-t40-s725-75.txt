40 C.F.R. § 725.75 — Inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:40:725.75
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-725.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 725 (Reporting Requirements And Review Processes For Microorganisms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

EPA will conduct inspections under section 11 of the Act to assure compliance with section 5 of the Act and this part, to verify that information required by EPA under this part is true and correct, and to audit data submitted to EPA under this part.
