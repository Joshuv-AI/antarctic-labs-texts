Fisheries of the Exclusive Economic Zone Off Alaska; Exchange of 
Flatfish in the Bering Sea and Aleutian Islands Management Area

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Temporary rule; reallocation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

[[Page 62342]]

SUMMARY: NMFS is exchanging unused yellowfin sole and flathead sole 
Community Development Quota (CDQ) for rock sole CDQ acceptable 
biological catch (ABC) reserves in the Bering Sea and Aleutian Islands 
(BSAI) management area. This action is necessary to allow the CDQ 
fishery to optimize their total rock sole harvest in the BSAI.

DATES: Effective October 1, 2026, through December 31, 2026.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Steve Whitney, 907-586-7228.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: NMFS manages the groundfish fishery in the 
BSAI according to the Fishery Management Plan for Groundfish of the 
BSAI Management Area (FMP) prepared and recommended by the North 
Pacific Fishery Management Council under authority of the Magnuson-
Stevens Fishery Conservation and Management Act (Magnuson-Stevens Act). 
Regulations governing fishing by U.S. vessels in accordance with the 
FMP appear at subpart H of 50 CFR part 600 and 50 CFR part 679.
    The 2026 flathead sole, rock sole, and yellowfin sole CDQ 
allocations of the TACs specified for these species in the BSAI are 
3,852 metric tons (mt), 8,025 mt, and 15,515 mt, respectively, as 
established by the final 2026 and 2027 harvest specifications for 
groundfish in the BSAI (91 FR 11750, March 10, 2026). The 2026 flathead 
sole, rock sole, and yellowfin sole CDQ ABC reserves are 5,532 mt, 
8,905 mt, and 13,122 mt, respectively, as established by the final 2026 
and 2027 harvest specifications for groundfish in the BSAI (91 FR 
11750, March 10, 2026).
    The Norton Sound Economic Development Corporation (NSEDC) has 
requested that NMFS exchange 200 mt of flathead sole TAC and 700 mt of 
yellowfin sole TAC allocated to the CDQ sector for 900 mt of rock sole 
CDQ ABC reserves pursuant to Sec. Sec.  679.31(d) and 679.4(p). NMFS 
approves the completed Flatfish Exchange Application submitted by 
NSEDC, which meets all the criteria specified at Sec.  679.4(p)(3). 
Specifically, NSEDC has sufficient CDQ ABC reserves of rock sole and is 
exchanging an equal amount of unused flathead sole and yellowfin sole 
TACs allocated to the CDQ sector. Approval will not cause harvest to 
exceed an ABC or ABC reserve for any of these species, NSEDC has not 
received at least three approved flatfish exchanges, and NMFS can 
approve the exchange before the end of the calendar year in which it 
would be effective. Therefore, NMFS exchanges 200 mt of flathead sole 
TAC and 700 mt of yellowfin sole TAC allocated to the CDQ sector for 
900 mt of rock sole CDQ ABC reserves in the BSAI. This action also 
decreases and increases the TACs and CDQ ABC reserves by the 
corresponding amounts. Tables 13 and 15 of the final 2026 and 2027 
harvest specifications for groundfish in the BSAI (91 FR 11750, March 
10, 2026) are revised as follows:

    Fishermen are reminded that all actions and closures remain in full 
force and effect. This reallocation does not rescind or modify closures 
to directed fishing or actions prohibiting retention of a groundfish 
species.

Classification

    NMFS issues this action pursuant to section 305(d) of the Magnuson-
Stevens Act. This action is required by 50 CFR part 679, which was 
issued pursuant to

[[Page 62343]]

section 304(b) of the Magnuson-Stevens Act, and is exempt from review 
under Executive Order 12866.
    Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 553(b)(B), there is good cause to waive prior 
notice and an opportunity for public comment on this action, as notice 
and comment would be impracticable and contrary to the public interest, 
as it would prevent NMFS from responding to the most recent fisheries 
data in a timely fashion and would not allow for the ongoing harvest of 
rock sole in the BSAI by the CDQ sector. NMFS was unable to publish a 
notice providing time for public comment because the NSEDC Flatfish 
Exchange Application was received on September 18, 2026, and this is 
the most recent, relevant data on flathead sole, rock sole, and 
yellowfin. Without this inseason adjustment, NMFS could not allow the 
CDQ fishery for rock sole in the BSAI to be harvested in an expedient 
manner.
    There is good cause under 5 U.S.C. 553(d)(3) to waive the 30-day 
delay in the effective date of this action. This finding is based upon 
the reasons provided above for waiver of prior notice and opportunity 
for public comment.
