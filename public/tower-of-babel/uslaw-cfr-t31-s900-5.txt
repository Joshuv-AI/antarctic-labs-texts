31 C.F.R. § 900.5 — Form of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:31:900.5
Creator: Federal Claims Collection Standards (Department Of The Treasury—Department Of Justice)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-900.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 900 (Scope Of Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Claims may be paid in the form of money or, when a contractual basis exists, the Government may demand the return of specific property or the performance of specific services.
