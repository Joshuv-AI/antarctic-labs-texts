25 U.S.C. § 354 — Lands not liable for debts prior to final patent
Citation: us:usc:25:354
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section354
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 9 (Allotment Of Indian Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No lands acquired under the provisions of this Act shall, in any event, become liable to the satisfaction of any debt contracted prior to the issuing of the final patent in fee therefor.
