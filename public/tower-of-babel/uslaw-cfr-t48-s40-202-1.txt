48 C.F.R. § 40.202-1 — 40.202-1 Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:48:40.202-1
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-40.202-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 40 (Information Security And Supply Chain Security). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Section 40.202 prescribes policies and procedures regarding the procurement and operation of unmanned aircraft systems, which includes unmanned aircraft (i.e., drones) and associated elements.
(b) The authorities in 40.202 expire on December 22, 2028 (section 1833 of Pub. L. 118-31, 41 U.S.C. 3901 note prec.).
