16 U.S.C. § 410ii–7 — Authorization of appropriation
Citation: us:usc:16:410ii–7
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410ii–7
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX–G (Chaco Culture National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Effective October 1, 1981, there are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this subchapter but not to exceed $11,000,000 for acquisition and $500,000 for development.
