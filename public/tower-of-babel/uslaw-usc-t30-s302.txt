30 U.S.C. § 302 — Assignment of lease; subletting
Citation: us:usc:30:302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section302
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 5 (Lease Of Oil And Gas Deposits In Or Under Railroads And Other Rights-Of-Way). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The right conferred by this chapter may, subject to the approval of the Secretary of the Interior, be assigned or sublet by the owner thereof to any corporation, firm, association, or individual.
