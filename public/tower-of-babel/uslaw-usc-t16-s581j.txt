16 U.S.C. § 581j — Congressional declaration of policy on reforestation and revegetation
Citation: us:usc:16:581j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section581j
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3 (Forests; Forest Service; Reforestation; Management); Subchapter II (Investigations, Experiments, And Tests Affecting Reforestation And Forest Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the declared policy of the Congress to accelerate and provide a continuing basis for the needed reforestation and revegetation of national-forest lands and other lands under administration or control of the Forest Service of the Department of Agriculture in order to obtain the benefits hereinbefore enumerated.
