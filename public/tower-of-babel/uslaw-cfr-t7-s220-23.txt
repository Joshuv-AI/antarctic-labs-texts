7 C.F.R. § 220.23 — Seamless Summer Option non-congregate meal service.
Citation: us:cfr:7:220.23
Creator: Food And Nutrition Administration, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-220.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 220 (School Breakfast Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A school food authority participating in the National School Lunch Program's Seamless Summer Option, and which is approved to offer a non-congregate meal service, must comply with the provisions specified in § 210.34 of this chapter.
