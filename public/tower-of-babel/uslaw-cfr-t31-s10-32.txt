31 C.F.R. § 10.32 — Practice of law.
Citation: us:cfr:31:10.32
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-10.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 10 (Practice Before The Internal Revenue Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in the regulations in this part may be construed as authorizing persons not members of the bar to practice law.
