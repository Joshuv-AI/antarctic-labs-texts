29 C.F.R. § 4022.83 — PBGC reimbursement of benefit underpayments.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4022.83
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4022.83
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4022 (Benefits Payable In Terminated Single-Employer Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the PBGC determines that there has been a net benefit underpayment made with respect to a participant, it shall pay the participant or beneficiary the amount of the net underpayment, determined in accordance with § 4022.81(c), in a single payment.
