42 C.F.R. § 456.131 — Continued stay review required.
Citation: us:cfr:42:456.131
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-456.131
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 456 (Utilization Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The UR plan must provide for a review of each beneficiary's continued stay in the hospital to decide whether it is needed, in accordance with the requirements of §§ 456.132 through 456.137.
