20 C.F.R. § 418.3665 — Can your request for a hearing or case review be dismissed?
Citation: us:cfr:20:418.3665
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-418.3665
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 418 (Medicare Subsidies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will dismiss your request for a hearing or case review under any of the following conditions:
(a) At any time before notice of the decision is mailed, you ask that your request for administrative review be withdrawn; or
(b) You failed to request administrative review timely and did not have good cause for missing the deadline for requesting review.
