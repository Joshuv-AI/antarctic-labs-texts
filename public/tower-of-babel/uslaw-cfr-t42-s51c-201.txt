42 C.F.R. § 51c.201 — c.201 Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:42:51c.201
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-51c.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 51c (PART 51c—GRANTS FOR COMMUNITY HEALTH SERVICES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations of this subpart, in addition to the regulations of subpart A of this part, are applicable to grants awarded pursuant to section 330(c) of the Act for projects for planning and developing community health centers which will serve medically underserved populations.
