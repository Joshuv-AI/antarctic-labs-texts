43 C.F.R. § 3152.5 — Modification of exploration permit.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3152.5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3152.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3150 (Onshore Oil And Gas Geophysical Exploration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A permittee may request, and the authorized officer may approve a modification of an exploration permit.
(b) The authorized officer may, after consultation with the permittee, require modifications determined necessary.
