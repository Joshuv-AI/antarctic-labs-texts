46 U.S.C. § 107 — Exclusive economic zone
Citation: us:usc:46:107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section107
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 1 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this title, the term “exclusive economic zone” means the zone established by Presidential Proclamation 5030 of March 10, 1983 (16 U.S.C. 1453 note).
