42 U.S.C. § 1550 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:42:1550
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1550
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 9 (Housing Of Persons Engaged In National Defense); Subchapter IV (General Provisions Affecting Subchapters Ii To Vii). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of subchapters II to VII of this chapter, or the application thereof to any persons or circumstances, is held invalid, the remainder of said subchapters, or application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
