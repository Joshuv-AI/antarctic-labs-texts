10 U.S.C. § 140 — General Counsel
Citation: us:usc:10:140
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section140
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 4 (Office Of The Secretary Of Defense). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There is a General Counsel of the Department of Defense, appointed from civilian life by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.
(b)
The General Counsel is the chief legal officer of the Department of Defense. He shall perform such functions as the Secretary of Defense may prescribe.
