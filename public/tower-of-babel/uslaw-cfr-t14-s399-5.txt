14 C.F.R. § 399.5 — Arrangement of policy statements.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 399 (Statements Of General Policy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The statements of general policy relating to the various duties and functions of the Department are grouped according to subject matter in the following subparts; the titles of the subparts indicate the general subject matter included therein.
