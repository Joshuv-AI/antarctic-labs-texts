7 C.F.R. § 1948.96 — Audit requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1948.96
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1948.96
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1948 (Rural Development). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Audit requirements for Site Development and Acquisition Grants will be made in accordance with RD Instruction 1942-G.
(b) Audits for planning grants made in accordance with State statutes or regulatory agencies will be acceptable provided they are prepared in sufficient detail to permit Rural Development to determine that grant funds have been used in compliance with the proposal, any applicable laws and regulations, and the grant agreement. A copy of the audit shall be submitted to the State Director as soon as possible but in no case later than 90 days following the period covered by the grant.
