37 U.S.C. § 202 — Pay grades: retired Coast Guard rear admirals (lower half)
Citation: us:usc:37:202
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section202
Context: United States Code, Title 37 (Pay And Allowances Of The Uniformed Services); Chapter 3 (Basic Pay). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An officer of the Coast Guard holding a permanent appointment in the grade of rear admiral (lower half) on the retired list, and who in time of war or national emergency has served satisfactorily on active duty for two years in that grade or in a higher grade, is entitled when on active duty to the basic pay of a rear admiral.
