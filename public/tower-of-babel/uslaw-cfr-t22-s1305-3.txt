22 C.F.R. § 1305.3 — Production prohibited unless approved.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1305.3
Creator: Millennium Challenge Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1305.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1305 (Release Of Official Information And Testimony By Mcc Personnel As Witnesses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No employee or former employee shall, in response to a demand of a court or other authority, disclose any information relating to materials contained in the files of MCC, or disclose any information or produce any material acquired as part of the performance of the person's official duties, or because of the person's official status, without the prior, written approval of the General Counsel.
