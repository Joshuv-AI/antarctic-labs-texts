23 C.F.R. § 1225.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:1225.2
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration And Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-1225.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1225 (Operation Of Motor Vehicles By Intoxicated Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to specify the steps that States must take to qualify for incentive grant funds in accordance with 23 U.S.C. 163; and the steps that States must take to avoid the withholding of funds as required by Section 351 of Public Law 106-346—Appendix.
