48 C.F.R. § 1842.7002 — 1842.7002 Travel outside of the United States.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1842.7002
Creator: Chapter 18—National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1842.7002
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1842 (Contract Administration And Audit Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 1852.242-71, Travel Outside of the United States, in cost-reimbursement solicitations and contracts where a contractor may travel outside of the United States and it is appropriate to require Government approval of the travel.
