52 U.S.C. § 20987 — Consultation with Standards Board and Board of Advisors
Citation: us:usc:52:20987
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section20987
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 209 (Election Administration Improvement); Subchapter II (Commission); Part C (Studies And Other Activities To Promote Effective Administration Of Federal Elections). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission shall carry out its duties under this part in consultation with the Standards Board and the Board of Advisors.
