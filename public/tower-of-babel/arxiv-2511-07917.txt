Abstract
. We establish logical equivalence between statements involving • the Cuntz C ∗ C^{*} -algebra 𝒪 ∞ \mathcal{O}_{\infty} with its canonical diagonal; • graph C ∗ C^{*} -algebras with their canonical diagonals; • Leavitt path algebras over general fields with their canonical diagonals; • Leavitt path algebras over ℤ \mathbb{Z} ; • topological full groups; • groupoids; and • the automorphism x ↦ − x x\mapsto-x on certain K 0 K_{0} - and homology groups equal to ℤ \mathbb{Z} . Deciding whether these equivalent statements are true or false is of importance in studies of geometric classification of diagonal-preserving isomorphism between graph C ∗ C^{*} -algebras and Leavitt path algebras, mirroring a similar hindrance studied by Cuntz more than 40 years ago.

Lemma 2.4
.

Let
A
be a
*
-ring and let
D
be a commutative
*
-subring of
A
. Suppose

are projections in
D
such that
p_{i}p_{j}=0
for all

. Then
\left[\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_{i}\right)\otimes e_{1,1}\right]_{D^{s}}=\left[\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_{i}\otimes e_{i,i}\right]_{D^{s}}
.

Proof.

Set
S=\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_{i}\otimes e_{i,1}
. Then
S^{*}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_{i}\otimes e_{1,i}
. Note that
S,S^{*}
are elements of
\mathsf{M}_{\mathbb{N}}(A)
and

\displaystyle S^{*}S

\displaystyle=\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}p_{i}p_{j}\otimes e_{1,i}e_{j,1}

\displaystyle SS^{*}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}p_{i}p_{j}\otimes e_{i,j}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_{i}\otimes e_{1,1}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_{i}\otimes e_{i,i}.

\displaystyle=\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_{i}\right)\otimes e_{1,1}

Let
x=\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}a_{i}\otimes e_{i,i}\in D^{s}
, where all but finitely many of the
a_{i}
s are zero. Then

\displaystyle SxS^{*}

\displaystyle=\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}(p_{i}\otimes e_{i,1})x(p_{j}\otimes e_{1,j})=\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}(p_{i}\otimes e_{i,1})(a_{k}\otimes e_{k,k})(p_{j}\otimes e_{1,j})

\displaystyle=\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}p_{i}a_{1}p_{j}\otimes e_{i,j}=\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}p_{i}p_{j}a_{1}\otimes e_{i,j}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_{i}a_{1}\otimes e_{i,i}\in D^{s}\quad\text{and}

\displaystyle S^{*}xS

\displaystyle=\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}(p_{i}\otimes e_{1,i})x(p_{j}\otimes e_{j,1})=\sum_{i,j=1}^{n}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}(p_{i}\otimes e_{1,i})(a_{k}\otimes e_{k,k})(p_{j}\otimes e_{j,1})

\displaystyle=\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_{i}a_{i}p_{i}\otimes e_{1,1}\in D^{s}.

Therefore,
S\in\mathcal{N}_{s}^{*}(A,D)
which completes the proof that

\left[\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_{i}\right)\otimes e_{1,1}\right]_{D^{s}}=\left[\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_{i}\otimes e_{i,i}\right]_{D^{s}}.

∎
