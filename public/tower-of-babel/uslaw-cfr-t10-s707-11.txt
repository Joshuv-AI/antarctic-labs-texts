10 C.F.R. § 707.11 — Drugs for which testing is performed.
Citation: us:cfr:10:707.11
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-707.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 707 (Workplace Substance Abuse Programs At Doe Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where testing is performed under this part, at a minimum, contractors will be required to test for the use of the following drugs or classes of drugs: marijuana; cocaine; opiates; phencyclidine; and amphetamines. However, when conducting reasonable suspicion or occurrence testing, the contractor may test for any drug listed in Schedules I or II of the Controlled Substances Act.
