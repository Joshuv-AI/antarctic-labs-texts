24 C.F.R. § 908.112 — Extension of time.
Citation: us:cfr:24:908.112
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-908.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 908 (Electronic Transmission Of Required Family Data For Public Housing, Indian Housing, And The Section 8 Rental Voucher, And Moderate Rehabilitation Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The HUD Field Office may grant an HA an extension of time, of a reasonable period, for implementation of the requirements of § 908.104, if it determines that such electronic submission is infeasible because of one of the following:
(a) Lack of staff resources;
(b) Insufficient financial resources to purchase the required hardware, software or contractual services; or
(c) Lack of adequate infrastructure, including, but not limited to, the inability to obtain telephone service to transmit the required data.
