2 C.F.R. § 2520.137 — Who in NSF may grant an exception to let an excluded person participate in a covered transaction?
Citation: us:cfr:2:2520.137
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2520.137
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2520 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The NSF Director and the Deputy Director have the authority to grant an exception to let an excluded person participate in a covered transaction.
