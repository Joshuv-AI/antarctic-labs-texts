14 C.F.R. § 437.95 — Inspection of additional reusable suborbital vehicles.
Citation: us:cfr:14:437.95
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-437.95
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 437 (Experimental Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A permittee may launch or reenter additional reusable suborbital vehicles of the same design under the permit after the FAA inspects each additional reusable suborbital vehicle.
