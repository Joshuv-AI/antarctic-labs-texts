45 C.F.R. § 73.601 — Disciplinary action.
Citation: us:cfr:45:73.601
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-73.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 73 (Standards Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Violations of the regulations contained in this part and parts 735, 2634, 2635, 5501, and 5502 of title 5 of the Code of Federal Regulations may be cause for disciplinary action, which may be in addition to any penalty prescribed by law.
