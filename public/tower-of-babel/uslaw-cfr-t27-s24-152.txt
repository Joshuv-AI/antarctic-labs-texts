27 C.F.R. § 24.152 — Consents of surety.
Citation: us:cfr:27:24.152
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-24.152
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 24 (Wine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consents of surety to changes in the terms of bonds will be executed on TTB Form 5000.18 by the principal and by the surety with the same formality and evidence of authority as is required for the execution of bonds.
