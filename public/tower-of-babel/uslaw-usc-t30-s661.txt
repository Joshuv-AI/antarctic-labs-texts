Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 18 (Coal Research And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
This chapter may be cited as the “Coal Research and Development Act of 1960”.
(b)
In this chapter:
(1)
The term “research” means scientific, technical, and economic research and the practical application of that research.
(2)
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Energy.
