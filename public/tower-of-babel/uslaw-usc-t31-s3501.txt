Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 35 (Accounting And Collection); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, “executive agency” does not include (except in section 3513) a corporation, agency, or instrumentality subject to chapter 91 of this title.
