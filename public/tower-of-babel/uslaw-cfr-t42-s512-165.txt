42 C.F.R. § 512.165 — Innovation center model termination by CMS.
Citation: us:cfr:42:512.165
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-512.165
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 512 (Standard Provisions For Mandatory Innovation Center Models And Specific Provisions For Certain Models). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) CMS may terminate an Innovation Center model for reasons including, but not limited to, the following:
(1) CMS determines that it no longer has the funds to support the Innovation Center model.
(2) CMS terminates the Innovation Center model in accordance with section 1115A(b)(3)(B) of the Act.
(b) If CMS terminates an Innovation Center model, CMS provides written notice to the model participant specifying the grounds for model termination and the effective date of such termination.
