29 U.S.C. § 49k — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:29:49k
Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 4B (Federal Employment Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to make such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
