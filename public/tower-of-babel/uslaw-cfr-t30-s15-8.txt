30 C.F.R. § 15.8 — Quality assurance.
Citation: us:cfr:30:15.8
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-15.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 15 (Requirements For Approval Of Explosives And Sheathed Explosive Units). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Applicants granted an approval or an extension of approval under this part shall manufacture the explosive or sheathed explosive unit as approved.
(b) Applicants shall immediately report to the MSHA Approval and Certification Center, any knowledge of explosives or sheathed explosive units that have been distributed that do not meet the specifications of the approval.
