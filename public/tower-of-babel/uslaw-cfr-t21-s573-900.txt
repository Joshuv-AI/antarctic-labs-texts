21 C.F.R. § 573.900 — Pyrophyllite.
Citation: us:cfr:21:573.900
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-573.900
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 573 (Food Additives Permitted In Feed And Drinking Water Of Animals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pyrophyllite (aluminum silicate monohydrate) may be safely used as the sole anticaking aid, blending agent, pelleting aid, or carrier in animal feed when incorporated therein in an amount not to exceed 2 percent in complete animal feed.
