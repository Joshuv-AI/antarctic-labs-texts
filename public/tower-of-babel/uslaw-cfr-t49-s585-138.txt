49 C.F.R. § 585.138 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:49:585.138
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-585.138
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 585 (Phase-In Reporting Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All terms defined in 49 U.S.C. 30102 are used in their statutory meaning.
(b) Bus, gross vehicle weight rating or GVWR, multipurpose passenger vehicle, passenger car, and truck are used as defined in 49 CFR 571.3.
(c) Production year means the 12-month period between September 1 of one year and August 31 of the following year, inclusive.
