16 C.F.R. § 314.6 — Exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:16:314.6
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-314.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 314 (Standards For Safeguarding Customer Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 314.4(b)(1), (d)(2), (h), and (i) do not apply to financial institutions that maintain customer information concerning fewer than five thousand consumers.
