40 C.F.R. § 147.3102 — Plugging and abandonment plans.
Citation: us:cfr:40:147.3102
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-147.3102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 147 (State, Tribal, And Epa-Administered Underground Injection Control Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In lieu of the requirements of § 144.28(c)(1) and (2) (i)-(iii) of this chapter, owners and operators of Class II wells shall comply with the plugging and abandonment provisions of § 147.3108 of this subpart.
