10 C.F.R. § 431.26 — Preemption of State regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:10:431.26
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-431.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 431 (Energy Efficiency Program For Certain Commercial And Industrial Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any State regulation providing for any energy conservation standard, or other requirement with respect to the energy efficiency or energy use, of an electric motor that is not identical to a Federal standard in effect under this subpart is preempted by that standard, except as provided for in Section 345(a) and 327(b) and (c) of the Act.
