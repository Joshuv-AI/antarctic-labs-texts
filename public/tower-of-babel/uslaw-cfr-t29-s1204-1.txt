29 C.F.R. § 1204.1 — Making and maintaining contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1204.1
Creator: National Mediation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1204.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1204 (Labor Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the duty of all carriers, their officers, agents, and employees to exert every reasonable effort to make and maintain contracts covering rates of pay, rules, and working conditions.
