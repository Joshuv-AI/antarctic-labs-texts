12 C.F.R. § 192.425 — Termination of conversion.
Citation: us:cfr:12:192.425
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-192.425
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 192 (Conversions From Mutual To Stock Form). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A conversion may be terminated by:
(a) A savings association's members failing to approve the conversion at its members' meeting;
(b) A savings association before its members' meeting; or
(c) A savings association after the members' meeting, but only if the appropriate Federal banking agency concurs.
