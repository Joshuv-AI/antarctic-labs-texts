46 C.F.R. § 308.531 — Endorsement of surety bond increasing or decreasing amount of coverage, Form MA-311.
Citation: us:cfr:46:308.531
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-308.531
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 308 (War Risk Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Standard Form of Endorsement which shall be used in increasing or decreasing the amount of a surety bond, Form MA-311, may be obtained from MARAD's underwriting agent or MARAD.
