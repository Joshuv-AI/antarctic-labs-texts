47 C.F.R. § 3.61 — Reporting address.
Citation: us:cfr:47:3.61
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-3.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 3 (Authorization And Administration Of Accounting Authorities In Maritime And Maritime Mobile-Satellite Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All reports must be received at the following address no later than the required reporting date: Accounting Authority Certification Officer, Financial Operations Center, Federal Communications Commission, at the address indicated in 47 CFR 0.401(a).
