21 C.F.R. § 346.12 — Vasoconstrictor active ingredients.
Citation: us:cfr:21:346.12
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-346.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 346 (Anorectal Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The active ingredient of the product consists of any of the following when used in the concentration or within the concentration range established for each ingredient.
(a) Ephedrine sulfate 0.1 to 1.25 percent.
(b) Epinephrine 0.005 to 0.01 percent.
(c) Epinephrine hydrochloride 0.005 to 0.01 percent.
(d) Phenylephrine hydrochloride 0.25 percent.
