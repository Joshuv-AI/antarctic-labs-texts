40 C.F.R. § 408.50 — Applicability; description of the remote Alaskan crab meat processing subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:408.50
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-408.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 408 (Canned And Preserved Seafood Processing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the processing, in remote Alaska, of dungeness, tanner, and king crab meat. The effluent limitations contained in subpart E are applicable to facilities not covered under subpart D.
