33 U.S.C. § 2501 — Findings
Citation: us:usc:33:2501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2501
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 38 (Dumping Of Medical Waste By Public Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Congress finds the following:
(1)
The washing ashore of potentially infectious medical wastes from public vessels of the United States may pose serious and widespread risks to public health and to the welfare of coastal communities.
(2)
Current Federal law provides inadequate protections against the disposal of such wastes from such vessels into ocean waters.
(3)
Operators of such vessels must take immediate action to stop disposing of such wastes into ocean waters.
