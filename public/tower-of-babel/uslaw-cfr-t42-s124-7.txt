42 C.F.R. § 124.7 — Use of grant funds.
Citation: us:cfr:42:124.7
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-124.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 124 (Medical Facility Construction And Modernization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any funds granted pursuant to this subpart, as well as funds assured by the applicant for the project, shall be expended solely for carrying out the approved project in accordance with section 1625 of the Act, the regulations of this subpart, the terms and conditions of the grant award, and the applicable cost principles prescribed by 2 CFR parts 200 and 300, subpart E.
