20 C.F.R. § 416.1225 — An approved plan to achieve self-support; general.
Citation: us:cfr:20:416.1225
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-416.1225
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 416 (Supplemental Security Income For The Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you are blind or disabled, we will pay you SSI benefits and will not count resources that you use or set aside to use for expenses that we determine to be reasonable and necessary to fulfill an approved plan to achieve self-support.
