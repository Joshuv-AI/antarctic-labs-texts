43 C.F.R. § 3205.15 — How will I know whether my direct use lease will be issued?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3205.15
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3205.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If BLM decides to issue you a direct use lease, it will do so in accordance with this subpart and subpart 3206.
(b) If BLM decides to deny your application for a direct use lease, it will advise you of its decision in writing.
