33 C.F.R. § 117.231 — Brandywine Creek.
Citation: us:cfr:33:117.231
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-117.231
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 117 (Drawbridge Operation Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The draw of the Conrail bridge, mile 1.1, the Church Street bridge, mile 1.3, and the Sixteenth Street bridge, mile 1.7, all at Wilmington, need not be opened for the passage of vessels.
