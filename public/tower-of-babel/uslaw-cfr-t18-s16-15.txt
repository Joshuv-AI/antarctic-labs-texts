18 C.F.R. § 16.15 — Commission recommendation to Congress.
Citation: us:cfr:18:16.15
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-16.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 16 (Procedures Relating To Takeover And Relicensing Of Licensed Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of a recommendation from any Federal department or agency, a proposal of any party, or on the Commission's own motion, and after notice and opportunity for hearing, the Commission may determine that a project may be taken over by the United States, issue an order on its findings and recommendations, and forward a copy to Congress.
