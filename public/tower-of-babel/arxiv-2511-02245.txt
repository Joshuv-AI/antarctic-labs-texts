Role of on-site Coulomb energy and negative-charge transfer in a Dirac semi-metal Preprint: APS/123-QED
A. R. Shelke, C. -W. Chuang, S. Hamamoto, M. Oura, M. Yoshimura, N. Hiraoka, C.-N. Kuo, C.-S. Lue, A. Fujimori, A. Chainani

Abstract
Angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy in combination with band structure calculations have shown that the layered transition metal dichalcogenide NiTe 2 is a type-II Dirac semimetal. However, there are conflicting conclusions in the literature regarding the role of electron correlations in NiTe 2 . We study the core-level and valence band electronic structure of single crystal NiTe 2 using soft and hard X-ray photoemission spectroscopy (SXPES, HAXPES), X-ray absorption spectroscopy (XAS) and Ni 2 ​ p − 3 ​ d 2p-3d resonant photoemission spectroscopy(resonant-PES) to quantify electronic parameters in NiTe 2 . The on-site Coulomb energy ( U d ​ d U_{dd} ) in the Ni 3 ​ d 3d states is quantified from measurements of the Ni 3 ​ d 3d single particle density of states and the two-hole correlation satellite. The Ni 2 ​ p 2p core level and L L -edge XAS spectra are analyzed by charge transfer cluster model calculations using the experimentally estimated U d ​ d U_{dd} (= 3.7 eV), and the results show that NiTe 2 exhibits a negative charge-transfer energy ( Δ \Delta = -2.8 eV). The same type of cluster model analysis of NiO L L -edge XAS confirms its well-known strongly correlated charge-transfer insulator character, with U d ​ d U_{dd} = 7.0 eV and Δ \Delta = 6.0 eV. The d d - p p hybridization strength T e ​ g T_{eg} for NiTe 2 < < NiO, and indicates that the reduced U d ​ d U_{dd} in NiTe 2 compared to NiO is not due to an increase in T e ​ g T_{eg} . The increase in d n d^{n} count on the Ni site in NiTe 2 by nearly one electron is attributed to negative- Δ \Delta and a reduced U d ​ d U_{dd} . However, since U d ​ d U_{dd} > > | Δ | |\Delta| , the results indicate the important role of a finite repulsive U d ​ d U_{dd} in making NiTe 2 a moderately correlated p p -type Dirac semi-metal.

Conclusions

In conclusion, the core-level and valence band electronic structure of single crystal NiTe

was investigated for quantifying electronic parameters in NiTe

between Ni

2
<
NiO, and indicates that the reduced
