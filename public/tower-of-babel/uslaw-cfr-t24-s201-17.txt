24 C.F.R. § 201.17 — Prepayment provision.
Citation: us:cfr:24:201.17
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-201.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 201 (Title I Property Improvement And Manufactured Home Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The note shall contain a provision permitting full or partial prepayment of the loan without penalty, except that the borrower may be assessed reasonable and customary charges for recording a release of the lender's security interest in the property, if permitted by State law.
