CR vector bundles

We first introduce CR manifolds of high codimension.

Definition 2.1
.

Let
E
be a smooth manifold of dimension

•

,
T^{0,1}E:=\overline{T^{1,0}E}
,

•

[\mathcal{C}^{\infty}(E,T^{1,0}E),\mathcal{C}^{\infty}(E,T^{1,0}E)]\subset\mathcal{C}^{\infty}(E,T^{1,0}E)
.

We call the pair
(E,T^{1,0}E)
CR manifold of codimension
d
.

Let
(E,T^{1,0}E)
and
(F,T^{1,0}F)
be two arbitrary CR manifolds. A smooth map

is called a CR map if
(d_{x}f)(T_{x}^{1,0}E)\subseteq T_{f(x)}^{1,0}F
for any
x\in E

is the (
\mathbb{C}
-linear extension to
\mathbb{C}TE
of the) differential of

at

.

We now introduce the definition of CR vector bundles.

Definition 2.2
.

A complex vector bundle
\pi\,:\,E\rightarrow X
of rank
d
is called a CR vector bundle if

(i)

E
is a CR manifold of codimension
1+d
,

(ii)

\pi\colon E\to X
is a CR submersion,

(iii)

E\oplus E\ni(\xi_{1},\xi_{2})\to\xi_{1}+\xi_{2}\in E
and
\mathbb{C}\times E\ni(\lambda,\xi)\to\lambda\xi\in E
are CR maps.

A smooth section
s\in\mathcal{C}^{\infty}(U,E)
defined on an open set
U\subset E
is called a CR section if the map
s\colon U\to E
is CR.

Note that it is easy to see that

and

are CR manifolds.

Let

be a CR vector bundle over

5.
The asymptotics of the analytic torsion on CR manifolds for CR line bundles

In this section, we first recall the Mellin transform, then we define the analytic torsion for the CR vector bundle
E
over the CR manifold
X

The goal of this section is to establish Bismut-Vasserot type asymptotics of the analytic torsion for

as

5.1.
Mellin transformation

We first introduce some notations. Let
g(t)\in\mathcal{C}^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}_{+})
. We write

\mbox{$g(t)\sim\sum^{\infty}_{j=0}g_{-m+j}t^{-m+j}$ as $t\rightarrow 0^{+}$},

where

,

for all

.

Let
\Gamma(z)
be the Gamma function on
\mathbb{C}
. Then for
{\rm Re\,}z>0

\Gamma(z)=\int^{\infty}_{0}e^{-t}t^{z-1}dt.

\Gamma(z)^{-1}=z+O(z^{2})\ \ \mbox{near $z=0$}.

(5.1)

We suppose that

verifies the following two conditions:

\mbox{$f(t)\sim\sum^{\infty}_{j=0}f_{-m+j}t^{-m+j}$ as $t\rightarrow 0^{+}$},

(5.2)

where

,

,

.

II.

For every
\delta>0

,

\lvert f(t)\rvert\leq Ce^{-ct},\ \ \forall t\geq\delta.

(5.3)

Definition 5.1
.

The

of

is the function defined for

M[f](z)=\frac{1}{\Gamma(z)}\int^{\infty}_{0}f(t)t^{z-1}dt.

(5.4)

We recall the following result, cf.

extends to a meromorphic function on

\left\{\ell-j;\,\ell,j\in\mathbb{Z}\right\},

(5.5)

and

\begin{split}&\frac{\partial M[f]}{\partial z}(0)=\int^{1}_{0}(f(t)-\sum^{k}_{j=0}f_{-k+j}t^{-k+j})\frac{1}{t}dt\\
&\quad+\int^{\infty}_{1}f(t)\frac{1}{t}dt+\sum^{k-1}_{j=0}\frac{f_{-k+j}}{j-k}-\Gamma^{\prime}(1)f_{0}.\end{split}

(5.6)

Proof.

\begin{split}&\int^{1}_{0}f(t)t^{z-1}dt\\

From (
