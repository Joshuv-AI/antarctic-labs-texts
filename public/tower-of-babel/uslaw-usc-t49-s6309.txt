49 U.S.C. § 6309 — National transportation atlas database
Citation: us:usc:49:6309
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section6309
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 63 (Bureau Of Transportation Statistics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
The Director shall develop and maintain a national transportation atlas database that is comprised of geospatial databases that depict—
(1)
transportation networks;
(2)
flows of people, goods, vehicles, and craft over the transportation networks; and
(3)
social, economic, and environmental conditions that affect or are affected by the transportation networks.
(b) Intermodal Network Analysis.—
The databases referred to in subsection (a) shall be capable of supporting intermodal network analysis.
