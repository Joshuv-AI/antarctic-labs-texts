21 C.F.R. § 1143.13 — Effective date.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1143.13
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1143.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1143 (Minimum Required Warning Statements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as stated in paragraph (b) of this section, this part will take effect 24 months after May 10, 2016. The effective date will be with respect to the date of manufacture, provided that, in any case, beginning 30 days after the effective date, a manufacturer may not introduce into the domestic commerce of the United States any product, irrespective of the date of manufacture, that is not in conformance with this part.
(b) The requirement to submit a warning plan to FDA under § 1143.5(c)(3) will take effect 12 months after May 10, 2016.
