5 C.F.R. § 838.931 — Court orders that provide temporary awards of former spouse survivor annuities.
Citation: us:cfr:5:838.931
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-838.931
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 838 (Court Orders Affecting Retirement Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A provision in a court order that temporarily awards a former spouse survivor annuity satisfies the requirements of § 838.804(b)(2), but the temporary award becomes permanent on the date on which OPM is barred from honoring a modification of the court order (the date of retirement or death, or, in the case of a post-retirement divorce, the date of the initial court order), as provided in sections 8341(h)(4) and 8445(d) of title 5, United States Code.
