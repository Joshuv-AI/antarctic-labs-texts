A purely analytic derivation of Bonnet surfaces
Robert Conte, Alfred Michel Grundland

Abstract
Bonnet has characterized his surfaces by a geometric condition. What is done here is a characterization of the same surfaces by two analytic conditions: (i) the mean curvature H H of a surface in ℝ 3 \mathbb{R}^{3} should admit a reduction to an ordinary differential equation; (ii) this latter equation should possess the Painlevé property.
