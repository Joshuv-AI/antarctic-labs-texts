7 U.S.C. § 5104 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:7:5104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section5104
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 82 (State Agricultural Loan Mediation Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary and the Farm Credit Administration shall prescribe such regulations as may be necessary to carry out this chapter. The regulations prescribed by the Secretary shall require qualifying States to adequately train mediators to address all of the issues covered by the mediation program of the State.
