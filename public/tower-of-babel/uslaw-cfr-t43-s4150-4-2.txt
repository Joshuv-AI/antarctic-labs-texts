43 C.F.R. § 4150.4-2 — -2 Impoundment.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4150.4-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4150.4-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4100 (Grazing Administration—Exclusive Of Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After 5 days from delivery of the notice under § 4150.4-1(a) of this title or any time after 5 days from publishing and posting the notice under § 4150.4-1(b) of this title, unauthorized livestock may be impounded without further notice any time within the 12-month period following the effective date of the notice.
