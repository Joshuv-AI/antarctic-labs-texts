Abstract
. In this paper, we establish a suitable version of the Hahn-Banach theorem within the framework of Colombeau spaces, a class of spaces used to model generalized functions. Our approach addresses the case where maps are defined ε \varepsilon -wise, which simplifies the framework and makes the extension of linear functionals more manageable. As an application of our main result, we demonstrate the separation of convex sets in Colombeau spaces.

2.1.1.
Colombeau spaces

Let

In the following, all the asymptotic relations in

We set

\displaystyle:=\left\{(u_{\varepsilon})\in\prod_{\varepsilon\in I}E_{\varepsilon}\mid\forall n\in\mathbb{N}\,\exists N\in\mathbb{N}:\,p_{\varepsilon n}(u_{\varepsilon})=O(\rho_{\varepsilon}^{-N})\right\},

For any

2.1.4.
Internal sets and strongly internal
sets

We recall from
[
12
]
that a subset
A\in\mathcal{G}_{E}

is called internal if there exists a net
(A_{\varepsilon})
of subsets

A_{\varepsilon}\subseteq E_{\varepsilon}
such that

A:=\{u\in\mathcal{G}_{E}\mid\exists[u_{\varepsilon}]=u\,\forall^{0}\varepsilon:\,u_{\varepsilon}\in A_{\varepsilon}\}

where the notation
\exists[u_{\varepsilon}]=u
refers to “there exists
a representative
(u_{\varepsilon})
of
u
”. The net
(A_{\varepsilon})
is
said to be a representative of
A
, and the internal set
A
is
said to be generated by the net
(A_{\varepsilon})
. For a subset
J\subseteq\mathbb{N}
,
we denote by
A_{J}
the internal set of
\mathcal{G}_{E_{J}}
generated
by the net
(A_{\varepsilon})
.

An internal set
A
is said to be sharply bounded if

\forall i\in\mathbb{N}\,\exists N_{i}\,\forall u\in A:\,p_{i}(u)\leq\mathchoice{\hbox{\rm d}\rho}{\hbox{\rm d}\rho}{\scalebox{0.75}{$\hbox{\rm d}\rho$}}{\scalebox{0.35}{$\hbox{\rm d}\rho$}}^{-N_{i}}.

A representative
(A_{\varepsilon})
of an internal set is said to be sharply
bounded if

\forall i\in\mathbb{N}\,\exists N_{i}\,\forall^{0}\varepsilon\,\forall u\in A_{\varepsilon}:\,p_{\varepsilon i}(u)\leq\rho_{\varepsilon}^{-N_{i}}.

Clearly, any internal set having a sharply bounded representative
is sharply bounded. The converse holds when
E
is a family of normed
spaces
[
12
, Lemma 3.3]
.

When
E
is a family of normed spaces, an internal set
A
is said
to be functionally compact set (
[
5
, Definition 52]
), and
we write
A\Subset_{\mathrm{f}}\mathcal{G}_{E}
, if
A
has a sharply
bounded representative made of compact subsets. This type of sets
are not necessary compact sets with respect to the sharp topology,
but they satisfy many properties similar to those satisfied by compact
sets in normed spaces.

Any internal set
A\subseteq\mathcal{G}_{E}
is closed (with respect
to the sharp topology). For more results about internal sets, we refer
the reader to
[
12
]
.

A subset
A\subseteq\mathcal{G}_{E}
is said to be strongly internal
if there exists a net
(A_{\varepsilon})
of subsets
A_{\varepsilon}\subseteq E_{\varepsilon}

such that

A:=\{u\in\mathcal{G}_{E}\mid\forall[u_{\varepsilon}]=u\,\forall^{0}\varepsilon:\,u_{\varepsilon}\in A_{\varepsilon}\}.

where the notation
\forall[u_{\varepsilon}]=u
refers to “for any representative

(u_{\varepsilon})
of
u
”.

.

.

Proof.

By assumption, the net

\forall(u_{\varepsilon})\in\mathcal{N}_{E_{J}}:\,(T_{\varepsilon}(u_{\varepsilon}))\in\mathcal{N}_{\mathbb{C}}

We claim that

\forall n\in\mathbb{N}\,\exists m\in\mathbb{N}\,\forall^{0}\varepsilon\,\forall u\in E_{\varepsilon}:\,\max_{j\in J}p_{\varepsilon j}(u)\leq\rho_{\varepsilon}^{m}\Longrightarrow|T_{\varepsilon}(u)|\leq\rho_{\varepsilon}^{n}.

Indeed, if (

) does not hold, then there exist

and a non-increasing sequence

,
and a sequence

of

such that

Define

and

,
which contradicts (

). Assertion (
