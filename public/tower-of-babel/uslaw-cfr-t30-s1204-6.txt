30 C.F.R. § 1204.6 — May I appeal if ONRR denies my request for prepayment or other relief?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1204.6
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1204.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1204 (Alternatives For Marginal Properties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If ONRR denies your request for prepayment relief under subpart B of this part or other relief under § 1204.203, you may appeal under 30 CFR part 1290.
