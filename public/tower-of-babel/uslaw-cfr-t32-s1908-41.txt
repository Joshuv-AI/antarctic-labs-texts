32 C.F.R. § 1908.41 — Right of further appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1908.41
Creator: Central Intelligence Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1908.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1908 (Public Requests For Mandatory Declassification Review Of Classified Information Pursuant To Sec. 3.5 Of Executive Order 13526). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A right of further appeal may be available to the Interagency Security Classification Appeals Panel established pursuant to section 5.3 of the Order. Action by that Panel will be the subject of rules to be promulgated by the Information Security Oversight Office.
