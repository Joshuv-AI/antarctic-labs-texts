20 C.F.R. § 340.3 — When amounts recoverable to be recovered.
Citation: us:cfr:20:340.3
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-340.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 340 (Recovery Of Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Amounts recoverable shall be recovered in all cases except those in which recovery is waived under § 340.10 or a compromise is approved under § 340.13.
