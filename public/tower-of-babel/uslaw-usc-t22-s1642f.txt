22 U.S.C. § 1642f — Prevention of double benefits
Citation: us:usc:22:1642f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1642f
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 21 (Settlement Of International Claims); Subchapter IV (Claims Against Czechoslovakia). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In determining the amount of any award by the Commission there shall be deducted all amounts the claimant has received from any source on account of the same loss or losses with respect to which such award is made.
