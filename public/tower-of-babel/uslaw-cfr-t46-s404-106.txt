46 C.F.R. § 404.106 — Ratemaking step 6: Calculate initial base rates.
Citation: us:cfr:46:404.106
Creator: Coast Guard (Great Lakes Pilotage), Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-404.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 404 (Great Lakes Pilotage Ratemaking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director calculates initial base hourly rates by dividing the projected needed revenue from § 404.105 (step 5) by averages of past hours worked in each district's designated and undesignated waters, using available and reliable data for a multi-year period set in accordance with § 401.220(a) of this chapter.
