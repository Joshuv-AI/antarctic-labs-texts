48 C.F.R. § 18.125 — 18.125 Protest to GAO.
Citation: us:cfr:48:18.125
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 18 (Emergency Acquisitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When urgent and compelling circumstances exist, agency protest override procedures allow the head of the contracting activity to determine that the contracting process may continue after GAO has received a protest. (See 33.104(b) and (c).)
