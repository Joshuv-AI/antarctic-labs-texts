32 C.F.R. § 553.18 — Eligibility for burial in U.S. Soldiers' and Airmen's Home National Cemetery.
Citation: us:cfr:32:553.18
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-553.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 553 (Army Cemeteries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only the residents of the Armed Forces Retirement Home are eligible for interment in the U.S. Soldiers' and Airmen's Home National Cemetery. Resident eligibility criteria for the Armed Forces Retirement Home is provided for at 24 U.S.C. 412.
