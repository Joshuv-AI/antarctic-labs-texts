34 C.F.R. § 463.31 — What is an English language acquisition program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:463.31
Creator: Office Of Career, Technical, And Adult Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-463.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 463 (Adult Education And Family Literacy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term “English language acquisition program” means a program of instruction—
(a) That is designed to help eligible individuals who are English language learners achieve competence in reading, writing, speaking, and comprehension of the English language; and
(b) That leads to—
(1) Attainment of a secondary school diploma or its recognized equivalent; and
(2) Transition to postsecondary education and training; or
(3) Employment.
