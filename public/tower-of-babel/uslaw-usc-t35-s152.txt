35 U.S.C. § 152 — Issue of patent to assignee
Citation: us:usc:35:152
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section152
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part II (Patentability Of Inventions And Grant Of Patents); Chapter 14 (Issue Of Patent). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Patents may be granted to the assignee of the inventor of record in the Patent and Trademark Office, upon the application made and the specification sworn to by the inventor, except as otherwise provided in this title.
