42 U.S.C. § 300gg–7 — Prohibition on excessive waiting periods
Citation: us:usc:42:300gg–7
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300gg–7
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XXV (Requirements Relating To Health Insurance Coverage); Part A (Individual And Group Market Reforms); Subpart I (General Reform). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A group health plan and a health insurance issuer offering group health insurance coverage shall not apply any waiting period (as defined in section 300gg–3(b)(4) of this title) that exceeds 90 days.
