21 C.F.R. § 160.100 — Eggs.
Citation: us:cfr:21:160.100
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-160.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 160 (Eggs And Egg Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No regulation shall be promulgated fixing and establishing a reasonable definition and standard of identity for the food commonly known as eggs.
