Fixed points of DT transformations, cluster exponents and degrees of Weyl groups
Antoine de Saint Germain, Jiang-Hua Lu

Abstract
. Inspired by a recent work of Y. Mizuno, we show that the DT transformations on a cluster ensemble of finite type admit unique totally positive fixed points, and that the exponents of the linearizations of the DT transformations at the fixed points are precisely the degrees of the Weyl group of the corresponding finite root system.

Introduction

1.2.
Statements of main results

We will work with
cluster ensembles
({\mathcal{A}},{\mathcal{X}},p)
as defined by Fock and Goncharov
[
FG09
]
, where
{\mathcal{A}}
is a cluster
K_{2}
-variety,
{\mathcal{X}}
is a cluster Poisson variety, and
p:{\mathcal{A}}\to{\mathcal{X}}
is an
ensemble map
(see
\lx@sectionsign
2.2

The
cluster Donaldson-Thomas transformations

or
DT transformations
for short, on a cluster ensemble
({\mathcal{A}},{\mathcal{X}},p)

is a pair of biregular automorphisms
(see
\lx@sectionsign
2.4

{\rm DT}_{\mathcal{A}}:{\mathcal{A}}\longrightarrow{\mathcal{A}}\quad\text{ and }\quad{\rm DT}_{\mathcal{X}}:{\mathcal{X}}\longrightarrow{\mathcal{X}}

and Y. Soibelman’s notion of DT transformations in the context of general 3-Calabi-Yau varieties
(see, for example,
[
GS18
]
). The existence of cluster DT transformations has important consequences for the underlying cluster ensemble; for instance it implies that the Fock-Goncharov
duality conjectures
[
FG09
]
hold for the ensemble
[
GHKK18
]
. For this reason, cluster DT transformations have become an important object of study.

From now onwards, we fix an indecomposable finite root system
\Phi
of rank
n
, and let
({\mathcal{A}},{\mathcal{X}},p)
be a
cluster ensemble of the same Cartan-Killing type as
\Phi
(
\lx@sectionsign
2.5
), and we will simply say that
({\mathcal{A}},{\mathcal{X}},p)
is of type
\Phi
. We also assume that both
{\mathcal{A}}
and
{\mathcal{X}}
have trivial coefficients.
It is then well-known that DT transformations for
({\mathcal{A}},{\mathcal{X}},p)
exist
[
FZ03b
,
GHKK18
]
and are
periodic. More precisely,

({\rm DT}_{\mathcal{A}})^{{\bf h}+2}=({\rm DT}_{\mathcal{X}})^{{\bf h}+2}={\rm Id},

(1.2)

where
{\bf h}
is the Coxeter number of
\Phi

, postulated by A. Zamolodchikov in
[
Zam91

and proved in full generality by B. Keller
[
Kel13
]

Denote by
{{\mathcal{A}}^{+}}
and
{{\mathcal{X}}^{+}}
the sets

{\mathcal{A}}
and
{\mathcal{X}}

preserves
{{\mathcal{A}}^{+}}
and
{\rm DT}_{\mathcal{X}}
preserves
{{\mathcal{X}}^{+}}
(see §

{\rm DT}_{{\mathcal{A}}^{+}}
be the restriction of
{\rm DT}_{\mathcal{A}}
to
{{\mathcal{A}}^{+}}
and
{\rm DT}_{{\mathcal{X}}^{+}}
the restriction of
{\rm DT}_{\mathcal{X}}
to
{{\mathcal{X}}^{+}}
. By
[

has fixed points in
{{\mathcal{A}}^{+}}
and
{\rm DT}_{{\mathcal{X}}^{+}}
has fixed points in
{{\mathcal{X}}^{+}}
. We prove the uniqueness of

Theorem A
(Theorem
3.1
).
For any cluster ensemble
({\mathcal{A}},{\mathcal{X}},p)
of finite type,

{\rm DT}_{{\mathcal{A}}^{+}}
has a unique fixed point
{\bf a}
in
{{\mathcal{A}}^{+}}
, and

{\rm DT}_{{\mathcal{X}}^{+}}
has a unique fixed point
{\bf b}
in
{{\mathcal{X}}^{+}}
. Moreover,
p({\bf a})={\bf b}
.

As
{{\mathcal{A}}^{+}}
and
{{\mathcal{X}}^{+}}
are parametrized by
({\mathbb{R}}_{>0})^{n}
using any cluster, both
{{\mathcal{A}}^{+}}
and
{{\mathcal{X}}^{+}}
are
smooth manifolds. With the fixed points
{\bf a}\in{{\mathcal{A}}^{+}}
and
{\bf b}\in{{\mathcal{X}}^{+}}
as in Theorem A,
let
T_{{\bf a}}{{\mathcal{A}}^{+}}
and
T_{{\bf b}}{{\mathcal{X}}^{+}}
be the respective
tangent space of
{{\mathcal{A}}^{+}}
at
{\bf a}
and of
{{\mathcal{X}}^{+}}
at
{\bf b}
, and set

{\rm dt}_{\mathcal{A}}={\rm d}_{\bf a}{\rm DT}_{{\mathcal{A}}^{+}}\in{\rm End}_{\mathbb{R}}(T_{{\bf a}}{{\mathcal{A}}^{+}})\hskip 14.45377pt\mbox{and}\hskip 14.45377pt{\rm dt}_{\mathcal{X}}={\rm d}_{\bf b}{\rm DT}_{{\mathcal{X}}^{+}}\in{\rm End}_{\mathbb{R}}(T_{{\bf b}}{{\mathcal{X}}^{+}}).

Theorem B.

We now take a step back and introduce the notion of

for more general cluster ensembles.
We first recall a definition from linear algebra.

Definition 1.1

For an

e^{\frac{2\pi\sqrt{-1}}{{\bf q}}m_{j}}
with
j\in[1,n]
.

\diamond

When

is the vector space spanned by a finite root system
\Phi

of
\Phi
is the Coxeter number
{\bf h}
, and
and it is

Definition 1.2

For cluster ensembles

not necessarily of finite type, if DT transformations on

\diamond

By a

Given a mutation matrix

\textstyle{t^{\prime}}
