25 C.F.R. § 224.140 — What must the Secretary do if the Tribe fails to respond to or does not comply with the Secretary's order?
Citation: us:cfr:25:224.140
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-224.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 224 (Tribal Energy Resource Agreements Under The Indian Tribal Energy Development And Self Determination Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Tribe does not respond to or does not comply with the Secretary's order under § 224.138(a)(3), the Secretary may take any actions the Secretary deems appropriate to protect the physical trust asset, which may include the immediate reassumption of all activities the Tribe assumed under the TERA. The procedures in subpart G of this part do not apply to reassumption under this section.
