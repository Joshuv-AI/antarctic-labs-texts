28 C.F.R. § 27.7 — Right to appeal to or seek corrective relief from the U.S. Merit Systems Protection Board.
Citation: us:cfr:28:27.7
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-27.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 27 (Whistleblower Protection For Federal Bureau Of Investigation Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An FBI whistleblower may appeal to, or seek corrective relief from, the U.S. Merit Systems Protection Board in accordance with the provisions of 5 U.S.C. 2303(d).
