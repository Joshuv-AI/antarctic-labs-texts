46 C.F.R. § 129.530 — General alarm.
Citation: us:cfr:46:129.530
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-129.530
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 129 (Electrical Installations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel must be fitted with a general alarm that complies with subpart 113.25 of this chapter.
