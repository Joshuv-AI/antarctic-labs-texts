43 C.F.R. § 3585.5-4 — -4 Notice of exploration.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3585.5-4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3585.5-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3580 (Special Leasing Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants for exploration licenses shall publish a Notice of Exploration inviting other parties to participate in exploration under license on a pro rata cost sharing basis.
