32 C.F.R. § 1662.18 — The FOIA Exemption 1: National defense and foreign policy.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1662.18
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1662.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1662 (Freedom Of Information Act (Foia) Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FOIA exempts from disclosure records that are specifically authorized under criteria established by an executive order to be kept secret in the interest of national defense or foreign policy and are in fact properly classified pursuant to such executive order.
