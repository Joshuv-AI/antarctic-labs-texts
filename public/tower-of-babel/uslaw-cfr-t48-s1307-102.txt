48 C.F.R. § 1307.102 — 1307.102 Policy.

Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1307 (Acquisition Planning). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with FAR 7.102, it is the Department's policy to perform acquisition planning and conduct market research in order to promote the acquisition of commercial items and provide for full and open competition.
