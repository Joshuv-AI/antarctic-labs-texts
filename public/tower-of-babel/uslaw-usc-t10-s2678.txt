10 U.S.C. § 2678 — Feral horses and burros: removal from military installations
Citation: us:usc:10:2678
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2678
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 159 (Real Property; Related Personal Property; And Lease Of Non-Excess Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When feral horses or burros are found on an installation under the jurisdiction of the Secretary of a military department, the Secretary may use helicopters and motorized equipment for their removal.
