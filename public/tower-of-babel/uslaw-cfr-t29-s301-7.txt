29 C.F.R. § 301.7 — Hearings.
Citation: us:cfr:29:301.7
Creator: National Railroad Adjustment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-301.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 301 (Rules Of Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Oral hearings will be granted if requested by the parties or either of them and due notice will be given the parties of the time and date of the hearing.
(b) The parties are, however, charged with the duty and responsibility of including in their original written submission all known relevant, argumentative facts and documentary evidence.
