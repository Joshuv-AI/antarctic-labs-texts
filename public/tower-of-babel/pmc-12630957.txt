Shaking table test of isolated and non-isolated low-rise masonry structure
Authors: Zheng Yao, Wang Ruoxi, Zhang Youchao
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
To improve the safety and functional retention of masonry structures under moderate to severe earthquakes, a systematic study of the mechanical properties of lead rubber bearings (LRBs) and shaking table tests of seismic isolation masonry models was conducted. Firstly, based on the characteristics of low-rise masonry structure houses and common wall sizes, five small-diameter lead-rubber isolation bearings were designed, and vertical performance and horizontal stiffness tests were carried out. The mechanical performance parameters such as equivalent horizontal stiffness, post-yield stiffness, equivalent damping ratio, and vertical stiffness were obtained. The relationship curve and fitting formula between horizontal displacement, shock absorption coefficient and their influencing factors were calculated. Subsequently, a typical two-story brick structure house without structural columns in a village was selected as the test prototype. A vibration table comparison test with and without seismic isolation layer was designed at a 1:2 scale and full counterweight. Using response spectrum analysis and numerical simulation, three seismic waves were selected for both isolated and non-isolated structures, and sensor placement and loading schemes were designed. Based on the comparison of isolation and non-isolation test phenomena, especially the structural damage of the isolation layer, combined with the dynamic characteristics of white noise sweep frequency, acceleration, displacement, interlayer shear force and interlayer displacement angle, the isolation effect is analyzed and the isolation layer model design is verified. The results show that the vertical compression stiffness of LRB No. 4 is relatively stable, the hysteresis curve is full, the horizontal displacement is less than 60.5 mm, the damping coefficient is less than 0.4, the post-yield stiffness is 149.7 N/mm-167.8 N/mm, and the equivalent horizontal stiffness is 193.9 N/mm-218.65 N/mm. The first two periods of the isolation model are longer and the natural frequency is low, about 25% of the non-isolation model. When the peak acceleration is 0.4 g, the reduction rate of the top layer increases to about 48%, and the reduction rate of the first layer increases to about 40%. The displacement reduction rate of the top floor is about 24% under the action of Tangshan waves, about 36% under the action of Jiangyou waves, and up to 40% under the action of artificial waves. The test results verified the rationality of the low masonry isolation model structure and the isolation effect of lead core rubber bearing(LRB) + Frictionless sliding bearing(FSB).

Introduction

China is a vast country plagued by earthquakes, since the 21st century, there have been more than 8 devastating earthquakes of magnitude 6 or above, of which more than 5 have occurred in the rural area1. The macroscopic investigation after the earthquake shows that the damage and collapse of village houses is an important cause of casualties and property losses2. Among them, the masonry structure below 4 floors is the most widely used form of rural architecture in China3, it is one of the important topics in the field of structural engineering to study the seismic technology of village buildings with masonry structure and formulate the relevant technical standards4–6. The seismic isolation technology of laminated rubber bearing (LRB) has been studied theoretically, experimentally and applied in engineering for many years which has been developed as a mature passive seismic reaction control technology7–12. In particular, it has been widely used in multi-storey frame structure buildings in urban13–15. However, it is suitable for small diameter, low cost and easy construction rubber isolation technology for low buildings in villages and towns and the bottom frame plus layer masonry structure, to be further developed and studied.

This paper takes the two-story brick masonry building with single compartment as the prototype, placed a sliding support isolation layer with small diameter LRB, the bottom frame structure and 1/2 scale model of upper masonry structure were designed and made, the shaking table test was carried out. In order to reduce cost and facilitate construction and maintenance, isolation layer is not provided with isolation ditch, therefore a combined isolation layer is set above the indoor floor. A joist is arranged on the isolation layer to support first floor wall. In order to overcome the torsion effect, the door is disconnected at the upper joist, and the sliding supports with friction coefficient of 0 are set at both sides of the door and the center of the southern longitudinal wall. After the test is completed, the isolation layer of the same model is fixed and the comparison test of the non-isolation structure is carried out. The test was completed on the 4 m×4 m vibration table of the Structural and Seismic Laboratory of Xi ‘an University of Architecture and Science and Technology. Through the comparing the failure and dynamic characteristics of isolated and non-isolated structures under different seismic levels, verifying the applicability of the seismic isolation technology of low masonry structure residential buildings and the rubber bearing combination of bottom frame and upper masonry structure.

Test profile

Model design

Masonry structure houses in Chinese rural are widely used in the form of 1–3 layers masonry structure or upper layer masonry structure with bottom frame (3–5 layers)16. According to the size of the shaking table, the brick masonry structure with single compartment, two layers and no structural column ring beam is selected, the 1/2 scale model was made, the plane size was 2100 mm×2400 mm, the single-layer height was 1650 mm, and the total height of the model was 3300 mm. The north (front) longitudinal wall has a 450 mm × 1050 mm door hole, and the south longitudinal wall (back) has a 750 mm×900 mm window hole. Isolation supports (LRB) are provided at four corners of the house, and sliding supports are provided at the isolation layer at both sides of the door opening and the center of the window wall. The support part of the slip support shall be of the same dimensions as the LRB specification at the four corners of the house, to ensure that the sliding bearing and the isolation bearing have the same isolation layer thickness and vertical stiffness. The structural plane layout and isolation support layout are shown in Fig. 1.

The wall thickness of the model is 120 mm and MU10 sintered brick is used, the masonry slurry is M7. 5, floor and roof board using cast-in-place concrete board. The lintel of doors and Windows is made of cast-in-place concrete with a strength of C30. According to the preliminary numerical simulation results, there is a high probability that the superstructure of the isolation model does not destroy in the test. Therefore, the isolation structure test is carried out first using the same model, then the isolation layer is fixed by setting channel steel and filling plain concrete, conducting tests on non-isolated structures.

Isolation layer structure above the floor

Traditional rubber isolation supports are generally set above the foundation and under a floor, double beams, double plates and isolation ditch are required18. In this paper, the isolation layer is set above the indoor floor, the combined isolation layer is arranged between the joist under the first-floor wall and the joist on the indoor floor. The isolation layer is filled with masonry, the bottom of the filling wall is fixed with the lower joist, and a flexible cushion layer is arranged between the top of the filling wall and the joist. The isolation support is sealed with galvanized iron sheet and glass glue, the waterproof structure of the isolation layer is filled with flexible materials, and the outside is brushed with cement mortar and waterproof paint, shown in Fig. 5 (The drawings are marked with instructions: 1-upper joist; 2-lower joist; 3-Fill the wall; 4-waterproof cover plate; 5-Nail shooting; 6-isolation bearing; 7-Upper embedded plate; 8-Upper thread sleeve; 9-Upper connection bolt; 10-Upper built-in screw; 11- connecting plate; 12-lower connecting plate; 13-lower embedded plate; 14-lower thread sleeve; 15- lower connection bolt; 16-Flexible cushion layer; 17-lower built-in screw; 18-glass adhesive sealing layer; 19-waterproof coating layer; 20-outside mortar screed layer; 21-Inside mortar screed).

Experimental loading scheme

Shaking table test is a process of damage accumulation and irreversible, when the seismic wave is input to the model, the response generated by the excitation in the structure should be followed from small to large input19. The horizontal X and Y seismic waves are alternately input to the structure, without considering the vertical seismic waves20. The dynamic response of the structure increases from small to large, and the upward values of the seismic waves at the first period point are summed by X: Y weighted by 1:0.85, the input sequence of seismic waves is determined from small to large according to the sum value21. The seismic wave input sequence of isolated structure is Xi ‘an artificial wave, Tangshan east-west wave, Jiangyou 92 wave; The input sequence of seismic waves in non-isolated structures is El-Centro wave, Xi ‘an artificial wave and Songfan wave. Time history acceleration curve of seismic wave El-Centro wave, Jiangyou 92 wave, Xi ‘an artificial wave shown in Fig. 7.

The selected seismic wave is entered in turn under each peak acceleration, the model is scanned by white noise before and after the input of ground motion. Testing phenomenon

Seismic isolation structure

Under 50gal input (6 degrees of defense: Jiangyou, Tangshan and artificial waves), the model remained in an elastic working state as a whole. Under the influence of the artificial waves, only slight oscillation of the external galvanized iron sheet accessory of the support was observed. At the 150gal input level (7.5 degrees of defense), the deformation and damage were mainly concentrated in the outer coating and waterproofing structure of the isolation layer. However, the waterproofing structure did not fail, and the isolation layer showed a slight lateral movement. The upper masonry still remained elastic. Jiangyou Wave 200gal seismic (8 degrees of defense) input, there is a significant displacement of the superstructure; Tangshan wave entering along the longitudinal wall, it can be seen that the relative displacement between the joist and the isolation layer; The artificial wave 200gal is input in the direction of the transverse wall, some of the waterproof paint on the outside of the isolation layer has fallen off, Jiangyou X, Y wave test phenomenon shown in Fig. 8.

Non-isolated structure

Under the action of 6 and 7 degrees of earthquake protection, cracks along joists and floors appear in the bottom corners of the first-floor wall and second-floor wall, and the wall has the phenomenon of lifting, but no cracks appear in other parts of the model. Under the 8-degree earthquake, oblique cracks and horizontal cracks along the brick seam appear in the four corners of the transverse wall of the model side elevation, and small cracks appear in the four corners of the door and window openings of the longitudinal wall. Under the 9-degree earthquake, the oblique cracks along the brick cracks appear at the four corners of the two-story door opening and the first-story window opening, and extend to the position close to the floor and roof. Under the action of the rare earthquake of magnitude 8.5-degree, many diagonal cracks along the brick cracks appear in the first layer wall, and the original cracks extend diagonally to the four corners of the wall. When it is loaded to the north and south of Tangshan Wave, the wall of the first layer of the south and north facades is cut along the inside of the transverse wall and loses the bearing capacity, shown in Fig. 11.

Fig. 11Cracks in the north and east facades of the non-isolated model.

Cracks in the north and east facades of the non-isolated model.

The specific destruction process is described and test phenomenon analysis below: Loading begins to crack the wall, first of all, the longitudinal wall of the window appears cracks, the cracks are located on the upper side of the floor and the upper joist and the wall connection; Then is the longitudinal wall of the door, before cracks appear on the door on the lintel plastering around some subtle cracks, from the point of view of the force analysis, the cracks appear because the elasticity of the wall material is different, caused by mutual extrusion mortar; This kind of cracking will not cause the reduction of bearing capacity, but will damage the integrity of the structure. After the input of Tangshan wave in the 8 degree rare earthquake, the first-floor wall and joist slip slightly, the reason is mortar strength is high, and the slip reduces the transmission of the earthquake to the upper house. After the input of Jiangyou wave, there are obvious cracks in the transverse wall of the superstructure wall, and the wall in the southwest corner is damaged and the bricks fall off.

Comparative analysis of test results

Dynamic behavior of structure

At the beginning of the test and after the input of each seismic wave, the model was swept by white noise, and the natural vibration frequencies of the first two orders of the isolated and non-isolated structure models were obtained, as shown in Fig. 12. It can be seen that the natural vibration frequency of the non-isolated structure is basically unchanged before loading to 200gal, and the frequency decreases significantly after 200gal until it is destroyed. The first 2 natural vibration frequencies of isolated structures gradually decrease before 200gal, then remain basically unchanged, and slightly decrease when reaching 800gal. The natural vibration frequency of the isolation structure is mainly affected by the isolation layer, the deformation of the isolation support is small in the initial stage, and the stiffness is relatively large. With the increase of seismic strength, the deformation increases, the stiffness decreases, the isolation effect increases, and the natural vibration frequency decreases. Moreover, the change of vibration intensity of the vibration isolation structure after 200gal is small, which reflects that the rubber isolation bearing has better fatigue resistance. It can be seen that for earthquakes with more than 8 degrees of fortification intensity, the isolation effect of this kind of isolation structure is fully played.

Acceleration response

Non-isolated model, Fig. 15 show change of dynamic magnification coefficient of each measuring point of input on different mesa on the east elevation of Songpan wave in X direction, it can be seen from the figure that: (1) the dynamic amplification coefficient β of the measuring point of the first floor slab and 1/2 wall height of the building reaches the maximum value at 9 degrees, while the second floor slab and 1/2 wall height reach the maximum value at 7.5 degrees of fortification. From the analysis of the test data of the above isolated and non-isolated low-rise masonry structures, we can be seen: The isolation model effectively extends the structural period. Specifically, it causes a transition from the high - frequency acceleration region to the low - frequency acceleration region. When the isolation layer begins to function (approximately 0.15 g), the isolation model exhibits enhanced energy dissipation and damping, the effective input energy and peak inertial force of the structure decrease. From the analysis of acceleration and shear data, we can be seen that the isolation model reduces the high-frequency components’ influence on the acceleration and shear pulse excitation of the brittle masonry. The deformation is concentrated in the controllable isolation layer, and the shear and tensile strains between the upper masonry layers decrease, avoiding the nonlinearity of the masonry material. Due to the installation of sliding bearings combined with lead core bearings, the model’s vibration mode and torsional participation were reduced, the internal forces and deformations became more uniform, ensuring the upper part’s stiffness and integrity. At the same time, it can be observed that although the acceleration/shear force decreased significantly, it was at the cost of a larger displacement of the isolation layer.

Conclusion

To apply the technology of LRB to masonry structure in villages and towns, the isolation layer composed of LRB and sliding support is designed and set above the floor. In this study, the typical brick masonry houses without structural columns in 2-story villages and towns were used as test prototypes, and the scale was 1:2, the vibration table comparison test of the house model with the isolation support and the house model with the superstructure fixed on the foundation respectively, giving the following preliminary conclusions:

The structural model constructed using seismic isolation technology and the structural measures taken in this paper have achieved the expected seismic design goals, under the excitation of an earthquake with a peak acceleration of 800gal (Jiangyou wave, artificial wave), the seismic isolation structure model did not show any cracking or damage, and the seismic isolation bearings were in basically good condition (the displacement of the Tangshan wave of 510gal exceeded the limit).In this experiment, to verify the applicability of waterproof structure and facilitate the observation of bearing deformation, one side of the central axis of the model is waterproof, and the other side is not set. When loaded to 200 and 300gal, the galvanized iron sheet and mortar surface layer in the waterproof construction can be observed to break off, then the joist on the isolation layer is completely detached from the foundation, and the torsion Angle is relatively stable with the increase of the seismic wave intensity. It shows that the waterproof structure adopted in this experiment is basically not damaged under 8 degrees fortification intensity earthquake, the separation of the superstructure under the earthquake with the intensity of more than 8 degrees fortification (200gal) does not affect the deformation of the isolation layer.The vibration table comparison test shows that the acceleration, interlayer displacement and interlayer shear of the isolated structure have obvious damping effects, when the peak acceleration of 400gal is equivalent to the earthquake of 8 degrees, the damping rate of the top acceleration is 67%, the inter-story displacement is reduced by at least 34%, and the damping rate of the base shear is 68%. The shaking table test proves that the masonry structure building model with rubber bearing isolation technology has comparatively good seismic performance.
