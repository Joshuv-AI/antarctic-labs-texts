7 C.F.R. § 3.50 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3.50
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3 (Debt Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart provides USDA procedures for use of administrative wage garnishment to garnish a debtor's disposable pay to satisfy delinquent nontax debt owed to USDA creditor agencies.
