28 U.S.C. § 1653 — Amendment of pleadings to show jurisdiction
Citation: us:usc:28:1653
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1653
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 111 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Defective allegations of jurisdiction may be amended, upon terms, in the trial or appellate courts.
