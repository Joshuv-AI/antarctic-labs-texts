49 C.F.R. § 1185.6 — Jointly used terminal properties.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1185.6
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1185.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1185 (Interlocking Officers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person holding the position of officer or director of a carrier is relieved from the provisions of this part to the extent that he or she may also hold a directorship and any other position to which that person may be elected or appointed with a terminal railroad the properties of which are operated or used by the carrier jointly with other carriers.
