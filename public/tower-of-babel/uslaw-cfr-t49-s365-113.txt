49 C.F.R. § 365.113 — Changing the request for authority or filing supplementary evidence after the application is filed.
Citation: us:cfr:49:365.113
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-365.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 365 (Rules Governing Applications For Operating Authority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Once the application is filed, the applicant may supplement evidence only with approval of the FMCSA.
(b) Amendments to the application generally are not permitted, but in appropriate instances may be entertained at the discretion of the FMCSA.
