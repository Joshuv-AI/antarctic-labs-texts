7 C.F.R. § 160.20 — More than one grade in a container.
Citation: us:cfr:7:160.20
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-160.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 160 (Regulations And Standards For Naval Stores). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a sample from the bottom of a barrel or drum shows not more than one grade lower than that of a top sample taken in accordance with § 160.19, the grade of the rosin shall be that of such top sample: Provided, That if such “bottom head” sample shows more than one grade lower than the top sample, the grade assigned to the rosin shall be that of the darkest or lowest grade of rosin in the container.
