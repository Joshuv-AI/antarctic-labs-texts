12 C.F.R. § 1075.103 — Eligible victims.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1075.103
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1075.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1075 (Consumer Financial Civil Penalty Fund Rule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A victim is eligible for payment from the Civil Penalty Fund if a final order in a Bureau enforcement action imposed a civil penalty for the violation or violations that harmed the victim.
