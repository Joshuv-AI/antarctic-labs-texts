46 C.F.R. § 31.35-1 — -1 Electrical installations, lighting and power equipment, batteries, etc.—TB/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:31.35-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-31.35-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 31 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All tank vessels are subject to the regulations contained in subchapter J (Electrical Engineering) of this chapter except as such regulations are modified by the regulations in this subchapter for tank vessels.
