46 C.F.R. § 183.520 — Lifeboat winches.
Citation: us:cfr:46:183.520
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-183.520
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 183 (Electrical Installation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each electric power operated lifeboat winch must meet, 111.95 in subchapter J and § 160.015 in subchapter Q of this chapter, or other standard specified by the Commandant.
