5 U.S.C. § 13124 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:5:13124
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section13124
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part IV (Ethics Requirements); Chapter 131 (Ethics In Government); Subchapter II (Office Of Government Ethics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In promulgating rules and regulations pertaining to financial disclosure, conflict of interest, and ethics in the executive branch, the Director shall issue rules and regulations in accordance with chapter 5 of this title. Any person may seek judicial review of any such rule or regulation.
