32 C.F.R. § 1605.59 — Signing official papers.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1605.59
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1605.59
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1605 (Selective Service System Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Official papers issued by a local board may be signed by any member of the board or compensated employee of the area office, or any compensated employee of the Selective Service System whose official duties require him to perform administrative duties at the area office except when otherwise prescribed by the Director of Selective Service.
