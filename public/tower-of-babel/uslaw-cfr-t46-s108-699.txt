46 C.F.R. § 108.699 — Substitution of life preservers.
Citation: us:cfr:46:108.699
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-108.699
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 108 (Design And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A work vest may not be substituted for a required life preserver—
(a) For the life saving equipment requirements of this part; or
(b) For use during drills and emergencies.
