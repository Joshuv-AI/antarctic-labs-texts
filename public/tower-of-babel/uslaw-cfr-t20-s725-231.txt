20 C.F.R. § 725.231 — Domicile.
Citation: us:cfr:20:725.231
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-725.231
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 725 (Claims For Benefits Under Part C Of Title Iv Of The Federal Mine Safety And Health Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For purposes of this subpart, the term “domicile” means the place of an individual's true, fixed, and permanent home.
(b) The domicile of a deceased miner or surviving spouse is determined as of the time of death.
(c) If an individual was not domiciled in any State at the pertinent time, the law of the District of Columbia is applied.
