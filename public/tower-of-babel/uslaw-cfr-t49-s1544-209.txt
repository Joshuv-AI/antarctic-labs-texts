49 C.F.R. § 1544.209 — Use of metal detection devices.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1544.209
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1544.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1544 (Aircraft Operator Security: Air Carriers And Commercial Operators). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No aircraft operator may use a metal detection device within the United States or under the aircraft operator's operational control outside the United States to inspect persons, unless specifically authorized under a security program under this part. No aircraft operator may use such a device contrary to its security program.
(b) Metal detection devices must meet the calibration standards established by TSA.
