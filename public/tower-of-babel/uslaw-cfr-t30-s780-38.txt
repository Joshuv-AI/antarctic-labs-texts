30 C.F.R. § 780.38 — Support facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:30:780.38
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-780.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 780 (Surface Mining Permit Applications—Minimum Requirement For Reclamation And Operation Plan). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each applicant for a surface coal mining and reclamation permit shall submit a description, plans, and drawings for each support facility to be constructed, used, or maintained within the proposed permit area. The plans and drawings shall include a map, appropriate cross sections, design drawings, and specifications sufficient to demonstrate compliance with § 816.181 of this chapter for each facility.
