42 C.F.R. § 1003.1400 — Basis for civil money penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:42:1003.1400
Creator: Office Of Inspector General-Health Care, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-1003.1400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 1003 (Civil Money Penalties, Assessments And Exclusions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The OIG may impose a civil money penalty against any individual or entity described in 45 CFR 171.103(a)(2) that commits information blocking, as set forth in 45 CFR part 171.
