50 U.S.C. § 2112 — Reemployment
Citation: us:usc:50:2112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section2112
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 38 (Central Intelligence Agency Retirement And Disability); Subchapter II (Central Intelligence Agency Retirement And Disability System); Part H (Retired Participants Recalled, Reinstated, Or Reappointed In Agency Or Reemployed In Government). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A participant retired under this subchapter shall not, by reason of that retired status, be barred from employment in Federal Government service in any appointive position for which the participant is qualified.
