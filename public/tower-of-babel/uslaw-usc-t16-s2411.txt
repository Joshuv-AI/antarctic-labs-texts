16 U.S.C. § 2411 — Federal agency cooperation
Citation: us:usc:16:2411
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section2411
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 44 (Antarctic Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each Federal department or agency whose activities affect Antarctica shall utilize, to the maximum extent practicable, its authorities in furtherance of the purposes of this chapter, and shall cooperate with the Director in carrying out the purposes of this chapter.
