17 C.F.R. § 232.103 — Liability for transmission errors or omissions in documents filed via EDGAR.
Citation: us:cfr:17:232.103
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-232.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 232 (Regulation S-T—General Rules And Regulations For Electronic Filings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An electronic filer shall not be subject to the liability and anti-fraud provisions of the federal securities laws with respect to an error or omission in an electronic filing resulting solely from electronic transmission errors beyond the control of the filer, where the filer corrects the error or omission by the filing of an amendment in electronic format as soon as reasonably practicable after the electronic filer becomes aware of the error or omission.
