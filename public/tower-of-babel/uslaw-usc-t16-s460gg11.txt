16 U.S.C. § 460gg–11 — Civil and criminal jurisdiction of Idaho and Oregon
Citation: us:usc:16:460gg–11
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460gg–11
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XCI (Hells Canyon National Recreation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall diminish, enlarge, or modify any right of the States of Idaho, Oregon, or any political subdivisions thereof, to exercise civil and criminal jurisdiction within the recreation area or of rights to tax persons, corporations, franchises, or property, including mineral or other interests, in or on lands or waters within the recreation area.
