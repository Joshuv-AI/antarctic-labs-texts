45 C.F.R. § 144.212 — Confidentiality of information.
Citation: us:cfr:45:144.212
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-144.212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 144 (Requirements Relating To Health Insurance Coverage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Data collected and reported under the requirements of this subpart are subject to the confidentiality of information requirements specified in regulations under 42 CFR part 401, subpart B, and 45 CFR part 5, subpart F.
