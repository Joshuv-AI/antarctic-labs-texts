46 C.F.R. § 535.102 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:46:535.102
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-535.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 535 (Ocean Common Carrier And Marine Terminal Operator Agreements Subject To The Shipping Act Of 1984). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements those provisions of the Act that govern agreements by or among ocean common carriers and agreements among marine terminal operators and among one or more marine terminal operators and one or more ocean common carriers. This part also sets forth more specifically certain procedures provided for in the Act.
