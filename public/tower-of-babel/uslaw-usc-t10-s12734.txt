10 U.S.C. § 12734 — Time not creditable toward years of service
Citation: us:usc:10:12734
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12734
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1223 (Retired Pay For Non-Regular Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Service in an inactive status may not be counted in any computation of years of service under this chapter.
(b)
Time spent after retirement (without pay) for failure to conform to standards and qualifications prescribed under section 12641 of this title may not be credited in a computation of years of service under this chapter.
