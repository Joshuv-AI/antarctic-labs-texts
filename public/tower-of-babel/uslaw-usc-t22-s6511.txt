22 U.S.C. § 6511 — Abolition of United States Arms Control and Disarmament Agency
Citation: us:usc:22:6511
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6511
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 74 (Foreign Affairs Agencies Consolidation); Subchapter II (United States Arms Control And Disarmament Agency); Part A (Abolition And Transfer Of Functions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Arms Control and Disarmament Agency is abolished.
