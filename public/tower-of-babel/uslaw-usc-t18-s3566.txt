18 U.S.C. § 3566 — Implementation of a sentence of probation
Citation: us:usc:18:3566
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3566
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 227 (Sentences); Subchapter B (Probation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The implementation of a sentence of probation is governed by the provisions of subchapter A of chapter 229.
