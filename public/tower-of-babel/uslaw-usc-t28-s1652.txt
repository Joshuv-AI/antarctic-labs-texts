28 U.S.C. § 1652 — State laws as rules of decision
Citation: us:usc:28:1652
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1652
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 111 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The laws of the several states, except where the Constitution or treaties of the United States or Acts of Congress otherwise require or provide, shall be regarded as rules of decision in civil actions in the courts of the United States, in cases where they apply.
