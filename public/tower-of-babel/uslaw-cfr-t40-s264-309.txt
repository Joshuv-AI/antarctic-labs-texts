40 C.F.R. § 264.309 — Surveying and recordkeeping.
Citation: us:cfr:40:264.309
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-264.309
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 264 (Standards For Owners And Operators Of Hazardous Waste Treatment, Storage, And Disposal Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner or operator of a landfill must maintain the following items in the operating record required under § 264.73:
(a) On a map, the exact location and dimensions, including depth, of each cell with respect to permanently surveyed benchmarks; and
(b) The contents of each cell and the approximate location of each hazardous waste type within each cell.
