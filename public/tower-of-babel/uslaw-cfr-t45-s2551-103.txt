45 C.F.R. § 2551.103 — Must a sponsor be required to enroll non-stipended Senior Companions?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2551.103
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2551.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2551 (Senior Companion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. Enrollment of non-stipended Senior Companions is not a condition for a sponsor to receive a new or continuation grant.
