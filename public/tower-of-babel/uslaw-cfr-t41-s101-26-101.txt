41 C.F.R. § 101-26.101 — -26.101 Utilization of long supply and excess personal property.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-26.101
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-26.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-26 (26—Procurement Sources And Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the fullest extent practicable, agencies shall utilize inventories in long supply, as prescribed in subpart 101-27.3, and excess personal property, as prescribed in part 101-43, as a first source of supply in fulfilling their requirements.
