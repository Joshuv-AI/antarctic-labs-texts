16 C.F.R. § 702.2 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:16:702.2
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-702.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 702 (Pre-Sale Availability Of Written Warranty Terms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part establish requirements for sellers and warrantors for making the terms of any written warranty on a consumer product available to the consumer prior to sale.
