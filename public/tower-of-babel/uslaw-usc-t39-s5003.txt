39 U.S.C. § 5003 — Establishment of post roads
Citation: us:usc:39:5003
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section5003
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part V (Transportation Of Mail); Chapter 50 (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The following are post roads:
(1)
the waters of the United States, during the time the mail is carried thereon;
(2)
railroads or parts of railroads and air routes in operation;
(3)
canals, during the time the mail is carried thereon;
(4)
public roads, highways, and toll roads during the time the mail is carried thereon; and
(5)
letter-carrier routes established for the collection and delivery of mail.
