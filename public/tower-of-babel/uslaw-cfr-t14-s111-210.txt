14 C.F.R. § 111.210 — Format for reporting information.
Citation: us:cfr:14:111.210
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-111.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 111 (Pilot Records Database). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each reporting entity must report to the PRD all records required by this subpart for each individual the reporting entity employed as a pilot in a form and manner prescribed by the Administrator.
