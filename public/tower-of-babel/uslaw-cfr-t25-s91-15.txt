25 C.F.R. § 91.15 — Suspension or amendment of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:25:91.15
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-91.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 91 (Government Of Indian Villages, Osage Reservation, Oklahoma). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part may be suspended or amended at any time by the Secretary of the Interior: Provided, That such amendments or suspension shall not serve to change the terms or conditions of any mortgage approved in accordance with § 91.8(a).
