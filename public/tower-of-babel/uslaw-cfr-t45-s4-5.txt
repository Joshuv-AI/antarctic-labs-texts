45 C.F.R. § 4.5 — Effect of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:45:4.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-4.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 4 (Service Of Process). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part are intended solely to identify Department officials who are authorized to accept service of process. Litigants must comply with all requirements pertaining to service of process that are established by statute and court rule even though they are not repeated in these regulations.
