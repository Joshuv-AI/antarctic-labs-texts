46 C.F.R. § 32.05-5 — -5 Vessel's name on equipment—TB/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:32.05-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-32.05-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 32 (Special Equipment, Machinery, And Hull Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The equipment of all tank vessels, such as fire hose, fire axes, lifeboats, life rafts, life preservers, and lifeboats, shall be painted or branded with the name of the vessel upon which they are used.
