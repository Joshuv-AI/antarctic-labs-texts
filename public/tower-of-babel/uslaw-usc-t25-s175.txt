25 U.S.C. § 175 — United States attorneys to represent Indians
Citation: us:usc:25:175
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section175
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 5 (Protection Of Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In all States and Territories where there are reservations or allotted Indians the United States attorney shall represent them in all suits at law and in equity.
