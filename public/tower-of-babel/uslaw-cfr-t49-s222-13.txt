49 C.F.R. § 222.13 — Who is responsible for compliance?
Citation: us:cfr:49:222.13
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-222.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 222 (Use Of Locomotive Horns At Public Highway-Rail Grade Crossings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person, including but not limited to a railroad, contractor for a railroad, or a local or State governmental entity that performs any function covered by this part, must perform that function in accordance with this part.
