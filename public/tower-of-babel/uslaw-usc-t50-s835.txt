50 U.S.C. § 835 — Nonapplicability of administrative procedure provisions
Citation: us:usc:50:835
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section835
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 23 (Internal Security); Subchapter III (Personnel Security Procedures In National Security Agency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subchapter II of chapter 5, and chapter 7, of title 5, shall not apply to the use or exercise of any authority granted by this subchapter.
