48 C.F.R. § 1333.101 — 1333.101 Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1333.101
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1333.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1333 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Protest Decision Authority means agency officials above the level of the contracting officer who have been designated by the Procurement Executive to issue agency protest decisions under Executive Order 12979.
