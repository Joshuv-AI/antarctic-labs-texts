31 C.F.R. § 586.106 — Delegation of certain authorities of the Secretary of the Treasury.
Citation: us:cfr:31:586.106
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-586.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 586 (Chinese Military-Industrial Complex Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any action that the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized to take pursuant to Executive Order (E.O.) 13959 of November 12, 2020, as amended by E.O. 14032 of June 3, 2021, and any further Executive orders issued pursuant to the national emergency declared therein, may be taken by the Director of OFAC or by any other person to whom the Secretary of the Treasury has delegated authority so to act.
