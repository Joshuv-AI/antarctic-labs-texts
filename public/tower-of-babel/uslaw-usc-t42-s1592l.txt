42 U.S.C. § 1592l — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:1592l
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1592l
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 9 (Housing Of Persons Engaged In National Defense); Subchapter IX (Defense Housing And Community Facilities And Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated—
(a)
such sums, not exceeding $100,000,000, as may be necessary for carrying out the provisions and purposes of this subchapter relating to community facilities and services in critical defense housing areas; and
(b)
such sums, not exceeding $100,000,000, as may be necessary for carrying out the provisions and purposes of this subchapter relating to housing in critical defense housing areas.
