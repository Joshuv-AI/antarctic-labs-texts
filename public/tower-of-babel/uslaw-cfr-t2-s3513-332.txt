2 C.F.R. § 3513.332 — What methods must I use to pass requirements down to participants at lower tiers with whom I intend to do business?
Citation: us:cfr:2:3513.332
Creator: Export-Import Bank Of The United States
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-3513.332
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 3513 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To communicate the requirements, you must include a term or condition in the transaction requiring the participants' compliance with subpart C of this part and requiring them to include a similar term or condition in lower-tiered covered transactions.
