26 U.S.C. § 3301 — Rate of tax
Citation: us:usc:26:3301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section3301
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 23 (Federal Unemployment Tax Act). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby imposed on every employer (as defined in section 3306(a)) for each calendar year an excise tax, with respect to having individuals in his employ, equal to 6 percent of the total wages (as defined in section 3306(b)) paid by such employer during the calendar year with respect to employment (as defined in section 3306(c)).
