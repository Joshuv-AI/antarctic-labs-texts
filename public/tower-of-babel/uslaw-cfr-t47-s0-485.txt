47 C.F.R. § 0.485 — Commercial radio operator examinations.
Citation: us:cfr:47:0.485
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-0.485
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 0 (Commission Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Generally, written and telegraphy examinations for commercial radio operator licenses shall be conducted at locations and times specified by commercial operator license examination managers. (See § 13.209 of this chapter). When the FCC conducts these examinations, they shall take place at locations and times specified by the FCC.
