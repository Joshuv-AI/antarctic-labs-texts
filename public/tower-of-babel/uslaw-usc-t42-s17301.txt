42 U.S.C. § 17301 — Prohibition on market manipulation
Citation: us:usc:42:17301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section17301
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 152 (Energy Independence And Security); Subchapter VII (Improved Management Of Energy Policy); Part B (Prohibitions On Market Manipulation And False Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is unlawful for any person, directly or indirectly, to use or employ, in connection with the purchase or sale of crude oil 1 gasoline or petroleum distillates at wholesale, any manipulative or deceptive device or contrivance, in contravention of such rules and regulations as the Federal Trade Commission may prescribe as necessary or appropriate in the public interest or for the protection of United States citizens.
