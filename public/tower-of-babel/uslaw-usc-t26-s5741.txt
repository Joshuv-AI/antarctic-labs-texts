26 U.S.C. § 5741 — Records to be maintained
Citation: us:usc:26:5741
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5741
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 52 (Tobacco Products And Cigarette Papers And Tubes); Subchapter E (Records Of Manufacturers And Importers Of Tobacco Products And Cigarette Papers And Tubes, And Export Warehouse Proprietors). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every manufacturer of tobacco products, processed tobacco, or cigarette papers and tubes, every importer, and every export warehouse proprietor shall keep such records in such manner as the Secretary shall by regulation prescribe. The records required under this section shall be available for inspection by any internal revenue officer during business hours.
