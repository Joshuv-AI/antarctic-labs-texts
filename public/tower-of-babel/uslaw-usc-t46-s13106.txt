46 U.S.C. § 13106 — Computation decisions about State amounts expended
Citation: us:usc:46:13106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section13106
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part I (State Boating Safety Programs); Chapter 131 (Recreational Boating Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Consistent with regulations prescribed by the Secretary, the computation by a State of amounts expended for the State recreational boating safety program shall include—
(1)
the acquisition, maintenance, and operating costs of land, facilities, equipment, and supplies;
(2)
personnel salaries and reimbursable expenses;
(3)
the costs of training personnel;
(4)
public boat safety education;
(5)
the costs of carrying out the program; and
(6)
other expenses that the Secretary considers appropriate.
(b)
The Secretary shall decide an issue arising out of the computation made under subsection (a) of this section.
