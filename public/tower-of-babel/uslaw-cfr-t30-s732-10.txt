30 C.F.R. § 732.10 — Information collection.
Citation: us:cfr:30:732.10
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-732.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 732 (Procedures And Criteria For Approval Or Disapproval Of State Program Submissions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The information collection requirements contained in 30 CFR 732.16(a) and 732.17(b) have been approved by the Office of Management and Budget under 44 U.S.C. 3507 and assigned clearance number 1029-0024. The information is needed to afford a State the opportunity to modify or amend its State program and will be used by OSM to determine whether the amendment meets the provisions of the Act.
