8 U.S.C. § 1204 — Immediate relative and special immigrant visas
Citation: us:usc:8:1204
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1204
Context: United States Code, Title 8 (Aliens And Nationality); Chapter 12 (Immigration And Nationality); Subchapter II (Immigration); Part III (Issuance Of Entry Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A consular officer may, subject to the limitations provided in section 1201 of this title, issue an immigrant visa to a special immigrant or immediate relative as such upon satisfactory proof, under regulations prescribed under this chapter, that the applicant is entitled to special immigrant or immediate relative status.
