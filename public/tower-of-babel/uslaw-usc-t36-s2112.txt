36 U.S.C. § 2112 — Care and maintenance of Surrender Tree site
Citation: us:usc:36:2112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section2112
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (United States Government Organizations Involved With Observances And Ceremonies); Chapter 21 (American Battle Monuments Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The American Battle Monuments Commission is responsible for the care and maintenance of the Surrender Tree site in Santiago, Cuba.
