20 C.F.R. § 255.2 — Overpayments.
Citation: us:cfr:20:255.2
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-255.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 255 (Recovery Of Overpayments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An overpayment, within the meaning of this part, is made in any case in which an individual receives a payment under the Railroad Retirement Act, all or part of which payment he or she is not entitled to receive.
