45 C.F.R. § 2105.61 — Must you submit an appeal before seeking judicial review?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2105.61
Creator: Commission Of Fine Arts
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2105.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2105 (Requirements For Compliance With The Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before seeking review by a court of the Agency's adverse determination, you generally must first submit a timely administrative appeal.
