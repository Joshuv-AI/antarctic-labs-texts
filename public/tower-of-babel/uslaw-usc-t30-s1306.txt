30 U.S.C. § 1306 — Effect on rights of owner of coal in Alaska to conduct surface mining operations
Citation: us:usc:30:1306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1306
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 25 (Surface Mining Control And Reclamation); Subchapter VII (Administrative And Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as increasing or diminishing the rights of any owner of coal in Alaska to conduct or authorize surface coal mining operations for coal which has been or is hereafter conveyed out of Federal ownership to the State of Alaska or pursuant to the Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act [43 U.S.C. 1601 et seq.]: Provided, That such surface coal mining operations meet the requirements of this chapter.
