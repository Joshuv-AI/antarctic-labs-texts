5 U.S.C. § 101 — Executive departments
Citation: us:usc:5:101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section101
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 1 (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Executive departments are:
The Department of State.
The Department of the Treasury.
The Department of Defense.
The Department of Justice.
The Department of the Interior.
The Department of Agriculture.
The Department of Commerce.
The Department of Labor.
The Department of Health and Human Services.
The Department of Housing and Urban Development.
The Department of Transportation.
The Department of Energy.
The Department of Education.
The Department of Veterans Affairs.
The Department of Homeland Security.
