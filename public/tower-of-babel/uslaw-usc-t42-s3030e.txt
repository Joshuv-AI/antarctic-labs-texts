42 U.S.C. § 3030e — Grants for establishment and operation of nutrition projects
Citation: us:usc:42:3030e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3030e
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter III (Grants For State And Community Programs On Aging); Part C (Nutrition Services); Subpart i (Congregate Nutrition Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Assistant Secretary shall carry out a program for making grants to States under State plans approved under section 3027 of this title for the establishment and operation of nutrition projects that—
(1)
5 or more days a week (except in a rural area where such frequency is not feasible (as defined by the Assistant Secretary by regulation) and a lesser frequency is approved by the State agency), provide at least one hot or other appropriate meal per day and any additional meals which the recipient of a grant or contract under this subpart may elect to provide;
(2)
shall be provided in congregate settings, including adult day care facilities and multigenerational meal sites; and
(3)
provide nutrition education, nutrition counseling, and other nutrition services, as appropriate, based on the needs of meal participants.
