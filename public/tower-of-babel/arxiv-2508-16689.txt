Comment on “Magnetic moments in the Poynting theorem, Maxwell equations, Dirac equation, and QED”
Jerrold Franklin

Abstract
The paper “Magnetic moments in the Poynting theorem, Maxwell equations, Dirac equation, and QED”, arXiv:2501.02022, by Peter J. Mohr, purports to show that Maxwell’s equation, ∇ ⁣ ⋅ ​ 𝐁 = 0 \mbox{\boldmath$\nabla\cdot$}{\bf B}=0 , and Poynting’s theorem require significant modications for the 𝐁 \bf B field of a magnetic dipole. We show here that, because of critical errors in the paper, these claims are false, and that Maxwell’s equation and the Poynting theorem are the same for a magnetic moment as for any other charge-current distribution.
