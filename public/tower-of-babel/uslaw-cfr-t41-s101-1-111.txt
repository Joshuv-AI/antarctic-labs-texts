41 C.F.R. § 101-1.111 — -1.111 Retention of FPMR amendments.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-1.111
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-1.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Retention of FPMR amendments and removed pages will provide a history of FPMR issuances and facilitate determining which regulations were in effect at particular times.
