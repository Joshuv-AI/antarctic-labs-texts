50 U.S.C. § 3520 — General Counsel of Central Intelligence Agency
Citation: us:usc:50:3520
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3520
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 46 (Central Intelligence Agency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Appointment
There is a General Counsel of the Central Intelligence Agency, appointed from civilian life by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.
(b) Chief legal officer
The General Counsel is the chief legal officer of the Central Intelligence Agency.
(c) Functions
The General Counsel of the Central Intelligence Agency shall perform such functions as the Director may prescribe.
