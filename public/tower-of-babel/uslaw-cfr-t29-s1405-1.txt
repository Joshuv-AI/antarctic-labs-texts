29 C.F.R. § 1405.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1405.1
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1405.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1405 (Part-Time Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations implement Public Law 95-437, the Federal Employees Part-time Career Employment Act of 1978, by establishing a continuing program in the Federal Mediation and Conciliation Service (FMCS) to provide career part-time employment opportunities.
