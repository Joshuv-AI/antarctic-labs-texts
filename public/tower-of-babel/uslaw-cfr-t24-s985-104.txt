24 C.F.R. § 985.104 — PHA right of appeal of overall rating.
Citation: us:cfr:24:985.104
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-985.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 985 (Section 8 Management Assessment Program (Semap) And Small Rural Pha Assessments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An PHA may appeal its overall performance rating to HUD by providing justification of the reasons for its appeal. An appeal made to a HUD hub or program center or to the HUD Troubled Agency Recovery Center and denied may be further appealed to the Assistant Secretary.
