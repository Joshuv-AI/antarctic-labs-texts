48 C.F.R. § 634.003 — 634.003 Responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:48:634.003
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 634 (Major System Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Procurement Executive is the agency head's designee for the purposes of FAR 34.003(a).
(b) The Under Secretary for Management is the agency head for the purposes of FAR 34.003(c) and the acquisition executive for the purposes of A-109.
