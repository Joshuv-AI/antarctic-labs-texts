34 U.S.C. § 10365 — Distribution of funds
Citation: us:usc:34:10365
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10365
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XV (Criminal Child Support Enforcement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Federal share of a grant made under this subchapter may not exceed 75 percent of the total costs of the project described in the application submitted under section 10362(a) of this title for the fiscal year for which the project receives assistance under this subchapter.
