19 C.F.R. § 144.12 — Contents of entry summary; estimated duties.
Citation: us:cfr:19:144.12
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-144.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 144 (Warehouse And Rewarehouse Entries And Withdrawals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The entry summary, Customs Form 7501, or its electronic equivalent shall show the value, classification, and rate of duty as approved by the Center director at the time the entry summary is filed. However, no deposit of estimated duties shall be required until the merchandise is withdrawn for consumption.
