26 U.S.C. § 6602 — Interest on erroneous refund recoverable by suit
Citation: us:usc:26:6602
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6602
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 67 (Interest); Subchapter A (Interest On Underpayments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any portion of an internal revenue tax (or any interest, assessable penalty, additional amount, or addition to tax) which has been erroneously refunded, and which is recoverable by suit pursuant to section 7405, shall bear interest at the underpayment rate established under section 6621 from the date of the payment of the refund.
