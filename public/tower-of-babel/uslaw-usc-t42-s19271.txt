42 U.S.C. § 19271 — Establishment of blockchain and cryptocurrency specialist position within OSTP
Citation: us:usc:42:19271
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section19271
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 163 (Research And Development, Competition, And Innovation); Subchapter VI (Miscellaneous Science And Technology Provisions); Part F (Blockchain Specialist). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Office of Science and Technology Policy shall establish or designate a blockchain and cryptocurrencies advisory specialist position within the Office to coordinate Federal activities and advise the President on matters of research and development relating to blockchain, cryptocurrencies, and distributed ledger technologies.
