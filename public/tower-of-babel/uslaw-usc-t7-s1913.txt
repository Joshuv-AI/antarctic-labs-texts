7 U.S.C. § 1913 — Authority of Secretary of Agriculture under other provisions of law and to establish and consult with advisory committees
Citation: us:usc:7:1913
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1913
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 49 (Consultation On Agricultural Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this Act shall be deemed to limit the authority of the Secretary of Agriculture under other provision of law or to establish or consult with advisory committees.
