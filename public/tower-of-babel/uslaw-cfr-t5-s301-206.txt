5 C.F.R. § 301.206 — Within-grade increases.
Citation: us:cfr:5:301.206
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-301.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 301 (Overseas Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee serving under an overseas limited appointment of indefinite duration or an overseas limited term appointment in a position subject to the General Schedule, is eligible for within-grade increases in accordance with subpart D of part 531 of this chapter.
