5 C.F.R. § 3801.102 — Detailed or assigned special agents of certain Departmental components.
Citation: us:cfr:5:3801.102
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-3801.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 3801 (Supplemental Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The Department Of Justice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding a detail or assignment to another entity, any special agent of the Federal Bureau of Investigation or Drug Enforcement Administration who is subject to the regulations or standards of ethical conduct of that entity pursuant to § 2635.104 of this title shall also remain subject to the regulations in this part.
