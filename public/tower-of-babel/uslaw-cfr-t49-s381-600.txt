49 C.F.R. § 381.600 — Do waivers, exemptions, and pilot programs preempt State laws and regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:49:381.600
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-381.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 381 (Waivers, Exemptions, And Pilot Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. During the time period that a waiver, exemption, or pilot program authorized by this part is in effect, no State shall enforce any law or regulation that conflicts with or is inconsistent with the waiver, exemption, or pilot program with respect to a person operating under the waiver or exemption or participating in the pilot program.
