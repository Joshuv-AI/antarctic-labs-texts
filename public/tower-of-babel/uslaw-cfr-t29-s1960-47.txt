29 C.F.R. § 1960.47 — Results of investigations.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1960.47
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1960.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1960 (Basic Program Elements For Federal Employee Occupational Safety And Health Programs And Related Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each agency shall keep occupational safety and health committees advised of agency activity regarding allegations of reprisal and any agency determinations thereof. Agency officials shall provide copies of reprisal investigation findings, if any, to the Secretary and to the appropriate safety and health committee.
