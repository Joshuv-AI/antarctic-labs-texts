22 U.S.C. § 453 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:22:453
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section453
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 9 (Foreign Wars, War Materials, And Neutrality); Subchapter II (Neutrality). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may, from time to time, promulgate such rules and regulations, not inconsistent with law, as may be necessary and proper to carry out any of the provisions of this subchapter; and he may exercise any power or authority conferred on him by this subchapter through such officer or officers, or agency or agencies, as he shall direct.
