31 C.F.R. § 363.60 — How do I discontinue my participation in my payroll savings plan?
Citation: us:cfr:31:363.60
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-363.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 363 (Regulations Governing Securities Held In Treasurydirect). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may discontinue your participation in your payroll savings plan by arranging with your employer or financial institution to discontinue sending funds.
