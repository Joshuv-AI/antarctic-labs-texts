49 C.F.R. § 1540.113 — Inspection of airman certificate.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1540.113
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1540.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1540 (Civil Aviation Security: General Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each individual who holds an airman certificate, medical certificate, authorization, or license issued by the FAA must present it for inspection upon a request from TSA.
