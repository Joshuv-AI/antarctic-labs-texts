36 C.F.R. § 520.1 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:36:520.1
Creator: Smithsonian Institution
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-520.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 520 (Rules And Regulations Governing The Buildings And Grounds Of The National Zoological Park Of The Smithsonian Institution). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules and regulations in this part apply to all buildings and grounds of the National Zoological Park of the Smithsonian Institution, as defined in sec. 3, 78 Stat. 366; 40 U.S.C. 193v(1)(B), and to all persons entering in or on such buildings and grounds, hereinafter referred to as the premises.
