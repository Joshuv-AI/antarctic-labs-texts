33 C.F.R. § 5.19 — Disenrollment.
Citation: us:cfr:33:5.19
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-5.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 5 (Coast Guard Auxiliary). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A member of the Auxiliary will be disenrolled on the member's request, upon ceasing to possess the qualifications for membership, for cause, or upon direction of the Commandant.
