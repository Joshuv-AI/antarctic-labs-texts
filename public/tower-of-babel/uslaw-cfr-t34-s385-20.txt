34 C.F.R. § 385.20 — What are the application procedures for these programs?
Citation: us:cfr:34:385.20
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-385.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 385 (Rehabilitation Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary gives the designated State agency an opportunity to review and comment on applications submitted from within the State that it serves. The procedures to be followed by the applicant and the State are in 34 CFR 75.155 through 75.159.
