10 U.S.C. § 2560 — Aircraft and vehicles: limitation on leasing to non-Federal agencies
Citation: us:usc:10:2560
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2560
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 152 (Issue Of Supplies, Services, And Facilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense (or Secretary of a military department) may not lease to a non-Federal agency in the United States any aircraft or vehicle owned or operated by the Department of Defense if suitable aircraft or vehicles are commercially available in the private sector. However, nothing in the preceding sentence shall affect authorized and established procedures for the sale of surplus aircraft or vehicles.
