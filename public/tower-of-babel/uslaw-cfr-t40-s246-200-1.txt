40 C.F.R. § 246.200-1 — -1 Requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:40:246.200-1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-246.200-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 246 (Source Separation For Materials Recovery Guidelines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

High-grade paper generated by office facilities of over 100 office workers shall be separated at the source of generation, separately collected, and sold for the purpose of recycling.
