29 C.F.R. § 4901.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4901.1
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4901.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4901 (Disclosure And Public Inspection Of Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part contains PBGC's general rules implementing the Freedom of Information Act. This part sets forth generally the categories of records accessible to the public, types of records subject to prohibitions or restrictions on disclosure, and procedures whereby members of the public may access and inspect PBGC records.
