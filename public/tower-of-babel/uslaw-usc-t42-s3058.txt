42 U.S.C. § 3058 — Establishment
Citation: us:usc:42:3058
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3058
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter XI (Allotments For Vulnerable Elder Rights Protection Activities); Part A (State Provisions); Subpart i (General State Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Assistant Secretary, acting through the Administration, shall establish and carry out a program for making allotments to States to pay for the cost of carrying out vulnerable elder rights protection activities.
