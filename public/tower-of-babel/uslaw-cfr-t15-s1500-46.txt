15 C.F.R. § 1500.46 — Compensation and reimbursement.
Citation: us:cfr:15:1500.46
Creator: Office Of The Under-Secretary For Economic Affairs, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-1500.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 1500 (Concrete Masonry Research, Education, And Promotion). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Members and any alternates of the Board shall serve without compensation.
(b) If approved by the Board, members or alternates shall be reimbursed for reasonable travel expenses, which may include per diem allowance or actual subsistence incurred while away from their homes or regular places of business in the performance of services for the Board.
