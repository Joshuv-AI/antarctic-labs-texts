41 C.F.R. § 101-30.101-10 — -30.101-10 GSA section of the Federal Supply Catalog.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-30 (30—Federal Catalog System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

GSA section of the Federal Supply Catalog means a series of supply catalogs issued by GSA as an integral part of the Federal Supply Catalog. These catalogs indicate the source for obtaining supplies and services and contain ordering instructions and related supply management data.
