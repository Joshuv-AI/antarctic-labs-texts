45 C.F.R. § 620.7 — Severability.

Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 620 (Cybercorps® Scholarship For Service (Sfs) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any provision of this part or its application to any person, act, or practice is held invalid, the remainder of the part or the application of its provisions to any person, act, or practice shall not be affected thereby.
