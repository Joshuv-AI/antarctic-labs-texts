12 U.S.C. § 1748e — Mortgages on property in Alaska
Citation: us:usc:12:1748e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1748e
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing); Subchapter VIII (Armed Services Housing Mortgage Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The second sentence of section 1715d of this title, as amended, relating to housing in the State of Alaska, shall not apply to mortgages insured under this subchapter on property in said State.
