7 C.F.R. § 1421.13 — Special loan deficiency payments.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1421.13
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1421.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1421 (Grains And Similarly Handled Commodities—Marketing Assistance Loans And Loan Deficiency Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a)(1) Eligible producers of unshorn pelts produced from live sheep and hay and silage derived from an eligible loan commodity as provided in § 1421.5 are eligible to request unshorn pelt, hay, and silage quantities for a LDP under subpart C of this part.
(2) Unshorn pelts, hay, and silage derived from an eligible loan commodity are not eligible to be pledged as collateral to obtain a MAL under subpart B of this part.
