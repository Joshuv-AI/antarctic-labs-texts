48 C.F.R. § 45.302 — 45.302 Contracts with foreign governments or international organizations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:45.302
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-45.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 45 (Government Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests by, or for the benefit of, foreign Governments or international organizations to use Government property shall be processed in accordance with agency procedures.
