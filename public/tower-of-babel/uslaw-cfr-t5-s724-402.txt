5 C.F.R. § 724.402 — Best practices study.
Citation: us:cfr:5:724.402
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-724.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 724 (Implementation Of Title Ii Of The Notification And Federal Employee Antidiscrimination And Retaliation Act Of 2002). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) OPM will conduct a comprehensive study in the executive branch to identify best practices for taking appropriate disciplinary actions against Federal employees for conduct that is inconsistent with Federal Antidiscrimination and Whistleblower Protection Laws.
(b) The comprehensive study will include a review of agencies' discussions of their policies for taking such disciplinary actions as reported under § 724.302 of subpart C of this part.
