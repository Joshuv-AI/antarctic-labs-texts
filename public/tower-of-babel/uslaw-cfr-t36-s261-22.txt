36 C.F.R. § 261.22 — Unauthorized use of “Smokey Bear” and “Woodsy Owl” symbol.
Citation: us:cfr:36:261.22
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-261.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 261 (Prohibitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Manufacture, importation, reproduction, or use of “Smokey Bear” except as provided under §§ 271.2, 271.3, or 271.4 is prohibited.
(b) Manufacture, importation, reproduction, or use of “Woodsy Owl” except as provided under §§ 272.2, 272.3, or 272.4 is prohibited.
