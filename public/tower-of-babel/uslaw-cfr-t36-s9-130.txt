36 C.F.R. § 9.130 — May I cross Federal property to reach the boundary of my oil and gas right?
Citation: us:cfr:36:9.130
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-9.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 9 (Minerals Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Regional Director may grant you the privilege of access, subject to the provisions of any applicable law, on, across, or through federally owned or administered lands or waters in any System unit outside of Alaska to reach the boundary of your oil and gas right.
