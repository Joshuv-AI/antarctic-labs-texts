16 U.S.C. § 556b — Use of appropriations for expenses of transporting automobiles of employees between points in Alaska
Citation: us:usc:16:556b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section556b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3 (Forests; Forest Service; Reforestation; Management); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds available to the Forest Service may be used, in accordance with regulations prescribed by the Secretary of Agriculture for expenses of transporting automobiles of employees of that Service between points in Alaska in connection with transfers of official stations of such employees to meet the needs of the Service.
