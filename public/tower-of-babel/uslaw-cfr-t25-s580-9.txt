25 C.F.R. § 580.9 — What is the effective date of the Commission's final decision?
Citation: us:cfr:25:580.9
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-580.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 580 (Rules Of General Application In Appeal Proceedings Before The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission's final decision is effective immediately unless the Commission provides otherwise in the decision.
