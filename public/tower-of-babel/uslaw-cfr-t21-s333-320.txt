21 C.F.R. § 333.320 — Permitted combinations of active ingredients.
Citation: us:cfr:21:333.320
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-333.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 333 (Topical Antimicrobial Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Resorcinol identified in § 333.310(b) may be combined with sulfur identified in § 333.310(f).
(b) Resorcinol monoacetate identified in § 333.310(c) may be combined with sulfur identified in § 333.310(f).
