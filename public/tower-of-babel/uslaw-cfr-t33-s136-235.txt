33 C.F.R. § 136.235 — Compensation allowable.
Citation: us:cfr:33:136.235
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-136.235
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 136 (Oil Spill Liability Trust Fund; Claims Procedures; Designation Of Source; And Advertisement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The amount of compensation allowable is limited to the actual net reduction or loss of earnings or profits suffered. Calculations for net reductions or losses must clearly reflect adjustments for—
(a) All income resulting from the incident;
(b) All income from alternative employment or business undertaken;
(c) Potential income from alternative employment or business not undertaken, but reasonably available;
(d) Any saved overhead or normal expenses not incurred as a result of the incident; and
(e) State, local, and Federal taxes.
