7 C.F.R. § 760.1708 — Application for payments for milk loss.
Citation: us:cfr:7:760.1708
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-760.1708
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 760 (Indemnity Payment Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The affected farmer or the affected farmer's legal representative must sign and file an application for payment on a form which is approved for that purpose by the Deputy Administrator. The form must be filed with the county FSA office for the county where the farm headquarters are located no later than close of business on January 23, 2026, for 2023 and 2024 losses.
