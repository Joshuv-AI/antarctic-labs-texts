48 C.F.R. § 22.1906 — 22.1906 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:22.1906
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-22.1906
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 22 (Application Of Labor Laws To Government Acquisitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insert the clause at 52.222-55, Minimum Wages for Contractor Workers Under Executive Order 14026, in solicitations and contracts that include the clause at 52.222-6, Construction Wage Rate Requirements, or 52.222-41, Service Contract Labor Standards, where work is to be performed, in whole or in part, in the United States.
