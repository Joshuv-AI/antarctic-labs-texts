5 C.F.R. § 430.302 — Coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:5:430.302
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-430.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 430 (Performance Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to—
(a) All senior executives covered by subchapter II of chapter 31 of title 5, United States Code; and
(b) Agencies as defined in § 430.303.
