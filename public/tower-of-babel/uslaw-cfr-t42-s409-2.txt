42 C.F.R. § 409.2 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:42:409.2
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-409.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 409 (Hospital Insurance Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subparts A through G of this part describe the benefits available under Medicare Part A and set forth the limitations on those benefits, including certain amounts of payment for which beneficiaries are responsible.
