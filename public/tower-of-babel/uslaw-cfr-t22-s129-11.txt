22 C.F.R. § 129.11 — Maintenance of brokering records by registrants.
Citation: us:cfr:22:129.11
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-129.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 129 (Registration And Licensing Of Brokers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person who is required to register pursuant to this part (including those registered in accordance with § 129.3(d)) must maintain records concerning brokering activities in accordance with § 122.5 of this subchapter.
