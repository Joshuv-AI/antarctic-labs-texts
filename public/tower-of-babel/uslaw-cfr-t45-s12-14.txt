45 C.F.R. § 12.14 — Compliance inspections and reports.
Citation: us:cfr:45:12.14
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-12.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 12 (Disposal And Utilization Of Surplus Real Property For Public Health Purposes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department will make or have made such compliance inspections as are necessary and will require of the transferee or lessee such compliance reports and actions as are deemed necessary.
