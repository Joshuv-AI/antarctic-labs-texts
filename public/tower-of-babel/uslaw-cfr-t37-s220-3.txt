37 C.F.R. § 220.3 — Copyright Claims Board Handbook.
Citation: us:cfr:37:220.3
Creator: U.S. Copyright Office, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-220.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 220 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Copyright Claims Board may issue a handbook explaining the Board's practices and procedures. The handbook may be viewed on, downloaded from, or printed from the Board's website. The handbook will not override any existing statute or regulation.
