48 U.S.C. § 2192 — Right of Puerto Rico to determine its future political status
Citation: us:usc:48:2192
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section2192
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 20 (Puerto Rico Oversight, Management, And Economic Stability); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be interpreted to restrict Puerto Rico’s right to determine its future political status, including by conducting the plebiscite as authorized by Public Law 113–76.
