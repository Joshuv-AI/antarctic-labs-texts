12 C.F.R. § 1070.51 — Authority and responsibilities of the Chief Privacy Officer.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1070.51
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1070.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1070 (Disclosure Of Records And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chief Privacy Officer is authorized to:
(a) Develop, implement, and maintain an organization-wide privacy program;
(b) Respond to requests for access to, accounting of, or amendment of records contained in a system of records maintained by the CFPB;
(c) Approve the publication of new systems of records and amend existing systems of record; and
(d) File any necessary reports related to the Privacy Act.
