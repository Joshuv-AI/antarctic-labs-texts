46 C.F.R. § 52.15-5 — -5 Tube connections (modifies PWT-9 and PWT-11).
Citation: us:cfr:46:52.15-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-52.15-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 52 (Power Boilers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Tubes, pipe, and nipples must be attached to sheets, heads, headers, and fittings as indicated in PWT-11 of Section I of the ASME BPVC (incorporated by reference; see § 52.01-1) except as noted otherwise in this section.
(b) (Replaces PWT-9.2 and PWT-11.3.) Threaded boiler tubes are not permitted as described by PWT-9.2 and PWT-11.3 of Section I of the ASME BPVC.
(c) In welded wall construction employing stub and welded wall panels that are field welded, 10 percent of the field welds must be checked using any acceptable nondestructive test method.
(d) Nondestructive testing of the butt-welded joints must meet the requirements of § 56.95-10 of this subchapter.
