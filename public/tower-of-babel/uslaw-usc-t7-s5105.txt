7 U.S.C. § 5105 — Report
Citation: us:usc:7:5105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section5105
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 82 (State Agricultural Loan Mediation Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 2 years after December 20, 2018, the Secretary shall submit to Congress a report describing—
(1)
the effectiveness of the State mediation programs receiving matching grants under this chapter;
(2)
recommendations for improving the delivery of mediation services to producers;
(3)
the steps being taken to ensure that State mediation programs receive timely funding under this chapter; and
(4)
the savings to the States as a result of having a mediation program.
