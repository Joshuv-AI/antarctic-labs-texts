40 C.F.R. § 1039.401 — General provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1039.401
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1039.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1039 (Control Of Emissions From New And In-Use Nonroad Compression-Ignition Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We may perform in-use testing of any engine subject to the standards of this part. However, we will limit recall testing to the first 75 percent of each engine's useful life as specified in § 1039.101(g).
