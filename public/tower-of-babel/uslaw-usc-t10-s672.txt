10 U.S.C. § 672 — Reference to chapter 1209
Citation: us:usc:10:672
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section672
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 39 (Active Duty). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Provisions of law relating to service of members of reserve components on active duty are set forth in chapter 1209 of this title (beginning with section 12301).
