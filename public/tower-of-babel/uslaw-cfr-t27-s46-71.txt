27 C.F.R. § 46.71 — Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:27:46.71
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-46.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 46 (Miscellaneous Regulations Relating To Tobacco Products And Cigarette Papers And Tubes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the requirements necessary to implement 26 U.S.C. 5708, concerning payments which may be made by the United States in respect to the internal revenue taxes paid or determined and customs duties paid on tobacco products, and cigarette papers and tubes removed, which were lost, rendered unmarketable, or condemned by a duly authorized official by reason of a disaster occurring in the United States on or after September 3, 1958.
