10 U.S.C. § 9441a — Cadets: appointment by the President
Citation: us:usc:10:9441a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9441a
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 953 (United States Air Force Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Cadets at the Academy shall be appointed by the President alone. An appointment is conditional until the cadet is admitted.
