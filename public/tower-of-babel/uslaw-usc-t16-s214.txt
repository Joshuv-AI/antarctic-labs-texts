16 U.S.C. § 214 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:16:214
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section214
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXIII (Abraham Lincoln Birthplace National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Abraham Lincoln Birthplace National Historical Park shall be under the control of the National Park Service and administered under such regulations not inconsistent with law as it may from time to time prescribe.
