7 U.S.C. § 6013 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:6013
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section6013
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 89 (Pecan Promotion And Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There are authorized to be appropriated for each fiscal year such sums as are necessary to carry out this chapter.
(b) Administrative expenses
Funds appropriated to carry out this chapter shall not be available for payment of the expenses or expenditures of the Board in administering any provision of any plan issued under this chapter.
