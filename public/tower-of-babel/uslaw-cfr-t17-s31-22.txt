17 C.F.R. § 31.22 — Prohibited trading in leverage contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:17:31.22
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-31.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 31 (Leverage Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No futures commission merchant or introducing broker shall offer to enter into, enter into, confirm the execution of, or solicit or accept orders for any leverage contract.
