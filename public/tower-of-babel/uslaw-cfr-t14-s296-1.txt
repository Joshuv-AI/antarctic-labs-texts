14 C.F.R. § 296.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:14:296.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-296.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 296 (Indirect Air Transportation Of Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes rules for the indirect air transportation of property. It creates a class of air carriers to provide this air transportation and grants exemptions from certain provisions of the Subtitle VII of Title 49 of the United States Code (Transportation).
