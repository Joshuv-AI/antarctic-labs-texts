5 C.F.R. § 7001.103 — Statutory prohibition against interests in contracts to carry mail and acting as agent for contractors.
Citation: us:cfr:5:7001.103
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-7001.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 7001 (Supplemental Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The United States Postal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 440 of title 18, United States Code, makes it unlawful for any Postal Service employee to become interested in any contract for carrying the mail, or to act as agent, with or without compensation, for any contractor or person offering to become a contractor in any business before the Postal Service.
