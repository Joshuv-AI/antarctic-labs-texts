14 C.F.R. § 1217.101 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1217.101
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1217.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1217 (Duty-Free Entry Of Space Articles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to qualifying articles entered or withdrawn from warehouse for consumption in the customs territory of the United States, and to articles brought into the customs territory of the United States by NASA from space or from foreign country as part of the NASA international program.
