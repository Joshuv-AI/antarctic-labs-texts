28 U.S.C. § 955 — Practice of law restricted
Citation: us:usc:28:955
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section955
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part III (Court Officers And Employees); Chapter 57 (General Provisions Applicable To Court Officers And Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The clerk of each court and his deputies and assistants shall not practice law in any court of the United States.
