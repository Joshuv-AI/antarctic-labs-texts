29 C.F.R. § 525.11 — Issuance of certificates.
Citation: us:cfr:29:525.11
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-525.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 525 (Employment Of Workers With Disabilities Under Special Certificates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Upon consideration of the criteria cited in these regulations, a special certificate may be issued.
(b) If a special minimum wage certificate is issued, a copy shall be sent to the employer. If denied, the employer will be notified in writing and told the reasons for the denial, as well as the right to petition under § 525.18.
