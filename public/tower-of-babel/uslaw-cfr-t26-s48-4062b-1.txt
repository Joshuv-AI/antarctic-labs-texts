26 C.F.R. § 48.4062(b)-1 — (b)-1 Rebuilt parts or accessories sold on an exchange basis.
Citation: us:cfr:26:48.4062(b)-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-48.4062(b)-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 48 (Manufacturers And Retailers Excise Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The sale price of a rebuilt part or accessory on which the tax is to be computed shall not include the value of a like part or accessory accepted in exchange. The total amount charged in excess of the amount allowed for a like article accepted in an exchange will be the basis for tax. For example, if a rebuilt automobile engine is sold for $100, plus another automobile engine, the tax on the rebuilt engine will be computed on the basis of $100.
