46 C.F.R. § 532.4 — NVOCC rules tariff.
Citation: us:cfr:46:532.4
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-532.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 532 (Nvocc Negotiated Rate Arrangements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before entering into NRAs under this Part, an NVOCC must provide electronic access to its rules tariffs to the public free of charge.
