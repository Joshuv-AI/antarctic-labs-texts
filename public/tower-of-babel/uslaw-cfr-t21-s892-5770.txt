21 C.F.R. § 892.5770 — Powered radiation therapy patient support assembly.
Citation: us:cfr:21:892.5770
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-892.5770
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 892 (Radiology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A powered radiation therapy patient support assembly is an electrically powered adjustable couch intended to support a patient during radiation therapy.
(b) Classification. Class II.
