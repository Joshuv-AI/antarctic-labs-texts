38 U.S.C. § 315 — Regional offices
Citation: us:usc:38:315
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section315
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part I (General Provisions); Chapter 3 (Department Of Veterans Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary may establish such regional offices and such other field offices within the United States, its Territories, Commonwealths, and possessions, as the Secretary considers necessary.
(b)
The Secretary may maintain a regional office in the Republic of the Philippines until December 11, 2026.
