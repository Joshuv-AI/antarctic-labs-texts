46 U.S.C. § 10503 — Exhibiting merchant mariners’ documents
Citation: us:usc:46:10503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section10503
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 105 (Coastwise Voyages). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Before signing the agreement required by section 10502 of this title, a seaman required by section 8701 of this title to have a merchant mariner’s document shall exhibit to the master a document issued to the seaman and appropriately endorsed for the capacity in which the seaman is to serve.
