40 C.F.R. § 33.207 — Can an entity reapply to EPA for MBE or WBE certification?
Citation: us:cfr:40:33.207
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-33.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 33 (Participation By Disadvantaged Business Enterprises In United States Environmental Protection Agency Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An entity which has been denied MBE or WBE certification may reapply for certification at any time 12 months or more after the date of the most recent determination by EPA OSDBU to decline the application.
