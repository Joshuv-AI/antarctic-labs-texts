42 C.F.R. § 414.1510 — Beneficiary qualifications for coverage of services.
Citation: us:cfr:42:414.1510
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-414.1510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 414 (Payment For Part B Medical And Other Health Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To qualify for Medicare coverage of home infusion therapy services, a beneficiary must meet each of the following requirements:
(a) Under the care of an applicable provider. The beneficiary must be under the care of an applicable provider, as defined in section 1861(iii)(3)(A) of the Act as a physician, nurse practitioner, or physician assistant.
(b) Under a physician plan of care. The beneficiary must be under a plan of care that meets the requirements for plans of care specified in § 414.1515.
