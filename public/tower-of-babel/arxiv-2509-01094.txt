Abstract
. Based on a judicious partitioning of the preimage of the pseudohyperbolic disk under the composition symbols, in this article, we show that the boundedness and compactness of the sum of two generalized weighted composition operators with different symbols and orders between Bergman spaces are properly determined by each of individual summands. Keywords : Generalized weighted composition operator; Bergman space; Doubling weight.

1.
Introduction

Let
H(\mathbb{D})
denote the space of all analytic functions in the open unit disc
\mathbb{D}=\{z\in\mathbb{C}:|z|<1\}
. For a nonnegative function
\omega\in L^{1}([0,1])
, its extension to
\mathbb{D}
, defined by
\omega(z)=\omega(|z|)
for all
z\in\mathbb{D}
, is called a radial weight. The set of doubling weights, denoted by
\hat{\mathcal{D}}
, consists of all radial weights
\omega
such that (see
[
13
]
)

\hat{\omega}(r)\leq C\hat{\omega}\left(\frac{r+1}{2}\right),\,\,\,0\leq r<1

for some constant
C=C(\omega)\geq 1
. Here
\hat{\omega}(z)=\int_{|z|}^{1}\omega(t)dt
.
Futhermore, if
\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}
and satisfies

\displaystyle\hat{\omega}(r)\geq C\hat{\omega}\left(1-\frac{1-r}{K}\right),\,\,\,0\leq r<1

for some other constants
K=K(\omega)>1
and
C=C(\omega)>1
. We refer to
\omega
as a two-sides doubling weight and denote it by
\omega\in\mathcal{D}
. For any
\lambda\in\mathbb{D}
, the Carleson square at
\lambda\in\mathbb{D}
is defined by

S(\lambda)=\left\{re^{\mathrm{i\theta}}:|\lambda|\leq r<1,|\mbox{Arg }\lambda-\theta|<\frac{1-|\lambda|}{2}\right\}.

For a radial weight
\omega
, let
\omega(S(\lambda))=\int_{S(\lambda)}\omega(z)dA(z)
.
Here and henceforth,
dA
denotes the normalized Lebesgue area measure on
\mathbb{D}
.
Obviously,
\omega(S(\lambda))\approx(1-|\lambda|)\hat{\omega}(\lambda)
.
Further properties of doubling weights can be found in
[
13
,
14
,
15
]
and references therein.

For
0<p<\infty
and a given
\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}
, the Bergman space
A_{\omega}^{p}
consists of all functions
f\in H(\mathbb{D})
such that

\|f\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}}^{p}=\int_{\mathbb{D}}|f(z)|^{p}\omega(z)dA(z)<\infty.

As usual, we write
A_{\alpha}^{p}
for the classical weighted Bergman space induced by the standard radial weight
\omega(z)=(\alpha+1)(1-|z|^{2})^{\alpha}
with
-1<\alpha<\infty
. Throughout this paper, we assume that
\hat{\omega}(z)>0
for all
z\in\mathbb{D}
. Otherwise
A_{\omega}^{p}=H(\mathbb{D})
.
In
[
14
]
, the authors characterized the Littlewood-Paley formula on Bergman spaces induced by radial weights.
That is, when
\omega
is a radial weight,
0<p<\infty
and
k\in\mathbb{N}
,

\displaystyle\int_{\mathbb{D}}|f(z)|^{p}\omega(z)dA(z)\approx\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}|f^{(j)}(0)|^{p}+\int_{\mathbb{D}}|f^{(k)}(z)|^{p}(1-|z|^{2})^{kp}\omega(z)dA(z)

(1)

holds for all
f\in H(\mathbb{D})
if and only if
\omega\in\mathcal{D}
.

Let
S(\mathbb{D})
be the class of all analytic self-maps of
\mathbb{D}
. For
n\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\},\varphi\in S(\mathbb{D})
, and
u\in H(\mathbb{D})
, the generalized weighted composition operator
uD_{\varphi}^{(n)}
is defined by

uD_{\varphi}^{(n)}f=u\left(f^{(n)}\circ\varphi\right),\quad f\in H(\mathbb{D}).

The operator
uD_{\varphi}^{(n)}
, which is also called a weighted differentiation composition operator, was introduced by Zhu in
[
25
]
.
As special cases, the generalized weighted composition operators
uD_{\varphi}^{(n)}
include multiplication operators
M_{u}
(
n=0
and
\varphi(z)=z
), composition operator
C_{\varphi}
(
n=0
and
u(z)\equiv 1
) and weighted composition operators
uC_{\varphi}
(
n=0
).
Much effort has been expended on characterizing those symbols which induce bounded (compact) composition and weighted composition operators.
Readers interested in this topic can refer to
[
6
,
8
,
9
,
16
,
17
,
24
]
.
Moreover, when
u\equiv 1
, the operator
uD_{\varphi}^{(n)}
reduces to the differentiation-composition operator
D_{\varphi}^{(n)}
; when
u\equiv 1
and
\varphi(z)=z
,
uD_{\varphi}^{(n)}
it simplifies to the
n
-th differentiation operator
D^{(n)}
. So, the generalized weighted composition operator attracted a lot of attentions since it covers a lot of classical operators.

The products of
C_{\varphi}
,
M_{u}
and
D^{(n)}
can be obtained in six ways, such as
M_{u}C_{\varphi}D
,
M_{u}DC_{\varphi}
,
C_{\varphi}M_{u}D
,
DM_{u}C_{\varphi}
,
C_{\varphi}DM_{u}
and
DC_{\varphi}M_{u}
.
In order to treated those operators in a unified manner, Stević, Sharma and Bhat
[
18
,
19
]
introduced an operator

T_{u_{0},u_{1},\varphi}=u_{0}D_{\varphi}^{(0)}+u_{1}D_{\varphi}^{(1)}

and characterized the boundedness, compactness and essential norm of
T_{u_{0},u_{1},\varphi}:A_{\alpha}^{p}\to A_{\alpha}^{p}
with some assumptions.
In 2020, Wang, Wang and Guo
[
21
]
introduced an natural extension of
T_{u_{0},u_{1},\varphi}
, denoted by

T_{n,\varphi,\vec{u}}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}u_{k}D_{\varphi}^{(k)},

in which,
\vec{u}=\{u_{0},u_{1},...u_{n}\}\subset H(\mathbb{D}),\varphi\in S(\mathbb{D}),

and characterized the boundedness and compactness of the operator
T_{n,\varphi,\vec{u}}

mapping from admissible spaces
X
, which includes Bergman spaces, Hardy spaces and so on, into the
k
-th weighted type spaces
\mathcal{W}_{\mu}^{(k)}
and its little-oh subspces
\mathcal{W}_{\mu,0}^{(k)}
, which include the weighted type spaces, weighted Bloch spaces, weighted Zygmund spaces and their little-oh subspaces but not the Bergman spaces and Hardy spaces.
See, for example,
[
11
,
20
,
22
,
23
]
for more investigations about these operators.
In 2024, the current authors
[
10
]
showed that, if
1<p,q<\infty
and
\omega,\upsilon\in\mathcal{D}
,

\|T_{n,\varphi,\vec{u}}\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}\to A_{\upsilon}^{q}}\approx\sum_{k=0}^{n}\|u_{k}D_{\varphi}^{(k)}\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}\to A_{\upsilon}^{q}},\quad\|T_{n,\varphi,\vec{u}}\|_{e,A_{\omega}^{p}\to A_{\upsilon}^{q}}\approx\sum_{k=0}^{n}\|u_{k}D_{\varphi}^{(k)}\|_{e,A_{\omega}^{p}\to A_{\upsilon}^{q}}.

Here,
\|\cdot\|_{e,A_{\omega}^{p}\to A_{\upsilon}^{q}}
is the essential norm of an operator from
A_{\omega}^{p}
to
A_{\upsilon}^{q}
.
More precisely, the essential norm of a bounded operator
T
from a Banach space
X
to another Banach space
Y
is defined as

\|T\|_{e,X\to Y}=\inf\Big\{\|T-K\|_{X\to Y};K:X\to Y\mbox{ is compact}\Big\}.

Obviously,
T
is compact if and only if
\|T\|_{e,X\to Y}=0
.

In
[
1
]
, Acharyya and Ferguson introduced the sum of generalized weighted composition operators
T_{n,\vec{\varphi},\vec{u}}
and generalized the operator
T_{n,\varphi,\vec{u}}
, which is defined by

T_{n,\vec{\varphi},\vec{u}}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}u_{k}D_{\varphi_{k}}^{(n)},\qquad\vec{u}:=\{u_{k}\}_{k=0}^{n}\subset H(\mathbb{D}),\,\vec{\varphi}:=\{\varphi_{k}\}_{k=0}^{n}\subset S(\mathbb{D}),

and investigated the order-boundedness of
T_{n,\vec{\varphi},\vec{u}}:A_{\alpha}^{p}\to A_{\beta}^{q}
and the compactness of
T_{n,\vec{\varphi},\vec{u}}:A_{\alpha}^{p}\to H^{\infty}
.
In
[
10
]
, we estimated the norm and essential of
T_{n,\vec{\varphi},\vec{u}}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to H^{\infty}
.
Existing results demonstrate that the properties of
T_{n,\vec{\varphi},\vec{u}}
are entirely determined by each of its individual summands.
This naturally leads to the following question:

Q1
: what can be said about the operator
T_{n,\vec{\varphi},\vec{u}}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to A_{\upsilon}^{q}
?

In 2021, Choe et al.
[
4
]
established complete characterizations in terms of Carleson measures for bounded and compact differences of weighted composition operators
uC_{\varphi}-vC_{\psi}
acting on the standard weighted Bergman spaces over the unit disk.
A principal innovation resides in the judicious partitioning of
\varphi^{-1}(E_{s}(r))
, coupled with the strategic selection and rigorous estimation of
\left|u-v\frac{1-\overline{b}\varphi}{1-\overline{b}\psi}\right|
across each partition.
Here
E_{s}(a)
is a pseudohyperbolic disk centered at
a\in\mathbb{D}
with radius
r\in(0,1)
.
Their work yielded a significant breakthrough by resolving the open question about the compactness for differences of composition operators on Hardy spaces, originally posed by Shapiro and Sundberg in their seminal work
[
17
]
.
Building upon this foundational methodology, subsequent research has systematically extended these results to diverse settings: to different standard weighted Bergman spaces on the unit disk
[
5
]
, to multidimensional analogues in the unit ball setting
[
3
]
, and more recently, to weighted Bergman spaces induced by two-sides doubling weights
[
2
]
.

Motivated by these researches, this short note presents an affirmative answer to the Question Q1 when
T_{n,\vec{\varphi},\vec{u}}
only has two summands.
Unlike the reference
[
10
]
, the proof of this conclusion is based on estimates of test functions on different subsets of the preimage of the pseudohyperbolic disk
E_{s}(a)
under the composition symbol
\varphi
.

Theorem 1
.

Suppose
1<p,q<\infty
,
0\leq n<m<\infty
,
\omega\in\mathcal{D}
,
\upsilon
is a positive Borel measure on
\mathbb{D}
.
Then, for all
\varphi,\psi\in S(\mathbb{D})
and
u_{m},u_{n}\in H(\mathbb{D})
,

\|u_{n}D_{\varphi}^{(n)}+u_{m}D^{(m)}_{\psi}\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\upsilon}^{q}}\approx\|u_{n}D_{\varphi}^{(n)}\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\upsilon}^{q}}+\|u_{m}D^{(m)}_{\psi}\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\upsilon}^{q}}.

Moreover, if
p\leq q
and
u_{n}D_{\varphi}^{(n)}+u_{m}D^{(m)}_{\psi}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\upsilon}^{q}
is bounded,

\|u_{n}D_{\varphi}^{(n)}+u_{m}D^{(m)}_{\psi}\|_{e,A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\upsilon}^{q}}\approx\|u_{n}D_{\varphi}^{(n)}\|_{e,A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\upsilon}^{q}}+\|u_{m}D^{(m)}_{\psi}\|_{e,A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\upsilon}^{q}}.

By (
2
) and Theorem
A
in section 2, we can obtain geometric characterizations of the norm and essential norm of
u_{n}D_{\varphi}^{(n)}
and
u_{m}D^{(m)}_{\psi}
. Indeed, as important objects in operator theory, weighted composition operators and differentiation operators on Bergman spaces have been extensively investigated, with further characterizations available in
[
12
,
15
]
and the references therein.

Throughout this paper, the letter
C
will represent constants, which may vary from one occurrence to another. For two positive functions
f
and
g
, we use the notation
f\lesssim g
to denote that there exists a positive constant
C
, independent of the arguments, such that
f\leq Cg
. Similarly,
f\approx g
indicates that
f\lesssim g
and
g\lesssim f
.

2.
preliminaries

This section establishes fundamental lemmas that constitute the mathematical foundation for proving our principal theorem, which are to be systematically employed throughout the proof of the main conclusion.

The theory of pull-back measures has been instrumental in characterizing the boundedness and compactness of (generalized) weighted composition operators between Bergman spaces.
For any
u\in H(\mathbb{D}),\varphi\in S(\mathbb{D}),0<q<\infty,\upsilon
is a positive Borel measure on
\mathbb{D}
, the pull-back measure
\mu_{u,\varphi,q,\upsilon}
is defined by

\mu_{u,\varphi,q,\upsilon}(E)=\int_{\varphi^{-1}(E)}|u(z)|^{q}d\upsilon(z).

Here,
E\subset\mathbb{D}
is a measurable set. Then,

\displaystyle\|uD_{\varphi}^{(n)}\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\upsilon}^{q}}=\|D^{(n)}\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\mu_{u,\varphi,q,\upsilon}}^{q}}.

(2)

Therefore, the behaviors of
uD_{\varphi}^{(n)}
between Bergman spaces are determined by the embedding derivatives of Bergman spaces into Lebesgue spaces.
As usual, we write
\rho(a,z)=|\varphi_{a}(z)|=\left|\frac{a-z}{1-\overline{a}z}\right|
for the pseudohyperbolic distance between
z

and
a
, and
E_{s}(a)=\{z\in\mathbb{D}:\rho(a,z)<s\}
for the pseudohyperbolic disc with center
a\in\mathbb{D}
and radius
s\in(0,1)
.
Moreover, a sequence
\{a_{i}\}_{i=1}^{\infty}
is called a
\delta
-lattice if
D=\cup_{i=1}^{\infty}E_{\delta}(a_{i})
and
E_{\delta/2}(a_{i})
are pairwise disjoint.
Then, Theorem 3 in
[
15
]
(also see
[
12
, Theorem 1.2]
) can be stated as follows.

Theorem A
.

Let
0<p,q<\infty,n\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\},\omega\in\mathcal{D},0<s<1,\mu
be a positive Borel
measure on
\mathbb{D}
. Then the following
statements hold.

(i)

When
0<p\leq q<\infty
,
D^{(n)}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\mu}^{q}
is bounded if and only if

\sup_{z\in\mathbb{D}}\frac{\mu(E_{s}(z))}{(1-|z|)^{nq}\omega(S(z))^{\frac{q}{p}}}<\infty.

Moreover,

\|D^{(n)}\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\mu}^{q}}^{q}\approx\sup_{z\in\mathbb{D}}\frac{\mu(E_{s}(z))}{(1-|z|)^{nq}\omega(S(z))^{\frac{q}{p}}}.

(ii)

When
0<q<p<\infty
, the following statements are equivalent:

(iia)

D^{(n)}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\mu}^{q}
is bounded;

(iib)

D^{(n)}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\mu}^{q}
is compact;

(iic)

the maximum function

M_{\omega}(\mu,n)(z):=\frac{\mu(E_{s}(z))}{(1-|z|)^{nq}\omega(S(z))},\,\,\,z\in\mathbb{D}

belongs to
L_{\omega}^{\frac{p}{p-q}}
.

Moreover,

\|D^{(n)}\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\mu}^{q}}^{q}\approx\|M_{\omega}(\mu,n)\|_{L_{\omega}^{\frac{p}{p-q}}}.

When
\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}
and
0<p<\infty
, let

\displaystyle f_{\lambda,\gamma,\omega,p}(z)=\left(\frac{1-|\lambda|^{2}}{1-\overline{\lambda}z}\right)^{\gamma}\frac{1}{\omega(S(\lambda))^{\frac{1}{p}}},\,\,\lambda,z\in\mathbb{D}.

According to
[
13
, Lemma 3.1]
, there exists
\gamma_{\#}=\gamma_{\#}(\omega,p)
, whenever
\gamma>\gamma_{\#}
,

\displaystyle\|f_{\lambda,\gamma,\omega,p}\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}}\approx 1,\,\,\,\,\lambda\in\mathbb{D}.

For brevity, we denote
f_{\lambda,\gamma,\omega,p}
by
f_{\lambda,\gamma}
.
Obviously, if
\gamma
is fixed,
f_{\lambda,\gamma}
converges to 0 uniformly on any compact subset of
\mathbb{D}
as
|\lambda|\to 1
.
The functions
\{f_{\lambda,\gamma}\}
are widely used as test functions in investigating the operator theory on Bergman spaces.

Lemma 4.3 in
[
4
]
, which is immediate from the triangle inequality,
will be repeatedly used in the proof of the main result.
It is included here for easier references and expressed in a reorganized format.

Lemma 2
.

Let
\varepsilon>0
. If
\eta
and
\zeta
are nonzero complex numbers such that
(1+\varepsilon)|\zeta|\leq|\eta|
, then

|\eta|+|\zeta|\geq|\eta+\zeta|\geq\frac{\varepsilon}{2+\varepsilon}(|\eta|+|\zeta|).

When
\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}
and
1<p<\infty
, by Theorem 7 in
[
14
]
,
A_{\omega}^{p}
is reflexive.
Using Lemma 2.1 in
[
7
]
, the reflexivity of a Banach space induces a complete characterization of compact operators on them. Thus, we have the following lemma.

Lemma 3
.

Suppose
1<p,q<\infty
,
\omega\in\hat{\mathcal{D}}
,
\mu
is a positive Borel measure on
\mathbb{D}
. If
K:A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\mu}^{q}
is bounded,
K
is compact if and only if
\|K{f_{n}}\|_{L_{\mu}^{q}}\to 0
as
n\to\infty
whenever
\{f_{n}\}
is bounded in
A_{\omega}^{p}
and uniformly converges to 0 on any compact subset of
\mathbb{D}
as
n\to\infty
.

We conclude this section by extending the classical essential norm estimation framework to derivative embeddings between Bergman and Lebesgue spaces.

Lemma 4
.

Let
1<p\leq q<\infty,n\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\},\omega\in\mathcal{D},0<s<1,\mu
be a positive Borel
measure on
\mathbb{D}
. If
D^{(n)}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\mu}^{q}
is bounded,

\|D^{(n)}\|_{e,A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\mu}^{q}}^{q}\approx\limsup_{|z|\to 1}\frac{\mu(E_{s}(z))}{(1-|z|)^{nq}\omega(S(z))^{\frac{q}{p}}}.

Proof.

For any
r\in(0,1)
, let
\chi_{r}
be the characteristic function of
r\mathbb{D}
. That is
\chi_{r}(z)=1
when
|z|<r
and
\chi_{r}(z)=0
when
|z|\geq r
. By Lemma
3
and Theorem
A
,
\chi_{r}D^{(n)}:A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\mu}^{q}
is compact and

\|D^{(n)}\|_{e,A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\mu}^{q}}^{q}\leq\|D^{(n)}-\chi_{r}D^{(n)}\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\mu}^{q}}\approx\sup_{z\in\mathbb{D}}\frac{(\mu-\mu\chi_{r})(E_{s}(z))}{(1-|z|)^{nq}\omega(S(z))^{\frac{q}{p}}}.

Letting
r\to 1
, we show

\|D^{(n)}\|_{e,A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\mu}^{q}}^{q}\lesssim\limsup_{|z|\to 1}\frac{\mu(E_{s}(z))}{(1-|z|)^{nq}\omega(S(z))^{\frac{q}{p}}}.

On the other hand, letting
\gamma>\gamma_{\#}
be fixed, for any compact operator
K:A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\mu}^{q}
, when
\lambda\in\mathbb{D}
, we have

\displaystyle\|(D^{(n)}-K)f_{\lambda,\gamma}\|_{L_{\mu}^{q}}^{q}

\displaystyle\gtrsim\int_{\mathbb{D}}|D^{(n)}f_{\lambda,\gamma}(z)|^{q}d\mu(z)-\int_{\mathbb{D}}|Kf_{\lambda,\gamma}(z)|^{q}d\mu(z)

\displaystyle\geq\int_{E_{s}(\lambda)}|D^{(n)}f_{\lambda,\gamma}(z)|^{q}d\mu(z)-\int_{\mathbb{D}}|Kf_{\lambda,\gamma}(z)|^{q}d\mu(z).

Letting
|\lambda|\to 1
, by Lemma
3
, we claim

\|D^{(n)}-K\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\mu}^{q}}^{q}\gtrsim\limsup_{|\lambda|\to 1}\frac{\mu(E_{s}(\lambda))}{(1-|\lambda|)^{nq}\omega(S(\lambda))^{\frac{q}{p}}}.

Since
K
is arbitrary, we get the desired lower estimate. The proof is complete.

3.
Proof of Theorem
1

Proof.

The upper estimates of the norm and essential norm of
u_{n}D_{\varphi}^{(n)}+u_{m}D^{(m)}_{\psi}
are obvious.
Consequently, it suffices to establish the lower estimates in our analysis.
Invoking (
1
) with Lemmas 4 and 5 in
[
10
]
, without loss of generality, we can assume
0=n<m<\infty
.

For any
a\in\mathbb{D}\backslash\{0\}
, let
a_{N}:=ae^{N(1-|a|)\mathrm{i}}
for some
N>0
, which will be fixed later, and

\Omega_{s}(a):=\left\{w\in\mathbb{D}:|1-\overline{a}w|<4\sup_{z\in E_{s}(a)}|1-\overline{a}z|\right\}.

Then,
\sup\limits_{a\in\mathbb{D}}\rho(a,a_{N})<1
.
By Lemma 4.30 in
[
24
]
, there exists a constant
C_{0}=C_{0}(s)
, for any
a,z\in\mathbb{D}
and
w\in\Omega_{s}(a)
, we have

\displaystyle|1-\overline{a}w|<C_{0}(1-|a|)

(3)

and

\displaystyle|1-\overline{a_{N}}z|\approx|1-\overline{a}z|.

(4)

Letting
N=4C_{0}
, as
|a|
approaches 1, we have
2C_{0}\leq\frac{|e^{N(1-|a|)\mathrm{i}}-1|}{1-|a|}\leq 6C_{0}
.
By
E_{s}(a)\subset\Omega_{s}(a)
and (
3
),

\displaystyle\left|\frac{1-\overline{a_{N}}z}{1-\overline{a_{N}}w}\right|\leq\frac{|e^{N(1-|a|)\mathrm{i}}-1|+|1-\overline{a}z|}{\big||e^{N(1-|a|)\mathrm{i}}-1|-|1-\overline{a}w|\big|}\leq 7,\quad\mbox{ when }z\in E_{s}(a),w\in\Omega_{s}(a).

(5)

Meanwhile, by the definition of the set
\Omega_{s}(a)
, we have

\displaystyle\left|\frac{1-\overline{a}z}{1-\overline{a}w}\right|\leq\frac{1}{4},\quad\mbox{ when }z\in E_{s}(a),w\not\in\Omega_{s}(a).

(6)

For brief, put

T=u_{0}C_{\varphi}+u_{m}D^{(m)}_{\psi},\,\quad\|\cdot\|_{A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\upsilon}^{q}}=\|\cdot\|,\quad\|\cdot\|_{e,A_{\omega}^{p}\to L_{\upsilon}^{q}}=\|\cdot\|_{e},

and

Q_{b}=\frac{1-\overline{b}\varphi}{1-\overline{b}\psi},\quad(x)_{m}=x(x+1)\cdots(x+m-1),\quad v_{x}=\frac{(2x)_{m}}{(x)_{m}}.

Let
\gamma>\gamma_{\#}
be fixed.
Since
v_{\gamma}>1
, we can choose
\varepsilon>0
small enough such that
\eta:=\frac{1+\varepsilon}{v_{\gamma}}<1
.
Then, we construct a decomposition of the set
\varphi^{-1}(E_{s}(a))
in the following manner:

\displaystyle G_{1}(a)

\displaystyle=\left\{z\in F_{1}(a):\frac{|v_{\gamma}(1-Q_{a_{N}}^{\gamma})|}{|1-v_{\gamma}|}>1+\varepsilon\right\},

\displaystyle G_{2}(a)

\displaystyle=\left\{z\in F_{1}(a):\frac{|v_{\gamma}(1-Q_{a_{N}}^{\gamma})|}{|1-v_{\gamma}|}\leq 1+\varepsilon,\,|Q_{a_{N}}^{\gamma}|>\frac{1+\eta}{2}\right\},

\displaystyle G_{3}(a)

\displaystyle=\left\{z\in F_{1}(a):\frac{|v_{\gamma}(1-Q_{a_{N}}^{\gamma})|}{|1-v_{\gamma}|}\leq 1+\varepsilon,\,|Q_{a_{N}}^{\gamma}|\leq\frac{1+\eta}{2}\right\},

\displaystyle G_{4}(a)

\displaystyle=\varphi^{-1}(E_{s}(a))\backslash F_{1}(a).

Here,

\displaystyle F_{1}(a):=\varphi^{-1}(E_{s}(a))\cap\psi^{-1}(\Omega_{s}(a)).

Therefore, by (
5
), we find

\displaystyle|Q_{a_{N}}(z)|\leq 7\quad\mbox{ when }\quad z\in F_{1}(a).

(7)

The proof will be completed through three sequential parts:

Part (a):
1<p\leq q<\infty
and the lower estimate of
\|T\|
.

For any given
k\geq 1
, as
|a|
approaches 1, by (
3
), (
4
) and (
7
), we get

\displaystyle\int_{F_{1}(a)}\left|Tf_{a_{N},k\gamma}\right|^{q}d\upsilon

\displaystyle\gtrsim\frac{1}{\omega(S(a))^{\frac{q}{p}}}\int_{F_{1}(a)}\left|u_{0}+\frac{(k\gamma)_{m}\overline{a_{N}}^{m}u_{m}}{(1-\overline{a_{N}}\psi)^{m}}Q_{a_{N}}^{k\gamma}\right|^{q}d\upsilon
