40 C.F.R. § 705.5 — Substances for which reports must be submitted.
Citation: us:cfr:40:705.5
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-705.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 705 (Reporting And Recordkeeping Requirements For Certain Per- And Polyfluoroalkyl Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of this part apply to all chemical substances and mixtures containing a chemical substance (including articles) that are a PFAS, consistent with the definition of PFAS at § 705.3.
