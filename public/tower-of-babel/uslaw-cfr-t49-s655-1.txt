49 C.F.R. § 655.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:655.1
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-655.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 655 (Prevention Of Alcohol Misuse And Prohibited Drug Use In Transit Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to establish programs to be implemented by employers that receive financial assistance from the Federal Transit Administration (FTA) and by contractors of those employers, that are designed to help prevent accidents, injuries, and fatalities resulting from the misuse of alcohol and use of prohibited drugs by employees who perform safety-sensitive functions.
