46 C.F.R. § 154.908 — Inert gas generator: Location.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.908
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.908
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as allowed in paragraph (b) of this section, an inert gas generator must be located in the main machinery space or a space that is not in the cargo area and does not have direct access to any accommodation, service, or control space.
(b) An inert gas generator that does not use flame burning equipment may be located in the cargo area if specially approved by the Commandant (CG-OES).
