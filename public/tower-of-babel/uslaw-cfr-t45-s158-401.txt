45 C.F.R. § 158.401 — HHS enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:45:158.401
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-158.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 158 (Issuer Use Of Premium Revenue: Reporting And Rebate Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

HHS enforces the reporting and rebate requirements described in subparts A and B, including but not limited to:
(a) The requirement that such reports be submitted timely.
(b) The requirement that the data reported complies with the definitions and criteria set forth in this part.
(c) The requirement that rebates be paid timely and accurately.
