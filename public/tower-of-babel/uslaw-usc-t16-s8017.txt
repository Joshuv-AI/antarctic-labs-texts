16 U.S.C. § 8017 — Savings clause
Citation: us:usc:16:8017
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section8017
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 99 (Maritime Security And Fisheries Enforcement); Subchapter I (Programs To Combat Iuu Fishing And Increase Maritime Security). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No provision of section 8001 of this title or of this subchapter shall impose, or be interpreted to impose, any duty, responsibility, requirement, or obligation on the Department of Defense, the Department of the Navy, the United States Coast Guard when operating as a service in the Department of Homeland Security, or any official or component of either.
