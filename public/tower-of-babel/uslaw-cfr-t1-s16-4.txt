1 C.F.R. § 16.4 — Authorizing duties.
Citation: us:cfr:1:16.4
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-16.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 16 (Agency Representatives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency authorizing officer is responsible for furnishing, to the Director of the Federal Register, a current mailing list of officers or employees of the agency who are authorized to receive the Federal Register, the Code of Federal Regulations, and the Weekly Compilation of Presidential Documents for official use.
