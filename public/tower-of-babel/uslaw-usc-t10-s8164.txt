10 U.S.C. § 8164 — Marine Corps officers: limitation on power to command
Citation: us:usc:10:8164
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8164
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 821 (Officers In Command). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Officers of the Marine Corps may not command vessels or naval shipyards.
