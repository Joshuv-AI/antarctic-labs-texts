47 C.F.R. § 32.6232 — Circuit equipment expense.
Citation: us:cfr:47:32.6232
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-32.6232
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 32 (Uniform System Of Accounts For Telecommunications Companies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This account shall include expenses associated with circuit equipment. Circuit equipment expenses shall be maintained in the following subaccounts: 6232.1 Electronic, 6232.2 Optical.
(b) This subaccount 6232.1 Electronic shall include expenses associated with electronic circuit equipment.
(c) This subaccount 6232.2 Optical shall include expenses associated with optical circuit equipment.
