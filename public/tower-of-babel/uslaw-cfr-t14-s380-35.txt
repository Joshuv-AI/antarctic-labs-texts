14 C.F.R. § 380.35 — Disbursements from depository account.
Citation: us:cfr:14:380.35
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-380.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 380 (Public Charters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No charter operator or direct air carrier shall cause its agents or the depository bank to make disbursements or payments from deposits except in accordance with the provisions of this part.
