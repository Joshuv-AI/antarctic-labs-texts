16 C.F.R. § 500.15 — Units of count, more than one ply.
Citation: us:cfr:16:500.15
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-500.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 500 (Regulations Under Section 4 Of The Fair Packaging And Labeling Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the commodity is in distinct usable units made up of one or more components or ply, the statement of net quantity of contents shall (in addition to complying with the requirements of linear and area measurement declaration for each unit as specified in § 500.12) include the number of ply and the total number of usable units.
