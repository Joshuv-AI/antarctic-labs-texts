39 C.F.R. § 962.4 — (Rule 4) Referral of complaint.
Citation: us:cfr:39:962.4
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-962.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 962 (Administrative False Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After the Respondent files a Hearing Petition, the Reviewing Official, upon receiving a copy of the Petition, will promptly transmit a copy of the Postal Service's Complaint to the Presiding Officer.
