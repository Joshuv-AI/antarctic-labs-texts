Spectra of T-vertex and T-edge neighbourhood corona of Two Graphs
Indranil Mukherjee, Suvra Kanti Chakraborty, Arpita Das

Abstract
The T T -graph T ⁡ ( G ) T(G) of a graph G G is the graph whose vertices are the vertices and edges of G G , with two vertices of T ⁡ ( G ) T(G) are adjacent if and only if the corresponding elements of G G are adjacent or incident. In this paper, we determine the adjacency and Laplacian spectra of T T -vertex neighborhood corona and T T -edge neighborhood corona of a connected regular graph with an arbitrary regular graph in terms of their eigenvalues. Moreover, applying these results we construct some non-regular A A -cospectral and L L -cospectral graphs.

In recent years, construction of cospectral graphs for different matrices is one of the interesting research problem in the area of spectral graph theory.
All graphs considered in this paper are simple and undirected. Let
