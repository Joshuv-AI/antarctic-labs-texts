22 C.F.R. § 713.3 — How do I request nonpublic records or testimony?
Citation: us:cfr:22:713.3
Creator: Us International Development Finance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-713.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 713 (Production Of Nonpublic Records And Testimony Of Employees In Legal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To request nonpublic records or the testimony of an DFC employee, you must submit a written request as described in § 713.4 to the Vice-President/General Counsel of DFC. If you serve a subpoena on DFC or an DFC employee before submitting a written request and receiving a final determination, DFC will oppose the subpoena on the grounds that you failed to follow the requirements of this part.
