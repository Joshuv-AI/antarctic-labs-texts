46 C.F.R. § 5.51 — Construction of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:5.51
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-5.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 5 (Marine Investigation Regulations—Personnel Action). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part shall be construed so as to obtain a just, speedy, and economical determination of the issues presented.
