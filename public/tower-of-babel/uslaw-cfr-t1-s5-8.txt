1 C.F.R. § 5.8 — Form of citation.
Citation: us:cfr:1:5.8
Creator: Administrative Committee Of The Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-1/section-5.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 1 (General Provisions), Part 5 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-23 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Without prejudice to any other form of citation, Federal Register material may be cited by volume and page number, and the short form “FR” may be used for “Federal Register”. For example, “37 FR 6803” refers to material beginning on page 6803 of volume 37 of the daily issues.
