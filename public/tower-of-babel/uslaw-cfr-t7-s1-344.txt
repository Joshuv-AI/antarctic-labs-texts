7 C.F.R. § 1.344 — Deposit to Treasury of the United States.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1.344
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1.344
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1 (Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All amounts collected pursuant to this subpart shall be deposited as miscellaneous receipts in the Treasury of the United States.
