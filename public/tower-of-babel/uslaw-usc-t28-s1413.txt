28 U.S.C. § 1413 — Venue of cases under chapter 5 of title 3
Citation: us:usc:28:1413
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1413
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part IV (Jurisdiction And Venue); Chapter 87 (District Courts; Venue). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding the preceding provisions of this chapter, a civil action under section 1346(g) may be brought in the United States district court for the district in which the employee is employed or in the United States District Court for the District of Columbia.
