22 C.F.R. § 23.1 — Remittances made payable to the Department of State.
Citation: us:cfr:22:23.1
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-23.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 23 (Finance And Accounting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise specified in this title, remittances of moneys shall be drawn payable to the Department of State and sent to the Department for action and deposit. (See §§ 21.2, 22.2, and 51.40 of this chapter.)
