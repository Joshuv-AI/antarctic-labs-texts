10 U.S.C. § 1184 — Removal of officer: action by Secretary upon recommendation of board of inquiry
Citation: us:usc:10:1184
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1184
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 60 (Separation Of Regular Officers For Substandard Performance Of Duty Or For Certain Other Reasons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the military department concerned may remove an officer from active duty if the removal of such officer from active duty is recommended by a board of inquiry convened under section 1182 of this title.
