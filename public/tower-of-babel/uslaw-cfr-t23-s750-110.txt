23 C.F.R. § 750.110 — State regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:23:750.110
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-750.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 750 (Highway Beautification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State may elect to prohibit signs permissible under the standards in this part without forfeiting its rights to any benefits provided for in the act.
