42 U.S.C. § 2344 — Cash sales
Citation: us:usc:42:2344
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2344
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 24 (Disposal Of Atomic Energy Communities); Subchapter IV (Sales Of Property For Private Use). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All sales shall be for cash, and the buyer shall arrange for the necessary financing, except as provided in subchapter V of this chapter.
