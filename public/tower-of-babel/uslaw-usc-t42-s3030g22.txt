42 U.S.C. § 3030g–22 — Payment requirement
Citation: us:usc:42:3030g–22
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3030g–22
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter III (Grants For State And Community Programs On Aging); Part C (Nutrition Services); Subpart iii (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Payments made by a State agency or an area agency on aging for nutrition services (including meals) provided under part A, B, or C may not be reduced to reflect any increase in the level of assistance provided under section 3030a of this title.
