47 C.F.R. § 25.286 — Antenna painting and lighting.
Citation: us:cfr:47:25.286
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-25.286
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 25 (Satellite Communications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner of an earth station antenna structure must comply with all applicable painting, marking, and/or lighting requirements in part 17 of this chapter. In the event of default by the owner, the station licensee will be responsible for ensuring that such requirements are met.
