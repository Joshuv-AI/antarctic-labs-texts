48 C.F.R. § 39.104 — 39.104 Information technology services.
Citation: us:cfr:48:39.104
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-39.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 39 (Acquisition Of Information Technology). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When acquiring information technology services, solicitations must not describe any minimum experience or educational requirement for proposed contractor personnel unless the contracting officer determines that the needs of the agency—
(a) Cannot be met without that requirement; or
(b) Require the use of other than a performance-based acquisition (see subpart 37.6).
