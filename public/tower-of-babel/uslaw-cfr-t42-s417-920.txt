42 C.F.R. § 417.920 — Planning and initial development.
Citation: us:cfr:42:417.920
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-417.920
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 417 (Health Maintenance Organizations, Competitive Medical Plans, And Health Care Prepayment Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Under section 1304 of the PHS Act, grants and loan guarantees were awarded for projects for planning and initial development of HMOs.
(b) Planning projects included projects for any of the following:
(1) Establishment of an HMO.
(2) Significant expansion of the HMO's enrollment or geographic area.
(c) Initial development projects included projects for any of the following:
(1) Establishment of an HMO.
(2) Significant expansion of the HMO's enrollment or geographic area.
(3) Expansion of the range or amount of services furnished by the HMO.
