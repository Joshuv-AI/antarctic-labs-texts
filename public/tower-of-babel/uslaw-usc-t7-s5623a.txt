7 U.S.C. § 5623a — Supplemental agricultural trade promotion program
Citation: us:usc:7:5623a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section5623a
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 87 (Export Promotion); Subchapter II (Agricultural Export Programs); Part A (Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary of Agriculture shall carry out a program to encourage the accessibility, development, maintenance, and expansion of commercial export markets for United States agricultural commodities.
(b) Funding
Of the funds of the Commodity Credit Corporation, the Secretary of Agriculture shall make available to carry out this section $285,000,000 for fiscal year 2027 and each fiscal year thereafter.
