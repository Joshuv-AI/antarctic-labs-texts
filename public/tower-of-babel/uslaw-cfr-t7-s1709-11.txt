7 C.F.R. § 1709.11 — Award conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1709.11
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1709.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1709 (Assistance To High Energy Cost Communities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to all other grant requirements, all approved applicants will be required to do the following:
(a) Enter into a grant agreement with the Agency in form and substance acceptable to the Agency;
(b) Request advances or reimbursements, as applicable, as provided in the grant agreement; and
(c) Maintain a financial management system that is acceptable to the Agency.
