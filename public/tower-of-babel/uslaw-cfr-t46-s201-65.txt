46 C.F.R. § 201.65 — Effective date of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:46:201.65
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-201.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 201 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The publication or service of any substantive rule shall be made not less than 30 days prior to its effective date except: (a) As otherwise provided by the Administration for good cause found and published in the Federal Register or (b) in the case of rules granting or recognizing exemption or relieving restriction, interpretative rules, and statements of policy.
