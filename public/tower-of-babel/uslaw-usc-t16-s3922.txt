16 U.S.C. § 3922 — Federal acquisition
Citation: us:usc:16:3922
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3922
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 59 (Wetlands Resources); Subchapter III (State And Federal Wetlands Acquisition). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to purchase wetlands or interests in wetlands, which are not acquired under the authority of the Migratory Bird Conservation Act of 1929 (16 U.S.C. 715–715s), consistent with the wetlands priority conservation plan established under section 3921 of this title.
