21 C.F.R. § 870.1310 — Vessel dilator for percutaneous catheterization.
Citation: us:cfr:21:870.1310
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-870.1310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 870 (Cardiovascular Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A vessel dilator for percutaneous catheterization is a device which is placed over the guide wire to enlarge the opening in the vessel, and which is then removed before sliding the catheter over the guide wire.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
