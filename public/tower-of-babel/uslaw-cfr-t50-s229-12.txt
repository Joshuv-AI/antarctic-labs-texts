50 C.F.R. § 229.12 — Consultation with the Secretary of the Interior.
Citation: us:cfr:50:229.12
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-229.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 229 (Authorization For Commercial Fisheries Under The Marine Mammal Protection Act Of 1972). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Administrator will consult with the Secretary of the Interior prior to taking actions or making determinations under this part that affect or relate to species or population stocks of marine mammals for which the Secretary of the Interior is responsible under the Act.
