5 C.F.R. § 330.106 — Funding.
Citation: us:cfr:5:330.106
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-330.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 330 (Recruitment, Selection, And Placement (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each year, OPM will charge a fee for the agency's share of the cost of providing employment information to the public and to Federal employees as authorized by 5 U.S.C. 3330(f).
