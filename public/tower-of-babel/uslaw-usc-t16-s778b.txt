16 U.S.C. § 778b — Cooperation with Secretary of the Interior
Citation: us:usc:16:778b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section778b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 10C (Fish Research And Experimentation Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized to cooperate in carrying out the provisions of this chapter by furnishing such information and assistance as may be requested by the Secretary of Agriculture.
