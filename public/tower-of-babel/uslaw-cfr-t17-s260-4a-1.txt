17 C.F.R. § 260.4a-1 — a-1 Exempted securities under section 304(a)(8).
Citation: us:cfr:17:260.4a-1
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-260.4a-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 260 (General Rules And Regulations, Trust Indenture Act Of 1939). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of the Trust Indenture Act of 1939 shall not apply to any security that has been or will be issued otherwise than under an indenture. The same issuer may not claim this exemption within a period of twelve consecutive months for more than $50,000,000 aggregate principal amount of any securities.
