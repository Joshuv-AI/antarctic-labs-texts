16 U.S.C. § 6875 — Reports
Citation: us:usc:16:6875
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6875
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 87B (Modernizing Access To Public Waters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 1 year after December 26, 2025, and annually thereafter through March 30, 2034, the Secretaries shall submit a report that describes the progress made by the Secretaries with respect to meeting the requirements of this chapter to—
(1)
the Committee on Natural Resources of the House of Representatives;
(2)
the Committee on Energy and Commerce of the House of Representatives;
(3)
the Committee on Agriculture of the House of Representatives;
(4)
the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources of the Senate; and
(5)
the Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry of the Senate.
