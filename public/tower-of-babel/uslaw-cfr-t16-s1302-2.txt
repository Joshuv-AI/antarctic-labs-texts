16 C.F.R. § 1302.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1302.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1302.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1302 (Ban Of Extremely Flammable Contact Adhesives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this rule is to ban extremely flammable contact adhesives which have been found to present an unreasonable risk of injury to consumers of burns resulting from explosive and flashback fire.
