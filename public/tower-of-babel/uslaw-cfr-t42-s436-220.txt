42 C.F.R. § 436.220 — Individuals who would meet the income and resource requirements under AFDC if child care costs were paid from earnings.
Citation: us:cfr:42:436.220
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-436.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 436 (Eligibility In Guam, Puerto Rico, And The Virgin Islands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The agency may provide Medicaid to any group or groups of individuals specified under § 436.201(a)(4), (a)(5), and (a)(6) who would meet the income and resource requirements under the State's AFDC plan if their work-related child care costs were paid from their earnings rather than by a State agency as a service expenditure.
(b) The agency may use this option only if the State's AFDC plan deducts work-related child care costs from income to determine the amount of AFDC.
