31 C.F.R. § 347.42 — Supplements, amendments, or revisions.
Citation: us:cfr:31:347.42
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-347.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 347 (Regulations Governing Retirement Savings Bonds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may at any time, or from time to time, prescribe additional, supplemental, amendatory, or revised rules and regulations governing retirement savings bonds.
