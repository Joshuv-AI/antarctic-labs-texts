31 C.F.R. § 561.407 — Conducting or facilitating a financial transaction with the Central Bank of Iran or a designated Iranian financial institution.
Citation: us:cfr:31:561.407
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-561.407
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 561 (Iranian Financial Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A foreign financial institution conducts or facilitates a financial transaction with the Central Bank of Iran or a designated Iranian financial institution if it maintains an account for such entities or engages in a financial transaction directly or indirectly with such entities.
