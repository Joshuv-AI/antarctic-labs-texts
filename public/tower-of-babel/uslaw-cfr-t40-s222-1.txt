40 C.F.R. § 222.1 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:40:222.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-222.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 222 (Action On Ocean Dumping Permit Applications Under Section 102 Of The Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Decisions as to the issuance, denial, or imposition of conditions on general, special, emergency, and research permits under section 102 of the Act will be made by application of the criteria of parts 227 and 228 of this chapter. Final action on any application for a permit will, to the extent practicable, be taken within 180 days from the date a complete application is filed.
