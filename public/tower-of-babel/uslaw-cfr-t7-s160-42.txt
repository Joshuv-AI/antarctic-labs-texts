7 C.F.R. § 160.42 — Limitation of license.
Citation: us:cfr:7:160.42
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-160.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 160 (Regulations And Standards For Naval Stores). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The license issued by the Administrator to a licensed inspector of naval stores shall state the name of the processing plant or plants at which the licensee may perform the duties of an official inspector, and shall be countersigned by such official as may be designated and authorized.
