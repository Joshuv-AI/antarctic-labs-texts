22 C.F.R. § 133.215 — What must I include in my drug-free awareness program?
Citation: us:cfr:22:133.215
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-133.215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 133 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must establish an ongoing drug-free awareness program to inform employees about—
(a) The dangers of drug abuse in the workplace;
(b) Your policy of maintaining a drug-free workplace;
(c) Any available drug counseling, rehabilitation, and employee assistance programs; and
(d) The penalties that you may impose upon them for drug abuse violations occurring in the workplace.
