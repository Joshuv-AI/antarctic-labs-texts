15 U.S.C. § 9010 — Resources and services in languages other than English
Citation: us:usc:15:9010
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9010
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 116 (Coronavirus Economic Stabilization (Cares Act)); Subchapter I (Keeping American Workers Paid And Employed). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Administrator shall provide the resources and services made available by the Administration to small business concerns in the 10 most commonly spoken languages, other than English, in the United States, which shall include Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, and Korean.
(b) Authorization of appropriations
There is authorized to be appropriated to the Administrator $25,000,000 to carry out this section.
