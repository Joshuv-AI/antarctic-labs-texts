Editorial expression of concern (EEoC): Berberine suppresses tumorigenicity and growth of nasopharyngeal carcinoma cells by inhibiting STAT3 activation induced by tumor associated fibroblasts
Authors: Chi Man Tsang, Yuk Chun Cheung, Vivian Wai-Yan Lui, Yim Ling Yip, Guitao Zhang, Victor Weitao Lin, Kenneth Chat-Pan Cheung, Yibin Feng, Sai Wah Tsao
Published: 2026

Editorial expression of concern (EEoC): BMC Cancer 13, 619 (2013)

https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2407-13-619

The Editor is issuing this editorial expression of concern to alert readers that after publication irregularities were spotted in Fig. 4B and overlap was noted in Fig. 2 between the p-STAT3 5 mg/kg and 10 mg/kg images. Furthermore the authors have not provided documentary evidence of ethics approval for this study.

Chi Man Tsang, Yuk Chun Cheung, Vivian Wai-Yan Lui, Yim Ling Yip, Victor Weitao Lin, Kenneth Chat-Pan Cheung, Yibin Feng and Sai Wah Tsao have not responded to any correspondence from the editor about this editorial expression of concern.
