19 U.S.C. § 2701 — Authority to grant duty-free treatment
Citation: us:usc:19:2701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2701
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 15 (Caribbean Basin Economic Recovery). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may proclaim duty-free treatment (or other preferential treatment) for all eligible articles from any beneficiary country in accordance with the provisions of this chapter.
