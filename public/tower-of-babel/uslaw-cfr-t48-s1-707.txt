48 C.F.R. § 1.707 — 1.707 Signatory authority.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1.707
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1.707
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a D&F is required, it shall be signed by the appropriate official in accordance with agency regulations. Authority to sign or delegate signature authority for the various D&F's is as shown in the applicable FAR part.
