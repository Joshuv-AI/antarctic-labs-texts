40 C.F.R. § 60.29a — a Plan revisions by the Administrator.
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.29a
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.29a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After notice and opportunity for public hearing in each affected State, and meaningful engagement for any significant revision, the Administrator may revise any provision of an applicable Federal plan if:
(a) The provision was promulgated by the Administrator; and
(b) The plan, as revised, will be consistent with the Act and with the requirements of this subpart.
