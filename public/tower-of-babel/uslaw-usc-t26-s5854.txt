26 U.S.C. § 5854 — Exportation of firearms exempt from transfer tax
Citation: us:usc:26:5854
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5854
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 53 (Machine Guns, Destructive Devices, And Certain Other Firearms); Subchapter B (General Provisions And Exemptions); Part II (Exemptions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A firearm may be exported without payment of the transfer tax imposed under section 5811 provided that proof of the exportation is furnished in such form and manner as the Secretary may by regulations prescribe.
