22 C.F.R. § 124.6 — Termination of manufacturing license agreements and technical assistance agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:22:124.6
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-124.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 124 (Agreements, Off-Shore Procurement, And Other Defense Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The U.S. party to a manufacturing license or a technical assistance agreement must inform the Directorate of Defense Trade Controls in writing of the impending termination of the agreement not less than 30 days prior to the expiration date of such agreement.
