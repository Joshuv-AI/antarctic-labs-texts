36 U.S.C. § 153506 — Exclusive right to name
Citation: us:usc:36:153506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section153506
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1535 (National Tropical Botanical Garden). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation has the exclusive right to use and to allow others to use the name “National Tropical Botanical Garden”.
