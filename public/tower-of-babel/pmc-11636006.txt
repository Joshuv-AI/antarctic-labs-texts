Correction: Mechanism of cold exposure delaying wound healing in mice
Authors: Fu-Xing-Zi Li, Jun-Jie Liu, Li-Min Lei, Ye-Hui Li, Feng Xu, Xiao Lin, Rong-Rong Cui, Ming-Hui Zheng, Bei Guo, Su-Kang Shan, Ke-Xin Tang, Chang-Chun Li, Yun-Yun Wu, Jia-Yue Duan, Ye-Chi Cao, Yan-Lin Wu, Si-Yang He, Xi Chen, Feng Wu, Ling-Qing Yuan

Correction: Journal of Nanobiotechnology (2024) 22:723

10.1186/s12951-024-03009-y

In this article, Fu-Xing-Zi Li and Jun-Jie Liu should have been denoted as equally contributing authors.

The affiliation details for Jun-Jie Liu were incorrectly given as “Xiangya Stomatological Hospital and Xiangya School of Stomatology, Hunan Key Laboratory of Oral Health Research, Central South University, Changsha, Hunan, 410008, China”.

But should have been "Department of Periodontal Division, Hunan Xiangya Stomatological Hospital, Central South University, Changsha, Hunan 410008, China".

The original article has been corrected.
