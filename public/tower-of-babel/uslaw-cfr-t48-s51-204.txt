48 C.F.R. § 51.204 — 51.204 Use of interagency fleet management system (IFMS) vehicles and related services.
Citation: us:cfr:48:51.204
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-51.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 51 (Use Of Government Sources By Contractors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contractors authorized to use interagency fleet management system (IFMS) vehicles and related services shall comply with the requirements of 41 CFR 101-39 and 41 CFR 101-38.301-1 and the operator's packet furnished with each vehicle. See 41 CFR 101-6.4 for additional guidance for home-to-work use of Government vehicles.
