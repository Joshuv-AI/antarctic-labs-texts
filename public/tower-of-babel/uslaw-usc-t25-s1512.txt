25 U.S.C. § 1512 — Authorization of appropriations for interest payments
Citation: us:usc:25:1512
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1512
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 17 (Financing Economic Development Of Indians And Indian Organizations); Subchapter III (Interest Subsidies And Administrative Expenses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated for fiscal year 1985, and for each fiscal year thereafter, an amount which does not exceed $5,500,000 for purposes of making interest payments authorized under this subchapter. Sums appropriated under this section, shall remain available until expended.
