42 U.S.C. § 6611 — Establishment of Office
Citation: us:usc:42:6611
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6611
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 79 (Science And Technology Policy, Organization And Priorities); Subchapter II (Office Of Science And Technology Policy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established in the Executive Office of the President an Office of Science and Technology Policy (hereinafter referred to in this subchapter as the “Office”).
