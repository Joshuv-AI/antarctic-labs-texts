12 C.F.R. § 617.7605 — How should System institutions document whether the borrower had the financial resources to avoid foreclosure?
Citation: us:cfr:12:617.7605
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-617.7605
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 617 (Borrower Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The right of first refusal applies only to borrowers who did not have the financial resources to avoid foreclosure or voluntary conveyance. A System institution must clearly document in its files whether the borrower had the resources to avoid foreclosure or voluntary conveyance.
