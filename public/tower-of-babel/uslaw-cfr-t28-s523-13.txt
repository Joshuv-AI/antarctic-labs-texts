28 C.F.R. § 523.13 — Community corrections center good time.
Citation: us:cfr:28:523.13
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-523.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 523 (Computation Of Sentence). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Extra good time for an inmate in a Federal or contract Community Corrections Center is awarded automatically, beginning on arrival at the facility and continuing as long as the inmate is confined at the Center, unless the award is disallowed.
