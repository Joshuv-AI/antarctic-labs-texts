20 U.S.C. § 76e — Housing or exhibiting objects of art possessed by Smithsonian Institution
Citation: us:usc:20:76e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section76e
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter IV (Smithsonian Gallery Of Art). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Such objects of art as the Government or the Smithsonian Institution now possess, or such as may hereafter be acquired, may be housed or exhibited in the Gallery, with the approval of and under such regulations as the Regents and Secretary of the Smithsonian Institution may prescribe.
