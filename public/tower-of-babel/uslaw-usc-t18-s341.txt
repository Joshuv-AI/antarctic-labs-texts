18 U.S.C. § 341 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:18:341
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section341
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 17A (Common Carrier Operation Under The Influence Of Alcohol Or Drugs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, the term “common carrier” means a locomotive, a rail carrier, a sleeping car carrier, a bus transporting passengers in interstate commerce, a water common carrier, and an air common carrier.
