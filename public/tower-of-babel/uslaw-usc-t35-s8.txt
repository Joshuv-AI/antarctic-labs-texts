35 U.S.C. § 8 — Classification of patents
Citation: us:usc:35:8
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section8
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part I (United States Patent And Trademark Office); Chapter 1 (Establishment, Officers And Employees, Functions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director may revise and maintain the classification by subject matter of United States letters patent, and such other patents and printed publications as may be necessary or practicable, for the purpose of determining with readiness and accuracy the novelty of inventions for which applications for patent are filed.
