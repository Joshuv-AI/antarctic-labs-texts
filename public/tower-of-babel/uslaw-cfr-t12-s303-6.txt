12 C.F.R. § 303.6 — Investigations and examinations.
Citation: us:cfr:12:303.6
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-303.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 303 (Filing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FDIC may examine or investigate and evaluate facts related to any filing under this chapter to the extent necessary to reach an informed decision and take any action necessary or appropriate under the circumstances.
