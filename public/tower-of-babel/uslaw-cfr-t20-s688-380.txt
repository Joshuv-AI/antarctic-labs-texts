20 C.F.R. § 688.380 — What is the role of the YouthBuild grantee in the one-stop delivery system?
Citation: us:cfr:20:688.380
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-688.380
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 688 (Provisions Governing The Youthbuild Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In those local areas where the grantee operates its YouthBuild program, the grantee is a required partner of the local one-stop delivery system and is subject to the provisions relating to such partners described in part 678 of this chapter.
