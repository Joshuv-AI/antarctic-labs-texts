15 U.S.C. § 719l — Antitrust laws
Citation: us:usc:15:719l
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section719l
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 15C (Alaska Natural Gas Transportation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter, and no action taken hereunder, shall imply or effect an amendment to, or exemption from, any provision of the antitrust laws.
