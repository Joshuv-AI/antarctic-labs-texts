41 C.F.R. § 102-42.50 — -42.50 Types of appraisals.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-42.50
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-42.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-42 (42—Utilization, Donation, And Disposal Of Foreign Gifts And Decorations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your agency may allow—
(a) Written commercial appraisals conducted by an appraisal firm or trade organization; and
(b) Retail value appraisals where the gift's value can be reliably determined by reviewing current, non-discounted retail catalogs, retail price lists, or retail website valuations.
