45 C.F.R. § 160.414 — Limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:45:160.414
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-160.414
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 160 (General Administrative Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No action under this subpart may be entertained unless commenced by the Secretary, in accordance with § 160.420, within 6 years from the date of the occurrence of the violation.
