3 U.S.C. § 453 — Election of proceeding
Citation: us:usc:3:453
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title3-section453
Context: United States Code, Title 3 (The President); Chapter 5 (Extension Of Certain Rights And Protections To Presidential Offices); Subchapter III (Administrative And Judicial Dispute Resolution Procedures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 90 days after a covered employee receives notice of the end of the period of mediation, but no sooner than 30 days after receipt of such notification, such covered employee may either—
(1)
file a complaint with the appropriate agency, as determined under section 454; or
(2)
file a civil action under section 1346(g) of title 28.
