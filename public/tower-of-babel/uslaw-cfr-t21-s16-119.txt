21 C.F.R. § 16.119 — Reconsideration and stay of action.
Citation: us:cfr:21:16.119
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-16.119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 16 (Regulatory Hearing Before The Food And Drug Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After any final administrative action that is the subject of a hearing under this part, any party may petition the Commissioner for reconsideration of any part or all of the decision or action under § 10.33 or may petition for a stay of the decision or action under § 10.35.
