54 U.S.C. § 200510 — Inapplicability of matching provisions
Citation: us:usc:54:200510
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section200510
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 2005 (Urban Park And Recreation Recovery Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amounts authorized for Guam, American Samoa, the Virgin Islands, and the Northern Mariana Islands are not subject to the matching provisions of this chapter, and may be subject only to such conditions, reports, plans, and agreements, if any, as the Secretary may determine.
