14 C.F.R. § 142.71 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:142.71
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-142.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 142 (Training Centers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the training center recordkeeping requirements for trainees enrolled in a course, and instructors and evaluators designated to instruct a course, approved in accordance with subpart B of this part.
