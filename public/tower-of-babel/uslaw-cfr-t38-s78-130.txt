38 C.F.R. § 78.130 — Faith-based organizations.
Citation: us:cfr:38:78.130
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-78.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 78 (Staff Sergeant Parker Gordon Fox Suicide Prevention Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Organizations that are faith-based are eligible, on the same basis as any other organization, to participate in SSG Fox SPGP under this part in accordance with 38 CFR part 50.
