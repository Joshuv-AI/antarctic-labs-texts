12 C.F.R. § 708a.111 — a.111 Limit on compensation of officials.
Citation: us:cfr:12:708a.111
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-708a.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 708a (PART 708a—BANK CONVERSIONS AND MERGERS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No director or senior management official of an insured credit union may receive any economic benefit in connection with the conversion of a credit union other than compensation and other benefits paid to directors or senior management officials of the converted institution in the ordinary course of business.
