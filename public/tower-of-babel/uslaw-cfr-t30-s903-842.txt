30 C.F.R. § 903.842 — Federal inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:30:903.842
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-903.842
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 903 (Arizona). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Part 842 of this chapter, Federal Inspections, applies to all coal exploration and surface coal mining and reclamation operations.
(b) In addition to the requirements of part 842 of this chapter, OSM will furnish copies of inspection reports when requested by a designated Arizona State agency with jurisdiction over mining.
