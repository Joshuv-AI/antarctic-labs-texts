16 U.S.C. § 4603 — Administrative services
Citation: us:usc:16:4603
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4603
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 66 (Take Pride In America Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior shall provide such facilities, administrative services, personnel, and support to the TPIA Program as the Secretary determines is necessary and appropriate.
