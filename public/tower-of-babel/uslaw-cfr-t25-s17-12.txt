25 C.F.R. § 17.12 — Approval.
Citation: us:cfr:25:17.12
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-17.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 17 (Action On Wills Of Osage Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After hearings have been concluded in conformity with this part the superintendent shall approve or disapprove the wills of deceased Osage Indians.
