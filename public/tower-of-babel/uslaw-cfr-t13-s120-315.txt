13 C.F.R. § 120.315 — Interest rate and loan limit.
Citation: us:cfr:13:120.315
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-120.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 120 (Business Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The interest rate on direct DAL loans is three percent. There is an administrative limit of $150,000 on a direct DAL loan.
