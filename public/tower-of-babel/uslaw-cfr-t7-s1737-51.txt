7 C.F.R. § 1737.51 — Approval of loan design.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1737.51
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1737.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1737 (Pre-Loan Policies And Procedures Common To Insured And Guaranteed Telecommunications Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

RUS shall notify the borrower when the preloan data concerning the system design and costs and subscriber projections have been approved. If found acceptable, RUS will approve the LD with any required changes. A copy of the approved LD, with any significant changes, as determined by RUS, will be returned to the borrower.
