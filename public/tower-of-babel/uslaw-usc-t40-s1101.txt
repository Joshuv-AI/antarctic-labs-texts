40 U.S.C. § 1101 — Policy
Citation: us:usc:40:1101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section1101
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 11 (Selection Of Architects And Engineers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The policy of the Federal Government is to publicly announce all requirements for architectural and engineering services and to negotiate contracts for architectural and engineering services on the basis of demonstrated competence and qualification for the type of professional services required and at fair and reasonable prices.
