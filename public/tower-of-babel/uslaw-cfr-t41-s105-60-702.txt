41 C.F.R. § 105-60.702 — -60.702 Requirements to preserve FOIA records.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-60.702
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-60.702
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-60 (60—Public Availability Of Agency Records And Informational Materals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

GSA shall preserve all correspondence pertaining to the requests that it receives under this subpart, as well as copies of all requested records, until disposition or destruction is authorized pursuant to title 44 of the United States Code or the General Records Schedule 4.2 of the NARA. GSA shall not dispose of or destroy records while they are the subject of a pending request, appeal, or lawsuit under FOIA.
