46 C.F.R. § 2.01-50 — -50 Persons other than crew on towing, oyster, or fishing steam vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:46:2.01-50
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-2.01-50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 2 (Vessel Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A steam vessel engaged in towing, oyster dredging and planting, and fishing may be permitted to carry persons in addition to its crew.
(b) The application for a permit to carry such persons may be included in the application described in § 2.01-1. If granted it is endorsed on the certificate of inspection.
