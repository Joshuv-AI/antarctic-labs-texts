42 C.F.R. § 21.44 — Clinical or other practical demonstration.
Citation: us:cfr:42:21.44
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-21.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 21 (Commissioned Officers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the discretion of the Surgeon General a candidate for appointment to any grade up to and including the senior assistant grade in the Regular Corps may be required to perform successfully a clinical or other practical demonstration which, if required, shall constitute a part of the professional examination.
