37 U.S.C. § 803 — Commissioned officers of the Army, Air Force, or Space Force: forfeiture of pay when dropped from rolls
Citation: us:usc:37:803
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section803
Context: United States Code, Title 37 (Pay And Allowances Of The Uniformed Services); Chapter 15 (Prohibitions And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A commissioned officer of the Army, the Air Force, or the Space Force who is dropped from the rolls under section 1161(b) of title 10 for absence without authority for three months forfeits all pay due or to become due.
