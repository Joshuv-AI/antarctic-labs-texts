48 C.F.R. § 3437.601 — 3437.601 General.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3437 (Service Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the Department's policy that all new service contracts be performance-based, with clearly defined deliverable and performance standards. Any deviations from this policy must be fully justified in writing and approved by the HCA.
