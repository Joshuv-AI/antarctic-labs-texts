46 C.F.R. § 140.1000 — Statutory penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:46:140.1000
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-140.1000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 140 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Violations of the provisions of this subchapter will subject the violator to the applicable penalty provisions of Subtitle II of Title 46, and Title 18, United States Code.
