12 U.S.C. § 2711 — Nonapplicability of other laws
Citation: us:usc:12:2711
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2711
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 28 (Emergency Mortgage Relief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any provision of law which limits the nature, amount, term, form, or rate of interest, or the nature, amount, or form of security of loans or advances of credit, loans, or advances of credit may be made in accordance with the provisions of this chapter without regard to such provision of law.
