31 U.S.C. § 6105 — Oversight responsibility of Director
Citation: us:usc:31:6105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section6105
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 61 (Program Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall have oversight responsibility for the exercise of all authorities and responsibilities in this chapter not specifically assigned to the Director.
