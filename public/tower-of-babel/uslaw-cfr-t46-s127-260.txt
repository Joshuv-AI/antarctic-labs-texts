46 C.F.R. § 127.260 — Ventilation for accommodations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:127.260
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-127.260
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 127 (Construction And Arrangements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each accommodation space must be adequately ventilated in a manner suitable for the purpose of the space.
(b) Each vessel of 100 or more gross tons must be provided with a mechanical ventilation system unless the cognizant OCMI is satisfied that a natural system, such as opening windows, portholes, or doors, will accomplish adequate ventilation in ordinary weather.
