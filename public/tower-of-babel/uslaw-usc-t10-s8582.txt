10 U.S.C. § 8582 — Deferred annuity policy required
Citation: us:usc:10:8582
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8582
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Education And Training); Chapter 857 (Retirement Of Civilian Members Of The Teaching Staffs Of The United States Naval Academy And United States Naval Postgraduate School). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each civilian member, as a part of his contract of employment, shall carry, during his employment, a deferred annuity policy, having no cash surrender or loan provision, in a joint-stock life insurance corporation that is incorporated under the laws of a State and has a charter restriction that its business must be conducted without profit to its stockholders.
