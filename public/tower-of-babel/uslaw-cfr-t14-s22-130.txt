14 C.F.R. § 22.130 — Suitability and durability of materials.
Citation: us:cfr:14:22.130
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-22.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 22 (Design, Production, And Airworthiness Requirements For Non-Type Certificated Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The suitability and durability of materials used for products and articles must account for the likely environmental conditions expected in service, the failure of which could prevent continued safe flight and landing.
