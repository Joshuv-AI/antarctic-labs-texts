(a) Insert the clause at 3052.225-71, Make PPE in America, in solicitations and contracts, regardless of dollar value, when procuring any item covered under 3025.7102-1(a).
(b) Insert the provision at 3052.225-72, Make PPE in America Certificate, in solicitations containing the clause at 3052.225-71.
