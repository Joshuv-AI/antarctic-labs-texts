40 C.F.R. § 85.2302 — Definition of model year.
Citation: us:cfr:40:85.2302
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-85.2302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 85 (Control Of Air Pollution From Mobile Sources). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Model year means the manufacturer's annual production period (as determined under § 85.2304) which includes January 1 of such calendar year, provided, that if the manufacturer has no annual production period, the term “model year” shall mean the calendar year.
