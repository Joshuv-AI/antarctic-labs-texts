31 C.F.R. § 0.201 — Acting within scope of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:31:0.201
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-0.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 0 (Department Of The Treasury Employee Rules Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee shall not engage in any conduct or activity that is in excess of his or her authority or is otherwise contrary to any law, regulation, or Department policy.
