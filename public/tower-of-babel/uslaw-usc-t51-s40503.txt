51 U.S.C. § 40503 — Biomedical research fellowships
Citation: us:usc:51:40503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section40503
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 405 (Biomedical Research In Space). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator and the Director of the National Institutes of Health shall create a joint program of graduate research fellowships in biomedical research described in section 40501(a) of this title. Fellowships under such program may provide for participation in approved research conferences and symposia.
