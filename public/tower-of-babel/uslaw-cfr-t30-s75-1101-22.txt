30 C.F.R. § 75.1101-22 — -22 Inspection of dry powder chemical systems.
Citation: us:cfr:30:75.1101-22
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-75.1101-22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 75 (Mandatory Safety Standards—Underground Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each dry powder chemical system shall be examined weekly and a functional test of the complete system shall be conducted at least once each year.
(b) Where the dry powder chemical system has been actuated, all components of the system shall be cleaned immediately by flushing all powder from pipes and hoses and all hose damaged by fire shall be replaced.
