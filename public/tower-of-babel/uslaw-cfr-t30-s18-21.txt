30 C.F.R. § 18.21 — Machines equipped with powered dust collectors.
Citation: us:cfr:30:18.21
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-18.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 18 (Electric Motor-Driven Mine Equipment And Accessories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Powered dust collectors on machines submitted for approval shall meet the applicable requirements of Part 33 of this chapter (Bureau of Mines Schedule 25B), and shall bear the approval number assigned by MSHA.
