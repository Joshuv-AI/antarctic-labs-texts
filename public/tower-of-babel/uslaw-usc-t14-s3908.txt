14 U.S.C. § 3908 — Vessel deemed public vessel
Citation: us:usc:14:3908
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section3908
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 39 (Coast Guard Auxiliary). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

While assigned to authorized Coast Guard duty, any motorboat or yacht shall be deemed to be a public vessel of the United States and a vessel of the Coast Guard within the meaning of sections 937 and 938 of this title and other applicable provisions of law.
