15 U.S.C. § 713a–11a — Interest prohibited when reimbursing Corporation for net realized losses
Citation: us:usc:15:713a–11a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section713a–11a
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 15 (Economic Recovery); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After September 30, 1964, the portion of borrowings from Treasury equal to the unreimbursed realized losses recorded on the books of the Commodity Credit Corporation after September 30 of the fiscal year in which such losses are realized, shall not bear interest and interest shall not be accrued or paid thereon.
