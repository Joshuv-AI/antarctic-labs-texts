15 U.S.C. § 1681r — Unauthorized disclosures by officers or employees
Citation: us:usc:15:1681r
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1681r
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 41 (Consumer Credit Protection); Subchapter III (Credit Reporting Agencies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any officer or employee of a consumer reporting agency who knowingly and willfully provides information concerning an individual from the agency’s files to a person not authorized to receive that information shall be fined under title 18, imprisoned for not more than 2 years, or both.
