Pacific Fishery Management Council; Public Meeting

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Pacific Fishery Management Council's (Pacific Council) Ad 
Hoc Marine Planning Committee (MPC) will hold an online public meeting.

DATES: The online meeting will be held Friday, October 2, 2026, from 9 
a.m. to 5 p.m. Pacific Time, or until business for the day has been 
completed.

ADDRESSES: This meeting will be held online. Specific meeting 
information, including a proposed agenda and directions on how to 
attend the meeting and system requirements, will be provided in the 
meeting announcement on the Pacific Council's website (see https://www.pcouncil.org).
    Council address: Pacific Fishery Management Council, 7700 NE 
Ambassador Place, Suite 101, Portland, Oregon 97220-1384.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: For general inquiries: Kerry Griffin, 
Staff Officer, Pacific Council, (503) 820-2409.
    For technical assistance in accessing the meeting: Mr. Hayden York, 
[email protected], 503-820-2424.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The purpose of this online meeting is for 
the MPC to consider current marine planning issues, including oil and 
gas leasing, NOAA Aquaculture Opportunity Areas, deep-sea mineral 
mining, and other ocean development or planning activities that may 
impact commercial or recreational fishing and West Coast fishing 
dependent communities. The MPC will provide a report in the Advanced 
Briefing Book for the Pacific Council's November 2026 meeting.
    Although non-emergency issues not contained in the meeting agenda 
may be discussed, those issues may not be the subject of formal action 
during this meeting. Action will be restricted to those issues 
specifically listed in this document and any issues arising after 
publication of this document that require emergency action under 
section 305(c) of the Magnuson-Stevens Fishery Conservation and 
Management Act, provided the public has been notified of the intent to 
take final action to address the emergency.

Special Accommodations

    Requests for sign language interpretation or other auxiliary aids 
should be directed to Mr. Hayden York (see FOR FURTHER INFORMATION 
CONTACT) at least 10 days prior to the meeting date.
