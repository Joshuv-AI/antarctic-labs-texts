10 U.S.C. § 462 — Financial assistance to certain employees in acquisition of critical skills
Citation: us:usc:10:462
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section462
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 22 (National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency); Subchapter III (Personnel Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense may establish an undergraduate training program with respect to civilian employees of the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency that is similar in purpose, conditions, content, and administration to the program established by the Secretary of Defense under section 16 of the National Security Agency Act of 1959 (50 U.S.C. 3614) for civilian employees of the National Security Agency.
