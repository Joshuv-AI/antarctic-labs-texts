34 C.F.R. § 637.1 — What is the Minority Science and Engineering Improvement Program (MSEIP)?
Citation: us:cfr:34:637.1
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-637.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 637 (Minority Science And Engineering Improvement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Minority Science and Engineering Improvement Program (MSEIP) is designed to effect long-range improvement in science and engineering education at predominantly minority institutions, and to increase the flow of underrepresented ethnic minorities, particularly minority women, into scientific and technological careers.
