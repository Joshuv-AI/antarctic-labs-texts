Fast microdosimetry algorithms in particle therapy: Benchmark and applicability guidelines
Authors: Giorgio Cartechini, Marta Missiaggia, Francesco Giuseppe Cordoni
Published: 2026

Microdosimetry is pivotal in bridging the gap between the physical characteristics of radiation fields and their biological effects, particularly in charged particle therapy. However, the significant computational demands of calculating microdosimetric spectra have limited its widespread application in clinical practice.

Recent advances have focused on balancing computational efficiency and simulation accuracy, leading to the development of several fast algorithms for calculating microdosimetric quantities.

In this work, we present a systematic evaluation of the main approaches available in the literature: (i) Super Track, 1 (ii) MONAS, 2 (iii) the Analytical Microdosimetric Function (AMF), 3 and (iv) specific energy Look‐Up Tables (LUTs) as proposed in. 4 The accuracy of these approaches is benchmarked through a detailed comparison of microdosimetric spectra and fluence and dose‐average lineal energy (yF and yD) values against experimental reference data for proton, helium, and carbon ion beams. 5 , 6

In addition to the physical validation, the computed microdosimetric quantities are applied within the main microdosimetry‐based radiobiological models to predict cell survival. 2 This integrated approach enables a critical assessment of each algorithm's physical accuracy and biological relevance. This study will define each model's applicability range and provide practical recommendations for its appropriate use in research and clinical settings.
