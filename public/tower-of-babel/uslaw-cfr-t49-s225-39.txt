49 C.F.R. § 225.39 — FRA policy on covered data.
Citation: us:cfr:49:225.39
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-225.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 225 (Railroad Accidents/Incidents: Reports Classification, And Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FRA will not include covered data (as defined in § 225.5) in its periodic summaries of data on the number of occupational injuries and illnesses.
