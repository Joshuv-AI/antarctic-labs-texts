29 C.F.R. § 780.818 — Employees not engaged in processing.
Citation: us:cfr:29:780.818
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-780.818
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 780 (Exemptions Applicable To Agriculture, Processing Of Agricultural Commodities, And Related Subjects Under The Fair Labor Standards Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees engaged in operations which are not an integral part of processing of the named commodities will not come within the exemption. The following activities are not considered exempt under section 13(b)(15):
(a) Office and general clerical work.
(b) Feeding and housing millhands and visitors (typically this is called the “boarding house”).
(c) Hauling raw sugar or molasses away from the mill.
(d) Any work outside the grinding season.
