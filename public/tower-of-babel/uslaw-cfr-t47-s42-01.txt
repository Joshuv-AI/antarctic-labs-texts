47 C.F.R. § 42.01 — Applicability.

Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 42 (Preservation Of Records Of Communication Common Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes the regulations governing the preservation of records of communication common carriers that are fully subject to the jurisdiction of the Commission.
