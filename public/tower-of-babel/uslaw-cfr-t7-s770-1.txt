7 C.F.R. § 770.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:770.1
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-770.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 770 (Indian Tribal Land Acquisition Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part contains the Agency's policies and procedures for making and servicing loans to assist a Native American tribe or tribal corporation with the acquisition of land interests within the tribal reservation or Alaskan community.
