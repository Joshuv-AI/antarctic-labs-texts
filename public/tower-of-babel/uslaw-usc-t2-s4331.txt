2 U.S.C. § 4331 — Computation of compensation for stenographic assistance of committees payable from Senate contingent fund
Citation: us:usc:2:4331
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4331
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 43 (Congressional Committees); Subchapter III (Senate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Compensation for stenographic assistance of committees paid out of the items under “Contingent Expenses of the Senate” on and after June 27, 1956 shall be computed at such rates and in accordance with such regulations as may be prescribed by the Committee on Rules and Administration, notwithstanding, and without regard to any other provision of law.
