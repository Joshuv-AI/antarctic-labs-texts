40 C.F.R. § 721.50 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:721.50
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-721.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 721 (Significant New Uses Of Chemical Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart B identifies certain significant new uses of chemical substances identified in subpart E of this part. The provisions of this subpart B apply only when referenced as applying to a chemical substance identified in subpart E of this part.
