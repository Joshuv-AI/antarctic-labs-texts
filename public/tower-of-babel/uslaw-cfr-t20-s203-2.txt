20 C.F.R. § 203.2 — General definition of employee.
Citation: us:cfr:20:203.2
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-203.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 203 (Employees Under The Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual shall be an employee whenever (a) he is engaged in performing compensated service for an employer or (b) he is in an employment relation to an employer, or (c) he is an employee representative, or (d) he is an officer of an employer.
