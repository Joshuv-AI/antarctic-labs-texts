22 C.F.R. § 92.28 — Signature of affiant on affidavit.
Citation: us:cfr:22:92.28
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-92.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 92 (Notarial And Related Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The signature of the affiant is indispensable. The affiant should always sign the affidavit in the presence of the notarizing officer.
