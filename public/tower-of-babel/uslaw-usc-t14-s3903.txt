14 U.S.C. § 3903 — Eligibility; enrollments
Citation: us:usc:14:3903
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section3903
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 39 (Coast Guard Auxiliary). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Auxiliary shall be composed of nationals of the United States, as defined in section 101(a)(22) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(22)), and aliens lawfully admitted for permanent residence, as defined in section 101(a)(20) of the Immigration and Nationality Act (8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(20))—
(1)
who—
(A)
are owners, sole or part, of motorboats, yachts, aircraft, or radio stations; or
(B)
by reason of their special training or experience are deemed by the Commandant to be qualified for duty in the Auxiliary; and
(2)
who may be enrolled therein pursuant to applicable regulations.
