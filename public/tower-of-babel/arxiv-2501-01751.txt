Gap theorems for complete submanifolds in the hyperbolic space
Jianling Liu, Yong Luo

Abstract
Based on the seminal Simons’ formula, Shen [ 18 ] and Lin-Xia [ 15 ] obtained gap theorems for compact minimal submanifolds in the unit sphere in the late 1980’s. Then due to the effect of Xu [ 21 ] , Ni [ 16 ] , Yun [ 25 ] and Xu-Gu [ 22 ] , we achieved a comprehensive understanding of gap phenomena of complete submanifolds with parallel mean curvature vector field in the sphere or in the Euclidean space. But such kind of results in case of the hyperbolic space were obtained by Wang-Xia [ 20 ] , Lin-Wang [ 14 ] and Xu-Xu [ 24 ] until relatively recently and are not quite complete so far. In this paper first we continue to study gap theorems for complete submanifolds with parallel mean curvature vector field in the hyperbolic space, which generalize or extend several results in the literature. Second we prove a gap theorem for complete hypersurfaces with constant scalar curvature n ⁡ ( 1 − n ) n(1-n) in the hyperbolic space, which extends related results due to Bai-Luo [ 3 ] in cases of the Euclidean space and the unit sphere. Such kind of results in case of the hyperbolic space are more complicated, due to some extra bad terms in the Simons’ formula, and one of main ingredients of our proofs is an estimate for the first eigenvalue of complete submanifolds in the hyperbolic space obtained by Lin [ 13 ] . Keywords: Submanifolds, parallel mean curvature vector field, constant scalar curvature, the hyperbolic space.

\displaystyle 1\leq A,B,C,\cdots\leq n+m;\qquad 1\leq i,j,k,l,\cdots\leq n;\qquad n+1\leq\alpha\leq n+m.

The structure equations of

\displaystyle dw_{A}=-\sum\limits_{B}w_{AB}\wedge w_{B},\qquad w_{AB}+w_{BA}=0,

\displaystyle dw_{AB}=-\sum\limits_{C}w_{AC}\wedge w_{CB}+\frac{1}{2}\sum\limits_{C,D}K_{ABCD}w_{C}\wedge w_{D},

The structure equations of

\displaystyle dw_{i}=-\sum\limits_{j}w_{ij}\wedge w_{j},\qquad w_{ij}+w_{ji}=0,

\displaystyle dw_{ij}=-\sum\limits_{k}w_{ik}\wedge w_{kj}+\frac{1}{2}\sum\limits_{k,l}R_{ijkl}w_{k}\wedge w_{l},

\displaystyle R_{ijkl}=-(\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}-\delta_{il}\delta_{jk})+\sum\limits_{\alpha}(h_{ik}^{\alpha}h_{jl}^{\alpha}-h_{il}^{\alpha}h_{jk}^{\alpha}).

\displaystyle 2(L-1)\int_{M}\phi|Å|\langle\nabla|Å|,\nabla\phi\rangle\text{d}v

Substituting it into the above inequality, we get

\displaystyle\int_{B_{R}(q)}|\nabla|Å||^{2}\text{d}v

\displaystyle\leq\rho\int_{B_{2R}(q)}|\nabla\phi|^{2}|Å|^{2}\text{d}v

|Å|=0,
i.e.

is umbilical. Furthermore

Proof of Theorem
1.11

Proof. Assume that
M^{n}
is a complete hypersurface in
\mathbb{H}^{n+1}
with scalar curvature
R=n(1-n)
. Let
B_{r}
be a geodesic ball centered at a fixed point of
M^{n}
with radius
r
, and choose
\psi
to be a cut-off function with the properties

\displaystyle\gamma=\begin{cases}1,\qquad\text{on }B_{R},\\
0,\qquad\text{on }M\setminus B_{2R},\\
0\leq\psi\leq 1,|\nabla\psi|\leq\frac{C_{15}(n)}{R},\qquad\text{on }B_{2R}\setminus B_{R}.\end{cases}

We denote by
f=|H|
. Multiplying
\psi^{2}f^{2k-2}(k\geq 1)
on both sides of inequality (
2.7
) and integrating it over
M
we obtain

\displaystyle\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{2k-2}\Box H\text{d}v\geq n(1-n)\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{2k}\text{d}v-2n^{3}\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{2k+2}\text{d}v.

(4.1)

By integrating by parts and Cauchy-Schwarz’s inequality, we have

\displaystyle\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{2k-2}\Box H\text{d}v

\displaystyle=2\int_{M}\psi f^{2k-2}(-P_{1})(\nabla\psi,\nabla H)\text{d}v+2(k-1)\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{2k-3}(-P_{1})(\nabla H,\nabla|H|)\text{d}v

\displaystyle+\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{2k-2}\sum\limits_{i,j}(-P_{1})_{ijj}H_{i}\text{d}v

\displaystyle=2\int_{M}\psi f^{2k-2}(-P_{1})(\nabla\psi,\nabla H)\text{d}v+2(k-1)\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{2k-3}(-P_{1})(\nabla H,\nabla|H|)\text{d}v

\displaystyle\leq 2\int_{M}\psi f^{2k-2}(-P_{1})(\nabla\psi,\nabla H)\text{d}v-2\delta(k-1)\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{2k-2}|\nabla H|^{2}\text{d}v

\displaystyle\leq 2\int_{M}\psi f^{2k-2}|P_{1}||\nabla\psi||\nabla H|\text{d}v-2\delta(k-1)\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{2k-2}|\nabla H|^{2}\text{d}v,

where in the second equality we used

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i,j}(-P_{1})_{ijj}H_{i}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i,j}(h_{ijj}H_{i}-nH_{j}H_{i}\delta_{ij})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum\limits_{i,j}(h_{jji}H_{i}-nH_{j}H_{i}\delta_{ij})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 0.

Note that

\displaystyle|P_{1}|^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle|A|^{2}-2n^{2}H^{2}+n^{3}H^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle n^{2}(n-1)H^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle n^{2}(n-1)f^{2}.

By Cauchy-Schwarz’s inequality for any
\tau
such that
0<\tau<2
, we have

\displaystyle\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{2k-2}\Box H\text{d}v

\displaystyle\leq 2n\sqrt{n-1}\int_{M}\psi f^{2k-1}|\nabla\psi||\nabla H|\text{d}v-2\delta(k-1)\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{2k-2}|\nabla H|^{2}\text{d}v

\displaystyle\leq\tau\delta(k-1)\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{2k-2}|\nabla H|^{2}\text{d}v+\frac{n^{2}(n-1)}{\tau\delta(k-1)}\int_{M}|\nabla\psi|^{2}f^{2k}\text{d}v

\displaystyle-2\delta(k-1)\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{2k-2}|\nabla H|^{2}\text{d}v

\displaystyle\leq(\tau-2)\delta(k-1)\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{2k-2}|\nabla f|^{2}\text{d}v+\frac{n^{2}(n-1)}{\tau\delta(k-1)}\int_{M}|\nabla\psi|^{2}f^{2k}\text{d}v

\displaystyle=(\tau-2)\delta\frac{k-1}{k^{2}}\int_{M}\psi^{2}|\nabla f^{k}|^{2}\text{d}v+\frac{n^{2}(n-1)}{\tau\delta(k-1)}\int_{M}|\nabla\psi|^{2}f^{2k}\text{d}v.

By the above inequality and (
4.1
) we finally arrive at

\displaystyle(2-\tau)\delta\frac{k-1}{k^{2}}\int_{M}\psi^{2}|\nabla f^{k}|^{2}\text{d}v+n(1-n)\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{2k}\text{d}v

\displaystyle\leq 2n^{3}\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{2k+2}\text{d}v+\frac{n^{2}(n-1)}{\tau\delta(k-1)}\int_{M}|\nabla\psi|^{2}f^{2k}\text{d}v.

Let
k=\frac{n}{2}
in the above inequality we obtain

\displaystyle(2-\tau)\delta\frac{2n-4}{n^{2}}\int_{M}\psi^{2}|\nabla f^{\frac{n}{2}}|^{2}\text{d}v+n(1-n)\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{n}\text{d}v

\displaystyle\leq 2n^{3}\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{n+2}\text{d}v+\frac{2n^{2}(n-1)}{\tau\delta(n-2)}\int_{M}|\nabla\psi|^{2}f^{n}\text{d}v.

(4.2)

Assume that
D(n)nC_{12}(n,\delta)<1
. By Lemma
2.2
we have

\displaystyle\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{n+2}\text{d}v

\displaystyle=\int_{M}(\psi f^{\frac{n}{2}})^{2}f^{2}\text{d}v

\displaystyle\leq(\int_{M}f^{n}\text{d}v)^{\frac{2}{n}}(\int_{M}(\psi f^{\frac{n}{2}})^{\frac{2n}{n-2}}\text{d}v)^{\frac{n-2}{n}}

\displaystyle\leq C_{12}(n,\delta)^{2}C_{13}(n)\int_{M}|\nabla(\psi f^{\frac{n}{2}})|^{2}\text{d}v

\displaystyle\leq 2C_{12}(n,\delta)^{2}C_{13}(n)(\int_{M}|\nabla\psi|^{2}f^{n}\text{d}v+\int_{M}\psi^{2}|\nabla f^{\frac{n}{2}}|^{2}\text{d}v).

Combing the above two inequalities we get

\displaystyle E_{1}\int_{M}\psi^{2}|\nabla f^{\frac{n}{2}}|^{2}\text{d}v+E_{2}\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{n}\text{d}v\leq E_{3}\int_{M}|\nabla\psi|^{2}f^{n}\text{d}v,

(4.3)

where

E_{1}=(2-\tau)\delta\frac{2n-4}{n^{2}}-4n^{3}C_{12}(n,\delta)^{2}C_{13}(n),

E_{2}=(1-n)n,

E_{3}=\frac{2n^{2}(n-1)}{\tau\delta(n-2)}+4n^{3}C_{12}(n,\delta)^{2}C_{13}(n).

Using Lemma
2.3
and Cauchy-Schwarz’s inequality we have for any
\kappa>0

\displaystyle\int_{M}\psi^{2}f^{n}\text{d}v

\displaystyle\leq\frac{4}{(n-1)^{2}(1-nD(n)||H||_{n})^{2}}\int_{M}|\nabla(\psi f^{\frac{n}{2}})|^{2}\text{d}v

\displaystyle\leq\frac{4}{(n-1)^{2}(1-nD(n)||H||_{n})^{2}}[(1+\frac{1}{\kappa})\int_{M}|\nabla\psi|^{2}f^{n}\text{d}v+(1+\kappa)\int_{M}\psi^{2}|\nabla f^{\frac{n}{2}}|^{2}\text{d}v].

Substituting the above inequality into (
4.3
) yields

F_{1}\int_{M}\psi^{2}|\nabla f^{\frac{n}{2}}|^{2}\text{d}v\leq F_{2}\int_{M}|\nabla\psi|^{2}f^{n}\text{d}v,

where

\displaystyle F_{1}

\displaystyle=(1-n)n\frac{4(1+\kappa)}{(n-1)^{2}(1-nD(n)||H||_{n})^{2}}+(2-\tau)\delta\frac{2n-4}{n^{2}}-4n^{3}C_{12}(n,\delta)^{2}C_{13}(n),

\displaystyle F_{2}

\displaystyle=-[(1-n)n\frac{4(1+\frac{1}{\kappa})}{(n-1)^{2}(1-nD(n)||H||_{n})^{2}}]+\frac{2n^{2}(n-1)}{\tau\delta(n-2)}+4n^{3}C_{12}(n,\delta)^{2}C_{13}(n).

Since
\delta>\frac{n^{3}}{(n-1)(n-2)}
, when
\tau,\kappa
and
C_{12}(n,\delta)
are sufficiently small we have
F_{1}>0
.
Then there exists a constant
C_{16}(n,\delta)
depending only on
n
and
\delta
such that

\int_{M}\psi^{2}|\nabla|H|^{\frac{n}{2}}|^{2}\text{d}v\leq\frac{C_{16}(n,\delta)}{R^{2}}\int_{M}|H|^{n}\text{d}v.

2.2
) we see that
