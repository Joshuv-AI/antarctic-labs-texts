43 C.F.R. § 3809.335 — What happens when my notice expires?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3809.335
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3809.335
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3800 (Mining Claims Under The General Mining Laws). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When your notice expires, you must—
(1) Cease operations, except reclamation; and
(2) Complete reclamation promptly according to your notice.
(b) Your reclamation obligations continue beyond the expiration or any termination of your notice until you satisfy them.
