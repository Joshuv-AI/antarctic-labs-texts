Introduction

Total variation regularization has become a standard in image processing and inverse problems, valued for its capability to suppress noise without losing edge sharpness. A starting point for this development was the publication of the Rudin-Osher-Fatemi (ROF) denoising model [55], which consists in minimizing the total variation balanced by a squared \documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$L^2$$\end{document}L2 distance from the noisy datum.

Taut strings and invariant φ φ-minimal sets The taut string problem, which seems to originate in the operations research literature of the 1950 s, see [29, 51], can be stated as follows.
