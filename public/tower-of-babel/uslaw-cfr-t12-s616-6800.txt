12 C.F.R. § 616.6800 — Disclosure requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:12:616.6800
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-616.6800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 616 (Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each System institution must give to each lessee a copy of all lease documents signed by the lessee within a reasonable time following lease closing.
(b) Each System institution must make its decision on a lease application as soon as possible and provide prompt written notice of its decision to the applicant.
