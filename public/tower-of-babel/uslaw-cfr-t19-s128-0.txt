19 C.F.R. § 128.0 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:19:128.0
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-128.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 128 (Express Consignments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth requirements and procedures for the clearance of imported merchandise carried by express consignment operators and carriers, including couriers, under special procedures.
