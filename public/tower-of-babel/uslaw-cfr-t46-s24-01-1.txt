46 C.F.R. § 24.01-1 — -1 Purpose of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:24.01-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-24.01-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 24 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the regulations in this subchapter is to set forth uniform minimum requirements for uninspected commercial vessels, certain motor vessels, vessels propelled by sail carrying passengers for hire, and barges carrying passengers for hire.
