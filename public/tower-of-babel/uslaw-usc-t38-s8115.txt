38 U.S.C. § 8115 — Acceptance of certain property
Citation: us:usc:38:8115
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section8115
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part VI (Acquisition And Disposition Of Property); Chapter 81 (Acquisition And Operation Of Hospital And Domiciliary Facilities; Procurement And Supply; Enhanced-Use Leases Of Real Property); Subchapter I (Acquisition And Operation Of Medical Facilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may accept from any State or other political subdivision, or from any person, any building, structure, equipment, or grounds suitable for the care of disabled persons, with due regard to fire or other hazards, state of repair, and all other pertinent considerations. The President may designate which agency of the Federal Government shall have the control and management of any property so accepted.
