14 C.F.R. § 145.101 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:14:145.101
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-145.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 145 (Repair Stations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A certificated repair station must provide housing, facilities, equipment, materials, and data that meet the applicable requirements for the issuance of the certificate and ratings the repair station holds.
