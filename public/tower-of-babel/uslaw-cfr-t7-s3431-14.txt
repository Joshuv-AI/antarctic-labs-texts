7 C.F.R. § 3431.14 — Priority.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3431.14
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3431.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3431 (Veterinary Medicine Loan Repayment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to NVMSA, the Secretary will give priority to agreements with veterinarians for the practice of food animal medicine in veterinarian shortage situations, as determined by the Secretary. The Secretary may establish additional criteria in the RFA for assigning priority levels to veterinarian shortage situations nominated for award.
