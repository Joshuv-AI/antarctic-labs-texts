15 U.S.C. § 3503 — “Antitrust law” defined
Citation: us:usc:15:3503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section3503
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 61 (Soft Drink Interbrand Competition). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, the term “antitrust law” means the Sherman Act (15 U.S.C. 1 et seq.), the Clayton Act (15 U.S.C. 12 et seq.), and the Federal Trade Commission Act (15 U.S.C. 41 et seq.).
