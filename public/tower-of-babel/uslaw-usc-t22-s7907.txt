22 U.S.C. § 7907 — Fellowship and exchange programs
Citation: us:usc:22:7907
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section7907
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 86 (Climate Change Technology Deployment In Developing Countries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State, in coordination with the Secretary of Energy, the Secretary of Commerce, and the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency, shall carry out fellowship and exchange programs under which officials from developing countries visit the United States to acquire expertise and knowledge of best practices to reduce greenhouse gas intensity in their countries.
