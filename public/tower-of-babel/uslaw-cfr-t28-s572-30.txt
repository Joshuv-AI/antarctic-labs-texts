28 C.F.R. § 572.30 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:28:572.30
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-572.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 572 (Parole). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Bureau of Prisons provides the U.S. Parole Commission with a Violator Report for use at the revocation hearing of a parole or mandatory release violator, when that hearing is conducted in an institution of the Bureau of Prisons.
