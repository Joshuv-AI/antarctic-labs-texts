40 C.F.R. § 13.3 — Interagency claims.
Citation: us:cfr:40:13.3
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-13.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 13 (Claims Collection Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This regulation does not apply to debts owed EPA by other Federal agencies. Such debts will be resolved by negotiation between the agencies or by referral to the General Accounting Office (GAO).
