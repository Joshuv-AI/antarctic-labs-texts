21 C.F.R. § 56.112 — Review by institution.
Citation: us:cfr:21:56.112
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-56.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 56 (Institutional Review Boards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Research covered by these regulations that has been approved by an IRB may be subject to further appropriate review and approval or disapproval by officials of the institution. However, those officials may not approve the research if it has not been approved by an IRB.
