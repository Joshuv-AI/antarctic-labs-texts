7 C.F.R. § 1775.63 — Objectives.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1775.63
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1775.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1775 (Technical Assistance Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The objectives of the program are to:
(a) Reduce or eliminate pollution of water resources, and
(b) Improve planning and management of solid waste sites.
