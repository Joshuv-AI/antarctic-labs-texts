26 U.S.C. § 812 — Definition of company’s share and policyholder’s share
Citation: us:usc:26:812
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section812
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter L (Insurance Companies); Part I (Life Insurance Companies); Subpart D (Accounting, Allocation, And Foreign Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Company’s share
For purposes of section 805(a)(4), the term “company’s share” means, with respect to any taxable year beginning after December 31, 2017, 70 percent.
(b) Policyholder’s share
For purposes of section 807, the term “policyholder’s share” means, with respect to any taxable year beginning after December 31, 2017, 30 percent.
