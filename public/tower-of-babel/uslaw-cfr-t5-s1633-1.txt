5 C.F.R. § 1633.1 — Cross-reference to employee ethical conduct standards and financial disclosure regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1633.1
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1633.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1633 (Standards Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees of the Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board (Board) are subject to the executive branch-wide Standards of Ethical conduct at 5 CFR part 2635, the Board regulations at 5 CFR part 8601 which supplement the executive branch-wide standards, and the executive branch-wide financial disclosure regulations at 5 CFR part 2634.
