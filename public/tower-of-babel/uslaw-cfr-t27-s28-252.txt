27 C.F.R. § 28.252 — Air express or freight bills of lading.
Citation: us:cfr:27:28.252
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-28.252
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 28 (Exportation Of Alcohol). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where the exportation is made by air express or air freight, a bill of lading issued by the conveying airline is considered for the purpose of this part to be an export bill of lading if it otherwise conforms to the requirements of § 28.250.
