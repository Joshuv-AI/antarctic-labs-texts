2 U.S.C. § 6619 — Advance payments for computer programing services
Citation: us:usc:2:6619
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6619
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 65 (Senate Officers And Administration); Subchapter III (Sergeant At Arms And Doorkeeper Of Senate); Part B (General Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the Senate, subject to the approval of the Committee on Rules and Administration, is on and after July 6, 1981, authorized to enter into contracts which provide for the making of advance payments for computer programing services.
