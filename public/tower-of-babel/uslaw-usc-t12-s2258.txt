12 U.S.C. § 2258 — Jurisdiction
Citation: us:usc:12:2258
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2258
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 23 (Farm Credit System); Subchapter V (Farm Credit Administration Organization); Part B (Farm Credit Administration Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each institution of the System shall for the purposes of jurisdiction be deemed to be a citizen of the State, commonwealth, or District of Columbia in which its principal office is located.
