17 C.F.R. § 249.1701 — Form SBSEF, for application for registration as a security-based swap execution facility or to amend such application or registration.
Citation: us:cfr:17:249.1701
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-249.1701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 249 (Forms, Securities Exchange Act Of 1934). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form shall be used for application for registration as a security-based swap execution facility, pursuant to section 3D of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (15 U.S.C. 78c-4) and § 242.803 of this chapter, or to amend such application or registration.
