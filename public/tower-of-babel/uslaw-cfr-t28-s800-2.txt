28 C.F.R. § 800.2 — Mission.
Citation: us:cfr:28:800.2
Creator: Court Services And Offender Supervision Agency For The District Of Columbia
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-800.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 800 (Organization And Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CSOSA's mission is to increase public safety, prevent crime, reduce recidivism, and support the fair administration of justice in close collaboration with the community.
