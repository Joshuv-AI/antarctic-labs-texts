25 C.F.R. § 17.11 — Inspection of wills and approval as to form during testator's lifetime.
Citation: us:cfr:25:17.11
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-17.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 17 (Action On Wills Of Osage Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a will has been executed and filed with the superintendent during the lifetime of the testator, the will shall be considered by the special attorney who may endorse on such will “approved as to form.” A will shall be held in absolute confidence and its contents shall not be divulged prior to the death of the testator.
