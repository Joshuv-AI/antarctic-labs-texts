21 U.S.C. § 2014 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:21:2014
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section2014
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 25 (Miscellaneous Anti-Drug Abuse Provisions); Subchapter II (National Methamphetamine Information Clearinghouse). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated—
(1)
for fiscal year 2007—
(A)
$500,000 to establish the NMIC and Council; and
(B)
such sums as are necessary for the operation of the NMIC and Council; and
(2)
for each of fiscal years 2008 and 2009, such sums as are necessary for the operation of the NMIC and Council.
