26 U.S.C. § 7486 — Refund, credit, or abatement of amounts dis­allowed
Citation: us:usc:26:7486
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section7486
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 76 (Judicial Proceedings); Subchapter D (Court Review Of Tax Court Decisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In cases where assessment or collection has not been stayed by the filing of a bond, then if the amount of the deficiency determined by the Tax Court is disallowed in whole or in part by the court of review, the amount so disallowed shall be credited or refunded to the taxpayer, without the making of claim therefor, or, if collection has not been made, shall be abated.
