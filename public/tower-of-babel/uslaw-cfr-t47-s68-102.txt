47 C.F.R. § 68.102 — Terminal equipment approval requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:47:68.102
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-68.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 68 (Connection Of Terminal Equipment To The Telephone Network). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Terminal equipment must be approved in accordance with the rules and regulations in subpart C of this part, or connected through protective circuitry that is approved in accordance with the rules and regulations in subpart C.
