38 U.S.C. § 1726 — Reimbursement for loss of personal effects by natural disaster
Citation: us:usc:38:1726
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1726
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 17 (Hospital, Nursing Home, Domiciliary, And Medical Care); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous Provisions Relating To Hospital And Nursing Home Care And Medical Treatment Of Veterans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall, under regulations which the Secretary shall prescribe, reimburse veterans in Department hospitals and domiciliaries for any loss of personal effects sustained by fire, earthquake, or other natural disaster while such effects were stored in designated locations in Department hospitals or domiciliaries.
