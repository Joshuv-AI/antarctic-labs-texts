46 C.F.R. § 142.300 — Excepted vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:46:142.300
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-142.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 142 (Fire Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Excepted vessels, as defined in § 136.110 of this subchapter, need not comply with the provisions of §§ 142.315 through 142.330.
