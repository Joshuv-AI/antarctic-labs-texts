46 C.F.R. § 113.50-10 — -10 Additional requirements for passenger vessels.
Citation: us:cfr:46:113.50-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-113.50-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 113 (Communication And Alarm Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each passenger vessel must have a public address system capable of broadcasting separately or collectively to the following stations:
(a) Survival craft stations, port.
(b) Survival craft stations, starboard.
(c) Survival craft embarkation stations, port.
(d) Survival craft embarkation stations, starboard.
(e) Public spaces used for passenger assembly points.
(f) Crew quarters.
(g) Accommodation spaces and service spaces.
