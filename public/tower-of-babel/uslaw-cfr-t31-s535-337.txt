31 C.F.R. § 535.337 — Funds.
Citation: us:cfr:31:535.337
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-535.337
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 535 (Iranian Assets Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this part, the term funds shall mean monies in trust, escrow and similar special funds held by non-banking institutions, currency and coins. It does not include accounts created under § 535.568.
