ℤ L \mathbb{Z}_{L} symmetry breaking in SU(N) Fermi-Hubbard dots at zero and finite temperature.
Loïc Herviou, Elodie Campan, Pierre Nataf

Abstract
We address the SU(N) Fermi-Hubbard model on a chain, with N N the number of degenerate orbitals, or colors, for each fermion. In the limit of both large number of colors N N and particles, and small number of sites L ≥ 2 L\geq 2 , the model is proved to undergo a ℤ L \mathbb{Z}_{L} symmetry breaking for attractive local interaction amplitude U U . Using a combination of Exact Diagonalization with full SU(N) symmetry, generalized L-levels Holstein-Primakoff transformation, Hartree-Fock method and large-N saddle point approximation of the partition function, we extend the results obtained in [PRA 111, L020201 (2025)] to L ≥ 3 L\geq 3 and finite temperature T > 0 T>0 . In particular, we show that at T = 0 T=0 for U < U c ∼ − 1 / N U<U_{c}\sim-1/N , the ground state is L-fold degenerate, while for positive temperatures, the critical temperature is both proportional to N N and U U , i.e. T c ∝ − U ​ N T_{c}\propto-UN , making this phase transition particularly suitable for large-N fermions.

The irreducible representations (irreps) of SU(N) are labelled by Young Diagrams (YDs) of shape

We consider finite-temperature transitions for
M=N
particles.
The partition function of the system is:

Z_{L,N}=\sum\limits_{\mathrm{irrep}\,\alpha}D^{N}_{\alpha}\text{Tr}e^{-\beta H_{\overline{\alpha}}},

(18)

where the summation runs over all the
N
-box irreps
\alpha

(resp.

\overline{\alpha}
(resp.
\alpha

\overline{\alpha}

For positive

On the other hand, for

V.1
Interplay between energies and multipliticities in the Boltzmann weights for
L=2

At finite-temperature, the exact mapping between the LMG model and the SU(N) 2-sites FHM (implying energy spectrum equivalence for a given N-box irrep
\overline{\alpha}

Nataf_2025

For the orbital irrep
\overline{\alpha}=(M_{1},M_{2})

of shapes
\overline{\alpha}
(or
\alpha

D_{\alpha}^{\mathrm{LMG}}=\binom{N}{M_{1}}\frac{M_{1}+1-M_{2}}{M_{1}+1}.

(19)

On the other hand, the multiplicity for the SU(
N
) model is the dimension of
\alpha
seen as an irrep of SU(
N
)

VI
Conclusions and Perspectives

To conclude, we have extended the results of
Nataf_2025

While the analytical methods (L-levels HP transformation, saddle point approximation) are rather standard, their use is, to the best of our knowledge, original to study the SU(N) FHM on a L-sites ring for a large number of colors

(equal to the number of particles) and a small number of sites
L

They lead to simple phase diagrams with measurables predictions: the order of parameters

takahashi2012
;
taie2020observation
;

;
