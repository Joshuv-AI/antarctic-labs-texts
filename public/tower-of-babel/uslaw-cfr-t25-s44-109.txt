25 C.F.R. § 44.109 — What happens if the grant recipient is overpaid?
Citation: us:cfr:25:44.109
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-44.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 44 (Grants Under The Tribally Controlled Schools Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If the Secretary has mistakenly overpaid the grant recipient, then the Secretary will notify the grant recipient of the overpayment. The grant recipient must return the overpayment within 30 days after the final determination that overpayment occurred.
(b) When the grant recipient returns the money to the Secretary, the Secretary will distribute the money equally to all schools in the system.
