34 C.F.R. § 12.3 — What other regulations apply to this program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:12.3
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-12.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 12 (Disposal And Utilization Of Surplus Federal Real Property For Educational Purposes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following regulations apply to this program:
(a) 34 CFR parts 100, 104, and 106.
(b) 41 CFR part 101-47.
(c) 34 CFR part 85.
