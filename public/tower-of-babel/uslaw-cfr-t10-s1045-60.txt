10 C.F.R. § 1045.60 — Does an unauthorized public release of RD, FRD, or TFNI result in its declassification?
Citation: us:cfr:10:1045.60
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1045.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1045 (Nuclear Classification And Declassification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The unauthorized disclosure of RD, FRD, or TFNI does not automatically result in its declassification. However, if a disclosure is sufficiently authoritative or credible, the Director, Office of Environment, Health, Safety and Security will examine the possibility of declassifying the information.
