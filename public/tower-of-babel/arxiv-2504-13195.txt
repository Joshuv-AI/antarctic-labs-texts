On prime-producing sieves and distribution of α ​ p − β \alpha p-\beta mod 1 1
Runbo Li

Abstract
. The author proves that there are infinitely many primes p p such that ‖ α ​ p − β ‖ < p − 28 87 \|\alpha p-\beta\|<p^{-\frac{28}{87}} , where α \alpha is an irrational number and β \beta is a real number. This sharpens a result of Jia (2000) and provides a new triple ( γ , θ , ν ) = ( 59 87 , 28 87 , 1 29 ) (\gamma,\theta,\nu)=(\frac{59}{87},\frac{28}{87},\frac{1}{29}) that can produce special primes in Ford and Maynard’s work on prime-producing sieves. Our minimum amount of Type-II information required ( ν = 1 29 \nu=\frac{1}{29} ) is less than any previous work on this topic using only traditional Type-I and Type-II information.
