SMALL BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION


Data Collection Available for Public Comments

ACTION: 60-Day notice and request for comments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Small Business Administration (SBA) intends to request 
approval from the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) for the 
collection of information described below. The Paperwork Reduction Act 
(PRA) requires federal agencies to publish a notice in the Federal 
Register concerning each proposed collection of information before 
submission to OMB, and to allow 60 days for public comment in response 
to the notice. This notice complies with that requirement.

DATES: Submit comments on or before December 28, 2026.

ADDRESSES: Send all comments to Savannah Wilburn, Office of Field 
Operations, [email protected], Small Business Administration.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Savannah Wilburn, Office of Field 
Operations, [email protected], (404) 331-7711 and Shauniece 
Carter, Interim Agency Clearance Officer, [email protected], 
(202) 935-6942.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: Capturing more targeted information for SBA 
events would significantly streamline event planning and execution 
while strengthening the SBA's core mission of aiding, counseling, and 
assisting and protecting the interests of small businesses. By 
collecting relevant details upfront at registration including, but not 
limited to, business size, industry, NAICS codes, and current 
capabilities and needs, SBA can transform reactive data collection into 
a proactive, efficient process. In addition to registration, SBA may 
use this generic clearance to collect information for activities to 
benefit small businesses at all SBA outreach events including, but not 
limited to, events conducted under the authority granted by 15 U.S.C. 
637(b)(1)(A) and section 132(a) of Division K in Public Law 108-447, as 
reauthorized by the annual Appropriations Act.

Solicitation of Public Comments

    SBA is requesting comments on (a) Whether the collection of 
information is necessary for the agency to properly perform its 
functions; (b) whether the burden estimates are accurate; (c) whether 
there are ways to minimize the burden, including through the use of 
automated techniques or other forms of information technology; and (d) 
whether there are ways to enhance the quality, utility, and clarity of 
the information.

Summary of Information Collection

    PRA Number: To be assigned.
    Title: SBA Events Generic Clearances.
    Description of Respondents: Small businesses, large corporations, 
community organizations and federal, state and local governments.
    Total Estimated Responses: 30,000.
    Total Estimated Hour Burden: 5,000.

Shauniece Carter,
