29 C.F.R. § 406.7 — Personal responsibility of signatories of reports.
Citation: us:cfr:29:406.7
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-406.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 406 (Reporting By Labor Relations Consultants And Other Persons, Certain Agreements With Employers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each individual required to file a report under this part shall be personally responsible for the filing of such report and for any statement contained therein which he knows to be false.
