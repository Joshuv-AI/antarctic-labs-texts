41 C.F.R. § 109-50.400 — -50.400 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-50.400
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-50.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-50 (50—Special Doe Disposal Authorities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart contains policy to be followed when it is proposed to sell or otherwise transfer DOE personal property located in a mixed facility to the contractor who is the operator of that facility.
