36 C.F.R. § 1211.130 — Effect of employment opportunities.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1211.130
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1211.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1211 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Sex In Education Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The obligation to comply with these Title IX regulations is not obviated or alleviated because employment opportunities in any occupation or profession are or may be more limited for members of one sex than for members of the other sex.
