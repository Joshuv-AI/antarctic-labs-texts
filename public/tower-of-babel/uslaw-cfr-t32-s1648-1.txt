32 C.F.R. § 1648.1 — Authority of local board.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1648.1
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1648.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1648 (Classification By Local Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A local board shall consider and determine all claims which it receives in accord with § 1633.2 or § 1648.6 of this chapter. No action shall be taken by the board in the absence of a quorum of its prescribed membership.
