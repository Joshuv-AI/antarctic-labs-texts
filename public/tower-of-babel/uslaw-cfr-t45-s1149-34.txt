45 C.F.R. § 1149.34 — How are motions for discovery handled?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1149.34
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1149.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1149 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Motions for discovery must be handled according to the following:
(a) A party seeking discovery may file a motion with the ALJ. Such a motion must be accompanied by a copy of the requested discovery, or in the case of depositions, a summary of the scope of the proposed deposition.
(b) Within 10 days of service, a party may file an opposition to the motion and/or a motion for protective order.
