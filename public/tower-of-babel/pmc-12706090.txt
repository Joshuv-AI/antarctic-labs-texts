Author Correction: Strong crystalline thermal insulation induced by extended antibonding states
Authors: Ruihuan Cheng, Chen Wang, Niuchang Ouyang, Christophe Candolfi, Emmanuel Guilmeau, Xingchen Shen, Yue Chen
Published: 2025

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-025-63300-w, published online 26 August 2025

In the version of the article initially published, Christophe Candolfi (Université de Lorraine, CNRS, IJL, F-54000 Nancy, France) and Emmanuel Guilmeau (CRISMAT, CNRS, Normandie Univ, ENSICAEN, UNICAEN, 14000 Caen, France) were missing from the author list, while the affiliation ‘CRISMAT, CNRS, Normandie Univ, ENSICAEN, UNICAEN, 14000 Caen, France’ for Xingchen Shen (CRISMAT, CNRS, Normandie Univ, ENSICAEN, UNICAEN, 14000 Caen, France and MOE Key Laboratory of Material Physics and Chemistry under Extraordinary Conditions, School of Physical Science and Technology, Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi’an 710072, China) was missing. The author names and affiliations are now amended in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
