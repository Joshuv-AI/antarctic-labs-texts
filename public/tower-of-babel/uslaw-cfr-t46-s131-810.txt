46 C.F.R. § 131.810 — General alarm bell.
Citation: us:cfr:46:131.810
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-131.810
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 131 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each general alarm bell must be identified by red letters at least 13 millimeters ( 1/2-inch) high that state the following: “GENERAL ALARM—WHEN BELL RINGS GO TO YOUR STATION.”
