31 C.F.R. § 203.14 — Scope of the subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:31:203.14

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 203 (Payment Of Federal Taxes And The Treasury Tax And Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to all TT&L depositaries that accept FTD coupons and governs the acceptance and processing of those coupons.
