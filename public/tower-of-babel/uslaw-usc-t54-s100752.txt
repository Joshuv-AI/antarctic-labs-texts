54 U.S.C. § 100752 — Destruction of animals and plant life
Citation: us:usc:54:100752
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section100752
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1007 (Resource Management); Subchapter IV (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may provide for the destruction of such animals and plant life as may be detrimental to the use of any System unit.
