42 C.F.R. § 447.201 — State plan requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:447.201
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-447.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 447 (Payments For Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A State plan must provide that the requirements in this subpart are met.
(b) The plan must describe the policy and the methods to be used in setting payment rates for each type of service included in the State's Medicaid program.
