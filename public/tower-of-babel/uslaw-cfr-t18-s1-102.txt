18 C.F.R. § 1.102 — Words denoting number, gender and so forth.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1.102
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1 (Rules Of General Applicability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In determining the meaning of any provision of this chapter, unless the context indicates otherwise:
(a) The singular includes the plural;
(b) The plural includes the singular;
(c) The present tense includes the future tense; and
(d) Words of one gender include the other gender.
