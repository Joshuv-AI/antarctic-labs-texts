20 C.F.R. § 686.760 — What services are provided to former enrollees?
Citation: us:cfr:20:686.760
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-686.760
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 686 (The Job Corps Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Up to 3 months of employment services, including career services offered through a one-stop center, may be provided to former enrollees.
(b) According to procedures issued by the Secretary, other career transition services as determined appropriate may be provided to former enrollees.
