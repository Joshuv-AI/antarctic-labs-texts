49 C.F.R. § 40.27 — May an employer require an employee to sign a consent or release in connection with the DOT drug and alcohol testing program?
Citation: us:cfr:49:40.27
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-40.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 40 (Procedures For Transportation Workplace Drug And Alcohol Testing Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, as an employer, you must not require an employee to sign a consent, release, waiver of liability, or indemnification agreement with respect to any part of the drug or alcohol testing process covered by this part (including, but not limited to, collections, laboratory testing, MRO and SAP services).
