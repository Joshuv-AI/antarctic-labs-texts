16 U.S.C. § 525 — Rights-of-way for wagon roads or railroads
Citation: us:usc:16:525
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section525
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 2 (National Forests); Subchapter I (Establishment And Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the form provided by existing law the Secretary of the Interior may file and approve surveys and plats of any right of way for a wagon road, railroad, or other highway over and across any national forest when in his judgment the public interests will not be injuriously affected thereby.
