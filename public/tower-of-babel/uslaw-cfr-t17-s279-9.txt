17 C.F.R. § 279.9 — Form PF, reporting by investment advisers to private funds.
Citation: us:cfr:17:279.9
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-279.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 279 (Forms Prescribed Under The Investment Advisers Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form shall be filed pursuant to Rule 204(b)-1 (§ 275.204(b)-1 of this chapter) by certain investment advisers registered or required to register under section 203 of the Act (15 U.S.C. 80b-3) that act as an investment adviser to one or more private funds.
