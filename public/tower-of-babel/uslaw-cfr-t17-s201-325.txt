17 C.F.R. § 201.325 — Evidence: Presentation under oath or affirmation.
Citation: us:cfr:17:201.325
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-201.325
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 201 (Rules Of Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A witness at a hearing for the purpose of taking evidence shall testify under oath or affirmation.
