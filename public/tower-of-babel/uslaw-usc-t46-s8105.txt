46 U.S.C. § 8105 — Fishing vessel exemption
Citation: us:usc:46:8105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section8105
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Manning Of Vessels); Chapter 81 (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, neither the International Convention on Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping for Seafarers, 1978, nor any amendment to such convention, shall apply to a fishing vessel, including a fishing vessel used as a fish tender vessel.
