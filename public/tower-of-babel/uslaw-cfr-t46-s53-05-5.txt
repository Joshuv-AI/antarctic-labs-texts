46 C.F.R. § 53.05-5 — -5 Discharge capacities and valve markings.
Citation: us:cfr:46:53.05-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-53.05-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 53 (Heating Boilers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The discharge capacities and valve markings must be as indicated in HG-402 of Section IV of the ASME BPVC (incorporated by reference; see § 53.01-1). The discharge capacities must be certified by the National Board of Boiler and Pressure Vessel Inspectors.
