19 U.S.C. § 4345 — Joint strategic plan for the enforcement of intellectual property rights
Citation: us:usc:19:4345
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4345
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 28 (Trade Facilitation And Trade Enforcement); Subchapter III (Import-Related Protection Of Intellectual Property Rights). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commissioner and the Director of U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement shall include in the joint strategic plan required by section 4314 of this title—
(1)
a description of the efforts of the Department of Homeland Security to enforce intellectual property rights;
(2)
a list of the 10 United States ports of entry at which U.S. Customs and Border Protection has seized the most merchandise, both by volume and by value, that infringes intellectual property rights during the most recent 2-year period for which data are available; and
(3)
a recommendation for the optimal allocation of personnel, resources, and technology to ensure that U.S. Customs and Border Protection and U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement are adequately enforcing intellectual property rights.
