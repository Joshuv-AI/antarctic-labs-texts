46 U.S.C. § 6303 — Rights of parties in interest
Citation: us:usc:46:6303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section6303
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part D (Marine Casualties); Chapter 63 (Investigating Marine Casualties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In an investigation conducted under this chapter, the following shall be allowed to be represented by counsel, to cross-examine witnesses, and to call witnesses:
(1)
an owner,
(2)
any holder of a license or certificate of registry,
(3)
any holder of a merchant mariner’s document,
(4)
any other person whose conduct is under investigation, and
(5)
any other party in interest.
