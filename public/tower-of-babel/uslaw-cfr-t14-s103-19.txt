14 C.F.R. § 103.19 — Operations in prohibited or restricted areas.
Citation: us:cfr:14:103.19
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-103.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 103 (Ultralight Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may operate an ultralight vehicle in prohibited or restricted areas unless that person has permission from the using or controlling agency, as appropriate.
