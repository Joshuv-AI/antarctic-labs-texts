5 U.S.C. § 1012 — Responsibilities of Library of Congress
Citation: us:usc:5:1012
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section1012
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 10 (Federal Advisory Committees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to section 552 of this title, the Administrator shall provide for the filing with the Library of Congress of at least 8 copies of each report made by each advisory committee and, where appropriate, background papers prepared by consultants. The Librarian of Congress shall establish a depository for the reports and papers and make them available for public inspection and use.
