15 C.F.R. § 30.51 — Statistical information required for import entries.
Citation: us:cfr:15:30.51
Creator: Bureau Of The Census, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-30.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 30 (Foreign Trade Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The information required for statistical purposes is, in most cases, also required by CBP regulations for other purposes. Refer to the CBP website at cbp.gov to download “Instructions for Preparation of CBP-7501” for completing the entry summary documentation (CBP Form-7501). Refer to the Customs and Trade Automated Interface Requirements for instructions on submitting an Automated Commercial Environment (ACE) Automated Broker Interface (ABI) electronic record or instructions for completing CBP-226 for declaring any equipment, repair parts, materials purchased, or expense for repairs incurred outside of the United States.
