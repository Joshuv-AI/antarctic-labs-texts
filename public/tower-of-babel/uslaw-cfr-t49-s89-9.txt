49 C.F.R. § 89.9 — Redelegation.
Citation: us:cfr:49:89.9
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-89.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 89 (Implementation Of The Federal Claims Collection Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each officer to whom authority is delegated under § 89.5 may redelegate and authorize successive redelegations of the authority within the organization under his or her jurisdiction.
