10 C.F.R. § 9.39 — Annual report to the Attorney General of the United States and Director of the Office of Government Information Services.
Citation: us:cfr:10:9.39
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-9.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 9 (Public Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) On or before February 1 of each year, the NRC will submit a report covering the preceding fiscal year to the Attorney General of the United States and to the Director of the Office of Government Information Services that will include the information required by 5 U.S.C. 552(e)(1).
(b) The NRC will make its annual FOIA reports available to the public at the NRC website, https://www.nrc.gov.
