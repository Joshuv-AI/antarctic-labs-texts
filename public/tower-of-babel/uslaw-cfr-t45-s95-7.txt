45 C.F.R. § 95.7 — Time limit for claiming payment for expenditures made after September 30, 1979.
Citation: us:cfr:45:95.7
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-95.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 95 (General Administration—Grant Programs (Public Assistance, Medical Assistance And State Children'S Health Insurance Programs)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under the programs listed in § 95.1, we will pay a State for a State agency expenditure made after September 30, 1979, only if the State files a claim with us for that expenditure within 2 years after the calendar quarter in which the State agency made the expenditure. Section 95.19 lists the exceptions to this rule.
