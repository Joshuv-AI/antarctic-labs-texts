18 U.S.C. § 1660 — Receipt of pirate property
Citation: us:usc:18:1660
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1660
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 81 (Piracy And Privateering). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, without lawful authority, receives or takes into custody any vessel, goods, or other property, feloniously taken by any robber or pirate against the laws of the United States, knowing the same to have been feloniously taken, shall be imprisoned not more than ten years.
