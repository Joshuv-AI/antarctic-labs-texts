43 C.F.R. § 3598.5 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3598.5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3598.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3590 (Solid Minerals (Other Than Coal) Exploration And Mining Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Orders or decisions issued under the regulations in this part may be appealed as provided in part 4 of this title. Orders issued under § 3598.4(c) of this title shall be effective during the pendency of any appeal.
