9 C.F.R. § 557.12 — Foreign canned or packaged fish and fish products bearing trade labels; sampling and inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:9:557.12
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-557.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 557 (Importation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Foreign canned or packaged fish and fish products bearing on their immediate containers trade labels that have or have not been approved in accordance with the regulations in § 541.7 of this subchapter are to be sampled and inspected in the same manner as provided by § 327.12 of this chapter for foreign canned meat food products.
