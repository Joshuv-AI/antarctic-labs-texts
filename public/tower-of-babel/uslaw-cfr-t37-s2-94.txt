37 C.F.R. § 2.94 — Action after expungement or reexamination.
Citation: us:cfr:37:2.94
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-2.94
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 2 (Rules Of Practice In Trademark Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon termination of an expungement or reexamination proceeding, the Office shall issue a notice of termination that memorializes the final disposition of the proceeding as to each of the goods and/or services at issue in the proceeding. Where appropriate, the registration will be cancelled, in whole or in part.
