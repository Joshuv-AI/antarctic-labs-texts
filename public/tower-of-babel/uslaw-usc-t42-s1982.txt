42 U.S.C. § 1982 — Property rights of citizens
Citation: us:usc:42:1982
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1982
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 21 (Civil Rights); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All citizens of the United States shall have the same right, in every State and Territory, as is enjoyed by white citizens thereof to inherit, purchase, lease, sell, hold, and convey real and personal property.
