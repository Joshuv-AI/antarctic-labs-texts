29 U.S.C. § 713 — Transfer of funds
Citation: us:usc:29:713
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section713
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 16 (Vocational Rehabilitation And Other Rehabilitation Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Except as provided in subsection (b) of this section, no funds appropriated under this chapter for any program or activity may be used for any purpose other than that for which the funds were specifically authorized.
(b)
No more than 1 percent of funds appropriated for discretionary grants, contracts, or cooperative agreements authorized by this chapter may be used for the purpose of providing non-Federal panels of experts to review applications for such grants, contracts, or cooperative agreements.
