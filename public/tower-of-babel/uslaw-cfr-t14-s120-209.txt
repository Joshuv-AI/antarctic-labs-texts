14 C.F.R. § 120.209 — Requirement for notice.
Citation: us:cfr:14:120.209
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-120.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 120 (Drug And Alcohol Testing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before performing an alcohol test under this subpart, each employer shall notify a covered employee that the alcohol test is required by this subpart. No employer shall falsely represent that a test is administered under this subpart.
