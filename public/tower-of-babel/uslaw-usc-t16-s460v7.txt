16 U.S.C. § 460v–7 — Availability of land and water conservation fund moneys
Citation: us:usc:16:460v–7
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460v–7
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXXX (Flaming Gorge National Recreation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds hereafter appropriated and available for the acquisition of lands and waters and interests therein in the national forest system pursuant to section 200306 of title 54 shall be available for the acquisition of any lands, waters, and interests therein within the boundaries of the recreation area.
