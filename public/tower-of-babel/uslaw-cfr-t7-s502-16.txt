7 C.F.R. § 502.16 — Penalties and other law.
Citation: us:cfr:7:502.16
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-502.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 502 (Conduct On Beltsville Agriculture Research Center Property, Beltsville, Maryland). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whoever shall be found guilty of violating the rules and regulations in this part is subject to fine of not more than $50 or imprisonment of not more than 30 days, or both (see 40 U.S.C. 318c). Nothing contained in the rules and regulations in this part shall be construed as abrogating or authorizing the abrogation of any other regulations or any Federal law or any laws and regulations of the State of Maryland.
