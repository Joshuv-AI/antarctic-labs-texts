22 U.S.C. § 8004 — United States compliance with its nuclear nonproliferation treaty obligations
Citation: us:usc:22:8004
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8004
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 87 (United States And India Nuclear Cooperation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter constitutes authority for any action in violation of an obligation of the United States under the NPT.
