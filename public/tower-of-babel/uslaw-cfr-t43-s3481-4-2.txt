43 C.F.R. § 3481.4-2 — -2 What are some examples of circumstances that qualify for a temporary interruption of coal severance?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3481.4-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3481.4-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3480 (Coal Exploration And Mining Operations Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Movement, failure, or repair of major equipment, such as draglines or longwalls; overburden removal; adverse weather; employee absences;
(b) Inability to sever coal due to orders issued by governmental authorities for cessation or relocation of the coal severance operations; and
(c) Inability to sell or distribute coal severed from the lease or LMU out of or away from the lease or LMU.
