54 U.S.C. § 101938 — Sunset

Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1019 (Concessions And Commercial Use Authorizations); Subchapter III (Commercial Services Authorization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authority given to the Secretary under this subchapter shall expire 9 years after the date of the enactment of this subchapter.
