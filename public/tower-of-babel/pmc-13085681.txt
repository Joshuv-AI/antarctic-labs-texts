Following publication of the original article [1], an error was identified in the Ethics approval and consent to participate section.

The updated Ethics approval and consent to participate is given below and the changes have been highlighted in bold typeface.

Ethics approval and consent to participate

Informed consent was obtained from each participant. For participants under 18 years old, electronic consent from their parents or guardians was obtained. Ethical approval was obtained from Research Ethical Committee, Nafeer Alafia Hospital, Northern State Ministry of Health, Sudan. The study was conducted according to the principles and guidelines of The Declaration of Helsinki. Informed consent was obtained from each participant.

The original article [1] has been corrected.
