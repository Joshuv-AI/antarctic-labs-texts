24 C.F.R. § 1005.411 — Disclosure and verification of Social Security and Employer Identification Numbers or Tax Identification Number.
Citation: us:cfr:24:1005.411
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1005.411
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1005 (Loan Guarantees For Indian Housing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All Borrowers must meet applicable requirements for the disclosure and verification of Social Security, Employer Identification Numbers, or Tax Identification Numbers.
