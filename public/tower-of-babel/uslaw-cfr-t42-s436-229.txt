42 C.F.R. § 436.229 — Optional targeted low-income children.
Citation: us:cfr:42:436.229
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-436.229
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 436 (Eligibility In Guam, Puerto Rico, And The Virgin Islands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency may provide Medicaid to—
(a) All individuals under age 19 who are optional targeted low-income children as defined in § 436.3; or
(b) Reasonable categories of these individuals.
