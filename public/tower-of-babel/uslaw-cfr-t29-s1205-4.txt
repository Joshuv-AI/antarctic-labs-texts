29 C.F.R. § 1205.4 — Substantive rules.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1205.4
Creator: National Mediation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1205.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1205 (Notices In Re: Railway Labor Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The only substantive rules issued by the National Mediation Board are those authorized under section 2, Ninth, of the Railway Labor Act to implement the procedure of determining employee representation.
