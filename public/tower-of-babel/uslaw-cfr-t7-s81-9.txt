7 C.F.R. § 81.9 — Inspection and certification of diversion.
Citation: us:cfr:7:81.9
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-81.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 81 (Prune/Dried Plum Diversion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the removal of the prune-plum trees is complete, the producer(s) will notify the Committee on a form provided by the Committee. The Committee will certify that the trees approved for removal from the block or orchard, as the case may be, have been removed, and notify AMS.
