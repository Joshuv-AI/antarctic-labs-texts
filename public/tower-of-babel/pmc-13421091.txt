Correction: Application of XGBoost and Random Forest algorithms for body weight prediction in Blackbelly sheep using biometric measurements
Authors: Miguel Ángel Gastelum Delgado, Cem Tırınk, Rosa Inés Parra-Cortés, Ignacio Vázquez Martínez, Armando Gomez-Vazquez, Aldenamar Cruz-Hernandez, Enrique Camacho-Pérez, Dany Alejandro Dzib-Cauich, Hasan Önder, Uğur Şen, Kadyrbai Chekirov, Yüksel Aksoy, Hilal Tozlu Çelik, Olatunbosun Odu, Alfonso J Chay-Canul
Published: 2026

Correction: BMC Vet Res 22, 218 (2026)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12917-026-05349-3

Following publication of the original article [1], after carefully reviewing the final published version, the authors unfortunately noticed that several corrections clearly indicated in the proof file, which was revised together with the corresponding author, were not reflected in the published article.

In addition, the order of the figures appears to have been mixed during production, and some figure captions do not correspond to the figures currently presented in the article. Since figures are essential for the accurate interpretation of the results, this issue may cause confusion for readers.

The original article has been corrected.
