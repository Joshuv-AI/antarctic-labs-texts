33 U.S.C. § 3605 — Application with other laws
Citation: us:usc:33:3605
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3605
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 49 (Integrated Coastal And Ocean Observation System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter supersedes or limits the authority of any agency to carry out its responsibilities and missions under other laws.
