32 C.F.R. § 776.50 — Communication with person represented by counsel.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 776 (Professional Conduct Of Attorneys Practicing Under The Cognizance And Supervision Of The Judge Advocate General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In representing a client, a covered attorney shall not communicate about the subject of the representation with a party the covered attorney knows to be represented by another attorney in the matter, unless the covered attorney has the consent of the other attorney or is authorized by law to do so.
