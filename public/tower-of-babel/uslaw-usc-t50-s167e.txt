50 U.S.C. § 167e — Intragovernmental cooperation
Citation: us:usc:50:167e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section167e
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 10 (Helium Gas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense and the Chairman of the Atomic Energy Commission may each designate representatives to cooperate with the Secretary in carrying out the purposes of this chapter, and shall have complete right of access to plants, data, and accounts.
