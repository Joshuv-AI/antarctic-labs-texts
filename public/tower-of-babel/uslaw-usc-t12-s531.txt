12 U.S.C. § 531 — Exemption from taxation
Citation: us:usc:12:531
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section531
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 4 (Taxation); Subchapter I (Federal Reserve Banks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Federal reserve banks, including the capital stock and surplus therein and the income derived therefrom shall be exempt from Federal, State, and local taxation, except taxes upon real estate.
