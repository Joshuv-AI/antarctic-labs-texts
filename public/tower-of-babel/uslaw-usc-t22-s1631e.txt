22 U.S.C. § 1631e — Rules by district courts; appeals
Citation: us:usc:22:1631e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1631e
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 21 (Settlement Of International Claims); Subchapter II (Vesting And Liquidation Of Bulgarian, Hungarian, And Rumanian Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The district courts of the United States are given jurisdiction to make and enter all such rules as to notice and otherwise, and all such orders and decrees, and to issue such process as may be necessary and proper in the premises to enforce the provisions of this subchapter, with a right of appeal from the final order or decree of such court as provided in chapter 83 of title 28.
