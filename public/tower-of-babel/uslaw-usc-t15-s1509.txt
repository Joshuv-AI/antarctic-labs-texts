15 U.S.C. § 1509 — Designation of officer to sign routine papers
Citation: us:usc:15:1509
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1509
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 40 (Department Of Commerce). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may designate an officer of the Department to sign minor routine official papers and documents during the temporary absence of the Secretary, the Under Secretary, and the Assistant Secretaries of the Department.
