42 C.F.R. § 61.14 — Separate consideration of information concerning moral character or loyalty.
Citation: us:cfr:42:61.14
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-61.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 61 (Fellowships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No information in the records or possession of the Public Health Service concerning the moral character or loyalty of a fellow will be made available to any fellowship committee involved in recommending appointments of fellows.
