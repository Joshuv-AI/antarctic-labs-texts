18 U.S.C. § 3300 — Recruitment or use of child soldiers
Citation: us:usc:18:3300
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section3300
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part II (Criminal Procedure); Chapter 213 (Limitations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No person may be prosecuted, tried, or punished for a violation of section 2442 unless the indictment or the information is filed not later than 10 years after the commission of the offense.
