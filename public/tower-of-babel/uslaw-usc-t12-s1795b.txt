12 U.S.C. § 1795b — National Credit Union Administration Central Liquidity Facility; establishment; management; jurisdiction
Citation: us:usc:12:1795b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1795b
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 14 (Federal Credit Unions); Subchapter III (Central Liquidity Facility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is created the National Credit Union Administration Central Liquidity Facility. The Central Liquidity Facility, an instrumentality of the United States, shall exist within the National Credit Union Administration and be managed by the Board. The United States district court shall have original jurisdiction over any case to which the Board on behalf of the Facility is a party, without regard to the amount in controversy.
