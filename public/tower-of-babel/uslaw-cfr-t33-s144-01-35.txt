33 C.F.R. § 144.01-35 — -35 Litter.
Citation: us:cfr:33:144.01-35
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-144.01-35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 144 (Lifesaving Appliances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On each manned platform a Stokes litter, or other suitable safety litter capable of being safely hoisted with an injured person, shall be provided and kept in an accessible place.
