43 C.F.R. § 1865.0-2 — -2 Objective.
Citation: us:cfr:43:1865.0-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1865.0-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1860 (Conveyances, Disclaimers And Correction Documents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The objective of a correction document is to eliminate from the chain of title errors in patents or other documents of conveyance that have been issued by the United States under laws administered by the Bureau of Land Management or its predecessors and that pertain to the disposal of the public lands or of an interest therein.
