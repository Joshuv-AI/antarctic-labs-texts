In the original version of this article, an unintended error occurred in Figure 3A. A photomicrograph of NTA-Spy1 cells at 96 h had been stored with the 48 h images and was subsequently used in the 48 h panel during revision of the figure. The file itself retained the correct 96 h label, which allowed the error to be identified upon review of the original data. We, the authors, confirm that the quantitative analyses were performed using the correct time points and are unaffected by this error.

Figure 3 has now been corrected online, with the appropriate 48 h photomicrograph in panel A, and is also provided below. This correction does not affect the results, interpretation, or conclusions of the study. We sincerely apologize for this oversight.
