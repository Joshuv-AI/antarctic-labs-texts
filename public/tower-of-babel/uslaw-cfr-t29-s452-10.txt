29 C.F.R. § 452.10 — Retaliation for exercising rights.
Citation: us:cfr:29:452.10
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-452.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 452 (General Statement Concerning The Election Provisions Of The Labor-Management Reporting And Disclosure Act Of 1959). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 609, which prohibits labor organizations or their officials from disciplining members for exercising their rights under the Act, and section 610, which makes it a crime for any person to use or threaten force or violence for the purpose of interfering with or preventing the exercise of any rights protected under the Act, apply to rights relating to the election of officers under title IV.
