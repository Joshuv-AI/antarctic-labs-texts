43 C.F.R. § 8340.0-2 — -2 Objectives.
Citation: us:cfr:43:8340.0-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-8340.0-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 8340 (Off-Road Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The objectives of these regulations are to protect the resources of the public lands, to promote the safety of all users of those lands, and to minimize conflicts among the various uses of those lands.
