43 C.F.R. § 4610.4-3 — -3 Allocation of funds appropriated.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4610.4-3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4610.4-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4600 (Leases Of Grazing Land—Pierce Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Moneys received in the administration of lands leased under the Pierce Act, when appropriated by the Congress, will be allocated to the budgets of the State Director for disbursement in accordance with that Act and the regulations in this part. Records of disbursements thereof will be maintained under existing procedure.
