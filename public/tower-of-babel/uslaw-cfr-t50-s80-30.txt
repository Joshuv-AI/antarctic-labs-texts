50 C.F.R. § 80.30 — Why must a State fish and wildlife agency certify the number of paid license holders?
Citation: us:cfr:50:80.30
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-80.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 80 (Administrative Requirements, Pittman-Robertson Wildlife Restoration And Dingell-Johnson Sport Fish Restoration Acts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State fish and wildlife agency must certify the number of individuals having paid licenses to hunt and paid licenses to fish because the Service uses these data in statutory formulas to apportion funds in the Wildlife Restoration and Sport Fish Restoration programs among the States.
