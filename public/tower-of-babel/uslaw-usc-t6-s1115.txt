6 U.S.C. § 1115 — TSA personnel limitations
Citation: us:usc:6:1115
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section1115
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 4 (Transportation Security); Subchapter II (Transportation Security Enhancements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any statutory limitation on the number of employees in the Transportation Security Administration does not apply to employees carrying out this chapter.
