42 U.S.C. § 12132 — Discrimination
Citation: us:usc:42:12132
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12132
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 126 (Equal Opportunity For Individuals With Disabilities); Subchapter II (Public Services); Part A (Prohibition Against Discrimination And Other Generally Applicable Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to the provisions of this subchapter, no qualified individual with a disability shall, by reason of such disability, be excluded from participation in or be denied the benefits of the services, programs, or activities of a public entity, or be subjected to discrimination by any such entity.
