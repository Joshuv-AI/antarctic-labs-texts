42 C.F.R. § 86.37 — Terms and conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:86.37
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-86.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 86 (Grants For Education Programs In Occupational Safety And Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All direct traineeship awards shall be subject to the following terms and conditions:
(a) Training must be carried out at an institution found by the Secretary to provide a well-rounded course of instruction in the particular area of training for which the traineeship is awarded.
(b) No direct traineeship may be utilized to compensate any trainee for personal services or employment on behalf of the United States or any person.
