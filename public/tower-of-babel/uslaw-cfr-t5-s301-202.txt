5 C.F.R. § 301.202 — Appointment of citizens recruited outside overseas areas.
Citation: us:cfr:5:301.202
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-301.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 301 (Overseas Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an agency determines that unusual or emergency conditions make it infeasible to appoint from a register, it may give an overseas limited appointment to a United States citizen recruited in an area where an overseas limited appointment is not authorized.
