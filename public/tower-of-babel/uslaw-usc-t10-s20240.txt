10 U.S.C. § 20240 — Acceptance of promotions; oath of office
Citation: us:usc:10:20240
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section20240
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Space Force); Chapter 2005 (Officers); Subchapter III (Promotions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Acceptance.—
An officer who is appointed to a higher grade under section 20239 of this title is considered to have accepted the appointment on the date on which the appointment is made unless the officer expressly declines the appointment.
(b) Oath.—
An officer who has served continuously since taking the oath of office prescribed in section 3331 of title 5 is not required to take a new oath upon appointment to a higher grade under section 20239 of this title.
