15 C.F.R. § 734.12 — Effect on foreign laws and regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:15:734.12
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-734.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 734 (Scope Of The Export Administration Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who complies with any of the license or other requirements of the EAR is not relieved of the responsibility of complying with applicable foreign laws and regulations. Conversely, any person who complies with the license or other requirements of a foreign law or regulation is not relieved of the responsibility of complying with U.S. laws and regulations, including the EAR.
