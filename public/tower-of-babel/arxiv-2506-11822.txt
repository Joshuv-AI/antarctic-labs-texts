The Central Emission Vertex of two gravitons
Damiano Barcaro, Vittorio Del Duca

Abstract
It has recently been shown that there exists an s s -channel sequence of classical corrections Rothstein:2024nlq to the H H diagram computed long ago by Amati, Ciafaloni and Veneziano Amati:1990xe . At leading logarithmic accuracy, those corrections feature the gravity BFKL kernel as a crucial element, and may be computed through either rapidity renormalisation group equations or amplitudes built through s s -channel unitarity cuts. In this paper, we evaluate six-graviton amplitudes in next-to-multi-Regge kinematics, and compute for the first time the Central Emission Vertex for the emission of two gravitons, which is relevant to evaluate the corrections to the gravity BFKL kernel, and thus to go beyond the leading logarithmic accuracy.

5
Conclusions

In this paper, we have verified the Regge factorisation of four-, five- and six-point amplitudes which feature the exchange of a graviton in the
t
channel, and we have re-derived the graviton CEV in the spinor-helicity formalism.

The main item of the paper, though, is the computation for the first time of the two-graviton CEV, given in eqs. (
38
) and (
125
), through the evaluation of six-graviton amplitudes in next-to-multi-Regge kinematics. As expected, the CEV for two gravitons of positive helicities (
38
), which comes from MHV amplitudes, has a simple form; while the CEV for two gravitons of opposite helicities (
125
), which comes from NMHV amplitudes, is very involved. The two-graviton CEV will be useful in evaluating the next-to-leading-order corrections to the gravity BFKL kernel, which we leave to future work.
