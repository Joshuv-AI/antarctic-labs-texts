17 C.F.R. § 270.12d2-1 — d2-1 Definition of insurance company for purposes of sections 12(d)(2) and 12(g) of the Act.
Citation: us:cfr:17:270.12d2-1
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-270.12d2-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 270 (Rules And Regulations, Investment Company Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of sections 12(d)(2) and 12(g) of the Act [15 U.S.C. 80a-12(d)(2) and 80a-12(g)], insurance company shall include a foreign insurance company as that term is used in rule 3a-6 under the Act (17 CFR 270.3a-6).
