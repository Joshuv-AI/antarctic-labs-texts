46 C.F.R. § 30.10-19a — -19a Control space—TB/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:30.10-19a
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-30.10-19a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 30 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term control space means an enclosed space in which is located a ship's radio, main navigating equipment, or emergency source of power or in which is located centralized fire recording or fire control equipment, but not including firefighting apparatus that must be located in the cargo area or individual pieces of firefighting equipment.
