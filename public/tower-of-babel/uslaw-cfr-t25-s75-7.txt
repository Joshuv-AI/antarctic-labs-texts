25 C.F.R. § 75.7 — Applications for enrollment.
Citation: us:cfr:25:75.7
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-75.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 75 (Revision Of The Membership Roll Of The Eastern Band Of Cherokee Indians, North Carolina). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each adult person who believes he meets the requirements for enrollment established herein may submit to the Tribal Enrollment Office an application for enrollment as a member of the Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians.
