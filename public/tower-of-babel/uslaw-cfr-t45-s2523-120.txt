45 C.F.R. § 2523.120 — Must Federal agencies consult with State Commissions?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2523.120
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2523.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2523 (Agreements With Other Federal Agencies For The Provision Of Americorps Program Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Federal agencies must provide a description of the manner in which the proposed AmeriCorps program(s) is coordinated with the application of the State in which the projects will be conducted. Agencies must also describe proposed efforts to coordinate AmeriCorps activities with State Commissions and other funded AmeriCorps programs within the State in order to build upon existing programs and not duplicate efforts.
