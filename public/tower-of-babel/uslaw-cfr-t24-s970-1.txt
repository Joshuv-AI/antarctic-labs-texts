24 C.F.R. § 970.1 — Purpose.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 970 (Public Housing Program—Demolition Or Disposition Of Public Housing Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part states requirements for HUD approval of a public housing agency's application for demolition or disposition (in whole or in part) of public housing developments assisted under Title I of the U.S. Housing Act of 1937 (Act). The regulations in 2 CFR part 200 are not applicable to this part.
