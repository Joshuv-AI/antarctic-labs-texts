40 U.S.C. § 125 — Library memberships
Citation: us:usc:40:125
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section125
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 1 (General); Subchapter III (Administrative And General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amounts appropriated may be used, when authorized by the Administrator of General Services, for payment in advance for library memberships in societies whose publications are available to members only, or to members at a lower price than that charged to the general public.
