19 U.S.C. § 1643 — Application of customs reorganization act
Citation: us:usc:19:1643
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1643
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part VI (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The rights, privileges, powers, and duties vested in or imposed upon the Secretary of the Treasury by this chapter shall be subject to the provisions of subdivision (a) of section 2073 of this title.
