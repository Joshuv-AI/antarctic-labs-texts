40 C.F.R. § 610.61 — Engine dynamometer tests.
Citation: us:cfr:40:610.61
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-610.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 610 (Fuel Economy Retrofit Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator will choose a test procedure or procedures from various engine dynamometer durability test procedures used by research organizations in government, the oil industry, engine manufacturing companies, and independent laboratories.
