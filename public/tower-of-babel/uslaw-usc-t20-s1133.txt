20 U.S.C. § 1133 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:20:1133
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1133
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter VII (Graduate And Postsecondary Improvement Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this subchapter—
(1)
to authorize national graduate fellowship programs—
(A)
in order to attract students of superior ability and achievement, exceptional promise, and demonstrated financial need, into high-quality graduate programs and provide the students with the financial support necessary to complete advanced degrees; and
(B)
that are designed to—
(i)
sustain and enhance the capacity for graduate education in areas of national need, including those areas critical to United States national and homeland security needs, such as science, technology, engineering, and mathematics; and
(ii)
encourage talented students to pursue scholarly careers in the humanities, social sciences, and the arts; and
(2)
to promote postsecondary programs.
