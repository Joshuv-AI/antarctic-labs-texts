28 C.F.R. § 549.40 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:28:549.40
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-549.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 549 (Medical Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This subpart describes procedures for voluntary and involuntary psychiatric evaluation, hospitalization, care, and treatment, in a suitable facility, for persons in Bureau of Prisons (Bureau) custody. These procedures are authorized by 18 U.S.C. Chapter 313 and 18 U.S.C. 4042.
(b) This subpart applies to inmates in Bureau custody, as defined in 28 CFR part 500.
