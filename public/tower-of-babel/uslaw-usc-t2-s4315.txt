2 U.S.C. § 4315 — Overtime pay for FBI employees detailed to House Committee on Appropriations
Citation: us:usc:2:4315
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4315
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 43 (Congressional Committees); Subchapter II (House Of Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Federal Bureau of Investigation, notwithstanding any other provision of law, may in any fiscal year pay all administrative uncontrollable overtime accrued by its employees while on detail to the Committee on Appropriations.
