32 C.F.R. § 245.3 — Responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:32:245.3
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-245.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 245 (Plan For The Emergency Security Control Of Air Traffic (Escat)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Secretary of Defense for Networks and Information Integration will ensure the responsibilities of the DoD are implemented. The DOT and the DHS shall implement the procedures and actions requested by the Department of Defense.
