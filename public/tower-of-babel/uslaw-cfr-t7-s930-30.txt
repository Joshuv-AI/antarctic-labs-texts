7 C.F.R. § 930.30 — Powers.
Citation: us:cfr:7:930.30
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-930.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 930 (Tart Cherries Grown In The States Of Michigan, New York, Pennsylvania, Oregon, Utah, Washington, And Wisconsin). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board shall have the following powers:
(a) To administer this part in accordance with its terms and provisions;
(b) To make rules and regulations to effectuate the terms and provisions of this part;
(c) To receive, investigate, and report to the Secretary complaints of violations of this part; and
(d) To recommend to the Secretary amendments to this part.
