40 C.F.R. § 256.63 — Requirements for public participation in the permitting of facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:40:256.63
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-256.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 256 (Guidelines For Development And Implementation Of State Solid Waste Management Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Before approving a permit application (or renewal of a permit) for a resource recovery or solid waste disposal facility the State shall hold a public hearing to solicit public reaction and recommendations on the proposed permit application if the State determines there is a significant degree of public interest in the proposed permit.
(b) This hearing shall be held in accord with 40 CFR 25.5.
