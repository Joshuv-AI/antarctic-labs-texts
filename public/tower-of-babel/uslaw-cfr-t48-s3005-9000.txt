48 C.F.R. § 3005.9000 — 3005.9000 Applicability (USCG).
Citation: us:cfr:48:3005.9000
Creator: Chapter 30—Department Of Homeland Security, Homeland Security Acquisition Regulation (Hsar)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3005.9000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3005 (Publicizing Contract Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracts awarded by the U.S. Coast Guard using the procedures in (HSAR) 48 CFR 3037.104-91 are expressly authorized for the Coast Guard under 10 U.S.C. 1091, and are exempt from (FAR) 48 CFR part 5.
