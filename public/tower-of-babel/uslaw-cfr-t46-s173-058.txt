46 C.F.R. § 173.058 — Double bottom requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:46:173.058
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-173.058
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 173 (Special Rules Pertaining To Vessel Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each new sailing school vessel which has a mean length greater than 165 feet (50.3 meters) and is certificated for exposed water service must comply with the double bottom requirements in §§ 171.105 through 171.109, inclusive, of this subchapter.
