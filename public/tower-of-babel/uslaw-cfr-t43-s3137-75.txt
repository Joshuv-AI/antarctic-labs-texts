43 C.F.R. § 3137.75 — May I perform additional development outside established participating areas to fulfill continuing development obligations?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3137.75
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3137.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3130 (Oil And Gas Leasing: National Petroleum Reserve, Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may perform additional development either within or outside a participating area, depending on the terms of the unit agreement.
