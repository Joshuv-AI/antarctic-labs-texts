9 C.F.R. § 93.515 — Appearance of disease among swine in quarantine.
Citation: us:cfr:9:93.515
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-93.515
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 93 (Importation Of Certain Animals, Birds, Fish, And Poultry, And Certain Animal, Bird, And Poultry Products; Requirements For Means Of Conveyance And Shipping Containers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any contagious disease appears among swine during the quarantine period special precautions shall be taken to prevent spread of the infection to other animals in the quarantine station or to those outside the grounds. The affected swine shall be disposed of as the Administrator may direct, depending upon the nature of the disease.
