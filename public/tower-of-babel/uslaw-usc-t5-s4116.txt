5 U.S.C. § 4116 — Training program assistance
Citation: us:usc:5:4116
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section4116
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart C (Employee Performance); Chapter 41 (Training). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Office of Personnel Management, on request of an agency, shall advise and assist in the establishment, operation, and maintenance of the training programs and plans of the agency under this chapter, to the extent of its facilities and personnel available for that purpose.
