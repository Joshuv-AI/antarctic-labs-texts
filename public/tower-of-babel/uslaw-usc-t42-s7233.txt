42 U.S.C. § 7233 — Experts and consultants
Citation: us:usc:42:7233
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7233
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter VI (Administrative Provisions); Part B (Personnel Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may obtain services as authorized by section 3109 of title 5, at rates not to exceed the daily rate prescribed for grade GS–18 of the General Schedule under section 5332 of title 5 for persons in Government service employed intermittently.
