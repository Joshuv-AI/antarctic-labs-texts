29 C.F.R. § 530.8 — Preservation of individual homeworker certificates.
Citation: us:cfr:29:530.8
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-530.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 530 (Employment Of Homeworkers In Certain Industries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A copy of all certificates provided to the employer under this part shall be maintained for a period of at least three years after the last employment under the certificate.
