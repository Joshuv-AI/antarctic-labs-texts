Following publication of the original article [1], it was noted that, due to proofing oversight, the final version has several errors. Listed below are the errors. The original article has been corrected.

Page 2: The first subsection of the Results section is incorrectly written as “Proteogenomic analysis of UCC”. This should have been written as “Proteogenomic analysis of LACC”.

Page 2: The first subsection of the Results section is incorrectly written as “Proteogenomic analysis of UCC”. This should have been written as “Proteogenomic analysis of LACC”.

Page 3: Incomplete sentences remain in the published version, which may confuse readers and hinder a full understanding of the manuscript. The sentence “Furthermore, we identified genes/proteins whose expression or phosphorylation levels correlated with alterations of (Fig. 1E)” should be replaced with “Furthermore, we identified genes/proteins whose expression or phosphorylation levels correlated with alterations of PAG-LACC involved in keratinization (KRT5/10/13/14/16) and TP53 regulation (DNA2/UBC/TPX2) (Fig. 1E).”
