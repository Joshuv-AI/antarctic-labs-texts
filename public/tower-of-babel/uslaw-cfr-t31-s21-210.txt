31 C.F.R. § 21.210 — Reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:31:21.210
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-21.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 21 (New Restrictions On Lobbying). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No reporting is required with respect to payments of reasonable compensation made to regularly employed officers or employees of a person.
