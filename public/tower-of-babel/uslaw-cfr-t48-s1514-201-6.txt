48 C.F.R. § 1514.201-6 — 1514.201-6 Solicitation provisions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1514 (Sealed Bidding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Contracting Officer shall insert the solicitation provision at 1552.214-71, Contract Award-Other Factors-Sealed Bidding, in invitations for bids when it is appropriate to describe other factors that will be used in evaluating bids for award.
