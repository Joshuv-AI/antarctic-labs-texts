41 C.F.R. § 101-27.209 — -27.209 Utilization and distribution of shelf-life items.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-27.209
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-27.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-27 (27—Inventory Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where it is determined that specified quantities of both Type I and Type II shelf-life items will not be used within the shelf-life period, such quantities shall be utilized or distributed in accordance with this section.
