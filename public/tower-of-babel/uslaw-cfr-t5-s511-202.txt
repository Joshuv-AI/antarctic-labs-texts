5 C.F.R. § 511.202 — Authority of agency.
Citation: us:cfr:5:511.202
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-511.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 511 (Classification Under The General Schedule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to the provisions of subpart F of this part and § 511.203, an agency may determine whether a position is subject to, or is excluded from, chapter 51 of title 5, United States Code, by section 5102(c) (7) and (8) thereof.
