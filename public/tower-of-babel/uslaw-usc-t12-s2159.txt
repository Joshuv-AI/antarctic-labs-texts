12 U.S.C. § 2159 — Purchase and sale of obligations
Citation: us:usc:12:2159
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2159
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 23 (Farm Credit System); Subchapter IV (Provisions Applicable To Two Or More Classes Of Institutions Of The System); Part A (Funding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each bank of the System may purchase its own obligations and the obligations of other banks of the System and may provide for the sale of obligations issued by it, consolidated obligations, or Systemwide obligations through a fiscal agent or agents, by negotiation, offer, bid, syndicate sale, and to deliver such obligations by book entry, wire transfer, or such other means as may be appropriate.
