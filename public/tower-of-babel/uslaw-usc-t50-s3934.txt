50 U.S.C. § 3934 — Stay or vacation of execution of judgments, attachments, and garnishments
Citation: us:usc:50:3934
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3934
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 50 (Servicemembers Civil Relief); Subchapter II (General Relief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Court action upon material affect determination
If a servicemember, in the opinion of the court, is materially affected by reason of military service in complying with a court judgment or order, the court may on its own motion and shall on application by the servicemember—
(1)
stay the execution of any judgment or order entered against the servicemember; and
(2)
vacate or stay an attachment or garnishment of property, money, or debts in the possession of the servicemember or a third party, whether before or after judgment.
(b) Applicability
This section applies to an action or proceeding commenced in a court against a servicemember before or during the period of the servicemember’s military service or within 90 days after such service terminates.
