16 U.S.C. § 460bbb–11 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:460bbb–11
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460bbb–11
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter CXIII (Smith River National Recreation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such funds as may be necessary to carry out this subchapter and the amendments made by this subchapter.
