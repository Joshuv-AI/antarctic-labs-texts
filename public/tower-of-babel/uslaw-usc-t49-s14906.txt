49 U.S.C. § 14906 — Evasion of regulation of carriers and brokers
Citation: us:usc:49:14906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section14906
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Motor Carriers, Water Carriers, Brokers, And Freight Forwarders); Chapter 149 (Civil And Criminal Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person, or an officer, employee, or agent of that person, that by any means tries to evade regulation provided under this part for carriers or brokers is liable to the United States for a civil penalty of at least $2,000 for the first violation and at least $5,000 for a subsequent violation, and may be subject to criminal penalties.
