43 C.F.R. § 3252.13 — How long may I collect information from my temperature gradient well?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3252.13
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3252.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may collect information from your temperature gradient well for as long as your permit allows.
