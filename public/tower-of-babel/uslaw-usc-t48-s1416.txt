48 U.S.C. § 1416 — Regulation of trade
Citation: us:usc:48:1416
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1416
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 8 (Guano Islands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The introduction of guano from such islands, rocks, or keys shall be regulated as in the coasting trade between different parts of the United States, and the same laws shall govern the vessels concerned therein.
