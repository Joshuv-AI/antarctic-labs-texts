7 C.F.R. § 51.1578 — Off-Grade potatoes.

Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-51.1578

Potatoes which fail to meet the requirements of any of the foregoing grades shall be Off-Grade potatoes.
