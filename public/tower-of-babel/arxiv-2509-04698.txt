Super homology groups of differential forms and vector fields on Euclidean line
Kentaro Mikami, Tadayoshi Mizutani

Abstract
The arXiv:2105.09738 claims several stuffs. In particular, we recall the following two. (1) Vector fields and differential forms become a Lie superalgebra structure for each manifold. (2) For an n-dimensional Euclidean space, vector fields and differential forms with polynomial coefficients become a double weighted Lie superalgebra. By using Euler vector field, the Betti numbers are 0 except the last one if the primary weight and the secondary weight are different. Now, a simple question arises: What happens when the primary weight and the secondary weight are equal? This note shall give a complete answer to the question for the case n = 1 n=1 . MSC(Math Subject Classification)2020 are 17B10, 17B15.
