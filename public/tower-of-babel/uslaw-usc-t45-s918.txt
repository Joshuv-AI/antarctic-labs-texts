45 U.S.C. § 918 — Authority of Railroad Retirement Board
Citation: us:usc:45:918
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section918
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 18 (Milwaukee Railroad Restructuring). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Board may prescribe such regulations as may be necessary to carry out its duties under this chapter.
(b)
In carrying out its duties under this chapter, the Board may exercise such of the powers, duties, and remedies provided in subsections (a), (b), and (d) of section 362 of this title as are not inconsistent with the provisions of this chapter.
