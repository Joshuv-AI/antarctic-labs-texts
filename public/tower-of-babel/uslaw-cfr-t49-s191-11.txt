49 C.F.R. § 191.11 — Distribution system: Annual report.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 191 (Transportation Of Natural And Other Gas By Pipeline; Annual, Incident, And Other Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) General. Except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section, each operator of a distribution pipeline system must submit an annual report for that system on DOT Form PHMSA F 7100.1-1. This report must be submitted each year, not later than March 15, for the preceding calendar year.
(b) Not required. The annual report requirement in this section does not apply to a master meter system, a petroleum gas system that serves fewer than 100 customers from a single source, or an individual service line directly connected to a production pipeline or a gathering line other than a regulated gathering line as determined in § 192.8.
