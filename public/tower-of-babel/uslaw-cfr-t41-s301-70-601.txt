41 C.F.R. § 301-70.601 — -70.601 Reevaluation of transportation and subsistence expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-70.601
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-70.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-70 (70—Internal Policy And Procedure Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must reevaluate the payment of transportation and subsistence expenses to threatened law enforcement/investigative employees every 30 days based on the same factors the agency considered when the agency first authorized the payment of the expenses.
