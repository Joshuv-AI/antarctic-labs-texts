7 C.F.R. § 412.6 — Timing of responses to requests.
Citation: us:cfr:7:412.6
Creator: Federal Crop Insurance Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-412.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 412 (Public Information—Freedom Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general, FCIC will respond to requests according to their order of receipt.
(b) Existing responsive documents or information may be maintained in RMA's field offices. Therefore, extra time may be necessary to search and collect the documents.
