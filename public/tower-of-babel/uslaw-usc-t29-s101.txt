29 U.S.C. § 101 — Issuance of restraining orders and injunctions; limitation; public policy
Citation: us:usc:29:101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section101
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 6 (Jurisdiction Of Courts In Matters Affecting Employer And Employee). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No court of the United States, as defined in this chapter, shall have jurisdiction to issue any restraining order or temporary or permanent injunction in a case involving or growing out of a labor dispute, except in a strict conformity with the provisions of this chapter; nor shall any such restraining order or temporary or permanent injunction be issued contrary to the public policy declared in this chapter.
