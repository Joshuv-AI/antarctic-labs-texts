10 U.S.C. § 2829 — Multi-year contracts for supplies and services
Citation: us:usc:10:2829
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2829
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 169 (Military Construction And Military Family Housing); Subchapter II (Military Family Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary concerned may make contracts for periods of up to four years for supplies and services for the management, maintenance, and operation of military family housing and may pay the costs of such contracts for each year out of annual appropriations for that year.
