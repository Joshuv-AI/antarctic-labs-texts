17 C.F.R. § 239.63 — Form ID, application for EDGAR access.
Citation: us:cfr:17:239.63
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-239.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 239 (Forms Prescribed Under The Securities Act Of 1933). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Form ID must be filed by electronic filers, or by their account administrators, to request EDGAR access and to authorize account administrators to manage the electronic filer's EDGAR account.
