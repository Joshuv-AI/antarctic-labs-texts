46 C.F.R. § 58.10-10 — -10 Diesel engine installations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:58.10-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-58.10-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 58 (Main And Auxiliary Machinery And Related Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The requirements of § 58.10-5 (a), (c), and (d) apply to diesel engine installations.
(b) A diesel engine air intake on a mobile offshore drilling unit must not be in a classified location.
(c) A diesel engine exhaust on a mobile offshore drilling unit must not discharge into a classified location.
