47 C.F.R. § 80.854 — Radiotelephone installation.
Citation: us:cfr:47:80.854
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-80.854
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 80 (Stations In The Maritime Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The radiotelephone installation includes:
(a) A radiotelephone transmitter;
(b) A receiver as specified in § 80.858(a);
(c) A main source of energy;
(d) A reserve source of energy, when required by § 80.860(a);
(e) An antenna system.
