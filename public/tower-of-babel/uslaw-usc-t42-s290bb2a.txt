42 U.S.C. § 290bb–2a — Medical treatment of narcotics addiction; report to Congress
Citation: us:usc:42:290bb–2a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section290bb–2a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter III–A (Substance Abuse And Mental Health Services Administration); Part B (Centers And Programs); Subpart 1 (Center For Substance Abuse Treatment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Health and Human Services, after consultation with the Attorney General and with national organizations representative of persons with knowledge and experience in the treatment of narcotic addicts, shall determine the appropriate methods of professional practice in the medical treatment of the narcotic addiction of various classes of narcotic addicts, and shall report thereon from time to time to the Congress.
