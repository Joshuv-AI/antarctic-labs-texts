31 C.F.R. § 515.582 — Importation of certain goods and services produced by independent private sector entrepreneurs.
Citation: us:cfr:31:515.582
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-515.582
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 515 (Cuban Assets Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons subject to U.S. jurisdiction are authorized to engage in all transactions, including payments, necessary to import certain goods and services produced by independent private sector entrepreneurs, as defined in § 515.340. The list of goods and services eligible for importation under this section is located at https://www.state.gov/the-state-departments-section-515-582-list/.
