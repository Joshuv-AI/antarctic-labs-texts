26 U.S.C. § 2203 — Definition of executor
Citation: us:usc:26:2203
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section2203
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 11 (Estate Tax); Subchapter C (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The term “executor” wherever it is used in this title in connection with the estate tax imposed by this chapter means the executor or administrator of the decedent, or, if there is no executor or administrator appointed, qualified, and acting within the United States, then any person in actual or constructive possession of any property of the decedent.
