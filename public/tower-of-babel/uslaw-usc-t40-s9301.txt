40 U.S.C. § 9301 — Maintenance and administration
Citation: us:usc:40:9301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section9301
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part D (Public Buildings, Grounds, And Parks In The District Of Columbia); Chapter 93 (Theodore Roosevelt Island). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the National Park Service shall maintain and administer Theodore Roosevelt Island as a natural park for the recreation and enjoyment of the public.
