19 U.S.C. § 3738 — Assistance from United States private sector to prevent and reduce HIV/AIDS in sub-Saharan Africa
Citation: us:usc:19:3738
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section3738
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 23 (Extension Of Certain Trade Benefits To Sub-Saharan Africa); Subchapter III (Economic Development Related Issues). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of the Congress that United States businesses should be encouraged to provide assistance to sub-Saharan African countries to prevent and reduce the incidence of HIV/AIDS in sub-Saharan Africa. In providing such assistance, United States businesses should be encouraged to consider the establishment of an HIV/AIDS Response Fund in order to provide for coordination among such businesses in the collection and distribution of the assistance to sub-Saharan African countries.
