24 C.F.R. § 115.303 — Eligible activities for capacity building funds.
Citation: us:cfr:24:115.303
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-115.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 115 (Certification And Funding Of State And Local Fair Housing Enforcement Agencies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The primary purposes of capacity-building funding are to provide for complaint activities and to support activities that produce increased awareness of fair housing rights and remedies. All such activities must support the agency's administration and enforcement of its fair housing law and address matters affecting fair housing that are cognizable under the Fair Housing Act.
