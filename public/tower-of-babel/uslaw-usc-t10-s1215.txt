10 U.S.C. § 1215 — Members other than Regulars: applicability of laws
Citation: us:usc:10:1215
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1215
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 61 (Retirement Or Separation For Physical Disability). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The laws and regulations that entitle any retired member of a regular component of the armed forces to pay, rights, benefits, or privileges extend the same pay, rights, benefits, or privileges to any other member of the armed forces who is not a member of a regular component and who is retired, or to whom retired pay is granted, because of physical disability.
