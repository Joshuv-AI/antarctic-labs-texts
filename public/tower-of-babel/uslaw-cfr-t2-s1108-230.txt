2 C.F.R. § 1108.230 — Indirect costs (also known as “Facilities and Administrative,” or F&A, costs).
Citation: us:cfr:2:1108.230
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1108.230
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1108 (Definitions Of Terms Used In Subchapters A Through F Of This Chapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Indirect costs means those costs incurred for a common or joint purpose benefitting more than one cost objective, and not readily assignable to the cost objectives specifically benefitted, without effort disproportionate to the results achieved.
