7 C.F.R. § 1726.205 — Multiparty lump sum quotations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1726.205
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1726.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1726 (Electric System Construction Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The borrower or its engineer must contact a sufficient number of suppliers or contractors to assure competition and so that at least three bids will be received. On the basis of written lump sum quotations, the borrower will select the supplier or contractor based on the lowest evaluated cost.
