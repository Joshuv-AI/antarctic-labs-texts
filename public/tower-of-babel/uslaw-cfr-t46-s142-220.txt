46 C.F.R. § 142.220 — Fire hazards to be minimized.
Citation: us:cfr:46:142.220
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-142.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 142 (Fire Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each towing vessel must be maintained and operated so as to minimize fire hazards and to ensure the following:
(a) All bilges and void spaces are kept free from accumulation of combustible and flammable materials and liquids insofar as practicable.
(b) Storage areas are kept free from accumulation of combustible and flammable materials insofar as practicable.
