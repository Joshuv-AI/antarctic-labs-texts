12 U.S.C. § 635n — Prohibition of loans, guarantees, and insurance as to sales of defense articles or services
Citation: us:usc:12:635n
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section635n
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 6A (Export-Import Bank Of The United States); Subchapter II (Export Financing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Bank shall not extend loans, guarantees, or insurance under this subchapter in connection with the sale of defense articles or defense services.
