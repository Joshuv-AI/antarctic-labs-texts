On the real part of elastic scattering amplitude
S.M. Troshin, N.E. Tyurin

Abstract
We discuss dominance of imaginary part of the elastic scattering amplitude and argue in favor of approximation based on this dominance.

Conclusion

Finally, we would like to note that the scattering picture that follows from dominance of the imaginary part of elastic amplitude over its real part seems to be in a full compliance with results of the current experimental and theoretical studies. The impact parameter analysis of the LHC data implies transition from a black disc to a black ring picture of hadron interactions. This conclusion remains to be valid and leaves almost no room for the other interpretations.
