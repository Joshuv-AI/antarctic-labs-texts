33 C.F.R. § 127.1511 — International shore connection.
Citation: us:cfr:33:127.1511
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-127.1511
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 127 (Waterfront Facilities Handling Liquefied Natural Gas And Liquefied Hazardous Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each marine transfer area for LHG that receives foreign flag vessels must have an international shore connection meeting the requirements of ASTM F1121-87 (Reapproved 2019) (incorporated by reference, see § 127.003).
