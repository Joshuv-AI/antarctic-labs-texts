12 C.F.R. § 1271.39 — Review of books and records.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1271.39
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1271.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1271 (Miscellaneous Federal Home Loan Bank Operations And Authorities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FHFA shall examine the Financing Corporation at least annually to determine whether the Financing Corporation is performing its functions in accordance with the requirements of section 21 of the Bank Act (12 U.S.C. 1441) and this subpart.
