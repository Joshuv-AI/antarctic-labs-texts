6 U.S.C. § 1001 — Designation of liaison office of Department of State
Citation: us:usc:6:1001
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section1001
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 3 (Security And Accountability For Every Port); Subchapter III (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State shall designate a liaison office within the Department of State to assist the Secretary, as appropriate, in negotiating cargo security-related international agreements.
