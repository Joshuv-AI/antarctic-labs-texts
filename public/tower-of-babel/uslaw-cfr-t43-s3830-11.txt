43 C.F.R. § 3830.11 — Which minerals are locatable under the General Mining Law?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3830.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3830.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3830 (Administration Of Mining Claims And Sites; General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Minerals are locatable if they are:
(a) Subject to the General Mining Law;
(b) Not leasable under the Mineral Leasing Acts; and
(c) Not salable under the Mineral Materials Act of 1947 and Surface Resources Act of 1955, 30 U.S.C. 601-615 (see parts 3600 through 3620 of this chapter).
