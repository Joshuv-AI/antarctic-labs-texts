37 C.F.R. § 1.445 — International application filing, processing and search fees.
Citation: us:cfr:37:1.445
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-1.445
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 1 (Rules Of Practice In Patent Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The following fees and charges for international applications are established by law or by the director under the authority of 35 U.S.C. 376:
(1) A transmittal fee (see 35 U.S.C. 361(d) and PCT Rule 14) consisting of:
(i) A basic portion:
(A) For an international application having a receipt date that is on or after January 19, 2025:
(B) For an international application having a receipt date that is on or after December 29, 2022, and before January 19, 2025:
(C) For an international application having a receipt date that is on or after October 2, 2020, and before December 29, 2022:
(D) For an international application having a receipt date that is on or after January 1, 2014, and before October 2, 2020:
(E) For an international application having a receipt date that is before January 1, 2014: $240.00.
(ii) A non-electronic filing fee portion for any international application designating the United States of America that is filed on or after November 15, 2011, other than by the USPTO patent electronic filing system, except for a plant application:
(2) A search fee (see 35 U.S.C. 361(d) and PCT Rule 16):
(i) For an international application having a receipt date that is on or after January 19, 2025:
(ii) For an international application having a receipt date that is on or after April 1, 2023, and before January 19, 2025:
(iii) For an international application having a receipt date that is on or after October 2, 2020, and before April 1, 2023:
(iv) For an international application having a receipt date that is on or after January 1, 2014, and before October 2, 2020:
(v) For an international application having a receipt date that is before January 1, 2014: $2,080.00.
(3) A supplemental search fee when required, per additional invention:
(i) For an international application having a receipt date that is on or after January 19, 2025:
(ii) For an international application having a receipt date that is on or after April 1, 2023, and before January 19, 2025:
(iii) For an international application having a receipt date that is on or after October 2, 2020, and before April 1, 2023:
(iv) For an international application having a receipt date that is on or after January 1, 2014, and before October 2, 2020:
(v) For an international application having a receipt date that is before January 1, 2014: $2,080.00.
(4) A fee equivalent to the transmittal fee in paragraph (a)(1) of this section that would apply if the USPTO was the Receiving Office for transmittal of an international application to the International Bureau for processing in its capacity as a Receiving Office (PCT Rule 19.4).
(5) Late furnishing fee for providing a sequence listing in response to an invitation under PCT Rule 13ter:
(6) Late payment fee pursuant to PCT Rule 16bis.2.
(b) The international filing fee shall be as prescribed in PCT Rule 15.
