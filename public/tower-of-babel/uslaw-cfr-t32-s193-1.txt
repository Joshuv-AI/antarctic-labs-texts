32 C.F.R. § 193.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:32:193.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-193.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 193 (Highways For National Defense). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth policy, responsibilities, and authority in matters pertaining to Department of Defense highway needs and, when appropriate, to the highway needs of other Federal agencies, during peacetime and emergencies in the United States and its territories and possessions.
