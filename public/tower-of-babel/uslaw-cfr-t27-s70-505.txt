27 C.F.R. § 70.505 — Requirements on persons intending to file claim.
Citation: us:cfr:27:70.505
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-70.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 70 (Procedure And Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who, having paid the tax with respect to an article, desires to claim refund or credit of any amount of such tax to which the provisions of this subpart are applicable must:
(a) File a claim, as provided in § 70.506, and
(b) Comply with any other provisions of law or regulations which may apply to the claim.
