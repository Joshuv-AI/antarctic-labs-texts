44 C.F.R. § 7.947 — Remedial action by recipient.
Citation: us:cfr:44:7.947
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-7.947
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 7 (Nondiscrimination In Federally-Assisted Programs (Fema Reg. 5)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where FEMA finds a recipient has discriminated on the basis of age, the recipient shall take any remedial action that FEMA may require to overcome the effects of the discrimination. If another recipient exercises control over the recipient that had discriminated, FEMA may require both recipients to take remedial action.
