43 C.F.R. § 4.762 — Does any distribution of the estate occur while a petition for reconsideration is pending?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.762
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.762
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Project Director must not initiate distribution of any portion of the estate while the petition for reconsideration is pending. If the petition is filed by a party in interest and not the Project Director, the presiding officer will issue a notice of receipt of the petition to the Project Director as soon as practicable.
