41 C.F.R. § 109-39.101-1 — -39.101-1 Agency cooperation.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-39 (39—Interagency Fleet Management Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Management and Program Secretarial Officer (PSO) for their respective organizations shall designate representatives to coordinate with GSA concerning the establishment of a GSA fleet management system to serve their organization.
