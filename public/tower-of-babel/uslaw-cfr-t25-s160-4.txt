25 C.F.R. § 160.4 — Prompt payment of irrigation charges by lessees.
Citation: us:cfr:25:160.4
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-160.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 160 (Inclusion Of Liens In All Patents And Instruments Executed). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Superintendents will also see that irrigation charges are promptly paid by lessees, and where such charges are not so paid take appropriate and prompt action for their collection. Such unpaid charges are a lien against the land, and accordingly any failure on the part of the superintendents to collect same increases the obligation against the land.
