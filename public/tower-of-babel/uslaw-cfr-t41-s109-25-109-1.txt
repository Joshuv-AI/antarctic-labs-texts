41 C.F.R. § 109-25.109-1 — -25.109-1 Identification of idle equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-25.109-1
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-25.109-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-25 (25—General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At a minimum, management walk-throughs shall be conducted to provide for coverage of all operating and storage areas at least once every two years to identify idle and unneeded personal property.
