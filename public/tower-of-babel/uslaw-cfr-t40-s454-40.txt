40 C.F.R. § 454.40 — Applicability; description of manufacture of tall oil rosin, pitch and fatty acids subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:454.40
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-454.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 454 (Gum And Wood Chemicals Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the manufacture of tall oil rosin, pitch and fatty acids.
