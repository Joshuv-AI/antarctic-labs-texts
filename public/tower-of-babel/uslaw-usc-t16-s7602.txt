16 U.S.C. § 760–2 — Establishment of fish hatchery in Montana
Citation: us:usc:16:760–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section760–2
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 9A (Preservation Of Fishery Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized to establish, construct, equip, operate, and maintain a new fish hatchery in the vicinity of Miles City, Montana.
