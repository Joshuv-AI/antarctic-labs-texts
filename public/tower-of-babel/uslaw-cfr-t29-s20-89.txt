29 C.F.R. § 20.89 — Prior provision of rights with respect to debt.
Citation: us:cfr:29:20.89
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-20.89
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 20 (Federal Claims Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent that the rights of the debtor in relation to the same debt have been previously provided by the creditor agency under some other statutory or regulatory authority, the creditor agency is not required to duplicate those efforts before taking salary offset.
