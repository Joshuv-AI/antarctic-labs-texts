27 C.F.R. § 11.38 — Discontinued products.
Citation: us:cfr:27:11.38
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-11.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 11 (Consignment Sales). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a producer or importer discontinues the production or importation of a product, a trade buyer's inventory of that product may be returned for cash or credit against outstanding indebtedness.
