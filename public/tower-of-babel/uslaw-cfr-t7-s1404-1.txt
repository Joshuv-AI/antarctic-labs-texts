7 C.F.R. § 1404.1 — General statement.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1404.1
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1404.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1404 (Assignment Of Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the manner in which a person may assign a cash payment which is made by the Farm Service Agency (FSA) or the Commodity Credit Corporation (CCC). Such payments may only be assigned in the manner set forth in this part.
