36 U.S.C. § 90110 — Immunity of United States Government
Citation: us:usc:36:90110
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section90110
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 901 (Help America Vote Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States Government is not liable for any debts, defaults, acts, or omissions of the foundation. The full faith and credit of the Government does not extend to any obligation of the foundation.
