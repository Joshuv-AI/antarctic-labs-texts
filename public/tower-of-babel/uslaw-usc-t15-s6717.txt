15 U.S.C. § 6717 — Definition of State
Citation: us:usc:15:6717
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6717
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 93 (Insurance); Subchapter I (State Regulation Of Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this subchapter, the term “State” means any State of the United States, the District of Columbia, any territory of the United States, Puerto Rico, Guam, American Samoa, the Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands, the Virgin Islands, and the Northern Mariana Islands.
