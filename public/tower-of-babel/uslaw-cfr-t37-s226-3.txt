37 C.F.R. § 226.3 — Effect of counterclaims on a smaller claims proceeding.
Citation: us:cfr:37:226.3
Creator: U.S. Copyright Office, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-226.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 226 (Smaller Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where a claimant has chosen to proceed via a smaller claims proceeding, a respondent only may assert a counterclaim that seeks total monetary relief of $5,000 or less (exclusive of attorneys' fees or costs). Any permissible counterclaims asserted by a respondent shall be adjudicated under the procedures set forth in this part.
