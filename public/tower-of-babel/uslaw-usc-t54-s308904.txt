54 U.S.C. § 308904 — Guidelines and regulations
Citation: us:usc:54:308904
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section308904
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3089 (Save America’S Treasures Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall develop any guidelines and prescribe any regulations that the Secretary determines to be necessary to carry out this chapter.
