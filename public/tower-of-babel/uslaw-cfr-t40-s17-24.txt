40 C.F.R. § 17.24 — Settlement.
Citation: us:cfr:40:17.24
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-17.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 17 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Epa Administrative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A prevailing party and EPA counsel may agree on a proposed settlement of an award before final action on the application, either in connection with a settlement of the underlying proceeding or after the underlying proceeding has been concluded. If the party and EPA counsel agree on a proposed settlement of an award before an application has been filed, the application shall be filed with the proposed settlement.
