25 C.F.R. § 304.9 — Placards; display of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:25:304.9
Creator: Indian Arts And Crafts Board, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-304.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 304 (Navajo, Pueblo, And Hopi Silver, Use Of Government Mark). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every dealer offering for sale silver bearing the Government mark may display in a prominent place a placard setting forth the standards and the regulations in this part, such placard to be furnished by the Indian Arts and Crafts Board.
