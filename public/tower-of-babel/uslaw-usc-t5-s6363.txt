5 U.S.C. § 6363 — Establishment of leave banks
Citation: us:usc:5:6363
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section6363
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart E (Attendance And Leave); Chapter 63 (Leave); Subchapter IV (Voluntary Leave Bank Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each agency that establishes a leave bank program under section 6362 shall establish 1 or more leave banks in accordance with regulations prescribed by the Office of Personnel Management.
