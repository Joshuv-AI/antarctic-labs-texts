6 U.S.C. § 424 — Increased micro-purchase threshold for certain procurements
Citation: us:usc:6:424
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section424
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter VIII (Coordination With Non-Federal Entities; Inspector General; United States Secret Service; Coast Guard; General Provisions); Part F (Federal Emergency Procurement Flexibility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the administration of section 1902 of title 41 with respect to a procurement referred to in section 422 of this title, the amount specified in subsections (a), (d), and (e) of such section 1902 shall be deemed to be $7,500.
