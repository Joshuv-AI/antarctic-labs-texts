43 C.F.R. § 45.58 — When will the hearing record close?
Citation: us:cfr:43:45.58
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-45.58
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 45 (Conditions And Prescriptions In Ferc Hydropower Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The hearing record will close when the ALJ closes the hearing, unless he or she directs otherwise.
(b) Evidence may not be added after the hearing record is closed, but the transcript may be corrected under § 45.56(b).
