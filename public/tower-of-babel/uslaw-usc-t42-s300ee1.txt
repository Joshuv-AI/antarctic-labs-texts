42 U.S.C. § 300ee–1 — Establishment of office with respect to minority health and acquired immune deficiency syndrome
Citation: us:usc:42:300ee–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300ee–1
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XXIII (Prevention Of Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Health and Human Services, acting through the Director of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, shall establish an office for the purpose of ensuring that, in carrying out the duties of the Secretary with respect to prevention of acquired immune deficiency syndrome, the Secretary develops and implements prevention programs targeted at minority populations and provides appropriate technical assistance in the implementation of such programs.
