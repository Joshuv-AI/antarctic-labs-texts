31 C.F.R. § 14.5 — Certification.
Citation: us:cfr:31:14.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-14.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 14 (Right To Financial Privacy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prior to obtaining the requested records pursuant to a formal written request, an official of a rank designated by the head of the requesting Departmental unit shall certify in writing to the financial institution that the Departmental unit has complied with the applicable provisions of the Act.
