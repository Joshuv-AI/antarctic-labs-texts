40 C.F.R. § 1045.601 — What compliance provisions apply to these engines?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1045.601
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1045.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1045 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Engine and vessel manufacturers, as well as owners, operators, and rebuilders of engines subject to the requirements of this part, and all other persons, must observe the provisions of this part, the requirements and prohibitions in 40 CFR part 1068, and the provisions of the Clean Air Act.
