41 C.F.R. § 304-9.6 — -9.6 Reimbursement for non-authorized expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-9.6
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-9.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-9 (9—Contributions And Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies are not required to reduce employee reimbursement when a donor pays for expenses the government cannot reimburse, for example, travel expenses for an employee's immediate family.
