25 C.F.R. § 217.3 — Referral of questions by superintendent.
Citation: us:cfr:25:217.3
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-217.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 217 (Management Of Tribal Assets Of Ute Indian Tribe, Uintah And Ouray Reservation, Utah, By The Tribe And The Ute Distribution Corp.). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The superintendent shall refer all questions and problems related to the management of the assets as they come to his attention, together with his analysis of alternative solutions to each question or problem, to the business committee and to the board of directors for resolution. Such referrals shall be in writing and shall be addressed to the joint managers at such addresses as they furnish to the superintendent and to each other from time to time.
