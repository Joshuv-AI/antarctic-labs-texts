21 C.F.R. § 1.672 — What is the duration of direct accreditation?
Citation: us:cfr:21:1.672
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1.672
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1 (General Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FDA will grant direct accreditation of a third-party certification body for a period not to exceed 4 years.
