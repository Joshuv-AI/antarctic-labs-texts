42 U.S.C. § 4278 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:4278
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4278
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 53 (Advisory Commission On Intergovernmental Relations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
