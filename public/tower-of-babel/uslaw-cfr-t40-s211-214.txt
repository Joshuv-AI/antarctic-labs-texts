40 C.F.R. § 211.214 — Removal of label.
Citation: us:cfr:40:211.214
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-211.214
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 211 (Product Noise Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 10(a)(4) of the Act prohibits any person from removing, prior to sale, any label required by this subpart, by either physical removal or defacing or any other physical act making the label and its contents not accessible to the ultimate purchaser prior to sale.
