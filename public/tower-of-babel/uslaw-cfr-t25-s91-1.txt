25 C.F.R. § 91.1 — Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 91 (Government Of Indian Villages, Osage Reservation, Oklahoma). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the regulations in this part is to establish policies and procedures for the government of Indian villages, Osage Reservation, Oklahoma.
