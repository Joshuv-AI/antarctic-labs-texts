14 C.F.R. § 27.991 — Fuel pumps.
Citation: us:cfr:14:27.991
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-27.991
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 27 (Airworthiness Standards: Normal Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Compliance with § 27.955 may not be jeopardized by failure of—
(a) Any one pump except pumps that are approved and installed as parts of a type certificated engine; or
(b) Any component required for pump operation except, for engine driven pumps, the engine served by that pump.
