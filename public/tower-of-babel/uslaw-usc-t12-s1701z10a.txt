12 U.S.C. § 1701z–10a — Biennial survey of economic and housing market conditions
Citation: us:usc:12:1701z–10a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1701z–10a
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall, not less than biennially, survey national, regional, and local economic and housing market conditions in a manner that provides data comparable to the data collected in such survey conducted in 1981.
