41 C.F.R. § 102-33.40 — -33.40 Acquiring Government aircraft.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.40
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To acquire Government aircraft, you must follow the requirements in:
(a) 31 U.S.C. 1343;
(b) OMB Circular A-126 Revised;
(c) OMB Circular A-11, Part 2, Section 25.5;
(d) OMB Circular A-76; and
(e) OMB Circular A-94.
