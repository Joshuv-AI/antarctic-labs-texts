46 C.F.R. § 111.01-13 — -13 Limitations on porcelain use.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.01-13
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.01-13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Porcelain must not be used for lamp sockets, switches, receptacles, fuse blocks, or other electric equipment where the item is solidly mounted by machine screws or their equivalent, unless the porcelain piece is resiliently mounted.
