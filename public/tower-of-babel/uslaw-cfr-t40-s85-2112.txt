40 C.F.R. § 85.2112 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:85.2112
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-85.2112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 85 (Control Of Air Pollution From Mobile Sources). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of §§ 85.2112 through 85.2122 apply to emission related automotive aftermarket parts which are to be installed in or on 1968 and later model year light-duty vehicles and light-duty trucks.
