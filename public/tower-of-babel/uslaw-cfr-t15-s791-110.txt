15 C.F.R. § 791.110 — Classified national security information.
Citation: us:cfr:15:791.110
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-791.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 791 (Securing The Information And Communications Technology And Services Supply Chain). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In any review of a determination made under this part, if the determination was based on classified national security information, such information may be submitted to the reviewing court ex parte and in camera. This section does not confer or imply any right to review in any tribunal, judicial or otherwise.
