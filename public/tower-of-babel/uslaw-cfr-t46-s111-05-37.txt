46 C.F.R. § 111.05-37 — -37 Overcurrent devices.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.05-37
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.05-37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A permanently grounded conductor must not have an overcurrent device unless the overcurrent device simultaneously opens each ungrounded conductor of the circuit.
(b) The neutral conductor of the emergency-main switchboard bus-tie must not have a switch or circuit breaker.
