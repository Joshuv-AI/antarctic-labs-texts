20 C.F.R. § 255.3 — When overpayments are to be recovered.
Citation: us:cfr:20:255.3
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-255.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 255 (Recovery Of Overpayments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Overpayments shall be recovered in all cases except those in which recovery is waived under § 255.10 of this part or administrative relief from recovery is granted under § 255.16 of this part, or where the overpayment is compromised or recovery is terminated or suspended under § 255.18 or § 255.19 of this part.
