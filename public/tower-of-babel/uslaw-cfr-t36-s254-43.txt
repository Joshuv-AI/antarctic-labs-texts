36 C.F.R. § 254.43 — Surveys.
Citation: us:cfr:36:254.43
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-254.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 254 (Landownership Adjustments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All necessary tract surveys of National Forest System land shall be conducted by a licensed private surveyor under Forest Service instructions, contracted by the person applying for the conveyance, or by a Forest Service surveyor. The person will also be required to have all Federal property boundaries resulting from a conveyance marked and posted to Forest Service standards.
