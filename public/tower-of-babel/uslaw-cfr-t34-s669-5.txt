34 C.F.R. § 669.5 — What definitions apply?
Citation: us:cfr:34:669.5
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-669.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 669 (Language Resource Centers Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following definitions apply to this part:
(a) The definitions in 34 CFR 655.4.
(b) “Language Resource Center” means a coordinated concentration of educational research and training resources for improving the nation's capacity to teach and learn foreign languages.
