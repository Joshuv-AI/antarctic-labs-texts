46 C.F.R. § 154.412 — Cargo tank corrosion allowance.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.412
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.412
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A cargo tank must be designed with a corrosion allowance if the cargo tank:
(a) is located in a space that does not have inert gas or dry air; or
(b) carries a cargo that corrodes the tank material.
