Some new Liouville type theorems for 3D steady tropical climate model
Yanyan Dong, Yan Fang, Zhibing Zhang

Abstract
. In this paper, we establish two major classes of Liouville type results for the three-dimensional stationary tropical climate model. The first class is obtained under the assumptions imposed on u , v , θ u,v,\theta whereas the second one relies on the assumptions imposed on u , v , ∇ θ u,v,\nabla\theta . Using the energy method and an iteration argument, we obtain Liouville type theorems under the condition that Lebesgue norms of the smooth solutions on the annulus satisfy some power-law growth conditions. As a consequence, we show that a smooth solution is trivial provided that it belongs to some Lebesgue spaces or satisfies some decay conditions at infinity. Furthermore, with the aid of a contradiction argument and by developing a systematic framework to handle the energy function associated with the non-trivial solutions, we obtain a logarithmic improvement of our Liouville type theorems. Our new framework is very effective for establishing logarithmic improvements of Liouville type theorems for coupled fluid equations.

Introduction

We consider the following stationary tropical climate model in
\mathbb{R}^{3}
:

\left\{\begin{array}[]{ll}-\Delta u+(u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla\pi+\divg(v\otimes v)=0,\\
-\Delta v+(u\cdot\nabla)v+\nabla\theta+(v\cdot\nabla)u=0,\\
-\Delta\theta+u\cdot\nabla\theta+\divg v=0,\\
\divg u=0,\end{array}\right.

(1.1)

where
u=(u_{1},u_{2},u_{3})
is the barotropic mode,
v=(v_{1},v_{2},v_{3})
is the first baroclinic mode of vector velocity,
\theta
is the temperature, and
\pi
is the pressure.
When
v=0
and
\theta
is a constant, (
1.1
) is reduced to the Navier-Stokes equations.
The tropical climate model is derived by Frierson, Majda and Pauluis
[
19
]
for large-scale dynamics of precipitation fronts in the tropical atmosphere.
More relevant background on the tropical climate model can be found in
[
25
,
26
]
and the references therein.
Mathematically, (
1.1
) exhibits a distinctive structure arising from the coupling between the divergence-free vector field
u
and the non-divergence-free vector field
v
. Such mixed coupling structure brings essential mathematical difficulties and analytical challenges.

In recent years, Liouville type theorems for the fluid equations have attracted increasing attention. There are many interesting Liouville type results for the Navier-Stokes equations.
In this paper, we only refer to several results relevant to ours. For instance, Galdi
[
20
]
proved that
u
is a trivial solution under the assumption that
u\in L^{\frac{9}{2}}(\mathbb{R}^{3})
. Subsequently, Chae and Wolf
[
8
]
made a logarithmic improvement to Galdi’s result by assuming that
u
satisfies

\int_{\mathbb{R}^{3}}|u|^{\frac{9}{2}}\left\{\log\left(2+\frac{1}{|u|}\right)\right\}^{-1}dx<+\infty.

Seregin
[
28
]
handled the pressure term by using a Bogovskii map on the ball, and proved that
u
is trivial if
u
fulfils

\displaystyle\sup_{R>0}R^{\beta}\left(\frac{1}{|B_{R}|}\int_{B_{R}}|u|^{p}dx\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}<+\infty,~~~~~~~~~~~~with~~~~~~~~~~~~\frac{3}{2}<p<3~~~~~~~~~~~~and~~~~~~~~~~~~\beta>\frac{6p-3}{8p-6}.

Recently, Cho et al.
[
13
]
got an improvement on the result of Seregin. They showed that
u
is trivial if
u
satisfies one of the following conditions

\displaystyle(\mathrm{i})\liminf_{R\rightarrow+\infty}R^{-\left(\frac{2}{p}-\frac{1}{3}\right)}\|u\|_{L^{p}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{R})}<+\infty,\;\frac{3}{2}<p<3,

\displaystyle(\mathrm{ii})\liminf_{R\rightarrow+\infty}R^{-\frac{1}{3}}\|u\|_{L^{3}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{R})}=0.

Very recently, Cho-Yang
[
14
]
got a logarithmic improvement of Liouville type results. Specifically, they proved the triviality of the smooth solution if

\limsup_{R\rightarrow+\infty}\frac{\|u\|_{L^{p}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{\frac{3R}{2}})}}{R^{\frac{2}{p}-\frac{1}{3}}(\ln R)^{\frac{3}{p}-1}}<+\infty,\;\frac{3}{2}<p<3.

For more Liouville type results for the Navier-Stokes equations, see
[
1
,

,

,

,

,

,

and the references therein.

However, there are relatively few Liouville type results for the tropical climate model so far. Ding and Wu
[
16
]
investigated the tropical climate model and proved Liouville type theorems under one of the following assumptions

\displaystyle(\mathrm{i})~~~~u\in L^{p}\left(\mathbb{R}^{3}\right)~~~~~~~~~~~~\text{and}~~~~~~~~~~~~v,\theta\in L^{p}\left(\mathbb{R}^{3}\right)\cap L^{3}\left(\mathbb{R}^{3}\right)~~~~~~~~~~~~\text{with}~~~~~~~~~~~~3\leq p\leq\frac{9}{2},

\displaystyle(\mathrm{ii})~~~~u\in L^{3}\left(\mathbb{R}^{3}\right),v\in L^{2}\left(\mathbb{R}^{3}\right)~~~~~~~~~~~~\text{and}~~~~~~~~~~~~\nabla u,\nabla v,\nabla\theta\in L^{2}\left(\mathbb{R}^{3}\right).

Later, Cho et al.
[
11
]
improved the result of Ding and Wu by removing the condition
\nabla u,\nabla v\in L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{3})
from the above assumption
(\mathrm{ii})

Recently, Cho et al.
[
12
]
established an improved Liouville type theorem for (
1.1
) under one of the following conditions

\displaystyle(\mathrm{i})\;p\in\left(\frac{3}{2},3\right),\;q,r\in[1,2),\;\liminf_{R\rightarrow+\infty}[G(u;p,R)+H(v;q,R)+H(\theta;r,R)]<+\infty,

(1.2a)

\displaystyle(\mathrm{ii})\liminf_{R\rightarrow+\infty}[G(u;3,R)+H(v;2,R)]=0,\;\limsup_{R\rightarrow+\infty}H(\theta;2,R)<+\infty,

(1.2b)

\displaystyle(\mathrm{iii})\liminf_{R\rightarrow+\infty}[G(u;3,R)+H(\theta;2,R)]=0,\;\limsup_{R\rightarrow+\infty}H(v;2,R)<+\infty,

(1.2c)

where the functions

and

G(f;p,R):=R^{-\left(\frac{2}{p}-\frac{1}{3}\right)}\|f\|_{L^{p}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{\frac{R}{2}})}~~~~~~~~~~~~and~~~~~~~~~~~~H(f;p,R):=R^{-\left(\frac{3}{2p}-\frac{1}{4}\right)}\|f\|_{L^{p}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{\frac{R}{2}})}.

By means of the energy method and an iteration argument, we establish Liouville type theorems under the condition that Lebesgue norms of the smooth solutions on the annulus satisfy some power-law growth conditions. As a consequence, we show that a smooth solution is trivial provided that it belongs to some Lebesgue spaces or satisfies some decay conditions at infinity.
It is not difficult to verify that Liouville type theorem under (

while Liouville type theorem under (

) or (

On the other hand, using a contradiction argument and developing a systematic framework to handle the energy function associated with the non-trivial solutions, we extend Cho-Yang’s logarithmic improvement of Liouville type theorems for the Navier-Stokes equations to (
1.1
). In
[

, Cho-Yang proved their logarithmic improvement result via a case-by-case analysis based on

\displaystyle\liminf_{R\rightarrow+\infty}\frac{\|u\|_{L^{3}(B_{2R}\backslash B_{\frac{3R}{2}})}}{R^{\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{p}}(\ln R)^{\frac{3}{p}-1}}<+\infty\text{ or }=+\infty.

To the best of our knowledge, their method cannot be applied to establish Liouville type results of the logarithmic version for the fluid equations coupled with the Navier-Stokes equations. Our new approach avoids the case-by-case discussion and is very effective for the coupled fluid equations.

In this paper, we establish two major classes of Liouville type results. The first class is obtained under the assumptions imposed on
u,v,\theta
whereas the second one relies on the assumptions imposed on
u,v,\nabla\theta
. In order to show our Liouville type results, we need to introduce some notations. Let
A_{R}
denote the annulus
B_{2R}\backslash\overline{B_{\frac{3R}{2}}}
. For simplicity, we denote

\displaystyle X_{p,\alpha}(R)=R^{-\alpha}\|u\|_{L^{p}\left(A_{R}\right)},\;X_{p,\alpha,\lambda}(R)=R^{-\alpha}(\ln R)^{-\lambda}\|u\|_{L^{p}\left(A_{R}\right)},

\displaystyle Y_{q,\beta}(R)=R^{-\beta}\|v\|_{L^{q}\left(A_{R}\right)},\;Y_{q,\beta,\mu}(R)=R^{-\beta}(\ln R)^{-\mu}\|v\|_{L^{q}\left(A_{R}\right)},

\displaystyle Z_{r,\gamma}(R)=R^{-\gamma}\|\theta\|_{L^{r}\left(A_{R}\right)},\;Z_{r,\gamma,\nu}(R)=R^{-\gamma}(\ln R)^{-\nu}\|\theta\|_{L^{r}\left(A_{R}\right)},

\displaystyle Z^{\prime}_{r,\gamma}(R)=R^{-\gamma}\|\nabla\theta\|_{L^{r}\left(A_{R}\right)},\;Z^{\prime}_{r,\gamma,\nu}(R)=R^{-\gamma}(\ln R)^{-\nu}\|\nabla\theta\|_{L^{r}\left(A_{R}\right)}.

Assumption 1.1
.

Let
(p,q,r)\in\left(\frac{3}{2},\frac{9}{2}\right]\times[1,6]\times[1,6]

Assumption 1.2

(i)

Let

(i)

(iii)

Lemma 2.1
.

(
See
[
30
, Lemma 1]
)

Let

be a bounded Lipschitz domain in
\mathbb{R}^{3}
. Denote
L_{0}^{\sigma}(E):=\{f\in L^{\sigma}(E):\int_{E}fdx=0\}
with
1<\sigma<\infty
. There exists a linear operator

\mathrm{Bog}:L_{0}^{\sigma}(E)\rightarrow W_{0}^{1,\sigma}(E),

such that for any

is a vector field satisfying

w\in W_{0}^{1,\sigma}(E),\hskip 8.5359pt\mathrm{div}w=f,\hskip 8.5359pt\|\nabla w\|_{L^{\sigma}(E)}\leq C_{\mathrm{Bog}}(E,\sigma)\|f\|_{L^{\sigma}(E)},

where the constant

is independent of
f
. By using a rescaling argument, we see

C_{\mathrm{Bog}}(RE,\sigma)=C_{\mathrm{Bog}}(E,\sigma),\text{ where $RE=\{Rx:x\in E\}$ and $R>0$.}

The second lemma is the so-called Poincaré-Sobolev inequality. Let
(f)_{\Omega}
represent the mean value of the function
f
over
\Omega
.

Lemma 2.2
.

(
See
[
22
, Theorem 3.15]
and
[
11
, Remark 2]
)

Let
\Omega\subset\mathbb{R}^{n}
be a bounded Lipschitz domain. Assume

. Then there exists a positive constant

such that

\|f-(f)_{\Omega}\|_{L^{\frac{np}{n-p}}(\Omega)}\leq C(n,p,\Omega)\|\nabla f\|_{L^{p}(\Omega)}\text{ for every $f\in W^{1,p}(\Omega)$.}

By using a rescaling argument, we see that the constant
C(n,p,R\Omega)
does not depend on
R
, i.e.,
C(n,p,R\Omega)=C(n,p,\Omega)
.

The third lemma is the so-called Poincaré inequality.

Lemma 2.3
.

(
See
[
18
, p.292, Theorem 1]
)

be a bounded

. Then there exists a positive constant

(4.43)

\displaystyle C\chi_{2}(r)[R\ln RE^{\prime}(R)]^{\frac{6p-3\min\{r,2p^{\prime}\}(p-1)}{(6-\min\{r,2p^{\prime}\})p}}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{20}E(R_{2})+C[R\ln RE^{\prime}(R)]^{\tau},

(4.44)

\displaystyle C\chi_{3}(q,r)\left[R\ln RE^{\prime}(R)\right]^{\frac{6-3\min\{q,2\}}{12-2\min\{q,2\}}+\frac{6-3\min\{r,2\}}{12-2\min\{r,2\}}}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{20}E(R_{2})+C[R\ln RE^{\prime}(R)]^{\tau}.

(4.45)

Combining (

), (

), (

), (

), (

) and (

), we obtain

\displaystyle E(R)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}E(R)+\frac{1}{4}E(R_{2})+C[R\ln RE^{\prime}(R)]^{\tau}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{3}{4}E(R)+C[R\ln RE^{\prime}(R)]^{\tau},

which implies

\displaystyle E(R)

\displaystyle\leq C\left[R\ln RE^{\prime}(R)\right]^{\tau}.

Consequently, it follows that

\displaystyle\ln\ln R-\ln\ln R_{0}=\int_{R_{0}}^{R}\frac{1}{\rho\ln\rho}d\rho\leq\int_{R_{0}}^{R}\frac{CE^{\prime}(\rho)}{E^{\frac{1}{\tau}}(\rho)}d\rho\leq CE(R_{0})^{1-\frac{1}{\tau}}<+\infty.

Letting

, the above inequality leads to a contradiction. Therefore,
E(R)\equiv 0
.

Thanks to the simple inequality

we conclude that

force

to be zero.
∎
