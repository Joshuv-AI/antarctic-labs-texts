ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY

40 CFR Part 82


Protection of Stratospheric Ozone; CFR Correction

    This rule is being published by the Office of the Federal Register 
to correct an editorial or technical error that appeared in the most 
recent annual revision of the Code of Federal Regulations.
    In Title 40 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Parts 82 to 84, 
revised as of July 1, 2024, in appendix B to subpart G, in the table 
titled ``Refrigerants--Acceptable Subject to Use Conditions'', 
footnotes 4 through 7 are added to read as follows:

Appendix B to Subpart G of Part 82--Substitutes Subject To Use 
Restrictions and Unacceptable Substitutes

Refrigerants--Acceptable Subject to Use Conditions

* * * * *
    \4\ SAE, J639 NOV2020, Safety and Design Standards for Motor 
Vehicle Refrigerant Vapor Compression Systems, Revised November 
2020.
    \5\ SAE, J1739 JAN2021, Potential Failure Mode and Effects 
Analysis (FMEA) Including Design FMEA, Supplemental FMEA-MSR, and 
Process FMEA, Revised January 2021.
    \6\ SAE, J2844 JAN2013, R-1234yf (HFO-1234yf) New Refrigerant 
Purity and Container Requirements for Use in Mobile Air-Conditioning 
Systems, Revised January 2013.
