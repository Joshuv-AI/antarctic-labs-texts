15 U.S.C. § 5307 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:15:5307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section5307
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 79 (Metal Casting Competitiveness Research Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary for carrying out this chapter $5,000,000 for each of the fiscal years 1991, 1992, 1993, 1994, 1995, 1996, and 1997, to be derived from such sums as are otherwise authorized under section 13451(e) of title 42.
