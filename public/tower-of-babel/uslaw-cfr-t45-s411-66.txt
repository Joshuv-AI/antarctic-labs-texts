45 C.F.R. § 411.66 — Protection of UCs from contact with alleged abusers.
Citation: us:cfr:45:411.66
Creator: Office Of Refugee Resettlement, Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-411.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 411 (Standards To Prevent, Detect, And Respond To Sexual Abuse And Sexual Harassment Involving Unaccompanied Children). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

ORR and care provider facility staff, contractors, and volunteers suspected of perpetrating sexual abuse or sexual harassment must be suspended from all duties that would involve or allow access to UCs pending the outcome of an investigation.
