28 C.F.R. § 522.12 — Relationship between existing criminal sentences imposed under the U.S. or D.C. Code and new civil contempt commitment orders.
Citation: us:cfr:28:522.12
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-522.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 522 (Admission To Institution). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a criminal sentence imposed under the U.S. Code or D.C. Code exists when a civil contempt commitment is ordered, we delay or suspend credit towards service of the criminal sentence for the duration of the civil contempt commitment, unless the committing judge orders otherwise.
