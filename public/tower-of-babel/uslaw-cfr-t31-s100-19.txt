31 C.F.R. § 100.19 — Disposition of counterfeit notes and coins.
Citation: us:cfr:31:100.19
Creator: Monetary Offices, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-100.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 100 (Exchange Of Paper Currency And Coin). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All counterfeit notes and coin found in remittances are cancelled and delivered to the U.S. Secret Service of the Department of the Treasury or to the nearest local office of that Service, a receipt for the same being forwarded to the sender. Communications with respect thereto should be addressed to the Director, U.S. Secret Service, Department of the Treasury, Washington, DC 20223.
