40 U.S.C. § 14522 — Consent of States
Citation: us:usc:40:14522
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section14522
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 145 (Special Appalachian Programs); Subchapter II (Administrative). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subtitle does not require a State to engage in or accept a program under this subtitle without its consent.
