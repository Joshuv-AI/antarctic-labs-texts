36 U.S.C. § 21502 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:36:21502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section21502
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 215 (American Hospital Of Paris). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of the corporation is to maintain a hospital in the vicinity of Paris, France, to provide medical and surgical care to citizens of the United States.
