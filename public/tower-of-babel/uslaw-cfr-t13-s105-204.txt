13 C.F.R. § 105.204 — Assistance to SBA employees or members of their household.
Citation: us:cfr:13:105.204
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-105.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 105 (Standards Of Conduct And Employee Restrictions And Responsibilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Without the prior written approval of the Standards of Conduct Committee, no SBA Assistance, other than Disaster loans under subparagraphs (1) and (2) of section 7(b) of the Small Business Act, shall be furnished to a person when the sole proprietor, partner, officer, director or significant stockholder of the person is an SBA employee or a household member.
