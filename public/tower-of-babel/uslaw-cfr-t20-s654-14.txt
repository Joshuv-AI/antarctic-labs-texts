20 C.F.R. § 654.14 — Filing of complaints.
Citation: us:cfr:20:654.14
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-654.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 654 (Special Responsibilities Of The Employment Service System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Complaints arising under subpart B of this part alleging that the Department of Labor has violated the labor surplus area regulations shall be made pursuant to the procedures set forth at § 654.9 of this part.
