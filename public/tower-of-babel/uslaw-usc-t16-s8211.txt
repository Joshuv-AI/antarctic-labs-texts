16 U.S.C. § 8211 — Nonapplicability of Federal Advisory Committee Act
Citation: us:usc:16:8211
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section8211
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 101 (National Fish Habitat Conservation Through Partnerships). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Chapter 10 of title 5 (commonly known as the “Federal Advisory Committee Act”), shall not apply to—
(1)
the Board; or
(2)
any Partnership.
