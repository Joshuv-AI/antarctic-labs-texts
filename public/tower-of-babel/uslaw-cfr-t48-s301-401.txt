48 C.F.R. § 301.401 — 301.401 Deviations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:301.401
Creator: Chapter 3—Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-301.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 301 (Hhs Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officers are not permitted to deviate from the FAR or HHSAR without seeking proper approval. With full acknowledgement of FAR 1.102(d) regarding innovative approaches, any deviation to FAR or the HHSAR requires approval by the Senior Procurement Executive (SPE).
