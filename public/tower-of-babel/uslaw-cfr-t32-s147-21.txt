32 C.F.R. § 147.21 — Expanding investigations.
Citation: us:cfr:32:147.21
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-147.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 147 (Adjudicative Guidelines For Determining Eligibility For Access To Classified Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Investigations and reinvestigations may be expanded under the provisions of Executive Order 12968 (60 FR 40245, 3 CFR 1995 Comp., p. 391) and other applicable statutes and Executive Orders.
