49 C.F.R. § 385.1017 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:49:385.1017
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-385.1017
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 385 (Safety Fitness Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any motor carrier that the Agency determines to be in violation of this subpart shall be subject to the civil or criminal penalty provisions of 49 U.S.C. 521(b) and applicable regulations.
