42 C.F.R. § 403.250 — Loss ratio calculations: General provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:403.250
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-403.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 403 (Special Programs And Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Basic formula. The expected loss ratio is calculated by determining the ratio of benefits to premiums.
(b) Calculations. The insuring organization must calculate loss ratios according to the provisions of §§ 403.251, 403.253, and 403.254.
