42 C.F.R. § 84.111 — Gas masks; required components.

Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-84.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 84 (Approval Of Respiratory Protective Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each gas mask described in § 84.110 shall, where its design requires, contain the following component parts:
(1) Facepiece or mouthpiece and noseclip;
(2) Canister or cartridge;
(3) Canister harness;
(4) External check valve; and
(5) Breathing tube.
(b) The components of each gas mask shall meet the minimum construction requirements set forth in subpart G of this part.
