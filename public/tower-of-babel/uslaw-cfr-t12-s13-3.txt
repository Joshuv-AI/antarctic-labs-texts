12 C.F.R. § 13.3 — Business conduct.
Citation: us:cfr:12:13.3
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-13.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 13 (Government Securities Sales Practices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A bank that is a government securities broker or dealer shall observe high standards of commercial honor and just and equitable principles of trade in the conduct of its business as a government securities broker or dealer.
