28 C.F.R. § 2.25 — Hearings by videoconference.
Citation: us:cfr:28:2.25
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-2.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 2 (Parole, Release, Supervision And Recommitment Of Prisoners, Youth Offenders, And Juvenile Delinquents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may conduct a parole determination hearing (including a rescission hearing), a probable cause hearing, an institutional revocation hearing, and a parole termination hearing by videoconference between the hearing examiner and the prisoner or releasee.
