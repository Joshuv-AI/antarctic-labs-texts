23 C.F.R. § 750.307 — FHWA project approval.
Citation: us:cfr:23:750.307
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-750.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 750 (Highway Beautification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Authorization to proceed with acquisitions on a sign removal project shall not be issued until such time as the State has submitted to FHWA the following:
(a) A general description of the project.
(b) The total number of signs to be acquired.
(c) The total estimated cost of the sign removal project, including a breakdown of incidental, acquisition and removal costs.
