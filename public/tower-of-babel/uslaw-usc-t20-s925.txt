20 U.S.C. § 925 — Budget request for school construction funds for Director of Dependents’ Education
Citation: us:usc:20:925
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section925
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 25A (Overseas Defense Dependents’ Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall include in his budget for each fiscal year a separate request for funds for construction of school facilities by the Director.
