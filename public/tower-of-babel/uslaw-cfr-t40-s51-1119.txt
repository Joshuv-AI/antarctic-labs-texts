40 C.F.R. § 51.1119 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:51.1119
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-51.1119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 51 (Requirements For Preparation, Adoption, And Submittal Of Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As of revocation of the 1997 ozone NAAQS on April 6, 2015, as set forth in § 50.10(c), the provisions of subpart AA shall replace the provisions of subpart X, §§ 51.900 to 51.918, which cease to apply except for § 51.907 for the anti-backsliding purposes of § 51.1105(c)(2). See subpart X § 51.919.
