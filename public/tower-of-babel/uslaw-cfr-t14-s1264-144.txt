14 C.F.R. § 1264.144 — Deposit in Treasury of United States.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1264.144
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1264.144
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1264 (Implementation Of The Program Fraud Civil Penalties Act Of 1986). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All amounts collected pursuant to this part shall be deposited as miscellaneous receipts in the Treasury of the United States, except as provided in 31 U.S.C. 3806(g).
