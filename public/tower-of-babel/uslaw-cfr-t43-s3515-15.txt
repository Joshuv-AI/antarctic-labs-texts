43 C.F.R. § 3515.15 — May BLM initiate an exchange?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3515.15
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3515.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. When we do:
(a) We will notify you that we are prepared to consider exchange of a mineral lease if you relinquish your existing leasing rights.
(b) We may exchange all or any part of the lands under your preference right lease application(s) or lease(s).
