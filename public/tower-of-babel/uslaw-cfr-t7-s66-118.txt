7 C.F.R. § 66.118 — Other claims.
Citation: us:cfr:7:66.118
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-66.118
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 66 (National Bioengineered Food Disclosure Standard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart will prohibit regulated entities from making other claims regarding bioengineered foods, provided that such claims are consistent with applicable Federal law.
