42 C.F.R. § 137.265 — May a Tribe be reimbursed for actual and reasonable close out costs incurred after the effective date of reassumption?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.265
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.265
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, a Tribe may be reimbursed for actual and reasonable close out costs incurred after the effective date of reassumption.
