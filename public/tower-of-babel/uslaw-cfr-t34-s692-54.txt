34 C.F.R. § 692.54 — What eligibility requirements must a student satisfy to participate in the SLEAP Program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:692.54
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-692.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 692 (Leveraging Educational Assistance Partnership Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To receive assistance under the SLEAP Program, a student must meet the eligibility requirements contained in § 692.40.
