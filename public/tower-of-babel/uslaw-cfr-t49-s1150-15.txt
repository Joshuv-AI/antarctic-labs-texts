49 C.F.R. § 1150.15 — Information about offeror.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1150.15
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1150.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1150 (Certificate To Construct, Acquire, Or Operate Railroad Lines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The name and address of the offeror of the rail service continuation payment.
(b) Sufficient information to establish the financial responsibility of the offeror for the proposed undertaking, or if the offeror is a State or municipal corporation or authority, a statement that it has authority to perform the service or enter into the agreement for subsidy.
