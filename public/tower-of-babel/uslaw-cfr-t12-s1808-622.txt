12 C.F.R. § 1808.622 — Compliance with government requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1808.622
Creator: Community Development Financial Institutions Fund, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1808.622
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1808 (Community Development Financial Institutions Bond Guarantee Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In carrying out its responsibilities pursuant to any agreements associated with the CDFI Bond Guarantee Program, all Qualified Issuers, Eligible CDFIs, Program Administrators, Servicers, and the Master Servicer/Trustee shall comply with all applicable Federal, State, and local laws, regulations, and ordinances, OMB Circulars, and Executive Orders, including restrictions on lending to entities with delinquent Federal debt.
