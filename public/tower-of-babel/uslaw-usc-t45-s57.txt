45 U.S.C. § 57 — Who included in term “common carrier”
Citation: us:usc:45:57
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section57
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 2 (Liability For Injuries To Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The term “common carrier” as used in this chapter shall include the receiver or receivers or other persons or corporations charged with the duty of the management and operation of the business of a common carrier.
