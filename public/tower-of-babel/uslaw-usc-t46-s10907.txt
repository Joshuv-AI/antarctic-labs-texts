46 U.S.C. § 10907 — Permission to make complaint
Citation: us:usc:46:10907
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section10907
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 109 (Proceedings On Unseaworthiness). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
A master may not refuse to permit, deny the opportunity to, or hinder a seaman who wishes to make a complaint authorized by this chapter.
(b)
A master violating this section is liable to the United States Government for civil penalty of $500.
