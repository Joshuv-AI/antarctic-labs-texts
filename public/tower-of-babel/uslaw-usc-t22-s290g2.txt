22 U.S.C. § 290g–2 — Law governing reports to the President and the Congress
Citation: us:usc:22:290g–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section290g–2
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XXII (African Development Fund). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of section 286b of this title, shall apply with respect to the Fund to the same extent as with respect to the International Bank for Reconstruction and Development and the International Monetary Fund.
