20 U.S.C. § 3447 — Effect of transfers
Citation: us:usc:20:3447
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section3447
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 48 (Department Of Education); Subchapter III (Transfers Of Agencies And Functions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The transfer of a function or office from an officer or agency to the Secretary or to the Department includes any aspects of such function or office vested in a subordinate of such officer or in a component of such agency.
