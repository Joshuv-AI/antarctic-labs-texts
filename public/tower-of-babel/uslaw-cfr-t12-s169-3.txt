12 C.F.R. § 169.3 — Holders of proxies.
Citation: us:cfr:12:169.3
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-169.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 169 (Proxies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No proxy of a mutual savings association with a term greater than eleven months or solicited at the expense of the association may designate as holder anyone other than the board of directors [trustees] as a whole, or a committee appointed by a majority of such board.
