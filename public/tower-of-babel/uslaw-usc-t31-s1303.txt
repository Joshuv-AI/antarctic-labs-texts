31 U.S.C. § 1303 — Effect of changes in titles of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:31:1303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section1303
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 13 (Appropriations); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Expenditures for a particular object or purpose authorized by a law (and referred to in that law by the specific title previously used for the appropriation item in the appropriation law concerned) may be made from a corresponding appropriation item when the specific title is changed or eliminated from a later appropriation law.
