2 C.F.R. § 1128.700 — Purpose of FMS Article VII.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1128.700
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1128.700
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1128 (Recipient Financial And Program Management: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FMS Article VII of the general terms and conditions specifies requirements for program income that recipients earn. The article thereby implements OMB guidance in 2 CFR 200.80 and 200.307.
