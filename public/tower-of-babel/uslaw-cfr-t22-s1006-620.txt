22 C.F.R. § 1006.620 — Do Federal agencies coordinate suspension and debarment actions?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1006.620
Creator: Inter-American Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1006.620
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1006 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, when more than one Federal agency has an interest in a suspension or debarment, the agencies may consider designating one agency as the lead agency for making the decision. Agencies are encouraged to establish methods and procedures for coordinating their suspension and debarment actions.
