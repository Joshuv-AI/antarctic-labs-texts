45 C.F.R. § 504.5 — Documents to accompany forms.
Citation: us:cfr:45:504.5
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-504.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 504 (Filing Of Claims And Procedures Therefor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All claims filed pursuant to sections 5(a) and 6(f) of the Act must be accompanied by evidentiary documents, instruments, and records as outlined in the instruction sheet attached to the claim form.
