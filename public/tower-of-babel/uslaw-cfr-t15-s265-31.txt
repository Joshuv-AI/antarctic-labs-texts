15 C.F.R. § 265.31 — Closing the site.
Citation: us:cfr:15:265.31
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-265.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 265 (Regulations Governing Traffic And Conduct On The Grounds Of The National Institute Of Standards & Technology, Gaithersburg, Maryland, And Boulder And Fort Collins, Colorado). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As determined by the Director (Director, NIST Boulder Laboratories, for sites in Colorado), the site may be closed to the public in emergency situations and at such other times as may be necessary for the orderly conduct of the Government's business. At such times no person shall enter the site except authorized individuals, who may be required to sign a register and display identification when requested by a uniformed guard.
