54 U.S.C. § 104904 — Hire of work animals, vehicles, and equipment with or without personal services
Citation: us:usc:54:104904
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section104904
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1049 (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may hire, with or without personal services, work animals and animal-drawn and motor-propelled vehicles and equipment at rates to be approved by the Secretary and without compliance with section 6101 of title 41.
