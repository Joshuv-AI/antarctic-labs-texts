50 C.F.R. § 1.7 — Regional director.
Citation: us:cfr:50:1.7
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-1.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 1 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Regional director means the official in charge of a region of the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service or the authorized representative of such official.
