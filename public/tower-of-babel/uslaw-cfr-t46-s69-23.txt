46 C.F.R. § 69.23 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:46:69.23
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-69.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 69 (Measurement Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Measurement organizations are authorized to charge a fee for measurement services. Information on fees is available directly from the organizations.
