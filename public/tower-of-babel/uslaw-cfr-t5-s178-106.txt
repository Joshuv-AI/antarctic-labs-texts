5 C.F.R. § 178.106 — Form of claim settlements.
Citation: us:cfr:5:178.106
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-178.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 178 (Procedures For Settling Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OPM will send a settlement to the claimant advising whether the claim may be allowed in whole or in part. If OPM requested an agency report or if the agency forwarded the claim on behalf of the claimant, OPM also will send the agency a copy of the settlement.
