40 C.F.R. § 194.31 — Application of release limits.

Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-194.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 194 (Criteria For The Certification And Re-Certification Of The Waste Isolation Pilot Plant'S Compliance With The 40 Cfr Part 191 Disposal Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The release limits shall be calculated according to part 191, appendix A of this chapter, using the total activity, in curies, that will exist in the disposal system at the time of disposal.
