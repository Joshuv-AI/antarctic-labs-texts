10 C.F.R. § 55.3 — License requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:10:55.3
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-55.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 55 (Operators' Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person must be authorized by a license issued by the Commission to perform the function of an operator or a senior operator as defined in this part.
