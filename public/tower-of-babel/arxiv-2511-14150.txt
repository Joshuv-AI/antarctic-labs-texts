Mass spectra and Mott transitions of neutral mesons at finite temperature and magnetic field in frame of three-flavor Polyakov-extended Nambu-Jona-Lasino model
Luyang Li 1, Shijun Mao 2

Abstract
Mass spectra and Mott transitions of neutral mesons K 0 , π 0 , η , η ′ K_{0},\pi_{0},\eta,\eta^{\prime} at finite temperature and magnetic field are investigated in a three-flavor PNJL model. We focus on the effect of gluons, which is simulated by the Polyakov potential and the inverse magnetic catalysis (IMC) effect, which is mimicked by using a magnetic field dependent coupling constant. Mass spectra show similar structure when introducing the contribution of Polyakov potential and IMC effect. The mass of K 0 K_{0} meson m K 0 m_{K_{0}} is controlled by chiral symmetry breaking and restoration. It increases with temperature in the low temperature region, and shows a mass jump at the Mott transition. Further increasing temperature, m K 0 m_{K_{0}} firstly decreases and then increases with temperature. π 0 \pi_{0} meson is not only the pseudo-Goldstone boson of chiral symmetry breaking, but also influenced by the flavor mixing of π 0 − η − η ′ \pi_{0}-\eta-\eta^{\prime} . The behavior of m π 0 m_{\pi_{0}} is different from m K 0 m_{K_{0}} only at high temperature region, which decreases with temperature. For η \eta and η ′ \eta^{\prime} mesons, they are affected by both the U A ​ ( 1 ) U_{A}(1) anomaly and the flavor mixing of π 0 − η − η ′ \pi_{0}-\eta-\eta^{\prime} . The mass of η \eta meson m η m_{\eta} decreases with temperature in low temperature region and then shows a jump at its Mott transition. After that m η m_{\eta} firstly decreases and later increases with temperature. η ′ \eta^{\prime} mesons is a resonant state in vacuum and in medium, and its mass m η ′ m_{\eta^{\prime}} continuously decreases and then increases with temperature. The mass jumps of K 0 , π 0 , η K_{0},\pi_{0},\eta mesons are caused by the dimension reduction of the constituent quarks under external magnetic field. In the PNJL model, the Mott transition temperature of K 0 , π 0 K_{0},\ \pi_{0} mesons ( η \eta meson) decrease (increases) with magnetic field. The IMC effect leads to the lower value for meson Mott transition temperature.

III.1
gluon effect

As shown in Fig.
1
upper panel, with fixed magnetic field
eB=20m^{2}_{\pi}
, the mass of
K_{0}
meson
m_{K_{0}}
(see red lines) increases with temperature in the low temperature region. And then, it shows an abrupt jump at the Mott transition temperature
T^{K_{0}}_{\text{Mott}}=190
MeV, where the
K_{0}
meson changes from the bound state with
m_{K_{0}}<m_{d}+m_{s}
to the resonant state
m_{K_{0}}>m_{d}+m_{s}
. After that,
m_{K_{0}}
firstly decreases and then increases with temperature. As pseudo-Goldstone boson of chiral symmetry spontaneous breaking, when the constituent quark mass decreases, the mass of
K_{0}
meson will generally increase. Therefore chiral symmetry restoration is expected to induce an intersection between the
K_{0}
meson mass
m_{K_{0}}
and the sum of two constituent quark masses
m_{d}+m_{s}
, which defines the Mott transition of
K_{0}
meson
mott1
;
zhuang
;
mott2
;
mott3
. Based on the polarization function (
16
), with
p_{z}=0
and lowest Landau level
l=0
, the integral term
\frac{1}{(E_{s}+E_{d})^{2}-k^{2}_{0}}
diverges when
k_{0}=m_{d}+m_{s}
. This infrared divergence leads to the mass jump of
K_{0}
meson at the Mott transition. It should be mentioned that, when the
K_{0}
meson mass
m_{K_{0}}
reaches the sum of the effective masses of constituent quarks corresponding to higher Landau levels
\sqrt{2l|Q_{d}B|+m_{d}^{2}}+\sqrt{2l|Q_{s}B|+m_{s}^{2}}
with
l=1,2,...
, such infrared divergence still exists. Therefore, when the mass of
K_{0}
meson
m_{K_{0}}
is large enough, it may undergo more than one jump, which is, however, not observed in the current numerical calculations. Besides, the integral term
\frac{1}{(E_{s}-E_{d})^{2}-k^{2}_{0}}
also has infrared divergence with small value of
k_{0}
, which is not relevant for the
K_{0}
meson mass.

Under external magnetic field,

IV
summary

Mass spectra and Mott transitions of neutral mesons
K_{0},\pi_{0},\eta,\eta^{\prime}
at finite temperature and magnetic field are investigated in a three-flavor PNJL model. One the one hand, we consider the effect of gluons, which is simulated by the Polyakov potential, by making comparison between the results of PNJL model and NJL model. On the other hand, we study the inverse magnetic catalysis (IMC) effect through the magnetic field dependent coupling constant in the PNJL model, which modifies the quark interaction and Polyakov potential, respectively.

Mass spectra show similar structure when introducing the contribution of Polyakov potential and IMC effect. The mass of
K_{0}
meson
m_{K_{0}}
is controlled by chiral symmetry breaking and restoration. It increases with temperature in the low temperature region, and shows a mass jump at the Mott transition. Further increasing temperature,
m_{K_{0}}
firstly decreases and then increases with temperature.
\pi_{0}
meson is not only the pseudo-Goldstone boson of chiral symmetry breaking, but also influenced by the flavor mixing of
\pi_{0}-\eta-\eta^{\prime}
. The behavior of
m_{\pi_{0}}
is different from
m_{K_{0}}
only at high temperature region, which decreases with temperature. For
\eta
and
\eta^{\prime}
mesons, they are affected by both the
U_{A}(1)
anomaly and the flavor mixing of
\pi_{0}-\eta-\eta^{\prime}
. The mass of
\eta
meson
m_{\eta}
decreases with temperature in low temperature region and then shows a jump at its Mott transition. After that
m_{\eta}
firstly decreases and later increases with temperature.
\eta^{\prime}
mesons is a resonant state in vacuum and in medium, and its mass
m_{\eta^{\prime}}
continuously decreases and then increases with temperature. Including the gluon effect and IMC effect modifies the value of meson masses.

The mass jumps of
K_{0},\pi_{0},\eta
mesons at their Mott transitions are caused by the dimension reduction of the constituent quarks under external magnetic field. Introducing the gluon effect leads to some changes on their Mott transition temperatures. In the PNJL model, the Mott transition temperature of
K_{0},\ \pi_{0}
mesons (
\eta
meson) decrease (increases) with magnetic field. However, in the NJL model, the Mott transition temperature of
K_{0}
meson shows non-monotonic behavior in strong magnetic field region. The IMC effect, simulated both in
G(eB)
and
T_{0}(eB)
scheme, leads to the lower value for meson Mott transition temperature.
