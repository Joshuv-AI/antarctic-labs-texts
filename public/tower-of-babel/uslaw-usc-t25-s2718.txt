25 U.S.C. § 2718 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:25:2718
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2718
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 29 (Indian Gaming Regulation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Subject to section 2717 of this title, there are authorized to be appropriated, for fiscal year 1998, and for each fiscal year thereafter, an amount equal to the amount of funds derived from the assessments authorized by section 2717(a) of this title.
(b)
Notwithstanding section 2717 of this title, there are authorized to be appropriated to fund the operation of the Commission, $2,000,000 for fiscal year 1998, and $2,000,000 for each fiscal year thereafter. The amounts authorized to be appropriated in the preceding sentence shall be in addition to the amounts authorized to be appropriated under subsection (a).
