12 C.F.R. § 217.402 — Identification as a global systemically important BHC.
Citation: us:cfr:12:217.402
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-217.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 217 (Capital Adequacy Of Bank Holding Companies, Savings And Loan Holding Companies, And State Member Banks (Regulation Q)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A bank holding company is a global systemically important BHC if its method 1 score, as calculated under § 217.404, equals or exceeds 130 basis points. Subject to § 217.400(b)(2), a bank holding company must calculate its method 1 score on an annual basis by December 31 of each year.
