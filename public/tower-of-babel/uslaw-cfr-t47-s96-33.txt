47 C.F.R. § 96.33 — Authorization.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 96 (Citizens Broadband Radio Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any party meeting the requirements set forth in § 96.5 is eligible to operate a CBSD on a General Authorized Access basis.
(b) CBSDs used for General Authorized Access must register with the SAS and comply with its instructions.
