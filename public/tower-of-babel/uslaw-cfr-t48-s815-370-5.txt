48 C.F.R. § 815.370-5 — 815.370-5 Solicitation provision.
Citation: us:cfr:48:815.370-5
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-815.370-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 815 (Contracting By Negotiation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use the provision at 852.215-72, Notice of Intent to Re-solicit, in competitive solicitations, including solicitations using FAR part 12 procedures for the acquisition of commercial items that will be solicited for fewer than 30 days, unless an exception at 815.370-4 applies.
