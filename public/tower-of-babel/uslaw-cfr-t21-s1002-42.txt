21 C.F.R. § 1002.42 — Confidentiality of records furnished by dealers and distributors.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1002.42
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1002.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1002 (Records And Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All information furnished to manufacturers by dealers and distributors pursuant to this part shall be treated by such manufacturers as confidential information which may be used only as necessary to notify persons pursuant to section 535 of the Act.
