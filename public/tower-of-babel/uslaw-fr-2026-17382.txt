NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION

[Notice 26-047]


Performance Review Board, Senior Executive Service (SES)

AGENCY: National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA).

ACTION: Notice of Membership of SES Performance Review Board.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Civil Service Reform Act of 1978, Public Law 95-454 
(Section 405) requires that appointments of individual members to the 
Performance Review Board (PRB) be published in the Federal Register.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The performance review function for the SES 
in NASA is being performed by the NASA PRB. The following individuals 
are serving on the Board:

Performance Review Board

Chairperson, Deputy Administrator
Associate Administrator
Deputy Associate Administrator
Chief Human Capital Officer
Associate Administrator, Research Technology Mission Directorate
KSC Center Director & Launch Operations

Stephanie Martin,
Lead, Executive Performance Management, Office of the Chief Human 
Capital Officer.
