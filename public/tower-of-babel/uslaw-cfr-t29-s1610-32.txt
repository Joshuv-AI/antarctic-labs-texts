29 C.F.R. § 1610.32 — Production prohibited unless approved by the Legal Counsel.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1610.32
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1610.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1610 (Availability Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No employee or former employee of the Commission shall, in response to a demand of a court or other authority, produce any material contained in the files of the Commission or disclose any information or produce any material acquired as part of the performance of his official duties or because of his official status without the prior approval of the Legal Counsel.
