12 U.S.C. § 3048 — Cooperation with Federal agencies offering programs for consumer cooperatives in disseminating information
Citation: us:usc:12:3048
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section3048
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 31 (National Consumer Cooperative Bank); Subchapter II (Office Of Self-Help Development And Technical Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Office shall work closely with all United States Government agencies offering programs for which consumer cooperatives may be eligible to assure that information concerning all such programs is made available to eligible cooperatives.
