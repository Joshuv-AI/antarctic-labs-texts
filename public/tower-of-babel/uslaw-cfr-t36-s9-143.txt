36 C.F.R. § 9.143 — When will the NPS release my financial assurance?
Citation: us:cfr:36:9.143
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-9.143
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 9 (Minerals Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will release your financial assurance within 30 days after the Regional Director:
(a) Determines that you have met all applicable reclamation operating standards and any additional reclamation requirements that may be included in your operations permit; or
(b) Accepts a new operator's financial assurance under § 9.160(b) or (c).
