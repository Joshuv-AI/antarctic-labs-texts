49 C.F.R. § 604.41 — Standard of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:49:604.41
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-604.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 604 (Charter Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The PO shall issue a recommended decision or shall rule in a party's favor only if the decision or ruling is supported by a preponderance of the evidence.
