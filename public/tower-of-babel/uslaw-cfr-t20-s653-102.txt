20 C.F.R. § 653.102 — Job information.
Citation: us:cfr:20:653.102
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-653.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 653 (Services Of The Wagner-Peyser Act Employment Service System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All SWAs must make job order information conspicuous and available to MSFWs by all reasonable means. Such information must, at minimum, be available through internet labor exchange systems and through the one-stop centers. SWAs must ensure ES staff at one-stop centers provide assistance to MSFWs to access job order information easily and efficiently.
