46 U.S.C. § 51511 — Midshipman status in the Navy Reserve
Citation: us:usc:46:51511
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section51511
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Merchant Marine Service); Chapter 515 (State Maritime Academy Support Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A citizen of the United States attending a State maritime academy may be appointed by the Secretary of the Navy as a midshipman in the Navy Reserve (including the Merchant Marine Reserve, Navy Reserve).
