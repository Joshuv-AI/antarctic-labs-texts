16 C.F.R. § 1117.7 — Confidentiality of reports.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1117.7
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1117.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1117 (Reporting Of Choking Incidents Involving Marbles, Small Balls, Latex Balloons And Other Small Parts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The confidentiality provisions of section 6 of the CPSA, 15 U.S.C. 2055, apply to reports submitted under this part. The Commission shall afford information submitted under this part the protection afforded to information submitted under section 15(b), in accordance with section 6(b)(5) of the CPSA and subpart G of part 1101 of title 16 of the CFR.
