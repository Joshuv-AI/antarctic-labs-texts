19 C.F.R. § 200.735-119 — -119 Information not known by employees.
Citation: us:cfr:19:200.735-119
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-200.735-119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 200 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any information required to be included in a statement of employment and financial interests or supplementary statement, including holdings placed in trust, is not known to the employee but is known to another person, the employee shall request that other person to submit information on his behalf.
