33 U.S.C. § 152 — Regulation of length of towlines
Citation: us:usc:33:152
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section152
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 3 (Navigation Rules For Harbors, Rivers, And Inland Waters Generally); Subchapter I (Preliminary). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commandant of the Coast Guard shall prepare regulations limiting the length of hawsers between towing vessels and seagoing barges in tow and the length of such tows within any of the inland waters of the United States designated and defined from time to time pursuant to section 151 of this title, and such regulations shall have the force of law.
