25 C.F.R. § 213.49 — Scope of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:25:213.49
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-213.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 213 (Leasing Of Restricted Lands Of Members Of Five Civilized Tribes, Oklahoma, For Mining). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part shall apply in so far as practicable to land purchased for Indians under the Oklahoma Indian Welfare Act of June 26, 1936 (49 Stat. 1967; 25 U.S.C. 501-509), as well as to other lands of individual Indians of the Five Civilized Tribes.
