5 C.F.R. § 8.3 — Appointment of persons not citizens of the United States.
Citation: us:cfr:5:8.3
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-8.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 8 (Appointments To Overseas Positions (Rule Viii)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons who are not citizens of the United States may be recruited overseas and appointed to overseas positions without regard to the Civil Service Act.
