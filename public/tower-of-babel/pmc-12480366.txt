Trends in the Prevalence of Anemia, 2012–2021
Published: 2023

The age-standardized prevalence of anemia among Korean adults ≥10 years in 2021 was more than four times higher in women (13.6%) than in men (3.1%), and it remained from 2020 (Figure 1). The prevalence of anemia was the highest among men and women aged ≥70 years (Figure 2).
