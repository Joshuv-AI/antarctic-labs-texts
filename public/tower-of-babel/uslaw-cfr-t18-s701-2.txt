18 C.F.R. § 701.2 — Creation and basic authority.

Creator: Water Resources Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-701.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 701 (Council Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Water Resources Council was established by the Water Resources Planning Act of 1965 (Pub. L. 89-80, 79 Stat. 244, as amended (42 U.S.C. 1962-1962d-5)). The rules and regulations of this part are promulgated by authority of section 402 of the Act (42 U.S.C. 1962d-1).
