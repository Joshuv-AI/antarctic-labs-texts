39 U.S.C. § 2805 — Inherently Governmental functions
Citation: us:usc:39:2805
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section2805
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part III (Modernization And Fiscal Administration); Chapter 28 (Strategic Planning And Performance Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The functions and activities of this chapter shall be considered to be inherently Governmental functions. The drafting of strategic plans, performance plans, and program performance reports under this section shall be performed only by employees of the Postal Service.
