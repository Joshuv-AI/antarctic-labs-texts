18 C.F.R. § 367.4582 — Account 458.2, Indirect costs charged to non-associate companies.
Citation: us:cfr:18:367.4582
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-367.4582
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 367 (Uniform System Of Accounts For Centralized Service Companies Subject To The Provisions Of The Public Utility Holding Company Act Of 2005, Federal Power Act And Natural Gas Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This account must include recovery of those indirect costs of services performed for non-associate companies that cannot be specifically assigned and therefore must be allocated. This account must not include any compensation for use of equity capital or inter-company interest on indebtedness.
