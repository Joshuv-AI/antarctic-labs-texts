7 C.F.R. § 7.20 — Prohibition on dual office.
Citation: us:cfr:7:7.20
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-7.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 7 (Selection And Functions Of Farm Service Agency State And County Committees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A member of the county committee cannot, during the time they are a committee member, also serve as:
(1) The secretary to the county committee;
(2) A member of the State committee; or
(3) A county executive director or any other county office employee.
(b) [Reserved]
