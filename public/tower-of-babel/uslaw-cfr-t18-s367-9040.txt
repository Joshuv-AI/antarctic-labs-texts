18 C.F.R. § 367.9040 — Account 904, Uncollectible accounts.
Citation: us:cfr:18:367.9040
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-367.9040
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 367 (Uniform System Of Accounts For Centralized Service Companies Subject To The Provisions Of The Public Utility Holding Company Act Of 2005, Federal Power Act And Natural Gas Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This account must be charged with amounts sufficient to provide for losses from uncollectible service company revenues. Concurrent credits must be made to account 144, Accumulated provision for uncollectible accounts—Credit (§ 367.1440). Losses from uncollectible accounts also must be charged to account 144 (§ 367.1440).
