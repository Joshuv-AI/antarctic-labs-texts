Figure 2 was missing from this article [1]; the figure should have appeared as shown below.

Omics approaches and their applications in halophilic microorganisms. As shown in the figure, metagenomics identifies microbial community composition (both cultivable and non-cultivable species) and dynamics in contaminated environments. Metatranscriptomics, via mRNA sequencing, reveals gene expression, identifies active microorganisms in biodegradation, and allows rapid monitoring of responses to environmental changes like salinity. Metaproteomics uncovers protein sequencing and active metabolic pathways. Additionally, advances in bioinformatics enable better integration of omics data, while metabolomics further elucidates microbiota dynamics in biodegradation
