10 U.S.C. § 18238 — Army National Guard of United States; Air National Guard of United States: limitation on relocation of units
Citation: us:usc:10:18238
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section18238
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 1803 (Facilities For Reserve Components). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A unit of the Army National Guard of the United States or the Air National Guard of the United States may not be relocated or withdrawn under this chapter without the consent of the governor of the State or, in the case of the District of Columbia, the commanding general of the National Guard of the District of Columbia.
