33 C.F.R. § 3.65-1 — -1 USCG Northwest District.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 3 (Coast Guard Areas, Districts, Sectors, Marine Inspection Zones, And Captain Of The Port Zones). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The District Office is in Seattle, Wash.
(b) The USCG Northwest District shall comprise Washington, Oregon, Idaho, and Montana; and the ocean area bounded by a line from California-Oregon state line westerly to latitude 40° N. longitude, 150° W., thence northeasterly to latitude 54°40′ N., longitude 140° W., thence due east to the Canadian coast.
