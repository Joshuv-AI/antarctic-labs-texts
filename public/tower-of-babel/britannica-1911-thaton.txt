THATÔN, a town and district in the Tenasserim division of
Lower Burma. The town is situated below a hill range, 10 m. from the sea. It was formerly the capital of the Talaing
kingdom and a sea-port. Pop. (1901) 14,342. The district has
an area of 5079 sq. m.; pop. (1901) 343,510, showing an increase
of 29 per cent. in the decade. It was formerly a subdivision
of Amherst district, but was formed in 1895 out of
part of that and of Shwegyin district, which has now ceased to
exist. The staple crop is rice, but a good deal of tobacco also
is grown. The railway from Pegu to Martaban, recently opened,
passes through this district and is calculated to increase its
prosperity and population.
