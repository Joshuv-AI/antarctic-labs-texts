30 C.F.R. § 56.19072 — Skips and cages in same compartment.
Citation: us:cfr:30:56.19072
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-56.19072
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 56 (Safety And Health Standards—Surface Metal And Nonmetal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When combinations of cages and skips are used in the same compartment, the cages shall be enclosed to protect personnel from flying material and the hoist speed reduced to man-speed as defined in standard 56.19061, but not to exceed 1,000 feet per minute. Muck shall not be hoisted with personnel during shift changes.
