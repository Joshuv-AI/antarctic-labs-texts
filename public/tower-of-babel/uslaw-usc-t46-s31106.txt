46 U.S.C. § 31106 — Exoneration and limitation
Citation: us:usc:46:31106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section31106
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 311 (Suits Involving Public Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States is entitled to the exemptions from and limitations of liability provided by law to an owner, charterer, operator, or agent of a vessel.
