29 C.F.R. § 408.3 — Form of initial report.
Citation: us:cfr:29:408.3
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-408.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 408 (Labor Organization Trusteeship Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On and after the effective date of this section, every labor organization required to file an initial report under § 408.2 shall file such report on United States Department of Labor Form LM-15 entitled “Trusteeship Report” in the detail required by the instructions accompanying such form and constituting a part thereof.
