15 C.F.R. § 971.207 — Antitrust information.
Citation: us:cfr:15:971.207
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-971.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 971 (Deep Seabed Mining Regulations For Commercial Recovery Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order to support the antitrust review referenced in § 971.211, the application must contain information sufficient, in the applicant's view and based on preapplication consultations pursuant to § 971.200(g), to identify the applicant and describe any significant existing market share it has with respect to the mining or marketing of the metals proposed to be recovered under the permit.
