7 U.S.C. § 5235 — Authorization of additional appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:5235
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section5235
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 83 (Agricultural Competitiveness And Trade); Subchapter II (Agricultural Trade Initiatives); Part B (Foreign Agricultural Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated for the Service, in addition to any sums otherwise authorized to be appropriated by any provision of law other than this section, $20,000,000 for each of the fiscal years 1988, 1989, and 1990 for market development activities, including—
(1)
expansion of the agricultural attache service;
(2)
expansion of international trade policy activities of the Service;
(3)
enhancement of the Service worldwide market information system;
(4)
increasing the number of trade shows and exhibitions conducted by the Service and upgrading the quality of United States representation at trade shows and exhibitions; and
(5)
developing markets for value-added beef, pork, and poultry products.
