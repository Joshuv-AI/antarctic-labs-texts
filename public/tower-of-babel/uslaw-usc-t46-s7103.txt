46 U.S.C. § 7103 — Licenses for radio officers
Citation: us:usc:46:7103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section7103
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Merchant Seamen Licenses, Certificates, And Documents); Chapter 71 (Licenses And Certificates Of Registry). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
A license as radio officer may be issued only to an applicant who has a first-class or second-class radiotelegraph operator license issued by the Federal Communications Commission.
(b)
Except as provided in section 7318 of this title, this part does not affect the status of radiotelegraph operators serving on board vessels operating only on the Great Lakes.
