22 U.S.C. § 1643g — Transfer of records
Citation: us:usc:22:1643g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1643g
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 21 (Settlement Of International Claims); Subchapter V (Claims Against Cuba And China). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State shall transfer or otherwise make available to the Commission such records and documents relating to claims authorized by this subchapter as may be required by the Commission in carrying out its functions under this subchapter.
