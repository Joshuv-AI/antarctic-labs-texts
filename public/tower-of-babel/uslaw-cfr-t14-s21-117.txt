14 C.F.R. § 21.117 — Issue of supplemental type certificates.
Citation: us:cfr:14:21.117
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-21.117
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 21 (Certification Procedures For Products And Articles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An applicant is entitled to a supplemental type certificate if the FAA finds that the applicant meets the requirements of §§ 21.113 and 21.115.
(b) A supplemental type certificate consists of—
(1) The approval by the FAA of a change in the type design of the product; and
(2) The type certificate previously issued for the product.
