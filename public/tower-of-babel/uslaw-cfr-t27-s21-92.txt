27 C.F.R. § 21.92 — Denaturants listed as U.S.P. or N.F.
Citation: us:cfr:27:21.92
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-21.92
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 21 (Formulas For Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Denaturing materials and products listed in this part as “U.S.P.” or “N.F.” shall meet the specifications set forth in the current United States Pharmacopoeia or National Formulary, or the latest volume of these publications in which the denaturants appeared as official preparations.
