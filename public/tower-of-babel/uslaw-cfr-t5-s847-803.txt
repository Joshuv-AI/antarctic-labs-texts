5 C.F.R. § 847.803 — When do employees make the election to use their NAFI service to qualify for an immediate retirement under CSRS or FERS?
Citation: us:cfr:5:847.803
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-847.803
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 847 (Elections Of Retirement Coverage By Current And Former Employees Of Nonappropriated Fund Instrumentalities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees about to retire must make their election to credit NAFI service under this subpart no later than the date of separation on which their retirement is based.
