43 C.F.R. § 17.315 — Affirmative action by recipients.
Citation: us:cfr:43:17.315
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-17.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 17 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs Of The Department Of The Interior). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Even in the absence of a finding of discrimination, a recipient may take affirmative action to overcome the effects of conditions that resulted in limited participation in the recipient's program or activity on the basis of age.
