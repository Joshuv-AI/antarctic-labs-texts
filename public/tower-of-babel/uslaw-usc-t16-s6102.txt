16 U.S.C. § 6102 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:16:6102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6102
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 80 (Neotropical Migratory Bird Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of this chapter are—
(1)
to perpetuate healthy populations of neotropical migratory birds;
(2)
to assist in the conservation of neotropical migratory birds by supporting conservation initiatives in the United States, Canada, Latin America, and the Caribbean; and
(3)
to provide financial resources and to foster international cooperation for those initiatives.
