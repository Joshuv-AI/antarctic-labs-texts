47 C.F.R. § 5.308 — Stop buzzer.
Citation: us:cfr:47:5.308
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-5.308
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 5 (Experimental Radio Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A “Stop Buzzer” point of contact must be identified and available at all times during operation of each experiment conducted under a program license. A “stop buzzer” point of contact is a person who can address interference concerns and cease all transmissions immediately if interference occurs.
