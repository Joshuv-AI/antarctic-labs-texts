21 U.S.C. § 155 — Permits for importation
Citation: us:usc:21:155
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section155
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 5 (Viruses, Serums, Toxins, Antitoxins, And Analogous Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture is authorized to issue permits for the importation into the United States of viruses, serums, toxins, and analogous products, for use in the treatment of domestic animals, which are not worthless, contaminated, dangerous, or harmful.
