10 U.S.C. § 3131 — Availability of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:10:3131
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3131
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart A (General); Chapter 207 (Budgeting And Appropriations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Funds appropriated to the Department of Defense for research and development remain available for obligation for a period of two consecutive years.
(b)
Funds appropriated to the Department of Defense for research and development may be used—
(1)
for the purposes of section 4141 of this title; and
(2)
for purposes related to research and development for which expenditures are specifically authorized in other appropriations of the Department of Defense.
