48 C.F.R. § 16.105 — 16.105 Solicitation provision.
Citation: us:cfr:48:16.105
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-16.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 16 (Types Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall complete and insert the provision at 52.216-1, Type of Contract, in a solicitation unless it is for—
(a) A fixed-price acquisition made under simplified acquisition procedures; or
(b) Information or planning purposes.
