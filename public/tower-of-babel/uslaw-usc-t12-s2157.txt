12 U.S.C. § 2157 — Bonds as investments
Citation: us:usc:12:2157
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2157
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 23 (Farm Credit System); Subchapter IV (Provisions Applicable To Two Or More Classes Of Institutions Of The System); Part A (Funding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The bonds, debentures, and other similar obligations issued under the authority of this chapter shall be lawful investments for all fiduciary and trust funds and may be accepted as security for all public deposits.
