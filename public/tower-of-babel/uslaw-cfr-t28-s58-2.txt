28 C.F.R. § 58.2 — Authorization to appoint standing trustees.
Citation: us:cfr:28:58.2
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-58.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 58 (Regulations Relating To The Bankruptcy Reform Acts Of 1978 And 1994). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each U.S. Trustee is authorized, subject to the approval of the Deputy Attorney General, or his delegate, to appoint and remove one or more standing trustees to serve in cases under chapters 12 and 13 of title 11, U.S. Code.
