42 U.S.C. § 11378 — Administrative costs
Citation: us:usc:42:11378
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11378
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 119 (Homeless Assistance); Subchapter IV (Housing Assistance); Part B (Emergency Solutions Grants Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A recipient may use up to 7.5 percent of any annual grant received under this part for administrative purposes. A recipient State shall share the amount available for administrative purposes pursuant to the preceding sentence with local governments funded by the State.
