48 C.F.R. § 7.404 — 7.404 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:7.404
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-7.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 7 (Acquisition Planning). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert a clause substantially the same as the clause in 52.207-5, Option to Purchase Equipment, in solicitations and contracts involving a rental or lease agreement with option to purchase.
