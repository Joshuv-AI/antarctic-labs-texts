5 C.F.R. § 1201.92 — Criteria for certifying interlocutory appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1201.92
Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1201.92
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1201 (Practices And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The judge will certify a ruling for review only if the record shows that:
(a) The ruling involves an important question of law or policy about which there is substantial ground for difference of opinion; and
(b) An immediate ruling will materially advance the completion of the proceeding, or the denial of an immediate ruling will cause undue harm to a party or the public.
