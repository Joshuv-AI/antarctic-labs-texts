9 C.F.R. § 201.3 — Applicability of regulations in this part.
Citation: us:cfr:9:201.3
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-201.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 201 (Administering The Packers And Stockyards Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part, when governing or affecting contracts, shall apply to any poultry growing arrangement, swine production contract, or any other livestock or poultry contract entered into, amended, altered, modified, renewed or extended after February 7, 2012.
