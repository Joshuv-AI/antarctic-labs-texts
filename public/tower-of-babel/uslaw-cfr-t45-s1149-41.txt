45 C.F.R. § 1149.41 — Are protective orders available?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1149.41
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1149.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1149 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A party or prospective witness or deponent may file a motion for a protective order with respect to discovery sought by an opposing party or with respect to the hearing, seeking to limit the availability of an individual or disclosure of evidence.
