48 C.F.R. § 2812.302 — 2812.302 Tailoring of provisions and clauses for the acquisition of commercial items.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2812.302
Creator: Chapter 28—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2812.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2812 (Acquisition Of Commercial Items). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The HCA, or designee at a level at or above the BPC, is authorized to approve the contracting officer's request for waiver for an individual contract action submitted under FAR 12.302(c). The SPE is authorized to approve the contracting officer's request for wavier for a class of contracts submitted under FAR 12.302(c).
