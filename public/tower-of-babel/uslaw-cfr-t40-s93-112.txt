40 C.F.R. § 93.112 — Criteria and procedures: Consultation.
Citation: us:cfr:40:93.112
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-93.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 93 (Determining Conformity Of Federal Actions To State Or Federal Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Conformity must be determined according to the consultation procedures in this subpart and in the applicable implementation plan, and according to the public involvement procedures established in compliance with 23 CFR part 450. Until the implementation plan revision required by § 51.390 of this chapter is fully approved by EPA, the conformity determination must be made according to § 93.105 (a)(2) and (e) and the requirements of 23 CFR part 450.
