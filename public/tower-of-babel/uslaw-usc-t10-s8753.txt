10 U.S.C. § 8753 — Tolls and fares: payment or reimbursement
Citation: us:usc:10:8753
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8753
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 873 (Procurement Of Supplies And Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Naval appropriations chargeable for transportation or travel are available for the payment or reimbursement of ferry, bridge, and similar tolls and of streetcar, bus, and similar fares.
