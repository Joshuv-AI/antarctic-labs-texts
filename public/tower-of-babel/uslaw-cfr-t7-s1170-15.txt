7 C.F.R. § 1170.15 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1170.15
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1170.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1170 (Dairy Product Mandatory Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the cease and desist order is confirmed by the Administrator, Agricultural Marketing Service, the manufacturer or person may appeal the order in the appropriate United States District Court not later than 30 days after the date of the confirmation of the order.
