2 C.F.R. § 417.137 — Who in the USDA may grant an exception to let an excluded person participate in a covered transaction?
Citation: us:cfr:2:417.137
Creator: Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-417.137
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 417 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within the USDA, a debarring official may grant an exception to let an excluded person participate in a covered transaction as provided under 2 CFR 180.135.
