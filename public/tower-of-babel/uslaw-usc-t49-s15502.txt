49 U.S.C. § 15502 — Authority for pipeline carriers to establish rates, classifications, rules, and practices
Citation: us:usc:49:15502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section15502
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Pipeline Carriers); Chapter 155 (Rates). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A pipeline carrier providing transportation or service subject to this part shall establish—
(1)
rates and classifications for transportation and service it may provide under this part; and
(2)
rules and practices on matters related to that transportation or service.
