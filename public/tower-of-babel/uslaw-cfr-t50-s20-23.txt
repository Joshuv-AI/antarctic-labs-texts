50 C.F.R. § 20.23 — Shooting and hawking hours.
Citation: us:cfr:50:20.23
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-20.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 20 (Migratory Bird Hunting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may take migratory game birds except during the hours open to shooting, which includes hawking (taking by falconry), as prescribed in subpart K of this part and 50 CFR 21.180 and 21.183 of this chapter.
