54 U.S.C. § 101116 — Corporate powers
Citation: us:usc:54:101116
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section101116
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1011 (Donations); Subchapter II (National Park Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The National Park Foundation shall have the power to enter into contracts, to execute instruments, and generally to do any and all lawful acts necessary or appropriate to its purposes.
