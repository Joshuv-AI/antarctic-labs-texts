30 C.F.R. § 14.9 — Disclosure of information.
Citation: us:cfr:30:14.9
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-14.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 14 (Requirements For The Approval Of Flame-Resistant Conveyor Belts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All proprietary information concerning product specifications and performance submitted to MSHA by the applicant will be protected.
(b) MSHA will notify the applicant or approval holder of requests for disclosure of information concerning its conveyor belts, and provide an opportunity to present its position prior to any decision on disclosure.
