46 U.S.C. § 30704 — Loading, stowage, custody, care, and delivery
Citation: us:usc:46:30704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30704
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 307 (Liability Of Water Carriers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A carrier may not insert in a bill of lading or shipping document a provision avoiding its liability for loss or damage arising from negligence or fault in loading, stowage, custody, care, or proper delivery. Any such provision is void.
