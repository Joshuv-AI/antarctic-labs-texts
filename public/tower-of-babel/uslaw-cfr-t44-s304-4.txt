44 C.F.R. § 304.4 — Allocations.
Citation: us:cfr:44:304.4
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-304.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 304 (Consolidated Grants To Insular Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For each Federal fiscal year concerned, the Administrator, FEMA, shall allocate to each participating insular area an amount not less than the sum of grants for the two programs which the Administrator, FEMA, has determined such insular area would otherwise be entitled to receive for such fiscal year.
