12 C.F.R. § 1412.7 — Filing instructions.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1412.7
Creator: Farm Credit System Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1412.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1412 (Golden Parachute And Indemnification Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests to make excess nondiscriminatory severance plan payments and permitted golden parachute payments shall be submitted in writing to the FCA and the Corporation. The request shall be in letter form and shall contain all relevant factual information as well as the reasons why such approval should be granted.
