14 C.F.R. § 1221.106 — Establishment of the NASA Flag.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1221.106
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1221.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1221 (The Nasa Seal And Other Devices, And The Congressional Space Medal Of Honor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The NASA Flags for interior and exterior use were created by the NASA Administrator in January 1960. Complete design, size, and color of the NASA interior and exterior flags for manufacturing purposes are detailed in U.S. Army QMG Drawing 5-1-269, revision September 14, 1960. The NASA Flags shall be used as set forth in § 1221.113.
