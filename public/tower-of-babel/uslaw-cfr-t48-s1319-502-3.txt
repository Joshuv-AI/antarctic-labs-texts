48 C.F.R. § 1319.502-3 — 1319.502-3 Partial set-asides.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1319.502-3
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1319.502-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1319 (Small Business Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A partial set-aside shall not be made if there is a reasonable expectation that only two capable concerns (one large and one small) will respond with offers unless the set-aside is authorized by the designee set forth in CAM 1301.70.
