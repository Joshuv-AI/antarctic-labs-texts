16 C.F.R. § 1105.3 — A more satisfactory standard.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1105.3
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1105.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1105 (Contributions To Costs Of Participants In Development Of Consumer Product Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In considering whether a contribution is likely to result in a more satisfactory standard, the Commission shall consider:
(a) The need for representation of one or more particular interests, expertise, or points of view in the development proceeding; and
(b) The extent to which particular interests, points of view, or expertise can reasonably be expected to be represented if the Commission does not provide any financial contribution.
