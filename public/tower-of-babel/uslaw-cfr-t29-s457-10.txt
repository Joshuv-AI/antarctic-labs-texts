29 C.F.R. § 457.10 — CSRA; FSA; CAA; LMRDA.

Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 457 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CSRA means the Civil Service Reform Act of 1978; FSA means the Foreign Service Act of 1980; CAA means the Congressional Accountability Act of 1995; LMRDA means the Labor-Management Reporting and Disclosure Act of 1959, as amended.
