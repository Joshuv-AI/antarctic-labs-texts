15 U.S.C. § 78bbb — Application of Securities Exchange Act of 1934
Citation: us:usc:15:78bbb
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section78bbb
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2B–1 (Securities Investor Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, the provisions of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 [15 U.S.C. 78a et seq.] (hereinafter referred to as the “1934 Act”) apply as if this chapter constituted an amendment to, and was included as a section of, such Act.
