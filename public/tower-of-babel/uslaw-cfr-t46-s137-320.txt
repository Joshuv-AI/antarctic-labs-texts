46 C.F.R. § 137.320 — Vessels holding a valid load line certificate.
Citation: us:cfr:46:137.320
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-137.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 137 (Vessel Compliance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A drydock and internal structural examination performed for a towing vessel to maintain a valid load line certificate issued in accordance with subchapter E of this chapter would count as an examination required under § 137.300.
