16 U.S.C. § 7304 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:7304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section7304
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 92 (Forest Landscape Restoration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary and the Secretary of the Interior such sums as are necessary to carry out this chapter.
