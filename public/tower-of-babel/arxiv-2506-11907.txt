Lexicographical ordering by spectral moments of bicyclic hypergraphs
Hong Zhou, Changjiang Bu

Abstract
For bicyclic hypergraphs, ordering by spectral moment ( S S -order) is investigated in this paper. We give the first and last hypergraphs in an S S -order of linear bicyclic uniform hypergraphs with given girth and number of edges.
