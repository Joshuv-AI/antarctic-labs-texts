50 C.F.R. § 38.8 — Consistency with Federal law.
Citation: us:cfr:50:38.8
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-38.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 38 (Midway Atoll National Wildlife Refuge). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any provisions of the laws of the State of Hawaii, as they now appear or as they may be amended or recodified, which are adopted by this part will apply only to the extent that they are not in conflict with any applicable Federal law or regulation.
