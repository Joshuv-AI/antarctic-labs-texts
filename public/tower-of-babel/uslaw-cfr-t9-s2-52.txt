9 C.F.R. § 2.52 — How to obtain tags.
Citation: us:cfr:9:2.52
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-2.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 2 (Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Dealers or exhibitors may obtain, at their own expense, official tags from commercial tag manufacturers. 4 At the time the dealer or exhibitor is issued a license or is registered, the Department will assign identification letters and numbers and inform them of the identification letters and numbers to be used on the official tags.
