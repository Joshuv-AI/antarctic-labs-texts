29 C.F.R. § 2204.407 — Commission review.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2204.407
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2204.407
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2204 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Proceedings Before The Occupational Safety And Health Review Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Either the applicant or the Secretary may seek review of the judge's decision on the fee application, and the Commission may grant such a petition for review or direct review of the decision on the Commission's own initiative. Review by the Commission shall be in accordance with §§ 2200.91 and 2200.92 of this chapter.
