33 C.F.R. § 238.8 — Other participation.
Citation: us:cfr:33:238.8
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-238.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 238 (Water Resources Policies And Authorities: Flood Damage Reduction Measures In Urban Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to providing flood damage reduction works in urban areas, the Corps may provide related services to State and local governments on a reimbursable basis. Under Title III of the Inter-governmental Cooperation Act of 1968, specialized or technical services for which the Corps has specific expertise may be furnished only when such services cannot be procured reasonably and expeditiously from private firms (see ER 1140-2-303).
