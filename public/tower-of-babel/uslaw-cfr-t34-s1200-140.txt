34 C.F.R. § 1200.140 — Employment.
Citation: us:cfr:34:1200.140
Creator: National Council On Disability
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-1200.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 1200 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The National Council On Disability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No qualified individual with handicaps shall, on the basis of handicap, be subjected to discrimination in employment under any program or activity conducted by the agency. The definitions, requirements, and procedures of section 501 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 (29 U.S.C. 791), as established by the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission in 29 CFR part 1614, shall apply to employment in federally conducted programs or activities.
