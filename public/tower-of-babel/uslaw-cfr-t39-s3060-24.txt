39 C.F.R. § 3060.24 — Competitive products fund report.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3060.24
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3060.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3060 (Accounting Practices And Tax Rules For The Theoretical Competitive Products Enterprise). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 90 days of the close of each fiscal year the Postal Service will provide the most recent report of the activity of the Competitive Products Fund as provided to the Secretary of the Treasury under 39 U.S.C. 2011(i)(1).
