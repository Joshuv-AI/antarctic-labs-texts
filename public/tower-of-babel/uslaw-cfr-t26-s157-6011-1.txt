26 C.F.R. § 157.6011-1 — -1 General requirement of return, statement, or list.
Citation: us:cfr:26:157.6011-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-157.6011-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 157 (Excise Tax On Structured Settlement Factoring Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every person liable for tax under section 5891 must file a return with respect to the tax in accordance with the forms and instructions provided by the Internal Revenue Service.
