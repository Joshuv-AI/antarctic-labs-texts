46 U.S.C. § 11503 — Duties of consular officers related to insubordination
Citation: us:usc:46:11503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section11503
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Merchant Seamen Protection And Relief); Chapter 115 (Offenses And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
A consular officer shall use every means to discountenance insubordination on vessels of the United States, including employing the aid of local authorities.
(b)
When a seaman is accused of insubordination, a consular officer shall inquire into the facts and proceed as provided in section 11106 of this title. If the consular officer discharges the seaman, the officer shall endorse the agreement required by this part and enter in the vessel’s official logbook the cause and particulars of the discharge.
