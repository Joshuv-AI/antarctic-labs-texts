10 C.F.R. § 719.5 — What contracts are not covered by this part?
Citation: us:cfr:10:719.5
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-719.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 719 (Contractor Legal Management Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part does not cover any contract under which the Department is not responsible for directly reimbursing the contractor for legal costs, such as fixed price contracts.
