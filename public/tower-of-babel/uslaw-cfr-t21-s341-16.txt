21 C.F.R. § 341.16 — Bronchodilator active ingredients.
Citation: us:cfr:21:341.16
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-341.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 341 (Cold, Cough, Allergy, Bronchodilator, And Antiasthmatic Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The active ingredients of the product consist of any of the following when used within the dosage limits established for each ingredient:
(a) Ephedrine.
(b) Ephedrine hydrochloride.
(c) Ephedrine sulfate.
(d) Epinephrine.
(e) Epinephrine bitartrate.
(f) Racephedrine hydrochloride.
(g) Racepinephrine hydrochloride.
