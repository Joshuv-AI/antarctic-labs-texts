33 U.S.C. § 3904 — Eligible entities
Citation: us:usc:33:3904
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3904
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 52 (Water Infrastructure Finance And Innovation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The following entities are eligible to receive assistance under this chapter:
(1)
A corporation.
(2)
A partnership.
(3)
A joint venture.
(4)
A trust.
(5)
A Federal, State, or local governmental entity, agency, or instrumentality.
(6)
A tribal government or consortium of tribal governments.
(7)
A State infrastructure financing authority.
