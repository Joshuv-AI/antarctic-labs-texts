15 U.S.C. § 80b–18c — Rule of construction relating to the Commodities Exchange Act
Citation: us:usc:15:80b–18c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section80b–18c
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2D (Investment Companies And Advisers); Subchapter II (Investment Advisers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall relieve any person of any obligation or duty, or affect the availability of any right or remedy available to the Commodity Futures Trading Commission or any private party, arising under the Commodity Exchange Act (7 U.S.C. 1 et seq.) governing commodity pools, commodity pool operators, or commodity trading advisors.
