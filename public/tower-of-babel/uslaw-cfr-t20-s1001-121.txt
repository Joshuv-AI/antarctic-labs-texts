20 C.F.R. § 1001.121 — Performance standard on facilities and support for Veterans' Employment and Training Service (VETS) staff.
Citation: us:cfr:20:1001.121
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Veterans' Employment And Training Service, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-1001.121
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 1001 (Services For Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each State agency shall provide adequate and appropriate facilities and administrative support such as office space, furniture, telephone, equipment, and supplies to VETS staff.
