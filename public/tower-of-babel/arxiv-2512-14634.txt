We employ the following notation:

Let

Definition 2.1
.

Put

\mu_{H}:=\inf\left\{\lambda\in\mathbb{R}_{>0}\mid\text{ the $\mathbb{R}$-divisor $K_{X}+\lambda H$ is pseudo-effective}\right\}.

The number
\mu_{H}
is called Fujita invariant of
(X,H)
. The smallest extremal face
\Delta_{H}
of the Mori cone
\overline{\mathbb{NE}(X)}
that contains

is called the Fujita face of

. The Fujita rank of

is defined by

. Note that

if and only if

.

Let
\phi_{H}X\rightarrow Y
be the contraction given by the
\Delta_{H}
. Then either
\phi_{H}
is a birational morphism or a conic bundle with
Y\cong\mathbb{P}^{1}
.
In the former case, the
\mathbb{R}
-divisor

is said to be of type
B(r_{H})
and in the latter case it is said
to be of type
C(r_{H})
.

Suppose that

is of type
B(r_{H})
. Then

K_{X}+\mu_{H}H\equiv\sum\limits_{i=1}^{r_{H}}a_{i}E_{i},

where
E_{1},\ldots,E_{r_{H}}
are curves contained in
\Delta_{H}
and
a_{1},\ldots,a_{r_{H}}
are positive real numbers such that
a_{i}<1
for every

.

Suppose that
H
is of type
C(r_{H})
. Note that
r_{H}=9-d
. There are a

-curve

and
(8-d)
disjoint curves

, each of which is contained in a distinct fiber of

, such that

K_{X}+\mu_{H}H\equiv aB+\sum\limits_{i=1}^{8-d}a_{i}E_{i},

for some positive real number

and non-negative real numbers
a_{1},\ldots,a_{8-d}<1
.
