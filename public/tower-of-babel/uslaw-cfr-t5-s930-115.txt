5 C.F.R. § 930.115 — Requests for waiver of requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:5:930.115
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-930.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 930 (Programs For Specific Positions And Examinations (Miscellaneous)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies may request authority from OPM to waive requirements in this subpart. OPM may grant exceptions or waivers when it finds these waivers or exceptions are in the interest of good administration and meet the objectives of this program.
