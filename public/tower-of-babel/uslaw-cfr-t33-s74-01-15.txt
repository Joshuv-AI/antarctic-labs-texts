33 C.F.R. § 74.01-15 — -15 Charges for placement of temporary aids.
Citation: us:cfr:33:74.01-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-74.01-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 74 (Charges For Coast Guard Aids To Navigation Work). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Charges for placement of temporary aids will be reimbursable and in accordance with Subpart 74.20 of this part. Where the placement of temporary aids other than those specified is made, a reasonable equivalence will be determined, and charges made accordingly.
