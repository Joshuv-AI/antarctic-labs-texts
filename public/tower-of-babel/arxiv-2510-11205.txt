Comparison Theorems and the Intermediate Ricci Curvature Assumption
Yujie Wu

Abstract
. We explore the notion of m m -intermediate Ricci curvature assumption introduced by Brendle-Hirsch-Johne further. If a manifold has non-negative m m -intermediate Ricci curvature and stable weighted slicing of order m − 1 m-1 , Σ 0 ⊃ … ⊃ Σ m − 1 \Sigma_{0}\supset...\supset\Sigma_{m-1} , then the last slice Σ m − 1 \Sigma_{m-1} has almost non-negative Ricci curvature in the spectral sense. We prove comparison theorems on the diameter and in-radius bound for stable weighted (respectively free boundary) slicing in such manifolds (respectively with mean convex boundary).



1.
Introduction

In
[
BHJ24
]
, Brendle-Hirsch-Johne introduced the notion of “
m
-intermediate curvature” on a Riemannian manifold
M^{n}
(
1\leq m\leq n
), where
1
-intermediate curvature is equal to Ricci curvature, and
n
-intermediate Ricci curvature is equal to a multiple of scalar curvature.

For a Riemannian manifold
X
, given
p\in X
and
e_{p},e_{q}\in T_{p}X
we denote the sectional curvature as
K_{X}(e_{p},e_{q}):=\text{Rm}_{X}(e_{p},e_{q},e_{q},e_{p})
where
\text{Rm}(\cdot,\cdot,\cdot,\cdot)
is the Riemann curvature tensor with the convention

\text{Rm}(e_{p},e_{q},e_{q},e_{p})=\langle\nabla_{e_{p}}\nabla_{e_{q}}e_{q}-\nabla_{e_{q}}\nabla_{e_{p}}e_{q}-\nabla_{[e_{p},e_{q}]}e_{q},e_{p}\rangle.

Definition 1.1

(Brendle-Hirsh-Johne,
[
BHJ24
]
)
.

Suppose
(X^{n},g)
is a Riemannian manifold (possibly with boundary). Given a point
p\in X
and orthonormal vectors
\{e_{1},...,e_{m}\}
of
T_{p}X
, we define the
m
-intermediate Ricci curvature,

C_{m}(e_{1},...,e_{m}):=\sum_{p=1}^{m}\sum_{q=p+1}^{n}K_{X}(e_{p},e_{q})

We say that
(X,g)
has non-negative
m
-intermediate curvature at
p\in X
if
C_{m}(e_{1},...,e_{m})\geq 0
for any choice of orthonormal vectors
\{e_{1},...,e_{m}\}\subset T_{p}X
, and we say the manifold
X
has non-negative
m
-intermediate curvature if it has non-negative
m
-intermediate curvature at any point
p\in X
.

Remark 1.2
.

Having
C_{2}\geq 0
is the same as having non-negative Bi-Ricci curvature as introduced by Shen and Ye in
[
SY96
]
.

It’s a classical theorem of Bonnet and Meyers that
M^{m}\times\mathbb{S}^{1}
does not admit a metric of positive Ricci curvature for any closed manifold
M
; and Geroch’s conjecture states that the torus
\mathbb{T}^{n}
has no complete metric of positive scalar curvature, this was resolved by Schoen and Yau using minimal hypersurfaces for
n\leq 7

[
SY79
]
and by Gromov and Lawson for all dimensions using spinors
[
GL83
]
. Furthermore, Chodosh and Li generalized this conjecture in proving that aspherical manifolds of dimension
4
or
5
also do not admit a metric of positive scalar curvature
[
CL24
]
.
Brendle-Hirsch-Johne (
[
BHJ24
]
) proved that this can be generalized to an obstruction of
m
-intermediate curvature for manifolds of the type
M^{n-m}\times\mathbb{T}^{m}
.

Theorem 1.3

(Brendle-Hirsh-Johne,
[
BHJ24
]
)
.

Let
(N^{n},g)
be a closed Riemannian manifold,
2\leq m\leq n-1,m^{2}-2-n(m-2)\geq 0
and
N
has
C_{m}>0
, then
N
has no stable weighted slicing of order
m
(such that the last slice is compact). If
n\leq 7

(in this case we always have
m^{2}-2-n(m-2)\geq 0
), suppose
(N^{n},g)
is a closed orientable Riemannian manifold with a degree-nonzero map
F:N^{n}\rightarrow M^{n-m}\times\mathbb{T}^{m}
for some closed orientable manifold
M
, then
N
has a stable weighted slicing of order
m
; in particular, any such manifold
N
does not admit a metric of positive
m
-intermediate curvature.

We will recall the definition of “stable weighted slicing of order
m
” in section
2
.

Theorem
1.3
has been extended by Chen (
[
Che24
]
) to manifolds of the type
M^{n-m}\times\mathbb{T}^{m}\times X
for a closed manifold
M
and an arbitrary manifold
X
when
3\leq n\leq 5
, or when
n-m\leq 2,6\leq n\leq 7
.
On the other hand, manifolds of the type
M^{n-m}\times\mathbb{T}^{m}
can have non-negative
m
-intermediate curvature. In this case, Chu-Kwong-Lee (
[
CKL25
]
) proved that when
n\leq 5
and
\mathcal{C}_{m}\geq 0
, then
M^{n-m}\times\mathbb{T}^{m}
must be isometrically covered by
X^{n-m}\times\mathbb{R}^{m}
for some compact
X
with
\Ric_{X}\geq 0
; Xu (
[
Xu25
]
) later extended this to the case when
n=6
and provided counterexamples to Theorem
1.3
when
m^{2}-2-n(m-2)<0
.

In this paper we look at manifolds of the type
N^{n}=M^{k+1}\times\mathbb{T}^{m-1}
with
n=k+m
, where we can find stable minimal slicing of order
m-1
, and if
\mathcal{C}_{m}>0
, then the last slice
\Sigma_{m-1}
have almost positive Ricci curvature in the spectral sense, that is
\Sigma^{k+1}:=\Sigma_{m-1}
is a manifold with a smooth positive function
f
, such that for some
\gamma>0,\alpha>0
,

\displaystyle-\gamma\Delta_{\Sigma}f+\Ric_{\Sigma}f\geq-\alpha|\nabla_{\Sigma}\log f|^{2}.

For suitable choices of
\gamma,\alpha,k,m
, we can prove comparison theorems for such slicings, generalizing the results of Shen-Ye on Bi-Ricci curvature.

Theorem 1.4

(Shen-Ye,
[
SY96
]
)
.

Assume that
\mathcal{C}_{2}\geq k>0
and
m\leq 5
, then any closed stable minimal hypersurface
S^{m-1}
in
M^{m}
has
\text{diam}(S)\leq\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{c(m)k}}
, where
c(3)=\frac{3}{4},c(4)=\frac{1}{2},c(5)=\frac{1}{4}
.

Generalization of the result of Shen-Ye has been applied to the slicing of
\mu
-bubbles, in obtaining obstructions to positive intermediate curvature assumptions in aspherical manifolds under suitable dimensional restrictions by Mazurowski-Wang-Yao (
[
MWY25
]
). The role of the dimensional restriction to such obstructions has been studied by Xu, in showing that there is a complete metric on
\mathbb{S}^{4}\times\mathbb{R}^{2}
with uniformly positive
\mathcal{C}_{2}
and infinite Urysohn width (
[
Xu25
]
). Below we proved a sharp control in diameter for such slicing.

Theorem 1.5

(Bonnet-Meyers Theorem for the
(m-1)
-th Slice)
.

Take a complete Riemannian manifold
N^{n}
with positive intermediate curvature
C_{m}\geq k_{0}>0
, let
n<m+2+\frac{2}{m-1}
(this implies
m^{2}-2-n(m-2)\geq 0
). If we have stable minimal slicing of order
m-1
for
\max\{1,n-6\}\leq m\leq n-1
,
N=\Sigma_{0}\supset\Sigma_{1}\supset\Sigma_{2}...\supset\Sigma_{m-1}
, then
\text{diam}(\Sigma_{m-1})\leq\frac{\pi}{\sqrt{k_{0}C_{0}}}
, where

C_{0}:=-\frac{n(m-1)-m(m+1)}{2(m^{2}-2-n(m-2))}>0,\quad\text{when }n<m+2+\frac{2}{m-1}.

The constant for
m=2
is the same constant as obtained in
[
SY96
]
. The bound is sharp and obtained by the embedding of
\mathbb{S}^{3}\hookrightarrow\mathbb{T}^{m-1}\times\mathbb{S}^{3}
, with
C_{m}\geq 2
and
C_{0}=\frac{1}{2}
.

Remark 1.6
.

We note that the compactness of
\Sigma_{m-1}
is not assumed a priori.

Assuming
C_{m}\geq 0
and
m
-convexity, we have the following theorem for stable free boundary weighted slicing of order
m
.

Let
(X,\partial X,g)
be a Riemannian manifold with boundary, and we denote the second fundamental form
\mathrm{I\!I}_{\partial X}(Y,Z)=-\langle\nabla_{Y}Z,\nu_{\partial X}\rangle
for
\nu_{\partial X}
the outward pointing unit normal along
\partial X
and
Y,Z
vector fields along
\partial X
.

Definition 1.7

(
m
-convexity assumption)
.

Take
(X^{n},\partial X,g)
a Riemannian manifold with boundary,
1\leq m\leq n-1
, we say
X
is (strictly)
m
-convex at a point
p\in X
if for any choice of orthonormal vectors
\{e_{1},...,e_{m}\}\subset T_{p}{\partial X}
,
\sum_{i=1}^{m}\mathrm{I\!I}_{\partial X}(e_{i},e_{i})>0
. We denote
\mathrm{I\!I}^{\partial X}_{m}\geq k_{0}>0
if
\sum_{i=1}^{m}\mathrm{I\!I}_{\partial X}(e_{i},e_{i})>k_{0}
for any choice of orthonormal vectors at any point on
\partial X
and say
X
is uniformly
m
-convex.

Theorem 1.8

(Stable Weighted Free Boundary Slicing)
.

Let
(X^{n},\partial X,g)
be a Riemannian manifold with boundary, assume
C_{m}\geq 0
and
\mathrm{I\!I}^{\partial X}_{m}>0
, if
m^{2}-2-n(m-2)\geq 0
then
X
has no stable free boundary weighted slicing of order
m
such that the last slice
\Sigma_{m}
is compact.

Corollary 1.9
.

Let
Y
be a compact orientable smooth manifold, then any smooth orientable Riemannian manifold
(N^{n},\partial N)
that maps to
(Y,\partial Y)\times\mathbb{T}^{m}
with non-zero degree (
\partial N
is mapped to
\partial Y\times\mathbb{T}^{m}
), has a stable free boundary weighted slicing of order
m
when
n\leq 7
. If
m^{2}-2-n(m-2)\geq 0
, the product manifold
(Y,\partial Y)\times\mathbb{T}^{m}
has no metric of non-negative intermediate curvature and uniformly
m
-convex boundary.

For stable weighted free boundary slicing we have the following comparison result for the in-radius (see
[
Gro19
]
).

Theorem 1.10

(In-Radius Bound)
.

If a manifold
X^{n}
has intermediate curvature
C_{m}\geq 0
and uniformly positive mean curvature
H_{\partial X}\geq H_{0}>0
. Let let
n<m+2+\frac{2}{m-1}
(this implies
m^{2}-2-n(m-2)\geq 0
), if we have stable minimal free boundary slicing of order
m-1
for
\max\{1,n-6\}\leq m\leq n-1
,
X=\Sigma_{0}\supset\Sigma_{1}...\supset\Sigma_{m-1}
, and the last slice has compact
\partial\Sigma_{m-1}
, then the in-radius of
\Sigma_{m-1}
is uniformly bounded,
\text{Rad}_{in}(\Sigma_{m-1}):=\sup_{x\in\Sigma_{m-1}}d_{\Sigma_{m-1}}(x,\partial\Sigma_{m-1})\leq\frac{1}{C_{0}H_{0}}
, for the same
C_{0}
defined in Theorem
1.5
.

Note that
\mathbb{B}^{3}\times\mathbb{T}^{m-1}
has
C_{0}=\frac{1}{2}
,
\mathcal{C}_{m}\geq 0,H_{0}=2
and every point in
\mathbb{B}^{3}\times\{z\}
for
z\in\mathbb{T}^{m-1}
is at most distance
1
from the boundary.

Preliminary

We first recall the notion of stable weighted slicing introduced in
[
BHJ24
]
, an idea that appeared in Schoen and Yau’s resolution of Geroch conjecture in
[

Definition 2.1

(Stable weighted slicing of order
m
,
[
BHJ24
]
)
.

Let
1\leq m\leq n-1
and
(N^{n},g)
a Riemannian manifold. A stable weighted slicing of order
m
consists of a collection of two-sided and smooth immersions
\Sigma_{k}\hookrightarrow\Sigma_{k-1},1\leq k\leq m
and a collection of positive functions
\rho_{k}\in C^{\infty}(\Sigma_{k})
such that,

•

\Sigma_{0}=N,\rho_{0}=1
;

•

For each
1\leq k\leq m
,
\Sigma_{k}
is a two-sided hypersurface in
\Sigma_{k-1}
and a stable critical point to the weighted area functional among hypersurfaces in
\Sigma_{k-1}

\mathcal{H}_{k}(\Sigma):=\int_{\Sigma}\rho_{k-1}d\mathcal{H}^{n-k}

•

For
1\leq k\leq m
,
\rho_{k}=v_{k}\cdot\rho_{k-1}\rvert_{\Sigma_{k}}
for
v_{k}
a positive first eigenfunction of the stability operator associated to
\mathcal{H}^{k}
.

We have the following corresponding defintion for free boundary slicing.

Definition 2.2

(Stable free boundary weighted slicing of order
m
)
.

Let
1\leq m\leq n-1
and
(X^{n},\partial X,g)
a Riemannian manifold with boundary. A stable free boundary weighted slicing of order
m
consists of a collection of two-sided and smooth immersions
(\Sigma_{k},\partial\Sigma_{k})\hookrightarrow(\Sigma_{k-1},\partial\Sigma_{k-1}),1\leq k\leq m

\rho_{k}\in C^{\infty}(\Sigma_{k})
such that,

•

\Sigma_{0}=X,\rho_{0}=1

•

For each
1\leq k\leq m
,
\Sigma_{k}
is a two-sided free boundary hypersurface in
\Sigma_{k-1}
and a stable critical point to the weighted area functional among variations whose restriction to
\partial\Sigma_{k-1}
lies in

(variations that maps
\partial\Sigma_{k-1}
to
\partial\Sigma_{k-1}
),

\mathcal{H}_{k}(\Sigma):=\int_{\Sigma}\rho_{k-1}d\mathcal{H}^{n-k}

•

For
1\leq k\leq m
,
\rho_{k}=v_{k}\cdot\rho_{k-1}\rvert_{\Sigma_{k}}

v_{k}

\mathcal{H}^{k}

5.

Definition 5.1

(Spectral Ricci Curvature)
.

For a constant
\gamma\geq 0
, we say that a complete Riemannian manifold
M
has almost non-negative Ricci curvature in the spectral sense if,
there is
\alpha^{\prime}\in(0,1)
and

u>0
and

-\gamma\Delta u+\Ric\cdot u=\lambda_{1}u-\alpha u|\nabla\log u|^{2},

\gamma>0,\alpha\geq 0,\lambda_{1}\geq 0
and
\lambda_{1}=0

M
is non-compact.

Here we denote
\Ric(x)
as
\Ric_{M}(x):=\inf_{v\in T_{x}M,g(v,v)=1}\Ric(v,v)\in\text{Lip}_{\text{loc}}(M)
.

(Spectral Mean Convexity)
.

For a constant
\gamma\geq 0
, we say that a complete Riemannian manifold
M

H_{0}\geq 0

\displaystyle\gamma\nabla_{\nu_{\partial M}}\log u+H_{\partial M}\geq H_{0}.
