45 C.F.R. § 502.3 — Effect of nonpublication.
Citation: us:cfr:45:502.3
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-502.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 502 (Public Information-Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except to the extent that a person has actual and timely notice of the terms thereof, no person will in any manner be required to resort to, or be adversely affected by, any matter required to be published in the Federal Register and not so published.
