26 U.S.C. § 2045 — Prior interests
Citation: us:usc:26:2045
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section2045
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 11 (Estate Tax); Subchapter A (Estates Of Citizens Or Residents); Part III (Gross Estate). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise specifically provided by law, sections 2034 to 2042, inclusive, shall apply to the transfers, trusts, estates, interests, rights, powers, and relinquishment of powers, as severally enumerated and described therein, whenever made, created, arising, existing, exercised, or relinquished.
