42 U.S.C. § 11714 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:42:11714
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11714
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 122 (Native Hawaiian Health Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter, or the application of any such provision to any person or circumstances is held to be invalid, the remainder of this chapter, and the application of such provision or amendment to persons or circumstances other than those to which it is held invalid, shall not be affected thereby.
