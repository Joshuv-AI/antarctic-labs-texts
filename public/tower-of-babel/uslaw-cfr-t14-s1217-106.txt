14 C.F.R. § 1217.106 — Articles brought into the United States by NASA from space.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1217.106
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1217.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1217 (Duty-Free Entry Of Space Articles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to U.S. note 1 subchapter VIII of chapter 98, HTSUS, articles brought into the customs territory of the United States by NASA from space shall not be considered an importation, and no certification or entry of such materials through U.S. Customs shall be required. This provision is applicable to articles brought to the U.S. from space whether or not the articles were launched into space aboard a NASA vehicle.
