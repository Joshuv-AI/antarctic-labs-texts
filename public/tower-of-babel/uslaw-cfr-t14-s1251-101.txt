14 C.F.R. § 1251.101 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1251.101
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1251.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1251 (Nondiscrimination On Basis Of Disability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to each recipient of Federal financial assistance from the National Aeronautics and Space Administration and to each program or activity that receives such assistance.
