11 C.F.R. § 9405.12 — Waiver or reduction of charges.
Citation: us:cfr:11:9405.12
Creator: Election Assistance Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-9405.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 9405 (Procedures For Disclosure Of Records Under The Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Records responsive to a request will be furnished without charge when the Chief FOIA Officer determines, based on all available information, that disclosure of the requested information is in the public interest because it is likely to contribute significantly to public understanding of the operations or activities of the government and is not primarily in the commercial interest of the requester.
