40 C.F.R. § 62.14540a — a What must I do if I plan to permanently close my CISWI?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.14540a
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.14540a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you plan to permanently close your CISWI rather than comply with the Federal plan, you must submit a legally binding closure agreement, including the date of closure, to the Administrator by January 10, 2025, for sources that will not operate on or after the compliance date under this subpart.
