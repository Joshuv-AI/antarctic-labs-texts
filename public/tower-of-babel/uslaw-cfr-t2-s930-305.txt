2 C.F.R. § 930.305 — Government approval of changes in plans.
Citation: us:cfr:2:930.305
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-930.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 930 (Other Transaction Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Department of Energy must approve any changes in project plans that may result in a need for additional Federal funding to be provided to the other transaction agreement.
