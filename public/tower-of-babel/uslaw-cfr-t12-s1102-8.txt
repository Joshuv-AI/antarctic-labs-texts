12 C.F.R. § 1102.8 — Waiver extension.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1102.8
Creator: Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1102.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1102 (Appraiser Regulation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ASC may initiate an extension of temporary waiver relief and shall follow §§ 1102.6, 1102.7 and 1102.9. A State Appraisal Agency also may seek an extension of temporary waiver relief by forwarding an additional written Request for Temporary Waiver to the ASC. A request for an extension from a State Appraisal Agency shall be subject to all the requirements of this subpart.
