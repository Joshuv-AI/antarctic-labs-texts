FLRW-Cosmology in Scalar-Vector-Tensor Theories of Gravity
Metin Gürses, and Yaghoub Heydarzade

Abstract
We generalize our previous theorem for FLRW spacetime within the framework of generic metric gravity theories. In our earlier work, we demonstrated that, in the absence of matter fields, the field equations of any generic gravity theory reduce to the Einstein field equations with an effective perfect fluid source. In the present study, we extend this analysis by incorporating scalar and vector fields into a generic gravity theory and show that the resulting field equations remain equivalent to the Einstein field equations with a perfect fluid distribution. We further verify our findings by explicitly examining recently developed Einstein-scalar and Einstein-Proca field theories.

3
Conclusion

In this work, we have extended our previous theorem for FLRW spacetimes in generic metric gravity theories based on Riemannian geometry to a broader class of modified gravitational models, including scalar-tensor and vector-tensor theories. We demonstrated that, independently of the detailed form of the underlying Lagrangian, the symmetry of the FLRW spacetime enforces the corresponding field equations to take the form of an effective perfect fluid at the background level.

To illustrate the scope of this result, we analyzed the regularized scalar-tensor and vector-tensor four-dimensional Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet theories. In both cases, the modified field equations in an FLRW background reduce to a perfect-fluid form, with effective energy density and pressure governed by the background evolution of the scalar or vector degrees of freedom. These examples confirm that even modified gravity theories with higher-curvature origins and additional dynamical fields obey the same structural constraints imposed by cosmological symmetry.
