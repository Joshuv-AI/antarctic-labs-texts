Office of the Managing Director: Chief Data Officer; Fiscal Year 
2026-2030 Strategic Plan; Correction

AGENCY: National Transportation Safety Board.

ACTION: Notice; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The NTSB published a document in the Federal Register of June 
10, 2026, concerning request for comments on the draft FY 2026-2030 
Strategic Plan. The document contained an incorrect URL to the draft 
plan.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Warren Randolph, Chief Data Officer, 
[email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 

Correction

    In the Federal Register of June 10, 2026, in FR Doc.2026-11627 on 
page 35275 in the third column in the Summary section, correct the URL 
to the draft plan. The draft strategic plan is available at the 
following corrected URL: https://www.ntsb.gov/about/reports/Documents/DRAFT-FY%202026-2030-Strategic-Plan.pdf.

DATES: Comments must be received within 14 days from the posting of 
this notice.

ADDRESSES: Written comments may be submitted electronically or via U.S. 
mail. Respondents are encouraged to submit comments electronically to 
ensure timely receipt. Please include your name, title, organization, 
postal address, telephone number, and email address.
