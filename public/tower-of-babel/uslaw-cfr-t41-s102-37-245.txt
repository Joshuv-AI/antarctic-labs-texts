41 C.F.R. § 102-37.245 — -37.245 SASP audits.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.245
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.245
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For each year in which a SASP receives $1,000,000 or more a year in surplus property or other Federal assistance, it must be audited in accordance with 2 CFR part 200. GSA's donation program should be identified by Catalog of Federal Domestic Assistance number 39.003 when completing the required schedule of Federal assistance.
