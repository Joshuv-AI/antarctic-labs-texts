43 C.F.R. § 2742.5 — Consistency with other laws.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2742.5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2742.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2740 (Recreation And Public Purposes Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provision of the Recreation and Public Purposes Act prohibiting disposal for any use authorized under any other law does not apply to conveyances under this subpart.
