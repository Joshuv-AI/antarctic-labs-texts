43 C.F.R. § 3162.3-3 — -3 Other lease operations.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3162.3-3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3162.3-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3160 (Onshore Oil And Gas Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prior to commencing any operation on the leasehold which will result in additional surface disturbance, other than those authorized under § 3162.3-1 or § 3162.3-2, the operator shall submit a proposal on Form 3160-5 to the authorized officer for approval. The proposal shall include a surface use plan of operations.
