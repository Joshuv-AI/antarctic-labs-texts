21 C.F.R. § 225.165 — Equipment cleanout procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:21:225.165
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-225.165
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 225 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice For Medicated Feeds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Adequate procedures shall be established and used for all equipment used in the production and distribution of medicated feeds to avoid unsafe contamination of medicated and nonmedicated feeds.
