33 C.F.R. § 20.1309 — Admissibility of respondents' criminal records and records with the Coast Guard before entry of findings and conclusions.
Citation: us:cfr:33:20.1309
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-20.1309
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 20 (Rules Of Practice, Procedure, And Evidence For Formal Administrative Proceedings Of The Coast Guard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The prior disciplinary record of the respondent is admissible when offered by him or her.
(b) The prior disciplinary record of the respondent is admissible when offered by the Coast Guard representative to impeach the credibility of evidence offered by the respondent.
(c) The use of a judgment of conviction is permissible on the terms prescribed by § 20.1307.
