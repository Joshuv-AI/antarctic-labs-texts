Seidel product formula in equivariant quantum K K -theory of flag varieties
Takeshi Ikeda, Takafumi Kouno, Satoshi Naito

Abstract
. We prove a Seidel product formula for the torus-equivariant quantum K K -theory of a generalized flag variety G / P . G/P. This is a natural generalization of the corresponding results by Buch, Chaput, and Perrin for the cominuscule flag varieties. Our proof is based on the K K -theoretic Peterson isomorphism, due to Kato. We also use a version of the K K -theoretic nil-Hecke algebra associated with the extended affine Weyl group, which was studied by Ikeda, Shimozono, and Yamaguchi.
