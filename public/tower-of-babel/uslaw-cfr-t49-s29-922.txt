49 C.F.R. § 29.922 — May a party appeal a Civilian Board of Contract Appeals decision?
Citation: us:cfr:49:29.922
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-29.922
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 29 (Tribal Transportation Self-Governance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A party may appeal a decision of the Civilian Board of Contract Appeals within 120 days to the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit.
