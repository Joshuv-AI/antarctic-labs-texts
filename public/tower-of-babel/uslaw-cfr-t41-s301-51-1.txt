41 C.F.R. § 301-51.1 — -51.1 Government contractor-issued travel charge card mandatory use.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-51.1
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-51.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-51 (51—Paying Travel Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees are required to use the Government contractor-issued travel charge card for all official travel expenses unless:
(a) Payment through the card is impractical (e.g., a vendor does not accept the travel charge card) or imposes unreasonable burdens or costs; or
(b) The Administrator of General Services or the agency head or their designee has granted an exemption under § 301-51.2.
