5 C.F.R. § 6.6 — Revocation of exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:6.6
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-6.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 6 (Exceptions From The Competitive Service (Rule Vi)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OPM may remove any position from or may revoke in whole or in part any provision of Schedule A, B, C, or D. Notice of OPM's decision making these changes shall be published in the Federal Register.
