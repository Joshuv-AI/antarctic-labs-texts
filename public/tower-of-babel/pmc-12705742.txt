Correction: Long term health related quality of life among individuals after COVID-19 recovery in a multicentric community-based study
Authors: Shubhanjali Roy, Alveena Malik, Alka Singh, Shomik Ray
Published: 2025

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-16082-6, published online 01 October 2025.

The original version of this Article contained errors.

In the Methods section,

“Participants were recruited from multiple healthcare facilities across different regions to ensure diversity in socioeconomic backgrounds and health statuses.”

now reads:

“Participants were recruited through systematic household sampling across selected wards in two cities (Delhi and Bhubaneswar), ensuring diversity in socioeconomic backgrounds and health statuses.”

The original Article has been corrected.
