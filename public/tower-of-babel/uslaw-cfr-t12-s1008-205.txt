12 C.F.R. § 1008.205 — The Bureau's establishment of nationwide mortgage licensing system and registry.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1008.205
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1008.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1008 (S.A.F.E. Mortgage Licensing Act—State Compliance And Bureau Registration System (Regulation H)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Bureau determines that the NMLSR established by CSBS and AARMR does not meet the minimum requirements of subpart D of this part, the Bureau will establish and maintain a nationwide mortgage licensing system and registry.
