10 U.S.C. § 10101 — Reserve components named
Citation: us:usc:10:10101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10101
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1003 (Reserve Components Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The reserve components of the armed forces are:
(1)
The Army National Guard of the United States.
(2)
The Army Reserve.
(3)
The Navy Reserve.
(4)
The Marine Corps Reserve.
(5)
The Air National Guard of the United States.
(6)
The Air Force Reserve.
(7)
The Coast Guard Reserve.
