46 C.F.R. § 189.25-40 — -40 Sanitary inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:46:189.25-40
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-189.25-40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 189 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) At each inspection for certification and periodic inspection, the quarters, toilets, and washing spaces, galleys, serving pantries, lockers, etc., shall be examined by the marine inspector to be assured that they are in a sanitary condition.
