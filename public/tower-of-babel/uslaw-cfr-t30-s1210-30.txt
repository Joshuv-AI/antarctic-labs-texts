30 C.F.R. § 1210.30 — What are my responsibilities as a reporter/payor?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1210.30
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1210.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1210 (Forms And Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each reporter/payor must submit accurate, complete, and timely information to ONRR according to the requirements in this part. If you discover an error in a previous report, you must file an accurate and complete amended report within 30 days of your discovery of the error. If you do not comply, ONRR may assess civil penalties under 30 CFR part 1241.
