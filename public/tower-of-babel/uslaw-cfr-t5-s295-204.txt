5 C.F.R. § 295.204 — Service of subpoenas or requests.
Citation: us:cfr:5:295.204
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-295.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 295 (Testimony By Opm Employees Relating To Official Information And Production Of Official Records In Legal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subpoenas or requests for official records or information or testimony must be served on the General Counsel, U.S. Office of Personnel Management, 1900 E Street, NW., Washington, DC 20415.
