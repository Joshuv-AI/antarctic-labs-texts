49 C.F.R. § 24.7 — Federal agency waiver of regulations in this part.
Citation: us:cfr:49:24.7
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-24.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 24 (Uniform Relocation Assistance And Real Property Acquisition For Federal And Federally Assisted Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Federal agency funding the project may waive any requirement in this part not required by law if it determines that the waiver does not reduce any assistance or protection provided to an owner or displaced person under this part. Any request for a waiver shall be justified on a case-by-case basis.
