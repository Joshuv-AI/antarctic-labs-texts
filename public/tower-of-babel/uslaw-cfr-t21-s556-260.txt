(b) Tolerances. The tolerances for ethopabate, measured as metaphenetidine, are:
(1) Chickens. (i) Liver: 1.5 ppm.
(ii) Kidney: 1.5 ppm.
(iii) Muscle: 0.5 ppm.

(c) Related conditions of use. See § 558.58 of this chapter.
