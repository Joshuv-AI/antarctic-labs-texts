19 C.F.R. § 202.3 — Preliminary inquiry.
Citation: us:cfr:19:202.3
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-202.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 202 (Investigations Of Costs Of Production). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the receipt of an application properly filed, the Commission will make a preliminary inquiry for the purpose of determining whether there is good and sufficient reason for a full investigation. If such determination is in the affirmative, a full investigation will be instituted.
