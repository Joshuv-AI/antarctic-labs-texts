16 U.S.C. § 7010 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:7010
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section7010
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 89 (Pacific Whiting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary such sums as may be necessary to carry out the obligations of the United States under the Agreement and this chapter.
