Future mobile information networks
Authors: Hao Yin, Xiaohu You, Jiangxing Wu, Yongming Huang, Xinsheng Ji, Wei Xu, Jiao Zhang, Zhengang Pan
Published: 2026

Traditional mobile communication networks have served as the foundational infrastructure for the evolution and development of mobile communication technologies, progressing through multiple generational standards from 1G through 4G to the current 5G Future mobile information networks represent a new generation of mobile communication networks centered on 6G, designed to address the entire lifecycle of information services. As a critical digital information infrastructure serving the entirety of society and industry, future mobile information networks will transcend traditional communication boundaries to achieve deep integration of multi-dimensional capabilities encompassing communications, sensing, computing, and artificial intelligence, alongside ubiquitous three-dimensional coverage integrating space, air, and terrestrial systems [1,2]. This evolution will engender a paradigm shift toward “on-demand service” and “everything as a service.” As these applications increasingly integrate communication, sensing, computing, and artificial intelligence technologies, the imperative for reliable endogenous security becomes ever more critical. Addressing these challenges necessitates innovative frameworks and interdisciplinary solutions. This special issue assembles 5 articles that collectively examine these transformative developments, charting the evolution of future mobile information networks while unlocking new avenues for interdisciplinary inquiry.

Novel frameworks and technologies for integrating Data Technology (DT), Operation Technology (OT), Information Technology (IT), and Communication Technology (CT), collectively referred to as DOICT, within future mobile information networks are formulated. Yin et al. [3] review the foundational theories, network architectures, key technologies, and enabling devices underpinning the development of future mobile information networks, and highlight critical challenges facing DOICT hyper-converged architectures, including design considerations, application requirements, and sustainable low-carbon operations. Wu et al. [4] propose the super-converged generative information network (SoGIN) to build an AI-empowered, highly integrated, efficiently collaborative, and trustworthy intelligent information network system through the deep convergence of DOICT. Leveraging an innovative hyper-converged architecture that unifies DOICT, Zhou et al. [5] introduce space-air-ground integrated low-altitude aerial vehicular networks (SAG-LAAVN) to deliver ubiquitous intelligent connectivity for three-dimensional intelligent transportation systems (ITS), offering seamless coverage, enhanced resilience, and improved quality of experience across diverse operational scenarios.

The advancement of information-theoretic foundations and enabling technologies is imperative for enhancing network capacity, energy efficiency, and latency performance in future mobile information networks. Gao et al. [6] provide a comprehensive review of recent progress in non-asymptotic fundamental limits, presenting various achievability and converse bounds along with their information-theoretic approximations and underlying theoretical insights. Yang et al. [7] introduce a machine synesthesia (SoM)-driven analog precoding framework for integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) in sub-THz systems, which reduces implementation complexity through the joint exploitation of communication and sensing channel statistics, with an architecture adaptively tailored to specific data and task requirements.

In the cybersecurity dimension, 6G must not only address inherent security risks in cloud resource orchestration, microservices architectures, and database management, but also confront novel threats arising from emerging technologies like AI, integrated sensing-communication, and quantum computing. Wu et al. [4] propose an integrated availability, reliability, and trustworthiness architecture to establish multi-layered defense capabilities spanning network communications, data processing, and application services.

The contributions assembled in this special issue on Future Mobile Information Networks collectively advance theoretical foundations and practical innovations in next-generation mobile networking, while charting a roadmap for future research. Biography

Xiaohu You has been working with the National Mobile Communications Research Laboratory at Southeast University, where he holds the rank of Director and Chief Professor. Now, he is also the Director and Chief Scientist of Purple Mountain Laboratory, and the Deputy Director of Pengcheng Laboratory. His research interests include broadband wireless transmission, mobile communication systems, and advanced signal processing and its applications. He was selected as an IEEE Fellow in 2011. He won the Tan Kah Kee Science Award in 2014 and the IET Achievement Medal in 2021. He was awarded an Academician of the Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2023.
