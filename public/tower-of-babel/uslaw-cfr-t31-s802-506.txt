31 C.F.R. § 802.506 — Determination not to undertake an investigation.
Citation: us:cfr:31:802.506
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-802.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 802 (Regulations Pertaining To Certain Transactions By Foreign Persons Involving Real Estate In The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Committee determines, during the review period described in § 802.503, not to undertake an investigation of a notified covered real estate transaction, action under section 721 shall be concluded. An official at the Department of the Treasury shall promptly inform the parties to a covered real estate transaction in writing of a determination of the Committee not to undertake an investigation and to conclude action under section 721.
