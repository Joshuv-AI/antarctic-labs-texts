7 C.F.R. § 58.247 — Insect and rodent control program.
Citation: us:cfr:7:58.247
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-58.247
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 58 (Grading And Inspection, General Specifications For Approved Plants And Standards For Grades Of Dairy Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to any commercial pest control service, if one is utilized, a specially designated employee should be made responsible for the performance of a regularly scheduled insect and rodent control program as outlined in University of Wisconsin Extension Bulletin A2518 or subsequent revisions thereof, or one equivalent thereto.
