39 C.F.R. § 951.7 — Notice of disbarment; exclusion from practice.
Citation: us:cfr:39:951.7
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-951.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 951 (Eligibility To Practice Before The Postal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the disbarment of any person, notice thereof will be given to the heads of the departments of the Postal Service and to the other Executive Departments, and thereafter, until otherwise ordered, such disbarred persons will not be entitled to practice before the Postal Service or any department thereof.
