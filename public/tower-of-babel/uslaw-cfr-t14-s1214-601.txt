14 C.F.R. § 1214.601 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1214.601
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1214.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1214 (Space Flight). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Mementos. Flags, patches, insignia, medallions, minor graphics, and similar items of little commercial value, especially suited for display by the individuals or groups to whom they have been presented.
