29 C.F.R. § 502.47 — Certification.
Citation: us:cfr:29:502.47
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-502.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 502 (Enforcement Of Contractual Obligations For Temporary Alien Agricultural Workers Admitted Under Section 218 Of The Immigration And Nationality Act (Suspended 6-29-2009)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of a complaint seeking review of a decision issued pursuant to this part filed in a U.S. District Court, after the administrative remedies have been exhausted, the Chief Administrative Law Judge or, where the case has been the subject of administrative review, the ARB shall promptly index, certify and file with the appropriate U.S. District Court, a full, true, and correct copy of the entire record, including the transcript of proceedings.
