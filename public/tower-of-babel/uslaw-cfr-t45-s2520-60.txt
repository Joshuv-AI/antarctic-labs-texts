45 C.F.R. § 2520.60 — What government-wide requirements apply to staff fundraising under my AmeriCorps grant?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2520.60
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2520.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2520 (General Provisions: Americorps Subtitle C Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must follow OMB Guidance published at 2 CFR part 200 and AmeriCorps implementing regulations at 2 CFR Chapter XXII. In particular, see 2 CFR 200.442—Fundraising and Investment Management Costs.
