40 C.F.R. § 426.120 — Applicability; description of the incandescent lamp envelope manufacturing subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:426.120
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-426.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 426 (Glass Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the processes by which (a) raw materials are melted in a furnace and mechanically processed into incandescent lamp envelopes or (b) incandescent lamp envelopes are etched with hydrofluoric acid to produce frosted envelopes.
