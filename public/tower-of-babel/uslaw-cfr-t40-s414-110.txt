40 C.F.R. § 414.110 — Applicability; description of the subcategory of indirect discharge point sources.
Citation: us:cfr:40:414.110
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-414.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 414 (Organic Chemicals, Plastics, And Synthetic Fibers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to the process wastewater discharges resulting from the manufacture of the OCPSF products and product groups defined by § 414.11 from any indirect discharge point source.
