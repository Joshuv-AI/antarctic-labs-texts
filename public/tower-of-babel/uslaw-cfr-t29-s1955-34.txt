29 C.F.R. § 1955.34 — Fees of witnesses.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1955.34
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1955.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1955 (Procedures For Withdrawal Of Approval Of State Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Witnesses, including witnesses for depositions, shall be paid the same fees and mileage that are paid witnesses in the courts of the United States. Fees shall be paid by the party at whose instance the witness appears, and the person taking a deposition shall be paid by the party at whose instance the deposition is taken.
