31 C.F.R. § 601.2 — Distinctiveness requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:31:601.2
Creator: Bureau Of Engraving And Printing, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-601.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 601 (Distinctive Paper And Distinctive Counterfeit Deterrents For United States Federal Reserve Notes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary of the Treasury has adopted distinctive paper and distinctive counterfeit deterrents:
(1) In which the United States has an exclusive property interest; or
(2) That are not otherwise in commercial use or the public domain and are necessary for preventing the counterfeiting of United States Federal Reserve notes.
(b) The distinctive paper and counterfeit deterrents are used in United States Federal Reserve notes.
