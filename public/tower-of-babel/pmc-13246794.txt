Correction: Measuring dissolved oxygen in Miso for forensic medicine and semisolid food analysis
Authors: Yoshiro Koda, Mikiko Soejima
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-17556-3, published online 28 August 2025

The original version of this Article contained an error in the Discussion section.

“The Shizuoka District Court in 2024 as well as the Tokyo High Court judges in 2023 concluded that the blood stains on cotton rags did not retain any redness after being placed in miso for > 1 year, aligning with the experimental results obtained by the aforementioned prosecutor.”

now reads:

“The Shizuoka District Court in 2024 as well as the Tokyo High Court judges in 2023 concluded that the blood stains on cotton rags did not retain any redness after being placed in miso for > 1 year, contrary to the experimental results obtained by the aforementioned prosecutor.”

The original Article has been corrected.
