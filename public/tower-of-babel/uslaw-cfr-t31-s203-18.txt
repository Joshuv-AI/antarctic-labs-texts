31 C.F.R. § 203.18 — Scope of the subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:31:203.18
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-203.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 203 (Payment Of Federal Taxes And The Treasury Tax And Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart governs the operation of the investment program, including the rules that TT&L depositaries must follow in crediting and debiting TIP main account balances, SDI account balances, and TIO account balances, and pledging collateral security.
