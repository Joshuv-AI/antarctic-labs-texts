7 U.S.C. § 3811 — Issuance of regulations; maintenance of rec­ords
Citation: us:usc:7:3811
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section3811
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 69 (Swine Health Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to issue such regulations and to require the maintenance of such records as he deems necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
