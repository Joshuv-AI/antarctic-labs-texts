9 C.F.R. § 310.12 — Sternum to be split; abdominal and thoracic viscera to be removed.
Citation: us:cfr:9:310.12
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-310.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 310 (Post-Mortem Inspection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The sternum of each carcass shall be split and the abdominal and thoracic viscera shall be removed at the time of slaughter in order to allow proper inspection.
