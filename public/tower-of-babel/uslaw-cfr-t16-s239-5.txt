16 C.F.R. § 239.5 — Performance of warranties or guarantees.
Citation: us:cfr:16:239.5
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-239.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 239 (Guides For The Advertising Of Warranties And Guarantees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A seller or manufacturer should advertise that a product is warranted or guaranteed only if the seller or manufacturer, as the case may be, promptly and fully performs its obligations under the warranty or guarantee.
