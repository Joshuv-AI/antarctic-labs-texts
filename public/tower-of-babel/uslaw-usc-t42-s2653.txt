42 U.S.C. § 2653 — Limitation or repeal of other provisions for recovery of hospital and medical care costs
Citation: us:usc:42:2653
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2653
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 32 (Third Party Liability For Hospital And Medical Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter does not limit or repeal any other provision of law providing for recovery by the United States of the costs of care and treatment described in section 2651 of this title.
