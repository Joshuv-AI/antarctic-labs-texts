12 C.F.R. § 268.706 — Program accessibility: Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:12:268.706
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-268.706
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 268 (Rules Regarding Equal Opportunity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in § 268.707 of this subpart, no qualified individual with a disability shall, because the Board's facilities are inaccessible to or unusable by individuals with a disability, be denied the benefits of, be excluded from participation in, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity conducted by the Board.
