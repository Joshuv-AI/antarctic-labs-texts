4 C.F.R. § 8.1 — Applicable law and regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:4:8.1
Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-4/section-8.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 8 (Insurance And Annuities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of subpart G, title 5, United States Code and implementing regulations for the Executive Branch covering compensation for work injuries, retirement, unemployment compensation, life insurance, and health insurance apply to Government Accountability Office employees.
