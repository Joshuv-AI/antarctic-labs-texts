34 C.F.R. § 76.126 — What regulations apply to the consolidated grant applications for insular areas?
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.126
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.126
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following regulations apply to those programs included in a consolidated grant:
(a) The regulations in §§ 76.125 through 76.137; and
(b) The regulations that apply to each specific program included in a consolidated grant for which funds are used.
