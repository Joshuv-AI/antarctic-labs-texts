19 C.F.R. § 204.4 — Supplemental investigations.
Citation: us:cfr:19:204.4
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-204.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 204 (Investigations Of Effects Of Imports On Agricultural Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An investigation for the purposes of section 22(d) of the Agricultural Adjustment Act, as amended, will be made upon request of the President, or upon the Commission's own motion when in its judgment there is good and sufficient reason therefor. A public hearing will be held in connection with each such supplemental investigation.
