41 C.F.R. § 301-11.3 — -11.3 Subsistence expense reimbursement methods.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-11.3
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-11.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-11 (11—Subsistence Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subsistence expenses will be reimbursed primarily using the lodgings-plus per diem method. Subsistence expenses may also be reimbursed using the actual expense or the reduced per diem methods. Agencies may allow a different method to be used each calendar day. See appendix A to this part to find out where to access per diem rates for various types of Government travel.
