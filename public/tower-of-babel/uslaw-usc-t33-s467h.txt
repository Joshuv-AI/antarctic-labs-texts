33 U.S.C. § 467h — Reports
Citation: us:usc:33:467h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section467h
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 9 (Protection Of Navigable Waters And Of Harbor And River Improvements Generally); Subchapter VII (Dam Inspection Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 90 days after the end of each odd-numbered fiscal year, the Administrator shall submit a report to Congress that—
(1)
describes the status of the Program;
(2)
describes the progress achieved by Federal agencies during the 2 preceding fiscal years in implementing the Federal Guidelines for Dam Safety;
(3)
describes the progress achieved in dam safety by States participating in the Program; and
(4)
includes any recommendations for legislative and other action that the Administrator considers necessary.
