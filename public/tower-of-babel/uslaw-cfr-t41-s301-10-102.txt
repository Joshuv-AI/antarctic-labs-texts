41 C.F.R. § 301-10.102 — -10.102 Handling of unused Government transportation items.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-10.102
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-10.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-10 (10—Transportation Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any unused ticket or refund applications are the property of the Government and must be returned to the agency in accordance with agency procedures. Employees are not authorized to receive or keep a refund or credit for unused transportation, except as provided in § 301-10.123.
