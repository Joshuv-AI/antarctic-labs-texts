10 U.S.C. § 6361 — Payment of costs of operation and maintenance of infrastructure at Nevada National Security Site
Citation: us:usc:10:6361
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section6361
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part VI (Elements Of Department Of Defense And Other Matters); Subpart B (Atomic Energy Defense); Chapter 608 (Administrative Matters); Subchapter IV (Other Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law and effective as of September 30, 1996, the costs associated with operating and maintaining the infrastructure at the Nevada National Security Site, Nevada, with respect to any activities initiated at the site after that date by the Department of Defense pursuant to a work-for-others agreement may be paid for from funds authorized to be appropriated to the Department of Energy for activities at the Nevada National Security Site.
