38 U.S.C. § 1135 — Additional compensation for dependents
Citation: us:usc:38:1135
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1135
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 11 (Compensation For Service-Connected Disability Or Death); Subchapter IV (Peacetime Disability Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any veteran entitled to compensation at the rates provided in section 1134 of this title, and whose disability is rated not less than 30 percent, shall be entitled to additional monthly compensation for dependents as provided in section 1115 of this title.
