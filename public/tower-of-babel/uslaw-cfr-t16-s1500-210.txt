16 C.F.R. § 1500.210 — Responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1500.210
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1500.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1500 (Hazardous Substances And Articles: Administration And Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of these regulations (16 CFR subchapter C of chapter II) with respect to the doing of any act shall be applicable also to the causing of such act to be done.
