46 C.F.R. § 195.11-20 — -20 Marking and label plate.
Citation: us:cfr:46:195.11-20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-195.11-20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 195 (Vessel Control And Miscellaneous Systems And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All vans shall be provided with a label plate stating light weight, gross weight, and power requirements where applicable.
(b) For vans subject to inspection label plates shall provide space for the date of initial inspection, the marine inspector's initials, and stamp. Space shall also be provided for the reinspection stamping.
