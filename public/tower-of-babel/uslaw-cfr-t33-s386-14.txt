33 C.F.R. § 386.14 — Reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:33:386.14
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-386.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 386 (Credit Assistance For Water Resources Infrastructure Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The borrower will provide annual audited financial statements, a public benefits report, and other reports to the Corps in the form and manner agreed upon in the credit agreement. These other reports may include, but are not limited to, an updated financial model and construction reports. The Corps may conduct periodic financial and compliance reviews or audits of the borrower and its project, as determined necessary by the Corps.
