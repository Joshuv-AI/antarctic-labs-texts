41 C.F.R. § 102-37.260 — -37.260 SASP reports to GSA.

Year: 2026

(a) Quarterly report on donations. Submit GSA Form 3040 by the 25th of the month following the quarter being reported.
(b) Additional reports. Make other reports as GSA may require to report to Congress on the status and progress of the donation program.
