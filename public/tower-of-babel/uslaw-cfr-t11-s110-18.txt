11 C.F.R. § 110.18 — Voting age population.
Citation: us:cfr:11:110.18
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-110.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 110 (Contribution And Expenditure Limitations And Prohibitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There is annually published by the Department of Commerce in the Federal Register an estimate of the voting age population based on an estimate of the voting age population of the United States, of each State, and of each Congressional district. The term voting age population means resident population, 18 years of age or older.
