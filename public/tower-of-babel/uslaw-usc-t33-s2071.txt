33 U.S.C. § 2071 — Inland navigation rules
Citation: us:usc:33:2071
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2071
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 34 (Inland Navigational Rules); Subchapter II (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Department in which the Coast Guard is operating may issue inland navigation regulations applicable to all vessels upon the inland waters of the United States and technical annexes that are as consistent as possible with the respective annexes to the International Regulations.
