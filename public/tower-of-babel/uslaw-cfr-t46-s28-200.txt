46 C.F.R. § 28.200 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:28.200
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-28.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 28 (Requirements For Commercial Fishing Industry Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each documented commercial fishing industry vessel must meet the requirements of this subpart in addition to the requirements of subparts A and B of this part if it:
(a) Operates beyond the Boundary Lines;
(b) Operates with more than 16 individuals on board; or
(c) Is a fish tender vessel engaged in the Aleutian trade.
