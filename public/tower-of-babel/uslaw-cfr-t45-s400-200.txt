45 C.F.R. § 400.200 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:45:400.200
Creator: Office Of Refugee Resettlement, Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-400.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 400 (Refugee Resettlement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart specifies when, and the extent to which, Federal funding (FF) is available under this regulation in expenditures for determining eligibility and for providing assistance and services to refugees determined eligible under this part, and prescribes limitations and conditions on FF for those expenditures.
