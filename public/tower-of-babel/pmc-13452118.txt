Authors: Yanxia Huang, Jing Zhang, Lidan Hou, Ge Wang, Hui Liu, Rui Zhang, Xiaoyu Chen, Jinshui Zhu
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: J Exp Clin Cancer Res 36, 194 (2017)
