20 C.F.R. § 901.42 — Representation.
Citation: us:cfr:20:901.42
Creator: Joint Board For The Enrollment Of Actuaries
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-901.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 901 (Regulations Governing The Performance Of Actuarial Services Under The Employee Retirement Income Security Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A respondent or proposed respondent may appear at conference or hearing in person or may be represented by counsel or other representative. The Executive Director may be represented by an attorney or other employee of the Treasury Department.
