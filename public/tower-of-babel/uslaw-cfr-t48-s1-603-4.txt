48 C.F.R. § 1.603-4 — 1.603-4 Termination.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1.603-4
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1.603-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Termination of a contracting officer appointment will be by letter, unless the Certificate of Appointment contains other provisions for automatic termination. Terminations may be for reasons such as reassignment, termination of employment, or unsatisfactory performance. No termination shall operate retroactively.
