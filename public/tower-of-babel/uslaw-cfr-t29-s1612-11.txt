29 C.F.R. § 1612.11 — Public access to records.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1612.11
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1612.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1612 (Government In The Sunshine Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All requests for information shall be submitted in writing to the Chairman of the agency. Requests to inspect or copy the electronic recordings or minutes of agency meetings or portions thereof will be considered under the provisions of § 1612.4 of this part.
