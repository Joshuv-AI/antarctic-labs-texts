49 C.F.R. § 1110.4 — Participation.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1110.4
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1110.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1110 (Procedures Governing Informal Rulemaking Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person may participate in rulemaking proceedings by submitting written information or views. In addition, the Board may invite persons to present oral arguments, participate in informal conferences, appear at informal fact-finding hearings, or participate in any other proceedings. Information contained in written submissions will be given the same consideration.
