16 U.S.C. § 2706 — New impoundments
Citation: us:usc:16:2706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section2706
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 47 (Small Hydroelectric Power Projects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter authorizes (1) the loan of funds for construction of any new dam or other impoundment, or (2) the simple and expeditious licensing of any such new dam or other impoundment.
