43 C.F.R. § 4.744 — What happens if no timely objection to the preliminary decision is filed?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.744
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.744
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If no written objection to a preliminary decision is timely filed in accordance with § 4.743(a), the presiding officer will issue a final decision.
