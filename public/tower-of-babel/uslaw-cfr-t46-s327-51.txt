46 C.F.R. § 327.51 — Statute of limitations not tolled by administrative consideration of claims.
Citation: us:cfr:46:327.51
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-327.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 327 (Seamen'S Claims; Administrative Action And Litigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The statute of limitations for filing a civil action under 46 U.S.C. 30101(b) is not tolled by the MARAD's administrative consideration of a claim.
