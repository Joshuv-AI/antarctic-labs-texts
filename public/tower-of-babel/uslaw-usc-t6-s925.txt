6 U.S.C. § 925 — Border Patrol unit for United States Virgin Islands
Citation: us:usc:6:925
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section925
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 3 (Security And Accountability For Every Port); Subchapter I (Security Of United States Seaports); Part B (Port Operations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary may establish at least 1 Border Patrol unit for the United States Virgin Islands.
(b) Report
Not later than 180 days after October 13, 2006, the Secretary shall submit a report to the appropriate congressional committees that includes the schedule, if any, for carrying out subsection (a).
