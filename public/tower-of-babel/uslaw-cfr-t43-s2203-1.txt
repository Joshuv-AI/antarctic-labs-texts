43 C.F.R. § 2203.1 — Opportunity for public comment and public meeting on exchange proposal.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2203.1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2203.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2200 (Exchanges: General Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon acceptance of a proposal for a fee exchange of Federal coal deposits, the authorized officer shall publish and distribute a notice of exchange proposal as set forth in § 2201.2 of this title.
