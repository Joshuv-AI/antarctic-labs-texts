49 U.S.C. § 44509 — Demonstration projects
Citation: us:usc:49:44509
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section44509
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart iii (Safety); Chapter 445 (Facilities, Personnel, And Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation may carry out under this chapter demonstration projects that the Secretary considers necessary for research and development activities under this chapter.
