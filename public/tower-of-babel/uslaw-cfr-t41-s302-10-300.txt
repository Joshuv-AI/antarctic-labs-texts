41 C.F.R. § 302-10.300 — -10.300 Advance of funds.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-10.300
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-10.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-10 (10—Allowances For Transportation Of Mobile Homes And Boats Used As A Primary Residence). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a commercial carrier transports a mobile home, employees may receive an advance of funds when they are responsible for arranging and paying a commercial carrier to transport their mobile home. However, the advance may not exceed the estimated amount allowable. Employees are not authorized an advance of funds for any payment made directly to the carrier by the agency.
