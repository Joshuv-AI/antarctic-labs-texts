48 C.F.R. § 204.7107 — 204.7107 Contract accounting classification reference number (ACRN) and agency accounting identifier (AAI).
Citation: us:cfr:48:204.7107
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-204.7107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 204 (Administrative And Information Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Traceability of funds from accounting systems to contract actions is accomplished using ACRNs and AAIs. Follow the procedures at PGI 204.7107 for use of ACRNs and AAIs.
