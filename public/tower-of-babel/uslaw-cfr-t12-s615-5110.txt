12 C.F.R. § 615.5110 — Authority to issue (other funding).
Citation: us:cfr:12:615.5110
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-615.5110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 615 (Funding And Fiscal Affairs, Loan Policies And Operations, And Funding Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any Farm Credit bank may issue Farm Credit Investment Bonds directly to those eligible as set forth in § 615.5120(a). The bonds are subject to the limitations contained in the Federal Reserve Board's Regulation Q.
