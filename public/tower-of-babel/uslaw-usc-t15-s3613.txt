15 U.S.C. § 3613 — Limitation of actions
Citation: us:usc:15:3613
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section3613
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 62 (Condominium And Cooperative Conversion Protection And Abuse Relief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No action shall be maintained to enforce any right or liability created by this chapter unless brought within six years after such cause of action accrued, except that an action pursuant to section 3608 of this title must be brought within four years after October 8, 1980.
