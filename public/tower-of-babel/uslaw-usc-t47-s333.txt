47 U.S.C. § 333 — Willful or malicious interference
Citation: us:usc:47:333
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section333
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 5 (Wire Or Radio Communication); Subchapter III (Special Provisions Relating To Radio); Part I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No person shall willfully or maliciously interfere with or cause interference to any radio communications of any station licensed or authorized by or under this chapter or operated by the United States Government.
