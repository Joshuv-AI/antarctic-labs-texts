16 U.S.C. § 1101 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:16:1101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1101
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 22 (International Parks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this chapter:
(a)
The term “Commission” means the Roosevelt Campobello International Park Commission.
(b)
The term “United States members” means members of the Commission appointed by the President. The term “Canadian members” means members of the Commission appointed by the appropriate authorities in Canada.
