14 C.F.R. § 406.123 — Waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:14:406.123
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-406.123
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 406 (Investigations, Enforcement, And Administrative Review). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Waivers of any rights provided by statute or regulation must be in writing or by stipulation made at a hearing and entered into the record. The parties must set forth the precise terms of the waiver and any conditions.
