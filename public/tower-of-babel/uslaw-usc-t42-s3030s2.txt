42 U.S.C. § 3030s–2 — Maintenance of effort
Citation: us:usc:42:3030s–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3030s–2
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter III (Grants For State And Community Programs On Aging); Part E (National Family Caregiver Support Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds made available under this part shall supplement, and not supplant, any Federal, State, or local funds expended by a State or unit of general purpose local government (including an area agency on aging) to provide services described in section 3030s–1 of this title.
