48 C.F.R. § 2831.101 — 2831.101 Objectives.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2831 (Contract Cost Principles And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The SPE is the official authorized to grant individual deviations from the cost principles of FAR part 31.
(b) Requests for class deviations from the cost principles set forth in FAR part 31 will be forwarded through the SPE prior to submission to the CAAC.
