30 C.F.R. § 56.3201 — Location for performing scaling.
Citation: us:cfr:30:56.3201
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-56.3201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 56 (Safety And Health Standards—Surface Metal And Nonmetal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Scaling shall be performed from a location which will not expose persons to injury from falling material, or other protection from falling material shall be provided.
