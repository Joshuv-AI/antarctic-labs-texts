16 U.S.C. § 583k–4 — Cooperative agreements

Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3 (Forests; Forest Service; Reforestation; Management); Subchapter VI (National Forest System Trails Stewardship). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary may enter into a cooperative agreement with any State, tribal, local governmental, and private entity to carry out this subchapter.
(b) Contents
Cooperative agreements authorized under this section may—
(1)
improve trail maintenance in a priority area;
(2)
implement the strategy; or
(3)
advance trail maintenance in a manner deemed appropriate by the Secretary.
