10 U.S.C. § 20001 — Single military personnel management system
Citation: us:usc:10:20001
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section20001
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Space Force); Chapter 2001 (Space Force Personnel System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Members of the Space Force shall be managed through a single military personnel management system, without component.
