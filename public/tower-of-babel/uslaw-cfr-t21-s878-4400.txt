21 C.F.R. § 878.4400 — Electrosurgical cutting and coagulation device and accessories.
Citation: us:cfr:21:878.4400
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-878.4400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 878 (General And Plastic Surgery Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. An electrosurgical cutting and coagulation device and accessories is a device intended to remove tissue and control bleeding by use of high-frequency electrical current.
(b) Classification. Class II.
