13 C.F.R. § 106.400 — Non-Fee Based SBA-Sponsored Activity.
Citation: us:cfr:13:106.400
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-106.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 106 (Cosponsorships, Fee And Non-Fee Based Sba-Sponsored Activities And Gifts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator (or designee) may provide assistance directly to small business concerns through Non-Fee Based SBA-Sponsored Activities under section 8(b)(1)(A) of the Small Business Act.
