10 U.S.C. § 12210 — Attending Physician to the Congress: reserve grade
Citation: us:usc:10:12210
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12210
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1205 (Appointment Of Reserve Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

While serving as Attending Physician to the Congress, a Reserve holds the reserve grade of major general or rear admiral, as appropriate.
