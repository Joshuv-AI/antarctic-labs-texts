10 C.F.R. § 960.5 — Preclosure guidelines.
Citation: us:cfr:10:960.5
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-960.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 960 (General Guidelines For The Preliminary Screening Of Potential Sites For A Nuclear Waste Repository). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The guidelines in this subpart specify the factors to be considered in evaluating and comparing sites on the basis of expected repository performance before closure. The preclosure guidelines are separated into three system guidelines and eleven technical guidelines.
