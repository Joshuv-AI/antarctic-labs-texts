45 C.F.R. § 1169.9 — Fees charged to locate, review, or copy records.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1169.9
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1169.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1169 (Privacy Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) NEH will not charge fees for the search or review of requested records, or the amendment or correction of records.
(b) NEH will not charge fees for providing the first copy of a record or any portion of a record to whom the record pertains. NEH will otherwise charge copying fees at the same rate, and using the same procedures, that NEH has established for FOIA requests.
