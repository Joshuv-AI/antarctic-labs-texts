46 C.F.R. § 351.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:46:351.1
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-351.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 351 (Depositories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to set forth the criteria necessary for depositories of funds under all programs authorized by the Merchant Marine Act, 1936, as amended (46 U.S.C. 1101 et seq.) (Act).
