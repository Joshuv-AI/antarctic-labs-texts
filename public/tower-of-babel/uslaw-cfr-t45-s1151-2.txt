45 C.F.R. § 1151.2 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1151.2
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1151.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1151 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to each recipient of financial assistance from the National Endowment for the Arts and to each program or activity that receives such assistance.
