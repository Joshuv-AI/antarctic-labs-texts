33 C.F.R. § 165.1701 — Port Valdez, Valdez, Alaska—safety zone.
Citation: us:cfr:33:165.1701
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-165.1701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 165 (Regulated Navigation Areas And Limited Access Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The waters within the following boundaries are a safety zone—The area within 200 yards of any waterfront facility at the Trans-Alaska Pipeline Valdez Terminal complex or vessels moored or anchored at the Trans-Alaska Pipeline Valdez Terminal complex and the area within 200 yards of any tank vessel maneuvering to approach, moor, unmoor, or depart the Trans-Alaska Pipeline Valdez Terminal complex.
