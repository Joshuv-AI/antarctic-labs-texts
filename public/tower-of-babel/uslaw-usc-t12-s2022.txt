12 U.S.C. § 2022 — Liens on stock
Citation: us:usc:12:2022
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2022
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 23 (Farm Credit System); Subchapter I (Farm Credit Banks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Farm Credit Banks shall have a first lien on the stock or participation certificates it issues for the payment of any liability of the stockholders to the bank.
