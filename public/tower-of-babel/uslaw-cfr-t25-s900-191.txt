25 C.F.R. § 900.191 — Are employees of self-determination contractors providing health services under the self-determination contract protected by FTCA?
Citation: us:cfr:25:900.191
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior, And Indian Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-900.191
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 900 (Contracts Under The Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. For the purpose of Federal Tort Claims Act coverage, an Indian tribe or tribal organization and its employees performing medical-related functions under a self-determination contract are deemed a part of the Public Health Service if the employees are acting within the scope of their employment in carrying out the contract.
