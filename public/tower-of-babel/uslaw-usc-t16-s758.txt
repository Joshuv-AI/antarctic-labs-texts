16 U.S.C. § 758 — Exploration, investigation, development, and maintenance of fishing resources and industry of Pacific Ocean
Citation: us:usc:16:758
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section758
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 9A (Preservation Of Fishery Resources). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the policy of the United States to provide for the exploration, investigation, development, and maintenance of the fishing resources and development of the high seas fishing industry of the United States and its island possessions in the tropical and subtropical Pacific Ocean and intervening seas, for the benefit of the residents of the Pacific island possessions and of the people of the United States.
