43 U.S.C. § 1600h — Separability
Citation: us:usc:43:1600h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1600h
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 32B (Colorado River Floodway). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, the remainder of the chapter and the application of such provision to other persons not similarly situated or to other circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
