12 C.F.R. § 617.7405 — On what policies are loan restructurings based?
Citation: us:cfr:12:617.7405
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-617.7405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 617 (Borrower Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Loan restructurings must be made in accordance with the policy adopted by the supervising bank board of directors under section 4.14A(g) of the Act.
