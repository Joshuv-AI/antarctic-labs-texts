29 C.F.R. § 4007.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4007.1
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4007.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4007 (Payment Of Premiums). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part, which applies to all plans that are covered by title IV of ERISA, provides procedures for paying the premiums imposed by sections 4006 and 4007 of ERISA. (See part 4006 of this chapter for premium rates and computational rules.)
