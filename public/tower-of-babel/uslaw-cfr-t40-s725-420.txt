40 C.F.R. § 725.420 — Recipient microorganisms.
Citation: us:cfr:40:725.420
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-725.420
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 725 (Reporting Requirements And Review Processes For Microorganisms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following recipient microorganisms are eligible for either exemption under this subpart:
(a) Acetobacter aceti.
(b) Aspergillus niger.
(c) Aspergillus oryzae.
(d) Bacillus licheniformis.
(e) Bacillus subtilis.
(f) Clostridium acetobutylicum.
(g) Escherichia coli K-12.
(h) Penicillium roqueforti.
(i) Saccharomyces cerevisiae.
(j) Saccharomyces uvarum.
(k) Trichoderma reesei strain QM6a and its derivatives used only in submerged standard industrial fermentation operations as defined at 40 CFR 725.3.
(l) Bacillus amyloliquefaciens subsp. amyloliquefaciens.
