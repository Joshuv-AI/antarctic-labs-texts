13 C.F.R. § 124.207 — Can an applicant reapply for admission to the 8(a) BD program?
Citation: us:cfr:13:124.207
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-124.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 124 (8(a) BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT/SMALL DISADVANTAGED BUSINESS STATUS DETERMINATIONS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A concern which has been declined for 8(a) BD program participation may submit a new application for admission to the program at any time after 90 calendar days from the date of the Agency's final decision to decline.
