42 U.S.C. § 668 — Encouragement of States to adopt civil procedure for establishing paternity in contested cases
Citation: us:usc:42:668
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section668
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter IV (Grants To States For Aid And Services To Needy Families With Children And For Child-Welfare Services); Part D (Child Support And Establishment Of Paternity). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the administration of the child support enforcement program under this part, each State is encouraged to establish and implement a civil procedure for establishing paternity in contested cases.
