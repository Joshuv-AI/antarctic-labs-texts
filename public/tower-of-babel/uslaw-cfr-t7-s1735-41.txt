7 C.F.R. § 1735.41 — Notes.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1735.41
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1735.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1735 (General Policies, Types Of Loans, Loan Requirements—Telecommunications Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Loans are represented by one or more notes. Interest accrues only on funds advanced. There are no loan commitment fees or charges. See RUS Bulletin 320-12 for additional information. This CFR part supersedes those portions of RUS Bulletin 320-12 “Loan Payments and Statements” with which it is in conflict.
