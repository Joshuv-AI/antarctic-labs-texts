29 C.F.R. § 4901.3 — Electronic reading room.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4901.3
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4901.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4901 (Disclosure And Public Inspection Of Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

PBGC will maintain an electronic reading room on its website, www.pbgc.gov, where persons may inspect in an electronic format all records made available for such purposes under this part.
