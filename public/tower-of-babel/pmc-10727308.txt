Correction to: Long‐Term Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability of Alirocumab in 8242 Patients Eligible for 3 to 5 Years of Placebo‐Controlled Observation in the ODYSSEY OUTCOMES Trial
Published: 2023

In the article by Shaun G Goodman, et al, “Long‐Term Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability of Alirocumab in 8242 Patients Eligible for 3 to 5 Years of Placebo‐Controlled Observation in the ODYSSEY OUTCOMES Trial,” which published online on September 13, 2023 (J Am Heart Assoc. 2023;12:e029216. DOI: 10.1161/JAHA.122.029216) and was included in the September 19, 2023 issue of the journal, a correction was needed.

In the second paragraph of page 2, the reference provided for the following sentence was incorrectly cited as #3 but should have been cited as reference #2: “In prior prespecified analysis, alirocumab reduced death (4‐year Kaplan‐Meier estimate, 5.4% versus 7.0%; P=0.01) compared with placebo.”

The authors regret the error.

The online version of the article has been updated and is available here: https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/JAHA.122.029216
