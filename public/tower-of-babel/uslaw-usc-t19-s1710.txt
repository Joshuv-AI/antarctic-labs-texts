19 U.S.C. § 1710 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:19:1710
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1710
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 5 (Smuggling). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any clause, sentence, paragraph, or part of this Act, or the application thereof to any person, or circumstances, is held invalid, the application thereof to other persons, or circumstances, and the remainder of the Act, shall not be affected thereby.
