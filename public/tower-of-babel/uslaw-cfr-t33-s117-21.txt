33 C.F.R. § 117.21 — Signalling for an opened drawbridge.
Citation: us:cfr:33:117.21
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-117.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 117 (Drawbridge Operation Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a vessel approaches a drawbridge with the draw in the open position, the vessel shall give the opening signal. If no acknowledgment is received within 30 seconds, the vessel may proceed, with caution, through the open draw.
