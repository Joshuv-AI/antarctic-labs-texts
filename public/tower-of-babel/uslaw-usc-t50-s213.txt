50 U.S.C. § 213 — Jurisdiction of confiscation proceedings
Citation: us:usc:50:213
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section213
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 13 (Insurrection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Such prizes and capture shall be condemned in the district court of the United States having jurisdiction of the amount, or in admiralty in any district in which the same may be seized, or into which they may be taken and proceedings first instituted.
