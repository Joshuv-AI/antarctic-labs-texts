14 U.S.C. § 3738a — Direct commissioning authority for individuals with critical skills
Citation: us:usc:14:3738a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section3738a
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 37 (Coast Guard Reserve); Subchapter II (Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An individual with critical skills that the Commandant considers necessary for the Coast Guard to complete its missions who is not currently serving as an officer in the Coast Guard may be commissioned into the Coast Guard at a grade up to and including commander.
