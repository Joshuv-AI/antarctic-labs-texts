16 U.S.C. § 831n–2 — Bonds; limitation of issuance under sections 831n and 831n–1
Citation: us:usc:16:831n–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section831n–2
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12A (Tennessee Valley Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No bonds shall be issued by the Corporation after the date of enactment of this section under section 831n or 831n–1 of this title.
