29 C.F.R. § 501.38 — Notice of docketing.
Citation: us:cfr:29:501.38
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-501.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 501 (Enforcement Of Contractual Obligations For Temporary Agricultural Workers Admitted Under Section 218 Of The Immigration And Nationality Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of an Order of Reference, the Chief ALJ shall appoint an ALJ to hear the case. The ALJ shall promptly notify all interested parties of the docketing of the matter and shall set the time and place of the hearing. The date of the hearing shall be not more than 60 calendar days from the date on which the Order of Reference was filed.
