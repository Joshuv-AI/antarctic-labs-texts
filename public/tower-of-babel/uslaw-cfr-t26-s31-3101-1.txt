26 C.F.R. § 31.3101-1 — -1 Measure of employee tax.
Citation: us:cfr:26:31.3101-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-31.3101-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 31 (Employment Taxes And Collection Of Income Tax At Source). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The employee tax is measured by the amount of wages received after 1954 with respect to employment after 1936. See § 31.3121(a)-1, relating to wages; and §§ 31.3121(b)-1 to 31.3121(b)-4, inclusive, relating to employment. For provisions relating to the time of receipt of wages, see § 31.3121(a)-2.
