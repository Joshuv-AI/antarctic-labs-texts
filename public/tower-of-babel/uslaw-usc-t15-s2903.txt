15 U.S.C. § 2903 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:15:2903
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2903
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 56 (National Climate Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, unless the context otherwise requires:
(1)
The term “Board” means the Climate Program Policy Board.
(2)
The term “Office” means the National Climate Program Office.
(3)
The term “Program” means the National Climate Program.
(4)
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Commerce.
