49 C.F.R. § 384.409 — Notification of noncompliance.
Citation: us:cfr:49:384.409
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-384.409
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 384 (State Compliance With Commercial Driver'S License Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If FMCSA determines that a State is not in substantial compliance with § 384.209, or if FMCSA issues a decertification order prohibiting a State from issuing commercial driver's licenses, FMCSA will notify commercial learner's permit and commercial driver's license holders of these actions by publication of a Federal Register notice.
