Research of digital management on sport: An analysis of bibliometrics using CiteSpace Software
Authors: Gang Xu, Qian Yang, Qiang Li, Hongli Yu
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Digital management on sport (DMS) has become a hot topic around sports management. However, a more comprehensive and in-depth investigation into the structure and progression of this field is necessary. We used CiteSpace software to conduct a bibliometric analysis, revealing dynamic patterns in the evolution of co-citation, collaboration, and keyword co-occurrence within this field. Data gathers from the Web of Science Core Collection, which includes Social Sciences Citation Index (SSCI), Arts & Humanities Citation Index (A&HCI), Emerging Sources Citation Index (ESCI) and Science Citation Index Expanded (SCI-EXPANDED), and SCOPUS. Published works in this field have shown a consistent upward trend since 2013. England institutions are leading the way in cooperation, while the US is the most collaborative region with different priorities for collaboration in various topics. The most influential author is the one who receives the most citations, but unfortunately, the most frequently co-cited author shows “ANONYMOUS.” Highly cited journals typically exert a substantial influence. Additionally, further study is expected to concentrate on efficacy and validation. Since 2018, the growth in publications on DMS reflects increasing academic interest. Australia’s institutions lead in collaboration, though international cooperation is limited. High co-citation authors hold significant influence and research focuses on management, social media, and physical activity.

2.2. Research methodology

A wide range of software applications are accessible for doing bibliometric statistics, each with its own set of pros and shortcomings.[19] For example, VOSviewer provides user-friendly functionalities that enable fast analysis and visualization, allowing for a swift review of correlations in literature. Nevertheless, its ability to do intricate clustering and temporal analysis is restricted, and it encounters difficulties when dealing with extensive literature datasets. HistCite demonstrates exceptional proficiency in doing frequency analysis, but, it lacks the capability to thoroughly investigate co-occurrence or co-citation connections. SATI is proficient in analyzing co-occurrence and co-citation data, but it does not have the capability to visually represent the chronological development of study topics. RefViz is user-friendly for keyword analysis and grouping, however it has constraints when it comes to integrated and complete analysis.[15]

Instead, CiteSpace, a Java-based bibliometric analysis software, represents a cutting-edge tool in information visualization technology, adept at handling multi-dimensional, time-based, and dynamic complex network analysis. Widely adopted in scientometrics, CiteSpace facilitates the depiction of a domain’s scientific knowledge through various network types. For instance, in a timeline network, clusters summarize similar nodes, with node size indicating keyword frequency and line color representing the time of initial keyword and co-occurrence.[20] Through the assessment of cluster size and the mean publication year, researchers could identify and trace the evolution of field hotspots.[21] Additionally, It was highlighted that burst detection in CiteSpace could reveal study hotspots by analyzing literature with unusually high citation rates over a period, breakout words with unusually high occurrence frequencies, and milestone literature marked by prominent citation rates. Strategic diagrams further describe research hotspots through a clustering and co-word matrix, defined by degrees of novelty and attention.[22]

More precisely, bibliometric visual analysis of the literature on DMS is conducted using the CiteSpace software. This software has gained global recognition from numerous researchers due to its robust visualization of data and quantitative analytical skills, which effectively illustrate the dynamic development of study area. CiteSpace has been effectively employed in prior bibliometric research across many fields, including metaverse, resilient shipping network, and diet in Alzheimer’s disease.[23–25] The objective of this study is to analyze the key features and notable aspects of DMS research, including its current publishing status, collaborative relationships, research development, and emerging areas of interest. This analysis will help researchers gain a more comprehensive understanding of the field. The operation homepage and selection criteria are presented in Figure 1.

Operational running homepage.

By using CiteSpace, we obtained results and visually represented the networks through the following procedures (Fig. 2). Obtain the dataset comprising 896 items extracted from SCOPUS and WoS, removing redundant data to achieve the final dataset spanning 2013 to 2023. Set the time range from January 2013 to December 2023 and configure the data processing parameters to include the keywords, author keywords, abstract and title plus as sources for text analysis. Identify the node types for authors, institutions, and countries, and generate visual collaboration networks that illustrate the connections between these nodes, allowing for the identification of key stakeholders in DMS research over the past decade. Define node types for cited authors, references, and journals to assess the co-citation landscape within this study area. Specify node types for keywords and generate networks of keyword co-occurrences, providing insight into the evolution of research, current areas of focus, and noteworthy developments. This analysis of each component offers a cohesive understanding and comprehensive view of DMS research, helping to build a knowledge framework according to study findings and forecast further study directions and areas of interest.[26,27]

3.1. Publication statistics

Publication statistics offer a comprehensive summary of the current publication status and trends in a specific subject. They help scholars determine the popularity and emergence of the field, as well as identify the preferred topics and journals among scholars in that field.[28]

Figure 3 depicts the yearly publishing of articles on DMS from January 2013 to November 2023. Overall, there is a growing body of study on DMS. Specifically, it went through 3 stages of development:

Annual publication analysis.

The period from 2013 to 2018 can be described as the childhood stage. During this period, the annual publication count was minimal, with less than 10 publications per year. This indicates that research on DMS was not a primary focus during this stage. However, these publications played a crucial role in establishing the groundwork for future expansion. The second phase, known as the puberty stage, occurred between 2018 and 2020. During this period, there was a marginal rise in the number, which eventually settled at approximately 30 per year. This trend suggests the development and expansion of DMS research. The third phase, which occurred between 2020 and 2023, witnessed a consistent and gradual rise in the number of articles, starting at approximately 50. Despite minor fluctuations in certain years, the overall growth potential of the subject remains evident. The year characterized by the greatest quantity of publications (2023, 221 publications) is about twice as high as the year with the lowest number of publications (2020, 105 publications) over this period. That suggests that DMS research has garnered heightened interest from scholars. Additionally, it is important to mention that our analysis did not include literature produced after 2023. Consequently, the data for 2023 had a comparatively higher value compared to previous years. In conclusion, the field of DMS has garnered significant interest over the last 10 years, and it is imperative for current scholars to further allocate resources towards research in this domain.

3.3. Status of co-citation

Co-citation analysis is a technique aimed at uncovering the relationships between journals, scholarly works, and authors. When pieces of literature, authors, or 2 journals are co-cited in other articles indicates a possible correlation, implying their relevance to a specific study area. The frequency with which 2 entities are co-cited strengthens the perceived relationship, reflecting a common focus within the academic community and a comparable level of influence. This method offers critical insights into the current research landscape and highlights the key contributors who hold a foundational and influential position in the field. Co-cited references, which may or may not be included in our database, are those cited together in the articles we analyze. These references help reveal the relative significance of research contributions, identifying those with greater foundational importance and impact.[15]

These results provide valuable insights that the highly co-cited publications in this area encompass diverse subject matters. They will be crucial in guiding future study, enabling scholars to thoroughly examine and reference papers from many fields.

4. Discussion

4.1. Current challenges

Based on the analysis provided, it can be concluded that DMS is an interdisciplinary field that encompasses a wide range of issues. This diversity of themes adds complexity to the definition of DMS and gives rise to new interdisciplinary challenges. The aforementioned analysis and framework indicate that current research faces the subsequent issues, which warrant the attention of researchers and practitioners.

(1) The precise function of DMS goals remains ambiguous. Sport is commonly regarded as a means of maintaining and enhancing physical well-being. Some scholars argue that there is a connection between sport and digital management, although the latter is often seen as complex. Can digital management offer advantages to the field of sports? What are the ways in which it offers advantages? What are the specific responsibilities and ethical obligations of various stakeholders involved in the DMS process? These difficulties continue to provide challenges for the ongoing development of the DSM.

(2) The method to attain DMS is yet unclear. Various intricate aspects significantly impact the effectiveness and efficiency of accomplishing DMS; these factors have varying effects on DMS. Furthermore, our understanding of the specific methods we can employ to accomplish DMS remains limited, except from the ones we have previously addressed (such as validation and efficacy programs). Moreover, the existing policies and remedies may not effectively enhance DMS, which is also a difficult task.

(3) DMS exhibits unique traits in many situations, posing challenges to both research and practice. Are DMS activities implemented in impoverished areas, for example? Will citizens and villagers have a comparable perception of the benefits of DMS? Can universal theories be applied in all scenarios? Do smaller institutions get the same level of success in implementing Document Management Systems (DMS)? Various scenarios present challenges for future study in DMS.

4.2. Comparison discussions

We conducted a comparative analysis of our study with previous literature reviews on DMS and identified several disparities. This comparative discussion enhances understanding of the field, highlighting the originality and contribution of this research.[46]

(1) This research uses extensive visual clusters to convey data and demonstrate the interconnections across many literary works, offering researchers a lucid and intuitive comprehension of the essential knowledge foundation in the field. These clusters are generated through quantitative analysis. Additionally, we present detailed information regarding the clusters and the dynamics of keywords, allowing researchers to closely investigate specific developments in the DMS research area over different time periods and to identify emerging trends within this domain. In contrast to prior studies that often rely on subjective textual summaries or comparisons – which can cause personal bias and excessive detail – some literature reviews in this area tend to list key works and compare the results manually.[47,48] While these reviews offer valuable insights, they can be challenging to comprehend due to their reliance on textual descriptions and may overlook crucial aspects of this field.

(2) This research constructs a comprehensive framework by performing an in-depth analysis that encompasses co-citation, collaboration, co-occurrence analysis, and publication data. This approach guarantees a coherent logical structure across key facets of DMS research, facilitating a better understanding of critical insights in the field. In contrast, some studies convey their findings through tables or textual explanations,[48,49] which may not be as effective in capturing the core elements of the research. Furthermore, several works propose frameworks derived from their analytical outcomes,[50,51] supporting the validity of the framework applied in this research. While these frameworks differ in their specific focuses, ours stands out for its broader scope. This methodology is set to be a significant reference for future research.

(3) This study examines present obstacles and prospective research avenues, offering significant insights for academics to comprehend modern issues and feasible remedies through cluster analysis and the existing knowledge framework. Its objective is to encourage further exploration in this field. However, prior research has identified challenges and future research opportunities, though these studies tend to focus on niche aspects and do not offer a complete overview of the future of “DMS.” Furthermore, as these studies were published nearly 5 years ago, their relevance for guiding current research may be limited.

5. Conclusion and limitations

Using CiteSpace software, we performed a bibliometric analysis of literature from the SCOPUS and WoS database. Our analysis includes publication trends over the past decade, co-citation networks, collaboration patterns, and keyword co-occurrence. During this period, we observed significant growth in DMS, largely driven by advancements in digital technologies. The following key findings were identified:

(1) The analysis of yearly publications reveals a consistent and compound growth in the quantity of publications in DMS since 2018. This indicates that the academic communities in various disciplines are showing a growing interest in this topic.

(2) The analysis of the collaboration network reveals that institutions in AUSTRALIA engage in cooperation more often in this sector. Additionally, various regions may commence cooperation in different areas of study. Nevertheless, there is currently limited international collaboration among writers, indicating the need to promote greater participation and cooperation in study endeavors.

(3) Analysis of the co-citation network indicates that the author with the greatest number of co-citations has the greatest influence. Additionally, journals that receive a high number of citations typically have a large number of publications. Moreover, the most often cited sources cover a broad spectrum of topics. Consequently, we recommend that scholars consult these highly cited references to enhance their knowledge base and invigorate their study endeavors.

(4) This study of the co-occurrence network of keywords reveals that the primary areas of research are management, social media, physical activity, and the creation of influence. It is expected that future research will mostly concentrate on examining the effectiveness and validation of these areas. In order to make important contributions in the discipline, researchers must possess a thorough understanding of the implications associated with these keywords.

This study is innovative in the following manner. From a knowledge standpoint, we thoroughly examine and assess the research advancements of DMS using the views of cooperation, co-occurrence and co-citation. These perspectives offer a comprehensive, integrated and multidimensional approach to provide readers with ample information to comprehend the dynamics of the subject. In theory, we establish a novel knowledge framework for this topic, which helps to enhance the comprehension of the logical structure and progress of DMS study in a more vivid, clear, and efficient manner. In practical terms, we determine the characteristics of future research, which helps academics discover study interests that are more suitable and allows them to continue their research more successfully and efficiently.

There are certain limitations to this study. Given the evolving nature of scientific study, the findings are time-sensitive and may pave the way for future discoveries. However, we relied on SCOPUS and WoS database for our data. It is important to recognize that authors in certain regions may favor publishing in other indexes and archives, potentially leading to regional bias. Furthermore, we focused exclusively on English, Spanish, and Chinese language papers, as they are widely used in academic circles. Nonetheless, research published in other different languages is also valuable, and excluding English, Spanish and Chinese language papers may result in an incomplete analysis of some aspects.

We plan to conduct a further bibliometric analysis of recent publications, incorporating new discoveries. This study will include articles published after November 2023. Additionally, we aim to expand our analysis by incorporating more databases, such as EBSCO and CNKI, to enhance the representativeness of our findings. We will also include research published in languages other than English, Spanish, and Chinese, such as Portuguese and German, to broaden our understanding of DMS research. Furthermore, we may study differences in research development across studies published in different languages.
