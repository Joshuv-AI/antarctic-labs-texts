21 C.F.R. § 640.60 — Source Plasma.
Citation: us:cfr:21:640.60
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-640.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 640 (Additional Standards For Human Blood And Blood Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The proper name of the product shall be Source Plasma. The product is defined as the fluid portion of human blood collected by plasmapheresis and intended as source material for further manufacturing use. The definition excludes single donor plasma products intended for intravenous use.
