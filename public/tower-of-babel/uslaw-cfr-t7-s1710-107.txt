7 C.F.R. § 1710.107 — Amount lent for acquisitions.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1710.107
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1710.107
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1710 (General And Pre-Loan Policies And Procedures Common To Electric Loans And Guarantees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The maximum amount that will be lent for an acquisition is limited to the value of the property, as determined by RUS. If the acquisition price exceeds this amount, the borrower shall provide the remainder without RUS financial assistance.
