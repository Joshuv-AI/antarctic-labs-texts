40 C.F.R. § 79.7 — Samples for test purposes.
Citation: us:cfr:40:79.7
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-79.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 79 (Registration Of Fuels And Fuel Additives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the Administrator requires for test purposes a fuel or additive which is not readily available in the open market, he may request the manufacturer of such fuel or additive to furnish a sample in a reasonable quantity. The fuel or additive manufacturer shall comply with such request within 30 days.
