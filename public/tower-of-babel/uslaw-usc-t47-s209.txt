47 U.S.C. § 209 — Orders for payment of money
Citation: us:usc:47:209
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section209
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 5 (Wire Or Radio Communication); Subchapter II (Common Carriers); Part I (Common Carrier Regulation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If, after hearing on a complaint, the Commission shall determine that any party complainant is entitled to an award of damages under the provisions of this chapter, the Commission shall make an order directing the carrier to pay to the complainant the sum to which he is entitled on or before a day named.
