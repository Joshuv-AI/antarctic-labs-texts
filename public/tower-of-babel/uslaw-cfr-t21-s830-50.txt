21 C.F.R. § 830.50 — Changes that require use of a new device identifier.
Citation: us:cfr:21:830.50
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-830.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 830 (Unique Device Identification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Whenever you make a change to a device that is required to bear a unique device identifier (UDI) on its label, and the change results in a new version or model, you must assign a new device identifier to the new version or model.
(b) Whenever you create a new device package, you must assign a new device identifier to the new device package.
