31 C.F.R. § 315.50 — Change of name.
Citation: us:cfr:31:315.50
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-315.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 315 (Regulations Governing U.S. Savings Bonds, Series A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, J, And K, And U.S. Savings Notes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An owner, coowner, or beneficiary whose name is changed by marriage, divorce, annulment, order of court, or in any other legal manner after the issue of bond should submit the bond with a request for reissue to substitute the new name for the name inscribed on the bond. Documentary evidence may be required in any appropriate case.
