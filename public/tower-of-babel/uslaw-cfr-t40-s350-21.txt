40 C.F.R. § 350.21 — Adverse health effects.
Citation: us:cfr:40:350.21
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-350.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 350 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Governor or State emergency response commission shall identify the adverse health effects associated with each of the chemicals claimed as trade secret and shall make this information available to the public. The material safety data sheets submitted to the State emergency response commissions may be used for this purpose.
