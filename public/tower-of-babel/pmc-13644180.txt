Authors: Yayun Chi, Sheng Huang, Mengying Liu, Liang Guo, Xuxia Shen, Jiong Wu
Published: 2026

The Editors-in-Chief have retracted this Article. Following publication, concerns were raised by the authors, including but not limited to the following issues:

The Migration image in the Control (Con) group partially overlaps with the Invasion image in the same group in Fig. 3.

The Migration image in the Control (Con) group partially overlaps with the Invasion image in the same group in Fig. 3.

The Migration image in the siCCND3 group partially overlaps with the Invasion image in the same group in Fig. 3.

The Migration image in the siCCND3 group partially overlaps with the Invasion image in the same group in Fig. 3.

The Editors-in-Chief therefore no longer have confidence in the results and conclusions presented in this Article.

The authors have not responded to any correspondence from the Editor about this retraction.
