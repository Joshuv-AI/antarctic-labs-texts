32 C.F.R. § 2400.10 — Presumption of damage.
Citation: us:cfr:32:2400.10
Creator: Office Of Science And Technology Policy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-2400.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 2400 (Regulations To Implement E.O. 12356; Office Of Science And Technology Policy Information Security Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unauthorized disclosure of foreign government information, the identity of a confidential foreign source, or intelligence sources or methods, is presumed to cause damage to the national security.
