33 C.F.R. § 125.55 — Outstanding Port Security Card Applications.
Citation: us:cfr:33:125.55
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-125.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 125 (Identification Credentials For Persons Requiring Access To Waterfront Facilities Or Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person who has filed an application for a Coast Guard Port Security Card and who did not receive such a document prior to May 1, 1956, shall submit a new application in accordance with the requirements of this part.
