33 C.F.R. § 104.140 — Alternative Security Programs.
Citation: us:cfr:33:104.140
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-104.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 104 (Maritime Security: Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A vessel owner or operator may use an Alternative Security Program as approved under § 101.120 of this subchapter if:
(a) The Alternative Security Program is appropriate to that class of vessel;
(b) The vessel is not subject to the International Convention for Safety of Life at Sea, 1974; and
(c) The Alternative Security Program is implemented in its entirety.
