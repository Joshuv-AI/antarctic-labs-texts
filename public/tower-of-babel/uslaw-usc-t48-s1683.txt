48 U.S.C. § 1683 — Auditing of transactions of Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands
Citation: us:usc:48:1683
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1683
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 14 (Trust Territory Of The Pacific Islands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All financial transactions of the Trust Territory, including such transactions of all agencies or instrumentalities established or utilized by such Trust Territory, may be audited by the Government Accountability Office, at its discretion, in accordance with chapter 35 of title 31.
