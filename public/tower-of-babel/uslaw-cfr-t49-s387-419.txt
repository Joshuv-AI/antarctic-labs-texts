49 C.F.R. § 387.419 — Electronic filing of surety bonds, certificates of insurance and cancellations.
Citation: us:cfr:49:387.419
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-387.419
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 387 (Minimum Levels Of Financial Responsibility For Motor Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insurers must electronically file certificates of insurance, surety bonds, and other securities and agreements and notices of cancellation in accordance with the requirements and procedures set forth at § 387.323.
