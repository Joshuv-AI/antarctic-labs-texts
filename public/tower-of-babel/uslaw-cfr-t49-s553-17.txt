49 C.F.R. § 553.17 — Participation of interested persons.
Citation: us:cfr:49:553.17
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-553.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 553 (Rulemaking Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any interested person may participate in rulemaking proceeding by submitting comments in writing containing information, views or arguments.
(b) In his discretion, the Administrator may invite any interested person to participate in the rulemaking procedures described in § 553.25.
