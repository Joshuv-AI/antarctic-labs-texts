Abstract
. In this paper, we introduce the notion of uniformly- S S -pseudo-projective ( u u - S S -pseudo-projective) modules as a generalization of u u - S S -projective modules. Let R R be a ring and S S a multiplicative subset of R R . An R R -module P P is said to be u u - S S -pseudo-projective if for any submodule K K of P P , there is s ∈ S s\in S such that for any u u - S S -epimorphism f : P → P K f:P\to\frac{P}{K} , s ​ f sf can be lifted to an endomorphism g : P → P g:P\to P . Some properties of this notion are obtained. For example, we prove that an R R -module M M is u u - S S -quasi-projective if and only if M ⊕ M M\oplus M is u u - S S -pseudo-projective. Also, we prove that if A ⊕ B A\oplus B is u u - S S -pseudo-projective, then any u u - S S -epimorphism f : A → B f:A\to B u u - S S -splits. Several new characterizations of u u - S S -semisimple rings are given.
