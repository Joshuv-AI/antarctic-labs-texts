Following publication of the original article [1], the authors identified an error in the author names of all authors. The given name and family name were erroneously transposed.

The incorrect author names are:

Damiani Elisa, Domizi Roberta, Carsetti Andrea, Antolini Riccardo, Adrario Erica and Donati Abele.

The correct author names are:

Elisa Damiani, Roberta Domizi, Andrea Carsetti, Riccardo Antolini, Erica Adrario and Abele Donati.

The author group has been updated above and the original article [1] has been corrected.
