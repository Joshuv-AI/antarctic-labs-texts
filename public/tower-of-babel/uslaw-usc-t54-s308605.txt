54 U.S.C. § 308605 — Private property protection
Citation: us:usc:54:308605
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section308605
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3086 (United States African-American Burial Grounds Preservation Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter—
(1)
authorizes the Secretary to require or affect the management or use of private property without the written consent of the owner of the private property;
(2)
prohibits the Secretary from providing land management guidance or requirements relating to private property as a condition of a grant provided to the owner of the private property under this chapter; or
(3)
shall be construed as creating any new regulatory burden on any Federal, State, Tribal, or private entity.
