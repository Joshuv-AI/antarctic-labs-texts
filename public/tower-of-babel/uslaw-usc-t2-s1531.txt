2 U.S.C. § 1531 — Regulatory process
Citation: us:usc:2:1531
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1531
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 25 (Unfunded Mandates Reform); Subchapter II (Regulatory Accountability And Reform). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each agency shall, unless otherwise prohibited by law, assess the effects of Federal regulatory actions on State, local, and tribal governments, and the private sector (other than to the extent that such regulations incorporate requirements specifically set forth in law).
