7 C.F.R. § 1740.59 — Application submission.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1740.59
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1740.59
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1740 (Rural Econnectivity Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Applications must be submitted through the Agency's online application system.
(b) The Agency may publish additional application submission requirements in a notice in the Federal Register.
(c) Unless otherwise identified in the notice, applicants can only submit one application under any funding window.
