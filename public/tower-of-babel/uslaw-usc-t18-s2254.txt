18 U.S.C. § 2254 — Civil forfeiture
Citation: us:usc:18:2254
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2254
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 110 (Sexual Exploitation And Other Abuse Of Children). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any property subject to forfeiture pursuant to section 2253 may be forfeited to the United States in a civil case in accordance with the procedures set forth in chapter 46.
