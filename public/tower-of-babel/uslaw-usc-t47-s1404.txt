47 U.S.C. § 1404 — National security restrictions on use of funds and auction participation
Citation: us:usc:47:1404
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section1404
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 13 (Public Safety Communications And Electromagnetic Spectrum Auctions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Use of funds
No funds made available by subchapter II or III may be used to make payments under a contract to a person described in subsection (c).
(b) Auction participation
A person described in subsection (c) may not participate in a system of competitive bidding under section 309(j) of this title—
(1)
that is required to be conducted by this chapter; or
(2)
in which any spectrum usage rights for which licenses are being assigned were made available under clause (i) of subparagraph (G) of paragraph (8) of such section, as added by section 6402.
(c) Person described
A person described in this subsection is a person who has been, for reasons of national security, barred by any agency of the Federal Government from bidding on a contract, participating in an auction, or receiving a grant.
