36 C.F.R. § 219.60 — Secretary's authority.
Citation: us:cfr:36:219.60
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-219.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 219 (Planning). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart restricts the Secretary of Agriculture from exercising any statutory authority regarding the protection, management, or administration of NFS lands.
