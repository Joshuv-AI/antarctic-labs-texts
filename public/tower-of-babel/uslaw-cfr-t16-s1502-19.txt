16 C.F.R. § 1502.19 — Commencement of functions.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1502.19
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1502.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1502 (Procedures For Formal Evidentiary Public Hearing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The functions of the presiding officer begin upon designation and end upon the filing of the initial decision.
