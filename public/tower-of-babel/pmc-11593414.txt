In addition to the change in Table 4, text alterations were made to the Section 3.4.2, second paragraph (fourth sentence) of Foaming Properties:

“When treated with laccase R and TG, the foaming stability of LW samples had slight changes.”

The wording of the Conclusions section, the third sentence in the first paragraph, was also modified to reflect the Corrections to Table 4:

“Compared with the control (LW with no enzyme), laccase R improved protein solubility, emulsion stability, and foaming ability, while TG improved emulsion stability.”

The authors state that the scientific conclusions are unaffected. This correction was approved by the Academic Editor. The original publication has also been updated.
