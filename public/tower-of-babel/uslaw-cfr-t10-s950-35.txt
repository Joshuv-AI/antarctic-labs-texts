10 C.F.R. § 950.35 — Payment of final claim determination.
Citation: us:cfr:10:950.35
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-950.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 950 (Standby Support For Certain Nuclear Plant Delays). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Once a Final Claim Determination is reached by the methods set forth in this subpart, the parties intend that such a Final Claim Determination shall constitute a final settlement of the claim and the sponsor may immediately present to the Department a Final Claim Determination for payment.
