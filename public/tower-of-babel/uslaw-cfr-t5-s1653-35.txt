5 C.F.R. § 1653.35 — Calculating entitlement.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1653.35
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1653.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1653 (Court Orders And Legal Processes Affecting Thrift Savings Plan Accounts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A tax levy or criminal restitution order can only require the payment of a stated dollar amount from the TSP. The payee's entitlement will be the lesser of:
(a) The dollar amount stated in the tax levy or enforcement letter; or
(b) The vested account balance on the date of disbursement, minus any outstanding loan balance.
