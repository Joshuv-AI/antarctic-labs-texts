7 C.F.R. § 869.105 — Care of agricultural products.
Citation: us:cfr:7:869.105
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Federal Grain Inspection Service, Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-869.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 869 (Regulations For The United States Warehouse Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each warehouse operator must at all times, including during any period of suspension of their license, exercise such care in regard to stored and non-storage agricultural products in their custody as required in the applicable licensing agreement.
