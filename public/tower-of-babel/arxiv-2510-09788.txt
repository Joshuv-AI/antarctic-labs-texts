Abstract
. In this article, we prove the p p -adic Kazhdan-Lusztig hypothesis for GL n ​ ( F ) \mathrm{GL}_{n}(F) . In this section we summarize the Langlands classification theorem as it appears in

]
.
The result of the classification theorem is crucial for defining standard representations, which are a key component of the

Let

\displaystyle\mathfrak{a}_{M}

\displaystyle:=\mathrm{Hom}(X^{\ast}(M)_{F},\mathbb{R})

\displaystyle\mathfrak{a}_{M}^{\ast}

\displaystyle:=X^{\ast}(M)_{F}\otimes\mathbb{R}.

Suppose

is a minimal Levi subgroup, and let
\Sigma
be the roots of the maximal torus
A
of
M_{0}
.
Each parabolic

A minimal standard parabolic

For any standard parabolic

\displaystyle\Delta_{P}

\displaystyle:=\{\alpha_{0}|_{\mathfrak{a}_{M}}:\alpha_{0}\in\Delta_{P_{0}}-\Delta_{P_{0}\cap M}\},

\displaystyle\mathfrak{a}_{P}^{\ast,+}

\displaystyle:=\{\lambda\in\mathfrak{a}_{M}^{\ast}:\forall\alpha\in\Delta_{P},\alpha^{\vee}(\lambda)>0\}.

Theorem 2.1

(
[

]

.

For any irreducible admissible representation

of

Definition 1
.

of

Given

\check{\psi}(g):=\psi(g^{-1})^{\vee}.

\displaystyle\mathcal{H}(G,\tau)

3.3.

Our goal for this section is to recall Vogan’s version of the

In order to articulate this, we must first review the local Langlands correspondence for

The local Langlands correspondence is fundamentally about the

which maps (isomorphism classes of) irreducible representations to (equivalence classes of)

The general definition will not concern us here, and in fact, we will find is easier to focus on

Definition 11

\mathrm{Ad}(\sigma(w))N=\lVert w\rVert N

\displaystyle=0.

We write

For a multisegment

\text{rec}\left(Q(\Delta_{1},\ldots,\Delta_{r})\right)=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{r}(\omega^{a_{i}}\eta_{n_{i}}\otimes\sigma_{i},I_{n_{i}}\otimes N_{i}),

describes a bijection between the smooth irreducible representations of
\mathrm{GL}_{n}(F)
and Weil-Deligne representations.
We call the first factor

For the choice of basis appearing the the definition of

The perspective taken in

where the semi-direct product is given by the action

Let
