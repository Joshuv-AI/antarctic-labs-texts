12 C.F.R. § 1026.27 — Language of disclosures.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1026.27
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1026.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1026 (Truth In Lending (Regulation Z)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Disclosures required by this part may be made in a language other than English, provided that the disclosures are made available in English upon the consumer's request. This requirement for providing English disclosures on request does not apply to advertisements subject to §§ 1026.16 and 1026.24.
