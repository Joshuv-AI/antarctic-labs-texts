10 U.S.C. § 8253 — Loans: Supply Corps officers
Citation: us:usc:10:8253
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8253
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 829 (Miscellaneous Prohibitions And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided by law, an officer in the Supply Corps on active duty may not advance or lend any sum of money, public or private, or any article or commodity and may not extend credit to any officer of the naval service on active duty.
