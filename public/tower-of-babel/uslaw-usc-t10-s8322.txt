10 U.S.C. § 8322 — Officers: 30 years
Citation: us:usc:10:8322
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8322
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 841 (Voluntary Retirement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
An officer of the Regular Navy or the Regular Marine Corps holding a permanent appointment in the grade of warrant officer, W–1, or above who applies for retirement after completing 30 or more years of active service may, in the discretion of the Secretary of the Navy, be retired.
(b)
For the purpose of this section, an officer’s years of active service are computed by adding all his active service in the armed forces.
