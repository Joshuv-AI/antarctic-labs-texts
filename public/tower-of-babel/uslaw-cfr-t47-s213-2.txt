47 C.F.R. § 213.2 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:47:213.2
Creator: Office Of Science And Technology Policy And National Security Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-213.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 213 (Government And Public Correspondence Telecommunications Precedence System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The precedence system contained herein is applicable to:
(a) Users of Government service facilities, whether owned or leased.
(b) Users of public correspondence service facilities of the communication common carriers, to U.S. domestic and international communication common carriers, and to the extent possible by agreement between the latter and their foreign correspondents.
