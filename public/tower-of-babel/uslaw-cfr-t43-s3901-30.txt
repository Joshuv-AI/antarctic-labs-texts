43 C.F.R. § 3901.30 — Computing acreage holdings.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3901.30
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3901.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3900 (Oil Shale Management—General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In computing the maximum acreage an entity may hold under a Federal lease, on either public lands or acquired lands, in any one state, acquired lands and public lands are counted separately. An entity may hold up to the maximum acreage of each at the same time.
