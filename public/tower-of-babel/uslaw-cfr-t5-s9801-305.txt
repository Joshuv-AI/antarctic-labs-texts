5 C.F.R. § 9801.305 — Assistance in preparing request to amend a record or to appeal an initial adverse determination.
Citation: us:cfr:5:9801.305
Creator: Council Of The Inspectors General On Integrity And Efficiency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9801.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9801 (Privacy Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requesters may seek assistance in preparing a request to amend a record or an appeal of an initial adverse determination, or to learn further of the provisions for judicial review, by contacting CIGIE's Privacy Officer by email at privacy@cigie.gov or by mail at Privacy Officer, Council of the Inspectors General on Integrity and Efficiency, 1717 H Street NW., Suite 825, Washington, DC 20006.
