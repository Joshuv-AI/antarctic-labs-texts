32 C.F.R. § 1803.41 — Right of further appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1803.41
Creator: National Counterintelligence Center
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1803.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1803 (Public Requests For Mandatory Declassification Review Of Classified Information Pursuant To Section 3.6 Of Executive Order 12958). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A right of further appeal is available to the ISCAP established pursuant to § 5.4 of this Order. Action by that Panel will be the subject of rules to be promulgated by the Information Security Oversight Office (ISOO).
