42 C.F.R. § 137.340 — May a Self-Governance Tribe contribute funding to a project?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.340
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.340
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, the Self-Governance Tribe and the Secretary may jointly fund projects. The construction project agreement should identify the Secretarial amount and any Tribal contribution amount that is being incorporated into the construction project agreement. The Self-Governance Tribe does not have to deposit its contribution with the Secretary.
