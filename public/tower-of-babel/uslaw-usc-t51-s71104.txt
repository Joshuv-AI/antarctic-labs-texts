51 U.S.C. § 71104 — Planetary radar
Citation: us:usc:51:71104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section71104
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 711 (Near-Earth Objects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall maintain a planetary radar that is comparable to the capability provided through the Deep Space Network Goldstone facility of the Administration.
