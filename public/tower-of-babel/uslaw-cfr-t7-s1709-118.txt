7 C.F.R. § 1709.118 — Submission of applications.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1709.118
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1709.118
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1709 (Assistance To High Energy Cost Communities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise provided in the grant announcement, a complete original application package and two copies must be submitted by the application deadline to RUS at the address specified in the applicable announcement. Instructions for submittal of applications electronically will be established in the grant announcement.
