34 C.F.R. § 644.2 — Who is eligible for a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:34:644.2
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-644.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 644 (Educational Opportunity Centers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following entities are eligible for a grant to carry out an Educational Opportunity Centers project:
(a) An institution of higher education.
(b) A public or private agency or organization, including a community-based organization with experience in serving disadvantaged youth.
(c) A secondary school.
(d) A combination of the types of institutions, agencies, and organizations described in paragraphs (a), (b), and (c) of this section.
