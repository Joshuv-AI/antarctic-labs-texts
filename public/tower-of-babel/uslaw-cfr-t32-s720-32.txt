32 C.F.R. § 720.32 — Certificates of full faith and credit.
Citation: us:cfr:32:720.32
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-720.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 720 (Delivery Of Personnel; Service Of Process And Subpoenas; Production Of Official Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Judge Advocate General, the Deputy Judge Advocate General, or any Assistant Judge Advocate General is authorized to execute certificates of full faith and credit certifying the signatures and authority of officers of the Department of the Navy.
