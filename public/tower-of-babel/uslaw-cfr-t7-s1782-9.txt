7 C.F.R. § 1782.9 — Environmental review requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1782.9
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1782.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1782 (Servicing Of Water And Waste Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Servicing actions involving lease or sale of Agency-owned property must comply with the environmental review requirements in accordance with 7 CFR part 1970.
