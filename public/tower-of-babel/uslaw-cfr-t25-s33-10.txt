25 C.F.R. § 33.10 — Issuance of procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:25:33.10
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-33.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 33 (Transfer Of Indian Education Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Secretary—Indian Affairs, directly or through the Commissioner of Indian Affairs, shall issue procedures in the Bureau of Indian Affairs Manual governing the provision of support services to the Bureau's Education Office function.
