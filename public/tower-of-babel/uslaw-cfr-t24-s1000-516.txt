24 C.F.R. § 1000.516 — What reporting period is covered by the annual performance report?
Citation: us:cfr:24:1000.516
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1000.516
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1000 (Native American Housing Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the first annual performance report to be submitted under NAHASDA, the period to be covered is October 1, 1997, through September 30, 1998. This first report must be submitted by January 31, 1999. Subsequent annual performance reports must cover the period that coincides with the recipient's program year.
