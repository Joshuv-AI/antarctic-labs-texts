29 C.F.R. § 38.82 — Notice that complaint will not be accepted.
Citation: us:cfr:29:38.82
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-38.82
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 38 (Implementation Of The Nondiscrimination And Equal Opportunity Provisions Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a complaint will not be accepted, the Director must notify the complainant, in writing, about that fact, and provide the complainant the Director's reasons for making that determination.
