24 C.F.R. § 990.325 — Record retention requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:24:990.325
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-990.325
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 990 (The Public Housing Operating Fund Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The PHA shall retain all documents related to all financial management and activities funded under the Operating Fund for a period of five fiscal years after the fiscal year in which the funds were received.
