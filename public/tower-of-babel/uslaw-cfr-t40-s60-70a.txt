40 C.F.R. § 60.70a — a Applicability and designation of affected facility.
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.70a
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.70a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The provisions of this subpart are applicable to each nitric acid production unit, which is the affected facility.
(b) This subpart applies to any nitric acid production unit that commences construction or modification after October 14, 2011.
