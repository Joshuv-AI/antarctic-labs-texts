28 C.F.R. § 513.34 — Protection of individual privacy—disclosure of records to third parties.
Citation: us:cfr:28:513.34
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-513.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 513 (Access To Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Information that concerns an individual and is contained in a system of records maintained by the Bureau shall not be disclosed to any person, or to another agency except under the provisions of the Privacy Act, 5 U.S.C. 552a, the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. 552, and Departmental regulations.
(b) Lists of Bureau inmates shall not be disclosed.
