21 U.S.C. § 379a — Presumption of existence of jurisdiction
Citation: us:usc:21:379a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section379a
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 9 (Federal Food, Drug, And Cosmetic Act); Subchapter VII (General Authority); Part A (General Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In any action to enforce the requirements of this chapter respecting a device, tobacco product, food, drug, or cosmetic the connection with interstate commerce required for jurisdiction in such action shall be presumed to exist.
