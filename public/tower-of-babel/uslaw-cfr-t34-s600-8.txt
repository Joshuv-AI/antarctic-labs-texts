34 C.F.R. § 600.8 — Treatment of a branch campus.
Citation: us:cfr:34:600.8
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-600.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 600 (Institutional Eligibility Under The Higher Education Act Of 1965, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A branch campus of an eligible proprietary institution of higher education or a postsecondary vocational institution must be in existence for at least two years as a branch campus after the branch is certified as a branch campus before seeking to be designated as a main campus or a free-standing institution.
