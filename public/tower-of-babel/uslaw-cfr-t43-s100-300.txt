43 C.F.R. § 100.300 — Must I submit an appraisal or valuation to the Department?
Citation: us:cfr:43:100.300
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-100.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 100 (Waiving Departmental Review Of Appraisals And Valuations Of Indian Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Appraisals and valuations of Indian property must be submitted to us if relied upon or required for transactions requiring Secretarial approval under titles 25 and 43 of the CFR (other than those under the Federal Land Policy and Management Act).
