49 C.F.R. § 89.31 — Use of professional debt collection agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:49:89.31
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-89.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 89 (Implementation Of The Federal Claims Collection Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Debts over 90 days delinquent (see § 89.21(b)(4)) may be turned over to professional debt collection agencies except for those debts owed by State and local governments, other Federal agencies, current employees, and other debts prohibited by statute from being turned over to commercial collection agencies.
