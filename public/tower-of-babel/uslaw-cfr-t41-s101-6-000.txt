41 C.F.R. § 101-6.000 — -6.000 Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-6.000
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-6.000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-6 (6—Miscellaneous Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth miscellaneous regulations regarding Federal Property Management Regulations matters which do not come within the scope of any other subchapter of chapter 101.
