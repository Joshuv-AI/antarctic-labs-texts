29 C.F.R. § 580.11 — Appointment of Administrative Law Judge and notification of prehearing conference and hearing date.
Citation: us:cfr:29:580.11
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-580.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 580 (Civil Money Penalties—Procedures For Assessing And Contesting Penalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt from the Administrator of an Order of Reference, the Chief Administrative Law Judge shall appoint an Administrative Law Judge to hear the case. The Administrative Law Judge shall notify all interested parties of the time and place of a prehearing conference and of the hearing.
