27 C.F.R. § 22.68 — Notice of permanent discontinuance.
Citation: us:cfr:27:22.68
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-22.68
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 22 (Distribution And Use Of Tax-Free Alcohol). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A permittee who permanently discontinues the use of tax-free alcohol shall file a written notice with the appropriate TTB officer to cover the discontinuance. The notice will be accompanied by the permit, and contain—
(1) A request to cancel the permit,
(2) A statement of the disposition made, as provided in § 22.154, of all tax-free and recovered alcohol, and
(3) The date of discontinuance.
