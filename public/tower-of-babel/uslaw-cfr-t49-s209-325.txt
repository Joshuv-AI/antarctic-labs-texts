49 C.F.R. § 209.325 — Finality of decision.
Citation: us:cfr:49:209.325
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-209.325
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 209 (Railroad Safety Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The initial decision of the presiding officer shall become final 35 days after issuance. Such decisions are not precedent.
(b) Exception. The initial decision shall not become final if, within 35 days after issuance of the decision, any party files an appeal under § 209.327. The timely filing of such an appeal shall stay the order in the initial decision.
