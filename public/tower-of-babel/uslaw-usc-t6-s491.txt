6 U.S.C. § 491 — National Homeland Security Council
Citation: us:usc:6:491
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section491
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter IX (National Homeland Security Council). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established within the Executive Office of the President a council to be known as the “Homeland Security Council” (in this subchapter referred to as the “Council”).
