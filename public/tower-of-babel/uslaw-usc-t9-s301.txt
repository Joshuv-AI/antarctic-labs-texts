9 U.S.C. § 301 — Enforcement of Convention
Citation: us:usc:9:301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title9-section301
Context: United States Code, Title 9 (Arbitration); Chapter 3 (Inter-American Convention On International Commercial Arbitration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Inter-American Convention on International Commercial Arbitration of January 30, 1975, shall be enforced in United States courts in accordance with this chapter.
