7 C.F.R. § 65.250 — Slaughter.
Citation: us:cfr:7:65.250
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-65.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 65 (Country Of Origin Labeling Of Lamb, Chicken, And Goat Meat, Perishable Agricultural Commodities, Macadamia Nuts, Pecans, Peanuts, And Ginseng). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Slaughter means the point in which a livestock animal (including chicken) is prepared into meat products (covered commodities) for human consumption. For purposes of labeling under this part, the word harvested may be used in lieu of slaughtered.
