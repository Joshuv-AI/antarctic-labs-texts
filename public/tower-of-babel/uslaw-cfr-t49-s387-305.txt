49 C.F.R. § 387.305 — Combination vehicles.
Citation: us:cfr:49:387.305
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-387.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 387 (Minimum Levels Of Financial Responsibility For Motor Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following combinations will be regarded as one motor vehicle for purposes of this part, (a) a tractor and trailer or semitrailer when the tractor is engaged solely in drawing the trailer or semitrailer, and (b) a truck and trailer when both together bear a single load.
