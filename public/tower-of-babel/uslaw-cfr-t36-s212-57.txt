36 C.F.R. § 212.57 — Monitoring of effects of motor vehicle use on designated roads and trails and in designated areas.
Citation: us:cfr:36:212.57
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-212.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 212 (Travel Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For each administrative unit of the National Forest System, the responsible official shall monitor the effects of motor vehicle use on designated roads and trails and in designated areas under the jurisdiction of that responsible official, consistent with the applicable land management plan, as appropriate and feasible.
