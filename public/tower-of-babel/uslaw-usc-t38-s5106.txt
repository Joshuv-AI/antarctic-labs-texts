38 U.S.C. § 5106 — Furnishing of information by other agencies
Citation: us:usc:38:5106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section5106
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part IV (General Administrative Provisions); Chapter 51 (Claims, Effective Dates, And Payments); Subchapter I (Claims). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The head of any Federal department or agency shall provide such information to the Secretary as the Secretary may request for purposes of determining eligibility for or amount of benefits, or verifying other information with respect thereto. The cost of providing information to the Secretary under this section shall be borne by the department or agency providing the information.
