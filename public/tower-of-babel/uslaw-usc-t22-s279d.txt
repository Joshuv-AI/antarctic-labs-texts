22 U.S.C. § 279d — Limitation on power of Conference to impose new obligations on United States
Citation: us:usc:22:279d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section279d
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter VI (United Nations Food And Agriculture Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In adopting this subchapter the Congress does so with the understanding that paragraph 2 of article XIII does not authorize the Conference of the Organization to so modify the provisions of its Constitution as to involve any new obligation for the United States.
