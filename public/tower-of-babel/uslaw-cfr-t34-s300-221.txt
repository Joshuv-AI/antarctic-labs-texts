34 C.F.R. § 300.221 — Notification of LEA or State agency in case of ineligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:34:300.221
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.221
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 300 (Assistance To States For The Education Of Children With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the SEA determines that an LEA or State agency is not eligible under Part B of the Act, then the SEA must—
(a) Notify the LEA or State agency of that determination; and
(b) Provide the LEA or State agency with reasonable notice and an opportunity for a hearing.
