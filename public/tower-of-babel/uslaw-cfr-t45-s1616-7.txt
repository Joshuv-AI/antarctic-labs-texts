45 C.F.R. § 1616.7 — Language ability.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1616.7
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1616.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1616 (Attorney Hiring). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In areas where a significant number of clients speak a language other than English as their principal language, a recipient shall adopt employment policies that insure that legal assistance will be provided in the language spoken by such clients.
