27 C.F.R. § 21.105-T1 — -T1 Cornmint oil (Mentha arvensis and Mentha canadensis).
Citation: us:cfr:27:21.105-T1
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-21.105-T1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 21 (Formulas For Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Specific gravity at 25 °C. 0.895 to 0.905.
(b) Refractive index at 20 °C. 1.4580 to 1.4590.
(c) Optical rotation at 20 °C. −18° to −36°.
(d) Alcohol content (as menthol). 65 percent minimum.
(e) Ketone content (as menthone). 5 percent minimum.
