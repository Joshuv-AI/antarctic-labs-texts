31 C.F.R. § 363.204 — What registrations are available for my marketable Treasury securities held in TreasuryDirect ®?
Citation: us:cfr:31:363.204
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-363.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 363 (Regulations Governing Securities Held In Treasurydirect). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may register your marketable Treasury securities in any form of registration permitted by § 363.20 of this part.
