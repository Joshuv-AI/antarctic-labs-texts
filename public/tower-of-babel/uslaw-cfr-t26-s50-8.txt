26 C.F.R. § 50.8 — Due date and place for filing returns and paying tax.
Citation: us:cfr:26:50.8
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-50.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 50 (Regulations Relating To The Tax Imposed With Respect To Certain Hydraulic Mining). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The return for a taxable year shall be filed with, and the tax shall be paid to, the district director at San Francisco, California, on or before September 30 of the calendar year in which the taxable year ends. The tax is due and payable on such date without assessment by, or notice from, the district director.
