Irreducible components of Toric Complete Intersections
Andrey Zhizhin

Abstract
An equivariant linear system on a toric variety is a linear system invariant under the torus action. We study the number of irreducible components of the complete intersection of general divisors from a fixed collection of equivariant linear system on a toric variety X X . An explicit formula for the number of components was obtained in [ KH16 ] for the case X = T n X=T^{n} over ℂ \mathbb{C} and generalized to an algebraically closed field of arbitrary characteristic in [ Zhi24 ] . Building on these results, we give a recursive formula for an arbitrary toric variety.

Equivariant Linear Systems

The ultimate goal of this section is to define the combinatorial datum of an equivariant linear system on a toric variety and then to develop the technique for restricting that datum to oribts of the toric variety. In the first two subsections we discuss the general notion of divisor pullback (
