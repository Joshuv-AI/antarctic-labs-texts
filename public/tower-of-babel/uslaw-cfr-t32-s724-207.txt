32 C.F.R. § 724.207 — Disposition of applications for discharge review.
Citation: us:cfr:32:724.207
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-724.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 724 (Naval Discharge Review Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

One of three dispositions will be made of an application for review of a discharge:
(a) The application may be rejected for reason of:
(1) Absence of jurisdiction;
(2) Previous review on the same evidence; or
(b) The application may be withdrawn by the applicant; or
(c) The application may be accepted and the discharge reviewed by the NDRB, resulting in,
(1) Change to the discharge, or
(2) No change.
