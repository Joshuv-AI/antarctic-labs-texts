48 C.F.R. § 6105.506 — 6105.506 Reconsideration of Board decision [Rule 506].
Citation: us:cfr:48:6105.506
Creator: Chapter 61—Civilian Board Of Contract Appeals, General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-6105.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 6105 (Decisions Authorized Under 31 U.S.C. 3529). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency or the affected employee may request reconsideration. A request must be received by the Board within 30 calendar days after the date the decision was issued. The request should state the reasons why the Board should consider the request. Mere disagreement with a decision or re-argument of points already made is not a sufficient ground for reconsideration.
