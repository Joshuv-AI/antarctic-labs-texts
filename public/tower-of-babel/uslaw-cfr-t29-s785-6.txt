29 C.F.R. § 785.6 — Definition of “employ” and partial definition of “hours worked”.
Citation: us:cfr:29:785.6
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-785.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 785 (Hours Worked). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

By statutory definition the term “employ” includes (section 3(g)) “to suffer or permit to work.” The act, however, contains no definition of “work”. Section 3(o) of the Fair Labor Standards Act contains a partial definition of “hours worked” in the form of a limited exception for clothes-changing and wash-up time.
