49 C.F.R. § 260.29 — Third party consultants.
Citation: us:cfr:49:260.29
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-260.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 260 (Regulations Governing Loans And Loan Guarantees Under The Railroad Rehabilitation And Improvement Financing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants may utilize independent third-party consultants to prepare a financial evaluation of the proposed project and the applicant, if approved by FRA. Providing such an evaluation would greatly assist FRA in the evaluation of the application and would significantly reduce the time necessary for FRA to process the application. We encourage the use of third party consultants.
