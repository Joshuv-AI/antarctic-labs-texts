5 C.F.R. § 185.141 — Stay pending appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:5:185.141
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-185.141
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 185 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An initial decision is stayed automatically pending disposition of a motion for reconsideration or of an appeal to the authority head.
(b) No administrative stay is available following a final decision of the authority head.
