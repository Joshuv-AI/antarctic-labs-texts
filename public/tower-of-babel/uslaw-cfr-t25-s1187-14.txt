25 C.F.R. § 1187.14 — What additional items must a joint application include?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1187.14
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1187.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1187 (Indian Business Incubators Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A joint application must:
(a) Contain a certification that each participant of the joint project is an eligible entity under § 1187.3;
(b) Demonstrate that together the participants meet the requirements of § 1187.13; and
(c) Identify which of the entities submitting the joint application will be the lead contact for the purposes of grant management.
