38 C.F.R. § 17.130 — Payment for treatment dependent upon preference prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:38:17.130
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-17.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 17 (Medical). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No reimbursement or payment of services not previously authorized will be made when such treatment was procured through private sources in preference to available Government facilities.
