6 U.S.C. § 321p — National planning and education
Citation: us:usc:6:321p
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section321p
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter V (National Emergency Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall, to the extent practicable—
(1)
include in national planning frameworks the threat of an EMP or GMD event; and
(2)
conduct outreach to educate owners and operators of critical infrastructure, emergency planners, and emergency response providers at all levels of government regarding threats of EMP and GMD.
