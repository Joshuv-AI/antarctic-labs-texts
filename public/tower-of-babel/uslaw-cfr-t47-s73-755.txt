47 C.F.R. § 73.755 — Modulation monitors.
Citation: us:cfr:47:73.755
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-73.755
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 73 (Radio Broadcast Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The licensee of each international broadcast station shall have a modulation monitor in operation at the transmitter.
