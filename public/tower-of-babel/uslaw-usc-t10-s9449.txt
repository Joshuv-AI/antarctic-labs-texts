10 U.S.C. § 9449 — Cadets: organization; service; instruction
Citation: us:usc:10:9449
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9449
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 953 (United States Air Force Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
A cadet shall perform duties at such places and of such type as the President may direct.
(b)
The course of instruction at the Academy is four years.
(c)
The Secretary of the Air Force shall so arrange the course of studies at the Academy that cadets are not required to pursue their studies on Sunday.
