12 C.F.R. § 150.420 — How long must I keep these records?
Citation: us:cfr:12:150.420
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-150.420
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 150 (Fiduciary Powers Of Federal Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must keep fiduciary records for three years after the termination of the account or the termination of any litigation relating to the account, whichever is later.
