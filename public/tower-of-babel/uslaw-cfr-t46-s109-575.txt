46 C.F.R. § 109.575 — Accumulation of liquids on helicopter decks.
Citation: us:cfr:46:109.575
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-109.575
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 109 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The master or person in charge shall ensure that no liquids are allowed to accumulate on the helicopter decks.
