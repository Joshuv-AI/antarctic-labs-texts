19 U.S.C. § 4209 — Application of certain provisions
Citation: us:usc:19:4209
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4209
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 27 (Bipartisan Congressional Trade Priorities And Accountability). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of applying sections 2135, 2136, and 2137 of this title—
(1)
any trade agreement entered into under section 4202 of this title shall be treated as an agreement entered into under section 2111 or 2112 of this title, as appropriate; and
(2)
any proclamation or Executive order issued pursuant to a trade agreement entered into under section 4202 of this title shall be treated as a proclamation or Executive order issued pursuant to a trade agreement entered into under section 2112 of this title.
