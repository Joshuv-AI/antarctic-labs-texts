29 U.S.C. § 559 — Rented quarters
Citation: us:usc:29:559
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section559
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 12 (Department Of Labor). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Where any office, bureau, or branch of the public service transferred to the Department of Labor by this Act is occupying rented buildings or premises, it may continue to do so until other suitable quarters are provided for its use.
