49 U.S.C. § 31302 — Commercial driver’s license requirement
Citation: us:usc:49:31302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section31302
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Commercial); Chapter 313 (Commercial Motor Vehicle Operators). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No individual shall operate a commercial motor vehicle without a valid commercial driver’s license issued in accordance with section 31308. An individual operating a commercial motor vehicle may have only one driver’s license at any time and may have only one learner’s permit at any time.
