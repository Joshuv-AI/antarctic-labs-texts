32 C.F.R. § 158.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:32:158.2
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-158.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 158 (Operational Contract Support (Ocs) Outside The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to contracts and contractor personnel supporting DoD Components operating outside the United States in contingency operations, humanitarian assistance, or peace operations and other activities, including operations and exercises as determined by a Combatant Commander or as directed by the Secretary of Defense.
