16 U.S.C. § 80d–1 — Use of appropriations for road construction
Citation: us:usc:16:80d–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section80d–1
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter VIII (Kings Canyon National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After June 22, 1946, no part of appropriations made for the National Park Service shall be available for road construction in Kings Canyon National Park, California, except on the floor of the canyon of the South Fork of the Kings River and the Grant Grove section of that park.
