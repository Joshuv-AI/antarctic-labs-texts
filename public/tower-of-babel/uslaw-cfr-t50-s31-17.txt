50 C.F.R. § 31.17 — Disposal of furs and pelts.
Citation: us:cfr:50:31.17
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-31.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 31 (Wildlife Species Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The disposition of animals and the pelts or carcasses thereof accruing to the United States through the trapping programs shall be sold by public auction or on the open market unless required for official purposes.
