48 C.F.R. § 1901.404 — 1901.404 Class deviations.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1901.404
Creator: Chapter 19—Broadcasting Board Of Governors
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1901.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1901 (The Broadcasting Board Of Governors Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Class deviations affecting more than one contracting action shall be authorized only by the Board Procurement Executive, unless FAR 1.405(e) is applicable, and shall be subject to the limitations set forth in FAR 1.404. Requests shall include the same information as cited in 1901.403.
