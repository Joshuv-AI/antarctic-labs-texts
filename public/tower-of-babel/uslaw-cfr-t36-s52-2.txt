36 C.F.R. § 52.2 — What is the purpose of a commercial services contract?
Citation: us:cfr:36:52.2
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-52.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 52 (Visitor Experience Improvements Authority Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The National Park Service (NPS) will use commercial services contracts to expand, modernize, and improve the condition of commercial facilities and commercial services provided to visitors in a park area. Commercial services contracts are limited to those that are necessary and appropriate for public use and enjoyment of the park area in which they are located and consistent with the preservation and conservation of the resources and values of the park area.
