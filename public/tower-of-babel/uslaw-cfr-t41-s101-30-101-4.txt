41 C.F.R. § 101-30.101-4 — -30.101-4 Federal item identification.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-30.101-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-30 (30—Federal Catalog System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal item identification means the approved item identification for the item of supply, plus the national stock number assigned to that item identification. It consists of four basic elements: The name of the item, the identifying characteristics, the Federal Supply Classification code, and the national item identification number.
