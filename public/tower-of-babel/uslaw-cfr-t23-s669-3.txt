23 C.F.R. § 669.3 — Policy.
Citation: us:cfr:23:669.3
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-669.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 669 (Enforcement Of Heavy Vehicle Use Tax). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the policy of the FHWA that each state require registrants of heavy trucks as described in 26 CFR part 41 to provide proof of payment of the vehicle use tax either before lawfully registering or within 4 months after lawfully registering such vehicles as provided for under a suspension registration system.
