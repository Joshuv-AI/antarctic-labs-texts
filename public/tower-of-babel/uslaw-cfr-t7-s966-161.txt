7 C.F.R. § 966.161 — Reapportionment of Committee Membership.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-966.161
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 966 (Tomatoes Grown In Florida). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to § 966.25, industry membership on the Florida Tomato Committee shall be reapportioned as follows:
(a) District 1—five members and their alternates.
(b) District 2—five members and their alternates.
