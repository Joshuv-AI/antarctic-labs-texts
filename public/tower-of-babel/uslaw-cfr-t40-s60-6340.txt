40 C.F.R. § 60.6340 — What must I include in the notifications of achievement of my increments of progress?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.6340
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.6340
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your notification of achievement of increments of progress must include three items:
(a) Notification that the increment of progress has been achieved.
(b) Any items required to be submitted with the increment of progress (§§ 60.6355 through 60.6375).
(c) The notification must be signed by the owner or operator of the municipal waste combustor.
