41 C.F.R. § 302-9.300 — -9.300 Eligibility for transportation of a POV within CONUS at Government expense.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-9.300
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-9.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-9 (9—Allowances For Transportation And Emergency Or Temporary Storage Of A Privately Owned Vehicle). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies may pay for transportation of a POV within CONUS at Government expense when the distance that the POV is to be shipped is 600 miles or more, and the employee is:
(a) An employee who transfers within CONUS in the interest of the Government; or
(b) A new appointee or student trainee relocating to the first official station within CONUS.
(c) Employees may be authorized to transport only the number of POVs equal to the number of people on the relocation travel orders, who are licensed drivers, not to exceed two, while relocating within CONUS.
