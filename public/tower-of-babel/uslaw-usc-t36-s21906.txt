36 U.S.C. § 21906 — Headquarters and meetings
Citation: us:usc:36:21906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section21906
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 219 (The American National Theater And Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may have its headquarters and hold its meetings at places the corporation decides are best.
