2 C.F.R. § 3485.937 — ED Deciding Official.
Citation: us:cfr:2:3485.937
Creator: Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-3485.937
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 3485 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ED Deciding Official is an officer of the Department who has delegated authority under the procedures of the Department of Education to decide whether to affirm a suspension or enter a debarment.
