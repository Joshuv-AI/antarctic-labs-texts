40 C.F.R. § 305.10 — Appearances.
Citation: us:cfr:40:305.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-305.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 305 (Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, And Liability Act (Cercla) Administrative Hearing Procedures For Claims Against The Superfund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party may appear in person or by counsel or other representative. A partner may appear on behalf of a partnership and an officer may appear on behalf of a corporation. Persons who appear as counsel or other representative must conform to the standards of conduct and ethics required of practitioners before the courts of the United States.
