30 C.F.R. § 36.50 — Tests of fuel tank.
Citation: us:cfr:30:36.50
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-36.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 36 (Approval Requirements For Permissible Mobile Diesel-Powered Transportation Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The fuel tank shall be inspected and tested to determine whether: (a) It is fuel-tight, (b) the vent maintains atmospheric pressure within the tank, and (c) the vent and closure restrict the outflow of liquid fuel.
