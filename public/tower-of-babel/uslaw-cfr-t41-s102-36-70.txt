41 C.F.R. § 102-36.70 — -36.70 Acquiring excess personal property for non-Federal activities.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-36.70
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-36.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-36 (36—Disposition Of Excess Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may acquire and furnish excess personal property for use by your nonappropriated fund activities, contractors, cooperatives, and project grantees, and other eligible recipients when you have specific statutory authority to do so.
