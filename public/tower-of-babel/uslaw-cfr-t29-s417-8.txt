29 C.F.R. § 417.8 — Appearances.
Citation: us:cfr:29:417.8
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-417.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 417 (Procedure For Removal Of Local Labor Organization Officers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department of Labor does not maintain a register of persons or attorneys who may participate at hearings. Any interested person may appear and be heard in person or be represented by counsel.
