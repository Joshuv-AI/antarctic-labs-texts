21 C.F.R. § 582.3336 — Gum guaiac.
Citation: us:cfr:21:582.3336
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-582.3336
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 582 (Substances Generally Recognized As Safe). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Product. Gum guaiac.
(b) Tolerance. 0.1 percent (equivalent antioxidant activity 0.01 percent).
(c) Limitations, restrictions, or explanation. This substance is generally recognized as safe when used in edible fats or oils in accordance with good manufacturing or feeding practice.
