36 C.F.R. § 21.12 — Lost bath tickets.
Citation: us:cfr:36:21.12
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-21.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 21 (Hot Springs National Park; Bathhouse Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A patron who loses his ticket may continue to receive service, without additional charge, for the number of units remaining in the ticket. Records of lost tickets, and of service given thereunder, shall be maintained as required by the Superintendent. Lost tickets shall have no redemption value.
