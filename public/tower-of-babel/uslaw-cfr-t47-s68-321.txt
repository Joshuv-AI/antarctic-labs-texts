47 C.F.R. § 68.321 — Location of responsible party.
Citation: us:cfr:47:68.321
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-68.321
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 68 (Connection Of Terminal Equipment To The Telephone Network). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The responsible party for a Supplier's Declaration of Conformity must designate an agent for service of process that is physically located within the United States.
