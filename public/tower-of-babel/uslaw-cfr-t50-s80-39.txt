50 C.F.R. § 80.39 — What must a State fish and wildlife agency do if it becomes aware of errors in its certified license data?
Citation: us:cfr:50:80.39
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-80.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 80 (Administrative Requirements, Pittman-Robertson Wildlife Restoration And Dingell-Johnson Sport Fish Restoration Acts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State fish and wildlife agency must submit revised certified data on license holders within 90 days after it becomes aware of errors in its certified data. The State may become ineligible to participate in the benefits of the relevant Act if the State becomes aware of errors in its certified data and does not resubmit accurate certified data within 90 days.
