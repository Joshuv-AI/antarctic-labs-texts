32 C.F.R. § 724.118 — Documentary discharge review.
Citation: us:cfr:32:724.118
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-724.118
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 724 (Naval Discharge Review Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A formal session of the NDRB convened for the purpose of reviewing, on the basis of documentary data, an applicant's discharge. The Documentary data shall include the application together with all information accompanying that application, available service records, and any other information considered relevant by the NDRB.
