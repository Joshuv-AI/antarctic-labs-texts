47 C.F.R. § 17.54 — Rated lamp voltage.
Citation: us:cfr:47:17.54
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-17.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 17 (Construction, Marking, And Lighting Of Antenna Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To insure the necessary lumen output by obstruction lights, the rated voltage of incandescent lamps used shall correspond to be within 3 percent higher than the voltage across the lamp socket during the normal hours of operation.
