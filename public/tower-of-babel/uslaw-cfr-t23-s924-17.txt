23 C.F.R. § 924.17 — MIRE fundamental data elements.
Citation: us:cfr:23:924.17
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-924.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 924 (Highway Safety Improvement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The MIRE fundamental data elements shall be collected on all public roads, as listed in Tables 1, 2, and 3 of this section. For the purpose of MIRE fundamental data elements applicability, the term open to public travel is consistent with 23 CFR 460.2(c).
