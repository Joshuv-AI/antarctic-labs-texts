14 C.F.R. § 257.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:257.2
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-257.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 257 (Disclosure Of Code-Sharing Arrangements And Long-Term Wet Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to the following:
(a) Direct air carriers and foreign air carriers that participate in code-sharing arrangements or long-term wet leases involving scheduled passenger air transportation; and
(b) Ticket agents doing business in the United States that sell scheduled passenger air transportation services involving code-sharing arrangements or long-term wet leases.
