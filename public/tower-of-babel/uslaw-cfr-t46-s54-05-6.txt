46 C.F.R. § 54.05-6 — -6 Toughness test temperatures.
Citation: us:cfr:46:54.05-6
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-54.05-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 54 (Pressure Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each toughness test must be conducted at temperatures not warmer than −20 °F or 10 °F below the minimum service temperature, whichever is lower, except that for service at or below −320 °F, the tests may be conducted at the service temperature in accordance with § 54.25-10(a)(2).
