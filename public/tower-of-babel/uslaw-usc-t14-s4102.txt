14 U.S.C. § 4102 — Penalty
Citation: us:usc:14:4102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section4102
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 41 (General Provisions For Coast Guard Reserve And Auxiliary). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, without proper authority, flies from any building, aircraft, motorboat, yacht, or other vessel, any flag or pennant or displays any identifying insignia or wears any uniform or insignia of the Reserve or the Auxiliary shall be fined not more than $500.
