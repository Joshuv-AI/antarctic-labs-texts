50 C.F.R. § 23.8 — What are the information collection requirements?
Citation: us:cfr:50:23.8
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-23.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 23 (Convention On International Trade In Endangered Species Of Wild Fauna And Flora (Cites)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Management and Budget approved the information collection requirements for application forms and reports contained in this part and assigned OMB Control Number 1018-0093. We cannot collect or sponsor a collection of information and you are not required to provide information unless it displays a currently valid OMB control number.
