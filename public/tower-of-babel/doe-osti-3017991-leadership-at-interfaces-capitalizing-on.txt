Parting advice and inspiration from interfacial philosophies
• Critical thinking matters now more than ever
• Be 10% more skeptical of people you agree with—and
  10% more charitable to people you disagree with
 • Thoughtful disagreement doesn’t start with “You’re wrong!”
 It begins with “I’d love to understand your thinking better”
  Let ideas flow like music: remember, harmony is the
 pleasing arrangement of different tones, voices, or
 instruments—not the combination of identical sounds
• Make a list of your values, and revisit it every year
• Stay present, notice more, focus on being (vs. doing)
 • Insights flow naturally and effortlessly when you are
 present, well-rested and leaning into your inner craft
 • Give ourselves and others the privilege of our own process
