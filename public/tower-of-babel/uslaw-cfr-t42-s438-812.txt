42 C.F.R. § 438.812 — Costs under risk and nonrisk contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:42:438.812
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-438.812
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 438 (Managed Care). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Under a risk contract, the total amount the State agency pays for carrying out the contract provisions is a medical assistance cost.
(b) Under a nonrisk contract—
(1) The amount the State agency pays for the furnishing of medical services to eligible beneficiaries is a medical assistance cost; and
(2) The amount the State agency pays for the contractor's performance of other functions is an administrative cost.
