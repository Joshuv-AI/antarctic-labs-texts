31 C.F.R. § 34.301 — Responsibility for administration—Direct Component.
Citation: us:cfr:31:34.301
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-34.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 34 (Resources And Ecosystems Sustainability, Tourist Opportunities, And Revived Economies Of The Gulf Coast States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Treasury is responsible for awarding and administering grants and grant agreements under this subpart. Treasury will develop and apply policies and procedures consistent with the Act and Federal laws and policies on grants. Treasury also will establish and implement a program to monitor compliance with its grant agreements.
