8 U.S.C. § 1524 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:8:1524
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1524
Context: United States Code, Title 8 (Aliens And Nationality); Chapter 12 (Immigration And Nationality); Subchapter IV (Refugee Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There are authorized to be appropriated for each of fiscal years 2000 through 2002 such sums as may be necessary to carry out this subchapter.
(b)
The authority to enter into contracts under this subchapter shall be effective for any fiscal year only to such extent or in such amounts as are provided in advance in appropriation Acts.
