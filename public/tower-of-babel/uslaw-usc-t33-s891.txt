33 U.S.C. § 891 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:33:891
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section891
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 17 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration); Subchapter III (Noaa Fleet Modernization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter, the term—
(1)
“NOAA” means the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration within the Department of Commerce.
(2)
“NOAA fleet” means the fleet of research vessels owned or operated by NOAA.
(3)
“Plan” means the NOAA Fleet Replacement and Modernization Plan described in section 891b of this title.
(4)
“Secretary” means the Secretary of Commerce.
(5)
“UNOLS” means University-National Oceanographic Laboratory System.
