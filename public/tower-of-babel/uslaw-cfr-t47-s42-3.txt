47 C.F.R. § 42.3 — Protection and storage of records.
Citation: us:cfr:47:42.3
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-42.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 42 (Preservation Of Records Of Communication Common Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The carrier shall protect records subject to the regulations in this part from damage from fires, and other hazards and, in the selection of storage spaces, safeguard the records from unnecessary exposure to deterioration.
