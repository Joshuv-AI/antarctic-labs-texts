5 C.F.R. § 2638.602 — Agency regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2638.602
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2638.602
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2638 (Executive Branch Ethics Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each agency may, subject to the prior approval of the Office of Government Ethics, issue regulations not inconsistent with this part and this subchapter, using the procedures set forth in § 2635.105 of this chapter.
