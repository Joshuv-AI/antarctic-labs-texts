5 C.F.R. § 2638.202 — Furnishing records and information generally.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2638.202
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2638.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2638 (Executive Branch Ethics Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consistent with sections 402 and 403 of the Act, each agency must furnish to the Director all information and records in its possession which the Director deems necessary to the performance of the Director's duties, except to the extent prohibited by law. All such information and records must be provided to the Office of Government Ethics in a complete and timely manner.
