14 C.F.R. § 93.315 — Requirements for commercial Special Flight Rules Area operations.
Citation: us:cfr:14:93.315
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-93.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 93 (Special Air Traffic Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each person conducting commercial Special Flight Rules Area operations must be certificated in accordance with Part 119 for Part 135 or 121 operations and hold appropriate Grand Canyon National Park Special Flight Rules Area operations specifications.
