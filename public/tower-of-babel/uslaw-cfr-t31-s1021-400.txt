31 C.F.R. § 1021.400 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:31:1021.400
Creator: Financial Crimes Enforcement Network, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-1021.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 1021 (Rules For Casinos And Card Clubs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Casinos and card clubs are subject to the recordkeeping requirements set forth and cross referenced in this subpart. Casinos and card clubs should also refer to subpart D of part 1010 of this chapter for recordkeeping requirements contained in that subpart which apply to casinos and card clubs.
