49 C.F.R. § 835.8 — Testimony by current Board employees regarding prior activity.
Citation: us:cfr:49:835.8
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-835.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 835 (Testimony Of Board Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any testimony regarding any accident within the Board's jurisdiction, or any expert testimony arising from employment prior to Board service is prohibited absent approval by the General Counsel. Approval shall only be given if testimony will not violate § 835.1 and § 835.3, and is subject to whatever conditions the General Counsel finds necessary to promote the purposes of this part as set forth in § 835.1 and § 835.3.
