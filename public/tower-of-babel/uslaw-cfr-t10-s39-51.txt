10 C.F.R. § 39.51 — Use of a sealed source in a well without a surface casing.
Citation: us:cfr:10:39.51
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-39.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 39 (Licenses And Radiation Safety Requirements For Well Logging). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The licensee may use a sealed source in a well without a surface casing for protecting fresh water aquifers only if the licensee follows a procedure for reducing the probability of the source becoming lodged in the well. The procedure must be approved by the Commission pursuant to § 39.13(c) or by an Agreement State.
