Higher-order bulk photovoltaic effects, quantum geometry and application to p p -wave magnets
Motohiko Ezawa

Abstract
The injection and shift currents are generalized to the ℓ \ell th-order injection and shift currents for the longitudinal conductivities in the two-band model, where ℓ \ell is the power of the applied electric field. In addition, the formulas for the higher-order injection current are expressed in terms of the quantum metric and the higher-order shift current in terms of the higher-order quantum connection. Then, they are applied to p p -wave magnets. It is shown that the injection and shift currents are zero. On the other hand, the ℓ \ell th-order injection and shift currents with odd ℓ \ell are nonzero when the direction of the Néel vector of the p p -wave magnet points to an in-plane direction.

IV.1
Injection current

The injection current at the zero temperature is calculated in the
Hamiltonian (
63

\sigma_{\text{inject}}^{x;x^{2}}=-\tau\frac{2\pi e^{3}}{\hbar^{2}}\sum_{k_{x}}\Delta_{+-}^{x}g_{xx}\frac{\delta\left(k_{x}-k_{\pm}\right)}{2\left|\partial_{k_{x}}\omega_{+-}\right|}.

(78)

By using the Hamiltonian (
63
), we have

\displaystyle\sum_{k_{x}}

\displaystyle\Delta_{+-}^{x}g_{xx}\frac{\delta\left(k_{x}-k_{0}\right)}{2\left|\partial_{k_{x}}\omega_{+-}\right|}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{k_{x}=k_{\pm}}\frac{2\left(J^{2}k_{x}+BJ_{z}\right)}{\sqrt{B^{2}+J^{2}k_{x}^{2}+2BJ_{z}}}\frac{B^{2}J_{\parallel}^{2}}{4\left(B^{2}+J^{2}k_{x}^{2}+2BJ_{z}\right)^{2}}

\displaystyle\times\frac{\sqrt{B^{2}+J^{2}k_{x}^{2}+2BJ_{z}}}{4\left|J^{2}k_{x}+BJ_{z}\right|}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{k_{x}=k_{\pm}}\frac{\left(J^{2}k_{x}+BJ_{z}\right)}{2\left|J^{2}k_{x}+BJ_{z}\right|}\frac{B^{2}J_{\parallel}^{2}}{4\left(B^{2}+J^{2}k_{x}^{2}+2BJ_{z}\right)^{2}},

(79)

where we have used

\Delta_{+=}^{x}=\frac{2\left(J^{2}k_{x}+BJ_{z}\right)}{\sqrt{B^{2}+J^{2}k_{x}^{2}+2BJ_{z}}}.

(80)

By using

B^{2}+J^{2}k_{0}^{2}+2BJ_{z}=\frac{\omega^{2}}{4},

(81)

and

2\left(J^{2}k_{0}+BJ_{z}\right)=\eta\sqrt{J^{2}\omega^{2}-4B^{2}J_{\parallel}^{2}},

(82)

we have

\displaystyle\sum_{k_{x}}\Delta_{mn}^{x}g_{xx}\delta\left(\omega_{+-}-\omega\right)

\displaystyle=\sum_{\eta=\pm}\frac{\eta\sqrt{J^{2}\omega^{2}-4B^{2}J_{\parallel}^{2}}}{2\left|\eta\sqrt{J^{2}\omega^{2}-4B^{2}J_{\parallel}^{2}}\right|}\frac{B^{2}J_{\parallel}^{2}}{4\left(\frac{\omega^{2}}{4}\right)^{2}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{\eta=\pm}\eta\frac{2B^{2}J_{\parallel}^{2}}{\omega^{4}}=0.

(83)

Then, the injection current is exactly zero.
