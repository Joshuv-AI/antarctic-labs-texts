15 U.S.C. § 80b–17 — Penalties
Citation: us:usc:15:80b–17
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section80b–17
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2D (Investment Companies And Advisers); Subchapter II (Investment Advisers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who willfully violates any provision of this subchapter, or any rule, regulation, or order promulgated by the Commission under authority thereof, shall, upon conviction, be fined not more than $10,000, imprisoned for not more than five years, or both.
