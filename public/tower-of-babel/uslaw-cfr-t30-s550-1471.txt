30 C.F.R. § 550.1471 — Does the penalty affect whether I owe interest?
Citation: us:cfr:30:550.1471
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-550.1471
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 550 (Oil And Gas And Sulfur Operations In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you do not pay the penalty by the date required under § 550.1475(d), BOEM will assess you late payment interest on the penalty amount at the same rate interest is assessed under 30 CFR 1218.54.
