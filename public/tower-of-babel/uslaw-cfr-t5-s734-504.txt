5 C.F.R. § 734.504 — Contributions to political action committees through voluntary payroll allotments prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:5:734.504
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-734.504
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 734 (Political Activities Of Federal Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee described in § 734.502(a) may not financially contribute to a political action committee through a voluntary allotment made under § 550.311(b) of this title.
