46 C.F.R. § 202.2 — Time and place for filings.
Citation: us:cfr:46:202.2
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-202.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 202 (Procedures Relating To Review By Secretary Of Transportation Of Actions By Maritime Subsidy Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All petitions, requests and replies relating to Secretarial review of Maritime Subsidy Board actions shall be filed with the Office of the Secretary of Transportation, Department of Transportation. Such papers shall be filed in accordance with the provisions of and within the time periods prescribed by Department Order 117-A.
