17 C.F.R. § 260.4d-10 — d-10 Exemption for securities issued pursuant to § 230.802 of this chapter.
Citation: us:cfr:17:260.4d-10
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-260.4d-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 260 (General Rules And Regulations, Trust Indenture Act Of 1939). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any debt security, whether or not issued under an indenture, is exempt from the Act if made in compliance with § 230.802 of this chapter.
