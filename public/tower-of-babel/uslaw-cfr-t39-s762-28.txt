39 C.F.R. § 762.28 — Release of original disbursement postal money orders.
Citation: us:cfr:39:762.28
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-762.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 762 (Disbursement Postal Money Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An original Disbursement Postal Money Order may be released to a responsible endorser only upon receipt of a properly authorized request showing the reason it is required.
