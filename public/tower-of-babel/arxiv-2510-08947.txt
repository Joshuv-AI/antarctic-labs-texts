On positive solutions of Lane-Emden equations on the integer lattice graphs


Abstract
In this paper, we investigate the existence and nonexistence of positive solutions to the Lane-Emden equations − Δ ​ u = Q ​ | u | p − 2 ​ u -\Delta u=Q|u|^{p-2}u on the d d -dimensional integer lattice graph ℤ d \mathbb{Z}^{d} , as well as in the half-space and quadrant domains, under the zero Dirichlet boundary condition in the latter two cases. Here, d ≥ 2 d\geq 2 , p > 0 p>0 , and Q Q denotes a Hardy-type positive potential satisfying Q ⁡ ( x ) ∼ ( 1 + | x | ) − α Q(x)\sim(1+|x|)^{-\alpha} with α ∈ [ 0 , + ∞ ] \alpha\in[0,+\infty] . We identify the Sobolev super-critical regions of the parameter pair ( α , p ) (\alpha,p) for which the existence of positive solutions is established via variational methods. In contrast, within the Serrin sub-critical regions of ( α , p ) (\alpha,p) , we demonstrate nonexistence by iteratively analyzing the decay behavior at infinity, ultimately leading to a contradiction. Notably, in the full-space and half-space domains, there exists an intermediate regions between the Sobolev critical line and the Serrin critical line where the existence of positive solutions remains an open question. Such an intermediate region does not exist in the quadrant domain.

Part
(iii)
.
The proof is similar to the one of Proposition
4.1
. Suppose that, by contradiction, there is a positive function verifying (
1.1
) according to the maximum principle.

We claim that there exists
c_{0}>0
and
n_{0}\geq 1
such that

u_{0}(x)\geq c_{0}x_{1}x_{2}(1+|x|)^{-d-2}\hskip 9.24994pt{\rm for\ all}\ \,x\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}_{*}.

From the comparison principle, there exists
d_{0}>0
and
n_{0}\geq 1
such that

u_{0}(x)\geq\frac{u_{0}(e_{11})}{\Phi_{d,+}(e_{11},e_{11})}\,\Phi_{d,*}(x,e_{11})\geq d_{0}(1+|x|)^{-d}\hskip 9.24994pt{\rm for}\ \,x\in{\cal A}_{1}\cap\mathbb{Z}^{d}.

Let
\tau_{0}=-d<0
satisfy that

-\Delta u_{0}(x)\geq d_{0}^{q}x_{1}^{q}x_{2}^{q}|x|^{-\alpha-q(d+2)}\geq d_{0}^{q}|x|^{\tau_{1}},\hskip 9.24994pt\forall\,x\in({\cal A}_{1}\cap\mathbb{Z}^{d})\setminus B_{n_{0}},

(6.14)

where

q=p-1,\hskip 9.24994pt\tau_{1}=-qd-\alpha.

Thus, for
q\in(0,\frac{d-\alpha}{d})
, it holds that

\tau_{1}-\tau_{0}=-qd-\alpha+d>0.

Case 1:
q\in(0,\frac{-\alpha}{d})
with
\alpha\in(-\infty,0)
.
Note that
-qd-\alpha\geq 0
, then

Q(x)u_{0}(x)^{q}\geq d_{0}^{q}(1+|x|)^{(-d)q-\alpha},\hskip 9.24994pt\forall x\in({\cal A}_{1}\cap\mathbb{Z}^{d})\setminus B_{n_{0}}

(6.15)

and a contradiction follows by Lemma
6.2
with
f(x)=d_{0}^{q}(1+|x|)^{q(-d)-\alpha}
.

Case 2:
q\in\big(\frac{-\alpha}{d},\frac{d-\alpha}{d}\big)\cap(0,+\infty)
with
\alpha\in(-\infty,d)
.

By Proposition
4.1
, there exists
d_{1}>0
such that

u_{0}(x)\geq d_{1}(1+|x|)^{\tau_{1}},\hskip 9.24994pt\forall\,x\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}\cap{\cal A}_{1},

where

\tau_{1}:=-qd-\alpha\in(-d,0)
.

Recall that

\tau_{j+1}:=q\tau_{j}-\alpha,\hskip 9.24994pt\forall\,j\in\mathbb{N}_{+},

which is an increasing sequence.

If
\tau_{j+1}=\tau_{j}q-\alpha\in(-d,0)
,
it follows by Proposition
4.1
that there exist integer
d_{j}>0
such that

u_{0}(x)\geq d_{j}(1+|x|)^{\tau_{j+1}}\hskip 9.24994pt{\rm in}\ \,{\cal A}_{1}\cap\mathbb{Z}^{d}.

If
q\tau_{j+1}-\alpha\geq 0
, we are done by Lemma
6.2
.

Now we claim that the iteration must stop after a finite number of times. It infers by Lemma
4.4
that

j\mapsto\tau_{j}
is strictly increasing thanks to
0<q<\frac{d-\alpha}{d}.

Note that for
q\in[1,+\infty)\cap\big(\frac{-\alpha}{d},\frac{d-\alpha}{d}\big)
,
\tau_{j}\to+\infty
, then there exists
j_{0}\in\mathbb{N}
such that

q\tau_{j_{0}+1}-\alpha\geq 0
and a contradiction could be derived for

1\leq q<\frac{d-\alpha}{d}
.

For
q\in(0,1)\cap\big(\frac{-\alpha}{d},\frac{d-\alpha}{d}\big)
,
\tau_{j}\to\tilde{\tau}_{q}:=\frac{-\alpha}{1-q}>0
as
j\to+\infty
, then there exists
j_{0}\in\mathbb{N}
such that

q\tau_{j_{0}}-\alpha\leq 0
and
q\tau_{j_{0}+1}-\alpha\geq 0
. This means we can get a contradiction and we are done.

Case 3:
q=\frac{d-\alpha}{d}>1

From (
6.15
), we have that

Q(x)u_{0}(x)^{q}\geq d_{0}^{q}(1+|x|)^{(-d)q-\alpha},\hskip 9.24994pt\forall x\in({\cal A}_{1}\cap\mathbb{Z}^{d})\setminus B_{n_{0}}.

(6.16)

v_{1}=\Phi_{d,+}\ast\big(e+|\cdot|)^{-d}\ln(e+|\cdot|)\chi_{{\cal A}_{1}\setminus B_{n_{0}}}\big)\hskip 9.24994pt{\rm for}\ \forall\,x\in\mathbb{Z}^{d}_{*}.

From Lemma
6.1

(ii)
