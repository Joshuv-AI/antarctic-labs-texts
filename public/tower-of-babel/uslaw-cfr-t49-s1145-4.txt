49 C.F.R. § 1145.4 — Negotiations.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1145.4
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1145.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1145 (Reciprocal Switching For Inadequate Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At least five days prior to petitioning for prescription of a reciprocal switching agreement hereunder, the petitioner must seek to engage in good faith negotiations to resolve its dispute with the incumbent rail carrier.
