31 C.F.R. § 370.46 — Are there any situations in which the Bureau of the Fiscal Service may waive these regulations?
Citation: us:cfr:31:370.46
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-370.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 370 (Electronic Transactions And Funds Transfers Relating To United States Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We reserve the right, in our discretion, to waive any provision of these regulations in any case or class of cases. We may do so if such action is not inconsistent with law and will not subject the United States to substantial expense or liability.
