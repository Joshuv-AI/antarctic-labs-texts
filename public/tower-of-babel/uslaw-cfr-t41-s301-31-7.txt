41 C.F.R. § 301-31.7 — -31.7 Expense tracking and documentation requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-31.7
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-31.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-31 (31—Threatened Law Enforcement/Investigative Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees must keep track of actual expenses as described in part 301-11 of this subchapter, and must provide receipts or any other documentation required by their agency for reimbursement. However, in instances when documentation might compromise the security of the individuals involved, the head of the agency may waive these requirements.
