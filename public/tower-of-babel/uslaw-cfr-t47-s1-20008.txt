47 C.F.R. § 1.20008 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:47:1.20008
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-1.20008
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 1 (Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event of a telecommunications carrier's violation of this subchapter, the Commission shall enforce the penalties articulated in 47 U.S.C. 503(b) of the Communications Act of 1934 and 47 CFR 1.80.
