30 C.F.R. § 942.784 — Underground mining permit applications—Minimum requirements for reclamation and operation plan.
Citation: us:cfr:30:942.784
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-942.784
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 942 (Tennessee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 784 of this chapter, Underground Mining Permit Applications—Minimum Requirements for Reclamation and Operation Plan, shall apply to any person who makes application for a permit to conduct underground coal mining operations.
