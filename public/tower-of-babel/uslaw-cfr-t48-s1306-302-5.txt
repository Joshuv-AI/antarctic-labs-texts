48 C.F.R. § 1306.302-5 — 1306.302-5 Authorized or required by statute.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1306.302-5
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1306.302-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1306 (Competition Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with Executive Order 13457, a sole source acquisition may not be justified on the basis of any earmark included in any non-statutory source, except when otherwise required by law or when an earmark meets the criteria for funding set out in Executive Order 13457.
