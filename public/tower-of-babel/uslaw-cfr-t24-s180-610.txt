24 C.F.R. § 180.610 — Waiver of right to appear.
Citation: us:cfr:24:180.610
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-180.610
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 180 (Consolidated Hud Hearing Procedures For Civil Rights Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If all parties waive their right to appear before the ALJ, the ALJ need not conduct an oral hearing. Such waivers shall be in writing and filed with the ALJ. The ALJ shall make a record of the pleadings and relevant written evidence submitted by the parties. These documents may constitute the evidence in the proceeding, and the decision may be based upon this evidence.
