7 C.F.R. § 3406.19 — Proposal review—research.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3406.19
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3406.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3406 (1890 Institution Capacity Building Grants Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The proposal evaluation process includes both internal staff review and merit evaluation by peer review panels comprised of scientists, educators, business representatives, and Government officials who are highly qualified to render expert advice in the areas supported. Peer review panels will be selected and structured to provide optimum expertise and objective judgment in the evaluation of proposals.
