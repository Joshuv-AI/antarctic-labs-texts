49 C.F.R. § 605.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:605.1
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-605.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 605 (School Bus Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to prescribe policies and procedures to implement the prohibition in 49 U.S.C. 5323(f) regarding the provision of school bus transportation service that exclusively transports students and school personnel in competition with a private school bus operator.
