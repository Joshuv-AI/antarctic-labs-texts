Comment on “Improved measurement of η / η ′ \eta/\eta^{\prime} mixing in B ( s ) 0 → J / ψ η ( ′ ) B_{(s)}^{0}\to J/\psi\eta^{(\prime)} decays”
Ying Chen, Geng Li

Abstract
Instead of the glueball- η ′ \eta^{\prime} mixing ansatz, the latest measured ratios of the branching fractions of B ( s ) 0 → J / ψ η ( ′ ) B_{(s)}^{0}\to J/\psi\eta^{(\prime)} decays by LHCb can be understood by including the contribution from the light quark annihilation effect enhanced by the QCD U A ​ ( 1 ) \mathrm{U}_{A}(1) anomaly for light pseudoscalar mesons.



Very recently, the LHCb collaboration reported their improved measurement of the ratios of the branching fractions of the decay processes
B_{(s)}^{0}\to J/\psi\eta^{(\prime)}

Aaij et al. 2025
. The precise results are

\displaystyle\frac{\mathcal{B}(B^{0}\to J/\psi\eta^{\prime})}{\mathcal{B}(B^{0}\to J/\psi\eta)}=0.48\pm 0.06,~~~\frac{\mathcal{B}(B^{0}\to J/\psi\eta)}{\mathcal{B}(B_{s}^{0}\to J/\psi\eta)}=(2.16\pm 0.18)\times 10^{-2},

(1)

\displaystyle\frac{\mathcal{B}(B_{s}^{0}\to J/\psi\eta^{\prime})}{\mathcal{B}(B_{s}^{0}\to J/\psi\eta)}=0.80\pm 0.03,~~~\frac{\mathcal{B}(B^{0}\to J/\psi\eta^{\prime})}{\mathcal{B}(B_{s}^{0}\to J/\psi\eta^{\prime})}=(1.33\pm 0.14)\times 10^{-2},

where the errors are added in quadrature. By assuming the possible
\eta^{\prime}
-glueball mixing, the authors of Ref.
Aaij et al. 2025

\eta/\eta^{\prime}
mixing angle
\phi_{P}

\eta^{\prime}
through the flavor wave functions of
\eta

\eta^{\prime}

;
Cheng et al. 2009

\displaystyle|\eta\rangle

\displaystyle=\cos\phi_{P}|\eta_{l}\rangle-\sin\phi_{P}|\eta_{s}\rangle,

(2)

\displaystyle|\eta^{\prime}\rangle

\displaystyle=\cos\phi_{G}\left(\sin\phi_{P}|\eta_{l}\rangle+\cos\phi_{P}|\eta_{s}\rangle\right)+\sin\phi_{G}|G\rangle,

|\eta_{l}\rangle

(u\bar{u}+d\bar{d})/\sqrt{2}

\eta^{\prime}

Obviously, ignoring the EA contribution
A_{EA}
and assuming the
\eta^{\prime}
-glueball mixing one obtains the ratios in Eq. (
3
) and Eq. (
4
). However, the assumption of
\eta^{\prime}
-glueball mixing is not convincing, as addressed above. In constrast, the QCD
\mathrm{U}_{A}(1)
anomaly may introduce important non-perturbative interaction between gluons and flavor singlet pseudoscalar mesons. First, the relatively heavy mass of
\eta^{\prime}
can be explained by this kind of interaction through the Witten-Veneziano machanism
Witten 1979a
;
Witten 1979b
;
Veneziano 1979
. Secondly,
\eta
and
\eta^{\prime}
have large production
rates in the
J/\psi
radiative decays where
\eta
and
\eta^{\prime}
are generated purely through gluons. Recent lattice QCD studies also show that the production form factors of
\eta(\eta^{\prime})
in
J/\psi
radiative decays are large
Jiang et al. 2023b
;
Shi et al. 2024
;
Batelaan et al. 2025
. On the other hand, a pervious lattice QCD calculation come out with a production factor of pseudoscalar glueball in the
J/\psi
radiative decay that is comparable with those of light pesudoscalar mesons
Gui et al. 2019
. Furthermore, the scalar form factor
f_{0}
for the semileptionic decays
D_{s}\to\eta^{(\prime)}\bar{l}\nu_{l}
has been calculated in lattice QCD
Bali et al. 2015
, where a large contribution from the disconnected diagram (similar to
EA
and corresponding to the gluonic production of
\eta^{\prime}
)
is observed to be important for
D_{s}\to\eta^{\prime}
.
These facts manifest that the usual OZI rule for the production of
\eta
states is violated due to the
\mathrm{U}_{A}(1)
anomaly. Therefore, if we discard the
\eta^{\prime}
-glueball mixing ansatz but take the
EA
contribution
A_{EA}
into account, then the ratios in Eq. (
3
) and Eq. (
4
) are modified as

\displaystyle R_{d}

\displaystyle=\tan^{2}\phi_{P}\frac{(1+4\alpha)^{2}}{(1+\alpha)^{2}},~~~R_{\eta}=\frac{\tau_{B^{0}}m_{B^{0}}}{\tau_{B_{s}^{0}}m_{B_{s}^{0}}}\left|\frac{V_{cd}}{V_{cs}}\right|^{2}\frac{\cot^{2}\phi_{P}}{2}\frac{(1+\alpha)^{2}}{(1-\alpha)^{2}},

(8)

\displaystyle R_{s}

\displaystyle=\cot^{2}\phi_{P}\frac{(1+2\alpha)^{2}}{(1-\alpha)^{2}},~~~R_{\eta^{\prime}}=\frac{\tau_{B^{0}}m_{B^{0}}}{\tau_{B_{s}^{0}}m_{B_{s}^{0}}}\left|\frac{V_{cd}}{V_{cs}}\right|^{2}\frac{\tan^{2}\phi_{P}}{2}\frac{(1+4\alpha)^{2}}{(1+2\alpha)^{2}},

from

7

To summarize, instead of the
\eta^{\prime}
-glueball mixing ansatz adopted by LHCb, we interpret the ratios of the branching fractions of
B_{(s)}^{0}\to J/\psi\eta^{(\prime)}
decays by including the light quark annihilation effect which can be important for the strong interaction production of light pseudoscalar mesons (
\eta
and
\eta^{\prime}
in this comment) because of enhancement from the QCD
\mathrm{U}_{A}(1)
anomaly. The amplitude ratio of this annihilation effect to the dominant mechanism (tree-level color suppression process) is determined to be
\alpha=-0.039(10)
, which implies that the annihilation effect decreases the decay rates of
B_{(s)}^{0}\to J/\psi\eta^{\prime}
by roughly 32% (16%). Using this
\alpha
value, the values of the
\eta-\eta^{\prime}
mixing angle
\phi_{P}
determined from different ratios
R_{d,s,\eta,\eta^{\prime}}
agree with each other better than without considering the annihilation effect.
