14 C.F.R. § 1259.602 — Conflict of interest.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1259.602
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1259.602
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1259 (National Space Grant College And Fellowship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any member of the Panel who has a personal or financial interest in an issue for consideration before the Panel shall abstain from all discussion and voting on such issue.
