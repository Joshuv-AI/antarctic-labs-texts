30 C.F.R. § 256.72 — Compensatory payments as production.
Citation: us:cfr:30:256.72
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-256.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 256 (Leasing Of Sulphur Or Oil And Gas In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an oil and gas lessee makes compensatory payments and if the lease is not being maintained in force by other production of oil or gas in paying quantities or by other approved drilling or reworking operations, such payments shall be considered as the equivalent of production in paying quantities for all purposes of the lease.
