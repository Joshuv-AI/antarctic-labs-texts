10 C.F.R. § 430.44 — Consolidation.
Citation: us:cfr:10:430.44
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-430.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 430 (Energy Conservation Program For Consumer Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DOE may consolidate any or all matters at issue in two or more proceedings docketed where there exist common parties, common questions of fact and law, and where such consolidation would expedite or simplify consideration of the issues. Consolidation shall not affect the right of any party to raise issues that could have been raised if consolidation had not occurred.
