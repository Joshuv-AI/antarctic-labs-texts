12 C.F.R. § 45.12 — Capital.
Citation: us:cfr:12:45.12
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-45.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 45 (Margin And Capital Requirements For Covered Swap Entities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A covered swap entity shall comply with:
(a) In the case of a covered swap entity that is a national bank or Federal savings association, the minimum capital requirements as generally provided 12 CFR part 3.
(b) In the case of a covered swap entity that is a Federal branch or agency of a foreign bank, the capital adequacy guidelines applicable as generally provided under 12 CFR 28.14.
