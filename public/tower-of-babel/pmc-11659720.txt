Introduction
Published: 2024

We are very happy to present this special issue of the EFSA Journal dedicated to EFSA's Fellowship Programme (EU‐FORA). It brings together the risk assessment pursuits of the fellows and the work programmes they were engaged in during the seventh cycle of the Programme.

I congratulate the newly graduates and would like to acknowledge their professionalism, engagement and desire to become part of the EU‐FORA community. In the past 12 months they have managed to significantly increase their knowledge of food risk assessment, contributing towards the knowledge development in their respective areas and the building of a European risk assessment culture. Together with my team, we hope they continue to invest in the Community as Alumni, and in themselves as food risk assessment experts.

Finally, we would like to thank everyone who was involved and contributed to yet another cycle of this well‐established initiative, extending our gratitude to EFSA's Management and Staff, Member States and competent organisations for shaping it. And, of course, to our training consortium and the fellows for making it possible.

On behalf of the EU‐FORA Programme.

Victoria Villamar
