43 C.F.R. § 30.223 — What is a prehearing conference?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.223
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.223
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before a hearing, the judge may order the parties to appear for a conference to:
(a) Simplify or clarify the issues;
(b) Obtain stipulations, admissions, agreements on documents, understandings on matters already of record, or similar agreements that will avoid unnecessary proof;
(c) Limit the number of expert or other witnesses to avoid excessively cumulative evidence;
(d) Facilitate agreements disposing of all or any of the issues in dispute; or
(e) Resolve such other matters as may simplify and shorten the hearing.
