25 C.F.R. § 171.720 — For what period does an Annual Assessment Waiver apply?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.720
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.720
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Annual Assessment Waivers are only valid for the year in which they are granted. To obtain an Annual Assessment Waiver for a subsequent year, you must reapply.
