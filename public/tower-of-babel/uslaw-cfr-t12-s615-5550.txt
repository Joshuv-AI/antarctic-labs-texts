12 C.F.R. § 615.5550 — Bankers' acceptances.
Citation: us:cfr:12:615.5550
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-615.5550
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 615 (Funding And Fiscal Affairs, Loan Policies And Operations, And Funding Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Banks for cooperatives may rediscount with other purchasers the acceptances they have created. The bank for cooperatives' board of directors, under established policies, may delegate this authority to management.
