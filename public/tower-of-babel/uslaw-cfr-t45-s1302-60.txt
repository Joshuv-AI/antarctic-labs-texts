45 C.F.R. § 1302.60 — Full participation in program services and activities.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1302.60
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1302.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1302 (Program Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A program must ensure enrolled children with disabilities, including but not limited to those who are eligible for services under IDEA, and their families receive all applicable program services delivered in the least restrictive possible environment and that they fully participate in all program activities.
