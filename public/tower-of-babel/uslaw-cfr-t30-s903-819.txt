30 C.F.R. § 903.819 — Special performance standards—Auger mining.
Citation: us:cfr:30:903.819
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-903.819
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 903 (Arizona). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 819 of this chapter, Special Permanent Program Performance Standards—Auger Mining, applies to any person who conducts surface coal mining operations that include auger mining.
