28 C.F.R. § 35.176 — Alternative means of dispute resolution.
Citation: us:cfr:28:35.176
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-35.176
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 35 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability In State And Local Government Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where appropriate and to the extent authorized by law, the use of alternative means of dispute resolution, including settlement negotiations, conciliation, facilitation, mediation, factfinding, minitrials, and arbitration, is encouraged to resolve disputes arising under the Act and this part.
