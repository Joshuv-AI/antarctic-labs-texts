22 C.F.R. § 71.3 — American claimants to foreign estates and inheritances.
Citation: us:cfr:22:71.3
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-71.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 71 (Protection And Welfare Of Citizens And Their Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where treaty provisions, local laws, or established usage permit, a consular officer should protect the interests of American citizens claiming foreign estates and inheritances.
