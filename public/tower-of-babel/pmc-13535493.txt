2026 Kidney Cancer Research Summit Abstracts

ABSTRACT
IntroductionThe 8th Kidney Cancer Research Summit (KCRS) took place on July 23rd and 24th, 2026 as a hybrid event, both in Boston, Massachusetts at the InterContinental Hotel, and virtually via Zoom webinar. The conference format consists of oral presentations and a poster session in an intimate setting that enables dynamic discussion among attendees. The keynote address to begin the meeting, “Membrane Synthesis as a Potential Achilles’ Heel of Renal Carcinoma,” was delivered by Craig Thompson of Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center.Following the elimination of the Department of Defense’s Kidney Cancer Research Program awards for Fiscal Year (FY) 2025, funding was restored at a reduced level of $15 million in FY2026. This shortfall, down from $50 million in both FY2023 and FY2024, exacerbated a general reduction in federal funding reflected in lower success rates and funding levels for biomedical research grants from the National Institutes of Health. This year’s meeting featured a session, Sustaining Momentum in Kidney Cancer Research in an Era of Reduced Federal Funding, focused on how researchers and institutions have pivoted to keep long-term collaborative research projects going in this funding environment. Other sessions included Current Role of Artificial Intelligence in Kidney Cancer; Best of KCRS – Plenary Abstract Session; Oral Abstract Presentations; Next-Generation Immunotherapy: Emerging Science and New Approaches; Shaping the Next Standard of Care for RCC Brain Metastases; Trials in Progress Presentations; RCC Breakthroughs: Highlights from AACR, ASCO, ESMO, ASCO GU; and Rapid Oral Abstract Presentations. There was also a poster session, as well as a fireside chat with Dr. Arie Belldegrun on his career path as a clinician, academic, and biotech entrepreneur.KCRS was inaugurated in 2019 to congregate the community of researchers conducting projects involving kidney cancer with clinicians, industry partners, and patient advocates in order to foster new collaborations and generate constructive feedback. KCRS is funded through sponsors, partnerships, and grassroots supporters organized by KidneyCAN, a nationwide network of kidney cancer patients, caregivers, and family members who are committed to accelerating cures for kidney cancer. Their goal is to give current and future projects the best chance to succeed and ensure the orientation of goals towards raising the standard of care and improving patient outcomes across all histologies and stages of this disease.This supplement contains a complete list of scheduled abstract presentations and posters that were accepted as of May 26th, 2026. Please be aware that some abstracts may have been withdrawn or rescheduled at the authors’ request.
