46 C.F.R. § 169.741 — Personal flotation devices and ring life buoys.
Citation: us:cfr:46:169.741
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-169.741
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 169 (Sailing School Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each personal flotation device and ring life buoy must be marked with the vessel's name.
