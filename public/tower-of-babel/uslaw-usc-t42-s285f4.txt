42 U.S.C. § 285f–4 — Microbicide research and development
Citation: us:usc:42:285f–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section285f–4
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter III (National Research Institutes); Part C (Specific Provisions Respecting National Research Institutes); Subpart 6 (National Institute Of Allergy And Infectious Diseases). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Institute, acting through the head of the Division of AIDS, shall, consistent with the peer-review process of the National Institutes of Health, carry out research on, and development of, safe and effective methods for use by women to prevent the transmission of the human immunodeficiency virus, which may include microbicides.
