48 C.F.R. § 1828.372 — 1828.372 Clause for minimum insurance coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1828.372
Creator: Chapter 18—National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1828.372
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1828 (Bonds And Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with FAR 28.306(b) and 28.307, the contracting officer may insert a clause substantially as stated at 1852.228-75, Minimum Insurance Coverage, in fixed-price solicitations and in cost-reimbursement contracts. The contracting officer may modify the clause to require additional coverage, such as vessel liability, and higher limits if appropriate for a particular acquisition.
