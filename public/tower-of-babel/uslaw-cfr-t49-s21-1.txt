49 C.F.R. § 21.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:21.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-21.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 21 (Nondiscrimination In Federally-Assisted Programs Of The Department Of Transportation—Effectuation Of Title Vi Of The Civil Rights Act Of 1964). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to effectuate the provisions of title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (hereafter referred to as the Act) to the end that no person in the United States shall, on the grounds of race, color, or national origin, be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of, or be otherwise subjected to discrimination under any program or activity receiving Federal financial assistance from the Department of Transportation.
