20 C.F.R. § 218.15 — When a parent annuity begins.
Citation: us:cfr:20:218.15
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-218.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 218 (Annuity Beginning And Ending Dates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A parent annuity begins on the later of either the date chosen by the applicant or the earliest date permitted by law. The earliest date permitted by law is the latest of—
(a) The first day of the month in which the employee dies;
(b) The first day of the month in which the claimant attains age 60; or
(c) The first day of the sixth month before the month in which the application is filed.
