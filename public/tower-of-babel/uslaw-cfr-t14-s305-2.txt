14 C.F.R. § 305.2 — Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:14:305.2
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-305.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 305 (Rules Of Practice In Informal Nonpublic Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of, and as used in this part, the term investigation means a non-adjudicatory, informal nonpublic investigation for the purpose of determining whether formal enforcement action should be instituted with respect to alleged violations of law.
