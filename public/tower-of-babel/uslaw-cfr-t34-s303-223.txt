34 C.F.R. § 303.223 — Control of funds and property.
Citation: us:cfr:34:303.223
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-303.223
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 303 (Early Intervention Program For Infants And Toddlers With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State must ensure that—
(a) The control of funds provided under this part, and title to property acquired with those funds, will be in a public agency for the uses and purposes provided in this part; and
(b) A public agency will administer the funds and property.
