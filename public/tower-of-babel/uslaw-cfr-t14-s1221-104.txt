14 C.F.R. § 1221.104 — Establishment of the NASA Logotype.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1221.104
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1221.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1221 (The Nasa Seal And Other Devices, And The Congressional Space Medal Of Honor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The NASA Logotype (also called “the Worm”) was approved by the Commission of Fine Arts and the NASA Administrator. It symbolizes NASA's role in aeronautics and space from 1975 to 1992 and was retired between 1992-2020. The NASA Logotype shall be used as set forth in § 1221.111.
