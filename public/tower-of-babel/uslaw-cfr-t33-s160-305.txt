33 C.F.R. § 160.305 — Exceptions.
Citation: us:cfr:33:160.305
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-160.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 160 (Ports And Waterways Safety—General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requirements in this subpart do not apply to crewmembers and operators on a vessel bound for a U.S. port or place of destination under force majeure.
