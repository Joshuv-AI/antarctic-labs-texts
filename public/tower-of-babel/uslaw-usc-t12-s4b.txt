12 U.S.C. § 4b — Deputy Comptroller for the supervision and examination of Federal savings associations
Citation: us:usc:12:4b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4b
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 1 (The Comptroller Of The Currency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Comptroller of the Currency shall designate a Deputy Comptroller, who shall be responsible for the supervision and examination of Federal savings associations.
