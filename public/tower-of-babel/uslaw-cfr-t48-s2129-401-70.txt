48 C.F.R. § 2129.401-70 — 2129.401-70 FEGLI Program contract clauses.

Creator: Chapter 21—Office Of Personnel Management, Federal Employees Group Life Insurance Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2129.401-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2129 (Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The fixed-price contract clauses in FAR subpart 29.4 are inappropriate for the FEGLI Program because of the limited cost-redetermination of FEGLI Program contracts. The clauses at FAR 52.229-1, 52.229-2, 52.229-3, and 52.229-4 shall not be inserted into FEGLI Program contracts.
