25 C.F.R. § 2.707 — May the LBO and interested parties file a response to the statement of reasons?
Citation: us:cfr:25:2.707
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-2.707
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 2 (Appeals From Administrative Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Any interested party, as well as the LBO, may file a response to the statement of reasons, thereby becoming a participant.
