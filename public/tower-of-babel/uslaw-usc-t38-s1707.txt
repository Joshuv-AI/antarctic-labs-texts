38 U.S.C. § 1707 — Limitations
Citation: us:usc:38:1707
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1707
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 17 (Hospital, Nursing Home, Domiciliary, And Medical Care); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Funds appropriated to carry out this chapter may not be used for purposes that are inconsistent with the Assisted Suicide Funding Restriction Act of 1997 (42 U.S.C. 14401 et seq.).
(b)
The Secretary may furnish sensori-neural aids only in accordance with guidelines prescribed by the Secretary.
