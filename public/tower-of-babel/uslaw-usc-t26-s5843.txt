26 U.S.C. § 5843 — Records and returns
Citation: us:usc:26:5843
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5843
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 53 (Machine Guns, Destructive Devices, And Certain Other Firearms); Subchapter B (General Provisions And Exemptions); Part I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Importers, manufacturers, and dealers shall keep such records of, and render such returns in relation to, the importation, manufacture, making, receipt, and sale, or other disposition, of firearms as the Secretary may by regulations prescribe.
