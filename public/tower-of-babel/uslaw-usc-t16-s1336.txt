16 U.S.C. § 1336 — Cooperative agreements; regulations
Citation: us:usc:16:1336
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1336
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 30 (Wild Horses And Burros: Protection, Management, And Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to enter into cooperative agreements with other landowners and with the State and local governmental agencies and may issue such regulations as he deems necessary for the furtherance of the purposes of this chapter.
