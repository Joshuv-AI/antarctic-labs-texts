11 U.S.C. § 302 — Joint cases
Citation: us:usc:11:302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section302
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 3 (Case Administration); Subchapter I (Commencement Of A Case). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
A joint case under a chapter of this title is commenced by the filing with the bankruptcy court of a single petition under such chapter by an individual that may be a debtor under such chapter and such individual’s spouse. The commencement of a joint case under a chapter of this title constitutes an order for relief under such chapter.
(b)
After the commencement of a joint case, the court shall determine the extent, if any, to which the debtors’ estates shall be consolidated.
