31 C.F.R. § 1024.400 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:31:1024.400
Creator: Financial Crimes Enforcement Network, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-1024.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 1024 (Rules For Mutual Funds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Mutual funds are subject to the recordkeeping requirements set forth and cross referenced in this subpart. Mutual funds should also refer to subpart D of part 1010 of this chapter for recordkeeping requirements contained in that subpart which apply to mutual funds.
