15 C.F.R. § 15.21 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:15:15.21
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-15.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 15 (Legal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart provides implementing policies governing involuntary child or child and spousal support allotments for officers of the uniformed service of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), and prescribes applicable procedures.
