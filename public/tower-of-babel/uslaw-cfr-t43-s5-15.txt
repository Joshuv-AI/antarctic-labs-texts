43 C.F.R. § 5.15 — When must I ask permission from individual Indians to conduct filming and photography?
Citation: us:cfr:43:5.15
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-5.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 5 (Commercial Filming And Similar Projects And Still Photography On Certain Areas Under Department Jurisdiction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Anyone who desires to go on to the land of an Indian to make pictures, television productions, or soundtracks is expected to observe the ordinary courtesy of first obtaining permission from the Indian and of observing any conditions attached to this permission.
