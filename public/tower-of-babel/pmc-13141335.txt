Retraction Note: Mechanism of HCV’s resistance to IFN-α in cell culture involves expression of functional IFN-α receptor 1
Authors: Sibnarayan Datta, Sidhartha Hazari, Partha K Chandra, Maria Samara, Bret Poat, Feyza Gunduz, William C Wimley, Hansjorg Hauser, Mario Koster, Christophe Lamaze, Luis A Balart, Robert F Garry, Srikanta Dash
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: Virology Journal 2011, 8:351

10.1186/1743-422X-8-351

The Editor in Chief has retracted this article because of multiple concerns about image overlaps, namely:

The IFN-α2b (−) panel in the S-5/15 group in Fig. 8B appears to partially overlap with the IFN-α2b panel in the R-17/3 group in Fig. 8B in this Article.

The IFN-α2b (−) panel in the S-5/15 group in Fig. 8B appears to partially overlap with the IFN-α2b panel in the R-17/3 group in Fig. 8B in this Article.

The IFNAR1 band in the Cured S-5/15 panel in Fig. 2A appears to overlap with the β-actin band in Fig. 11E in this Article.

The IFNAR1 band in the Cured S-5/15 panel in Fig. 2A appears to overlap with the β-actin band in Fig. 11E in this Article.

The Stat1 band in the Cured R-17/3 panel in Fig. 2A appears to overlap with the Stat1 band in Fig. 11C in this Article.

The Stat1 band in the Cured R-17/3 panel in Fig. 2A appears to overlap with the Stat1 band in Fig. 11C in this Article.

The authors were unable to provide the original data on request. The Editor-in-Chief therefore no longer has confidence in the study.

Authors William C Wimley, Hansjorg Hauser, Mario Koster, Christophe Lamaze, Robert F Garry and Srikanta Dash agree to this retraction. Authors Sibnarayan Datta, Sidhartha Hazari, Partha K Chandra, Maria Samara, Bret Poat, Feyza Gunduz and Luis A Balart have not responded to any correspondence from the editor or publisher about this retraction.
