On Generalized Free Electron Laser Equation
Dharmendra Kumar Singh

Abstract
The aim of this paper is to derive a solution for a generalized free electron laser equation in terms of the incomplete Mittag-Leffler function and in terms of the incomplete Wright function.. Keywords: Incomplete Mittag-Leffler function, Incomplete Wright function, Caputo Derivative.

Theorem 2.1

Let
b,c,\omega,\in C,\rho\in\Re,a>0,\rho>0,\Re(b)>0
and
g(t)
is assumed to be continuous on every finite interval

\left[0,T\right]
,
0<T<\infty
, and of the exponential order
e^{\rho\mu}
, when
\mu\to\infty
. Then for the generalized Free Electron Laser equation for the fractional integro-differential equation of Volterra type

D^{a}_{\mu}h(\mu)=\omega\int_{0}^{\mu}t^{b-1}E^{[c;x]}_{\rho,b}(i\zeta t^{\rho})h(\mu-t)dt+\delta g(t)~~~~(0\leq\mu\leq 1).

(2.1)

together with the initial conditions

D^{a-r}_{\mu}h(\mu)|_{\mu}=0=b_{r},r=1,...,n=-[-\Re(a)],\left(n-1<a\leq n\right);n\in N,

(2.2)

where
b_{1},b_{2},b_{3},....,b_{r}\in\Re
, there exist a unique continuous solution given by

h(\mu)=\sum_{r=1}^{n}b_{r}y_{r}(\mu)+\delta\int_{0}^{\mu}\aleph(\mu-t)g(t)dt

(2.3)

where

y_{r}(\mu)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\omega^{k}\mu^{a+(a+b)k-r}E^{[c;x]k}_{\rho,1+a+(a+b)k-r}(i\zeta\mu^{\rho})\\

(2.4)

y_{r}(\mu)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\omega^{k}\mu^{a+(a+b)k-r}\frac{1}{\Gamma(ck)}{{}_{1}}\overline{\Psi}_{1}\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}[ck,1;xk]\\
(1+a+(a+b)k-r,\rho)\end{array}|i\zeta\mu^{\rho}\right]

(2.5)

\aleph(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\omega^{k}x^{a+(a+b)k-1}E^{[c;x]k}_{\rho,a+(a+b)k}(i\zeta x^{\rho})

(2.6)

\aleph(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\omega^{k}x^{a+(a+b)k-1}\frac{1}{\Gamma(ck)}{{}_{1}}\overline{\Psi}_{1}\left[\begin{array}[]{cc}[ck,1;xk]\\
(a+(a+b)k,\rho)\end{array}|i\zeta\mu^{\rho}\right]

(2.7)

Proof.

Taking Laplace on both side of the integro-differential equation (
2.1
) and using (
1.12
) and (
1.5
), we find that

L\{D^{a}_{\mu}h{\left(\mu\right)}\}=L\{\omega\int^{\mu}_{0}t^{b-1}h(\mu-t)E^{[c;x]}_{\rho,b}(i\zeta t^{\rho})dt+\delta g(t)\}

(2.8)

s^{a}H(s)-\sum_{r=0}^{n}s^{r-1}D^{a-r}_{\mu}h(\mu)|_{\mu=0}=\omega s^{-b}(1-i\zeta s^{-\rho})^{-[c;x]}H(s)+\delta G(s)

(2.9)

\left[s^{a}-\omega s^{-b}(1-i\zeta s^{-\rho})^{-[c;x]}\right]H(s)=\sum_{r=0}^{n}s^{r-1}D^{a-r}_{\mu}h(\mu)|_{\mu=0}+\delta G(s)

(2.10)

\left[s^{a}-\omega s^{-b}(1-i\zeta s^{-\rho})^{-[c;x]}\right]H(s)=\sum_{r=0}^{n}s^{r-1}b_{r}+\delta G(s),

(2.11)

where
D^{a-r}_{\mu}h(\mu)|_{\mu=0}
.

H(s)=\sum_{r=1}^{n}s^{r-1}b_{r}\left[s^{a}-\omega s^{-b}(1-i\zeta s^{-\rho})^{-[c;x]}\right]^{-1}+\delta G(s)\left[s^{a}-\omega s^{-b}(1-i\zeta s^{-\rho})^{-[c;x]}\right]^{-1}

H(s)=\sum_{r=1}^{n}s^{r-1}b_{r}s^{-a}\left[1-\omega s^{-a}s^{-b}(1-i\zeta s^{-\rho})^{-[c;x]}\right]^{-1}

+\delta G(s)s^{-a}\left[1-\omega s^{-a}s^{-b}(1-i\zeta s^{-\rho})^{-[c;x]}\right]^{-1}

=\sum_{r=1}^{n}s^{r-1}b_{r}s^{-a}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left(\omega s^{-a}s^{-b}\right)^{k}(1-i\zeta s^{-\rho})^{-[c;x]k}

+\delta G(s)s^{-a}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left(\omega s^{-a}s^{-b}\right)^{k}(1-i\zeta s^{-\rho})^{-[c;x]k}

(2.12)

where
|\omega s^{-a}s^{-b}(1-i\zeta s^{-\rho})^{-[c;x]}|<1
. Taking the inverse Laplace transform on both sides of (
2.12
), we get

L^{-1}\{H(s)\}=L^{-1}\{\sum_{r=1}^{n}s^{r-1}b_{r}s^{-a}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left(\omega s^{-a}s^{-b}\right)^{k}(1-i\zeta s^{-\rho})^{-[c;x]k}\}

+L^{-1}\{\delta G(s)s^{-a}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left(\omega s^{-a}s^{-b}\right)^{k}(1-i\zeta s^{-\rho})^{-[c;x]k}\}

h(s)=L^{-1}\{\sum_{r=1}^{n}s^{r-1}b_{r}s^{-a}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left(\omega s^{-a}s^{-b}\right)^{k}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(i\zeta s^{-\rho})^{n}([c;x]k)_{n}\frac{1}{n!}\}

+L^{-1}\{\delta G(s)s^{-a}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\left(\omega s^{-a}s^{-b}\right)^{k}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(i\zeta s^{-\rho})^{n}([c;x]k)_{n}\frac{1}{n!}\}

h(s)=\sum_{r=1}^{n}b_{r}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\omega^{k}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(i\zeta)^{n}([c;x]k)_{n}\frac{1}{n!}L^{-1}\{s^{r-a-(a+b)k-\rho n}\}

+\delta\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\omega^{k}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(i\zeta)^{n}([c;x]k)_{n}\frac{1}{n!}L^{-1}\{G(s)s^{-a-(a+b)k-\rho n}\}

(2.13)

Using
L^{-1}\{\frac{1}{s^{n}}\}=\frac{t^{n-1}}{(n-1)!}
in the above expression, then after some calculation, we arrive at

=\sum_{r=1}^{n}b_{r}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\omega^{k}\mu^{a+(a+b)k-r}E^{[c;x]k}_{\rho,1+a+(a+b)k-r}(i\zeta\mu^{\rho})

+\delta\int_{0}^{\mu}\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\omega^{k}(\mu-t)^{a+(a+b)k-1}E^{[c;x]k}_{\rho,a+(a+b)k}(i\zeta(\mu-t)^{\rho})g(t)dt.

(2.14)

Which is the required result and by using (
1.15
) in (
2.14
), we can achieve (
2.5
) and (
2.7

Theorem 2.2

s^{a}H(s)-\sum_{r=0}^{n-1}s^{a-r-1}\frac{d^{a}}{d\mu^{a}}h(\mu)|_{\mu=0}=\omega s^{-b}(1-i\zeta s^{-\rho})^{-[c;x]}H(s)+\delta G(s).

\left[s^{a}-\omega s^{-b}(1-i\zeta s^{-\rho})^{-[c;x]}\right]H(s)=\sum_{r=0}^{n-1}s^{a-r-1}\frac{d^{a}}{d\mu^{a}}h(\mu)|_{\mu=0}+\delta G(s).

where
