36 U.S.C. § 21501 — Organization

Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 215 (American Hospital Of Paris). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Federal Charter.—
American Hospital of Paris (in this chapter, the “corporation”) is a federally chartered corporation.
(b) Place of Incorporation.—
The corporation is declared to be incorporated in the District of Columbia.
(c) Perpetual Existence.—
Except as otherwise provided, the corporation has perpetual existence.
