45 C.F.R. § 630.110 — Are any of my Federal assistance awards exempt from this part?
Citation: us:cfr:45:630.110
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-630.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 630 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part does not apply to any award that the Director or designee determines that the application of this part would be inconsistent with the international obligations of the United States or the laws or regulations of a foreign government.
