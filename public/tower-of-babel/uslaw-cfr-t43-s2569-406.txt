43 C.F.R. § 2569.406 — What are the rules about the number of parcels and size of the parcel for my selection?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2569.406
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2569.406
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2560 (Alaska Occupancy And Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You may apply for only one parcel.
(b) The parcel cannot be less than 2.5 acres or more than 160 acres.
