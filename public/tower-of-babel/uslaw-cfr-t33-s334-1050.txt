33 C.F.R. § 334.1050 — Oakland Outer Harbor adjacent to the Military Ocean Terminal, Bay Area, Pier No. 8 (Port of Oakland Berth No. 10); restricted area.
Citation: us:cfr:33:334.1050
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-334.1050
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 334 (Danger Zone And Restricted Area Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The area. Within 100 feet of the pier.
(b) The regulations. No persons and no vessels or other craft, except vessels of the U.S. Government or vessels duly authorized by the Commander, Oakland Army Base, shall enter this area.
