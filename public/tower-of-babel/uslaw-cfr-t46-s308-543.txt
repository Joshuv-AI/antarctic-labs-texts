46 C.F.R. § 308.543 — Cancellation.
Citation: us:cfr:46:308.543
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-308.543
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 308 (War Risk Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Facultative war risk insurance is not subject to cancellation by the Assured unless the goods are not shipped within thirty (30) days following the effective date of binding, and then only if the policy is returned for cancellation.
