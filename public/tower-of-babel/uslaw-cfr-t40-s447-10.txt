40 C.F.R. § 447.10 — Applicability; description of the oil-base solvent wash ink subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:447.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-447.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 447 (Ink Formulating Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the production of oil-base ink where the tank washing system uses solvents. When a plant is subject to effluent limitations covering more than one subcategory the discharge limitation shall be the aggregate of the limitations applicable to the total production covered in each subcategory.
