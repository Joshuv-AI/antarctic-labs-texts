10 U.S.C. § 9066 — Regular Air Force: composition
Citation: us:usc:10:9066
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9066
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 907 (The Air Force). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Regular Air Force is the component of the Air Force that consists of persons whose continuous service on active duty in both peace and war is contemplated by law, and of retired members of the Regular Air Force.
(b)
The Regular Air Force includes—
(1)
the officers and enlisted members of the Regular Air Force;
(2)
the professors, registrar, and cadets at the United States Air Force Academy; and
(3)
the retired officers and enlisted members of the Regular Air Force.
