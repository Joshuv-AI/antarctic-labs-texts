54 U.S.C. § 101321 — Service employee training
Citation: us:usc:54:101321
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section101321
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1013 (Employees); Subchapter II (Service Career Development, Training, And Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall develop a comprehensive training program for employees in all professional careers in the workforce of the Service for the purpose of ensuring that the workforce has available the best up-to-date knowledge, skills, and abilities with which to manage, interpret, and protect the resources of the System.
