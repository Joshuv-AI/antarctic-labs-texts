40 C.F.R. § 63.9280 — What is the purpose of subpart PPPPP?
Citation: us:cfr:40:63.9280
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-63.9280
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 63 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants For Source Categories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart PPPPP establishes national emission standards for hazardous air pollutants (NESHAP) for engine test cells/stands located at major sources of hazardous air pollutants (HAP) emissions. This subpart also establishes requirements to demonstrate initial and continuous compliance with the emission limitations contained in this NESHAP.
