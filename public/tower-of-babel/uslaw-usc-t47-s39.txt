47 U.S.C. § 39 — Amendment, modification, etc., of rights granted
Citation: us:usc:47:39
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section39
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 2 (Submarine Cables). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No right shall accrue to any government, person, or corporation under the terms of sections 34 to 39 of this title that may not be rescinded, changed, modified, or amended by the Congress.
