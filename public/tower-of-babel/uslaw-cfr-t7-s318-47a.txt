7 C.F.R. § 318.47a — a Administrative instructions relating to Guam.
Citation: us:cfr:7:318.47a
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-318.47a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 318 (State Of Hawaii And Territories Quarantine Notices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The plants, products and articles specified in § 318.47(c) may be moved from Hawaii into or through Guam without restriction under this subpart.
