25 U.S.C. § 2207 — Full faith and credit to tribal actions under tribal ordinances limiting descent and distribution of trust or restricted or controlled lands
Citation: us:usc:25:2207
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2207
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 24 (Indian Land Consolidation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary in carrying out his responsibility to regulate the descent and distribution of trust lands under section 372 of this title, and other laws, shall give full faith and credit to any tribal actions taken pursuant to subsections (a) and (b) of section 2205 of this title, which provision shall apply only to estates of decedent’s whose deaths occur on or after the effective date of tribal ordinances adopted pursuant to this chapter.
