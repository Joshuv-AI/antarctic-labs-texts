Integration of a categorical operad
Dominik Trnka

Abstract
. I describe Grothendieck construction for non-symmetric operads with values in categories, and hence in groupoids and posets. The construction produces a 2-category which is operadicaly fibered over the category Δ s \Delta_{s} of finite non-empty ordinals and surjections. I describe an inverse for the construction, yielding a 1-1 correspondence of non-symmetric categorical operads and operadic 2-categories (split-)fibered over Δ s \Delta_{s} , which resembles the correspondence of categorical presheaves and fibered categories. The result provides a new characterization of non-symmetric categorical operads and tools to study them.

An operad

By integrating an operad we mean finding a new bigger object which contains all the operations of the operad and faithfully encodes the composition of operations.
We also require, that the operad can be reconstructed from its integration.

I derive the results for constant-free non-symmetric categorical operads, i.e. operads over the operadic category

To close this introductory section, I comment on categorical operads and where to find them.
In the literature, categorical operads often appear either as operads with values in groupoids, or operads valued in partially ordered sets. Some of the most classical operads also carry a natural structure of a poset or groupoid.

(

)
.

-operad is a collection of sets
\mathcal{P}_{n}
,
n\geq 0
, together with maps

\mathcal{P}_{m}\times\mathcal{P}_{p}\to\mathcal{P}_{n},

whenever
m+p\geq n
, subject to associativity and unitality conditions. In effect, the composition maps are generated by maps

\mathcal{P}_{m}\times\mathcal{P}_{p}\to\mathcal{P}_{m+p}

for an
m,p\in\mathbb{N}
and maps

\mathcal{P}_{m}\to\mathcal{P}_{m+1}.

This strongly resembles differential graded algebras. One could possibly obtain them by considering an enrichment.

The comparison to classical categorical fibrations and Grothendieck construction is provided by the following

Proposition 24
.

Any 1-category
\mathscr{C}
(viewed as a 2-category with only identity 2-cells) is a non-symmetric operadic 2-category with constantly zero cardinality functor. A categorical
\mathscr{C}
-operad
\mathcal{P}
is then an ordinary functor
\mathcal{P}\colon\mathscr{C}^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathrm{Cat}
. The integration recovers the classical Grothendieck construction on a categorical presheaf and operadic fibrations over
\mathscr{C}
recover categorical fibration over
\mathscr{C}
.∎
