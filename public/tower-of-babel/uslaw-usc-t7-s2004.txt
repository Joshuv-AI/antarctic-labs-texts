7 U.S.C. § 2004 — Expedited clearing of title to inventory property
Citation: us:usc:7:2004
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2004
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit); Subchapter IV (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Farmers Home Administration may employ local attorneys, on a case-by-case basis, to process all legal procedures necessary to clear the title to foreclosed properties in the inventory of the Farmers Home Administration. Such attorneys shall be compensated at not more than their usual and customary charges for such work.
