31 C.F.R. § 539.203 — Evasions; attempts; conspiracies.
Citation: us:cfr:31:539.203
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-539.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 539 (Weapons Of Mass Destruction Trade Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any transaction by any United States person or within the United States on or after the effective date that evades or avoids, has the purpose of evading or avoiding, or attempts to violate any of the prohibitions set forth in this part is prohibited. Any conspiracy formed for the purpose of engaging in a transaction prohibited by this part is prohibited.
