12 C.F.R. § 168.4 — Report.
Citation: us:cfr:12:168.4
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-168.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 168 (Security Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The security officer for each Federal savings association shall report at least annually to the association's board of directors on the implementation, administration, and effectiveness of the security program.
