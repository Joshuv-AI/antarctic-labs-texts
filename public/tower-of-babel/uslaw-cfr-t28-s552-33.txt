28 C.F.R. § 552.33 — Media.
Citation: us:cfr:28:552.33
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-552.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 552 (Custody). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Warden shall assign staff to handle all news releases and news media inquiries in accordance with the rule on Contact with News Media (see 28 CFR 540.65).
