12 C.F.R. § 710.8 — Certificate of dissolution and liquidation.
Citation: us:cfr:12:710.8
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-710.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 710 (Voluntary Liquidation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 120 days after the final distribution of assets to members is started, a duly executed Certificate of Dissolution and Liquidation shall be filed with the Regional Director.
