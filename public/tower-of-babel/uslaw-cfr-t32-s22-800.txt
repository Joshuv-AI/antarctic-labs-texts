32 C.F.R. § 22.800 — Purpose and relation to other parts.
Citation: us:cfr:32:22.800
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-22.800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 22 (DoD GRANTS AND AGREEMENTS—AWARD AND ADMINISTRATION). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth grants officers' and DoD Components' responsibilities for post-award administration, by providing DoD-specific requirements on payments; debt collection; claims, disputes and appeals; and closeout audits.
