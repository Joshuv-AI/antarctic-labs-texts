40 C.F.R. § 1060.510 — How do I test EPA Low-Emission Fuel Lines for permeation emissions?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1060.510
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1060.510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1060 (Control Of Evaporative Emissions From New And In-Use Nonroad And Stationary Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For EPA Low-Emission Fuel Lines, measure emissions according to SAE J2260, which is incorporated by reference in § 1060.810.
