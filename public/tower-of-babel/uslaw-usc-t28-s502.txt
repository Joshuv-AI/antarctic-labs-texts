28 U.S.C. § 502 — Seal
Citation: us:usc:28:502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section502
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 31 (The Attorney General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall have a seal for the Department of Justice. The design of the seal is subject to the approval of the President.
