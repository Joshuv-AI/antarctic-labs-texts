7 U.S.C. § 1738 — Establishment of Facility
Citation: us:usc:7:1738
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1738
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 41 (Food For Peace); Subchapter VI (Enterprise For The Americas Initiative). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established in the Department of the Treasury an entity to be known as the “Enterprise for the Americas Facility” (hereafter referred to in this subchapter as the “Facility”).
