25 U.S.C. § 281 — Children taking lands in severalty not excluded
Citation: us:usc:25:281
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section281
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 7 (Education Of Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the expenditure of money appropriated for any of the purposes of education of Indian children, those children of Indians who have taken or may take lands in severalty under any existing law shall not, by reason thereof, be excluded from the benefits of such appropriation.
