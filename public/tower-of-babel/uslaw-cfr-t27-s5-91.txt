27 C.F.R. § 5.91 — Use of the term “pure.”
Citation: us:cfr:27:5.91
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-5.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 5 (Labeling And Advertising Of Distilled Spirits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Distilled spirits labels, containers, or packaging may not bear the word “pure” unless it:
(a) Refers to a particular ingredient used in the production of the distilled spirits, and is a truthful representation about that ingredient;
(b) Is part of the bona fide name of a permittee or retailer for which the distilled spirits are bottled; or
(c) Is part of the bona fide name of the permittee that bottled the distilled spirits.
