26 U.S.C. § 6804 — Attachment and cancellation
Citation: us:usc:26:6804
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6804
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 69 (General Provisions Relating To Stamps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise expressly provided in this title, the stamps referred to in section 6801 shall be attached, protected, removed, canceled, obliterated, and destroyed, in such manner and by such instruments or other means as the Secretary may prescribe by rules or regulations.
