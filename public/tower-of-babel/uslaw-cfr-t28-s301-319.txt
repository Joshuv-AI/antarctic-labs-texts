28 C.F.R. § 301.319 — Exclusiveness of remedy.
Citation: us:cfr:28:301.319
Creator: Federal Prison Industries, Inc., Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-301.319
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 301 (Inmate Accident Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inmates who are subject to the provisions of these Inmate Accident Compensation regulations are barred from recovery under the Federal Tort Claims Act (28 U.S.C. 2671 et seq.). Recovery under the Inmate Accident Compensation procedure was declared by the U.S. Supreme Court to be the exclusive remedy in the case of work-related injury. U.S. v. Demko, 385 U.S. 149 (1966).
