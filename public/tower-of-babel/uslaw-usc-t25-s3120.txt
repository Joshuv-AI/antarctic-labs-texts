25 U.S.C. § 3120 — Trust responsibility
Citation: us:usc:25:3120
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section3120
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 33 (National Indian Forest Resources Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to diminish or expand the trust responsibility of the United States toward Indian forest lands, or any legal obligation or remedy resulting therefrom.
