33 C.F.R. § 187.303 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:33:187.303
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-187.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 187 (Vessel Identification System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to a savings clause provided under state law, this subpart applies to any transaction, certificate of title, or record relating to a vessel, even if the transaction, certificate of title, or record was entered into or created before the effective date of the State law.
