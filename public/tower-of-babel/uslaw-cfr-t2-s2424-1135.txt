2 C.F.R. § 2424.1135 — Do Federal agencies coordinate limited denial of participation actions?
Citation: us:cfr:2:2424.1135
Creator: Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2424.1135
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2424 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal agencies do not coordinate limited denial of participation actions. As stated in § 2424.1100, a limited denial of participation is a HUD-specific action and applies only to HUD activities.
