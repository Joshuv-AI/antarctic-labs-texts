7 C.F.R. § 70.78 — Fees or charges for grading service performed under cooperative agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:7:70.78
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-70.78
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 70 (Voluntary Grading Of Poultry Products And Rabbit Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fees or charges to be made to an applicant for any grading service which differ from those listed in §§ 70.70 through 70.77, shall be provided for by a cooperative agreement.
