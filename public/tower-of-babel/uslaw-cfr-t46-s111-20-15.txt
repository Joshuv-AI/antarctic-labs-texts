46 C.F.R. § 111.20-15 — -15 Protection of transformers against overcurrent.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.20-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.20-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each transformer must have protection against overcurrent that meets Article 450 of NFPA 70 or IEC 60092-303:1980 (both incorporated by reference; see § 110.10-1 of this subchapter).
