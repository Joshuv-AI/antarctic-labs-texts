Abstract
The recent JUNO measurements of θ 12 \theta_{12} and Δ ​ m 21 2 \Delta m^{2}_{21} open a new avenue for probing flavor symmetric structures in the lepton sector. Motivated by this, we study a model in which μ − τ \mu-\tau reflection symmetry naturally emerges from an underlying A 4 A_{4} flavor symmetry within a type-II seesaw framework. Beyond its standard predictions of θ 23 = 45 ∘ \theta_{23}=45^{\circ} and δ CP = ± π / 2 \delta_{\rm CP}=\pm\pi/2 , the framework yields testable predictions for θ 12 \theta_{12} that can be probed by JUNO. Correlations between θ 12 \theta_{12} and model parameters further enhance the model’s predictivity. Future measurements at DUNE and T2HK will provide complementary tests of this scenario.

We now present a detailed numerical analysis of the model based on the neutrino mass matrix obtained in Eq. (
7

\mu-\tau
reflection symmetry. Since the charged lepton mass matrix is diagonal, the leptonic mixing arises solely from the neutrino sector. As a consequence of the
\mu-\tau

\theta_{23}=45^{\circ}

\delta_{\rm CP}=\pm\pi/2

In light of the recent JUNO results
[
12

\sin^{2}\theta_{12}
and
\Delta m^{2}_{21}

We discuss two viable scenarios corresponding to the two possible vev alignments of the scalar
\Delta^{\prime}

\langle\Delta^{\prime}\rangle\propto(1,\omega,\omega^{2})^{T}

\langle\Delta^{\prime}\rangle\propto(-1,\omega,\omega^{2})^{T}

The model involves four free parameters: two real parameters

and
D
, and one complex parameter

\displaystyle A=\left[10^{-4},10^{-1}\right],\quad|D|=\left[10^{-4},10^{-1}\right],\quad r=\left[10^{-4},10^{-1}\right],\quad\theta=\left[0,2\pi\right],

(9)

\displaystyle\Delta m^{2}_{21}=[6.94,8.14]\times 10^{-5}\ \text{eV}^{2},\quad\Delta m^{2}_{31}=[2.47,2.63]\times 10^{-3}\ \text{eV}^{2},

\displaystyle\sin^{2}{\theta_{12}}=[0.271,0.369],\quad\sin^{2}{\theta_{13}}=[0.02000,0.02405].

As noted above, the mixing angle

4
Conclusions

We have developed a framework in which
\mu-\tau
reflection symmetry is naturally realized as a consequence of an underlying
A_{4}
flavor symmetry. Neutrino masses are generated via the type-II seesaw mechanism through the introduction of two
SU(2)_{L}
triplet scalars:
\Delta_{i}
and
\Delta^{\prime}
,
transforming as singlets and a triplet under
A_{4}
, respectively. The specific vev alignments of these scalars lead to an exact
\mu-\tau
reflection symmetry in the neutrino sector. Depending on the choice of vev alignment for
\Delta^{\prime}
, two distinct viable scenarios emerge.
An additional
Z_{3}
symmetry ensures a diagonal charged lepton mass matrix, leaving the leptonic mixing entirely governed by the neutrino sector. The resulting
\mu-\tau
reflection symmetry then fixes the atmospheric mixing angle to
\theta_{23}=45^{\circ}
and the Dirac CP-violating phase to
\delta_{\rm CP}=\pm\pi/2
.

Beyond the well known predictions arising from
\mu-\tau
reflection symmetry, our framework yields nontrivial and testable implications for the solar mixing angle
\theta_{12}
. In particular, when combined with the solar mass-squared difference
\Delta m^{2}_{21}
, the model predictions can be directly confronted with the latest JUNO measurements. Among the two distinct scenarios, one predicts

Appendix A
Tensor Product Rules of
\boldsymbol{A_{4}}

The
A_{4}
symmetry is a non-abelian discrete flavor group. It corresponds to the group of even permutations of four objects and is isomorphic to the symmetry group of a regular tetrahedron. The group contains 12 elements and can be generated by two generators
S
and
T
obeying the relations:

S^{2}=T^{3}=(ST)^{3}=I.

(11)

In the basis where
S
and
T
are real matrices, the generators are given by,

S=\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}1&0&0\\
0&-1&0\\
0&0&-1\\
\end{array}\right)\,,\quad T=\left(\begin{array}[]{ccc}0&1&0\\
0&0&1\\
1&0&0\\
\end{array}\right)\;.

(12)

The
A_{4}
group has four irreducible representations, three of them are one dimensional (i.e., three singlets)
1
,
1^{\prime}
, and
1^{\prime\prime}
, and one of them is three dimensional (triplet)
3
.
The multiplication rules for these representations are given as follows:

\displaystyle 1\times 1

\displaystyle=1=1^{\prime}\times 1^{\prime\prime},\quad 1^{\prime}\times 1^{\prime}=1^{\prime\prime},\quad 1^{\prime\prime}\times 1^{\prime\prime}=1^{\prime},

(13)

\displaystyle 1\times 3

\displaystyle=3,\quad 3\times 3=1+1^{\prime}+1^{\prime\prime}+3_{S}+3_{A}\quad.

where
3_{S}
and
3_{A}
denote the two independent triplet contractions arising from the product of two triplets.
If
a=\left(a_{1},a_{2},a_{3}\right)
and
b=\left(b_{1},b_{2},b_{3}\right)
are two triplets then their multiplication rules are constructed as follows
[
73
]
:

\begin{array}[]{lll}\left(ab\right)_{1}&=&a_{1}b_{1}+a_{2}b_{2}+a_{3}b_{3}\,,\\
\left(ab\right)_{1^{\prime}}&=&a_{1}b_{1}+\omega a_{2}b_{2}+\omega^{2}a_{3}b_{3}\,,\\
\left(ab\right)_{1^{\prime\prime}}&=&a_{1}b_{1}+\omega^{2}a_{2}b_{2}+\omega a_{3}b_{3}\,,\\
\left(ab\right)_{3_{S}}&=&\left(a_{2}b_{3}+a_{3}b_{2},a_{3}b_{1}+a_{1}b_{3},a_{1}b_{2}+a_{2}b_{1}\right)^{T}\,,\\
\left(ab\right)_{3_{A}}&=&\left(a_{2}b_{3}-a_{3}b_{2},a_{3}b_{1}-a_{1}b_{3},a_{1}b_{2}-a_{2}b_{1}\right)^{T}\,,\end{array}

(14)

where
\omega
is the cube root of unity defined by
\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}
. The triplets
3_{S}
and
3_{A}
denotes the symmetric and antisymmetric contractions, respectively.

Appendix B
Determination of Lepton Mixing Parameters

The neutrino mass matrix given in Eq. (
7
) can be diagonalized by a unitary transformation as follows

U_{\nu}^{T}\mathcal{M}_{\nu}U_{\nu}=\text{diag}(m_{1},m_{2},m_{3})\,,

(15)

where
U_{\nu}
is the unitary matrix and
m_{1,2,3}
denote the physical neutrino mass eigenvalues. In the present framework, the charged lepton mass matrix is diagonal. Consequently,
U_{\nu}
directly corresponds to the leptonic mixing (PMNS) matrix,

U_{\rm PMNS}\equiv U_{\nu}\,.

(16)

We choose the symmetric parametrization of the lepton mixing matrix
[
74
,
81
]
, given by

U_{\rm PMNS}=P(\delta_{1},\delta_{2},\delta_{3})\,U_{23}(\theta_{23},\phi_{23})\,U_{13}(\theta_{13},\phi_{13})\,U_{12}(\theta_{12},\phi_{12})\,,

(17)

where
P(\delta_{1},\delta_{2},\delta_{3})
is a diagonal matrix of unphysical phases and the
U_{ij}
are complex rotations in the
ij
plane, as for example,

U_{23}(\theta_{23},\phi_{23})=\left(\begin{matrix}1&0&0\\
0&\cos\theta_{23}&\sin\theta_{23}\,e^{-i\phi_{23}}\\
0&-\sin\theta_{23}\,e^{i\phi_{23}}&\cos\theta_{23}\end{matrix}\right)\,.

(18)

The phases
\phi_{12}
and
\phi_{13}
are relevant for neutrinoless double beta decay, while the combination
\delta_{CP}=\phi_{13}-\phi_{12}-\phi_{23}
is the usual Dirac CP-violating phase measured in neutrino oscillations. The leptonic mixing angles can be extracted from the elements of the PMNS matrix through the following relations:

\displaystyle s_{13}=|U_{\rm PMNS}|_{13},\quad s_{12}=|U_{\rm PMNS}|_{12}/c_{13},\quad s_{23}=|U_{\rm PMNS}|_{23}/c_{13}.

(19)

where
s_{13,12,23}
and
c_{13}
denote the shorthand notations for
\sin{\theta_{13,12,23}}
and
\cos{\theta_{13}}
, respectively.
