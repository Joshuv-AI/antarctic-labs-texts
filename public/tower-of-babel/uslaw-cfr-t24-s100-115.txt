24 C.F.R. § 100.115 — Residential real estate-related transactions.
Citation: us:cfr:24:100.115
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-100.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 100 (Discriminatory Conduct Under The Fair Housing Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term residential real estate-related transactions means:
(a) The making or purchasing of loans or providing other financial assistance—
(1) For purchasing, constructing, improving, repairing or maintaining a dwelling; or
(2) Secured by residential real estate; or
(b) The selling, brokering or appraising of residential real property.
