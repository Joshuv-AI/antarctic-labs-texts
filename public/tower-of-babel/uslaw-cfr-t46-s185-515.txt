46 C.F.R. § 185.515 — Passenger safety bill.
Citation: us:cfr:46:185.515
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-185.515
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 185 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) On vessels described by 46 CFR 175.110(d) of this chapter, a passenger safety bill must be posted by the master in each cabin or stateroom, and in passenger accommodation spaces.
(b) Each passenger safety bill required by this section must list:
(1) The embarkation station and the number and location of the survival craft to which each passenger is assigned, if applicable;
(2) The fire and emergency signal and the abandon ship signal;
(3) Essential action that must be taken in an emergency; and
(4) If immersion suits are provided for passengers, the location of the suits and illustrated instructions on the method of donning the suits.
