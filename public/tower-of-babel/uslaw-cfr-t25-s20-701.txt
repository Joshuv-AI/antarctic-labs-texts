25 C.F.R. § 20.701 — Does a recipient receive financial assistance while an appeal is pending?
Citation: us:cfr:25:20.701
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-20.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 20 (Financial Assistance And Social Services Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, if you appeal under this subpart, financial assistance will be continued or reinstated to insure there is no break in financial assistance until the Superintendent or his/her designated representative makes a decision. The Superintendent or his/her designated representative can adjust payments or recover overpayments to conform with his/her decision.
