29 C.F.R. § 1955.45 — Effect of appeal of administrative law judge's decision.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1955.45
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1955.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1955 (Procedures For Withdrawal Of Approval Of State Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An administrative law judge's decision shall be stayed pending a decision on appeal to the Secretary. If there are no exceptions filed to the decisions of the administrative law judge, the administrative law judge's decision shall be published in the Federal Register as a final decision and served upon the parties.
