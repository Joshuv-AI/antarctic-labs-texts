30 C.F.R. § 550.165 — If I have a State lease, what fees do I have to pay for a right-of-use and easement?
Citation: us:cfr:30:550.165
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-550.165
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 550 (Oil And Gas And Sulfur Operations In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When you apply for a right-of-use and easement, you must pay:
(a) A nonrefundable filing fee as specified in § 550.125; and
(b) The first year's rental as specified in § 550.160(g).
