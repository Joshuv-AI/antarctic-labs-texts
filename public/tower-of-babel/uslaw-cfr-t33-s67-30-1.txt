33 C.F.R. § 67.30-1 — -1 Class “C” structures.
Citation: us:cfr:33:67.30-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-67.30-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 67 (Aids To Navigation On Artificial Islands And Fixed Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Class “C” structures shall be the structures erected in an area where Class “C” requirements must be met.
