31 C.F.R. § 361.10 — Recoveries.
Citation: us:cfr:31:361.10
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-361.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 361 (Claims Pursuant To The Government Losses In Shipment Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If relief is granted, the consignor shall take all necessary and reasonable steps to recover the lost, destroyed or damaged valuables, or their value. All recoveries and repayments, in connection with valuables for which replacement has been made out of the Fund, shall be forwarded to the Secretary for credit to the Fund.
