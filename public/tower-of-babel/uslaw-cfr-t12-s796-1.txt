12 C.F.R. § 796.1 — What is the purpose and scope of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:12:796.1
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-796.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 796 (Post-Employment Restrictions For Certain Ncua Examiners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part identifies those National Credit Union Administration (NCUA) employees who are subject to the special, post-employment restrictions in section 1786(w) of the Act and implements those restrictions as they apply to NCUA employees.
