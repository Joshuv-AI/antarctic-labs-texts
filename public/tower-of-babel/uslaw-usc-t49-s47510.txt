49 U.S.C. § 47510 — Tradeoff allowance
Citation: us:usc:49:47510
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section47510
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Airport Development And Noise); Chapter 475 (Noise); Subchapter I (Noise Abatement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding another law or a regulation prescribed or order issued under that law, the tradeoff provisions contained in appendix C of part 36 of title 14, Code of Federal Regulations, apply in deciding whether an aircraft complies with subpart I of part 91 of title 14.
