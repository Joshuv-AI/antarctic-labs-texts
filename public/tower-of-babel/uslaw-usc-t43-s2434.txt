43 U.S.C. § 2434 — Termination of authority
Citation: us:usc:43:2434
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section2434
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 42 (Rural Water Supply); Subchapter II (Twenty-First Century Water Works). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Subject to subsection (b), the authority of the Secretary to carry out this subchapter terminates on the date that is 10 years after December 22, 2006.
(b) Exception
The termination of authority under subsection (a) shall have no effect on—
(1)
any loans guaranteed by the United States under this subchapter; or
(2)
the administration of any loan guaranteed under this subchapter before the effective date of the termination of authority.
