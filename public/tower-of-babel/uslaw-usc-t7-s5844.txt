7 U.S.C. § 5844 — Definitions and authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:7:5844
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section5844
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 88 (Research); Subchapter II (National Genetic Resources Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Definitions
For purposes of this subchapter:
(1)
The term “program” means the National Genetic Resources Program.
(2)
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Agriculture.
(3)
The term “Director” means the Director of the National Genetic Resources Program.
(b) Authorization of appropriations
There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this subchapter—
(1)
such sums as are necessary for each of fiscal years 1991 through 2013; and
(2)
$1,000,000 for each of fiscal years 2014 through 2023.
