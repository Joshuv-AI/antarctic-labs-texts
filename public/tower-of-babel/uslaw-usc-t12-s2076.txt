12 U.S.C. § 2076 — Other services
Citation: us:usc:12:2076
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2076
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 23 (Farm Credit System); Subchapter II (Farm Credit Associations); Part A (Production Credit Associations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each production credit association may provide technical assistance to borrowers, applicants, and members and may make available to them at their option such financial related services appropriate to their on-farm and aquatic operations as is determined feasible by the board of directors of each Farm Credit Bank, under regulations prescribed by the Farm Credit Administration.
