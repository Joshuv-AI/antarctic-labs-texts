14 C.F.R. § 5.91 — Competencies and training.
Citation: us:cfr:14:5.91
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-5.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 5 (Safety Management Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person required to have an SMS under this part must provide training to each individual identified in § 5.23 of this part to ensure the individuals attain and maintain the competencies necessary to perform their duties relevant to the operation and performance of the SMS.
