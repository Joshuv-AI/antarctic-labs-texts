31 C.F.R. § 11.5 — Application for permit.
Citation: us:cfr:31:11.5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-11.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 11 (Operation Of Vending Facilities By The Blind On Federal Property Under The Control Of The Department Of The Treasury). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applications for permits for the operation of vending facilities other than cafeterias shall be made in writing and submitted for the review and approval of the head of the appropriate Treasury bureau or that official's designee.
