14 U.S.C. § 2770 — Special instruction at universities
Citation: us:usc:14:2770
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2770
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 27 (Pay, Allowances, Awards, And Other Rights And Benefits); Subchapter III (Payments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Coast Guard personnel may be assigned for special instruction at private or state colleges or universities, and their expenses, including tuition, books, laboratory equipment and fees, and school supplies, may be defrayed by the Coast Guard.
