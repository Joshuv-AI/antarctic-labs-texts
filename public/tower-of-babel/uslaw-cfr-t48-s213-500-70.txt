48 C.F.R. § 213.500-70 — 213.500-70 Only one offer.
Citation: us:cfr:48:213.500-70
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-213.500-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 213 (Simplified Acquisition Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If only one offer is received in response to a competitive solicitation issued using simplified acquisition procedures authorized under FAR subpart 13.5, follow the procedures at 215.371-2.
