31 C.F.R. § 92.1 — Manufacture of medals.
Citation: us:cfr:31:92.1
Creator: Monetary Offices, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-92.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 92 (United States Mint Operations And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

With the approval of the Director of the Mint, dies for medals of a national character designated by Congress may be executed at the Philadelphia Mint, and struck in such field office of the Mints and Assay Offices as the Director shall designate.
