10 U.S.C. § 8027 — Chief of Legislative Affairs
Citation: us:usc:10:8027
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8027
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 803 (Department Of The Navy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There is a Chief of Legislative Affairs in the Department of the Navy.
(b)
The Chief of Legislative Affairs shall perform legislative affairs functions as specified for the Office of the Secretary of the Navy by section 8014(c)(1)(F) of this title.
