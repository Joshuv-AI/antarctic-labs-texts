41 C.F.R. § 101-1.104-1 — -1.104-1 Publication.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FPMR will be published in the Federal Register, in looseleaf form, and in accumulated form in the Code of Federal Regulations. Temporary-type FPMR will be published in the Notices section of the Federal Register 1 and in looseleaf form.
