46 C.F.R. § 189.25-38 — -38 Pollution prevention.
Citation: us:cfr:46:189.25-38
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-189.25-38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 189 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At each inspection for certification and periodic inspection, the inspector shall examine the vessel to determine that it meets the vessel design and equipment requirements for pollution prevention in 33 CFR part 155, subpart B.
