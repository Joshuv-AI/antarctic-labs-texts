31 C.F.R. § 510.515 — Third-country diplomatic and consular funds transfers.
Citation: us:cfr:31:510.515
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-510.515
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 510 (North Korea Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section, U.S. depository institutions, U.S.-registered brokers or dealers in securities, and U.S.-registered money transmitters are authorized to process funds transfers necessary for the operating expenses or other official business of third-country diplomatic or consular missions in North Korea.
(b) This section does not authorize funds transfers involving accounts blocked pursuant to § 510.201(d).
