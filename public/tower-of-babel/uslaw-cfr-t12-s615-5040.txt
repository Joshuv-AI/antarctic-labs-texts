12 C.F.R. § 615.5040 — Borrowings from financial institutions other than commercial banks.
Citation: us:cfr:12:615.5040
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-615.5040
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 615 (Funding And Fiscal Affairs, Loan Policies And Operations, And Funding Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Farm Credit banks may borrow from other financial institutions, such as insurance companies, Federal agencies, or Federal reserve banks.
