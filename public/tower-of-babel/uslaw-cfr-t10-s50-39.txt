10 C.F.R. § 50.39 — Public inspection of applications.
Citation: us:cfr:10:50.39
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-50.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 50 (Domestic Licensing Of Production And Utilization Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applications and documents submitted to the Commission in connection with applications may be made available for public inspection in accordance with the provisions of the regulations contained in part 2 of this chapter.
