20 C.F.R. § 687.150 — What are the requirements for submitting applications for National Dislocated Worker Grants?
Citation: us:cfr:20:687.150
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-687.150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 687 (National Dislocated Worker Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department will publish guidance on the requirements for submitting applications for DWGs. Requirements may vary depending on the DWG. A project implementation plan must be submitted after receiving the DWG award, unless otherwise specified.
