10 U.S.C. § 8802 — Sales: members of Army, Air Force, and Space Force; prices
Citation: us:usc:10:8802
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8802
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 879 (Ships’ Stores And Commissary Stores). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Navy and the Marine Corps shall sell subsistence supplies to any member of the Army, the Air Force, or the Space Force at prices charged members of the naval service.
