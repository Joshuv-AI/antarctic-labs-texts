25 C.F.R. § 169.412 — When will BIA issue a decision on an appeal from a right-of-way decision?
Citation: us:cfr:25:169.412
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-169.412
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 169 (Rights-Of-Way Over Indian Land). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BIA will issue a decision on an appeal from a right-of-way decision within 60 days of receipt of all pleadings.
