21 C.F.R. § 314.630 — Postmarketing safety reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:21:314.630
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-314.630
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 314 (Applications For Fda Approval To Market A New Drug). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Drug products approved under this subpart are subject to the postmarketing recordkeeping and safety reporting requirements applicable to all approved drug products, as provided in §§ 314.80 and 314.81.
