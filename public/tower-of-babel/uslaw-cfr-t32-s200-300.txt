32 C.F.R. § 200.300 — Basis for civil money penalties and assessments.
Citation: us:cfr:32:200.300
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-200.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 200 (Civil Money Penalty Authorities For The Tricare Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The DHA may impose a penalty and an assessment against any person who it determines in accordance with this part has violated section 1128B(b) of the Act by unlawfully offering, paying, soliciting, or receiving remuneration to induce or in return for the referral of business paid for, in whole or in part, by TRICARE/CHAMPUS.
