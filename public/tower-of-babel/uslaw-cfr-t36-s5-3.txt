36 C.F.R. § 5.3 — Business operations.
Citation: us:cfr:36:5.3
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-5.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 5 (Commercial And Private Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Engaging in or soliciting any business in park areas, except in accordance with the provisions of a permit, contract, or other written agreement with the United States, except as such may be specifically authorized under special regulations applicable to a park area, is prohibited.
