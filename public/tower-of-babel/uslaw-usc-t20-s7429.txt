20 U.S.C. § 7429 — State educational agency review
Citation: us:usc:20:7429
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7429
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter VI (Indian, Native Hawaiian, And Alaska Native Education); Part A (Indian Education); Subpart 1 (Formula Grants To Local Educational Agencies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Before submitting an application to the Secretary under section 7424 of this title, a local educational agency shall submit the application to the State educational agency, which may comment on such application. If the State educational agency comments on the application, the agency shall comment on all applications submitted by local educational agencies in the State and shall provide those comments to the respective local educational agencies, with an opportunity to respond.
