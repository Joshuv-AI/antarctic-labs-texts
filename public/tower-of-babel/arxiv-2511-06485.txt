On the Asymptotic Palindrome Density of Fibonacci Infinite Words
Duaa Abdullah

Abstract
. In this paper, we investigate the combinatorial and density properties of infinite words generated by Fibonacci-type morphisms, focusing on their subword structure, palindrome density, and extremal statistical behaviors. Using the morphism 0 → 01 0\to 01 , 1 → 0 1\to 0 , we define the word 𝕐 \mathbb{Y} and establish new results relating its density components dens ⁡ ( λ , n ) \mathrm{dens}(\lambda,n) , dens ⁡ ( α , n ) \mathrm{dens}(\alpha,n) , and dens ⁡ ( β , n ) \mathrm{dens}(\beta,n) , deriving explicit formulae and bounds on their behavior. We further prove a general density theorem for infinite words with paired subwords, showing that the density is bounded above by 1 φ 1 \frac{1}{\varphi_{1}} , where φ 1 = ( 1 + 5 ) / 2 \varphi_{1}=(1+\sqrt{5})/2 is the golden ratio. Our approach connects the structure of Fibonacci and Thue–Morse sequences to central binomial coefficients, and yields precise asymptotic and combinatorial interpretations for the observed densities.



Keywords:
Morse, Fibonacci, Word, Density, Morphism.
