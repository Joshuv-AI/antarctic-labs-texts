10 U.S.C. § 12682 — Reserves: discharge upon becoming ordained minister of religion
Citation: us:usc:10:12682
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12682
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1221 (Separation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations to be prescribed by the Secretary of Defense, a Reserve who becomes a regular or ordained minister of religion is entitled upon his request to a discharge from his reserve enlistment or appointment.
