12 C.F.R. § 741.218 — Involuntary liquidation and creditor claims.
Citation: us:cfr:12:741.218
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-741.218
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 741 (Requirements For Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any credit union which is insured pursuant to title II of the Act shall adhere to the applicable provisions in part 709 of this chapter. Section 709.3 of this chapter applies only to federal credit unions.
