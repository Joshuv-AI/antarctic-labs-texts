48 C.F.R. § 1552.209-71 — 1552.209-71 Organizational conflicts of interest.

Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1552 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed in 1509.507-2, insert the following contract clause in all contracts except:
(a) When specific clauses are required per EPAAR part 1509;
(b) When the procurement is with another Federal agency (however, the provision is included in contracts with SBA and its subcontractor under the 8(a) program); and
(c) When the procurement is accomplished through simplified acquisition procedures, use of the clause is optional.
Alternate I (SEP 1998). Contracts for other than Superfund work shall include Alternate I in this clause in lieu of paragraph (e).
