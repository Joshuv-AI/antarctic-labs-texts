45 C.F.R. § 614.8 — Application to Board Executive Committee.
Citation: us:cfr:45:614.8
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-614.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 614 (Government In The Sunshine Act Regulations Of The National Science Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All the provisions of this part applicable to the National Science Board shall apply equally to the Executive Committee of the Board whenever the Executive Committee is meeting pursuant to its authority to act on behalf of the Board.
