20 U.S.C. § 1070d–31 — Statement of purpose
Citation: us:usc:20:1070d–31
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1070d–31
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter IV (Student Assistance); Part A (Grants To Students In Attendance At Institutions Of Higher Education); Subpart 6 (Robert C. Byrd Honors Scholarship Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this subpart to establish a Robert C. Byrd Honors Scholarship Program to promote student excellence and achievement and to recognize exceptionally able students who show promise of continued excellence.
