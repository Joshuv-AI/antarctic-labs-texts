43 C.F.R. § 2361.30 — Unauthorized use and occupancy.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2361.30
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2361.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2360 (National Petroleum Reserve In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who violates or fails to comply with regulations of this subpart is subject to prosecution, including trespass and liability for damages, pursuant to the appropriate laws.
