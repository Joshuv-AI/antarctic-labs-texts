41 C.F.R. § 109-38.401-2 — -38.401-2 Use of self-service pumps.

Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-38.401-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-38 (38—Motor Equipment Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is DOE policy that motor vehicle operators shall use self-service pumps in accordance with the provisions of 41 CFR 101-38.401-2.
