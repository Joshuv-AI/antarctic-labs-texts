45 C.F.R. § 1149.64 — What happens if an initial decision is appealed?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1149.64
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1149.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1149 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An initial decision is stayed automatically pending disposition of a motion for reconsideration or of an appeal to the authority head.
(b) No administrative stay is available following a final decision of the authority head.
