20 C.F.R. § 680.350 — May Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act title I adult and dislocated worker funds be used to directly support adult education and literacy activities?
Citation: us:cfr:20:680.350
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-680.350
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 680 (Adult And Dislocated Worker Activities Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, under WIOA sec. 134(c)(3)(D)(x), title I funds may provide adult education and literacy activities if they are provided concurrently or in combination with one or more of the following training services:
(a) Occupational skills training, including training for nontraditional employment;
(b) OJT;
(c) Incumbent worker training (as described in §§ 680.780, 680.790, 680.800, 680.810, and 680.820);
(d) Programs that combined workplace training and related instruction, which may include cooperative education programs;
(e) Training programs operated by the private sector;
(f) Skill upgrading and retraining; or
(g) Entrepreneurial training.
