17 C.F.R. § 38.255 — Risk controls for trading.
Citation: us:cfr:17:38.255
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-38.255
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 38 (Designated Contract Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The designated contract market must establish and maintain risk control mechanisms to prevent and reduce the potential risk of price distortions and market disruptions, including, but not limited to, market restrictions that pause or halt trading in market conditions prescribed by the designated contract market.
