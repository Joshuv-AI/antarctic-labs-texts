25 C.F.R. § 291.12 — Who will monitor and enforce tribal compliance with the Class III gaming procedures?
Citation: us:cfr:25:291.12
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-291.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 291 (Class Iii Gaming Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Indian tribe and the State may have an agreement regarding monitoring and enforcement of tribal compliance with the Indian tribe's Class III gaming procedures. In addition, under existing law, the NIGC will monitor and enforce tribal compliance with the Indian tribe's Class III gaming procedures.
