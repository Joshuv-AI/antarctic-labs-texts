42 U.S.C. § 5195b — Administration of subchapter
Citation: us:usc:42:5195b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5195b
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 68 (Disaster Relief); Subchapter IV–B (Emergency Preparedness). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter shall be carried out by the Administrator of the Federal Emergency Management Agency.
