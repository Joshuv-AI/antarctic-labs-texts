10 C.F.R. § 2.500 — Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:10:2.500
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-2.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 2 (Agency Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes procedures applicable to licensing proceedings which involve the consideration in separate hearings of an application for a license to manufacture nuclear power reactors under subpart F of part 52 or subpart H of part 53 of this chapter.
