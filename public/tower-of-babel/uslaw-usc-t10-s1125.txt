10 U.S.C. § 1125 — Recognition for accomplishments: award of trophies
Citation: us:usc:10:1125
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1125
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 57 (Decorations And Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense may—
(1)
award medals, trophies, badges, and similar devices to members, units, or agencies of an armed force under his jurisdiction for excellence in accomplishments or competitions related to that armed force; and
(2)
provide badges or buttons in recognition of special service, good conduct, and discharge under conditions other than dishonorable.
