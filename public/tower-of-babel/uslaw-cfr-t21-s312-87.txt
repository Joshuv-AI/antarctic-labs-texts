21 C.F.R. § 312.87 — Active monitoring of conduct and evaluation of clinical trials.
Citation: us:cfr:21:312.87
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-312.87
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 312 (Investigational New Drug Application). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For drugs covered under this section, the Commissioner and other agency officials will monitor the progress of the conduct and evaluation of clinical trials and be involved in facilitating their appropriate progress.
