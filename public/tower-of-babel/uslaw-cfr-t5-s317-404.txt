5 C.F.R. § 317.404 — Retention of qualifications standards.
Citation: us:cfr:5:317.404
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-317.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 317 (Employment In The Senior Executive Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a qualifications standard is changed, or a position is cancelled, the former standard shall be retained for 2 years.
