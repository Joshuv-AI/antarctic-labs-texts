20 C.F.R. § 202.2 — Company or person principally engaged in carrier business.
Citation: us:cfr:20:202.2
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-202.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 202 (Employers Under The Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any company or person principally engaged in carrier business is an employer.
