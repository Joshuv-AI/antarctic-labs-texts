42 U.S.C. § 4525 — Real property taxation
Citation: us:usc:42:4525
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4525
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 59 (National Urban Policy And New Community Development); Part B (Development Of New Communities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this part shall be construed to exempt any real property that may be acquired and held by the Secretary as a result of the exercise of lien or subrogation rights from real property taxation to the same extent, according to its value, as other real property is taxed.
