43 C.F.R. § 3256.11 — How may I appeal a BLM decision regarding my exploration operations?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3256.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3256.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may appeal a BLM decision regarding your exploration operations in accordance with § 3200.5.
