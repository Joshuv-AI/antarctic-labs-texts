28 C.F.R. § 32.21 — Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:28:32.21
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-32.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 32 (Public Safety Officers' Death, Disability, And Educational Assistance Benefit Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consistent with § 32.1, this subpart contains provisions applicable to claims made under the Act—
(a) At 34 U.S.C. 10281(b); or
(b) At 34 U.S.C. 10286 or Public Law 107-37, with respect to a public safety officer's disability.
