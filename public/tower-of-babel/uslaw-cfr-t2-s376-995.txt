2 C.F.R. § 376.995 — Principal (HHS supplement to government-wide definition at 2 CFR 180.995).
Citation: us:cfr:2:376.995
Creator: Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-376.995
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 376 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Principal means individuals, in addition to those listed at 2 CFR 180.995, who participate in HHS covered transactions including:
(a) Providers of federally required audit services; and
(b) Researchers.
