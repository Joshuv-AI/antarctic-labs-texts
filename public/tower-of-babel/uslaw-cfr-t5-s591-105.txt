5 C.F.R. § 591.105 — Program administration.
Citation: us:cfr:5:591.105
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-591.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 591 (Allowances And Differentials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agency that provides a uniform allowance or furnishes uniforms under this subpart must establish policies to administer the program sufficient to ensure compliance with 5 U.S.C. 5901 and this subpart.
