20 C.F.R. § 422.827 — Offset against tax refunds.
Citation: us:cfr:20:422.827
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-422.827
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 422 (Organization And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will take action to effect Administrative Offset against tax refunds due to debtors in accordance with the provisions of 31 U.S.C. 3720A through referral for centralized Administrative Offset under § 422.825.
