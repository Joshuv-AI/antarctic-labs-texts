24 C.F.R. § 91.600 — Waiver authority.
Citation: us:cfr:24:91.600
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-91.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 91 (Consolidated Submissions For Community Planning And Development Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon determination of good cause, HUD may, subject to statutory limitations, waive any provision of this part. Each such waiver must be in writing and must be supported by documentation of the pertinent facts and grounds.
