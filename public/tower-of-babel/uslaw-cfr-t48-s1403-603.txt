48 C.F.R. § 1403.603 — 1403.603 Responsibilities of the contracting officer.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1403.603
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1403.603
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1403 (Improper Business Practices And Personal Conflicts Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The CO shall prepare a written determination and findings for the signature of the HCA when requesting authorization to allow a contract award to a Government employee or business concern or other organization owned or substantially owned or controlled by one or more Government employees.
