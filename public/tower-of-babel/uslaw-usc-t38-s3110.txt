38 U.S.C. § 3110 — Leaves of absence
Citation: us:usc:38:3110
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3110
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 31 (Training And Rehabilitation For Veterans With Service-Connected Disabilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall prescribe such regulations as the Secretary determines necessary for granting leaves of absence to veterans pursuing rehabilitation programs under this chapter. During authorized leaves of absence, a veteran shall be considered to be pursuing such program.
