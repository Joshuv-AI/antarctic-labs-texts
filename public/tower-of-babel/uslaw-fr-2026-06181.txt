ELECTION ASSISTANCE COMMISSION


Sunshine Act Meetings

AGENCY: Election Assistance Commission.

ACTION: Sunshine Act notice; notice of public meeting agenda.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Public Meeting: U.S. Election Assistance Commission Standards 
Board 2026 Annual Meeting.

DATES: Thursday, April 16, 2026, 9:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. CT and Friday, 
April 17, 2026, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. CT. The public registration 
deadline for the event is on or before April 10, 2026. Written comments 
should be received no later than 24 hours prior to the start of the 
meeting.

ADDRESSES: The Drake, 140 East Walton Place, Chicago, IL 60611.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Kristen Muthig, Telephone: (202) 897-
9285, Email: [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 
    Purpose: In accordance with the Government in the Sunshine Act 
(Sunshine Act), Public Law 94-409, as amended (5 U.S.C. 552b), the U.S. 
Election Assistance Commission (EAC) will hold its annual meeting of 
the EAC Standards Board to conduct regular business, discuss EAC 
updates and upcoming programs, and discuss other relevant election 
topics.
    Agenda: The EAC's Standards Board will hold its 2026 Annual Meeting 
to conduct regular business, and discuss EAC updates and upcoming 
programs.
    Registration is required to attend. Please register by April 10, 
2026, on the event page at eac.gov/events/2026/04/16/2026-eac-standards-board-annual-meeting.
    Public comments on the meeting must be submitted in writing on 
regulations.gov (docket ID: EAC-2026-0101). Comments must be received 
no later than 24 hours prior to the start of the meeting.
    Background: The Help America Vote Act (HAVA) designates a 110-
member Standards Board to assist EAC in carrying out its mandates under 
the law. The board consists of 55 state election officials selected by 
their respective chief state election official, and 55 local election 
officials selected through a process supervised by the chief state 
election official.
    The full agenda will be posted in advance on the EAC website: 
eac.gov/events/2026/04/16/2026-eac-standards-board-annual-meeting.
    Status: This meeting will be open to the public.

Seton Parsons,
