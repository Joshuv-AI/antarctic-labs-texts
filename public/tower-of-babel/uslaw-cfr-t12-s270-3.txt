12 C.F.R. § 270.3 — Governing principles.
Citation: us:cfr:12:270.3
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-270.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 270 (Open Market Operations Of Federal Reserve Banks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As required by section 12A of the Federal Reserve Act, the time, character, and volume of all purchases and sales of obligations in the open market by Federal Reserve banks are governed with a view to accommodating commerce and business and with regard to their bearing upon the general credit situation of the country.
