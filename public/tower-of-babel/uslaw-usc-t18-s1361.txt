18 U.S.C. § 1361 — Government property or contracts
Citation: us:usc:18:1361
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1361
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 65 (Malicious Mischief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever willfully injures or commits any depredation against any property of the United States, or of any department or agency thereof, or any property which has been or is being manufactured or constructed for the United States, or any department or agency thereof, or attempts to commit any of the foregoing offenses, shall be punished as follows:
If the damage or attempted damage to such property exceeds the sum of $1,000, by a fine under this title or imprisonment for not more than ten years, or both; if the damage or attempted damage to such property does not exceed the sum of $1,000, by a fine under this title or by imprisonment for not more than one year, or both.
