33 C.F.R. § 136.203 — Proof.
Citation: us:cfr:33:136.203
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-136.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 136 (Oil Spill Liability Trust Fund; Claims Procedures; Designation Of Source; And Advertisement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the requirements of Subparts A and B of this part, a claimant must establish—
(a) That the actions taken were necessary to prevent, minimize, or mitigate the effects of the incident;
(b) That the removal costs were incurred as a result of these actions;
(c) That the actions taken were determined by the FOSC to be consistent with the National Contingency Plan or were directed by the FOSC.
