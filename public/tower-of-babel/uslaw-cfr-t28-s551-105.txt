28 C.F.R. § 551.105 — Custody.
Citation: us:cfr:28:551.105
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-551.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 551 (Miscellaneous). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Staff ordinarily will supervise a pretrial inmate as if classified “In” custody.
(b) Where circumstances warrant, staff may supervise a pretrial inmate according to procedures for other custody levels.
