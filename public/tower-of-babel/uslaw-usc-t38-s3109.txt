38 U.S.C. § 3109 — Entitlement to independent living services and assistance
Citation: us:usc:38:3109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3109
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 31 (Training And Rehabilitation For Veterans With Service-Connected Disabilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In any case in which the Secretary has determined under section 3106(e) of this title that the achievement of a vocational goal by a veteran currently is not reasonably feasible, such veteran shall be entitled, in accordance with the provisions of section 3120 of this title, to a program of independent living services and assistance designed to enable such veteran to achieve maximum independence in daily living.
