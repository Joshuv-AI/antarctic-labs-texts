4 U.S.C. § 146 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:4:146
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title4-section146
Context: United States Code, Title 4 (Flag And Seal, Seat Of Government, And The States); Chapter 5 (Official Territorial Papers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this chapter, there are authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, sums of not more than $50,000 for any one fiscal year.
