10 U.S.C. § 7325 — Computation of years of service: voluntary retirement; enlisted members
Citation: us:usc:10:7325
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7325
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 741 (Retirement For Length Of Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
For the purpose of determining whether an enlisted member of the Army may be retired under section 7314 or 7317 of this title, his years of service are computed by adding all active service in the armed forces and service computed under section 3683 1 of this title.
(b)
Time required to be made up under section 972(a) of this title may not be counted in determining years of service under subsection (a).
