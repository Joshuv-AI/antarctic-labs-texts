42 C.F.R. § 59.214 — Additional conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:59.214
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-59.214
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 59 (Grants For Family Planning Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may with respect to any grant award impose additional conditions prior to or at the time of any award when in his judgment such conditions are necessary to assure or protect advancement of the approved project, the interests of public health, or the conservation of grant funds.
