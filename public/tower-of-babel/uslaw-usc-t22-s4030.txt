22 U.S.C. § 4030 — Suitability reviews for Foreign Service Institute instructors
Citation: us:usc:22:4030
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4030
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter VII (Career Development, Training, And Orientation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall take reasonable steps to ensure that all instructors at the Foreign Service Institute, including direct hires and contractors, who provide language instruction are—
(1)
subject to suitability reviews and background investigations; and
(2)
subject to periodic background checks or reinvestigations to the extent consistent with Department and Executive policy for other Department personnel.
