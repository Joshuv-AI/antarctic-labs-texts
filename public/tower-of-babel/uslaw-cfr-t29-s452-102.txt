29 C.F.R. § 452.102 — Notice in mail ballot election.
Citation: us:cfr:29:452.102
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-452.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 452 (General Statement Concerning The Election Provisions Of The Labor-Management Reporting And Disclosure Act Of 1959). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the election is conducted by mail and no separate notice is mailed to the members, the ballots must be mailed to the members no later than fifteen days prior to the date when they must be mailed back in order to be counted.
