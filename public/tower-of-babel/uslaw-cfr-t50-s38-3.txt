50 C.F.R. § 38.3 — Executive authority; duration.
Citation: us:cfr:50:38.3
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-38.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 38 (Midway Atoll National Wildlife Refuge). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The executive authority of the Secretary of the Interior over the Midway Islands will be exercised by the Service Regional Director. The executive authority of the Service Regional Director may be redelegated to the Refuge Manager, Midway Atoll National Wildlife Refuge.
