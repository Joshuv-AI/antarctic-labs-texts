43 C.F.R. § 10010.19 — Format.
Citation: us:cfr:43:10010.19
Creator: Utah Reclamation Mitigation And Conservation Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-10010.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 10010 (Policies And Procedures For Implementing The National Environmental Policy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An EA may be prepared in any format useful to facilitate planning and decision making.
(b) An EA may be combined with any other planning or decision making document; however, that portion which analyzes the environmental impacts of the proposal and alternatives will be clearly and separately identified and not spread throughout or interwoven into other sections of the document.
