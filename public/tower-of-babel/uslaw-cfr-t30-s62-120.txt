30 C.F.R. § 62.120 — Action level.
Citation: us:cfr:30:62.120
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-62.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 62 (Occupational Noise Exposure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If during any work shift a miner's noise exposure equals or exceeds the action level the mine operator must enroll the miner in a hearing conservation program that complies with § 62.150 of this part.
