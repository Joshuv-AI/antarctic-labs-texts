16 U.S.C. § 6814 — Limitation on use of fees for employee bonuses
Citation: us:usc:16:6814
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6814
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 87 (Federal Lands Recreation Enhancement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, fees collected under the authorities of the chapter may not be used for employee bonuses.
