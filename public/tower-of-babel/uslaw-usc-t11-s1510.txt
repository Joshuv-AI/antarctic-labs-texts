11 U.S.C. § 1510 — Limited jurisdiction
Citation: us:usc:11:1510
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1510
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 15 (Ancillary And Other Cross-Border Cases); Subchapter II (Access Of Foreign Representatives And Creditors To The Court). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The sole fact that a foreign representative files a petition under section 1515 does not subject the foreign representative to the jurisdiction of any court in the United States for any other purpose.
