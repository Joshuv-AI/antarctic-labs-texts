22 C.F.R. § 401.21 — Consultation.
Citation: us:cfr:22:401.21
Creator: International Joint Commission, United States And Canada
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-401.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 401 (Rules Of Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may meet or consult with the applicant, the Governments and other persons or their counsel at any time regarding the plan of hearing, the mode of conducting the inquiry, the admitting or proof of certain facts or for any other purpose.
