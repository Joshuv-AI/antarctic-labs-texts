46 C.F.R. § 294.7 — Procedural waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:46:294.7
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-294.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 294 (Tanker Security Program (Tsp)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In consultation with DOW, MARAD may, at MARAD's own initiation or in response to a request by an interested party, after a finding of good cause, suspend, revoke, amend, or waive any requirement of the regulations in this part, subject to the provisions of the Administrative Procedure Act and any statutory limitations.
