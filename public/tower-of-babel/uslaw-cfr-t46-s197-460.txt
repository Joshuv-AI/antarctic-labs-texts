46 C.F.R. § 197.460 — Diving equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:46:197.460
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-197.460
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 197 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The diving supervisor shall insure that the diving equipment designated for use in a dive under § 197.346 is inspected before each dive.
