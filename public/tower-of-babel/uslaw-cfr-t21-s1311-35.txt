21 C.F.R. § 1311.35 — Number of CSOS digital certificates needed.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1311.35
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1311.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1311 (Requirements For Electronic Orders And Prescriptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A purchaser of Schedule I and II controlled substances must obtain a separate CSOS certificate for each registered location for which the purchaser will order these controlled substances.
