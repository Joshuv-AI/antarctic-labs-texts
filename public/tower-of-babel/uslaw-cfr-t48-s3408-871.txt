48 C.F.R. § 3408.871 — 3408.871 Paperwork reduction.
Citation: us:cfr:48:3408.871
Creator: Chapter 34—Department Of Education Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-3408.871
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 3408 (Required Sources Of Supplies And Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer must insert the clause at 3452.208-72 (Paperwork Reduction Act) in all solicitations and contracts in which the contractor will develop forms or documents for public use.
