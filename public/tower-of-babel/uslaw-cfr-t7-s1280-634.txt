7 C.F.R. § 1280.634 — Confidentiality.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1280.634
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1280.634
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1280 (Lamb Promotion, Research, And Information Order). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The names of persons voting in the referendum and ballots shall be confidential and the contents of the ballots shall not be divulged except as the Secretary may direct. The public may witness the opening of the ballot box and the counting of the votes but may not interfere with the process.
