25 C.F.R. § 273.152 — When is the annual report due?
Citation: us:cfr:25:273.152
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-273.152
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 273 (Education Contracts Under Johnson-O'Malley Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All existing contracting parties must submit the annual report to the BIE Director on or before September 15 of each year and covering the previous academic year.
