22 U.S.C. § 6982 — Administrative authorities
Citation: us:usc:22:6982
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6982
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 77 (United States-China Relations); Subchapter IV (Trade And Rule Of Law Issues In The People’S Republic Of China); Part B (Assistance To Develop Commercial And Labor Rule Of Law). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out the programs authorized by section 6981 of this title, the Secretary of Commerce and the Secretary of Labor (in consultation with the Secretary of State) may utilize any of the authorities contained in the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961 [22 U.S.C. 2151 et seq.] and the Foreign Service Act of 1980 [22 U.S.C. 3901 et seq.].
