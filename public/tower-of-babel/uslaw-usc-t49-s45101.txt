49 U.S.C. § 45101 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:49:45101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section45101
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart iii (Safety); Chapter 451 (Alcohol And Controlled Substances Testing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, “controlled substance” means any substance under section 102 of the Comprehensive Drug Abuse Prevention and Control Act of 1970 (21 U.S.C. 802) specified by the Administrator of the Federal Aviation Administration.
