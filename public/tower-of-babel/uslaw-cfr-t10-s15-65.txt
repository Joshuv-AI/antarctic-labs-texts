10 C.F.R. § 15.65 — Referral of a compromise offer.
Citation: us:cfr:10:15.65
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-15.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 15 (Debt Collection Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The NRC may refer a debtor's firm written offer of compromise, which is substantial in amount, to the Civil Division or other appropriate litigating division in DOJ using a CCLR accompanied by supporting data and particulars concerning the debt.
