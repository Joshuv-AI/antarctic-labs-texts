23 C.F.R. § 752.6 — Scenic overlooks.
Citation: us:cfr:23:752.6
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-752.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 752 (Landscape And Roadside Development). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Scenic overlooks shall be located and designed as appropriate to the site and the scenic view with consideration for safety, access, and convenience of the motorist. Scenic overlooks may provide facilities equivalent to those provided in safety rest area.
