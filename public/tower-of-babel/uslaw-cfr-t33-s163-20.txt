33 C.F.R. § 163.20 — Bunching of tows.
Citation: us:cfr:33:163.20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-163.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 163 (Towing Of Barges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In all cases where tows can be bunched, it should be done.
(b) Tows navigating in the North and East Rivers of New York must be bunched above a line drawn between Robbins Reef Light and Owls Head, Brooklyn, but the quarantine anchorage and the north entrance to Ambrose Channel shall be avoided in the process of bunching tows.
(c) Tows must be bunched above the mouth of the Schuylkill River, Pa.
