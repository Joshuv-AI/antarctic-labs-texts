5 C.F.R. § 1303.10 — Availability of proactive disclosures.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1303.10
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1303.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1303 (Public Information Provisions Of The Administrative Procedures Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OMB makes available records that are required by the FOIA to be made available for public inspection in an electronic format. OMB information pertaining to matters issued, adopted, or promulgated by OMB that is within the scope of 5 U.S.C. 552(a)(2) is available electronically on OMB's website at www.whitehouse.gov/omb/. Additionally, for help accessing these materials, you may contact OMB's FOIA Officer at (202) 395-3642.
