43 C.F.R. § 4.1365 — Status of decision pending administrative review.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.1365
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.1365
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The filing of a request for review shall not stay the effectiveness of the OSMRE decision pending completion of administrative review.
