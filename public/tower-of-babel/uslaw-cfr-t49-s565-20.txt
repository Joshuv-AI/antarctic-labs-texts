49 C.F.R. § 565.20 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:565.20
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-565.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 565 (Vehicle Identification Number (Vin) Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part specifies the format, content and physical requirements for a vehicle identification number (VIN) system and its installation to simplify vehicle identification information retrieval and to increase the accuracy and efficiency of vehicle recall campaigns.
