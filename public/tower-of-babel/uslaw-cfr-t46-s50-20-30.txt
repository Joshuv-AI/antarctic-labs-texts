46 C.F.R. § 50.20-30 — -30 Alternative materials or methods of construction.
Citation: us:cfr:46:50.20-30
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-50.20-30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 50 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When new or alternative materials, procedures, designs, or methods of construction are submitted for approval and are not addressed by regulations, the Commandant will act to approve or disapprove.
