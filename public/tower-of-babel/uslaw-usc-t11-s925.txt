11 U.S.C. § 925 — Effect of list of claims
Citation: us:usc:11:925
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section925
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 9 (Adjustment Of Debts Of A Municipality); Subchapter II (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A proof of claim is deemed filed under section 501 of this title for any claim that appears in the list filed under section 924 of this title, except a claim that is listed as disputed, contingent, or unliquidated.
