5 C.F.R. § 300.507 — Documentation and oversight.
Citation: us:cfr:5:300.507
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-300.507
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 300 (Employment (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies are required to maintain records and provide oversight to establish that their use of temporary help service firms is consistent with these regulations. As needed, OPM may require agencies to provide information on their use of temporary help service firms.
