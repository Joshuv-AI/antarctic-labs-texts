46 C.F.R. § 159.001-2 — -2 Right of appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:46:159.001-2
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-159.001-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 159 (Approval Of Equipment And Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person directly affected by a decision or action taken under this subchapter, by or on behalf of the Coast Guard, may appeal to the Deputy for Operations Policy and Capabilities (CG-DCO-D) as provided in § 1.03-15 of this chapter.
