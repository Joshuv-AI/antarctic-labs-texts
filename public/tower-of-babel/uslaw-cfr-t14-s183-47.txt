14 C.F.R. § 183.47 — Qualifications.
Citation: us:cfr:14:183.47
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-183.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 183 (Representatives Of The Administrator). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To qualify for consideration as an ODA, the applicant must—
(a) Have sufficient facilities, resources, and personnel, to perform the functions for which authorization is requested;
(b) Have sufficient experience with FAA requirements, processes, and procedures to perform the functions for which authorization is requested; and
(c) Have sufficient, relevant experience to perform the functions for which authorization is requested.
