24 C.F.R. § 3282.502 — Departmental implementation.
Citation: us:cfr:24:3282.502
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-3282.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 3282 (Manufactured Home Procedural And Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent that SAAs or any parties contracting with the Secretary do not perform functions called for under these regulations, those functions shall be carried out by the Secretary with its own personnel or through other appropriate parties.
