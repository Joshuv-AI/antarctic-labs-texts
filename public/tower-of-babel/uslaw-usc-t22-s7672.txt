22 U.S.C. § 7672 — Sense of Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 83 (United States Leadership Against Hiv/Aids, Tuberculosis, And Malaria); Subchapter IV (Authorization Of Appropriations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Increase in HIV/AIDS antiretroviral treatment
It is a sense of the Congress that an urgent priority of United States assistance programs to fight HIV/AIDS should be the rapid increase in distribution of antiretroviral treatment so that—
(1)
by the end of fiscal year 2004, at least 500,000 individuals with HIV/AIDS are receiving antiretroviral treatment through United States assistance programs;
(2)
by the end of fiscal year 2005, at least 1,000,000 such individuals are receiving such treatment; and
(3)
by the end of fiscal year 2006, at least 2,000,000 such individuals are receiving such treatment.
(b) Effective distribution of HIV/AIDS funds
It is the sense of Congress that, of the amounts appropriated pursuant to the authorization of appropriations under section 7671 of this title for HIV/AIDS assistance, 10 percent should be used for orphans and vulnerable children.
