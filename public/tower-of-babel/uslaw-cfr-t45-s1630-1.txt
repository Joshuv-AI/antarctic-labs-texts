45 C.F.R. § 1630.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1630.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1630.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1630 (Cost Standards And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is intended to provide uniform standards for allowability of costs and to provide a comprehensive, fair, timely, and flexible process for the resolution of questioned costs.
