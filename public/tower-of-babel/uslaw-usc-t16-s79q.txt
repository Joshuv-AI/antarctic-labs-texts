16 U.S.C. § 79q — Pledge of full faith and credit of United States for payment of compensation for lands, etc., taken
Citation: us:usc:16:79q
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section79q
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter VII (Redwood National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Congress further acknowledges and directs that the full faith and credit of the United States is pledged to the prompt payment of just compensation as provided for by the fifth amendment to the Constitution of the United States for those lands and properties taken by this Act.
