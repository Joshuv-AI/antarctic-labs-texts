42 C.F.R. § 456.112 — Records and reports.
Citation: us:cfr:42:456.112
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-456.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 456 (Utilization Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The UR plan must describe—
(a) The types of records that are kept by the committee; and
(b) The type and frequency of committee reports and arrangements for their distribution to appropriate individuals.
