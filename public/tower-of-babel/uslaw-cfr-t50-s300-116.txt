50 C.F.R. § 300.116 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:50:300.116
Creator: International Fishing And Related Activities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-300.116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 300 (International Fisheries Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person or harvesting vessel found to be in violation of the Act, this subpart, or any permit issued under this subpart will be subject to the civil and criminal penalty provisions and forfeiture provisions prescribed in the Act, 15 CFR part 904, and other applicable laws.
