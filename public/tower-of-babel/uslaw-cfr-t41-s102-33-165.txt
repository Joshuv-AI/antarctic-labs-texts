41 C.F.R. § 102-33.165 — -33.165 Carrying passengers on Government aircraft.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.165
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.165
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You may carry passengers only on aircraft that you operate or require contractually to be operated in accordance with the rules and requirements in 14 CFR; and
(b) For certain kinds of travel, your agency must justify passengers' presence on Government aircraft.
