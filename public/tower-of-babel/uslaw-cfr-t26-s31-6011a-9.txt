26 C.F.R. § 31.6011(a)-9 — (a)-9 Instructions to forms control as to which form is to be used.
Citation: us:cfr:26:31.6011(a)-9
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-31.6011(a)-9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 31 (Employment Taxes And Collection Of Income Tax At Source). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding provisions in this part which specify the use of a particular form for a return or other document required by this part, the use of a different form may be required by the latter form's instructions. In such case, the latter form shall be completed in accordance with its instructions.
