5 C.F.R. § 6.5 — Assignment of excepted employees.
Citation: us:cfr:5:6.5
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-6.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 6 (Exceptions From The Competitive Service (Rule Vi)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person who is serving under an excepted appointment shall be assigned to the work of a position in the competitive service without prior approval of OPM.
