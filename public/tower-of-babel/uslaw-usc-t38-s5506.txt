38 U.S.C. § 5506 — Definition of “fiduciary”
Citation: us:usc:38:5506
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section5506
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part IV (General Administrative Provisions); Chapter 55 (Minors, Incompetents, And Other Wards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this chapter and chapter 61 of this title, the term “fiduciary” means—
(1)
a person who is a guardian, curator, conservator, committee, or person legally vested with the responsibility or care of a claimant (or a claimant’s estate) or of a beneficiary (or a beneficiary’s estate); or
(2)
any other person having been appointed in a representative capacity to receive money paid under any of the laws administered by the Secretary for the use and benefit of a minor, incompetent, or other beneficiary.
