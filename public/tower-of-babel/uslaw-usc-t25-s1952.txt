25 U.S.C. § 1952 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:25:1952
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1952
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 21 (Indian Child Welfare); Subchapter III (Recordkeeping, Information Availability, And Timetables). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Within one hundred and eighty days after November 8, 1978, the Secretary shall promulgate such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
