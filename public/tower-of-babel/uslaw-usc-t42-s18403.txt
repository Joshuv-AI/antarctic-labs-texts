42 U.S.C. § 18403 — Goal for Agency space technology
Citation: us:usc:42:18403
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section18403
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 159 (Space Exploration, Technology, And Science); Subchapter VIII (Aeronautics And Space Technology). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is critical that NASA maintain an Agency space technology base that helps align mission directorate investments and supports long term needs to complement mission-directorate funded research and support, where appropriate, multiple users, building upon its Innovative Partnerships Program and other partnering approaches.
