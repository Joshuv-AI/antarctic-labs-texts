7 C.F.R. § 1782.16 — Defeasance of Agency indebtedness.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1782.16
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1782.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1782 (Servicing Of Water And Waste Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Defeasance, or amending outstanding loan instruments and agreements to permit defeasance of Agency debt instruments, is prohibited.
