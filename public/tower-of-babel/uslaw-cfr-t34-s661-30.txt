34 C.F.R. § 661.30 — How does the Secretary evaluate an application?
Citation: us:cfr:34:661.30
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-661.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 661 (Business And International Education Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary evaluates an application for a grant under this program on the basis of the criteria in § 661.31. The Secretary informs applicants of the maximum possible score for each criterion in the application package or in a notice published in the Federal Register.
