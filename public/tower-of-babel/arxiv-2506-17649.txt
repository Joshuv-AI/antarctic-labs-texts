On K K -stability of one-nodal prime Fano threefolds of genus 12 12
Elena Denisova, Anne-Sophie Kaloghiros

Abstract
. We show that general one-nodal prime Fano threefolds of genus 12 12 are K-polystable.

2.
Preliminary results on explicit K-stability of Fano threefolds

All varieties considered are defined over
\mathbb{C}
.
Let
X
be a Fano variety with at most Kawamata log terminal
singularities of dimension
n\geq 2
, and let
G
be a reductive subgroup in
\mathrm{Aut}(X)
.
Let
\beta(\Xi)=A_{X}(\Xi)-S_{X}(\Xi)
,
where
A_{X}(\Xi)
is the log discrepancy of the divisor
\Xi
.

Theorem 2.1
.

[
13
, Corollary 4.14]

Suppose that
\beta(\Xi)>0
for every
G
-invariant prime divisor
\Xi
over
X
.
Then
X
is
K
-polystable.

Recall the definition of the number
\alpha_{G,Z}(X)
, where
Z\subset X
is a
G
-invariant subvariety:

\alpha_{G,Z}(X)=\mathrm{sup}\left\{\lambda\in\mathbb{Q}\ \left|\ \begin{aligned} &\text{the~pair}\ \left(X,\lambda D\right)\ \text{is log canonical at general point of $Z$ for any},\\
&\text{effective $G$-invariant $\mathbb{Q}$-divisor}\ D\ \text{on}\ X\ \text{such that}\ D\sim_{\mathbb{Q}}-K_{X}\\
\end{aligned}\right.\right\}.

Then
\alpha_{G}(X)\leqslant\alpha_{G,Z}(X)
.

Lemma 2.2
.

[
1
, 1.44]

Let
f\colon\widetilde{X}\to X
be an arbitrary
G
-equivariant birational morphism, let
\Xi
be a
G
-invariant prime divisor in
X
such that
Z\subseteq f(\Xi)
, then we have

\frac{A_{X}(\Xi)}{S_{X}(\Xi)}\geqslant\frac{n+1}{n}\alpha_{G,Z}(X).

In particular, the existence of a
G
-invariant divisor
\Xi
over
X
with
\beta(\Xi)<0
and
Z\subset c_{X}(\Xi)
implies that
\alpha_{G,Z}(X)<\frac{3}{4}
, so that
Z
is contained in
\Nklt(X,B_{X})
for some
B_{X}\sim_{\mathbb{Q}}-\lambda K_{X}
and rational number
\lambda<\frac{3}{4}
.

Theorem 2.3
.

[
1
, Corollary 1.110]

Let
X
be a smooth Fano threefold, let
Y
be an irreducible normal surface in the threefold
X
,
let
Z
be an irreducible curve in
Y
, and let
\Xi
be a prime divisor over the threefold
X
such that
C_{X}(\Xi)=Z
. Then

\frac{A_{X}(\Xi)}{S_{X}(\Xi)}\geqslant\min\Bigg\{\frac{1}{S_{X}(Y)},\frac{1}{S\big(W^{Y}_{\bullet,\bullet};Z\big)}\Bigg\}

and

S\big(W^{Y}_{\bullet,\bullet};Z\big)=\frac{3}{(-K_{X})^{3}}\int_{0}^{\tau}\big(P(u)^{2}\cdot Y\big)\cdot\ord_{Z}\Big(N(u)\big|_{Y}\Big)du+\\
+\frac{3}{(-K_{X})^{3}}\int_{0}^{\tau}\int_{0}^{\infty}\mathrm{vol}\big(P(u)\big|_{Y}-vZ\big)dvdu

where
P(u)
is the positive part of the Zariski decomposition of the divisor
-K_{X}-uY
, and
N(u)
is its negative part.

Remark 2.4
.

An expression for

can be computed in the more general context of
\mathbb{Q}
-factorial Mori Dream spaces
[
1

We recall a few results on nonklt centres of pairs
(X,B_{X})
where

for

when

admits morphisms to projective spaces.

(

Suppose

Lemma 5.3
.

Let
\Xi

∎

Remark 5.4
