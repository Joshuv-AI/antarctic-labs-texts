42 C.F.R. § 417.474 — Effective date and term of contract.
Citation: us:cfr:42:417.474
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-417.474
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 417 (Health Maintenance Organizations, Competitive Medical Plans, And Health Care Prepayment Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Effective date. The contract must specify its effective date, which may not be earlier than the date it is signed by both CMS and the HMO or CMP.
(b) Term. The contract must specify the duration of its term as follows:
(1) For the initial term, at least 12 months, but no more than 23 months.
(2) For any subsequent term, 12 months.
