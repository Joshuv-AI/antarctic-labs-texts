5 C.F.R. § 843.207 — Agent of next of kin.
Citation: us:cfr:5:843.207
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-843.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 843 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Death Benefits And Employee Refunds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a deceased employee or retiree has not named a beneficiary and one of the next of kin entitled makes a claim for the accrued benefit, other next of kin entitled to share in the unexpended balance or accrued benefit may designate the one who made the claim to act as their agent to receive their distributive shares.
