10 C.F.R. § 708.41 — Referral to another agency.
Citation: us:cfr:10:708.41
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-708.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 708 (Doe Contractor Employee Protection Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding the provisions of this part, the Secretary of Energy retains the right to request that a complaint filed under this part be accepted by another Federal agency for investigation and factual determinations.
