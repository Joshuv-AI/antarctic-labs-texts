49 C.F.R. § 71.13 — Samoa zone.
Citation: us:cfr:49:71.13
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-71.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 71 (Standard Time Zone Boundaries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The eighth zone, the Samoa standard time zone, includes that part of the United States that is between 169 degrees 30 minutes west longitude and 172 degrees 30 minutes west longitude, but does not include any part of the States of Hawaii and Alaska.
