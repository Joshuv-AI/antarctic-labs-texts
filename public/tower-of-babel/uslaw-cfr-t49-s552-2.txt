49 C.F.R. § 552.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:552.2
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-552.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 552 (Petitions For Rulemaking, Defect, And Noncompliance Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to enable the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration to identify and respond on a timely basis to petitions for rulemaking or defect or noncompliance decisions, and to inform the public of the procedures following in response to such petitions.
