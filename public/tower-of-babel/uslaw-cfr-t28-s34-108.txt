28 C.F.R. § 34.108 — Selection of reviewers.
Citation: us:cfr:28:34.108
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-34.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 34 (Ojjdp Competition And Peer Review Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Program Manager, through the Director of the OJJDP program division with responsibility for a particular program or project will propose a selection of peer reviewers from an extensive and varied pool of juvenile justice and delinquency prevention experts for approval by the Administrator. The selection process for peer reviewers is detailed in the OJJDP “Peer Review Guideline”.
