7 C.F.R. § 654.10 — Operation and maintenance agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:7:654.10
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-654.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 654 (Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A duly authorized official of the sponsor(s) must execute an O&M agreement with NRCS prior to NRCS furnishing financial assistance.
