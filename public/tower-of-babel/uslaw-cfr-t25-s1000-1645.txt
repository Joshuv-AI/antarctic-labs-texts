25 C.F.R. § 1000.1645 — Is FTCA the exclusive remedy for a tort claim, including a claim concerning personal injury or death, resulting from the performance of a compact or funding agreement?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.1645
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.1645
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1000 (Annual Funding Agreements Under The Tribal Self-Government Act Amendments To The Indian Self-Determination And Education Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, except as explained in § 1000.1610(b). No claim may be filed against a Tribe/Consortium or employee based upon performance of a compact or funding agreement. All claims shall be filed against the United States and are subject to the limitations and restrictions of FTCA.
