The

In the inclusive tagging approach, all reconstructed particles other than the signal are used to define the tag-

B\to K\nu\bar{\nu}

15
]

6
Conclusion

Electroweak penguin and lepton-flavor-violating
B
decays provide sensitive probes of the Standard Model and beyond. Recent results from Belle and Belle II demonstrate the competitiveness, and in some cases the uniqueness, of
B
-factories compared to other flavor experiments
[
1
]
. While analyzing and accumulating more Run 2 data, Belle II is refining its methodologies and exploring new approaches to enhance sensitivity in
B
decays with missing energy, as well as establishing frameworks for the quantitative reinterpretation of its results.
