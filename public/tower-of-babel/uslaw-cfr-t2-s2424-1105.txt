2 C.F.R. § 2424.1105 — Who may issue a limited denial of participation?
Citation: us:cfr:2:2424.1105
Creator: Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2424.1105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2424 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary designates HUD officials who are authorized to impose a limited denial of participation, affecting any participant and/or their affiliates, except mortgagees approved by the Federal Housing Administration (FHA).
