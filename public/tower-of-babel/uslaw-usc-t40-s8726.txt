40 U.S.C. § 8726 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:40:8726
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section8726
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part D (Public Buildings, Grounds, And Parks In The District Of Columbia); Chapter 87 (Physical Development Of National Capital Region); Subchapter III (Planning Process). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amounts necessary to carry out this subchapter may be appropriated from money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated and from any appropriate appropriation law, except the annual District of Columbia Appropriation Act.
