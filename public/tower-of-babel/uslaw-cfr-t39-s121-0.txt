39 C.F.R. § 121.0 — Market-dominant product service standards.
Citation: us:cfr:39:121.0
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-121.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 121 (Service Standards For Market-Dominant Mail Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Service standards in this part are contingent upon proper acceptance before the applicable day-zero Critical Entry Time (CET). Applying the service standards appearing in this part, effective service standards for combinations of 5-digit originating ZIP Codes and 5-digit destinating ZIP Codes can be found in a lookup table at www.usps.com.
