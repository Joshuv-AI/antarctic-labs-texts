29 C.F.R. § 530.10 — Delegation of authority to grant, deny, or cancel an individual homeworker certificate.
Citation: us:cfr:29:530.10
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-530.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 530 (Employment Of Homeworkers In Certain Industries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may from time to time designate and appoint members of the Administrator's staff or State Agencies as his authorized representatives with full power and authority to grant, deny, or cancel homework certificates.
