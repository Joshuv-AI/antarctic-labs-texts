Higher Genus Gromov-Witten Invariants from Projective Bundles on Smooth Log Calabi-Yau pairs
Benjamin Zhou

Abstract
. Let ( X , E ) (X,E) be a smooth log Calabi-Yau pair consisting of a smooth Fano surface X X and a smooth anti-canonical divisor E E . We obtain certain higher genus local Gromov-Witten invariants from the projectivization of the canonical bundle Z := ℙ ⁡ ( K X ⊕ 𝒪 X ) Z:=\mathbb{P}(K_{X}\oplus\mathcal{O}_{X}) , using the degeneration formula for stable log maps [ KLR23 ] . We evaluate an invariant in the degeneration using the relationship between q q -refined tropical curve counting and logarithmic Gromov-Witten theory with λ g \lambda_{g} -insertion [ Bou19 ] . As a corollary, we use flops to prove a blow up formula for higher genus invariants of Z Z . Additionally assuming X X is toric, we prove an all-genus correspondence between open invariants of an outer Aganagic-Vafa brane L ⊂ K X L\subset K_{X} and closed invariants of Z Z that generalizes a genus-0 open-closed equality of [ Cha11 ] to all-genus, by using an argument in [ GRZZ25 ] .

Introduction

A log Calabi-Yau pair
(X,E)
consists of a smooth complex projective surface with a possibly singular anti-canonical divisor
E\in|-K_{X}|

[
BBvG24
]
. A smooth log Calabi-Yau pair
(X,E)
additionally requires

; by adjunction, the anti-canonical divisor
E
is a smooth elliptic curve. For smooth log Calabi-Yau pairs, the logarithmic Gromov-Witten theory of
X
with multiple contact orders along

is equated with tropical curve counting
[
Gra22
]
, as well as with open Gromov-Witten invariants of an outer Aganagic-Vafa brane in

in all-genus
[
GRZ24
]

[
GRZZ25
]
.

Log Calabi-Yau pairs also provide 2-dimensional examples of Gross-Siebert mirror symmetry
[
GHK15
]

[
GS16
]
. Roughly, the Gross-Siebert program associates to

a mirror dual
\check{X}
, which is a quasiprojective variety obtained by gluing together cluster torus charts via wall crossing transformations. The mirror dual
\check{X}
is given by the spectrum of an algebra

defined by
theta functions

\theta_{p}:\check{X}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}
which count

, or the tropical analogue of holomorphic discs, in
\check{X}

[
GS16
]

[
GHK15
]
. Let

be the monoid of effective curve classes. For smooth log Calabi-Yau pairs

,

is an
\mathbb{N}
-graded algebra indexed by theta functions

for

The multiplication rule
