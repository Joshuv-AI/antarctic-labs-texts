33 U.S.C. § 48 — Noxubee River, Mississippi
Citation: us:usc:33:48
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section48
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 1 (Navigable Waters Generally); Subchapter II (Waters Declared Nonnavigable: Change Of Name). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

That portion of the Noxubee River in Noxubee County, in the State of Mississippi is declared to be a nonnavigable stream within the meaning of the Constitution and laws of the United States.
The right of Congress to amend or repeal this section is expressly reserved.
