12 U.S.C. § 3422 — Applicability to Securities and Exchange Commission
Citation: us:usc:12:3422
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section3422
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 35 (Right To Financial Privacy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 [15 U.S.C. 78a et seq.], this chapter shall apply with respect to the Securities and Exchange Commission.
