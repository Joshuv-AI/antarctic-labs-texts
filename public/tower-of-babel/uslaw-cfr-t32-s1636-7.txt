32 C.F.R. § 1636.7 — Impartiality.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1636.7
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1636.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1636 (Classification Of Conscientious Objectors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Boards may not give preferential treatment to one religion over another, and all beliefs whether of a religious, ethical, or moral nature are to be given equal consideration.
