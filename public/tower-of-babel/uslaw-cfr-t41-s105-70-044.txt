41 C.F.R. § 105-70.044 — -70.044 Right to administrative offset.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-70.044
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-70.044
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-70 (70—Implementation Of The Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Of 1986). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The amount of any penalty or assessment which has become final, or for which a judgment has been entered under § 105-70.042 or § 105-70.043, or any amount agreed upon in a compromise or settlement under § 105-70.046, may be collected by administrative offset under 30 U.S.C. 3716, except that an administrative offset may not be made under this subsection against a refund of an overpayment of Federal taxes, then or later owing by the United States to the defendant.
