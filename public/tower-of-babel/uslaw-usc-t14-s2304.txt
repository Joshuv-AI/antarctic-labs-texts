14 U.S.C. § 2304 — Compulsory retirement at age of sixty-two
Citation: us:usc:14:2304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2304
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 23 (Personnel; Enlisted). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any enlisted member who has reached the age of sixty-two shall be retired from active service.
