26 U.S.C. § 5871 — Penalties
Citation: us:usc:26:5871
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5871
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 53 (Machine Guns, Destructive Devices, And Certain Other Firearms); Subchapter D (Penalties And Forfeitures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who violates or fails to comply with any provision of this chapter shall, upon conviction, be fined not more than $10,000, or be imprisoned not more than ten years, or both.
