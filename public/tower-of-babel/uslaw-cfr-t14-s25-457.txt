14 C.F.R. § 25.457 — Wing flaps.
Citation: us:cfr:14:25.457
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-25.457
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 25 (Airworthiness Standards: Transport Category Airplanes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Wing flaps, their operating mechanisms, and their supporting structures must be designed for critical loads occurring in the conditions prescribed in § 25.345, accounting for the loads occurring during transition from one flap position and airspeed to another.
