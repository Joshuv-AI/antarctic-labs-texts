20 C.F.R. § 658.700 — Scope and purpose of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:20:658.700
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-658.700
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 658 (Administrative Provisions Governing The Wagner-Peyser Act Employment Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth the procedures which the Department must follow upon either discovering independently or receiving from other(s) information indicating that SWAs may not be adhering to ES regulations.
