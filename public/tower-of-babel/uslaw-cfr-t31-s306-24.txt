31 C.F.R. § 306.24 — Collection of fees on definitive securities.
Citation: us:cfr:31:306.24
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-306.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 306 (General Regulations Governing U.S. Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A fee shall be charged for each registered security, as defined in § 306.115 (a), issued as a result of a transfer, exchange, reissue, withdrawal from book-entry, or the granting of relief on account of loss, theft, destruction, mutilation, or defacement. The applicable fee, and the basis for its determination, will be published by notice in the Federal Register.
