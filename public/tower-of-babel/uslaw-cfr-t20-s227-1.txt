20 C.F.R. § 227.1 — Introduction.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 227 (Computing Supplemental Annuities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part explains how to compute a supplemental annuity. A supplemental annuity is payable to an employee who meets the requirements in § 216.12 of this chapter.
