47 C.F.R. § 212.4 — Other requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:47:212.4
Creator: Office Of Science And Technology Policy And National Security Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-212.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 212 (Procedures For Obtaining International Telecommunication Service For Use During A Wartime Emergency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Government, other than Executive departments and agencies of the United States, having need for emergency international telecommunication service, shall present their requirements through the appropriate sponsor to NCS.
(b) The private sector, including carriers, having need for emergency international telecommunication service, shall present their requirements to the Federal Communications Commission (FCC).
