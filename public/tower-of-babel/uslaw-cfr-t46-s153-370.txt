46 C.F.R. § 153.370 — Minimum relief valve setting for ambient temperature cargo tanks.
Citation: us:cfr:46:153.370
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-153.370
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 153 (Ships Carrying Bulk Liquid, Liquefied Gas, Or Compressed Gas Hazardous Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The relief valve setting for a containment system that carries a cargo at ambient temperature must at least equal the cargo's vapor pressure at 46 °C (approx. 115 °F).
