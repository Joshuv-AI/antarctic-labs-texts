40 C.F.R. § 425.05 — Compliance dates.
Citation: us:cfr:40:425.05
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-425.05
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 425 (Leather Tanning And Finishing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The compliance date for new source performance standards (NSPS) and pretreatment standards for new sources (PSES) is the date the new source commences discharge. The compliance date for BPT effluent limitations and guidelines and pretreatment standards for existing sources to no later than March 31, 1989.
