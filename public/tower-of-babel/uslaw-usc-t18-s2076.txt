18 U.S.C. § 2076 — Clerk of United States District Court
Citation: us:usc:18:2076
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2076
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 101 (Records And Reports). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, being a clerk of a district court of the United States, willfully refuses or neglects to make or forward any report, certificate, statement, or document as required by law, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
