5 C.F.R. § 875.405 — May a spouse, domestic partner, or other qualified relative of a workforce member apply for coverage?
Citation: us:cfr:5:875.405
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-875.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 875 (Federal Long Term Care Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A spouse, domestic partner, or other qualified relative of a workforce member may apply for coverage with full underwriting at any time following the marriage or commencing date of the domestic partnership, outside of a suspension period as described in § 875.110.
