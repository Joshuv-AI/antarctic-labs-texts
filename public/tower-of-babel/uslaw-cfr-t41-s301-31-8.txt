41 C.F.R. § 301-31.8 — -31.8 Travel advance availability.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-31.8
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-31.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-31 (31—Threatened Law Enforcement/Investigative Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees may receive a travel advance under § 301-51.200 of this chapter for up to a 30-day period at a time to cover allowable expenses, subject to the requirement to reimburse the agency for any portion of the advance disallowed or not spent. The travel advance may not exceed the maximum allowable amount authorized in this part.
