42 C.F.R. § 436.10 — State plan requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:436.10
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-436.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 436 (Eligibility In Guam, Puerto Rico, And The Virgin Islands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State plan must—
(a) Provide that the requirements of this part are met; and
(b) Specify the groups to whom Medicaid is provided, as specified in subparts B, C, and D of this part, and the conditions of eligibility for individuals in those groups.
