10 U.S.C. § 742 — Rank: warrant officers
Citation: us:usc:10:742
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section742
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 43 (Rank And Command). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Among warrant officer grades, warrant officer grades of a higher numerical designation are senior to warrant officer grades of a lower numerical designation.
(b)
Rank among warrant officers of the same grade, and date of rank of warrant officers, is determined in the same manner as prescribed in section 741 of this title for officers in grades above warrant officer grades.
