48 C.F.R. § 1451.102 — 1451.102 Authorization to use Government supply sources.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1451.102
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1451.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1451 (Uses Of Government Sources By Contractors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the CO decides to authorize a contractor to use Government supply sources under the conditions prescribed in FAR 51.102, a written request for a FEDSTRIP activity address code (See FPMR 101-26.203) shall be made through the acquisition office FEDSTRIP point of contact.
