15 U.S.C. § 7312 — Construction
Citation: us:usc:15:7312
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section7312
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 99 (National Construction Safety Team). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to confer any authority on the National Institute of Standards and Technology to require the adoption of building standards, codes, or practices.
