16 C.F.R. § 308.6 — Access to information.
Citation: us:cfr:16:308.6
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-308.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 308 (Trade Regulation Rule Pursuant To The Telephone Disclosure And Dispute Resolution Act Of 1992). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any common carrier that provides telecommunication services to any provider of pay-per-call services shall make available to the Commission, upon written request, any records and financial information maintained by such carrier relating to the arrangements (other than for the provision of local exchange service) between such carrier and any provider of pay-per-call services.
