Correction: Metagenomic profiling of tobacco root endophytes reveals a disease-suppressive Enterobacter strain against Fusarium solani
Authors: Frontiers Production Office
Published: 2026

A correction has been made to the Affiliations.

In the original article, Tianmiao Li, Xiaoyu Zhou, and Fei Xiong were incorrectly linked with affiliation 2 Dali Tobacco Company of Yunnan, Dali, China. This should have been Yunnan Academy of Tobacco Agricultural Sciences, Kunming, China. Yunnan Academy of Tobacco Agricultural Sciences, Kunming, China has thus been assigned to affiliation 1, State Key Laboratory of Elemento-Organic Chemistry and Department of Chemical Biology, National Pesticide Engineering Research Center (Tianjin), Nankai University, Tianjin, China to affiliation 2, and Dali Tobacco Company of Yunnan, Dali, China to affiliation 3.

The affiliations for the other authors were correct in the original article, but their numbering has been updated to reflect this change.

The final author list and affiliations thus read as follows:

“Tianmiao Li1, 2†, Xiaoyu Zhou1, 2†, Fei Xiong1, 2†, Tianfu Zhao3, Ning Jiang1, Yongzhan Cai4, Yanxia Hu3, Canhua Lu1, Yuanhu Xuan2* and Xiaotong Gai1*
