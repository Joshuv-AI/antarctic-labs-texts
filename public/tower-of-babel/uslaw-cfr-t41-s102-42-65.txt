41 C.F.R. § 102-42.65 — -42.65 Gifts or decorations not disposed of by the Senate Commission on Art.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-42.65
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-42.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-42 (42—Utilization, Donation, And Disposal Of Foreign Gifts And Decorations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Senate Commission on Art does not dispose of a gift or decoration, it must be reported to GSA for disposal. If GSA does not dispose of the gift or decoration within one year of the Commission's reporting, the Commission may:
(a) Request that GSA return the gift or decoration for the Commission to dispose of it independently; or
(b) Allow GSA to continue disposal efforts in accordance with this part.
