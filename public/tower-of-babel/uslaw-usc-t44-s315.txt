44 U.S.C. § 315 — Branches of Government Publishing Office; limitations
Citation: us:usc:44:315
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section315
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 3 (Government Publishing Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Money appropriated by any Act may not be used for maintaining more than one branch of the Government Publishing Office in any one building occupied by an executive department of the Government, and a branch of the Government Publishing Office may not be established unless specifically authorized by law.
