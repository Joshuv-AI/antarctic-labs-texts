43 C.F.R. § 3100.33 — Option statements.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3100.33
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3100.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3100 (Oil And Gas Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each option holder must file in the proper BLM office within 90 days after June 30 and December 31 of each year a statement showing:
(a) Any changes to the statements submitted under § 3100.31(b); and
(b) The number of acres covered by each option and the total acreage of all options held in each State.
