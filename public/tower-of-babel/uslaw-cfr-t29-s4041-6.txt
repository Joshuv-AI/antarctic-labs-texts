29 C.F.R. § 4041.6 — Effect of failure to provide required information.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4041.6
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4041.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4041 (Termination Of Single-Employer Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a plan administrator fails to provide any information required under this part within the specified time limit, the PBGC may assess a penalty under section 4071 of ERISA. The PBGC may also pursue any other equitable or legal remedies available to it under the law, including, if appropriate, the issuance of a notice of noncompliance under § 4041.31.
