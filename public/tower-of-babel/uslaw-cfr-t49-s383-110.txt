49 C.F.R. § 383.110 — General requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:49:383.110
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-383.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 383 (Commercial Driver'S License Standards; Requirements And Penalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All drivers of CMVs must have the knowledge and skills necessary to operate a CMV safely as contained in this subpart. The specific types of items that a State must include in the knowledge and skills tests that it administers to CDL applicants are included in this subpart.
