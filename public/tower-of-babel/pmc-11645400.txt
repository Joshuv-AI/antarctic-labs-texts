Author Correction: Visualization of the Cdc48 AAA+ ATPase protein unfolding pathway
Authors: Ian Cooney, Heidi L Schubert, Karina Cedeno, Olivia N Fisher, Richard Carson, John C Price, Christopher P Hill, Peter S Shen
Published: 2024

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-024-51835-3, published online 29 August 2024

The original version of this Article contained an error in the Data Availability section the EMDB code EMD-42045 and associated hyperlink were incorrectly used instead of EMD-42025. The correct version of the Data availability is:

Cryo-EM reconstructions and atomic models have been deposited in the Electron Microscopy Data Bank (EMDB) and Protein Data Bank (PDB), respectively, under the following accession numbers: EMD-42076, PDB 8UB4 (Consensus); EMD-41992, PDB 8U7T (Class 1); EMD-42038, PDB 8U9P (Class 2); EMD-42057, PDB 8UAA (Class 3); EMD-42025, PDB 8U8I (Class 4); EMD-42032, PDB 8U9C (Class 5); EMD-42039, PDB 8U9Q (Class 6); EMD-42042, PDB 8U9Z (Class 7); EMD-42043, PDB 8UA0 (Class 8); EMD-42047, PDB 8UA1 (Class 9). The mass spectrometry data generated in this study have been deposited in the ProteomeXchange (PRIDE) database under accession code PXD048280.

This has been corrected in the PDF and HTML versions of the Article.
