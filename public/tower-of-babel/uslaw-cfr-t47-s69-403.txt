47 C.F.R. § 69.403 — Marketing expense (Account 6610).
Citation: us:cfr:47:69.403
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-69.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 69 (Access Charges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Marketing expense shall be apportioned among the interexchange category and all access elements in the same proportions as the combined investment that is apportioned pursuant to § 69.309.
