15 U.S.C. § 2695a — Model guidelines for siting of school facilities
Citation: us:usc:15:2695a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2695a
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 53 (Toxic Substances Control); Subchapter V (Healthy High-Performance Schools). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 18 months after December 19, 2007, the Administrator, in consultation with the Secretary of Education and the Secretary of Health and Human Services, shall issue voluntary school site selection guidelines that account for—
(1)
the special vulnerability of children to hazardous substances or pollution exposures in any case in which the potential for contamination at a potential school site exists;
(2)
modes of transportation available to students and staff;
(3)
the efficient use of energy; and
(4)
the potential use of a school at the site as an emergency shelter.
