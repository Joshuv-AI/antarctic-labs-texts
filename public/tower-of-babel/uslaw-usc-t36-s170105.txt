36 U.S.C. § 170105 — Exclusive right to name, seals, emblems, and badges
Citation: us:usc:36:170105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section170105
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1701 (Paralyzed Veterans Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation and its State and regional organizations and local chapters or posts have the exclusive right to use the name “Paralyzed Veterans of America” and seals, emblems, and badges the corporation lawfully adopts.
