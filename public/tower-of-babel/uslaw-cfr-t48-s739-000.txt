48 C.F.R. § 739.000 — Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:48:739.000
Creator: Chapter 7—Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-739.000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 739 (Acquisition Of Information Technology). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes acquisition policies and procedures for use in acquiring—
(a) Information technology, as defined in this part, consistent with the Federal Information Technology Acquisition Reform Act (FITARA).
(b) Information and communication technology (ICT), as defined in FAR 2.101.
