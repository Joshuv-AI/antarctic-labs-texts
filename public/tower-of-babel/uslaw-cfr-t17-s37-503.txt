17 C.F.R. § 37.503 — Provide information to the Commission.
Citation: us:cfr:17:37.503
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-37.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 37 (Swap Execution Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A swap execution facility shall provide information in its possession to the Commission upon request, in a form and manner that the Commission approves.
