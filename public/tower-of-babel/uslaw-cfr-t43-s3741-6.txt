43 C.F.R. § 3741.6 — Acquisition of Leasing Act minerals in lands covered by mining claims and millsites.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3741.6
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3741.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3740 (Public Law 585; Multiple Mineral Development). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Leasing Act minerals in lands covered by mining claims and millsites located after the date of the Act or validated pursuant to the Act may be acquired under the mineral leasing laws, upon appropriate application therefor being filed prior to the issuance of patent to such mining claims or millsites, or after the issuance of patent, if the patent contains a reservation of Leasing Act minerals to the United States as provided in section 4 of the Act.
