Published: 2024

Correction to: Cell Death and Disease 10.1038/s41419-021-04287-2, published online 29 October 2021

The author notes that the original version of this article contains inaccuracies in Figs. 6L and 7D.

Due to negligence in image layout, the author mistakenly pasted the image of the NC group for the control group in Fig. 6L (Vimentin). The author mistakenly pasted the transwell image of Fig. 4D (upANXA7 group) for Fig. 7D (upSRI group). The correct Figs. 6L and 7D are provided below. The modifications to Figs. 6L and 7D do not affect the results or conclusions reported in the paper.

The author apologizes for any inconvenience caused.
