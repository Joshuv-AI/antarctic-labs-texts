33 C.F.R. § 181.27 — Information displayed near hull identification number.
Citation: us:cfr:33:181.27
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-181.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 181 (Manufacturer Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

With the exception of the characters “US-”, which constitute the country of origin code for the United States, if information is displayed on the boat within 2 inches of the 12-character hull identification number (HIN), that information must be separated from the HIN by means of borders or must be on a separate label, so that it will not be interpreted as part of the hull identification number.
