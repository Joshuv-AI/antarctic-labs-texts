25 U.S.C. § 416g — Contract for water, sewerage, law enforcement, or other public services
Citation: us:usc:25:416g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section416g
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 12 (Lease, Sale, Or Surrender Of Allotted Or Unallotted Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Papago Council and the Salt River Pima-Maricopa Community Council, with the approval of the Secretary of the Interior, may contract with the State of Arizona or its political subdivisions for the furnishing of water, sewerage, law enforcement, or other public services on terms and conditions deemed advantageous to the tribe and individual Indian landowners.
