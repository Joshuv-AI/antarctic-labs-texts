49 C.F.R. § 232.602 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:232.602
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-232.602
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 232 (Brake System Safety Standards For Freight And Other Non-Passenger Trains And Equipment; End-Of-Train Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to all railroads that operate a freight car or freight train governed by this part and equipped with an ECP brake system. Unless specifically excepted or modified in this section, all of the other requirements contained in this part are applicable to a freight car or freight train equipped with an ECP brake system.
