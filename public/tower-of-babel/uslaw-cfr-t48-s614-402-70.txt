48 C.F.R. § 614.402-70 — 614.402-70 Waiver of public opening of bids.
Citation: us:cfr:48:614.402-70
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-614.402-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 614 (Sealed Bidding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Overseas posts may request waiver of the public opening of bids if that activity is inconsistent with local law or legal practice, or with post security. For that purpose, the Procurement Executive must approve a deviation in accordance with 601.470.
