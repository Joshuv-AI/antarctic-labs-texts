42 U.S.C. § 300aa–6 — Authorization of appropriations

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter XIX (Vaccines); Part 1 (National Vaccine Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
To carry out this part other than section 300aa–2(9) of this title there are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 2004 and 2005.
(b)
To carry out section 300aa–2(9) of this title there are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 2004 and 2005.
