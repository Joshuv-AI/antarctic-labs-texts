5 C.F.R. § 301.302 — Overseas appointing procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:5:301.302
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-301.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 301 (Overseas Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Overseas agencies are required to insure that selection of employees for local hire appointments in the overseas area is made on the basis of the ability, knowledge, and skills of eligible candidates, in accordance with applicable law and regulation.
