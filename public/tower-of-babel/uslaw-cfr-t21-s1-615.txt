21 C.F.R. § 1.615 — What records procedures must an accreditation body have to qualify for recognition?
Citation: us:cfr:21:1.615
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1.615
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1 (General Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An accreditation body seeking recognition must demonstrate that it has:
(a) Implemented written procedures to establish, control, and retain records (including documents and data) for the period of time necessary to meet its contractual and legal obligations pertaining to this subpart and to provide an adequate basis for evaluating its program and performance; and
(b) The capability to meet the applicable reporting and notification requirements of this subpart, if recognized.
