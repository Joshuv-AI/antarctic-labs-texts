45 C.F.R. § 1330.20 — Peer review purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1330.20
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1330.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1330 (National Institute For Disability, Independent Living, And Rehabilitation Research). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of peer review is to insure that:
(a) Those activities supported by the National Institute on Disability, Independent Living, and Rehabilitation Research (NIDILRR) are of the highest scientific, administrative, and technical quality; and
(b) Activity results may be widely applied to appropriate target populations and rehabilitation problems.
