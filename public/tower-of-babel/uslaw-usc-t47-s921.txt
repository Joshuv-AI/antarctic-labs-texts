47 U.S.C. § 921 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:47:921
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section921
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 8 (National Telecommunications And Information Administration); Subchapter II (Transfer Of Auctionable Frequencies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this subchapter:
(1)
The term “allocation” means an entry in the National Table of Frequency Allocations of a given frequency band for the purpose of its use by one or more radiocommunication services.
(2)
The term “assignment” means an authorization given to a station licensee to use specific frequencies or channels.
(3)
The term “the 1934 Act” means the Communications Act of 1934 (47 U.S.C. 151 et seq.).
