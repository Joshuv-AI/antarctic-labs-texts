9 C.F.R. § 312.1 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:9:312.1
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-312.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 312 (Official Marks, Devices And Certificates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The marks, devices, and certificates prescribed or referenced in this part shall be official marks, devices, and certificates for purposes of the Act, and shall be used in accordance with the provisions of this part and the regulations cited therein.
