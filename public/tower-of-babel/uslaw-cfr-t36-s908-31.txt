36 C.F.R. § 908.31 — Time for filing request for review.
Citation: us:cfr:36:908.31
Creator: Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-908.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 908 (Policy And Procedures To Facilitate The Retention Of Displaced Businesses And Residents In The Pennsylvania Avenue Development Area). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who files a request for review must do so within one year of the date of the determination for which review is sought.
