34 C.F.R. § 692.70 — How does the Secretary allot funds to the States?
Citation: us:cfr:34:692.70
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-692.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 692 (Leveraging Educational Assistance Partnership Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For fiscal year 2010-2011, the Secretary allots to each eligible State that applies for SLEAP funds an amount in accordance with the provisions in § 692.10 prior to calculating allotments for States applying for GAP funds under subpart C of this part.
