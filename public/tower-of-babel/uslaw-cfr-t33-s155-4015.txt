33 C.F.R. § 155.4015 — Vessel owners and operators who must follow this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:33:155.4015
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-155.4015
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 155 (Oil Or Hazardous Material Pollution Prevention Regulations For Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must follow this subpart if your vessel carries group I-IV oils, and is required by § 155.1015 or § 155.5015 to have a vessel response plan.
