49 U.S.C. § 47532 — Judicial review
Citation: us:usc:49:47532
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section47532
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Airport Development And Noise); Chapter 475 (Noise); Subchapter II (National Aviation Noise Policy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An action taken by the Secretary of Transportation under any of sections 47528–47531 or 47534 of this title is subject to judicial review as provided under section 46110 of this title.
