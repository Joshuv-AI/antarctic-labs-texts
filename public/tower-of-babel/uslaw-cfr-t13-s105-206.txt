13 C.F.R. § 105.206 — Applicable rules and directions.
Citation: us:cfr:13:105.206
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-105.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 105 (Standards Of Conduct And Employee Restrictions And Responsibilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every employee shall follow all agency rules, regulations, operating procedures, instructions and other proper directions in the performance of his official functions.
