20 C.F.R. § 416.1423 — Expedited appeals process—general.
Citation: us:cfr:20:416.1423
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-416.1423
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 416 (Supplemental Security Income For The Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

By using the expedited appeals process you may go directly to a Federal district court without first completing the administrative review process that is generally required before the court will hear your case.
