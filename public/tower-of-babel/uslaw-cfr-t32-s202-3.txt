32 C.F.R. § 202.3 — Notification of formation of a Restoration Advisory Board.
Citation: us:cfr:32:202.3
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-202.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 202 (Restoration Advisory Boards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prior to establishing a RAB, an installation shall notify potential stakeholders of its intent to form a RAB. In announcing the formation of a RAB, the installation should describe the purpose of a RAB and discuss opportunities for membership.
