41 C.F.R. § 102-71.200 — -71.200 No applications approved.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-71 (71—Real Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) At the end of the 30-day holding period described in § 102-71.170(a), HHS will notify GSA, or the landholding agency, as appropriate, if an expression of interest has been received for a certain property. Where there is no expression of interest, GSA or the landholding agency, as appropriate, will proceed with disposal in accordance with applicable law.
(b) Upon notice from HHS that all applications have been disapproved, or if no initial applications have been received within 75 days after an expression of interest, or no final application has been received within 45 days after an approved initial application, disposal may proceed in accordance with applicable law.
