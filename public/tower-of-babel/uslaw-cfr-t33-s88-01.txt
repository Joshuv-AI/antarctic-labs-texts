33 C.F.R. § 88.01 — Purpose and applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:33:88.01
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-88.01
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 88 (Annex V: Pilot Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to all vessels operating on United States inland waters and to United States vessels operating on the Canadian waters of the Great Lakes to the extent there is no conflict with Canadian law.
