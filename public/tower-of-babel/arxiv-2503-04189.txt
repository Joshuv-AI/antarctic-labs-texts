Partial Differential Operators and Fourier Integral Operators in the Gevrey setting and applications
Benjamin Colmey 1 and Richard Lascar 2

Abstract
Résumé. Nous présentons et prouvons ici des résultats semi-classiques concernant la construction de solutions asymptotiques par la méthode WKB pour les équations pseudo-différentielles de type principal réel. Il s’agit d’une version Gevrey ; les versions régulières C ∞ C^{\infty} et analytiques peuvent être trouvées dans Hörmander [ 1 ] et Sjöstrand [ 2 ] . Nous présentons ici trois versions en fonction du degré d’entrée dans le domaine complexe.

Introduction

In what follows, we introduce definitions of Gevrey symbols and of the related P.D.O.s. Then we state our main results.

We work in the semiclassical frame involving a small parameter
h\in(0,1]
. We note for
m,k\in\mathbb{R},s>1
by
\Sigma^{m,k}_{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n}\times\mathbb{R}^{n})
the set of Gevrey symbols, i.e., we write
a\in\Sigma^{m,k}_{s}
iff for some
C>0
we have

(1.1)

|\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\partial_{\xi}^{\beta}a(x,\xi;h)|\leq C^{1+(|\alpha|+|\beta|)}\alpha!^{s}\beta!^{s}h^{-m}\langle\xi\rangle^{k-|\beta|}

for all
\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb{N}^{n}
,
(x,\xi)\in\mathbb{R}^{n}\times\mathbb{R}^{n}
,
h\in(0,1]
.

We note
\tilde{S}^{m}_{s}
for the class
\Sigma^{m,0}_{s}
and by
S^{m}_{s}=h^{-m}\mathcal{G}^{s}_{b}(\mathbb{R}^{2n})

As usual, semiclassical P.D.O.s are defined by:

(1.2)

a(x,hD,h)u=\frac{1}{(2\pi h)^{n}}\int\int e^{i(x-y)\cdot\xi/h}a(x,\xi;h)u(y)\,d\xi\,dy

for a given
a\in\Sigma^{m,k}_{s}
. We write:
a(x,hD;h)=\mathrm{Op}_{h}a\quad\text{and have}\quad\mathrm{Op}_{h}a=\mathrm{Op}_{1}a_{h},\text{ with }a_{h}(x,\xi)=a(x,h\xi)
.

Hypothesis and main results

Let
P=P(x,hD,h)
be a semiclassical P.D.O. of order 0 in
\mathbb{R}^{n}
,
n\geq 2
, i.e., with
\sigma_{p}(x,\xi)\in\mathcal{G}^{s}_{b}(\mathbb{R}^{2n})
. Let
p(x,\xi)
be the principal symbol of
P
, and
H_{p}
be the related Hamiltonian field. We introduce the following hypothesis near
(x_{0},\xi_{0})\in T^{*}\mathbb{R}^{n}
:

(H1)

p
is real,

(H2)

dp(x_{0},\xi_{0})\neq 0
,

(H3)

p(x_{0},\xi_{0})=0
.

It is customary to say that
P
is of real principal type at
(x_{0},\xi_{0})
. As a heuristic, let us say that assuming more stronger assumptions than in the smooth case, we expect to find asymptotics modulo smaller remainders, i.e.,

\mathcal{O}(1)\exp\left(-\frac{1}{C}h^{-1/s}\right)\text{instead of }\mathcal{O}(h^{\infty}).
We do obtain it, but we pay by reformulating parts of the theory.

Main results

Here we shall prove 3 main theorems:

Theorem 1.
Let

P=p(x,hD;h)

\mathcal{G}^{s}

(x_{0},\xi_{0})\in T^{*}\mathbb{R}^{n}

(1.4)

Under these hypotheses, one may solve the WKB problem near

(1.5)

\displaystyle\frac{1}{h}e^{-i\varphi/h}P(ae^{i\varphi/h})-b\in\mathcal{O}_{s}(h^{\infty})\quad\text{close to }x_{0},

\displaystyle a|_{S}=a_{0}.

The smallness condition in Theorem 1 is:

Notation 1.6.

g(\bullet,h)\in\mathcal{G}^{s}_{b}(\mathbb{R}^{n})

Stationary phase and symbol calculus in the Gevrey setting

\psi\in\mathcal{G}^{s}_{0}(\mathbb{R}^{n})
supported close to
x_{0}
. Let
V
be a conic closed subset such that:

V\cap\{\phi^{\prime}_{x}(x,\theta);\xi\in\operatorname{supp}\psi,(x,\theta)\in\operatorname{conesuppa},\phi^{\prime}_{\theta}(x,\theta)=0\}=\varnothing.

We aim at proving that for any
\xi

\in V(\xi_{0})\subset\subset V
, one has:

\widehat{\psi u}(\xi/h)=\mathcal{O}(1)e^{-\frac{1}{C}h^{-1/s}},\quad\text{for }\xi\in V(\xi_{0}).

Moreover:

\widehat{\psi u}(\xi/h)=\int e^{\frac{i}{h}(\phi(x,\theta)-x\xi)}a(x,\theta)\psi(x)\,dx\,d\theta.

We perform in the integrand (A.1.4) a
\mathcal{G}^{s}
dyadic partition of unity in
\theta\in\mathbb{R}^{N}
. It has been already used in previous sections.

We rewrite (A.1.4):

\widehat{\psi u}(\xi/h)=\sum_{\nu=0}^{\infty}\int e^{\frac{i}{h}(\phi(x,\theta)-x\xi)}a(x,\theta)\chi_{\nu}(\theta)\psi(x)\,dx\,d\theta.

We set
R_{\nu}=R={2^{\nu-1}}
and write for
\nu\geq 1
the terms in
A.1.5
as:

R^{N}\int e^{\frac{i}{h}(R\phi(x,\theta)-x\xi)}\psi(x)a(x,R\theta)\chi(\theta)dxd\theta

We evaluate each term by Gevrey non-stationary phase lemma 2.1 of these notes. Let
\Phi(x,\xi,\theta)
be the phase:

\Phi(x,\xi,\theta)=\frac{(R\phi(x,\theta)-x\xi)}{R+|\xi|}.

We have, for
\xi\in V
,
(x,\theta)\in\text{supp}\,\psi(x)a(x,R\theta)\chi(\theta)
, the bound:

|\Phi^{\prime}_{x}|+|\Phi^{\prime}_{\theta}|\geq c^{\prime}\frac{R|\theta|+|\xi|}{R+|\xi|}\geq c>0.

Deducing from (A.1.7), the bound:

CA^{k}k!^{s}h^{-m_{0}}h^{k}R^{N}(R+|\xi|)^{-k}h^{N},

for
k
large enough with respect to
N
. So, one has the bound:

CA^{k}k!^{s}R^{-1}h^{-m_{0}+k}(1+|\xi|)^{-k+1}\text{ for }k\geq k_{0},\xi\in V.

We may
sum
these bounds since:

\sum_{\nu\geq 1}^{\infty}2^{-\nu+1}=2\quad(R=2^{\nu-1}).

Hence, we deduce for
\widehat{\psi u}(\xi/h)
the bound
|\widehat{\psi u}(\xi/h)|\leq C(e^{-\frac{1}{C}h^{-1/s}})
for
\xi\in V(\xi_{0})\subset\subset{V}
, which is
(A.2.3)
.

We end with a remark:

Remark A.1.3

We may drop the homogeneity assumption of
\phi
with respect to
\theta\in\mathbb{R}^{N}
if we assume
a(x,\theta)\in S^{m_{0}}_{s}
and has its
\theta
-support in a fixed compact of
\mathbb{R}^{N}
. In this case, we drop the dyadic partition of unity in the proof.

Appendix B
Borel Lemma in Gevrey Symbol Classes

We introduced earlier classes
\tilde{S}_{s}^{m^{\prime}}
( resp
S_{s}^{m^{\prime}})_{s}
. Formal symbols are sequences
(a_{j})
of symbols in
\tilde{S}_{s}^{m^{\prime}-j}
(resp.
S_{s}^{m^{\prime}-j})
such that

\lvert\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\partial_{\xi}^{\beta}a_{j}\rvert\leq C^{1+\alpha+\beta+j}\alpha!^{s}\beta!^{s}j!^{s}h^{-m^{\prime}+j}\langle\xi\rangle^{-j-|\beta|}

\leq C^{1+\alpha+\beta+j}\alpha!^{s}\beta!^{s}j!^{s}h^{-m^{\prime}+j}.

Using the result of Boutet de Monvel-Krée
[
10
]
from the Carleson Theorem
[
9
]
, we may realize Gevrey formal symbols into symbols. It is a consequence of a Borel argument.

Proposition A.2.1

Let
(a_{j})
be a formal symbol in
S_{s}^{m^{\prime}}
(resp. in
\tilde{S}_{s}^{m^{\prime}})
. There exists
a\in S_{s}^{m^{\prime}}\text{resp.}\tilde{S}_{s}^{m^{\prime}})
such that for
N>0
,
\alpha
,
\beta
one has

\lvert\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\partial_{\xi}^{\beta}(a-\sum_{j<N}a_{j})\rvert\leq C^{1+\alpha+\beta+N}\alpha!^{s}\beta!^{s}N!^{s}h^{-m^{\prime}+N}.

resp.

\leq C^{1+\alpha+\beta+N}\alpha!^{s}\beta!^{s}N!^{s}h^{-m^{\prime}+N}\langle\xi\rangle^{-|\beta|-N}.

First we prove (A.2.2).

Proof

(a_{j}(x,\xi)){h^{-j}j!}

[

\partial_{t}^{j}g(0,x,\xi/h)=j!h^{-j}a_{j}(x,\xi,h).

Setting
a(x,\xi,h)=g(h,x,\xi,h)
for small
h\in(0,1]
, one has by Taylor’s formula:

\lvert\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\partial_{\xi}^{\beta}(a-\sum_{j<N}a_{j})\rvert\leq\sup_{\theta\in(0,1)}\lvert\partial_{t}^{N}\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\partial_{\xi}^{\beta}g(\theta h,x,\xi,h)\rvert\frac{h^{N}}{N!}.

From (A.2.5), one has

\lvert\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\partial_{\xi}^{\beta}(a-\sum_{j<N}a_{j})\rvert\leq C^{1+\alpha+\beta+N}N!^{s}\alpha!^{s}\beta!^{s}h^{-m^{\prime}+N}.

g being Gevrey
s+1
in
t,s
w.r.t.
(x,\xi)
one has by (A.2.5)(A.2.6). For obtaining a similar result for the class
\widetilde{S}_{s}^{m^{\prime}}

\xi
(see
[
1
]
). Let us write with
\chi_{0}\in\mathcal{G}_{0}^{s}(\mathbb{R}^{n})

1=\chi_{0}(\xi)+\sum_{\nu>0}\chi(2^{-\nu}\xi),

and set

a_{0}^{j}(x,\xi)=\chi_{0}(\xi)a^{j}(x,\xi),\quad a_{v}^{j}(x,\xi)=\chi(2^{-\nu}\xi)a^{j}(x,\xi).

The sequence

\tilde{a}_{\nu}^{j}(x,\xi)=a_{\nu}^{j}(x,2^{\nu}\xi),\text{ one has }

\lvert\partial_{x}^{\alpha}\partial_{\xi}^{\beta}\tilde{a}_{v}^{j}\rvert\leq C^{1+\alpha+\beta+j}\left(\frac{h}{2^{\nu}}\right)^{j}\alpha!^{s}\beta!^{s}j!^{s}h^{-m^{\prime}}

By (A.2.8), one has that the sequence
(\tilde{a}_{v}^{j})_{j\geq 0}
is a formal

(See (A.2.1)) with the small parameter

We realize the sequence
