49 C.F.R. § 374.501 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:374.501
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-374.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 374 (Passenger Carrier Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part apply to incidental charter rights authorized under 49 U.S.C. 13506. These regulations do not apply to interpreting authority contained in a certificate to transport passengers in special and/or charter operations.
