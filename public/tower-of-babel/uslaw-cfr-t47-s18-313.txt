47 C.F.R. § 18.313 — Radio frequency exposure requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:47:18.313
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-18.313
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 18 (Industrial, Scientific, And Medical Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Radio frequency devices operating under the provisions of this part are subject to the radio frequency radiation exposure requirements specified in §§ 1.1307(b), 1.1310, 2.1091, and 2.1093 of this chapter, as appropriate.
