25 C.F.R. § 1187.50 — How will OIED evaluate awardees' performance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1187.50
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1187.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1187 (Indian Business Incubators Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Not later than one year after the date on which OIED awards a grant to an eligible applicant under the IBIP, and annually thereafter for the duration of the grant, OIED will conduct an evaluation of, and prepare a report on, the awardee, which will:
(a) Describe the performance of the eligible applicant; and
(b) Be used in determining the ongoing eligibility of the eligible applicant.
