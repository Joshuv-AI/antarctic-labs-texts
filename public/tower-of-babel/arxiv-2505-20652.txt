Definition 2
.

The set-coloring Ramsey number
\mathrm{R}_{r,s}(k)
is the least
n\in\mathbb{N}
such that every coloring
\chi:E\left(K_{n}\right)\rightarrow\binom{[r]}{s}
contains a monochromatic clique of size
k
, that is, a set
S\subset V\left(K_{n}\right)
with
|S|=k
and a color
i\in[r]
such that
i\in\chi(e)
for every
e\in\binom{S}{2}
.

If
s=1
, then
\mathrm{R}_{r,s}(k)=\mathrm{R}_{r}(k)
. The exploration of set-coloring Ramsey numbers began in the 1960s through the work of Erdős, Hajnal and Rado
[
8
]
. They proposed the conjecture that
\mathrm{R}_{r,r-1}(k)\leqslant 2^{\delta(r)k}
for some function
\delta(r)\rightarrow 0
as
r\rightarrow\infty
. This conjecture was confirmed by Erdős and Szemerédi
[
10
]
in 1972, they showed that

2^{\Omega(k/r)}\leqslant\mathrm{R}_{r,r-1}(k)\leqslant r^{O(k/r)}.

The lower bound is established through a straightforward random coloring approach, while the upper bound was demonstrated by Erdős and Szemerédi. They proved that in any 2-coloring where one color has a density of at most

1/r
, there exists a monochromatic clique of size
c(r)\log n
, where
c(r)=\Omega(r/\log r)
. This finding was then utilized concerning the color assigned to the majority of edges. In the case of more general values of
s
, significant advancements were made only recently by by Conlon, Fox, He, Mubayi, Suk and Verstraëte
[
4
]
, who demonstrated the bound for
\mathrm{R}_{r,s}(k)
. For the lower bound they proved

\mathrm{R}_{r,s}(k)\leqslant\exp\left(\frac{ck(r-s)^{2}}{r}\log\frac{r}{\min\{s,r-s\}}\right)

where
c
is a positive constant.
Also by connecting the relation between error-correcting codes and set-Ramsey number, they show that

\mathrm{R}_{r,s}(k)\geqslant\exp\left(\frac{c^{\prime}k(r-s)^{3}}{r^{2}}\right)

where
c^{\prime}
is a positive constant. They also observed that a straightforward random coloring provides a lower bound of
\mathrm{R}_{r,s}(k)
which is stronger when
r-s\ll\sqrt{r}
. That is
\mathrm{R}_{r,s}(k)\geqslant 2^{\Omega(k(r-s)/r)}
. In 2022, Le
[
14
]
gave the following lower bound.

Theorem 1.1
.

[
14
]

For all positive integers

such that
r>s,k\geq 3
, we have

\mathrm{R}_{r,s}(k)>\left\lfloor\frac{k}{e}r^{-1/k}\left(\frac{r}{s}\right)^{(k-1)/2}\right\rfloor.

The set-coloring Ramsey number has important applications on error-correcting codes; see
[
4
,
5
]
. A natural extension of Ramsey numbers, initially proposed by Erdős, Hajnal and Rado in 1965

Theorem 2.1

. Let

Theorem 3.5

\begin{split}\Pr[A_{S}]&\leq\left((1-p)^{m}+(r-1)\left(\frac{s-1+p}{r-1}\right)^{m}\right){{n\choose 2}\choose m}\\[5.69046pt]
&=\left((1-p)^{m}+(s+p-1)\left(\frac{s-1+p}{r-1}\right)^{m-1}\right){{n\choose 2}\choose m}\\[5.69046pt]

&=s(1-p)^{m-1}{{n\choose 2}\choose m},\end{split}

.

\Pr[\underset{S}{\bigwedge}\overline{A_{S}}]>0

\mathrm{R}_{r,s}(G)>N

\begin{split}y\Pr[A_{S}](N_{AA}+1)&\leq yse^{-p(m-1)}\left(\frac{Ne}{n}\right)^{n}{{n\choose 2}\choose m}\\[5.69046pt]
