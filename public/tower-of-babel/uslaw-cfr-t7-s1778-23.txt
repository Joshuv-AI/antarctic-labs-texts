7 C.F.R. § 1778.23 — Grant closing and disbursement of funds.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1778.23
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1778.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1778 (Emergency And Imminent Community Water Assistance Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Grants will be closed in accordance with § 1780.45 of part 1780 of this chapter.
(b) RUS Bulletin 1780-12, “Water or Waste Grant Agreement,” will be executed by all applicants.
(c) The Agency's policy is not to disburse grant funds from the Treasury until they are actually needed by the applicant. Grant funds will be disbursed by using multiple advances.
