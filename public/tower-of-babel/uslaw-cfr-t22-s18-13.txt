22 C.F.R. § 18.13 — Representation.
Citation: us:cfr:22:18.13
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-18.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 18 (Regulations Concerning Post Employment Conflict Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A respondent or proposed respondent may appear in person or he/she may be represented by counsel or other representative. The Director General may be represented by an attorney or other employee of the Department.
