30 C.F.R. § 941.828 — Special performance standards—in situ processing.
Citation: us:cfr:30:941.828
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-941.828
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 941 (South Dakota). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 828 of this chapter, Special Permanent Program Performance Standards—In Situ Processing, shall apply to any person who conducts in situ processing activities.
