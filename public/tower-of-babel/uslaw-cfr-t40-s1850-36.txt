40 C.F.R. § 1850.36 — Special procedures: Medical records.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1850.36
Creator: Gulf Coast Ecosystem Restoration Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1850.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1850 (Availability Of Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event the Council receives a request pursuant to § 1850.33 for access to medical records (including psychological records) and the appropriate Council official determines disclosure could be harmful to the individual to whom they relate, he or she may refuse to disclose the records directly to the requester but shall transmit them to a physician designated by that individual.
