41 C.F.R. § 102-42.60 — -42.60 Gifts and decorations received by Senators and Senate employees.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-42.60
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-42.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-42 (42—Utilization, Donation, And Disposal Of Foreign Gifts And Decorations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Gifts and decorations received by Senators and Senate employees are deposited with the Secretary of the Senate for disposal by the Senate Commission on Art under 5 U.S.C. 7342(e)(2). GSA is responsible for the disposal of gifts or decorations received by Members and employees of the House of Representatives.
