41 C.F.R. § 102-117.20 — -117.20 Procurement requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-117.20
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-117.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-117 (117—Transportation Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

GSA leverages the Federal Government's collective buying power to procure transportation services at reduced rates. It uses standardized agreements to ensure consistency and protect the government's interests. Therefore, agencies subject to this FMR part must select an option provided in § 102-117.15 to procure transportation.
