28 U.S.C. § 589 — Staff and other employees
Citation: us:usc:28:589
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section589
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 39 (United States Trustees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States trustee may employ staff and other employees on approval of the Attorney General.
