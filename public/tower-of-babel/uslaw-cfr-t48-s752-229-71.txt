48 C.F.R. § 752.229-71 — 752.229-71 Reporting of Foreign Taxes

Creator: Chapter 7—Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-752.229-71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 752 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed in (48 CFR) AIDAR 729.402-70(b), insert the following clause in applicable solicitations and resulting contracts. The contracting officer must insert the address and point of contact at the Embassy, Mission, or M/CFO/CMP as appropriate under paragraph (d) of this clause.
