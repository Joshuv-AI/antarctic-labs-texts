40 C.F.R. § 98.367 — Records that must be retained.
Citation: us:cfr:40:98.367
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-98.367
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 98 (Mandatory Greenhouse Gas Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the information required by § 98.3(g), you must retain the calibration records for all monitoring equipment, including the method or manufacturer's specification used for calibration.
