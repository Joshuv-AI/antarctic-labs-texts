25 C.F.R. § 10.8 — What minimum records must be kept and reports made at each detention, community residential, or holding facility in Indian country?
Citation: us:cfr:25:10.8
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-10.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 10 (Indian Country Detention Facilities And Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, Office of Law Enforcement Services, BIA, will develop all necessary requirements for maintaining records, reporting data, and archiving information. These requirements will be published in 69 BIAM, “Indian Country Detention Facilities and Programs.”
