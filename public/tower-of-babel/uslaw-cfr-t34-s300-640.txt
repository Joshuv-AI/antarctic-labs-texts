34 C.F.R. § 300.640 — Annual report of children served—report requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:34:300.640
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-300.640
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 300 (Assistance To States For The Education Of Children With Disabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The SEA must annually report to the Secretary on the information required by section 618 of the Act at the times specified by the Secretary.
(b) The SEA must submit the report on forms provided by the Secretary.
