15 U.S.C. § 3606 — Federal Housing Administration mortgage or loan insurance; expedition of application process and decision
Citation: us:usc:15:3606
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section3606
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 62 (Condominium And Cooperative Conversion Protection And Abuse Relief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Where an application for mortgage or loan insurance in connection with a conversion or purchase of a rental housing project being undertaken by a tenants’ organization is submitted, the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development shall expedite the processing of the application in every way and shall make a final decision on such application at the earliest practicable time.
