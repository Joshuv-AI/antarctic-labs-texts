46 C.F.R. § 161.013-17 — -17 Manufacturer notification.
Citation: us:cfr:46:161.013-17
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-161.013-17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 161 (Electrical Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each manufacturer certifying lights in accordance with the specifications of this subpart must send written notice to the Commandant (CG-ENG) according to § 159.005-1 of this subchapter within 30 days after first certifying the lights and send a new notice every 5 years thereafter as long as it certifies lights.
