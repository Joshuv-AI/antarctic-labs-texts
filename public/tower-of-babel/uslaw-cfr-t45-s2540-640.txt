45 C.F.R. § 2540.640 — When will the reviewing official make a decision on the proposed action?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2540.640
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2540.640
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2540 (General Administrative Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The reviewing official will issue a decision within 45 days of receipt of your response.
