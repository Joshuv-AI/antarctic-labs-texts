2 U.S.C. § 282d — Official mail matter
Citation: us:usc:2:282d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section282d
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 9 (Office Of Legislative Counsel); Subchapter II (House Of Representatives); Part II (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Legislative Counsel may send the official mail matter of the Office as franked mail under section 3210 of title 39.
