14 C.F.R. § 437.67 — Tracking a reusable suborbital rocket.
Citation: us:cfr:14:437.67
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-437.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 437 (Experimental Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A permittee must—
(a) During permitted flight, measure in real time the position and velocity of its reusable suborbital rocket; and
(b) Provide position and velocity data to the FAA for post-flight use.
