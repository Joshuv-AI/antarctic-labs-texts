45 C.F.R. § 1703.209 — Notice of meeting in Federal Register.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1703.209
Creator: National Commission On Libraries And Information Science
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1703.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1703 (Government In The Sunshine Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Immediately following each public announcement required by this subpart, the following information, as applicable, shall be submitted for publication in the Federal Register:
(a) Notice of the time, place, and subject matter of a meeting;
(b) Whether the meeting is open or closed;
(c) Any change in one of the preceding; and
(d) The name and telephone number of an agency official who will respond to requests for information about the meeting.
