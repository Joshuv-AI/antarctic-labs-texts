16 U.S.C. § 6707 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:6707
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6707
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 86 (Southwest Forest Health And Wildfire Prevention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
There is authorized to be appropriated to carry out this chapter $15,000,000 for each fiscal year.
(b) Limitation
No funds made available under subsection (a) shall be used to pay the costs of constructing any facilities.
