12 C.F.R. § 1203.28 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1203.28
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1203.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1203 (Equal Access To Justice Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any party, other than the United States, that is dissatisfied with the final decision on an application for award of fees and expenses under this part may seek judicial review as provided in 5 U.S.C. 504(c)(2).
