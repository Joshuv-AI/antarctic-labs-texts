42 C.F.R. § 600.100 — Program description.
Citation: us:cfr:42:600.100
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-600.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 600 (Administration, Eligibility, Essential Health Benefits, Performance Standards, Service Delivery Requirements, Premium And Cost Sharing, Allotments, And Reconcilation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State Basic Health Program (BHP) is operated consistent with a BHP Blueprint that has been certified by the Secretary to meet the requirements of this part. The BHP Blueprint is developed by the State for certification by the Secretary in accordance with the processes described in this subpart.
