26 C.F.R. § 156.6091-2 — -2 Exceptional cases.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 156 (Excise Tax On Greenmail). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding the provisions of § 156.6091-1, the Commissioner may permit the filing of any tax return under chapter 54 (Greenmail) of the Code in any local Internal Revenue Service office.
