49 C.F.R. § 178.348-3 — -3 Pumps, piping, hoses and connections.
Citation: us:cfr:49:178.348-3
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-178.348-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 178 (Specifications For Packagings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each pump and all piping, hoses and connections on each cargo tank motor vehicle must conform to § 178.345-9, except that the use of nonmetallic pipes, valves, or connections are authorized on DOT 412 cargo tanks.
