10 U.S.C. § 12647 — Commissioned officers: retention in active status while assigned to Selective Service System or serving as United States property and fiscal officers
Citation: us:usc:10:12647
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12647
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1219 (Standards And Procedures For Retention And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding chapters 843, 1407, and 1409 of this title, a reserve commissioned officer, other than a commissioned warrant officer, who is assigned to the Selective Service System or who is a property and fiscal officer appointed, designated, or detailed under section 708 of title 32, may be retained in an active status in that assignment or position until he becomes 62 years of age.
