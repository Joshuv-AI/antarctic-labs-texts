42 U.S.C. § 4721 — Declaration of purpose
Citation: us:usc:42:4721
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4721
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 62 (Intergovernmental Personnel Program); Subchapter II (Strengthening State And Local Personnel Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this subchapter is to assist State and local governments to strengthen their staffs by improving their personnel administration.
