42 C.F.R. § 137.436 — Will the decisions on appeal be available for the public to review?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.436
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.436
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, all final decisions must be published for the Department under this subpart. Decisions can be found on the Department's website.
