40 C.F.R. § 63.10434 — In what form and for how long must I keep my records?
Citation: us:cfr:40:63.10434
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-63.10434
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 63 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants For Source Categories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Your records must be in a form suitable and readily available for expeditious review.
(b) You must keep each record for 5 years following the date of each record.
(c) You must keep each record onsite for at least 2 years after the date of each record. You may keep the records offsite for the remaining 3 years.
