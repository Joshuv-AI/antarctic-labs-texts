10 C.F.R. § 611.207 — Small automobile and component manufacturers.
Citation: us:cfr:10:611.207
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-611.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 611 (Advanced Technology Vehicles Manufacturer Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In this section, the term “covered firm” means a firm that—
(1) Employs less than 500 individuals; and
(2) Manufactures automobiles or components of automobiles.
(b) Set Aside—Of the amount of funds that are used to provide awards for each fiscal year under this subpart, not less than 10 percent shall be used to provide awards to covered firms or consortia led by a covered firm.
