7 C.F.R. § 1.215 — How soon before I need the official information or testimony relating to official information should I submit my demand?
Citation: us:cfr:7:1.215
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1.215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1 (Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must submit your demand, including all information identified in § 1.214, at least 14 calendar days before the date when you need the official information or testimony relating to official information.
