38 C.F.R. § 78.120 — Amount and payment of grants.
Citation: us:cfr:38:78.120
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-78.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 78 (Staff Sergeant Parker Gordon Fox Suicide Prevention Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Amount of grants. The maximum funding that a grantee may be awarded under this part is $750,000 per fiscal year.
(b) Payment of grants. Grantees are to be paid in accordance with the timeframes and manner set forth in the NOFO.
