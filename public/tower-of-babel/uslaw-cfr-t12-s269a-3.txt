12 C.F.R. § 269a.3 — a.3 Intervenor.
Citation: us:cfr:12:269a.3
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-269a.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 269a (PART 269a—DEFINITIONS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term intervenor means the party in a proceeding whose intervention has been permitted or directed by the panel or its designated officer.
