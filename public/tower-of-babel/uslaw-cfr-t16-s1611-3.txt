16 C.F.R. § 1611.3 — Flammability—general requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1611.3
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1611.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1611 (Standard For The Flammability Of Vinyl Plastic Film). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rate of burning shall not exceed 1.2 in./sec as judged by the average of five determinations lengthwise and five determinations transverse to the direction of processing, when the material is tested with the SPI flammability tester in accordance with the method described in § 1611.4.
