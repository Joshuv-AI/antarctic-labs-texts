7 U.S.C. § 8505 — Treatment of brown tree snakes as nonmailable matter
Citation: us:usc:7:8505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section8505
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 111 (Brown Tree Snake Control And Eradication). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A brown tree snake constitutes nonmailable matter under section 3015 of title 39.
