42 U.S.C. § 12809 — Rehabilitation of State and local government in rem properties
Citation: us:usc:42:12809
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12809
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 130 (National Affordable Housing); Subchapter II (Investment In Affordable Housing); Part D (Specified Model Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary shall make available a model program under which States and units of general local government may convert in rem properties to provide affordable permanent housing for the homeless by leasing such properties to nonprofit organizations and permitting such organizations to rehabilitate the properties.
(b) Target
The program shall target vacant properties for rehabilitation by nonprofit organizations.
