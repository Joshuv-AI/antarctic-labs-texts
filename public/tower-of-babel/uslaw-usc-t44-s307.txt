44 U.S.C. § 307 — Director of the Government Publishing Office: night work
Citation: us:usc:44:307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section307
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 3 (Government Publishing Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Government Publishing Office shall cause the public printing in the Government Publishing Office to be done at night as well as through the day, when the exigencies of the public service require it.
