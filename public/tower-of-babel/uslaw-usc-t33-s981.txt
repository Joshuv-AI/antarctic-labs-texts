33 U.S.C. § 981 — Creation of Corporation
Citation: us:usc:33:981
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section981
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 19 (Saint Lawrence Seaway). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby created, subject to the direction and supervision of the Secretary of Transportation, a body corporate to be known as the Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation (hereinafter referred to as the “Corporation”).
