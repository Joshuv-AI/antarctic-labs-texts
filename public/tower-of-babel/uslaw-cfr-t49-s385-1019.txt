49 C.F.R. § 385.1019 — Service and computation of time.
Citation: us:cfr:49:385.1019
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-385.1019
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 385 (Safety Fitness Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Service of documents and computations of time will be made in accordance with §§ 386.6 and 386.8 of this subchapter. All documents that are required to be served or filed must be served or filed with a certificate of service.
