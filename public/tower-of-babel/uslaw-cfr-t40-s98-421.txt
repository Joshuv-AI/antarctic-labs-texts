40 C.F.R. § 98.421 — Reporting threshold.
Citation: us:cfr:40:98.421
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-98.421
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 98 (Mandatory Greenhouse Gas Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any supplier of CO2 who meets the requirements of § 98.2(a)(4) of subpart A of this part must report the mass of CO2 captured, extracted, imported, or exported.
