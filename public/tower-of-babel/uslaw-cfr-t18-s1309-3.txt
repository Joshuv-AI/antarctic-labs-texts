18 C.F.R. § 1309.3 — What is the purpose of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:18:1309.3
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1309.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1309 (Nondiscrimination With Respect To Age). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to effectuate the Act in all programs or activities of recipients which receive financial assistance from TVA, and to inform the public and the recipients of financial assistance from TVA of the Act's requirements and how it will be enforced.
