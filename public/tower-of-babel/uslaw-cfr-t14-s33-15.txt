14 C.F.R. § 33.15 — Materials.
Citation: us:cfr:14:33.15
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-33.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 33 (Airworthiness Standards: Aircraft Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The suitability and durability of materials used in the engine must—
(a) Be established on the basis of experience or tests; and
(b) Conform to approved specifications (such as industry or military specifications) that ensure their having the strength and other properties assumed in the design data.
