50 U.S.C. § 523 — Employment of civilian personnel
Citation: us:usc:50:523
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section523
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 20 (Wind Tunnels); Subchapter II (Air Engineering Development Center). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Air Force is authorized to employ such civilian personnel as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this subchapter without regard to the limitation on maximum number of employees imposed by section 14(a) 1 of the Federal Employees Pay Act of 1946 (5 U.S.C. 947(g)).
