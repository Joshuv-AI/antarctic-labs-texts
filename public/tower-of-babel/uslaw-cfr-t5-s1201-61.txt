5 C.F.R. § 1201.61 — Exclusion of evidence and testimony.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1201.61
Creator: Merit Systems Protection Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1201.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1201 (Practices And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any evidence and testimony that is offered in the hearing and excluded by the judge will be described, and that description will be made a part of the record.
