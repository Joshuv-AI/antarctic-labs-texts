32 C.F.R. § 1642.5 — Impartiality.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1642.5
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1642.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1642 (Classification Of Registrants Deferred Because Of Hardship To Dependents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Boards shall consider all questions in a claim for classification in Class 3-A with equal consideration of race, creed, color, sex or ethnic background.
(b) Boards may not give precedence to one type of dependency hardship over another.
