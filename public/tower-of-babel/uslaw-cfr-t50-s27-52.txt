50 C.F.R. § 27.52 — Introduction of plants and animals.
Citation: us:cfr:50:27.52
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-27.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 27 (Prohibited Acts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Plants and animals or their parts taken elsewhere shall not be introduced, liberated, or placed on any national wildlife refuge except as authorized.
