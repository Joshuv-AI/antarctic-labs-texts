42 U.S.C. § 6631 — Establishment of Committee
Citation: us:usc:42:6631
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6631
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 79 (Science And Technology Policy, Organization And Priorities); Subchapter III (President’S Committee On Science And Technology). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall establish within the Executive Office of the President a President’s Committee on Science and Technology (hereinafter referred to as the “Committee”).
