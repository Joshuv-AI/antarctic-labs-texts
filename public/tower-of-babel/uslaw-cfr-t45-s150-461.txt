45 C.F.R. § 150.461 — Failure to pay assessment.
Citation: us:cfr:45:150.461
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-150.461
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 150 (Cms Enforcement In Group And Individual Insurance Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any entity fails to pay an assessment after it becomes a final order, or after the court has entered final judgment in favor of CMS, CMS refers the matter to the Attorney General, who brings an action against the entity in the appropriate United States district court to recover the amount assessed.
