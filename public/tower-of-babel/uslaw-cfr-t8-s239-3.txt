8 C.F.R. § 239.3 — Effect of filing notice to appear.
Citation: us:cfr:8:239.3
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-239.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 239 (Initiation Of Removal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The filing of a notice to appear shall have no effect in determining periods of unlawful presence as defined in section 212(a)(9)(B) of the Act.
