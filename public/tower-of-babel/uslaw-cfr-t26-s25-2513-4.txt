26 C.F.R. § 25.2513-4 — -4 Joint and several liability for tax.
Citation: us:cfr:26:25.2513-4
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-25.2513-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 25 (Gift Tax; Gifts Made After December 31, 1954). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If consent to the application of the provisions of section 2513 is signified as provided in § 25.2513-2, and not revoked as provided in § 25.2513-3, the liability with respect to the entire gift tax of each spouse for such “calendar period” (as defined in § 25.2502-1(c)(1)) is joint and several. See paragraph (d) of § 25.2511-1.
