44 C.F.R. § 9.15 — Planning programs affecting land use.
Citation: us:cfr:44:9.15
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-9.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 9 (Floodplain Management And Protection Of Wetlands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency shall take floodplain management into account when formulating or evaluating any water and land use plans. No plan may be approved unless it:
(a) Reflects consideration of flood hazards and floodplain management and wetlands protection; and
(b) Prescribes planning procedures to implement the policies and requirements of the Orders and this regulation.
