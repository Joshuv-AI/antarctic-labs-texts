28 C.F.R. § 49.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:28:49.1
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-49.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 49 (Antitrust Civil Process Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part are issued in compliance with the requirements imposed by the provisions of section 4(c) of the Antitrust Civil Process Act, as amended (15 U.S.C. 1313(c)). The terms used in this part shall be deemed to have the same meaning as similar terms used in that Act.
