22 C.F.R. § 53.3 — Attempt of a citizen to enter without a valid passport.
Citation: us:cfr:22:53.3
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-53.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 53 (Passport Requirement And Exceptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The appropriate officer at the port of entry shall report to the Department of State any citizen of the United States who attempts to enter the United States contrary to the provisions of this part, so that the Department of State may apply the waiver provisions of § 53.2(h) and § 53.2(i) to such citizen, if appropriate.
