11 U.S.C. § 323 — Role and capacity of trustee
Citation: us:usc:11:323
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section323
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 3 (Case Administration); Subchapter II (Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The trustee in a case under this title is the representative of the estate.
(b)
The trustee in a case under this title has capacity to sue and be sued.
