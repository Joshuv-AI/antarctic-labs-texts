46 C.F.R. § 154.906 — Inert gas generators.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.906
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.906
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The inert gas generator must:
(a) Produce an inert gas containing less than 5% oxygen by volume;
(b) Have a device to continuously sample the discharge of the generator for oxygen content; and
(c) Have an audible and visual alarm in the cargo control station that alarms when the inert gas contains 5% or more oxygen by volume.
