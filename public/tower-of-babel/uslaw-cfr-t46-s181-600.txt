46 C.F.R. § 181.600 — Fire axe.
Citation: us:cfr:46:181.600
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-181.600
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 181 (Fire Protection Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A vessel of more than 19.8 meters (65 feet) in length must have at least one fire axe located in or adjacent to the primary operating station.
