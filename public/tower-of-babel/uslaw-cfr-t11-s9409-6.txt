11 C.F.R. § 9409.6 — Service of subpoenas or requests.
Citation: us:cfr:11:9409.6
Creator: Election Assistance Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-9409.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 9409 (Testimony By Commission Employees Relating To Official Information And Production Of Official Records In Legal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subpoenas or requests for official records or information or testimony must be served on the General Counsel, U.S. Election Assistance Commission, 1335 East-West Highway, Suite 4300, Silver Spring, MD 20910.
