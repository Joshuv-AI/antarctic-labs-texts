42 U.S.C. § 2000b–3 — “Complaint” defined
Citation: us:usc:42:2000b–3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2000b–3
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 21 (Civil Rights); Subchapter III (Public Facilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A complaint as used in this subchapter is a writing or document within the meaning of section 1001, title 18.
