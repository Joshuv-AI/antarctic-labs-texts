49 C.F.R. § 392.15 — Prohibited driving status.
Citation: us:cfr:49:392.15
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-392.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 392 (Driving Of Commercial Motor Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No driver, who holds a commercial learner's permit or a commercial driver's license, shall operate a commercial motor vehicle if prohibited by § 382.501(a) of this subchapter.
