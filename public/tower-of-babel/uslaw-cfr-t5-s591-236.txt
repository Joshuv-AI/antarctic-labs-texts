5 C.F.R. § 591.236 — When do COLA and post differential payments end?
Citation: us:cfr:5:591.236
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-591.236
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 591 (Allowances And Differentials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to § 591.237(a), agencies stop paying an employee a COLA or post differential on—
(a) Separation,
(b) The effective date of assignment or transfer to a new official worksite outside the COLA or post differential area, or
(c) In the case of an employee on detail to temporary duty in a post differential area, the ending date of the detail.
