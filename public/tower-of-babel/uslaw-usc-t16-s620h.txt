16 U.S.C. § 620h — Savings provision
Citation: us:usc:16:620h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section620h
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 4 (Protection Of Timber, And Depredations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in sections 620 to 620j of this title, or regulations issued under sections 620 to 620j of this title, shall be construed to abrogate or affect any timber sale contract entered into before August 20, 1990.
