30 C.F.R. § 586.211 — What payments are required for an Alternate Use RUE?
Citation: us:cfr:30:586.211
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-586.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 586 (Alternate Uses Of Existing Facilities On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will establish rental or other payments for an Alternate Use RUE on a case-by-case basis, as set forth in the Alternate Use RUE grant, depending on our assessment of the following factors:
(a) The effect on the original OCS Lands Act approved activity;
(b) The size and scale of the proposed alternate use activities;
(c) The income, if any, expected to be generated from the proposed alternate use activities; and
(d) The type of alternate use activities.
