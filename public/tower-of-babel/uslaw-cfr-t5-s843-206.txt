5 C.F.R. § 843.206 — Designation of beneficiary—proof of receipt.
Citation: us:cfr:5:843.206
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-843.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 843 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Death Benefits And Employee Refunds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Upon receipt of a designation of beneficiary, the agency (or OPM) will mark the designation to show the date of receipt.
(b) The date of receipt of designation of beneficiary is presumed to be the date marked by the agency (or OPM).
