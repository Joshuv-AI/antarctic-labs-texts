20 U.S.C. § 106 — Purchases through the General Services Administration
Citation: us:usc:20:106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section106
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 6 (American Printing House For The Blind). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after September 8, 1978, the American Printing House for the Blind is authorized to make purchases through the General Services Administration.
