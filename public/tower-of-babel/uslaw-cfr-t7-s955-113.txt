7 C.F.R. § 955.113 — Fiscal period.
Citation: us:cfr:7:955.113
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-955.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 955 (Vidalia Onions Grown In Georgia). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to § 955.13, fiscal period shall mean the period beginning January 1 and ending December 31 of each year, except that the fiscal period that began on September 16, 1998, shall end on December 31, 1999.
