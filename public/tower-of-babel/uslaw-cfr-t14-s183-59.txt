14 C.F.R. § 183.59 — Inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:14:183.59
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-183.59
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 183 (Representatives Of The Administrator). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator, at any time and for any reason, may inspect an ODA Holder's or applicant's facilities, products, components, parts, appliances, procedures, operations, and records associated with the authorized or requested functions.
