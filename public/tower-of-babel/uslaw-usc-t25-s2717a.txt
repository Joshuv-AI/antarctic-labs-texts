25 U.S.C. § 2717a — Availability of class II gaming activity fees to carry out duties of Commission
Citation: us:usc:25:2717a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section2717a
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 29 (Indian Gaming Regulation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In fiscal year 1990 and thereafter, fees collected pursuant to and as limited by section 2717 of this title shall be available to carry out the duties of the Commission, to remain available until expended.
