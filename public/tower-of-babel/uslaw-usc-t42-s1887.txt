42 U.S.C. § 1887 — Indemnification of grantees, contractors, and subcontractors under ocean drilling program; approvals and certifications by Director
Citation: us:usc:42:1887
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1887
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 16 (National Science Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Foundation is on and after November 25, 1985, authorized to indemnify grantees, contractors, and subcontractors associated with the ocean drilling program under the provisions of section 3861 of title 10, with all approvals and certifications required thereby made by the Director of the National Science Foundation.
