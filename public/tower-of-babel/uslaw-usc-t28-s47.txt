28 U.S.C. § 47 — Disqualification of trial judge to hear appeal
Citation: us:usc:28:47
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section47
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part I (Organization Of Courts); Chapter 3 (Courts Of Appeals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No judge shall hear or determine an appeal from the decision of a case or issue tried by him.
