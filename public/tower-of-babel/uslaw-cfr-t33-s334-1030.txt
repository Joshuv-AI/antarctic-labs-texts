33 C.F.R. § 334.1030 — Oakland Inner Harbor adjacent to Alameda Facility, Naval Supply Center, Oakland; restricted area.
Citation: us:cfr:33:334.1030
Creator: Corps Of Engineers, Department Of The Army, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-334.1030
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 334 (Danger Zone And Restricted Area Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The area. Within 100 feet of the Alameda Facility wharf.
(b) The regulations. No persons and no vessels or other craft, except vessels of the United States Government or vessels duly authorized by the Commanding Officer, Naval Supply Center, Oakland, shall enter this area.
