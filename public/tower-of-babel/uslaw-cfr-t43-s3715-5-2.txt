43 C.F.R. § 3715.5-2 — -2 What happens to property I leave behind?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3715.5-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3715.5-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3710 (Public Law 167; Act Of July 23, 1955). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any property you leave on the public lands beyond the 90-day period described in § 3715.5-1 becomes property of the United States and is subject to removal and disposition at BLM's discretion consistent with applicable laws and regulations. You are liable for the costs BLM incurs in removing and disposing of the property.
