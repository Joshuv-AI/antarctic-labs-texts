50 C.F.R. § 452.09 — Consolidated and joint proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:50:452.09
Creator: Joint Regulations (United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior And National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce); Endangered Species Committee Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-452.09
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 452 (Consideration Of Application By The Secretary). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When the Secretary is considering two or more related exemption applications, the Secretary may consider them jointly and prepare a joint report if doing so would expedite or simplify consideration of the issues.
(b) When the Secretaries of the Interior and Commerce are considering two or more related exemption applications, they may consider them jointly and prepare a joint report if doing so would expedite or simplify consideration of the issues.
