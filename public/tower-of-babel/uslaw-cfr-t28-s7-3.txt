28 C.F.R. § 7.3 — Eligibility for reward.
Citation: us:cfr:28:7.3
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-7.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 7 (Rewards For Capture Of Escaped Federal Prisoners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A reward may be paid to any person, except an official or employee of the Department of Justice or a law-enforcement officer of the U.S. Government, who personally captures and surrenders an escaped Federal prisoner to proper officials, or who assists in the capture, of an escaped Federal prisoner.
