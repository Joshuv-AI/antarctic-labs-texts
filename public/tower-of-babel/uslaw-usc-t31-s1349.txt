31 U.S.C. § 1349 — Adverse personnel actions
Citation: us:usc:31:1349
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section1349
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 13 (Appropriations); Subchapter III (Limitations, Exceptions, And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
An officer or employee of the United States Government or of the District of Columbia government violating section 1341(a) or 1342 of this title shall be subject to appropriate administrative discipline including, when circumstances warrant, suspension from duty without pay or removal from office.
(b)
An officer or employee who willfully uses or authorizes the use of a passenger motor vehicle or aircraft owned or leased by the United States Government (except for an official purpose authorized by section 1344 of this title) or otherwise violates section 1344 shall be suspended without pay by the head of the agency. The officer or employee shall be suspended for at least one month, and when circumstances warrant, for a longer period or summarily removed from office.
