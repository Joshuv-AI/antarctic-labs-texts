16 C.F.R. § 1750.2 — Transfer of functions.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1750.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1750.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1750 (Standard For Devices To Permit The Opening Of Household Refrigerator Doors From The Inside). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Effective May 14, 1973, section 30(c) of the Consumer Product Safety Act (86 Stat. 1231; 15 U.S.C. 2079(c)) transferred functions under the Refrigerator Safety Act from the Secretary of Commerce and the Federal Trade Commission to the Consumer Product Safety Commission.
