12 C.F.R. § 340.3 — What are the restrictions on the sale of assets by the FDIC if the buyer wants to finance the purchase with a loan from the FDIC?
Citation: us:cfr:12:340.3
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-340.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 340 (Restrictions On Sale Of Assets Of A Failed Institution By The Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person may not borrow money or accept credit from the FDIC in connection with the purchase of any assets of a failed institution from the FDIC if:
(a) There has been a default with respect to one or more obligations totaling in excess of $1,000,000 owed by that person or its associated person; and
(b) The person or its associated person made any fraudulent misrepresentations in connection with any such obligation(s).
