35 U.S.C. § 363 — International application designating the United States: Effect
Citation: us:usc:35:363
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section363
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part IV (Patent Cooperation Treaty); Chapter 36 (International Stage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An international application designating the United States shall have the effect, from its international filing date under article 11 of the treaty, of a national application for patent regularly filed in the Patent and Trademark Office.
