45 C.F.R. § 2544.145 — What will be done with property that is not accepted?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2544.145
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2544.145
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2544 (Solicitation And Acceptance Of Donations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In general, property offered to the Corporation but not accepted will be returned to the offeror. If the offeror is unknown or the donation would spoil if returned, the property will either be disposed of in accordance with Federal Property Management regulations (41 CFR chapter 101) or given to local charities determined by the Corporation.
