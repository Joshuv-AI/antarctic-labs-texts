21 C.F.R. § 882.4370 — Pneumatic cranial drill motor.
Citation: us:cfr:21:882.4370
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-882.4370
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 882 (Neurological Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A pneumatic cranial drill motor is a pneumatically operated power source used with removable rotating surgical cutting tools or drill bits on a patient's skull.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
