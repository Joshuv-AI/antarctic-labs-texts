42 C.F.R. § 62.29 — Under what circumstances can the Loan Repayment Program obligation be discharged in bankruptcy?
Citation: us:cfr:42:62.29
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-62.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 62 (National Health Service Corps Scholarship And Loan Repayment Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any payment obligation incurred under § 62.27 of this subpart may be released by a discharge in bankruptcy under title 11 of the United States Code only if such discharge is granted after the expiration of the five-year period beginning on the first date that payment was required, and only if the bankruptcy court finds that nondischarge of the obligation would be unconscionable.
