28 C.F.R. § 2.9 — Study prior to sentencing.
Citation: us:cfr:28:2.9
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-2.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 2 (Parole, Release, Supervision And Recommitment Of Prisoners, Youth Offenders, And Juvenile Delinquents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an adult Federal offender has been committed to an institution by the sentencing court for observation and study prior to sentencing, under the provisions of 18 U.S.C. 4205(c), the report to the sentencing court is prepared and submitted directly by the Bureau of Prisons.
