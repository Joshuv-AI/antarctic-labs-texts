49 C.F.R. § 195.252 — Backfilling.
Citation: us:cfr:49:195.252
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-195.252
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 195 (Transportation Of Hazardous Liquids By Pipeline). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a ditch for a pipeline is backfilled, it must be backfilled in a manner that:
(a) Provides firm support under the pipe; and
(b) Prevents damage to the pipe and pipe coating from equipment or from the backfill material.
