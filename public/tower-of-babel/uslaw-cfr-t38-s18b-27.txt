38 C.F.R. § 18b.27 — b.27 Extension of time or postponement.
Citation: us:cfr:38:18b.27
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 18b (PART 18b—PRACTICE AND PROCEDURE UNDER TITLE VI OF THE CIVIL RIGHTS ACT OF 1964 AND PART 18 OF THIS CHAPTER). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Requests for extension of time should be served on all parties and should set forth the reasons for the application. Applications may be granted upon a showing of good cause by the applicant. From the designation of a presiding officer until the issuance of a decision such requests should be addressed to the presiding officer. Answers to such requests are permitted, if made promptly.
