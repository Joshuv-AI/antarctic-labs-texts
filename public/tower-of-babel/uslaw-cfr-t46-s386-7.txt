46 C.F.R. § 386.7 — Disturbances.
Citation: us:cfr:46:386.7
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-386.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 386 (Regulations Governing Public Buildings And Grounds At The United States Merchant Marine Academy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any loitering, disorderly conduct or other conduct on Academy property which creates loud or unusual noise or a nuisance which unreasonably obstructs the use of any area, including entrances, foyers, lobbies, corridors, offices, elevators, stairways, or parking lots; or impedes or disrupts the performance of official duties by Government employees or Midshipmen activities is prohibited.
