45 C.F.R. § 506.5 — Persons not eligible to award of civilian detention benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:45:506.5
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-506.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 506 (Eligibility Requirements For Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual is disqualified as a “civilian American citizen” under the Act, and thus is precluded from receiving an award of detention benefits, if that person:
(a) Voluntarily, knowingly, and without duress, gave aid to or collaborated with or in any manner served the detaining hostile force; or
(b) While detained, was a regularly appointed, enrolled, enlisted, or inducted member of the Armed Forces of the United States.
