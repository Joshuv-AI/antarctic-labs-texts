34 C.F.R. § 76.659 — Use of public school personnel.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.659
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.659
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A subgrantee may use program funds to make public personnel available in other than public facilities:
(a) To the extent necessary to provide equitable program benefits designed for students enrolled in a private school; and
(b) If those benefits are not normally provided by the private school.
