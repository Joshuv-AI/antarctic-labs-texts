14 C.F.R. § 3.301 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:3.301
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-3.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 3 (General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to individuals who:
(a) Do not have a U.S. physical address of record on file with the FAA;
(b) Have a foreign address of record on file with the FAA; and
(c) Hold or apply for certificates, ratings, or authorizations under part 47, 61, 63, 65, 67, or 107 of this chapter.
