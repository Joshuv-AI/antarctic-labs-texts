48 C.F.R. § 1349.402-3 — 1349.402-3 Procedure for default
Citation: us:cfr:48:1349.402-3
Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1349.402-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1349 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No action relating to a default termination, including issuance of a show cause letter, cure notice, or notice of default, shall be taken unless notice has been provided to Procurement Counsel and the Procurement Executive, and the action has been reviewed for legal sufficiency.
