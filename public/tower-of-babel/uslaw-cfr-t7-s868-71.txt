7 C.F.R. § 868.71 — Official certificate requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:868.71
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Federal Grain Inspection Service, Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-868.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 868 (General Regulations And Standards For Certain Agricultural Commodities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Official certificates shall—
(a) Be on standard printed forms prescribed in the instructions;
(b) Be in English;
(c) Be typewritten or handwritten in ink and be clearly legible;
(d) Show the results of inspection services in a uniform, accurate, and concise manner;
(e) Show the information required by §§ 868.70-868.75; and
(f) Show only such other information and statements of fact as are provided in the instructions authorized by the Administrator.
