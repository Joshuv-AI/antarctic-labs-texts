33 C.F.R. § 66.01-13 — -13 When must my newly manufactured equipment comply with these rules?
Citation: us:cfr:33:66.01-13
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-66.01-13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 66 (Private Aids To Navigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After March 8, 2004, equipment manufactured for use as a private aid to navigation must comply with the rules in this part.
