42 U.S.C. § 1856a–1 — Authority to enter into contracts with State and local governmental entities
Citation: us:usc:42:1856a–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1856a–1
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 15A (Reciprocal Fire Protection Agreements); Subchapter I (Protection Of United States Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, in fiscal year 1992 and thereafter, the Secretary of the Interior, the Secretary of Agriculture, the Secretary of Energy, the Secretary of the Army, and the Secretary of the Smithsonian Institution are authorized to enter into contracts with State and local governmental entities, including local fire districts, for procurement of services in the presuppression, detection, and suppression of fires on any units within their jurisdiction.
