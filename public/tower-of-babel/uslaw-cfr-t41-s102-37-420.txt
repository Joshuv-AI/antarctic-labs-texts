41 C.F.R. § 102-37.420 — -37.420 FAA's responsibilities.

Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Determine the property requirements of any State, political subdivision of a State, or tax-supported organization for public airport use;
(b) Set eligibility requirements for public airports and make eligibility determinations;
(c) Certify that property listed on a transfer request is desirable or necessary for public airport use;
(d) Advise GSA of FAA officials authorized to certify transfer requests and notify GSA of any changes in signatory authority;
(e) Determine and enforce compliance with the terms and conditions under which surplus personal property is transferred for public airport use; and
(f) Authorize public airports to visit holding agencies for the purpose of screening and selecting property for transfer, including:
(1) Issuing a screening pass or letter of authorization to only those persons who are qualified to screen.
(2) Maintaining a current record of screeners operating under FAA authority and making such records available to GSA upon request.
(3) Recovering any expired or invalid screener authorizations.
