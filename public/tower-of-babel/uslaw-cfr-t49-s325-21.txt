49 C.F.R. § 325.21 — Scope of the rules in this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:49:325.21
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-325.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 325 (Compliance With Interstate Motor Carrier Noise Emission Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this subpart specify criteria for sound level measurement systems which are used to make the sound level measurements specified in subpart D and subpart E of this part.
