39 C.F.R. § 965.7 — Default.
Citation: us:cfr:39:965.7
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-965.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 965 (Mail Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A party who fails to file the submittal required by § 965.5 may be held in default and the presiding officer may issue an initial decision that mail be delivered to the other party.
