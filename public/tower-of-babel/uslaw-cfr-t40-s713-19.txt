40 C.F.R. § 713.19 — Recordkeeping requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:40:713.19
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-713.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 713 (Reporting Requirements For The Tsca Inventory Of Mercury Supply, Use, And Trade). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each person who is subject to the reporting requirements of this part must retain records that document any information reported to EPA. Records relevant to a reporting year must be retained for a period of 3 years beginning on the last day of the reporting year. Submitters are encouraged to retain their records longer than 3 years to ensure that past records are available as a reference when new submissions are being generated.
