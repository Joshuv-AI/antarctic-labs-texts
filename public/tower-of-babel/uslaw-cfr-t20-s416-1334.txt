20 C.F.R. § 416.1334 — Termination due to death of recipient.
Citation: us:cfr:20:416.1334
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-416.1334
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 416 (Supplemental Security Income For The Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Eligibility for benefits ends with the month in which the recipient dies. Payments are terminated effective with the month after the month of death.
