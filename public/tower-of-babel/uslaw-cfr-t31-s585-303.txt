31 C.F.R. § 585.303 — Entity.
Citation: us:cfr:31:585.303
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-585.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 585 (Hong Kong-Related Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term entity means a government or instrumentality of such government, partnership, association, trust, joint venture, corporation, group, subgroup, or other organization, including an international organization.
