22 U.S.C. § 7552 — Sense of Congress regarding protecting Afghanistan’s President
Citation: us:usc:22:7552
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section7552
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 82 (Afghanistan Freedom Support); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the sense of Congress that—
(1)
any United States physical protection force provided for the personal security of the President of Afghanistan should be composed of United States diplomatic security, law-enforcement, or military personnel, and should not utilize private contracted personnel to provide actual physical protection services;
(2)
United States allies should be invited to volunteer active-duty military or law enforcement personnel to participate in such a protection force; and
(3)
such a protection force should be limited in duration and should be succeeded by qualified Afghan security forces as soon as practicable.
