34 U.S.C. § 60902 — Application
Citation: us:usc:34:60902
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section60902
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 609 (Homicide Victims’ Families’ Rights). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each agency shall develop a written application to be used for designated persons to request a case file review under section 60901 of this title.
