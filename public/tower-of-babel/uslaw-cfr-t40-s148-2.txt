40 C.F.R. § 148.2 — Definitions.

Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-148.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 148 (Hazardous Waste Injection Restrictions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Injection interval means that part of the injection zone in which the well is screened, or in which the waste is otherwise directly emplaced.
Transmissive fault or fracture is a fault or fracture that has sufficient permeability and vertical extent to allow fluids to move between formations.
