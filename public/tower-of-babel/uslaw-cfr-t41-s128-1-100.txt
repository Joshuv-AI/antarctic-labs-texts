41 C.F.R. § 128-1.100 — -1.100 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:41:128-1.100
Creator: Chapter 128—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-128-1.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 128-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart introduces the Department of Justice Property Management Regulations (JPMR) as part of the Federal Property Management Regulations System (FPMR) (41 CFR part 101); states its relationship to the FPMR; and provides instructions for the issuance and use of these property management policies and procedures of the Department of Justice.
