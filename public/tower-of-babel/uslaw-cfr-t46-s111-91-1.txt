46 C.F.R. § 111.91-1 — -1 Power, control, and interlock circuits.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.91-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.91-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each electric power, control, and interlock circuit of an elevator or dumbwaiter must meet ASME A17.1 (incorporated by reference; see 46 CFR 110.10-1).
