14 C.F.R. § 262.9 — Contract of carriage.
Citation: us:cfr:14:262.9
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-262.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 262 (Travel Credits Or Vouchers Due To A Serious Communicable Disease). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A carrier shall not include terms or conditions in its contract of carriage inconsistent with the carriers' obligations as specified by this part. Any such action will be considered an unfair and deceptive practice within the meaning of 49 U.S.C. 41712.
