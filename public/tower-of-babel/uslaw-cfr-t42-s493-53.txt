42 C.F.R. § 493.53 — Notification requirements for laboratories issued a certificate for provider-performed microscopy (PPM) procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:42:493.53
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-493.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 493 (Laboratory Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Laboratories issued a certificate for PPM procedures must notify HHS or its designee—
(a) Before performing and reporting results for any test of moderate or high complexity, or both, in addition to tests specified as PPM procedures or any test or examination that is not specified under § 493.15(c), for which it does not have a registration certificate as required in subpart C or subpart D, as applicable, of this part; and
(b) Within 30 days of any change in—
(1) Ownership;
(2) Name;
(3) Location; or
(4) Director.
