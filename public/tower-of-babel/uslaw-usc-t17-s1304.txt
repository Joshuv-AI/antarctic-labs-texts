17 U.S.C. § 1304 — Commencement of protection
Citation: us:usc:17:1304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title17-section1304
Context: United States Code, Title 17 (Copyrights); Chapter 13 (Protection Of Original Designs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The protection provided for a design under this chapter shall commence upon the earlier of the date of publication of the registration under section 1313(a) or the date the design is first made public as defined by section 1310(b).
