5 C.F.R. § 610.402 — Coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:5:610.402
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-610.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 610 (Hours Of Duty). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations contained in this subpart apply only to flexible work schedules and compressed work schedules established under subchapter 11 of chapter 61 of title 5, United States Code.
