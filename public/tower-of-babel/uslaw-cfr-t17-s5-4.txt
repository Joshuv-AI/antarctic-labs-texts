17 C.F.R. § 5.4 — Applicability of part 4 of this chapter to commodity pool operators and commodity trading advisors.
Citation: us:cfr:17:5.4
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-5.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 5 (Off-Exchange Foreign Currency Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 4 of this chapter applies to any person required pursuant to the provisions of this part 5 to register as a commodity pool operator or as a commodity trading advisor. Failure by any such person to comply with the requirements of part 4 will constitute a violation of this section and the relevant section of part 4.
