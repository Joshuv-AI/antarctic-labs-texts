40 C.F.R. § 63.6007 — How do I monitor and collect data to demonstrate continuous compliance with the emission limitations for puncture sealant application affected sources?
Citation: us:cfr:40:63.6007
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-63.6007
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 63 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants For Source Categories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must monitor and collect data to demonstrate continuous compliance with the emission limitations for puncture sealant application affected sources as specified in table 13 to this subpart.
(b) You must monitor and collect data according to the requirements in § 63.6003(b) and (c).
