2 C.F.R. § 700.7 — Advance payment.
Citation: us:cfr:2:700.7
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-700.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 700 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Advance payment mechanisms include, but are not limited to, Letter of Credit, Treasury check and electronic funds transfer and must comply with applicable guidance in 31 CFR part 205.
