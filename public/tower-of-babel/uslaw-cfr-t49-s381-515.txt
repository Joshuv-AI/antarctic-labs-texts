49 C.F.R. § 381.515 — May the FMCSA remove approved participants from a pilot program?
Citation: us:cfr:49:381.515
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-381.515
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 381 (Waivers, Exemptions, And Pilot Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator will immediately revoke participation in a pilot program of a motor carrier, CMV, or driver for failure to comply with the terms and conditions of the pilot program, or if continued participation is inconsistent with the goals and objectives of the safety regulations.
