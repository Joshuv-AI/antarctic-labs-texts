20 U.S.C. § 1685 — Authority under other laws unaffected
Citation: us:usc:20:1685
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1685
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 38 (Discrimination Based On Sex Or Blindness). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall add to or detract from any existing authority with respect to any program or activity under which Federal financial assistance is extended by way of a contract of insurance or guaranty.
