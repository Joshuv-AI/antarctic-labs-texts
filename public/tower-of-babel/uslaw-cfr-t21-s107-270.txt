21 C.F.R. § 107.270 — Compliance with this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:21:107.270
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-107.270
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 107 (Infant Formula). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recalling firm may satisfy the requirements of this subpart by any means reasonable calculated to meet the obligations set forth in this Subpart E. The recall guidance in subpart C of part 7 of this chapter specify procedures that may be useful to a recalling firm in determining how to comply with these regulations.
