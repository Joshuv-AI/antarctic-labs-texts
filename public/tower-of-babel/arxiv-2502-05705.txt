Rank growth of elliptic curves over S 3 S_{3} extensions with fixed quadratic resolvents
Daniel Keliher, Sun Woo Park

Abstract
. We study the probability with which an elliptic curve E / k E/k , subject to some technical conditions, gains rank upon base extension to an S 3 S_{3} -cubic extension K / k K/k with quadratic resolvent field F / k F/k , all three fields of which are subject to some mild technical conditions. To do so, we determine the distribution (under a non-standard ordering) of Selmer ranks of an auxiliary abelian variety associated to E E and S 3 S_{3} -cubic extensions K / k K/k following ideas of Klagsbrun, Mazur, and Rubin. One corollary of this distribution is that E E gains rank by at most one upon base extension to K K with probability at least 31.95 % 31.95\% .



1.
Introduction

Questions related to expected ranks (or ranks of Selmer groups) of elliptic curves have a rich history. In
[
SD08
]
, Swinnerton-Dyer determined the probability distribution of the rank of the 2-Selmer group of quadratics twists by
d
of elliptic curve with full rational two torsion as the number of prime factors of
d
grows arbitrarily large. This was the first work to view the successive manipulation of local conditions of the Selmer group as a Markov process. In
[
Kan13
]
, Kane obtained the same distribution in the same setting, but without the “non-standard” ordering on twists adopted by Swinnerton-Dyer.

In
[
KMR14
]
, Klagsbrun, Mazur, and Rubin determined the average size of the
p
-Selmer ranks of an elliptic curve meeting some mild conditions. Their work similarly adopts this Markov chain perspective and relies on a different non-standard ordering, which we likewise adopt in this paper (see Definition
7.2
). Explicit versions of the results of
[
KMR14
]
were proved in the function field setting by the second author
[
Par22
]
.

Finally, a series of papers by Smith
[
Smi23a
]
,
[
Smi23b
]
, determined the distribution of
2^{\infty}
-Selmer coranks, essentially resolving (assuming the finiteness of the Tate-Shafarevich group) a conjecture of Goldfeld
[
Gol79
]
, under the usual ordering of quadratic twists.

Results like these are also connected to enumerating field extensions (say, with prescribed degree and Galois group) for which a fixed elliptic curve either does or does not gain rank upon base extension. For quadratic extensions, this is Goldfeld’s conjecture, but a bit more is known. Lemke Oliver and Thorne
[
LOT19
]
gave lower bounds for the number of degree
n\geq 2
,
S_{n}
-extensions
K/\mathbb{Q}
for which a fixed
E/\mathbb{Q}

does
gain rank upon base extension to
K
. The first author
[
Kel22
]
gave lower bounds for the number of quartic
A_{4}
and
S_{4}
-extensions for which a fixed
E/\mathbb{Q}
of rank zero
does not
gain rank upon base change. Pathak and Ray
[
PR24
]
likewise gave lower bounds on the number of meta-abelian extensions for which a fixed curve of rank zero does not gain rank. For all three results, the lower bounds on the number of extensions meeting the prescribed rank growth behavior are a density zero (but still an infinitely large) set among the set of degree
n
number fields with bounded discriminant and specified Galois group when compared to known asymptotics, or to Malle’s predictions
[
Mal02
]
,
[
Mal04
]
, for the number of such extensions.

In this present work, our motivating question is the following. For a fixed elliptic curve
E
defined over a number field
k
, how often does the rank of
E
change upon base extension to an
S_{3}
-cubic extension of
k
? Here we deal with the generic case in which the Galois group of the 3-torsion field of
E/k
contains
\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{F}_{3})
.

A different manifestation of this problem comes via studying Selmer groups of cubic twists family of an elliptic curve with
j
-invariant zero. Producing a trivial Selmer group for such a twist of a curve by
d
essentially produces a cubic field obtained by adjoining
\sqrt[3]{d}
for which the original curve does not gain rank upon base change. In
[
ABS24
]
, Alpöge, Bhargava and Shnidman determined the average size of 2-Selmer groups in such a family. The average size of the 3-Selmer groups of the same family was determined by Koymans and Smith in
[
KS24
]
.

2.
Selmer Groups

Here, we briefly introduce Selmer groups of abelian varieties. For a more thorough treatment, see e.g.
[
Sil86
]
or
[
Cas91
]
.

Throughout this section, let
A
and
A^{\prime}
be abelian varieties over a number field,
k
, together with an isogeny
\phi:A\to A^{\prime}
. From these, one has the short exact sequence

0\to\ker\phi\to A\overset{\phi}{\to}A^{\prime}\to 0.

After taking the long exact sequence on Galois cohomology groups and quotienting it appropriately, one arrives at the short exact sequence

0\to A^{\prime}(k)/\phi(A(k))\to H^{1}(k,\ker\phi)\to H^{1}(k,A)[\phi]\to 0.

Above, the map
A^{\prime}(k)/\phi(A(k))\to H^{1}(k,\ker\phi)
, the global Kummer map, is given by sending
\bar{P}\in A^{\prime}(k)/\phi(A(k))
to the class in
H^{1}(k,\ker\phi)
of maps all cohomologous to the map
G_{k}\to\ker\phi
given by
\sigma\mapsto\bar{P}^{\sigma}-\bar{P}
. One likewise defines the local Kummer maps with
k_{v}
in place of
k
for every place
v
of
k
.

Further, for each place
v
of
k
, there are restrictions maps:

A^{\prime}(k)/\phi(A(k))\to A^{\prime}(k_{v})/\phi(A(k_{v}))

given by inclusion of points; there are also

H^{1}(k,\ker\phi)\to H^{1}(k_{v},\ker\phi)\quad\text{and}\quad H^{1}(k,A)\to H^{1}(k_{v},A)

given by the restriction of maps for the absolute Galois group
G_{k}
to the decomposition group at
v
. Finally, define the
restricted cohomology
group by

H^{1}_{f}(k_{v},\ker\phi):=\mathrm{Image}\left(A^{\prime}(k_{v})/\phi(A(k_{v}))\to H^{1}(k_{v},\ker\phi)\right).

Definition 2.1
.

The
\phi
-Selmer group
, denoted
\mathrm{Sel}_{\phi}(A/k)
, is given by the exactness of

0\to A^{\prime}(k)/\phi(A(k))\to\mathrm{Sel}_{\phi}(A/k)\to\prod_{v}H^{1}(k_{v},\ker\phi)/H^{1}_{f}(k_{v},\ker\phi).

Example 2.2

(The
p
-Selmer group of an elliptic curve)
.

Fix a prime
p
and let
E
be an elliptic curve over
k
. Consider the multiplication-by-
p
maps
[p]:E\to E
. Then

0\to E(k)/pA(k)\to\mathrm{Sel}_{p}(E/k)\to\prod_{v}H^{1}(k_{v},E[p])/H^{1}_{f}(k_{v},E[p])

gives the
p
-Selmer group of
E
.

Remark 2.3
.

For an abelian variety
A
defined over a number field
k
, and
p
a prime, we have that

\mathrm{rk}(A(k))\leq\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{p}}\mathrm{Sel}_{p}(A/k).

A major theme will be to control
\mathrm{Sel}_{p}(A/k)
, namely force it to have dimension zero, by judicious choices of twists. This in turn gives us an understanding of the rank of
A(k)
. For instance, if
E^{d}/k
is the quadratic twist of an elliptic curve
E/k
by some
d\in k^{\times}/(k^{\times})^{2}
, then with
F=k(\sqrt{d})
, one has

\mathrm{rk}(E^{d}(k))=\mathrm{rk}(E(F))-\mathrm{rk}(E(k))\leq\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{2}}\mathrm{Sel}_{2}(E^{d}/k).

The idea is then to understand the 2-Selmer group of
E^{d}
from the data of
\mathrm{Sel}_{2}(E/k)
and
F=k(\sqrt{d})
. See, for example,
[
MR10
]
, where, among other things, the authors bound the number of
F
for which the
\mathrm{Sel}_{2}(E^{F}/k)
has a prescribed
\mathbb{F}_{2}
-dimension under some mild conditions.

3.
Parameterizing
S_{3}
Cubic Extensions

In this section we recall, for a quadratic extension of number fields

, how to construct
S_{3}
-cubic extensions
K/k
for which the unique quadratic subfield of the Galois closure
\tilde{K}/k
of
K/k
is

. That is,
\tilde{K}/k
is a Galois
S_{3}
-extension containing the
S_{3}
-cubic extension

and has unique quadratic sub-extension

. We follow the set-up of
[
CM11
]
and modify their notations somewhat to suit our purposes.

Let
k
be a number field, and let
\zeta_{3}
be a primitive cube root of unity. Throughout,
F/k
will denote a quadratic extension of
k
. Now, let
F_{z}=F(\zeta_{3})
and
k_{z}=k(\zeta_{3})
. Let
\tau_{2}
be the generator of
\mathrm{Gal}(F/k)\simeq C_{2}
and
\tau
be the generator of
\mathrm{Gal}(F_{z}/F)
.

S_{3}
-cubic extensions

with quadratic resolvent
F
depend on
\tau,\tau_{2}
and their actions on
\zeta_{3}
. We need three cases, for each of which we define a subset
T
of the group ring
\mathbb{Z}[\mathrm{Gal}(F_{z}/k)]
.

(i)

T=\{\tau_{2}+1\}
when
\tau
is trivial and
\tau_{2}
fixes
\zeta_{3}
; in this case
\zeta_{3}\in k
.

(ii)

T=\{\tau_{2}-1\}
when
\tau
is trivial and
\tau_{2}
does not fix
\zeta_{3}
; in this case
F=k(\zeta_{3})
.

(iii)

T=\{\tau+1,\tau_{2}+1\}
when
\tau
is nontrivial and does not fix
\zeta_{3}
; in this case
\zeta_{3}\notin F
.

Note that cases (i) , (ii), and (iii) above correspond to cases (3), (4), and (5) in
[
CM11
, Remark 2.2]

We now state how to obtain
S_{3}
-cubic extensions with a fixed quadratic resolvent field. The following lemma is the content of Proposition 2.7 and Lemma 2.8 in

]

.

Let

There exists a bijection between isomorphism classes of

4.2.

The local conditions that define

Definition 4.6
.

Let

be a non-degenerate quadratic form on a finite dimensional

- vector space

, denote by

the projection morphism to the

is

if the following two conditions hold for every

.

(1)

The subspace
\pi_{i}(W)\subset V
is a maximal isotropic subspace of
V
with respect to the quadratic form
q
.

(2)

The quadratic form
q
over
V
is trivial over
\pi_{i}(W)
.

We identify the local conditions which defines
\mathrm{Sel}_{1-\sigma_{3}}(B_{K/k}/k)
at primes

depends on the splitting behavior of

As stated in Proposition 7.2 of
[

]
