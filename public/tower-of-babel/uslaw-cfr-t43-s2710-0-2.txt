43 C.F.R. § 2710.0-2 — -2 Objective.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2710.0-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2710.0-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2710 (Sales: Federal Land Policy And Management Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The objective is to provide for the orderly disposition at not less than fair market value of public lands identified for sale as part of the land use planning process.
