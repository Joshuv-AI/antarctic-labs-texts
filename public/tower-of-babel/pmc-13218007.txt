Correction: Furin as target for suppression of mosquito-borne viruses
Authors: Alejandra Centurión, Bodunrin Omokungbe, Markus Oberpaul, Ludwig Dersch, Sabrina Stiehler, Cross Chambers, Marcus Lechner, Tim Lüddecke, Alejandra Vilcinskas, Torsten Steinmetzer, Kornelia Hardes
Published: 2026

Correction: Virology Journal (2026) 23:92

In the sentence beginning ‘This ubiquitous type-I trans­membrane protein.....’ in the Background section of this article [1], the arrow (↓) was inadvertently converted into the letter “i”. The corrected sentence is: “This ubiquitous type-I transmembrane protein contains a Ca2+-dependent subtilisin-like serine protease domain and preferentially cleaves its substrates after the multibasic motif R-X-(R/K)-R↓-X [13].”

The original article has been corrected.
