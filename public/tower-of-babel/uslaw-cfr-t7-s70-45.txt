7 C.F.R. § 70.45 — Misleading labeling.
Citation: us:cfr:7:70.45
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-70.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 70 (Voluntary Grading Of Poultry Products And Rabbit Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The use of the terms “Government Graded” and “Federal-State Graded” or terms of similar import in the labeling or advertising of any product without stating in the labeling or advertisement the U.S. grade of the product as determined by an authorized grader.
