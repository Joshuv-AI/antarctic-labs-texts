41 C.F.R. § 301-73.2 — -73.2 Agency responsibilities for Federal travel management program.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-73.2
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-73.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-73 (73—Travel Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must—
(a) Designate an authorized representative to administer the program, establish internal policies and procedures to govern use of the program, and require employees to use ETS instead of another Travel Management Service (TMS) or TMC, except when an exception has been granted under § 301-50.2 of this chapter; and
(b) Ensure agency-contracted TMC complements ETS and supports data exchange in an efficient and cost-effective manner.
