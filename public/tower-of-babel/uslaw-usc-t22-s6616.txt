22 U.S.C. § 6616 — Authority of Secretary of State to facilitate transition
Citation: us:usc:22:6616
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6616
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 74 (Foreign Affairs Agencies Consolidation); Subchapter VI (Transition); Part B (Reorganization Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any provision of this chapter, the Secretary of State, with the concurrence of the head of the appropriate Federal agency exercising functions transferred under this chapter, may transfer the whole or part of such functions prior to the effective dates established in this chapter, including the transfer of personnel and funds associated with such functions.
