46 U.S.C. § 7503 — Dangerous drugs as grounds for denial
Citation: us:usc:46:7503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section7503
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Merchant Seamen Licenses, Certificates, And Documents); Chapter 75 (General Procedures For Licensing, Certification, And Documentation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A license, certificate of registry, or merchant mariner’s document authorized to be issued under this part may be denied to an individual who—
(1)
within 10 years before applying for the license, certificate, or document, has been convicted of violating a dangerous drug law of the United States or of a State; or
(2)
when applying, has ever been a user of, or addicted to, a dangerous drug unless the individual provides satisfactory proof that the individual is cured.
