42 C.F.R. § 71.46 — Issuance of Deratting Certificates and Deratting Exemption Certificates.
Citation: us:cfr:42:71.46
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-71.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 71 (Foreign Quarantine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Valid Deratting Certificates or Deratting Exemption Certificates are not required for ships to enter a U.S. seaport. In accordance with Article 17 of the International Health Regulations, the Public Health Service may perform rodent infestation inspections and issue Deratting Certificates and Deratting Exemption Certificates.
