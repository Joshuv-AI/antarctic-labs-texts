15 C.F.R. § 18.9 — Awards against other agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:15:18.9
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-18.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 18 (Attorney'S Fees And Other Expenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an applicant is entitled to an award because it prevailed over another agency of the United States that participated in a proceeding before the Department and took a position that was not substantially justified, the award or an appropriate portion of the award shall be made against that agency.
