46 C.F.R. § 15.816 — Automatic radar plotting aids (ARPA).
Citation: us:cfr:46:15.816
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-15.816
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 15 (Manning Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every person in the required complement of deck officers, including the Master, on seagoing vessels equipped with automatic radar plotting aids (ARPA), except those vessels listed in § 15.105(f) and (g), must hold an appropriate STCW endorsement valid for vessels equipped with ARPA.
