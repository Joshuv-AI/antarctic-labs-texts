49 U.S.C. § 5567 — Preference for preserving buildings of historic or architectural significance
Citation: us:usc:49:5567
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section5567
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 55 (Intermodal Transportation); Subchapter II (Terminals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amtrak shall give preference to the use of rail passenger terminal facilities that will preserve buildings of historic or architectural significance.
