2 U.S.C. § 5307 — Certification of salary and mileage accounts
Citation: us:usc:2:5307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5307
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 53 (House Of Representatives Members); Subchapter I (Member Pay). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Salary and mileage accounts of Representatives and Delegates shall be certified by the Speaker of the House of Representatives; and such certificates shall be conclusive upon all the departments and officers of the Government.
