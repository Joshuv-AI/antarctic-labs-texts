21 C.F.R. § 211.52 — Washing and toilet facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:21:211.52
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-211.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 211 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice For Finished Pharmaceuticals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Adequate washing facilities shall be provided, including hot and cold water, soap or detergent, air driers or single-service towels, and clean toilet facilities easily accesible to working areas.
