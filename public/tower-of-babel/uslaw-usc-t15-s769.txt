15 U.S.C. § 769 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:15:769
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section769
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 16B (Federal Energy Administration); Subchapter I (Federal Energy Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter—
(1)
any reference to “function” or “functions” shall be deemed to include references to duty, obligation, power, authority, responsibility, right, privilege, and activity, or the plural thereof, as the case may be; and
(2)
any reference to “perform” or “performance”, when used in relation to functions, shall be deemed to include the exercise of power, authority, rights, and privileges.
