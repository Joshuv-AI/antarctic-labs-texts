10 U.S.C. § 1167 — Members under confinement by sentence of court-martial: separation after six months confinement
Citation: us:usc:10:1167
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1167
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 59 (Separation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in regulations prescribed by the Secretary of Defense, a member sentenced by a court-martial to a period of confinement for more than six months may be separated from the member’s armed force at any time after the sentence to confinement has become final under chapter 47 of this title and the member has served in confinement for a period of six months.
