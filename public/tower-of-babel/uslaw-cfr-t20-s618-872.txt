20 C.F.R. § 618.872 — Travel under the Trade Adjustment Assistance Program.
Citation: us:cfr:20:618.872
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-618.872
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 618 (Trade Adjustment Assistance Under The Trade Act Of 1974, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) TAA Program participants are subject to the FTR at 41 CFR chapters 300 through 304 for all travel paid for with TAA Program funds.
(b) Except for the definition of “commuting area,” States may not apply State or local travel policies and restrictions to TAA Program participants receiving reimbursements for travel under the Act.
(c) In instances where the FTR is silent or defers to the Federal agency's travel policies, the State must apply the relevant policies of the Department.
