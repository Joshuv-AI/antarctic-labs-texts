46 C.F.R. § 31.25-1 — -1 Load lines required—TB/OCL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:31.25-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-31.25-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 31 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All tank vessels of 150 gross tons or over, or 79 feet in length or greater, navigating the oceans, coastwise waters, and Great Lakes are subject to the regulations in parts 42 to 45, inclusive, subchapter E (Load Lines), of this chapter, as applicable.
