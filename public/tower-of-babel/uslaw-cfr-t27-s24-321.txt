27 C.F.R. § 24.321 — Decolorizing material record.
Citation: us:cfr:27:24.321
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-24.321
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 24 (Wine). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A proprietor who treats juice or wine to remove excess color with activated carbon or any other decolorizing material shall maintain a record to show:
(a) The date the decolorizing material is added to the juice or wine;
(b) The type (e.g. grape variety or kind of wine) and volume of juice or wine treated with decolorizing material; and
(c) The kind and quantity of decolorizing material used to treat the juice or wine.
