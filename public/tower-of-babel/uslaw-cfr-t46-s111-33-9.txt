46 C.F.R. § 111.33-9 — -9 Ventilation exhaust.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.33-9
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.33-9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The exhaust of each forced-air semiconductor converter system must:
(a) Terminate in a location other than a hazardous location under Subpart 111.105 of this part; and
(b) Not impinge upon any other electric device.
