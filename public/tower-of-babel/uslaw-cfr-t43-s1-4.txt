43 C.F.R. § 1.4 — Disqualifications.
Citation: us:cfr:43:1.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1 (Practices Before The Department Of The Interior). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No individual may practice before the Department if such practice would violate the provisions of 18 U.S.C. 203, 205, or 207.
