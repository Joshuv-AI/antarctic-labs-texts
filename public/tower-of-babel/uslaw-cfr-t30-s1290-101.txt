30 C.F.R. § 1290.101 — What leases are subject to this part?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1290.101
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1290.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1290 (Appeal Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to:
(a) All Federal mineral leases onshore and on the Outer Continental Shelf (OCS); and
(b) All federally-administered mineral leases on Indian tribal and individual Indian mineral owners' lands, regardless of the statutory authority under which the lease was issued or maintained.
