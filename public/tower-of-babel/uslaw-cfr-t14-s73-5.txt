14 C.F.R. § 73.5 — Bearings; radials; miles.
Citation: us:cfr:14:73.5
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-73.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 73 (Special Use Airspace). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All bearings and radials in this part are true from point of origin.
(b) Unless otherwise specified, all mileages in this part are stated as statute miles.
