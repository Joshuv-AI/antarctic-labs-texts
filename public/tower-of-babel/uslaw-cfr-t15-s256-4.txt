15 C.F.R. § 256.4 — Qualifications.
Citation: us:cfr:15:256.4
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-256.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 256 (Research Associate Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each candidate selected to serve as a Research Associate must be determined to be scientifically qualified by the Sponsor and by the NIST, and found by NIST to be of good moral character and to possess suitable personal qualities.
