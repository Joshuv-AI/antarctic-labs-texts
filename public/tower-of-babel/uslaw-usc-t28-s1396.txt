28 U.S.C. § 1396 — Internal revenue taxes
Citation: us:usc:28:1396
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1396
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part IV (Jurisdiction And Venue); Chapter 87 (District Courts; Venue). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any civil action for the collection of internal revenue taxes may be brought in the district where the liability for such tax accrues, in the district of the taxpayer’s residence, or in the district where the return was filed.
