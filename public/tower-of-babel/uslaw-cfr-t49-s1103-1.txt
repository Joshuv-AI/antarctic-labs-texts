49 C.F.R. § 1103.1 — Register of practitioners.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1103.1
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1103.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1103 (Practitioners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board maintains a register containing the names of all non-attorneys entitled to practice before it. The register is maintained according to the individual non-attorney practitioner's name and not by corporate or firm name. Corporations and firms are not admitted or recognized as practitioners before the Board.
