5 C.F.R. § 734.403 — Participation in elections.
Citation: us:cfr:5:734.403
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-734.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 734 (Political Activities Of Federal Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each employee covered under this subpart retains the right to:
(a) Register and vote in any election;
(b) Take an active part, as a candidate or in support of a candidate, in a nonpartisan election; and
(c) Serve as an election judge or clerk, or in a similar position, to perform nonpartisan duties as prescribed by State or local law.
