40 C.F.R. § 58.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:58.3
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-58.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 58 (Ambient Air Quality Surveillance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to:
(a) State air pollution control agencies.
(b) Any local air pollution control agency to which the State has delegated authority to operate a portion of the State's SLAMS network.
(c) Owners or operators of proposed sources.
