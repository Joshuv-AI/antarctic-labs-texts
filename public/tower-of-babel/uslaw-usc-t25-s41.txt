25 U.S.C. § 41 — Special agents and commissioners
Citation: us:usc:25:41
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section41
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 2 (Officers Of Indian Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All special agents and commissioners not appointed by the President shall be appointed by the Secretary of the Interior.
