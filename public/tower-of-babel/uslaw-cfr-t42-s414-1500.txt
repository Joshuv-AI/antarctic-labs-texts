42 C.F.R. § 414.1500 — Basis, purpose, and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:42:414.1500
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-414.1500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 414 (Payment For Part B Medical And Other Health Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements section 1861(iii) of the Act with respect to the requirements that must be met for Medicare payment to be made for home infusion services furnished to eligible beneficiaries.
