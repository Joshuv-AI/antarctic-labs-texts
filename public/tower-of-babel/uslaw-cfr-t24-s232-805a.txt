24 C.F.R. § 232.805a — a Mortgagee's late charge.
Citation: us:cfr:24:232.805a
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-232.805a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 232 (Mortgage Insurance For Nursing Homes, Intermediate Care Facilities, Board And Care Homes, And Assisted Living Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Mortgage insurance premiums which are paid to the Commissioner more than 15 days after the billing date or due date, whichever is later, shall include a late charge of 4 percent of the amount of the payment due, except that no late charge shall be required with respect to any case for which HUD fails to render a proper billing to the mortgagee.
