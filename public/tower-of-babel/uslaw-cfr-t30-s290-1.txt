30 C.F.R. § 290.1 — What is the purpose of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:30:290.1
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-290.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 290 (Appeal Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to explain the procedures for appeals of Bureau of Safety and Environmental Enforcement (BSEE) decisions and orders issued under 30 CFR chapter II.
