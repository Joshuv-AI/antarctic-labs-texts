9 C.F.R. § 314.4 — Suppression of odors in preparing inedible products.
Citation: us:cfr:9:314.4
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-314.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 314 (Handling And Disposal Of Condemned Or Other Inedible Products At Official Establishments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tanks, fertilizer driers, and other equipment used in the preparation of inedible product must be operated in a manner that will suppress odors incident to such preparation which could adulterate edible product or create insanitary conditions.
