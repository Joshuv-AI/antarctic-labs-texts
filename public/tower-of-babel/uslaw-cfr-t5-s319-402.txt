5 C.F.R. § 319.402 — Scientific and professional positions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:319.402
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-319.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 319 (Employment In Senior-Level And Scientific And Professional Positions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) ST positions are filled without competitive examination under 5 U.S.C. 3325.
(b) ST positions are not subject to the citizenship requirements in 5 CFR part 338, subpart A. Agencies, however, must observe any restrictions on the employment of noncitizens in applicable appropriations acts.
(c) ST employees acquire competitive status immediately upon appointment. They are not required to serve a probationary or trial period.
