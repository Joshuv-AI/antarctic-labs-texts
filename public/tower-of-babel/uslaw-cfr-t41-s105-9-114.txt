41 C.F.R. § 105-9.114 — -9.114 Assurance of compliance and recipient assessment of age distinctions.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-9 (9—Discrimination Prohibited On The Basis Of Age). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Assurance of compliance. Each recipient of Federal financial assistance from GSA signs a written assurance as specified by GSA that it intends to comply with the Act and this part.
(b) Recipient assessment of age distinctions. (1) As part of a compliance review under § 105-82.715 of this chapter or complaint investigation under § 105-82.718 of this chapter, GSA may require a recipient employing the equivalent of 15 or more employees to complete a written self-evaluation of any age distinction imposed in its program or activity receiving Federal financial assistance from GSA to assess the recipient's compliance with the Act.
(2) If an assessment indicates a violation of the Act and this part, the recipient takes corrective action.
