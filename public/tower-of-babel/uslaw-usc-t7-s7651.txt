7 U.S.C. § 7651 — Nutrient composition data
Citation: us:usc:7:7651
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7651
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 103 (Agricultural Research, Extension, And Education Reform); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous Provisions); Part B (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary of Agriculture shall update, on a periodic basis, nutrient composition data.
(b) Report
Not later than 180 days after June 23, 1998, the Secretary shall submit to the Committee on Agriculture of the House of Representatives and the Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry of the Senate a report that describes—
(1)
the method the Secretary will use to update nutrient composition data, including the quality assurance criteria that will be used and the method for generating the data; and
(2)
the timing for updating the data.
