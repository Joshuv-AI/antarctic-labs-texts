26 U.S.C. § 3502 — Nondeductibility of taxes in computing taxable income
Citation: us:usc:26:3502
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section3502
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 25 (General Provisions Relating To Employment Taxes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The taxes imposed by section 3101 of chapter 21, and by sections 3201 and 3211 of chapter 22 shall not be allowed as a deduction to the taxpayer in computing taxable income under subtitle A.
(b)
The tax deducted and withheld under chapter 24 shall not be allowed as a deduction either to the employer or to the recipient of the income in computing taxable income under subtitle A.
