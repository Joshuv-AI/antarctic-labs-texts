18 C.F.R. § 368.1 — Promulgation.
Citation: us:cfr:18:368.1
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-368.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 368 (Preservation Of Records Of Holding Companies And Service Companies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is prescribed and promulgated as the regulations governing the preservation of records by any holding company and by any service company within a holding company system subject to the jurisdiction of the Commission under the Public Utility Holding Company Act of 2005 (42 U.S.C. §§ 16451 et seq.).
