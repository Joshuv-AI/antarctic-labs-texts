34 U.S.C. § 60701 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:34:60701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section60701
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 607 (Project Safe Neighborhoods Block Grant Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this chapter—
(1)
the term “firearms offenses” means an offense under section 922 or 924 of title 18;
(2)
the term “Program” means the Project Safe Neighborhoods Block Grant Program established under section 60702 of this title; and
(3)
the term “transnational organized crime group” has the meaning given such term in section 2708(k)(6) of title 22.
