48 C.F.R. § 619.705-4 — 619.705-4 Reviewing the subcontracting plan.
Citation: us:cfr:48:619.705-4
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-619.705-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 619 (Small Business Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OSDBU shall review subcontracting plans to determine if small, small disadvantaged, women-owned small, HUBZone small, veteran-owned small, and service-disabled veteran-owned small business concerns are afforded the maximum practicable opportunity to participate as subcontractors. OSDBU shall recommend to the contracting officer changes needed to subcontracting plans found to be deficient.
