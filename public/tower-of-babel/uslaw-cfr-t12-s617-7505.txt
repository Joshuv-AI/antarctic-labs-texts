12 C.F.R. § 617.7505 — How will the qualified lender know when FCA is considering issuing a distressed loan restructuring directive?
Citation: us:cfr:12:617.7505
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-617.7505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 617 (Borrower Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When FCA intends to issue a distressed loan restructuring directive, it will notify the qualified lender in writing. The notice will state:
(a) The reasons FCA intends to issue a distressed loan restructuring directive;
(b) The proposed contents of the distressed loan restructuring directive; and
(c) Any other relevant information.
