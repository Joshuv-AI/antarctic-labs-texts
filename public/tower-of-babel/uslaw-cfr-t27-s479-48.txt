27 C.F.R. § 479.48 — Failure to pay special (occupational) tax.
Citation: us:cfr:27:479.48
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-479.48
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 479 (Machine Guns, Destructive Devices, And Certain Other Firearms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who engages in a business taxable under 26 U.S.C. 5801, without timely payment of the tax imposed with respect to such business (see § 479.34) shall be liable for such tax, plus the interest and penalties thereon (see 26 U.S.C. 6601 and 6651). In addition, such person may be liable for criminal penalties under 26 U.S.C. 5871.
