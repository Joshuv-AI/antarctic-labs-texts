10 C.F.R. § 626.3 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:10:626.3
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-626.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 626 (Procedures For Acquisition Of Petroleum For The Strategic Petroleum Reserve). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedures in this part apply to the acquisition of petroleum products by DOE for the Strategic Petroleum Reserve through purchase or exchange, as well as to deferrals of contractually scheduled deliveries.
