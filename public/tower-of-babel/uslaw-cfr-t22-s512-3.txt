22 C.F.R. § 512.3 — Use of procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:22:512.3
Creator: United States Agency For Global Media
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-512.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 512 (Collection Of Debts Under The Debt Collection Act Of 1982). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Procedures authorized by this regulation (including but not limited to referral to a debt collection agency, administrative offset, or salary offset) may be used singly or in combination, providing the requirements of the applicable law and regulation are satisfied.
