5 C.F.R. § 301.301 — Eligibility under the authority of Executive Order 12362.
Citation: us:cfr:5:301.301
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-301.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 301 (Overseas Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees who serve under overseas local hire appointments as defined in § 315.608(b) of this chapter and meet the eligibility criteria of § 315.608(a) of this chapter are eligible for noncompetitive career-conditional, term, or temporary limited appointment when they return to the United States.
