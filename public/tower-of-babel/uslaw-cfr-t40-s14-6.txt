40 C.F.R. § 14.6 — Time limits for filing a claim.
Citation: us:cfr:40:14.6
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-14.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 14 (Employee Personal Property Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim under this part is considered by the EPA Claims Officer only if it is in writing and received within two years after the claim accrues. The EPA Claims Officer may consider a claim not filed within this period when the claim accrued during a period of armed conflict and the requirements of 31 U.S.C. 3721(g) are met.
