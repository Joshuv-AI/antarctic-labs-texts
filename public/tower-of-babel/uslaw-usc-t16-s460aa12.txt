16 U.S.C. § 460aa–12 — Authorization of appropriations; availability of land and water conservation fund money
Citation: us:usc:16:460aa–12
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460aa–12
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXXXV (Sawtooth National Recreation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated for the purposes of this subchapter not more than $47,802,000 for the acquisition of lands and interests in lands and not more than $26,241,000 for development. Money appropriated from the land and water conservation fund shall be available for the acquisition of lands, waters, and interests therein within the recreation area.
