45 C.F.R. § 1629.5 — What losses must the bond cover?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1629.5
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1629.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1629 (Bonding Requirements For Recipients). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The bond must provide recovery for loss caused by such acts as fraud, dishonesty, larceny, theft, embezzlement, forgery, misappropriation, wrongful abstraction, wrongful conversion, willful misapplication, or any other fraudulent or dishonest act committed by an employee, officer, director, agent, or volunteer.
