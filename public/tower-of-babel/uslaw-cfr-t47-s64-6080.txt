47 C.F.R. § 64.6080 — Per-Call, Per-Connection or Per-Communication Charges.
Citation: us:cfr:47:64.6080
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-64.6080
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 64 (Miscellaneous Rules Relating To Common Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Provider must not impose a Per-Call, Per-Connection, or Per-Communication Charge on a Consumer for any Incarcerated People's Communications Services communication.
