5 C.F.R. § 734.601 — Employees who work on an irregular or occasional basis.
Citation: us:cfr:5:734.601
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-734.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 734 (Political Activities Of Federal Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee who works on an irregular or occasional basis or is a special Government employee as defined in 18 U.S.C. 202(a) is subject to the provisions of the applicable subpart of this part when he or she is on duty.
