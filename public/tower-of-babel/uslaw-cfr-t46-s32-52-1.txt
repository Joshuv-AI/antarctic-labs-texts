46 C.F.R. § 32.52-1 — -1 Bilge pumps on tank vessels constructed or converted on or after November 19, 1952—TB/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:32.52-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-32.52-1

The number and arrangement of bilge pumps on each tank vessel shall conform to the requirements of subchapter F (Marine Engineering) of this chapter, except as hereinafter provided in this subpart.
