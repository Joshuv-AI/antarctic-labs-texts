41 C.F.R. § 101-39.4901 — -39.4901 Obtaining standard and optional forms.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-39.4901
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-39.4901
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-39 (39—Interagency Fleet Management Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Standard and optional forms referenced in part 101-39 may be obtained through the General Services Administration, Inventory and Requisition Management Branch, Attn: FCNI, Washington, DC 20406, or through regional GSA Federal Supply Service Bureaus. GSA regional offices will provide support to requesting activities needing forms.
