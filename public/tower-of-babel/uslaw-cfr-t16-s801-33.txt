16 C.F.R. § 801.33 — Consummation of an acquisition by acceptance of tendered shares of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:16:801.33
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-801.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 801 (Coverage Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The acceptance for payment of any shares tendered in a tender offer is the consummation of an acquisition of those shares within the meaning of the act.
