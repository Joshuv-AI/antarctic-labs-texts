Homotopy types of S 2 ​ k − 1 S^{2k-1} -bundles over S 2 ​ k S^{2k} Journal: xx
Zhongjian Zhu, Jianzhong Pan

Abstract
In this paper, we give the new necessary and sufficient conditions on the attaching map f f of the top cell of a CW-complex having the homotopy type of the total space of S 2 ​ k − 1 S^{2k-1} -fibration over S 2 ​ k S^{2k} for any k ≥ 2 k\geq 2 . As an application, we give the homotopy classification of the S 2 ​ k − 1 S^{2k-1} -bundles over S 2 ​ k S^{2k} for some specific numbers k ≥ 2 k\geq 2 in our subsequent paper.
