21 C.F.R. § 16.40 — Commissioner.
Citation: us:cfr:21:16.40
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-16.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 16 (Regulatory Hearing Before The Food And Drug Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever the Commissioner has delegated authority on a matter for which a regulatory hearing is available under this part, the functions of the Commissioner under this part may be performed by any of the officials to whom the authority has been delegated, e.g., a center director.
