21 C.F.R. § 56.114 — Cooperative research.
Citation: us:cfr:21:56.114
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-56.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 56 (Institutional Review Boards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In complying with these regulations, institutions involved in multi-institutional studies may use joint review, reliance upon the review of another qualified IRB, or similar arrangements aimed at avoidance of duplication of effort.
