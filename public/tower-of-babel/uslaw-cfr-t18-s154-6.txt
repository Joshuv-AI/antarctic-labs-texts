18 C.F.R. § 154.6 — Acceptance for filing not approval.
Citation: us:cfr:18:154.6
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-154.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 154 (Rate Schedules And Tariffs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The acceptance for filing of any tariff, contract or part thereof does not constitute approval by the Commission. Any filing which does not comply with any applicable statute, rule, or order, may be rejected.
