21 C.F.R. § 874.5800 — External nasal splint.
Citation: us:cfr:21:874.5800
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-874.5800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 874 (Ear, Nose, And Throat Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. An external nasal splint is a rigid or partially rigid device intended for use externally for immobilization of parts of the nose.
(b) Classification. Class I (general controls). The device is exempt from the premarket notification procedures in subpart E of part 807 of this chapter subject to the limitations in § 874.9.
