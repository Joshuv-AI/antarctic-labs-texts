12 C.F.R. § 626.6015 — Nondiscriminatory appraisal.
Citation: us:cfr:12:626.6015
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-626.6015
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 626 (Nondiscrimination In Lending). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No Farm Credit institution shall discriminate against any person on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, handicap, familial status, or national origin when conducting, using, or relying upon an appraisal of residential real property that is subject to sale, rental, or other financing transaction.
