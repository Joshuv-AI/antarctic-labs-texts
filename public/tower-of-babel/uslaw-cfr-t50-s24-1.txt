50 C.F.R. § 24.1 — Purpose of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:50:24.1
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-24.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 24 (Importation And Exportation Of Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations contained in this part are for the purpose of establishing ports for the importation, exportation and reexportation of plants.
