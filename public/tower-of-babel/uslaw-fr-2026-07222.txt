POSTAL SERVICE


International Product Change--International Priority Airmail, 
Commercial ePacket, Priority Mail Express International, Priority Mail 
International & First-Class Package International Service Agreements

AGENCY: Postal Service.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Postal Service gives notice of filing requests with the 
Postal Regulatory Commission to add certain International Priority 
Airmail, Commercial ePacket, Priority Mail Express International, 
Priority Mail International & First-Class Package International Service 
contracts to the list of Negotiated Service Agreements in the 
Competitive Product List in the Mail Classification Schedule.

DATES: Date of notice: April 14, 2026.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Christopher C. Meyerson, (202) 268-
7820.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The United States Postal Service hereby 
gives notice that, pursuant to 39 U.S.C. 3642 and 3632(b)(3), it filed 
with the Postal Regulatory Commission the following requests:
