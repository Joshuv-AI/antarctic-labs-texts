25 C.F.R. § 211.52 — Fees.
Citation: us:cfr:25:211.52
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-211.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 211 (Leasing Of Tribal Lands For Mineral Development). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise authorized by the Secretary, each permit, lease, sublease, or other contract, or assignment, thereof shall be accompanied by a filing fee of $75.00 at the time of filing.
