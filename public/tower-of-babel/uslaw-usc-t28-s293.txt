28 U.S.C. § 293 — Judges of the Court of International Trade
Citation: us:usc:28:293
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section293
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part I (Organization Of Courts); Chapter 13 (Assignment Of Judges To Other Courts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
1 The Chief Justice of the United States may designate and assign temporarily any judge of the Court of International Trade to perform judicial duties in any circuit, either in a court of appeals or district court, upon presentation of a certificate of necessity by the chief judge or circuit justice of the circuit in which the need arises.
