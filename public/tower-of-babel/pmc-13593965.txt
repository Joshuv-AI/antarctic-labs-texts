Correction: Retractions of Health Economic Evaluations: A Systematic Review
Authors: Eric Julian Manalastas, Aditi Hombali, Juliette C. Thompson, David Alexander Scott
Published: 2026

Correction: PharmacoEconomics - Open (2026) 10:215–227 https://doi.org/10.1007/s41669-025-00624-9

In the abstract, the sentence “Despite retraction, studies were included in subsequent evidence reviews (6/17 studies, 35%) and one was used to inform clinical practice recommendations (1/17 studies, 6%)” was corrected to“Four already-retracted economic evaluations were included in subsequent evidence reviews (4/17 studies, 24%) and one was used to inform clinical practice recommendations (1/17 studies, 6%).” In the Results section of the article, the sentence “In spite of retraction, 6/17 studies [37, 39, 42, 43, 47, 50] were incorporated into subsequent systematic reviews as part of the economic evidence base—without being marked as retracted [62–69]” was replaced by the following: “Six of the 17 economic evaluations [37, 39, 42, 43, 47, 50] were cited in 8 systematic reviews [62–69]. In 4/8 reviews [63, 64, 66, 67], the literature search had already been conducted or the review manuscript had already been accepted, prior to the retraction of the economic evaluation. In 1/8 reviews [68], the literature search was only conducted post-retraction. Thus, the review incorporated the already-retracted economic evaluation (a downstream effect). In the remaining 3/8 reviews [62, 65, 69], literature search dates were not reported. But the economic evaluations had already been retracted when these reviews were submitted to their respective journals. Therefore, it was assumed that these reviews likely incorporated the already-retracted economic evaluations (possible downstream effects).” Finally, in the Discussion section, the sentence “These include incorporation of the evaluations in subsequent evidence synthesis [62–67] and even in clinical practice guidelines [70]” was corrected to read “These include incorporation of the evaluations in subsequent evidence synthesis [62, 65, 68, 69] and even in clinical practice guidelines [70].” The original article has been corrected.
