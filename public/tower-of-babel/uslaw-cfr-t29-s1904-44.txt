29 C.F.R. § 1904.44 — Retention and updating of old forms.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1904.44
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1904.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1904 (Recording And Reporting Occupational Injuries And Illnesses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must save your copies of the OSHA 200 and 101 forms for five years following the year to which they relate and continue to provide access to the data as though these forms were the OSHA 300 and 301 forms. You are not required to update your old 200 and 101 forms.
