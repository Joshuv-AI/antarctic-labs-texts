Direct Generation of Human Neuronal Cells from Adult Astrocytes by Small Molecules
Authors: Longfei Gao, Wuqiang Guan, Min Wang, Huihan Wang, Jiali Yu, Qing Liu, Binlong Qiu, Yongchun Yu, Yifang Ping, Xiuwu Bian, Li Shen, Gang Pei
Published: 2026

Main text

(Stem Cell Reports 8, 538–547; March 14, 2017)

The authors identified an inadvertent error in Figure S2I of the supplemental information in the originally published article, where two representative image panels were incorrectly placed under the wrong experimental group during image archiving and figure assembly. After re-examining the original raw data acquired in 2016, the authors replaced the affected panels with the correct representative images from the original dataset; the corrected supplemental figure can be seen here. This correction does not affect any quantitative analyses, data interpretation, results, or conclusions of the study. The authors apologize for this oversight.
