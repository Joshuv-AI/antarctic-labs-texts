16 C.F.R. § 1031.14 — Observation criteria.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1031.14
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1031.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1031 (Commission Participation And Commission Employee Involvement In Voluntary Standards Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Commission official or employee may, on occasion, attend voluntary standards meetings for the sole purpose of observation, with the advance approval of his or her supervisor and any other person designated by agency management procedures. Commission officials and employees shall notify the Voluntary Standard Coordinator, for information purposes, prior to observing a voluntary standards meeting.
