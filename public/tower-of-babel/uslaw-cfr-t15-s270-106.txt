15 C.F.R. § 270.106 — Conflicts of interest related to service on a Team.
Citation: us:cfr:15:270.106
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-270.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 270 (National Construction Safety Teams). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Team members who are not Federal employees will be Federal Government contractors.
(b) Contracts between NIST and Team members will include appropriate provisions to ensure that potential conflicts of interest that arise prior to award or during the contract are identified and resolved.
