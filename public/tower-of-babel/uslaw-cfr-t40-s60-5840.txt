40 C.F.R. § 60.5840 — Who must complete the operator training course? By when?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.5840
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.5840
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Three types of employees must complete the EPA or state-approved operator training course:
(1) Chief facility operators.
(2) Shift supervisors.
(3) Control room operators.
(b) Those employees must complete the operator training course by the later of three dates:
(1) Six months after your municipal waste combustor initial startup.
(2) September 10, 2026.
(3) The date before an employee assumes responsibilities that affect operation of the municipal waste combustor.
