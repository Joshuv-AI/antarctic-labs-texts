31 C.F.R. § 363.54 — What is the minimum amount of a book-entry savings bond that I must hold in my account?
Citation: us:cfr:31:363.54
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-363.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 363 (Regulations Governing Securities Held In Treasurydirect). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each bond held in your account must have a redemption value of at least $25. If you request a transaction that would reduce the remaining redemption value of the bond to an amount less than $25, we will not permit the transaction to occur.
