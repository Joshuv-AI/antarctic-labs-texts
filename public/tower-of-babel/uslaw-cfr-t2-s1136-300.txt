2 C.F.R. § 1136.300 — Purpose of OAR Article III.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1136.300
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1136.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1136 (Other Administrative Requirements: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OAR Article III addresses remedies for noncompliance, including suspension and termination of awards. It thereby implements OMB guidance in 2 CFR 200.338 through 200.340 and 200.342.
