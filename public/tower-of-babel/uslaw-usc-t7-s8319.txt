7 U.S.C. § 8319 — Surveillance of zoonotic diseases
Citation: us:usc:7:8319
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section8319
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 109 (Animal Health Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Health and Human Services, through the Commissioner of Food and Drugs and the Director of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, and the Secretary of Agriculture shall coordinate the surveillance of zoonotic diseases.
