45 C.F.R. § 2522.400 — What process does AmeriCorps use to select new grantees?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2522.400
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2522.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2522 (Americorps Participants, Programs, And Applicants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

AmeriCorps uses a multi-stage process, which may include review by panels of experts, AmeriCorps staff review, and approval by the Chief Executive Officer or the Board of Directors, or their designee.
