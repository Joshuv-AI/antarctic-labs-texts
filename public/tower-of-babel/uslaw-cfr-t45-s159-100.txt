45 C.F.R. § 159.100 — Basis and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:45:159.100
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-159.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 159 (Health Care Reform Insurance Web Portal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes provisions governing a Web portal that will provide information on health insurance coverage options in each of the 50 States and the District of Columbia. It sets forth data submission requirements for health insurance issuers. It covers the individual market and the small group market.
