5 C.F.R. § 2412.14 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2412.14
Creator: Federal Labor Relations Authority, General Counsel Of The Federal Labor Relations Authority And Federal Service Impasses Panel
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2412.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2412 (Privacy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who knowingly and willfully requests or obtains any record concerning an individual from the Authority, the General Counsel, the IG, or the Panel under false pretenses will be subject to criminal prosecution under 5 U.S.C. 552a(i)(3), which provides that such person shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and fined not more than $5,000.
