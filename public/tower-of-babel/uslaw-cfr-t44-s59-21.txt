44 C.F.R. § 59.21 — Purpose of subpart.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 59 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart lists actions that must be taken by a community to become eligible and to remain eligible for the Program.
