27 C.F.R. § 27.175 — Receipt of distilled spirits by consignee.
Citation: us:cfr:27:27.175
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-27.175
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 27 (Importation Of Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Proprietors of distilled spirits plants who receive imported spirits under this subpart shall follow the requirements in 27 CFR part 19 for spirits received by transfer in bond. Proprietors of distilled spirits plants may receive such imported spirits even if they are exempt from bond requirements under § 19.151(d) of this chapter. However, proprietors are not required to file application on TTB Form 5100.16 to receive imported spirits from customs custody.
