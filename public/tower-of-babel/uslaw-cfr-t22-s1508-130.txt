22 C.F.R. § 1508.130 — Does exclusion under the Federal procurement system affect a person's eligibility to participate in nonprocurement transactions?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1508.130
Creator: African Development Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1508.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1508 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any Federal agency excludes a person under the FAR on or after August 25, 1995, the excluded person is also ineligible to participate in nonprocurement covered transactions under this part. Therefore, an exclusion under the FAR has reciprocal effect in Federal nonprocurement transactions.
