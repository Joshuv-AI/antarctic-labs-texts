45 C.F.R. § 2525.240 — Is there a limit on the amount of an Eligible Individual's education award that AmeriCorps will disburse to an institution for a given period of enrollment?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2525.240
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2525.240
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2525 (National Service Trust). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. AmeriCorps' disbursement from an Eligible Individual's education award for any period of enrollment may not exceed the difference between:
(a) The Eligible Individual's educational expenses, determined by the institution; and
(b) The Eligible Individual's estimated financial assistance for that period under part A of Title IV of the Higher Education Act.
