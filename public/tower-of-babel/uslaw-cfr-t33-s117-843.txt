33 C.F.R. § 117.843 — Trent River.
Citation: us:cfr:33:117.843
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-117.843
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 117 (Drawbridge Operation Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The draw of the U.S. 70 Bridge, mile 0.0, at New Bern:
(a) Shall open on the hour and the half hour from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m., except during the times authorized in accordance with (a) (2) of this section.
(b) Shall remain closed from 7:30 a.m. to 8:30 a.m. and from 4:30 p.m. to 6 p.m., Monday through Friday; except holidays.
(c) Shall open on signal from 10 p.m. to 6 a.m.
(d) Shall open upon request at all times for vessels as defined in § 117.31.
