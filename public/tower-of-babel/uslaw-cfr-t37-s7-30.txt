37 C.F.R. § 7.30 — Effect of cancellation or expiration of international registration.
Citation: us:cfr:37:7.30
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-7.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 7 (Rules Of Practice In Filings Pursuant To The Protocol Relating To The Madrid Agreement Concerning The International Registration Of Marks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the International Bureau notifies the Office of the cancellation or expiration of an international registration, in whole or in part, the Office shall cancel, in whole or in part, the corresponding pending or registered extension of protection to the United States. The date of cancellation of an extension of protection or relevant part shall be the date of cancellation or expiration of the corresponding international registration or relevant part.
