2 C.F.R. § 182.515 — Are there any exceptions to those actions?
Citation: us:cfr:2:182.515
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Government-Wide Guidance For Federal Financial Assistance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-182.515
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 182 (Government-Wide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For a particular award, the Federal agency head may waive, in writing, a suspension of payments under an award, suspension or termination of an award, or suspension or debarment of a recipient if the agency head determines that such a waiver would be in the public interest. This exception authority cannot be delegated to any other official.
