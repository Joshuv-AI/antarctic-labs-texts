Searching for leptophilic composite asymmetric dark sector at e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} colliders Preprint: CPTNP-2025-034
Chih-Ting Lu 1,2 , Changbin Xi 1

Abstract
Composite asymmetric dark matter (ADM) models provide a well-motivated paradigm that simultaneously explains dark matter (DM) relic density and matter-antimatter asymmetry. In these models, the mass of the DM candidate (the lightest dark baryon) is generated through the dark confinement scale dynamics. Although the leptophilic composite ADM model offers a viable framework, comprehensive studies of its collider phenomenology are absent. This work systematically explores novel signatures from leptophilic composite asymmetric dark sector at both low-energy and high-energy e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} colliders as well as other existing collider constraints. We demonstrate detectability of TeV-scale mediators along with sub-GeV to GeV-scale lightest dark mesons at Belle II and its proposed far detector, GAZELLE, as well as CEPC experiments. Moreover, these experiments exhibit complementary coverage of the model parameter space.

VI
Summary and discussion

We explored novel signatures of a leptophilic QCD-like composite asymmetric dark sector at
e^{+}e^{-}
colliders by simulating dark parton showering, hadronization, and detector response to map the experimental reach. The study reveals a sharp transition in the dark pion proper decay length at the muon threshold (Fig.
2
). Recast constraints from mono-photon searches and from slepton/stau reinterpretations limit the low-mass bi-charged scalar mediator region (Figs.
3
and
4
), while combined projections for BaBar, Belle II, L-GAZELLE, and CEPC produce the characteristic band-shaped sensitivity in the
(m_{X},\kappa)
plane as summarized in Fig.
11
. After applying cosmological constraints on the
\pi_{d}
lifetime, nearly all parameter space for
10~\text{MeV}\leq m_{\pi_{d}}<2m_{\mu}
and
m_{X}\lesssim 10~\text{TeV}
is excluded. Additionally, for
2m_{\mu}\leq m_{\pi_{d}}<2m_{\tau}
, a significant fraction of parameter space with
\kappa\gtrsim 1
and
m_{X}\lesssim 10~\text{TeV}
is also excluded.

e^{+}e^{-}
colliders.

Finally, we outline promising directions for future work. High-energy muon colliders could directly produce the bi-charged scalar mediator via Drell–Yan processes and thereby provide an independent probe of the parameter space. Advanced data-scouting or trigger-level analyses for low-
p_{T}

2m_{\tau}

\tau

\tau
