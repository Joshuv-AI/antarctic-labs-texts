21 C.F.R. § 7.12 — Guaranty.
Citation: us:cfr:21:7.12
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-7.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 7 (Enforcement Policy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In case of the giving of a guaranty or undertaking referred to in section 303(c)(2) or (3) of the act, each person signing such guaranty or undertaking shall be considered to have given it.
