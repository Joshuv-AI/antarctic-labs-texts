47 C.F.R. § 27.1212 — License term.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 27 (Miscellaneous Wireless Communications Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) BRS/EBS licenses shall be issued for a period of 10 years beginning with the date of grant.
(b) An initial BTA authorization shall be issued for a period of ten years from the date the Commission declared bidding closed in the MDS auction.
