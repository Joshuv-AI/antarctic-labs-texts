24 C.F.R. § 203.664 — Processing defaulted mortgages on property located on Indian land.
Citation: us:cfr:24:203.664
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-203.664
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 203 (Single Family Mortgage Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before a mortgagee requests that the Secretary accept assignment under § 203.350(b) of a mortgage insured pursuant to section 248 of the National Housing Act (§ 203.43h), the mortgagee must submit documents showing that the requirements of § 203.604 have been met.
