Correction (Authors' Contribution): Assessment of Bidirectional and Threshold-Response Associations Between Cognitive Function and Physical Performance: Nationwide Cross-Sectional Study
Authors: Huixiu Hu, Lanying Xie, Yuqing Hao, Yajie Zhao, Huanhuan Luo, Kang Yu, Chao Sun
Published: 2025

In “Assessment of Bidirectional and Threshold-Response Associations Between Cognitive Function and Physical Performance: Nationwide Cross-Sectional Study” [1], the authors noted one error in the author information.

In the original paper, a footnote in the author metadata to designate equal contribution was present for authors LX and YH.

The footnote has been added to author HH as well, indicating that HH, LX, and YH contributed equally.

The correction will appear in the online version of the paper on the JMIR Publications website, together with the publication of this correction notice. Because this was made after submission to PubMed, PubMed Central, and other full-text repositories, the corrected article has also been resubmitted to those repositories.
