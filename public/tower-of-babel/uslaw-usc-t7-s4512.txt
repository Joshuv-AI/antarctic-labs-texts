7 U.S.C. § 4512 — Administrative provisions
Citation: us:usc:7:4512
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section4512
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 76 (Dairy Research And Promotion); Subchapter I (Dairy Promotion Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Nothing in this subchapter may be construed to preempt or supersede any other program relating to dairy product promotion organized and operated under the laws of the United States or any State.
(b)
The provisions of this subchapter applicable to orders shall be applicable to amendments to orders.
