45 C.F.R. § 617.16 — Prohibition against intimidation or retaliation.
Citation: us:cfr:45:617.16
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-617.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 617 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From Nsf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient may not engage in acts of intimidation or retaliation against a person who:
(a) Attempts to assert a right protected by the Act, or
(b) Cooperates in any mediation, investigation, hearing or other part of NSF's investigation, conciliation, and enforcement process.
