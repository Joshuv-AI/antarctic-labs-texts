24 C.F.R. § 5.110 — Waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:24:5.110
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-5.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 5 (General Hud Program Requirements; Waivers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon determination of good cause, the Secretary may, subject to statutory limitations, waive any provision of this title and delegate this authority in accordance with section 106 of the Department of Housing and Urban Development Reform Act of 1989 (42 U.S.C. 3535(q)).
