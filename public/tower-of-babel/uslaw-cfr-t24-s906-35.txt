24 C.F.R. § 906.35 — Inapplicability of section 18 of the United States Housing Act of 1937.
Citation: us:cfr:24:906.35
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-906.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 906 (Public Housing Homeownership Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of section 18 of the 1937 Act (42 U.S.C. 1437p) do not apply to disposition of public housing dwelling units under a homeownership program approved by HUD under this part, or to the sale of a unit to a PRE to operate as public housing and sell to a low-income family within 5 years, under the requirements of § 906.19.
