20 C.F.R. § 1011.010 — Who is eligible to apply for a HIRE Vets Medallion Award?
Citation: us:cfr:20:1011.010
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Veterans' Employment And Training Service, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-1011.010
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 1011 (Hire Vets Medallion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All employers who employ at least one employee are eligible to apply for a HIRE Vets Medallion Award. To qualify for a HIRE Vets Medallion Award, an employer must satisfy all application requirements.
