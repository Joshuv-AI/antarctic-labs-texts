15 U.S.C. § 690n — Removal or suspension of directors or officers
Citation: us:usc:15:690n
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section690n
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 14B (Small Business Investment Program); Subchapter III (Investment Division Programs); Part C (Renewable Fuel Capital Investment Pilot Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Using the procedures for removing or suspending a director or an officer of a licensee set forth in section 687e of this title (to the extent such procedures are not inconsistent with the requirements of this part), the Administrator may remove or suspend any director or officer of any Renewable Fuel Capital Investment company.
