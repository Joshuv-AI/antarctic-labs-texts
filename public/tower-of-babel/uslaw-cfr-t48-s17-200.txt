48 C.F.R. § 17.200 — 17.200 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:17.200
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-17.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 17 (Special Contracting Methods). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes policies and procedures for the use of option solicitation provisions and contract clauses. Except as provided in agency regulations, this subpart does not apply to contracts for
(a) Services involving the construction, alteration, or repair (including dredging, excavating, and painting) of buildings, bridges, roads, or other kinds of real property;
(b) Architect-engineer services; and
(c) Research and development services.
