34 C.F.R. § 75.233 — Setting the amount of the grant.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.233
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.233
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Subject to any applicable matching or cost-sharing requirements, the Secretary may fund up to 100 percent of the allowable costs in the applicant's budget.
(b) In deciding what percentage of the allowable costs to fund, the Secretary may consider any other financial resources available to the applicant.
