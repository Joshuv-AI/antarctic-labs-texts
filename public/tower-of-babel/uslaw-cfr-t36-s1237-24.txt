36 C.F.R. § 1237.24 — What are special considerations for storage and maintenance of aerial photographic records?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1237.24
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1237.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1237 (Audiovisual, Cartographic, And Related Records Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Mark each aerial film container with a unique identification code to facilitate identification and filing.
(b) Mark aerial film indexes with the unique aerial film identification codes or container codes for the aerial film that they index. Also, file and mark the aerial indexes in such a way that they can easily be retrieved by area covered.
