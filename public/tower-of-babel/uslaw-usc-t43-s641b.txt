43 U.S.C. § 641b — Filing of application for quitclaim deeds
Citation: us:usc:43:641b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section641b
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 14 (Grants Of Desert Lands To States For Reclamation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall not issue such quitclaim deeds or patents to any State, however, unless that State files a proper application for the transfer of these lands within three years after August 13, 1954.
