14 U.S.C. § 952 — Construction of Coast Guard vessels and assignment of vessel projects
Citation: us:usc:14:952
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section952
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 9 (Administration); Subchapter II (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The assignment of Coast Guard vessel conversion, alteration, and repair projects shall be based on economic and military considerations and may not be restricted by a requirement that certain parts of Coast Guard shipwork be assigned to a particular type of shipyard or geographical area or by a similar requirement.
