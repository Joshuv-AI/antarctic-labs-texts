33 U.S.C. § 585 — Reserve component training at water resources development projects
Citation: us:usc:33:585
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section585
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 12 (River And Harbor Improvements Generally); Subchapter II (Pay And Allowances; Traveling Expenses And Subsistence). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
In carrying out military training activities or otherwise fulfilling military training requirements, units or members of a reserve component of the Armed Forces may perform services and furnish supplies in support of a water resources development project or program of the Corps of Engineers without reimbursement.
(b) Exception
This section shall not apply to any member of a reserve component of the Armed Forces who is employed by the Corps of Engineers on a full-time basis.
