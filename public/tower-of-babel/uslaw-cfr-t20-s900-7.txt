20 C.F.R. § 900.7 — Delegations of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:20:900.7
Creator: Joint Board For The Enrollment Of Actuaries
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-900.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 900 (Statement Of Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As occasion warrants, the Joint Board may delegate functions to the Chairman or the Executive Director, including the authority to receive applications and to give notice of actions. Any such delegation of authority is conferred by resolution of the Board.
