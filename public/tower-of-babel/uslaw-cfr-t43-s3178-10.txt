43 C.F.R. § 3178.10 — Facility and equipment ownership.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3178.10
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3178.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3170 (Onshore Oil And Gas Production). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The operator is not required to own or lease the equipment or facility that uses oil or gas royalty free. The operator is responsible for obtaining all authorizations, measuring production, reporting production, and all other applicable requirements.
