2 U.S.C. § 1866 — Certification of vouchers
Citation: us:usc:2:1866
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1866
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 28 (Architect Of The Capitol); Subchapter IV (Appropriations And Expenditures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall not be a duty of the Architect of the Capitol to certify any pay roll or other voucher covering any expenditure from any appropriation for the Senate Office Building, or for any other building or activity, unless the obligation involved was incurred by him or under his direction.
