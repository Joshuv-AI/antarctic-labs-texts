36 C.F.R. § 261.56 — Use of vehicles off National Forest System roads.
Citation: us:cfr:36:261.56
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-261.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 261 (Prohibitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When provided by an order, it is prohibited to possess or use a vehicle off National Forest System roads.
