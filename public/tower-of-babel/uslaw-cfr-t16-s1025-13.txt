16 C.F.R. § 1025.13 — Amendments and supplemental pleadings.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1025.13
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1025.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1025 (Rules Of Practice For Adjudicative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Presiding Officer may allow appropriate amendments and supplemental pleadings which do not unduly broaden the issues in the proceedings or cause undue delay.
