40 C.F.R. § 426.70 — Applicability; description of the automotive glass laminating subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:426.70
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-426.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 426 (Glass Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges of pollutants resulting from the processes which laminate a plastic sheet between two layers of glass, and which prepare the glass for lamination such as cutting, bending and washing, to produce automobile windshields.
