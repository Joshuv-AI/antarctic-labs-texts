16 U.S.C. § 470aaa–8 — Confidentiality
Citation: us:usc:16:470aaa–8
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section470aaa–8
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1C (Paleontological Resources Preservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Information concerning the nature and specific location of a paleontological resource shall be exempt from disclosure under section 552 of title 5 and any other law unless the Secretary determines that disclosure would—
(1)
further the purposes of this chapter;
(2)
not create risk of harm to or theft or destruction of the resource or the site containing the resource; and
(3)
be in accordance with other applicable laws.
