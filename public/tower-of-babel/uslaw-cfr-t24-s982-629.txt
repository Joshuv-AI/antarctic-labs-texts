24 C.F.R. § 982.629 — Homeownership option: Additional PHA requirements for family search and purchase.
Citation: us:cfr:24:982.629
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-982.629
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 982 (Section 8 Tenant-Based Assistance: Housing Choice Voucher Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The PHA may establish the maximum time for a family to locate a home, and to purchase the home.
(b) The PHA may require periodic family reports on the family's progress in finding and purchasing a home.
(c) If the family is unable to purchase a home within the maximum time established by the PHA, the PHA may issue the family a voucher to lease a unit or place the family's name on the waiting list for a voucher.
