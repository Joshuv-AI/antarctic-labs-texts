46 U.S.C. § 7307 — Able seamen—unlimited
Citation: us:usc:46:7307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section7307
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Merchant Seamen Licenses, Certificates, And Documents); Chapter 73 (Merchant Mariners’ Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The required service for the endorsement of able seaman—unlimited, qualified for unlimited service on a vessel on any waters, is at least 18 months 1 service on deck on board vessels operating at sea or on the Great Lakes.
