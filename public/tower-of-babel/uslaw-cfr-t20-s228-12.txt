20 C.F.R. § 228.12 — Computation of the tier I annuity component of a child's insurance annuity.
Citation: us:cfr:20:228.12
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-228.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 228 (Computation Of Survivor Annuities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The tier I annuity component of a child's insurance annuity is 75 percent of the PIA computed under § 228.10 of this part. The amount may be adjusted for the family maximum. See § 228.14 of this part.
