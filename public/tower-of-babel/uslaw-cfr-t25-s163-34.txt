25 C.F.R. § 163.34 — Environmental compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:25:163.34
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-163.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 163 (General Forestry Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Actions taken by the Secretary under the regulations in this part must comply with the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969, applicable Council on Environmental Quality Regulations, and tribal laws and regulations.
