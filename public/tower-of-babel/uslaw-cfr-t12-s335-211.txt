12 C.F.R. § 335.211 — Registration and reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:12:335.211
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-335.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 335 (Securities Of State Nonmember Banks And State Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons with securities subject to registration under Exchange Act sections 12(b) and 12(g), required to report under Exchange Act section 13, and subject to this part shall follow the applicable and currently effective SEC regulations issued under section 12(b) of the Exchange Act as codified at 17 CFR part 240.
