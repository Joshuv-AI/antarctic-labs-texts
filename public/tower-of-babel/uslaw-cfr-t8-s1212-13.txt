8 C.F.R. § 1212.13 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1212.13
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1212.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1212 (Documentary Requirements: Nonimmigrants; Waivers; Admission Of Certain Inadmissible Aliens; Parole). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of part 1212 are separate and severable from one another. In the event that any provision in part 1212 is stayed, enjoined, not implemented, or otherwise held invalid, the remaining provisions shall nevertheless be implemented as an independent rule and continue in effect.
