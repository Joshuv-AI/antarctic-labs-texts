Abstract
. Let H ⁡ ( 2 , 1 , t ¯ ) H(2,1;\underline{t}) be Hamiltonian superalgebras over 𝔽 \mathbb{F} , an algebraically closed field of prime characteristic p > 3 p>3 , which are non-restricted simple Lie superalgebras, generally. In this paper, we study generalized χ \chi -reduced simple modules over H ⁡ ( 2 , 1 , t ¯ ) H(2,1;\underline{t}) . We proved that all generalized χ \chi -reduced Kac modules of H ⁡ ( 2 , 1 , t ¯ ) H(2,1;\underline{t}) are simple with p p -characters χ \chi of height 0. Additionally, the isomorphism classes of these simple H ⁡ ( 2 , 1 , t ¯ ) H(2,1;\underline{t}) -modules are classified and their dimensions are determined.

2.1.
Generalized restricted structure of Hamiltonian superalgebras

Set
\underline{t}=(t_{1},t_{2})\in\mathbb{N}^{2}
and
\mathcal{O}(m;\underline{t})
be the divided power algebra of two variables
x_{1}
and
x_{2}
with
\mathbb{F}
-basis
\{x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}\mid 0\leq i_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,0\leq i_{2}\leq p^{t_{2}}-1\}
under the formul

x_{k}^{(j_{1})}x_{k}^{(j_{2})}=C_{j_{1}+j_{2}}^{j_{1}}x_{k}^{(j_{1}+j_{2})},\,\,\forall 0\leq j_{1},j_{2}\leq p^{t_{k}}-1,k=1,2.

Set
\Lambda(1)
be the Grassmann superalgebra of one variable
\xi
. Set
\Lambda(2,1;\underline{t})=\mathcal{O}(2;\underline{t})\otimes_{\mathbb{F}}\Lambda(1)
. Then
\Lambda(2,1;\underline{t})
has an
\mathbb{F}
-basis

\{x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}\xi^{j}\mid 0\leq i_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,0\leq i_{2}\leq p^{t_{2}}-1,j=0,1\}

and is
\mathbb{Z}_{2}
-graded with

\Lambda(2,1;\underline{t})_{\bar{0}}={\rm span}_{\mathbb{F}}\{x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}\mid 0\leq i_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,0\leq i_{2}\leq p^{t_{2}}-1\}

and

\Lambda(2,1;\underline{t})_{\bar{1}}={\rm span}_{\mathbb{F}}\{x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}\xi\mid 0\leq i_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,0\leq i_{2}\leq p^{t_{2}}-1\}.

Moreover,
\Lambda(2,1;\underline{t})
are associative superalgebras in which the multiplication is given by

\displaystyle x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}\xi^{j}\cdot x_{1}^{(s_{1})}x_{2}^{(s_{2})}\xi^{l}=C_{i_{1}+s_{1}}^{i_{1}}C_{i_{2}+s_{2}}^{i_{2}}x_{1}^{(i_{1}+s_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2}+s_{2})}\xi^{j+l},

\displaystyle 0\leq i_{1},s_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,0\leq i_{2},s_{2}\leq p^{t_{2}}-1,j,l\in\{0,1\}

with the convention that

\displaystyle x_{1}^{(k_{1})}x_{2}^{(k_{2})}\xi^{l}=0,\text{ for }k_{1}\geq p^{t_{1}}\text{ or }k_{2}\geq p^{t_{2}}\text{ or }l>1.

D_{1},D_{2},D_{3}
are special super-derivations of
\Lambda(2,1;\underline{t})
satisfying

\mathcal{D}_{k}(x^{(i)}\xi^{j})=x^{(i-1)}\mathcal{D}_{k}(x)\xi^{j}+jx^{(i)}\mathcal{D}_{k}(\xi)

for
0\leq i\leq p^{t_{1}+t_{2}}-1
,
j=0,1
and
k=1,2,3
.

Set

W(2,1;\underline{t})=\{\sum_{i=1}^{3}f_{i}D_{i}\mid f_{i}\in\Lambda(2,1;\underline{t})\}

and

\displaystyle i^{\prime}=\left\{\begin{matrix}2,&i=1,\\
1,&i=2,\\
3,&i=3,\end{matrix}\right.\qquad\sigma(i)=\left\{\begin{matrix}1,&i=1,\\
-1,&i=2,\\
1,&i=3,\end{matrix}\right.\qquad\tau(i)=\left\{\begin{matrix}\overline{0},&i=1,2,\\
\overline{1},&i=3.\end{matrix}\right.

Define a linear mapping
D_{H}:\Lambda(2,1;\underline{t})\longrightarrow W(2,1;\underline{t})
so that

D_{H}(f)=\sum_{i=1}^{3}f_{i}D_{i},\quad\forall f\in\Lambda(2,1;\underline{t}),

where

f_{i}=\sigma(i^{\prime})(-1)^{\tau(i^{\prime})d(f)}D_{i^{\prime}}(f),\quad i=1,2,3.

By direct calculation, we can know

\overline{H}(2,1;\underline{t})={\rm span}_{\mathbb{F}}\{D_{H}(f)\mid f\in\Lambda(2,1;\underline{t})\}.

D_{1}
,
D_{2}
and
D_{3}
are special super-derivations of
\Lambda(2,1;\underline{t})
.
An examination of the commutative subalgebra of
\overline{H}(2,1;\underline{t})
shows that

D_{H}(f)=\sum_{i=1}^{3}\sigma(i)(-1)^{\tau(i)d(f)}D_{i}(f)D_{i^{\prime}}.

It follows from Lemma 2.10 in
[
18
]
that

\displaystyle[D_{H}(f),D_{H}(g)]=

\displaystyle D_{H}(\sum_{i=1}^{3}\sigma(i)(-1)^{\tau(i)d(f)}D_{i}(f)D_{i^{\prime}}(g))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle D_{H}(D_{H}(f)(g)),\quad\forall f,g\in\Lambda(2,1;\underline{t}).

A straightforward calculation implies that

\displaystyle H(2,1;\underline{t})=

\displaystyle{\rm span}_{\mathbb{F}}\{D_{H}(f)\mid f\in\mathop{\oplus}\limits_{i=0}^{\xi-1}\Lambda(2,1;\underline{t})_{i}\}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\rm span}_{\mathbb{F}}\{D_{H}({x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}}\xi^{j})\mid 0\leq i_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,\,0\leq i_{2}\leq p^{t_{2}}-1,\,j=0,1,\,i_{1}+i_{2}+j\geq 1\}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\rm span}_{\mathbb{F}}\{x_{1}^{(i_{1}-1)}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}\xi^{j}D_{2}-x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2}-1)}\xi^{j}D_{1}-jx_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}D_{3}

\displaystyle\mid 0\leq i_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,\,0\leq i_{2}\leq p^{t_{2}}-1,\,j=0,1,\,i_{1}+i_{2}+j\geq 1\}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle H_{\bar{0}}\oplus H_{\bar{1}},

where

\displaystyle H_{\bar{0}}=

\displaystyle{\rm span}_{\mathbb{F}}\{D_{H}(x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})})\mid 0\leq i_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,\,0\leq i_{2}\leq p^{t_{2}}-1,\,i_{1}+i_{2}\geq 1\}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\rm span}_{\mathbb{F}}\{x_{1}^{(i_{1}-1)}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}D_{2}-x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2}-1)}D_{1}\mid 0\leq i_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,\,0\leq i_{2}\leq p^{t_{2}}-1,\,i_{1}+i_{2}\geq 1\}

and

\displaystyle H_{\bar{1}}=

\displaystyle{\rm span}_{\mathbb{F}}\{D_{H}(x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}\xi)\mid 0\leq i_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,\,0\leq i_{2}\leq p^{t_{2}}-1\}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle{\rm span}_{\mathbb{F}}\{x_{1}^{(i_{1}-1)}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}\xi D_{2}-x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2}-1)}\xi D_{1}-x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}D_{3}\mid 0\leq i_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,\,0\leq i_{2}\leq p^{t_{2}}-1\}.

It is a routine to check that
H(2,1;\underline{t})
are Lie subsuperalgebras of the derivation superalgebras of
\Lambda(2,1;\underline{t})
under the usual Lie bracket (
[

and referred to as Hamiltonian superalgebras, the even part of which contains a subalgebra isomorphic to the usual Hamiltonian algebras(

The following notion of a generalized restricted Lie superalgebra generalizes the notion of a restricted Lie superalgebra.

Definition 2.1
.

(
[
19
]
)

Let
L=L_{\bar{0}}\oplus L_{\bar{1}}
be a Lie superalgebra,
if the even part
L_{\bar{0}}
is a generalized restricted Lie algebra and the odd part
L_{\bar{1}}
as an adjoint
L_{\bar{0}}
-module is a generalized restricted module,i.e., there exists an ordered basis
E=\{e_{i}\mid i\in I\}
of
L_{\bar{0}}
, an non-negative
|I|
-tuple
\mathbf{s}=(s_{i})_{i\in I}
and a generalized restricted map
\varphi_{\mathrm{s}}:E\rightarrow L_{\bar{0}}
sending
e_{i}
to
e_{i}^{\varphi_{\mathbf{s}}}
such that
(\operatorname{ad}e_{i})^{p^{s_{i}}}(y)=(\operatorname{ad}e_{i}^{\varphi_{\mathbf{s}}})(y)
for all
y\in L
, then
L
is a generalized restricted Lie superalgebra (GR Lie superalgebra) associated with
E
and
\varphi_{\mathbf{s}}
.

Remark 2.2
.

(\mathrm{i})
One usually denotes the GR Lie superalgebra mentioned in
Definition
2.1

by
(L,E,\varphi_{\mathbf{s}})
to indicate the ordered basis
E
and the generalized restricted map
\varphi_{\mathbf{s}}
.

(\mathrm{ii})

Any restricted Lie superalgebra
(L,[p])
is a GR Lie superalgebra, where
\varphi_{\mathbf{s}}=\left.[p]\right|_{E}
and
\mathbf{s}=(s_{i})_{i\in I}
with
s_{i}=1,\forall i\in I
.

Next, we always assume
\mathfrak{g}=H(2,1,\underline{t})
are Hamiltonian superalgebras, We have the following conclusions.

Proposition 2.3
.

Hamiltonian superalgebras are GR Lie superalgebras associated with an ordered basis

\displaystyle\{

\displaystyle D_{H}(x_{1}),D_{H}(x_{2}),\cdots,D_{H}(x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}),\cdots,D_{H}(x_{1}^{(p^{t_{1}}-1)}),D_{H}(x_{2}^{(p^{t_{2}}-1)}),D_{H}(x_{1}^{(p^{t_{1}}-1)}x_{2}^{(p^{t_{2}}-1)})

\displaystyle\mid 0\leq i_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,\,0\leq i_{2}\leq p^{t_{2}}-1,\,i_{1}+i_{2}\geq 1\}

and generalized restricted map
\varphi_{\mathbf{s}}
that satisfy

\varphi_{\mathbf{s}}(D_{H}(x_{1}x_{2}))=D_{H}(x_{1}x_{2}),

\varphi_{\mathbf{s}}(D_{H}(x_{1}^{(j_{1})}x_{2}^{(j_{2})}))=0\,\,\text{ for }j_{1},j_{2}\neq 1\text{ at the same time},

where
\mathbf{s}=(t_{2},t_{1},1,1,\cdots,1)
.

Proof.

From
[
11
]
it follows that
\mathfrak{g}_{\bar{0}}
are GR Lie algebras. So we only need to show that

\displaystyle(\operatorname{ad}D_{H}(x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}))^{p^{s_{i}}}(y)=(\operatorname{ad}D_{H}(x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})})^{\varphi_{\mathbf{s}}})(y)

for all
y\in\mathfrak{g}_{\bar{1}},\,0\leq i_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,\,0\leq i_{2}\leq p^{t_{2}}-1.

Indeed, for one thing,

\displaystyle[\underbrace{D_{H}(x_{1}x_{2}),\cdots,[D_{H}(x_{1}x_{2})}_{p\,\text{times}},D_{H}(x_{1}^{(j_{1})}x_{2}^{(j_{2})}\xi)]\cdots]=(i_{2}-i_{1})^{p}D_{H}(x_{1}^{(j_{1})}x_{2}^{(j_{2})}\xi),

for another

\displaystyle[D_{H}(x_{1}x_{2}),D_{H}(x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}\xi)]=(i_{2}-i_{1})D_{H}(x_{1}^{(j_{1})}x_{2}^{(j_{2})}\xi).

Thus
\varphi_{\mathbf{s}}(D_{H}(x_{1}x_{2}))=D_{H}(x_{1}x_{2})
.
And on the one hand, for
j_{1}=1,\,j_{2}=0,\,0\leq i_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,\,0\leq i_{2}\leq p^{t_{2}}-1
,

\displaystyle[\underbrace{D_{H}(x_{1}),\cdots,[D_{H}(x_{1})}_{p^{t_{2}}\,\text{times}},D_{H}(x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}\xi)]\cdots]=

\displaystyle[\underbrace{D_{H}(x_{1}),\cdots,[D_{H}(x_{1})}_{p^{t_{2}-1}\,\text{times}},D_{H}(x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2}-1)}\xi)]\cdots]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\cdots=D_{H}(x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2}-p^{t_{2}})}\xi)=0,

for
j_{1}=0,\,j_{2}=1,\,0\leq i_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,\,0\leq i_{2}\leq p^{t_{2}}-1
,

\displaystyle[\underbrace{D_{H}(x_{2}),\cdots,[D_{H}(x_{2})}_{p^{t_{1}}\,\text{times}},D_{H}(x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}\xi)]\cdots]=

\displaystyle[\underbrace{D_{H}(x_{2}),\cdots,[D_{H}(x_{2})}_{p^{t_{1}-1}\,\text{times}},D_{H}(x_{1}^{(i_{1}-1)}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}\xi)]\cdots]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\cdots=D_{H}(x_{1}^{(i_{1}-p^{t_{1}})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}\xi)=0,

for
1\leq j_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,\,1\leq j_{2}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1\text{ with }j_{1},j_{2}\neq 1\text{ at the same time},\,0\leq i_{1}\leq p^{t_{1}}-1,\,0\leq i_{2}\leq p^{t_{2}}-1
,

\displaystyle[\underbrace{D_{H}(x_{1}^{(j_{1})}x_{2}^{(j_{2})}),\cdots,[D_{H}(x_{1}^{(j_{1})}x_{2}^{(j_{2})})}_{p\text{ times}},D_{H}(x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}\xi)]\cdots]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(C_{i_{1}+j_{1}-1}^{j_{1}-1}C_{i_{2}+j_{2}-1}^{j_{2}}-C_{i_{1}+j_{1}-1}^{j_{1}}C_{i_{2}+j_{2}-1}^{j_{2}-1})(C_{i_{1}+2j_{1}-2}^{j_{1}-1}C_{i_{2}+2j_{2}-2}^{j_{2}}-C_{i_{1}+2j_{1}-2}^{j_{1}}C_{i_{2}+2j_{2}-2}^{j_{2}-1})

\displaystyle\cdots(C_{i_{1}+kj_{1}-k}^{j_{1}-1}C_{i_{2}+kj_{2}-k}^{j_{2}}-C_{i_{1}+kj_{1}-k}^{j_{1}}C_{i_{2}+kj_{2}-k}^{j_{2}-1})\cdots(C_{i_{1}+pj_{1}-p}^{j_{1}-1}C_{i_{2}+pj_{2}-p}^{j_{2}}-C_{i_{1}+pj_{1}-p}^{j_{1}}C_{i_{2}+pj_{2}-p}^{j_{2}-1})

\displaystyle D_{H}(x_{1}^{(i_{1}+pj_{1}-p)}x_{2}^{(i_{2}+pj_{2}-p)}\xi)=0,

on the other we have

[0,D_{H}(x_{1}^{(i_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2})}\xi)]=0.

Consequently,
\varphi_{\mathbf{s}}(D_{H}(x_{1}^{(j_{1})}x_{2}^{(j_{2})}))=0\,\,\text{ for }j_{1},j_{2}\neq 1\text{ at the same time},

∎

Lemma 2.5

The zero graded component

\displaystyle\longmapsto h

\displaystyle x_{1}D_{2}

\displaystyle\longmapsto f

\displaystyle x_{2}D_{1}

\displaystyle\longmapsto e

Subcase 2.2 :

\displaystyle w=

\displaystyle D_{3}\cdot w=\sum_{j=0}^{N}e_{i_{1},i_{2},j,k}D_{1}^{i_{1}+j}D_{2}^{i_{2}+j}D_{3}\otimes v_{\lambda,k,0}\in M.

Then

\displaystyle(x_{1}^{(i_{1}+N-1)}x_{2}^{(i_{2}+N)}\xi D_{2}-x_{1}^{(i_{1}+N)}x_{2}^{(i_{2}+N-1)}\xi D_{1}-x_{1}^{(i_{1}+N)}x_{2}^{(i_{2}+N)}D_{3})\cdot\sum_{j=0}^{N}c_{i_{1},i_{2},j,k}D_{1}^{i_{1}+j}D_{2}^{i_{2}+j}D_{3}\otimes v_{\lambda,k,1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{N}(-1)^{l_{1}+l_{2}}e_{i_{1},i_{2},j,k}C_{i_{1}+j}^{l_{1}}C_{i_{2}+j}^{l_{2}}D_{1}^{i_{1}+j-l_{1}}D_{2}^{i_{2}+j-l_{2}}(x_{1}^{(i_{1}+N-l_{1}-1)}x_{2}^{(i_{2}+N-l_{2})}\xi D_{2}

\displaystyle-x_{1}^{(i_{1}+N-l_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2}+N-l_{2}-1)}\xi D_{1}-x_{1}^{(i_{1}+N-l_{1})}x_{2}^{(i_{2}+N-l_{2})}D_{3})D_{3}\otimes v_{\lambda,k,1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(-1)^{i_{1}+i_{2}}(\lambda-2k-1)(-1)^{i_{1}+i_{2}}e_{i_{1},i_{2},N-1,k}(1\otimes v_{\lambda,k,1}+D_{3}\otimes v_{\lambda,k,1})

\displaystyle+(-1)^{i_{1}+i_{2}}(\lambda-2k-1)(e_{i_{1},i_{2},N,k}(i_{1}+N)(i_{2}+N)+i_{2}-i_{1})D_{1}D_{2}\otimes v_{\lambda,k,1}

\displaystyle+(-1)^{i_{1}+i_{2}}(\lambda-2k-1)e_{i_{1},i_{2},N,k}(i_{1}+N)(i_{2}+N)D_{1}D_{2}D_{3}\otimes v_{\lambda,k,1}

\displaystyle+(-1)^{i_{1}+i_{2}}e_{i_{1},i_{2},N,k}((i_{1}+N)D_{1}D_{3}\otimes v_{\lambda,k+1,0}-(\lambda-k)(i_{2}+N)D_{2}D_{3}\otimes v_{\lambda,k,0})

\displaystyle+(-1)^{i_{1}+i_{2}}e_{i_{1},i_{2},N,k}(C_{i_{1}+N}^{2}D_{1}^{2}\otimes v_{\lambda,k+1,1}-k(\lambda-k)C_{i_{2}+N}^{2}D_{2}^{2}\otimes v_{\lambda,k-1,1})\in M.

Subcase 2.2.1 :
