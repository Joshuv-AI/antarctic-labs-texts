51 U.S.C. § 70701 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:51:70701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section70701
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 707 (Human Space Flight Independent Investigation Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1) Commission.—
The term “Commission” means a Commission established under this chapter.
(2) Incident.—
The term “incident” means either an accident or a deliberate act.
