12 C.F.R. § 225.196 — Information to be presented to the Appraisal Subcommittee by participating States.
Citation: us:cfr:12:225.196
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-225.196
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 225 (Bank Holding Companies And Change In Bank Control (Regulation Y)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each State electing to register AMCs for purposes of permitting AMCs to provide appraisal management services relating to covered transactions in the State must submit to the Appraisal Subcommittee the information required to be submitted by Appraisal Subcommittee regulations or guidance concerning AMCs that operate in the State.
