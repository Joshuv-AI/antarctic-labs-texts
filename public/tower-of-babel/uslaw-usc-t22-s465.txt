22 U.S.C. § 465 — Detention of vessels
Citation: us:usc:22:465
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section465
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 9 (Foreign Wars, War Materials, And Neutrality); Subchapter III (Prevention Of Offenses Against Neutrality). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may employ such part of the land or naval forces of the United States as he may deem necessary to carry out the purposes of sections 462 and 465 of this title, and sections 756, 960, 963 to 967, and 3058 of title 18.
