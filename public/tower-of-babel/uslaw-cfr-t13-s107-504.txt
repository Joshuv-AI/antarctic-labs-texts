13 C.F.R. § 107.504 — Equipment and office requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:13:107.504
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-107.504
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 107 (Small Business Investment Companies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Technology. You must have access to technology to securely send and receive emails, scan documents, and prepare and submit electronic information and reports required by SBA.
(b) Accessible office. You must maintain an office that is open to the public during normal working hours.
