36 C.F.R. § 1201.7 — Why does NARA have to collect debts?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1201.7
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1201.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1201 (Collection Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Federal agencies are required to try to collect claims of the Federal Government for money, funds, or property arising out of the agency's activities.
