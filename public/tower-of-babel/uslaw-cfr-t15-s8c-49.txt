15 C.F.R. § 8c.49 — c.49 Program accessibility: Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:15:8c.49
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-8c.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 8c (PART 8c—ENFORCEMENT OF NONDISCRIMINATION ON THE BASIS OF HANDICAP IN PROGRAMS OR ACTIVITIES CONDUCTED BY THE DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in § 8c.50, no qualified individual with handicaps shall, because the agency's facilities are inaccessible to or unusable by individuals with handicaps, be denied the benefits of, be excluded from participation in, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity conducted by the agency.
