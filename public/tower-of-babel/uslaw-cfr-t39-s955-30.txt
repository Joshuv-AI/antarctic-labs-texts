39 C.F.R. § 955.30 — (Rule 30) Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:39:955.30
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-955.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 955 (Rules Of Practice Before The Postal Service Board Of Contract Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part governs proceedings in all appeals docketed by the Board on or after October 1, 2025, and to appeals filed before that date, unless doing so is inequitable or unfair.
