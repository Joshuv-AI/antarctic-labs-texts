17 C.F.R. § 38.700 — Core Principle 13.
Citation: us:cfr:17:38.700
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-38.700
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 38 (Designated Contract Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The board of trade shall establish and enforce disciplinary procedures that authorize the board of trade to discipline, suspend, or expel members or market participants that violate the rules of the board of trade, or similar methods for performing the same functions, including delegation of the functions to third parties.
