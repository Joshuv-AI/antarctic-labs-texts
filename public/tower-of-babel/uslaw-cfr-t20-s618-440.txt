20 C.F.R. § 618.440 — Applying for a relocation allowance.
Citation: us:cfr:20:618.440
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-618.440
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 618 (Trade Adjustment Assistance Under The Trade Act Of 1974, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Forms. To receive a relocation allowance, an AAW must apply to the State using the State's process.
(b) Submittal. An AAW must apply for a relocation allowance and the State must approve the worker for a relocation allowance before the relocation begins. The State must make a timely determination on a relocation application submitted to allow the worker to promptly begin the relocation.
