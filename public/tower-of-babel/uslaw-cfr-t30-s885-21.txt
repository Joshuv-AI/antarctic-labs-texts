30 C.F.R. § 885.21 — What happens if I do not comply with applicable Federal law or the terms of my grant?
Citation: us:cfr:30:885.21
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-885.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 885 (Grants For Certified States And Indian Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you or your subgrantee materially fails to comply with an award, a reclamation plan, or a Federal statute or regulation, including statutes relating to nondiscrimination, we may take appropriate remedial actions. Enforcement actions and procedures must follow 43 CFR part 12.
