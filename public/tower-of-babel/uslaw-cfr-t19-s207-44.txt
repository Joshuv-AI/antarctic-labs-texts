19 C.F.R. § 207.44 — Consolidation of investigations.
Citation: us:cfr:19:207.44
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-207.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 207 (Investigations Of Whether Injury To Domestic Industries Results From Imports Sold At Less Than Fair Value Or From Subsidized Exports To The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may, when appropriate, consolidate continued investigations under section 704(g) or section 734(g) of the Act with investigations to review agreements for the elimination of injury under section 704(h) or section 734(h) of the Act.
