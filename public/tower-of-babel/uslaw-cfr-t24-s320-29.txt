24 C.F.R. § 320.29 — Guaranty.
Citation: us:cfr:24:320.29
Creator: Government National Mortgage Association, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-320.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 320 (Guaranty Of Mortgage-Backed Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

With respect to bond-type securities, the Association will guarantee the timely payment of principal of and interest on such securities, subject to the terms and conditions of the securities. The Association's guaranty is backed by the full faith and credit of the United States.
