11 U.S.C. § 1531 — Presumption of insolvency based on recognition of a foreign main proceeding
Citation: us:usc:11:1531
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1531
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 15 (Ancillary And Other Cross-Border Cases); Subchapter V (Concurrent Proceedings). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the absence of evidence to the contrary, recognition of a foreign main proceeding is, for the purpose of commencing a proceeding under section 303, proof that the debtor is generally not paying its debts as such debts become due.
