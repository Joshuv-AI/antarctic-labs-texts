5 U.S.C. § 3107 — Employment of publicity experts; restrictions
Citation: us:usc:5:3107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section3107
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart B (Employment And Retention); Chapter 31 (Authority For Employment); Subchapter I (Employment Authorities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Appropriated funds may not be used to pay a publicity expert unless specifically appropriated for that purpose.
