5 C.F.R. § 1310.1 — Policy guidelines.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1310.1
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1310.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1310 (Omb Circulars). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In carrying out its responsibilities, the Office of Management and Budget issues policy guidelines to Federal agencies to promote efficiency and uniformity in Government activities. These guidelines are normally in the form of circulars.
