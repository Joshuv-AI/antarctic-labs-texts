16 U.S.C. § 460gg–10 — Ranching, grazing, etc., as valid uses of area
Citation: us:usc:16:460gg–10
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460gg–10
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XCI (Hells Canyon National Recreation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Ranching, grazing, farming, timber harvesting, and the occupation of homes and lands associated therewith, as they exist on December 31, 1975, are recognized as traditional and valid uses of the recreation area.
