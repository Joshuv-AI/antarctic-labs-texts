30 C.F.R. § 1290.103 — Who may file an appeal?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1290.103
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1290.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1290 (Appeal Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If you receive an order that adversely affects you or your lessee, you may appeal that order except as provided under § 1290.104.
(b) If you are a lessee and you receive a Notice of Order, and if you contest the order, you may either appeal the order or join in your designee's appeal under § 1290.106.
