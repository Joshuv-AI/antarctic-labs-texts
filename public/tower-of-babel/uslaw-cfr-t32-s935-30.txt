32 C.F.R. § 935.30 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:32:935.30
Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-935.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 935 (Wake Island Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to any act made criminal in this part, any act committed on Wake Island that would be criminal if committed on board a merchant vessel or other vessel belonging to the United States is a criminal offense and shall be adjudged and punished according to the laws applicable on board those vessels on the high seas.
