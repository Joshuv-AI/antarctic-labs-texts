46 U.S.C. § 55120 — Transshipment of imported merchandise intended for immediate exportation
Citation: us:usc:46:55120
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section55120
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part D (Promotional Programs); Chapter 551 (Coastwise Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Homeland Security may prescribe regulations for the transshipment and transportation of merchandise that is imported into the United States by sea for immediate exportation to a foreign port by sea, or by a river, the right to ascend or descend which for the purposes of commerce is secured by treaty to the citizens of the United States and the subjects of a foreign power.
