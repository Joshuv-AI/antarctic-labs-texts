42 C.F.R. § 136.204 — Authorization by an urban Indian organization.
Citation: us:cfr:42:136.204
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-136.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 136 (Indian Health). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An urban Indian organization may authorize for purchase items and services for an eligible urban Indian as those terms are defined in 25 U.S.C. 1603(f) and (h) according to section 503 of the IHCIA and applicable regulations. Services and items furnished by physicians and other health care professionals and non-hospital-based entities shall be subject to the payment methodology set forth in § 136.203.
