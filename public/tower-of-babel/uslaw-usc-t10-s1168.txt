10 U.S.C. § 1168 — Discharge or release from active duty: limitations
Citation: us:usc:10:1168
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1168
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 59 (Separation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
A member of an armed force may not be discharged or released from active duty until his discharge certificate or certificate of release from active duty, respectively, and his final pay or a substantial part of that pay, are ready for delivery to him or his next of kin or legal representative.
(b)
This section does not prevent the immediate transfer of a member to a facility of the Department of Veterans Affairs for necessary hospital care.
