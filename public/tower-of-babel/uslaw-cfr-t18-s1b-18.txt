18 C.F.R. § 1b.18 — b.18 Right to submit statements.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1b.18
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1b.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1b (PART 1b—RULES RELATING TO INVESTIGATIONS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person may, at any time during the course of an investigation, submit documents, statements of facts or memoranda of law for the purpose of explaining said person's position or furnishing evidence which said person considers relevant regarding the matters under investigation.
