31 C.F.R. § 50.2 — Responsible office.
Citation: us:cfr:31:50.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-50.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 50 (Terrorism Risk Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The office responsible for the administration of the Terrorism Risk Insurance Act in the Department of the Treasury is the Terrorism Risk Insurance Program Office within the Federal Insurance Office. The Treasury Assistant Secretary for Financial Institutions prescribes the regulations under the Act.
