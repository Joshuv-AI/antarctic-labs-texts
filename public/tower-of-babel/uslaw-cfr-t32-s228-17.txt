32 C.F.R. § 228.17 — Distribution of unauthorized materials.
Citation: us:cfr:32:228.17
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-228.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 228 (Security Protective Force). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Distributing, posting or affixing materials, such as pamphlets, handbills, or flyers, on protected property is prohibited, except as provided by § 228.16, as authorized by the NSA Director of Security or his designee at each Agency facility, or when conducted as part of authorized Government activities.
