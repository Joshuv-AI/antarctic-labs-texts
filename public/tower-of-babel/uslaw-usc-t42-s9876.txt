42 U.S.C. § 9876 — Report
Citation: us:usc:42:9876
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9876
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 105 (Community Services Programs); Subchapter IV (Grants To States For Planning And Development Of Dependent Care Programs And For Other Purposes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Within three years after October 30, 1984, the Secretary shall prepare and transmit to the Senate Committee on Labor and Human Resources and the House Committee on Education and Labor a report concerning the activities conducted by the States with amounts provided under this subchapter.
