Influence of photon inverse emission on forward–backward asymmetry in dilepton production at LHC
V. A. Zykunov

Abstract
Contribution of photon inverse emission to the dilepton production process in hadron collisions for Large Hadron Collider (LHC) experimental program is calculated in details. Numerical analysis of inverse emission effects on observable quantities (cross sections and forward-backward asymmetry) is performed in a wide kinematical region including the CMS LHC experiment in Run3/HL regime which corresponds to ultra-high energies and dilepton invariant masses. Effective technics of additive relative corrections is applied for analysis of the influence of different radiative contributions on forward–backward asymmetry.

Detection of NP traces is one of the main perspectives for the development of modern physics.

The Drell–Yan process

is produced due to another mechanism. One of such options is the production of a dilepton in photon fusion,
another (but not last) is the boson (gluon or photon) inverse emission production.

Most probably, NP will manifest itself in LHC experiments not via direct detection of new particles
and/or new phenomena but in the form of minor deviations from SM predictions.
The discovery of new physical phenomena in such a scenario is only possible
by an extremely precise comparison of the experimental data with theoretical predictions.
This requires the corresponding increasing of the accuracy of the theoretical description
of the microworld processes under study and provision of experiments
to be conducted at Run3/HL LHC with reliable and precision programs
for calculating effects related to radiative corrections (RCs).
Currently, a large number of various and mutually complementing programs and computer codes are

This paper also presents the physical content of one of these programs,

author for estimating electroweak and QCD corrections to the Drell–Yan process.

The contribution of photon inverse emission mechanism gives in the same order of perturbation theory as
electroweak RCs to Drell–Yan process or two photon fusion mechanism.
So, the goal of present paper is the detailed calculation of photon inverse emission contribution

Conclusion

The observed forward–backward asymmetry in the production of dileptons in hadron collisions via the
photon inverse emission channel has been examined with an accuracy and in detailes.
The theoretically obtained results should be considered in the future experimental program of
CMS LHC, which will be focused on ultrahigh energies and invariant masses of a dilepton
(at least for the region
M>4
TeV)
that correspond to the regime of Run3/HL LHC, since they turned out to be on the level
of expected statistical and systematic [experimental] errors.
