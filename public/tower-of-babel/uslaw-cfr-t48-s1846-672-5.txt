48 C.F.R. § 1846.672-5 — 1846.672-5 Packing list instructions.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1846.672-5
Creator: Chapter 18—National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1846.672-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1846 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Copies of the MIRR may be used as a packing list. The packing list copies shall be in addition to the copies of the MIRR required for distribution (see 1846.673) and shall be marked “PACKING LIST”.
