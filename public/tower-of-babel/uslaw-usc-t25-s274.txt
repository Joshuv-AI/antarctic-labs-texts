25 U.S.C. § 274 — Employment of Indian girls and boys as assist­ants
Citation: us:usc:25:274
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section274
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 7 (Education Of Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commissioner of Indian Affairs shall employ Indian girls as assistant matrons and Indian boys as farmers and industrial teachers in all Indian schools when it is practicable to do so.
