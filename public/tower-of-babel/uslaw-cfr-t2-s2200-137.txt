2 C.F.R. § 2200.137 — Who in the Corporation for National and Community Service may grant an exception to let an excluded person participate in a covered transaction?
Citation: us:cfr:2:2200.137
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2200.137
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2200 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chief Executive Officer (or another official designated by the Chief Executive Officer) has the authority to grant an exception to let an excluded person participate in a covered transaction, as provided in the OMB guidance at 2 CFR 180.135.
