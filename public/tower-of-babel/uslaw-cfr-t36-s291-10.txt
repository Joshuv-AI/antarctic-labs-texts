36 C.F.R. § 291.10 — Collecting.
Citation: us:cfr:36:291.10
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-291.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 291 (Paleontological Resources Preservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A paleontological resource may only be collected from National Forest System lands in accordance with the casual collecting provisions in §§ 291.11 and 291.12, or in accordance with a permit issued by the Authorized Officer as identified in § 291.13.
