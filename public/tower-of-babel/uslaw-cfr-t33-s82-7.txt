33 C.F.R. § 82.7 — Sidelights for unmanned barges.
Citation: us:cfr:33:82.7
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-82.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 82 (72 Colregs: Interpretative Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An unmanned barge being towed may use the exception of COLREGS Rule 24(h). However, this exception only applies to the vertical sector requirements.
