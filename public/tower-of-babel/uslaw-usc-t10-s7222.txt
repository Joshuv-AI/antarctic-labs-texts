10 U.S.C. § 7222 — Rank: commissioned officers serving under temporary appointments
Citation: us:usc:10:7222
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7222
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 725 (Rank And Command). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may, in accordance with the needs of the Army, adjust dates of rank of commissioned officers of the Army serving in temporary grades.
