36 C.F.R. § 52.41 — Does the VEIA expire?
Citation: us:cfr:36:52.41
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-52.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 52 (Visitor Experience Improvements Authority Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The Director may not award a contract under the VEIA after December 16, 2025, unless extended by law. However, contracts awarded under the VEIA may continue beyond such date, subject to the terms of the particular contract.
