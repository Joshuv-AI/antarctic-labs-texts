27 C.F.R. § 26.71 — Termination of bonds.
Citation: us:cfr:27:26.71
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-26.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 26 (Liquors And Articles From Puerto Rico And The Virgin Islands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any bond given under the provisions of this subpart may be terminated as to future transactions—
(a) Pursuant to application of surety as provided in § 26.72;
(b) On approval of a superseding bond;
(c) On notification by the principal to the appropriate TTB officer that he has discontinued transactions under the bond (including for the reason that the principal is exempt from bond requirements under §§ 26.66(c), 26.67(b), or 26.68(b)); or
(d) On notification by the principal to the appropriate TTB officer that he has discontinued business.
