7 U.S.C. § 2544 — Research exemption
Citation: us:usc:7:2544
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2544
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 57 (Plant Variety Protection); Subchapter III (Plant Variety Protection And Rights); Part K (Infringement Of Plant Variety Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The use and reproduction of a protected variety for plant breeding or other bona fide research shall not constitute an infringement of the protection provided under this chapter.
