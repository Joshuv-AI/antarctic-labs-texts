14 C.F.R. § 211.13 — Amendments to applications.
Citation: us:cfr:14:211.13
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-211.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 211 (Applications For Permits To Foreign Air Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant shall submit any information required by this part that is omitted from the original application, or any additional information, as an amendment to the original application. Applicants shall consecutively number amendments to applications and shall comply with the requirements of this subpart.
