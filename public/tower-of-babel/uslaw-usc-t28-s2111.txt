28 U.S.C. § 2111 — Harmless error
Citation: us:usc:28:2111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2111
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 133 (Review—Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On the hearing of any appeal or writ of certiorari in any case, the court shall give judgment after an examination of the record without regard to errors or defects which do not affect the substantial rights of the parties.
