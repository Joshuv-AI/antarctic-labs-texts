7 C.F.R. § 1405.4 — Delegations of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1405.4
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1405.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1405 (Loans, Purchases, And Other Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The delegations of authority relating to the CCC programs and activities are set forth in the by-laws of CCC and in dockets approved by the CCC Board of Directors. Copies of the By-laws and the dockets may be obtained from the Secretary of CCC.
