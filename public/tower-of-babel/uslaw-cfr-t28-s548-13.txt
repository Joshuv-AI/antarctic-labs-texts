28 C.F.R. § 548.13 — Schedules and facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:28:548.13
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-548.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 548 (Religious Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Under the general supervision of the Warden, chaplains shall schedule and direct the institution's religious activities.
(b) The Warden may relieve an inmate from an institution program or assignment if a religious activity is also scheduled at that time.
(c) Institutions shall have space designated for the conduct of religious activities.
