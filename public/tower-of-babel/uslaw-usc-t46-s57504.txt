46 U.S.C. § 57504 — Charter or sale of vessels acquired by Department of Transportation
Citation: us:usc:46:57504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section57504
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Government-Owned Merchant Vessels); Chapter 575 (Construction, Charter, And Sale Of Vessels); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Vessels transferred to or otherwise acquired by the Department of Transportation in any manner may be chartered or sold by the Secretary of Transportation as provided in this chapter.
