7 C.F.R. § 52.3182 — Varietal types of dried prunes.
Citation: us:cfr:7:52.3182
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-52.3182
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 52 (Processed Fruits And Vegetables, Processed Products Thereof, And Certain Other Processed Food Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Type I. French; or Robe; or a mixture of French and Robe.
(b) Type II. Italian.
(c) Type III. Imperial; or Sugar; or a mixture of Imperial and Sugar.
(d) Type IV. Any other types; or mixtures of any types other than mixtures in Type I and Type III of this section.
