13 C.F.R. § 307.6 — Revolving Loan Funds established for lending.
Citation: us:cfr:13:307.6
Creator: Economic Development Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-307.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 307 (Economic Adjustment Assistance Investments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Economic Adjustment Assistance Grants to capitalize or recapitalize RLFs most commonly fund business lending, but also may fund public infrastructure or other authorized lending activities. The requirements in this subpart apply to EDA-funded RLFs. Special award conditions may contain appropriate modifications of these requirements.
