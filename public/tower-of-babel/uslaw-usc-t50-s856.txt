50 U.S.C. § 856 — Continuing offense
Citation: us:usc:50:856
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section856
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 23 (Internal Security); Subchapter V (Registration Of Certain Persons Trained In Foreign Espionage Systems). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Failure to file a registration statement as required by this subchapter is a continuing offense for as long as such failure exists, notwithstanding any statute of limitation or other statute to the contrary.
