EXPORT-IMPORT BANK OF THE UNITED STATES


Notice of Open Meeting for the 2026-2027 EXIM Advisory and Sub-
Saharan Africa Advisory Committees

    Time and Date: Friday, March 20th, 2026 from 1:00 p.m.-2:00 p.m. 
ET.
    Place: Microsoft Teams: The joint meeting will be held virtually 
for the committees, EXIM's Board of Directors, support staff, and all 
other participants.
    Registration and Public Comment: Virtual Public Participation: The

allotted for questions or comments submitted online. Members of the 
public may also file written statements before or after the meeting to
