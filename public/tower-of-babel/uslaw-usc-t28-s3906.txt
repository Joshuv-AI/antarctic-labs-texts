28 U.S.C. § 3906 — Payments
Citation: us:usc:28:3906
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section3906
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 179 (Judicial Review Of Certain Actions By Presidential Offices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A judgment, award, or compromise settlement against the United States under this chapter (including any interest and costs) shall be paid—
(1)
under section 1304 of title 31, if it arises out of an action commenced in a district court of the United States (or any appeal therefrom); or
(2)
out of amounts otherwise appropriated or available to the office involved, if it arises out of an appeal from an administrative proceeding under chapter 5 of title 3.
