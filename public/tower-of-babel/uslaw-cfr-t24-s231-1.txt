24 C.F.R. § 231.1 — Eligibility requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:24:231.1
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-231.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 231 (Housing Mortgage Insurance For The Elderly). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements set forth in 24 CFR part 200, subpart A, apply to multifamily project mortgages insured under section 231 of the National Housing Act (12 U.S.C. 1715v), as amended.
