30 C.F.R. § 33.22 — Mode of use.
Citation: us:cfr:30:33.22
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-33.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 33 (Dust Collectors For Use In Connection With Rock Drilling In Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A unit or system may be designed for use in connection with percussion and/or rotary drilling in any combination of the following drilling positions: (1) Vertically upward, (2) upward at angles to the vertical, (3) horizontally, and (4) downward.
(b) Dust-collector units may be designed for use with specific drilling equipment or at specific drilling speeds.
