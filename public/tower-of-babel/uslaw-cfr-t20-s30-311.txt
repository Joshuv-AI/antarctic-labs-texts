20 C.F.R. § 30.311 — What happens if the claimant does not object to the recommended decision or request a hearing within 60 days?
Citation: us:cfr:20:30.311
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-30.311
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 30 (Claims For Compensation Under The Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act Of 2000, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If the claimant does not file a written statement that objects to the recommended decision and/or requests a hearing within the period of time allotted in § 30.310, the FAB may issue a final decision accepting the recommendation of the district office as provided in § 30.316.
(b) If the recommended decision accepts all or part of a claim for compensation, the FAB may issue a final decision at any time after receiving written notice from the claimant that he or she waives any objection to all or part of the recommended decision.
