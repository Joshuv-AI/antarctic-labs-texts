10 U.S.C. § 4236 — Negotiation of price for technical data before development, production, or sustainment of major weapon systems
Citation: us:usc:10:4236
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section4236
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart F (Major Systems, Major Defense Acquisition Programs, And Weapon Systems Development); Chapter 322 (Major Systems And Major Defense Acquisition Programs Generally); Subchapter II (Contracting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense shall ensure, to the maximum extent practicable, that the Department of Defense, before selecting a contractor for the engineering and manufacturing development of a major weapon system, production of a major weapon system, or sustainment of a major weapon system, negotiates a price for technical data to be delivered under a contract for such development, production, or sustainment.
