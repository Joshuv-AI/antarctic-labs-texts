15 U.S.C. § 1214 — “Interstate commerce” defined
Citation: us:usc:15:1214
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1214
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 26 (Household Refrigerators). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, the term “interstate commerce” includes commerce between one State, Territory, possession, the District of Columbia, or the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico and another State, Territory, possession, the District of Columbia, or the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico.
