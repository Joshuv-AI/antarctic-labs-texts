46 C.F.R. § 112.30-5 — -5 Transfer of emergency loads.
Citation: us:cfr:46:112.30-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-112.30-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 112 (Emergency Lighting And Power Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If there is a reduction of potential of the normal source by 15 to 40 percent, the emergency loads under § 112.15-5 must transfer automatically from the normal source to the emergency power source.
