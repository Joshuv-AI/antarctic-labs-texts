40 C.F.R. § 47.110 — Eligible applicants.
Citation: us:cfr:40:47.110
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-47.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 47 (National Environmental Education Act Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any local education agency (including any tribal education agency), college or university, State education agency or environmental agency, not-for-profit organization, or noncommercial educational broadcasting entity may submit an application to the Administrator in response to the solicitations described in § 47.120.
