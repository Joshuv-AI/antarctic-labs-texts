14 C.F.R. § 385.8 — Exercise of authority in “acting” capacity.
Citation: us:cfr:14:385.8
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-385.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 385 (Staff Assignments And Review Of Action Under Assignments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless the assignment provides otherwise, staff members serving in an “acting” capacity may exercise the authority assigned to the staff members for whom they are acting.
