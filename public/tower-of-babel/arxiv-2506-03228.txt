A simple mechanism for unstable degeneracies in local Hamiltonians
José Garre Rubio

Abstract
If a local Hamiltonian eigenstate is mapped to another state by local operators commuting with the Hamiltonian terms, the latter is also an eigenstate. This basic observation implies a no-go result for being a unique ground state, short-range entangled and protected against local perturbations.



Identifying the mechanisms that stabilize the ground space of a local, gapped Hamiltonian is a central question in quantum many-body physics, with known examples including global symmetries
Landau and Lifschitz 1980
and topological order
Kitaev 2003
. Understanding unstable degeneracies, in turn, may offer insights into stable quantum phases of matter. Recently, Ref.
Gioia and Thorngren 2023
showed that the W-state cannot be the unique ground state of a local Hamiltonian, and that such degeneracy is inherently unstable. Motivated by this, we propose a simple mechanism that enforces degenerate yet unstable energy levels, reproducing the W-state result in the ground space of frustration-free Hamiltonians.

Let us consider a state
|\psi\rangle
that is mapped to
|\phi\rangle
by a set of finite-range operators
\{O_{i}\}_{i\in\mathcal{I}}

1

The indices
i,j
denotes where the operator is centered
:

O_{i}|\psi\rangle=|\phi\rangle,\ {\rm for\ all}\ i\in\mathcal{I}\ .

(1)

Suppose that
|\psi\rangle
is an eigenvector of each term in a finite-range Hamiltonian
H=\sum h_{j}
, i.e.,
h_{j}|\psi\rangle=e_{j}|\psi\rangle
for all
j
, and that for each
h_{j}
there exists an operator
O_{i}
such that
[h_{j},O_{i}]=0
. Then,
|\phi\rangle
is also an eigenvector of
h_{j}
with the same eigenvalue
e_{j}
, since

h_{j}|\phi\rangle=h_{j}O_{i}|\psi\rangle=O_{i}h_{j}|\psi\rangle=e_{j}O_{i}|\psi\rangle=e_{j}|\phi\rangle
.

Some comments are in order:
(1) Since we assume that the operators
O_{i}
are local, this excludes global symmetries and topologically ordered states.
(2) The map from
|\psi\rangle
to
|\phi\rangle
can be one-way local, since the operators
O_{i}
may be non-invertible. So going from
|\phi\rangle
to
|\psi\rangle
might require non-local (i.e., non-LOCC) operations.
(3) For frustration-free Hamiltonians satisfying
h_{j}|\psi\rangle=0
, this construction implies that
|\psi\rangle
cannot be the unique ground state. In particular, this proves that the W state
|W\rangle=N^{-1/2}\sum_{s}\sigma^{+}_{s}|0\rangle^{\otimes N}
, and any of its Dicke-state generalizations, cannot be the unique ground state of any finite-range, frustration-free Hamiltonian. This follows from the fact that
O_{i}|W\rangle=|0\rangle^{\otimes N}
, where
O_{i}=N^{1/2}\sigma^{-}_{i}
for any site
i
. The
|W\rangle
example also illustrates that the commutation condition
[h_{j},O_{i}]=0
can be satisfied trivially when the supports of
h_{j}
and
O_{i}
do not overlap.

We now focus on ground states of gapped local Hamiltonians. What about Hamiltonians that are not necessarily frustration-free? Hastings
Hastings 2006
showed that any finite-range gapped Hamiltonian is equal to another one with exponentially decaying interactions,
||[h_{j},O_{i}]||\leq e^{-|i-j|}
, where the local energy on the original ground state is exponentially small:
||h_{j}|\psi\rangle||\leq e^{-N}
for system size
N
—i.e., the system is almost frustration-free. Then,

||h_{j}|\phi\rangle||=||h_{j}O_{i}|\psi\rangle||\leq||O_{i}||e^{-N}+e^{-|i-j|}\ ,

(2)

which shows that the local energy of
|\phi\rangle
is also exponentially small,
O(e^{-N})
. Thus,
|\phi\rangle
can also be regarded as a ground state, provided that for each
h_{j}
there exists a local operator
O_{i}
, mapping
|\psi\rangle
to
|\phi\rangle
, commuting with
h_{j}
.

The impossibility of such
|\psi\rangle
being the unique ground state of a gapped local Hamiltonian implies that it is not short-range entangled. Assuming instead a power-law decay of correlations, we can show that the degeneracy is unstable under arbitrarily small local perturbations:
H_{\lambda}=H-\lambda\sum_{j}V_{j}
. To see this, we compute:

\displaystyle\langle H_{\lambda}\rangle_{\psi}-\langle H_{\lambda}\rangle_{\phi}

\displaystyle=-\lambda\sum_{j}(\langle V_{j}\rangle_{\psi}-\langle O^{\dagger}_{i}V_{j}O_{i}\rangle_{\psi})

(3)

\displaystyle\leq c\lambda N^{1-\alpha}\langle V_{j_{0}}\rangle_{\psi}\ ,

(4)

where we assume
[V_{j},O_{i}]=0
and choose
|i-j|\leq N
, together with
\sum_{j}\langle V_{j}\rangle=N\langle V_{j_{0}}\rangle
, and the algebraic decay:

\langle O^{\dagger}_{i}O_{i}\otimes V_{j}\rangle_{\psi}-\langle O^{\dagger}_{i}O_{i}\rangle_{\psi}\langle V_{j}\rangle_{\psi}\leq c|i-j|^{-\alpha}\ .

(5)

Eq. (
4
) does not decay exponentially,
O(\lambda^{N})
, as expected for degeneracy splitting in stable phases
Hastings and Wen 2005
. Note that
|W\rangle
satisfies (
5
) for a
Z
-local perturbation:
\langle O^{\dagger}_{i}O_{i}\otimes Z_{j}\rangle_{W}-\langle O^{\dagger}_{i}O_{i}\rangle_{W}\langle Z_{j}\rangle_{W}=2N^{-1}
with
O_{i}=N^{1/2}\sigma^{-}_{i}
.

Motivated by the ground state case, we wonder whether unstable degeneracies also arise for locally convertible eigenstates, in the whole spectrum, of local Hamiltonians. Similar results can also be derived for the steady (mixed) states of frustration-free local Lindbladians mapped by local quantum channels.
