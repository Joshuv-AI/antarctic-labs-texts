Correction to: Nature 10.1038/s41586-024-07874-3 Published online 7 August 2024

In the version of this article initially published, there was a mistake in the RNA blot section of the Methods, where in the text now reading “Following prehybridization … the membrane was hybridized overnight with 2 nM FAM (final concentration) (6-carboxyfluorescein)”, the text “2 nM FAM (final concentration)” appeared initially as “20 µM FAM”. The affiliation for Svetlana Belukhina, Mikhail Skutel, Anna Chechenina and Artem Isaev has also been updated. The changes have been made in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
