42 U.S.C. § 291j–10 — Payments
Citation: us:usc:42:291j–10
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section291j–10
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter IV (Construction And Modernization Of Hospitals And Other Medical Facilities); Part C (Construction Or Modernization Of Emergency Rooms). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Grants under this part shall be paid in advance or by way of reimbursement, in such installments and on such conditions, as in the judgment of the Secretary will best carry out the purposes of this part.
