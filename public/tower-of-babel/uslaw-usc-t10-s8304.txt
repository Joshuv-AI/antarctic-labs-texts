10 U.S.C. § 8304 — Availability of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:10:8304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8304
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 837 (Decorations And Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Navy may spend from appropriations for the pay of the Navy or the Marine Corps, as appropriate, amounts necessary to provide and replace medals of honor, Navy crosses, distinguished-service medals, silver star medals, and Navy and Marine Corps Medals, and associated bars, emblems, and insignia.
