43 U.S.C. § 2703 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:43:2703
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section2703
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 45 (State Control Of Noxious Plants On Federal Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are hereby authorized to be appropriated to departments or agencies of the Federal Government such sums as the Congress may determine to be necessary to carry out the purposes of this chapter.
