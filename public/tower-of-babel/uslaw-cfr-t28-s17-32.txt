28 C.F.R. § 17.32 — Notification of classification changes.
Citation: us:cfr:28:17.32
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-17.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 17 (Classified National Security Information And Access To Classified Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All known holders of information affected by unscheduled classification changes actions shall be notified promptly of such changes by the original classifier or the authority making the change in classification.
