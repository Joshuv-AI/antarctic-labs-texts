48 C.F.R. § 237.173-5 — 237.173-5 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:237.173-5
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-237.173-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 237 (Service Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insert the clause at 252.237-7010, Prohibition on Interrogation of Detainees by Contractor Personnel, in solicitations and contracts, including solicitations and contracts using FAR part 12 procedures for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services, that are for the provision of services.
