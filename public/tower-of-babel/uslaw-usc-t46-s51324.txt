46 U.S.C. § 51324 — Unfilled vacancies

Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Merchant Marine Service); Chapter 513 (United States Merchant Marine Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
In the event of an unfilled vacancy for any critical position at the United States Merchant Marine Academy, the Secretary of Transportation may appoint, without regard to the provisions of subchapter I of chapter 33 of title 5, other than sections 3303 and 3328 of that title, a qualified candidate for the purposes of filling up to 20 of such positions.
(b) Critical Position Defined.—
In this section, the term “critical position” means a position that contributes to the improvement of—
(1)
the culture or infrastructure of the Academy;
(2)
student health and well being;
(3)
Academy governance; or
(4)
any other priority areas identified by the Council.
