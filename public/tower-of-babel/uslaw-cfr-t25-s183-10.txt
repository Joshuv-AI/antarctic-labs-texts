25 C.F.R. § 183.10 — How can the Tribe use income from the Lease Fund?
Citation: us:cfr:25:183.10
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-183.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 183 (Use And Distribution Of The San Carlos Apache Tribe Development Trust Fund And San Carlos Apache Tribe Lease Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Tribe may use income from the Lease Fund for the following purposes:
(a) For economic development purposes;
(b) For community development purposes; and
(c) For administrative costs reasonably related to the above.
