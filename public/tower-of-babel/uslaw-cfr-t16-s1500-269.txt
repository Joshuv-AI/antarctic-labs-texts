16 C.F.R. § 1500.269 — Application for authorization.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1500.269
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1500.269
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1500 (Hazardous Substances And Articles: Administration And Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Application for authorization to relabel or perform other action to bring the hazardous substance into compliance with the act may be filed only by the owner or consignee and shall:
(a) Contain detailed proposals for bringing the article into compliance with the act.
(b) Specify the time and place where such operations will be carried out and the approximate time for their completion.
