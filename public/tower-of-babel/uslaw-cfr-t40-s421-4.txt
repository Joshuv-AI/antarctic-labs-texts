40 C.F.R. § 421.4 — Compliance date for pretreatment standards for existing sources (PSES).
Citation: us:cfr:40:421.4
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-421.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 421 (Nonferrous Metals Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The PSES compliance deadline in subparts A through M is March 8, 1987. The PSES compliance deadline for plants in subparts N through AE is September 20, 1988.
