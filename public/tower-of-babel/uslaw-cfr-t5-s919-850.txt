5 C.F.R. § 919.850 — What is the standard of proof in a debarment action?
Citation: us:cfr:5:919.850
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-919.850
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 919 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In any debarment action, we must establish the cause for debarment by a preponderance of the evidence.
(b) If the proposed debarment is based upon a conviction or civil judgment, the standard of proof is met.
