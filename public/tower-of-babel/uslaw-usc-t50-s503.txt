50 U.S.C. § 503 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:50:503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section503
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 19 (Guided Missiles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, not to exceed $75,000,000 to carry out the purposes of sections 501 and 502 of this title.
