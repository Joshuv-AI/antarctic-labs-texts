25 C.F.R. § 11.403 — Unlawful restraint.
Citation: us:cfr:25:11.403
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-11.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 11 (Courts Of Indian Offenses And Law And Order Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person commits a misdemeanor if he or she knowingly:
(a) Restrains another unlawfully in circumstances exposing him or her to risk of serious bodily injury; or
(b) Holds another in a condition of involuntary servitude.
