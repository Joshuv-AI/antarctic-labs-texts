5 C.F.R. § 536.403 — Documentation.
Citation: us:cfr:5:536.403
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-536.403
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 536 (Grade And Pay Retention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The application of the provisions of this part shall be documented in writing as a permanent part of the employee's Official Personnel Folder. As a minimum this documentation will include a copy of the letter described in § 536.404.
