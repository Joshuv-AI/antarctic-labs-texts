21 C.F.R. § 133.114 — Cheddar cheese for manufacturing.
Citation: us:cfr:21:133.114
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-133.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 133 (Cheeses And Related Cheese Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Cheddar cheese for manufacturing conforms to the definition and standard of identity prescribed for cheddar cheese by § 133.113, except that the milk is not pasteurized, curing is not required, and the provisions of paragraph (b)(3)(iv) of that section do not apply.
