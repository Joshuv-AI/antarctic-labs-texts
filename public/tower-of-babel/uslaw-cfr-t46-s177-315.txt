46 C.F.R. § 177.315 — Vessels of not more than 19.8 meters (65 feet) in length carrying not more than 12 passengers.
Citation: us:cfr:46:177.315
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-177.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 177 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The scantlings for a vessel of not more than 19.8 meters (65 feet) in length carrying not more than 12 passengers that do not meet the standards in § 177.300 or § 177.310 may be approved by the cognizant OCMI if the builder of the vessel establishes to the satisfaction of the OCMI that the design and construction of the vessel is adequate for the intended service.
