20 C.F.R. § 702.402 — Employer's duty to furnish; duration.
Citation: us:cfr:20:702.402
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-702.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 702 (Administration And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the duty of the employer to furnish appropriate medical care (as defined in § 702.401(a)) for the employee's injury, and for such period as the nature of the injury or the process of recovery may require.
