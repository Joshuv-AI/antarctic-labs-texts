40 C.F.R. § 23.9 — Timing of Administrator's action under the Atomic Energy Act.
Citation: us:cfr:40:23.9
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-23.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 23 (Judicial Review Under Epa-Administered Statutes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless the Administrator otherwise explicitly provides in a particular order, the time and date of the entry of an order for purposes of 28 U.S.C. 2344 shall be at 1:00 p.m. eastern time (standard or daylight, as appropriate) on the date that is two weeks after the date when notice thereof is published in the Federal Register.
