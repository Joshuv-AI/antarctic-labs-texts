46 C.F.R. § 56.75-20 — -20 Brazing qualification.
Citation: us:cfr:46:56.75-20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-56.75-20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 56 (Piping Systems And Appurtenances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The qualification of the performance of brazers and brazing operators must be in accordance with the requirements of Part C, Section IX of the ASME Code (incorporated by reference; see § 56.01-2) and part 57 of this subchapter.
(b) Manufacturers shall perform those tests required by paragraph (a) of this section prior to performing production brazing.
