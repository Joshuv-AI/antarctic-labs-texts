42 C.F.R. § 136a.32 — a.32 Delayed implementation.

Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-136a.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 136a (PART 136a—INDIAN HEALTH). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The eligibility requirements in subparts A and B of this part become effective March 16, 1988.
(b) During the six month delayed implementation period the former eligibility regulations will apply.
