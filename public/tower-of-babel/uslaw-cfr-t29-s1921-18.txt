29 C.F.R. § 1921.18 — Witnesses and fees.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1921.18
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1921.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1921 (Rules Of Practice In Enforcement Proceedings Under Section 41 Of The Longshoremen'S And Harbor Workers' Compensation Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Witnesses subpoenaed by any party shall be paid the same fees and mileage as are paid for like services in the District Courts of the United States. The witness fees and mileage shall be paid by the party at whose instance the witnesses appear.
