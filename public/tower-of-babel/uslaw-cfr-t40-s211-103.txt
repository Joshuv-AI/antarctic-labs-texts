40 C.F.R. § 211.103 — Number and gender.
Citation: us:cfr:40:211.103
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-211.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 211 (Product Noise Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In this part, words in the singular will be understood to include the plural, and words in the masculine gender will be understood to include the feminine, and vice versa, as the case may require.
