28 C.F.R. § 51.66 — Form of petition.
Citation: us:cfr:28:51.66
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-51.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 51 (Procedures For The Administration Of Section 5 Of The Voting Rights Act Of 1965, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A petition under this subpart may be made by informal letter and shall state the name, address, and telephone number of the petitioner, the change requested, and the reasons for the change.
