25 C.F.R. § 900.242 — What is the effective date of retrocession?
Citation: us:cfr:25:900.242
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior, And Indian Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-900.242
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 900 (Contracts Under The Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The retrocession is effective on the date which is the earliest date among:
(a) One year from the date of the Indian tribe or tribal organization's request;
(b) The date the contract expires; or
(c) A mutually agreed-upon date.
