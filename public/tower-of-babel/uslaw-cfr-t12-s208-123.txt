12 C.F.R. § 208.123 — Reduced reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:12:208.123
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-208.123
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 208 (Membership Of State Banking Institutions In The Federal Reserve System (Regulation H)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A covered depository institution may file the FFIEC 051 version of the report of condition, or any successor thereto, which shall provide for reduced reporting for the reports of condition for the first and third calendar quarters for a year.
