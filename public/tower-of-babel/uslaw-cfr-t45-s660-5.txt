45 C.F.R. § 660.5 — What is the Director's obligation with respect to Federal interagency coordination?
Citation: us:cfr:45:660.5
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-660.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 660 (Intergovernmental Review Of The National Science Foundation Programs And Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, to the extent practicable, consults with and seeks advice from all other substantially affected Federal departments and agencies in an effort to assure full coordination between such agencies and the Foundation regarding programs and activities covered under these regulations.
