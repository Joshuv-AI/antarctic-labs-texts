20 U.S.C. § 4517 — Appropriations for universities
Citation: us:usc:20:4517
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section4517
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 57 (James Madison Memorial Fellowship Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby appropriated to each recipient University named above or to the trustee of the fund designated by the President of the University the sum of $800,000 to carry out the provisions of section 4516 of this title, to be available on November 1, 1987, and to remain available until expended.
