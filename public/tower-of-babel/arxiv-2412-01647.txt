Abstract
From a study of the subleading structure of the asymptotic equations of motion in Einstein-Yang-Mills theory, we construct charges that are conserved up to quadratic order in non-radiative vacuum. We then show that these higher spin charges obey the celestial s ​ w 1 + ∞ sw_{1+\infty} symmetry algebra found earlier from the OPE of positive-helicity conformally soft gluons and gravitons.
