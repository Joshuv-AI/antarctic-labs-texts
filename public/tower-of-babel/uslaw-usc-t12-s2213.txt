12 U.S.C. § 2213 — Regulation and examination
Citation: us:usc:12:2213
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2213
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 23 (Farm Credit System); Subchapter IV (Provisions Applicable To Two Or More Classes Of Institutions Of The System); Part E (Service Organizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporations organized under this part shall be institutions of the Farm Credit System and shall be subject to the same regulation and examination by the Farm Credit Administration as are the organizing bank or banks under this chapter.
