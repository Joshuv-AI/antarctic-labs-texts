46 C.F.R. § 58.25-30 — -30 Automatic restart.
Citation: us:cfr:46:58.25-30
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-58.25-30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 58 (Main And Auxiliary Machinery And Related Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each control system for main and auxiliary steering gear and each power actuating system must restart automatically when electrical power is restored after it has failed.
