26 C.F.R. § 31.3121(a)(14)-1 — (a)(14)-1 Payments by employer to survivor or estate of former employee.
Citation: us:cfr:26:31.3121(a)(14)-1
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-31.3121(a)(14)-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 31 (Employment Taxes And Collection Of Income Tax At Source). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term “wages” does not include any payment by an employer to a survivor or the estate of a former employee made after 1972 and after the calendar year in which such employee died.
