7 C.F.R. § 75.48 — Identification number.
Citation: us:cfr:7:75.48
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-75.48
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 75 (Provisions For Inspection And Certification Of Quality Of Agricultural And Vegetable Seeds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may require the use of official identification numbers in connection with seed certificated or sampled under the Act. When identification numbers are required, they shall be specified by the Director and shall be attached to, or stamped, printed, or stenciled on the lot of seed certificated or sampled in a manner specified by the Director.
