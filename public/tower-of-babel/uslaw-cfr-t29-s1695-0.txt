29 C.F.R. § 1695.0 — Applicability.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1695 (Guidance Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes general procedures that apply to guidance documents of the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC or Commission) under all statutes enforced by the Commission.
