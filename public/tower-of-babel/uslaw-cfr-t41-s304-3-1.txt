41 C.F.R. § 304-3.1 — -3.1 Acceptance of non-Federal source travel expense payments.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-3.1
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-3.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-3 (3—Employee Responsibility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies may accept payments for an employee's travel to a meeting from a non-Federal source under this part and in accordance with agency policy. Employees may accept such a payment only when their agency specifically authorizes such acceptance under the requirements of this part. Except as provided in § 304-3.11, agencies must approve acceptance of such payment in advance of employee travel.
