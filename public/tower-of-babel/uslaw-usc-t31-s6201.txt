31 U.S.C. § 6201 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:31:6201
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section6201
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 62 (Consolidated Federal Funds Report). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, the term—
(1)
“Director” means the Director of the Office of Management and Budget;
(2)
“State” means any State, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, the District of Columbia, Guam, American Samoa, the Virgin Islands, the Government of the Northern Mariana Islands, and the Trust Territory of the Pacific Islands; and
(3)
“municipality” means any subcounty unit of general local government that received Federal assistance in the fiscal year that is the subject of the report.
