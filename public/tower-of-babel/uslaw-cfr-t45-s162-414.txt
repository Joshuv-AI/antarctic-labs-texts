45 C.F.R. § 162.414 — Implementation specifications: Health care clearinghouses.
Citation: us:cfr:45:162.414
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-162.414
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 162 (Administrative Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A health care clearinghouse must use the NPI of any health care provider (or subpart(s), if applicable) that has been assigned an NPI to identify that health care provider on all standard transactions where that health care provider's identifier is required.
