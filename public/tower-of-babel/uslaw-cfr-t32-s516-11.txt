32 C.F.R. § 516.11 — Service of criminal process outside the United States.
Citation: us:cfr:32:516.11
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-516.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 516 (Litigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Army Regulation 630-10 and international treaties, such as status of forces agreements, govern the service of criminal process of foreign courts and the surrender of soldiers to foreign civilian law enforcement officials.
