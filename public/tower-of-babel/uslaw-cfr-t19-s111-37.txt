19 C.F.R. § 111.37 — Misuse of license or permit.
Citation: us:cfr:19:111.37
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-111.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 111 (Customs Brokers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A broker must not allow his license, permit or name to be used by or for any unlicensed person (including a broker whose license or permit is under suspension), other than his own employees authorized to act for him, in the solicitation, promotion or performance of any customs business or transaction.
