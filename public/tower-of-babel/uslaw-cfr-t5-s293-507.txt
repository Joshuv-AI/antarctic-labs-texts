5 C.F.R. § 293.507 — Maintenance and content of the Employee Medical Folder.
Citation: us:cfr:5:293.507
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-293.507
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 293 (Personnel Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency head must maintain all appropriate employee occupational medical records in the EMFS. When an EMF is established for an employee, as required in § 293.504, the agency's EMFS must be searched to obtain all records designated for retention in the EMF.
