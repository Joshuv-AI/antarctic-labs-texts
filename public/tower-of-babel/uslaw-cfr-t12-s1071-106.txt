12 C.F.R. § 1071.106 — Delegations of authority.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1071 (Rule Implementing Equal Access To Justice Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may delegate authority to take final action on matters pertaining to the Equal Access to Justice Act in particular cases.
