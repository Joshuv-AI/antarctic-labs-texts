7 C.F.R. § 989.23 — File.
Citation: us:cfr:7:989.23
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-989.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 989 (Raisins Produced From Grapes Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

File means transmit or deliver to the Secretary or Committee, as the case may be, and such act shall be deemed to have been accomplished at the time:
(a) Of actual receipt by the Secretary or Committee in the event of personal delivery;
(b) Of receipt at the office of the telegraph company, in case submission is by telegram; or
(c) Shown by the postmark, in case submission is by mail.
