33 U.S.C. § 3024 — Promotion by selection to permanent grades above lieutenant (junior grade)
Citation: us:usc:33:3024
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3024
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 43 (National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration Commissioned Officer Corps); Subchapter II (Appointment And Promotion Of Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Promotion to fill vacancies in each permanent grade above the grade of lieutenant (junior grade) shall be made by selection from the next lower grade upon recommendation of the personnel board.
