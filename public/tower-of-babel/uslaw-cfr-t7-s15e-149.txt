7 C.F.R. § 15e.149 — e.149 Program accessibility: Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:7:15e.149
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-15e.149
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 15e (PART 15e—ENFORCEMENT OF NONDISCRIMINATION ON THE BASIS OF HANDICAP IN PROGRAMS OR ACTIVITIES CONDUCTED BY THE UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in § 15e.150, no qualified individual with handicaps shall, because the agency's facilities are inaccessible to or unusable by individuals with handicaps, be denied the benefits of, be excluded from participation in, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity conducted by the agency.
