Minor Modification Approval

AGENCY: Susquehanna River Basin Commission.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This notice lists the minor modifications approved for 
previously approved projects by the Susquehanna River Basin Commission 
during the period set forth in DATES.

DATES: September 1-30, 2026.

ADDRESSES: Susquehanna River Basin Commission, 4423 North Front Street, 
Harrisburg, PA 17110-1788.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jason E. Oyler, General Counsel and 
Secretary to the Commission, telephone: (717) 238-0423, ext. 1312; fax 
(717) 238-2436; email: [email protected]. Regular mail inquiries may be 
sent to the above address.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This notice lists previously approved 
projects, receiving approval of minor modification or a corrective 
modification, described below, pursuant to 18 CFR 806.18 or to 
Commission Resolution Nos. 2013-11 and 2015-06, for the time period 
specified above.
    1. Expand Operating LLC--Susquehanna River--Standing Stone Twp 
(Franklin) Docket No. 20260615, Standing Stone Township, Bradford 
County, Pa.; modification approval to change the intake design; 
Approval Date: September 2, 2026.
    2. New York State--Office of Parks, Recreation, and Historic 
Preservation--Indian Hills State Golf Course Docket No. 20260616, Towns 
of Erwin and Lindley, Steuben County, NY; modification approval to 
change the intake design; Approval Date: September 3, 2026.
    Authority: Public Law 91-575, 84 Stat. 1509 et seq., 18 CFR parts 
806 and 808.
