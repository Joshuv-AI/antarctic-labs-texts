48 C.F.R. § 46.709 — 46.709 Warranties of commercial products and commercial services.
Citation: us:cfr:48:46.709
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-46.709
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 46 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer should take advantage of commercial warranties, including extended warranties, where appropriate and in the Government's best interests, offered by the contractor for the repair and replacement of commercial products and commercial services (see part 12).
