12 C.F.R. § 1215.11 — FHFA determination.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1215.11
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1215.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1215 (Production Of Fhfa Records, Information, And Employee Testimony In Third-Party Legal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Director makes FHFA's determinations regarding demands and requests.
(b) The Director will notify the demanding or requesting party of FHFA's determination, the reasons for the approval or rejection of the demand or request, and any conditions that the Director may impose on the release of records, information, or testimony.
