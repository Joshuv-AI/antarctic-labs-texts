47 C.F.R. § 63.50 — Amendment of applications.
Citation: us:cfr:47:63.50
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-63.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 63 (Extension Of Lines, New Lines, And Discontinuance, Reduction, Outage And Impairment Of Service By Common Carriers; And Grants Of Recognized Private Operating Agency Status). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any application may be amended as a matter of right prior to the date of any final action taken by the Commission or designation for hearing. Amendments to applications shall be signed and submitted in the same manner, and with the same number of copies as was the original application. If a petition to deny or other formal objections have been filed to the application, the amendment shall be served on the parties.
