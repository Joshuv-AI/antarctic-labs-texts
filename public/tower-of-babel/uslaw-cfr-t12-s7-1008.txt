12 C.F.R. § 7.1008 — Preparation by a national bank of income tax returns for customers or public.
Citation: us:cfr:12:7.1008
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-7.1008
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 7 (Activities And Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A national bank may assist its customers in preparing their tax returns, either gratuitously or for a fee.
