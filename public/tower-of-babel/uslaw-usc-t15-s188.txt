15 U.S.C. § 188 — Publication of commercial information
Citation: us:usc:15:188
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section188
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 5 (Statistical And Commercial Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Commerce shall publish official notifications, from time to time, of such commercial information communicated to him by diplomatic and consular officers, as he may deem important to the public interests, in such newspapers, not to exceed three in number, as he may select.
