37 C.F.R. § 104.42 — Finality of settlement or denial of claims.
Citation: us:cfr:37:104.42
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-104.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 104 (Legal Processes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only a decision of the Director or the General Counsel regarding settlement or denial of any claim under this subpart may be considered final for the purpose of judicial review.
