42 U.S.C. § 273a — National living donor mechanisms
Citation: us:usc:42:273a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section273a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties); Part H (Organ Transplants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may establish and maintain mechanisms to evaluate the long-term effects associated with living organ donations by individuals who have served as living donors.
