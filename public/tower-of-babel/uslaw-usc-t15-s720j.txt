15 U.S.C. § 720j — Sense of Congress concerning participation by small business concerns
Citation: us:usc:15:720j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section720j
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 15D (Alaska Natural Gas Pipeline). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Definition of small business concern
In this section, the term “small business concern” has the meaning given the term in section 632(a) of this title.
(b) Sense of Congress
It is the sense of Congress that—
(1)
an Alaska natural gas transportation project would provide significant economic benefits to the United States and Canada; and
(2)
to maximize those benefits, the sponsors of the Alaska natural gas transportation project should maximize the participation of small business concerns in contracts and subcontracts awarded in carrying out the project.
