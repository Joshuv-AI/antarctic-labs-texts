2 U.S.C. § 134 — Incidental expenses of law library
Citation: us:usc:2:134
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section134
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 5 (Library Of Congress). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The incidental expenses of the law library shall be paid out of the appropriations for the Library of Congress.
