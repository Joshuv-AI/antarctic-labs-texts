40 C.F.R. § 192.30 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:192.30
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-192.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 192 (Health And Environmental Protection Standards For Uranium And Thorium Mill Tailings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to the management of uranium byproduct materials under section 84 of the Atomic Energy Act of 1954 (henceforth designated “the Act”), as amended, during and following processing of uranium ores, and to restoration of disposal sites following any use of such sites under section 83(b)(1)(B) of the Act.
