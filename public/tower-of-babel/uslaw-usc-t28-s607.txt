28 U.S.C. § 607 — Practice of law prohibited
Citation: us:usc:28:607
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section607
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part III (Court Officers And Employees); Chapter 41 (Administrative Office Of United States Courts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An officer or employee of the Administrative Office shall not engage directly or indirectly in the practice of law in any court of the United States.
