28 C.F.R. § 20.38 — Sanction for noncompliance.
Citation: us:cfr:28:20.38
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-20.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 20 (Criminal Justice Information Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Access to systems managed or maintained by the FBI is subject to cancellation in regard to any agency or entity that fails to comply with the provisions of subpart C of this part.
