42 U.S.C. § 14942 — Documents of other Convention countries
Citation: us:usc:42:14942
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section14942
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 143 (Intercountry Adoptions); Subchapter IV (Administration And Enforcement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Documents originating in any other Convention country and related to a Convention adoption case shall require no authentication in order to be admissible in any Federal, State, or local court in the United States, unless a specific and supported claim is made that the documents are false, have been altered, or are otherwise unreliable.
