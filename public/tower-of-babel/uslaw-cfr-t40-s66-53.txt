40 C.F.R. § 66.53 — Decisions on petitions.
Citation: us:cfr:40:66.53
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-66.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 66 (Assessment And Collection Of Noncompliance Penalties By Epa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within thirty days after receiving a petition for reconsideration under § 66.52 the Administrator shall:
(a) Accept the penalty calculation of the owner or operator to the extent the Administrator concludes it is correct; or
(b) Grant a hearing to the extent he does not conclude that the petition is correct.
