27 C.F.R. § 13.41 — Authority to revoke certificates.
Citation: us:cfr:27:13.41
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-13.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 13 (Labeling Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Certificates of label approval, certificates of exemption from label approval, and distinctive liquor bottle approvals, previously approved on TTB Form 5100.31, may be revoked by the appropriate TTB officer upon a finding that the label or bottle at issue is not in compliance with the applicable laws or regulations.
