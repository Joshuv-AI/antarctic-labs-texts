49 C.F.R. § 525.9 — Duration of exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:49:525.9
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-525.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 525 (Exemptions From Average Fuel Economy Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An exemption may be granted under this part for not more than three model years.
