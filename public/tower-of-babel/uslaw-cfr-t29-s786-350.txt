29 C.F.R. § 786.350 — Exclusion from definition of “employee” of volunteers at private non-profit food banks.
Citation: us:cfr:29:786.350
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-786.350
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 786 (Miscellaneous Exemptions And Exclusions From Coverage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 3(e)(5) of the Fair Labor Standards Act excludes from the definition of the term “employee” individuals who volunteer their services solely for humanitarian purposes at private non-profit food banks and who receive groceries from the food banks.
