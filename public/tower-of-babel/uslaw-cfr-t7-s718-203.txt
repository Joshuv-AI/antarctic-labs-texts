7 C.F.R. § 718.203 — County committee action to reconstitute a farm.
Citation: us:cfr:7:718.203
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-718.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 718 (Provisions Applicable To Multiple Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Action to reconstitute a farm may be initiated by the county committee, the farm owner, or the operator with the concurrence of the owner of the farm. Any request for a farm reconstitution shall be filed with the county committee.
