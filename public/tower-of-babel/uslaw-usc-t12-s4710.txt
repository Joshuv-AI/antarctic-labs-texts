12 U.S.C. § 4710 — Collection and compilation of information
Citation: us:usc:12:4710
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4710
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 47 (Community Development Banking); Subchapter I (Community Development Banking And Financial Institutions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Fund shall—
(1)
collect and compile information pertinent to community development financial institutions that will assist in creating, developing, expanding, and preserving such institutions; and
(2)
make such information available to promote the purposes of this subchapter.
