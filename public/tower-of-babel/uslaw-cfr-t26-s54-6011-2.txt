26 C.F.R. § 54.6011-2 — -2 General requirement of return, statement, or list.

Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 54 (Pension Excise Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Effective for any Form 8928 that is due on or after January 1, 2010, any person liable for tax under section 4980B, 4980D, 4980E, or 4980G of the Code shall file a return with respect to the tax on Form 8928. The return must include the information required by Form 8928 and the instructions issued with respect to it.
