7 C.F.R. § 634.30 — Appeals in USDA administered projects.
Citation: us:cfr:7:634.30
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-634.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 634 (Rural Clean Water Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The participant in a USDA-administered RCWP project may appeal decisions of the administering agency in accordance with part 614 of this title.
