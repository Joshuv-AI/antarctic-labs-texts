19 U.S.C. § 2572 — Exemptions
Citation: us:usc:19:2572
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2572
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 13 (Trade Agreements Act Of 1979); Subchapter II (Technical Barriers To Trade (Standards)); Part D (Definitions And Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter does not apply to—
(1)
any standards activity engaged in by any Federal agency or State agency for the use (including, but not limited to, use with respect to research and development, production, or consumption) of that agency or the use of another such agency; or
(2)
any standards activity engaged in by any private person solely for use in the production or consumption of products by that person.
