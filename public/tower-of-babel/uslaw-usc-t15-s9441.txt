15 U.S.C. § 9441 — Stakeholder outreach
Citation: us:usc:15:9441
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9441
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 119 (National Artificial Intelligence Initiative); Subchapter III (Department Of Commerce Artificial Intelligence Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out the activities under section 278h–1 of this title as amended by title III of this Act,1 the Director shall—
(1)
solicit input from university researchers, private sector experts, relevant Federal agencies, Federal laboratories, State, Tribal, and local governments, civil society groups, and other relevant stakeholders;
(2)
solicit input from experts in relevant fields of social science, technology ethics, and law; and
(3)
provide opportunity for public comment on guidelines and best practices developed as part of the Initiative, as appropriate.
