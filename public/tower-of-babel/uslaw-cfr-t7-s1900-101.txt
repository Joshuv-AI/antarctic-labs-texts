7 C.F.R. § 1900.101 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1900.101
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1900.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1900 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart provides Rural Development policy concerning:
(a) The applicability of Federal rather than State Law in the conduct of Rural Development operations, and
(b) The liability of an auctioneer for conversion of personal property mortgaged to Rural Development.
