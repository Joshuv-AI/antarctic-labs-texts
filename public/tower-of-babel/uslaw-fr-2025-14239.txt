Amended Notice of Meeting

Deafness and Other Communication Disorders Advisory Council, September 
04, 2025, 01:00 p.m. to September 05, 2025, 01:05 p.m., National

9:30 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. The meeting is partially Closed to the public.
