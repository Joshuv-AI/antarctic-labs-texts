36 C.F.R. § 13.1106 — Pets.
Citation: us:cfr:36:13.1106
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-13.1106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 13 (National Park System Units In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pets are prohibited except—
(a) On the Bartlett Cove Public Use Dock;
(b) On the beach between the Bartlett Cove Public Use Dock and the National Park Service Administrative Dock;
(c) Within 100 feet of Bartlett Cove Developed Area park roads or parking areas unless otherwise posted;
(d) On a vessel on the water; or
(e) Within Glacier Bay National Preserve.
