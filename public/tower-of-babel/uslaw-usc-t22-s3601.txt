22 U.S.C. § 3601 — Congressional statement of purpose
Citation: us:usc:22:3601
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3601
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 51 (Panama Canal). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this chapter to provide legislation necessary or desirable for the implementation of the Panama Canal Treaty of 1977 between the United States of America and the Republic of Panama and of the related agreements accompanying that Treaty.
