Correction: Circular RNA ACVR2A suppresses bladder cancer cells proliferation and metastasis through miR-626/EYA4 axis
Authors: Wei Dong, Junming Bi, Hongwei Liu, Dong Yan, Qingqing He, Qianghua Zhou, Qiong Wang, Ruihui Xie, Yinjie Su, Meihua Yang, Tianxin Lin, Jian Huang
Published: 2025

Correction: Mol Cancer 18, 95 (2019)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12943-019-1025-z

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors reported that during a recent review of their original data, they discovered an error in Fig. 5D. In the original figure, the “T24 Migration” image of “EYA4-si2” and the “T24 Invasion” image of “EYA4-si2” partially overlap. The error occurred during figure assembly. Specifically, while taking photos for the transwell migration/invasion assays, three random images from each group were taken and saved in TIFF format in their corresponding folders. Since both migration and invasion images of “T24-EYA4-si2” were saved under the same filename in separate folders, an incorrect image file was inadvertently imported during the composite figure preparation in Adobe Illustrator CS6.

All experiments were repeated at least three times, and this error occurred solely during figure preparation. It does not affect the integrity of the data, results, or conclusions of the paper. The correct Fig. 5D is shown below.
