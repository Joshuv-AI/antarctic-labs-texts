30 U.S.C. § 49f — Fees of recorders in Alaska for filing proofs of work and improvements
Citation: us:usc:30:49f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section49f
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 2 (Mineral Lands And Regulations In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The recorders for the several divisions or districts of Alaska shall collect the sum of $1.50 as a fee for the filing, recording, and indexing annual proofs of work and improvements for each claim so recorded under the provisions of section 49e of this title.
