41 C.F.R. § 102-37.65 — -37.65 SASP eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.65
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To receive transfers of surplus property, a SASP must:
(a) Have a GSA-approved State plan of operation; and
(b) Provide the certifications and agreements as set forth in §§ 102-37.110 and 102-37.115.
