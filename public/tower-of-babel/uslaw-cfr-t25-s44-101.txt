25 C.F.R. § 44.101 — What directives apply to a grantee under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:44.101
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-44.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 44 (Grants Under The Tribally Controlled Schools Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In making a grant under this part the Secretary will use only:
(a) The Tribally Controlled Schools Act;
(b) The regulations in this part; and
(c) Guidelines, manuals, and policy directives agreed to by the grantee.
