18 C.F.R. § 281.214 — Notice, complaint and remedy.

Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-281.214
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 281 (Natural Gas Curtailment Under The Natural Gas Policy Act Of 1978). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Complaint. Any interested person may file a complaint concerning an alleged violation of this subpart under § 385.206 of this chapter.
(b) Remedy. If the Commission determines that a violation of this subpart has occurred, it shall take whatever action it deems appropriate in the circumstances. Such action may include payback, in kind or in dollars, by the person benefitting from the violation.
