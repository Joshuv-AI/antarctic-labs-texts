32 C.F.R. § 241.13 — Implementation.
Citation: us:cfr:32:241.13
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-241.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 241 (Pilot Program For Temporary Exchange Of Information Technology Personnel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The DoD CIO is responsible for administering, coordinating and implementing the Pilot Program for the Temporary Exchange of Information Personnel, referred to as the Information Technology Exchange Program (ITEP) pilot. The DoD CIO will coordinate with DoD Components.
