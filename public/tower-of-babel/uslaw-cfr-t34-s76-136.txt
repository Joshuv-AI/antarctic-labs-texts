34 C.F.R. § 76.136 — Under what programs may consolidated grant funds be spent?
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.136
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.136
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insular Areas may only use and administer funds under State-administered formula grant programs during a fiscal year for which the Insular Area is entitled to receive funds under an appropriation for that program.
