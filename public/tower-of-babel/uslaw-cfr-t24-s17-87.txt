24 C.F.R. § 17.87 — Determination of indebtedness.
Citation: us:cfr:24:17.87
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-17.87
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 17 (Administrative Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In determining that an employee is indebted to HUD, the Secretary will review the debt to make sure that it is valid and past due.
