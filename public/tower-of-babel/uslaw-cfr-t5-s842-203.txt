5 C.F.R. § 842.203 — General eligibility requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:5:842.203
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-842.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 842 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Basic Annuity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee must have at least 5 years of civilian service creditable under FERS to be eligible for an annuity under this subpart, except as provided under part 846 of this chapter.
