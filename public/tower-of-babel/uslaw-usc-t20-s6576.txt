20 U.S.C. § 6576 — Rule of construction on equalized spending
Citation: us:usc:20:6576
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section6576
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 70 (Strengthening And Improvement Of Elementary And Secondary Schools); Subchapter I (Improving The Academic Achievement Of The Disadvantaged); Part F (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed to mandate equalized spending per pupil for a State, local educational agency, or school.
