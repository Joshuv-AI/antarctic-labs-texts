46 C.F.R. § 131.710 — Approved work vests.
Citation: us:cfr:46:131.710
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-131.710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 131 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each buoyant work vest carried aboard must be approved under subpart 160.053 of this chapter or, as a commercial hybrid personal flotation device, under subpart 160.077 of this chapter.
