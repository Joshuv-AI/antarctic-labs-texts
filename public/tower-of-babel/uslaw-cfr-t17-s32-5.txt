17 C.F.R. § 32.5 — Option transactions entered into prior to the effective date of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:17:32.5
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-32.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 32 (Regulation Of Commodity Option Transactions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing contained in this part shall be construed to affect any lawful activities that occurred prior to the effective date of this part.
