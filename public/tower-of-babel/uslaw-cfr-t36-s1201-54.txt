36 C.F.R. § 1201.54 — Can a judgment I have obtained against the United States be used to satisfy a debt that I owe to NARA?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1201.54
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1201.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1201 (Collection Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Collection by offset against a judgment obtained by a debtor against the United States will be accomplished in accordance with 31 U.S.C. 3728 and 31 U.S.C. 3716.
