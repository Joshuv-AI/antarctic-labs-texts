7 C.F.R. § 1124.44 — Classification of producer milk.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1124.44
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1124.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1124 (Milk In The Pacific Northwest Marketing Area). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the provisions provided in § 1000.44, for purposes of this part 1124, § 1000.44(a)(3)(iv) applies to fluid milk products and bulk fluid cream products received or acquired for distribution from a producer-handler.
