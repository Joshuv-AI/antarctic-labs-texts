13 C.F.R. § 146.210 — Reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:13:146.210
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-146.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 146 (New Restrictions On Lobbying). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No reporting is required with respect to payments of reasonable compensation made to regularly employed officers or employees of a person.
