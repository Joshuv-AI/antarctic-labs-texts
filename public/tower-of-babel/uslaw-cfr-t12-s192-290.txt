12 C.F.R. § 192.290 — Remedial measures for prohibited solicitations.
Citation: us:cfr:12:192.290
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-192.290
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 192 (Conversions From Mutual To Stock Form). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. If a solicitation violates § 192.285, the appropriate Federal banking agency may require remedial measures, including:
(1) Correction of the violation by a retraction and a new solicitation;
(2) Rescheduling the members' meeting; or
(3) Any other actions necessary to ensure a fair vote.
(b) Other action. The appropriate Federal banking agency also may bring an enforcement action against the violator.
