46 C.F.R. § 56.60-3 — -3 Ferrous materials.
Citation: us:cfr:46:56.60-3
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-56.60-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 56 (Piping Systems And Appurtenances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Ferrous pipe used for saltwater service must be protected against corrosion by hotdip galvanizing or by the use of extra heavy schedule material.
(b) (Reproduces 124.2.C.) Carbon or alloy steel having carbon content of more than 0.35 percent must not be used in welded construction, nor be shaped by oxygen-cutting process or other thermal-cutting process.
