Retraction Note: Omega-3 polyunsaturated fatty acid attenuates traumatic brain injury-induced neuronal apoptosis by inducing autophagy through the upregulation of SIRT1-mediated deacetylation of Beclin-1
Authors: Xiangrong Chen, Zhigang Pan, Zhongning Fang, Weibin Lin, Shukai Wu, Fuxing Yang, Yasong Li, Huangde Fu, Hongzhi Gao, Shun Li
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: Chen et al. J Neuroinflammation 15, 310 (2018)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12974-018-1345-8

The Editors-in-Chief have retracted this article. After publication, the following concerns were raised:

In figure 2D, three sham panels appear to overlap with three Sham+ω-3 panels with frameshift

In figure 2D, three sham panels appear to overlap with three Sham+ω-3 panels with frameshift

Four panels in figure 2D appear to overlap with rotation or frameshift with figure 3A

Four panels in figure 2D appear to overlap with rotation or frameshift with figure 3A

the TBI+ω-3 panel in figure 6A appears to overlap with the Sham panel in three other articles by the same author group, figures 5D in [1], 5B in [2], and 6A in [3], and the TBI+ω-3 panel in [1] and [3] and the TBI panel in [2] with frameshift.

the TBI+ω-3 panel in figure 6A appears to overlap with the Sham panel in three other articles by the same author group, figures 5D in [1], 5B in [2], and 6A in [3], and the TBI+ω-3 panel in [1] and [3] and the TBI panel in [2] with frameshift.

In figure 7A, Sham appears to overlap with Sham+ω-3 with rotation

In figure 7A, Sham appears to overlap with Sham+ω-3 with rotation

The authors were able to provide the original images but a correction could not be issued due to the severity of the concerns raised. The Editors-in-Chief have lost confidence in the data and conclusions of this article.

Author Xiangrong Chen did not explicitly state whether or not they agree with this retraction. All remaining authors did not respond to correspondence from the Publisher about this retraction.
