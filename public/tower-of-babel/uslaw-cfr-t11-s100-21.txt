11 C.F.R. § 100.21 — Employer (52 U.S.C. 30101(13)).
Citation: us:cfr:11:100.21
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-100.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 100 (Scope And Definitions (52 U.S.C. 30101)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employer means the organization or person by whom an individual is employed, and not the name of his or her supervisor.
