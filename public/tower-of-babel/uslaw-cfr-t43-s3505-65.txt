43 C.F.R. § 3505.65 — What information must I include in my extension request?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3505.65
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3505.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your request must:
(a) Show that you have met the conditions for extension in § 3505.62;
(b) Describe your previous diligent prospecting activities on the permit; and
(c) Show how much additional time you need to complete prospecting work.
