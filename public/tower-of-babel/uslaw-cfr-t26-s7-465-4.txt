26 C.F.R. § 7.465-4 — -4 Insufficient records.
Citation: us:cfr:26:7.465-4
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-7.465-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 7 (Temporary Income Tax Regulations Under The Tax Reform Act Of 1976). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If sufficient records do not exist to accurately determine under § 7.465-2 the amount which a taxpayer is at risk on the effective date, the amount at risk shall be the taxpayer's basis in the activity reduced (but not below zero) by the taxpayer's share of amounts described in section 465(b) (3) or (4) with respect to the activity on the day before the effective date.
