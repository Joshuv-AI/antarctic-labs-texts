34 C.F.R. § 690.13 — Notification of expected family contribution.
Citation: us:cfr:34:690.13
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-690.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 690 (Federal Pell Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary sends a student's application information and EFC as calculated by the central processor to the student on an SAR and allows each institution designated by the student to obtain an ISIR for that student.
