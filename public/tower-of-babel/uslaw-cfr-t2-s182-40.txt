2 C.F.R. § 182.40 — How is the guidance maintained?
Citation: us:cfr:2:182.40
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Government-Wide Guidance For Federal Financial Assistance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-182.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 182 (Government-Wide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The OMB publishes proposed changes to the guidance in the Federal Register for public comment, considers comments with the help of appropriate interagency working groups, and then issues any changes to the guidance in final form.
