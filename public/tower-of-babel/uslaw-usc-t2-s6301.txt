2 U.S.C. § 6301 — Senators’ salaries
Citation: us:usc:2:6301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6301
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 63 (Senate Members); Subchapter I (Member Pay). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Senators elected, whose term of office begins on the 3d day of January, and whose credentials in due form of law shall have been presented in the Senate, may receive their compensation from the beginning of their term.
