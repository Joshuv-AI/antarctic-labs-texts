10 U.S.C. § 2568 — Retention of combat uniforms by members deployed in support of contingency operations
Citation: us:usc:10:2568
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2568
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Property); Chapter 152 (Issue Of Supplies, Services, And Facilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of a military department may authorize a member of the armed forces under the jurisdiction of the Secretary who has been deployed in support of a contingency operation for at least 30 days to retain, after that member is no longer so deployed, the combat uniform issued to that member as organizational clothing and individual equipment.
