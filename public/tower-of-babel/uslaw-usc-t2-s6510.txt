2 U.S.C. § 6510 — Materials, supplies, and fuel payments from Senate contingent fund
Citation: us:usc:2:6510
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6510
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 65 (Senate Officers And Administration); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Payments from the contingent fund of the Senate for materials and supplies (including fuel) purchased on and after July 8, 1935, through the Administrator of General Services shall be made by check upon vouchers approved by the Committee on Rules and Administration of the Senate.
