49 C.F.R. § 821.15 — Motion to disqualify a Board Member.
Citation: us:cfr:49:821.15
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-821.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 821 (Rules Of Practice In Air Safety Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A motion requesting that a Board Member disqualify himself or herself from participating in a proceeding under this part shall be filed in writing with the Board.
