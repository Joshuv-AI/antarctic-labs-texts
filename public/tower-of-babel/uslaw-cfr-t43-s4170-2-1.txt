43 C.F.R. § 4170.2-1 — -1 Penal provisions under the Taylor Grazing Act.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4170.2-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4170.2-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4100 (Grazing Administration—Exclusive Of Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under section 2 of the Act any person who willfully commits an act prohibited under § 4140.1(b), or who willfully violates approved special rules and regulations is punishable by a fine of not more than $500.
