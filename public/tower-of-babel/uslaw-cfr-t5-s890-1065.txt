5 C.F.R. § 890.1065 — Deciding whether to suspend or debar a provider in a case that also involves penalties and assessments.
Citation: us:cfr:5:890.1065
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-890.1065
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 890 (Federal Employees Health Benefits Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In a case where both penalties and assessments and debarment are proposed concurrently, OPM must decide the proposed debarment under the same criteria and procedures as if it had been proposed separately from penalties and assessments.
