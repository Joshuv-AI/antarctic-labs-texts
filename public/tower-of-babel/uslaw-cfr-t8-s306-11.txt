8 C.F.R. § 306.11 — Preliminary application form; filing; examination.
Citation: us:cfr:8:306.11
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-306.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 306 (Special Classes Of Persons Who May Be Naturalized: Virgin Islanders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person of the class described in § 306.1 shall submit to the Service on Form N-350 preliminary application to renounce Danish citizenship, in accordance with the instructions contained therein. The applicant shall be notified in writing when and where to appear before a representative of the Service for examination as to his eligibility to renounce Danish citizenship and for assistance in filing the renunciation.
