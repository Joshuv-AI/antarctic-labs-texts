49 C.F.R. § 563.6 — Requirements for vehicles.
Citation: us:cfr:49:563.6
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-563.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 563 (Event Data Recorders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vehicle equipped with an EDR must meet the requirements specified in § 563.7 for data elements, § 563.8 for data format, § 563.9 for data capture, § 563.10 for crash test performance and survivability, and § 563.11 for information in owner's manual.
