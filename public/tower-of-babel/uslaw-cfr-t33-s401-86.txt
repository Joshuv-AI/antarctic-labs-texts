33 C.F.R. § 401.86 — Security for damages or injury.
Citation: us:cfr:33:401.86
Creator: Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-401.86
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 401 (Seaway Regulations And Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An officer may detain a vessel that causes:
(a) Damage to property of the Corporation;
(b) Damage to goods or cargo stored on property of the Corporation; or
(c) Injury to employees of the Corporation; until security satisfactory to the Corporation has been provided.
