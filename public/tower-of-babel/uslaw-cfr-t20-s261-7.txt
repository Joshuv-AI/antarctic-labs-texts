20 C.F.R. § 261.7 — Effect of revised decision.
Citation: us:cfr:20:261.7
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-261.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 261 (Administrative Finality). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A revised decision is binding unless:
(a) The revised decision is reconsidered or appealed in accord with part 260 of this chapter;
(b) The three-member Board reviews the revised decision; or
(c) The revised decision is further revised consistent with this part.
