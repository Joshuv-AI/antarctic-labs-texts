On structured condition number of rational matrix functions Journal: Linear Algebra and its Applications
Ritwik Prabin Kalita, Anshul Prajapati, Punit Sharma

Abstract
We derive the necessary and sufficient conditions for the simple eigenvalues of rational matrix functions with symmetry structure to have the same normwise condition number with respect to arbitrary and structure-preserving perturbations. We obtain an exact expression for the structured condition number of simple eigenvalues of symmetric, skew-symmetric and T-even/odd rational matrix functions, and tight bounds are obtained for simple eigenvalues of Hermitian, skew-Hermitian, ∗ * -even/odd, ∗ * -palindromic and T-palindromic rational matrix functions.

Condition numbers play an important role in the sensitivity analysis of problems that compute eigen-elements of a matrix function. If the matrix function with additional symmetry structures are considered, then in that case, it is advisable to do the sensitivity analysis under structure-preserving perturbations. Eigenvalue condition number together with eigenvalue backward error bound the error in eigenvalue computation. The
eigenvalue backward errors have been well studied for matrix polynomials with various structures
[
5
,
6
,
16
,
19
]
. Recently, in
[
17
]
, authors have studied the structured eigenvalue backward error of rational matrix functions
G(z)
of the form (
1.2
) with structures from Table
1
.
The condition number for an unstructured matrix polynomial
P(z)=A_{0}+zA_{1}+\cdots z^{d}A_{d}
was discussed in
[
19
]
, which was later extended to various symmetry structures on
P(z)
in
[
4
]
.
In
[
15
]
, the condition number of
unstructured
(with no symmetry structures) RMFs in the transfer function form was considered and computable formulas were obtained. However, no work has been done on the structured condition number of RMFs with symmetry structures.

Our work is mainly inspired by
[
4
]
and aims at deriving computable formulas for the structured condition number of RMF’s with structures from Table
1
. We derive necessary and sufficient conditions under which the unstructured and the structured condition numbers of simple eigenvalues of RMF
G(z)
(
1.2
) with structures from Table
1
, are equal. Our results are generalization of the techniques used in
[
4
]
for matrix polynomials with symmetry structures such as T-palindromic,
*
-palindromic, T-alternating and
*
-alternating. We note that, this generalization is not immediate and requires a careful dealing of the weights
w_{j}(z):=\frac{s_{j}(z)}{q_{j}(z)}
while perturbing the stuctured RMF (
1.2
). As far as we know, this is the first attempt to compute structured eigenvalue condition number of RMFs.

This paper is organized as follows: In Section

Inspired by

In the following,

Assumption

\displaystyle:~\lambda\in{\mathbb{C}}~\text{ be such\,that}~\lambda~\text{is a simple eigenvalue of }G(z),\,w_{j}(\lambda)\neq 0~\text{for}\,j=1,\ldots,m

\displaystyle\hskip 8.5359pt~\text{and}~\lambda~\text{is not a pole of}~G(z).

(2.1)

Motivated by the perturbation analysis in

\|\Delta G(z)\|_{M,v}={\big\|{\left({\|\Delta_{A_{0}}\|}_{M},\ldots,{\|\Delta_{A_{d}}\|}_{M},{\|\Delta_{E_{1}}\|}_{M},\ldots,{\|\Delta_{E_{m}}\|}_{M}\right)\big\|}_{v}}.

We use the norm

to measure the perturbations in

. Thus, the norm of the perturbation
\Delta G(z)
of the form (
2.2
), is given by

\|\Delta G(z)\|_{2,\infty}=\max\{\|\Delta_{A_{0}}\|,\ldots,\|\Delta_{A_{d}}\|,\|\Delta_{E_{1}}\|,\ldots,\|\Delta_{E_{m}}\|\}.

Note that, for computing the condition number of simple eigenvalues of matrix polynomial

Definition 2.2
.

Given a RMF

of the form (
1.2
), and a simple eigenvalue
\lambda\in{\mathbb{C}}
, let

be a right eigenvector of unit norm and

be a corresponding left eigenvector of unit norm so that
G(\lambda)x=0
and
y^{*}G(\lambda)=0
. Further, let

\mathbb{S}\subseteq{({\mathbb{C}}^{n,n})}^{d+m+1}
. Then

\displaystyle\kappa^{\mathbb{S}}(\lambda,G):

\displaystyle=\lim_{\epsilon\to 0}\sup\bigg\{\frac{|\Delta\lambda|}{\epsilon}:(G(\lambda+\Delta\lambda)+\Delta G(\lambda+\Delta\lambda))(x+\Delta x)=0,

\displaystyle\hskip 42.67912pt(\Delta_{A_{0}},\ldots,\Delta_{A_{d}},\Delta_{E_{1}},\ldots,\Delta_{E_{m}})\in\mathbb{S},\,\Delta G(z)=\sum_{k=0}^{d}z^{k}\Delta_{A_{k}}+\sum_{j=1}^{m}w_{j}(z)\Delta_{E_{j}},

\displaystyle\hskip 56.9055pt\|\Delta_{A_{k}}\|\leq\epsilon,~\|\Delta_{E_{j}}\|\leq\epsilon~~k=0,\ldots,d,~j=1,\ldots,m\bigg\}

(2.4)

is called the

of

and
\mathbb{S}
.

When
\mathbb{S}={({\mathbb{C}}^{n,n})}^{d+m+1}

and is denoted by

in (

Expanding the constraint

\Delta\lambda=-\frac{y^{*}\Delta G(\lambda)x}{y^{*}G^{\prime}(\lambda)x}+\mathcal{O}(\epsilon^{2}).

(2.5)

Note that,

\displaystyle\kappa^{\mathbb{S}}(\lambda,G):

\displaystyle=\lim_{\epsilon\to 0}\sup\bigg\{\frac{|y^{*}\Delta G(\lambda)x|}{\epsilon|y^{*}G^{\prime}(\lambda)x|}:(\Delta_{A_{0}},\ldots,\Delta_{A_{d}},\Delta_{E_{1}},\ldots,\Delta_{E_{m}})\in\mathbb{S},

\displaystyle\hskip 42.67912pt\,\Delta G(z)=\sum_{k=0}^{d}z^{k}\Delta_{A_{k}}+\sum_{j=1}^{m}w_{j}(z)\Delta_{E_{j}},~\|\Delta_{A_{k}}\|\leq\epsilon,~\|\Delta_{E_{j}}\|\leq\epsilon\bigg\}.

(2.6)

For the matrix polynomial case, that is, when

\kappa(\lambda,P)=\frac{\sum_{k=0}^{d}|\lambda|^{k}}{|y^{*}P^{\prime}(\lambda)x|}.

(2.7)

A similar expression can be obtained for the condition number
\kappa(\lambda,G)
for a RMF of the form (

). To see this, from (

\displaystyle\kappa(\lambda,G)

\displaystyle\leq\lim_{\epsilon\to 0}\sup\bigg\{\frac{\left(\sum_{k=0}^{d}|\lambda|^{k}\|\Delta_{A_{k}}\|+\sum_{j=1}^{m}|w_{j}(\lambda)|\|\Delta_{E_{j}}\|\right)\|x\|\|y\|}{\epsilon|y^{*}G^{\prime}(\lambda)x|}:\|\Delta_{A_{k}}\|\leq\epsilon,\|\Delta_{E_{j}}\|\leq\epsilon\bigg\}

where
\alpha=\sum_{k=0}^{d}|\lambda|^{k}+\sum_{j=1}^{m}|w_{j}(\lambda)|
, and

and

are unit norm vectors. This gives an upper bound to

, which is attained by perturbations,
\Delta_{A_{k}}=-\epsilon\text{sign}(\lambda^{k})M

and
\Delta_{E_{j}}=-\epsilon\text{sign}(w_{j}(\lambda))M
for

\displaystyle y^{*}\Delta G(\lambda)x

\displaystyle=\sum_{k=0}^{d}\lambda^{k}(-\epsilon\text{sign}(\lambda^{k}))+\sum_{j=1}^{m}w_{j}(\lambda)(-\epsilon\text{sign}(w_{j}(\lambda)))=-\epsilon\alpha,

which gives

3
Structured condition number

As mentioned in
[
4
]
, if the matrix function
G(z)
is structured, that is, the coefficient matrices
A_{k}
’s and
E_{j}
’s, and the scalar functions
w_{j}(z)
’s follow symmetry structures from Table
1
, then it is of practical interest to understand the sensitivity of
\lambda
with respect to structure-preserving perturbations. In this section, we consider the structured condition number
\kappa^{\mathbb{S}}(\lambda,G)
of a simple eigenvalue
\lambda
of
G(z)
with structures from Table
1
.

We aim at deriving necessary and sufficient conditions such that the unstructured and the structured condition number for a given simple eigenvalue
\lambda
are equal, that is,
\kappa(\lambda,G)=\kappa^{\mathbb{S}}(\lambda,G)
. For this, we give the following lemma, which is a generalized version of
[
4
, Lemma 3.2]
for the rational matrix functions and will be useful in characterizing the simple eigenvalues of
G(z)
with
\kappa(\lambda,G)=\kappa^{\mathbb{S}}(\lambda,G)
for various structures under consideration.

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
\mathbb{S}\subseteq{({\mathbb{C}}^{n,n})}^{d+m+1}
and let
G(z)
be a RMF of the form (
1.2
).
Let
(\lambda,x,y)
be an eigentriplet of
G(z)
such that
\lambda
satisfy the assumption (
2
), and
\|x\|=1
and
\|y\|=1
. If
\lambda\neq 0
, then
\kappa^{{\mathbb{S}}}(\lambda,G)=\kappa(\lambda,G)
if and only if there exists
(\Delta_{A_{0}},\ldots,\Delta_{A_{d}},\Delta_{E_{1}},\ldots,\Delta_{E_{m}})\in{{\mathbb{S}}}
such that
\Delta_{A_{k}}x=\omega\text{sign}(\lambda^{k})y
for
k=0,\ldots,d
and
\Delta_{E_{j}}x=\omega\text{sign}(w_{j}(\lambda))y
for
j=1,\ldots,m
, where
\omega\in\mathbb{C}
such that
|\omega|=1
.

If
\lambda=0
, then
\kappa^{{\mathbb{S}}}(0,G)=\kappa(0,G)
if and only if
(\Delta_{A_{0}},\ldots,\Delta_{A_{k}},\Delta_{E_{1}},\ldots,\Delta_{E_{m}})\in{\mathbb{S}}
such that
\Delta_{A_{0}}x=\omega y
and
\Delta_{E_{j}}x=\omega\text{sign}(w_{j}(\lambda))y
for
j=1,\ldots,m
.

Proof.

In view of (
2
), we have

\kappa^{\mathbb{S}}(\lambda,G)=\limsup_{\epsilon\to 0}\frac{|y^{\ast}\Delta G(\lambda)x|}{\epsilon|y^{\ast}G^{\prime}(\lambda)x|},

(3.1)

where
\Delta G(\lambda):=\sum_{k=0}^{d}\lambda^{k}\Delta_{A_{k}}+\sum_{j=1}^{m}w_{j}(\lambda)\Delta_{E_{j}}
is such that
(\Delta_{A_{0}},\ldots,\Delta_{A_{d}},\Delta_{E_{1}},\ldots,\Delta_{E_{m}})\in{{\mathbb{S}}}
and
\|\Delta_{A_{k}}\|\leq\epsilon
for
k=0,\ldots,d
and
\|\Delta_{E_{j}}\|\leq\epsilon
for
j=1,\ldots,m
.
By taking
\alpha_{k}=\frac{y^{*}\Delta_{A_{k}}x}{\epsilon}
and
\beta_{j}=\frac{y^{*}\Delta_{E_{j}}x}{\epsilon}
, we have

\kappa^{\mathbb{S}}(\lambda,G)=\limsup_{\epsilon\to 0}\frac{\big|\sum_{k=0}^{d}\alpha_{k}\lambda^{k}+\sum_{j=1}^{m}\beta_{j}w_{j}(\lambda)\big|}{|y^{\ast}G^{\prime}(\lambda)x|}.

(3.2)

Note that
|\alpha_{k}|=\big|\frac{y^{*}\Delta_{A_{k}}x}{\epsilon}\big|\leq\|y\|\|x\|=1
, as
\|\Delta_{A_{k}}\|\leq\epsilon
and
x,y
are unit norm vectors. Similarly, we have
|\beta_{j}|\leq 1
. This implies from (
3.2
) that

\kappa^{\mathbb{S}}(\lambda,G)\leq\frac{\sum_{k=0}^{d}|\lambda^{k}|+\sum_{j=1}^{m}|w_{j}(\lambda)|}{|y^{\ast}G^{\prime}(\lambda)x|}=\kappa(\lambda,G).

Thus, equality of
\kappa^{\mathbb{S}}(\lambda,G)
and
\kappa(\lambda,G)
holds if and only if there exists
\Delta G(\lambda)
with coefficient matrices

(\Delta_{A_{0}},\ldots,\Delta_{A_{d}},\Delta_{E_{1}},\ldots,\Delta_{E_{m}})\in{{\mathbb{S}}}
, such that

\|\Delta_{A_{k}}\|\leq 1
for
k=0,1,\ldots,d
,
\|\Delta_{E_{j}}\|\leq 1
for
j=1,\ldots,m
and
|y^{\ast}\Delta G(\lambda)x|=\sum_{k=0}^{d}|\lambda|^{k}
+
\sum_{j=1}^{m}|w_{j}(\lambda)|
. This further reduces to,

\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{d}|\lambda|^{k}+\sum_{j=1}^{m}|w_{j}(\lambda)|=|y^{\ast}\Delta G(\lambda)x|

\displaystyle=\big|\sum_{k=0}^{d}\lambda^{k}y^{*}\Delta_{A_{k}}\,x+\sum_{j=1}^{m}y^{*}\Delta_{E_{j}}\,x\,w_{j}(\lambda)\big|

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{k=0}^{d}|\lambda^{k}||y^{*}\Delta_{A_{k}}\,x|+\sum_{j=1}^{m}|y^{*}\Delta_{E_{j}}\,x||w_{j}(\lambda)|

\displaystyle\leq\sum_{k=0}^{d}|\lambda|^{k}+\sum_{j=1}^{m}|w_{j}(\lambda)|,

which implies that equality must hold at each step, i.e.,

\displaystyle|y^{*}\Delta_{A_{k}}x|=1~~\text{for}~k=0,\ldots,d,\quad|y^{*}\Delta_{E_{j}}x|=1,~\text{for}~~j=1,\ldots,m,

and

\big|\sum_{k=0}^{d}\lambda^{k}y^{*}\Delta_{A_{k}}\,x+\sum_{j=1}^{m}y^{*}\Delta_{E_{j}}\,x\,w_{j}(\lambda)\big|=\sum_{k=0}^{d}|\lambda^{k}y^{*}\Delta_{A_{k}}\,x|+\sum_{j=1}^{m}|w_{j}(\lambda)y^{*}\Delta_{E_{j}}\,x|.

This is possible if and only if
\lambda^{k}y^{*}\Delta_{A_{k}}x
for
k=0,\ldots,d
and
w_{j}(\lambda)y^{\ast}\Delta_{E_{j}}x
for
j=1,\ldots,m
are collinear, that is,

y^{\ast}\Delta_{A_{k}}x\lambda^{k}=a_{k}\,\omega\text{ and }y^{\ast}\Delta_{E_{j}}\,x\,w_{j}(\lambda)=e_{j}\,\omega,

(3.3)

for some
\omega\in{\mathbb{C}}
with
|\omega|=1
and
a_{k},e_{j}>0
. From (
3.3
), we have
a_{k}={|\lambda|}^{k}
and
e_{j}=|w_{j}(\lambda)|
, and thus

y^{\ast}\Delta_{A_{k}}x=\omega\frac{|\lambda|^{k}}{\lambda^{k}}=\omega\text{sign}(\lambda^{k})\quad\text{and}\quad y^{\ast}\Delta_{E_{j}}x=\omega\frac{|w_{j}(\lambda)|}{w_{j}(\lambda)}=\omega\text{sign}(w_{j}(\lambda)).

(3.4)

If
\lambda\neq 0
, using Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, (
3.4
) can be further reduced to

\Delta_{A_{k}}x=\omega\text{sign}((\lambda)^{k})y\text{ and }\Delta_{E_{j}}x=\omega\text{sign}(w_{j}(\lambda))y.

If
\lambda=0

\kappa^{{\mathbb{S}}}(0,G)=\kappa(0,G)
if and only if
\Delta_{A_{0}}x=\omega y\text{ and }\Delta_{E_{j}}x=\omega\text{sign}(w_{j}(\lambda))y
.
∎

3.1
Symmetric and skew-symmetric structures

In this section, we examine RMFs

Theorem 3.3
.

Let
G(z)
be a symmetric RMF of the form (
1.2
), and let
(\lambda,x,y)
be an eigentriplet of
G(z)
such that
\lambda
satisfy the assumption (
2
), and
\|x\|=1
and
\|y\|=1
. Then,

\kappa^{\rm sym}(\lambda,G)=\kappa(\lambda,G).

Proof.

If
\lambda\neq 0
, then by Lemma
3.1
,
\kappa^{{\rm sym}}(\lambda,G)=\kappa(\lambda,G)
if and only if there exists
(\Delta_{A_{0}},\ldots,\Delta_{A_{d}},\Delta_{E_{1}},\ldots,\Delta_{E_{m}})\in(\text{Sym}(n))^{d+m+1}

satisfying
\Delta_{A_{k}}x=\omega\text{sign}((\lambda)^{k})y\text{ and }\Delta_{E_{j}}x=\omega\text{sign}(w_{j}(\lambda))y
. In view of Lemma

, there always exist such symmetric matrices
\Delta_{A_{k}}
and
\Delta_{E_{j}}
. The case

∎

In the following result, we obtain structured condition number
\kappa^{\rm ssym}(\lambda,G)
, when

is skew-symmetric.

Theorem 3.4
.

Let
G(z)
be a skew-symmetric RMF and let
(\lambda,x,y)
be an eigentriplet of
G(z)
such that
\lambda
satisfy the assumption (

\kappa^{\rm ssym}(\lambda,G)=c\cdot\kappa(\lambda,G),

(3.5)

where

. In particular,

\kappa^{\rm ssym}(\lambda,G)=\kappa(\lambda,G)
if and only if
x^{T}y=0
.

Proof.

We first show that
\kappa^{\rm ssym}(\lambda,G)\leq c\cdot\kappa(\lambda,G)
. For this, in view of (
2
) and Theorem
2.3
, it is enough to show that

\sup\big\{\big|y^{*}(\sum_{k=0}^{d}\lambda^{k}\Delta_{A_{k}}+\sum_{j=1}^{m}w_{j}(\lambda)\Delta_{E_{j}})x\big|\big\}\leq c\cdot\alpha,

(3.6)

where supremum is taken over skew-symmetric matrices
\Delta_{A_{k}}
and
\Delta_{E_{j}}
such that
\|\Delta_{A_{k}}\|\leq 1,\|\Delta_{E_{j}}\|\leq 1
for
k=0,\ldots,d
,
j=1,\ldots,m
,
and
\alpha=\sum_{k=0}^{d}|\lambda|^{k}+\sum_{j=1}^{m}|w_{j}(\lambda)|
. In view of Theorem
3.2
, this is equivalent to show that

\sup_{\alpha_{k},\beta_{k},\delta_{j},\gamma_{j}\in{\mathbb{R}}}\left\{\big|\sum_{k=0}^{d}\lambda^{k}c(\alpha_{k}+i\beta_{k})+\sum_{j=1}^{m}w_{j}(\lambda)c(\delta_{j}+i\gamma_{j})\big|~:~\alpha_{k}^{2}+\beta_{k}^{2}\leq 1,~\delta_{j}^{2}+\gamma_{j}^{2}\leq 1\right\}\leq c\cdot\alpha,

(3.7)

which follows immediately by using the triangular inequality as

\displaystyle\Big|\sum_{k=0}^{d}\lambda^{k}c(\alpha_{k}+i\beta_{k})+\sum_{j=1}^{m}w_{j}(\lambda)c(\delta_{j}+i\gamma_{j})\Big|

\displaystyle\leq c\Big(\sum_{k=0}^{d}|\lambda|^{k}|\alpha_{k}+i\beta_{k}|+\sum_{j=1}^{m}|w_{j}(\lambda)||\delta_{j}+i\gamma_{j}|\Big)

\displaystyle\leq c\alpha.

This proves the inequality in (
3.5
). The equality in (
3.5
) holds, if we show that there exist skew-symmetric matrices
\hat{\Delta}_{A_{k}}
and
\hat{\Delta}_{E_{j}}
such that
\|\hat{\Delta}_{A_{k}}\|\leq 1,\|\hat{\Delta}_{E_{j}}\|\leq 1
and

\Big|y^{*}(\sum_{k=0}^{d}\lambda^{k}\hat{\Delta}_{A_{k}}+\sum_{j=1}^{m}w_{j}(\lambda)\hat{\Delta}_{E_{j}})x\Big|=c\alpha.

Define
\hat{\Delta}_{A_{k}}=\text{sign}(\lambda^{k})R
for
k=0,\ldots,d
and
\hat{\Delta}_{E_{j}}=\text{sign}(w_{j}(\lambda))R
, where
R
is a unit 2-norm skew-symmetric matrix satisfying

. Note that such a choice of

is possible by Theorem
3.2
. This implies that

\displaystyle\Big|y^{*}(\sum_{k=0}^{d}\lambda^{k}\hat{\Delta}_{A_{k}}+\sum_{j=1}^{m}w_{j}(\lambda)\hat{\Delta}_{E_{j}})x\Big|

\displaystyle=\Big|\sum_{k=0}^{d}\lambda^{k}y^{*}\hat{\Delta}_{A_{k}}x+\sum_{j=1}^{m}w_{j}(\lambda)y^{*}\hat{\Delta}_{E_{j}}x\Big|

\displaystyle=c\Big(\sum_{k=0}^{d}|\lambda|^{k}+\sum_{j=1}^{m}|w_{j}(\lambda)|\Big).

and hence the proof.
∎

.

or

\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\kappa(\lambda,G)\leq\kappa^{\rm Herm}(\lambda,G).

(3.8)

Proof.

First suppose that
\lambda\neq 0

\kappa^{\text{Herm}}(\lambda,G)=\kappa(\lambda,G)

such that
\|\Delta_{A_{k}}\|\leq 1
and
\|\Delta_{E_{j}}\|\leq 1

\displaystyle\Delta_{A_{k}}x=\omega\text{sign}(\lambda^{k})y,~\text{ for }k=0,\ldots,d\quad\text{and}\quad\Delta_{E_{j}}x=\omega\text{sign}(w_{j}(\lambda))y~\text{ for }j=1,\ldots,m,

for some

In view of Lemma
2.4
, there exist Hermitian matrices

\displaystyle\omega\text{sign}(\lambda^{k})x^{*}y\in{\mathbb{R}},~\text{ for }k=0,1,\ldots,d\quad\text{and}\quad\omega\text{sign}(w_{j}(\lambda))x^{*}y\in{\mathbb{R}},~\text{ for }j=1,\ldots,m.

On comparing
\arg(w)

) holds if and only if

or

(3.11)

since

, as

\lambda\in{\mathbb{R}}
.
This shows that for
\lambda\neq 0

(3.13)

where

, and the supremum is taken over

\in(\text{Herm}(n))^{d+m+1}

. In view of Theorem

\sup_{\alpha_{k},\beta_{k},\delta_{j},\gamma_{j}\in{\mathbb{R}}}\{|\sum_{k=0}^{d}\lambda^{k}(\alpha_{k}+ic\beta_{k})+\sum_{j=1}^{m}w_{j}(\lambda)(\delta_{j}+ic\gamma_{j})|~:~\alpha_{k}^{2}+\beta_{k}^{2}\leq 1,\delta_{j}^{2}+\gamma_{j}^{2}\leq 1\}\geq\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\alpha.

Let us define the set

\displaystyle S

\displaystyle:=\{s:s=(\alpha_{0},\alpha_{1},\ldots,\alpha_{d},\beta_{0},\beta_{1},\ldots,\beta_{d},\delta_{1},\ldots,\delta_{m},~\gamma_{1},\ldots,\gamma_{m})\in{\mathbb{R}}^{2(d+m)+2},

\displaystyle\sup_{s\in S}|H_{s}(\lambda)|

\displaystyle\geq\sum_{k=0}^{d}|\lambda|^{k}\max\{|\cos(k\theta)|,|\sin(k\theta)|\}+\sum_{j=1}^{m}|w_{j}(\lambda)|\max\{|\cos(\theta_{j})|,|\sin(\theta_{j})|\}

\kappa^{\rm pal_{T}}(\lambda,G)=\kappa(\lambda,G)

(3.36)

Proof.

Using Lemmas

\displaystyle\omega\text{sign}(w_{j}(\lambda))x^{*}y\in{\mathbb{R}}\quad\text{for}~~j=1,\ldots,m.

4
Conclusion

We have extended the framework presented in
[
4
]
to compute the structured condition number of RMFs
G(z)
of the form (
1.2
) for structures from Table
1
. We have derived the necessary and sufficient conditions for each structure considered, under which unstructured and structured condition numbers are equal. We have also obtained exact expression for the structured condition number of simple eigenvalues of symmetric, skew-symmetric, and T-even/odd rational matrix functions, and established tight bounds for Hermitian, skew-Hermitian,
*
-even/odd,
*
-palindromic and T-palindromic rational matrix functions. To our knowledge, no other work has been done in the literature to compute the condition number of RMFs with symmetry structures.

Future research may involve computing the condition number under general perturbations in
G(z)
, i.e., when the scalar functions
w_{j}
’s in (
2.2
) are also perturbed. Another possibility may involve examining other forms, such as the system matrix associated with the RMF, to compute the structured condition number.
