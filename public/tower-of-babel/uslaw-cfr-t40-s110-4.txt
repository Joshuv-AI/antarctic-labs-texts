40 C.F.R. § 110.4 — Chemical or biological agents.
Citation: us:cfr:40:110.4
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-110.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 110 (Discharge Of Oil). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The addition of any chemical or biological agent, or any other substance, to oil to be discharged that would circumvent the provisions of this part is prohibited.
