45 C.F.R. § 2531.20 — Funding priorities.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2531.20
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2531.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2531 (Purposes And Availability Of Grants For Investment For Quality And Innovation Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Corporation may choose to set priorities (and to periodically revise such priorities) that limit the types of innovative and model programs and support activities it will undertake or fund in a given fiscal year. In setting these priorities, the Corporation will seek to concentrate funds on those activities that will be most effective and efficient in fulfilling the purposes of this part.
