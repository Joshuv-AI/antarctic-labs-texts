27 C.F.R. § 28.149 — Losses.
Citation: us:cfr:27:28.149
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-28.149
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 28 (Exportation Of Alcohol). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When there has been a loss of beer or beer concentrate while in transit from the brewery to a port for exportation, or for lading as supplies on a vessel or aircraft, or to a foreign-trade zone, the provisions of subpart O of this part, with respect to losses are applicable.
