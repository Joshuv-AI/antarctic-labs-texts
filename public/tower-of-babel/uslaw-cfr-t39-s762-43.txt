39 C.F.R. § 762.43 — Issuance of substitute disbursement postal money order.
Citation: us:cfr:39:762.43
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-762.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 762 (Disbursement Postal Money Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon approval of the undertaking of indemnity, application, or statement of claim, the Money Order Division shall issue to the claimant a substitute Disbursement Postal Money Order showing such information as may be necessary to identify the original instrument.
