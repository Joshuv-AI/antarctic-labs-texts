46 U.S.C. § 6302 — Public investigations
Citation: us:usc:46:6302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section6302
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part D (Marine Casualties); Chapter 63 (Investigating Marine Casualties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each investigation conducted under this chapter and regulations prescribed under this chapter shall be open to the public, except when evidence affecting the national security is to be received.
