14 C.F.R. § 152.119 — Contract requirements and procurement standards.
Citation: us:cfr:14:152.119
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-152.119
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 152 (Airport Aid Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent applicable, all grant agreements, contracts, and subcontracts involving airport development projects or airport planning must be in accordance with the contract requirements in appendices A and C, as applicable, and the procurement standards in Attachment O of Office of Management and Budget Circular A-102 (42 FR 45828).
