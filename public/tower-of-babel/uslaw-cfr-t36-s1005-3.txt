36 C.F.R. § 1005.3 — Business operations.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1005.3
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1005.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1005 (Commercial And Private Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Engaging in or soliciting any business in the area administered by the Presidio Trust, except in accordance with the provisions of a permit, contract, or other written agreement with the United States, is prohibited.
