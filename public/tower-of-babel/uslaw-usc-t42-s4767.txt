42 U.S.C. § 4767 — Termination of grants
Citation: us:usc:42:4767
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4767
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 62 (Intergovernmental Personnel Program); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever the Office, after giving reasonable notice and opportunity for hearing to the State or general local government concerned, finds—
(1)
that a program or project has been so changed that it no longer complies with the provisions of this chapter; or
(2)
that in the operation of the program or project there is a failure to comply substantially with any such provision;
the Office shall notify the State or general local government of its findings and no further payments may be made to such government by the Office until it is satisfied that such noncompliance has been, or will promptly be, corrected. However, the Office may authorize the continuance of payments to those projects approved under this chapter which are not involved in the noncompliance.
