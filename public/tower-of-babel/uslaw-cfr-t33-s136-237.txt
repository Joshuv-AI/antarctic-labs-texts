33 C.F.R. § 136.237 — Authorized claimants.
Citation: us:cfr:33:136.237
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-136.237
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 136 (Oil Spill Liability Trust Fund; Claims Procedures; Designation Of Source; And Advertisement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim for net costs of providing increased or additional public services during or after removal activities, including protection from fire, safety, or health hazards, caused by a discharge of oil may be presented only by a State or a political subdivision of a State incurring the costs.
