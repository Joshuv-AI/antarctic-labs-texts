43 C.F.R. § 2866.41 — How will the BLM calculate rent for communications facilities ancillary to a linear grant or other use authorization?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2866.41
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2866.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2860 (Communications Uses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a communications facility is authorized as ancillary to (i.e., used for the sole purpose of internal communications) a grant or some other type of use authorization (e.g., a mineral lease or sundry notice), the BLM will determine the rent using the linear rent schedule (see § 2866.20) or rent scheme associated with the other authorization, and not the communications uses rent schedule.
