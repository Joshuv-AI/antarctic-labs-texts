14 C.F.R. § 147.3 — Certificate required.
Citation: us:cfr:14:147.3
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-147.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 147 (Aviation Maintenance Technician Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may operate an aviation maintenance technician school without, or in violation of, an aviation maintenance technician school certificate and the operations specifications issued under this part.
