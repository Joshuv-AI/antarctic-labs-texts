5 C.F.R. § 2634.1008 — Public access to a Certificate of Divestiture.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2634.1008
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2634.1008
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2634 (Executive Branch Financial Disclosure, Qualified Trusts, And Certificates Of Divestiture). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Certificate of Divestiture issued pursuant to the provisions of this subpart is available to the public in accordance with the rules of § 2634.603.
