5 U.S.C. § 6129 — Administration of leave and retirement provisions
Citation: us:usc:5:6129
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section6129
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart E (Attendance And Leave); Chapter 61 (Hours Of Work); Subchapter II (Flexible And Compressed Work Schedules). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of administering sections 6303(a), 6304, 6307(a) and (d), 6323, 6326, 6327, and 8339(m) of this title, in the case of an employee who is in any program under this subchapter, references to a day or workday (or to multiples or parts thereof) contained in such sections shall be considered to be references to 8 hours (or to the respective multiples or parts thereof).
