28 C.F.R. § 14.5 — Review by legal officers.
Citation: us:cfr:28:14.5
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-14.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 14 (Administrative Claims Under Federal Tort Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority to adjust, determine, compromise, and settle a claim under the provisions of section 2672 of title 28, United States Code, shall, if the amount of a proposed compromise, settlement, or award exceeds $5,000, be exercised by the head of an agency or his designee only after review by a legal officer of the agency.
