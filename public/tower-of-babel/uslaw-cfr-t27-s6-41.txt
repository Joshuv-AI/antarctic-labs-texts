27 C.F.R. § 6.41 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:27:6.41
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-6.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 6 (“Tied-House”). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to the exceptions listed in subpart D, the act by an industry member of furnishing, giving, renting, lending, or selling any equipment, fixtures, signs, supplies, money, services, or other things of value to a retailer constitutes a means to induce within the meaning of the Act.
