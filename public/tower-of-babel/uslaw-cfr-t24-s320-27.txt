24 C.F.R. § 320.27 — Mortgages.
Citation: us:cfr:24:320.27
Creator: Government National Mortgage Association, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-320.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 320 (Guaranty Of Mortgage-Backed Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Guaranteed securities issued under these provisions must be based on and backed by mortgages pooled under trust arrangements satisfactory to the Association. Such mortgages must meet the requirements of the Bond Guide.
