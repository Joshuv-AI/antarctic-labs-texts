48 C.F.R. § 970.2672-2 — 970.2672-2 Requirements.

Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-970.2672-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 970 (Doe Management And Operating Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements set forth in subpart 926.71, Implementation of Section 3161 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 1993, for contractors and subcontractors to provide a hiring preference for employees under Department of Energy contracts whose employment in positions at a Department of Energy Defense Nuclear Facility is terminated (except for a termination for cause) applies to management and operating contracts.
