14 C.F.R. § 137.55 — Business name: Commercial agricultural aircraft operator.
Citation: us:cfr:14:137.55
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-137.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 137 (Agricultural Aircraft Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may operate under a business name that is not shown on his commercial agricultural aircraft operator certificate.
