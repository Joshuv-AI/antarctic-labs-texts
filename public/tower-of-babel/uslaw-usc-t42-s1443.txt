42 U.S.C. § 1443 — Provisions as controlling over other laws
Citation: us:usc:42:1443
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1443
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 8A (Slum Clearance, Urban Renewal, And Farm Housing); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Insofar as the provisions of any other law are inconsistent with the provisions of this Act, the provisions of this Act shall be controlling.
