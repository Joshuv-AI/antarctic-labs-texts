47 C.F.R. § 5.123 — Inspection of stations.
Citation: us:cfr:47:5.123
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-5.123
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 5 (Experimental Radio Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All stations and records of stations in the authorized under this part shall be made available for inspection at any time while the station is in operation or shall be made available for inspection upon reasonable request of an authorized representative of the Commission.
