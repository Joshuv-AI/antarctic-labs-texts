14 C.F.R. § 385.30 — Persons who may petition for review.
Citation: us:cfr:14:385.30
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-385.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 385 (Staff Assignments And Review Of Action Under Assignments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Petitions for review may be filed by the applicant; by persons who have availed themselves of the opportunity, if any, to participate in the matter at the staff action level; and by persons who have not had opportunity to so participate or show good and sufficient cause for not having participated: Provided, That such persons, other than the applicant, disclose a substantial interest which would be adversely affected by the respective staff action.
