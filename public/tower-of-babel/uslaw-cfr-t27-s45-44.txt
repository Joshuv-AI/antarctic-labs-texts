27 C.F.R. § 45.44 — Notice for cigars.
Citation: us:cfr:27:45.44
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-45.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 45 (Removal Of Tobacco Products And Cigarette Papers And Tubes, Without Payment Of Tax, For Use Of The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before removal under this part, every package of cigars shall have adequately imprinted on it, or on a label securely affixed to it—
(a) The designation “cigars”;
(b) The quantity of cigars contained in the package; and
(c) For small cigars, the classification of the product for tax purposes (i.e., either “small” or “little”).
