12 U.S.C. § 7 — Chief of examining division
Citation: us:usc:12:7
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section7
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 1 (The Comptroller Of The Currency). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Comptroller of the Currency may designate a national bank examiner to act as chief of the examining division in his office.
