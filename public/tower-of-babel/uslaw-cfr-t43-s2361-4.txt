43 C.F.R. § 2361.4 — Responsibility.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2361.4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2361.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2360 (National Petroleum Reserve In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Consistent with the statutory requirements to conduct an expeditious program of oil and gas leasing, the Bureau of Land Management (BLM) is responsible for the management of the Reserve, the protection of surface values from environmental degradation, and to prepare rules and regulations necessary to carry out management and protection duties.
