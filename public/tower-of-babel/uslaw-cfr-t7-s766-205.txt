7 C.F.R. § 766.205 — Shared Appreciation Payment Agreement rates and terms.
Citation: us:cfr:7:766.205
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-766.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 766 (Direct Loan Servicing—Special). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The interest rate for Shared Appreciation Payment Agreements is the Agency's SA amortization rate.
(b) The term of the Shared Appreciation Payment Agreement is based on the borrower's repayment ability and the useful life of the security. The term will not exceed 25 years.
