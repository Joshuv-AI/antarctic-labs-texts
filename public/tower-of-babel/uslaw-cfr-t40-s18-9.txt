40 C.F.R. § 18.9 — Duration of Environmental Protection Research Fellowships.
Citation: us:cfr:40:18.9
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-18.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 18 (Environmental Protection Research Fellowships And Special Research Consultants For Environmental Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Initial appointments to Environmental Protection Research fellowships may be made for varying periods not in excess of 5 years. Such an appointment may be extended for varying periods not in excess of 5 years for each period in accordance with procedures and requirements established by the Administrator or designee.
