19 C.F.R. § 165.47 — Potential penalties and other actions.
Citation: us:cfr:19:165.47
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-165.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 165 (Investigation Of Claims Of Evasion Of Antidumping And Countervailing Duties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CBP and other government agencies reserve the right to undertake additional investigations or enforcement actions in cases covered by these provisions. Nothing within this part prevents CBP from assessing penalties of any sort related to such cases or taking action under any other relevant laws.
