28 U.S.C. § 1822 — Competency of interested persons; share of penalties payable
Citation: us:usc:28:1822
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1822
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 119 (Evidence; Witnesses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person interested in a share of any fine, penalty or forfeiture incurred under any Act of Congress, may be examined as a witness in any proceeding for the recovery of such fine, penalty or forfeiture by any party thereto. Such examination shall not deprive the witness of his share.
