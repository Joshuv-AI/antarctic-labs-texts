20 U.S.C. § 9132 — Administration
Citation: us:usc:20:9132
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section9132
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 72 (Museum And Library Services); Subchapter II (Library Services And Technology); Part 1 (Basic Program Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Not more than 4 percent of the total amount of funds received under this subchapter for any fiscal year by a State may be used for administrative costs.
(b) Construction
Nothing in this section shall be construed to limit spending for evaluation costs under section 9134(c) of this title from sources other than this subchapter.
