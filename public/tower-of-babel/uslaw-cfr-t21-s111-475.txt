21 C.F.R. § 111.475 — Under this subpart M, what records must you make and keep?
Citation: us:cfr:21:111.475
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-111.475
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 111 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice In Manufacturing, Packaging, Labeling, Or Holding Operations For Dietary Supplements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must make and keep records required under this subpart M in accordance with subpart P of this part.
(b) You must make and keep the following records:
(1) Written procedures for holding and distributing operations; and
(2) Records of product distribution.
