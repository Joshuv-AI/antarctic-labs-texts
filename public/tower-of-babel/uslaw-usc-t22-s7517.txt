22 U.S.C. § 7517 — Relationship to other authority
Citation: us:usc:22:7517
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section7517
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 82 (Afghanistan Freedom Support); Subchapter I (Economic And Democratic Development Assistance For Afghanistan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authority to provide assistance under this subchapter is in addition to any other authority to provide assistance to the Government of Afghanistan.
