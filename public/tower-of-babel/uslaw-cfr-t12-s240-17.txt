12 C.F.R. § 240.17 — Reservation of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:12:240.17
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-240.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 240 (Retail Foreign Exchange Transactions (Regulation Nn)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may modify the disclosure, recordkeeping, capital and margin, reporting, business conduct, documentation, or other standards or requirements under this part for a specific retail forex transaction or a class of retail forex transactions if the Board determines that the modification is consistent with safety and soundness and the protection of retail forex customers.
