Formations of, Acquisitions by, and Mergers of Bank Holding

Correction

    This corrects notice FR Doc. 2026-11203. Paragraph 1 is corrected 
to read:
    1. Mascoma Mutual Financial Services Corporation, (``Mascoma''), 
Lebanon, New Hampshire; to merge with Androscoggin Bancorp, MHC, and 
thereby indirectly acquire Androscoggin Bancorp, Inc., and Androscoggin 
Savings Bank, all of Lewiston, Maine. In addition, Mascoma, through the 
acquisition of Portland Trust Company, LLC, Portland, Maine, would 
engage in providing trust company functions pursuant to section 
225.28(b)(5) of the Board's Regulation Y.
