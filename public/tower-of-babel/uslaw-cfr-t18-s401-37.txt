18 C.F.R. § 401.37 — Sequence of approval.
Citation: us:cfr:18:401.37
Creator: Delaware River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-401.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 401 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A project will be considered by the Commission under Section 3.8 of the Compact either before or after any other state or federal review, in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative Agreement applicable to such project.
