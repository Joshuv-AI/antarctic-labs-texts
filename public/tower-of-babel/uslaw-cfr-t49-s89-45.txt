49 C.F.R. § 89.45 — Department determination.

Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-89.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 89 (Implementation Of The Federal Claims Collection Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Following review of the information, the Department notifies the debtor with a written decision that includes the supporting rationale.
(b) If the Department either sustains or amends its determination, it shall notify the debtor that the debt is being referred to the IRS for offset against the debtor's Federal income tax refund. If the Department determines that there is no legally enforceable debt or that full payment has been made, the case will be closed.
