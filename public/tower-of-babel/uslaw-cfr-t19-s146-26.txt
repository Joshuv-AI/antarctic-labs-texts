19 C.F.R. § 146.26 — System review.
Citation: us:cfr:19:146.26
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-146.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 146 (Foreign Trade Zones). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The operator shall perform an annual internal review of the inventory control and recordkeeping system and shall report to the port director any deficiency discovered and corrective action taken, to ensure that the system meets the requirements of this part.
