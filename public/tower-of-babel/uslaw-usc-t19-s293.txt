19 U.S.C. § 293 — Documented vessels touching at foreign ports
Citation: us:usc:19:293
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section293
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 3 (The Tariff And Related Provisions); Part 2 (Report, Entry, And Unlading Of Vessels And Vehicles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any United States documented vessel with a registry or coastwise endorsement, or both, may engage in trade between one port in the United States and one or more ports within the same, with the privilege of touching at one or more foreign ports during the voyage, and land and take in thereat merchandise, passengers and their baggage, and letters, and mails.
