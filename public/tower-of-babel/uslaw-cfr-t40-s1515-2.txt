40 C.F.R. § 1515.2 — What kind of records does CEQ maintain?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1515.2
Creator: Council On Environmental Quality
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1515.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1515 (Freedom Of Information Act Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CEQ carries out responsibilities under the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969, as amended (42 U.S.C. 4321-4347); the Environmental Quality Improvement Act of 1970, as amended (42 U.S.C. 4371-4375); Reorganization Plan No. 1 of 1977 (July 15, 1977); and various Executive orders, among other authorities. CEQ maintains certain records on these subjects, among others.
