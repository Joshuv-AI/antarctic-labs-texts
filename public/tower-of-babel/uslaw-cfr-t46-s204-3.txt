46 C.F.R. § 204.3 — Claims not payable.
Citation: us:cfr:46:204.3
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-204.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 204 (Claims Against The Maritime Administration Under The Federal Tort Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim is not payable under the regulations in this part 204, if such tort claim is excluded from the scope of the Federal Tort Claims Act, as amended, pursuant to 28 U.S.C. 2680.
