41 C.F.R. § 105-74.510 — -74.510 What actions will the Federal Government take against a recipient determined to have violated this part?
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-74.510
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-74.510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-74 (74—Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a recipient is determined to have violated this part, as described in § 105-74.500 or § 105-74.505, the General Services Administration may take one or more of the following actions—
(a) Suspension of payments under the award;
(b) Suspension or termination of the award; and
(c) Suspension or debarment of the recipient under 41 CFR part 105-68, for a period not to exceed five years.
