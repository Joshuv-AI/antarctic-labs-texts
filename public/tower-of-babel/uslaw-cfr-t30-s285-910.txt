30 C.F.R. § 285.910 — What must I do when I remove my facility?
Citation: us:cfr:30:285.910
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-285.910
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 285 (Renewable Energy And Alternate Uses Of Existing Facilities On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must remove all facilities to a depth of 15 feet below the mudline, unless otherwise authorized by BSEE.
(b) Within 60 days after you remove a facility, you must verify to BSEE that you have cleared the site.
