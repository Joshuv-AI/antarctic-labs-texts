20 U.S.C. § 9518 — Agreements
Citation: us:usc:20:9518
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section9518
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 76 (Education Research, Statistics, Evaluation, Information, And Dissemination); Subchapter I (Education Sciences Reform); Part A (The Institute Of Education Sciences). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Institute may carry out research projects of common interest with entities such as the National Science Foundation and the Eunice Kennedy Shriver National Institute of Child Health and Human Development through agreements with such entities that are in accordance with section 1231 of this title.
