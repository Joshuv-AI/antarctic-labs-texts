Correction: Gender differences in autonomic and psychological stress responses among educators: a heart rate variability and psychological assessment study
Authors: Andrea Calderón-García, Estela Alvarez-Gallardo, Pedro Belinchón-deMiguel, Vicente Javier Clemente-Suárez
Published: 2026

The Ethics statement was erroneously given as: “The studies involving human/animal participants were reviewed and approved by the European University of Madrid, Spain, ethics Committee under code CIPI/213006.55.”

The correct Ethics statement is: “The studies involving human participants were reviewed and approved by the European University of Madrid, Spain, Ethics Committee under code CIPI/22.178.”

A correction has been made to section 2 Methodology, sub-section 2.1 Design, setting, and participants, last paragraph:

“The study was approved by the European University of Madrid, Spain, Ethics Committee under code CIPI/22.178.”

The original version of this article has been updated.
