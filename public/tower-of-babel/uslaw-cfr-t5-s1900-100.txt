5 C.F.R. § 1900.100 — Cross-references to employee ethical conduct standards and financial disclosure regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1900.100
Creator: Appalachian Regional Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1900.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1900 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Officers and employees of the Appalachian Regional Commission Federal Staff are subject to the Standards of Ethical Conduct for Employees of the Executive Branch at 5 CFR part 2635 and the executive branch-wide financial disclosure regulations at 5 CFR part 2634.
