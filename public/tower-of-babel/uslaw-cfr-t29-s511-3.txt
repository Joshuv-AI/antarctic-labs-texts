29 C.F.R. § 511.3 — Composition and appointment of committees.
Citation: us:cfr:29:511.3
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-511.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 511 (Wage Order Procedure For American Samoa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An industry committee will be composed of residents of American Samoa and residents of the United States outside of American Samoa. The Secretary will appoint as members of each committee an equal number of persons representing:
(a) The public,
(b) Employees in the industry, and
(c) Employers in the industry.
