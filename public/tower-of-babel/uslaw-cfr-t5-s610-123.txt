5 C.F.R. § 610.123 — Travel on official time.
Citation: us:cfr:5:610.123
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-610.123
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 610 (Hours Of Duty). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Insofar as practicable travel during nonduty hours shall not be required of an employee. When it is essential that this be required and the employee may not be paid overtime under § 550.112(e) of this chapter the official concerned shall record his reasons for ordering travel at those hours and shall, upon request, furnish a copy of his statement to the employee concerned.
