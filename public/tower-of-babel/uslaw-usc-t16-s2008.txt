16 U.S.C. § 2008 — Utilization of available information and data
Citation: us:usc:16:2008
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section2008
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 40 (Soil And Water Resources Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the implementation of this chapter, the Secretary shall utilize information and data available from other Federal, State, tribal, and local governments, and private organizations and he shall coordinate his actions with the resource appraisal and planning efforts of other Federal agencies and avoid unnecessary duplication and overlap of planning and program efforts.
