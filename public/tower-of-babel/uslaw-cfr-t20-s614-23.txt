20 C.F.R. § 614.23 — Finality of findings.
Citation: us:cfr:20:614.23
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-614.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 614 (Unemployment Compensation For Ex-Servicemembers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The findings of a Federal military agency referred to in §§ 614.21 and 614.22, and the Schedules of Remuneration issued by the Department pursuant to the Act and § 614.12, shall be final and conclusive for all purposes of the UCX Program, including appeal and review pursuant to § 614.7 or § 614.17.
