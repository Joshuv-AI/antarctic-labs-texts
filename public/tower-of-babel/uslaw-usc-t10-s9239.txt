10 U.S.C. § 9239 — Enlisted members: officers not to use as servants
Citation: us:usc:10:9239
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9239
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 929 (Miscellaneous Prohibitions And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No officer of the Air Force or the Space Force may use an enlisted member of the Air Force or the Space Force as a servant.
