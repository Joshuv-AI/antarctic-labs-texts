45 C.F.R. § 1357.20 — Child abuse and neglect programs.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1357.20
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1357.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1357 (Requirements Applicable To Title Iv-B). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State agency must assure that, with regard to any child abuse and neglect programs or projects funded under title IV-B of the Act, the requirements of section 106(b)(1) and (2) of the Child Abuse Prevention and Treatment Act, as amended, are met. These requirements relate to the State plan and assurances required for the Child Abuse and Neglect State Grant Program.
