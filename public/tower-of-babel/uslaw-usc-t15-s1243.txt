15 U.S.C. § 1243 — Manufacture, sale, or possession within specific jurisdictions; penalty
Citation: us:usc:15:1243
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1243
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 29 (Manufacture, Transportation, Or Distribution Of Switchblade Knives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, within any Territory or possession of the United States, within Indian country (as defined in section 1151 of title 18), or within the special maritime and territorial jurisdiction of the United States (as defined in section 7 of title 18), manufactures, sells, or possesses any switchblade knife, shall be fined not more than $2,000 or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.
