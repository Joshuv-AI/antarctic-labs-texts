46 U.S.C. § 4501 — Application
Citation: us:usc:46:4501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section4501
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Inspection And Regulation Of Vessels); Chapter 45 (Uninspected Commercial Fishing Industry Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
This chapter applies to an uninspected vessel which is a fishing vessel, fish processing vessel, or fish tender vessel.
(b)
This chapter does not apply to the carriage of bulk dangerous cargoes regulated under chapter 37 of this title.
