49 C.F.R. § 1511.1 — Applicability and purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1511.1
Creator: Transportation Security Administration, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1511.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1511 (Aviation Security Infrastructure Fee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part prescribes the imposition of a fee on air carriers and foreign air carriers in air transportation to pay for the costs of providing U.S. civil aviation security services as described in 49 U.S.C. 44940.
(b) For purposes of this part, the fee will be described as the “Aviation Security Infrastructure Fee.”
