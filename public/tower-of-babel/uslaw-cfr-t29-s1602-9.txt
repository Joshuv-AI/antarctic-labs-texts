29 C.F.R. § 1602.9 — Commission's remedy for employer's failure to file report.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1602.9
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1602.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1602 (Recordkeeping And Reporting Requirements Under Title Vii, The Ada, Gina, And The Pwfa). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any employer failing or refusing to file Report EEO-1 when required to do so may be compelled to file by order of a U.S. District Court, upon application of the Commission.
