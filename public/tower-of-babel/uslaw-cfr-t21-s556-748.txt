21 C.F.R. § 556.748 — Tylvalosin.
Citation: us:cfr:21:556.748
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-556.748
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 556 (Tolerances For Residues Of New Animal Drugs In Food). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Acceptable daily intake (ADI). The ADI for total residues of tylvalosin is 47.7 µg/kg of body weight per day.
(b) Tolerances. A tolerance for tylvalosin in edible tissues of swine is not required.
(c) Related conditions of use. See §§ 520.2645 and 558.633 of this chapter.
