30 C.F.R. § 27.30 — Inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:30:27.30
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-27.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 27 (Methane-Monitoring Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A detailed inspection shall be made by MSHA of the equipment and all components and functions related to safety in operation, which shall include:
(a) Examining materials, workmanship, and design to determine conformance with paragraph (a) of § 27.20.
(b) Comparing components and subassemblies with the drawings and specifications to verify conformance with the requirements of this part.
