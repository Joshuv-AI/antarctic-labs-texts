49 C.F.R. § 585.2 — Phase-in reports.
Citation: us:cfr:49:585.2
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-585.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 585 (Phase-In Reporting Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each report submitted to NHTSA under this part shall:
(a) Identify the manufacturer;
(b) State the full name, title, and address of the official responsible for preparing the report;
(c) Identify the production year being reported on;
(d) Contain a statement regarding whether or not the manufacturer complied with the requirements of the Federal motor vehicle safety standard addressed by the report, for the period covered by the report, and the basis for that statement;
(e) Be written in the English language; and
(f) Be submitted to: Administrator, National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, 1200 New Jersey Avenue SE, Washington, DC 20590.
