46 C.F.R. § 52.01-40 — -40 Materials and workmanship.
Citation: us:cfr:46:52.01-40
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-52.01-40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 52 (Power Boilers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All materials to be used in any of the work specified in the various sections of this part must be free from injurious defects and must have a workmanlike finish. The construction work must be executed in a workmanlike manner with proper tools or equipment and must be free from defects which would impair strength or durability.
