10 U.S.C. § 8667 — Changing category or type: limitations
Citation: us:usc:10:8667
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8667
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 863 (Naval Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unless they have been specifically made available for the purpose, funds appropriated for the repair or alteration of naval vessels may not be used to make repairs or alterations of any vessel that would change its category or type.
