37 C.F.R. § 2.43 — Service mark.
Citation: us:cfr:37:2.43
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-2.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 2 (Rules Of Practice In Trademark Cases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In an application to register a service mark, the application shall specify and contain all the elements required by the preceding sections for trademarks, but shall be modified to relate to services instead of to goods wherever necessary.
