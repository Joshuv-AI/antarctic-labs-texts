7 C.F.R. § 1437.404 — Information collection requirements under the Paperwork Reduction Act; OMB control number.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1437.404
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1437.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1437 (Noninsured Crop Disaster Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An agency may not conduct or sponsor, and a person is not required to respond to, a collection of information unless it displays a currently valid OMB control number. The OMB control number for the regulation in this part is 0560-0175.
