19 U.S.C. § 1887 — Limitation on imports under section 624 of title 7
Citation: us:usc:19:1887
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1887
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 7 (Trade Expansion Program); Subchapter II (Trade Agreements); Part VI (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing contained in this chapter shall be construed to affect in any way the provisions of section 624 of title 7, or to apply to any import restriction heretofore or hereafter imposed under such section.
