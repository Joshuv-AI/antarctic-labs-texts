37 C.F.R. § 43.6 — Controlling legal authority; no unwritten or non-public binding policy or guidance.
Citation: us:cfr:37:43.6
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-43.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 43 (Decision Circulation And Review Within The Patent Trial And Appeal Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any other provision of this part, all decisions of the Board are expected to comport with all applicable statutes, regulations, binding case law, and written Office policy and guidance applicable to Board proceedings. There shall be no unwritten Office or Board policy or guidance that is binding on any panel of the Board. All written policy and guidance binding on panels of the Board shall be made public.
