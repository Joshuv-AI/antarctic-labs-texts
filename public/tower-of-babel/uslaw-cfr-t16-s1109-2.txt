16 C.F.R. § 1109.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1109.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1109.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1109 (Conditions And Requirements For Relying On Component Part Testing Or Certification, Or Another Party'S Finished Product Testing Or Certification, To Meet Testing And Certification Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to set forth the conditions and requirements under which passing component part test reports, certification of component parts of consumer products, or finished product testing or certification procured or issued by another party, can be used to meet, in whole or in part, the testing and certification requirements of sections 14(a) and 14(i) of the CPSA.
