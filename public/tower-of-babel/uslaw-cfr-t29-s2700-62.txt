29 C.F.R. § 2700.62 — Name of miner witness.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2700.62
Creator: Federal Mine Safety And Health Review Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2700.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2700 (Procedural Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An ALJ shall not, until 2 days before a hearing, disclose or order a person to disclose to an operator or its agent the name of a miner who is expected by the ALJ to testify or whom a party expects to summon or call as a witness.
