42 U.S.C. § 7382e — Waste management education research consortium (WERC)

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7382e
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter XIV (Albert Einstein Distinguished Educator Fellowships). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) 1 In general
The Secretary is authorized to establish a partnership of Department of Energy laboratories, academic institutions, and private sector industries to conduct environmentally-related education programs, including programs involving environmentally conscious manufacturing and waste management activities that have undergraduate and graduate educational training as a component.
