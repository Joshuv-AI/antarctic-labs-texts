7 C.F.R. § 1735.40 — General.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1735 (General Policies, Types Of Loans, Loan Requirements—Telecommunications Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Terms and conditions of loans are set forth in a mortgage, note, and loan contract. Provisions of the mortgage and loan contract are implemented by provisions in RUS Bulletins and Regulations. Forms of the mortgage, note, and loan contract can be obtained from RUS.
