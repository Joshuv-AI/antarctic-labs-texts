40 C.F.R. § 222.5 — Time and place of hearings.
Citation: us:cfr:40:222.5
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-222.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 222 (Action On Ocean Dumping Permit Applications Under Section 102 Of The Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Hearings shall be held in the State in closest proximity to the proposed dump site, whenever practicable, and shall be set for the earliest practicable date no less than 30 days after the receipt of an appropriate request for a hearing or a determination by the Administrator or the Regional Administrator, as the case may be, to hold such a hearing without such a request.
