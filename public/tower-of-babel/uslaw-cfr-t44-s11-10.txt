44 C.F.R. § 11.10 — Scope of regulation.
Citation: us:cfr:44:11.10
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-11.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 11 (Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This regulation applies to claims asserted under the Federal Tort Claims Act against the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA). It does not include any contractor with FEMA.
