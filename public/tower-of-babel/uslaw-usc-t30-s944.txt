30 U.S.C. § 944 — Statement of reasons for denial of claim
Citation: us:usc:30:944
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section944
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 22 (Mine Safety And Health); Subchapter IV (Black Lung Benefits); Part C (Claims For Benefits After December 31, 1973). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any individual whose claim for benefits under this subchapter is denied shall receive from the Secretary a written statement of the reasons for denial of such claim, and a summary of the administrative hearing record or, upon good cause shown, a copy of any transcript thereof.
