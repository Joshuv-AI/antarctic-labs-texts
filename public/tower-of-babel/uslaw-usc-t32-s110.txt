32 U.S.C. § 110 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:32:110
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title32-section110
Context: United States Code, Title 32 (National Guard); Chapter 1 (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall prescribe regulations, and issue orders, necessary to organize, discipline, and govern the National Guard.
