Squares of subcubic planar graphs without cycles of length 4–8 are 6-choosable
Seog-Jin Kim, Rong Luo

Abstract
The square of a graph G G , denoted G 2 G^{2} , has the same vertex set as G G and an edge between any two vertices at distance at most 2 2 in G G . Wegner (1977) conjectured that for a planar graph G G , χ ⁡ ( G 2 ) ≤ 7 \chi(G^{2})\leq 7 if Δ ⁡ ( G ) = 3 \Delta(G)=3 , χ ⁡ ( G 2 ) ≤ Δ ⁡ ( G ) + 5 \chi(G^{2})\leq\Delta(G)+5 if 4 ≤ Δ ⁡ ( G ) ≤ 7 4\leq\Delta(G)\leq 7 , and χ ⁡ ( G 2 ) ≤ ⌊ 3 ​ Δ ​ ( G ) / 2 ⌋ \chi(G^{2})\leq\lfloor 3\Delta(G)/2\rfloor if Δ ⁡ ( G ) ≥ 8 \Delta(G)\geq 8 , and Thomassen (2018) confirmed the conjecture for Δ ⁡ ( G ) = 3 \Delta(G)=3 . Dvořák et al. (2008) and Feder et al. (2021) further conjectured that χ ⁡ ( G 2 ) ≤ 6 \chi(G^{2})\leq 6 for cubic bipartite planar graphs. A natural question is whether this bound also holds for the list-chromatic number, i.e., whether χ ℓ ​ ( G 2 ) ≤ 6 \chi_{\ell}(G^{2})\leq 6 for such graphs. More generally, it is of interest to determine sufficient conditions ensuring χ ℓ ​ ( G 2 ) ≤ 6 \chi_{\ell}(G^{2})\leq 6 for subcubic planar graphs. In this paper, we prove that χ ℓ ​ ( G 2 ) ≤ 6 \chi_{\ell}(G^{2})\leq 6 for subcubic planar graphs containing no k k -cycles for 4 ≤ k ≤ 8 4\leq k\leq 8 , improving a result of Cranston and Kim (2008). Introduction

The
square
of a graph
G
, denoted
G^{2}
, is obtained by adding an edge between any two vertices of
G
that are at distance at most
2
, while keeping the vertex set unchanged. A graph is

if its maximum degree
\Delta(G)
is at most
3
, and a cycle of length
k
is called a
k
-cycle
. The
girth
of
G
, denoted
g(G)
, is the length of its shortest cycle.

Graph coloring and, in particular, coloring squares of graphs has received considerable attention. Let
\chi(G)
denote the
chromatic number
of
G
. A

for
G
assigns to each vertex a list of colors, and
G
is
L
-colorable
if it admits a proper coloring

with
f(v)\in L(v)
for all

. If
G
is
L
-colorable whenever each list has size at least
k
, then
G
is
k
-choosable
, and the
list chromatic number

\chi_{\ell}(G)
is the smallest such
k
. Let
x
be a vertex in
G
. Denote by
N_{G}(x)
the set of vertices adjacent to
x
.

One of the most well-known open problems in this area is Wegner’s conjecture:

Conjecture 1

(
[
15
]
)
.

For a planar graph
G
,

\chi(G^{2})\leq\begin{cases}7,&\text{if }\Delta(G)=3,\\
\Delta(G)+5,&\text{if }4\leq\Delta(G)\leq 7,\\
\lfloor 3\Delta(G)/2\rfloor,&\text{if }\Delta(G)\geq 8.\end{cases}

The case
\Delta(G)=3
was confirmed by Thomassen
[
13
]
and independently by Hartke, Jahanbekam, and Thomas
[
8
]
using extensive computer-assisted case analysis. The conjecture for
\Delta(G)\geq 4
remains open, with recent progress on subcubic planar graphs documented in
[
4
,
9
,
10
]
.

Another closely related conjecture concerns cubic bipartite planar graphs:

Conjecture 2

(
[
6
,
7
]
)
.

If
G
is a cubic bipartite planar graph, then
\chi(G^{2})\leq 6
.

This bound is tight, as exemplified by the hexagonal prism. Feder et al.
[
7
]
verified Conjecture
2
in special cases, showing that
\chi(G^{2})\leq 6
if the faces of a bipartite cubic plane graph can be three-colored red, blue, and green such that red faces have even size and blue and green faces have sizes divisible by four. In particular, this implies
\chi(G^{2})\leq 6
if all face sizes are multiples of four.

It is natural to extend these questions to list colorings:

Question 3
.

Does
\chi_{\ell}(G^{2})\leq 6
hold for cubic bipartite planar graphs?

Currently, it is unknown even whether the square of a cubic bipartite planar graph is 7-choosable. More generally, identifying sufficient conditions ensuring
\chi_{\ell}(G^{2})\leq 6
for subcubic planar graphs is an interesting direction.

This line of research echoes the approach taken for Steinberg’s conjecture, which stated that planar graphs without 4- or 5-cycles are 3-colorable. Though disproved by Cohen-Addad et al.
[
14
]
, it inspired the question suggested by Erdős (1991) on the minimum
k
such that planar graphs without cycles of lengths
\{4,\dots,k\}
are 3-colorable. Borodin et al.
[
3
]
, improving earlier results
[
1
,
11
]
, showed that
k\leq 7
, i.e., planar graphs with no cycles of lengths
4
through
7
are
3
-colorable.

Following this perspective, one can ask:

Problem 4
.

Determine the smallest
k
such that
\chi_{\ell}(G^{2})\leq 6
whenever
G
is a subcubic planar graph with no cycles of lengths
\{4,\dots,k\}
.

Cranston and Kim
[
5
]
showed that
\chi_{\ell}(G^{2})\leq 6
for subcubic planar graphs of girth at least 9. In this paper, we improve upon their result and resolve Problem
4
with the following theorem:

Theorem 5
.

If
G
is a subcubic planar graph containing no
k
-cycles for
4\leq k\leq 8
, then
\chi_{\ell}(G^{2})\leq 6
.

The
graph polynomial
of a graph
G
is defined by

P_{G}(\bm{x})=\prod_{u\sim v,\;u<v}(x_{u}-x_{v}),

where
u\sim v
means
u
and
v
are adjacent, and

\bm{x}=(x_{v})_{v\in V(G)}
is a vector of variables indexed by vertices of
G
.

Theorem 21

(
[
2
]
)
.

(Combinatorial Nullstellensatz)

Let
\mathbb{F}
be a field, and let

f(x_{1},\ldots,x_{n})\in\mathbb{F}[x_{1},\ldots,x_{n}]
.
Suppose
\deg(f)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}t_{i}
with each
t_{i}\geq 0
, and the coefficient of

\prod_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}^{t_{i}}
is nonzero.
If
S_{1},\ldots,S_{n}\subseteq\mathbb{F}
satisfy
|S_{i}|\geq t_{i}+1
for all
i
,
then there exist
s_{i}\in S_{i}
such that

f(s_{1},\ldots,s_{n})\neq 0
.

The following consequence is frequently used in list coloring.

Theorem 22
.

Let
G
be a graph with
V(G)=\{v_{1},\ldots,v_{n}\}
and list assignment
L
.
If
P_{G}(\bm{x})
has a monomial
\prod_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}^{t_{i}}
with nonzero coefficient and

|L(v_{i})|\geq t_{i}+1
for each
i
, then
G
is
L
-colorable.
