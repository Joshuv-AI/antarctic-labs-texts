38 U.S.C. § 1838 — Biennial report
Citation: us:usc:38:1838
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1838
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 18 (Benefits For Children Of Vietnam Veterans And Certain Other Veterans); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not less frequently than once every two years, the Secretary shall submit to Congress a report setting forth the following:
(1)
The number of covered children receiving health care or benefits under this chapter as of the date on which the report is submitted.
(2)
The number of covered children for which the Department is paying for or providing a social worker as of such date.
(3)
Metrics on outreach conducted under section 1836(b) of this title.
