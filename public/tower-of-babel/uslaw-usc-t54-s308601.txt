54 U.S.C. § 308601 — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3086 (United States African-American Burial Grounds Preservation Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1) Burial ground.—
The term “burial ground” means any natural or prepared physical location, whether originally below, on, or above the surface of the earth, into which human remains are deposited as a part of the death rite or ceremony of a culture.
(2) Historic.—
The term “historic”, with respect to a property, means a property that can reasonably be considered to date back at least 50 years.
(3) Program.—
The term “Program” means the United States African-American Burial Grounds Preservation Program established under section 308602(a).
