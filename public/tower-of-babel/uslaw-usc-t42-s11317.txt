42 U.S.C. § 11317 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:42:11317
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11317
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 119 (Homeless Assistance); Subchapter II (United States Interagency Council On Homelessness). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this subchapter:
(1)
The term “Council” means the United States Interagency Council on Homelessness established in section 11311 of this title.
(2)
The term “Federal agency” has the meaning given the term “agency” in section 551(1) of title 5.
