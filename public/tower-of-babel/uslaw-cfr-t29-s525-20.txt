29 C.F.R. § 525.20 — Relation to other laws.
Citation: us:cfr:29:525.20
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-525.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 525 (Employment Of Workers With Disabilities Under Special Certificates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No provision of these regulations, or of any special minimum wage certificate issued thereunder, shall excuse noncompliance with any other Federal or State law or municipal ordinance establishing higher standards.
