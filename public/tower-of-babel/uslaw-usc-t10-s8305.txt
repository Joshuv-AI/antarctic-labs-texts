10 U.S.C. § 8305 — Commemorative or special medals: facsimiles and ribbons
Citation: us:usc:10:8305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8305
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 837 (Decorations And Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary of the Navy, members of the naval service may wear, in place of commemorative or special medals awarded to them, miniature facsimiles of such medals and ribbons symbolic of the awards.
