14 C.F.R. § 1204.500 — Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1204.500
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1204.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1204 (Administrative Authority And Policy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes various delegations of authority to, and designations of, National Aeronautics and Space Administration officials and other Government officials acting on behalf of the agency to carry out prescribed functions of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration.
