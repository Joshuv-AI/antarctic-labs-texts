19 U.S.C. § 2071 — Establishment of Service; Commissioner; appointment
Citation: us:usc:19:2071
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section2071
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 10 (Customs Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be in the Department of the Treasury a service to be known as the United States Customs Service, and a Commissioner of Customs.1 The Commissioner of Customs,1 who shall be appointed by the President by and with the advice and consent of the Senate, shall—
(1)
be at the head of the United States Customs Service;
(2)
carry out the duties and powers prescribed by the Secretary of the Treasury; and
(3)
report to the Secretary of the Treasury through such other officials as may be designated by the Secretary.
