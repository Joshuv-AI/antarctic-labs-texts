7 C.F.R. § 1150.115 — Research.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1150.115
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Milk), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1150.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1150 (Dairy Promotion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Research means studies testing the effectiveness of market development and promotion efforts, studies relating to the nutritional value of milk and dairy products, and other related efforts to expand demand for dairy products.
