10 C.F.R. § 501.65 — Publication of notice of availability of draft EIS.
Citation: us:cfr:10:501.65
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-501.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 501 (Administrative Procedures And Sanctions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Notice of Availability of any draft EIS will be published in the Federal Register and comments thereon will also be solicited. Interested persons may request a hearing on any draft EIS. Such hearing must be requested within thirty (30) days of publication of the Notice of Availability of the draft EIS.
