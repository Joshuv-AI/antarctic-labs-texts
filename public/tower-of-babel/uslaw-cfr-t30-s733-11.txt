30 C.F.R. § 733.11 — General requirements for maintaining State programs.
Citation: us:cfr:30:733.11
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-733.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 733 (Early Identification Of Corrective Action, Maintenance Of State Programs, Procedures For Substituting Federal Enforcement Of State Programs, And Withdrawing Approval Of State Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

States with an approved State program shall implement, administer, enforce and maintain it in accordance with the Act, this chapter and the provisions of the approved State program.
