49 C.F.R. § 553.39 — Effect of petition for reconsideration on time for seeking judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:49:553.39
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-553.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 553 (Rulemaking Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The filing of a timely petition for reconsideration of any rule issued under this part postpones the expiration of the statutory period in which to seek judicial review of that rule only as to the petitioner, and not as to other interested persons. For the petitioner, the period for seeking judicial review will commence at the time the agency takes final action upon the petition for reconsideration.
