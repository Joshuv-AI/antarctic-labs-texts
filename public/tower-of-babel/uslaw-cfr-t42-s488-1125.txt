42 C.F.R. § 488.1125 — Consistency of survey results.
Citation: us:cfr:42:488.1125
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-488.1125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 488 (Survey, Certification, And Enforcement Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A survey agency or accrediting organization must provide a corrective action plan to CMS for any disparity rates that are greater than the threshold established by CMS.
