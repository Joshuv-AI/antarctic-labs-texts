14 U.S.C. § 940 — Supplies and equipment from stock
Citation: us:usc:14:940
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section940
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 9 (Administration); Subchapter II (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Supplies and equipment for special work of the Coast Guard may be furnished from general stock and the applicable appropriation reimbursed therefor from the respective appropriations for such special work.
