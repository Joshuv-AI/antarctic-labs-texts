22 U.S.C. § 1641h — Funds for payment of claims
Citation: us:usc:22:1641h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section1641h
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 21 (Settlement Of International Claims); Subchapter III (Claims Against Bulgaria, Hungary, Rumania, Italy, And The Soviet Union). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All payments authorized under this subchapter shall be disbursed exclusively from the claims fund attributable to the country with respect to which the claims are allowed pursuant to this subchapter. All amounts covered into the Treasury to the credit of the claims funds created by section 1641a of this title are hereby permanently appropriated for the making of the payments authorized under this subchapter.
