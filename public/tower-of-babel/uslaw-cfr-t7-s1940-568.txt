7 C.F.R. § 1940.568 — Single Family Housing programs appropriations not allocated by State.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1940.568
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1940.568
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1940 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following program funds are kept in a National Office reserve and are available as determined administratively:
(a) Section 523 Self-Help Technical Assistance Grants.
(b) Section 523 Land Development Fund.
(c) Section 524 Rural Housing Site Loans.
(d) Section 509 Compensation for Construction Defects.
(e) Section 502 Nonsubsidized Funds.
