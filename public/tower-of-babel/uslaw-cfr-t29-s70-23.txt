29 C.F.R. § 70.23 — Action on appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:29:70.23
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-70.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 70 (Production Or Disclosure Of Information Or Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Solicitor of Labor, or designee, will review the appellant's appeal and make a determination de novo whether the action of the component was proper and in accordance with the applicable law.
