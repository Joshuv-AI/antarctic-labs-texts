46 C.F.R. § 67.259 — Requirements for amendments to notice of claim of lien.
Citation: us:cfr:46:67.259
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-67.259
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 67 (Documentation Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An amendment to notice of claim of lien presented for filing and recording must:
(a) Be signed by or on behalf of each original claimant or last assignee of record; and
(b) Recite the nature of the change being effected by the instrument.
