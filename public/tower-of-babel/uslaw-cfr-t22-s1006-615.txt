22 C.F.R. § 1006.615 — How does the Inter-American Foundation notify a person of a suspension or debarment action?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1006.615
Creator: Inter-American Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1006.615
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1006 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The suspending or debarring official sends a written notice to the last known street address, facsimile number, or e-mail address of—
(1) You or your identified counsel; or
(2) Your agent for service of process, or any of your partners, officers, directors, owners, or joint venturers.
(b) The notice is effective if sent to any of these persons.
