40 C.F.R. § 79.14 — Termination of registration of fuels.

Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-79.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 79 (Registration Of Fuels And Fuel Additives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Registration may be terminated by the Administrator if the fuel manufacturer requests such termination in writing.
