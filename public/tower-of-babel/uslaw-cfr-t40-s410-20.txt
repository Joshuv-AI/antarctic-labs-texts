40 C.F.R. § 410.20 — Applicability; description of the wool finishing subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:410.20
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-410.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 410 (Textile Mills Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to process wastewater discharges resulting from the following types of textile mills: wool finishers, including carbonizing, fulling, dyeing, bleaching, rinsing, fireproofing, and other such similar processes.
