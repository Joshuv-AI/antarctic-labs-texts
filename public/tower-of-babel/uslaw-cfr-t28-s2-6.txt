28 C.F.R. § 2.6 — Withheld and forfeited good time.
Citation: us:cfr:28:2.6
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-2.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 2 (Parole, Release, Supervision And Recommitment Of Prisoners, Youth Offenders, And Juvenile Delinquents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

While neither a forfeiture of good time nor a withholding of good time shall bar a prisoner from receiving a parole hearing, section 4206 of title 18 of the U.S. Code permits the Commission to parole only those prisoners who have substantially observed the rules of the institution.
