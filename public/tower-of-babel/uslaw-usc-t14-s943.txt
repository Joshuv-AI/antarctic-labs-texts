14 U.S.C. § 943 — Arms and ammunition; immunity from taxation
Citation: us:usc:14:943
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section943
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 9 (Administration); Subchapter II (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No tax on the sale or transfer of firearms, pistols, revolvers, shells, or cartridges may be imposed on such articles when bought with funds appropriated for the Coast Guard.
