40 U.S.C. § 3105 — Buildings not to be draped in mourning
Citation: us:usc:40:3105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section3105
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part A (General); Chapter 31 (General); Subchapter I (Oversight And Regulation Of Public Buildings). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No building owned, or used for public purposes, by the Federal Government shall be draped in mourning nor may public money be used for that purpose.
