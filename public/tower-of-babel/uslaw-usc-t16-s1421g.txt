16 U.S.C. § 1421g — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:1421g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1421g
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 31 (Marine Mammal Protection); Subchapter V (Marine Mammal Health And Stranding Response). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated—
(1)
to the Secretary for carrying out this subchapter (other than sections 1421d and 1421f of this title) $250,000 for each of fiscal years 2023 through 2028;
(2)
to the Secretary for carrying out section 1421f of this title, $250,000 for each of fiscal years 2023 through 2028; and
(3)
to the Fund, $500,000 for for 1 each of fiscal years 2023 through 2028.
