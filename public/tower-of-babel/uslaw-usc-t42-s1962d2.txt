42 U.S.C. § 1962d–2 — Delegation of functions
Citation: us:usc:42:1962d–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1962d–2
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 19B (Water Resources Planning); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Council is authorized to delegate to any member or employee of the Council its administrative functions under section 1962a–4 of this title and the detailed administration of the grant program under subchapter III.
