29 C.F.R. § 785.20 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:29:785.20
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-785.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 785 (Hours Worked). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under certain conditions an employee is considered to be working even though some of his time is spent in sleeping or in certain other activities.
