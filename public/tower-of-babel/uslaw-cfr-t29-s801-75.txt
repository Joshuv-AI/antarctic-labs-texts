29 C.F.R. § 801.75 — Certification of official record.
Citation: us:cfr:29:801.75
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-801.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 801 (Application Of The Employee Polygraph Protection Act Of 1988). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of timely notice of appeal to a United States District Court of a Decision and Order issued under this part, the Chief Administrative Law Judge shall promptly certify and file with the appropriate United States District Court, a full, true, and correct copy of the entire record, including the transcript of proceedings.
