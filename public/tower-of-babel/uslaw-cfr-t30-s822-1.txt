30 C.F.R. § 822.1 — Scope.

Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-822.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 822 (Special Permanent Program Performance Standards—Operations In Alluvial Valley Floors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth additional requirements for surface coal mining and reclamation operations on or which affect alluvial valley floors in the arid and semiarid regions of the country.
