21 C.F.R. § 822.24 — What are my responsibilities once I am notified that I am required to conduct postmarket surveillance?
Citation: us:cfr:21:822.24
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-822.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 822 (Postmarket Surveillance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must submit your plan to conduct postmarket surveillance to us within 30 days from receipt of the order (letter) notifying you that you are required to conduct postmarket surveillance of a device. The manufacturer shall commence surveillance not later than 15 months after the day the order was issued.
