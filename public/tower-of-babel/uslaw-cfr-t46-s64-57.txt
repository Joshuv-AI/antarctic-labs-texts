46 C.F.R. § 64.57 — Acceptance of pressure relief devices.
Citation: us:cfr:46:64.57
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-64.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 64 (Marine Portable Tanks And Cargo Handling Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A pressure relief device for an MPT must be—
(a) From a supplier accepted under chapter I of title 46, Code of Federal Regulations; or
(b) Accepted by the Coast Guard in accordance with the procedures in § 50.25-10 of this subchapter.
