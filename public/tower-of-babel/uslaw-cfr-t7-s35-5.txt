7 C.F.R. § 35.5 — Package.
Citation: us:cfr:7:35.5
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-35.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 35 (Export Grapes And Plums). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Package means any container of any variety of vinifera species table grapes.
