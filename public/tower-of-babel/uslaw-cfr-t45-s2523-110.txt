45 C.F.R. § 2523.110 — Can Federal agencies submit multiple applications?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2523.110
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2523.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2523 (Agreements With Other Federal Agencies For The Provision Of Americorps Program Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. The Corporation will only consider one application from a Federal agency for each AmeriCorps competition. The application may propose more than one program, however, and the Corporation may choose to fund any or all of those programs.
