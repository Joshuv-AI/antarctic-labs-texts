45 C.F.R. § 2400.63 — Excluded graduate study.
Citation: us:cfr:45:2400.63
Creator: James Madison Memorial Fellowship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2400.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2400 (Fellowship Program Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

James Madison Fellowships do not provide support for study toward doctoral degrees, for the degree of master of arts in public affairs or public administration. The Foundation may at its discretion, upon request of the Fellow, provide tuition only assistance toward teacher certification.
