35 U.S.C. § 306 — Appeal
Citation: us:usc:35:306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section306
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part III (Patents And Protection Of Patent Rights); Chapter 30 (Prior Art Citations To Office And Ex Parte Reexamination Of Patents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The patent owner involved in a reexamination proceeding under this chapter may appeal under the provisions of section 134, and may seek court review under the provisions of sections 141 to 144, with respect to any decision adverse to the patentability of any original or proposed amended or new claim of the patent.
