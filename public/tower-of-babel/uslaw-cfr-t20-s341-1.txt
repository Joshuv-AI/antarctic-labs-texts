20 C.F.R. § 341.1 — Lien.
Citation: us:cfr:20:341.1
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-341.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 341 (Statutory Lien Where Sickness Benefits Paid). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After notice in accordance with this part, the Board shall have a lien upon any sum or damages paid or payable to an employee based upon an infirmity for which the employee received sickness benefits.
