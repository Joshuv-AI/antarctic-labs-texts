16 C.F.R. § 1219.2 — Requirements for full-size baby cribs.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1219.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1219.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1219 (Safety Standard For Full-Size Baby Cribs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each full-size baby crib must comply with all applicable provisions of ASTM F1169-25, Standard Consumer Safety Specification for Full-Size Baby Cribs, approved December 15, 2025. The Director of the Federal Register approves this incorporation by reference in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 552(a) and 1 CFR part 51. You may obtain a copy from ASTM International, 100 Barr Harbor Drive, P.O. Box 0700, West Conshohocken, PA 19428 or at https://www.astm.org/READINGLIBRARY/.
