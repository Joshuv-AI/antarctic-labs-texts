10 U.S.C. § 8413 — Aviation cadets: appointment as reserve officers
Citation: us:usc:10:8413
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8413
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Education And Training); Chapter 851 (Officer Procurement Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
An aviation cadet who fulfills the requirements of section 2003 of this title may be appointed an ensign in the Navy Reserve or a second lieutenant in the Marine Corps Reserve and designated a naval aviator.
(b)
Aviation cadets who complete their training at approximately the same time are considered for all purposes to have begun their commissioned service on the same date, and the decision of the Secretary of the Navy in this regard is conclusive.
