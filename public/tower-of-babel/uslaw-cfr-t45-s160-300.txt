45 C.F.R. § 160.300 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:45:160.300
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-160.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 160 (General Administrative Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to actions by the Secretary, covered entities, business associates, and others with respect to ascertaining the compliance by covered entities and business associates with, and the enforcement of, the applicable provisions of this part 160 and parts 162 and 164 of this subchapter.
