25 C.F.R. § 900.205 — To what non-medical-related claims against self-determination contractors does FTCA apply?
Citation: us:cfr:25:900.205
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior, And Indian Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-900.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 900 (Contracts Under The Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It applies to:
(a) All tort claims arising from the performance of self-determination contracts under the authority of the Act on or after October 1, 1989; and
(b) Any tort claims first filed on or after October 24, 1989, regardless of when the incident which is the basis of the claim occurred.
