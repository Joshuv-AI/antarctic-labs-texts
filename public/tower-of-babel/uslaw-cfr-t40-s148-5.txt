40 C.F.R. § 148.5 — Waste analysis.
Citation: us:cfr:40:148.5
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-148.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 148 (Hazardous Waste Injection Restrictions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Generators of hazardous wastes that are disposed of into Class I injection wells must comply with the applicable requirements of § 268.7 (a) and (b). Owners or operators of Class I hazardous waste injection wells must comply with the applicable requirements of § 268.7(c).
