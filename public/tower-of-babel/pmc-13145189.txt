ABSTRACT NUMBER: ESOC2026LT10 LT10 - EFFECT OF INTRAVENOUS TIROFIBAN VS PLACEBO AFER INTRAVENOUS TENECTEPLASE ON EXCELLENT OUTCOME IN PATIENTS WITH ACUTE ISCHEMIC STROKE: THE INSTANT RANDOMIZED CLINICAL TRIAL
Authors: Xianghong Liu, Fan Zhang, Cong Zhang, Raul Nogueira, Götz Thomalla, Mayank Goyal, Thanh N Nguyen, Jeffrey Saver, Qingwu Yang, Zhongming Qiu, Guoyong Zeng
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackground and aimsThe safety and efficacy of early administration of tirofiban after intravenous thrombolysis in patients with acute ischemic stroke remains uncertain.MethodsIn this multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial, acute ischemic stroke patients without large or medium-vessel occlusion, without cardioembolic etiology, and who had an insufficient clinical response to intravenous tenecteplase were enrolled. Within 4 to 24 hours after intravenous tenecteplase, patients were randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive intravenous tirofiban or placebo. The primary outcome was excellent outcome (a score of 0 to 1 on the modified Rankin scale; range, 0 to 6, with higher scores indicating more severe disability) at 90 days. Safety outcomes were symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage within 48 hours and 90-day mortality.ResultsA total of 359 patients underwent randomization: 177 to the tirofiban group and 182 to the placebo group. Excellent outcome at 90 days was observed in 113 patients (63.8%) in the tirofiban group and in 95 patients (52.5%) in the placebo group (risk ratio, 1.22; 95% confidence interval, 1.02-1.46; P=0.03). Symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage within 48 hours occurred in 1 patient (0.9%) in the tirofiban group and no patients in the placebo group; 90-day mortality was 0.6% and 1.7%, respectively.ConclusionsAmong acute ischemic stroke patients without large or medium-vessel occlusion or cardioembolic stroke who had an inadequate clinical response to intravenous tenecteplase, early adjunctive tirofiban increased the likelihood of an excellent outcome at 90 days.
