2 C.F.R. § 421.500 — Who in the USDA determines that a recipient other than an individual violated the requirements of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:2:421.500
Creator: Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-421.500
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 421 (Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).
