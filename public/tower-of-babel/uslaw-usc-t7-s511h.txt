7 U.S.C. § 511h — Publication of information relating to tobacco
Citation: us:usc:7:511h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section511h
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 21A (Tobacco Inspection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to collect, publish, and distribute, by telegraph, mail, or otherwise without cost to the grower, timely information on the market supply and demand, location, disposition, quality, condition, and market prices for tobacco.
