15 U.S.C. § 77jj — Loans to Corporation from Reconstruction Finance Corporation authorized
Citation: us:usc:15:77jj
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section77jj
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2A (Securities And Trust Indentures); Subchapter II (Foreign Securities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Reconstruction Finance Corporation is authorized to loan out of its funds not to exceed $75,000 for the use of the Corporation.
