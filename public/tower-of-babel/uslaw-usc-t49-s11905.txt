49 U.S.C. § 11905 — Disobedience to subpoenas
Citation: us:usc:49:11905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section11905
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Rail); Chapter 119 (Civil And Criminal Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person not obeying a subpoena or requirement of the Board to appear and testify or produce records shall be fined at least $100 but not more than $5,000, imprisoned for not more than one year, or both.
