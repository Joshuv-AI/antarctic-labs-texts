21 C.F.R. § 173.385 — Sodium methyl sulfate.
Citation: us:cfr:21:173.385
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-173.385
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 173 (Secondary Direct Food Additives Permitted In Food For Human Consumption). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sodium methyl sulfate may be present in pectin in accordance with the following conditions.
(a) It is present as the result of methylation of pectin by sulfuric acid and methyl alcohol and subsequent treatment with sodium bicarbonate.
(b) It does not exceed 0.1 percent by weight of the pectin.
