Correction to: “Independent Effects of Vitamin D on Leukocyte Telomere Length and Activity: An RCT in Asian Indian Women With Prediabetes”
Published: 2025

In the above-named article by Bhatt SP, Pandey S, and Misra A (J. Endocr Soc. 2025; 9(9); doi: 10.1210/jendso/bvaf124), there were errors in the Research Design and Methods section and the acknowledgment section.

In the originally published article, in the Research Design and Methods section, the fifth sentence read, “Vitamin D (cholecalciferol) supplementation of 60 000 IU/week (Commercial name Calcirol, Cipla, India) was given to the intervention group for 8 weeks and lactose granules were given to the placebo group.” In the corrected article, the information for “Calcirol” has been updated to “Calcirol, manufactured by Cadila Pharmaceuticals Limited” and the sentence now reads, “Vitamin D (cholecalciferol) supplementation of 60 000 IU/week (Commercial name Calcirol, manufactured by Cadila Pharmaceuticals Limited) was given to the intervention group for 8 weeks and lactose granules were given to the placebo group.”

In the originally published article, in the acknowledgment section, the last sentence read, “Authors thanks Cipla (India) for providing cholecalciferol sachets and matching placebo.” In the corrected article, the information for “Calcirol” has been updated to “Calcirol (manufactured by Cadila Pharmaceuticals Limited)” and the sentence now reads, “Authors thank Cadila Pharmaceuticals Limited for providing Calcirol cholecalciferol sachets and matching placebo.”

The article has been corrected online.
