43 C.F.R. § 3135.4 — When is a suspension of operations and production effective?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3135.4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3135.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3130 (Oil And Gas Leasing: National Petroleum Reserve, Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A suspension of operations and production is effective—
(a) The first day of the month in which you file the application for suspension or BLM requires the suspension; or
(b) Any other date BLM specifies in the decision document.
