49 U.S.C. § 11327 — Supplemental orders
Citation: us:usc:49:11327
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section11327
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Rail); Chapter 113 (Finance); Subchapter II (Combinations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When cause exists, the Board may make appropriate orders supplemental to an order made in a proceeding under sections 11322 through 11326 of this title.
