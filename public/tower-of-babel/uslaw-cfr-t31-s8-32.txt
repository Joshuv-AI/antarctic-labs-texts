31 C.F.R. § 8.32 — Prompt disposition of pending matters.
Citation: us:cfr:31:8.32
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-8.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 8 (Practice Before The Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco And Firearms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No attorney, certified public accountant, or enrolled practitioner may unreasonably delay the prompt disposition of any matter before the Bureau.
