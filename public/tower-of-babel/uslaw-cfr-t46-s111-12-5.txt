46 C.F.R. § 111.12-5 — -5 Construction and testing of generators.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.12-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.12-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each generator must meet the applicable requirements for construction and testing in section 4-8-3 of the ABS Marine Vessel Rules (incorporated by reference; see § 110.10-1 of this subchapter) except that each one for a mobile offshore drilling unit must meet the requirements in section 6-1-7 of the ABS MOU Rules (incorporated by reference; see § 110.10-1 of this subchapter).
