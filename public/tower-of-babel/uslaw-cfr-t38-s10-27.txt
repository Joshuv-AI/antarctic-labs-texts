38 C.F.R. § 10.27 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:38:10.27
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-10.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 10 (Adjusted Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of §§ 10.28 to 10.47, the word Act as used herein refers to the World War Adjusted Compensation Act, as amended; the word Veteran refers to that term as defined in section 2 of title I of said Act; the word Director refers to the Secretary of Veterans Affairs.
