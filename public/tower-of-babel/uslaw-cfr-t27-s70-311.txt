27 C.F.R. § 70.311 — Authority for establishment, alteration, and distribution of stamps, marks, or labels.
Citation: us:cfr:27:70.311
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-70.311
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 70 (Procedure And Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The appropriate TTB officer may establish, and from time to time alter, renew, replace, or change the form, style, character, material, and device of any stamp, mark, or label under any provision of the law relating to Subtitle E of the Internal Revenue Code (or to any provision of Subtitle F which relates to Subtitle E).
