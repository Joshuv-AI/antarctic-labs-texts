12 U.S.C. § 1701h–1 — Housing for elderly persons advisory committee
Citation: us:usc:12:1701h–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1701h–1
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Housing and Urban Development shall establish, in accordance with the provisions of section 1701h of this title, an advisory committee on matters relating to housing for elderly persons.
