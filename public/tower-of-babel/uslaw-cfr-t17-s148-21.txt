17 C.F.R. § 148.21 — Filing and service of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:17:148.21
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-148.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 148 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Covered Adjudicatory Proceedings Before The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any application for an award or other pleading or document related to an application shall be filed and served on all parties to the adjudicatory proceeding, except as provided in § 148.12(b) for confidential financial information.
