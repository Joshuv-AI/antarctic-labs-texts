47 C.F.R. § 87.449 — Frequencies.
Citation: us:cfr:47:87.449
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-87.449
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 87 (Aviation Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following frequencies in the 72-76 MHz band are assignable to operational fixed stations using vertical polarization, if no harmful interference is caused to TV reception on Channels 4 and 5. These frequencies are shared with the Land Mobile and the Maritime Mobile Services.
