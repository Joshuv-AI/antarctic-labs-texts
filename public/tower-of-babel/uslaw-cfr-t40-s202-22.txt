40 C.F.R. § 202.22 — Visual exhaust system inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:40:202.22
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-202.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 202 (Motor Carriers Engaged In Interstate Commerce). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No motor carrier subject to these regulations shall operate any motor vehicle of a type to which this regulation is applicable unless the exhaust system of such vehicle is (a) free from defects which affect sound reduction; (b) equipped with a muffler or other noise dissipative device; and (c) not equipped with any cut-out, bypass, or similar device.
