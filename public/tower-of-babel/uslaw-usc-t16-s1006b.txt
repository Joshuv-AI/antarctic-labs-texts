16 U.S.C. § 1006b — Territorial application

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 18 (Watershed Protection And Flood Prevention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this chapter shall be applicable to Hawaii, Alaska, Puerto Rico, and the Virgin Islands.
