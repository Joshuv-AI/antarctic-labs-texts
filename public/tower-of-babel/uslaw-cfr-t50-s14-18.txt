50 C.F.R. § 14.18 — Marine mammals.
Citation: us:cfr:50:14.18
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-14.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 14 (Importation, Exportation, And Transportation Of Wildlife). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person subject to the jurisdiction of the United States who has lawfully taken a marine mammal on the high seas and who is authorized to import such marine mammal in accordance with the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972 and implementing regulations (50 CFR parts 18 and 216) may import such marine mammal at any port or place.
