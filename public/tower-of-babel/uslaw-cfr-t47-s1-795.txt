47 C.F.R. § 1.795 — Reports regarding interstate rates of return.

Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-1.795
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 1 (Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Carriers shall file reports regarding interstate rates of return on FCC Form 492 as required by part 65 of this chapter.
