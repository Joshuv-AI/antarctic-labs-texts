22 U.S.C. § 2430d — Repayment of principal
Citation: us:usc:22:2430d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2430d
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter III–A (Enterprise For The Americas Initiative). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Currency of payment
The principal amount of each new obligation issued pursuant to section 2430c(b) of this title shall be repaid in United States dollars.
(b) Deposit of payments
Principal repayments of new obligations shall be deposited in the United States Government account established for principal repayments of the obligations for which those obligations were exchanged.
