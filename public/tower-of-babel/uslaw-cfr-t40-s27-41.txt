40 C.F.R. § 27.41 — Stay pending appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:40:27.41
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-27.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 27 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An initial decision is stayed automatically pending disposition of a motion for reconsideration or of an appeal to the Environmental Appeals Board.
(b) No administrative stay is available following a final decision of the Environmental Appeals Board.
