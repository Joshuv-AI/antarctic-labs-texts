25 C.F.R. § 166.818 — How are the proceeds from trespass distributed?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.818
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.818
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise provided by tribal law:
(a) We will treat any amounts recovered under § 166.812 of this subpart as proceeds from the sale of agricultural property from the Indian agricultural land upon which the trespass occurred.
(b) Proceeds recovered under § 166.812 of this subpart may be distributed to:
(1) Repair damages of the Indian agricultural land and property;
(2) Reimburse the affected parties, including the permittee for loss due to the trespass, as negotiated and provided in the permit; and
(3) Reimburse for costs associated with the enforcement of this subpart.
(c) If any money is left over after the distribution of the proceeds described in paragraph (b) of this section, we will return it to the trespasser or, where we cannot identify the owner of the impounded property within 180 days, we will deposit the net proceeds of the sale into the accounts of the landowners where the trespass occurred.
