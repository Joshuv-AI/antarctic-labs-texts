14 C.F.R. § 323.15 — Report to be filed after strikes.
Citation: us:cfr:14:323.15
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-323.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 323 (Terminations, Suspensions, And Reductions Of Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Within 15 days following resumption of service after a strike, an air carrier shall file a report with DOT containing a list of all flights that were canceled, the date they were canceled, and the date service was resumed.
(b) The report shall be marked for the attention of the Director, Office of Aviation Analysis.
