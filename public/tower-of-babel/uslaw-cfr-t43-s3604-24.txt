43 C.F.R. § 3604.24 — Who may remove materials on my behalf?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3604.24
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3604.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3600 (Mineral Materials Disposal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You may allow your agent to extract mineral materials under your free use permit.
(b) Your agent may charge you only for extraction services and must not—
(1) Charge you for the materials extracted, processed, or removed; or
(2) Take mineral materials from the permit area as payment for services rendered to you, or as a donation or gift.
