Ecalle’s dimorphic transportation and Brown’s lifting
Hanamichi Kawamura

Abstract
. Brown’s lifting procedure shows that one can solve the double shuffle equations modulo products from solutions of linearized ones. In this paper, we reveal that it is described in the framework of Ecalle’s dimorphic transportation.

1.
Introduction

We have been interested in the double shuffle structure in the framework
\mathsf{DMR}_{0}
as a group and
\mathfrak{dmr}_{0}
as a Lie algebra.
They are originally formulated in a non-commutative power series (or polynomial) ring, but there is another setting which is constructed in the so-called
flexion theory
established by Ecalle.
In particular, it is important to investigate a concrete solution of the double shuffle equation modulo products (
=
an element of
\mathfrak{dmr}_{0}
).
Brown
[
B
]
got some systems of such solutions, and Matthes–Tasaka
[
MT
]
started to compare Brown’s and Ecalle’s solutions.
Furthermore, recently Furusho–Hirose–Komiyama
[
FHK
]
revealed that one of Brown’s system
\{\psi_{2n+1}\}_{n}
can be exactly described as Ecalle’s
polar singulates
.
On the other hand, Brown
[
B
]
and Ecalle
[
E1
]
also independently obtained a method to get solutions
\chi_{B}(f),\chi_{E}(f)\in\mathfrak{ds}_{\mathcal{Q}}
of the double shuffle equations admitting some poles by lifting each solution
f\in\mathfrak{ls}_{\mathcal{Q}}
of the “linearlized” double shuffle equations.
In this note, we reveal that Brown’s lifting
\mathfrak{ls}_{\mathcal{Q}}\ni f\mapsto\chi_{B}(f)\in\mathfrak{ds}_{\mathcal{Q}}
essentially coincides with the operator
\mathsf{adari}(\mathsf{par})
, which is an example of Ecalle’s lifting.

Theorem 1.1
.

We have

\mathsf{swap}\circ\mathsf{anti}\circ\chi_{B}=\mathsf{adari}(\mathsf{par})\circ\mathsf{swap}\circ\mathsf{anti}.

While the original preprint
[
B
]
does not include the proof that
\chi_{B}(f)
gives a solution in
\mathfrak{ds}_{\mathcal{Q}}
for each
f\in\mathfrak{ls}_{\mathcal{Q}}
, Kimura–Tasaka’s report
[
KT
]
present that assertion as a open conjecture. We can give an affirmative answer to it by Theorem
1.1
and the machinery of Ecalle’s dimorphic transportation.

Corollary 1.2
.

For any
f\in\mathfrak{ls}_{\mathcal{Q}}
, we have
\chi_{B}(f)\in\mathfrak{ds}_{\mathcal{Q}}
.
