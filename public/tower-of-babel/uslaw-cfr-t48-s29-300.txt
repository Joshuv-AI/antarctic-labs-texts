48 C.F.R. § 29.300 — 29.300 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:29.300
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-29.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 29 (Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes the policies and procedures regarding the exemption or immunity of Federal Government purchases and property from State and local taxation.
