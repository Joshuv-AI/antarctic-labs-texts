21 C.F.R. § 119.1 — Dietary supplements containing ephedrine alkaloids.
Citation: us:cfr:21:119.1
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-119.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 119 (Dietary Supplements That Present A Significant Or Unreasonable Risk). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Dietary supplements containing ephedrine alkaloids present an unreasonable risk of illness or injury under conditions of use recommended or suggested in the labeling, or if no conditions of use are recommended or suggested in the labeling, under ordinary conditions of use. Therefore, dietary supplements containing ephedrine alkaloids are adulterated under section 402(f)(1)(A) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act.
