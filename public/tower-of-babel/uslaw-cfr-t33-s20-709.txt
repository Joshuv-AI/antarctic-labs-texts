33 C.F.R. § 20.709 — Closing of the record.
Citation: us:cfr:33:20.709

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-20.709
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 20 (Rules Of Practice, Procedure, And Evidence For Formal Administrative Proceedings Of The Coast Guard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When the ALJ closes the hearing, he or she shall also close the record of the proceeding, as described in § 20.903, unless he or she directs otherwise. Even after the ALJ closes it, he or she may reopen it.
(b) The ALJ may correct the transcript of the hearing by appropriate order.
