7 U.S.C. § 3903 — Annual program review to achieve coordination with National Cancer Institute program
Citation: us:usc:7:3903
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section3903
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 70 (Animal Cancer Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture and the Director of the National Institutes of Health shall annually review the research program conducted under this chapter in order to coordinate the program with the National Cancer Institute research program.
