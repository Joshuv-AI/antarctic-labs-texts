43 C.F.R. § 2932.41 — What stipulations must I follow?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2932.41
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2932.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2930 (Permits For Recreation On Public Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must follow all stipulations in your approved Special Recreation Permit. BLM may impose stipulations and conditions to meet management goals and objectives and to protect lands and resources and the public interest.
