16 U.S.C. § 777l — State use of contributions
Citation: us:usc:16:777l
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section777l
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 10B (Fish Restoration And Management Projects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A State may use contributions of funds, real property, materials, and services to carry out an activity under this chapter in lieu of payment by the State of the State share of the cost of such activity. Such a State share shall be considered to be paid in an amount equal to the fair market value of any contribution so used.
