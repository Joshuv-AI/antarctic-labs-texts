46 C.F.R. § 545.3 — Interpretation of § 515.23(b) of this chapter—Payment pursuant to a claim against an ocean transportation intermediary.
Citation: us:cfr:46:545.3
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-545.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 545 (Interpretations And Statements Of Policy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claimant seeking to settle a claim in accordance with § 515.23(b)(1) of this chapter should promptly provide to the financial responsibility provider all documents and information relating to and supporting its claim for the purpose of evaluating the validity and subject matter of the claim.
