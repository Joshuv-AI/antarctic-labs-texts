31 C.F.R. § 902.5 — Further review of compromise offers.
Citation: us:cfr:31:902.5
Creator: Federal Claims Collection Standards (Department Of The Treasury—Department Of Justice)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-902.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 902 (Standards For The Compromise Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an agency is uncertain whether to accept a firm, written, substantive compromise offer on a debt that is within the agency's delegated compromise authority, it may refer the offer to the Civil Division or other appropriate litigating division in the Department of Justice, using a CCLR accompanied by supporting data and particulars concerning the debt. The Department of Justice may act upon such an offer or return it to the agency with instructions or advice.
