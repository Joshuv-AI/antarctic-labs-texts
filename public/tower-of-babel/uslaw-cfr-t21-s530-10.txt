21 C.F.R. § 530.10 — Provision permitting extralabel use of animal drugs.
Citation: us:cfr:21:530.10
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-530.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 530 (Extralabel Drug Use In Animals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An approved new animal drug or human drug intended to be used for an extralabel purpose in an animal is not unsafe under section 512 of the act and is exempt from the labeling requirements of section 502(f) of the act if such use is:
(a) By or on the lawful written or oral order of a licensed veterinarian within the context of a valid veterinarian-client-patient relationship; and
(b) In compliance with this part.
