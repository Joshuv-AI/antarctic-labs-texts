22 C.F.R. § 905.3 — Separation for cause.
Citation: us:cfr:22:905.3
Creator: Foreign Service Grievance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-905.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 905 (Burden Of Proof). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In separation for cause cases, the agency has the burden of establishing, by a preponderance of the evidence, that the proposed separation is for such cause as will promote the efficiency of the service.
