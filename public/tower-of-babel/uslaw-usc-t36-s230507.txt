36 U.S.C. § 230507 — Exclusive right to name, seals, emblems, and badges
Citation: us:usc:36:230507
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section230507
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 2305 (Vietnam Veterans Of America, Inc.). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation has the exclusive right to use the names “The Vietnam Veterans of America, Inc.”, “Vietnam Veterans of America, Inc.”, and “Vietnam Veterans of America”, and seals, emblems, and badges the corporation adopts. This section does not affect any vested rights.
