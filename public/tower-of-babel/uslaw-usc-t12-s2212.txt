12 U.S.C. § 2212 — Powers of Farm Credit Administration
Citation: us:usc:12:2212
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2212
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 23 (Farm Credit System); Subchapter IV (Provisions Applicable To Two Or More Classes Of Institutions Of The System); Part E (Service Organizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Farm Credit Administration shall have power, under rules and regulations prescribed by the Farm Credit Administration, to provide for the organization of any corporation chartered under this part and the territory within which its operations may be carried on, and to approve amendments consistent with this chapter to charters or articles of service corporations.
