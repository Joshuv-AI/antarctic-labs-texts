15 U.S.C. § 176 — Collection of commercial statistics
Citation: us:usc:15:176
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section176
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 5 (Statistical And Commercial Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A purpose of the Bureau of Foreign and Domestic Commerce is the collection, arrangement, and classification of such statistical information as may be procured, showing, or tending to show, each year the condition of the manufactures, domestic trade, currency, and banks of the several States and Territories.
