46 C.F.R. § 2.10-125 — -125 Fees for examination of foreign tankships.
Citation: us:cfr:46:2.10-125
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-2.10-125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 2 (Vessel Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each foreign tankship of a country party to the International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea, 1974 as amended, must pay:
(a) For examination for the issuance of a Certificate of Compliance under § 2.01-6(a)(2)(i) of this part, or examination for the annual endorsement to a Certificate of Compliance, a fee of $1,100.
(b) For examination for the issuance of a Tank Vessel Examination Letter under § 2.01-6(a)(3) of this part, a fee of $1,100.
