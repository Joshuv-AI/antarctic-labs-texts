16 U.S.C. § 838h — Uniform schedules of rates and charges for sale of Federal power and transmission of non-Federal power; allocation of cost recovery
Citation: us:usc:16:838h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section838h
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12G (Pacific Northwest Federal Transmission System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The said schedules of rates and charges for transmission, the said schedules of rates and charges for the sale of electric power, or both such schedules, may provide, among other things, for uniform rates or rates uniform throughout prescribed transmission areas. The recovery of the cost of the Federal transmission system shall be equitably allocated between Federal and non-Federal power utilizing such system.
