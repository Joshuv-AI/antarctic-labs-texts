25 C.F.R. § 256.28 — I have a mobile home; am I eligible for help?
Citation: us:cfr:25:256.28
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-256.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 256 (Housing Improvement Program (Hip)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. If you meet the eligibility criteria in § 256.6 and funding is available, you can receive any of the HIP services identified in § 256.7. If you request Category B services and your mobile home has exterior walls less than three inches thick, you must be considered for Category C services.
