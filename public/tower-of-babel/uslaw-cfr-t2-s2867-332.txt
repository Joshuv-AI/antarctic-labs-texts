2 C.F.R. § 2867.332 — What method must a participant use to pass requirements down to participants at lower tiers with whom the participant intends to do business?
Citation: us:cfr:2:2867.332
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2867.332
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2867 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A participant must include a term or condition in lower-tier transactions requiring lower-tier participants to comply with subpart C of the OMB guidance in 2 CFR part 180, as supplemented by this subpart.
