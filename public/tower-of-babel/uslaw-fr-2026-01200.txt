DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Office of the Director, National Institutes of Health; Amended 
Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the Council of 
Councils, January 29, 2026 10:00 a.m. to January 29, 2026, 05:25 p.m., 
National Institutes of Health, Building 1, Room 260, 1 Center Drive, 
Bethesda, MD 20892, Virtual Meeting, which was published in the Federal 
Register on January 13, 2026, 91 FR 1542, Doc. 00546.
    This meeting is being amended to change the closed session from 
2:00 p.m. to 5:25 p.m. to 1:00 p.m. to 2:00 p.m.; and adding a second 
open session from 2:00 p.m. to 5:25 p.m. The meeting is Virtual and 
partially closed to the public.

Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
