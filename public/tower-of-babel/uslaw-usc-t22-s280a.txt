22 U.S.C. § 280a — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:22:280a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section280a
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter VII (South Pacific Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When used in this subchapter—
(1)
the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of State;
(2)
the term “Government agency” means any department, independent establishment, or other agency of the Government of the United States, or any corporation wholly owned by the Government of the United States; and
(3)
the term “Commission” means the South Pacific Commission.
