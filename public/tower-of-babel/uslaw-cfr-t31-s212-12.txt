31 C.F.R. § 212.12 — Amendment of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:31:212.12
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-212.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 212 (Garnishment Of Accounts Containing Federal Benefit Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part may be amended only by a rulemaking issued jointly by Treasury and all of the benefit agencies as defined in § 212.3.
