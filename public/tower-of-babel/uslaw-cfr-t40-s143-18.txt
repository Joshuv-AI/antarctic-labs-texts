40 C.F.R. § 143.18 — Required labeling of solder and flux that is not lead free.
Citation: us:cfr:40:143.18
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-143.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 143 (Other Safe Drinking Water Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Solder and flux that is not “lead free” as defined in § 143.12(a)(1) must bear a prominent label stating that it is illegal to use the solder or flux in the installation or repair of any plumbing providing water for human consumption.
