49 C.F.R. § 1016.109 — Awards against other agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1016.109
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1016.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1016 (Special Procedures Governing The Recovery Of Expenses By Parties To Board Adjudicatory Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an applicant is entitled to an award because it prevails over another agency of the United States that participates in a proceeding before this agency and takes a position that is not substantially justified, the award or an appropriate portion of the award shall be made against that agency.
