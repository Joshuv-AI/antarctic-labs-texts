49 C.F.R. § 382.115 — Starting date for testing programs.
Citation: us:cfr:49:382.115
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-382.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 382 (Controlled Substances And Alcohol Use And Testing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) All domestic-domiciled employers must implement the requirements of this part on the date the employer begins commercial motor vehicle operations.
(b) All foreign-domiciled employers must implement the requirements of this part on the date the employer begins commercial motor vehicle operations in the United States.
