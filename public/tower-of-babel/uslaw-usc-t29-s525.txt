29 U.S.C. § 525 — Service of process
Citation: us:usc:29:525
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section525
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 11 (Labor-Management Reporting And Disclosure Procedure); Subchapter VII (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this chapter, service of summons, subpena, or other legal process of a court of the United States upon an officer or agent of a labor organization in his capacity as such shall constitute service upon the labor organization.
