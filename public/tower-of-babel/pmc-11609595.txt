Reviewers acknowledgement
Published: 2024

The editors of FEBS Open Bio would like to thank all those who have given their time and expertise to review articles submitted for publication in Volume 14. Although FEBS Open Bio does not seek to judge the importance of submissions, our reviewers carefully scrutinize the experimental design and results of all papers, and challenge authors about their conclusions.
