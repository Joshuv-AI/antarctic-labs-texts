7 U.S.C. § 5601 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:7:5601
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section5601
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 87 (Export Promotion); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this chapter to increase the profitability of farming and to increase opportunities for United States farms and agricultural enterprises by—
(1)
increasing the effectiveness of the Department of Agriculture in agricultural export policy formulation and implementation;
(2)
improving the competitiveness of United States agricultural commodities and products in the world market; and
(3)
providing for the coordination and efficient implementation of all agricultural export programs.
