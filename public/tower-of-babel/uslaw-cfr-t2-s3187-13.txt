2 C.F.R. § 3187.13 — Federal evaluation—Cooperation by a non-Federal entity.
Citation: us:cfr:2:3187.13
Creator: Institute Of Museum And Library Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-3187.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 3187 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A non-Federal entity shall cooperate in any evaluation by the Director of the particular IMLS Federal financial assistance program in which the non-Federal entity has participated.
