47 C.F.R. § 78.57 — Station inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:47:78.57
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-78.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 78 (Cable Television Relay Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The station and all records required to be kept by the licensee shall be made available for inspection upon request by any authorized representative of the Commission.
