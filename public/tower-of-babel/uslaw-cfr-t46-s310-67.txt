46 C.F.R. § 310.67 — Academy regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:310.67
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-310.67
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 310 (Merchant Marine Training). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Superintendent of the Academy is delegated authority to issue all regulations necessary for the accomplishment of the Academy's mission.
