42 U.S.C. § 420 — Disability provisions inapplicable if benefit rights impaired
Citation: us:usc:42:420
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section420
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter II (Federal Old-Age, Survivors, And Disability Insurance Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

None of the provisions of this subchapter relating to periods of disability shall apply in any case in which their application would result in the denial of monthly benefits or a lump-sum death payment which would otherwise be payable under this subchapter; nor shall they apply in the case of any monthly benefit or lump-sum death payment under this subchapter if such benefit or payment would be greater without their application.
