24 C.F.R. § 200.76 — Smoke detectors.
Citation: us:cfr:24:200.76
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-200.76
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 200 (Introduction To Fha Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Smoke detectors and alarm devices must be installed in accordance with standards and criteria acceptable to the Commissioner for the protection of occupants in any dwelling or facility bedroom or other primary sleeping area.
