14 C.F.R. § 249.20 — Preservation of records by certificated air carriers.
Citation: us:cfr:14:249.20
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-249.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 249 (Preservation Of Air Carrier Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each certificated air carrier shall retain its records according to the provisions of this section. Unless otherwise specified in the “Schedule of Records,” each retention period shall begin on the date when the records are created or otherwise come into the possession of the carrier.
