46 U.S.C. § 57304 — Eligible acquisition dates
Citation: us:usc:46:57304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section57304
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Government-Owned Merchant Vessels); Chapter 573 (Vessel Trade-In Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

At the option of the owner, the acquisition of an obsolete vessel under this chapter shall occur—
(1)
when the owner contracts for the construction or purchase of a new vessel; or
(2)
within 5 days of the actual date of delivery of the new vessel to the owner.
