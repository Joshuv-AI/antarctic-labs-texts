20 U.S.C. § 1011d — Student representation
Citation: us:usc:20:1011d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1011d
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter I (General Provisions); Part B (Additional General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall, in appointing individuals to any commission, committee, board, panel, or other body in connection with the administration of this chapter, include individuals who are, at the time of appointment, attending an institution of higher education.
