7 C.F.R. § 15.71 — Form of documents to be filed.
Citation: us:cfr:7:15.71
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-15.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 15 (Nondiscrimination). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All copies of documents filed in a proceeding shall be dated, signed in ink, shall show the address and position or title of the signatory, and shall show the docket number and title of the proceeding on the front page.
