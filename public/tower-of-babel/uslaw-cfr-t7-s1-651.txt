7 C.F.R. § 1.651 — What are the parties' rights during the hearing?
Citation: us:cfr:7:1.651
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1.651
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1 (Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each party has the following rights during the hearing, as necessary to assure full and accurate disclosure of the facts:
(a) To present testimony and exhibits, consistent with the requirements in §§ 1.621(c), 1.622(c), 1.625(c), 1.642(b), and 1.652;
(b) To make objections, motions, and arguments; and
(c) To cross-examine witnesses and to conduct re-direct and re-cross examination as permitted by the ALJ.
