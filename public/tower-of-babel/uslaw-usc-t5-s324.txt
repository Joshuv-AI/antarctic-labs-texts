5 U.S.C. § 324 — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:5:324
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section324
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 3 (Powers); Subchapter III (Federal Government Service Delivery). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter may be construed to diminish or reduce the authority of agency Chief Information Officers for information resources management provided in section 11315 of title 40 or those authorities to manage information resources to accomplish agency missions as established in section 3502 of title 44.
