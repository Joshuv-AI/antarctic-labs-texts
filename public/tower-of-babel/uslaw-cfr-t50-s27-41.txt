50 C.F.R. § 27.41 — General provisions.
Citation: us:cfr:50:27.41
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-27.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 27 (Prohibited Acts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Carrying, possessing, or discharging firearms, fireworks, or explosives on national wildlife refuges is prohibited unless specifically authorized under the provisions of this subchapter C.
