31 C.F.R. § 8.34 — Knowledge of client's omission.
Citation: us:cfr:31:8.34
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-8.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 8 (Practice Before The Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco And Firearms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each attorney, certified public accountant, or enrolled practitioner who knows that a client has not complied with applicable law, or has made an error in or omission from any document, affidavit, or other paper which the law requires the client to execute, shall advise the client promptly of the fact of such noncompliance, error, or omission.
