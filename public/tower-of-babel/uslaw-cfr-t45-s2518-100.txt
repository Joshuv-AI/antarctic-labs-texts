45 C.F.R. § 2518.100 — What is the purpose of a Service-Learning Clearinghouse?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2518.100
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2518.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2518 (Service-Learning Clearinghouse). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Corporation will provide financial assistance, from funds appropriated to carry out the activities listed under parts 2531 through 2534 of this chapter, to public or private nonprofit organizations that have extensive experience with service-learning, including use of adult volunteers to foster service-learning, to establish a clearinghouse, which will carry out activities, either directly or by arrangement with another such organization, with respect to information about service-learning.
