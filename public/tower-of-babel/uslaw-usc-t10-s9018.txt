10 U.S.C. § 9018 — Administrative Assistant
Citation: us:usc:10:9018
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9018
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 903 (Department Of The Air Force). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Air Force may appoint an Administrative Assistant in the Office of the Secretary of the Air Force. The Administrative Assistant shall perform such duties as the Secretary may prescribe.
