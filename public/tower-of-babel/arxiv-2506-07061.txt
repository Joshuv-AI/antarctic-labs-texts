Bialgebras induced by special left Alia algebras Thanks: * Corresponding author
Tianshui Ma 1,2*, Chan Zhao, Huihui Zheng

Abstract
. Special left Alia algebras were introduced by Dzhumadil’daev in [J. Math. Sci. (N.Y.) 161(2009), 11-30] when studying the classification of algebras with skew-symmetric identity of degree 3. A special left Alia algebra (resp. coalgebra) ( A , [ , ] ( f , g ) ) (A,[,]_{(f,g)}) (resp. ( A , Δ ( F , G ) ) (A,\Delta_{(F,G)}) ) is constructed by a commutative associative algebra (resp. cocommutative coassociative coalgebra) ( A , ⋅ ) (A,\cdot) (resp. ( A , δ ) (A,\delta) ) together with two linear maps f , g : A ⟶ A f,g:A\longrightarrow A (resp. F , G : A ⟶ A F,G:A\longrightarrow A ). We find that if ( ( A , ⋅ ) , f ) ((A,\cdot),f) (resp. ( ( A , δ ) , F ) ((A,\delta),F) ) is a Nijenhuis associative algebra (resp. coassociative coalgebra) such that f ∘ g = g ∘ f f\circ g=g\circ f (resp. F ∘ G = G ∘ F F\circ G=G\circ F ), then ( ( A , [ , ] ( f , g ) ) , f ) ((A,[,]_{(f,g)}),f) (resp. ( ( A , Δ ( F , G ) ) , F ) ((A,\Delta_{(F,G)}),F) ) is a Nijenhuis left Alia algebra (resp. coalgebra). A bialgebraic structure, named Nijenhuis associative D-bialgebra and denoted by ( ( A , ⋅ , δ ) , f , F ) ((A,\cdot,\delta),f,F) , for ( ( A , ⋅ ) , f ) ((A,\cdot),f) and ( ( A , δ ) , F ) ((A,\delta),F) was presented in [J. Algebra 639(2024), 150-186]. In this paper, we investigate the bialgebraic structure, named Nijenhuis left Alia bialgebra and denoted by ( ( A , [ , ] , Δ ) , N , S ) ((A,[,],\Delta),N,S) , for a Nijenhuis left Alia algebra ( ( A , [ , ] ) , N ) ((A,[,]),N) and a Nijenhuis left Alia coalgebra ( ( A , Δ ) , S ) ((A,\Delta),S) , such that Nijenhuis special left Alia bialgebra ( ( A , [ , ] ( f , g ) , Δ ( F , G ) ) , f , F ) ((A,[,]_{(f,g)},\Delta_{(F,G)}),f,F) can be induced by Nijenhuis commutative cocommutative associative D-bialgebra ( ( A , ⋅ , δ ) , f , F ) ((A,\cdot,\delta),f,F) . We also provide a method to construct Nijenhuis operators on a left Alia algebra (resp. coalgebra).

1.
Preliminaries and Introduction

Nijenhuis operators are an important class of operators in mathematics, with widespread applications in geometry, algebra, and analysis. Serving as a bridge connecting geometry, algebra, analysis, and discrete mathematics, the study of the Nijenhuis operator continuously drives the development of multiple disciplines.
Let
(A,\diamond)
be an algebra. Then a
Nijenhuis operator on
(A,\diamond)
is a linear map
N:A\longrightarrow A
such that for all
x,y\in A
, the condition below holds:

\displaystyle N(x)\diamond N(y)+N^{2}(x\diamond y)=N(N(x)\diamond y+x\diamond N(y)).

(1)

In this case, we call the pair
((A,\diamond),N)
a
Nijenhuis algebra
.

A
left anti-Lie-admissible algebra (abbr. left Alia algebra)
(also named a 0-Alia algebra) is a pair
(A,[,])
consisting of a vector space
A
and a bilinear map
[,]:A\otimes A\rightarrow A
satisfying the symmetric Jacobi identity

\displaystyle[[x,y],z]+[[y,z],x]+[[z,x],y]=[[y,x],z]+[[z,y],x]+[[x,z],y],

(2)

where
x,y,z\in A
. This class of algebras was introduced by Dzhumadil’daev in
[
3
]
when studying the classification of algebras with skew-symmetric identity of degree 3. There are various typical examples of left Alia algebras, including Lie algebras, anti-pre-Lie algebras
[
7
]
and mock-Lie algebras
[
11
,
12
]
, etc.

Let
(A,\cdot)
be a commutative associative algebra,
f,g:A\longrightarrow A
be linear maps. Define a multiplication
[,]_{(f,g)}
on
A
by

\displaystyle[x,y]_{(f,g)}:=x\cdot f(y)+g(x\cdot y),~\forall~x,y\in A.

(3)

Then by
[
3
, Theorem 6.2]
, we know that
(A,[,]_{(f,g)})
is a left Alia algebra, which is called by Dzhumadil’daev a
special left Alia algebra with respect to
(A,\cdot,f,g)
. Furthermore, we find that if
f
is a Nijenhuis operator on
(A,\cdot)
and
f\circ g=g\circ f
, then
f
is a Nijenhuis operator on
(A,[,]_{(f,g)})
(see Theorem
4.4
). Dually, let
(A,\delta)
be a cocommutative coassociative coalgebra,
F,G:A\longrightarrow A
be linear maps. Define a comultiplication
\Delta_{(F,G)}
on
A
by

\displaystyle\Delta_{(F,G)}(x)=x_{[1]}\otimes F(x_{[2]})+G(x)_{[1]}\otimes G(x)_{[2]},

(4)

where
\delta(x)=x_{[1]}\otimes x_{[2]},~\forall~x\in A
, then
(A,\Delta_{(F,G)})
is a left Alia coalgebra, which is called a
special left Alia coalgebra with respect to
(A,\delta,F,G)
. Further, if
F
is a Nijenhuis operator on
(A,\delta)
and
F\circ G=G\circ F
, then
F
is a Nijenhuis operator on
(A,\Delta_{(F,G)})
(see Proposition
4.6
).

In 2024, Ma and Long
[
8
]
presented a bialgebraic theory for Nijenhuis associative algebras, and introduced the notion of a Nijenhuis associative D-bialgebra
((A,\cdot,\delta),f,F)
by combining a Nijenhuis associative algebra
((A,\cdot),f)
and a Nijenhuis coassociative coalgebra
((A,\delta),F)
. Is there a bialgebraic structure
((A,[,]_{f,g},\Delta_{F,G}),f,F)
on the special left Alia algebra
(A,[,]_{(f,g)})
that can be derived by a Njienhuis commutative and cocommutative associative D-bialgebra
((A,\cdot,\delta),f,F)
) ? That is to say,

This is the motivation for writing this article.

In this paper we will explore the bialgebraic structure on a Nijenhuis left Alia algebra such that the special case
((A,[,]_{(f,g)},\Delta_{(g,f)}),f,F)
can be induced by Nijenhuis commutative cocommutative associative D-bialgebra
((A,\cdot,\delta),f,F)
. Also we find that Nijenhuis operators can be derived from a class of left Alia bialgebras.

This paper is organized as follows: In Section
2
, we give the bialgebraic structure on a Nijenhuis left Alia algebra by means of the equivalences among matched pair of Nijenhuis left Alia algebras and Manin triple of Nijenhuis left Alia algebras. In Section
3
, we focus on a class of Nijenhuis left Alia bialgebras, named triangular Nijenhuis left Alia bialgebras, which induces the compatible condition between the left Alia Yang-Baxter equation and Nijenhuis operators. We also provide some constructions of Nijenhuis left Alia bialgebras through relative Rota-Baxter operators. In Section
4
, we apply the bialgebraic theory to Nijenhuis special left Alia algebras and obtain that the necessary and sufficient condition for a Nijenhuis special left Alia algebra and a Nijenhuis special left Alia coalgebra to be a Nijenhuis special left Alia bialgebra. We also find that a class of Nijenhuis special left Alia algebras and Nijenhuis special left Alia coalgebras produce Nijenhuis special left Alia bialgebra automatically, then achieve the motivation of writing this article. In Section
5
, we present a method to construct Nijenhuis operators on left Alia (co)algebra based on a kind of left Alia bialgebras, which means that left Alia algebra and Nijenhuis operator on it are closely connected.
