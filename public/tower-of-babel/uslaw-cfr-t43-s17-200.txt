43 C.F.R. § 17.200 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:17.200
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-17.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 17 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs Of The Department Of The Interior). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to implement section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 and its subsequent amendments, which are designed to eliminate discrimination on the basis of handicap in any program or activity receiving Federal financial assistance.
