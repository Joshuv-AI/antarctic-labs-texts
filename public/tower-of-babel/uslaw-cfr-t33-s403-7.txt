33 C.F.R. § 403.7 — Action on applications; notices of requirements. [Rule 7]
Citation: us:cfr:33:403.7
Creator: Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-403.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 403 (Rules Of Procedure Of The Joint Tolls Review Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may at any time require the whole or any part of an application, answers or reply to be verified by affidavit, by giving a notice to that effect to the party from whom the affidavit is required. It the notice is not complied with, the Board may set aside the application, answer or reply or strike out any part not verified according to the notice. [Rule 7]
