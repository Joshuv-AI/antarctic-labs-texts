24 C.F.R. § 1000.315 — Is a recipient required to report changes to the Formula Current Assisted Stock (FCAS) on the Formula Response Form?
Citation: us:cfr:24:1000.315
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1000.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1000 (Native American Housing Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A recipient shall report changes to information related to the IHBG formula on the Formula Response Form, including corrections to the number of Formula Current Assisted Stock (FCAS), during the time period required by HUD. This time period shall be not less than 60 days from the date of the HUD letter transmitting the form to the recipient.
(b) The Formula Response Form is the only mechanism that a recipient shall use to report changes to the number of FCAS.
