20 C.F.R. § 404.1583 — How we determine disability for blind persons who are age 55 or older.
Citation: us:cfr:20:404.1583
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-404.1583
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 404 (Federal Old-Age, Survivors And Disability Insurance (1950- )). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will find that you are eligible for disability insurance benefits even though you are still engaging in substantial gainful activity, if—
(a) You are blind;
(b) You are age 55 or older; and
(c) You are unable to use the skills or abilities like the ones you used in any substantial gainful activity which you did regularly and for a substantial period of time. (However, you will not be paid any cash benefits for any month in which you are doing substantial gainful activity.)
