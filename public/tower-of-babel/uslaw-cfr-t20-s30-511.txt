20 C.F.R. § 30.511 — What is an “overpayment” for purposes of EEOICPA?
Citation: us:cfr:20:30.511
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-30.511
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 30 (Claims For Compensation Under The Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act Of 2000, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An “overpayment” is any amount of compensation paid under sections 7384s, 7384t, 7384u, 7385s-2 or 7385s-3 of the EEOICPA to a recipient that constitutes, as of the time OWCP makes such payment:
(a) Payment where no amount is payable under this part; or
(b) Payment in excess of the correct amount determined by OWCP.
