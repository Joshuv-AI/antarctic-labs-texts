45 C.F.R. § 158.611 — Settlement authority.
Citation: us:cfr:45:158.611
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-158.611
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 158 (Issuer Use Of Premium Revenue: Reporting And Rebate Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in § 158.606 through § 158.610 of this subpart limits the authority of HHS to settle any issue or case described in the notice furnished in accordance with § 158.603 of this subpart or to compromise on any penalty provided for in §§ 158.606 through 158.610 of this subpart.
