42 C.F.R. § 408.27 — Rounding the monthly premium.
Citation: us:cfr:42:408.27
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-408.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 408 (Premiums For Supplementary Medical Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any monthly premium that is not a multiple of 10 cents is rounded to the nearest multiple of 10 cents, and any odd multiple of 5 cents is rounded to the next higher multiple of 10 cents.
