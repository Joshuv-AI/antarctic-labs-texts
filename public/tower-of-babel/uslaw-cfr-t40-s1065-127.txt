40 C.F.R. § 1065.127 — Exhaust gas recirculation.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1065.127
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1065.127
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1065 (Engine-Testing Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use the exhaust gas recirculation (EGR) system installed with the engine or one that represents a typical in-use configuration. This includes any applicable EGR cooling devices.
