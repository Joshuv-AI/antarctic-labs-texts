46 C.F.R. § 151.03-11 — -11 Coastwise.
Citation: us:cfr:46:151.03-11
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-151.03-11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 151 (Barges Carrying Bulk Liquid Hazardous Material Cargoes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This designation refers to all vessels normally navigating the waters of any ocean or the Gulf of America 20 nautical miles or less offshore.
