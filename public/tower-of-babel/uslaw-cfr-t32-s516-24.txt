32 C.F.R. § 516.24 — Preservation of evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:32:516.24
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-516.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 516 (Litigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Because documents needed for litigation or administrative proceedings are subject to routine destruction, the SJA or legal adviser will ensure that all relevant documents are preserved.
