40 C.F.R. § 241.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:40:241.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-241.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 241 (Solid Wastes Used As Fuels Or Ingredients In Combustion Units). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part identifies the requirements and procedures for the identification of solid wastes used as fuels or ingredients in combustion units under section 1004 of the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act and section 129 of the Clean Air Act.
