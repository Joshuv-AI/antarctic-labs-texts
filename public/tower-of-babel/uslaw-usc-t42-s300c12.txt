42 U.S.C. § 300c–12 — Sudden infant death syndrome research
Citation: us:usc:42:300c–12
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300c–12
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter IX (Genetic Diseases, Hemophilia Programs, And Sudden Infant Death Syndrome); Part B (Sudden Unexpected Infant Death, Sudden Infant Death Syndrome, And Sudden Unexpected Death In Childhood). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

From the sums appropriated to the Eunice Kennedy Shriver National Institute of Child Health and Human Development, the Secretary shall assure that there are applied to research of the type described in subparagraphs (A) and (B) of subsection (b)(1) 1 of this section such amounts each year as will be adequate, given the leads and findings then available from such research, in order to make maximum feasible progress toward identification of infants at risk of sudden infant death syndrome and prevention of sudden infant death syndrome.
