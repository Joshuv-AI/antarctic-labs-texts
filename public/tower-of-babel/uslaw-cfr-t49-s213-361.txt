49 C.F.R. § 213.361 — Right of way.
Citation: us:cfr:49:213.361
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-213.361
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 213 (Track Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The track owner in Class 8 and 9 shall submit a barrier plan, termed a “right-of-way plan,” to the Federal Railroad Administration for approval. At a minimum, the plan will contain provisions in areas of demonstrated need for the prevention of—
(a) Vandalism;
(b) Launching of objects from overhead bridges or structures into the path of trains; and
(c) Intrusion of vehicles from adjacent rights of way.
