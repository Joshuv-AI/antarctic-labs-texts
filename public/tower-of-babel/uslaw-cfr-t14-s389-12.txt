14 C.F.R. § 389.12 — Payment of fees and charges.
Citation: us:cfr:14:389.12
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-389.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 389 (Fees And Charges For Special Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The fees charged for services and resources shall be paid for electronically at http://www.pay.gov, a secure government-wide collection portal, except for charges for reporting services that are performed under competitive bid contracts with non-Government firms. Fees for reporting are payable to the firms providing the services. Payments to pay.gov can be made directly from a bank account or by credit/debit card.
