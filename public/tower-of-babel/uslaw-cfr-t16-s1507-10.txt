16 C.F.R. § 1507.10 — Rockets with sticks.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1507.10
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1507.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1507 (Fireworks Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Rockets with sticks (including skyrockets and bottle rockets) shall utilize a straight and rigid stick to provide a direct and stable flight. Such sticks shall remain straight and rigid and attached to the driver so as to prevent the stick from being damaged or detached during transportation, handling, and normal operation.
