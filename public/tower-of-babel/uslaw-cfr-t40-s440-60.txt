40 C.F.R. § 440.60 — Applicability; description of the tungsten ore subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:440.60
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-440.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 440 (Ore Mining And Dressing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart F are applicable to discharges from (a) mines that produce tungsten ore and (b) mills that process tungsten ore by either the gravity separation or froth-flotation methods.
