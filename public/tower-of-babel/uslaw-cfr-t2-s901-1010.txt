2 C.F.R. § 901.1010 — Suspending official (Department of Energy supplement to government-wide definition at 2 CFR 180.1010).
Citation: us:cfr:2:901.1010
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-901.1010
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 901 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The suspending official for the Department of Energy, exclusive of NNSA, is the Director, Office of Procurement and Assistance Management, DOE. The suspending official for NNSA is the Director, Office of Acquisition and Supply Management, NNSA.
