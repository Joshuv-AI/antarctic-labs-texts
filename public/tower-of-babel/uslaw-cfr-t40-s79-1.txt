40 C.F.R. § 79.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:79.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-79.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 79 (Registration Of Fuels And Fuel Additives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations of this part apply to the registration of fuels and fuel additives designated by the Administrator, pursuant to section 211 of the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 1857f-6c, as amended by section 9, Pub. L. 91-604).
