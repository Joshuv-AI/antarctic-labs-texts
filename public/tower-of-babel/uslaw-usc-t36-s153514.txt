36 U.S.C. § 153514 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:36:153514
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section153514
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1535 (National Tropical Botanical Garden). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
Subject to subsection (b), there is authorized to be appropriated to the corporation for operation and maintenance expenses $500,000 for each of fiscal years 2008 through 2017.
(b) Limitation.—
Any Federal funds made available under subsection (a) shall be matched on a 1-to-1 basis by non-Federal funds.
