31 C.F.R. § 800.229 — Involvement.
Citation: us:cfr:31:800.229
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-800.229
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 800 (Regulations Pertaining To Certain Investments In The United States By Foreign Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term involvement means the right or ability to participate, whether or not exercised, including by doing any of the following:
(a) Providing input into a final decision;
(b) Consulting with or providing advice to a decisionmaker;
(c) Exercising special approval or veto rights;
(d) Participating on a committee with decisionmaking authority; or
(e) Advising on the appointment officers or selecting employees who are engaged in substantive decisionmaking.
