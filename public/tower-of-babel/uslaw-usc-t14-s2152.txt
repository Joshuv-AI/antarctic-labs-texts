14 U.S.C. § 2152 — Voluntary retirement after twenty years’ service
Citation: us:usc:14:2152
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2152
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 21 (Personnel; Officers); Subchapter II (Discharges; Retirements; Revocation Of Commissions; Separation For Cause). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any regular commissioned officer who has completed twenty years’ active service in the Coast Guard, Navy, Army, Air Force, Marine Corps, or Space Force, or the Reserve components thereof, including active duty for training, at least ten years of which shall have been active commissioned service, may, upon his own application, in the discretion of the President, be retired from active service.
