15 C.F.R. § 768.9 — Appeals of negative foreign availability determinations.
Citation: us:cfr:15:768.9
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-768.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 768 (Foreign Availability Determination Procedures And Criteria). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Appeals of negative determinations will be conducted according to the standards and procedures described in part 756 of the EAR. A Presidential decision (NSO) to deny a license or continue controls notwithstanding a determination of foreign availability is not subject to appeal.
