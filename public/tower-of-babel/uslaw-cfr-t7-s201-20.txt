7 C.F.R. § 201.20 — Germination
Citation: us:cfr:7:201.20
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-201.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 201 (Federal Seed Act Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The label shall show the percentage of germination for each kind, kind and variety, kind and type, or kind and hybrid of agricultural seed comprising more than 5 percent of the whole. The label shall show the percentage of germination for each kind, kind and variety, kind and type, or kind and hybrid of agricultural seed comprising 5 percent of the whole or less if the seed is identified individually on the label.
