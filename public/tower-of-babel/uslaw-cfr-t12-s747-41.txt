12 C.F.R. § 747.41 — Stays pending judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:12:747.41
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-747.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 747 (Administrative Actions, Adjudicative Hearings, Rules Of Practice And Procedure, And Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The commencement of proceedings for judicial review of a final decision and order of the NCUA Board may not, unless specifically ordered by the NCUA Board or a reviewing court, operate as a stay of any order issued by the NCUA Board. The NCUA Board may, in the NCUA Board's discretion, and on such terms as the NCUA Board finds just, stay the effectiveness of all or any part of an order pending a final decision on a petition for review of that order.
