43 C.F.R. § 3435.3-4 — -4 Determination of value.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3435.3-4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3435.3-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3430 (Noncompetitive Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The value of the land to be leased, or added by lease modification, or of the bidding rights to be issued in exchange shall, to the satisfaction of the lessee or lease applicant and the Secretary, be equal to the estimated fair market value of the lease or lease application to be relinquished.
