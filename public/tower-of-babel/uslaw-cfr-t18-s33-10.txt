18 C.F.R. § 33.10 — Additional information.
Citation: us:cfr:18:33.10
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-33.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 33 (Applications Under Federal Power Act Section 203). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director of the Office of Energy Market Regulation, or his designee, may, by letter, require the applicant to submit additional information as is needed for analysis of an application filed under this part.
