46 U.S.C. § 55336 — Civil penalty
Citation: us:usc:46:55336
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section55336
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part D (Promotional Programs); Chapter 553 (Passenger And Cargo Preferences); Subchapter III (American Great Lakes Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After notice and an opportunity for a hearing, the Secretary of Transportation may impose a civil penalty of not more than $1,000,000 on the owner of an American Great Lakes vessel for any act for which the designation may be revoked under section 55335 of this title.
