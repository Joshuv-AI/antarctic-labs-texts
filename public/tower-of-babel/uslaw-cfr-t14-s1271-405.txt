14 C.F.R. § 1271.405 — Penalty procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1271.405
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1271.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1271 (New Restrictions On Lobbying). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies shall impose and collect civil penalties pursuant to the provisions of the Program Fraud and Civil Remedies Act, 31 U.S.C. sections 3803 (except subsection (c)), 3804, 3805, 3806, 3807, 3808, and 3812, insofar as these provisions are not inconsistent with the requirements herein.
