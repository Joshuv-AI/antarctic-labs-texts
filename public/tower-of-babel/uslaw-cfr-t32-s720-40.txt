32 C.F.R. § 720.40 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:720.40
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-720.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 720 (Delivery Of Personnel; Service Of Process And Subpoenas; Production Of Official Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This instruction:
(a) Implements 32 CFR part 146.
(b) Establishes policy and procedures for requesting the return to the United States of, or other action affecting, Department of the Navy (DON) personnel and employees serving outside the United States, and family members accompanying them, in compliance with court orders.
