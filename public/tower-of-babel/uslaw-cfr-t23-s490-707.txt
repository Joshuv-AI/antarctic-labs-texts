23 C.F.R. § 490.707 — National performance management measures for traffic congestion.
Citation: us:cfr:23:490.707
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-490.707
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 490 (National Performance Management Measures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There are two performance measures to assess traffic congestion for the purpose of carrying out the CMAQ program (referred to collectively as the CMAQ Traffic Congestion measures. They are:
(a) Annual Hours of Peak Hour Excessive Delay (PHED) Per Capita (referred to as the PHED measure); and
(b) Percent of Non-SOV Travel.
