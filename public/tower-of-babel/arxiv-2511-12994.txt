Vanishing of weight one syzygies of projective varieties
Debjit Basu

Abstract
. In this article we study conditions under which weight one Koszul cohomology vanishes on projective varieties. As corollary of more general results, we obtain statements on the so-called property ( M q ) (M_{q}) reflecting on the higher syzygies of minimal surfaces and higher dimensional projective varieties. We also provide lower bounds on ℓ \ell such that L = ℓ ​ B L=\ell B and adjoint L ′ = K X + ℓ ​ B L^{\prime}=K_{X}+\ell B satisfies ( M q ) (M_{q}) for base point free and ample line bundle B B on a projective variety X. Property ( M q ) (M_{q}) is complementary to property − ( N p ) -(N_{p}) though it is far less studied. Notably, our bounds for property ( M q ) (M_{q}) for both L L and L ′ L^{\prime} are equal to or better than existing bounds for property ( N p ) (N_{p}) , as established by other authors—particularly in relation to Mukai’s conjecture and its higher-dimensional analogues. By considering both properties ( M q ) (M_{q}) and ( N p ) (N_{p}) , we gain a significantly deeper understanding of the minimal free resolution.



Introduction

In this article we study the vanishing of weight one syzygies of smooth projective varieties embedded by a complete linear series.

We begin this section by proving a theorem about projective normality of adjoint linear series on surfaces with nef canonical bundle.

Theorem 6.1
.

Let
X
be a surface with nef canonical bundle. Let
d\geq 1
be an integer and
B
be an ample and base point free line bundle on
X
such that the line bundle
\Lambda=K_{X}+B
is base point free. Let
\ell,m,k
be integers and

be any rational number. Then, for any nef line bundle
N
on
X,
the line bundles

satisfy the vanishing

(6.1)

H^{1}(M_{L}\otimes(mL^{\prime}+N))=0\hskip 14.22636pt\text{ for integer }m\geq 1

if one of the following conditions are satisfied

\begin{cases}\textbf{ }m\ell^{\prime}+\ell\geq 2d+2&\text{ if }((2d-1)B-2K_{X}\cdot\Lambda)>0\text{ or }((2d-1)B-2K_{X}\cdot\Lambda)\geq 0\text{ with }h^{0}(\Lambda)\geq 4\\
\textbf{ }m\ell^{\prime}+\ell\geq 2d+3&\text{ if }(dB-K_{X}\cdot\Lambda)>0\text{ or }(dB-K_{X}\cdot\Lambda)\geq 0\text{ with }h^{0}(\Lambda)\geq 4\\
\textbf{ }m\ell^{\prime}+\ell\geq 2d+4&\text{ if }(dB-K_{X}\cdot\Lambda)\geq 0.\end{cases}

Moreover,
L
is normally generated if one of the following conditions are satisfied

\begin{cases}\textbf{ }\ell\geq d+1&\text{ if }((2d-1)B-2K_{X}\cdot\Lambda)>0\text{ or }((2d-1)B-2K_{X}\cdot\Lambda)\geq 0\text{ with }h^{0}(\Lambda)\geq 4\\
\textbf{ }\ell\geq d+2&\text{ if }(dB-K_{X}\cdot\Lambda)\geq 0.\end{cases}

The vanishing (

) above is equivalent to the surjectivity

(6.2)

H^{0}(L)\otimes H^{0}(mL^{\prime}+N)\xrightarrow{\alpha}H^{0}(mL^{\prime}+L+N)

By Observation to prove the surjectivity of
\alpha
it is enough to prove the surjectivity of

(6.3)

H^{0}(B)\otimes H^{0}(mL^{\prime}+N)\xrightarrow[\hskip 17.07182pt]{\beta}H^{0}(mL^{\prime}+B+N)

and

(6.4)

H^{0}(\Lambda)\otimes H^{0}(mL^{\prime}+(\ell-1)B+N)\xrightarrow[\hskip 17.07182pt]{\gamma}H^{0}(mL^{\prime}+L+N)

H^{i}(G-iB)=H^{i}(K_{X}+(m\ell^{\prime}-i)B+N^{\prime})

and

or equivalently if
m\ell^{\prime}-2\geq 1
or
\ell^{\prime}\geq 3\geq\bigg\lceil\dfrac{3}{m}\bigg\rceil.
Now by Bertini’s theorem, there is a smooth curve
C
in
|B|
and there is a diagram

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces H^{0}(G)\otimes H^{0}(\mathcal{O}_{X})}

{\lx@inpgf@ignorespaces H^{0}(G)}
