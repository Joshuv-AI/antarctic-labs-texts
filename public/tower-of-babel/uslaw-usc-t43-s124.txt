43 U.S.C. § 124 — Consolidation of land offices
Citation: us:usc:43:124
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section124
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 5 (Land Districts). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall be the duty of the Secretary of the Interior to consolidate the district land offices where practicable and consistent with the public interests.
