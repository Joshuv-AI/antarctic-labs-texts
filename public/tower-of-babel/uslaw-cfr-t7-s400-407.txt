7 C.F.R. § 400.407 — Restricted access.
Citation: us:cfr:7:400.407
Creator: Federal Crop Insurance Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-400.407
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 400 (General Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Manager, other officer, or employee of FCIC or an authorized person may have access to the SSNs and EINs obtained pursuant to this subpart, only for the purpose of establishing and maintaining a system of records necessary for the effective administration of the Act.
