37 C.F.R. § 42.7 — Management of the record.
Citation: us:cfr:37:42.7
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-42.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 42 (Trial Practice Before The Patent Trial And Appeal Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Board may expunge any paper directed to a proceeding or filed while an application or patent is under the jurisdiction of the Board that is not authorized under this part or in a Board order or that is filed contrary to a Board order.
(b) The Board may vacate or hold in abeyance any non-Board action directed to a proceeding while an application or patent is under the jurisdiction of the Board unless the action was authorized by the Board.
