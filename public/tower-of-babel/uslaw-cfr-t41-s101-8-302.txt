41 C.F.R. § 101-8.302 — -8.302 General prohibitions.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-8.302
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-8.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-8 (8—Nondiscrimination In Programs Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No qualified handicapped persons shall, on the basis of handicap, be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity that receives Federal assistance from GSA.
