28 C.F.R. § 551.113 — Counseling.
Citation: us:cfr:28:551.113
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-551.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 551 (Miscellaneous). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) When consistent with institution security and good order, pretrial inmates may be allowed the opportunity to receive counseling services with convicted inmates.
(b) Staff shall ensure that pretrial inmates who do not receive counseling services with convicted inmates have access to other counseling services.
