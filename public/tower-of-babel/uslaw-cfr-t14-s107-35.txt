14 C.F.R. § 107.35 — Operation of multiple small unmanned aircraft.
Citation: us:cfr:14:107.35
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-107.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 107 (Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person may not manipulate flight controls or act as a remote pilot in command or visual observer in the operation of more than one unmanned aircraft at the same time.
