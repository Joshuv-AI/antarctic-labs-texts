5 C.F.R. § 359.807 — Records.
Citation: us:cfr:5:359.807
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-359.807
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 359 (Removal From The Senior Executive Service; Guaranteed Placement In Other Personnel Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency shall preserve all records relating to an action under this subpart for at least one year from the effective date of the action.
