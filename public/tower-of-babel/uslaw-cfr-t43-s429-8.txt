43 C.F.R. § 429.8 — Is there a fee for uses involving a Reclamation easement?
Citation: us:cfr:43:429.8
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-429.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 429 (Use Of Bureau Of Reclamation Land, Facilities, And Waterbodies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Reclamation will not charge a use fee for a consent document. However, depending upon the complexity of your requested use and issues associated with it, Reclamation may charge an application fee and administrative costs, unless waived in accordance with subpart F of this part.
