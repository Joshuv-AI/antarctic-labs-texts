16 U.S.C. § 213 — Execution of instruments necessary to carry out purposes of gift
Citation: us:usc:16:213
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section213
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXIII (Abraham Lincoln Birthplace National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President of the United States of America and the Secretary of War are authorized to execute, in the name of the United States of America, such instrument or instruments as may be or may become necessary to comply with or carry out the terms and conditions of such gift or gifts and to secure the full benefit therefrom.
