34 C.F.R. § 609.43 — Under what conditions does the Secretary terminate a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:34:609.43
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-609.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 609 (Strengthening Historically Black Graduate Institutions Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary terminates any grant under which funds were not expended if an institution loses—
(a) Its accredited status; or
(b) Its legal authority in the State in which it is located.
