19 U.S.C. § 4341 — Definition of intellectual property rights
Citation: us:usc:19:4341
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4341
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 28 (Trade Facilitation And Trade Enforcement); Subchapter III (Import-Related Protection Of Intellectual Property Rights). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter, the term “intellectual property rights” refers to copyrights, trademarks, and other forms of intellectual property rights that are enforced by U.S. Customs and Border Protection or U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement.
