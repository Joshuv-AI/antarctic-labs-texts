30 C.F.R. § 1206.363 — When is an ONRR audit, review, reconciliation, monitoring, or other like process considered final?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1206.363
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1206.363
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1206 (Product Valuation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any provision in these regulations to the contrary, no audit, review, reconciliation, monitoring, or other like process that results in a redetermination by ONRR of royalty or fees due under this subpart is considered final or binding as against the Federal Government or its beneficiaries until ONRR formally closes the audit period in writing.
