Medicare Program; Alternative Payment Model (APM) Incentive 
Payment Advisory for Clinicians--Request for Current Billing 
Information for Qualifying APM Participants; Correction

AGENCY: Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), Department of 
Health and Human Services (HHS).

ACTION: Payment advisory; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document corrects a technical error in the payment 
advisory that appeared in the August 13, 2026, Federal Register titled 
``Medicare Program; Alternative Payment Model (APM) Incentive Payment 
Advisory for Clinicians--Request for Current Billing Information for 
Qualifying APM Participants''.

DATES: Applicability Date: This correcting document is applicable 
August 13, 2026.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Tanya Dorm, (667) 290-9530.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Background

    In FR Doc. 2026-16472 of August 13, 2026, entitled '' Medicare 
Program; Alternative Payment Model (APM) Incentive Payment Advisory for 
Clinicians-Request for Current Billing Information for Qualifying APM 
Participants'' (91 FR 52305), there was a technical error that is 
identified and corrected in this correcting document.

II. Summary of Errors

    On page 52305, we inadvertently did not provide a hyperlink 
corresponding to the web address where relevant QPs who anticipated 
receiving an APM Inventive Payment but have not received a payment, may 
provide CMS with updated Medicare billing information.''

III. Correction of Errors

    In FR Doc. 2026-16472 of August 13, 2026, (91 FR 52305), make the 
following correction:
    1. On page 52305, second column, third full paragraph, lines 10 and 
11, remove the phrase ``2026 QP Notice for APM Incentive'' and add in 
its place ``https://d2g5m5leph8kam.cloudfront.net/s3fs/s3fs-public/
2026-08/2026-qp-notice-for-apm-incentive-
payment.zip?VersionId=EfxfCWv.NH0e9Nd59vkJe6AGs8qa8bsK''.
    The Director of the Office of Strategic Operations and Regulatory 
Affairs of the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), Kathleen 
Cantwell, having reviewed and approved this document, authorizes Chyana 
Woodyard, who is the Federal Register Liaison, to electronically sign 
this document for purposes of publication in the Federal Register.
