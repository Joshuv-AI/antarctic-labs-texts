36 C.F.R. § 291.7 — Public awareness and education.
Citation: us:cfr:36:291.7
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-291.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 291 (Paleontological Resources Preservation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chief of the Forest Service will establish a program to increase public awareness about the significance of paleontological resources on National Forest System lands.
