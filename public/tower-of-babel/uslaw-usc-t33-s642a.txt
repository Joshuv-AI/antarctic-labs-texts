33 U.S.C. § 642a — Rank, pay, and allowances of Corps of Engineers officers serving as President of Mississippi River Commission
Citation: us:usc:33:642a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section642a
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 13 (Mississippi River Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any officer of the Corps of Engineers who has served or shall serve four years as President of the Mississippi River Commission and who has been or shall subsequently be retired, shall, from the date of such retirement, receive the rank, pay, and allowances of a retired major general.
