7 U.S.C. § 228d — Annual assessment of cattle and hog industries
Citation: us:usc:7:228d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section228d
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 9 (Packers And Stockyards); Subchapter V (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than March 1 of each year, the Secretary shall submit to Congress and make publicly available a report that—
(1)
assesses the general economic state of the cattle and hog industries;
(2)
describes changing business practices in those industries; and
(3)
identifies market operations or activities in those industries that appear to raise concerns under this chapter.
