46 C.F.R. § 541.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:46:541.1
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-541.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 541 (Demurrage And Detention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes the minimum information that must be included on or with demurrage and detention invoices. It also establishes procedures that must be adhered to when invoicing for demurrage or detention.
