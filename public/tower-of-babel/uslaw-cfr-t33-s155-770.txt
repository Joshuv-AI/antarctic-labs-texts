33 C.F.R. § 155.770 — Draining into bilges.
Citation: us:cfr:33:155.770
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-155.770
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 155 (Oil Or Hazardous Material Pollution Prevention Regulations For Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may intentionally drain oil or hazardous material from any source into the bilge of a vessel.
