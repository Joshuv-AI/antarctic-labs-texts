34 C.F.R. § 681.36 — Consequence of using an agent.
Citation: us:cfr:34:681.36
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-681.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 681 (Health Education Assistance Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The delegation of functions to a servicing agency or other party does not relieve a lender or holder of its responsibilities under the HEAL program.
