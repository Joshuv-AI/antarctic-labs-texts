16 C.F.R. § 1018.35 — Termination of membership.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1018.35
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1018.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1018 (Advisory Committee Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Advisory committee membership may be terminated at any time upon a determination by the Commission that such action is appropriate.
