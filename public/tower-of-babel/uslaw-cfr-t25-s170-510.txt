25 C.F.R. § 170.510 — What funds are available for Tribal Transportation Facility Bridge activities?
Citation: us:cfr:25:170.510
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-170.510
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 170 (Tribal Transportation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Funds are made available in 23 U.S.C. 202(d) for improving deficient bridges eligible for the TTP.
