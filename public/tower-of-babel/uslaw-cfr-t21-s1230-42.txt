21 C.F.R. § 1230.42 — Invoices.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1230.42
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1230.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1230 (Regulations Under The Federal Caustic Poison Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As soon as the importer makes entry, the invoices covering containers and the public stores packages shall be made available, with the least possible delay, for inspection by the representative of the district. When no sample is desired the invoice shall be stamped by the district “No sample desired, Food and Drug Administration, Department of Health and Human Services, per (initials of inspecting officer).”
