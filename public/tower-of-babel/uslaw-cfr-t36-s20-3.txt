36 C.F.R. § 20.3 — Maximum number of permittees.
Citation: us:cfr:36:20.3
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-20.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 20 (Isle Royale National Park; Commercial Fishing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Commercial fishermen to whom the annual revocable permits may be granted shall not exceed the maximum number of persons conducting commercial fishing operations from bases in the area comprising the Park at any one time during the period from April 1, 1937 to December 31, 1939, inclusive.
