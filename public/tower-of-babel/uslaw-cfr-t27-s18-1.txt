27 C.F.R. § 18.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:27:18.1
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-18.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 18 (Production Of Volatile Fruit-Flavor Concentrate). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part relate to the qualification and operation (including activities incident thereto) of plants for the manufacture of volatile fruit-flavor concentrate (essence). The regulations in this part apply to the several States of the United States and the District of Columbia.
