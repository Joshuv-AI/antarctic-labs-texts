22 U.S.C. § 286ff — Fund interest rates
Citation: us:usc:22:286ff
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section286ff
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XV (International Monetary Fund And Bank For Reconstruction And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Treasury shall instruct the United States Executive Director of the Fund to propose and work for the adoption of Fund policies regarding the rate of remuneration paid on use of member’s quota subscriptions and the rate of charges on Fund drawings to bring those rates in line with market rates.
