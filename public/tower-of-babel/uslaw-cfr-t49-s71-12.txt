49 C.F.R. § 71.12 — Hawaii-Aleutian zone.
Citation: us:cfr:49:71.12
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-71.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 71 (Standard Time Zone Boundaries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The seventh zone, the Hawaii-Aleutian standard time zone, includes the entire State of Hawaii and, in the State of Alaska, that part of the Aleutian Islands that is west of 169 degrees 30 minutes west longitude.
