14 C.F.R. § 389.27 — Refund of fee.
Citation: us:cfr:14:389.27
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-389.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 389 (Fees And Charges For Special Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any fee charged under this part may be refunded in full or in part upon request if the document for which it is charged is withdrawn before final action is taken. Such requests shall be filed in accordance with § 389.23.
