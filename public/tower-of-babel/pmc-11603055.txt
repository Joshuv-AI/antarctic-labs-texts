Transformer abnormal heat accurate identification method based on AHIPDNet
Authors: Liu Haoyu, Gao Shuguo, Tian Xu, Zang Qian, Guo Meng, Li Keyu, Pei Shaotong, Wang Weiqi
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Transformer is an important part of the power system, and its operation status is related to the safety and stability of the power system. In order to effectively improve the recognition accuracy of abnormal transformer heat, an improved YOLOv8 target detection algorithm model is proposed. The introduction of the SPD-Conv convolutional layer effectively improves the model’s recognition ability of the small target, and the introduction of the HAT hybrid attention mechanism and Dyhead multiple attention even if the information focusing on the abnormal hot spot is realized. The HAT hybrid attention mechanism and Dyhead multi-attention mechanism are introduced to better focusing of the model on the information of abnormal hot spots. Finally, the AHIPDNet transformer abnormal heat recognition method is formed. After experimental verification, the algorithm model achieves 93.1% recognition accuracy, which satisfies the demand of accurate recognition and localization of transformer’s abnormal heat in the field.

Introduction

Transformer is an important part of the power system. Its equipment failure will affect the safe and stable operation of the power system, and may even lead to widespread power outages. Through the regular inspection of the transformer, the timely detection of hidden equipment failure and processing, the stability of transformer operation can be improved and the failure rate can be reduced. Therefore, how to carry out accurate identification of hidden faults on the transformer is the core of the transformer inspection1.

Transformer early failure is often accompanied by the emergence of local anomalous hot spots2. Currently, infrared detection methods are used to identify anomalous hot spots, so as to realize the detection of equipment failure. The infrared inspection is to identify the image captured by the infrared camera manually3, and this method has the problem of low efficiency and high labor cost. With the development of deep learning technology, automatic identification of abnormal hot spots through deep learning technology4 has been recognized and applied by the transformer operation and maintenance department owing to its high efficiency.

Regarding the transformer abnormal heat automatic identification algorithm, currently there are a large number of studies. Based on the YOLOv8 algorithm and infrared thermal imaging technology, Chen et al.5 developped a rapid identification of abnormal heat of power equipment relying on cloud edge collaboration. Cong et al.6 adopted the SPPF module of ASPPF to improve YOLOv5 and to achieve accurate identification of abnormal heating fault detection of power substation equipment. Zhang et al.7 utilized an enhanced feature pyramid network (FPN) and introduced a dynamic soft label allocation strategy for ConvNeXt target detection technology to achieve high accuracy detection of infrared images of power equipment. Ou et al.8 proposed a Faster-RCNN network that discards some advanced convolutional layers and increases the aspect ratio of anchors to realize high-precision, high-speed, and highly robust infrared detection of substation electrical equipment. Li et al.9 proposed a YOLOv4-Tiny model combining global information aggregation module and spatial transformer network together with a comprehensive and improved cross-fusion structure of multi-scale features to realize a high detection accuracy of infrared images of electrical equipment. Regarding the detection accuracy of power equipment in infrared image detection model, Zhou et al.10 proposed a model that uses U-Net convolutional neural network and infrared image to realize excellent substation detection. Tang et al.11 improved RetinaNet for accurate recognition of tilted electrical equipment using rotated rectangle framework, path aggregation module, and attention mechanism. Xiang et al.12 utilized deformable convolution and C2f module synthesis to form DCNC2f module, and introduced multi-scale attention mechanism module to improve YOLOv8 model to form high-precision infrared detection algorithm for substation equipment.

The above work uses deep learning models for target detection of substation power equipment, and all of them have realized effective detection accuracy with improved speed. Nevertheless, with the automatic identification of abnormal transformer hot spots constructed based on the target detection algorithm, potential problems that require further attention have emerged. The infrared image of the transformer captured in the actual field is smaller than the overall image due to the shooting distance or the actual hot spot, which greatly affects the model’s identification and detection. This has a great impact on the recognition and detection of the model, which can lead to a significant decrease in the detection accuracy of the model.

In this paper, in response to the problems in the above research and the need for accurate identification of abnormal heat spots, the YOLOv8 algorithm is improved, and the Algorithm for Accurate Detection of Abnormal Heat Infrared (AHIPDNet) is proposed and constructed. YOLOv8 Algorithm principles

YOLOv8 algorithm is a new algorithm improved by the team of YOLOv5 algorithm on its basis, which introduces new improvements on the basis of the success of the previous YOLO series13–20algorithms to realize the performance of target detection of SOTA. Owing to their excellent performance, YOLOv8 and the YOLO series of target detection algorithms have been heavily applied in the electric power field21–23.

YOLOv8 still adopts the same structure as the YOLO series, i.e., it consists of three parts: Backbone, Neck, and Head.

In the backbone network, YOLOv8 still adopts the CSPDarkNet53 structure similar to that of YOLOv5, and only replaces its C3 structure with a C2f module with multiple BottleNeck structures as shown in Fig. 1, which introduces more hopping structures and additional Split operations than the C3 module, which realizes the acquisition of more gradient flow information in a lighter weight.

In the neck network part, except that the C3 module is replaced by the C2f module as in the Backbone part, the two convolutional connection layers are removed in the Neck part, which makes its FPN structure closer to that of BiFPN24.

AHIPDNet

Although the YOLOv8 algorithm achieves SOTA detection results, its development is targeted at large-scale object detection datasets such as COCO, and is not optimized for small or low-resolution targets. In order to achieve highly accurate recognition of abnormal heat in infrared images, this paper takes the YOLOv8 algorithm as the benchmark algorithm, and improves its convolutional layer, backbone network and detection head in a targeted way, forming the AHIPDNet al.gorithm.

The convolutional layer is the core of the convolutional neural network, and its performance determines the detection effect of the model. In this paper, SPD-Conv25 is introduced to replace the original convolutional layer, and the extraction of low-resolution features is realized by the down-sampling of SPD-Conv without losing the learning information.

The backbone network and the detection head firstly enhance the algorithm’s ability to extract low-resolution target features effectively by adding the HATtention super-resolution attention mechanism26 and the Dyhead multi-head attention mechanism27, while the SIoU28 loss function is introduced in the detection head to improve the convergence speed and detection accuracy of the algorithm.

SPD-Conv

The current target detection models based on convolutional neural networks such as R-CNN series and YOLO series are designed for high-quality image information, such as Microsoft COCO and Pascal VOC datasets. However, when the resolution of their input images is lowered, their accuracy rate significantly decreases. The source of this problem lies in the fact that the pooling layer or the step-length-convolutional layer skips part of the pixel information of the input image, and the high-resolution or large-target image has a large amount of redundant information to ensure that the model can still learn the features after skipping part of the information. However, when inputting the infrared image, due to its lower resolution or small target, the image will no longer have redundant information, and the conventional model will produce the problems of feature insufficiency and information loss.

Here, the convolutional layer in YOLOv8 is improved to deal with the problems mentioned above, and in the specific improvement, the space-to-depth convolution (SPD-Conv) is introduced. The composition and principle of SPD-Conv are shown in Fig. 2, which mainly consists of space-to-depth layer and non-step-length convolution.

Fig. 2SPD-Conv Composition and Principle.

SPD-Conv Composition and Principle.

The main role of the space-to-depth layer is to preserve the image feature information while realizing the dimensional downsampling of the feature map, and its main principle is shown in Fig. 2(a). Firstly, by extracting the pixel blocks of the image, four feature maps with unchanged channel dimensions and spatial dimensions of S/2 of the original image are formed. Then four feature maps are merged in the channel dimensions to form the feature maps with spatial dimensions of 1/4 of the original feature maps, and the feature maps with channel dimensions 4 times of the original feature maps. The space-to-depth layer can be realized to reduce the spatial dimension without losing the spatial information.

Non-step convolution is the convolution with step size 1, and its role in SPD-Conv is shown in Fig. 2(b), i.e., the feature map formed by the space-to-depth layer is processed to generate the feature map with the required channel dimension of C2. Since the step size of the non-compensated convolutional layer is 1, it moves pixel by pixel on the feature map and does not skip pixels as in the case of the traditional convolution, so that the feature map is efficiently realized without loss of feature information. This can effectively accomplish the dimensionality reduction of the feature map channel without loss of feature information.

SPD-Conv utilizes a combination of space-to-depth layers and non-step-length convolution to effectively improve the model’s ability to process low-resolution images. Here, it was used to replace the convolutional and pooling layers of the YOLOv8 algorithm.

HAT hybrid attention mechanism

Conventional attention mechanisms are mainly used for the diagnosis of conventional resolution images without considering the recognition of low-resolution images. For the infrared images of transformer components, a super-resolution attention mechanism is introduced to improve the accuracy of attention for the recognition of small or low-resolution targets.

The Hybird Attention Transformer (HAT) hybrid attention mechanism used was proposed by ChenXiangyu et al. in 2023, and its main structure is shown in Fig. 3. For a given low-resolution image input, a conventional convolutional layer is firstly used to carry out shallow feature extraction; then multiple residual hybrid attention groups are used. For a given low-resolution image input, a conventional convolutional layer is used for shallow feature extraction. Then multiple Residual Hybrid Attention Groups (RHAGs) and a conventional convolution are used for deep feature extraction. Finally, global residual linking is used to complete the splicing of the deep and shallow features, and the reconstruction module is used to realize the reconstruction of the low-resolution image to the high-resolution image.

Fig. 3Main structure of HAT.

Main structure of HAT.

The RHAG module is shown in Fig. 3, which includes several Hybrid Attention Blocks (HAB), an Overlapping Cross-Attention Block (OCAB), and a regular convolutional layer. The HAB module consists of a window self-attention mechanism and a channel attention mechanism. After the features enter the HAB module, they are first normalized, and then the input features are first divided into local feature windows by the window self-attention mechanism, and the self-attention is computed in each window. After that, the channel attention mechanism is used to compute the global information, so as to achieve the weighting of features using the global information. At last, the weighted features of the two types of attention mechanisms are summed up with the original input features through the convolution layer, so that the network can utilize both local and cross-attention mechanisms simultaneously. The OCAB module realizes the establishment of cross-connections between multiple windows in window attention by introducing overlapping cross-attention layers, which achieves a significant improvement in the feature expression ability of the network.

The reconstruction module adopted by HAT utilizes Pixel-Shuffle to achieve up-sampling fusion features. In our model, it is added to the backbone network of the model. Since the HAT module can realize the super-resolution attention mechanism for low-resolution images, it can significantly improve the feature extraction ability of the model for low-resolution images to achieve a significant increase in accuracy.

Since the introduction of the HAT module into the backbone network can be done in series and parallel, according to the literature29, the actual effect of the two ways is the same. Therefore, the HAT hybrid attention module and the Backbone part of the parallel connection are used to add the HAT module to the network.

Dyhead multiple attention detection head mechanism

Due to the low differentiation of the background information of the infrared image, the hot spot caused by some defects is relatively small compared to the normal abnormality. The deep learning algorithm is very easy to be interfered by the complex environment and small targets when recognizing the defects of the transformer components. Therefore, it is necessary to effectively improve the model’s attention to defects, and focus more resources on the extraction of feature information of defects.

In the hyper-parameter setting, in order to avoid the situation that the model has not enough training rounds to achieve the highest accuracy rate, the training rounds are set to 5000 and the training strategy of terminating the training when the mAP is not improved in more than 300 Epoch trainings is adopted.

LabelImg software is used to annotate a total of 2,133 images collected in the field, including 721 small targets and 1,412 large targets. The data obtained after labeling is constructed to form the transformer abnormal heating dataset. Then, the transformer abnormal heating dataset is devided into training set and testing set according to the ratio of 7:3, which is used as the benchmark for later experiments.

In order to guarantee the quality of the annotation, the group standard T/SDPEA 0015-2019,29;29.240.99, “Labeling Specification for Image Data Set of Hidden Danger/Defect Target Recognition in Transmission and Trasformation Patrol” issued by China Shandong Provincial Electric Power Enterprises Association in 2019 is adopted. Firstly, the annotators are required to carry out self-review, and then special inspectors are assigned to be responsible for the second review of the annotation results.

As can be seen from Table 3, the YOLOv8 network has a detection speed of 22 frames/s, a detection accuracy of 78.7% for small targets, and a detection accuracy of 97.5% for large targets when no improvements are made.

Network-1 introduced SPD-Conv to replace some of the convolutional layers in the network, and there was essentially no improvement in the detection accuracy for large targets, but the detection rate for small targets improved by 2.6–81.3%. The reason for the improvement is that for large targets, the loss of information caused by traditional convolution does not affect the detection. However, for small targets, due to the minimal pixel information, the loss of information caused by traditional convolution will seriously affect the accuracy of the algorithm, and SPD-Conv avoids the loss of pixel information compared with the traditional convolutional layer, and adopts stepless convolution to retain more feature information compared with the step convolution. Therefore, the detection accuracy is improved, and the stepless convolution can retain more feature information than the step-length convolution. Therefore, it significantly improves the input feature information and the model’s recognition accuracy for small targets.

Network-2 introduces the HAT hybrid attention mechanism module on the basis of network-1, which enhances the small target detection accuracy by 5.6%, and the small target detection accuracy reaches 86.9%, which is owing to the fact that the HAT hybrid attention mechanism module adopts the super-resolution attention mechanism, which can realize stronger feature extraction effect for the low-resolution infrared image.

Network-3 uses the Dyhead multiple attention detection module on the basis of Network-2, which is also effective in improving the detection accuracy of small targets, because it can effectively focus the model’s attention on the recognition of the target identification, and has a stronger anti-interference ability for the single color-convergent background in the infrared image.

Finally, Network-4, i.e. DETIDNET, on the basis of network-3, adopts the SIoU loss function to replace the original CIoU loss function. The SIoU solves the problem of the drift of the prediction frame of the CIoU loss function so that the detection accuracy of Net-3 has been slightly improved by 0.8%.

Since the modules introduced or replaced here have improved the complexity of the model, the detection speed of AHIPDNet is reduced by 10 frames/s and the GFLOPs are improved by 23.9 compared to the original YOLOv8.

The results of the ablation experiments show that the proposed improvement to YOLOv8 improves the detection accuracy of the model for small targets by 10.2%, which is a significant improvement and verifies the effectiveness of the DETIDNET algorithm proposed here.

Conclusion

In this study, the AHIPDNet transformer anomalous heat accurately identification algorithm model is proposed, which effectively improves the identification accuracy of transformer anomalous heat spots that are smaller or have more complex backgrounds. This study improves the YOLOv8 algorithm by firstly using SPD-Conv to improve the conventional convolutional layer, which enhances the feature extraction ability of the model for small targets without affecting the detection speed, and which significantly improves the recognition accuracy of the model for small targets. Then, the HAT hybrid attention mechanism and the Dyhead attention mechanism are introduced in the neck and the head network, which realizes the model’s ability to recognize the abnormal hot spots in infrared images by better focusing on the key information, which has a significant impact on the detection of small targets, especially in the complex background. Finally, the SIoU loss function is adopted in the loss function part, which solves the problems of slow convergence speed and the convergence oscillation of the model. The AHIPDNet proposed in this study confirms its excellent performance, especially in the detection accuracy of small targets, by performing training and ablation experiments and comparison experiments on the infrared image dataset constructed. This study advances the application of target detection algorithms in the field of abnormal heat detection of power equipment, which is of positive significance in promoting smart grid and provides ideas for similar work.

The AHIPDNet algorithm model has more potential application in the electric power field, and future research is proposed to focus on the following aspects:

The next step is to carry out a detection model for insulators, converter valves and other power equipment for abnormal heat detection.6.3. Model lightweight: Further research can use information distillation, model pruning and hardware acceleration to optimize the inference speed of the model to achieve high-speed inference of the model on the edge of the end of the device or mobile device, so that inspectors can carry out real-time inspection at the inspection site.
