33 C.F.R. § 143.302 — Hazardous location requirements on foreign vessels engaged in OCS activities.
Citation: us:cfr:33:143.302
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-143.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 143 (Design And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel that is documented under the laws of a foreign nation and is constructed after April 2, 2018 must comply with the requirements of 46 CFR subpart 111.108 prior to engaging in OCS activities.
