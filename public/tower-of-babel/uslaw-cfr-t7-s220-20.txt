7 C.F.R. § 220.20 — Free and reduced price breakfasts.
Citation: us:cfr:7:220.20
Creator: Food And Nutrition Administration, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-220.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 220 (School Breakfast Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The determination of the children to whom free and reduced price breakfasts are to be served because of inability to pay the full price thereof, and the serving of the breakfasts to such children, shall be effected in accordance with part 245 of this chapter.
