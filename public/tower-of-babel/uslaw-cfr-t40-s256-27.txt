40 C.F.R. § 256.27 — Recommendation for schedules leading to compliance with the prohibition of open dumping.
Citation: us:cfr:40:256.27
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-256.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 256 (Guidelines For Development And Implementation Of State Solid Waste Management Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In reviewing applications for compliance schedules under § 256.26, the State should consider the availability of processing and disposal facilities, the likelihood of environmental damage from disposal at available facilities, the existence of State or substate requirements (including other compliance schedules) applicable to available facilities, cost constraints, existing contractual agreements and other pertinent factors.
