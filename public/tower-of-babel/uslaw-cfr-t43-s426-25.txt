43 C.F.R. § 426.25 — Reclamation audits.
Citation: us:cfr:43:426.25
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-426.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 426 (Acreage Limitation Rules And Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Reclamation will conduct reviews of a district's administration and enforcement of and landholder compliance with Federal reclamation law and these regulations. These reviews may include, but are not limited to:
(a) Water district reviews;
(b) In-depth reviews; and
(c) Audits.
