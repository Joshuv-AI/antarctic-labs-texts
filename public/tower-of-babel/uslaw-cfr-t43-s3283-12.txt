43 C.F.R. § 3283.12 — Are transferees and successors in interest of Federal geothermal leases bound by the terms and conditions of the unit agreement?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3283.12
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3283.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3280 (Geothermal Resources Unit Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The terms and conditions of the unit agreement are binding on transferees and successors in interest to Federal geothermal leases committed to a unit agreement.
