24 C.F.R. § 93.408 — Performance reports.
Citation: us:cfr:24:93.408
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-93.408
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 93 (Housing Trust Fund). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each grantee must develop and maintain a system to track the use of its HTF funds, and submit annual performance and management reports on its HTF program in accordance with 24 CFR 91.520. HUD will make the performance and management reports publicly available.
