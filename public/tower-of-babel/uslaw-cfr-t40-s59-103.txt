40 C.F.R. § 59.103 — Container labeling requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:40:59.103
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-59.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 59 (National Volatile Organic Compound Emission Standards For Consumer And Commercial Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each regulated entity subject to this subpart must clearly display on each automobile refinish coating or coating component container or package, the day, month, and year on which the product was manufactured, or a code indicating such date.
