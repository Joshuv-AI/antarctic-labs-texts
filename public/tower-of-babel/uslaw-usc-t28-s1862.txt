28 U.S.C. § 1862 — Discrimination prohibited
Citation: us:usc:28:1862
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1862
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part V (Procedure); Chapter 121 (Juries; Trial By Jury). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No citizen shall be excluded from service as a grand or petit juror in the district courts of the United States or in the Court of International Trade on account of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, or economic status.
