17 C.F.R. § 4.15 — Continued applicability of antifraud section.
Citation: us:cfr:17:4.15
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-4.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 4 (Commodity Pool Operators And Commodity Trading Advisors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of section 4o of the Act shall apply to any person even though such person is exempt from registration under this part 4, and it shall continue to be unlawful for any such person to violate section 4o of the Act.
