14 C.F.R. § 152.311 — Availability of contractor's records.
Citation: us:cfr:14:152.311
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-152.311
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 152 (Airport Aid Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The sponsor or planning agency shall include in each contract of the cost reimbursable type a clause that allows any authorized representative of the Administrator, the Secretary of Transportation, or the Comptroller General of the United States access to the contractor's records pertinent to the contract for the purposes of accounting and audit.
