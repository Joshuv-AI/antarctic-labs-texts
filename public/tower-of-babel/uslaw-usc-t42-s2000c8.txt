42 U.S.C. § 2000c–8 — Personal suits for relief against discrimination in public education
Citation: us:usc:42:2000c–8
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2000c–8
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 21 (Civil Rights); Subchapter IV (Public Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall affect adversely the right of any person to sue for or obtain relief in any court against discrimination in public education.
