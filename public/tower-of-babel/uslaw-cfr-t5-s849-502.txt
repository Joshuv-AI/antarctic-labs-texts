5 C.F.R. § 849.502 — Liability for misused funds.
Citation: us:cfr:5:849.502
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-849.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 849 (Representative Payees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A representative payee who misuses benefits, as determined in § 849.501(a), is responsible for repayment of the misused benefits.
(b) OPM will seek restitution from the former representative payee.
