5 C.F.R. § 9800.111 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:5:9800.111
Creator: Council Of The Inspectors General On Integrity And Efficiency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9800.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9800 (Freedom Of Information Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be construed to entitle any person, as of right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under FOIA.
