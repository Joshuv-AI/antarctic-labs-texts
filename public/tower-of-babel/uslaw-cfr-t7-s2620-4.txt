7 C.F.R. § 2620.4 — Denials.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2620.4
Creator: Office Of Inspector General, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2620.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2620 (Availability Of Information To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If it is determined that a requested record is exempt from mandatory disclosure and that discretionary release would be improper, the Counsel to the Inspector General or the Counsel's designee shall give written notice of denial in accordance with subpart A of part 1 of this title.
