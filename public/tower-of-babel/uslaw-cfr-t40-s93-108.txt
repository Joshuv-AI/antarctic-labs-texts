40 C.F.R. § 93.108 — Fiscal constraints for transportation plans and TIPs.
Citation: us:cfr:40:93.108
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-93.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 93 (Determining Conformity Of Federal Actions To State Or Federal Implementation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Transportation plans and TIPs must be fiscally constrained consistent with DOT's metropolitan planning regulations at 23 CFR part 450 in order to be found in conformity.
