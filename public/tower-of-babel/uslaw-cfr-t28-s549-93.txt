28 C.F.R. § 549.93 — Definition of “child molestation.”
Citation: us:cfr:28:549.93
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-549.93
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 549 (Medical Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this subpart, “child molestation” includes any unlawful conduct of a sexual nature with, or sexual exploitation of, a person under the age of 18 years.
