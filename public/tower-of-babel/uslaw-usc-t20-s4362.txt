20 U.S.C. § 4362 — Purchases through General Services Administration
Citation: us:usc:20:4362
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section4362
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 55 (Education Of The Deaf); Subchapter II (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after September 8, 1978, Gallaudet University and the National Technical Institute for the Deaf are authorized to make purchases through the General Services Administration.
