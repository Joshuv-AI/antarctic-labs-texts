42 C.F.R. § 59a.13 — a.13 Who is eligible for a grant?
Citation: us:cfr:42:59a.13
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-59a.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 59a (PART 59a—NATIONAL LIBRARY OF MEDICINE GRANTS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise prohibited by law, any public or private nonprofit organization which is authorized and qualified to operate a medical library is eligible for a grant under this subpart.
