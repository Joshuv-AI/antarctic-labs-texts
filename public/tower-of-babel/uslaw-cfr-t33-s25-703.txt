33 C.F.R. § 25.703 — Claims payable.
Citation: us:cfr:33:25.703
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-25.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 25 (Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim for damage to or loss of real or personal property caused by a military member of the Coast Guard is payable under this subpart when the damage or loss results from:
(a) Willful or intentional acts;
(b) Wrongful taking; or
(c) Riotous, violent, and reckless conduct or acts of depredation by an individual or group that evidences willfulness.
