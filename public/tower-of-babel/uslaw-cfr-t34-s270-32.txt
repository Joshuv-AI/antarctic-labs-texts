34 C.F.R. § 270.32 — What limitation is imposed on providing Equity Assistance under this program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:270.32
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-270.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 270 (Equity Assistance Center Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient of a grant under this program may not use funds to assist in the development or implementation of activities or the development of curriculum materials for the direct instruction of students to improve their academic and vocational achievement levels.
