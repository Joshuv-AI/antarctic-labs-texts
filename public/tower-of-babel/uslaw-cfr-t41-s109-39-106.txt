41 C.F.R. § 109-39.106 — -39.106 Unlimited exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-39.106
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-39.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-39 (39—Interagency Fleet Management Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Management and Program Secretarial Officer (PSO) for their respective organizations shall make the determination that an unlimited exemption from inclusion of a motor vehicle in a fleet management system is warranted. A copy of the determination shall be forwarded to GSA and to the Office of Management.
