17 C.F.R. § 275.0-3 — -3 References to rules and regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:17:275.0-3
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-275.0-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 275 (Rules And Regulations, Investment Advisers Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term rules and regulations refers to all rules and regulations adopted by the Commission pursuant to the Act, including the forms for registration and reports and the accompanying instructions thereto.
