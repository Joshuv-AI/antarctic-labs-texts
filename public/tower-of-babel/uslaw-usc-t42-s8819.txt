42 U.S.C. § 8819 — Review; reorganization
Citation: us:usc:42:8819
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section8819
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 96 (Biomass Energy And Alcohol Fuels); Subchapter I (General Biomass Energy Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The President shall review periodically the progress of the Secretary of Agriculture and the Secretary of Energy in carrying out the purposes of this subchapter.
(b)
If the President determines it necessary in order to achieve such purposes the President may, in accordance with the provisions of chapter 9 of title 5, provide for a reorganization, including any required realignment of the respective programs of the Secretaries under this subchapter.
