34 C.F.R. § 34.30 — Application of payments and accrual of interest.
Citation: us:cfr:34:34.30
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-34.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 34 (Administrative Wage Garnishment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We apply payments received through a garnishment in the following order—
(a) To costs incurred to collect the debt;
(b) To interest accrued on the debt at the rate established by—
(1) The terms of the obligation under which it arises; or
(2) Applicable law; and
(c) To outstanding principal of the debt.
