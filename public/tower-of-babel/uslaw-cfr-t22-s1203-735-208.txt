22 C.F.R. § 1203.735-208 — -208 Misuse of information.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1203.735-208
Creator: United States International Development Cooperation Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1203.735-208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1203 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of furthering a private interest, an employee shall not, except as provided in § 1203.735-204(c) directly or indirectly use, or allow the use of, official information obtained through or in connection with Government employment which has not been made available to the general public.
