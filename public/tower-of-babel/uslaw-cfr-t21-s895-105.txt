21 C.F.R. § 895.105 — Electrical stimulation devices for self-injurious or aggressive behavior.
Citation: us:cfr:21:895.105
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-895.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 895 (Banned Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Electrical stimulation devices for self-injurious or aggressive behavior are aversive conditioning devices that apply a noxious electrical stimulus to a person's skin to reduce or cease self-injurious or aggressive behavior.
