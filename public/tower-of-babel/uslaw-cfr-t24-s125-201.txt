24 C.F.R. § 125.201 — Administrative Enforcement Initiative.
Citation: us:cfr:24:125.201
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-125.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 125 (Fair Housing Initiatives Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrative Enforcement Initiative provides funding to State and local fair housing agencies administering fair housing laws recognized by the Assistant Secretary under § 115.6 of this subchapter as providing rights and remedies which are substantially equivalent to those provided in title VIII.
