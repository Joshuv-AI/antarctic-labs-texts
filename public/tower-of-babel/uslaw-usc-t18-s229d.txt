18 U.S.C. § 229D — Injunctions
Citation: us:usc:18:229D
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section229D
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 11B (Chemical Weapons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States may obtain in a civil action an injunction against—
(1)
the conduct prohibited under section 229 or 229C of this title; or
(2)
the preparation or solicitation to engage in conduct prohibited under section 229 or 229D 1 of this title.
