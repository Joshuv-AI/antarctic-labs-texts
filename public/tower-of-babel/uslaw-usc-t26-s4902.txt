26 U.S.C. § 4902 — Liability of partners
Citation: us:usc:26:4902
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section4902
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 40 (General Provisions Relating To Occupational Taxes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any number of persons doing business in copartnership at any one place shall be required to pay but one special tax.
