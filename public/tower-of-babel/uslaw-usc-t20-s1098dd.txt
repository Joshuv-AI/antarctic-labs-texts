20 U.S.C. § 1098dd — Use of professional judgment
Citation: us:usc:20:1098dd
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1098dd
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter IV (Student Assistance); Part G–1 (Higher Education Relief Opportunities For Students). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A financial aid administrator shall be considered to be making a necessary adjustment in accordance with section 1087tt(a) of this title if the administrator makes adjustments with respect to the calculation of the expected student or parent contribution (or both) of an affected individual, and adequately documents the need for the adjustment.
