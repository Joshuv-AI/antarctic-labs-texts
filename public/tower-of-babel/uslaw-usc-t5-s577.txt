5 U.S.C. § 577 — Arbitrators
Citation: us:usc:5:577
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section577
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 5 (Administrative Procedure); Subchapter IV (Alternative Means Of Dispute Resolution In The Administrative Process). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The parties to an arbitration proceeding shall be entitled to participate in the selection of the arbitrator.
(b)
The arbitrator shall be a neutral who meets the criteria of section 573 of this title.
