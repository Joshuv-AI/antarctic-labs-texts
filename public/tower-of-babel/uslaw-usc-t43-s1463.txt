43 U.S.C. § 1463 — Disposition of receipts
Citation: us:usc:43:1463
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1463
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 31 (Department Of The Interior). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All sums received under the provisions of section 1460 of this title shall be deposited in the Treasury to the credit of miscellaneous receipts.
