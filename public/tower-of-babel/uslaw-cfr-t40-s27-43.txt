40 C.F.R. § 27.43 — Collection of civil penalties and assessments.
Citation: us:cfr:40:27.43
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-27.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 27 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 3806 and 3808(b) of title 31, United States Code, authorize actions for collection of civil penalties and assessments imposed under this part and specify the procedures for such actions.
