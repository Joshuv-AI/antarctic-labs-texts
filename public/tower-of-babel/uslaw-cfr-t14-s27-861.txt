14 C.F.R. § 27.861 — Fire protection of structure, controls, and other parts.
Citation: us:cfr:14:27.861
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-27.861
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 27 (Airworthiness Standards: Normal Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each part of the structure, controls, rotor mechanism, and other parts essential to a controlled landing that would be affected by powerplant fires must be fireproof or protected so they can perform their essential functions for at least 5 minutes under any foreseeable powerplant fire conditions.
