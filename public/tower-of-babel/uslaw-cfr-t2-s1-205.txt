2 C.F.R. § 1.205 — Applicability to Federal financial assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1.205
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1 (About Title 2 Of The Code Of Federal Regulations And Subtitle A). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The types of instruments that are subject to the guidance in this subtitle vary from one portion of the guidance to another. All portions of the guidance apply to grants and cooperative agreements, and some portions also apply to other types of Federal financial assistance.
