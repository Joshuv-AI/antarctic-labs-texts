7 U.S.C. § 3603 — Delegation of powers and duties
Citation: us:usc:7:3603
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section3603
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 67 (Implementation Of International Sugar Agreement, 1977). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may exercise any power or duty conferred on him by this chapter through such agencies or offices of the United States as he shall designate. Such agencies or offices shall issue such regulations as they determine are necessary to implement this chapter.
