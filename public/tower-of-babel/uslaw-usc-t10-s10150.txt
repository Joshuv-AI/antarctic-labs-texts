10 U.S.C. § 10150 — Ready Reserve: transfer back from Standby Reserve
Citation: us:usc:10:10150
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10150
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1005 (Elements Of Reserve Components). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations to be prescribed by the Secretary of Defense, and by the Secretary of Homeland Security with respect to the Coast Guard when it is not operating as a service in the Navy, a member of the Standby Reserve who has not completed his required period of service in the Ready Reserve may be transferred to the Ready Reserve when the reason for his transfer to the Standby Reserve no longer exists.
