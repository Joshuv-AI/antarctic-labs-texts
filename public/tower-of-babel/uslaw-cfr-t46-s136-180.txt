46 C.F.R. § 136.180 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:46:136.180
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-136.180
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 136 (Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person directly affected by a decision or action taken under this subchapter, by or on behalf of the Coast Guard, may appeal in accordance with 46 CFR 1.03.
