Abstract.

The first formal development of functions with bounded variation in normed spaces is attributed to Chistyakov
[
5
]
, and was later extended to the context of 2-normed spaces by Cure, Ferrer S., and Ferrer V.
[
6
]
. In this paper, we elaborate on this extension (Definition
4.5
), exploring its fundamental properties. The foundational characteristics of functions of bounded 2-variation within the context of generalized two-normed spaces are explored in detail (see Theorems
4.4
,
4.12
,
4.13
). Additionally, it is shown that a function of bounded variation defined on a semi-normed space can generate a function of bounded 2-variation in the context of generalized two-normed spaces (Proposition
4.10
).

Keywords.

Generalized two-normed spaces; Functions of bounded 2-variation; Bounded variation; Seminormed structures.

1.
Introduction

Chistyakov, in
[
3
,
4
,
5
]
, conducted a detailed study of the concept of variation for functions defined on a subset
E\subset\mathbb{R}
, whose values belong to a normed or metric space, using the notion of
p
-variation in the sense of Wiener, as well as the classical variation introduced by Jordan
[
8
]
, among other approaches. From the need to extend the scope of bounded variation functions, extensions towards more general structures
[
7
,
6
]
have emerged. In this paper, we propose a new perspective on these ideas that generalizes these notions within the setting of generalized two-formed spaces.

The notion of bounded variation has proven essential in various practical applications, such as image processing, image restoration, and noise removal. Examples include the high-order total variation model proposed by Banothu
[
1
]
, which enhances gamma noise removal while preserving details without staircase effects; the curvature-dependent model for image restoration by Nan, Qiao, and Zhang
[
11
]
, which maintains edge sharpness through elastic energy and total variation; and the approach by Bi, Li, Cai, and Zhang
[
2
]
, which introduces a non-convex variation for image reconstruction, optimizing both visual and quantitative results in complex images. These applications highlight the importance of studying functions of bounded variation within more general frameworks.

We guarantee that the concept of bounded variation function is well defined in the structure of generalized two-normed spaces which constitutes a significant advance in the discovery of new applications of this theory in more general contexts.

2.
Research gap

Although Chistyakov
[
5
]
and Cure, Ferrer S., and Ferrer V.
[
6
]
have made significant advancements in normed and two-normed spaces, respectively, there has been no comprehensive exploration of how these definitions can be generalized or adapted to generalized two-normed spaces, which provide a broader and more flexible framework for functional analysis. Despite their wide application in areas such as the theory of operators
[
9
]
, the study of functions of bounded variation in the context of generalized two-normed spaces remains largely unexplored, representing a significant lacune in the field.

The classical theory of functions of bounded variation has proven crucial in practical applications such as image restoration and noise removal, where variation controls the smoothness of solutions. However, understanding how these functions behave in generalized two-normed spaces could unlock new possibilities for modeling and analyzing these problems. Despite the well-established applications of functions of bounded variation, the absence of a rigorous framework for their study in these spaces limits the potential to extend classical results to more general and complex contexts.

We attempt to fill this gap by investigating two fundamental questions:

(i) How can one adequately define the notion of functions of bounded variation in generalized two-normed spaces, while respecting their fundamental characteristics?

(ii) What conditions must be fulfilled for functions of bounded variation in generalized two-formed spaces to retain the essential properties of classical functions of bounded variation?

3.
Preliminaries

Definition 3.1
.

[
9
,
10
]

Let
A,B
be vector spaces over
\mathbb{C}
.

\mathbb{W}\subset A\times B
will represent a non-empty set such that for each
a\in A
,
b\in B
the following characteristics are satisfied.

•

\mathbb{W}^{b}=\{a\in A:(a,b)\in\mathbb{W}\}
is a vector subspace of
A
.

•

\mathbb{W}_{a}=\{b\in B:(a,b)\in\mathbb{W}\}
is a vector subspace of
B
.

The mapping
\|\cdot,\cdot\|\colon\mathbb{W}\longrightarrow[0,\infty)
which satisfies:

(G1)

\|a,\alpha b\|=|\alpha|\|a,b\|=\|\alpha a,b\|
for each
\alpha\in\mathbb{C}
and each
(a,b)\in\mathbb{W}
;

(G2)

\|a,b+c\|\leq\|a,b\|+\|a,c\|
for
a\in A
,
b,c\in B
such that
(a,b),(a,c)\in\mathbb{W}
;

(G3)

\|a+b,c\|\leq\|a,c\|+\|b,c\|
for
a,b\in A
,
c\in B
such that
(a,c),(b,c)\in\mathbb{W}
.

is known as a
generalized two-norm on
\mathbb{W}
.

When
\mathbb{W}
coincides with
A\times B
, then
(A\times B,\|\cdot,\cdot\|)
is said to be a generalized two-normed space. If, in addition, the two vector spaces
A,B
are equal, we simply write
(A,\|\cdot,\cdot\|)
.

Example 3.2
.

Consider
A=\mathbb{C}^{2}
and
B=\mathbb{C}
. Let
\mathbb{W}=\mathbb{C}^{2}\times\mathbb{C}
be a set such that for every
k\in\mathbb{C}
and
w\in\mathbb{C}^{2}
, the sets
\mathbb{W}^{k}=\{w\in\mathbb{C}^{2}:(w,k)\in\mathbb{W}\}
and
\mathbb{W}_{w}=\{k\in\mathbb{C}:(w,k)\in\mathbb{W}\}
are vector subspaces of
\mathbb{C}^{2}
and
\mathbb{C}
, respectively.

The application
\|\cdot,\cdot\|\colon\mathbb{W}\longrightarrow[0,\infty)
, defined by
\|w,k\|=\|(w_{1},w_{2}),k\|=\sqrt{|w_{1}|^{2}+|w_{2}|^{2}}|k|
is a generalized two-norm on
\mathbb{W}
.

In fact,

(G1) Let
w\in\mathbb{C}^{2}
,
k\in\mathbb{C}
, and
\beta\in\mathbb{C}
such that
(w,k)\in\mathbb{W}
.

\displaystyle\|w,\beta k\|

\displaystyle=\sqrt{|w_{1}|^{2}+|w_{2}|^{2}}|\beta k|=|\beta|\sqrt{|w_{1}|^{2}+|w_{2}|^{2}}|k|=|\beta|\|w,k\|

\displaystyle=\sqrt{|\beta|^{2}(|w_{1}|^{2}+|w_{2}|^{2})}|k|=\sqrt{|\beta w_{1}|^{2}+|\beta w_{2}|^{2}}|k|=|\beta w,k\|.

(G2) Let
w\in\mathbb{C}^{2}
and
k,l\in\mathbb{C}
such that
(w,k),(w,l)\in\mathbb{W}
.

\displaystyle\|w,k+l\|

\displaystyle=\sqrt{|w_{1}|^{2}+|w_{2}|^{2}}|k+l|\leq\sqrt{|w_{1}|^{2}+|w_{2}|^{2}}|k|+\sqrt{|w_{1}|^{2}+|w_{2}|^{2}}|l|

\displaystyle=\|w,k\|+\|w,l\|.

(G3) Let
w,l\in\mathbb{C}^{2}
and
k\in\mathbb{C}
such that
(w,k),(w,k)\in\mathbb{W}
.

\displaystyle\|w+l,k\|

\displaystyle=\sqrt{|w_{1}+l_{1}|^{2}+|w_{2}+l_{2}|^{2}}|k|

\displaystyle\leq\sqrt{|w_{1}|^{2}+|w_{2}|^{2}}|k|+\sqrt{|l_{1}|^{2}+|l_{2}|^{2}}|k|=\|w,k\|+\|l,k\|.

4.
Main results

4.1.
Bounded generalized 2-variation

In the present section we construct a type of variation for definite mappings of a closed interval of real numbers in a generalized two-normed space.

Definition 4.1
.

Let
(\mathbb{W}\subset A\times B,\|\cdot,\cdot\|)
be a generalized two-normed space and
k\in B
.
Let us consider a mapping
g\colon[a,b]\subset\mathbb{R}\longrightarrow\mathbb{W}^{k}
and
P\in\mathcal{P}([a,b])
a partition.

We will call the sum
\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{n}\|g(t_{i})-g(t_{i-1}),k\|
the 2-variation of
g
associated with
k
with respect to the partition
P
in the generalized two-normed space
(\mathbb{W},\|\cdot,\cdot\|)
. We will write the above by
V_{a}^{b}[g,\mathbb{W}^{k};P]
.

The supremum of the set
\left\{V_{a}^{b}[g,\mathbb{W}^{k};P]\colon P\in\mathcal{P}([a,b])\right\}
, assuming that this exists, will be called
2-variation of
g
associated with
k
in the generalized two-normed space
(\mathbb{W},\|\cdot,\cdot\|)
,
and we will write it by
V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})
.

It is clear that the non-negativity of
V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})
is guaranteed.

Remark 4.2
.

Note that since
\mathbb{W}^{k}
is a vector subspace of
X
,
(g(s)-g(t))\in\mathbb{W}^{k}
for all
s,t\in[a,b]
.

Example 4.3
.

Consider
(\mathbb{W}\subset\mathbb{C}^{2}\times\mathbb{C},\|\cdot,\cdot\|)
with the generalized two-norm given in
3.2
. Let
\sqrt{2}\in\mathbb{C}
be fixed and
g\colon[a,b]\longrightarrow\mathbb{W}^{\sqrt{2}}
be a function defined by
g(x)=(xi,xi)
. We will now demonstrate that

g
possesses bounded generalized 2-variation.

Indeed, let
P=\left\{x_{0},\cdots,x_{n}\right\}\in\mathcal{P}([a,b])
, then

\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{n}\|g(x_{j})-g(x_{j-1}),\sqrt{2}\|

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\|(ix_{j},ix_{j})-(ix_{j-1},ix_{j-1}),\sqrt{2}\|

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\|(ix_{j}-ix_{j-1},ix_{j}-ix_{j-1}),\sqrt{2}\|

\displaystyle=\sqrt{2}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\sqrt{|ix_{j}-ix_{j-1}|^{2}+|ix_{j}-ix_{j-1}|^{2}}

\displaystyle=\sqrt{2}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\sqrt{|x_{j}-x_{j-1}|^{2}+|x_{j}-x_{j-1}|^{2}}

\displaystyle=2\sum_{j=1}^{n}\sqrt{|x_{j}-x_{j-1}|^{2}}=2\sum_{j=1}^{n}(x_{j}-x_{j-1})

\displaystyle=2(b-a).

It is concluded that the set
\{V^{a}_{b}[g,\mathbb{W}^{\sqrt{2}};P]\colon P\in\mathcal{P}([a,b])\}
is upper bounded by
2(b-a)
. Thus verifying that
g
is a bounded 2-variance function in
(\mathbb{W}\subset\mathbb{C}^{2}\times\mathbb{C},\|\cdot,\cdot\|)
.

Theorem 4.4
.

Given a two-normed space
(\mathbb{W}\subset A\times B,\|\cdot,\cdot\|)
,
k,l\in B
fixed,
\alpha\in\mathbb{C}
and two applications
g,h\colon[a,b]\to\mathbb{W}^{k}
, are verified:

(i)

V_{a}^{b}(\alpha g,\mathbb{W}^{k})=|\alpha|V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})
.

(ii)

V_{a}^{b}(g+h,\mathbb{W}^{k})\leq V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})+V_{a}^{b}(h,\mathbb{W}^{k})
.

(iii)

V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k}\cap\mathbb{W}^{l})\leq V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})+V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{l})
.

Proof.

(i) and (ii) are a consequence of
\mathbb{W}^{k}
being a vector subspace of
A
.

(iii) Let
g\colon[a,b]\longrightarrow\mathbb{W}^{k}\cap\mathbb{W}^{l}
. Thus,

\displaystyle V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k}\cap\mathbb{W}^{l})

\displaystyle=\sup\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\|g(t_{i})-g(t_{i-1}),p\|\colon p\in\mathbb{W}^{k}\cap\mathbb{W}^{l}\right\}

\displaystyle\leq\sup\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\|g(t_{i})-g(t_{i-1}),p\|\colon p\in\mathbb{W}^{k}\cap\mathbb{W}^{l}\right\}

\displaystyle\ \ \ +\sup\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\|g(t_{i})-g(t_{i-1}),p\|\colon p\in\mathbb{W}^{k}\cap\mathbb{W}^{l}\right\}

\displaystyle\leq V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})+V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{l})

∎

4.2.
Applications with bounded generalized 2-variation

From these definitions, we are now able to define what an application with bounded generalized 2-variation is.

Definition 4.5
.

Consider a generalized two-normed space
(\mathbb{W}\subset A\times B,\|\cdot,\cdot\|)
and
k\in B
fixed. We refer to an application
g\colon[a,b]\to\mathbb{W}^{k}

of bounded generalized 2-variation
if the set

\left\{V_{a}^{b}[g,\mathbb{W}^{k};P]\colon P\in\mathcal{P}([a,b])\right\}

is upper bounded.

Remark 4.6
.

The notation
BV([a,b],\mathbb{W}^{k})
will be used to represent the family of functions defined on
[a,b]
that possesing bounded generalized 2-variation, specifically,

BV([a,b],\mathbb{W}^{k})=\left\{g\colon[a,b]\to\mathbb{W}^{k}\colon V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})<\infty\right\}.

Remark 4.7
.

Let
(X,\|\cdot,\cdot\|)
be a two-normed space in the Gähler sense. If
g
is a function with bounded
(2,k)
-variation over
[a,b]
in
X
, as defined in
[
6
]
, and
\|\cdot,\cdot\|
is interpreted as a symmetric generalized two-norm with
\mathbb{W}=X\times X
, then it follows that
V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})=V_{a}^{b}(g,X,k)
. Consequently,
g
can be classified as a function of bounded generalized 2-variation in
X
.

The next proposition ensures that, in a semi-normed space, every function of bounded variation belongs to the set of functions with bounded generalized 2-variation, considering the generalized two-norm defined in Proposition
3.7
.

Proposition 4.8
.

Let
(X,\|\cdot\|)
be a semi-normed space and
k\in X
. Consider the symmetric generalized two-norm defined by
\|x,y\|=\|x\|\|y\|
. If
g:[a,b]\longrightarrow(X,\|\cdot\|)
is a function of bounded variation on
[a,b]
, then
g
is also of bounded generalized 2-variation in
(X,\|\cdot,\cdot\|)
.

Proof.

Given that
g
has bounded variation in
(X,\|\cdot\|)
, there exists a positive constant
p\in\mathbb{R}^{+}
such that

V_{a}^{b}(g,X)=\sup\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\|g(t_{i})-g(x_{i-1})\|\right\}<p.

(4.1)

Then,

\displaystyle V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})

\displaystyle=\sup\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\|g(t_{i})-g(t_{i-1}),k\|\right\}=\sup\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left\|g(t_{i})-g(t_{i-1})\right\|\left\|k\right\|\right\}

\displaystyle=\left\|k\right\|\sup\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left\|g(t_{i})-g(t_{i-1})\right\|\right\}<\left\|k\right\|p.

As a result, the boundedness of the generalized 2-variation of the function
g
in the space
(X,\|\cdot,\cdot\|)
is guaranteed.
∎

Proposition 4.9
.

Let
g\colon[a,b]\longrightarrow\mathbb{W}^{k}
be a function with bounded generalized 2-variation, where
(\mathbb{W}\subset A\times B,\|\cdot,\cdot\|)
is a generalized two-normed space. For
s,m\in[a,b]
with

a\leq s<m\leq b
, we have

\|g(s)-g(m),k\|\leq V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k}).

Proof.

Let
P_{0}=\{a,s,m,b\}
be a partition of
[a,b]
.

Then, since
V_{a}^{b}[g,\mathbb{W}^{k};P_{0}]\in\left\{V_{a}^{b}[g,\mathbb{W}^{k};P]\colon P\in\mathcal{P}([a,b])\right\}
, we have

\displaystyle V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})

\displaystyle=\sup\bigg\{V_{a}^{b}[g,\mathbb{W}^{k};P]\colon P\in\mathcal{P}([a.b])\bigg\}\geq V_{a}^{b}[g,\mathbb{W}^{k};P_{0}]

\displaystyle=\|g(s)-g(a),k\|+\left\|g(m)-g(s),k\right\|+\|g(b)-g(m),k\|

\displaystyle\geq\left\|g(m)-g(s),k\right\|.

In this way,

\|g(s)-g(m),k\|\leq V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})\text{ for each $a\leq s<m\leq b$.}

∎

The following theorem establishes that any function with bounded generalized 2-variation in a generalized two-normed space is also
(2,k)
-bounded.

Theorem 4.10
.

In a generalized two-normed space
(\mathbb{W}\subset A\times B,\|\cdot,\cdot\|)
with
k\in B
, it holds that
g\colon[a,b]\longrightarrow\mathbb{W}^{k}
is
(2,k)
-bounded whenever
g
has bounded generalized 2-variation.

Proof.

Given that
g
has bounded generalized 2-variation, there exists a constant
\sigma\in\mathbb{R}^{+}
such that

V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})\leq\sigma.

(4.2)

Let
x\in(a,b)
. Consider the partition
P_{0}=\{a,x,b\}
. Note that

\|g(x)-g(a),k\|\leq\|g(x)-g(a),k\|+\|g(b)-g(x),k\|=V_{a}^{b}[g,\mathbb{W}^{k};P_{0}].

Furthermore, using Proposition
3.8
, we have

\left\|g(x),k\right\|-\left\|g(a),k\right\|\leq\big|\left\|g(x),k\right\|-\left\|g(a),k\right\|\big|\leq\left\|g(x)-g(a),k\right\|.

(4.3)

By applying the inequalities (
4.2
) and (
4.3
), it follows that

\displaystyle\sigma

\displaystyle\geq V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})\geq V_{a}^{b}[g,\mathbb{W}^{k};P_{0}]=\|g(x)-g(a),k\|+\|g(b)-g(x),k\|

\displaystyle\geq\|g(x)-g(a),k\|\geq\left\|g(x),k\right\|-\left\|g(a),k\right\|

Consequently,

\left\|g(x),k\right\|\leq\left\|g(a),k\right\|+\sigma\leq\left\|g(a),k\right\|+\|g(b),k\|+\sigma

(4.4)

for all
x\in[a,b]
. Thus, the function
g
is
(2,k)
-bounded on the interval
[a,b]
.
∎

The converse of the aforementioned theorem does not hold in all cases, as demonstrated by the following counterexample.

Example 4.11
.

Consider the space
\left(\mathbb{W}\subset\mathbb{C}^{2}\times\mathbb{C},\|\cdot,\cdot\|\right)
, where the generalized two-norm is specified in Example
3.2
as

\|z,k\|:=\sqrt{|z_{1}|^{2}+|z_{2}|^{2}}|k|.

Let
k=-\frac{1}{2}i\in\mathbb{C}
be. Let us see that
g\colon[\sqrt{3},2]\to\mathbb{W}^{-\frac{1}{2}i}
defined by

g(x)=\left\{\begin{matrix}(2i,-i),\ \text{if}\ x\ \text{is rational},\ x\in[\sqrt{3},2],\\
\\
\ \ (0,0),\ \text{if}\ x\ \text{is irrational},\ x\in[\sqrt{3},2].\end{matrix}\right.

Let us verify that
g
is
(2,k)
-bounded, but not of bounded generalized 2-variation.

Indeed, if
x
is rational,

\|g(x),-\frac{1}{2}i\|=\|(2i,-i),-\frac{1}{2}i\|=\sqrt{|2i|^{2}+|-i|^{2}}|-\frac{1}{2}i|=\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}.

If
x
is irrational,

\|g(x),-\frac{1}{2}i\|=\|(0,0),-\frac{1}{2}i\|=\sqrt{|0|^{2}+|0|^{2}}|-\frac{1}{2}i|=0.

Hence,

\|g(x),-\frac{1}{2}i\|=\left\{\begin{matrix}\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2},\ \text{if}\ x\ \text{is rational},\ x\in[\sqrt{3},2],\\
\\
\ \ 0,\ \text{if}\ x\ \text{is irrational},\ x\in[\sqrt{3},2].\end{matrix}\right.

Therefore,
\|g(x),-\frac{1}{2}i\|\leq\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}
for all
x\in[\sqrt{3},2].
Thus,
g
is
(2,k)
-bounded in
[\sqrt{3},2]
.

Next we will show an application
g
described above does not satisfy the condition of bounded variability in a generalized two-normed space.

We will construct a partition of the interval
[\sqrt{3},2]
as follows:

We take as first element the irrational
\sqrt{3}
, second element a rational number and so on we will alternate between rationals and irrationals until we get to the rational 2.

Then,

\displaystyle V_{\sqrt{3}}^{2}\left(g,\mathbb{W}^{-\frac{1}{2}i}\right)

\displaystyle=\sup\Bigg\{\sum_{j=1}^{n}\|g(t_{j})-g(x_{j-1}),k\|:P\in\mathcal{P}[a,b]\Bigg\}

\displaystyle\geq\sum_{j=1}^{n}\left\|g(x_{j})-g(x_{j-1}),-i\right\|

\displaystyle=\|g(x_{1})-g(x_{0}),-i\|+\cdots+\|g(x_{n})-g(x_{n-1}),-i\|

\displaystyle=\|(2i,-i),-i\|+\cdots+\|(2i,-i),-i\|

\displaystyle=\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}+\cdots+\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}=n\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}.

In the partition of
[\sqrt{3},2]
constructed above it is evident that the set
\left\{V_{a}^{b}[g,\mathbb{W}^{k};P]\colon P\in\mathcal{P}([a,b])\right\}
is not upper bounded, which implies that the application
g
does not possess bounded generalized 2-variation.

In the following section, we show that the set of functions under the definition given in
4.5
turns out to be a vector space.

4.3.
New applications of bounded generalized 2-variation

Theorem 4.12
.

For any pair of applications on a generalized two-normed space and a scalar, it is satisfied that both the scalar multiple and the sum are also functions of bounded generalized 2-variation.

Proof.

If
g,h\in BV([a,b],\mathbb{W}^{k})
, then there exist
\lambda,\gamma\in\mathbb{R}^{+}
such that

V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})\leq\lambda\quad\text{ and }\quad V_{a}^{b}(h,\mathbb{W}^{k})\leq\gamma.

Then, taking
\left|\alpha\right|\lambda=M
and
\lambda+\gamma=N
, and using Theorem
4.4
, we obtain

V_{a}^{b}(\alpha g,\mathbb{W}^{k})\leq M\quad\text{ and }\quad V_{a}^{b}(g+h,\mathbb{W}^{k})\leq N.

Thus it is evident that both scalar multiples and sums of applications of bounded generalized 2-variation preserve the property of bounded generalized 2-variation.
∎

Theorem 4.13
.

If
g\in BV([\alpha,\beta],\mathbb{W}^{k})
and
\lambda\in[\alpha,\beta]
, then it is satisfied that

V_{\alpha}^{\lambda}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})+V_{\lambda}^{\beta}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})\leq V_{\alpha}^{\beta}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k}).

Proof.

It is straightforward to verify that
V_{\alpha}^{\lambda}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})
and
V_{\lambda}^{\beta}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})
are both finite.

Let
\epsilon>0
be given.

We take partitions
P_{[\alpha,\lambda]}=\{t_{0},t_{1},\cdots,t_{j}\}\in\mathcal{P}([\alpha,\lambda])
and
P_{[\lambda,\beta]}=\{t_{j},t_{j+1},\cdots,t_{n}\}\in\mathcal{P}([\lambda,\beta])
such that
t_{n}=\lambda
,

V_{\alpha}^{\lambda}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})-\frac{1}{2}\epsilon\leq\sum_{i=1}^{j}\|g(t_{i})-g(t_{i-1}),k\|

and

V_{\lambda}^{\beta}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})-\frac{1}{2}\epsilon\leq\sum_{p=j+1}^{n}\|g(t_{p})-g(t_{p-1}),k\|.

Then,

V_{\alpha}^{\lambda}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})+V_{\lambda}^{\beta}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})-\epsilon\leq\sum_{i=1}^{n}\|g(t_{p})-g(t_{p-1}),k\|\leq V_{\alpha}^{\beta}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k}).

Thus, it is proven.
∎

Now, we guarantee the existence of a generalized symmetric two-norm for any function space with bounded generalized 2-variation.

Theorem 4.14
.

Given a generalized two-normed space
(\mathbb{W}\subset A\times B,\|\cdot,\cdot\|)
. The function

\|\cdot,\cdot\|_{2G}\colon BV([a,b],\mathbb{W}^{k})\times BV([a,b],\mathbb{W}^{k})\longrightarrow[0,\infty)

defined by

\|f,h\|_{2G}:=V_{a}^{b}(f,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left\|h(a),k\right\|+V_{a}^{b}(h,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left\|f(a),k\right\|

is a generalized symmetric two-norm on
BV([a,b],\mathbb{W}^{k})
.

Proof.

(S1)

\displaystyle\|f,h\|_{2G}

\displaystyle=V_{a}^{b}(f,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left\|h(a),k\right\|+V_{a}^{b}(h,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left\|f(a),k\right\|

\displaystyle=V_{a}^{b}(h,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left\|f(a),k\right\|+V_{a}^{b}(f,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left\|h(a),k\right\|=\|h,f\|_{2G}

(S2)

\displaystyle\|\alpha f,h\|_{2G}

\displaystyle=V_{a}^{b}(\alpha f,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left\|h(a),k\right\|+V_{a}^{b}(h,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left\|\alpha f(a),k\right\|

\displaystyle=\left|\alpha\right|V_{a}^{b}(f,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left\|h(a),k\right\|+\left|\alpha\right|V_{a}^{b}(h,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left\|f(a),k\right\|=\left|\alpha\right|\|f,h\|_{2G}

(S3)

\displaystyle\|f,h\|_{2G}+\|g,h\|_{2G}

\displaystyle=V_{a}^{b}(f,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left\|h(a),k\right\|+V_{a}^{b}(h,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left\|f(a),k\right\|

\displaystyle\ \ \ +V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left\|h(a),k\right\|+V_{a}^{b}(h,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left\|g(a),k\right\|

\displaystyle=\left(V_{a}^{b}(f,\mathbb{W}^{k})+V_{a}^{b}(g,\mathbb{W}^{k})\right)\left\|h(a),k\right\|

\displaystyle\ \ \ +V_{a}^{b}(h,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left(\left\|f(a),k\right\|+\left\|g(a),k\right\|\right)

\displaystyle\geq V_{a}^{b}(f+g,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left\|h(a),k\right\|+V_{a}^{b}(h,\mathbb{W}^{k})\left\|f(a)+g(a),k\right\|

\displaystyle=\|f+g,h\|_{2G}.

The above allows us to state that every space of functions of bounded variation is in fact a generalized symmetric two-normed space.
∎

Note that in Example
4.3
,

\displaystyle\|f,f\|_{2G}

\displaystyle=2V_{a}^{b}(f,\mathbb{W}^{\sqrt{2}})\|f(a),\sqrt{2}\|=2(2(b-a))\|f(a),\sqrt{2}\|

\displaystyle=4(b-a)\sqrt{|ai|^{2}+|ai|^{2}}|\sqrt{2}|=8|a|(b-a).

5.
Conclusions

In semi-normed spaces, the concept of a function with bounded variation naturally extends to a function of bounded generalized variation (Proposition
4.8
). Furthermore, the notion of bounded variation is well-defined in generalized two-normed spaces (Definition
4.5
).
Thus, the notion presented in this research generalizes the one introduced by Chistyakov
[
5
]
. Moreover, Remark
4.7
shows that the results given by Cure, Ferrer S., and Ferrer V.
[
6
]
are a consequence of the findings of this work. In generalized two-normed spaces, the variation of a function determines an upper bound on the distance between the images of functions with bounded variation under the generalized two-norm (Proposition
4.9
). Additionally, the space of functions exhibiting generalized bounded variation in such spaces can be regarded as a generalized symmetric two-normed space (Theorem
4.14
).
