45 C.F.R. § 286.280 — When are annual reports due?
Citation: us:cfr:45:286.280
Creator: Office Of Family Assistance (Assistance Programs), Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-286.280
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 286 (Tribal Tanf Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The annual report required by § 286.275 is due 90 days after the end of the Fiscal Year which it covers.
(b) The first annual report for a Tribe must include all months of operation since the plan was approved.
