12 U.S.C. § 548 — State taxation
Citation: us:usc:12:548
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section548
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 4 (Taxation); Subchapter III (National Bank Shares). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of any tax law enacted under authority of the United States or any State, a national bank shall be treated as a bank organized and existing under the laws of the State or other jurisdiction within which its principal office is located.
