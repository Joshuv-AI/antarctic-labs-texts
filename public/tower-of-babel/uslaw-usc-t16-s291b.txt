16 U.S.C. § 291b — Administration, protection, development, and maintenance
Citation: us:usc:16:291b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section291b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXXVI (George Rogers Clark National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior shall administer, protect, develop, and maintain the George Rogers Clark National Historical Park in accordance with the provisions of the Act of August 25, 1916 (39 Stat 535; 16 U.S.C. 1 et seq.),1 as amended and supplemented.
