10 U.S.C. § 3681 — Leasing of commercial vehicles and equipment
Citation: us:usc:10:3681
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3681
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart C (Contracting Methods And Contract Types); Chapter 258 (Other Types Of Contracts Used For Procurements For Particular Purposes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Defense may use leasing in the acquisition of commercial vehicles and equipment whenever the Secretary determines that such leasing is practicable and efficient.
