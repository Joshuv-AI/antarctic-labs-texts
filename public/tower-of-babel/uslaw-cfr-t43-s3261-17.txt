43 C.F.R. § 3261.17 — How do I amend my operations plan or drilling permit?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3261.17
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3261.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If BLM has not yet approved your operations plan or drilling permit, send us your amended plan and completed and signed permit application.
