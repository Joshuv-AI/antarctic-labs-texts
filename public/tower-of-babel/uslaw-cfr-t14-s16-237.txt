14 C.F.R. § 16.237 — Waiver of procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:14:16.237
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-16.237
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 16 (Rules Of Practice For Federally-Assisted Airport Enforcement Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The hearing officer shall waive such procedural steps as all parties to the hearing agree to waive before issuance of an initial decision.
(b) Consent to a waiver of any procedural step bars the raising of this issue on appeal.
(c) The parties may not by consent waive the obligation of the hearing officer to enter an initial decision on the record.
