12 U.S.C. § 214d — Prohibition on conversion
Citation: us:usc:12:214d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section214d
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 2 (National Banks); Subchapter XV (Conversion Of National Banks Into State Banks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A national banking association may not convert to a State bank or State savings association during any period in which the national banking association is subject to a cease and desist order (or other formal enforcement order) issued by, or a memorandum of understanding entered into with, the Comptroller of the Currency with respect to a significant supervisory matter.
