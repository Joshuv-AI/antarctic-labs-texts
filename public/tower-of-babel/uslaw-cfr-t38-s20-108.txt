38 C.F.R. § 20.108 — Rule 108. Delegation of authority to Chairman and Vice Chairman, Board of Veterans' Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:38:20.108
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-20.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 20 (Board Of Veterans' Appeals: Rules Of Practice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chairman and/or Vice Chairman have authority delegated by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to:
(a) Approve the assumption of appellate jurisdiction of an adjudicative determination which has not become final in order to grant a benefit, and
(b) Order VA Central Office investigations of matters before the Board.
