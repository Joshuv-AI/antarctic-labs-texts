46 C.F.R. § 160.176-6 — -6 Procedure for approval of design or material revision.
Citation: us:cfr:46:160.176-6
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-160.176-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 160 (Lifesaving Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each change in design, material, or construction must be approved by the Commandant before being used in lifejacket production.
(b) Determinations of equivalence of design, construction, and materials may only be made by the Commandant.
