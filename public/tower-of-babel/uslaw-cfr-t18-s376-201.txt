18 C.F.R. § 376.201 — Emergency condition defined.
Citation: us:cfr:18:376.201
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-376.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 376 (Organization, Mission, And Functions; Operations During Emergency Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this subpart, emergency conditions:
(a) Shall commence:
(1) At the time of an armed attack upon the United States, or its territories or possessions;
(2) At the time the Commission is officially notified of the likelihood or imminence of such an attack; or
(3) At a time specified by the authority of the President; or
(4) At such time that the Commission's Continuity of Operations Plan is activated; and
(b) Shall continue until the Commission is officially notified of the end of such conditions.
