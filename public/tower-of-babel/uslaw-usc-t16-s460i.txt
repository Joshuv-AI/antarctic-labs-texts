16 U.S.C. § 460i — Delegation of powers; regulations
Citation: us:usc:16:460i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460i
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXVII (Cottage Site Developments And Uses In Reservoir Areas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Army may delegate any authority conferred upon him by this subchapter to any officer or employee of the Department of the Army. Any such officer or employee shall exercise the authority so delegated under rules and regulations approved by the Secretary.
