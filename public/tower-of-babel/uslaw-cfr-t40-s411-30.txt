40 C.F.R. § 411.30 — Applicability; description of the materials storage piles runoff subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:411.30
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-411.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 411 (Cement Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the runoff of rainfall which derives from the storage of materials including raw materials, intermediate products, finished products and waste materials which are used in or derived from the manufacture of cement under either Subcategory—A or B.
