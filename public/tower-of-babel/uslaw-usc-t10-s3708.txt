10 U.S.C. § 3708 — Right to examine contractor records
Citation: us:usc:10:3708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3708
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart D (General Contracting Provisions); Chapter 271 (Truthful Cost Or Pricing Data (Truth In Negotiations)). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of evaluating the accuracy, completeness, and currency of cost or pricing data required to be submitted by this chapter, the head of an agency shall have the authority provided by section 3841(b)(2) of this title.
