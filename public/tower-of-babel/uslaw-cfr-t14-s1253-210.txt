14 C.F.R. § 1253.210 — Military and merchant marine educational institutions.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1253.210
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1253.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1253 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Sex In Education Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These Title IX regulations do not apply to an educational institution whose primary purpose is the training of individuals for a military service of the United States or for the merchant marine.
