. We compute k ​ o ∗ ​ ( K ⁡ ( ℤ / 2 , 2 ) ) ko_{*}(K({\mathbb{Z}}/2,2)) and k ​ o ∗ ​ ( K ⁡ ( ℤ / 2 , 2 ) ) ko^{*}(K({\mathbb{Z}}/2,2)) , the connective K ​ O KO -homology and -cohomology groups of the mod 2 Eilenberg MacLane space K ⁡ ( ℤ / 2 , 2 ) K({\mathbb{Z}}/2,2) , using the Adams spectral sequence.
