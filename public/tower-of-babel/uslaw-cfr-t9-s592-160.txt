9 C.F.R. § 592.160 — When an application may be withdrawn.
Citation: us:cfr:9:592.160
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-592.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 592 (Voluntary Inspection Of Egg Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application for service may be withdrawn by the applicant at any time before the service is performed upon payment, by the applicant, of all expenses incurred by the Agency in connection with such application.
