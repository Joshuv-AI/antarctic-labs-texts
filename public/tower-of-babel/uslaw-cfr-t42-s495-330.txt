42 C.F.R. § 495.330 — Termination of FFP for failure to provide access to information.
Citation: us:cfr:42:495.330
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-495.330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 495 (Standards For The Electronic Health Record Technology Incentive Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) HHS terminates FFP at any time if the Medicaid agency fails to provide State and Federal representatives with full access to records relating to HIT planning and implementation efforts, and the systems used to interoperate with electronic HIT, including on-site inspection.
(b) The Department may request such access at any time to determine whether the conditions in this subpart are being met.
