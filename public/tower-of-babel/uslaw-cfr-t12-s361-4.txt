12 C.F.R. § 361.4 — What contracts are eligible for this outreach program?
Citation: us:cfr:12:361.4
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-361.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 361 (Minority And Women Outreach Program Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FDIC outreach program applies to all contracts entered into by the FDIC. The outreach program is incorporated into FDIC policies and guidelines governing contracting and the retention of legal services.
