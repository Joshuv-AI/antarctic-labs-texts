47 C.F.R. § 32.2410 — Cable and wire facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:47:32.2410
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-32.2410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 32 (Uniform System Of Accounts For Telecommunications Companies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This account shall be used by companies to record the original cost of cable and wire facilities of the type and character detailed in Accounts 2411 through 2441.
