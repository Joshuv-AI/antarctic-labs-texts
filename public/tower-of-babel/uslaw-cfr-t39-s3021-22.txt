39 C.F.R. § 3021.22 — Making documents available for inspection by the public.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3021.22
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3021.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3021 (Rules For Appeals Of Postal Service Determinations To Close Or Consolidate Post Offices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Copies of all filings (including the administrative record) related to an appeal shall be available for public inspection at the post offices where the final determination was posted. The Secretary will notify participants that they may view copies of filings at post offices where the final determination was posted.
