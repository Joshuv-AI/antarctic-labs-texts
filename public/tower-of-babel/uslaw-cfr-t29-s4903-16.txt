29 C.F.R. § 4903.16 — How will PBGC refer debts owed to PBGC to private collection agencies?
Citation: us:cfr:29:4903.16
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4903.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4903 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

PBGC will transfer delinquent debts owed to PBGC to the Financial Management Service to obtain debt collection services provided by private collection agencies. See § 4903.10 of this part.
