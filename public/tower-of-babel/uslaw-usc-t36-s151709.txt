36 U.S.C. § 151709 — Civil action by Attorney General for equitable relief
Citation: us:usc:36:151709
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section151709
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1517 (National Film Preservation Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General may bring a civil action in the United States District Court for the District of Columbia for appropriate equitable relief if the corporation—
(1)
engages or threatens to engage in any act, practice, or policy that is inconsistent with the purposes in section 151702 of this title; or
(2)
refuses, fails, or neglects to carry out its obligations under this chapter or threatens to do so.
