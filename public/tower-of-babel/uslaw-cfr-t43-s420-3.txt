43 C.F.R. § 420.3 — Adjacent lands.
Citation: us:cfr:43:420.3
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-420.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 420 (Off-Road Vehicle Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When administratively feasible, the regulation of off-road vehicle use on Reclamation lands will be compatible with such use as permitted by recreation-managing agencies on adjacent lands (both public and private).
