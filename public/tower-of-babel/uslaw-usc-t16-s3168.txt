16 U.S.C. § 3168 — Injunctive relief
Citation: us:usc:16:3168
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3168
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 51 (Alaska National Interest Lands Conservation); Subchapter IV (Transportation And Utility Systems In And Across, And Access Into, Conservation System Units). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No court shall have jurisdiction to grant any injunctive relief lasting longer than ninety days against any action pursuant to this subchapter except in conjunction with a final judgment entered in a case involving an action pursuant to this subchapter.
