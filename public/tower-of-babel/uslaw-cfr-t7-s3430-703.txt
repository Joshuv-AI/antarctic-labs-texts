7 C.F.R. § 3430.703 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3430.703
Creator: National Institute Of Food And Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3430.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3430 (Competitive And Noncompetitive Non-Formula Federal Assistance Programs—General Award Administrative Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be eligible to receive an award under this subpart, the recipient shall be—
(a) An institution of higher education (as defined in § 3430.702);
(b) A National Laboratory;
(c) A Federal research agency;
(d) A State research agency;
(e) A private sector entity (as defined in § 3430.702 of this part);
(f) A nonprofit organization; or
(g) A consortium of two or more entities listed in paragraphs (a) through (f) of this section.
