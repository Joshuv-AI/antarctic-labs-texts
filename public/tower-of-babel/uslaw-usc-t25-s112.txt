25 U.S.C. § 112 — Persons present at delivery of goods and money
Citation: us:usc:25:112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section112
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 4 (Performance By United States Of Obligations To Indians); Subchapter II (Disbursement Of Moneys And Supplies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The superintendent, agent, or subagent, together with such military officer as the President may direct, shall be present, and certify to the delivery of all goods and money required to be paid or delivered to the Indians.
