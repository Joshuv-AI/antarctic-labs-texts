28 U.S.C. § 2718 — Interest on balances due department
Citation: us:usc:28:2718
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2718
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 173 (Attachment In Postal Suits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In suits for balances due the Post Office Department may recover interest at the rate of 6 per centum per year from the time of default.
