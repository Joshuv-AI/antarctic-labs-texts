8 C.F.R. § 236.25 — No private rights.
Citation: us:cfr:8:236.25
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-236.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 236 (Apprehension And Detention Of Inadmissible And Deportable Aliens; Removal Of Aliens Ordered Removed). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart is an exercise of the Secretary's enforcement discretion. This subpart—
(a) Is not intended to and does not supplant or limit otherwise lawful activities of the Department or the Secretary; and
(b) Is not intended to and does not create any rights, substantive or procedural, enforceable at law by any party in any matter, civil or criminal.
