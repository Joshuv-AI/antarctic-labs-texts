42 C.F.R. § 426.516 — Role of Medicare Managed Care Organizations (MCOs) and State agencies in the NCD review process.
Citation: us:cfr:42:426.516
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-426.516
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 426 (Review Of National Coverage Determinations And Local Coverage Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Medicare MCOs and Medicaid State agencies may participate in the NCD review process only if they meet the amicus participant criteria listed in §§ 426.510(f)(3) and 426.513.
