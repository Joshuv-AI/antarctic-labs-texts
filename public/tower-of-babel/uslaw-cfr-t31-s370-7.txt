31 C.F.R. § 370.7 — How can my financial institution change my designated deposit account?
Citation: us:cfr:31:370.7
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-370.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 370 (Electronic Transactions And Funds Transfers Relating To United States Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If your financial institution requests us to make a change in your deposit account number or type of your account, we will change the information without requiring any confirmation from you. The request from the financial institution must be made following the Operating Rules of the National Automated Clearing House Association. The financial institution's request will be deemed an agreement by the institution to indemnify us and you for any loss resulting from the requested change.
