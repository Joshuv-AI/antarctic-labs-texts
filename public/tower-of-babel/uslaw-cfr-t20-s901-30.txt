20 C.F.R. § 901.30 — Authority to suspend or terminate enrollment.
Citation: us:cfr:20:901.30
Creator: Joint Board For The Enrollment Of Actuaries
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-901.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 901 (Regulations Governing The Performance Of Actuarial Services Under The Employee Retirement Income Security Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under section 3042(b) of ERISA the Joint Board may, after notice and opportunity for a hearing, suspend or terminate the enrollment of an enrolled actuary if the Joint Board finds that such enrolled actuary
(a) Has failed to discharge his/her duties under ERISA, or
(b) Does not satisfy the requirements for enrollment in effect at the time of his/her enrollment.
