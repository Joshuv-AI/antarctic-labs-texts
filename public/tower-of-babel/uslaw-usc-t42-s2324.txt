42 U.S.C. § 2324 — Posting of lists showing appraised value
Citation: us:usc:42:2324
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2324
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 24 (Disposal Of Atomic Energy Communities); Subchapter II (Lots, Appraisals, And Prices). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Lists showing the appraised value of each parcel of property to be offered for sale to priority purchasers shall, prior to the offering of such property for sale, be made available for public inspection, at reasonable times, at the offices of the Commission at the community.
