2 U.S.C. § 5349 — Limitation on printed copies of U.S. Code to House
Citation: us:usc:2:5349
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5349
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 53 (House Of Representatives Members); Subchapter III (Expenses And Allowances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
None of the funds made available in any fiscal year may be to provide an aggregate number of more than 50 printed copies of any edition of the United States Code to all offices of the House of Representatives.
(b)
This section shall apply with respect to fiscal year 2019 and each succeeding fiscal year.
