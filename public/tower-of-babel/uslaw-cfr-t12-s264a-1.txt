12 C.F.R. § 264a.1 — a.1 What is the purpose and scope of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:12:264a.1
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-264a.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 264a (PART 264a—POST-EMPLOYMENT RESTRICTIONS FOR SENIOR EXAMINERS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part identifies those officers and employees of the Federal Reserve that are subject to the special post-employment restrictions set forth in section 10(k) of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act (FDI Act) and implements those restrictions as they apply to officers and employees of the Federal Reserve.
