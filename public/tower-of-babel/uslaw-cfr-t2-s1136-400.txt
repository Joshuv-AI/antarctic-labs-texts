2 C.F.R. § 1136.400 — Purpose of OAR Article IV.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1136.400
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1136.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1136 (Other Administrative Requirements: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OAR Article IV addresses claims, disputes, and appeals under awards. It thereby provides the award terms and conditions required by the DoDGARs at 32 CFR 22.815 and also implements OMB guidance in 2 CFR 200.341.
