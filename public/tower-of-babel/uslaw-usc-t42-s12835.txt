42 U.S.C. § 12835 — Citizen participation

Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 130 (National Affordable Housing); Subchapter II (Investment In Affordable Housing); Part F (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall ensure that each participating jurisdiction, and each jurisdiction seeking to become a participating jurisdiction, complies with the requirements of section 12707 of this title.
