48 C.F.R. § 26.402 — 26.402 Policy.
Citation: us:cfr:48:26.402
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-26.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 26 (Other Socioeconomic Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Government encourages executive agencies and their contractors, to the maximum extent practicable and safe, to donate excess apparently wholesome food to nonprofit organizations that provide assistance to food-insecure people in the United States.
