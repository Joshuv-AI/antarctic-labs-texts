5 U.S.C. § 10201 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:5:10201
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section10201
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart I (Miscellaneous); Chapter 102 (United States Secret Service Uniformed Division Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter—
(1)
the term “member” means an employee of the United States Secret Service Uniformed Division having the authorities described under section 3056A(b) of title 18;
(2)
the term “Secretary” means the Secretary of the Department of Homeland Security; and
(3)
the term “United States Secret Service Uniformed Division” has the meaning given that term under section 3056A of title 18.
