14 C.F.R. § 111.320 — Reporting errors and requesting corrections.
Citation: us:cfr:14:111.320
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-111.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 111 (Pilot Records Database). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A pilot who identifies an error or inaccuracy in that pilot's PRD records must report the error or inaccuracy to the PRD in a form and manner acceptable to the Administrator.
