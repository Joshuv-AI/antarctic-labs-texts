5 C.F.R. § 591.229 — How does OPM inform agencies and employees of COLA rate changes?
Citation: us:cfr:5:591.229
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-591.229
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 591 (Allowances And Differentials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OPM publishes COLA area survey summary reports, MEG and PEG indexes, and COLA rates in the Federal Register. OPM makes survey data and other information available to the public to the extent authorized by the Freedom of Information Act and the Privacy Act.
