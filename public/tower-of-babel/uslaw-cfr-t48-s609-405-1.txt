48 C.F.R. § 609.405-1 — 609.405-1 Continuation of current contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:48:609.405-1
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-609.405-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 609 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Procurement Executive is the agency head's designee for the purposes of FAR 9.405-1. The decision whether to terminate a current contract shall be made in consideration of the circumstances listed in 649.101-70.
