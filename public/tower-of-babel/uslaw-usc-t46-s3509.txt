46 U.S.C. § 3509 — Medical standards
Citation: us:usc:46:3509
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section3509
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Inspection And Regulation Of Vessels); Chapter 35 (Carriage Of Passengers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The owner of a vessel to which section 3507 applies shall ensure that—
(1)
a physician is always present and available to treat any passengers who may be on board the vessel in the event of an emergency situation;
(2)
the vessel is in compliance with the Health Care Guidelines for Cruise Ship Medical Facilities established by the American College of Emergency Physicians; and
(3)
the initial safety briefing given to the passengers on board the vessel includes—
(A)
the location of the vessel’s medical facilities; and
(B)
the appropriate steps passengers should follow during a medical emergency.
