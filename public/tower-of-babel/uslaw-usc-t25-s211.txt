25 U.S.C. § 211 — Creation of Indian reservations
Citation: us:usc:25:211
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section211
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 6 (Government Of Indian Country And Reservations); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No Indian reservation shall be created, nor shall any additions be made to one heretofore created, within the limits of the States of New Mexico and Arizona, except by Act of Congress.
