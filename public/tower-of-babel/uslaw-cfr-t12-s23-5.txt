12 C.F.R. § 23.5 — Requirement for separate records.
Citation: us:cfr:12:23.5
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-23.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 23 (Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a national bank enters into both CEBA Leases and Section 24(Seventh) Leases, the bank's records must distinguish the CEBA Leases from the Section 24(Seventh) Leases.
