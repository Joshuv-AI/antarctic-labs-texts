49 C.F.R. § 230.52 — Water glass valves.
Citation: us:cfr:49:230.52
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-230.52
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 230 (Steam Locomotive Inspection And Maintenance Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All water glasses shall be equipped with no more than two valves capable of isolating the water glass from the boiler. They shall also be equipped with a drain valve capable of evacuating the glass when it is so isolated.
