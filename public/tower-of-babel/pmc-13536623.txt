Correction: A dual-targeting bio-liposomes nanodrug repair endothelial cell dysfunction and restore macrophage cholesterol flow homeostasis to treat early atherosclerosis
Authors: Qi Zhang, Shengchao Ma, Xue Kang, Yi Liu, Fei Ma, Feifei Yu, Xiaolan Luo, Guizhong Li, Yinju Hao, Huiping Zhang, Bin Liu, Yideng Jiang
Published: 2026

Correction: Journal of Nanobiotechnology (2025) 23:365

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12951-025-03436-5

In this article Fig. 1 appeared incorrectly: While preparing Fig. 1F, the authors inadvertently used an image from the Evol (1.25 nM) treatment group as a representative image for the control group. Authors have now corrected the figure and are providing here a revised version of Fig. 1, which displays the correct image. This error does not affect the Dil-oxLDL fluorescence analysis in Fig. 1F nor the conclusions of this study. Additionally, while preparing Fig. 2B, the scale bar for the TEM image of L@ES NPs and HA-ML@ES NPs was incorrectly labeled as “200 µm”; it has now been corrected to “200 nm”. This revision does not affect any of the reported results, interpretations, or conclusions of the study.
