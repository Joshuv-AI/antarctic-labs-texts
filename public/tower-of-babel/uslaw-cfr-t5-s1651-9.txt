5 C.F.R. § 1651.9 — Participant's next of kin.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1651.9
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1651.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1651 (Death Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the account is to be paid to the participant's next of kin under § 1651.2(a)(6), the next of kin of the participant will be determined in accordance with the state law of the participant's domicile at the time of death.
