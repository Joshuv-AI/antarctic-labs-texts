46 C.F.R. § 12.701 — Credentials required for entry-level and miscellaneous ratings.
Citation: us:cfr:46:12.701
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-12.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 12 (Requirements For Rating Endorsements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every person employed or engaged on a vessel subject to 46 U.S.C. 8701 must produce an MMC or MMD with the appropriate endorsement to the Master or Person in Charge (PIC), if appropriate, before signing shipping articles.
