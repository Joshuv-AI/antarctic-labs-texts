17 C.F.R. § 180.2 — Prohibition on price manipulation.
Citation: us:cfr:17:180.2
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-180.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 180 (Prohibition Against Manipulation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It shall be unlawful for any person, directly or indirectly, to manipulate or attempt to manipulate the price of any swap, or of any commodity in interstate commerce, or for future delivery on or subject to the rules of any registered entity.
