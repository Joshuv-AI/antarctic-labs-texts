15 U.S.C. § 6761 — Coordination with Financial Industry Regulatory Authority
Citation: us:usc:15:6761
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6761
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 93 (Insurance); Subchapter III (National Association Of Registered Agents And Brokers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Association shall coordinate with the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority in order to ease any administrative burdens that fall on members of the Association that are subject to regulation by the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority, consistent with the requirements of this subchapter and the Federal securities laws.
