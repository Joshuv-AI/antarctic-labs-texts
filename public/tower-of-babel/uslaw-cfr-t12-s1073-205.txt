12 C.F.R. § 1073.205 — No requirement for duplicate notice.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1073.205
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1073.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1073 (Procedures For Bureau Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where the Director previously has given a debtor any of the required notice and review opportunities with respect to a particular debt, the Director is not required to duplicate such notice and review opportunities prior to initiating administrative offset.
