ABSTRACT NUMBER: ESOC2026A1060 MOTHERSHIP VERSUS DRIP AND SHIP STRATEGIES FOR MECHANICAL THROMBECTOMY IN ACUTE ISCHEMIC STROKE: A SYSTEMATIC REVIEW AND META-ANALYSIS
Authors: Lei Guo, Yang Xiang, Shu Yang, Guo Tang, Quan Li, Chunling Li, Nengwei Yu, Fu-Qiang Guo, Jun Zhang
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackground and aimsThe optimal prehospital referral strategy for patients with acute ischemic stroke due to large vessel occlusion (AIS-LVO) remains controversial. This systematic review and meta-analysis compared the clinical outcomes of mothership versus drip-and-ship strategies (Fig. 1).MethodsA systematic review of PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, and the Cochrane Library from inception to November 2025 identified studies comparing mothership and drip-and-ship strategies in AIS-LVO patients undergoing mechanical thrombectomy (Fig. 2). Random-effects meta-analyses were performed to estimate pooled odds ratios (ORs) for primary efficacy outcomes, including 90-day mRS 0–2, 90-day mRS 0–3, and successful recanalization. Safety outcomes included symptomatic intracranial hemorrhage (sICH) and 90-day all-cause mortality.ResultsA total of 42 cohort studies comprising 25,005 patients were included. The mothership strategy was associated with a 15% increased likelihood of achieving 90-day mRS 0–2 (OR 1.15, 95%CI 1.03–1.29, I2=53%) (Fig. 3). However, no statistically significant between-group differences were observed for 90-day mRS 0–3 (OR: 1.12, 95% CI: 0.97–1.29; I2 = 52%), successful recanalization (OR: 1.03, 95% CI: 0.90–1.18; I2 = 60%), sICH (OR: 0.84, 95% CI: 0.69–1.02; I2 = 22%), and 90-day mortality (OR: 0.99, 95% CI: 0.85–1.15; I2 = 60%).ConclusionsThe mothership strategy was associated with better 90-day functional independence (mRS 0–2) compared with drip-and-ship, while no significant differences were found for 90-day mRS 0–3, successful recanalization, sICH, or 90-day mortality (PROSPERO ID: CRD420251000352).Conflict of interestThis study was supported by the Chengdu Medical Research Project (Grant No. 2025435).
