31 U.S.C. § 1519 — Criminal penalty
Citation: us:usc:31:1519
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section1519
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 15 (Appropriation Accounting); Subchapter II (Apportionment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An officer or employee of the United States Government or of the District of Columbia government knowingly and willfully violating section 1517(a) of this title shall be fined not more than $5,000, imprisoned for not more than 2 years, or both.
