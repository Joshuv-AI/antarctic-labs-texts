5 C.F.R. § 2638.401 — In general.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2638.401
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2638.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2638 (Executive Branch Ethics Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Government Ethics has authority, pursuant to sections 402(b)(9) and 402(f)(1) of the Act, to take the action described in this subpart with respect to deficiencies in agency ethics programs. Agency ethics programs comprise the matters described in this subchapter for which agencies are responsible.
