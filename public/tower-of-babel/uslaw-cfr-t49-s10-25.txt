49 C.F.R. § 10.25 — Mailing lists.
Citation: us:cfr:49:10.25
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-10.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 10 (Maintenance Of And Access To Records Pertaining To Individuals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual's name and address is not sold or rented unless such action is specifically authorized by law. This provision shall not be construed to require the withholding of names and addresses otherwise permitted to be made public.
