10 U.S.C. § 8295 — Distinguished flying cross
Citation: us:usc:10:8295
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8295
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 837 (Decorations And Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may award a distinguished flying cross of appropriate design with accompanying ribbon to any person who, while serving in any capacity with the Navy or the Marine Corps, distinguishes himself by heroism or extraordinary achievement while participating in an aerial flight.
