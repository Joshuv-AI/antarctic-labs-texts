40 C.F.R. § 179.60 — Designation and qualifications of presiding officer.
Citation: us:cfr:40:179.60
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-179.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 179 (Formal Evidentiary Public Hearing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The presiding officer in a hearing will be an administrative law judge qualified under 5 U.S.C. 3l05 and designated by the Agency's chief administrative law judge.
