27 C.F.R. § 20.114 — Tobacco flavor general-use formula.
Citation: us:cfr:27:20.114
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-20.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 20 (Distribution And Use Of Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This tobacco flavor general-use formula authorizes the production of any finished article made with alcohol denatured in accordance with S.D.A. Formula No. 4 or S.D.R. Formula No. 4 which—
(a) Contains flavors sufficient to ensure that the article is unfit for beverage or internal human use,
(b) May contain other ingredients, and
(c) Is packaged, labeled, and sold or used as a tobacco flavor only.
