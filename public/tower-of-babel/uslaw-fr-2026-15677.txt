Petition for Reconsideration of Action in Rulemaking Proceeding

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Petition for reconsideration.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Petitions for Reconsideration (Petitions) have been filed in 
the Commission's proceeding by Chris Webber on behalf of CRW Consulting 
LLC and Kristen Corra on behalf of Schools, Health & Libraries 
Broadband Coalition.

DATES: Oppositions to the Petition must be filed on or before August 
18, 2026. Replies to oppositions to the Petition must be filed on or 
before August 28, 2026.

ADDRESSES: Federal Communications Commission, 45 L Street NE, 
Washington, DC 20554.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Jennifer Mensah, Attorney Advisor, 
202-418-1387, or [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: This is a summary of the Commission's 
document, Report No. 3235, released on July 28, 2026. The full text of 
the Petition can be accessed online via the Commission's Electronic 
Comment Filing System at http://apps.fcc.gov/ecfs/ and downloaded at 
https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-423672A1.pdf. The 
Commission will not send a Congressional Review Act (CRA) submission to 
Congress or the Government Accountability Office pursuant to the CRA, 5 
U.S.C. 801(a)(1)(A), because no rules are being adopted by the 
Commission.
