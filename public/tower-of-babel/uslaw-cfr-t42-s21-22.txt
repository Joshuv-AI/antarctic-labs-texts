42 C.F.R. § 21.22 — Submission of application and evidence of qualifications.
Citation: us:cfr:42:21.22
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-21.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 21 (Commissioned Officers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Application form. Every candidate for examination for appointment as an officer shall submit a written application on such form as may be prescribed by the Surgeon General.
(b) Documentary evidence. The application shall be accompanied by such documentary evidence as may be required by the Surgeon General.
