36 C.F.R. § 1225.20 — When do agencies have to get GAO approval for schedules?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1225.20
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1225.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1225 (Scheduling Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If an agency requests a deviation from the GRS related to accountable officer records that would authorize a retention period shorter than the retention period provided in the GRS, the agency must obtain approval from the Comptroller General.
(b) This approval must be obtained before NARA will approve the proposed agency records schedule.
