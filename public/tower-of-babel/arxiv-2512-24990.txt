Abstract
. The Fourier extension conjecture in d ≥ 2 d\geq 2 dimensions is, ‖ f ​ σ d − 1 ^ ‖ L p ​ ( ℝ d ) ≤ C p ‖ f ‖ L p ​ ( σ d − 1 ) , for f ∈ L p ( σ d − 1 ) and p > 2 ​ d d − 1 , \left\|\widehat{f\sigma_{d-1}}\right\|_{L^{p}\left(\mathbb{R}^{d}\right)}\leq C_{p}\left\|f\right\|_{L^{p}\left(\sigma_{d-1}\right)},\ \text{for }f\in L^{p}\left(\sigma_{d-1}\right)\text{ and }p>\frac{2d}{d-1}, where σ d − 1 \sigma_{d-1} is surface measure on the sphere 𝕊 d − 1 \mathbb{S}^{d-1} or a smooth compactly supported measure on the paraboloid ℙ d − 1 \mathbb{P}^{d-1} . We give a proof of this conjecture on the paraboloid that begins with a decomposition suggested in [ Saw8 ] of writing a smooth Alpert projection as a sum of pieces whose Fourier extensions are localized. This is then used in the case d = 3 d=3 to establish the trilinear equivalence of the Fourier extension conjecture given in C. Rios and E. Sawyer [ RiSa ] . A key aspect of the proof is that the trilinear equivalence only requires an averaging over grids, which converts a difficult exponential sum into an oscillatory integral with periodic amplitude, that is then used to prove the localization on the Fourier side. Finally, we extend this argument to all dimensions d ≥ 2 d\geq 2 using bilinear analogues of the smooth Alpert trilinear inequalities, which generalize those in Tao, Vargas and Vega [ TaVaVe ] .

2.1.
Periodic Stationary Phase and Rapid Decay

Here we prove two lemmas that give improved stationary phase and rapid decay

\varphi\left(z\right)=\varphi\left(z+\alpha\right)

Lemma 2

(Periodic Stationary Phase Lemma)
.

Let
N\in\mathbb{N}
. Suppose
that
\varphi\in C^{\kappa}\left(\mathbb{R}^{d-1}\right)
is smooth at
scale
1
and periodic at scale
1

be a smooth cutoff function such that
\psi=1
on

\left[-1,1\right]
and vanishes outside
\left[-2,2\right]

any
\beta,a\in\mathbb{R}^{d-1}

(2.1)

\left|\int\psi\left(\frac{z}{N}\right)e^{i\beta z}\varphi\left(z\right)e^{-i\gamma\left|z+a\right|^{2}}dz\right|\lesssim\min\left\{\gamma^{-\frac{d-1}{2}},N^{d-1}\right\}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{d}}.

Proof of Lemma
2
.

First we reduce the proof to the case
\beta=0
,
simply by completing the square in the exponent:

\displaystyle\left|\int\psi\left(\frac{z}{N}\right)e^{i\beta\cdot z}\varphi\left(z\right)e^{-i\gamma\left|z+a\right|^{2}}dz\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\int\psi\left(\frac{z}{N}\right)\varphi\left(z\right)e^{-i\gamma\left(\left|z-\left(a-\frac{\beta}{2\gamma}\right)\right|^{2}+\left|a\right|^{2}-\left|a-\frac{\beta}{2\gamma}\right|^{2}\right)}dz\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\int\psi\left(\frac{z}{N}\right)\varphi\left(z\right)e^{-i\gamma\left|z-a^{\prime}\right|^{2}}dz\right|\lesssim\min\left\{\gamma^{-\frac{d-1}{2}},N^{d-1}\right\}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{d}},

where
a^{\prime}=a-\frac{\beta}{2\gamma}
. Thus we may assume
\beta=0
.

For simplicity we will consider first the case
d=2
, when what we must show
is

\left|\int\psi\left(\frac{x}{N}\right)\varphi\left(x\right)e^{-i\gamma x^{2}}dx\right|\lesssim\min\left\{\gamma^{-\frac{1}{2}},N\right\}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{2}}

scale
1
and periodic at scale

Consider the case

with

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{\alpha}\equiv\int\psi\left(\frac{x}{N}\right)e^{i\alpha x}e^{-i\gamma x^{2}}dx=\int\psi\left(\frac{x}{N}\right)e^{-i\gamma\left(x^{2}-\frac{\alpha x}{\gamma}\right)}dx

\displaystyle=\int\psi\left(\frac{x}{N}\right)e^{-i\gamma\left[\left(x-\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}\right)^{2}-\frac{\alpha^{2}}{4\gamma^{2}}\right]}dx=e^{i\frac{\alpha^{2}}{4\gamma}}\int\psi\left(\frac{x}{N}\right)e^{-i\gamma\left(x-\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}\right)^{2}}dx

\displaystyle\left|\int\psi\left(\frac{z}{N}\right)e^{i\beta z}\varphi\left(z\right)e^{-2\pi i\gamma\left|z+a\right|^{2}}dz\right|

\displaystyle\lesssim\min\left\{\left(\frac{1}{2\gamma\left|a\right|}\right)^{\tau-1}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}\min\left\{\gamma^{-\frac{1}{2}}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau-1}},N^{d-1}\right\},N^{d-1}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{L^{\infty}}\right\},

\displaystyle\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \text{for }\left|a\right|\geq 4N.

Proof of Lemma
3
.

We first prove the lemma in dimension
d=2
with

\beta=0
, where we have

\displaystyle\mathcal{I}_{\alpha}\equiv\int\psi\left(\frac{x}{N}\right)e^{2\pi i\alpha x}e^{-2\pi i\gamma\left(x+a\right)^{2}}dx=e^{-2\pi i\alpha a}\int\psi\left(\frac{x}{N}\right)e^{2\pi i\alpha\left(x+a\right)}e^{-2\pi i\gamma\left(x+a\right)^{2}}dx

\displaystyle=e^{-2\pi i\alpha a}\int\psi\left(\frac{x}{N}\right)e^{-2\pi i\gamma\left(\left(x+a\right)^{2}-\frac{\alpha\left(x+a\right)}{\gamma}\right)}dx

\displaystyle=e^{-2\pi i\alpha a}\int\psi\left(\frac{x}{N}\right)e^{-2\pi i\gamma\left[\left(x+a-\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}\right)^{2}-\frac{\alpha^{2}}{4\gamma^{2}}\right]}dx=e^{-2\pi i\alpha a}e^{2\pi i\frac{\alpha^{2}}{4\gamma}}\int\psi\left(\frac{x}{N}\right)e^{-2\pi i\gamma\left(x+a-\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}\right)^{2}}dx

\displaystyle\overset{\text{(}x=y-a+\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}\text{)}}{=}e^{-2\pi i\alpha a}e^{2\pi i\frac{\alpha^{2}}{4\gamma}}\int\psi\left(\frac{y-a+\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}}{N}\right)e^{-2\pi i\gamma y^{2}}dy,

and so by integration by parts,

\displaystyle\left|\mathcal{I}_{\alpha}\right|=\left|\int\psi\left(\frac{y-a+\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}}{N}\right)e^{-2\pi i\gamma y^{2}}dy\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\int\psi\left(\frac{y-a+\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}}{N}\right)\left(\frac{1}{-4\pi i\gamma y}\frac{d}{dy}\right)^{\tau}e^{-2\pi i\gamma y^{2}}dy\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\int\left(\frac{d}{dy}\frac{1}{4\pi i\gamma y}\right)^{\tau}\psi\left(\frac{y-a+\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}}{N}\right)e^{-2\pi i\gamma y^{2}}dy\right|.

Now we note that since
\operatorname*{Supp}\psi\subset\left[-2,2\right]
,
we have

\operatorname*{Supp}\psi\left(\frac{y-a+\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}}{N}\right)\subset\left[-2N+a-\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma},2N+a-\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}\right]\equiv\tau_{a-\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}}\left[-2N,2N\right].

Let

Q_{m}\equiv\left[-mN,mN\right].

If
\tau_{a-\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}}\left[-2N,2N\right]
intersects

Q_{m+1}
but not
\ Q_{m}
for some
m\in\mathbb{N}
, which course uniquely
determines
m
, then
\gamma\left|y\right|\approx\gamma m2^{s}
and
we get

\displaystyle\left|\mathcal{I}_{\alpha}\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\int\left(\frac{d}{dy}\frac{1}{4\pi i\gamma y}\right)^{\tau}\psi\left(\frac{y-a+\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}}{N}\right)e^{-2\pi i\gamma y^{2}}dy\right|

\displaystyle\lesssim\int\left(\frac{1}{\gamma\left|y\right|}\right)^{\tau}\left|\left(\frac{d}{dy}\right)^{\tau}\left\{\psi\left(\frac{y-a+\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}}{N}\right)\right\}\right|dy

\displaystyle\approx\int\left(\frac{1}{\gamma m2^{s}}\right)^{\tau}\left(\frac{1}{N}\right)^{\tau}\left|\left(\frac{d}{dy}\right)^{\tau}\psi\left(\frac{y-a+\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}}{N}\right)\right|dy

\displaystyle\lesssim N\left(\frac{1}{\gamma m2^{s}}\right)^{\tau}\left(\frac{1}{N}\right)^{\tau}\left\|\psi\right\|_{\tau}\ .

Otherwise,
\tau_{a-\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}}\left[-2N,2N\right]
intersects

\left[-N,N\right]
, and we simply use the stationary phase estimate.

(2.5)

\left|\mathcal{I}_{\alpha}\right|\lesssim\min\left\{\gamma^{-\frac{1}{2}},N\right\}\ .

Finally then we have

\displaystyle\left|\mathcal{I}\right|\equiv\left|\int\psi\left(\frac{x}{N}\right)\varphi\left(x\right)e^{-2\pi i\gamma\left(x+a\right)^{2}}dx\right|=\left|\int\psi\left(\frac{x-a}{N}\right)\varphi\left(x-a\right)e^{-2\pi i\gamma x^{2}}dz\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\int\psi\left(\frac{x-a}{N}\right)\left(\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\widehat{\varphi}\left(k\right)e^{2\pi ik\left(x-a\right)}\right)e^{-2\pi i\gamma x^{2}}dx\right|

\displaystyle\lesssim\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\left|\widehat{\varphi}\left(k\right)\right|\left|\int\psi\left(\frac{x-a}{N}\right)e^{2\pi ik\left(x-a\right)}e^{-2\pi i\gamma x^{2}}dx\right|=\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}}\left|\widehat{\varphi}\left(k\right)\right|\left|\mathcal{I}_{k}\right|,

which is at most

\left|\mathcal{I}\right|=\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}:\ \left[-N,N\right]\cap\tau_{a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}}\left[-2N,2N\right]\neq\emptyset}\left|\widehat{\varphi}\left(k\right)\right|\left|\mathcal{I}_{k}\right|+\sum_{m\in\mathbb{N}}\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}:\ \tau_{a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}}\left[-2N,2N\right]\sim m}\left|\widehat{\varphi}\left(k\right)\right|\left|\mathcal{I}_{k}\right|\equiv\mathcal{A}+\mathcal{B}.

The first term
\mathcal{A}
will be estimated using (
2.4
)
and (
2.5
) as follows,

\mathcal{A}\lesssim\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}:\ \left[-N,N\right]\cap\tau_{a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}}\left[-2N,2N\right]\neq\emptyset}C_{\tau}\left|\frac{1}{2\pi k}\right|^{\tau}\min\left\{\gamma^{-\frac{1}{2}},N\right\}\ .

We now note that
\left[-N,N\right]\cap\left[a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}-2N,a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}+2N\right]\neq\emptyset
if and only if

k\in\left[-2\gamma\left(3N-a\right),2\gamma\left(3N+a\right)\right].

Thus if
a\geq 4N
, we have

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}

\displaystyle\lesssim\left(\sum_{k=-2\gamma\left(3N-a\right)}^{2\gamma\left(3N+a\right)}\left|\frac{1}{2\pi k}\right|^{\tau}\right)\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}\min\left\{\gamma^{-\frac{1}{2}},N\right\}

\displaystyle\approx\left(\int_{2\gamma\left(a-3N\right)}^{2\gamma\left(3N+a\right)}\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^{\tau}dx\right)\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}\min\left\{\gamma^{-\frac{1}{2}},N\right\}

\displaystyle=\left\{\left(\frac{1}{2\gamma\left(a-3N\right)}\right)^{\tau-1}-\left(\frac{1}{2\gamma\left(3N+a\right)}\right)^{\tau-1}\right\}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}\min\left\{\gamma^{-\frac{1}{2}},N\right\}

\displaystyle\lesssim\left(\frac{1}{2\gamma a}\right)^{\tau-1}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}\min\left\{\gamma^{-\frac{1}{2}},N\right\}.

Now we turn to the second term
\mathcal{B}
, and write

\displaystyle\mathcal{B}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m\in\mathbb{N}}\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}:\ \tau_{a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}}\left[-2N,2N\right]\sim m}\left|\widehat{\varphi}\left(k\right)\right|\left|\mathcal{I}_{k}\right|

\displaystyle=\sum_{m\in\mathbb{N}}\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}:\ \tau_{a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}}\left[-2N,2N\right]\sim m}C_{\tau}\left|\frac{1}{2\pi k}\right|^{\tau}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}N\left(\frac{1}{\gamma m2^{s}}\right)^{\tau}\left(\frac{1}{N}\right)^{\tau}\left\|\psi\right\|_{\tau}

\displaystyle=\sum_{m\in\mathbb{N}}N\left(\frac{1}{\gamma m2^{s}}\right)^{\tau}\left(\frac{1}{N}\right)^{\tau}\left\|\psi\right\|_{\tau}\left\{\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}:\ \tau_{a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}}\left[-2N,2N\right]\sim m}C_{\tau}\left|\frac{1}{2\pi k}\right|^{\tau}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}\right\}.

We now compute the smallest and largest
k
for which
\tau_{a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}}\left[-2N,2N\right]\sim m
when
a\geq 4N
, i.e.

(2.6)

\tau_{a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}}\left[-2N,2N\right]\cap Q_{m}=\emptyset\text{
and }\tau_{a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}}\left[-2N,2N\right]\cap Q_{m+1}\neq\emptyset.

Consider first the left hand condition in (
2.6
) when

a\geq 4N
, which is

\left[a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}-2N,a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}+2N\right]\cap\left[-mN,mN\right]=\emptyset,

i.e.

k\notin\left[2\gamma\left(a-\left(m+2\right)N\right),2\gamma\left(a+\left(m+2\right)N\right)\right].

Now consider the right hand condition in (
2.6
) when
a\geq 4N
, which is

\left[a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}-2N,a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}+2N\right]\cap\left[-\left(m+1\right)N,\left(m+1\right)N\right]\neq\emptyset,

i.e.

k\in\left[2\gamma\left(a-\left(m+3\right)N\right),2\gamma\left(a+\left(m+3\right)N\right)\right].

Altogether then,

\displaystyle k

\displaystyle\in\left[2\gamma\left(a-\left(m+3\right)N\right),2\gamma\left(a+\left(m+3\right)N\right)\right]

\displaystyle\setminus\left[2\gamma\left(a-\left(m+2\right)N\right),2\gamma\left(a+\left(m+2\right)N\right)\right].

We now use that the series
\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}:\ k\neq 0\text{ and }\tau_{a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}}\left[-2N,2N\right]\sim m}\left|\frac{1}{k}\right|^{\tau}
is bounded, which gives

\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}:\ \tau_{a-\frac{k}{2\gamma}}\left[-2N,2N\right]\sim m}C_{\tau}\left|\frac{1}{2\pi k}\right|^{\tau}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}\lesssim C_{\tau}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}\ ,

and hence

\displaystyle\mathcal{B}

\displaystyle\lesssim\sum_{m\in\mathbb{N}}N\left(\frac{1}{\gamma m2^{s}}\right)^{\tau}\left(\frac{1}{N}\right)^{\tau}\left\|\psi\right\|_{\tau}C_{\tau}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}

\displaystyle\approx\sum_{m\in\mathbb{N}}N\left(\frac{1}{\gamma m2^{s}}\right)^{\tau}\left(\frac{1}{N}\right)^{\tau}\left\|\psi\right\|_{\tau}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}

\displaystyle\lesssim N\left(\frac{1}{\gamma 2^{s}}\right)^{\tau}\left(\frac{1}{N}\right)^{\tau}\left\|\psi\right\|_{\tau}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}=N\left(\frac{1}{N}\right)^{\tau}\left(\frac{1}{\gamma N}\right)^{\tau}\left\|\psi\right\|_{\tau}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}.

Combining estimates we have for
a\geq 4N
,

\displaystyle\left|\mathcal{I}\right|

\displaystyle=\mathcal{A}+\mathcal{B}

\displaystyle\lesssim\left(\frac{1}{2\gamma a}\right)^{\tau-1}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}\min\left\{\gamma^{-\frac{1}{2}},N\right\}

\displaystyle+N\left(\frac{1}{N}\right)^{\tau}\left(\frac{1}{\gamma N}\right)^{\tau}\left\|\psi\right\|_{\tau}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}

\displaystyle\lesssim\left(\frac{1}{2\gamma a}\right)^{\tau-1}\min\left\{\gamma^{-\frac{1}{2}},N\right\}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}.

since

\left(\frac{1}{2\gamma a}\right)^{\tau-1}\gamma^{-\frac{1}{2}}\gtrsim N\left(\frac{1}{N}\right)^{\tau}\left(\frac{1}{\gamma N}\right)^{\tau}\ \text{\ \ if }a\lesssim N^{2+\frac{1}{\tau-1}}\gamma^{\frac{1}{2\left(\tau-1\right)}}.

If on the other hand
a\leq 4N
, then the same arguments give the same
estimates, and so altogether we have in
d=2
dimensions,

\left|\mathcal{I}\right|\lesssim\left(\frac{1}{2\gamma\left|a\right|}\right)^{\tau-1}\min\left\{\gamma^{-\frac{1}{2}},N\right\}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau}}\ .

The case of
\beta\in\mathbb{R}
follows upon replacing
k
by

in
the arguments above, which doesn’t adversely affect the estimates since

\left|\left(k+\beta\right)-k\right|_{\infty}\leq 1
.

In the case of general dimension

, the reader can easily verify that
the same arguments as above show that

\left|\mathcal{I}\right|\lesssim\left(\frac{1}{2\gamma\left|a\right|}\right)^{\tau-1}\min\left\{\gamma^{-\frac{d-1}{2}}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{C^{\tau-1}},N^{d-1}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{L^{\infty}}\right\}\ .

Of course we also have the trivial estimate
\left|\mathcal{I}\right|\lesssim N^{d-1}\left\|\varphi\right\|_{L^{\infty}}
, and this
completes the proof of Lemma
3
.

2.2.
Smooth Alpert wavelets

Recall from
[
Saw7
]
the smooth Alpert projections
\left\{\bigtriangleup_{Q;\kappa}\right\}_{Q\in\mathcal{D}}
and corresponding wavelets
\left\{h_{Q;\kappa}^{a}\right\}_{Q\in\mathcal{G},\ a\in\Gamma_{d}}
of moment
vanishing order
\kappa
in
\mathbb{R}^{d}

\mathfrak{C}\left(Q\right)
of
Q

Q

L_{Q;k}^{2}\left(\mu\right)\equiv\left\{f={\displaystyle\sum\limits_{Q^{\prime}\in\mathfrak{C}\left(Q\right)}}\mathbf{1}_{Q^{\prime}}p_{Q^{\prime};k}\left(x\right):\int_{Q}f\left(x\right)x_{i}^{\ell}d\mu\left(x\right)=0,\ \ \ \text{for }0\leq\ell\leq\kappa-1\text{ and }1\leq i\leq d\right\},

where

is a polynomial in
\mathbb{R}^{d}
of degree
\left|\alpha\right|=\alpha_{1}+...+\alpha_{d}
at most
\kappa-1
, and

x^{\alpha}=x_{1}^{\alpha_{1}}x_{2}^{\alpha_{2}}...x_{d-1}^{\alpha_{d-1}}
.
Let
d_{Q;\kappa}\equiv\dim L_{Q;\kappa}^{2}\left(\mu\right)
be the
dimension of the finite dimensional linear space
L_{Q;\kappa}^{2}\left(\mu\right)
. Moreover, for each
a\in\Gamma_{d}
, we may assume the wavelet

is a translation and dilation of the unit wavelet

, where
Q_{0}=\left[0,1\right)^{d}
is the unit
cube in
\mathbb{R}^{d}
.

Continuing to recall
[
Saw7
]
, for a small positive constant
\eta>0
, we
define a smooth approximate identity by
\phi_{\eta}\left(x\right)\equiv\eta^{-n}\phi\left(\frac{x}{\eta}\right)
where
\phi\in C_{c}^{\infty}\left(B_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}\left(0,1\right)\right)

unit integral,
\int_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}\phi\left(x\right)dx=1
, and
vanishing moments of
positive
order less than
\kappa
, i.e.

(2.7)

\int\phi\left(x\right)x^{\gamma}dx=\delta_{\left|\gamma\right|}^{0}=\left\{\begin{array}[c]{ccc}1&\text{ if }&\left|\gamma\right|=0\\
0&\text{ if }&0<\left|\gamma\right|<\kappa\end{array}\right..

The
smooth
Alpert ‘wavelets’ are defined by

h_{Q;\kappa}^{a,\eta}\equiv h_{Q;\kappa}^{a}\ast\phi_{\eta\ell\left(Q\right)}=h_{Q;\kappa}^{a}\ast\phi_{s}^{\eta},\ \ \ \ \ \text{if }\ell\left(Q\right)=2^{-s},

where a consequence of the moment vanishing of

is that

and
h_{Q;\kappa}^{a}
coincide outside a small
\eta
-halo of the skeleton of
Q
, which in turn leads to the boundedness and
invertibility on

of the linear operator

S_{\kappa,\eta}^{\mathcal{G}}
defined below. We also have for

\displaystyle\int h_{Q;\kappa}^{a,\eta}\left(x\right)x^{\beta}dx=\int\phi_{\eta\ell\left(I\right)}\ast h_{Q;\kappa}^{a}\left(x\right)x^{\beta}dx=\int\int\phi_{\eta\ell\left(I\right)}\left(y\right)h_{Q;\kappa}^{a}\left(x-y\right)x^{\beta}dx

\displaystyle=\int\phi_{\eta\ell\left(I\right)}\left(y\right)\left\{\int h_{Q;\kappa}^{a}\left(x-y\right)x^{\beta}dx\right\}dy=\int\phi_{\eta\ell\left(I\right)}\left(y\right)\left\{\int h_{Q;\kappa}^{a}\left(x\right)\left(x+y\right)^{\beta}dx\right\}dy

\displaystyle=\int\phi_{\eta\ell\left(I\right)}\left(y\right)\left\{0\right\}dy=0,

by translation invariance of Lebesgue measure.

Define

to be the bounded invertible linear

that satisfies

, and

\displaystyle=\sum_{J^{\prime}\in\mathcal{D}_{s}\left[U\right]}\left\langle T_{\mathcal{D}+v}^{-1}f,h_{J^{\prime}+v;\kappa}^{\eta}\right\rangle\frac{e^{-2\pi i2^{2s}c_{J^{\prime}}\cdot m}}{2N+1}e^{-2\pi i2^{2s}v\cdot m},\ \ \ \ \ \text{for }v\in\left[-1,1\right]^{d-1}.

From Theorem
10
we recall that
\left\langle T_{\mathcal{D}+v}^{-1}f,h_{J^{\prime}+v;\kappa}^{\eta}\right\rangle
is a smooth function
of
v
at scale
2^{-s}
for
J^{\prime}\in\mathcal{D}_{s}\left[U\right]
,
and hence that the function
A_{0}\left(v\right)=\sum_{J^{\prime}\in\mathcal{D}_{s}\left[U\right]}\left\langle T_{\mathcal{D}+v}^{-1}f,h_{J^{\prime}+v;\kappa}^{\eta}\right\rangle\frac{1}{2N+1}
is also.
Now we note that
A_{m}
satisfies the following periodicity formula for

n\in\Gamma_{s}
with taxicab norm
\left|n\right|_{1}\leq 1
,

(4.4)

A_{m}\left(v+n\right)=\sum_{J^{\prime}\in\mathcal{D}_{s}\left[U\right]}\left\langle T_{\mathcal{D}+v}^{-1}f,h_{J^{\prime}+v+n;\kappa}^{\eta}\right\rangle\frac{e^{-2\pi i2^{2s}c_{J^{\prime}}\cdot m}}{2N+1}e^{-2\pi i2^{2s}\left(v+n\right)\cdot m}=A_{m}\left(v\right)e^{-2\pi i2^{2s}n\cdot m}

since
J^{\prime}\in\mathcal{D}_{s}
are the same cubes as
J^{\prime}\in\mathcal{D}_{s}+n
in this case.

Combining this with the formula for
\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{D}+v},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle
in (
3.11
) and the formula for

\mathcal{E}g_{m}\left(\xi\right)
in (
4.2
), we obtain

(4.5)

\displaystyle\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{D}+v},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\mathcal{E}g_{m}\left(\xi\right)

\displaystyle=A_{m}\left(v\right)\int\left\{\sum_{J\in\mathcal{G}_{s}\left[U\right]}\psi\left(c_{J}\right)e^{2\pi i2^{2s}m\cdot c_{J}}e^{-i\xi^{\prime}\cdot c_{J}}e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|c_{J}\right|^{2}}e^{-i\xi_{d}2x\cdot c_{J}}\right\}e^{-i\xi^{\prime}\cdot x}e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|x\right|^{2}}2^{-s\frac{d-1}{2}}h_{s;\kappa}^{\eta}\left(x\right)dx

\displaystyle=2^{-s\frac{d-1}{2}}\int A_{m}\left(v\right)\left\{\sum_{J\in\mathcal{D}_{s}\left[U\right]+v}\psi\left(c_{J}\right)e^{2\pi i2^{2s}m\cdot c_{J}}e^{-i\xi^{\prime}\cdot c_{J}}e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|c_{J}\right|^{2}}e^{-i\xi_{d}2x\cdot c_{J}}\right\}e^{-i\xi^{\prime}\cdot x}e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|x\right|^{2}}h_{s;\kappa}^{\eta}\left(x\right)dx

\displaystyle=2^{-s\frac{d-1}{2}}\int A_{m}\left(v\right)\Omega_{I}^{m}\left(\xi,x,v\right)e^{-i\xi^{\prime}\cdot x}e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|x\right|^{2}}h_{s;\kappa}^{\eta}\left(x\right)dx.

Here we have denoted the sum in braces by
\Omega_{I}^{m}\left(\xi,x,v\right)
, which is a function of the three variables
\xi
,
x
and

v
defined by

\displaystyle\Omega_{I}^{m}\left(\xi,x,v\right)

\displaystyle\equiv\sum_{J\in\mathcal{D}_{s}\left[U\right]+v}\psi\left(c_{J}\right)e^{-i2^{2s}m\cdot c_{J}}e^{-i\xi^{\prime}\cdot c_{J}}e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|c_{J}\right|^{2}}e^{-i\xi_{d}2x\cdot c_{J}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{J\in\mathcal{D}_{s}\left[U\right]+v}\psi\left(c_{J}+v\right)e^{-i\left(2^{2s}m+\xi^{\prime}\right)\cdot c_{J}}e^{-i\xi_{d}c_{J}\cdot\left(c_{J}+2x\right)}.

In general, such unit lattice exponential sums
\Omega_{I}^{m}\left(\xi,x,v\right)
are difficult to estimate, and we will address this in the
next subsection by averaging the product
A_{m}\left(v\right)\Omega_{I}^{m}\left(\xi,x,v\right)
over all grids
\mathcal{G}=\mathcal{D}+v\in\mathbb{G}
, in order to obtain a much more tractable oscillatory
integral, but with a periodic amplitude. Note that the vector
m\in\Gamma_{s}

only appears explicitly in
A_{m}\left(v\right)
and in the exponential

e^{-i\left(2^{2s}m+\xi^{\prime}\right)\cdot c_{J}}
, where it will
ultimately serve only to ‘translate’ the exponential sum by
2^{2s}m\in 2^{s}\mathbb{Z}^{d-1}
.

4.1.
Circumventing the exponential sum difficulty

If
U
is not necessarily contained in the dyadic grid
\mathcal{G}
, define

\mathcal{G}_{s}\left[U\right]\equiv\left\{J\in\mathcal{G}_{s}:J\cap U\neq\emptyset\right\},

so that this definition reduces to the previous definition when
U\in\mathcal{G}
. To circumvent the difficulty of estimating exponential sums, we
take the average
\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}
over grids
\mathcal{G}\in\mathbb{G}
(which do not in general contain the cube
U
). We will also
write
\mathcal{G}=\mathcal{D}+v
interchangeably in what follows.

Thus we must estimate,

(4.6)

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\mathcal{E}g_{m}\left(\xi\right)=\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\sum_{J\in\mathcal{G}_{s}\left[U\right]}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\varphi_{J}^{m}\psi\left(c_{J}\right)\mathcal{E}h_{J;\kappa}^{\eta}\left(\xi\right)

\displaystyle=\int\left\{\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\sum_{J\in\mathcal{G}_{s}\left[U\right]}\psi\left(c_{J}\right)\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle e^{2\pi i2^{2s}m\cdot c_{J}}e^{-i\xi^{\prime}\cdot c_{J}}e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|c_{J}\right|^{2}}e^{-i\xi_{d}2x\cdot c_{J}}\right\}2^{-s\frac{d-1}{2}}h_{s;\kappa}^{\eta}\left(x\right)dx

\displaystyle=\int\left\{\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\sum_{J\in\mathcal{G}_{s}\left[U\right]}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\Omega_{I}^{m}\left(\xi,x,v\right)\right\}e^{-i\xi^{\prime}\cdot x}e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|x\right|^{2}}2^{-s\frac{d-1}{2}}h_{s;\kappa}^{\eta}\left(x\right)dx

\displaystyle=\int\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x\right)e^{-i\xi^{\prime}\cdot x}e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|x\right|^{2}}2^{-s\frac{d-1}{2}}h_{s;\kappa}^{\eta}\left(x\right)dx,

where the factor
2^{-s\frac{d-1}{2}}
arises from the sequence
\varphi^{m}

and where

\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x\right)\equiv\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\left\{\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\Omega_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x,v\right)\right\}.

Of course we have

\displaystyle\left|\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\mathcal{E}g_{m}\left(\xi\right)\right|

\displaystyle\leq 2^{-s\frac{d-1}{2}}\sup_{x\in\operatorname*{Supp}h_{s;\kappa}^{\eta}}\left|\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x\right)\right|\int\left|h_{s;\kappa}^{\eta}\left(x\right)\right|dx

\displaystyle\lesssim 2^{-s\left(d-1\right)}\sup_{x\in\operatorname*{Supp}h_{s;\kappa}^{\eta}}\left|\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x\right)\right|\approx 2^{-s\left(d-1\right)}\left|\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,0\right)\right|,

and we will later show that
\sup_{x\in\operatorname*{Supp}h_{s;\kappa}^{\eta}}\left|\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x\right)\right|\approx\left|\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,0\right)\right|
, thus giving

(4.7)

\left|\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\mathcal{E}g_{m}\left(\xi\right)\right|\lesssim 2^{-s\left(d-1\right)}\left|\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,0\right)\right|.

We now show that the
averaged
exponential sum
\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x\right)
is actually a well-behaved oscillatory integral with
parameters
\xi,x,v
where
\mathcal{G}=\mathcal{D}+v
.

In order to transform the expectation

(4.8)

\displaystyle\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x\right)

\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\Omega_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x,\mathcal{G}\right)

\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\sum_{J\in\mathcal{G}_{s}\left[U\right]}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\psi\left(c_{J}\right)e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|c_{J}\right|^{2}}e^{-i\left(2^{2s}m+\xi^{\prime}\right)\cdot c_{J}}e^{-i2\xi_{d}x\cdot c_{J}}

\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\sum_{n\in\Gamma_{s}^{\mathcal{G}}}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\psi\left(n\right)e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|n\right|^{2}}e^{-i\left(-2\pi 2^{2s}m+\xi^{\prime}+2\xi_{d}x\right)\cdot n},

into an oscillatory integral, recall the special cube
U_{s\text{ }}
centered
at the origin of side length
2^{-s}
for which
h_{s;\kappa}^{\eta}=h_{U_{s};\kappa}^{\eta}
. Here we are also identifying the sum over

J\in\mathcal{G}_{t}\left[I\right]
with the sum over
n\in\Gamma_{s}
.

Now using the periodicity of
A_{m}
and
A_{m}\left(v\right)=\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{D}+v},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle
, together with

\Gamma_{s}^{\mathcal{G}}=\Gamma_{s}+v
, we can write

\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\Omega_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x,v\right)=A_{m}\left(v\right)\sum_{n\in\Gamma_{s}+v}\psi\left(n\right)e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|n\right|^{2}}e^{-i\left(-2\pi 2^{2s}m+\xi^{\prime}+2\xi_{d}x\right)\cdot n},

where the elements
n
arise as translates of the centers
c_{J\text{ }}
of
the
J
at level
s
that are contained in
I
. In fact, with
N\equiv 2^{s}

and
\mathbb{Z}_{N^{-1}}^{d-1}=N^{-1}\mathbb{Z}^{d-1}\cap Q\left(0,1\right)
, we have

(4.9)

\Gamma_{s}^{\mathcal{G}}=\mathbb{Z}_{N^{-1}}^{d-1}+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right),

where

\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)=\left(\nu_{1}\left(\mathcal{G}\right),...,\nu_{d-1}\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)\in\left[0,N^{-1}\right)^{d-1}

is the unique vector in the cube
\left[-N^{-1},N^{-1}\right)^{d-1}
such
that
\mathcal{G}-\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)=\mathcal{D}
, where

\mathcal{D}
is the standard dyadic grid in
\mathbb{R}^{d-1}
.

For convenience in notation define

(4.10)

N=2^{s}\text{, }\lambda=\xi_{d}\text{, }\beta_{m}=2^{2s}m+\xi^{\prime}+2\xi_{d}x\text{ and }\alpha_{m}=\frac{\beta_{m}}{2\lambda}=\frac{-2\pi 2^{2s}m+\xi^{\prime}}{2\xi_{d}}+x,

and now write the sum
\Omega_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x,\mathcal{G}\right)
as

(4.11)

\displaystyle\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \Omega_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x,\mathcal{G}\right)=\sum_{n\in\Gamma_{s}+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)}\psi\left(n\right)e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|n\right|^{2}}e^{-i\left(-2\pi 2^{2s}m+\xi^{\prime}+2\xi_{d}x\right)\cdot n}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n\in\Gamma_{s}}\psi\left(n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right|^{2}}e^{-i\left(-2\pi 2^{2s}m+\xi^{\prime}+2\xi_{d}x\right)\cdot\left(n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n\in\Gamma_{s}}\psi\left(n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)e^{-i\lambda\left|n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right|^{2}}e^{-i\beta_{m}\cdot\left(n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)}=\sum_{n\in\Gamma_{s}}\psi\left(n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)e^{-i\left\{\lambda\left|n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right|^{2}+\beta_{m}\cdot\left(n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)\right\}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n\in\Gamma_{s}}\psi\left(n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)\prod_{k=1}^{d-1}e^{-i\left\{\lambda\left(n_{k}+\nu_{k}\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)^{2}+\beta_{m,k}\left(n_{k}+\nu_{k}\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)\right\}}=\sum_{n\in\Gamma_{s}}\psi\left(n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)\prod_{k=1}^{d-1}e^{-i\lambda\left\{\left(n_{k}+\nu_{k}\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)^{2}+\frac{\beta_{m,k}}{\lambda}\left(n_{k}+\nu_{k}\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)\right\}}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n\in\Gamma_{s}}\psi\left(n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)\prod_{k=1}^{d-1}e^{-i\lambda\left\{\left(n_{k}+\nu_{k}\left(\mathcal{G}\right)+\frac{\beta_{m,k}}{2\lambda}\right)^{2}-\frac{\beta_{m,k}^{2}}{4\lambda^{2}}\right\}}=e^{-i\left(d-1\right)\frac{\left|\beta_{m}\right|^{2}}{4\lambda}}\sum_{n\in\Gamma_{s}}\psi\left(n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)e^{-i\lambda\left|n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)+\frac{\beta_{m}}{2\lambda}\right|^{2}}.

The reader should keep in mind that the functions
\alpha_{m}
and
\beta_{m}

also depend on the variable
x
. Then from the periodicity of
A_{m}
we
obtain

\displaystyle\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x\right)=\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\left\{A_{m}\left(v\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)\Omega_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x,\mathcal{G}\right)\right\}

\displaystyle=e^{i\left(d-1\right)\frac{\left|\beta\right|^{2}}{4\lambda}}\left\{\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}A_{m}\left(v\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)\sum_{n\in\Gamma_{s}}\psi\left(n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)e^{-i\lambda\left|n+v\left(\mathcal{G}\right)+\alpha_{m}\right|^{2}}\right\},

where
N
,
\lambda
,
\beta
and
\alpha
are as in (
4.10
).
Note that the parameters
N,\lambda,\beta,\alpha
are independent of the grid

\mathcal{G}\in\mathbb{G}
, and again that
\beta,\alpha
are functions of
x

in (
4.6
) as well.

To evaluate the expectation

\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\left(A_{m}\left(\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)\sum_{n\in\Gamma_{s}}\psi\left(n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)e^{-i\lambda\left|n+\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)+\alpha_{m}\right|^{2}}\right),

we replace
\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)
by
v
with the understanding
that
v=\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)
is a function of the grid

\mathcal{G}\
given by (
4.9
), and we consider the expression

S_{N^{-1}}\left(v\right)\equiv A_{m}\left(v\right)\sum_{n\in\Gamma_{s}}\psi\left(n+v\right)e^{-i\lambda\left|r+v+\alpha_{m}\right|^{2}}.

First we note that the expectation
\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}S_{N^{-1}}\left(\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)
over all grids
\mathcal{G}\in\mathbb{G}
equals the average
N^{d-1}\int_{\left[0,N^{-1}\right)^{d-1}}S_{N^{-1}}\left(v\right)dv
of
S_{N^{-1}}
over the cube
\left[0,N^{-1}\right)^{d-1}
, which we denote by the expectation
\mathbb{E}_{\left[0,N^{-1}\right)^{d-1}}
, so that we have

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}S_{N^{-1}}\left(\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)=\mathbb{E}_{\left[0,N^{-1}\right)^{d-1}}A_{m}\left(v\right)\sum_{n\in\Gamma_{s}}\psi\left(n+v\right)e^{i\lambda\left|n+v+\alpha_{m}\right|^{2}}

\displaystyle=N^{d-1}\int_{\left[0,N^{-1}\right)^{d-1}}\sum_{n\in\Gamma_{s}}A_{m}\left(n+v\right)\psi\left(n+v\right)e^{i\lambda\left|n+v+\alpha_{m}\right|^{2}}dv

\displaystyle=N^{d-1}\int_{\left[0,N^{-1}\right)^{d-1}}\sum_{n\in\Gamma_{s}}\left(A_{m}\psi\right)\left(n+v\right)e^{i\lambda\left|n+v+\alpha_{m}\right|^{2}}dv,

by the periodicity of
A_{m}
given in (
4.4
), i.e.

A_{m}\left(v+n\right)=A_{m}\left(v\right)e^{-2\pi i2^{2s}n\cdot m}=A_{m}\left(v\right),\ \ \ \ \ n\in\Gamma_{s}=2^{-s}\mathbb{Z}_{N}^{d-1},

which shows that
A_{m}
can be extended to a function on all of

\mathbb{R}^{d-1}
with period
2^{-s}
. Now we make the change of variable

y=n+\nu+\alpha_{m}
, and then we can add up over the translates of
\left[0,N^{-1}\right)^{d-1}+\alpha_{m}
to obtain

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}S_{N^{-1}}\left(\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)=2^{s\left(d-1\right)}\sum_{n\in\mathbb{Z}_{N^{-1}}^{d-1}}\int_{\left[0,N^{-1}\right)^{d-1}+\alpha_{m}+n}\left(A_{0}\psi\right)\left(y-\alpha\right)e^{i\lambda\left|y\right|^{2}}dy

\displaystyle=2^{s\left(d-1\right)}\int_{\left[-1,1\right)^{d-1}+\alpha_{m}}\left(A_{0}\psi\right)\left(y-\alpha_{m}\right)e^{i\lambda\left|y\right|^{2}}dy

\displaystyle=2^{s\left(d-1\right)}\int_{\left[-1,1\right)^{d-1}+\alpha_{m}}\left(A_{0}\psi\right)\left(y+\frac{2\pi 2^{2s}m}{2\xi_{d}}-\frac{1}{2}\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{\xi_{d}}-x\right)e^{i\lambda\left|y\right|^{2}}dy,

since
\alpha_{m}=\frac{-2\pi 2^{2s}m+\xi^{\prime}}{2\xi_{d}}+x
, and hence

(4.12)

\displaystyle\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x\right)

\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\left\{A_{m}\left(\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)\Omega_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x,\mathcal{G}\right)\right\}

\displaystyle=2^{s\left(d-1\right)}\int_{Q\left(\alpha,1\right)}\left(A_{m}\psi\right)\left(y-\alpha_{m}\right)e^{i\lambda\left|y\right|^{2}}dy

\displaystyle=2^{s\left(d-1\right)}\int\left(A_{m}\psi\right)\left(y+\frac{2\pi 2^{2s}m}{2\xi_{d}}-\frac{1}{2}\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{\xi_{d}}-x\right)e^{i\lambda\left|y\right|^{2}}dy

\displaystyle=\int\delta_{2^{-s}}\left(A_{m}\psi\right)\left(z+\frac{2\pi 2^{3s}m}{2\xi_{d}}-2^{s}\frac{1}{2}\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{\xi_{d}}-2^{s}x\right)e^{i\frac{\xi_{d}}{2^{2s}}\left|z\right|^{2}}dz,

where

Q\left(0,1\right)\equiv\prod_{k=1}^{d-1}\left[-1,1\right),\ \ \ \ \ \text{and }Q\left(\alpha_{m},1\right)=Q\left(0,1\right)+\alpha_{m},

and
\alpha_{m}
and
\lambda
are given in (
4.10
), and since
the support of the function
y\rightarrow\psi\left(y-\alpha_{m}\right)
is
contained in
U+\alpha_{m}
, which is in turn contained in
Q\left(\alpha,1\right)
. Thus we have the following formula,

(4.13)

\displaystyle\ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\mathcal{E}g_{m}\left(\xi\right)=\int\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x\right)e^{-i\xi^{\prime}\cdot x}e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|x\right|^{2}}2^{-s\frac{d-1}{2}}h_{s;\kappa}^{\eta}\left(x\right)dx

\displaystyle=2^{-s\frac{d-1}{2}}\int\left\{\int\delta_{2^{-s}}\left(A_{m}\psi\right)\left(z+\frac{2\pi 2^{3s}m}{2\xi_{d}}-2^{s}\frac{1}{2}\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{\xi_{d}}-2^{s}x\right)e^{i\frac{\xi_{d}}{2^{2s}}\left|z\right|^{2}}dz\right\}e^{-i\xi^{\prime}\cdot x}e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|x\right|^{2}}h_{s;\kappa}^{\eta}\left(x\right)dx.

4.2.
Exploiting moment vanishing

To make further progress in the case
\left|\xi\right|\leq 2^{\left(1-\delta\right)s}
, we will need the improved estimate (
2.1
) in
the Periodic Stationary Phase Lemma
2
. Indeed, using
(
4.12
) we then obtain

\displaystyle\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x\right)\equiv\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\left\{\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\Omega_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x,v\right)\right\}

\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\left\{A_{m}\left(\nu\left(\mathcal{G}\right)\right)\Omega_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x,\mathcal{G}\right)\right\}

\displaystyle=2^{-s\left(d-1\right)}\int\delta_{2^{-s}}\left(A_{m}\psi\right)\left(z+\frac{2\pi 2^{3s}m}{2\xi_{d}}-2^{s}\frac{1}{2}\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{\xi_{d}}-2^{s}x\right)e^{i\frac{\xi_{d}}{2^{2s}}\left|z\right|^{2}}dz

\displaystyle\lesssim 2^{-s\left(d-1\right)}\left(\frac{2^{s}}{\sqrt{\xi_{d}}}\right)^{d-1}=\left(\frac{1}{\xi_{d}}\right)^{\frac{d-1}{2}},

and so

\nabla\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x\right)=2^{s}2^{-s\left(d-1\right)}\int\left[\nabla\delta_{2^{-s}}\left(A_{m}\psi\right)\right]\left(z+\frac{2\pi 2^{3s}m}{2\xi_{d}}-2^{s}\frac{1}{2}\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{\xi_{d}}-2^{s}x\right)e^{i\frac{\xi_{d}}{2^{2s}}\left|z\right|^{2}}dz\sim 2^{s}\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x\right),

and also

\displaystyle\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\mathcal{E}g_{m}\left(\xi\right)

\displaystyle\lesssim\sum_{k=1}^{\frac{2-\delta}{1-\delta}\delta s}\left\|\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\mathcal{E}g_{m}\right\|_{L^{\frac{2d}{d-1}}\left(B_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}\left(0,2^{\frac{s}{1-\delta}}\right)\cap\left\{\left|\xi^{\prime}\right|\leq 2^{k}\xi_{d}\right\}\cap\left\{2^{\left(1-\delta\right)s}\leq\xi_{d}\leq 2^{\frac{s}{1-\delta}}\right\}\right)}

\displaystyle\lesssim\sum_{k=1}^{\frac{2-\delta}{1-\delta}\delta s}\left[s2^{k\left(d-1\right)}\right]^{\frac{d-1}{2d}}\approx s^{\frac{d-1}{2d}}\left(2^{\frac{2-\delta}{1-\delta}\delta s}\right)^{\frac{\left(d-1\right)^{2}}{2d}}\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\frac{2d}{d-1}}\left(U\right)}

\displaystyle=s^{\frac{d-1}{2d}}2^{\frac{2-\delta}{1-\delta}\frac{\left(d-1\right)^{2}}{2d}\delta s}\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\frac{2d}{d-1}}\left(U\right)}=2^{C_{d}\delta s}\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\frac{2d}{d-1}}\left(U\right)},\ \ \ \ \ \text{for all }m,

where the constant
2^{C_{d}\delta s}
is a small growth factor.

We summarize these two results in the following lemma.

Lemma 29
.

For all
m
we have

\displaystyle\left\|\mathcal{E}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle g_{m}\right\|_{L^{\frac{2d}{d-1}}\left(B_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}\left(0,2^{\frac{s}{1-\delta}}\right)\cap\left\{\xi:\ \left|\xi_{d}\right|\leq 2^{-ds}\right\}\right)}^{\frac{2d}{d-1}}\lesssim\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\frac{2d}{d-1}}\left(U\right)}^{\frac{2d}{d-1}},

\displaystyle\left\|\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\mathcal{E}g_{m}\right\|_{L^{\frac{2d}{d-1}}\left(B_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}\left(0,2^{\frac{s}{1-\delta}}\right)\cap\left\{\xi_{d}>2^{\left(1-\delta\right)s}\right\}\cap\left\{2^{\left(1-\delta\right)s}\leq\xi_{d}\leq 2^{\frac{s}{1-\delta}}\right\}\right)}\lesssim 2^{C_{d}\delta s}\left\|f\right\|_{L^{\frac{2d}{d-1}}\left(U\right)},

5.1.
The zero case

This subsection deals with estimating the norm

\displaystyle\left\|\mathcal{E}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{0}\right\rangle g_{0}\right\|_{L^{\frac{2d}{d-1}}\left(B_{\mathbb{R}^{d-1}}\left(0,2^{\frac{s}{1-\delta}}\right)\right)}

\displaystyle\lesssim\left\|\mathcal{E}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{0}\right\rangle g_{0}\right\|_{L^{\frac{2d}{d-1}}\left(Q_{\mathbb{R}^{d-1}}\left(0,2^{\frac{s}{1-\delta}}\right)\cap\left\{0<\xi_{d}\leq 2^{-sd}\right\}\right)}

\displaystyle+\left\|\mathcal{E}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{0}\right\rangle g_{0}\right\|_{L^{\frac{2d}{d-1}}\left(B_{\mathbb{R}^{d-1}}\left(0,2^{\frac{s}{1-\delta}}\right)\cap\left\{2^{-sd}\leq\xi_{d}\leq 2^{\left(1-\varepsilon\right)s}\right\}\right)}

\displaystyle+\left\|\mathcal{E}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{0}\right\rangle g_{0}\right\|_{L^{\frac{2d}{d-1}}\left(Q_{\mathbb{R}^{d-1}}\left(0,2^{\frac{s}{1-\delta}}\right)\cap 2^{\left(1-\delta\right)s}\leq\xi_{d}\leq 2^{\frac{s}{1-\delta}}\right)}

In both of the cases

Thus we may assume

access to the improved estimate in the Improved Periodic Lemma
28

(2) from moment vanishing. Then we can estimate the integral
\left|\int\left(A_{0}\psi\right)\left(y-\frac{1}{2}\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{\xi_{d}}\right)e^{i\xi_{d}\left|y\right|^{2}}dy\right|
by rapid

We now recall that

\displaystyle\alpha_{0}=\alpha_{0}\left(\xi,x\right)=\frac{\xi^{\prime}+2\xi_{d}x}{2\xi_{d}}=\frac{1}{2}\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{\xi_{d}}+x,

5.1.1.
Integration over the unit cone

Recall that
2^{-sd}\leq\xi_{d}\leq 2^{\left(1-\delta\right)s}

not

28

\displaystyle 2^{-s\left(d-1\right)}\Gamma_{U}^{0}\left(\xi,0\right)

\displaystyle=\int\left(A_{0}\psi\right)\left(y-\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{2\xi_{d}}\right)e^{i\xi_{d}\left|y\right|^{2}}dy

\displaystyle\overset{\left(y=2^{-s}z\right)}{=}\int\left(A_{0}\psi\right)\left(2^{-s}z-\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{2\xi_{d}}\right)e^{i\frac{\xi_{d}}{2^{2s}}\left|z\right|^{2}}2^{-s\left(d-1\right)}dz,

i.e.

(5.3)

\Gamma_{U}^{0}\left(\xi,0\right)=\int\left(A_{0}\psi\right)\left(2^{-s}z-\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{2\xi_{d}}\right)e^{i\frac{\xi_{d}}{2^{2s}}\left|z\right|^{2}}dz=\int\delta_{2^{-s}}\left(A_{0}\psi\right)\left(z-2^{s}\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{2\xi_{d}}\right)e^{i\frac{\xi_{d}}{2^{2s}}\left|z\right|^{2}}dz

where the function

.
If
\left|\xi^{\prime}\right|\lesssim\xi_{d}

\left|\frac{1}{2}\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{\xi_{d}}\right|\leq\frac{1}{2}

\displaystyle\left|\Gamma_{U}^{0}\left(\xi,0\right)\right|=\left|\int\left(A_{0}\psi\right)\left(2^{-s}z-\frac{1}{2}\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{\xi_{d}}\right)e^{i\frac{\xi_{d}}{2^{2s}}\left|z\right|^{2}}dz\right|

\displaystyle=\left|\sum_{\lambda\in\mathbb{Z}_{N}^{d-1}:\ \left|\lambda\right|\leq C2^{s}}\int_{Q\left(\lambda,1\right)}\left(A_{0}\psi\right)\left(2^{-s}z-\frac{1}{2}\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{\xi_{d}}\right)e^{i\frac{\xi_{d}}{2^{2s}}\left|z\right|^{2}}dz\right|

\displaystyle\leq\left|\sum_{\lambda\in\mathbb{Z}_{N}^{d-1}:\ \lambda\sim\frac{1}{2}2^{s}\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{\xi_{d}}}\int_{Q\left(\lambda,1\right)}\left(A_{0}\psi\right)\left(2^{-s}z-\frac{1}{2}\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{\xi_{d}}\right)e^{i\frac{\xi_{d}}{2^{2s}}\left|z\right|^{2}}dz\right|

\displaystyle+\sum_{\lambda\in\mathbb{Z}_{N}^{d-1}:\ \lambda\not\sim\frac{1}{2}2^{s}\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{\xi_{d}}}\left|\int_{Q\left(\lambda,1\right)}\left(A_{0}\psi\right)\left(2^{-s}z-\frac{1}{2}\frac{\xi^{\prime}}{\xi_{d}}\right)e^{i\frac{\xi_{d}}{2^{2s}}\left|z\right|^{2}}dz\right|,

where

Then from (

(5.4)

\displaystyle\left|\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{m}\right\rangle\mathcal{E}g_{m}\left(\xi\right)\right|=\left|\int\Gamma_{U}^{m}\left(\xi,x\right)e^{-i\xi^{\prime}\cdot x}e^{-i\xi_{d}\left|x\right|^{2}}2^{-s\frac{d-1}{2}}h_{s;\kappa}^{\eta}\left(x\right)dx\right|

\displaystyle\left\|\mathbb{E}_{\mathbb{G}}\left\langle\mathbf{a}^{f,\mathcal{G}},\varphi^{0}\right\rangle\mathcal{E}g_{0}\right\|_{L^{\frac{2d}{d-1}}\left(B_{\mathbb{R}^{d}}\left(0,2^{\frac{s}{1-\delta}}\right)\cap\left\{\left|\xi^{\prime}\right|\leq\xi_{d}\right\}\cap\left\{\xi_{d}>2^{-sd}\right\}\right)}^{\frac{2d}{d-1}}

N

[
RiSa
