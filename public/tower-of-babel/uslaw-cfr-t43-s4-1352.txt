43 C.F.R. § 4.1352 — Who may file; where to file; when to file.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.1352
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.1352
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The applicant or operator may file a request for hearing on OSM's preliminary finding of permanent permit ineligibility.
(b) The request for hearing must be filed with DCHD, within 30 days of receipt of the preliminary finding by the applicant or operator.
(c) Failure to file a timely request constitutes a waiver of the opportunity for a hearing before OSM makes its final finding concerning permanent permit ineligibility. Any untimely request will be denied.
