20 U.S.C. § 194 — Advisory council
Citation: us:usc:20:194
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section194
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 11 (National Arboretum). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture is authorized to create an advisory council in relation to the plan and development of the national arboretum to be established under this chapter, to include representatives of national organizations interested in the work of the arboretum.
