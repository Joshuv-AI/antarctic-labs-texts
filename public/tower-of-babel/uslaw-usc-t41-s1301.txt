41 U.S.C. § 1301 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:41:1301
Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 13 (Acquisition Councils); Subchapter I (Federal Acquisition Regulatory Council). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this subchapter, the term “Council” means the Federal Acquisition Regulatory Council established under section 1302(a) of this title.
