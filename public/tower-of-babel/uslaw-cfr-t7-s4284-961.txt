7 C.F.R. § 4284.961 — Grant monitoring.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4284.961
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4284.961
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4284 (Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Awards will be monitored by Agency personnel in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, and policies (see § 4284.908 for more information). The Agency may terminate or suspend the award for lack of adequate or timely progress, reporting, documentation, or for failure to comply with Agency requirements.
