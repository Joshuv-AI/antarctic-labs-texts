21 C.F.R. § 346.18 — Astringent active ingredients.
Citation: us:cfr:21:346.18
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-346.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 346 (Anorectal Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The active ingredient of the product consists of any of the following when used within the concentration range established for each ingredient:
(a) Calamine, within a concentration range of 5 to 25 percent by weight per dosage unit (based on the zinc oxide content of calamine).
(b) Witch hazel, 10 to 50 percent.
(c) Zinc oxide, within a concentration range of 5 to 25 percent by weight per dosage unit.
