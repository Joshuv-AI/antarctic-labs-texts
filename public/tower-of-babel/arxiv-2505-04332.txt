The ratio of γ / π 0 \gamma/\pi^{0} production rates in neutrino-nucleus interactions at the Δ \Delta resonance mass region
Ara N. Ioannisian

Abstract
We study the dependence of neutrino-induced γ / π 0 \gamma/\pi^{0} production ( ν μ ( − ) μ + A → ν μ ( − ) μ ( μ ) + γ / π 0 + X \stackrel{{\scriptstyle{(-)}}}{{\nu_{\mu}}}+A\to\stackrel{{\scriptstyle{(-)}}}{{\nu_{\mu}}}(\mu)+\gamma/\pi^{0}+X ) on the target nucleus A, at the Δ \Delta resonance mass region. Our conclusion is based on experimental data for π 0 \pi^{0} production rates at photon-nucleus interactions from the A2 collaboration at the Mainz MAMI accelerator. We assume that Δ \Delta resonance decays are independent of the production mechanism (via photon, Z, or W boson). In Neutral Current (NC) interactions, the 1 ​ π 0 + X 1\pi^{0}+X production scales as A 2/3 , reflecting the nuclear surface area. In contrast, photons from Δ \Delta decays typically escape the nucleus, resulting in a cross-section proportional to the atomic number A. Thus, in NC interactions, the ratio of γ \gamma production to π 0 \pi^{0} production is proportional to A 1/3 . In Charged Current (CC) ν μ \nu_{\mu} ( ν ¯ μ \bar{\nu}_{\mu} ) -induced production of Δ + \Delta^{+} ( Δ 0 \Delta^{0} ) will be proportional to the number of neutrons (protons) in the nucleus. After Δ \Delta decay, due to the charge universality in strong interactions, the suppression factor for π 0 \pi^{0} escaping the nucleus must also follow A -1/3 , as in NC interactions. We predict the ratio of the γ / π 0 \gamma/\pi^{0} production rates in NC and CC interactions and for ν μ \nu_{\mu} and ν ¯ μ \bar{\nu}_{\mu} beams: Argon target : ∼ \sim 3.1% (NC/CC ν μ / ν ¯ μ \nu_{\mu}/\bar{\nu}_{\mu} ) Water target : ∼ \sim 1.9% (NC), ∼ \sim 2.3% (CC ν μ \nu_{\mu} ), ∼ \sim 1.7% (CC ν ¯ μ \bar{\nu}_{\mu} ) Liquid Scintillator target : ∼ \sim 1.7% (NC), ∼ \sim 2.1% (CC ν μ \nu_{\mu} ), ∼ \sim 1.6% (CC ν ¯ μ \bar{\nu}_{\mu} ) The accuracy of our predictions is limited due to uncertainties in the decay rate Δ + / 0 → p / n + γ \Delta^{+/0}\to p/n+\gamma and the presence of other (minor) channels of neutrino-induced γ / π 0 \gamma/\pi^{0} production in the Δ \Delta mass region. We also discuss solving the MiniBooNE anomaly by looking at the CC single photon and single neutral pion production rates at the SBN program experiments at Fermilab.

In this note, we discussed the
\Delta
resonance contribution to the
\gamma
to
\pi^{0}
production rates ratio in neutrino-nucleus interaction.
We provide semi-quantitative predictions for this ratio in common detector materials such as water, liquid scintillator, and argon.
A better understanding and measurement of the
\gamma/\pi^{0}
production ratio will help constrain background models. They may provide a new avenue for investigating anomalies such as the low-energy events excess anomaly at the MiniBooNE. .
