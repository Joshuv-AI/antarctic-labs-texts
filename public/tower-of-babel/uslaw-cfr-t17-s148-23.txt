17 C.F.R. § 148.23 — Reply.
Citation: us:cfr:17:148.23
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-148.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 148 (Implementation Of The Equal Access To Justice Act In Covered Adjudicatory Proceedings Before The Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 15 days after service of an answer, the applicant may file a reply. If the reply is based on any alleged facts not already in the record of the adjudicatory proceeding, the applicant shall include with the reply either supporting affidavits or a request for further proceedings under § 148.26 of this part.
