28 U.S.C. § 2463 — Property taken under revenue law not re­pleviable
Citation: us:usc:28:2463
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2463
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 163 (Fines, Penalties And Forfeitures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All property taken or detained under any revenue law of the United States shall not be repleviable, but shall be deemed to be in the custody of the law and subject only to the orders and decrees of the courts of the United States having jurisdiction thereof.
