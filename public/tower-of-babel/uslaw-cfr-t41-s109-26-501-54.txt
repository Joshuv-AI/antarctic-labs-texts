41 C.F.R. § 109-26.501-54 — -26.501-54 Communications equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-26.501-54
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-26.501-54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-26 (26—Procurement Sources And Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Communications equipment considered to be essential for the accomplishment of security and safety responsibilities is exempt from the requirements of 41 CFR 101-26.501. The Fleet Manager shall approve the installation of communications equipment in motor vehicles.
