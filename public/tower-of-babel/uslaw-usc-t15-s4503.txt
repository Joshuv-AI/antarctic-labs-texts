15 U.S.C. § 4503 — Deposit of remainder of excess amount into Treasury as indirect restitution
Citation: us:usc:15:4503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section4503
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 71 (Petroleum Overcharge Distribution And Restitution). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The amount that remains from the excess amount described in section 4502(c) 1 of this title after all disbursements have been made for a fiscal year under section 4502(d) 1 of this title shall be deposited by the Secretary of the Treasury into the general fund of the Treasury.
