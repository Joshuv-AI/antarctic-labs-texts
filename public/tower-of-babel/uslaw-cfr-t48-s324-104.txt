48 C.F.R. § 324.104 — 324.104 Restrictions on Contractor Access to Government or Third Party Information.
Citation: us:cfr:48:324.104
Creator: Chapter 3—Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-324.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 324 (Protection Of Privacy And Freedom Of Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall establish the restrictions that govern the contractor employees' access to Government or third party information in order to protect the information from unauthorized use or disclosure.
