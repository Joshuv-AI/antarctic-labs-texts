Thank you to our 2025 reviewers
Published: 2025

As Editor-in-Chief of Molecular Therapy Nucleic Acids (MTNA), I would like to extend my deepest gratitude to all the reviewers who have generously volunteered their time, insight, and scientific rigor to evaluate submissions for our journal. Your thoughtful assessments and constructive feedback are the cornerstone of our editorial process, ensuring that every published article meets the highest standards of scientific excellence, ethical integrity, and translational relevance.

Thanks to your dedication, MTNA continues to serve as a leading platform for cutting-edge research in oligonucleotide therapeutics and delivery. From foundational mechanistic studies to innovative clinical applications of oligonucleotide-based drugs, the work you help curate shapes the future of our field and accelerates the translation of novel modalities into meaningful patient outcomes.

Your commitment to scientific quality and your willingness to engage with complex and emerging topics have been instrumental to the journal’s growth and impact. We are sincerely grateful for your contributions and look forward to your continued support in the coming year as we strive to expand the reach, relevance, and rigor of MTNA.

With appreciation,

Paloma Giangrande, Editor-in-Chief
