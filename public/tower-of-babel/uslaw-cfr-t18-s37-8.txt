18 C.F.R. § 37.8 — Obligations of OASIS users.
Citation: us:cfr:18:37.8
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-37.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 37 (Open Access Same-Time Information Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each OASIS user must notify the Responsible Party one month in advance of initiating a significant amount of automated queries. The OASIS user must also notify the Responsible Party one month in advance of expected significant increases in the volume of automated queries.
