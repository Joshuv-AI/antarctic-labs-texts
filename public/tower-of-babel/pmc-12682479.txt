Retraction: Poor condyle position after mandibular reconstruction and a classification system for patients with “VSCU” based on computed tomography: a cross-sectional study with retrospective data collection
Authors: Kexin Wang, Zongxuan He, Xiaochen Yang, Wei Shang, Lin Wang
Published: 2025

The article titled “Poor condyle position after mandibular reconstruction and a classification system for patients with “VSCU” based on computed tomography: a cross-sectional study with retrospective data collection” (1), published in Vol 14, No 4 (April 2024) issue of Quantitative Imaging in Medicine and Surgery, has been retracted at the request of all the authors due to an interest conflict with Wuhan University. Some clinical data used in this article originated from Wuhan University, but the source has not been declared or acknowledged.

Given the mentioned conflict of interest, all authors have unanimously agreed to retract this article.

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may have caused to the journal, reviewers, and readers. We deeply appreciate the time and effort the editorial team and peer reviewers dedicated to the initial evaluation process.
