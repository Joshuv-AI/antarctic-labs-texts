28 C.F.R. § 345.59 — Inmate performance pay.
Citation: us:cfr:28:345.59
Creator: Federal Prison Industries, Inc., Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-345.59
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 345 (Federal Prison Industries (Fpi) Inmate Work Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inmate workers for FPI may also receive Inmate Performance Pay for participation in programs where this award is made. However, inmate workers may not receive both Industries Pay and Performance Pay for the same program activity. For example, an inmate assigned to a pre-industrial class may not receive FPI pay as well as inmate performance pay for participation in the class.
