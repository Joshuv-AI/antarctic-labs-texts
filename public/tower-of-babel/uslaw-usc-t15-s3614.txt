15 U.S.C. § 3614 — Waiver of rights as void
Citation: us:usc:15:3614
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section3614
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 62 (Condominium And Cooperative Conversion Protection And Abuse Relief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any condition, stipulation, or provision binding any person to waive compliance with any provisions of this chapter shall be void.
