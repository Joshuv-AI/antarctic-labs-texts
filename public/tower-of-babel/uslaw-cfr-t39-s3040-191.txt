39 C.F.R. § 3040.191 — Docket and notice of minor corrections to product descriptions.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3040.191
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3040.191
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3040 (Product Lists And The Mail Classification Schedule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Commission shall take the actions identified in paragraphs (b) through (f) of this section.
(b) Establish a docket for each proposal to correct a product description in the Mail Classification Schedule;
(c) Publish notice of the proposal on its website;
(d) Designate an officer of the Commission to represent the interests of the general public in the docket; and
(e) Provide interested persons with an opportunity to comment on whether the proposed corrections are consistent with the policies and the applicable criteria of chapter 36 of title 39 of the United States Code, the applicable requirements of this part, and any applicable Commission directives and orders.
(f) Publish the notice, or an abstract thereof, in the Federal Register.
