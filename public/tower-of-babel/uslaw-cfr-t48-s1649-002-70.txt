48 C.F.R. § 1649.002-70 — 1649.002-70 Applicability of the FAR to FEHB acquisitions.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1649.002-70
Creator: Chapter 16—Office Of Personnel Management Federal Employees Health Benefits Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1649.002-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1649 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Termination of FEHB contracts because of withdrawal of approval is controlled by 5 U.S.C. 8902(e) and 5 CFR 890.204.
(b) Termination of FEHB contracts because of nonrenewal of the contract at the end of the contract term is controlled by 5 U.S.C. 8902(a) and 5 CFR 890.205.
(c) The procedures for settlement of contracts after they are terminated shall be those contained in FAR part 49.
