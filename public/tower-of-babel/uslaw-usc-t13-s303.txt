13 U.S.C. § 303 — Secretary of Treasury functions
Citation: us:usc:13:303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title13-section303
Context: United States Code, Title 13 (Census); Chapter 9 (Collection And Publication Of Foreign Commerce And Trade Statistics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To assist the Secretary to carry out the provisions of this chapter, the Secretary of the Treasury shall collect information in the form and manner prescribed by the regulations issued pursuant to this chapter from persons engaged in foreign commerce or trade and from the owners or operators of carriers.
