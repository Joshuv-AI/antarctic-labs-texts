A Note on the Rogers-Szegö Polynomial q q -Differential Operators
Ronald Orozco López

Abstract
In this paper, we introduce the Rogers-Szegö deformed q q -differential operators g n ​ ( b ​ D q | u ) \mathrm{g}_{n}(bD_{q}|u) based on q q -differential operator D q D_{q} . The motivation for introducing the operators g n ​ ( b ​ D q ) \mathrm{g}_{n}(bD_{q}) is that their limit turns out to be the q q -exponential operator T ⁡ ( b ​ D q ) \mathrm{T}(bD_{q}) given by Chen. The deformed homogeneous Al-Salam-Carlitz polynomials Ψ m ( q − n ) ​ ( u ​ b , x | u ​ q − 1 ) \Psi_{m}^{(q^{-n})}(ub,x|uq^{-1}) can easily be represented by using the operators g n ​ ( b ​ D q | u ) \mathrm{g}_{n}(bD_{q}|u) . Identities relating the new general Al-Salam-Carlitz polynomial, defined by Cao et al., the generalized, and homogeneous Al-Salam-Carlitz polynomials Φ m ( q n ) ​ ( b , x | q ) \Phi_{m}^{(q^{n})}(b,x|q) and basic hypergeometric series are given.

Introduction

We begin with some notation and terminology for basic hypergeometric series
[
5
]
. Let
|q|<1
and the
q
-shifted factorial be defined by

\displaystyle(a;q)_{n}

\displaystyle=\prod_{k=0}^{n-1}(1-q^{k}a),

\displaystyle(a;q)_{\infty}

\displaystyle=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}(a;q)_{n}=\prod_{k=0}^{\infty}(1-aq^{k}).

The multiple
q
-shifted factorial is defined by

\displaystyle(a_{1},a_{2},\ldots,a_{m};q)_{n}

\displaystyle=(a_{1};q)_{n}(a_{2};q)_{n}\cdots(a_{m};q)_{n},

\displaystyle(a_{1},a_{2},\ldots,a_{m};q)_{n}

\displaystyle=(a_{1};q)_{\infty}(a_{2};q)_{\infty}\cdots(a_{m};q)_{\infty}.

The
q
-binomial coefficient is defined by

\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}=\frac{(q;q)_{n}}{(q;q)_{k}(q;q)_{n-k}}.

The
{}_{r}\phi_{s}
basic hypergeometric series is define by

{}_{r}\phi_{s}\left(\begin{array}[]{c}a_{1},a_{2},\ldots,a_{r}\\
b_{1},\ldots,b_{s}\end{array};q,z\right)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a_{1},a_{2},\ldots,a_{r};q)_{n}}{(q,b_{1},b_{2},\ldots,b_{s};q)_{n}}[(-1)^{n}q^{\binom{n}{2}}]^{1+s-r}z^{n},\ |z|<1.

In this paper, we will frequently use the
q
-binomial theorem:

{}_{1}\phi_{0}(a;q,z)=\frac{(az;q)_{\infty}}{(z;q)_{\infty}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(a;q)_{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}z^{n}.

(1)

The
q
-exponential
\mathrm{e}_{q}(z)
is defined by

\mathrm{e}_{q}(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{z^{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}={}_{1}\phi_{0}\left(\begin{array}[]{c}0\\
-\end{array};q,-z\right)=\frac{1}{(z;q)_{\infty}}.

Another
q
-analogue of the classical exponential function is

\mathrm{E}_{q}(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}q^{\binom{n}{2}}\frac{z^{n}}{(q;q)_{n})}={}_{1}\phi_{1}\left(\begin{array}[]{c}0\\
0\end{array};q,-z\right)=(-z;q)_{\infty}.

The following easily verified identities will be frequently used in this paper:

\displaystyle(a;q)_{n}

\displaystyle=\frac{(a;q)_{\infty}}{(aq^{n};q)_{\infty}},

(2)

\displaystyle(a;q)_{n+k}

\displaystyle=(a;q)_{n}(aq^{n};q)_{k},

(3)

\displaystyle(a;q)_{n-k}

\displaystyle=\frac{(a;q)_{n}}{(a^{-1}q^{1-n};q)_{k}}(-qa^{-1})^{k}q^{\binom{k}{2}-nk}.

(4)

The
q
-differential operator
D_{q}
is defined by:

D_{q}f(x)=\frac{f(x)-f(qx)}{x}

and the Leibniz rule for
D_{q}

D_{q}^{n}\{f(x)g(x)\}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}q^{k(n-k)}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}D_{q}^{k}\{f(x)\}D_{q}^{n-k}\{g(q^{k}x)\}.

(5)

Chen and Liu
[
3
]
introduced the
q
-exponential operator

\mathrm{T}(bD_{q})=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(bD_{q})^{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}.

In this paper, we need the general Al-Salam-Carlitz polynomials,
[
6
]

\phi_{n}^{\scalebox{0.75}{\mbox{$\displaystyle\left(\begin{array}[]{c}a,b,c\\
d,e\end{array}\right)$}}}(x,y|q)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}\frac{(a,b,c;q)_{n}}{(d,e;q)_{n}}x^{n-k}y^{k}

(6)

and

\psi_{n}^{\scalebox{0.75}{\mbox{$\displaystyle\left(\begin{array}[]{c}a,b,c\\
d,e\end{array}\right)$}}}(x,y|q)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}(-1)^{k}q^{k(k-n)}\frac{(a,b,c;q)_{n}}{(d,e;q)_{n}}x^{n-k}y^{k},

(7)

with generating functions

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\phi_{n}^{\scalebox{0.75}{\mbox{$\displaystyle\left(\begin{array}[]{c}a,b,c\\
d,e\end{array}\right)$}}}(x,y|q)\frac{t^{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(xt;q)_{\infty}}{}_{3}\phi_{2}\left(\begin{array}[]{c}a,b,c\\
d,e\end{array};q,yt\right),

\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\psi_{n}^{\scalebox{0.75}{\mbox{$\displaystyle\left(\begin{array}[]{c}a,b,c\\
d,e\end{array}\right)$}}}(x,y|q)\frac{t^{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{(xt;q)_{\infty}}{}_{3}\phi_{3}\left(\begin{array}[]{c}a,b,c\\
0,d,e\end{array};q,-yt\right).

The generalized Al-Salam-Carlitz polynomials are defined by

\phi_{n}^{(a,b,c)}(x,y|q)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}\frac{(a,b;q)_{k}}{(c;q)_{k}}x^{k}y^{n-k}

and

\psi_{n}^{(a,b,c)}(x,y|q)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}(-1)^{k}q^{\binom{k+1}{2}-nk}\frac{(a,b;q)_{k}}{(c;q)_{k}}x^{k}y^{n-k}.

The homogeneous Al-Salam-Carlitz polynomials is

\displaystyle\Psi_{n}^{(a)}(x,y|q)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}(-1)^{k}q^{\binom{k+1}{2}-nk}(a;q)_{k}x^{k}y^{n-k}.

The Hahn polynomials are defined by

\Phi_{n}^{(a)}(x,y|q)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}(a;q)_{k}x^{k}y^{n-k}.

(8)

The generating function for the homogeneous Hahn polynomials is

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\Phi_{n}^{(\alpha)}(x,y|q)\frac{t^{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}=\frac{(\alpha xt;q)_{\infty}}{(xt,yt;q)_{\infty}}

with
\max\{|xt|,|yt|\}<1
. The classical Rogers-Szegö polynomials

\mathrm{h}_{n}(x|q)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}x^{k}

is obtained when set
\alpha=0
and
y=1
in the homogeneous Hahn polynomials Eq.(
8
). When
\alpha=0
in Eq.(
8
) we obtain the generalized Rogers-Szegö:

\mathrm{r}_{n}(x,y|q)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}x^{k}y^{n-k}.

2
Deformed Rogers-Szegö polynomial operators

Definition 1
.

Define the deformed Rogers-Szegö operator of degree
n
based on
D_{q}
by

\mathrm{g}_{n}\left(bD_{q}|u\right)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}u^{\binom{n-k}{2}}b^{k}D_{q}^{k}.

(9)

If
u=1
, define the
n
-th Rogers-Szegö
q
-operator based on
D_{q}
as

\mathrm{h}_{n}\left(bD_{q}|q\right)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}b^{k}D_{q}^{k}.

(10)

If
u=q
, define the
n
-th
q
-shifted factorial operators based on
D_{q}
as

\mathrm{s}_{n}(bD_{q}|q)=q^{\binom{n}{2}}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}q^{\binom{k}{2}}(q^{1-n}b)^{k}D_{q}^{k}=q^{\binom{n}{2}}(-q^{1-n}bD_{q};q)_{n}.

(11)

Define the deformed
q
-exponential operator as

\mathrm{T}(bD_{q},u)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}u^{\binom{k}{2}}\frac{(bD_{q})^{k}}{(q;q)_{k}}.

(12)

Then

\displaystyle\mathrm{T}(bD_{q},u)

\displaystyle=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}u^{-\binom{n}{2}}\mathrm{g}_{n}(u^{n-1}bD_{q}|u),

\displaystyle\mathrm{T}(bD_{q})

\displaystyle=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\mathrm{h}_{n}(bD_{q}|q),

\displaystyle\mathrm{E}(bD_{q})

\displaystyle=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}q^{-\binom{n}{2}}\mathrm{s}_{n}(q^{n-1}bD_{q}|q)

where

\mathrm{E}(bD_{q})=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}q^{\binom{n}{2}}\frac{(bD_{q})^{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}.

Then the operator
\mathrm{T}(bD_{q},u)
generalizes to the operators
\mathrm{T}(bD_{q})
and
\mathrm{E}(bD_{q})
.

Definition 2
.

We define the deformed homogeneous Al-Salam-Carlitz polynomials as

\displaystyle\Psi_{n}^{(a)}(x,y|u)

\displaystyle=\sum_{k}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}(-1)^{k}u^{\binom{k+1}{2}-nk}(a;q)_{k}x^{k}y^{n-k}

and the deformed generalized Al-Salam-Carlitz polynomials as

\Psi_{n}^{(a,b,c)}(x,y|u)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}u^{\binom{k+1}{2}-nk}\frac{(a,b;q)_{k}}{(c;q)_{k}}x^{k}y^{n-k}.

Theorem 1
.

\mathrm{g}_{n}\left(bD_{q}|u\right)\{x^{m}\}=\begin{cases}x^{m},&\text{ if }n=0;\\
u^{\binom{n}{2}}\Psi_{m}^{(q^{-n})}(ub,x|uq^{-1}),&\text{ if }n\geq 1.\end{cases}

Also,

\mathrm{T}(bD_{q},u)\{x^{m}\}=\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}u^{-\binom{n}{2}}g_{n}(u^{n-1}bD_{q})\{x^{m}\}=(x\oplus_{1,u}b)_{q}^{(m)}.

Proof.

If
n=0
,
\mathrm{g}_{0}(bD_{q}|q)
is the identity operator. If
n\neq 0
, then

\displaystyle\mathrm{g}_{n}\left(bD_{q}|u\right)\{x^{m}\}

\displaystyle=u^{\binom{n}{2}}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}u^{\binom{k+1}{2}-nk}b^{k}D_{q}^{k}\{x^{m}\}

\displaystyle=u^{\binom{n}{2}}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{n}{k}_{q}u^{\binom{k+1}{2}-nk}b^{k}\frac{(q;q)_{m}}{(q;q)_{m-k}}x^{m-k}

\displaystyle=u^{\binom{n}{2}}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{m}{k}_{q}u^{\binom{k+1}{2}-nk}\frac{(q;q)_{n}}{(q;q)_{n-k}}b^{k}x^{m-k}

\displaystyle=u^{\binom{n}{2}}\sum_{k=0}^{m}\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{}{m}{k}_{q}(-1)^{k}(uq^{-1})^{\binom{k}{2}-nk}(q^{-n};q)_{k}(ub)^{k}x^{m-k}

\displaystyle=u^{\binom{n}{2}}\Psi_{m}^{(q^{-n})}(ub,x|uq^{-1}).

Finally,

\displaystyle\mathrm{T}(bD_{q},u)\{x^{m}\}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}u^{\binom{n}{2}}\frac{b^{n}D_{q}^{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}\{x^{m}\}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n=0}^{m}\frac{(q;q)_{m}}{(q;q)_{n}(q;q)_{m-n}}b^{n}x^{m-n}

\displaystyle=(x\ominus_{1,uq^{-1}}b)_{q}^{(m)}.

The proof is completed.
∎
