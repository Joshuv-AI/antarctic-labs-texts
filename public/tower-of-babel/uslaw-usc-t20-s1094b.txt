20 U.S.C. § 1094b — Assignment of identification numbers
Citation: us:usc:20:1094b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1094b
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter IV (Student Assistance); Part G (General Provisions Relating To Student Assistance Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall assign to each participant in subchapter IV programs, including institutions, lenders, and guaranty agencies, a single Department of Education identification number to be used to identify its participation in each of the subchapter IV programs.
