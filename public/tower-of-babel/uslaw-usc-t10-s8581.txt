10 U.S.C. § 8581 — Civilian member: definition; exceptions
Citation: us:usc:10:8581
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8581
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Education And Training); Chapter 857 (Retirement Of Civilian Members Of The Teaching Staffs Of The United States Naval Academy And United States Naval Postgraduate School). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
In this chapter, the term “civilian member” means a civilian member of the teaching staff of the United States Naval Academy or the United States Naval Postgraduate School. It includes the Provost and Chief Academic Officer, senior professors, professors, associate professors, assistant professors, chief instructors, assistant chief instructors, and instructors.
(b)
This chapter does not apply to any civilian member who was employed at the Naval Academy or the Postgraduate School on January 16, 1936, and who did not elect to participate in the benefits provided by the Act of January 16, 1936, ch. 3 (49 Stat. 1092).
(c)
This chapter does not apply to any person who was a civilian member after September 30, 1956.
