42 C.F.R. § 424.121 — Scope of payments.
Citation: us:cfr:42:424.121
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-424.121
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 424 (Conditions For Medicare Payment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to the conditions set forth in this subpart—
(a) Medicare Part A pays, in the amounts specified in § 413.74 of this chapter, for emergency and nonemergency inpatient hospital services furnished by a foreign hospital.
(b) Medicare Part B pays for certain physicians' services and ambulance services furnished in connection with covered inpatient care in a foreign hospital, as specified in § 424.124.
(c) All other services furnished outside the United States are excluded from Medicare coverage, as specified in § 411.9 of this chapter.
