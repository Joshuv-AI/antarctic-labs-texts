10 U.S.C. § 10303 — Navy Reserve Policy Board
Citation: us:usc:10:10303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10303
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1009 (Reserve Forces Policy Boards And Committees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A Navy Reserve Policy Board shall be convened at least once annually at the seat of government to consider, recommend, and report to the Secretary of the Navy on reserve policy matters. At least half of the members of the Board must be officers of the Navy Reserve.
