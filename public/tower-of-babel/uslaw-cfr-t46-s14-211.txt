46 C.F.R. § 14.211 — Posting of copy of shipping articles.
Citation: us:cfr:46:14.211
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-14.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 14 (Shipment And Discharge Of Merchant Mariners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On commencement of a foreign, intercoastal, or coastwise voyage (including a voyage on the Great Lakes), each master or individual in charge of a vessel when shipping articles are required must ensure that a legible copy of the articles, unsigned by the mariner, and without the next of kin information, is posted at a place accessible to the crew.
