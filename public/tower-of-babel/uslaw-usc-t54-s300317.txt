54 U.S.C. § 300317 — State
Citation: us:usc:54:300317
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section300317
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3003 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this division, the term “State” means—
(1)
a State, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, Guam, American Samoa, the Virgin Islands, and the Northern Mariana Islands; and
(2)
the Republic of the Marshall Islands, the Federated States of Micronesia, and the Republic of Palau.
