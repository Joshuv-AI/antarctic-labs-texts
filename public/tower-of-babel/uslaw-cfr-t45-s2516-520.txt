45 C.F.R. § 2516.520 — How does a State, Indian tribe, or community-based entity review the merits of an application?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2516.520
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2516.520
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2516 (School-Based Service-Learning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In reviewing the merits of an application for a subgrant under this part, a Corporation grantee must use the criteria and priorities in § 2516.500.
