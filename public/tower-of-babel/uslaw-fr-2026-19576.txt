SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Abstract

    The United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO) directly 
sponsors and organizes events, including research-related initiatives, 
meetings, workshops, presentations, panels, advisory committees, and 
outreach events. The USPTO routinely registers attendees for these 
events. This new generic clearance request will allow the USPTO to 
provide a quick and efficient process for the approval of registration 
and application forms for USPTO-sponsored events that go beyond what is 
necessary to register. This also includes one-time miscellaneous forms 
that are primarily of an administrative nature and are necessary to 
achieve mission objectives. The information provided to the USPTO will 
have no effect on an individual's application for other USPTO programs 
or benefits.
    The USPTO seeks to collect common data from interested respondents 
such as name, organization, email address, phone number, address, 
country, state, city or town, special accommodation requests, and how 
the respondent became aware of the pertinent event or conference. The 
information will be used by the USPTO to assess attendance, to make 
logistical arrangements for events such as venue selection and seating, 
and to enable USPTO staff to adequately prepare and facilitate post-
event follow-ups. Additionally, the information will help ensure that 
participants have an effective, efficient, and satisfying experience 
with USPTO programs and services. If applicable, the information 
collection may be used to facilitate respondents' payment for travel 
and hotel expenses.
    As part of this proposed information collection, the USPTO seeks, 
when applicable, to receive greater feedback from those respondents 
participating in USPTO programs. To obtain such feedback, the USPTO may 
solicit or receive other respondent background information such as 
their group size, sector experience, and experience with USPTO 
services. Depending on the topic of the program(s), the USPTO may also 
ask respondents about topics specific to the nature of the USPTO 
programs and services.
    In addition to hosting events, the USPTO also participates in 
events organized by outside groups such as universities, trade 
associations, and businesses. As part of this information collection, 
the USPTO may use speaker invitation forms, questionnaires, and related 
items to learn more about these organizations and to determine if they 
will provide beneficial opportunities for the mission and goals of the 
agency.
    This information collection covers a variety of methods used by the 
USPTO to register or involve the public in its events and programs. The 
estimated number of annual responses and burden hours requested in this 
information collection are based on estimates from other Department of 
Commerce and USPTO generic clearances. Each specific request for 
clearance under this generic information collection will detail 
estimates with the following information: respondent types, number of 
respondents, number of responses, time per response, burden hours, and 
associated costs.

II. Method of Collection

    Items in this information collection may be submitted 
electronically, by phone, or in paper form by mail, fax, or hand 
delivery.

III. Data

    OMB Control Number: 0651-New.
    Forms: None.
    Type of Review: New information collection.
    Affected Public: Private sector.
    Respondent's Obligation: Voluntary.
    Frequency: On occasion.
    Estimated Number of Annual Respondents: 100,000 respondents.
    Estimated Number of Annual Responses: 100,000 responses.
    Estimated Time per Response: The USPTO estimates that the responses 
in this information collection will take the public approximately 5 
minutes (0.08 hours) to complete. This includes the time to gather the 
necessary information, create the document(s), and submit the completed 
item to the USPTO.
    Estimated Total Annual Respondent Burden Hours: 8,333 hours.
    Estimated Total Annual Respondent Hourly Cost Burden: $2,072,500. 
The USPTO estimates the hourly cost burden for this information 
collection uses a blended rate for the respondent cost burden 
calculations, averaging the rates of attorneys, paralegals, and pro se

[[Page 60602]]

respondents. Each specific request for clearance under this generic 
information collection will detail estimates for the hourly cost 
burden.
    The USPTO uses an attorney rate of $550 per hour, which is the 
average billing rate for intellectual property work in all firms as 
shown in the 2025 Report of the Economic Survey published by the 
American Intellectual Property Law Association (AIPLA).\1\ The hourly 
rate for paraprofessionals/paralegals is estimated at $134 as shown in 
the 2024 Utilization and Compensation Survey published by the National 
Association of Legal Assistants (NALA).\2\ The USPTO derives the pro se 
wage rate from the mean hourly wage ($62.13) for physical scientists 
according to the data from the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS).\3\

    The combined average of these respondent rates is $248.71. Using

    Estimated Total Annual Respondent Non-hourly Cost Burden: $0. Each 
specific request for clearance under this generic information

IV. Request for Comments

    The USPTO is soliciting public comments to:
    (a) Evaluate whether the collection of information is necessary for 
the proper performance of the functions of the Agency, including 
whether the information will have practical utility;
    (b) Evaluate the accuracy of the Agency's estimate of the burden of 
the proposed collection of information, including the validity of the 
methodology and assumptions used;
    (c) Enhance the quality, utility, and clarity of the information to 
be collected; and
    (d) Minimize the burden of the collection of information on those 
who intend to respond, including through the use of appropriate 
automated, electronic, mechanical, or other technological collection 
techniques or other forms of information technology, e.g., permitting 
electronic submission of responses.
    All comments submitted in response to this notice are a matter of 
public record. The USPTO will include or summarize each comment in the 
request to OMB to approve this information collection. Before including 
an address, phone number, email address, or other personally 
identifiable information (PII) in a comment, be advised that the entire 
comment--including PII--may be made publicly available at any time. 
While one may request in a comment to withhold PII from public view, 
the USPTO cannot guarantee that it will be able to do so.
