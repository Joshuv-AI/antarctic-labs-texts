42 U.S.C. § 300d–46 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:42:300d–46
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section300d–46
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter X (Trauma Care); Part D (Trauma Centers Operating In Areas Severely Affected By Drug-Related Violence). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this part, the term “uncompensated care costs” means unreimbursed costs from serving self-pay, charity, or Medicaid patients, without regard to payment under section 1396r–4 of this title, all of which are attributable to emergency care and trauma care, including costs related to subsequent inpatient admissions to the hospital.
