39 C.F.R. § 777.11 — General policy.
Citation: us:cfr:39:777.11
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-777.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 777 (Relocation Assistance And Real Property Acquisition Policies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is the policy of the Postal Service to comply voluntarily with the provisions of the Uniform Relocation Assistance and Real Property Acquisition Policies Act of 1970 (Pub. L. 91-646; 84 Stat. 1894), hereinafter referred to as the Act.
