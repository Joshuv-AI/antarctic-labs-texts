12 U.S.C. § 4524 — Audits by GAO
Citation: us:usc:12:4524
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4524
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 46 (Government Sponsored Enterprises); Subchapter I (Supervision And Regulation Of Enterprises); Part A (Financial Safety And Soundness Regulator). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Comptroller General may audit the operations of the Agency, and any such audit shall be conducted in accordance with generally accepted Government auditing standards. All books, records, accounts, reports, files, and property belonging to, or used by, the Agency shall be made available to the Comptroller General.
