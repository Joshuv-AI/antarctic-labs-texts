7 C.F.R. § 789.41 — Rating authorizations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:789.41
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-789.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 789 (Agriculture Priorities And Allocations System (Apas)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A rating authorization is an official action granting specific priority rating authority that:
(1) Permits a person to place a priority rating on an order for an item or service not normally ratable under this part; or
(2) Authorizes a person to modify a priority rating on a specific order or series of contracts or orders.
(b) To request priority rating authority, see section § 789.21.
