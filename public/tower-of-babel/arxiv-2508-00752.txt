The representation theory of somewhere-to-below shuffles
Darij Grinberg

Abstract
Abstract. The somewhere-to-below shuffles are the elements t ℓ := cyc ℓ + cyc ℓ , ℓ + 1 + cyc ℓ , ℓ + 1 , ℓ + 2 + ⋯ + cyc ℓ , ℓ + 1 , … , n t_{\ell}:=\operatorname*{cyc}\nolimits_{\ell}+\operatorname*{cyc}\nolimits_{\ell,\ell+1}+\operatorname*{cyc}\nolimits_{\ell,\ell+1,\ell+2}+\cdots+\operatorname*{cyc}\nolimits_{\ell,\ell+1,\ldots,n} (for ℓ ∈ { 1 , 2 , … , n } \ell\in\left\{1,2,\ldots,n\right\} ) in the group algebra 𝐤 ⁡ [ S n ] \mathbf{k}\left[S_{n}\right] of the n n -th symmetric group S n S_{n} . Basics

We recall some notations from

Let

Let

For any integers

For each

Fix an integer

and

For any

for any single

For any

and call it a

.

The trivial representation

The symbol “

-module (so that the tensor product

-module). This

We fix this order once and for all

5

5

For any subset

•

3.1
The theorem

Lemma 3.5

By definition, we have

Next, we recall that the set
