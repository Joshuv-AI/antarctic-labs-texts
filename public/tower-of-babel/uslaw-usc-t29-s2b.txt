29 U.S.C. § 2b — Studies of productivity and labor costs in industries
Citation: us:usc:29:2b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section2b
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 1 (Labor Statistics); Subchapter I (Bureau Of Labor Statistics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Bureau of Labor Statistics of the United States Department of Labor is authorized and directed to make continuing studies of productivity and labor costs in the manufacturing, mining, transportation, distribution, and other industries.
