9 C.F.R. § 2.127 — Publication of lists of persons subject to the provisions of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:9:2.127
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-2.127
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 2 (Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

APHIS will publish on its website lists of persons licensed or registered in accordance with the provisions of this part. The lists may also be obtained upon request from the Deputy Administrator.
