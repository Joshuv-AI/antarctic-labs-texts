49 U.S.C. § 30124 — Nonuse of safety belts
Citation: us:usc:49:30124
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section30124
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (General); Chapter 301 (Motor Vehicle Safety); Subchapter II (Standards And Compliance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A motor vehicle safety standard prescribed under this chapter may not require a manufacturer to comply with the standard by using a safety belt interlock designed to prevent starting or operating a motor vehicle if an occupant is not using a safety belt.
