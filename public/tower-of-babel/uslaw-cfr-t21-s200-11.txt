21 C.F.R. § 200.11 — Use of octadecylamine in steam lines of drug establishments.
Citation: us:cfr:21:200.11
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-200.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 200 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Food and Drug Administration will not object to the use of octadecylamine in steam lines where the steam may be used for autoclaving surgical instruments and gauze if the octadecylamine in the steam is not more than 2.4 parts per million.
