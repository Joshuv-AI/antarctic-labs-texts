5 C.F.R. § 900.702 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:5:900.702
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-900.702
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 900 (Intergovernmental Personnel Act Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to each activity, program or project receiving Federal financial assistance from the Office of Personnel Management from the date this subpart is approved. The duration of the applicability is the period of time for which the assistance is authorized.
