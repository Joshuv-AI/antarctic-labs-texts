42 U.S.C. § 5850 — Unresolved safety issues plan
Citation: us:usc:42:5850
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5850
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 73 (Development Of Energy Sources); Subchapter II (Nuclear Regulatory Commission; Nuclear Whistleblower Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission shall develop a plan providing for the specification and analysis of unresolved safety issues relating to nuclear reactors and shall take such action as may be necessary to implement corrective measures with respect to such issues. Such plan shall be submitted to the Congress on or before January 1, 1978 and progress reports shall be included in the annual report of the Commission thereafter.
