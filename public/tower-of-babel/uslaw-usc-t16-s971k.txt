16 U.S.C. § 971k — Savings clause

Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 16A (Atlantic Tunas Convention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall have the effect of diminishing the rights and obligations of any Nation under Article VIII(3) of the Convention.
