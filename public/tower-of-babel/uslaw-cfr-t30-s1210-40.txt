30 C.F.R. § 1210.40 — Will ONRR keep the information I provide confidential?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1210.40
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1210.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1210 (Forms And Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ONRR will treat information obtained under this part as confidential to the extent permitted by law as specified at 43 CFR part 2.
