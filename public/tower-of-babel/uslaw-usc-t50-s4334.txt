50 U.S.C. § 4334 — Insurance of property
Citation: us:usc:50:4334
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section4334
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 53 (Trading With The Enemy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Alien Property Custodian may procure insurance in such amounts, and from such insurers, as he believes will adequately protect him against loss in connection with property or interest or proceeds held by him.
