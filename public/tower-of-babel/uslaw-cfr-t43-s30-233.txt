43 C.F.R. § 30.233 — What will the judge do with the original record?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.233
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.233
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The judge must send the original record to the designated LTRO under 25 CFR part 150.
(b) The judge must also send a copy of:
(1) The order to the agency originating the probate, and
(2) The order and inventory to other affected agencies.
