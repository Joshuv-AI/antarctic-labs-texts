5 C.F.R. § 315.501 — Transfer.
Citation: us:cfr:5:315.501
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-315.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 315 (Career And Career-Conditional Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to part 335 of this chapter, an agency may appoint by transfer to a competitive service position, without a break in service of a single workday, a current career or career-conditional employee of another agency.
