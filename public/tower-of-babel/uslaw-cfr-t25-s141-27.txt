25 C.F.R. § 141.27 — Trade in imitation Indian crafts prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:25:141.27
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-141.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 141 (Business Practices On The Navajo, Hopi And Zuni Reservations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may introduce or possess for disposition or sale within the exterior boundaries of the Hopi, Navajo or Zuni Reservations any object that is represented to be an Indian handicraft unless the object was produced by an Indian or Indians with the help of only such devices as allow the manual skill of the maker to condition the shape and design of each individual's product.
