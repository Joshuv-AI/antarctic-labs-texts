40 C.F.R. § 430.50 — Applicability; description of the papergrade sulfite subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:430.50
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-430.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 430 (The Pulp, Paper, And Paperboard Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart apply to discharges resulting from the: Integrated production of pulp and paper at papergrade sulfite mills, where blow pit pulp washing techniques are used; and the integrated production of pulp and paper at papergrade sulfite mills where vacuum or pressure drums are used to wash pulp.
