Wave-particle duality and entanglement in neutrino oscillation
Rajrupa Benerjee, Pratidhwani Swain, Prasanta K. Panigrahi, Sudhanwa Patra

Abstract
We analyze neutrino oscillations from the perspective of quantum complementarity by extending the familiar wave–particle duality into a exact triality relation among visibility, predictability, and entanglement. Using a density-matrix approach, we construct explicit measures of these quantities in both two- and three-flavor frameworks and show that they satisfy an exact conservation-like identity throughout oscillation evolution. In this picture, visibility corresponds to interference strength, predictability to flavor imbalance, and entanglement to the reduction of purity due to flavor correlations. We demonstrate that neutrino oscillations naturally exhibit regimes of maximal entanglement, vanishing coherence, and entanglement monogamy constraining flavor correlations. Our results establish that long-baseline neutrino oscillations realize the triality relation in an energy-dependent way, with T2K probing vacuum-like coherence–entanglement balance and DUNE revealing the decisive role of matter effects in redistributing the quantum information budget.

III
Triality in Neutrino Oscillations

In this section, we will demonstrate analytically how neutrino oscillations satisfy an exact quantum triality relation linking visibility, predictability, and entanglement or mixedness.

•

Predictability (
\mathcal{P}
)
measures the flavor imbalance, or the extent to which one outcome dominates. It is defined in terms of diagonal elements of the density matrix
\rho
as

\mathcal{P}^{2}=\frac{n}{n-1}\sum_{i}\bigg(\rho_{ii}-\frac{1}{n}\bigg)^{2}

(10)

which vanishes for completely democratic mixing and approaches unity when a single flavor dominates.

Considering three flavor neutrino oscillation with
n=3
and using initial flavor
\alpha=e
, the elements of dynamical evolution density matrix are expressed in terms of neutrino probabilities as
\rho_{11}=P_{ee}
,
\rho_{22}=P_{e\mu}
,
\rho_{33}=P_{e\tau}
. Thus, the modified relation for Predictability (
\mathcal{P}
) in terms oscillation probabilities is given by

\displaystyle\mathcal{P}^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{3}{2}\sum^{3}_{\beta=1}\bigg(P_{\alpha\to\beta}-\frac{1}{3}\bigg)^{2}

(11)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{3}{2}\bigg(P^{2}_{ee}+P^{2}_{e\mu}+P^{2}_{e\tau}\bigg)-\frac{1}{2}\,.

For two flavor case, predictability measures the imbalance between the two flavor outcomes and is given in terms of neutrino oscillation probabilities,

\displaystyle\mathcal{P}^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2\big(P^{2}_{ee}+P^{2}_{e\mu}\big)-1=\big(P_{ee}-P_{e\mu}\big)^{2}

\displaystyle\mbox{or,}\,\mathcal{P}

\displaystyle\propto

\displaystyle\mid P_{\alpha\alpha}-P_{\alpha\beta}\mid=|P_{ee}-P_{e\mu}|

(12)

•

Visibility (
\mathcal{V}
)
quantifies the interference strength or change of one neutrino flavor to another and is related to the magnitude of the off-diagonal elements of the density matrix
\rho
as
Roy et al. 2022

\mathcal{V}^{2}=\frac{n}{n-1}\sum_{i\neq j}|\rho_{ij}|^{2}

(13)

After some simplification, the modified expression for Visibility (
\mathcal{V}
) in terms of oscillation probabilities for three flavor neutrino framework is found to be

\displaystyle\mathcal{V}^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 3\bigg(P_{ee}P_{e\mu}+P_{e\mu}P_{e\tau}+P_{e\tau}P_{ee}\bigg)\,.

(14)

Visibility mainly controls the amplitude of oscillatory interference for two flavor neutrino case,

\displaystyle\mathcal{V}^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 2\sum_{\alpha\neq\beta}|\rho_{\alpha\beta}(t)|^{2}=4P_{ee}P_{e\mu}

(15)

\displaystyle\mbox{or,}\,\mathcal{V}

\displaystyle\propto

\displaystyle 2|\rho_{\alpha\beta}(t)|=\sin(2\theta)|\sin\big(\frac{\Delta m^{2}\,L}{4\,E}\big)|

•

Entanglement
\mathcal{E}
reflects the loss of purity of a single-flavor subsystem due to correlations with other flavors. It can be expressed in terms of the density matrix purity,

\mathcal{E}^{2}=\frac{n}{n-1}\bigg(\sum_{i\neq j}\rho_{ii}\rho_{jj}-\sum_{i\neq j}|\rho_{ij}|^{2}\bigg)

(16)

Using some known density matrix algebra, the simplified form of
Entanglement
\mathcal{E}
is read as

\displaystyle\mathcal{E}^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{n}{n-1}\bigg(1-\sum_{i}|\rho_{ii}|^{2}-\sum_{i\neq j}|\rho_{ij}|^{2}\bigg)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 1-\bigg[\frac{n}{n-1}\sum_{i}\big(\rho_{ii}-\frac{1}{n}\big)^{2}\bigg]

\displaystyle\hskip 42.67912pt-\bigg[\frac{n}{n-1}\sum_{i\neq j}|\rho_{ij}|^{2}\bigg]

Where we used
\sum_{i}\big(\rho_{ii}-\frac{1}{n}\big)^{2}=\sum_{i}\big(\rho^{2}_{ii}-\frac{1}{n}\big)
in the 2nd term of above relation. Interestingly, the second and third terms are nothing but the usual Predictability (
\mathcal{P}^{2}
) and Visibility (
\mathcal{V}^{2}
). With density matrix formalism in neutrino oscillation, we show that they follow an exact triality relation

\mathcal{P}^{2}+\mathcal{V}^{2}+\mathcal{E}^{2}=1\,

(17)

The third component of the triality relation is quantified using the I-concurrence, which in this context characterizes the effective (genuine tripartite) entanglement, or mixedness, among the flavor subspaces. For clarity and without loss of generality, we shall employ the term entanglement throughout to denote the quantum correlations shared among the three flavor states.

Also in this framework, the standard wave–particle duality emerges as a limiting case of the triality relation. Predictability quantifies the “particle-like” aspect by measuring the distinguishability of flavor paths, while visibility captures the “wave-like” nature through interference among transition amplitudes. In two-flavor oscillations, these two quantities saturate the complementarity bound,
\mathcal{P}^{2}+\mathcal{V}^{2}=1
, leaving no room for additional contributions. In the three-flavor case, however, the residual share is carried by entanglement (quantified here via I-concurrence), which encodes the genuine quantum correlations among the three flavor subspaces. Thus, neutrino oscillations extend the familiar duality relation into a triality structure at different energies and baselines, the balance can shift from particle-like predictability to wave-like coherence or to flavor entanglement, revealing a richer interplay than is possible in two-path interferometers.

V
Conclusion

We have demonstrated that neutrino oscillations satisfy an exact quantum triality relation linking visibility or coherence, entanglement, and distinguishability for quantum systems, in general. Within a density-matrix formulation, these quantities emerge as direct measures of oscillation interference or coherence, flavor imbalance, and loss of purity due to inter-flavor correlations. Coherence manifests itself in the visibility of the individual neutrino flavors, in the interferometric scenario due to the superposition of the three different flavor states in general.

In conclusion, by applying the triality framework to
T2K
and
DUNE
, we identified characteristic energies where the balance among predictability, visibility, and entanglement shifts in a nontrivial manner. These results establish long-baseline neutrino oscillations as a natural realization of the triality relation, with the quantum information budget redistributed by baseline length and matter effects.
T2K
exhibits a vacuum-like pattern with modest entanglement, while
DUNE
’s strong matter potential enhances either coherence or entanglement depending on the oscillation phase.

An additional perspective is provided by the flavor imbalance, defined as the deviation of the flavor populations from an equal statistical mixture. This quantity highlights the extent to which one flavor channel dominates the oscillation outcome, thereby linking the predictability–entanglement tradeoff to experimentally measurable appearance and disappearance probabilities. At the oscillation maxima, the imbalance is minimized, reflecting the near-equal partition among flavor outcomes, whereas away from these energies it grows, biasing the triality distribution toward predictability or coherence.

Finally, the framework naturally embeds the familiar wave–particle duality where predictability reflects the “particle-like” flavor identity, visibility the “wave-like” interference, and entanglement supplies the additional degree of freedom unique to the three-flavor case. Neutrino oscillations thus extend the standard duality into a genuine triality, positioning them as a testbed where complementarity relations, previously studied mainly in optical interferometers, acquire direct physical significance in elementary particle physics.

The complementary behavior of
T2K
and
DUNE
demonstrates that entanglement among flavor components is indispensable for a complete description of oscillations, providing a direct connection between quantum foundations and neutrino phenomenology and positioning neutrinos as a testbed for complementarity relations traditionally explored in optical systems.

It will be worthwhile to probe the effect of decoherence arising from environmental interaction of the quantum system can lead to the sudden death of entanglement.
Thus, neutrino oscillations represent an intrinsic three-path interferometer where quantum triality can be tested under realistic conditions. This will be perticularly relevant for matter interaction in the context of neutrino oscillation.
