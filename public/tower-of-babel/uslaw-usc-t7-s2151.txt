7 U.S.C. § 2151 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:7:2151
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2151
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 54 (Transportation, Sale, And Handling Of Certain Animals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to promulgate such rules, regulations, and orders as he may deem necessary in order to effectuate the purposes of this chapter.
