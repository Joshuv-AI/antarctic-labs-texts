33 U.S.C. § 702a–1b — Further modification; adoption
Citation: us:usc:33:702a–1b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section702a–1b
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 15 (Flood Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The project for flood control of the Lower Mississippi River adopted by sections 642a, 702a to 702a–1a, 702a–2 to 702d, and 702e to 702h, 702i to 702m, and 704 of this title is modified and, as modified, is authorized and adopted.
