46 C.F.R. § 188.10-69 — -69 Scientific laboratory.
Citation: us:cfr:46:188.10-69
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-188.10-69
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 188 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This term means those spaces on board an oceanographic research vessel used primarily for scientific experimentation or research, and are so identified.
