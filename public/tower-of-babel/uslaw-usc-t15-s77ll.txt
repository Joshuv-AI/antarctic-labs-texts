15 U.S.C. § 77ll — Effective date of subchapter
Citation: us:usc:15:77ll
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section77ll
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2A (Securities And Trust Indentures); Subchapter II (Foreign Securities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter shall not take effect until the President finds that its taking effect is in the public interest and by proclamation so declares.
