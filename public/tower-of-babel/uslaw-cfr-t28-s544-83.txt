28 C.F.R. § 544.83 — Inmate tutors.
Citation: us:cfr:28:544.83
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-544.83
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 544 (Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Institutions may establish an inmate tutor/aide program. Guidelines shall be developed regarding the training and supervision of inmate tutors/aides where such programs are available.
