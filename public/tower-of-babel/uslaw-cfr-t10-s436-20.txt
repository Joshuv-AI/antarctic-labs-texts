10 C.F.R. § 436.20 — Net savings.
Citation: us:cfr:10:436.20
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-436.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 436 (Federal Energy Management And Planning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For a retrofit project, net savings may be found by subtracting life cycle costs based on the proposed project from life cycle costs based on not having it. For a new building design, net savings is the difference between the life cycle costs of an alternative design and the life cycle costs of the basic design.
