14 C.F.R. § 135.109 — Pilot in command or second in command: Designation required.
Citation: us:cfr:14:135.109
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-135.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 135 (Operating Requirements: Commuter And On Demand Operations And Rules Governing Persons On Board Such Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each certificate holder shall designate a—
(1) Pilot in command for each flight; and
(2) Second in command for each flight requiring two pilots.
(b) The pilot in command, as designated by the certificate holder, shall remain the pilot in command at all times during that flight.
