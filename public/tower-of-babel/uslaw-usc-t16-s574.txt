16 U.S.C. § 574 — Damages caused private property in protection, administration, and improvement of national forests; reimbursement
Citation: us:usc:16:574
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section574
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3 (Forests; Forest Service; Reforestation; Management); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture is authorized to reimburse owners of private property for damage or destruction thereof caused by employees of the United States in connection with the protection, administration, or improvement of the national forests, payment to be made from any funds appropriated for the protection, administration, and improvement of the national forests: Provided, That no payment in excess of $2,500 shall be made on any such claim.
