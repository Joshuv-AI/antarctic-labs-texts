42 U.S.C. § 11822 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:42:11822
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11822
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 123 (Drug Abuse Education And Prevention); Subchapter II (Program For Runaway And Homeless Youth). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 180 days after the end of a fiscal year for which funds are appropriated to carry out this subchapter, the Secretary shall submit to the President, the Speaker of the House of Representatives, and the President pro tempore of the Senate a report that contains—
(1)
a description of the types of projects and activities for which grants were made under this subchapter for such fiscal year,
(2)
a description of the number and characteristics of the youth and families served by such projects and activities, and
(3)
a description of exemplary projects and activities for which grants were made under this subchapter for such fiscal year.
