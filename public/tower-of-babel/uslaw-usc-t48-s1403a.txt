48 U.S.C. § 1403a — Expenditure of bond proceeds for public improvements
Citation: us:usc:48:1403a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1403a
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 7 (Virgin Islands); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The proceeds of the bond issues or other obligations herein authorized shall be expended only for the public improvements set forth in section 1403 of this title, or for the reduction of the debt created by such bond issue or obligation, unless otherwise authorized by the Congress.
