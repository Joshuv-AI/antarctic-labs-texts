33 U.S.C. § 1982 — Relationship to Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act
Citation: us:usc:33:1982
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1982
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 33A (Marine Debris Research, Prevention, And Reduction); Subchapter IV (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter supersedes, or limits the authority of the Secretary of the Interior under, the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act (43 U.S.C. 1331 et seq.).
