16 U.S.C. § 7710 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:7710
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section7710
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 96 (North Pacific Fisheries Convention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated out of funds made available to the Secretary and the Secretary of State $300,000 for each of fiscal years 2017 through 2021 to carry out this chapter and to pay the United States contribution to the Commission under Article 12 of the North Pacific Fisheries Convention.
