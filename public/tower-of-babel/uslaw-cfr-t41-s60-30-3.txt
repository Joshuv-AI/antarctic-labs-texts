41 C.F.R. § 60-30.3 — -30.3 Computation of time.
Citation: us:cfr:41:60-30.3
Creator: Chapter 60—Office Of Federal Contract Compliance Programs, Equal Employment Opportunity, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-60-30.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 60-30 (30—Rules Of Practice For Administrative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In computing any period of time under this part or in an order issued hereunder, the time begins with the day following the act, event, or default, and includes the last day of the period, unless it is a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday observed by the Federal Government in which event it includes the next business day.
