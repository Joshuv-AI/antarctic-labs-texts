22 C.F.R. § 40.62 — Failure to attend removal proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:22:40.62
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-40.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 40 (Regulations Pertaining To Both Nonimmigrants And Immigrants Under The Immigration And Nationality Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An alien who without reasonable cause failed to attend, or to remain in attendance at, a hearing initiated on or after April 1, 1997, under INA 240 to determine inadmissibility or deportability shall be ineligible for a visa under INA 212(a)(6)(B) for five years following the alien's subsequent departure or removal from the United States.
