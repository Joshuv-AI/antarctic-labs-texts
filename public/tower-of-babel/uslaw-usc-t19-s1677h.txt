19 U.S.C. § 1677h — Drawback treatment
Citation: us:usc:19:1677h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1677h
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of any law relating to the drawback of customs duties, countervailing duties and antidumping duties imposed by this subtitle shall not be treated as being regular customs duties.
