46 C.F.R. § 154.435 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.435
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.435
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The design of a semi-membrane tank, the supporting insulation for the tank, and the supporting hull structure for the tank must be specially approved by the Commandant (CG-ENG).
(b) A semi-membrane tank must be designed to meet:
(1) § 154.425 through § 154.432;
(2) § 154.437 through § 154.440; or
(3) § 154.444 through § 154.449.
