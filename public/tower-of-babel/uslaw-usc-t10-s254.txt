10 U.S.C. § 254 — Proclamation to disperse
Citation: us:usc:10:254
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section254
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And General Military Powers); Chapter 13 (Insurrection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever the President considers it necessary to use the militia or the armed forces under this chapter, he shall, by proclamation, immediately order the insurgents to disperse and retire peaceably to their abodes within a limited time.
