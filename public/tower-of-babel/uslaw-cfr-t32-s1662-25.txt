32 C.F.R. § 1662.25 — The FOIA Exemptions 8 and 9: Records on financial institutions; records on wells.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1662.25
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1662.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1662 (Freedom Of Information Act (Foia) Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Exemption 8 exempts from disclosure records about regulation or supervision of financial institutions. Exemption 9 exempts from disclosure geological and geophysical information and data, including maps, concerning wells.
