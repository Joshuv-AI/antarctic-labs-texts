30 U.S.C. § 1228 — Interagency cooperation
Citation: us:usc:30:1228
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1228
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 25 (Surface Mining Control And Reclamation); Subchapter III (State Mining And Mineral Resources Research Institutes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall, by such means as he deems appropriate, clarify agency responsibility for Federal mining and mineral resources research and provide for interagency coordination of such research, including the research authorized by this subchapter. Such coordination shall include—
(1)
continuing review of the adequacy of the Government-wide program in mining and mineral resources research;
(2)
identification and elimination of duplication and overlap between agency programs;
(3)
identification of technical needs in various mining and mineral resources research categories;
(4)
recommendations with respect to allocation of technical effort among Federal agencies;
(5)
review of technical manpower needs, and findings concerning management policies to improve the quality of the Government-wide research effort; and
(6)
actions to facilitate interagency communication at management levels.
