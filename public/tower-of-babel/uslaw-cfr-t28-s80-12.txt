28 C.F.R. § 80.12 — Accounting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:28:80.12
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-80.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 80 (Foreign Corrupt Practices Act Opinion Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Neither the submission of a request for an FCPA Opinion, its pendency, nor the issuance of an FCPA Opinion, shall in any way alter the responsibility of an issuer to comply with the accounting requirements of 15 U.S.C. 78m(b)(2) and (3).
