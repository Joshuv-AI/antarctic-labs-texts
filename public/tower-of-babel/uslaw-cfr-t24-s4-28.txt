24 C.F.R. § 4.28 — Civil penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:24:4.28
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-4.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 4 (Hud Reform Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever any employee knowingly and materially violates the prohibition in Section 103, the Department may impose a civil money penalty on the employee in accordance with the provisions of 24 CFR part 30.
