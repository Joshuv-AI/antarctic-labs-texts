Algebraic Magnetism Invariants of Self-Actions of Diagonalizable Monoid Schemes
Arnaud Mayeux

Abstract




Classification:
20M14, 20M32, 14L15, 14L30

keywords
algebraic magnetism, pure magnets, diagonalizable monoid scheme, commutative monoid, cancellative monoid, lattice of submonoids, Green’s relations, algebraic monoid, algebraic semigroup, actions of algebraic monoids, sharp monoid

Abstract:
We provide a method to compute the pure magnets of the action of a diagonalizable monoid scheme on itself. This is described in terms of minimal generators of the sharp monoid obtained quotienting by the face of invertible elements. In particular, in this example, algebraic magnetism detects sharpness and minimal generators of the monoid modulo invertible elements.

Proposition 2.1

if
M^{*}=0
and
\#\mho(a)=2^{\#E}+1
if
M^{*}\neq 0
.

Proof.

If
M^{*}=0
this is Proposition
3.1
.
Otherwise,
[
13
, Proposition 1.3.3]
shows that the sharp monoid
M/M^{*}
is cancellative (integral). Therefore it is enough to apply Propositions
3.1
and
4.5
. The last assertion is immediate.
∎

5
Examples

1.

Let
a
be the action of

on

corresponding to
{\mathbb{N}}\subset{\mathbb{Z}}

4.6
implies that
\mho(a)=\{0,{\mathbb{N}}\}

2.

Let
a

{\mathbb{G}}_{m}^{2}

({\mathbb{Z}}\times{\mathbb{N}})/({\mathbb{Z}}\times{\mathbb{N}})^{*}\simeq{\mathbb{N}}

4.6

\mho(a)=\{0,{\mathbb{Z}},{\mathbb{Z}}\times{\mathbb{N}}\}

does not contain

3.

Let
a

A([(1,-1),(1,0),(1,1)\rangle

(1,-1),(1,0)

Let

a

((2n_{1}-1+\ldots+2n_{s}-1)<2n-1

4.6

6.

Let
N

\mathbb{N}
, and let
a

A({\mathbb{N}})

\mho(a)
