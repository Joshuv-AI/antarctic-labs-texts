40 C.F.R. § 61.97 — Exemption from the reporting and testing requirements of 40 CFR 61.10.
Citation: us:cfr:40:61.97
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-61.97
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 61 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All facilities designated under this subpart are exempt from the reporting requirements of 40 CFR 61.10.
