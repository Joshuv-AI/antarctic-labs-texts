25 C.F.R. § 39.113 — What are the special accountability requirements for the gifted and talented program?
Citation: us:cfr:25:39.113
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-39.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 39 (The Indian School Equalization Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a school identifies more than 13 percent of its student population as gifted and talented the Bureau will immediately audit the school's gifted and talented program to ensure that all identified students:
(a) Meet the gifted and talented requirement in the regulations; and
(b) Are receiving gifted and talented services.
