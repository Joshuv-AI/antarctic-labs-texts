10 C.F.R. § 1044.07 — How can you find out if a particular person is authorized to receive a protected disclosure?
Citation: us:cfr:10:1044.07
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1044.07
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1044 (Security Requirements For Protected Disclosures Under Section 3164 Of The National Defense Authorization Act For Fiscal Year 2000). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must contact the Department of Energy Inspector General for help in determining whether a particular person is authorized to receive the classified or unclassified controlled nuclear information you wish to disclose. The Inspector General will contact the Office of Personnel Security as necessary to determine the security access authorization of the person to receive the protected disclosure.
