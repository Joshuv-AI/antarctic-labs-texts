29 U.S.C. § 1145 — Delinquent contributions
Citation: us:usc:29:1145
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section1145
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 18 (Employee Retirement Income Security Program); Subchapter I (Protection Of Employee Benefit Rights); Part 5 (Administration And Enforcement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every employer who is obligated to make contributions to a multiemployer plan under the terms of the plan or under the terms of a collectively bargained agreement shall, to the extent not inconsistent with law, make such contributions in accordance with the terms and conditions of such plan or such agreement.
