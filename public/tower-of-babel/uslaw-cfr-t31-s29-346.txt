31 C.F.R. § 29.346 — Reduction for survivor benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:31:29.346
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-29.346
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 29 (Federal Benefit Payments Under Certain District Of Columbia Retirement Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a retiree elects a reduction for a survivor annuity, the ratio of the unreduced Federal Benefit Payment to the unreduced total annuity is multiplied by the reduced total annuity to determine the reduced Federal Benefit Payment. (See example 10 of appendix A of this subpart.)
