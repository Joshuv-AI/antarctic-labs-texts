15 U.S.C. § 2212 — Fire safety effectiveness statements
Citation: us:usc:15:2212
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2212
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 49 (Fire Prevention And Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator is authorized to encourage owners and managers of residential multiple-unit, commercial, industrial, and transportation structures to prepare Fire Safety Effectiveness Statements, pursuant to standards, forms, rules, and regulations to be developed and issued by the Administrator.
