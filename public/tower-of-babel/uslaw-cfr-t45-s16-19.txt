45 C.F.R. § 16.19 — How to calculate deadlines.
Citation: us:cfr:45:16.19
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-16.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 16 (Procedures Of The Departmental Grant Appeals Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In counting days, include Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays; but if a due date would fall on a Saturday, Sunday or Federal holiday, then the due date is the next Federal working day.
