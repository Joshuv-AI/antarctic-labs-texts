42 U.S.C. § 1304 — Reservation of right to amend or repeal
Citation: us:usc:42:1304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1304
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter XI (General Provisions, Peer Review, And Administrative Simplification); Part A (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The right to alter, amend, or repeal any provision of this chapter is hereby reserved to the Congress.
