15 U.S.C. § 9597 — Executive Order 11625
Citation: us:usc:15:9597
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9597
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 120 (Minority Business Development); Subchapter VII (Administrative Powers Of The Agency; Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The powers and duties of the Agency shall be determined—
(1)
in accordance with this chapter and the requirements of this chapter; and
(2)
without regard to Executive Order 11625 (36 Fed. Reg. 19967; relating to prescribing additional arrangements for developing and coordinating a national program for minority business enterprise).
