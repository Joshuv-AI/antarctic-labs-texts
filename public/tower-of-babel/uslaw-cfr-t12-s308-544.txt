12 C.F.R. § 308.544 — Deposit in Treasury of United States.
Citation: us:cfr:12:308.544
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-308.544
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 308 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All amounts collected pursuant to this subpart will be deposited as miscellaneous receipts in the Treasury of the United States, except as provided in 31 U.S.C. 3806(g).
