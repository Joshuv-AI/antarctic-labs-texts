16 U.S.C. § 782 — Sponges of less than prescribed size; possession prima facie evidence
Citation: us:usc:16:782
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section782
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 11 (Regulation Of Landing, Curing, And Sale Of Sponges Taken From Gulf Of Mexico And Straits Of Florida). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The presence of sponges of a diameter of less than five inches on any vessel or boat of the United States engaged in sponging in the waters of the Gulf of Mexico or the Straits of Florida outside of State territorial limits, or the possession of any sponges of less than the said diameter sold or delivered by such vessels, shall be prima facie evidence of a violation of the provisions of this chapter.
