19 U.S.C. § 1366 — General Agreement on Tariff and Trade un­affected
Citation: us:usc:19:1366
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1366
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part III (Promotion Of Foreign Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The enactment of sections 1352(a), (c), 1354, and 1360 to 1367 of this title, and section 624(f) of title 7, shall not be construed to determine or indicate the approval or disapproval by the Congress of the Executive Agreement known as the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade.
