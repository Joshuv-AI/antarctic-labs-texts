7 C.F.R. § 2903.3 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2903.3
Creator: Office Of Energy Policy And New Uses, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2903.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2903 (Biodiesel Fuel Education Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Eligibility is limited to nonprofit organizations and institutions of higher education that have demonstrated both knowledge of biodiesel fuel production, use, or distribution and the ability to conduct educational and technical support programs.
(b) Award recipients may subcontract to organizations not eligible to apply provided such organizations are necessary for the conduct of the project.
