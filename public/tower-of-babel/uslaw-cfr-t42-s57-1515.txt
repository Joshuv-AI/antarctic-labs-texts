42 C.F.R. § 57.1515 — Loan closing.
Citation: us:cfr:42:57.1515
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-57.1515
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 57 (Grants For Construction Of Teaching Facilities, Educational Improvements, Scholarships And Student Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Closing of any loan with respect to which a guarantee is made or interest subsidies are paid under this subpart shall be accomplished at such time as may be agreed upon by the parties to such loan and found acceptable to the Secretary.
