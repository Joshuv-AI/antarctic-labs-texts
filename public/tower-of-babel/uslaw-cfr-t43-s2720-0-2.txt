43 C.F.R. § 2720.0-2 — -2 Objectives.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2720.0-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2720.0-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2720 (Conveyance Of Federally-Owned Mineral Interests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The objective is to allow consolidation of surface and subsurface or mineral ownership where there are no known mineral values or in those instances where the reservation interferes with or precludes appropriate non-mineral development and such development is a more beneficial use of the land than the mineral development.
