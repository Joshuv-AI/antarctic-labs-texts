15 U.S.C. § 5411 — Construction
Citation: us:usc:15:5411
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section5411
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 80 (Fasteners). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to limit or otherwise affect the authority of any consensus standards organization to establish, modify, or withdraw any standards and specifications under any other law or authority.
