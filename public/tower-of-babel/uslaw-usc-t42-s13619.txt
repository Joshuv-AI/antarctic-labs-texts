42 U.S.C. § 13619 — “Covered section 8 housing” defined
Citation: us:usc:42:13619
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section13619
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 135 (Residency And Service Requirements In Federally Assisted Housing); Subchapter II (Authority To Provide Preferences For Elderly Residents And Units For Disabled Residents In Certain Section 8 Assisted Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this subchapter, the term “covered section 8 housing” means housing described in section 13641(2)(G) of this title that was originally designed for occupancy by elderly families.
