17 C.F.R. § 12.400 — Scope and applicability of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:17:12.400
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-12.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 12 (Rules Relating To Reparations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules set forth in this subpart are applicable to proceedings forwarded pursuant to § 12.26 (b) and (c) of these rules. Except as provided in §§ 12.106(e) and 12.403(b) of these rules, the rules set forth in this subpart are not applicable to proceedings forwarded pursuant to § 12.26(a) of the Reparation Rules.
