33 C.F.R. § 110.26 — Marblehead Harbor, Marblehead, Mass.
Citation: us:cfr:33:110.26
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-110.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 110 (Anchorage Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The area comprises that portion of the harbor lying between the extreme low water line and southwestward of a line bearing 336° from Marblehead Neck Light to a point on Peach Point at latitude 42°31′03″, longitude 70°50′30″.
