40 C.F.R. § 139.12 — Boilers.
Citation: us:cfr:40:139.12
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-139.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 139 (Discharges Incidental To The Normal Operation Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The requirements in paragraphs (b) and (c) of this section apply to discharges resulting from boiler blowdown.
(b) The discharge from boiler blowdown must be minimized in port.
(c) Additional standards applicable to discharges from boilers when a vessel is operating in federally-protected waters are contained in § 139.40(d).
