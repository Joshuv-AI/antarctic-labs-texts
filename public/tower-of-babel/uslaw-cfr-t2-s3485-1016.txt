2 C.F.R. § 3485.1016 — Title IV, HEA participant.
Citation: us:cfr:2:3485.1016
Creator: Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-3485.1016
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 3485 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A title IV, HEA participant is—
(a) An institution described in 34 CFR 600.4, 600.5, or 600.6 that provides postsecondary education; or
(b) A lender, third-party servicer, or guaranty agency, as those terms are defined in 34 CFR 668.2 or 682.200.
