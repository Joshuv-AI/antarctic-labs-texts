32 C.F.R. § 536.31 — Opening claim files.
Citation: us:cfr:32:536.31
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-536.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 536 (Claims Against The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A claim file will be opened when:
(a) Information that requires investigation under § 536.23 is received.
(b) Records or other documents are requested by a potential claimant or legal representative.
(c) A claim is filed.
