11 U.S.C. § 1327 — Effect of confirmation
Citation: us:usc:11:1327
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1327
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 13 (Adjustment Of Debts Of An Individual With Regular Income); Subchapter II (The Plan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The provisions of a confirmed plan bind the debtor and each creditor, whether or not the claim of such creditor is provided for by the plan, and whether or not such creditor has objected to, has accepted, or has rejected the plan.
(b)
Except as otherwise provided in the plan or the order confirming the plan, the confirmation of a plan vests all of the property of the estate in the debtor.
(c)
Except as otherwise provided in the plan or in the order confirming the plan, the property vesting in the debtor under subsection (b) of this section is free and clear of any claim or interest of any creditor provided for by the plan.
