46 U.S.C. § 55119 — Yukon River
Citation: us:usc:46:55119
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section55119
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part D (Promotional Programs); Chapter 551 (Coastwise Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Section 55102 of this title does not apply to the transportation of merchandise on the Yukon River until the Alaska Railroad is completed and the Secretary of Transportation finds that proper facilities will be available for transportation by citizens of the United States to properly handle the traffic.
