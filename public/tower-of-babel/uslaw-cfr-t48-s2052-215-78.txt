48 C.F.R. § 2052.215-78 — 2052.215-78 Travel approvals and reimbursement—Alternate 1.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2052.215-78
Creator: Chapter 20—Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2052.215-78
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2052 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As prescribed in 2015.209-70(d), the contracting officer shall insert the following clause in cost reimbursement solicitations and contracts which include a ceiling amount on travel. Requests for foreign travel must be submitted to the NRC 30 days in advance of the travel date.
