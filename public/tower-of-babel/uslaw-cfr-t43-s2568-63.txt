43 C.F.R. § 2568.63 — Under what circumstances does BLM reject the appointment of a personal representative?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2568.63
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2568.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2560 (Alaska Occupancy And Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the appointment process is incomplete at the time of allotment application filing, the prospective personal representative must file the proof of appointment with BLM within 18 months after the application filing deadline or BLM will reject the application.
