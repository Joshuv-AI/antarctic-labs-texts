33 C.F.R. § 101.540 — Electronic TWIC inspection requirements for vessels, facilities, and OCS facilities not in Risk Group A.
Citation: us:cfr:33:101.540
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-101.540
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 101 (Maritime Security: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A vessel or facility not in Risk Group A may use the electronic TWIC inspection requirements of § 101.535 in lieu of visual TWIC inspection. If electronic TWIC inspection is used, the recordkeeping requirements of § 104.235(b)(9) and (c) of this subchapter, or § 105.225(b)(9) and (c) of this subchapter, as appropriate, apply.
