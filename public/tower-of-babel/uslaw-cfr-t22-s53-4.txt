22 C.F.R. § 53.4 — Optional use of a valid passport.
Citation: us:cfr:22:53.4
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-53.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 53 (Passport Requirement And Exceptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be construed to prevent a citizen from using a valid U.S. passport in a case in which that passport is not required by this part 53, provided such travel is not otherwise prohibited.
