10 U.S.C. § 8247 — Sale of meals by general messes
Citation: us:usc:10:8247
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8247
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 827 (Rations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under such regulations as the Secretary of the Navy prescribes, meals may be sold by general messes afloat and ashore.
