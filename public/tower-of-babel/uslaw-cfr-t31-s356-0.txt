31 C.F.R. § 356.0 — What authority does the Treasury have to sell and issue securities?
Citation: us:cfr:31:356.0
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-356.0
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 356 (Sale And Issue Of Marketable Book-Entry Treasury Bills, Notes, And Bonds (Department Of The Treasury Circular, Fiscal Service Series No. 1-93)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Chapter 31 of Title 31 of the United States Code authorizes the Secretary of the Treasury to issue United States obligations, and to offer them for sale with the terms and conditions that the Secretary prescribes.
