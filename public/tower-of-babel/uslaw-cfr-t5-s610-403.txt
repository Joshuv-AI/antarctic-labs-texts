5 C.F.R. § 610.403 — Definitions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 610 (Hours Of Duty). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In this subpart, Agency, Credit Hours, and Employee have the meaning given these terms in section 6121 of title 5, United States Code.
