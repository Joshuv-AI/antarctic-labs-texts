48 C.F.R. § 1432.102 — 1432.102 Description of contract financing methods.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1432.102
Creator: Chapter 14—Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1432.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1432 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use of progress payments based on a percentage or stage of completion are authorized for construction contracts. Progress payments for other than construction, alteration, and repair contracts require the CO to write a determination that:
(a) Payments based on costs would be impracticable; and
(b) Adequate measures exist for determining quality standards and the percentage of work accomplished.
