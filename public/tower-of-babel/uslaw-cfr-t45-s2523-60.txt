45 C.F.R. § 2523.60 — May Federal agencies enter into partnerships or participate in consortia?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2523.60
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2523.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2523 (Agreements With Other Federal Agencies For The Provision Of Americorps Program Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Such partnerships or consortia may consist of other Federal agencies, Indian Tribes, subdivisions of States, community based organizations, institutions of higher education, or other non-profit organizations. Partnerships and consortia must be approved by the Corporation.
