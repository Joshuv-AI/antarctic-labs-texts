46 C.F.R. § 308.534 — Certificate to be attached to closing report, Form MA-313-A.
Citation: us:cfr:46:308.534
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-308.534
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 308 (War Risk Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The standard form of Certificate to be attached to the closing report, Form MA-313-A, may be obtained from MARAD's underwriting agent or MARAD and shall be filed each month.
