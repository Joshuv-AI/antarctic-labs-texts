13 C.F.R. § 102.38 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:13:102.38
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-102.38
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 102 (Record Disclosure And Privacy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart shall be construed to entitle any person, as a right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the Privacy Act.
