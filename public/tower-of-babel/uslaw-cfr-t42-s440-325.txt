42 C.F.R. § 440.325 — State plan requirements: Coverage and benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:42:440.325
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-440.325
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 440 (Services: General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to requirements in §§ 440.345 and 440.365, States may elect to provide any of the following types of health benefits coverage:
(a) Benchmark coverage in accordance with § 440.330.
(b) Benchmark-equivalent coverage in accordance with § 440.335.
