34 U.S.C. § 40315 — Enforcement and implementation
Citation: us:usc:34:40315
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section40315
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 403 (Criminal Justice Identification, Information, And Communication); Subchapter II (Exchange Of Criminal History Records For Noncriminal Justice Purposes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All departments, agencies, officers, and employees of the United States shall enforce the Compact and cooperate with one another and with all Party States in enforcing the Compact and effectuating its purposes. For the Federal Government, the Attorney General shall make such rules, prescribe such instructions, and take such other actions as may be necessary to carry out the Compact and this subchapter.
