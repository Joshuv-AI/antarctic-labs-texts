Long-lived resonances of massive scalar fields in the Reissner-Nordström black-hole spacetime: Analytic treatment in the large-mass regime
Shahar Hod

Abstract
The physical and mathematical properties of the composed Reissner-Nordström-black-hole-massive-scalar-field system are studied analytically in the dimensionless large-mass M ​ μ ≫ 1 M\mu\gg 1 regime [here { M , μ } \{M,\mu\} are respectively the mass of the central black hole and the proper mass of the scalar field]. It is proved that, for a given value Q ¯ ≡ Q / M {\bar{Q}}\equiv Q/M of the dimensionless charge parameter of the central black hole, the system is characterized by the presence of quasi-resonances, linearized perturbation modes with arbitrarily long lifetimes. In particular, using analytical techniques, we determine the black-hole-field critical mass spectrum { M ​ μ crit ​ ( Q ¯ ) } \{M\mu_{\text{crit}}({\bar{Q}})\} which characterizes the long-lived resonances of the composed physical system.
