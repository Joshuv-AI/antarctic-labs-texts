31 U.S.C. § 507 — Office of Electronic Government
Citation: us:usc:31:507
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section507
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 5 (Office Of Management And Budget); Subchapter I (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Office of Electronic Government, established under section 3602 of title 44, is an office in the Office of Management and Budget.
