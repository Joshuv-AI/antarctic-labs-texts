21 C.F.R. § 864.7140 — Activated whole blood clotting time tests.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-864.7140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 864 (Hematology And Pathology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. An activated whole blood clotting time tests is a device, used to monitor heparin therapy for the treatment of venous thrombosis or pulmonary embolism by measuring the coagulation time of whole blood.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
