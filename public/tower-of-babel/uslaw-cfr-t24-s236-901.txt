24 C.F.R. § 236.901 — Audit.
Citation: us:cfr:24:236.901
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-236.901
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 236 (Mortgage Insurance And Interest Reduction Payment For Rental Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where a State or local government receives interest reduction payments under section 236(b) of the National Housing Act or is the mortgagor of a mortgage insured or held by the Commissioner under this part, it shall conduct audits in accordance with HUD audit requirements at 2 CFR part 200, subpart F.
