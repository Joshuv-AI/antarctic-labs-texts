45 C.F.R. § 144.204 — Applicability of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:45:144.204
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-144.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 144 (Requirements Relating To Health Insurance Coverage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations contained in this subpart for reporting data apply only to those insurers that have issued qualified long-term care insurance policies to individuals under a qualified State long-term care insurance partnership. They do not apply to the reporting of data by insurers for States with a Medicaid State plan amendment that established a long-term care partnership on or before May 14, 1993.
