30 C.F.R. § 556.1000 — Leases formerly eligible for a bonus or royalty credit.
Citation: us:cfr:30:556.1000
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-556.1000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 556 (Leasing Of Sulfur Or Oil And Gas And Financial Assurance Requirements In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Bonus or royalty credits were available to lessees with leases:
(a) In effect on December 20, 2006, and located in:
(1) The Eastern Planning Area and within 125 miles of the coastline of the State of Florida; or,
(2) The Central Planning Area and within the Desoto Canyon OPD, the Destin Dome OPD, or the Pensacola OPD and within 100 miles of the coastline of the State of Florida.
(b) The deadline for applying for such a bonus or royalty credit was October 14, 2010; therefore, lessees may no longer apply for such credits.
