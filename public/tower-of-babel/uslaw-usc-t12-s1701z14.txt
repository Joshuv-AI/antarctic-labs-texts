12 U.S.C. § 1701z–14 — Lower cost technology demonstration program
Citation: us:usc:12:1701z–14
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1701z–14
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 13 (National Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Housing and Urban Development is authorized to develop and implement a demonstration program utilizing lower cost building technology for projects located on inner-city vacant land.
