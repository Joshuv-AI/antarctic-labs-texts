7 C.F.R. § 61.4 — Supervisor of cottonseed inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:7:61.4
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-61.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 61 (Cottonseed Sold Or Offered For Sale For Crushing Purposes (Inspection, Sampling And Certification)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, whenever he deems necessary, may designate an officer of the Division as supervisor of cottonseed inspection who shall supervise the inspection and sampling of cottonseed and perform such other duties as may be required of him in administering the act and the regulations.
