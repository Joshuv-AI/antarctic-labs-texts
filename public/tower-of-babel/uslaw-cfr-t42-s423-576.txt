42 C.F.R. § 423.576 — Effect of a coverage determination.
Citation: us:cfr:42:423.576
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-423.576
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 423 (Voluntary Medicare Prescription Drug Benefit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The coverage determination is binding on the Part D plan sponsor and the enrollee unless it is reviewed and revised under §§ 423.580 through 423.604 and §§ 423.2000 through 423.2140 or is reopened and revised under § 423.1978.
