30 C.F.R. § 47.53 — Alternative for hazardous waste.
Citation: us:cfr:30:47.53
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-47.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 47 (HAZARD COMMUNICATION (HazCom)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the mine produces or uses hazardous waste, the operator must provide potentially exposed miners and designated representatives access to available information for the hazardous waste that—
(a) Identifies its hazardous chemical components,
(b) Describes its physical or health hazards, or
(c) Specifies appropriate protective measures.
