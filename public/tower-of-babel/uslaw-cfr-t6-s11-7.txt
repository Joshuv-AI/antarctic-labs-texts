6 C.F.R. § 11.7 — Private collection agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:6:11.7
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-11.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 11 (Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DHS will transfer delinquent DHS debts to the Treasury Department's Financial Management Service to obtain debt collection services provided by private collection agencies.
