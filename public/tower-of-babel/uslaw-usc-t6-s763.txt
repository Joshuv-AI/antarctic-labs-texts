6 U.S.C. § 763 — Transfer of Noble Training Center
Citation: us:usc:6:763
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section763
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 2 (National Emergency Management); Subchapter II (Comprehensive Preparedness System); Part B (Additional Preparedness). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Noble Training Center is transferred to the Center for Domestic Preparedness. The Center for Domestic Preparedness shall integrate the Noble Training Center into the program structure of the Center for Domestic Preparedness.
