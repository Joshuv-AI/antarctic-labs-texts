Establishment of United States Area Navigation Route T-581 in the 
Vicinity of Missoula, Montana

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This action corrects a final rule published by the FAA in the 
Federal Register on August 3, 2026, establishing United States Area 
Navigation (RNAV) Route T-581 in the vicinity of Missoula, Montana. 
Specifically, this action corrects the description of the route in the 
regulatory text of the final rule.

DATES: The effective date of the final rule published in the Federal 
Register on August 3, 2026, remains 0901 UTC, October 29, 2026. The 
Director of the Federal Register approves this incorporation by 
reference action under 14 CFR part 71, subject to the annual revision 
of FAA Order JO 7400.11 and publication of conforming amendments.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

[[Page 59056]]

History

    The FAA published a final rule in the Federal Register for Docket 
No. FAA-2025-3288 (91 FR 48756; August 3, 2026), establishing RNAV 
Route T-581. The legal description in that final rule for T-581 
contains two route points that should not be listed in the description 
due to them being a turn of less than one degree. This action corrects 
these errors.

Correction to the Final Rule

    Accordingly, pursuant to the authority delegated to me, the final 
rule for Docket No. FAA-2025-3288, as published in the Federal Register 
on August 3, 2026 (91 FR 48756; FR Doc. 2026-15675), is corrected as 
follows:

0
On page 48757, in the third column, under the heading ``Paragraph 6011 
United States Area Navigation Routes,'' revise the table across all 
three columns to read as follows:

T-581 Missoula, MT (MSO) TO Kalispell, MT (FCA) [NEW]
 
Missoula, MT (MSO)                              VOR/DME                        (Lat. 46[deg]54'28.68'' N, long. 114[deg]05'01.15'' W)
Kalispell, MT (FCA)                             VOR/DME                        (Lat. 48[deg]12'50.77'' N, long. 114[deg]10'33.21'' W)
