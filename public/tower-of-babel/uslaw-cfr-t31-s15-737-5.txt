31 C.F.R. § 15.737-5 — -5 Records.
Citation: us:cfr:31:15.737-5
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-15.737-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 15 (Post Employment Conflict Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There are made available to public inspection at the Office of Director of Practice the roster of all persons prohibited from practice before the Department. Other records may be disclosed upon specific request, in accordance with appropriate disclosure regulations of the Department.
