10 U.S.C. § 10151 — Standby Reserve: composition
Citation: us:usc:10:10151
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section10151
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization And Administration); Chapter 1005 (Elements Of Reserve Components). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Standby Reserve consists of those units or members, or both, of the reserve components, other than those in the Ready Reserve or Retired Reserve, who are liable for active duty only as provided in sections 12301 and 12306 of this title.
