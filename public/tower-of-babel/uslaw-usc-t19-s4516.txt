19 U.S.C. § 4516 — Trade Representative authority
Citation: us:usc:19:4516
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4516
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 29 (United States–Mexico–Canada Agreement Implementation); Subchapter I (Approval Of, And General Provisions Relating To, The Usmca). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If a country (other than the United States) that has signed the USMCA does not enact implementing legislation, the Trade Representative is authorized to enter into negotiations with the other country that has signed the USMCA to consider how the applicable provisions of the USMCA can come into force with respect to the United States and that other country as promptly as possible.
