43 C.F.R. § 4610.5 — Improvements by the United States on leased lands.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4610.5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4610.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4600 (Leases Of Grazing Land—Pierce Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedure in placing improvements on any lands leased under the Pierce Act, will, so far as practicable, be the same as provided under subpart 4120 of subchapter D.
