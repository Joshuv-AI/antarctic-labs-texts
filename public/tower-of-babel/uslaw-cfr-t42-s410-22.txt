42 C.F.R. § 410.22 — Limitations on services of an optometrist.
Citation: us:cfr:42:410.22
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-410.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 410 (Supplementary Medical Insurance (Smi) Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Medicare Part B pays for the services of a doctor of optometry, which he or she is legally authorized to perform in the State in which he or she performs them, if the services are among those described in section 1861(s) of the Act and § 410.10 of this part.
