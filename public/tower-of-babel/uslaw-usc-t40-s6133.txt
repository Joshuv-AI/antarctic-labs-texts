40 U.S.C. § 6133 — Property in the Supreme Court Building and grounds
Citation: us:usc:40:6133
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section6133
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part C (Federal Building Complexes); Chapter 61 (United States Supreme Court Building And Grounds); Subchapter IV (Prohibitions And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is unlawful to step or climb on, remove, or in any way injure any statue, seat, wall, fountain, or other erection or architectural feature, or any tree, shrub, plant, or turf, in the Supreme Court Building or grounds.
