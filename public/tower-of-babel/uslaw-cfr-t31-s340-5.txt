31 C.F.R. § 340.5 — Notice of intent to bid.
Citation: us:cfr:31:340.5
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-340.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 340 (Regulations Governing The Sale Of Treasury Bonds Through Competitive Bidding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any individual, or organization, syndicate, or other group which intends to submit a bid, must, when required by the public notice, give written notice of such intent on Form PD 3555 at the place and within the time specified in the public notice. The filing of such notice will not constitute a commitment to bid.
