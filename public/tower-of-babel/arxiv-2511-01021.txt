Extension of p p -compact operators in Banach spaces
Sainik Karak, Tanmoy Paul

Abstract
. We analyze various consequences in relation to the extension of operators T : X → Y T:X\to Y that are p p -compact, as well as the extension of operators T : X → Y T:X\to Y whose adjoints T ∗ : Y ∗ → X ∗ T^{*}:Y^{*}\to X^{*} are p p -compact. In most cases, we discuss these extension properties when the underlying spaces, either domain or codomain, are P λ P_{\lambda} spaces. We also answer if these extensions are almost norm-preserving in such circumstances where the extension T ~ \widetilde{T} of a T T exists. It is observed that an operator can often be extended to a larger domain when the codomain is appropriately extended as well. Specific assumptions might enable us to obtain an extension of an operator that maintains the same range. In this context, both necessary and sufficient conditions are established for a Banach space to qualify as a L 1 L_{1} -predual.

-predual.

Proof.

From the assumption on

Proposition 3.8
.

For Banach spaces
X,Y
the following are equivalent.

(a)

For every
T\in K_{p}^{d}(Y,X)
,
Z\supseteq Y
and
\varepsilon>0
, there exists
\widetilde{T}\in K_{p}^{d}(Z,X)
with
\kappa_{p}^{d}(\widetilde{T})\leq(\lambda+\varepsilon)\,\kappa_{p}^{d}(T)
and
\kappa_{p}^{d}(\widetilde{T}|_{Y}-T)\leq\varepsilon
.

(b)

For every
T\in K_{p}^{d}(Y,X),Z\supseteq Y
and
\varepsilon>0
, there exists extension
\widetilde{T}\in K_{p}^{d}(Z,X)
with
\kappa_{p}^{d}(\widetilde{T})\leq(\lambda+\varepsilon)\,\kappa_{p}^{d}(T)
.

Proof.

It remains to prove
(a)\Rightarrow(b)
. We follow similar techniques used in the proof of Proposition
2.14
.

Using similar arguments stated in Proposition
2.14
, we get a sequence
(\widetilde{T}_{n})\subseteq K_{p}^{d}(Z,X)
satisfying:

(3)

\kappa_{p}^{d}(\widetilde{T}_{1})\leq\,(\lambda+\varepsilon)\,\kappa_{p}^{d}(T),\quad\kappa_{p}^{d}(\widetilde{T}_{1}|_{Y}-T)<\frac{\varepsilon}{2}.

for
n=1
and for
n\geq 2
we have,

\kappa_{p}^{d}(\widetilde{T}_{n})\leq\,(\lambda+1)\,\kappa_{p}^{d}\Big(T-(\widetilde{T}_{1}+\widetilde{T}_{2}+...+\widetilde{T}_{n-1})|_{Y}\Big),

(4)

\kappa_{p}^{d}\Big(\widetilde{T}_{n}|_{Y}-\big(T-(\widetilde{T}_{1}+\widetilde{T}_{2}+...+\widetilde{T}_{n-1})|_{Y}\big)\Big)<\frac{\varepsilon}{2^{n}}.

For
n\geq 2
, we have
\kappa_{p}^{d}(\widetilde{T}_{n})\leq(\lambda+1)\varepsilon/2^{n-1}
. Hence, the series
\sum\limits_{n=1}^{\infty}\widetilde{T}_{n}
converges in the
\kappa_{p}^{d}
norm topology to an operator
\widetilde{T}\in K_{p}^{d}(Z,X)
satisfying
\widetilde{T}|_{Y}=T
. In fact, from equation
4
for every
\delta>0
, there exists
m
such that
\kappa_{p}^{d}((T-(\widetilde{T_{1}}+\widetilde{T_{2}}+\ldots+\widetilde{T_{m}})|_{Y}))<\delta
.

\mbox{Also~}\kappa_{p}^{d}(\widetilde{T})\leq\kappa_{p}^{d}(\widetilde{T}_{1})+\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}(\lambda+1)\varepsilon/2^{n-1}\leq(\lambda+\varepsilon)\kappa_{p}^{d}(T)+(\lambda+1)\varepsilon.

Since
\varepsilon>0
is arbitrary,
(b)
follows.
∎

Theorem 3.9
.

(a)

Let
X
be a Banach space such that every
T\in K_{p}^{d}(Y,X)
has an extension
\widetilde{T}\in K_{p}^{d}(Z,X)
, where
(Z\supseteq Y)
. Then there is a constant
\eta
so that for every such
Y,Z
, and
T
there exists a
p
-compact extension
\widetilde{T}
with
\kappa_{p}^{d}(\widetilde{T})\leq\eta\kappa_{p}^{d}(T)
.

(b)

Let
X
be a Banach space such that every
T\in K_{p}^{d}(X,Y)
has an extension
\widetilde{T}\in K_{p}^{d}(Z,Y)
, where
(Z\supseteq X)
. Then there exists a constant
\eta
so that for every such
Y,Z
, and
T
there exists a
p
-compact extension
\widetilde{T}
with
\kappa_{p}^{d}(\widetilde{T})\leq\eta\kappa_{p}^{d}(T)
.

Proof.

(a).
The proof proceeds in the same manner as Theorem
2.12
. It remains to prove that
\kappa_{p}^{d}(T_{n}^{\prime})=\kappa_{p}^{d}(T_{n})
, where
T,T_{n}^{\prime},T_{n},Y
and
Y_{n}
are as in theorem
2.12
.

Therefore, we need to prove that
\kappa_{p}((T_{n}^{\prime})^{*})=\kappa_{p}(T_{n}^{*})
. First we observe that
Y_{n}^{*}\supseteq T_{n}^{*}(X^{*})\cong\big((T_{n}^{\prime})^{*}\big)(X^{*})\subseteq(0,...,Y_{n}^{*},0,...)\cong Y_{n}^{*}
. In particular for
x^{*}\in X^{*}
and
y=(y_{1},...,y_{n},...)\in Y
,
T_{n}^{\prime*}(x^{*})(y)=x^{*}(T_{n}^{\prime}(y))=x^{*}(T_{n}y_{n})=(T_{n}^{*})(x^{*})(y_{n})=\Big((0,...,(T_{n}^{*})(x^{*}),0,...\Big)(y)
.

In this way, we get both the sets
T_{n}^{*}(B_{X^{*}})
and
T_{n}^{\prime*}(B_{X^{*}})
are the same. It follows that
\kappa_{p}(T_{n}^{\prime*})=\kappa_{p}(T_{n}^{*})
.

(b).
We first claim the following.

Claim:
There exists a
P_{1}
-space
W
such that for any Banach space
Y
and

T\in K_{p}^{d}(X,Y)
there exists an extension
\widetilde{T}\in K_{p}^{d}(W,Y)
such that

\kappa_{p}^{d}(\widetilde{T})\leq\eta\kappa_{p}^{d}(T)
.

Suppose no such
\eta
exists. Then for every
n
there is a
P_{1}
space

W_{n}\supseteq X
, a Banach space
Y_{n}
and
T_{n}\in K_{p}^{d}(X,Y)
with
\kappa_{p}^{d}(T_{n})=1
such that any
extension
\widetilde{T}_{n}\in K_{p}^{d}(W_{n},Y_{n})
of
T_{n}
satisfies

\kappa_{p}^{d}(\widetilde{T}_{n})\geq n^{3}
. Let
Y=\oplus_{c_{0}}Y_{n}
and consider
T_{n}:X\to Y
as each
Y_{n}
is a subspace of
Y
. Now define
T:X\to Y\text{ by }T=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{T_{n}}{n^{2}}
. Clearly,
T\in K_{p}^{d}(X,Y)
since each
T_{n}\in K_{p}^{d}(X,Y)
. Now observe that

\bigoplus_{\ell_{\infty}}W_{n}=W
is a
P_{1}
space as each
W_{n}
is a
P_{1}
space and

W
contains
X
. By our hypothesis there exists an extension

\widetilde{T}\in K_{p}^{d}(W,Y)
of
T
. Then the restriction of
n^{2}\widetilde{T}
to

W_{n}
is an extension of
T_{n}
. From our assumption

\kappa_{p}^{d}(n^{2}\widetilde{T})\geq n^{3}
, which leads to

\kappa_{p}^{d}(\widetilde{T})\geq n
, for all
n
, a clear contradiction.

Next, assume that
Z
is a Banach space and
Z\supseteq X
and let

T\in K_{p}^{d}(X,Y)
. From the above claim, there exists an extension

\widetilde{T}\in K_{p}^{d}(Z,Y)
. Moreover, the identity

I:X\to X
has an extension
\widetilde{I}:Z\to W
with
\|\widetilde{I}\|=1
,
which follows from the property of
P_{1}
-space. Clearly

\widetilde{T}\circ\widetilde{I}:Z\rightarrow Y
is an extension of
T
, moreover,

\widetilde{T}\circ\widetilde{I}\in K_{p}^{d}(Z,Y)
and
\kappa_{p}^{d}(\widetilde{T}\circ\widetilde{I})\leq\kappa_{p}^{d}(\widetilde{T})\|\widetilde{I}\|\leq\eta\kappa_{p}^{d}(T)
.
∎
