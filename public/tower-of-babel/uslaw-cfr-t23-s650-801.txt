23 C.F.R. § 650.801 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:650.801
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-650.801
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 650 (Bridges, Structures, And Hydraulics). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this regulation is to establish policy and to set forth coordination procedures for Federal-aid highway bridges which require navigational clearances.
