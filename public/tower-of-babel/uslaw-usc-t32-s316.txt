32 U.S.C. § 316 — Detail of members of Army National Guard for rifle instruction of civilians
Citation: us:usc:32:316
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title32-section316
Context: United States Code, Title 32 (National Guard); Chapter 3 (Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may detail officers and noncommissioned officers of the Army National Guard to duty as instructors at rifle ranges for the training of civilians in the use of military arms.
