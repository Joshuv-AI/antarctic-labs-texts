40 C.F.R. § 46.160 — Evaluation of applications.
Citation: us:cfr:40:46.160
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-46.160
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 46 (Fellowships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

EPA will evaluate your application based on criteria identified in the request for applications or program announcement. Evaluation criteria may include:
(a) The relevance of your proposed studies to EPA's mission.
(b) Your potential for success, as reflected by your academic record, letters of reference, and any other available information.
(c) The availability of EPA funds.
