29 C.F.R. § 2590.701-5 — -5 Evidence of creditable coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:29:2590.701-5
Creator: Employee Benefits Security Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-2590.701-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 2590 (Rules And Regulations For Group Health Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. The rules for providing certificates of creditable coverage and demonstrating creditable coverage have been superseded by the prohibition on preexisting condition exclusions. See § 2590.715-2704 for rules prohibiting the imposition of a preexisting condition exclusion.
(b) Applicability. The provisions of this section apply beginning December 31, 2014.
