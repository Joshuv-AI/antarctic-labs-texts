32 C.F.R. § 34.53 — Disputes and appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:32:34.53
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-34.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 34 (Administrative Requirements For Grants And Agreements With For-Profit Organizations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Recipients have the right to appeal certain decisions by grants officers. In resolving such issues, DoD policy is to use Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) techniques, to the maximum practicable extent. See 32 CFR 22.815 for standards for DoD Components' dispute resolution and formal, administrative appeal procedures.
