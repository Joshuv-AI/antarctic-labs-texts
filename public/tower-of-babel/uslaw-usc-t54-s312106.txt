54 U.S.C. § 312106 — Consultation with National Park System Advisory Board
Citation: us:usc:54:312106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section312106
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3121 (National Trust For Historic Preservation In The United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out its functions under this chapter, the National Trust may consult with the National Park System Advisory Board on matters relating to the selection of sites, buildings, and objects to be preserved and protected pursuant to this chapter.
