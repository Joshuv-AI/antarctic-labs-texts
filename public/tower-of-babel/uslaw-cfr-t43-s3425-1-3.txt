43 C.F.R. § 3425.1-3 — -3 Qualifications of the applicant.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3425.1-3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3425.1-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3420 (Competitive Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any applicant for a lease shall meet the qualifications required of a lessee as specified in subpart 3472 of this title.
