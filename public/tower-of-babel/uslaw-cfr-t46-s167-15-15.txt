46 C.F.R. § 167.15-15 — -15 Application for inspection of a new nautical school ship or a conversion of a vessel to a nautical school ship.
Citation: us:cfr:46:167.15-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-167.15-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 167 (Public Nautical School Ships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prior to the commencement of the construction of a new nautical school ship, or a conversion of a vessel to a nautical school ship, application for the approval of contract plans and specifications and for a certificate of inspection shall be made in writing by the owner or agent to the Officer in Charge, Marine Inspection, at the nearest local Marine Inspection Office, U.S. Coast Guard.
