42 U.S.C. § 12682 — Actions under national service laws to be subject to availability of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:12682
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12682
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 129 (National And Community Service); Subchapter IV (Authorization Of Appropriations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No action involving the obligation or expenditure of funds may be taken under one of the national service laws (as defined in section 12511(15) 1 of this title) unless and until the Corporation for National and Community Service has sufficient appropriations available at the time such action is taken to satisfy the obligation to be incurred or make the expenditure to be made.
