49 C.F.R. § 393.91 — Buses, aisle seats prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:49:393.91
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-393.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 393 (Parts And Accessories Necessary For Safe Operation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No bus shall be equipped with aisle seats unless such seats are so designed and installed as to automatically fold and leave a clear aisle when they are unoccupied. No bus shall be operated if any seat therein is not securely fastened to the vehicle.
