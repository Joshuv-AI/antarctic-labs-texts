7 C.F.R. § 634.10 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:7:634.10
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-634.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 634 (Rural Clean Water Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

RCWP is applicable in project areas that meet the criteria for eligibility contained in § 634.12 and are authorized for funding by the Administrator, NRCS.
