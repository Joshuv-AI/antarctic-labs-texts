32 C.F.R. § 274.7 — Acceptance of bids.
Citation: us:cfr:32:274.7
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-274.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 274 (Regulations Governing Competitive Bidding On U.S. Government Guaranteed Military Export Loan Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Opening bids. Bids will be opened at the time and place specified in the public notice.
(b) Acceptance of successful bid. The Secretary of Defense or his designee will notify any successful bidder of acceptance in the manner and form specified in the public notice.
