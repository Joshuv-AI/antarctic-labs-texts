46 U.S.C. § 30530 — Liability as master, officer, or seaman not affected
Citation: us:usc:46:30530
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30530
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 305 (Exoneration And Limitation Of Liability); Subchapter II (Exoneration And Limitation Of Liability). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter does not affect the liability of an individual as a master, officer, or seaman, even though the individual is also an owner of the vessel.
