48 C.F.R. § 34.005-4 — 34.005-4 Demonstration contracts.
Citation: us:cfr:48:34.005-4
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-34.005-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 34 (Major System Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever practicable, contracts for the demonstration phase should provide for contractors to submit, by the end of the phase, priced proposals, totally funded by the Government, for full-scale development. The contracting officer should provide contractors with operational test conditions, performance criteria, life cycle cost factors, and any other selection criteria necessary for the contractors to prepare their proposals.
