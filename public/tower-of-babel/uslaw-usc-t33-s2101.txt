33 U.S.C. § 2101 — Congressional statement of findings and purpose
Citation: us:usc:33:2101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2101
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 35 (Artificial Reefs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Congress finds that—
(1)
although fishery products provide an important source of protein and industrial products for United States consumption, United States fishery production annually falls far short of satisfying United States demand;
(2)
overfishing and the degradation of vital fishery resource habitats have caused a reduction in the abundance and diversity of United States fishery resources;
(3)
escalated energy costs have had a negative effect on the economics of United States commercial and recreational fisheries;
(4)
commercial and recreational fisheries are a prominent factor in United States coastal economies and the direct and indirect returns to the United States economy from commercial and recreational fishing expenditures are threefold; and
(5)
properly designed, constructed, and located artificial reefs in waters covered under this chapter can enhance the habitat and diversity of fishery resources; enhance United States recreational and commercial fishing opportunities; increase the production of fishery products in the United States; increase the energy efficiency of recreational and commercial fisheries; and contribute to the United States and coastal economies.
(b)
The purpose of this chapter is to promote and facilitate responsible and effective efforts to establish artificial reefs in waters covered under this chapter.
