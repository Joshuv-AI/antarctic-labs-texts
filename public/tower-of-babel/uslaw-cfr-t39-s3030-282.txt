39 C.F.R. § 3030.282 — Increased pricing efficiency.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3030.282
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3030.282
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3030 (Regulation Of Rates For Market Dominant Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For a workshare discount that is equal to the cost avoided by the Postal Service for not providing the applicable service, no proposal to adjust a rate associated with that workshare discount may change the size of the discount.
(b) For a workshare discount that exceeds the cost avoided by the Postal Service for not providing the applicable service, no proposal to adjust a rate associated with that workshare discount may increase the size of the discount.
(c) For a workshare discount that is less than the cost avoided by the Postal Service for not providing the applicable service, no proposal to adjust a rate associated with that workshare discount may decrease the size of the discount.
(d) No proposal to adjust a rate associated with a workshare discount may increase the absolute value of the difference between the workshare discount and the cost avoided by the Postal Service for not providing the applicable service, unless it is set in accordance with a Commission order issued pursuant to § 3030.286.
