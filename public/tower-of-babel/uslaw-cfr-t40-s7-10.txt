40 C.F.R. § 7.10 — Purpose of this part.
Citation: us:cfr:40:7.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-7.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 7 (Nondiscrimination In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Assistance From The Environmental Protection Agency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements: Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, as amended; section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, as amended; the Age Discrimination Act of 1975, as amended; and section 13 of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act Amendments of 1972, Public Law 92-500, (collectively, the Acts).
