46 C.F.R. § 76.05-15 — -15 Fire main system.
Citation: us:cfr:46:76.05-15
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-76.05-15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 76 (Fire Protection Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Fire pumps, hydrants, hose, and nozzles shall be installed on the following vessels:
(1) On all self-propelled vessels.
(2) After July 1, 1957, on all barges with sleeping accommodations for more than six persons.
(b) The arrangement and details of the fire main system shall be as set forth in subpart 76.10.
