18 C.F.R. § 1316.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1316.1

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1316.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1316 (General Conditions And Certifications For Incorporation In Contract Documents Or Actions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets out the text of certain conditions and certifications which may be included by reference in certain TVA contract documents or actions. The provisions set out in this part are not automatically incorporated in all TVA actions.
