25 C.F.R. § 30.120 — How will the Secretary notify BIE-funded schools that they have been identified for school support and improvement activities?
Citation: us:cfr:25:30.120
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-30.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 30 (Standards, Assessments, And Accountability System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary will notify each BIE-funded school that has been identified for comprehensive support and improvement as described in § 30.111(h).
