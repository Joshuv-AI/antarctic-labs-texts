32 C.F.R. § 1627.3 — Classification of volunteers.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1627.3
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1627.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1627 (Volunteers For Induction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a registrant who is eligible to volunteer files an Application for Voluntary Induction, he shall be classified in Class 1-A and processed for induction.
