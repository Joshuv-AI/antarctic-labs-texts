30 C.F.R. § 1218.560 — How do I submit Form ONRR-4444?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1218.560
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1218.560
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1218 (Collection Of Royalties, Rentals, Bonuses, And Other Monies Due The Federal Government). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may obtain a copy of Form ONRR-4444 and instructions from ONRR. This form is posted at http://www.onrr.gov/FM/Forms/default.htm. Submit the completed, signed form to the address designated on Form ONRR-4444 instructions.
