13 C.F.R. § 121.409 — What size standard applies in an unrestricted procurement for Certificate of Competency purposes?
Citation: us:cfr:13:121.409
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-121.409
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 121 (Small Business Size Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of receiving a Certificate of Competency in an unrestricted procurement, the applicable size standard is that corresponding to the NAICS code set forth in the solicitation. The offeror need not be the manufacturer of any of the items acquired.
