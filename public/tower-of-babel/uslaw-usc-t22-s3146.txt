22 U.S.C. § 3146 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:22:3146
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3146
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 46A (Foreign Direct Investment And International Financial Data). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this Act—
(1)
the terms “foreign”, “direct investment”, “international investment”, “United States”, “business enterprise”, “foreign person”, and “United States person” have the meanings given those terms in section 3102 of this title; and
(2)
the term “foreign direct investment in the United States” means direct investment by foreign persons in any business enterprise that is a United States person.
