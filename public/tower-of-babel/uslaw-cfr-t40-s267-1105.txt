40 C.F.R. § 267.1105 — What do I do if my containment building contains areas both with and without secondary containment?
Citation: us:cfr:40:267.1105
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-267.1105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 267 (Standards For Owners And Operators Of Hazardous Waste Facilities Operating Under A Standardized Permit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For these containment buildings, you must:
(a) Design and operate each area in accordance with the requirements enumerated in §§ 267.1101 through 267.1103.
(b) Take measures to prevent the release of liquids or wet materials into areas without secondary containment.
(c) Maintain in the facility's operating log a written description of the operating procedures used to maintain the integrity of areas without secondary containment.
