18 C.F.R. § 1303.2 — Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1303.2
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1303.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1303 (Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tobacco product means cigarettes, cigars, little cigars, pipe tobacco, smokeless tobacco, snuff, and chewing tobacco.
