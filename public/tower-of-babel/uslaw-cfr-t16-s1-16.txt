16 C.F.R. § 1.16 — Petition for exemption from trade regulation rule.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1.16
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1 (General Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person to whom a rule would otherwise apply may petition the Commission for an exemption from such rule. Petitions for exemptions will be handled in the same manner and pursuant to the same procedures as prescribed in § 1.31 of this chapter.
