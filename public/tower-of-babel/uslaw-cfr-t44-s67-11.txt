44 C.F.R. § 67.11 — Notice of final determination.
Citation: us:cfr:44:67.11
Creator: Federal Emergency Management Agency, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-44/section-67.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 44 (Emergency Management and Assistance), Part 67 (Appeals From Proposed Flood Elevation Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Federal Insurance Administrator's notice of the final flood elevation determination for a community shall be in written form and published in the Federal Register, and copies shall be sent to the CEO, all individual appellants and the State Coordinating Agency.
