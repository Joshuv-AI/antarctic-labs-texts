15 U.S.C. § 77u — Hearings by Commission
Citation: us:usc:15:77u
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section77u
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2A (Securities And Trust Indentures); Subchapter I (Domestic Securities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All hearings shall be public and may be held before the Commission or an officer or officers of the Commission designated by it, and appropriate records thereof shall be kept.
