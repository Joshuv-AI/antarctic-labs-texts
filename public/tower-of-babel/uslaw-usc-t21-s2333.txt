21 U.S.C. § 2333 — Regulatory authority
Citation: us:usc:21:2333
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title21-section2333
Context: United States Code, Title 21 (Food And Drugs); Chapter 28 (Sanctions With Respect To Foreign Traffickers Of Illicit Synthetic Opioids); Subchapter II (Other Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 90 days after December 20, 2019, the President shall issue such regulations as are necessary to carry out this chapter, including guidance with respect to what activities are included under the definition of “opioid trafficking” under section 2302(8) of this title.
