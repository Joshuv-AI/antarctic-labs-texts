42 C.F.R. § 414.500 — Basis and scope.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 414 (Payment For Part B Medical And Other Health Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements provisions of 1833(h)(8) of the Act and 1834A of the Act—procedures for determining the basis for, and amount of, payment for a clinical diagnostic laboratory test (CDLT).
