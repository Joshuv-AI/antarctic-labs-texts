48 C.F.R. § 237.7000 — 237.7000 Scope.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-237.7000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 237 (Service Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart—
(a) Applies to contracts for mortuary services (the care of remains) for military personnel within the United States; and
(b) May be used as guidance in areas outside the United States for mortuary services for deceased military and civilian personnel.
