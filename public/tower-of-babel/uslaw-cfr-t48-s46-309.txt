48 C.F.R. § 46.309 — 46.309 Research and development contracts (short form).
Citation: us:cfr:48:46.309
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-46.309
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 46 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 52.246-9, Inspection of Research and Development (Short Form), in solicitations and contracts for research and development when the clause prescribed in 46.307 or the clause prescribed in 46.308 is not used.
