40 C.F.R. § 35.655 — Basis for allotment.
Citation: us:cfr:40:35.655
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.655
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator allots pesticide program implementation cooperative agreement funds to each Regional Office. Regional Offices award funds to Tribes and Intertribal Consortia based on their programmatic needs and applicable EPA guidance.
