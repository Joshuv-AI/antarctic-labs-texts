34 C.F.R. § 75.218 — Applications not evaluated or selected for funding.
Citation: us:cfr:34:75.218
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-75.218
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 75 (Direct Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary informs an applicant if its application—
(1) Is not evaluated; or
(2) Is not selected for funding.
(b) If an applicant requests an explanation of the reason its application was not evaluated or selected, the Secretary provides that explanation.
