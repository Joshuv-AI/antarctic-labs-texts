Correction: White matter microstructure alterations in early psychosis and schizophrenia
Authors: Tommaso Pavan, Yasser Alemán-Gómez, Raoul Jenni, Pascal Steullet, Zoé Schilliger, Daniella Dwir, Martine Cleusix, Luis Alameda, Kim Q. Do, Philippe Conus, Patric Hagmann, Paul Klauser, Ileana Jelescu
Published: 2025

Correction to: Translational Psychiatry 10.1038/s41398-025-03397-1, published online 23 May 2025

In the originally published version of this article, the title of Figure 4 was inadvertently left as “Slightly modified to accommodate the request,” which was a placeholder comment inserted during the final proofing stage. This was not the intended figure title and does not reflect the scientific content of the figure.

The correct title of Figure 4 should read:

“Average effect sizes across regions of interest showing significant changes in the EP and SZ groups.”

This correction does not affect the scientific integrity or conclusions of the article. The original article has been corrected.
