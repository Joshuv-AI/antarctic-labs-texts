22 U.S.C. § 2805 — Withdrawal of Federal recognition or participation
Citation: us:usc:22:2805
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2805
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 40 (International Expositions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may withdraw Federal recognition or participation whenever he finds that continuing recognition or participation would be inconsistent with the national interest and with the purposes of this chapter.
