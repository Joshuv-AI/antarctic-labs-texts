DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


National Cancer Institute; Amended Notice of Meeting

    Notice is hereby given of a change in the meeting of the National 
Cancer Advisory Board (NCAB), December 01, 2026, 05:00 p.m. to December 
02, 2026, 04:00 p.m., National Cancer Institute--Shady Grove, 9609 
Medical Center Drive, Rockville, MD 20850 which was published in the 
Federal Register on June 11, 2026, FR Doc. No. 2026-11662, 91 FR 35490.
    The meeting notice is amended to reflect changes to the meeting 
date, time, and format. The meeting will now be held virtually on 
February 11, 2027, as follows: the open session will be held from 9:30 
a.m. to 11:40 a.m. for the NCAB Subcommittee Meetings and from 12:00 
p.m. to 4:00 p.m. for the Director's and Program reports and 
presentations and Board business. The closed session will be held from 
4:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. for grant application review. The meeting will 
be partially closed to the public.

    Dated: October 14, 2026.
Kuleni D. Onnen,
Program Analyst, Office of Federal Advisory Committee Policy.
