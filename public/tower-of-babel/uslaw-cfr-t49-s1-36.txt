49 C.F.R. § 1.36 — Delegations to the Assistant Secretary for Governmental Affairs.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1.36
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1 (Organization And Delegation Of Powers And Duties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Assistant Secretary for Governmental Affairs is delegated authority to:
(a) Establish procedures for responding to Congressional correspondence; and
(b) Supervise the Deputy Assistant Secretary for Tribal Government Affairs.
