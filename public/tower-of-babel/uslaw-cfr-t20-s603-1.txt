20 C.F.R. § 603.1 — What are the purpose and scope of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:20:603.1
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-603.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 603 (Federal-State Unemployment Compensation (Uc) Program; Confidentiality And Disclosure Of State Uc Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to implement the requirements of Federal UC law concerning confidentiality and disclosure of UC information. This part applies to States and State UC agencies, as defined in § 603.2(f) and (g).
