40 C.F.R. § 68.200 — Recordkeeping.
Citation: us:cfr:40:68.200
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-68.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 68 (Chemical Accident Prevention Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The owner or operator shall maintain records supporting the implementation of this part at the stationary source for five years, unless otherwise provided in subpart D of this part.
