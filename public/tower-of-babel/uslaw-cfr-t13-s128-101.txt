13 C.F.R. § 128.101 — What type of assistance is available under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:13:128.101
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-128.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 128 (Veteran Small Business Certification Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting officers are authorized to restrict competition or award sole source contracts or orders to eligible SDVOSBs. In addition, 48 CFR chapter 8 authorizes VA contracting officers to restrict competition or award sole source contracts or orders to eligible VOSBs and SDVOSBs.
