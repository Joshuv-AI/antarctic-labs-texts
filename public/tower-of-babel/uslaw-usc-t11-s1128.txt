11 U.S.C. § 1128 — Confirmation hearing
Citation: us:usc:11:1128
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1128
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 11 (Reorganization); Subchapter II (The Plan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
After notice, the court shall hold a hearing on confirmation of a plan.
(b)
A party in interest may object to confirmation of a plan.
