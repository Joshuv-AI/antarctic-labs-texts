Method to measure quarkonium wave function using B c B_{c} decay
Yukinari Sumino

Abstract
We propose a method that enables a direct experimental probe of the quarkonium wave function defined in potential nonrelativistic QCD (pNRQCD) using the three-body decay of the B c B_{c} meson. We show that the momentum distribution of the spectator c c -quark in the partonic decay is proportional to the absolute square of the momentum-space wave function of the B c B_{c} state. It would provide, for the first time, an experimentally accessible probe of the quarkonium wave function.

A quantitative assessment of the experimental momentum resolution is left for future work, but typical resolutions for charmed hadrons at LHCb (of order

Although our estimates of the corrections to the LO predictions are very naive and crude,
once experimental data are available, comparison with the theoretical predictions would certainly
deepen our understanding and motivate us for more detailed and accurate analyses.
