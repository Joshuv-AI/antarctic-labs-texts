46 C.F.R. § 64.91 — Relief valve for the cargo pump discharge.
Citation: us:cfr:46:64.91
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-64.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 64 (Marine Portable Tanks And Cargo Handling Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The cargo pump discharge must have a relief valve that is—
(a) Fitted between the cargo pump discharge and the shut-off valve, with the relief valve discharge piped back to the cargo pump suction or returned to the tank; and
(b) Set at the maximum design pressure of the piping and discharge hose, or less.
