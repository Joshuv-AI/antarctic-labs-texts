45 C.F.R. § 96.47 — Primary care.
Citation: us:cfr:45:96.47
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-96.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 96 (Block Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applications for direct funding of Indian tribes and tribal organizations under the primary care block grant must comply with 42 CFR part 51c (Grants for Community Health Services).
