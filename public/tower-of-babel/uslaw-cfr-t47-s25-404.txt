47 C.F.R. § 25.404 — Submission of down payment and filing of long-form applications.
Citation: us:cfr:47:25.404
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-25.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 25 (Satellite Communications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A high bidder that meets its down payment obligations in a timely manner must, within thirty (30) business days after being notified that it is a high bidder, submit an amendment to its pending application to provide the information required by § 25.144.
