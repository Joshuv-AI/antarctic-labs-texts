34 U.S.C. § 12111 — Support of Federal prisoners in non-Federal institutions
Citation: us:usc:34:12111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section12111
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter I (Prisons); Part A (Violent Offender Incarceration And Truth-In-Sentencing Incentive Grants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Attorney General may make payments to States and units of local government for the purposes authorized in section 4013 of title 18.
(b) Authorization of appropriations
Notwithstanding any other provision of this part other than section 12108(a)(2) of this title, there are authorized to be appropriated from amounts authorized under section 12108 of this title for each of fiscal years 1996 through 2000 such sums as may be necessary to carry out this section.
