Polymer Science & Technology: Viewpoint Development Prospects of Polypropylene Industry
Authors: Zhong Wei, Yue Ru, Chao Jiang, Guicun Qi, Jinliang Qiao
Published: 2026

In the past 50 years, polymer science and technology have not made significant breakthroughs, resulting in a continuous decrease in the threshold for the construction of new production facilities. Currently, most products are showing a serious overcapacity. Polyolefins, which account for over 60% of synthetic resin consumption, are quite representative of overcapacity. The major products of polyolefins are polyethylene (PE) and polypropylene (PP) with an annual consumption of about 250 million tons. The cost of PE produced from different sources of ethylene varies greatly. Compared with PE produced with coal from chemical industry and ethylene plants, PE produced with ethane from oilfield-associated gas or shale gas has absolute cost advantages and strong market competitiveness. However, PP is completely different. The costs of PP produced from different sources such as ethylene plants, oil refining, propane dehydrogenation (PDH), or coal to chemical industry (CTO/MTO) are almost the same, leading to more intense competition. PP, which has no cost disadvantage in China, has even poorer economic benefits than PE, which has a significant cost disadvantage in China.

At present, with the lowest density among general synthetic resins, PP is the second largest synthetic resin in the world. PP is a truly green and environmentally friendly material because it can be produced and processed at low temperature and recycled easily. It can save energy and reduce emissions when replacing other materials. The global annual production of PP has exceeded 90 million tons. China, as a net exporter, is the largest producer and consumer country, with a production of over 38 million tons. The major challenge the PP industry faces is the rapid decrease in operating rate of production facilities due to overcapacity. The global average operating rate of facilities was about 77.8% in 2023, while in China it was about 74% in 2024. It is estimated that the global PP production capacity will continue to grow in the future and may exceed 130 million tons per year by 2030. Most production enterprises will face losses due to the very low operating rate of production facilities. Without significant technological breakthroughs, the development prospects of the PP industry are not optimistic. Based on the basic research foundation and industry status, the authors believe that there is a high possibility of technological breakthroughs in the following two areas, which may help the PP industry overcome difficulties and usher in new glory.

(1) Exploring new areas of PP application is the most important research direction to overcome its oversupply. The following products are particularly noteworthy:(1) PP for high-voltage cable. With the development of intermittent energy sources such as solar and wind power, the construction of long-distance high-voltage transmission networks will inevitably have rapid growth, which will bring opportunities for high-voltage cable materials. Traditional high-voltage cable materials are cross-linked low-density PE materials, which have high costs and are difficult to recycle after disposal. At present, non-cross-linked high-voltage PP cable materials have already reached the voltage withstand level of traditional cross-linked low-density PE materials and have green attributes such as simple processing and easy recycling. As long as the technology is further improved, the cost is further reduced, and promotion is strengthened, more PP for high-voltage cable will be applied.(2) Die drawing processing technology. The use of die drawing processing technology to prepare PP wood, luggage, and bulletproof materials is expected to significantly increase the consumption of PP, which may shift PP from oversupply to supply shortage. For example, PP wood prepared using PP resin and inorganic fillers as raw materials, retaining all the excellent properties of natural wood, can be processed using traditional wood processing equipment and overcome the shortcomings of natural wood. Not only does it increase strength, but it can also be flame-retardant, insect-resistant, and water-resistant and does not require coating protection. At present, the product is exclusively produced by an American company (Evolves) with a production capacity of less than 30,000 tons per year. The world’s timber consumption is about 2 billion m3, while China’s is about 600 million m3 and heavily relies on imports. Calculated from a density of 0.8 g/cm3, global wood consumption is 1.6 billion tons, with China accounting for 480 million tons, far exceeding the global plastic consumption of 300 million tons and the global PP consumption of 90 million tons. PP sheets produced using the same principle have also been successfully applied in luggage (Samsonite, TUMI, and other brands), bulletproof materials, and other fields. If technological breakthroughs can lead to cost reduction, the situation of supply shortage of PP resin will not be far away.(3) Functional PP prepared using reactor granular technology. Functional PP prepared using reactor granular technology (RGT) includes high-molecular-weight polar PP, high-heat-resistance PP containing reversible cross-linking bonds, and high-performance PP grafted with acrylic acid/salt, acrylamide, or ionic liquids. High-performance PP prepared using RGT technology has been commercially applied, such as Catalloy and Hivalloy produced by Lyondell Basell Industries. The preparation of high-molecular-weight polar PP by solid-phase grafting has been proven to be feasible. The preparation of high-performance PP grafted with acrylic acid/salt, acrylamide, or ionic liquid through solid-phase grafting as well as high-heat-resistance PP containing reversible cross-linking bonds using RGT has not been reported in the literature and deserves in-depth study. Once a breakthrough is achieved, it will significantly increase the consumption of PP.

Expanding the application of propylene to reduce its oversupply is also an important direction for reducing the oversupply of PP. The following research directions are particularly noteworthy:(1) Preparation of 4-methyl-1-pentene through propylene dimerization. The preparation of 4-methyl-1-pentene through propylene dimerization is an important research direction for expanding the application field of propylene. As the only crystalline highly transparent resin, poly­(4-methyl-1-pentene) (PMP) is an engineering plastic with excellent performance. PMP, with a density of 0.833–0.835 g/cm3, has the lowest density among thermoplastic resins. It has been applied in optical instruments and medical and electronic fields such as ECMO membrane lungs. 4-Methyl-1-pentene is also an ideal comonomer for gas-phase PE. If a breakthrough is made in the dimerization of propylene to significantly reduce the cost of 4-methyl-1-pentene, it will greatly increase the consumption of propylene in this area.(2) Preparation of traditional polyolefin copolymers. The preparation of traditional polyolefin copolymers such as ethylene–propylene rubber from only propylene is also a feasible way to expand the application field of propylene. At present, scientists have made significant progress in catalysts for chain-walking olefin polymerization, making it possible to prepare ethylene–propylene rubber using only propylene.(3) Development of new propylene and ethylene copolymers. The development of both new propylene copolymers and ethylene copolymers is also expected to significantly increase propylene consumption and increase added value due to the mutual substitution of PP and PE in some application fields. The self-stabilizing precipitation polymerization method invented by Chinese scientists can prepare olefin–maleic anhydride alternating copolymers at low cost, which is expected to form a market exceeding 10 million tons in wood adhesives, coatings, insulation materials, nylon chain extenders, and other aspects. Both propylene–maleic anhydride alternating copolymer and ethylene–maleic anhydride alternating copolymer have competitiveness in raw material costs compared with other olefin–maleic anhydride alternating copolymers.
