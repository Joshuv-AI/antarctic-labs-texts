21 C.F.R. § 862.1020 — Acid phosphatase (total or prostatic) test system.
Citation: us:cfr:21:862.1020
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-862.1020
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 862 (Clinical Chemistry And Clinical Toxicology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. An acid phosphatase (total or prostatic) test system is a device intended to measure the activity of the acid phosphatase enzyme in plasma and serum.
(b) Classification. Class II (special controls). The device is exempt from the premarket notification procedures in subpart E of part 807 of this chapter subject to the limitations in § 862.9.
