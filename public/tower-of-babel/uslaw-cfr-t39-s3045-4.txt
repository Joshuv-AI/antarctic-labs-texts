39 C.F.R. § 3045.4 — Review.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3045.4
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3045.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3045 (Rules For Market Tests Of Experimental Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Commission will establish a docket for each market test initiated under this part and post the filing on its website at http://www.prc.gov. The notice shall:
(1) Describe the general nature of the proceeding;
(2) Refer to the legal authority under which the proceeding is to be conducted;
(3) Identify an officer of the Commission to represent the interests of the general public in the docket;
(4) Specify a period for public comment; and
(5) Include such other information as the Commission deems appropriate.
(b) The notice, or an abstract thereof, shall be published in the Federal Register.
