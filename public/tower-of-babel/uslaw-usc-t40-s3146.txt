40 U.S.C. § 3146 — Effect on other federal laws
Citation: us:usc:40:3146
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section3146
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part A (General); Chapter 31 (General); Subchapter IV (Wage Rate Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter does not supersede or impair any authority otherwise granted by federal law to provide for the establishment of specific wage rates.
