Equating Inv-Quinv formulas for the q q -Whittaker and modified Hall-Littlewood functions
Aritra Bhattacharya

Abstract
. We explain the equality between the two sets of formulas for q q -Whittaker functions and modified Hall-Littlewood functions obtained by Haglund, Haiman and Loehr - the Inv formula and Ayyer, Mandelshtam and Martin - the Quinv formula by use of weighted path symmetric functions introduced by Carlsson and Mellit.
