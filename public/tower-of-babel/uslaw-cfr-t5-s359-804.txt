5 C.F.R. § 359.804 — Length of furlough.
Citation: us:cfr:5:359.804
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-359.804
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 359 (Removal From The Senior Executive Service; Guaranteed Placement In Other Personnel Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A furlough may not extend more than one year. It may be made only when the agency intends to recall the appointee within one year.
