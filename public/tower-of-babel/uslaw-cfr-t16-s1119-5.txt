16 C.F.R. § 1119.5 — Enforcement notification.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1119.5
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1119.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1119 (Civil Penalty Factors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person will be informed in writing if it is believed that the person has violated the law and if the Commission intends to seek a civil penalty. Any person who receives such a writing will have an opportunity to submit evidence and arguments that it should not pay a penalty or should not pay a penalty in the amount sought by the Commission.
