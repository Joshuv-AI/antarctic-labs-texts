42 C.F.R. § 401.603 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:401.603
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-401.603
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 401 (General Administrative Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this subpart—
Claim means any debt owed to CMS.
Debtor means any individual, partnership, corporation, estate, trust or other legal entity against which CMS has a claim.
Extended repayment schedule means installment payments to pay back a debt.
