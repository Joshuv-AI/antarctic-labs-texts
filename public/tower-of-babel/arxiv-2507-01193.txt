Ryotaro Minato, Akira Sato, Ryosuke Suda, Masato Yamanaka

Abstract
We propose a new channel for probing charged lepton flavor violation (CLFV): the decay of true muonium into a lepton pair of different flavor, ( μ + ​ μ − ) → μ ± ​ e ∓ (\mu^{+}\mu^{-})\to\mu^{\pm}e^{\mp} . This purely leptonic two-body decay provides a clean experimental signature in the form of energetic, oppositely charged leptons. It is sensitive not only to photonic dipole interactions but also to four-fermion contact interactions, and is free from hadronic uncertainties in theoretical predictions. We evaluate the branching ratios induced by scalar-, vector-, and dipole-type CLFV operators. Our results show that the branching ratio can reach up to 𝒪 ⁡ ( 10 − 20 ) \mathcal{O}(10^{-20}) within current experimental bounds. Such a decay may be observable at future high-luminosity muon beamlines or at dedicated e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} or μ + ​ μ − \mu^{+}\mu^{-} colliders.

II
Interaction, Reaction rate

To evaluate the branching ratio of the CLFV decay
(\mu^{+}\mu^{-})\to\mu^{\pm}e^{\mp}
, we work within the framework of effective field theory.
The effective Lagrangian governing the CLFV decay of TM is given by

\begin{split}\mathcal{L}&=-\frac{1}{\Lambda^{2}}\Bigl[g_{LL}^{S}(\overline{{\psi_{e}}}P_{L}\psi_{\mu})(\overline{\psi_{\mu}}P_{L}\psi_{\mu})+g_{RR}^{S}(\overline{\psi_{e}}P_{R}\psi_{\mu})(\overline{\psi_{\mu}}P_{R}\psi_{\mu})\\
&~~+g_{LL}^{V}(\overline{\psi_{e}}\gamma^{\mu}P_{L}\psi_{\mu})(\overline{\psi_{\mu}}\gamma_{\mu}P_{L}\psi_{\mu})+g_{RR}^{V}(\overline{\psi_{e}}\gamma^{\mu}P_{R}\psi_{\mu})(\overline{\psi_{\mu}}\gamma_{\mu}P_{R}\psi_{\mu})\\
&~~+g_{LR}^{V}(\overline{\psi_{e}}\gamma^{\mu}P_{L}\psi_{\mu})(\overline{\psi_{\mu}}\gamma_{\mu}P_{R}\psi_{\mu})+g_{RL}^{V}(\overline{\psi_{e}}\gamma^{\mu}P_{R}\psi_{\mu})(\overline{\psi_{\mu}}\gamma_{\mu}P_{L}\psi_{\mu})\\
&~~+vA_{L}\overline{\psi_{e}}\sigma^{\mu\nu}P_{L}\psi_{\mu}F_{\mu\nu}+vA_{R}\overline{\psi_{e}}\sigma^{\mu\nu}P_{R}\psi_{\mu}F_{\mu\nu}\\
&~~+\text{H.c.}\Bigr],\end{split}

(2)

where
\sigma^{\mu\nu}=(i/2)[\gamma^{\mu},\gamma^{\nu}]

,

P_{R}=(1+\gamma_{5})/2

v
is the vacuum expectation value of Higgs field, and

\Lambda

The branching ratio of TM CLFV decay is given by

\begin{split}\text{BR}\left((\mu^{+}\mu^{-})\to\mu^{\pm}e^{\mp}\right)=\tau_{\text{TM}}\cdot\Gamma\left(\left(\mu^{+}\mu^{-}\right)\to\mu^{\pm}e^{\mp}\right),\end{split}

(3)

where
\tau_{\text{TM}}
represents the lifetime of TM. Both channels

(\mu^{+}\mu^{-})\to\mu^{+}e^{-}

(\mu^{+}\mu^{-})\to\mu^{-}e^{+}

In this work, taking unpolarized muons for the 1S initial state, we assume the ratio
of singlet to triplet is 1:3.
The singlet decays via
2\gamma

\tau_{\rm TM}(2\gamma)=0.602~{\rm ps}

e^{+}e^{-}

\tau_{\rm TM}(e^{+}e^{-})=1.81~{\rm ps}

Weighting by 1:3 yields
\tau_{\text{TM}}=1.51\,\text{ps}=2.29\times 10^{12}\,\text{GeV}^{-1}

The decay rate is expressed as

\begin{split}\Gamma\left(\left(\mu^{+}\mu^{-}\right)\to\mu^{\pm}e^{\mp}\right)=\sigma v_{\text{rel}}|\psi_{\mu}|^{2},\end{split}

\mu^{+}\mu^{-}\to\mu^{\pm}e^{\mp}

We consider the CLFV decay of TM in vacuum, in the absence of both electric and magnetic fields.
Under this environment, the spatial size of TM is approximately the same as
that of muon, and is estimated as
\mathcal{O}\left(\bigl(\alpha\mu_{\text{TM}}\bigr)^{-1}\right)

Here

\mu_{\text{TM}}=m_{\mu}/2

Neglecting the spatial momenta of the initial muons, their 1S wave functions are given by

\begin{split}\psi_{\mu}^{1S}(r)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}}(\alpha\mu_{\text{TM}})^{3/2}e^{-\alpha\mu_{\text{TM}}r},\end{split}

(5)

r

Finally, we comment briefly on the experimental signature and background rejection
for stopped TM decays. This two-body decay at rest produces a clean signal:

|\bm{p}_{\mu}|=|\bm{p}_{e}|\simeq 3m_{\mu}/4

The high precision in tracking, timing, and angular resolution of charged leptons
enables efficient signal identification using appropriate kinematic cuts.

IV
Summary

In summary, we have proposed a new process for testing CLFV and its underlying
physics: the decay of true muonium (TM),
(\mu^{+}\mu^{-})\to\mu^{\pm}e^{\mp}
.
This purely leptonic, two-body decay offers a distinct experimental signature:
energetic, back-to-back charged leptons with a momentum of

|\bm{p_{\mu(e)}}|\simeq 3m_{\mu}/4
.
Theoretically, it is also free from hadronic uncertainties.
Using the effective field theory approach, we evaluated the branching ratio of

(\mu^{+}\mu^{-})\to\mu^{\pm}e^{\mp}
under the single-operator dominance scenario
for scalar-, vector-, and dipole-type CLFV operators. Our analysis includes
interference effects between multiple operators, which could increase the branching
ratio. We found that the branching ratio could reach
\mathcal{O}(10^{-20})

within the current experimental bounds on CLFV couplings, as constrained by the
MEG II experiment, for example.
Such a branching ratio may be probed at future high-luminosity muon beamlines.
We emphasize the potential complementarity of this
process with ongoing searches for new physics in rare muon decays and highlight
its utility in probing flavor structure in the lepton sector.
A detailed study is necessary to establish this new process as a probe of flavor
structure. This study should include a comprehensive formulation of the TM dynamics,
experimental backgrounds, and complementarity with other CLFV processes.
We will address these issues in our future work.
