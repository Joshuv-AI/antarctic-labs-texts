14 C.F.R. § 103.15 — Operations over congested areas.
Citation: us:cfr:14:103.15
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-103.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 103 (Ultralight Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may operate an ultralight vehicle over any congested area of a city, town, or settlement, or over any open air assembly of persons.
