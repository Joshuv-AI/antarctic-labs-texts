10 U.S.C. § 7067 — Army Medical Department
Citation: us:usc:10:7067
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7067
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Organization); Chapter 707 (The Army). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is an Army Medical Department in the Army. The Army Medical Department consists of—
(1)
the Surgeon General;
(2)
the Assistant Surgeons General;
(3)
the Medical Corps;
(4)
the Dental Corps;
(5)
the Veterinary Corps;
(6)
the Medical Service Corps;
(7)
the Army Nurse Corps; and
(8)
the Army Medical Specialist Corps.
