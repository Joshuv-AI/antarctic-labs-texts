48 C.F.R. § 22.902 — 22.902 Handling complaints.
Citation: us:cfr:48:22.902
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-22.902
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 22 (Application Of Labor Laws To Government Acquisitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies shall bring complaints regarding a contractor's compliance with this policy to that contractor's attention (in writing, if appropriate), stating the policy, indicating that the contractor's compliance has been questioned, and requesting that the contractor take any appropriate steps that may be necessary to comply.
