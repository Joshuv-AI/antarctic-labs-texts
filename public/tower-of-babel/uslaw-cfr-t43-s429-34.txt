43 C.F.R. § 429.34 — Who is the decisionmaker for Reclamation's final determinations?
Citation: us:cfr:43:429.34
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-429.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 429 (Use Of Bureau Of Reclamation Land, Facilities, And Waterbodies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The appropriate Regional Director, or the Regional Director's designee, makes any final determination associated with an action taken under this rule and will send that final determination in writing to you by mail.
(b) The Regional Director's final determination will take effect upon the date of the final determination letter.
