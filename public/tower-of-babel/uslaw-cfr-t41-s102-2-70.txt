41 C.F.R. § 102-2.70 — -2.70 What are individual and class deviations?
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-2.70
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-2.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-2 (2—Federal Management Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual deviation is intended to affect only one action. A class deviation is intended to affect more than one action (e.g., multiple actions, the actions of more than one agency, or individual agency actions that are expected to recur).
