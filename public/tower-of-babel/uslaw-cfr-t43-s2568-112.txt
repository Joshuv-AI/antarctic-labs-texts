43 C.F.R. § 2568.112 — What do I do if BLM notifies me that I am eligible to choose an alternative allotment?
Citation: us:cfr:43:2568.112
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2568.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2560 (Alaska Occupancy And Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must file a request for an alternative allotment in the Alaska State Office as stated in section 2568.71 and follow all the requirements you did for your original allotment application.
