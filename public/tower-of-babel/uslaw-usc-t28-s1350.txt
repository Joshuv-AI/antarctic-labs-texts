28 U.S.C. § 1350 — Alien’s action for tort
Citation: us:usc:28:1350
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1350
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part IV (Jurisdiction And Venue); Chapter 85 (District Courts; Jurisdiction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The district courts shall have original jurisdiction of any civil action by an alien for a tort only, committed in violation of the law of nations or a treaty of the United States.
