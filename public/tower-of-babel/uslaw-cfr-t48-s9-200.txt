48 C.F.R. § 9.200 — 9.200 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:9.200
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-9.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 9 (Contractor Qualifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart implements 10 U.S.C. 3243 and 41 U.S.C. 3311 and prescribes policies and procedures regarding qualification requirements and the acquisitions that are subject to such requirements.
