42 U.S.C. § 1862u — NSF support of research on opioid addiction
Citation: us:usc:42:1862u
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1862u
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 16 (National Science Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the National Science Foundation, in consultation with the Director of the National Institutes of Health, shall support merit-reviewed and competitively awarded research on the science of opioid addiction.
