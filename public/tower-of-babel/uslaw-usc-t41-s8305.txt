41 U.S.C. § 8305 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:41:8305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section8305
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 83 (Buy American). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 60 days after the end of each fiscal year, the Secretary of Defense shall submit to Congress a report on the amount of purchases by the Department of Defense from foreign entities in that fiscal year. The report shall separately indicate the dollar value of items for which this chapter was waived pursuant to—
(1)
a reciprocal defense procurement memorandum of understanding described in section 8304(a) of this title;
(2)
the Trade Agreements Act of 1979 (19 U.S.C. 2501 et seq.); or
(3)
an international agreement to which the United States is a party.
