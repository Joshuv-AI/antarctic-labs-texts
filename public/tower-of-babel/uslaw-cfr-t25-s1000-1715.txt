25 C.F.R. § 1000.1715 — What is “imminent jeopardy” to a trust asset?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1000.1715
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary, Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1000.1715
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1000 (Annual Funding Agreements Under The Tribal Self-Government Act Amendments To The Indian Self-Determination And Education Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Imminent jeopardy means an immediate threat and likelihood of significant devaluation, degradation, damage, or loss of a trust asset, or the intended benefit from the asset caused by the actions or inactions of a Tribe/Consortium in performing trust functions. This includes disregarding Federal trust standards and/or Federal law while performing trust functions if the disregard creates such an immediate threat.
