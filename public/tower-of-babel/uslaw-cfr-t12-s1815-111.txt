12 C.F.R. § 1815.111 — Actions that require an environmental assessment.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1815.111
Creator: Community Development Financial Institutions Fund, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1815.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1815 (Environmental Quality). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a Project or action is not one that normally requires an EIS and does not qualify for categorical exclusion, the Fund shall prepare, or have prepared, an environmental assessment.
