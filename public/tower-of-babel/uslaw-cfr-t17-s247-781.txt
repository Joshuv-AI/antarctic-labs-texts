17 C.F.R. § 247.781 — Exemption from the definition of “broker” for banks for a limited period of time.
Citation: us:cfr:17:247.781
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-247.781
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 247 (Regulation R—Exemptions And Definitions Related To The Exceptions For Banks From The Definition Of Broker). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A bank is exempt from the definition of the term “broker” under section 3(a)(4) of the Act (15 U.S.C. 78c(a)(4)) until the first day of its first fiscal year commencing after September 30, 2008.
