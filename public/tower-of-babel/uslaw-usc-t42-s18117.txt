42 U.S.C. § 18117 — Oversight
Citation: us:usc:42:18117
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section18117
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 157 (Quality, Affordable Health Care For All Americans); Subchapter VI (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Inspector General of the Department of Health and Human Services shall have oversight authority with respect to the administration and implementation of this title 1 as it relates to such Department.
