22 C.F.R. § 140.11 — Minimum enforcement procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:22:140.11
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-140.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 140 (Prohibition On Assistance To Drug Traffickers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 140.6 through 140.10 represent the minimum procedures that each agency providing assistance must apply in order to implement FAA Section 487. Under individual circumstances, however, additional measures may be appropriate. In those cases, agencies providing assistance are encouraged to take additional steps, as necessary, to ensure that the statutory restrictions are enforced.
