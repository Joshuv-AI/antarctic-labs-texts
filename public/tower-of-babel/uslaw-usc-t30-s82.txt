30 U.S.C. § 82 — New or supplemental patents, in case of lands subsequently classified as noncoal
Citation: us:usc:30:82
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section82
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 3 (Lands Containing Coal, Oil, Gas, Salts, Asphaltic Materials, Sodium, Sulphur, And Building Stone); Subchapter II (Coal Land Entries Under Nonmineral Land Laws With Reservation Of Coal To United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior is authorized and directed in cases where patents for public lands have been issued to entrymen under the provisions of sections 81 and 83 to 85 of this title, reserving to the United States all coal deposits therein, and lands so patented are subsequently classified as noncoal in character, to issue new or supplemental patents without such reservation.
