22 C.F.R. § 143.22 — Notice to subrecipients.
Citation: us:cfr:22:143.22
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-143.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 143 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where a recipient passes on Federal financial assistance from an agency to subrecipients, the recipient shall provide the subrecipients written notice to their obligations under these regulations.
