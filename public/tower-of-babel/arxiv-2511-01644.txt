\displaystyle=D_{T_{7}}F_{i}D_{T_{7}}h,1\leq i\leq 6.

From (
2.3
), we deduce that
D_{T_{7}}F_{i}=(T_{i}D_{T_{7}}-D_{T^{*}_{7}}\tilde{F}_{7-i}T_{7})|_{\mathcal{D}_{T_{7}}}
for
1\leq i\leq 6.

(2)

For
h_{1},h_{2}\in\mathcal{H}
, we have

\displaystyle\langle(T_{7}F_{i}-\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}T_{7})D_{T_{7}}h_{1},D_{T^{*}_{7}}h_{2}\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle D_{T^{*}_{7}}T_{7}F_{i}D_{T_{7}}h_{1},h_{2}\rangle-\langle D_{T^{*}_{7}}\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}T_{7}D_{T_{7}}h_{1},h_{2}\rangle

(2.4)

\displaystyle=\langle T_{7}(D_{T_{7}}F_{i}D_{T_{7}})h_{1},h_{2}\rangle-\langle(D_{T^{*}_{7}}\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}D_{T^{*}_{7}})T_{7}h_{1},h_{2}\rangle

\displaystyle=\langle T_{7}(T_{i}-T^{*}_{7-i}T_{7})h_{1},h_{2}\rangle-\langle(T^{*}_{i}-T_{7-i}T^{*}_{7})^{*}T_{7}h_{1},h_{2}\rangle

\displaystyle=0,1\leq i\leq 6.

Therefore, it follows from (
2.4
) that
T_{7}F_{i}=\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}T_{7}|_{\mathcal{D}_{T_{7}}}
for
1\leqslant i\leqslant 6
.

(3)

For
h\in\mathcal{H}
, we observe that

\displaystyle(F^{*}_{i}D_{T_{7}}D_{T^{*}_{7}}-F_{7-i}T^{*}_{7})D_{T^{*}_{7}}h

\displaystyle=F^{*}_{i}D_{T_{7}}D^{2}_{T^{*}_{7}}h-F_{7-i}T^{*}_{7}D_{T^{*}_{7}}h

(2.5)

\displaystyle=F^{*}_{i}D_{T_{7}}(I-T_{7}T^{*}_{7})h-F_{7-i}D_{T_{7}}T^{*}_{7}h

\displaystyle=F^{*}_{i}D_{T_{7}}h-(F^{*}_{i}D_{T_{7}}T_{7}+F_{7-i}D_{T_{7}})T^{*}_{7}h

\displaystyle=F^{*}_{i}D_{T_{7}}h-D_{T_{7}}T_{7-i}T^{*}_{7}h\,\,(\text{by Proposition \ref{FiFj}})

\displaystyle=D_{T_{7}}T^{*}_{i}h-T^{*}_{7}\tilde{F}^{*}_{7-i}D_{T^{*}_{7}}h-D_{T_{7}}T_{7-i}T^{*}_{7}h\,\,(\text{by Part $(1)$})

\displaystyle=D_{T_{7}}(T^{*}_{i}-T_{7-i}T^{*}_{7})h-T^{*}_{7}\tilde{F}^{*}_{7-i}D_{T^{*}_{7}}h

\displaystyle=D_{T_{7}}D_{T^{*}_{7}}\tilde{F}_{i}D_{T^{*}_{7}}h-T^{*}_{7}\tilde{F}^{*}_{7-i}D_{T^{*}_{7}}h

\displaystyle=(D_{T_{7}}D_{T^{*}_{7}}\tilde{F}_{i}-T^{*}_{7}\tilde{F}^{*}_{7-i})D_{T^{*}_{7}}h,1\leq i\leq 6.

It yields from (
2.5
) that
(F^{*}_{i}D_{T_{7}}D_{T^{*}_{7}}-F_{7-i}T^{*}_{7})|_{\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}}=D_{T_{7}}D_{T^{*}_{7}}\tilde{F}_{i}-T^{*}_{7}\tilde{F}^{*}_{7-i}
for
1\leqslant i\leqslant 6
.

This completes the proof.

Theorem 2.5
.

Let
F_{i},1\leq i\leq 6
be fundamental operators of a
\Gamma_{E(3;3;1,1,1)}
-contraction

\operatorname{Ran}T_{7}

for
1\leq i,j\leq 6

(2)

[F_{i},F^{*}_{i}]=[F_{7-i},F^{*}_{7-i}]
for
1\leqslant i\leqslant 6

\textbf{T}=(T_{1},\dots,T_{7})
is a
\Gamma_{E(3;3;1,1,1)}
-contraction, it follows from Proposition
2.4
that

for

. Thus, we have

\displaystyle\tilde{F}^{*}_{j}\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}T_{7}D_{T_{7}}

\displaystyle=T_{7}F_{j}F_{i}D_{T_{7}}

(2.6)

\displaystyle=T_{7}F_{i}F_{j}D_{T_{7}}

\displaystyle=\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}\tilde{F}^{*}_{j}T_{7}D_{T_{7}}.

This completes the proof of part
(1)
of the theorem.

(2)

By Proposition
2.2
, we observe that

for

from left in both sides for

Similarly, we also obtain

\displaystyle T_{i}T_{7-i}-T^{*}_{7-i}T_{7-i}T_{7}=D_{T_{7}}F_{i}F_{7-i}D_{T_{7}}+D_{T_{7}}F_{i}F^{*}_{i}D_{T_{7}}T_{7}~{\rm{for}}~1\leq i\leq 6.

Subtracting (
2.9
)-(
2.8

2.3

Sz.-Nagy and Foias
[

T
defined on a Hilbert space
\mathcal{H}

\mathbb{T}=P_{\mathcal{H}_{T}}(M_{z}\otimes I)_{|_{\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}}}}
on the Hilbert space
\mathcal{H}_{T}=(H^{2}(\mathbb{D})\otimes\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}})\ominus M_{\Theta_{T}}(H^{2}(\mathbb{D})\otimes\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}}),
where
M_{z}
denotes the multiplication operator on
H^{2}(\mathbb{D})
and
M_{\Theta_{T}}
represents the multiplication operator from
H^{2}(\mathbb{D})\otimes\mathcal{D}_{T}
into
H^{2}(\mathbb{D})\otimes\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}}
associated with the multiplication
\Theta_{T}
, which is the characteristic function of
T,
as defined in section
1.
In this section, we describe a model for a pure
\Gamma_{E(3;3;1,1,1)}
-contraction and a pure
\Gamma_{E(3;2;1,2)}
-contraction.

We now produce functional model for a pure
\Gamma_{E(3;3;1,1,1)}
-contraction. In order to prove this, we define
W:\mathcal{H}\to H^{2}(\mathbb{D})\otimes\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}
by

\displaystyle W(h)

\displaystyle=\sum_{n\geqslant 0}z^{n}\otimes D_{T^{*}_{7}}T^{*n}_{7}h.

(3.1)

Since
T_{7}
is a pure isometry, one can easily deduced that
W
is isometry. The adjoint of
W
is given by

\displaystyle W^{*}(z^{n}\otimes\xi)

\displaystyle=T^{n}_{7}D_{T^{*}_{7}}\xi\,\,\text{for}\,\,n\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\},\xi\in\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}.

(3.2)

We only state the following lemma. See
[
SPal1
]
for the proof.

Lemma 3.1
.

Let
T_{7}
be contraction. Then

\displaystyle WW^{*}+M_{\Theta_{T_{7}}}M^{*}_{\Theta_{T_{7}}}=I_{H^{2}(\mathbb{D})\otimes\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}}.

(3.3)

The following theorem describes the functional models for a pure
\Gamma_{E(3;3;1,1,1)}
-contraction.

Theorem 3.2
.

Let
\textbf{T}=(T_{1},\dots,T_{7})
be a
\Gamma_{E(3;3;1,1,1)}
-contraction on a Hilbert space
\mathcal{H}
. Suppose that
\tilde{F}_{i},1\leq i\leq 6
are fundamental operators of
\textbf{T}^{*}=(T^{*}_{1},\dots,T^{*}_{7})
. Then

(1)

T_{i}
is unitarily equivalent to
P_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}(I\otimes\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}+M_{z}\otimes\tilde{F}_{7-i})_{|_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}}
for
1\leqslant i\leqslant 6
, and

(2)

T_{7}
is unitarily equivalent to
P_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}(M_{z}\otimes I_{\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}})_{|_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}}
,

where
\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}=(H^{2}(\mathbb{D})\otimes\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}})\ominus M_{\Theta_{T_{7}}}(H^{2}(\mathbb{D})\otimes\mathcal{D}_{T_{7}}).

Proof.

Since
W
is an isometry, it implies that
WW^{*}
is the projection onto the
\operatorname{Ran}W
. Also, as
T_{7}
is a pure, it yields that
M_{\Theta_{T_{7}}}
is an isometry. Thus, by Lemma
3.1
, it follows that
W(\mathcal{H})=\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}
. Note that

\displaystyle W^{*}(I\otimes\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}+M_{z}\otimes\tilde{F}_{7-i})(z^{n}\otimes\xi)

\displaystyle=W^{*}(z^{n}\otimes\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}\xi)+W^{*}(z^{n+1}\otimes\tilde{F}_{7-i}\xi)

(3.4)

\displaystyle=T^{n}_{7}D_{T^{*}_{7}}\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}\xi+T^{n+1}_{7}D_{T^{*}_{7}}\tilde{F}_{7-i}\xi

\displaystyle=T^{n}_{7}(D_{T^{*}_{7}}\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}+T_{7}D_{T^{*}_{7}}\tilde{F}_{7-i})\xi

\displaystyle=T^{n}_{7}(\tilde{F}_{i}D_{T^{*}_{7}}+\tilde{F}^{*}_{7-i}D_{T^{*}_{7}}T^{*}_{7})^{*}\xi

\displaystyle=T^{n}_{7}(D_{T^{*}_{7}}T^{*}_{i})^{*}\xi\,\,(\text{by Lemma 2.7 of \cite[cite]{[\@@bibref{}{ABD}{}{}]}})

\displaystyle=T_{i}T^{n}_{7}D_{T^{*}_{7}}\xi

\displaystyle=T_{i}W^{*}(z^{n}\otimes\xi)~{\rm{for}}~1\leqslant i\leqslant 6.

Thus, from (
3.4
), we conclude that
W^{*}(I\otimes\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}+M_{z}\otimes\tilde{F}_{7-i})=T_{i}W^{*},1\leq i\leq 6
on the vectors of the form
z^{n}\otimes\xi
for all
n\geq 0
and
\xi\in\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}},
which span
H^{2}(\mathbb{D})\otimes\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}
. This shows that

W^{*}(I\otimes\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}+M_{z}\otimes\tilde{F}_{7-i})=T_{i}W^{*},1\leq i\leq 6~{\rm{on}}~~H^{2}(\mathbb{D})\otimes\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}

and hence we have
W^{*}(I\otimes\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}+M_{z}\otimes\tilde{F}_{7-i})W=T_{i},1\leq i\leq 6.
Therfore, we deduce that
T_{i}
is unitarily equivalent to
P_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}(I\otimes\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}+M_{z}\otimes\tilde{F}_{7-i})_{|_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}}
for
1\leqslant i\leqslant 6
. Observe that

\displaystyle W^{*}(M_{z}\otimes I_{\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}})(z^{n}\otimes\xi)

\displaystyle=W^{*}(z^{n+1}\otimes\xi)

(3.5)

\displaystyle=T^{n+1}_{7}D_{T^{*}_{7}}\xi

\displaystyle=T_{7}(T^{n}_{7}D_{T^{*}_{7}}\xi)

\displaystyle=T_{7}W^{*}(z^{n}\otimes\xi).

Hence it follows from (
3.5
) that
W^{*}(M_{z}\otimes I_{\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}})=T_{7}W^{*}
on the vectors of the form
z^{n}\otimes\xi
for all
n\geq 0
and
\xi\in\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}.
By the same argument we also conclude that
T_{7}
is unitarily equivalent to
P_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}(M_{z}\otimes I_{\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}})_{|_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}}
. This completes the proof.
∎

It is important to note that the unitary equivalence does not guarantee that the tuple

\left(P_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}(I\otimes\tilde{F}^{*}_{1}+M_{z}\otimes\tilde{F}_{6})_{|_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}},\dots,P_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}(I\otimes\tilde{F}^{*}_{6}+M_{z}\otimes\tilde{F}_{1})_{|_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}},P_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}(M_{z}\otimes I_{\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}})_{|_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}}\right)

P_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}(I\otimes\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}+M_{z}\otimes\tilde{F}_{7-i})|_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}
commutes with
P_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}(M_{z}\otimes I_{\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}})|_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}
for all

(4.1)

The following result given by Sz.-Nagy and Foias
[
43
]
states that the characteristic function of a completely non-unitary contraction is a complete unitary invariant.

Theorem 4.1

(Nagy-Foias)
.

Two completely non-unitary contractions are unitarily equivalent if and only if their characteristic functions coincide.

In this section, we give a complete set of unitary invariant for a pure
\Gamma_{E(3;3;1,1,1)}
-contraction and a pure
\Gamma_{E(3;2;1,2)}
-contraction.

Proposition 4.2
.

If two
\Gamma_{E(3;3;1,1,1)}
-contractions
\textbf{T}=(T_{1},\dots,T_{7})
and
\textbf{T}^{{}^{\prime}}=(T^{{}^{\prime}}_{1},\dots,T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7})
defined on
\mathcal{H}
and
\mathcal{H}^{{}^{\prime}}
respectively are unitarily equivalent, then their fundamental operators
F_{i},1\leq i\leq 6
and
F^{{}^{\prime}}_{j},1\leq j\leq 6
respectively are also unitarily equivalent.

Proof.

Let
U:\mathcal{H}\to\mathcal{H}^{{}^{\prime}}
be the unitary such that
UT_{i}=T^{{}^{\prime}}_{i}U
for
1\leq i\leq 7.
Then we have
UT^{*}_{i}=T^{{}^{\prime}*}_{i}U
for
1\leqslant i\leqslant 7
. We note that

\displaystyle UD^{2}_{T_{7}}

\displaystyle=U(I-T^{*}_{7}T_{7})=U-T^{{}^{\prime}*}_{7}UT_{7}=U-T^{{}^{\prime}*}_{7}T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}U=D^{2}_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}U.

(4.2)

It follows from (
4.2
) that
UD_{T_{7}}=D_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}U
. Let
\tilde{U}=U|_{\mathcal{D}_{T_{7}}}
. Then we have
\tilde{U}\in\mathcal{B}(\mathcal{D}_{T_{7}},\mathcal{D}_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}})
and so
\tilde{U}D_{T_{7}}=D_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}\tilde{U}
. Note that for
1\leq i\leq 6,

\displaystyle D_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}\tilde{U}F_{i}\tilde{U}^{*}D_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}

\displaystyle=\tilde{U}D_{T_{7}}F_{i}D_{T_{7}}\tilde{U}^{*}

(4.3)

\displaystyle=\tilde{U}(T_{i}-T^{*}_{7-i}T_{7})\tilde{U}^{*}

\displaystyle=T^{{}^{\prime}}_{i}-T^{{}^{\prime}*}_{7-i}T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}

\displaystyle=D_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}F^{{}^{\prime}}_{i}D_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}.

Thus, we conclude that
F^{{}^{\prime}}_{i}=\tilde{U}F_{i}\tilde{U}^{*}
for
1\leqslant i\leqslant 6
. This completes the proof.
∎

The following proposition is a partial converse of the previous proposition for a pure
\Gamma_{E(3;3;1,1,1)}
-contraction.

Proposition 4.3
.

Let
\textbf{T}=(T_{1},\dots,T_{7})
and
\textbf{T}^{{}^{\prime}}=(T^{{}^{\prime}}_{1},\dots,T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7})
be two pure
\Gamma_{E(3;3;1,1,1)}
-contractions on the Hilbert spaces
\mathcal{H}
and
\mathcal{H}^{{}^{\prime}},
respectively, such that their characteristic functions of
T_{7}
and
T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}
coincide. Also, assume that the fundamental operators
(\tilde{F}_{1},\dots,\tilde{F}_{6})
of
\textbf{T}^{*}=(T^{*}_{1},\dots,T^{*}_{7})
and
(F^{{}^{\prime}}_{1*},\dots,F^{{}^{\prime}}_{6*})
of
\textbf{T}^{{}^{\prime}*}=(T^{{}^{\prime}*}_{1},\dots,T^{{}^{\prime}*}_{7})
are unitarily equivalent by the same unitary that is involved in the coincidence of the characteristic functions of
T_{7}
and
T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}.
Then
T
is unitarily equivalent to
\textbf{T}^{{}^{\prime}}
.

Proof.

Let
U:\mathcal{D}_{T_{7}}\to\mathcal{D}_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}
and
U_{*}:\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}\to\mathcal{D}_{T^{{}^{\prime}*}_{7}}
be unitary operators such that
U_{*}\tilde{F}_{i}=F^{{}^{\prime}}_{i*}U_{*}
for
1\leqslant i\leqslant 6
and
U_{*}\Theta_{T_{7}}(z)=\Theta_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}(z)U
for all
z\in\mathbb{D}
. Let
\tilde{U}_{*}:=I\otimes U^{*}:H^{2}(\mathbb{D})\otimes\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}\to H^{2}(\mathbb{D})\otimes\mathcal{D}_{T^{{}^{\prime}*}_{7}}
be the operator defined by

\displaystyle\tilde{U}_{*}(z^{n}\otimes\eta)=z^{n}\otimes U_{*}\eta\,\,\text{for}\,\,n\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\},\eta\in\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}.

(4.4)

Note that
\tilde{U}_{*}
is a unitary and

\displaystyle\tilde{U}_{*}(M_{\Theta_{T_{7}}}f(z))

\displaystyle=\tilde{U}_{*}(\Theta_{T_{7}}(z)f(z))

(4.5)

\displaystyle=U_{*}\Theta_{T_{7}}(z)f(z)

\displaystyle=\Theta_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}(z)Uf(z)

\displaystyle=M_{\Theta_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}}(Uf(z))

for all
f\in H^{2}(\mathbb{D})\otimes\mathcal{D}_{T_{7}}
and
z\in\mathbb{D}
. It follows from (
4.5
) that
\tilde{U}_{*}
maps
\operatorname{Ran}M_{\Theta_{T_{7}}}
onto
\operatorname{Ran}M_{\Theta_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}}
. As
\tilde{U}_{*}
is unitary, we conclude that

\displaystyle\tilde{U}_{*}(\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}})

\displaystyle=\tilde{U}_{*}((\operatorname{Ran}M_{\Theta_{T_{7}}})^{\perp})

(4.6)

\displaystyle=(\tilde{U}_{*}\operatorname{Ran}M_{\Theta_{T_{7}}})^{\perp}

\displaystyle=(\operatorname{Ran}M_{\Theta_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}})^{\perp}

\displaystyle=\mathcal{H}_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}.

By definition of
\tilde{U}_{*},
we observe that for
1\leqslant i\leqslant 6
,

\displaystyle\tilde{U}_{*}(I\otimes\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}+M_{z}\otimes\tilde{F}_{7-i})^{*}

\displaystyle=(I\otimes U_{*})(I\otimes\tilde{F}_{i}+M^{*}_{z}\otimes\tilde{F}^{*}_{7-i})

(4.7)

\displaystyle=I\otimes U_{*}\tilde{F}_{i}+M^{*}_{z}\otimes U_{*}\tilde{F}^{*}_{7-i}

\displaystyle=I\otimes F^{{}^{\prime}}_{i*}U_{*}+M^{*}_{z}\otimes F^{{}^{\prime}*}_{(7-i)*}U_{*}

\displaystyle=(I\otimes F^{{}^{\prime}*}_{i*}+M_{z}\otimes F^{{}^{\prime}}_{(7-i)*})^{*}(I_{H^{2}}\otimes U_{*})

\displaystyle=(I\otimes F^{{}^{\prime}*}_{i*}+M_{z}\otimes F^{{}^{\prime}}_{(7-i)*})^{*}\tilde{U}_{*}.

Also, by the definition of
\tilde{U}_{*},
it follows that

\displaystyle\tilde{U}_{*}(M_{z}\otimes I_{\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}})

\displaystyle=(I\otimes U_{*})(M_{z}\otimes I_{\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}})

(4.8)

\displaystyle=(M_{z}\otimes I_{\mathcal{D}_{T^{{}^{\prime}*}_{7}}})(I_{H^{2}}\otimes U_{*})

\displaystyle=(M_{z}\otimes I_{\mathcal{D}_{T^{{}^{\prime}*}_{7}}})\tilde{U}_{*}.

Thus,
\mathcal{H}_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}=\tilde{U}_{*}(\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}})
is a co-invariant subspace of

(I\otimes F^{{}^{\prime}*}_{i}+M_{z}\otimes F^{{}^{\prime}}_{7-i})\,\,\text{for}\,\,1\leqslant i\leqslant 6\,\,\text{and}\,\,(M_{z}\otimes I_{\mathcal{D}_{T_{7}}}).

Consequently, we derive

\displaystyle P_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}(I\otimes F^{*}_{i}+M_{z}\otimes F_{7-i})_{|_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}}

\displaystyle\cong P_{\mathcal{H}_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}}(I\otimes F^{{}^{\prime}*}_{i}+M_{z}\otimes F^{{}^{\prime}}_{7-i})_{|_{\mathcal{H}_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}}}

for
1\leqslant i\leqslant 6
and

\displaystyle P_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}(M_{z}\otimes I_{\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}})_{|_{\mathcal{H}_{T_{7}}}}

\displaystyle\cong P_{\mathcal{H}_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}}(M_{z}\otimes I_{\mathcal{D}_{T^{{}^{\prime}*}_{7}}})_{|_{\mathcal{H}_{T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}}}},

and the corresponding unitary operator that unitarizes them is
U_{*}:\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}_{7}}\to\mathcal{D}_{T^{{}^{\prime}*}_{7}}
. Therefore,
T
and
\textbf{T}^{{}^{\prime}}
are unitarily equivalent. This completes the proof.

∎

Combining the Proposition
4.2
and Proposition
4.3
, we prove the main result of this section, the unitary invariance for a pure
\Gamma_{E(3;3;1,1,1)}
-contraction.

Theorem 4.4
.

Let
\textbf{T}=(T_{1},\dots,T_{7})
and
\textbf{T}^{{}^{\prime}}=(T^{{}^{\prime}}_{1},\dots,T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7})
be two pure
\Gamma_{E(3;3;1,1,1)}
-contractions on the Hilbert spaces
\mathcal{H}
and
\mathcal{H}^{{}^{\prime}},
respectively. Suppose
(\tilde{F}_{1},\dots,\tilde{F}_{6})
and
(F^{{}^{\prime}}_{1*},\dots,F^{{}^{\prime}}_{6*})
are fundamental operators of
\textbf{T}^{*}=(T^{*}_{1},\dots,T^{*}_{7})
and
\textbf{T}^{{}^{\prime}*}=(T^{{}^{\prime}*}_{1},\dots,T^{{}^{\prime}*}_{7}),
respectively. Then
T
is unitarily equivalent to
\textbf{T}^{{}^{\prime}}
if and only if the characteristic functions of
T_{7}
and
T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}
coincide and
(\tilde{F}_{1},\dots,\tilde{F}_{6})

is unitarily equivalent to
(F^{{}^{\prime}}_{1*},\dots,F^{{}^{\prime}}_{6*})
by the same unitary that is involved in the coincidence of the characteristic functions of
T_{7}
and
T^{{}^{\prime}}_{7}.

Proof.

Since
T
is unitarily equivalent to
\textbf{T}^{{}^{\prime}}
, so are
\textbf{T}^{*}=(T^{*}_{1},\dots,T^{*}_{7})
and
\textbf{T}^{{}^{\prime}*}=(T^{{}^{\prime}*}_{1},\dots,T^{{}^{\prime}*}_{7}).
It follows from Proposition
4.2
that
(\tilde{F}_{1},\dots,\tilde{F}_{6})
and
(F^{{}^{\prime}}_{1*},\dots,F^{{}^{\prime}}_{6*})
are unitarily equivalence. This completes the proof.

∎

\Gamma_{E(3;3;1,1,1)}
-contraction, c.n.u

-contraction and c.n.u. tetrablock contraction. A model for a class of c.n.u.

-contraction
(S_{1},\dots,S_{n-1},P)
that satisfying

\displaystyle S^{*}_{i}P=PS^{*}_{i}\,\,\text{for}\,\,1\leqslant i\leqslant n-1

(5.1)

can be found in [Theorem 4.5,
[
23
]
]. Let
\mathcal{A},\mathcal{A}_{*}
be defined as

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}

\displaystyle=SOT-\lim_{n\to\infty}T^{*n}_{7}T^{n}_{7}\,\,\text{and}\,\,\mathcal{A}_{*}=SOT-\lim_{n\to\infty}T^{n}_{7}T^{*n}_{7}.

(5.2)

Define an operator
V:\overline{\operatorname{Ran}}\mathcal{A}\rightarrow\overline{\operatorname{Ran}}\mathcal{A}
by

\displaystyle V(\mathcal{A}^{1/2}x)

\displaystyle=\mathcal{A}^{1/2}T_{7}x.

(5.3)

Observe that

\displaystyle\mathcal{A}^{1/2}\mathcal{A}_{*}\mathcal{A}^{1/2}V(\mathcal{A}^{1/2}x)=\mathcal{A}^{1/2}\mathcal{A}_{*}\mathcal{A}T_{7}x.

(5.4)

\displaystyle Qx

(5.5)

We only state the following proposition. The proof is similar to Theorem

. Therefore, we skip the proof.

Proposition 5.1
.

Let
\textbf{T}=(T_{1},\dots,T_{7})
be a
\Gamma_{E(3;3;1,1,1)}
-contraction on a Hilbert space
\mathcal{H}
. Let
F_{1},\dots,F_{6}
and
\tilde{F}_{1},\dots,\tilde{F}_{6}
be the fundamental operators of
T
and
\textbf{T}^{*}
respectively. Then

(1)

\tilde{F}^{*}_{i}D_{T^{*}_{7}}\mathcal{A}^{1/2}|_{\overline{\operatorname{Ran}}\mathcal{A}}+T_{7}T^{*}_{7}\tilde{F}_{7-i}D_{T^{*}_{7}}\mathcal{A}^{1/2}V=D_{T^{*}_{7}}T_{i}\mathcal{A}^{1/2}|_{\overline{\operatorname{Ran}}\mathcal{A}}
,

(2)

\tilde{F^{*}_{i}}D_{T^{*}_{7}}T^{*}_{7}+T_{7}T^{*}_{7}\tilde{F}_{7-i}D_{T^{*}_{7}}=D_{T^{*}_{7}}T_{i}T^{*}_{7}

for

.

We recall the following theorem from
[

]
.

Theorem 5.2

(Durszt,
[

]
)
.

If
T
is a c.n.u. contraction on Hilbert space
\mathcal{H}
then there exists an isometry
W:\mathcal{H}\to(H^{2}(\mathbb{D})\otimes\mathcal{D}_{T})\oplus(L^{2}(\mathbb{T})\otimes\mathcal{D}_{T^{*}})

\displaystyle S^{*}_{1}-\tilde{S}_{2}S^{*}_{3}=D_{S^{*}_{3}}\hat{G}_{1}D_{S^{*}_{3}}\,\,\text{and}\,\,\tilde{S}^{*}_{2}-S_{1}S^{*}_{3}=D_{S^{*}_{3}}\hat{\tilde{G}}_{2}D_{S^{*}_{3}},

where
\hat{G}_{1}f(\zeta)=\hat{\tilde{G}}_{2}f(\zeta)=\overline{\alpha}a_{1}+(1-|\alpha|^{2})^{1/2}a_{2}\zeta
as
S_{1}=\tilde{S}_{2}
.

Note that the constant term in
(I\otimes\hat{G}^{*}_{1}+M^{*}_{e^{it}}\otimes T_{7}T^{*}_{7}\hat{\tilde{G}}_{2})W_{2}
is
D_{S^{*}_{3}}S_{1}\mathcal{A}
. Thus, we get

\displaystyle D_{S^{*}_{3}}S_{1}\mathcal{A}f(\zeta)

\displaystyle=D_{S^{*}_{3}}S_{1}(|\alpha|^{2}a_{0}+a_{1}\zeta+a_{2}\zeta^{2}+\dots)

(6.9)

\displaystyle=D_{S^{*}_{3}}(\alpha|\alpha|^{2}a_{0}+a_{1}\zeta+a_{2}\zeta^{2}+\dots)

\displaystyle=\alpha|\alpha|^{2}a_{0}\zeta+(1-|\alpha|^{2})^{1/2}a_{1}\zeta^{2},

Also, the constant term in
W_{2}S_{1}
is
D_{S^{*}_{3}}\mathcal{A}S_{1}
. Thus, we have

\displaystyle D_{S^{*}_{3}}\mathcal{A}S_{1}f(\zeta)

\displaystyle=D_{S^{*}_{3}}\mathcal{A}(\alpha a_{0}\zeta+a_{1}\zeta^{2}+a_{2}\zeta^{3}+\dots)

(6.10)

\displaystyle=D_{S^{*}_{3}}(\alpha a_{0}\zeta+a_{1}\zeta^{2}+a_{2}\zeta^{3}+\dots)

\displaystyle=\alpha a_{0}\zeta+(1-|\alpha|^{2})^{1/2}a_{1}\zeta^{2}.

This shows that the constant terms of
D_{S^{*}_{3}}S_{1}\mathcal{A}
and
D_{S^{*}_{3}}\mathcal{A}S_{1}
are not equal, which leads to a contradiction. Hence, the model described in Theorem
5.6
is not a c.n.u.
\Gamma_{E(3;2;1,2)}
-contraction.
