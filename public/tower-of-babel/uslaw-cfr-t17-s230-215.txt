17 C.F.R. § 230.215 — Accredited investor.
Citation: us:cfr:17:230.215
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-230.215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 230 (General Rules And Regulations, Securities Act Of 1933). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term accredited investor as used in section 2(a)(15)(ii) of the Securities Act of 1933 (15 U.S.C. 77b(a)(15)(ii)) shall have the same meaning as the definition of that term in rule 501(a) under the Act (17 CFR 230.501(a)).
