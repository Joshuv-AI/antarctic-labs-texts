20 C.F.R. § 702.351 — Withdrawal of controversion of issues set for formal hearing; effect.
Citation: us:cfr:20:702.351
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-702.351
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 702 (Administration And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever a party withdraws his controversion of the issues set for a formal hearing, the administrative law judge shall halt the proceedings upon receipt from said party of a signed statement to that effect and forthwith notify the district director who shall then proceed to dispose of the case as provided for in § 702.315.
