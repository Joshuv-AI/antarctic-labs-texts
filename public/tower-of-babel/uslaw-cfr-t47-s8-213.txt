47 C.F.R. § 8.213 — Limitations on grants to use the FCC IoT Label.
Citation: us:cfr:47:8.213
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-8.213
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 8 (Part 8— INTERNET TRANSPARENCY FOR CONSUMERS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A grant of authorization to use the FCC IoT Label remains effective until set aside, revoked or withdrawn, rescinded, surrendered, or a termination date is otherwise established by the Commission.
(b) No person shall, in any advertising matter, brochure, etc., use or make reference to the FCC IoT Label or the Cyber Trust Mark in a deceptive or misleading manner.
