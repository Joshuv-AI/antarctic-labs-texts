25 C.F.R. § 15.203 — What information must Tribes provide BIA to complete the probate file?
Citation: us:cfr:25:15.203
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-15.203
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 15 (Probate Of Indian Estates, Except For Members Of The Osage Nation And The Five Civilized Tribes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Tribes must provide any information that we require or request to complete the probate file. This information may include enrollment and family history data or property title documents that pertain to any pending probate matter, and a copy of Tribal probate orders where they exist.
