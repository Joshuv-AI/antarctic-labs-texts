36 U.S.C. § 230306 — Exclusive right to name, seals, emblems, and badges
Citation: us:usc:36:230306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section230306
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 2303 (Veterans Of World War I Of The United States Of America, Incorporated). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation and its subordinate divisions have the exclusive right to use the name “Veterans of World War I of the United States of America, Incorporated”. The corporation has the exclusive right to use, and to allow others to use, seals, emblems, and badges the corporation adopts.
