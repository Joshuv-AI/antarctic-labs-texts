14 C.F.R. § 223.5 — Responsibility of agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:14:223.5
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-223.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 223 (Free And Reduced-Rate Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Federal Aviation Administration, National Transportation Safety Board, National Weather Service, and the Postal Service shall be responsible for the following:
(a) The issuance of any credentials or certificates to their personnel eligible for free or reduced-rate transportation under this part; and
(b) The promulgation of any internal rules that are necessary to obtain compliance by such personnel with this part.
