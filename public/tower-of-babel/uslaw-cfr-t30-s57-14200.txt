30 C.F.R. § 57.14200 — Warnings prior to starting or moving equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:30:57.14200
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-57.14200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 57 (Safety And Health Standards—Underground Metal And Nonmetal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before starting crushers or moving self-propelled mobile equipment, equipment operators shall sound a warning that is audible above the surrounding noise level or use other effective means to warn all persons who could be exposed to a hazard from the equipment.
