7 C.F.R. § 4280.152 — Notification of unsuccessful Applicants.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4280.152
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4280.152
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4280 (Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants whose applications are not eligible for financial assistance through this program, who are removed from funding consideration due to a high level of risk, or whose applications did not score high enough to be funded will be notified as soon as practicable. No further processing of the application will occur.
