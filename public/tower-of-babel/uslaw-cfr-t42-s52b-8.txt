42 C.F.R. § 52b.8 — b.8 How will NIH monitor the use of facilities constructed with federal funds?
Citation: us:cfr:42:52b.8
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-52b.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 52b (PART 52b—NATIONAL INSTITUTES OF HEALTH CONSTRUCTION GRANTS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NIH may monitor the use of each facility constructed with funds awarded under this part to ensure its continued use for the originally authorized research purpose, by means of reviewing periodic facility use certifications or reports, site visits, and other appropriate means.
