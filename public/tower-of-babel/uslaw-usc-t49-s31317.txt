49 U.S.C. § 31317 — Procedure for prescribing regulations
Citation: us:usc:49:31317
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section31317
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Commercial); Chapter 313 (Commercial Motor Vehicle Operators). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Regulations prescribed by the Secretary of Transportation to carry out this chapter (except section 31307) shall be prescribed under section 553 of title 5 without regard to sections 556 and 557 of title 5.
