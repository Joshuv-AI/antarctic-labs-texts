12 C.F.R. § 150.210 — Must I conduct another review of an account after I accept it?
Citation: us:cfr:12:150.210
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-150.210
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 150 (Fiduciary Powers Of Federal Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After you accept a fiduciary account for which you have investment discretion, you must conduct a prompt review of all assets of the account to evaluate whether they are appropriate, individually and collectively, for the account.
