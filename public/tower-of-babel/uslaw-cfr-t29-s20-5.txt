29 C.F.R. § 20.5 — Examination of records relating to the claim; opportunity for full explanation of the claim.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 20 (Federal Claims Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Following receipt of the notice specified in § 20.4, the debtor may request to examine and copy the information to be disclosed to the consumer credit reporting agency, in accordance with 5 U.S.C. 552a.
