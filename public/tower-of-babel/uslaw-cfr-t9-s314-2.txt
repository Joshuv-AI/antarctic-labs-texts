9 C.F.R. § 314.2 — Tanking and other facilities for inedible products to be separate from edible product facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:9:314.2
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-314.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 314 (Handling And Disposal Of Condemned Or Other Inedible Products At Official Establishments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All tanks and equipment used for rendering, otherwise preparing, or storing inedible products must be in rooms or compartments separate from those used for preparing or storing edible products. There may be a connection between rooms or compartments containing inedible products and those containing edible products as long as it does not cause the adulteration of edible product or create insanitary conditions.
