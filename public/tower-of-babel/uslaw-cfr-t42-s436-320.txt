42 C.F.R. § 436.320 — Medically needy coverage of the aged.
Citation: us:cfr:42:436.320
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-436.320
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 436 (Eligibility In Guam, Puerto Rico, And The Virgin Islands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the agency provides Medicaid to the medically needy, it may provide Medicaid to individuals who—
(a) Are 65 years of age and older, as provided for in § 436.520; and
(b) Meet the income and resource requirements of subpart I of this part.
