9 C.F.R. § 327.8 — Import products; equipment and means of conveyance used in handling to be maintained in sanitary condition.
Citation: us:cfr:9:327.8
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-327.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 327 (Imported Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Compartments of steamships, sailing vessels, railroad cars, and other means of conveyance transporting any product to the United States, and all trucks, chutes, platforms, racks, tables, tools, utensils, and all other devices used in moving and handling any product offered for importation into the United States, shall be maintained in a sanitary condition.
