50 C.F.R. § 216.31 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:50:216.31
Creator: National Marine Fisheries Service, National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-216.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 216 (Regulations Governing The Taking And Importing Of Marine Mammals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of this subpart, the definitions set forth in 50 CFR part 217 shall apply to all threatened and endangered marine mammals, unless a more restrictive definition exists under the MMPA or part 216.
