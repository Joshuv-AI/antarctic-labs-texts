27 C.F.R. § 40.163 — Semimonthly tax return periods.
Citation: us:cfr:27:40.163
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-40.163
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 40 (Manufacture Of Tobacco Products, Cigarette Papers And Tubes, And Processed Tobacco). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in § 40.164, the periods to be covered by semimonthly tax returns are from the 1st day of each month through the 15th day of that month and from the 16th day of each month through the last day of that month.
