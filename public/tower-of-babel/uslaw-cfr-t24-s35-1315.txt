24 C.F.R. § 35.1315 — Collection and laboratory analysis of samples.
Citation: us:cfr:24:35.1315
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-35.1315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 35 (Lead-Based Paint Poisoning Prevention In Certain Residential Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All paint chip, dust, or soil samples shall be collected and analyzed in accordance with standards established either by a State or Indian tribe under a program authorized by EPA in accordance with 40 CFR part 745, subpart Q, or by the EPA in accordance with 40 CFR 745.227, and as further provided in this subpart.
