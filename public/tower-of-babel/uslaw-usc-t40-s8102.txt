40 U.S.C. § 8102 — Protection of Federal Government buildings in District of Columbia
Citation: us:usc:40:8102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section8102
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part D (Public Buildings, Grounds, And Parks In The District Of Columbia); Chapter 81 (Administrative); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General and the Secretary of the Treasury may prohibit—
(1)
a vehicle from parking or standing on a street or roadway adjacent to a building in the District of Columbia—
(A)
at least partly owned or possessed by, or leased to, the Federal Government; and
(B)
used by law enforcement authorities subject to their jurisdiction; and
(2)
a person or entity from conducting business on property immediately adjacent to a building described in paragraph (1).
