41 C.F.R. § 102-37.75 — -37.75 State plan requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.75
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State legislature must ensure the plan conforms to the provisions of 40 U.S.C. 549(e) and includes information and assurances as required by GSA. It may also include in the plan other provisions not inconsistent with the purposes of title 40, U.S. Code, and the requirements of this part.
