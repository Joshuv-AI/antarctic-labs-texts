15 U.S.C. § 8231 — Rule of construction
Citation: us:usc:15:8231
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section8231
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 108 (State-Based Insurance Reform); Subchapter III (Rule Of Construction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter or the amendments made by this subtitle 1 shall be construed to modify, impair, or supersede the application of the antitrust laws. Any implied or actual conflict between this chapter and any amendments to this chapter and the antitrust laws shall be resolved in favor of the operation of the antitrust laws.
