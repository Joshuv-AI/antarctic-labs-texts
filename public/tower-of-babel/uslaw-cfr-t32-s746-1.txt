32 C.F.R. § 746.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:746.1
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-746.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 746 (Licensing Of Government Inventions In The Custody Of The Department Of The Navy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements Department of Defense Directive 5535.3 of November 2, 1973 and 41 CFR subpart 101-4.1, and sets forth the policy, terms, conditions, and procedures for the licensing of rights in domestic patents and patent applications vested in the United States of America and in the custody of the Department of the Navy.
