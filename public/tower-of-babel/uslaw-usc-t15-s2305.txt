15 U.S.C. § 2305 — Full and limited warranting of a consumer product
Citation: us:usc:15:2305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2305
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 50 (Consumer Product Warranties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall prohibit the selling of a consumer product which has both full and limited warranties if such warranties are clearly and conspicuously differentiated.
