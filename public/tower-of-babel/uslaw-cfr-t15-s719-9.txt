15 C.F.R. § 719.9 — Summary decision.
Citation: us:cfr:15:719.9
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-719.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 719 (Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ALJ may render a summary decision disposing of all or part of a proceeding on the motion of any party to the proceeding, provided that there is no genuine issue as to any material fact and the party is entitled to summary decision as a matter of law.
