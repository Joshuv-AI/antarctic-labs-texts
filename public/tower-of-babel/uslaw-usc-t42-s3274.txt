42 U.S.C. § 3274 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:3274
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3274
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 40 (Soil Information Assistance For Community Planning And Resource Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are hereby authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this chapter, such sums to remain available until expended.
