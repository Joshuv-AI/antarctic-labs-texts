48 C.F.R. § 27.400 — 27.400 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:27.400
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-27.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 27 (Patents, Data, And Copyrights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth policies and procedures regarding rights in data and copyrights, and acquisition of data. The policy statement in 27.402 applies to all executive agencies. The remainder of the subpart applies to all executive agencies except the Department of Defense.
