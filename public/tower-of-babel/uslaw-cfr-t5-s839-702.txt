5 C.F.R. § 839.702 — How do these rules apply to me if I don't have an election right under the FERCCA, but I did have a qualifying retirement coverage error?
Citation: us:cfr:5:839.702
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-839.702
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 839 (Correction Of Retirement Coverage Errors Under The Federal Erroneous Retirement Coverage Corrections Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After your retirement coverage is corrected to the proper plan, your retirement deductions will be adjusted in accordance with subpart H of this part and your Social Security taxes will be adjusted in accordance with subpart I of this part, if applicable. You may also file a claim for losses in accordance with subpart L of this part.
