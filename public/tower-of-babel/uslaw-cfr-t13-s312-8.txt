13 C.F.R. § 312.8 — Investment rates.
Citation: us:cfr:13:312.8
Creator: Economic Development Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-312.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 312 (Regional Innovation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Minimum investment rate. There is no minimum investment rate for a project.
(b) Maximum investment rate. The maximum investment rate for a project shall not exceed 50 percent.
