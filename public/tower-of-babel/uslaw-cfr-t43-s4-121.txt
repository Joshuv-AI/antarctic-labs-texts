43 C.F.R. § 4.121 — Case management.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.121
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.121
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An ALJ is vested with the general authority to regulate the course of the proceedings and the conduct of parties to ensure that cases are resolved fairly, efficiently, and in compliance with applicable laws and orders.
