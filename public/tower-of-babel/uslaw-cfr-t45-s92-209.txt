45 C.F.R. § 92.209 — Nondiscrimination on the basis of association.
Citation: us:cfr:45:92.209
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-92.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 92 (Nondiscrimination In Health Programs Or Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A covered entity must not exclude from participation in, deny the benefits of, or otherwise discriminate against an individual or entity in its health programs and activities on the basis of the respective race, color, national origin, sex, age, or disability of the individual and another person with whom the individual or entity has a relationship or association.
