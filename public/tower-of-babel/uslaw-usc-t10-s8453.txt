10 U.S.C. § 8453 — Midshipmen: appointment
Citation: us:usc:10:8453
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8453
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Education And Training); Chapter 853 (United States Naval Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Midshipmen at the Naval Academy shall be appointed by the President alone. An appointment is conditional until the midshipman is admitted.
