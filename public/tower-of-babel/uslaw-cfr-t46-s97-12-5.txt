46 C.F.R. § 97.12-5 — -5 Bulk solid cargoes that may liquefy.
Citation: us:cfr:46:97.12-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-97.12-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 97 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the information provided in § 97.12-3(a) or (b) indicates that the bulk solid cargo to be carried is prone to liquefy during carriage, due to small particle sizes and moisture content, then the requirements contained in § 148.450 of this chapter apply.
