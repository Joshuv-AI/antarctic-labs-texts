42 C.F.R. § 405.947 — Notice to the beneficiary of applicable plan's request for a redetermination.
Citation: us:cfr:42:405.947
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-405.947
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 405 (Federal Health Insurance For The Aged And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A CMS contractor must send notice of the applicable plan's appeal to the beneficiary.
(b) Issuance and content of the notice must comply with CMS instructions.
