41 C.F.R. § 51-9.102 — -9.102 Availability of records.
Citation: us:cfr:41:51-9.102
Creator: Chapter 51—Committee For Purchase From People Who Are Blind Or Severely Disabled
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-51-9.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 51-9 (9—Privacy Act Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Committee records pertaining to an individual shall be made available to the subject individual to the greatest extent possible. Disclosures of records to other than the subject individual will be made only in accordance with subpart 51-9.2 of this part.
