33 U.S.C. § 680 — Examination of mines; reports
Citation: us:usc:33:680
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section680
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 14 (California Debris Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Said commission, or a committee therefrom, or officer of said corps assigned to duty under its orders, shall, whenever deemed necessary, visit said territory and all mines operating under the provisions of this chapter. A report of such examination shall be placed on file.
