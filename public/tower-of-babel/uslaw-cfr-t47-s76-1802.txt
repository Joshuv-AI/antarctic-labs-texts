47 C.F.R. § 76.1802 — Annual employment report.
Citation: us:cfr:47:76.1802
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-76.1802
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 76 (Multichannel Video And Cable Television Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each employment unit with six or more full-time employees shall file an annual employment report on FCC Form 395-A with the Commission on or before September 30 of each year.
