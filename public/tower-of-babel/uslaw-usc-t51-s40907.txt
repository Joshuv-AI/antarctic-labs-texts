51 U.S.C. § 40907 — Museums
Citation: us:usc:51:40907
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section40907
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 409 (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator may provide grants to, and enter into cooperative agreements with, museums and planetariums to enable them to enhance programs related to space exploration, aeronautics, space science, Earth science, or microgravity.
