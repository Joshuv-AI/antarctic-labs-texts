43 C.F.R. § 30.212 — May I waive notice of the hearing or the form of notice?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.212
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may waive your right to notice of the hearing and the form of notice by:
(a) Appearing at the hearing and participating in the hearing without objection; or
(b) Filing a written waiver with the judge before the hearing.
