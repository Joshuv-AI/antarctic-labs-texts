34 C.F.R. § 681.59 — Records and Federal access after a school is no longer a HEAL school.
Citation: us:cfr:34:681.59
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-681.59
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 681 (Health Education Assistance Loan Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event a school ceases to participate in the HEAL program, the school (or its successor, in the case of a school which undergoes a change in ownership) must retain all required HEAL records and provide the Secretary of Education, the Comptroller General of the United States, and any of their authorized representatives access to them.
