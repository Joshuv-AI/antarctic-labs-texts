28 U.S.C. § 1347 — Partition action where United States is joint tenant
Citation: us:usc:28:1347
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1347
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part IV (Jurisdiction And Venue); Chapter 85 (District Courts; Jurisdiction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The district courts shall have original jurisdiction of any civil action commenced by any tenant in common or joint tenant for the partition of lands where the United States is one of the tenants in common or joint tenants.
