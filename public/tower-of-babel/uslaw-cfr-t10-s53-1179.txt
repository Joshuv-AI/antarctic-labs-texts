10 C.F.R. § 53.1179 — Duration of renewal.
Citation: us:cfr:10:53.1179
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-53.1179
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 53 (Risk-Informed, Technology-Inclusive Regulatory Framework For Commercial Nuclear Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each renewal of an early site permit may be for not less than 10, nor more than 20 years, plus any remaining years on the early site permit then in effect before renewal.
