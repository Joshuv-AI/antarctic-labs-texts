7 C.F.R. § 945.7 — Certified seed potatoes.
Citation: us:cfr:7:945.7
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-945.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 945 (Irish Potatoes Grown In Certain Designated Counties In Idaho, And Malheur County, Oregon). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Certified seed potatoes means and includes all potatoes officially certified and tagged, marked, or otherwise appropriately identified, under the supervision of the official seed potato certifying agency of the State in which the potatoes are grown, or other seed certification agencies which the Secretary may designate.
