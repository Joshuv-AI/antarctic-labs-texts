34 C.F.R. § 657.2 — What entities are eligible to receive an allocation of fellowships?
Citation: us:cfr:34:657.2
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-657.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 657 (Foreign Language And Area Studies Fellowships Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary awards an allocation of fellowships (grant) to an institution of higher education or to a consortium of institutions of higher education.
