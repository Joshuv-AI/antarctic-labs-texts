22 C.F.R. § 136.2 — Authority.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 136 (Personal Property Disposition At Posts Abroad). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 303(a) of the State Department Basic Authorities Act of 1956 authorizes the Secretary of State to issue regulations to carry out the purposes of title III of that Act.
