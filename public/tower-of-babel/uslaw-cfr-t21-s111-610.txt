21 C.F.R. § 111.610 — What records must be made available to FDA?
Citation: us:cfr:21:111.610
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-111.610
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 111 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice In Manufacturing, Packaging, Labeling, Or Holding Operations For Dietary Supplements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must have all records required under this part, or copies of such records, readily available during the retention period for inspection and copying by FDA when requested.
(b) If you use reduction techniques, such as microfilming, you must make suitable reader and photocopying equipment readily available to FDA.
