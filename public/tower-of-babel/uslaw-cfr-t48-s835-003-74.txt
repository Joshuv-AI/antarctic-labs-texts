48 C.F.R. § 835.003-74 — 835.003-74 Facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:48:835.003-74
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-835.003-74
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 835 (Research And Development Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the contracting officer determines that the facilities to be assigned to perform effort on a research and development (R&D) contract are critical to the success of the R&D effort, the contracting officer shall insert the clause at 852.235-73, Facilities, in the solicitation and contract.
