7 C.F.R. § 170.9 — What type of information does the application require?
Citation: us:cfr:7:170.9
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-170.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 170 (Usda Farmers Market). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The application for participation in the USDA Farmers Market will provide market management with information on contacts, farm location, type of farming operation, types of products grown, and business practices, including insurance coverage.
