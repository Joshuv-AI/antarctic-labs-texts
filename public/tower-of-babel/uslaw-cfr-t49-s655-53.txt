49 C.F.R. § 655.53 — Supervisor acting as collection site personnel.
Citation: us:cfr:49:655.53
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-655.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 655 (Prevention Of Alcohol Misuse And Prohibited Drug Use In Transit Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employer shall not permit an employee with direct or immediate supervisory responsibility or authority over another employee to serve as the urine or oral fluid collector, breath alcohol technician, or saliva-testing technician for a drug or alcohol test of the employee.
