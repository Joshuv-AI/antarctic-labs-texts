28 C.F.R. § 549.71 — Inmates affected.
Citation: us:cfr:28:549.71
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-549.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 549 (Medical Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to:
(a) Any individual incarcerated in an institution under the Bureau's jurisdiction; or
(b) Any other individual, as designated by the Director, who has been charged with or convicted of an offense against the United States.
