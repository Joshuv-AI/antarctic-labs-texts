International Comparison of Daily Smoking Rates among OECD Countries (2023)
Authors: Doyeon Kim, 도연 김
Published: 2026

According to Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) data on daily smoking rates as of 2023, the rate in Republic of Korea was 15.3%, which is 2.1%p higher than OECD average (13.2%). The daily smoking rate for men was 26.8%, which is 10.4%p higher than the OECD average, while the rate for women was 3.8%, which is 6.4%p lower than the OECD average (Figure 1).
