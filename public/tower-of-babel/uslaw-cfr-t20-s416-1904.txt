20 C.F.R. § 416.1904 — Authorization to withhold SSI benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:20:416.1904
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-416.1904
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 416 (Supplemental Security Income For The Aged, Blind, And Disabled). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We may withhold your SSI benefit payment and send it to the State to repay the State for the interim assistance it gave to you, if—
(a) We have an interim assistance agreement with the State at the time your authorization goes into effect; and
(b) Your authorization is in effect at the time we make the SSI benefit payment.
