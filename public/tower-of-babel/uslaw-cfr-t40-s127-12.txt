40 C.F.R. § 127.12 — Signature and certification standards for electronic reporting.
Citation: us:cfr:40:127.12
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-127.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 127 (Npdes Electronic Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The signatory and certification requirements identified in 40 CFR part 3 (including, in all cases, subpart D to part 3), § 122.22, and 40 CFR 403.12(l) must also apply to electronic submissions of NPDES information (see § 127.11) by NPDES permittees, facilities, and entities subject to this part [see § 127.1(a)].
