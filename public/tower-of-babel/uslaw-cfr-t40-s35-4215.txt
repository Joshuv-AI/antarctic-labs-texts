40 C.F.R. § 35.4215 — What if my group can't find an adequate number of potential sources for a technical advisor or other contractor?
Citation: us:cfr:40:35.4215
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.4215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In situations where only one adequate bidder can be found, your group may request written authority from the EPA award official to contract with the sole bidder.
