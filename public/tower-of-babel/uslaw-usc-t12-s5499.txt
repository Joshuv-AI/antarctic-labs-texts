12 U.S.C. § 5499 — Open data publication
Citation: us:usc:12:5499
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section5499
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 53 (Wall Street Reform And Consumer Protection); Subchapter V (Bureau Of Consumer Financial Protection); Part A (Bureau Of Consumer Financial Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All public data assets published by the Bureau shall be—
(1)
made available as an open Government data asset (as defined in section 3502 of title 44);
(2)
freely available for download;
(3)
rendered in a human-readable format; and
(4)
accessible via application programming interface where appropriate.
