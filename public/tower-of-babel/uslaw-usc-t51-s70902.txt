51 U.S.C. § 70902 — Allocation of International Space Station research budget
Citation: us:usc:51:70902
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section70902
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 709 (International Space Station). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator shall allocate at least 15 percent of the funds budgeted for International Space Station research to ground-based, free-flyer, and International Space Station life and microgravity science research that is not directly related to supporting the human exploration program, consistent with section 40904 of this title.
