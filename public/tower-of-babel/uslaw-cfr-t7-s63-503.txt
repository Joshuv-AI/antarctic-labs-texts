7 C.F.R. § 63.503 — Separability.
Citation: us:cfr:7:63.503

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-63.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 63 (National Sheep Industry Improvement Center). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any provision of the part is declared invalid or the applicability thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the validity of the remainder of this subpart, or the applicability thereof to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
