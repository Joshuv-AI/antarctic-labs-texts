21 C.F.R. § 1303.01 — Scope of part 1303.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1303.01
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1303.01
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1303 (Quotas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Procedures governing the establishment of production and manufacturing quotas on basic classes of controlled substances listed in schedules I and II pursuant to section 306 of the Act (21 U.S.C. 826) are governed generally by that section and specifically by the sections of this part.
