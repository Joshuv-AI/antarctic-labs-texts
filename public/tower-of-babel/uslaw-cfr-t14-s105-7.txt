14 C.F.R. § 105.7 — Use of alcohol and drugs.
Citation: us:cfr:14:105.7
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-105.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 105 (Parachute Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may conduct a parachute operation, and no pilot in command of an aircraft may allow a person to conduct a parachute operation from that aircraft, if that person is or appears to be under the influence of—
(a) Alcohol, or
(b) Any drug that affects that person's faculties in any way contrary to safety.
