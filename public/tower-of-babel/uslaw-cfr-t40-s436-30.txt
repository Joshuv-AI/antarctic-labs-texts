40 C.F.R. § 436.30 — Applicability; description of the construction sand and gravel subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:436.30
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-436.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 436 (Mineral Mining And Processing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to the mining and the processing of sand and gravel for construction or fill uses, except that on-board processing of dredged sand and gravel which is subject to the provisions of 33 CFR part 230 of this chapter will not be governed by the provisions of this subpart.
