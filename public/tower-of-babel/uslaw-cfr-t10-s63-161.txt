10 C.F.R. § 63.161 — Emergency plan for the geologic repository operations area through permanent closure.
Citation: us:cfr:10:63.161
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-63.161
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 63 (Disposal Of High-Level Radioactive Wastes In A Geologic Repository At Yucca Mountain, Nevada). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DOE shall develop and be prepared to implement a plan to cope with radiological accidents that may occur at the geologic repository operations area, at any time before permanent closure and decontamination or decontamination and dismantlement of surface facilities. The emergency plan must be based on the criteria of § 72.32(b) of this chapter.
