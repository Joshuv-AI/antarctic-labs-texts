32 C.F.R. § 2102.31 — Disclosure of a record to persons other than the individual to whom it pertains.
Citation: us:cfr:32:2102.31
Creator: National Security Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-2102.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 2102 (Rules And Regulations To Implement The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as provided by the Privacy Act, 5 U.S.C. 552a(b), the NSC will not disclose a record concerning an individual to another person or agency without the prior written consent of the individual to whom the record pertains.
