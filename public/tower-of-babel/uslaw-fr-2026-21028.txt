FEDERAL MARITIME COMMISSION


Notice of Agreements Filed

    The Commission hereby gives notice of filing of the following 
agreements under the Shipping Act of 1984. Interested parties may 
submit comments, relevant information, or documents regarding the 
agreement to the Secretary by email at [email protected], or by mail, 
Federal Maritime Commission, 800 North Capitol Street, Washington, DC 
20573. Comments will be most helpful to the Commission if received 
within 12 days of the date this notice appears in the Federal Register, 
and the Commission requests that comments be submitted within 7 days on 
agreements that request expedited review. Copies of agreements are 
available through the Commission's website (www.fmc.gov) or by 
contacting the Office of General Counsel at (202) 523-5740 or 
[email protected].

    Agreement No.: 012492-001.
    Agreement Name: Schuyler Line/U.S. Ocean Space Charter and 
Cooperative Working Agreement.
    Parties: Schuyler Line Navigation Company, LLC; and U.S. Ocean, 
L.L.C.
    Filing Party: Julia Bonestroo Banegas, Jones Walker LLP.
    Synopsis: The Amendment deletes joint contracting and procurement 
authority from the Agreement, updates the parties' addresses, updates 
the reference in Article 5 to the U.S. Department of Defense Universal 
Service Contract from U.S.C. 08 to U.S.C. 10 and successor contracts; 
and makes conforming changes.
    Proposed Effective Date: 10/7/2026.
    Location: https://www2.fmc.gov/eAgreementsSP/Public/AgreementHistory/2058.
