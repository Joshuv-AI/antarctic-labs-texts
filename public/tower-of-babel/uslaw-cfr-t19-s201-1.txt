19 C.F.R. § 201.1 — Applicability of part.
Citation: us:cfr:19:201.1
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-201.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 201 (Rules Of General Application). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part relates generally to functions and activities of the Commission under various statutes and other legal authority. Rules having special application appear separately in parts 202 through 207, inclusive, and parts 210, 212 and 213, of this chapter. In case of inconsistency between a rule of general application and a rule of special application, the latter is controlling.
