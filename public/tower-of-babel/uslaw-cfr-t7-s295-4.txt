7 C.F.R. § 295.4 — Program evaluation status reports.
Citation: us:cfr:7:295.4
Creator: Food And Nutrition Administration, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-295.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 295 (Availability Of Information And Records To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FNS also publishes summaries of objectives and findings of completed studies and projects concerning evaluation of FNS food assistance programs. A copy of the current status report on completed studies may be obtained by writing the Director, Office of Analysis and Evaluation, Food and Nutrition Service, USDA, 3101 Park Center Drive, Alexandria, VA 22302-1500.
