45 C.F.R. § 81.1 — Scope of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:45:81.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-81.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 81 (Practice And Procedure For Hearings Under Part 80 Of This Title). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules of procedure in this part supplement §§ 80.9 and 80.10 of this subtitle and govern the practice for hearings, decisions, and administrative review conducted by the Department of Health and Human Services, pursuant to Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (section 602, 78 Stat. 252) and part 80 of this subtitle.
