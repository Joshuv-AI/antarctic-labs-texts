30 C.F.R. § 560.223 — How do I measure natural gas production for a lease issued in a sale held after November 2000?
Citation: us:cfr:30:560.223
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-560.223
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 560 (Outer Continental Shelf Oil And Gas Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must measure natural gas production subject to the royalty suspension volume for your lease as follows: 5.62 thousand cubic feet of natural gas, measured according to 30 CFR part 250, subpart L, equals one barrel of oil equivalent.
