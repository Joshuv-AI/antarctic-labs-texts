28 C.F.R. § 16.55 — Other rights and services.
Citation: us:cfr:28:16.55
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-16.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 16 (Production Or Disclosure Of Material Or Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart shall be construed to entitle any person, as of right, to any service or to the disclosure of any record to which such person is not entitled under the Privacy Act, the Social Security Fraud Reduction Act, or the Judicial Redress Act.
