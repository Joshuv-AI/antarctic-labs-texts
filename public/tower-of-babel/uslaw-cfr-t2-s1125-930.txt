2 C.F.R. § 1125.930 — Debarring official (DoD supplement to Governmentwide definition at 2 CFR 180.930).

Creator: Department Of Defense

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1125 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DoD Components' debarring officials for nonprocurement transactions are the same officials identified in 48 CFR part 209, subpart 209.4, as debarring officials for procurement contracts.
