36 C.F.R. § 1206.44 — Who is eligible for sub-grants?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1206.44
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1206.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1206 (National Historical Publications And Records Commission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All organizations located within a State that has an active State historical records board and entities defined in § 1206.54 may be eligible, as determined by the board.
