29 C.F.R. § 1987.115 — Special circumstances; waiver of rules.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1987.115
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1987.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1987 (Procedures For Handling Retaliation Complaints Under Section 402 Of The Fda Food Safety Modernization Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In special circumstances not contemplated by the provisions of the rules in this part, or for good cause shown, the ALJ or the ARB on review may, upon application, after three days notice to all parties, waive any rule or issue such orders that justice or the administration of FSMA requires.
