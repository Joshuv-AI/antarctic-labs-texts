46 C.F.R. § 92.05-1 — -1 Fire hazards to be minimized.
Citation: us:cfr:46:92.05-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-92.05-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 92 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The general construction of the vessel shall be such as to minimize fire hazards insofar as is reasonable and practicable.
