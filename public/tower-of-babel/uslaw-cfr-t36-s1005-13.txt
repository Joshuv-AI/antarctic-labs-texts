36 C.F.R. § 1005.13 — Nuisances.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1005.13
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1005.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1005 (Commercial And Private Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The creation or maintenance of a nuisance upon the federally owned lands of the area administered by the Presidio Trust or upon any private lands within the boundaries of the area administered by the Presidio Trust under the exclusive legislative jurisdiction of the United States is prohibited.
