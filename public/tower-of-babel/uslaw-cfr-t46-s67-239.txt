46 C.F.R. § 67.239 — Requirements for assumptions of mortgages.
Citation: us:cfr:46:67.239
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-67.239
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 67 (Documentation Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An assumption of mortgage presented for filing and recording must:
(a) Be signed by or on behalf of each original mortgagor, each mortgagee, and each assuming party; and
(b) Recite the following:
(1) The name and address of each original mortgagor and the interest in the vessel mortgaged; and
(2) The name and address of each assuming party and the interest in the mortgage assumed.
