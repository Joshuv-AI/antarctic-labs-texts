Text Correction

Text Correction

In the original publication, there was a mistake in two texts of Sections 2 and 3.

Original text 1 in Section 2.5:

“Overall, none of the algorithms for inferring malignancy from CNVs achieved accuracy > 61% (Figure 7).”

Original text 2 in Section 3:

“In our study, we found that popular algorithms for identifying cancer cells from inferred CNVs from gene expression data [25,49,50] were only ~60% accurate.”

A correction has been made to Section 2.5 Case 2: Analysis of Gene Expression and Section 3 Discussion:

Corrected text 1 in Section 2.5:

“Overall, none of the algorithms for inferring malignancy from CNVs achieved accuracy > 67% (Figure 7).”

Corrected text 2 in Section 3:

“In our study, we found that popular algorithms for identifying cancer cells from inferred CNVs from gene expression data [25,49,50] were only ~66% accurate.”

The authors state that the scientific conclusions are unaffected. This correction was approved by the Academic Editor. The original publication has also been updated.
