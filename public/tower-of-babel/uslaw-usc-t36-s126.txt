36 U.S.C. § 126 — National Hispanic Heritage Month
Citation: us:usc:36:126
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section126
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part A (Observances And Ceremonies); Chapter 1 (Patriotic And National Observances). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President is requested to issue each year a proclamation—
(1)
designating September 15 through October 15 as National Hispanic Heritage Month; and
(2)
calling on the people of the United States, especially the educational community, to observe National Hispanic Heritage Month with appropriate ceremonies and activities.
