26 C.F.R. § 20.2052-1 — -1 Exemption.

Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-20.2052-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 20 (Estate Tax; Estates Of Decedents Dying After August 16, 1954). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An exemption of $60,000 is allowed as a deduction under section 2052 from the gross estate of a decedent who was a citizen or resident of the United States at the time of his death. For the amount of the exemption allowed as a deduction from the gross estate of a decedent who was a nonresident not a citizen of the United States, see paragraph (a)(3) of § 20.2106-1.
