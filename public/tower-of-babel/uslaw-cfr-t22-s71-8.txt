22 C.F.R. § 71.8 — Assistance to American Red Cross.
Citation: us:cfr:22:71.8
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-71.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 71 (Protection And Welfare Of Citizens And Their Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Officers and employees of the Foreign Service may cooperate fully with the American Red Cross within their respective districts and subject to the limitations prescribed in § 102.806 (22 CFR, 1947 Supp.). They shall, however, avoid taking an active part in the solicitation of memberships or the collection of funds.
