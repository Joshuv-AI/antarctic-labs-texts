12 U.S.C. § 2292 — Annual report to the President and Congress
Citation: us:usc:12:2292
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2292
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 24 (Federal Financing Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Bank shall, as soon as practicable after the end of each fiscal year, transmit to the President and the Congress an annual report of its operations and activities.
