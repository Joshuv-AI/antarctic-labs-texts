On higher order Horadam 3-parameter generalized quaternions Volume: Volume , Number 0,
Gamaliel Morales

Abstract
. Recently, Kuloğlu et al. [ 6 ] introduced the higher order Horadam numbers. In this study, novel 3-parameter generalized quaternion sequences of higher order Horadam numbers, which have not been studied before, are defined by investigating the relationship between generalized quaternions, which are important mathematical objects used in physics and mathematics, and higher order Horadam quaternions, which are extensions of the higher order Horadam numbers to quaternion algebra. Also, the recurrence relations of sequences whose members are higher order Horadam 3-parameter generalized quaternions are described. Furthermore, certain properties of these generalized quaternions are presented, such as the generating and exponential function, summation and Binet formula, and some identities resulting from these quaternions are obtained.

Introduction

Kuloğlu
et al.
in
[
6
]
introduced a number sequence
\left(W_{n}(s)\right)_{n\geq 0}
, called higher order Horadam sequence and
s\geq 1
is a fixed integer. This sequence is defined as follows

(1)

W_{n}(s)=\frac{W_{sn}}{W_{s}}=\frac{1}{A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}}\left[A\alpha^{sn}-B\beta^{sn}\right],

where
W_{n}=\frac{1}{\alpha-\beta}\left[A\alpha^{n}-B\beta^{n}\right]
is the
n
th Horadam number (also
\alpha
and
\beta
are distinct and nonzero real numbers,
A=W_{1}-W_{0}\beta
and
B=W_{1}-W_{0}\alpha
). Moreover, the authors proved that the recurrence relation for the higher order Horadam numbers has the following form

(2)

W_{n+2}(s)=\left(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s}\right)W_{n+1}(s)-\left(\alpha\beta\right)^{s}W_{n}(s),\ \ n\geq 0,

with initial conditions
W_{0}(s)=\frac{1}{A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}}\left[W_{0}(\alpha-\beta)\right]
and
W_{1}(s)=1
. The roots of the characteristic equation
\lambda^{2}-\left(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s}\right)\lambda+\left(\alpha\beta\right)^{s}=0
, associated with the recurrence relation (
2
) are
\alpha^{s}
and
\beta^{s}
. Considering the initial values
W_{0}=0
and
W_{1}=1
in the Horadam sequence, we obtain a particular case of the higher order Horadam numbers, called higher order generalized Fibonacci numbers. This sequence is denoted by

(3)

U_{n}(s)=\frac{U_{sn}}{U_{s}}=\frac{1}{\alpha^{s}-\beta^{s}}\left[\alpha^{sn}-\beta^{sn}\right],

where
\alpha
,
\beta
are given by (
1

\alpha+\beta=1
and
\alpha\beta=-1

F_{n}(s)
. Many interesting properties of the higher order Horadam numbers are given in
[
6
]
. Among others, the well-known are

\left[W_{n}(s)\right]^{2}-W_{n-1}(s)W_{n+1}(s)=AB(\alpha\beta)^{s(n-1)}\left[\frac{\alpha^{s}-\beta^{s}}{A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}}\right]^{2}\ (n\geq 1),

\left[W_{n}(s)\right]^{2}-W_{n-m}(s)W_{n+m}(s)=AB(\alpha\beta)^{s(n-m)}\left[\frac{\alpha^{sm}-\beta^{sm}}{A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}}\right]^{2}

W_{m+1}(s)W_{n}(s)-W_{m}(s)W_{n+1}(s)=AB(\alpha\beta)^{sm}\frac{\left[\alpha^{s}-\beta^{s}\right]\left[\alpha^{s(n-m)}-\beta^{s(n-m)}\right]}{\left[A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}\right]^{2}},

[

\left[U_{n}(s)\right]^{2}-U_{n-m}(s)U_{n+m}(s)=(\alpha\beta)^{s(n-m)}\left[U_{m}(s)\right]^{2}\ (n\geq m)

and

U_{m+1}(s)U_{n}(s)-U_{m}(s)U_{n+1}(s)=(\alpha\beta)^{sm}U_{n-m}(s).

Note that if we replace
s=1

1

3

In this paper, we will use the following identities of the higher order Horadam numbers:

(4)

\sum_{r=0}^{n}W_{r}(s)=\frac{(\alpha\beta)^{s}W_{n}(s)-W_{n+1}(s)+W_{1}(s)+\left(1-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\right)W_{0}(s)}{(\alpha\beta)^{s}-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})+1},

\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}W_{n}(s)\lambda^{n}=\frac{W_{0}(s)+\left[W_{1}(s)-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})W_{0}(s)\right]\lambda}{1-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\lambda+(\alpha\beta)^{s}\lambda^{2}}

(6)

W_{n}(s)=W_{0}(s)U_{n+1}(s)+\left[1-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})W_{0}(s)\right]U_{n}(s).

The next theorems present some basic properties of the higher order Horadam 3-parameter generalized quaternions.

Theorem 2.1
.

Let
n\geq 0
be an integer. Then

\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n+2}(s)=(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n+1}(s)-(\alpha\beta)^{s}\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s),

where
\textnormal{{Q}}W_{0}(s)
and
\textnormal{{Q}}W_{1}(s)
are given in (
7
).

Proof.

By formulas (
7
) and (
2
), we get

\displaystyle(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})

\displaystyle\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n+1}(s)-(\alpha\beta)^{s}\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)

\displaystyle=(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\left[W_{n+1}(s)\textbf{1}+W_{n+2}(s)\textbf{i}+W_{n+3}(s)\textbf{j}+W_{n+4}(s)\textbf{k}\right]

\displaystyle\ \ -(\alpha\beta)^{s}\left[W_{n}(s)\textbf{1}+W_{n+1}(s)\textbf{i}+W_{n+2}(s)\textbf{j}+W_{n+3}(s)\textbf{k}\right]

\displaystyle=\left[\left(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s}\right)W_{n+1}(s)-\left(\alpha\beta\right)^{s}W_{n}(s)\right]\textbf{1}

\displaystyle\ \ +\left[\left(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s}\right)W_{n+2}(s)-\left(\alpha\beta\right)^{s}W_{n+1}(s)\right]\textbf{i}

\displaystyle\ \ +\left[\left(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s}\right)W_{n+3}(s)-\left(\alpha\beta\right)^{s}W_{n+2}(s)\right]\textbf{j}

\displaystyle\ \ +\left[\left(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s}\right)W_{n+4}(s)-\left(\alpha\beta\right)^{s}W_{n+3}(s)\right]\textbf{k}

\displaystyle=W_{n+2}(s)\textbf{1}+W_{n+3}(s)\textbf{i}+W_{n+4}(s)\textbf{j}+W_{n+5}(s)\textbf{k}

\displaystyle=\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n+2}(s),

which ends the proof.
∎

Theorem 2.2
.

Let
n\geq 0
be an integer. Then

\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)\textnormal{{Q}}^{\dagger}W_{n}(s)=\frac{A^{2}\alpha^{2sn}\Phi_{\alpha}(s)-2AB(\alpha\beta)^{sn}\Phi_{\alpha\beta}(s)+B^{2}\beta^{2sn}\Phi_{\beta}(s)}{\left[A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}\right]^{2}},

where
\Phi_{\alpha}(s)=1+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\alpha^{2s}+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{3}\alpha^{4s}+\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\alpha^{6s}
,
\Phi_{\alpha\beta}(s)=1+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}(\alpha\beta)^{s}+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{2s}+\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{3s}
and
\Phi_{\beta}(s)=1+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\beta^{2s}+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{3}\beta^{4s}+\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\beta^{6s}
.

Proof.

Using the definition of the conjugate of a 3-parameter generalized quaternion, we obtain

\displaystyle\left[A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}\right]^{2}\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)\textnormal{{Q}}^{\dagger}W_{n}(s)

\displaystyle=\left[A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}\right]^{2}\left\{\begin{array}[]{c}\left[W_{n}(s)\right]^{2}+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\left[W_{n+1}(s)\right]^{2}\\
+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{3}\left[W_{n+2}(s)\right]^{2}+\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\left[W_{n+3}(s)\right]^{2}\end{array}\right\}

\displaystyle=A^{2}\alpha^{2sn}-2AB(\alpha\beta)^{sn}+B^{2}\beta^{2sn}

\displaystyle\ \ +\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\left[A^{2}\alpha^{2s(n+1)}-2AB(\alpha\beta)^{s(n+1)}+B^{2}\beta^{2s(n+1)}\right]

\displaystyle\ \ +\lambda_{1}\lambda_{3}\left[A^{2}\alpha^{2s(n+2)}-2AB(\alpha\beta)^{s(n+2)}+B^{2}\beta^{2s(n+2)}\right]

\displaystyle\ \ +\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\left[A^{2}\alpha^{2s(n+3)}-2AB(\alpha\beta)^{s(n+3)}+B^{2}\beta^{2s(n+3)}\right]

\displaystyle=A^{2}\alpha^{2sn}\left[1+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\alpha^{2s}+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{3}\alpha^{4s}+\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\alpha^{6s}\right]

\displaystyle\ \ -2AB(\alpha\beta)^{sn}\left[1+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}(\alpha\beta)^{s}+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{2s}+\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{3s}\right]

\displaystyle\ \ +B^{2}\beta^{2sn}\left[1+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\beta^{2s}+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{3}\beta^{4s}+\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\beta^{6s}\right]

\displaystyle=A^{2}\alpha^{2sn}\Phi_{\alpha}(s)-2AB(\alpha\beta)^{sn}\Phi_{\alpha\beta}(s)+B^{2}\beta^{2sn}\Phi_{\beta}(s),

where
\Phi_{\alpha}(s)=1+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\alpha^{2s}+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{3}\alpha^{4s}+\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\alpha^{6s}
,
\Phi_{\alpha\beta}(s)=1+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}(\alpha\beta)^{s}+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{2s}+\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{3s}
and
\Phi_{\beta}(s)=1+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}\beta^{2s}+\lambda_{1}\lambda_{3}\beta^{4s}+\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}\beta^{6s}
. Hence we get the result.
∎

The next theorem gives the Binet formulas for the higher order Horadam 3-parameter generalized quaternions.

Theorem 2.3
.

Let
n\geq 0
be an integer. Binet formulas for
\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)
have the following form

\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)=\frac{A\alpha^{sn}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-B\beta^{sn}\Theta_{\beta}(s)}{A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}},

where
\Theta_{\alpha}(s)=\textnormal{{1}}+\alpha^{s}\textnormal{{i}}+\alpha^{2s}\textnormal{{j}}+\alpha^{3s}\textnormal{{k}}
and
\Theta_{\beta}(s)=\textnormal{{1}}+\beta^{s}\textnormal{{i}}+\beta^{2s}\textnormal{{j}}+\beta^{3s}\textnormal{{k}}
.

Proof.

By formulas (
7
) and (
1
), we get

\displaystyle\left[A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}\right]

\displaystyle\textbf{Q}W_{n}(s)

\displaystyle=\left[A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}\right]\left[W_{n}(s)\textbf{1}+W_{n+1}(s)\textbf{i}+W_{n+2}(s)\textbf{j}+W_{n+3}(s)\textbf{k}\right]

\displaystyle=\left[A\alpha^{sn}-B\beta^{sn}\right]\textbf{1}+\left[A\alpha^{s(n+1)}-B\beta^{s(n+1)}\right]\textbf{i}

\displaystyle\ \ +\left[A\alpha^{s(n+2)}-B\beta^{s(n+2)}\right]\textbf{j}+\left[A\alpha^{s(n+3)}-B\beta^{s(n+3)}\right]\textbf{k}

\displaystyle=A\alpha^{sn}\left[\textbf{1}+\alpha^{s}\textbf{i}+\alpha^{2s}\textbf{j}+\alpha^{3s}\textbf{k}\right]-B\beta^{sn}\left[\textbf{1}+\beta^{s}\textbf{i}+\beta^{2s}\textbf{j}+\beta^{3s}\textbf{k}\right]

\displaystyle=A\alpha^{sn}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-B\beta^{sn}\Theta_{\beta}(s),

where
\Theta_{\alpha}(s)=\textbf{1}+\alpha^{s}\textbf{i}+\alpha^{2s}\textbf{j}+\alpha^{3s}\textbf{k}
and
\Theta_{\beta}(s)=\textbf{1}+\beta^{s}\textbf{i}+\beta^{2s}\textbf{j}+\beta^{3s}\textbf{k}

∎

3.
Catalan, Cassini and d’Ocagne identities for the higher order Horadam 3-parameter generalized quaternions

Now we will give some identities for the higher order Horadam 3-parameter generalized quaternion, these identities are easily proved using the Binet formulas in Theorem
2.3
. Furthermore, using
\Theta_{\alpha}(s)=\textbf{1}+\alpha^{s}\textbf{i}+\alpha^{2s}\textbf{j}+\alpha^{3s}\textbf{k}
and
\Theta_{\beta}(s)=\textbf{1}+\beta^{s}\textbf{i}+\beta^{2s}\textbf{j}+\beta^{3s}\textbf{k}
, we get

\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\Theta_{\beta}(s)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{c}\left[1-\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}(\alpha\beta)^{s}-\lambda_{1}\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{2s}-\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{3s}\right]\textbf{1}\\
+(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\textbf{i}+(\alpha^{2s}+\beta^{2s})\textbf{j}+(\alpha^{s3}+\beta^{3s})\textbf{k}\\
-(\alpha\beta)^{s}(\alpha^{s}-\beta^{s})\left[\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{s}\textbf{i}-\lambda_{2}(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\textbf{j}+\lambda_{1}\textbf{k}\right]\end{array}\right\}

and

\Theta_{\beta}(s)\Theta_{\alpha}(s)=\left\{\begin{array}[]{c}\left[1-\lambda_{1}\lambda_{2}(\alpha\beta)^{s}-\lambda_{1}\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{2s}-\lambda_{2}\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{3s}\right]\textbf{1}\\
+(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\textbf{i}+(\alpha^{2s}+\beta^{2s})\textbf{j}+(\alpha^{s3}+\beta^{3s})\textbf{k}\\
+(\alpha\beta)^{s}(\alpha^{s}-\beta^{s})\left[\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{s}\textbf{i}-\lambda_{2}(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\textbf{j}+\lambda_{1}\textbf{k}\right]\end{array}\right\}.

The two-element commutator,
\Theta_{\beta}(s)
and
\Theta_{\alpha}(s)

(9)

\displaystyle\left[\Theta_{\beta}(s),\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\right]

\displaystyle=\Theta_{\beta}(s)\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\Theta_{\beta}(s)

\displaystyle=2(\alpha\beta)^{s}(\alpha^{s}-\beta^{s})\left[\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{s}\textbf{i}-\lambda_{2}(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\textbf{j}+\lambda_{1}\textbf{k}\right].

(Catalan identity for
\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)

.

Let

\displaystyle-\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n-m}(s)\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n+m}(s)

+\alpha^{sm}\left[\Theta_{\beta}(s),\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\right]\end{array}\right\},

\left[\Theta_{\beta}(s),\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\right]=2(\alpha\beta)^{s}(\alpha^{s}-\beta^{s})\left[\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{s}\textnormal{{i}}-\lambda_{2}(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\textnormal{{j}}+\lambda_{1}\textnormal{{k}}\right]

Proof.

By formula (

2.3
, we get

\displaystyle\left[A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}\right]^{2}\left\{\left[\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)\right]^{2}-\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n-m}(s)\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n+m}(s)\right\}

\displaystyle=\left[A\alpha^{sn}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-B\beta^{sn}\Theta_{\beta}(s)\right]\left[A\alpha^{sn}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-B\beta^{sn}\Theta_{\beta}(s)\right]

Theorem 3.3

(d’Ocagne identity for
\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)
)
.

Let

be integers such that

\displaystyle\textnormal{{Q}}W_{m+1}(s)

\displaystyle\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)-\textnormal{{Q}}W_{m}(s)\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n+1}(s)

\displaystyle=\frac{AB(\alpha\beta)^{sm}\left(\alpha^{s}-\beta^{s}\right)}{\left[A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}\right]^{2}}\left\{\begin{array}[]{c}\left(\alpha^{s(n-m)}-\beta^{s(n-m)}\right)\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\Theta_{\beta}(s)\\

Proof.

By formula (
9
) and Theorem

, we get

\displaystyle\left[A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}\right]^{2}\left[\textnormal{{Q}}W_{m+1}(s)\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)-\textnormal{{Q}}W_{m}(s)\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n+1}(s)\right]

\displaystyle=\left[A\alpha^{s(m+1)}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-B\beta^{s(m+1)}\Theta_{\beta}(s)\right]\left[A\alpha^{sn}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-B\beta^{sn}\Theta_{\beta}(s)\right]

\displaystyle\ \ -\left[A\alpha^{sm}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-B\beta^{sm}\Theta_{\beta}(s)\right]\left[A\alpha^{s(n+1)}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-B\beta^{s(n+1)}\Theta_{\beta}(s)\right]

\displaystyle=-AB\alpha^{s(m+1)}\beta^{sn}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\Theta_{\beta}(s)-AB\alpha^{sn}\beta^{s(m+1)}\Theta_{\beta}(s)\Theta_{\alpha}(s)

\displaystyle\ \ +AB\alpha^{sm}\beta^{s(n+1)}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\Theta_{\beta}(s)+AB\alpha^{s(n+1)}\beta^{sm}\Theta_{\beta}(s)\Theta_{\alpha}(s)

\displaystyle=AB\left(\alpha^{s}-\beta^{s}\right)\left[\alpha^{sn}\beta^{sm}\Theta_{\beta}(s)\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-\alpha^{sm}\beta^{sn}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\Theta_{\beta}(s)\right]

\displaystyle=AB(\alpha\beta)^{sm}\left(\alpha^{s}-\beta^{s}\right)\left[\alpha^{s(n-m)}\Theta_{\beta}(s)\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-\beta^{s(n-m)}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\Theta_{\beta}(s)\right]

\displaystyle=AB(\alpha\beta)^{sm}\left(\alpha^{s}-\beta^{s}\right)\left\{\begin{array}[]{c}\left(\alpha^{s(n-m)}-\beta^{s(n-m)}\right)\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\Theta_{\beta}(s)\\
+\alpha^{s(n-m)}\left[\Theta_{\beta}(s),\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\right]\end{array}\right\},

where
\left[\Theta_{\beta}(s),\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\right]=2(\alpha\beta)^{s}(\alpha^{s}-\beta^{s})\left[\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{s}\textbf{i}-\lambda_{2}(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\textbf{j}+\lambda_{1}\textbf{k}\right]
. Hence we get the result.
∎

Note that for

in Theorem

we have the next identity for the higher order Horadam 3-parameter generalized quaternions.

.

Let
n

\displaystyle\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n+1}(s)\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)

\displaystyle-\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n+1}(s)

.

4.
Other identities of the higher order Horadam 3-parameter generalized quaternions

Theorem 4.1
.

Let
n\geq 0
be an integer. Then the summation formula for the higher order Horadam 3-parameter generalized quaternions is as follows

\sum_{r=0}^{n}\textnormal{{Q}}W_{r}(s)=\frac{(\alpha\beta)^{s}\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)-\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n+1}(s)+\sigma(s)\Pi}{(\alpha\beta)^{s}-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})+1}-\Phi,

where

\displaystyle\sigma(s)

\displaystyle=W_{1}(s)+\left(1-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\right)W_{0}(s),

\displaystyle\Pi

\displaystyle=\textnormal{{1}}+\textnormal{{i}}+\textnormal{{j}}+\textnormal{{k}},

\displaystyle\Phi

\displaystyle=W_{0}(s)\textnormal{{i}}+\left[W_{0}(s)+W_{1}(s)\right]\textnormal{{j}}+\left[(1-(\alpha\beta)^{s})W_{0}(s)+(1+\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})W_{1}(s)\right]\textnormal{{k}},

and
W_{0}(s)
and
W_{1}(s)
defined as in (
1
).

Proof.

Using formulas (
7
) and (
4
), we get

\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n}\left[W_{r}(s)\textbf{1}+W_{r+1}(s)\textbf{i}+W_{r+2}(s)\textbf{j}+W_{r+3}(s)\textbf{k}\right]

\displaystyle=\textbf{1}\sum_{r=0}^{n}W_{r}(s)+\textbf{i}\sum_{r=1}^{n+1}W_{r}(s)+\textbf{j}\sum_{r=2}^{n+2}W_{r}(s)+\textbf{k}\sum_{r=3}^{n+3}W_{r}(s)

\displaystyle=\left[\frac{(\alpha\beta)^{s}W_{n}(s)-W_{n+1}(s)+\sigma(s)}{(\alpha\beta)^{s}-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})+1}\right]\textbf{1}

\displaystyle\ \ +\left[\frac{(\alpha\beta)^{s}W_{n+1}(s)-W_{n+2}(s)+\sigma(s)}{(\alpha\beta)^{s}-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})+1}-W_{0}(s)\right]\textbf{i}

\displaystyle\ \ +\left[\frac{(\alpha\beta)^{s}W_{n+2}(s)-W_{n+3}(s)+\sigma(s)}{(\alpha\beta)^{s}-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})+1}-W_{0}(s)-W_{1}(s)\right]\textbf{j}

\displaystyle\ \ +\left[\frac{(\alpha\beta)^{s}W_{n+3}(s)-W_{n+4}(s)+\sigma(s)}{(\alpha\beta)^{s}-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})+1}-W_{0}(s)-W_{1}(s)-W_{2}(s)\right]\textbf{k}

\displaystyle=\frac{(\alpha\beta)^{s}\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)-\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n+1}(s)+\sigma(s)\Pi}{(\alpha\beta)^{s}-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})+1}-\Phi,

where
\sigma(s)=W_{1}(s)+\left(1-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\right)W_{0}(s)
,
\Pi=\textbf{1}+\textbf{i}+\textbf{j}+\textbf{k}
and
\Phi=W_{0}(s)\textnormal{{i}}+\left[W_{0}(s)+W_{1}(s)\right]\textnormal{{j}}+\left[(1-(\alpha\beta)^{s})W_{0}(s)+(1+\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})W_{1}(s)\right]\textnormal{{k}}
.
∎

,

+2\alpha^{s(n-m+1)}\beta^{s}\left[\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{s}\textnormal{{i}}-\lambda_{2}(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\textnormal{{j}}+\lambda_{1}\textnormal{{k}}\right]\end{array}\right\}.

\displaystyle\textnormal{{Q}}W_{m}(s)\textnormal{{Q}}U_{n}(s)-\textnormal{{Q}}U_{m}(s)\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)

\displaystyle=\left[\frac{A\alpha^{sm}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-B\beta^{sm}\Theta_{\beta}(s)}{A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}}\right]\left[\frac{\alpha^{sn}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-\beta^{sn}\Theta_{\beta}(s)}{\alpha^{s}-\beta^{s}}\right]

\displaystyle=\frac{(A-B)\left(\alpha^{sn}\beta^{sm}\Theta_{\beta}(s)\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-\alpha^{sm}\beta^{sn}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\Theta_{\beta}(s)\right)}{\left[\alpha^{s}-\beta^{s}\right]\left[A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}\right]}

\displaystyle=\frac{(A-B)(\alpha\beta)^{sm}\left(\alpha^{s(n-m)}\Theta_{\beta}(s)\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-\beta^{s(n-m)}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\Theta_{\beta}(s)\right)}{\left[\alpha^{s}-\beta^{s}\right]\left[A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}\right]}.

\displaystyle\textnormal{{Q}}W_{m}(s)\textnormal{{Q}}U_{n}(s)-\textnormal{{Q}}U_{m}(s)\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)

\displaystyle\ \ +\frac{2(A-B)(\alpha\beta)^{s(m+1)}\alpha^{s(n-m)}\left[\lambda_{3}(\alpha\beta)^{s}\textbf{i}-\lambda_{2}(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\textbf{j}+\lambda_{1}\textbf{k}\right]}{A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}}

5.
Generating functions and matrix generators

In this section, we will give the generating functions for the higher order Horadam 3-parameter generalized quaternions. Similarly like Horadam and higher order Horadam sequence in (
5
), these sequences can be considered as the coefficients of the power series expansion of the corresponding generating functions.

Theorem 5.1
.

The generating function of the higher order Horadam 3-parameter generalized quaternions has the following form

g\left(\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s);\lambda\right)=\frac{A\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-B\Theta_{\beta}(s)-\left[A\beta^{s}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-B\alpha^{s}\Theta_{\beta}(s)\right]\lambda}{\left[A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}\right]\left[1-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\lambda+(\alpha\beta)^{s}\lambda^{2}\right]}.

Proof.

Let

g\left(\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s);\lambda\right)=\textnormal{{Q}}W_{0}(s)+\textnormal{{Q}}W_{1}(s)\lambda+\textnormal{{Q}}W_{2}(s)\lambda^{2}+\cdots

be the generating function of the higher order Horadam 3-parameter generalized quaternions. Hence

\displaystyle(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\lambda g\left(\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s);\lambda\right)

\displaystyle=(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\textnormal{{Q}}W_{0}(s)\lambda+(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\textnormal{{Q}}W_{1}(s)\lambda^{2}+\cdots

\displaystyle(\alpha\beta)^{s}\lambda^{2}g\left(\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s);\lambda\right)

\displaystyle=(\alpha\beta)^{s}\textnormal{{Q}}W_{0}(s)\lambda^{2}+(\alpha\beta)^{s}\textnormal{{Q}}W_{1}(s)\lambda^{3}+\cdots.

Using the recurrence (
2
), we get

\displaystyle\left[1-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\lambda+(\alpha\beta)^{s}\lambda^{2}\right]

\displaystyle g\left(\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s);\lambda\right)

\displaystyle=\textnormal{{Q}}W_{0}(s)+\left[\textnormal{{Q}}W_{1}(s)-(a^{s}+\beta^{s})\textnormal{{Q}}W_{0}(s)\right]\lambda.

Thus

g\left(\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s);\lambda\right)=\frac{\textnormal{{Q}}W_{0}(s)+\left[\textnormal{{Q}}W_{1}(s)-(a^{s}+\beta^{s})\textnormal{{Q}}W_{0}(s)\right]\lambda}{1-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\lambda+(\alpha\beta)^{s}\lambda^{2}}.

Using Theorem
2.3
, we obtain

\displaystyle\textnormal{{Q}}W_{0}(s)

\displaystyle=\frac{A\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-B\Theta_{\beta}(s)}{A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}},

\displaystyle\textnormal{{Q}}W_{1}(s)

\displaystyle=\frac{A\alpha^{s}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-B\beta^{s}\Theta_{\beta}(s)}{A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}}.

Finally, we can write

g\left(\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s);\lambda\right)=\frac{A\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-B\Theta_{\beta}(s)-\left[A\beta^{s}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-B\alpha^{s}\Theta_{\beta}(s)\right]\lambda}{\left[A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}\right]\left[1-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\lambda+(\alpha\beta)^{s}\lambda^{2}\right]}.

∎

In the same way we can prove the next results.

Corollary 5.2
.

The generating function of the higher order generalized Fibonacci 3-parameter generalized quaternions has the following form

g\left(\textnormal{{Q}}U_{n}(s);\lambda\right)=\frac{\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-\Theta_{\beta}(s)-\left[\beta^{s}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-\alpha^{s}\Theta_{\beta}(s)\right]\lambda}{\left[\alpha^{s}-\beta^{s}\right]\left[1-(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})\lambda+(\alpha\beta)^{s}\lambda^{2}\right]}.

Theorem 5.3
.

The exponential generating function of the higher order Horadam 3-parameter generalized quaternions is

\sum_{n\geq 0}\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)\frac{\lambda^{n}}{n!}=\frac{A\Theta_{\alpha}(s)e^{\alpha^{s}\lambda}-B\Theta_{\beta}(s)e^{\beta^{s}\lambda}}{A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}}.

Proof.

Using Theorem
2.3
, we have

\displaystyle\sum_{n\geq 0}\textnormal{{Q}}W_{n}(s)\frac{\lambda^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\sum_{n\geq 0}\left[\frac{A\alpha^{sn}\Theta_{\alpha}(s)-B\beta^{sn}\Theta_{\beta}(s)}{A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}}\right]\frac{\lambda^{n}}{n!}

\displaystyle=\frac{A\Theta_{\alpha}(s)\sum_{n\geq 0}\alpha^{sn}\frac{\lambda^{n}}{n!}-B\Theta_{\beta}(s)\sum_{n\geq 0}\beta^{sn}\frac{\lambda^{n}}{n!}}{A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}}

\displaystyle=\frac{A\Theta_{\alpha}(s)e^{\alpha^{s}\lambda}-B\Theta_{\beta}(s)e^{\beta^{s}\lambda}}{A\alpha^{s}-B\beta^{s}}.

∎

Now, we introduce a matrix generator for the higher order Horadam numbers. The higher order Horadam

.

Let

be the higher order Horadam matrix given in (

\left[\textnormal{{H}}(s)\right]^{n}=\left[\begin{array}[]{rr}U_{n+1}(s)&-(\alpha\beta)^{s}U_{n}(s)\\
U_{n}(s)&-(\alpha\beta)^{s}U_{n-1}(s)\end{array}\right].

Proof.

If

\displaystyle\left[\textnormal{{H}}(s)\right]^{n+1}

\displaystyle=\left[\textnormal{{H}}(s)\right]^{n}\textnormal{{H}}(s)

\displaystyle=\left[\begin{array}[]{rr}U_{n+1}(s)&-(\alpha\beta)^{s}U_{n}(s)\\
U_{n}(s)&-(\alpha\beta)^{s}U_{n-1}(s)\end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}[]{rr}\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s}&-(\alpha\beta)^{s}\\
1&0\end{array}\right]

\displaystyle=\left[\begin{array}[]{rr}(\alpha^{s}+\beta^{s})U_{n+1}(s)-(\alpha\beta)^{s}U_{n}(s)&-(\alpha\beta)^{s}U_{n+1}(s)\\

U_{n+1}(s)&-(\alpha\beta)^{s}U_{n}(s)\end{array}\right].

Thus, the proof is completed.
∎
