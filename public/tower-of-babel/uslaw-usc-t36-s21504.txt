36 U.S.C. § 21504 — Acquisition and management of property
Citation: us:usc:36:21504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section21504
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 215 (American Hospital Of Paris). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property, in the United States and France, to carry out the purposes of the corporation.
