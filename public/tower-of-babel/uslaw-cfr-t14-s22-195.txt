14 C.F.R. § 22.195 — Ground and flight testing.
Citation: us:cfr:14:22.195
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-22.195
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 22 (Design, Production, And Airworthiness Requirements For Non-Type Certificated Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The aircraft must have been ground and flight tested under documented production acceptance test procedures to—
(a) Verify aircraft performance data;
(b) Ensure the aircraft has no hazardous operating characteristics;
(c) Ensure the aircraft is in a condition for safe operation; and
(d) Ensure the aircraft can safely conduct towing and any aerial work operation designated by the manufacturer.
