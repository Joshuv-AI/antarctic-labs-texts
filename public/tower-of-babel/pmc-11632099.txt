Publisher Correction: Diversity and inclusion have greater support than most Americans think
Authors: Naomi Isenberg, Markus Brauer
Published: 2024

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-76761-8, published online 19 November 2024

The legend of Figure 1 in the original version of this Article was incomplete.

The legend of Figure 1:

“Participants’ perceived agreement with the diversity statements in Studies 1–4.”

now reads:

“Participants’ perceived agreement with the diversity statements in Studies 1-4. All statements expressed a position in favor of diversity and inclusion, except in Study 4 where half of the statements communicated an anti-diversity position. The blue “violins” show participants’ perceived agreement averaged across the statements used in the study. The thick horizontal lines show actual agreement.”

The original Article has been corrected.
