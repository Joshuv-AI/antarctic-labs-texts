43 C.F.R. § 3215.11 — Must I replace my bond after BLM collects against it?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3215.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3215.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If BLM collects against your bond, before you conduct any further operations you must either:
(a) Post a new bond equal to the value of the original bond; or
(b) Restore your existing bond to the original face amount.
