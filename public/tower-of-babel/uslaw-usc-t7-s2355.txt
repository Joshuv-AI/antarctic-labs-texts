7 U.S.C. § 2355 — Effect of defective execution
Citation: us:usc:7:2355
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2355
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 57 (Plant Variety Protection); Subchapter I (Plant Variety Protection Office); Part B (Legal Provisions As To The Plant Variety Protection Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any document to be filed in the Plant Variety Protection Office and which is required by any law or regulation to be executed in a specified manner may be provisionally accepted by the Secretary despite a defective execution, provided a properly executed document is submitted within such time as may be prescribed.
