37 C.F.R. § 501.4 — Determination of inventions and rights.
Citation: us:cfr:37:501.4
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-501.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 501 (Uniform Patent Policy For Rights In Inventions Made By Government Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each Government agency has the approval of the Secretary to determine whether the results of research, development, or other activity in the agency constitute an invention within the purview of Executive Order 10096, as amended by Executive Order 10930 and Executive Order 10695, and to determine the rights in and to the invention in accordance with the provisions of §§ 501.6 and 501.7.
