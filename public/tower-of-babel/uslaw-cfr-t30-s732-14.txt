30 C.F.R. § 732.14 — Resubmission of State programs.
Citation: us:cfr:30:732.14
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-732.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 732 (Procedures And Criteria For Approval Or Disapproval Of State Program Submissions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If, by a final decision, the program is disapproved, the State may submit another proposed State program to the Director at any time. Resubmitted State programs must meet the requirements of § 731.14 and will be acted upon pursuant to §§ 732.11-732.16.
