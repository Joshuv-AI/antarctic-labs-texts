34 C.F.R. § 381.1 — What is the Protection and Advocacy of Individual Rights program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:381.1
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-381.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 381 (Protection And Advocacy Of Individual Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This program is designed to support a system in each State to protect the legal and human rights of eligible individuals with disabilities.
