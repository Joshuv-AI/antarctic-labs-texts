42 U.S.C. § 12898 — Implementation
Citation: us:usc:42:12898
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12898
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 130 (National Affordable Housing); Subchapter IV (Hope For Homeownership Of Multifamily And Single Family Homes); Part B (Hope For Homeownership Of Single Family Homes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than the expiration of the 180-day period beginning on the date funds authorized under this part first become available for obligation, the Secretary shall by notice establish such requirements as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this part. Such requirements shall be subject to section 553 of title 5. The Secretary shall issue regulations based on the initial notice before the expiration of the 8-month period beginning on the date of the notice.
