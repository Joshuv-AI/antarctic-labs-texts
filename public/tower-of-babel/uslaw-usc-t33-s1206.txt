33 U.S.C. § 1206 — Exemptions; terms and conditions
Citation: us:usc:33:1206
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1206
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 24 (Vessel Bridge-To-Bridge Communication). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may, if he considers that marine navigational safety will not be adversely affected or where a local communication system fully complies with the intent of this concept but does not conform in detail, issue exemptions from any provisions of this chapter, on such terms and conditions as he considers appropriate.
