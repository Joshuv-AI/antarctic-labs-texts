8 C.F.R. § 215.21 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:8:215.21
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-215.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 215 (Controls Of Aliens Departing From The United States; Electronic Visa Update System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to establish an electronic visa update system for nonimmigrant aliens who hold a passport issued by an identified country containing a U.S. nonimmigrant visa of a designated category.
