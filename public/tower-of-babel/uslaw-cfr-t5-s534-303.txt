5 C.F.R. § 534.303 — Basic pay for executive level positions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:534.303
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-534.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 534 (Pay Under Other Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Rates of basic pay for executive level positions of temporary organizations may not exceed the rate for level III of the Executive Schedule.
