2 C.F.R. § 1201.108 — Inquiries.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1201.108
Creator: Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1201.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1201 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inquiries regarding Part 1201 should be addressed to the DOT Component making the award, cognizant agency for indirect costs, cognizant or oversight agency for audit, or pass-through entities as appropriate. DOT Components will, in turn, direct the inquiry to the Office of Chief Financial Officer, Department of Transportation.
