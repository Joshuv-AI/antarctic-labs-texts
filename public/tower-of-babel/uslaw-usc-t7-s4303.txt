7 U.S.C. § 4303 — Floral research and promotion orders
Citation: us:usc:7:4303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section4303
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 74 (Floral Research And Consumer Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To effectuate the declared policy of this chapter, the Secretary shall, subject to the provisions of this chapter, issue and, from time to time, may amend orders applicable to persons engaged in production, sale, importation, or handling of flowers and plants. Such orders shall be applicable to all production or marketing areas, or both, in the United States.
