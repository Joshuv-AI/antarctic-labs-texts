36 C.F.R. § 1235.3 — What standards are used as guidance for this part?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1235.3
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1235.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1235 (Transfer Of Records To The National Archives Of The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations conform to guidance provided in ISO 15489-1:2001. Paragraphs 8.3 (Designing and implementing records systems), 9.6 (Storage and handling), and 9.7 (Access) are particularly relevant to this part.
