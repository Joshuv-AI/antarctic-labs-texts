12 C.F.R. § 619.9155 — Federal land credit association.
Citation: us:cfr:12:619.9155
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-619.9155
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 619 (Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term Federal land credit association refers to a Federal land bank association that has received a transfer of direct long-term real estate lending authority pursuant to section 7.6 of the Act.
