7 C.F.R. § 1455.10 — Eligible grant applicants.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1455.10
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1455.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1455 (Voluntary Public Access And Habitat Incentive Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A State or Tribal government may apply for a VPA-HIP grant.
(b) Any applications received by an individual or entity that is not a State or tribal government will not be considered.
