27 C.F.R. § 26.164a — a Package gauge record.
Citation: us:cfr:27:26.164a
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-26.164a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 26 (Liquors And Articles From Puerto Rico And The Virgin Islands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When required in this part, with respect to Puerto Rican spirits, a package gauge record shall be prepared to show:
(a) The date prepared;
(b) The related transaction form and its serial number;
(c) The producer or rectifier (processor) of the spirits, and his name, address, and plant registration number; and
(d) For each package, the:
(1) Package identification or serial number;
(2) Kind of spirits;
(3) Gross weight;
(4) Tare;
(5) Net weight;
(6) Proof gallons; and
(7) Proof.
