36 C.F.R. § 1202.10 — Does NARA handle access to and disclosure of records of defunct agencies in the custody of NARA?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1202.10
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1202.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1202 (Regulations Implementing The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, records of defunct agencies in the custody of NARA at a NARA record center are covered by the provisions of this part.
