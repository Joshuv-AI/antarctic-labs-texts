41 C.F.R. § 102-38.115 — -38.115 State and local personal property sales.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-38.115
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-38.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-38 (38—Sale Of Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may sell Government personal property to State and local governments through:
(a) Competitive sale to the public;
(b) Negotiated sale, through the appropriate SASP; or
(c) Negotiated sale at fixed price through the appropriate SASP. This method of sale can be used prior to a competitive sale to the public.
