5 C.F.R. § 572.103 — Recordkeeping.
Citation: us:cfr:5:572.103
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-572.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 572 (Travel And Transportation Expenses; New Appointees And Interviews). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each agency will maintain records of payments made under this authority and will make those records available to OPM on request.
