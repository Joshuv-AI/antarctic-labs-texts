12 C.F.R. § 192.455 — Initial balance of liquidation account.
Citation: us:cfr:12:192.455
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-192.455
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 192 (Conversions From Mutual To Stock Form). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The initial balance of the liquidation account is the savings association's net worth in the statement of financial condition included in the final offering circular.
