7 C.F.R. § 3555.205 — Special requirements for condominiums.
Citation: us:cfr:7:3555.205
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-3555.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 3555 (Guaranteed Rural Housing Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Loans may be guaranteed for condominium units in condominium projects that meet all the requirements of this part, as well as the standards for condominium standards established by HUD, Fannie Mae, VA, or Freddie Mac, including those related to self-certification, warranty, underwriting, and ineligible condominium projects.
