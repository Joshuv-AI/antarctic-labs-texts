36 C.F.R. § 1212.505 — How are violations of this part determined for recipients who are individuals?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1212.505
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1212.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1212 (Governmentwide Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual recipient is in violation of the requirements of this part if the Archivist of the United States or designee determines, in writing, that—
(a) The recipient has violated the requirements of subpart C of this part; or
(b) The recipient is convicted of a criminal drug offense resulting from a violation occurring during the conduct of any award activity.
