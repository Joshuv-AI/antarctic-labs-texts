16 U.S.C. § 460lll–48 — Tennessee Valley Authority transfer costs
Citation: us:usc:16:460lll–48
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460lll–48
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter CXXIII (Land Between The Lakes Protection); Part C (Transfer Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any costs incurred by Tennessee Valley Authority associated with the transfer under this part shall be derived from funding described in section 460lll–49 of this title.
