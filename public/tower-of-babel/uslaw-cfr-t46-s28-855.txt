46 C.F.R. § 28.855 — Electrical distribution systems.
Citation: us:cfr:46:28.855
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-28.855
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 28 (Requirements For Commercial Fishing Industry Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each electrical distribution system which has a neutral bus or conductor must have the neutral bus or conductor grounded.
(b) A grounded electrical distribution system must have only one connection to ground. This ground connection must be at the switchboard.
