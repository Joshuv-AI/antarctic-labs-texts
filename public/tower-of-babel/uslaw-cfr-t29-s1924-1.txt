29 C.F.R. § 1924.1 — Applicable safety standards.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1924.1
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1924.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1924 (Safety Standards Applicable To Workshops And Rehabilitation Facilities Assisted By Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The safety standards provided in 41 CFR part 50-204 shall have effect to the extent applicable to any workshop or rehabilitation facility assisted by a grant pursuant to section 12 or section 13 of the Vocational Rehabilitation Act Amendments of 1965, 79 Stat. 1284, 1286.
