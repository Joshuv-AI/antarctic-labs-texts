7 C.F.R. § 1425.2 — Administration.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1425.2
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1425.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1425 (Cooperative Marketing Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) On behalf of the Commodity Credit Corporation (CCC), the Farm Service Agency (FSA) will administer the provisions of this part under the general direction and supervision of the Deputy Administrator for Farm Programs.
(b) In the field, the provisions of this part will be administered by the State and county FSA committees.
