10 U.S.C. § 9431 — Establishment; Superintendent; faculty
Citation: us:usc:10:9431
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9431
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training); Chapter 953 (United States Air Force Academy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There is in the Department of the Air Force an Air Force Academy (hereinafter in this chapter referred to as the “Academy”) for the instruction and preparation for military service of selected persons called “cadets”. The organization of the Academy shall be prescribed by the Secretary of the Air Force.
(b)
There shall be at the Academy the following:
(1)
A Superintendent.
(2)
A Dean of the Faculty.
(3)
A Commandant of Cadets.
(4)
25 permanent professors.
(5)
A chaplain.
(6)
A director of admissions.
