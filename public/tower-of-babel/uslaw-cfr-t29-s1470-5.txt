29 C.F.R. § 1470.5 — Effect on other issuances.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1470.5
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1470.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1470 (Uniform Administrative Requirements For Grants And Cooperative Agreements To State And Local Governments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All other grants administration provisions of codified program regulations, program manuals, handbooks and other nonregulatory materials which are inconsistent with this part are superseded, except to the extent they are required by statute, or authorized in accordance with the exception provision in § 1470.6.
