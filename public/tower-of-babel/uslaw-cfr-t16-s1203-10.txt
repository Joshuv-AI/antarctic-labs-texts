16 C.F.R. § 1203.10 — Selecting the test headform.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1203.10
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1203.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1203 (Safety Standard For Bicycle Helmets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A helmet shall be tested on the smallest of the headforms appropriate for the helmet sample. A headform size is appropriate for a helmet if all of the helmet's sizing pads are partially compressed when the helmet is equipped with its thickest sizing pads and positioned correctly on the reference headform.
