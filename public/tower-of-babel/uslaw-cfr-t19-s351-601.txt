19 C.F.R. § 351.601 — Annual list and quarterly update of subsidies.
Citation: us:cfr:19:351.601
Creator: International Trade Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-351.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 351 (Antidumping And Countervailing Duties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary will make the determinations called for by section 702(a) of the Trade Agreements Act of 1979, as amended (19 U.S.C. 1202 note) based on the available information, and will publish the annual list and quarterly updates described in such section in the Federal Register.
