Introduction

All graphs considered in this paper are finite, simple, and undirected.
A graph

The

The clique number

of

is the maximum integer

such that

For a graph

for every induced subgraph

graph.
For a graph

is

-free
if

Let

be a family of graphs. We say that

-free

-free for every member

of
\cal F

If there exists a function

\cal{F}
is
\chi
-
bounded class
, and call

a

of

.
The concept of

-boundedness was raised by Gyárfás in 1975
[
10
]
.
Studying what families of graphs are

-bounded, and finding the optimal binding function for
\chi
-bounded class are important problems in this area.
Since the clique number is a trivial lower bound of the chromatic number, if a family of
\chi
-bounded graphs has a linear binding function, then it must be asymptotically optimal up to a constant factor.
We refer the readers to
[
20
]
for a survey on
\chi
-bounded problems.

Erdős
[
9
]
showed that for any positive integers

This result motivates the study of the chromatic number of

-free graphs for some

Based on this, Gyárfás
[

[

-bounded.
Due to
[

, if no member of

is a forest, then a necessary condition for

-free graphs is that

Hence, it is natural to consider the case when

contains infinite number of induced cycles.

A hole in a graph is an induced cycle of length at least
4
.
A hole is said to be

Addario-Berry et al.
[

, proved that every even hole free graph has a vertex whose neighbors are the union of two cliques, which implies that

However, the situation becomes much more complicated for odd hole free graphs.
The Strong Perfect Graph Theorem

asserts that a graph is perfect if and only if it induces neither odd holes nor their complements.
Confirming a conjecture of Gyárfás
[

]

proved that odd hole free graphs are
\chi
-bounded with binding function
\frac{2^{2^{\omega(G)+2}}}{48(\omega(G)+2)}
.
Hoáng and McDiarmid
[

conjectured that

for an odd hole free graph
G
.
A graph is said to be

if every hole has length 4. Sivaraman
[
16
]
conjectured that

for all short-holed graphs whereas the best known upper bound is
\chi(G)\leq 10^{20}2^{\omega(G)^{2}}
due to Scott and Seymour
[
20
]
.
Morever, Scott and Seymour
[
18
]
proved that for every integer

, there exists

has

holes of consecutive lengths.
In addition, Scott and Seymour
[

-bounded.

The study on the chromatic number of graphs with odd hole of exactly one length witnesses much progress recently.
The girth of a graph

A graph is
\ell
-holed
if all of its induced cycles of length at least four have length exactly
\ell
.
Cook et al.
[
8
]
gave a complete description of the
\ell
-holed graphs for every

.
It is clearly that for every

In this paper, we prove the following.

Theorem 1.1

For an odd integer
\ell\geq 7
, let

be an
\ell
-holed graph. Then
\chi(G)\leq{\lceil{\ell\over{\ell-1}}\omega(G)\rceil}
.

Note that Theorem
1.1
improves the upper bound

when

for
\ell
-holed graphs with odd
\ell\geq 7
.
It is worthwhile to mention that Theorem

is sharp:
consider the clique blow-up of

(replacing every vertex of

by a clique of arbitrary size).

Now we briefly sketch the proof of Theorem

.
By the structural description of the
\ell
-holed graphs, we know that

is either a blow-up of a cycle of length
\ell
, or a blow-up of an
\ell
-framework.
When

is a blow-up of an
\ell
-cycle, we give an explicit coloring with chromatic number at most

.
When

is a blow-up of an
\ell
-framework, we prove by contradiction.
Let

be a minimal counterexample.
We can deduce that
m\leq 4
.
Then in each case we give a specific coloring of

The organization of this paper is as follows.
In Section 2, we give the structure of
\ell
-holed graphs and some useful lemmas.
In Section 3, we introduce two colorings: cyclic coloring and balanced coloring,
which will be applied several times in the subsequent proof.
We present an explicit coloring to show that the blow-up of
\ell

Finally, we prove that the blow-up of
