42 U.S.C. § 239h — Relationship to other laws
Citation: us:usc:42:239h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section239h
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter I (Administration And Miscellaneous Provisions); Part C (Smallpox Emergency Personnel Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as explicitly provided herein, nothing in this part shall be construed to override or limit any rights an individual may have to seek compensation, benefits, or redress under any other provision of Federal or State law.
