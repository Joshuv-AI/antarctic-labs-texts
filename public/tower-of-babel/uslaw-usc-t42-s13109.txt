42 U.S.C. § 13109 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:13109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section13109
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 133 (Pollution Prevention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated to the Administrator $8,000,000 for each of the fiscal years 1991, 1992, and 1993 for functions carried out under this chapter (other than State Grants),1 and $8,000,000 for each of the fiscal years 1991, 1992, and 1993, for grant programs to States issued pursuant to section 13104 of this title.
