22 C.F.R. § 51.41 — Documentary evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:22:51.41
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-51.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 51 (Passports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant must provide documentary evidence that he or she is a U.S. citizen or non-citizen national.
