16 U.S.C. § 460ll–2 — Rattlesnake National Recreation Area
Citation: us:usc:16:460ll–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460ll–2
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XCVI (Rattlesnake National Recreation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An area of land as generally depicted as the “Rattlesnake National Recreation Area” on a map entitled “Rattlesnake National Recreation area and Wilderness—Proposed”, and dated October 1, 1980, is hereby established as the Rattle­snake National Recreation Area.
