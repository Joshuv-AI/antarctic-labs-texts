48 C.F.R. § 835.010 — 835.010 Scientific and technical reports.
Citation: us:cfr:48:835.010
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 835 (Research And Development Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 852.235-75, Scientific and Technical Reports, in all research and development (R&D) solicitations and contracts.
