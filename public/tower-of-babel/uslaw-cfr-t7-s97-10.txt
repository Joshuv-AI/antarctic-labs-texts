7 C.F.R. § 97.10 — Parts of an application to be filed together.
Citation: us:cfr:7:97.10
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-97.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 97 (Plant Variety And Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All parts of an application, including exhibits, should be submitted to the Office together, otherwise, each part shall be accurately and clearly referenced to the application.
