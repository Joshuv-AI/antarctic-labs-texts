47 C.F.R. § 18.303 — Prohibited frequency bands.
Citation: us:cfr:47:18.303
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-18.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 18 (Industrial, Scientific, And Medical Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Operation of ISM equipment within the following safety, search and rescue frequency bands is prohibited: 490-510 kHz, 2170-2194 kHz, 8354-8374 kHz, 121.4-121.6 MHz, 156.7-156.9 MHz, and 242.8-243.2 MHz.
