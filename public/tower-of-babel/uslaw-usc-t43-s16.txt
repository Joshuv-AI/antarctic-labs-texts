43 U.S.C. § 16 — Engrossing and recording patents
Citation: us:usc:43:16
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section16
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 1 (Bureau Of Land Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The engrossing and recording of patents for public lands may be done by means of typewriters or other machines, under regulations to be made by the Secretary of the Interior and approved by the President.
