19 C.F.R. § 113.44 — Assent of sureties to an extension of a bond.
Citation: us:cfr:19:113.44
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-113.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 113 (Cbp Bonds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Extension prescribed by law or regulations. The assent of the sureties to any extension of the period prescribed in a bond is not necessary when the extension is authorized by law or regulations.
(b) Other extension. The assent of the sureties must be obtained before any extension of the period prescribed in a bond other than an extension authorized by law or regulation, is allowed.
