42 C.F.R. § 137.123 — Once stable base funding is negotiated, do funding amounts change from year to year?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.123
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.123
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Stable base funding amounts are subject to adjustment:
(a) Annually only to reflect changes in Congressional appropriations by sub-sub activity excluding earmarks;
(b) By mutual agreement of the Self-Governance Tribe and the Secretary; or
(c) As a result of full or partial retrocession or reassumption.
