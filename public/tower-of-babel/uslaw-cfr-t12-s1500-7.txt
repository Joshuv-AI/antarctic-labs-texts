12 C.F.R. § 1500.7 — How do the statutory cross marketing and sections 23A and B limitations apply to merchant banking investments?
Citation: us:cfr:12:1500.7
Creator: Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1500.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1500 (Merchant Banking Investments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Certain cross-marketing limitations and limitations under sections 23A and 23B of the Federal Reserve Act (12 U.S.C. 371c, 371c-1) applicable to merchant banking investments are set forth in the Board's Regulation Y, 12 CFR 225.176.
