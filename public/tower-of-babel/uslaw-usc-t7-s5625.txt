7 U.S.C. § 5625 — Combination of programs
Citation: us:usc:7:5625
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section5625
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 87 (Export Promotion); Subchapter II (Agricultural Export Programs); Part A (Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commodity Credit Corporation may carry out a program under which commercial export credit guarantees available under section 5622 of this title are combined with direct credits from the Commodity Credit Corporation under section 5621 of this title to reduce the effective rate of interest on export sales of agricultural commodities.
