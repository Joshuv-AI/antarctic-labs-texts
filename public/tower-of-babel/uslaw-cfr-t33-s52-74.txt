33 C.F.R. § 52.74 — Report of settlement.
Citation: us:cfr:33:52.74
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-52.74
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 52 (Board For Correction Of Military Records Of The Coast Guard). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When payment is made pursuant to the order of the Board, the Board may request the Coast Guard to notify it of the name of any person to whom payment was made and of the amount of the payment.
