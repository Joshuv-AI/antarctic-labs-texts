43 C.F.R. § 3120.33 — Agency inventory of leasing.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3120.33
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3120.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3120 (Competitive Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Until August 16, 2032, the BLM will from time to time calculate, for the preceding 1-year period before it issues a wind or solar energy right-of-way, the acreage for which expressions of interest have been submitted to the BLM and the sum total of acres offered for lease.
