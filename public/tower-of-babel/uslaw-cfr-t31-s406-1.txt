31 C.F.R. § 406.1 — Secret Service officers authorized to make seizures of gold.
Citation: us:cfr:31:406.1
Creator: Secret Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-406.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 406 (Seizure And Forfeiture Of Gold For Violations Of Gold Reserve Act Of 1934 And Gold Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All agents of the U.S. Secret Service, in addition to officers of the customs, are hereby authorized and designated to seize any gold which may be subject to forfeiture for violations of the Gold Reserve Act of 1934 (31 U.S.C. 440-445) and the Gold Regulations.
