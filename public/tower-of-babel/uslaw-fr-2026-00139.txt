DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

[Project No. 15407-000]


Nightfall Renewables Inc.; Notice of Effectiveness of Withdrawal 
of Preliminary Permit Application

    On June 24, 2025, Nightfall Renewables Inc. (Nightfall Renewables) 
filed a preliminary permit application for the Juniper Pumped Storage 
Project No. 15407 (Juniper Project). On November 17, 2025, Nightfall 
Renewables filed a notice of withdrawal of the preliminary permit 
application.
    No motion in opposition to the notice of withdrawal has been filed, 
and the Commission has taken no action to disallow the withdrawal. 
Pursuant to Rule 216(b) of the Commission's Rules of Practice and 
Procedure,\1\ the withdrawal of the application became effective on 
December 2, 2025, and this proceeding is hereby terminated.
