21 C.F.R. § 812.66 — Significant risk device determinations.
Citation: us:cfr:21:812.66
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-812.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 812 (Investigational Device Exemptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an IRB determines that an investigation, presented for approval under § 812.2(b)(1)(ii), involves a significant risk device, it shall so notify the investigator and, where appropriate, the sponsor. A sponsor may not begin the investigation except as provided in § 812.30(a).
