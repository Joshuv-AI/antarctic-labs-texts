30 C.F.R. § 49.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:30:49.1
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-49.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 49 (Mine Rescue Teams). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements the provisions of Section 115(e) of the Federal Mine Safety and Health Act of 1977. Every operator of an underground mine shall assure the availability of mine rescue capability for purposes of emergency rescue and recovery.
