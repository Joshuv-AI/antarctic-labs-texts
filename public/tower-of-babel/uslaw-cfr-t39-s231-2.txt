39 C.F.R. § 231.2 — Security Control Officer.
Citation: us:cfr:39:231.2
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-231.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 231 (Protection Of Post Offices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The postmaster or a supervisor designated by the postmaster shall act as Security Control Officer for each post office. The Security Control Officer shall be responsible for the general security of the post office, its stations and branches, in accordance with rules and regulations issued by the Chief Postal Inspector.
