AC 187-1V: Schedule of Charges Outside the United States

AGENCY: Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), DOT.

ACTION: Notice of availability.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) is announcing the 
availability of Advisory Circular (AC) 187-1V which transmits an 
updated schedule of charges for services of FAA Flight Standards 
Aviation Safety Inspectors outside the United States. The advisory 
circular has been updated in accordance with the procedures listed in 
14 CFR part 187, Appendix A.

DATES: This AC is effective on October 1, 2026.

ADDRESSES: How to obtain copies: A copy of this publication may be 
downloaded from: http://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/advisory_circulars.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Ms. Danuta Pronczuk, Flight Standards 
Service, AFS-50, Federal Aviation Administration, 800 Independence 
Avenue SW, Washington, DC 20591, telephone (202) 267-0923, 
[email protected].
