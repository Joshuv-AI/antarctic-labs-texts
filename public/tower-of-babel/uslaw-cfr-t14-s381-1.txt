14 C.F.R. § 381.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:14:381.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-381.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 381 (Special Event Tours). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is ensure that air travelers who have purchased tours to special events will receive the promised admission to the event. This part expands the “Super Bowl rule” to other events.
