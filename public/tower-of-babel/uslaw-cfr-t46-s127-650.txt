46 C.F.R. § 127.650 — Bulk liquid cargo limitations.
Citation: us:cfr:46:127.650
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-127.650
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 127 (Construction And Arrangements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding § 125.110 of this subchapter, no OSV carrying more than 240 total persons may carry flammable or combustible liquid cargoes of Grade D or higher in bulk.
