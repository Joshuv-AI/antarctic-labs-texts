10 U.S.C. § 931g — Art. 131g. Wrongful interference with adverse administrative proceeding
Citation: us:usc:10:931g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section931g
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter X (Punitive Articles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person subject to this chapter who, having reason to believe that an adverse administrative proceeding is pending against any person subject to this chapter, wrongfully acts with the intent—
(1)
to influence, impede, or obstruct the conduct of the proceeding; or
(2)
otherwise to obstruct the due administration of justice;
shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
