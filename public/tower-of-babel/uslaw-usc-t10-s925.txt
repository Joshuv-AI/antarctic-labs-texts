10 U.S.C. § 925 — Art. 125. Kidnapping
Citation: us:usc:10:925
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section925
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter X (Punitive Articles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person subject to this chapter who wrongfully—
(1)
seizes, confines, inveigles, decoys, or carries away another person; and
(2)
holds the other person against that person’s will;
shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
