45 U.S.C. § 366a — Effect of Internal Revenue Code
Citation: us:usc:45:366a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section366a
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 11 (Railroad Unemployment Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of the Railroad Unemployment Insurance Act, as herein amended, shall be in full force and effect notwithstanding the enactment of the Internal Revenue Code.
