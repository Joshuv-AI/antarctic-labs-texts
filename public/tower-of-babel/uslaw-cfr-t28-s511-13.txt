28 C.F.R. § 511.13 — Searches before entering, or while inside, a Bureau facility or Bureau grounds.
Citation: us:cfr:28:511.13
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-511.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 511 (General Management Policy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Bureau staff may search you and your belongings (for example, bags, boxes, vehicles, containers in vehicles, jackets or coats, etc.) before entering, or while inside, any Bureau facilities or Bureau grounds, to keep out prohibited objects.
