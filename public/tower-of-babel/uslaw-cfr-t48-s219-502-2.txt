48 C.F.R. § 219.502-2 — 219.502-2 Total small business set-asides.
Citation: us:cfr:48:219.502-2
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-219.502-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 219 (Small Business Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless the contracting officer determines that the criteria for set-aside cannot be met, set aside for small business concerns acquisitions for—
(1) Construction, including maintenance and repairs, under $3.5 million;
(2) Dredging under $2 million; and
(3) Architect-engineer services for military construction or family housing projects under $1 million (10 U.S.C. 2855).
