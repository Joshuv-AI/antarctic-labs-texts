14 C.F.R. § 61.85 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:14:61.85
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-61.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 61 (Certification: Pilots, Flight Instructors, And Ground Instructors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant for a student pilot certificate:
(a) Must make that application in a form acceptable to the Administrator; and
(b) Must submit the application to a Flight Standards office, a designated pilot examiner, an airman certification representative associated with a pilot school, a flight instructor, or other person authorized by the Administrator.
