25 C.F.R. § 175.130 — What information must I provide when I request service?
Citation: us:cfr:25:175.130
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-175.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 175 (Electric Power Utilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At a minimum, you must provide the utility with the following information when you request service:
(a) Your full legal name or the legal name of the entity needing service;
(b) Your taxpayer identification number;
(c) Your billing address;
(d) Your service address; and
(e) Any additional information required by the utility.
