42 U.S.C. § 16233 — Low-cost renewable hydrogen and infrastructure for vehicle propulsion
Citation: us:usc:42:16233
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section16233
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter IX (Research And Development); Part C (Renewable Energy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall—
(1)
establish a research, development, and demonstration program to determine the feasibility of using hydrogen propulsion in light-weight vehicles and the integration of the associated hydrogen production infrastructure using off-the-shelf components; and
(2)
identify universities and institutions that—
(A)
have expertise in researching and testing vehicles fueled by hydrogen, methane, and other fuels;
(B)
have expertise in integrating off-the-shelf components to minimize cost; and
(C)
within 2 years can test a vehicle based on an existing commercially available platform with a curb weight of not less than 2,000 pounds before modifications, that—
(i)
operates solely on hydrogen;
(ii)
qualifies as a light-duty passenger vehicle; and
(iii)
uses hydrogen produced from water using only solar energy.
