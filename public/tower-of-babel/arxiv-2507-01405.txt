Smooth minimal surfaces of general type with p g = 0 p_{g}=0 , K 2 = 7 K^{2}=7 and involutions
Yifan Chen, YongJoo Shin, Han Zhang

Abstract
Lee and the second named author studied involutions on smooth minimal surfaces S S of general type with p g ​ ( S ) = 0 p_{g}(S)=0 and K S 2 = 7 K_{S}^{2}=7 . They gave the possibilities of the birational models W W of the quotients and the branch divisors B 0 B_{0} induced by involutions σ \sigma on the surfaces S S . In this paper we improve and refine the results of Lee and the second named author. We exclude the case of the Kodaira dimension κ ⁡ ( W ) = 1 \kappa(W)=1 when the number k k of isolated fixed points of an involution σ \sigma on S S is nine. The possibilities of branch divisors B 0 B_{0} are reduced for the case k = 9 k=9 , and are newly given for the case k = 11 k=11 . Moreover, we show that if the branch divisor B 0 B_{0} has three irreducible components, then S S is an Inoue surface.
