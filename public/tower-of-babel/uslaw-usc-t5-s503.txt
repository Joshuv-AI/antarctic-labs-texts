5 U.S.C. § 503 — Witness fees and allowances
Citation: us:usc:5:503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section503
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 5 (Administrative Procedure); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
For the purpose of this section, “agency” has the meaning given it by section 5721 of this title.
(b)
A witness is entitled to the fees and allowances allowed by statute for witnesses in the courts of the United States when—
(1)
he is subpenaed under section 304(a) of this title; or
(2)
he is subpenaed to and appears at a hearing before an agency authorized by law to hold hearings and subpena witnesses to attend the hearings.
