25 C.F.R. § 12.35 — Do Indian country law enforcement officers complete any special training?
Citation: us:cfr:25:12.35
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-12.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 12 (Indian Country Law Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Law enforcement personnel of any program funded by the Bureau of Indian Affairs must not perform law enforcement duties until they have successfully completed a basic law enforcement training course prescribed by the Director. The Director will also prescribe mandatory supplemental and in-service training courses.
