41 C.F.R. § 304-3.10 — -3.10 Agency advance approval for non-Federal source travel.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-3.10
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-3.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-3 (3—Employee Responsibility). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees must receive advance agency approval before performing travel paid by a non-Federal source to attend a meeting, except as provided in § 304-3.11.
