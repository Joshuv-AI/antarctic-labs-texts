51 U.S.C. § 70706 — Public meetings, information, and hearings
Citation: us:usc:51:70706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section70706
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 707 (Human Space Flight Independent Investigation Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Public Meetings and Release of Public Versions of Reports.—
A Commission shall—
(1)
hold public hearings and meetings to the extent appropriate; and
(2)
release public versions of the reports required under this chapter.
(b) Public Hearings.—
Any public hearings of a Commission shall be conducted in a manner consistent with the protection of information provided to or developed for or by the Commission as required by any applicable statute, regulation, or Executive order.
