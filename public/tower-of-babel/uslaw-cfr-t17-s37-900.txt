17 C.F.R. § 37.900 — Core Principle 9—Timely publication of trading information.
Citation: us:cfr:17:37.900
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-37.900
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 37 (Swap Execution Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. The swap execution facility shall make public timely information on price, trading volume, and other trading data on swaps to the extent prescribed by the Commission.
(b) Capacity of swap execution facility. The swap execution facility shall be required to have the capacity to electronically capture and transmit trade information with respect to transactions executed on the facility.
