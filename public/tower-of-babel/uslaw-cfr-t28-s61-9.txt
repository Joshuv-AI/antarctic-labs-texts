28 C.F.R. § 61.9 — Emergencies.
Citation: us:cfr:28:61.9
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-61.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 61 (Procedures For Implementing The National Environmental Policy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CEQ shall be consulted when emergency circumstances make it necessary to take a major federal action with significant environmental impact without following otherwise applicable procedural requirements under NEPA.
