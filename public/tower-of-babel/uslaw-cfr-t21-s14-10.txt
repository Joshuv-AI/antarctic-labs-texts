21 C.F.R. § 14.10 — Applicability to Congress.
Citation: us:cfr:21:14.10
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-14.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 14 (Public Hearing Before A Public Advisory Committee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to Congress, individual Members of Congress, and other employees or representatives of Congress in the same way that they apply to any other member of the public, except that disclosure of advisory committee records to Congress is governed by § 20.87.
