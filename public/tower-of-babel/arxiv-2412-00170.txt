On existence and properties of roots of third Painlevé’ transcendents
S.I. Tertychniy

Abstract
. A closed proof of existence of solutions to third Painlevé’ equation (Painlevé’ transcendents) vanishing at arbitrary given point distinct from zero is presented. The structure of the set of Painlevé’ transcendents with common parameters vanishing at a given point is described. The approximate explicit representation of a Painlevé’ transcendent in vicinity of its non-zero root is given.



1.
Preliminaries

The name
third Painlevé’ equations
refers to one among six subfamilies of the family
of
nonlinear second order ordinary differential equations called Painlevé’ equations
which are distinguished by possessing the so called Painlevé property
and which are not reduced to differential equations solved in known functions.
This property means that for all solutions of the corresponding equations
all the movable singularities (i.e. singularities whose location depends on what a solution is considered)
are poles. Equivalent manifestation of the Painlevé property is the independence of locations of multi-valued singularities (branch points)
of the choice of a solution; in other words, all they must be determined by the equation itself
[
1
]

3.
Specificity
of roots of third Painlevé’ transcendents

Zeros of third Painlevé’ transcendents play important role in the theory of monodromy preserving deformations
of linear second order differential equations.
In our case these equations are named

Generally speaking, this kind relationship takes place for all subfamilies of the Painlevé’ family and the Heun’ family, see
[
5

At the time, this was an independent reason of the arising of interest to Painlevé’ equations
which served as another starting point for their finding and investigations.
The role of the roots of solutions to equations from the third Painlevé’ subfamily

[

We discuss below the basic issues concerning the existence of
