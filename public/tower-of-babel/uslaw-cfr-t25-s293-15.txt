25 C.F.R. § 293.15 — When may the Secretary disapprove a compact or amendment?
Citation: us:cfr:25:293.15
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-293.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 293 (Class Iii Tribal-State Gaming Compacts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may disapprove a compact or amendment only if:
(a) It violates:
(1) Any provision of IGRA;
(2) Any other provision of Federal law that does not relate to jurisdiction over gaming on Indian lands; or
(3) The trust obligations of the United States to Indians; or
(b) The documents required in § 293.8 are not submitted and the parties have been informed in writing of the missing documents and are provided with an opportunity to supply those documents.
