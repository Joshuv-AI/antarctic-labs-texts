29 C.F.R. § 1601.91 — Request for title VII interpretation or opinion.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1601.91
Creator: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1601.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1601 (Procedural Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any interested person desiring a written title VII interpretation or opinion from the Commission may make such a request. However, issuance of title VII interpretations or opinions is discretionary.
