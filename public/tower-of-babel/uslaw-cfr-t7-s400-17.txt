7 C.F.R. § 400.17 — Creditors.
Citation: us:cfr:7:400.17
Creator: Federal Crop Insurance Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-400.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 400 (General Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An interest of a person in an insured crop existing by virtue of a lien, mortgage, garnishment, levy, execution, bankruptcy, involuntary transfer or other similar interest shall not entitle the holder of the interest to any benefit under the contract.
