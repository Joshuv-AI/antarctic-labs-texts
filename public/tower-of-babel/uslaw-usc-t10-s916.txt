10 U.S.C. § 916 — Art. 116. Riot or breach of peace
Citation: us:usc:10:916
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section916
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter X (Punitive Articles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person subject to this chapter who causes or participates in any riot or breach of the peace shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
