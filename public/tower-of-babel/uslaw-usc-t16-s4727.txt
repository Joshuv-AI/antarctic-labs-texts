16 U.S.C. § 4727 — Intentional introductions policy review
Citation: us:usc:16:4727
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4727
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 67 (Aquatic Nuisance Prevention And Control); Subchapter III (Prevention And Control Of Aquatic Nuisance Species Dispersal). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Within one year of November 29, 1990, the Task Force shall, in consultation with State fish and wildlife agencies, other regional, State and local entities, potentially affected industries and other interested parties, identify and evaluate approaches for reducing the risk of adverse consequences associated with intentional introduction of aquatic organisms and submit a report of their findings, conclusions and recommendations to the Congress.
