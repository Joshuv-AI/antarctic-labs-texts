31 C.F.R. § 589.207 — Prohibited exportation, reexportation, sale, or supply of goods, services, or technology to the Crimea region of Ukraine.
Citation: us:cfr:31:589.207
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-589.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 589 (Ukraine-/Russia-Related Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise authorized, the exportation, reexportation, sale, or supply, directly or indirectly, from the United States, or by a United States person, wherever located, of any goods, services, or technology to the Crimea region of Ukraine is prohibited.
