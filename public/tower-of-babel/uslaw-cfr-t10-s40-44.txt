10 C.F.R. § 40.44 — Amendment of licenses at request of licensee.
Citation: us:cfr:10:40.44
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-40.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 40 (Domestic Licensing Of Source Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applications for amendment of a license shall be filed on NRC Form 313 in accordance with § 40.31 and shall specify the respects in which the licensee desires the license to be amended and the grounds for such amendment.
