48 C.F.R. § 32.409-2 — 32.409-2 Recommendation for disapproval.
Citation: us:cfr:48:32.409-2
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-32.409-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 32 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If recommending disapproval, the contracting officer shall, under agency procedures, transmit—
(a) The items prescribed in 32.409-1(a), (b), and (c); and
(b) The recommendation for disapproval and the reasons.
