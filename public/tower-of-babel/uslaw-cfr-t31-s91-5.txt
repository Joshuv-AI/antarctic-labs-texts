31 C.F.R. § 91.5 — Compliance with signs and directions.
Citation: us:cfr:31:91.5
Creator: Monetary Offices, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-91.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 91 (Regulations Governing Conduct In Or On The Bureau Of The Mint Buildings And Grounds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Persons in and on the property shall comply with the instructions of uniformed Bureau of the Mint guards (U.S. Special Policemen), other authorized officials, and official signs of a prohibitory or directory nature.
