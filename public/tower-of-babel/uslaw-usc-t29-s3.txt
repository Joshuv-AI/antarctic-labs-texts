29 U.S.C. § 3 — Commissioner; appointment and tenure of office; compensation
Citation: us:usc:29:3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section3
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 1 (Labor Statistics); Subchapter I (Bureau Of Labor Statistics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Bureau of Labor Statistics shall be under the charge of a Commissioner of Labor Statistics, who shall be appointed by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate; he shall hold his office for four years, unless sooner removed, and shall receive a salary.
