16 C.F.R. § 23.7 — Disclosure of surface-layer application of rhodium.
Citation: us:cfr:16:23.7
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-23.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 23 (Guides For The Jewelry, Precious Metals, And Pewter Industries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is unfair or deceptive to fail to disclose a surface-layer application of rhodium on products marked or described as precious metal.
