9 C.F.R. § 322.5 — Uninspected tallow, stearin, oleo oil, etc., not to be exported unless certified as prescribed.
Citation: us:cfr:9:322.5
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-322.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 322 (Exports 1). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No tallow, stearin, oleo oil, or the rendered fat derived from the carcasses of livestock, that has not been inspected and passed, and so marked in compliance with the regulations in this subchapter shall be exported, unless the product has been denatured as required by § 314.5 or § 325.13 of this subchapter or identified and marked as prescribed by § 325.11 of this subchapter.
