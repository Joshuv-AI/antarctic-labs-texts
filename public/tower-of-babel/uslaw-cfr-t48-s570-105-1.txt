48 C.F.R. § 570.105-1 — 570.105-1 Contracting by negotiation.
Citation: us:cfr:48:570.105-1
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-570.105-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 570 (Acquiring Leasehold Interests In Real Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting by negotiation is appropriate for acquiring space in a building through a lease contract. The contracting officer will usually need to conduct discussions with offerors about their proposals and consider factors other than price in making the award.
