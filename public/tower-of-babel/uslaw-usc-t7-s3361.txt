7 U.S.C. § 3361 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:7:3361
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section3361
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 64 (Agricultural Research, Extension, And Teaching); Subchapter XIV (Institutions Of Higher Education In Insular Areas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this subchapter, the term “eligible institution” means an institution of higher education (as defined in section 1001(a) of title 20) in an insular area that has demonstrable capacity to carry out teaching and extension programs in the food and agricultural sciences.
