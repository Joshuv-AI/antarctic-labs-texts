20 C.F.R. § 726.202 — Who may underwrite an operator's liability.
Citation: us:cfr:20:726.202
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-726.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 726 (Black Lung Benefits; Requirements For Coal Mine Operator'S Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each coal mine operator who is not authorized to self-insure shall insure and keep insured the payment of benefits as required by the Act with any stock company or mutual company or association, or with any other person, or fund, including any State fund while such company, association, person, or fund is authorized under the law of any State to insure workmen's compensation.
