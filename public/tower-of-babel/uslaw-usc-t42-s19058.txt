42 U.S.C. § 19058 — Biological field stations and marine laboratories
Citation: us:usc:42:19058
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section19058
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 163 (Research And Development, Competition, And Innovation); Subchapter III (National Science Foundation For The Future); Part E (Fundamental Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director shall continue to support enhancing, repairing and maintaining research instrumentation, laboratories, telecommunications and housing at biological field stations and marine laboratories.
