15 C.F.R. § 310.6 — Recognition by the President.
Citation: us:cfr:15:310.6
Creator: International Trade Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-310.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 310 (Official U.S. Government Recognition Of And Participation In International Expositions Held In The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the President concurs in the favorable reports from the Secretaries of State and Commerce, he may grant Federal recognition to the exposition by indicating his concurrence to the two Secretaries and authorizing them to seek BIE registration.
