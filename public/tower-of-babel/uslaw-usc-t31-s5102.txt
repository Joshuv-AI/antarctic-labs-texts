31 U.S.C. § 5102 — Standard weight
Citation: us:usc:31:5102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section5102
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 51 (Coins And Currency); Subchapter I (Monetary System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The standard troy pound of the National Institute of Standards and Technology of the Department of Commerce shall be the standard used to ensure that the weight of United States coins conforms to specifications in section 5112 of this title.
