19 C.F.R. § 182.61 — Rules of origin.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 182 (United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations, implementing the rules of origin provisions of General Note 11, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), and Chapters Four and Six of the USMCA, are contained in Appendix A to this part.
