5 C.F.R. § 293.302 — Establishment of Official Personnel Folder.
Citation: us:cfr:5:293.302
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-293.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 293 (Personnel Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each agency shall establish an Official Personnel Folder (OPF) for each employee occupying a position subject to this part, except as provided in § 293.306. Except as provided in the Guide to Personnel Recordkeeping, there will be only one OPF maintained for each employee regardless of service in various agencies.
