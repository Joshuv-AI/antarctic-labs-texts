25 C.F.R. § 115.502 — Who inherits the money in an IIM account when an account holder dies?
Citation: us:cfr:25:115.502
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-115.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 115 (Trust Funds For Tribes And Individual Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At the end of all probate procedures, funds remaining in a decedent's estate account will be distributed from the decedent's estate account and paid directly to or deposited into an IIM account of the decedent's heirs, beneficiaries, or other persons or entities entitled by law to receive the funds, where applicable. See 25 CFR part 15.
