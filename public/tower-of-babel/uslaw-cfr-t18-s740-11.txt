18 C.F.R. § 740.11 — Federal/State coordination.
Citation: us:cfr:18:740.11
Creator: Water Resources Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-740.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 740 (State Water Management Planning Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Council will coordinate the program under this part with similar or related programs of other Federal agencies in an effort to achieve consistency and compatibility in the administration of Federal programs.
