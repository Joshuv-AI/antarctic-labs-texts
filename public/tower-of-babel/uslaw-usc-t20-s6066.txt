20 U.S.C. § 6066 — Public schools
Citation: us:usc:20:6066
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section6066
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 68 (National Education Reform); Subchapter X (Miscellaneous); Part A (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in section 5890 1 of this title, nothing in this chapter shall be construed to authorize the use of funds under subchapter III 1 of this chapter to directly or indirectly benefit any school other than a public school.
