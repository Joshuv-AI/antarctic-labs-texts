10 U.S.C. § 1441 — Annuities in addition to other payments
Citation: us:usc:10:1441
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1441
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 73 (Annuities Based On Retired Or Retainer Pay); Subchapter I (Retired Serviceman’S Family Protection Plan). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An annuity under this subchapter is in addition to any pension or other payment to which the beneficiary is entitled under any other provision of law, and may not be considered as income under any law administered by the Department of Veterans Affairs.
