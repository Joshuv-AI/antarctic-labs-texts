40 C.F.R. § 420.115 — Pretreatment standards for existing sources (PSES).
Citation: us:cfr:40:420.115
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-420.115
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 420 (Iron And Steel Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any existing source subject to this subpart which introduces pollutants into a publicly owned treatment works must comply with 40 CFR part 403.
