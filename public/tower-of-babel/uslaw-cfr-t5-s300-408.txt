5 C.F.R. § 300.408 — Corrective action.
Citation: us:cfr:5:300.408
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-300.408
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 300 (Employment (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon evidence of failure to comply with these regulations, OPM may, pursuant to its authority, order the agency to take appropriate corrective action.
