34 C.F.R. § 299.4 — What requirements apply to the consolidation of State and local administrative funds?
Citation: us:cfr:34:299.4
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-299.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 299 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An SEA may adopt and use its own reasonable standards in determining whether—
(a) The majority of its resources for administrative purposes comes from non-Federal sources to permit the consolidation of State administrative funds in accordance with section 8201 of the ESEA; and
(b) To approve an LEA's consolidation of its administrative funds in accordance with section 8203 of the ESEA.
