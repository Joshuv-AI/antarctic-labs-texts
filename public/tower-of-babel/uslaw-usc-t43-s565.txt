43 U.S.C. § 565 — Terms of sale of town lots; installments of price
Citation: us:usc:43:565
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section565
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter XV (Town Sites, Parks, Playgrounds, And School Sites). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the sale of town lots under the provisions of sections 562 and 563 of this title the Secretary of the Interior may, in his discretion, require payments for such town lots in full at time of sale or in annual installments, not exceeding five, with interest at the rate of 6 per centum per annum on deferred payments.
