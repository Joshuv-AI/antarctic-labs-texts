20 U.S.C. § 1444 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:20:1444
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1444
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 33 (Education Of Individuals With Disabilities); Subchapter III (Infants And Toddlers With Disabilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of carrying out this subchapter, there are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 2005 through 2010.
