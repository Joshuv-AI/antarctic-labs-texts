5 C.F.R. § 536.404 — Issuance of employee letters.
Citation: us:cfr:5:536.404
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-536.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 536 (Grade And Pay Retention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an employee is entitled to grade and/or pay retention, the employing agency shall give to the employee, with a copy of the Notification of Personnel Action (SF-50) documenting entitlement to grade and/or pay retention, a letter describing the circumstances warranting grade and/or pay retention, and the nature of that entitlement.
