22 C.F.R. § 92.20 — Administering an affirmation.
Citation: us:cfr:22:92.20
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-92.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 92 (Notarial And Related Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In administering an affirmation the procedure followed is generally the same as in the case of an oath, but the formula is varied by the use of the following words: “You do solemnly, sincerely, and truly affirm and declare that . . ., and this you do under the pains and penalties of perjury.”
