46 C.F.R. § 126.250 — Period of validity for a Certificate of Inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:46:126.250
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-126.250
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 126 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A Certificate of Inspection is valid for 5 years.
(b) A Certificate of Inspection may be suspended and withdrawn or revoked by the cognizant OCMI at any time for noncompliance with the requirements of this subchapter or other applicable laws.
