Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 609 (Homicide Victims’ Families’ Rights). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter applies in the case of any cold case murder occurring on or after January 1, 1970.
