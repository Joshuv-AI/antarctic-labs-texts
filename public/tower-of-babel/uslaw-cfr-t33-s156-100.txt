33 C.F.R. § 156.100 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:33:156.100
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-156.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 156 (Oil And Hazardous Material Transfer Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to the transfer of oil or hazardous material on the navigable waters or contiguous zone of the United States to, from, or within each vessel with a capacity of 250 barrels or more; except that, this subpart does not apply to transfer operations within a public vessel.
