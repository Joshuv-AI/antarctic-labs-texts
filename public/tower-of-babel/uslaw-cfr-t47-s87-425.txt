47 C.F.R. § 87.425 — Interference.
Citation: us:cfr:47:87.425
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-87.425
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 87 (Aviation Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Control towers and RCOs must not cause harmful interference to control towers or RCOs at adjacent airports. If interference between adjacent control towers or RCOs exists, the Commission will direct the licensees how to eliminate the interference.
