Pullback formula for vector-valued hermitian modular forms on U n , n \mathrm{U}_{n,n} Thanks: This work was supported by JST SPRING, Grant Number JPMJSP2110.
Nobuki TAKEDA

Abstract
. Using a differential operator D D which sends a scalar-valued Siegel modular form to the tensor product of two vector-valued Siegel modular forms, under a certain condition, we give the pullback formula for vector-valued hermitian modular forms on any CM field. We also give equivalence conditions for differential operators to have the above properties, which is an extension of Ibukiyama’s result for hermitian modular forms.

Introduction

In the case of Siegel modular forms, the pullback of Siegel Eisenstein series to the direct product of two Siegel upper half-spaces has been studied by many people.
Garrett
[
9
]
proved the result in the scalar-valued case.
Using the method of Garrett
[

]
and differential operators that preserve the automorphic properties,
this theory was generalized for the symmetric tensor valued case (e.g.
[

]
,

]

),

[

]
,
[
19
]
).
Kozima
[
20
]
proved it in the general case by using the differential operators generalized by Ibukiyama
[
13
,
14
]
.
In the case of hermitian modular forms, several results have been formulated (e.g.
[
25
]
,
[
21
]
,
[
26
]

This formula (called the “pullback formula”) has been used for studying the Fourier
coefficients of vector-valued Klingen-Eisenstein series, the algebraicity of vector-valued Siegel modular forms and congruences of Siegel modular forms.

We first aim to describe differential operators on hermitian modular forms following Ibukiyama

The case of Siegel modular forms is reinterpreted in terms of representation theory in

A similar argument can be made for the case of hermitian modular forms as well.

This paper is organized as follows:
In Section 2,
we explain hermitian modular forms and the terminology used in this paper.
In Section 3, we first give some formulas on derivatives.
Next, we give the equivalent condition for a differential operator on hermitian modular forms
to preserve the automorphic properties.
In Section 4,
we define the hermitian Eisenstein series and Klingen–Eisenstein series.
In Section 5, we first state the well-known fact of the double coset decomposition.
Then we show the calculation of the pullback formula can be reduced to a local computation
and prove the pullback formula.

(3.2)

We take a family

Lemma 5

Using the above notation, we have

3.2.
Differential Operators on Automorphic Forms

Let
n_{1},\ldots,n_{d}
be positive integers such that
n_{1}\geq\cdots\geq n_{d}\geq 1

and put
n=n_{1}+\cdots+n_{d}\geq 2
. We embed
\mathfrak{H}_{n_{1}}^{\mathbf{a}}\times\cdots\times\mathfrak{H}_{n_{d}}^{\mathbf{a}}
in
\mathfrak{H}_{n}^{\mathbf{a}}
and

G_{(n_{1})}\times\cdots\times G_{(n_{d})}
in
G_{(n)}
diagonally.

Let
(\rho_{s},V_{s})
be a representation of
\mathcal{K}_{(n_{s})}^{\mathbb{C}}
for
s=1,\ldots,d
,
and
\kappa=(\kappa_{v})_{v}\in\mathbf{a}
a family of positive integers.

We will consider
V:=V_{1}\otimes\cdots\otimes V_{d}
-valued differential operators
\mathbb{D}

on scalar-valued functions of
\mathfrak{H}_{n}^{\mathbf{a}}
, satisfying Condition (A) below:

Condition (A)
.

For any modular form
F\in M_{\kappa}(\Gamma_{K}^{(n)})
, we have

\mathrm{Res}(\mathbb{D}(F))\in\bigotimes_{i=1}^{d}M_{\det^{\kappa}\rho_{n_{i}}}(\Gamma_{K}^{(n_{i})}),

where
\mathrm{Res}
means the restriction of a function on
\mathfrak{H}_{n}^{\mathbf{a}}
to

\mathfrak{H}_{n_{1}}^{\mathbf{a}}\times\cdots\times\mathfrak{H}_{n_{d}}^{\mathbf{a}}
.

Remark 2
.

(1)

Such differential operators have been constructed for several vector-valued cases in
[
5
]
.

(2)

Using the method of Ban
[
2
]
, representation-theoretic interpretation of the differential operators
satisfying Condition (A) in the symplectic case was given in
[
29
]
.

(3)

This Condition (A) corresponds to Case (I) in
[
13
]
.
The other Case (II) in
[
13
]
is a generalization of the Rankin-Cohen type differential operators in
[
6
]
,
and a representation-theoretic interpretation in the symplectic and unitary cases was given by Ban
[
2
]
.
Rankin–Cohen-type differential operators on hermitian modular forms have been examined by Dunn
[
7
]
for the scalar-valued case,
and have even been explicitly constructed.

We will consider the Howe duality for the Weil representation.

Definition 8
.

Let
L_{n,\kappa}=(\mathbb{C}[M_{n,\kappa},M_{n,\kappa}])^{\mathbf{a}}

(1)

We define the
(\mathfrak{g}_{n,\mathbb{C}},K)
-module structure

on

\displaystyle l_{n,\kappa}(\kappa_{v,i,j})

\displaystyle=\sum_{s=1}^{\kappa_{v}}X_{v,i,s}\frac{\partial}{\partial X_{v,j,s}}+\kappa_{v}\delta_{i,j},

\displaystyle l_{n,\kappa}(\kappa^{\prime}_{v,i,j})

\displaystyle=\sum_{s=1}^{\kappa_{v}}Y_{v,i,s}\frac{\partial}{\partial Y_{v,j,s}},

\displaystyle l_{n,\kappa}(\pi^{+}_{v,i,j})

\displaystyle=\sqrt{-1}\sum_{s=1}^{\kappa_{v}}X_{v,i,s}Y_{v,j,s},

\displaystyle l_{n,\kappa}(\pi^{-}_{v,i,j})

\displaystyle=\sqrt{-1}\sum_{s=1}^{\kappa_{v}}\frac{\partial^{2}}{\partial X_{v,i,s}\partial Y_{v,j,s}}

on the
v

,
and

Here,

For

(2)

We define the left action of the family of unitary groups

For an irreducible algebraic representation
(\lambda,V_{\lambda})
of
\mathrm{U}(\kappa)

L_{n,\kappa}(\lambda)=\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{U}(\kappa)}(V_{\lambda},L_{n,\kappa})

and a
(\mathfrak{g}_{n,\mathbb{C}},K)
-module structure

L_{n,\kappa}
.
We denote by

the unitary lowest weight

-module with lowest

-type
\sigma
.
Let

\sigma
,
and

the highest weight module of
\mathrm{U}(\kappa)
with a highest weight
\lambda
.
We will sometimes identify the irreducible representation of

with the finite dimensional irreducible representation of
\prod_{v\in\mathbf{a}}(\mathrm{GL}_{n}(\mathbb{C})\times\mathrm{GL}_{n}(\mathbb{C}))
.

The following notations are provided to write down the decomposition of

Let

Proposition 10

(1)
