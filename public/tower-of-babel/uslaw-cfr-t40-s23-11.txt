40 C.F.R. § 23.11 — Holidays.
Citation: us:cfr:40:23.11
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-23.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 23 (Judicial Review Under Epa-Administered Statutes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the date determined under §§ 23.2 to 23.10 falls on a Federal holiday, then the time and date of the Administrator's action shall be at 1:00 p.m. eastern time on the next day that is not a Federal holiday.
