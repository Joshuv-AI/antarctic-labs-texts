22 U.S.C. § 6727 — National security exception
Citation: us:usc:22:6727
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6727
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 75 (Chemical Weapons Convention Implementation); Subchapter II (Inspections). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Consistent with the objective of eliminating chemical weapons, the President may deny a request to inspect any facility in the United States in cases where the President determines that the inspection may pose a threat to the national security interests of the United States.
