25 C.F.R. § 307.5 — Hand seal press and certificates to be furnished.
Citation: us:cfr:25:307.5
Creator: Indian Arts And Crafts Board, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-307.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 307 (Navajo All-Wool Woven Fabrics; Use Of Government Certificate Of Genuineness). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each licensee will be furnished, upon payment of the registration and license fees specified in § 307.6 one hand seal press and a supply of blank Government certificates, which shall be used only in accordance with this license, and shall remain at all times the property of the Board.
