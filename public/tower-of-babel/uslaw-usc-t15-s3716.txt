15 U.S.C. § 3716 — Critical industries

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 63 (Technology Innovation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Identification of industries and development of plan
The Secretary shall—
(1)
identify those civilian industries in the United States that are necessary to support a robust manufacturing infrastructure and critical to the economic security of the United States; and
(2)
list the major research and development initiatives being undertaken, and the substantial investments being made, by the Federal Government, including its research laboratories, in each of the critical industries identified under paragraph (1).
(b) Initial report
The Secretary shall submit a report to the Congress within 1 year after February 14, 1992, on the actions taken under subsection (a).
