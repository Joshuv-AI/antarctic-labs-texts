29 C.F.R. § 779.515 — Regulations should be consulted.
Citation: us:cfr:29:779.515
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-779.515
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 779 (The Fair Labor Standards Act As Applied To Retailers Of Goods Or Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This discussion in subpart F of this part is intended only to indicate the general requirements of the recordkeeping regulations. Each employer subject to any provision of the Act should consult the regulations to determine what records he must maintain and the period for which they must be preserved.
