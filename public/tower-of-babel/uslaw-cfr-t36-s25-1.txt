36 C.F.R. § 25.1 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:36:25.1
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-25.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 25 (National Military Parks; Licensed Guide Service Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part are made prescribed and published for the regulation and maintenance of licensed guide service at all national military parks where such service has been established or hereafter may be authorized in the discretion of the Secretary of the Interior upon the recommendation of the Director of the National Park Service.
