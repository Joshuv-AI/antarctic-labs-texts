5 C.F.R. § 1601.40 — Lifecycle Funds.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1601.40
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1601.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1601 (Participants' Choices Of Tsp Funds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Executive Director will establish TSP Lifecycle Funds, which are target date asset allocation portfolios. The TSP Lifecycle Funds will invest solely in the funds established pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 8438(b)(1)(A)-(E).
