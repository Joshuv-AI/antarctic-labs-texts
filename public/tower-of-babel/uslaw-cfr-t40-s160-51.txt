40 C.F.R. § 160.51 — Specimen and data storage facilities.
Citation: us:cfr:40:160.51
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-160.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 160 (Good Laboratory Practice Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Space shall be provided for archives, limited to access by authorized personnel only, for the storage and retrieval of all raw data and specimens from completed studies.
