Publisher Correction: Electric, thermal, and thermoelectric magnetoconductivity for Weyl/multi-Weyl semimetals in planar Hall set-ups induced by the combined effects of topology and strain
Authors: Leonardo Medel, Rahul Ghosh, Alberto Martín‑Ruiz, Ipsita Mandal
Published: 2024

Correction to: Scientific Reports10.1038/s41598-024-68615-0, published on 13 September 2024

The original version of this Article contained an error in Reference 65, which was incorrectly given as:

Ghosh, R., Haidar, F. & Mandal, I. arXiv e-prints (2024), arXiv:2408.01422[cond-mat.mes-hall].

The correct reference is listed below:

Ghosh, R., Haidar, F. & Mandal, I. Linear response in planar Hall and thermal Hall setups for Rarita-Schwinger-Weyl semimetals, arXiv e-prints (2024), arXiv:2408.01422 [cond-mat.mes-hall].

The original Article has been corrected.
