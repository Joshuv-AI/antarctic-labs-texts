36 C.F.R. § 64.14 — Project inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:36:64.14
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-64.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 64 (Grants And Allocations For Recreation And Conservation Use Of Abandoned Railroad Rights-Of-Way). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All State and local projects will receive a final inspection by the Bureau. Final inspections will be conducted prior to final payment of Federal funds. Progress inspections will be conducted as deemed necessary by the Bureau. Preapproval inspections will also be conducted prior to project selection at the discretion of the appropriate Bureau Regional Office.
