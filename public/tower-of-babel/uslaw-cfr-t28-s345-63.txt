28 C.F.R. § 345.63 — Funds due deceased inmates.
Citation: us:cfr:28:345.63
Creator: Federal Prison Industries, Inc., Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-345.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 345 (Federal Prison Industries (Fpi) Inmate Work Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Funds due a deceased inmate for work performed for FPI are payable to a legal representative of the inmate's estate or in accordance with the law of descent and distribution of the state of domicile.
