6 U.S.C. § 464c — Student housing
Citation: us:usc:6:464c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section464c
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter VIII (Coordination With Non-Federal Entities; Inspector General; United States Secret Service; Coast Guard; General Provisions); Part H (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In fiscal year 2004 and thereafter, students attending training at any Center site shall reside in on-Center or Center-provided housing, insofar as available and in accordance with Center policy.
