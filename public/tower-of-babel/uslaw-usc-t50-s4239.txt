50 U.S.C. § 4239 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:50:4239
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section4239
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 52 (Restitution For World War Ii Internment Of Japanese-Americans And Aleuts); Subchapter II (Aleutian And Pribilof Islands Restitution). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this subchapter, or the application of such provision to any person or circumstance, is held invalid, the remainder of this subchapter and the application of such provision to other persons not similarly situated or to other circumstances shall not be affected by such invalidation.
