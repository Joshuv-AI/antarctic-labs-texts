A survey of Chernoff and Hoeffding bounds
Alexandros V. Gerbessiotis

Abstract
This is a survey paper that discusses the original bounds of the seminal papers by Chernoff and Hoeffding. Moreover, it includes a variety of derivative bounds in a variety of forms. Complete proofs are provided as needed. The intent is to provide a repository of reference bounds for the interested researcher.

\lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{(m(r)-u)^{n}}{P(S_{n}\leq nr)}=0.

Lemma 1

(Of Eq. (

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\inf_{t>0}\left(e^{-rt}M_{X}(t)\right)^{n}

\displaystyle\inf_{t>0}\left(e^{-rt}(1+p(e^{t}-1))\right)^{n}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\inf_{t>0}\left(\exp{(f(t))}\right)^{n}

(12)

As indicated by Eq. (

f(t)=\ln{((1+p(e^{t}-1))}-rt
.
Consider

Equating to zero

and solving for

Continuing with the second derivative we find

\exp{(rt)}=\left((1-p)r/(p(1-r))\right)^{r}=\frac{(1-p)^{r}r^{r}}{p^{r}(1-r)^{r}}.

Lemma 4

\displaystyle P(S_{n}\geq nr)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle e^{-nrt}E(e^{tS_{n}})

(27)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle e^{-nrt}e^{npt}E(e^{t(S_{n}-np)})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle e^{-nrt+npt}E(\prod_{i}e^{t(X_{i}-p)})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle e^{-nrt+npt}\prod_{i}E(e^{t(X_{i}-p)})

\displaystyle P(S_{n}\geq nr)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle e^{-nrt+npt}\prod_{i}E(e^{t(X_{i}-p)})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle e^{-nrt+npt}\prod_{i}\exp{\left(\frac{t^{2}}{8}\right)}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle e^{-nrt+npt}\exp{\left(n\frac{t^{2}}{8}\right)}

Theorem 3

Let
X_{i}
be an independent random variable,
i=1,\ldots,n
.
Let
S_{n}=\sum_{i}X_{i}
,
\bar{X}=S_{n}/n
, where

and
p=E(\bar{X})
and
E(S_{n})=np
.
Then for any
h
such that
0<h<1-p
we have the following.

\displaystyle P(\bar{X}-p\geq h))=P(S_{n}\geq(p+h)n)\leq\exp{\left(\frac{-2n^{2}h^{2}}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(b_{i}-a_{i})^{2}}\right)}.

(71)

Proof:

In order to prove Eq.(
71
) we work out similarly
to the other bound of Eq.(
55
).
Consider a
t>0
and the monotonically
increasing and convex function
f(x)=e^{tx}
.

\displaystyle P(\bar{X}-p\geq h))

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle P(S_{n}-E(S_{n})\geq hn)

(72)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle P(e^{t(S_{n}-E(S_{n}))}\geq e^{thn)})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\frac{E(e^{t(S_{n}-E(S_{n}))})}{e^{htn}}

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle e^{-htn}E(e^{t(S_{n}-E(S_{n}))})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\inf_{h>0}e^{-htn}E(e^{t(S_{n}-E(S_{n}))})

We then examine
E(e^{t(S_{n}-E(S_{n}))})
. Note that
X_{i}

are independent random variables for Eq.(

) to be valid.

\displaystyle E(e^{t(S_{n}-E(S_{n}))})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle E(e^{t(\sum_{i}X_{i}-E(\sum_{i}X_{i}))})

(73)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\prod_{i=1}^{n}E(e^{t(X_{i}-E(X_{i}))})

Since
E(X_{i}-E(X_{i}))=0
, the conditions of
Proposition
1
are satisfied.
Therefore

E(e^{t(X_{i}-E(X_{i}))}\leq\exp{\left(\frac{t^{2}(b_{i}-a_{i})^{2}}{8}\right)}.

(74)

Eq.(
72
) by way of Eq.(

) and
Eq.(

\displaystyle P(\bar{X}-p\geq h))

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\inf_{h>0}e^{-htn}E(e^{t(S_{n}-E(S_{n}))})

(75)

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\inf_{h>0}e^{-htn}\prod_{i=1}^{n}E(e^{t(X_{i}-E(X_{i}))})

\displaystyle\leq

\displaystyle\inf_{h>0}e^{-htn}\prod_{i=1}^{n}\exp{\left(\frac{t^{2}(b_{i}-a_{i})^{2}}{8}\right)}

\displaystyle\inf_{h>0}\exp{\left(-htn+t^{2}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{(b_{i}-a_{i})^{2}}{8}\right)}

\displaystyle\exp{\left(-\frac{2h^{2}n^{2}}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(b_{i}-a_{i})^{2}}\right)}.
