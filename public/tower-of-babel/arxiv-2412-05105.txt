The flow space

Let

{\rm CAT}(0)

In the presence of a cocompact isometric action local compactness implies completeness and so

The flow on

\mathbb{R}

3.

Let
X
be the Davis–Moussong realization
[
6
]
[
1
, Chapter 12]
of a building: it is a complete
{\rm CAT}(0)
space.
It is also a finite dimensional affine cell complex and has therefore finite covering dimension.
There is a compact
model chamber

D
on which
\operatorname{Aut}(X)
acts through a finite quotient and an equivariant topological quotient map
\tau\colon X\to D
.
The image
\tau(x)
is the
type
of
x
.
Images of splittings of
\tau
are (closed)
chambers
.
An automorphisms of

is said to be type-preserving if its action on

The type-preserving automorphisms form a finite index subgroup of
\operatorname{Aut}(X)
.
Let
\mathcal{A}
be an
apartment system
for

, meaning that

(B0)

Every

is a subcomplex of

that is isomorphic to the Davis–Moussong realization of a Coxeter complex.

(B1)

For

there is an
A\in\mathcal{A}
such that
x,y\in A
.

(B2)

For
A,A^{\prime}\in\mathcal{A}
there is an isomorphism
\iota\colon A\to A^{\prime}
that restricts to the identity on
A\cap A^{\prime}
.

Let
G
be a topological group with an action on
X
.
The type preserving subgroup
G_{0}
of
G
consists of all
g
that act type-preservingly on
X
.
We will require that
G_{0}
acts
strongly transitively
on
X
.
This means that
G_{0}

acts transitively on pairs
(C,A)
where
A\in\mathcal{A}
and
C
is a chamber of
A
.
Equivalently,
G_{0}
acts transitively on
\mathcal{A}
and
G_{0,A}=\{g\in G_{0}\mid gA=A\}
acts transitively on chambers of
A
.
Since chambers are compact, this means in particular that
G_{0}
(and thus

) act cocompactly on
X
.

Lemma 3.1
.

Under these assumptions the
A
are closed convex subspaces satisfying

,
(2.2b)
, and

Proof.

Observe that every apartment

is a geodesic metric space that is isometrically embedded in

Since

Let

and let

be a chamber of

such that

. By

there is an isomorphism

restricting to the identity on

.
This shows

The full group of type-preserving automorphisms of any apartment

Write
