41 C.F.R. § 105-10.311 — -10.311 Decisions on record or review by the responsible GSA official.
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-10.311
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-10.311
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-10 (10—Nondiscrimination In Programs Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever a record is certified to the responsible GSA official for decision or he reviews the decision of a hearing examiner pursuant to § 105-10.310, or whenever the responsible GSA official conducts the hearing, the applicant or recipient shall be given reasonable opportunity to file with him briefs or other written statements of its contentions, and a copy of the final decision of the responsible GSA official shall be given in writing to the applicant or recipient, and to the complainant, if any.
