-gonal with a unique

Remark 2.5
.

Let

be a smooth curve with a base-point-free pencil

.
The first (or the last if one prefers) scrollar invariant of

When

Lemma 2.6
.

Let
X\subset\mathbb{P}^{5}
be a smooth curve of degree
d
and genus
g
lying on a smooth surface scroll.

(i)

Assume

,

. Let

. We have

\,m(X,g^{1}_{a})=b-2
.

(ii)

. Let

be the pencil cut out by the fiber
\mathbb{F}_{2}\xrightarrow{\ \ }\mathbb{P}^{1}

\,m(X,g^{1}_{a})=b-2a

Proof.

(i)

From the sequence

0\xrightarrow{\ \ }\cal{O}_{Q}(-a,-b+t)\xrightarrow{\ \ }\cal{O}_{Q}(0,t)\xrightarrow{\ \ }\cal{O}_{X}(0,t)\xrightarrow{\ \ }0,

(10)

h^{0}(Q,\cal{O}_{Q}(-a,-b+t))=0,h^{1}(Q,\cal{O}_{Q}(0,t))=0,h^{0}(Q,\cal{O}_{Q}(0,t))=t+1~\,\forall t\geq-1.

For all

, the long cohomology exact sequence of (
10
) gives
\dim|tg^{1}_{a}|=t
if and only
h^{1}(Q,\cal{O}_{Q}(-a,-b+t))=0
. By the Künneth formula,

h^{1}(Q,\cal{O}_{Q}(-a,-b+t))=h^{1}(\mathbb{P}^{1},\cal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^{1}}(-a))\cdot h^{0}(\cal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^{1}}(-b+t)).

Thus
h^{1}(Q,\cal{O}_{Q}(-a,-b+t))=0
if and only if

, hence

.

(ii)

For each
t\geq 1
,

h^{0}(\mathbb{F}_{2},\cal{O}_{\mathbb{F}_{2}}(-ah+(-b+t)f))=0,h^{0}(\mathbb{F}_{2},\cal{O}_{\mathbb{F}_{2}}(tf))=t+1,h^{1}(\mathbb{F}_{2},\cal{O}_{\mathbb{F}_{2}}(tf))=0.

Thus
