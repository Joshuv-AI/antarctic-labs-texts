10 C.F.R. § 719.13 — Who at the Department receives and reviews the Legal Management Plan?
Citation: us:cfr:10:719.13
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-719.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 719 (Contractor Legal Management Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contractors must submit a Legal Management Plan to Department Counsel. If the contractor has not been notified of the assignment of Department Counsel, the contractor must submit the Legal Management Plan to the contracting officer and the DOE Deputy General Counsel for Litigation and Enforcement or the NNSA Deputy General Counsel as appropriate.
