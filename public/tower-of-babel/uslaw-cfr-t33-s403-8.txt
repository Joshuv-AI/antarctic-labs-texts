33 C.F.R. § 403.8 — Proceedings; stay or adjournment. [Rule 8]
Citation: us:cfr:33:403.8
Creator: Great Lakes St. Lawrence Seaway Development Corporation, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-403.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 403 (Rules Of Procedure Of The Joint Tolls Review Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may stay proceedings or any part of the proceedings as it thinks fit or may from time to time adjourn any proceedings before it. [Rule 8]
