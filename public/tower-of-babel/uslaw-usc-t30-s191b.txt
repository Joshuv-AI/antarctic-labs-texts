30 U.S.C. § 191b — Collection of unpaid and underpaid royalties and late payment interest owed by lessees
Citation: us:usc:30:191b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section191b
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 3A (Leases And Prospecting Permits); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Beginning in fiscal year 1996 and thereafter, the Secretary shall take appropriate action to collect unpaid and underpaid royalties and late payment interest owed by Federal and Indian mineral lessees and other royalty payors on amounts received in settlement or other resolution of disputes under, and for partial or complete termination of, sales agreements for minerals from Federal and Indian leases.
