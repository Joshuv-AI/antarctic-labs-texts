21 C.F.R. § 336.80 — Professional labeling.
Citation: us:cfr:21:336.80
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-336.80
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 336 (Antiemetic Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The labeling provided to health professionals (but not to the general public) may contain the following additional indications.
(a) For products containing cyclizine hydrochloride, dimenhydrinate, and diphenhydramine hydrochloride identified in § 336.10 (a), (b), and (c). “For the treatment of vertigo of motion sickness.”
(b) For products containing meclizine hydrochloride identified in § 336.10(d). “For the treatment of vertigo.”
