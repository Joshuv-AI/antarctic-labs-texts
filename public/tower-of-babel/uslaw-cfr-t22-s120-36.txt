22 C.F.R. § 120.36 — Significant military equipment.

Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-120.36
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 120 (Purpose And Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Significant military equipment means articles for which special export controls are warranted because of their capacity for substantial military utility or capability.
(b) Significant military equipment includes:
(1) Items in § 121.1 of this subchapter that are preceded by an asterisk; and
(2) All classified articles enumerated in § 121.1 of this subchapter.
