25 C.F.R. § 39.601 — Is school board training for Bureau-operated schools considered a school board expense subject to the limitation?
Citation: us:cfr:25:39.601
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-39.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 39 (The Indian School Equalization Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No, school board training for Bureau-operated schools is not considered a school board expense subject to the limitation in § 39.600.
