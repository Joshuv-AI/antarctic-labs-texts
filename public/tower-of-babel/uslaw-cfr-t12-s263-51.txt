12 C.F.R. § 263.51 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:12:263.51
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-263.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 263 (Rules Of Practice For Hearings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in subparts B through G of this part:
(a) Secretary means the Secretary of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System.
(b) Member bank means any bank that is a member of the Federal Reserve System.
(c) Institution has the same meaning as that assigned to it in subpart A of this part, and includes any foreign bank with a representative office in the United States.
