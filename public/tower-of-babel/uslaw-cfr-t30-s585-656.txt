30 C.F.R. § 585.656 — What must I do if I cease activities approved in my GAP before the end of my term?
Citation: us:cfr:30:585.656
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-585.656
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 585 (Renewable Energy On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must notify BOEM any time you cease activities under your approved GAP without an approved suspension. If you cease activities for an indefinite period that exceeds 6 months, BOEM may cancel your lease or grant under § 585.422, as applicable, and you must initiate the decommissioning process, as set forth in 30 CFR part 285, subpart I.
