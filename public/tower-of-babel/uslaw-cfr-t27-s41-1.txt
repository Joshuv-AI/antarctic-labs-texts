27 C.F.R. § 41.1 — Importation of tobacco products, cigarette papers and tubes, and processed tobacco.
Citation: us:cfr:27:41.1
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-41.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 41 (Importation Of Tobacco Products, Cigarette Papers And Tubes, And Processed Tobacco). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part contains regulations relating to tobacco products, cigarette papers and tubes, and processed tobacco imported into the United States from a foreign country or brought into the United States from Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands, or a possession of the United States.
