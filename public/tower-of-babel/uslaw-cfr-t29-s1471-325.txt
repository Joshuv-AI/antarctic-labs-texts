29 C.F.R. § 1471.325 — What happens if I do business with an excluded person in a covered transaction?
Citation: us:cfr:29:1471.325
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1471.325
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1471 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If as a participant you knowingly do business with an excluded person, we may disallow costs, annul or terminate the transaction, issue a stop work order, debar or suspend you, or take other remedies as appropriate.
