22 U.S.C. § 279c — Congressional authority necessary for acceptance of new obligations in Organization
Citation: us:usc:22:279c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section279c
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter VI (United Nations Food And Agriculture Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unless Congress by law authorizes such action, neither the President nor any person or agency shall on behalf of the United States accept any amendment under paragraph 1 of article XX of the Constitution of the Organization involving any new obligation for the United States.
