43 C.F.R. § 3272.10 — What must I submit to BLM in my utilization plan?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3272.10
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3272.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Submit to BLM an application describing:
(a) The proposed facilities as required by § 3272.11; and
(b) The anticipated environmental impacts and how you propose to mitigate those impacts, as required by § 3272.12.
