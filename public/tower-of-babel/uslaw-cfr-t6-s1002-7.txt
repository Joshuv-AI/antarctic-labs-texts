6 C.F.R. § 1002.7 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:6:1002.7
Creator: Privacy And Civil Liberties Oversight Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-1002.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 1002 (Implementation Of The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person who makes a false statement in connection with any request for a record or an amendment or correction thereto under this part is subject to the penalties prescribed in 18 U.S.C. 494 and 495 and 5 U.S.C. 552a(i)(3).
