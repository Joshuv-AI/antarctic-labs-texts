32 C.F.R. § 228.6 — Security inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:32:228.6
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-228.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 228 (Security Protective Force). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any personal property, including but not limited to any packages, briefcases, containers or vehicles brought into, while on, or being removed from protected property are subject to inspection. A search of a person may accompany an investigative stop or an arrest.
