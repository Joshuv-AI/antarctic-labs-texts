16 U.S.C. § 5107 — Financial assistance
Citation: us:usc:16:5107
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section5107
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 71 (Atlantic Coastal Fisheries Cooperative Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary and the Secretary of the Interior may provide financial assistance to the Commission and to the States to carry out their respective responsibilities under this chapter, including—
(1)
the preparation, implementation, and enforcement of coastal fishery management plans; and
(2)
State activities that are specifically required within such plans.
