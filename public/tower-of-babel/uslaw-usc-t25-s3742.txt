25 U.S.C. § 3742 — Trust responsibility
Citation: us:usc:25:3742
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section3742
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 39 (American Indian Agricultural Resource Management); Subchapter III (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to diminish or expand the trust responsibility of the United States toward Indian trust lands or natural resources, or any legal obligation or remedy resulting therefrom.
