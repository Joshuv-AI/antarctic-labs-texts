Black Bounces in f ⁡ ( Q ) f(Q) Gravity with Magnetic Source
Pınar KİREZLİ, Doğukan TAŞER

Abstract
In this study, the source of the black bounce (BB) is discussed in the context of f ⁡ ( Q ) f(Q) theory. A body of research has been dedicated to the study of symmetric BB solutions that are generated by a combination of a scalar field with a non-zero potential and a magnetic charge within the framework of nonlinear electrodynamics. As exact solutions are not obtained, the metric functions of Simpson-Visser (SV) and Bardeen type of BB are studied in the field equations. BB solutions are obtained by violating at least one energy condition for the SV and Bardeen type for an ordinary scalar field in f ⁡ ( Q ) f(Q) gravity, which is only achieved by a phantom scalar field in general relativity.
