5 C.F.R. § 1651.13 — How to apply for a death benefit.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1651.13
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1651.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1651 (Death Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To apply for a TSP death benefit, a potential beneficiary must contact the ThriftLine for instructions on providing a certified copy of the participant's death certificate, along with any other information as required by the TSP.
