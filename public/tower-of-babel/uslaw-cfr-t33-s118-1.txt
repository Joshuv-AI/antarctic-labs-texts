33 C.F.R. § 118.1 — General requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:33:118.1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-118.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 118 (Bridge Lighting And Other Signals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All persons owning or operating bridges over the navigable waters of the United States or any international bridge constructed after March 23, 1906, shall maintain at their own expense the lights and other signals required by this part.
