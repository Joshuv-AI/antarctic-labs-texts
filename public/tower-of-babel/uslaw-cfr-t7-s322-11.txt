7 C.F.R. § 322.11 — Ports of entry.
Citation: us:cfr:7:322.11

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 322 (Bees, Beekeeping Byproducts, And Beekeeping Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Shipments of honeybees, honeybee germ plasm, and bees other than honeybees imported under this subpart may enter the United States only at a port of entry staffed by an APHIS inspector. 2
