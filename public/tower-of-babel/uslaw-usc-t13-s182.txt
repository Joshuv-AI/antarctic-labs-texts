13 U.S.C. § 182 — Surveys
Citation: us:usc:13:182
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title13-section182
Context: United States Code, Title 13 (Census); Chapter 5 (Censuses); Subchapter IV (Interim Current Data). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may make surveys deemed necessary to furnish annual and other interim current data on the subjects covered by the censuses provided for in this title.
