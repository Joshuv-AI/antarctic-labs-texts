Anatomy of f 0 ​ ( 980 ) f_{0}(980) via the signal π ​ π \pi\pi state in semileptonic D s D_{s} decays
Shan Cheng, Ling-yun Dai, Jian-ming Shen, Shu-lei Zhang

Abstract
The nature of light scalar mesons remains an unsolved puzzle in hadron physics, complicated by their large widths and controversial partonic structure. We propose to resolve this problem in a comprehensive study of isoscalar two-pion state ( [ π ​ π ] S [\pi\pi]_{\rm S} ), the signal channel of f 0 ​ ( 980 ) f_{0}(980) , through its light-cone distribution amplitudes ( 2 ​ π 2\pi DAs). A key finding is that charge parity forces the twist-3 2 ​ π 2\pi DAs to be asymmetric in the momentum fractions of the quark and antiquark. This property stands in stark contrast to the symmetric twist-3 distribution amplitudes of a conventional f 0 ​ ( 980 ) f_{0}(980) meson, as derived from QCD sum rules in the narrow-width approximation. We calculate the D s → [ π ​ π ] S D_{s}\to[\pi\pi]_{\rm S} transition form factors and discover a significant cancellation between the twist-2 and twist-3 contributions. This leads to a QCD prediction for the D s + → [ π ​ π ] S ​ e + ​ ν e D_{s}^{+}\to[\pi\pi]_{\rm S}e^{+}\nu_{e} decay width that is significantly smaller than the experimental measurement. Our results indicate that the lowest Fock state is not the dominant configuration of the isoscalar two-pion state produced in charm decays, in contrast to the case in B B decays. Future precision measurements can constrain the high-twist 2 ​ π 2\pi DAs associated with multi-particle Fock states, offering a new path to elucidate the structure of the light scalar mesons.

Conclusions.
–We propose to describe the structure of an energetic two-pion system by means of
2\pi
DAs.
We find that the charge-parity constraint implies that both the twist-2 and twist-3
2\pi
DAs for the lowest Fock state
are asymmetric under the exchange of quark and antiquark momentum fractions.
This asymmetry stands in stark contrast to the near-symmetric form of the conventional twist-3 LCDAs used for the single
f_{0}
meson.
Consequently, the twist-2 and twist-3 contributions to the
D_{s}\to[\pi\pi]_{\rm S}
form factor exhibit a significant cancellation,
even though the twist-3 component remains formally dominant.
This destructive interference is the opposite of the constructive addition found in the
D_{s}\to f_{0}
form factor from the twist-2 and twist-3
f_{0}
LCDAs.
Using our current determination of the
2\pi
DAs, we find that the predicted rate for
D_{s}\to[\pi\pi]_{\rm S}e^{+}\nu_{e}
decay,
derived via
D_{l4}
formalism, is significantly smaller than the recent BESIII measurement.
This discrepancy suggests that a simple
q\bar{q}
component is not the predominant structure for the isoscalar two-pion system
produced in charm decays, highlighting the rich and scale-dependent nature of two-pion states in exclusive processes.
