5 C.F.R. § 9801.204 — Special procedures: Medical records.
Citation: us:cfr:5:9801.204
Creator: Council Of The Inspectors General On Integrity And Efficiency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9801.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9801 (Privacy Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event CIGIE receives a request pursuant to § 9801.201 for access to medical records (including psychological records) whose disclosure CIGIE determines would be harmful to the individual to whom they relate, it may refuse to disclose the records directly to the requester but shall transmit them to a physician designated by the requester.
