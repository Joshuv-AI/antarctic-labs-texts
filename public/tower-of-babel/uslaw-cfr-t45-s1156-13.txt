45 C.F.R. § 1156.13 — Information requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1156.13
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1156.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1156 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each recipient shall:
(a) Make available to the Endowment, upon request, information necessary to determine whether the recipient is complying with the regulations in this part.
(b) Permit reasonable access by the Endowment to the books, accounts and other recipient facilities and sources of information to the extent necessary to determine whether the recipient is in compliance with the Act.
