42 C.F.R. § 110.45 — Multiple survivors.
Citation: us:cfr:42:110.45
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-110.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 110 (Countermeasures Injury Compensation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Multiple survivors of the same deceased injured countermeasure recipient may file Request Forms separately or together. Multiple survivors may also submit one set of any required documentation on behalf of all of the requesting survivors as long as such documentation is identical for each survivor.
