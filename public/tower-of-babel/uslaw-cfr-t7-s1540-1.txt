7 C.F.R. § 1540.1 — Applicability of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1540.1
Creator: Foreign Agricultural Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1540.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1540 (International Agricultural Trade). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to requests for emergency relief from duty-free imports of perishable products filed with the Department of Agriculture under section 213(f) of the Caribbean Basin Economic Recovery Act of 1983, title II of Pub. L. 98-67, 97 Stat. 384 (19 U.S.C. 2701 et seq.) (the Act).
