13 C.F.R. § 107.1800 — Licensee's agreement to terms and conditions in §§ 107.1810 and 107.1820.
Citation: us:cfr:13:107.1800
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-107.1800
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 107 (Small Business Investment Companies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any Licensee that violates the terms and conditions of its Leverage is subject to SBA remedies. The terms, conditions and remedies in § 107.1810 apply to outstanding Debentures issued after April 25, 1994. The terms, conditions and remedies in § 107.1820 apply to outstanding Participating Securities issued after April 25, 1994, or if you have Earmarked Assets in your portfolio.
