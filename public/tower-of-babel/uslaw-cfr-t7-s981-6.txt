7 C.F.R. § 981.6 — Shelled almonds.
Citation: us:cfr:7:981.6
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-981.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 981 (Almonds Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Shelled almonds mean almonds after the shells are removed and includes any form those almonds might take. Additional almond products may be included by the Secretary from time to time upon consideration of a recommendation from the Board or other pertinent information.
