7 C.F.R. § 319.75-4 — -4 Treatments.
Citation: us:cfr:7:319.75-4
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-319.75-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 319 (Foreign Quarantine Notices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prior to moving into the United States from the port of entry, a regulated article listed in § 319.75-2(a) shall be treated for possible infestation with khapra beetle in accordance with part 305 of this chapter.
