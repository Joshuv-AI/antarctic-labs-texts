42 C.F.R. § 37.102 — Transfer of affected miner to less dusty area.
Citation: us:cfr:42:37.102
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-37.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 37 (Specifications For Medical Examinations Of Coal Miners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any miner who, in the judgment of NIOSH, has evidence of the development of pneumoconiosis, must be afforded the option of transferring from his or her position to another position in an area of the mine where the concentration of respirable dust in the mine atmosphere is in compliance with the MSHA requirements in 30 CFR part 90. A classification of one or more of the miner's chest radiographs as showing category 1 ( 1/0, 1/1, 1/2), category 2 ( 2/1, 2/2, 2/3), or category 3 ( 3/2, 3/3, 3/+) simple pneumoconiosis, or complicated pneumoconiosis (ILO Classification) will be accepted as such evidence. NIOSH will, at its discretion, also accept other medical examinations provided to NIOSH for review, such as computed tomography scans of the chest or lung biopsies, as evidence of the development of pneumoconiosis.
(b) Any transfer under this section shall be in accordance with the procedures specified in 30 CFR part 90.
