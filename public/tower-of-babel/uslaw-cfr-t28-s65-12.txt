28 C.F.R. § 65.12 — Other assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:28:65.12
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-65.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 65 (Emergency Federal Law Enforcement Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with the purposes and limitations of this subdivision, members of the Federal law enforcement community may provide needed assistance in the form of equipment, training, intelligence information, and personnel. The application may include requests for assistance of this nature.
