35 U.S.C. § 390 — Publication of international design application
Citation: us:usc:35:390
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section390
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part V (The Hague Agreement Concerning International Registration Of Industrial Designs); Chapter 38 (International Design Applications). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The publication under the treaty of an international design application designating the United States shall be deemed a publication under section 122(b).
