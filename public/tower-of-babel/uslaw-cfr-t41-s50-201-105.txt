41 C.F.R. § 50-201.105 — -201.105 Hours worked.
Citation: us:cfr:41:50-201.105
Creator: Chapter 50—Public Contracts, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-50-201.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 50-201 (201—General Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In determining the hours for which an employee is employed, there shall be excluded any time which is excluded by section 3(o) of the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938, as amended, from the computation of hours worked for purposes of sections 6 and 7 of that act.
