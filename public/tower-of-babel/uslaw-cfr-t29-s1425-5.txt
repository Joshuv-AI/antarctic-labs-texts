29 C.F.R. § 1425.5 — Referral to FSIP.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1425.5
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1425.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1425 (Mediation Assistance In The Federal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the mediation process has been completed and the parties are at a negotiation impasse, the Service or the parties may request consideration of the matter by the Federal Services Impasses Panel. The Service shall not refer a case to FSIP until the mediation process has been exhausted and the parties are at a negotiation impasse.
