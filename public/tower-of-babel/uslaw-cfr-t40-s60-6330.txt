40 C.F.R. § 60.6330 — What are my requirements for meeting increments of progress and achieving final compliance?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.6330
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.6330
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you plan to achieve compliance more than 1 year but less than 3 years following the effective date of state plan approval and a permit modification is not required, or more than 1 year but less than 3 years following the date of issuance of a revised construction or operation permit if a permit modification is required, you must meet five increments of progress:
(a) Submit a final control plan.
(b) Submit a notification of retrofit contract award.
(c) Initiate onsite construction.
(d) Complete onsite construction.
(e) Achieve final compliance.
