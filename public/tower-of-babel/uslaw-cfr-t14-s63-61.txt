14 C.F.R. § 63.61 — Flight navigator courses.
Citation: us:cfr:14:63.61
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-63.61
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 63 (Certification: Flight Crewmembers Other Than Pilots). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant for approval of a flight navigator course must submit a letter to the Administrator requesting approval, and must also submit three copies of the course outline, a description of his facilities and equipment, and a list of the instructors and their qualifications. Requirements for the course are set forth in appendix B to this part.
