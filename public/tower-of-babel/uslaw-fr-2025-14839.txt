DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Potomac Energy Center, LLC; Notice of Institution of Section 206 
Proceeding and Refund Effective Date

    On July 31, 2025, the Commission issued an order in Docket No. 
EL25-101-000 pursuant to section 206 of the Federal Power Act (FPA), 16 
U.S.C. 824e, instituting an investigation to determine whether Potomac 
Energy Center, LLC's Rate Schedule is unjust, unreasonable, unduly 
discriminatory or preferential, or otherwise unlawful. Potomac Energy 
Center, LLC, 192 FERC ] 61,090 (2025).
    The refund effective date in Docket No. EL25-101-000, established 
pursuant to section 206(b) of the FPA, will be the date of publication 
of this notice in the Federal Register.
    Any interested person desiring to be heard in Docket No. EL25-101-
000 must file a notice of intervention or motion to intervene, as 
appropriate, with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, in 
accordance with Rule 214 of the Commission's Rules of Practice and 
Procedure, 18 CFR 385.214 (2024), within 21 days of the date of 
issuance of the order.
