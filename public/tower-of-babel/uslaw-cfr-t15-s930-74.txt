15 C.F.R. § 930.74 — OCS activities subject to State agency review.
Citation: us:cfr:15:930.74
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-930.74
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 930 (Federal Consistency With Approved Coastal Management Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except for States which do not anticipate coastal effects resulting from OCS activities, management program lists required pursuant to § 930.53 shall include a reference to OCS plans which describe in detail federal license or permit activities affecting any coastal use or resource.
