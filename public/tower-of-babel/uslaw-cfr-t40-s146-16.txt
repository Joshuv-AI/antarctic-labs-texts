40 C.F.R. § 146.16 — Requirements for new Class I municipal wells in certain parts of Florida.
Citation: us:cfr:40:146.16
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-146.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 146 (Underground Injection Control Program: Criteria And Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Prior to commencing injection, any Class I municipal disposal well in one of the counties identified in § 146.15(f) that is not an existing Class I municipal disposal well as defined in § 146.15(b) of this section shall meet all of the requirements for existing wells seeking authorization to inject pursuant to § 146.15.
