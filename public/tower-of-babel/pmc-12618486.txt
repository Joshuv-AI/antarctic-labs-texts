Research on the influence law and its causes of inert gas injection port depth and air leakage sealing distance on the spontaneous combustion hazard zone in high-gas mines
Authors: Xihua Zhou, Yu Cao, Gang Bai
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractDue to the fact that gas drainage carried out in high-gas mines to address gas issues may exacerbate the spontaneous combustion of coal in goaf areas, and fires may disrupt normal production, it is necessary to adopt appropriate fire prevention and suppression measures. This paper takes Yangquan No. 5 Mine as the background and investigates the influence of the inert gas injection port depth and air leakage sealing length on the spontaneous combustion hazard zone under gas drainage conditions by combining experiments and simulations. The reasons for the influence were explained from the perspectives of the flow field and concentration flux field. The results show that the best effect is achieved when inert gas is injected at the junction of the oxidation zone and the heat dissipation zone, as the inert gas coverage is most balanced at this point. The relationship between the distance of air leakage sealing and the area of the oxidation zone has been determined. Blindly increasing the distance of air leakage sealing will be counterproductive. Starting from the intake side, the optimal sealing length is the average of the horizontal distances from the low-level suction roadway and the high-level suction roadway to the intake airflow roadway, and the oxidation zone area can be reduced by up to 45%. This research outcome has guiding significance for the reasonable determination of on-site fire prevention process parameters.Keywords: Coal spontaneous combustion, High and low level suction roadways, Spontaneous combustion danger zone, Inert gas injection, Seal air leakageSubject terms: Energy science and technology, Engineering, Environmental sciences

Introduction

Coal plays a dominant role in China’s energy consumption. In 2024, coal consumption increased by 1.7%, and production increased by 1.2%. The proportion of coal consumption in total energy consumption was 53.2%. Spontaneous combustion of coal poses a significant threat to the normal mining of coal mines1–3. Spontaneous combustion of coal may occur when the heat storage is greater than the heat dissipation4–7. With the increase in mining depth, the problem of coal spontaneous combustion becomes increasingly serious. Some mines also have problems, such as large gas outflow and severe spontaneous combustion of abandoned coal in goaf areas. For example, the Yangquan mining area in Shanxi Province has such problems. Although the coal from Yangquan No. 5 Mine is anthracite and should be less prone to spontaneous combustion, due to the complex geological structure, the mining process is slow, which leads to a relatively serious problem of spontaneous combustion in actual operation. Gas drainage can reduce the gas concentration in the upper corner, but at the same time, it will increase the air leakage from the working face to the goaf area, increase the oxygen supply in the goaf area, expand the oxidation zone area, and push the oxidation zone deeper into the goaf area, thereby aggravating the coal spontaneous combustion. If the problem of coal spontaneous combustion under gas drainage conditions cannot be effectively controlled, accidents similar to the coal spontaneous combustion-induced gas explosion at the Jilin Babao Coal Mine are likely to occur. It is very necessary to carry out research on fire prevention from the perspectives of phenomena and causes to improve its effectiveness.

Many scholars have conducted research on fire prevention in goaf areas, mostly using CFD and experimental methods. Taraba8 conducted numerical simulations of the flow field, mass transfer field and temperature field in the goaf under advancing conditions, and determined that the advancement rate should be greater than 1 m per day. Yuan9 conducted numerical simulations of different inert gas injection positions and established that different injection positions should be considered under different conditions. Wang10 conducted a numerical simulation study on the explosion hazard zone in the goaf under drainage conditions and concluded that injecting inert gas into the deep part is more effective than injecting it into the shallow part. Liu11 injected inert gas in the gap between the oxidation zone and the heat dispersion zone, effectively reducing the area of the oxidation zone. Shi12 researched injecting liquid nitrogen into the goaf areas to lower the temperature. Zhang13 conducted numerical simulations of different inert gas injection positions to determine the reasonable injection position and flow rate of inert gas. Zhang14 utilized nitrogen to suppress the spontaneous combustion of secondary oxidized coal, significantly reducing the fire-extinguishing cycle compared to natural fire suppression. Ding15 found that CO2 could raise the emergence temperature of CO in coal by 5℃ and reduce the concentration of CO. Yan16 conducted experiments on the suppression of spontaneous combustion by CO2 and nitrogen, and determined from the perspective of oxygen consumption rate that CO2 was more effective. Zhou17,18 compared the effects of CO2 and N2 on coal spontaneous combustion from the perspective of functional groups and conducted research on free radicals under different oxygen concentrations, determining the key temperatures and concentrations. Many scholars have prepared materials to prevent coal from spontaneous combustion from different perspectives19–26.

Inert gas injection and air leakage sealing are widely used in coal mines due to their simplicity. However, there is a lack of research on the influence of inert gas injection depth and air leakage sealing length under the conditions of high and low level suction roadway on the area of the oxidation zone and the reasons for this influence, which requires further investigation. This paper takes the 8316 working face of Yangquan No. 5 Mine as the background. After conducting a programmed temperature rise experiment, the coal spontaneous combustion parameters obtained are substituted into the multi-field coupling equation of the goaf. A study was conducted on five positions of inert gas injection ports and 199 lengths of air leakage sealing. The influence laws of inert gas injection depth and air leakage sealing distance on the oxidation zone are analyzed from both qualitative and quantitative perspectives, and the reasons for these laws are explained from the perspectives of the flow field and concentration flux field. Finally, reasonable fire prevention process parameters are obtained.

Experimental methods and multi-field coupling models

Experimental methods for the spontaneous combustion characteristics of coal

The experiments on the oxygen consumption characteristics of coal samples were conducted using a self-built programmed temperature rising experimental system, as shown in Fig. 1. 19 g of coal sample with a particle size of 40–80 mesh was loaded into the reaction tube for the experiment. The temperature of the furnace was set at 30 ℃-100 ℃ with a test point every 10 ℃, and 100 ℃-200 ℃ with a test point every 20℃. The heating rate was 1 ℃/min from 30 ℃-100℃ and 2℃/min from 100 ℃-200 ℃. After reaching the temperature point, the system was held for 20 min to ensure that the coal body was fully heated. The inlet gas atmosphere was dry air with an oxygen concentration of 20.5% and a flow rate of 50 ml/min (under standard conditions).

The intake airflow roadway is 4 m in height and 5.2 m in width, while the return airway is 4 m in height and 5 m in width. The goaf is set to be 200 m in strike, 200 m in the dip direction, and 75 m in height. The geometric configuration is shown in Fig. 2.

Results and discussion

Oxygen consumption rate of spontaneous combustion of coal

The experimental results are shown in Fig. 3. The oxygen concentration at the outlet rapidly decreases as the temperature rises. From the oxygen consumption rate, it can be seen that at 30 ℃ and 37 ℃, the oxygen consumption rates are relatively close. However, when the temperature of the coal sample rises to 45 ℃, there is a slight increase in the oxygen consumption rate, and then the oxygen consumption rate increases even more significantly. At this point, the coal begins to accelerate oxidation, which may trigger spontaneous combustion. This proves that anthracite is indeed not prone to spontaneous combustion at around 30 ℃, but once it accumulates heat or is heated externally, it may accelerate oxidation and trigger spontaneous combustion. This is consistent with the previously stated view that when the coal mining process is slow, anthracite may accumulate heat and cause spontaneous combustion.

The distribution of the oxidation zone without fire prevention measures

Taking a cross-sectional height of 0.5 m to calculate the oxidation zones, the oxygen concentration range of the oxidation zone is calculated as 18% to 10%. As shown in Fig. 4, before fire prevention and extinguishing measures were taken, the simulated range of the oxidation zone on the intake air side was 34.15–77.9 m. According to the measurement results of the oxidation zone in the same coal seam working face, the range of the oxidation zone on the intake air side was 33.8–78 m. The numerical simulation results of the oxidation zone width are basically the same as the historical data, which proves that the results are relatively reliable.

The influence of the depth of the inert gas injection port on the oxidation zone and the reasons for the influence

The influence of the depth of the inert gas injection port on the morphology of the oxidation zone

As shown in Fig. 5, when the inert gas injection port is relatively close to the working face, the reduction in the extent of the oxidation zone in the dip direction is relatively small. The best effect is achieved when the inert gas injection position is at the front of the oxidation zone and relatively close to it, which has a good reduction effect on both the strike and dip directions of the oxidation zone. After entering the oxidation zone, the inert gas injection effect rapidly decreases. This is because at this time, the inert gas, under the influence of the air leakage flow field, migrates to the deeper part of the goaf, and its coverage capacity for the dip direction of the oxidation zone decreases.

The influence of the depth of the inert gas injection Port on the area of the oxidation zone

Analysis of the reasons for the influence of the depth of the inert gas injection port on the oxidation zone

To accurately analyze the cause of the influence law of the inert gas injection port depth on the oxidation zone area, the flow velocity field is analyzed first. According to the different depths of the inert gas injection port, it can be classified into three types.

① If the inert gas injection port is located in the heat dissipation zone, the inert gas will be affected by the air leakage flow field. At this time, the gas flow rate in the heat dissipation zone is relatively fast, causing the inert gas to move rapidly towards the deep part of the goaf and fail to move in the dip direction in time, resulting in poor coverage of the inert gas over the oxidation zone in the dip direction.

② If the inert gas injection port is located near the junction of the heat dissipation zone and the oxidation zone, although the inert gas will move towards the deep part of the goaf due to the air leakage flow field, the air leakage flow rate here is relatively slow, so the inert gas has a stronger ability to move in the dip direction compared to when the injection port is located in the dissipation zone. At this time, the inert gas can cover both the deep part of the goaf and some areas in the dip direction.

③ If the inert gas injection port is located further into the oxidation zone or in the suffocation zone, although the gas flow rate is slower at this time, allowing the inert gas to have a better ability to move in the dip direction, due to its overly rearward location, the inert gas injection port misses part of the oxidation zone, weakening the coverage ability over the front part of the oxidation zone, resulting in insufficient coverage of the inert gas over the oxidation zone.

To further verify this conclusion, streamline diagrams at a height of 0.5 m, nitrogen flux maps, and oxidation zone distribution maps were drawn.

As shown in Fig. 7, from the streamline diagram, it can be seen that as the inert gas injection port gradually deepens, the gas flow capacity at the inert gas injection port towards the return air side increases; that is, the coverage ability in the inclined direction is stronger.

Streamlines at different depths of inert gas injection ports.

As shown in Fig. 8, it can be intuitively seen from the nitrogen flow line diagram that as the inert gas injection port advances deeper into the goaf, the coverage capacity of the inert gas in the dip direction increases. However, as the inert gas injection port advances deeper into the goaf, the inert gas will also miss the front part of the oxidation zone. Therefore, considering comprehensively, the effect is best when the inert gas injection port is located near the junction of the heat dissipation zone and the oxidation zone.

Nitrogen flux lines and oxidation zone distribution at different inert gas injection depths.

This proves that for inert gas injection, a drag pipe should be used to control the inert gas injection point to always remain at a certain depth into the goaf, keeping the injection position at the optimal point.

The influence of different lengths of air leakage sealing on the oxidation zone

The influence of the distance of air leakage sealing on the morphology of the oxidation zone

Due to the large number of groups, only the distribution of the oxidation zone with the sealing leakage air length being an integer multiple of 20 m is presented here to obtain the influence law of the sealing leakage air length on the morphology of the oxidation zone. As shown in Fig. 9, it can be seen that the oxidation zone on the intake air side changes significantly with the increase of the sealing leakage air distance, gradually shifting towards the working face and the return air side. This transformation of the oxidation zone morphology is conducive to heat conduction and can reduce the temperature in the goaf. In case of coal spontaneous combustion leading to excessive carbon monoxide, due to the oxidation zone being closer to the working face, when inert gas injection, three-phase foam injection and other fire prevention and extinguishing measures are taken, the diffusion and flow are more likely to cover the high-temperature points, effectively improving the fire extinguishing efficiency. Even if there is no coal spontaneous combustion, it can also reduce the difficulty of fire prevention. However, the oxidation zone on the return air side gradually moves backward and widens, which is not conducive to fire prevention. Therefore, a balance point needs to be found.

The influence of air leakage sealing distance on the area of oxidation zone

When classifying the oxidation zone based on oxygen concentration, the oxidation zone is also divided according to wind speed. Areas where the wind speed is between 0.1 and 0.24 m per minute and where the wind speed component along the strike direction is greater than 0 are also classified as the oxidation zone.

As shown in Fig. 10, the area of the oxidation zone based on wind speed continuously decreases with the increase of the distance of the air leakage seal, and drops rapidly after 150 m. The reason is that the wind direction changes at this time. The area of the oxidation zone based on oxygen concentration shows a trend of first slowly decreasing, then rapidly decreasing, and finally stabilizing and slightly increasing. When the oxygen concentration is used as the judgment index, the relationship between the distance of the air leakage sealing and the reduction value of the oxidation zone area is approximately in the form of an S-shaped function.

Analysis of the reasons for the influence of the distance of air leakage sealing on the oxidation zone

The velocity streamline diagram at a height of 0.5 m and the distribution map of the oxidation zone determined by oxygen concentration and wind speed were drawn. As shown in Fig. 11, the black area represents the oxidation zone determined by oxygen concentration, and the red area represents the oxidation zone determined by wind speed.

Streamline and oxidation zone distribution under different lengths of air leakage sealing.

Due to the suction, the air leakage volume of the working face increases. The suction roadway is located relatively close to the return airway. If the air leakage on the intake air side can be blocked, the starting point of the air flow extracted from the working face in the suction roadway will shift towards the return air side. This can significantly reduce the area through which the air leakage is caused by suction.

When the distance for sealing the air leakage is less than 40 m, the oxidation zone determined by oxygen concentration shows no significant change. This is because when the distance for sealing the air leakage is relatively short, the length of the oxidation zone along the inclined direction does not change much. However, when it exceeds 40 m, the length of the oxidation zone along the inclined direction decreases significantly, thereby greatly reducing the area of the oxidation zone. The reason why the change trend gradually levels off at approximately 150 m is that the midpoint of the projections of the high and low extraction roadways is approximately at 150 m. At this point, the air leakage channel is basically the shortest, so the change trend of the oxidation zone gradually levels off. In conclusion, sealing air leakage should not blindly pursue length; otherwise, it may backfire. The distance should be determined based on actual conditions. Under the conditions studied in this paper, the reasonable distance for sealing air leakage starting from the intake side is 40 to 150 m.

Conclusions

The best effect is achieved when the inert gas injection port is located at the junction of the oxidation zone and the heat dissipation zone. This is because the inert gas coverage is most balanced at this time. The method of injecting inert gas through a drag tube should be adopted.

The length of the wind leakage sealing should not be blindly increased, as it may be counterproductive. Under the conditions of high extraction roadway and low extraction roadway, the maximum distance should be the average of the distances from the extraction roadway to the intake roadway.
