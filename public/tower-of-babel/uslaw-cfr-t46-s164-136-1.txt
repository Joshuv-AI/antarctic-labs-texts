46 C.F.R. § 164.136-1 — -1 Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:46:164.136-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-164.136-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 164 (Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes requirements for approval of fire doors as required by the International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea (SOLAS). Products approved under these requirements may be used where fire doors of the same class are required in domestic vessels.
