34 C.F.R. § 200.87 — Responsibilities for participation of children in private schools.
Citation: us:cfr:34:200.87
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-200.87
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 200 (Title I—Improving The Academic Achievement Of The Disadvantaged). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An SEA and its operating agencies must conduct programs and projects under this subpart in a manner consistent with the basic requirements of section 8501 of the ESEA.
