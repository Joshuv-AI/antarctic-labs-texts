19 U.S.C. § 1487 — Value in entry; amendment
Citation: us:usc:19:1487
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1487
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part III (Ascertainment, Collection, And Recovery Of Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The importer of record or his agent may, under such regulations as the Secretary of the Treasury may prescribe, at the time entry is made, make in the entry such additions to or deductions from the cost or value given in the invoice as, in his opinion, may raise or lower the same to the value of such merchandise.
