11 C.F.R. § 111.14 — Witness fees and mileage (52 U.S.C. 30107 (a)(5)).
Citation: us:cfr:11:111.14
Creator: Federal Election Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-11/section-111.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 111 (COMPLIANCE PROCEDURE (52 U.S.C. 30109, 30107(a))). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Witnesses subpoenaed to appear for depositions shall be paid the same fees and mileage as witnesses in the courts of the United States. Such fees may be tendered at the time the witness appears for such deposition, or within a reasonable time thereafter.
