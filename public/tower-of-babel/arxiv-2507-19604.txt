On finite β \beta -expansions for the set of natural numbers
Túlio O. Carvalho, Catharina M. Moreira

Abstract
We present a study of the problem of finiteness of the β \beta -expansions for the set of natural numbers, condition F 1 F_{1} in brief, for three families of Pisot numbers for which the β \beta -expansion of 1 is not a non-decreasing sequence. We show a class of simple β \beta -numbers which display a puzzling behaviour, in the sense that an infinite subset of such β \beta satisfy F 1 F_{1} , whereas a complementary infinite subset does not. This puzzle is organized by the residue class modulo 3 of an integer coefficient of the main family of polynomials discussed. We prove that, when F 1 F_{1} does not hold, the complement of Fin ⁡ ( β ) {\rm Fin}(\beta) is infinite. Finally, we give a concise sufficient condition for F 1 F_{1} , which is the finitude of the β \beta -expansion of ( β − ⌊ β ⌋ ) 2 (\beta-\lfloor\beta\rfloor)^{2} .
