18 C.F.R. § 420.33 — Payment of bills.
Citation: us:cfr:18:420.33
Creator: Delaware River Basin Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-420.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 420 (Basin Regulations—Water Supply Charges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The amount due for each quarter shall bear interest at the rate of 1 percent per month for each day it is unpaid beginning 30 days after the due date of the quarterly report for the first three quarters and 30 days after the bill is rendered for the fourth quarter.
