44 U.S.C. § 1704 — Superintendent of Documents: pay of employees for night, Sunday, holiday, and overtime work
Citation: us:usc:44:1704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1704
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 17 (Distribution And Sale Of Public Documents). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Employees in the office of the Superintendent of Documents may be paid for night, Sunday, holiday, and overtime work at rates not in excess of the rates of additional pay for this work allowed other employees of the Government Publishing Office under section 305 of this title.
