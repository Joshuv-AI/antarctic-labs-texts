31 C.F.R. § 321.14 — Transmittal to and settlement with a Federal Reserve Processing Site.
Citation: us:cfr:31:321.14
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-321.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 321 (Payments By Banks And Other Financial Institutions Of Definitive United States Savings Bonds And United States Savings Notes (Freedom Shares)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except for eligible securities redeemed on exchange pursuant to § 321.8, a paying agent shall transmit for payment all redeemed securities to a Federal Reserve Processing Site in accordance with the Federal Reserve Operating Circular.
