22 U.S.C. § 290i–4 — Restrictions
Citation: us:usc:22:290i–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section290i–4
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter XXIV (African Development Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
1 Unless authorized by law, neither the President, nor any person or agency, shall, on behalf of the United States—
(1)
subscribe to additional shares of stock of the Bank;
(2)
vote for or agree to any amendment of the agreement which increases the obligations of the United States, or which changes the purpose or functions of the Bank; or
(3)
make a loan or provide other financing to the Bank, except that funds for technical assistance may be provided to the Bank by a United States agency created pursuant to an Act of Congress which is authorized by law to provide funds to international organizations.
