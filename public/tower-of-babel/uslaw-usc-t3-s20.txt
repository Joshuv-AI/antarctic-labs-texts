3 U.S.C. § 20 — Resignation or refusal of office
Citation: us:usc:3:20
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title3-section20
Context: United States Code, Title 3 (The President); Chapter 1 (Presidential Elections And Vacancies). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The only evidence of a refusal to accept, or of a resignation of the office of President or Vice President, shall be an instrument in writing, declaring the same, and subscribed by the person refusing to accept or resigning, as the case may be, and delivered into the office of the Secretary of State.
