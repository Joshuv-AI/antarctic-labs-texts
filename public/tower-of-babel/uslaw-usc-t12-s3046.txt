12 U.S.C. § 3046 — Financial analysis and market surveys at request of eligible cooperative
Citation: us:usc:12:3046
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section3046
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 31 (National Consumer Cooperative Bank); Subchapter II (Office Of Self-Help Development And Technical Assistance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Office may, at the request of any eligible cooperative, provide a financial analysis of the applicant’s capital structure and needs and its cost of operations, survey the market for the goods or services the cooperative makes or desires to make available to its members or patrons or the users of its facilities.
