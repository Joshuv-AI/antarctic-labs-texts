10 C.F.R. § 33.12 — Applications for specific licenses of broad scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:33.12
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-33.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 33 (Specific Domestic Licenses Of Broad Scope For Byproduct Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person may file an application for specific license of broad scope on NRC Form 313, “Application for Material License,” in accordance with the provisions of § 30.32 of this chapter.
