17 C.F.R. § 200.205 — Effect upon existing Commission organization, delegations, and rules.
Citation: us:cfr:17:200.205
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-200.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 200 (Organization; Conduct And Ethics; And Information And Requests). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided herein, all outstanding Commission organizational statements, delegations of authority, orders, rules and regulations shall remain in force and effect during emergency conditions, subject to all lawful requirements and such changes as may be authorized by or in the name of the Chairman or the Commission.
