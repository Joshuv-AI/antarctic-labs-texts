43 C.F.R. § 3427.0-1 — -1 Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3427.0-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3427.0-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3420 (Competitive Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to set out the protection that shall be afforded qualified surface owners of split estate lands (43 CFR 3400.0-5) and the requirements for submission of evidence of written surface owner consent from qualified surface owners of split estate lands.
