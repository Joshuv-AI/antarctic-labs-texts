22 C.F.R. § 309.12 — Purpose.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 309 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart provides Peace Corps' policies and procedures for the collection by salary offset of a Federal employee's pay to satisfy certain past due debts owed the United States Government.
