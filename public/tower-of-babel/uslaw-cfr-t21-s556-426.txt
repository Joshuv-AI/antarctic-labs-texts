21 C.F.R. § 556.426 — Moxidectin.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 556 (Tolerances For Residues Of New Animal Drugs In Food). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Acceptable daily intake (ADI). The ADI for total residue of moxidectin is 4 µg/kg of body weight per day.
(b) Tolerances. The tolerances for moxidectin (marker residue) are:
(1) Cattle. (i) Fat (target tissue): 900 ppb.
(ii) Liver: 200 ppb.
(iii) Muscle: 50 ppb.
(iv) Milk: 40 ppb.
(2) Sheep. (i) Fat (target tissue): 900 ppb.
(ii) Liver: 200 ppb.
(iii) Muscle: 50 ppb.
(c) Related conditions of use. See §§ 520.1454, 522.1450, and 524.1450 of this chapter.
