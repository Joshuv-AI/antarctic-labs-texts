48 C.F.R. § 212.271 — 212.271 Limitation on acquisition of right-hand drive passenger sedans.
Citation: us:cfr:48:212.271
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-212.271
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 212 (Acquisition Of Commercial Products And Commercial Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

10 U.S.C. 2253(a)(2) limits the authority to purchase right-hand drive passenger sedans to a cost of not more than $55,000 per vehicle.
