47 C.F.R. § 96.19 — Operation near Canadian and Mexican borders.
Citation: us:cfr:47:96.19
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-96.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 96 (Citizens Broadband Radio Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Citizens Broadband Radio Service operation in the 3550-3700 MHz band is subject to current and future international agreements with Mexico and Canada. The terms of these agreements shall be implemented by the SAS.
