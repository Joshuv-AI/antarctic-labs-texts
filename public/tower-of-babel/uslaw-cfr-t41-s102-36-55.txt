41 C.F.R. § 102-36.55 — -36.55 Agency responsibilities in transfer order processing.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-36.55
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-36.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-36 (36—Disposition Of Excess Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whether the excess is for your use or for use by a non-Federal recipient that you sponsor, you must:
(a) Ensure that only authorized Federal officials of your agency sign the Standard Form (SF) 122 prior to submission to GSA for approval.
(b) Ensure that excess personal property approved for transfer is used for authorized official purpose(s).
(c) Advise GSA of names of agency officials that are authorized to approve SF 122s and notify GSA of any changes in signatory authority.
