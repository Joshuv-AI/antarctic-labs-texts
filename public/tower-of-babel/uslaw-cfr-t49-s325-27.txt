49 C.F.R. § 325.27 — Use of a windscreen.
Citation: us:cfr:49:325.27
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-325.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 325 (Compliance With Interstate Motor Carrier Noise Emission Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A properly installed windscreen, of the type recommended by the manufacturer of the Sound Level Measurement System, shall be used during the time that noise emission measurements are being taken.
