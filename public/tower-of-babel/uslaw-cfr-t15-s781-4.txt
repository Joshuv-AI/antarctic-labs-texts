15 C.F.R. § 781.4 — U.S. Government requests for information needed to satisfy the requirements of the APR or the Act.
Citation: us:cfr:15:781.4
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-781.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 781 (General Information And Overview Of The Additional Protocol Regulations (Apr)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

From time-to-time, one or more U.S. Government agencies (i.e., the Department of Defense, the Department of Energy, the NRC, or BIS) may contact a location to request information that the U.S. Government has determined to be necessary to satisfy certain requirements of the APR or the Act (e.g., clarification requests or vulnerability assessments). If the manner of providing such information is not specified in the APR, the agency in question will provide the location with appropriate instructions.
