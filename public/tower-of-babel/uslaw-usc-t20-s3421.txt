20 U.S.C. § 3421 — Office of General Counsel
Citation: us:usc:20:3421
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section3421
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 48 (Department Of Education); Subchapter II (Establishment Of The Department). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be in the Department an Office of General Counsel, to be administered by the General Counsel appointed under section 3412(b) of this title. The General Counsel shall provide legal assistance to the Secretary concerning the programs and policies of the Department.
