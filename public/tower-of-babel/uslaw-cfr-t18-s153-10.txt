18 C.F.R. § 153.10 — Authorization not exclusive.
Citation: us:cfr:18:153.10
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-153.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 153 (Applications For Authorization To Construct, Operate, Or Modify Facilities Used For The Export Or Import Of Natural Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No authorization granted pursuant to subpart B of this part and section 3 of the Natural Gas Act shall be deemed to prevent the Commission from granting authorization under subpart B to any other person at the same general location, or to prevent any other person from making application for such authorization.
