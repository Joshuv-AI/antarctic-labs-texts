2 C.F.R. § 1130.505 — Content of PROP Article V.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1130.505
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1130.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1130 (Property Administration: General Award Terms And Conditions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A DoD Component's general terms and conditions must either:
(a) Include the wording appendix E to this part provides for PROP Article V to specify requirements for use and disposition of federally owned property; or
(b) Reserve PROP Article V if there is no possibility of recipients or subrecipients being accountable for federally owned property under awards using those terms and conditions.
