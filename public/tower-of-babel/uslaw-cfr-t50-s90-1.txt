50 C.F.R. § 90.1 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:50:90.1
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-90.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 90 (Feeding Depredating Migratory Waterfowl). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person having an interest in a crop and who is suffering damage due to depredations by migratory waterfowl may file a complaint and apply for surplus grain for use in feeding programs to augment the natural source of food available to migratory waterfowl to aid in the prevention of crop damage by such birds, as provided for in these regulations.
