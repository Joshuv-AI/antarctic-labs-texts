12 C.F.R. § 713.7 — May the NCUA Board require a federally insured credit union to secure additional insurance coverage?
Citation: us:cfr:12:713.7
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-713.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 713 (Fidelity Bond And Insurance Coverage For Federally Insured Credit Unions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The NCUA Board may require additional coverage when the NCUA Board determines that a federally insured credit union's current coverage is inadequate. The federally insured credit union must purchase this additional coverage within 30 days.
