47 C.F.R. § 9.21 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:47:9.21
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-9.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 9 (911 Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules in this subpart apply to States or taxing jurisdictions that collect 911 fees or charges (as defined in this subpart) from commercial mobile services, IP-enabled voice services, and other emergency communications services.
