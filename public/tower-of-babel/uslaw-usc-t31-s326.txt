31 U.S.C. § 326 — Availability of appropriations for certain expenses
Citation: us:usc:31:326
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section326
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 3 (Department Of The Treasury); Subchapter II (Administrative). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary of the Treasury, an appropriation for the Department of the Treasury available to pay travel expenses also is available to pay expenses to attend meetings of organizations related to the function or activity for which the appropriation is made.
(b)
The Secretary may approve reimbursement to agents on protective missions for subsistence expenses authorized by law without regard to rates and amounts established under section 5702 of title 5.
