22 U.S.C. § 3009 — Printing and binding costs
Citation: us:usc:22:3009
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3009
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 45 (Commission On Security And Cooperation In Europe). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of costs relating to printing and binding, including the costs of personnel detailed from the Government Publishing Office, the Commission shall be deemed to be a committee of the Congress.
