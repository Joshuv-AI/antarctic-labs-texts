10 U.S.C. § 2034 — Educational institutions not maintaining units of Junior Reserve Officers’ Training Corps: issuance of arms, tentage, and equipment
Citation: us:usc:10:2034
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2034
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 102 (Junior Reserve Officers’ Training Corps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of a military department may issue arms, tentage, and equipment to an educational institution at which no unit of the Junior Reserve Officers’ Training Corps is maintained if the educational institution—
(1)
offers a course in military training prescribed by that Secretary; and
(2)
has a student body of at least 50 students who are in a grade above the eighth grade.
