31 U.S.C. § 711 — General authority
Citation: us:usc:31:711
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section711
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 7 (Government Accountability Office); Subchapter II (General Duties And Powers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Comptroller General may—
(1)
prescribe regulations to carry out the duties and powers of the Comptroller General;
(2)
delegate the duties and powers of the Comptroller General to officers and employees of the Government Accountability Office as the Comptroller General decides is necessary to carry out those duties and powers;
(3)
regulate the practice of representatives of persons before the Office; and
(4)
administer oaths to witnesses when auditing and settling accounts.
