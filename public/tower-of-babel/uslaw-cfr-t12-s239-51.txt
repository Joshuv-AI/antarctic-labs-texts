12 C.F.R. § 239.51 — Acquiring another insured stock depository institution as part of a conversion.
Citation: us:cfr:12:239.51
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-239.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 239 (Mutual Holding Companies (Regulation Mm)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a mutual holding company converts to stock form, the subsidiary savings association may acquire for cash or stock another insured depository institution that is already in the stock form of ownership.
