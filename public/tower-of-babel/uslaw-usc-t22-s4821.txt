22 U.S.C. § 4821 — Diplomatic Security Service
Citation: us:usc:22:4821
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4821
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 58 (Diplomatic Security); Subchapter II (Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of State may establish a Diplomatic Security Service, which shall perform such functions as the Secretary may determine.
