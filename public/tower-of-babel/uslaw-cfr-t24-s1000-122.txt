24 C.F.R. § 1000.122 — May NAHASDA grant funds be used as matching funds to obtain and leverage funding, including any Federal or state program and still be considered an affordable housing activity?
Citation: us:cfr:24:1000.122
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1000.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1000 (Native American Housing Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There is no prohibition in NAHASDA against using grant funds as matching funds.
