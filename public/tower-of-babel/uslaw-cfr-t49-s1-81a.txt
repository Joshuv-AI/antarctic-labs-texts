49 C.F.R. § 1.81a — a Redelegation by all Administrators.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1.81a
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1.81a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1 (Organization And Delegation Of Powers And Duties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise specifically provided in this part, each Administrator may redelegate and authorize successive redelegations of authority within the organization under that official's jurisdiction.
