46 C.F.R. § 160.174-1 — -1 Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:46:160.174-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-160.174-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 160 (Lifesaving Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart contains construction and performance requirements, and approval tests for thermal protective aids that are designed to minimize the occurrence of or aid in the recovery from hypothermia (lowered body temperature) during long periods in a survival craft.
