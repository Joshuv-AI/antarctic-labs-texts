54 U.S.C. § 103305 — Travel expenses incident to study of battlefields
Citation: us:usc:54:103305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section103305
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1033 (National Military Parks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Mileage of officers of the Army and actual expenses of civilian employees traveling on duty in connection with the studies, surveys, and field investigations of battlefields shall be paid from the appropriations made to meet expenses for those purposes.
