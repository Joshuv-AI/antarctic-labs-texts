42 C.F.R. § 1002.5 — State plan requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:42:1002.5
Creator: Office Of Inspector General-Health Care, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-1002.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 1002 (Program Integrity—State-Initiated Exclusions From Medicaid). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The plan must provide that the requirements of this subpart are met. However, the provisions of these regulations are minimum requirements. The agency may impose broader sanctions if it has the authority to do so under State law.
