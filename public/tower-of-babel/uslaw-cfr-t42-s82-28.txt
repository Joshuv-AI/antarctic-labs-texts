42 C.F.R. § 82.28 — Who can review NIOSH dose reconstruction files on individual claimants?
Citation: us:cfr:42:82.28
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-82.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 82 (Methods For Conducting Dose Reconstruction Under The Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program Act Of 2000). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Claimants and DOL will be provided individual dose reconstruction files, upon request. Claimants should note, however, that a complete summary of the data and methods used in a dose reconstruction will be included in the “NIOSH Report of Dose Reconstruction under EEOICPA”.
(b) Researchers and the public will be provided limited access to NIOSH dose reconstruction files, subject to provisions and restrictions of the Privacy Act for the protection of confidential information on individuals.
