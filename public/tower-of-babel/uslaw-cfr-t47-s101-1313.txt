47 C.F.R. § 101.1313 — License term.
Citation: us:cfr:47:101.1313
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-101.1313
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 101 (Fixed Microwave Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The license term for stations authorized under this subpart is ten years from the date of original issuance or renewal.
