40 U.S.C. § 3707 — Contractor certification or contract clause in acquisition of commercial items not required
Citation: us:usc:40:3707
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section3707
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part A (General); Chapter 37 (Contract Work Hours And Safety Standards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In a contract to acquire a commercial product (as defined in section 103 of title 41) or a commercial service (as defined in section 103a of title 41), a certification by a contractor or a contract clause may not be required to implement a prohibition or requirement in this chapter.
