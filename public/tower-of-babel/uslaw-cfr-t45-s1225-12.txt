45 C.F.R. § 1225.12 — Precomplaint procedure.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1225.12
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1225.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1225 (Member And Volunteer Discrimination Complaint Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant, candidate, member or volunteer who believes that he or she is among a group of present or former CNCS national service applicants, candidates, members or volunteers, who have been illegally discriminated against and who wants to be an agent for the class shall follow those precomplaint procedures outlined in § 1225.8.
