7 U.S.C. § 2542 — Grandfather clause
Citation: us:usc:7:2542
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2542
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 57 (Plant Variety Protection); Subchapter III (Plant Variety Protection And Rights); Part K (Infringement Of Plant Variety Protection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall abridge the right of any person, or the successor in interest of the person, to reproduce or sell a variety developed and produced by such person more than one year prior to the effective filing date of an adverse application for a certificate of plant variety protection.
