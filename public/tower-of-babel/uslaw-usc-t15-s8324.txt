15 U.S.C. § 8324 — Savings clause
Citation: us:usc:15:8324
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section8324
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 109 (Wall Street Transparency And Accountability); Subchapter I (Regulation Of Over-The-Counter Swaps Markets); Part B (Regulation Of Swap Markets). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of this title,1 nothing in this subtitle shall be construed as divesting any appropriate Federal banking agency of any authority it may have to establish or enforce, with respect to a person for which such agency is the appropriate Federal banking agency, prudential or other standards pursuant to authority granted by Federal law other than this title.1
