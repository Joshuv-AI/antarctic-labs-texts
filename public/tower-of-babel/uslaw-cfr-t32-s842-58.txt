32 C.F.R. § 842.58 — Right of subrogation, indemnity, and contribution.
Citation: us:cfr:32:842.58
Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-842.58
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 842 (Administrative Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Air Force has all the rights of subrogation, indemnity and contribution, as local law permits. However, settlement authorities will not seek contribution or indemnity from U.S. military members or civilian employees whose conduct gave rise to U.S. Government liability, or whenever it would be harmful to international relations.
