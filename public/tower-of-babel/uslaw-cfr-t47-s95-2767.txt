47 C.F.R. § 95.2767 — MURS transmitting power limit.
Citation: us:cfr:47:95.2767
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-95.2767
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 95 (Personal Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each MURS transmitter type must be designed such that the transmitter power output does not exceed 2 Watts under normal operating conditions.
