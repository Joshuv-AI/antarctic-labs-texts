34 U.S.C. § 10726 — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:34:10726
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10726
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXXIX (Prevention, Investigation, And Prosecution Of White Collar Crime). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Bureau of Justice Assistance shall promulgate such rules and regulations as are necessary to carry out this subchapter, including rules and regulations for submitting and reviewing applications under section 10725 of this title.
