46 U.S.C. § 31302 — Availability of instruments, copies, and information
Citation: us:usc:46:31302
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section31302
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 313 (Commercial Instruments And Maritime Liens); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall—
(1)
make any instrument filed or recorded with the Secretary under this chapter available for public inspection;
(2)
on request, provide a copy, including a certified copy, of any instrument made available for public inspection under this chapter; and
(3)
on request, provide a certificate containing information included in an instrument filed or recorded under this chapter.
