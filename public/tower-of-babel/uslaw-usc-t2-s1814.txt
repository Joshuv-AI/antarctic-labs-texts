2 U.S.C. § 1814 — Repairs of Capitol
Citation: us:usc:2:1814
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section1814
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 28 (Architect Of The Capitol); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All improvements, alterations, additions, and repairs of the Capitol Building shall be made by the direction and under the supervision of the Architect of the Capitol.
