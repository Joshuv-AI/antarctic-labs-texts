2 U.S.C. § 287a — Parliamentarian
Citation: us:usc:2:287a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section287a
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 9C (Office Of Parliamentarian Of House Of Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The management, supervision, and administration of the Office shall be vested in the Parliamentarian, who shall be appointed by the Speaker of the House of Representatives without regard to political affiliation and solely on the basis of fitness to perform the duties of the position. Any person so appointed shall serve at the pleasure of the Speaker.
