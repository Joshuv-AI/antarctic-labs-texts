14 C.F.R. § 151.99 — Modifications of programming standards.
Citation: us:cfr:14:151.99
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-151.99
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 151 (Federal Aid To Airports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, Airports, Service, or the Regional Director concerned may, on individual projects, when necessary for adaptation to meet local conditions, modify any standard set forth in or incorporated into this subpart, if he determines that the modification will provide an acceptable level of safety, economy, durability, or workmanship.
