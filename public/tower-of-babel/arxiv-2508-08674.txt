Abstract
. Motivated by Schoen’s conjecture on the volume functional for closed hyperbolic manifolds, we generalize the volume comparison theorem of Hu-Ji-Shi and establish an volume comparison result for symmetric spaces of non-compact type of rank 1 under a scalar curvature condition. Furthermore, we establish a rigidity result. Our proof utilizes the normalized Ricci-DeTurck flow to analyze the asymptotic behavior of the volume functional and derive monotonicity properties. This result extends the classical volume comparison framework to non-compact symmetric spaces.



1.
Introduction

The volume of a Riemannian manifold is a fundamental geometric quantity that plays a central role in geometric analysis. Classical examples highlighting its importance include various volume comparison theorems. In the Bishop-Gromov volume comparison theorem, a lower bound on the Ricci curvature is always assumed. However, in
[
17
]
, R. Schoen proposed the following conjecture concerning the volume functional
\mathrm{Vol}(\cdot)
on a closed hyperbolic manifold.

Conjecture 1.1
.

Let
(M^{n},g_{0})
be a closed hyperbolic manifold. Let
g
be another metric on
M
with scalar curvature
R(g)\geq R(g_{0})
, then
\mathrm{Vol}_{g}(M)\geq\mathrm{Vol}_{g_{0}}(M)
.

This conjecture remained largely open until the three-dimensional case was resolved as a corollary of the work of R.Hamilton and G.Perelman on the geometrization conjecture
[
14
,
13
]
. Subsequently, I.Agol, P.Storm, and W.Thurston established similar results for compact hyperbolic 3-manifolds with minimal surface boundaries
[
1
]
. In
[
12
]
, P.Miao and L.Tam demonstrated that the conjecture for closed manifolds does not directly extend to manifolds with boundary under only Dirichlet boundary conditions. Specifically, they provided a counterexample involving geodesic balls in three-dimensional hyperbolic space, using variations of the volume functional under scalar curvature constraints. In
[
21
]
, W.Yuan prove the a volume comparison theorem for geodesic balls in V-Static spaces under some boundary conditions. A natural question is whether an analogous conjecture or result holds for noncompact manifolds with hyperbolic metrics, or more generally, for conformally compact manifolds. In
[
8
]
, X.Hu, D.Ji and Y.Shi give an affirmative answer for perturbative setting. In this paper, we generalize their result to symmetric spaces of non-compact type of rank 1.

In order to state our main result, we need to introduce some notations and basic concepts. Let
(M^{n},g_{0})
be a Riemannian manifold. We will use
\|\cdot\|_{C^{k}}
for the global norm,
|\cdot|
for the pointwise norm,

(or simply
d(x)
) for the distance function between
x,p_{0}\in M
,

for the covariant derivative,
R_{ijkl}
for the Riemann curvature tensor with indices
i,j,k,l
,
R_{ij}
(or simply
\mathrm{Rm}
) for the Ricci curvature tensor with indices
i,j
,

for the geodesic ball at center
x\in M
with radius

and
d\mu_{g_{0}}
the volume form. All these are with respect to the background metric
g_{0}
. Moreover, we use
g_{0}^{ij}
for the inverse of the metric
g_{0}
and
\hat{g}^{ij}
for the inverse of another metric
\hat{g}
.

We call an isometry
\Phi:M\rightarrow M
with
\Phi(p)=p
, a reflection at
p\in M
, if
d\Phi|_{p}=-\mathrm{id}_{T_{p}M}
. A Riemannian manifold

is called a

if for arbitrary point

. One of most important properties of symmetric spaces is that the Riemann curvature tensors of symmetric spaces are parallel, i.e.

By the de-Rham Decomposition Theorem, the universal covering of the symmetric spaces can be expressed as a product

of irreducible symmetric spaces. All

of

(1.1)

\displaystyle V_{g_{0}}(g)=\lim_{i\rightarrow\infty}\left(\mathrm{Vol}(\Omega_{i},g)-\mathrm{Vol}(\Omega_{i},g_{0})\right)

where

is an arbitrary exhaustion of

by compact sets. We shall see that if

, where

exists (See the
Lemma

). Along the idea of
[
8
]
, we will utilize the normalized Ricci-DeTurck flow (NRDF for short in the seq) to prove the following "volume" comparison theorem for symmetric space of non-compact type of rank 1.

Theorem 1.1

Suppose that

is a simply connected irreducible symmetric space of non-compact type of rank 1 with

In particular, if

Remark 1.1

If

This paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we review the foundational geometric framework of symmetric spaces including the definition of irreducible symmetric spaces of non-compact type, their root systems and key decomposition theorems. Additionally, we derive explicit expressions for curvature and Laplacian operators along geodesics, which are critical for later analysis. In Section 3, we introduce the normalized Ricci flow and the normalized Ricci-DeTurck flow and review the short-time existence results and stability results under some perturbation conditions. In Section 4, following the approach of
[

4.2
and lemma

Theorem

1.1

2.

Normalized Ricci flows and normalized Ricci-DeTurck flows

In this section, we introduce the normalized Ricci flow and the normalized DeTurck Ricci flow, along with their stability results on symmetric spaces of non-compact type.

3.1.

Let

(3.1)

\displaystyle\left\{\begin{aligned} \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\tilde{g}(t)&=-2\left(\mathrm{Ric}[\tilde{g}(t)]-\frac{R_{0}}{n}\tilde{g}(t)\right)\\

(3.2)

\hat{g}(\cdot,0)&=g\end{aligned}\right.

where

(3.3)

\displaystyle W_{j}=\hat{g}_{jk}\hat{g}^{pq}\left(\hat{\Gamma}_{pq}^{k}-\Gamma_{pq}^{k}\right),

We see that NRF and NRDF are both non-linear equations, a common way to tackling the non-linear equations is to do the linearization. Define operator
