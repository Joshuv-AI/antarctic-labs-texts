Study on terrain acquisition and processing technology of BDS-3 auxiliary mountain highway
Authors: Guangtai Lin, Shijian Li, Jianjun Wang, Yongyou Li, Jingjun Qin, Rong Yan
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractThe operation mode of UAV equipped with LiDAR can significantly reduce the efficiency and cost of highway terrain acquisition. At present, due to the complicated terrain and weak signal of mountain road, it is difficult to collect terrain data. In view of the above problems, this paper puts forward the technology of BDS assisted terrain acquisition and processing on complex mountain roads. This paper describes in detail the new signal unfuzzy acquisition and tracking algorithm and multi-antenna positioning and attitude determination technology and equipment of BDS-3 mountain highway, through millimeter wave Lidar altimetry technology, imitation ground flight, air-ground multi-source data combined with air three-direction method and other technologies. To realize efficient 3D collection and 3D reconstruction of massive original images of terrain of complex mountain traffic lines in the range of 100 km. Finally, a case study of 100 km mountain road is taken to verify the effectiveness of the method.Keywords: BDS-3, Mountain road, UAV, LIDAR, Terrain acquisition, Real 3DSubject terms: Engineering, Civil engineering

Introduction

Compared with the traditional highway construction manual RTK (Real-time Kinematic) survey operation, the UAV-equipped LiDAR operation mode can cover a large area in a short time without being restricted by the complex terrain in mountainous areas, and obtain three-dimensional spatial information with more accurate data and safer operation. However, due to the sparse distribution of base stations in mountainous areas and the weak signal strength, traditional GPS signals often experience coverage blind spots, causing communication interruptions in data acquisition terminals. The development of portable BDS-3 positioning devices and aerial scanning with LiDAR mounted on UAVs are considered key technologies for terrain acquisition in mountain road construction. This technology, first introduced in the surveying and mapping industry, primarily utilizes UAV LiDAR to scan densely vegetated areas, generating laser point clouds. These 3D point clouds are then processed using air-ground integrated joint settlement algorithms and filtering techniques to remove vegetation, resulting in a 3D surface model applicable in earth and rock acquisition, temporary construction planning, schedule management, and environmental monitoring. It is estimated that this method costs only 10% of traditional manual RTK operations1, 2.

The proliferation of UAV technology offers new opportunities for re-surveying original terrain and has established UAV LiDAR as a key method for acquiring highway three-dimensional terrain data3–6.For example, Meng et al.4–6 systematically discussed error sources and improvement measures of airborne LiDAR technology, demonstrating its efficacy in monitoring surface deformations, landslides, and natural disasters. Recent studies have shown that UAV-LiDAR is superior to photogrammetric point clouds in dense vegetation and complex terrain conditions7, 8.

To further enhance this manuscript’s impact, we acknowledge the importance of integrating advanced data analytics and machine learning algorithms in terrain acquisition technologies. Machine learning (ML) techniques have been increasingly applied to complex data analytics challenges, such as predicting material properties9, 10, detecting defects in amorphous alloys11, and mapping the evolution of grain boundaries in materials12. Recent studies have demonstrated the effectiveness of ML in identifying flow defects in metallic glasses13 and predicting atomic energetics in alloys14. Moreover, computational methods to analyze complex energy landscapes have provided valuable insights into the physical properties and behaviors of materials under stress15–17. The understanding of such energy landscapes has been enhanced through studies like those on fractal energy landscapes18 and defect correlations in metallic glasses19. Applying these advanced analytical methods to terrain data could yield significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency.

The novelty of this paper lies in the introduction of a three-dimensional terrain acquisition and processing technology based on BDS-3 satellite positioning, which enables the efficient three-dimensional capture and reconstruction of vast original terrain images along complex mountainous roadways over an area of 100 km. This represents an innovative extension of the application scope for BDS-3 satellite positioning technology, significantly enhancing the quality of terminal received signals and providing an efficient solution for the terrain acquisition and three-dimensional reconstruction of mountainous highways. The main contribution of this paper is the development of this novel three-dimensional terrain acquisition and processing technology, which not only improves the accuracy of terrain acquisition but also significantly increases the efficiency of three-dimensional reconstruction, offering robust support for the digital construction and management of mountainous highways.

BDS-3 mountain signal enhancement

BOC(n,n) fuzzy acquisition and tracking algorithm of BDS-3

Because of the complex terrain and few base stations, the signal in mountainous areas is generally weak. The traditional GPS signal coverage has a blind area, and the data acquisition terminal is prone to communication failure. In order to solve the signal problem in mountainous areas, the general practice is to improve the signal coverage and quality by adding base stations or using enhancers. This study reduces signal errors and effects in mountainous areas through the use of high-precision measurement equipment and techniques. Through the capture algorithm based on the Correlation Shift Side-Peak Cancellation Technique (CSSPeCT), the orthogonal demodulation method is used to generate two carriers with the spatial phase Angle at right angles. To achieve the purpose of removing the carrier after the down-conversion signal. Then, the product of the current subcarrier and the pseudo-random code is multiplied, and the local BOC(Broadcast Organization Clock) signal is generated by spreading the spectrum, which is equally divided into two parts according to the width of each PRN chip, and the first half plus the last half is zeroed to form an odd branch signal. In the same way, the second half and the first half are returned to zero to form an even branch signal, the odd branch signal is coherent with the received signal, and then the coherent value lags half the code slice to take the opposite number, and the obtained new correlation function is multiplied with the original value. BOC tracking ambiguity is eliminated by reconstructing self-subcorrelational function, which is mainly improved in the code tracking loop. Therefore, the receiver still uses the traditional tracking loop to complete carrier synchronization. Figure 2 shows the structure diagram of the code tracking loop proposed by the research and development results of this research and development. When receiving signals, the receiver first tracks the I and Q local carriers generated by the loop for carrier stripping; meanwhile, the code ring generates SPRN1 and SPRNN locally as two local auxiliary codes and performs ahead of lag delay. After the received signal is correlated with the local code, the phase of the local code is adjusted by the phase discriminator and the code NCO (Numerically Controlled Oscillator).

Attitude fixing technology and equipment

Aiming at the difficulties of BDS-3/GNSS satellite navigation signal processing, low positioning accuracy and high monitoring cost in the complex environment of highway engineering, the relationship between satellite navigation signal propagation path, signal processing and high-precision positioning and orientation is studied, and the dual-frequency RTK/INS deep-coupled high-precision positioning and attitude determination technology of BDS-3 with multiple antennas is developed. To realize multi-dimensional and high-precision real-time dynamic measurement of uneven deformation of highway earthwork.

In order to overcome the defects of the traditional BDS RTK technology, in the current BDS RTK technology, the linear attenuation error model is replaced by a regional network error model, which uses a network composed of multiple BDS-3 reference stations to estimate the error model of a region, which can provide more effective correction data for users in the network coverage area. It provides the technical basis for the practical application of RTK module.

The reliability prediction process of observations is introduced in the pre-processing of observations, and the observations with high reliability are prioritised from the star selection, and the DOP distribution of the current constellation is taken into account. Some satellites only use the dual-bandwidth lane combination mode, which can effectively reduce the matrix dimension and minimize the influence of the correctness of the ambiguity search. In the process of matrix calculation, the process of memory acquisition, calculation and release in matrix operation is planned in advance by decompressing the process of correlation matrix operation, and the fuzzy variance covariance matrix is reused to the maximum extent. The pre-processing of data in advance reduces the need for calculation accuracy, simplifies the 8-byte floating point calculation to 4-byte floating point calculation, and thus improves the memory resource and MIPS computing resource consumption.

The multi-source deep coupling structure of scalar deep combination and vector deep combination is adopted, that is, the channel closed loop is maintained when the channel signal is good, and the channel is opened when the signal is seriously weakened or interrupted, so as to realize the adaptive opening and closing of the channel. This structure is easy to implement in embedded system, and also concentrates the advantages of traditional scalar and vector deep combination in performance. It can realize the continuous and stable tracking of pseudo-range Doppler in complex signal environment, and also can ensure the carrier phase tracking under better signal conditions. SNR-FFT estimation of signal energy can quickly and accurately judge signal weakening and recovery, and adaptively control channel tracking loop disconnection and recovery, so as to realize continuous Doppler and pseudo-code tracking when some signals are interrupted, and rapid recovery when all signals are interrupted, so as to improve the continuity and accuracy of pseudo-range and Doppler observation. The multi-antenna BDS-3 attitude measurement technology adopts three BDS-3 satellite signal receiving antennas fixed on the surface of the carrier according to certain rules. On the premise that the 3 antenna can receive the signal of the BDS 3 satellite, the satellite signal received by each BDS 3 antenna is processed to obtain the observed carrier phase and the measured code phase. The coordinates of the base vector between antennas in the local horizontal coordinate system are calculated using the measured values combined with the least square attitude solution algorithm, and the attitude of the carrier is solved by coordinate transformation. Through reasonable arrangement of BDS-3 antenna, accurate calculation of the whole circle ambiguity and attitude parameter estimation, to improve the accuracy of UAV navigation and positioning and motion attitude. Mountainous terrain UAV high-precision data acquisition

FMCW millimeter wave radar altimetry

Aiming at the low accuracy of terrain acquisition by UAV in mountainous terrain, the FMCW millimeter wave radar is equipped with photogrammetry and ground imitation flight technology. The principle of UAV radar imitation flight is that after setting the imitation altitude, the flight controller adjusts the flight attitude in real time according to the actual altitude detected by the radar, and makes the flight track fit smoothly with the ground through the filtering algorithm. Unlike traditional pulse radar systems that periodically emit short pulses, MMwave radar based on frequency-modulated continuous wave (FMCW) technology can continuously transmit FM signals to measure distance as well as Angle and speed. By applying FMCW millimeter wave radar technology to remote sensing UAV, UAV has high precision and all-weather radar imitation flight ability. Compared with traditional ground imitation flight, MMW radar has higher ground imitation accuracy and can be operated all day and all weather, which is suitable for different scenarios and distances, and meets the demand for high-precision ground imitation flight under complex terrain conditions of highway engineering projects. This study adopts the architecture of mimicry radar with antenna + millimeter wave MMIC + MCU. Thanks to the radar’s antenna design, RF chip power, sensitivity, ADC support and algorithm detection, it can support up to 300 m height measurement.Terrain-following flight of a drone in mountainous areas is shown in Fig. 5.

Mountain imitation flight.

Simulated flight control over complex terrain

After obtaining the altitude measurement data of the UAV and the ground, the flight state of the UAV should be adjusted in time, and the UAV should be controlled to make corresponding flight actions according to the fluctuation of the ground. The ground imitation flight method based on a single vertical down sensor, such as the simple ground imitation flight realized by laser sensor and sonar sensor, can only adapt to the relatively flat terrain. When encountering the complex environment composed of mountains, hills, vegetation and other topographs, the adaptability is poor due to the inability to predict the changes of the ground terrain. To solve the above problems, by obtaining the vertical distance and oblique distance between the UAV and the ground, based on the vertical distance, oblique distance and the Angle between the vertical distance and oblique distance, the UAV imitation flight state is adjusted. The vertical distance is used to control the UAV to maintain the preset height with the ground, and the oblique distance is used as the basis for the terrain prediction and judgment of the UAV, which realizes the imitation flight of the UAV in various different environments such as mountains, hills, flat land and vegetation. This not only improves the operational efficiency of the UAV and the ability of the UAV to adapt to the environment, but also improves the reliability and safety of the UAV.

Forward obstacle or ground identification flight control

In the course of UAV flight, when there is a certain slope on the ground, the UAV cannot accurately identify the obstacle or ground in front of it, which will lead to conflicts between obstacle avoidance operation and imitation flight. For example, when the slope in front of the drone is within a certain range, the drone could have flown in imitation of the ground, but it mistakenly identifies the ground in front of it as an obstacle and conducts obstacle avoidance operations, thus affecting the operation process and efficiency. In order to solve this problem, this paper studies the identification and flight control technology of obstacles or ground in front of UAV during flight. The point cloud data of the area in front of UAV is obtained by sensors, and several sub-regions are divided. The obstacles or ground in the area in front of UAV are identified based on the results of altitude and elevation fitting, so as to avoid the conflict between imitation flight and obstacle avoidance operation, and improve the operational efficiency and stability of UAV.

Laser radar ground retest

Aiming at the problem that it is difficult to obtain real original ground data due to dense vegetation in the early stage of study construction, the research and development results of this research and development results use UAV as the carrier, use 3D laser scanning technology to scan the cloud data of the study site, and study the extraction of ground and non-ground point data based on the filter algorithm of “cloth simulation”, and finally obtain a high-precision DTM model. The measured design square quantity is calculated after matching with the design section data.

Laser point cloud data processing

In the actual study environment, in the face of the complex construction terrain environment, the slope based dynamic grid segmentation method is used to establish the grid index of the point cloud after eliminating the gross error. Secondly, the lowest point in the neighborhood grid of each grid is used to establish the surface equation to fit the elevation value, and the elevation is normalized by calculating the difference between the real elevation and the fitting elevation. Then cloth simulation algorithm is used to simulate the falling process of cloth to get the final shape of terrain cloth, and then the ground point is extracted by threshold limit. On the basis of ensuring the accuracy of filtering in a large range of complex scenes, it has strong self-adaptability to different terrain and improves the efficiency of filtering calculation.

Combining progressive morphological filtering and color information, the ground points are obtained by point cloud coordinates and color information. The obtained ground points are used to calculate the earthwork. The method first divides the point cloud into blocks with the minimum size larger than the maximum building size in the area, then selects the lowest point and ground points within a certain height difference from the point cloud as ground seed points, establishes the initial sparse triangulation network, calculates the Angle and distance between the remaining points in the point cloud and the triangulation network, and compares them with the set iteration Angle and iteration distance. If it is less than the set iteration value, it is accepted as the ground point, otherwise it is rejected as the ground point, and the above iteration is repeated until no new ground points are added to the triangulation network to realize the process of triangulation network encryption, and the ground point is finally obtained. For LiDAR point cloud, the ground laser points are obtained by cloth filtering algorithm, and the ground points obtained are used to make cross sections. The point cloud is flipped, and if a piece of fabric falls from above under gravity, the resulting fabric represents the current terrain.

Retest calculation

This study adopts the section method to carry out the retest calculation. Section method is to measure a section in its vertical direction according to the pile number distance along the longitudinal section line, and the square quantity between the two sections is equal to the product of the average cross-sectional area of the two sections and the section spacing.

Figure 6 shows by extracting the cross sectional lines of each pile number in the specified interval, points are extracted from the model and the broken line section line is generated. The profile of the corresponding pile number is found in the design drawing, and the elevation at the center line of the roadbed design (that is, the middle pile point) is inserted into the design profile as the comparison point. The section difference is calculated by comparing with the cross-sectional design line and the remeasured ground line.

Mountain terrain modeling data processing

Image space integrated solution

Using the method of air-ground integrated multi-source data combined with air-three-directional data, the difference in resolution, photography Angle and image radiation characteristics of air-ground images is overcome according to the combination of oblique photography and moving measurement data sources. The problem of matching the turn point between multi-source images is solved through the comprehensive application of POS assistance, DSM guidance and anti-deformation moment invariant feature matching. This study adopted a distributed optimization scheme to modify the global beam method adjustment into an iterative solution method based on conjugate gradient, which realized distributed and high-precision parallel parallel space three, effectively allocated the calculation peak value evenly to multiple links of the algorithm pipeline, and greatly improved the efficiency and quality of post-processing of oblique photography data. The specific research technical route is shown in Fig. 7.

Technical route of image air-space integrated solution.

Multi-source images combined with space tridirectional

Automatic regional network space-three encryption, loading tilt photography and ground close-up region images into the automatic modeling system, applying the robust visual feature extraction method of multi-source images, and aiming at the characteristics of large tilt Angle and large geometric deformation, using the multi-view image cross-scale similarity measurement matching operator construction method. A deep learning network framework and training model integrating feature detection, direction estimation and feature description are designed. Combining the visual and geometric context information to extend the local feature descriptors, the beam method adjustment model based on the preconditioned conjugate gradient method can reduce the calculation cost of factorization of large-scale normal equations, improve the convergence speed by optimizing the distribution of eigenvalues, and obtain the accuracy results consistent with the direct linear solution.

Semi-global intensive matching of a large range of complex scenes combined with open space

Aiming at the need of generating high-precision and high-density image point clouds in a large range of complex scenes, the objective function of selecting stereo image pairs was constructed by using the optimal matching model selection method based on multi-knowledge constraints, and the linear optimal adaptive matching model was adopted. Based on the calculation method of matching cost based on deep learning, a neural network structure suitable for large scene images is designed. Image blocks are taken as network input, and after neural network training and learning, accurate matching cost is obtained and mismatching of weak texture areas is reduced. Parallel operation based feature dimension reduction parallax map generation technology, multi-angle parallax map fusion point cloud generation and point cloud refinement method, introduce object semantic knowledge as prior information, combined with explicit mathematical model, improve Markov random field energy model, the feature dimension reduction expression, combined with smoothing constraints, based on parallel operation to generate accurate parallax map. The 3D point cloud of the scene can be obtained from the parallax information and the parameter information of the camera. Combine surface features and line features to optimize point clouds, and generate high-precision dense point clouds that meet the requirements of large scenes.

Feature-driven 3D reconstruction and texture mapping

Aiming at the intelligent and efficient modeling and texture mapping of multi-source heterogeneous data in complex mountain scenes on a large scale, the 3D surface reconstruction technology of multi-source data of different scales is adopted, which integrates the uniform distribution of global mesh. The 3D surface model and the structured fine model of multi-source data can be constructed by local feature preserving technology and scene semantic information assisted data filling technology. The neural network point cloud smoothing algorithm with geometric feature constraints takes into account the geometric feature information of the point cloud while training the neural network, and improves the surface reconstruction of Poisson equation by taking into account the multi-condition constraints of uniform grid distribution. At the same time, the position of vertices and the shape of mesh are evenly distributed. Through the feature extraction method of buildings and structures with local feature preservation, principal component analysis is carried out on the extracted feature lines and surfaces to determine the contour feature lines of the model, and the structured fine geometry model is constructed according to the topological relationship between feature lines and surfaces. Finally, texture mapping technology based on view correlation is used to generate 3D reality model.

Conclusion

In this study, BDS assisted terrain acquisition and processing technology of complex mountain roads is proposed, and tested on 100 km mountain roads. For the field mountain road, this technology can effectively improve the accuracy of the original ground resurvey, compared with the traditional manual RTK operation mode, the terrain continuity, uniformity and integrity are better, and the engineering time is about 20% of the traditional time, which has significant quality and cost advantages.

Although the technology has been successfully applied and validated in specific projects. But there are still some limitations that need to be addressed. First of all, the remeasurement accuracy of the in-situ surface largely depends on the penetration of the lidar. In this study, the long-range lidar was used for testing. For areas with dense vegetation that liDAR could not penetrate, RTK was required to assist the measurement to ensure adequate measurement accuracy. Secondly, there is a lack of specific technical standards for both the subgrade earthwork receiving based on laser point cloud and the 3D plotting receiving based on real scene. The formulation of relevant standards is conducive to the promotion of this technology in highway construction projects.
