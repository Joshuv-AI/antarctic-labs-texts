40 C.F.R. § 799.12 — Test results.
Citation: us:cfr:40:799.12
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-799.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 799 (Identification Of Specific Chemical Substance And Mixture Testing Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as set forth in specific chemical test rules in subpart B of this part, a positive or negative test result in any of the tests required under subpart B is defined in the TSCA test guidelines published by NTIS.
