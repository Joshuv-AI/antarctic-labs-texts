24 C.F.R. § 40.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:24:40.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-40.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 40 (Accessibility Standards For Design, Construction, And Alteration Of Publicly Owned Residential Structures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes standards for the design, construction, and alteration of publicly owned residential structures to insure that physically handicapped persons will have ready access to, and use of, such structures.
