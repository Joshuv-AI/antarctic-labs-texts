42 C.F.R. § 84.51 — Entry and escape, or escape only; classification.
Citation: us:cfr:42:84.51
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-84.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 84 (Approval Of Respiratory Protective Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Respirators described in subparts H through L of this part shall be classified for use as follows:
(a) Entry and escape. Respirators designed and approved for use during entry into a hazardous atmosphere, and for escape from a hazardous atmosphere; or
(b) Escape only. Respirators designed and approved for use only during escape from a hazardous atmosphere.
