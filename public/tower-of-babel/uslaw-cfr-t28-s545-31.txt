28 C.F.R. § 545.31 — Training.
Citation: us:cfr:28:545.31
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-545.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 545 (Work And Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Warden shall ensure that staff receive training on their roles in, and on the operation of, the work and performance pay program. The Warden shall also ensure that the inmate population is informed of the work and performance pay program, and of the hourly rates paid to inmate workers.
