7 C.F.R. § 1250.315 — Part and subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1250.315
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1250.315
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1250 (Egg Research And Promotion). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part means the Egg Research and Promotion Order and all rules, regulations, and supplemental order issued pursuant to the act and the order. “Subpart” refers to the aforesaid order or any other portion or segment of this part.
