26 U.S.C. § 4103 — Certain additional persons liable for tax where willful failure to pay
Citation: us:usc:26:4103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section4103
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 32 (Manufacturers Excise Taxes); Subchapter A (Automotive And Related Items); Part III (Petroleum Products); Subpart B (Special Provisions Applicable To Fuels Tax). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In any case in which there is a willful failure to pay the tax imposed by section 4041(a)(1) or 4081, each person—
(1)
who is an officer, employee, or agent of the taxpayer who is under a duty to assure the payment of such tax and who willfully fails to perform such duty, or
(2)
who willfully causes the taxpayer to fail to pay such tax,
shall be jointly and severally liable with the taxpayer for the tax to which such failure relates.
