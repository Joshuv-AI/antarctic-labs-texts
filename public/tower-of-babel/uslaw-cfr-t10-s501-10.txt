10 C.F.R. § 501.10 — Order of precedence.
Citation: us:cfr:10:501.10
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-501.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 501 (Administrative Procedures And Sanctions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If there is any conflict or inconsistency between the provisions of this part and any other provisions or parts of this chapter, except for general procedures which are unique to part 515 of this chapter, the provisions of this part will control respect to procedure.
