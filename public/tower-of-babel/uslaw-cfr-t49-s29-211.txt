49 C.F.R. § 29.211 — Is technical assistance available to a Tribe to overcome rejection of a final offer?
Citation: us:cfr:49:29.211
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-29.211
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 29 (Tribal Transportation Self-Governance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department must provide technical assistance to overcome the objections stated in the Department's rejection of a final offer.
