29 C.F.R. § 515.7 — Transmission of official mail.
Citation: us:cfr:29:515.7
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-515.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 515 (Utilization Of State Agencies For Investigations And Inspections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subject to the requirements of law and of the regulations of the Post Office Department, franked self-addressed envelopes may be used for communications from the field staff to a State official designated by the Division and the Secretary of Labor, and for communication from the State agency to the Division or the Secretary of Labor.
