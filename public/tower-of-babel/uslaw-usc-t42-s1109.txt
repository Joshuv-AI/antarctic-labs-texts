42 U.S.C. § 1109 — Federal Employees Compensation Account
Citation: us:usc:42:1109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1109
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter IX (Employment Security Administrative Financing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is hereby established in the Unemployment Trust Fund a Federal Employees Compensation Account which shall be used for the purposes specified in section 8509 of title 5. For the purposes provided for in section 1104(e) of this title, such account shall be maintained as a separate book account.
