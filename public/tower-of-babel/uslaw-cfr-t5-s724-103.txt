5 C.F.R. § 724.103 — Agency obligations.
Citation: us:cfr:5:724.103
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-724.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 724 (Implementation Of Title Ii Of The Notification And Federal Employee Antidiscrimination And Retaliation Act Of 2002). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Federal agency (or its successor agency) must reimburse the Judgment Fund for payments covered by the No FEAR Act. Such reimbursement must be made within a reasonable time as described in § 724.104.
