41 C.F.R. § 109-43.307-2.51 — -43.307-2.51 Holding hazardous personal property.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-43.307-2.51
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-43.307-2.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-43 (43—Utilization Of Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Excess or surplus hazardous personal property shall not be commingled with non-hazardous personal property while waiting disposition action.
