48 C.F.R. § 1523.303-71 — 1523.303-71 Decontamination of Government-furnished property.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1523.303-71
Creator: Chapter 15—Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1523.303-71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1523 (Environmental, Conservation, Occupational Safety, And Drug-Free Workplace). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracting Officers shall insert the contract clause at 1552.245-70, Decontamination of Government-Furnished Property, when it is anticipated that a Contractor will use Government-furnished or Contractor-acquired property in the clean-up of hazardous or toxic substances in the environment.
