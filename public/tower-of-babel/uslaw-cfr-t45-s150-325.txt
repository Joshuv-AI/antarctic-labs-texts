45 C.F.R. § 150.325 — Settlement authority.
Citation: us:cfr:45:150.325
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-150.325
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 150 (Cms Enforcement In Group And Individual Insurance Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in §§ 150.315 through 150.323 limits the authority of CMS to settle any issue or case described in the notice furnished in accordance with § 150.307 or to compromise on any penalty provided for in §§ 150.315 through 150.323.
