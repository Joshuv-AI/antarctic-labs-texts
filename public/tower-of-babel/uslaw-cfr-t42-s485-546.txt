42 C.F.R. § 485.546 — Condition of participation: Skilled nursing facility distinct part unit.
Citation: us:cfr:42:485.546
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-485.546
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 485 (Conditions Of Participation: Specialized Providers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the REH provides skilled nursing facility services in a distinct part unit, the services furnished by the distinct part unit must be separately licensed and certified and comply with the requirements of participation for long-term care facilities specified in part 483, subpart B, of this chapter.
