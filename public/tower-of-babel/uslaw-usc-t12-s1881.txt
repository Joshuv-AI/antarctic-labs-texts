12 U.S.C. § 1881 — “Federal supervisory agency” defined
Citation: us:usc:12:1881
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1881
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 19 (Security Measures For Banks And Savings Associations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter the term “Federal supervisory agency” means the appropriate Federal banking agency, as defined in section 1813(q) of this title.
