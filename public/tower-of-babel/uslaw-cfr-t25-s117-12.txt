25 C.F.R. § 117.12 — Costs of recording and conveyancing.
Citation: us:cfr:25:117.12
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-117.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 117 (Deposit And Expenditure Of Individual Funds Of Members Of The Osage Tribe Of Indians Who Do Not Have Certificates Of Competency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The superintendent may expend the surplus funds of an Indian to make direct payment of recording fees and costs, of conveyancing, including abstracting costs, which are properly payable by the Indian.
