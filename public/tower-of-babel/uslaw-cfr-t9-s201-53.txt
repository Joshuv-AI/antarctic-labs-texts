9 C.F.R. § 201.53 — Persons subject to the Act not to circulate misleading reports about market conditions or prices.
Citation: us:cfr:9:201.53
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-201.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 201 (Administering The Packers And Stockyards Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No packer, swine contractor, live poultry dealer, stockyard owner, market agency, or dealer shall knowingly make, issue, or circulate any false or misleading reports, records, or representation concerning the market conditions or the prices or sale of any livestock, meat, or live poultry.
