25 C.F.R. § 304.5 — Dies to identify tribe.
Citation: us:cfr:25:304.5
Creator: Indian Arts And Crafts Board, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-304.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 304 (Navajo, Pueblo, And Hopi Silver, Use Of Government Mark). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Dies are marked with name of tribe. A Navajo stamp will be used where the marker is a Navajo Indian; similarly, for Zuni, Hopi, and Rio Grande Pueblo.
