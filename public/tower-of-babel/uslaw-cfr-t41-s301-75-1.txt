41 C.F.R. § 301-75.1 — -75.1 Authorization of pre-employment interview travel expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-75.1
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-75.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-75 (75—Pre-Employment Interview Travel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies may pay pre-employment interview travel expenses if determined to be in the Government's best interest. However, pre-employment travel expenses may not be authorized to offset or defray other expenses not allowable under this part.
