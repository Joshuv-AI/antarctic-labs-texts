46 C.F.R. § 58.01-45 — -45 Machinery space, ventilation.
Citation: us:cfr:46:58.01-45
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-58.01-45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 58 (Main And Auxiliary Machinery And Related Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each machinery space must be ventilated to ensure that, when machinery or boilers are operating at full power in all weather including heavy weather, an adequate supply of air is maintained for the operation of the machinery and for the safety, efficiency, and comfort of the crew.
