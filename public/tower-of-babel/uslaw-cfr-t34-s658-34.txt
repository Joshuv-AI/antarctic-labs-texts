34 C.F.R. § 658.34 — What additional factors does the Secretary consider in selecting grant recipients?
Citation: us:cfr:34:658.34
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-658.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 658 (Undergraduate International Studies And Foreign Language Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to applying the selection criteria in, as appropriate §§ 658.31, 658.32, and 658.33, the Secretary, to the extent practicable and consistent with the criterion of excellence, seeks to encourage diversity by ensuring that a variety of types of projects and institutions receive funding.
