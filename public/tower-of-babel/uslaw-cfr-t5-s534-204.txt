5 C.F.R. § 534.204 — Previous authorizations.
Citation: us:cfr:5:534.204
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-534.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 534 (Pay Under Other Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart do not terminate any authorization approved by the Civil Service Commission or the Office of Personnel Management before February 15, 1979, and such authorizations remain in effect until modified or terminated by an agency or the Office of Personnel Management in accordance with the provisions of this subpart.
