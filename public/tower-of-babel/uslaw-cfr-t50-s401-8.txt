50 C.F.R. § 401.8 — Availability of funds.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 401 (Anadromous Fisheries Conservation, Development And Enhancement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The period of availability of funds to the States or other non-Federal interests for obligation shall be established by the administering Federal agency.
