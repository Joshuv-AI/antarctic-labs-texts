46 U.S.C. § 53305 — Eligible fund deposits
Citation: us:usc:46:53305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section53305
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part C (Financial Assistance Programs); Chapter 533 (Construction Reserve Funds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A construction reserve fund may include deposits of—
(1)
the proceeds from the sale of a vessel;
(2)
indemnities for the loss of a vessel;
(3)
earnings from the operation of a documented vessel and from services incident to the operation; and
(4)
interest or other amounts accrued on deposits in the fund.
