37 C.F.R. § 204.9 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:37:204.9
Creator: U.S. Copyright Office, Library Of Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-204.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 204 (Privacy Act: Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within two years of the receipt of a final adverse administrative determination, an individual may seek judicial review of that determination as provided in 5 U.S.C. 552a(g)(1).
