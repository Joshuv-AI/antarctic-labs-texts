16 U.S.C. § 3231 — Areas subject to national need recommendation process
Citation: us:usc:16:3231
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section3231
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 51 (Alaska National Interest Lands Conservation); Subchapter VII (National Need Mineral Activity Recommendation Process). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The process contained in this subchapter shall apply to all public lands within Alaska except for lands within units of the National Park System and the Arctic National Wildlife Refuge.
