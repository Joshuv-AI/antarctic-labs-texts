38 C.F.R. § 21.5900 — Administration of benefits program—chapter 107, title 10 U.S.C.
Citation: us:cfr:38:21.5900
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-21.5900
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 21 (Veteran Readiness And Employment And Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In administering benefits payable under Chapter 107, Title 10 U.S.C, VA will be bound by the provisions of the §§ 21.5700, 21.5800 and 21.5900 series of regulations.
