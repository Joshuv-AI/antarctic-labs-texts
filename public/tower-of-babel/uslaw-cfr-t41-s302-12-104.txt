41 C.F.R. § 302-12.104 — -12.104 Paying an employee for losses incurred on the sale of a residence.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-12.104
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-12.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-12 (12—Use Of A Relocation Services Company). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under a home sale program, agencies may not pay an employee for losses incurred on the sale of a residence, but this does not preclude an agency reimbursing a relocation services company for losses incurred while the contractor holds the property.
