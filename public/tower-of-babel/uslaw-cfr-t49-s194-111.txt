49 C.F.R. § 194.111 — Response plan retention.
Citation: us:cfr:49:194.111
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-194.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 194 (Response Plans For Onshore Oil Pipelines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each operator shall maintain relevant portions of its response plan at:
(1) The operator's headquarters and other locations from which response activities may be conducted, for example in field offices, supervisors' vehicles, or spill response trailers; or
(2) Electronic storage on a readily accessible secured portable device.
(b) Each operator shall provide a copy of its response plan to each qualified individual.
