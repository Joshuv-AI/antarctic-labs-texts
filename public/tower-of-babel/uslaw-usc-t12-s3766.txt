12 U.S.C. § 3766 — Computation of time
Citation: us:usc:12:3766
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section3766
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 38A (Single Family Mortgage Foreclosure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Periods of time provided for in this chapter shall be calculated in consecutive calendar days, including the day or days on which the actions or events occur or are to occur for which the period of time is provided and including the day on which an event occurs or is to occur from which the period is to be calculated.
