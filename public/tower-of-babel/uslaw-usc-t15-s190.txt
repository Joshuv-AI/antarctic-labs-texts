15 U.S.C. § 190 — Discussions in commercial reports of partisan questions
Citation: us:usc:15:190
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section190
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 5 (Statistical And Commercial Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No part of the consular and other commercial reports of the Department of Commerce, including circular letters to chambers of commerce, discussing partisan political, religious, or moral questions shall be published.
