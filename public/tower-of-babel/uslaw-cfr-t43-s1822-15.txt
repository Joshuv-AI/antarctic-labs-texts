43 C.F.R. § 1822.15 — If I miss filing a required document or payment within the specified period, can BLM consider it timely filed anyway?
Citation: us:cfr:43:1822.15
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1822.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1820 (Application Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM may consider it timely filed if:
(a) The law does not prohibit BLM from doing so;
(b) No other BLM regulation prohibits doing so; and
(c) No intervening third party interests or rights have been created or established during the intervening period.
