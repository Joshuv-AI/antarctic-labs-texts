( t , q ) (t,q) -Series Invariants of Seifert Manifolds
Louisa Liles

Abstract
. Gukov, Pei, Putrov, and Vafa developed a q q -series invariant of negative definite plumbed 3 3 -manifolds with spin c structures, building on earlier work of Lawrence and Zagier [ 7 ] . This was recently generalized to an an infinite family of two-variable ( t , q ) (t,q) -series invariants by Akhmechet, Johnson, and Krushkal (AJK) [ 1 ] . We calculate one such series for all Seifert manifolds with b 1 = 0 . b_{1}=0. These results extend a previous theorem of [ 11 ] to any number of exceptional fibers and the Reduction Theorem of [ 6 ] to the two-variable case. As a consequence, a previous result of [ 11 ] on modularity properties and radial limits is enhanced to a larger family of manifolds. We also calculate the infinite collection of ( t , q ) (t,q) -series invariants for three infinite families of manifolds, finding mixed modularity properties for one such family.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let

be a Seifert manifold, and let

. Then:

\widehat{\vphantom{\rule{5.0pt}{10.0pt}}\smash{\widehat{Z}}\,}\!_{0}(M)(t^{2},q^{|H|})=q^{\Delta}\mathcal{L}_{A}(\text{s.e.}(f_{0}(t,z))).

The full statement of the above appears as Theorem
3.4
, and some definitions are postponed until then. The key takeaway is that

Let

\delta_{i}

Definitions 2.1

Definition 2.3
.

Let

\sum_{k}a_{k}z^{k}\text{ and }\sum_{k}b_{k}z^{-k}.

\text{s.e.}(f):=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{k}a_{k}z^{k}+\frac{1}{2}\sum_{k}b_{k}z^{-k}.

Lemma 2.4

The coefficient on

\text{s.e.}((z-z^{-1})^{2-d})\implies n\equiv d\pmod{2}.

The conclusion follows from the fact that the Laurent expansion of

(-\sum_{i\geq 0}z^{2i+1})^{d-2},

and the expansion on

(\sum_{i\geq 0}z^{-(2i+1)})^{d-2}.

2.4.
Modular and quantum modular forms

This section provides a brief overview of modular and quantum modular forms of half-integral weight. For more details, we direct the reader to
[
15
,
16
]
.

Let
\mathbb{H}
denote the upper half complex plane.
Modular forms can be thought of as holomorphic functions
f:\mathbb{H}\to\mathbb{C}
that behave “nicely” with respect to the action of
\text{SL}(2,\mathbb{Z})
on
\mathbb{H}
by linear fractional transformations. The meaning of “nicely” is specified in Definition
2.9
below. For
\gamma=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\
c&d\end{pmatrix}\in\text{SL}(2,\mathbb{Z})
, its action on
\tau\in\mathbb{H}
is given by

\gamma\tau:=\frac{a\tau+b}{c\tau+d}.

Often functions are not modular with respect to the action of
\text{SL}(2,\mathbb{Z}),
but rather with respect to some subgroup. Two subgroups that appear in the theory of half-integral weight modular forms are

\displaystyle\Gamma_{1}(N)

\displaystyle:=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\
c&d\end{pmatrix}\in\text{SL}(2,\mathbb{Z}):\ a\equiv d\equiv 1\bmod{N},\ c\equiv 0\bmod{N}\right\};

\displaystyle\Gamma(N)

\displaystyle:=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\
c&d\end{pmatrix}\in\text{SL}(2,\mathbb{Z}):\ a\equiv d\equiv 1\bmod{N},\ b\equiv c\equiv 0\bmod{N}\right\},

where
N
is called the
level
of the subgroup. We also define, for
d
odd,

\varepsilon_{d}:=\begin{cases}1&\text{if }d\equiv 1\bmod{4};\\
i&\text{if }d\equiv 3\bmod{4},\end{cases}

and the Petersson slash operator of weight
k\in\frac{\mathbb{Z}}{2}
is given by

f|_{k}\gamma(\tau):=\begin{cases}(c\tau+d)^{-k}f(\gamma\tau)&\text{ if }k\in\mathbb{Z};\\
\varepsilon_{d}^{2k}\left(\frac{c}{d}\right)(c\tau+d)^{-k}f(\gamma\tau)&\text{ if }k\in\frac{1}{2}+\mathbb{Z},\end{cases}

where
(\frac{\cdot}{\cdot})
denotes the Jacobi symbol.

Definition 2.9
.

Let
\Gamma\leq\text{SL}(2,\mathbb{Z})
be of level
N
with
4|N.
A holomorphic function
f:\mathbb{H}\to\mathbb{C}
is a
modular (resp. cusp) form

Proposition 4.1

Let

We now prove Proposition

Proof.

Let
\varepsilon_{i}=\pm 1

C(\varepsilon_{1},\varepsilon_{2},\varepsilon_{3},m)=\begin{cases}-\frac{1}{2}\varepsilon_{1}\varepsilon_{2}\varepsilon_{3}[t^{\sum_{i}\varepsilon_{i}+m}+t^{-(\sum_{i}\varepsilon_{i}+m)}]&m\text{ odd}\\

C(\varepsilon_{1},\varepsilon_{2},\varepsilon_{3},m)=-C(-\varepsilon_{1},-\varepsilon_{2},-\varepsilon_{3},-m).

If

2j
th root of unity

∎
