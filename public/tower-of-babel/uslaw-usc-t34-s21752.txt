34 U.S.C. § 21752 — Best practices and model legislation for guardianship proceedings
Citation: us:usc:34:21752
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section21752
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 217 (Elder Abuse Prevention And Prosecution); Subchapter V (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General shall publish best practices for improving guardianship proceedings and model legislation relating to guardianship proceedings for the purpose of preventing elder abuse.
