Elliptic flow of D s D_{s} as evidence of the sequential hadronization mechanism in the hot QCD medium
Zi-Xuan Xu, Jiaxing Zhao, Wei Dai, Ben-Wei Zhang, Pengfei Zhuang

Abstract
Heavy flavor production serves as an ideal probe of the hadronization mechanism in the hot and dense QCD medium created in relativistic heavy-ion collisions. Heavy quarks acquire elliptic flow through strong coupling with the medium, making v 2 v_{2} a sensitive observable for probing the hadronization time. In this Letter, we study the production and elliptic flow of D s D_{s} and D 0 D^{0} mesons in P ​ b + P ​ b Pb+Pb collisions at s NN = 5.02 \sqrt{s_{\rm NN}}=5.02 TeV within a Langevin dynamics framework for heavy quarks in the QCD medium, combined with the sequential or simultaneous coalescence plus fragmentation at the hadronization hypersurface. We find that, within the sequential hadronization framework, the earlier-produced D s D_{s} exhibits a smaller v 2 v_{2} than the D 0 D^{0} in the intermediate p T p_{\mathrm{T}} region. This reversed ordering behavior aligns with preliminary ALICE measurements and provides strong evidence for the heavy flavor sequential hadronization mechanism.



Introduction.–

It is widely accepted that relativistic heavy-ion collisions create a deconfined state of Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD) matter, the quark–gluon plasma (QGP). The QGP has a short lifetime, and as it expands and cools below the critical temperature
T_{c}
, it hadronizes. This process, dominated by non-perturbative QCD dynamics, differs markedly from hadronization in the vacuum, which happens in
e^{+}+e^{-}
collisions. Heavy quarks are produced in the early stage, and their number remains approximately conserved, making them ideal probes of the non-perturbative hadronization mechanism, which can be further constrained using heavy-quark effective theory. A precise characterization of heavy-flavor hadronization in the hot medium can further constrain the evolution of heavy quarks and the dynamical properties of the QGP. Various transport-plus-hadronization models have achieved reasonably successful descriptions of heavy-flavor production in heavy-ion collisions, see for instance
Minissale et al. 2021
;
Plumari et al. 2018
;
Cao et al. 2015
;
Cao et al. 2020
;
Gossiaux et al. 2009
;
Nahrgang et al. 2014
;
Zhao et al. 2024a
;
Zhao et al. 2025
;
Song et al. 2015
;
Song et al. 2016
;
Ravagli and Rapp 2007
;
He and Rapp 2019
;
Andronic et al. 2021
;
Beraudo et al. 2015
;
Beraudo et al. 2018a
and the review papers
Beraudo et al. 2018b
;
Zhao et al. 2024b
;
Fries et al. 2025
. Most of these hadronization models assume that heavy quarks hadronize instantaneously on the phase transition hypersurface with a critical temperature
T_{c}
.

Inspired by the behavior of quarkonia, which can be formed and dissociated in the QGP due to their large binding energies—a phenomenon confirmed experimentally
STA 2025
;
Chatrchyan et al. 2012
—a sequential hadronization mechanism for open heavy flavors should naturally exist, arising from their different dissociation temperatures
Shi et al. 2020
. When incorporating charm-quark number conservation, this mechanism enhances the yields of early-formed charm hadrons while suppressing those produced later
Zhao et al. 2018
;
Zhao et al. 2021
.
Nevertheless, isolating this effect unambiguously via the yield of heavy flavor hadrons from other coalescence dynamics remains challenging.

The purpose of this Letter is to propose a sensitive probe of the heavy-flavor hadronization mechanism, focusing on the hadronization sequence. Early-formed charm hadrons are expected to experience weaker medium interactions and thus exhibit smaller elliptic flow coefficients,
v_{2}
, than those produced later.
Although the hadronization temperatures of different charmed hadrons differ modestly, the strong coupling of charm quarks to the medium and their substantial energy loss during the late stage of QGP evolution
Altenkort et al. 2023
are expected to induce a visible difference in their elliptic flow. Consequently, the emergence of a
v_{2}
hierarchy among charm hadrons—such as
v_{2}(D_{s}^{+})<v_{2}(D^{0})
—naturally points to a sequential hadronization mechanism.
Recent high-precision ALICE measurements in
Pb+Pb
collisions at
\sqrt{s_{\rm NN}}=5.36
TeV
Torres 2025
;
Wu 2025
show that
v_{2}(D_{s}^{+})
is smaller than
v_{2}(D^{0})
in the intermediate-
p_{\rm T}
region—contradicting all transport model predictions based on simultaneous coalescence
Zhao et al. 2024b
.
This observation provides strong evidence for a sequential hadronization mechanism. To explore this systematically while accounting for other effects—such as fragmentation contributions and hadronic evolution—we investigate charmed-hadron production and collective flow in
Pb+Pb
collisions at
\sqrt{s_{\rm NN}}=5.02
TeV within a Langevin+hydrodynamics transport framework, implementing both sequential and simultaneous hadronization schemes.

Sequential Hadronization–

Summary–
In the simultaneous coalescence scenario,
v_{2}
exhibits a positive hierarchy with the mass of the constituent light quarks, which means the
v_{2}
of
D_{s}
is always higher than that of
D^{0}
. The most recent ALICE preliminary results show a reversal of the
v_{2}
hierarchy between
D^{0}
and
D_{s}
at the Intermediate
p_{\mathrm{T}}
region. In this Letter, we have developed an updated Langevin transport model with sequential coalescence plus fragmentation hadronization approach to predict the elliptic flow
v_{2}
of
D^{0}
- and
D_{s}
-mesons in
30-50\%
centrality Pb+Pb collisions at
\sqrt{s_{\rm NN}}=5.02
TeV as a funcitons of
p_{\mathrm{T}}
.
We find that hadronic re-scattering indeed contributes additional elliptic flow to
D^{0}
, but it can still not lead to the reversal of the
v_{2}
hierarchy between
D^{0}
and
D_{s}
. However, after incorporating the sequential coalescence mechanism for
D_{s}
with other processes retained, the
v_{2}
of
D_{s}
mesons shows significant suppression at
p_{T}\approx 2-5
GeV/c. The reverse of such a
v_{2}
hierarchy is observed. It successfully reproduces the ALICE preliminary results. It indicates that the elliptic flow of
D_{s}
provides compelling evidence of the sequential hadronization mechanism and supplies a unique opportunity to further constrain the hadronization processes of charm hadrons.
