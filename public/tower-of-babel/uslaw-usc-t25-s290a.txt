25 U.S.C. § 290a — Bureau appropriations as not limiting transportation of school children
Citation: us:usc:25:290a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section290a
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 7 (Education Of Indians). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after October 12, 1984, no part of any appropriation to the Bureau of Indian Affairs under this or any other act shall be used to subject the transportation of school children to any limitation on travel or transportation expenditures for Federal employees.
