20 C.F.R. § 640.9 — Information, reports and studies.
Citation: us:cfr:20:640.9
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-640.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 640 (Standard For Benefit Payment Promptness—Unemployment Compensation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State shall furnish to the Secretary of Labor such information and reports and make such studies as the Secretary decides are necessary or appropriate to carry out this part.
