7 U.S.C. § 2122 — Authorization of appropriations for cotton ginning studies
Citation: us:usc:7:2122
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2122
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 53 (Cotton Research And Promotion). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of section 2121 of this title there is authorized to be appropriated, after June 30, 1931, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated, such sums as may be necessary.
