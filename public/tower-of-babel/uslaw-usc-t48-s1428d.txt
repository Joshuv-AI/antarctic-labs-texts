48 U.S.C. § 1428d — Report for inclusion in annual report by Governor
Citation: us:usc:48:1428d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1428d
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 8A (Guam); Subchapter VI (Guam Development Fund). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Governor of Guam shall include in the annual report to Congress required pursuant to section 1422 of this title a report on the administration of this subchapter.
