46 U.S.C. § 7108 — Termination of licenses and certificates of registry
Citation: us:usc:46:7108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section7108
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Merchant Seamen Licenses, Certificates, And Documents); Chapter 71 (Licenses And Certificates Of Registry). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When the holder of a license or certificate of registry, the duration of which is conditioned under section 7106 or 7107 of this title, fails to hold the license required as a condition, the license or certificate of registry issued under this part is terminated.
