42 U.S.C. § 14408 — Relation to other laws
Citation: us:usc:42:14408
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section14408
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 138 (Assisted Suicide Funding Restriction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of this chapter supersede other Federal laws (including laws enacted after April 30, 1997) except to the extent such laws specifically supersede the provisions of this chapter.
