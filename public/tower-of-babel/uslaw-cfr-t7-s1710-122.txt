7 C.F.R. § 1710.122 — Equal opportunity and nondiscrimination.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1710.122
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1710.122
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1710 (General And Pre-Loan Policies And Procedures Common To Electric Loans And Guarantees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Borrowers are required to comply with certain regulations on nondiscrimination in program services and benefits and on equal employment opportunity as set forth in RUS Bulletins 20-15 and 20-19 or their successors; 7 CFR parts 15 and 15b; and 45 CFR part 90.
