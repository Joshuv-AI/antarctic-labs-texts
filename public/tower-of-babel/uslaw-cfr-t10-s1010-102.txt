10 C.F.R. § 1010.102 — Cross-references to employee ethical conduct standards, financial disclosure regulations, and other conduct rules.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1010.102
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1010.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1010 (Conduct Of Employees And Former Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees of DOE are subject to the Standards of Ethical Conduct for Employees of the Executive Branch at 5 CFR part 2635, the DOE regulation at 5 CFR part 3301 which supplements the executive branch-wide standards, the executive branch-wide financial disclosure regulations at 5 CFR part 2634, the executive branch-wide financial interests regulations at 5 CFR part 2640, and the executive branch-wide employee responsibilities and conduct regulation at 5 CFR part 735.
