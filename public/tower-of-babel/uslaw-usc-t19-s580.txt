19 U.S.C. § 580 — Interest in suits on bonds for recovery of duties
Citation: us:usc:19:580
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section580
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 3 (The Tariff And Related Provisions); Part 6 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon all bonds, on which suits are brought for the recovery of duties, interest shall be allowed, at the rate of 6 per centum a year, from the time when said bonds became due.
