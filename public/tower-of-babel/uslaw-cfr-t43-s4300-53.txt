43 C.F.R. § 4300.53 — Can BLM reduce the size of the land in my permit?
Citation: us:cfr:43:4300.53
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4300.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4300 (Grazing Administration; Alaska; Reindeer; General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. BLM may reduce it at any time but must notify you at least 30 days before taking this action. BLM can reduce the area when:
(a) BLM determines that the area is too large for the number of reindeer you are grazing; or
(b) When disposal, withdrawal, natural causes, such as drought or fire, or any other reason in § 4300.50 so requires.
