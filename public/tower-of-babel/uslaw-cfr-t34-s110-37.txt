34 C.F.R. § 110.37 — Procedure for disbursal of funds to an alternate recipient.
Citation: us:cfr:34:110.37
Creator: Office For Civil Rights, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-110.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 110 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If the Secretary withholds funds from a recipient under these regulations, the Secretary may disburse the funds withheld directly to an alternate recipient: any public or nonprofit private organization or agency, or State or political subdivision of the State.
(b) The Secretary requires any alternate recipient to demonstrate—
(1) The ability to comply with the Act and these regulations; and
(2) The ability to achieve the goals of the Federal statute authorizing the Federal financial assistance.
