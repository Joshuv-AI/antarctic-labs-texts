    On July 31, 2026, pursuant to Rule 212 of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure,\1\ the Cities of Anaheim, Azusa, Banning, 
Colton, Pasadena, and Riverside, California (Six Cities) filed a motion 
to rescind the June 18, 2026, show cause order issued by the Commission 
in the above-referenced proceeding \2\ as directed to each of the Six 
Cities as Participating Transmission Owners in the California 
Independent System Operator Corporation. Six Cities request that the 
Commission waive the answer period applicable to this motion.
