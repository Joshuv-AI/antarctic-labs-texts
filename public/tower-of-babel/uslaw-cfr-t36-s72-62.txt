36 C.F.R. § 72.62 — Amendments to approved grants.
Citation: us:cfr:36:72.62
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-72.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 72 (Urban Park And Recreation Recovery Act Of 1978). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Changes which alter the scope of any approved UPARR competitive grant must be submitted to and approved by NPS. Once a grant offer is made, based upon the preapplication, no increases in the amount of UPARR funding specified in the original proposal will be considered. Such changes should be the basis of a new proposal or application.
