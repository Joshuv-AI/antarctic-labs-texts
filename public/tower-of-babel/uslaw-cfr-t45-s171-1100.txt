45 C.F.R. § 171.1100 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:45:171.1100
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-171.1100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 171 (Information Blocking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets forth the information that will be posted on the Office of the National Coordinator for Health Information Technology's (ONC) public website about actors that have been determined by the HHS Office of Inspector General to have committed information blocking.
