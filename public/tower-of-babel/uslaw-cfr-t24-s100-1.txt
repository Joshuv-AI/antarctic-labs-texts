24 C.F.R. § 100.1 — Authority.
Citation: us:cfr:24:100.1
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-100.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 100 (Discriminatory Conduct Under The Fair Housing Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This regulation is issued under the authority of the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development to administer and enforce title VIII of the Civil Rights Act of 1968, as amended by the Fair Housing Amendments Act of 1988 (the Fair Housing Act).
