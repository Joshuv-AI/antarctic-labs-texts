33 C.F.R. § 157.28 — Discharges from tank barges exempted from certain design requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:33:157.28
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-157.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 157 (Rules For The Protection Of The Marine Environment Relating To Tank Vessels Carrying Oil In Bulk). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The person in charge of a tank barge exempted under § 157.08(g) from the requirements in §§ 157.11, 157.13, 157.15, and 157.23 shall ensure that while the barge is proceeding en route:
(a) Cargo tanks are not ballasted or washed; and
(b) Oil or oily mixtures are not discharged.
