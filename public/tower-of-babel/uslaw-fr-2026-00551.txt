DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Advisory Committee on the Readjustment of Veterans, Notice of 
Meeting Cancellation

    The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) gives notice under the 
Federal Advisory Committee Act, 5 U.S.C. Ch. 10, that the Advisory 
Committee on the Readjustment of Veterans meetings, previously 
scheduled to be held on January 27, at the Tulsa Vet Center, and on 
January 28 at the Muscogee Creek Nation Veterans Office, have been 
cancelled.
    For more information, please Joshua A. Mathis, Designated Federal 
Officer, Readjustment Counseling Service (10RCS), at 313-310-3891 or 
email [email protected].

    Dated: January 12, 2026.
Jelessa M. Burney,
Federal Advisory Committee Management Officer.
