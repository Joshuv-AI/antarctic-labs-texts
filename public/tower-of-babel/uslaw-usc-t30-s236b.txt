30 U.S.C. § 236b — Existing leases within naval petroleum reserves not affected
Citation: us:usc:30:236b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section236b
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 3A (Leases And Prospecting Permits); Subchapter IV (Oil And Gas). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this act shall be construed as affecting existing leases within the borders of the naval petroleum reserves, or agreements concerning operations thereunder or in relation thereto.
