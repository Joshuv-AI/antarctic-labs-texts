7 U.S.C. § 6999 — Judicial review
Citation: us:usc:7:6999
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section6999
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 98 (Department Of Agriculture Reorganization); Subchapter VIII (National Appeals Division). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A final determination of the Division shall be reviewable and enforceable by any United States district court of competent jurisdiction in accordance with chapter 7 of title 5.
