DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

Department of the Army


Notice of Termination of Environmental Impact Statement for the 
Orange and Rockland Utilities Transmission Line Upgrade Project, West 
Point, New York

AGENCY: Department of the Army, DoD.

ACTION: Notice of termination.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The Department of the Army (Army) is terminating the 
preparation of

[[Page 67825]]

an EIS addressing the Orange and Rockland Utilities Transmission Line 
Upgrade project at West Point, New York.

DATES: This termination is effective on the date of this notice.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Dr. David Guldenzopf, Director for 
Environmental Compliance and Mission Readiness, Office of the Assistant 
Secretary of the Army for Installations, Energy and Environment, at 
(703) 459-7756, [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: The Department of the Army hereby provides 
notice of termination of the subject EIS, which was initiated by a 
Notice of Intent to Prepare an Environmental Impact Statement. The 
Notice of Intent was published in the Federal Register on 15 July 2022 
(87 FR 42448). The EIS concerned potential environmental impacts 
associated with real estate transactions and a related utilities 
infrastructure upgrade at West Point, NY. Accordingly, the EIS 
initiated on July 15, 2022, is hereby terminated.
    Authority: National Environmental Policy Act of 1969, 42 U.S.C. 
4321 et seq.; Department of War NEPA Implementing Procedures, April 30, 
2026.

James W. Satterwhite, Jr.,
Army Federal Register Liaison Officer.
