RETRACTION: UBE2C Induces Cisplatin Resistance via ZEB1/2‐Dependent Upregulation of ABCG2 and ERCC1 in NSCLC Cells
Authors: Journal of Oncology
Published: 2025

RETRACTION: Y. Wu, D. Jin, X. Wang, et al., “UBE2C Induces Cisplatin Resistance via ZEB1/2‐Dependent Upregulation of ABCG2 and ERCC1 in NSCLC Cells,” Journal of Oncology, 2019, 8607859, https://doi.org/10.1155/2019/8607859.

The above article, published online on 01 January 2019 in Wiley Online Library (https://wileyonlinelibrary.com), and its correction (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/2020/5025641), has been retracted by John Wiley & Sons Ltd. The retraction has been agreed due to multiple concerns with the figures, some of which have been raised on PubPeer [1]. Investigation of the concerns identified the following issues:
