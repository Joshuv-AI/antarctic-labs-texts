21 C.F.R. § 864.5620 — Automated hemoglobin system.
Citation: us:cfr:21:864.5620
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-864.5620
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 864 (Hematology And Pathology Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. An automated hemoglobin system is a fully automated or semi-automated device which may or may not be part of a larger system. The generic type of device consists of the reagents, calibrators, controls, and instrumentation used to determine the hemoglobin content of human blood.
(b) Classification. Class II (performance standards).
