19 U.S.C. § 81s — Offenses
Citation: us:usc:19:81s
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section81s
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 1A (Foreign Trade Zones). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In case of a violation of this chapter, or any regulation under this chapter, by the grantee, any officer, agent or employee thereof responsible for or permitting any such violation shall be subject to a fine of not more than $1,000. Each day during which a violation continues shall constitute a separate offense.
