14 C.F.R. § 71.5 — Reporting points.
Citation: us:cfr:14:71.5
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-71.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 71 (Designation Of Class A, B, C, D, And E Airspace Areas; Air Traffic Service Routes; And Reporting Points). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The reporting points listed in subpart H of FAA Order JO 7400.11M (incorporated by reference, see § 71.1) consist of geographic locations at which the position of an aircraft must be reported in accordance with part 91 of this chapter.
