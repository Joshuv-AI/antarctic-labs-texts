42 C.F.R. § 137.57 — How is a funding agreement amended during the effective period of the funding agreement?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.57
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A funding agreement may be amended by the parties as provided for in the funding agreement, Title V, or this part.
