7 C.F.R. § 56.13 — Cancellation of license.
Citation: us:cfr:7:56.13
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-56.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 56 (Voluntary Grading Of Shell Eggs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon termination of the services of a licensed grader, the grader's license shall be immediately surrendered for cancellation.
