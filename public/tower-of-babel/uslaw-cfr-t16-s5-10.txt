16 C.F.R. § 5.10 — Cross-reference to executive branch-wide regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:16:5.10
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-5.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 5 (Standards Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees, including Commissioners, political appointees, and special Government employees, of the Federal Trade Commission are subject to and should refer to the executive branch-wide financial disclosure regulations at 5 CFR part 2634, and to the procedures for filing and review of financial disclosure reports found in Chapter 5 of the FTC Administrative Manual.
