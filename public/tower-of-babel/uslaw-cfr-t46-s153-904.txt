46 C.F.R. § 153.904 — Limitations in the endorsement.
Citation: us:cfr:46:153.904
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-153.904
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 153 (Ships Carrying Bulk Liquid, Liquefied Gas, Or Compressed Gas Hazardous Materials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may operate a tankship unless that person complies with all limitations in the endorsement on the tankship's Certificate of Inspection or Certificate of Compliance.
