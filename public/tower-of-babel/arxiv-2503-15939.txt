A note on 𝒟 ~ J + \mathcal{\tilde{D}}^{+}_{J} -operator
Dexie Lin, Hengyu Zhou

Abstract
In almost Kähler manifolds, one of the challenges is to construct an elliptic operator on functions that plays a role analogous to the ∂ ∂ ¯ \partial\bar{\partial} operator in complex or Kähler manifolds. One of the aims of this paper is to revisit the 𝒟 ~ J + \mathcal{\tilde{D}}^{+}_{J} -operator introduced in [ 12 ] . We will provide some local analysis estimates and highlight several difficulties that remain to be addressed. Additionally, we use the Atiyah-Hitchin-Singer operator to demonstrate that every d d -exact ( 1 , 1 ) (1,1) -form is globally 𝒟 ~ J + \mathcal{\tilde{D}}^{+}_{J} -exact for any compact taming symplectic 4 4 -manifold.

Introduction

One prominent direction in the study of symplectic manifolds involves extending equations from Kähler manifolds to symplectic manifolds. Among these, the Calabi-Yau equation plays an important role in the study of symplectic
4
-manifolds. This is programmed by Donaldson in
[
6
]
. A major challenge in solving this equation lies in the absence of the
\partial\bar{\partial}
-lemma in the symplectic manifolds.

•

Weinkove
[
13
, Section 2-Almost Kähler potential]
introduced the concept of almost Kähler potentials for compact almost Kähler
4
-manifolds.

•

Around the same time, Lejmi
[
11
, Theorem 2.1]
observed that any almost complex
4
-manifold admits a locally compatible symplectic form. In his proof, Lejmi defined an elliptic operator on almost complex
4
-manifolds.

Based on these contributions, Tan, Wang, Zhou, and Zhu
[
12
, Definition 2.5]
combined the elliptic operators introduced by Weinkove and Lejmi to define a generalized
\sqrt{-1}\partial\bar{\partial}
-operator, called the
\tilde{D}^{+}_{J}
-operator, for compact tamed symplectic
4
-manifolds. In their work, they made progress toward resolving Donaldson’s taming conjecture
[
6
]
under a certain condition (
h^{-}_{J}=b^{+}-1
).
Additionally, Tan, Wang, Zhou, and Zhu introduced the
\mathcal{W}
-operator, which can be interpreted as the
Jd
-operator augmented with additional terms. This naturally raises the following question:

Question
.

Let
a
be a real-valued
1
-form satisfying
d^{-}_{J}a=0
. Does there exist a real-valued function
f
such that
\mathcal{\tilde{D}}^{+}_{J}(f)=da
?

A classical approach to such problems is the
L^{2}
-method, as seen in
[
9
]
, analogous to the
\bar{\partial}
-problem solved by Hörmander. During a paper-reading seminar, the authors observed certain challenges related to the local analysis of the
\mathcal{\tilde{D}}^{+}_{J}
-operator, as detailed in Lemma
3.5
and Remark
3.6
. One of the goals of this paper is to outline these difficulties concerning the local exactness of the
\mathcal{\tilde{D}}^{+}_{J}
-operator, as discussed in Lemma
3.5
.
Furthermore, we employ the Atiyah-Hitchin-Singer operator
[
1
]
to address the aforementioned question. Our main result is the following:

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
(M,J,g,F)
be a compact almost Hermitian
4
-manifold, where
J
is tamed by a symplectic form
\omega
. If a real-valued
(1,1)
-form
\psi
can be expressed as
\psi=da
for some real-valued
1
-form
a
, then
\psi
is
\mathcal{\tilde{D}}^{+}_{J}
-exact.

Here, a compact manifold mean a compact manifolds without boundary.

Key Words:
taming symplectic condition,
4
-manifolds,
\mathcal{\tilde{D}}^{+}_{J}
-operator
