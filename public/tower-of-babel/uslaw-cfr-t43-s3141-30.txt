43 C.F.R. § 3141.30 — Land use plans.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3141.30
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3141.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3140 (Leasing In Special Tar Sand Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No lease will be issued under this subpart unless the lands have been included in a land use plan which meets the requirements under 43 CFR part 1600 or an approved Minerals Management Plan of the National Park Service. The decision to hold a lease sale and issue leases will be in conformance with the appropriate plan.
