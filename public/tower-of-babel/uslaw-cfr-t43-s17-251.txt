43 C.F.R. § 17.251 — Drug and alcohol addicts.
Citation: us:cfr:43:17.251
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-17.251
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 17 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs Of The Department Of The Interior). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient that operates a general hospital or outpatient facility may not discriminate in admission or treatment against a drug or alcohol abuser or addict who is suffering from a medical condition, because of the person's drug or alcohol abuse or addiction.
