12 C.F.R. § 192.465 — Retention of voting rights based on liquidation sub-accounts.
Citation: us:cfr:12:192.465
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-192.465
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 192 (Conversions From Mutual To Stock Form). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Eligible account holders or supplemental eligible account holders do not retain any voting rights based on their liquidation sub-accounts.
