42 U.S.C. § 2000ff–11 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:42:2000ff–11
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2000ff–11
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 21F (Prohibiting Employment Discrimination On The Basis Of Genetic Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out this chapter (except for section 2000ff–7 of this title).
