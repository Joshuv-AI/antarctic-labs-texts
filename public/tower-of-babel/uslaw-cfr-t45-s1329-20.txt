45 C.F.R. § 1329.20 — Centers for Independent Living (CIL) program.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1329.20
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1329.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1329 (State Independent Living Services And Centers For Independent Living). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

State allotments of Part C, funds shall be based on section 721(c) of the Act, and distributed to Centers within the State in accordance with the order of priorities in sections 722(e) and 723(e) of the Act.
