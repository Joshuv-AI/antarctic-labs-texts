50 C.F.R. § 27.72 — Audio equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:50:27.72
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-27.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 27 (Prohibited Acts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The operation or use of audio devices including radios, recording and playback devices, loudspeakers, television sets, public address systems and musical instruments so as to cause unreasonable disturbance to others in the vicinity is prohibited.
