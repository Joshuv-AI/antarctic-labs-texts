42 C.F.R. § 56.105 — Accord with health planning.
Citation: us:cfr:42:56.105
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-56.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 56 (Grants For Migrant Health Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A grant may be made under this part only if the applicable requirements of title XV of the Act relating to review and approval by the appropriate health planning agencies have been met.
