7 U.S.C. § 504 — “Person” defined
Citation: us:usc:7:504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section504
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 21 (Tobacco Statistics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The word “person” as used in this chapter shall be held to embrace also any partnership, corporation, association, or other legal entity.
