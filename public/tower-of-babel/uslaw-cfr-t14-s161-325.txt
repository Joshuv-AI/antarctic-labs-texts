14 C.F.R. § 161.325 — Availability of data and comments on an implemented restriction.
Citation: us:cfr:14:161.325
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-161.325
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 161 (Notice And Approval Of Airport Noise And Access Restrictions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant shall retain all relevant supporting data and all comments relating to an approved restriction for as long as the restriction is in effect and shall make these materials available for inspection upon request by the FAA. This information shall be made available for inspection by any person during the pendency of any petition for reevaluation found justified by the FAA.
