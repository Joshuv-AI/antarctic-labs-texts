Television Broadcasting Services Elko, Nevada

AGENCY: Federal Communications Commission.

ACTION: Final rule.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This document amends the Table of TV Allotments (table) of the 
Federal Communications Commission's (Commission) rules by substituting 
VHF channel 10 for UHF channel 20 at Elko, Nevada in response to a 
petition for rulemaking filed by Reno (KENV-TV) Licensee, Inc. 
(Licensee), the licensee of full power television station KENV-DT 
(KENV-DT or Station), Elko, Nevada. The Media Bureau (Bureau) 
previously granted a petition for rulemaking submitted by the Licensee 
to substitute UHF channel 20 for VHF channel 10 at Elko in the table, 
and subsequently granted a construction permit for its new channel. The 
Licensee states that it was unable to complete construction of the 
channel 20 facility by the deadline specified in the Station's 
construction permit. The Licensee filed the instant petition to amend 
the table to substitute VHF channel 10 for UHF channel 20 and allow 
KENV-DT to continue to operate on channel 10 at its licensed parameters 
and provide uninterrupted service to viewers. The staff engineering 
analysis finds that the proposal is in compliance with the Commission's 
principal community coverage and technical requirements, including the 
interference protection requirements. In support of its channel 
substitution request, the Licensee filed comments reaffirming its 
interest and stating that it commits to promptly take any and all steps 
that may be necessary to effectuate the channel substitution proposed 
in the petition. An individual commenter, Steven Quinn Singleton, also 
filed comments, which, for the reasons discussed in the Report and 
Order referenced below, the Bureau denied.
