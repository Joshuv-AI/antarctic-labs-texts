7 U.S.C. § 282 — Punishment for unlawful importation
Citation: us:usc:7:282
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section282
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 11 (Honeybees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who violates any provision of section 281 of this title or any regulation issued under it is guilty of an offense against the United States and shall, upon conviction, be fined not more than $1,000, or imprisoned for not more than one year, or both.
