22 U.S.C. § 3777 — Investigation of accident or injury giving rise to claim
Citation: us:usc:22:3777
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3777
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 51 (Panama Canal); Subchapter I (Administration And Regulations); Part 4 (Claims For Injuries To Persons Or Property); Subpart ii (Vessel Damage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a claim may not be considered under this subpart, or an action for damages lie thereon, unless, prior to the departure from the Panama Canal of the vessel involved—
(1)
an investigation of the accident or injury giving rise to the claim has been completed, which shall include a hearing by the Board of Local Inspectors of the Commission; and
(2)
the basis for the claim has been laid before the Commission.
