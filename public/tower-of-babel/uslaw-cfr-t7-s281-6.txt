7 C.F.R. § 281.6 — Liabilities and sanctions.
Citation: us:cfr:7:281.6
Creator: Food And Nutrition Administration, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-281.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 281 (Administration Of Snap On Indian Reservations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An ITO administering SNAP on a reservation is subject to the same liabilities and Federal sanctions as is any other State agency. FNS shall monitor administration of the Program and conduct reviews through the Performance Reporting System described in part 275. When necessary, warning procedures and other Federal sanctions prescribed in part 276 will be implemented.
