25 C.F.R. § 171.710 — Can I receive irrigation water if I am granted an Annual Assessment Waiver?
Citation: us:cfr:25:171.710
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-171.710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 171 (Irrigation Operation And Maintenance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. Water will not be delivered in any quantity to your farm unit if you have been granted an Annual Assessment Waiver.
