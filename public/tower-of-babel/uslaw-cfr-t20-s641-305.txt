20 C.F.R. § 641.305 — Who is responsible for developing and submitting the State Plan?
Citation: us:cfr:20:641.305
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-641.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 641 (Provisions Governing The Senior Community Service Employment Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Governor, or the highest governmental official, of each State is responsible for developing and submitting the State Plan to the Department.
