7 C.F.R. § 283.9 — Withdrawal of appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:7:283.9
Creator: Food And Nutrition Administration, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-283.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 283 (Appeals Of Quality Control (“Qc”) Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At any time before the ALJ files an initial decision, the State agency may withdraw its appeal and agree to pay the full amount of the claim. By withdrawing an appeal, the State agency waives all opportunity to appeal or seek further administrative or judicial review on the claim or related matters.
