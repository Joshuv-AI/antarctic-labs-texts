29 C.F.R. § 1911.4 — Additional or alternative procedural requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1911.4
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1911.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1911 (Rules Of Procedure For Promulgating, Modifying, Or Revoking Occupational Safety Or Health Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon reasonable notice to interested persons, the Assistant Secretary may in any particular proceeding prescribe additional or alternative procedural requirements:
(a) In order to expedite the conduct of the proceeding;
(b) In order to provide greater procedural protection to interested persons whenever it is found necessary or appropriate to do so; or
(c) For any other good cause which may be consistent with the applicable laws.
