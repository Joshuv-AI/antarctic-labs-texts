15 C.F.R. § 1500.84 — Notice and advance registration.
Citation: us:cfr:15:1500.84
Creator: Office Of The Under-Secretary For Economic Affairs, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-1500.84
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 1500 (Concrete Masonry Research, Education, And Promotion). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At least 30 days before a referendum is to be conducted under this Order, the Secretary shall notify all manufacturers of the period during which the referendum will occur through publication in the Federal Register. The notice shall explain any registration and voting procedures. A manufacturer who chooses to vote in any referendum conducted under this Order shall register with the Secretary prior to the voting period.
