32 C.F.R. § 226.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:226.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-226.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 226 (Shelter For The Homeless). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements 10 U.S.C. 2556 by establishing DoD policy, assigning responsibilities, and prescribing procedures for providing shelter for the homeless on military installations.
