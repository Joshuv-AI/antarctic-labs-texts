Synopsis

    With this Public Notice, the Wireless Telecommunications Bureau 
(Bureau) establishes GN Docket No. 26-289, which is captioned 
``Eligible Space Station Operator Transition Plans for the Upper C-
Band.'' This Public Notice also details the process for notice and 
comment on the eligible space station operators' initial Transition 
Plans. On July 24, 2026, the Commission released the Upper C-band R&O, 
91 FR 48700, which adopted rules to make 160 megahertz of mid-band 
spectrum available for flexible use throughout the contiguous United 
States by transitioning incumbent Fixed Satellite Service (FSS) 
operations out of 4.0-4.16 GHz.
    The Upper C-band R&O required that each eligible space station 
operator publicly file no later than November 5, 2026, an initial 
Transition Plan detailing all relocation steps and estimated costs 
necessary to clear 4.0-4.16 GHz. The Upper C-band R&O directed the 
Bureau to establish a dedicated docket for these purposes and to issue 
a public notice seeking stakeholder input on the initial Transition 
Plans. Comments on the initial Transition Plans are due on November 30, 
2026. All initial Transition Plans and responsive comments should be 
filed only in GN Docket No. 26-289.
