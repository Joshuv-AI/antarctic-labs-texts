15 U.S.C. § 68i — Application of other laws
Citation: us:usc:15:68i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section68i
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2 (Federal Trade Commission; Promotion Of Export Trade And Prevention Of Unfair Methods Of Competition); Subchapter III (Labeling Of Wool Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provision of this subchapter shall be held to be in addition to, and not in substitution for or limitation of, the provisions of any other Act of the United States.
