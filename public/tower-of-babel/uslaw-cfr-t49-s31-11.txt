49 C.F.R. § 31.11 — Referral of complaint and answer to the ALJ.
Citation: us:cfr:49:31.11
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-31.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 31 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of an answer, the reviewing official shall refer the matter to an ALJ by filing the complaint and answer in accordance with § 31.26.
