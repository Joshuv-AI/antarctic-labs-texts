42 U.S.C. § 11291 — Providing information for personnel of Social Security Administration
Citation: us:usc:42:11291
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section11291
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 118 (Alzheimer’S Disease And Related Dementias Research); Subchapter V (Educational Activities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary shall develop a mechanism to ensure the prompt provision of the most current information concerning Alzheimer’s disease and related dementias to the Commissioner of Social Security, particularly information which will increase the understanding of personnel of the Social Security Administration concerning such disease and dementias.
(b)
The Commissioner of Social Security shall ensure that information received under subsection (a) is provided to personnel of the Social Security Administration, particularly personnel involved in the process of determining, for purposes of titles II and XVI of the Social Security Act [42 U.S.C. 401 et seq., 1381 et seq.], whether an individual is under a disability.
