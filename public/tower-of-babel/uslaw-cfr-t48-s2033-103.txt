48 C.F.R. § 2033.103 — 2033.103 Protests to the agency.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2033.103
Creator: Chapter 20—Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2033.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2033 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Protests to the agency are first considered by the contracting officer. In accordance with FAR 33.103(d)(4), the protestor may appeal the contracting officer's decision by delivering or providing a written request to the agency Director, Division of Contracts or Property Management, or designee, to conduct an independent review of the Contracting Officer's decision.
