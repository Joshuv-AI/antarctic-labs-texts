7 U.S.C. § 7655 — Safe food handling education
Citation: us:usc:7:7655
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7655
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 103 (Agricultural Research, Extension, And Education Reform); Subchapter III (Miscellaneous Provisions); Part B (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture shall continue to develop a national program of safe food handling education for adults and young people to reduce the risk of food-borne illness. The national program shall be suitable for adoption and implementation through State cooperative extension services and school-based education programs.
