16 C.F.R. § 1028.112 — Review by institution.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1028.112
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1028.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1028 (Protection Of Human Subjects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Research covered by this policy that has been approved by an IRB may be subject to further appropriate review and approval or disapproval by officials of the institution. However, those officials may not approve the research if it has not been approved by an IRB.
