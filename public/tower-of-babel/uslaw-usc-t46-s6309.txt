46 U.S.C. § 6309 — Voyage data recorder access
Citation: us:usc:46:6309
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section6309
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part D (Marine Casualties); Chapter 63 (Investigating Marine Casualties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Coast Guard shall have full, concurrent, and timely access to and ability to use voyage data recorder data and audio held by any Federal agency in all marine casualty investigations, regardless of which agency is the investigative lead.
