12 C.F.R. § 366.2 — What is the scope of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:12:366.2
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-366.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 366 (Minimum Standards Of Integrity And Fitness For An Fdic Contractor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part applies to a person who submits an offer to perform or performs, directly or indirectly, a contractual service or function on our behalf.
(b) This part does not apply to:
(1) An FDIC employee for the purposes of title 18, United States Code; or
(2) The FDIC when we operate an insured depository institution such as a bridge bank or conservatorship.
