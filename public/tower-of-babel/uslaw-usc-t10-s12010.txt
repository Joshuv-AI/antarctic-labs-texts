10 U.S.C. § 12010 — Computations for Navy Reserve and Marine Corps Reserve: rule when fraction occurs in final result
Citation: us:usc:10:12010
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12010
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1201 (Authorized Strengths And Distribution In Grade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When there is a fraction in the final result of any computation under this chapter for the Navy Reserve or the Marine Corps Reserve, a fraction of one-half or more is counted as one, and a fraction of less than one-half is disregarded.
