26 U.S.C. § 9722 — Sham transactions
Citation: us:usc:26:9722
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section9722
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 99 (Coal Industry Health Benefits); Subchapter D (Other Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If a principal purpose of any transaction is to evade or avoid liability under this chapter, this chapter shall be applied (and such liability shall be imposed) without regard to such transaction.
