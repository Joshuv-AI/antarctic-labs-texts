16 U.S.C. § 754e — Funds for contaminant sample analyses
Citation: us:usc:16:754e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section754e
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 9 (Fish And Wildlife Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In fiscal year 2012 and hereafter, of the amount provided for environmental contaminants, up to $1,000,000 may remain available until expended for contaminant sample analyses.
