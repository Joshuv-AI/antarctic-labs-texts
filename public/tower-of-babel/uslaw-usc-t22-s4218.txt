22 U.S.C. § 4218 — False certificate as to ownership of property
Citation: us:usc:22:4218
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4218
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter XIV (Powers, Duties And Liabilities Of Consular Officers Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any consul or vice consul falsely and knowingly certifies that property belonging to foreigners is property belonging to citizens of the United States, he shall be punishable by imprisonment for not more than three years, and by a fine of not more than $10,000.
