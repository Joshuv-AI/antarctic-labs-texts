7 C.F.R. § 1925.2 — Definition of tax.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1925.2
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1925.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1925 (Taxes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For the purpose of this instruction, the word “tax” means all taxes, assessments, levies, irrigation and water charges or other similar obligations which are or will, on nonpayment, become a lien upon the real estate prior to the mortgage securing the Agency loan.
