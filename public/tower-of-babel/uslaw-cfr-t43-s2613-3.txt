43 C.F.R. § 2613.3 — Allowance of preference right.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2613.3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2613.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2610 (Carey Act Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a person's application is approved, such person shall have 90 days to submit an application for entry under another land law, and shall be entitled to a preference right of entry under other law if and when the lands are determined to be suitable for entry under such law pursuant to the regulations found in part 2400 of this chapter.
