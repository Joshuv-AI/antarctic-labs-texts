46 C.F.R. § 64.71 — Marking of pressure relief devices.
Citation: us:cfr:46:64.71
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-64.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 64 (Marine Portable Tanks And Cargo Handling Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A pressure relief device must be plainly and permanently marked with the—
(a) Set pressure rating;
(b) Rated flow capacity expressed as cubic feet of standard air (60 °F 14.7 psia) per minute and the pressure at which the flow capacity is determined;
(c) Manufacturer's name and identifying number; and
(d) Pipe size of inlet.
