41 C.F.R. § 303-70.3 — -70.3 Death-related expenses during leave or non-workdays.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 303-70 (70—Agency Requirements For Payment Of Expenses Connected With The Death Of Certain Employees And Immediate Family Members). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must pay death-related expenses when an employee dies while on leave or on a non-workday, while on TDY or stationed OCONUS, provided the requirements in § 303-70.1 are met. However, payment cannot exceed the amount allowed if death had occurred while on duty at the TDY station or at the official station OCONUS.
