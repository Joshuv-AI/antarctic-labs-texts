25 C.F.R. § 292.14 — Where must a tribe file an application for a Secretarial Determination?
Citation: us:cfr:25:292.14
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-292.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 292 (Gaming On Trust Lands Acquired After October 17, 1988). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A tribe must file its application for a Secretarial Determination with the Regional Director of the BIA Regional Office having responsibility over the land where the gaming establishment is to be located.
