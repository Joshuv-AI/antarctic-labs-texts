33 U.S.C. § 2733 — Bligh Reef light
Citation: us:usc:33:2733
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2733
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 40 (Oil Pollution); Subchapter II (Prince William Sound Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation shall within one year after August 18, 1990, install and ensure operation of an automated navigation light on or adjacent to Bligh Reef in Prince William Sound, Alaska, of sufficient power and height to provide long-range warning of the location of Bligh Reef.
