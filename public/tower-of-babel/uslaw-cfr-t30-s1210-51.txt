30 C.F.R. § 1210.51 — Who must submit royalty reports?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1210.51
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1210.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1210 (Forms And Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any person who pays royalty to ONRR must submit royalty reports to ONRR.
(b) Before you pay or report to ONRR, you must obtain a payor code. To obtain a payor code, refer to the ONRR Minerals Revenue Reporter Handbook for instructions and ONRR contact information (also see § 1210.56 for information on how to obtain a handbook).
