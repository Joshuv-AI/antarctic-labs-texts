42 U.S.C. § 5148 — Nonliability of Federal Government
Citation: us:usc:42:5148
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5148
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 68 (Disaster Relief); Subchapter III (Major Disaster And Emergency Assistance Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Federal Government shall not be liable for any claim based upon the exercise or performance of or the failure to exercise or perform a discretionary function or duty on the part of a Federal agency or an employee of the Federal Government in carrying out the provisions of this chapter.
