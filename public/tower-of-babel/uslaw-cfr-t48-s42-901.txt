48 C.F.R. § 42.901 — 42.901 General.
Citation: us:cfr:48:42.901
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-42.901
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 42 (Contract Administration And Audit Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contract administration office shall take prompt action to determine the potential impact of a contractor bankruptcy on the Government in order to protect the interests of the Government.
