41 C.F.R. § 301-52.9 — -52.9 Late payment fee entitlement.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-52.9
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-52.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-52 (52—Claiming Reimbursement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees will receive a late payment fee if the agency fails to reimburse them within 30 calendar days after submission of a proper travel claim to the approving official.
