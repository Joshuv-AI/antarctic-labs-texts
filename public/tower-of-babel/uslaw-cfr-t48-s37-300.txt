48 C.F.R. § 37.300 — 37.300 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:37.300
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-37.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 37 (Service Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes procedures for contracting for dismantling or demolition of buildings, ground improvements, and other real property structures and for the removal of such structures or portions of them (hereafter referred to as dismantling, demolition, or removal of improvements).
