14 C.F.R. § 382.71 — What other aircraft accessibility requirements apply to carriers?
Citation: us:cfr:14:382.71
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-382.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 382 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability In Air Travel). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) As a carrier, you must maintain all aircraft accessibility features in proper working order.
(b) You must ensure that any replacement or refurbishing of the aircraft cabin or its elements does not reduce the accessibility of that element to a level below that specified for new aircraft in this part.
