40 C.F.R. § 35.4085 — Can my group get an “advance payment” to help us get started?
Citation: us:cfr:40:35.4085
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-35.4085
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 35 (State And Local Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, a maximum of $5,000.00 in the form of an advance payment is available to new recipients.
