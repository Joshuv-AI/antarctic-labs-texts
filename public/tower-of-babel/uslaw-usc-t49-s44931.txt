49 U.S.C. § 44931 — Authority to exempt
Citation: us:usc:49:44931
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section44931
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart iii (Safety); Chapter 449 (Security); Subchapter II (Administration And Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Homeland Security may grant an exemption from a regulation prescribed in carrying out sections 44901, 44903, 44906, 44909(c), and 44935–44937 of this title when the Secretary decides the exemption is in the public interest.
