30 C.F.R. § 585.419 — What are my immediate responsibilities if I receive a suspension order?
Citation: us:cfr:30:585.419
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-585.419
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 585 (Renewable Energy On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must comply with the terms of a suspension order upon receipt and take any action prescribed within the time set forth therein.
