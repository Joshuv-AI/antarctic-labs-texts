28 C.F.R. § 51.53 — Information considered.
Citation: us:cfr:28:51.53
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-51.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 51 (Procedures For The Administration Of Section 5 Of The Voting Rights Act Of 1965, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Attorney General shall base a determination on a review of material presented by the submitting authority, relevant information provided by individuals or groups, and the results of any investigation conducted by the Department of Justice.
