Issuance of Multiple Exemptions

AGENCY: Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

ACTION: Notice; issuance.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is issuing a 
single notice to announce the issuance of six exemptions in response to 
requests as detailed in the available documents.

DATES: This notice contains six exemptions that the NRC granted during 
the period from July 1, 2026, to September 30, 2026, in response to 
requests submitted by four licensees.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Introduction

    During the period from July 1, 2026, through September 30, 2026, 
the NRC granted the included six exemptions in response to requests 
submitted by the following licensees: Constellation Energy Generation, 
LLC, Tennessee Valley Authority, Entergy Operations, Inc., and Vistra 
Operations Company LLC.
    The details of the exemptions that have been issued are provided in 
the available documents.

II. Availability of Documents

    The tables in this notice provide transparency regarding the number

name, facility name, docket number, document description, document 
date, and ADAMS accession number for each exemption issued. Additional 
details on each exemption issued, including the exemption request 
submitted by the respective licensee and the NRC's decision, are 
provided in each exemption approval listed in the following tables. For 
additional directions on accessing information in ADAMS, see the
