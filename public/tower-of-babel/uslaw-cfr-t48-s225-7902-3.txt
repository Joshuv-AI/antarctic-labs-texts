48 C.F.R. § 225.7902-3 — 225.7902-3 Policy.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 225 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DoD will facilitate maximum use of the DTC Treaties by prospective contractors responding to DoD solicitations and by contractors eligible to export qualifying defense articles under DoD contracts in accordance with 22 CFR 126.16(g) and 22 CFR 126.17(g).
