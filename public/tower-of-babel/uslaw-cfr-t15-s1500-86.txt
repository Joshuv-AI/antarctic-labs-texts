15 C.F.R. § 1500.86 — Separability.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-1500.86
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 1500 (Concrete Masonry Research, Education, And Promotion). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any provision of this Order is declared invalid or the applicability thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, the validity of the remainder of this Order or the applicability thereof to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
