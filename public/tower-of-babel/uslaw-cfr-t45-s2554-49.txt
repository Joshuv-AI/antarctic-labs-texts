45 C.F.R. § 2554.49 — How are civil penalties and assessments collected?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2554.49
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2554.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2554 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 3806 and 3808(b) of title 31, United States Code, authorize actions for collection of civil penalties and assessments imposed under this Part and specify the procedures for such actions.
