25 C.F.R. § 75.5 — Removal of deceased persons from the roll.
Citation: us:cfr:25:75.5
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-75.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 75 (Revision Of The Membership Roll Of The Eastern Band Of Cherokee Indians, North Carolina). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The name of any person who was not alive as of midnight August 21, 1957, shall be stricken from the basic membership roll by the Tribal Enrollment Office upon receipt of a death certificate or other evidence of death acceptable to the Tribal Enrollment Office.
