29 C.F.R. § 801.72 — Responsibility of the Office of Administrative Law Judges.
Citation: us:cfr:29:801.72
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-801.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 801 (Application Of The Employee Polygraph Protection Act Of 1988). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of the Secretary's Notice of Intent to Modify or Vacate the Decision and Order of an Administrative Law Judge, the Chief Administrative Law Judge shall, within fifteen (15) days, forward a copy of the complete hearing record to the Secretary.
