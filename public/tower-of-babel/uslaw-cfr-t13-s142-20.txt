13 C.F.R. § 142.20 — How are papers served?
Citation: us:cfr:13:142.20
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-142.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 142 (Administrative False Claims Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except for service of a complaint or a notice of hearing under §§ 142.11 and 142.14(b) respectively, service of papers must be made as prescribed by § 134.204 of this chapter.
