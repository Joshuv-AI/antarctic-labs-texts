7 C.F.R. § 1488.17 — Assignment.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1488.17
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1488.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1488 (Financing Of Sales Of Agricultural Commodities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The exporter shall not assign any claim or rights or any amounts payable under the financing agreement, in whole or in part, without written approval of the Vice President, CCC, or the Controller, CCC.
