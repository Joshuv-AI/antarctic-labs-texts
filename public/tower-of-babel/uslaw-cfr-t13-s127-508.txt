13 C.F.R. § 127.508 — May SBA appeal a contracting officer's decision not to make a requirement available for award as a WOSB Program contract?
Citation: us:cfr:13:127.508
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-127.508
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 127 (Women-Owned Small Business Federal Contract Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may appeal a contracting officer's decision not to make a particular requirement available for award under the WOSB Program.
