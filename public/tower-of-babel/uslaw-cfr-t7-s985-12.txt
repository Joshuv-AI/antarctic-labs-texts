7 C.F.R. § 985.12 — Salable quantity.
Citation: us:cfr:7:985.12
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-985.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 985 (Marketing Order Regulating The Handling Of Spearmint Oil Produced In The Far West). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Salable quantity means the total quantity of each class of oil which handlers may purchase from, or handle on behalf of, producers during a marketing year.
