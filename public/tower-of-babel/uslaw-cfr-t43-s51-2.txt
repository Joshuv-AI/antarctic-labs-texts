43 C.F.R. § 51.2 — Authority.
Citation: us:cfr:43:51.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-51.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 51 (Subsistence Management Regulations For Public Lands In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary of the Interior and Secretary of Agriculture issue the regulations in this part pursuant to authority vested in Title VIII of the Alaska National Interest Lands Conservation Act (ANILCA), 16 U.S.C. 3101-3126.
