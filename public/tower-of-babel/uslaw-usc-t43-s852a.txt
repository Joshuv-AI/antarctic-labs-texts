43 U.S.C. § 852a — Applications for unsurveyed lands; regulations; acreage requirements
Citation: us:usc:43:852a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section852a
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 20 (Reservations And Grants To States For Public Purposes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior may issue regulations governing applications for unsurveyed lands. If he establishes any minimum acreage requirements, they shall provide for selection of tracts of reasonable size, taking into consideration location, terrain, and adjacent land ownership and uses.
