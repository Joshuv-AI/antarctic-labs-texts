6 C.F.R. § 115.172 — Evidentiary standard for administrative investigations.
Citation: us:cfr:6:115.172
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-115.172
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 115 (Sexual Abuse And Assault Prevention Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When an administrative investigation is undertaken, the agency shall impose no standard higher than a preponderance of the evidence in determining whether allegations of sexual abuse are substantiated.
