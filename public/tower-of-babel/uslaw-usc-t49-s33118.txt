49 U.S.C. § 33118 — Preemption of State and local law
Citation: us:usc:49:33118
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section33118
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Information, Standards, And Requirements); Chapter 331 (Theft Prevention). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When a motor vehicle theft prevention standard prescribed under section 33102 or 33103 of this title is in effect, a State or political subdivision of a State may not have a different motor vehicle theft prevention standard for a motor vehicle or major replacement part.
