Abstract
This paper discusses the joint distribution of sample variances and covariances, expressed in quadratic forms in a matrix population arising in comparing the differences among groups under homogeneity of variance. One major concern of this article is to compare K K different populations, by assuming that the mean values of x 11 ( k ) , x 12 ( k ) , … , x 1 ​ p ( k ) , x 21 ( k ) , x 22 ( k ) , … x_{11}^{(k)},x_{12}^{(k)},\dots,x_{1p}^{(k)},x_{21}^{(k)},x_{22}^{(k)},\dots , x 2 ​ p ( k ) , … , x n ​ 1 ( k ) , x n ​ 2 ( k ) , … , x_{2p}^{(k)},\dots,x_{n1}^{(k)},x_{n2}^{(k)},\dots, x n ​ p ( k ) x_{np}^{(k)} in each population are M ( k ) M^{(k)} ( n × p n\times p ), k = 1 , 2 , … , K k=1,2,\dots,K and M M ( n × p n\times p ) a fixed matrix, with this hypothesis H 0 : M ( 1 ) = M ( 2 ) = ⋯ = M ( k ) = M , H_{0}:M^{(1)}=M^{(2)}=\dots=M^{(k)}=M, when the inter-group covariances are neglected and the intra-group covariances are equal. The N N intra-group variances and 1 2 ​ N ​ ( N − 1 ) \frac{1}{2}N(N-1) intra-group covariances where N = n ​ p N=np are classified into four categories T 1 T_{1} , T 1 ⁤ 1 2 T_{1\frac{1}{2}} , T 2 T_{2} and T 3 T_{3} according to the spectral forms of the precision matrix. The joint distribution of the sample variances and covariances is derived under these four scenarios. Besides, the moment generating function and the joint distribution of latent roots are explicitly calculated. As an application, we consider a classification problem in the discriminant analysis where the two populations should have different intra-group covariances. The distribution of the ratio of two quadratic forms is considered both in the central and non-central cases, with their exact power tabulated for different n n and p p .

(2.3)
