14 C.F.R. § 389.13 — Fees for services.
Citation: us:cfr:14:389.13
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-389.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 389 (Fees And Charges For Special Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Fees for services and resources described in this subpart and subpart C of this part are pursuant to those fees set forth in 49 CFR part 7, subpart F, §§ 7.41 through 7.43, 7.45 and 7.46.
