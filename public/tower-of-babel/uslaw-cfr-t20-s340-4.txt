20 C.F.R. § 340.4 — Methods of recovery of amounts recoverable.
Citation: us:cfr:20:340.4
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-340.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 340 (Recovery Of Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An amount recoverable may be recovered by any one or a combination of the methods described in §§ 340.5, 340.6, 340.7, and 340.8.
