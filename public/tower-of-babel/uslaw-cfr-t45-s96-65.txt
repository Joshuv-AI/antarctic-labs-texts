45 C.F.R. § 96.65 — Discovery.
Citation: us:cfr:45:96.65
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-96.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 96 (Block Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The use of interrogatories, depositions, and other forms of discovery shall not be allowed.
