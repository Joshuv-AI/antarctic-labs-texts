45 C.F.R. § 509.7 — Presettlement conference.
Citation: us:cfr:45:509.7
Creator: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Of The United States, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-509.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 509 (Filing Of Claims And Procedures Therefor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission on its own motion or initiative, or upon the application of a claimant for good cause shown, may direct that a presettlement conference be held with respect to any issue involved in a claim.
