49 C.F.R. § 574.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:574.2
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-574.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 574 (Tire Identification And Recordkeeping). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to facilitate notification to purchasers of defective or nonconforming tires, pursuant to Sections 30118 and 30119 of Title 49, United States Code, so that they may take appropriate action in the interest of motor vehicle safety.
