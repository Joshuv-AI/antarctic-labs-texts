16 U.S.C. § 230h — Change in name of Chalmette National Historical Park
Citation: us:usc:16:230h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section230h
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXV (Jean Lafitte National Historical Park And Preserve); Part A (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The area described in the Act of October 9, 1962 (76 Stat. 755), as the “Chalmette National Historical Park” is hereby redesignated as the Chalmette Unit of the Jean Lafitte National Historical Park and Preserve. Any references to the Chalmette National Historical Park shall be deemed to be references to said Chalmette Unit.
