16 C.F.R. § 1105.12 — Advance contributions.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1105.12
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1105.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1105 (Contributions To Costs Of Participants In Development Of Consumer Product Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may make its contribution in advance upon specific request, and the contribution may be made without regard to section 3648 of the Revised States of the United States (31 U.S.C. 529).
