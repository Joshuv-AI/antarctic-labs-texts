14 C.F.R. § 147.31 — Early testing.
Citation: us:cfr:14:147.31
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-147.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 147 (Aviation Maintenance Technician Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a student satisfactorily completes the general portion of a certificated aviation maintenance technician school's curriculum, the school may issue an authenticated document that demonstrates the student's preparedness to take the mechanic general written test in accordance with § 65.75(c) of this chapter.
