43 C.F.R. § 17.208 — Administrative requirements for small recipients.
Citation: us:cfr:43:17.208
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-17.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 17 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs Of The Department Of The Interior). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director may require any recipient with fewer than fifteen employees, or any class of such recipients, to comply with §§ 17.206 and 17.207, in whole or in part, when the Director finds a violation of this subpart or finds that such compliance will not significantly impair the ability of the recipient or class of recipients to provide benefits or services.
