7 C.F.R. § 652.42 — Recertification.
Citation: us:cfr:7:652.42
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-652.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 652 (Technical Service Provider Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A decertified technical service provider may apply to be re-certified under the certification provisions of this part after the period of decertification has expired. A technical service provider may not utilize the certification renewal process in an attempt to be recertified after being decertified.
