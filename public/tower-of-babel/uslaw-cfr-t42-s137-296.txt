42 C.F.R. § 137.296 — How does a Self-Governance Tribe comply with NEPA and NHPA?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.296
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.296
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Self-Governance Tribes comply with NEPA and the NHPA by adopting and following:
(a) their own environmental review procedures;
(b) the procedures of the IHS; and/or
(c) the procedures of another Federal agency.
