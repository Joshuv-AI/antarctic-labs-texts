30 C.F.R. § 1202.53 — Minimum royalty.
Citation: us:cfr:30:1202.53
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1202.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1202 (Royalties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For leases that provide for minimum royalty payments, the lessee shall pay the minimum royalty as specified in the lease.
