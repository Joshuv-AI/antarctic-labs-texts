25 C.F.R. § 291.5 — Where must the proposal requesting Class III gaming procedures be filed?
Citation: us:cfr:25:291.5
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-291.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 291 (Class Iii Gaming Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any proposal requesting Class III gaming procedures must be filed with the Director, Indian Gaming Management Staff, Bureau of Indian Affairs, U.S. Department of the Interior, MS 2070-MIB, 1849 C Street NW, Washington, DC 20240.
