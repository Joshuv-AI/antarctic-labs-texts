40 C.F.R. § 60.2035 — How are these new source performance standards structured?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.2035
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.2035
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These new source performance standards contain the eleven major components listed in paragraphs (a) through (k) of this section:
(a) Preconstruction siting analysis;
(b) Waste management plan;
(c) Operator training and qualification;
(d) Emission limitations and operating limits;
(e) Performance testing;
(f) Initial compliance requirements;
(g) Continuous compliance requirements;
(h) Monitoring;
(i) Recordkeeping and reporting;
(j) Definitions; and
(k) Tables.
