43 C.F.R. § 1865.3 — Issuance of patent or document of conveyance on motion of authorized officer.
Citation: us:cfr:43:1865.3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1865.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1860 (Conveyances, Disclaimers And Correction Documents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authorized officer may initiate and make corrections in patents or other documents of conveyance on his/her own motion, if all existing owners agree.
