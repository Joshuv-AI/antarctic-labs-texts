43 C.F.R. § 2547.6 — Lands not subject to disposal under this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2547.6
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2547.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2540 (Color-Of-Title And Omitted Lands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart shall not apply to any lands within the National Forest System, defined in the Act of August 17, 1974 (16 U.S.C. 1601), the National Park System, the National Wildlife Refuge System, and the National Wild and Scenic Rivers System.
