9 C.F.R. § 354.30 — Who may obtain inspection service.
Citation: us:cfr:9:354.30
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-354.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 354 (Voluntary Inspection Of Rabbits And Edible Products Thereof). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An application for inspection service may be made by any interested person, including, but not being limited to, the United States, any State, county, municipality, or common carrier, and any authorized agent of the foregoing.
