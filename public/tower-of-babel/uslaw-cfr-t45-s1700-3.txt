45 C.F.R. § 1700.3 — Membership.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1700.3
Creator: National Commission On Libraries And Information Science
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1700.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1700 (Organization And Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Commission is composed of the Librarian of Congress, the Director of the Institute of Museum and Library Services (who serves as an ex officio, nonvoting member), and 14 members appointed by the President, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate.
(b) The President designates one of the members of the Commission as the Chairperson.
