19 C.F.R. § 132.21 — Regulations applicable.
Citation: us:cfr:19:132.21
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-132.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 132 (Quotas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the regulations applicable to all mail importations (see part 145 of this chapter), the regulations in this subpart shall apply to mail importations of absolute quota merchandise.
