29 U.S.C. § 776 — Measuring of project outcomes and performance
Citation: us:usc:29:776
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section776
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 16 (Vocational Rehabilitation And Other Rehabilitation Services); Subchapter III (Professional Development And Special Projects And Demonstrations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commissioner may require that recipients of grants under this subchapter submit information, including data, as determined by the Commissioner to be necessary to measure project outcomes and performance, including any data needed to comply with the Government Performance and Results Act.
