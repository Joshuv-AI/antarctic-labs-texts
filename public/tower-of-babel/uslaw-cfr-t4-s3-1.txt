4 C.F.R. § 3.1 — Appointment, promotion, and assignment.
Citation: us:cfr:4:3.1
Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-4/section-3.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 3 (Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees of GAO shall be appointed, promoted and assigned solely on the basis of merit and fitness, but without regard to the provisions of title 5, United States Code, governing appointments and other personnel actions in the competitive service.
