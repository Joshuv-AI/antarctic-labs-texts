9 C.F.R. § 592.170 — Order of service.
Citation: us:cfr:9:592.170
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-592.170
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 592 (Voluntary Inspection Of Egg Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Service shall be performed, insofar as practicable, in the order in which applications therefor are made except that precedence may be given to any application for an appeal.
