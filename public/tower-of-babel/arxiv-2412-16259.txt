Abstract
Let 𝚔 \mathtt{k} be an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero and n , m n,m coprime positive integers. Let 𝔤 o \stackrel{{\scriptstyle{\rm o}}}{{\mathfrak{g}}} be the Lie superalgebra 𝔤 ​ 𝔩 ​ ( n | m ) \mathfrak{gl}(n|m) with root system Δ \Delta . Using Δ \Delta , Sergeev and Veselov, [ SV2 ] introduced an action of the Weyl groupoid 𝔚 \mathfrak{W} , in connection with their study of the the Grothendieck group of finite dimensinonal graded 𝔤 \mathfrak{g} -modules. We denote the subgroupoid of 𝔚 \mathfrak{W} with morphisms corresponding to isotropic roots by 𝔗 i ​ s ​ o \mathfrak{T}_{iso} . Later, [ SV101 ] the same authors defined an action of 𝔚 \mathfrak{W} on X = 𝚔 n | m X=\mathtt{k}^{n|m} such that the invariant algebra 𝒪 ​ ( X ) 𝔚 \mathcal{O}(X)^{\mathfrak{W}} is isomorphic to the algebra of quantum integrals for the deformed Calogero-Moser system introduced in [ SV1 ] . This completely integrable system depends on a non-zero parameter κ \kappa . When κ = − m / n \kappa=-m/n we study a certain infinite 𝔗 i ​ s ​ o \mathfrak{T}_{iso} -orbit O for this action. 2.5

and

\mu
by
\mu_{1}=m
,

\mu_{i}=\lambda_{i}
for
