22 U.S.C. § 5724 — Consultation with Congress
Citation: us:usc:22:5724
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section5724
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 66 (United States-Hong Kong Policy); Subchapter II (Status Of Hong Kong In United States Law). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out this subchapter, the President shall consult appropriately with the Congress.
