22 C.F.R. § 228.31 — Authority to approve waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:22:228.31
Creator: Agency For International Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-228.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 228 (Rules For Procurement Of Commodities And Services Financed By Usaid). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority to approve waivers of established policies under this regulation is delegated within USAID. Recipients or contractors shall request any necessary waivers through the USAID agreement or contracting officer.
