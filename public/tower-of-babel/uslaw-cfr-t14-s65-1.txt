14 C.F.R. § 65.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:65.1
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-65.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 65 (Certification: Airmen Other Than Flight Crewmembers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes the requirements for issuing the following certificates and associated ratings and the general operating rules for the holders of those certificates and ratings:
(a) Air-traffic control-tower operators.
(b) Aircraft dispatchers.
(c) Mechanics.
(d) Repairmen.
(e) Parachute riggers.
