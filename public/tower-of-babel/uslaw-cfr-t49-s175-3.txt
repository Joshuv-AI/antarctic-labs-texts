49 C.F.R. § 175.3 — Unacceptable hazardous materials shipments.
Citation: us:cfr:49:175.3
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-175.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 175 (Carriage By Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A hazardous material that is not prepared for shipment in accordance with this subchapter may not be offered or accepted for transportation or transported aboard an aircraft.
