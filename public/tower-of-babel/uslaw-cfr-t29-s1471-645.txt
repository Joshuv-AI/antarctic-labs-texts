29 C.F.R. § 1471.645 — Do other Federal agencies know if the Federal Mediation and Conciliation Service agrees to a voluntary exclusion?
Citation: us:cfr:29:1471.645
Creator: Federal Mediation And Conciliation Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1471.645
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1471 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Yes, we enter information regarding a voluntary exclusion into the EPLS.
(b) Also, any agency or person may contact us to find out the details of a voluntary exclusion.
