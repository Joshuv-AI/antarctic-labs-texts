17 C.F.R. § 232.310 — Marking changed material.
Citation: us:cfr:17:232.310
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-232.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 232 (Regulation S-T—General Rules And Regulations For Electronic Filings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Provisions requiring the marking of changed materials are satisfied in ASCII and HTML documents by inserting the tag <R>before and the tag </R>following a paragraph containing changed material. HTML documents may be marked to show changed materials within paragraphs. Financial statements and notes thereto need not be marked for changed material.
