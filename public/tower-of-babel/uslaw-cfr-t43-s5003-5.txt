43 C.F.R. § 5003.5 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:43:5003.5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-5003.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 5000 (Administration Of Forest Management Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a court holds any provisions of the regulations in this subpart or their applicability to any person or circumstances invalid, the remainder of this subpart and its applicability to other people or circumstances will not be affected.
