20 C.F.R. § 10.224 — What happens if OWCP finds that the employee is not entitled to COP after it has been paid?
Citation: us:cfr:20:10.224
Creator: Office Of Workers' Compensation Programs, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-10.224
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 10 (Claims For Compensation Under The Federal Employees' Compensation Act, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where OWCP finds that the employee is not entitled to COP after it has been paid, the employee may chose to have the time charged to annual or sick leave, or considered an overpayment of pay under 5 U.S.C. 5584. The employer must correct any deficiencies in COP as directed by OWCP.
