24 C.F.R. § 100.300 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:24:100.300
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Equal Opportunity, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-100.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 100 (Discriminatory Conduct Under The Fair Housing Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to effectuate the exemption in the Fair Housing Amendments Act of 1988 that relates to housing for older persons.
