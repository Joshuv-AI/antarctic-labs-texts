42 C.F.R. § 34.6 — Applicability of foreign quarantine regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:42:34.6
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-34.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 34 (Medical Examination Of Aliens). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Aliens arriving at a port of the United States shall be subject to the applicable provisions of 42 CFR part 71, Foreign Quarantine, with respect to examination and quarantine measures.
