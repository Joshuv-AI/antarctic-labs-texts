45 C.F.R. § 410.1704 — Treatment of an individual whom ORR has determined to be an adult.
Citation: us:cfr:45:410.1704
Creator: Office Of Refugee Resettlement, Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-410.1704
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 410 (Care And Placement Of Unaccompanied Children). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the procedures in this subpart would result in ORR reasonably concluding that an individual is an adult, despite the individual's claim to be under the age of 18, ORR shall treat such person as an adult for all purposes.
