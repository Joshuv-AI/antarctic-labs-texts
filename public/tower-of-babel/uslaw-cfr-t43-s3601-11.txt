43 C.F.R. § 3601.11 — When will environmental considerations prevent BLM from disposing of mineral materials?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3601.11
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3601.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3600 (Mineral Materials Disposal). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM will not dispose of mineral materials if we determine that the aggregate damage to public lands and resources would exceed the public benefits that BLM expects from the proposed disposition.
