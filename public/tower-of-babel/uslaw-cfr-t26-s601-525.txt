26 C.F.R. § 601.525 — Certification of copies of documents.
Citation: us:cfr:26:601.525
Creator: Internal Revenue Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-601.525
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 26 (Internal Revenue), Part 601 (Statement Of Procedural Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of paragraph (e) of § 601.504 with respect to certification of copies are applicable to a power of attorney or a tax information authorization required to be filed under § 601.522 or § 601.523.
