7 C.F.R. § 633.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:7:633.1
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-633.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 633 (Water Bank Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part set forth the policies, procedures, and requirements for the Water Bank Program (WBP) as administered by the Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) for program implementation.
