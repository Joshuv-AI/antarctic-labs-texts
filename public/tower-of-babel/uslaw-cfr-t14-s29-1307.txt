14 C.F.R. § 29.1307 — Miscellaneous equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:14:29.1307
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-29.1307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 29 (Airworthiness Standards: Transport Category Rotorcraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following is required miscellaneous equipment:
(a) An approved seat for each occupant.
(b) A master switch arrangement for electrical circuits other than ignition.
(c) Hand fire extinguishers.
(d) A windshield wiper or equivalent device for each pilot station.
(e) A two-way radio communication system.
