47 C.F.R. § 15.11 — Cross reference.
Citation: us:cfr:47:15.11
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-15.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 15 (Radio Frequency Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of subparts A, H, I, J and K of part 2 apply to intentional and unintentional radiators, in addition to the provisions of this part. Also, a cable system terminal device and a cable input selector switch shall be subject to the relevant provisions of part 76 of this chapter.
