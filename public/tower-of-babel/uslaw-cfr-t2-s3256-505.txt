2 C.F.R. § 3256.505 — Who in the NEA determines that a recipient who is an individual violated the requirements of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:2:3256.505
Creator: National Endowment For The Arts
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-3256.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 3256 (Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Chairman of the National Endowment for the Arts is the official authorized to make the determination under 2 CFR 182.505.
