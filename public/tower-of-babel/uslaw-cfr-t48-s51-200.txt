48 C.F.R. § 51.200 — 51.200 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:51.200
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-51.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 51 (Use Of Government Sources By Contractors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes policies and procedures for the use by contractors of interagency fleet management system (IFMS) vehicles and related services. In this subpart, the terms contractors and contracts include subcontractors and subcontracts (see 45.102).
