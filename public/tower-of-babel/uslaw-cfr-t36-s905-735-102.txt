36 C.F.R. § 905.735-102 — -102 Adoption of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:36:905.735-102
Creator: Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-905.735-102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 905 (Standards Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under the authority of 5 CFR 735.104(f), the Corporation adopts the following sections of the Civil Service Commission regulations on “Employee Responsibilities and Conduct” found in part 735 of title 5, Code of Federal Regulations: §§ 735.202 (a), (d), (e), (f) through 735.210; 735.302; 735.303(a); 735.304; 735.305(a); 735.306; 735.404 through 735.411; and 735.412 (b) and (d).
