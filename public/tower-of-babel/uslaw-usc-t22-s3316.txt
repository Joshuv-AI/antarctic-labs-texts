22 U.S.C. § 3316 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:22:3316
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3316
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 48 (Taiwan Relations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of the chapter and the application of such provision to any other person or circumstance shall not be affected thereby.
