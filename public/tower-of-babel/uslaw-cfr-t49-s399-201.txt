49 C.F.R. § 399.201 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:399.201
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-399.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 399 (Employee Safety And Health Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes step, handhold, and deck requirements on commercial motor vehicles. These requirements are intended to enhance the safety of motor carrier employees.
