43 U.S.C. § 485i — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:43:485i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section485i
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter X (Payment Of Construction Charges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to perform any and all acts and to make such rules and regulations as may be necessary and proper for the purpose of carrying the provisions of this subchapter into full force and effect.
