33 U.S.C. § 426–2 — Board on Coastal Engineering Research
Citation: us:usc:33:426–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section426–2
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 9 (Protection Of Navigable Waters And Of Harbor And River Improvements Generally); Subchapter I (In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The functions of the Coastal Engineering Research Center established by section 426–1 of this title, shall be conducted with the guidance and advice of a Board on Coastal Engineering Research, constituted by the Chief of Engineers in the same manner as the present Beach Erosion Board.
