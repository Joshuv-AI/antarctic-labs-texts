22 U.S.C. § 4041 — Administration
Citation: us:usc:22:4041
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 52 (Foreign Service); Subchapter VIII (Foreign Service Retirement And Disability); Part I (Foreign Service Retirement And Disability System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In accordance with such regulations as the President may prescribe, the Secretary of State shall administer the Foreign Service Retirement and Disability System (hereinafter in this part referred to as the “System”), originally established pursuant to section 18 of the Act of May 24, 1924 (43 Stat. 144).
