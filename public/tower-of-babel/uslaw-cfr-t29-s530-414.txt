29 C.F.R. § 530.414 — Equal Access to Justice Act.
Citation: us:cfr:29:530.414
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-530.414
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 530 (Employment Of Homeworkers In Certain Industries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Proceedings under this part are not subject to the provisions of the Equal Access to Justice Act. In any hearing conducted pursuant to these regulations, Administrative Law Judges shall have no power or authority to award attorney fees or other litigation expenses pursuant to the Equal Access to Justice Act.
