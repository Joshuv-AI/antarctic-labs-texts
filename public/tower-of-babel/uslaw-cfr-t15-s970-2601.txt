15 C.F.R. § 970.2601 — Additional information.
Citation: us:cfr:15:970.2601
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-970.2601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 970 (Deep Seabed Mining Regulations For Exploration Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any United States citizen filing notice under § 970.2402 or § 970.2501 of this part shall provide such additional information as the Administrator may require as necessary and appropriate to implement section 101 of the Act.
