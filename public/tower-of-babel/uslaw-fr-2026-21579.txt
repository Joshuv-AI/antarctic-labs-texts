SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: 
    Title: Process for Review of Swaps for Mandatory Clearing (OMB 
Control No. 3038-0097). This is a request for extension of a currently 
approved information collection.
    Abstract: The Commodity Exchange Act and Commission regulations 
require a derivatives clearing organization (DCO) that wishes to accept 
a swap for clearing to be eligible to clear the swap and to submit the 
swap to the Commission for a determination as to whether the swap is 
required to be cleared. Commission Regulation 39.5 sets forth the 
process for these submissions. The Commission will use the information 
in this collection to determine whether a DCO that wishes to accept a 
swap for clearing is eligible to clear the swap and whether the swap 
should be required to be cleared. An agency may not conduct or sponsor, 
and a person is not required to respond to, a collection of information 
unless it displays a currently valid OMB control number.
    On July 28, 2026, the Commission published in the Federal Register 
notice of the proposed extension of this information collection and 
provided 60 days for public comment on the proposed extension, 91 FR 
47225 (``60-Day Notice''). The Commission received one comment from an 
anonymous submitter regarding its PRA burden estimates.\1\ The comment 
asked how the Commission modeled or concluded that each registered DCO 
will submit one swap submission per year at an estimated 40 hours per 
submission.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    \1\ Anonymous Commenter Letter.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The Commission's estimate of one submission per DCO per year is 
intended to account for variations in the number of swap submissions 
received by year by DCO. Some DCOs may be required to file multiple 
swap submissions in connection with the launch of new clearing 
initiatives, while other DCOs may not file any swap submissions in a 
given year.
    The Commission's estimate of 40 hours per DCO submission is based 
on the Commission's experience reviewing the submissions. It considers 
the scope of topics the submissions must address, the level of detail 
typically provided by DCOs, the Commission's possible engagement with 
DCOs regarding submissions, and the nature of the information that may 
be sought by Commission staff. Generally, DCOs also have aggregated and 
presented information about new cleared swaps in other contexts, such 
as public marketing materials or as part of ongoing DCO supervision.
    The comment also sought the Commission's rationale for its estimate 
that the regulation does not entail separate operation and maintenance 
costs for DCOs. The regulation requires only the provision of certain 
swap information that is part of other rule submissions done in the 
ordinary course of launching new cleared swap offerings. DCOs typically 
comply with this regulation along with other required Commission 
reporting and notifications.
    Burden Statement: The respondent burden for this collection is 
estimated to be as follows:
    Estimated Number of Respondents: 24.
    Estimated Average Burden Hours per Respondent: 40.
    Estimated Total Annual Burden Hours: 960.
    Frequency of Collection: On occasion. A DCO is only required to 
make a one-time submission before it first accepts a swap for clearing.

    There are no capital costs or operating and maintenance costs 
associated with this collection.
