31 C.F.R. § 0.106 — Corrective action.
Citation: us:cfr:31:0.106
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-0.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 0 (Department Of The Treasury Employee Rules Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee's violation of generally accepted rules of conduct, the standards in this part, or the standards in other applicable regulations may result in appropriate corrective or disciplinary action, in addition to any penalty prescribed by law.
