48 C.F.R. § 232.703-70 — 232.703-70 Military construction appropriations act restriction.
Citation: us:cfr:48:232.703-70
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-232.703-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 232 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Annual military construction appropriations acts restrict the use of funds appropriated by the acts for payments under cost-plus-fixed-fee contracts (see 216.306(c)).
