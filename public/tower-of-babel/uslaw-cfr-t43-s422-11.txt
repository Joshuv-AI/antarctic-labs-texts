43 C.F.R. § 422.11 — Position sensitivity and investigations.
Citation: us:cfr:43:422.11
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-422.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 422 (Law Enforcement Authority At Bureau Of Reclamation Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each law enforcement contract or cooperative agreement must include a provision requiring the CLEO to certify that each officer who exercises authority under the Act has completed an FBI criminal history check and is satisfactorily cleared.
