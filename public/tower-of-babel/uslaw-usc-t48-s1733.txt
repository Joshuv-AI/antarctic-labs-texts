48 U.S.C. § 1733 — Qualifications for Office of Delegate
Citation: us:usc:48:1733
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1733
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 16 (Delegates To Congress); Subchapter II (American Samoa). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To be eligible for the office of Delegate a candidate shall—
(a)
be at least twenty-five years of age on the date of the election;
(b)
owe allegiance to the United States;
(c)
be an inhabitant of the Territory of American Samoa; and
(d)
not be, on the date of the election, a candidate for any other office.
