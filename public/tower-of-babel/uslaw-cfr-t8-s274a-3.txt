8 C.F.R. § 274a.3 — a.3 Continuing employment of unauthorized aliens.
Citation: us:cfr:8:274a.3
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-274a.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 274a (PART 274a—CONTROL OF EMPLOYMENT OF ALIENS). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employer who continues the employment of an employee hired after November 6, 1986, knowing that the employee is or has become an unauthorized alien with respect to that employment, is in violation of section 274A(a)(2) of the Act.
