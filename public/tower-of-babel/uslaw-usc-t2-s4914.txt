2 U.S.C. § 4914 — Academic year and summer term for page program
Citation: us:usc:2:4914
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4914
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 49 (Congressional Pages); Subchapter II (House Of Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The page program shall consist of the two semesters of the academic year, plus a non-academic summer term.
