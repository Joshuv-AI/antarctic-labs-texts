48 C.F.R. § 23.108 — 23.108 Required Environmental Protection Agency purchasing programs.
Citation: us:cfr:48:23.108
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-23.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 23 (Environment, Sustainable Acquisition, And Material Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with 23.104(c), contracting officers shall, after meeting statutory purchasing program requirements in 23.107, purchase to the maximum extent practicable products and services that meet EPA purchasing program requirements described in 23.108-1 through 23.108-3.
