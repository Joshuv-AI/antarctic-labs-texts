28 C.F.R. § 17.43 — Reinvestigation requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:28:17.43
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-17.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 17 (Classified National Security Information And Access To Classified Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees who are eligible for access to classified information shall be subject to periodic reinvestigations and may also be reinvestigated if, at any time, there is reason to believe that they may no longer meet the standards for access.
