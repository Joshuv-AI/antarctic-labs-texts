5 C.F.R. § 894.702 — May I participate in open season and make changes to my enrollment as an annuitant or compensationer?
Citation: us:cfr:5:894.702
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-894.702
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 894 (Federal Employees Dental And Vision Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Annuitants and compensationers may participate in open season and make enrollment changes under the same circumstances as active employees or employees of the United States Postal Service.
