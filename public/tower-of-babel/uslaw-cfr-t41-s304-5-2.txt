41 C.F.R. § 304-5.2 — -5.2 Approval authority for non-Federal source payment acceptance.
Citation: us:cfr:41:304-5.2
Creator: Chapter 304—Payment Of Travel Expenses From A Non-Federal Source
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-304-5.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 304-5 (5—Agency Responsibilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An official at the highest practical administrative level who can evaluate the requirements in § 304-5.3 must approve acceptance of such payments.
