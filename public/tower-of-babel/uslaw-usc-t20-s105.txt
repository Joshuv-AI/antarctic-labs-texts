20 U.S.C. § 105 — Books for Library of Congress
Citation: us:usc:20:105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section105
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 6 (American Printing House For The Blind). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The distribution of embossed books manufactured by the American Printing House for the Blind at Louisville, Kentucky, out of the income of the fund provided by sections 101, 102, and 104 of this title, shall include one copy of every book so manufactured to be deposited in the Library of Congress at Washington.
