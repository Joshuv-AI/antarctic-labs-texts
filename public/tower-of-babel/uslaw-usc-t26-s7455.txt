26 U.S.C. § 7455 — Service of process
Citation: us:usc:26:7455
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section7455
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 76 (Judicial Proceedings); Subchapter C (The Tax Court); Part II (Procedure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The mailing by certified mail or registered mail of any pleading, decision, order, notice, or process in respect of proceedings before the Tax Court shall be held sufficient service of such pleading, decision, order, notice, or process.
