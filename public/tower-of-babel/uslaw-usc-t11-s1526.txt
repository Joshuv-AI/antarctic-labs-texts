11 U.S.C. § 1526 — Cooperation and direct communication between the trustee and foreign courts or foreign representatives
Citation: us:usc:11:1526
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1526
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 15 (Ancillary And Other Cross-Border Cases); Subchapter IV (Cooperation With Foreign Courts And Foreign Representatives). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Consistent with section 1501, the trustee or other person, including an examiner, authorized by the court, shall, subject to the supervision of the court, cooperate to the maximum extent possible with a foreign court or a foreign representative.
(b)
The trustee or other person, including an examiner, authorized by the court is entitled, subject to the supervision of the court, to communicate directly with a foreign court or a foreign representative.
