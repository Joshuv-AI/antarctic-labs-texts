36 C.F.R. § 1192.2 — Equivalent facilitation.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1192.2
Creator: Architectural And Transportation Barriers Compliance Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1192.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1192 (Americans With Disabilities Act (Ada) Accessibility Guidelines For Transportation Vehicles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Departures from particular technical and scoping requirements of these guidelines by use of other designs and technologies are permitted where the alternative designs and technologies used will provide substantially equivalent or greater access to and usability of the vehicle. Departures are to be considered on a case-by-case basis by the Department of Transportation under the procedure set forth in 49 CFR 37.7.
