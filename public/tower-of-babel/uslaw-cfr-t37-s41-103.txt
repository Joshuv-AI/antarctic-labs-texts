37 C.F.R. § 41.103 — Jurisdiction over involved files.
Citation: us:cfr:37:41.103
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-41.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 41 (Practice Before The Patent Trial And Appeal Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board acquires jurisdiction over any involved file when the Board initiates a contested case. Other proceedings for the involved file within the Office are suspended except as the Board may order.
