12 C.F.R. § 4.66 — Oversight and monitoring.
Citation: us:cfr:12:4.66
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-4.66
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 4 (Organization And Functions, Availability And Release Of Information, Contracting Outreach Program, Post-Employment Restrictions For Senior Examiners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Deputy Comptroller for Resource Management shall appoint an Outreach Program Manager, who shall appoint an Outreach Program Specialist. The Outreach Program Manager is primarily responsible for program advocacy, oversight and monitoring.
