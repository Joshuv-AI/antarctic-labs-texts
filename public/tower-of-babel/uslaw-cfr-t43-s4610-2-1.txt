43 C.F.R. § 4610.2-1 — -1 Form of lease.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4610.2-1
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4610.2-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4600 (Leases Of Grazing Land—Pierce Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Leases under the Pierce Act should conform in general to a form approved by the Director. This form is believed adaptable for use in all of the States within which grazing districts have been established under the Taylor Grazing Act. Leases under the Pierce Act must be executed by the lessor in the manner prescribed by the laws of the State within which the lands leased are situated.
