22 C.F.R. § 1006.525 — Whom do I ask if I have questions about a person in the EPLS?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1006.525
Creator: Inter-American Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1006.525
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1006 (Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you have questions about a person in the EPLS, ask the point of contact for the Federal agency that placed the person's name into the EPLS. You may find the agency point of contact from the EPLS.
