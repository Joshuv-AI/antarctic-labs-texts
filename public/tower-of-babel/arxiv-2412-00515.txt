Analysis of the form factors B c → D ( ∗ ) B_{c}\rightarrow D^{(*)} , D s ( ∗ ) D_{s}^{(*)} and relevant nonleptonic decays
Bin Wu 1, Guo-Liang Yu 1,2, Zhi-Gang Wang 1, Ze Zhou 1, Jie Lu 3

Abstract
This article is devoted to calculating the form factors of B c → D ∗ B_{c}\to D^{*} , B c → D B_{c}\to D , B c → D s ∗ B_{c}\to D_{s}^{*} and B c → D s B_{c}\to D_{s} transitions in the framework of three-point QCD sum rules. At the QCD side, the contributions of ⟨ q ¯ ​ q ⟩ \langle\overline{q}q\rangle , ⟨ q ¯ ​ g s ​ σ ​ G ​ q ⟩ \langle\overline{q}g_{s}\sigma Gq\rangle , ⟨ g s 2 ​ G 2 ⟩ \langle g_{s}^{2}G^{2}\rangle , ⟨ f 3 ​ G 3 ⟩ \langle f^{3}G^{3}\rangle and ⟨ q ¯ ​ q ⟩ ​ ⟨ g s 2 ​ G 2 ⟩ \langle\overline{q}q\rangle\langle g_{s}^{2}G^{2}\rangle are taken into account. With the obtained form factors, the decay widths and branching ratios of several two-body nonleptonic decay processes B c → η c ​ D ∗ B_{c}\to\eta_{c}D^{*} , η c ​ D \eta_{c}D , J / ψ ​ D ∗ J/\psi D^{*} , J / ψ ​ D J/\psi D , η c ​ D s ∗ \eta_{c}D_{s}^{*} , η c ​ D s \eta_{c}D_{s} , J / ψ ​ D s ∗ J/\psi D_{s}^{*} and J / ψ ​ D s J/\psi D_{s} are predicted. These results about the form factors and decay properties of B c B_{c} meson provide useful information for us to study the heavy-quark dynamics.

II
Three-point QCDSR for the form factors

In the framework of QCDSR, the form factors are obtained by equating correlation functions in phenomenological and QCD sides, where correlation function is represented in hadronic and quark-gluon languages respectively. Thus, the first step to obtain form factor is to write the following three-point correlation function,

\displaystyle\Pi(p,p^{\prime})={i^{2}}\int d^{4}xd^{4}ye^{ipx}e^{i(p-p^{\prime})y}\left\langle 0\right|T\{{J_{X}}(x){J}(y)J_{B_{c}}^{+}(0)\}\left|0\right\rangle

are interpolating currents which have the same quantum numbers with these mesons.

is transition current which is extracted from the low-energy effective Hamiltonian. These currents are written as follows,

\displaystyle J_{B_{c}}(0)

\displaystyle=\bar{c}(0)i{\gamma_{5}}b(0)

\displaystyle J_{D}(x)

\displaystyle=\bar{c}(x)i{\gamma_{5}}d(x)

\displaystyle J_{D_{s}}(x)

\displaystyle=\bar{c}(x)i{\gamma_{5}}s(x)

\displaystyle{J^{D^{*}}_{\mu}}(x)

\displaystyle=\bar{c}(x){\gamma_{\mu}}d(x)

\displaystyle{J^{D_{s}^{*}}_{\mu}}(x)

\displaystyle=\bar{c}(x){\gamma_{\mu}}s(x)

\displaystyle J(y)

\displaystyle=\bar{q}(y)\Gamma b(y)

\displaystyle\times\int d^{4}k\frac{1}{[(k-p^{\prime})^{2}-m_{c}^{2}](k^{2}-m_{d(s)}^{2})[(k+p-p^{\prime})^{2}-m_{b}^{2}]}

(21)

II.3
QCD sum rules for form factors

After the spectral densities have been calculated, the sum rules for form factors can be obtained by matching the phenomenological side and the QCD side. To eliminate the contributions from excited and continuum states, the threshold parameters
s_{0}
and
u_{0}
are introduced, and the double Borel transformation is preformed to both the phenomenological side and the QCD side. Taking also the vector form factors of
B_{c}\to D
transition as an example, the sum rules can be expressed as,

\displaystyle\bigg\{B\Big[(1-A)f_{+}(Q^{2})+Af_{0}(Q^{2})\Big]p_{\mu}

\displaystyle+B\Big[(1+A)f_{+}(Q^{2})-Af_{0}(Q^{2})\Big]p^{\prime}_{\mu}\bigg\}\times\exp\Big(-\frac{m_{{B_{c}}}^{2}}{M^{2}}-\frac{m_{D}^{2}}{kM^{2}}\Big)

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{4\pi^{2}}\int\limits_{s_{\min}}^{s_{0}}\int\limits_{u_{\min}}^{u_{0}}dsdu\Big[\rho_{1}(s,u,Q^{2})p_{\mu}+\rho_{2}(s,u,Q^{2})p^{\prime}_{\mu}\Big]

\displaystyle\times\exp\Big(-\frac{s}{M^{2}}-\frac{u}{kM^{2}}\Big)

(22)

where substitutions of
p^{2}\rightarrow-P^{2}
,
p^{\prime 2}\rightarrow-P^{\prime 2}
and
q^{2}\rightarrow-Q^{2}
are conducted, the threshold parameters
s_{0}
and
u_{0}
serve as the upper limits of integral. After performing double Borel transformation with respect to
P^{2}
and
P^{\prime 2}
, there are two Borel parameters
M^{2}
and
M^{\prime 2}
. We then take
M^{\prime 2}=kM^{2}
with the factor
k=m_{X}^{2}/m_{B_{c}}^{2}
Bracco:2011pg
. The double Borel transformation can enhance the contribution of ground state while suppress those of excited and continuum states. It is noted that the physical quantities, such as the form factor, extracted from QCD sum rules should be independent of Borel parameters. That is to say, we should find a plateau where the form factors are stable and convergent. This plateau is commonly called the Borel Window or Borel platform.

V
Conclusions

In this work, the form factors for
B_{c}\to D^{(*)}
and
B_{c}\to D_{s}^{(*)}
transition processes are systematically analyzed in the framework of three-point QCDSR. The numerical results in space-like region (
Q^{2}>0
) are firstly calculated and then are fitted into analytical functions using the
z-
series parameterizations approach. With these analytical functions, we obtain the form factors at
Q^{2}=0
and
Q^{2}=-M^{2}
by extrapolating the results into time-like region (
Q^{2}<0
). Using these form factors, the decay widths and branching ratios of two-body nonleptonic decays including
B_{c}\to\eta_{c}D^{*}
,
\eta_{c}D
,
J/\psi D^{*}
,
J/\psi D
,
\eta_{c}D_{s}^{*}
,
\eta_{c}D_{s}
,
J/\psi D_{s}^{*}
and
J/\psi D_{s}
are obtained with the factorization method. All of these results about form factors, decay widths and branching ratios obtained in this work not only provide useful information for further studying the heavy-quark dynamics but also are helpful to the experiments of heavy flavor physics in the future.
