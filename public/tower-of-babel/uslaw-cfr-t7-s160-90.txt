7 C.F.R. § 160.90 — False, misleading, or deceitful practices.
Citation: us:cfr:7:160.90
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-160.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 160 (Regulations And Standards For Naval Stores). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No label or other means or practice used in connection with the sale of naval stores in commerce or of anything offered as such shall be false, misleading, or deceitful in any manner.
