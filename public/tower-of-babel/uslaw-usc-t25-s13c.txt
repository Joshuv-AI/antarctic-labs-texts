25 U.S.C. § 13c — Source of funds to pay cost of lunches for nonboarding public school students
Citation: us:usc:25:13c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section13c
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 1 (Bureau Of Indian Affairs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after October 12, 1984, any cost of providing lunches to nonboarding students in public schools from funds appropriated under this or any other Act for the Bureau of Indian Affairs shall be paid from the amount of such funds otherwise allocated for the schools involved without regard to the cost of providing lunches for such students.
