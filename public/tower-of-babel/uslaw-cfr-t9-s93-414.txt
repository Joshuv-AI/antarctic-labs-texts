9 C.F.R. § 93.414 — Milk from quarantined ruminants.
Citation: us:cfr:9:93.414
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-93.414
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 93 (Importation Of Certain Animals, Birds, Fish, And Poultry, And Certain Animal, Bird, And Poultry Products; Requirements For Means Of Conveyance And Shipping Containers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Milk or cream from ruminants quarantined under the provisions of this part shall not be used by any person other than those in charge of such ruminants, nor be fed to any animals other than those within the same enclosure, without permission of the APHIS representative or inspector in charge of the quarantine station and subject to such restrictions as he or she may consider necessary to each instance. No milk or cream shall be removed from the quarantine premises except in compliance with all State and local regulations.
