12 C.F.R. § 1223.25 — Office of Finance.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1223.25
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1223.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1223 (Minority And Women Inclusion). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All sections of this part and the standards issued under it shall apply to the Office of Finance, as defined in § 1201.1 of this chapter, in the same manner in which it applies to the regulated entities, unless the Office of Finance is otherwise specifically addressed or excluded.
