54 U.S.C. § 304110 — Report by Secretary to Council
Citation: us:usc:54:304110
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section304110
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3041 (Advisory Council On Historic Preservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To assist the Council in discharging its responsibilities under this division, the Secretary at the request of the Chairman shall provide a report to the Council detailing the significance of any historic property, describing the effects of any proposed undertaking on the affected property, and recommending measures to avoid, minimize, or mitigate adverse effects.
