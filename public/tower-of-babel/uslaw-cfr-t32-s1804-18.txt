32 C.F.R. § 1804.18 — Termination of access.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1804.18
Creator: National Counterintelligence Center
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1804.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1804 (Access By Historical Researchers And Former Presidential Appointees Pursuant To Section 4.5 Of Executive Order 12958). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Coordinator shall cancel any authorization whenever the security clearance of a requester (or research associate, if any) has been canceled or whenever the Director, NACIC determines that continued access would not be in compliance with one or more of the requirements of § 1804.14(a).
