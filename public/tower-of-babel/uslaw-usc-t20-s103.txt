20 U.S.C. § 103 — Publications for National Library for the Blind
Citation: us:usc:20:103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section103
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 6 (American Printing House For The Blind). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Two copies of each of the publication printed by the American Printing House for the Blind shall be furnished free of charge to the National Library for the Blind located at 1729 H Street Northwest, Washington, District of Columbia.
