Treewidth 2 in the Planar Graph Product Structure Theorem
Marc Distel Kevin Hendrey Nikolai Karol David R. Wood Jung Hon Yip

Abstract
We prove that every planar graph is contained in H 1 ⊠ H 2 ⊠ K 2 H_{1}\boxtimes H_{2}\boxtimes K_{2} for some graphs H 1 H_{1} and H 2 H_{2} both with treewidth 2. This resolves a question of Liu, Norin and Wood [arXiv:2410.20333]. We also show this result is best possible in the following sense: for any c ∈ ℕ c\in\mathbb{N} , there is a planar graph G G such that for any tree T T and graph H H with tw ⁡ ( H ) ⩽ 2 \tw(H)\leqslant 2 , G G is not contained in H ⊠ T ⊠ K c H\boxtimes T\boxtimes K_{c} .

t
-distension
of

if
|V(P_{vw})|=t
for each edge

. Observe that every distension of a planar graph is planar.

Lemma 10
.

For any

, there exists a planar graph
G
such that for any tree-partition
\mathcal{P}
of

\mathcal{Q}
of
G

Proof.

Let
t:=8c^{2}+2c-1
. Let
F
be the fan on
t+1
vertices with centre

. Let

be the path

. Let

be the
t
-distension of
F
, and let

be the
t
-distension of

. Since

is planar,
J
is planar, and

is planar. Consider any tree-partition
\mathcal{P}
of
G
and any partition
\mathcal{Q}
of
G
with
|P\cap Q|\leqslant c
for each
P\in\mathcal{P}
and

.

Since
|V(F)|=t+1\geqslant c^{2}+c+1
, by

8
applied to

and the induced partitions of

, there exists
v_{2}\in B

Q_{1}\neq Q_{2}
. Let
C
be the

J_{v_{1}v_{2}}-v_{1}-v_{2}
.

Since
|V(J_{v_{1}v_{2}})|=t+2=8c^{2}+2c+1
, if
P_{1}\neq P_{2}
then by

9
, there exist vertices
v_{3},v_{4}
such that
\{v_{1},v_{2},v_{3},v_{4}\}
is a
4
-clique

with

pairwise distinct, as desired. So we may assume that

Consider any vertex

and

Theorem 11
.

For any
c\in\mathbb{N}
there exists a planar graph

\textstyle\sim\\[-1.72218pt]

Theorem

strengthens a result of

, who proved it when

The next lemma implies that the graph
G
in

11
has bounded treewidth and bounded pathwidth. In particular, since
G
is a distension of a distension of a fan

, and since

, we have

. (With a more detailed analysis, one can prove

Lemma 12
.

For any graph
G
and any distension
\widehat{G}
of
G
,

\tw(\widehat{G})\leqslant\max\{\tw(G),3\}\quad\text{and}\quad\pw(\widehat{G})\leqslant\pw(G)+2.

Proof.

We first prove the treewidth bound. Consider a tree-decomposition
(B_{x}:x\in V(T))
of
G
with width
\tw(G)
. Apply the following operation for each edge
vw\in E(G)
. Let
x_{0}\in V(T)
such that
v,w\in B_{x_{0}}
. Say
P_{vw}=(u_{1},\dots,u_{t})
is the path complete to
\{v,w\}
in
\widehat{G}
. Add new vertices
x_{1},\dots,x_{t-1}
and edges
x_{0}x_{1},x_{1}x_{2},\dots,x_{t-2}x_{t-1}
to
T
. Let
B_{x_{i}}:=\{v,w,u_{i},u_{i+1}\}
for each
i\in\{1,\dots,t-1\}
. We obtain a tree-decomposition of
\widehat{G}
, in which every new bag has size 4. Thus
\tw(\widehat{G})\leqslant\max\{\tw(G),3\}
.

We now prove the pathwidth bound. Let
(B_{1},\dots,B_{m})
be a path-decomposition of
G
with width
\pw(G)
. By duplicating bags, we may assume there is an injection
f:E(G)\to\{1,\dots,m\}
such that
v,w\in B_{f(vw)}
for each edge
vw\in E(G)
. For each edge
vw\in E(G)
, if
i:=f(vw)
and
P_{vw}=(u_{1},\dots,u_{t})
as above, then insert the sequence of bags
B_{i}\cup\{u_{1},u_{2}\},B_{i}\cup\{u_{2},u_{3}\},\dots,B_{i}\cup\{u_{t-1},u_{t}\}
between
B_{i}
and
B_{i+1}
.
Since
f
is an injection,
we obtain a path-decomposition of
\widehat{G}
, in which each bag has size at most two more than the original bag. Thus
\pw(\widehat{G})\leqslant\pw(G)+2
.
∎
