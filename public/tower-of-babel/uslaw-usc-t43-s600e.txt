43 U.S.C. § 600e — Authorization of appropriations for public recreation facilities
Citation: us:usc:43:600e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section600e
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter XVII (Legislation Applicable To Particular Projects Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such amounts, but not more than $1,100,000, as may be necessary for the investigation, preparation of plans, construction and acquisition of lands authorized in section 600d of this title.
