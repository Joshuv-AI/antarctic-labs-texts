2 U.S.C. § 4521 — Mode of payment
Citation: us:usc:2:4521
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4521
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 45 (Congressional Pay And Benefits); Subchapter I (General); Part B (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The compensation of Members and Delegates shall be passed as public accounts, and paid out of the public Treasury.
