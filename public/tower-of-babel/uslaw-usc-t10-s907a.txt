10 U.S.C. § 907a — Art. 107a. Parole violation
Citation: us:usc:10:907a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section907a
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47 (Uniform Code Of Military Justice); Subchapter X (Punitive Articles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person subject to this chapter—
(1)
who, having been a prisoner as the result of a court-martial conviction or other criminal proceeding, is on parole with conditions; and
(2)
who violates the conditions of parole;
shall be punished as a court-martial may direct.
