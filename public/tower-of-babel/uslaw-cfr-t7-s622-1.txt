7 C.F.R. § 622.1 — Purpose.

Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 622 (Watershed Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the general policies for planning and carrying out watershed projects under Pub. L. 83-566, 68 Stat. 666 (16 U.S.C. 1001 et seq.) and flood prevention projects under Pub. L. 78-534, 58 Stat. 889 (33 U.S.C. 701b-1).
