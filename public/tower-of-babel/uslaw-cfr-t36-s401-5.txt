36 C.F.R. § 401.5 — Control and supervision of materials, design, and building.
Citation: us:cfr:36:401.5
Creator: American Battle Monuments Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-401.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 401 (Monuments And Memorials). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission controls the design and prescribes regulations for the building of all memorial monuments and buildings commemorating the service of American Armed Forces that are built in a foreign country or political division of the foreign country that authorizes the Commission to carry out those duties and powers.
