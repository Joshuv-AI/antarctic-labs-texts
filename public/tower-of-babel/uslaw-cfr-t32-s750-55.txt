32 C.F.R. § 750.55 — Attorney's fees.
Citation: us:cfr:32:750.55
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-750.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 750 (General Claims Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Attorney's fees not in excess of 20 percent of any settlement may be allowed. Attorney's fees so determined are to be paid out of the amount awarded and not in addition to the award. These fee limitations shall be incorporated in any settlement agreement secured from a claimant.
