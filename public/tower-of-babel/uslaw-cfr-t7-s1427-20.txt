7 C.F.R. § 1427.20 — Handling payments of $9.99 or less and collections not exceeding $24.99.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1427.20
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1427.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1427 (Cotton). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Amounts of $9.99 or less will be paid to the producer only upon request.
(b) Total aggregate deficiencies of $24.99 or less involving the same facts or basis of liability, including interest, may be disregarded unless CCC demands in writing that they be paid.
