Depth and Associated Primes in Group Cohomology
James A. Schafer

Abstract




Introduction

If
R
is a Noetherian local ring and
M
is a finitely generated
R
-module then the depth of
M
is defined to be the length of a maximal
M
-sequence in the maximal ideal,
\mathfrak{m}
, of
R
. Serre demonstrated that

\text{depth}\,M\leq\omega_{M}=\text{ the minimum dimension of an associated prime of }M,

that is the minimum dimension of a prime ideal equal to the annihilator of an element of
M
. This result was easily translated to
*
-local
k
-algebras
A
, i.e., graded
k
-algebras with a unique homogeneous maximal ideal. There are easy examples of strict inequality. After many calculations of the cohomology rings of finite groups, Carlson
[
4
]
, asked whether the above inequality was true if
A=H^{*}(G,k)
where
G
was a finite group and
k
was a field whose charateristic divided the order of
G
. Carlson showed it was true if
\dim H^{*}(G,k)=2
. Duflot
[
5
]
had shown
\text{depth}\,H^{*}(G,k)\geq p
-rank of the center of a Sylow
p
-subgroup of
G
. A result for
p
-groups by Green
[
7
]
and later extended by Kuhn
[
9
]
to all finite groups showed that if the Duflot lower bound was an equality the conjecture was true. In
[
12
]
it is shown that if
\dim H^{*}(G,k)-\text{depth}\,H^{*}(G,k)\leq 1
the conjecture is true. To my knowledge nothing else is known.

It is a fact,
[
10
]
, that
\text{depth}\,H^{*}(C_{G}E,k)\geq\text{depth}\,H^{*}(G,k)
always. The aim of this paper to show that the Conner Conjecture is equivalent to the following: There exists an elementary abelian
p
-subgroup of
G
of rank equal to
\omega_{G}=\omega_{H^{*}(G,k)}
with
\text{depth}\,H^{*}(C_{G}E,k)=\text{depth}\,H^{*}(G,k)
.

It is also true, (
[
2
]
,
[
14
]
,
[
15
]
), that if
P
is an associated prime of
H^{*}(G,k)
, then

P=\mathfrak{p}_{E}=\ker\{\text{res}:H^{*}(G,k)\to H^{*}(E,k)/\text{rad}\}

for some elementary abelian
p
-subgroup of
G
. We also show that if
\mathfrak{p}_{E}=\text{ann}\,y
and
\dim\mathfrak{p}_{E}=\omega_{G}
then
\mathfrak{P}_{E}=\ker\{\text{res}:H^{*}(C_{G}E,k)\to H^{*}(E,k)/\text{rad}\}
is an associated prime of
H^{*}(C_{G}E,k)
of dimension
\omega_{G}=\omega_{C_{G}E}
and in fact
\mathfrak{P}_{E}=\text{rad}(\text{ann}\,(\text{res}\,y))
.

Theorem 0.1
.

Let

be a monomorphism of rings and
P\in\text{Ass}\,\,R
, then there exists

with
f^{-1}Q=P
.

Proof.

Let

be a minimal primary decomposition of
(0)\subseteq S
with primes
P_{i}=\text{rad}\,Q_{i}
. Then
\text{Ass}\,\,S=\{P_{i}\}
. Since the contraction of a prmary ideal is primary and the contraction of the radical of an ideal is the radical of the contraction,
(0)=f^{-1}(0)=\bigcap f^{-1}Q_{i}
is a primary decompositon of
(0)\subseteq R
with primes
\{f^{-1}P_{i}\}
. Since any primary decompostion of an ideal can be refined to a minimal primary decomposition,
\text{Ass}\,\,R\subseteq\{f^{-1}P_{i}\}
.
∎

Remark 1

This does not mean that if
