49 C.F.R. § 196.109 — What must an excavator do if damage to a pipeline from excavation activity causes a leak where product is released from the pipeline?
Citation: us:cfr:49:196.109
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-196.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 196 (Protection Of Underground Pipelines From Excavation Activity). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If damage to a pipeline from excavation activity causes the release of any PHMSA regulated natural and other gas or hazardous liquid as defined in part 192, 193, or 195 of this chapter from the pipeline, the excavator must promptly report the release to appropriate emergency response authorities by calling the 911 emergency telephone number.
