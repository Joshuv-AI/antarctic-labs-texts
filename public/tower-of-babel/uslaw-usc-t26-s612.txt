26 U.S.C. § 612 — Basis for cost depletion
Citation: us:usc:26:612
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section612
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter I (Natural Resources); Part I (Deductions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided in this subchapter, the basis on which depletion is to be allowed in respect of any property shall be the adjusted basis provided in section 1011 for the purpose of determining the gain upon the sale or other disposition of such property.
