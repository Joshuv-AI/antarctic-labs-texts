10 C.F.R. § 62.5 — Specific exemptions.
Citation: us:cfr:10:62.5
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-62.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 62 (Criteria And Procedures For Emergency Access To Non-Federal And Regional Low-Level Waste Disposal Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission may, upon application of any interested person or upon its own initiative, grant an exemption from the requirements of the regulations in this part that it determines is authorized by law and will not endanger life or property or the common defense and security and is otherwise in the public interest.
