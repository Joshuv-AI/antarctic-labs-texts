25 C.F.R. § 559.7 — May a tribe submit documents required by this part electronically?
Citation: us:cfr:25:559.7
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-559.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 559 (Facility License Notifications And Submissions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Tribes wishing to submit documents electronically should contact the Commission for guidance on acceptable document formats and means of transmission.
