15 U.S.C. § 15b — Limitation of actions
Citation: us:usc:15:15b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section15b
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 1 (Monopolies And Combinations In Restraint Of Trade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any action to enforce any cause of action under section 15, 15a, or 15c of this title shall be forever barred unless commenced within four years after the cause of action accrued. No cause of action barred under existing law on the effective date of this Act shall be revived by this Act.
