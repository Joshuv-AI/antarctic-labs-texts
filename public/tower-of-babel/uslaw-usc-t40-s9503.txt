40 U.S.C. § 9503 — Record of property
Citation: us:usc:40:9503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section9503
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part D (Public Buildings, Grounds, And Parks In The District Of Columbia); Chapter 95 (Washington Aqueduct And Other Public Works In The District Of Columbia). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Chief of Engineers shall keep in the office a complete record of all land and other property connected with or belonging to the Washington Aqueduct and other public works under the charge of the Chief of Engineers, together with accurate plans and surveys of the public grounds and reservations in the District of Columbia.
