7 U.S.C. § 1509 — Exemption of indemnities from levy
Citation: us:usc:7:1509
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1509
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 36 (Crop Insurance); Subchapter I (Federal Crop Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Claims for indemnities under this subchapter shall not be liable to attachment, levy, garnishment, or any other legal process before payment to the insured or to deduction on account of the indebtedness of the insured or the estate of the insured to the United States except claims of the United States or the Corporation arising under this subchapter.
