28 C.F.R. § 28.11 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:28:28.11
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-28.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 28 (Dna Identification System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DNA analysis means analysis of the deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA) identification information in a bodily sample.
DNA sample means a tissue, fluid, or other bodily sample of an individual on which a DNA analysis can be carried out.
