Double Copy Map for Double Field Theory on an Arbitrary Constant Background Preprint: APS/123-QED
Rasim Yılmaz

Abstract
Double field theory (DFT) can be constructed up to third order as the double copy of Yang-Mills theory. In this construction, one starts with Yang-Mills theory on Minkowski space and the resulting DFT action is also defined on Minkowski space. In this work, I extend this double copy procedure to obtain a DFT action on a constant DFT background starting from two Yang-Mills actions constructed around nontrivial background gauge fields on Minkowski space.



I
Introduction

The Bern-Carrasco-Johansson double copy framework
Bern et al. 2008
;
Bern et al. 2010
is a fascinating and powerful idea that reveals deep connections between gravity and gauge theories at the level of scattering amplitudes. This idea takes its origin from the KLT relations
Kawai et al. 1986
, which state that closed string tree-level amplitudes can be obtained from open string amplitudes. In addition to being at the center of the modern amplitude program (
Dixon 1996
,
Elvang and Huang 2013
), this framework also provides new insights about the relation of gravity and gauge theories. In recent years, the double copy idea has also been extended to the level of classical solutions (see, for example,
Monteiro et al. 2014
;
Luna et al. 2015
;
Carrillo-González et al. 2018
;
Kim et al. 2020
). Usually, to relate classical solutions of gravity to the classical solutions of Yang-Mills theory, one starts with a perturbative metric around a Minkowski space background and relates the perturbation part of the metric to the two copies of Yang-Mills gauge fields. However, in
Adamo et al. 2018
and
Bahjat-Abbas et al. 2017
, the authors show that it is also possible to construct a perturbative gravity theory around a nontrivial background metric by a double copy procedure. There are two approaches that one can use for this construction, which are called the type-A and the type-B double copies by the authors of
Bahjat-Abbas et al. 2017
. In the type-B double copy one starts with the Yang-Mills field around a non-dynamical curved metric and constructs the gravity theory around the same curved metric. In the type-A double copy, one starts with a Yang-Mills field around a non-trivial background gauge field in Minkowski space and obtains a gravity theory around a non-dynamical curved metric. In this procedure, the nontrivial background gauge field creates the non-dynamical curved background metric by the double copy procedure and the dynamical gauge field creates the perturbative part of the metric for the gravity theory.

In recent years, the applications of double copy idea have been extended to many different theories (see
Berman et al. 2021
;
Angus et al. 2021
;
Cho et al. 2022
;
Berman et al. 2022
;
Jonke and Lescano 2025
). There are also some applications of the double copy procedure at the level of actions. One of the interesting results on this subject relates the usual Yang-Mills action on a Minkowski space to the DFT action on a Minkowski space
Diaz-Jaramillo et al. 2022
. DFT is a modern framework in theoretical physics to implement T-duality, a symmetry of string theory, to the field theories defined on a double configuration space
Hull and Zwiebach 2009
;
Hohm et al. 2010a
;
Hohm et al. 2010b
. Double configuration space includes the dual coordinates in addition to the usual coordinates and doubles the number of dimensions to have a manifest
O(D,D)
duality (see
Aldazabal et al. 2013
for a detailed review). The double structure of DFT implies a relation with the double copy idea and there are some works based on this relation,
Lee 2018
;
Cho and Lee 2019
;
Lescano and Roychowdhury 2022
. Recently the idea to construct DFT as double copy of Yang-Mills is also extended to the higher derivative theories (see
Lescano et al. 2023
;
Lescano and Rodríguez 2024a
;
Lescano and Rodríguez 2024b
;
Yılmaz 2024
), and to the heterotic sector of DFT (see
Cho and Lee 2019
;
Lescano and Roychowdhury 2022
;
Yılmaz 2025
). There are also algebraic approaches to relate DFT action to the double copy of Yang-Mills theory (see for example
Bonezzi et al. 2022
;
Bonezzi et al. 2023a
;
Bonezzi et al. 2023b
;
Bonezzi et al. 2024
).

In
Diaz-Jaramillo et al. 2022
, the authors take the Yang-Mills action on a Minkowski space as a starting point and the DFT action they obtain is defined on the Minkowski space. The purpose of this work is to extend this analysis to the DFT action constructed around an arbitrary constant DFT background. In section 2, I review the type-A double copy idea for classical solutions and in section 3, I attempt to apply this idea in the context of DFT. The results of this section imply that a naive application of double copy procedure to a Yang-Mills action constructed around an arbitrary constant gauge field and Minkowski metric cannot give a DFT action around arbitrary constant DFT background. The main result of this work is given in section 4, where I follow a slightly different route from the usual literature on double copy construction of DFT. Although this different route can be interpreted as a slight notation change, it seems to give a more natural way to implement double copy idea for a DFT action on an arbitrary constant background. In that section, I start with two Yang-Mills actions which are perturbatively constructed around nontrivial background gauge fields, on Minkowski space and obtain DFT action defined on an arbitrary constant DFT background by using double copy procedure. The last section is devoted to the discussions of the results, their importance for the current literature, and possible implications for the future works.

II
Type-A Double Copy

Here, I review the type-A double copy idea, following
Bahjat-Abbas et al. 2017
. In the usual literature, the starting point of the double copy for classical solutions is the Kerr-Schild metric. Consider the full metric as,

g_{\mu\nu}=\bar{g}_{\mu\nu}+\kappa h_{\mu\nu},

(1)

where the perturbation part is given by,

h_{\mu\nu}=\frac{\kappa}{2}\phi k_{\mu}k_{\nu},

(2)

with
\phi(x)
is scalar function,
k_{\mu}
is a 4-vector that is null and geodesic both with respect to the background and the full metric
Bahjat-Abbas et al. 2017
. So one has,

g^{\mu\nu}k_{\mu}k_{\nu}=\bar{g}^{\mu\nu}k_{\mu}k_{\nu}=0,\quad k^{\mu}D_{\mu}k_{\nu}=0,

(3)

where
D_{\mu}
is covariant derivative associated with the background metric. Choosing the background metric as Minkowski,
\bar{g}_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{\mu\nu}
, one finds a linear form for the Ricci tensor of the full metric (
1
) as,

R_{\nu}^{\mu}=\frac{\kappa}{2}\partial_{\rho}\left(\partial_{\nu}h^{\mu\rho}+\partial^{\mu}h_{\nu}^{\rho}-\partial^{\rho}h_{\nu}^{\mu}\right).

(4)

For a given scalar field
\phi
and Kerr-Schild vector
k_{\mu}
, a Yang-Mills gauge field can be defined as single copy of (
2
) as
Bahjat-Abbas et al. 2017
,

A_{\mu}^{a}=c^{a}\phi k_{\mu},

(5)

where
c^{a}
is an arbitrary constant color vector. The upshot is that when
g_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{\mu\nu}+\kappa h_{\mu\nu}
with
h_{\mu\nu}
defined as (
2
), the corresponding single copy (
5
) satisfies the linearised Yang-Mills equations, namely,

\partial^{\mu}F_{\mu\nu}^{a}=0.

(6)

This double copy construction is introduced for the Kerr-Schild metric which is defined around a Minkowski background. As shown in
Bahjat-Abbas et al. 2017
, this construction can also be extended to the curved background. There are two possible approaches in
Bahjat-Abbas et al. 2017
, namely, type-A and type-B double copies. Here, I concentrate on type-A double copy. In this approach, a gauge field has a non-trivial background field
\bar{A}^{a}_{\mu}
in Minkowski space, and the corresponding double copy gives a perturbative metric defined on a non-dynamical curved background
\bar{g}_{\mu\nu}
, where
\bar{g}_{\mu\nu}
and
\bar{A}^{a}_{\mu}
are also related by a double copy relation
Bahjat-Abbas et al. 2017
. The idea can be schematically given as
Bahjat-Abbas et al. 2017
,

Figure 1:
Type-A double copy

A simple example can be constructed, by starting with single Kerr-Schild metrics around Minkowski space. One can split up the solutions according to,

\displaystyle g_{\mu\nu}

\displaystyle=\eta_{\mu\nu}+\phi k_{\mu}k_{\nu}

(7)

\displaystyle=\eta_{\mu\nu}+\phi_{1}k_{\mu}k_{\nu}+\phi_{2}k_{\mu}k_{\nu},

where,

\phi_{1}=\xi\phi,\quad\phi_{2}=(1-\xi)\phi,\quad 0\leq\xi\leq 1.

(8)

The corresponding single copy for the gauge field reads,

A_{\mu}^{a}=c^{a}\left[\phi_{1}k_{\mu}+\phi_{2}k_{\mu}\right].

(9)

One can interpret equation (
7
) as,

g_{\mu\nu}=\bar{g}_{\mu\nu}+\tilde{h}_{\mu\nu},

(10)

where,

\bar{g}_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{\mu\nu}+\phi_{1}k_{\mu}k_{\nu}\quad\text{and}\quad\tilde{h}_{\mu\nu}=\phi_{2}k_{\mu}k_{\nu}.

(11)

Similarly, (
9
) can be interpreted as,

A_{\mu}^{a}=\bar{A}_{\mu}^{a}+\tilde{A}_{\mu}^{a},

(12)

where,

\bar{A}_{\mu}^{a}=c^{a}\phi_{1}k_{\mu}\quad\text{and}\quad\tilde{A}_{\mu}^{a}=c^{a}\phi_{2}k_{\mu}.

(13)

Hence, (
11
) and (
13
) imply a double copy relation as

\bar{A}_{\mu}^{a}\rightarrow\bar{g}_{\mu\nu}\quad\text{and}\quad\tilde{A}_{\mu}^{a}\rightarrow\tilde{h}_{\mu\nu}.

(14)

This is an example of type-A curved space double copy for classical solutions. A Yang-Mills gauge field defined around a non-trivial background gauge field is copied to the perturbative metric field with a non-trivial background, where the background fields are also related by double copy
Bahjat-Abbas et al. 2017
.

III
Type-A Double Copy for DFT

Inspired by the results of the previous section, here, I apply the double copy prescription to Yang-Mills action around a nontrivial background gauge field. I start with the standard Yang-Mills action and then expand the gauge field
A_{\mu}{}^{a}
around a constant background
\bar{A}_{\mu}{}^{a}
as,

\mathcal{A}_{\mu}{}^{a}={A}_{\mu}{}^{a}+a_{\mu}{}^{a},

(15)

where
a_{\mu}{}^{a}
is a small perturbation. Then, the field strength tensor becomes

F_{\mu\nu}{}^{a}=\partial_{\mu}a_{\nu}{}^{a}-\partial_{\nu}a_{\mu}{}^{a}+g_{\mathrm{YM}}f^{a}{}_{bc}({A}_{\mu}{}^{b}+a_{\mu}{}^{b})({A}_{\nu}{}^{c}+a_{\nu}{}^{c}).

(16)

By using (
16
), Yang-Mills action reads,

S_{\mathrm{YM}}=-\frac{1}{4}\int d^{D}x\kappa_{ab}(F_{\mu\nu}{}^{a})(F^{\mu\nu b})

(17)

which can be expanded as,

S_{\mathrm{YM}}=S^{0}_{\mathrm{YM}}+S^{(1)}_{\mathrm{YM}}+S^{(2)}_{\mathrm{YM}}+\cdots,

(18)

where
S^{0}_{\mathrm{YM}}
is the action for the background field,
S^{(1)}_{\mathrm{YM}}
is linear in
a_{\mu}{}^{a}
and
S^{(2)}_{\mathrm{YM}}
corresponds to quadratic terms. The quadratic part is of particular interest and is given by,

\displaystyle S^{(2)}_{\mathrm{YM}}=-\frac{1}{4}\int_{x}\bigg(

\displaystyle\kappa_{ab}(\partial_{\mu}a_{\nu}{}^{a}-\partial_{\nu}a_{\mu}{}^{a})(\partial^{\mu}a^{\nu b}-\partial^{\nu}a^{\mu b})

(19)

\displaystyle+4g_{\mathrm{YM}}f_{abc}(\partial_{\mu}a_{\nu}{}^{a}-\partial_{\nu}a_{\mu}{}^{a}){A}^{\mu b}{a}^{\nu c}

\displaystyle+2g^{2}_{\mathrm{YM}}\kappa^{ab}f_{acd}f_{bef}\left({A}_{\mu}{}^{c}{A}_{\nu}{}^{d}a^{\mu e}a^{\nu f}\right.

\displaystyle\left.+{A}_{\mu}{}^{c}a_{\nu}{}^{d}{A}^{\mu e}a^{\nu f}+{A}_{\mu}{}^{c}a_{\nu}{}^{d}a^{\mu e}{A}^{\nu f}\right)\bigg),

where the indices are raised and lowered by Minkowski metric. Now, I apply double copy prescription to (
19
). The corresponding double copy maps are given as
Diaz-Jaramillo et al. 2022
,

\displaystyle a_{\mu}{}^{a}(k)\rightarrow e_{\mu\nu}(k,\bar{k}),

(20)

\displaystyle\kappa_{ab}\rightarrow\frac{1}{2}\bar{\Pi}^{\nu\sigma}(\bar{k}),

\displaystyle f_{abc}\rightarrow\frac{i}{4}\bar{\pi}^{{\nu}{\sigma}{\tau}}\left(\bar{k}_{1},\bar{k}_{2},\bar{k}_{3}\right),

where the cubic projector is defined by

\pi^{\nu\sigma\tau}\left(k_{i}\right)\equiv\eta^{\nu\sigma}\bar{k}_{12}^{\tau}+\eta^{\sigma\tau}\bar{k}_{23}^{\nu}+\eta^{\tau\nu}\bar{k}_{31}^{\sigma}.

(21)

Apart from these, I also define a double copy map for the background gauge field as

{A}_{\mu}{}^{a}(k)\rightarrow E_{\mu\nu}(k,\bar{k}).

(22)

Now, I substitute (
20
) and (
22
) into (
19
). To apply this prescription, it is convenient to pass to the momentum space. Let me divide the action (
19
) into three and start with the first part, namely,

S^{(2)}_{\mathrm{1YM}}=-\frac{1}{4}\int_{x}(\kappa_{ab}(\partial_{\mu}a_{\nu}{}^{a}-\partial_{\nu}a_{\mu}{}^{a})(\partial^{\mu}a^{\nu b}-\partial^{\nu}a^{\mu b})).

(23)

The double copy of this action is familiar from
Diaz-Jaramillo et al. 2022
. Passing to the momentum space, applying the double copy prescription, (
23
) becomes

\displaystyle S_{\mathrm{1DC}}^{(2)}=-\frac{1}{4}\int_{k,\bar{k}}

\displaystyle\left(k^{2}e^{\mu\nu}e_{\mu{\nu}}-k^{\mu}k^{\rho}e_{\mu{\nu}}e_{\rho}{}^{{\nu}}-\bar{k}^{{\nu}}\bar{k}^{{\sigma}}e_{\mu{\nu}}e^{\mu}{}_{{\sigma}}\right.

(24)

\displaystyle\left.-k^{2}\phi^{2}+2\phi k^{\mu}\bar{k}^{\nu}e_{\mu\bar{\nu}}\right),

in momentum space. Passing to the position space, it reads

\displaystyle S_{\mathrm{1DC}}^{(2)}=\frac{1}{4}\int d^{D}x

\displaystyle d^{D}\bar{x}\left(e^{\mu{\nu}}\square e_{\mu{\nu}}+\partial^{\mu}e_{\mu{\nu}}\partial^{\rho}e_{\rho}{}^{{\nu}}\right.

(25)

\displaystyle\left.+\bar{\partial}^{{\nu}}e_{\mu{\nu}}\bar{\partial}^{{\sigma}}e^{\mu}{}_{{\sigma}}-\phi\square\phi+2\phi\partial^{\mu}\bar{\partial}^{{\nu}}e_{\mu{\nu}}\right).

Then, I can continue with the second piece of (
19
), namely,

S^{(2)}_{\mathrm{2YM}}=-\int_{x}g_{\mathrm{YM}}f_{abc}(\partial_{\mu}a_{\rho}{}^{a}-\partial_{\rho}a_{\mu}{}^{a}){A}^{\mu b}a^{\rho c}

(26)

Upon Fourier transforming to momentum space this becomes,

S_{2\mathrm{YM}}^{(2)}=-ig_{\mathrm{YM}}\int_{k}f_{abc}\left(k_{\rho}a_{\mu}^{a}(k)-k_{\mu}a_{\rho}^{a}(k)\right){A}^{\mu b}{a}^{\rho c}(-k).

(27)

Imposing the double copy prescriptions (
20
),(
22
) on (
27
), with
g_{\mathrm{YM}}\rightarrow\frac{1}{2}
, one gets,

\displaystyle S_{2\mathrm{DC}}^{(2)}=\frac{1}{8}

\displaystyle\bigg(\int_{k,\bar{k}}\bar{\pi}^{\nu\sigma\kappa}\eta^{\mu\tau}k^{\rho}e_{\mu\nu}(k,\bar{k})E_{\tau\sigma}e_{\rho\kappa}(-k,-\bar{k})

(28)

\displaystyle-\bar{\pi}^{\nu\sigma\kappa}\eta^{\rho\tau}k^{\mu}e_{\mu\nu}(k,\bar{k})E_{\tau\sigma}e_{\rho\kappa}(-k,-\bar{k})\bigg).

Here, some problems arise. One can see that this proposal is incomplete since
\bar{\pi}^{\nu\sigma\kappa}
depends on three momenta, while in the above terms there are only two
e
fields. Although I tried several ways to continue from this point, I could not find a consistent way to obtain DFT action on an arbitrary constant background following this route. Instead, in the next section, I offer a slightly different way of thinking the double copy procedure in DFT context, and show how it can help us to construct DFT action on an arbitrary constant DFT background.

IV
Type-A Double Copy with an Alternative Formulation

Now I introduce an alternative point of view for the double copy procedure in DFT context. The key observation is that while in the usual double copy procedure for scattering amplitudes one starts with two (same or different) Yang-Mills theories to obtain gravity amplitudes, the double copy procedure for DFT action uses only one Yang-Mills action and maps its color indices to the second set of indices in DFT. In this section, I start with two Yang-Mills actions and introduce double copy maps to obtain the usual DFT action. Consider two Yang-Mills actions given by,

\displaystyle S_{\mathrm{YM1}}

\displaystyle=-\int d^{D}x\left(\frac{1}{4}\kappa_{ab}F^{\mu\rho a}F_{\mu\rho}{}^{b}\right),

(29)

\displaystyle S_{\mathrm{YM2}}

\displaystyle=-\int d^{D}\bar{x}\left(\frac{1}{4}\bar{\kappa}_{ab}\bar{F}^{\nu\sigma a}\bar{F}_{\nu\sigma}{}^{b}\right),

where
\bar{F}_{\nu\sigma}{}^{a}
is the field strength corresponding to gauge field
\bar{A}_{\nu}{}^{a}
. With these two actions, the structure of DFT action takes the form,

S_{DFT}\equiv S_{YM1}\otimes S_{YM2}.

(30)

Firstly, I consider the kinetic part of the DFT action, which corresponds to the double copy of quadratic parts of Yang-Mills actions. Passing to the momentum space, the quadratic parts of the Yang-Mills actions read,

\displaystyle S^{(2)}_{\mathrm{YM1}}

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{2}\int_{k}\kappa_{ab}k^{2}\Pi^{\mu\rho}(k)\mathcal{A}_{\mu}{}^{a}(-k)\mathcal{A}_{\rho}{}^{b}(k),

(31)

\displaystyle S^{(2)}_{\mathrm{YM2}}

\displaystyle=-\frac{1}{2}\int_{\bar{k}}\bar{\kappa}_{ab}\bar{k}^{2}\bar{\Pi}^{\nu\sigma}(\bar{k})\bar{\mathcal{A}}_{\nu}{}^{a}(-\bar{k})\bar{\mathcal{A}}_{\sigma}{}^{b}(\bar{k}).

At this point, I follow a different route from the usual literature on the double copy formulation of DFT following
Bern et al. 2010
. In
Bern et al. 2010
, the authors start with the Yang-Mills action, whose quadratic part is given in Lorenz gauge by,

\mathcal{S}^{(2)}_{YM}=\frac{1}{2}\int d^{4}k_{1}d^{4}k_{2}\delta^{4}\left(k_{1}+k_{2}\right)k_{2}^{2}\left[A^{\mu}\left(k_{1}\right)A_{\mu}\left(k_{2}\right)\right].

(32)

Then they write the double copy action schematically as,

\displaystyle\mathcal{S}_{DC}^{(2)}\sim\frac{1}{4}

\displaystyle\int d^{4}k_{1}d^{4}k_{2}\delta^{4}\left(k_{1}+k_{2}\right)k_{2}^{2}

(33)

\displaystyle\times\left(A^{\mu}\left(k_{1}\right)A_{\mu}\left(k_{2}\right)\tilde{A}^{\sigma}\left(k_{1}\right)\tilde{A}_{\sigma}(k_{2})\right),

and obtain the gravity action by implementing the map,

A_{\mu}(k)\tilde{A}_{\nu}(k)\rightarrow h_{\mu\nu}(k).

(34)

For the cubic terms, the process is similar but now one should also be careful about the color indices. In
Bern et al. 2010
, the authors encode the information of the structure constants in the anti-symmetrization and cyclicity of the interaction terms, and then turn off the color indices. For the construction of DFT action as double copy of Yang-Mills, I follow a similar route.

Starting with the actions (
29
), and using the map

\kappa_{ab}\rightarrow\frac{1}{2}\bar{\Pi}^{\nu\sigma}(\bar{k}),

(35)

the double copy action at quadratic order, in momentum space, can be written schematically as,

S_{\mathrm{DC}}^{(2)}=-\frac{1}{4}\int_{k,\bar{k}}k^{2}\bar{\Pi}^{\nu\sigma}\Pi^{\mu\rho}\mathcal{A}_{\mu}(-k)\mathcal{A}_{\rho}(k)\bar{\mathcal{A}}_{\nu}(-\bar{k})\bar{\mathcal{A}}_{\sigma}(\bar{k}),

(36)

where I turned off the color indices by encoding the color information using (
35
). Now, I define the double copy map for the fields. For the usual Yang-Mills action around Minkowski spacetime, the map can be written as

\mathcal{A}_{\mu}(k)\bar{\mathcal{A}}_{\nu}(\bar{k})\rightarrow e_{\mu\nu}(k,\bar{k}),

(37)

and inserting (
37
) into (
36
) leads to the quadratic part of usual DFT action on Minkowski spacetime. To obtain DFT action on an arbitrary constant DFT background, I consider Yang-Mills action around a nontrivial background gauge field, namely,

\mathcal{A}_{\mu}(k)=A_{\mu}+a_{\mu}(k),

(38)

where
A_{\mu}
is the background gauge field and
a_{\mu}
is the perturbation part. Inserting (
38
) into (
36
) gives,

\displaystyle S_{\mathrm{DC}}^{(2)}=-\frac{1}{4}\int_{k,\bar{k}}k^{2}\bar{\Pi}^{\nu\sigma}\Pi^{\mu\rho}

\displaystyle(A_{\mu}+a_{\mu}(-k))(\bar{A}_{\nu}+\bar{a}_{\nu}(-\bar{k}))

(39)

\displaystyle\times(A_{\rho}+a_{\rho}(k))(A_{\sigma}+a_{\sigma}(\bar{k})).

Now, one needs to determine double copy maps for the fields. A consistent choice for the perturbation part is

a_{\mu}(-k)\bar{a}_{\nu}(-\bar{k})\rightarrow e_{\mu\nu}(-k,-\bar{k}).

(40)

Similarly for the multiplication of two background gauge fields one can choose,

A_{\mu}\bar{A}_{\nu}\rightarrow\tilde{E}_{\mu\nu},

(41)

where,

\tilde{E}_{\mu\nu}\equiv E_{\mu\nu}-\eta_{\mu\nu}.

(42)

For the multiplication of background gauge field and perturbation part of the gauge field, a double copy map schematically can be given as

A_{\mu}\bar{a}_{\nu}(-\bar{k})\rightarrow C\tilde{E}_{\mu}{}^{\alpha}e_{\alpha\nu}\quad\text{and}\quad a_{\mu}(k)\bar{A}_{\nu}\rightarrow C\tilde{E}^{\alpha}{}_{\nu}e_{\mu\alpha},

(43)

where
C
is a constant which will be determined. Inserting (
40
),(
41
) and (
43
) into (
36
) gives

S_{\mathrm{DC}}^{(2)}=-\frac{1}{4}\int_{k,\bar{k}}k^{2}\bar{\Pi}^{\nu\sigma}(\bar{k})\Pi^{\mu\rho}(k)e^{\prime}_{\mu\nu}(-k,-\bar{k})e^{\prime}_{\rho\sigma}(k,\bar{k}),

(44)

where

e^{\prime}_{\mu\nu}\equiv e_{\mu\nu}+\tilde{E}_{\mu\nu}+C(\tilde{E}_{\mu}{}^{\alpha}e_{\alpha\nu}+\tilde{E}^{\alpha}{}_{\nu}e_{\mu\alpha}).

(45)

The action (
44
) represents the quadratic part of the DFT action in Minkowski spacetime for the primed field
e^{\prime}_{\mu\nu}
. The question is if this action is equal to the DFT action for
e_{\mu\nu}
around a constant background
E_{\mu\nu}
for some choice of constant
C
in (
43
), namely,

S^{(2)}(E_{\mu\nu},e_{\mu\nu})\overset{?}{=}S^{(2)}(\eta_{\mu\nu},e^{\prime}_{\mu\nu}).

(46)

An answer to this question is already given in
Hohm et al. 2010a
, where the authors constructed a background independent form of DFT action. In
Hohm et al. 2010a
, the authors showed that,

S\left[E_{ij}-\chi_{ij},e_{ij}+\chi_{ij}-f_{ij}(\chi,e)\right]=S\left[E_{ij},e_{ij}\right]+O\left(e^{3}\right),

(47)

where
\chi_{ij}
is an infinitesimal, constant part of the field
e_{ij}
and,

f_{ij}(\chi,e)=\frac{1}{2}\left(\chi_{i}{}^{k}e_{kj}+\chi^{k}{}_{j}e_{ik}\right)+O\left(e^{2}\right).

(48)

Choosing
\chi_{ij}=E_{ij}-n_{ij}\equiv\tilde{E}_{ij}
, one has

f_{ij}(\chi,e)=\frac{1}{2}\left(\tilde{E}_{i}{}^{k}e_{kj}+\tilde{E}^{k}{}_{j}e_{ik}\right)+O\left(e^{2}\right),

(49)

and (
47
) becomes

S\left[\eta_{ij},e^{\prime}_{ij}\right]=S\left[E_{ij},e_{ij}\right]+O\left(e^{3}\right)

(50)

where

e^{\prime}_{ij}\equiv e_{ij}+\tilde{E}_{ij}-\frac{1}{2}\left(\tilde{E}_{i}{}^{k}e_{kj}+\tilde{E}^{k}{}_{j}e_{ik}\right).

(51)

One can see that (
50
) implies
C=-\frac{1}{2}
in (
45
) so that (
46
) is satisfied.

Now, I continue with the cubic order. Considering the cubic part of the actions (
29
), the double copy action at cubic order, in momentum space, can be written as,

\displaystyle S_{\mathrm{DC}}^{(3)}=

\displaystyle-\frac{ig_{\mathrm{YM}}}{6(2\pi)^{D/2}}\int_{K_{1},K_{2},K_{3}}\delta\left(K_{1}+K_{2}+K_{3}\right)

(52)

\displaystyle\times\bar{\Pi}^{\sigma\tau\kappa}\Pi^{\mu\nu\rho}\mathcal{A}_{1\mu}\mathcal{A}_{2\nu}\mathcal{A}_{3\rho}\bar{\mathcal{A}}_{1\sigma}\bar{\mathcal{A}}_{2\tau}\bar{\mathcal{A}}_{3\kappa}.

Again, defining the gauge fields as (
38
), the cubic part of the double copy action becomes

\displaystyle S_{\mathrm{DC}}^{(3)}=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{48(2\pi)^{D/2}}\int dK_{1}dK_{2}dK_{3}\delta\left(K_{1}+K_{2}+K_{3}\right)

(53)

\displaystyle\times\bar{\Pi}^{\sigma\tau\kappa}(\bar{k}_{1},\bar{k}_{2},\bar{k}_{3})\Pi^{\mu\nu\rho}\left(k_{1},k_{2},k_{3}\right)

\displaystyle\times(A_{\mu 1}+a_{\mu 1})(A_{\nu 2}+a_{\nu 2})(A_{\rho 3}+a_{\rho 3})

\displaystyle\times(\bar{A}_{\sigma 1}+\bar{a}_{\sigma 1})(\bar{A}_{\tau 2}+\bar{a}_{\tau 2})(\bar{A}_{\kappa 3}+\bar{a}_{\kappa 3}),

where the color indices are turned off and their information is encoded in the projectors. Using the maps

\displaystyle A_{\mu 1}\bar{A}_{\sigma 1}\rightarrow E_{\mu\sigma},

(54)

\displaystyle a_{\mu 1}\bar{a}_{\sigma 1}\rightarrow e_{\mu\sigma}(k_{1},\bar{k}_{1}),

\displaystyle A_{\mu 1}\bar{a}_{\sigma 1}\rightarrow-\frac{1}{2}E_{\mu}{}^{\alpha}e_{\alpha\sigma}(k_{1},\bar{k}_{1}),

\displaystyle a_{\mu 1}\bar{A}_{\sigma 1}\rightarrow-\frac{1}{2}E^{\alpha}{}_{\sigma}e_{\mu\alpha},

the action (
53
) becomes,

\displaystyle S_{\mathrm{DC}}^{(3)}=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{48(2\pi)^{D/2}}\int dK_{1}dK_{2}dK_{3}\delta\left(K_{1}+K_{2}+K_{3}\right)

(55)

\displaystyle\times\bar{\Pi}^{\sigma\tau\kappa}(\bar{k}_{1},\bar{k}_{2},\bar{k}_{3})\Pi^{\mu\nu\rho}\left(k_{1},k_{2},k_{3}\right)e^{\prime}_{1\mu\sigma}e^{\prime}_{2\nu\tau}e^{\prime}_{3\rho\kappa},

where prime fields defined as in (
45
) with
C=-\frac{1}{2}
. Then, (
55
) defines the cubic part of the DFT action for prime field. The result of
Hohm et al. 2010a
, namely (
50

V
Discussion and Conclusion

Double copy is a very powerful tool to calculate gravity amplitudes by using Yang-Mills amplitudes. In recent years, there are also many results which show the power of double copy idea for classical solutions. At the level of actions, the literature is much more limited. A promising result in that sense is the formulation of perturbative DFT action up to third order by double copy procedure. In
Diaz-Jaramillo et al. 2022
, the authors construct DFT action on Minkowski background by using double copy of Yang-Mills theory. In this work, I offer a possible way to extend this construction to the arbitrary constant DFT background.

Inspired by the type-A double copy idea of
Bahjat-Abbas et al. 2017
, in section 3, I start with a Yang-Mills action which is constructed around a nontrivial background gauge field, on Minkowski background. The results of this section show that a naive application of double copy procedure on this Yang-Mills action does not yield the DFT action on an arbitrary constant DFT background.

In section 4, I make a slight change on how I think about double copy procedure in DFT context. The motivation of this change comes from the construction of double copy Lagrangian in
Bern et al. 2010
. It is important to explain this change in here. In the usual literature on double copy construction of DFT, one maps one gauge field to the one DFT field, by mapping the color indices to the second spacetime indices. In section 4 of this paper, the product of two gauge fields is mapped to one DFT field, and the color indices are turned off after their information is encoded to the kinematic factors. This can also be interpreted as a slight notation change. However, the results of Section 4 imply that this way of thinking double copy can give us a more natural way to construct DFT action on arbitrary constant DFT background by double copy.

In conclusion, DFT action on an arbitrary constant DFT background can be written as double copy of two Yang-Mills fields, which are constructed around nontrivial background gauge fields, on Minkowski space. In other words, type-A double copy idea can be applied to the construction of DFT action by double copy. This construction of DFT Lagrangian on an arbitrary background by a double copy procedure can have important implications on the double copy formalism at the Lagrangian level. Noting the relation of DFT with closed massless sector of string theory and supergravity, this construction can also have further implications for these theories.
