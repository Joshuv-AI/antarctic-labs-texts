46 C.F.R. § 64.55 — Relief device location.
Citation: us:cfr:46:64.55
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-64.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 64 (Marine Portable Tanks And Cargo Handling Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A pressure relief device must be located on an MPT in a place that—
(a) Is the highest practical point of the tank; and
(b) Allows direct communication with the vapor space.
