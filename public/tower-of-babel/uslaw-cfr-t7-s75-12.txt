7 C.F.R. § 75.12 — When application deemed filed.

Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-75.12

An application shall be deemed filed when received by the Division or the Federal Seed Laboratory.
