7 C.F.R. § 993.72 — Reports of acquisitions, sales, uses, and shipments.
Citation: us:cfr:7:993.72
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-993.72
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 993 (Dried Prunes Produced In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each handler shall file such reports of his acquisitions, sales, uses, and shipments of prunes, as may be requested by the committee.
