52 U.S.C. § 30126 — Prohibition of contributions by minors
Citation: us:usc:52:30126
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section30126
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 301 (Federal Election Campaigns); Subchapter I (Disclosure Of Federal Campaign Funds). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An individual who is 17 years old or younger shall not make a contribution to a candidate or a contribution or donation to a committee of a political party.
