41 C.F.R. § 303-70.501 — -70.501 Continuing relocation expenses when an employee dies in transit from OCONUS to CONUS or after reporting to the new CONUS station.

Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the immediate family chooses to continue the relocation, agencies must continue payment of relocation expenses, provided the immediate family was included on the employee's relocation travel orders. (See § 303-70.502.)
