26 U.S.C. § 5369 — Inventories
Citation: us:usc:26:5369
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5369
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter F (Bonded And Taxpaid Wine Premises); Part II (Operations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each proprietor of premises subject to the provisions of this subchapter shall take and report such inventories as the Secretary may by regulations prescribe.
