25 C.F.R. § 39.604 — Is there a separate weight for school board training at Bureau-operated schools?
Citation: us:cfr:25:39.604
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-39.604
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 39 (The Indian School Equalization Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. There is an ISEP weight not to exceed 1.2 WSUs to cover school board training and expenses at Bureau-operated schools.
