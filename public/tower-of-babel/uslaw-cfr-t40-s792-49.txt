40 C.F.R. § 792.49 — Laboratory operation areas.
Citation: us:cfr:40:792.49
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-792.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 792 (Good Laboratory Practice Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Separate laboratory space and other space shall be provided, as needed, for the performance of the routine and specialized procedures required by studies.
