46 U.S.C. § 51503 — Regional maritime academies
Citation: us:usc:46:51503
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section51503
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Merchant Marine Service); Chapter 515 (State Maritime Academy Support Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Governors of the States cooperating to sponsor a regional maritime academy shall designate in writing one of those States to conduct the affairs of that academy. A regional maritime academy is eligible for assistance from the United States Government on the same basis as a State maritime academy sponsored by a single State.
