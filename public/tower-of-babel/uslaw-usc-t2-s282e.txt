2 U.S.C. § 282e — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:2:282e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section282e
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 9 (Office Of Legislative Counsel); Subchapter II (House Of Representatives); Part II (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated, for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1971, and for each fiscal year thereafter, such sums as may be necessary to carry out this subchapter and to increase the efficiency of the Office and the quality of the services which it provides.
