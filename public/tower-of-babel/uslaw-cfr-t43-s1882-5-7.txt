43 C.F.R. § 1882.5-7 — -7 Nondiscrimination.
Citation: us:cfr:43:1882.5-7
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1882.5-7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1880 (Financial Assistance, Local Governments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person shall, on the grounds of race, color, religion, national origin or sex be excluded from participation in, be denied the benefits of or be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity funded in whole or part with funds made available under this subpart.
