18 U.S.C. § 2235 — Search warrant procured maliciously
Citation: us:usc:18:2235
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2235
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 109 (Searches And Seizures). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever maliciously and without probable cause procures a search warrant to be issued and executed, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
