Notice of Public Meeting of the Illinois Advisory Committee;

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Liliana Schiller, Support Services 
Specialist, at [email protected] or (202) 770-1856.

FR Document Number 2025-13944 on page 34836, third column, correct the 
meeting time to: 3:30 p.m.-5:00 p.m. CT.
