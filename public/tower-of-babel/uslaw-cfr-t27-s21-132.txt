27 C.F.R. § 21.132 — Toluene.
Citation: us:cfr:27:21.132
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-21.132
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 21 (Formulas For Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Specific Gravity at 15.56°/15.56 °C. 0.80 to 0.90.
(b) Boiling point ( °C). 110.6.
(c) Distillation range ( °C). Not more than 1 percent by volume should distill below 109, and not less than 99 percent by volume below 112.
(d) Odor. Characteristic odor.
