31 U.S.C. § 5155 — Providing engraved plates of portraits of deceased members of Congress
Citation: us:usc:31:5155
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section5155
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 51 (Coins And Currency); Subchapter V (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On conditions the Secretary of the Treasury decides, the Secretary may send an engraved plate of a portrait of a deceased Senator or Representative to an heir or legal representative of such a Senator or Representative.
