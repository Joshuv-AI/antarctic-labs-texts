46 C.F.R. § 172.087 — Cargo loading assumptions.
Citation: us:cfr:46:172.087
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-172.087
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 172 (Special Rules Pertaining To Bulk Cargoes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The calculations required in this subpart must be done for cargo weights and densities up to and including the maximum that is to be endorsed on the Certificate of Inspection in accordance with § 151.04-1(c) of this chapter.
(b) For each condition of loading and operation, each cargo tank must be assumed to have its maximum free surface.
