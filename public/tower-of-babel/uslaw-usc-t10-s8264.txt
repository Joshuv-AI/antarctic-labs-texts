10 U.S.C. § 8264 — Shore patrol duty: payment of expenses
Citation: us:usc:10:8264
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8264
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 831 (Miscellaneous Rights And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An officer, midshipman, or cadet of the naval service who is assigned to shore patrol duty away from his vessel or other duty station may be paid his actual services.
