46 C.F.R. § 154.439 — Tank design.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.439
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.439
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An independent tank type A must meet the deep tank standard of the American Bureau of Shipping published in “Rules for Building and Classing Steel Vessels”, 1981, and must:
(a) Withstand the internal pressure determined under § 154.407;
(b) Withstand loads from tank supports calculated under §§ 154.470 and 154.471; and
(c) Have a corrosion allowance that meets § 154.412.
