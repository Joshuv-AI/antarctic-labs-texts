17 C.F.R. § 243.102 — No effect on antifraud liability.
Citation: us:cfr:17:243.102
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-243.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 243 (Regulation Fd). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No failure to make a public disclosure required solely by § 243.100 shall be deemed to be a violation of Rule 10b-5 (17 CFR 240.10b-5) under the Securities Exchange Act.
