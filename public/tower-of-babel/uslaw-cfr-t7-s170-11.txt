7 C.F.R. § 170.11 — How are farmers and vendors selected for participation in the USDA Farmers Market?
Citation: us:cfr:7:170.11
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-170.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 170 (Usda Farmers Market). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

USDA reviews all applications and selects participants based primarily on the type of farmer or vendor (i.e., fruit, vegetable, herb, baker) and secondly, on the specific types of products to be sold. The selection of the participants is conducted by the market management to ensure a balanced product mix of fruits, vegetables, herbs, value-added products, and baked goods.
