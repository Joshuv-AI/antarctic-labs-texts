27 C.F.R. § 447.54 — Administrative procedures inapplicable.
Citation: us:cfr:27:447.54
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-447.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 447 (Importation Of Arms, Ammunition And Implements Of War). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The functions conferred under section 38, Arms Export Control Act of 1976, as amended, are excluded from the operation of Chapter 5, Title 5, United States Code, with respect to Rule Making and Adjudication, 5 U.S.C. 553 and 554.
