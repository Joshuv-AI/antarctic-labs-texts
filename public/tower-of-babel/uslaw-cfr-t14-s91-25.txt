14 C.F.R. § 91.25 — Aviation Safety Reporting Program: Prohibition against use of reports for enforcement purposes.
Citation: us:cfr:14:91.25
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-91.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 91 (General Operating And Flight Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator of the FAA will not use reports submitted to the National Aeronautics and Space Administration under the Aviation Safety Reporting Program (or information derived therefrom) in any enforcement action except information concerning accidents or criminal offenses which are wholly excluded from the Program.
