40 C.F.R. § 444.17 — New source performance standards (NSPS).
Citation: us:cfr:40:444.17
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-444.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 444 (Waste Combustors Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any new source subject to this subpart must achieve the following performance standards: Standards for TSS, arsenic, cadmium, chromium, copper, lead, mercury, silver, titanium, zinc and pH are the same as the corresponding limitation specified in § 444.13.
