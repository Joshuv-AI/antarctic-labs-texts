46 C.F.R. § 50.10-23 — -23 Marine Safety Center.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 50 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term Marine Safety Center refers to the Commanding Officer, Marine Safety Center, U.S. Coast Guard Stop 7430, 2703 Martin Luther King Jr. Avenue SE, Washington, DC 20593-7430 for mail. Send mail in a printed or electronic format. Information for submitting the documents electronically can be found at https://www.uscg.mil/HQ/MSC.
