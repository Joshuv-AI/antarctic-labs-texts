    Title: Certain Transfers of Domestic Stock or Securities by U.S. 
Persons to Foreign Corporations.
    OMB Control Number: 1545-1478.
    Regulation Number: T.D. 8702.
    Abstract: This regulation relates to certain transfers of stock or 
securities of domestic corporations to foreign corporations pursuant to 
the corporate organization, reorganization, or liquidation provisions 
of the Internal Revenue Code. Under section 367(a), these transfers 
generally result in the recognition of gain unless an applicable 
exception is satisfied. To qualify for the exception provided under 26 
CFR 1.367(a)-3(c), the U.S. target company must comply with applicable 
reporting requirements and certain other conditions. The information 
collected is used by the IRS to determine whether the requirements for 
the exception have been satisfied.
    Current Actions: There is no change to the previously approved 
information collection.
    Type of Review: Extension of a currently approved collection.
    Affected Public: Business or other for-profit organizations.

    Estimated Total Annual Burden Hours: 1,000.
