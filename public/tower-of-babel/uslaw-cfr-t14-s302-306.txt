14 C.F.R. § 302.306 — Dismissal or rejection of incomplete applications.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 302 (Rules Of Practice In Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Dismissal or rejection. The Department may dismiss or reject any application for exemption that does not comply with the requirements of this part.
(b) Additional data. The Department may require the filing of additional data with respect to any application for exemption, answer, or reply.
