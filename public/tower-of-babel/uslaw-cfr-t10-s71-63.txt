10 C.F.R. § 71.63 — Special requirement for plutonium shipments.
Citation: us:cfr:10:71.63
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-71.63
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 71 (Packaging And Transportation Of Radioactive Material). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Shipments containing plutonium must be made with the contents in solid form, if the contents contain greater than 0.74 TBq (20 Ci) of plutonium.
