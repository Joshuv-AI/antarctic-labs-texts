42 C.F.R. § 1008.53 — Affected parties.
Citation: us:cfr:42:1008.53
Creator: Office Of Inspector General-Health Care, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-1008.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 1008 (Advisory Opinions By The Oig). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An advisory opinion issued by the OIG will have no application to any individual or entity that does not join in the request for the opinion. No individual or entity other than the requestor(s) may rely on an advisory opinion.
