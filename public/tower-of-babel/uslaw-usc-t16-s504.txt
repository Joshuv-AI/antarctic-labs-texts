16 U.S.C. § 504 — Purchases of tree seeds, cones, forage plant seed, and nursery stock for national forests
Citation: us:usc:16:504
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section504
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 2 (National Forests); Subchapter I (Establishment And Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The provisions of section 6101 of title 41 shall not apply to any purchase by the Forest Service of forest-tree seed or cones or of forage plant seed when the amount involved does not exceed $10,000, nor to any purchase of forest-tree nursery stock when the amount involved does not exceed $500, whenever, in the discretion of the Secretary of Agriculture, such method is in the public interest.
