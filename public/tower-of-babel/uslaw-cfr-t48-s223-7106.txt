48 C.F.R. § 223.7106 — 223.7106 Contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:223.7106
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-223.7106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 223 (Environment, Sustainable Acquisition, And Material Safety). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Use the basic or the alternate of the clause at 252.223-7006, Prohibition on Storage, Treatment, and Disposal of Toxic or Hazardous Materials, in all solicitations and contracts which require, may require, or permit contractor access to a DoD installation.
(a) Use the basic clause, unless a determination is made under 223.7104(a)(10).
(b) Use the alternate I clause when the Secretary of the military department issues a determination under the exception at 223.7104(a)(10).
