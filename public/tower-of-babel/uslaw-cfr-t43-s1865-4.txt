43 C.F.R. § 1865.4 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:43:1865.4
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1865.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1860 (Conveyances, Disclaimers And Correction Documents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant or claimant adversely affected by a decision of the authorized officer made pursuant to the provisions of this subpart shall have a right of appeal pursuant to 43 CFR part 4.
