16 U.S.C. § 6852 — Interagency data standardization
Citation: us:usc:16:6852
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section6852
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 87A (Modernizing Access To Public Land). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 30 months after April 29, 2022, the Secretaries shall jointly develop and adopt interagency standards to ensure compatibility and interoperability among applicable Federal databases with respect to the collection and dissemination of data—
(1)
relating to recreation sites on Federal land; and
(2)
used to depict locations at which recreation uses are available to the public.
