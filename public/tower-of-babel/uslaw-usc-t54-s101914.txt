54 U.S.C. § 101914 — Term of concession contracts
Citation: us:usc:54:101914
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section101914
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 1019 (Concessions And Commercial Use Authorizations); Subchapter II (Commercial Visitor Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A concession contract entered into pursuant to this subchapter shall generally be awarded for a term of 10 years or less. The Secretary may award a contract for a term of up to 20 years if the Secretary determines that the contract terms and conditions, including the required construction of capital improvements, warrant a longer term.
