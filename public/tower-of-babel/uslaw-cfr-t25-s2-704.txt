25 C.F.R. § 2.704 — How long do I have to file an appeal of a Tribal representative recognition decision?
Citation: us:cfr:25:2.704
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-2.704
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 2 (Appeals From Administrative Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You have 10 days after you receive the Tribal representative recognition decision to file a Notice of Appeal.
