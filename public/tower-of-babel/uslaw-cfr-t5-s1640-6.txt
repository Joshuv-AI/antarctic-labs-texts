5 C.F.R. § 1640.6 — Methods of providing information.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1640.6
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1640.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1640 (Periodic Participant Statements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The TSP or its record keeper will furnish the information described in this part to participants by making it available on the TSP website. A participant can request paper copies of that information by calling the ThriftLine, submitting a request through the TSP website, or by writing to the TSP record keeper.
