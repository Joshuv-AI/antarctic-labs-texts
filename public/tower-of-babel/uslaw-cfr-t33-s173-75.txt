33 C.F.R. § 173.75 — Temporary certificate.
Citation: us:cfr:33:173.75
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-173.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 173 (Vessel Numbering And Casualty And Accident Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A temporary certificate valid for not more than 60 days after it is issued may be issued by an issuing authority pending the issue of a certificate of number. A temporary certificate is not valid after the date that the owner receives the certificate of number from the issuing authority.
