31 C.F.R. § 551.207 — Prohibited importation of charcoal from Somalia.
Citation: us:cfr:31:551.207
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-551.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 551 (Somalia Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The importation into the United States, directly or indirectly, of charcoal from Somalia is prohibited.
(b) The prohibition in paragraph (a) of this section applies except to the extent provided by statutes, or in regulations, orders, directives, or licenses that may be issued pursuant to this part, and notwithstanding any contract entered into or any license or permit granted prior to the effective date.
