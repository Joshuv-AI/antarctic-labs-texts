31 C.F.R. § 351.68 — Are taxpayer identification numbers (TINs) required for registration of book-entry Series EE savings bonds?
Citation: us:cfr:31:351.68
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-351.68
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 351 (Offering Of United States Savings Bonds, Series Ee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The TIN of each person named in the registration is required to purchase a book-entry bond.
