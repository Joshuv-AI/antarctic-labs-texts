42 U.S.C. § 13284 — Cost-sharing
Citation: us:usc:42:13284
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section13284
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 134 (Energy Policy); Subchapter IV (Electric Motor Vehicles); Part A (Electric Motor Vehicle Commercial Demonstration Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Requirement
The Secretary shall require at least 50 percent of the costs directly and specifically related to any project under this part to be from non-Federal sources. Such share may be in the form of cash, personnel, services, equipment, and other resources.
(b) Reduction
The Secretary may reduce the amount of costs required to be provided by non-Federal sources under subsection (a) if the Secretary determines that the reduction is necessary and appropriate—
(1)
considering the technological risks involved in the project; and
(2)
in order to meet the objectives of this part.
