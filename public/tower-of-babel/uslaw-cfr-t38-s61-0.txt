38 C.F.R. § 61.0 — Purpose.

Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 61 (Va Homeless Providers Grant And Per Diem Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements the VA Homeless Providers Grant and Per Diem Program which consists of the following components: capital grants, per diem, special need capital and non-capital grants, and technical assistance grants.
