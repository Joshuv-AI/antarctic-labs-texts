42 U.S.C. § 13601 — Compliance by owners as condition of Federal assistance
Citation: us:usc:42:13601
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section13601
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 135 (Residency And Service Requirements In Federally Assisted Housing); Subchapter I (Standards And Obligations Of Residency In Federally Assisted Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Housing and Urban Development shall require owners of federally assisted housing (as such term is defined in section 13641(2) of this title), as a condition of receiving housing assistance for such housing, to comply with the procedures and requirements established under this subchapter.
