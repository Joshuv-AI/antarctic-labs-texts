43 C.F.R. § 404.60 — Does this rule contain an information collection that requires approval by the Office of Management and Budget (OMB)?
Citation: us:cfr:43:404.60
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-404.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 404 (Reclamation Rural Water Supply Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. This rule does contain an information collection that is approved by OMB, under Control Number 1006-0029. The Paperwork Reduction Act provides that an agency may not conduct or sponsor, and a person is not required to respond to, a collection of information unless it displays a valid OMB control number.
