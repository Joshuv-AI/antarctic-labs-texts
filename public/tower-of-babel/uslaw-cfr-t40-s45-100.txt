40 C.F.R. § 45.100 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:40:45.100
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-45.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 45 (Training Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes the policies and procedures for the award of training assistance by the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA). The provisions of this part supplement EPA's general grant regulations and procedures 2 CFR parts 200 and 1500.
