29 C.F.R. § 403.10 — Publication of reports required by this part.
Citation: us:cfr:29:403.10
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-403.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 403 (Labor Organization Annual Financial Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Inspection and examination of any report or other document filed as required by this part, and the furnishing by the Office of Labor-Management Standards of copies thereof to any person requesting them, shall be governed by part 70 of this title.
