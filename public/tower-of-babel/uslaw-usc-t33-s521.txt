33 U.S.C. § 521 — Regulations and orders
Citation: us:usc:33:521
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section521
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 11 (Bridges Over Navigable Waters); Subchapter II (Alteration Of Bridges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to prescribe such rules and regulations, and to make and issue such orders, as may be necessary or appropriate for carrying out the provisions of this subchapter.
