5 C.F.R. § 2634.502 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:2634.502
Creator: Office Of Government Ethics
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-2634.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 2634 (Executive Branch Financial Disclosure, Qualified Trusts, And Certificates Of Divestiture). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this subpart (unless otherwise indicated), the term “trust restrictions” means the applicable provisions of title I of the Ethics in Government Act of 1978, subpart D of this part, and the trust instrument.
