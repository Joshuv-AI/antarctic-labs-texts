24 C.F.R. § 792.101 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:24:792.101
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Housing And Urban Development (Housing Assistance Programs And Public And Indian Housing Programs)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-792.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 792 (Public Housing Agency Section 8 Fraud Recoveries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to encourage public housing agencies (PHAs) to investigate and pursue instances of tenant and owner fraud and abuse in the operation of the Section 8 housing assistance payments programs.
