45 C.F.R. § 2552.51 — What are the terms of service of a Foster Grandparent?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2552.51
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2552.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2552 (Foster Grandparent Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Foster Grandparent shall serve a minimum of 260 hours annually, or a minimum of 5 hours per week. A Foster Grandparent may serve a maximum of 2080 hours annually, or a maximum of 40 hours per week. Within these limitations, a sponsor may set service policies consistent with local needs.
