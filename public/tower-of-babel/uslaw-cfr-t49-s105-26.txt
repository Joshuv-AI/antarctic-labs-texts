49 C.F.R. § 105.26 — Obtaining records on file with PHMSA.
Citation: us:cfr:49:105.26
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-105.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 105 (Hazardous Materials Program Definitions And General Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To obtain records on file with PHMSA, other than those described in § 105.25, you must file a request with PHMSA under the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) (5 U.S.C. 552). The procedures for filing a FOIA request are contained in 49 CFR part 7.
