40 C.F.R. § 62.15100 — What types of training must I do?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.15100
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.15100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There are two types of required training:
(a) Training of operators of municipal waste combustion units using the EPA or a State-approved training course.
(b) Training of plant personnel using a plant-specific training course.
