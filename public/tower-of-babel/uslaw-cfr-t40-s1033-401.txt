40 C.F.R. § 1033.401 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1033.401
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1033.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1033 (Control Of Emissions From Locomotives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of this subpart are applicable to certificate holders for locomotives subject to the provisions of this part. These requirements may also be applied to other manufacturers/remanufacturers as specified in § 1033.1(d).
