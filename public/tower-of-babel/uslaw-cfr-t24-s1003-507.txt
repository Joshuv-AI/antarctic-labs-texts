24 C.F.R. § 1003.507 — Public access to program records.
Citation: us:cfr:24:1003.507
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-1003.507
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 1003 (Community Development Block Grants For Indian Tribes And Alaska Native Villages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding the provisions of 2 CFR 200.337, grantees shall provide citizens with reasonable access to records regarding the past use of ICDBG funds, consistent with applicable State and tribal laws regarding privacy and obligations of confidentiality.
