41 C.F.R. § 101-25.110-3 — -25.110-3 Tires accompanying new motor vehicles.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-25.110-3
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-25.110-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-25 (25—General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The tire identifications and recordkeeping regulations issued by the Department of Transportation require each motor vehicle manufacturer or his designee to maintain a record of tires on or in each vehicle shipped by him together with the name and address of the first purchaser.
