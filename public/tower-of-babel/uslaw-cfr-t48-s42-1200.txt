48 C.F.R. § 42.1200 — 42.1200 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:42.1200
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-42.1200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 42 (Contract Administration And Audit Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes policies and procedures for—
(a) Recognition of a successor in interest to Government contracts when contractor assets are transferred;
(b) Recognition of a change in a contractor's name; and
(c) Execution of novation agreements and change-of-name agreements by the responsible contracting officer.
