42 C.F.R. § 447.325 — Other inpatient and outpatient facility services: Upper limits of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:42:447.325
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-447.325
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 447 (Payments For Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency may pay the customary charges of the provider but must not pay more than the prevailing charges in the locality for comparable services under comparable circumstances.
