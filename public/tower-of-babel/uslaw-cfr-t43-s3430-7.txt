43 C.F.R. § 3430.7 — Trespass.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3430.7
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3430.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3430 (Noncompetitive Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Mining operations conducted prior to the effective date of a lease shall constitute an act of trespass and be subject to penalties specified by § 9239.5 of this title.
