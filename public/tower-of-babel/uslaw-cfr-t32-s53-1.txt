32 C.F.R. § 53.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:53.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-53.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 53 (Wearing Of The Uniform). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes limitations on wearing of the uniform by members of the Armed Forces, and establishes policy with respect to wearing of the uniform by former members of the Armed Forces.
