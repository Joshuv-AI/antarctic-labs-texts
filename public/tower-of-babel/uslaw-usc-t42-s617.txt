42 U.S.C. § 617 — Limitation on Federal authority
Citation: us:usc:42:617
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section617
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter IV (Grants To States For Aid And Services To Needy Families With Children And For Child-Welfare Services); Part A (Block Grants To States For Temporary Assistance For Needy Families). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No officer or employee of the Federal Government may regulate the conduct of States under this part or enforce any provision of this part, except to the extent expressly provided in this part.
