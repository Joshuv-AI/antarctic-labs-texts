42 U.S.C. § 217a–1 — Advisory committees; prohibition of consideration of political affiliations
Citation: us:usc:42:217a–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section217a–1
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter I (Administration And Miscellaneous Provisions); Part A (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All appointments to advisory committees established to assist in implementing the Public Health Service Act [42 U.S.C. 201 et seq.],1 and the Comprehensive Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism Prevention, Treatment, and Rehabilitation Act of 1970 [42 U.S.C. 4541 et seq.], shall be made without regard to political affiliation.
