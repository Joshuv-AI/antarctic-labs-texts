40 C.F.R. § 413.70 — Applicability: Description of the electroless plating subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:413.70
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-413.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 413 (Electroplating Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart apply to discharges resulting from the electroless plating of a metallic layer on a metallic or nonmetallic substrate.
