12 U.S.C. § 419 — Delivery of notes prior to delivery to banks
Citation: us:usc:12:419
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section419
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 3 (Federal Reserve System); Subchapter XII (Federal Reserve Notes). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When such notes have been prepared, the notes shall be delivered to the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System subject to the order of the Secretary of the Treasury for the delivery of such notes in accordance with this chapter.
