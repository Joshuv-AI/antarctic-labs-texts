46 C.F.R. § 193.05-5 — -5 Fire main system.
Citation: us:cfr:46:193.05-5
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-193.05-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 193 (Fire Protection Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Fire pumps, hydrants, hose, and nozzles shall be installed on all manned vessels.
(b) Except as provided for in § 193.10-10(e), the fire main must be a pressurized or a remotely controlled system.
(c) The arrangements and details of the fire main system shall be as set forth in subpart 193.10.
