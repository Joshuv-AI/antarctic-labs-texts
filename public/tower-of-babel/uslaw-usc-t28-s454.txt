28 U.S.C. § 454 — Practice of law by justices and judges
Citation: us:usc:28:454
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section454
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part I (Organization Of Courts); Chapter 21 (General Provisions Applicable To Courts And Judges). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any justice or judge appointed under the authority of the United States who engages in the practice of law is guilty of a high misdemeanor.
