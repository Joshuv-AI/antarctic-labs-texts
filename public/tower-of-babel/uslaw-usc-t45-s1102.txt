45 U.S.C. § 1102 — Statement of purpose
Citation: us:usc:45:1102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section1102
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 20 (Northeast Rail Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is therefore declared to be the purpose of the Congress in this subtitle to provide for—
(1)
the removal by a date certain of the Federal Government’s obligation to subsidize the freight operations of Conrail;
(2)
transfer of Conrail commuter service responsibilities to one or more entities whose principal purpose is the provision of commuter service; and
(3)
an orderly return of Conrail freight service to the private sector.
