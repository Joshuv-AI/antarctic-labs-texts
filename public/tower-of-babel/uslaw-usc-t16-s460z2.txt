16 U.S.C. § 460z–2 — Inland sector; establishment as buffer sector
Citation: us:usc:16:460z–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section460z–2
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LXXXIV (Oregon Dunes National Recreation Area). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The portion of the recreation area delineated as the “Inland Sector” on the map referenced in section 460z–3 of this title is hereby established as an inland buffer sector in order to promote such management and use of the lands, waters, and other properties within such sector as will best protect the values which contribute to the purposes set forth in section 460z of this title.
