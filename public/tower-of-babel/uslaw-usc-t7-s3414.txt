7 U.S.C. § 3414 — Other programs relating to wheat or wheat food research or nutrition education
Citation: us:usc:7:3414
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section3414
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 65 (Wheat And Wheat Foods Research And Nutrition Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to preempt or interfere with the workings of any other program relating to wheat or wheat foods research or nutrition education organized and operating under the laws of the United States or any State.
