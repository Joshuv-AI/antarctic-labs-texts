5 U.S.C. § 570a — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:5:570a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section570a
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part I (The Agencies Generally); Chapter 5 (Administrative Procedure); Subchapter III (Negotiated Rulemaking Procedure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated such sums as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this subchapter.
