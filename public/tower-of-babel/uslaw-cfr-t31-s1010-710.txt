31 C.F.R. § 1010.710 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:31:1010.710
Creator: Financial Crimes Enforcement Network, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-1010.710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 1010 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart provides that the Director, FinCEN, or his designee, either unilaterally or upon request, may issue administrative rulings interpreting the application of this chapter.
