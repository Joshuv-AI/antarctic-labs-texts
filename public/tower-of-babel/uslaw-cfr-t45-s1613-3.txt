45 C.F.R. § 1613.3 — Prohibition.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1613.3
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1613.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1613 (Restrictions On Legal Assistance With Respect To Criminal Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Corporation funds shall not be used to provide legal assistance with respect to a criminal proceeding, unless authorized by this part.
