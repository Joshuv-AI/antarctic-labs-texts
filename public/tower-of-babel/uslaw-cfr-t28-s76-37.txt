28 C.F.R. § 76.37 — Collection of civil penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:28:76.37
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-76.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 76 (Rules Of Procedure For Assessment Of Civil Penalties For Possession Of Certain Controlled Substances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Collection of any penalty shall be the responsibility of the United States Attorney having jurisdiction over the matter.
(b) The United States Attorney having jurisdiction over the matter may commence a civil action in any appropriate district court of the United States for the purpose of recovering the amount assessed and an amount representing interest at a rate computed in accordance with 28 U.S.C. 1961.
