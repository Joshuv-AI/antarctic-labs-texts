25 C.F.R. § 256.27 — Is my Federal government-assisted house eligible for services?
Citation: us:cfr:25:256.27
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-256.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 256 (Housing Improvement Program (Hip)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. The intention of this program is to assist the neediest of the needy, who have never received services from any other Federal entity.
