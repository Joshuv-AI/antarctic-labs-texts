7 C.F.R. § 1942.21 — Statewide nonmetropolitan median household income.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1942.21
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1942.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1942 (Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Statewide nonmetropolitan median household income means the median household income of the State's nonmetropolitan counties and portions of metropolitan counties outside of cities, towns or places, of 50,000 or more population.
