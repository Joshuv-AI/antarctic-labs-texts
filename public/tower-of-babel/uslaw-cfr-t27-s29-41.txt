27 C.F.R. § 29.41 — Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:27:29.41
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-29.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 29 (Stills And Miscellaneous Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this subpart relate to the manufacture, removal, and use of stills and condensers, and to the notice, registration, and recordkeeping requirements therefor.
