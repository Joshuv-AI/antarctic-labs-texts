34 U.S.C. § 10724 — Application
Citation: us:usc:34:10724
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10724
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXXIX (Prevention, Investigation, And Prosecution Of White Collar Crime). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To be eligible for an award of a grant or cooperative agreement under this subchapter, an entity shall submit to the Director of the Bureau of Justice Assistance an application in such form and manner, and containing such information, as required by the Director of the Bureau of Justice Assistance.
