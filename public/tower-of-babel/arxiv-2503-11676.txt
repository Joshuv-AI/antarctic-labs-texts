The aim of this short note is to prove the following asymptotic formula of

Theorem 1
.

Let
f(n)
be defined as above. Then we have

f(n)=2^{\left(\frac{1}{2}+o(1)\right)\frac{(\log n)^{2}}{\log p\log q}}.

Proof.

Throughout the proof,
n
is supposed to be sufficiently large and
\epsilon
is an arbitrarily small positive number.

We first give the upper bound of
f(n)
. The number of pairs
(\alpha,\beta)\in\mathbb{N}^{2}
such that
p^{\alpha}q^{\beta}\leq N
can be computed as

\displaystyle\sum_{p^{\alpha}q^{\beta}\leq n}1

\displaystyle=\sum_{\alpha\leq\log n/\log p}\sum_{\beta\leq\log(n/p^{\alpha})/\log q}1

\displaystyle=\sum_{\alpha\leq\log n/\log p}\left\{\left\lfloor\frac{\log n-\alpha\log p}{\log q}\right\rfloor+1\right\}

\displaystyle=\frac{(\log n)^{2}}{2\log p\log q}+O(\log n).

(1)

Let
\mathscr{E}=\left\{((\alpha,\beta)\in\mathbb{N}^{2}):p^{\alpha}q^{\beta}\leq n\right\}
. Then by (
Proof.
) we have

|\mathscr{E}|=\frac{(\log n)^{2}}{2\log p\log q}+O(\log n),

from which we obtain

f(n)\leq 2^{|\mathscr{E}|}=2^{\left(\frac{1}{2}+o(1)\right)\frac{(\log n)^{2}}{\log p\log q}}.

For the lower bound of
f(n)
, let
\mathscr{E}_{\epsilon}=\left\{(\alpha,\beta)\in\mathbb{N}^{2}):p^{\alpha}q^{\beta}\leq n^{1-\epsilon}\right\}
. Then, by the same argument as (
Proof.
) we have

\displaystyle|\mathscr{E}_{\epsilon}|=\frac{(1-\epsilon)^{2}(\log n)^{2}}{2\log p\log q}+O(\log n).

(2)

Let
P^{\mathscr{E}_{\epsilon}}=\left\{S:S\subset\mathscr{E}_{\epsilon}\right\}
. Then for any
S\in P^{\mathscr{E}_{\epsilon}}
, by (
2

\displaystyle\sum_{(\alpha,\beta)\in S}p^{\alpha}q^{\beta}\leq n^{1-\epsilon}|S|\leq n^{1-\epsilon}|\mathscr{E}_{\epsilon}|\ll n^{1-\epsilon}(\log n)^{2}<n^{1-\epsilon/2}.

(3)

Now, we need a nice result of Yu

Lemma. Let
p,q>1
be integers with
(p,q)=1

\epsilon>0

cn/(\log n)^{1+\epsilon}.

From (
3

\displaystyle n-\sum_{(\alpha,\beta)\in S}p^{\alpha}q^{\beta}\geq n-n^{1-\epsilon/2}>\frac{1}{2}n

(4)

for any

4

Lemma

\gg n/(\log n)^{2}>n^{1-\epsilon}.

Hence, for any

there corresponds with an expression of
n

f(n)\geq\left|P^{\mathscr{E}_{\epsilon}}\right|=2^{|\mathscr{E}_{\epsilon}|}=2^{\frac{(1-\epsilon)^{2}(\log n)^{2}}{2\log p\log q}+O(\log n)},

from which our theorem clearly follows.
