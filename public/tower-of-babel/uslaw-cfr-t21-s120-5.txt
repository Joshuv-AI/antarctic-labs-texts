21 C.F.R. § 120.5 — Current good manufacturing practice.
Citation: us:cfr:21:120.5
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-120.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 120 (Hazard Analysis And Critical Control Point (Haccp) Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided by § 117.5(c), parts 110 and 117 of this chapter apply in determining whether the facilities, methods, practices, and controls used to process juice are safe, and whether the food has been processed under sanitary conditions.
