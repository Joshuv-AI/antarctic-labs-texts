49 C.F.R. § 845.33 — Investigation to remain open.
Citation: us:cfr:49:845.33
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-845.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 845 (Rules Of Practice In Transportation: Investigative Hearings; Meetings, Reports, And Petitions For Reconsideration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board never officially closes an investigation, but provides for the submission of new and pertinent evidence by any interested person. If the Board finds such evidence is relevant and probative, the evidence shall be made a part of the public docket and, where appropriate, the Board will provide parties an opportunity to examine such evidence and to comment thereon.
