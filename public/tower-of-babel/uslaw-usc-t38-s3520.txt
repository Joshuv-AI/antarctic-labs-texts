38 U.S.C. § 3520 — Educational and vocational counseling
Citation: us:usc:38:3520
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3520
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 35 (Survivors’ And Dependents’ Educational Assistance); Subchapter III (Program Of Education). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may, upon request, arrange for educational or vocational counseling for persons eligible for benefits under this chapter to assist such persons in selecting their educational, vocational, or professional objectives and in developing their programs of education.
