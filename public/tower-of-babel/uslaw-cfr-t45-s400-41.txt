45 C.F.R. § 400.41 — Definitions

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 400 (Refugee Resettlement Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this subpart—
Applicant for asylum means an individual who has applied for, but has not been granted, asylum under section 208 of the Act.
Asylee means an individual who has been granted asylum under section 208 of the Act.
