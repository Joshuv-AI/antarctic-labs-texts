5 C.F.R. § 300.502 — Coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:5:300.502
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-300.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 300 (Employment (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) These regulations apply to the competitive service and to Schedules A and B in the excepted service.
(b) Agencies may not use temporary help services for the Senior Executive Service or for the work of managerial or supervisory positions.
