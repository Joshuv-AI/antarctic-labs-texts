46 U.S.C. § 58105 — Preferences for cargo in which charterer has interest
Citation: us:usc:46:58105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section58105
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part G (Restrictions And Penalties); Chapter 581 (Restrictions And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A contractor receiving an operating-differential subsidy, or a charterer under chapter 575 of this title, may not unjustly discriminate in any manner so as to give preference, directly or indirectly, to cargo in which the contractor or charterer has a direct or indirect ownership, purchase, or vending interest.
