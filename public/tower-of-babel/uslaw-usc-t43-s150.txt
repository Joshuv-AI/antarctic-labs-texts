43 U.S.C. § 150 — Withdrawals of land for Indian reservations prohibited
Citation: us:usc:43:150
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section150
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 6 (Withdrawal From Settlement, Location, Sale, Or Entry). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No public lands of the United States shall be withdrawn by Executive Order, proclamation, or otherwise, for or as an Indian reservation except by act of Congress.
