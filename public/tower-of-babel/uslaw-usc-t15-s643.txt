15 U.S.C. § 643 — Fair charge for use of Government-owned property
Citation: us:usc:15:643
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section643
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 14A (Aid To Small Business). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To the fullest extent the Administration deems practicable, it shall make a fair charge for the use of Government-owned property and make and let contracts on a basis that will result in a recovery of the direct costs incurred by the Administration.
