34 C.F.R. § 606.25 — What priority does the Secretary use in awarding cooperative arrangement grants?
Citation: us:cfr:34:606.25
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-606.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 606 (Developing Hispanic-Serving Institutions Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Among applications for cooperative arrangement grants, the Secretary gives priority to proposed cooperative arrangements that are geographically and economically sound, or will benefit the institutions applying for the grant.
