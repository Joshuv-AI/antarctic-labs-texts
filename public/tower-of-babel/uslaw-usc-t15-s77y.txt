15 U.S.C. § 77y — Jurisdiction of other Government agencies over securities
Citation: us:usc:15:77y
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section77y
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 2A (Securities And Trust Indentures); Subchapter I (Domestic Securities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall relieve any person from submitting to the respective supervisory units of the Government of the United States information, reports, or other documents that may be required by any provision of law.
