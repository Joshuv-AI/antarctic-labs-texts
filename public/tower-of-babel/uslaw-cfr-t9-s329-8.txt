9 C.F.R. § 329.8 — Authority for condemnation or seizure under other provisions of law.
Citation: us:cfr:9:329.8
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-329.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 329 (Detention; Seizure And Condemnation; Criminal Offenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this part relating to seizure, condemnation and disposition of articles or livestock do not derogate from authority for condemnation or seizure conferred by other provisions of the Act, or other laws.
