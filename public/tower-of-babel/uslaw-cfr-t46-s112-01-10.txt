46 C.F.R. § 112.01-10 — -10 Automatic emergency lighting and power system.
Citation: us:cfr:46:112.01-10
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-112.01-10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 112 (Emergency Lighting And Power Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An automatic emergency lighting and power system is one in which a reduction in potential from the ship's service power and lighting plant causes the emergency power source to supply power to the emergency loads.
