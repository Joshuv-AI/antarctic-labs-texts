14 U.S.C. § 2926 — Family child care providers
Citation: us:usc:14:2926
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2926
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 29 (Coast Guard Family Support, Child Care, And Housing); Subchapter II (Coast Guard Child Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
Not less frequently than quarterly, the Commandant shall ensure that each family child care provider is subject to inspection.
(b) Responsibility for Inspections.—
Of the quarterly inspections under subsection (a) each year—
(1)
3 inspections shall be carried out by a representative of the Coast Guard installation served by the family child care provider concerned; and
(2)
1 inspection shall be carried out by a representative of the Coast Guard child development services work-life programs.
