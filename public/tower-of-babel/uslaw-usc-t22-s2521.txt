22 U.S.C. § 2521 — Foreign language proficiency
Citation: us:usc:22:2521
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2521
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 34 (The Peace Corps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No person shall be assigned to duty as a volunteer under this chapter in any foreign country or area unless at the time of such assignment he possesses such reasonable proficiency as his assignment requires in speaking the language of the country or area to which he is assigned.
