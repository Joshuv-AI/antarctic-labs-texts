Correct

Evidence Review

The initial searches identified 1,327 records for Search Strategy 1 (PubMed, n = 441; Ichushi-Web, n = 886), 2,596 records for Search Strategy 2 (PubMed, n = 1,249; Ichushi-Web, n = 1,347), and 2,264 records for Search Strategy 3 (PubMed, n = 853; Ichushi-Web, n = 1,411). The PubMed search strategies are provided in the Supplemental Methods. After removing duplicates, Search Strategies 1, 2, and 3 yielded 1,252, 2,392, and 2,086 records, respectively. Ultimately, 63 unique articles met the eligibility criteria (Supplemental Methods). These articles were distributed across the four themes as follows: 14 for Theme 1, 37 for Theme 2, seven for Theme 3, and 13 for Theme 4, thus reflecting overlaps where certain articles addressed multiple thematic areas. Research activity peaked between 2006 and the mid-2010s, with a relative paucity of recent evidence.
