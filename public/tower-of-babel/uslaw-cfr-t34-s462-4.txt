34 C.F.R. § 462.4 — What are the transition rules for using tests to measure educational gain for the National Reporting System for Adult Education (NRS)?
Citation: us:cfr:34:462.4
Creator: Office Of Career, Technical, And Adult Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-462.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 462 (Measuring Educational Gain In The National Reporting System For Adult Education). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A State or an eligible provider may continue to measure educational gain for the NRS using tests that the Secretary has identified in the most recent notice published in the Federal Register until the Secretary announces through a notice published in the Federal Register a date by which such tests may no longer be used.
