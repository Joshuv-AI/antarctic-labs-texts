12 C.F.R. § 617.7520 — How does the FCA issue a directive and when will it be effective?
Citation: us:cfr:12:617.7520
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-617.7520
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 617 (Borrower Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A distressed loan restructuring directive is effective immediately on receipt by the qualified lender, or on such later date as may be specified by FCA, and will remain effective and enforceable until it is stayed, modified, or terminated by FCA.
