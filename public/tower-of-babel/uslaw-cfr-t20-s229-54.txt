20 C.F.R. § 229.54 — Reduction for social security benefit paid to employee on another person's earnings record.
Citation: us:cfr:20:229.54
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-229.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 229 (Social Security Overall Minimum Guarantee). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The employee PIA amount under the overall minimum, after any age reduction, is reduced, but not below zero, by the amount of any social security benefit being paid to the employee on another person's earnings record.
