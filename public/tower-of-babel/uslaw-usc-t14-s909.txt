14 U.S.C. § 909 — Small boat station rescue capability
Citation: us:usc:14:909
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section909
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 9 (Administration); Subchapter I (Real And Personal Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall ensure that each Coast Guard small boat station (including a seasonally operated station) maintains, within the area of responsibility for the station, at least 1 vessel that is fully capable of performing offshore rescue operations, taking into consideration prevailing weather, marine conditions, and depositional geologic features such as sand bars.
