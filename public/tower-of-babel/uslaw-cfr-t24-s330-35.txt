24 C.F.R. § 330.35 — Investors.
Citation: us:cfr:24:330.35
Creator: Government National Mortgage Association, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-330.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 330 (Guaranty Of Multiclass Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Association guaranteed multiclass securities may not be suitable investments for all investors. No investor should purchase securities of any class unless the investor understands, and is able to bear, the prepayment, yield, liquidity and market risks associated with the class. The Association assumes no obligation or liability to any person with regard to determining the suitability of such securities for such investor.
