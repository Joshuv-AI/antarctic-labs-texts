25 C.F.R. § 514.8 — Where should fees, quarterly statements, and other communications about fees be sent?
Citation: us:cfr:25:514.8
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-514.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 514 (Fees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Remittances, quarterly statements, and other communications about fees shall be sent to the Commission by the methods provided for in the rates of fees notice published in the Federal Register.
