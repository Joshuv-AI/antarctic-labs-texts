47 C.F.R. § 26.2 — Frequencies.
Citation: us:cfr:47:26.2
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-26.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 26 (Space Launch Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following frequencies are available for assignment on a nationwide, non-exclusive basis for Space Launch Services:
(a) 2025-2110 MHz;
(b) 2200-2290 MHz; and
(c) 2360-2395 MHz.
