39 C.F.R. § 954.21 — Motion for reconsideration.
Citation: us:cfr:39:954.21
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-954.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 954 (Denial, Suspension, Or Revocaton Of Periodicals Mail Privileges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 10 days from the date thereof, or such longer period as may be fixed by the Judicial Officer, either party may file a motion for reconsideration of a final Agency decision.
