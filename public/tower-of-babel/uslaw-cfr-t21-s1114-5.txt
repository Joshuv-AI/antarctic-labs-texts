21 C.F.R. § 1114.5 — Application submission.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1114.5
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1114.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1114 (Premarket Tobacco Product Applications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An applicant may submit a PMTA to demonstrate that a new tobacco product meets the requirements to receive a marketing granted order. A new tobacco product may not be introduced or delivered for introduction into interstate commerce under this part until FDA has issued a marketing granted order for the product.
