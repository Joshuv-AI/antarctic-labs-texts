25 C.F.R. § 256.17 — How long will I have to wait for work on my house?
Citation: us:cfr:25:256.17
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-256.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 256 (Housing Improvement Program (Hip)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

How long it takes to do work on your house depends on:
(a) Your position on the priority list;
(b) Whether funds are available;
(c) The type of work to be done;
(d) The climate and seasonal conditions where your house is located;
(e) The availability of a contractor; and
(f) Other unforeseen factors, such as infrastructure availability.
