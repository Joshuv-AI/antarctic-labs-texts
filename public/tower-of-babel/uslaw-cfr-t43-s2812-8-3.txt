43 C.F.R. § 2812.8-3 — -3 Disposition of property on termination of permit.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2812.8-3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2812.8-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2810 (Tramroads And Logging Roads). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the expiration or other termination of the permittee's rights, in the absence of an agreement to the contrary, the permittee will be allowed 6 months in which to remove or otherwise dispose of all property or improvements, other than the road and usable improvements to the road, placed by him on the right-of-way, but if not removed within this period, all such property and improvements shall become the property of the United States.
