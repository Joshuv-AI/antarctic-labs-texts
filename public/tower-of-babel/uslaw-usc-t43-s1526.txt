43 U.S.C. § 1526 — Water salvage programs
Citation: us:usc:43:1526
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1526
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 32 (Colorado River Basin Project); Subchapter III (Authorized Units; Protection Of Existing Uses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall undertake programs for water salvage and ground water recovery along and adjacent to the main stream of the Colorado River. Such programs shall be consistent with maintenance of a reasonable degree of undistributed habitat for fish and wildlife in the area, as determined by the Secretary.
