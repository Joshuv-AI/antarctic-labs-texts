42 U.S.C. § 252 — Medical examination of aliens
Citation: us:usc:42:252
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section252
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties); Part C (Hospitals, Medical Examinations, And Medical Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Surgeon General shall provide for making, at places within the United States or in other countries, such physical and mental examinations of aliens as are required by the immigration laws, subject to administrative regulations prescribed by the Attorney General and medical regulations prescribed by the Surgeon General with the approval of the Secretary.
