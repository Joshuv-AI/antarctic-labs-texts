9 C.F.R. § 78.6 — Steers and spayed heifers.
Citation: us:cfr:9:78.6
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-78.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 78 (Brucellosis). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Steers and spayed heifers may be moved interstate in accordance with 9 CFR part 86 and without further restriction under this subpart.
