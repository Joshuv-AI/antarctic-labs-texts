33 C.F.R. § 149.340 — What are the requirements for lifesaving equipment that is not required by this subchapter?
Citation: us:cfr:33:149.340
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-149.340
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 149 (Deepwater Ports: Design, Construction, And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each item of lifesaving equipment on a deepwater port that is not required by this subchapter must be approved by the Commandant (CG-5P).
