43 C.F.R. § 3106.74 — Effective date of transfer.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3106.74
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3106.74
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3100 (Oil And Gas Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The signature of the authorized officer on the official form will constitute approval of the assignment of record title or transfer of operating rights (sublease) which will take effect as of the first day of the lease month following the date of filing in the proper BLM office of all documents and statements required by this subpart and an appropriate bond, if one is required.
