18 U.S.C. § 34 — Penalty when death results
Citation: us:usc:18:34
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section34
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 2 (Aircraft And Motor Vehicles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever is convicted of any crime prohibited by this chapter, which has resulted in the death of any person, shall be subject also to the death penalty or to imprisonment for life.
