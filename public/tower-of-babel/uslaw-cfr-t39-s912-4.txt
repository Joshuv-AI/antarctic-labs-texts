39 C.F.R. § 912.4 — Place of filing.
Citation: us:cfr:39:912.4
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-912.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 912 (Procedures To Adjudicate Claims For Personal Injury Or Property Damage Arising Out Of The Operation Of The U.S. Postal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Claims should be filed with the Tort Claims Coordinator for the Postal Service District Office where the accident occurred, but may be filed at any office of the Postal Service, or sent directly to the Chief Counsel, Torts, General Law Service Center, USPS National Tort Center, 1720 Market Street, Room 2400, St. Louis, MO 63155-9948.
