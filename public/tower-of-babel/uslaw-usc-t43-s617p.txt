43 U.S.C. § 617p — Claims of United States; priority
Citation: us:usc:43:617p
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section617p
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12A (Boulder Canyon Project); Subchapter I (Boulder Canyon Project Act). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in title 11, claims of the United States arising out of any contract authorized by this subchapter shall have priority over all others, secured or unsecured.
