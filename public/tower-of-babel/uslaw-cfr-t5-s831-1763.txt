5 C.F.R. § 831.1763 — Lump-sum credit.
Citation: us:cfr:5:831.1763
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-831.1763
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 831 (Retirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an individual performs phased employment, the lump-sum credit will be reduced by any annuity that is paid or accrued during phased employment.
