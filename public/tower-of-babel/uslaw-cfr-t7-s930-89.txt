7 C.F.R. § 930.89 — Personal liability.
Citation: us:cfr:7:930.89
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-930.89
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 930 (Tart Cherries Grown In The States Of Michigan, New York, Pennsylvania, Oregon, Utah, Washington, And Wisconsin). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No member or alternate member of the Board and no employee or agent of the Board shall be held personally responsible, either individually or jointly with others, in any way whatsoever, to any person for errors in judgment, mistakes, or other acts, either of commission or omission, as such member, alternate member, employee, or agent, except for acts of dishonesty, willful misconduct, or gross negligence.
