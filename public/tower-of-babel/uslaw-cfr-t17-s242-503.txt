17 C.F.R. § 242.503 — Certain foreign research reports.
Citation: us:cfr:17:242.503
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-242.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 242 (Regulations M, Sho, Ats, Ac, Nms, Se, And Sbsr, And Customer Margin Requirements For Security Futures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A foreign person, located outside the United States and not associated with a registered broker or dealer, who prepares a research report concerning a foreign security and provides it to a U.S. person in the United States in accordance with the provisions of § 240.15a-6(a)(2) of this chapter shall be exempt from the requirements of this regulation.
