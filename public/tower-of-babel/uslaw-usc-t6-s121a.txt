6 U.S.C. § 121a — Homeland Security Intelligence Program
Citation: us:usc:6:121a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section121a
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 1 (Homeland Security Organization); Subchapter II (Information Analysis); Part A (Information And Analysis; Access To Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established within the Department of Homeland Security a Homeland Security Intelligence Program. The Homeland Security Intelligence Program constitutes the intelligence activities of the Office of Intelligence and Analysis of the Department that serve predominantly departmental missions.
