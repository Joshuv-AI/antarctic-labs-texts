2 C.F.R. § 1108.290 — Personal property.
Citation: us:cfr:2:1108.290
Creator: Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1108.290
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1108 (Definitions Of Terms Used In Subchapters A Through F Of This Chapter). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Personal property means property other than real property. It may be tangible, having physical existence, or intangible, such as copyrights, patents, and securities.
