36 U.S.C. § 153705 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:153705
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section153705
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1537 (National Woman’S Relief Corps, Auxiliary To The Grand Army Of The Republic). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
adopt and amend a constitution and bylaws for the management of its property and the regulation of its affairs;
(2)
adopt and alter a corporate seal;
(3)
choose officers as the corporation requires;
(4)
make contracts;
(5)
acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property as necessary or convenient to carry out the purposes of the corporation; and
(6)
sue and be sued.
