36 C.F.R. § 264.1 — Definitions.

Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 264 (Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The term Insignia means the Official Forest Service Insignia as shown here
(b) The term Chief means the Chief of the Forest Service, U.S. Department of Agriculture, or a person designated to act for the Chief.
