47 C.F.R. § 97.217 — Telemetry.
Citation: us:cfr:47:97.217
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-97.217
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 97 (Amateur Radio Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Telemetry transmitted by an amateur station on or within 50 km of the Earth's surface is not considered to be codes or ciphers intended to obscure the meaning of communications.
