28 C.F.R. § 22.21 — Use of identifiable data.
Citation: us:cfr:28:22.21
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-22.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 22 (Confidentiality Of Identifiable Research And Statistical Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Research or statistical information identifiable to a private person may be used only for research or statistical purposes.
