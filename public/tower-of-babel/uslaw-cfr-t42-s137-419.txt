42 C.F.R. § 137.419 — What authority does the IBIA have under §§ 137.415 through 137.436?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.419
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.419
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The IBIA has the authority:
(a) to conduct a hearing on the record;
(b) to permit the parties to engage in full discovery relevant to any issue raised in the matter;
(c) to issue a recommended decision; and
(d) to take such action as necessary to insure rights specified in § 137.430.
