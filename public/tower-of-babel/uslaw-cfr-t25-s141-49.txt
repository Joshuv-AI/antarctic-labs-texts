25 C.F.R. § 141.49 — Usury prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:25:141.49
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-141.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 141 (Business Practices On The Navajo, Hopi And Zuni Reservations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No reservation business may take or receive money, goods, or other things of value for a loan or forbearance on a debt that exceeds in value the principal plus twenty-four percent (24 percent) per annum finance charge. Any reservation business contracting for, reserving, or receiving directly or indirectly, any greater amount shall forfeit the finance charge.
