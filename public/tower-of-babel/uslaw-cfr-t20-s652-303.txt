20 C.F.R. § 652.303 — How do the requirements of part 603 of this chapter apply to wage records?
Citation: us:cfr:20:652.303
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-652.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 652 (Establishment And Functioning Of State Employment Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All information collected by the State in wage records referred to in § 652.302 is subject to the confidentiality regulations at part 603 of this chapter.
