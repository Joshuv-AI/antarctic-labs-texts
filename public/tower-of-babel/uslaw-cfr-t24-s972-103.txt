24 C.F.R. § 972.103 — Definition of “conversion.”
Citation: us:cfr:24:972.103
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-972.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 972 (Conversion Of Public Housing To Tenant-Based Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For purposes of this subpart, the term “conversion” means the removal of public housing units from the inventory of a PHA, and the provision of tenant-based or project-based assistance for the residents of the public housing units that are being removed. The term “conversion,” as used in this subpart, does not necessarily mean the physical removal of the public housing development.
