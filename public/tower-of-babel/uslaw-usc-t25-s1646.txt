25 U.S.C. § 1646 — Authorization for emergency contract health services
Citation: us:usc:25:1646
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1646
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 18 (Indian Health Care); Subchapter III–A (Access To Health Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

With respect to an elderly or disabled Indian receiving emergency medical care or services from a non-Service provider or in a non-Service facility under the authority of this chapter, the time limitation (as a condition of payment) for notifying the Service of such treatment or admission shall be 30 days.
