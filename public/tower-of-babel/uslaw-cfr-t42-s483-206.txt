42 C.F.R. § 483.206 — Transfers, discharges and relocations subject to appeal.
Citation: us:cfr:42:483.206
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-483.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 483 (Requirements For States And Long Term Care Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) “Facility” means a certified entity, either a Medicare SNF or a Medicaid NF (See § 483.5).
(b) A resident has appeal rights when he or she is transferred from—
(1) A certified bed into a noncertified bed; and
(2) A bed in a certified entity to a bed in an entity which is certified as a different provider.
(c) A resident has no appeal rights when he or she is moved from one bed in the certified entity to another bed in the same certified entity.
