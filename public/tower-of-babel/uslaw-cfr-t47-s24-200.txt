47 C.F.R. § 24.200 — Scope.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-24.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 24 (Personal Communications Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart sets out the regulations governing the licensing and operations of personal communications services authorized in the 1850-1910 and 1930-1990 MHz bands.
