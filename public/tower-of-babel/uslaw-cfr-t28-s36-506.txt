28 C.F.R. § 36.506 — Alternative means of dispute resolution.
Citation: us:cfr:28:36.506
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-36.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 36 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Disability By Public Accommodations And In Commercial Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where appropriate and to the extent authorized by law, the use of alternative means of dispute resolution, including settlement negotiations, conciliation, facilitation, mediation, factfinding, minitrials, and arbitration, is encouraged to resolve disputes arising under the Act and this part.
