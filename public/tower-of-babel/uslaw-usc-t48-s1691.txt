48 U.S.C. § 1691 — Fiscal control and accounting procedures for plan for use of grant
Citation: us:usc:48:1691
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1691
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 14 (Trust Territory Of The Pacific Islands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The plan provided for in section 1689 of this title shall set forth such fiscal control and accounting procedures as may be necessary to assure proper disbursement, repayment, and accounting for such funds.
