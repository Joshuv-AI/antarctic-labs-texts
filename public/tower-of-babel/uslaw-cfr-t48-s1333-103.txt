48 C.F.R. § 1333.103 — 1333.103 Protests to the agency.

Creator: Chapter 13—Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1333 (Protests, Disputes, And Appeals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Insert provision 1352.233-70, Agency Protests, in all DOC solicitations, except these issued by the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office.
(b) All agency protest decisions shall be reviewed by Procurement Counsel before submission to the protester.
