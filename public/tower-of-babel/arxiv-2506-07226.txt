1.
Introduction

Let
\mathbb{H}
be an arbitrary Hilbert space, endowed with the inner product
\left<\cdot,\cdot\right>
and induced norm
\|\cdot\|.
The notation
\mathbb{B}(\mathbb{H})
will be used to denote the
C^{*}
-algebra of all bounded linear operators on
\mathbb{H}
. Upper case letters will be used to denote the element of
\mathbb{B}(\mathbb{H})
. For
S\in\mathbb{B}(\mathbb{H})
, the adjoint operator
S^{*}
is the operator defined by
\left<Sx,y\right>=\left<x,S^{*}y\right>
for
x,y\in\mathbb{H}
, and the operator norm of
S
is defined by
\|S\|=\sup_{\|x\|=1}\|Sx\|
. If an operator
S\in\mathbb{B}(\mathbb{H})
satisfies
\left<Sx,x\right>\geq 0
for all
x\in\mathbb{H}
, it will be a positive operator. In this case, we write
S\geq O
. Here
\left|S\right|
stands for the positive operator
{{\left({{S}^{*}}S\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}
. Related to the operator norm, the numerical radius of
S\in\mathbb{B}(\mathbb{H})
is defined by
\omega(S)=\sup_{\|x\|=1}|\left<Sx,x\right>|
. This latter quantity defines a norm on
\mathbb{B}(\mathbb{H})
that is equivalent to the operator norm, where we have the equivalence

\frac{1}{2}\|S\|\leq\omega(S)\leq\|S\|,S\in\mathbb{B}(\mathbb{H}).

(1.1)

Several numerical radius inequalities that improve upon those in (
1.1
) have been recently established in
[
6
,
15
,
19
,
21
,
22
,
23
]
.

Sharpening the second inequality in (
1.1
), Kittaneh showed that
[
9
]

\omega\left(S\right)\leq\frac{1}{2}\left(\left\|S\right\|+{{\left\|{{S}^{2}}\right\|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right).

(1.2)

Let
r\left(\cdot\right)
denote the spectral radius. It has been shown in
[
4
, Theorem 2.1]
that

\omega\left(S\right)\leq\frac{1}{2}\left(\left\|S\right\|+\sqrt{r\left(\left|S\right|\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)}\right).

(1.3)

Of course (
1.3
) improves (
1.2
) since

\sqrt{r\left(\left|S\right|\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)}\leq{{\left\|\;\left|S\right|\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\;\right\|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}={{\left\|{{S}^{2}}\right\|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}.

A further upper estimate of the numerical radius can be found in
[
7
, Corollary 3.3]
, presented as follows

{{\omega}^{2}}\left(S\right)\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{\left|S\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}\right\|+\frac{1}{2}\omega\left(\left|S\right|\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right).

(1.4)

Let
S\in\mathbb{B}(\mathbb{H})
.
S
is accretive (resp. dissipative) if in its Cartesian decomposition
S=\Re S+i\Im S
,
\Re S=\frac{S+{{S}^{*}}}{2}
(resp.
\Im S=\frac{S-{{S}^{*}}}{2i}
) is positive and
S
is accretive-dissipative if both
\Re T
and
\Im S
are positive.

The first inequality in (
1.1
) has been improved considerably in
[
16
]
. Indeed, it has been shown in
[
16
, Theorem 2.6]
that if
S
is an accretive-dissipative operator, then

\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\left\|S\right\|\leq\omega\left(S\right).

(1.5)

As we proceed in this paper, the following basic facts will be used (see, e.g.,
[
17
, Ineq. (2.3) and Ineq. (2.4)]
):

\left\|\Re S\right\|,\left\|\Im S\right\|\leq\omega\left(S\right),

(1.6)

for any
S\in\mathbb{B}(\mathbb{H})
.

The paper includes two main results, namely Corollary
2.2
and Theorem
2.5
. Our first main result concerns an improvement for (
1.3
), which is as follows:

\omega\left(S\right)\leq\frac{1}{2}\max\left\{\left\|\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|+{{\left({{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\left|S\right|\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|,\left\|\left|S\right|+{{\left({{\left|S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\left|S\right|^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|\right\}.

(1.7)

To achieve (
1.7
), we propose an upper bound for
2\times 2
operator matrices
\omega\left(\left[\begin{matrix}O&S\\
{{T}^{*}}&O\\
\end{matrix}\right]\right)
.

Our second main result is similar to (
1.7
), with the difference that the condition involving accretive-dissipative has been replaced by a weaker condition involving accretive (or dissipative). The derived bound leads to an improvement for the first inequality in (
1.1

S\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathbb{H}\right)
is such that
S

\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\left\|S\right\|\leq\omega\left(S\right).

\left\|A+B\right\|\leq\max\left\{\left\|A+\left|{{B}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{A}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right|\right\|,\left\|B+\left|{{A}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{B}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right|\right\|\right\}.

Lemma 1.2

Let

\left\|A+B\right\|\leq\left\|\;\left|A\right|+\left|B\right|\;\right\|.

It should be interesting to notice that the above lemma is also true when

2.
Results

We begin this section with the following result, which proposes an upper bound for
\omega\left(S\right)
.

Theorem 2.1
.

Let
S\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathbb{H}\right)
. If
f
,
g
are nonnegative continuous functions on
\left[0,\infty\right)
satisfying
f\left(t\right)g\left(t\right)=t\left(t\geq 0\right)
, then

{{\omega}^{2}}\left(S\right)\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\|\Re\left({{f}^{4}}\left(\left|S\right|\right)+{{g}^{4}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)+2{{f}^{2}}\left(\left|S\right|\right){{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)\right)\right\|.

Proof.

Let
x\in\mathbb{H}
be a unit vector. We have

\displaystyle{{\displaystyle\left|\left\langle Sx,x\right\rangle\right|}^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq\left\langle{{f}^{2}}\left(\left|S\right|\right)x,x\right\rangle\left\langle{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)x,x\right\rangle

\displaystyle\leq{{\left(\frac{1}{2}\left(\left\langle{{f}^{2}}\left(\left|S\right|\right)x,x\right\rangle+\left\langle{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)x,x\right\rangle\right)\right)}^{2}}

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}{{\left\langle\left({{f}^{2}}\left(\left|S\right|\right)+{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)\right)x,x\right\rangle}^{2}}

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\langle{{\left({{f}^{2}}\left(\left|S\right|\right)+{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)\right)}^{2}}x,x\right\rangle

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\left\langle\left({{f}^{4}}\left(\left|S\right|\right)+{{g}^{4}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)+{{f}^{2}}\left(\left|S\right|\right){{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)+{{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right){{f}^{2}}\left(\left|S\right|\right)\right)x,x\right\rangle

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\left\langle\left({{f}^{4}}\left(\left|S\right|\right)+{{g}^{4}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)+2\Re\left({{f}^{2}}\left(\left|S\right|\right){{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)\right)\right)x,x\right\rangle

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\left\langle\Re\left({{f}^{4}}\left(\left|S\right|\right)+{{g}^{4}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)+2{{f}^{2}}\left(\left|S\right|\right){{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)\right)x,x\right\rangle

where the initial inequality is derived from Lemma
1.7
, the subsequent inequality results from the arithmetic-geometric mean inequality and the third inequality follows from Lemma
1.8
. Indeed, we have demonstrated that

{{\left|\left\langle Sx,x\right\rangle\right|}^{2}}\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\langle\Re\left({{f}^{4}}\left(\left|S\right|\right)+{{g}^{4}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)+2{{f}^{2}}\left(\left|S\right|\right){{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)\right)x,x\right\rangle

for any unit vector
x\in\mathbb{H}
. By taking the supremum over all unit vectors
x\in\mathbb{H}
, we get

{{\omega}^{2}}\left(S\right)\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\|\Re\left({{f}^{4}}\left(\left|S\right|\right)+{{g}^{4}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)+2{{f}^{2}}\left(\left|S\right|\right){{g}^{2}}\left(\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)\right)\right\|,

as required.
∎

Corollary 2.1
.

Let
S\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathbb{H}\right)
. Then for any
0\leq t\leq 1

{{\omega}^{2}}\left(S\right)\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\|\Re\left({{\left|S\right|}^{4\left(1-t\right)}}+{{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{4t}}+2{{\left|S\right|}^{2\left(1-t\right)}}{{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{2t}}\right)\right\|.

In particular,

{{\omega}^{2}}\left(S\right)\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\|\Re\left({{\left|S\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}+2\left|S\right|\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)\right\|.

(2.1)

Remark 2.1
.

The constant
\frac{1}{4}
is best possible in (
2.1
). Indeed, if we assume that (
2.5
) holds with a constant
c>0
, i.e.,

{{\omega}^{2}}\left(S\right)\leq c\left\|\Re\left({{\left|S\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}+2\left|S\right|\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)\right\|,

then selecting
S
as a normal operator (satisfying
{{S}^{*}}S=S{{S}^{*}}
) and utilizing the property that for normal operator
\omega\left(S\right)=\left\|S\right\|
, we conclude that
1\leq 4c
thereby establishing the optimality of the constant.

Remark 2.2
.

The fact that

\displaystyle{{\omega}^{2}}\left(S\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}\left\|\Re\left({{\left|S\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}+2\left|S\right|\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}\omega\left({{\left|S\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}+2\left|S\right|\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}\omega\left({{\left|S\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}\right)+\frac{1}{2}\omega\left(\left|S\right|\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{4}\left\|{{\left|S\right|}^{2}}+{{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{2}}\right\|+\frac{1}{2}\omega\left(\left|S\right|\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\right)

is apparent. This implies that our result improves upon (
1.4
).

We adopt some strategies from
[
20
, Theorem 2.1]

Theorem 2.2
.

Let
S,T\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathbb{H}\right)
. Then

\omega\left(\left[\begin{matrix}O&S\\
{{T}^{*}}&O\\
\end{matrix}\right]\right)\leq\frac{1}{2}\max\left\{\alpha,\beta\right\},

where

\alpha=\max\left\{\left\|\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|+{{\left({{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\left|{{T}^{*}}\right|{{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|,\left\|\left|S\right|+{{\left({{\left|S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\left|T\right|{{\left|S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|\right\},

and

\beta=\max\left\{\left\|\left|{{T}^{*}}\right|+{{\left({{\left|{{T}^{*}}\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|{{\left|{{T}^{*}}\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|,\left\|\left|T\right|+{{\left({{\left|T\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\left|S\right|{{\left|T\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|\right\}.

Proof.

Applying Lemma
1.1
on the right-hand side of Lemma
1.2
implies the relation

\left\|A+B\right\|\leq\max\left\{\left\|\left|A\right|+\left|{{\left|B\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|A\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right|\right\|,\left\|\left|B\right|+\left|{{\left|A\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|B\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right|\right\|\right\}

(2.2)

that holds for self-adjoint operators
A,B\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathbb{H}\right)
.
Now, let
S,T\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathbb{H}\right)
be a pair of arbitrary operators and let
A=\left[\begin{matrix}O&S\\
{{S}^{*}}&O\\
\end{matrix}\right]
,
B=\left[\begin{matrix}O&T\\
{{T}^{*}}&O\\
\end{matrix}\right]
.
Obviously,
A
and
B
are self-adjoint, so utilizing (
2.2
), we have that

\displaystyle\left\|A+B\right\|

\displaystyle=\left\|\left[\begin{matrix}O&S+T\\
{{S}^{*}}+{{T}^{*}}&O\\
\end{matrix}\right]\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\max\left\{\left\|\left|\left[\begin{matrix}O&S\\
{{S}^{*}}&O\\
\end{matrix}\right]\right|+\left|{{\left|\left[\begin{matrix}O&T\\
{{T}^{*}}&O\\
\end{matrix}\right]\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\left[\begin{matrix}O&S\\
{{S}^{*}}&O\\
\end{matrix}\right]\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right|\right\|,\right.

\displaystyle\left.\left\|\left|\left[\begin{matrix}O&T\\
{{T}^{*}}&O\\
\end{matrix}\right]\right|+\left|{{\left|\left[\begin{matrix}O&S\\
{{S}^{*}}&O\\
\end{matrix}\right]\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\left[\begin{matrix}O&T\\
{{T}^{*}}&O\\
\end{matrix}\right]\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right|\right\|\right\}

\displaystyle=\max\left\{\left\|\left[\begin{matrix}\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|+{{\left({{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\left|{{T}^{*}}\right|{{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}&O\\
O&\left|S\right|+{{\left({{\left|S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\left|T\right|{{\left|S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\\
\end{matrix}\right]\right\|,\right.

\displaystyle\left.\left\|\left[\begin{matrix}\left|{{T}^{*}}\right|+{{\left({{\left|{{T}^{*}}\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|{{\left|{{T}^{*}}\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}&O\\
O&\left|T\right|+{{\left({{\left|T\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\left|S\right|{{\left|T\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\\
\end{matrix}\right]\right\|\right\}

\displaystyle=\max\left\{\alpha,\beta\right\},

where

\alpha=\max\left\{\left\|\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|+{{\left({{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\left|{{T}^{*}}\right|{{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|,\left\|\left|S\right|+{{\left({{\left|S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\left|T\right|{{\left|S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|\right\},

and

\beta=\max\left\{\left\|\left|{{T}^{*}}\right|+{{\left({{\left|{{T}^{*}}\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|{{\left|{{T}^{*}}\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|,\left\|\left|T\right|+{{\left({{\left|T\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\left|S\right|{{\left|T\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|\right\}.

O&|S|\\
\end{matrix}\right]^{2}=\left[\begin{matrix}|S^{*}|^{2}&O\\

(2.3)

valid for

\left\|A+B\right\|=\left\|\left[\begin{matrix}O&S+T\\

\left\|S+T\right\|\leq\max\left\{\alpha,\beta\right\}.

(2.4)

If we replace

.

Let

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\max\left\{\left\|\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|+{{\left({{\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\left|S\right|\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|,\left\|\left|S\right|+{{\left({{\left|S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\left|{{S}^{*}}\right|\left|S\right|^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|\right\}

Theorem 2.4
.

Let
S\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathbb{H}\right)
. Then

\left\|S\right\|\leq\omega\left(S\right)+\sqrt{r\left(\left|\Re S\right|\left|\Im S\right|\right)}.

Proof.

We know that if
T
is a normal operator, then
iT
is also a normal operator. So, from (
2.7
), we can write

\left\|A+iB\right\|\leq\max\left\{\left\|\left|A\right|+\left|{{\left|B\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|A\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right|\right\|,\left\|\left|B\right|+\left|{{\left|A\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|B\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right|\right\|\right\}.

Let
S=A+iB
be the Cartesian decomposition of
S
, where
A=\Re S
and
B=\Im S
. We obtain

\displaystyle\left\|S\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\max\left\{\left\|\left|\Re S\right|+\left|{{\left|\Im S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\Re S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right|\right\|,\left\|\left|\Im S\right|+\left|{{\left|\Re S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\Im S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right|\right\|\right\}

\displaystyle\leq\max\left\{\left\|\;\left|\Re S\right|\;\right\|+\left\|\;\left|{{\left|\Im S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\Re S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right|\;\right\|,\left\|\;\left|\Im S\right|\;\right\|+\left\|\;\left|{{\left|\Re S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\Im S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right|\;\right\|\right\}

\displaystyle=\max\left\{\left\|\Re S\right\|+\left\|{{\left|\Im S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\Re S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|,\left\|\Im S\right\|+\left\|{{\left|\Re S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\Im S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|\right\}

\displaystyle=\max\left\{\left\|\Re S\right\|+\left\|{{\left|\Re S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\Im S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|,\left\|\Im S\right\|+\left\|{{\left|RS\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\Im S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|\right\}

\displaystyle=\max\left\{\left\|\Re S\right\|,\left\|\Im S\right\|\right\}+\left\|{{\left|\Re S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\Im S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|

i.e.,

\left\|S\right\|\leq\max\left\{\left\|\Re S\right\|,\left\|\Im S\right\|\right\}+\left\|{{\left|\Re S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\Im S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|.

1.6
) that

\left\|S\right\|\leq\omega\left(S\right)+\left\|{{\left|\Re S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\Im S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|.

On the other hand, we know that

\left\|{{\left|\Re S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\Im S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|={{\left\|{{\left|\Re S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\Im S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\Im S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left|\Re S\right|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|}^{\frac{1}{2}}}=\sqrt{r\left(\left|\Re S\right|\left|\Im S\right|\right)},

which in turn implies the desired result.

Theorem 2.5
.

Let
S\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathbb{H}\right)
be an accretive operator. Then

\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\left\|S\right\|\leq\omega\left(S\right).

The above inequality is also true when
S
is a dissipative operator.

Proof.

According to the assumption
\Re S\geq O
. If we use the triangle inequality for the operator norm and Lemma
1.4
, we can write

\displaystyle{{\left\|S\right\|}^{2}}

\displaystyle=\left\|S{{S}^{*}}\right\|

\displaystyle=\left\|\left(\Re S+i\Im S\right){{\left(\Re S+i\Im S\right)}^{*}}\right\|

\displaystyle=\left\|\left(\Re S+i\Im S\right)\left(\Re S-i\Im S\right)\right\|

\displaystyle=\left\|{{\left(\Re S\right)}^{2}}+{{\left(\Im S\right)}^{2}}-i\left(\left(\Re S\right)\left(\Im S\right)-\left(\Im S\right)\left(\Re S\right)\right)\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\left\|{{\left(\Re S\right)}^{2}}+{{\left(\Im S\right)}^{2}}\right\|+\left\|\left(\Re S\right)\left(\Im S\right)-\left(\Im S\right)\left(\Re S\right)\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\left\|{{\left(\Re S\right)}^{2}}\right\|+\left\|{{\left(\Im S\right)}^{2}}\right\|+\left\|\left(\Re S\right)\left(\Im S\right)-\left(\Im S\right)\left(\Re S\right)\right\|

\displaystyle\leq{{\left\|\Re S\right\|}^{2}}+{{\left\|\Im S\right\|}^{2}}+\left\|\Re S\right\|\left\|\Im S\right\|

i.e.,

{{\left\|S\right\|}^{2}}\leq{{\left\|\Re S\right\|}^{2}}+{{\left\|\Im S\right\|}^{2}}+\left\|\Re S\right\|\left\|\Im S\right\|.

On the other hand, if we apply (
1.6
), we infer that

{{\left\|\Re S\right\|}^{2}}+{{\left\|\Im S\right\|}^{2}}+\left\|\Re S\right\|\left\|\Im S\right\|\leq 3{{\omega}^{2}}\left(S\right).

Indeed, we have shown that

{{\left\|S\right\|}^{2}}\leq 3{{\omega}^{2}}\left(S\right),

which is equivalent to the desired result.

The other case (when
\Im S\geq O
) can be proven similarly; we delete the details.
∎

We obtain the well-known inequality

\omega\left(ST\right)\leq 4\omega\left(S\right)\omega\left(T\right),\;S,T\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathbb{H}\right)

from (

) and the fact that the operator norm is sub-multiplicative.
We can enhance this approximation under specific conditions, as demonstrated in the final result of this paper.

Corollary 2.4
.

Let
S,T\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathbb{H}\right)
. If

S
and
T
are accretive,

or

S
and
T
are dissipative,

or

S
is accretive (resp. dissipative) and
T
is dissipative (resp. accretive),

then

\omega\left(ST\right)\leq 3\omega\left(S\right)\omega\left(T\right).

It follows from Theorem
2.5
that

\displaystyle\omega\left(ST\right)

\displaystyle\leq\left\|ST\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\left\|S\right\|\left\|T\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{3}{\sqrt{3}}\omega\left(S\right)\cdot\frac{3}{\sqrt{3}}\omega\left(T\right)

\displaystyle=3\omega\left(S\right)\omega\left(T\right),

as required

Remark 2.6

Let the assumptions of Theorem

\displaystyle{{\left\|S\right\|}^{2}}

\displaystyle=\left\|S{{S}^{*}}\right\|

\displaystyle=\left\|\left(\Re S+i\Im S\right)\left(\Re S-i\Im S\right)\right\|

\displaystyle=\left\|{{\left(\Re S\right)}^{2}}+{{\left(\Im S\right)}^{2}}-i\left(\left(\Re S\right)\left(\Im S\right)-\left(\Im S\right)\left(\Re S\right)\right)\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\left\|{{\left(\Re S\right)}^{2}}+{{\left(\Im S\right)}^{2}}\right\|+\left\|\left(\Re S\right)\left(\Im S\right)-\left(\Im S\right)\left(\Re S\right)\right\|

\displaystyle=\left\|{{\left(\frac{S+{{S}^{*}}}{2}\right)}^{2}}+{{\left(\frac{S-{{S}^{*}}}{2i}\right)}^{2}}\right\|+\left\|\left(\Re S\right)\left(\Im S\right)-\left(IS\right)\left(\Re S\right)\right\|

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\left\|S{{S}^{*}}+{{S}^{*}}S\right\|+\left\|\left(\Re S\right)\left(\Im S\right)-\left(\Im S\right)\left(\Re S\right)\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{2}\left\|S{{S}^{*}}+{{S}^{*}}S\right\|+\left\|\Re S\right\|\left\|\Im S\right\|,

The inequality
\left\|{{S}^{2}}\right\|\leq{{\left\|S\right\|}^{2}}
for any bounded linear operator
S\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathbb{H}\right)
is a fundamental result in operator theory. As a consequence, it is important to establish upper bounds for the nonnegative difference
{{\left\|S\right\|}^{2}}-\left\|{{S}^{2}}\right\|
under various assumptions. In the following result, we aim to derive such an inequality.

Theorem 2.6
.

Let
S\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathbb{H}\right)
be an accretive operator. Then

{{\left\|S\right\|}^{2}}-\left\|{{S}^{2}}\right\|\leq 2\left\|\Re S\right\|\left\|\Im S\right\|.

(2.9)

The above inequality is also true when
S
is a dissipative operator.

Proof.

Notice that

\displaystyle\left\|\left(\Re S\right)\left(\Im S\right)-\left(\Im S\right)\left(\Re S\right)\right\|

\displaystyle=\left\|\left(\frac{S+{{S}^{*}}}{2}\right)\left(\frac{S-{{S}^{*}}}{2i}\right)-\left(\frac{S-{{S}^{*}}}{2i}\right)\left(\frac{S+{{S}^{*}}}{2}\right)\right\|

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{2}\left\|{{S}^{*}}S-S{{S}^{*}}\right\|.

Therefore, from Lemma
1.4
, we have

\displaystyle\left\|{{S}^{*}}S-S{{S}^{*}}\right\|

\displaystyle=2\left\|\left(\Re S\right)\left(IS\right)-\left(\Im S\right)\left(\Re S\right)\right\|

\displaystyle\leq 2\left\|\Re S\right\|\left\|\Im S\right\|.

On the other hand, Lemma
1.5
ensures that

\displaystyle{{\left\|S\right\|}^{2}}-\left\|{{S}^{2}}\right\|

\displaystyle={{\left\|S\right\|}^{2}}-\left\|{{\left({{S}^{*}}S\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left(S{{S}^{*}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|

\displaystyle=\max\left\{\left\|{{S}^{*}}S\right\|,\left\|S{{S}^{*}}\right\|\right\}-\left\|{{\left({{S}^{*}}S\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{{\left(S{{S}^{*}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right\|

\displaystyle\leq\left\|{{S}^{*}}S-S{{S}^{*}}\right\|,

due to the fact that
\left\|\;\left|A\right|\left|B\right|\;\right\|=\left\|A{{B}^{*}}\right\|
for any
A,B\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathbb{H}\right)
(see, e.g.,
[
12
, (8)]
).
∎

Remark 2.7
.

It follows from (
2.8
) and (
2.9
) that

{{\left\|S\right\|}^{2}}\leq\left\|\Re S\right\|\left\|\Im S\right\|+\min\left\{\left\|\Re S\right\|\left\|\Im S\right\|+\left\|{{S}^{2}}\right\|,\frac{1}{2}\left\|S{{S}^{*}}+{{S}^{*}}S\right\|\right\}.

Let
{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\cdot\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}
denote any unitarily invariant norm, i.e., a norm with the property that
{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|UAV\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}={\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|A\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}
for all
A
, and for all unitary
U
,
V
.
As an extension of the definition of the numerical radius, it has been defined in
[
1
]
that

{{\omega}_{{\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\cdot\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|}}}\left(A\right)=\underset{\theta\in\mathbb{R}}{\mathop{\sup}}\,{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\Re\left({{e}^{i\theta}}A\right)\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}.

(2.10)

The essential properties and the inequalities associated with this concept have been examined in the same reference.

The following result concerning the unitarily invariant norms may be stated.

Theorem 2.7
.

Let
S\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathbb{H}\right)
.

•

If
S
is an accretive operator, then

{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|S{{S}^{*}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}\leq{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|{{\left(\Re S\right)}^{2}}+{{\left(\Im S\right)}^{2}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}+\left\|\Re S\right\|\;{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\Im S\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}.

(2.11)

•

If
S
is a dissipative operator, then

{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|S{{S}^{*}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}\leq{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|{{\left(\Re S\right)}^{2}}+{{\left(\Im S\right)}^{2}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}+\left\|\Im S\right\|\;{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\Re S\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}.

Proof.

Assume that
S
is accretive. It follows from Lemma
1.6
that

\displaystyle{{{\displaystyle\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|S{{S}^{*}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}

\displaystyle={{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\left(\Re S+i\Im S\right)\left(\Re S-i\Im S\right)\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}

\displaystyle={{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|{{\left(\Re S\right)}^{2}}+{{\left(\Im S\right)}^{2}}-i\left(\left(\Re S\right)\left(\Im S\right)-\left(\Im S\right)\left(\Re S\right)\right)\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}

\displaystyle={{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|{{\left(\Re S\right)}^{2}}+{{\left(\Im S\right)}^{2}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}+{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\left(\Re S\right)\left(\Im S\right)-\left(\Im S\right)\left(\Re S\right)\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}

\displaystyle\leq{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|{{\left(\Re S\right)}^{2}}+{{\left(\Im S\right)}^{2}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}+\left\|\Re S\right\|\;{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\Im S\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}

i.e.,

{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|S{{S}^{*}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}\leq{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|{{\left(\Re S\right)}^{2}}+{{\left(\Im S\right)}^{2}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}+\left\|\Re S\right\|\;{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\Im S\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}},

which completes the inequality in the first case.

Now, assume that
S
is dissipative. If we apply the same method as in the above, we can write

{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|S{{S}^{*}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}\leq{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|{{\left(\Re S\right)}^{2}}+{{\left(\Im S\right)}^{2}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}+\left\|\Im S\right\|\;{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\Re S\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}},

thanks to Lemma
1.6
.
∎

We can conclude the following result as a direct consequence of Theorem
2.7
.

Corollary 2.5
.

Let
S\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathbb{H}\right)
be an accretive dissipative operator. Then

{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|S{{S}^{*}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}\leq{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|{{\left(\Re S\right)}^{2}}+{{\left(\Im S\right)}^{2}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}+\min\left\{\left\|\Re S\right\|\;{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\Im S\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}},\left\|\Im S\right\|\;{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\Re S\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}\right\}.

Theorem
2.7
also enables us to prove the following result.

Theorem 2.8
.

Let
S\in\mathbb{B}\left(\mathbb{H}\right)
be an accretive operator. Then

{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|S{{S}^{*}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}\leq{{\omega}_{{\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\cdot\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|}}}\left(S\right)\left(2{{\omega}_{{\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\cdot\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|}}}\left(S\right)+\omega\left(S\right)\right).

The above inequality is also true when
S
is a dissipative operator.

Proof.

Assume that
S
is accretive. In this case, (
2.11
) holds. It observes from the triangle inequality for the unitarily invariant norm, (
2.10
), and (
1.6
) that

\displaystyle{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|S{{S}^{*}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}

\displaystyle\leq{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|{{\left(\Re S\right)}^{2}}+{{\left(\Im S\right)}^{2}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}+\left\|\Re S\right\|\;{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\Im S\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}

\displaystyle\leq{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|{{\left(\Re S\right)}^{2}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}+{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|{{\left(\Im S\right)}^{2}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}+\left\|\Re S\right\|\;{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\Im S\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}

\displaystyle\leq 2\omega_{{\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\cdot\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|}}^{2}\left(S\right)+{{\omega}_{{\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\cdot\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|}}}\left(S\right)\;\left\|\Re S\right\|

\displaystyle\leq 2\omega_{{\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\cdot\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|}}^{2}\left(S\right)+{{\omega}_{{\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\cdot\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|}}}\left(S\right)\;\omega\left(S\right)

\displaystyle={{\omega}_{{\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\cdot\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|}}}\left(S\right)\left(2{{\omega}_{{\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\cdot\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|}}}\left(S\right)+\omega\left(S\right)\right)

i.e.,

{{{\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|\kern-1.07639pt\left|S{{S}^{*}}\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|\kern-1.07639pt\right|}}}\leq{{\omega}_{{\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\cdot\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|}}}\left(S\right)\left(2{{\omega}_{{\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\kern-0.75346pt\left|\cdot\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|\kern-0.75346pt\right|}}}\left(S\right)+\omega\left(S\right)\right).

The same result also holds when
