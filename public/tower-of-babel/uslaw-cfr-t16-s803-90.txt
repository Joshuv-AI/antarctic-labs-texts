16 C.F.R. § 803.90 — Separability.
Citation: us:cfr:16:803.90
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-803.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 803 (Transmittal Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any provision of the rules in this subchapter (H) (including the Notification and Report Form) or the application of any such provision to any person or circumstances is held invalid, neither the other provisions of the rules nor the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall be affected thereby.
