10 U.S.C. § 1186 — Officer considered for removal: voluntary retirement or discharge
Citation: us:usc:10:1186
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1186
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 60 (Separation Of Regular Officers For Substandard Performance Of Duty Or For Certain Other Reasons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
At any time during proceedings under this chapter with respect to the removal of an officer from active duty, the Secretary of the military department concerned may grant a request by the officer—
(1)
for voluntary retirement, if the officer is qualified for retirement; or
(2)
for discharge in accordance with subsection (b)(2).
(b)
An officer removed from active duty under section 1184 of this title shall—
(1)
if eligible for voluntary retirement under any provision of law on the date of such removal, be retired in the grade and with the retired pay for which he would be eligible if retired under such provision; and
(2)
if ineligible for voluntary retirement under any provision of law on the date of such removal—
(A)
be honorably discharged in the grade then held, in the case of an officer whose case was brought under subsection (a) of section 1181 of this title; or
(B)
be discharged in the grade then held, in the case of an officer whose case was brought under subsection (b) of section 1181 of this title.
(c)
An officer who is discharged under subsection (b)(2) is entitled, if eligible therefor, to separation pay under section 1174(a)(2) of this title.
