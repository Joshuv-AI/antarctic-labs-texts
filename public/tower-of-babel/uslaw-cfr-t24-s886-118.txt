24 C.F.R. § 886.118 — Amount of housing assistance payments in projects receiving other HUD assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:24:886.118
Creator: Office Of The Assistant Secretary For Housing-Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development (Section 8 Housing Assistance Programs, Section 202 Direct Loan Program, Section 202 Supportive Housing For The Elderly Program And Section 811 Supportive Housing For Persons With Disabilities Program)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-886.118
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 886 (Section 8 Housing Assistance Payments Program—Special Allocations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) For any Section 221(d)(3) BMIR, Section 236, or Section 202 project, the Housing Assistance Payment shall be the amount by which the rent payable by the eligible Family under Section 8 is less than the subsidized rent (which subsidy shall not be reduced by reason of any Section 8 assistance).
(b) In no event may any tenant benefit from more than one of the following subsidies: Rent Supplements, Section 236 deep subsidies, Section 23 leasing assistance, and Section 8 housing assistance.
