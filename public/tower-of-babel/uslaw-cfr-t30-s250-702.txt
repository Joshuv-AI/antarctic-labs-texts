30 C.F.R. § 250.702 — May I obtain departures from these requirements?
Citation: us:cfr:30:250.702
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-250.702
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 250 (Oil And Gas And Sulphur Operations In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may apply for a departure from these requirements as described in § 250.142. Your request must include a justification showing why the departure is necessary. You must identify and discuss the departure you are requesting in your APD (see § 250.414(h)) or your APM.
