28 C.F.R. § 51.51 — Purpose of the subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:28:51.51
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-51.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 51 (Procedures For The Administration Of Section 5 Of The Voting Rights Act Of 1965, As Amended). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to inform submitting authorities and other interested parties of the factors that the Attorney General considers relevant and of the standards by which the Attorney General will be guided in making substantive determinations under section 5 and in defending section 5 declaratory judgment actions.
