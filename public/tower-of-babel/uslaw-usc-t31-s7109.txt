31 U.S.C. § 7109 — Appropriations available for joint financing
Citation: us:usc:31:7109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section7109
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 71 (Joint Funding Simplification). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An appropriation available for technical assistance or personnel training under an assistance program is available for technical assistance and training for a project proposed or approved for joint financing involving the program and another assistance program.
