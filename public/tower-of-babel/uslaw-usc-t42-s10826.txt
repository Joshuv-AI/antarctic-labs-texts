Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 114 (Protection And Advocacy For Individuals With Mental Illness); Subchapter I (Protection And Advocacy Systems); Part B (Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Secretary shall carry out this subchapter through the Administrator of the Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration.
(b) Regulations
Not later than 6 months after November 27, 1991, the Secretary shall promulgate final regulations to carry out this subchapter and subchapter III.
