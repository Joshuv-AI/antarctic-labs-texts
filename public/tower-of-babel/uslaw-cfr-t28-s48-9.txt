28 C.F.R. § 48.9 — Extensions of time.
Citation: us:cfr:28:48.9
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-48.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 48 (Newspaper Preservation Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any of the time periods established by these Regulations may be extended for good cause, upon timely application to the Attorney General, or to the administrative law judge if one has been appointed.
