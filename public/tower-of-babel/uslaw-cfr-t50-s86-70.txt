50 C.F.R. § 86.70 — What standards must I follow when constructing a BIG-funded facility?
Citation: us:cfr:50:86.70
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-86.70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 86 (Boating Infrastructure Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must design and build a BIG-funded facility so that each structure meets Federal, State, and local standards.
(b) A Region or a State may require you to have plans reviewed by a subject-matter expert if there are questions as to the safety, structural stability, durability, or other construction concerns for projects that will cost more than $100,000.
