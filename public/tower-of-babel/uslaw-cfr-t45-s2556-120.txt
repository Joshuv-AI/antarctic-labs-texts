45 C.F.R. § 2556.120 — How does a VISTA sponsor ensure the participation of people in the communities to be served?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2556.120
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2556.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2556 (Volunteers In Service To America). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) To the maximum extent practicable, the people of the communities to be served by VISTA members must participate in planning, developing, and implementing programs.
(b) The sponsor must articulate in its project application how it will engage or continue to engage relevant communities in the development and implementation of programs.
