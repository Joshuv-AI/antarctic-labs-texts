49 C.F.R. § 79.7 — Award.
Citation: us:cfr:49:79.7
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-79.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 79 (Medals Of Honor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Secretary of Transportation decides that it is warranted, the Secretary shall award the Medal on behalf of and in the name of the President of the United States.
