30 C.F.R. § 937.762 — Criteria for designating areas as unsuitable for surface coal mining operations.
Citation: us:cfr:30:937.762
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-937.762
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 937 (Oregon). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 762 of this chapter, Criteria for Designating Areas Unsuitable for Surface Coal Mining Operations, shall apply to surface coal mine operations beginning one year after May 28, 1982.
