22 U.S.C. § 2523 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:22:2523
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2523
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 34 (The Peace Corps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application of any provision to any circumstances or persons shall be held invalid, the validity of the remainder of this chapter and the applicability of such provision to other circumstances or persons shall not be affected thereby.
