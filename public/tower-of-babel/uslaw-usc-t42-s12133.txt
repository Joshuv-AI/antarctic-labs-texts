42 U.S.C. § 12133 — Enforcement
Citation: us:usc:42:12133
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section12133
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 126 (Equal Opportunity For Individuals With Disabilities); Subchapter II (Public Services); Part A (Prohibition Against Discrimination And Other Generally Applicable Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The remedies, procedures, and rights set forth in section 794a of title 29 shall be the remedies, procedures, and rights this subchapter provides to any person alleging discrimination on the basis of disability in violation of section 12132 of this title.
