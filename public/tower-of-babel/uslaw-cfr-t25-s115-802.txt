25 C.F.R. § 115.802 — May a tribe make a request to OTFM to receive information about its trust account more frequently?
Citation: us:cfr:25:115.802
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-115.802
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 115 (Trust Funds For Tribes And Individual Indians). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, a tribe may contact OTFM at any time to:
(a) Request information about account transactions and balances;
(b) Make arrangements to access account information electronically; or
(c) Receive a monthly statement.
