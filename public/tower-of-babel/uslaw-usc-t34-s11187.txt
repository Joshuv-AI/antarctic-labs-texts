34 U.S.C. § 11187 — Limitations on use of funds
Citation: us:usc:34:11187
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section11187
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 111 (Juvenile Justice And Delinquency Prevention); Subchapter II (Programs And Offices); Part F (General And Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

None of the funds made available to carry out this subchapter may be used to advocate for, or support, the unsecured release of juveniles who are charged with a violent crime.
