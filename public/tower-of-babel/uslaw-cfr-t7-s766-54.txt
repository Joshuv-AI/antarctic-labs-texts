7 C.F.R. § 766.54 — Borrower application requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:7:766.54
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-766.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 766 (Direct Loan Servicing—Special). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Requests for DSA. (1) A borrower must submit a request for DSA in writing within eight months from the date the natural disaster was designated.
(2) All borrowers must sign the DSA request.
(b) Required financial information. (1) The borrower must submit actual production, income, and expense records for the production cycle in which the disaster occurred unless the Agency already has this information.
(2) The borrower must provide any additional information requested by the Agency.
