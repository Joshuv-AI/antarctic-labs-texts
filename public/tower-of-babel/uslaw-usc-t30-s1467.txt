30 U.S.C. § 1467 — Jurisdiction of courts
Citation: us:usc:30:1467
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1467
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 26 (Deep Seabed Hard Mineral Resources); Subchapter III (Enforcement And Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The district courts of the United States shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any case or controversy arising under the provisions of this chapter. These courts may, at any time—
(1)
enter restraining orders or prohibitions;
(2)
issue warrants, process in rem, or other process;
(3)
prescribe and accept satisfactory bonds or other security; and
(4)
take such other actions as are in the interest of justice.
