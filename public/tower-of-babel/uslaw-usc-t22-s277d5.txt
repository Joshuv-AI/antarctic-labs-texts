22 U.S.C. § 277d–5 — Availability of prior appropriations; restriction to projects agreed to under treaty
Citation: us:usc:22:277d–5
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section277d–5
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 7 (International Bureaus, Congresses, Etc.); Subchapter IV (International Boundary And Water Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Funds heretofore appropriated to the Department of State under the heading “International Boundary and Water Commission, United States and Mexico” shall be available for the purposes of sections 277d–1 to 277d–5 of this title: Provided, That authorizations under said sections shall apply only to projects agreed upon by the two Governments in accordance with the treaty of February 3, 1944.
