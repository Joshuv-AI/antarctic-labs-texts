32 C.F.R. § 1605.11 — Governor.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1605.11
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1605.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1605 (Selective Service System Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Governor is authorized to recommend a person to be appointed by the President as State Director of Selective Service for his State, who shall represent the Governor in all Selective Service matters.
