8 C.F.R. § 214.200 — Scope of this subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:8:214.200
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-214.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 214 (Nonimmigrant Classes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart governs the submission and adjudication of an Application for T Nonimmigrant Status, including a request by a principal applicant on behalf of an eligible family member for derivative status.
