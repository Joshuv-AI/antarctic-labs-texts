24 C.F.R. § 970.33 — Effect on the Operating Fund Program and Capital Fund Program.
Citation: us:cfr:24:970.33
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Public And Indian Housing, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-970.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 970 (Public Housing Program—Demolition Or Disposition Of Public Housing Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of 24 CFR part 990, the Public Housing Operating Fund Program, and 24 CFR part 905, the Public Housing Capital Fund Program, apply.
