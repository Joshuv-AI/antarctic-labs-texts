40 C.F.R. § 62.11 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.11
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions promulgated in this part and the various applications thereof are distinct and severable. If any provision of this part or the application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, such invalidity shall not affect other provisions or application of such provision to other persons or circumstances which can be given effect without the invalid provision or application.
