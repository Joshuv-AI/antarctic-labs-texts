12 C.F.R. § 1282.40 — Failure to comply.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1282.40
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1282.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1282 (Enterprise Housing Goals And Mission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Director determines that an Enterprise has not complied with, or there is a substantial probability that an Enterprise will not comply with, the duty to serve a particular underserved market in a given year and the Director determines that such compliance is or was feasible, the Director will follow the procedures in 12 U.S.C. 4566(b).
