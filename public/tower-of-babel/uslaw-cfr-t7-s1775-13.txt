7 C.F.R. § 1775.13 — Grant agreement.

Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1775 (Technical Assistance Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants selected for funding will complete a grant agreement, RUS Guide 1775-1, which outlines the terms and conditions of the grant award.
