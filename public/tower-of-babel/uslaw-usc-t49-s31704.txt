49 U.S.C. § 31704 — Vehicle registration
Citation: us:usc:49:31704
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section31704
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Commercial); Chapter 317 (Participation In International Registration Plan And International Fuel Tax Agreement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After September 30, 1996, a State that is not participating in the International Registration Plan may not establish, maintain, or enforce a commercial motor vehicle registration law, regulation, or agreement that limits the operation in that State of a commercial motor vehicle that is not registered under the laws of the State, if the vehicle is registered under the laws of a State participating in the Plan.
