16 U.S.C. § 21d — Existing claims, locations, and entries as affected by revised boundaries
Citation: us:usc:16:21d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section21d
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter V (Yellowstone National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing herein contained shall affect any valid existing claim, location, or entry under the land laws of the United States, whether for homestead, mineral right of way, or any other purposes whatsoever, or shall affect the right of any such claimant, locator, or entryman to the full use and enjoyment of his land.
