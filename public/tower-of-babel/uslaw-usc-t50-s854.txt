50 U.S.C. § 854 — Rules, regulations, and forms
Citation: us:usc:50:854
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section854
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 23 (Internal Security); Subchapter V (Registration Of Certain Persons Trained In Foreign Espionage Systems). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General may at any time, make, prescribe, amend, and rescind such rules, regulations and forms as he deems necessary to carry out the provisions of this subchapter.
