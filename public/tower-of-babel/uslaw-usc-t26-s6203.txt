26 U.S.C. § 6203 — Method of assessment
Citation: us:usc:26:6203
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6203
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 63 (Assessment); Subchapter A (In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The assessment shall be made by recording the liability of the taxpayer in the office of the Secretary in accordance with rules or regulations prescribed by the Secretary. Upon request of the taxpayer, the Secretary shall furnish the taxpayer a copy of the record of the assessment.
