12 C.F.R. § 1208.30 — Interest, penalties, and administrative costs.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1208.30
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1208.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1208 (Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where FHFA is the creditor agency, FHFA shall assess interest, penalties, and administrative costs pursuant to 31 U.S.C. 3717 and the FCCS, 31 CFR chapter IX.
