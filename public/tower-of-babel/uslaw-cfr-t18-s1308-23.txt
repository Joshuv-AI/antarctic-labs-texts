18 C.F.R. § 1308.23 — Finality of decisions.
Citation: us:cfr:18:1308.23
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1308.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1308 (Contract Disputes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A decision by a Hearing Officer on an appeal shall be the decision of the Board and shall be final, subject only to amendment under § 1308.37(c), reconsideration under § 1308.38 or appeal pursuant to sections 8(g)(2) and 10(b) of the Act.
