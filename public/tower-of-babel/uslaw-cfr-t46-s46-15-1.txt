46 C.F.R. § 46.15-1 — -1 Procedure for determination of subdivision load line.

Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 46 (Subdivision Load Lines For Passenger Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedure for determining the subdivision load line as well as special construction features of the vessel must be as set forth in subpart 72.01 and parts 170 and 171 of this chapter.
