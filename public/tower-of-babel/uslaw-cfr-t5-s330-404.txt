5 C.F.R. § 330.404 — Displacement of preference eligibles occupying restricted positions in contracting out situations.
Citation: us:cfr:5:330.404
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-330.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 330 (Recruitment, Selection, And Placement (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An individual agency and OPM both have additional responsibilities when the agency decides, in accordance with the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) Circular A-76, to contract out the work of a preference eligible who holds a restricted position. These additional responsibilities as described in §§ 330.405 and 330.406 are applicable if a preference eligible holds a competitive service position (other than in the Government Publishing Office) that is:
(a) A restricted position as designated in 5 U.S.C. 3310 and § 330.401; and
(b) In the competitive service tenure group, as defined in § 351.203 of this chapter.
