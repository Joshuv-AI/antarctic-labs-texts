7 C.F.R. § 4290.240 — Limitations on non-cash capital contributions in Private Capital.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4290.240
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4290.240
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4290 (Rural Business Investment Company (“Rbic”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Non-cash capital contributions to a RBIC or Applicant are included in Private Capital only if they are approved by the Agency and they fall into one of the following categories:
(a) Direct obligations of, or obligations guaranteed as to principal and interest by, the United States having a term of no more than one year.
(b) Services rendered or to be rendered to you, priced at no more than their fair market value.
(c) Other non-cash assets approved by the Agency.
