42 U.S.C. § 10108 — Applicability to transportation
Citation: us:usc:42:10108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10108
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 108 (Nuclear Waste Policy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to affect Federal, State, or local laws pertaining to the transportation of spent nuclear fuel or high-level radioactive waste.
