7 C.F.R. § 1780.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1780.2
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1780.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1780 (Water And Waste Loans And Grants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Provide loan and grant funds for water and waste projects serving the most financially needy communities. Financial assistance should result in reasonable user costs for rural residents, rural businesses, and other rural users.
