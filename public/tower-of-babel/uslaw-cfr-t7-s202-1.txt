7 C.F.R. § 202.1 — Meaning of words.
Citation: us:cfr:7:202.1
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-202.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 202 (Federal Seed Act Administrative Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part, words in the singular form shall be deemed to import the plural, and vice versa, as the case may require.
