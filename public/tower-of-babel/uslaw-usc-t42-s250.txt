42 U.S.C. § 250 — Medical care and treatment of Federal prisoners
Citation: us:usc:42:250
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section250
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6A (Public Health Service); Subchapter II (General Powers And Duties); Part C (Hospitals, Medical Examinations, And Medical Care). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Service shall supervise and furnish medical treatment and other necessary medical, psychiatric, and related technical and scientific services, authorized by section 4005 of title 18, in penal and correctional institutions of the United States.
