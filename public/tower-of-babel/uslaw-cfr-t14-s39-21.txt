14 C.F.R. § 39.21 — Where can I get information about FAA-approved alternative methods of compliance?
Citation: us:cfr:14:39.21
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-39.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 39 (Airworthiness Directives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each airworthiness directive identifies the office responsible for approving alternative methods of compliance. That office can provide information about alternatives it has already approved.
