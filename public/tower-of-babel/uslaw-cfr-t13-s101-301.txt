13 C.F.R. § 101.301 — Who should receive information or allegations of waste, fraud, and abuse?
Citation: us:cfr:13:101.301
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-101.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 101 (Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office of Inspector General should receive all information or allegations of waste, fraud, or abuse regarding SBA programs and operations.
