12 C.F.R. § 741.209 — Management official interlocks.
Citation: us:cfr:12:741.209
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-741.209
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 741 (Requirements For Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any credit union which is insured pursuant to title II of the Act shall adhere to the requirements stated in part 711 of this chapter concerning management official interlocks, issued under the provisions of the Depository Institution Management Interlocks Act (12 U.S.C. 3201 et seq.).
