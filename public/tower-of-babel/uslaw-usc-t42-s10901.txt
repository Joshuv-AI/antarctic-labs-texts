42 U.S.C. § 10901 — Authority of Secretary to make grants
Citation: us:usc:42:10901
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10901
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 115 (Child Development Associate Scholarship Assistance Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to make a grant for any fiscal year to any State receiving a grant under title XX of the Social Security Act [42 U.S.C. 1397 et seq.] for such fiscal year to enable such State to award scholarships to eligible individuals within the State who are candidates for the Child Development Associate credential.
