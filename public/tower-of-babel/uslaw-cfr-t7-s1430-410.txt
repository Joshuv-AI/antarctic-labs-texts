7 C.F.R. § 1430.410 — Effect of failure to pay administrative fees or premiums.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1430.410
Creator: Commodity Credit Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1430.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1430 (Dairy Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A participating dairy operation that fails to pay a required administrative fee or premium payment due upon application to DMC or for a calendar year of coverage:
(1) Remains legally obligated to pay such administrative fee or premium, as applicable; and
(2) Upon such failure to pay the administrative fee when due, loses coverage under DMC for the coverage year; and
(3) Upon such failure to pay the premium fee or receivable when due, a subsequent DMC contract cannot be approved.
(b) CCC may take such actions as necessary to collect unpaid administrative fees and premium payments.
