36 U.S.C. § 220103 — Membership
Citation: us:usc:36:220103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section220103
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 2201 (United Service Organizations, Incorporated). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in this chapter, the rights, privileges, and designation of classes of members are as provided in the bylaws. The membership of the corporation consists of—
(1)
nine individuals designated by the President; and
(2)
representatives of the civilian organizations listed in section 220102(4) of this title, and of the public at large, as provided in the bylaws.
