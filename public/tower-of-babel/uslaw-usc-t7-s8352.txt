7 U.S.C. § 8352 — Authorization of expenditures for the eradication and control of predatory and other wild animals
Citation: us:usc:7:8352
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section8352
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 109A (Control Of Wild Animals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture is authorized to make such expenditures for equipment, supplies, and materials, including the employment of persons and means in the District of Columbia and elsewhere, and to employ such means as may be necessary to execute the functions imposed upon him by section 8351 of this title.
