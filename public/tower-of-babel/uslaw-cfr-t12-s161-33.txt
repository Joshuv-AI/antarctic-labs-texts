12 C.F.R. § 161.33 — Note account.
Citation: us:cfr:12:161.33
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-161.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 161 (Definitions For Regulations Affecting All Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term note account means a note, subject to the right of immediate call, evidencing funds held by depositories electing the note option under applicable United States Treasury Department regulations. Note accounts are not savings accounts or savings deposits.
