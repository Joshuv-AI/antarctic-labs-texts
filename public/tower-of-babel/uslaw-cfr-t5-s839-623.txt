5 C.F.R. § 839.623 — If I decide to keep the FERS election in effect, may I change the effective date of the FERS election?
Citation: us:cfr:5:839.623
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026

No, If you decide to keep FERS, the original FERS election will remain unchanged.
