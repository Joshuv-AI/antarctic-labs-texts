7 C.F.R. § 984.11 — Merchantable walnuts.
Citation: us:cfr:7:984.11
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-984.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 984 (Walnuts Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Inshell. Merchantable inshell walnuts means all inshell walnuts meeting the minimum grade and size regulations effective pursuant to § 984.50.
(b) Shelled. Merchantable shelled walnuts means all shelled walnuts meeting the minimum grade and size regulations effective pursuant to § 984.50.
