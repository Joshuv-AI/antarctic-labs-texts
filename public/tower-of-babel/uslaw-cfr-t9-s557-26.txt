9 C.F.R. § 557.26 — Official import inspection marks and devices.
Citation: us:cfr:9:557.26
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-557.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 557 (Importation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The official inspection legend and other marks to be applied to imported fish and fish products are as required by 9 CFR 327.26 for meat food products prepared from cattle, sheep, swine, and goats.
