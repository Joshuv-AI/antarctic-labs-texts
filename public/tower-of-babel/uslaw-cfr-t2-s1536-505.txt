2 C.F.R. § 1536.505 — Who in the Environmental Protection Agency determines that a recipient who is an individual violated the requirements of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:2:1536.505
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1536.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1536 (Requirements For Drug-Free Workplace (Financial Assistance)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).
