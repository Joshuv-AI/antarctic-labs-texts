16 U.S.C. § 715c — Areas recommended for approval; character
Citation: us:usc:16:715c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section715c
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 7 (Protection Of Migratory Game And Insectivorous Birds); Subchapter III (Migratory Bird Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior may not recommend any area for purchase or rental under the terms of this subchapter unless the Secretary of the Interior—
(1)
has determined that such area is necessary for the conservation of migratory birds; and
(2)
has consulted with the county or other unit of local government in which such area is located and with the Governor of the State concerned or the appropriate State agency.
