49 C.F.R. § 179.202-1 — -1 Applicability.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 179 (Specifications For Tank Cars). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each tank built under these specifications must conform to the general requirements of § 179.200 and the prescriptive standards in §§ 179.202-1 through 179.202-11, or the performance standard requirements of § 179.202-12.
