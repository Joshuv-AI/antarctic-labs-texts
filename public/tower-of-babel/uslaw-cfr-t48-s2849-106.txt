48 C.F.R. § 2849.106 — 2849.106 Fraud or other criminal conduct.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2849.106
Creator: Chapter 28—Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2849.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2849 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the contracting officer has reason to suspect fraud or other criminal conduct related to the settlement negotiations of a terminated contract, the contracting officer shall discontinue the negotiations and report the facts supporting the suspicion through the HCA or designee to the OIG.
