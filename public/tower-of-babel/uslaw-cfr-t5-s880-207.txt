5 C.F.R. § 880.207 — Adjustment of accounts after finding of death.
Citation: us:cfr:5:880.207
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-880.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 880 (Retirement And Insurance Benefits During Periods Of Unexplained Absence). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After a missing annuitant is determined to be dead under § 880.205, OPM will review the case to determine whether additional benefits are payable or excess insurance premiums have been withheld.
