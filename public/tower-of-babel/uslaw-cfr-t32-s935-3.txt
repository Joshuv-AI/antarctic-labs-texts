32 C.F.R. § 935.3 — Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:32:935.3
Creator: Department Of The Air Force
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-935.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 935 (Wake Island Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In this part—
(a) General Counsel means the General Counsel of the Air Force or his successor in office.
(b) Commander means the Commander, Wake Island.
(c) Commander, Wake Island means the Commander of Pacific Air Forces or such subordinate commissioned officer of the Air Force to whom he may delegate his authority under this part.
(d) He or his includes both the male and female sexes, unless the context implies otherwise.
(e) Judge includes Judges of the Wake Island Court and Court of Appeals.
