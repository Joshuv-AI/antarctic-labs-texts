18 C.F.R. § 1309.8 — Who has the burden of proving that an action is excepted?
Citation: us:cfr:18:1309.8
Creator: Tennessee Valley Authority
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-1309.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 1309 (Nondiscrimination With Respect To Age). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The burden of proving that an age distinction or other action falls within the exceptions outlined in §§ 1309.6 and 1309.7 is on the recipient of financial assistance from TVA.
