48 C.F.R. § 11.001 — 11.001 Definitions.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 11 (Describing Agency Needs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part—
Reconditioned means restored to the original normal operating condition by readjustments and material replacement.
Remanufactured means factory rebuilt to original specifications.
