32 C.F.R. § 269.3 — Civil monetary penalty inflation adjustment.
Citation: us:cfr:32:269.3
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-269.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 269 (Civil Monetary Penalty Inflation Adjustment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department must, not later than July 1, 2016 and not later than January 15 of every year thereafter—
(a) By regulation, adjust each civil monetary penalty provided by law within the jurisdiction of the Department by the inflation adjustment described in § 269.4; and
(b) Publish each such update in the Federal Register.
