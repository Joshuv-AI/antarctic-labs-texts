Abstract
In this contribution, our motive is to unify the categories associated with F F -transforms and fuzzy pretopological spaces as a new category 𝐐𝐮𝐚 {\bf Qua} , whose object classes are success measurements of answers and morphisms are pairs of success measurements of transformations. Specifically, the categories of spaces with L L -valued fuzzy partitions, L L -valued fuzzy lower transformation systems, L L -valued fuzzy pretopological spaces, and Čech L L -valued fuzzy interior spaces with the morphisms as pairs of L L -valued fuzzy relations between the underlying sets of corresponding objects have intriguing relationships with the category 𝐐𝐮𝐚 {\bf Qua} .

Complete lattices,

-fuzzy lower transformation system,
L
-fuzzy pretopology, Category.

Definition 2.5

A
complete residuated lattice
is an algebra
(L,\wedge,\vee,\ast,0,1)
such that

(i)

(L,\wedge,\vee,0,1)
is a complete lattice with the least element
0
and the greatest element
1

(ii)

(L,\ast,1)
is a commutative monoid;

(iii)

a\ast\bigvee\limits_{i\in J}b_{i}=\bigvee\limits_{i\in J}a\ast b_{i},\,\forall\,\{b_{i}:i\in J\}\subseteq L
.

Definition 2.6

A
complete co-residuated lattice
is an algebra
(L,\wedge,\vee,\#,0,1)
suchh that

(i)

(L,\wedge,\vee,0,1)
is a complete lattice with the least element 0 and the greatest element 1;

(ii)

(L,\#,0)
is a commutative monoid;

(iii)

a\#\bigwedge\limits_{i\in J}b_{i}=\bigwedge\limits_{i\in J}a\#b_{i},\,\forall\,\{b_{i}:i\in J\}\subseteq L
.

Definition 2.7

A
negator
on
L
is a decreasing function
\neg:L\rightarrow L
such that
\neg 0=1,\neg 1=0
. Also,
\neg
is said to be an
involutive negator
if
\neg\neg a=a,\,\forall\,a\in L
.

Throughout this work, we consider that

is a complete residuated and co-residuated lattice and
\neg
is an involutive negator, in which
\ast
and
\#
associated by absorptin law, i.e.,
(a\#b)\ast c\leq a\#(b\ast c),\,\forall\,a,b,c\in L
and dual with respect
\neg

\neg a\ast\neg b=\neg(a\#b),\,\forall\,a,b\in L
. For an involutive negator
\neg
,
\bigwedge\limits_{i\in J}(\neg a_{i})=\neg(\bigvee\limits_{i\in J}a_{i})
and
\bigvee\limits_{i\in J}(\neg a_{i})=\neg(\bigwedge\limits_{i\in J}a_{i}),\,\forall\,\{a_{i}:i\in J\}\subseteq L.

Remark 2.3

(i)

The following is the motivation for this particular morphism condition.

Let
\mathcal{M},\mathcal{M}^{\prime}\in{\bf Qua}
and
(g_{*},g^{*}):\mathcal{M}\rightarrow\mathcal{M}^{\prime}
be
{\bf Qua}
-morphisms. Furthermore, let

M
answer in response to the question

and success of this response is measured by

. At the same time,

into the type
M^{\prime}

m^{\prime}_{*}
. The whole success of this operation is determined by
g_{*}(m_{*},m^{\prime}_{*})\ast\mathbf{M}(m^{*},m_{*})
, which is the result of the cooperation of
g_{*}(m_{*},m^{\prime}_{*})
and
\mathbf{M}(m^{*},m_{*})
. On the other hand, if
m^{\prime*}
is a type
M^{\prime}

m^{\prime}_{*}
will react to it and the success of this response is determined by

. At the same time,

measures the success of transformation of a question
m^{\prime*}
into the type
M
question
m^{*}
. The whole success of this operation is determined by
g^{*}(m^{\prime*},m^{*})\#\mathbf{M}^{\prime}(m^{\prime*},m^{\prime}_{*})
, which is the result of the competition between
g^{*}(m^{\prime*},m^{*})
and
\mathbf{M}^{\prime}(m^{\prime*},m^{\prime}_{*})
. Thus the defining condition of

-valued fuzzy homomorphism is that the result of cooperation of

and
\mathbf{M}(m^{*},m_{*})
should not grater than the

M^{*}(m^{\prime*},m^{*})
and
\mathbf{M}^{\prime}(m^{\prime*},m^{\prime}_{*})
.

(ii)

The class of object of the category
{\bf Qua}
is clearly success measurements of responses and morphisms are pairs of success measurements of transformations.

Proposition 3.1

Spaces with

5
Conclusion

In view of the work carried out in
[
45
]
towards the importance of
{\bf Qua}
category in the unification of categories associated with fuzzy automata, herein, we have tried to unify the categories associated with
F
-transforms and fuzzy pretopological spaces. Specifically, we introduced the categories
{\bf LSpaceFP}
,
{\bf LFtrans}^{\downarrow}
,
{\bf LFPrTop}
and
{\bf LFCInt}
and their morphisms are as pairs of
L
-valued fuzzy relations (respectively, the categories
{\bf SpaceFP}
,
{\bf Ftrans}^{\downarrow}
,
{\bf FPrTop}
and
{\bf CInt}
and their morphisms are as functions). Interestingly, we have underlaid the connections of the categories
{\bf LSpaceFP}
,
{\bf LFtrans}^{\downarrow}
,
{\bf LFPrTop}
and
{\bf LFCInt}
with the category
{\bf Qua}
. Further, we show a functorial relationship among the categories
{\bf LSpaceFP}
,
{\bf LFtrans}^{\downarrow}
,
{\bf LFPrTop}
and
{\bf LFCInt}
. Interestingly, there exist two pairs of functors
(F_{3},F^{\prime}_{3})
and
(F_{6},F^{\prime}_{6})
that have adjoint property. In the future, it will be interesting to see the unification of categories associated with the general fuzzy relations in the framework of the
{\bf Qua}
category.
