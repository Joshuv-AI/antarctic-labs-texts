28 C.F.R. § 202.104 — Delegation of authorities.
Citation: us:cfr:28:202.104
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-202.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 202 (Access To U.S. Sensitive Personal Data And Government-Related Data By Countries Of Concern Or Covered Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any action that the Attorney General is authorized to take pursuant to the Order or pursuant to this part may be taken by the Assistant Attorney General for National Security or by any other person to whom the Attorney General or Assistant Attorney General for National Security in writing delegates authority so to act.
