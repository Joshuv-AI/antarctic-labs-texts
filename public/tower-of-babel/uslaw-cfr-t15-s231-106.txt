15 C.F.R. § 231.106 — Knowingly.
Citation: us:cfr:15:231.106
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-231.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 231 (Clawbacks Of Chips Funding). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Knowingly means acting with knowledge that a circumstance exists or is substantially certain to occur, or with an awareness of a high probability of its existence or future occurrence. Such awareness can be inferred from evidence of the conscious disregard of facts known to a person or of a person's willful avoidance of facts.
