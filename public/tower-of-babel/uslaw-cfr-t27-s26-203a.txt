27 C.F.R. § 26.203a — a Containers in excess of 1 gallon (3.785 liters).
Citation: us:cfr:27:26.203a
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-26.203a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 26 (Liquors And Articles From Puerto Rico And The Virgin Islands). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Containers of distilled spirits brought into the United States from the Virgin Islands, having a capacity in excess of 1 gallon (3.785 liters), are required to be marked in accordance with customs regulations (19 CFR chapter I).
