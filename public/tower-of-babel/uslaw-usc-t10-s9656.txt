10 U.S.C. § 9656 — Aircraft and equipment: civilian aviation schools
Citation: us:usc:10:9656
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9656
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 971 (Issue Of Serviceable Material Other Than To Armed Forces). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Air Force, under regulations to be prescribed by him, may lend aircraft, aircraft parts, and aeronautical equipment and accessories that are required for instruction, training, and maintenance, to accredited civilian aviation schools at which personnel of the Department of the Air Force or the Department of the Army are pursuing a course of instruction and training under detail by competent orders.
