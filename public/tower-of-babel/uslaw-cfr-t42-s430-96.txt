42 C.F.R. § 430.96 — Record for decision.
Citation: us:cfr:42:430.96
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-430.96
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 430 (Grants To States For Medical Assistance Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The transcript of testimony, exhibits, and all papers and requests filed in the proceedings, except the correspondence section of the docket, including rulings and any recommended or initial decision constitute the exclusive record for decision.
