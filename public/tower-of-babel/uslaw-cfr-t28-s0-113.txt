28 C.F.R. § 0.113 — Redelegation of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:28:0.113
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-0.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 0 (Organization Of The Department Of Justice). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Director, U.S. Marshals Service, is authorized to redelegate to any of his subordinates any of the powers and functions vested in him by this subpart, except that the authority to approve “other necessary expenditures in the line of duty” of U.S. Marshals and Deputy U.S. Marshals may not be delegated below the Assistant Director level.
