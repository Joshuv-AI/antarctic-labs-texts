25 C.F.R. § 273.142 — Are advance payments on a contract allowed under the Johnson-O'Malley Act?
Citation: us:cfr:25:273.142
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-273.142
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 273 (Education Contracts Under Johnson-O'Malley Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Payments to States, public school districts and Indian corporations will be made in advance or by way of reimbursement and in such installments and on such conditions as the BIE Director deems necessary to carry out the purposes of the Act.
