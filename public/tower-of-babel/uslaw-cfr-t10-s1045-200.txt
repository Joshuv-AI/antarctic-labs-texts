10 C.F.R. § 1045.200 — Is there a cost for an MDR review?
Citation: us:cfr:10:1045.200
Creator: Department Of Energy (General Provisions)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1045.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1045 (Nuclear Classification And Declassification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. The fees, including waivers, reductions, and categorizations, are the same for an MDR as for providing records under the FOIA as defined in 10 CFR 1004.9.
