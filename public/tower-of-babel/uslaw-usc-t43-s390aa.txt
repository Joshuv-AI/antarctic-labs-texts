43 U.S.C. § 390aa — Congressional declaration of purpose; short title
Citation: us:usc:43:390aa
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section390aa
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter I–A (Reclamation Reform). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter shall amend and supplement the Act of June 17, 1902, and Acts supplementary thereto and amendatory thereof (43 U.S.C. 371), hereinafter referred to as “Federal reclamation law”. This subchapter may be referred to as the “Reclamation Reform Act of 1982”.
