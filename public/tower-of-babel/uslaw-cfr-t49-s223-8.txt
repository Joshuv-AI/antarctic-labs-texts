49 C.F.R. § 223.8 — Additional requirements for passenger equipment.
Citation: us:cfr:49:223.8
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-223.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 223 (Safety Glazing Standards—Locomotives, Passenger Cars And Cabooses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the requirements contained in this part, requirements for emergency window exits and window safety glazing on passenger equipment, as defined in § 238.5 of this chapter, are also found in part 238 of this chapter.
