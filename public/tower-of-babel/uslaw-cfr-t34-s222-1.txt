34 C.F.R. § 222.1 — What is the scope of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:34:222.1
Creator: Office Of Elementary And Secondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-222.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 222 (Impact Aid Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part govern the provision of financial assistance under title VIII of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 (ESEA) to local educational agencies (LEAs) in areas affected by Federal activities.
