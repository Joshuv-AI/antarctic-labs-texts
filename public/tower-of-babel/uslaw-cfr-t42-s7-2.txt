42 C.F.R. § 7.2 — Establishment of a user charge.
Citation: us:cfr:42:7.2
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-7.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 7 (Distribution Of Reference Biological Standards And Biological Preparations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in § 7.6, a user charge shall be imposed to cover the cost to CDC of producing and distributing reference biological standards and biological preparations.
