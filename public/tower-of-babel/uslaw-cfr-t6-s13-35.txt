6 C.F.R. § 13.35 — The record.
Citation: us:cfr:6:13.35
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-13.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 13 (Administrative Remedies For False Claims And Statements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The hearing will be recorded and transcribed. Transcripts may be obtained following the hearing from the Presiding Officer at a cost not to exceed the actual cost of duplication.
(b) The transcript of testimony, exhibits and other evidence admitted at the hearing, and all papers and requests filed in the proceeding constitute the record for the decision by the Presiding Officer and the Authority Head.
(c) The record may be inspected and copied (upon payment of a reasonable fee) by anyone, unless otherwise ordered by the Presiding Officer pursuant to § 13.24.
