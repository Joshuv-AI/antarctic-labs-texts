41 U.S.C. § 6304 — Certain contracts limited to one-year term
Citation: us:usc:41:6304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section6304
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 63 (General Contract Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided, an executive department shall not make a contract for stationery or other supplies for a term longer than one year from the time the contract is made.
