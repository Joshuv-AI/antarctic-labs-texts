16 U.S.C. § 825s–4 — Southwestern Power Administration; deposit and availability of advance payments
Citation: us:usc:16:825s–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section825s–4
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12 (Federal Regulation And Development Of Power); Subchapter III (Licensees And Public Utilities; Procedural And Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding section 3302 of title 31, beginning in fiscal year 2005 and thereafter, such funds as are received by the Southwestern Power Administration from any State, municipality, corporation, association, firm, district, or individual as advance payment for work that is associated with Southwestern’s transmission facilities, consistent with that authorized in section 825s of this title, shall be credited to this account and be available until expended.
