1.
Introduction

The notion of prime ideals in the theory of rings as an extention
of the notion of prime number in the ring of integers has an important position in that theory, as might be expected from the central place occupied by the primes in arithmetic. For this reason, many papers were written abut prime ideals and thier generalizations.
Looking at the respective studies, the concept of
Endo
-prime ideals of a commutative ring as an expansion of prime ideals has been introduced and studied in
[
1
]
and
[
15
]
. Let
R
be a commutative ring and
\theta:R\longrightarrow R
be a fixed endomorphism. An ideal

of

\theta
-prime ideal if, whenever

and

u\in I
or
\theta(v)\in I
. Moreover, a generalization of this notion called
\theta
-primary ideals was proposed in
[
14
]
.

A hyperstructure is an algebraic structure that has at least one multi-valued operation, known as a hyperoperation. It is well-known that hyperstructure theory was proposed by a French mathematician in 1934
[
12
]
. He defined the notion of hypergroups as an expansion of groups. This has been the starting point for the study of many types of hyperrings such as general hyperrings, Krasner hyperrings and multiplicative hyperrings. The Krasner hyperrings introduced by Krasner are obtained by considering the addition as a hyperoperation and the multiplication as a binary operation
[
10
]
. Krasner
(m,n)
-hyperrings as an expansion of Krasner hyperrings were presented in
[
13
]
. More details about these hyperrings and fundamental relations on them were given in
[
5
,
6
,
7
,
11
,
16
,
17
]
. The important hyperideals of a Krasner
(m,n)
-hyperring, namely,
n
-ary prime and
n
-ary primary hyperideals were studies in
[
2
]
. The generalizations of these notions such as
(k,n)
-absorbing,
(k,n)
-absorbing primary hyperideals,
weakly
(k,n)
-absorbing and weakly
(k,n)
-absorbing primary hyperideals were investigated in
[
8
]
and
[
9
]
.

In this paper, we aim to analyze a class
of hyperideals established on an endomorphism in a Krasner
(m,n)
-hyperring. The paper is organized as follows. In
Section 2, we start by recalling some background material. In
Section 3, the notion of

-ary

-prime hyperideals associated with an endomorphism

In Theorem

5.
conclusion

The current paper has introduced the notion of
n
-ary
Endo
-prime hyperideals using an endomorphism
\theta
and investigated their properties. Although the class of
n
-ary
Endo
-prime hyperideals is a generalization of

-ary

-prime hyperideal may not be an

-ary prime hyperideal.
We concluded that a hyperideal

n
-ary
Endo
-prime hyperideal, however, the inclusion
\theta(E)\subseteq E
always hold for every
n
-ary
Endo
-prime hyperideal

We presented several characterizations of this new concept under certain conditions. The findings discussed in this paper enhance the comprehension of the structure of Krasner
(m,n)
-hyperrings with respect to an endomorphism
\theta

Endo
-prime hyperideals in specific cases. Furthermore, we presented the concept of
n
-ary

n
-ary
Endo
-prime hyperideals. We showed that an

-ary

-ary
Endo
-prime hyperideal. We ontained that the radical of an

-ary
Endo
-prime hyperideal. As a new research subject, we suggest the notion of

-prime subhypemodules.
