54 U.S.C. § 306112 — Waiver of provisions in event of natural disaster or imminent threat to national security
Citation: us:usc:54:306112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section306112
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3061 (Program Responsibilities And Authorities); Subchapter I (In General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall promulgate regulations under which the requirements of this subchapter (except section 306108) may be waived in whole or in part in the event of a major natural disaster or an imminent threat to national security.
