36 C.F.R. § 3.19 — May I operate a submersible within park waters?
Citation: us:cfr:36:3.19
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-3.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 3 (Boating And Water Use Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The use of manned or unmanned submersibles may only occur in accordance with a permit issued by the superintendent under § 1.6 of this chapter.
