25 U.S.C. § 3013 — Enforcement
Citation: us:usc:25:3013
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section3013
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 32 (Native American Graves Protection And Repatriation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States district courts shall have jurisdiction over any action brought by any person alleging a violation of this chapter and shall have the authority to issue such orders as may be necessary to enforce the provisions of this chapter.
