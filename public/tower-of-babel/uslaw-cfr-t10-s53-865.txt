10 C.F.R. § 53.865 — Quality assurance.
Citation: us:cfr:10:53.865
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-53.865
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 53 (Risk-Informed, Technology-Inclusive Regulatory Framework For Commercial Nuclear Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each holder of an OL or COL under this part must develop, implement, and maintain a quality assurance program in accordance with appendix B of part 50 of this chapter. A written quality assurance program manual must be developed and used to guide the conduct of the program.
