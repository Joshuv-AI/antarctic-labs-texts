36 U.S.C. § 154112 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:36:154112
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section154112
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1541 (Naval Sea Cadet Corps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation shall submit an annual report to the Secretary of the Navy on the activities of the corporation during the prior calendar year. The Secretary shall communicate to Congress any part of the report that the Secretary considers appropriate.
