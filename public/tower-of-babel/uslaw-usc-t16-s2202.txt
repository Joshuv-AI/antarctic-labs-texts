16 U.S.C. § 2202 — Payments to agricultural producers for carrying out water conservation or water enhancing measures; criteria
Citation: us:usc:16:2202
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section2202
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 42 (Emergency Conservation Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary is authorized to make payments to agricultural producers who carry out emergency water conservation or water enhancing measures (including measures carried out to assist confined livestock) during periods of severe drought as determined by the Secretary.
