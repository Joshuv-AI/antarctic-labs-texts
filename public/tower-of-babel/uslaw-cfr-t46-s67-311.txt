46 C.F.R. § 67.311 — Alteration of Certificate of Documentation.
Citation: us:cfr:46:67.311
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-67.311
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 67 (Documentation Of Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person other than a documentation officer shall intentionally alter a Certificate of Documentation.
