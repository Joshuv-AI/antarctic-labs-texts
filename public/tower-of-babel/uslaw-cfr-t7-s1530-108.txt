7 C.F.R. § 1530.108 — Revocation or surrender of licenses.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1530.108
Creator: Foreign Agricultural Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1530.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1530 (The Refined Sugar Re-Export Program, The Sugar Containing Products Re-Export Program, And The Polyhydric Alcohol Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A license may be revoked upon written notice by the Licensing Authority.
(b) A licensee may surrender a license when the sum of all credits is equal to or greater than the sum of all charges.
