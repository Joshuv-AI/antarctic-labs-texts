22 C.F.R. § 191.20 — Eligibility for benefits.
Citation: us:cfr:22:191.20
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-191.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 191 (Hostage Relief Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A person designated as a hostage or Family Member of a hostage under subpart A of this subchapter shall be eligible for benefits under this subpart.
