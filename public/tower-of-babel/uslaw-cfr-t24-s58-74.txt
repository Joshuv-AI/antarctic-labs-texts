24 C.F.R. § 58.74 — Time for objecting.
Citation: us:cfr:24:58.74
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-58.74
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 58 (Environmental Review Procedures For Entities Assuming Hud Environmental Responsibilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All objections must be received by HUD (or the State) within 15 days from the time HUD (or the State) receives the recipient's RROF and the related certification, or within the time period specified in the notice, whichever is later.
