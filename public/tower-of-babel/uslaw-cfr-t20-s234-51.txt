20 C.F.R. § 234.51 — Persons to whom a lump-sum refund payment is payable.
Citation: us:cfr:20:234.51
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-234.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 234 (Lump-Sum Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees receive their lump-sum refund payment from the Board, without applying for it, at the time their regular annuity is awarded. If an employee dies without receiving payment of a regular annuity, the lump-sum refund payment is payable to the employee's survivors in the same order of priority as shown for the RLS in § 234.44.
