46 C.F.R. § 172.005 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:172.005
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-172.005
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 172 (Special Rules Pertaining To Bulk Cargoes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to each vessel that carries one of the following cargoes in bulk:
(a) Grain.
(b) A cargo listed in Table 30.25-1 of this chapter.
(c) A cargo regulated under 33 CFR part 157.
(d) A cargo listed in Table 151.01-10(b) of this chapter.
(e) A cargo listed in Table I of part 153 of this chapter.
(f) A cargo listed in Table 4 of part 154 of this chapter.
(g) Any dry bulk cargo carried in a new Great Lakes vessel.
