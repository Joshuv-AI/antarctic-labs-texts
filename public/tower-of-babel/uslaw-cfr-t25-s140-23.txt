25 C.F.R. § 140.23 — Credit at trader's risk.
Citation: us:cfr:25:140.23
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-140.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 140 (Licensed Indian Traders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Credit given Indians will be at the trader's own risk, as no assistance will be given by Government officials in the collection of debts against Indians. Traders shall not accept pawns or pledges of personal property by Indians to obtain credit or loans.
