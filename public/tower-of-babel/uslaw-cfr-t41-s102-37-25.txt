41 C.F.R. § 102-37.25 — -37.25 Transportation and other costs.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.25
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The transferee is responsible for any packing, shipping, or transportation charges associated with the transfer of surplus property for donation. Those costs may be passed on to donees that receive the property.
