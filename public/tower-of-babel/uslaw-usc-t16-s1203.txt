16 U.S.C. § 1203 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:1203
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1203
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 25 (Jellyfish Or Sea Nettles, Other Such Pests, And Seaweed In Coastal Waters: Control Or Elimination). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is authorized to be appropriated not to exceed $500,000 for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1968, $750,000 for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1969, $1,000,000 for the period beginning July 1, 1969, and ending June 30, 1973, and $400,000 for each of the fiscal years ending June 30, 1974, June 30, 1975, June 30, 1976, and June 30, 1977.
