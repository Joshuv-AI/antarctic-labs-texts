43 C.F.R. § 2533.0-8 — -8 Land subject to allotment.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2533.0-8
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2533.0-8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2530 (Indian Allotments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An allotment under this section may be made for lands containing coal and oil and gas with reservation of the mineral contents to the United States, but not for lands valuable for metalliferous minerals. The rules governing the conduct of fourth-section applications under the Act of February 8, 1887 as amended, apply equally to applications under said section 31.
