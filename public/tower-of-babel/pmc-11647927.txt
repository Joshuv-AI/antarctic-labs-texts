Radiation medicine from the perspective of radiation disaster medical science research
Authors: Satoshi Tashiro
Published: 2024

In the aftermath of the Fukushima Daiichi Nuclear Power Plant accident, the Research Institute for Radiation Biology and Medicine at Hiroshima University, in collaboration with the Atomic Bomb Disease Institute at Nagasaki University and the Fukushima Global Medical Science Center at Fukushima Medical University, established the Network-based Center for Radiation Disaster and Medical Science Research in 2016. This initiative was launched with the aim of creating an advanced and integrated academic foundation for radiation disaster and medical sciences, disseminating the outcomes to the public and contributing to the international community.

As part of the activities of this research center, an international symposium is organized annually to foster interaction and collaboration among researchers in this field. On 20 February 2023, the 7th International Symposium on Radiation Medicine, themed ‘Radiation Medicine from the Perspective of Radiation Disaster Medical Science Research’, was held in Hiroshima. This supplementary issue is composed of the presentations delivered at that symposium.

The event witnessed the enthusiastic participation of over 140 attendees from both domestic and international institutions, resulting in vibrant exchanges of information and ideas. The keynote lecture was delivered by Dr Elizabeth Ann Ainsbury from the United Kingdom Health Security Agency, titled ‘Networking in Biological and Physical Retrospective Dosimetry in Europe and Beyond’. Dr Ainsbury provided a comprehensive review of various biological and physical retrospective dosimetry techniques used to monitor radiation exposure and support rapid response in both routine and emergency situations, along with an insightful overview of the latest developments and future directions in dose assessment technology.

The symposium featured a series of lectures by experts from around the world on topics such as ‘Health Risk Research of Low-Dose Radiation Exposure’, ‘Medical Care for Radiation Injuries’, ‘Impact on Society and Radiation Protection’ and ‘Research on Medical Radiation’. These sessions were complemented by poster presentations aligned with the themes. In this supplementary issue, 15 presenters, including Dr Ainsbury, have contributed their work in the form of review articles or original research articles.

It is our hope that this supplementary issue will serve as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners engaged in both fundamental and clinical aspects of radiation disaster medicine.
