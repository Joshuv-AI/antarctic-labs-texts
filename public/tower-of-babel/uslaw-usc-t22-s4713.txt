22 U.S.C. § 4713 — Latin American exchanges
Citation: us:usc:22:4713
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section4713
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 57 (United States Scholarship Program For Developing Countries). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Of any funds authorized to be appropriated for activities authorized by this chapter, not less than 25 percent shall be allocated to fund grants and exchanges to Latin America and the Caribbean.
