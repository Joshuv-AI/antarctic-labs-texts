Matching between
Collinear Twist-3 and TMD Fragmentation Function

Abstract




We investigate the consistency between the collinear twist-3 factorization and the
transverse-momentum-dependent (TMD) factorization
for the transversely polarized hyperon production
in semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering,
ep\to e\Lambda^{\uparrow}X
.
In particular, we focus on the contributions from the
twist-3 fragmentation functions (FFs) and the TMD polarizing FF
in the region of the hyperon’s intermediate transverse momentum
P_{T}
,

\Lambda_{\rm QCD}\ll P_{T}\ll Q
, where both frameworks are valid.
In this region the polarizing FF can be expressed in terms of the twist-3 FFs including the
purely gluonic ones,
and the resulting TMD factorization formula for
ep\to e\Lambda^{\uparrow}X

agrees with the small-
P_{T}
limit of the corresponding twist-3 cross section.
This matching of the two calculations indicates that the two frameworks describe
the same effect in QCD
and provide complementary frameworks for the process in different kinematic regions.

Introduction

Understanding the mechanism for the
transverse polarization of hyperons produced in unpolarized
collisions

It has been understood by now that effects of particular multi-parton

and the transverse momentum dependent (TMD) factorization

Collinear twist-3 factorization is designed to describe SSAs in the
region of the large transverse momentum of the final hadron
\Lambda_{\rm QCD}\ll P_{T}\lesssim Q

while the TMD factorization describes those in the small-
P_{T}
region
\Lambda_{\rm QCD}\lesssim P_{T}\ll Q
.
Relation between these two frameworks has also been studied for some processes, and it’s been shown that
they match in the region of the intermediate transverse momentum (
\Lambda_{\rm QCD}\ll P_{T}\ll Q

for

and Drell-Yan processes etc

Although the twist-3

QCD coupling due to the technical complexities,
there have been some studies to include

for
ep^{\uparrow}\to e\pi X
[

,

,

,

[

,

[

More recently, a complete NLO twist-3 distribution function (DF)

ep^{\uparrow}\to hX
(
h=\pi
, jet) has been derived and its factorization property
has been proven
[
31
,
32
]
.

In this paper we investigate the consistency between the collinear twist-3
factorization and the TMD factorization at LO
in the intermediate transverse momentum region
for the
hyperon polarization in semi-inclusive deep inelastic scattering (SIDIS)
ep\to e\Lambda^{\uparrow}X
.
In the collinear twist-3 factorization, two effects participate in this process: One is
(i) the twist-3 unpolarized DF in the initial proton
convoluted with the twist-2 transversity fragmentation function (FF) for the final hyperon
and the corresponding partonic hard cross section, and the other is
(ii) the twist-3 FFs for the final hyperon convoluted with
the unpolarized DF in the proton and the partonic hard cross section.
In (ii), two contributions exist; (a) the twist-3 quark FFs and (b) the
twist-3 gluon FFs.
The complete LO twist-3 cross section formula
for
ep\to e\Lambda^{\uparrow}X
has been derived
for
(i) in
[
21
,
33
]
, for (ii)(a)
in
[
33
]
and for (ii)(b) in
[
34
]
.
In the TMD factorization, corresponding
to the above (i) and (ii), there are two contributions:
(I) the Boer-Mulders function in the initial proton combined with
the transversity FF for the hyperon
[
35
]

and (II) the polarizing FF combined with
the unpolarized DF in the proton contribute to
the hyperon polarization
[
36
]
.
So far, it’s been shown that (i) and (I) match at intermediate transverse
momentum
\Lambda_{\rm QCD}\ll P_{T}\ll Q
[
21
]
,
while no such relation has been established between (ii) and (II).

The purpose of this paper is to investigate the relation between (ii) and (II)
for
ep\to e\Lambda^{\uparrow}X
.
So far such study on the matching between the two frameworks
has been mostly for the distribution side,
and the study on the FF contribution to SSA has been performed only for the process

ep^{\uparrow}\to e\pi X
in
[
22
]
, which shows
the twist-3 FF contribution matches with that from the Collins
function
[
37
]
.
This paper extends the study to
ep\to e\Lambda^{\uparrow}X
.
For (ii) and (II), extra complication occurs
compared to
ep^{\uparrow}\to e\pi X
:
Owing to the chiral-even nature of the relevant twist-3 FFs and the polarizing FF,
a purely gluonic channel (above (ii)(b)) enters the calculation,
for which the complete twist-3 cross
section was derived only recently
[
34
]
.
Here we will show that the TMD polarizing FF contribution can be expressed
in terms of the collinear twist-3 quark and gluon FFs in the region of
large intrinsic transverse momentum,
and the resulting TMD factorization formula agrees with
that obtained from the small-
P_{T}
limit
of the collinear twist-3 cross section.

This paper is organized as follows:
In section 2, we summarize the kinematics for
ep\to e\Lambda^{\uparrow}X
.
In section 3, we summarize the collinear twist-3 FFs and the TMD polarizing FF relevant to the present study.
In section 4, we recall the structure of the twist-3 FF contribution for
ep\to e\Lambda^{\uparrow}X

from
[
33
,
34
]

and derive its small-
P_{T}
limit.
In section 5, we present a perturbative calculation of the TMD polarizing FF in the
region of the large intrinsic transverse momentum,
and show that the two frameworks indeed match at
\Lambda_{\rm QCD}\ll P_{T}\ll Q
.
Section 6 is devoted to a brief summary.

\displaystyle q_{t}^{\mu}\equiv q^{\mu}-\frac{P_{h}\cdot q}{p\cdot P_{h}}p^{\mu}-\frac{p\cdot q}{p\cdot P_{h}}P_{h}^{\mu}

(3)

Hadron frame

As in

\displaystyle p^{\mu}=\frac{Q}{2x_{bj}}(1,0,0,1),

(4)

\displaystyle q^{\mu}=(0,0,0,-Q).

(5)

Defining the azimuthal angles for the hadron plane and the lepton plane

\displaystyle P_{h}^{\mu}=\frac{z_{f}Q}{2}\left(1+\frac{q_{T}^{2}}{Q^{2}},\frac{2q_{T}}{Q}\cos{\chi},\frac{2q_{T}}{Q}\sin{\chi},-1+\frac{q_{T}^{2}}{Q^{2}}\right),

(6)

\displaystyle\ell^{\mu}=\frac{Q}{2}(\cosh{\psi},\sinh{\psi}\cos{\phi},\sinh{\psi}\sin{\phi},-1),

\displaystyle=\frac{P_{T}}{|\vec{P}_{h}|}=\frac{2q_{T}Q}{q_{T}^{2}+Q^{2}}.

\displaystyle\frac{d^{6}\sigma}{dx_{bj}dQ^{2}dz_{f}dq_{T}^{2}d\phi d\chi}=\frac{\alpha_{em}^{2}}{128\pi^{4}x_{bj}^{2}S_{ep}^{2}Q^{2}}z_{f}L^{\rho\sigma}(\ell,\ell^{\prime})W_{\rho\sigma}(p,q,P_{h}),

Collinear twist-3 FFs

3.1.1
Twist-3 quark FFs

Three kinds of twist-3 quark FFs
[
39
]
contribute to

ep\to e\Lambda^{\uparrow}X

(1) Intrinsic FFs:

They are defined from the lightcone correlation function of the quark fields as

\displaystyle\Delta(z)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{X}\int\frac{d\lambda}{2\pi}\mathrm{e}^{-i\frac{\lambda}{z}}\langle 0|\psi(0)|\Lambda^{\uparrow}X\rangle\langle\Lambda^{\uparrow}X|\bar{\psi}(\lambda w)|0\rangle{}

\displaystyle=\gamma_{5}{\vtop{\halign{#\cr\hfil/\hfil\crcr$S$\crcr}}}_{\perp}{\vtop{\halign{#\cr\hfil/\hfil\crcr$P$\crcr}}}_{h}\frac{H_{1}(z)}{z}+M_{h}\epsilon^{\alpha S_{\perp}wP_{h}}\gamma_{\alpha}\frac{D_{T}(z)}{z}+M_{h}\gamma_{5}{\vtop{\halign{#\cr\hfil/\hfil\crcr$S$\crcr}}}_{\perp}\frac{G_{T}(z)}{z}+\cdots,

where

is the number of color SU(N),
and
|\Lambda^{\uparrow}\rangle
is the state vector for the hyperon
with mass

(
P_{h}^{2}=M_{h}^{2}

S_{\perp}
(
S_{\perp}^{2}=-1

In the twist-3 accuracy,

can be treated as lightlike and

w

P_{h}\cdot w=1

for
P_{h}=(|\vec{P}_{h}|,\vec{P}_{h})
,

w=\frac{1}{2|\vec{P}_{h}|^{2}}(|\vec{P}_{h}|,-\vec{P}_{h})
.
Here and below, we use the notation

\epsilon^{\alpha S_{\perp}wP_{h}}\equiv\epsilon^{\alpha\mu\nu\lambda}S_{\perp\mu}w_{\nu}P_{h\lambda}
and
the gauge-link operators which restore the color-gauge invariance of FFs are implicit.

H_{1}(z)
is the twist-2 transversity fragmentation
contributing to
ep\to e\Lambda^{\uparrow}X
and
pp\to\Lambda^{\uparrow}X

combined with the twist-3 unpolarized distribution in the nucleon

E_{F}(x_{1},x_{2})
[
33
,
40
,
41
,
42
]
,
which we shall not discuss here.

D_{T}(z)
and
G_{T}(z)
are the
intrinsic
twist-3 quark fragmentation function,
in particular,

D_{T}(z)
is T-odd and contributes to

ep\to e\Lambda^{\uparrow}X
.
In (
3.1.1
) and below, the flavor indices for the quark FFs are suppressed for simplicity.

(2) Kinematical FFs:

Twist-3 kinematical quark fragmentation functions are defined from the
correlation functions containing the transverse derivative of the fields:

\displaystyle\Delta^{\alpha}_{\partial}(z)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{X}\int\frac{d\lambda}{2\pi}\mathrm{e}^{-i\frac{\lambda}{z}}\langle 0|\psi(0)|\Lambda^{\uparrow}X\rangle\langle\Lambda^{\uparrow}X|\bar{\psi}(\lambda w)|0\rangle\overset{\leftarrow}{\partial}{}^{\alpha}{}

\displaystyle=-iM_{h}\epsilon^{\alpha S_{\perp}wP_{h}}{\vtop{\halign{#\cr\hfil/\hfil\crcr$P$\crcr}}}_{h}\frac{D_{1T}^{\perp(1)}(z)}{z}+iM_{h}S_{\perp}^{\alpha}\gamma_{5}{\vtop{\halign{#\cr\hfil/\hfil\crcr$P$\crcr}}}_{h}\frac{G_{1T}^{\perp(1)}(z)}{z}+\cdots,

where

\bar{\psi}(\lambda w)\overset{\leftarrow}{\partial}{}^{\alpha}\equiv\lim_{\xi\to 0}(d/d\xi_{\alpha})\bar{\psi}(\lambda w+\xi)
.

D_{1T}^{\perp(1)}(z)
and
G_{1T}^{\perp(1)}(z)

with its superscript
(1)
denoting the

-moment of the

[
36
]
. (See eq. (
47
) below.)

G_{1T}^{\perp(1)}(z)
is T-even, while

D_{1T}^{\perp(1)}(z)
is T-odd and contributes to
ep\to e\Lambda^{\uparrow}X
.

(3) Dynamical FFs:

They are defined as

\displaystyle\Delta^{\alpha}_{F}(z,z_{1})

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{X}\iint\frac{d\lambda d\mu}{(2\pi)^{2}}\mathrm{e}^{-i\frac{\lambda}{z_{1}}}\mathrm{e}^{-i\mu\left(\frac{1}{z}-\frac{1}{z_{1}}\right)}\langle 0|\psi(0)|\Lambda^{\uparrow}X\rangle\langle\Lambda^{\uparrow}X|\bar{\psi}(\lambda w)gF^{\alpha w}(\mu w)|0\rangle{}

\displaystyle=M_{h}\epsilon^{\alpha S_{\perp}wP_{h}}{\vtop{\halign{#\cr\hfil/\hfil\crcr$P$\crcr}}}_{h}\frac{\widehat{D}^{*}_{FT}(z,z_{1})}{z}-iM_{h}S_{\perp}^{\alpha}\gamma_{5}{\vtop{\halign{#\cr\hfil/\hfil\crcr$P$\crcr}}}_{h}\frac{\widehat{G}^{*}_{FT}(z,z_{1})}{z}+\cdots,

3.1.2
Twist-3 gluon FFs

Twist-3 gluon fragmentation functions can be also classified into three
types

30

(1) Intrinsic FFs:

Intrinsic fragmentation functions are defined from
the lightcone correlation functions of the
field strength
F^{\mu\nu}_{a}

\displaystyle\widehat{\Gamma}^{\alpha\beta}(z)

\displaystyle=\frac{1}{N^{2}-1}\sum_{X}\int\frac{d\lambda}{2\pi}\mathrm{e}^{-i\frac{\lambda}{z}}\langle 0|F^{w\beta}_{a}(0)|\Lambda^{\uparrow}X\rangle\langle\Lambda^{\uparrow}X|F^{w\alpha}_{a}(\lambda w)|0\rangle{}

\displaystyle=iM_{h}\epsilon^{\alpha\beta wS_{\perp}}\Delta\widehat{G}_{3T}(z)+M_{h}\epsilon^{P_{h}wS_{\perp}\{\alpha}w^{\beta\}}\Delta\widehat{G}_{3\bar{T}}(z)+\cdots,

\Delta\widehat{G}_{3T}(z)
and
\Delta\widehat{G}_{3\bar{T}}(z)

the intrinsic twist-3 fragmentation functions.

\Delta\widehat{G}_{3\bar{T}}(z)

contributes to the hyperon polarization.

(2) Kinematical FFs:

Kinematical fragmentation functions are defined from the correlation functions
containing the derivative of the field strengths:

\displaystyle\widehat{\Gamma}^{\alpha\beta\gamma}_{\partial}(z)=\frac{1}{N^{2}-1}\sum_{X}\int\frac{d\lambda}{2\pi}\mathrm{e}^{-i\frac{\lambda}{z}}\langle 0|F^{w\beta}_{a}(0)|\Lambda^{\uparrow}X\rangle\langle\Lambda^{\uparrow}X|F^{w\alpha}_{a}(\lambda w)|0\rangle\overset{\leftarrow}{\partial}{}^{\gamma}{}

\displaystyle=-i\frac{M_{h}}{2}g_{\perp}^{\alpha\beta}\epsilon^{P_{h}wS_{\perp}\gamma}\widehat{G}_{T}^{(1)}(z)+\frac{M_{h}}{2}\epsilon^{P_{h}w\alpha\beta}S_{\perp}^{\gamma}\Delta\widehat{G}^{(1)}_{T}(z){}

\displaystyle-i\frac{M_{h}}{8}\left(\epsilon^{P_{h}wS_{\perp}\{\alpha}g_{\perp}^{\beta\}\gamma}+\epsilon^{P_{h}w\gamma\{\alpha}S^{\beta\}}_{\perp}\right)\Delta\widehat{H}^{(1)}_{T}(z)+\cdots,

where

\Delta\widehat{G}^{(1)}_{T}(z)

\widehat{G}^{(1)}_{T}(z)
and
\Delta\widehat{H}^{(1)}_{T}(z)
are T-odd

k^{2}_{T}/M^{2}_{h}

44

.

(3) Dynamical FFs:

Dynamical gluon FFs are defined from the correlation
functions of three field-strengths. Two types of FFs
exist, depending on the
contraction of the color indices by two structure constants of color SU(N)

-if^{abc}
,
d^{abc}
:

\displaystyle\widehat{\Gamma}^{\alpha\beta\gamma}_{FA}\left(\frac{1}{z_{1}},\frac{1}{z_{2}}\right)=\frac{-if_{abc}}{N^{2}-1}\sum_{X}\iint\frac{d\lambda d\mu}{(2\pi)^{2}}\mathrm{e}^{-i\frac{\lambda}{z_{1}}}\mathrm{e}^{-i\mu\left(\frac{1}{z_{2}}-\frac{1}{z_{1}}\right)}\langle 0|F^{w\beta}_{b}(0)|\Lambda^{\uparrow}X\rangle\langle\Lambda^{\uparrow}X|F^{w\alpha}_{a}(\lambda w)gF^{w\gamma}_{c}(\mu w)|0\rangle{}

\displaystyle=-M_{h}\left(\widehat{N}_{1}\left(\frac{1}{z_{1}},\frac{1}{z_{2}}\right)g^{\alpha\gamma}_{\perp}\epsilon^{P_{h}wS_{\perp}\beta}+\widehat{N}_{2}\left(\frac{1}{z_{1}},\frac{1}{z_{2}}\right)g^{\beta\gamma}_{\perp}\epsilon^{P_{h}wS_{\perp}\alpha}-\widehat{N}_{2}\left(\frac{1}{z_{2}}-\frac{1}{z_{1}},\frac{1}{z_{2}}\right)g^{\alpha\beta}_{\perp}\epsilon^{P_{h}wS_{\perp}\gamma}\right),

(32)

and

\displaystyle\widehat{\Gamma}^{\alpha\beta\gamma}_{FS}\left(\frac{1}{z_{1}},\frac{1}{z_{2}}\right)=\frac{d_{abc}}{N^{2}-1}\sum_{X}\iint\frac{d\lambda d\mu}{(2\pi)^{2}}\mathrm{e}^{-i\frac{\lambda}{z_{1}}}\mathrm{e}^{-i\mu\left(\frac{1}{z_{2}}-\frac{1}{z_{1}}\right)}\langle 0|F^{w\beta}_{b}(0)|\Lambda^{\uparrow}X\rangle\langle\Lambda^{\uparrow}X|F^{w\alpha}_{a}(\lambda w)F^{w\gamma}_{c}(\mu w)|0\rangle{}

\displaystyle=-M_{h}\left(\widehat{O}_{1}\left(\frac{1}{z_{1}},\frac{1}{z_{2}}\right)g^{\alpha\gamma}_{\perp}\epsilon^{P_{h}wS_{\perp}\beta}+\widehat{O}_{2}\left(\frac{1}{z_{1}},\frac{1}{z_{2}}\right)g^{\beta\gamma}_{\perp}\epsilon^{P_{h}wS_{\perp}\alpha}+\widehat{O}_{2}\left(\frac{1}{z_{2}}-\frac{1}{z_{1}},\frac{1}{z_{2}}\right)g^{\alpha\beta}_{\perp}\epsilon^{P_{h}wS_{\perp}\gamma}\right),

where the support of each function is

and

Four functions,

\widehat{N}_{1,2}\left(\frac{1}{z_{1}},\frac{1}{z_{2}}\right)

\widehat{O}_{1,2}\left(\frac{1}{z_{1}},\frac{1}{z_{2}}\right)

are complex functions and their imaginary parts are T-odd,

\displaystyle\widehat{N}_{1}\left(\frac{1}{z_{1}},\frac{1}{z_{2}}\right)=-\widehat{N}_{1}\left(\frac{1}{z_{2}}-\frac{1}{z_{1}},\frac{1}{z_{2}}\right),

\displaystyle\widehat{O}_{1}\left(\frac{1}{z_{1}},\frac{1}{z_{2}}\right)=\widehat{O}_{1}\left(\frac{1}{z_{2}}-\frac{1}{z_{1}},\frac{1}{z_{2}}\right).

(34)

For the complete description of the hyperon polarization,
another type of dynamical FFs constructed from the gluon’s
field strength and the quark and anti-quark correlation functions are necessary:

6
Conclusion

In this paper we have investigated the consistency between the
collinear twist-3 FF contribution and the TMD polarizing FF contribution
at
\Lambda_{\rm QCD}\ll P_{T}\ll Q

for
ep\to e\Lambda^{\uparrow}X
in LO QCD.
Although the collinear twist-3 FFs give rise to all five
azimuthal asymmetries for this process, only
\sin\Phi_{S}
-asymmetry
survives in the leading inverse power of
P_{T}/Q
.
In the TMD factorization, the
\sin\Phi_{S}
-asymmetry is caused by the TMD polarizing FF.
Calculating the dependence of
the polarizing FF on the intrinsic transverse momentum
k_{\perp}

in perturbative QCD at
k_{\perp}\gg\Lambda_{\rm QCD}
,
the FF can be expressed in terms of
the collinear twist-3 FFs including purely gluonic ones.
Inserting this expression into the TMD factorization
formula, we found the result completely agrees with that obtained
as the small
P_{T}
-limit of the collinear twist-3 FF contribution to the cross section.
This indicates that the two frameworks describe the polarization
consistently in the whole kinematics region.
