7 U.S.C. § 518f — Limitation on total expenditures
Citation: us:usc:7:518f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section518f
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 21C (Tobacco Reform); Subchapter I (Transitional Payments To Tobacco Quota Holders And Producers Of Tobacco). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The total amount expended by the Secretary from the Tobacco Trust Fund to make payments under sections 518a and 518b of this title and for the other authorized purposes of the Fund shall not exceed $10,140,000,000.
