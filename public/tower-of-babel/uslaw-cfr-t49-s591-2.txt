49 C.F.R. § 591.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:591.2
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-591.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 591 (Importation Of Vehicles And Equipment Subject To Federal Safety, Bumper And Theft Prevention Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to ensure that motor vehicles and motor vehicle equipment permanently imported into the United States conform with theft prevention standards issued under part 541 of this chapter and that they conform with, or are brought into conformity with, all applicable Federal motor vehicle safety standards issued under part 571 of this chapter and bumper standards issued under part 581 of this chapter. The purpose of this part is also to ensure that nonconforming vehicles and equipment items imported on a temporary basis are ultimately either exported or abandoned to the United States.
