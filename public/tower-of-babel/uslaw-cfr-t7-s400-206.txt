7 C.F.R. § 400.206 — Financial qualifications for acceptability.
Citation: us:cfr:7:400.206
Creator: Federal Crop Insurance Corporation, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-400.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 400 (General Administrative Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The financial statement of an entity must show total allowable assets in excess of liabilities and the ability of the entity to meet current liabilities by the use of current assets.
