14 C.F.R. § 252.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:252.2
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 252 (Smoking Aboard Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to operations of air carriers engaged in interstate, intrastate and foreign air transportation and to foreign air carriers engaged in foreign air transportation.
