14 C.F.R. § 1221.204 — Proceedings of the NASA Incentive Awards Board.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1221.204
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1221.204
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1221 (The Nasa Seal And Other Devices, And The Congressional Space Medal Of Honor). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The NASA Incentive Awards Board shall thoroughly consider the facts giving rise to a recommendation for nomination and shall prepare a report for the Administrator. The Board should, to the extent practicable, coordinate its efforts with those of the astronaut's employing agency, as appropriate. Its final report must take into account any pertinent information submitted by the employing agency.
