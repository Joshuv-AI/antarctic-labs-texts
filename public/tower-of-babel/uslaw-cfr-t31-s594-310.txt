31 C.F.R. § 594.310 — Specially designated global terrorist; SDGT.
Citation: us:cfr:31:594.310
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-594.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 594 (Global Terrorism Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term specially designated global terrorist or SDGT means any person whose property and interests in property are blocked pursuant to § 594.201(a).
