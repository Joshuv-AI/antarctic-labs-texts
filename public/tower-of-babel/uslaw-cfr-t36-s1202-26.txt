36 C.F.R. § 1202.26 — Who will make sure that my record is accurate?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1202.26
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1202.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1202 (Regulations Implementing The Privacy Act Of 1974). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The system manager ensures that all records used by NARA to make a determination about any individual are maintained with such accuracy, relevancy, timeliness, and completeness as is reasonably possible to ensure fairness to you.
