49 C.F.R. § 625.41 — Standards for measuring the condition of capital assets.
Citation: us:cfr:49:625.41
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-625.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 625 (Transit Asset Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A capital asset is in a state of good repair if it meets the following objective standards—
(a) The capital asset is able to perform its designed function;
(b) The use of the asset in its current condition does not pose an identified unacceptable safety risk; and
(c) The life-cycle investment needs of the asset have been met or recovered, including all scheduled maintenance, rehabilitation, and replacements.
