12 U.S.C. § 245 — Vacancies during recess of Senate
Citation: us:usc:12:245
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section245
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 3 (Federal Reserve System); Subchapter II (Board Of Governors Of The Federal Reserve System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President shall have power to fill all vacancies that may happen on the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System during the recess of the Senate by granting commissions which shall expire with the next session of the Senate.
