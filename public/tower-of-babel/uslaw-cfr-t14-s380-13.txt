14 C.F.R. § 380.13 — Prohibition on sale of round trips with open returns.
Citation: us:cfr:14:380.13
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-380.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 380 (Public Charters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The charter operator shall not accept any participant's payment for return transportation unless the participant has specified a particular return flight.
