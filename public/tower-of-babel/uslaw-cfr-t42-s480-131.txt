42 C.F.R. § 480.131 — Access to medical records for the monitoring of QIOs.
Citation: us:cfr:42:480.131
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-480.131
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 480 (Acquisition, Protection, And Disclosure Of Quality Improvement Organization Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

CMS or any person, organization or agency authorized by the Department or Federal statute to monitor a QIO will have access to medical records maintained by institutions or health care practitioners on Medicare patients. The monitor can require copies of the records.
