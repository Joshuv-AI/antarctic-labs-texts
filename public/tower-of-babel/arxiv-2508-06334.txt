Production of 𝝅 + ​ 𝝅 − \pi^{+}\pi^{-} pairs in diffractive photon-proton and in proton-proton collisions revisited, in particular concerning the Drell-Söding contribution
Piotr Lebiedowicz, Otto Nachtmann, Antoni Szczurek

Abstract
We discuss the central exclusive photoproduction of π + ​ π − \pi^{+}\pi^{-} pairs in photon-proton and in proton-proton collisions at high energies. The ρ 0 \rho^{0} , ω \omega , f 2 ​ ( 1270 ) f_{2}(1270) , and non-resonant (Drell-Söding) contributions are considered. The calculation is based on the tensor-pomeron model that includes not only the dominant pomeron exchange but also reggeon and odderon exchanges. In the Drell-Söding contribution we have different subenergies for the π + ​ p \pi^{+}p and π − ​ p \pi^{-}p systems. In the method which we propose now we take this into account. Respecting the gauge-invariance constraints is then a nontrivial problem for which, however, we present a solution here. In this way we improve the corresponding calculations presented in JHEP 01, 151 (2015) and in Phys. Rev. D 91, 074023 (2015). The revised model leads to enhanced cross sections and gives an increased skewing of the ρ 0 \rho^{0} spectral shape. For the p ​ p → p ​ p ​ π + ​ π − pp\to pp\pi^{+}\pi^{-} reaction, we calculate differential cross sections as function of the two-pion invariant mass, pion transverse momentum and pion pseudorapidity. Predictions of proton-pion and proton-pion-pion invariant mass distributions and the distribution in the proton-proton four-momentum transfer squared are also presented. This research is relevant in the context of ALICE, ATLAS, CMS, and LHCb measurements in p ​ p pp collisions, even when the leading protons are not detected and instead only rapidity-gap conditions are checked experimentally. Our results can also serve as basis for the description of coherent π + ​ π − \pi^{+}\pi^{-} production in ultra-peripheral p p A and AA collisions at the LHC. The formulas given in our paper can directly be used for the analysis of photoproduction and small- Q 2 Q^{2} electroproduction in e ​ p ep collisions at high energies. Such data exist from the HERA experiments and will be obtained in the future at the electron-ion colliders.

In our present paper we shall use the framework of the

In detail:
the result given in (2.27) of
Bolz:2014mya

to the production of pointlike pions.
In reality pions are extended objects and a simple way to take

Here we follow this phenomenological way,

\gamma\gamma\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
to a separate study.
We get then

\displaystyle{\cal M}^{(\rm DS)}_{\mu,\mathfrak{s^{\prime}},\mathfrak{s}}(k_{1},k_{2},p^{\prime},q,p)|_{\gamma}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle e^{3}F_{M}(q^{2})\bigg[(q-2k_{1})_{\mu}(q-k_{1}+k_{2})_{\nu}\frac{1}{\hat{t}-m_{\pi}^{2}}

(2.42)

\displaystyle+(q-2k_{2})_{\mu}(q+k_{1}-k_{2})_{\nu}\frac{1}{\hat{u}-m_{\pi}^{2}}-2g_{\mu\nu}\bigg]F(\hat{t},\hat{u},\hat{s})\,F_{M}(t)\,\frac{1}{t}

\displaystyle\times\bar{u}_{\mathfrak{s^{\prime}}}(p^{\prime})\bigg[\gamma^{\nu}F_{1}(t)+\frac{i}{2m_{p}}\sigma^{\nu\lambda}(p^{\prime}-p)_{\lambda}F_{2}(t)\bigg]u_{\mathfrak{s}}(p)\,,

where

\displaystyle\hat{t}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(q-k_{1})^{2}\,

\displaystyle\hat{u}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(q-k_{2})^{2}\,

\displaystyle\hat{s}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(k_{1}+k_{2})^{2}=M_{\pi\pi}^{2}\,.

(2.43)

F_{M}(t)
is defined in (

),
and
F(\hat{t},\hat{u},\hat{s})
as

\displaystyle F(\hat{t},\hat{u},\hat{s})=\frac{[F(\hat{t})]^{2}+[F(\hat{u})]^{2}}{1+[\tilde{F}(\hat{s})]^{2}}

with the exponential parametrization

\displaystyle F(\hat{u})=\exp\left(\frac{\hat{u}-m_{\pi}^{2}}{\Lambda_{\pi}^{2}}\right)\,,

\displaystyle\tilde{F}(\hat{s})=\exp\left(\frac{-(\hat{s}-4m_{\pi}^{2})}{\Lambda_{\pi}^{2}}\right)\,.

\Lambda_{\pi}=1.7
GeV,
estimated from the comparison of the model results
for the
\gamma\gamma\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}

Boyer:1990vu
;
Behrend:1992hy
;
Heister:2003ae
;
Nakazawa:2004gu
;
Mori:2007bu

For the
\rho_{\mathbb{R}}
-exchange contribution
we find now with the methods of our present paper
the following results:

\displaystyle{\cal M}^{(\rm DS)}_{\mu,\mathfrak{s^{\prime}},\mathfrak{s}}(k_{1},k_{2},p^{\prime},q,p)|_{\rho_{\mathbb{R}}}={\cal M}_{\mu,\mathfrak{s^{\prime}},\mathfrak{s}}^{(a+b+c)}(k_{1},k_{2},p^{\prime},q,p)|_{\rho_{\mathbb{R}}}\,,

(2.46)

where

\displaystyle{\cal M}_{\mu,\mathfrak{s^{\prime}},\mathfrak{s}}^{(a)}(k_{1},k_{2},p^{\prime},q,p)|_{\rho_{\mathbb{R}}}=

\displaystyle\;e\left[\frac{(2k_{1}-q)_{\mu}}{-2k_{1}\cdot q+q^{2}+i\varepsilon}F_{M}(q^{2})-q_{\mu}\frac{1-F_{M}(q^{2})}{q^{2}}\right]{\cal M}_{\mathfrak{s^{\prime}},\mathfrak{s}}^{(0,\,a)}(k_{2},p^{\prime},q-k_{1},p)|_{\rho_{\mathbb{R}}}\,,

\displaystyle{\cal M}_{\mathfrak{s^{\prime}},\mathfrak{s}}^{(0,\,a)}(k_{2},p^{\prime},q-k_{1},p)|_{\rho_{\mathbb{R}}}=

\displaystyle\;{\cal F}_{\rho_{\mathbb{R}}\pi p}(2\bar{\nu},t)\left[1+(1-\alpha_{\rho_{\mathbb{R}}}(t))\frac{\varkappa}{2}\,g\left(\frac{1-\alpha_{\rho_{\mathbb{R}}}(t)}{2},\varkappa\right)\right](k_{2}-k_{1}+q)^{\nu}\bar{u}_{\mathfrak{s^{\prime}}}(p^{\prime})\gamma_{\nu}u_{\mathfrak{s}}(p)\,;

\displaystyle-e\left[\frac{(2k_{2}-q)_{\mu}}{-2k_{2}\cdot q+q^{2}+i\varepsilon}F_{M}(q^{2})-q_{\mu}\frac{1-F_{M}(q^{2})}{q^{2}}\right]{\cal M}_{\mathfrak{s^{\prime}},\mathfrak{s}}^{(0,\,b)}(k_{1},p^{\prime},q-k_{2},p)|_{\rho_{\mathbb{R}}}\,,

\displaystyle{\cal M}_{\mathfrak{s^{\prime}},\mathfrak{s}}^{(0,\,b)}(k_{1},p^{\prime},q-k_{2},p)|_{\rho_{\mathbb{R}}}=

\displaystyle\;{\cal F}_{\rho_{\mathbb{R}}\pi p}(2\bar{\nu},t)\left[1-(1-\alpha_{\rho_{\mathbb{R}}}(t))\frac{\varkappa}{2}\,g\left(\frac{1-\alpha_{\rho_{\mathbb{R}}}(t)}{2},-\varkappa\right)\right](k_{2}-k_{1}-q)^{\nu}\bar{u}_{\mathfrak{s^{\prime}}}(p^{\prime})\gamma_{\nu}u_{\mathfrak{s}}(p)\,;

\displaystyle{\cal M}_{\mu,\mathfrak{s^{\prime}},\mathfrak{s}}^{(c)}(k_{1},k_{2},p^{\prime},q,p)|_{\rho_{\mathbb{R}}}=

\displaystyle p(p_{a},\lambda_{a})+p(p_{b},\lambda_{b})\to p(p_{1},\lambda_{1})+\pi^{+}(p_{3})+\pi^{-}(p_{4})+p(p_{2},\lambda_{2})\,,

\displaystyle p_{34}

\displaystyle(p_{a}+q_{2})^{2}\,,

\displaystyle=

Eventually we should also

due to the proton-proton interactions to the Born amplitudes
discussed above.
How to treat approximately these effects was discussed
in Sec. III C of
Lebiedowicz:2014bea
.
The absorption reduces the cross section for photoproduction processes by about 10% at LHC energies.
In this work, the focus is on the non-resonant Drell-Söding term,
rather than a precise study of absorptive effects.
These effects depend on the kinematic conditions
in a particular experiment.
Therefore, in comparing our model results to experimental data,
these effects should be taken into account
at the amplitude level.

V
Conclusions

In the present paper we have proposed a new model for
the non-resonant (Drell-Söding, DS) contribution
to the reactions

\gamma^{(*)}p\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}p
and

pp\to pp\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
.
In this way we have improved the corresponding calculations
presented in

Bolz:2014mya
and
Lebiedowicz:2014bea
.
The calculations have been done using the tensor-pomeron approach
including the secondary reggeon exchanges.
We have discussed in detail our assumptions which we use
in the evaluation of the Drell-Söding term
in the above two reactions; see (
1.1
) and (
1.2
).
Our main improvement compared to
Bolz:2014mya
;
Lebiedowicz:2014bea
is best explained looking at Fig.
1
.
In Figs.
1
(a) and
1
(b) we have Regge exchanges
with different subenergies squared
s_{2}
, and
s_{1}
, respectively.
This leads to different Regge factors, where, in fact,
we replace
s_{1,2}
by the correct Regge variables
2\nu_{1,2}

which are defined in (
A3
).
We determined the result for the diagram of Fig.
1
(c)
from the gauge-invariance condition.
This turned out to be a nontrivial task.
Then we have given the complete set of amplitudes,
resonant and nonresonant, for the

\gamma^{(*)}p\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}p
reaction
in Sec.
II.2
and for the

pp\to pp\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
reaction in Sec.
III
.
In Sec.
IV
we have given numerical results
for the
pp\to pp\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
reaction
at c.m. energy
\sqrt{s}=13
TeV.
The improved two-pion continuum model
for the
pp\to pp\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
reaction
gives a larger cross section
for the DS contribution
by a factor of about 3.5 compared to the previous one
obtained in
Lebiedowicz:2014bea
.
As a result, the interference effect, arising from
the resonant
\rho(770)
and the two-pion continuum contributions,
is more pronounced
and leads to larger skewing of the observed
spectral shape of
\rho^{0}
.
Our findings should be important for the ongoing and planned
measurements of the
pp\to pp\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
reaction by
the ALICE, ATLAS, CMS, and LHCb Collaborations at the LHC, even
when the leading protons are not detected
and only rapidity-gap conditions are checked.
Our findings are also important for the resonant (
\rho^{0}\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}
) and non-resonant dipion production
in ultra-peripheral
p
A and AA collisions.

Finally we emphasize that a detailed comparison of our improved model
for real photoproduction

\gamma p\to\pi^{+}\pi^{-}p

with the corresponding HERA data
H1:2020lzc

would be most welcome.
This can be done by the corresponding experimentalists.
A comparison of theory and experiment for the total cross section,
the two-pion invariant mass distribution, and other
differential cross sections,
would allow to get precise numbers for our model parameters.
These are in essence the intercepts and slope parameters
of the pomeron and reggeons and their couplings
to the proton and the pions.
Most of these parameters,
except for those connected with the odderon,
are already quite well known from other reactions;
see for instance
Donnachie:2002
;
Ewerz:2013kda
.
With the experimental study of the reactions considered by us
here we shall, thus, get a detailed check
of the basic structures of the tensor-pomeron model.
The production of
f_{2}
decaying to
\pi^{+}\pi^{-}

can occur by photon plus odderon fusion,
and thus offers the chance to detect and study odderon effects.
All these topics can also be studied in the future at
the electron-ion colliders, e.g.,
EIC
Accardi:2012qut
;
Aschenauer:2017jsk
;
AbdulKhalek:2021gbh
;
Anderle:2021wcy

and LHeC
LHeCStudyGroup:2012zhm
;
Bordry:2018gri
.

Appendix A
Kinematic relations, propagators, and vertices

We start with kinematic relations for the amplitude (
2.3
).
We have energy-momentum conservation

\displaystyle q+p=k_{1}+k_{2}+p^{\prime}

(A1)

and we define the following variables:

\displaystyle s

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(q+p)^{2}=(k_{1}+k_{2}+p^{\prime})^{2}\,,

\displaystyle t

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(p-p^{\prime})^{2}=(q-k_{1}-k_{2})^{2}\,,

\displaystyle s_{1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(p^{\prime}+k_{1})^{2}=(p+q-k_{2})^{2}\,,

\displaystyle u_{1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(p-k_{1})^{2}=(p^{\prime}-q+k_{2})^{2}\,,

\displaystyle s_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(p^{\prime}+k_{2})^{2}=(p+q-k_{1})^{2}\,,

\displaystyle u_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(p-k_{2})^{2}=(p^{\prime}-q+k_{1})^{2}\,,

\displaystyle M_{\pi\pi}^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(k_{1}+k_{2})^{2}=(p-p^{\prime}+q)^{2}\,;

(A2)

\displaystyle\nu_{1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}(s_{1}-u_{1})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}\left[(p+p^{\prime},k_{1}-k_{2})+(p+p^{\prime},q)\right]\,,

\displaystyle\nu_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}(s_{2}-u_{2})

(A3)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{4}\left[(p+p^{\prime},k_{2}-k_{1})+(p+p^{\prime},q)\right]\,.

We have

\displaystyle\nu_{1}^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{16}[(p+p^{\prime},q)^{2}+(p+p^{\prime},k_{1}-k_{2})^{2}+2(p+p^{\prime},k_{1}-k_{2})(p+p^{\prime},q)]\,,

\displaystyle\nu_{2}^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{16}[(p+p^{\prime},q)^{2}+(p+p^{\prime},k_{1}-k_{2})^{2}-2(p+p^{\prime},k_{1}-k_{2})(p+p^{\prime},q)]\,,

(A4)

and we define

\displaystyle\bar{\nu}^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(\nu_{1}^{2}+\nu_{2}^{2})\,,

\displaystyle\varkappa

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{2(q,p+p^{\prime})(p+p^{\prime},k_{1}-k_{2})}{16\bar{\nu}^{2}}\,.

(A5)

Note that
\nu_{1}^{2}
and
\nu_{2}^{2}
differ
only in the sign of the term which is linear in
q
.
This is important for our calculations.

From (
A2
) and (
A3
) we find

\displaystyle\nu_{1}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(p+p^{\prime},k_{1})\geqslant m_{p}m_{\pi}>0\,,

\displaystyle\nu_{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1}{2}(p+p^{\prime},k_{2})\geqslant m_{p}m_{\pi}>0\,.

(A6)

We have

\displaystyle|\varkappa|

\displaystyle\leqslant

\displaystyle 1\,,

(A7)

\displaystyle 16\nu_{1}^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 16\bar{\nu}^{2}(1+\varkappa)\,,

\displaystyle 16\nu_{2}^{2}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 16\bar{\nu}^{2}(1-\varkappa)\,.

(A8)

For real
\lambda
we have

\displaystyle(16\nu_{2}^{2})^{-\lambda}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(16\bar{\nu}^{2})^{-\lambda}+(16\nu_{2}^{2})^{-\lambda}-(16\bar{\nu}^{2})^{-\lambda}

(A9)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(16\bar{\nu}^{2})^{-\lambda}\left[1+(1-\varkappa)^{\lambda}-1\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(16\bar{\nu}^{2})^{-\lambda}\left[1+\lambda\varkappa\,g(\lambda,\varkappa)\right]\,,

where we define

\displaystyle g(\lambda,\varkappa)=\frac{(1-\varkappa)^{-\lambda}-1}{\lambda\varkappa}\,.

(A10)

For
|\varkappa|<1
we can expand
g(\lambda,\varkappa)

in a power series in
\varkappa
which shows that there

g(\lambda,\varkappa)
is an analytic function in
\varkappa

for all complex and real values of
\lambda
:

\displaystyle g(\lambda,\varkappa)=1+\frac{\lambda+1}{2!}\varkappa+\frac{(\lambda+1)(\lambda+2)}{3!}\varkappa^{2}+\ldots\,.

(A11)

Inserting the explicit expression for
\varkappa
from (
A5
)
in (
A9
) we get

\displaystyle(16\nu_{2}^{2})^{-\lambda}=(16\bar{\nu}^{2})^{-\lambda}\left[1+\lambda\varkappa\,g(\lambda,\varkappa)\right]=(16\bar{\nu}^{2})^{-\lambda}\left[1+\lambda\frac{2(q,p+p^{\prime})(p+p^{\prime},k_{1}-k_{2})}{16\bar{\nu}^{2}}g(\lambda,\varkappa)\right].

(A12)

In a completely analogous way we get

\displaystyle(16\nu_{1}^{2})^{-\lambda}=(16\bar{\nu}^{2})^{-\lambda}\left[1-\lambda\frac{2(q,p+p^{\prime})(p+p^{\prime},k_{1}-k_{2})}{16\bar{\nu}^{2}}g(\lambda,-\varkappa)\right].

(A13)

Next we list and discuss propagators and vertices which we need
for our work.
We use the framework of QCD plus electromagnetism to the lowest relevant order.
Thus, all our propagators and vertices respect
the symmetries:
parity (
P
),
charge conjugation (
C
),
time reversal (
T
),
and
\Theta=CPT
.

(A19)

\displaystyle\Delta_{F}(k^{\prime 2})\,\widehat{\Gamma}_{\lambda}^{(\gamma\pi\pi)}(k^{\prime},k)|_{k^{2}=m_{\pi}^{2}}

\displaystyle\frac{(k^{\prime}+k)_{\lambda}}{k^{\prime 2}-m_{\pi}^{2}+i\varepsilon}-\left[(k^{\prime}+k)_{\lambda}q^{\prime 2}-q^{\prime}_{\lambda}(k^{\prime 2}-m_{\pi}^{2})\right]

\displaystyle k^{\prime}-k\,.

From (

\displaystyle\widehat{\Gamma}_{\lambda}^{(\gamma\pi\pi)}(k^{\prime},k)|_{k^{2}=m_{\pi}^{2}}

\displaystyle(k^{\prime}+k)_{\lambda}\left[1-q^{\prime 2}\,\widetilde{B}(k^{\prime 2}-m_{\pi}^{2},0,q^{\prime 2})+(k^{\prime 2}-m_{\pi}^{2})\,C(k^{\prime 2}-m_{\pi}^{2})\right]

Specializing in (

\displaystyle\widehat{\Gamma}_{\lambda}^{(\gamma\pi\pi)}(k^{\prime},k)|_{k^{2}=k^{\prime 2}=m_{\pi}^{2}}

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(k^{\prime}+k)_{\lambda}\left[1-q^{\prime 2}\,\widetilde{B}(0,0,q^{\prime 2})\right]

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle(k^{\prime}+k)_{\lambda}\,F_{\rm em}^{(\pi)}(q^{\prime 2})\,,

\displaystyle F_{\rm em}^{(\pi)}(q^{\prime 2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle 1-q^{\prime 2}\,\widetilde{B}(0,0,q^{\prime 2})\,,

(A24)

\displaystyle\widetilde{B}(0,0,q^{\prime 2})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{1-F_{\rm em}^{(\pi)}(q^{\prime 2})}{q^{\prime 2}}\,.

(A25)

For our calculations of the Drell-Söding term

\displaystyle(i=1,2)\,.
