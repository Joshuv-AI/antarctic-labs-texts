34 U.S.C. § 10425 — Evaluation
Citation: us:usc:34:10425
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10425
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XVIII (Residential Substance Abuse Treatment For State Prisoners). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each State that receives a grant under this subchapter shall submit to the Attorney General an evaluation not later than March 1 of each year in such form and containing such information as the Attorney General may reasonably require.
