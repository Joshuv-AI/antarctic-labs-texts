40 C.F.R. § 174.539 — Cry51Aa2.834__16 protein; exemption from the requirement of a tolerance.
Citation: us:cfr:40:174.539
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-174.539
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 174 (Procedures And Requirements For Plant-Incorporated Protectants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Residues of the Cry51Aa2.834__16 protein, which is a modified protein derived from the Cry51Aa2 protein of Bacillus thuringiensis, in or on cotton are exempt from the requirement of a tolerance, when the Cry51Aa2.834__16 protein is used as a plant-incorporated protectant.
