24 C.F.R. § 0.1 — Cross-reference to employees ethical conduct standards and financial disclosure regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:24:0.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-0.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 0 (Standards Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees of the Department of Housing and Urban Development (Department) are subject to the executive branch-wide standards of ethical conduct at 5 CFR part 2635, the Department's regulation at 5 CFR part 7501 which supplements the executive branch-wide standards, and the executive branch-wide financial disclosure regulation at 5 CFR part 2634.
