43 C.F.R. § 3102.53 — Evidence of compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3102.53
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3102.53
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3100 (Oil And Gas Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authorized officer may request at any time further evidence of compliance and qualification from any party holding or seeking to hold an interest in a lease. Failure to comply with the request of the authorized officer will result in adjudication of the action based on the incomplete submission.
