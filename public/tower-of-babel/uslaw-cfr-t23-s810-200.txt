23 C.F.R. § 810.200 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:810.200
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-810.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 810 (Mass Transit And Special Use Highway Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this subpart is to implement 23 U.S.C. 142(g), which permits the Federal Highway Administrator to authorize a State to make available to a publicly-owned mass transit authority existing highway rights-of-way for rail or other non-highway public mass transit facilities.
