7 C.F.R. § 981.450 — Exempt dispositions.
Citation: us:cfr:7:981.450
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-981.450
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 981 (Almonds Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As provided in § 981.50, any handler disposing of almonds for crushing into oil, or for animal feed, may have the kernel weight of these almonds excluded from their program obligations, so long as:
(a) The handler qualifies as, or delivers such almonds to, a Board-approved accepted user;
(b) Each delivery is made directly to the accepted user by June 30 of each crop year; and
(c) Each delivery is certified to the Board by the handler on ABC Form 8.
