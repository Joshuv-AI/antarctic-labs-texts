48 C.F.R. § 846.370-1 — 846.370-1 Rejected goods.
Citation: us:cfr:48:846.370-1
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-846.370-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 846 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 852.246-71, Rejected Goods, in solicitations and contracts for the acquisition of supplies, equipment or perishable goods. Perishable goods include such items as packing house and dairy products, bread and bakery products, fresh and frozen fruits, and vegetables.
