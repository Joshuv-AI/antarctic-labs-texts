8 C.F.R. § 274.2 — Delegation of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:8:274.2
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-274.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 274 (Seizure And Forfeiture Of Conveyances). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All powers provided to Fines, Penalties and Forfeitures Officers in 19 CFR parts 162 and 171 are provided to the Chief, Office of Border Patrol or his designees, for purposes of administering seizures and forfeitures made by Border Patrol Officers.
