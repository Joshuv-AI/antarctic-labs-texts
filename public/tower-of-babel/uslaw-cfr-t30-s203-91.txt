30 C.F.R. § 203.91 — What is in a post-production development report?
Citation: us:cfr:30:203.91
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-203.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 203 (Relief Or Reduction In Royalty Rates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For each cost category in the deep water cost report, you must compare actual costs up to the date when production starts to your planned pre-production costs. If your application included more than one development scenario, you need to compare actual costs with those in your scenario of most likely development. Also, you must have this report certified by an independent CPA according to § 203.81(c).
