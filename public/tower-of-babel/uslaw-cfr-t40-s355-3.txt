40 C.F.R. § 355.3 — Which section contains the definitions of the key words used in this part?
Citation: us:cfr:40:355.3
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-355.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 355 (Emergency Planning And Notification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The definitions of key words used in this part are in § 355.61. It is important to read the definitions for these key words because the definition explains the word's specific meaning associated with the regulations in this part.
