34 U.S.C. § 10464 — Regulations or guidelines
Citation: us:usc:34:10464
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10464
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XX (Grants To Improve The Criminal Justice Response And Enforcement Of Protection Orders). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 120 days after September 13, 1994, the Attorney General shall publish proposed regulations or guidelines implementing this subchapter. Not later than 180 days after September 13, 1994, the Attorney General shall publish final regulations or guidelines implementing this subchapter.
