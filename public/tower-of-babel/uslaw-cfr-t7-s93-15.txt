7 C.F.R. § 93.15 — Fees for analytical testing of oilseeds.
Citation: us:cfr:7:93.15
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-93.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 93 (Processed Fruits And Vegetables). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The fee charged for any laboratory analysis for oilseeds shall be obtained from the Laboratory Director for aflatoxin laboratories at the Dothan administrative office as listed in 7 CFR 93.14(a).
