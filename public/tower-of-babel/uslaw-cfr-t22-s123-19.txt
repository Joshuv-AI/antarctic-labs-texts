22 C.F.R. § 123.19 — Canadian and Mexican border shipments.
Citation: us:cfr:22:123.19
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-123.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 123 (Licenses For The Export And Temporary Import Of Defense Articles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A shipment originating in Canada or Mexico which incidentally transits the United States en route to a delivery point in the same country that originated the shipment is exempt from the requirement for an in transit license.
