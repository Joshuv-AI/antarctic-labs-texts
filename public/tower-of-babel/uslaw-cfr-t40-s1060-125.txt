40 C.F.R. § 1060.125 — What maintenance instructions must I give to buyers?
Citation: us:cfr:40:1060.125
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1060.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1060 (Control Of Evaporative Emissions From New And In-Use Nonroad And Stationary Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Give ultimate purchasers written instructions for properly maintaining and using the emission control system. You may not specify any maintenance more frequently than once per year. For example, if you produce cold-weather equipment that requires replacement of fuel cap gaskets or O-rings, provide clear instructions to the ultimate purchaser, including the required replacement interval.
