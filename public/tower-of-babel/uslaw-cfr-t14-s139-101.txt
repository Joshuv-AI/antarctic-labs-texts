14 C.F.R. § 139.101 — General requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:14:139.101
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-139.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 139 (Certification Of Airports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except as otherwise authorized by the Administrator, no person may operate an airport specified under § 139.1 of this part without an Airport Operating Certificate or in violation of that certificate, the applicable provisions, or the approved Airport Certification Manual.
(b) Each certificate holder shall adopt and comply with an Airport Certification Manual as required under § 139.203.
