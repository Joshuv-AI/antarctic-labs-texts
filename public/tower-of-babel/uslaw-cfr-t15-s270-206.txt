15 C.F.R. § 270.206 — Public briefings and requests for information.

Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-270.206
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 270 (National Construction Safety Teams). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) NIST will establish methods to provide updates to the public on its planning and progress of an investigation. Methods may include:
(1) A public Web site;
(2) Mailing lists, to include an emphasis on e-mail;
(3) Semi-annual written progress reports;
(4) Media briefings; and
(5) Public meetings.
(b) Requests for information on the plans and conduct of an investigation should be submitted to the NIST Public and Business Affairs Division.
