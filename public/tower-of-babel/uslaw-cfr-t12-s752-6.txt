12 C.F.R. § 752.6 — What are the types of applications that can be filed?
Citation: us:cfr:12:752.6
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-752.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 752 (Consent To Service Of Persons Convicted Of, Or Who Have Program Entries For, Certain Criminal Offenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The NCUA will accept applications from—
(1) An individual; or
(2) An insured credit union applying on behalf of an individual.
(b) An individual or an insured credit union may file applications at separate times. Under either approach, the application(s) must be filed with the appropriate NCUA field office, as required by this part.
