43 C.F.R. § 3400.3-2 — -2 Department of Defense lands.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3400.3-2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3400.3-2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3400 (Coal Management: General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may issue leases with the consent of the Secretary of Defense on acquired lands set apart for military or naval purposes only if the leases are issued to a governmental entity which:
(a) Produces electrical energy for sale to the public;
(b) Is located in the state in which the leased lands are located; and
(c) Has production facilities in that state, and will use the coal produced from the lease within that state.
