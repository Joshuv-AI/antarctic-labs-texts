33 U.S.C. § 3401 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:33:3401
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section3401
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 47 (Ocean Exploration); Subchapter I (Exploration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this subchapter is to establish the national ocean exploration program within the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration.
