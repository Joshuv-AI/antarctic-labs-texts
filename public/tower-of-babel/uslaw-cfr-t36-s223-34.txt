36 C.F.R. § 223.34 — Advance payment.
Citation: us:cfr:36:223.34
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-223.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 223 (Sale And Disposal Of National Forest System Timber). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sale contracts shall provide that timber and forest products be paid for in advance of cutting, unless the contract authorizes the purchaser to furnish a payment guarantee satisfactory to the Forest Service. Advance payments found to be in excess of amounts due the United States shall be refunded to the current holder of the contract or to successors in interest. (90 Stat. 2959; 16 U.S.C. 472a.)
