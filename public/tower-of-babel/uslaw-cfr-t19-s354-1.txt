19 C.F.R. § 354.1 — Scope.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 354 (Procedures For Imposing Sanctions For Violation Of An Antidumping Or Countervailing Duty Administrative Protective Order). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the procedures for imposing sanctions for violation of an administrative protective order issued under 19 CFR 351.306, or successor regulations, as authorized by 19 U.S.C. 1677f(c).
