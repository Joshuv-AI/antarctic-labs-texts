8 C.F.R. § 265.1 — Reporting change of address.
Citation: us:cfr:8:265.1
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-265.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 265 (Notices Of Address). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except for those exempted by section 263(b) of the Act, all aliens in the United States required to register under section 262 of the Act must report each change of address and new address within 10 days of such change in accordance with instructions provided by USCIS.
