9 C.F.R. § 151.8 — Eligibility of an animal for certification.
Citation: us:cfr:9:151.8
Creator: Animal And Plant Health Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-151.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 151 (Recognition Of Breeds And Books Of Record Of Purebred Animals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be eligible for certification under the act, an animal must be purebred of a recognized breed and have been registered in good faith in a book of record listed in § 151.9 and must not have been registered on inspection without regard to purity of breeding.
