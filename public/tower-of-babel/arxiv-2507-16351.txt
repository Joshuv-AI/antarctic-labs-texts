Introduction

Turán-type problem is determining the maximum number of edges in a hereditary family of graphs, which is one of the most central topics in extremal graph theory. Let
\mathrm{ex}(n,H)
denote the maximum number of edges among all
n
-vertex
H
-free graphs. In 1941, Turán
[
16
]
gave the exact value of
\mathrm{ex}(n,K_{r})
and characterized the only extremal graph is the balanced complete
(r-1)
-partite graph, where
K_{r}
is a complete graph with
r
vertices. Later in 1946, Erdős and Stone
[
4
]
generalized this result asymtotically by proving
\mathrm{ex}(n,H)=(1-\frac{1}{\chi(H)-1}+o(1))\binom{n}{2}
for an arbitrary graph
H
, where
\chi(H)
denotes the chromatic number of
H
.

In 2016, Dowden
[
3
]
initiated the study of Turán-type problems when host graphs are planar graphs. We use
\mathrm{ex}_{\mathcal{P}}(n,H)
to denote the maximum number of edges among all
n
-vertex
H
-free planar graphs. Dowden studied the planar Turán numbers of
C_{4}
and
C_{5}
, where
C_{k}
is a cycle with
k
vertices. Ghosh, Győri, Martin, Paulos and Xiao
[
6
]
gave the exact value for
C_{6}
. Shi, Walsh and Yu
[
15
]
, and Győri, Li and Zhou
[
8
]
gave the exact value for
C_{7}
, independently. Until now, the planar Turán number of
C_{k}
is still unknown for
k\geq 8
.

Cranston, Lidický, Liu and Shantanam
[
2
]
first gave the lower bound for general cycles as
\mathrm{ex}_{\mathcal{P}}(n,C_{k})>\frac{3(k-1)}{k}n-\frac{6(k+1)}{k}
for
k\geq 11
and sufficiently large
n
. Lan and Song
[
11
]
improved the lower bound to
\mathrm{ex}_{\mathcal{P}}(n,C_{k})>(3-\frac{3-\frac{2}{k-1}}{k-6+\lfloor\frac{k-1}{2}\rfloor})n+c_{k}
for
n\geq k\geq 11
, where
c_{k}
is a constant. Recently, Győri, Varga and Zhu
[
7
]
improved the lower bound by giving a new construction to show that
\mathrm{ex}_{\mathcal{P}}(n,C_{k})\geq 3n-6-\frac{6\cdot 3^{\log_{2}3}n}{k^{\log_{2}3}}
for
k\geq 7
and sufficiently large
n
. Shi, Walsh and Yu
[
14
]
gave an upper bound and proved that
\mathrm{ex}_{\mathcal{P}}(n,C_{k})\leq 3n-6-\frac{n}{4k^{\log_{2}3}}
for all
n,k\geq 4
.

We use
tC_{k}
to denote the union of
t
vertex-disjoint
k
-cycles and
t\mathcal{C}
to denote the union of
t
vertex-disjoint cycles without length restrictions. Lan, Shi and Song
[
9
]
showed that
\mathrm{ex}_{\mathcal{P}}(n,t\mathcal{C})=3n-6
for

and the extremal graph is a maximal planar graph, which also indicates that
\mathrm{ex}_{\mathcal{P}}(n,tC_{k})=3n-6
for
t,k\geq 3
. Later, Fang, Lin and Shi
[
5
]
proved that
\mathrm{ex}_{\mathcal{P}}(n,2\mathcal{C})=2n-1
. For the planar Turán number of two vertex-disjoint cycles under length restrictions, Lan, Shi and Song
[
10
]
proved that
\mathrm{ex}_{\mathcal{P}}(n,2C_{3})=\lceil 5n/2\rceil-5
for
n\geq 6
. Li
[
12
]
proved that
\mathrm{ex}_{\mathcal{P}}(n,C_{3}\cup C_{4})=\lfloor 5n/2\rfloor-4
for

, where
C_{3}\cup C_{4}
denotes the union of two vertex-disjoint cycles
C_{3}
and
C_{4}
. Fang, Lin and Shi
[
5

proved that for
n\geq 2661

\mathrm{ex}_{\mathcal{P}}(n,2C_{4})=19n/7-6
if

proved that

Let
