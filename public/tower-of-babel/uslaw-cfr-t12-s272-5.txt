12 C.F.R. § 272.5 — Notice and public procedure.
Citation: us:cfr:12:272.5
Creator: Federal Reserve System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-272.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 272 (Rules Of Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There ordinarily is no published notice of proposed action by the Committee or public procedure thereon, as described in section 553 of title 5 of the United States Code, because such notice and procedure are impracticable, unnecessary, or contrary to the public interest.
