45 C.F.R. § 2551.113 — What financial obligation does AmeriCorps incur for non-AmeriCorps funded projects?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2551.113
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2551.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2551 (Senior Companion Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Issuance of an NGA to a sponsor of a non-AmeriCorps funded project does not create a financial obligation on the part of AmeriCorps for any costs associated with the project.
