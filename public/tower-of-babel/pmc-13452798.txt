Author Correction: Clinically-guided models or foundation models? predicting cervical spondylotic myelopathy from electronic health records
Authors: Salim Yakdan, Ben Warner, Zoher Ghogawala, Wilson Z. Ray, Mohamad Bydon, Michael P. Steinmetz, Richard T. Griffey, Randi Foraker, Adam Wilcox, Chenyang Lu, Jacob K. Greenberg
Published: 2026

Correction to: npj Digital Medicine 10.1038/s41746-026-02337-7, published online 20 January 2026

Authors identified several preprocessing issues with the cohort-control lists that led to errors in the reported population counts and some model performance metrics, including meaningful drops in AURPRC. Notably, these updated results do not affect the general conclusions of the paper related to the greater generalizability of clinically-guided models compared to foundation models. Additionally, this oversight did not impact any of the demographic characteristics reported in the manuscript, which reflect results from the test dataset which was not impacted by the error. These have been corrected. The Discussion section has also been revised (first and fifth paragraphs) to clarify the interpretation of the corrected results and to ensure that the discussion accurately reflects the updated analyses.
