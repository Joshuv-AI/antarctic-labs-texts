32 C.F.R. § 2002.6 — CUI Executive Agent (EA).
Citation: us:cfr:32:2002.6
Creator: Information Security Oversight Office, National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-2002.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 2002 (Controlled Unclassified Information (Cui)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Section 2(c) of the Order designates NARA as the CUI Executive Agent (EA) to implement the Order and to oversee agency efforts to comply with the Order, this part, and the CUI Registry.
(b) NARA has delegated the CUI EA responsibilities to the Director of ISOO. Under this authority, ISOO staff carry out CUI oversight responsibilities and manage the Federal CUI program.
