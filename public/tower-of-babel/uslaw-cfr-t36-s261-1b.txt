36 C.F.R. § 261.1b — b Penalty.
Citation: us:cfr:36:261.1b
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-261.1b
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 261 (Prohibitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless otherwise provided by law, the punishment for violation of any prohibition in or order issued under this part shall be imprisonment of not more than six months or a fine in accordance with the applicable provisions of 18 U.S.C. 3571 or both.
