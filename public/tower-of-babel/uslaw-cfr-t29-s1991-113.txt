29 C.F.R. § 1991.113 — Judicial enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1991.113
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1991.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1991 (Procedures For The Handling Of Retaliation Complaints Under The Criminal Antitrust Anti-Retaliation Act (Caara)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever any person has failed to comply with a preliminary order of reinstatement or a final order issued by the Secretary under CAARA, including one approving a settlement agreement, the Secretary or the person on whose behalf the order was issued may file a civil action seeking enforcement of the order in the United States district court for the district in which the violation was found to have occurred.
