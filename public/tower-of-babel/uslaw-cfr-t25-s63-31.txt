25 C.F.R. § 63.31 — Can both the Bureau of Indian Affairs and tribes operate Indian child protection and family violence prevention programs?
Citation: us:cfr:25:63.31
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-63.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 63 (Indian Child Protection And Family Violence Prevention). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. However, tribes are encouraged to develop and operate programs to protect Indian children and reduce the incidence of family violence in Indian country.
