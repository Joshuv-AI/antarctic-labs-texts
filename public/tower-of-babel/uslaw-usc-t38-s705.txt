38 U.S.C. § 705 — Telephone service for medical officers and facility directors
Citation: us:usc:38:705
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section705
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part I (General Provisions); Chapter 7 (Employees); Subchapter I (General Employee Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may pay for official telephone service and rental in the field whenever incurred in case of official telephones for directors of centers, hospitals, independent clinics, domiciliaries, and medical officers of the Department where such telephones are installed in private residences or private apartments or quarters, when authorized under regulations prescribed by the Secretary.
