32 C.F.R. § 724.108 — Administrative discharge.
Citation: us:cfr:32:724.108
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-724.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 724 (Naval Discharge Review Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A discharge upon expiration of enlistment or required period of service, or prior thereto, in a manner prescribed by the Commandant of the Marine Corps or the Commander, Naval Personnel Command, but specifically excluding separation by sentence of a general court-martial.
