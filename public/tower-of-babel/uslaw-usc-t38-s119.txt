38 U.S.C. § 119 — Contracting for statistical analyses and data evaluation
Citation: us:usc:38:119
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section119
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part I (General Provisions); Chapter 1 (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
The Secretary may enter into a contract or other agreement with an academic institution or other qualified entity, as determined by the Secretary, to carry out statistical analyses and data evaluation as required of the Secretary by law.”.1
(b) Rule of Construction.—
Nothing in this section may be construed to limit the authority of the Secretary to enter into contracts or other agreements for statistical analyses and data evaluation under any other provision of law.
