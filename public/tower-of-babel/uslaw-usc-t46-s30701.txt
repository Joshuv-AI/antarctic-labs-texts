46 U.S.C. § 30701 — Definition
Citation: us:usc:46:30701
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section30701
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 307 (Liability Of Water Carriers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “carrier” means the owner, manager, charterer, agent, or master of a vessel.
