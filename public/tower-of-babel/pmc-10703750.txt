Correction to: Epidemiological Study Regarding the Incidence of Venous Thromboembolism in Patients After Cancer Remission
Authors: Miki Imura, Jun Katada, Taro Shiga
Published: 2023

Correction to: Cardiol Ther (2022) 11:611–623 10.1007/s40119-022-00285-3

Authors would like to correct the typos and missing of reference in this publication as listed here.

In the Table 2, the title for “Incidence” was mistyped as “per person-years”. It should have been “% per year” instead.

In the Fig. 2, proportion of VTE-free patients on the vertical axis was mistyped as “0.895, 0.890”. It should have been “0.985, 0.980”.

In the Fig. 3c, there was a blank of cancer type. The fourth curve from the top indicates “Lung”.

On page 9, line 14, the reference was missing: Horsted F, West J, Grainge MJ. Risk of venous thromboembolism in patients with cancer: a systematic review and meta-analysis. PLoS Med. 2012;9(7):e1001275.

The original article has been corrected.
