29 C.F.R. § 553.233 — “Regular rate” defined.
Citation: us:cfr:29:553.233
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-553.233
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 553 (Application Of The Fair Labor Standards Act To Employees Of State And Local Governments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rules for computing an employee's “regular rate”, for purposes of the Act's overtime pay requirements, are set forth in part 778 of this title. These rules are applicable to employees for whom the section 7(k) exemption is claimed when overtime compensation is provided in cash wages. However, wherever the word “workweek” is used in part 778, the words “work period” should be substituted.
