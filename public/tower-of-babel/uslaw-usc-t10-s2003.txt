10 U.S.C. § 2003 — Aeronautical rating as pilot: qualifications
Citation: us:usc:10:2003
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section2003
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Training And Education); Chapter 101 (Training Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To be eligible to receive an aeronautical rating as a pilot in the Army or Air Force or be designated as a naval aviator, a member of an armed force must successfully complete an undergraduate pilot course of instruction prescribed or approved by the Secretary of his military department.
