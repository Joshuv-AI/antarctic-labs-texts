20 U.S.C. § 1721 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:20:1721
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1721
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 39 (Equal Educational Opportunities And Transportation Of Students); Subchapter I (Equal Educational Opportunities); Part 6 (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this subchapter or of any amendment made by this subchapter, or the application of any such provision to any person or circumstance, is held invalid, the remainder of the provisions of this subchapter and of the amendments made by this subchapter and the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected thereby.
