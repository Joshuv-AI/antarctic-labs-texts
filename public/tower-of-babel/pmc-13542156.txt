Correction: Diversity, richness, and species composition of fruit flies with new and first records from Meghalaya, India
Authors: Kennedy Ningthoujam, K J David, Sunita Chetry, Hissay Cheday Bhutia, Bihari Mohapatra Niladri, Kisan Rymbai, Jyotim Gogoi, Deepika Sorahia, Chada Anu Reddy, Pradeep Kumar Chandra, Mareena Sorokhaibam, Khaba Moirangthem, Mahesh Pathak, Uma Ngangom, Lourembam Sanajaoba, K M Ajaykumara, Bimal Kumar Sahoo, Yanglem Herojit Singh
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-56503-8, published online 08 June 2026

The original version of the Article contained errors. In the original version of this Article Jyotim Gogoi was omitted as a corresponding author. Correspondence and requests for materials should also be addressed to jyotimgogoi@gmail.com.

In addition, in the Materials and methods section under the subheading 1) Alpha diversity:, the indices referenced were inadvertently attributed to spiders. As a result,

“The following indices were worked out to assess the diversity and distribution of spiders in the observed ecosystems.”

now reads:

“The following indices were worked out to assess the diversity and distribution of fruit flies in the observed ecosystems.”

Finally, in the Result and discussion section under the subheading Principal component analysis, percentages reported for Dim1 and Dim2 do not match with Figure 12 as referenced. As a result,

Dim1 (11.1%) and Dim2 (9.8%) together explained 20.93% of total variance, revealing partial ecosystem separation typical of diverse insect communities (Fig. 12).

now reads:

Dim1 (11.2%) and Dim2 (10.6%) together explained 21.8% of total variance, revealing partial ecosystem separation typical of diverse insect communities (Fig. 12).

The original Article has been corrected.
