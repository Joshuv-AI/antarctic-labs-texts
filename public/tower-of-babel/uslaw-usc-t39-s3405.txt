39 U.S.C. § 3405 — Markings
Citation: us:usc:39:3405
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title39-section3405
Context: United States Code, Title 39 (Postal Service); Part IV (Mail Matter); Chapter 34 (Armed Forces And Free Postage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All matter relating to blind or other handicapped persons mailed under section 3403 or 3404 of this title, shall bear the words “Free Matter for the Blind or Handicapped”, or words to that effect specified by the Postal Service, in the upper right-hand corner of the address area.
