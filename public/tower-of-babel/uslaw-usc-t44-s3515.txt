44 U.S.C. § 3515 — Administrative powers
Citation: us:usc:44:3515
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section3515
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 35 (Coordination Of Federal Information Policy); Subchapter I (Federal Information Policy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon the request of the Director, each agency (other than an independent regulatory agency) shall, to the extent practicable, make its services, personnel, and facilities available to the Director for the performance of functions under this subchapter.
