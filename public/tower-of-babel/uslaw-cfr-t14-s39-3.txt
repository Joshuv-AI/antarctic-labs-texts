14 C.F.R. § 39.3 — Definition of airworthiness directives.
Citation: us:cfr:14:39.3
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-39.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 39 (Airworthiness Directives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FAA's airworthiness directives are legally enforceable rules that apply to the following products: aircraft, aircraft engines, propellers, and appliances.
