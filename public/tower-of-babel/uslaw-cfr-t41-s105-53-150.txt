41 C.F.R. § 105-53.150 — -53.150 Organization and functions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-53 (53—Statement Of Organization And Functions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Regional offices have been established in 11 cities throughout the United States. Each regional office is headed by a Regional Administrator who reports to the Associate Administrator for Operations and Industry Relations. The geographic composition of each region is shown in § 105-53.151.
