42 C.F.R. § 50.208 — Program or project requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:50.208
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-50.208
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 50 (Policies Of General Applicability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A program or project must, with respect to any sterilization procedure or hysterectomy it performs or arranges, meet all requirements of this subpart.
(b) The program or project shall maintain sufficient records and documentation to assure compliance with these regulations, and must retain such data for at least 3 years.
(c) The program or project shall submit other reports as required and when requested by the Secretary.
