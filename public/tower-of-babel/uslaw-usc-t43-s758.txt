43 U.S.C. § 758 — Delivery of patent contingent on refund of cost of survey
Citation: us:usc:43:758
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section758
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 18 (Survey Of Public Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In all cases of the survey of private land claims the cost of the same shall be refunded to the Treasury by the owner before the delivery of the patent.
