43 C.F.R. § 3836.12 — What work qualifies as assessment work?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3836.12
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3836.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3836 (Annual Assessment Work Requirements For Mining Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Assessment work includes, but is not limited to—
(a) Drilling, excavations, driving shafts and tunnels, sampling (geochemical or bulk), road construction on or for the benefit of the mining claim; and
(b) Geological, geochemical, and geophysical surveys.
