7 C.F.R. § 1752.25 — Special terms.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1752.25
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1752.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1752 (Servicing Of Telecommunications Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Administrator determines the servicing actions in this part would not protect the Government's interest due to unique circumstances of the debtor, the Agency reserves the right to negotiate special terms to maximize the Government's recovery on the debt.
