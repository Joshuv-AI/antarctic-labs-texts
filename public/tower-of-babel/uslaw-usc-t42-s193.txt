42 U.S.C. § 193 — Assistant chief
Citation: us:usc:42:193
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section193
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 6 (The Children’S Bureau). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be in the Children’s Bureau, until otherwise provided for by law, an assistant chief, to be appointed by the Secretary of Health and Human Services.
