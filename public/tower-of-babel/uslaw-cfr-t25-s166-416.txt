25 C.F.R. § 166.416 — May a permittee make a grazing rental payment in advance of the due date?
Citation: us:cfr:25:166.416
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-166.416
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 166 (Grazing Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Rent may be paid no more than 30 days in advance, unless otherwise specified in the permit.
