7 C.F.R. § 1951.896 — Appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1951.896
Creator: Rural Housing Service, Rural Business-Cooperative Service, And Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1951.896
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1951 (Servicing And Collections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any appealable adverse decision made by FmHA or its successor agency under Public Law 103-354 which affects the borrower may be appealed upon written request of the aggrieved party in accordance with subpart B of part 1900 of this chapter.
