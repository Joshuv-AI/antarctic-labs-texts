2 C.F.R. § 2701.503 — Relation to other audit requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:2:2701.503
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-2701.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 2701 (Uniform Administrative Requirements, Cost Principles, And Audit Requirements For Federal Awards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Non-Federal entities that are not subject to the requirements of the Single Audit Act and that are performing projects under SBA awards will be required to submit copies of their audited financial statements for their most recently completed fiscal year. Costs associated with the auditing of a non-Federal entity's financial statements may be included in its negotiations for an indirect cost rate agreement in accordance with 2 CFR 200.425.
