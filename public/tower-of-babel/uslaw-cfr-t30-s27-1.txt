30 C.F.R. § 27.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:30:27.1
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-27.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 27 (Methane-Monitoring Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part set forth the requirements for methane-monitoring systems or components thereof to procure certification for their incorporation in or with permissible equipment that is used in gassy mines, tunnels, or other underground workings and procedures for applying for such certification.
