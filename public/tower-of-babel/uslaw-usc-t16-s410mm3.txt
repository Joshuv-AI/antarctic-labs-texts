16 U.S.C. § 410mm–3 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:410mm–3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410mm–3
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX–K (Great Basin National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Not more than $800,000 are authorized to be appropriated for development of the park.
(b)
Not more than $200,000 are authorized to be appropriated for acquisition of lands and interests in land within the park.
