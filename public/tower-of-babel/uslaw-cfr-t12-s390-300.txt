12 C.F.R. § 390.300 — Note account.
Citation: us:cfr:12:390.300
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-390.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 390 (Regulations Transferred From The Office Of Thrift Supervision). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term note account means a note, subject to the right of immediate call, evidencing funds held by depositories electing the note option under applicable United States Treasury Department regulations. Note accounts are not savings accounts or savings deposits.
