25 U.S.C. § 1836 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:25:1836
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section1836
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 20 (Tribally Controlled Colleges And Universities Assistance); Subchapter II (Tribally Controlled College Or University Endowment Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out the provisions of this subchapter, $10,000,000 for fiscal year 2009 and such sums as may be necessary for each of the five succeeding fiscal years.
(b)
Any funds appropriated pursuant to subsection (a) are authorized to remain available until expended.
