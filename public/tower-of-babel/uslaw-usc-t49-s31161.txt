49 U.S.C. § 31161 — International cooperation
Citation: us:usc:49:31161
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section31161
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part B (Commercial); Chapter 311 (Commercial Motor Vehicle Safety); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation is authorized to use funds made available by section 31110 to participate and cooperate in international activities to enhance motor carrier, commercial motor vehicle, driver, and highway safety by such means as exchanging information, conducting research, and examining needs, best practices, and new technology.
