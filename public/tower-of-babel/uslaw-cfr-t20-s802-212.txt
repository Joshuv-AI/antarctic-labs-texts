20 C.F.R. § 802.212 — Response to petition for review.
Citation: us:cfr:20:802.212
Creator: Benefits Review Board, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-802.212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 802 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Within 30 days after the receipt of a petition for review, each party upon whom it was served may submit to the Board a brief, memorandum, or other statement in response to it.
(b) Arguments in response briefs shall be limited to those which respond to arguments raised in petitioner's brief and to those in support of the decision below. Other arguments will not be considered by the Board (see § 802.205(b)).
