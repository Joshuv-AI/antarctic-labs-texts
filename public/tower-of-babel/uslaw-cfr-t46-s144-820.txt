46 C.F.R. § 144.820 — Guards in dangerous places.
Citation: us:cfr:46:144.820
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-144.820
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 144 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An exposed hazard such as gears and rotating machinery, must be protected by a cover, guard or rail. This is not meant to restrict access to towing equipment such as winches, drums, towing gear or steering compartment equipment necessary for the operation of the vessel.
