28 C.F.R. § 551.82 — Mailing inmate manuscripts.
Citation: us:cfr:28:551.82
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-551.82
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 551 (Miscellaneous). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An inmate may mail a manuscript as general correspondence, in accordance with part 540, subpart B of this chapter. An inmate may not circulate his manuscript within the institution.
