41 C.F.R. § 102-34.90 — -34.90 Government motor vehicle identification.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-34.90
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-34.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-34 (34—Motor Vehicle Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All Government motor vehicles must display the following identification unless exempted under this subpart:
(a) “For Official Use Only”;
(b) “U.S. Government”; and
(c) Identification that readily identifies the agency owning the vehicle.
