46 C.F.R. § 154.1155 — Hand hose line: Coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:46:154.1155
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-154.1155
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 154 (Safety Standards For Self-Propelled Vessels Carrying Bulk Liquefied Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The coverage for the area for a hand hose line under § 154.1150 must not exceed the length of the hand hose line except the coverage for the protection of areas that are inaccessible to personnel must not exceed one-half the projection of the hose at its rated discharge, or 10 m (32.8 ft.), whichever is less.
