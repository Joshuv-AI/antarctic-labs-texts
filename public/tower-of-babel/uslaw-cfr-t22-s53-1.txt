22 C.F.R. § 53.1 — Passport requirement; definitions.

Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-53.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 53 (Passport Requirement And Exceptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) It is unlawful for a citizen of the United States, unless excepted under 22 CFR 53.2, to enter or depart, or attempt to enter or depart, the United States, without a valid U.S. passport.
(b) For purposes of this part “United States” means “United States” as defined in section 215(c) of the Immigration and Nationality Act of 1952, as amended (8 U.S.C. 1185(c)).
