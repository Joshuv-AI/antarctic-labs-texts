2 U.S.C. § 355 — Administrative support services
Citation: us:usc:2:355
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section355
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 11 (Citizens’ Commission On Public Service And Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator of General Services shall provide administrative support services for the Commission on a reimbursable basis.
