46 C.F.R. § 289.4 — Insurance by owners.
Citation: us:cfr:46:289.4
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-289.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 289 (Insurance Of Construction-Differential Subsidy Vessels, Operating-Differential Subsidy Vessels And Of Vessels Sold Or Adjusted Under The Merchant Ship Sales Act 1946). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Owners of vessels covered in § 289.2 will not be required to arrange commercial insurance to cover the interest of the United States, exclusive of its mortgage interest, but the United States reserves the right to require, whenever the contracts so provide, that this be done at some future date, should it deem it necessary.
