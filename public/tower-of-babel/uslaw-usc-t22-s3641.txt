22 U.S.C. § 3641 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:22:3641
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section3641
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 51 (Panama Canal); Subchapter I (Administration And Regulations); Part 2 (Employees); Subpart i (Panama Canal Commission Personnel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this part—
(1)
“Executive agency” has the meaning given that term in section 105 of title 5;
(2)
“uniformed services” has the meaning given that term in section 2101(3) of title 5;
(3)
“competitive service” has the meaning given that term in section 2102 of title 5; and
(4)
“United States”, when used in a geographic sense, means each of the several States and the District of Columbia.
