14 C.F.R. § 1214.401 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1214.401
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1214.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1214 (Space Flight). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart applies to all persons provided by NASA for flight to the International Space Station, including U.S. Government employees, uniformed members of the Armed Services, U.S. citizens who are not employees of the U.S. Government, and foreign nationals.
