36 U.S.C. § 2115 — Acquisition, operation, and maintenance of Lafayette Escadrille Memorial
Citation: us:usc:36:2115
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section2115
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (United States Government Organizations Involved With Observances And Ceremonies); Chapter 21 (American Battle Monuments Commission). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The American Battle Monuments Commission may enter into an agreement with the Lafayette Escadrille Memorial Foundation to acquire, operate, and maintain the Lafayette Escadrille Memorial in Marnes-la-Coquette, France. Under such an agreement, the Commission shall make necessary arrangements to ensure the ongoing maintenance of the memorial, including the cemetery at the memorial that contains the remains of 49 aviators of the United States who died during World War I.
