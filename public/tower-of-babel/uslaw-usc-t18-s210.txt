18 U.S.C. § 210 — Offer to procure appointive public office
Citation: us:usc:18:210
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section210
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 11 (Bribery, Graft, And Conflicts Of Interest). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever pays or offers or promises any money or thing of value, to any person, firm, or corporation in consideration of the use or promise to use any influence to procure any appointive office or place under the United States for any person, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
