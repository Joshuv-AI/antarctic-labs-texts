28 C.F.R. § 513.32 — Guidelines for disclosure.
Citation: us:cfr:28:513.32
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-513.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 513 (Access To Records). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Bureau provides for the disclosure of agency information pursuant to applicable laws, e.g. the Freedom of Information Act (5 U.S.C. 552), and the Privacy Act (5 U.S.C. 552a).
