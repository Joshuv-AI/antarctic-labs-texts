19 U.S.C. § 4501 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:19:4501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4501
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 29 (United States–Mexico–Canada Agreement Implementation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this Act is to approve and implement the Agreement between the United States of America, the United Mexican States, and Canada entered into under the authority of section 4202(b) of this title.
