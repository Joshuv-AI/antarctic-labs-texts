15 U.S.C. § 6006 — “Approved bicycle helmet” defined
Citation: us:usc:15:6006
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section6006
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 86 (Children’S Bicycle Helmet Safety). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “approved bicycle helmet” means a bicycle helmet that meets—
(1)
any interim standard described in section 6004(b) of this title, pending establishment of a final standard under section 6004(c) of this title; and
(2)
the final standard, once it is established under section 6004(c) of this title.
