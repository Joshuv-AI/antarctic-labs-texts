22 C.F.R. § 1102.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:22:1102.1

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1102.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1102 (Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to prescribe rules, guidelines and procedures to implement the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA), 5 U.S.C. 552, as amended on November 21, 1974, by Public Law 93-502, and on October 27, 1986, by Public Law 99-570.
