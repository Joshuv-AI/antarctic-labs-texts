42 C.F.R. § 423.604 — Effect of a reconsideration determination.
Citation: us:cfr:42:423.604
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-423.604
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 423 (Voluntary Medicare Prescription Drug Benefit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A reconsideration determination is final and binding on the enrollee and the Part D plan sponsor, unless the enrollee files a request for a hearing under the provisions of § 423.2014.
