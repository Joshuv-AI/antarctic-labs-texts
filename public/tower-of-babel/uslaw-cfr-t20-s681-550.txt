20 C.F.R. § 681.550 — Are Individual Training Accounts permitted for youth participants?
Citation: us:cfr:20:681.550
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-681.550
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 681 (Youth Activities Under Title I Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. In order to enhance individual participant choice in their education and training plans and provide flexibility to service providers, the Department allows WIOA Individual Training Accounts (ITAs) for OSY, ages 16 to 24 using WIOA youth funds when appropriate.
