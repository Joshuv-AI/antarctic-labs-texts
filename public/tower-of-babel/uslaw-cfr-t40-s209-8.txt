40 C.F.R. § 209.8 — Submission of a remedial plan.
Citation: us:cfr:40:209.8
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-209.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 209 (Rules Of Practice Governing Proceedings Under The Noise Control Act Of 1972). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Administrator may require the respondent to submit a remedial plan. Notice of this requirement and the due date will be given in the complaint. If the respondent requests a hearing, the remedial plan required by the complaint need not be submitted. The final order may include a requirement that the respondent submit a remedial plan.
(b) A respondent may always submit a remedial plan voluntarily in pursuit of informal settlement.
