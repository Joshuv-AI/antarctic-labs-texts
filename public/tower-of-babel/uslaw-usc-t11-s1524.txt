11 U.S.C. § 1524 — Intervention by a foreign representative
Citation: us:usc:11:1524
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section1524
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 15 (Ancillary And Other Cross-Border Cases); Subchapter III (Recognition Of A Foreign Proceeding And Relief). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon recognition of a foreign proceeding, the foreign representative may intervene in any proceedings in a State or Federal court in the United States in which the debtor is a party.
