7 C.F.R. § 761.202 — Timing of allocations.
Citation: us:cfr:7:761.202
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-761.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 761 (Farm Loan Programs; General Program Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Agency's National Office allocates funds for FO, CL, and OL loans to the State Offices on a fiscal year basis, as made available by the Office of Management and Budget. However, the National Office will retain control over the funds when funding or administrative constraints make allocation to State Offices impractical.
