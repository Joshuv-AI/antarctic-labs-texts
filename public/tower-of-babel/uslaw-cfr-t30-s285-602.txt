30 C.F.R. § 285.602 — What records must I maintain?
Citation: us:cfr:30:285.602
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-285.602
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 285 (Renewable Energy And Alternate Uses Of Existing Facilities On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Until BOEM releases your financial assurance under 30 CFR 585.534, you must maintain and provide to BSEE, upon request, all data and information related to compliance with the required terms and conditions of your lease, grant, reports submitted under this part and approved plans.
