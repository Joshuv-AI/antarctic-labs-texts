10 U.S.C. § 9591 — Utilities: proceeds from overseas operations
Citation: us:usc:10:9591
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9591
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 967 (Utilities And Services). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

During actual or threatened hostilities, proceeds from operating a public utility in connection with operations of the Air Force or the Space Force in the field overseas are available for that utility until the close of the fiscal year following that in which they are received.
