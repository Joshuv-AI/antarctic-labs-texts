45 C.F.R. § 77.2 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:45:77.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-77.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 77 (Remedial Actions Applicable To Letter Of Credit Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part apply to all recipient organizations under any program administered by the Department through which the organization receives Federal funds under a letter of credit.
