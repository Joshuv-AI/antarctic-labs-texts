12 C.F.R. § 1251.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1251.1
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1251.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1251 (Contributions To The Housing Trust And Capital Magnet Funds). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to implement a prohibition against an Enterprise redirecting the cost of any allocation to the Housing Trust Fund or the Capital Magnet Fund to originators of mortgages purchased or securitized by an Enterprise.
