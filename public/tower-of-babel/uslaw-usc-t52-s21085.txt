52 U.S.C. § 21085 — Methods of implementation left to discretion of State
Citation: us:usc:52:21085
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section21085
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 209 (Election Administration Improvement); Subchapter III (Uniform And Nondiscriminatory Election Technology And Administration Requirements); Part A (Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The specific choices on the methods of complying with the requirements of this subchapter shall be left to the discretion of the State.
