32 C.F.R. § 767.26 — Criminal law.
Citation: us:cfr:32:767.26
Creator: Department Of The Navy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-767.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 767 (Guidelines For Permitting Archaeological Investigations And Other Activities Directed At Sunken Military Craft And Terrestrial Military Craft Under The Jurisdiction Of The Department Of The Navy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in these regulations is intended to prevent the United States from pursuing criminal sanctions for plundering of wrecks, larceny of Government property, or violation of applicable criminal law, whether the infringement pertains to a sunken military craft, a terrestrial military craft or other craft under the jurisdiction of the DON.
