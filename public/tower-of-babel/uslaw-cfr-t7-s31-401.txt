7 C.F.R. § 31.401 — Cost of samples for wool grades.
Citation: us:cfr:7:31.401
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-31.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 31 (Purchase Of Wool And Wool Top Samples). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Complete set: $22 each, delivered to any destination within the United States and $24 each, delivered to any destination outside the United States.
(b) Individual sample: $2 each, delivered to any destination within the United States and $2.50 each, delivered to any destination outside the United States.
