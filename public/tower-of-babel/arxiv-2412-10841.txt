4.
Applications to minimal horospherical embeddings

Now our purpose is to explain an application of toric surfaces
S_{r}

to the classification of minimal smooth projective quasi-homogeneous
3
-folds
having a regular action of the group

{SL}(2)\times{{\mathbb{G}}}_{m}
. The complete classificatiion was obtained by Z. Guan

[
Gu97
,
Gu99
,
Gu00
]
. His main result in
[

, Theorem 4.1]

claims that any smooth minimal projective
3
-fold
having a quasi-homogeneous regular action of the group

belongs to one of the following 5 types:

(1)

projective space

(2)

3
-dimensional quadric
in
{\mathbb{P}}^{4}

(3)

{{\mathbb{P}}}^{2}
-bundles over
{{\mathbb{P}}}^{1}

(4)

{\mathbb{P}}^{1}
-bundles over
{{\mathbb{P}}}^{2}

(5)

The earlier classification results of Mukai, Umemura and Nakano

]

(see
[

In the investigation of such varieties

]
and the Mori theory in dimension

-variety of rank

-fold

({\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}(1,0)+{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}(0,1),D)
and
all its faces.

Example 5.2
.

Another natural

G
-embedding of
G/H\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{P}}^{3}
extends the
previous one by adding at infinity the projective plane
{\mathbb{P}}^{2}
consisiting
of
3
more
G
-orbits: a point,
{\mathbb{C}}^{2}\setminus(0,0)
, and

{\mathbb{P}}^{1}
. This embedding of
G/H
corresponds to a colored
fan
\Sigma_{2}
having additional
3
colored cones

({\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}(1,0)+{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}(-1,-1),D)
,

({\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}(-1,-1),\emptyset)
, and
({\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}(0,1)+{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}(-1,-1),\emptyset).

There is a natural operation which produces from any colored fan
\Sigma

the uncolored fan
\Sigma^{\prime}
by replacing every colored cone

(\sigma,{\mathcal{F}}_{\sigma})
by the pair
(\sigma,\emptyset)
.
The bijection
between cones in
\Sigma^{\prime}
and
\Sigma
defines
a proper surjective
G
-equivariant morphism
X_{\Sigma^{\prime}}\to X_{\Sigma}
.
Let
Y_{\Sigma^{\prime}}
be
T
-equivariant embedding of the torus
N_{G}(H)/H

corresponding to the uncolored
fan
\Sigma^{\prime}
. Then the horospherical embedding
G/H\hookrightarrow X_{\Sigma^{\prime}}
can be described as a toric fibration over
G/P
with the
fiber
Y_{\Sigma^{\prime}}
.

Example 5.3
.

If we apply the above procedure to the colored fan
\Sigma_{2}

corresponding to the horospherical embedding
G/H\hookrightarrow{\mathbb{P}}^{3}
we obtain the uncolored fan
\Sigma^{\prime}_{2}
defining
the horospherical embedding

G/H\hookrightarrow X_{\Sigma^{\prime}_{2}}\cong{\mathbb{P}}_{{\mathbb{P}}^{1}}({\mathcal{O}}\oplus{\mathcal{O}}\oplus{\mathcal{O}}(1)).

The horospheric variety
X_{\Sigma^{\prime}_{2}}
is a
{\mathbb{P}}^{2}
-bundle over
G/B\cong{\mathbb{P}}^{1}

and it
can be obtained as a blow-up
of the
G
-invariant line in
{\mathbb{P}}^{3}=X_{\Sigma_{2}}
which is the closure
of the
1
-dimensional
G
-orbit corresponding to the colored
cone
({\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}(1,0),D)
.

Equivariant birational contractions in the Mori theory
for spherical varieties were described by Brion
[
Br93
]

using methods similar to the ones for toric varieties

[
Re83
]
. It is important to remark that for spherical varieties
extremal rays in the cone of effective
1
-cycles are generated
by classes of closures of
1
-dimensional
G
-orbits. Therefore,
the corresponding Mori contractions are
G
-equivariant.

In contrast to toric varieties the
G
-equivariant birational geometry
of spherical varieties is more rich due to existence of colors.
For smooth projective spherical varieties of rank
2
Brion has
generalized the factorization theorem for equivariant
birational maps between smooth projective toric surfaces
[
Br94
]
.

For our purposes, we summarize some known results in the case
of
3
-dimensional horospherical
SL(2)\times{\mathbb{G}}_{m}
-varieties:

Theorem 5.4
.

Let
X_{\Sigma}
be a
projective
3
-dimensional horospherical

SL(2)\times{\mathbb{G}}_{m}
-variety corresponding to a
2
-dimensional colored fan

\Sigma
. Then
X_{\Sigma}
is smooth if and only if two conditions
are safisfied:

(1)

every cone
\sigma
in
\Sigma
is generated by a part of
a
{\mathbb{Z}}
-basis of the lattice
N
;

(2)

if a cone
(\sigma,D)\in\Sigma

has the color
D
, then the lattice point
\rho(D)\in N
must be
one of its primitive generators.

Theorem 5.5
.

Let
X_{\Sigma}
be a smooth
projective
3
-dimensional horospherical

SL(2)\times{\mathbb{G}}_{m}
-variety corresponding to a
2
-dimensional
colored fan

\Sigma
. Then there exist exactly
3
types of equivariant blow-ups of

X_{\Sigma}
:

(1)

the blow up of a
SL(2)\times{\mathbb{G}}_{m}
-fixed point in

X_{\Sigma}
corresponding to a
2
-dimensional colored cone

(\sigma,D)
, where
\sigma={\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu+{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu^{\prime}
for
some
{\mathbb{Z}}
-basis
\nu,\nu^{\prime}
of
N
. If
\nu^{\prime}=\rho(D)
, then we set

\nu^{\prime\prime}:=\nu+\nu^{\prime}
and the colored
fan of the blow-up
is obtained by the subdivision of
(\sigma,D)
into two
2
-dimensional
subcones
(\sigma^{\prime},\emptyset)
and
(\sigma^{\prime\prime},D)
, where

\sigma^{\prime}={\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu+{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu^{\prime\prime}
and
\sigma^{\prime\prime}={\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu^{\prime}+{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu^{\prime\prime}
.

(2)

the blow up of a closed
1
-dimensional
SL(2)\times{\mathbb{G}}_{m}
-orbit
in
X_{\Sigma}
corresponding to a
2
-dimensional uncolored cone

(\sigma,\emptyset)
, where
\sigma={\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu+{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu^{\prime}
for
some
{\mathbb{Z}}
-basis
\nu,\nu^{\prime}
of
N
. Again we set

\nu^{\prime\prime}:=\nu+\nu^{\prime}
and the colored
fan of this blow-up
is obtained by the subdivision of
(\sigma,\emptyset)

into two
2
-dimensional
uncolored subcones
(\sigma^{\prime},\emptyset)
and
(\sigma^{\prime\prime},\emptyset)
,
where
\sigma^{\prime}={\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu+{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu^{\prime\prime}
and

\sigma^{\prime\prime}={\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu^{\prime}+{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu^{\prime\prime}
.

(3)

the blow up of the closure of a
1
-dimensional

SL(2)\times{\mathbb{G}}_{m}
-orbit
in
X_{\Sigma}
corresponding to the
1
-dimensional colored cone

({\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\rho(D),D)
. The fan of this blow-up
is obtained by deleting the color
D
from all colored
cones of
\Sigma
.

Corollary 5.6
.

A smooth
projective
3
-dimensional horospherical

SL(2)\times{\mathbb{G}}_{m}
-variety defined by a colored fan
\Sigma

is minimal (i.e. does not admit any Mori contraction to another
smooth projective
3
-fold) if and only if
\Sigma
does not contain
two
2
-dimensional colored cones
({\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu+{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu^{\prime\prime},{\mathcal{F}}_{1})
and
({\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu^{\prime\prime}+{\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu^{\prime},{\mathcal{F}}_{2})
with the uncolored common face
({\mathbb{R}}_{\geq 0}\nu^{\prime\prime},\emptyset)

such that
\nu^{\prime\prime}=\nu+\nu^{\prime}
.
