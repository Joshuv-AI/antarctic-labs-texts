10 U.S.C. § 7562 — Clothing
Citation: us:usc:10:7562
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7562
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 765 (Issue Of Serviceable Material To Armed Forces). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may prescribe the quantity and kind of clothing to be issued annually to members of the Army.
