25 C.F.R. § 290.16 — Can the per capita payments of minors and legal incompetents be deposited into accounts held by BIA or OTFM?
Citation: us:cfr:25:290.16
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-290.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 290 (Tribal Revenue Allocation Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No. The Secretary will not accept any deposits of payments or funds derived from net gaming revenues to any account held by BIA or OTFM.
