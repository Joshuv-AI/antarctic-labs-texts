42 U.S.C. § 6992j — Effective date
Citation: us:usc:42:6992j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section6992j
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 82 (Solid Waste Disposal); Subchapter X (Demonstration Medical Waste Tracking Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The regulations promulgated under this subchapter shall take effect within 90 days after promulgation, except that, at the time of promulgation, the Administrator may provide for a shorter period prior to the effective date if he finds the regulated community does not need 90 days to come into compliance.
