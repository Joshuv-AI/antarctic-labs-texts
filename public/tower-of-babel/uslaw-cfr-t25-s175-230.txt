25 C.F.R. § 175.230 — Why are changes to purchased power costs not included in the procedure for adjusting electric power rates?
Citation: us:cfr:25:175.230
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-175.230
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 175 (Electric Power Utilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Changes to purchased power costs are not included in the procedure for adjusting electric power rates because unforeseen increases in the cost of purchased power are:
(a) Not under our control;
(b) Determined by current market rates; and
(c) Subject to market fluctuations that can occur at an undetermined time and frequency.
