12 C.F.R. § 347.121 — Requirements for insured state nonmember bank to close a foreign branch.
Citation: us:cfr:12:347.121
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-347.121
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 347 (International Banking). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A bank must comply with the written notification requirement contained in § 303.182(d) when it closes a foreign branch.
