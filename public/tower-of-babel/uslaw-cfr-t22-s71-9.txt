22 C.F.R. § 71.9 — Presentation of Americans at foreign courts.
Citation: us:cfr:22:71.9
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-71.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 71 (Protection And Welfare Of Citizens And Their Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The chief of the mission concerned may exercise his discretion in the matter of procuring the presentation of American citizens at the court of the country to which he is accredited.
