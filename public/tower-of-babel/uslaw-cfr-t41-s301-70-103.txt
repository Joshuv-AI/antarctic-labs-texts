41 C.F.R. § 301-70.103 — -70.103 Prohibition on preventing POV use.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-70.103
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-70.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-70 (70—Internal Policy And Procedure Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies may not prohibit an employee from using a POV on official travel, but if the employee elects to use a POV instead of the authorized method of transportation, agencies must limit reimbursement to the constructive cost of the authorized method of transportation (see § 301-10.305 of this chapter) and charge leave for any duty hours that are missed as a result of travel by POV.
