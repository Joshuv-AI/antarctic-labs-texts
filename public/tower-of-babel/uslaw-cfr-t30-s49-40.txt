30 C.F.R. § 49.40 — Requirements for large coal mines.
Citation: us:cfr:30:49.40
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-49.40
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 49 (Mine Rescue Teams). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At mines with more than 36 underground employees, one of the two certified mine rescue teams shall be an individual mine-site team or a composite team.
