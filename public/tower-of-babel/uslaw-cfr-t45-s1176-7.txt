45 C.F.R. § 1176.7 — Publicizing vacancies.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1176.7
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1176.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1176 (Part-Time Career Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When applicants from outside the Federal service are desired, part-time vacanies may be publicized through various recruiting means, such as:
(a) Federal Job Information Centers.
(b) State Employment Offices.
(c) Veterans' Administration Recruiting Bulletins.
