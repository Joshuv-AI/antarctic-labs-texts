15 U.S.C. § 2402 — Congressional statement of purpose
Citation: us:usc:15:2402
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section2402
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 51 (National Productivity And Quality Of Working Life); Subchapter I (Findings, Purpose, And Policy; Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It is the purpose of this chapter—
(1)
to establish a national policy which will encourage productivity growth consistent with needs of the economy, the natural environment, and the needs, rights, and best interests of management, the work force, and consumers; and
(2)
to establish as an independent establishment of the executive branch a National Center for Productivity and Quality of Working Life to focus, coordinate, and promote efforts to improve the rate of productivity growth.
