42 C.F.R. § 456.506 — Waiver options for Medicaid agency.
Citation: us:cfr:42:456.506
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-456.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 456 (Utilization Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The agency may apply for a waiver at any time it has the procedures referred to under § 456.505(b) in operation at least—
(1) On a demonstration basis; or
(2) In any part of the State.
(b) Any hospital or mental hospital participating under the plan that is not covered by a waiver must continue to meet all the UR plan requirements under subpart C or D of this part.
