47 C.F.R. § 51.207 — Local dialing parity.
Citation: us:cfr:47:51.207
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-51.207
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 51 (Interconnection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A LEC shall permit telephone exchange service customers within a local calling area to dial the same number of digits to make a local telephone call notwithstanding the identity of the customer's or the called party's telecommunications service provider.
