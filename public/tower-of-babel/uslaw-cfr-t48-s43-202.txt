48 C.F.R. § 43.202 — 43.202 Authority to issue change orders.
Citation: us:cfr:48:43.202
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-43.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 43 (Contract Modifications). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Change orders shall be issued by the contracting officer except when authority is delegated to an administrative contracting officer (see 42.202(c)).
