41 U.S.C. § 4702 — Prohibition on release of contractor proposals
Citation: us:usc:41:4702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section4702
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 47 (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Definition.—
In this section, the term “proposal” means a proposal, including a technical, management, or cost proposal, submitted by a contractor in response to the requirements of a solicitation for a competitive proposal.
(b) Prohibition.—
A proposal in the possession or control of an executive agency may not be made available to any person under section 552 of title 5.
(c) Nonapplication.—
Subsection (b) does not apply to a proposal that is set forth or incorporated by reference in a contract entered into between the agency and the contractor that submitted the proposal.
