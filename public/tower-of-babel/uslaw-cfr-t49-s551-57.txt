49 C.F.R. § 551.57 — Who may sign the Designation by Foreign Manufacturer?
Citation: us:cfr:49:551.57
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-551.57
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 551 (Procedural Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only an official or employee of the foreign manufacturer with authority to appoint an agent may sign the Designation by Foreign Manufacturer.
