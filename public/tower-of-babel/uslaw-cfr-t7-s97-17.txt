7 C.F.R. § 97.17 — Papers of completed application to be retained.
Citation: us:cfr:7:97.17
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-97.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 97 (Plant Variety And Protection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The papers submitted with a completed application shall be retained by the Office except as provided in § 97.23(c). After issuance of a certificate of protection the Office will furnish copies of the application and related papers to any person upon payment of the specified fee.
