24 C.F.R. § 232.595 — Eligibility of title.
Citation: us:cfr:24:232.595
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-232.595
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 232 (Mortgage Insurance For Nursing Homes, Intermediate Care Facilities, Board And Care Homes, And Assisted Living Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order for the property which is to be the security for a loan to be insured under this subpart to be eligible for insurance, the Commissioner shall determine that the title to the property is vested in the borrower as of the date the security instrument is filed for record. The title evidence will be examined by the Commissioner and the endorsement of the credit instrument for insurance shall be evidence of its acceptability.
