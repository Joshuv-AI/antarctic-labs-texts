29 C.F.R. § 779.111 — Buyers and their assistants.
Citation: us:cfr:29:779.111
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-779.111
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 779 (The Fair Labor Standards Act As Applied To Retailers Of Goods Or Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Buyers and their assistants, employed by retail businesses, as a regular part of their duties, generally travel across State lines, or use the mails, telegraph, or telephone for interstate communication to order goods; or they regularly send or receive, across State lines, written reports, messages or other documents. These activities of such employees constitute engagement “in commerce” within the meaning of the Act.
