12 C.F.R. § 1075.110 — Reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1075.110
Creator: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1075.110
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1075 (Consumer Financial Civil Penalty Fund Rule). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Fund Administrator must issue regular reports, on at least an annual basis, that describe how funds in the Civil Penalty Fund have been allocated, the basis for those allocations, and how funds that have been allocated to classes of victims have been distributed. These reports will be made available on www.consumerfinance.gov.
