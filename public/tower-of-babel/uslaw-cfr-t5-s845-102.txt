5 C.F.R. § 845.102 — Definitions.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 845 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In this subpart—
FERS means the Federal Employees Retirement System as described in chapter 84 of title 5, United States Code.
FERS basic benefits means any benefits payable under subchapter II, IV, or V of chapter 84 of title 5, United States Code.
Fund means the Civil Service Retirement Fund.
