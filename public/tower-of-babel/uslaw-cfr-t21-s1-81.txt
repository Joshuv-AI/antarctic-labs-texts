21 C.F.R. § 1.81 — Rejection of entry filing.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1.81
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1.81
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1 (General Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

FDA may reject an entry filing for failure to provide complete and accurate information that is required pursuant to this subpart.
