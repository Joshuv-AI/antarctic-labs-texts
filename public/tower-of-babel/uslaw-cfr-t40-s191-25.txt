40 C.F.R. § 191.25 — Compliance with other Federal regulations.

Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-191.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 191 (Environmental Radiation Protection Standards For Management And Disposal Of Spent Nuclear Fuel, High-Level And Transuranic Radioactive Wastes). Compliance with the provisions in this subpart does not negate the necessity to comply with any other applicable Federal regulations or requirements.
