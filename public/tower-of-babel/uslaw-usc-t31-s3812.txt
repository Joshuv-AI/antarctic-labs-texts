31 U.S.C. § 3812 — Prohibition against delegation
Citation: us:usc:31:3812
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section3812
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 38 (Administrative Remedies For False Claims And Statements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any function, duty, or responsibility which this chapter specifies be carried out by the Attorney General or an Assistant Attorney General designated by the Attorney General, shall not be delegated to, or carried out by, any other officer or employee of the Department of Justice.
