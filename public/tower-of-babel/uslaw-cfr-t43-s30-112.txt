43 C.F.R. § 30.112 — What must a complete probate file contain?
Citation: us:cfr:43:30.112
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-30.112
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 30 (Indian Probate Hearings Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A probate file must contain the documents and information described in 25 CFR 15.202 and any other relevant information.
