49 C.F.R. § 826.33 — Reply.

Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-826.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 826 (Rules Implementing The Equal Access To Justice Act Of 1980). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 15 days after service of an answer, the applicant may file a reply. If the reply is based on any alleged facts not already in the record of the proceeding, the applicant shall include with the reply either supporting affidavits or a request for further proceedings under § 826.36.
