24 C.F.R. § 213.277 — Right and liability under the Cooperative Management Housing Insurance Fund.
Citation: us:cfr:24:213.277
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-213.277
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 213 (Cooperative Housing Mortgage Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No mortgagor or mortgagee shall have any vested right in a credit balance in either the General Surplus Account or the Participating Reserve Account. No mortgagor or mortgagee shall be subject to any liability arising under the mutuality of the Cooperative Management Housing Insurance Fund.
