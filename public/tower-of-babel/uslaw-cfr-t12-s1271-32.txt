12 C.F.R. § 1271.32 — Authority to establish investment policies and procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1271.32
Creator: Federal Housing Finance Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1271.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1271 (Miscellaneous Federal Home Loan Bank Operations And Authorities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Directorate shall have authority to establish investment policies and procedures with respect to Financing Corporation funds provided that the investment policies and procedures are consistent with the requirements of section 21(g) of the Bank Act (12 U.S.C. 1441(g)). The Directorate shall promptly notify FHFA in writing of any changes to the investment policies and procedures.
