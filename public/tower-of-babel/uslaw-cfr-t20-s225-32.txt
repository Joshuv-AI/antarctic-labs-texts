20 C.F.R. § 225.32 — DRC's and the Special Minimum PIA.
Citation: us:cfr:20:225.32
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-225.32
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 225 (Primary Insurance Amount Determinations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Delayed retirement credits cannot be added to the Special Minimum PIA. Delayed retirement credits can only be added to the regular PIA's used in computing the benefits outlined in § 225.31.
