16 U.S.C. § 837f — Purchaser priority on Pacific Northwest power; amendment of existing contracts and new contracts to include priority provisions
Citation: us:usc:16:837f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section837f
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 12F (Pacific Northwest Consumer Power Preference; Reciprocal Priority In Other Regions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall offer to amend, without imposing any other requirements as a condition to such amendment, all existing contracts for the sale or exchange of electric power generated at Federal hydroelectric plants in the Pacific Northwest to include, and shall include in all new contracts, provisions giving the purchaser priority on electric power generated at such plants in conformity with the provisions of this chapter.
