19 U.S.C. § 4064 — Termination of relief authority
Citation: us:usc:19:4064
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section4064
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 26 (Dominican Republic-Central America Free Trade); Subchapter III (Relief From Imports); Part A (Relief From Imports Benefiting From The Agreement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) General rule
Subject to subsection (b), no import relief may be provided under this part after the date that is 10 years after the date on which the Agreement enters into force.
(b) Exception
If an article for which relief is provided under this part is an article for which the period for tariff elimination, set out in the Schedule of the United States to Annex 3.3 of the Agreement, is greater than 10 years, no relief under this part may be provided for that article after the date on which that period ends.
