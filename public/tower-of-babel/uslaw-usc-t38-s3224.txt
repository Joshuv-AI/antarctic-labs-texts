38 U.S.C. § 3224 — Death of participant
Citation: us:usc:38:3224
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section3224
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part III (Readjustment And Related Benefits); Chapter 32 (Post-Vietnam Era Veterans’ Educational Assistance); Subchapter II (Eligibility; Contributions; And Matching Fund). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In the event of a participant’s death, the amount of such participant’s unused contributions to the fund shall be paid to the living person or persons first listed below:
(1)
The beneficiary or beneficiaries designated by such participant under such participant’s Servicemembers’ Group Life Insurance policy.
(2)
The surviving spouse of the participant.
(3)
The surviving child or children of the participant, in equal shares.
(4)
The surviving parent or parents of the participant, in equal shares.
If there is no such person living, such amount shall be paid to such participant’s estate.
