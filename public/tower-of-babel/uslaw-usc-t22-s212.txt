22 U.S.C. § 212 — Persons entitled to passport
Citation: us:usc:22:212
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section212
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 4 (Passports). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No passport shall be granted or issued to or verified for any other persons than those owing allegiance, whether citizens or not, to the United States.
