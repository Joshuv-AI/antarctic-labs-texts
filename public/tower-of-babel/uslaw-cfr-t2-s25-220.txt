2 C.F.R. § 25.220 — Use of award term.
Citation: us:cfr:2:25.220
Creator: Office Of Management And Budget Government-Wide Guidance For Federal Financial Assistance
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-25.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 25 (Unique Entity Identifier And System For Award Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A Federal agency must include the award term in Appendix A in all Federal financial assistance agreements (see § 25.400) to accomplish the purpose of § 25.100.
(b) A Federal agency may use different letters and numbers than those in Appendix A to designate the paragraphs of the award term.
