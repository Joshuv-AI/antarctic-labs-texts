43 C.F.R. § 426.26 — Severability.
Citation: us:cfr:43:426.26
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-426.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 426 (Acreage Limitation Rules And Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any provision of these regulations or the application of these rules to any person or circumstance is held invalid, then the sections of these rules or their applications which are not held invalid will not be affected.
