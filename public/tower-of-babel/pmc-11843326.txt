Psychedelics and Entactogens: Call for Papers
Authors: Craig
W. Lindsley, Jacob M. Hooker, Kelly Chibale, Christa E. Müller, Squire J. Booker
Published: 2024

Psychedelics (drugs that change an individual’s perception of reality) and entactogens (drugs that provide emotional openness and/or apathy) together represent one of the most exciting areas of research in neuropsychiatry. Historical psychedelic drugs, in modern, low-dose clinical trials, show rapid and sustained efficacy across a broad range of psychiatric disorders. However, the goal has been to develop non-hallucinogenic psychedelics that maintain clinical efficacy but without the risk of “tripping”. Early efforts focused on modifying the “usual suspect” 5-HT2A ligands; however, with so many companies and academics in this space, novel chemical matter and pharmacology are emerging. In 2021, we published a Special Issue in ACS Pharmacology & Translational Science (Vol. 4, Issue 2) focused on Psychedelics and New Advances in Preclinical and Clinical Studies. The papers in that collection were highly read and cited and helped to propel the field forward. With all the advances in the field today, it is time to highlight once again the promise of psychedelic science with a Call for Papers across multiple ACS Journals to showcase innovation in medicinal chemistry, molecular pharmacology, neuropharmacology, and clinical trial design applied to novel psychedelics and entactogens. Beyond these areas, we are also excited about research that investigates the role of artificial intelligence in drug discovery and optimization for psychedelic compounds; applications of neuroimaging, electrophysiology, and digital biomarkers in psychedelic research; and new considerations and advances for clinical trial design for psychedelics and entactogens. The time is now to highlight all the amazing research in this field, and we invite you to submit your work to one of the five Journals in this special Call for Papers.

The deadline for submission for this Virtual Special Issue is June 30, 2025. Articles accepted into a Virtual Special Issue are published in the next regular issue of the respective journal. Once the majority of articles are accepted into the Virtual Special Issue, they are assembled on a dedicated web page and promoted as a special collection. Manuscripts can be submitted through the ACS Publishing Center, selecting “Psychedelics and Entactogens” in the Special Issue Selection.

Please consider the relevant instructions to authors before submitting a manuscript to the Journal of Medicinal Chemistry, ACS Chemical Neuroscience, ACS Medicinal Chemistry Letters, ACS Pharmacology & Translational Science, or ACS Bio & Med Chem Au.
