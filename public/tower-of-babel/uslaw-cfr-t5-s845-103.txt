5 C.F.R. § 845.103 — Prohibition against collection of debts.
Citation: us:cfr:5:845.103
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-845.103
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 845 (Federal Employees Retirement System—Debt Collection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Debts may be collected from FERS basic benefits only to the extent expressly authorized by Federal statute.
(b) When collection of a debt from FERS basic benefits is authorized under paragraph (a) of this section, the collection will be made in accordance with this part.
