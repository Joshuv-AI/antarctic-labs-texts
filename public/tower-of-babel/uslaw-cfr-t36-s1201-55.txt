36 C.F.R. § 1201.55 — How will NARA collect debts through Administrative Wage Garnishment?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1201.55
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1201.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1201 (Collection Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

NARA will collect debts through Administrative Wage Garnishment in accordance with the Administrative Wage Garnishment regulations issued by the Treasury. NARA adopts, for the purposes of this subpart, the Treasury's Administrative Wage Garnishment regulations in 31 CFR 285.11.
