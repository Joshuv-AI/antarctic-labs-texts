Foreign-Trade Zones Board

[B-120-2026]


Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) 272, Notification of Proposed Production 
Activity; Infinera Corporation; (Telecommunications Devices); 
Allentown, Pennsylvania

    The Lehigh Valley Economic Development Corporation, grantee of FTZ 
272, submitted a notification of proposed production activity to the 
FTZ Board (the Board) on behalf of Infinera Corporation (Infinera) for 
Infinera's facility in Allentown, Pennsylvania within FTZ 272. The 
notification conforming to the requirements of the Board's regulations 
(15 CFR 400.22) was received on September 8, 2026.
    Pursuant to 15 CFR 400.14(b), FTZ production activity would be 
limited to the specific foreign-status material(s)/component(s) and 
specific finished product(s) described in the submitted notification 
(summarized below) and subsequently authorized by the Board. The 
benefits that may stem from conducting production activity under FTZ 
procedures are explained in the background section of the Board's 
website--accessible via www.trade.gov/ftz.

[[Page 60581]]

    The proposed finished products include: Optical Transmit/Receive 
Subassembly (TROSA)--a telecommunications device that allows fiber 
optic data transmission; and Transceiver Photonic Integrated Circuit 
(PIC)--a telecommunications device that allows fiber optic data 
transmission (duty free).
    The proposed foreign-status materials/components include: Optical 
Glass Block; Optical Isolator; Fiber Optic Cable Assembly; Ceramic 
Metal Hermetic Package; Ceramic Metal Sealing Lid; Optical Polarizing 
Beam Splitter/Combiner; Thermo-Electric Cooler; Capacitor (Surface 
Mount Type); Bare Printed Flexible Circuit (Radio Frequency type); Bare 
Printed Flexible Circuit (Direct Current type); Thermistor; Control 
Board Assembly; Silicon Germanium Heat Dissipator; Fiber Optic Cable 
Adaptor; Stainless Steel Alignment sleeve; Optical Photodetector; 
Optical Glass Lens; Micro-Electro-Mechanical Systems Variable Optical 
Attenuator; Optical Isolator; Optical Wavelength Locker; Optical 
Wavelength Locker Beam Splitter; Optical Beam Expander; Optical Mirror 
Prism; Optical Light Absorber; Optical Cable Adaptor; and Flexible 
Circuit (Direct Current type) Populated with Resistors, Capacitors, and 
Integrated Circuit Chips (duty rate ranges from duty-free to 4.5%).
    The request indicates that certain materials/components are subject 
to duties under section 232 of the Trade Expansion Act of 1962 (section 
232) or section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974 (section 301), depending 
on the country of origin. The applicable section 232 and section 301 
decisions require subject merchandise to be admitted to FTZs in 
privileged foreign status (19 CFR 146.41).
    Public comment is invited from interested parties. Submissions 
shall be addressed to the Board's Executive Secretary and sent to: 
[email protected]. The closing period for their receipt is November 3, 
2026.
    A copy of the notification will be available for public inspection 
in the ``Online FTZ Information System'' section of the Board's 
website.
