6 C.F.R. § 27.100 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:6:27.100
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-27.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 27 (Chemical Facility Anti-Terrorism Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to enhance the security of our Nation by furthering the mission of the Department as provided in 6 U.S.C. 111(b)(1) and by lowering the risk posed by certain chemical facilities.
