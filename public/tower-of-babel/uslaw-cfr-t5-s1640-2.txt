5 C.F.R. § 1640.2 — Information regarding account.
Citation: us:cfr:5:1640.2
Creator: Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-1640.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 1640 (Periodic Participant Statements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The TSP or its record keeper will provide to each participant four (4) times each calendar year the information described in §§ 1640.3, 1640.4, and 1640.5. Plan participants can obtain account balance information on a more frequent basis from the TSP Web site and the ThriftLine.
