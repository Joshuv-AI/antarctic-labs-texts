17 C.F.R. § 240.12d1-6 — d1-6 Withdrawal of certification.
Citation: us:cfr:17:240.12d1-6
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-240.12d1-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 240 (General Rules And Regulations, Securities Exchange Act Of 1934). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An exchange may, by notice to the Commission, withdraw its certification prior to the time that the registration to which it relates first becomes effective pursuant to § 240.12d1-1.
