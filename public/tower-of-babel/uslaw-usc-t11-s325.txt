11 U.S.C. § 325 — Effect of vacancy
Citation: us:usc:11:325
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section325
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 3 (Case Administration); Subchapter II (Officers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A vacancy in the office of trustee during a case does not abate any pending action or proceeding, and the successor trustee shall be substituted as a party in such action or proceeding.
