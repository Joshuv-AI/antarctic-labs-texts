16 C.F.R. § 301.22 — Disclosure of damaged furs.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 301 (Rules And Regulations Under Fur Products Labeling Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The term damaged fur, as used in this part, means a fur, which, because of a known or patent defect resulting from natural causes or from processing, is of such a nature that its use in a fur product would decrease the normal life and durability of such product.
