46 U.S.C. § 50104 — Studies of general maritime problems
Citation: us:usc:46:50104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section50104
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part A (General); Chapter 501 (Policy, Studies, And Reports). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Transportation shall study all maritime problems arising in carrying out the policy in section 50101 of this title.
