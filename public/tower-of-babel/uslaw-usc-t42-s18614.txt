42 U.S.C. § 18614 — Technology transfer and transitions assessment
Citation: us:usc:42:18614
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section18614
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 161 (Department Of Energy Research And Innovation); Subchapter I (Laboratory Modernization And Technology Transfer). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 1 year after September 28, 2018, and as often as the Secretary determines to be necessary thereafter, the Secretary shall transmit to the appropriate committees of Congress a report that includes recommended changes to the policy of the Department and legislative changes to section 16391 of this title to improve the ability of the Department to successfully transfer new energy technologies to the private sector.
