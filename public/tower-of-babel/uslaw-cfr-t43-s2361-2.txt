43 C.F.R. § 2361.2 — Objectives.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2361.2
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2361.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2360 (National Petroleum Reserve In Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The objective of this subpart is to provide for the protection of the environmental, fish and wildlife, and historical or scenic values of the Reserve so that activities which are or might be detrimental to such values will be carefully controlled to the extent consistent with the requirements of the Act for the exploration and production of oil and gas resources in the Reserve.
