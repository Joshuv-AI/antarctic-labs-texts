10 C.F.R. § 605.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:10:605.1
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-605.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 605 (The Office Of Science Financial Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the policies and procedures applicable to the award and administration of grants and cooperative agreements by the DOE Office of Science (SC) for basic and applied research, educational and/or training activities, conferences and related activities.
