Retraction of: Tracking Plasmodium knowlesi Through Fecal DNA for Monitoring Zoonotic Transmission in Wild Macaques Across Southeast and South Asia
Published: 2026

This is a retraction of Dhammika Leshan Wannigama, Mohan Amarasiri, Phatthranit Phattharapornjaroen, Cameron Hurst, Charin Modchang, Bernadina Cynthia, Kazuhiko Miyanaga, Longzhu Cui, Stefan Fernandez, Nada M Melhem, Sirirat Luk-in, Andrew C Singer, Naveen Kumar Devanga Ragupathi, Thin Sandi Htun, Kazunari Sei, Natharin Ngamwongsatit, Ng Sin Hui, Yoshitaka Shimotai, Puey Ounjai, Phitsanuruk Kanthawee, Ratana Tacharoenmuang, Alice Kauba, Chorney Eang, Lavinia Romone, Mahesh Dharne, Juliana Calabria de Araujo, Lazarus G Ndatuwong, W K C P Werawatte, Traychit Chanthasiri, Jinxin Zhao, Hirotake Mori, John Jefferson V Besa, Özgür Kurt, Talerngsak Kanjanabuch, Ali Reza Zahraei-Ramazani, Paul G Higgins, Tetsuji Aoyagi, Anthony Kicic, Sam Trowsdale, Parichart Hongsing, Xiaonan Yang, Yangzhong Wang, Aisha Khatib, Daisuke Sano, Kenji Shibuya, Shuichi Abe, Hiroshi Hamamoto, Tracking Plasmodium knowlesi Through Fecal DNA for Monitoring Zoonotic Transmission in Wild Macaques Across Southeast and South Asia, The Journal of Infectious Diseases, Volume 233, Issue 5, 15 May 2026, Pages e1238–e1243, https://doi.org/10.1093/infdis/jiag128

Following article publication, a reader notified the Editors of concerns regarding the sample collection methodology and the analyses of sequence data in the article and sequence data in the GenBank database. The Editors conducted an investigation in accordance with guidelines established by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) and contacted the authors, who confirmed that they were unable to fully retrieve and verify the original raw sequencing files and associated metadata.

The article is being retracted by the Editors and the authors in accordance with COPE guidelines, as it has been determined that these issues undermine the reliability of the results and cast doubt on the conclusions of the research. All authors agree with the retraction.
