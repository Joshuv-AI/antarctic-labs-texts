16 U.S.C. § 590z–10 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:16:590z–10
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section590z–10
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3C (Water Conservation); Subchapter II (Conservation And Utilization Projects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To carry out the purposes of this subchapter there is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any money in the Treasury not otherwise appropriated (1) for the Department of the Interior such sums as may be necessary to carry out its functions under this subchapter, and (2) for the Department of Agriculture such sums as may be necessary to carry out its functions under this subchapter.
