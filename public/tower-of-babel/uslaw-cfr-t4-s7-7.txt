4 C.F.R. § 7.7 — Other appeals and grievances.
Citation: us:cfr:4:7.7
Creator: Government Accountability Office
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-4/section-7.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 4 (Accounts), Part 7 (Personnel Relations And Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The personnel system shall provide procedures for the processing of complaints and grievances which are not otherwise provided for.
