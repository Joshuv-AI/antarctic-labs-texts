19 U.S.C. § 81t — Separability
Citation: us:usc:19:81t
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section81t
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 1A (Foreign Trade Zones). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application of such provision to certain circumstances be held invalid, the remainder of this chapter and the application of such provisions to circumstances other than those as to which it is held invalid shall not be affected thereby.
