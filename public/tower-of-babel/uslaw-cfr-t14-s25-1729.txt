14 C.F.R. § 25.1729 — Instructions for Continued Airworthiness: EWIS.
Citation: us:cfr:14:25.1729
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-25.1729
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 25 (Airworthiness Standards: Transport Category Airplanes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The applicant must prepare Instructions for Continued Airworthiness applicable to EWIS in accordance with Appendix H sections H25.4 and H25.5 to this part that are approved by the FAA.
