29 C.F.R. § 570.141 — Good faith defense.
Citation: us:cfr:29:570.141
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-570.141
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 570 (Child Labor Regulations, Orders And Statements Of Interpretation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A provision is contained in section 12(a) of the Act relieving any purchaser from liability thereunder who ships or delivers for shipment in commerce goods which he acquired in good faith in reliance on written assurance from the producer, manufacturer, or dealer that the goods were produced in compliance with section 12, and which he acquired for value without notice of any violation. 36
