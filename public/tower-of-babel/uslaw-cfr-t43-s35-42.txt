43 C.F.R. § 35.42 — Judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:43:35.42
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-35.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 35 (Administrative Remedies For Fraudulent Claims And Statements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 3805 of title 31, U.S. Code, authorizes judicial review by an appropriate U.S. District Court of a final decision of the Secretary imposing penalties or assessment under this part and specifies the procedures for such review.
