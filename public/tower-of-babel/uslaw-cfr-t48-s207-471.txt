48 C.F.R. § 207.471 — 207.471 Funding requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:48:207.471
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-207.471
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 207 (Acquisition Planning). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Fund leases in accordance with DoD Financial Management Regulation (FMR) 7000.14-R, Volume 2A, Chapter 1.
(b) DoD leases are either capital leases or operating leases. See FMR 7000.14-R, Volume 4, Chapter 6, section 060206.
(c) Use procurement funds for capital leases, as these are essentially installment purchases of property.
