41 C.F.R. § 302-9.301 — -9.301 Authorized origin and destination points when transporting a POV within CONUS.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-9.301
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-9.301
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-9 (9—Allowances For Transportation And Emergency Or Temporary Storage Of A Privately Owned Vehicle). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If authorized to transport a POV within CONUS, the transportation must originate as shown in paragraphs (a) and (b) of this section; the destination must be the new official station:
(a) Transferee—old official station.
(b) New appointee or student trainee—place of actual residence.
