24 C.F.R. § 201.6 — Disclosure and verification of Social Security and Employer Identification Numbers.
Citation: us:cfr:24:201.6
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-201.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 201 (Title I Property Improvement And Manufactured Home Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be eligible for loan insurance under this part, the borrower must meet the requirements for the disclosure and verification of Social Security and Employer Identification Numbers, as provided by part 200, subpart U, of this chapter.
