Author Correction: Genome analysis of Channel millet reveals a wild dodecaploid shaped by environmental variability
Authors: Rahul Chandora, Agnelo Furtado, Lena Constantin, Jennifer Silcock, Michael C Westaway, Robert J Henry
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-026-73794-7, published online 30 May 2026

In the version of this article initially published, there was an error in the number of S. italica nucleotide-binding site–leucine-rich repeats (NLRs) presented in Supplementary Figs. 27, 28 and in the “Evolutionary dynamics of disease resistance genes” section of the Results, where in the text now reading “Lineage effects were evident; domesticated diploid S. italica harboured 368 NLRs,” 368 appeared originally as “688.” The text and Supplementary Information are now amended in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
