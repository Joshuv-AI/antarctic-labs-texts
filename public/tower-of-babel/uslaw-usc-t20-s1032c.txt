20 U.S.C. § 1032c — Evaluation
Citation: us:usc:20:1032c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1032c
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter II (Teacher Quality Enhancement); Part B (Enhancing Teacher Education); Subpart 1 (Preparing Teachers For Digital Age Learners). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not less than ten percent of the funds awarded to an eligible consortium to carry out a project under this subpart shall be used to evaluate the effectiveness of such project.
