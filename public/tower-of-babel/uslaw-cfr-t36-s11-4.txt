36 C.F.R. § 11.4 — Penalties.
Citation: us:cfr:36:11.4
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-11.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 11 (Arrowhead And Parkscape Symbols). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whoever manufactures, sells or uses the “Arrowhead Symbol” or the “Parkscape Symbol” in violation of the regulations of this part shall be subject to the penalties prescribed in section 701 of title 18 of the United States Code.
