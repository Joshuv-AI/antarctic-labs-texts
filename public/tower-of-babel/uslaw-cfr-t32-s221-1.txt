32 C.F.R. § 221.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:32:221.1
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-221.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 221 (Dod Identity Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The purpose of the overall part is to implement policy, assign responsibilities, and provide procedures for DoD personnel identification.
(b) This part establishes implementation guidelines for DoD Self-Service (DS) Logon Program.
