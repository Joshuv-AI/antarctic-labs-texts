41 C.F.R. § 102-118.50 — -118.50 Government contractor issued charge cards.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-118.50
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-118.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-118 (118—Transportation Payment And Audit). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Government contractor issued charge card:
(a) May be used to acquire freight and small parcel transportation.
(b) Must be used (except when a GTR is allowed) for passenger transportation.
