29 U.S.C. § 1398 — Withdrawal not to occur because of change in business form or suspension of contributions during labor dispute
Citation: us:usc:29:1398
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section1398
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 18 (Employee Retirement Income Security Program); Subchapter III (Plan Termination Insurance); Part 1 (Employer Withdrawals). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of this part, an employer shall not be considered to have withdrawn from a plan solely because—
(1)
an employer ceases to exist by reason of—
(A)
a change in corporate structure described in section 1369(b) of this title, or
(B)
a change to an unincorporated form of business enterprise,
if the change causes no interruption in employer contributions or obligations to contribute under the plan, or
(2)
an employer suspends contributions under the plan during a labor dispute involving its employees.
For purposes of this part, a successor or parent corporation or other entity resulting from any such change shall be considered the original employer.
