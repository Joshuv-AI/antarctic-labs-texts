7 C.F.R. § 201.26a — a Vegetable seed mixtures.
Citation: us:cfr:7:201.26a
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-201.26a
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 201 (Federal Seed Act Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Vegetable seed mixtures for seeding/planting purposes shall be designated as a mixture on the label, and each seed component shall be listed on the label in the order of predominance.
