15 C.F.R. § 908.21 — Report form.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 908 (Maintaining Records And Submitting Reports On Weather Modification Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Public Law 92-205 and these rules should be studied carefully prior to reporting. Reports required by these rules shall be submitted on forms obtainable on request from the Administrator, or on an equivalent format. 1 In special situations, such alterations to the forms as the circumstances thereto may render necessary may be made, provided they do not depart from the requirements of these rules or of Public Law 92-205.
