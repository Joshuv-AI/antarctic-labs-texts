21 C.F.R. § 1306.01 — Scope of part 1306.
Citation: us:cfr:21:1306.01
Creator: Drug Enforcement Administration, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1306.01
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1306 (Prescriptions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth the process and procedures for dispensing, by way of prescribing and administering controlled substances to ultimate users. The purpose of such procedures is to provide safe and efficient methods for dispensing controlled substances while providing effective controls against diversion.
