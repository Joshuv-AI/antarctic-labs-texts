45 C.F.R. § 57.4 — Acceptance and use of volunteer services.
Citation: us:cfr:45:57.4
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-57.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 57 (Volunteer Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary, or his designee, shall establish requirements for: Accepting volunteer services from individuals or groups of individuals, using volunteer services, giving appropriate recognition to volunteers, and maintaining records of volunteer services.
