5 C.F.R. § 6.4 — Removal of incumbents of excepted positions.
Citation: us:cfr:5:6.4
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-6.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 6 (Exceptions From The Competitive Service (Rule Vi)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as required by statute, the Civil Service Rules and Regulations shall not apply to removals from positions listed in Schedules A, C, D, E, Policy/Career, or G, or from positions excepted from the competitive service by statute. The Civil Service Rules and Regulations shall apply to removals from positions listed in Schedule B of persons who have competitive status.
