33 C.F.R. § 160.105 — Compliance with orders.
Citation: us:cfr:33:160.105
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-160.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 160 (Ports And Waterways Safety—General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each person who has notice of the terms of an order issued under this subpart must comply with that order.
