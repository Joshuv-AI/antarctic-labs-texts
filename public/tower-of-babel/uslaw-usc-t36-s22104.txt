36 U.S.C. § 22104 — Powers
Citation: us:usc:36:22104
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section22104
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 221 (The American Society Of International Law). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation may—
(1)
adopt and amend a constitution, bylaws, and regulations for the management of its property and the regulation of its affairs;
(2)
adopt and alter a corporate seal;
(3)
choose officers, managers, and agents as the activities of the corporation require;
(4)
make contracts;
(5)
acquire, own, lease, encumber, and transfer property as necessary to carry out the purposes of the corporation;
(6)
borrow money, issue instruments of indebtedness, and secure its obligations by granting security interests in its property;
(7)
publish a journal and other publications;
(8)
sue and be sued; and
(9)
do any other act necessary and proper to carry out the purposes of the corporation.
