14 U.S.C. § 314 — Captains of the port
Citation: us:usc:14:314
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section314
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 3 (Composition And Organization); Subchapter II (Positions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any officer, including any petty officer, may be designated by the Commandant as captain of the port or ports or adjacent high seas or waters over which the United States has jurisdiction, as the Commandant deems necessary to facilitate execution of Coast Guard duties.
