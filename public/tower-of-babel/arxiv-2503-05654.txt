p-adic Delsarte-Goethals-Seidel-Kabatianskii-Levenshtein-Pfender Bound
K. Mahesh Krishna

Abstract




Abstract
: We introduce the notion of p-adic spherical codes (in particular, p-adic kissing number problem). We show that the one-line proof for a variant of the Delsarte-Goethals-Seidel-Kabatianskii-Levenshtein upper bound for spherical codes, obtained by Pfender
[J. Combin. Theory Ser. A, 2007]
, extends to p-adic Hilbert spaces.

Keywords
: Spherical code, Kissing number, Linear programming, p-adic Hilbert space.

Mathematics Subject Classification (2020)
: 52C17, 12J25, 46S10, 11D88.

1.
Introduction

Let
d\in\mathbb{N}
and
\theta\in[0,2\pi)
. A set
\{\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
of unit vectors in
\mathbb{R}^{d}
is said to be
(d,n,\theta)
-spherical code

[
42
]
in
\mathbb{R}^{d}
if

(1)

\displaystyle\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle\leq\cos\theta,\quad\forall 1\leq j,k\leq n,j\neq k.

Since

\displaystyle\langle\tau,\omega\rangle=\frac{2-\|\tau-\omega\|^{2}}{2},\quad\forall\tau,\omega\in\mathbb{R}^{d},

we can rewrite Inequality (
1
) as

\displaystyle\|\tau_{j}-\tau_{k}\|\geq\sqrt{2(1-\cos\theta)},\quad\forall 1\leq j,k\leq n,j\neq k.

Fundamental problem associated with spherical codes is the following.

n

(d,n,\theta)

\{\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
in
\mathbb{R}^{d}
?

Problem
1.1
has connection even with sphere packing
[
16
]
. Most used method for obtaining upper bounds on spherical codes is the Delsarte-Goethals-Seidel-Kabatianskii-Levenshtein
bound which we recall. Let
n\in\mathbb{N}
be fixed. The Gegenbauer polynomials are defined inductively as

\displaystyle G_{0}^{(n)}(r)

\displaystyle\coloneqq 1,\quad\forall r\in[-1,1],

\displaystyle G_{1}^{(n)}(r)

\displaystyle\coloneqq r,\quad\forall r\in[-1,1],

\displaystyle\quad\vdots

\displaystyle G_{k}^{(n)}(r)

\displaystyle\coloneqq\frac{(2k+n-4)rG_{k-1}^{(n)}(r)-(k-1)G_{k-2}^{(n)}(r)}{k+n-3},\quad\forall r\in[-1,1],\quad\forall k\geq 2.

Then the family
\{G_{k}^{(n)}\}_{k=0}^{\infty}
is orthogonal on the interval
[-1,1]
with respect to the weight

\displaystyle\rho(r)\coloneqq(1-r^{2})^{\frac{n-3}{2}},\quad\forall r\in[-1,1].

Theorem 1.2
.

[
18
,
21
]
(
Delsarte-Goethals-Seidel-Kabatianskii-Levenshtein Linear Programming Bound
)
Let
\{\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
be a
(d,n,\theta)
-spherical code in
\mathbb{R}^{d}
.
Let
P
be a real polynomial satisfying following conditions.

(i)

P(r)\leq 0
for all
-1\leq r\leq\cos\theta
.

(ii)

Coefficients in the Gegenbauer expansion

\displaystyle P=\sum_{k=0}^{m}a_{k}G_{k}^{(n)}

satisfy

\displaystyle a_{0}>0,\quad a_{k}\geq 0,~\forall 1\leq k\leq m.

Then

\displaystyle n\leq\frac{P(1)}{a_{0}}.

In 2007, Pfender gave a one-line proof for a variant of Theorem
1.2
.

Theorem 1.3
.

[
36
]
(
Delsarte-Goethals-Seidel-Kabatianskii-Levenshtein-Pfender Bound
)
Let
\{\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
be a
(d,n,\theta)
-spherical code in
\mathbb{R}^{d}
. Let
c>0
and
\phi:[-1,1]\to\mathbb{R}
be a function satisfying following.

(i)

\displaystyle\sum_{j=1}^{n}\sum_{k=1}^{n}\phi(\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle)\geq 0.

(ii)

\phi(r)+c\leq 0
for all
-1\leq r\leq\cos\theta
.

Then

\displaystyle n\leq\frac{\phi(1)+c}{c}.

In particular, if
\phi(1)+c\leq 1
, then
n\leq 1/c
.

In this paper, we introduce the notion of p-adic spherical codes. We show that Theorem
1.3
, can be easily extended for p-adic Hilbert spaces.

2.
p-adic spherical Codes

We begin from the definition of p-adic Hilbert space.

Definition 2.1
.

[
19
,
24
,
20
]

Let
\mathbb{K}
be a non-Archimedean complete valued field (with valuation
|\cdot|
) and
\mathcal{X}
be a non-Archimedean Banach space (with norm
\|\cdot\|
) over
\mathbb{K}
. We say that
\mathcal{X}
is a
p-adic Hilbert space
if there is a map (called as p-adic inner product)
\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle:\mathcal{X}\times\mathcal{X}\to\mathbb{K}
satisfying following.

(i)

If
x\in\mathcal{X}
is such that
\langle x,y\rangle=0
for all
y\in\mathcal{X}
, then
x=0
.

(ii)

\langle x,y\rangle=\langle y,x\rangle
for all
x,y\in\mathcal{X}
.

(iii)

\langle\alpha x+y,z\rangle=\alpha\langle x,z\rangle+\langle y,z\rangle
for all
\alpha\in\mathbb{K}
, for all
x,y,z\in\mathcal{X}
.

(iv)

|\langle x,y\rangle|\leq\|x\|\|y\|
for all
x,y\in\mathcal{X}
.

Following is the standard example which we consider in the paper.

Example 2.2
.

[
24
]

Let
p
be a prime. For
d\in\mathbb{N}
, let
\mathbb{Q}_{p}^{d}
be the standard p-adic Hilbert space equipped with the inner product

\displaystyle\langle(a_{j})_{j=1}^{d},(b_{j})_{j=1}^{d}\rangle:=\sum_{j=1}^{d}a_{j}b_{j},\quad\forall(a_{j})_{j=1}^{d},(b_{j})_{j=1}^{d}\in\mathbb{Q}_{p}^{d}

and norm

\displaystyle\|(x_{j})_{j=1}^{d}\|:=\max_{1\leq j\leq d}|x_{j}|,\quad\forall(x_{j})_{j=1}^{d}\in\mathbb{Q}_{p}^{d}.

We introduce p-adic spherical codes as follows.

Definition 2.3
.

Let
d\in\mathbb{N}
and
\theta\in[0,2\pi)
. A set
\{\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
of vectors in
\mathbb{Q}_{p}^{d}
is said to be
p-adic
(d,n,\theta)
-spherical code
in
\mathbb{Q}_{p}^{d}
if following conditions hold.

(i)

\|\tau_{j}\|=1
for all
1\leq j\leq n
.

(ii)

\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{j}\rangle=1
for all
1\leq j\leq n
.

(iii)

(2)

|2-2\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|\geq 2(1-\cos\theta),\quad\forall 1\leq j,k\leq n,j\neq k.

We call the case
\theta=\pi/3
as the
p-adic kissing number problem
.

Let
\{\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
be a p-adic
(d,n,\theta)
-spherical code in
\mathbb{Q}_{p}^{d}
.
Since

\displaystyle\|\tau_{j}-\tau_{k}\|^{2}\geq|\langle\tau_{j}-\tau_{k},\tau_{j}-\tau_{k}\rangle|=|2-2\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|,\quad\forall 1\leq j,k\leq n,

Inequality (
2
) gives

(3)

\displaystyle\|\tau_{j}-\tau_{k}\|\geq\sqrt{2(1-\cos\theta)},\quad\forall 1\leq j,k\leq n,j\neq k.

However, note that Inequality (
3
) may not give Inequality (
2
). Note that we can formulate the definition of general p-adic
(d,n,\theta)
-spherical code by replacing Inequality (
2
) with Inequality (
3
) in Definition
2.3
. It is also possible to consider Definition
2.3
by removing conditions (i) or (ii).
Following is the p-adic version of Theorem
1.3
.

Theorem 2.4
.

(
p-adic Delsarte-Goethals-Seidel-Kabatianskii-Levenshtein-Pfender Spherical Codes Bound
)
Let
\{\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
be a p-adic
(d,n,\theta)
-spherical code in
\mathbb{Q}_{p}^{d}
. Let
c>0
and
\phi:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb{R}
be a function satisfying following.

(i)

\sum_{1\leq j,k\leq n}\phi(|2-2\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|)\geq 0
.

(ii)

\phi(r)+c\leq 0
for all
r\in[2(1-\cos\theta),\infty)
.

Then

\displaystyle n\leq\frac{\phi(0)+c}{c}.

In particular, if
\phi(0)+c\leq 1
, then
n\leq 1/c
.

Proof.

Define
\psi:[0,\infty)\ni r\mapsto\psi(r)\coloneqq\phi(r)+c\in\mathbb{R}
. Then

\displaystyle\sum_{1\leq j,k\leq n}\psi(|2-2\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|)

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\psi(|2-2\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{j}\rangle|)+\sum_{1\leq j,k\leq n,j\neq k}\psi(|2-2\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|)

\displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\psi(0)+\sum_{1\leq j,k\leq n,j\neq k}\psi(|2-2\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|)

\displaystyle=n(\phi(0)+c)+\sum_{1\leq j,k\leq n,j\neq k}(\phi(|2-2\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|+c)

\displaystyle\leq n(\phi(0)+c)+0=n(\phi(0)+c).

We also have

\displaystyle\sum_{1\leq j,k\leq n}\psi(\phi(|2-2\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|))=\sum_{1\leq j,k\leq n}(\phi(|2-2\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|)+c)=\sum_{1\leq j,k\leq n}\phi(|2-2\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|)+cn^{2}.

Therefore

\displaystyle cn^{2}\leq\sum_{1\leq j,k\leq n}\phi(|2-2\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|)+cn^{2}\leq n(\phi(0)+c).

∎

Corollary 2.5
.

(
p-adic Delsarte-Goethals-Seidel-Kabatianskii-Levenshtein-Pfender Kissing Number Bound
)
Let
\{\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
be a p-adic
(d,n,\pi/3)
-spherical code in
\mathbb{Q}_{p}^{d}
. Let
c>0
and
\phi:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb{R}
be a function satisfying following.

(i)

\sum_{1\leq j,k\leq n}\phi(|2-2\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|)\geq 0
.

(ii)

\phi(r)+c\leq 0
for all
r\in[1,\infty)
.

Then

\displaystyle n\leq\frac{\phi(0)+c}{c}.

In particular, if
\phi(0)+c\leq 1
, then
n\leq 1/c
.

Following generalization of Theorem
2.4
is easy.

Theorem 2.6
.

Let
\{\tau_{j}\}_{j=1}^{n}
be a p-adic
(d,n,\theta)
-spherical code in
\mathbb{Q}_{p}^{d}
. Let
c>0
and

\displaystyle\phi:\{|2-2\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|:1\leq j,k\leq n\}\to\mathbb{R}

be a function satisfying following.

(i)

\sum_{1\leq j,k\leq n}\phi(|2-2\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|)\geq 0
.

(ii)

\phi(r)+c\leq 0
for all
r\in\{|2-2\langle\tau_{j},\tau_{k}\rangle|:1\leq j,k\leq n,j\neq k\}
.

Then

\displaystyle n\leq\frac{\phi(0)+c}{c}.

In particular, if
\phi(0)+c\leq 1
, then
n\leq 1/c
.

Note that, in the paper, we can replace
\mathbb{Q}_{p}^{d}
by any p-adic Hilbert space.
