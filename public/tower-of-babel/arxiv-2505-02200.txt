Automatic boundedness of some operators between ordered and topological vector spaces


Abstract
We study topological boundedness of order-to-topology bounded and order-to-topology continuous operators from ordered vector spaces to topological vector spaces. The uniform boundedness principle for such operators is investigated.

Main results

We start with the following theorem which asserts that collectively order-to-topology bounded sets
quite often agree with collectively
ru
-to-topology continuous sets.

Theorem 2.1
.

Let
X
be an OVS and
(Y,\tau)
a TVS. Then
\text{\bf L}_{o{\tau}b}(X,Y)\subseteq\text{\bf L}_{r{\tau}c}(X,Y)
.
If additionally
X_{+}
is generating then
\text{\bf L}_{o{\tau}b}(X,Y)=\text{\bf L}_{r{\tau}c}(X,Y)
.

Proof.

Assume, in contrary,
{\cal T}\in\text{\bf L}_{o{\tau}b}(X,Y)\setminus\text{\bf L}_{r{\tau}c}(X,Y)
.
Then,
{\cal T}x_{\alpha}\not\convctau 0
for some
x_{\alpha}\convr 0
. So, there exists an absorbing
U\in\tau(0)
such that,
for every
\alpha
there exist
\alpha^{\prime}\geq\alpha
and
T_{\alpha}\in{\cal T}
with
T_{\alpha}x_{\alpha^{\prime}}\notin U
.
Since
x_{\alpha}\convr 0
, for some
u\in X_{+}
there exists an increasing sequence

(\alpha_{n})
of indices with
\pm nx_{\alpha}\leq u
for
\alpha\geq\alpha_{n}
.
It follows from
{\cal T}\in\text{\bf L}_{o{\tau}b}(X,Y)
that

{\cal T}[-u,u]\subseteq NU
for some
N\in\mathbb{N}
.
Since
nx_{\alpha}\in[-u,u]
for
\alpha\geq\alpha_{n}
and
(\alpha_{n})^{\prime}\geq\alpha_{n}
then

nx_{(\alpha_{n})^{\prime}}\in[-u,u]
, and hence
T_{\alpha_{n}}(nx_{(\alpha_{n})^{\prime}})\in NU
for every
n
.
In particular,
T_{\alpha_{N}}(x_{(\alpha_{N})^{\prime}})\in U
which is absurd.
We conclude
\text{\bf L}_{o{\tau}b}(X,Y)\subseteq\text{\bf L}_{r{\tau}c}(X,Y)
.

Now, suppose
X_{+}
is generating, and let
{\cal T}\in\text{\bf L}_{r{\tau}c}(X,Y)
. Assume, in contrary,
{\cal T}\notin\text{\bf L}_{o{\tau}b}(X,Y)
.
Since
X=X_{+}-X_{+}
, there exist
x\in X_{+}
and an absorbing
U\in\tau(0)
with
{\cal T}[-x,x]\not\subseteq nU
for every
n\in\mathbb{N}
.
Find sequences
(x_{n})
in
[-x,x]
and
(T_{n})
in
{\cal T}
with
T_{n}x_{n}\notin nU
for all
n
.
Since
\frac{1}{n}x_{n}\convr 0
then
{\cal T}(\frac{1}{n}x_{n})\convctau 0
.
So, there exists a sequence
(n_{k})
such that
T(\frac{1}{n}x_{n})\in U
for all
n\geq n_{k}
and
T\in{\cal T}
.
Then
T_{n_{1}}x_{n_{1}}\in n_{1}U
, which is a contradiction. Therefore,
{\cal T}\in\text{\bf L}_{o{\tau}b}(X,Y)
.
The proof is complete.
∎

Theorem 2.5

Let

\text{\bf L}_{o{\tau}b}(X,Y)\subseteq\text{\bf L}_{n{\tau}b}(X,Y)

Corollary 2.6

Every collectively order-to-norm bounded set of operators from an OBS with a closed generating cone to a NS is uniformly bounded.

Corollary 2.7

Every order-to-norm bounded operator from an OBS with a closed generating cone to a NS is bounded.

Corollary 2.9

Every order-to-weak compact operator from an OBS with a closed generating cone to a Banach space is continuous.

Let

Let

be
o
\tau

Then

is not bounded for some

Since

[0,u]
is bounded, say
\sup\limits_{x\in[0,u]}\|x\|\leq M

Pick a sequence

-
\sum\limits_{k=n}^{\infty}2^{-k}u_{k}

Then

. Let

for all

Since

Since

then
y_{n}\convo 0
. We deduce from

.
Since the topology

.
Therefore, the sequence

is norm bounded. This is absurd because

Corollary 2.16

Every order-to-topology continuous operator from a normal OBS to a Banach space with a dual topology is order-to-norm bounded.
