30 C.F.R. § 282.31 — Suspension of production or other operations.
Citation: us:cfr:30:282.31
Creator: Bureau Of Safety And Environmental Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-282.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 282 (Operations In The Outer Continental Shelf For Minerals Other Than Oil, Gas, And Sulphur). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A lessee may submit a request for a suspension of production or other operations. The request shall include justification for granting the requested suspension, a schedule of work leading to the initiation or restoration of production or other operations, and any other information the Director may require.
