20 C.F.R. § 408.425 — How do we establish your eligibility for SSI?
Citation: us:cfr:20:408.425
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-408.425
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 408 (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To qualify for SVB, you must have been eligible for SSI for the month of December 1999, the month in which Public Law 106-169 was enacted, and for the month in which you filed your application for SVB. You do not have to submit evidence of this. We will use our SSI record of your eligibility to determine if you meet these requirements.
