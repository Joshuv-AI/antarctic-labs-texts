Exponential Decays of Steklov Eigenfunctions for the Magnetic Laplacian
Zhongwei Shen

Abstract
Consider the Dirichlet-to-Neumann map Λ β \Lambda_{\beta} associated with the Schrödinger operator ( D + β ​ 𝐀 ) 2 (D+\beta\mathbf{A})^{2} with a magnetic potential in a bounded Lipschitz domain Ω \Omega , where β > 1 \beta>1 is the field strength parameter. Assume that the magnetic field 𝐁 = ∇ × 𝐀 \mathbf{B}=\nabla\times\mathbf{A} is of finite type. We show that if β > β 0 \beta>\beta_{0} , the ground state for Λ β \Lambda_{\beta} decays exponentially away from a neighborhood of the subset of ∂ Ω \partial\Omega , on which 𝐁 \mathbf{B} vanishes to the maximal order. Keywords . Steklov Eigenfunction; Exponential Decay; Schrödinger Operator; Magnetic Field. MR (2020) Subject Classification : 35P20; 35Q40.

Let

Consider the Schrödinger operator with a magnetic potential,

also called the magnetic Laplacian,
where

The asymptotic behaviors of eigenvalues and eigenfunctions for the operator (

\partial\Omega

The asymptotic expansions of the ground state energies, with remainder estimates,

under various assumptions.
For a survey and references in this area as well as applications to the theory of superconductivity,
we refer the reader to two expository books,
[
4

by S. Fournais and B. Helffer and
[
13
]
by N. Raymond.

,

,

,

,

,

]
.
Moreover, the ground states decay exponentially away from the subset of

, on which

vanishes to the highest order

,

In recent years, there is a growing interest in the study of spectrum properties of

For references in the case of the Laplacian on a compact manifold, we refer the reader to a survey article

Let

\partial\Omega
.
The Dirichlet-to-Neumann map associated with

is defined by

\Lambda_{\beta}:f\to n\cdot(D+\beta\mathbf{A})u,

(1.2)

where

\left\{\begin{aligned} (D+\beta\mathbf{A})^{2}u&=0&\quad&\text{ in }\Omega,\\
u&=f&\quad&\text{ on }\partial\Omega.\end{aligned}\right.

Let

Under the assumption that

c\beta^{\frac{1}{\kappa_{0}+2}}\leq\lambda^{D\!N}(\beta\mathbf{A},\Omega)\leq C\beta^{\frac{1}{\kappa_{0}+2}}

,

The purpose of this paper is to study the exponential decays of eigenfunctions for the operator

.

We shall assume that the magnetic field

is of finite type on
\overline{\Omega}

throughout this paper.
This means that there exist some integer
\kappa_{*}\geq 0

\sum_{|\alpha|\leq\kappa_{*}}|\partial^{\alpha}\mathbf{B}(x)|\geq\tau_{*}

.
The following is the main result of the paper.

Theorem 1.1
.

Let
\Omega
be a bounded Lipschitz domain in
\mathbb{R}^{d}
,
d\geq 2
and
\mathbf{A}\in C^{\infty}(\overline{\Omega},\mathbb{R}^{d})
.
Assume that
\mathbf{B}
is of finite type on
\overline{\Omega}
.
Let
u\in H^{1}(\Omega;\mathbb{C})
be a weak solution of

\left\{\begin{aligned} (D+\beta\mathbf{A})^{2}u&=0&\quad&\text{ in }\Omega,\\
n\cdot(D+\beta\mathbf{A})u&=\lambda u&\quad&\text{ on }\partial\Omega\end{aligned}\right.

(1.6)

for some
\lambda>0
.
Then, if
\beta>\beta_{0}
,

|u(x)|\leq C\lambda^{\frac{d-1}{2}}\exp\left\{-\varepsilon d_{\beta}(x,W_{\beta}(C\lambda))\right\}\|u\|_{L^{2}(\partial\Omega)}

(1.7)

for any
x\in\overline{\Omega}
.
The constants
C>1,\beta_{0}>1,\varepsilon>0
depend only on
\Omega
and
\mathbf{B}
.

We need to introduce the notations used in (
1.7
). First, for
t>0
,

W_{\beta}(t)=\big\{x\in\partial\Omega:\widetilde{m}(x,\beta\mathbf{B})\leq t\big\}

is a magnetic well on the boundary
\partial\Omega
, where

\widetilde{m}(x,\beta\mathbf{B})=\sum_{|\alpha|\leq\kappa_{*}}\beta^{\frac{1}{|\alpha|+2}}|\partial^{\alpha}\mathbf{B}(x)|^{\frac{1}{|\alpha|+2}}.

(1.9)

Next, for
x\in\overline{\Omega}
and
E\subset\overline{\Omega}
, the distance
d_{\beta}(x,E)
is defined by

d_{\beta}(x,E)=\inf\big\{d_{\beta}(x,y):\ y\in E\big\},

where
d_{\beta}(x,y)
is an Agmon distance with respect to the metric
\widetilde{m}(x,\beta\mathbf{B})ds^{2}
, given by

d_{\beta}(x,y)=\inf\left\{\int_{0}^{1}\widetilde{m}(\gamma(t),\beta\mathbf{B})|\gamma^{\prime}(t)|\,dt:\ \ \ \gamma:[0,1]\to\overline{\Omega},\gamma(0)=x\text{ and }\gamma(1)=y\right\}.

(1.10)

Let
x\in\overline{\Omega}
.
Under the finite-type condition (
1.5
), there exists an integer
\kappa=\kappa(x)\geq 0
such that

\partial^{\alpha}\mathbf{B}(x)=0
for any
\alpha
with
|\alpha|\leq\kappa-1
, and

\partial^{\alpha}\mathbf{B}(x)\neq 0
for some
\alpha
with
|\alpha|=\kappa
.
Let

\kappa_{0}=\max\{\kappa(x):x\in\partial\Omega\}

(1.11)

and

\Gamma_{0}=\big\{x\in\partial\Omega:\kappa(x)=\kappa_{0}\big\}.

Note that by (
1.4
), if
\lambda=\lambda^{D\!N}(\beta\mathbf{A},\Omega)
, then
\lambda\approx\beta^{\frac{1}{\kappa_{0}+2}}
and

W_{\beta}(C\lambda)\subset\bigg\{x\in\partial\Omega:\sum_{|\alpha|\leq\kappa_{0}-1}\beta^{\frac{1}{|\alpha|+2}}|\partial^{\alpha}\mathbf{B}(x)|^{\frac{1}{|\alpha|+2}}\leq C\beta^{\frac{1}{\kappa_{0}+2}}\bigg\}

(1.12)

for
\beta>\beta_{0}
.
It follows by Theorem
1.1

that the eigenfunctions associated with
\lambda^{D\!N}(\beta\mathbf{A},\Omega)

are exponentially localized in a neighborhood of
\Gamma_{0}
.

For

It follows that

\displaystyle(D+\mathbf{A})^{2}(ue^{\varepsilon\psi})

\displaystyle=2(D+\mathbf{A})(ue^{\varepsilon\psi})\cdot\varepsilon D\psi+ue^{\varepsilon\psi}\left\{-\varepsilon^{2}D\psi\cdot D\psi+\varepsilon D^{2}\psi\right\}.

\displaystyle\int_{\Omega}|(D+\mathbf{A})(ue^{\varepsilon\psi})|^{2}

\displaystyle=\int_{\Omega}(D+\mathbf{A})^{2}(ue^{\varepsilon\psi})\cdot\overline{u}e^{\varepsilon\psi}+i\int_{\partial\Omega}n\cdot(D+\mathbf{A})(ue^{\varepsilon\psi})\cdot\overline{u}e^{\varepsilon\psi}

\displaystyle+\int_{\Omega}e^{2\varepsilon\psi}|u|^{2}\left\{-\varepsilon^{2}D\psi\cdot D\psi+\varepsilon D^{2}\psi\right\}

\displaystyle+i\int_{\partial\Omega}g\overline{u}e^{2\varepsilon\psi}+i\varepsilon\int_{\partial\Omega}n\cdot D\psi|u|^{2}e^{2\varepsilon\psi}.

The inequality (

the equality above.
Note that we have used the integration by parts in a Lipschitz domain

This can justified by approximating

from inside by a sequence of smooth domains
with uniform Lipschitz characters.
