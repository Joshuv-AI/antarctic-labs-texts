41 C.F.R. § 101-26.704 — -26.704 Purchase of nonperishable subsistence (NPS) items.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-26.704
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-26.704
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-26 (26—Procurement Sources And Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

With the exception of condiment packages in Federal supply classes 8940 and 8950, managed by the Defense Logistics Agency's Defense Personnel Support Center, all nonperishable subsistence items in Federal supply group 89, Subsistence Items, are managed by and available from the Veterans Administration (VA). These items are listed in the Subsistence Catalog, which is available from the Director, Supply Service (134A), Veterans Administration, Washington, DC 20420.
