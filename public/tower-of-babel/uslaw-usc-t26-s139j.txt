26 U.S.C. § 139J — Certain contributions to Trump accounts
Citation: us:usc:26:139J
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section139J
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 1 (Normal Taxes And Surtaxes); Subchapter B (Computation Of Taxable Income); Part III (Items Specifically Excluded From Gross Income). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Gross income of an account beneficiary shall not include any qualified general contribution to a Trump account of the account beneficiary.
(b) Definitions
Any term used in this section which is used in section 530A shall have the meaning given such term under section 530A.
