5 C.F.R. § 838.1016 — Receipt of multiple court orders.
Citation: us:cfr:5:838.1016
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-838.1016
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 838 (Court Orders Affecting Retirement Benefits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event that OPM receives two or more qualifying court orders—
(a) When there are two or more former spouses, the court orders will be honored in the order in which they were issued to the maximum extent possible under §§ 831.641 of this chapter and 838.1006.
(b) Where there are two or more court orders relating to the same former spouse, the one issued last will be honored.
