9 C.F.R. § 309.10 — Onset of parturition.
Citation: us:cfr:9:309.10
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-309.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 309 (Ante-Mortem Inspection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any livestock showing signs of the onset of parturition shall be withheld from slaughter until after parturition and passage of the placenta. Slaughter or other disposition may then be permitted if the animal is otherwise acceptable.
