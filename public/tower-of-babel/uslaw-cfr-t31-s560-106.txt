31 C.F.R. § 560.106 — Delegation of certain authorities of the Secretary of the Treasury.
Citation: us:cfr:31:560.106
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-560.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 560 (Iranian Transactions And Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any action that the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized to take pursuant to: E.O. 12613 of October 29, 1987, E.O. 12957 of March 15, 1995, E.O. 12959 of May 6, 1995, E.O. 13059 of August 19, 1997, E.O. 13599 of February 5, 2012, E.O. 13846 of August 6, 2018, and any further Executive orders relating to the national emergency declared in E.O. 12957, may be taken by the Director of OFAC or by any other person to whom the Secretary of the Treasury has delegated authority so to act.
