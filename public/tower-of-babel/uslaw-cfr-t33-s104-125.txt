33 C.F.R. § 104.125 — Noncompliance.
Citation: us:cfr:33:104.125
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-104.125
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 104 (Maritime Security: Vessels). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a vessel must temporarily deviate from the requirements of this part, the vessel owner or operator must notify the cognizant COTP, and either suspend operations or request and receive permission from the COTP to continue operating.
