7 U.S.C. § 8203 — Assistance
Citation: us:usc:7:8203
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section8203
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 108 (Tree Assistance Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to section 8204 of this title, the assistance provided by the Secretary to eligible orchardists for losses described in section 8202 of this title shall consist of—
(1)
reimbursement of 75 percent of the cost of replanting trees lost due to a natural disaster, as determined by the Secretary, in excess of 15 percent mortality (adjusted for normal mortality); or
(2)
at the option of the Secretary, sufficient seedlings to reestablish a stand.
