7 U.S.C. § 1949 — Graduation of borrowers with operating loans or guarantees to private commercial credit
Citation: us:usc:7:1949
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1949
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 50 (Agricultural Credit); Subchapter II (Operating Loans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall establish a plan, in coordination with activities under sections 2006a, 2006b, 2006c, and 2006d of this title, to encourage each borrower with an outstanding loan under this subchapter or with respect to whom there is an outstanding guarantee under this subchapter to graduate to private commercial or other sources of credit.
