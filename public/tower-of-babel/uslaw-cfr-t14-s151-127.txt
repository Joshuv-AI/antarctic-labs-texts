14 C.F.R. § 151.127 — Accounting and audit.
Citation: us:cfr:14:151.127
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-151.127
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 151 (Federal Aid To Airports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of § 151.55 relating to accounting and audit of project costs are also applicable to advance planning proposal costs. However, the requirement of segregating and grouping costs applies only to § 151.55(a) (5) and (7) classifications.
