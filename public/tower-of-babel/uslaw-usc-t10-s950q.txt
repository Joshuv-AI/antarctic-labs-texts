10 U.S.C. § 950q — Principals
Citation: us:usc:10:950q
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section950q
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 47A (Military Commissions); Subchapter VIII (Punitive Matters). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person punishable under this chapter who—
(1)
commits an offense punishable by this chapter, or aids, abets, counsels, commands, or procures its commission;
(2)
causes an act to be done which if directly performed by him would be punishable by this chapter; or
(3)
is a superior commander who, with regard to acts punishable by this chapter, knew, had reason to know, or should have known, that a subordinate was about to commit such acts or had done so and who failed to take the necessary and reasonable measures to prevent such acts or to punish the perpetrators thereof,
is a principal.
