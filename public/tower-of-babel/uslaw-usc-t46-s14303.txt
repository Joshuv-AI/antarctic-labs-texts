46 U.S.C. § 14303 — Tonnage Certificate
Citation: us:usc:46:14303
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section14303
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part J (Measurement Of Vessels); Chapter 143 (Convention Measurement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
After measuring a vessel under this chapter, the Secretary shall issue, on request of the owner, an International Tonnage Certificate (1969) and deliver it to the owner or master of the vessel. For a vessel to which the Convention does not apply, the Secretary shall prescribe a certificate to be issued as evidence of a vessel’s measurement under this chapter.
(b)
The certificate issued under this section shall be maintained as required by the Secretary.
