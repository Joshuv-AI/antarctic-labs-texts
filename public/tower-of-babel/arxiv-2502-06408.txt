Abstract
Let A A and G G be finite groups such that A A acts coprimely on G G by automorphisms. For any fixed prime divisor p p of | G | |G| , we provide a complete characterization of the structure of a group G G in which every maximal A A -invariant subgroup of order divisible by p p is nilpotent. Keywords: maximal invariant subgroup; Sylow subgroup; nilpotent group MSC(2020): 20D10; 20D15

We first prove that

By the hypothesis, every maximal

of is

For
