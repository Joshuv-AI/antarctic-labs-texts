Equitably Coloring Planar and Outerplanar Graphs
Daniel W. Cranston, Reem Mahmoud

Abstract
A proper s s -coloring of an n n -vertex graph is equitable if every color class has size ⌊ n / s ⌋ \lfloor n/s\rfloor or ⌈ n / s ⌉ \lceil n/s\rceil . A necessary condition to have an equitable s s -coloring is that every vertex v v appears in an independent set of size at least ⌊ n / s ⌋ \lfloor n/s\rfloor . That is min v ∈ V ⁡ ( G ) ⁡ α v ⩾ ⌊ n / s ⌋ \min_{v\in V(G)}\alpha_{v}\geqslant\lfloor n/s\rfloor . Various authors showed that when G G is a tree and s ⩾ 3 s\geqslant 3 this obvious necessary condition is also sufficient. Kierstead, Kostochka, and Xiang asked whether this result holds more generally for all outerplanar graphs. We show that the answer is No when s = 3 s=3 , but that the answer is Yes when s ⩾ 6 s\geqslant 6 . The case s ∈ { 4 , 5 } s\in\{4,5\} remains open. We also prove an analogous result for planar graphs, with a necessary and sufficient hypothesis. Fix s ⩾ 40 s\geqslant 40 . Let G G be a planar graph, and let w 0 , w 1 w_{0},w_{1} be its 2 2 vertices with largest degrees. If there exist disjoint independent sets I 0 , I 1 I_{0},I_{1} such that | I 0 | = ⌊ n / s ⌋ |I_{0}|=\lfloor n/s\rfloor and | I 1 | = ⌊ ( n + 1 ) / s ⌋ |I_{1}|=\lfloor(n+1)/s\rfloor and w 0 , w 1 ∈ I 0 ∪ I 1 w_{0},w_{1}\in I_{0}\cup I_{1} , then G G has an equitable s s -coloring.

3.1
Proof Overview

The proof of the Main Theorem is somewhat involved. So here we first present an overview.
We typically refer to an “equitable

A

for

of

We strengthen the above equipartitioning result to allow
