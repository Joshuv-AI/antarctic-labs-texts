38 C.F.R. § 3.1710 — Escheat (payment of burial benefits to an estate with no heirs).
Citation: us:cfr:38:3.1710
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-3.1710
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 3 (Adjudication). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

VA will not pay burial benefits if the payment would escheat (that is, would be turned over to the State because there are no heirs to the estate of the person to whom such benefits would be paid).
