40 C.F.R. § 405.90 — Applicability; description of the condensed milk subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:405.90
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-405.90
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 405 (Dairy Products Processing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the manufacture of condensed whole milk, condensed skim milk, sweetened condensed milk and condensed buttermilk.
