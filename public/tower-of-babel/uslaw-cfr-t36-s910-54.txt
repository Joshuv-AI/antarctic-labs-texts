36 C.F.R. § 910.54 — Build-to height.
Citation: us:cfr:36:910.54
Creator: Pennsylvania Avenue Development Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-910.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 910 (General Guidelines And Uniform Standards For Urban Planning And Design Of Development Within The Pennsylvania Avenue Development Area). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Build-to height means a specified minimum height of development to which the exterior wall of a building in a development must rise. Minor deviations from the build-to height for architectural embellishments and articulations of the cornice and roof level are permitted, unless otherwise prohibited by the applicable Square Guidelines or the District of Columbia's codes and regulations.
