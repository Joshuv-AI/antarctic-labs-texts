49 C.F.R. § 378.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:49:378.1

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-378.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 378 (Procedures Governing The Processing, Investigation, And Disposition Of Overcharge, Duplicate Payment, Or Overcollection Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations set forth in this part govern the processing of claims for overcharge, duplicate payment, or overcollection for the transportation of property in interstate or foreign commerce by motor carriers and household goods freight forwarders subject to 49 U.S.C. subtitle IV, part B.
