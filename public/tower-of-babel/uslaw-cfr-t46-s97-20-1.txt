46 C.F.R. § 97.20-1 — -1 Unnecessary whistling prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:46:97.20-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-97.20-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 97 (Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The unnecessary sounding of the vessel's whistle is prohibited within any harbor limits of the United States.
