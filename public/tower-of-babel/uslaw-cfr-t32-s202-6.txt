32 C.F.R. § 202.6 — Selecting co-chairs.
Citation: us:cfr:32:202.6
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-202.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 202 (Restoration Advisory Boards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) DoD installation co-chair. The DoD installation co-chair shall be selected by the Installation Commander or equivalent, or in accordance with Military Component-specific guidance.
(b) Community co-chair. The community co-chair shall be selected by the community RAB members.
