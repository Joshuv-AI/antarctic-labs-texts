32 C.F.R. § 147.22 — Transferability.
Citation: us:cfr:32:147.22
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-147.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 147 (Adjudicative Guidelines For Determining Eligibility For Access To Classified Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Investigations that satisfy the requirements of a given standard and are current meet the investigative requirements for all levels specified for the standard. They shall be mutually and reciprocally accepted by all agencies.
