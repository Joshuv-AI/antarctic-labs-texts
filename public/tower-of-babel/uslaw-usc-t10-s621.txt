10 U.S.C. § 621 — Competitive categories for promotion
Citation: us:usc:10:621
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section621
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 36 (Promotion, Separation, And Involuntary Retirement Of Officers On The Active-Duty List); Subchapter II (Promotions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary of Defense, the Secretary of each military department shall establish competitive categories for promotion. Each officer whose name appears on an active-duty list shall be carried in a competitive category of officers. Officers in the same competitive category shall compete among themselves for promotion.
