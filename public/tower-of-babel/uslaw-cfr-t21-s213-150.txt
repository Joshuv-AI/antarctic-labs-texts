21 C.F.R. § 213.150 — Warehousing and distribution procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:21:213.150
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-213.150
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 213 (Current Good Manufacturing Practice For Medical Gases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Written procedures shall be established, and followed, describing the distribution of medical gases and including a system by which the distribution of each lot can be readily determined to facilitate its recall if necessary.
(b) Written procedures shall be established, and followed, describing the warehousing of medical gases, including quarantine of such gases before release by the quality unit.
