25 C.F.R. § 20.312 — What amounts will the Bureau deduct from income or other resources?
Citation: us:cfr:25:20.312
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-20.312
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 20 (Financial Assistance And Social Services Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The social services worker will deduct the following amounts from income, or other resources:
(a) The first $2,000 of liquid resources annually available to the household;
(b) Any home produce from a garden, livestock, and poultry used by the applicant or recipient and his/her household for their consumption; and
(c) Resources specifically excluded by federal statute.
