46 C.F.R. § 128.230 — Penetrations of hulls and watertight bulkheads—materials and pressure design.
Citation: us:cfr:46:128.230
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-128.230
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 128 (Marine Engineering: Equipment And Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Each piping penetration, in each bulkhead required by this subchapter to be watertight, must meet the requirements for materials and pressure design of subchapter F of this chapter.
(b) Each overboard discharge and shell connection, up to and including required shut-off valves, must meet the requirements for materials and pressure design of subchapter F of this chapter.
