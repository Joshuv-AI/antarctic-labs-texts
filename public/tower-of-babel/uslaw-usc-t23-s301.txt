23 U.S.C. § 301 — Freedom from tolls
Citation: us:usc:23:301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title23-section301
Context: United States Code, Title 23 (Highways); Chapter 3 (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as provided in section 129 of this title with respect to certain toll bridges and toll tunnels, all highways constructed under the provisions of this title shall be free from tolls of all kinds.
