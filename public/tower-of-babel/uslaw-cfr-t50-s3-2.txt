50 C.F.R. § 3.2 — Federal financial assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:50:3.2
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-3.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 3 (Nondiscrimination—Contracts, Permits, And Use Of Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of 43 CFR part 17, which implements title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, are applicable to any program or activity for which Federal financial assistance is provided under any law administered by the Service, including the programs and activities that are federally assisted under the laws listed in Appendix A of 43 CFR part 17.
