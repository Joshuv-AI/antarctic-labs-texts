34 C.F.R. § 34.23 — Exclusions from garnishment.
Citation: us:cfr:34:34.23
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-34.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 34 (Administrative Wage Garnishment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) We do not garnish your wages if we have credible evidence that you—
(1) Were involuntarily separated from employment; and
(2) Have not yet been reemployed continuously for at least 12 months.
(b) You have the burden of informing us of the circumstances surrounding an involuntary separation from employment.
