30 C.F.R. § 762.13 — Land exempt from designation as unsuitable for surface coal mining operations.
Citation: us:cfr:30:762.13
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-762.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 762 (Criteria For Designating Areas As Unsuitable For Surface Coal Mining Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The requirements of this part do not apply to—
(a) Lands on which surface coal mining operations were being conducted on the date of enactment of the Act;
(b) Lands covered by a permit issued under the Act; or
(c) Lands where substantial legal and financial commitments in surface coal mining operations were in existence prior to January 4, 1977.
