25 C.F.R. § 124.1 — What is the purpose of this part?
Citation: us:cfr:25:124.1
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-124.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 124 (Deposits Of Proceeds From Lands Withdrawn For Native Selection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part provides contact information on depositing proceeds from contracts, leases, permits, rights-of-way, or easements pertaining to lands withdrawn for Native selection under the Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act. All Federal agencies and the State of Alaska must use this part when making deposits of this type.
