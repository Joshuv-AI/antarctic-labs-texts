Quantum correlation of neutral charmed mesons at BESIII
*

Abstract
BESIII has recently accumulated a large data sample near the ψ ⁡ ( 3770 ) \psi(3770) production threshold corresponding to an integrated luminosity of 20 fb − 1 \text{\,fb}^{-1} . Neutral D 0 D ¯ 0 {{D}^{0}}{{\kern 1.00006pt\overline{\kern-1.00006ptD}}{}^{0}} pairs produced at the ψ ⁡ ( 3770 ) \psi(3770) are in a C C -odd correlated state, providing a unique laboratory to measure the strong-phase differences between D 0 {D}^{0} and D ¯ 0 {\kern 1.00006pt\overline{\kern-1.00006ptD}}{}^{0} decays. These parameters are essential inputs to the study of CP violation in heavy-flavor physics, primarily in the determinations of the CKM angle gamma and charm-mixing parameters. These proceedings report new measurements of strong-phase differences in different neutral D D decays at BESIII, including new measurements of the strong phases in D → K + ​ K − ​ π + ​ π − {D}\to K^{+}K^{-}\pi^{+}\pi^{-} decays. Additionally reported is the first observation of correlated D ​ D DD pairs produced at e + ​ e − e^{+}e^{-} center-of-mass energies above the ψ ⁡ ( 3770 ) \psi(3770) threshold, where e + ​ e − → D ∗ ​ D ¯ e^{+}e^{-}\to{{D}^{*}}{\kern 1.00006pt\overline{\kern-1.00006pt{D}}} and e + e − → D ∗ D ¯ ∗ e^{+}e^{-}\to{{D}^{*}}{{\kern 1.00006pt\overline{\kern-1.00006ptD}}{}^{*}} processes also occur. These processes produce both C C -odd and C C -even correlated D ​ D ¯ {D}{\kern 1.00006pt\overline{\kern-1.00006pt{D}}} pairs, which allow for new measurement techniques to determine strong-phases from previously unused datasets.

At energies above the open-charm pair production threshold, the processes

-odd constrained
{D}{\kern 1.00006pt\overline{\kern-1.00006pt{D}}}
