42 U.S.C. § 2374 — Utilities transferable
Citation: us:usc:42:2374
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2374
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 24 (Disposal Of Atomic Energy Communities); Subchapter VI (Utilities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All utilities are authorized to be transferred under this subchapter, but shall not include property which the Commission determines to be needed for its own use.
