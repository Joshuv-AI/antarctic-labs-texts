17 C.F.R. § 37.406 — Trade reconstruction.
Citation: us:cfr:17:37.406
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-37.406
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 37 (Swap Execution Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The swap execution facility shall have the ability to comprehensively and accurately reconstruct all trading on its facility. All audit-trail data and reconstructions shall be made available to the Commission in a form, manner, and time that is acceptable to the Commission.
