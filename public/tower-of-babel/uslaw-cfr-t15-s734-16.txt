15 C.F.R. § 734.16 — Transfer (in-country).
Citation: us:cfr:15:734.16
Creator: Bureau Of Industry And Security, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-734.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 734 (Scope Of The Export Administration Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as set forth in § 734.18(a)(3), a Transfer (in-country) is a change in end use or end user of an item within the same foreign country. Transfer (in-country) is synonymous with In-country transfer.
