7 C.F.R. § 1789.163 — Waiver.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1789.163
Creator: Rural Utilities Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1789.163
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1789 (Use Of Consultants Funded By Borrowers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

RUS may waive any requirement or procedure of this subpart by determining that its application in a particular situation would not be in the government's interest, except that certain provision that the subject contracts are subject to the provisions of the FAR (48 CFR ch. 1) and AGAR (48 CFR ch. 4).
