46 U.S.C. § 9304 — Pilotage pools
Citation: us:usc:46:9304
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section9304
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Manning Of Vessels); Chapter 93 (Great Lakes Pilotage). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary may authorize the formation of a pool by a voluntary association of United States registered pilots to provide for efficient dispatching of vessels and rendering of pilotage services.
(b)
For pilotage pools, the Secretary may—
(1)
limit the number of the pools;
(2)
prescribe regulations for their operation and administration;
(3)
prescribe a uniform system of accounts;
(4)
perform audits and inspections; and
(5)
require coordination on a reciprocal basis with similar pool arrangements authorized by the appropriate agency of Canada.
