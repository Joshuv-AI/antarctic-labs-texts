20 C.F.R. § 802.304 — Purpose of oral argument.
Citation: us:cfr:20:802.304
Creator: Benefits Review Board, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-802.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 802 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Oral argument may be held by the Board in any case:
(a) When there is a novel issue not previously considered by the Board; or
(b) When in the interests of justice oral argument will serve to assist the Board in carrying out the intent of any of the Acts; or
(c) To resolve conflicting decisions by administrative law judges on a substantial question of law.
