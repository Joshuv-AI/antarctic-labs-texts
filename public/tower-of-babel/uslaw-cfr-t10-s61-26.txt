10 C.F.R. § 61.26 — Amendment of license.
Citation: us:cfr:10:61.26
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-61.26
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 61 (Licensing Requirements For Land Disposal Of Radioactive Waste). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An application for amendment of a license must be filed in accordance with § 61.20 and shall fully describe the changes desired.
(b) In determining whether an amendment to a license will be approved, the Commission will apply the criteria set forth in § 61.23.
