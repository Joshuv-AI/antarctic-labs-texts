46 C.F.R. § 42.11-20 — -20 Application for annual survey.
Citation: us:cfr:46:42.11-20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-42.11-20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 42 (Domestic And Foreign Voyages By Sea). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The owner, master, or agent of a vessel holding a load line certificate shall apply to the assigning and issuing authority who issued the certificate for the annual survey required by § 42.09-40 or the International Convention on Load Lines, 1966.
