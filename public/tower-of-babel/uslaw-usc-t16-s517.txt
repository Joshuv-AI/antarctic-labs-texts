16 U.S.C. § 517 — Title to lands to be acquired
Citation: us:usc:16:517
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section517
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 2 (National Forests); Subchapter I (Establishment And Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture may do all things necessary to secure the safe title in the United States to the lands to be acquired under this Act, but no payment shall be made for any such lands until the title shall be satisfactory to the Attorney General or his designee and shall be vested in the United States.
