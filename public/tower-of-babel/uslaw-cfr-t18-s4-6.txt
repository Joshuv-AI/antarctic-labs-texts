18 C.F.R. § 4.6 — Burden of proof.
Citation: us:cfr:18:4.6
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-4.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 4 (Licenses, Permits, Exemptions, And Determination Of Project Costs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The burden of proof to sustain each item of claimed cost shall be upon the licensee and only such items as are in the opinion of the Commission supported by satisfactory proof may be entered in the electric plant accounts of the licensee.
