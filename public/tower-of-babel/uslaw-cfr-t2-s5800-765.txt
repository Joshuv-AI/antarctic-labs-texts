2 C.F.R. § 5800.765 — May I ask the suspending official to reconsider a decision to suspend me?
Citation: us:cfr:2:5800.765
Creator: Election Assistance Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-5800.765
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 5800 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes. Within 30 days of receiving a final notice of suspension, you may make a written request for the suspending official to reconsider your suspension.
