Strengthening the Link Between Fullerenes and a Subset of Diffuse Interstellar Bands
Daniel Majaess, Tina A. Harriott, Halis Seuret, Cercis Morera-Boado, Lou Massa, Chérif F. Matta

Abstract
A debate persists regarding the correlation between the DIBs 9577 and 9632 Å, and whether they share a common molecular carrier (i.e., C + 60 {}_{60}^{+} ). A robust high correlation determination emerges after bridging the baseline across an order of magnitude ( ≃ 50 − 700 \simeq 50-700 mÅ, r = 0.93 ± 0.02 r=0.93\pm 0.02 ), and nearly doubling the important higher equivalent width domain by adding new Mg II-corrected sightlines. Moreover, additional evidence is presented of possible DIB linkages to fullerenes, whereby attention is drawn to DIBs at 7470.38, 7558.44, and 7581.47 Å, which match the Campbell experimental results for C + 70 {}_{70}^{+} within 1 Å, and the same is true of 6926.48 and 7030.26 Å for C 2 + 70 {}_{70}^{2+} . Yet their current correlation uncertainties are unsatisfactory and exacerbated by expectedly low EWs (e.g., E ​ W ¯ = 4 \overline{EW}=4 mÅ for 6926.48 Å), and thus further observations are required to assess whether they represent a bona fide connection or numerical coincidence.

III
Conclusions

DIBs linked to the buckminsterfullerene cation at 9577 and 9632 Å are highly correlated (Fig.
1
,
r=0.93\pm 0.02
). That assessment stemmed from an analysis spanning a lucrative EW baseline, and bridging low to high EWs provides a confident conclusion. The EWs were tied to existing
[
11
,
18
]
and newly corrected estimates
[
20
]
. Regarding the latter, 9632 Å datapoints linked to relatively cooler stars within
Ramírez-Tannus et al. [20]
were corrected here for Mg II contamination (top right panel of Fig.
1
). Their addition reaffirms the overarching conclusion of a high Pearson correlation (Table
1
).

The analysis was expanded to identify DIBs associated with fullerenes beyond C
{}_{60}^{+}
(i.e., C
{}_{70}^{+}
and C
{}_{70}^{2+}
). Several DIBs overlap with the
Campbell et al. [6]
,
Campbell et al. [5]
laboratory wavelengths to within an Angstrom (Table
3
, e.g., DIB 7558.44 Å and C
{}_{70}^{+}

\lambda_{e}=7558.4
Å). A complimentary correlation analysis was limited by low EWs and statistics (Fig.
2
, Table
4
, e.g.,
\overline{EW}=4
mÅ for 6926.48 Å). Additional observations over an extensive EW baseline are required to assess whether those DIBs are linked to fullerene cations. The current evidence, though inconclusive, provides sufficient impetus to pursue such observations. Future work may include relinquishing a strict
<1
Å criterion, and exploring matches relative to fractionary offsets within a FWHM (e.g.,
|{\rm DIB}-\lambda_{e}|<\frac{1}{x}{\rm FWHM}
