28 U.S.C. § 2677 — Compromise
Citation: us:usc:28:2677
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section2677
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 171 (Tort Claims Procedure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General or his designee may arbitrate, compromise, or settle any claim cognizable under section 1346(b) of this title, after the commencement of an action thereon.
