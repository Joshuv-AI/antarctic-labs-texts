7 C.F.R. § 782.16 — Designating end use on form FSA-751.
Citation: us:cfr:7:782.16
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-782.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 782 (End-Use Certificate Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) If the end use specified on the applicable form FSA-751, Wheat Consumption and Resale Report, is “export,” the exporter must specify the final destination, by country, on form FSA-751.
(b) If the end user utilizes the wheat for purposes other than milling, brewing, malting, distilling, export, or manufacturing, such use must be specifically designated on form FSA-751.
