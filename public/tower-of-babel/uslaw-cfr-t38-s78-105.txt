38 C.F.R. § 78.105 — Ineligible activities.
Citation: us:cfr:38:78.105
Creator: Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-78.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 38 (Pensions, Bonuses, and Veterans' Relief), Part 78 (Staff Sergeant Parker Gordon Fox Suicide Prevention Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any other section in this part, grantees are not authorized to use suicide prevention services grant funds to pay for the following:
(a) Direct cash assistance to participants.
(b) Those legal services prohibited pursuant to § 78.80(g).
(c) Medical or dental care and medicines except for clinical services authorized pursuant to § 78.60.
(d) Any activities considered illegal under Federal law.
