41 C.F.R. § 303-70.303 — -70.303 Mortuary services and transportation for an immediate family member who dies in transit.
Citation: us:cfr:41:303-70.303
Creator: Chapter 303—Payment Of Expenses Connected With The Death Of Certain Employees
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-303-70.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 303-70 (70—Agency Requirements For Payment Of Expenses Connected With The Death Of Certain Employees And Immediate Family Members). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies must furnish transportation, if requested by the employee, when an immediate family member, residing with the employee, dies while in transit to or from the employee's duty station OCONUS. Agencies must follow § 303-70.301 for transportation expenses and furnish mortuary services only if the conditions in § 303-70.300 are met.
