17 C.F.R. § 240.15b1-6 — b1-6 Notice to brokers and dealers of requirements regarding lost securityholders and unresponsive payees.
Citation: us:cfr:17:240.15b1-6
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-240.15b1-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 240 (General Rules And Regulations, Securities Exchange Act Of 1934). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Brokers and dealers are hereby notified of Rule 17Ad-17 (§ 240.17Ad-17), which addresses certain requirements with respect to lost securityholders and unresponsive payees that may be applicable to them.
