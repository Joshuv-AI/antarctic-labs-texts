20 U.S.C. § 1011e — Financial responsibility of foreign students
Citation: us:usc:20:1011e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1011e
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter I (General Provisions); Part B (Additional General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter or any other Federal law shall be construed to prohibit any institution of higher education from requiring a student who is a foreign national (and not admitted to permanent residence in the United States) to guarantee the future payment of tuition and fees to such institution by—
(1)
making advance payment of such tuition and fees;
(2)
making deposits in an escrow account administered by such institution for such payments; or
(3)
obtaining a bond or other insurance that such payments will be made.
