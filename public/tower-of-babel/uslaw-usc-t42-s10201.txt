42 U.S.C. § 10201 — Judicial review
Citation: us:usc:42:10201
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10201
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 108 (Nuclear Waste Policy); Subchapter II (Research, Development, And Demonstration Regarding Disposal Of High-Level Radioactive Waste And Spent Nuclear Fuel). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Judicial review of research and development activities under this subchapter shall be in accordance with the provisions of section 10139 of this title.
