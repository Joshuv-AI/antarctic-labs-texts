DEPARTMENT OF VETERANS AFFAIRS


Geriatric and Gerontology Advisory Committee, Notice of Meeting 
Cancellation

    The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) gives notice under the 
Federal Advisory Committee Act, 5 U.S.C. Ch. 10., that the Geriatric 
and Gerontology Advisory Committee, previously scheduled to be held on 
June 3, 2026, has been cancelled. The Committee will reschedule the 
meeting and announce it later in the Federal Register.
    For more information, please contact Mr. Michael Stevens, 
Designated Federal Officer, at (989) 930-9945 or via email at 
[email protected].

     Dated: May 27, 2026.
LaTonya L. Small,
Federal Advisory Committee Management Officer.
