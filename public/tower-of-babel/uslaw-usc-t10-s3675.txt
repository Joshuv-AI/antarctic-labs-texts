10 U.S.C. § 3675 — Capital lease or lease-purchase treated as an acquisition
Citation: us:usc:10:3675
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section3675
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart C (Contracting Methods And Contract Types); Chapter 257 (Contracts For Long-Term Lease Or Charter Of Vessels, Aircraft, And Combat Vehicles). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
If a lease or charter covered by this chapter is a capital lease or a lease-purchase—
(1)
the lease or charter shall be treated as an acquisition and shall be subject to all applicable statutory and regulatory requirements for the acquisition of aircraft, naval vessels, or combat vehicles; and
(2)
funds appropriated to the Department of Defense for operation and maintenance may not be obligated or expended for the lease or charter.
(b) Definitions.—
In this section, the terms “capital lease” and “lease-purchase” have the meanings given those terms in Appendix B to Office of Management and Budget Circular A–11, as in effect on January 6, 2006.
