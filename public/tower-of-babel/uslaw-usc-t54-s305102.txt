54 U.S.C. § 305102 — Duties of Secretary in providing a national historic light station program
Citation: us:usc:54:305102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section305102
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3051 (Historic Light Station Preservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To provide a national historic light station program, the Secretary shall—
(1)
collect and disseminate information concerning historic light stations;
(2)
foster educational programs relating to the history, practice, and contribution to society of historic light stations;
(3)
sponsor or conduct research and study into the history of light stations;
(4)
maintain a listing of historic light stations; and
(5)
assess the effectiveness of the program established by this chapter regarding the conveyance of historic light stations.
