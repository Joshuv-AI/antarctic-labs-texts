46 C.F.R. § 402.100 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:46:402.100
Creator: Coast Guard (Great Lakes Pilotage), Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-402.100
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 402 (Great Lakes Pilotage Rules And Orders). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to implement those provisions of the Great Lakes Pilotage Regulations (part 401 of this chapter) which authorize or require the Commandant to issue supplementary rules and orders.
