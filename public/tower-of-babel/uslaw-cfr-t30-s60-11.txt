30 C.F.R. § 60.11 — Methods of compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:30:60.11
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-60.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 60 (Respirable Crystalline Silica). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The mine operator shall install, use, and maintain feasible engineering controls, supplemented by administrative controls when necessary, to keep each miner's exposure at or below the PEL, except as specified in § 60.14.
(b) Rotation of miners shall not be considered an acceptable administrative control used for compliance with this part.
