54 U.S.C. § 302108 — Review of threats to historic property
Citation: us:usc:54:302108
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section302108
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3021 (National Register Of Historic Places). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

At least once every 4 years, the Secretary, in consultation with the Council and with State Historic Preservation Officers, shall review significant threats to historic property to—
(1)
determine the kinds of historic property that may be threatened;
(2)
ascertain the causes of the threats; and
(3)
develop and submit to the President and Congress recommendations for appropriate action.
