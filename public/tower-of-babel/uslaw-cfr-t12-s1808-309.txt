12 C.F.R. § 1808.309 — Restrictions on uses of Bond Proceeds and Bond Loan proceeds.
Citation: us:cfr:12:1808.309
Creator: Community Development Financial Institutions Fund, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-1808.309
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 1808 (Community Development Financial Institutions Bond Guarantee Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Pursuant to 12 U.S.C. 47123a(c)(5), Bond Loan proceeds shall not be used for:
(a) Political activities;
(b) Lobbying, whether directly or through other parties;
(c) Outreach;
(d) Counseling services;
(e) Travel expenses;
(f) For the salaries or administrative costs of the Qualified Issuer or any recipients of Bond Proceeds, other than those costs covered by Bond Issuance Fees;
(g) To fund the Risk-Share Pool;
(h) To pay fees other than Bond Issuance Fees; or
(i) Any other use as may be specified in the applicable Notice of Guarantee Availability.
