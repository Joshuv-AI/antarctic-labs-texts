10 C.F.R. § 830.6 — Recordkeeping.
Citation: us:cfr:10:830.6
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-830.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 830 (Nuclear Safety Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A contractor must maintain complete and accurate records as necessary to substantiate compliance with the requirements of this part.
