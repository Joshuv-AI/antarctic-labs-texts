41 C.F.R. § 302-12.4 — -12.4 Expenses paid if using an RSC to ship household goods in excess of the maximum weight allowance.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-12.4
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-12.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-12 (12—Use Of A Relocation Services Company). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an employee uses a relocation services company to ship HHG in excess of the maximum weight allowance, the agency will pay the portion of the fee attributable to 18,000 pounds net weight. Employees must pay the rest.
