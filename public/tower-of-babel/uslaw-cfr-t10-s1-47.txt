10 C.F.R. § 1.47 — NRC Regional Offices.
Citation: us:cfr:10:1.47
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-1.47
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 1 (Statement Of Organization And General Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each Regional Administrator executes established NRC policies and assigned programs relating to inspection, enforcement, licensing, State agreements, State liaison, and emergency response within Regional boundaries set out in § 1.5(b) of this part.
