25 C.F.R. § 214.15 — Homesteads.
Citation: us:cfr:25:214.15
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-214.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 214 (Leasing Of Osage Reservation Lands, Oklahoma, For Mining, Except Oil And Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Lessees and those acting under them shall not conduct prospecting or mining operations within or upon any homestead selection without written consent of the Secretary of the Interior.
