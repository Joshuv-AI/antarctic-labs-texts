31 C.F.R. § 560.523 — Exportation of equipment and services relating to information and informational materials.
Citation: us:cfr:31:560.523
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-560.523
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 560 (Iranian Transactions And Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Specific licenses may be issued on a case-by-case basis for the exportation of equipment and services necessary for the establishment of news wire feeds or other transmissions of information and informational materials.
