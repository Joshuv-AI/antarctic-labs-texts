15 C.F.R. § 904.214 — Stipulations.
Citation: us:cfr:15:904.214
Creator: National Oceanic And Atmospheric Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-904.214
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 904 (Civil Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The parties may, by stipulation, agree upon any matters involved in the administrative proceeding and include such stipulations in the record with the consent of the Judge. Written stipulations must be signed and served on all parties.
