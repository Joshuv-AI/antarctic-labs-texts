13 C.F.R. § 126.102 — What is the effect of the HUBZone program on the section 8(d) subcontracting program?
Citation: us:cfr:13:126.102
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-126.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 126 (Hubzone Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The HUBZone Act of 1997 amended the section 8(d) subcontracting program to include certified HUBZone small business concerns in the formal subcontracting plans described in § 125.3 of this title.
