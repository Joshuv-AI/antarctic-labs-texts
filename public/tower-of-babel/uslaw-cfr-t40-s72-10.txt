40 C.F.R. § 72.10 — Availability of information.
Citation: us:cfr:40:72.10
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-72.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 72 (Permits Regulation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The availability to the public of information provided to, or otherwise obtained by, the Administrator under the Acid Rain Program shall be governed by part 2 of this chapter.
