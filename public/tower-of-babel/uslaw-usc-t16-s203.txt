16 U.S.C. § 203 — Sale and removal of timber; charges for leases and privileges
Citation: us:usc:16:203
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section203
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXII (Lassen Volcanic National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior may sell and permit the removal of such matured or dead or down timber as he may deem necessary or advisable for the protection or improvement of the park, and may exact such charges as he deems proper for leases and all other privileges granted under this section and sections 201 and 202 of this title.
