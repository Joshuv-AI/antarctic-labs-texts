42 C.F.R. § 51c.502 — c.502 Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:51c.502
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-51c.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 51c (PART 51c—GRANTS FOR COMMUNITY HEALTH SERVICES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Equipment means nonexpendable personal property as defined in 2 CFR 200.1.
(b) Existing building means a completed or substantially completed structure, and may include the realty on which it is or is to be located.
(c) Modernization means the alteration, repair, remodeling and/or renovation of a building (including the initial equipment thereof and improvements to the building's site) which, when completed, will render the building suitable for use by the project for which the grant is made.
