43 C.F.R. § 3910.21 — Lands subject to exploration.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3910.21
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3910.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3910 (Oil Shale Exploration Licenses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The BLM may issue oil shale exploration licenses for all Federal lands subject to leasing under § 3900.10 of this chapter, except lands that are in an existing oil shale lease or in preference right leasing areas under the R, D and D program. The BLM may issue exploration licenses for lands in preference right lease areas only to the R, D and D lessee.
