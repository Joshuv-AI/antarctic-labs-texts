33 C.F.R. § 127.601 — Fire equipment: General.
Citation: us:cfr:33:127.601
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-127.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 127 (Waterfront Facilities Handling Liquefied Natural Gas And Liquefied Hazardous Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Fire equipment and systems provided in addition to the requirements in this subpart must meet the requirements of this subpart.
(b) The following must be red or some other conspicuous color and be in locations that are readily accessible:
(1) Hydrants and standpipes.
(2) Hose stations.
(3) Portable fire extinguishers.
(4) Fire monitors.
(c) Fire equipment, if applicable, must bear the approval of Underwriters Laboratories, Inc., the Factory Mutual Research Corp., or the Coast Guard.
