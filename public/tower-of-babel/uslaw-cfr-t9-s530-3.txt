9 C.F.R. § 530.3 — Access to establishments.
Citation: us:cfr:9:530.3
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-530.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 530 (General Requirements; Definitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of 9 CFR 300.6 apply to fish processing establishments and related industries as they do to other establishments subject to the FMIA.
