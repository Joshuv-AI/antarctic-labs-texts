17 U.S.C. § 706 — Copies of Copyright Office records
Citation: us:usc:17:706
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title17-section706
Context: United States Code, Title 17 (Copyrights); Chapter 7 (Copyright Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Copies may be made of any public records or indexes of the Copyright Office; additional certificates of copyright registration and copies of any public records or indexes may be furnished upon request and payment of the fees specified by section 708.
(b)
Copies or reproductions of deposited articles retained under the control of the Copyright Office shall be authorized or furnished only under the conditions specified by the Copyright Office regulations.
