22 U.S.C. § 2516 — Use of foreign currencies
Citation: us:usc:22:2516
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2516
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 34 (The Peace Corps). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever possible, expenditures incurred in carrying out functions under this chapter shall be paid for in such currency of the country or area where the expense is incurred as may be available to the United States.
