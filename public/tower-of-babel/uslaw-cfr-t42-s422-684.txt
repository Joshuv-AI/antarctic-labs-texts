42 C.F.R. § 422.684 — Prehearing and summary judgment.
Citation: us:cfr:42:422.684
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-422.684
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 422 (Medicare Advantage Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Prehearing. The hearing officer may schedule a prehearing conference if he or she believes that a conference would more clearly define the issues.
(b) Summary judgment. Either party to the hearing may ask the hearing officer to rule on a motion for summary judgment.
