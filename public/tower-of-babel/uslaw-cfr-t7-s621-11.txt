7 C.F.R. § 621.11 — Who may obtain assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:7:621.11
Creator: Natural Resources Conservation Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-621.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 621 (River Basin Investigations And Surveys). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Assistance is available to conservation districts, communities. county governments, regional planning boards, other planning groups, and State and Federal agencies. Local groups express their desires for a cooperative study to the governor or appropriate State agency.
