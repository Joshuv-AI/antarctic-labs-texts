26 U.S.C. § 2524 — Extent of deductions
Citation: us:usc:26:2524
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section2524
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 12 (Gift Tax); Subchapter C (Deductions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The deductions provided in sections 2522 and 2523 shall be allowed only to the extent that the gifts therein specified are included in the amount of gifts against which such deductions are applied.
