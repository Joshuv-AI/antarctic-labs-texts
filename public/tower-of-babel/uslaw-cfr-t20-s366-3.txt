20 C.F.R. § 366.3 — Reasonable attempt to notify.
Citation: us:cfr:20:366.3
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-366.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 366 (Collection Of Debts By Federal Tax Refund Offset). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order to constitute a reasonable attempt to notify the debtor the Board must have used a mailing address for the debtor obtained from the Internal Revenue Service pursuant to section 6103 (m)(2) or (m)(4) of the Internal Revenue Code within a period of one year preceding the attempt to notify the debtor, whether or not the Board has used any other address maintained by the Board for the debtor.
