16 C.F.R. § 1306.2 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1306.2
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1306.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1306 (Ban Of Hazardous Lawn Darts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this rule is to prohibit the sale of lawn darts, which have been found to present an unreasonable risk of skull puncture injuries to children.
