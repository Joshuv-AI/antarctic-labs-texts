42 C.F.R. § 455.512 — Medicaid RAC provider appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:42:455.512
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-455.512
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 455 (Program Integrity: Medicaid). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

States must provide appeal rights under State law or administrative procedures to Medicaid providers that seek review of an adverse Medicaid RAC determination.
