27 U.S.C. § 218 — Civil penalties
Citation: us:usc:27:218
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title27-section218
Context: United States Code, Title 27 (Intoxicating Liquors); Chapter 8 (Federal Alcohol Administration Act); Subchapter II (Alcoholic Beverage Labeling). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person who violates the provisions of this subchapter shall be subject to a civil penalty of not more than $10,000, and each day shall constitute a separate offense.
