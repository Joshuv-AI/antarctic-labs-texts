15 U.S.C. § 1849 — Federal Reserve banks as fiscal agents
Citation: us:usc:15:1849
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1849
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 45 (Emergency Loan Guarantees To Business Enterprises). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any Federal Reserve bank which is requested to do so shall act as fiscal agent for the Board. Each such fiscal agent shall be reimbursed by the Board for all expenses and losses incurred by it in acting as agent on behalf of the Board.
