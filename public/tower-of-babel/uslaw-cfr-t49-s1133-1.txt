49 C.F.R. § 1133.1 — Freight bill filing requirement under modified procedure.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1133.1
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1133.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1133 (Recovery Of Damages). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If, under modified procedure (for general rules governing modified procedure, see part 1112), an award of damages is sought, complainant should submit the paid freight bills or properly certified copies with its statement when there are not more than 10 shipments; if more than 10 shipments are involved, complainant should retain the documents.
