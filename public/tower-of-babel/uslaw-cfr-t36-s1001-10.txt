36 C.F.R. § 1001.10 — Symbolic signs.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1001.10
Creator: Presidio Trust
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1001.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1001 (General Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The signs pictured in 36 CFR 1.10 provide general information and regulatory guidance in the area administered by the Presidio Trust. Certain of the signs designate activities that are either allowed or prohibited. Activities symbolized by a sign bearing a slash mark are prohibited.
(b) The use of other types of signs not herein depicted is not precluded.
