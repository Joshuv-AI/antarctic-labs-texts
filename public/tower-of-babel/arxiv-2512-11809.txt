Strong completeness of L p L_{p} -type vector lattices
Youssef Azouzi

Abstract
Let E E be a Dedekind complete Riesz space with weak unit e e , equipped with a conditional expectation operator T T . We prove that the spaces L p ​ ( T ) , L^{p}\left(T\right), p ∈ [ 1 , ∞ ] p\in\left[1,\infty\right] , with their natural vector-valued norms, are strongly complete, extending the p = 2 p=2 case of Kuo, Kalauch, and Watson. This resolves a question that has remained open for several years. We begin by studying a general type of convergence and its unbounded modification, unifying and generalizing order, norm, and absolute weak convergence while providing simpler proofs. As an application, we consider vector-valued norms and their unbounded variants, generalizing strong convergence in L p L^{p} -spaces and convergence in probability. This framework establishes the completeness of L p ​ ( T ) L^{p}\left(T\right) and of the universal completion E u E^{u} , reinforcing the uo-completeness of universally complete vector lattices. Finally we apply our main theorem to obtain a new result in ergodicity for conditional preserving systems.

4
Strong completeness

We fix now a conditional Riesz triple
\left(E,e,T\right)
as it is
defined above. The main objective in this section is to show that the space

L^{p}\left(T\right)
is strongly complete for all
p\in\left[1,\infty\right].
To the best of our knowledge, such completeness has only
been established for tow specific values of
p.
The case
p=\infty
is no so
hard and was treated in
[
25
]
, whiereas the case
p=2
is significantly
more complex and was recently proved in
[
18
]
. It is noteworthy that
the issue of strong sequential completeness has already been adressed. The
initial result, presented in
[
25
]
, establishes that the space

L^{1}\left(T\right)
is strongly sequentially complete. The author, in

[
4
]
, subsequently resolved the problem for the remaining values of

p.
In this paper we presente a comprehensive solution of the problem of
strong completeness. Indeed our main result states that proving strong
completeness for a single value of
p\in[1,\infty)
is sufficent to
extend the result for the other values. It should be noted that for the

T
-strong convergence in
L^{1}\left(T\right)
the corresponding
unbounded convergence is the convergence in
T
-conditional probability. This
extension mirrors the behavior observed in classical
L^{1}\left(\mathbb{P}\right)
spaces.

The main result of this section is the following.

Theorem 19

Let
\left(E,e,T\right)
be a conditional Riesz triple and

p\in\left(1,\infty\right).
Then the following statements are equivalent.

(i)

L^{1}\left(T\right)

(ii)

Proof.
(i)
\Longrightarrow
(ii) Assume that

\left(\left\|x_{\alpha}-x_{\beta}\right\|_{T,p}\right)_{\left(\alpha,\beta\right)\in A\times A}

By considering a tail of the net

(x_{\alpha}^{-})_{\alpha\in A}

and that, for all

, we have

(x_{\alpha}\wedge L)\wedge y=(x_{\alpha}\wedge y)\wedge L\overset{\rho}{\longrightarrow}(\ell\wedge y)\wedge L=y\wedge L.

Hence, by considering the net
(x_{\alpha}\wedge L)
instead of
(x_{\alpha})

and
L
instead of
\ell
, we may assume that
x_{\alpha}\leq\ell=\sup nu
. In
particular, for every
\alpha\in A
and
n\in\mathbb{N}
, we have

\left(x_{\alpha}\wedge nu\right)_{n}\overset{o}{\longrightarrow}x_{\alpha}\text{ and }\left(x_{\alpha}\wedge nu\right)_{\alpha}\overset{o}{\longrightarrow}nu.

(
\left(\ast\right)
)

For fixed
\alpha\in A
, we can find a net
(u_{\gamma})_{\gamma\in\Gamma}

with
u_{\gamma}\downarrow 0
and such that for every
\gamma\in\Gamma
there
is
n_{\gamma}
satisfying:

|x_{\alpha}\wedge nu-x_{\alpha}|\leq u_{\gamma}\qquad n\geq n_{\gamma}.

Pick
\gamma
in
\Gamma
and choose
n_{\gamma}
as above. Then we have for
all
n\geq n_{\gamma}
,

\displaystyle x_{\beta}-x_{\alpha}

\displaystyle=x_{\beta}-x_{\beta}\wedge(n+1)u

\displaystyle+x_{\beta}\wedge(n+1)u-x_{\alpha}\wedge nu+x_{\alpha}\wedge nu-x_{\alpha}

\displaystyle\geq x_{\beta}\wedge(n+1)u-x_{\alpha}\wedge nu-|x_{\alpha}\wedge nu-x_{\alpha}|

\displaystyle\geq x_{\beta}\wedge(n+1)u-nu-u_{\gamma}.

It follows that

\displaystyle T|x_{\beta}-x_{\beta^{\prime}}|\wedge u

\displaystyle\geq\lim_{\beta}T\left((x_{\beta}\wedge(n+1)u-nu-u_{\gamma})\wedge u\right)

\displaystyle=T\left((u-u_{\gamma})\wedge u\right),

where the last equality follows from
(\ast)
. Since this holds for every

\gamma
in
\Gamma
we conclude that

\sup\limits_{\beta,\beta\geq\alpha}T|x_{\beta}-x_{\beta^{\prime}}|\wedge u\geq Tu.

But this occurs for every
\alpha\in A
, which contradicts the fact
that the net
(x_{\alpha})
is Cauchy with respect with
T
-conditional
probability and completes the proof.

Lemma 25

Let

\left|x\wedge ne-x\right|^{p}\overset{o}{\longrightarrow}0.
Hence

\left\|x\wedge ne-x\right\|_{T,p}\longrightarrow 0
, which proves the lemma.

Theorem 26

Let
\left(E,e,T,S\right)
