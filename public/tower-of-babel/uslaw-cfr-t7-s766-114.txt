7 C.F.R. § 766.114 — State-certified mediation or voluntary meeting of creditors.
Citation: us:cfr:7:766.114
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-766.114
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 766 (Direct Loan Servicing—Special). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A borrower who is unable to develop a feasible plan but is otherwise eligible for primary loan servicing may request:
(1) State-certified mediation; or
(2) Voluntary meeting of creditors when a State does not have a certified mediation program.
(b) Any negotiation of the Agency's appraisal must be completed before State-certified mediation or voluntary meeting of creditors.
