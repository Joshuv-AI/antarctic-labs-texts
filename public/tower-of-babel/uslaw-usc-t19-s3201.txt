19 U.S.C. § 3201 — Authority to grant duty-free treatment
Citation: us:usc:19:3201
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section3201
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 20 (Andean Trade Preference). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may proclaim duty-free treatment (or other preferential treatment) for all eligible articles from any beneficiary country in accordance with the provisions of this chapter.
