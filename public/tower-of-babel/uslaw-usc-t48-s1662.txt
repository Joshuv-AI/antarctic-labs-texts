48 U.S.C. § 1662 — Sovereignty of United States extended over Swains Island
Citation: us:usc:48:1662
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1662
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 13 (Eastern Samoa). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The sovereignty of the United States over American Samoa is extended over Swains Island, which is made a part of American Samoa and placed under the jurisdiction of the administrative and judicial authorities of the government established therein by the United States.
