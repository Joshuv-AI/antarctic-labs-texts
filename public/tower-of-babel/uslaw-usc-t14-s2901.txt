14 U.S.C. § 2901 — Work-life policies and programs
Citation: us:usc:14:2901
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2901
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 29 (Coast Guard Family Support, Child Care, And Housing); Subchapter I (Coast Guard Families). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commandant is authorized—
(1)
to establish an office for the purpose of developing, promulgating, and coordinating policies, programs, and activities related to the families of Coast Guard members;
(2)
to implement and oversee policies, programs, and activities described in paragraph (1) as the Commandant considers necessary; and
(3)
to perform such other duties as the Commandant considers necessary.
