49 C.F.R. § 262.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:262.1
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-262.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 262 (Implementation Of Program For Capital Grants For Rail Line Relocation And Improvement Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of this part is to carry out the statutory mandate set forth in 49 U.S.C. 20154 requiring the Secretary of Transportation to promulgate regulations implementing a capital grants program to provide financial assistance for local rail line relocation and improvement projects.
