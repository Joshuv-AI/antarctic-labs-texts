4 U.S.C. § 71 — Permanent seat of Government
Citation: us:usc:4:71
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title4-section71
Context: United States Code, Title 4 (Flag And Seal, Seat Of Government, And The States); Chapter 3 (Seat Of The Government). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All that part of the territory of the United States included within the present limits of the District of Columbia shall be the permanent seat of government of the United States.
