40 U.S.C. § 3708 — Criminal penalties
Citation: us:usc:40:3708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section3708
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part A (General); Chapter 37 (Contract Work Hours And Safety Standards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A contractor or subcontractor having a duty to employ, direct, or control a laborer or mechanic employed in the performance of work contemplated by a contract to which this chapter applies that intentionally violates this chapter shall be fined under title 18, imprisoned for not more than six months, or both.
