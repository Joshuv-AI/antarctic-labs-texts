49 U.S.C. § 32708 — Confidentiality of information
Citation: us:usc:49:32708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section32708
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Information, Standards, And Requirements); Chapter 327 (Odometers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) General.—
Information obtained by the Secretary of Transportation under this chapter related to a confidential matter referred to in section 1905 of title 18 may be disclosed only—
(1)
to another officer or employee of the United States Government for use in carrying out this chapter; or
(2)
in a proceeding under this chapter.
(b) Withholding Information From Congress.—
This section does not authorize information to be withheld from a committee of Congress authorized to have the information.
