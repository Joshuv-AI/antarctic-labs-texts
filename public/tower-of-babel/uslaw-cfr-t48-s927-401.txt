48 C.F.R. § 927.401 — 927.401 Definitions.
Citation: us:cfr:48:927.401
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-927.401
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 927 (Patents, Data, And Copyrights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Technical data means data (other than computer software) of a scientific or technical nature. Technical data does not include computer software, but does include manuals and instructional materials and technical data formatted as a computer database (see appendix A to subpart D of 2 CFR part 910).
