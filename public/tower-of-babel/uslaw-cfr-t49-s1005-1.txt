49 C.F.R. § 1005.1 — Applicability of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1005.1
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1005.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1005 (Principles And Practices For The Investigation And Voluntary Disposition Of Loss And Damage Claims And Processing Salvage). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations set forth in this part shall govern the processing of claims for loss, damage, injury, or delay to property transported or accepted for transportation, in interstate or foreign commerce, by each railroad, express company, motor carrier, water carrier, and freight forwarder (hereinafter called carrier), subject to the Interstate Commerce Act.
