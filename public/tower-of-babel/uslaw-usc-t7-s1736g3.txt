7 U.S.C. § 1736g–3 — Use of certain local currency
Citation: us:usc:7:1736g–3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1736g–3
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 41 (Food For Peace); Subchapter IV (General Authorities And Requirements). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Local currency payments received by the United States pursuant to agreements entered into under subchapter II (as in effect on November 27, 1990) may be utilized by the Secretary in accordance with section 1708 of this title (as in effect on November 27, 1990).
