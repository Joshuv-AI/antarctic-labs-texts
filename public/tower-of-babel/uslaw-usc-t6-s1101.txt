6 U.S.C. § 1101 — Definitions

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 4 (Transportation Security); Subchapter I (Transportation Security Planning And Information Sharing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this subchapter, the following terms apply:
(1) Department
The term “Department” means the Department of Homeland Security.
(2) Secretary
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Homeland Security.
