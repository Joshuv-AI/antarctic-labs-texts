15 C.F.R. § 90.5 — Who may file a request for a challenge.
Citation: us:cfr:15:90.5
Creator: Bureau Of The Census, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-90.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 90 (Procedure For Challenging Population Estimates). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A request for a challenge of a population estimate generated by the Census Bureau may be filed only by the chief executive officer or highest elected official of a governmental unit. In those instances where the FSCPE member agency represents a non-functioning county or statistical equivalent, the governor will serve as the chief executive officer or highest elected official.
