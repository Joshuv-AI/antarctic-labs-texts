28 C.F.R. § 552.30 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:28:552.30
Creator: Bureau Of Prisons, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-552.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 552 (Custody). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Bureau of Prisons primary objectives in all hostage situations are to safely free the hostage(s) and to regain control of the institution.
