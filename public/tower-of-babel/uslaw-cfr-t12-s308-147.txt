12 C.F.R. § 308.147 — Investigations confidential.
Citation: us:cfr:12:308.147
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-308.147
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 308 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Investigations shall be confidential. Information and documents obtained by the FDIC in the course of such investigations shall not be disclosed, except as provided in part 309 of this chapter and as otherwise required by law.
