33 C.F.R. § 150.810 — Reporting a problem with an aid to navigation.
Citation: us:cfr:33:150.810
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-150.810
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 150 (Deepwater Ports: Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Any problem affecting the operation or characteristics of a navigation aid at the deepwater port must be reported to the District Commander by the fastest means available. The report must identify:
(1) The navigation aid affected;
(2) The aid's location;
(3) The nature of the problem; and
(4) The estimated repair time.
(b) When the problem is corrected, the District Commander must be notified.
