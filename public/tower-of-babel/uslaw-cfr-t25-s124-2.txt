25 C.F.R. § 124.2 — Who should an agency or the State of Alaska contact for information?
Citation: us:cfr:25:124.2
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-124.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 124 (Deposits Of Proceeds From Lands Withdrawn For Native Selection). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a Federal agency or the State of Alaska receives proceeds covered by this part, it must deposit the proceeds to the credit of the United States Department of the Interior, Office of the Special Trustee for American Indians. For further information including depositing instructions, contact: Office of the Special Trustee for American Indians, Attention: Division of Trust Funds Accounting, 4400 Masthead Street NE., Albuquerque, New Mexico 87109.
