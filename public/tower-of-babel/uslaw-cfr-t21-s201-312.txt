21 C.F.R. § 201.312 — Magnesium sulfate heptahydrate; label declaration on drug products.
Citation: us:cfr:21:201.312
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-201.312
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 201 (Labeling). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Magnesium sulfate heptahydrate should be listed on the label of a drug product as epsom salt, which is its common or usual name.
