32 C.F.R. § 1630.43 — Class 4-D: Minister of religion.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1630.43
Creator: Selective Service System
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1630.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1630 (Classification Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accord with part 1645 of this chapter any registrant shall be placed in Class 4-D who is a:
(a) Duly ordained minister of religion; or
(b) Regular minister of religion.
