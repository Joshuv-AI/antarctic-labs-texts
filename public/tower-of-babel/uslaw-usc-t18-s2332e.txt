18 U.S.C. § 2332e — Requests for military assistance to enforce prohibition in certain emergencies
Citation: us:usc:18:2332e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2332e
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 113B (Terrorism). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Attorney General may request the Secretary of Defense to provide assistance under section 382 of title 10 1 in support of Department of Justice activities relating to the enforcement of section 2332a of this title during an emergency situation involving a weapon of mass destruction. The authority to make such a request may be exercised by another official of the Department of Justice in accordance with section 382(f)(2) of title 10.1
