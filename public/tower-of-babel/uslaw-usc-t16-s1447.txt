16 U.S.C. § 1447 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:16:1447
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1447
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 32A (Regional Marine Research Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this chapter is to establish regional research programs, under effective Federal oversight, to—
(1)
set priorities for regional marine and coastal research in support of efforts to safeguard the water quality and ecosystem health of each region; and
(2)
carry out such research through grants and improved coordination.
