20 C.F.R. § 220.182 — Before a disability annuity is stopped.
Citation: us:cfr:20:220.182
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-220.182
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 220 (Determining Disability). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Before the Board stops a disability annuity, it will give the annuitant a chance to explain why it should not do so.
