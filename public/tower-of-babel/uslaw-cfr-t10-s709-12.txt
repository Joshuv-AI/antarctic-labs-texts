10 C.F.R. § 709.12 — Defining polygraph examination questions.
Citation: us:cfr:10:709.12
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-709.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 709 (Counterintelligence Evaluation Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The examiner determines the exact wording of the polygraph questions based on the examiner's pretest interview of the covered person, the covered person's understanding of the questions, established test question procedures from the Department of Defense Polygraph Institute, and other input from the covered person.
