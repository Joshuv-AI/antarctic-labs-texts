10 U.S.C. § 9317 — Thirty years or more: regular enlisted members
Citation: us:usc:10:9317
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section9317
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 941 (Retirement For Length Of Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A regular enlisted member of the Air Force or the Space Force who has at least 30 years of service computed under section 9325 of this title shall be retired upon his request.
