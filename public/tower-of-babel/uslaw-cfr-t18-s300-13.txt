18 C.F.R. § 300.13 — Waiver of filing requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:18:300.13
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-300.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 300 (Confirmation And Approval Of The Rates Of Federal Power Marketing Administrations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator must request waiver of any requirement of this subpart if an application that does not fully comply with that requirement is not to be considered deficient. The request must state the Administrator's reasons for such noncompliance and show good cause for any waiver.
