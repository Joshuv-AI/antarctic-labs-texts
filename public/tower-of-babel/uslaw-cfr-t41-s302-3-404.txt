41 C.F.R. § 302-3.404 — -3.404 Payment for TCS expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-3.404
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-3.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-3 (3—Relocation Allowance By Specific Type). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Payment of TCS expenses stops once the employee's temporary official station becomes their permanent official station. The agency may not pay any TCS expenses incurred beginning the day the employee's temporary official station becomes their permanent official station.
