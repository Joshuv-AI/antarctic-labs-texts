40 C.F.R. § 25.9 — Permit enforcement.
Citation: us:cfr:40:25.9
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-25.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 25 (Public Participation In Programs Under The Resource Conservation And Recovery Act, The Safe Drinking Water Act, And The Clean Water Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each agency administering a permit program shall develop internal procedures for receiving evidence submitted by citizens about permit violations and ensuring that it is properly considered. Public effort in reporting violations shall be encouraged, and the agency shall make available information on reporting procedures. The agency shall investigate alleged violations promptly.
