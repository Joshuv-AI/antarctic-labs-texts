25 C.F.R. § 162.218 — Is there a standard agricultural lease form?
Citation: us:cfr:25:162.218
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-162.218
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 162 (Leases And Permits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Based on the need for flexibility in advertising, negotiating and drafting of appropriate lease terms and conditions, there is no standard agricultural lease form that must be used. We will assist the Indian landowners in drafting lease provisions that conform to the requirements of this part.
