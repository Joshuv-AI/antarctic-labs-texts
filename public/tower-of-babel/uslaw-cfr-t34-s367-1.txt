34 C.F.R. § 367.1 — What is the Independent Living Services for Older Individuals Who Are Blind program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:367.1
Creator: Office Of Special Education And Rehabilitative Services, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-367.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 367 (Independent Living Services For Older Individuals Who Are Blind). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This program supports projects that—
(a) Provide any of the independent living (IL) services to older individuals who are blind that are described in § 367.3(b);
(b) Conduct activities that will improve or expand services for these individuals; and
(c) Conduct activities to help improve public understanding of the challenges of these individuals.
