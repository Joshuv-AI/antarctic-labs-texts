46 U.S.C. § 56307 — Return of vessels
Citation: us:usc:46:56307
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section56307
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part E (Control Of Merchant Marine Capabilities); Chapter 563 (Emergency Acquisition Of Vessels). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When a vessel requisitioned for use but not ownership is returned to the owner, the Secretary of Transportation shall—
(1)
return the vessel in a condition at least as good as when taken, less ordinary wear and tear; or
(2)
pay the owner an amount sufficient to recondition the vessel to that condition, less ordinary wear and tear.
