36 U.S.C. § 151712 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:36:151712
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section151712
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1517 (National Film Preservation Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As soon as practicable after the end of each fiscal year, the corporation shall submit a report to Congress on the activities of the corporation during the prior fiscal year, including a complete statement of its receipts, expenditures, and investments.
