33 U.S.C. § 2315a — Transparency in accounting and administrative expenses
Citation: us:usc:33:2315a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section2315a
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 36 (Water Resources Development); Subchapter V (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On the request of a non-Federal interest, the Secretary shall provide to the non-Federal interest a detailed accounting of the Federal expenses associated with a water resources project.
