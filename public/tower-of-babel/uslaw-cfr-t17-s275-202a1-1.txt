17 C.F.R. § 275.202(a)(1)-1 — (a)(1)-1 Certain transactions not deemed assignments.
Citation: us:cfr:17:275.202(a)(1)-1
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-275.202(a)(1)-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 275 (Rules And Regulations, Investment Advisers Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A transaction which does not result in a change of actual control or management of an investment adviser is not an assignment for purposes of section 205(a)(2) of the Act.
