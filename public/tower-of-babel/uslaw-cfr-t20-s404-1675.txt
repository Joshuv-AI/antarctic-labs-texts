20 C.F.R. § 404.1675 — Finding of substantial failure.
Citation: us:cfr:20:404.1675
Creator: Social Security Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-404.1675
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 404 (Federal Old-Age, Survivors And Disability Insurance (1950- )). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A finding of substantial failure with respect to a State may not be made unless and until the State is afforded an opportunity for a hearing.
