14 U.S.C. § 565 — Use of unmanned aircraft systems
Citation: us:usc:14:565
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section565
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 5 (Functions And Powers); Subchapter IV (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

With respect to any unmanned aircraft system procured by the Coast Guard, the Commandant shall ensure that such system be used to support the primary duties of the Coast Guard pursuant to section 102.
