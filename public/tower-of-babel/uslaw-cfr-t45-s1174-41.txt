45 C.F.R. § 1174.41 — Rights to administrative offset.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1174.41
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1174.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1174 (Program Fraud Civil Remedies Act Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authority may make an administrative offset under 31 U.S.C. 3716 to collect the amount of any penalty or assessment which has become final, for which a judgment has been entered, or which the parties agree upon in a compromise or settlement. However, the authority may not make an administrative offset under this subsection against a Federal tax refund that the United States owes to the defendant then or at a later time.
