22 C.F.R. § 51.50 — Form of payment.
Citation: us:cfr:22:51.50
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-51.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 51 (Passports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Passport fees must be paid in U.S. currency or in other forms of payments permitted by the Department.
