47 C.F.R. § 95.575 — FRS modulation limits.
Citation: us:cfr:47:95.575
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-95.575
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 95 (Personal Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each FRS transmitter type must be designed such that the peak frequency deviation does not exceed 2.5 kHz, and the highest audio frequency contributing substantially to modulation must not exceed 3.125 kHz.
