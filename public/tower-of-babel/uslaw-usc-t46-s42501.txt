46 U.S.C. § 42501 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:46:42501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section42501
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Actions To Address Foreign Practices); Chapter 425 (National Shipper Advisory Committee). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1) Commission.—
The term “Commission” means the Federal Maritime Commission.
(2) Committee.—
The term “Committee” means the National Shipper Advisory Committee established under section 42502.
