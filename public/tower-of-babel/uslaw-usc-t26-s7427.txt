26 U.S.C. § 7427 — Tax return preparers
Citation: us:usc:26:7427
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section7427
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 76 (Judicial Proceedings); Subchapter B (Proceedings By Taxpayers And Third Parties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In any proceeding involving the issue of whether or not a tax return preparer has willfully attempted in any manner to understate the liability for tax (within the meaning of section 6694(b)), the burden of proof in respect to such issue shall be upon the Secretary.
