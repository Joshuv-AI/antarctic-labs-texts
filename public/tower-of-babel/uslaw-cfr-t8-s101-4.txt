8 C.F.R. § 101.4 — Registration procedure.
Citation: us:cfr:8:101.4
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-101.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 101 (Presumption Of Lawful Admission). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedure for an application for creation of a record of lawful permanent residence and a Permanent Resident Card, Form I-551, for a person eligible for presumption of lawful admission for permanent residence under § 101.1 or § 101.2 or for lawful permanent residence as a person born in the United States to a foreign government employee under § 101.3 is described in § 264.2 of this chapter.
