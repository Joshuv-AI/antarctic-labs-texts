43 C.F.R. § 404.7 — What types of projects are eligible for consideration under the program?
Citation: us:cfr:43:404.7
Creator: Bureau Of Reclamation, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-404.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 404 (Reclamation Rural Water Supply Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be eligible for consideration under the program, a rural water supply project must:
(a) Meet the definition of a rural water supply project in § 404.2; and
(b) Be located in a Reclamation State, as defined in § 404.2.
