14 C.F.R. § 35.24 — Strength.
Citation: us:cfr:14:35.24
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-35.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 35 (Airworthiness Standards: Propellers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The maximum stresses developed in the propeller may not exceed values acceptable to the Administrator considering the particular form of construction and the most severe operating conditions.
