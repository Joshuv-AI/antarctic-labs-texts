45 C.F.R. § 2502.80 — What may the General Counsel do upon receipt of the process and pleadings and report of circumstances?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2502.80
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2502 (Employee Indemnification Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Where appropriate, the General Counsel may request that the Department of Justice provide legal representation for the AmeriCorps employee.
