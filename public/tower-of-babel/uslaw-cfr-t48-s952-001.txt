48 C.F.R. § 952.001 — 952.001 General policy.
Citation: us:cfr:48:952.001
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-952.001
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 952 (Solicitation Provisions And Contract Clauses). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It is DOE policy to use the prescribed FAR and DOE contract clauses wherever practicable. Uniformity in the use of contract clauses helps to ensure impartial treatment of all contractors, expedites negotiation and contract review, and facilitates contract administration.
