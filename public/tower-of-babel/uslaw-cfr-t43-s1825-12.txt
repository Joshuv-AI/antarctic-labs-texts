43 C.F.R. § 1825.12 — When does relinquished land become available again for other application or appropriation?
Citation: us:cfr:43:1825.12
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1825.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1820 (Application Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Relinquished land may not again become available until BLM notes the filed relinquishment of an interest on the land records maintained by the BLM office having jurisdiction over the lands involved. If you have any questions regarding the availability of a particular tract of land, you should contact the BLM office having jurisdiction over the lands or records.
