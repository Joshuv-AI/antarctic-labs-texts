31 C.F.R. § 375.1 — Where are the rules for the redemption operation located?
Citation: us:cfr:31:375.1
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-375.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 375 (Marketable Treasury Securities Redemption Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions in this part and the redemption operation announcement govern the redemption of marketable Treasury securities under 31 U.S.C. 3111. (See § 375.10.)
