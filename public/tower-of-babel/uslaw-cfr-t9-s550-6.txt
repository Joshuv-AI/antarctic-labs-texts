9 C.F.R. § 550.6 — Information and reports required from official establishment operators.
Citation: us:cfr:9:550.6
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-550.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 550 (Records Required To Be Kept). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The information and reporting requirements in 9 CFR 320.6 for operators of official establishments apply with respect to fish and fish products as they do with respect to other species amenable to the FMIA.
