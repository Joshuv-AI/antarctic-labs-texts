43 C.F.R. § 2650.5-5 — -5 Cemetery sites and historical places.
Citation: us:cfr:43:2650.5-5
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-2650.5-5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 2650 (Alaska Native Selections). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only those cemetery sites and historical places to be conveyed under section 14(h)(1) of the Act shall be surveyed.
