21 C.F.R. § 1.914 — Under what circumstances will we waive a requirement of this subpart?
Citation: us:cfr:21:1.914
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-1.914
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 1 (General Enforcement Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will waive any requirement of this subpart with respect to any class of persons, vehicles, food, or nonfood products, when we determine that:
(a) The waiver will not result in the transportation of food under conditions that would be unsafe for human or animal health; and
(b) The waiver will not be contrary to the public interest.
