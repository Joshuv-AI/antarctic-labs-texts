7 C.F.R. § 1260.640 — Application for Certification Form.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1260.640
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1260.640
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1260 (Beef Promotion And Research). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following official form, “Application for Certification of Association or Organization,” must be completed and submitted to the Department by eligible State organizations or associations seeking certification by the Secretary. This form may be reproduced.
