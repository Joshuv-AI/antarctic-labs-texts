25 C.F.R. § 39.221 — What is a full school month?
Citation: us:cfr:25:39.221
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-39.221
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 39 (The Indian School Equalization Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A full school month is each 30-day period following the first day that residential services are provided to students based on the school residential calendar.
