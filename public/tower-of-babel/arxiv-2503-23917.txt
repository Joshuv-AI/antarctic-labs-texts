A construction of curvature-adapted hypersurfaces in the product of symmetric spaces
Naoyuki Koike

Abstract
In this paper, we give a construction of curvature-adapted hypersurfaces in the product G 1 / K 1 × G 2 / K 2 G_{1}/K_{1}\times G_{2}/K_{2} of (Riemannian) symmetric spaces G i / K i G_{i}/K_{i} ( i = 1 , 2 i=1,2 ). By this construction, we obtain many examples of curvature-adapted hypersurfaces in G 1 / K 1 × G 2 / K 2 G_{1}/K_{1}\times G_{2}/K_{2} . Also, we calculate the eigenvalues of the shape operator and the normal Jacobi operator of the curvature-adapted hypersurfaces obtained by this construction.

Basic notions and facts

2.1
Curvature-adapted hypersurfaces

Let
M
be a connected oriented hypersurface in a Riemannian manifold
(\widetilde{M},\widetilde{g})
immersed by an immersion
f:M\hookrightarrow\widetilde{M}

and
\bm{N}
be a unit normal vector field of this hypersurface
M
.
Denote by
\widetilde{\nabla}
and
\widetilde{R}
the Riemannian connection and the curvature tensor of
\widetilde{g}
, respectively.
Also, denote by
A
and
\widetilde{R}(\bm{N})
the shape tensor and the normal Jacobi operator of the submanifold
M
for
\bm{N}
, where

\widetilde{R}(\bm{N})
is the
(1,1)
-tensor field on
M
defined by

\widetilde{R}(\bm{N})_{p}:=df_{p}^{-1}\circ\widetilde{R}_{f(p)}(\cdot,\bm{N}_{p})\bm{N}_{p}\circ df_{p}\quad\,\,(p\in M).

If, for each
p\in M
,
A_{p}
and
\widetilde{R}(\bm{N})_{p}
commutes (i.e.,
[A_{p},\widetilde{R}(\bm{N})_{p}]=0
) for each
p\in M
, then
the hypersurface
M
is said to be
curavture-adapted
.
This notion was introduced by Berndt-Vanhecke (
[
1
]
).

Assume that
M
is curvature-adapted. Denote by
g
the induced metric
f^{\ast}\widetilde{g}
on
M
.
Since
A_{p}
and
\widetilde{R}(\bm{N})_{p}
are symmetric
(1,1)
-tensor on
(T_{p}M,g_{p})
, they are diagonalizeble with respect to an orthonormal basis
and we have the orthogonal decompositions

\displaystyle{T_{p}M=\mathop{\oplus}_{i\in I_{A,p}}E_{i,p}^{A}}
cosisting of eigenspaces of
A_{p}
and the orthogonal decomposition

\displaystyle{T_{p}M=}

\displaystyle{\mathop{\oplus}_{i\in I_{R,p}}E_{i,p}^{R}}
consisting of the eigenspace of
\widetilde{R}(\bm{N})_{p}
.
Define two families
\{\lambda_{i,p}\}_{i\in I_{A,p}}
and
\{\mu_{i,p}\}_{i\in I_{R,p}}
of real numbers by

A_{p}|_{E_{i,p}^{A}}=\lambda_{i,p}\,{\rm id}\quad\,\,{\rm and}\quad\,\,\widetilde{R}(\bm{N})_{p}|_{E_{i,p}^{R}}=\mu_{i,p}\,{\rm id}.

Define linear subspaces

(
(i,j)\in I_{A,p}\times I_{R,p}
) on
M
by

E_{ij,p}:=E_{i,p}^{A}\cap E_{j,p}^{R}
. Set

I_{AR,p}:=\{(i,j)\in I_{A,p}\times I_{R,p}\,|\,{\rm dim}\,E_{ij,p}\not=0\}.

Since
A_{p}
and
\widetilde{R}(\bm{N})_{p}
commute, they have simultaneously daigonalizable, that is,
the following orthogonal decomposition holds:

T_{p}M=\mathop{\oplus}_{(i,j)\in I_{AR,p}}E_{ij,p}.

This orthogonal decomposition is called the
common eigenspace decomposition
of
A_{p}
and
\widetilde{R}(\bm{N})_{p}
.

Take

\bm{Y}
be a Jacobi field along

\bm{Y}^{\prime}(s)
(resp.

M

[formula]

where

respectively.
