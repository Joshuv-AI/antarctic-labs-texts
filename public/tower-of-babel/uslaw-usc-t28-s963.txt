28 U.S.C. § 963 — Courts defined
Citation: us:usc:28:963
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section963
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part III (Court Officers And Employees); Chapter 57 (General Provisions Applicable To Court Officers And Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

As used in this chapter, unless the context indicates otherwise, the words “court” and “courts” include the Supreme Court of the United States and the courts enumerated in section 610 of this title.
