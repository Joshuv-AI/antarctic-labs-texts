16 U.S.C. § 230e — Establishment; notice in Federal Register; administration
Citation: us:usc:16:230e
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section230e
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XXV (Jean Lafitte National Historical Park And Preserve); Part A (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall administer the park in accordance with the provisions of this part, the Act of August 25, 1916 (39 Stat. 535),1 the Act of August 21, 1935 (49 Stat. 666),1 and any other statutory authorities available to him for the conservation and management of natural, historical, and cultural resources.
