45 U.S.C. § 366 — Separability
Citation: us:usc:45:366
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2024
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title45-section366
Context: United States Code, Title 45 (Railroads); Chapter 11 (Railroad Unemployment Insurance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application there of to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances, and the remainder of this chapter shall not be affected thereby.
