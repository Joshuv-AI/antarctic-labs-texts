29 C.F.R. § 4003.33 — Where to submit request for reconsideration.
Citation: us:cfr:29:4003.33
Creator: Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-4003.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 4003 (Rules For Administrative Review Of Agency Decisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A request for reconsideration must be submitted to the Director of the department within PBGC that issued the initial determination, except that a request for reconsideration of an initial determination described in § 4003.1(d)(2)(ii) must be submitted to the Director. See § 4000.4 of this chapter for information on where to file.
