43 U.S.C. § 421g — Existing rights unaffected
Citation: us:usc:43:421g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section421g
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter III (Institution And Construction Of Projects). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in sections 421a to 421h of this title shall be construed to repeal or limit the procedural and substantive requirements of sections 372 and 383 of this title.
