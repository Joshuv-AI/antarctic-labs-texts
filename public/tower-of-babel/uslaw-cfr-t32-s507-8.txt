32 C.F.R. § 507.8 — Authority to sell.
Citation: us:cfr:32:507.8
Creator: Department Of The Army
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-507.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 507 (Manufacture, Sale, Wear, And Quality Control Of Heraldic Items). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No certificate of authority to manufacture is required for selling controlled heraldic items listed in § 507.13. However, all sellers must ensure that all articles they sell bear hallmarks assigned by TIOH and are manufactured by certified manufacturers in conformance with applicable Government specifications.
