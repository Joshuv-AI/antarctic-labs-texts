45 C.F.R. § 606.50 — Program accessibility: Discrimination prohibited.
Citation: us:cfr:45:606.50
Creator: National Science Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-606.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 606 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The National Science Foundation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as otherwise provided in § 606.51, no qualified individual with handicaps shall, because the Foundation's facilities are inaccessible to or unusable by individuals with handicaps, be denied the benefits of, be excluded from participation in, or otherwise be subjected to discrimination under any program or activity conducted by the Foundation.
