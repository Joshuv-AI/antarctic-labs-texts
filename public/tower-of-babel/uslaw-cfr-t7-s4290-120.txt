7 C.F.R. § 4290.120 — Plan to invest in Rural Areas.
Citation: us:cfr:7:4290.120
Creator: Rural Business-Cooperative Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-4290.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 4290 (Rural Business Investment Company (“Rbic”) Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An Applicant must agree that if licensed as a RBIC, it will make Developmental Capital investments in Enterprises that will create wealth and job opportunities in Rural Areas and among individuals living in those areas.
