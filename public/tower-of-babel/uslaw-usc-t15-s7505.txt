15 U.S.C. § 7505 — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:15:7505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section7505
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 101 (Nanotechnology Research And Development). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) National Science Foundation
There are authorized to be appropriated to the Director of the National Science Foundation to carry out the Director’s responsibilities under this chapter—
(1)
$385,000,000 for fiscal year 2005;
(2)
$424,000,000 for fiscal year 2006;
(3)
$449,000,000 for fiscal year 2007; and
(4)
$476,000,000 for fiscal year 2008.
(b) Department of Energy
There are authorized to be appropriated to the Secretary of Energy to carry out the Secretary’s responsibilities under this chapter—
(1)
$317,000,000 for fiscal year 2005;
(2)
$347,000,000 for fiscal year 2006;
(3)
$380,000,000 for fiscal year 2007; and
(4)
$415,000,000 for fiscal year 2008.
(c) National Aeronautics and Space Administration
There are authorized to be appropriated to the Administrator of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration to carry out the Administrator’s responsibilities under this chapter—

$34,100,000 for fiscal year 2005;
(2)
$37,500,000 for fiscal year 2006;
(3)
$40,000,000 for fiscal year 2007; and
(4)
$42,300,000 for fiscal year 2008.
(d) National Institute of Standards and Technology

$80,000,000 for fiscal year 2007; and
