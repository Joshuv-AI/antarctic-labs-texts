25 C.F.R. § 533.7 — Void agreements.
Citation: us:cfr:25:533.7
Creator: National Indian Gaming Commission, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-533.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 533 (Approval Of Management Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Management contracts and changes in persons with a financial interest in or management responsibility for a management contract, that have not been approved by the Chairman in accordance with the requirements of part 531 of this chapter and this part, are void.
