46 C.F.R. § 108.215 — Insect screens.
Citation: us:cfr:46:108.215
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-108.215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 108 (Design And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Accommodation spaces must be protected against the admission of insects.
(b) Insect screens must be installed when natural ventilation is provided.
