32 C.F.R. § 1803.22 — Requests barred by res judicata.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1803.22
Creator: National Counterintelligence Center
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1803.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1803 (Public Requests For Mandatory Declassification Review Of Classified Information Pursuant To Section 3.6 Of Executive Order 12958). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Coordinator shall respond to the requester and deny any request where the information in question has been the subject of a classification review within the previous two (2) years or is the subject of pending litigation in the federal courts.
