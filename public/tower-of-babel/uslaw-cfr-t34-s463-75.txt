34 C.F.R. § 463.75 — Who is eligible to receive education services through the Integrated English Literacy and Civics Education program?
Citation: us:cfr:34:463.75
Creator: Office Of Career, Technical, And Adult Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-463.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 463 (Adult Education And Family Literacy Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Individuals who otherwise meet the definition of “eligible individual” and are English language learners, including professionals with degrees and credentials obtained in their native countries, may receive Integrated English Literacy and Civics Education services.
