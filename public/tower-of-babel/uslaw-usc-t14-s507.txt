14 U.S.C. § 507 — Appointment of judges
Citation: us:usc:14:507
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section507
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 5 (Functions And Powers); Subchapter I (General Powers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may appoint civilian employees of the department in which the Coast Guard is operating as appellate military judges, available for assignment to the Coast Guard Court of Criminal Appeals as provided for in section 866(a) of title 10.
