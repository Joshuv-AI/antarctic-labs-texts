32 C.F.R. § 147.24 — The national agency check.
Citation: us:cfr:32:147.24
Creator: Office Of The Secretary Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-147.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 147 (Adjudicative Guidelines For Determining Eligibility For Access To Classified Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The National Agency Check is a part of all investigations and reinvestigations. It consists of a review of;
(a) Investigative and criminal history files of the FBI, including a technical fingerprint search;
(b) OPM's Security/Suitability Investigations Index;
(c) DoD's Defense Clearance and Investigations Index;
(d) Such other national agencies (e.g., CIA, INS) as appropriate to the individual's background.
