48 C.F.R. § 9901.307 — 9901.307 Exemptions and waivers.
Citation: us:cfr:48:9901.307
Creator: Chapter 99—Cost Accounting Standards Board, Office Of Federal Procurement Policy, Office Of Management And Budget
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-9901.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 9901 (Rules And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Board may exempt classes or categories of contractors and subcontractors from cost accounting standards requirements, and establish procedures for waiver of the requirements with respect to individual contracts and subcontracts. The official records of the Board shall be documented with supporting justification for class or category exemptions and individual waivers.
