47 C.F.R. § 76.1604 — Charges for customer service changes.
Citation: us:cfr:47:76.1604
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-76.1604
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 76 (Multichannel Video And Cable Television Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If a cable operator establishes a higher charge for changes effected solely by coded entry on a computer terminal or by other similarly simple methods, as provided in § 76.980(d), the cable system must notify all subscribers in writing that they may be subject to such a charge for changing service tiers more than the specified number of times in any 12 month period.
