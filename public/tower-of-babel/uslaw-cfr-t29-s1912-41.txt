29 C.F.R. § 1912.41 — Legal services.
Citation: us:cfr:29:1912.41
Creator: Occupational Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-1912.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 1912 (Advisory Committees On Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Solicitor of Labor shall provide such legal assistance as may be necessary or appropriate for advisory committees to carry out their functions in accordance with the requirements of this part.
