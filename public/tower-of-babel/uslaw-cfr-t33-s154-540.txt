33 C.F.R. § 154.540 — Discharge removal.
Citation: us:cfr:33:154.540
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-154.540
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 154 (Facilities Transferring Oil Or Hazardous Material In Bulk). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each facility to which this part applies must have a means to safely remove discharged oil or hazardous material, within one hour of completion of the transfer, from the containment required by § 154.530 of this part without discharging the oil or hazardous material into the water.
