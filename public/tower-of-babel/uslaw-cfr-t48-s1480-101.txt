48 C.F.R. § 1480.101 — 1480.101 Scope of part.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1480 (Acquisitions Under The Buy Indian Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part implements policies and procedures for the procurement of supplies, general services, architect and engineering (A&E) services, or construction while giving preference to Indian Economic Enterprises under authority of the Buy Indian Act (25 U.S.C. 47).
