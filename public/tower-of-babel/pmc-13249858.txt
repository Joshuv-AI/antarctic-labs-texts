Correction: Effects of DRG pulsed radiofrequency parameters on the clinical outcome for battered sensory never syndrome: a prospective, triple-blind, randomized controlled trial
Authors: Qiao Wang, Rong Hu, Rui Han, Yuncheng Ni, Haocheng Zhou, Dong Huang, Yuzhao Huang, Qiufang Deng
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-026-36277-9, published online 19 January 2026

The original version of this Article contained errors in the Title, Abstract, Figure 4. For the Title and the abstract, the word “nerve” in “battered sensory nerve syndrome” was give as “never”.

As a result, the Title of the article was changed from: “Effects of DRG pulsed radiofrequency parameters on the clinical outcome for battered sensory never syndrome: a prospective, triple-blind, randomized controlled trial.”

to now read: “Effects of DRG pulsed radiofrequency parameters on the clinical outcome for battered sensory never syndrome: a prospective, triple-blind, randomized controlled trial.”

Additionally the first sentence of the abstract was changed form: “Battered Sensory Never Syndrome (BSNS) is a condition characterized by persistent, long-term mechanical compression of the sensory roots, dorsal root ganglia (DRG), and sensory fibers of the spinal nerve roots.”

to read: “Battered Sensory Nerve Syndrome (BSNS) is a condition characterized by persistent, long-term mechanical compression of the sensory roots, dorsal root ganglia (DRG), and sensory fibers of the spinal nerve roots.”

Lastly Figure 4 specifically Figure 4C was duplicated from 4B. The legend was not affected. The original Fig. 4 appears below.Fig. 4Efficacy comparison between 42 °C (3 min + 12 min) and 55 °C (3 min + 12 min) PRF groups. (A). The NRS scores within 12 months, the 55 °C PRF group was significantly lower than that of the 42 °C PRF group at 1 month postoperatively. Unpaired Student’s t-test: **p < 0.01. (B) the number of patients with effective treatment 1 month after PRF. Fisher’s exact test: *p < 0.05. (C) the number of patients with ODI reduction ≥ 50% one month after PRF. Fisher’s exact test: *p < 0.05. NRS, Numeric Rating Scale; Effective treatment: NRS > 50% reduction; ODI, Oswestry Disability Index.
