14 C.F.R. § 136.35 — Prohibition of commercial air tour operations over the Rocky Mountain National Park.
Citation: us:cfr:14:136.35
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-136.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 136 (Commercial Air Tours And National Parks Air Tour Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All commercial air tour operations in the airspace over the Rocky Mountain National Park are prohibited regardless of altitude.
