50 C.F.R. § 29.152 — Will the Service adjust the amount required for my financial assurance?
Citation: us:cfr:50:29.152
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-29.152
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 29 (Land Use Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Service may require, or you may request, an adjustment to the financial assurance amount because of any circumstances that increase or decrease the estimated costs established under § 29.151.
