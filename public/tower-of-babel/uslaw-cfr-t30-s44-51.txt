30 C.F.R. § 44.51 — Finality for purposes of judicial review.
Citation: us:cfr:30:44.51
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-44.51
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 44 (Rules Of Practice For Petitions For Modification Of Mandatory Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Only a decision by the Assistant Secretary shall be deemed final agency action for purposes of judicial review. A decision by an Administrator or administrative law judge which becomes final for lack of appeal is not deemed final agency action for purposes of 5 U.S.C. 704.
