10 C.F.R. § 205.6 — Extension of time.
Citation: us:cfr:10:205.6
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-205.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 205 (Administrative Procedures And Sanctions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a document is required to be filed within a prescribed time, an extension of time to file may be granted by the office with which the document is required to be filed upon good cause shown.
