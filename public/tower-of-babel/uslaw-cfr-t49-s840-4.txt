49 C.F.R. § 840.4 — Information to be given in notification.
Citation: us:cfr:49:840.4
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-840.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 840 (Rules Pertaining To Notification Of Railroad Accidents). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The notice required by § 840.3 shall include the following information:
(a) Name and title of person reporting.
(b) Name of railroad.
(c) Location of accident (relate to nearest city).
(d) Time and date of accident.
(e) Description of accident.
(f) Casualties:
(1) Fatalities.
(2) Injuries.
(g) Property damage (estimate).
(h) Name and telephone number of person from whom additional information may be obtained.
