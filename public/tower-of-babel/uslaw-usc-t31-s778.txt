31 U.S.C. § 778 — Dependency and disability decisions
Citation: us:usc:31:778
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section778
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 7 (Government Accountability Office); Subchapter V (Annuities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The General Counsel of the Government Accountability Office shall decide a question of dependency, disability, or dependency and disability under sections 773–776 of this title. A decision under this section is final.
