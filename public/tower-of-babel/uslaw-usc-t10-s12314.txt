10 U.S.C. § 12314 — Reserves: kinds of duty
Citation: us:usc:10:12314
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12314
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1209 (Active Duty). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a member of a reserve component who is on active duty other than for training may, under regulations prescribed by the Secretary concerned, be detailed or assigned to any duty authorized by law for members of the regular component of the armed force concerned.
