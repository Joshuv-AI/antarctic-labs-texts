7 C.F.R. § 502.13 — Weapons and explosives.
Citation: us:cfr:7:502.13
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-502.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 502 (Conduct On Beltsville Agriculture Research Center Property, Beltsville, Maryland). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person while in or on BARC property shall carry firearms, other dangerous or deadly weapons, or explosives, either openly or concealed, except as officially authorized for official purposes.
