10 U.S.C. § 6211 — Accelerated schedule for isolating high-level nuclear waste at the Defense Waste Processing Facility, Savannah River Site
Citation: us:usc:10:6211
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section6211
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part VI (Elements Of Department Of Defense And Other Matters); Subpart B (Atomic Energy Defense); Chapter 604 (Defense Environmental Cleanup Matters); Subchapter IV (Savannah River Site, South Carolina). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Energy shall accelerate the schedule for the isolation of high-level nuclear waste in glass canisters at the Defense Waste Processing Facility at the Savannah River Site, South Carolina, if the Secretary determines that the acceleration of such schedule—
(1)
will achieve long-term cost savings to the Federal Government; and
(2)
could accelerate the removal and isolation of high-level nuclear waste from long-term storage tanks at the site.
