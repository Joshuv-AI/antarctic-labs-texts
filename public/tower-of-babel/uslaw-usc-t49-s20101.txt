49 U.S.C. § 20101 — Purpose
Citation: us:usc:49:20101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section20101
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Safety); Chapter 201 (General); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purpose of this chapter is to promote safety in every area of railroad operations and reduce railroad-related accidents and incidents.
