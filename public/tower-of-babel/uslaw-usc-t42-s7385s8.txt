42 U.S.C. § 7385s–8 — Medical benefits
Citation: us:usc:42:7385s–8
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7385s–8
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter XVI (Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program); Part E (Contractor Employee Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A covered DOE contractor employee shall be furnished medical benefits specified in section 7384t of this title for the covered illness to the same extent, and under the same conditions and limitations, as an individual eligible for medical benefits under that section is furnished medical benefits under that section.
