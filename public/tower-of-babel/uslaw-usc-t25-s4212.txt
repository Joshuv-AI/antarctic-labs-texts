25 U.S.C. § 4212 — Training and technical assistance
Citation: us:usc:25:4212
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section4212
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 43 (Native American Housing Assistance And Self-Determination); Subchapter VII (Other Housing Assistance For Native Americans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated for assistance for a national organization representing Native American housing interests for providing training and technical assistance to Indian housing authorities and tribally designated housing entities such sums as may be necessary for each of fiscal years 2009 through 2013.
