48 C.F.R. § 1832.501-1 — 1832.501-1 Customary progress payment rates. (NASA supplements paragraph (a))
Citation: us:cfr:48:1832.501-1
Creator: Chapter 18—National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1832.501-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1832 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The customary progress payment rate for all NASA contracts is 85 percent for large business, 90 percent for small business, 95 percent for small disadvantaged business, and 100 percent for Phase II contracts in the Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) and Small Business Technology Transfer (STTR) programs. The contracting officer shall insert the applicable percentage in paragraphs (a) and (b) of the clause at FAR 52.232-16.
