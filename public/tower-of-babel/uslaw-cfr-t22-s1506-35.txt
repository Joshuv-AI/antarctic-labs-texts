22 C.F.R. § 1506.35 — Will USADF execute a release after full payment of a compromised amount?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1506.35
Creator: African Development Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1506.35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1506 (Collection Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon receipt of a payment in full or a compromised amount of a claim, USADF will prepare and execute a release.
