5 C.F.R. § 576.104 — Additional agency requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:5:576.104
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-576.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 576 (Voluntary Separation Incentive Payments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After OPM approves an agency plan for Voluntary Separation Incentive Payments, the agency must immediately notify OPM of any subsequent changes in the conditions that served as the basis for the approval of the Voluntary Separation Incentive Payment authority.
