23 C.F.R. § 1.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:1.1
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-1.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 1 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The purpose of the regulations in this part is to implement and carry out the provisions of Federal law relating to the administration of Federal aid for highways.
