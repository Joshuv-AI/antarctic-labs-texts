45 C.F.R. § 1616.6 — Equal employment opportunity.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1616.6
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1616.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1616 (Attorney Hiring). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient shall adopt employment qualifications, procedures, and policies that meet the requirements of applicable laws prohibiting discrimination in employment, and shall take affirmative action to insure equal employment opportunity.
