38 U.S.C. § 7905 — Requirement of honorable discharge for veterans receiving assistance
Citation: us:usc:38:7905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section7905
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part V (Boards, Administrations, And Services); Chapter 79 (Information Security Education Assistance Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No veteran shall receive financial assistance under this chapter unless the veteran was discharged from the Armed Forces under honorable conditions.
