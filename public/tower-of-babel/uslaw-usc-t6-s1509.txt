6 U.S.C. § 1509 — Exception to limitation on authority of Secretary of Defense to disseminate certain information
Citation: us:usc:6:1509
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title6-section1509
Context: United States Code, Title 6 (Domestic Security); Chapter 6 (Cybersecurity); Subchapter I (Cybersecurity Information Sharing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding subsection (c)(3) of section 393 of title 10, the Secretary of Defense may authorize the sharing of cyber threat indicators and defensive measures pursuant to the policies, procedures, and guidelines developed or issued under this subchapter.
