45 C.F.R. § 1603.3 — What is LSC's policy on presentation of testimony and production of documents?
Citation: us:cfr:45:1603.3
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1603.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1603 (Testimony By Employees And Production Of Documents In Proceedings Where The United States Is Not A Party). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In any proceedings to which this part applies, no employee may provide testimony or produce documents concerning information acquired in the course of performing official duties or because of the person's official relationship with LSC unless authorized by the General Counsel or the OIG Legal Counsel pursuant to this part based on his or her determination that compliance with the request would promote LSC's objectives.
