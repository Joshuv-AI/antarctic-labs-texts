36 C.F.R. § 264.11 — Use of symbol.
Citation: us:cfr:36:264.11
Creator: Forest Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-264.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 264 (Property Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in § 264.12, use of the Mount St. Helens National Volcanic Monument official symbol, including a facsimile thereof, in total or in part, is restricted to official signs, publications, and other materials of the Forest Service, U.S. Department of Agriculture.
