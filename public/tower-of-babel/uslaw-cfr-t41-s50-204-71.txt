41 C.F.R. § 50-204.71 — -204.71 Safety relief devices for compressed gas containers.
Citation: us:cfr:41:50-204.71
Creator: Chapter 50—Public Contracts, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-50-204.71
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 50-204 (204—Safety And Health Standards For Federal Supply Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Compressed gas cylinders, portable tanks, and cargo tanks shall have pressure relief devices installed and maintained in accordance with Compressed Gas Association Pamphlets S-1.1-1963 and 1965 addenda and S-1.2-1963.
