17 C.F.R. § 274.202 — Form 3, initial statement of beneficial ownership of securities.
Citation: us:cfr:17:274.202
Creator: Securities And Exchange Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-274.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 274 (Forms Prescribed Under The Investment Company Act Of 1940). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This form shall be filed pursuant to § 270.30h-1 for initial statements of beneficial ownership of securities required to be filed pursuant to section 30(h) of the Investment Company Act of 1940 (15 U.S.C. 80a-29(h)). (Same as § 249.103 of this chapter.)
