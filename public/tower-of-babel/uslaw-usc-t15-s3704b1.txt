15 U.S.C. § 3704b–1 — Recovery of operating costs through fee collections
Citation: us:usc:15:3704b–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section3704b–1
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 63 (Technology Innovation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Operating costs for the National Technical Information Service associated with the acquisition, processing, storage, bibliographic control, and archiving of information and documents shall be recovered primarily through the collection of fees.
