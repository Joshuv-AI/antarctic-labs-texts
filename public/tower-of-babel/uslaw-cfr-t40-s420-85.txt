40 C.F.R. § 420.85 — Pretreatment standards for existing sources (PSES).
Citation: us:cfr:40:420.85
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-420.85
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 420 (Iron And Steel Manufacturing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in 40 CFR 403.7 and 403.13, any existing source subject to this subpart which introduces pollutants into a publicly owned treatment works must comply with 40 CFR part 403 and achieve the following pretreatment standards for existing sources.
(a) Salt bath descaling, oxidizing—(1) Batch, sheet and plate.
(2) Batch, rod and wire.
(3) Batch, pipe and tube.
(4) Continuous.
(b) Salt bath descaling, reducing—(1) Batch.
(2) Continuous.
