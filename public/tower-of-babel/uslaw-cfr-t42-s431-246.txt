42 C.F.R. § 431.246 — Corrective action.
Citation: us:cfr:42:431.246
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-431.246
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 431 (State Organization And General Administration). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency must promptly make corrective payments, retroactive to the date an incorrect action was taken, and, if appropriate, provide for admission or readmission of an individual to a facility if—
(a) The hearing decision is favorable to the applicant or beneficiary; or
(b) The agency decides in the applicant's or beneficiary's favor before the hearing.
