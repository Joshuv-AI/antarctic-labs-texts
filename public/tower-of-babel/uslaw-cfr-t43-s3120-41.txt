43 C.F.R. § 3120.41 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:43:3120.41
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3120.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3120 (Competitive Leases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The lands available for competitive lease sale under this subpart will be described in a Notice of Competitive Lease Sale.
(b) The time, date, and place of the competitive lease sale will be stated in the notice.
(c) The notice will include an identification of, and a copy of, stipulations applicable to each parcel.
