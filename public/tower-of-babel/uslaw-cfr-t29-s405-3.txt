29 C.F.R. § 405.3 — Form of annual report.
Citation: us:cfr:29:405.3
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-405.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 405 (Employer Reports). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

On and after the effective date of this section, every employer required to file an annual report by section 203(a) of the Act and § 405.2 shall file such report on the United States Department of Labor Form LM-10 entitled, “Employer Report” 1 in the detail required by the instructions 1 accompanying such form and constituting a part thereof.
