23 C.F.R. § 750.502 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:23:750.502
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-750.502
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 750 (Highway Beautification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart apply to signs adjacent to the Interstate and primary systems which are required to be controlled under 23 U.S.C. 131.
