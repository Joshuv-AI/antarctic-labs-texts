29 U.S.C. § 155 — National Labor Relations Board; principal office, conducting inquiries throughout country; participation in decisions or inquiries conducted by member
Citation: us:usc:29:155
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section155
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 7 (Labor-Management Relations); Subchapter II (National Labor Relations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The principal office of the Board shall be in the District of Columbia, but it may meet and exercise any or all of its powers at any other place. The Board may, by one or more of its members or by such agents or agencies as it may designate, prosecute any inquiry necessary to its functions in any part of the United States. A member who participates in such an inquiry shall not be disqualified from subsequently participating in a decision of the Board in the same case.
