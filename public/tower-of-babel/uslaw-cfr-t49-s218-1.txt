49 C.F.R. § 218.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:49:218.1
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-218.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 218 (Railroad Operating Practices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes minimum requirements for railroad operating rules and practices. Each railroad may prescribe additional or more stringent requirements in its operating rules, timetables, timetable special instructions, and other special instructions.
