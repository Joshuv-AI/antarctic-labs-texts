14 C.F.R. § 141.75 — Aircraft requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:14:141.75
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-141.75
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 141 (Pilot Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following items must be carried on each aircraft used for flight training and solo flights:
(a) A pretakeoff and prelanding checklist; and
(b) The operator's handbook for the aircraft, if one is furnished by the manufacturer, or copies of the handbook if furnished to each student using the aircraft.
