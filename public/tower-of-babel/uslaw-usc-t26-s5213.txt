26 U.S.C. § 5213 — Withdrawal of distilled spirits from bonded premises on determination of tax
Citation: us:usc:26:5213
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section5213
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 51 (Distilled Spirits, Wines, And Beer); Subchapter C (Operation Of Distilled Spirits Plants); Part II (Operations On Bonded Premises); Subpart A (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Subject to the provisions of section 5173, distilled spirits may be withdrawn from the bonded premises of a distilled spirits plant on payment or determination of tax thereon, in approved containers, under such regulations as the Secretary shall prescribe.
