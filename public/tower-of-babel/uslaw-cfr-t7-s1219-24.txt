7 C.F.R. § 1219.24 — Secretary.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1219.24
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1219.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1219 (Hass Avocado Promotion, Research, And Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Secretary means the Secretary of Agriculture of the United States or any other officer or employee of the Department to whom authority has heretofore been delegated, or to whom authority may hereafter be delegated, to act in the Secretary's stead.
