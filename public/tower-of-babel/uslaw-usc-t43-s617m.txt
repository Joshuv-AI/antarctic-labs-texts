43 U.S.C. § 617m — Reclamation law applicable
Citation: us:usc:43:617m
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section617m
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12A (Boulder Canyon Project); Subchapter I (Boulder Canyon Project Act). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter shall be deemed a supplement to the reclamation law, which said reclamation law shall govern the construction, operation, and management of the works herein authorized, except as otherwise therein provided.
