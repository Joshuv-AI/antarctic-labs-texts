1.4.

U_{\varepsilon}^{{\rm res}}({L\mathfrak{sl}_{n+1}})

In 1997, Chari and Pressley showed that for any finite-dimensional irreducible
U_{\varepsilon}^{{\rm res}}({L\mathfrak{sl}_{n+1}})

, there exist integers
\xi_{1},\ldots,\xi_{m}
and indexes

is isomorphic to a subquotient of

. They gave a necessary and sufficient condition for the tensor product of fundamental modules of
U_{\varepsilon}^{{\rm res}}({L\mathfrak{sl}_{2}})
to be irreducible, where
\varepsilon
is a root of unity of odd order, see
[
9
, Theorem 9.6]
. In 2007, Abe in
[
1
]
gave a necessary and sufficient condition for the tensor product of fundamental modules of
U_{\varepsilon}^{{\rm res}}({L\mathfrak{sl}_{n+1}})
to be irreducible, where
\varepsilon^{2\ell+1}=1
,
\ell\geq 2
.

Fix
\varepsilon
such that
\varepsilon^{2\ell}=1
for some
\ell\geq 2
. As an application of our path description, we obtain a necessary and sufficient condition for the tensor product of two fundamental representations of
U_{\varepsilon}^{{\rm res}}({L\mathfrak{sl}_{n+1}})
to be irreducible. Additionally, we obtain a necessary condition for the tensor product of two or more fundamental representations of
U_{\varepsilon}^{{\rm res}}({L\mathfrak{sl}_{n+1}})
to be irreducible.

Theorem 1.4

(Theorem
4.9
, Corollary
4.11
)
.

Let

,
i_{1},\ldots,i_{m}\in I
,
\xi_{1},\ldots,\xi_{m}\in\mathbb{Z}
, and

Theorem 4.9
.

Let

\displaystyle h(i_{1},i_{2})\not\equiv h_{0}(i_{1},i_{2})+2s\,(\text{mod }2\ell)\ \text{and }\ h(i_{1},i_{2})\not\equiv-h_{0}(i_{1},i_{2})-2s\,(\text{mod }2\ell),
