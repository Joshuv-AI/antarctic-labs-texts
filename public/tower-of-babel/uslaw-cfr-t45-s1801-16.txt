45 C.F.R. § 1801.16 — Closing date for receipt of nominations.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1801.16
Creator: Harry S. Truman Scholarship Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1801.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1801 (Harry S. Truman Scholarship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Foundation announces in its Bulletin of Information and in the Federal Register and posts on its website (http://www.truman.gov) the date and address at which the Foundation must receive nominations. Nominations not received by this date at the address specified will not be considered.
