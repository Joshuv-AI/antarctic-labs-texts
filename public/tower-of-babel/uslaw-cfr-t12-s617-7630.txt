12 C.F.R. § 617.7630 — Does this Federal requirement affect any state property laws?
Citation: us:cfr:12:617.7630
Creator: Farm Credit Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-617.7630
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 617 (Borrower Rights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The rights provided under section 4.36 of the Act and this section do not affect any right of first refusal under the law of the state in which the property is located.
