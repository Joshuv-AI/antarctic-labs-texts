40 C.F.R. § 113.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:40:113.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-113.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 113 (Liability Limits For Small Onshore Storage Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart establishes size classifications and associated liability limits for small onshore oil storage facilities with fixed capacity of 1,000 barrels or less.
