14 C.F.R. § 61.191 — Additional flight instructor ratings.
Citation: us:cfr:14:61.191
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-61.191
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 61 (Certification: Pilots, Flight Instructors, And Ground Instructors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A person who applies for an additional flight instructor rating on a flight instructor certificate must meet the eligibility requirements listed in § 61.183 of this part that apply to the flight instructor rating sought.
(b) A person who applies for an additional rating on a flight instructor certificate is not required to pass the knowledge test on the areas listed in § 61.185(a)(1) of this part.
