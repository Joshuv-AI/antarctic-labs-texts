40 C.F.R. § 205.54 — Test procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:40:205.54
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-205.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 205 (Transportation Equipment Noise Emission Controls). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The procedures described in this and subsequent sections will be the test program to determine the conformity of vehicles with the standards set forth in § 205.52 for the purposes of Selective Enforcement Auditing and Testing by the Administrator.
