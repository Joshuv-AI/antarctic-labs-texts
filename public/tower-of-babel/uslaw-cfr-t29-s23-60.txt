29 C.F.R. § 23.60 — Antiretaliation.
Citation: us:cfr:29:23.60
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-23.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 23 (Increasing The Minimum Wage For Federal Contractors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

It shall be unlawful for any person to discharge or in any other manner discriminate against any worker because such worker has filed any complaint or instituted or caused to be instituted any proceeding under or related to Executive Order 14026 or this part, or has testified or is about to testify in any such proceeding.
