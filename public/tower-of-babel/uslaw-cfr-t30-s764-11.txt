30 C.F.R. § 764.11 — General process requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:30:764.11
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-764.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 764 (State Processes For Designating Areas Unsuitable For Surface Coal Mining Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each State shall establish a process enabling objective decisions to be made on which, if any, land areas of the State are unsuitable for all or certain types of surface coal mining operations. These decisions shall be based on competent, scientifically sound data and other relevant information. This process shall include the requirements listed in this part.
