46 C.F.R. § 182.405 — Fuel restrictions.
Citation: us:cfr:46:182.405
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-182.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 182 (Machinery Installation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The use of alternative fuels, other than diesel fuel or gasoline, as fuel for an internal combustion engine will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis by the Commandant.
