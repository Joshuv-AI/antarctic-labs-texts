50 U.S.C. § 2354 — Termination
Citation: us:usc:50:2354
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section2354
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 40 (Defense Against Weapons Of Mass Destruction); Subchapter IV (Coordination Of Policy And Countermeasures Against Proliferation Of Weapons Of Mass Destruction). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

After September 30, 1999, the President—
(1)
is not required to maintain a National Coordinator for Nonproliferation Matters under section 2351 of this title; and
(2)
may terminate the Committee on Nonproliferation established under section 2352 of this title.
