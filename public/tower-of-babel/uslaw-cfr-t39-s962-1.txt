39 C.F.R. § 962.1 — (Rule 1) Purpose.

Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-962.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 962 (Administrative False Claims Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes the procedures governing the hearing and appeal rights of any person alleged to be liable for civil penalties and assessments under the Administrative False Claims Act of 2021 (codified at 31 U.S.C. 3801-12).
