42 U.S.C. § 3057h — Eligibility
Citation: us:usc:42:3057h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3057h
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter X (Grants For Native Americans); Part B (Native Hawaiian Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A public or nonprofit private organization having the capacity to provide services under this part for Native Hawaiians is eligible for assistance under this part only if—
(1)
the organization will serve at least 50 individuals who have attained 60 years of age or older; and
(2)
the organization demonstrates the ability to deliver supportive services, including nutrition services.
