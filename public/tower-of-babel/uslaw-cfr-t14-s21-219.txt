14 C.F.R. § 21.219 — Transferability.
Citation: us:cfr:14:21.219
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-21.219
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 21 (Certification Procedures For Products And Articles). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Class I provisional airworthiness certificates are not transferable. Class II provisional airworthiness certificates may be transferred to an air carrier eligible to apply for a certificate under § 21.213(b).
