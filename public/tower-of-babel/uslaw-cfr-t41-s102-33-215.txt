41 C.F.R. § 102-33.215 — -33.215 Replacing aircraft through exchange/sale.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.215
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Refer to the procedures in 41 CFR part 102-39 for replacing aircraft via exchange/sale. Exchange/sell an aircraft to another executive agency or negotiate a fixed-price sale to a SASP.
