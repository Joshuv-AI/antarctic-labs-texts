25 U.S.C. § 389c — Cancellation of charges in absence of lien or contract for payment
Citation: us:usc:25:389c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title25-section389c
Context: United States Code, Title 25 (Indians); Chapter 11 (Irrigation Of Allotted Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Where irrigation assessments against any such lands remained unpaid at the time the Indian title to such lands became extinguished and no lien existed and attached to such lands for the payment of charges so assessed and no contract for the payment of such charges was entered into, the Secretary shall cancel all such charges.
