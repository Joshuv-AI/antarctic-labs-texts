40 C.F.R. § 60.5379c — c What are my requirements for meeting increments of progress and achieving final compliance?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.5379c
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.5379c
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must meet one increment of progress as specified in paragraph (a) of this section and you must submit the Notification of Compliance report as specified in paragraph (b) of this section.
(a) Submit a final compliance control plan on or before 28 months after the state plan submittal deadline specified in § 60.5362c(c).
(b) Submit a Notification of Compliance report on or before 60 days after the state plan compliance date as specified in § 60.5420c.
