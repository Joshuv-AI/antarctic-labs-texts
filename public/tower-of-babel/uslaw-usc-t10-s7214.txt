10 U.S.C. § 7214 — Corps of Engineers: detail of officers to assist Mayor of District of Columbia
Citation: us:usc:10:7214
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7214
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 723 (Special Appointments, Assignments, Details, And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The President may detail not more than three officers assigned to the Corps of Engineers to assist the Mayor of the District of Columbia in discharging his duties.
