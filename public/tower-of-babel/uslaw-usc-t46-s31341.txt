46 U.S.C. § 31341 — Persons presumed to have authority to procure necessaries
Citation: us:usc:46:31341
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section31341
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Chapter 313 (Commercial Instruments And Maritime Liens); Subchapter III (Maritime Liens). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The following persons are presumed to have authority to procure necessaries for a vessel:
(1)
the owner;
(2)
the master;
(3)
a person entrusted with the management of the vessel at the port of supply; or
(4)
an officer or agent appointed by—
(A)
the owner;
(B)
a charterer;
(C)
an owner pro hac vice; or
(D)
an agreed buyer in possession of the vessel.
(b)
A person tortiously or unlawfully in possession or charge of a vessel has no authority to procure necessaries for the vessel.
