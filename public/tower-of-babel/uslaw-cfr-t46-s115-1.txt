46 C.F.R. § 115.1 — Preemptive effect.
Citation: us:cfr:46:115.1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-115.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 115 (Inspection And Certification). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part have preemptive effect over State or local regulations in the same field.
