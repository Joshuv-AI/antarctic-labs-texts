48 C.F.R. § 627.303 — 627.303 Contract clauses.
Citation: us:cfr:48:627.303
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-627.303
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 627 (Patents, Data, And Copyrights). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Procurement Executive is the agency head's designee to make the determinations addressed in FAR 27.303. Determinations proposed to be issued by the Procurement Executive shall be reviewed by the Office of the Legal Adviser.
