SUMMARY: The Caribbean Fishery Management Council (CFMC) will hold the 
190th public hybrid meeting to address the items contained in the 
tentative agenda included in the SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION.

DATES: The 190th CFMC public hybrid meeting will be held on August 11, 
2026, from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and on August 12, 2026, from 9 a.m. to 
5:15 p.m., AST. A closed meeting will be held on August 11, 2026, from 
5:15 p.m. to 5:45 p.m., AST. The meeting will be held at the Courtyard 
by Marriott Isla Verde Beach Resort, 7012 Boca de Cangrejos Avenue, 
Carolina, Puerto Rico 00979.
