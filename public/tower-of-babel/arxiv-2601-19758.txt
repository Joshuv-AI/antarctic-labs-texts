Pureness and stable rank one for reduced twisted group C ∗ \mathrm{C}^{\ast} -algebras of certain group extensions
Felipe Flores, Mario Klisse, Mícheál Ó Cobhthaigh, Matteo Pagliero

Abstract
. The purpose of this note is to prove two results. First, we observe that discrete groups with property P PHP \mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{PHP}} in the sense of Ozawa give rise to completely selfless reduced twisted group C ∗ \mathrm{C}^{\ast} -algebras, thereby extending a theorem of Ozawa from the untwisted to the twisted case. Second, we show that reduced (twisted) C ∗ \mathrm{C}^{\ast} -algebras of some group extensions of the form finite-by- G G , with G G having the property P PHP \mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{PHP}} , have stable rank one and are pure, which implies strict comparison. Our results do not assume rapid decay, and extend a theorem of Raum–Thiel–Vilalta. Examples covered by our results include reduced twisted group C ∗ \mathrm{C}^{\ast} -algebras of all acylindrically hyperbolic groups and all lattices in SL ⁡ ( n , ℝ ) {\rm SL}(n,\mathbb{R}) for n ≥ 2 n\geq 2 .



Introduction

The notion of
strict comparison of positive elements
, or
strict comparison
for short, was introduced by Blackadar in
[
6
]
as a
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-analogue of the property of II
1
-factors that Murray-von Neumann comparison of projections is determined by their trace. We refer the reader to
[
2
, Definition 3.3]
for the definition of strict comparison, and to the same article for a detailed discussion on this topic.
Suffice it to say that it is a regularity property for
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebras that plays a prominent role in both the Toms-Winter conjecture and Elliott’s classification program (see, for example,
[
17
,
30
,
27
]
), and has also found applications in continuous model theory
[
15
]
, dynamical systems
[
13
]
, and time-frequency analysis
[
4
]
.

Establishing strict comparison is usually a difficult problem, especially for non-nuclear C
∗
-algebras. This is well exemplified by the fact that, until recently, it was an open problem whether the reduced group
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra of the free group
\mathbb{F}_{n}
has strict comparison when
2\leq n<\infty
.
The situation drastically changed thanks to the breakthrough article of Amrutam, Gao, Kunnawalkam Elayavalli, and Patchell
[
1
]
, in which they proved that reduced group
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebras of finitely generated acylindrically hyperbolic groups with trivial finite radical and satisfying the rapid decay property are
selfless
in the sense of Robert when equipped with the canonical trace.

Definition

(see
[
26
, Definition 2.1]
and
[
21
, Section 6]
)
.

Let
(A,\rho)
be a
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra equipped with a GNS-faithful state. Then
(A,\rho)
is said to be
selfless

if
A\not\cong\mathbb{C}
and the first factor embedding into the reduced free product
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra
\iota\colon(A,\rho)\hookrightarrow(A,\rho)\star(A,\rho)
is
existential
, that is, there exists an ultrafilter
\mathcal{U}
and a state-preserving embedding
\theta\colon(A,\rho)\star(A,\rho)\hookrightarrow(A^{\mathcal{U}},\rho^{\mathcal{U}})
such that
\theta\circ\iota
agrees with the diagonal embedding.

Moreover,
(A,\rho)
is said to be
completely selfless
if for every
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra
C
,
\theta
induces a
\ast
-homomorphism

((A,\rho)\star(A,\rho))\otimes C\longrightarrow((A,\rho)\otimes C)^{\mathcal{U}}.

When equipped with a tracial state,
[
26
, Theorem 3.1]
shows that a selfless
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra is simple, monotracial with unique quasitracial state, has strict comparison, and stable rank one in the sense of Rieffel
[
25
]
, that is, the collection of all invertible elements is dense.

A
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra is
pure
, according to Winter’s original definition (see
[
29

), if it has strict comparison and it is almost divisible.
Almost divisibility, like strict comparison, is automatic for II
1
-factors. In a II
1
-factor, it is the property that for every projection
p
and
n\geq 2
there exists a projection
q
on which the unique trace
\tau
has value
\frac{1}{n}\tau(p)
.
We refer the reader to
[
29
,
2
,
22
]
for a comprehensive study of pure
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebras.
The key result relating pure and selfless
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebras is
[
2
, Theorem 5.13]
: Every simple, unital, non-elementary
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra with a unique normalised quasitrace and strict comparison is pure.
In particular, (completely) selfless tracial
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebras are pure.
As a result, the main theorem of
[
1
]
shows that many group
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebras, including
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}_{r}(\mathbb{F}_{n})
, are pure and have stable rank one.
This result has inspired new works on selfless
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebras arising from two types of constructions: (twisted) group algebras
[
28
,
14
,
21
,
16
,
23
]
, and graph products
[
12
,
11
,
8
]
, which include free products.
In this note, we are concerned with the first of these two classes.
In particular, we continue the line of work started by Raum–Thiel–Vilalta
[
23
]
, whose objective is to first transfer the regularity properties mentioned before from the untwisted case to the case of twisted algebras, and then to use these results to obtain regularity properties for
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebras of certain group extensions.

In
[
21
]
, Ozawa introduced property
\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{PHP}}
for countable groups and showed that it guarantees complete selflessness of the reduced group
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra. As our first result, we observe that this property also implies that every reduced twisted group
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra is completely selfless.

Theorem A
.

Let
G
be a countable discrete group with property
\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{PHP}}
and let
\omega\in Z^{2}(G,\mathbb{T})
be a 2-cocycle. Then the reduced twisted group
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}_{\rm r}(G,\omega)
is completely selfless.

In particular,
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}_{\rm r}(G,\omega)
is simple, pure, has stable rank one, and a unique trace that is also the unique quasitracial state.

The class of groups with property
\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{PHP}}
includes all groups with a topologically free extreme boundary action. For instance, acylindrically hyperbolic groups with trivial finite radical admit such an action by the main theorem of
[
31
]
.
Moreover, every lattice in
{\rm PSL}(n,\mathbb{R})
, for
n\geq 2
, satisfies property
\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{PHP}}
by
[
21
, Theorem 16]
.
We observe that, in particular, for groups with a topologically free extreme boundary, and with the
rapid decay
property, Theorem
A
can be obtained by combining
[
21
, Theorem 1]
and
[
23
, Theorem A]
. We stress that our result does
not
require assuming the rapid decay property.
We also mention here that one can proceed exactly as in the proof of
[
23
, Corollary 2.1]
, which uses structural results from
[
2
,
3
]
, and describe the Cuntz semigroup of the
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebras covered by Theorem
A
as follows:

Corollary B
.

Let
G
be a countable discrete group with property
\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{PHP}}
and let
\omega\in Z^{2}(G,\mathbb{T})
be a 2-cocycle.
Then the Cuntz semigroup of
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}_{r}(G,\omega)
is

\mathrm{Cu}(\mathrm{C}^{\ast}_{r}(G,\omega))\cong V(\mathrm{C}^{\ast}_{r}(G,\omega))\sqcup(0,\infty]\,,

where
V(\mathrm{C}^{\ast}_{r}(G,\omega))
denotes the Murray–von Neumann semigroup of
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}_{r}(G,\omega)
.

Next, we consider the (twisted) reduced group
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra of a group
\widetilde{G}
arising from a short exact sequence

1\longrightarrow K\longrightarrow\widetilde{G}\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow 1\,,

where
K
is finite. We refer to such an extension as being
finite-by-
G
.

Theorem C
.

Let
G
be a countable discrete group,
\widetilde{G}
a finite-by-
G
extension, and
\omega\in Z^{2}(\widetilde{G},\mathbb{T})
. Assume that every reduced twisted group
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra over a finite index subgroup of
G
is selfless with respect to the canonical trace. Then
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}_{\rm r}(\widetilde{G},\omega)
is pure and has stable rank one.

Among the group extensions covered by Theorem
C
, we highlight the following. Recall that a group is said to be acylindrically hyperbolic if it admits a non-elementary acylindrical action on a Gromov hyperbolic metric space
[
20
]
.

Corollary D
.

Let
G
be a countable discrete group, and
\omega\in Z^{2}(G,\mathbb{T})
. Assume that
G
is either

(i)

acylindrically hyperbolic, or

(ii)

a lattice in
{\rm SL}(n,\mathbb{R})
, for
n\geq 2
.

Then the reduced twisted group
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebra
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}_{\rm r}(G,\omega)
is pure and has stable rank one.

We observe that Corollary
D
, part (i), removes the rapid decay assumption from Raum–Thiel–Vilalta’s Theorem B
[
23
]
.
Moreover, note that for

,
{\rm SL}(n,\mathbb{Z})
does not have the rapid decay property (see
[
7

has stable rank one.

Definition 1.1

(see
[
21
, Section 8]
)
.

A discrete group
G
has property
\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{PHP}}
if, for every finite subset
F\subseteq G
and every
\varepsilon>0
, there exists an integer
n\in\mathbb{N}
, elements
t_{i}\in G
, and subsets
C_{i}\subseteq D_{i}\subseteq G
for
1\leq i\leq n
, satisfying that the members of the set

\{aC_{i}\mid a\in F,\,i=1,\ldots,n\}\cup\{bt_{j}^{-1}(G\setminus D_{j})\mid b\in F,\,j=1,\ldots,n\}

are mutually disjoint and that

\sup_{x\in G}\;\#\{i\mid x\in D_{i}\}\cup\{j\mid x\in t_{j}^{-1}(G\setminus C_{j})\}\leq\varepsilon n^{1/2}.

Definition 1.2
.

Let
G
be a countable discrete group and
G\curvearrowright X
an action on a compact topological space
X
. The action
G\curvearrowright X
is called an

if it is minimal and
extremely proximal

U,V\subseteq X

g\in G
such that
g(X\setminus U)\subseteq V
.

Proposition 1.3
.

Let
G
be a countable discrete group, and
G\curvearrowright X
a topologically free extreme boundary action. Suppose that
H\leq G
is a finite-index subgroup. Then
H\curvearrowright X
is a topologically free extreme boundary action.

Proof.

It is evident that
H\curvearrowright X
remains topologically free. Since extremely proximal actions are proximal

Recall that a
2

\displaystyle\omega(y,z)\omega(x,yz)

\displaystyle=\omega(x,y)\omega(xy,z),

\displaystyle\omega(x,{\sf e})

\displaystyle=\omega({\sf e},y)=1,

As usual, the set of all

Given a

\lambda^{\omega}_{g}\delta_{h}=\omega(g,h)\delta_{gh},\quad\quad\text{for all }g,h\in G.

When the cocycle is trivial, namely

The following lemma describes the structure of

contains a finite normal subgroup. It can be proven by decomposing the algebra as a twisted crossed product (see

Lemma 1.4
.

Let
G
be a countable discrete group,
\widetilde{G}
a finite-by-
G
extension with sequence

1\longrightarrow K\longrightarrow\widetilde{G}\longrightarrow G\longrightarrow 1,

and
\omega\in Z^{2}(\widetilde{G},\mathbb{T})
. Then,
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}_{\rm r}(\widetilde{G},\omega)
is isomorphic to a finite direct sum of

-algebras of the form

\mathrm{C}^{\ast}_{\rm r}(H,\nu)\otimes\mathbb{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})\otimes\mathbb{M}_{|G/H|}(\mathbb{C}),

where

,

, and
\nu\in Z^{2}(H,\mathbb{T})
only depends on the direct summand.

Proof of Theorem
C
.

By invoking Lemma
1.4
, we can write
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}_{\rm r}(\widetilde{G},\omega)
as a direct sum of
\mathrm{C}^{\ast}
-algebras of the form

\mathrm{C}^{\ast}_{\rm r}(H,\nu)\otimes\mathbb{M}_{n}(\mathbb{C})\otimes\mathbb{M}_{|G/H|}(\mathbb{C}),

where

is a finite-index subgroup of

Proof of Corollary

Assume that

Then

For the second case, we appeal to
