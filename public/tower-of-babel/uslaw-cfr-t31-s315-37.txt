31 C.F.R. § 315.37 — Payment during lives of both coowners.
Citation: us:cfr:31:315.37
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-315.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 315 (Regulations Governing U.S. Savings Bonds, Series A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, J, And K, And U.S. Savings Notes). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A savings bond registered in coownership form will be paid to either coowner upon surrender with an appropriate request, and, upon payment (as determined in § 315.43), the other coowner will cease to have any interest in the bond. If both coowners request payment and payment is to be made by check, the check will be drawn in the form, “John A. Jones and Mary C. Jones”.
