49 U.S.C. § 80102 — Application
Citation: us:usc:49:80102
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section80102
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 801 (Bills Of Lading). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter applies to a bill of lading when the bill is issued by a common carrier for the transportation of goods—
(1)
between a place in the District of Columbia and another place in the District of Columbia;
(2)
between a place in a territory or possession of the United States and another place in the same territory or possession;
(3)
between a place in a State and a place in another State;
(4)
between a place in a State and a place in the same State through another State or a foreign country; or
(5)
from a place in a State to a place in a foreign country.
