40 C.F.R. § 60.2987 — What compliance schedule must I include in my state plan?
Citation: us:cfr:40:60.2987
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-60.2987
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 60 (). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your state plan must include compliance schedules that require existing incineration units as defined in § 60.2992 to achieve final compliance as expeditiously as practicable after approval of the state plan but not later than the earlier of the following dates:
(a) July 1, 2030.
(b) Three years after the effective date of state plan approval.
