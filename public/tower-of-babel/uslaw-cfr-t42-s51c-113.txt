42 C.F.R. § 51c.113 — c.113 Applicability of 2 CFR parts 200 and 300.
Citation: us:cfr:42:51c.113
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-51c.113
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 51c (PART 51c—GRANTS FOR COMMUNITY HEALTH SERVICES). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of 2 CFR parts 200 and 300, establishing uniform administrative requirements and cost principles, shall apply to all grants under this part.
