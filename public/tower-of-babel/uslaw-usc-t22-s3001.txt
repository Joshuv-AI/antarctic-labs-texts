22 U.S.C. § 3001 — Commission on Security and Cooperation in Europe; establishment
Citation: us:usc:22:3001
Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 45 (Commission On Security And Cooperation In Europe). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There is established the Commission on Security and Cooperation in Europe (hereafter in this chapter referred to as the “Commission”).
