14 C.F.R. § 294.87 — Compliance with Canadian licenses.
Citation: us:cfr:14:294.87
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-294.87
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 294 (Canadian Charter Air Taxi Operators). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A registrant shall not, in the performance of operations authorized by this part, use any aircraft or conduct any operations except in accordance with the authority and conditions contained in the registrant's applicable Canadian licenses.
