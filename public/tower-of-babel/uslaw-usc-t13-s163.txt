13 U.S.C. § 163 — Authority of other agencies
Citation: us:usc:13:163
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title13-section163
Context: United States Code, Title 13 (Census); Chapter 5 (Censuses); Subchapter III (Governments). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This subchapter does not revoke or impair the authority of any other Federal agency with respect to the collection or release of information.
