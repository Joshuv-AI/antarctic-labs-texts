18 C.F.R. § 154.400 — Additional requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:18:154.400
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-154.400
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 154 (Rate Schedules And Tariffs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to the requirements of subparts A, B, and C of this part, any proposal to implement a limited rate change must comply with this subpart.
