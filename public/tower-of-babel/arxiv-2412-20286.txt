Revisiting Hugo Volger’s paper Über die Existenz der freien Algebren
Matías Menni and Walter Tholen

Abstract
We give a modern account of Volger’s 1967 paper which, motivated by the construction of free algebras for a Lawvere-Linton theory, gives a very constructive proof that the left Kan extension of a product-preserving 𝖲𝖾𝗍 \mathsf{Set} -valued functor is product-preserving. We also analyze how it anticipates, and in part even exceeds, subsequent work of the 1970s.



Keywords:
Algebraic theory, free algebra, Kan extension, final functor, weakly cofiltered category.

Mathematics Classification:
18C10, 08B20, 18A40, 18D20.

Comparison with the Borceux-Day paper

In the context of categories enriched in a symmentric monoidal-closed category
\mathcal{V}
, with their Theorem 1.5 Borceux and Day
[
5
]
express the finite-product preservation by left Kan extensions equivalently as the compatibility of coends with finite products. More precisely, they show that, given
\mathcal{V}
-categories
\mathcal{B}
and
\mathcal{D}
with finite
\mathcal{V}
-products,
\mathcal{B}
small and
\mathcal{D}
with small
\mathcal{V}
-colimits, the following conditions are equivalent:

(i)

For all
\mathcal{V}
-functors
F:\mathcal{B}\to\mathcal{C}
and
X:\mathcal{B}\to\mathcal{D}
, if
X
preserves finite
\mathcal{V}
-products, so does
{\mathrm{Lan}}_{F}X:\mathcal{C}\to\mathcal{D}
.

(ii)

For all
\mathcal{V}
-functors
H_{1},H_{2}:\mathcal{B}^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathcal{V}
and
X:\mathcal{B}\to\mathcal{D}
, if
X
preserves finite
\mathcal{V}
-products, then the canonical morphism

\int^{(b_{1},b_{2})}(H_{1}b_{1}\times H_{2}b_{2})\otimes X(b_{1}\times b_{2})\longrightarrow\left(\int^{b_{1}}H_{1}b_{1}\otimes Xb_{1}\right)\times\left(\int^{b_{2}}H_{2}b_{2}\otimes Xb_{2}\right)

is an isomorphism.

In their Example 3.1, Borceux and Day
[
5
]
show that, for
\mathcal{V}

cartesian
closed,
\mathcal{D}:=\mathcal{V}
satisfies these equivalent conditions. In fact, their short supporting calculation entails a Fubini-type analogue of the proof of Lemma
4.3
:

\displaystyle\int^{(b_{1},b_{2})}(H_{1}b_{1}\times H_{2}b_{2})\otimes X(b_{1}\times b_{2})

\displaystyle\cong\int^{b_{1}}\!\!\!\!\int^{b_{2}}(H_{1}b_{1}\times H_{2}b_{2})\otimes(Xb_{1}\times Xb_{2})

\displaystyle\cong\int^{b_{1}}H_{1}b_{1}\otimes\int^{b_{2}}H_{2}b_{2}\otimes(Xb_{1}\times Xb_{2})

\displaystyle\cong\int^{b_{1}}H_{1}b_{1}\otimes\left(Xb_{1}\times\int^{b_{2}}H_{2}b_{2}\otimes Xb_{2}\right)

\displaystyle\cong\left(\int^{b_{1}}H_{1}b_{1}\otimes Xb_{1}\right)\times\left(\int^{b_{2}}H_{2}b_{2}\otimes Xb_{2}\right)\,.

In particular, Theorem
4.4
follows.

For
\mathcal{V}
just monoidal closed (not necessarily cartesian closed) with finite products and small co-

limits, Borceux and Day
[
6
]
take the satisfaction of property (ii) as part of the defining conditions for
\mathcal{V}
to be a
\pi
-category
and then formulate property (i) with
\mathcal{D}=\mathcal{V}
as their Proposition 2.2.1. This is the starting point for their study of Lawvere-style of
\mathcal{V}
-enriched algebraic theories, categories and functors, which includes the existence proof of free algebras and, in fact, of left adjoints to algebraic functors in the enriched setting.
