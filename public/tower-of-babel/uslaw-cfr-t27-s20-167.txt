27 C.F.R. § 20.167 — Recovered and restored denatured spirits tanks.
Citation: us:cfr:27:20.167
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-20.167
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 20 (Distribution And Use Of Denatured Alcohol And Rum). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Suitable storage tanks shall be provided for recovered and restored denatured spirits. Each storage tank for recovered and restored denatured spirits shall be—
(a) Durably marked to show its capacity and use,
(b) Equipped for locking to control access to the contents, and
(c) Provided with an accurate means of measuring its contents.
