30 C.F.R. § 769.19 — Regulatory policy.
Citation: us:cfr:30:769.19
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-769.19
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 769 (Petition Process For Designation Of Federal Lands As Unsuitable For All Or Certain Types Of Surface Coal Mining Operations And For Termination Of Previous Designations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Once an area of Federal lands is designated as unsuitable for all or certain types of surface coal mining operations, any permit or lease shall be conditioned in a manner so as to limit or prohibit surface coal mining operations on the designated areas in accordance with the designation.
