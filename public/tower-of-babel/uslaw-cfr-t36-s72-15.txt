36 C.F.R. § 72.15 — Preliminary Action Program.
Citation: us:cfr:36:72.15
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-72.15
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 72 (Urban Park And Recreation Recovery Act Of 1978). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

During an initial interim period, the Action Program requirements, as described in §§ 72.11, 72.12 and 72.13 may be satisfied by local governments' submission of a Preliminary Action Program. The initial interim period shall end on January 1, 1981. Communities are required to submit four (4) copies of the Preliminary Action Program.
