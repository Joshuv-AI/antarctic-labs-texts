16 U.S.C. § 404c–2 — Fugitives from justice
Citation: us:usc:16:404c–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section404c–2
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter XLVII (Mammoth Cave National Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

All fugitives from justice taking refuge in the park shall be subject to the same laws as fugitives from justice found in the Commonwealth of Kentucky.
