Privacy Act of 1974; Implementation

AGENCY: Bureau of Justice Statistics, Office of Justice Programs, 
United States Department of Justice.

ACTION: Notice of proposed rulemaking.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In the Notice section of today's Federal Register, the Office 
of Justice Programs (OJP), a component within the United States 
Department of Justice (DOJ or Department), has published a notice of a 
new system of records, Bureau of Justice Statistics (BJS) Statistical 
Data, JUSTICE/OJP-017, for the statistical data collections that BJS 
conducts under 34 U.S.C. 10132. In this notice of proposed rulemaking, 
the OJP proposes to exempt this system of records from certain 
provisions of the Privacy Act in order to comply with the 
confidentiality provisions of 34 U.S.C. 10231 and 34 U.S.C. 30303 and 
the requirement of 34 U.S.C. 10134 that the data are to be used solely 
for statistical or research purposes. Additionally, data are to be used 
for authorized purposes under the Prison Rape Elimination Act of 2003 
(PREA; 44 U.S.C. 3563) and the

[[Page 65511]]

Death in Custody Reporting Act of 2013 (DCRA; Pub. L. 113-242). 
Consistent with the data use requirements set forth in 34 U.S.C. 10134, 
no adverse determinations are made from these records as to any 
identifiable individual. For the reasons provided below, the Department 
proposes to amend its Privacy Act regulations by establishing an 
exemption for records in this system from certain provisions of the 
Privacy Act. Public comment is invited.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: BJS is a Federal statistical agency and is a 
principal source of official statistics on crime and justice. BJS's 
mission is to collect, analyze, and disseminate information on crime, 
criminal offenders, victims of crime, and the operation of justice 
systems at all levels of government. ``BJS Statistical Data'' is a new 
system of records that includes information collected under BJS's 
authority from sources including survey respondents and from 
administrative records that are routinely maintained by other entities 
including Federal, state, local, and tribal criminal justice or 
governmental agencies and offices. The purpose of this system of 
records is to maintain data collected under BJS's authority through 
questionnaires, paper and electronic surveys, interviews, focus groups, 
and administrative records to support its statistical mission. BJS 
regularly obtains records with names, dates of birth, addresses, social 
security numbers, and other identification numbers, to complete its 
statistical work.

PART 16--PRODUCTION OR DISCLOSURE OF MATERIAL OR INFORMATION

0
1. The authority citation for part 16 continues to read as follows:

    Authority:  5 U.S.C. 301, 552, 552a, 553; 28 U.S.C. 509, 510, 
534; 31 U.S.C. 3717.

Subpart E--Exemption of Records Systems Under the Privacy Act

0
1. Amend Sec.  16.100 by adding paragraphs (c) and (d) to read as 
follows:
    (c) The following system of records is exempted from subsections 5 
U.S.C. 552a(c)(3); (d); (e)(1), (e)(4)(G), and (H); and (f) of the 
Privacy Act:
    (1) BJS Statistical Data, (JUSTICE/OJP--017).
    These exemptions apply only to the extent that records in this 
system are subject to exemption pursuant to 5 U.S.C. 552a (k)(4).
    (d) The reasons for asserting these exemptions are to comply with 
the confidentiality provisions of 34 U.S.C. 10231 and 34 U.S.C. 30303 
and the requirement of 34 U.S.C. 10134 that the data is to be used 
solely for statistical purposes and for authorized purposes under the 
PREA (44 U.S.C. 3563) and DCRA (Pub. L. 113-242), and to avoid unlawful 
consideration of these records whose sole statistical use comports 
fully with a basic purpose of the Privacy Act, namely, that no adverse 
disclosures are made from these records as to any identifiable 
individual.
