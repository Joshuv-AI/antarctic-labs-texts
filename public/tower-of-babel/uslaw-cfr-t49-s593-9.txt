49 C.F.R. § 593.9 — Effect of affirmative determinations; lists.
Citation: us:cfr:49:593.9
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-593.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 593 (Determinations That A Vehicle Not Originally Manufactured To Conform To The Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards Is Eligible For Importation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A notice of grant is sufficient authority for the importation by persons other than the petitioner of any vehicle of the same model specified in the grant.
(b) The Administrator publishes annually in the Federal Register a list of determinations made under Sec. 593.7, and Sec. 593.8.
