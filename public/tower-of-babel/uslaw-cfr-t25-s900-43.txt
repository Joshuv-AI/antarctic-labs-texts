25 C.F.R. § 900.43 — What are the general financial management system standards that apply to a tribal organization carrying out a self-determination contract?
Citation: us:cfr:25:900.43
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior, And Indian Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-900.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 900 (Contracts Under The Indian Self-Determination And Education Assistance Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A tribal organization shall expend and account for contract funds in accordance with the procedures of the tribal organization.
