28 U.S.C. § 550 — Clerical assistants, messengers, and private process servers
Citation: us:usc:28:550
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section550
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part II (Department Of Justice); Chapter 35 (United States Attorneys). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States attorneys may employ clerical assistants, messengers, and private process servers on approval of the Attorney General.
