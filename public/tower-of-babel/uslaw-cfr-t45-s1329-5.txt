45 C.F.R. § 1329.5 — Indicators of minimum compliance.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1329.5
Creator: Administration For Children And Families, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1329.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1329 (State Independent Living Services And Centers For Independent Living). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be eligible to receive funds under this part, a Center must comply with the standards in section 725(b) and assurances in section 725(c) of the Act, with the indicators of minimum compliance, and the requirements contained in the terms and conditions of the grant award.
