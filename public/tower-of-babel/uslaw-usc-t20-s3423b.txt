20 U.S.C. § 3423b — Office of Non-Public Education
Citation: us:usc:20:3423b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section3423b
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 48 (Department Of Education); Subchapter II (Establishment Of The Department). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be in the Department an Office of Non-Public Education to ensure the maximum potential participation of non-public school students in all Federal educational programs for which such students are eligible.
