16 U.S.C. § 670m — Indian rights; State or Federal jurisdiction regulating Indian rights
Citation: us:usc:16:670m
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section670m
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 5C (Conservation Programs On Government Lands); Subchapter II (Conservation Programs On Public Lands). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this subchapter shall enlarge or diminish or in any way affect (1) the rights of Indians or Indian tribes to the use of water or natural resources or their rights to fish, trap, or hunt wildlife as secured by statute, agreement, treaty, Executive order, or court decree; or (2) existing State or Federal jurisdiction to regulate those rights either on or off reservations.
