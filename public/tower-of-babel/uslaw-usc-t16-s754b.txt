16 U.S.C. § 754b — Funds from private entities credited to Resource Management account
Citation: us:usc:16:754b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section754b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 9 (Fish And Wildlife Service). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, in fiscal year 1999 and thereafter, sums provided by private entities for activities pursuant to reimbursable agreements shall be credited to the “Resource Management” account and shall remain available until expended.
