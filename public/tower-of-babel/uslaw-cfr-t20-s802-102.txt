20 C.F.R. § 802.102 — Applicability of part 801 of this chapter.
Citation: us:cfr:20:802.102
Creator: Benefits Review Board, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-802.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 802 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 801 of this chapter VII sets forth rules of general applicability covering the composition, authority, and operation of the Benefits Review Board and definitions applicable to this chapter. The provisions of part 801 of this chapter are fully applicable to this part 802.
