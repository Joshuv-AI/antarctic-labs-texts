38 U.S.C. § 8528 — Investment of the Fund
Citation: us:usc:38:8528
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section8528
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part VI (Acquisition And Disposition Of Property); Chapter 85 (Disposition Of Deceased Veterans’ Personal Property); Subchapter II (Death While Patient Of Department Facility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Money in the Fund not required for current disbursements may be invested and reinvested by the Secretary of the Treasury in interest-bearing obligations of the United States or in obligations guaranteed as to both principal and interest by the United States.
