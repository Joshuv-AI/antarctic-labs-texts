46 C.F.R. § 171.120 — Specific applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:46:171.120
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-171.120
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 171 (Special Rules Pertaining To Vessels Carrying Passengers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each vessel that is 100 gross tons or more must comply with § 171.122 and each vessel under 100 gross tons must comply with § 171.124.
