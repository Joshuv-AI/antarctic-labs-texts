36 U.S.C. § 170103 — Membership
Citation: us:usc:36:170103
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section170103
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 1701 (Paralyzed Veterans Of America). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

An individual is eligible for membership in the corporation if the individual—
(1)
is a citizen of the United States;
(2)
was regularly enlisted, inducted, or commissioned, and was accepted for or on active duty, in the Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force, or Coast Guard of the United States or an ally of the United States;
(3)
(A)
was separated from service in the Armed Forces under conditions other than dishonorable; or
(B)
is on active duty or must continue to serve after the cessation of hostilities; and
(4)
has suffered a spinal cord injury or disease, whether or not service connected in origin.
