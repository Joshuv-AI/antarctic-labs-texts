49 C.F.R. § 850.3 — Relationship to Coast Guard marine investigation regulations and procedures.
Citation: us:cfr:49:850.3
Creator: National Transportation Safety Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-850.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 850 (Coast Guard—National Transportation Safety Board Marine Casualty Investigations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Coast Guard's responsibility to investigate marine casualties is not eliminated nor diminished by the regulations in this part.
(b) In those instances where the Board conducts an investigation in which the Coast Guard also has responsibility under R.S. 4450 (46 U.S.C. 239), the proceedings are conducted independently, but so as to avoid duplication as much as possible.
