22 C.F.R. § 1506.34 — Can I pay a compromised claim in installments?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1506.34
Creator: African Development Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1506.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1506 (Collection Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The debtor may not pay a compromised claim in installments unless the CFO determines that payment in installments is necessary to effect collection.
