36 C.F.R. § 1211.200 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1211.200
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1211.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1211 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Sex In Education Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in §§ 1211.205 through 1211.235(a), these Title IX regulations apply to every recipient and to each education program or activity operated by such recipient that receives Federal financial assistance.
