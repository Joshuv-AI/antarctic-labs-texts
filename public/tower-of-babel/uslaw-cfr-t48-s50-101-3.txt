48 C.F.R. § 50.101-3 — 50.101-3 Records.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 50 (Extraordinary Contractual Actions And The Safety Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Agencies shall maintain complete records of all actions taken under this Subpart 50.1. For each request for relief processed, these records shall include, as a minimum—
(a) The contractor's request;
(b) All relevant memorandums, correspondence, affidavits, and other pertinent documents;
(c) The Memorandum of Decision (see 50.103-6 and 50.104-2); and
(d) A copy of the contractual document implementing an approved request.
