16 C.F.R. § 0.14 — Office of Administrative Law Judges.
Citation: us:cfr:16:0.14
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-0.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 0 (Organization). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Administrative law judges are officials to whom the Commission, in accordance with law, delegates the initial performance of statutory fact-finding functions and initial rulings on conclusions of law, to be exercised in conformity with Commission decisions and policy directives and with its Rules of Practice.
