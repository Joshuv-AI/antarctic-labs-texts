Abstract
. The main result of this paper is the characterization of zero-level integrable finite weight modules, over twisted affine Lie superalgebras. We prove that such a module is parabolically induced from a module which is obtained, in a prescribed way, from a module over a Lie algebra ℒ \mathscr{L} which is either a ℤ {\mathbb{Z}} -graded abelian Lie algebra or a direct sum of a ℤ {\mathbb{Z}} -graded abelian Lie algebra and the so-called quadratic Lie superalgebra 𝒬 \mathcal{Q} . We give also a complete characterization of both finite dimensional simple 𝒬 \mathcal{Q} -modules as well as bounded finite weight ℤ {\mathbb{Z}} -graded simple 𝒬 \mathcal{Q} -modules.

An affine Lie superalgebra, which is the super version of an affine Lie algebra, has a root space decomposition with respect to a finite dimensional Cartan subalgebra
{\mathcal{H}}
. An affine Lie superalgebra with zero odd part is just an affine Lie algebra. According to the classification, an affine Lie superalgebra with nonzero odd part is either of type
Y^{(1)}
where
Y
is the type of basic classical simple Lie superalgebra with nonzero odd part or of one of the types
D(m+1,n)^{(2)},A(2m,2n)^{(4)},A(2m-1,2n)^{(2)}
or
A(2m-1,2n-1)^{(2)};
see
[
15
]
. The
corresponding root system of an affine Lie superalgebra
\mathcal{L}
is decomposed into three parts: real roots (those roots which are not self-orthogonal), imaginary roots (those roots which are orthogonal to all roots) and non-singular roots (those roots which are self-orthogonal but not orthogonal to the entire root system); see
[
8
]
and
[
19
]
for the details.
An
\mathcal{L}
-module is called a weight module if it has a weight space decomposition
\mathcal{L}=\raisebox{0.43057pt}{\scalebox{.7}{$\displaystyle\bigoplus_{\alpha\in{\mathcal{H}}^{*}}\;$}}\mathcal{L}^{\alpha}
with respect to
{\mathcal{H}}
and called a finite weight module if all weight spaces are finite dimensional.
The affine Lie superalgebra
\mathcal{L}
has a canonical central element
c\in{\mathcal{H}}
acting on a simple weight module as a scalar called the level of a module.

There exist two determinative issues in the characterization of simple finite weight modules; the first one is that if the level is zero or not and the second one is the behavior of the actions of real root vectors. One knows that real root vectors act on a simple finite weight module either injectively or locally nilpotently; this in turn gives different classes of
\mathcal{L}
-modules. One of the important classes is the class of finite weight modules on which real root vectors act locally nilpotently; such modules are called integrable modules. For almost all affine Lie superlagbras with nonzero odd part, there is no simple integrable finite weight modules of nonzero-level; see
[
13
]
,
[
17
]
. The characterization of simple integrable finite weight modules of level zero over untwisted affine Lie superalgebras has been studied in
[
14
]
and
[
16
]
. In this work we characterize simple integrable finite weight modules of level zero over twisted affine Lie superalgebras.

The set of imaginary roots of an affine Lie superalgebra
\mathcal{L}
is a free abelian group of rank one; say, e.g.,
{\mathbb{Z}}\delta.
We show that the characterization of zero-level simple integrable finite weight modules are reduced to the characterization of zero-level simple
{\mathcal{H}}
-weight modules over
\raisebox{0.43057pt}{\scalebox{.7}{$\displaystyle\bigoplus_{k\in{\mathbb{Z}}}\;$}}\mathcal{L}^{k\delta}.
As the level is zero, we actually deal with simple
{\mathcal{H}}/{\mathbb{C}}c
-weight modules over
\mathfrak{L}:=\raisebox{0.43057pt}{\scalebox{.7}{$\displaystyle\bigoplus_{k\in{\mathbb{Z}}}\;$}}\mathcal{L}^{k\delta}/{\mathbb{C}}c.
The algebra
\mathfrak{L}
is a semi-direct product of a Lie superalgebra
\mathscr{L}
with
{\mathcal{H}}/{\mathbb{C}}c
. To get the characterization of simple integrable finite weight modules, we need to characterize
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded simple
\mathscr{L}
-modules. In the non-super cases and all super cases other than type
A(2m,2n)^{(4)},

\mathscr{L}
is a
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded abelian Lie algebra whose
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded simple
\mathscr{L}
-modules are known in the literature, but in the type
A(2m,2n)^{(4)},

\mathscr{L}
is a direct sum of a
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded abelian Lie algebra and a new
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded Lie superalgebra which is not abelian; we call this new Lie superalgebra the quadratic Lie superalgebra. It is

\mathcal{Q}=\underbrace{s^{2}{\mathbb{C}}[s^{\pm 4}]\oplus t^{2}{\mathbb{C}}[t^{\pm 4}]}_{\mathcal{Q}_{0}}\oplus\underbrace{t{\mathbb{C}}[t^{\pm 4}]\oplus t^{-1}{\mathbb{C}}[t^{\pm 4}]}_{\mathcal{Q}_{1}}

with the bracket

\begin{array}[]{lll}~[\mathcal{Q}_{0},\mathcal{Q}_{0}]=[t^{2}{\mathbb{C}}[t^{\pm 4}],\mathcal{Q}_{1}]:=\{0\},&~[t^{4k+1},t^{4k^{\prime}-1}]:=0,&~[t^{4k+1},t^{4k^{\prime}+1}]:=t^{4(k+k^{\prime})+2},\\
~[t^{4k-1},t^{4k^{\prime}-1}]:=-t^{4(k+k^{\prime})-2},&~[s^{4k-2},t^{4k^{\prime}+1}]:=t^{4(k+k^{\prime})-1},&~[s^{4k+2},t^{4k^{\prime}-1}]:=t^{4(k+k^{\prime})+1}.\end{array}

To obtain our desired characterization, we need to characterize
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded simple
\mathcal{Q}
-modules with finite dimensional homogenous spaces. We show that these modules are kind of loop modules based on finite dimensional simple
\mathcal{Q}
-modules. Moreover, we show that a finite dimensional simple
\mathcal{Q}
-module is in fact a finite dimensional simple module over a Clliford superalgebra with some compatibility property.

Here is the structure of the paper: Following this introduction, the paper has three additional sections. In Section 2, we prepare the necessary preliminaries for the main theorem, including characterizations of certain modules over graded commutative associative algebras and Clifford superalgebras; we address these topics in dedicated subsections. In Section 3, we characterize
{\mathbb{Z}}
-graded simple
\mathcal{Q}
-modules with finite dimensional homogenous spaces. The final section is devoted entirely to the proof of the main theorem.

.

Proof.

\displaystyle W:=\{v\in V\mid\mathcal{L}^{\dot{\alpha}+sp_{*}\delta}v=\{0\}\hskip 9.24994pt(\dot{\alpha}\in\dot{S},s\in{\mathbb{Z}},\boldsymbol{f}(\dot{\alpha})>0)\}.

, the cardinality
\mathcal{A}_{v_{0}},

Step 1.

If
\mathcal{A}_{v_{0}}\neq\emptyset,

{{\beta_{*}}}\in\mathcal{A}_{v_{0}}
with
\boldsymbol{f}({{\beta_{*}}})={\text{\rm{max}}}\{\boldsymbol{f}(\beta)\mid\beta\in\mathcal{A}_{v_{0}}\},

0\neq v\in\mathcal{L}^{{{\beta_{*}}}+sp_{*}\delta}v_{0}

and

To show that

suppose that
r\in{\mathbb{Z}}
and
\dot{\alpha}\in\dot{S}
with

\mathcal{L}^{\dot{\alpha}+rp_{*}\delta}v\subseteq\mathcal{L}^{\dot{\alpha}+rp_{*}\delta}\mathcal{L}^{{{\beta_{*}}}+sp_{*}\delta}v_{0}\subseteq\mathcal{L}^{{{\beta_{*}}}+sp_{*}\delta}\mathcal{L}^{\dot{\alpha}+rp_{*}\delta}v_{0}+\mathcal{L}^{{{\beta_{*}}}+\dot{\alpha}+(s+r)p_{*}\delta}v_{0}\subseteq\mathcal{L}^{{{\beta_{*}}}+\dot{\alpha}+(s+r)p_{*}\delta}v_{0}.
