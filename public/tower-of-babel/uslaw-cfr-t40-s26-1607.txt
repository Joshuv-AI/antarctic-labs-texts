40 C.F.R. § 26.1607 — Human Studies Review Board review of completed human research.
Citation: us:cfr:40:26.1607
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-26.1607
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 26 (Protection Of Human Subjects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In commenting on reports of completed research submitted to it by EPA, the Human Studies Review Board must consider the scientific merits and ethical aspects of the completed research, and must apply the appropriate standards in subpart Q of this part.
