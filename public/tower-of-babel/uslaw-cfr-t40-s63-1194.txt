40 C.F.R. § 63.1194 — Which general provisions apply?
Citation: us:cfr:40:63.1194
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-63.1194
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 63 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants For Source Categories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The general provisions in subpart A of this part define requirements applicable to all owners and operators affected by NESHAP in part 63. See Table 1 of this subpart for general provisions that apply (or don't apply) to you as an owner or operator subject to the requirements of this subpart.
