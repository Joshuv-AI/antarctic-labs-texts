7 C.F.R. § 932.37 — Compensation and expenses.
Citation: us:cfr:7:932.37
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-932.37
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 932 (Olives Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The members of the committee and alternates when acting as members or at the request of the committee or its chairperson shall serve without compensation, but shall be reimbursed for necessary expenses, as approved by the committee, incurred by them in the performance of their duties under this part.
