49 U.S.C. § 44739 — Pets on airplanes
Citation: us:usc:49:44739
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section44739
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart iii (Safety); Chapter 447 (Safety Regulation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Prohibition.—
It shall be unlawful for any person to place a live animal in an overhead storage compartment of an aircraft operated under part 121 of title 14, Code of Federal Regulations.
(b) Civil Penalty.—
The Administrator may impose a civil penalty under section 46301 for each violation of this section.
