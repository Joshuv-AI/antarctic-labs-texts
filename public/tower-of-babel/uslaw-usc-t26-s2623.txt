26 U.S.C. § 2623 — Taxable amount in case of direct skip
Citation: us:usc:26:2623
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section2623
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 13 (Tax On Generation-Skipping Transfers); Subchapter C (Taxable Amount). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of this chapter, the taxable amount in the case of a direct skip shall be the value of the property received by the trans­feree.
