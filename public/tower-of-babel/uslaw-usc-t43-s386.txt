43 U.S.C. § 386 — Application of excess-land provisions of reclamation laws to certain lands
Citation: us:usc:43:386
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section386
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 12 (Reclamation And Irrigation Of Lands By Federal Government); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The excess-land provisions of the Federal reclamation laws shall not be applicable to lands which on June 16, 1938, had an irrigation water supply from sources other than a Federal reclamation project and which will receive a supplemental supply from the Colorado-Big Thompson project.
