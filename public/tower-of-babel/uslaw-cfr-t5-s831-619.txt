5 C.F.R. § 831.619 — Marital status at time of retirement.
Citation: us:cfr:5:831.619
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-831.619
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 831 (Retirement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee or Member is unmarried at the time of retirement for all purposes under this subpart only if the employee or Member was unmarried on the date that the annuity begins to accrue.
