12 C.F.R. § 340.8 — Does this part apply in the case of a workout, resolution, or settlement of obligations?
Citation: us:cfr:12:340.8
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-340.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 340 (Restrictions On Sale Of Assets Of A Failed Institution By The Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The restrictions of §§ 340.3 and 340.4 do not apply if the sale or transfer of an asset resolves or settles, or is part of the resolution or settlement of, one or more obligations or claims that have been, or could have been, asserted by the FDIC against the person with whom the FDIC is settling regardless of the amount of such obligations or claims.
