34 C.F.R. § 81.39 — Submission of evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:34:81.39
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-81.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 81 (General Education Provisions Act—Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The ALJ schedules the submission of the evidence, whether oral or documentary, to occur within 90 days of the OALJ's receipt of an acceptable application for review under § 81.37.
(b) The ALJ may waive the 90-day requirement for good cause.
