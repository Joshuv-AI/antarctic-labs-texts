10 C.F.R. § 217.56 — Changes or cancellations of an allocation order.
Citation: us:cfr:10:217.56
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-217.56
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 217 (Energy Priorities And Allocations System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An allocation order may be changed or canceled by an official action of the Department of Energy.
