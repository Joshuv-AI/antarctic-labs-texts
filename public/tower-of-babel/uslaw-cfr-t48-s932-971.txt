48 C.F.R. § 932.971 — 932.971 Electronic submission of invoices/vouchers.
Citation: us:cfr:48:932.971
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-932.971
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 932 (Contract Financing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In general, Contracting Officers should insert the clause at 952.232-7, Electronic Submission of Invoices/Vouchers, in contracts. However, after consultation with the Office of the Chief Financial Officer, the Contracting Officer may approve alternate methods of submission.
