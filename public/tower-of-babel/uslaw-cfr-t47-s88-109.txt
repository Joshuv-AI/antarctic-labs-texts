47 C.F.R. § 88.109 — Time division duplex requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:47:88.109
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-88.109
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 88 (Uncrewed Aircraft System Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Transmitters operating in the 5030-5091 MHz band must comply with the time division duplex (TDD) frame structure and timing accuracy requirements specified in technical standard RTCA DO-362A (incorporated by reference, see § 88.117) sections 2.2.1.3 and 2.2.1.3.1.
