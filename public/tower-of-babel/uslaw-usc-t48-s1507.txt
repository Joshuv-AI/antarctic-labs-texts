48 U.S.C. § 1507 — Public lands
Citation: us:usc:48:1507
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title48-section1507
Context: United States Code, Title 48 (Territories And Insular Possessions); Chapter 11 (Alien Owners Of Land). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall not in any manner be construed to authorize aliens to acquire title from the United States to any of the public lands of the United States or to in any manner affect or change the laws regulating the disposal of the public lands of the United States.
