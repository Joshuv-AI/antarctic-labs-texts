5 C.F.R. § 720.302 — Definition.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 720 (Affirmative Employment Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this subpart, the terms veteran and disabled veteran have the meanings given to these terms in title 38 of the United States Code.
