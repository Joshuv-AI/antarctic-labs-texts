27 C.F.R. § 478.91 — Posting of license.
Citation: us:cfr:27:478.91
Creator: Bureau Of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, And Explosives, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-478.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 478 (Commerce In Firearms And Ammunition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any license issued under this part shall be kept posted and kept available for inspection on the premises covered by the license.
