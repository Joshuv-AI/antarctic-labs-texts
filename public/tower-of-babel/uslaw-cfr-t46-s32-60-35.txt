46 C.F.R. § 32.60-35 — -35 Tank vessels carrying Grade A liquid cargo—TB/ALL.
Citation: us:cfr:46:32.60-35
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-32.60-35
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 32 (Special Equipment, Machinery, And Hull Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Grade A liquids having a Reid vapor pressure in excess of 25 pounds per square inch shall be transported in cargo tanks which are independent of the hull.
(b) Barges carrying Grade A liquids having a Reid vapor pressure in excess of 25 pounds per square inch shall be of a Type III barge hull as defined in § 32.63-5(b)(3).
