18 C.F.R. § 4.200 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:18:4.200
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-4.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 4 (Licenses, Permits, Exemptions, And Determination Of Project Costs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to any application for amendment of a license, if the applicant seeks to:
(a) Make a change in the physical features of the project or its boundary, or make an addition, betterment, abandonment, or conversion, of such character as to constitute an alteration of the license;
(b) Make a change in the plans for the project under license; or
(c) Extend the time fixed in the license for commencement or completion of project works.
