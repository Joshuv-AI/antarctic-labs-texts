42 U.S.C. § 16001 — Energy efficiency in federally assisted housing
Citation: us:usc:42:16001
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section16001
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter V (Indian Energy). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Housing and Urban Development shall promote energy conservation in housing that is located on Indian land and assisted with Federal resources through—
(1)
the use of energy-efficient technologies and innovations (including the procurement of energy-efficient refrigerators and other appliances);
(2)
the promotion of shared savings contracts; and
(3)
the use and implementation of such other similar technologies and innovations as the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development considers to be appropriate.
