41 C.F.R. § 50-201.1202 — -201.1202 Complaints.
Citation: us:cfr:41:50-201.1202
Creator: Chapter 50—Public Contracts, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-50-201.1202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 50-201 (201—General Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever any officer or employee of the United States Government or of any agency thereof has any knowledge of, or receives any complaint with respect to, a breach or violation of the stipulations required under § 50-201.1, he shall transmit such complaint according to the usual practice in his department to the Department of Labor, together with such other information as he has in his possession.
