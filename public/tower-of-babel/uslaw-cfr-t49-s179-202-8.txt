49 C.F.R. § 179.202-8 — -8 Bottom outlets.
Citation: us:cfr:49:179.202-8
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-179.202-8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 179 (Specifications For Tank Cars). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the tank car is equipped with a bottom outlet, the handle must be removed prior to train movement or be designed with protection safety system(s) to prevent unintended actuation during train accident scenarios.
