48 C.F.R. § 4.1401 — 4.1401 Applicability.

(a) This subpart applies to all contracts with a value of $40,000 or more. Nothing in this subpart requires the disclosure of classified information.
(b) Reporting of subcontract information will be limited to the first-tier subcontractor.
