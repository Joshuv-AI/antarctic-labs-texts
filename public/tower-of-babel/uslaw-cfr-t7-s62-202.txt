7 C.F.R. § 62.202 — How to apply for service.
Citation: us:cfr:7:62.202
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-62.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 62 (Agricultural Marketing Service Audit Verification And Accreditation Programs (Avaap)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants may apply for services authorized under this part by contacting the Administrator's office and requesting specific service or program information at USDA, AMS, 1400 Independence Avenue SW, Room 3069-S, Washington, DC 20250-0294; by fax to: (202) 720-5115, or email to: AMSAdministratorOffice@usda.gov. Applicants may also visit: https://www.ams.usda.gov.
