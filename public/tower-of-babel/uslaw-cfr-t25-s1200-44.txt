25 C.F.R. § 1200.44 — What action will the Department take on requests for technical assistance?
Citation: us:cfr:25:1200.44
Creator: Office Of The Special Trustee For American Indians, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-1200.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 1200 (American Indian Trust Fund Management Reform Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will respond in writing to all requests for technical assistance and grants, advising of decision, availability of appropriate expertise and funding, and anticipated delivery of the service.
