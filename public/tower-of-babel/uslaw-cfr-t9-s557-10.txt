9 C.F.R. § 557.10 — Samples; inspection of consignments; refusal of entry; marking.
Citation: us:cfr:9:557.10
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-557.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 557 (Importation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions in 9 CFR 327.10 governing the taking of samples, the inspection of consignments, the refusal of entry, and the controlled pre-stamping of shipments of meat and meat food products apply with respect to fish and fish products.
