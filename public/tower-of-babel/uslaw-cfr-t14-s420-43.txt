14 C.F.R. § 420.43 — Duration.
Citation: us:cfr:14:420.43
Creator: Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-420.43
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 420 (License To Operate A Launch Site). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A license to operate a launch site remains in effect for five years from the date of issuance unless surrendered, suspended, or revoked before the expiration of the term and is renewable upon application by the licensee.
