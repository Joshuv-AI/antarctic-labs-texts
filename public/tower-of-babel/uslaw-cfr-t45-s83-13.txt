45 C.F.R. § 83.13 — State law and licensure requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:45:83.13
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-83.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 83 (Regulation For The Administration And Enforcement Of Sections 799A And 845 Of The Public Health Service Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The obligation of an entity to comply with this part is not obviated or alleviated by any State or local law which would render an applicant for or student enrolled in a training program ineligible on the basis of sex for any license or certificate requisite to the practice of the health profession for which such applicant seeks, or student pursues, training.
