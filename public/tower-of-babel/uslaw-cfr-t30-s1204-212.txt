30 C.F.R. § 1204.212 — What if I took relief for which I was ineligible?
Citation: us:cfr:30:1204.212
Creator: Office Of Natural Resources Revenue, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-1204.212
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 1204 (Alternatives For Marginal Properties). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If you took relief under this subpart for a period for which you were not eligible, you:
(a) May owe additional royalties and late payment interest determined under § 1218.54 from the date your additional payments were due until the date ONRR receives them; and
(b) May be subject to civil penalties.
