10 U.S.C. § 1596c — Programming language proficiency: special pay for proficiency beneficial for national security interests
Citation: us:usc:10:1596c
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1596c
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 81 (Civilian Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Authority.—
The Secretary of Defense, under the sole and exclusive discretion of the Secretary, may pay special pay under this section to an employee of the Department of Defense who—
(1)
has been certified by the Secretary to be proficient in a computer or digital programming language identified by the Secretary as being a language in which proficiency by civilian personnel of the Department is necessary because of national security interests; and
(2)
is assigned duties requiring proficiency in that programming language.
(b) Rate.—
The rate of special pay for an employee under this section shall be prescribed by the Secretary, but may not exceed 20 percent of the employee’s rate of basic pay.
(c) Relationship to Other Pay and Allowances.—
Special pay under this section is in addition to any other pay or allowances to which the employee is entitled.
(d) Regulations.—
The Secretary of Defense shall prescribe regulations to carry out this section.
