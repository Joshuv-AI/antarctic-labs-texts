34 C.F.R. § 675.42 — Allocation and reallocation.
Citation: us:cfr:34:675.42
Creator: Office Of Postsecondary Education, Department Of Education
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-675.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 675 (Federal Work-Study Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary allocates and reallocates funds based on each institution's approved request for Federal funds for the Work-Colleges program as a percent of the total of such approved requests for all applicant institutions.
