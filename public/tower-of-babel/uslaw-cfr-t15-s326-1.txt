15 C.F.R. § 326.1 — What is the Fellowship Program?
Citation: us:cfr:15:326.1
Creator: International Trade Administration, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-326.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 326 (U.S. And Foreign Commercial Service Pilot Fellowship Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The U.S. and Foreign Commercial Service Pilot Fellowship Program targets graduate students interested in increasing the level of knowledge and awareness of and employment with the Foreign Service, consistent with 22 U.S.C. 3905. The Program develops a source of trained individuals, from academic disciplines representing the skill needs of the Department, who are dedicated to representing the United States' commercial interests abroad.
