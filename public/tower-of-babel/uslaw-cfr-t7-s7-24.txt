7 C.F.R. § 7.24 — Chairperson of the county committee duties.
Citation: us:cfr:7:7.24
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-7.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 7 (Selection And Functions Of Farm Service Agency State And County Committees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The chairperson of the county committee or the person acting as the chairperson will preside at meetings of the county committee, certify such documents as may require the chairperson's certification, and perform such other duties as may be prescribed by the State committee.
