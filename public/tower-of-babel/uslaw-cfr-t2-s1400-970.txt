2 C.F.R. § 1400.970 — Nonprocurement transaction (Department of the Interior supplement to the definition at 2 CFR 180.970).
Citation: us:cfr:2:1400.970
Creator: Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-1400.970
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 2 (Federal Financial Assistance), Part 1400 (Nonprocurement Debarment And Suspension). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In addition to those listed in 2 CFR 180.970, the Department of the Interior includes the following as nonprocurement transactions:
(a) Federal acquisition of a leasehold interest or any other interest in real property;
(b) Concession contracts;
(c) Disposition of Federal real and personal property and natural resources; and
(d) Any other nonprocurement transactions between the Department and a person.
