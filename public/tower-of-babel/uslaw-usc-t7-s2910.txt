7 U.S.C. § 2910 — Preemption of other Federal and State programs; applicability of provisions to amendments to orders
Citation: us:usc:7:2910
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section2910
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 62 (Beef Research And Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
Nothing in this chapter may be construed to preempt or supersede any other program relating to beef promotion organized and operated under the laws of the United States or any State.
(b)
The provisions of this chapter applicable to the order shall be applicable to amendments to the order.
