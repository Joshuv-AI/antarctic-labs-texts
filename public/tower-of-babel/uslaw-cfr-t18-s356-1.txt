18 C.F.R. § 356.1 — Promulgation.
Citation: us:cfr:18:356.1
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-356.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 356 (Preservation Of Records For Oil Pipeline Companies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is prescribed and promulgated as the regulations governing the preservation of records by oil pipeline companies subject to the jurisdiction of the Commission, to the extent and in the manner set forth therein. This part is enforceable as of the date the oil pipeline company becomes subject to the jurisdiction of the Commission.
