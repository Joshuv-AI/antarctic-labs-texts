12 U.S.C. § 356 — Purchase of commercial paper from member banks and sale of same
Citation: us:usc:12:356
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section356
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 3 (Federal Reserve System); Subchapter IX (Powers And Duties Of Federal Reserve Banks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Every Federal reserve bank shall have power to purchase from member banks and to sell, with or without its indorsement, bills of exchange arising out of commercial transactions, as hereinbefore defined.
