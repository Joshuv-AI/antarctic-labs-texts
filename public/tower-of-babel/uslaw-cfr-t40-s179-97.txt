40 C.F.R. § 179.97 — Conferences during hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:40:179.97
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-179.97
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 179 (Formal Evidentiary Public Hearing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The presiding officer may schedule and hold conferences as needed to monitor the progress of the hearing, narrow and simplify the issues, and consider and rule on motions, requests, or other matters concerning the development of the evidence.
