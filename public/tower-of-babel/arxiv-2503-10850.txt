Abstract
Static spherically symmetric (SSS) solutions of f ⁡ ( R ) f(R) gravity are studied in the Einstein frame. The solutions involve SSS configuration mass M M and scalaron mass μ \mu (in geometrized units). For typical astrophysical masses, the dimensionless parameter M ​ μ M\mu has very large value. We found analytic solutions on a finite interval for M ​ μ → ∞ M\mu\to\infty in case of a family of scalaron potentials. The asymptotically flat solutions on ( 0 , ∞ ) (0,\infty) have been studied numerically for M ​ μ M\mu up to 10 20 10^{20} in case of the quadratic f ⁡ ( R ) f(R) model.

Discussion

We studied SSS configurations in the Einstein frame of the
f(R)
gravity and found a representation of the basic equations that allowed us to derive approximate solutions and to perform numerical calculations for rather high values of
M\mu

In case of potentials satisfying conditions (

The key formulas are (

In case of asymptotically flat systems, there are three main regions of the radial variable with different types of behavior of the solutions.

In the region A (
r\geq r_{0}\gg r_{g}
), we have small scalaron field that decays exponentially according to (
45

Region B. This is an intermediate region of

with subsequent (with decreasing

Regions B and C are significantly different from the Schwarzschild case. In C we have practically constant values of

Our results have been obtained in the Einstein frame. The metric coefficients in the Jordan frame can be obtained by the formula (
