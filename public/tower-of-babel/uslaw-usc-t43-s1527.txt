43 U.S.C. § 1527 — Fish and wildlife conservation and development
Citation: us:usc:43:1527
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section1527
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 32 (Colorado River Basin Project); Subchapter III (Authorized Units; Protection Of Existing Uses). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The conservation and development of the fish and wildlife resources and the enhancement of recreation opportunities in connection with the project works authorized pursuant to this subchapter shall be in accordance with the provisions of the Federal Water Project Recreation Act (79 Stat. 213) [16 U.S.C. 460l–12 et seq.], except as provided in section 1522 of this title.
