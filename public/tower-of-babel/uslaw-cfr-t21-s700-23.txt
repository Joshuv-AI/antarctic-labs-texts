21 C.F.R. § 700.23 — Chlorofluorocarbon propellants.
Citation: us:cfr:21:700.23
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-700.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 700 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The use of chlorofluorocarbons in cosmetics as propellants in self-pressurized containers is prohibited as provided in § 2.125 of this chapter.
