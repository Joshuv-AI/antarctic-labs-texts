13 C.F.R. § 112.5 — Discrimination in providing financial assistance.
Citation: us:cfr:13:112.5
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-112.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 112 (Nondiscrimination In Federally Assisted Programs Of Sba—Effectuation Of Title Vi Of The Civil Rights Act Of 1964). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Development companies and small business investment companies which apply for or receive any of the financial assistance described in § 112.2(a) may not discriminate, on the ground of race, color or national origin, in providing financial assistance to small business concerns.
