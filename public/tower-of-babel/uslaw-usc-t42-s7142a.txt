42 U.S.C. § 7142a — Designation of American Museum of Science and Energy
Citation: us:usc:42:7142a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7142a
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter II (Establishment Of Department). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
The Museum—
(1)
is designated as the “American Museum of Science and Energy”; and
(2)
shall be the official museum of science and energy of the United States.
(b) References
Any reference in a law, map, regulation, document, paper, or other record of the United States to the Museum is deemed to be a reference to the “American Museum of Science and Energy”.
(c) Property of the United States
(1) In general
The name “American Museum of Science and Energy” is declared the property of the United States.
(2) Use
The Museum shall have the sole right throughout the United States and its possessions to have and use the name “American Museum of Science and Energy”.
(3) Effect on other rights
This subsection shall not be construed to conflict or interfere with established or vested rights.
