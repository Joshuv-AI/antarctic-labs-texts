31 C.F.R. § 535.580 — Necessary living expenses of relatives of the former Shah of Iran.
Citation: us:cfr:31:535.580
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-535.580
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 535 (Iranian Assets Control Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The transfer, payment or withdrawal of property described in § 535.217 is authorized to the extent necessary to pay living expenses of any individual listed in that section. Living expenses for this purpose shall include food, housing, transportation, security and other personal expenses.
