5 C.F.R. § 847.908 — If a retiree who elected to credit NAFI service under subpart H of this part earns a supplemental annuity under 5 CFR part 837, how will that supplemental annuity be computed?
Citation: us:cfr:5:847.908
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026

This subpart does not affect supplemental annuities under 5 CFR part 837. Supplemental annuities will be computed in accordance with the provisions of that part.
