22 C.F.R. § 1506.46 — When is USADF not required to transfer a debt to BFS?
Citation: us:cfr:22:1506.46
Creator: African Development Foundation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-1506.46
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 1506 (Collection Of Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

USADF is not required to transfer a debt to BFS pursuant to § 1506.37(b) during the period of time that the debt:
(a) Is in litigation or foreclosure;
(b) Is scheduled for sale;
(c) Is at a private collection contractor;
(d) Is at a debt collection center if the debt has been referred to a Treasury-designated debt collection center;
(e) Is being collected by internal offset; or
(f) Is covered by an exemption granted by Treasury.
