5 U.S.C. § 8959 — Preemption
Citation: us:usc:5:8959
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section8959
Context: United States Code, Title 5 (Government Organization And Employees); Part III (Employees); Subpart G (Insurance And Annuities); Chapter 89A (Enhanced Dental Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The terms of any contract that relate to the nature, provision, or extent of coverage or benefits (including payments with respect to benefits) shall supersede and preempt any State or local law, or any regulation issued thereunder, which relates to dental benefits, insurance, plans, or contracts.
