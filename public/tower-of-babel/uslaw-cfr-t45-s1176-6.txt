45 C.F.R. § 1176.6 — Review and evaluation.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1176.6
Creator: National Foundation On The Arts And The Humanities
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1176.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1176 (Part-Time Career Employment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Regular employment reports will be used to determine levels of part-time employment. This program will also be designated an item of special interest to be reviewed during personnel management reviews.
