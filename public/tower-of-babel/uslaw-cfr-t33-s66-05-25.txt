33 C.F.R. § 66.05-25 — -25 Change and modification of State aids to navigation.
Citation: us:cfr:33:66.05-25
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-66.05-25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 66 (Private Aids To Navigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Wherever a State Administrator determines the need for change in State aids to navigation, he or she must inform the District Commander of the nature and extent of the changes, as soon as possible, but not less than 30 days in advance of making the changes.
