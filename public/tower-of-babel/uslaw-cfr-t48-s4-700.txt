48 C.F.R. § 4.700 — 4.700 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:4.700
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-4.700
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 4 (Administrative And Information Matters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart provides policies and procedures for retention of records by contractors to meet the records review requirements of the Government. In this subpart, the terms “contracts” and “contractors” include “subcontracts” and “subcontractors.”
