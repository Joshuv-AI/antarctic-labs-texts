43 C.F.R. § 3282.8 — What are the reasons BLM would not approve a revision of the participating area boundary?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3282.8
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3282.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3280 (Geothermal Resources Unit Agreements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

BLM will not approve a revision of the participating area boundary:
(a) If the unit operator does not submit the required information;
(b) If BLM determines that the new or additional technical information does not support a boundary revision; or
(c) If it reduces the size of a participating area because of depletion of the resource.
