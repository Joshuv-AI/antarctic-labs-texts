20 C.F.R. § 365.102 — Application.
Citation: us:cfr:20:365.102
Creator: Railroad Retirement Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-365.102
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 365 (Enforcement Of Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Conducted By The Railroad Retirement Board). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This regulation (§§ 365.101 through 365.170) applies to all programs or activities conducted by the agency, except for programs or activities conducted outside the United States that do not involve individuals with handicaps in the United States.
