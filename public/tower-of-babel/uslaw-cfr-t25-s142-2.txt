25 C.F.R. § 142.2 — What is the purpose of the Alaska Resupply Operation?
Citation: us:cfr:25:142.2
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-142.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 142 (Alaska Resupply Operation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Alaska Resupply Operation provides consolidated purchasing, freight handling and distribution, and necessary transportation services from Seattle, Washington to and from other points in Alaska or en route in support of the Bureau's mission and responsibilities.
