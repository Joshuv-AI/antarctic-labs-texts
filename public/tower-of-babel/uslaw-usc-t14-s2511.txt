14 U.S.C. § 2511 — Investigations of flag officers and Senior Executive Service employees
Citation: us:usc:14:2511
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2511
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 25 (Personnel; General Provisions); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In conducting an investigation into an allegation of misconduct by a flag officer or member of the Senior Executive Service serving in the Coast Guard, the Inspector General of the Department of Homeland Security shall—
(1)
conduct the investigation in a manner consistent with Department of Defense policies for such an investigation; and
(2)
consult with the Inspector General of the Department of Defense.
