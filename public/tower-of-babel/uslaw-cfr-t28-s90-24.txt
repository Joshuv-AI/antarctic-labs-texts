28 C.F.R. § 90.24 — Activities that may compromise victim safety and recovery.
Citation: us:cfr:28:90.24
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-90.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 90 (Violence Against Women). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Because of the overall purpose of the STOP Program to enhance victim safety and offender accountability, grant funds may not be used to support activities that compromise victim safety and recovery. The grant program solicitation each year will provide examples of such activities.
