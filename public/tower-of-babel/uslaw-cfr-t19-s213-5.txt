19 C.F.R. § 213.5 — Access to Commission resources.
Citation: us:cfr:19:213.5
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-213.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 213 (Trade Remedy Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Commission resources, in addition to the Office's resources, are available to an eligible small business to the same extent as those resources are available to members of the general public. No special rights of access to Commission resources shall be accorded to an eligible small business.
