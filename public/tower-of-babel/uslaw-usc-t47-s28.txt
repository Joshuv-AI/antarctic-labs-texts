47 U.S.C. § 28 — Penalties not to bar suits for damages
Citation: us:usc:47:28
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section28
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 2 (Submarine Cables). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The penalties provided in this chapter for the breaking or injury of a submarine cable shall not be a bar to a suit for damages on account of such breaking or injury.
