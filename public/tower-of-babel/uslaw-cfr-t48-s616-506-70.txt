48 C.F.R. § 616.506-70 — 616.506-70 DOSAR contract clause.
Citation: us:cfr:48:616.506-70
Creator: Chapter 6—Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-616.506-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 616 (Types Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The contracting officer shall insert the clause at 652.216-70, Ordering—Indefinite-Delivery Contract, whenever the clause at FAR 52.216-20, Definite Quantity, or the clause at FAR 52.216-21, Requirements, or the clause at FAR 52.216-22, Indefinite Quantity, is used.
