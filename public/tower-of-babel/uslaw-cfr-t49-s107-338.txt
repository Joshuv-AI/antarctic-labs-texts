49 C.F.R. § 107.338 — Prohibition of hazardous materials operations.
Citation: us:cfr:49:107.338
Creator: Pipeline And Hazardous Materials Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-107.338
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 107 (Hazardous Materials Program Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As provided for in subpart E of part 109 of this subchapter, a person who fails to pay a civil penalty in accordance with agreed upon installments or in full within prescribed time lines, is prohibited from conducting hazardous materials operations and shall immediately cease all hazardous materials operations.
