46 C.F.R. § 111.60-4 — -4 Minimum cable conductor size.
Citation: us:cfr:46:111.60-4
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-111.60-4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 111 (Electric Systems—General Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each cable conductor must be 18 AWG (0.82 mm 2) or larger except—
(a) Each power and lighting cable conductor must be 14 AWG (2.10 mm 2) or larger; and
(b) Each thermocouple, pyrometer, or instrumentation cable conductor must be 22 AWG (0.33 mm 2) or larger.
