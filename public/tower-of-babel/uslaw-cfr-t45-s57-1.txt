45 C.F.R. § 57.1 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:45:57.1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-57.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 57 (Volunteer Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations in this part apply to the acceptance of volunteer and uncompensated services for use in the operation of any health care facility of the Department or in the provision of health care.
