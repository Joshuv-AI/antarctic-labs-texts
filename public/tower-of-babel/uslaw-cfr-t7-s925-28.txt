7 C.F.R. § 925.28 — Powers.
Citation: us:cfr:7:925.28
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-925.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 925 (Grapes Grown In A Designated Area Of Southeastern California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The committee shall have the following powers:
(a) To administer the provisions of this part in accordance with its terms;
(b) To receive, investigate, and report to the Secretary complaints of violations of the provisions of this part;
(c) To make and adopt rules and regulations to effectuate the terms and provisions of this part; and
(d) To recommend to the Secretary amendments to this part.
