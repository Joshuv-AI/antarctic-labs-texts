42 U.S.C. § 15841 — Energy-efficient appliances
Citation: us:usc:42:15841
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section15841
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 149 (National Energy Policy And Programs); Subchapter I (Energy Efficiency); Part D (Public Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In purchasing appliances, a public housing agency shall purchase energy-efficient appliances that are Energy Star products or FEMP-designated products, as such terms are defined in section 8259b of this title, unless the purchase of energy-efficient appliances is not cost-effective to the agency.
