6 C.F.R. § 27.410 — Third-party actions.
Citation: us:cfr:6:27.410
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security, Office Of The Secretary
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-6/section-27.410
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 6 (Domestic Security), Part 27 (Chemical Facility Anti-Terrorism Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Nothing in this part shall confer upon any person except the Secretary a right of action, in law or equity, for any remedy including, but not limited to, injunctions or damages to enforce any provision of this part.
(b) An owner or operator of a chemical facility may petition the Executive Assistant Director to provide the Department's view in any litigation involving any issues or matters regarding this part.
