25 C.F.R. § 103.1 — What does this part do?
Citation: us:cfr:25:103.1
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-103.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 103 (Loan Guaranty, Insurance, And Interest Subsidy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part explains how to obtain and use a BIA loan guaranty or loan insurance agreement under the Program, and who may do so. It also describes how to obtain and use interest subsidy payments under the Program, and who may do so.
