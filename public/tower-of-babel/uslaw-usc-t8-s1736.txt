8 U.S.C. § 1736 — Check of lookout databases
Citation: us:usc:8:1736
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1736
Context: United States Code, Title 8 (Aliens And Nationality); Chapter 15 (Enhanced Border Security And Visa Entry Reform); Subchapter III (Visa Issuance). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Prior to the admission of an alien under the visa waiver program established under section 1187 of this title, the Immigration and Naturalization Service shall determine that the applicant for admission does not appear in any of the appropriate lookout databases available to immigration inspectors at the time the alien seeks admission to the United States.
