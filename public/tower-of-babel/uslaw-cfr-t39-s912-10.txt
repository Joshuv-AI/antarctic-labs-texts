39 C.F.R. § 912.10 — Action on approved claims.
Citation: us:cfr:39:912.10
Creator: United States Postal Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-912.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 912 (Procedures To Adjudicate Claims For Personal Injury Or Property Damage Arising Out Of The Operation Of The U.S. Postal Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In any case where the General Counsel or the General Counsel's designee, upon consideration of all the evidence submitted, finds that compensation is due a claimant, payment will be made by the U.S. Postal Service and in due course a settlement check will be forwarded to the claimant or his representative.
