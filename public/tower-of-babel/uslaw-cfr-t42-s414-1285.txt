42 C.F.R. § 414.1285 — Informal inquiry process.
Citation: us:cfr:42:414.1285
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-414.1285
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 414 (Payment For Part B Medical And Other Health Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

After the dissemination of the annual Physician Feedback reports, a group and a solo practitioner may contact CMS to inquire about its report and the calculation of the value-based payment modifier.
