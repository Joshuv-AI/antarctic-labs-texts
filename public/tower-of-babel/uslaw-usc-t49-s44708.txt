49 U.S.C. § 44708 — Inspecting and rating air navigation facilities
Citation: us:usc:49:44708
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section44708
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (Air Commerce And Safety); Subpart iii (Safety); Chapter 447 (Safety Regulation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administrator of the Federal Aviation Administration may inspect, classify, and rate an air navigation facility available for the use of civil aircraft on the suitability of the facility for that use.
