42 C.F.R. § 67.22 — Additional conditions.
Citation: us:cfr:42:67.22
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-67.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 67 (Agency For Health Care Policy And Research Grants And Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may, with respect to any grant awarded under this subpart, impose additional conditions prior to or at the time of any award when in the Administrator's judgment such conditions are necessary to assure or protect advancement of the approved project, the interest of the public health, or the conservation of grant funds.
