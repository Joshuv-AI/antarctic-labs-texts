34 U.S.C. § 10512 — Applications
Citation: us:usc:34:10512
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section10512
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 101 (Justice System Improvement); Subchapter XXIII (Dna Identification Grants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

To request a grant under this subchapter, the chief executive officer of a State or unit of local government shall submit an application in such form as the Attorney General may require.
