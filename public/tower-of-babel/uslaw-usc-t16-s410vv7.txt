16 U.S.C. § 410vv–7 — General management plan
Citation: us:usc:16:410vv–7
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section410vv–7
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 1 (National Parks, Military Parks, Monuments, And Seashores); Subchapter LIX–T (Marsh-Billings-Rockefeller National Historical Park). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than 3 complete fiscal years after August 26, 1992, the Secretary shall develop and transmit a general management plan for the park to the Committee on Natural Resources of the United States House of Representatives and to the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources of the United States Senate.
