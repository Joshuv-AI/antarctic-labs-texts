48 C.F.R. § 45.602 — 45.602 Reutilization of Government property.
Citation: us:cfr:48:45.602
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-45.602
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 45 (Government Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This section is applicable to the reutilization, including transfer and donation, of Government property that is not required for continued performance of a Government contract. Except for 45.602-1, this section does not apply to scrap other than scrap aircraft parts.
