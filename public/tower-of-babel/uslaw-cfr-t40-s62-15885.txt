40 C.F.R. § 62.15885 — What must I include in the notifications of achievement of compliance?
Citation: us:cfr:40:62.15885
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-62.15885
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 62 (Approval And Promulgation Of State Plans For Designated Facilities And Pollutants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Your notification of achievement of compliance must include the three items specified in paragraphs (a) through (c) of this section:
(a) Notification that the final control plan has been submitted and final compliance has been achieved;
(b) Any items required to be submitted with the final control plan and final compliance; and
(c) Signature of the owner or operator of the SSI unit.
