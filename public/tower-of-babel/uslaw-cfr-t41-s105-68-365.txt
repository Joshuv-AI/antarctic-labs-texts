41 C.F.R. § 105-68.365 — -68.365 What must I do if I learn of information required under § 105-68.355 after entering into a covered transaction with a higher tier participant?
Citation: us:cfr:41:105-68.365
Creator: Chapter 105—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-105-68.365
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 105-68 (68—Governmentwide Debarment And Suspension (Nonprocurement)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

At any time after you enter into a lower tier covered transaction with a person at a higher tier, you must provide immediate written notice to that person if you learn either that—
(a) You failed to disclose information earlier, as required by § 105-68.355; or
(b) Due to changed circumstances, you or any of the principals for the transaction now meet any of the criteria in § 105-68.355.
