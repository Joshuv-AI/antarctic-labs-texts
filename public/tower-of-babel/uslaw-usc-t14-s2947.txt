14 U.S.C. § 2947 — Reports
Citation: us:usc:14:2947
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title14-section2947
Context: United States Code, Title 14 (Coast Guard); Chapter 29 (Coast Guard Family Support, Child Care, And Housing); Subchapter III (Housing). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary shall prepare and submit to Congress, concurrent with the budget submitted pursuant to section 1105 of title 31, a report identifying the contracts or agreements for the conveyance of properties pursuant to this chapter executed during the prior calendar year.
