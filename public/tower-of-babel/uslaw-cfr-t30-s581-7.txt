30 C.F.R. § 581.7 — Disclosure of information to the public.
Citation: us:cfr:30:581.7
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-581.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 581 (Leasing Of Minerals Other Than Oil, Gas, And Sulphur In The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary shall make data and information available to the public in accordance with the requirements and subject to the limitations of the Act, the Freedom of Information Act (5 U.S.C. 552), and the implementing regulations (30 CFR parts 580, 582, and 43 CFR part 2).
