50 C.F.R. § 36.34 — Firearms.
Citation: us:cfr:50:36.34
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-36.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 36 (Alaska National Wildlife Refuges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The possession, use and transporting of firearms is authorized for hunting and personal protection in accordance with State and Federal laws unless prohibited or otherwise restricted by the Refuge Manager in accordance with the provisions of § 36.42.
