5 C.F.R. § 9701.346 — Pay progression for new supervisors.
Citation: us:cfr:5:9701.346
Creator: Department Of Homeland Security Human Resources Management System (Department Of Homeland Security—Office Of Personnel Management)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-9701.346
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 9701 (Department Of Homeland Security Human Resources Management System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

DHS will issue implementing directives requiring an employee newly appointed to or selected for a supervisory position to meet certain assessment or certification points as part of a formal training/developmental program. In administering performance pay increases for these employees under § 9701.342, DHS may take into account the employee's success in completing a formal training/developmental program, as well as his or her performance.
