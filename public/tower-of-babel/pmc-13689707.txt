Correction to: Complex all-comers and patients with diabetes and prediabetes treated with Xience Sierra everolimus-eluting stents: COASTLINE high-risk
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: Daphne van Vliet, Eline H Ploumen, Tineke H Pinxterhuis, Rosaly A Buiten, Adel Aminian, Carl E Schotborgh, Rutger L Anthonio, Paolo Zocca, Marc Hartmann, Martin G Stoel, Frits H A F de Man, Gerard C M Linssen, Carine J M Doggen, Liefke C van der Heijden, Marlies M Kok, Clemens von Birgelen, Complex all-comers and patients with diabetes and prediabetes treated with Xience Sierra everolimus-eluting stents: COASTLINE high-risk, European Heart Journal Open, Volume 5, Issue 6, November 2025, oeaf140, https://doi.org/10.1093/ehjopen/oeaf140

In the originally published version of this manuscript, the number of included all-comers treated with resolute-type ZES in the graphical abstract was incorrectly stated as 1,317. This should instead be 2,416.

This error has been corrected and the graphical abstract resupplied.
