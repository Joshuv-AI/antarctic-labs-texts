18 U.S.C. § 2515 — Prohibition of use as evidence of intercepted wire or oral communications
Citation: us:usc:18:2515
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2515
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 119 (Wire And Electronic Communications Interception And Interception Of Oral Communications). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever any wire or oral communication has been intercepted, no part of the contents of such communication and no evidence derived therefrom may be received in evidence in any trial, hearing, or other proceeding in or before any court, grand jury, department, officer, agency, regulatory body, legislative committee, or other authority of the United States, a State, or a political subdivision thereof if the disclosure of that information would be in violation of this chapter.
