21 C.F.R. § 607.39 — Misbranding by reference to establishment registration, validation of registration, or to registration number.
Citation: us:cfr:21:607.39
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-607.39
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 607 (Establishment Registration And Product Listing For Manufacturers Of Human Blood And Blood Products And Licensed Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Registration of an establishment, validation of registration, or assignment of a registration number does not in any way denote approval of the firm or its products nor does it mean that the products may be legally marketed. Any representation that creates an impression of official approval because of establishment registration, validation of registration, or possession of a registration number is misleading and constitutes misbranding.
