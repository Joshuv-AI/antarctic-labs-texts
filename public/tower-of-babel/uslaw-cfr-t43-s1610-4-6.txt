43 C.F.R. § 1610.4-6 — -6 Estimation of effects of alternatives.
Citation: us:cfr:43:1610.4-6
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-1610.4-6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 1600 (Planning, Programming, Budgeting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Field Manager, in collaboration with any cooperating agencies, will estimate and display the physical, biological, economic, and social effects of implementing each alternative considered in detail. The estimation of effects shall be guided by the planning criteria and procedures implementing the National Environmental Policy Act. The estimate may be stated in terms of probable ranges where effects cannot be precisely determined.
