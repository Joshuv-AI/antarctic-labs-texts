14 C.F.R. § 1261.418 — Transfer of debts to Treasury for collection.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1261.418
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1261.418
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1261 (Processing Of Monetary Claims (General)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Unless subject to an exception identified in 31 CFR 285.12(d), NASA shall transfer any debt that is more than 180 days delinquent to the Financial Management Service for debt collection services in accordance with the procedures described in 31 CFR 285.12.
