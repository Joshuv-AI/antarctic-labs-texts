45 C.F.R. § 2516.720 — What is the length of each type of grant?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2516.720
Creator: Corporation For National And Community Service
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2516.720
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2516 (School-Based Service-Learning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) One year is the maximum length of—
(1) A planning grant under § 2516.200 (a), (c) or (e); and
(2) A grant to a local partnership for activities in a nonparticipating State under § 2516.200 (b)(2) and (d)(2).
(b) All other grants are for a period of up to three years, subject to satisfactory performance and annual appropriations.
