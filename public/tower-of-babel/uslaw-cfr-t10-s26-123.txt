10 C.F.R. § 26.123 — Testing facility capabilities.
Citation: us:cfr:10:26.123
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-26.123
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 26 (Fitness For Duty Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each licensee testing facility shall have the capability, at the same premises, to perform either validity screening tests or initial validity tests or both, and initial drug tests for each drug and drug metabolite for which testing is conducted.
