Poincaré-Sobolev equations with the critical exponent and a potential in the hyperbolic space
Mousomi Bhakta, Debdip Ganguly, Diksha Gupta, Alok Kumar Sahoo

Abstract
. On the hyperbolic space, we study a semilinear equation with non-autonomous nonlinearity having a critical Sobolev exponent. The Poincaré-Sobolev equation on the hyperbolic space explored by Mancini and Sandeep [Ann. Sc. Norm. Super. Pisa Cl. Sci. 7 (2008)] resembles our equation. As seen from the profile decomposition of the energy functional associated with the problem, the concentration happens along two distinct profiles: localised Aubin-Talenti bubbles and hyperbolic bubbles. Standard variational arguments cannot obtain solutions because of nontrivial potential and concentration phenomena. As a result, a deformation-type argument based on the critical points at infinity of the associated variational problem has been carried out to obtain solutions for N > 6 . N>6. Conformal change of metric is used for proofs, enabling us to convert the original equation into a singular equation in a ball in ℝ N \mathbb{R}^{N} and perform a fine blow-up analysis.

\displaystyle\left|\frac{\partial\varphi^{\varepsilon,y}}{\partial y_{j}}(x)\right|\leq\max_{\overline{B}}\frac{\partial\varphi^{\varepsilon,y}}{\partial y_{j}}(x)\leq\max_{\partial B}\frac{\varepsilon^{\frac{N-2}{2}}|x-y|}{\left[\varepsilon^{2}+|x-y|^{2}\right]^{\frac{N}{2}}}\leq\frac{\varepsilon^{\frac{N-2}{2}}}{d^{N-1}},

so,

\left\|\frac{\partial\varphi^{\varepsilon,y}}{\partial y_{j}}\right\|_{\infty}\leq\frac{\varepsilon^{\frac{N-2}{2}}}{d^{N-1}}.

Similarly,

\left\|\frac{\partial\varphi^{\varepsilon,y}}{\partial\varepsilon}\right\|_{\infty}\leq\frac{\varepsilon^{\frac{N-4}{2}}}{d^{N-2}}.

Proof.

We begin by proving (

(5.17)

I1

\displaystyle I1=\int_{B\left(y,\frac{d}{4}\right)}\frac{\left|PV^{\varepsilon,y}\right|^{2}}{\left(1-|x|^{2}\right)^{2}}\mathrm{~d}x=\underbrace{\int_{B\left(y,\frac{d}{4}\right)}\frac{\left(V^{\varepsilon,y}\right)^{2}}{\left(1-|x|^{2}\right)^{2}}\mathrm{~d}x}_{I1.a}+\underbrace{\int_{B\left(y,\frac{d}{4}\right)}\frac{\left(\varphi^{\varepsilon,y}\right)^{2}}{\left(1-|x|^{2}\right)^{2}}\mathrm{~d}x}_{I1.b}-\underbrace{2\int_{B\left(y,\frac{d}{4}\right)}\frac{V^{\varepsilon,y}\varphi^{\varepsilon,y}}{\left(1-|x|^{2}\right)^{2}}\mathrm{~d}x}_{I1.c}.

Let’s first address

\displaystyle C(N)\int_{B\left(y,\frac{d}{4}\right)}\frac{\varepsilon^{N-2}}{\left(\varepsilon^{2}+\left|x-y\right|^{2}\right)^{N-2}\left(1-|x|^{2}\right)^{2}}\mathrm{~d}x

\displaystyle=C(N)\varepsilon^{2}\int_{|z|<\frac{d}{4\varepsilon}}\frac{1}{\left(1+|z|^{2}\right)^{N-2}\left(1-\left|z\varepsilon+y\right|^{2}\right)^{2}}\mathrm{~d}z.

Next, consider the following integral

\displaystyle\int_{|z|<\frac{d}{4\varepsilon}}\frac{1}{\left(1+|z|^{2}\right)^{N-2}}\left[\frac{1}{\left(1-\left|z\varepsilon+y\right|^{2}\right)^{2}}-\frac{1}{\left(1-\left|y\right|^{2}\right)^{2}}\right]\mathrm{~d}z

\displaystyle=\int_{|z|<\frac{d}{4\varepsilon}}\frac{1}{\left(1+|z|^{2}\right)^{N-2}}\left[\frac{\left(1-\left|y\right|^{2}\right)^{2}-\left(1-\left|z\varepsilon+y\right|^{2}\right)^{2}}{\left(1-\left|z\varepsilon+y\right|^{2}\right)^{2}\left(1-|y|^{2}\right)^{2}}\right]\mathrm{~d}z

\displaystyle=\int_{|z|<\frac{d}{4\varepsilon}}\left[\frac{\left(\varepsilon^{2}|z|^{2}+2z\varepsilon\cdot y\right)\left(1-\left|y\right|^{2}\right)+\left(1-\left|z\varepsilon+y\right|^{2}\right)\left(\varepsilon^{2}|z|^{2}+2z\varepsilon\cdot y\right)}{\left(1+|z|^{2}\right)^{N-2}\left(1-\left|z\varepsilon+y\right|^{2}\right)^{2}\left(1-\left|y\right|^{2}\right)^{2}}\right]\mathrm{~d}z

\displaystyle=\int_{|z|<\frac{d}{4\varepsilon}}\frac{1}{\left(1+|z|^{2}\right)^{N-2}}\frac{\left(\varepsilon^{2}|z|^{2}+2z\varepsilon\cdot y\right)}{\left(1-\left|z\varepsilon+y\right|^{2}\right)^{2}\left(1-\left|y\right|^{2}\right)}\mathrm{~d}z

\displaystyle\hskip 85.35826pt+\int_{|z|<\frac{d}{4\varepsilon}}\frac{1}{\left(1+|z|^{2}\right)^{N-2}}\frac{(\varepsilon^{2}|z|^{2}+2z\varepsilon\cdot y)}{\left(1-\left|z\varepsilon+y\right|^{2}\right)\left(1-\left|y\right|^{2}\right)^{2}}\mathrm{~d}z

Thus, we have

as

\displaystyle\int_{|z|<\frac{d}{4\varepsilon}}\frac{1}{\left(1+|z|^{2}\right)^{N-2}}\frac{1}{\left(1-\left|z\varepsilon+y\right|^{2}\right)^{2}}\mathrm{~d}z

Estimate 5.7

(

4.1
) and integration by parts, we obtain

\displaystyle\int_{B}(V^{\varepsilon,y})^{2^{*}-1}v=\int_{B}(-\Delta V^{\varepsilon,y})v=\int_{B}\nabla V^{\varepsilon,y}.\nabla v=0.

Thus, using this result and noting that

Observe that by applying Hölder’s inequality,

can be estimated as follows

\displaystyle T1.b=O\left(\left(\int_{B\setminus B(y,d)}(V^{\varepsilon,y})^{2^{*}}\mathrm{~d}x\right)^{\frac{2^{*}-1}{2^{*}}}\|v\|_{H_{0}^{1}(B)}\right)=O\left(\frac{\varepsilon^{\frac{N+2}{2}}}{d^{\frac{N+2}{2}}}\right).
