27 C.F.R. § 1.62 — Use of distilled spirits or wine for experimental purposes and in manufacture of nonbeverage products.
Citation: us:cfr:27:1.62
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-1.62
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 1 (Basic Permit Requirements Under The Federal Alcohol Administration Act, Nonindustrial Use Of Distilled Spirits And Wine, Bulk Sales And Bottling Of Distilled Spirits). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The use of distilled spirits or wine for experimental purposes and in the manufacture of (a) medicinal, pharmaceutical, or antiseptic products, including prescriptions compounded by retail druggists; (b) toilet preparations; (c) flavoring extracts, syrups, or food products; or (d) scientific, chemical, mechanical, or industrial products, provided such products are unfit for beverage use, is regarded as “industrial,” and will be excluded from any application of the term “nonindustrial use.”
