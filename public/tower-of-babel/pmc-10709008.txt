Rural recreation tourism in the Panxi region of China in the context of ecological welfare
Authors: Yanhong Wang, Lin Wang, Weiran Zhou, Qianbing Ying
Published: 2023

ABSTRACT
Urbanisation has improved living, health and welfare standards but degraded the ecological environment. Tourism models are constantly innovating, and the rural health model is attracting attention to rural recreation, and research on eco-welfare performance and tourism efficiency in ethnic minority rural areas is an important foundation for the construction of ecological civilisation and sustainable development in ethnic minority rural areas. The Panxi region of China has abundant sunshine, mild winters and a good ecological environment, and the rich tourism resources are stimulating rural health tourism. Herein, we used the Super-SBM model to measure eco-welfare performance and rural recreation and tourism efficiency in this region, then used the coupling coordination model to assess coupled co-scheduling of eco-welfare performance and rural health tourism efficiency, the Tobit model was constructed to test the influencing factors of the coupling coordination degree of eco-welfare performance and rural health tourism efficiency in Panxi region. The results show that the growth rate of social fixed asset investment, industrial structure and social development level have a positive effect on the coupling and coordination of eco-welfare performance and rural recreation and tourism efficiency in Panxi, while population density and ecological civilization degree have a negative effect on them. Therefore, the development of rural recreation tourism in Panxi can be promoted by playing a guiding role of the government, promoting the transformation of industrial structure, establishing citizens' awareness of green environmental protection and improving the efficiency of natural resources utilization.

Introduction

China's pursuit of rapid economic development, but the long-term extensive management and development model has gradually deteriorated the ecological environment, thereby affecting the living and welfare level of the people, sustainable development is an inevitable requirement for building a community of human destiny. Eco-welfare performance reflects the level of regional sustainable development, which is based on the consumption of ecological resources as inputs and human welfare as outputs, through exploring the characteristics of the changes between the two to achieve the minimisation of resource depletion and the maximisation of human welfare, and exploring the changes in the eco-welfare performance is of great significance to the protection of the ecological environment and the enhancement of people's well-being. Ecological and environmental problems not only affect sustainable economic and social development, but will also affect sustainable human development. The Twentieth Party Congress emphasised the importance of enhancing the people's well-being and the importance of actively promoting economic development and environmental protection is self-evident. The Central Committee of the Communist Party of China and the State Council issued the ‘Health China 2030’ planning outline, promote the integration of health and elderly care, tourism and leisure, and initiate new health industries [1]. The Party and the State have always been highly concerned about the physical welfare of citizens, with China's ecological environment, the aging of the population is becoming more and more prominent, this provides great development space for the health tourism industry, people's demand for recreation and tourism is increasing day by day, which is conducive to the citizens' physical conditioning. The State has introduced policies conducive to the development of tourism; for example, in June 2021, China's culture and tourism sector released the ‘14th Five-Year Plan’ for the development of culture and tourism, which proposed to improve the modern tourism system [2]. In May 2022, the State Council issued the 14th Five-Year Plan for National Health, emphasising the need to promote the integration of healthy ageing and tourism and fitness and leisure industries, and expand new forms and models of health [3]. Local policies such as the 14th Five-Year Plan for the Transformation and Upgrading of the Panxi Economic Zone proposed to build a cultural tourism economic belt in the region linked to recreation culture, ethnic culture and ‘Third Line’ culture, promote the deep integration of cultural tourism, and build a world-renowned sunshine and recreation tourism destinatio [4]. The development of rural health tourism in the Panxi region has received catalytic support from policies. Few previous studies on the sustainable development of ethnic minority tourism regions have been conducted from the perspective of system coupling of eco-welfare performance and tourism efficiency. The mutual promotion between the two systems of rural recreation tourism efficiency and eco-welfare performance relies on the efficient functioning of each system, which is also the key to the realisation of the goal of sustainable development of ethnic minority tourism regions.

Literature review

Ecological welfare

Eco-welfare is the human welfare derived from ecological inputs, and eco-welfare performance is the ratio between the level of welfare and the amount of resources consumed. This concept was first introduced by Daly in 1974 [5]. Eco-welfare performance can be evaluated by calculating human welfare per unit of natural depletion, thus assessing the sustainable development status of nations. Further research on eco-welfare performance was developed after introduction of the ecological footprint concept by Rees in 1992 [6]. Domestic scholars Zhu et al. [7] proposed that eco-welfare performance includes indicators of increasing human welfare, and studied its relationship with economic growth, showing that eco-welfare performance is equal to the ratio between the HDI and the ecological footprint. Moran et al. [8] argued that sustainable development is predicated on the carrying capacity of ecological resources and the environment, and defines eco-welfare performance as the efficiency of the level of human welfare resulting from ecological consumption.

Currently, research on eco-welfare performance mainly focuses on two aspects:(1)Measuring the eco-welfare performance of a region to analyse its changes. There are three main methods of measurement. One is the ratio method, Common based on ratio of human satisfaction to ecological inputs [9]. Abdallah et al. [10] used subjective and objective indicators to measure wellbeing as eco-welfare performance. Some foreign scholars have also used the ratio method to calculate ecological welfare including Jorgenson [11] and Kubiszewski et al. [12]. Behjat and Tarazkar [13] measured the eco-welfare performance in Iran from 1994 to 2014 as the ratio of HDI and ecological footprint, and showed that the level of eco-welfare performance in Iran decreases during this period. The second is the parametric method, represented by stochastic frontier analysis (SFA), which requires prior determination of functional relationships. For example, Dietz et al. [14] used this approach method to construct an eco-welfare performance index, whereas Xiao and Xiao [15] used the this approach to measure the eco-welfare performance of 79 cities in the Yellow River Basin from 2004 to 2017. The third, in contrast to the second, is a non-parametric approach, represented by Data Envelopment Analysis (DEA), which does not require prior determination of functional relationships. For example, Wang and Feng [16], Sun et al. [17] both used DEA models to measure the eco-welfare performance of Chinese cities. Zhang et al. [18]used the SBM-DEA model to examine the EWP of 102 countries, and the results showed that the EWP of countries with higher economic levels was much higher than that of countries with poorer economic levels. Zhao et al. [19]used the Super-EBM model for quantitative measurement of eco-welfare performance in China under constant payoffs of scale in order to differentiate effective decision units. Long and Wang [20], Li and Hu [21], and Bao et al. [22] measured the eco-welfare performance of cities in Shanghai, Jiangxi Province, and the Yangtze River Economic Belt in China using the Super-SBM model, respectively, and analyzed the differences between these cities.(2)Examining the influencing factors of eco-welfare performance in a certain region. Knight and Rosa [23] the eco-welfare performance of 105 countries was studied, and the results showed that climate, policies and economics can have an impact on it. Ren et al. [24] used the DEA method to compare the fficiency of economic transformation and the efficiency of ecological transformation in East China from the perspective of city clusters, and found that the evolutionary patterns of different urban agglomerations were very different. Bai et al. [25] used super DEA model to measure the eco-efficiency of 281 prefecture-level cities in China during the period of 2006–2013, and then used spatial econometrics to explore the relationship between urbanization and urban eco-efficiency. The results showed that urban eco-efficiency increased then fell then increased again as the level of urbanisation increased, and higher technology and good environmental policies can have a facilitating effect on urban eco-efficiency. Chen et al. [26] found that the technology level and urbanization rate were found to have a significant positive effect on the ecological welfare performance of cities in the Yellow River Basin. Wang and Wang [27] used impulse response functions and variance decomposition to analyse the extent to which scale effects, structural effects and technology effects impact eco-welfare performance in the Yangtze River Economic Belt. Li et al. [28] measured the eco-welfare performance of provincial-level cities in China from 2001 to 2015, and used spatial autocorrelation methods, the study showed that technological progress, medical care level and urbanization degree are the ones that have a greater impact.

Rural recreation tourism

Rural tourism originated in Europe in the 1830s [29]. The earliest definition of the concept of rural tourism was reported by academics who considered it a tourism activity that relies on rural households to provide services for tourists to experience rural life in rural areas [30]. Others argue that rural tourism has a rural regional character different from traditional tourism [31]. Hall [32] and Smith [33] pointed out that health tourism relies on favourable climatic conditions and ecological environment, including disease prevention, physical health improvement, mental balance, and cultural and leisure tourism related to health. Health tourism has also been defined as health and wellness tourism when interpreting the ‘National Health Tourism Demonstration Standards’ [34]. Rural recreation tourism draws on the ecological resources of the countryside to achieve physical wellbeing, recuperation and healthy ageing. Wen [35] defines the concept of rural health tourism as activities that promote health by drawing on the resources and environmental conditions of the countryside for holiday, relaxation and wellness. The term “efficiency” was first proposed by the famous economist Farrell, referring to the process of obtaining maximum output with minimal input in economic activities [36]. The efficiency of rural health tourism refers to the expected increase in output brought about by the integration of tourism and health development in a good resource environment in rural areas. Hartline et al. [37] were among the first to study tourism efficiency.

Most previous research on rural tourism efficiency has focused on two areas:(1)There are several main methods for calculating the efficiency of rural tourism, as follows. The first is DEA. For example, Aivaz and Vancea [38] used DEA to evaluate the productivity of tourism services, while Wang [39] used the DEA to calculate the efficiency of rural tourism in Fujian Province and analyzed both its scale and technical efficiencies. Liu et al. [40] used the SBM-DEA and ML index to measure the efficiency of poverty alleviation through tourism in concentrated and contiguous impoverished rural areas in China. The results showed that resource allocation and management capabilities were uneven among different regions. The second method is SFA. Liu et al. [41] used SFA to calculate the efficiency of rural tourism in the Nanjiang region of Sichuan, and quantitatively analyzed the impact of different influencing factors on the land use efficiency of different development types of villages in the region. The third is the empirical analysis method, as used by Fleischer and Pizam [42] for empirical analysis of the support of Israeli residents for rural tourism enterprises; the results showed that citizens are objectively rewarded for their support.(2)Study of the factors influencing the efficiency of rural tourism in a given region. For example, Jiang et al. [43] found that the efficiency of rural tourism in remote suburban counties of Beijing grows with GDP per capita and expenditure on agriculture, forestry and water affairs. Yu et al. [44] used the ‘cold and hot spot’ analysis method to measure the tourism efficiency of tourist villages in Luoyang; the study found that the reachability of transportation and the attractiveness of human settlements were not significant for the comprehensive efficiency of rural tourism, while population density and poor village factors were of great significance.

Relationship between eco-welfare performance and rural health tourism efficiency

Economic development requires investment in the ecological environment. Humans input material, financial, status and other welfare feedback obtained from the ecological environment into maintaining the ecological environment to improve eco-welfare performance, and improved eco-welfare performance will likely stimulate further human protect ecosystem. Good ecosystems provide a market for rural recreational tourism, thereby making it more efficient. Improving rural health tourism efficiency will further stimulate human awareness of protecting the ecological environment, reduce environmental pollution, promote regional economic development, and increase human welfare. Overview of the study area

Panxi is a combination of the two place names Panzhihua and Xichang. The unique climate conditions, favourable geographical location, good ecological environment, abundant health resources and sufficient medical conditions in the Panxi region are all advantages for the development of rural health tourism.

Geographic and economic factors

Geographic location

The Panxi region is in the southwestern part of Sichuan Province, China, covering area of 63,600 square kilometres with a population of 4,515,000. It includes the famous Panxi Rift Valley and Anning River Plain, covering 13 counties (cities and districts) in Panzhihua City, Liangshan Yi Autonomous Prefecture and Ya'an City. It is an appropriate area for development of the ecological rural health industry in China. The G5 (Beijing Kunming Expressway) in the Panxi region runs through the entire area, and the Chengdu to Wuding section in Yunnan has been opened to traffic. The direct flight from Chengdu to Panzhihua takes about 1 h, while the high-speed railway from Chengdu to Panzhihua takes about 2.5 h. Multiple expressways such as Yaxi and Panzhihua run through the entire Sichuan.

Comfortable climate conditions

Panzhihua is in the southern subtropical zone and is known as a “three-dimensional climate with a southern subtropical base”. It has distinct dry and rainy seasons. The microclimate is complex and varied with long sunshine and strong radiation. The average annual temperature is 20.3 °C, the number of sunshine hours is 2–3 times that of the Sichuan basin, the altitude is below 1500 m in the river valley, there is no winter throughout the year, the frost-free period lasts more than 300 days throughout the year, and it is known as a large ‘natural greenhouse’. Liangshan Prefecture has a subtropical monsoon climate with distinct wet and dry seasons and a remarkable three-dimensional climate.

Rich ecological resources

Panxi is very rich in species resources, with over 6000 species recorded. The solar energy and solar temperature production potential of crops in Panxi are the highest in the province, and the quality of characteristic agricultural products is excellent. During the 13th Five-Year Plan period, the regional GDP of the economic zone increased from 224 billion yuan in 2015 to 277.4 billion yuan in 2020, the value added to industry reached 94.47 billion yuan, the volume of subtropical fruits, winter and spring vegetables, and other special agricultural products doubled, and the value added to agriculture reached 50.36 billion yuan. The output value of the tertiary sector reached 115.24 billion RMB, the local general public budget revenue increased to 22.85 billion RMB, the forest coverage rate increased to 52 %, the ratio of good urban air quality days reached 95.8 %, and the ecological flow guarantee target for key rivers and lakes being exceeded 90 % [4].

Research methods

Super-SBM model

The Super-SBM model proposed by Tone (2001) was used [48]. When conducting DEA, the input-output efficiency is considered, but sometimes there will be more unexpected outputs. In such cases, the DEA model does not meet the requirements of a monotonic linear relationship. The traditional DEA model, ignores the input-output slack problem, which may lead to the measurement of the results due to radial and angular errors, thus affecting the accuracy of the efficiency measurement, the Super-SBM model used in this paper has the advantage of not only avoiding the bias caused by radial and angular metrics, but also taking into account the impact of the output of the non-desired factors in the production process, in the calculation of the eco-welfare performance, not only to take into account the human welfare desired outputs, but also take into account the non-desired outputs of pollution to the bad environment, the model is shown in equation (1):(1)minp*=1n∑n=1Nxnl−snxxnlxn11H+Q(∑h=1Hyhl+shyyhl+∑q=1Qbql+sqbbql)s.t.∑k=1Kzkyhk−shy=yhl,h=1,...,H;∑k=1Kzkbqk+sqb=bql,q=1,...,Q;∑k=1Kzkxnk+snx=xnl,n=1,...,N;zk≥0,shy≥0,snx≥0,k=1,...,K(k≠l)where k is the decision-making unit (DMU); xnk is the amount of investment; yhk is expected output; bqk is unexpected output; zk is weighted by DMU; snx is the relaxation variable representing inputs; shy is the relaxed variable representing expected output; and sqb is the relaxed variable representing unexpected outputs. A calculated efficiency value ≥ 1 indicates effective development whereas an efficiency value is < 1 indicates poor development and a loss of efficiency loss in DMU. It is necessary to make corresponding improvements in inputs and outputs.

Coupling coordination model

The main purpose of the coupled coordination degree model is to make two or more systems interact with each other and make leveraging resources to improve productivity. The model is represented in equation (2):(2){C=(2U1U2U1+U2)D=CT,T=αU1+βU2where C is the coupling degree of the two systems of eco-welfare performance and rural recreation tourism efficiency in Panxi region; U1 is the eco-welfare performance after normalisation treatment; U2 is the efficiency of rural health tourism after normalisation; D is the degree of coupling coordination; T is the composite evaluation index; and a and b are the importance of the two subsystems. As eco-welfare performance and rural health efficiency are equally important, α = β = 0.5 is used.

Tobit model

Tobin (1958) proposed the Tobit model, which is characterized by a truncation of the dependent variable [49]. Thus, in some cases Yi will be limited, and associated with Xi, so there is a linear regression model as equation (3):(3)Yi=β0+βT×Xi+Miwhere i = 1, 2, 3, Yi is the dependent variable; Xi is the explanatory variable; βT is an unknown parameter vector; and Mi∼N(0,σ2) is the disturbance term.

Ecological civilisation degree (X3), eco-welfare performance and rural recreation tourism efficiency coupling coordination showed a negative effect with a significant p-value of 0.005***, showing significance at the level. Generally speaking, the more urban green construction area, the better air quality will be for people's health level, which is beneficial for improving eco-welfare performance. However, from the Tobit regression results, it indicates that the green coverage of Panxi region is insufficient and has not improved the level of residents' health welfare. Therefore, it is necessary to increase the construction of green coverage rate in Panxi region.

Population density (X4) has a negative effect with a significant p-value of 0.000*** and does not show significance at the level. This indicates that the current population density in the Panxi region has no discernible impact on the coupling between eco-welfare performance and rural health tourism efficiency. Generally speaking, the greater the population concentration, the greater the pollution discharge, and the high concentration of population will lead to traffic congestion. The per capita enjoyment of public resources will decrease, gradually reducing the happiness of residents' lives, and thus reducing the eco-welfare performance of the Panxi region. Therefore, it is necessary to avoid excessive population density in the Panxi region, reasonably planning for the development of urbanization in Panxi, and reduce the possibility of vicious cycles in various systems.

The level of social development (X5) shows a positive effect on the Panxi region, with a significance p-value of 0.058 *, showing significance at the level. The construction of towns and cities will lead to accelerated population movements. Optimize the allocation of resource elements, promote the improvement of residents' living welfare, The rapid development of urbanization level will be in sharp contrast to the construction of rural areas, that the quality of rural ecosystems will be more attractive to the population, the construction of urbanization will to promote the development of rural recreation and tourism. This is mainly due to the fact that the perceptions citizens have changed, and they tend to pay more attention to spiritual pursuits, which provides a lot of room for the development of rural recreational tourism.

Conclusions and recommendations

Conclusions

This article is based on the Super-SBM model method to calculates the eco-welfare performance and rural health tourism efficiency in the Panxi region from 2016 to 2020. Then, the coupling coordination between the two was then further calculated using the coupling coordination model, and the Tobit model was further used to examine influencing factors. The results show that: (1) the overall eco-welfare performance of Panxi is high, in which the mean value of eco-welfare performance of Panzhihua City is 1.0235, Xichang City is 0.9718, and the mean value of eco-welfare performance of Panxi is 0.99765, and the ecological welfare level of Panxi is in the state of stable increase. (2) The efficiency of rural recreation tourism in Panzhihua City holds an increasing state, while Xichang gradually decreases, indicating that Xichang's development in this area has been constrained. (3) The influence of the growth rate of investment in fixed assets, industrial structure and social development level on the coupling of eco-welfare performance and rural recreation tourism efficiency in Panxi is positive, while population density and the degree of ecological civilisation are negative, and pure economic growth cannot bring about an increase in the coupling coordination of the two systems in Panxi, and a high degree of urbanisation leads to an increase in the coupling of eco-welfare performance and rural recreation tourism efficiency in Panxi, and technological advances have not worked for them.

The development of tourism in the Panxi region is supported by excellent and rich cultural and ecological resources, as well as medical resources and policies. By improving the coupling of eco-welfare performance and rural recreation tourism efficiency in the Panxi region, we can jointly create a rural recreation tourism destination in the Panxi region and promote the development of high-quality rural recreation tourism in the Panxi region under a better ecological welfare perspective, thereby contributing to social and economic development. To study the influencing factors of the coupled coordination degree of eco-welfare performance and rural recreation tourism efficiency in Panxi region of China, which is conducive to the coordinated development of the ecological environment and economy in Panxi region, and at the same time can also be used as a reference for the economic development of other ethnic minority regions.

Recommendations

Allowing guidance from the government

Government guidance plays a major role in the development of rural health tourism in the Panxi region. To develop rural health tourism, it is necessary to make long-term plans for the rural, recreational tourism, meet the diverse needs of tourists, strengthen investment in the rural health tourism industry, improve infrastructure, and create characteristic rural health tourism. The Tobit model shows that the degree of ecological civilisation and population density have a negative impact on the, coupled coordination of rural health tourism efficiency and ecological welfare performance in Panxi region, while the level of social development has a positive effect. The government should reasonably plan the population concentration while promoting urbanization development, and increase the green space coverage rate in the Panxi region.

Promoting the transformation of industrial structure

Strengthening the structural upgrading of secondary industry, changing the current crude development model, promoting the transformation of the economy from quantity to quality, accelerating the promotion of a circular economy and economic development can improve human welfare, but they can also pose a threat to the health of citizens. The characteristics of the Panxi region should be considered to development green and ecological leisure, as well as rural recreation and tourism. Tobit model tests showed that the influence of industrial structure on the eco-welfare performance and the coupling coordination of rural recreation and tourism in the Panxi region was positive; additionally, the secondary industries in both Panxi regions are developing rapidly, hence the government could introduce green and innovative industries and related advanced technologies, and prioritise promoting the development of green industries. The government could take on risky investment to alter the mindset of the enterprises concerned. Foreign enterprises with high pollution emissions are prohibited from entering the Panxi region, and the cost of reducing emissions is increased for existing high-polluting enterprises.

Promoting awareness of green environmental protection among citizens

Green space planning can improve the mental and physical welfare of citizens, as well as purifying air pollution. Tobit model tests showed that the growth rate of social investment as a whole has a positive effect on it. The natural ecology of the Panxi rural area is good, and in order to increase investment and construction in this area, it is necessary to establish awareness of green environmental protection among citizens, improve their knowledge of dealing with environmental issues, and fully leverage the role of schools, social media and various organisations. It is important to make people aware of the dangers of surface pollution of the environment, and make the natural ecological environment sustainable.

Improving the efficiency of natural resource use

To improve the coupling and coordination of eco-welfare performance and rural recreation tourism efficiency in the Panxi region, the eco-welfare performance and rural recreation tourism efficiency needs to be improved. A resource-sharing mechanism can be established in the Panxi region to increased utilization of resources, the Tobit model shows that population density has a negative impact. If population density is high, it is necessary to improve resource utilization, promote the recycling of resources, and mximize the use of land. The development of urbanisation is conducive to the coupling and coordination of eco-welfare performance and rural recreation tourism efficiency in the Panxi region; medical and educational standards can be improved and a habitable city can be created.
