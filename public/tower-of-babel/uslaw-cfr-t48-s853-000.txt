48 C.F.R. § 853.000 — 853.000 Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:48:853.000
Creator: Chapter 8—Department Of Veterans Affairs
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-853.000
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 853 (Forms). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part prescribes VA forms for use in the acquisition of goods and services. It only identifies forms that are used between VA and its contractors or the general public. It does not identify forms for uses internal to VA or between VA and another Federal agency.
