43 C.F.R. § 8224.0-6 — -6 Policy.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 8200 (Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No activities will be permitted within the Fossil Forest that would significantly disturb the land surface or impair the existing natural, educational, and scientific research values of the area.
