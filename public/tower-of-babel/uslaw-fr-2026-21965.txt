Alaska; Wildlife, Harvest, and Access; Correction

AGENCY: National Park Service, Interior.

ACTION: Final rule; correction.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: The National Park Service (NPS) is correcting a final rule 
that appeared in the Federal Register on October 19, 2026. The rule 
amended regulations for hunting, fishing, trapping, and other harvest-
related activities in Alaska and the procedures used to restrict public 
use and access in Alaska park areas, including for subsistence.

DATES: Effective November 18, 2026.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Don Striker, Regional Director, Alaska 
Regional Office, 240 West 5th Ave., Anchorage, AK 99501; phone (907) 
227-6163; email: [email protected]. Individuals in the United 
States who are

[[Page 67771]]

deaf, deafblind, hard of hearing, or have a speech disability may dial 
711 (TTY, TDD, or TeleBraille) to access telecommunications relay 
services. Individuals outside the United States should use the relay 
services offered within their country to make international calls to 
the point-of- contact in the United States.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: In FR Doc, 2026-21299 appearing on page 
65985 in the Federal Register of Monday, October 19, 2026, the 
following correction is made.


Sec.  13.420  [Corrected]

0
On page 65999, in the second column, in Sec.  13.420, in amendment 8, 
the instruction ``b. Revise the definitions for ``Handicraft''; and 
``Subsistence uses''.'' is corrected to read ``b. Revise the definition 
for ``Handicraft''; and the introductory text of the definition for 
``Subsistence uses''.''.
