46 U.S.C. § 44105 — Refusal of clearance
Citation: us:usc:46:44105
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section44105
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part C (Miscellaneous); Chapter 441 (Evidence Of Financial Responsibility For Passenger Transportation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Homeland Security shall refuse the clearance required by section 60105 of this title, at the port or place of departure from the United States, of a vessel that is subject to this chapter and does not have evidence issued by the Federal Maritime Commission of compliance with sections 44102 and 44103 of this title.
