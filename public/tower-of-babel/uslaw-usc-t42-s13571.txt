42 U.S.C. § 13571 — Purposes
Citation: us:usc:42:13571
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section13571
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 134 (Energy Policy); Subchapter XIII (Clean Air Coal Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The purposes of this subchapter are to—
(1)
promote national energy policy and energy security, diversity, and economic competitiveness benefits that result from the increased use of coal;
(2)
mitigate financial risks, reduce the cost of clean coal generation, and increase the marketplace acceptance of clean coal generation and pollution control equipment and processes; and
(3)
facilitate the environmental performance of clean coal generation.
