49 C.F.R. § 271.215 — Safety outreach process.
Citation: us:cfr:49:271.215
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-271.215
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 271 (Risk Reduction Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An RRP plan shall describe a railroad's processes for communicating safety information to railroad personnel and management pursuant to § 271.107.
