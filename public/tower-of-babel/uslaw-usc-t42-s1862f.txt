42 U.S.C. § 1862f — Research center consortia
Citation: us:usc:42:1862f
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1862f
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 16 (National Science Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In Foundation programs making grants to research centers, the Director shall encourage the formation of consortia that include research universities, two-year and four-year colleges, and the private sector.
