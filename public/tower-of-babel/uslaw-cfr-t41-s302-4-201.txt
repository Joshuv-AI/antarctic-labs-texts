41 C.F.R. § 302-4.201 — -4.201 Determination of authorized en route travel days for relocation travel.
Citation: us:cfr:41:302-4.201
Creator: Chapter 302—Relocation Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-302-4.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 302-4 (4—Allowances For Subsistence And Transportation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The authorized en route travel days are determined by dividing the total miles between the old residence and the new official station by the minimum driving distance the agency sets consistent with § 302-4.400 and rounding up to the next whole day.
