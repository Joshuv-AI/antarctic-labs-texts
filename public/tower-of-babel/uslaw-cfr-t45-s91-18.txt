45 C.F.R. § 91.18 — Age distinctions contained in HHS regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:45:91.18
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-91.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 91 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Age In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance From Hhs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any age distinctions contained in a rule or regulation issued by HHS shall be presumed to be necessary to the achievement of a statutory objective of the program or activity to which the rule or regulation applies, notwithstanding the provisions of § 91.13.
