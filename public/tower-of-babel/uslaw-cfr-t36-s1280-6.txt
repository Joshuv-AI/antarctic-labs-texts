36 C.F.R. § 1280.6 — Service animals.
Citation: us:cfr:36:1280.6
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1280.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1280 (Use Of Nara Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We allow service animals on or in NARA property and facilities in any area that the individual handling the service animal is otherwise entitled to enter. A service animal is any dog individually trained to do work or perform tasks for the benefit of an individual with a disability. You may not bring any other animals onto or into NARA property and facilities, except for official purposes.
