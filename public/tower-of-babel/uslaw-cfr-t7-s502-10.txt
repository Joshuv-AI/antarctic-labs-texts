7 C.F.R. § 502.10 — Photographs by visitors or for news, advertising, or commercial purposes.
Citation: us:cfr:7:502.10
Creator: Agricultural Research Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-502.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 502 (Conduct On Beltsville Agriculture Research Center Property, Beltsville, Maryland). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Photographs may be taken by visitors or for news purposes without prior permission. Photographs for advertising and commercial purposes may be taken at BARC only with the prior written approval of the Director, Beltsville Area.
