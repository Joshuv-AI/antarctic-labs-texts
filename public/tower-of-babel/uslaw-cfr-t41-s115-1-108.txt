41 C.F.R. § 115-1.108 — -1.108 Agency implementation and supplementation of FPMR.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-115-1.108
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 115-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) EPPMR implements and supplements the FPMR and follows the FPMR in style, arrangement and numbering sequence. Except to assure continuity and understanding FPMR material will not be repeated or paraphrased in the EPPMR.
(b) Implementing material expands upon related material in the FPMR. Supplementing material deals with subject material not covered in the FPMR.
