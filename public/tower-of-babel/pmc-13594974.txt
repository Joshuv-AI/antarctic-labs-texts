Expression of Concern on ‘ING1 regulates rRNA levels by altering nucleolar chromatin structure and mTOR localization’
Authors: Julian E Sale, Barry L Stoddard
Published: 2026

This is an Expression of Concern regarding: Uma Karthika Rajarajacholan, Subhash Thalappilly, Karl Riabowol, ING1 regulates rRNA levels by altering nucleolar chromatin structure and mTOR localization, Nucleic Acids Research, Volume 45, Issue 4, 28 February 2017, Pages 1776–1792, https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkw1161

In April 2026, the Editors were made aware of concerns regarding potential image irregularities in Figures 1B, 3H, 4D, and 5C of the above article, which were also raised on PubPeer (https://pubpeer.com/publications/A29F4B7E488D60D1F8E7106B186307). These concerns relate to the apparent duplication of PCR gel results and immunoblot bands within the article and with a figure previously published in a different journal article, under different experimental conditions and depicting a different protein.

The Editors have contacted the authors and have escalated to the institution for investigation in line with COPE guidance. In the interim, the Editors are publishing this Expression of Concern to alert readers while the outcome of the investigation is pending and advise readers to examine the details of this study with particular care.
