31 C.F.R. § 306.1 — Official agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:31:306.1
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-306.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 306 (General Regulations Governing U.S. Securities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Bureau of the Fiscal Service of the Department of the Treasury is charged with matters relating to transactions in securities. Correspondence concerning transactions in securities and requests for appropriate forms may be addressed to the Division of Customer Service, Parkersburg, WV 26102.
