8 C.F.R. § 1240.44 — Interpreter.
Citation: us:cfr:8:1240.44
Creator: Executive Office For Immigration Review, Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/section-1240.44
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 8 (Aliens and Nationality), Part 1240 (Proceedings To Determine Removability Of Aliens In The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person acting as interpreter in a hearing before an immigration judge under this part shall be sworn to interpret and translate accurately, unless the interpreter is an employee of the United States Government, in which event no such oath shall be required.
