12 U.S.C. § 1795i — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:12:1795i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1795i
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 14 (Federal Credit Unions); Subchapter III (Central Liquidity Facility). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The annual report required by section 1752a(d) of this title shall include a full report of the activities of the Facility.
