19 C.F.R. § 144.5 — Period of warehousing.
Citation: us:cfr:19:144.5
Creator: U.S. Customs And Border Protection, Department Of Homeland Security; Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-144.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 144 (Warehouse And Rewarehouse Entries And Withdrawals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Merchandise must not remain in a bonded warehouse beyond 5 years from the date of importation or such longer period of time as the Center director may at his discretion permit upon proper request being filed and good cause shown.
