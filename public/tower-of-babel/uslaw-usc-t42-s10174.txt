42 U.S.C. § 10174 — Consideration in siting facilities
Citation: us:usc:42:10174
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section10174
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 108 (Nuclear Waste Policy); Subchapter I (Disposal And Storage Of High-Level Radioactive Waste, Spent Nuclear Fuel, And Low-Level Radioactive Waste); Part G (Other Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, in siting Federal research projects, shall give special consideration to proposals from States where a repository is located.
