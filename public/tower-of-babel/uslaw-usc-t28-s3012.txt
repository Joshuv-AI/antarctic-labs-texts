28 U.S.C. § 3012 — Joinder of additional defendant
Citation: us:usc:28:3012
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section3012
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part VI (Particular Proceedings); Chapter 176 (Federal Debt Collection Procedure); Subchapter A (Definitions And General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The United States or the debtor may join as an additional defendant in an action or proceeding under this chapter any person reasonably believed to owe money (including money owed on account of a requirement to provide goods or services pursuant to a loan or loan guarantee extended under Federal law) to the debtor arising out of the transaction or occurrence giving rise to a debt.
