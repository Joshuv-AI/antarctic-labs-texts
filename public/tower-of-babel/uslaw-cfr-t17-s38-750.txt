17 C.F.R. § 38.750 — Core Principle 14.
Citation: us:cfr:17:38.750
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-38.750
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 38 (Designated Contract Markets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The board of trade shall establish and enforce rules regarding, and provide facilities for alternative dispute resolution as appropriate for, market participants and any market intermediaries.
