7 U.S.C. § 496 — Validity of other statutes dealing with same subject
Citation: us:usc:7:496
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section496
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 20 (Dumping Or Destruction Of Interstate Produce). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

This chapter shall not abrogate nor nullify any other statute, whether State or Federal, dealing with the same subjects as this chapter, but it is intended that all such statutes shall remain in full force and effect, except insofar only as they are inconsistent herewith or repugnant hereto.
