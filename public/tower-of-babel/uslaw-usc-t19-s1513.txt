19 U.S.C. § 1513 — Customs officer’s immunity
Citation: us:usc:19:1513
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section1513
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 4 (Tariff Act Of 1930); Part III (Ascertainment, Collection, And Recovery Of Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No customs officer shall be liable in any way to any person for or on account of—
(1)
any ruling or decision regarding the appraisement or the classification of any imported merchandise or regarding the duties, fees, and taxes charged thereon,
(2)
the collection of any dues, charges, duties, fees, and taxes on or on account of any imported merchandise, or
(3)
any other matter or thing as to which any person might under this chapter be entitled to protest or appeal from the decision of such officer.
