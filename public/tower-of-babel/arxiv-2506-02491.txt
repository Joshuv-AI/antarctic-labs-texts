On the Inversion Modulo a Power of an Integer
Guangwu Xu, Yunxiao Tian, Bingxin Yang

Abstract
Recently, Koç proposed a neat and efficient algorithm for computing x = a − 1 ( mod p k ) x=a^{-1}\pmod{p^{k}} for a prime p p based on the exact solution of linear equations using p p -adic expansions. The algorithm requires only addition and right shift per step. In this paper, we design an algorithm that computes x = a − 1 ( mod n k ) x=a^{-1}\pmod{n^{k}} for any integer n > 1 n>1 . The algorithm has a motivation from the schoolbook multiplication and achieves both efficiency and generality. The greater flexibility of our algorithm is explored by utilizing the build-in arithmetic of computer architecture, e.g., n = 2 64 n=2^{64} , and experimental results show significant improvements. This paper also contains some results on modular inverse based on an alternative proof of correctness of Koç algorithm. Key words: Multiplicative inverse, Modular arithmetic, Schoolbook multiplication.

Conclusions

In this paper, we develop method of computing inverse modulo a power of arbitrary integer bigger than
1
. In formulation, our algorithm is comparable with a recent algorithm of Koç for prime power, but with a motivation from the elementary schoolbook multiplication. Since the base (or radix) of our modulus is not restricted, we are able to compute
a^{-1}\pmod{n^{k}}
for suitable
n>1
. The experiment using
n=2^{64}
demonstrates a great performance over existing methods. The paper also describes an alternative proof of correctness of Koç algorithm and explains how to use the proof to derive
a^{-1}\pmod{n^{s}}
and
(n^{s})^{-1}\pmod{a}
for
s<k
from the procedure of
a^{-1}\pmod{n^{k}}
.
