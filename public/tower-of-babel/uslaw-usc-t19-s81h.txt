19 U.S.C. § 81h — Rules and regulations
Citation: us:usc:19:81h
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section81h
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 1A (Foreign Trade Zones). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Board shall prescribe such rules and regulations not inconsistent with the provisions of this chapter or the rules and regulations of the Secretary of the Treasury made hereunder and as may be necessary to carry out this chapter.
