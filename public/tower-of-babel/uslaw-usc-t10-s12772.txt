10 U.S.C. § 12772 — Reserve commissioned officers who have served as Attending Physician to the Congress: grade on transfer to Retired Reserve
Citation: us:usc:10:12772
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section12772
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel Generally); Chapter 1225 (Retired Grade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unless entitled to a higher grade under another provision of law, a reserve commissioned officer who is transferred to the Retired Reserve after having served in the position of Attending Physician to the Congress is entitled to be placed on the retired list established by section 12774(a) of this title in the grade held by the officer while serving in that position.
