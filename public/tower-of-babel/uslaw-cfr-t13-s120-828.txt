13 C.F.R. § 120.828 — Minimum level of 504 loan activity and restrictions on portfolio concentrations.
Citation: us:cfr:13:120.828
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-120.828
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 120 (Business Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A CDC is required to receive SBA approval of at least four 504 loan approvals during two consecutive fiscal years.
(b) A CDC's 504 loan portfolio must be diversified by business sector.
