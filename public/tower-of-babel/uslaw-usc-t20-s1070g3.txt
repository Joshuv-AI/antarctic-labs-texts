20 U.S.C. § 1070g–3 — Program period and funding
Citation: us:usc:20:1070g–3
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1070g–3
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 28 (Higher Education Resources And Student Assistance); Subchapter IV (Student Assistance); Part A (Grants To Students In Attendance At Institutions Of Higher Education); Subpart 9 (Teach Grants). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Beginning on July 1, 2008, there shall be available to the Secretary to carry out this subpart, from funds not otherwise appropriated, such sums as may be necessary to provide TEACH Grants in accordance with this subpart to each eligible applicant.
