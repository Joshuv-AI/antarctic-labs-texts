23 C.F.R. § 650.101 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:23:650.101
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-650.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 650 (Bridges, Structures, And Hydraulics). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To prescribe Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) policies and procedures for the location and hydraulic design of highway encroachments on flood plains, including direct Federal highway projects administered by the FHWA.
