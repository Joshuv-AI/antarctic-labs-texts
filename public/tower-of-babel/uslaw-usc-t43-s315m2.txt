43 U.S.C. § 315m–2 — Administration of leased lands
Citation: us:usc:43:315m–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section315m–2
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 8A (Grazing Lands); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The lands leased under sections 315m–1 to 315m–4 of this title shall be administered under the provisions of the Act of June 28, 1934 (48 Stat. 1269), as amended June 26, 1936 (49 Stat. 1976), commonly known as the Taylor Grazing Act.
