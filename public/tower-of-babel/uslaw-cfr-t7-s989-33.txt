7 C.F.R. § 989.33 — Alternate members.
Citation: us:cfr:7:989.33
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-989.33
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 989 (Raisins Produced From Grapes Grown In California). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The alternate for a member of the Committee shall act in the place and stead of such member (a) during his absence, and (b) in the event of his removal, resignation, disqualification, or death, until a successor for such member's unexpired term has been selected and has qualified.
