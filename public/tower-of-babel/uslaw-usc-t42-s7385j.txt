42 U.S.C. § 7385j — Coordination with other Federal radiation compensation laws
Citation: us:usc:42:7385j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section7385j
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 84 (Department Of Energy); Subchapter XVI (Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program); Part C (Treatment, Coordination, And Forfeiture Of Compensation And Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except in accordance with section 7384u of this title, an individual may not receive compensation or benefits under the compensation program for cancer and also receive compensation under the Radiation Exposure Compensation Act (42 U.S.C. 2210 note) or section 1112(c) of title 38.
