14 C.F.R. § 325.13 — Environmental evaluations and energy information not required.
Citation: us:cfr:14:325.13
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-325.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 325 (Essential Air Service Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Notwithstanding any provision of part 312 or part 313 of this chapter, a person filing a petition or appeal under this part is not required to file an environmental evaluation or energy information with the application.
