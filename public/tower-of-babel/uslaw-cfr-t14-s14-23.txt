14 C.F.R. § 14.23 — Reply.
Citation: us:cfr:14:14.23
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-14.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 14 (Rules Implementing The Equal Access To Justice Act Of 1980). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Within 15 days after service of an answer, the applicant may file a reply. If the reply is based on any alleged facts not already in the record of the proceeding, the applicant shall include with the reply either supporting affidavits or a request for further proceedings under § 14.26.
