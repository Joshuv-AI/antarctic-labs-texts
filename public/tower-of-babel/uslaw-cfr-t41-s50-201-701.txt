41 C.F.R. § 50-201.701 — -201.701 Definition of “person.”
Citation: us:cfr:41:50-201.701
Creator: Chapter 50—Public Contracts, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-50-201.701
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 50-201 (201—General Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Whenever used in the regulations in this part, the word person includes one or more individuals, partnerships, associations, corporations, legal representatives, trustees, trustees in bankruptcy, or receivers.
