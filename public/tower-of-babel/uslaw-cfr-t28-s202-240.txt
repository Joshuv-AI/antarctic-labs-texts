28 C.F.R. § 202.240 — Personal financial data.
Citation: us:cfr:28:202.240
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-202.240
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 202 (Access To U.S. Sensitive Personal Data And Government-Related Data By Countries Of Concern Or Covered Persons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term personal financial data means data about an individual's credit, charge, or debit card, or bank account, including purchases and payment history; data in a bank, credit, or other financial statement, including assets, liabilities, debts, or trades in a securities portfolio; or data in a credit report or in a “consumer report” (as defined in 15 U.S.C. 1681a(d)).
