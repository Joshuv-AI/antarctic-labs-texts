7 U.S.C. § 511k — Penalty for violations
Citation: us:usc:7:511k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section511k
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 21A (Tobacco Inspection). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any person violating any provision of sections 511d and 511i of this title shall be guilty of a misdemeanor and upon conviction thereof shall be fined not more than $1,000, or imprisoned not more than one year, or both.
