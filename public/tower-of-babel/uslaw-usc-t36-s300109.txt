36 U.S.C. § 300109 — Endowment fund
Citation: us:usc:36:300109
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section300109
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Chapter 3001 (The American National Red Cross). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The endowment fund of the corporation shall be kept and invested under the management and control of a board of trustees elected by the board of governors. The corporation shall prescribe policies and regulations on terms and tenure of office, accountability, and expenses of the board of trustees.
