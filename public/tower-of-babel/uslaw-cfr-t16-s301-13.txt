16 C.F.R. § 301.13 — Fur products having furs with different countries of origin.
Citation: us:cfr:16:301.13
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-301.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 301 (Rules And Regulations Under Fur Products Labeling Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When a fur product is composed of furs with different countries of origin the names of such countries shall be set forth in the required information in the order of predominance by surface areas of the furs in the fur product.
