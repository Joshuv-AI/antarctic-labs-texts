13 U.S.C. § 101 — Defective, dependent, and delinquent classes; crime
Citation: us:usc:13:101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title13-section101
Context: United States Code, Title 13 (Census); Chapter 3 (Collection And Publication Of Statistics); Subchapter V (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary may collect decennially statistics relating—
(1)
to the defective, dependent, and delinquent classes; and
(2)
to crime, including judicial statistics pertaining thereto.
(b)
The statistics authorized by subsection (a) of this section shall include information upon the following questions, namely: age, sex, color, nativity, parentage, literacy by race, color, nativity, and parentage, and such other questions relating to such subjects as the Secretary deems proper.
(c)
In addition to the decennial collections authorized by subsections (a) and (b) of this section, the Secretary may compile and publish annually statistics relating to crime and to the defective, dependent, and delinquent classes.
