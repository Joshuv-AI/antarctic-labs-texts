42 U.S.C. § 1548 — Rules and regulations; standards of safety, convenience, and health
Citation: us:usc:42:1548
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1548
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 9 (Housing Of Persons Engaged In National Defense); Subchapter IV (General Provisions Affecting Subchapters Ii To Vii). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Housing and Urban Development is authorized to make such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of subchapters II to VII of this chapter, and shall establish reasonable standards of safety, convenience, and health.
