17 C.F.R. § 37.1302 — Types of financial resources.
Citation: us:cfr:17:37.1302
Creator: Commodity Futures Trading Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/section-37.1302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 17 (Commodity and Securities Exchanges), Part 37 (Swap Execution Facilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Financial resources available to satisfy the requirements of § 37.1301 may include:
(a) The swap execution facility's own capital, meaning its assets minus its liabilities calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States; and
(b) Any other financial resource deemed acceptable by the Commission.
