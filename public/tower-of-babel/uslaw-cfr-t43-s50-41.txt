43 C.F.R. § 50.41 — What will the Secretary's decision include?
Citation: us:cfr:43:50.41
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-50.41
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 50 (Procedures For Reestablishing A Formal Government-To-Government Relationship With The Native Hawaiian Community). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The decision will respond to significant public comments and summarize the evidence, reasoning, and analyses that are the basis for the Secretary's determination regarding whether the request meets the criteria described in § 50.16 and is consistent with this part.
