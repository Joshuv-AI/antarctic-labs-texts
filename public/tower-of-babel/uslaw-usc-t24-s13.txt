24 U.S.C. § 13 — Admission of cases for study
Citation: us:usc:24:13
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title24-section13
Context: United States Code, Title 24 (Hospitals And Asylums); Chapter 1 (Navy Hospitals, Army And Navy Hospital, And Hospital Relief For Seamen And Others). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There may be admitted into marine hospitals for study persons with infectious or other diseases affecting the public health, and not to exceed ten cases in any one hospital at one time.
