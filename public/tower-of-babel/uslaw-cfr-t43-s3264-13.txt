43 C.F.R. § 3264.13 — What drilling and operational records must I maintain for each well?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3264.13
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3264.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3200 (Geothermal Resource Leasing). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must keep the following information for each well, and make it available for BLM to inspect, upon request:
(a) A complete and accurate drilling log, in chronological order;
(b) All other logs;
(c) Water or steam analyses;
(d) Hydrologic or heat flow tests;
(e) Directional surveys;
(f) A complete log of all subsequent well operations, such as cementing, perforating, acidizing, and well cleanouts; and
(g) Any other information regarding the well that could affect its status.
