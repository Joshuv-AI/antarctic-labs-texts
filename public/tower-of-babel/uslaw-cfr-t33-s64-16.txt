33 C.F.R. § 64.16 — Duration of marking on sunken vessels in navigable waters.
Citation: us:cfr:33:64.16
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-64.16
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 64 (Marking Of Structures, Sunken Vessels And Other Obstructions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Markings shall be maintained until:
(a) The sunken vessel or other obstruction is removed; or
(b) The right of the owner to abandon is legally established and exercised.
