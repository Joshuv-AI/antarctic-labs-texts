In a bipartite graph

In some sense, the size of a largest bi-hole in a bipartite graph corresponds to the “bipartite independence number” of that graph.
Axenovich, Sereni, Snyder, and Weber

They proved that the asymptotic behaviour of the function

\Theta\left(\frac{\ln\Delta}{\Delta}\cdot n\right)
