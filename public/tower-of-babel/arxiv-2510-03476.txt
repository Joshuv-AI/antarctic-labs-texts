Gross’s Conjecture: the dihedral case
Petar Bakić, Aleksander Horawa, Siyan Daniel Li-Huerta, Naomi Sweeting

Abstract
. Quaternionic modular forms on 𝖦 2 \mathsf{G}_{2} carry a surprisingly rich arithmetic structure. For example, they have a theory of Fourier expansions where the Fourier coefficients are indexed by totally real cubic rings. For quaternionic modular forms on 𝖦 2 \mathsf{G}_{2} associated via functoriality with certain modular forms on PGL 2 \mathrm{PGL}_{2} , Gross conjectured in 2000 that their Fourier coefficients encode L L -values of cubic twists of the modular form (echoing Waldspurger’s work on Fourier coefficients of half-integral weight modular forms). We prove Gross’s conjecture when the modular forms are dihedral, giving the first examples for which it is known.

1.3.
Related work

Fourier coefficients of cuspidal quaternionic modular forms have been extensively studied by Pollack; for example, he proved that, for all
k\geq 6
, there exists a basis of cuspidal quaternionic modular forms on
G
whose Fourier coefficients lie in
\mathbb{Q}^{\operatorname{ab}}

[
27

Let us explain why this is consistent with Theorem
1.2
. The projection formula yields

\displaystyle\operatorname{Ind}_{K}^{\mathbb{Q}}\chi\otimes\operatorname{Ind}_{E}^{\mathbb{Q}}\mathbbm{1}=\operatorname{Ind}_{KE}^{\mathbb{Q}}(\chi|_{KE}),

so we get
(\operatorname{Ind}_{K}^{\mathbb{Q}}\chi)\otimes V_{E}=\operatorname{Ind}_{KE}^{\mathbb{Q}}(\chi|_{KE})-\operatorname{Ind}_{K}^{\mathbb{Q}}\chi

\displaystyle L({\textstyle\frac{1}{2}},f\otimes V_{E})=\frac{L(\textstyle\frac{1}{2},\chi|_{KE})}{L(\frac{1}{2},\chi)}.

Blasius’s work
[
5
]
on Deligne’s conjecture shows that there are periods
c^{+}(\chi),c^{+}(\chi|_{KE})\in\mathbb{C}
such that

\displaystyle\frac{L(\textstyle\frac{1}{2},\chi)}{c^{+}(\chi)},\frac{L(\frac{1}{2},\chi|_{KE})}{c^{+}(\chi|_{KE})}\in\mathbb{Q}(\chi).

If the motive for
\chi
is realized in an abelian variety

with CM by
K
, then the motive for
\chi|_{KE}
is realized in

, so one can show that

. Therefore we get

\displaystyle\frac{L(\textstyle\frac{1}{2},f\otimes V_{E})}{c^{+}(\chi)^{2}}\in\mathbb{Q}(\chi)\subseteq\mathbb{Q}^{\operatorname{ab}}.

Because
c^{+}(\chi)^{2}
is independent of
E
, this yields the desired consistency with Pollack’s result.

Pollack
[
27
, Corollary 1.2.4]
also obtained the first result towards Gross’s conjecture: when

corresponds to the ring of integers of

Recently, assuming Arthur’s conjecture
