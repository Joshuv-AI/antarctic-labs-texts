47 C.F.R. § 52.3 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:47:52.3
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-52.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 52 (Numbering). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Commission shall have exclusive authority over those portions of the North American Numbering Plan (NANP) that pertain to the United States. The Commission may delegate to the States or other entities any portion of such jurisdiction.
