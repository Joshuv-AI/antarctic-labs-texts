31 C.F.R. § 596.503 — Financial transactions with a Terrorism List Government otherwise subject to 31 CFR chapter V.
Citation: us:cfr:31:596.503
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-596.503
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 596 (Terrorism List Governments Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

United States persons are authorized to engage in financial transactions with a Terrorism List Government that is subject to regulations contained in parts of 31 CFR chapter V other than this part to the extent and subject to the conditions stated in such other parts, or in any regulations, orders, directives, rulings, instructions, or licenses issued pursuant thereto.
