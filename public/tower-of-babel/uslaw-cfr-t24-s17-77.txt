24 C.F.R. § 17.77 — Stay of referral for offset.
Citation: us:cfr:24:17.77
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-17.77
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 17 (Administrative Claims). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the debtor timely submits evidence in accordance with § 17.69(b), the referral to the Treasury in § 17.65 shall be stayed until the date of the issuance of a written decision by an administrative judge of the OHA that determines that a debt or part of a debt is past due and legally enforceable.
