33 C.F.R. § 127.313 — Bulk storage.
Citation: us:cfr:33:127.313
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-127.313
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 127 (Waterfront Facilities Handling Liquefied Natural Gas And Liquefied Hazardous Gas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The operator must ensure that only the following flammable materials are stored in the marine transfer area for LNG:
(1) LNG.
(2) LPG.
(3) Vessel fuel.
(4) Oily waste from vessels.
(5) Solvents, lubricants, paints, and other fuels in the amount used for one day's operations and maintenance.
(b) Flammable liquids must be stored in accordance with NFPA 30 (incorporated by reference, see § 127.003).
