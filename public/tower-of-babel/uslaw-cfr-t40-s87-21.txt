40 C.F.R. § 87.21 — Exhaust emission standards for Tier 4 and earlier engines.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 87 (Control Of Air Pollution From Aircraft And Aircraft Engines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).
