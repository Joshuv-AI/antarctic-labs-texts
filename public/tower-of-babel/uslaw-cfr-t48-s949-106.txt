48 C.F.R. § 949.106 — 949.106 Fraud or other criminal conduct.
Citation: us:cfr:48:949.106
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-949.106
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 949 (Termination Of Contracts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any evidence of fraud or other criminal conduct in connection with the settlement of a contract termination shall be reported in accordance with 909.406.
