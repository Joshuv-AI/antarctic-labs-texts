29 C.F.R. § 452.42 — Membership in particular branch or segment of the union.
Citation: us:cfr:29:452.42
Creator: Office Of Labor-Management Standards, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-452.42
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 452 (General Statement Concerning The Election Provisions Of The Labor-Management Reporting And Disclosure Act Of 1959). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A labor organization may not limit eligibility for office to particular branches or segments of the union where such restriction has the effect of depriving those members who are not in such branch or segment of the right to become officers of the union. 27
