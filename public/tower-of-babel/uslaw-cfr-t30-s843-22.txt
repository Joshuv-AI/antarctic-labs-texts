30 C.F.R. § 843.22 — Enforcement actions at abandoned sites.
Citation: us:cfr:30:843.22
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-843.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 843 (Federal Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Office may refrain from issuing a notice of violation or cessation order for a violation at an abandoned site, as defined in § 842.11(e) of this chapter, if abatement of the violation is required under any previously issued notice or order.
