33 C.F.R. § 118.140 — Painting bridge piers.
Citation: us:cfr:33:118.140
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-118.140
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 118 (Bridge Lighting And Other Signals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The District Commander may require painting the sides of bridge channel piers below the superstructure facing traffic white or yellow when they are significantly darkened by weathering or other causes so as to be poorly visible against a dark background.
