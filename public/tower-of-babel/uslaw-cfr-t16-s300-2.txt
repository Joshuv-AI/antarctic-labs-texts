16 C.F.R. § 300.2 — General requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:16:300.2
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-300.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 300 (Rules And Regulations Under The Wool Products Labeling Act Of 1939). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each and every wool product subject to the act shall be marked by a stamp, tag, label, or other means of identification, in conformity with the requirements of the act and the rules and regulations thereunder.
