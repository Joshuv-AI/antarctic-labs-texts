7 C.F.R. § 42.101 — Meaning of words.
Citation: us:cfr:7:42.101
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-42.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 42 (Standards For Condition Of Food Containers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Words used in this part in the singular form shall be considered to import the plural, or vice versa, as the case may demand.
