36 U.S.C. § 22510 — Annual report
Citation: us:usc:36:22510
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section22510
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 225 (American War Mothers). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than January 1 of each year, the corporation shall submit a report to Congress on the activities of the corporation during the prior calendar year. The report may not be printed as a public document.
