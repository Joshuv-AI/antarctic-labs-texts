31 C.F.R. § 528.318 — United States person; U.S. person.
Citation: us:cfr:31:528.318
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-528.318
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 528 (International Criminal Court-Related Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term United States person or U.S. person means any United States citizen, permanent resident alien, entity organized under the laws of the United States or any jurisdiction within the United States (including a foreign branch, subsidiary, or employee of such entity), or any person lawfully in the United States.
