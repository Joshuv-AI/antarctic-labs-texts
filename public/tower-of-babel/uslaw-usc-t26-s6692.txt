26 U.S.C. § 6692 — Failure to file actuarial report
Citation: us:usc:26:6692
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section6692
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 68 (Additions To The Tax, Additional Amounts, And Assessable Penalties); Subchapter B (Assessable Penalties); Part I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The plan administrator (as defined in section 414(g)) of each defined benefit plan to which section 412 applies who fails to file the report required by section 6059 at the time and in the manner required by section 6059, shall pay a penalty of $1,000 for each such failure unless it is shown that such failure is due to reasonable cause.
