10 U.S.C. § 1136 — Honorable service requirement for award of military decorations
Citation: us:usc:10:1136
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1136
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 57 (Decorations And Awards). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No military decoration, including a medal, cross, or bar, or an associated emblem or insignia, may be awarded or presented to any person, or to a representative of the person, if the service of the person after the person distinguished himself or herself has not been honorable.
