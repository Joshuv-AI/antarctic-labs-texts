44 U.S.C. § 720 — Senate and House Manuals
Citation: us:usc:44:720
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section720
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 7 (Congressional Printing And Binding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Each House may order printed as many copies as it desires, of the Senate Manual and of the Rules and Manual of the House of Representatives, even though the cost exceed $500.
