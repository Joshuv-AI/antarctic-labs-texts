22 U.S.C. § 8516 — Authority to implement United Nations Security Council resolutions imposing sanctions with respect to Iran
Citation: us:usc:22:8516
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8516
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 92 (Comprehensive Iran Sanctions, Accountability, And Divestment); Subchapter I (Sanctions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In addition to any other authority of the President with respect to implementing resolutions of the United Nations Security Council, the President may prescribe such regulations as may be necessary to implement a resolution that is agreed to by the United Nations Security Council and imposes sanctions with respect to Iran.
