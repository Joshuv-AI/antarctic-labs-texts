15 U.S.C. § 715j — “President” as including agencies, officers and employees
Citation: us:usc:15:715j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section715j
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 15A (Interstate Transportation Of Petroleum Products). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Wherever reference is made in this chapter to the President such reference shall be held to include, in addition to the President, any agency, officer, or employee who may be designated by the President for the execution of any of the powers and functions vested in the President under this chapter.
