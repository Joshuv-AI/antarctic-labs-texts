24 C.F.R. § 213.267 — Effect of insurance endorsement.
Citation: us:cfr:24:213.267
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-213.267
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 213 (Cooperative Housing Mortgage Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

From the date of initial endorsement, the Commissioner and the mortgagee or lender shall be bound by the provisions of this subpart to the same extent as if they had executed a contract including the provisions of this subpart and the applicable sections of the Act.
