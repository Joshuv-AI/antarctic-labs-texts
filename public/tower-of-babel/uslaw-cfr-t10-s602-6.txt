10 C.F.R. § 602.6 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:10:602.6
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-602.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 602 (Epidemiology And Other Health Studies Financial Assistance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any individual or entity other than a Federal agency is eligible for a grant or cooperative agreement. An unaffiliated individual is also eligible for a grant or cooperative agreement.
