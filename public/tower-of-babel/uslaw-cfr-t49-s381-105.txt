49 C.F.R. § 381.105 — Who is required to comply with the rules in this part?
Citation: us:cfr:49:381.105
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-381.105
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 381 (Waivers, Exemptions, And Pilot Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must comply with the rules in this part if you are going to request a waiver or apply for an exemption.
(b) You should follow the instructions in subpart D of this part if you would like to recommend the agency initiate a pilot program.
