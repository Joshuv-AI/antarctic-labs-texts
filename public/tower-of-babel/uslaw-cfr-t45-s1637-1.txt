45 C.F.R. § 1637.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:45:1637.1
Creator: Legal Services Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-1637.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1637 (Representation Of Prisoners). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part is intended to ensure that recipients do not participate in any civil litigation on behalf of persons incarcerated in Federal, State or local prisons.
