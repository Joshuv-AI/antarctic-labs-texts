46 C.F.R. § 144.310 — Lifting requirements for a new vessel.
Citation: us:cfr:46:144.310
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-144.310
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 144 (Construction And Arrangement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each new vessel equipped for lifting must meet the requirements of part 173, subpart B, of this chapter.
