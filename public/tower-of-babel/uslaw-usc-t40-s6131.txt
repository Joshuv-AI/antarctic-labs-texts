40 U.S.C. § 6131 — Public travel in Supreme Court grounds
Citation: us:usc:40:6131
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section6131
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Part C (Federal Building Complexes); Chapter 61 (United States Supreme Court Building And Grounds); Subchapter IV (Prohibitions And Penalties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Public travel in, and occupancy of, the Supreme Court grounds is restricted to the sidewalks and other paved surfaces.
