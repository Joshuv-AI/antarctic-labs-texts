5 C.F.R. § 304.101 — Coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:5:304.101
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-304.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 304 (Expert And Consultant Appointments). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

These regulations apply to the appointment of experts and consultants as Federal employees under 5 U.S.C. 3109. They do not apply to the appointments of experts and consultants under other employment authorities or to the procurement of services by contracts under the procurement laws.
