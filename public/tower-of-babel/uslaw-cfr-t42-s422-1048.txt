42 C.F.R. § 422.1048 — Evidence.
Citation: us:cfr:42:422.1048
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-422.1048
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 422 (Medicare Advantage Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Evidence may be received at the hearing even though inadmissible under the rules of evidence applicable to court procedure. The ALJ rules on the admissibility of evidence.
