41 C.F.R. § 101-26.300 — -26.300 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:41:101-26.300
Creator: Chapter 101—Federal Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-101-26.300
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 101-26 (26—Procurement Sources And Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes policy and procedures governing the procurement by agencies of items of supply stocked by GSA, including reporting and obtaining adjustments for overages, shortages, and damages and the issue of used, repaired, and rehabilitated items in serviceable condition.
