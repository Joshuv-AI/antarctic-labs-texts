48 C.F.R. § 44.307 — 44.307 Reports.
Citation: us:cfr:48:44.307
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-44.307
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 44 (Subcontracting Policies And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The ACO shall distribute copies of CPSR reports; notifications granting, withholding, or withdrawing system approval; and Government recommendations for improvement of an approved system, including the contractor's response, to at least—
(a) The cognizant contract audit office;
(b) Activities prescribed by the cognizant agency; and
(c) The contractor (except that furnishing copies of the contractor's response is optional).
