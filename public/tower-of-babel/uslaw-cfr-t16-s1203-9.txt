16 C.F.R. § 1203.9 — Test headforms.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1203.9
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1203.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1203 (Safety Standard For Bicycle Helmets). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The headforms used for testing shall be selected from sizes A, E, J, M, and O, as defined by DRAFT ISO/DIS 6220-1983, in accordance with § 1203.10. Headforms used for impact testing shall be rigid and be constructed of low-resonance K-1A magnesium alloy.
