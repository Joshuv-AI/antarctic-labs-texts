39 C.F.R. § 3055.24 — Special Services.
Citation: us:cfr:39:3055.24
Creator: Postal Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-39/section-3055.24
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 39 (Postal Service), Part 3055 (Service Performance And Customer Satisfaction Reporting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

For each product within the Special Services group, report the percentage of time (rounded to one decimal place) that each product meets or exceeds its service standard.
