21 C.F.R. § 71.30 — Procedure for filing objections to regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:21:71.30
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-71.30
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 71 (Color Additive Petitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Objections and hearings relating to color additive regulations under section 721 (b) and (c) of the act shall be governed by parts 10, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, and 19 of this chapter.
(b) The fees specified in § 70.19 of this chapter shall be applicable.
