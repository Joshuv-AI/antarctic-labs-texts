27 C.F.R. § 19.596 — Processing records in general.
Citation: us:cfr:27:19.596
Creator: Alcohol And Tobacco Tax And Trade Bureau, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/section-19.596
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 27 (Alcohol, Tobacco Products and Firearms), Part 19 (Distilled Spirits Plants). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A proprietor who processes spirits must maintain daily records of transactions and operations in the processing account relating to:
(a) The manufacture of distilled spirits products;
(b) Finished products;
(c) The denaturation of spirits; and
(d) The manufacture of articles.
