20 U.S.C. § 3445 — Transfers from Department of Justice
Citation: us:usc:20:3445
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section3445
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 48 (Department Of Education); Subchapter III (Transfers Of Agencies And Functions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are transferred to the Secretary all functions of the Attorney General and of the Law Enforcement Assistance Administration with regard to the student loan and grant programs known as the law enforcement education program and the law enforcement intern program authorized by subsections (b), (c), and (f) of section 406 1 of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 1968.
