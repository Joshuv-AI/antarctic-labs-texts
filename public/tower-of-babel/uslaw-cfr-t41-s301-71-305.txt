41 C.F.R. § 301-71.305 — -71.305 Debt collection for unpaid travel advances.
Citation: us:cfr:41:301-71.305
Creator: Chapter 301—Temporary Duty (Tdy) Travel Allowances
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-301-71.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 301-71 (71—Agency Travel Accountability Requirements). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If an employee does not repay a travel advance when filing a travel claim, the agency should:
(a) Offset against the employee's salary, retirement credit, or other amounts owed to the employee;
(b) Deduct from any Government-owed amount; or
(c) Pursue any other legally permissible method of debt recovery.
