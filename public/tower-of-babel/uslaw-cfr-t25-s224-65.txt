25 C.F.R. § 224.65 — How may a Tribe assume additional activities under a TERA?
Citation: us:cfr:25:224.65
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-224.65
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 224 (Tribal Energy Resource Agreements Under The Indian Tribal Energy Development And Self Determination Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Tribe may assume additional activities related to the development of the same type of energy resource included in a TERA by negotiating with the Secretary an amendment to the existing TERA to include the additional activities.
