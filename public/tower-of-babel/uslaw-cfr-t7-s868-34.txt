7 C.F.R. § 868.34 — Partial inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:7:868.34
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Federal Grain Inspection Service, Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-868.34
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 868 (General Regulations And Standards For Certain Agricultural Commodities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When the entire lot is not accessible for examination or a representative sample cannot be obtained from the entire lot, the certificate shall state the estimated quantity of the commodity in the accessible portion and the quantity of the entire lot. The inspection shall be limited to the accessible portion. In addition, the words “Partial Inspection” shall be printed or stamped on the certificate.
