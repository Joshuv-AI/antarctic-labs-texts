31 U.S.C. § 306 — Fiscal Service

Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section306
Context: United States Code, Title 31 (Money And Finance); Chapter 3 (Department Of The Treasury); Subchapter I (Organization). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Fiscal Service is a service in the Department of the Treasury.
(b)
The head of the Fiscal Service is the Fiscal Assistant Secretary appointed under section 301(d) of this title.
(c)
The Fiscal Service has a—
(1)
Bureau of Government Financial Operations, having as its head a Commissioner of Government Financial Operations; and
(2)
Bureau of the Public Debt, having as its head a Commissioner of the Public Debt.
(d)
The Secretary of the Treasury may designate another officer or employee of the Department to act as the Fiscal Assistant Secretary when the Fiscal Assistant Secretary is absent or unable to serve or when the office of Fiscal Assistant Secretary is vacant.
