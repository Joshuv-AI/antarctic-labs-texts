2 U.S.C. § 5521 — Compensation of Chaplain of House
Citation: us:usc:2:5521
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section5521
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 55 (House Of Representatives Officers And Administration); Subchapter II (Chaplain). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The maximum per year gross rate of compensation of the Chaplain of the House of Representatives shall not exceed the greater of $173,900 or the rate of pay in effect for such position under an order issued by the Speaker of the House of Representatives pursuant to the authority of section 4532 of this title.
