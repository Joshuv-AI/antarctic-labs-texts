ASTRO's Advances in Radiation Oncology Outstanding Reviewers for 2023
Authors: Rachel B Jimenez
Published: 2024

In 2020, it was estimated that the doubling time of medical knowledge was a mere 73 days. In 2024, advances in the field of radiation oncology continue apace and our journal remains dedicated to keeping up. Our success in this endeavor is largely owed to the gifts of time, care, and diligence bestowed on our journal by our thoughtful peer reviewers and editorial board members. At a time of increasing clinician burnout and competing demands, our reviewers and editors selflessly donate their expertise to enhance scientific content and facilitate dissemination of needed insights to our entire radiation oncology community. We remain truly grateful to each of our peer reviewers and extend our particularly thanks to those providing outsize contributions this year.
