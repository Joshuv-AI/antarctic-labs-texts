22 C.F.R. § 142.9 — Administrative requirements for small recipients.
Citation: us:cfr:22:142.9
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-142.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 142 (Nondiscrimination On The Basis Of Handicap In Programs Or Activities Receiving Federal Financial Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary may require any recipient with fewer than fifteen employees, or any class of such recipients, to comply with § 142.7, in whole or in part, when the Secretary finds a violation of this part or finds that such compliance will not significantly impair the ability of the recipient or class of recipients to provide benefits or services.
