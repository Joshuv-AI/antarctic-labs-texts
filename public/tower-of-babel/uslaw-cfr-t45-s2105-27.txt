45 C.F.R. § 2105.27 — When will the Agency not notify a submitter of a request for their possibly confidential information?
Citation: us:cfr:45:2105.27
Creator: Commission Of Fine Arts
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-2105.27
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 2105 (Requirements For Compliance With The Freedom Of Information Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The notice requirements of § 2105.26 will not apply if:
(a) The information has been lawfully published or officially made available to the public; or
(b) Disclosure of the information is required by a statute other than the FOIA or by a regulation (other than this part) issued in accordance with the requirements of Executive Order 12600.
