47 C.F.R. § 32.17 — Interpretation of accounts.
Citation: us:cfr:47:32.17
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-32.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 32 (Uniform System Of Accounts For Telecommunications Companies). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the end that uniform accounting shall be maintained within the prescribed system, questions involving significant matters which are not clearly provided for shall be submitted to the Chief, Wireline Competition Bureau, for explanation, interpretation, or resolution. Questions and answers thereto with respect to this system of accounts will be maintained by the Wireline Competition Bureau.
