11 C.F.R. § 105.1 — Place of filing (52 U.S.C. 30102(g), 30104(g)).

Creator: Federal Election Commission

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 11 (Federal Elections), Part 105 (DOCUMENT FILING (52 U.S.C. 30102(g))). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All designations, statements, reports, and notices, as well as any modification(s) or amendment(s) thereto, required to be filed under the Act shall be filed in original form with, and received by, the Commission as defined in § 1.2.
