42 U.S.C. § 1003 — Residence outside the United States
Citation: us:usc:42:1003
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1003
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 7 (Social Security); Subchapter VIII (Special Benefits For Certain World War Ii Veterans). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of section 1001 of this title, with respect to any month, an individual shall be regarded as residing outside the United States if, on the first day of the month, the individual so resides outside the United States.
