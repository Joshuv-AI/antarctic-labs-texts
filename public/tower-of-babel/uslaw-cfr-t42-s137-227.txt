42 C.F.R. § 137.227 — How much time does the Secretary have to act on a waiver request?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.227
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.227
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Secretary must either approve or deny the requested waiver in writing within 90 days after receipt by the Secretary.
