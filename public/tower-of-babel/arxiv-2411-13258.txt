Path-length dependence of parton and jet energy loss from universal scaling laws
Guillaume Falmagne

Abstract

The theory of parton energy loss in quark-gluon plasma (QGP) and its associated jet quenching phenomenology in heavy ion collisions has evolved significantly over the past decade, driven by precise measurements from RHIC and LHC. Despite these advancements, fundamental questions remain, such as how parton energy loss in QGP depends on the medium path-length
L
. In these proceedings, we discuss how this question can be addressed through a data-driven strategy. More specifically, we will exploit the scaling properties of large-
p_{{}_{\perp}}
hadron spectra in heavy ion collisions, predicted by a simple analytic energy loss model and observed in experimental data. In addition, we show that
jet measurements
obey one such scaling law, offering the possibility to study jet energy loss in QGP within the same strategy.

In order to go a step further, it is instructive to relate the
\bar{\epsilon}
to the relevant physical quantities in heavy ion collisions. In the BDMPS formalism,
\bar{\epsilon}
should scale according to
[
2
]

\bar{\epsilon}\propto\langle z\rangle\,C_{k}\,\hat{q}_{0}\,\tau_{0}^{\alpha}\,L^{2-\alpha}\,,

(2)

where
\langle z\rangle
is the momentum fraction carried by the hadron,
C_{k}
is the color charge of the parton
k
,
L
is the medium path-length and
\hat{q}_{0}
stands for the transport coefficient at formation time
\tau_{0}
(
\tau_{0}\ll L
). Since the values of
\langle z\rangle
and
C_{k}
may differ for heavy hadrons, the analysis will focus on light hadrons from now on. The parameter
\alpha

2

\hat{q}(\tau)=\hat{q}_{0}\left(\tau_{0}/\tau\right)^{\alpha}

The initial transport coefficient
\hat{q}_{0}=\hat{q}(\tau_{0})
is proportional to the initial parton density
n_{0}
, which in the Bjorken picture can be estimated as

n_{0}=\frac{1}{A_{\perp}\tau_{0}}\,\left.\frac{{\rm d}N_{k}}{{\rm d}y}\right|_{y=0}\,\simeq\,\frac{3}{2}\,\frac{1}{A_{\perp}\tau_{0}}\,\left.\frac{{\rm d}N_{\rm ch}}{{\rm d}y}\right|_{y=0}\,,

(3)

where
A_{\perp}
is the transverse overlap area of the two crossing nuclei. Plugging (
3
) in (
2
) leads to the following parametric dependence,
[
2
]

\bar{\epsilon}\,=K\times\,\left(\frac{1}{A_{\perp}}\,\frac{{\rm d}N_{\rm ch}}{{\rm d}y}\,L^{\beta}\right)\,,

(4)

with
\beta=2-\alpha
. The geometric quantities
A_{\perp}
and
L
entering Eq. (
4
) are determined through an optical Glauber model, assuming hard sphere nuclear densities. The charged particle multiplicity data
{\rm d}N_{\textnormal{ch}}/{\rm d}\eta
at mid-rapidity is taken from PHENIX at RHIC and from
ALICE
and CMS at LHC (see
[
2
]
and references therein).
The energy loss scales
\bar{\epsilon}
extracted from the quenching of light hadrons are fitted using Eq. (
4
), with
\beta
(and
K

As can be seen in Figure

Once the dependence of
\bar{\epsilon}
with
L

v_{2}

R_{\textnormal{AA}}(u,n,\phi)=f\left(u\times\left(L/L(\phi)\right)^{\beta},n\right)\,,

(5)

where

\phi=0

L(\phi)=L\times\left(1-\textnormal{e}\,\cos\left(2\phi\right)\right)

\textnormal{e}=\left(L(\pi/2)-L(0)\right)/\left(L(\pi/2)+L(0)\right)

To first order in

\displaystyle\frac{v_{2}(u,n)}{\textnormal{e}}

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle\frac{v_{2}(p_{{}_{\perp}})}{\textnormal{e}}

\displaystyle\simeq

\displaystyle\frac{\beta}{2}\,\frac{p_{{}_{\perp}}}{R_{\textnormal{AA}}(p_{{}_{\perp}})}\,\frac{\partial R_{\textnormal{AA}}(p_{{}_{\perp}})}{\partial p_{{}_{\perp}}}\,.

In summary, relating the values of
\bar{\epsilon}

R_{\textnormal{AA}}
to the hadron multiplicity enables the determination of the
L

\bar{\epsilon}\propto L^{\beta}

We find that jet data obey the same relation, pointing to a similar path-length dependence of parton and jet energy loss in quark-gluon plasma. The LHC Runs 3 & 4 should allow for testing with unprecedented precision these scaling properties.
