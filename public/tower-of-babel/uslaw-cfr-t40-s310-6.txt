40 C.F.R. § 310.6 — Are states eligible?
Citation: us:cfr:40:310.6
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-310.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 310 (Reimbursement To Local Governments For Emergency Response To Hazardous Substance Releases). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

States are NOT eligible for reimbursement under this part, and states may NOT request reimbursement on behalf of their local governments.
