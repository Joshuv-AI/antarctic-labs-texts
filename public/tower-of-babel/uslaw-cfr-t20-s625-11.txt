20 C.F.R. § 625.11 — Provisions of State law applicable.
Citation: us:cfr:20:625.11
Creator: Employment And Training Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-20/section-625.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 20 (Employees' Benefits), Part 625 (Disaster Unemployment Assistance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The terms and conditions of the State law of the applicable State for an individual, which apply to claims for, and the payment of regular compensation, shall apply to applications for, and the payment of, DUA to each such individual, only as specifically set forth in the provisions of this part.
