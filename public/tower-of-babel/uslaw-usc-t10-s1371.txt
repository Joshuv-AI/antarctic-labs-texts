10 U.S.C. § 1371 — Warrant officers: general rule
Citation: us:usc:10:1371
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1371
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 69 (Retired Grade). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Unless entitled to a higher retired grade under some other provision of law, a warrant officer shall be retired in the highest regular or reserve warrant officer grade in which the warrant officer served satisfactorily, as determined by the Secretary concerned.
