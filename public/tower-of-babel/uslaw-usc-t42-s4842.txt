42 U.S.C. § 4842 — Consultation by Secretary with other departments and agencies
Citation: us:usc:42:4842
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4842
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 63 (Lead-Based Paint Poisoning Prevention); Subchapter V (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out their respective authorities under this chapter, the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development and the Secretary of Health and Human Services shall each cooperate with and seek the advice of the heads of any other departments or agencies regarding any programs under their respective responsibilities which are related to, or would be affected by, such authority.
