31 C.F.R. § 510.205 — Prohibited importation of goods, services, or technology from North Korea.
Citation: us:cfr:31:510.205
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-510.205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 510 (North Korea Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The importation into the United States, directly or indirectly, of any goods, services, or technology from North Korea is prohibited.
(b) The prohibitions in this section apply except to the extent provided by regulations, orders, directives, or licenses that may be issued pursuant to this part, and notwithstanding any contract entered into or any license or permit granted prior to the effective date.
