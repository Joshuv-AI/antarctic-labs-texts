17 U.S.C. § 705 — Copyright Office records: Preparation, maintenance, public inspection, and searching
Citation: us:usc:17:705
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title17-section705
Context: United States Code, Title 17 (Copyrights); Chapter 7 (Copyright Office). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Register of Copyrights shall ensure that records of deposits, registrations, recordations, and other actions taken under this title are maintained, and that indexes of such records are prepared.
(b)
Such records and indexes, as well as the articles deposited in connection with completed copyright registrations and retained under the control of the Copyright Office, shall be open to public inspection.
(c)
Upon request and payment of the fee specified by section 708, the Copyright Office shall make a search of its public records, indexes, and deposits, and shall furnish a report of the information they disclose with respect to any particular deposits, registrations, or recorded documents.
