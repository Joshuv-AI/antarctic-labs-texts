7 C.F.R. § 760.1714 — Overdisbursement.
Citation: us:cfr:7:760.1714
Creator: Farm Service Agency, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-760.1714
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 760 (Indemnity Payment Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the milk loss payment disbursed to an affected farmer exceeds the amount authorized under the regulations in this subpart, the affected farmer will be personally liable for repayment of the amount of such excess.
