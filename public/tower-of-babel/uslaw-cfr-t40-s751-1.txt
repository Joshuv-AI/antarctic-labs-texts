40 C.F.R. § 751.1 — Purpose.
Citation: us:cfr:40:751.1
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-751.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 751 (Regulation Of Certain Chemical Substances And Mixtures Under Section 6 Of The Toxic Substances Control Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part sets forth requirements under section 6(a) of the Toxic Substances Control Act, 15 U.S.C. 2605(a), regulating the manufacture (including import), processing, distribution in commerce, use, or disposal of certain chemical substances and mixtures in order to address unreasonable risks to the extent necessary so that the chemical substance or mixture no longer presents such risk.
