Editorial Expression of Concern: Ovarian stripping versus cystectomy: recurrence of endometriosis and pregnancy rate
Authors: Massimo Moscarini, Giusi Natalia Milazzo, Chiara Assorgi, Arianna Pacchiarotti, Donatella Caserta
Published: 2026

Editorial Expression of Concern: Arch Gynecol Obstet (2014) 290:163–167 10.1007/s00404-014-3158-z

The Editor-in-Chief is issuing an Editorial Expression of Concern for this article to alert readers that, after the publication of this article, concerns were raised regarding the data reported in this article, specifically the p values reported in Table 1. Due to the duration of time since the article was published, the authors have been unable to provide the raw data for this article. Readers are therefore advised to interpret the results and conclusions with caution. The authors did not state whether they agree to this Expression of Concern.
