43 C.F.R. § 3137.28 — What oil and gas resources of committed tracts does the unit agreement include?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3137.28
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3137.28
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3130 (Oil And Gas Leasing: National Petroleum Reserve, Alaska). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A unit agreement includes all oil and gas resources of committed tracts unless BLM approves unit agreement terms to the contrary pursuant to § 3137.50 of this subpart.
