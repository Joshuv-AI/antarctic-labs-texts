30 C.F.R. § 910.842 — Federal inspections.

Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-910.842
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 910 (Georgia). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Part 842 of this chapter, Federal Inspections, shall apply to all exploration and surface coal mining and reclamation operations.
(b) The Office will furnish a copy of any inspection report or enforcement action taken to the Georgia Department of Natural Resources upon request.
