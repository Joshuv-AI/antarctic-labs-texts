14 C.F.R. § 125.283 — Second-in-command qualifications.
Citation: us:cfr:14:125.283
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-125.283
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 125 (Certification And Operations: Aircraft Having A Seating Capacity Of 20 Or More Passengers Or A Maximum Payload Capacity Of 6,000 Pounds Or More; And Rules Governing Persons On Board Such Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No certificate holder may use any person, nor may any person serve, as second in command of an airplane unless that person—
(a) Holds at least a commercial pilot certificate with appropriate category and class ratings, and an instrument rating; and
(b) For flight under IFR, meets the recent instrument experience requirements prescribed for a pilot in command in part 61 of this chapter.
