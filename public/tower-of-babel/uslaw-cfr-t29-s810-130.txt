29 C.F.R. § 810.130 — Executive, Management, Research and Development, Engineering, and Other Personnel.
Citation: us:cfr:29:810.130
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-810.130
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 810 (High-Wage Components Of The Labor Value Content Requirements Under The United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement Implementation Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The average hourly base wage rate does not include any hours worked by:
(a) Executive or management staff who generally have the authority to make final decisions to hire, fire, promote, transfer and discipline employees;
(b) Workers engaged in research and development; or
(c) Engineers, mechanics, or technicians, if such personnel are not responsible for maintaining and ensuring the operation of the production line or tools and equipment used in the production of vehicles or parts.
