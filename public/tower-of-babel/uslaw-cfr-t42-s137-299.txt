42 C.F.R. § 137.299 — Are Federal funds available to cover the cost of Self-Governance Tribes carrying out environmental responsibilities?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.299
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.299
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, funds are available:
(a) for project-specific environmental costs through the construction project agreement; and
(b) for environmental review program costs through a funding agreement and/or a construction project agreement.
