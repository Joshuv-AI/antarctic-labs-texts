49 U.S.C. § 6308 — Proceeds of data product sales
Citation: us:usc:49:6308
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section6308
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Chapter 63 (Bureau Of Transportation Statistics). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding section 3302 of title 31, amounts received by the Bureau from the sale of data products for necessary expenses incurred may be credited to the Highway Trust Fund (other than the Mass Transit Account) for the purpose of reimbursing the Bureau for those expenses.
