42 U.S.C. § 4993 — Technical and financial assistance
Citation: us:usc:42:4993
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4993
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 66 (Domestic Volunteer Services); Subchapter I (National Volunteer Antipoverty Programs); Part C (Special Volunteer Programs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director may provide technical and financial assistance to Federal agencies, State and local governments and agencies, private nonprofit organizations, employers, and other private organizations that utilize or desire to utilize volunteers in carrying out the purpose of this part.
