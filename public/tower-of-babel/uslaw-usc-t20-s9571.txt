20 U.S.C. § 9571 — Interagency data sources and formats
Citation: us:usc:20:9571
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section9571
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 76 (Education Research, Statistics, Evaluation, Information, And Dissemination); Subchapter I (Education Sciences Reform); Part F (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, in consultation with the Director, shall ensure that the Department and the Institute use common sources of data in standardized formats.
