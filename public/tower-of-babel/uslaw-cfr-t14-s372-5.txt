14 C.F.R. § 372.5 — Suspension or revocation of authority.
Citation: us:cfr:14:372.5
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-372.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 372 (Overseas Military Personnel Charters). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Department reserves the power to suspend the authority of any charter operator, without hearing, if it finds that such action is necessary in order to protect the rights of the traveling public, or to revoke such authority for cause.
