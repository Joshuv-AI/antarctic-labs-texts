48 C.F.R. § 401.104 — 401.104 Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:48:401.104
Creator: Chapter 4—Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-401.104
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 401 (Agriculture Acquisition Regulation System). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The FAR and AGAR apply to all USDA acquisitions of supplies and services (including construction) which obligate appropriated funds, unless otherwise specified or excepted by law.
