35 U.S.C. § 173 — Term of design patent
Citation: us:usc:35:173
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title35-section173
Context: United States Code, Title 35 (Patents); Part II (Patentability Of Inventions And Grant Of Patents); Chapter 16 (Designs). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Patents for designs shall be granted for the term of 15 years from the date of grant.
