Holonomic étale sheaves are constructible
Ahmed Abbes and Takeshi Saito

Abstract
. Building on Beilinson’s work, “constructible sheaves are holonomic,” we introduce the notion of holonomicity for étale sheaves, without assuming a priori constructibility. We establish the converse of Beilinson’s result, showing that holonomic sheaves are indeed constructible. This can be seen as an étale analogue of Kashiwara’s theorem on holonomic 𝒟 X {\mathcal{D}}_{X} -modules.



For a coherent
{\mathcal{D}}_{X}
-module

{\mathcal{M}}

Following his construction,

One may consider this as an étale

The contents of the sections are summarized as follows.
In the brief Section 1,
we recall the fact that a closed conical subset of a vector bundle is
determined by its base and its projectivization.
In Sections 2 and 3,
we study two properties of morphisms
relative to a closed conical subset
C

of the cotangent bundle,
largely following
[
3
]
.
The notion of
C
-transversality applies when
C

is a subset for the target of a morphism

h\colon W\to X
,
while
C
-acyclicity applies when
C
is a subset
for the source of a morphism

f\colon X\to Y
.
Both notions enter the definition of the micro support of
a sheaf
{\mathcal{F}}
;
in
[
3
]

In Section 4, we extend the definition of micro support given in

[
3
]
to sheaves that are not necessarily constructible;
in
[
3
]
, constructibility is assumed.
In Section 5, we prove the existence of the singular support of

The proof follows that of
[

Finally, in Section 6, we prove the main result:
