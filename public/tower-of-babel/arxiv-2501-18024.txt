1.
Introduction

For positive integers
k

and
N
,
let
S_{k}(N)
denote the complex vector space of cusp forms of weight
k
on
\Gamma_{0}(N)
.
Let

f\in S_{k}(N)

be a newform with Fourier expansion

f=\sum_{n\geq 1}a(n)q^{n}
(see Section
2
for details).
The
L
-function for
f
, which is given by

(1.1)

L_{f}(s):=\sum^{\infty}_{n=1}\frac{a(n)}{n^{s}}=\left(\prod_{p\mid N}\frac{1}{1-a(p)p^{-s}}\right)\prod_{p\nmid N}\frac{1}{1-a(p)p^{-s}+p^{k-1-2s}},

has an analytic continuation to the complex plane.
Moreover, the completed
L
-function

L^{*}_{f}(s):=\left(\frac{\sqrt{N}}{2\pi}\right)^{s}\Gamma(s)L_{f}(s)

satisfies the functional equation

(1.2)

L^{*}_{f}(s)=\epsilon_{f}L^{*}_{f}(k-s)

for some
\epsilon_{f}\in\{\pm 1\}
.

The completed
L
-function can be written as

(1.3)

L^{*}_{f}(s)=N^{s/2}\int^{\infty}_{0}f(iy)y^{s}\frac{dy}{y},

which means that it arises as a “period integral” of
f
(see
[
KZ01
]
for more information on period integrals).
The period polynomial of
f
is the degree
k-2
polynomial

r_{f}(z):=\int^{i\infty}_{0}f(\tau)(\tau-z)^{k-2}d\tau.

Using (
1.3
) and expanding
(\tau-z)^{k-2}
, we can write
r_{f}(z)
as

(1.4)

r_{f}(z)=i^{k-1}N^{-\frac{k-1}{2}}\sum^{k-2}_{n=0}{\binom{k-2}{n}}(\sqrt{N}iz)^{n}L^{*}_{f}(k-1-n)

or as

r_{f}(z)=-\frac{(k-2)!}{(2\pi i)^{k-1}}\sum^{k-2}_{n=0}\frac{(2\pi iz)^{n}}{n!}L_{f}(k-1-n).

Thus,
r_{f}(z)
is a generating function for the critical values
L_{f}(1)
,
L_{f}(2)
,
\dots
,
L_{f}(k-1)
. These values are crucially important in arithmetic geometry and number theory
[
JMOS16
]

[
LMT17
]
.
For example, they appear in the context of the Bloch-Kato conjecture for special values of
L
-functions which generalizes the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture relating Tate-Shafarevich groups of elliptic curves to analytic properties of modular
L
-functions (see
[
BK90
]
for more details).
Moreover, by virtue of the functional equation (
1.2
) and (
1.4
), we see that

(1.5)

r_{f}(z)=-\epsilon_{f}i^{k}(\sqrt{N}z)^{k-2}r_{f}\left(-\frac{1}{Nz}\right).

Jin, Ma, Ono, and Soundararajan
[
JMOS16
]
showed that

satisfies an analogue of the Riemann hypothesis: all of its zeros are located on the circle
\{z\in\mathbb{C}\ :\ |z|=1/\sqrt{N}\}
, the circle of symmetry for the functional equation (

).
In this paper, for a positive integer
m
, we define an analogue of

for the

symmetric power

-function and prove the corresponding version of the Riemann hypothesis when

is squarefree and when either

is large enough, or

is large enough assuming that
k>2(\operatorname{log}_{2}(13e^{2\pi}/9)+m)+1

Stating our result requires introducing notation.
Suppose that

is a positive integer, that

is a squarefree positive integer and that

For each prime

and

.
Let

be a positive integer.
The

L_{m,f}(s):=\prod_{p}\prod_{0\leq i\leq m}(1-\alpha^{m-i}_{p}\beta^{i}_{p}p^{-s})^{-1},

which converges absolutely for

.

Newton and Thorne

Moreover, the completed

L_{m,f}^{*}(s):=N^{\frac{ms}{2}}\gamma_{m}(s)L_{m,f}(s),

satisfies the functional equation

(1.7)

L_{m,f}^{*}(s)=\epsilon_{m,f}L^{*}_{m,f}(m(k-1)+1-s)

for some
\epsilon_{m,f}\in\{\pm 1\}
.
Here
[
Zag77
, pg.
118
]

(1.8)

\gamma_{m}(s):=\begin{cases}(2\pi)^{-rs}\displaystyle\prod^{r-1}_{j=0}\Gamma(s-j(k-1))&\text{if }m=2r-1,\\
\pi^{-\frac{s}{2}}\Gamma\left(\frac{s}{2}-\left\lfloor\frac{r(k-1)}{2}\right\rfloor\right)\gamma_{2r-1}(s)&\text{if }m=2r.\end{cases}

Zagier defines

The main object of our investigation is

(2) Löbrich, Ma, and Thorner

2.
Background

For background on modular forms, see
[
DS05
]
.
Suppose that
k
and
N
are positive integers.
For a function
f(z)
on the upper half plane and

\gamma=\left(\begin{matrix}a&b\\
c&d\end{matrix}\right)\in\operatorname{GL}_{2}^{+}(\mathbb{Q}),

we have the weight

f(z)\big|_{k}\gamma:=\det(\gamma)^{\frac{k}{2}}(cz+d)^{-k}f\left(\frac{az+b}{cz+d}\right).

We denote by
S_{k}(N)
the space of cusp forms of weight
k
on
\Gamma_{0}(N)
. Forms in this space satisfy the transformation law

f\big|_{k}\gamma=f\ \ \text{ for }\ \ \gamma=\left(\begin{matrix}a&b\\
c&d\end{matrix}\right)\in\Gamma_{0}(N)

and vanish at the cusps of
\Gamma_{0}(N)
.

If
p
is prime, we denote by
T_{p}
the usual Hecke operator on

(see
[
DS05
, Chapter
5
]
). As in
[
DS05
, Chapter
5
]
, we use

even when

. We say that

is a newform if
a(1)=1
and it is an eigenform of all the

operators.

(1) Lemma

Lemma 2.2
.

Suppose that
m
is a positive integer, that
N
is a squarefree positive integer and that
k
is a positive integer with
k\geq 6
. Let

be a newform. Then for

|L_{m,f}(s)-1|<\frac{13}{9}\times 2^{m-\frac{k-1}{2}}.

Proof.

With minor changes, we follow the proof of the first assertion of
[

2.4
]

By
[

]
and a standard convolution identity for the Riemann zeta function, we have

|L_{m,f}(s)-1|\leq\sum^{\infty}_{n=2}\frac{d_{m+1}(n)}{n^{\frac{k-1}{2}}}=\zeta\left(\frac{k-1}{2}\right)^{m}-1.

Since

\zeta\left(\frac{k-1}{2}\right)^{m}-1<2^{m}\left(\zeta\left(\frac{k-1}{2}\right)-1\right)
