49 U.S.C. § 15505 — Prohibition against discrimination by pipeline carriers
Citation: us:usc:49:15505
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section15505
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Pipeline Carriers); Chapter 155 (Rates). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A pipeline carrier providing transportation or service subject to this part may not subject a person, place, port, or type of traffic to unreasonable discrimination.
