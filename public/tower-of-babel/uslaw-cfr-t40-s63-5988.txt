40 C.F.R. § 63.5988 — What emission limitations must I meet for puncture sealant application affected sources?
Citation: us:cfr:40:63.5988
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 63 (National Emission Standards For Hazardous Air Pollutants For Source Categories). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must meet each emission limit in either option 1 or option 2 of table 3 to this subpart that applies to you.
(b) If you use an add-on control device to meet the emission limits in table 3 to this subpart, you must also meet each operating limit in table 4 to this subpart that applies to you.
