42 U.S.C. § 9858k — Limitations on use of financial assistance for certain purposes
Citation: us:usc:42:9858k
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section9858k
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 105 (Community Services Programs); Subchapter II–B (Child Care And Development Block Grant). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Sectarian purposes and activities
No financial assistance provided under this subchapter, pursuant to the choice of a parent under section 9858c(c)(2)(A)(i)(I) of this title or through any other grant or contract under the State plan, shall be expended for any sectarian purpose or activity, including sectarian worship or instruction.
(b) Tuition
With regard to services provided to students enrolled in grades 1 through 12, no financial assistance provided under this subchapter shall be expended for—
(1)
any services provided to such students during the regular school day;
(2)
any services for which such students receive academic credit toward graduation; or
(3)
any instructional services which supplant or duplicate the academic program of any public or private school.
