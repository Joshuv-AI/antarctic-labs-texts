25 C.F.R. § 26.17 — Can more than one family member be financially assisted at the same time?
Citation: us:cfr:25:26.17
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-26.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 26 (Job Placement And Training Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Yes, more than one family member can be assisted, providing that each applicant is eligible.
