28 C.F.R. § 1.11 — Advisory nature of regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:28:1.11
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-1.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 1 (Executive Clemency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The regulations contained in this part are advisory only and for the internal guidance of Department of Justice personnel. They create no enforceable rights in persons applying for executive clemency, nor do they restrict the authority granted to the President under Article II, section 2 of the Constitution.
