14 C.F.R. § 1204.1508 — Time limitations for receiving comments on proposed direct Federal development.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1204.1508
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1204.1508
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1204 (Administrative Authority And Policy). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Except in unusual circumstances, the Administrator gives state processes or state, areawide, regional and local officials and entities at least 60 days from the date established by the Administrator to comment on proposed direct Federal development.
(b) This section also applies to comments in cases in which the review, coordination, and communication with the Agency has been delegated.
