2 U.S.C. § 2133 — Acceptance and supervision of works of fine arts
Citation: us:usc:2:2133
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section2133
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 30 (Operation And Maintenance Of Capitol Complex); Subchapter V (Historical Preservation And Fine Arts); Part D (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Joint Committee on the Library, whenever, in their judgment, it is expedient, are authorized to accept any work of the fine arts, on behalf of Congress, which may be offered, and to assign the same such place in the Capitol as they may deem suitable, and shall have the supervision of all works of art that may be placed in the Capitol.
