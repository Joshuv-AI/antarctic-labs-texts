18 U.S.C. § 2316 — Transportation of livestock
Citation: us:usc:18:2316
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section2316
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 113 (Stolen Property). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever transports in interstate or foreign commerce any livestock, knowing the same to have been stolen, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than five years, or both.
