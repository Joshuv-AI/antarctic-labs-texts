44 U.S.C. § 1101 — Printing and binding for the President
Citation: us:usc:44:1101
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2025
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title44-section1101
Context: United States Code, Title 44 (Public Printing And Documents); Chapter 11 (Executive And Judiciary Printing And Binding). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Director of the Government Publishing Office shall execute such printing and binding for the President as he may order and make requisition for.
