47 C.F.R. § 5.505 — Exemption from station identification requirement.
Citation: us:cfr:47:5.505
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-5.505
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 5 (Experimental Radio Service). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Compliance testing experimental licensees are exempt from complying with the station identification requirements of § 5.115.
