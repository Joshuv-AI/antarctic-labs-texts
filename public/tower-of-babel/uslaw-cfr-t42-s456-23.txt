42 C.F.R. § 456.23 — Post-payment review process.
Citation: us:cfr:42:456.23
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-456.23
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 456 (Utilization Control). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The agency must have a post-payment review process that—
(a) Allows State personnel to develop and review—
(1) Beneficiary utilization profiles;
(2) Provider service profiles; and
(3) Exceptions criteria; and
(b) Identifies exceptions so that the agency can correct misutilization practices of beneficiaries and providers.
