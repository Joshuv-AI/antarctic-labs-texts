14 C.F.R. § 141.21 — Inspections.
Citation: us:cfr:14:141.21
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-141.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 141 (Pilot Schools). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each holder of a certificate issued under this part must allow the Administrator to inspect its personnel, facilities, equipment, and records to determine the certificate holder's:
(a) Eligibility to hold its certificate;
(b) Compliance with 49 U.S.C. 40101 et seq., formerly the Federal Aviation Act of 1958, as amended; and
(c) Compliance with the Federal Aviation Regulations.
