31 C.F.R. § 35.2 — Applicability.
Citation: us:cfr:31:35.2
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-35.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 35 (Pandemic Relief Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part applies to states, territories, Tribal governments, metropolitan cities, nonentitlement units of local government, counties, and units of general local government that accept a payment or transfer of funds made under section 602 or 603 of the Social Security Act.
