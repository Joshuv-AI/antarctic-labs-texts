5 C.F.R. § 575.506 — When is an agency prohibited from paying an extended assignment incentive?
Citation: us:cfr:5:575.506
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-575.506
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 575 (Recruitment, Relocation, And Retention Incentives; Supervisory Differentials; And Extended Assignment Incentives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An extended assignment incentive may not be paid to the head of an agency, including an agency headed by a collegial body composed of two or more individual members.
(b) An agency may not begin paying an extended assignment incentive to an otherwise eligible employee who is receiving or fulfilling the requirements of a service agreement for the payment of a recruitment, relocation, or retention incentive. (See 5 CFR part 575, subparts A, B, and C.)
