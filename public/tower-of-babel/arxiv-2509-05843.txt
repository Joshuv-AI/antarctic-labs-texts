Abstract
. This paper introduces the notion of uniformly- S S -pseudo-injective ( u u - S S -pseudo-injective) modules as a generalization of u u - S S -injective modules. Let R R be a ring and S S a multiplicative subset of R R . An R R -module E E is said to be u u - S S -pseudo-injective if for any submodule K K of E E , there is s ∈ S s\in S such that for any u u - S S -monomorphism f : K → E f:K\to E , s ​ f sf can be extended to an endomorphism g : E → E g:E\to E . Several properties of this notion are studied. For example, we show that an R R -module M M is u u - S S -quasi-injective if and only if M ⊕ M M\oplus M is u u - S S -pseudo-injective. New classes of rings related to the class of Q ​ I QI -rings are introduced and characterized.
