9 C.F.R. § 559.3 — Criminal offenses.
Citation: us:cfr:9:559.3
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-559.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 559 (Detention, Seizure, Condemnation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The criminal provisions of the Act apply with respect to the inspection of fish and fish products as they do with respect to the inspection of other food products subject to the Act.
