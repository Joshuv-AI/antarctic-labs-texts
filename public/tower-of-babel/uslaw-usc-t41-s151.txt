41 U.S.C. § 151 — Agency head
Citation: us:usc:41:151
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title41-section151
Context: United States Code, Title 41 (Public Contracts); Chapter 1 (Definitions); Subchapter III (Division C Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In division C, the term “agency head” means the head or any assistant head of an executive agency, and may at the option of the Administrator of General Services include the chief official of any principal organizational unit of the General Services Administration.
