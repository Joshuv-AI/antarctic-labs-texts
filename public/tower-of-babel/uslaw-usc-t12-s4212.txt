12 U.S.C. § 4212 — Protection for declarants
Citation: us:usc:12:4212
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section4212
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 43 (Actions Against Persons Committing Bank Fraud Crimes); Subchapter I (Declarations Providing New Claims To United States). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A declarant under this subchapter shall enjoy the protections of section 3059A(e) 1 of title 18.
