2 U.S.C. § 354 — Use of United States mails
Citation: us:usc:2:354
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section354
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 11 (Citizens’ Commission On Public Service And Compensation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Commission may use the United States mails in the same manner and upon the same conditions as other departments and agencies of the United States.
