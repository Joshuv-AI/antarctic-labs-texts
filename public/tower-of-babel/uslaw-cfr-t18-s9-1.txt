18 C.F.R. § 9.1 — Filing.
Citation: us:cfr:18:9.1
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-9.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 9 (Transfer Of License Or Lease Of Project Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any licensee desiring to transfer a license or rights thereunder granted, and the person, association, corporation, State, or municipality desiring to acquire the same, shall jointly or severally file an application for approval of such transfer and acquisition. Such application shall be verified, shall conform to § 131.20 of this chapter, and shall be filed in accordance with § 4.32 of this chapter.
