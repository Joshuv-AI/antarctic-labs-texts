10 C.F.R. § 436.12 — Life cycle cost methodology.
Citation: us:cfr:10:436.12
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-436.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 436 (Federal Energy Management And Planning Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The life cycle cost methodology for this part is a systematic analysis of relevant costs, excluding sunk costs, over a study period, relating initial costs to future costs by the technique of discounting future costs to present values.
