50 C.F.R. § 85.45 — Public access to facilities and maintenance.
Citation: us:cfr:50:85.45
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-85.45
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 85 (Clean Vessel Act Grant Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All recreational vessels must have access to pumpout and dump stations funded under this grant program. Facilities shall be operated, maintained, and continue to be reasonably accessible to all recreational vessels for the full period of their useful life.
