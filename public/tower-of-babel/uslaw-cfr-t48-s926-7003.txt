48 C.F.R. § 926.7003 — 926.7003 Review of the procurement request.
Citation: us:cfr:48:926.7003
Creator: Chapter 9—Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-926.7003
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 926 (Other Socioeconomic Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any Energy Policy Act procurement, including basic research contracts with educational institutions, shall be reviewed in accordance with the Small Business and 8(a) Program Review Procedures in order to ensure that full consideration is given to the potential for making Energy Policy Act awards.
