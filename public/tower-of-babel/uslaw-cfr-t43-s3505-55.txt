43 C.F.R. § 3505.55 — What are my obligations to BLM under an approved prospecting permit?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3505.55
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3505.55
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3500 (Leasing Of Solid Minerals Other Than Coal And Oil Shale). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must:
(a) Pay your annual rental in a timely fashion. See §§ 3504.15 and 3504.16 of this part;
(b) Comply with all permit terms and stipulations the surface management agency attached to the permit;
(c) Conduct only those exploration activities approved as part of your existing exploration plan; and
(d) Discontinue activities following expiration of the initial term unless and until BLM extends your permit.
