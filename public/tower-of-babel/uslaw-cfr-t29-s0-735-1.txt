29 C.F.R. § 0.735-1 — -1 Cross-references to employee ethical conduct standards, financial disclosure regulations and other ethics regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:29:0.735-1
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-0.735-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 0 (Ethics And Conduct Of Department Of Labor Employees). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees of the Department of Labor (Department) are subject to the executive branch-wide standards of ethical conduct at 5 CFR part 2635, the Department's regulations at 5 CFR part 5201 which supplement the executive branch-wide standards, the executive branch financial disclosure regulations at 5 CFR part 2634, the conflicts of interest regulations at 5 CFR part 2640, and the post employment regulations at 5 CFR part 2641.
