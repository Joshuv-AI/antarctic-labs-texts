5 C.F.R. § 870.903 — Effective date of assignment.
Citation: us:cfr:5:870.903
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-870.903
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 870 (Federal Employees' Group Life Insurance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An assignment under this subpart is effective on the date the employing office receives the properly completed, signed, and witnessed assignment form.
