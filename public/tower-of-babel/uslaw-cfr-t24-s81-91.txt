24 C.F.R. § 81.91 — Maintenance of GSE Securities.
Citation: us:cfr:24:81.91
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-81.91
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 81 (The Secretary Of Hud'S Regulation Of The Federal National Mortgage Association (Fannie Mae) And The Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation (Freddie Mac)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A GSE Security may be maintained in the form of a Definitive GSE Security or a Book-entry GSE Security. A Book-entry GSE Security shall be maintained in the Book-entry System.
