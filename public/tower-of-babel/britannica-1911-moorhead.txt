MOORHEAD, a city and the county-seat of Clay county, Minnesota, U.S.A., opposite Fargo, North Dakota, on the E. bank of the Red River and about 215 m. N.W. of Minneapolis. Pop. (1890), 2088; (1900), 3730; (1905). 4794; (1910),

4840. Moorhead is served by the Great Northern and the
Northern Pacific railways. The city is the seat of one of the
state normal schools (1888) and of Concordia College (Norwegian
Lutheran; 1891), which in 1907–1908 had 500 students.
Moorhead, named in honour of James K. Moorhead (1806–1884),
a Republican representative in Congress from Pennsylvania
in 1859–1869, was settled in 1871, was incorporated as a
village in 1875, and was chartered as a city in 1881.
