42 C.F.R. § 137.18 — What criteria must an Indian Tribe satisfy to be eligible to participate in self-governance?
Citation: us:cfr:42:137.18
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-137.18
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 137 (Tribal Self-Governance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To be eligible to participate in self-governance, an Indian Tribe must have:
(a) Successfully completed the planning phase described in § 137.20;
(b) Requested participation in self-governance by resolution or other official action by the governing body of each Indian Tribe to be served; and
(c) Demonstrated, for three fiscal years, financial stability and financial management capability.
