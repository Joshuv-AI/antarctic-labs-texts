48 C.F.R. § 246.404 — 246.404 Government contract quality assurance for acquisitions at or below the simplified acquisition threshold.
Citation: us:cfr:48:246.404
Creator: Chapter 2—Defense Acquisition Regulations System, Department Of Defense
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-246.404
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 246 (Quality Assurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Do not require Government contract quality assurance at source for contracts or delivery orders valued at or below the simplified acquisition threshold unless the criteria at 246.402 have been met.
