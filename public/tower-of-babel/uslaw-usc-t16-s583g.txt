16 U.S.C. § 583g — Rules and regulations; delegation of powers and duties
Citation: us:usc:16:583g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section583g
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3 (Forests; Forest Service; Reforestation; Management); Subchapter IV (Sustained-Yield Forest Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of Agriculture and the Secretary of the Interior may severally prescribe such rules and regulations as may be appropriate to carry out the purposes of this subchapter. Each Secretary may delegate any of his powers and duties under this subchapter to other officers or employees of his Department.
