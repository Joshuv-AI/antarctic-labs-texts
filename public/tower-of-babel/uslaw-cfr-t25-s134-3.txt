25 C.F.R. § 134.3 — Period for payments extended.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 134 (Partial Payment Construction Charges On Indian Irrigation Projects). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).
