7 C.F.R. § 2903.8 — Program application materials.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2903.8
Creator: Office Of Energy Policy And New Uses, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2903.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2903 (Biodiesel Fuel Education Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

OEPNU will publish periodic program announcements to notify potential applicants of the availability of funds for competitive continuation grants. The program announcement will provide information about obtaining program application materials.
