48 C.F.R. § 2403.303-70 — 2403.303-70 Reporting requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:48:2403.303-70
Creator: Chapter 24—Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-2403.303-70
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 2403 (Improper Business Practices And Personal Conflicts Of Interest). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Potential anti-competitive practices such as described in FAR subpart 3.3, evidenced in bids or proposals, shall be reported to the Office of General Counsel through the Head of the Contracting Activity with a copy to the Senior Procurement Executive and the Inspector General. The Office of General Counsel will provide reports to the Attorney General as appropriate.
