22 U.S.C. § 8124 — Prohibited acts relating to complementary access
Citation: us:usc:22:8124
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section8124
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 88 (Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty—United States Additional Protocol Implementation); Subchapter II (Complementary Access). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall be unlawful for any person willfully to fail or refuse to permit, or to disrupt, delay, or otherwise impede, a complementary access authorized by this subchapter or an entry in connection with such access.
