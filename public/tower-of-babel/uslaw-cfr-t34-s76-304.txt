34 C.F.R. § 76.304 — Subgrantee shall make subgrant application available to the public.
Citation: us:cfr:34:76.304
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-34/section-76.304
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 34 (Education), Part 76 (State-Administered Formula Grant Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A subgrantee shall make any application, evaluation, periodic program plan, or report relating to each program available for public inspection.
