40 C.F.R. § 1090.1700 — Prohibited acts.
Citation: us:cfr:40:1090.1700
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-1090.1700
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 1090 (Regulation Of Fuels, Fuel Additives, And Regulated Blendstocks). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) No person may violate any prohibited act in this part or fail to meet a requirement that applies to that person under this part.
(b) No person may cause another person to commit an act in violation of this part.
