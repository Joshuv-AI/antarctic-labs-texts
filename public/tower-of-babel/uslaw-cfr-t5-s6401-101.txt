5 C.F.R. § 6401.101 — General.
Citation: us:cfr:5:6401.101
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-6401.101
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 6401 (Supplemental Standards Of Ethical Conduct For Employees Of The Environmental Protection Agency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In accordance with 5 CFR 2635.105, the regulations in this part apply to employees of the Environmental Protection Agency and supplement the Standards of Ethical Conduct for Employees of the Executive Branch contained in 5 CFR part 2635.
