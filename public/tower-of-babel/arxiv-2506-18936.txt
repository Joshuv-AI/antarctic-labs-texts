Neutron skin thickness for nat Pb, 159 Tb and effects of deformation on matter radii for Mg, Na, Ar isotopes
Shingo Tagami, Takaykui Myo, Masanobu Yahiro

Abstract
Background Data on reaction cross section σ R \sigma_{\rm R} of proton scattering are available for nat Pb (natural lead) and 159 Tb. In addition, data on σ R \sigma_{\rm R} and/or interaction ones σ I \sigma_{\rm I} are available for Mg, Na, Ar isotopes. Purpose Our aim is to determine neutron skin thickness r skin r_{\rm skin} for nat Pb, 159 Tb and to investigate effects of deformation for Mg, Na, Ar isotopes. Methods We use the chiral (Kyushu) g g -matrix folding model for lower energies and the folding model based on the Love-Franey (LF) t t -matrix for higher energies. Results For 159 Tb, our skin value is r skin = 0.273 ± 0.073 r_{\rm skin}=0.273\pm 0.073 fm. As for 24 Mg, our matter radius r m ​ ( σ R ) = 3.03 ± 0.08 r_{\rm m}(\sigma_{\rm R})=3.03\pm 0.08 fm determined from σ R \sigma_{\rm R} includes effects of deformation, whereas the corresponding r m ​ ( σ I ) = 2.79 ± 0.15 r_{\rm m}(\sigma_{\rm I})=2.79\pm 0.15 fm does not. Effects of deformation are seen for Mg isotopes, 21,23 Na, 40 Ar. Conclusion Our result r skin = 0.271 ± 0.008 r_{\rm skin}=0.271\pm 0.008 fm for nat Pb agrees with r skin 208 ​ ( PREX ​ - ​ II ) = 0.283 ± 0.071 r_{\rm skin}^{208}({\rm PREX\text{-}II})=0.283\pm 0.071 fm for 208 Pb, where we assume A = 208 A=208 for nat Pb.

\displaystyle(T_{R}+U({\bm{R}})-E){\Psi}^{(+)}=0,

\displaystyle U({\bm{R}})=U_{\rm CE}+U_{\rm LS}{\mbox{\boldmath$L$}}\cdot{\mbox{\boldmath$\sigma$}}+V_{\rm Coul}.

the direct and particle-exchange parts. The exchange part
is nonlocal, but it can be localized with

Validity of the approximation is shown in Ref.

The central part

is then described by the sum of
the direct component

and the localized exchange component

;

;
Furumoto et al. 2008

\displaystyle U_{\rm CE}\equiv V_{\rm CE}+iW_{\rm CE}=U_{\rm CE}^{\rm DR}+U_{\rm CE}^{\rm EX}

(6)

with

\displaystyle U_{\rm CE}^{\rm DR}({\mbox{\boldmath$R$}})\hskip-4.26773pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-4.26773pt\!\!\sum_{\alpha=p,n}\!\int\!\!\rho_{\alpha}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}})g^{\rm DR}_{p\alpha}(s;\rho_{\alpha})d{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}},

(7)

\displaystyle U_{\rm CE}^{\rm EX}({\mbox{\boldmath$R$}})\hskip-4.26773pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-4.26773pt-\!\!\sum_{\alpha=p,n}\!\int\!\!\rho_{\alpha}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}},{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}-{\mbox{\boldmath$s$}})g^{\rm EX}_{p\alpha}(s;\rho_{\alpha})j_{0}(K(R)s)d{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}},~~~~

(8)

where

(
W_{\rm CE}
) is the real (imaginary) part of

and
{\mbox{\boldmath$s$}}={\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}-{\mbox{\boldmath$R$}}

is the coordinate of an interacting nucleon
from the center-of-mass (c.m.) of target.

is

The direct and exchange parts,

and

\displaystyle\rho_{\alpha}=\rho^{\nu}_{\rm P}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm T}+{\mbox{\boldmath$s$}}/2)

(9)

at the midpoint of the interacting nucleon pair.

The direct part

(10)

(11)

of

II.3
Double-folding model

Nucleus-nucleus (PT) elastic scattering can be
described by the one-body Schrödinger equation,

\displaystyle(T_{R}+U_{\rm CE}({\bm{R}})+V_{\rm Coul}-E){\Psi}^{(+)}=0

with the central; folded potential
U(_{\rm CE}{\bm{R}})
shown blow.
Solving Eq. (

The folded potential

defined by

\displaystyle U^{\rm DR}({\mbox{\boldmath$R$}})\hskip-4.26773pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-4.26773pt\sum_{\mu,\nu}\int\rho^{\mu}_{\rm P}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm P})\rho^{\nu}_{\rm T}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm T})g^{\rm DR}_{\mu\nu}(s;\rho_{\mu\nu})d{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm P}d{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm T},

(21)

\displaystyle U^{\rm EX}({\mbox{\boldmath$R$}})\hskip-4.26773pt

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\hskip-4.26773pt\sum_{\mu,\nu}\int\rho^{\mu}_{\rm P}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm P},{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm P}-{\mbox{\boldmath$s$}})\rho^{\nu}_{\rm T}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm T},{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm T}+{\mbox{\boldmath$s$}})

(22)

\displaystyle~~\hskip-14.22636pt\times g^{\rm EX}_{\mu\nu}(s;\rho_{\mu\nu})\exp{[-i{\mbox{\boldmath$K$}}({\mbox{\boldmath$R$}})\cdot{\mbox{\boldmath$s$}}/M_{A}]}d{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm P}d{\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm T},~~~~

where

for the coordinate
R
between P and T and

for the mass number

) of P (T).
The coordinate

(

of an interacting nucleon
from the center-of-mass of the projectile (target).
The projectile (target) densities

(\rho^{\mu}_{\rm T}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm T})
) has the
z

\mu
(

The local momentum

\displaystyle\rho_{\mu\nu}=\sigma^{\mu}\left[\rho^{\nu}_{\rm T}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm T}+{\mbox{\boldmath$s$}}/2)+\rho^{\nu}_{\rm P}({\mbox{\boldmath$r$}}_{\rm P}-{\mbox{\boldmath$s$}}/2)\right]

at the midpoint of the interacting nucleon pair, where

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636ptg_{\mu\nu}^{\rm DR}(s;\rho_{\mu\nu})

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}\displaystyle{\frac{1}{4}\sum_{S}}\hat{S}^{2}g_{\mu\nu}^{S1}(s;\rho_{\mu\nu})\hskip 11.9501pt;\hskip 5.69046pt{\rm for}\hskip 2.84544pt\mu+\nu=\pm 1\vskip 5.69046pt\\
\displaystyle{\frac{1}{8}\sum_{S,T}}\hat{S}^{2}g_{\mu\nu}^{ST}(s;\rho_{\mu\nu}),\hskip 5.69046pt;\hskip 5.69046pt{\rm for}\hskip 2.84544pt\mu+\nu=0\end{cases}

(24)

\displaystyle\hskip 14.22636ptg_{\mu\nu}^{\rm EX}(s;\rho_{\mu\nu})

\displaystyle=\begin{cases}\displaystyle{\frac{1}{4}\sum_{S}}(-1)^{S+1}\hat{S}^{2}g_{\mu\nu}^{S1}(s;\rho_{\mu\nu})\hskip 9.67383pt;\hskip 5.69046pt{\rm for}\hskip 2.84544pt\mu+\nu=\pm 1\vskip 5.69046pt\\
\displaystyle{\frac{1}{8}\sum_{S,T}}(-1)^{S+T}\hat{S}^{2}g_{\mu\nu}^{ST}(s;\rho_{\mu\nu})\hskip 5.69046pt;\hskip 5.69046pt{\rm for}\hskip 2.84544pt\mu+\nu=0~~~~~\end{cases}

where

and
