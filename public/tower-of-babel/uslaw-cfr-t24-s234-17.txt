24 C.F.R. § 234.17 — Mortgagor and mortgagee requirements for maintaining flood insurance coverage.
Citation: us:cfr:24:234.17
Creator: Office Of Assistant Secretary For Housing—Federal Housing Commissioner, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-234.17
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 234 (Condominium Ownership Mortgage Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The maintenance of flood insurance coverage on the project by the condominium association will satisfy the requirements of § 203.16a of this chapter if such coverage protects the interest of the mortgagor in the family unit. For this purpose, “the interest of the mortgagor” is defined as insurance coverage equal to the replacement cost of the project less land costs.
