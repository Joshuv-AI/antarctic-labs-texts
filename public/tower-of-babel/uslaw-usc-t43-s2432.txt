43 U.S.C. § 2432 — Effect on the reclamation laws
Citation: us:usc:43:2432
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section2432
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 42 (Rural Water Supply); Subchapter II (Twenty-First Century Water Works). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Reclamation projects
Nothing in this subchapter supersedes or amends any Federal law associated with a project, or a portion of a project, constructed under the reclamation laws.
(b) No new or supplemental benefits
Any assistance provided under this subchapter shall not—
(1)
be considered to be a new or supplemental benefit for purposes of the Reclamation Reform Act of 1982 (43 U.S.C. 390aa et seq.); or
(2)
affect any contract in existence on December 22, 2006, that is executed under the reclamation laws.
