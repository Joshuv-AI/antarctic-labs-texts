36 C.F.R. § 34.10 — Saddle and pack animals.
Citation: us:cfr:36:34.10
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-34.10
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 34 (El Portal Administrative Site Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The use of saddle and pack animals is prohibited without a permit from the Superintendent.
