28 C.F.R. § 804.5 — Audit and public inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:28:804.5
Creator: Court Services And Offender Supervision Agency For The District Of Columbia
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-804.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 804 (Acceptance Of Gifts). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Records regarding the acceptance and use of gifts shall be made available for Federal Government audit.
(b) Public inspection of records regarding the acceptance and use of gifts shall be afforded through Freedom of Information Act requests (see 28 CFR part 802).
