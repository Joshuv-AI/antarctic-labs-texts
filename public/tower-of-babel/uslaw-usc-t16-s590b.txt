16 U.S.C. § 590b — Lands on which preventive measures may be taken
Citation: us:usc:16:590b
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section590b
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 3B (Soil Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The acts authorized in section 590a(1) and (2) of this title may be performed—
(a)
On lands owned or controlled by the United States or any of its agencies, with the cooperation of the agency having jurisdiction thereof; and
(b)
On any other lands, upon obtaining proper consent or the necessary rights or interests in such lands.
