29 U.S.C. § 11 — Bureau established
Citation: us:usc:29:11
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section11
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 2 (Women’S Bureau). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There shall be established in the Department of Labor a bureau to be known as the Women’s Bureau.
