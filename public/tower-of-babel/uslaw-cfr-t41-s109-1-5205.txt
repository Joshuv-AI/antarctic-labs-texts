41 C.F.R. § 109-1.5205 — -1.5205 Personal property management system changes.
Citation: us:cfr:41:109-1.5205
Creator: Chapter 109—Department Of Energy Property Management Regulations
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-109-1.5205
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 109-1 (1—Introduction). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any proposed significant change to a designated contractor's approved personal property management system shall be reviewed by the property administrator at the earliest possible time. Such changes should then be approved in writing on an interim basis, or disapproved in writing, by the property administrator as appropriate.
