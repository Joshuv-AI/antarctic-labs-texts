12 U.S.C. § 1458 — Territorial applicability
Citation: us:usc:12:1458
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section1458
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 11A (Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any other law, this chapter shall be applicable to the several States, the District of Columbia, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, and the territories and possessions of the United States.
