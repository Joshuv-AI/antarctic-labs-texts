49 U.S.C. § 30145 — Importing motor vehicles or equipment requiring further manufacturing
Citation: us:usc:49:30145
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section30145
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part A (General); Chapter 301 (Motor Vehicle Safety); Subchapter III (Importing Noncomplying Motor Vehicles And Equipment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Section 30112(a) of this title does not apply to a motor vehicle or motor vehicle equipment if the vehicle or equipment—
(1)
requires further manufacturing to perform its intended function as decided under regulations prescribed by the Secretary of Transportation; and
(2)
is accompanied at the time of importation by a written statement issued by the manufacturer indicating the applicable motor vehicle safety standard prescribed under this chapter with which it does not comply.
