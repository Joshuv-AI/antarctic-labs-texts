7 U.S.C. § 7812 — Regulations
Citation: us:usc:7:7812
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section7812
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 105 (Hass Avocado Promotion, Research, And Information). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary may issue such regulations as are necessary to carry out this chapter and the powers vested in the Secretary by this chapter, including regulations relating to the assessment of late payment charges and interest.
