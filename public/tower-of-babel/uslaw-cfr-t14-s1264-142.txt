14 C.F.R. § 1264.142 — Collection of civil penalties and assessments.
Citation: us:cfr:14:1264.142
Creator: National Aeronautics And Space Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-1264.142
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 1264 (Implementation Of The Program Fraud Civil Penalties Act Of 1986). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Sections 3806 and 3808(b) of Title 31, United States Code, authorize actions for collection of civil penalties and assessments imposed under this part and specify the procedures for such actions.
