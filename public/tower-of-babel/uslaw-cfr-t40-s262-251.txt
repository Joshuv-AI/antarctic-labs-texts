40 C.F.R. § 262.251 — Maintenance and operation of facility.
Citation: us:cfr:40:262.251
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-262.251
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 262 (Standards Applicable To Generators Of Hazardous Waste). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A large quantity generator must maintain and operate its facility to minimize the possibility of a fire, explosion, or any unplanned sudden or non-sudden release of hazardous waste or hazardous waste constituents to air, soil, or surface water which could threaten human health or the environment.
