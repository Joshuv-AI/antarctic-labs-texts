12 U.S.C. § 376 — Rate of interest paid to directors, etc.
Citation: us:usc:12:376
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section376
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 3 (Federal Reserve System); Subchapter X (Powers And Duties Of Member Banks). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No member bank shall pay to any director, officer, attorney, or employee a greater rate of interest on the deposits of such director, officer, attorney, or employee than that paid to other depositors on similar deposits with such member bank.
