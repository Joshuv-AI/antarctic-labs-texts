10 C.F.R. § 830.201 — Performance of work.
Citation: us:cfr:10:830.201
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-830.201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 830 (Nuclear Safety Management). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A contractor must perform work in accordance with the DOE-approved safety basis for a Hazard Category 1, 2, or 3 DOE nuclear facility and, in particular, with the hazard controls that ensure adequate protection of workers, the public, and the environment.
