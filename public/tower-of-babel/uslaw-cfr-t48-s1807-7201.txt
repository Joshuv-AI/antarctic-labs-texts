48 C.F.R. § 1807.7201 — 1807.7201 Definitions.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1807.7201
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1807 (Acquisition Planning). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Class of contracts means a grouping of acquisitions, either by dollar value or by the nature of supplies and services to be acquired.
Contract opportunity means planned new contract awards exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold (SAT).
