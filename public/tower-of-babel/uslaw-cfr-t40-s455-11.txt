40 C.F.R. § 455.11 — Compliance date for pretreatment standards for existing sources (PSES).
Citation: us:cfr:40:455.11
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-455.11
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 455 (Pesticide Chemicals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All discharges subject to pretreatment standards for existing sources (PSES) in subparts A and B of this part must comply with the standards no later than September 28, 1993.
