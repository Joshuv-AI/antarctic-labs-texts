18 C.F.R. § 430.29 — Appeals.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 430 (Ground Water Protection Area: Pennsylvania). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any person aggrieved by any action or decision of the Executive Director taken under these regulations shall be entitled upon timely filing of a request therefor, to a hearing in accordance with Article 6 of the Commission'a Rules of Practice and Procedure.
