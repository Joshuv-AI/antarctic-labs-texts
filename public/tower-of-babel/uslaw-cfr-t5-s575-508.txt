5 C.F.R. § 575.508 — What is the maximum amount of service that may be covered by an extended assignment incentive?
Citation: us:cfr:5:575.508
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-575.508
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 575 (Recruitment, Relocation, And Retention Incentives; Supervisory Differentials; And Extended Assignment Incentives). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee's total service under one or more extended assignment incentive service agreements with a particular agency for service in a particular territory, possession, or commonwealth may not exceed 5 years. For this purpose, a year is equal to 365 days, resulting in a total service limit of 1,825 days.
