18 U.S.C. § 1586 — Service on vessels in slave trade
Citation: us:usc:18:1586
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section1586
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part I (Crimes); Chapter 77 (Peonage, Slavery, And Trafficking In Persons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever, being a citizen or resident of the United States, voluntarily serves on board of any vessel employed or made use of in the transportation of slaves from any foreign country or place to another, shall be fined under this title or imprisoned not more than two years, or both.
