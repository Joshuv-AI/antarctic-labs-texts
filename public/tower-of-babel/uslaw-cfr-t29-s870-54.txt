29 C.F.R. § 870.54 — Standards governing the granting of an application for exemption.
Citation: us:cfr:29:870.54
Creator: Wage And Hour Division, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-870.54
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 870 (Restriction On Garnishment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Administrator may grant any application for the exemption of State-regulated garnishments whenever he finds that the laws of the State satisfy the policy expressed in § 870.51(a).
