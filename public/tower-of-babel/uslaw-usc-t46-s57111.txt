46 U.S.C. § 57111 — Definition of obsolete vessel
Citation: us:usc:46:57111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section57111
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part F (Government-Owned Merchant Vessels); Chapter 571 (General Authority). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter, the term “obsolete vessel” means a vessel that—
(1)
is or will be in the custody and control of the Maritime Administration for purposes of disposing of the vessel; and
(2)
has been determined by the Secretary of Transportation to be of insufficient value, with respect to the programs of the Maritime Administration, to warrant—
(A)
preserving for future use or spare parts harvesting; or
(B)
retaining in the National Defense Reserve Fleet.
