49 C.F.R. § 22.59 — Loan modifications.
Citation: us:cfr:49:22.59
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-22.59
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 22 (Short-Term Lending Program (Stlp)). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Any modification to the terms of the DOT OSDBU guarantee agreement must have prior written approval of the Director, and executed in writing as an Addendum to the original guarantee agreement.
