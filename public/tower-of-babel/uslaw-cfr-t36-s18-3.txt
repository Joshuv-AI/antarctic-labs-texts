36 C.F.R. § 18.3 — What property may be leased?
Citation: us:cfr:36:18.3
Creator: National Park Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-18.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 18 (Leasing Of Properties In Park Areas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) In general. The Director may lease any property (except non-historic land) under this part if the Director makes the determinations required by § 18.4.
(b) Non-historic land. Non-historic land may not be leased under this part. Certain non-historic land is eligible for leasing under 36 CFR part 17.
