38 U.S.C. § 1720B — Respite care
Citation: us:usc:38:1720B
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section1720B
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part II (General Benefits); Chapter 17 (Hospital, Nursing Home, Domiciliary, And Medical Care); Subchapter II (Hospital, Nursing Home, Or Domiciliary Care And Medical Treatment). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary may furnish respite care services to a veteran who is enrolled to receive care under section 1710 of this title.
(b)
For the purpose of this section, the term “respite care services” means care and services which—
(1)
are of limited duration;
(2)
are furnished on an intermittent basis to a veteran who is suffering from a chronic illness and who resides primarily at home; and
(3)
are furnished for the purpose of helping the veteran to continue residing primarily at home.
(c)
In furnishing respite care services, the Secretary may enter into contract arrangements.
