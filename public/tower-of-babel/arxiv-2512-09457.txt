t t -Fold s s -Blocking Sets and s s -Minimal Codes
Hao Chen Xu Pan, Conghui Xie

Abstract
Blocking sets and minimal codes have been studied for many years in projective geometry and coding theory. In this paper, we provide a new lower bound on the size of t t -fold s s -blocking sets without the condition t ≤ q t\leq q , which is stronger than the classical result of Beutelspacher in 1983. Then a lower bound on lengths of projective s s -minimal codes is also obtained. It is proved that ( s + 1 ) (s+1) -minimal codes are certainly s s -minimal codes. We generalize the Ashikhmin-Barg condition for minimal codes to s s -minimal codes. Many infinite families of s s -minimal codes satisfying and violating this generalized Ashikhmin-Barg condition are constructed. We also give several examples which are binary minimal codes, but not 2 2 -minimal codes. Key Words: t t -fold s s -blocking set, cutting s s -blocking set, minimal subcode, s-minimal code.

Preliminaries

This section introduces some notions and basic properties used in this paper.
Let
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
be the

k-1

\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k}

The
t
-fold
s
-blocking set in
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)

Definition 2.1
.

Let
t
,
s
and
k
be positive integers such that
1\leq s\leq k-1.

A
t
-fold
s
-blocking set of

B\subseteq\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)

|B\bigcap U|\geq t
for every projective subspace
U\subset\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
of codimension
s
.
When
s=t=1
,
B
is simply called a blocking set.

A cutting
s
-blocking set of
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
was introduced in
[
9
]
.
A cutting
s
-blocking set is actually a
(k-s)
-fold strong blocking set, which was introduced
independently in
[
17
]
.

Definition 2.2

(
[
9
]
)
.

Let
s
and
k
be positive integers such that
1\leq s\leq k-1.

A cutting
s
-blocking set of
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
is a set
B\subseteq\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
such that
U
is generated by
B\bigcap U
for every projective subspace
U\subset\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
of codimension
s
.
A cutting
1
-blocking set is called a cutting blocking set (also strong blocking set).

For a vector space
U
over
\mathbb{F}_{q}
of dimension
k
, we assume that

\,\,{\rm SUB}^{s}(U)=\{V\,|\,V\text{ is a subspace of $U$ with dimension }s\}

for
0\leq s\leq k.

For a nonempty subset
X
of
\mathbb{F}_{q}^{n}
, the
support
of
X
is

\mathrm{supp}(X)=\{j\in[n]\,|\,\text{there exists }\,\mathbf{x}=(x_{1},x_{2},\cdots,x_{n})\in X\text{ with }x_{j}\neq 0\},

and the
support weight
of

is
w(X)=|\mathrm{supp}(X)|.

For any vector
\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{n}
, the support weight of the subspace generated by
\mathbf{x}
is equal to the Hamming weight of
\mathbf{x}
denoted by

Definition 2.3
.

For an
[n,k]_{q}
-linear code
C
and
1\leq s\leq k
, the
s
-generalized Hamming weight (
s
-GHW) of

is defined as

The set
\{d_{1}(C),d_{2}(C),\cdots,d_{k}(C)\}
is called the generalized Hamming weight hierarchy of
C
.

When

, the parameter
d_{1}(C)
is the minimum Hamming weight of
C
.
For an
[n,k]_{q}
-linear code

, the

[
34

is provided as following:

1\leq d_{1}(C)<d_{2}(C)<\cdots<d_{k}(C)\leq n.

The monotonicity bound on the generalized Hamming weights produces
the Singleton bound on the generalized Hamming weights
as following:

d_{s}(C)\leq n-k+s

for
1\leq s\leq k.

The following Griesmer bounds for generalized Hamming weight of linear codes are proved in Theorem 1 of
[
24
]
.

Lemma 2.4
.

Let
C
be an
[n,k]_{q}
-linear code with the generalized Hamming weight hierarchy

(a)

3
Some new lower bound for the size of
t
-fold
s
-blocking sets

Let
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
be the
finite projective geometry of dimension
k-1
over
\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k}
.
Then there is a one-to-one correspondence between all the points of
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
and

{\rm SUB}^{1}(\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k}).

In the following theorem, we provide some new lower bound for the size of
t
-fold
s
-blocking sets. For a
t
-fold
s
-blocking set
B
of
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
, the complementary set of
B
in
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
is
denoted by
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)\backslash B
.

Theorem 3.1
.

Let
B
be a
t
-fold
s
-blocking set of
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
with
1\leq s\leq k-1
.

(a)

Then
|B|\geq t\frac{q^{k}-1}{q^{k-s}-1}.

(b)

If
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)\backslash B
does not generate
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q),

then
|B|\geq q^{k-1}.

(c)

If
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)\backslash B
generates
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q),
then

|B|\geq\min\{t\frac{q^{s+1}-1}{q-1},\,t+\frac{q^{2}(q^{s}-1)}{q-1}\}.

In particular, when
t\leq q,
we have that
|B|\geq t\frac{q^{s+1}-1}{q-1}.

Proof.

(a)
Suppose that
B
is a
t
-fold
s
-blocking set of
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
of size
n
.
Let
\{G_{1},G_{2},\cdots,G_{n}\}
be the subset of
\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k}
corresponding to
B
.
Let
\Lambda=\{(i,V)\,|\,V\in{\rm SUB}^{k-s}(\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k})\text{ and }G_{i}\in V\}.

Then we know that

\displaystyle|\Lambda|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{n}|\{V\in{\rm SUB}^{k-s}(\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k})\,\big|\,G_{i}\in V\}|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle n\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{0}{k-1}{k-s-1}_{q}.

Since
|B\bigcap V|\geq t
for every
V\in{\rm SUB}^{k-s}(\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k}),
we have that

\displaystyle|\Lambda|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{V\in{\rm SUB}^{k-s}(\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k})}|\{i\in[n]\,\big|\,G_{i}\in V\}|

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\sum_{V\in{\rm SUB}^{k-s}(\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k})}|B\bigcap V|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle t\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{0}{k}{k-s}_{q}.

Hence we get that

|B|=n\geq t\frac{\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{0}{k}{k-s}_{q}}{\genfrac{[}{]}{0.0pt}{0}{k-1}{k-s-1}_{q}}=t\frac{q^{k}-1}{q^{k-s}-1}.

(b)
Since
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)\backslash B
does not generate
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q),
there exists a hyperplane
U
such that
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)\backslash B\subseteq U.

Then
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)\backslash U\subseteq B

and

|B|\geq\frac{q^{k}-1}{q-1}-\frac{q^{k-1}-1}{q-1}=q^{k-1}.

(c)

Let
\{G_{1},G_{2},\cdots,G_{N}\}
be the subset of
\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k}
corresponding to
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)\backslash B
, where
N=\frac{q^{k}-1}{q-1}-|B|.

Let
C
be the linear code generated by the
k\times N
matrix

G=[G^{T}_{1},G^{T}_{2},\cdots,G^{T}_{N}].

Since
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)\backslash B
generates
\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k},

we know that
C
is an
[N,k]_{q}
-linear code.
For any
U\in{\rm SUB}^{s}(C),

there exist subspaces
V\in{\rm SUB}^{s}(\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k})
such that

U=\{\mathbf{y}G\,|\,\mathbf{y}\in V\}.

By Lemma
2.8
, we know that

\displaystyle w(U)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle n-m_{G}(V^{\bot})

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{q^{k}-1}{q-1}-|B|-(\frac{q^{k-s}-1}{q-1}-|B\bigcap V^{\bot}|)

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{q^{k}-q^{k-s}}{q-1}-|B|+|B\bigcap V^{\bot}|

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{q^{k}-q^{k-s}}{q-1}-|B|+t

and
d_{s}(C)\geq\frac{q^{k}-q^{k-s}}{q-1}-|B|+t.

By the Griesmer bound on the generalized Hamming weights, we have that

\displaystyle N

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle d_{s}(C)+\sum_{i=1}^{k-s}\lceil\frac{(q-1)d_{s}(C)}{q^{i}(q^{s}-1)}\rceil

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{q^{k}-q^{k-s}}{q-1}-|B|+t+\sum_{i=1}^{k-s}\lceil\frac{q^{k}-q^{k-s}-(|B|-t)(q-1)}{q^{i}(q^{s}-1)}\rceil

\displaystyle\geq

\displaystyle\frac{q^{k}-q^{k-s}}{q-1}-(|B|-t)+\sum_{i=1}^{k-s}\lceil q^{k-s-i}-\frac{(|B|-t)(q-1)}{q^{i}(q^{s}-1)}\rceil

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{q^{k}-q^{k-s}}{q-1}-(|B|-t)+\sum_{i=1}^{k-s}q^{k-s-i}-\sum_{i=1}^{k-s}\lfloor\frac{(|B|-t)(q-1)}{q^{i}(q^{s}-1)}\rfloor

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\frac{q^{k}-1}{q-1}-(|B|-t)-\sum_{i=1}^{k-s}\lfloor\frac{(|B|-t)(q-1)}{q^{i}(q^{s}-1)}\rfloor.

Since
N=\frac{q^{k}-1}{q-1}-|B|
, we know that

t\leq\sum_{i=1}^{k-s}\lfloor\frac{(|B|-t)(q-1)}{q^{i}(q^{s}-1)}\rfloor\leq\frac{(|B|-t)(q-1)}{q(q^{s}-1)}+\sum_{i=2}^{k-s}\lfloor\frac{(|B|-t)(q-1)}{q^{i}(q^{s}-1)}\rfloor.

We analysis the following two cases.

Case 1
. Suppose that
\frac{(|B|-t)(q-1)}{q^{2}(q^{s}-1)}<1,

which means
\sum\limits_{i=2}^{k-s}\lfloor\frac{(|B|-t)(q-1)}{q^{i}(q^{s}-1)}\rfloor=0.
Then we know that

t\leq\frac{(|B|-t)(q-1)}{q(q^{s}-1)}

and

|B|\geq t\frac{q^{s+1}-1}{q-1}.

Case 2
. Suppose that
\frac{(|B|-t)(q-1)}{q^{2}(q^{s}-1)}\geq 1,
which means

|B|\geq t+\frac{q^{2}(q^{s}-1)}{q-1}.

Hence
|B|\geq\min\{t\frac{q^{s+1}-1}{q-1},\,t+\frac{q^{2}(q^{s}-1)}{q-1}\}.

In particular, when
t\leq q,
we have that

∎

In this section, we provide a connection between

-minimal code, then

In the following definition, we introduce the notion of

-minimal subcode and

Definition 4.1
.

Let

[n,k]_{q}
-linear code. For

,

is called a

-minimal subcode of

, if there exists a

such that
\mathrm{supp}(V)\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(U)
then

If

U
is a
s
-minimal subcodes of
C
for every
U\in{\rm SUB}^{s}(C)
, then

is called a
s
-minimal code.

When
s=1
, a
s
-minimal linear code is also called a
minimal code

[
5
]
.

A cutting blocking set of
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
of size
n
corresponds to
a minimal
[n,k]_{q}
-linear code in
[
2
,
21
,
32
]
.
In the following theorem, we prove that a cutting
s
-blocking set of
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
of size
n
corresponds to
a projective
s
-minimal
[n,k]_{q}
-linear code.

Theorem 4.2
.

Let
s
and
k
be positive integers such that
1\leq s\leq k-1.

There exists a cutting
s
-blocking set of
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
of size
n
if and only if there exists a projective
s
-minimal
[n,k]_{q}
-linear code.

Proof.

(\Rightarrow)
Suppose that
B
is a cutting
s
-blocking set of
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
of size
n
.
Let
\{G_{1},G_{2},\cdots,G_{n}\}
be the subset of
\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k}
corresponding to
B
, where
\mathbf{0}\neq G_{i}\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k}
for all
1\leq i\leq n
.
Let
C
be an
[n,k]_{q}
-linear code generated by the
k\times n
matrix

G=[G^{T}_{1},G^{T}_{2},\cdots,G^{T}_{n}].

Since
\langle G_{i}\rangle\neq\langle G_{j}\rangle
for
i\neq j,

we know that
C
is projective.

Let
U_{1}
and
U_{2}
be in
{\rm SUB}^{s}(C)
such that
\mathrm{supp}(U_{1})\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(U_{2}).

There exist subspaces
V_{1}
and
V_{2}
in
{\rm SUB}^{s}(\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k})
such that

U_{1}=\{\mathbf{y}G\,|\,\mathbf{y}\in V_{1}\}\text{ and }U_{2}=\{\mathbf{y}G\,|\,\mathbf{y}\in V_{2}\}.

Let
\tilde{V}_{j}^{\bot}
be the projective subspace of
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
of codimension
s
corresponding to
V_{j}^{\bot}

for
j=1,2.

Since
[n]\backslash\mathrm{supp}(U_{j})=\{i\in[n]\,|\,G_{i}\in V_{j}^{\bot}\}
for
j=1,2

and
[n]\backslash\mathrm{supp}(U_{2})\subseteq[n]\backslash\mathrm{supp}(U_{1}),

we have that

B\bigcap\tilde{V}_{2}^{\bot}\subseteq B\bigcap\tilde{V}_{1}^{\bot}.

Since
B
is a cutting
s
-blocking set,
we get that

\tilde{V}_{2}^{\bot}=\langle B\bigcap\tilde{V}_{2}^{\bot}\rangle\subseteq\langle B\bigcap\tilde{V}_{1}^{\bot}\rangle=\tilde{V}_{1}^{\bot}

and
\tilde{V}_{1}^{\bot}=\tilde{V}_{2}^{\bot}.
Then
V_{1}=V_{2}
and
U_{1}=U_{2}
.
Hence
C
is a projective
s
-minimal
[n,k]_{q}
-linear code.

(\Leftarrow)
Suppose that
C
is a projective
s
-minimal
[n,k]_{q}
-linear code with a generator matrix

G=[G^{T}_{1},G^{T}_{2},\cdots,G^{T}_{n}],

where
\mathbf{0}\neq G_{i}\in\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k}
for all
1\leq i\leq n
.
Since
C
is projective,
we know that
\langle G_{i}\rangle\neq\langle G_{j}\rangle
for
i\neq j
.
Let
B
be a set of
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
of size
n
corresponding to
\{G_{1},G_{2},\cdots,G_{n}\}
.
Then the size of
B
is
n
.

Suppose that there exists a projective subspace
\tilde{V}_{1}
of
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
of codimension
s

such that
\tilde{V}_{1}
is not generated by
B\bigcap\tilde{V}_{1}.

Then there exists a projective subspace
\tilde{V}_{2}
of
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
of codimension
s

such that

B\bigcap V_{1}\subsetneqq B\bigcap V_{2}.

Let
V_{j}\in{\rm SUB}^{k-s}(\mathbb{F}_{q}^{k})
such that
V_{j}
corresponding
\tilde{V}_{j}
for
j=1,2.

Let
U_{j}=\{\mathbf{y}G\,|\,\mathbf{y}\in V_{j}^{\bot}\}
for
j=1,2.

Then we know that
U_{1}
and
U_{2}
are in
{\rm SUB}^{s}(C)
such that

\mathrm{supp}(U_{1})\subsetneqq\mathrm{supp}(U_{2}),

which is a contradiction.
Hence

is a cutting
s
-blocking set of
\mathrm{PG}(k-1,q)
of size
n
.
∎

For an

-linear code

Theorem 4.3
.

Let

be an
[n,k]_{q}
-linear code with a projection

for

. Then

Proof.

Let

By the definition of

-minimal subcode of

Theorem 4.5

Let

is a
(s+1)

(b)

(a)

-minimal code.

(s+1)
-minimal code, we have that

\mathrm{supp}(\langle\mathbf{x}+\alpha\mathbf{z},U_{1}\rangle)=\mathrm{supp}(\langle\mathbf{x}+\alpha\mathbf{z},U_{2}\rangle)

and

i_{0}\in\mathrm{supp}(U_{2})\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(\langle\mathbf{x}+\alpha\mathbf{z},U_{1}\rangle).

But we know that
i_{0}\notin\mathrm{supp}(U_{1})
and

which is a contradiction.
Hence

-minimal code.

(b)
It is a direct result of Statement

and Theorem
4.2
.

5

-Minimal subcodes of linear codes

In this section, we provide some properties of
s
-minimal subcodes, and give some sufficient conditions of that a subcode is a
s
-minimal subcode.
For an
[n,k]_{q}
-linear code
C
, let
H
be the parity-check matrix of
C
. The submatrix of
H

consisting columns indexed by a subset
X\subseteq[n]
, is denoted by
H(X)
.
The basic properties of
s
-minimal subcodes are characterized in the following lemma.

Lemma 5.1
.

Let
C
be an
[n,k]_{q}
-linear code with the generalized Hamming weight hierarchy
\{d_{1}(C),d_{2}(C),\cdots,d_{k}(C)\}
,
and let
H
be a parity-check matrix of
C
.

(a)

For
U\in{\rm SUB}^{s}(C)
,
U
is
a
s
-minimal subcode of
C
if and only if the rank of
H(\mathrm{supp}(U))
is
|\mathrm{supp}(U)|-s.

(b)

If
U
is
a
s
-minimal subcode of
C
, we have that
|\mathrm{supp}(U)|\leq n-k+s.

Proof.

(a)
Suppose that
U
is a
s
-minimal subcode of
C
.
Let
G_{U}
be a generator matrix of
U.

Then we know that

G_{U}(\mathrm{supp}(U))H^{T}(\mathrm{supp}(U))=\mathbf{0},

and the rank of
G_{U}(\mathrm{supp}(U))
is equal to
s
. Hence the rank of
H(\mathrm{supp}(U))
is less than or equal to
|\mathrm{supp}(U)|-s.

If the rank of
H(\mathrm{supp}(U))
is less than
|\mathrm{supp}(U)|-s,

then there exists
V\in{\rm SUB}^{s}(C)
such that
U\neq V
and
\mathrm{supp}(V)\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(U),

which is a contradiction. Hence the rank of
H(\mathrm{supp}(U))
is equal to
|\mathrm{supp}(U)|-s.

Suppose the rank of
H(\mathrm{supp}(U))
is equal to
|\mathrm{supp}(U)|-s.

For any
V\in{\rm SUB}^{s}(C)
such that
\mathrm{supp}(V)\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(U),

let
G_{V}
be a generator matrix of
V.
Then we have that

G_{V}(\mathrm{supp}(U))H^{T}(\mathrm{supp}(U))=\mathbf{0}

Since the rank of
G_{U}(\mathrm{supp}(U))
and the rank of
G_{V}(\mathrm{supp}(U))
are both equal to
s
, we have that
V=U.

(b)
Suppose that
U
is a
s
-minimal subcode of
C
.
Since the rank of
H(\mathrm{supp}(U))
is less than or equal to
n-k,

we have that
|\mathrm{supp}(U)|\leq n-k+s
by Statement
(a)
.
∎

In the following theorem, we generalize Ashikhminand-Barg theorem
[
5
]
from codewords to subcodes of a linear code.

.

which is a contradiction.

(b)

By Lemma

|\mathrm{supp}(U)|<d_{s+1}(C)

Generalized Ashikhminand-Barg theorem for
s

In this section, we provide a generalization of the Ashikhminand-Barg theorem for
s
-minimal codes.

Theorem 6.1
.

Let
C
be an
[n,k]_{q}
-linear code with the
s
-generalized Hamming weight
d_{s}(C)
and the
s
-maximum Hamming weight
D_{s}(C)
, where
1\leq s\leq k-1
.

(a)

If
D_{s}(C)<d_{s+1}(C),
then
C
is a
s
-minimal code.

(b)

If
\frac{D_{s}(C)}{d_{s}(C)}<\frac{q^{s+1}-1}{q^{s+1}-q},
then
C
is a
s
-minimal code.

Proof.

The proofs of both Statements
(a)
and
(b)
are similar.
For any
U\in{\rm SUB}^{s}(C)
, we know that
|\mathrm{supp}(U)|\leq D_{s}(C)<d_{s+1}(C).

By Theorem
5.2
, we know that
U
is a
s
-minimal subcode of
C,
and
C
is a
s
-minimal code.
∎

In the following example, we show that a simplex code of dimension
k
is a
s
-minimal code for
1\leq s\leq k-1.

Example 6.2
.

For
k\geq 2
, let
S_{q,k}
be a
k\times\frac{q^{k}-1}{q-1}
matrix over
\mathbb{F}_{q}
such that every two columns of
S_{q,k}
are linearly independent.
Let
\mathcal{S}_{q,k}
be the
[\frac{q^{k}-1}{q-1},k,q^{k-1}]_{q}
-linear code generated by
S_{q,k}.

It is well-known that

d_{s}(\mathcal{S}_{q,k})=D_{s}(\mathcal{S}_{q,k})=|\mathrm{supp}(U)|=\frac{q^{k}-q^{k-s}}{q-1}

for any
U\in{\rm SUB}^{s}(\mathcal{S}_{q,k})
and
1\leq s\leq k.

By Theorem
6.1
, we know that
\mathcal{S}_{q,k}
is a
s
-minimal code for
1\leq s\leq k-1.

In the following corollary, we prove that some linear codes constructed by punctured on simplex codes are
s
-minimal codes.

Corollary 6.3
.

Let
\mathcal{S}_{q,k}
be the
[\frac{q^{k}-1}{q-1},k,q^{k-1}]_{q}
-linear code generated by
S_{q,k}.

Let
C
be the linear code constructed by punctured on any
t
coordinates of
\mathcal{S}_{q,k}
.
If
1\leq s\leq k-1
and
t<q^{k-s-1},
then
C
is a
s
-minimal code.

Proof.

Let
\phi_{t}
be the linear map from
\mathcal{S}_{q,k}
to
C
by punctured on the
t
coordinates of
\mathcal{S}_{q,k}
.
Since the minimum Hamming weight of
\mathcal{S}_{q,k}
is
q^{k-1}
and
t<q^{k-s-1}<q^{k-1},

we know that
\phi_{t}
is bijective.
Then there exists
V\in{\rm SUB}^{s}(\mathcal{S}_{q,k})
for any
U\in{\rm SUB}^{s}(C)
such that
\phi_{t}(V)=U

and

w(V)-t\leq w(U)\leq w(V).

Hence we have that

D_{s}(C)\leq D_{s}(\mathcal{S}_{q,k})=\frac{q^{k}-q^{k-s}}{q-1}

and

d_{s+1}(C)\geq d_{s+1}(\mathcal{S}_{q,k})-t=\frac{q^{k}-q^{k-s-1}}{q-1}-t.

Since

d_{s+1}(C)-D_{s}(C)\geq q^{k-s-1}-t>0,

we know that
C
is a
s
-minimal code by Theorem
6.1
.
∎

-linear code

s
-minimal code by adding more columns in the generator matrix

Theorem 6.4
.

Let

be an

-generalized Hamming weight

n^{\prime}=\left\lceil\frac{(q^{s+1}-1)d_{s}(C)}{q^{s+1}-q}\right\rceil-D_{1}(C).

Then we construct an explicit
q
-ary
s
-minimal code
C^{\prime}\subset\mathbb{F}_{q}^{n+n^{\prime}}
with the
s
-maximum Hamming weight at least
D_{1}(C)+n^{\prime}
and the
s
-generalized Hamming weight
d_{s}(C)
satisfying

\frac{d_{s}(C^{\prime})}{D_{s}(C^{\prime})}\leq\frac{q^{s+1}-q}{q^{s+1}-1}.

Proof.

Let
G
be a generator matrix of
C
, where the first row
{\bf r}_{1}
is the codeword
(c_{1},\ldots,c_{n})
with maximum weight
D_{1}(C)
, and the second row
{\bf r}_{2}
is a codeword with the minimum weight
d_{1}(C)
.
Let
G^{\prime}
be a
k\times(n+n^{\prime})
matrix with the first row
{\bf r}_{1}^{\prime}=(c_{1}^{\prime},\ldots,c_{n}^{\prime},c_{1},\ldots,c_{n})
, where
c_{1}^{\prime},\ldots,c_{n}^{\prime}
are nonzero elements in
\mathbb{F}_{q}
, the remaining rows are of the form
{\bf r}_{i}^{\prime}=({\bf 0},{\bf r}_{i})
, where
{\bf 0}
denotes the zero vector in
\mathbb{F}_{q}^{n^{\prime}}
and
{\bf r}_{i}
is the
i
-th row of
G
, for
i=2,\ldots,k
.

It is clear that the rank of the matrix
G^{\prime}
remains
k
.
Denote by
C^{\prime}
the linear code in
\mathbb{F}_{q}^{n+n^{\prime}}
generated by
G^{\prime}
.
According to the defining of
C^{\prime}
, the
s
-generalized Hamming weight of
C^{\prime}
remains
d_{s}(C)
.
The
s
-maximum Hamming weight of
C^{\prime}
is at least
n^{\prime}+D_{1}(C)
, since the Hamming weight of the first row of
G^{\prime}
is
n^{\prime}+D_{1}(C)
.
We now prove that
C^{\prime}
is a minimal code.
Let
U_{1},U_{2}\in{\rm SUB}^{s}(C)
satisfying

\mathrm{supp}(U_{2})\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(U_{1}).

We analysis the following two cases.

Case 1
.
U_{1}
is the subspace of
C^{\prime}
formed by some linear combinations of rows
{\bf r}_{2}^{\prime},\ldots,{\bf r}_{k}^{\prime}
of
G^{\prime}
.
In this case, the support of
U_{1}
is in the last
n
coordinate positions.
Then the support of
U_{2}
is also in the last
n
coordinate positions, and
U_{2}
is some linear combinations of
{\bf r}_{2}^{\prime},\ldots,{\bf r}_{k}^{\prime}
rows of
G^{\prime}
.
Hence, from the minimality of the linear code
C
, we have
U_{2}=U_{1}
.

Case 2
.
U_{1}
is the subspace of
C^{\prime}
formed by some linear combinations of rows
{\bf r}_{1}^{\prime},\ldots,{\bf r}_{k}^{\prime}
of
G^{\prime}
.
Suppose that

{\bf x}_{1}=\mu_{1}{\bf r}_{1}^{\prime}+\mu_{2}{\bf c}\in U_{1},

where
\mu_{1}\neq 0
, and
{\bf c}
is a linear combination of rows
{\bf r}_{2}^{\prime},\ldots,{\bf r}_{k}^{\prime}
in
G^{\prime}
.
Let
U_{i}^{\prime}
be the subspace in
\mathbb{F}_{q}^{n}
obtained by puncturing the first
n^{\prime}
coordinates of all vectors in
U_{i}
,
i=1,2
.
They are two subspaces of code
C
, and we have
\mathrm{supp}(U_{2}^{\prime})\subseteq\mathrm{supp}(U_{1}^{\prime})
.
Then
U_{2}^{\prime}=U_{1}^{\prime}
according to the minimality of
C
.
Therefore,
U_{2}
cannot be a subspace formed by some linear combinations of only
{\bf r}_{2}^{\prime},\ldots,{\bf r}_{k}^{\prime}
in the generator matrix
G^{\prime}
.
Then the support of
U_{2}
has to include the first
n^{\prime}
coordinate positions.
From a similar argument as above, we also conclude that
U_{2}=U_{1}
.
Then the linear code
C^{\prime}
is
s
-minimal.

The
s
-maximum Hamming weight of
C^{\prime}
is at least

D_{s}(C^{\prime})\geq D_{1}(C)+n^{\prime}\ =\left\lceil\frac{(q^{s+1}-1)d_{s}(C)}{q^{s+1}-q}\right\rceil.

It is easy to verify that

\frac{d_{s}(C^{\prime})}{D_{s}(C^{\prime})}\leq\frac{d_{s}(C)}{D_{1}(C)+n^{\prime}}\leq\frac{q^{s+1}-q}{q^{s+1}-1}.

The
s
-minimal code
C^{\prime}
violates the generalized Ashkhmin-Barg condition.
The conclusion is proved.
∎

.
