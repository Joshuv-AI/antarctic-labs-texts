Andro Hydro, LLC; Notice of Effectiveness of Withdrawal of 
Request for Non-Capacity Amendment Application

    On June 20, 2023, and supplemented December 5, 2025, Andro Hydro, 
LLC, filed a non-capacity amendment application for the Riley-Jay-
Livermore Hydroelectric Project No. 2375. On March 4, 2026, Andro 
Hydro, LLC, filed a request to withdraw its amendment application.
    No motion in opposition to the request for withdrawal has been 
filed, and the Commission has taken no action to disallow the 
withdrawal. Pursuant to Rule 216(b) of the Commission's Rules of 
Practice and Procedure,\1\ the withdrawal of the application became 
effective on March 19, 2026, and this proceeding is hereby terminated.
