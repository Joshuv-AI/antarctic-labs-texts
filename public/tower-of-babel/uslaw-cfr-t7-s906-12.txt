7 C.F.R. § 906.12 — Container.
Citation: us:cfr:7:906.12
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-906.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 906 (Oranges And Grapefruit Grown In Lower Rio Grande Valley In Texas). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Container means any box, bag, crate, hamper, basket, package, bulk carton, or any other type of receptacle used in the packaging, transportation, sale, or other handling of fruit.
