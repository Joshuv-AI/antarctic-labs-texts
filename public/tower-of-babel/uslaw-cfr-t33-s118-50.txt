33 C.F.R. § 118.50 — Inspection.
Citation: us:cfr:33:118.50
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-118.50
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 118 (Bridge Lighting And Other Signals). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Lights and other signals required or authorized under this part are subject to inspection at any time by Coast Guard personnel or authorized agents.
