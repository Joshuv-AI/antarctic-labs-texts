48 C.F.R. § 538.7002-9 — 538.7002-9 Tribally controlled schools.
Citation: us:cfr:48:538.7002-9
Creator: Chapter 5—General Services Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-538.7002-9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 538 (Federal Supply Schedule Contracting). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

25 U.S.C. 2507(a)(6) allows tribally controlled schools, as defined under 25 U.S.C. 2511, that have an active grant pursuant to 25 U.S.C. chapter 27 to purchase supplies or services for the purposes of carrying out the grant.
