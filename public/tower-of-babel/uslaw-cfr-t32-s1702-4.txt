32 C.F.R. § 1702.4 — Notification to Office of General Counsel.
Citation: us:cfr:32:1702.4
Creator: Office Of The Director Of National Intelligence
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-1702.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 32 (National Defense), Part 1702 (Procedures Governing The Acceptance Of Service Of Process). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An ODNI employee who receives or has reason to expect to receive, service of process in an official, individual or combined individual and official capacity in a matter that may involve testimony or the furnishing of documents that could reasonably be expected to involve ODNI interests, shall promptly notify the OGC ((703) 275-2527) prior to responding to the service in any manner, and if possible, before accepting service.
