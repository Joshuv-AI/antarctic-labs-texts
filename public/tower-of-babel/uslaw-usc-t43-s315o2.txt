43 U.S.C. § 315o–2 — Animals and equipment for field employees
Citation: us:usc:43:315o–2
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title43-section315o–2
Context: United States Code, Title 43 (Public Lands); Chapter 8A (Grazing Lands); Subchapter I (Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary of the Interior may require field employees of the Bureau of Land Management to furnish horses and miscellaneous equipment necessary for the performance of their official work and may provide at Government expense forage, care, and housing for such animals and equipment.
