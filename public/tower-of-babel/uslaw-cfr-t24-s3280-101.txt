24 C.F.R. § 3280.101 — Scope.

Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 3280 (Manufactured Home Construction And Safety Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Subpart B states the planning requirements in manufactured homes. The intent of this subpart is to assure the adequacy of architectural planning considerations which assist in determining a safe and healthful environment.
