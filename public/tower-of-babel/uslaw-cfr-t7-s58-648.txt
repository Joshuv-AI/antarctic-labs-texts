7 C.F.R. § 58.648 — Microbiological requirements for ice cream.
Citation: us:cfr:7:58.648
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Standards, Inspections, Marketing Practices), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-58.648
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 58 (Grading And Inspection, General Specifications For Approved Plants And Standards For Grades Of Dairy Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The finished product shall contain not more than 50,000 bacteria per gram as determined by the standard plate count, and shall contain not more than 10 coliform organisms per gram for plain and not more than 20 coliform per gram in chocolate, fruit, nut or other flavors in three out of five samples.
