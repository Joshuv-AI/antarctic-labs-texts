36 U.S.C. § 240111 — Liability for acts of officers and agents
Citation: us:usc:36:240111
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section240111
Context: United States Code, Title 36 (Patriotic And National Observances, Ceremonies, And Organizations); Part B (Organizations); Chapter 2401 (Women’S Army Corps Veterans’ Association). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The corporation is liable for the acts of its officers or agents acting within the scope of their authority.
