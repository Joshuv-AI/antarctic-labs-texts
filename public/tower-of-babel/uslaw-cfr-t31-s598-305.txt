31 C.F.R. § 598.305 — Foreign person.
Citation: us:cfr:31:598.305
Creator: Office Of Foreign Assets Control, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-598.305
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 598 (Foreign Narcotics Kingpin Sanctions Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The term foreign person means any citizen or national of a foreign state, wherever located, or any entity not organized under the laws of the United States, but does not include a foreign state.
