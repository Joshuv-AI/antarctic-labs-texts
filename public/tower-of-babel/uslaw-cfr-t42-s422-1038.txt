42 C.F.R. § 422.1038 — Change in time and place of hearing.
Citation: us:cfr:42:422.1038
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-422.1038
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 422 (Medicare Advantage Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The ALJ may change the time and place for the hearing either on his or her own initiative or at the request of a party for good cause shown, or may adjourn or postpone the hearing.
(b) The ALJ may reopen the hearing for receipt of new evidence at any time before mailing the notice of hearing decision.
(c) The ALJ gives the parties reasonable notice of any change in time or place or any adjournment or reopening of the hearing.
