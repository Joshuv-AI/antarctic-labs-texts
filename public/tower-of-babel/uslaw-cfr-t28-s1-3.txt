28 C.F.R. § 1.3 — Eligibility for filing petition for commutation of sentence.
Citation: us:cfr:28:1.3
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-1.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 1 (Executive Clemency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No petition for commutation of sentence, including remission of fine, should be filed if other forms of judicial or administrative relief are available, except upon a showing of exceptional circumstances.
