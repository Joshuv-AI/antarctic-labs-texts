29 C.F.R. § 38.83 — Notice of complaint acceptance.
Citation: us:cfr:29:38.83
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/section-38.83
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 29 (Labor), Part 38 (Implementation Of The Nondiscrimination And Equal Opportunity Provisions Of The Workforce Innovation And Opportunity Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Director accepts the complaint for resolution, the Director must notify in writing the complainant, the respondent, and the grantmaking agency. The notice must:
(a) State that the complaint will be accepted;
(b) Identify the issues over which CRC has accepted jurisdiction; and
(c) Explain the reasons why any issues were rejected.
