25 C.F.R. § 11.306 — Search without a warrant.
Citation: us:cfr:25:11.306
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-11.306
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 11 (Courts Of Indian Offenses And Law And Order Code). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No law enforcement officer shall conduct any search without a valid warrant except:
(a) Incident to making a lawful arrest; or
(b) With the voluntary consent of the person being searched; or
(c) When the search is of a moving vehicle and the officer has probable cause to believe that it contains contraband, stolen property, or property otherwise unlawfully possessed.
