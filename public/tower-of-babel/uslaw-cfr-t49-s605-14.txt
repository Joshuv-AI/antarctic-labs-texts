49 C.F.R. § 605.14 — Agreement.
Citation: us:cfr:49:605.14
Creator: Federal Transit Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-605.14
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 605 (School Bus Operations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Except as provided in § 605.11, no assistance shall be provided under 49 U.S.C. chapter 53 unless the recipient has executed the Private Sector Protections category of the current annual Certifications and Assurances confirming that the recipient will not engage in school bus operations exclusively for the transportation of students and school personnel in competition with private school bus operators.
