14 C.F.R. § 211.12 — Filing and service.
Citation: us:cfr:14:211.12
Creator: Office Of The Secretary, Department Of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings)
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-211.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 211 (Applications For Permits To Foreign Air Carriers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

All types of applications for foreign air carrier permits (initial, renewal, amendment, or transfer) are filed as of the date the applications are received at the Department's Docket Facility. Each applicant shall serve those persons as required in part 302, subpart B, of this chapter.
