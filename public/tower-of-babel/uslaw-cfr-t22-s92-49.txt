22 C.F.R. § 92.49 — “Deposition” defined.
Citation: us:cfr:22:92.49
Creator: Department Of State
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-22/section-92.49
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 22 (Foreign Relations), Part 92 (Notarial And Related Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A deposition is the testimony of a witness taken in writing under oath or affirmation, before some designated or appointed person or officer, in answer to interrogatories, oral or written. (For the distinction between a deposition and an affidavit see § 92.22.)
