31 C.F.R. § 256.60 — How do I get paid for a Private Relief Bill?
Citation: us:cfr:31:256.60
Creator: Fiscal Service, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-256.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 256 (Obtaining Payments From The Judgment Fund And Under Private Relief Bills). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may apply for payment by sending a request letter along with supporting documentation, to include a copy of the private relief act and proof of your identity, to the address specified on the Fiscal Service Web site at http://www.fiscal.treasury.gov/privaterelief.
