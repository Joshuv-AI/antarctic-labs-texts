48 C.F.R. § 239.7400 — 239.7400 Scope.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 239 (Acquisition Of Information Technology). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This subpart prescribes policy and procedures for acquisition of telecommunications services and maintenance of telecommunications security. Telecommunications services meet the definition of information technology.
