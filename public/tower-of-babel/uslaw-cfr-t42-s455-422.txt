42 C.F.R. § 455.422 — Appeal rights.
Citation: us:cfr:42:455.422
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-455.422
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 455 (Program Integrity: Medicaid). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The State Medicaid agency must give providers terminated or denied under § 455.416 any appeal rights available under procedures established by State law or regulations.
