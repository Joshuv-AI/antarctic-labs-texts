21 C.F.R. § 178.2550 — 4-Hydroxymethyl-2,6-di-tert-butylphenol.
Citation: us:cfr:21:178.2550
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-178.2550
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 178 (Indirect Food Additives: Adjuvants, Production Aids, And Sanitizers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

4-Hydroxymethyl-2,6-di-tert-butyl-phenol may be safely used as an antioxidant in articles intended for use in contact with food, in accordance with the following prescribed conditions:
(a) The additive has a solidification point of 140°-141 °C.
(b) The concentration of the additive and any other permitted antioxidants in the finished food-contact article does not exceed a total of 0.5 milligram per square inch of food-contact surface.
