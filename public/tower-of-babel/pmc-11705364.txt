2024 Poultry science association annual meeting
Published: 2024

The 2024 PSA Annual Meeting was held July 15-18, 2024, at The Galt House in downtown Louisville, Kentucky.

Future annual meeting

The 113th Annual Meeting of the Poultry Science Association is scheduled for July 15-18, 2024, at The Galt House in downtown Louisville, Kentucky.

Membership dues

Annual dues for active membership in the Poultry Science Association for 2024 are $120, payable on or before January 1, 2024. Postdoctoral membership is $60 for postdoctoral students. Certification of eligibility is required. Graduate and undergraduate student membership is free of charge for full-time students following a curriculum in, or closely related to, poultry science or an allied field. Certification of eligibility is required. PSA Fellow dues are $80. Honorary and emeritus members pay no dues. Each Journal Section Editor, Subject Editor, and Editor-in-Chief receives a free membership. Article 5, Section 3, of the Constitution reads: “Any member whose dues have not been paid by June 1 of the current year shall be considered to have forfeited membership.”

Report of the board of directors

The Poultry Science Association has contracted Annual Meeting locations through 2028. The 2025 PSA Annual Meeting will be held in Raleigh, North Carolina; 2026 in Toronto, Ontario, Canada (in conjunction with the World's Poultry Congress); 2027 in Fort Worth, Texas (in conjunction with the American Association of Avian Pathologists); 2028 in Calgary, Alberta, Canada.

The PSA Latin American Scientific Conference is October 8-10, 2024, in Iguazú Falls, Paraná, Brazil. Over 700 attendees are expected.

With the success of the PSA Latin American Scientific Conference, the Board of Directors has given approval to holding a Pacific Rim Scientific Conference in Macau in October 2025.

The PSA staff launched a new and more powerful website utilizing the Higher Logic platform. Community networks will allow for networking and conversation between members. The staff is also searching for a new association management software that will serve as the database for all PSA membership, registration, renewals, etc.

The Board has approved the use of electronic posters (e-posters) at the 2025 PSA Annual Meeting in Raleigh. An ad hoc committee is working on the logistics and how the structure of the meeting may have to change to accommodate the poster presentations on shared kiosks.

The first Professional Development Conference will be held October 28-29 in Columbus, Ohio, and will feature speakers and presenters mostly in the areas of teaching, extension, and industry.

Plans are progressing for WPC2026 in Toronto. PSA was hired by the North American Branches to serve as the Professional Conference Organizer for the event. The PSA Annual Meeting will be held just prior to WPC.

The Board has hired a consultant to do a thorough review of the PSA Policies and Procedures. The firm is currently developing recommendations for Board approval and implementation.

The Board of Directors voted to keep publication charges unchanged for members for the coming year. In comparison to ADSA and ASAS, PSA members are receiving PSA member benefits at a lesser cost. Author publication charges are $1,500 for PSA members, $1,600 for ADSA (per their website), and $2,950 for ASAS (per their website). In addition, PSA annual membership dues are $120, with ADSA being $110 and ASAS $220.

Committee activity has increased significantly this year. Special thanks to the committee chairs, committee members and Board liaisons for making this happen.

The PSA Board voted to confer the title of PSA Fellows to four well-deserving PSA members: Gisela F. Erf, Janet E. Fulton, Michael H. Kogut and Karen Schwean-Lardner.

In line with the mission, vision and strategic plan of the Poultry Science Association, the Board of Directors is continuing to work hard towards advancing the discovery, dissemination, and application of knowledge in the poultry sciences.

Respectfully submitted,

Brian D. Fairchild

PSA President

Report of the editor-in-chief of poultry science®

The journal, Poultry Science®, is in excellent shape. The following acknowledgements must be made in appreciation for the continued success of the journal: David Busboom, Managing Editor, the staff at Elsevier including Marie Dekerle, Senior Publisher, and Venkat Gunasekaran, Journal Manager, as well as all the Section Editors, Associate Editors, Ad-hoc Reviewers, and Authors. Additional thanks are extended to the rest of the PSA Staff and the PSA Board of Directors.

Submissions to Poultry Science have increased steadily since 2014 and that increase is expected to continue. In 2023, Poultry Science received 2,059 manuscript submissions from 81 countries (up from 1,551 in 2022 and 1,349 in 2021) with 50% of those submissions being accepted for publication. So far for 2024, the journal has received 1,431 manuscript submissions with a 49% acceptance rate.

Metabolism and Nutrition remains the most popular section of the journal with the most submissions, followed by Immunology, Health and Disease.

The journal's Impact Factor fell from 4.40 to 3.80 (ranking #6 out of 80 Agriculture, Dairy, and Animal Science journals), placing the journal ahead of both the Journal of Dairy Science and the Journal of Animal Science. The Cite Score also increased from 7.4 to 7.6, ranking it #117 out of 490 Animal Science and Zoology journals. Poultry Science was the #1 ranked journal in growth for the first quarter of 2024.

The review time and decision time for manuscripts has been going down when compared to 2020. Time to First Standard Decision has decreased from 60 days to 47 days. However, the challenge remains in finding reviewers willing to assist in the review process. Members of the Association are encouraged to review manuscripts and support publishing quality science.

Thank you to Yuhua Farnell and Sunday Adedokun whose terms as Section Editor have ended. New Section Editors are Oluyinka Olukosi, Metabolism and Nutrition, and Ken Genovese, Microbiology and Food Safety. There are currently open spots for Section Editors of Genetics and Molecular Biology; Immunology, Health and Disease, Management and Production; Microbiology and Food Safety, Metabolism and Nutrition; Physiology and Reproduction; and Processing and Products.

Respectfully submitted,

Michael H. Kogut

Editor-in-Chief

Report of the editor-in-chief of the journal of applied poultry research

The Journal of Applied Poultry Research continues to perform very well. In 2023, the journal received 238 manuscript submissions (up from 168 in 2022), with a 39% acceptance rate. The journal has already received 131 manuscript submissions for 2024. The journal received submissions from 49 countries in 2023.

The average time from submission to acceptance in 2023 was 89.5 days, while the average time from submission to final version of record was 147 days.

The Impact Factor dropped from 1.9 to 1.6, ranking the journal #34 out of 80 Agriculture, Dairy, and Animal Science journals. However, the Cite Score increased from 3.9 to 4.1, ranking the journal #91 out of 490 Animal Science And Zoology journals.

The journal saw 780,555 full-text article downloads in 2023. So far for 2024, there have been 303,800 full-text article downloads.

Nutrition continues to be the most popular section for manuscript submissions. The United States, China and Iran were the top countries submitting manuscripts in 2023. So far for 2023, the top countries are the United States and China.

Securing reviewers to review manuscript submissions remains one of the biggest obstacles for The Journal of Applied Poultry Research. More submissions are needed, especially in the areas of Food Safety and Microbiology, Meat Bird Management, and Processing and Products.

Thank you to Rocio Crespo, Samuel Rochell and Christina Swaggerty, whose terms as Subject Editor have ended. A Subject Editor for Layer Management is still needed.

Thank you to David Busboom, Managing Editor, the staff at Elsevier including Marie Dekerle, Senior Publisher, and Venkat Gunasekaran, Journal Manager, as well as all the Section Editors, Associate Editors, Ad-hoc Reviewers, and Authors. Additional thanks are extended to the rest of the PSA Staff and the PSA Board of Directors.

Respectfully submitted,

Brian D. Fairchild

Editor-in-Chief

Report of the foundation committee

The PSA Foundation serves as a granting organization within the Poultry Science Association. The objective of the Foundation is to provide funding for: undergraduate and graduate scholarships and fellowships, enhancing targeted faculty initiatives, PSA symposia and conferences, lectures addressing concerns/issues for global community, and needs identified by the governing body and donors.

The Foundation is urging the development of Faculty/Industry Travel Awards to create an opportunity for members to connect with international colleagues.

The Foundation has also developed a program to improve teaching and teaching resources within PSA by offering grants to general education material for PSA members and travel awards for faculty to attend teaching and professional development conferences.

The upcoming Professional Development Conference is also supported by the Foundation, providing members with opportunities to improve in the areas of education, leadership and career development.

The James H. Denton Distinguished Service Award is also being awarded this week for the first time. Anthony J. Pescatore is the recipient.

Special thanks to Robert Beckstead, Gregoy Bedecarrats, and Daniel Shafer for their years of service to the PSA Foundation Committee.

Respectfully submitted,

Robert B. Beckstead, Chair
