46 U.S.C. § 3301 — Vessels subject to inspection
Citation: us:usc:46:3301
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section3301
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part B (Inspection And Regulation Of Vessels); Chapter 33 (Inspection Generally). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The following categories of vessels are subject to inspection under this part:
(1)
freight vessels.
(2)
nautical school vessels.
(3)
offshore supply vessels.
(4)
passenger vessels.
(5)
sailing school vessels.
(6)
seagoing barges.
(7)
seagoing motor vessels.
(8)
small passenger vessels.
(9)
steam vessels.
(10)
tank vessels.
(11)
fish processing vessels.
(12)
fish tender vessels.
(13)
Great Lakes barges.
(14)
oil spill response vessels.
(15)
towing vessels.
