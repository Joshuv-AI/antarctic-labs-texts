42 U.S.C. § 5065 — Protection against improper use
Citation: us:usc:42:5065
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section5065
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 66 (Domestic Volunteer Services); Subchapter IV (Administration And Coordination). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whoever falsely—
(1)
advertises or represents; or
(2)
publishes or displays any sign, symbol, or advertisement, reasonably calculated to convey the impression,
that an entity is affiliated with, funded by, or operating under the authority of the Corporation, VISTA, or any of the programs of the National Senior Service Corps may be enjoined under an action filed by the Attorney General, on a complaint by the Director.
