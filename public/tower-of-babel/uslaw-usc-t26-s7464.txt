26 U.S.C. § 7464 — Intervention by trustee of debtor’s estate
Citation: us:usc:26:7464
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section7464
Context: United States Code, Title 26 (Internal Revenue Code); Chapter 76 (Judicial Proceedings); Subchapter C (The Tax Court); Part II (Procedure). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The trustee of the debtor’s estate in any case under title 11 of the United States Code may intervene, on behalf of the debtor’s estate, in any proceeding before the Tax Court to which the debtor is a party.
