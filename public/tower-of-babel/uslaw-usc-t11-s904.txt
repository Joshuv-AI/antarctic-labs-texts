11 U.S.C. § 904 — Limitation on jurisdiction and powers of court
Citation: us:usc:11:904
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section904
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 9 (Adjustment Of Debts Of A Municipality); Subchapter I (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding any power of the court, unless the debtor consents or the plan so provides, the court may not, by any stay, order, or decree, in the case or otherwise, interfere with—
(1)
any of the political or governmental powers of the debtor;
(2)
any of the property or revenues of the debtor; or
(3)
the debtor’s use or enjoyment of any income-producing property.
