19 C.F.R. § 210.64 — Interlocutory appeals.
Citation: us:cfr:19:210.64
Creator: United States International Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-19/section-210.64
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 19 (Customs Duties), Part 210 (Adjudication And Enforcement). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There will be no interlocutory appeals to the Commission under § 210.24 on any matter connected with a motion for temporary relief that is decided by an administrative law judge prior to the issuance of the initial determination on the motion for temporary relief.
