On the geometric k k -colored crossing number of K n K_{n}
Benedikt Hahn, Bettina Klinz, Birgit Vogtenhuber

Abstract
We study the geometric k k -colored crossing number of complete graphs cr ¯ ¯ k ​ ( K n ) \overline{\overline{\textup{cr}}}_{k}(K_{n}) , which is the smallest number of monochromatic crossings in any k k -edge colored straight-line drawing of K n K_{n} . We substantially improve asymptotic upper bounds on cr ¯ ¯ k ​ ( K n ) \overline{\overline{\textup{cr}}}_{k}(K_{n}) for k = 2 , … , 10 k=2,\ldots,10 by developing a procedure for general k k that derives k k -edge colored drawings of K n K_{n} for arbitrarily large n n from initial drawings with a low number of monochromatic crossings. We obtain the latter by heuristic search, employing a MAX- k k -CUT -formulation of a subproblem in the process.

The doubling construction

We consider straight-line drawings of
K_{n}

k
-edge-coloring

For each color

A

Conclusion

We introduced a procedure that creates
k
-edge-colored straight-line drawings of
K_{n}
for large
n
with few monochromatic crossings, given an initial drawing for small
n
with this property.
By finding
k
-edge-colored drawings with few monochromatic crossings, we kickstarted this procedure to improve the upper bounds on the geometric
k
-colored crossing constant for
k=2,\ldots,10
.
While our method is applicable for larger
k
, gaining on the upper bound from the book crossing number in
[
10
,
24
]
for all
k
at once is still open.
We believe this is possible with a construction that does not arrange the points in convex position.

The lower bound on
\overline{\overline{\textup{cr}}}_{k}
in
4
is unlikely to be improved using the Crossing Lemma (unless a better one is found).
A more promising avenue could be the study of
\ell
-edges and
\leq\ell
-edges in a similar fashion as in
[
1
]
and previously
[
6
]
for the (non-colored) rectilinear crossing constant.
