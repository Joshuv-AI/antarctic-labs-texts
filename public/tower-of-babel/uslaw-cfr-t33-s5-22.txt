33 C.F.R. § 5.22 — Assignment to duties.
Citation: us:cfr:33:5.22
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-5.22
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 5 (Coast Guard Auxiliary). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Members of the Auxiliary will not be assigned duties until they have been found to be competent to perform such duties and have been designated by authority of the Commandant to perform such duties.
