46 C.F.R. § 327.21 — Definitions.

Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-327.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 327 (Seamen'S Claims; Administrative Action And Litigation). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following definitions apply to this subpart:
(a) Accrual date. The day on which the alleged wrongful act or omission results in injury or damage for which a claim is made.
(b) Claim. A written notification of an incident, signed by the claimant, describing the incident, and explaining why the United States is liable.
