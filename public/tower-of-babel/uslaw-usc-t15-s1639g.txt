15 U.S.C. § 1639g — Requests for payoff amounts of home loan
Citation: us:usc:15:1639g
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section1639g
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 41 (Consumer Credit Protection); Subchapter I (Consumer Credit Cost Disclosure); Part B (Credit Transactions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A creditor or servicer of a home loan shall send an accurate payoff balance within a reasonable time, but in no case more than 7 business days, after the receipt of a written request for such balance from or on behalf of the borrower.
