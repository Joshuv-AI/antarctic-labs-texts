42 C.F.R. § 418.20 — Eligibility requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:418.20
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-418.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 418 (Hospice Care). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order to be eligible to elect hospice care under Medicare, an individual must be—
(a) Entitled to Part A of Medicare; and
(b) Certified as being terminally ill in accordance with § 418.22.
