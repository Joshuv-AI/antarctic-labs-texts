41 C.F.R. § 102-33.195 — -33.195 Declassifying aircraft.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.195
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.195
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) A declassified aircraft is no longer an aircraft but a set of parts for ground use only.
(b) Retain documentation and traceability for all replacement parts, recorded under the correct Federal Supply Classification groups.
(c) For disposal of the property remaining after declassification of an aircraft, you must follow the property disposal regulations in 41 CFR parts 102-36, 102-37, 102-38 and 102-39.
