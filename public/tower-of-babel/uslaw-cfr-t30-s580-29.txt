30 C.F.R. § 580.29 — Will BOEM monitor the environmental effects of my activity?
Citation: us:cfr:30:580.29
Creator: Bureau Of Ocean Energy Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-580.29
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 580 (Prospecting For Minerals Other Than Oil, Gas, And Sulphur On The Outer Continental Shelf). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

We will evaluate the potential of proposed prospecting or scientific research activities for adverse impact on the environment to determine the need for mitigation measures.
