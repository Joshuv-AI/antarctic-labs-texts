49 C.F.R. § 534.7 — Situations not directly addressed by this part.
Citation: us:cfr:49:534.7
Creator: National Highway Traffic Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-534.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 534 (Rights And Responsibilities Of Manufacturers In The Context Of Changes In Corporate Relationships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

To the extent that this part does not directly address an issue concerning the rights and responsibilities of manufacturers in the context of a change in corporate relationships, the agency will make determinations based on interpretation of the statute and the principles reflected in the part.
