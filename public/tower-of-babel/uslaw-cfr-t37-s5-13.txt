37 C.F.R. § 5.13 — Petition for license; no corresponding application.
Citation: us:cfr:37:5.13
Creator: United States Patent And Trademark Office, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-37/section-5.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 37 (Patents, Trademarks, and Copyrights), Part 5 (Secrecy Of Certain Inventions And Licenses To Export And File Applications In Foreign Countries). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If no corresponding national, international design, or international application has been filed in the United States, the petition for license under § 5.12(b) must also be accompanied by a legible copy of the material upon which a license is desired. This copy will be retained as a measure of the license granted.
