49 C.F.R. § 377.217 — Interline settlement of revenues.
Citation: us:cfr:49:377.217
Creator: Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-377.217
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 377 (Payment Of Transportation Charges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be interpreted as affecting the interline settlement of revenues from traffic which is transported over through routes composed of lines of motor carriers subject to the Secretary's jurisdiction under 49 U.S.C. subtitle IV, part B.
