25 C.F.R. § 117.6 — Allowance for minors.
Citation: us:cfr:25:117.6
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-117.6
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 117 (Deposit And Expenditure Of Individual Funds Of Members Of The Osage Tribe Of Indians Who Do Not Have Certificates Of Competency). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The superintendent may disburse from the surplus funds of an Indian under 21 years of age not to exceed $300 quarterly for the support and maintenance of the minor. Disbursement may be made to the parent, guardian, or other person, school or institution having actual custody of the minor, or, when the minor is 18 years of age or over, disbursement may be made direct to the minor.
