Jacob’s ladders, E. C. Titchmarsh’s hypothesis (1934) and new ζ \zeta -equivalents of the Fermat-Wiles theorem or connections between Fermat’s rationals and the Gram’s sequence
Jan Moser

Abstract
. In connection of our proof (1980) of the Titchmarsh’s hypothesis (1934), we have obtained two asymptotic formulae (1991). In this paper we obtain three new ζ \zeta -equivalents of the Fermat-Wiles theorem based on the mentioned asymptotic formulae.
