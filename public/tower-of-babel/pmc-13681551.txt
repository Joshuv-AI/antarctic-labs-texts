hLife: Linking basic research and clinical applications
Authors: George Fu Gao, Chen Dong, Jules A Hoffmann
Published: 2023

Scientific journals are platforms for communication between authors and readers, and also among readers and the wider public. To promote health science and related medical science fields, we are pleased to announce the launch of a transdisciplinary journal named hLife, which combines the ideas of a healthier, happier and harmonious life together. hLife is a peer-reviewed, open-access monthly journal published online, sponsored by the Institute of Microbiology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, in cooperation with the Chinese Society of Biotechnology and the Shanghai Immune Therapy Institute. The editorial board consists of around 60 scientists from 12 countries or regions worldwide, among them two Nobel Laureates. Now, hLife is one of the “high starting point journals” of the Chinese Action Plan for Journal Excellence.

hLife aims to report and highlight original and high-impact discoveries in the health science field, linking basic research to clinical applications that could improve human health. The disciplines covered include all aspects of medical-related research and health policy, with hLife welcoming different content types, such as research articles, reviews, letters, perspectives, commentaries, resources, editorials, dialogues, and stories about scientists or scientific discoveries in the field of health science.

Achieving a healthier, happier and harmonious life is an eternal quest. From the dawn of early human civilization, when primitive people wished for health through witchcraft, herbal medicine and beliefs, to 2400 years ago, when Hippocrates swore his famous oath, experts around the world started a remarkable journey of medical exploration. With the development of anatomy and blood circulation, the era of modern medicine began. Since then, human beings have never stopped their quest to dive deeper into the structure, essence, and code of life, attempting to understand even the most smallest of details. Wherever there is disease, there is the eternal light of medical research. We forge ahead with a record of repeated triumphs over multiple diseases, and to continue that relentless quest for the hope of life. Every time a research finding is used to empower medicine, our defences are leveled up to better safeguard the dignity of life. Ensuring people can enjoy healthy lives, and promoting their well-being, is essential for sustainable development. The scientific community has carried out active explorations in many areas including in the prevention and control of infectious disease, protein structure prediction, immunotherapy, gene editing, mRNA vaccines, regenerative medicine, and so on. However, human beings still face major challenges. To take prevention and control of infectious disease as an example, pandemics triggered by different pathogens continue to spread human suffering, and countless related challenges have yet to be overcome. The prevention and control of infectious diseases caused by pathogens has become a high-priority imperative strategic field for all countries. Now, the biggest challenge is how we will live and protect human health in the future. The One Health notion, and collaboration among scientists from different disciplinary fields and countries, are important for developing our preparedness against potential pandemics. We seek to have, through the efforts of researchers across disciplines, more frontier scientific knowledge than can help to overcome these challenges. In the future, medicine is expected to be health-centric rather than disease-centric, which means that the focus will be shifted from diagnosis and treatment to disease prevention and health promotion.

hLife is committed to directly linking basic experimental research to clinical applications, with an emphasis on the bench-to-bedside connection. Knowledge obtained through basic research could rapidly be transformed into new clinical treatment methods. At the same time, the close cooperation between basic research scientists and clinicians helps improve the overall quality of medical treatments. hLife aims to promote cooperation among multidisciplinary research teams, allowing them to collaborate and leverage their advantages. Our mission is to provide both a window and a platform for the dissemination of innovative scientific information and the advancement of a sustainable scientific culture, with the ultimate goal of empowering us all to live healthier, happier and harmonious lives.

As hLife is a new journal breaking into a competitive field, we believe that establishing a strong scientific community is key to its success. As such, we would like to express our deep gratitude to the scientists who are engaged in health research for devoting their time to assist hLife in its development. We are convinced that with your help, hLife will rapidly become a top-tier medical journal in a short amount of time.

We invite you to stay tuned, as you will receive a lot of surprises from hLife.
