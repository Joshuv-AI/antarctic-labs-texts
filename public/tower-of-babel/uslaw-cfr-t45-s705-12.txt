45 C.F.R. § 705.12 — Special procedures: Information furnished by other agencies.
Citation: us:cfr:45:705.12
Creator: Commission On Civil Rights
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/section-705.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 705 (MATERIALS AVAILABLE PURSUANT TO 5 U.S.C. 552a). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

When records or information sought from the Commission include information furnished by other Federal agencies, the General Counsel shall consult with the appropriate agency prior to making a decision to disclose or to refuse to disclose the record, but the decision whether or not to disclose the record shall be made by the General Counsel.
