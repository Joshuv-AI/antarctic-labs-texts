10 U.S.C. § 14006 — Determination of years in grade
Citation: us:usc:10:14006
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section14006
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part III (Promotion And Retention Of Officers On The Reserve Active-Status List); Chapter 1401 (Applicability And Reserve Active-Status Lists). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purpose of chapters 1403 through 1411 of this title, an officer’s years of service in a grade are computed from the officer’s date of rank in grade as determined under section 741(d) of this title.
