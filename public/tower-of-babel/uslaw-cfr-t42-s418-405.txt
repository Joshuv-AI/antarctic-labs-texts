42 C.F.R. § 418.405 — Effect of coinsurance liability on Medicare payment.
Citation: us:cfr:42:418.405
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-418.405
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 418 (Hospice Care). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The Medicare payment rates established by CMS in accordance with § 418.306 are not reduced when the individual is liable for coinsurance payments. Instead, when establishing the payment rates, CMS offsets the estimated cost of services by an estimate of average coinsurance amounts hospices collect.
