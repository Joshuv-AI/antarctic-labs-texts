18 U.S.C. § 4041 — Bureau of Prisons; director and employees
Citation: us:usc:18:4041
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section4041
Context: United States Code, Title 18 (Crimes And Criminal Procedure); Part III (Prisons And Prisoners); Chapter 303 (Bureau Of Prisons). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Bureau of Prisons shall be in charge of a director appointed by and serving directly under the Attorney General. The Attorney General may appoint such additional officers and employees as he deems necessary.
