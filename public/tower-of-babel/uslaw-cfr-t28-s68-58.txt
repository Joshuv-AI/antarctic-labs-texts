28 C.F.R. § 68.58 — Filing of the official record.
Citation: us:cfr:28:68.58
Creator: Department Of Justice
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/section-68.58
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 28 (Judicial Administration), Part 68 (Rules Of Practice And Procedure For Immigration-Related Administrative Hearings Before Ocaho Administrative Law Judges). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon timely receipt of notification that an appeal has been taken, a certified copy of the record will be filed promptly with the appropriate United States Court.
