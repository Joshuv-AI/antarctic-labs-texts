16 U.S.C. § 719b–1 — Definition of State
Citation: us:usc:16:719b–1
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section719b–1
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 7 (Protection Of Migratory Game And Insectivorous Birds); Subchapter V (Junior Duck Stamp Conservation And Design Program). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For the purposes of this subchapter, the term “State” includes the District of Columbia, the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands, American Samoa, Guam, the Virgin Islands, and any other territory or possession of the United States.
