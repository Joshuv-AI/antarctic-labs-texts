22 U.S.C. § 2430i — Annual reports to Congress
Citation: us:usc:22:2430i
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section2430i
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 32 (Foreign Assistance); Subchapter III–A (Enterprise For The Americas Initiative). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The annual reports submitted pursuant to section 1738m of title 7 shall include a description of each Americas Framework Agreement and a description of any grants that have been extended by administering bodies pursuant to an Americas Framework Agreement.
