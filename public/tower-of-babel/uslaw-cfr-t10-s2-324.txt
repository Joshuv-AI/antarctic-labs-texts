10 C.F.R. § 2.324 — Order of procedure.
Citation: us:cfr:10:2.324
Creator: Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-2.324
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 2 (Agency Rules Of Practice And Procedure). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The presiding officer or the Commission will designate the order of procedure at a hearing. The proponent of an order will ordinarily open and close.
