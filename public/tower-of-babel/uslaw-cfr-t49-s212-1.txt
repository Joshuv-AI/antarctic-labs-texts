49 C.F.R. § 212.1 — Purpose and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:49:212.1
Creator: Federal Railroad Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-212.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 212 (State Safety Participation Regulations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part establishes standards and procedures for State participation in investigative and surveillance activities under the Federal railroad safety laws and regulations.
