16 U.S.C. § 1454 — Submittal of State program for approval
Citation: us:usc:16:1454
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1454
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 33 (Coastal Zone Management). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Any coastal state which has completed the development of its management program shall submit such program to the Secretary for review and approval pursuant to section 1455 of this title.
