43 C.F.R. § 4.1353 — Contents of request.
Citation: us:cfr:43:4.1353
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-4.1353
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 4 (Department Of The Interior Hearings And Appeals Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The request for hearing shall include—
(a) A clear statement of the facts entitling the one requesting the hearing to administrative relief;
(b) An explanation of the alleged errors in OSM's preliminary finding; and
(c) Any other relevant information.
