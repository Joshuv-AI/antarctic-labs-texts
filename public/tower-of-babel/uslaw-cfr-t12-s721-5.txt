12 C.F.R. § 721.5 — What limitations apply to a credit union engaging in activities approved under this part?
Citation: us:cfr:12:721.5
Creator: National Credit Union Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-721.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 721 (Incidental Powers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You must comply with any applicable NCUA regulations, policies, and legal opinions, as well as applicable state and federal law, if an activity authorized under this part is otherwise regulated or conditioned.
