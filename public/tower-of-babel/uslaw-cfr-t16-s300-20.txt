16 C.F.R. § 300.20 — Use of the terms “virgin” or “new.”
Citation: us:cfr:16:300.20
Creator: Federal Trade Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-300.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 300 (Rules And Regulations Under The Wool Products Labeling Act Of 1939). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The terms “virgin” or “new” as descriptive of a wool product, or any fiber or part thereof, shall not be used when the product, fiber or part so described is not composed wholly of new or virgin fiber which has never been reclaimed from any spun, woven, knitted, felted, braided, bonded, or otherwise manufactured or used product.
