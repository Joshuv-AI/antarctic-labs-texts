42 C.F.R. § 417.488 — Notice of termination and of available alternatives: Risk contract.
Citation: us:cfr:42:417.488
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-417.488
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 417 (Health Maintenance Organizations, Competitive Medical Plans, And Health Care Prepayment Plans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A risk contract must provide that the HMO or CMP agrees to give notice as follows if the contract is terminated:
(a) At least 60 days before the effective date of termination, to give its Medicare enrollees a written notice that—
(1) Specifies the termination date; and
(2) Describes the alternatives available for obtaining Medicare services after termination.
(b) To pay the cost of the written notices.
