42 C.F.R. § 485.601 — Basis and scope.
Citation: us:cfr:42:485.601
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-485.601
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 485 (Conditions Of Participation: Specialized Providers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Statutory basis. This subpart is based on section 1820 of the Act which sets forth the conditions for designating certain hospitals as CAHs.
(b) Scope. This subpart sets forth the conditions that a hospital must meet to be designated as a CAH.
