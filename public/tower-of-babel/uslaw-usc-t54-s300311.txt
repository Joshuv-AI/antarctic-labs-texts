54 U.S.C. § 300311 — National Register
Citation: us:usc:54:300311
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title54-section300311
Context: United States Code, Title 54 (National Park Service And Related Programs); Chapter 3003 (Definitions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this division, the term “National Register” means the National Register of Historic Places maintained under chapter 3021 of this title.
