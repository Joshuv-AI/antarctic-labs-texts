28 U.S.C. § 957 — Clerks ineligible for certain offices
Citation: us:usc:28:957
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section957
Context: United States Code, Title 28 (Judiciary And Judicial Procedure); Part III (Court Officers And Employees); Chapter 57 (General Provisions Applicable To Court Officers And Employees). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A clerk of a court or any of his deputies shall not be appointed a commissioner, master, referee or receiver in any case, unless there are special reasons requiring such appointment which are recited in the order of appointment.
