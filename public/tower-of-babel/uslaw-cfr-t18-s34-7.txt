18 C.F.R. § 34.7 — Filing requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:18:34.7
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-34.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 34 (Application For Authorization Of The Issuance Of Securities Or The Assumption Of Liabilities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applications must be filed with the Secretary of the Commission in accordance with filing procedures posted on the Commission's Web site at http://www.ferc.gov. If an applicant seeks to protect any portion of an application from public disclosure, the applicant must make its filing in accordance with the Commission's instructions for filing privileged materials and critical energy infrastructure information in this chapter.
