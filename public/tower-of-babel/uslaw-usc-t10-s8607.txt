10 U.S.C. § 8607 — Administration of liberated and occupied areas
Citation: us:usc:10:8607
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8607
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (General Administration); Chapter 861 (Secretary Of The Navy: Miscellaneous Powers And Duties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
The Secretary of the Navy may, out of any appropriation made for the purpose, provide for the administration of liberated and occupied areas by the Department of the Navy.
(b)
The Secretary, to the extent he considers proper, may delegate the authority conferred by this section to any person in the Department of the Navy, with or without the authority to make successive redelegations.
