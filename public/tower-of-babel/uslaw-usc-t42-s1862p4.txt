42 U.S.C. § 1862p–4 — Undergraduate broadening participation program
Citation: us:usc:42:1862p–4
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section1862p–4
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 16 (National Science Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Foundation shall continue to support the Historically Black Colleges and Universities Undergraduate Program, the Louis Stokes Alliances for Minority Participation program, the Tribal Colleges and Universities Program, and Hispanic-serving institutions as separate programs.
