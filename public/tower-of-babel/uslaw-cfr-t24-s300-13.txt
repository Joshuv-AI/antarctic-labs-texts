24 C.F.R. § 300.13 — Power of attorney.
Citation: us:cfr:24:300.13
Creator: Government National Mortgage Association, Department Of Housing And Urban Development
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-24/section-300.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 24 (Housing and Urban Development), Part 300 (General). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In order to efficiently carry out the purposes of the Association, the Association may appoint any person its true and lawful attorney-in-fact by publication in the Federal Register or by appointment from the President of the Association in writing. Any such attorney-in-fact shall have the power outlined in the publication or appointment.
