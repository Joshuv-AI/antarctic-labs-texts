10 C.F.R. § 860.8 — Applicability of other laws.
Citation: us:cfr:10:860.8
Creator: Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-10/section-860.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 10 (Energy), Part 860 (Trespassing On Department Of Energy Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this part shall be construed to affect the applicability of the provisions of State or other Federal laws.
