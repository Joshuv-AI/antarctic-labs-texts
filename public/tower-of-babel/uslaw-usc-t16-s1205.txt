16 U.S.C. § 1205 — General authority of Secretary for conducting studies, research, and investigations unaffected
Citation: us:usc:16:1205
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section1205
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 25 (Jellyfish Or Sea Nettles, Other Such Pests, And Seaweed In Coastal Waters: Control Or Elimination). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to alter, amend, repeal, modify, or diminish the present general authority of the Secretary of Commerce to conduct studies, research, and investigations related to the mission of the Department of Commerce.
