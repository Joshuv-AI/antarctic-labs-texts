30 U.S.C. § 823a — Principal office in District of Columbia; proceedings held elsewhere
Citation: us:usc:30:823a
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section823a
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 22 (Mine Safety And Health); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The principal office of the Commission shall be in the District of Columbia. Whenever the Commission deems that convenience of the public or the parties may be promoted, or delay or expense may be minimized, it may hold hearings or conduct other proceedings at any other place.
