46 C.F.R. § 550.501 — Participation of interested persons.
Citation: us:cfr:46:550.501
Creator: Federal Maritime Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-550.501
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 550 (Regulations To Adjust Or Meet Conditions Unfavorable To Shipping In The Foreign Trade Of The United States). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In the event that participation of interested persons is deemed necessary by the Commission, notice will be published in the Federal Register and interested persons will then be allowed to participate in this proceeding by the submission of written data, views or arguments, with or without opportunity to present same orally.
