18 C.F.R. § 12.5 — Responsibilities of licensee or applicant.
Citation: us:cfr:18:12.5
Creator: Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, Department Of Energy
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-12.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 12 (Safety Of Water Power Projects And Project Works). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A licensee or applicant must use sound and prudent engineering practices in any action relating to the design, construction, operation, maintenance, use, repair, or modification of a water power project or project works.
