12 U.S.C. § 2296 — Increase not authorized in amounts of obligations issued, sold, or guaranteed by Federal agencies
Citation: us:usc:12:2296
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title12-section2296
Context: United States Code, Title 12 (Banks And Banking); Chapter 24 (Federal Financing Bank). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as authorizing an increase in the amounts of obligations issued, sold, or guaranteed by any Federal agency which issues, sells, or guarantees obligations purchased by the Bank.
