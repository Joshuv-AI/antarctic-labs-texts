33 C.F.R. § 83.05 — Look-out (Rule 5).
Citation: us:cfr:33:83.05
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-33/section-83.05
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 33 (Navigation and Navigable Waters), Part 83 (Navigation Rules). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Every vessel shall at all times maintain a proper look-out by sight and hearing as well as by all available means appropriate in the prevailing circumstances and conditions so as to make a full appraisal of the situation and of the risk of collision.
