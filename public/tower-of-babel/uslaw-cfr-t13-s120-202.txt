13 C.F.R. § 120.202 — Loans for changes of ownership.
Citation: us:cfr:13:120.202
Creator: Small Business Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-13/section-120.202
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 13 (Business Credit and Assistance), Part 120 (Business Loans). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Borrower may use 7(a) loan proceeds to purchase a portion of or the entirety of an owner's interest in a business, or a portion of or the entirety of a business itself.
