10 U.S.C. § 8168 — Consular powers: senior officer present afloat
Citation: us:usc:10:8168
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section8168
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part II (Personnel); Chapter 821 (Officers In Command). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In any foreign port where there is no resident consul of the United States, or on the high seas, the senior officer present afloat has the powers of a consul in relation to mariners of the United States.
