25 C.F.R. § 273.175 — How will a Tribal governing body apply Indian preference requirements for contracts and subcontracts?
Citation: us:cfr:25:273.175
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-273.175
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 273 (Education Contracts Under Johnson-O'Malley Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A Tribal governing body may develop its own Indian preference requirements for its contracts and subcontracts.
