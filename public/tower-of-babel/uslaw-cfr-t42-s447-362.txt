42 C.F.R. § 447.362 — Upper limits of payment: Nonrisk contract.
Citation: us:cfr:42:447.362
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-447.362
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 447 (Payments For Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under a nonrisk contract, Medicaid payments to the contractor may not exceed—
(a) What Medicaid would have paid, on a fee-for-service basis, for the services actually furnished to beneficiaries: plus
(b) The net savings of administrative costs the Medicaid agency achieves by contracting with the plan instead of purchasing the services on a fee-for-service basis.
