51 U.S.C. § 30702 — Foreign contract limitation
Citation: us:usc:51:30702
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section30702
Context: United States Code, Title 51 (National And Commercial Space Programs); Chapter 307 (International Cooperation And Competition). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Administration shall not enter into any agreement or contract with a foreign government that grants the foreign government the right to recover profit in the event that the agreement or contract is terminated.
