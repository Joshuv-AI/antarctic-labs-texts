21 C.F.R. § 886.1360 — Visual field laser instrument.
Citation: us:cfr:21:886.1360
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-886.1360
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 886 (Ophthalmic Devices). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Identification. A visual field laser instrument is an AC-powered device intended to provide visible laser radiation that produces an interference pattern on the retina to evaluate retinal function.
(b) Classification. Class II.
