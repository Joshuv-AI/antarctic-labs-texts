3 C.F.R. § 100.1 — Ethical conduct standards and financial disclosure regulations.
Citation: us:cfr:3:100.1
Creator: Executive Office Of The President
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-3/section-100.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 3 (The President), Part 100 (Standards Of Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Employees of the Executive Office of the President are subject to the executive branch-wide standards of ethical conduct at 5 CFR part 2635, and the executive branch-wide financial disclosure regulations at 5 CFR part 2634.
