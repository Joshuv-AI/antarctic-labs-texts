14 C.F.R. § 101.5 — Operations in prohibited or restricted areas.
Citation: us:cfr:14:101.5
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-101.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 101 (Moored Balloons, Kites, Amateur Rockets, And Unmanned Free Balloons). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

No person may operate a moored balloon, kite, amateur rocket, or unmanned free balloon in a prohibited or restricted area unless he has permission from the using or controlling agency, as appropriate.
