48 C.F.R. § 25.000 — 25.000 Scope of part.
Citation: us:cfr:48:25.000
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 25 (Foreign Acquisition). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) This part provides policies and procedures for—
(1) Acquisition of foreign supplies, services, and construction materials; and
(2) Contracts performed outside the United States.
(b) It implements 41 U.S.C. chapter 83, Buy American; trade agreements; and other laws and regulations.
