7 U.S.C. § 1040 — Farmers’ Home Administration funds account
Citation: us:usc:7:1040
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section1040
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 33 (Farm Tenancy); Subchapter IV (General Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

When authorized by appropriation or other law, funds of the Farmers’ Home Administration available for administrative expenses may be placed in a single account.
