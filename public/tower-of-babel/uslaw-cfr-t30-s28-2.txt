30 C.F.R. § 28.2 — Approved fuses.
Citation: us:cfr:30:28.2
Creator: Mine Safety And Health Administration, Department Of Labor
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-28.2
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 28 (Fuses For Use With Direct Current In Providing Short-Circuit Protection For Trailing Cables In Coal Mines). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) On and after the effective date of this part, fuses shall be considered to be approved for use with direct current in providing short-circuit protection for trailing cables in coal mines only where such fuses are: (1) The same in all respects as those fuses which have been approved after meeting the minimum requirements for performance and short-circuit protection prescribed in this Part 28; and (2) maintained in an approved condition.
