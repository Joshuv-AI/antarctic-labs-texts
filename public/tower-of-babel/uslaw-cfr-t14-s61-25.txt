14 C.F.R. § 61.25 — Change of name.
Citation: us:cfr:14:61.25
Creator: Federal Aviation Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-61.25
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 14 (Aeronautics and Space), Part 61 (Certification: Pilots, Flight Instructors, And Ground Instructors). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) An application to change the name on a certificate issued under this part must be accompanied by the applicant's:
(1) Airman certificate; and
(2) A copy of the marriage license, court order, or other document verifying the name change.
(b) The documents in paragraph (a) of this section will be returned to the applicant after inspection.
