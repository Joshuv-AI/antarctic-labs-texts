OFFICE OF PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT


Agency Information Collection Request: 3206-NEW, Annuitant 
Verification Form, RI 20-129

AGENCY: Office of Personnel Management.

ACTION: 60-Day notice and request for comments.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: Retirement Services offers the general public and other 
Federal agencies the opportunity to comment on a new information 
collection request (ICR), Annuitant Verification Form, RI 20-129.

DATES: Comments are encouraged and will be accepted until December 18, 
2026.

ADDRESSES: You may submit comments using the Federal Rulemaking Portal: 
https://www.regulations.gov. Follow the instructions for submitting 
comments. The general policy for comments and other submissions from 
members of the public is to make these submissions available for public 
viewing at https://www.regulations.gov as they are received without 
change, including any personal identifiers or contact information.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT: Retirement Services Publications Team, 
Office of Personnel Management, 1900 E Street NW, Room 6500-AS, 
Washington, DC 20415, Attention: Cyrus S. Benson, or sent by email to 
[email protected] or eFax to [email protected].

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION: As required by the Paperwork Reduction Act, 
as amended (44 U.S.C. chapter 35), OPM is soliciting comments for this 
new collection (OMB No. 3206-NEW). The Office of Personnel Management 
is particularly interested in comments that:
    1. Evaluate whether the proposed collection of information is 
necessary for the proper performance of functions of the agency, 
including whether the information will have practical utility;
    2. Evaluate the accuracy of the agency's estimate of the burden of 
the proposed collection of information, including the validity of the 
methodology and assumptions used;
    3. Suggest ways to enhance the quality, utility, and clarity of the 
information to be collected; and
    4. Suggest ways to minimize the burden of the collection of 
information on those who are to respond, including through the use of 
appropriate automated, electronic, mechanical, or other technological 
collection techniques or other forms of information technology, e.g., 
permitting electronic submissions of responses.
    OPM receives requests to change contact information by various 
means of communication that do not provide sufficient opportunity to 
OPM to verify the identity of the individual initiating the request, 
such as when a caller is unable to provide a correct response to 
identification questions. OPM has created Form RI 20-129 to verify the 
identity of an annuitant who has requested changes to contact 
information. The verification will determine whether the individual is 
provided access to the annuitant's account.
