15 C.F.R. § 290.7 — Proposal selection process.
Citation: us:cfr:15:290.7
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-290.7
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 290 (Regional Centers For The Transfer Of Manufacturing Technology). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Upon the availability of funding to solicit applications to establish a new Manufacturing Extension Partnership (MEP) Center or to operate a pre-existing MEP Center, the Director shall publish a notice of funding opportunity on www.Grants.gov requesting submission of competitive proposals from eligible organizations.
