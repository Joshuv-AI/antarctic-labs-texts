34 U.S.C. § 12542 — Rural drug enforcement training
Citation: us:usc:34:12542
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026

Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 121 (Violent Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Subchapter VI (Rural Crime). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) Specialized training for rural officers
The Director of the Federal Law Enforcement Training Center shall develop a specialized course of instruction devoted to training law enforcement officers from rural agencies in the investigation of drug trafficking and related crimes.
(b) Authorization of appropriations
There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out subsection (a)—
(1)
$1,000,000 for fiscal year 1996;
(2)
$1,000,000 for fiscal year 1997;
(3)
$1,000,000 for fiscal year 1998;
(4)
$1,000,000 for fiscal year 1999; and
(5)
$1,000,000 for fiscal year 2000.
