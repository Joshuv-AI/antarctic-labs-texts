23 C.F.R. § 772.3 — Noise standards.
Citation: us:cfr:23:772.3
Creator: Federal Highway Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-23/section-772.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 23 (Highways), Part 772 (Procedures For Abatement Of Highway Traffic Noise And Construction Noise). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The highway traffic noise prediction requirements, noise analyses, noise abatement criteria, and requirements for informing local officials in this regulation constitute the noise standards mandated by 23 U.S.C. 109(1). All highway projects which are developed in conformance with this regulation shall be deemed to be in accordance with the FHWA noise standards.
