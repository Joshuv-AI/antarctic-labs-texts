12 C.F.R. § 101.8 — Evasion.
Citation: us:cfr:12:101.8
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-101.8
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 101 (Covered Savings Associations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The OCC may disapprove any notice submitted pursuant to this part if the OCC determines that the notice is made for the purpose of evading § 101.5, including as that section applies to a covered savings association terminating an election.
