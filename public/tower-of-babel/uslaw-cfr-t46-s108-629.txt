46 C.F.R. § 108.629 — Fire extinguishing system branch line valve.
Citation: us:cfr:46:108.629
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-108.629
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 108 (Design And Equipment). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each branch line valve of each fire extinguishing system must be marked with the name of the space or spaces it serves.
