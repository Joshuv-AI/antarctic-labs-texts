2 U.S.C. § 2135 — Private studios and works of art
Citation: us:usc:2:2135
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section2135
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 30 (Operation And Maintenance Of Capitol Complex); Subchapter V (Historical Preservation And Fine Arts); Part D (Miscellaneous). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No room in the Capitol shall be used for private studios or works of art, without permission from the Joint Committee on the Library, given in writing; and it shall be the duty of the Architect of the Capitol to carry this provision into effect.
