45 C.F.R. § 1612.4 — Grassroots lobbying.

Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 45 (Public Welfare), Part 1612 (Restrictions On Lobbying And Certain Other Activities). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

A recipient shall not engage in any grassroots lobbying.
