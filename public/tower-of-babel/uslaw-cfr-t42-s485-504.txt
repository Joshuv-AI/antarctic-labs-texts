42 C.F.R. § 485.504 — Basic requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:42:485.504
Creator: Centers For Medicare & Medicaid Services, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-485.504
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 485 (Conditions Of Participation: Specialized Providers). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Participation as an REH is limited to facilities that—
(a) Meet the definition in § 485.502.
(b) Have in effect a provider agreement as defined at § 489.3 of this chapter to provide services.
(c) Meet the conditions of participation set out in this subpart.
