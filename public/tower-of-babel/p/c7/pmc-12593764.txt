Correction: Epidemiology of ventilator associated events in intubated patients: a multicenter observational study
Authors: Esma Eryılmaz Eren, Duygu Mert, Fatma Eser, Seniha Senbayrak, Gamze Kalın, Gulden Eser Karlıdag, Tugce Simsek Bozok, Fatma Yekta Urkmez, Tuba Ilgar, Canan Demir, Secil Deniz, Derya Ozyigitoglu, Nefise Oztoprak, Safak Kaya, Meltem Arzu Yetkin, Yolanda Peña-López, Jordi Rello, Emine Alp Meşe, VAE Study Group
Published: 2025

Correction: BMC Infectious Diseases (2025) 25:1363

10.1186/s12879-025-11341-3.

Following publication of the original article [1], we were notified of a mistake in the last paragraph of the Discussion section.

Originally, it read: “ When comparing VAE and VAP as diagnostic tools, VAE demonstrates higher sensitivity (89.6%) and NPV (92.0%) than VAP. However, VAE proves less practical for diagnosis, given its lower sensitivity (55.6%) and PPV (48.3%).”

It should read: “When analyzing VAE for VAP diagnosis, VAE demonstrates higher specificity (89.6%) and NPV (92.0%) than VAP. However, VAE proves less practical for diagnosis, given its lower sensitivity (55.6%) and PPV (48.3%).”

The original article has been corrected.
