Author Correction: Engineering TadA ortholog-derived cytosine base editor without motif preference and adenosine activity limitation
Authors: Guoling Li, Xue Dong, Jiamin Luo, Tanglong Yuan, Tong Li, Guoli Zhao, Hainan Zhang, Jingxing Zhou, Zhenhai Zeng, Shuna Cui, Haoqiang Wang, Yin Wang, Yuyang Yu, Yuan Yuan, Erwei Zuo, Chunlong Xu, Jinhai Huang, Yingsi Zhou
Published: 2024

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-024-52485-1, published online 16 September 2024

In this article the affiliation details for Jinhai Huang were incorrectly given as ‘Eye Institute and Department of Ophthalmology, Eye and ENT Hospital, Fudan University, Key Laboratory of Myopia, Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences, Shanghai 200030, China’ but should have been ‘Eye Institute and Department of Ophthalmology, Eye & ENT Hospital, Fudan University; NHC Key Laboratory of Myopia and Related Eye Diseases; Key Laboratory of Myopia and Related Eye Diseases, Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences, Shanghai, China’. The original article has been corrected.
