In‐Doped ZnO Electron Transport Layer for High‐Efficiency Ultrathin Flexible Organic Solar Cells (Adv. Sci. 37/2024)
Authors: Xiujun Liu, Yitong Ji, Zezhou Xia, Dongyang Zhang, Yingying Cheng, Xiangda Liu, Xiaojie Ren, Xiaotong Liu, Haoran Huang, Yanqing Zhu, Xueyuan Yang, Xiaobin Liao, Long Ren, Wenliang Tan, Zhi Jiang, Jianfeng Lu, Christopher McNeill, Wenchao Huang
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Abstract Ultrathin Flexible Organic Solar Cells A 1.2‐micrometer‐thick ultra‐thin flexible organic solar cells based on the indium‐doped zinc oxide electron transport layer is developed, achieving an efficiency of 17.0% with a power‐per‐weight ratio of 40.4 W g−1, which is one of the highest values among ultra‐thin flexible organic solar cells. More details can be found in article number 2402158 by Wenchao Huang and co‐workers.
