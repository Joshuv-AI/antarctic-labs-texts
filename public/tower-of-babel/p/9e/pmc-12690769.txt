PDNO-22 Case series on the management protocol of pineoblastoma and medulloblastoma at Parirenyatwa Group of Hospitals, Harare, Zimbabwe.
Authors: Tafadzwa Moreen Dube, Simbarashe Sanyamandwe, Maximillian Dzowa, Shungu Ushewokunze, Luxwell Jokonya, Kazadi K Kalangu
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractObjectivesTo investigate the management protocol for pineoblastoma and medulloblastoma tumors at Parirenyatwa Hospital.MethodsThe clinical and surgical records of 4 pediatric patients diagnosed with pineoblastoma and medulloblastoma managed at Parirenyatwa hospital between November 2024 and April 2025 were accessed from the archives and analysed.ResultsFour cases of posterior fossa tumors with histological results showing small round blue cells and immunohistochemistry for medulloblastoma or pineoblastoma were analysed. The ages ranged from 2 to 5 years, with a 3:1 female-to-male ratio. All patients had ventriculoperitoneal shunting done for obstructive hydrocephalus before definitive craniotomy. All cases had a contrasted brain CT scan done, 1 patient had a pre-operative whole spine MRI scan, and none had cranio-spinal MRI postoperatively. All patients were discussed preoperatively in a multidisciplinary platform that included paediatricians, neurosurgeons, pathologists, radiologists and radio-oncologists. None of the patients had an early 72-hour post-operative CT scan done due to financial constraints as there is no Ct scan at hospital. Day 10 lumbar punctures were done on 3 patients, single patient had CSF taken at day 12 as parents were reluctant to give consent. The results showed CSF seeding of tumour in all cases. Medulloblastoma and pineoblastoma were equally represented. One patient received neoadjuvant chemotherapy, whilst others received standard adjuvant treatment. All the patients were reviewed by radiation oncologists at day 21 post operatively, but initiation of treatment was varied.ConclusionThe treatment protocol for medulloblastoma and pineoblastoma includes pre and operative imaging, surgical excision or biopsy, day 10 lumbar puncture to rule out spinal seeding, followed up by adjuvant chemoradiotherapy. For best outcomes, in-house affordable imaging should be available and consent for future procedures and tests should be granted at first contact.
