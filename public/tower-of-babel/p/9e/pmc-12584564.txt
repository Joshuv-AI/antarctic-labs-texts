Correction: Macrophage sensitivity to bexmarilimab-induced reprogramming is shaped by the tumor microenvironment
Published: 2025

Rannikko JH, Turpin R, Boström P, Virtakoivu R, Harth C, Takeda A, et al Macrophage sensitivity to bexmarilimab-induced reprogramming is shaped by the tumor microenvironment. J Immunother Cancer. 2025;13:e011292. https://doi.org/10.1136/jitc-2024-011292

In this article, figure 4 panel E mistakenly reported asterisks for unadjusted P-values<0.05. The panel E has now been replaced with a corrected figure reporting Benjamini-Hochberg-adjusted P-values<0.05. Consequently, upregulation of B cell receptor signaling remained statistically significant in cancer-free tissues of responsive patients, but not in non-responsive patients. Therefore, bexmarilimab-induced activation of B cell responses in cancer-free breast tissues is not independent of treatment sensitivity in the corresponding tumors, and this conclusion was removed from the article text.
