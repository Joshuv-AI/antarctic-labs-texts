OA2061. What do EU-Injury Data Base data teach us about sports injuries?
Authors: S Nijman, H Valkenberg
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundSports participation contributes to a healthy society. However, of 10 million Dutch people who exercise weekly, more than one-third sustain one or more injuries. As a result, athletes have to temporarily or permanently stop exercising. Sports injury prevention programs can prevent injuries, allowing people to continue exercising and meeting exercise guidelines. Research into the epidemiology and causes of sports injuries is crucial for effective interventions. In the Netherlands we use Dutch Injury Database (IDB) data for prioritizing what kind of sports injuries interventions should be developed.MethodsFor the current research we used IDB data from 15 EU countries to make an overview of available knowledge on sports injuries that needed treatment at Emergency Departments. We analysed what kind of injuries were sustained and what body parts were injured, and in which type of sports the injuries were sustained.ResultsData from 15 different European countries showed that over the period 2018-2022 data from 572,280 cases of athletes who visited the Emergency Department because of sports injuries were available in the IDB. Thirty percent of the injured athletes sustained a fracture, 28 percent visited the ED department with a contusion or bruise and 18 percent sustained sprains and strains. Sprains and strains occurred mostly in the ankle, 8 percent off all sports injuries. Further analyses showed in which types of sports most injuries were sustained and the most frequently occurring mechanisms of injury.ConclusionsIDB is a rich source of data on sports injuries. It is possible to analyse risk groups, sustained injuries and compare sports injuries patterns across various countries. Although there are differences between countries, in all countries athletes suffer from the same kinds of injuries and consequences. Therefore international cooperation in the development of effective interventions may be interesting.Key messages• The IDB Data Base contains valuable information for prevention of sports injuries.• Injury prevention benefits from international cooperation in epidemiology and interventions.TopicSports injuries, epidemiology, IDB data.
