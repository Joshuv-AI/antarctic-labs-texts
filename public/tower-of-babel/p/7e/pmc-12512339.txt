Assessment of the feed additives l‐carnitine (3a910) and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate (3a911) for all animal species for the renewal of their authorisation (Lonza Sales AG)
Authors: EFSA Panel on Additives and Products or Substances used in Animal Feed (FEEDAP), Roberto Edoardo Villa, Giovanna Azimonti, Eleftherios Bonos, Henrik Christensen, Mojca Durjava, Birgit Dusemund, Ronette Gehring, Boet Glandorf, Maryline Kouba, Marta López‐Alonso, Francesca Marcon, Carlo Nebbia, Alena Pechová, Miguel Prieto‐Maradona, Ilen Röhe, Katerina Theodoridou, Montserrat Anguita, Matteo L Innocenti, Joana P Firmino
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractFollowing a request from the European Commission, EFSA was asked to deliver a scientific opinion (safety and efficacy) on the application for the renewal of the authorisation of l‐carnitine (3a910) and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate (3a911) as nutritional feed additives. The additives are currently authorised for use in all animal species. The applicant has provided evidence that the additives currently in the market comply with the existing conditions of authorisation. Two examples of l‐carnitine preparations currently placed on the market are described in the application. There is no new evidence that would lead the EFSA Panel on Additives and Products or Substances used in Animal Feed (FEEDAP) to reconsider its previous conclusions. Thus, the FEEDAP Panel concluded that the additives remain safe for the target species, consumers and the environment. Regarding user safety, the additives are not irritants to skin and eyes nor skin sensitisers. In the absence of data on the different preparations, the FEEDAP Panel could not conclude on the safety for the user of any preparations containing l‐carnitine. There is no need to assess the efficacy of the additives in the context of the renewal of the authorisation.Keywords: l‐carnitine, l‐carnitine l‐tartrate, nutritional additives, pro‐vitamins and chemically well‐defined substances having similar effect, renewal safety, vitamins

INTRODUCTION

Background and Terms of Reference

Regulation (EC) No 1831/2003 1 establishes the rules governing the Community authorisation of additives for use in animal nutrition. In particular, Article 14(1) of that Regulation lays down that an application for renewal shall be sent to the Commission at the latest 1 year before the expiry date of the authorisation.

The European Commission received a request from Lonza Sales AG represented in the EU by Lonza Benelux BV 2 for the renewal of the authorisation of the additives l‐carnitine (3a910) and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate (3a911), when used as feed additives for all animal species (category: nutritional additives; functional group: vitamins, pro‐vitamins and chemically well‐defined substances having a similar effect).

According to Article 7(1) of Regulation (EC) No 1831/2003, the Commission forwarded the application to the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) as an application under Article 14(1) (renewal of the authorisation). The dossier was received on 22 May 2024 and the general information and supporting documentation are available at https://open.efsa.europa.eu/questions/EFSA‐Q‐2024‐00302. The particulars and documents in support of the application were considered valid by EFSA as of 14 August 2024.

According to Article 8 of Regulation (EC) No 1831/2003, EFSA, after verifying the particulars and documents submitted by the applicant, shall undertake an assessment in order to determine whether the feed additive complies with the conditions laid down in Article 5. EFSA shall deliver an opinion on the safety for the target animals, consumer, user and the environment and on the efficacy of the feed additives l‐carnitine and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate when used under the proposed conditions of use (see Section 3.1.3 ).

Additional information

The additives, l‐carnitine (3a910) and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate (3a911), 3 are currently authorised for use in feed for all animal species.

EFSA issued an opinion on the safety and efficacy of these products when used in feed for all animal species (EFSA FEEDAP Panel, 2012).

DATA AND METHODOLOGIES

Data

The present assessment is based on data submitted by the applicant in the form of a technical dossier 4 in support of the authorisation request for the use of l‐carnitine and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate as feed additives.

In accordance with Article 38 of the Regulation (EC) No 178/2002 5 and taking into account the protection of confidential information and of personal data in accordance with Articles 39 to 39e of the same Regulation, and of the Decision of EFSA's Executive Director laying down practical arrangements concerning transparency and confidentiality, 6 a non‐confidential version of the dossier has been published on Open.EFSA.

According to Article 32c(2) of Regulation (EC) No 178/2002 and the Decision of EFSA's Executive Director laying down the practical arrangements on pre‐submission phase and public consultations, EFSA carried out a public consultation on the non‐confidential version of the technical dossier from 03 January to 24 January 2025, for which no comments were received.

The confidential version of the technical dossier was subject to a target consultation of the interested Member States from 20 August 2024 to 20 November 2024, for which the received comments were considered for the assessment.

The FEEDAP Panel used the data provided by the applicant together with data from other sources, such as previous risk assessments by EFSA or other expert bodies, peer‐reviewed scientific papers, other scientific reports and experts' knowledge, to deliver the present output.

The European Union Reference Laboratory (EURL) considered that the conclusions and recommendations reached in the previous assessment regarding the methods used for the control of l‐carnitine and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate in animal feed are valid and applicable for the current application. 7

Methodologies

The approach followed by the FEEDAP Panel to assess the safety and efficacy of l‐carnitine and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate is in line with the principles laid down in Regulation (EC) No 429/2008 8 and the relevant guidance documents: Guidance on the renewal of the authorisation of feed additives (EFSA FEEDAP Panel, 2021).

ASSESSMENT

l‐carnitine (≥ 97% on as is basis) and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate (≥ 97% on as is basis) are currently authorised as nutritional additives (functional group: vitamins, pro‐vitamins and chemically well‐defined substances having similar effect) in feed and water for drinking for all animal species. This assessment regards the renewal of their authorisation.

The applicant indicated that, with the present application, it is seeking the authorisation for l‐carnitine (≥ 97%) and its solid preparation(s), and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate (≥ 97%), which will be described in the sections below.

Characterisation

Characterisation of the active substances

The additives l‐carnitine and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate are currently authorised with a minimum content of the active substances of 97%. The additives are produced by chemical synthesis, and the applicant declared that no modifications to the manufacturing process were performed since the previous application. 9

Characterisation of the preparations

The current authorisation states that l‐carnitine may be placed on the market and used as an additive consisting of a preparation. The applicant is asking for the authorisation of preparations of l‐carnitine with a minimum content of 10% carnitine and provided two examples of l‐carnitine preparations currently placed on the market, one with a minimum content of 10% carnitine, formulated with wheat bran, calcium carbonate and amorphous silica (E 551a) 13 , and another with a minimum of 50% carnitine with amorphous silica (E 551a). All the components of the preparations are either feed materials or authorised feed additives.

The Panel, however, considers that the microbial contamination and the levels of the impurities analysed in these preparations are of no concern.

Conditions of use

l‐carnitine and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate are currently authorised without a minimum or maximum content for use in complete feed and/or water for drinking for all animal species. Under ‘Other provisions’ of the authorisation, it is specified that:

–In the directions for use of the additive and premixture, indicate the storage and stability conditions.

In the directions for use of the additive and premixture, indicate the storage and stability conditions.

–For safety: breathing protection, safety glasses and gloves shall be worn during handling.

For safety: breathing protection, safety glasses and gloves shall be worn during handling.

–The additive may be used in water for drinking.

The additive may be used in water for drinking.

In addition, for l‐carnitine, the following is included:

– l‐carnitine may be placed on the market and used as an additive consisting of a preparation.

l‐carnitine may be placed on the market and used as an additive consisting of a preparation.

The applicant did not request any change in the current conditions of the authorisation.

Safety

In its previous opinion (EFSA FEEDAP Panel, 2012), the FEEDAP Panel concluded that l‐carnitine and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate are safe for the target species, which appear to have a wide margin of safety (> 10) at the levels typically used in feed (between 10 and 50 mg l‐carnitine/kg feed). The Panel also concluded that the use of l‐carnitine and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate as additives in animal nutrition is safe for consumers and the environment. Both products are not irritants to skin and eyes, but adverse effects on the respiratory tract could not be excluded.

The applicant declares that no adverse effects including accidents for target animals, consumers, users and/or the environment have been reported since the approval of the additives and preparations. 17

Extensive literature search

The applicant performed an extensive literature search to provide evidence that, in light of the current knowledge, the additives l‐carnitine and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate remain safe under the approved conditions for the target species, consumers, users and the environment. The search covered the period 2010–2024 using the LIVIVO, NCBI, Ovid and Toxinfo databases. A Google Scholar and a manual hand search were also used. The search protocol described the inclusion and exclusion criteria applied for the screening process. A total of 391 hits were considered for the screening, and after applying the exclusion criteria and deduplication, a total of 166 hits were considered eligible and relevant by the applicant for the safety of the additive (135 for the target species, 27 for the consumers, 4 for the users, 0 for the environment). The Panel reviewed all the papers and considered that none of them identified new information that would lead to reconsider its previous conclusions.

Safety for the target species, consumers and the environment

No new data were found in the literature search that would lead the Panel to revise the conclusions previously reached regarding the safety for the target species, consumers and environment. Considering the above and the fact that the manufacturing process and composition of the additives have not changed since the previous authorisation, the Panel concludes that l‐carnitine and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate remain safe for the target species, the consumer and the environment under the conditions of the current authorisation.

Safety for the user

In its previous assessment, the FEEDAP Panel concluded, based on studies, that l‐carnitine and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate are not irritating to skin and eyes and are not skin sensitisers. Adverse effects on the respiratory tract could not be fully excluded.

Based on the highest dusting potential measured (see Section 3.1 ), the FEEDAP Panel considers that the exposure of users through inhalation is likely.

No new studies were submitted with any of the preparations of l‐carnitine.

In the absence of new information, the FEEDAP Panel considers that the previous conclusions still apply to l‐carnitine and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate. In the absence of data on the different preparations, the FEEDAP Panel cannot conclude on the safety for the user of any preparations containing l‐carnitine.

Efficacy

The present application for renewal of the authorisation does not include a proposal for amending or supplementing the conditions of the original authorisation that would have an impact on the efficacy of the additive. Therefore, there is no need for assessing the efficacy of the additive in the context of the renewal of the authorisation.

Post‐market monitoring

The FEEDAP Panel considers that there is no need for specific requirements for a post‐market monitoring plan other than those established in the Feed Hygiene Regulation 18 and good manufacturing practice.

CONCLUSIONS

The applicant has provided evidence that the additives l‐carnitine and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate currently in the market comply with the existing conditions of authorisation.

The use of the feed additives in animal nutrition remains safe for the target species, the consumer and the environment under the conditions of the current authorisation.

As regards the safety for the user, the additives l‐carnitine and l‐carnitine l‐tartrate are not irritants to skin and eyes nor skin sensitisers. The FEEDAP Panel cannot conclude on the safety for the user of any preparations containing l‐carnitine.

There is no need to assess the efficacy of the additives in the context of the renewal of the authorisation.

RECOMMENDATION

The FEEDAP Panel notes that the values found for impurities and microbial contamination in one preparation were high and deserve attention/monitoring during the production process.
