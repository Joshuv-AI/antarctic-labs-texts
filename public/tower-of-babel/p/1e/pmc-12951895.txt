For Figure 2: Image credit: Sakkas H, Bozidis P, Franks A, Papadopoulou C, Oropouche Fever. A Review. Viruses [Internet]. 2018;10(4):175. Licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Nucleic Acids Res [Internet]. 2011;39(suppl_1):D576–82” and has now been corrected to “Romero-Alvarez D, Escobar LE. Oropouche fever, an emergent disease from the Americas. Microbes Infect [Internet]. 2018;20(3):135–46.”

The original article has been updated.
