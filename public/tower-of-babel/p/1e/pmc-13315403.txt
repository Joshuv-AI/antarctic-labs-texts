Intelligent predictive neural network analysis on two phase bioconvection flow of dusty hybrid nanofluid with Cattaneo Christov flux model and melting phenomena
Authors: Ali Raza, Imen Safra, Saba Liaqat, Farkhod Rakhmonov, Munawar Abbas, Durdana Rustamova Farkhad, Abdulbasit A Darem
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundThis analysis’s main purpose is to investigate the outcome of melting heating on microorganisms in a two-phase bioconvection flow of a dusty hybrid nanofluid via a sheet with Cattaneo-Christov flux model.MethodUsing an appropriate similarity transformation, constitutive partial differential equations are transformed into ordinary differential equations. Using reference datasets from numerical calculations, we train and assess the intelligent Bayesian regularized predictive neural network approach to forecast flow solutions under different physical parameter scenarios.ApplicationsThis model is valuable in advanced thermal and environmental engineering systems that combine two-phase flows, particulate matter, and microorganism-induced bioconvection. It is used in melting and solidification processes, including phase-change materials, metal casting, and thermal energy storage systems, where dusty hybrid nanofluid improve heat transport. The Cattaneo-Christov flux model’s addition makes it applicable to high-speed thermal transport and conduction of non-Fourier heat in micro- and nanoscale devices.OutcomesWhen the thermal and solutal relaxation parameters’ values rise, the solutal and thermal distribution improves. Histogram analysis, regression analysis, statistic transition, and Mean square error analysis all show that it is accurate when compared to reference data.Graphical abstract Keywords: Dusty hybrid nanofluid, Gyrotactic microbes, Melting heating, Cattaneo-Christov flux model, Artificial neural network

Microorganisms are microscopic living things that may be found in a variety of settings and are crucial to human health, industry, and ecology. Complex fluid–microbe dynamics can result from their movement and interactions, which have a substantial impact on fluid flow. While microorganisms and blood components influence blood rheology and immune response in the human body, bacteria and algae alter flow behavior in aquatic environments. In environmental, engineering, and medicinal research, it is essential to comprehend these relationships. By affecting thermal transport mechanisms in many settings, microorganisms have a major impact on how heat is transferred during fluid movement. Microbial activity can cause biofilm growth on solid surfaces in industrial applications like heat exchangers, which reduces heat transfer efficiency and functions as a thermal barrier, raising energy requirements. Microbial metabolism can alter the fluid’s thermal characteristics in natural aquatic systems, influencing temperature distribution and general thermal behavior. Neural networks

The development of machine learning algorithms (MLAs) has made it possible to address complicated scientific, technical, and mathematical problems. We employ SLAs (supervised learning algorithms) because they strictly look like human learning processes. By evaluating data and resolving problems SLAs, like humans, adjust weights in reaction to differences among anticipated and desired outcomes. Neural networks (NNs) are trained using the training dataset, which is a set of input-output pairs. To lower prediction errors, each training sample modifies the network’s weights using the back-propagation method.

This section uses the results of the suggested neural network back-propagation intelligent Bayesian regularized (BPIBRNN) procedure to analyze the impact of melting phenomena and the flux model of Cattaneo-Christov on heat transfer in steady 2D flow of dusty HNF via a sheet with permeable medium and gyrotactic microbes.

Final Remarks

In this study, the dusty hybrid nanofluid’s bioconvective flow over a Riga plate in the presence of mobile bacteria has been numerically investigated in relation to melting transfer of heat and Cattaneo–Christov thermal flux. The flow, heat, and mass transfer parameters were modeled, validated, and forecasted using an intelligent predictive neural network approach.
