Authors: Jinyu Wu, Yun Liao, Wen Wang, Xin Zhang
Published: 2026

There was a mistake in Figure 3 as published. Due to an inadvertent upload error during the submission/production process, Figure 3 was incorrectly duplicated from Figure 5B.

Distribution of trial status among registered CAR-T clinical trials for non-Hodgkin lymphoma. The donut chart summarizes the operational status of the included trials identified from the Trialtrove database. Most studies were classified as completed or actively open, whereas smaller proportions were terminated, closed, planned, or temporarily closed. These findings reflect the ongoing expansion and dynamic evolution of CAR-T clinical development in NHL.
