Correction to: Chromosome-level genome assembly of the common chiton, Liolophura japonica (Lischke, 1873)
Authors: Hong Kong Biodiversity Genomics Consortium
Published: 2025

This is a correction to: Hong Kong Biodiversity Genomics Consortium. Chromosome-level genome assembly of the common chiton, Liolophura japonica (Lischke, 1873). GigaByte, 2024 Apr 25; 2024: gigabyte123. https://doi.org/10.46471/gigabyte.123.

In the originally published version of this manuscript we believed the species sequenced was Liolophura japonica (Lischke, 1873) [1]. Based on a recent publication analysing the molecular phylogeny of molluscs [2], we agree with the authors that the species of our genome resource should be Liolophura sinensis instead of L. japonica.

Where any reference is made to L. japonica this should instead be L. sinensis. The metadata of the supported data in NCBI has also been updated, as has the title of the data in figshare [3].
