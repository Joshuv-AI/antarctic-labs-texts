Polyamine Anabolism Promotes Chemotherapy‐Induced Breast Cancer Stem Cell Enrichment (Adv. Sci. 40/2024)
Authors: Guangyu Ji, Jia Liu, Zhiqun Zhao, Jie Lan, You Yang, Zheng Wang, Huijing Feng, Kai Ji, Xiaofeng Jiang, Huize Xia, Guangyao Wei, Yajing Zhang, Yuhong Zhang, Xinlong Du, Yawen Wang, Yuanyuan Yang, Zhaojian Liu, Kai Zhang, Qi Mei, Rong Sun, Haiquan Lu
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Abstract Breast Cancer Stem Cells Article number 2404853 by Kai Zhang, Qi Mei, Rong Sun, Haiquan Lu, and co‐workers reveals the critical role of polyamine anabolism in chemotherapy‐induced breast cancer stem cell (BCSC) enrichment. Chemotherapy induces the transcription factor HIF‐1‐regulated ODC1 and SRM expression and activates polyamine anabolism, which finally leads to enrichment of BCSCs. This study provides a novel strategy for breast cancer treatment by identifying a novel specific HIF‐1 inhibitor, britannin, which effectively blocks chemotherapy‐induced polyamine anabolism and BCSC enrichment.
