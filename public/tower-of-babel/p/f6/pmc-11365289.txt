Retinectomy under air
Authors: Adam M Hanif, Benjamin K Young
Published: 2024

This video demonstrates the creation of an inferior retinectomy under air. Air may be an effective alternative to perfluorocarbon liquid for stabilizing the retina during retinectomy if performed incrementally.
