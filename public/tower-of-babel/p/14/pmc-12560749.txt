Correction to ‘PAM-interacting domain turn-helix 51 motifs can improve Cas9-SpRY activity’
Published: 2025

This is a correction to: Reto Eggenschwiler, Thomas Hoffmann, Oleg Dmytrenko, Mika Opitz, Marlene Ackel-Zakour, Pascal Wang, Shannon A McCallan, Mariane Fráguas-Eggenschwiler, Heiner Niemann, Atanas Patronov, Chase L Beisel, Tobias Cantz, PAM-interacting domain turn-helix 51 motifs can improve Cas9–SpRY activity, Nucleic Acids Research, Volume 53, Issue 15, 28 August 2025, gkaf782, https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaf782

The change made in the article is that the ModelArchive accession code “ma-yr1l4” has been replaced with “ma-g8dr2”.

Under the heading Data Availability, text now reads: “[…]and homology models are available in ModelArchive with the accession codes ma-g8dr2, ma-ww498, ma-ck81q, ma-ex6um, ma-8xk9d, ma-y71uv, ma-o6d2j, ma-dfc41.” instead of: "[…]and homology models are available in ModelArchive with the accession codes ma-yr1l4, ma-ww498, ma-ck81q, ma-ex6um, ma-8xk9d, ma-y71uv, ma-o6d2j, and ma-dfc41.e
