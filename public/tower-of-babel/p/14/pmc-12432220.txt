Correction: Androgen receptor signaling induces hemorrhage and angiogenesis in the irradiated bladder
Authors: Takahiro Komori, Hiroki Ide, Junichi Fukada, Yuto Baba, Kazuhiro Matsumoto, Hiroshi Miyamoto, Mototsugu Oya
Published: 2025

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-15245-9, published online 11 August 2025

The original version of this Article contained an error in the order of the Figures. Figure 2 was published as Figure 1, Figure 3 was published as Figure 2, Figure 5 was published as Figure 3, Figure 6 was published as Figure 4, Figure 1 was published as Figure 5, and Figure 4 was published as Figure 6. The Figure legends were correct.

The original Figs. 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 and accompanying legends appear below.

Cumulative incidence of gross hematuria in prostate cancer patients receiving RT with or without ADT.

Changes in AR expression in bladder cancer cells after irradiation. Quantitative RT-PCR of AR in UMUC3, 5637-AR, and 647 V-AR cultured for 24 h after 2 Gy irradiation. Expression of AR was normalized to that of GAPDH. Transcription amount is presented relative to that of control cells. Each value represents the mean (+ SD) from three independent experiments. *p < 0.05 (vs. control).

Changes in the expression of VEGFR genes after irradiation. Quantitative RT-PCR of FLT-1 (A) and KDR (B) in UMUC3, 5637-AR, and 647 V-AR cultured for 24 h after 2 Gy irradiation. Expression of each gene was normalized to that of GAPDH. Transcription amount is presented relative to that of control cells. Each value represents the mean (+ SD) from three independent experiments. *p < 0.05 (vs. control).

Effects of androgen on VEGFR genes in AR-positive (A, B) and AR-negative (C, D) bladder cancer cells. Quantitative RT-PCR of FLT1 (A, C) and KDR (B, D) in UMUC3/UMUC3-AR-shRNA, 5637-vector/5637-AR, and 647 V-vector/647V-AR cultured with ethanol (mock), 10 nmol/L DHT, and/or 1 µmol/L HF for 48 h and subsequently irradiated (2 Gy). Expression of each gene was normalized to that of GAPDH. Transcription amount is presented relative to that of mock treated cells. Each value represents the mean (+ SD) from three independent experiments. *p < 0.05 (vs. mock treatment). *p < 0.05 (vs. DHT treatment).

Effects of androgen deprivation on hematuria in radiation cystitis model mice. Seven days after surgery (orchiectomy or sham), all male C3H/Neu mice received irradiation (20 Gy). Cumulative incidence for the detection of hematuria via three consecutive positive urine tests was compared.

Immunostaining of VEGFR2 and CD34 in bladder resected from radiation cystitis model mice. VEGFR2 (A) and CD34 (B) expression in the bladders resected from radiation cystitis model mice receiving sham surgery (n = 8) or castration (n = 7). Bar graphs show H-score of VEGFR2 expression or MVD. Each value represents the mean (+ SD). *p < 0.05 (vs. sham surgery).

The original Article has been corrected.
