RETRACTION: Acute Toxicity Study of Zerumbone‐Loaded Nanostructured Lipid Carrier on BALB/c Mice Model
Authors: BioMed Research International
Published: 2025

RETRACTION: H. S. Rahman, A. Rasedee, H. H. Othman, et al., “Acute Toxicity Study of Zerumbone‐Loaded Nanostructured Lipid Carrier on BALB/c Mice Model,” BioMed Research International 2014, (2014): 563930, https://doi.org/10.1155/2014/563930.

The above article, published online on 08 September 2014 in Wiley Online Library (http://wileyonlinelibrary.com), has been retracted by agreement between the Journal Section Editor, Jinsong Ren, and John Wiley & Sons Ltd. The retraction has been agreed due to multiple concerns with the figures presented in the manuscript, initially raised on PubPeer [1].
