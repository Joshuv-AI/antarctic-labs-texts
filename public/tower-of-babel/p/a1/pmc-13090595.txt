The controversies in the clinical management of β-blockers in acute heart failure induced by rapid atrial fibrillation: A narrative review
Authors: Wenfang Zhu, Jinpeng Xu
Published: 2026

Dear Editorial Team,

Thank you for forwarding the letter regarding our manuscript. I sincerely apologize for the citation errors pointed out by the authors of the references we cited. After careful consideration, I fully understand and acknowledge these issues, and I would like to propose the following revisions. Please rest assured that these changes will not alter the core content or conclusions of the paper.

Removal of misleading citation

Original text:

“There are reports in the literature of multiple successful cases where esmolol rapidly controlled the heart rate to around 100 beats per minute under close monitoring, with no significant decrease in blood pressure, and subsequent improvement in cardiac function within a few days [1].”

Reason for change: The phrase was preceded by “Clinical experience suggests,” which indicates it is based on our clinical experience, not a cited study. Thus, it is inappropriate to cite this reference.

Revision: I propose completely removing this sentence, as it inaccurately conveys information supported by our study.

Reference: This part will no longer cite any references, as it is based on clinical experience rather than published research

Acknowledgment of other inaccurate citations

After thoroughly reviewing the manuscript, I acknowledge there are a few places where the citations were not entirely appropriate. I sincerely apologize for these mistakes, and here are the proposed revisions:

Original text

“A study attempted to use ‘low-dose β-blockers combined with milrinone’ to treat severe decompensated heart failure with a high heart rate. The results showed that this approach not only reduced the heart rate but also improved cardiac function without inducing severe hypotension [2].”

Issue: The study used β-blockers combined with dobutamine in most cases, and milrinone was only used in a few. The citation of Reference 2 is also not appropriate and should be changed to Reference 3.

Revision: I suggest changing this sentence to:

“A study attempted to use ‘β-blockers combined with inotropic support’ to treat severe decompensated heart failure with a high heart rate. The results showed that this approach not only reduced the heart rate but also improved cardiac function without inducing severe hypotension [3].”

Original text

“Subgroup analyses of studies such as AFFIRM suggest that in patients with atrial fibrillation and heart failure, simply controlling the heart rate can improve symptoms, and restoring sinus rhythm is not the only approach [4].”

Issue: Reference 4 is not appropriate, and should be changed to Reference 5.

Revision: I suggest changing this to:

“A study suggests that in patients with atrial fibrillation and heart failure, both controlling the heart rate and restoring sinus rhythm are the pillars of the therapeutic [5].”

Original text

“A retrospective observational study by Shinohara et al. demonstrated that combined use of Landiolol and Dobutamine effectively controlled heart rate while maintaining cardiac output in acute heart failure patients with low cardiac output and atrial fibrillation [6], [7].”

Issue: References [6], [7] are not appropriate and should be replaced with Reference 3.

Revision: I suggest changing this to:

“A study demonstrated that combined use of Landiolol and Dobutamine effectively controlled heart rate while maintaining cardiac output in acute heart failure patients with low cardiac output and atrial fibrillation [3].”

Original text

“Landiolol has been listed as one of the preferred rate control drugs for rapid atrial fibrillation complicated by heart failure in the Japanese atrial fibrillation treatment guidelines [8].”

Issue: Reference 8 is not appropriate, and should be replaced with Reference 9.

Revision: I suggest changing this to:

“Landiolol has been listed as one of the preferred rate control drugs for rapid atrial fibrillation complicated by heart failure in the Japanese pharmacotherapy of cardiac arrhythmias guidelines [9].”

Original text

“Some retrospective analyses suggest that continuing or initiating β-blockers early in patients with acute heart failure with acceptable blood pressure is associated with better post-discharge prognosis [10], [11], [12].”

Issue: Since the next section elaborates on this sentence in detail, no additional reference is needed here.

Revision: I suggest modifying this to:

“Some analyses suggest that continuing or initiating β-blockers early in patients with acute heart failure with acceptable blood pressure is associated with better post-discharge prognosis.”

Original text

“Another multicenter trial, STRONG-HF (2022), significantly reduced the 90-day readmission and death risk of patients by optimizing medications including β-blockers during hospitalization [12].”

Issue: The endpoint time and intervention period are inaccurately expressed.

Revision: I suggest modifying this to:

“Another multicenter trial, STRONG-HF (2022), significantly reduced the 180-day readmission and death risk of patients by optimizing medications including β-blockers during hospitalization and the early post-discharge period [12].”

I would like to emphasize that these modifications aim to improve the accuracy of the references and clarify certain points, without altering the main conclusions or core content of the paper. I hope these changes will resolve the concerns raised by the authors of the references we cited while maintaining the integrity of our original work.

Once again, I apologize for the citation errors and appreciate the opportunity to make these revisions. Thank you for your understanding and support.

Best regards,

Dr. Wenfang Zhu
