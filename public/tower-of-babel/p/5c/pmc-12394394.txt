Author Correction: FOXF1 inhibits invasion and metastasis of lung adenocarcinoma cells and enhances anti-tumor immunity via MFAP4/FAK signal axis
Authors: Zhenyu Wang, MengXia Xie, Zhongyue Jia, Ziwei Tao, Ping Zhao, Muying Ying
Published: 2025

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-72578-7, published online 13 September 2024

The original version of the Article contained an error in Figure 4. The FOXF1 (36h) image in panel D is a duplication of the shRNA (36h) image in panel F. The authors provided data for quantification shown in panel 4D, which corroborated the results in the article. The original Figure 4 and accompanying legend appear below.

The high expression of FOXF1 can significantly inhibit the malignant degree of LUAD cells. (a, b) The cell models of knockdown and overexpression of FOXF1 constructed in A549 and SPC-A1 cells. (c, d) Cell scratch experiment detecting the effect of overexpression of FOXF1 on the migration ability of A549 and SPC-A1 cells. (e, f) Cell scratch experiment detecting the effect of knockdown of FOXF1 on the migration ability of A549 and SPC-A1 cells. (g, h) Transwell experiment detecting the effect of overexpression or knockdown of FOXF1 on the invasion ability of A549 and SPC-A1 cells.

The original Article has been corrected.
