IFPHOR0027 Improving the Inclusiveness of Data by Building Trust with Local Communities: The Coproduction of a Simple Guide to How We Use Your Data
Authors: Neil Bendel
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractComplete, accurate, up to date and consistently recorded data is essential for effective service planning, quality improvement and monitoring the equity of public services. The quality of data on ethnicity and other protected characteristics remains variable and there are known to be significant data gaps and inaccuracies among communities experiencing inequalities linked, in part, to a lack of understanding of, and mistrust in, how personal data is collected, managed and used among both local residents and staff groups. Building on the recommendations of the UK Inclusive Data Taskforce, Manchester City Council, Greater Manchester ICB locality team and other local partners are committed to the development of an Inclusive Data Strategy with an initial focus on creating an environment of trust with local communities around the collection and use of personal data. We have co-produced a user-friendly version of Manchester City Council’s corporate privacy notice (“A simple guide to how we use your information”) with people with lived experience as a vehicle for reducing caution and suspicion among local communities and staff groups regarding how and why personal data is collected, managed and used. There is wider learning from this work and the potential for wider replication across the health and care system and academic partners.
