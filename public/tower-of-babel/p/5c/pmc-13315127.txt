Correction to: Cardiac Channelopathies: Clinical Diagnosis and Promising Therapeutics
Published: 2026

In the article by Dib Nehme et al, “Cardiac Channelopathies: Clinical Diagnosis and Promising Therapeutics,” which published on April 25, 2025 and appeared in the May 6, 2025 issue of the Journal (J Am Heart Assoc. 2025;14:e040072. DOI: 10.1161/JAHA.124.040072) a correction was needed.

In the sources of funding statement, the authors mistakenly indicated that author George W. Booz received support from the National Institute of General Medical Sciences of the National Institutes of Health under award number P20GM12133. This has now been removed from the Sources of Funding statement. The full statement now reads:

This work was supported by grants from the American University of Beirut Faculty of Medicine (grant numbers MPP‐320145; URB‐103949), by the Centre National de la Recherche Scientifique (grant numbers 103507/103487/103941/103944), and by Collaborative Research Stimulus (grant number 103556) funds and MPP‐320095 funds to F.A.Z., the Fondation de France grant number 0007511, and grant number ANR‐17‐CE17‐0015‐01 from the Agence Nationale de la Recherche to M.M. N.J.H. was supported by a scholarship of the Campus France Eiffel Program of Excellence for visiting international students (2021).

The correction has been made to the current online version of the article, which is available here: https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/JAHA.124.040072.
