Correction to: Economic and Clinical Burden of Managing Sickle Cell Disease with Recurrent Vaso-Occlusive Crises in the United States

Published: 2023

Correction to: Adv Ther (2023) 40:3543–3558 10.1007/s12325-023-02545-7

An error in Fig. 2b has been corrected; for the subgroup of patients with ≥ 2 VOCs per year and commercial insurance, the sample size should be 437.

An error in Supplementary Fig. 1a has been corrected; the lifetime healthcare costs for the subgroup of patients with ≥ 2 VOCs per year with a life expectancy of 50 years should be $4.5M.

An error in Supplementary Table 1 has been corrected; the prevalence of gallstones in patients aged ≥ 36 years should be 36 (9.2).

The original article and the supplementary material have been corrected.
