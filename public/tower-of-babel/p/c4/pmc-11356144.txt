In the original publication [1], there was a typographic error in the legend for Figure 2. The corrected legend appears below:

Figure 2. Standard haematoxylin–eosin (H&E) histochemical analysis of liver from representative images of fish fed control, PM20, and PM25 diets. We obtained 5 µm cross-sections from the paraffin-embedded liver of CTRL (panels (A,B)), PM20 (panels (C,D)), and PM25 (panels (E,F)) fish. Scale bar = 100 µm.
