In the published article, there was an error in the results description, as the corresponding data for male and female college students were interchanged:

“Specifically, this predictive relationship maintained statistical significance among female college students (β = 0.262, p < 0.001) and male college students (β = 0.544, p < 0.001).”

A correction has been made to 3 Results, 3.3.2 Moderating role of gender, paragraph 2. The corrected sentence appears below:

“Specifically, this predictive relationship maintained statistical significance among female college students (β = 0.544, p < 0.001) and male college students (β = 0.262, p < 0.001).”

The original version of this article has been updated.
