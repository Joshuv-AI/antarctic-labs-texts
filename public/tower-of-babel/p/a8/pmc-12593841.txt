Correction to: Correlates of virological non-suppression among children and adolescents aged 5–19 years on Dolutegravir-based regimens attending Gulu regional referral hospital, Northern Uganda: a mixed methods cross-sectional study
Authors: Vincent Okech Moro, John Mbaziira Ssenkusu, Flavia Matovu Kiweewa
Published: 2025

Correction to: 10.1186/s12879-025-11647-2.

Following publication of the original article [1], we were notified of a few grammatical errors which have been addressed. The tables have also been re-arranged in numerical order.

Aadditionally, the following statement under independent variables was deleted: “duration on ART, ART adherence level, ARV exposure, WHO clinical stage on DTG initiation, CD4 count on ART initiation, multi-month dispensing, history of TB infection, current line of treatment and nutritional status. Multi-month dispensing was defined as ARV refill of at least 3 months.”

In the Data processing section, “0.2” was corrected to “0.20” in the following sentence: “Variables that were found to have a p-value of ≤ 0.2 at bivariate level of Poisson regression analysis were included into the multivariate model and adjusted for confounders to determine which of them had an independent significant association with the virological non-suppression.”

The original article has been corrected.
