Approximation of magnetic Schrödinger operators with δ-interactions supported on networks
Authors: Markus Holzmann
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractThis paper deals with the approximation of a magnetic Schrödinger operator with a singular δ-potential that is formally given by (i∇+A)2+Q+αδΣ by Schrödinger operators with regular potentials in the norm resolvent sense. This is done for Σ being the finite union of C2-hypersurfaces, for coefficients A, Q, and α under almost minimal assumptions such that the associated quadratic forms are closed and sectorial, and Q and α are allowed to be complex-valued functions. In particular, Σ can be a graph in R2 or the boundary of a piecewise C2-domain. Moreover, spectral implications of the mentioned convergence result are discussed.Keywords: Schrödinger operator, Singular potential, Approximation in norm resolvent sense

Introduction

Schrödinger operators with singular δ-potentials supported on sets of measure zero play an important role in mathematical physics. Singular potentials supported on points were already used in the early days of quantum mechanics [29, 39] and later systematically analyzed from a mathematical point of view, see the monograph [1] and the references therein. In the last three decades, there was a substantial interest in singular potentials supported on hypersurfaces Σ in Rn, n≥2, such as curves in R2 or surfaces in R3, see the review paper [16], the monograph [19], or the research papers [5, 6, 9, 14, 15, 17, 18, 33], to mention just a few selected works. Such operators are formally given by

and they describe for attractive interactions (i.e., for α<0) the propagation of a quantum particle on Σ under the influence of a magnetic vector potential A and an electric potential Q allowing the tunneling of the particle between different parts of Σ, see [5, 16]; from this point of view, graph-like structures Σ⊂R2 are of particular interest. Moreover, operators of the form H0,0,α are useful in the design of photonic crystals with prescribed spectral gaps [22, 26].

Of course, the operators with the singular potentials are only idealized replacements of more realistic operators with regular potentials. The relation of the regular and the singular models was justified via an approximation procedure already in [29]. For coefficients A=Q=0 the approximation of one-, two-, and three-dimensional Schrödinger operators with δ-point interactions by Schrödinger operators with scaled regular potentials in the norm resolvent sense was treated systematically in the 1970s and 1980s, see the monograph [1] and the references therein; the latter convergence implies, in particular, the convergence of the spectra and of functions of the involved operators [27, 40] and thus, it justifies the usage of the idealized models as good approximations of their regular counterparts. The approximation of Schrödinger operators with δ-potentials supported on the sphere in R3 was considered in [2, 37], then the situation that Σ is the boundary of a star shaped domain and α is real-valued and smooth was investigated in [36], and unbounded and C2-smooth Σ in R2 and R3 and constant α∈R were treated in [17, 18]. It is worth mentioning that in [38] Laplacians with δ- (and other measure valued) potentials supported on quite general geometries were approximated, but only in the strong resolvent sense, which does not imply the convergence of the spectra of the approximating operators in the Hausdorff metric. Finally, in [4] the approximation of the Schrödinger operator with a δ-potential of real-valued strength α∈L∞(Σ) on one bounded or unbounded C2-smooth hypersurface Σ⊂Rn, n≥2, was shown, and in [5] the approximation of a two-dimensional Schrödinger operator with a homogeneous magnetic field and an electric δ-potential of real-valued strength α∈L∞(Σ) supported on a bounded C1,1-smooth curve was treated; note that [5] is the only work known to the author where the approximation of HA,Q,α for A≠0 was treated.

The main motivation in this paper is to show that the operator HA,Q,α formally given by (1.1) can be approximated in the norm resolvent sense by Schrödinger operators with scaled regular potentials under mild assumptions on A, Q, and α, and for a wide class of interaction supports Σ. Concerning the coefficients, from the point of view of quantum mechanics real-valued functions that are associated with self-adjoint operators are favored, but the analysis in this paper works equally well for complex-valued functions Q and α, yielding a relation to works on Schrödinger operators with non-real δ-potentials [10, 24, 28]. Moreover, the interaction support Σ will be allowed to be the finite union of bounded or unbounded C2-smooth hypersurfaces, and so classes of graph-like structures in R2, that are particularly important for the application to leaky quantum graphs, and piecewise C2-smooth Σ⊂Rn, which are an important subclass of Lipschitz domains, are contained.

The paper is organized as follows. Section 2 is of preliminary nature. In Subsect. 2.1 some useful properties of the space HA,Q from (1.5) are summarized, Subsect. 2.2 is devoted to the class of C2-hypersurfaces that build the network Σ, and in Subsect. 2.3 the operator HA,Q,α formally given by (1.1) is introduced. Section 3 is devoted to the proof of the main result of this paper, Theorem 1.1. Finally, in Sect. 4 some spectral implications that follow from the convergence in Theorem 1.1 are discussed.

Notations

In this paper, C denotes a generic constant that may change its value in between lines. For an open set Ω⊂Rn the set H1(Ω) is the L2-based Sobolev space of once weakly differentiable functions and Cbk(Ω), k∈N, is the set of all k times continuously differentiable functions with bounded partial derivatives up to order k. For a closed linear operator A in a Hilbert space, its domain, resolvent set, spectrum, and point spectrum are denoted by domA, ρ(A), σ(A), and σp(A). If A is self-adjoint, then its discrete and essential spectrum are σdisc(A) and σess(A), respectively.

Consequences for the spectrum of HA,Q,Vε

Since convergence in the norm resolvent sense implies the convergence of the spectra of the associated operators, Theorem 1.1 allows to transfer results on the spectrum of HA,Q,α to the spectrum of HA,Q,Vε. If Σ is one C2-hypersurface, some interesting consequences are elaborated in [4, Section 3.3]; in particular, in [4, Proposition 3.5] results on the existence of eigenvalues of H0,0,α (for A=Q=0) in various situations are transferred to H0,0,Vε. In this section, some further consequences are discussed, which cannot be obtained from the results in [4] due to the different assumptions there. Here, the focus lies in self-adjoint examples; however, since convergence in the norm resolvent sense also implies the convergence of the spectrum in the non-self-adjoint setting, see for instance [27, Section IV.3.5], also results on the spectrum of HA,Q,α for non-real α from, e.g., [10, 24, 28] could be transferred.

The starting point in all of the examples below is the fact that as a consequence of Theorem 1.1, the discrete and the essential spectra of HA,Q,Vε converge to the respective spectra of HA,Q,α. The claim in the following lemma follows from known results on the spectral convergence for families of operators that converge in the norm resolvent sense, see [27, Section IV.3.5] or [40, Satz 9.24] or also the proof of [4, Proposition 3.5] for a more detailed argument.
