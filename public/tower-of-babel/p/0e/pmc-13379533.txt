Correction to: Knockdown of Foxg1 in supporting cells increases the trans‑differentiation of supporting cells into hair cells in the neonatal mouse cochlea
Authors: Shasha Zhang, Yuan Zhang, Ying Dong, Lingna Guo, Zhong Zhang, Buwei Shao, Jieyu Qi, Han Zhou, Weijie Zhu, Xiaoqian Yan, Guodong Hong, Liyan Zhang, Xiaoli Zhang, Mingliang Tang, Chunjie Zhao, Xia Gao, Renjie Chai
Published: 2026

Correction to: Cellular and Molecular Life Sciences (2020) 77:1401–1419

10.1007/s00018-019-03291-2

In Figure 1G of this article, the immunofluorescent image labeled as the cochlear base turn of Sox2CreER/+ mice were inadvertently a duplicate of the image representing the cochlear middle turn of the same group of mice. To ensure the accuracy and integrity of our article, we hereby submit the correct immunofluorescent image of the cochlear base turn of Sox2CreER/+ mice. This correction does not affect the overall findings, conclusions, or any other data presented in the article.
