Correction: Neuroprotective effects of Shende’an tablet in the Parkinson’s disease model
Authors: Xiaoyan Sheng, Shuiyuan Yang, Xiaomin Wen, Xin Zhang, Yongfeng Ye, Peng Zhao, Limin Zang, Kang Peng, Enming Du, Sai Li
Published: 2024

Correction to: Chin Med (2021) 16:18 10.1186/s13020-021-00429-y

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors reported that there were inaccuracies in the subimages of Figs. 1c, 2a, 3a, and the HO-1 and β-actin protein bands in Fig. 6. They have corrected these errors with the accurate subimages in the correct versions. These corrections do not alter the outcomes or conclusions of their study.

The correct Figs. 1, 2, 3 and 6 have been provided in this Correction.

The incorrect Fig. 1 is:

Neuroprotection of SDA rat serum against 6-OHDA-induced toxicity in PC12 cells. a The dose-dependent effect of SDA rat serum on the viability of PC12 cells; Neuroprotection of SDA rat serum against 6-OHDA-induced toxicity in PC12 cells was measured by CCK-8 assay (b), flow cytometry assay (c), and Hoechst staining. d The values are presented as the mean ± SEM from three independent experiments (### P < 0.001 compared to control group; *P < 0.05, **P < 0.01 and ***P < 0.001 compared to 6-OHDA group)

The correct Fig. 1 is:

Neuroprotection of SDA rat serum against 6-OHDA-induced toxicity in PC12 cells. a The dose-dependent effect of SDA rat serum on the viability of PC12 cells; Neuroprotection of SDA rat serum against 6-OHDA-induced toxicity in PC12 cells was measured by CCK-8 assay (b), flow cytometry assay (c), and Hoechst staining. d The values are presented as the mean ± SEM from three independent experiments (###P < 0.001 compared to control group; *P < 0.05, **P < 0.01 and ***P < 0.001 compared to 6-OHDA group)
