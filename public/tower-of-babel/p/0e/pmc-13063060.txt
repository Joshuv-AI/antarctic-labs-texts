Correction to “Corrosion and Electrolysis Mechanisms of Elemental Cobalt in NaCl Solution”
Authors: Shengjiao Yang, Baochang Liu, Weiyi Yang, Hongxia Tan
Published: 2026

Correction 1 (for clearer expression):

The published version (Lines 544–545, in section 4.4.2. EIS for Corrosion):

(ii) the high-frequency side of the peak shifts toward nearly 105 Hz.

The version after correction:

(ii) the high-frequency side of the peak shifts toward a higher frequency, nearly 105 Hz.

Correction 2 (for grammatical error):

The published version (Lines 652–654, in section 4.4.6. EIS for Applying 0.5 V vs OCP):

This differs from the Nyquist plots without any applied voltage, where an inductive reactance semicircle appears after the capacitive semicircle when 0.5 V is applied.

The version after correction:

What is more, an inductive reactance semicircle appears after the capacitive semicircle when 0.5 V is applied, which differs from the Nyquist plots without any applied voltage (shown in Figure 6c–j).

Correction 3 (for wrong presentation on data):

The published version (Lines 670–672, in section 4.4.6. EIS for Applying 0.5 V vs OCP):

Notably, in the inductive semiloop response region (around 102 to 103 Hz), the magnitude for 4 M is marginally larger than that for 5 M.

The version after correction:

Notably, in the inductive semiloop response region (around 102 to 103 Hz), the magnitude for 5 M is marginally larger than that for 4 M.
