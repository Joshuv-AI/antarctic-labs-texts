Correction to “Effect of gamification with a support partner to increase physical activity in older adults at risk for Alzheimer's disease: The STEP 4Life randomized clinical trial”
Published: 2024

Greysen SR, Oon AL, Harkins K et al. Effect of gamificationwith a support partner to increase physical activity in older adults at risk for Alzheimer's disease: The STEP 4Life randomized clinical trial. Alzheimer's Dement. 2024;20(8):5450‐5459. https://doi.org/10.1002/alz.14058.

In paragraph 3 of the “Statistical Analysis” section, the equation listed was incorrect. It should have read: Stepsij=β0+β1Baselinestepcountij+β2Arminterventionij+β3MonthJanuaryij+β4MonthFebruaryij+⋯+β13MonthNovemberij+bi+εij where, Stepsij refers to Steps>0 recorded for participant i at day j, i=1,…,94,j=1,…,105,bi∼N(0,σb2) where bi refers to the participant random effect, which is the participant‐specific random intercept and εij∼N(0,σe2).

We apologize for this error.
