A lightweight multiscale attention network for 3D tumor segmentation in PET images
Authors: Lincen Jiang, Xinyuan Zheng, Wenpin Xu
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractAccurate segmentation of tumor PET images has a significance in disease monitoring and clinical treatment. However, the task of accurately segmenting PET images remains challenging due to insufficient access to spatial features of PET images. Therefore, we propose a multiscale attention-guided feature mechanism for 3D tumor segmentation (MA3DSeg) to achieve accurate segmentation of tumor PET images. Specifically, we first utilize a 3D-DX block to construct a lightweight encoder to minimise the redundant information generated across channel features and reduce the model complexity. Additionally, we employ multiscale attention-guided feature enhancement (MAFE) module for 3D networks to fuse channel features and spatial features to fully acquire spatial feature analysis of tumors. Furthermore, we propose explicit and implicit difference information in interaction boundary semantic (IBS) module to enhance the representation of segmentation edges. We conduct rigorous experiments on three publicly available datasets ECPC-IDS, Hecktor 2022 and AutoPET, which demonstrated superior performance than other competitive networks. MA3DSeg significantly improves Dice by 0.5 on Hecktor 2022 and RVD by 2.1 on ECPC-IDS compared with 3D UX-Net. The experimental results show that MA3DSeg achieves excellent tumor segmentation performance and generalization ability.Keywords: Tumor segmentation, Lightweight network, Multiscale attention-guided, PET imagesSubject terms: Health care, Medical research, Oncology

Introduction

Oncological diseases pose a major threat to human health, such as lymphomas, uterine fibroids and soft tissue tumors. Tumors show remarkable diversity in morphology, size and location, which adds to the complexity of clinical diagnosis. Therefore, automatic and accurate segmentation of tumors in Positron Emission Tomography (PET) and Computed Tomography (CT) images is an important guide for physicians clinical diagnosis1. PET imaging requires the injection of a fluorodeoxyglucose (18F-FDG) as a tracer, and depending on the metabolic characteristics of the tumor2. However, in addition to the tumor, the body’s metabolic organs also absorb large amounts of 18F-FDG, leading to confusion in differentiating between lesions.

The continuous development of deep learning has led to tremendous progress in PET image tumor segmentation. PET images are volumetric data types. It has been investigated how to segment volumetric data using 3D convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to expand the receptive field. Convolutional operations focus on local context,and lack attention to global context, leading to poor performance in processing texture-related targets.

To compensate for this, researchers have begun to explore the application of Transformer technology to the segmentation of medical images. In the field of 3D image segmentation, the UNETR3 and the Swin UNETR4 architectures demonstrate the capability of Transformer in extracting high-quality feature representations and exploiting global information. To improve segmentation accuracy by directly processing 3D along with patch and utilizing the Swin Transformer5 encoder. Nevertheless, Transformer has limitations in insufficient utilization of local features: the global attention mechanism of Transformer weakens the local spatial features.3D medical scanning images are composed of multiple slices, to mine the associations of neighboring slices of tumor images, some researchers have used Context Residual Networks (ConResNet)6 to introduce a new contextual residual decoder in the coder-decoder structure. Zhou et al.7 proposed feature pyramids to fuse multiscale contextual information by using dilation convolution. DeepMedic8 inputs multi-scale features from two channels to take into account both local and global information. nnUNet9 introduces optimize preprocessing, training, inference, and post-processing with minor modifications on the encoder-decoder.The traditional Transformers typically process each slice independently or adopt a simple stacking approach, failing to fully leverage the contextual information between slices.

With the in-depth study of medical image segmentation models utilizing the attention mechanism modules, spatial features are fully exploited, such as spatial attention10, channel attention11 and pixel-level attention12. These modules have been shown to play an effective role in medical image segmentation networks13,14. In the combined CNN(Convolutional Neural Network) and Transformer network, the high-resolution stage contains detailed information such as the texture and edges of the segmented target, while the low-resolution stage contains the attributes of the target15. Therefore, combining high-resolution and low-resolution feature maps can also better present the different size and morphological features of tumors16,17.

Based on the methods mentioned above, the limitations of 3D image segmentation include high computational cost and large memory consumption, which hinder the effective capture of the spatial relationships between slices. As a result, the feature representation of information within the slices is limited, making it difficult to capture the internal slice information of the 3D volume.

To address the above issues, we propose a novel network enhanced with multiscale attention-guided feature mechanism for 3D tumor segmentation (MA3DSeg) with fewer parameters and less consumption. MA3DSeg has been carefully designed to prioritize initial level spatial feature analysis before shifting the focus to deeper level channel-based features that ensures a comprehensive feature extraction process.

Inspired by 3D UX-Net18, we use depthwise separable convolution in the encoder, which significantly reduces the number of parameters, thus building lightweight network models. In decoder, multiscale attention-guided feature enhancement (MAFE) is introduced to capture a wide range of spatial information and augment the tumor features in the spatial dimension, and extract the fusion features from the context using global spatial and channel attention mechanisms. In additional, we propose the interaction boundary semantic(IBS) module that focuses on the edge segmentation region to improve the representation of edge details using explicit and implicit difference information. Related work

Turmor segmentation

Medical oncology imaging is PET and CT 3D images, so existing tumor segmentation methods focus on the challenges of segmentation arising from the imaging characteristics of the lesion region. Zou et al.19 proposed a two-branch tumor segmentation network using multipath downsampling to mitigate the effect of normal organ tissue features to improve segmentation accuracy. Huang et al.20 proposed ISA-Net to improve spatial attention, sampling multi-scale convolutional operations to obtain accurate positional information of tumors. Boundary segmentation of tumors has also made great progress, and existing methods mainly extract boundary features by enhancing the loss function21,22. However, how to overcome the continuous edge features of 3D slice images still needs to be urgently addressed to achieve more robust tumor segmentation.

Attentional mechanism

In pixel-level vision tasks, the use of attentional mechanisms can be more targeted in extracting features of the target, leading to an increase in the accuracy of the task in question. Chen et al.23 proposed multi-attention fusion, which activating attentional mechanisms using cross-resolution fusion and scale-awareness, thus enhancing the coding ability of the model. However, the current approach focuses on the weight share of features on spatial attention and channel attention and does not comprehensively consider the global interactions between voxels. MSRA-Net24 enhances spatial domain, frequency domain and channel attention through multiple receptive fields for localization of localized lesions. In addition, Attention U-Net25 proposes an attention gate that uses soft attention to realign the output features at each resolution of the encoder. CANet26 network employs multi-resolution input versions and multi-scale convolutional units to capture a large number of complementary features at different layers, which helps in learning local detail features of various scales.

PET segmentation

Due to the importance of accurate segmentation of PET images for clinical treatment, the field of tumor segmentation based on deep learning has progressed significantly. Most of the current results focus on the study of extracting 3D slice image features with multiple scales, as well as edge segmentation. Kang et al.27 constructed a feature extractor that extracts interpretable masks from the input, which helps to enhance feature extraction. Niu et al.28 introduced a simultaneous lung cancer lesion segmentation network, which accurately describes the edge region by dynamically correcting the weights and thresholds. Diao et al.29 constructed an evidence fusion network for PET-CT tumor segmentation based on uncertainty theory and achieved good segmentation results. Liu et al.30 proposed a method to introduce deep convolutional blocks into ConvNeXt, combining spatial and channel attention, to generate 3D medical image feature information containing multiple feature dimensions to improve segmentation performance.

Methods

Network architecture

The overview of MA3DSeg architecture is illustrated in Fig.1. In encoder, we consider taking advantage of the respective strengths of Swin Transformer and ConvNet to further complement each other. To this end, we develop a convolutional model named 3D-DX block which is inspired by ConvNeXt. And we have redesigned the hierarchical transformers based on 3D-DX block to further improve the usefulness of volumetric segmentation in 3D medical image datasets.The decoder goes through four upsampling stages, each of which seamlessly integrates three modules. MAFE module is designed to enhance the attention mechanism of the 3D network from spatial and channel dimensions. To achieve accurate segmentation of PET image edges, IBS is proposed to effective extraction of edge features based on diffusion theory to enhance lesion edge segmentation. Furthermore, Res-Block is used to assist IBS for tumor boundary segmentation.

The overview of MA3DSeg architecture.

3D-DX block

We propose 3D-DX block which further leverage convolution modules to enable the capabilities of hierarchical transformers. The 3D-DX block introduces several ConvNeXt priors, which complement the strength of Transformer to achieve lightweight and efficient 3D medical segmentation networks. It uses deep convolution with fewer model parameters to allow the network to learn how to capture a larger receptive field.

Specifically, 3D-DX block consists of double Depthwise Convolution, including Depthwise Separable Convolution (DSC) and Depthwise Convolutional Scaling (DCS), as shown in Fig.2. We acquire volumetric image features using depth-wise convolution with large kernel (LK) size to achieve a larger global receptive field. DSC employs LK with to extract initial feature for more global features, utilises LK-based deep convolution as a feature extraction backbone. It enhances the capture of spatial information through the large kernel convolution, which acts as the backbone of feature extraction to increase the receptive field, and enriches the features by extending the independent channels. DCS with kernel size to independent linear scaling for each channel feature. Enriching the feature representation by scaling and compressing each channel independently, the redundant information generated by the cross-channel context can be minimized. Finally, using the same DCS reduces the number of parameters required for convolutional computation and reduces the complexity of the model.

Multiscale attention-guided feature enhancement (MAFE)

The 3D image consists of multiple slices with insufficient feature correlation between the slices and does not adequately consider the critical interactions between spatial and channel features. Therefore, we propose MAFE module, which addresses the enhancement of multiscale attentional features. MAFE sequentially consists of Channel-aware Self-attention Module (CSM) and Spatial-aware Self-attention Module (SSM).

Channel-aware self-attention module (CSM)

The CSM module combines branches and channels by concatenating three different scales of channel branches from the input feature map, which is used to efficiently capture global context features and multiscale channel dependencies as shown in Fig.3. Then, the matrix consisting of the query vectors of all the attention points is subjected to a matrix multiplication operation to obtain the multiscale channel attention score matrix . Finally, the matrix and matrix are subjected to element-wise multiplication to enhance the multiscale channel features. Interaction boundary semantic (IBS) module

In PET images of tumor lesions, blurring of the edges between the lesion and normal body tissues as well as discontinuities in the lesion area are difficulties in segmentation. In Fig.4, the IBS module based on differential operators to achieve accurate classification of uncertain edge pixels by means of learnable boundary operators. The explicit and implicit differential information of IBS greatly improve the representation of boundary volumetric classification.

The illustration of the IBS module.

Due to the variability of distribution in 3D tumor PET images in x, y and z dimensions, traditional image-based edge operators are unable to obtain edge features. Therefore, we introduce the diffusion function in the edge features P of the precedent decoder to locate the uncertain boundary with semantic information. This is done by using the depth feature difference map as the interaction semantic guidance map, while the squared difference is used to fit the nonlinear features of the diffusion process ground. Data preprocessing

Due to the large number of 3D PET image slices, in order to eliminate invalid boundaries in the background region as well as to speed the model training, the slices were cropped to voxel size with the tumor in the centre and normalised preprocessing was carried out to improve the generalization ability and robustness of the model.

Effectiveness of 3D-DX Block

After adding the 3D-DX block to the baseline, the segmentation metrics were improved on all three datasets. Dice is improved by 0.7, 1.3 and 1.7 on ECPC-IDS , Hecktor 2022 and AutoPET, respectively. RVD, HD95 and ASSD scores are improved to 0.306, 13.589 and 3.114 on Hecktor 2022 dataset. Ihis suggests that the 3D-DX block plays a role in capturing a large receptive field, resulting in effective global contextual information that improves the accuracy of classification of segmented 3D voxels.

Therefore, the MAFE module strengthens the 3D images self attention mechanism and enhances the expressive ability between spatial and channel features, which helps to achieve beter tumor segmentation as shown in Fig.9.

Conclusion

In this paper, we present a lightweight MA3DSeg network for segmenting 3D tumors of PET images. We innovatively proposed 3D-DX block for the backbone network. Specifically, using the ConvNet block to adjust the inherent properties of the Swin Transformer to improve the volumetric segmentation performance with a smaller model capacity. MAFE is desgined to perform feature enhancement from the interaction between spatial and channel features to effectively suppress the interference of background noise. In addition, IBS module utilises explicit and implicit difference information are greatly improve the representation of inter-class boundaries and enhance boundary features. MA3DSeg is conducted sufficient experiments on three datasets, the results show that MA3DSeg achieves the optimal performance in 3D tumor segmentation compared to competing networks. MA3DSeg has great potential for clinical application in medical image segmentation.
