Author Correction: Everyday Uses of Music Listening and Music Technologies by Caregivers and People With Dementia: Survey and Focus Group Study
Authors: Dianna Vidas, Romina Carrasco, Ryan M Kelly, Jenny Waycott, Jeanette Tamplin, Kate McMahon, Libby M Flynn, Phoebe A Stretton-Smith, Tanara Vieira Sousa, Felicity A Baker
Published: 2024

In “Everyday Uses of Music Listening and Music Technologies by Caregivers and People With Dementia: Survey and Focus Group Study” (J Med Internet Res 2024;26:e54186) the authors noted one error.

The ninth author's name was listed as:

Tanara Viera Sousa

This has been changed to the following:

Tanara Vieira Sousa

The correction will appear in the online version of the paper on the JMIR Publications website on September 9, 2024, together with the publication of this correction notice. Because this was made after submission to PubMed, PubMed Central, and other full-text repositories, the corrected article has also been resubmitted to those repositories.
