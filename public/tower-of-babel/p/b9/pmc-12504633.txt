Author Correction: Multiplex bead assays enable integrated serological surveillance and reveal cross-pathogen vulnerabilities in Zambezia Province, Mozambique
Authors: Andrea C Carcelen, Celso Monjane, Sophie Bérubé, Saki Takahashi, Thebora Sultane, Imelda Chelene, Gretchen Cooley, E Brook Goodhew, Catriona Patterson, Kevin Tetteh, Manuel Mutambe, Melissa M Higdon, George Mwinnyaa, Gilberto Nhapure, Pedro Duce, Diana L Martin, Christopher Drakeley, William J Moss, Ivalda Macicame
Published: 2025

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-025-62305-9, published online 26 August 2025

In the version of the article initially published, in the Fig. 4 colour bar header and in the first sentence of the caption, the word “log” was missing from the text now reading “Adjusted log OR” and “Adjusted log odds ratios,” respectively. The figure and caption are updated in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
