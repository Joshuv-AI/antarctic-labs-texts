Incorrect Author group list:

Bonanno M1, Maggio MG1, Quartarone A1, De Nunzio AM2 and Calabrò RS1*

Correct Author group list:

Bonanno Mirjam1, Maggio Maria Grazia1, Quartarone Angelo1, De Nunzio Alessandro Marco2 and Calabrò Rocco Salvatore1*

Incorrect caption of Figure 4 without legends:

ROBINS-I assessment of the eleven non-RCTs studies

Key gait, balance, metabolic, and muscle responses provided by anti-gravity treadmill training

Legend: Supporting evidence: ‘Gait and balance functions’ [17–20, 23–26, 28, 30, 32]; ‘Metabolic responses’ [21, 24, 29]; ‘Muscle activation responses’ [17, 22, 32].

The original article has been corrected.
