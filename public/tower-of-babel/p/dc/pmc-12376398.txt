Correction: A new antifungal compound from Streptomyces diastatochromogenes
Authors: Ming-Yun Wang, Ke-Qi Ye, Dan-Ting Li, Chun-Li Guo, Jia-Cheng Ge, Xiao-Ping Yu, Xu-Ping Shentu
Published: 2025

Correction: BMC Biotechnology (2025) 25:82

10.1186/s12896-025-01012-1.

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors identified an error in Fig. 1. The compound labels “T1, T2, E2, E3” were inadvertently retained from an earlier draft. These should be revised to “1, 2, 4, 5” to align with the final nomenclature used throughout the text.

The original article has been corrected.
