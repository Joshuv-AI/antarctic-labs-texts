Selected Papers from 18th ESS Meeting☆
Authors: Tom Van Gerven, Jean-Yves Hihn
Published: 2025

This Special Issue reports on the 18th Meeting of the European Society of Sonochemistry (ESS), held from 27 till 31 May 2024, in Leuven, Belgium. Contributions have been selected from the presenters during the conference who reported on the latest advances in research, recent implementations in industry, and the current offer from technology providers. The meeting covered a wide span ranging from the fundamental aspects of acoustic and hydrodynamic cavitation [[1], [2], [3], [4], [5], [6]], over the design, engineering and scale up of ultrasonic devices, both from an experimental and modeling perspective [[7], [8], [9]], to applications in chemical synthesis, materials and nanoparticles production, surface treatment and cleaning, and environmental remediation [[10], [11], [12], [13], [14], [15], [16]].

It is worth mentioning that Quinten Goris [9] was one of the three Best Oral Presentation Awardees in the ESS18 Conference, while Vincenzo Fabbrizio [16] won the Best Poster Presentation Award. We also want to extend our thanks to the sponsors (Agfa Labs, REUS, FWO-Research Foundation Flanders) of the conference, without whom it would not have been possible to organize it.

Biographies

Tom VanGerven is Full Professor in the Department of Chemical Engineering at KU Leuven (Belgium). His research domain is centred on process intensification, where novel reactor and separation processes are designed using alternative energy sources (e.g., ultrasound) and modulated flow, in line with the current trend of electrification of chemical industry. In the research group, process intensification is applied to multiphase processes (liquid-liquid, solid-liquid and gas-solid-liquid) for chemical synthesis (e.g., organic synthesis, crystallisation and mineral carbonation) and recycling (e.g., leaching and extraction). He has co-authored 255 publications leading to an h-index of 54 (WoS data, February 2025). Currently he is the Chairman of the European Society of Sonochemistry (https://europeansocietyofsonochemistry.eu/), which is the hosting society of the ESS18 Conference.

Jean-YvesHihn is Full Professor in the University Marie et Louis Pasteur Besançon (France). His main lines of research concern electrochemistry, plating and anodic reactions, with an emphasis on processes control (pulsed currents, ultrasound). He leads the group “Surface Reactivity and Sonochemistry” involved in large-scale industrial projects in the fields of aeronautic, automotive, energy, electronics or watchmaking. He has co-authored about 150 publications, 8 patents, 7 books chapters, and has given more than 250 communications. He’s member of the Swiss and French Surfaces Treatment societies, the International Society of Electrochemistry (ISE) and the Electrochemical Society (ECS). He is the former president of the European Society of Sonochemistry (2018-2024) and vice-chair of the RT CNRS Cavitation. He is a laureate of an academic chair at the IRT M2P (2023-2027), NASF research award grant recipient (2024-2028) and invited Professor at the university Québec à Trois-Rivières (UQTR, Canada).
