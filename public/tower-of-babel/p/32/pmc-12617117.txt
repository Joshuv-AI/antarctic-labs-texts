PA20115 Strengthening Primary Health Care in Europe: A Delphi Study on Accessibility, Equity and Resilience
Authors: J Gómez Bravo, I Gefaell Larrondo, S Ares-Blanco, M Guisado-Clavero, J J Mira, C Pérez Esteve, S Bensemmane, P Astier-Peña
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractIntroductionPrimary Health Care (PHC) is essential for equitable and resilient health systems, yet faces barriers in implementation, resource allocation, and political prioritization. This study aimed to build a strategic roadmap by identifying core values, priorities, and strategies through expert consensus.MethodsA two-round Delphi study engaged 210 stakeholders from 35 countries, including PHC professionals, policymakers, and public health experts. Participants assessed the importance, feasibility, and policy prioritization of PHC values. Quantitative data were analyzed descriptively.ResultsResponse rates were 81.4% (171/210) in round one and 73.5% (97/132) in round two. Most respondents (89%) had a medical background. Consensus (>80%) on PHC values was achieved in round one.Recommendations includedinvesting in PHC workforce development in underserved areas; strengthening information systems and telehealth integration; improving governance models to support multidisciplinary and citizen-centered care; and adapting services for chronic care, end-of-life support, and standardized assessments. In round two, feasibility ratings ranged from 61–92%. Policy prioritization ratings were lower, at 22–51%.ConclusionsStakeholders strongly endorsed PHC values but highlighted systemic gaps between expert recommendations and political prioritization. Efforts should focus on aligning health policy with PHC values, increasing funding, strengthening chronic care, and translating consensus into actionable, sustainable strategies.Key Messages• Strong consensus exists on accessibility, equity, and coordinated care in PHC, but feasibility was rated low.• Adequate PHC funding and prioritization of chronic care remain insufficient, highlighting the need to better translate values into policy and practice.Topichealth policy, policymaking, primary health care
