In the original publication of this article [1], the affiliation details for authors “Qian Su, Yongqi Yu, He Chen, Yufeng Gao and Jiabin Li” were incorrectly given as “Anhui Medical University” but should have been “The First Affiliated Hospital of Anhui Medical University”. The corresponding affiliations for the authors have been updated in the author group.
