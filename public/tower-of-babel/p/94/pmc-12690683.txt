NODG-10 MULTIDISCIPLINARY MEETING IN NEURO-ONCOLOGY IN IVORY COAST : IMPACT ON THE MANAGEMENT OF CNS TUMORS
Authors: Drogba Landry, Karidioula Sie Jacques, Djonde Grace, Konan Landry, Yao Abo Wilfried
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractIntroductionThe management of central nervous system (CNS) tumors requires a multidisciplinary approach integrating neurosurgeons, oncologists, neuro-oncologists, radiotherapists, radiologists and pathologists. In Ivory Coast, a neuro-oncology RCP was established in 2019 to strengthen collaboration between specialists and improve the quality of therapeutic decisions. Few studies exist on the impact of these meetings in the African context.Objectiveevaluate the impact of neuro-oncology CPR on the management of CNS tumors, five years after its implementation.MethodologyA descriptive study was carried out based on the minutes of the 2024 meetings. A questionnaire was sent to regular participating doctors to collect their perceptions on the usefulness, limits and prospects of CPR.ResultsMeetings were held every two weeks by videoconference. In the absence of a quorum, discussions were conducted in the WhatsApp group. On average, 20 doctors attended each meeting. The session was administered on a rotating basis. In 2024, 41 cases were discussed (34 adults and 7 children). In 75% of cases, the staff led to a review of the diagnosis or therapeutic strategy. Participants highlighted an improvement in the quality of decisions and better interprofessional coordination. Among the limitations identified : the absence of a pediatric neutopathologist, compensated for by the contribution of an external expert.ConclusionNeuro-onco CPR in Ivory Coast optimizes the quality of therapeutic decisions and structures collaboration around CNS tumors. Its sustainability appears essential.
