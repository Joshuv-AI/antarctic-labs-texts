A binocular camera calibration method based on circle detection
Authors: Chengli Zhao, Chenyang Fan, Zhangyan Zhao
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Camera calibration is a crucial step in binocular measurement, and the accuracy of camera calibration largely determines the measurement accuracy of binocular vision. However, the calibration accuracy of existing calibration methods is difficult to satisfy the requirements of variable calibration environments and engineering applications. Therefore, based on the principle of Zhang's calibration method, a calibration method is proposed by combining bundle adjustment and diagonal constraints of the calibration target. Firstly, the improved Canny edge extraction algorithm is used to obtain the sub-pixel center of mass of ellipse (CME). Then, the Zhang's calibration method is used to obtain the initial values of the calibration parameters. The camera calibration parameters are optimized by using the bundle adjustment. Finally, diagonal constraints are used to further optimize the extrinsic camera position parameters. The experimental results show that compared to the other excellent methods, the proposed method can significantly improve calibration accuracy and has certain engineering application value.

Introduction

Visual measurement has been widely used in many fields due to its advantages of non-contact, high precision, fast speed, and simple operation [1], [2]. As a key part of visual measurement, camera calibration can significantly affect the measurement accuracy, so it has been studied by more scholars [3], [4]. Generally, current camera calibration methods calculate camera parameters by solving the projection relationship between space points and corresponding image points. Among these technologies, the self-calibration method [5] relies heavily on feature points in the environment, and the accuracy of these feature points is usually low, resulting in low measurement accuracy. In order to improve the reliability and accuracy of the calibration process, manual calibration targets are usually used in the calibration [6]. These calibration targets are usually manufactured with high precision and the three-dimensional information of their feature points is known, resulting in higher calibration accuracy. These feature points are usually distributed in the form of straight lines, planes or three-dimensional spaces. Therefore, according to the location distribution of feature points, manual calibration objects can be divided into three categories: 1D objects [7], 2D objects [8], and 3D objects [9].

The calibration method based on one-dimensional targets only requires a small number of feature points, but requires the addition of strict motion constraints [10], and the implementation conditions are high, so it is difficult to achieve high-precision calibration. The manufacturing process and cost of 3D objects are relatively high, so they are not widely used. Two-dimensional targets are widely used in camera calibration due to their flexibility. Zhang's calibration method [4] is based on a checkerboard pattern. The calibration process is simple, the cost of the calibration target is low, and the calibration process is easy to implement, which makes it one of the most influential camera calibration methods in practical applications.

To complete the three-dimensional measurement of the object to be measured, a binocular stereo vision system is usually required. The binocular camera measurement system can realize three-dimensional measurement. Binocular camera measurement system is widely used in advanced manufacturing [11], robotics [12], biomedicine [13], visual SLAM [14] and other fields. The parameters that need to be calibrated for the binocular camera measurement system are divided into intrinsic parameters and extrinsic parameters [15], [16].

Using Zhang's [4] method, the binocular camera measurement system based on plane calibration target can be easily constructed. The intrinsic parameters and distortion parameters of the camera are determined by the camera and lens. These parameters usually do not change and are generally not optimized [17], [18]. Taken image interference factor into consideration, Liu et al. [19] proposed a method to obtain the intrinsic parameters. In actual measurements, extrinsic parameters will change due to factors such as vibration, temperature, assembly, and lighting. Therefore, in order to obtain higher-precision measurement results, the extrinsic parameters of the cameras need to be optimized. The objective function is usually constructed based on the reprojection error, and then iterative calculation is used for the optimization of the extrinsic parameters. Similarly, Wang [20] and Yang [21] calculated the extrinsic parameters on the basis of obtaining the intrinsic parameters, they also use the objective function to minimize the reprojection error. Li [22] used a bundle adjustment method to minimize the reprojection error and optimized all parameters. A polarizer was used with the camera in literature [23], they also obtained the camera's parameters based on minimizing the reprojection error.

The general calibration method usually uses the checkerboard pattern or the circle pattern. In the circle pattern, the extracted feature point is the center of the circle. In the checkerboard pattern, the extracted feature point is the intersection between lines, the width of the line will cause errors in the extraction of the intersection. Therefore, among two-dimensional calibration target, the accuracy of solid circle calibration target is usually higher than that of other types of calibration target [24]. Shan et al. [25] proposed a stereo vision system calibration method based on solid circle targets, which proved the effectiveness of using solid circles for calibration, the added diagonal constraints also improve the measurement accuracy in their method. Ge [26] also developed a general calibration method for ring camera arrays, this method uses a rotating platform to establish the relationship between multiple cameras, thereby completing the unification of the coordinate system and quickly calculating the global coordinates. Baohua [27] proposed a dual-objective determination method based on single diagonal constraints, which incorporated multiple distortion parameters into the optimization algorithm.

Ellipse eccentricity generally cause error. In order to solve this problem, Yang [28] studied circle projection and proposes a corresponding calibration method. Zhang [29] established a mathematical model for error analysis of circle target feature extraction, analyzed the factors affecting ellipse eccentricity, and evaluated the impact of center extraction error on the accuracy of camera parameter calculation. Shiu [30] first proposed a method for reconstructing a space circle based on a perspective projection model. The ellipse on the image is projected into an elliptical cone, and the intersection of the plane and the elliptical cone is a circle. In the solution process, when the radius is determined, the ellipse equation has two sets of solutions, so the solution position of a single circle is uncertain. Zheng [31] et al. gave a geometric explanation for this uncertainty. Wang et al. [32] analyzed the pose parameters of a single circular feature. The measurement accuracy of the camera pose depends not only on the accuracy of feature extraction, but also on the position of the feature relative to the camera. They proposed an error analysis method based on a geometric model, which can also be extended to the error analysis of other coplanar features. Solid circle calibration targets have been shown to facilitate high-precision camera calibration. The gradual development of the field of camera calibration will further promote the application of visual measurement and related fields.

On the basis of previous research by scholars, this paper proposed binocular camera calibration method based on circle detection, which includes elliptical edge extraction, CME calculation, and optimization of intrinsic and extrinsic parameters. There are three main highlights in this paper. An CME extraction method based on improved Canny is proposed. A calibration optimization method that combines Zhang's calibration method and bundle adjustment is proposed. An extrinsic parameter optimization method based on two diagonal constraints is proposed.

The section 2 introduces CME sub-pixel calculation method based on the Canny edge detection, the mathematical model of binocular camera calibration, and the binocular camera calibration method based on diagonal constraints. The section 3 introduces comparative experiments. The section 4 summarizes the entire paper.

Methods

The extraction of CME

The extraction of CME includes two main steps introduced in section 2.1.1 and section 2.1.2. The first step is to use Canny to accurately extract the area where each ellipse is located on the image. The second step is to determine CME based on the extracted area.

Improved Canny edge detection

As shown in the Fig. 1, pixels are discrete, and pixel values usually do not vary continuously. Mathematically, the outline of an ellipse is a smooth curve, but from an image perspective, the outline of an ellipse is a polyline with unclear edges. In order to obtain a better extraction effect of CME, a series of image processing is required.Figure 1Ellipse detection.Figure 1

Ellipse detection.

When there is obvious edge information in the image, the usual neighborhood operations will inevitably cause the pixel values of the edge points in the image to change, making the edges of the image blurry. The Gaussian filter in the Canny detection [33] only considers the spatial position relationship between pixels, which can easily cause edge blur, thereby causing the loss of edge information. Therefore, it is difficult for the Gaussian filter method to smooth noise and protect edge information. In order to improve results, a bilateral filtering [34] is used. The selection of the convolution weights of the bilateral filter template not only depends on the pixel spatial position relationship of ordinary low-pass filtering, but also considers the similarity between pixels. The expression of bilateral filtering is as follows.(1)ID(i,j)=∑k,lI(k,l)ω(i,j,k,l)∑k,lω(i,j,k,l)(2)ωd(i,j,k,l)=exp⁡(−(i−k)2+(j−l)22σd2)(3)ωr(i,j,k,l)=exp⁡(−(i−k)2+(j−l)22σd2)

In the equation (1)-(3), ID represents the output pixel value. I(k,l) represents the value of each pixel in the window. ωd is the spatial domain Gaussian function. And ωr is the inter-pixel brightness Gaussian function.

Canny is a very effective edge detection algorithm. The implementation of this algorithm mainly has the following four steps:1)Use bilateral filter to filter the original image.2)The first-order partial derivatives in the horizontal and vertical directions are used to calculate the gradient amplitude G(x,y) and gradient direction θ(x,y) of the image.(4)G=Gx2+Gy2(5)θ=arctan⁡(Gy/Gx)3)Perform non-maximum suppression operation on the gradient image to refine the edges.4)Use high and low dual thresholds to connect the final edge. The choice of high and low thresholds has a crucial impact on the final edge connection. If the gradient value of a certain pixel is greater than the high threshold, it is directly regarded as a strong edge point. If the gradient value of the pixel is less than the low threshold, set it to 0 directly. If the gradient value of the pixel is between the high and low thresholds, then look for whether there are strong edge points in the eight neighborhoods of the pixel. If there is a strong edge point, the pixel is retained, otherwise it is excluded.

Binocular camera calibration based on diagonal constraints

The intrinsic parameters are Al and Ar, and the distortion coefficient are kl1, kl2, kr1 and kr2. The extrinsic parameters can also be obtained in this step. Conclusion

Based on the principle of Zhang's calibration method, combined with the bundle adjustment and the diagonal constraints of the calibration target, a calibration method based on the solid circle calibration target is proposed. First, the improved Canny edge extraction operator is used to preprocess the image, obtain the outline of the ellipse on the image, and calculate the sub-pixel CME within the outline range. The CME obtained by image processing is regarded as the projection of the center of the space circle on the image, and the coordinates of CME are substituted into Zhang's calibration method to obtain the initial values of the calibration parameters. The initial values of camera parameters obtained by Zhang's calibration method are substituted into the bundle adjustment algorithm for optimization, and finally the diagonal constraints are used to further optimize the extrinsic parameters.

The proposed method's calibration process is more complex and may take slightly longer than other methods, but it shows clear advantages in terms of accuracy and robustness. Based on the measurement results for point spacing and diagonals, the proposed approach outperforms the methods analyzed in this research paper.

Taken together, the proposed calibration method not only has the effect of significantly improving the calibration accuracy, but also can satisfy the needs of engineering applications. In future work, the accuracy of image extraction will be further improved and compared with more excellent calibration methods.
