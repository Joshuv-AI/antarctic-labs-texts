Editorial Expression of Concern: Arch Gynecol Obstet (2012) 286:109–114 10.1007/s00404-012-2242-5

The Editor-in-Chief is issuing an Editorial Expression of Concern for this article. Concerns have been raised regarding data reported in this article, specifically that the p values reported in Table 1 appear to be incorrect. The authors have not provided a response to the concerns raised or supplied their raw data. Readers are therefore advised to interpret the results and conclusions with caution.

Authors Allam M. Abdelmonem, Salah M. Rasheed have not responded to correspondence from the Publisher about this Expression of Concern. The Publisher could not obtain a current email address for author Abeer Sh Mohamed.
