Correction to: Near-universal prevalence of central adiposity in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction: the PARAGON-HF trial
Published: 2025

This is a correction to: Alexander Peikert, Muthiah Vaduganathan, Brian L Claggett, Ian J Kulac, Sheldon Litwin, Michael Zile, Akshay S Desai, Pardeep S Jhund, Jawad H Butt, Carolyn S P Lam, Felipe Martinez, Dirk J Van Veldhuisen, Faiez Zannad, Jean Rouleau, Martin Lefkowitz, John J V McMurray, Scott D Solomon, Milton Packer, Near-universal prevalence of central adiposity in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction: the PARAGON-HF trial, European Heart Journal, Volume 46, Issue 25, 1 July 2025, Pages 2372–2390, https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehaf057

In the originally published version, in the graphical abstract, the x-axis labelling for Body Mass Index (BMI) was inadvertently used for both the BMI spline plots and the plots representing waist-to-height ratio (WHtR). The correct x-axis labels for WHtR should read “0.4, 0.5, 0.6, 0.7, 0.8, 0.9”.

The image has been corrected in the online version of the article.
