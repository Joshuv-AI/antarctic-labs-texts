Targeting the LINC00265/miR-485-5p axis for diagnosis and therapeutic intervention in breast cancer
Authors: Qiang Jiang, Suya Han, Ru Chang, Jianzhen Pan, Bingjie Zhang
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundThe incidence of breast cancer ranks first among female tumors. Early diagnosis and treatment of breast cancer can effectively alleviate the suffering of patients, and attention to breast cancer diagnostic markers may provide a new theoretical basis for biological therapy.PurposeThe objective of this study was to investigate the diagnostic value of LINC00265 in breast cancer and to elucidate its pathological mechanisms.MethodsThere were 88 breast cancer patients as the experimental subjects and 72 healthy individuals as the control group. LINC00265 and miR-485-5p levels in serum and cell supernatants were determined by RT-qPCR analysis. Cell function was evaluated via CCK-8 and Transwell assays. The sponge function of LINC00265 on miR-485-5p was confirmed by conducting a dual-luciferase activity assay.ResultsLINC00265 was highly expressed, and miR-485-5p was downregulated in breast cancer. LINC00265 has diagnostic value in breast cancer and is closely related to lymphatic metastasis and TNM stage in patients. Silencing LINC00265 suppressed cell function, while knockdown of miR-485-5p restored this regulation.ConclusionSignificantly upregulated LINC00265 is a biomarker for the diagnosis of breast cancer, and LINC00265 binds to miR-485-5p to mediate tumor progression.Keywords: LINC00265, miR-485-5p, Breast cancer, Diagnosis

Introduction

Under the influence of various carcinogenic factors, the uncontrolled proliferation of mammary epithelial cells in women can easily lead to the occurrence of breast cancer [1]. Breast cancer has the highest incidence among female tumors, and its mortality rate has remained persistently high in China in recent years [2]. The exact cause of breast cancer is not clear, but the accumulation of high-risk factors, such as abnormal hormone secretion and genetics, increases the risk of the disease [3]. The symptoms of most breast cancer patients are not obvious in the early stage, and it is easy to be ignored and missed the best diagnosis time. Based on the comprehensive physical condition of the patient, conventional treatment methods such as drug therapy and surgical treatment are administered. Generally, the prognosis of patients with breast cancer is closely related to the developmental stage of the disease, the earlier the detection and diagnosis of the tumor, the greater the chance of survival [4, 5]. Therefore, new diagnostic strategies need to be developed to assist in the treatment of breast cancer, with improved treatment options that can reduce patient mortality.

Non-coding RNAs, lack protein-coding functions and include long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs) and microRNAs (miRNAs), which are widely involved in various biological processes [6]. Several studies have proposed that lncRNAs are predictive of cancer risk and clinical treatment [7, 8]. Moreover, lncRNAs combined with miRNAs have also been confirmed to regulate cancer progression [9]. For instance, lncRNA NR2F1-AS1 targets miRNA-338-3p to accelerate the progression of breast cancer [10]. Chen et al. promoted breast cancer development through the MCM3AP-AS1/miR-28-5p/CENPF axis [11]. LncRNA EILA was also thought to regulate the drug resistance of breast cancer by mediating cyclin E1 protein stability and CDK4/6 inhibitors [12]. Notably, lncRNAs, including LINC00265, are critical for colorectal cancer cell cycle and tumor pathways [13]. Additionally, LINC00265 was also involved in the treatment of bladder cancer [14], colorectal cancer [15], and gliomas [16], but in previous studies on breast cancer, no attention was given to the abnormal regulation of LINC00265.

This study revealed the role of LINC00265 as a non-invasive diagnostic indicator of breast cancer and its pathological mechanisms by conducting a comprehensive analysis of serum biomarkers and cellular functions, positioning LINC00265 at a biomarker of cancer diagnostics and targeted therapy development.

Materials and methods

Sample collection

Blood samples were collected from 88 breast cancer patients treated at The First Affiliated Hospital of Harbin Medical University from January 2023 to February 2024, and 72 healthy individuals who came to the hospital were randomly selected as controls. The obtained blood was placed in a centrifuge tube, centrifuged at 2000 rpm for 10 min to separate the serum, and stored at -80 °C refrigerator (DW-86L486; Haier, Qingdao, China). Patient selection criteria: patients diagnosed with breast cancer had not received prior radiotherapy or immunotherapy; had no significant genetic or major disease other than breast cancer. Exclusion criteria: patients with other serious diseases or cancers; patients who had received other experimental treatments; patients with unstable conditions that were not suitable for the study.

All included participants provided signed informed consent to participate in the study voluntarily, and the process was conducted according to ethical and legal standards under the supervision of the hospital ethics committee (Approval Number: 202207).

RT-qPCR

The total RNA in the serum samples and cell supernatants was extracted using a Trizol kit (15596026; Invitrogen, Carlsbad, CA, USA), and the value of OD260/OD280 was recorded using a microplate reader (Elx808; BioTek, VT, USA) to evaluate the purity of the RNA. cDNA was synthesized using an RNA Quantitative Reverse Transcription Kit (#218161; Qiagen, Germany; reverse transcription LINC00265) and TapMan microRNA reverse transcription kit (#4366597; Applied Biosystems, USA; reverse transcription miR-485-5p). The obtained cDNA was used as a template and added to the SYBR® qPCR Mix Kit (QPS-201; Toyobo, Osaka, Japan) configuration system, and RT-qPCR experiments were conducted in the LightCycler 480 system (Roche, Switzerland). LINC00265 and miR-485-5p expression were then normalized with GAPDH and U6 as endogenous control genes.

Cell acquisition and culture

The T47D, MCF-7, BT549 and MDA-MB-231 breast cancer cells selected in this study were purchased from Shanghai Cell Bank, as was the control cell line MCF-10A. Dulbecco’s modified Eagle’s medium (DMEM, 11966025, with 10% FBS; Gibco, USA) was used to incubate the cells at 37 °C.

Cell transfection

The cells were cultured to the logarithmic stage for transfection, and 3 × 105 cells were seeded in each well of a 6-well plate and incubated at 37 °C. Combined with the Lipofectamine 2000 (11668019; Invitrogen, Carlsbad, CA, USA) reagent and carrier, si-NC, ov-NC, ov- LINC00265, and si-LINC00265 were sequentially transfected into BT549 and MDA-MB-231 cells. The expression of LINC00265 was detected to evaluate the efficiency of cell transfection.

In addition, MDA-MB-231 cells were transfected with si-LINC00265 and the miR-485-5p inhibitor, ov-LINC00265 and miR-485-5p mimic for recovery assays.

CCK-8 proliferation assay

The cells were inoculated in 96-well plates, and CCK-8 reagent (#CK04-11; Dojindo, Tokyo, Japan) was added at a specified time. The absorbance was measured at 450 nm to indicate the cell proliferation ability.

Transwell assay

The cells were transferred to the upper part of the Transwell chamber, including cells at a density of 1 × 105 cells/well and DMEM medium (without FBS). The lower part of the Transwell chamber was partially filled with FBS-containing DMEM medium for the cell migration experiments. The invasion experiment involved coating with Matrigel (#354230; BD, San Jose, CA, USA), and the other steps were identical to those described above. After 24 h of incubation in an incubator at 37℃ with 5% CO2, the cells transferred to the lower chamber were washed with PBS, and 5 sites were randomly selected for counting.

Dual-luciferase reporter assay

The WT-LINC00265 and MUT-LINC00265 reporter plasmids were constructed using vectors, and then co-transfected with mimic NC, inhibitor NC, miR-485-5p mimic, or miR-485-5p inhibitor into MDA-MB-231 cells. The relative luciferase activity of the cells was determined by a dual-luciferase reporter gene detection kit (RG088S; Beyotime, Shanghai, China).

Statistical analysis

GraphPad Prism 7.0 and SPSS 26.0 software programs were used to process the experimental data. All in vitro assays were performed independently in triplicate, and each sample was tested in three technical replicates. Count data were expressed as n. Differences between the groups were analyzed by Student’s t-test and one-way ANOVA followed by Tukey’s post hoc test. The Chi-square (χ²) test was evaluated to assess the relationship between LINC00265 expression and clinical parameters. The diagnostic potential of LINC00265 dysregulation in breast cancer was evaluated ROC curve analysis. All results were considered to be statistically significant at P < 0.05.

Results

Expression and correlation of LINC00265 in breast cancer

LINC00265 in serum samples of patients with breast cancer was examined (Fig. 1A), indicating that LINC00265 was upregulated in tumor serum. Additionally, LINC00265 was elevated expression in T47D, MCF-7, BT549, and MDA-MB-231 cells compared to that in the mammary epithelial cell line MCF-10A (Fig. 1B). BT549 and MDA-MB-231 cells with relatively high expression were used for subsequent cell experiments.

Diagnostic ability of LINC00265 in breast cancer

To evaluate whether LINC00265 is related to the diagnosis of breast cancer, the diagnostic value of serum LINC00265 was analyzed via ROC curve. The AUC value was 0.840, and the sensitivity and specificity of the patients were 80.68% and 70.83%, suggesting that LINC00265 expression was very convincing in distinguishing breast cancer patients from healthy controls (Fig. 1C).

Function of abnormal expression of LINC00265 in breast cancer cells

Transfection of si-LINC00265 successfully downregulated the content of LINC00265 and ov-LINC00265 upregulated the intracellular level of LINC00265 (Fig. 2A). LINC00265 knockdown affected the growth ability of the BT549 cells, as did the viability of the MDA-MB-231 cells (Fig. 2B and C). Additionally, a low level of LINC00265 inhibited the number of migration (Fig. 2D) and invasion (Fig. 2E) of breast cancer cells, thus alleviating tumor progression. Conversely, upregulation of LINC00265 enhanced the biological functions of BT549 and MDA-MB-231 cells (Fig. 2B and E).

Specific binding of LINC00265 and miR-485-5p

The ENCORI website predicted the relationship between WT-LINC00265 and miR-485-5p, with complementary sites shown in Fig. 3A. To verify the interaction between LINC00265 and miR-485-5p, the luciferase reporter vectors WT-LINC00265 and MUT-LINC00265 were constructed. The miR-485-5p mimic inhibited WT-LINC00265 luciferase activity, while the miR-485-5p inhibitor enhanced WT-LINC00265 luciferase activity (Fig. 3B) but hardly affected the luciferase activity of MUT-LINC00265. The results of the RT-qPCR analysis demonstrated that miR-485-5p was downregulated in the serum (Fig. 3C) and cells (Fig. 3D) of breast cancer. As stated above, LINC00265 targeted and negatively mediated the miR-485-5p expression.

Effect of the miR-485-5p dysregulation on cells

Following LINC00265 knockdown via siRNA transfection, we observed the upregulation of miR-485-5p in cells, and this effect was reversed when the cells were co-transfected with si-LINC00265 and the miR-485-5p inhibitor (Fig. 4A). Silencing LINC00265 suppressed the growth and activity of breast cancer cells, while the mediation of miR-485-5p inhibitor reversed cell proliferation (Fig. 4B), migration (Fig. 4C) and invasion (Fig. 4D).

Discussion

In medical research, it has been widely used to regulate the progression of diseases by expression levels of various factors, among which lncRNAs and miRNAs are typical representatives involved in a variety of biological processes [17]. For instance, elevated CIAPIN1 and DBNDD1 expression in invasive breast cancer correlates with both the infiltration landscape of diverse immune cells and an adverse patient prognosis [18, 19]. Additionally, ubiquitin modification critically influences breast cancer treatment efficacy, metabolic reprogramming, and patient prognosis in the latest research [20]. Meanwhile, dysregulation of lncRNAs can lead to uncontrolled gene expression and contribute to the malignant development of human diseases. For example, upregulated lncRNA SBF2-AS1 plays a carcinogenic role in bladder cancer and might be a new therapeutic target [21]. Wang et al. revealed that the abnormally expressed lncRNA FTO-IT1 affects the glycolytic process of hepatocellular carcinoma, thereby affecting the survival of patients [22]. Significantly overexpressed lncRNA PTPRG-AS1 as a therapeutic target of breast cancer plays a tumor suppressor role by regulating miR-4659a-3p/QPCT axis [23]. Although the evidence regarding the effectiveness of the use of lncRNAs to predict breast cancer in clinical practice is not strong, the application of lncRNAs and the exploration of targeted therapy have brought new hope to patients.

Located on human chromosome 7p14.1, LINC00265 is a newly discovered lncRNA associated with cancer. According to existing literature, LINC00265 accelerated the progression of gastric cancer by regulating the miR-144-3p/CBX4 axis [24]. Similarly, LINC00265 has been shown to be carcinogenic in the growth of osteosarcoma [25]. Aligning with existing literature, this study demonstrated that dysregulated LINC00265 expression in breast cancer. Quantitative analysis revealed marked overexpression of LINC00265 in patient serum and cell samples, and its expression positively correlated with lymphatic metastasis progression and advanced TNM stages. Knockdown of LINC00265 inhibited the growth of breast cancer cells and the quantitative regulation of cell migration and invasion, while upregulation of LINC00265 activates the biological functions of these cells. In the study by Zhao and colleagues, LINC00265 levels were elevated in acute lymphoblastic leukemia, and silencing LINC00265 slowed cell progression [26]. Ge et al. reported that LINC00265 increased in colorectal cancer, which was related to lymph node metastasis and pathological progression, whereas downregulation of LINC00265 promoted cell apoptosis [27]. In addition, LINC00265 was also identified as a signaling molecule for the diagnosis of breast cancer by ROC curve analysis.

As functionally rich short regulatory non-coding RNAs, miRNAs regulate essential biological processes through specific binding to spongy lncRNAs [28]. Besides confirming that miR-28-5p [29] and miR-101-3p [30] are targets of LINC00265 in different diseases, we found that LINC00265 combined with miR-485-5p may mediate the occurrence of breast cancer. MiR-485-5p is located on chromosome 14q32.31, which targeting MUC1 has been confirmed to provide inhibitory function for breast cancer [31]. Reports suggest that miR-485-5p was a direct target of lncRNA SNHG7, which mediated the reproduction of gastric cancer cells [32]. Moreover, miR-485-5p was also involved in studies of colorectal cancer and hepatocellular carcinoma [33, 34]. The decreased expression of miR-485-5p was found to be a direct target of LINC00265 in this study and regulated the proliferation, migration, and invasion of breast cancer cells, which was consistent with the findings of previous reports. In addition, downregulation of miR-485-5p reversed the inhibitory effect of LINC00265 silencing on cell function. Therefore, it is reasonable to believe that in the mechanism of breast cancer, overexpression of LINC00265 as a sponge of miR-485-5p inhibited cell growth, thereby slowing down the degree of tumor progression.

As the sample size of this study was small, possible heterogeneity of non-coding RNAs in the experiment could not be avoided. Moreover, research on breast cancer cells was conducted in vitro, and there may be deeper and unknown molecular mechanisms that need to be explained. Therefore, before clinical application, in-depth studies with larger sample sizes and in vivo studies on breast cancer patients are needed to reveal the relationship between the LINC00265/miR-485-5p axis and breast cancer.

Conclusion

Overall, LINC00265 levels were abnormally elevated in breast cancer, while miR-485-5p expression was conversely decreased. The LINC00265/miR-485-5p axis regulated the progression of breast cancer, and LINC00265 directly targeting miR-485-5p alleviated tumor progression. This implies that LINC00265 is a potential diagnostic marker to provide new ideas for the development of breast cancer treatment.
