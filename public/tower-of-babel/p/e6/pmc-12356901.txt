Author Correction: Changes in human mandibular shape during the Terminal Pleistocene-Holocene Levant
Authors: Ariel Pokhojaev, Hadas Leah Levine, Tatiana Sella-Tunis, Rachel Sarig, Hila May
Published: 2025

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-019-45279-9, published online 19 June 2019

The original version of this Article contained an error in the name of Hadas Leah Levine, which was incorrectly given as Hadas Avni.

As a result, the Author contributions section now reads:

H.M. and A.P. created the GM protocol. H.L.L. and A.P. carried out the segmentation, tested the GM protocol reliability, and applied it to the mandible sample. T.S.T. and H.M. carried out the statistical analysis. R.S. examined teeth crowding. A.P. created the figures. H.M. wrote the main manuscript text. R.S. revised the manuscript. All authors read and approved the final manuscript.

The original Article has been corrected.
