Correction: Evaluation of traffic congestion mitigation techniques using an entropy-TOPSIS integrated method
Authors: Daniyal Hussain, Arshad Jamal, Asim Farooq, Meshal Almoshaogeh, Fawaz Alharbi, Danish Farooq
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-026-35814-w, published online 12 January 2026

The original online version of the Article contained errors.

First, author Meshal Almoshaogeh was omitted as a corresponding author. Correspondence and requests for materials should also be addressed to m.moshaogeh@qu.edu.sa.

In addition, the Acknowledgements section was incorrect. As a result,

“We thank the whole team for their dedicated work.”

now reads:

“The Researchers would like to thank the Deanship of Graduate Studies and Scientific Research at Qassim University for financial support (QU-APC-2026).”

Furthermore, the Funding section was incorrect. As a result,

“The researchers would like to thank the Deanship of Graduate Studies and Scientific Research at Qassim University for financial support (QU-APC-2025).”

now reads:

“The authors declare that no funds, grants, or other support were received from any organization during the preparation of this manuscript.”

Finally, in the Additional information section, to reflect the addition of author Meshal Almoshaogeh as a corresponding author,

“Correspondence and requests for materials should be addressed to A.F.”

now reads:

“Correspondence and requests for materials should be addressed to A.F. and M.A.”

The original Article has been corrected.
