Correction: Qingjie Fuzheng granules attenuate cancer cachexia by restoring gut microbiota homeostasis and suppressing IL-6/NF-κB signaling in colorectal adenocarcinoma
Authors: Yishun Jin, Lisha Lu, Hangju Hua, Biyin Chen, Wenzheng Fang, Kaimin Lin, Peng Ren, Zhenbo Geng, Ling Wang, Xiaohua Yan, Wujin Chen, Jiumao Lin
Published: 2025

Correction: Hereditas 162, 178 (2025)

10.1186/s41065-025-00541-1

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors identified an inaccuracy in the translation of the Chinese herbal medicine “清解扶正颗粒”. Throughout the text, the full name was incorrectly written as “Qingxie Fuzheng granules”. The correct translation for the full name should be “Qingjie Fuzheng granules”.
