ABSTRACT
AbstractUse of nanomaterials for improved thermal applications is a vibrant research area. These applications frequently occur in mechanical, thermal, chemical, and paint industries. Selection of individual nanomaterial characteristics is important to achieve fine results. Thus, the current work aims to study a problem by utilizing the unique structural properties of CNTs nanomaterials and (C2H6O2-H2O) 50:50% with key focus on the diameter and length factors involves in the thermal conductivity. The problem is then formulated for entropy and heat transfer through the use of similarity transforms and induction of controls like radiations, dissipation, mixed convection and convective heat condition effects. The temperature is examined very high near the surface due to convective factor. The mixed convection action over magnetically conducted nanofluid help to reduce the temperature and beneficial for engineering purpose with low heat transfer requirements. Introduction

Nanofluids are consistent mixture of nanoparticles and base fluids having good solvability. These particles may be metals, carbides, alloys, non-metals, carbon nanotubes (SWCNTs, MWCNTs) and many other nanomaterials. Mixing the nanoparticles in base fluids significantly enhance the basic properties like thermal conductivity, electrical conductivity, density, heat capacity and dynamic viscosity. Hence, thermal and entropy performance of a fluid system changes due to addition of nanoparticles in a common fluid. These physical processes have numerous applications in engineering and industrial zone for energy storage, heating/cooling of buildings, mechanical and chemical engineering, paint industry and coating. From the last few decades, researchers from various fields have shown their interest towards the development of more efficient nanofluids using the variety of nanoparticles1 and primary fluids.

Among the nanomaterials, carbon nanotubes is a more fascinating materials and obtained huge consideration of the researchers and scientists. The unique structure, thermal and electrical conductivities, heat capacity and dynamic viscosity of these nanomaterials playing crucial role in the entropy and thermal performance of resultant nanofluid. The CNTs divided into two classes commonly known as SWCNTs (single walled carbon nanotubes) and MWCNTs (multi walled carbon nanotubes) based on their structure. Inspired by thermophysical properties of CNTs, researchers use this material for thermal and entropy applications under varying flow parameters. Recently, Irfan et al.2 examined consequences of SWCNTs in Ellis fluid problem. The authors designed the model for uniform and non-uniform flow configurations. It is noted that addition of SWNTs enhanced the heat transfer ability of the base liquid. Reddy and Sreedevi3 discussed a problem related to CNTs importance for thermal augmentation in Maxwell type nanofluid. The problem configured for disk set up and considered physical constraints. In 2022, Haider et al.4 conducted analysis for combined effects of SWCNTs and MWCNTs for heat transport applications. Deep investigation of the results revealed that (SWCNTs-MWCNTs)/H2O is more efficient than SWCNTs/H2O. The study of nanofluid’s performance in the presence of magnetic dipole is very interesting topic. Such physical setup highly influenced the model momentum and energy transport. Hence, Kumar et al.5 extended the problem by considering convective heat and solar radiation effects. They concluded that increase in these factors greatly augmented the thermal rate of the model over the stretching surface.

Xue6 and Yamada-Ota7 models for CNTs obtained much popularity among the researchers community8. Yamada model contains the length and radius effects of CNTs which playing crucial role in the nanofluid performance. In 2022, Adnan9 performed mathematical analysis for (ZnO-MWCNTs)/H2O model. The author motivated by the thermophysical values of ZnO and MWCNTs and examined that the Biot number is a significant parameter for thermal improvement and preferred Xue CNTs model. Another heat transfer problem for MWCNTs/PG under gravity and radiative effects have been analyzed by Ashraf et al.10. Further, the Xue thermal conductivity relation used for the effectiveness of the problem and deduced that solar radiations is an excellent natural source which enhanced the performance significantly. Shafiq et al.11 reported the importance of CNTs nanomaterials for magnetized stagnant flow of nanoliquid through a variably thicker surface. Recently, Khadija et al.12 pointed that the heat transfer and can be depreciated for Rd and temperature quotient number for Casson liquid flow while increasing amount of CNTs enhanced it for shrinking surface case. For recent inventions in the field of thermal transport using nanofluids, see the Refs13–18.

Combination of SWCNTs and MWCNTs make a hybrid nanomaterial and its addition in the base fluid increases thermal conductivity, density and electrical conductivity which is important for heat transfer problems. Hence, Gowda et al.19 noted that homogenous mixture of (SWNTs-MWCNTs)/H2O with additional influence of heating source boosted the temperature field. Gul et al.20 and Bilal et al.21 conducted studies for steady and unsteady cases for thermal analysis due to insertion of CNTs in different working fluids. They used hybrid nanomaterials (Fe3O4-SWCNTs) and inspected that dispersion of these nanomaterials augmented the model efficiency. Recent interesting analysis regarding the nanofluids have been explored in the studies presented in the Refs22–25., respectively. In 2023, Obalalu et al.26 discussed the use of CNTs for practical applications. The authors suggested that efficiency of solar trough collector can be improved using CNTs nanomaterials. Further, significant physical constraints are also included in the problem development. Omrani et al.27 revealed the importance of MWCNTs shapes on viscosity and thermal conductivity of nanofluids which are two significant factors. The functional liquid is considered as carboxyl MWCNTs and water uniform mixture and examined Newtonian behavior. Further investigations regarding the nano enhanced applications have been discussed in the Refs28–32.

Although, numerous studies as discussed above have been presented for nanofluids prepared by SWCNTs, MWCNTs and different base liquids and achieved fascinating heat transfer results. However, the performance of nanofluid containing SWCNTs in hydrocarbon hybrid base liquid with 50:50% contribution for Riga wedge case using Yamada-Ota model is not reported so far which is a substantial gap and needs to conduct detailed study. For this reason, the RKF (see Refs37–39. method is preferred for the solution purposes. As, the method needs some amended form of the original problem.
