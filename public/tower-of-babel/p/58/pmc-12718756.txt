It synthesizes the latest guideline-driven therapies with practical, multidisciplinary strategies that span pharmacological interventions, nutritional approaches, and quality-of-life considerations. AI that sees beyond borders: Reliable glaucoma detection across diseases and institutionsThis article demonstrates the potential of artificial intelligence (AI) to transform glaucoma screening by analyzing routine color fundus photographs with high accuracy. Validated across multiple institutions, the AI system achieved a balanced accuracy of at least 80% in all external datasets. Even in images complicated by other eye diseases, it reached an area under the curve of 0.93 and a balanced accuracy of 80.9%. Its robust performance remained consistent across patient ethnicities, camera devices, and image qualities, underscoring its scalability. Given the global burden of glaucoma and the urgency of early detection to prevent irreversible blindness, this study highlights a clinically impactful and socially relevant advance.2

Measuring the waist, predicting the strength: Abdominal obesity as a marker of muscle weakness in diabetesThis study identifies waist circumference as a simple yet powerful determinant of relative muscle strength in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus. By showing that abdominal obesity predicts weaker handgrip and lower-limb strength independent of muscle mass, the findings highlight the value of waist measurement as an accessible screening tool. Given the rising global prevalence of diabetes and obesity, this study provides timely evidence that could guide earlier interventions to prevent frailty, disability, and related complications in diabetic populations.3

Cracking the code of lung cancer: SPI1–FARSB control of mTOR and tumor immunityThis study uncovers a novel regulatory mechanism in lung adenocarcinoma, showing that the transcription factor spi-1 proto-oncogene (SPI1) suppresses phenylalanyl-TRNA synthetase subunit beta (FARSB) expression and thereby blocks mammalian target of rapamycin (mTOR) pathway activation. By linking tumor metabolism to immune evasion, the authors demonstrate how FARSB promotes cancer cell proliferation and cluster of differentiation 8 (CD8)⁺ T cell exhaustion, while SPI1 restores anti-tumor immunity. These findings provide strong mechanistic insight and highlight FARSB as a promising therapeutic target, opening new avenues for immunotherapy in lung cancer.4

Balancing nutrition and risk: Rethinking surgery for proximal gastric cancerThis study provides a comprehensive comparison between proximal gastrectomy with double tract reconstruction and total gastrectomy with Roux-en-Y reconstruction in patients with proximal gastric cancer. The results show that proximal gastrectomy offers shorter hospital stays and better postoperative nutritional outcomes, though with a higher risk of reflux esophagitis, while maintaining comparable rates of surgical complications. These findings are clinically significant, as they highlight the trade-off between preserving nutrition and managing postoperative risks, offering surgeons valuable evidence to personalize treatment strategies for proximal gastric cancer.5
