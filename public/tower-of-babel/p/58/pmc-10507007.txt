Authors: Jingzhe Gao, Haixiao Wei
Published: 2023

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-023-40348-6, published online 21 August 2023

The Acknowledgements section in the original version of this Article was incomplete.

“This paper is supported by Zhejiang Provincial Philosophy and Social Sciences Planning Project and Hangzhou Philosophy and Social Sciences Planning Project [grant number Z23JC066].”

now reads:

“This paper is supported by Zhejiang Provincial Philosophy and Social Sciences Planning Project, National Natural Science Foundation of China [grant number 72304246] and Hangzhou Philosophy and Social Sciences Planning Project [grant number Z23JC066].”

The original Article has been corrected.
