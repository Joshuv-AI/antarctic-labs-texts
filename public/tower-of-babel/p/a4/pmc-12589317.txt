Editorial Expression of Concern: Arch Gynecol Obstet (2011) 284:1359–1365 10.1007/s00404-011-1851-8

The Editor-in-Chief is issuing an Editorial Expression of Concern for this article. Concerns have been raised about the data reported and the ethics committee approval for the study. However, the authors have not responded to correspondence from the Editor-in-Chief. Readers are therefore advised to interpret the results and conclusions with caution.
