A short-term load forecasting framework for air conditioning system based on model stacking
Authors: Tianjie Liu, Wenling Jiao, Zhiwei Huang, Jianting Yu, Xin Zhang, Luling Li
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractAccurate short-term air conditioning load forecasting is very important for controlling air conditioning energy consumption, and is also a requisite for realizing intelligent control of air conditioning units. This paper proposes a short-term load forecasting framework for air conditioning based on the concept of model stacking, which combines six mature machine learning models, including Lasso regression, Ridge regression, Random Forest, Support Vector Regression, eXtreme Gradient Boosting and Long Short-Term Memory, and trains new prediction models through model stacking. In order to realize the short term forecast function of air conditioning load, this paper presents a complete forecast framework, which includes operational procedures for feature screening, algorithm hyperparameters optimization, and cross stacking of prediction models. A real air conditioning system is employed for prediction analysis, the prediction results showed that 28 out of 36 control simulations achieved better prediction accuracy, with an average increase in R2 of 6.4%. Notably, simpler submodels in the meta-model yield better results in model stacking, whereas complex coupling models as the meta-model may degrade performance. The proposed framework not only improves prediction accuracy but also maintains reasonable training time and resource requirements, making it practical for real-world applications. These findings provide insights into implementation of model stacking and selecting the meta-model for short-term air conditioning load forecasting.Keywords: Air conditioning system, Load forecasting, Model stacking, Hyperparameter optimizationSubject terms: Engineering, Civil engineering

Introduction

As an important energy consumption area, the building industry is increasingly prominent in energy consumption, which has become one of the important factors restricting sustainable development1. The use of air conditioning, heating, ventilation, electrical appliances and other equipment is the main source of building energy consumption, of which the energy consumption of the air conditioning system accounts for a large proportion of the total energy consumption of the building (about 50%~60%)2, so the energy saving and optimization of the air conditioning system admits of no delay.

Air conditioning load forecasting is of great significance for building energy consumption monitoring and operation optimization. The operating state of the air conditioning system in the actual working process will be affected by many factors such as climate and personnel3,4. If dynamic control strategies cannot be formulated according to the changes in operating conditions, the equipment of the air conditioning system will be restricted in long-term low efficiency, and the overall energy consumption will be further increased. On the contrary, if we can obtain the accurate load prediction results, the operation strategy and equipment control of the air conditioning system can be developed in advance. In addition, since the air conditioning system is an important part of the power load, accurate air conditioning load prediction also helps to improve the stability of the power system.

A common method of building load forecasting in engineering is the physical model-based method5. A number of commercial software have been developed for load forecasting, such as TRNSYS6, DeST7, EnergyPlus8, eQuest9, etc. These software are characterized by building a detailed building physical model and setting multiple dimensions of the working parameters, so as to predict the future period of building load. However, accurate and comprehensive building models, personnel and equipment are difficult to obtain, so the recent research on load forecasting tend more to data-driven methods.

Data-driven load forecasting is to establish an accurate forecasting model based on big data technology through in-depth analysis of historical load data and relevant influencing factors10. Many data-driven methods, including linear regression, Support Vector Machine and random forest, had been applied in load forecasting11,12. Mashud13 applied quantile regression forest method to predict the interval heat load of commercial buildings, and compared it with the traditional gradient boosting regression algorithm, the results show that the regression forest has a higher calculation speed. According to the collected research reports, neural network is also a popular research direction, including Artificial Neural Network14, Convolutional Neural Network15, Recurrent Neural Network and Long Short Term Memory (LSTM). Jang16 set up three LSTM models to forecast the load of non-residential buildings, and analyzed the influence of different influencing factors on the forecast results. The case results show that the proposed method is effective for problems with time series characteristics. Fazlipour17 combined a multi-stage attention mechanism with the Deep LSTM to propose a new deep-stacked autoencoder model while solving the random initial weight problem. Dong18 and Jiang19 respectively used DwdAdam optimization algorithm and Bayesian optimization combined with attention mechanism to improve the LSTM prediction accuracy.

Many coupling models have also been developed. Nivethitha20 coupled the CNN and LSTM models, and the coupled model could extract both spatial and temporal features. The case analysis showed that the performance of the model was better than that of the original independent model. Chen2 combined Singular Spectrum Analysis and Convolutional Neural Network to predict the airport terminals air conditioning load, laying a foundation for the load prediction of large public institutions. In order to optimize individual models, many scholars also use intelligent optimization algorithms to improve the original model. Gao21 used an improved whale optimization algorithm to optimize the extreme learning machine model for predicting building cooling load. Analogously, Zhou22 coupled the whale optimization algorithm with Variational Mode Decomposition and an enhanced least square support vector machines, and the R2 value of the proposed model in the case is much higher than that of the single model. Wang23 proposed a control strategy for air conditioning systems that combines genetic algorithm and XGBoost, which improves the prediction accuracy while reducing the computational effort by 78%. As a whole, current researches focus on the coupling between different prediction algorithms and the coupling between prediction algorithms and optimization algorithms, and most of the coupling models get better results than the original models. However, the coupling mode between the model and the optimization algorithm is decided by the researchers, and it is not easy to compare the advantages and disadvantages of various algorithms because of the lack of theoretical basis.

Overall, there are several problems in the current load forecasting work. When an independent prediction model is used, it can only be predicted based on its own algorithm and structure, and its prediction ability is limited by the complexity and generalization ability of the model itself. When faced with complex or nonlinear problems, the predictive accuracy of independent models may decline. When coupling different models, it is necessary to design a complex coupling mechanism to integrate the output of different models, which increases the complexity and design difficulty of the system, and the selection and parameter setting of the coupling mechanism are also lack of standards.

Model stacking is a deep learning concept developed based on ensemble learning theory. To be brief, it involves training a new model to integrate the results obtained from different models, that is, stacking a new model on top of the existing model combination. The theoretical basis is that different prediction models have their own strengths and characteristics, which can extract different information from the data24. By bundling and learning the results of different models, there is an opportunity to weaken the shortcomings of the original model. The relevant theories of model stacking had been applied in many fields, such as wave height prediction25, soil heavy metal content prediction26, engineering construction cost prediction27, etc. Recent advancements in stacked forecasting frameworks, such as multi-stage feature selection28 and adaptive optimization techniques29, have demonstrated the value of integrating heterogeneous models and dynamic learning strategies. Sharma30 combined multi-phase feature selection technique, long short-term memory-based autoencoder and temporal convolution-based autoencoder to propose a cross-time series prediction framework that guarantees the effectiveness and robustness of specific deep-learning models. However, no application cases have been seen in the field of air conditioning cooling load prediction. Therefore, we hope to explore the potential application of model stacking in air conditioning load forecasting through the research in this article. We hypothesize that the proposed model stacking framework will significantly improve the accuracy of short-term load forecasting compared to individual models.

The primary contribution of this study is to propose a ‌stacked ensemble framework‌ that integrates six established machine learning models to enhance short-term HVAC load forecasting accuracy. By leveraging model stacking, our framework aims to mitigate the limitations of individual models in capturing nonlinear patterns and improve robustness in real-world scenarios. Unlike existing studies that focus on fixed coupling mechanisms or single-model optimizations, the proposed framework introduces a flexible stacking strategy that dynamically integrates heterogeneous models (linear, tree-based, and temporal) through cross-validation. This approach avoids manual coupling design and leverages simpler meta-models to amplify ensemble diversity, addressing the limitations of existing methods in handling nonlinear load fluctuations.

This article is organized as follows: The second section introduces various individual prediction algorithms involved in this article, as well as the selection of influencing factors and evaluation indicators for air conditioning load prediction; The third section provides a detailed explanation of the short-term load forecasting framework for air conditioning proposed in this article; The fourth section illustrates the workflow of the proposed framework through a practical case and analyzes the predicted results; final section provides the conclusion of the article.

Research method

Influence parameters

The selection of input feature parameters will directly affect the prediction model accuracy. The features that affect the load of building air conditioning can be divided into two categories: external features and internal features.

External features mainly refer to various meteorological parameters that have an impact on building air-conditioning load, and there is a strong correlation between meteorological parameters and air conditioning load. The meteorological parameters widely used as input features of load forecasting models include temperature, relative humidity, wind speed, air pressure, solar irradiance, etc. These external meteorological parameters can be obtained through the Meteorological Office31 or relevant weather prediction platforms.

Internal features are a little more complicated. The internal features that affect the air conditioning load include the thermal characteristics of the building envelope, the set temperature and humidity at the air conditioning system terminal, the personnel density in the building, personnel behavior, equipment utilization rate, etc. Among them, the shape and thermal performance of the building belong to the inherent characteristics of the building. For the existing building, such parameters will not change and can not be classified in input features for load prediction.

On the other hand, the set parameters at the building air conditioning terminal and personnel behavior will significantly affect the fluctuation of air conditioning load. However, for large buildings, it is difficult to obtain relevant data comprehensively and accurately even if modern monitoring methods are applied, so most of the current studies use historical data and time features to represent such features. For example, what day of the week it is, what hour of the day it is, whether it is a holiday, and so on.

In this study, the six individual prediction models (Lasso, Ridge, RF, SVR, XGBoost, LSTM) are selected based on their diversity in algorithmic principles (linear, tree-based, temporal) and proven performance in load forecasting literature. For instance, linear models (Lasso, Ridge) handle collinearity, tree-based models (RF, XGBoost) capture nonlinear patterns, and LSTM and SVR address temporal dependencies. This combination ensures comprehensive feature extraction and model robustness in the follow-up study.

Model improvement and framework

Based on the air conditioning load forecasting theory and a variety of individual load forecasting algorithms presented in the previous section, this section proposes a short-term load forecasting method for air conditioning systems based on model stacking, as shown in Fig. 1.

Basic operation flow of the proposed framework.

The implementation process of the proposed method is as follows:

Step 1: Obtain the operation data of the actual air conditioning system, select the feature parameters related to the short-term load of air conditioning initially, and preprocess the relevant feature parameters;

Step 2: Pearson correlation analysis is used to calculate the correlation coefficients between feature parameters and air conditioning cooling load. Parameters with strong correlation are selected as input features according to default condition, and the feature data set is divided into training set and test set as subsequent models input;

Step 3: A number of individual prediction models for short-term load of air conditioning systems are constructed, and the model hyperparameter optimization is carried out by random search method;

Step 4: Select the optimal individual prediction algorithm as the meta-model, and construct a two-layer stacking model for short-term load prediction of air conditioning. The model stacking algorithm including cross-validation is introduced to combine different types of machine learning algorithms;

Step 5: Train the obtained stacking model. Then the short-term load prediction of air conditioning system is realized, and the accuracy of the model is tested by several indicators.

The above steps are further elaborated as follows:

Firstly, the system operation data is read from the air conditioning energy management platform and the relevant feature parameters are preprocessed. The next step is to build individual prediction models for short term load of air conditioning system. Multiple models with mature algorithms and different basic logic should be selected as individual prediction algorithms, that is, individual prediction model 1, individual prediction model 2, … In this study, six individual prediction models given in the previous section are selected and constructed.

In machine learning/deep learning algorithms, most of the parameters need to be gradually optimized during the training process, but some hyperparameters, such as the number of hidden layers, learning rate and sampling ratio of the neural network, which are used to adjust the training process, need to be manually configured before training, and these hyperparameters directly affect the structure and effect of the training model. The common methods of hyperparameter optimization include Grid Search (GS), Random Search (RS) and Bayesian optimization. GS needs to traverse all possible hyperparameter combinations, and the computational cost increases exponentially with the parameter dimension, especially in the face of high-dimensional parameter space. In contrast, RS significantly reduces the number of iterations by randomly sampling a combination of parameters. Although Bayesian optimization can approach the optimal solution more efficiently through probabilistic proxy model, it needs to calculate the objective function gradient frequently and requires high hardware resources. This study is aimed at the actual air-conditioning system prediction scenario, which needs to balance model performance and computational cost. Therefore, this study selects a RS method to minimize the RMSE on the test set as the objective function for optimization. It should be noted that RS method does not guarantee to find the global optimal values, but it is more computationally efficient and suitable for high-dimensional parameter space34.

In Step 4, a two-layer stacking model for short-term load prediction will be constructed. Firstly, the individual models and data established in the previous step are used for load prediction, evaluation indicators of each individual model are calculated and compared, and a model with good comprehensive effect and relatively simple structure is selected as the second layer of the stacking model, which is also called the metalearner. The remaining independent prediction models that are not selected as meta-learners together form the first layer of the stacked model, the Base Learners (base learner 1, base learner 2,…). The primary role of these base learners is to generate new features that are passed to the meta-learner as input data. Through this mechanism, stacked models can fuse the predictions of multiple models to capture more complex and subtle patterns in the data. The overall two-layer stacking model execution process is shown in Fig. 2.

Workflow of the two-layer stacking model.

To improve the generalization ability of the stacked model and effectively avoid overfitting, we introduced a hierarchical K-fold cross-validation during the construction process. Considering the amount of computation, we use a hierarchical 5-fold cross-validation method, which means that the original training set is randomly and evenly divided into 5 subsets (“folds”). In each fold, 4 subsets are used as training data, while the remaining one is used as validation data. This process is carried out independently for each base learner, starting from k = 1 and ending at k = 5, to ensure that each subset has an opportunity to serve as the validation set, thus fully evaluating the performance of the model.

In each cross-validation iteration, the base learner is trained on the corresponding training subset and makes predictions on the validation subset. These predictions are then saved as input data to the meta-learner. In this way, each base learner provides five different sets of predictions that incorporate an understanding of the load data from multiple perspectives, greatly enriching the input feature set of the meta-learner.

Finally, in step 5, we use the new feature data set to train the second layer model, use the newly obtained test data for testing, and the prediction results are evaluated with relevant indicators.

Case study

Case description

The case in this study comes from the air conditioning system of a large one-stop urban commercial complex in Guangdong Province, China. In early work, the project equipped a large number of energy detection equipment, and built an energy management cloud platform and a local energy management platform (Fig. 3). The project data is collected in a period of 10 min, which makes it possible to predict the air conditioning load in the short term.

Energy management platform operation interface.

From the energy management platform of the air conditioning system, we exported the data of the commercial building from March 1 to June 30, 2024 for the construction of the load forecast model. Monitoring data can be directly exported from the platform in Excel format. We obtain about 3200 sets of data for model training after deleting some of the missing data. In order to improve computing efficiency, Excel data exported from the energy management platform is firstly converted into ‘.csv’ format. The experiments were conducted on a system with Intel Core i7-12700 K(32GB DDR4) / Windows 10(64-bit) / Python 3.8.5. Under this calculation condition, the time (training and forecasting) for single load forecasting using independent forecasting model and stacked model is about 15 ~ 31s and 19 ~ 55s respectively. Each prediction time is within the same order of magnitude and far less than the time required for subsequent air conditioning system operation control.

According to the analysis of features affecting the short-term load of air conditioners in Sect. 2.1, 12 input features of the model are preliminarily selected: outdoor air temperature, outdoor air humidity, air wet bulb temperature, outdoor wind speed, and chilled water temperature difference among external factors; Date feature parameter, hour feature parameter, minute feature parameter among internal factors; Cooling load before 10 min, 20 min, 30 min, and 24 h in historical data. The correlation coefficients among the feature parameters are calculated by Pearson correlation analysis, and the results are shown in Fig. 4.

On the other hand, as the best model in the independent prediction process, the prediction accuracy of stacking model with XGBoost as the metamodel is lower than that of the original individual prediction model. Specifically, out of 36 controlled trials spanning six periods, 28 trials have better predictions. This indicates that the proposed model stacking process can not guarantee to improve the accuracy of air conditioning load prediction, and the specific effect depends on the mode and combination of model stacking. Algorithms such as XGBoost, which are inherently based on assembling ideas, may have negative effects when applied to the proposed framework.

Meanwhile, we find that Ridge and Lasso have the best predictive effect among the stacking models, and both of these algorithms belonged to linear regression algorithms with relatively simple structure, and they did not perform well in the independent prediction process. However, the stacking models which use the complicated XGB algorithm as the meta-model perform worse. Therefore, we believe that when the proposed method is applied to air conditioning load forecasting, it is better to adopt the individual forecasting model with relatively simple structure as the metamodel, which can better integrate the features of complex models and form complementary advantages.

Conclusion

This study proposes a short-term load forecasting framework for air conditioning based on model stacking to improve the prediction accuracy of traditional models. The proposed method provides operational procedures for short-term air conditioning load forecasting feature screening, algorithm hyperparameter optimization, and cross stacking of prediction models. In this paper, a real air conditioning system is taken as an example for prediction analysis. Six machine learning models Lasso, Ridge, RF, SVR, XGBoost and LSTM are used, and different model stacking modes are selected for prediction. By analyzing the prediction results of different independent prediction models and stacked models, the following main conclusions can be drawn:

Comparing the individual prediction model with the stacking model using the model as the meta-model, most of the prediction accuracy is improved, and the average R2 index in the case is improved by 6.4%;

Comparing the individual prediction model with the stacking model using the model as the meta-model, most of the prediction accuracy is improved, and the average R2 index in the case is improved by 6.4%;

Not all stacking models achieved better predictive results than the original model, and 28 out of 36 controlled trials achieved better results, suggesting that attention should be paid to choosing the structure of the stacking model when performing model stacking;

Choosing the submodel with simpler structure in the meta-model shows better results in the model stacking, while choosing the complex coupling model as the meta-model may get worse, which provides a train of thought to choose the meta-model in the air conditioning load forecasting.
