Retraction Note: Methylation-induced suppression of BEX1 activates AKT/ERK/STAT3 signaling pathways regulating cell cycle and apoptosis in glioma
Authors: Li-Zhi Xiao, Shi Feng, Zhen He, Rui Mao, Kai Qian, Zhi-Ying Zhou, Huabao Cai, Mengyu Zhao, Cunzhi Wang, Tianhang Yu, Zi-Yu Zhao, Jie Li
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-14123-8, published online 13 August 2025

The Editors have retracted this Article.

After publication, concerns were raised about image irregularities in the Article. Specifically:

• Fig. 2D appears to have panels partially overlapping with Fig. 3C in1;

• Fig. 2D appears to have panels partially overlapping with Fig. 3C in1;

• Fig. 2F panel NC (LN 229) appears to partially overlap with Fig. 3L panel U251 SAM + 5-AzaC in the same article;

• Fig. 2F panel NC (LN 229) appears to partially overlap with Fig. 3L panel U251 SAM + 5-AzaC in the same article;

• Fig. 3E appears to have panels partially overlapping with Fig. 12C in3;

• Fig. 3E appears to have panels partially overlapping with Fig. 12C in3;

• Fig. 3L appears to have panels partially overlapping with with Fig. 8B in2;

• Fig. 3L appears to have panels partially overlapping with with Fig. 8B in2;

• Fig. 5G appears to have panels partially overlapping with Fig. 3C and 4J in1

• Fig. 5G appears to have panels partially overlapping with Fig. 3C and 4J in1

The Editors have therefore lost confidence in the contents of this Article. All Authors agree with this retraction.
