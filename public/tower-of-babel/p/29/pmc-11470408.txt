Measurement and spatial and temporal characterization of logistics efficiency--Based on a three-stage DEA model
Authors: Hanqi Song, Yufei Ma
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractThe logistics industry is an essential component of economic and social development. Enhancing its efficiency contributes to national economic progress. However, the sector faces challenges such as high costs and pollution. This paper conducts an empirical study on the logistics efficiency of 30 Chinese provinces (excluding Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region) from 2004 to 2021 using a three-stage DEA model. Findings reveal that before adjustment, five provinces, including Beijing and Shanghai, were on the efficiency frontier, reduced to three after adjustment. Other provinces exhibit room for improved logistics efficiency, particularly in addressing cost and pollution issues. The key to enhancing efficiency lies in technical and scale efficiency adjustments. External environmental factors significantly influence logistics efficiency; social retail goods consumption positively correlates, while Gross Regional Product negatively correlates. Overall, China's logistics efficiency displays an upward trend but with notable regional disparities, indicating higher efficiency in the eastern region and lower efficiency in central and western regions, resulting in an overall "high in the east, low in the west" pattern.Keywords: Logistics efficiency, Three-stage DEA model, total factor productivity

Introduction

The logistics industry (LI) has been established as a separate industry from the production and distribution chains as an independent link, and it has developed into an influential profession propelled by the availability of technology and consumer demand as well as the unique features of the sector and contemporary demands [1]. The productivity and quality of life in society are intimately correlated with the Effectiveness of the Logistics Industry (ELI); hence, increasing that sector's productivity has emerged as a critical strategy for raising the populace's speed and standard of living [2]. Based on statistics provided by China's National Bureau of Statistics, the added worth of China's Logistics Industry has shown a progressive upward trend, culminating in a value of 4706.1 billion yuan in 2021. This figure represents a notable rise of around 800 billion yuan compared to the preceding year, signifying a growth rate of 12.1 %. However, this still needs to catch up to the Chinese people's aspiration for the LI. Therefore, this article will measure the ELI and overall factor productivity to increase the ELI.

However, the LI persists in grappling with challenges such as exorbitant expenses, subpar efficiency, and ecological deterioration [3,4]. The China Statistical Yearbook (2022) states that as of 2021, 17.85 million tons of pollutants, including 2,747,800 tons of sulfur dioxide SO2, 9.73 million tons of nitrogen oxides, along with 538 play plenty of fine particles, were released from exhaust gases. Most air pollutants, in addition to industrial pollutants, are waste gases released by the LI. However, the LI needs help with excessive costs, poor productivity, and environmental contamination. Apart from industrial emissions, the primary sources of air pollution mainly consist of waste gases from logistics industries.

The LI will also use much energy daily, including oil, natural gas, primary electricity, and other energy sources. In 2021, the industry will use 541,309,000 tons of energy, 71.76 times more than in 1997. The LI is now recognized as a prominent factor in the escalation of air pollution, thus intensifying the issue of environmental pollution. The LI has emerged as a prominent factor in the escalation of air pollution, thus intensifying the issue of environmental pollution. This article examines the effectiveness of the country's green LI by focusing on environmental pollution and selecting relevant negative indicators for evaluation. The selection of the three-stage DEA model has been made in order to assess the ELI. The BCC model incorporates two environmental variables to mitigate the effect of outside variables and unpredictability on effectiveness measurement. Moreover, environmental pollution is regarded as an undesirable outcome. The BCC model is used to evaluate the efficacy of China's LI and the alterations in total factor productivity spanning 2004 to 2021. The assessments have significant consequences for the growth and improvement of China's LI.

The next portion of this study thoroughly examines the pertinent scholarly works. Literature review

The level of development of the logistics industry has become one of the most important indicators of the comprehensive strength of a country or region [5]. The efficiency of the logistics industry is an essential indicator of its development level [6]. The logistics industry can only improve its competitiveness by realizing higher efficiency in transportation [7]. The advantages of improving logistics efficiency are mainly concentrated in the following two aspects.

First, logistics efficiency reduces the cost aspect of the logistics industry. Liu Huajun (2021) believes that the high cost of logistics is one of the main problems affecting the development of the logistics industry [8]. Li Han (2023) believes that improving logistics efficiency can optimize logistics routes, improve the quality of service, and reduce transportation costs [9]. Innovation in the logistics industry also plays a vital role in increasing logistics efficiency. Modern information, such as data, can enhance logistics efficiency and improve the logistics industry's development [10]. High-value information improves logistics activities and the overall performance of logistics operations [11]. MATUSIEWICZ (2020) studied the logistics industry in the European Union and found that the Internet of Things (IoT) and the Internet can optimize the logistics process and create a more efficient and sustainable supply chain [12]. The spread of the New Crown Pneumonia epidemic has led residents to look for ways to purchase goods online and have them delivered to their homes by regular staff, realizing the ideal situation where a person can purchase whatever they need while sitting at home, making e-commerce proficiently used by all age groups [13]. This has expanded the consumer base of e-commerce logistics. This has also contributed to improvements in logistics technology, with the emergence of logistics methods such as buy online and pick up in-store (BOPS), intelligent lockers, and drone deliveries [14]. Although innovative Internet + logistics services models are gradually advancing [15], intelligent logistics and smart cities have been put forward one after another [16]. However, many problems still lead to logistics efficiency needing to reach people's expectations. Therefore, this paper selects the indicators that can show the innovation of the logistics industry, such as the population employed in the logistics industry and the capital investment in the logistics industry, as the input indicators.

Secondly, logistics efficiency improves the polluted environment aspect. Improving logistics efficiency improves the service quality of the logistics industry and reduces the negative impact of logistics operations on the environment and human health [17]. As an important part of every industry in the process of social production and reproduction, the logistics industry profoundly impacts ecological and other social issues [18,19]. Therefore, improving energy efficiency in logistics is crucial for environmental sustainability [20]. The European Commission has adopted a new approach to energy efficiency in logistics. In this regard, the European Commission has reduced the impact on transportation and the environment by taking the initiative of cargo bicycles to improve energy efficiency but reduced mobility and reliability over long distances [21]. As regional energy consumption and environmental pollution increase due to climbing populations [22], it has become essential to explore the impact of green logistics on sustainable development and energy efficiency. Participation in green logistics practices and the development of appropriate policies can help to alleviate increasing international environmental problems [23]. In Asian countries, the development of urban industrialization and urbanization can increase environmental pollution [24]. A low-carbon economy is a rare opportunity for China to realize the transformation of economic development mode [25]. China's logistics industry faces problems of high costs, low efficiency, and excessive pollutant emissions, which impose a heavy burden on the environment [26]. Improving the efficiency of green logistics can ensure sustainable development and solve the problem of environmental pollution [27]. Therefore, this paper selects the corresponding environmentally friendly logistics industry. This paper selects the corresponding environmental pollution indicators as the non-expected outputs to study green logistics efficiency.

Most of the existing research methods focus on traditional DEA, SFA, and SBM models to measure logistics efficiency. Hamdan (2008) argues that DEA is a linear planning technique that evaluates multiple input and output decision-making units. Therefore, DEA models measure the logistics industry's efficiency [28]. Moreover, later, Markovits-Somogyi (2014) used DEA and DEA-PC (pairwise comparison) to compare and analyze the logistics efficiency of European countries and concluded that DEA methodology can assess the efficiency of the distribution industry [29]. Since then, more scholars have begun to use the DEA method to assess logistics efficiency. Zhang Yunfeng (2018) used the traditional DEA method to study the sustainable development of China's logistics industry and concluded that the sustainable development of the logistics industry still has a long way to go [17]. Andrejic Uses the traditional DEA method to study the correlation between logistics efficiency and logistics cost, cargo quantity, turnover rate, etc., which is used to improve logistics efficiency [30]. Cao Bingru (2019) used traditional DEA methods to measure logistics efficiency. However, he believes that the choice of input-output variables greatly influences traditional DEA, so he suggests combining traditional DEA with other methods to evaluate [31]. Therefore, Wang Yuhong (2017) evaluated the static and dynamic green efficiency of the logistics industry using a combination of the super-efficient slack measurement (Super-SBM) model and the Malmquist index and concluded that the improvement of logistics efficiency could promote green logistics [32]. Hongwei Liu (2022) used the SBM model to study the efficiency of the retail logistics industry in Germany and found that the SBM model has more efficiency advantages compared to the DEA model [7]. Zeng Youxin (2013) used a three-stage DEA model to study the development of the logistics industry in Jiangsu Province, China, and concluded that the improvement of green logistics efficiency could reduce energy consumption and promote the sustainable development of green logistics [33]. From the above studies, it can be seen that the research on logistics efficiency has not reached a unified consensus on measurement, and it is rarely applied in the macro-level assessment of logistics efficiency [34]. It is generally recognized that macro-indicators often fail to reveal bottlenecks and problems in the specific operations and processes of logistics efficiency in a detailed manner, but it is also of great significance that the use of macro-indicators in the logistics industry as an economic pointer can provide information on the health of and trends in the overall logistics system and support high-level strategic decision-making [35,36].

It is evident that there are gaps in research regarding China's green logistics efficiency, and this paper's innovations primarily lie in three key areas. Firstly, in terms of research methodology, a three-stage DEA model is constructed to analyze the logistics industry's comprehensive technical efficiency, pure technical efficiency, and scale efficiency. The MML index model is also employed to evaluate the industry's total factor productivity, allowing for a comprehensive assessment of logistics efficiency across different stages. Secondly, environmental factors are incorporated into the logistics efficiency evaluation system. This includes consideration of both positive and non-expected outputs in indicator selection and the environmental impact of logistics industry pollution. Thirdly, two environmental variables, total retail sales of consumer goods and gross regional product, are introduced to mitigate the influence of external factors and random errors on logistics efficiency measurement. By summarizing these innovations, the paper concludes with recommendations for countermeasures to enhance China's logistics efficiency.

Logistics efficiency measurement

Three-stage DEA modeling

This paper suggests the introduction of a three-stage DEA model to mitigate the effects of environmental factors and random noise. In this model, the first stage remains the conventional DEA model, aimed at analyzing initial efficiency. The second stage involves eliminating environmental factors and statistical noise from the efficiency evaluation using the SFA model, constituting a crucial aspect of the three-stage DEA approach. The third stage reverts to the traditional DEA model, with minor adjustments made to the input-output data. Data and variables

Input variable indicator selection

(1) Number of employees in the logistics industry: This indicator is chosen to measure the level of labor input in the logistics industry. Specifically, employment in transportation, warehousing, and postal and telecommunication industries within urban collective units is selected. (2) Capital investment in the logistics industry: The investment in fixed assets within the transportation, warehousing, and postal industry is selected to represent capital investment in the logistics industry. (3) Length of transportation routes: The combined mileage of railways, highways, and waterways is selected to represent the length of transportation routes. This reflects the infrastructure development of road transportation within the logistics industry across different regions [37].

Output variable indicator selection

Desired output:

(1) Value added to the logistics industry: The value added by the transportation, storage, and postal industries is chosen to measure the final results of production activities within the logistics industry over a specific period. (2) Passenger transportation volume: This indicator reflects the contribution of the logistics industry through passenger transport services. (3) Freight transportation volume: This indicator represents how logistics transportation services contribute to the national economy and people's lives [38].

Non-desired output:

Sulfur dioxide emissions: This indicator reflects the environmental pollution caused by the development of the logistics industry [8]. To achieve the goal of "dual carbon,” China has issued a number of relevant policies, and different regions may have different regulatory intensities and focus on carbon dioxide emissions. Choosing sulfur dioxide as an indicator of green logistics efficiency consistent with China's national conditions can overcome part of the endogenous problem.

Selection of environment variables

Environmental variables refer to subjective factors that impact the logistics industry's efficiency but lie beyond logistics management's control. These external factors fluctuate over time and originate from external sources. Following an extensive review of literature and field research, the following 2 variables are selected to demonstrate the impact of outside environmental variables on industrial effectiveness: (1) The growth of total retail sales of consumer goods is an important driver of the growth of logistics demand, which reflects the economic development of a region [13,39]. (2) Gross regional product, indicative of the overall economic development of a given region [40]. The logistics industry encompasses transportation, warehousing, and postal services as defined in the National Economic Industry Classification.

This paper primarily employs the BCC method for the initial stage of measurement. In this paper, the information in the form of input variables (X1, X2, X3), desired output variables (Y1, Y2), and Undesired output (Z1) of 30 provinces (except Xinjiang province) in China for 2004–2021 were added through the BCC approach as decision-making entities using MaxDEA programs, and the outcomes are shown in Fig. 1.

Analysis of DEA results after three-stage adjustment

Based on the regression results from the second stage and utilizing the adjusted data, the BCC model was reapplied to assess the logistics efficiency and decomposition index status of 30 provinces in China. The logistics efficiency results from the first and third stages were compared. An increase in adjusted efficiency indicates a negative impact of environmental factors on logistics efficiency, while a decrease suggests a positive impact.

Before and after adjustment

Comparison of logistics efficiency results among the 30 provinces in China before and after adjustment (Fig. 2a, b, c, d, e, f) reveals that prior to adjustment, only five provinces in the eastern region—Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shanghai, and Hainan—along with Anhui Province in the central region, were situated on the efficiency frontier. After adjustment, only Beijing, Shanghai, and Anhui Province remained on the efficiency frontier (Fig. 2a and b and c). Overall, the comprehensive efficiency value increased in 12 provinces, remained unchanged in 3, and decreased in 15 provinces before and after adjustment (Fig. 2d and e and f). The external environment and the level of regional logistics development largely influence these variations.

This indicates that after eliminating environmental factors, the province's technology and logistics industry management levels are higher, and scale efficiency is the primary constraint on improving logistics industry efficiency. In 2021, the pure technical efficiency of Tibet Autonomous Region, Qinghai Province, Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region, and Heilongjiang Province tends to approach 1, but the scale efficiency is 0.040, 0.144, 0.334, and 0.514, respectively. The comprehensive efficiency is only 0.040, 0.133, 0.334, and 0.376, respectively, which is lower among the 30 provinces, owing to inadequate basic logistics facilities and weak development of the local logistics industry.

Discussion

Foster the integrated and coordinated development of China's logistics industry

China's logistics efficiency exhibits spatial disparities, inadequacies, and disjointedness. Hence, neighboring provinces can advance China's logistics sector's integrated and coordinated progress through various means such as spatial correlation, industrial linkages, market integration, resource sharing, and technological connectivity. This effort aims to enhance the concentration level of the logistics industry. Each province should identify its unique strengths and positioning, explore pathways and modes of cross-regional collaboration, and leverage the spill-over and radiation effects of leading provinces like Beijing, Shanghai, and Guangdong to narrow the efficiency gap in logistics, optimize resource allocation, and elevate its development.

Innovation drives progress and facilitates the transition and enhancement of capabilities

Leveraging intelligent logistics and other approaches to propel the evolution and upgrading of the logistics sector involves integrating next-generation digital technologies with logistics operations. This entails surmounting pivotal core technological challenges and deploying advanced technologies across China's entire province. Enhancing collaboration among industry, academia, and research institutions is crucial for further enhancing logistics efficiency and elevating the technological prowess of China's logistics industry. This collaboration involves data sharing, unmanned warehousing and distribution, and the adoption of novel logistics models.

Enhance the external environment and foster economic growth

Recognizing the constraints on logistics efficiency stemming from rising retail sales of consumer goods, the government can instigate change in residents' consumption patterns and attitudes by bolstering external consumption, attracting foreign investment, and reducing personal income taxes for residents. Additionally, to bolster logistics efficiency through increased regional GDP, the government can stimulate local economic expansion by fostering exchange and collaboration with neighboring provinces, capitalizing on each region's geographic traits and industrial strengths. Continuously enhancing local logistics infrastructure, fortifying logistics network nodes, and driving logistics efficiency forward are pivotal components of this endeavor.

Conclusions

This conclusion, compared with existing studies, describes significant differences in the level of development of China's logistics industry at both the temporal and spatial levels. However, the conclusions and discussion are somewhat different from the existing studies in terms of the correlation between the efficiency of the logistics industry and the total retail sales of consumer goods and regional GDP. It has been argued that regional GDP has no significant effect on the efficiency of the logistics industry and that the total retail sales of consumer goods are positively correlated with the efficiency of the logistics industry, and even the total retail sales of consumer goods are used as a proxy for the efficiency variable of the logistics industry, but this study argues that the total retail sales of consumer goods do not have a constant positive correlation with the efficiency of the logistics industry [41,42].

Utilizing relevant data, this study selects employed persons in the logistics industry, capital investment in the logistics industry, and transportation route length as input variables, and the value added of the logistics industry, volume of passenger transportation, and volume of freight transportation as output variables. Sulfur dioxide emission is considered the non-desired output variable. Employing the three-stage DEA model, the evaluation system of China's logistics efficiency from 2004 to 2021 is established, and each efficiency was measured. The findings are as follows: (1) Spatial Evolution: Significant disparities exist in the development level of China's logistics industry. After eliminating external environmental interference and random errors, noticeable changes in logistics efficiency are observed nationwide and in the eastern, central, and western regions. In the initial stage, technological progress primarily drives logistics efficiency. In contrast, in the third stage, logistics efficiency is primarily constrained by scale efficiency, underscoring the effectiveness of the three-phase DEA method in measuring China's logistics efficiency. Apart from four provinces such as Beijing and Shanghai, the logistics efficiency of the remaining provinces requires improvement to some extent, indicating that these four provinces demonstrate greater efficiency in terms of internal management, technical proficiency, and external scale of the logistics industry, positioning them as leaders in China's logistics efficiency. Conversely, provinces such as Jiangxi, Gansu, Guizhou, Qinghai, Hainan, Tibet, and Ningxia exhibit higher efficiency in purely technical terms but lower efficiency in comprehensive technical and scale terms, implying that although the management and technical proficiency of the logistics industry are high, low scale efficiency remains the primary constraint on logistics efficiency. Hence, these regions cannot solely rely on external environmental factors to inflate logistics efficiency but must seize and judiciously utilize development opportunities to foster logistics industry development and enhance logistics efficiency. (2) Time Evolution: The comprehensive technical efficiency of China's logistics industry demonstrates fluctuating increases with considerable disparities. (3) Influence of External Environmental Variables: Retail sales of consumer goods exhibit a negative correlation with regional logistics efficiency. Accelerated growth in retail sales of consumer goods positively promotes improvements in logistics efficiency and indirectly boosts the employment rate in the logistics industry. However, excessive inputs inevitably lead to redundancy and reduced logistics efficiency. Regional GDP helps reduce inputs into the efficiency of China's logistics industry. As the regional economy develops, factors such as redundancy of employees in the logistics industry, capital investment, and the length of transportation routes are reduced, leading to an increase in logistics efficiency. With regional economic development, each province's logistics infrastructure becomes more robust, and transportation networks become more developed, resulting in improved input-output ratios for the logistics industry. This entails reductions in the number of employees in the logistics industry, capital investment, and route length, thereby enhancing logistics efficiency.
