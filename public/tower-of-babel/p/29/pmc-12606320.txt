A multicriteria quality evaluation method for organized scientific research considering heterogeneous information
Authors: Cuilu Wang, Lei Zhang
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractTo solve the shortcomings existing in the multi-criteria quality evaluation of organized scientific research, such as the unclear construction process of evaluation indicators, the difficulty in handling heterogeneous evaluation information, the neglect of uncertainty characteristics in the weight calculation process, and the insufficiently scientific fusion mechanism of heterogeneous evaluation information, a multi-criteria quality evaluation method for organized scientific research considering heterogeneous information is proposed. Firstly, based on systems-theoretic accident modeling and processes theory, and the influence of the context is taken into consideration, the evaluation index of organized scientific research in universities has been constructed. Then, in accordance with the process of heterogeneous information transformation, weight determination model, information fusion mechanism and quality evaluation method, the multi-criteria quality evaluation method for organized scientific research considering heterogeneous information is explained. Finally, four engineering colleges in Shaanxi Province are selected as cases to verify the rationality of the proposed algorithm. The discussion and analysis show that the proposed method has reasonable construction indicators. It can consider the uncertainty and fuzziness characteristics of the data, and can handle heterogeneous evaluation information reasonably, and has certain superiority.Keywords: Organized scientific research, Multi-criteria evaluation, Heterogeneous information, System theorySubject terms: Electrical and electronic engineering, Information technology

With the rapid development of science and technology, scientific and technological innovation has gradually become a battlefield of fierce competition in various fields. As the main body of scientific research, universities play a key role in enhancing innovation capabilities and promoting major scientific and technological achievements1,2. Meanwhile, with the intense transformation of the current scientific research model, “organized scientific research in universities” has become a current research hotspot due to its systematic and scientific research model. Especially the research on the quality evaluation of organized scientific research in universities can help universities grasp the key points of scientific research, adjust and optimize resource allocation in a timely manner, and promote the high-quality and efficient development of scientific research. This model can promote the organization of scientific research activities and accelerate the innovation and reform of scientific research organization models3,4.

At present, certain research achievements have been made regarding the quality evaluation of scientific research levels in universities. Pinar M et al.5 utilize the Research Excellence Framework (REF) to conduct a comprehensive assessment of the research environment, research outcomes, and non-academic impacts of higher education institutions. The optimization of public fund allocation has been achieved. Cheng Z et al.6 build an evaluation framework including three dimensions: the sustainable trend of scientific research activities, research achievements related to the theme of sustainable development, and the sustainability of scientific research contributions, based on the educational concept of sustainable development and the functions of scientific research activities. Then the quality of scientific research in universities is evaluated. Ngoc N M et al.7 build a comprehensive evaluation of the public and private universities scientific research quality in Vietnam based on three aspects: research quality, teaching quality and internationalization level, and 13 indicators. Zhang E et al.8 evaluate the scientific research achievements of university humanities teachers from six aspects, including the evaluation subject, the evaluation object, the evaluation purpose and so on. Furthermore, currently, a relatively large number of evaluation methods are concentrated on machine learning and deep learning, such as Support Vector Machine (SVM)9, Radial Basis Function (RBF)10, BP neural network11, and other methods.

The above-mentioned evaluation methods have certain applicability, but there are still some flaws: (1) The process of constructing evaluation indicators is not clear. There are defects such as excessive reliance on expert experience and insufficiently scientific evaluation indicators. (2) It is difficult to handle heterogeneous evaluation information. Expert evaluation information often exists in the form of a single real number or a single text. But in actual evaluation information, there is heterogeneous information. For example, the quantity indicator of cultivating high-level innovative talents is more accurate when it is described with real numbers or interval numbers. The use of text evaluation information for the top-level design, development planning and policy improvement indicators of organized scientific research is more accurate. (3) The weight calculation process ignores the characteristics of uncertainty. Due to the certain uncertainty and fuzziness of the evaluation criteria, calculating weights in the form of real numbers will cause information distortion. (4) The information fusion mechanism of heterogeneous evaluation is still not scientific enough, especially when facing ambiguous and ignorant uncertain information. The research on the fusion mechanism is relatively lagging behind.

Establish evaluation indicators for organized scientific research in universities

Organized scientific research evaluation in universities is a complex systematic project. Its indicators have the characteristics of multi-factor cross-coupling and strong interactivity. Traditional indicator construction methods often ignore the interaction between each module and the analysis of context influence is insufficient. Therefore, in this paper, the STAMP method is introduced into the evaluation indicators construction for organized scientific research in universities. This model is widely applied in the risk indicators construction in fields such as aerospace12,13 and transportation14. But there are no relevant studies in the field of organized scientific research evaluation in universities yet. In the research evaluation, this method is based on system theory and regards the research object as a dynamic and interactive complex system. Its core assumption is that system failure results from the unexpected control relationships interaction among components rather than isolated faults. In the research evaluation, it is demonstrated as follows: emphasizing multi-level control structures, focusing on the integrity of constraint condition transmission, and using the reverse tracing method to identify system defects. Its adaptive boundaries mainly consist of three aspects: first, avoiding imbalance in granularity. When the evaluation object is a single disciplinary basic research, system modeling may become overly complex. Second, there needs to be a reasonable data threshold. It requires support from multiple levels and various types of interrelationships. Third, it has a time window. Due to the system’s feedback delay effect, the dynamic evaluation cycle should not be too short.

In this paper, the Systems Theoretic Process Analysis (STPA) method is employed. Following the analysis process shown in Fig. 2, a comprehensive evaluation system is constructed by building the interaction among the various parts of the organized scientific research quality evaluation task in universities. The method for constructing the organized research evaluation indicators for universities based on STAMP proposed in this paper is guided by systematic thinking. It emphasizes the interaction relationships among the overall system and its components and avoids local perspective bias. At the same time, it has dynamic evolvability. It focuses on the dynamic changes and constraint relationships to adapt to the complex environments requirements. It can consider the causal relationship in the context and mine more effective indicators, and has stronger superiority compared with other indicator construction methods.

STPA analysis process.

The determination of control behavior is not a subjective assumption but is based on the following key grounds: (1) System-level constraints: This is the most fundamental basis. All control behaviors must serve to prevent the system from violating predefined system-level constraints (such as “optimal quality and efficiency of organized scientific research in universities”). (2) Control Objectives: Each control loop has its specific control objectives, and control behavior is the concrete means to achieve these objectives. (3) System functional Requirements: The system is analyzed to clarify which control actions the system needs to perform in order to achieve its functions. (4) Component Interaction: The interactions between the controller and the actuator, sensors, and other controllers are analyzed to identify the necessary coordination, synchronization, or interlocking control behaviors. Meanwhile, the selection is made in accordance with the process of defining the analysis purpose, constructing the interactive control structure, and identifying the control behavior.

Meanwhile, to ensure that the selected control behaviors are both critical and comprehensive, the following strategies are adopted in this paper: (1) Structured Decomposition and Hierarchical Analysis: Starting from system-level security constraints, it is gradually decomposed to the control requirements and behaviors at the subsystem and component levels. The control hierarchy is clearly defined to ensure that control behaviors at all levels are covered, including high-level decision-making and low-level execution. (2) Exhaustive List of Control Loops: All control loops are systematically identified to avoid missing any important controllers or controlled processes. Pay special attention to the controller circuits, human–computer interaction and interaction between systems. (3) Iterative Refinement: STPA is an iterative process. After the initial identification of control behaviors, as behavior analysis, scenario analysis and constraint formulation deepen, new and more fine-grained control behavior requirements may be discovered, and the list of control behaviors needs to be continuously supplemented and improved.

The proposed construction method of organized scientific research evaluation indicators in universities based on STAMP is oriented towards systematic thinking. At the same time it has dynamic evolution, pays attention to the dynamic changes and constraint relationships of the system, adapts to the complex environments requirements. It can consider the causal relationship in the context and mine more effective indicators, and has stronger superiority compared with other indicator construction methods.
