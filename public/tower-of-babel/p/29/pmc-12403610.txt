Outbreak dates of virus could be predicted by their protein sequence
Authors: Peijun Zuo, Longlong Zuo, Zhihong Li, Xiaotong Zhou, Yanping Yu, Qinqing Wu, Yixiao Niu, Qiaocheng Chang, A. Bakr M. Rabie, Paul Lam, Liping Li
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
IntroductionSince 1970, monkey-pox, the last outbreak of smallpox, coronavirus was outbreak in the world for more than 50 years. To find if the outbreak dates could be predicted by their one-dimension protein sequence, the mathematical model was needed to establish between them.Methods(A) collecting the outbreak dates of monkey-pox, smallpox, and coronavirus, determine the outbreak time interval between the pathogen strain and the reference strain SARS-CoV-2 D614, z. (B) detecting the one-dimension antigenic amino acid sequence of the pathogen strain to determine the super-antigens. (C) calculating the super-antigen precision, determining the increase amount in antigen precision between the pathogen strain and the reference strain, x; y represents the number of tryptophan (W) in the super-antigen. Introduction

It is well known that one-dimension protein sequence can determine the three-dimension (3D) of the protein. Three-dimension of protein will determine its function. The protein function, for sure, include the triggering endemic, epidemic, and even pandemic of a pathogen on an exact date in the human population on the Earth. We will try to establish a connection between one-dimension protein sequence and the outbreak dates of an endemic, epidemic, and even pandemic pathogen such as smallpox, monkey-pox, and coronavirus.

Since 1350 B.C., smallpox was outbreak with endemic, epidemic and pandemic. Unfortunately, there was not recorded with the exact date. Since 1970, monkey-pox, the last outbreak of smallpox, and coronavirus was outbreak in the world for more than 50 years. To find if the outbreak dates were correlated with their one-dimension protein sequence, or super-antigens, the mathematical model was needed to establish between them.

Super-antigen (SAg) [1] is a type of substance that can activate many T cell clones and generate a strong immune response at extremely low concentrations (≤ 10−9 M). The super-antigen played key role in anti-tumor practice. For example, some studies suggest that by modulating the expression of integrins and E-cadherin (contains super-antigens), tumor cells can adjust their dependency on the extracellular matrix (ECM), allowing them to escape immune detection and apoptosis, thus facilitating their colonization and growth in distant tissues [2]. Infectious viral pathogens are antigens, and antibodies are only produced when antigens invade the human body. There are protective antibodies and non-protective antibodies. Non protective antibodies can cause antibody dependent enhancement (ADE) effects. ADE refers to the phenomenon where a specific antibody, after binding to a pathogen, not only fails to neutralize the pathogen but also enhances its infectivity or aggravates pathological reactions. Non protective antibodies should include IgE [3]. The immune system includes antibody system and cell immune system, such as macrophages [4], the complex of antigen and antibody will be swallowed by macrophages. The tumor cell is very similar to virus, both can be transmitted from one site to another place [5]. Although DNA mutation is very important in other study [6], we focus amino acid mutation in this study. In our previous work, we found super-antigens were correlated with the outbreak dates of severe acute respiratory syndrome (SARS), SARS-CoV-2 D614, SARS-CoV-2 D614G, SARS-CoV-2 Delta and SARS-CoV-2 Omicron [7]. However, all such virus was belonging to corona virus family, are they apply to other virus family, for example orth poxvirus family? This need to be studied.

The aim of this study was to provide a method for predicting the dates of outbreak of the causative strain.

Pathogens are the viruses, bacteria and parasites causing outbreaks around the world. Some infectious agents can cause global pandemics, while others spread only endemic. For example, within the same orth poxvirus family, smallpox (Variola Virus/VARV) caused global pandemic. Monkey-pox caused African endemic before May 7, 2022, but became epidemic out of Africa on that day. Smallpox was the only human infectious disease eliminated by the vaccine from cowpox. We need to learn from smallpox and cowpox vaccine to eliminate monkey-pox, or even coronavirus. As well as pox family, in the same coronavirus family, the strains causing global pandemics were severe acute respiratory syndrome (SARS) strain and the Corona Virus Disease 2019 (COVID-19, or SARS-Cov-2) coronavirus strain. The Middle East Respiratory Syndrome (MERS) was only found endemic in the Middle East. How to identify epidemic strains and endemic strains is urgently needed and of great significance.

The model is applicable to other viruses (such as Ortho poxvirus). Marburg virus is another example to fit this mathematic model. The details are shown in the discussion section. The Marburg virus provide more evidence to strengthen this claim.

Existing technology can only identify epidemic strains of cholera groups O1 and O139 [8]. The existing technology can only be used for epidemic strains of Vibrio cholera and cannot identify other bacteria, nor can it identify epidemic strains and endemic strains of viruses.

The other aim of this study was to provide a method for identifying epidemic and endemic pathogen strains.

Materials and method

Collecting the outbreak dates of monkey-pox, smallpox, coronavirus, determine the outbreak time interval between the pathogen strain and the reference strain SARS-CoV-2 D614

In 1970, the Democratic Republic of Congo reported its first case of human monkey-pox infection. The patient was a 9-month-old boy who was the only member of the family not vaccinated against smallpox [9]. Before May 7, 2022, the monkey-pox was endemic only in Africa.

From February 16 to April 11, 1972, during smallpox epidemic in Yugoslavia, a total of 175 people fell ill, of which 35 (20%) died [10]. The time interval between February 16, 1972 to reference date December 12, 2019 is 573.862 months.

On November 16, 2002, SARS broke out in Guangdong Province, China [11].

On September 1, 2012, Middle East Respiratory Syndrome outbreak. The MERS coronavirus was first recorded in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA) in 2012, and by 2014, 180 cases were detected in two years with a death rate of 43% [12]. It was only endemic in Middle East.

On December 12, 2019, 47 years after the last smallpox outbreak, the first wave of COVID-19 was outbreak, caused by the one-dimension protein sequence, SARS-CoV-2 D614 primary super-antigen [13, 14].

However, on March 10, 2020, during the epidemic, after 2.935 months of stability, the D614 super-antigen did mutate to D614G and became dominant [15], and caused the second wave of SARS-CoV-2 pandemic.

In October 2020, after 10 months of stability, the other one-dimension protein sequence, N148 secondary super-antigen mutated into mutated N148 (E156del, F157del, R158G). These N148 mutations caused the SARS-CoV-2 Delta sub-type pandemic, the third wave of the SARS-CoV-2 pandemic in the world [16]. The original paper did not mention the exact outbreak date of SARS-CoV-2 Delta sub-type.

On November 23, 2021, after 23.367 months of stability, the third one-dimension protein sequence, I358 super-antigen finally mutated into the SARS-CoV-2 Omicron fragment, triggering the fourth wave of the SARS-CoV-2 pandemic. The amino acid sequence of protein 7QO9_A contains Omicron sub-type. Initial case of SARS-CoV-2 Omicron sub-type reported to begin pandemic in South Africa [17].

On May 7, 2022, during the COVID-19 or SARS-CoV-2 was in pandemic, the monkey-pox started epidemic in UK and to the global out from endemic in Africa [18].

Detecting the one-dimension antigenic amino acid sequence of the pathogen strain to determine the super-antigens

The sequence of protein AGR36452 contains cadherin-like domain protein in monkey-pox virus.

The sequence of protein AAA69465 contains cadherin-like domain protein in smallpox virus.

For explore the molecular mechanism of cowpox vaccine was success to protect human from smallpox and monkey-pox, the sequence of protein CAD90748 contains cadherin-like domain protein in cowpox virus was also analyzed.

The sequence of protein AAP72986 contains spike protein in wild SARS.

The sequence of protein QJX19948 contains the spike protein in wild MERS.

The amino acid sequence of the spike protein QHD43416 is in wild SARS-CoV-2 D614, leading the first wave COVID-19 or SARS-CoV-2 outbreak.

The amino acid sequence of protein "7KDK_A" contains mutated SARS-CoV-2 D614G. This was the second wave COVID-19 or SARS-CoV-2 outbreak.

The amino acid sequence of protein "7V8B_A" contains the SARS-CoV-2 Delta sub-type, with complete mutations of N148 (E156del, F157del, R158G), T19R, G142D, L452R, T478K, D614G, P681R and D950N. This was the third wave COVID-19 or SARS-CoV-2 outbreak.

The amino acid sequence of protein 7QO9_A contains the SARS-CoV-2 Omicron sub-type. This was the fourth wave SARS-CoV-2 coronavirus outbreak.

For negative control, the amino acid sequence of spike protein [Bat SARS coronavirus HKU3, ACCESSION QND76034] was used to represent the strain of never infected to human host.

For smallpox, cowpox, monkey-pox, and coronaviruses, the fourth longest amino acid peptide chain is the primary super-antigen, the third longest amino acid peptide chain is the secondary super-antigen, the secondary longest amino acid peptide chain is the tertiary super-antigen, and the longest amino acid peptide chain is the quaternary super-antigen.

Through global population studies, we identified 17 such candidate viral one-dimension super-antigens in smallpox, cowpox, and monkey-pox. All were isolated from global populations. The time intervals between global pandemics caused by them, were searched and analyzed among real viruses hosted and spread among all human populations around the world.

Results

Correlation among super-antigen stability z, antigenic precision increased amounts x, and the number of tryptophan (W) it contains in smallpox, monkey-pox and SARS-CoV-2, y

If x represents the increase in antigen precision of the super-antigen, y represents the number of tryptophan super-antigen contains, and z represents the stability of the super-antigen when meet to or from the reference strain, the regression equation is z = 13.762x2 − 109.376x − 63.290y + 221.197, with a correlation coefficient of R = 1.0000000, shown in Fig. 2. After statistical testing, the probability of class I errors occurring is P = 0.008. The increase in antigen precision of super-antigens (no matter whether they contain tryptophan W), as well as the amount of tryptophan in super-antigens, has a dose-dependent relationship with the stability of super-antigens, which is statistically significant.Fig. 2Dose-dependent relationship among stability of super-antigen z, antigenic precision increased amounts x, and the number of W the super-antigen contains y. There is a dose-dependent relationship among stability of super-antigen z, antigenic precision increased amounts x, and the number of W the super-antigen contains y. The probability of class I errors occurring is P = 0.008, which is statistically significant. The predicted outbreak time are consistent with the actual history. The function of super-antigens is transmitted from the primary to the secondary, and then to the tertiary. This programmatic propagation process lasted for 3.5 years, highly consistent with mathematical models. After our first paper about superantigen was published [29], some scholars questioned that our article was not confirmed by animal experiments. We also introduced the concept of evidence levels. The lowest level of evidence is in vitro, followed by ex vivo, in vivo. Animal experiments correspond to in vivo. Higher levels of evidence include phase 1, 2, 3, and 4 clinical trials. The highest level we mentioned is in population, which refers to whole-population trials. No matter how high the level of evidence is, it cannot negate the conclusions of higher-level evidence. Since higher-level evidence exists, lower-level evidence is of little significance. Internationally, in-population evidence is now referred to as Real-World Evidence (RWE).
