Retraction Note: Graphene-based LSPR sensors enable polarization-independent detection of CEA antigens
Authors: Yousef Rafighirani, Hamid Bahador, Javad Javidan
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-17108-9, published online 26 August 2025

The Editors have retracted this Article.

After publication, concerns were raised regarding the use of non‑standard terminology. Further investigation by the Editors identified inconsistencies in the reporting and description of the methodology, along with multiple instances where cited references did not support the corresponding claims. The Authors’ response did not adequately address these issues. A post‑publication expert review further confirmed misrepresentation in the equivalent‑circuit modeling and methodological discrepancies. The Editors no longer have confidence in the content of the Article.

Authors Yousef Rafighirani, Hamid Bahador and Javad Javidan disagree with this retraction.
