Correction to: Community composition and abundance of wild bees at row crop-grassland interfaces in west central Nebraska
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: Araceli Gomez Villegas, Hannah E Stowe, Abigail Lyons, Rachele Dailey, Julie A Peterson, Autumn Smart, Community composition and abundance of wild bees at row crop-grassland interfaces in west central Nebraska, Environmental Entomology, Volume 54, Issue 3, June 2025, Pages 632–643, https://doi.org/10.1093/ee/nvaf040

In the originally published version of this manuscript, typographical errors appeared in the following species names in Table 1:

Andrena hynesi

Andrena heilianthi

Andrena illinoinensis

Calliopsis nebrascensis

Perdita perpadilla

Diadasia envata

Florilegus condingus

Colletes sussanae

Hylaeus messillae sp. group

These concerns were also raised on PubPeer (see https://pubpeer.com/publications/4248D49DA5079B41B3F528F6592886).

The species names have been corrected in the online version of the article to read as follows:

Andrena haynesi

Andrena helianthi

Andrena illinoiensis

Calliopsis nebraskensis

Perdita perpallida

Diadasia enavata

Florilegus condignus

Colletes susannae

Hylaeus mesillae sp. group
