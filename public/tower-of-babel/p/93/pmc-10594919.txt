Following publication of the original article [1], we have been informed that the first and last names of the first two and last two authors have been switched.

Originally published: Pecqueux Mathieu, Distler Marius, Weitz Jürgen and Kahlert Christoph. Correct name tagging: Mathieu Pecqueux, Marius Distler, Jürgen Weitz, Christoph Kahlert

Also, there were originally five author affiliations declared for authors 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10:

1 Department of Visceral, Thoracic and Vascular Surgery, University Hospital Carl Gustav Carus, Technische Universität Dresden, Fetscherstrasse 74, Dresden 01307, Germany. 2 National Center for Tumor Diseases (NCT/UCC), Dresden, Germany. 3 German Cancer Research Center (DKFZ), Heidelberg, Germany. 4 Faculty of Medicine and University Hospital Carl Gustav Carus, Technische Universität Dresden, Dresden, Germany. 5 Helmholtz-Zentrum Dresden-Rossendorf (HZDR), Dresden, Germany.

Affiliations 2–5 were all condensed under affiliation 2.

The original article has been corrected.
