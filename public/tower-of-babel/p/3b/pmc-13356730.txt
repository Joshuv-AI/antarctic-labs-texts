With Gratitude and Pride: Reflections From the Editors-in-Chief
Authors: Vinod K Rustgi, Shanthi Srinivasan
Published: 2026

Dear American Gastroenterological Association, Colleagues, Editorial Team, Associate Editors, and our all-important readers:

As our tenure as Editors-in-Chief comes to a close on June 30, we write to express our deepest gratitude to each of you.

Serving this community over the past 5 years has been an immense privilege. Together, we have witnessed and contributed to a period of remarkable growth and achievement for the journal. Most notably, we successfully secured the journal’s inaugural Impact Factor, a milestone that reflects the collective efforts of our authors, reviewers, editorial team, and readers. Alongside this accomplishment, the journal has continued to strengthen its academic influence and growing visibility within the gastroenterology community.

The journal has demonstrated sustained global engagement, receiving submissions from 44 countries and experiencing steady growth in volume—from 200 submissions in 2022 to 329 in 2025.

Through the dedication of our Associate Editors and reviewers, we maintained an efficient editorial process, achieving an average turnaround time of approximately 9 days from submission to first decision. This commitment to timely, rigorous peer review has been central to our mission of serving both authors and readers.

We are equally proud of the diversity that has become a hallmark of the journal. Over the past 5 years, we have broadened the representation of our Editorial Board, Associate Editors, authors, and reviewers, ensuring that the journal reflects the global community it serves. This diversity of expertise, geography, perspectives, and experiences has strengthened the quality of the science we publish and enriched the conversations within our field.

The accompanying cover collage provides a visual snapshot of our 5-year journey as Editors-in-Chief. The covers showcase many of the important scientific contributions, educational initiatives, and clinical insights that have appeared in the journal during this period. For us, they represent far more than individual issues—they reflect the collective efforts of a dedicated community committed to advancing gastroenterology and improving patient care. We hope this collage serves as both a celebration of what has been accomplished and an inspiration for what lies ahead.

We would like to extend our special appreciation to our outstanding Associate Editors—Fredric D. Gordon, Heba Iskandar, Alyson McGhan Johnson, Joseph K. Lim, Ravinder K. Mittal, Maximilian Reichert, Deborah C. Rubin, Mohamed I. Elsaid, David Katzka, and Desmond Leddin. Their expertise, judgment, and unwavering commitment to scientific excellence have been instrumental to the journal’s success. Through their thoughtful stewardship of the peer-review process and dedication to maintaining the highest editorial standards, they have helped shape the quality, integrity, and impact of the journal.

None of these achievements would have been possible without the unwavering commitment of our Associate Editors, Editorial Board members, reviewers, authors, and the dedicated publishing team. Special thanks to our amazing Gastro Hep Advances team at American Gastroenterological Association, including Jami Jones, Samantha Curtin, and Dianne King-McGavin. Your professionalism, expertise, and generosity of time have helped shape the journal into a respected and thriving platform for scientific exchange.

As we step down, we are absolutely delighted to welcome Dr Anisa Shaker as the incoming Editor-in-Chief. She brings exceptional expertise, energy, and vision to the role, and we have the utmost confidence that the journal will continue to flourish and reach even greater heights under her leadership.

Thank you for your support, collaboration, and friendship throughout this journey. We leave our roles with enormous pride in what we have achieved together and great optimism for the journal’s future.

With our warmest regards,

Vinod K Rustgi and Shanthi Srinivasan

Outgoing Editors-in-Chief, 2021–2026
