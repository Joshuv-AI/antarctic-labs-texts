Correction: Norepinephrine exacerbates LPS-induced cardiomyopathy via SIRT3/HO-1 axis-mediated ferroptosis
Authors: Dan Ma, Weilun Fang, Lei Cai, Wei Li, Han Su
Published: 2026

Correction: Critical Care (2025) 29:354

10.1186/s13054-025-05602-5

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors identified errors in the Figs. 1E, 3B and 6 A. Both the incorrect and correct information is given hereafter.

Since large amounts of figures were captured in this research, some figures were wrongly grouped and used (HE staining of Fig. 1E (LPS + NE group), Fig. 6A (WT and LPS + NE group) and DHE staining of Fig. 3B (Ctrl group)). It happened in the preparation stage, we mistakenly put pictures of other mice into these indicated groups. Actually, wrong figures used in HE staining of LPS + NE group are the only mistaken one in positive group (LPS + NE group in Figs. 1E and 6A). The other mistakes all occurred in control group (Fig. 6A).

For the wrongly used figures of HE staining in LPS + NE group, though HE staining are more direct way to show cardiomyopathy for readers, the expression of β-MHC, α-SMA (WB) and EF% (functional test) could verify exacerbated cardiomyopathy in LPS + NE mice more accurately (raw pictures of WB were given at the very beginning). In addition, the synergestic effects of LPS and catecholamines has been verified by previous studies, and no mistaken figures were found in the parts associated with mechanisms of SIRT3/HO-1 mediated ferroptosis. Therefore, these wrongly used figures won’t affect the integrity and authenticity of conclusions in this study.
