Trends in the Prevalence Gap of the Early Recognition of Symptoms of Stroke Between Cities or Provinces, During 2014–2023
Published: 2024

In 2023, the age-standardized prevalence of the early recognition of symptoms of stroke among individuals aged ≥19 years was the highest in Jeju (75.6%) and the lowest in Sejong-si (49.5%). The prevalence gap in the early recognition of symptoms of stroke between the highest and lowest groups was 26.1%p, which increased as compared to 19.8%p in 2022 (Figure 1).
