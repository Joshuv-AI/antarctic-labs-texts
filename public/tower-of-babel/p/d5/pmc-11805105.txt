ABSTRACT
ABSTRACTTeam-based learning (TBL) involves students working together to solve problems or complete tasks. Active learning, collaboration, and critical thinking are promoted. TBL uses pre-class preparation, in-class team activities, and evaluations to improve course comprehension and application in a dynamic learning environment. Organise WhatsApp groups for TBL in Ayurveda courses by assigning facilitator, recorder, and timekeeper roles. Group discussion, case studies, sharing resources, and scheduling in-depth discussions to improve TBL by encouraging active engagement, good communication, and timely task completion. Faculty or moderators monitor WhatsApp groups, enforce rules, and facilitate discussions. WhatsApp group collaborative learning may improve academic performance because students gain peer interactions and different perspectives. It enhances learning by encouraging active participation and comprehension. Communication, teamwork, and critical thinking are taught to help students succeed in their careers.

INTRODUCTION

Team-based learning (TBL) is an instructional technique where students work collaboratively to solve issues or complete tasks. It encourages active learning, cooperation, and critical thinking. TBL combines pre-class preparation, in-class team activities, and evaluations to promote deeper comprehension and application of course information, resulting in a dynamic learning environment.[1] Collaborative learning and peer engagement in academic environments promote varied perspectives, develop critical thinking, and boost communication abilities. Students learn from one another’s experiences, which leads to a better grasp and retention of knowledge. Collaboration encourages teamwork, problem-solving, and social skills, all of which are necessary for success in both professional and personal commitments.[2]

WHATSAPP GROUP AS A LEARNING PLATFORM

Create WhatsApp groups for a modest number of students. Assign distinct responsibilities to each group member, such as discussion leader, note-taker, presenter, fact-checker, and timekeeper. Rotate these positions on a regular basis to ensure that everyone is actively involved. Assign one Ayurvedic-related topic for each week. This could refer to a certain concept, herb, ailment, treatment method, or research paper.[3]

Encourage structured discussions in which students share their knowledge of the subject, ask questions, and critically examine data. Use multimedia, such as photographs, videos, and documents, to improve comprehension. Incorporate problem based learning (PBL) scenarios in which students are assigned a case study or clinical scenario connected to Ayurveda. They can discuss diagnosis, treatment plans, and reasons as a group.[4]

Encourage peer assessment of assignments, presentations, and research projects using WhatsApp groups. This encourages positive feedback and collaborative learning. Share useful study materials, articles, research papers, and web resources with the group. Encourage pupils to critically assess sources and share their results. Use quizzes, polls, and short evaluations in WhatsApp groups to test comprehension and reinforce learning objectives. Regularly solicit students’ input on the WhatsApp group’s success as a learning tool. Make improvements based on their feedback to improve learning outcomes.[56]

BENEFITS OF TEAM-BASED LEARNING

TBL in Ayurveda education promotes critical thinking by involving students in complicated case studies and discussions. It enhances communication abilities through group activities and presentations. TBL develops cooperation skills as students work together to solve challenges, mirroring real-world Ayurvedic practice, where multidisciplinary teamwork is essential for holistic patient care.[7]

IMPLEMENTATION IN AYURVEDA EDUCATION

WhatsApp groups for TBL in Ayurveda courses can be organized by assigning various tasks such as facilitator, recorder, and timekeeper. Groups analyze case studies, share resources, and set aside time for in-depth discussions. Clear standards enable active engagement, effective communication, and timely task fulfilment, which improves the TBL experience.[8]

Creating study groups enables for resource sharing, case study discussion, and peer teaching, which promotes collaborative learning. Students gain insight from other viewpoints, develop their comprehension through discussions, and improve their teaching skills by explaining subjects to peers. This technique improves recall, critical thinking, and participation in educational settings. COLLABORATIVE LEARNING ACTIVITIES

WhatsApp groups can accommodate a variety of activities, including group conversations on course subjects, quizzes to test knowledge, case studies to apply learning, and peer evaluations to provide feedback. These activities encourage active learning, critical thinking, cooperation, and communication skills, which enhance the educational experience in Ayurveda and other disciplines.[9]

MONITORING AND EVALUATION

Faculty or moderators play an important role in WhatsApp groups by monitoring activities, ensuring guidelines are followed, and facilitating discussions. They guide, clarify, and promote active engagement. Moderators create a positive learning atmosphere, encourage participation, and guarantee that learning objectives are met in Ayurvedic education and other disciplines.[10]

Quizzes, peer assessments, and project presentations are examples of assessment methods that can be used to evaluate individual and group performance in WhatsApp. These tools assess information retention, teamwork, communication, and critical thinking. They provide feedback for improvement, increase accountability, and ensure that learning objectives are met in Ayurvedic education and beyond.[11]

OUTCOMES AND FUTURE SCOPE

Participating in collaborative learning activities using WhatsApp groups might boost academic performance because students gain from peer interactions and various perspectives. It improves the learning experience by encouraging active engagement and a deeper comprehension. Students also learn professional skills, including communication, teamwork, and critical thinking, which will help them succeed in their future employment.[12]

Ayurvedic education can benefit from the future expansion and integration of technology-enhanced learning, such as virtual laboratorys, interactive simulations, and online courses. This method encourages global access, innovation, and lifelong learning. Incorporating artificial intelligence (AI) and data analytics can tailor learning experiences, improve outcomes, and advance the global transmission of Ayurvedic knowledge.[13]

CONCLUSION

TBL WhatsApp groups for Ayurveda undergraduates promote critical thinking, peer learning, and active cooperation. Discussions, teamwork, resource sharing, and peer teaching improve understanding. WhatsApp TBL models improve Ayurveda student engagement, retention, and holistic learning.
