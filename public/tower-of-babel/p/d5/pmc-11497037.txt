Emergent Ferroelectric Nematic and Heliconical Ferroelectric Nematic States in an Achiral “Straight” Polar Rod Mesogen (Adv. Sci. 39/2024)
Authors: Hiroya Nishikawa, Daichi Okada, Dennis Kwaria, Atsuko Nihonyanagi, Motonobu Kuwayama, Manabu Hoshino, Fumito Araoka
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Heliconical Ferroelectric Nematic Phase The heliconical ferroelectric nematic phase is a new helielectric state that spontaneously satisfies both polar and chiral symmetry breaking. In article number 2405718 Hiroya Nishikawa, Fumito Araoka, and co‐workers realize the heliconical arrangement of “straight”, “rigid”, and “achiral” polar molecules in the ferroelectric nematic phase. This finding suggests that even straight achiral rod mesogen can induce left‐ or right‐handed twisted dipole structures through electric dipole interactions, resulting in chiral symmetry breaking.
