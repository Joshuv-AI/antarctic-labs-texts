Retraction Note: Enhanced human activity recognition in medical emergencies using a hybrid deep CNN and bi-directional LSTM model with wearable sensors
Authors: Nishanth Adithya Chandramouli, Sivaramakrishnan Natarajan, Amal H Alharbi, Subhash Kannan, Doaa Sami Khafaga, Sekar Kidambi Raju, Marwa M Eid, El-Sayed M El-kenawy
Published: 2025

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-82045-y, published online 28 December 2024

The Editors have retracted this Article because the validity of its content cannot be verified. Concerns were raised because of the nonstandard vocabulary used in the Article. Further investigation found discrepancies between the publicly available datasets used and their description in this Article. The Editors therefore no longer have confidence in the integrity of the data in this Article.

All Authors disagree with the retraction.
