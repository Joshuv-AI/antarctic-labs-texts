Abstract 28: Cord Blood Derived Macrophages Promote the Differentiation of Oligodendrocyte Precursor Cells
Authors: Li Xu, Shane O'Neil, Hyunjung Min, April Ozamiz, Joanne Kurtzberg, Anthony Filiano
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractIntroductionKrabbe Disease (KD) is a rare neurodegenerative disorder caused by a mutation in the gene encoding galactocerebrosidase (GALC). GALC deficiency leads to accumulation of the toxic metabolite psychosine in myelin-forming oligodendrocytes causing severe demyelination. Presymptomatic hematopoietic stem cell transplants can slow the progression of disease, but ultimately there is no cure. We previously demonstrated that DUOC-01, a first-generation macrophage cell-therapy manufactured from banked human umbilical cord blood cells, which when injected into the corpus callosum, enhanced remyelination in immunocompromised mice treated with the demyelinating agent, cuprizone. Further, DUOC-01 could promote the maturation of oligodendrocyte precursor cells (OPCs) into mature oligodendrocytes via remyelinating factors (Remy-Macs).ObjectivesIn this study, we set out to test if DUOC-01 and their secreted Remy-Macs could rescue oligodendrocyte deficits and improve survival in GALC deficient mice.MethodsFirst, we generated primary OPC cultures from GALC deficient mice and measured OPC maturation by immunohistochemistry and qRT-PCR of myelin basic protein (MBP). To test if DUOC-01 impacted OPCs via secreted factors, we incorporated the use of transwell assays. To test if DUOC-01 or Remy-Macs could promote remyelination in vivo, we injected DUOC-01 or used Alzet osmotic pumps to deliver therapies into the cerebrospinal fluid (CSF).ResultsWe first tested the effects of DUOC-01 and Remy-Macs on primary OPC cultures isolated from neonatal control of GALC deficient mice. We observed comparable oligosphere growth in cultures derived from GALC deficient mice and their wild-type litter mates, indicating a normal function for twitcher OPC proliferation. However, OPC differentiation was compromised in GALC deficient mice, demonstrated by decreased MBP expression. DUOC-01 and Remy-Macs increased the production of MBP at both mRNA and protein levels. Additionally, infusion of Remy-Macs into the CSF of GALC deficient mice improved survival.DiscussionOur data demonstrated that DUOC-01 or Remy-Macs promoted the differentiation of OPCs isolated from GALC deficient mice. Further, infusing Remy-Macs into the CSF of GALC deficient mice extended life span. Our data suggest DUOC-01 or Remy-Macs have the potential to be further developed into a novel cell-therapy to treat Krabbe and other demyelinating diseases.
