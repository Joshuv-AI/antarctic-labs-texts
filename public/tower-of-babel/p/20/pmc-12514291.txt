Distributed storage database system for motion data based on blockchain technology
Authors: Peng Wang, Zhizhi Zhao
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractAs the importance of sports data in health management increases, traditional centralized storage faces security and privacy protection issues. Blockchain technology provides a new solution for the secure storage and sharing of sports data through its decentralized and tamper-proof characteristics. This paper constructs a distributed storage database system for sports data on the blockchain, which realizes the decentralized storage and sharing of data by storing sports data on the blockchain. The hash algorithm is used to store sports data to ensure the security and reliability of the data. The system uses smart contract technology to achieve multi-party secure computing and access control, ensuring that only users with access rights can access sports data. Experimental results show that the blockchain-based system is superior to traditional database systems in terms of storage efficiency (91% vs. 81%), data integrity (99.5% vs. 97.7%), and query response time (5.0ms vs. 9.9ms). The contribution of this paper is to provide an innovative solution for the secure storage and sharing of sports data, which can effectively protect the personal data privacy of athletes and support efficient data query and management.Keywords: Database system, Blockchain technology, Motion data, Distributed storageSubject terms: Environmental sciences, Environmental social sciences

Introduction

With rising health awareness and widespread adoption of wearable devices, motion data (such as steps and heart rate) has become critical for health management and sports monitoring. However, centralized storage systems face challenges like single-point failures, data tampering risks, and privacy breaches. Blockchain technology offers a decentralized, tamper-resistant solution for distributed motion data storage, ensuring consistency through multi-node data distribution and consensus mechanisms1. By leveraging smart contracts for access control and encryption for privacy protection, blockchain-based systems enable secure data sharing while maintaining reliability via synchronized updates and traceable history records, addressing key limitations of traditional architectures2,3.

In professional team sports, collecting and analyzing athlete monitoring data is a common practice, aimed at assessing fatigue and subsequent adaptation reactions, examining performance potential, and minimizing the risk of injury and illness. The athlete monitoring system should be based on appropriate data analysis and interpretation to quickly report simple, scientific, and effective feedback. Due to the suddenness exhibited in scientific applications, distributed/parallel file systems often encounter issues of load imbalance and resource contention. Liao Jianwei proposed an adaptive scheme that supported dynamic block data replication and an effective replica placement strategy to improve the performance of distributed file systems4. Sports is one of the most important aspects of daily life. With the rise of this field, it requires better prospects, infrastructure, and most importantly, management. Kumar Gaurav created a system that combined sports management and technology. Both schools and colleges had students who wished to perform in various sports events at the inter college, state, and national levels5. However, these scholars lack some technical arguments for exploring motion data storage systems. Through research, it has been found that blockchain technology is more helpful for motion data storage systems. Relevant literature on blockchain technology has been referred.

With the rapid increase in the deployment of Internet of Things (IoT) devices, storing and protecting a large amount of IoT data has become a major issue. The traditional cloud based IoT architecture poses extremely high computing and storage requirements for cloud servers6,7. Meanwhile, the strong dependence on centralized servers poses significant trust issues. To alleviate these issues, Li Ruinian proposed a distributed data storage scheme using blockchain and unverified passwords. This solution eliminated traditional centralized servers by utilizing blockchain miners who performed “transaction” verification and record auditing with the help of uncertified encryption8. Encryption in blockchain refers to a technical method that makes it difficult for unauthorized resources to access encrypted data. Ghazal Taher M. proposed a blockchain based encryption framework to provide secure solutions using computational intelligence methods9. However, these scholars have not conducted research on distributed storage database systems for motion data based on blockchain technology, only exploring them from a superficial perspective.

Although many studies in recent years have attempted to store motion data through traditional centralized database systems, these systems often face serious limitations. First, centralized systems are vulnerable to single point failures. Once there is a problem with the database server, the security and integrity of the data are threatened. Second, the centralized storage model cannot effectively solve the problem of data tampering. The immutability of data is crucial, especially in the recording and analysis of motion data.

This study constructs a distributed motion data storage database system based on blockchain technology, aiming to solve the limitations of traditional centralized database systems in data security, privacy protection, and high availability. Compared with existing centralized storage systems, blockchain technology provides a new and secure storage solution for motion data through the characteristics of decentralization, immutability, and encryption protection. In addition, the smart contract and multi-party secure computing mechanism proposed in this paper ensure data access control, further improving the security and reliability of the data storage system.

Distributed storage method for motion data based on blockchain technology

Blockchain technology and distributed storage of motion data

The provided document outlines a blockchain-based distributed storage database system for motion data, addressing challenges in traditional centralized systems such as single points of failure, data tampering risks, and privacy vulnerabilities. Blockchain technology, characterized by decentralization, immutability, and cryptographic security, enables decentralized storage and secure sharing of motion data (such as steps, heart rate, and velocity) across multiple nodes. Smart contracts enforce access control and multi-party secure computing, ensuring only authorized users can access data while maintaining audit trails. Data integrity is preserved through SHA-256 hash algorithms, generating unique identifiers for each data entry to detect tampering. Experimental results demonstrate the blockchain-oriented system achieves 91% storage efficiency, 99.5% data integrity, and an average Query response time of 5.0ms, outperforming traditional databases (81% efficiency, 97.7% integrity, 9.9ms latency). While blockchain systems exhibit higher write latency compared to traditional databases, they offer superior scalability (1 PB storage capacity vs. 0.5 PB), privacy protection via encryption/anonymization, and fault tolerance. Applications include sports monitoring, health management (secure patient data sharing among doctors/insurers), and sports competitions (tamper-proof performance records). The system balances advantages in security, consistency, and traceability against trade-offs in write performance, necessitating scenario-specific adoption. Future optimization focuses on enhancing scalability and performance while leveraging blockchain’s strengths in decentralized, secure data ecosystems.

Database design of distributed storage of motion data for blockchain technology

Database system requirements

The database design of distributed storage of motion data for blockchain technology needs to meet the following requirements:

Scalability: the database system needs to support distributed storage and multi-node deployment, and it is able to achieve horizontal and vertical scaling12,13.

High availability and fault tolerance: database systems need to have high availability and fault tolerance, which can ensure data security and reliability in the event of node failures or network congestion14,15.

High performance: database systems need to have high performance, and it is able to support high throughput and low latency data access16,17.

Data privacy and security: database systems need to have the ability to protect user privacy and data security, providing functions such as identity verification, access control, and data encryption18,19.

Data consistency and traceability: database systems need to ensure data consistency and traceability, that is, to ensure synchronization of data between different nodes and historical records of data changes20,21.

Overall design of database system

Based on the above requirements, this article designs a motion data distributed storage database system for blockchain technology.

The overall design of the system includes the following aspects:

Data storage: decentralized data storage is achieved based on blockchain technology, where motion data is dispersed and stored on multiple blockchain nodes, with each node having a local copy. Utilizing smart contract technology can achieve multi-party secure computing, access control, and data encryption functions, thereby ensuring the security and privacy of data.

Data synchronization: adopting consensus mechanisms and other technologies ensures the consistency and reliability of motion data, updating data copies of all nodes simultaneously when data changes and achieving data traceability and historical recording.

Identity verification and access control: utilizing technologies such as digital signature and public/private key encryption implements identity verification and access control mechanisms, ensuring that only users with access rights can access motion data.

Data query and analysis: using tools such as blockchain browsers queries and analyzes motion data, including functions such as displaying, filtering, and summarizing data, providing data support for athletes, coaches, and doctors.

Under this system’s security model, replay attacks are highly unlikely to succeed in this scenario because each request includes a timestamp and a one-time random number. The system can identify and reject any duplicate messages, making them virtually impossible to attack. Forgery attacks rely on the digital signature mechanism that requires attackers to generate valid signatures for verification, a forgery impossible under existing cryptographic assumptions. Data replacement attacks rely on hash functions and on-chain records, making any changes immediately detectable. Therefore, attackers cannot replace data without being detected. Collusion attacks rely on the blockchain-based consensus mechanism, which requires multiple independent nodes to jointly confirm the data. When the proportion of malicious nodes is insufficient to reach the consensus threshold, the likelihood of coordinated tampering is extremely low. In summary, under the assumption that existing cryptographic and blockchain consensus assumptions hold, the success rates of these attacks can be considered negligible in this system, ensuring the system’s probabilistic security.

Experiment on distributed storage database system for motion data

Experimental design

(1) Experimental purpose: this article aims to evaluate the performance differences between blockchain technology oriented distributed storage database systems for motion data and traditional database systems in key indicators, to help understand the advantages and disadvantages of blockchain technology in the field of motion data storage and provide reference basis for system selection.

(2) Preparation of experimental environment: a distributed storage database system for motion data oriented towards blockchain technology is set, deploying traditional database systems, such as relational database or NoSQL (Not only Structured Query Language) database. The same hardware devices are configured, including servers, storage devices, and network environment.

(3) Data preparation: motion datasets are prepared, including different types of motion data, such as step count, heart rate, calories, and so on, and the datasets are input into blockchain technology oriented systems and traditional database systems to ensure data consistency.

(4) Selection of experimental indicators: appropriate indicators are selected for experimental evaluation, such as storage space utilization, storage speed, data integrity, data privacy protection, security, data query response time, etc.

Storage space utilization: the same motion dataset in blockchain technology oriented systems and traditional database systems is stored, and the storage space utilization of the two systems are calculated, compared, and analyzed.

Storage speed: data write operations are performed in blockchain technology oriented systems and traditional database systems, and the average latency of data write is measured, recording the results. Read operations are performed, and the average latency of data read is measured.

Data integrity: for the two systems, data integrity is verified by modifying part of the data set or simulating data tampering, and the ability of the two systems to protect data integrity is analyzed, including data validation, anti tampering measures, etc.

Data privacy protection: experimental cases are designed, including storage and query operations of sensitive data. The privacy protection capabilities of blockchain technology oriented systems and traditional database systems for sensitive data are evaluated, including encryption, anonymization, and other measures.

Security: simulating unauthorized access or attacks, the security performance of blockchain technology oriented systems and traditional database systems is evaluated, including evaluation of identity verification, access control, and attack prevention.

Data query response time: a series of query operations are designed, including simple and complex queries, involving different data fields and aggregation operations, and the response time of the two systems to queries is measured, compared, and analyzed.

(5) Experimental operation and data collection: various operations are performed in the experimental design, recording experimental data. For each experimental indicator, eight repeated experiments are conducted to obtain stable results.

(6) Data analysis and result evaluation: statistical analysis on experimental data is conducted to compare the performance differences between blockchain technology oriented systems and traditional database systems in each indicator. Analyzing the reasons for differences can consider factors such as system architecture, data processing methods, and algorithms. The advantages and disadvantages of the two systems based on the experimental results are evaluated, providing targeted optimization and selection suggestions.

Discussion

This study proposes a distributed storage system for sports data based on blockchain technology and experimentally verifies its advantages in storage efficiency, data integrity, security, and query response time. Compared with traditional centralized database systems, blockchain technology provides a decentralized and tamper-proof storage solution for sports data, improves data security and reliability, and also provides a new solution for data privacy protection. The results show that the system oriented to blockchain technology has advantages in data integrity, storage space utilization, and data query response time. However, although this study has demonstrated the great potential of blockchain technology in sports data storage, there are still some limitations and challenges that deserve further exploration.

First, although the blockchain system in this study performs well in data storage efficiency and integrity, its write latency is relatively high, which may lead to performance bottlenecks in application scenarios that require high-frequency write operations. Although the write latency of the current system is lower than that of the traditional centralized storage system, it still fails to achieve the real-time performance required for large-scale, high-frequency data processing. Therefore, future research can focus on optimizing the consensus mechanism of blockchain technology, exploring more efficient consensus algorithms, reducing network latency, and thus improving the response speed and real-time processing capabilities of the system.

Secondly, this study assumes that the participants can trust the security of the blockchain network. In the data storage and sharing involving multiple parties, the trust model may be challenged. Although the blockchain itself has strong anti-tampering capabilities, in some scenarios, how to ensure the honesty of all nodes and how to deal with potential malicious nodes are issues that this study has not fully addressed. Therefore, future research should focus on designing more flexible and diverse trust models, especially in a multi-party collaborative environment, to ensure that the system can cope with the risks of malicious attacks and node failure.

In addition, the storage capacity problem of existing blockchain technology is also a challenge that cannot be ignored. Although the blockchain system has the advantages of decentralization and high fault tolerance, the storage pressure of the blockchain network increases with the increase in data volume. Especially in the scenario of motion data, as the amount of data increases, storage efficiency and query performance may be subject to certain limitations. The blockchain system in this study still has a certain gap in storage capacity compared with traditional databases. Therefore, future research can explore how to further optimize the storage mechanism of the blockchain and improve its performance in large-scale data storage scenarios.

Conclusions

This study proposes a distributed storage system for sports data based on blockchain technology. Experimental results show that the system is significantly superior to traditional centralized database systems in terms of storage space utilization, data integrity, query response time, etc. In particular, blockchain technology provides a safer and more reliable solution for decentralized data storage and privacy protection. Although this system has certain limitations in write latency and storage capacity, its advantages in data security and consistency make it have important application prospects in sports data storage and sharing.
