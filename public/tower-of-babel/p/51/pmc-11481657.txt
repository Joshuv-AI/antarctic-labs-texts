Research perspectives and trends in Artificial Intelligence-enhanced language education: A review
Authors: Lu Zheng, Yong Yang
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractArtificial Intelligence (AI) has had a significant impact on language education, arousing the related studies from different fields and perspectives. However, the extant review articles only focused on a certain aspect in the field, thus neglecting the multi-disciplinary and multi-perspective features of AI-enhanced language education. The aim of the current study is to bridge this gap by reviewing the 812 articles selected from Web of Science via CiteSpace. The study found that the existing studies were mainly conducted from the perspectives of computer science, linguistics and psychology. Besides, the individualized development of language education supported by technology was not obvious, and the degree of human-computer interaction was weak. Moreover, previous studies focused more on the advantages of AI-enhanced language education than the disadvantages, and the empirical research to analyze specific problems was insufficient. In the future, the research tends to strengthen the cooperation among related disciplines and focus on the individualized development of language education. In addition, empirical research should be strengthened to explore the efficiency of AI-enhanced language education in order to promote the reform of language education.Keywords: Multi-perspective features, Language education innovation, Second language learning, Corpus construction, Learner's cognition, Individualized development, Human-computer interaction

Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems or techniques that mimic human intelligence, such as visual or audio recognition, data analysis, data-based judgment or decision making, and machine learning techniques that allow computers to continuously improve their intelligence [1]. With the continuous progress of computer technology and the continuous maturity of AI, education researchers and practitioners are eager to use AI technology to enable the construction of education system, thus solving the problems in teaching and comprehensively promoting education reform [2]. Therefore, teaching and learning based on AI has become an inevitable trend.

At the very beginning, AI was almost applied in general education [3], with just a few studies focusing on AI in language education [[4], [5], [6]]. However, the studies on AI-enhanced language education attracted more and more scholars’ attention and substantial articles were published when Intelligent Computer-assisted Language Learning (ICALL) technology, as a new branch of Computer-assisted Language Learning (CALL), integrated natural language processing (NLP) and AI modeling techniques into CALL, providing a prerequisite for AI language education [7]. Accordingly, the challenges of AI-enhanced language education arose: Grimes and Warschauer [8] doubted the Automated Writing Evaluation (AWE) accuracy as it might not correctly evaluate the subjective features of natural languages. Similarly, some doubted the accuracy of evaluating Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) as students might not have smooth interactions with the Non-Player Characters (NPC) since ASR performance varied across contexts [9].

In order to cater to and cope with the research issues and possible challenges of global language education in the digital age, scholars and educators around the world have made beneficial exploration and practice. Under the new form of modern language education technology, the deep integration of information technology with language education is an urgently hot topic in language education. This integration makes AI-enhanced language education form a multi-disciplinary and multi-perspective research field, which has attracted the attention of scholars from different fields and achieved fruitful research results (The latest advances will be discussed in detail from relevant perspectives in the section “Research perspectives”). A small quantity of scholars has recently reviewed and analyzed specific topics on AI-enhanced language education, yielding four similar literature reviews. Methods

According to Hwang & Tsai [13] and Zou et al. [14], it is significant to review articles based on high-quality publications. The search was performed from the database Web of Science. The period defined for the search was from Jan. 1, 2000 to Dec. 31, 2022, with the application of Boolean logic (“artificial intelligence” or “AI”) AND (“language education” or “language teaching” or “language learning” or “language instruction” or “language tutoring”). To ensure both quality and accuracy, only peer-reviewed journal papers with full text available were included. This paper established the following inclusion and exclusion criteria (Table 2), and reviewed the articles obtained from the search to determine whether it was eligible for analysis.

Research perspectives (RQ 1)

According to the analysis above, we divided the studies on AI-enhanced language education into the three perspectives of computer science, linguistics and psychology, and reviewed the research foci on AI-enhanced language education from these three perspectives.

The perspective of computer science

The computer science perspective of AI-enhanced language education mainly explores the innovation of language education supported by computer technology. AI-enhanced language education originated from Computer-assisted Instruction (CAI) developed in 1950s. Universities and computer companies were the main research forces during this period, focusing on exploring computer hardware, system software and course software, and their research results were mainly applied to general education [7]. In the 1970s, the application of CAI was expanded and tended to be practical, including the development and application of language education and research. Among them, CALL was considered as a milestone in the development of the language education supported by computer technology, and played a crucial role in stimulating the interest of students in language learning and the realization of personalized learning.

As computer technology continues to advance, the progress in intelligent speech recognition system, natural language parsing system and machine learning technology has refined computer-enhanced language learning, making it smarter and more precise [11]. Thus, the development of ICALL was facilitated. It integrates NLP techniques and AI modeling techniques into CALL, forming a new branch of CALL. ICALL provides the prerequisite for AI-enhanced language education and plays an important role in the development of language education. In the following decades, ICALL has achieved remarkable results in second language acquisition and foreign language teaching [17,18]. However, this system has great limitations in the interaction with students, for instance, it is difficult for the computer to have bidirectional interaction with students.

With the advent of the artificial intelligence era in the 21st century, researchers have begun to explore more intelligent language education tools – “robots”, significantly surpassing the limitations of ICALL and ushering in the era of Robot-Assisted Language Learning (RALL). For instance, Kim et al. [17] designed and developed the chatbot “Ellie” based on the second language acquisition theory and the research results of chatbots. Besides, Chat GPT developed by Open AI in the United States represented one of the most advanced technologies which promoted the development of language education. The development of RALL technology has changed the practical model of CALL and has shown great potential in promoting the development of language education.

The perspective of linguistics

The linguistic perspective of AI-enhanced language education focuses on the role of AI and its related technologies in second language learning and corpus construction. The development of intelligent speech recognition, natural language analysis and deep learning technology has promoted the development and utilization of speech recognition system, natural language processing system and machine deep learning system [19]. These systems enable language learners to better memorize vocabulary, analyze grammar and understand texts in second language learning. Jia et al. [20] explained the design, development, evaluation, and validation of Artificial Intelligence in English Language Learning (AIELL) based on the above technologies. They believed that such a system can be used to acquire the skills of listening, specking, reading and writing in a real context, and can evaluate learners' learning results, which greatly improved the reading and writing ability of English learners. According to second language acquisition theory, English learners' reading and writing ability has a great influence on their spoken English and language learning skills. Based on this, Dong et al. [21] applied the Multiple Criteria Decision Making system (MCDM) to English multimodal online reading based on artificial intelligence. At the same time, their research also provided an effective decision-making tool for language learning experiments.

In addition, with the continuous progress of computer technology, various language teaching systems emerge one after another, and language researchers begin to pay attention to which teaching system can provide a more efficient teaching model. Hang et al. [22] believed that an efficient teaching system was capable of saving teachers and students’ energy and time, and improving teaching efficiency. In order to enable language learners to choose an efficient AI teaching system, they used the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) and Technique for Order Preference by Similarity to Ideal Solution optimization (TOPSIS) to rank the AI teaching system. The order was as follows: Natural language analysis system, expert system, machine learning, speech recognition system, etc. Teachers and students participating in language teaching activities can choose the appropriate system and teaching mode according to their needs, thus greatly improving the efficiency of AI-assisted language teaching.

Corpus development is also a crucial research topic for AI-enhanced language education. With the continuous demand of language teaching and research, it is imperative to collect, analyze and apply massive data. A large number of scholars have devoted themselves to the exploration and research of corpus, thus giving birth to the technology of corpus development. The application of corpora freed language learners from the mechanical memory of formulaic language and multiword sequences, and saved words and phrases in modular form for retrieval and processing as a whole [10].

However, some scholars had doubts about the validity of corpora, for instance, Sag et al. [23] believed that multi-word sequences were scattered and had no rules to follow, which made it impossible for machines to accurately locate and analyze them. Therefore, corpora had great difficulties in processing such language forms. In order to solve this problem, researchers connected language nodes together by weighted algorithms and language markers, and created an AI neural network that simulated the human brain system. This network system can analyze the regular structure of words and phrases and predict speech sequences, so as to generate language forms that meet the needs of use [24]. At present, the leading player in the development of AI neural network systems is the Generative Pre-trained Transformer (GPT) developed by open AI in the United States. GPT has massive corpus storage capabilities and powerful natural language analysis capabilities, which can generate human-like words, phrases, sentences and even longer language forms in real language environments, greatly promoting the development of language research and teaching.

The perspective of psychology

It's evident that learners' cognition, emotions, behavioral intentions, and thinking mode undergo significant changes as technology advances and AI-based teaching systems are increasingly integrated into language education. This has drawn widespread attention from scholars. Among them, the cognitive and emotional studies occupy a large proportion. For instance, in the investigation of the application of artificial intelligence in second language learning, it is found that language learners' emotional and cognitive levels of AI learning platform have a great impact on the application and popularization of AI teaching platform in language education [2]. This study is of great significance in the interactional harmony between human and AI in language education environment.

However, the AI-enhanced language teaching system has great limitations in dealing with learners' emotional discomfort and developing empathy strategies. To solve this problem, Zhai & Wibowo [12] reviewed the existing literature on AI-enhanced language learning and examined the development of empathic strategies to improve language learners’ learning outcomes. The research results showed that cultural background, empathy and emotional attitude of language learners had a significant impact on the development of empathic strategies. At the same time, various AI learning systems, including chatbots, should also be improved according to learners' cognitive and emotional status, so as to promote the benign development of AI-enhanced language learning.

In addition to cognition and emotion, the behavioral intention and thinking mode of teachers and students in language education are also important factors affecting the developmental effectiveness of AI-enhanced language education. An et al. [25] investigated the cognition, knowledge and behavioral intention of middle school English teachers in using AI to assist English teaching. Using Technological Pedagogical Content Knowledge (TPACK) and Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology (UTAUT) as theoretical frameworks, they conducted a survey in China's AI-educational demonstration zones. Their research mapped out the complex interrelationships between the various elements of education and provided theoretical options for English educators and policymakers to promote teachers' better use of AI for language instruction. With regard to thinking mode, Liu et al. [26] proposed an AI-supported English Writing (AIEW) approach based on a reflective thinking promotion mechanism to deepen learners' thinking and improve the quality of learners’English writing, and investigated the effectiveness of this AI-based learning approach through experiments. They believed that the proposed method not only significantly improved the English writing performance of the experimental group students, but also improved their self-efficacy and self-regulation ability and significantly reduced their cognitive load. The above psychological factors, such as emotion, cognition, behavioral intention and thinking mode, determine to a large extent the teachers' and students' views, evaluation and use efficiency of AI-enhanced language teaching, and have an important impact on promoting the development of various AI-related language teaching systems.

The limitations of the extant studies

AI-enhanced language education has experienced a development process from CALL to RALL, and its exploration involves a variety of research perspectives and has achieved fruitful research results. However, the development is still dominated by the related technologies of computer science, and the participation degree of related disciplines in language education is not high. In addition, the degree of human-computer interaction in language education is weak, the “spiritual communication” between human and machine has not been realized in the true sense, and the recognition and perception of human mental state by machine is not sensitive. The individualized development of AI-enhanced language education is not obvious from other disciplines. In terms of the effectiveness and applicability of AI-enhanced language education, the emphasis is placed more on the discussion of its advantages than the disadvantages, which greatly affects the objectivity of the evaluation. What's more, the empirical research used to analyze specific problems is weak and the sample is too small, which affects the reliability and validity of the research to a certain extent.

The trends of future research (RQ 2)

Research foci, limitations and trends.

We examined the findings and focal points of AI-enhanced language education from the perspectives of computer science, linguistics and psychology, and assessed the limitations of extant research. In light of these limitations, the article forecasts future research trends in three key aspects (see Fig. 7), which are elaborated below:

First, strengthen multidisciplinary cooperation to promote the development of intelligent language education system.

At present, computer science is still the main force in the development of intelligent technology for language education without the support of language and education disciplines, which is not conducive to the healthy development of intelligent language education. In the future, AI-enhanced language education needs to strengthen multidisciplinary cooperation and promote the integration of computer science, neuroscience, linguistics, pedagogy and psychology, so as to make intelligent language education more targeted and effective [18,27]. First of all, experts in the related areas work together to combine speech recognition, natural language analysis, neural networks, deep learning and other technologies with linguistics, pedagogy, psychology and other related disciplines to realize the upgrading of natural language analysis, corpus construction and emotion recognition technologies. Second, technical experts, language researchers and front-line language teaching practitioners collaborate across borders to keep theory guide practice and test theory in practice [28], providing teachers and students with an effective and friendly language learning system. Finally, on the basis of technological improvement, the forms and applications of language education tend to be expanded to benefit more language lovers and learners.

Second, promote the individualized development of language education and enhance human-computer interaction ability.

The barriers to enhancing language education through artificial intelligence are steadily diminishing as artificial intelligence technology continues to advance [29]. However, the research and development of AI language learning system has not yet taken the particularity of language education into account, which leads to the fact that language education has not yet achieved “intelligence” in a real sense to a certain extent [11]. Linguists, educators and psychologists are apt to lead the design of the curriculum framework, and then experts in the field of artificial intelligence are supposed to support the intelligent system according to the characteristics of the language curriculum, so as to produce language learning system with strong adaptability and personalization.

According to the relevant articles reviewed [1,6,18,21,25,[30], [31], [32], [33], [34]], the concrete steps are summarized as follows: To begin with, promote the integration of linguistic theory and AI technology. This requires linguistic and AI experts to cooperate and deeply integrate morphological theory, second language acquisition mechanism, universal grammar, functional grammar and language teaching method with speech recognition technology, natural language processing technology, deep learning and corpus development technology to make it conform to the law of language teaching [35,36], so as to enable language learners to learn the rules of vocabulary formation, sentence structure analysis, semantic logic and other language knowledge in a targeted and efficient way in the massive corpus. Moreover, the integration of language education theory and AI technology should be strengthened. Therefore, in order to make the learning system conform to the development law of learners with different classes and different needs, and enhance the pertinence and efficiency of the language learning system [37], it is necessary to develop AI learning tools under the guidance of language teaching laws, principles and methods. Finally, promote the integration of psychological theory and AI technology. For a long time, the development of AI learning systems has ignored the psychological factors of language learners, and most learning systems cannot accurately identify learners’ tone, emotion, attitude, etc., and cannot fully understand and interpret learners' needs, resulting in poor human-computer interaction [38,39]. Therefore, the future development of AI learning systems should be guided by cognitive, emotional and other psychological mechanisms, take learners as the center, and care for learners' interests, emotions, development needs and other factors displayed in the classroom, so as to better promote human-computer interaction and enhance the learning effect of language learners.

Third, the reform of language education should be promoted by shifting from the discussion of advantages to objective evaluation and strengthening the empirical research.

Technology supported language education has experienced decades of development. Although its advantages are obvious, its shortcomings are also worthy of attention [28,40], such as: the popularity and necessity in remote areas and the acceptance degree in conditional areas. Language education in these regions is still dominated by traditional offline education, which also reflects the development limitations of AI-enhanced language education [7]. This new teaching mode needs to be carried out under the environment of computer and network, and requires high computer technology. Therefore, for teachers and students in remote areas, this is a difficult problem to solve. In addition, teachers need to take time to persist in learning the use of AI learning platforms and develop teaching plans for the new mode in the case of heavy teaching tasks, which undoubtedly brings a huge burden to teachers.

Even in areas where conditions are satisfied, teachers and students' attitudes towards intelligent language education are not consistent [7,11]. The previous studies mainly focused on theoretical discussion, and empirical research was weak because of small research samples, which affected the reliability and validity to a certain extent. This requires the future research to establish an effective analysis model under the guidance of macro theory, carry out targeted field investigations, enlarge the size of research objects, produce effective data, and strengthen the universality and persuasiveness of the research. In areas and schools where conditions permit, the field investigations, such as collecting teaching journals, interviewing teachers and students, understanding the feelings expressed by teachers and students, are conducted to analyze students’ learning performance and thinking mode. Then, on the basis of the research data, specific recommendations are formed and provided to local education administration to effectively promote the use of AI teaching and learning systems in various schools.

However, compared with the progress of science and technology, the development of language education is lagging behind [2,22,41]. For example, in China, especially in primary and secondary schools, language education mainly focuses on the teaching of general language knowledge, thus the cultivation of language ability and quality is insufficient. The strength of people's language ability directly affects people's comprehensive quality and all-round development ability, and should be included in people's core quality education [41]. Therefore, the reform of language education is urgent. In the AI era, we are supposed to update the concept of language education, change the educational views, enrich the teaching content, effectively use intelligent tools, and increase the intensity of language education research to meet the requirements of the digital society in all aspects. The development and promotion of AI language teaching system has brought opportunities for the innovation of the language education. We should make full use of its advantages, effectively avoid its risks, and rationally use AI language teaching system to gradually promote the reform of language education.

Conclusion

Overall findings

AI-related technologies have been gradually integrated into language education and language research, and have had a significant impact on language teaching and research models. In order to make clear the research foci in this field, we reviewed 812 articles selected from the literature database Web of Science via CiteSpace. Through analysis, we found that AI-enhanced language education was mainly explored from the perspectives of computer science, linguistics and psychology. The perspective of computer science mainly explored the innovation of language education supported by computer technology; the linguistic perspective focused on the role of AI and its related technologies in second language learning and corpus construction; the psychological perspective concentrated on learners’ cognition, emotion, behavioral intention and thinking mode in AI-enhanced language learning.

The extant studies have their own limitations such as the lack of cooperation among related disciplines, the weakness of empirical research, more discussions on its advantages than the disadvantages of AI-enhanced language education though the fruitful research results are achieved. Based on the achievements and limitations, we forecasted that multidisciplinary cooperation, enhancement of empirical research and individualized development of language education were the trends for future research. In the context of the digital age, we are supposed to actively and rationally treat these trends, fully enjoy the benefits, appropriately avoid the demerits, and confidently and freely enter the "human-machine symbiosis" language society based on artificial intelligence.

Limitations of the current study and future directions

The current study also has its own limitations. For instance, in order to ensure quality, the review was very selective, only choosing journal articles from renowned database not including conference papers, dissertations, etc. Another limitation was that the citation frequency and reference network were not included in the review so as to make the study more focused and tightened. What's more, since this paper was written at the beginning of 2023, the literature data of the current study were selected from January 1, 2000 to December 31, 2022, and the data from 2023 until now were not included, so the research status in the latest year was not analyzed.

The future study tends to expand the selection period of the literature data，enlarge the review scope, conduct a more comprehensive review on the research foci and illustrate the future trends with sophisticated displays.
