Author Correction: Metagenomic fingerprints in bronchoalveolar lavage differentiate pulmonary diseases
Authors: Dongsheng Han, Chang Liu, Bin Yang, Fei Yu, Huifang Liu, Bin Lou, Yifei Shen, Hui Tang, Hua Zhou, Shufa Zheng, Yu Chen
Published: 2026

Correction to: npj Digital Medicine 10.1038/s41746-025-01977-5, published online 07 October 2025

In this article, there were some descriptive errors in the sections titled ‘Differences in host immune response, transposable elements expression, and immune cell abundance of different pulmonary diseases’ and ‘Copy number variants and CNV-derived tumor fraction of different pulmonary diseases,’ as well as in the legend of Figure 6. These have now been corrected, and the original article has been updated accordingly.
