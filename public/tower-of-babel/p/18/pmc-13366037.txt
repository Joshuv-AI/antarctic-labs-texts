IFPHPOS0046 Operationalising Inclusive Research: Development and Pilot Testing of an Inclusive Research Maturity Matrix
Authors: Eliza Varga, Joanne Elliott, Anna Coleman, Arpana Verma
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundInequities in health outcomes are closely linked to unequal participation and representation in health research. Despite growing policy commitments, including the UK National Institute for Health and Care Research (NIHR) inclusion strategy and “10 Steps to Inclusive Research,” there remains a lack of practical tools to operationalise inclusive principles across the research lifecycle. This study aimed to develop and pilot an Inclusive Research Maturity Matrix (IRMM) to support structured self-assessment and improvement.MethodsThe matrix was co‑designed with researchers and public involvement partners through an iterative six‑stage process involving domain identification, scoring framework development, resource mapping, and pilot implementation. The final tool comprises ten domains spanning study design, sampling, demographic data use, budgeting, methodological adaptation, patient and public involvement, and knowledge mobilisation. Each domain includes a guiding question, examples of evidence, and a four-point scoring framework. The IRMM was piloted with researchers across four NIHR infrastructures, followed by structured feedback collection.ResultsPilot findings indicated that the IRMM worked effectively for different study types and research stages, with completion times of 30–60 minutes. Participants reported that it supported structured reflection and identified previously overlooked areas. Common gaps included budgeting for inclusion, use of demographic data, and inclusive knowledge mobilisation. Participants valued its potential for informing funding applications and facilitating team discussions.DiscussionThe IRMM provides a practical, developmental tool to embed inclusive practices systematically. Further validation across diverse settings is needed, but it offers a promising approach to translating policy into action and advancing equitable research practice.
