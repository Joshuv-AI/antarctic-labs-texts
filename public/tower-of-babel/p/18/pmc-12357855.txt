Influence of joint fissure occurrence on stress distribution of coal wall in large mining height
Authors: Weibin Guo, Yichen Gao, Gang Wang
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractIn order to ensure the stability of coal wall of fully mechanized mining face with large mining height, Aiming at the prominent problem that the rib spalling rate of coal wall in fully mechanized mining face with large mining height is high and is significantly affected by joint cracks, this paper comprehensively uses theoretical analysis, numerical simulation and other research methods to explore the damage effect of joint cracks on coal wall, and obtains the influence law of joint surface occurrence on abutment pressure in limit equilibrium area. Based on the stability theory of coal wall pressure bar, the expression function of abutment pressure distribution in the limit equilibrium zone of large mining height working face is constructed. The research shows that under the condition of large mining height working face, with the increase of joint plane dip angle, the damage of coal shows a trend of increase–decrease–increase, and the abutment pressure at the same position in the limit equilibrium zone shows a trend of decrease–increase–decrease. Under certain conditions, with the increase of the azimuth angle of the joint surface, the damage of the coal decreases first and then increases. The bearing pressure at the same position in the limit equilibrium zone increases first and then decreases. With the increase of joint plane spacing, the number of joint planes gradually decreases, and the damage of coal shows a decreasing trend. The abutment pressure at the same position in the limit equilibrium zone increases. The dip angle and azimuth angle of the joint surface have a relatively small influence on the bearing pressure in the limit equilibrium zone.Keywords: Large height mining, Joint fissures, Limit equilibrium zone, Abutment pressure, Coal wall stabilitySubject terms: Energy science and technology, Engineering

Introduction

Coal is an important mineral resource in China, and many coal seams belong to thick coal seams. Therefore, the mining conditions and environment are more complicated, which makes the thick coal seams encounter various problems in the mining process1–3. The thickness of the caving immediate roof strata increases greatly, and the range and degree of damage and movement of the overlying strata increase greatly. The stress-deformation-shift- damage degree in the overlying strata of the stope is different from that of the ordinary fully mechanized mining face (mining height is less than 3.5 m)4,5: the main roof of the large mining height working face is more intense, and the phenomenon of coal wall spalling is more serious. Large-area and large-depth spalling is a significant feature of the mine pressure behavior of the large mining height working face. The intensification of rib spalling of coal wall increases the probability of support instability and end face roof fall in working face6. The incidence of similar accidents is more than 10% higher than that of ordinary fully mechanized mining face, which seriously threatens the personal safety of workers and reduces the technical and economic benefits of fully mechanized mining face with large mining height7,8.

Many experts and scholars at home and abroad have conducted research on the morphology, instability mechanism, and influencing factors of coal wall sheet gangs, simulation methods and distribution characteristics of joint cracks in coal seams, and have achieved fruitful research results, providing rich ideas for the research of this paper. However, it is still necessary to systematically study the damage effect of joint cracks on the coal body and its impact on the stability of the coal wall at the high mining height, so as to realize the dynamic stability control of the coal wall and support of the working face of the large mining height to ensure the safe and efficient mining of the working face at the high mining height.

There is a close relationship between the rib spalling and the advance abutment pressure of the working face9–11. The area between the coal wall and the peak point of the advance abutment pressure is the limit equilibrium area12, so the abutment pressure distribution in the limit equilibrium area is on the coal wall of the working face. The supporting force is mainly related to the supporting capacity of the coal wall and the mining height. The supporting capacity of the coal wall is closely related to the strength of the coal, and the strength of the coal is mainly affected by the structural plane and the strength of the coal block in the coal seam. Therefore, the occurrence of the structural plane in the coal seam will affect the distribution of the bearing pressure in the limit equilibrium zone of the large mining height working face13. In this paper, theoretical analysis and numerical calculation methods will be used to study and analyze the influence of mining height, coal seam joint fissure occurrence and coal mechanical parameters on the abutment pressure distribution and coal wall stability in the limit equilibrium area of large mining height working face. The research results of this paper can enrich the surrounding rock control theory of fully mechanized mining face with large mining height, and have important theoretical significance and practical value for the rational application of fully mechanized mining with large mining height and the control of coal and rock stability.

Effect of joint fracture surface on coal damage

The mechanical properties of rock mass with multiple joints and fissures are mainly affected by the inclination angle, azimuth angle, number of groups, unit size (spacing of cracks), normal stiffness of joint surface, tangential stiffness, pressure transfer coefficient and shear transfer coefficient. According to the previous conclusions, the pressure transfer coefficient of joints and fissures is related to the size of the cracks and the mechanical parameters of coal blocks, the shear transfer coefficient is not only related to the physical and mechanical parameters of the cracks, but also to its stress conditions. Therefore, under the same stress conditions, the inclination angle, azimuth angle, number of groups and unit size (spacing of cracks) of the joints and fissures in the rock mass will affect the mechanical properties of the rock mass with joints and fissures.

As a special kind of rock mass, due to the influence of original rock stress and mining stress, there are generally one or more groups of joint fissures with dominant occurrence in the coal. Therefore, the coal can be simplified into a special rock mass with one or more groups of regular distribution. Therefore, according to the Brady model established by Olsson14 and Brady15,16, the influence of the dip angle of the joint fissure surface on the mechanical properties of the joint fissure rock can be obtained, and the influence of the azimuth angle and spacing of the joint plane on the mechanical properties of the joint fissure coal can be further extended.

Establishment of fundamental numerical model

The discrete element method can directly show the joint fissure in rock mass, and it can consider the interaction between rock block and joint fissure surface, which is very suitable for calculating the mechanical behavior of complex jointed rock mass, and can intuitively simulate the generation, expansion and evolution process of internal fissure in rock mass. And mature commercial programs are already available (e.g. Itasca’s 2D software UDEC, 3D software 3DEC). In dealing with heterogeneity, anisotropy, complex boundary conditions and dynamic problems, finite element method has strong flexibility, mature methods and standardized software. This makes finite element method and discrete element method widely used in rock engineering. The 3DEC software, a discrete element numerical simulation tool, takes into account primary and secondary rock fractures, allowing for the modeling of various rock mechanics behaviors like fracture, delamination, rotation, and squeezing. It can accurately identify new contacts formed due to rock fracturing and collapse, making it highly suitable for dynamic movement simulations of mine roof strata (Table 1).

Main conclusions

This paper takes the typical large mining height fully mechanized working face as the engineering background, and comprehensively adopts the methods of theoretical analysis, numerical simulation and so on. The following conclusions are obtained:

Based on the Brady model, the damage effect of the occurrence (inclination, azimuth, and spacing) of the joint surface in the coal seam on the coal wall under the conditions of hard and medium hard coal seams is revealed. under certain conditions, with the increase of the azimuth of the joint surface, the damage of the joint and fissure coal body shows a change law of first decreasing and then increasing; with the increase of the spacing of the joint surface, the damage of the joint and fissure coal body gradually decreases. The influence of the inclination and azimuth of the joint surface on the damage of the joint and fissure coal body is relatively small.

Based on the Brady model, the damage effect of the occurrence (inclination, azimuth, and spacing) of the joint surface in the coal seam on the coal wall under the conditions of hard and medium hard coal seams is revealed. under certain conditions, with the increase of the azimuth of the joint surface, the damage of the joint and fissure coal body shows a change law of first decreasing and then increasing; with the increase of the spacing of the joint surface, the damage of the joint and fissure coal body gradually decreases. The influence of the inclination and azimuth of the joint surface on the damage of the joint and fissure coal body is relatively small.

The influence of the occurrence of penetrates joint fracture surface in coal seam on the abutment pressure in the limit equilibrium zone of large mining height working face is analyzed. The research shows that with the increase of the dip angle of the joint surface, the abutment pressure at the same position in front of the coal wall shows a trend of decrease–increase–decrease. Under certain conditions, with the increase of the azimuth angle of the joint surface, the bearing pressure at the same position in the limit equilibrium zone increases first and then decreases. With the increase of joint spacing, the bearing pressure at the same position in the limit equilibrium zone will increase. The influence of dip angle and azimuth angle of joint surface on the abutment pressure in the limit equilibrium zone is relatively small.
