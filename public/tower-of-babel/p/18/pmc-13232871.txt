The following sentence has been amended for clarity regarding the removal of Zostavax® from the market.

The initial, live zoster vaccine (Zostavax) was marketed in the United States by Merck.70 This vaccine, however, was only 51% effective; moreover, some elderly and immunocompromised recipients developed disseminated VZV infections from this live vaccine, and it was therefore removed from the market.70

The corrected sentence is as follows:

The initial, live zoster vaccine (Zostavax) was marketed in the United States by Merck.70 This vaccine, however, was only 51% effective; moreover, some elderly and immunocompromised recipients developed disseminated VZV infections from this live vaccine.70 It was later removed from the US market.
