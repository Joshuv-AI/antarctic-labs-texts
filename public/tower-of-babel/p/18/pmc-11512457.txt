Current Concept & Phases of Sport Injury Rehabilitation
Authors: Damayanti Tinduh
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractHamstring muscle injury was commonly found among football players. It needed some periods to recover even not in a fully manner. Post hamstring injury resulted some deficits in function and muscle performance. The deficits and its underlined mechanism were not fully understood. This article explained more about the deficits and its underlined mechanism among football player with post hamstring injury. Nunez et al study (2023) reported the biceps femoris long head (BFlh) fascicle lengths and pennation angle in elite professional football players showed no differences in previously BFlh injured vs uninjured legs or in dominant vs non-dominant limbs in uninjured players. The previously injured limb showed higher intrinsic tension in the muscle in the resting state, higher resistance to external force that deforms the muscle’s initial shape, and a significantly reduced ability to provide a gradual elongation of the muscle over time when constant tensile stress was applied, but a similar muscle architecture. Rodriguez et al study (2017) was reported the deficits was found more in Soleus than in Gastrocnemius. Slow aerobic type 1 muscle fibre was poorly recovered post traumatic injury compared with fast glycolytic type 2 muscle fibre. Slow aerobic type 1 muscle fibre secreted less granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (GCSF) to stimulate Pax7+ stem cell. The Pax7+ stem cell played a key role in muscle recovery post injury.
