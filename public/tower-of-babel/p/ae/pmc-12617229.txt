OA2048. MOVE – a registration of road traffic victims based on ambulance data
Authors: S Nijman
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractIssue/ProblemUntil recently, road authorities and policymakers in the Netherlands used information from police registers for their policies. But we know that Ambulance Services register at least four times more road traffic victims than the police does.Description of the ProblemRegisters of Ambulance Services contain information on the location of the accident, the age of the victim, the mode of transport of the victim and (if relevant) the counterparty. The problem is that it is not mandatory for Ambulance Services to share their data and that the required information is embedded in open text fields within their registration systems. Therefore it is not easy to collect the necessary data for prevention purposes.ResultsWe have developed machine learning algorithms to identify road traffic victims in the registers from Ambulance Services. We are able to extract the data we need and transform those data into coded variables on road traffic accidents. The data are disseminated through an online interactive dashboard, called MOVE. In the Netherlands, 24 out of 25 Ambulance Services have signed an agreement to share their data, and data from 15 services are already available in the dashboard. We aim to demonstrate the dashboard with nationwide coverage during the conference.LessonsIf you want to work with Ambulance Services, you need to convince them to cooperate with you and show them that their data are crucial for preventing accidents. It is essential to build the trust that it is possible to share data within current privacy regulations. Another success factor is to keep sharing results with participating Ambulance Services.Key messages• Ambulance Services record significantly more road traffic victims than the police, providing valuable data for accident prevention, particularly regarding risk groups and accident locations.• With a robust monitoring system, ambulance data can also be used to evaluate the effectiveness of road traffic accident prevention measures.TopicRoad Traffic Victims, Monitoring, Ambulance Services.
