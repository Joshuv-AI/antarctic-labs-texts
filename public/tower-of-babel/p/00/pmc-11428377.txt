Correction to: Evaluation of data collection bias of third molar stages of mineralisation for age estimation in the living
Published: 2024

This is a correction to: Inês de Oliveira Santos, Isabel Poiares Baptista, Ricardo Henrique Alves da Silva, Eugénia Cunha, Evaluation of data collection bias of third molar stages of mineralisation for age estimation in the living, Forensic Sciences Research, Volume 9, Issue 2, June 2024, owae004, https://doi.org/10.1093/fsr/owae004

The following changes have been made to the originally published article.

In section Results, the percentage of upper right third molars is corrected from “24.65%” to “24.64%” within the first sentence: “All available third molars were observed in the sample: 48 (lower right, 25.78%), 18 (upper right, 24.65%), 28 (upper left, 24.93%), and 38 (lower left, 24.65%).”

In Table 1, the notation “(N = 660)” in the Tooth (FDI) column is deleted.

The Disclosure statement is updated to read: “Eugénia Cunha initial holds the position of Editorial Board Member for Forensic Sciences Research and is blinded from reviewing or making decisions for the manuscript.” instead of: “Not applicable.”

These emendations have been made within the article.
