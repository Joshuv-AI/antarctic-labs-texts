WALANT Technique for Olecranon Fracture Osteosynthesis: A Clinical Case Video
Authors: Grigorios Kastanis, Mikela‐Rafaella Siligardou, Petros Kapsetakis, Panagiotis Ioannou, Alexandros Tsioupros, Ioannis Ktistakis, Constantinos Chaniotakis
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
ABSTRACTThe WALANT technique allows for the establishment of an optimal surgical field without the need for a tourniquet, while also permitting the assessment of the elbow under physiological forces (active movement) following fixation. There is limited data on the use of the WALANT technique in ORIF of olecranon fractures, and this case video represents the first such presentation in the literature.

Case Video

A 68‐year‐old female underwent open reduction and internal fixation (ORIF) of an olecranon fracture using a plate and screws, performed under local anesthesia with the Wide‐Awake Local Anesthesia No Tourniquet (WALANT) technique [1], without sedation. (Figures 1 and 2). The mixture (analgesic and hemostatic) consisted of 1% lidocaine with 1:100,000 adrenaline and 1 mL of 8.4% sodium bicarbonate. A total of 30 mL of the mixture was gradually injected into the surgical field preoperatively and intraoperatively. The video illustrates the active intraoperative range of motion of the elbow subsequent to the completion of osteosynthesis (Video 1).

(A) Lateral and (B) anteroposterior preoperative X‐ray views of the elbow showing an olecranon fracture.

(A) Lateral and (B) anteroposterior postoperative X‐ray views of the elbow. ORIF of the olecranon fracture with dorsal plate and screws.

The video demonstrates intraoperative active movements of the elbow following the completion of osteosynthesis. The patient remained awake and was able to follow intraoperative instructions. This allowed for the assessment of both active and passive elbow movements (flexion, extension, pronation, and supination). Additionally, the stability of osteosynthesis was evaluated after its completion. Video content can be viewed at https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ccr3.71146.
