SPCT-05 ISOLATED RACHIDIAN LYMHPOMA: A case report
Authors: Joris Uriel Mahussi Adjoglo, Omar Boladji Adebayo Badirou
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundThe prevalence of primary bone lymphoma is estimated at 3-7% of primary bone tumours and less than 2% of all lymphomas in adults [1]. Primary lymphoma of the spine is a rare entity in neurosurgery, characterised by malignant proliferation of lymphocytes in the spine.Case DescriptionWe present the case of a 61-year-old man with this condition. Initial symptoms were low back pain progressing to urinary retention. Investigations revealed vertebral and extradural lesions. The patient underwent surgery and was referred to haematology for follow-up of the lymphoma.ConclusionThis case highlights the importance of an integrated, multidisciplinary approach to the management of spinal B lymphoma.
