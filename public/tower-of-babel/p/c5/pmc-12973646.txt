Retraction Note: Post-COVID reactivation of latent Bartonella henselae infection: a case report and literature review
Authors: Yanzhao Dong, Ahmad Alhaskawi, Xiaodi Zou, Haiying Zhou, Sohaib Hasan Abdullah Ezzi, Vishnu Goutham Kota, Mohamed Hasan Abdulla Hasan Abdulla, Alenikova Olga, Sahar Ahmed Abdalbary, Hui Lu
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: BMC Infectious Diseases (2024) 24:422

10.1186/s12879-024-09336-7

The Editor has retracted this article. Concerns were raised regarding the availability of the NGS dataset. The authors were asked to deposit the NGS dataset in a public repository but have not done so. Therefore, the Editor has lost confidence in the conclusions presented in this article.

None of the authors have responded to correspondence regarding this retraction.
