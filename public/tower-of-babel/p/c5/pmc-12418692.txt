Correction: Therapeutic potential of NGF-enriched extracellular vesicles in modulating neuroinflammation and enhancing peripheral nerve remyelination
Authors: Hancheol Yeo, Yoo Jung Kim, Jaekwon Seok, Yeonjoo Kwak, Soo Bin Jang, Na Hee Lim, Kwonwoo Song, Junghoon Lee, Min Chul Cho, Soo Woong Kim, Ssang-Goo Cho
Published: 2025

Acta Neuropathologica Communications (2025) 13:118

10.1186/s40478-025-02033-9

In the sentence beginning ‘Specifically, the expression level of … the two groups (Supplementary Figure. 4).’ In Effect of NCCoe-NGF-EVs on LPS-induced neuronal inflammation under the Results section of this article [1], the terms ‘IL-6’ and ‘IL-1β’ were placed incorrectly in the sentence.

The corrected sentence is “Specifically, the expression level of TNF-α, COX-2, and IL-1β in NCCoe-NGF-EVs was lower than those in the NCC-EVs group, while the expression level of IL-6 was higher (Supplementary Figure. 4)”.

The original article has been corrected.
