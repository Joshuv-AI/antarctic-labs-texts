Author Correction: Exome analysis links kidney malformations to developmental disorders and reveals causal genes
Authors: Hila Milo Rasouly, Sarath Babu Krishna Murthy, Natalie Vena, Gundula Povysil, Andrew Beenken, Miguel Verbitsky, Shirlee Shril, Iris Lekkerkerker, Sandy Yang, Atlas Khan, David Fasel, Janewit Wongboonsin, Jeremiah Martino, Juntao Ke, Naama Elefant, Nikita Tomar, Ofek Harnof, Sergey Kisselev, Shiraz Bheda, Sivan Reytan-Miron, Tze Y Lim, Anna Jamry-Dziurla, Francesca Lugani, Jun Y Zhang, Maddalena Marasa, Victoria Kolupaeva, Emily E Groopman, Gina Jin, Iman Ghavami, Kelsey O Stevens, Arielle C Coughlin, Byum Hee Kil, Debanjana Chatterjee, Drew Bradbury, Jason Zheng, Karla Mehl, Maria Morban, Rachel Reingold, Stacy Piva, Xueru Mu
Published: 2025

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-025-62319-3, published online 7 August 2025

In this article the author name Adele Mitrotti was incorrectly written as Adele Mittrori.

In addition, several affiliations were incorrect: the affiliation for Monika Miklaszewska and Katarzyna Zachwieja should have been ‘Department of Pediatric Nephrology and Hypertension, Faculty of Medicine, Jagiellonian University Medical College, Krakow, Poland’; for Isabella Pisani, ‘Nephrology Unit, Parma University Hospital, Parma, Italy’; for Valeria Grandinetti, ‘Nephrology, Dialysis and Kidney Transplant Unit, IRCCS Azienda Ospedaliero-Universitaria di Bologna, Bologna, Italy’; and for Olga Baraldi, ‘Department of Medical and Surgical Sciences (DIMEC), Alma Mater Studiorum University of Bologna, Bologna, Italy.’

The original article has been corrected.
