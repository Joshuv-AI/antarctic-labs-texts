Correction to Supporting Information for Liu et al., Efficient quantum algorithm for dissipative nonlinear differential equations
Published: 2026

Correction to Supporting Information for “Efficient quantum algorithm for dissipative nonlinear differential equations,” by Jin-Peng Liu, Herman Øie Kolden, Hari K. Krovi, Nuno F. Loureiro, Konstantina Trivisa, and Andrew M. Childs, which published August 26, 2021; 10.1073/pnas.2026805118 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 118, e2026805118).

The authors note that the SI Appendix previously contained “a technical issue with Lemma 1, and therefore Theorem 1. The revised version adds the condition ||F0|| ≤ ||F2||.” As such, on page 3, Theorem 1, “Assume R < 1” should instead appear as “Assume R < 1 and ||F0|| ≤ ||F2||.” On page 5, Lemma 1, line 2, “Assume that R defined in [2] satisfies R < 1” should instead appear as “Assume that R defined in [2] satisfies R < 1, and assume that ||F0|| ≤ ||F2||.” Additionally, per the authors, “the proof of Lemma 1 introduces a new quantity η~ on the right-hand side of Equation 37, which was conflated with η in the original version. The proof of Lemma 1 refers to diagonal dominance of G+G† (instead of G) and reasons in terms of the eigenvalues of this new operator (divided by 2, for notational convenience).” The authors note that, “since the eigenvalues νj are now real, we simply write νj instead of Re(νj) throughout the proof.”

Additionally, the authors note that “the parameters we originally chose for the SEIR example did not satisfy the newly added condition of Theorem 1. We edited the SEIR example to choose parameters that are consistent with that condition.” On page 20 of the SI Appendix, in the second paragraph, “√2rtra < min {rvac, 1/Tlat, 1/Tinf} − (√3 − 1)” should appear as “√2rtra < min {rvac, 1/Tlat, 1/Tinf} − (√3 − 1) and Λ ≤ √2rtra/P” and “Essentially, the Carleman method only converges if the (nonlinear) transmission is sufficiently slower than the (linear) decay” should appear as “Essentially, the Carleman method converges if the (nonlinear) transmission is sufficiently slower than the (linear) decay and the travel flux is low compared to the transmission rate divided by the total population.” In the third paragraph of the same page, “we assume rvac > 1/Tlat ≈ 0.19 days−1” should appear as “we assume rvac > 1/Tlat ≈ 0.19 days−1 and negligible travel flux (Λ = 0)” and “a direct calculation gives R = 0.956” should appear as “a direct calculation gives R = 0.956 and ensures Λ ≤ √2rtra/P”. Finally, on the same page, the first footnote should appear as “For negligible travel flux Λ ≈ 0, the solution u(t) will decay to a small value, so the solution is not interesting in the asymptotic limit. However, in practice we are interested in the behavior over shorter times for which the population has not significantly decayed.” The SI Appendix has been corrected online.
