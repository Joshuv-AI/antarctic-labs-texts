The Umbelliprenin-CTAB cellulose nanocrystal delivery system prevents hyperglycemia in diabetic rats by activating the insulin receptor IR/PDK1 pathway
Authors: AmirBagher Ilkhani, Maryam Tehranipour, jina Khayatzade, Akram Eidi
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractToday, with the prevalence of metabolic diseases such as diabetes, which can be caused by insulin resistance, the need for targeted drug delivery seems essential. Using Nano crystals that contain antioxidant materials such as Umbelliprenin and are surface modified may be a solution to this problem. This study aims to investigate the IR/PDK1 pathway in the presence of cellulose nano-crystal surface-modified with cetyltrimethylammonium bromide containing Umbelliprenin (UCC-NCs delivery system). The UCC-NCs delivery system was synthesized and characterized by FTIR and zeta-potential methods. Forty-two Wistar male rats (aged 12 weeks, 250–300 g) were divided into seven groups of six. After induction of diabetes with a single dose of intraperitoneal injection of STZ (60 mg.kg− 1), the treatment groups were treated orally with doses of 5 and 10 mg.kg− 1UM and Nano crystals. After the treatment period, the rats were anesthetized, and blood was taken from the rats’ retro-orbital plexus area, and their serum glucose, total cholesterol (TC), triglyceride, and HDL levels were measured. RNA was extracted from the tissue samples, and cDNA was synthesized, and the expression of IR and PDK1 genes was examined by real-time. Pathological changes were evaluated in the prepared sections. The results showed that Nano crystals were much better and more effective than UM in improving glucose and blood factors. Gene expression analysis showed a significant increase in the expression of IR and PDK1 genes in the groups receiving Nano crystals compared to UM. Therefore, nano crystals were probably able to prevent hyperglycemia by activating the IR/PDK1 pathway through anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. It is suggested that this drug delivery system be further tested for metabolic diseases such as diabetes.Keywords: Cellulose nano crystals surface-modified with cetyltrimethylammonium bromide containing Umbelliprenin (UCC-NCs delivery system), Anti-diabetic, IR/PDK1 pathway, Pancreatic protective activity, UmbellipreninSubject terms: Biochemistry, Biotechnology, Drug discovery, Endocrinology, Nanoscience and technology

Introduction

Today, targeted drug delivery is the first need of patients. Delivering drugs to specific tissues at a specific time and in a controlled dose, while reducing side effects, provides faster and more specific treatment for patients1. When a drug is taken, the way it enters the target cells, the rate of its absorption by the cells, metabolism, and excretion of the drug are very important2. In this context, the production of drugs in the form of cellulose nanocrystals that have been surface-modified by special materials such as cetyltrimethylammonium bromide is of interest3.

Umbelliprenin is a prenylated coumarin compound belonging to the class of sesquiterpene coumarins extracted from the dried roots of Ferula szwitsiana. Umbelliprenin is found in various plant species such as celery, Coriandrum sativum, Citrus limon, and Angelica archangelica, which are consumed as food or used for food preparation4. Umbelliprenin activates the intracellular and extracellular pathways of apoptosis. It has also been shown to have antioxidant activity in vitro and anti-inflammatory activity in vivo5,6. Umbelliprenin inhibited angiogenesis and metastasis with downregulation of EGF/CoCl2-mediated PI3K/AKT/MAPK7–9. The pharmacological studies demonstrate that umbelliferone has diverse effects such as anti-diabetes, anti-cancer, anti-infection, anti-rheumatoid arthritis, neuroprotection, and improvement of liver, kidney, and myocardial tissue damage10,11. The compound Umbelliprenin, as one of the most common derivatives of Umbelliferone that is expected to show similar effects.

Perhaps Delivery of Umbelliprenin via a Nano crystals system can enhance the effects of this substance. Nano drug delivery systems are capable of prolonging the circulation time, using multiple renewable resources, high drug binding, improving drug solubility, and tumor accumulation through enhanced permeability and retention effects12. For this purpose, cetyltrimethylammonium bromide (cetolene) with the chemical formula C₁₆H₃₃N (CH₃)₃Br is known13.

Cetyltrimethylammonium bromide is a substance used in topical antibacterial and antifungal drugs, cosmetics, and hair care products. It is also a surfactant and emulsifier14. Due to its inherent biocompatibility, non-toxicity, in vivo biodegradability, and self-assembly capacity, it can be a good carrier in this system. This leads to a higher potential for hydrophobic drug loading and self-assembly into stable nanocrystals without the use of toxic chemical linkers15.

With increasing obesity and decreasing physical activity, the prevalence of diabetes is increasing worldwide and is one of the main causes of morbidity and mortality16. Insulin resistance is usually caused by impaired insulin secretion and increased glucose production due to improper functioning of pancreatic cells17. The insulin receptor plays a very important role in blood sugar regulation, a functional process that under certain conditions may lead to a wide range of clinical manifestations, including diabetes and cancer18.

Insulin signaling controls the access of blood glucose to the body’s cells. When insulin is low, especially in those who are highly insulin-sensitive, the body’s cells begin to transport and use fats that do not need to be transported across the membrane19,20. As such, insulin is also an important regulator of fat metabolism. The primary function of the activated insulin receptor is to increase the uptake of glucose by cells21.

This is why “insulin insensitivity” or reduced insulin receptor signaling leads to type 2 diabetes, in which cells are unable to absorb glucose, resulting in hyperglycemia, or increased blood sugar, and all the potential consequences of the disease22–27. Accordingly, some studies have shown that PDK1 is dysregulated in some diseases and could act as a modulator and therapeutic target. Therefore, in this study, the role of PDK1 signaling in IR resistance in the presence of Umbelliprenin-CTAB cellulose nanocrystal delivery system was investigated. The main objective of this study was to investigate the antidiabetic effect of UM and compare the effect of pure UM and its UCC-NCs delivery system form.

Materials and methods

This study is reported in accordance with ARRIVE guidelines.

Materials

STZ, which was used in the experiment, was supplied by Sigma-Aldrich (St. Louis, MO, USA). Cetyltrimethylammonium Bromide and Umbelliprenin, and CNCs were obtained from Alborz Nano Tehzih Rayan Company of Iran. All the solutions were prepared with deionized water. Rats were obtained from Razi Animal Center.

UCC-NCs delivery system preparation

300 mg of umbelliprenin was dissolved in 60 ml of pure chloroform. Then, 200 ml of CTAB aqueous solution (1.5% w/v) was added. The contents were homogenized for 30 min with the help of a sonicator probe (with a time of 2 s on and 1 s off, frequency 20 kHz, tip diameter 10 mm, 120 watts). Primary Nano crystals were formed. After that, 200 ml of CNCs’ stable suspension (3% w/v) was added to the Nano crystals suspension and sonicated for 30 min (with 2 s on and 1 s off, frequency 20 kHz, tip diameter 10 mm, 120 W). The final Nano crystals were stirred for 12 h at a temperature of 45 °C in a shaker incubator. Then it was washed three times with deionized water and three times with ethanol and centrifuged (3 min, 8000 rpm). Nano crystals were transferred to a desiccator under vacuum to moisten and remove chloroform28.

UCC-NCs delivery system characterization

FESEM microscopy was used to examine the shape of the Nano crystals. Their stability in water was examined by conducting the phytosomes’ surface charge (Zeta potential). Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR) was used for detecting chemical ingredients and atomic bond characteristics of functional groups associated with Umbelliprenin.

Animal and grouping

In this study, forty-two male Wistar rats (aged 12 weeks, 250–300 g) were obtained from the Razi Animal Center. During the experiment, they were kept under standard conditions and natural light, a dark cycle, and had free access to food and water. After grouping, the animals were placed next to each other for three days to facilitate adaptation. Animals were randomly divided into seven groups of six:

Control group: healthy animals without injection and treatment.

Control group: healthy animals without injection and treatment.

Negative control group (diabetic): receives a single dose of STZ (60 mg.kg− 1) as an intraperitoneal injection29.

Negative control group (diabetic): receives a single dose of STZ (60 mg.kg− 1) as an intraperitoneal injection29.

Positive control group: In this group, after inducing diabetes for 28 days, they receive an oral dose of metformin (300 mg.kg− 1).

Positive control group: In this group, after inducing diabetes for 28 days, they receive an oral dose of metformin (300 mg.kg− 1).

Experimental groups: In this group, after inducing diabetes for 28 days, they receive an oral dose of Nano crystal and Umbelliprenin (5 and 10 mg.kg− 1).

Experimental groups: In this group, after inducing diabetes for 28 days, they receive an oral dose of Nano crystal and Umbelliprenin (5 and 10 mg.kg− 1).

Induction of diabetes

After getting acquainted with the laboratory environment, all rats were fed a high-fat diet then to induction diabetes, STZ was injected intraperitoneally in a single dose (60 mg.kg− 1). Forty-eight hours after STZ injection, blood sampling is done from the rats’ retro-orbital plexus area and the amount of glucose is measured using a glucometer30. Rats whose plasma glucose level is more than 250 mg.dl− 1 are selected and used for further study.

Blood and tissue sampling

After twenty-eight days of treating rats with Umbelliprenin and Nano crystal in experimental groups, Blood sampling was done from the rats’ retro-orbital plexus area. The samples’ serum was provided by centrifuging (VS-1500CFNLL, Germany) at 2400 rpm for 6 min. The serum glucose, total cholesterol (TC), triglyceride, and HDL were measured by the related commercial kits (Pars Azmone, Tehran, Iran) applying an Abbott analyzer (Alcyon 300, USA).

For tissue sampling, animals were first anesthetized with ketamine and xylene (100 and 10 mg.kg− 1). Xylazine and ketamine are two anesthetic drugs that are often used together to induce anesthesia in animals and humans. Xylazine is a sedative and muscle relaxant that works by binding to alpha-2 adrenergic receptors. This leads to a decrease in central nervous system activity, resulting in relaxation and pain relief. Ketamine is an anesthetic drug that works by blocking NMDA receptors in the brain. This causes unconsciousness, memory loss, and pain reduction31, and samples were taken from the pancreas to evaluate tissue pathological changes and examine gene expression.

After taking the necessary samples from the animals to euthanize them, they are beheaded using a guillotine while they are under deep anesthesia and do not suffer any pain.

Examining pathological changes

Tissue samples were first placed in 10% saline formalin32. Then they enter the stages of tissue passage and the paraffinization process. Then, a microtome (Leica RM 2126) was used to cut a thin 4-µm slide, which was finally stained with hematoxylin & eosin (H&E) to study the pancreas islands’ histopathological characteristics by taking images at 400 magnification (ZEISS Germany). The possible tissue changes of the islets of Langerhans in different groups were investigated and compared.

Gene expression assay

To check the expression of the Insulin receptor (IR) and phosphoinositide‑dependent kinase 1 (PDK1) genes, freshly removed samples were immediately subjected to the process for RNA extraction. Extraction was done by (Pars Tos kit, Tehran, Iran) according to its protocol. Then, cDNA synthesis was done using the Pars Tos kit (Tehran, Iran) and its protocol. Primers IR, PDK1, PI3K, and Glut4 genes were designed (Table 1) and blasted by NCBI, and the expression of IR, PDK1, PI3K, and Glut4 genes was checked by the real-time PCR method.

Statistical analysis

The results are expressed as mean ± S.D. The statistical significance was determined by applying one-way ANOVA statistics (using Minitab 16). The *, **, and *** indicate the P-values lower than < 0.05, 0.01, and 0.001, respectively.

Results

Characterization of the UCC-NCs delivery system

The size of hydrated Nano crystal on a scale of 200 nm was measured by FESEM microscopy, the diameter of the Nano crystal is estimated to be around 50 nm (Fig. 1A). The results of evaluating the surface charge of Nano crystals show a negative zeta potential of 40mV that indicate their good stability in aqueous conditions (Fig. 1B). FTIR spectroscopy results shows the chemical structure of Nano crystal and the intra-molecular bonds and functional groups.

The UCC-NCs characteristics. (A) The FESEM size. (B) Zeta potential of UCC-NCs. (C) The UCC-NCs FTIR spectrum. Umbelliprenin-CTAB cellulose nanocrystal delivery system.

The results indicated the Cellulose presence by detecting the transmittance light peaks at 3346.04, 2900.10, and 1642.95 cm− 1, which reflect the OH and C-H vibrational bonds in the structure of cellulose31. The 1480.32 and 1371.94 cm− 1 represent C-H bending vibrations in the cellulose structure and, in relation to CTAB, they can be indicative of -CH₂ and -CH₃ bending vibrations in CTAB24. The peaks at 1317.20 cm− 1 and 1200.91 cm− 1 represent the C-H vibrations and C-O-C glycoside bonds in the polysaccharide structure of cellulose, and it is likely to represent part of the C-O-C vibrations in the ester and ether groups of Umbelliprenin. The peaks at 1162.99, 1112.20, and 1094.17 present the stretching vibration of the C-O and C-O-C bonds in the cellulose structure. Relationship with CTAB: May overlap with the C-N vibrations in the ammonium groups of CTAB. Peaks in the range of 1060.31 cm⁻¹ to 897.17 cm− 1 indicate the presence of C-O and β-glycoside linkages in the polysaccharide structure of cellulose. Relationship with CTAB: May be related to C-N and C-C bonds in CTAB. Finally, the Peaks below 900 cm⁻¹ (665.86 cm⁻¹, 617.12 cm⁻¹, 560.81 cm⁻¹, and 522.59 cm⁻¹). This region is usually associated with out-of-plane vibrations of carbohydrate rings. Regarding CTAB and Umbelliprenin: These bands can be attributed to torsional and bending vibrations of the functional groups present in these compounds32–34 (Fig. 1C).

The total amount of drug after the synthesis process was calculated to be 286 mg based on the absorbance reading of the supernatant after the first centrifugation (separation of Nanoparticles from the suspension).

Discussion

Finding treatments and therapeutic solutions for metabolic diseases, which are highly prevalent, is a priority in today’s medical world. Rapid and targeted drug delivery can save the lives of many people and make life more painless for them35. The results of this study showed that the use of UM in the form of surface-modified nanocrystals can show much better and more healing effects. In the treatment groups with UM Nano crystals, the levels of glucose, cholesterol, triglycerides, and HDL showed significant changes compared to the diabetic group. The results showed a significant decrease in HDL levels in the different diabetic groups and a significant increase in the Nano crystal treatment group. Apparently, Nano crystals were able to prevent complications caused by diabetes, such as the risk of heart complications, by increasing HDL.

Comparing Nano crystals to pure UM showed better results in the treatment groups. Apparently, the present Nano crystal, due to its zeta potential and appropriate size, was able to pass through the cell membrane well and induce its healing effects. Since the antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects of UM have been proven in previous studies, it was assumed that it was able to induce healing effects by suppressing inflammatory pathways36.

The main factors that cause diabetes are insulin resistance due to defects in insulin signaling, changes in the expression of insulin target proteins or genes, or metabolic defects. Insulin resistance appears to precede impaired insulin secretion. Normally, following insulin secretion, vesicles containing GLUT4 stores physically attach to the plasma membrane and help increase glucose uptake by the cell23. The results of GLUT4 gene expression were consistent with the aforementioned study, showing that gene expression decreased in the diabetic group and increased in the UM Nano crystal treatment group. Therefore, the improvement effects in the UM Nano crystal treatment group may be due to changes in GLUT4 gene expression.

By activating PI3K, insulin converts PIP2 to PIP3, which in turn activates other cytosolic mediators such as PDK1 and other proteins. Changes in the expression level or chemical composition of any of these molecules participating in this pathway could be involved in the development of the disease27. In the present study, the expression of the insulin receptor and PDK1 genes was examined, and it was shown that UM Nano crystals were able to significantly increase the expression of these two genes in the treatment groups. This increase is significantly greater than the groups receiving pure UM. Therefore, it is likely that UM Nano crystals, by regulating the increase in the IR/PDK1 pathway, were effective in improving the symptoms caused by the creation of a diabetic model. The results of measuring PI3K gene expression showed that this gene had higher expression in the presence of UM Nano crystals than in the negative control group, so it was probably able to effectively activate the above pathway. The role of PDK1 in the insulin signaling pathway for glucose metabolism was first described in 2002. It was found that PDK1 activates insulin-dependent PKB, but does not alter the activity of aberrant protein kinase C (PKC) in cells. Since activation of the mTOR protein kinase pathway, which is a pathway that coordinates nutritional and hormonal signals, can lead to insulin resistance, and activation of the IR/PDK1 pathway inhibits the mTOR pathway37, administration of UM Nano crystals may be an effective strategy for preventing diabetes and reducing blood sugar. It has been shown that PDK1 phosphorylation is insulin-dependent in a mouse hypothalamic cell line38, Our results were consistent with those of Tamgüney and Lemmer. In 2009, Soltani et al. investigated the antigenotoxicity effects of UM on human peripheral lymphocytes exposed to oxidative stress. It was found that amblyprenin, a prenylated coumarin (UMB), has protective properties against DNA damage in human lymphocytes. Our results are consistent with Soltani’s results in reducing oxidative stress39.

In this regard, Erfani et al. (2024) investigated the antidiabetic activity of purified compounds (Umbelliprenin, farnesiferol A, farnesiferol C, and samarkandin) from the assafoetida plant (Ferula assafoetida). Increased blood sugar after meals is considered an early sign of diabetes. Enzyme inhibitors, such as alpha-amylase and alpha-glucosidase inhibitors, are currently being studied as potential drugs for preventing postprandial hyperglycemia. In Erfani’s study, the effects of four purified 7-hydroxycoumarin derivatives from the plant Angustifolia were investigated: Umbelliprenin, farnesiferol A, farnesiferol C, and samarkandin. The results showed that Umbelliprenin significantly inhibited the activity of alpha-amylase and alpha-glucosidase (35.07% and 4.98%, respectively). Molecular docking results showed that Umbelliprenin with a farnesyl chain had a stronger inhibitory effect11. Therefore, the results of our study are consistent with the results of this research.

The results of pathological examinations of pancreatic sections show that in the treatment groups, the symptoms of atrophy and tissue necrosis decreased and the volume of the islets of Langerhans increased40. Improved tissue function in pancreatic and kidney tissues could be a good indication for improved complications of diabetes brought about by Nano crystals. Tissue improvement could result in better and greater insulin secretion and reduced oxidative stress caused by metabolic weakness. These effects are probably due to the antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties of UM, which are better and more visible in the form of Nano crystals and enter the cell. In this regard, Habib ollah and colleagues in 2023 investigated the therapeutic effects of melatonin-loaded chitosan/lecithin Nanoparticles on diabetic rats and found that it improved the physiological and tissue function of the pancreas41. Nanoparticles work better than pure active ingredients.

This study has several limitations. First, additional characterization data, including drug loading efficiency, encapsulation capacity, and in vitro drug release profiles, were not obtained.

Conclusion

The designed and surface-modified UM Nano crystals were able to reduce glucose levels in diabetic rats, increase PI3K, IR, PDK1, and GLUT4 gene expression, and improve pancreatic and kidney pathological changes much better than pure UM.
