Editorial Expression of Concern: Intravenous Tranexamic acid vs. sublingual Misoprostol in high-risk women for postpartum haemorrhage following Cesarean delivery; a randomised clinical trial
Authors: Mariam Dawoud, Maha Al-Husseiny, Omneya Helal, Moutaz Elsherbini, Mazen Abdel-Rasheed, Mona Sediek
Published: 2025

Editorial Expression of Concern: BMC Pregnancy Childbirth 23, 611 (2023).

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-023-05935-5.

The Editor is issuing an Expression of Concern. The original concerns related to discrepancies in the recruitment period described in the clinical trial registry and the Article, the feasibility of recruiting the number of patients described in the time frame, and the number of patients described in the Abstract of the study (315) and the Methods and Results (345). The authors provided an explanation; however, they did not provide their original data on request and therefore post-publication peer review was inconclusive as to the reliability of the reported results. Readers are therefore asked to exercise caution when interpreting the results. The authors disagree with this Editorial Expression of Concern.
