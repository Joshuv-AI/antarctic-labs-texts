Correction to: Overcoming transfusion-refractory anemia: partial splenic embolization to facilitate orthopedic surgery in β-thalassemia
Authors: Qing Qing Ye, Xian Gui Zhou, Guang Yang, Wan Tao Xie, An Yong Yu, Hai Zhen Duan
Published: 2026

Correction to: Annals of Hematology.

10.1007/s00277-026-06857-w.

In the published paper, the legends for Figs. 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 were misplaced and a missing citation in the Introduction was identified. The correct content is as follows:

Figure 3 legend should read:“Abdominal CT images. Panel a (Pre‑PSE): The spleen exhibits homogeneous parenchymal density. Panel b (10 days post‑PSE): A wedge‑shaped hypodense area is observed within the splenic parenchyma (as indicated by the white arrow), corresponding to the postembolization ischemic infarction zone.”

Figure 4 legend should read:“Line graph illustrating the trend of ferritin levels.”

Figure 5 legend should read:“Line graph showing the trend in hemoglobin levels.”

Figure 6 legend should read:“Digital subtraction angiography (DSA). The image depicts the post‑procedure findings following selective embolization of the peripheral branches of the splenic artery. The white arrow indicates that approximately 50% of the splenic parenchyma was embolized.”

Figure 7 legend should read:“Anteroposterior and lateral radiographs of the right femur. Figures a and c (anteroposterior and lateral X‑ray views one day postoperatively): Demonstrate satisfactory reduction and alignment of the fracture fragments. Figures b and d (anteroposterior and lateral X‑ray views one month postoperatively): Reveal blurred fracture lines with continuous bridging callus formation observed at the fracture site (as indicated by the white arrows).”

Introduction – The sentence ending with “which can lead to delays or increased risks in necessary surgical interventions” should be followed by citation 1.

This correction only affects the above sections; the conclusions and overall findings of the study remain unchanged. We apologize for any inconvenience caused to the readers and the editorial office.

The original article has been corrected.

The online version of the original article can be found at 10.1007/s00277-026-06857-w.
