Retraction: Postoperative complications of mediastinal cyst resection and their management
Authors: Hana Ajouz, Nestor Villamizar
Published: 2025

The authors have requested the retraction of the article (1) titled “Postoperative complications of mediastinal cyst resection and their management” (doi: 10.21037/med-22-30) due to citation errors, which may have arisen from a disruption in the reference order during the writing process. As the original file containing the embedded references is no longer available, the authors have decided to withdraw the article from publication.

The authors apologize for any inconvenience caused.

This retraction was submitted by the authors and approved by the editorial office of Mediastinum.
