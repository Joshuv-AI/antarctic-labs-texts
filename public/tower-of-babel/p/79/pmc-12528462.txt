Retraction Note: Long-term use of fluoxetine accelerates bone loss through the disruption of sphingolipids metabolism in bone marrow adipose tissue
Authors: Huili Zhang, Kefeng Li, Yanna Zhao, Yilan Zhang, Jiawen Sun, Shihong Li, Guangwu Lin
Published: 2025

Retraction Note to: Translational Psychiatry 10.1038/s41398-020-0819-5, published online 12 May 2020

This article has been retracted at the request of Authors Shihong Li, Guangwu Lin, Huili Zhang, Kefeng Li and Yanna Zhao. After publication, concerns were expressed regarding the reliability of data presented in Figure 3I. These concerns compromise the integrity of the study's conclusions.

Authors Shihong Li, Guangwu Lin, Kefeng Li and Yanna Zhao agree with this retraction. Author Huili Zhang does not agree with this retraction. Authors Yilan Zhang and Jiawen Sun did not respond to correspondence from the publisher about this retraction.
