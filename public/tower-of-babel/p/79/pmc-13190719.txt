Retraction Note: Fusion of transfer learning models for detection of alzheimer’s disease using bidirectional long short-term memory with equilibrium optimization algorithm
Authors: K. Renugadevi, T. Jayasankar
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-30456-w, published online 01 December 2025

The Editors have retracted this Article.

After publication of this Article, concerns were raised about unrealistic F1 scores, MCC values, and PR-AUC values. In addition, the methods lack details required to reproduce the work. Authors were unable to provide the underlying code and a satisfactory explanation for raised concerns. The Editors therefore no longer have confidence in the research presented in this Article.

The Authors did not respond to the Editors’ correspondence about this retraction.
