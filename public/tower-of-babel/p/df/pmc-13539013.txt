Retraction: InsightConnect: An AI-powered talent intelligence system for optimized workforce matching and forecasting
Published: 2026

At the request of Sage and the Journal Editor, the following article has been retracted:

Lee S-L, Hsu M-H, Wang MY-F, Wang Y-F. InsightConnect: An AI-powered talent intelligence system for optimized workforce matching and forecasting. Science Progress. 2026;109(2). doi:10.1177/00368504261462858

The Journal received a concern from a reader regarding the authenticity of the third author’s affiliation.

The third author was contacted for comment and asked to verify their institutional affiliation.

The author responded and provided an institutional ID, that was noted to have expired in 2023.

The institution was also contacted for comment and communicated that the last author is not affiliated with Pennsylvania State University’s Marketing Department.

Due to our inability to verify the third author’s identity, this article has been retracted.

Y.F.W. disagrees with the decision to retract. All other authors did not respond when notified of this retraction.
