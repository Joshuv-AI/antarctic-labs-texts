Authors: Shouchen Liu, Zhaoyu Yu

ABSTRACT
AbstractThe cold chain supply chain of e-commerce has the characteristics of long chain, dispersed production, heterogeneous information sources, etc., which is easy to cause the upstream and downstream information of the supply chain to be broken and opaque. Traditional traceability data is stored in each node enterprise, and there are problems such as low cooperation trust and poor authenticity in the upstream and downstream data transmission process of the supply chain, resulting in consumers' trust crisis on the authenticity of traceability information. Blockchain traceability system through the establishment of multi-party participation, joint maintenance of distributed database, and the use of cryptography and consensus mechanism to establish trust relationships, with data can not be tampered with, sharing of high credibility. On the basis of stochastic Petri net theory, this study constructed a blockchain e-commerce cold chain traceability model centering on the actual application of blockchain technology in logistics traceability to improve the reliability and validity of e-commerce cold chain traceability. Correlation matrix and invariants were used to analyze the validity of the model, and an isomorphic Markov chain was constructed to analyze the effectiveness evolution of the model. This study aims to deepen the understanding of the cold chain traceability system of blockchain e-commerce from the three aspects of the best optimization link, the best synergism scale, and the adaptive ability of the system to provide reference for the improvement of cold chain traceability ability by blockchain technology.Keywords: Blockchain, E-commerce cold chain, Traceability, Petri net, Incidence matrix

Introduction

In recent years, the improvement of residents' living standards has brought about higher requirements for commodity quality, and food safety has become the focus of attention. When consumers choose goods, they pay attention to the appearance quality of goods, origin, processing process, and other internal quality, which brings a huge market space for the traceability industry. They explore establishing a highly open traceability system covering the whole country, sharing and checking, and further extend the traceability management to the internal enterprises and fields. The traceability, tampering, and transparency of blockchain technology provide an experimental way to solve the cold chain traceability system of e-commerce. Thus, this study analyzes the overall business process of the cold chain traceability system based on the actual situation of applying blockchain technology in logistics traceability. The blockchain e-commerce cold chain traceability system is established. Petri net theory is used to analyze the boundedness, accessibility, activity, and security of the traceability system. The efficiency evolution law of the isomorphic Markov chain test model is constructed. The Internet of things technology is used during the production, processing, circulation, and consumption of all links to achieve strict control of the whole process, and the transparent e-commerce cold chain is used to ensure the supply of high-quality and reliable cold chain products and achieve the brand effect of cold chain products. Literature review

With the advent of the information age, blockchain technology has garnered significant attention. Since its inception in 2008, blockchain technology has been rapidly advancing and evolving each year. From the era of blockchain 1.0 represented by Bitcoin to the era of combining smart contracts and financial business exemplified by Ethereum (blockchain 2.0), and now to the era of integrating all aspects of life represented by hyperledger (blockchain 3.0), research on blockchain applications has progressively permeated various domains. Currently, academic research on blockchain technology and cold chain traceability focuses on different aspects, which can be broadly categorized into three levels.

Firstly, there have been studies conducted both domestically and internationally that explore applying blockchain technology to supply chain management. Dutta et al. Secondly, in terms of the combination of blockchain and agricultural product traceability, many researchers have proposed solutions. Zhang et al. However, scholars have not undertaken a comprehensive analysis of the actual efficiency of the blockchain traceability system from the management and decision-making level. Furthermore, they lack discussion on the internal law, optimal scale, and constraints of the efficiency of each link. Petri net has strong mathematical support. Design of e-commerce cold chain traceability system based on blockchain technology

Overview of blockchain

Blockchain is the application pattern of new computer technologies, such as point-to-point transmission, distributed data storage, encryption algorithms, and consensus mechanisms. The essence of blockchain is a distributed public ledger. With the help of blockchain technology, from the birth of digital commodity information, every link (manufacturing, processing, transportation, and transaction) can be recorded in blocks worldwide. In the blockchain, information is open and transparent, and data are exchanged directly between two nodes, without the involvement of a third party. Thus, data cannot be invented out of thin air, nor can it be deleted at will, and the data cannot be tampered with once recorded. From production to sales, each kind of commodity will go through a complex circulation process, and a national chain key is set at the key node of the circulation process. For a person, a key is a universal chain wallet. For an object, a key is a string of encrypted information that carries the details of the object. Thus, people and objects alike are marked by a blockchain key.

In the blockchain, tokens are tokens (i.e., licenses, passes, passwords, and passwords) that are separate from ownership. Permission to use them does not mean you own them. If the entire blockchain traceability system is established with tokens as the core, then the entire upstream and downstream information from production, processing, transportation, and sales is recorded on the blockchain to ensure that the data can be checked and cannot be tampered with. Tokens can then be matched to physical goods, either as circulation or exchange certificates or as rewards for nodes that maintain the ecological health of the entire system. Blockchain is meaningless without tokens, which carry commodity value, corporate value, and personal information value and act as a clearing system within the system. Therefore, the mode with tokens is the most likely development direction of traceability. The data storage platform of blockchain + e-commerce cold chain traceability is shown in Fig. 1

From the perspective of participants, first of all, all participants in the e-commerce cold chain traceability process will create a corresponding information file when registering, and the necessary information such as enterprise information, function, address and qualification certification should be recorded in the file. After successful registration, participants will receive a public key and private key, the public key is disclosed to all members of the blockchain, and the private key is used as the key to verify identity and information during the transaction process. Each participant can log into the user interface with a registered ID and access the specified blockchain network. The development and maintenance of the software must be responsible by a trusted unit, and there must be an authoritative organization to assume the responsibilities of the registration authority.

From the point of view of information flow, all information is stored in the blockchain and it supports access by authorized nodes. The access to information depends on the role and function of the participants in the supply chain. In addition, the operating rules of the blockchain are defined by the code and stored in the blockchain, and cannot be modified by a participant in the blockchain, thus guaranteeing the authenticity and validity of the data. To change the rules of blockchain operation, like data storage, it needs to be broadcast to all nodes and verified by key departments.

The food traceability chain will involve various organs and enterprises with different authority roles and status, thus reaffirming the significance of choosing the alliance chain. The difference between alliance chain and public chain lies not only in system centralization and participant identity, but also in consensus mechanism and incentive mechanism. According to the literature Angwei (2017) [27]the choice of alliance chain means that the trust mechanism in the blockchain system is no longer required to be PoW or PoS, and the nodes participating in the consensus mechanism can be reduced by reducing the degree of decentralization to overcome the waste of resources in the mining process. Maintaining the food traceability system is the responsibility of the participating nodes in the alliance chain, so the operation of the blockchain system does not require an incentive mechanism, that is, the use of the alliance chain can not rely on digital currency, simplify the recording ledger, and reduce the bandwidth required for system operation.

When authorized participants maintain product information documents, they are required to enter data information on the software application interface after connecting to the network with the private key. The system collects a range of information, including the current status of the product, the type of product, and the industry standards it is based on. Once the participant initiates the product transfer, the system reviews whether the data format is legal and conforms to industry standards through the embedded smart contract, and after the verification is successful, the information previously provided by the participant is packaged and entered. It can be seen that the time node of data packaging into blocks is controllable, so as to ensure that internal traceable data and external traceable data will not be packaged into the same block, resulting in contradictions in block information access rights (in order to protect enterprise privacy, the access rights of internal traceable information must be higher than that of external traceable information). When an authorized node conducts a "transaction" with another node, such as when a product flows from a processing plant to a logistics company, the product ownership relationship is transferred, and a unified agreement needs to be reached and digitally signed between the two nodes. The digital signature uses asymmetric encryption technology to ensure that the information is not tampered with and the identity information of the two nodes does not need to be disclosed.

When the "transaction" is notarized by the core node in the blockchain network and broadcast across the network, the block containing the transaction details becomes the latest link on the blockchain in order of time stamp. Once the handover is completed and the smart contract executes the agreement content, the authorized object of the system will be automatically transferred from the processing plant to the logistics enterprise, and the logistics enterprise can continue to maintain and supplement the product information. In the process of "transaction", both parties encrypt and decrypt the transaction information through asymmetric keys, which ensures that the information cannot be maliciously tampered with and solves the problem of integrity in the transaction process.

Result analysis and discussion

According to the intensity of efficiency, all regulated links are categorized into three modules. Firstly, at the level of strong efficiency, the main factors for adjustment include completing warehouse order processing, third-party order processing, customer arrival for goods collection, customer acceptance completion, and inspection and loading completion. If we understand the essence of operating an e-commerce cold chain traceability system as extracting disordered traceability information from the e-commerce cold chain supply chain and archiving it in real-time, orderly and distributed manner within a blockchain-based e-commerce cold chain traceability system; then these efficient links throughout the entire chain can be packaged and summarized as front-end modules serving the e-commerce cold chain traceability system. Secondly, at a medium efficiency level, adjustments are made to order receiving efficiency, vehicle scheduling efficiency, packaging operations efficiency and loading efficiency. Positioned between the front-end module and back-end module within the entire e-commerce cold chain supply chain; this link not only connects with upstream manufacturers and distributors of blockchain-based e-commerce cold chain traceability systems but also plays a crucial bridging role with downstream retailers and consumers of such systems. Therefore this link can be understood as a middle link within the blockchain traceability system. Finally, at a weak level of efficiency; key regulatory factors include information processing departments such as information centers along with node selection departments like logistics centers or distribution centers that focus on route optimization. Henceforth these links form a module which can be summarized as part of back-end modules embedded in blockchain-based e-commerce cold-chain random systems.

Under the guidance of the efficiency cost appeal, the simulation results have enhanced stakeholders' comprehension of the blockchain e-commerce cold chain traceability system from three perspectives, facilitating decision makers (managers) in targeted allocation of core resources and alleviating social tension within the e-commerce cold chain supply chain network space to promote intelligent decision-making realization. Firstly, it is crucial to identify optimization opportunities. Faced with substantial information accumulation at each link of the e-commerce cold chain supply chain, simulation results suggest that decision makers (managers) should prioritize resource concentration to swiftly enhance service efficiency in the front-end module, thereby significantly reducing overall workload adjustments. Secondly, understanding optimal efficiency scale is essential. Different modules possess distinct thresholds for achieving maximum efficiency regulation. Notably, when increasing front-end module efficiency serving the blockchain traceability system by 200 % from its initial value and enhancing mid-end link efficiency encompassing order reception, vehicle scheduling, packaging operations and loading by 300 % from its initial value while elevating back-end module embedded in blockchain traceability system performance output by 400 % from its initial value yields cost-effective outcomes. Beyond these thresholds, marginal growth rates gradually decline and enhancement capacity stabilizes. Lastly, adaptive perception systems play a pivotal role. The decentralized blockchain e-commerce cold chain traceability system replaces traditional zero-sum game approaches with win-win cooperation.When a link suddenly increases efficiency, other upstream and downstream links will correspondingly increase the amount of work to be handled per unit time. In this dynamic coordination process, the simulation results show that other links will produce adaptive responses, which will improve the efficiency to a certain extent.
