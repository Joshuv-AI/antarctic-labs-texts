Correction: 6-Gingerol attenuates hepatic ischemia/reperfusion injury through regulating MKP5-mediated P38/JNK pathway
Authors: Qiwen Yu, Jiye Li, Mengwei Cui, Chaopeng Mei, Qianqian He, Xiaoxiao Du
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-58392-1, published online 02 April 2024

The original version of this Article contained an error in Fig. 7, where the image displayed in the panel ‘I/R’ in Fig. 7I originated from the image of Fig. 2A panel ‘IR + 100 mg/kg’. As a result, the correct image for the Fig. 7I ‘I/R’ was omitted during figure preparation. The original Fig. 7 and accompanying legend appear below.

Knockout of MKP5 attenuated the hepatoprotective effect of 6-Gingerol. (A, B) Serum ALT and AST level, (C) H&E staining and (D) necrotic area statistics, (E–H) Il-1β, Il-6, Tnf-α and Mcp1 mRNA expression, (I) TUNEL staining (red fluorescence indicates TUNEL positives cells) and (J) statistical analysis, (K–N) MKP5 protein and P38/JNK pathway protein detection. **P < 0.01 versus sham group; ##P < 0.01 versus I/R group; $$P < 0.01 versus I/R group; ##P < 0.01 versus I/R + 100 mg/kg group.

The original Article has been corrected.
