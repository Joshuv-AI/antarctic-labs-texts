NODG-04 NEUROSURGICAL MANAGEMENT OF ORBITAL TUMORS : EXPERIENCE OF THE NEUROSURGERY DEPARTMENT OF IBN TOFAIL HOSPITAL CHU MOHAMED VI MARRAKECH
Authors: Zeus Mala, Y Ait m’barek, L Benantar, K Aniba
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractIntroductionOrbital tumors (OT) are masses that form in the orbit and its structures. Exophthalmos is the most common presenting symptom. Advances in medical imaging have improved diagnosis, but definitive confirmation still requires anatomopathological analysis. Treatment remains essentially surgical and multidisciplinary.Patients and methodsThis work is a retrospective study over 10 years from January 2014 to December 2024), on 30 cases of orbital tumors treated in the Neurosurgery department of the Ibn Tofail hospital at the Mohammed VI University Hospital in Marrakech. We describe the different epidemiological-clinical, paraclinical, therapeutic histological and progressive aspects of TOResultsThe frequency of TO was 3 cases per year, with a female predominance (sex ratio = 0.77), The average age was 26 years (2 years – 50 years). CT was performed for 25 patients, and MRI for 13 patients. 26 patients were selected, the subfrontal approach was the most used. The histological study, supplemented whenever necessary by the immunohistochemical study, showed that Meningiomas were the most frequent at 39.12%. 3 of our patients were referred to oncology for additional treatment with radiotherapy and/or chemotherapy. A simple progression was objective in 25 patients, 4 patients presented complications and a recurrence in a single patient.ConclusionOrbital tumors represent a diverse and serious pathology due to its retention on prognostic but sometimes also vital visual function. It requires multidisciplinary management, influenced by site and histological type.
