Author Correction: Operando monitoring of the functional role of tetrahedral cobalt centers for the oxygen evolution reaction
Authors: Yonggui Zhao, Nanchen Dongfang, Chong Huang, Rolf Erni, Jingguo Li, Han Zhao, Long Pan, Marcella Iannuzzi, Greta R. Patzke
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-025-55857-3, published online 10 January 2025

In the version of this article initially published, in the Methods “Materials characterizations” section, a reference was missing and is now cited (Triana, C., Cuello, G., Huang, C., & Zhao, Y. Insights into the disorder-reconstruction correlations of cobalt carbodiimide during water oxidation by pair distribution function analysis. Institut Laue-Langevin, 10.5291/ILL-DATA.6-06-53 (2024)), while in the Acknowledgements, thanks to the Institut Laue-Langevin for the allocation of beamtime (instrument D4, proposal 6-06-535) and to Gabriel Cuello for experimental assistance with neutron total scattering were missing. The Methods and Acknowledgments are now updated in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
