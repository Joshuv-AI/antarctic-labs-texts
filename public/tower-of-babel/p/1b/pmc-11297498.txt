Correction to: Recurrent venous thromboembolism and bleeding with extended anticoagulation: the VTE-PREDICT risk score
Published: 2024

This is a correction to: Maria A de Winter, Harry R Büller, Marc Carrier, Alexander T Cohen, John-Bjarne Hansen, Karin A H Kaasjager, Ajay K Kakkar, Saskia Middeldorp, Gary E Raskob, Henrik T Sørensen, Frank L J Visseren, Philip S Wells, Jannick A N Dorresteijn, Mathilde Nijkeuter, VTE-PREDICT study group, Recurrent venous thromboembolism and bleeding with extended anticoagulation: the VTE-PREDICT risk score, European Heart Journal, Volume 44, Issue 14, 7 April 2023, Pages 1231–1244, https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehac776.

The calculation formula included as Supplementary Material contains typographical errors which result in difficulties in predicting individual risks. An updated version of the supplementary material is included as part of this notice at European Heart Journal online.

These details have been corrected only in this correction notice to preserve the published version of record.
