Durysta pellet occlusion of XEN gel stent
Authors: Arkadiy Yadgarov
Published: 2025

A patient presented with eye pain and an acute intraocular pressure (IOP) spike to 58 mmHg three weeks after XEN gel stent implantation. Examination revealed a round Durysta® pellet obstructing the internal ostium of the XEN stent (Fig. 1a). The Durysta pellet had been injected intracamerally 3 months prior to Xen implantation. There was no aqueous flow, and the bleb was flat (Fig. 1b). YAG laser successfully dislodged the pellet, which floated inferiorly and settled in the inferior angle (arrow, Fig. 1c). The internal ostium was clear (Fig. 1d), and a bleb subsequently formed. IOP immediately dropped to 25 mmHg, and stabilized at 15 mmHg by the next day. The patient has remained stable at 6 month follow up, without re-occlusion of the Xen stent.
