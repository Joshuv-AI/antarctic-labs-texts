Correction for Jaen Maisonet et al., Small-molecule allosteric activator of ubiquitin-specific protease 7 (USP7)
Published: 2026

Correction for “Small-molecule allosteric activator of ubiquitin-specific protease 7 (USP7),” by Isabella Jaen Maisonet, Mona Sharafi, Emilie J. Korchak, Andres Salazar-Chaparro, Ariana S. Bratt, Tvesha Parikh, Anthony C. Varca, Binita Shah, Michael Darnowski, Mia Chung, Wei Pin Teh, Jianwei Che, Irina Bezsonova, and Sara J. Buhrlage, which published October 14, 2025; 10.1073/pnas.2510496122 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 122, e2510496122).

The authors note that their Acknowledgements statement was missing a funding recognition. The first sentence should read: “We would like to acknowledge generous support from the Helen Gurley Brown Foundation, the Jean Strouse Sharf and Lisa Sharf Green Cancer Research Fund, the Foundation for USP7-Related Diseases Grant, NIH R35GM128864, NIH 1R21NS135343, and NIH T32GM139775.”

The Acknowledgements section has been updated.
