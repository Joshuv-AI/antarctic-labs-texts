Correction to: Integrating intersectionality into child health research: Key considerations
Published: 2024

This is a correction to: Bukola Salami, Aleem Bharwani, Nicole Johnson, Tehseen Ladha, Michael Hart, Jaya Dixit, Susanne Benseler, Integrating intersectionality into child health research: Key considerations, Paediatrics & Child Health, 2024;, pxae033, https://doi.org/10.1093/pch/pxae033

The following changes have been made to the originally published manuscript.

In section INTERSECTIONALITY, in the second paragraph, the following sentence has been corrected:

Co-authors have multiple positionalities, and all but one author are tenured or tenure-track faculty members: a South Asian Muslim man, another Black woman, two South Asian Muslim immigrant women, an Indigenous man and a White immigrant woman.

The sentence now reads as follows:

Co-authors have multiple positionalities, and all but one author are tenured or tenure-track faculty members: a South Asian Muslim man, another Black woman, one South Asian Muslim Immigrant woman, one South Asian Hindu woman with a disability, an Indigenous man and a White immigrant woman.
