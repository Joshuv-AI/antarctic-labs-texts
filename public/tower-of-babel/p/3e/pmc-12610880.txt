4. Corrections to Wigner–Seitz Radius Mentioned in Section 5.1

The change in the effective masses affects the dimensionless Wigner–Seitz radius rs. This is important because we used rs to assess the possible relevance of the electron–electron interactions on the Landau level spectrum. The effective mass values for the conduction bands CB and CB+1 given in the original manuscript were significantly heavier than the corrected values shown in Table 1. Therefore, one may expect that the electron–electron interactions are less important than originally anticipated because of the larger kinetic energy (smaller effective mass) of the charge carriers.

The Wigner–Seitz radius is given by rs=1/(πneaB*), where ne is the electron density, aB*=aB(κme/m*) is the effective Bohr radius, m* is the effective mass, κ is the dielectric constant, and aB is the Bohr radius. Taking κ=5 and an electron density of n=4·1012 cm−2, one finds rs=2.35 for the heavier conduction band, where m*/me=0.22.
