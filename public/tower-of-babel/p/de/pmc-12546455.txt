Transcriptome and methane emission dataset of indica and japonica rice cultivars (93–11, Milyang352, Milyang392) at tillering and heading stages
Authors: Jae-Hyeon Oh, HwangWeon Jeong, Minwoo Kim, Mihyun Cho, Eunhee Kim
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractThis dataset comprises transcriptome profiles and methane emission measurements collected from roots and stems (including leaves) of three rice cultivars: Indica 93–11, Japonica Milyang352, and Milyang392 (an anther-culture line derived from 93–11 × Milyang352 with a high Indica gene proportion). Notably, Milyang392 exhibited the lowest methane emission among the three cultivars, highlighting its potential for climate-resilient breeding. Samples were collected at the tillering stage (June 28 and July 12, 2022) and heading stage (July 26 and August 11, 2022), with three biological replicates per condition, totaling 72 samples. RNA-Seq data were generated using the Illumina HiSeq platform (paired-end, 150 bp), and low-quality reads (Phred < 20, length < 50 bp) were filtered using Trimmomatic and BBDuk. Reads were aligned to the Oryza sativa pan-genome (TGSRICEPAN) using HISAT2, and gene-level read counts were computed using HTSeq.Differentially expressed genes (DEGs) were identified using DESeq2 with thresholds of |log2 fold change| ≥ 1 and adjusted p-value < 0.05. Maximum detected DEGs were 19,267 (root) and 17,165 (stem).Methane emissions were measured using gas chromatography (GC, Agilent 7890B) from the same biological replicates, showing the highest emission in 93–11 (15.2 mg/m²/h), followed by Milyang352 (12.8 mg/m²/h) and Milyang392 (9.5 mg/m²/h). These values correspond to 2.00 ± 0.12 kg/ha for 93–11, 1.57 ± 0.06 kg/ha for Milyang352, and 1.17 ± 0.01 kg/ha for Milyang392. The dataset is useful for studies of methane-related gene expression, rice metabolic pathways, and climate-resilient crop breeding, providing a valuable resource for machine learning applications and understanding genotype-specific responses.Keywords: Rice (Oryza sativa), Methane emission, Transcriptome, RNA-Seq, Indica–Japonica cross, Differential gene expression, Auxin signalling, Flavonoid biosynthesis

Background

Methane emissions from rice paddies significantly contribute to global greenhouse gases due to microbial activity under anaerobic conditions.

Rice roots release organic compounds, such as auxins and flavonoids, which serve as substrates or signaling molecules for methanogens. Understanding the molecular mechanisms by which rice genotypes influence these root exudates is crucial for developing strategies to mitigate methane emissions.

Understanding how different rice genotypes regulate these pathways under flooded conditions is crucial for developing climate-resilient cultivars.

This dataset focuses on transcriptomic responses of three rice cultivars—93–11 (Indica), Milyang352 (Japonica), and Milyang392 (an Indica-rich doubled haploid line)—grown in methane-producing paddy soils.

Root and stem tissues were sampled at two key developmental stages (tillering and heading) to capture dynamic gene expression patterns related to methane emissions.

By analyzing differential expression of auxin- and flavonoid-related genes and correlating them with methane emission data, this study provides insight into genotype-specific responses that influence methane production.

The data support further research on gene-environment interactions, microbial ecology, and greenhouse gas mitigation through rice breeding.

Data Description

This dataset comprises 72 high-quality transcriptomic samples obtained from Oryza sativa cultivars 93–11 (Indica), Milyang352 (Japonica), and Milyang392 (Indica-dominant DH line), collected at the tillering and heading stages under controlled flooded paddy conditions. It includes RNA-Seq raw data, processed gene counts, DEG outputs, and methane emission data measured using static chambers and gas chromatography. All RNA-Seq data consistently show high quality, with over 92.1 % of reads having a Q30 score (Table 2). Experimental Design, Materials and Methods

Plant materials and growth conditions

Cultivars 93–11 (Indica), Milyang352 (Japonica), and Milyang392 (Indica-dominant DH line) were cultivated in controlled flooded paddy soil at 25–30 °C. Sampling was performed at tillering (June 28, July 12) and heading (July 26, August 11) stages in 2022. Ambient environmental conditions during sampling periods in Jeonju, South Korea (based on KMA historical data):

RNA extraction and sequencing

Total RNA was extracted using the RNeasy Plant Mini Kit (Qiagen) and treated with DNase. RNA integrity was confirmed (RIN > 8) via Agilent 2100 Bioanalyzer. Sequencing was performed on the Illumina HiSeq platform with PE150 reads.

Read preprocessing and alignment

Low-quality reads were removed using Trimmomatic v0.39 and BBDuk v38.87. Clean reads were aligned to the Oryza sativa reference genome (IRGSP-1.0) using HISAT2 v2.2.1.

Gene quantification and DEG analysis

Read counts were generated using HTSeq v2.0.1. Differential expression was assessed with DESeq2 v1.36.0 (|log2FC| ≥ 1, adjusted p < 0.05).

Methane emission measurement

Methane emissions were measured using GC (Agilent 7890B) from static chambers. Measurements were performed on three biological replicates for each condition. Emission rates were calculated in mg/m²/h.
