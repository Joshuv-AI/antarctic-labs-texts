Message from Organising Chairman & Organising Secretary
Published: 2025

It gives us immense pleasure to present the Abstract Compendium of NAPCON 2025, Jaipur the 27th National Conference on Pulmonary Diseases, organized under the aegis of the Indian Chest Society at Jaipur — the vibrant Pink City of India. This year’s theme, “Era of Pulmonary Revolution – Back to Pink,” reflects our shared commitment to advancing the science and practice of respiratory medicine through innovation, collaboration, and compassion.

This abstract volume showcases a wide spectrum of research and clinical work contributed by clinicians, academicians, and young investigators from across the country. The studies and case reports featured here highlight contemporary trends, innovative ideas, and evidence-based approaches in diverse areas of pulmonology — including airway and interstitial lung diseases, interventional pulmonology, sleep medicine, and critical care.

Publication of these abstracts in Lung India is a recognition of the scientific spirit that defines NAPCON. It celebrates the enthusiasm, effort, and dedication of all contributors who continue to push the boundaries of respiratory medicine. We hope this compendium serves as both a valuable reference and an inspiration for ongoing research aimed at improving respiratory health and patient outcomes.

We extend our heartfelt gratitude to all authors, reviewers, faculty, and organizing team members for their unwavering support in making NAPCON 2025 a memorable and enriching academic experience. May this conference reinforce our commitment to excellence, ignite new ideas, and ultimately deliver better lung health for India.

We warmly welcome you all to NAPCON 2025 and look forward to your active participation in this scientific celebration.

Warm regards,

Dr. K. K. Sharma

Organising Chairman,

NAPCON 2025, Jaipur
