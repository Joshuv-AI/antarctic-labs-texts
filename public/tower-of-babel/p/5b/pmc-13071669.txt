Hsa_circ_0005372 function as both sponging miR-877-3p and interacting with RNA binding proteins DNMT1 to promote proliferation of human pulmonary smooth muscle cells
Authors: Danyan Su, Yuqin Huang, Bingbing Ye, Yanyun Huang, Yusheng Pang
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
BackgroundCircular RNAs (circRNAs) may affect pulmonary artery hypertension (PAH) by regulating the proliferation of pulmonary artery smooth muscle cells (PASMCs), but the specific mechanism has not been fully elucidated. This study aimed to explore the role of hsa_circ_0005372(circ_0005372) in the proliferation of PASMCs.MethodsInterference lentivirus was used to silence circ_0005372 expression in human PASMCs and to detect changes in cell proliferation by Cell Counting Kit-8 (CCK-8) and 5-ethynyl-2'-deoxyuridine (EdU). RNA antisense purification (RAP) technology was used to screen miRNAs and proteins that may bind to circ_0005372. A double luciferase experiment was used to verify the targeted regulation relationship between circ_0005372/miR-877 and miR-877/adenylate kinase 4 (AK4), and combined with the results of RAP and fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH) to analyze the mechanism of circ_0005372.ResultsCirc_0005372 in PASMCs subjected to hypoxic conditions for 24 h was upregulated, and circ_0005372 was mainly distributed in the cytoplasm. MiR-877-3p was downregulated, and AK4 was upregulated. It was also observed that circ_0005372 and miR-877-3p promoted PASMCs’ proliferation. Circ_0005372 could competitively bind to miR-877-3p and consequently upregulate the target gene of miR-877-3p, AK4. Furthermore, the dual luciferase reporter assay verified the binding between circ_0005372 and miR-877-3p, miR-877-3p and AK4. In addition, RAP protein products were analyzed by liquid chromatography mass spectrometry and it was found that circ_0005372 can bind to DNA methyltransferase 1 (DNMT1).ConclusionsThe results showed that circ_0005372 targeted the regulation of miR-877-3p/AK4 axis and may directly bind to DNMT1 to promote the proliferation of PASMCs.

Key findings

• Circ_0005372 targeted the regulation of miR-877-3p/adenylate kinase 4 (AK4) axis and may directly bind to DNA methyltransferase 1 to promote the proliferation of pulmonary artery smooth muscle cells (PASMCs).

The role of circ_0005372 in regulating PASMCs’ proliferation remains largely unexplored in prior literature. This study provides experimental evidence suggesting that circ_0005372 is functionally associated with the proliferative activity of PASMCs.

• Further study into the molecular mechanisms of circ_0005372 regulating PASMC proliferation is needed to explore new targets for PAH control.

Introduction

Pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) encompasses a group of diseases characterized by increased pulmonary vascular resistance. It can occur at any age during childhood and can cause right heart failure or even death in severe cases (1). The pathogenesis of PAH is complex, including thrombosis, pulmonary vasoconstriction, abnormal proliferation and apoptosis of the inner, middle, and outer membranes. The accumulation of extracellular matrix and excessive proliferation of vascular smooth muscle cells (VSMCs) play a crucial role in pulmonary vascular remodeling, thereby promoting pulmonary arteriolar thickening. VSMCs are the main cellular components of arteries and play important roles in various vascular diseases such as systemic hypertension, pulmonary hypertension, and vascular stenosis. Chronic hypoxic-induced PAH is a common type of PAH. Hypoxic pulmonary vasoconstriction (HPV) exists in the pulmonary ring that removes endothelial cells, so HPV is inherent in pulmonary artery smooth muscle cells (PASMCs). Meanwhile, hypoxia is a trigger that promotes PASMCs’ proliferation and participates in vascular remodeling of PAH (2).

Excessive proliferation of PASMCs is an important pathological mechanism of PAH and pulmonary vascular remodeling. The proliferation-promoting factor endothelin-1 (ET-1), platelet-derived growth factor (PDGF) stimulation, oxidative stress injury, and energy metabolism changes are involved in the mechanism of excessive PASMCs proliferation (3,4). However, the reasons for enhanced PASMCs’ proliferation are still unknown and require urgent investigation. The combination of several trigger factors and genetic, and more importantly, epigenetic mechanisms can change the proliferation and migration of PASMCs. Non-coding RNAs (ncRNAs) play an important role in epigenetics. Circular RNAs (circRNAs) are a type of ncRNAs that widely exist in various biological cells and function in the regulation of gene expression. Current evidence from several studies indicates that circRNAs promotes the proliferation and migration of PASMCs, thereby contributing to pulmonary vascular remodeling (5). CircNAP1L4 and chromatin-associated circFOXP1 regulate hypoxia-induced PASMCs’ proliferation (6,7). Despite these advances, the circRNA landscape in PAH remains largely unexplored, and the functional contribution of the vast majority of circRNAs is unknown. Therefore, from the perspective of circRNAs, we aimed to study the aetiology and pathogenesis of high pulmonary blood flow PAH. It is of great importance to reverse pulmonary vascular disease and explore new targets for PAH prevention and treatment.

We previously performed circRNAs microarray analysis [Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) entry: GSE171827] and found that the plasma of children with PAH exhibited significantly high expression levels of hsa_circ_0005372 (circ_0005372) (8). Circ_0005372, in the pathogenesis of PAH has not been fully investigated. Given its previously uncharacterized status in PAH, we hypothesized that circ_0005372 might play a distinct role in regulating PASMCs’ behavior and vascular remodeling. Therefore, this study aimed to identify the expression profile of circ_0005372 in PAH and to elucidate its functional mechanism in PASMCs. We provide the evidence that circ_0005372 is significantly upregulated in PAH and drives PASMCs’ proliferation by acting as a sponge for miR-877-3p, leading to the derepression of its target gene adenylate kinase 4 (AK4), and further exert its effect by binding to DNA methyltransferase 1 (DNMT1). Our findings not only uncover a novel circRNA-mediated regulatory axis in PAH pathogenesis but also position circ_0005372 as a potential diagnostic biomarker and therapeutic target, thereby offering new insights beyond the currently established circRNAs networks in PAH. We present this article in accordance with the MDAR reporting checklist (available at https://tp.amegroups.com/article/view/10.21037/tp-2025-1-886/rc).

Methods

Hypoxia cell model preparation

Human pulmonary artery smooth muscle cells (HPASMCs) were purchased from Punosai Life Science and Technology Co., Ltd. (Wuhan, China) and cultured in a complete medium containing 10% fetal bovine serum. The HPASMCs were isolated from the pulmonary trunk. Normoxic HPASMCs group: cells were cultured in a moist incubator continuously supplied with 21% O2, 5% CO2, and balanced with 74% N2 at 37 ℃. Hypoxia HPASMCs group: cells were placed in a special hypoxia incubator, mixed with 3% O2 and 5% CO2, and balanced with 99.9% N2 at 37 ℃. HPASMCs were exposed to hypoxia for 24, 48, and 72 h to evaluate the expression of circRNA, miRNA, and mRNA. All kits were used as per the manufacturer’s instructions.

Reverse transcription-quantitative polymerase chain reaction (qPCR)

The total RNA of cells was extracted using RNA-Quick Purification Kit (ES Science Biotechnology Co., Ltd., Shanghai, China). For circ_0005372 and AK4, total RNA was reverse transcribed into cDNA using HiScript RT SuperMix for qPCR (+gDNA wiper) (Vazyme Biotech Co., Ltd., Nanjing, China). Quantitative reverse transcription polymerase chain reaction (qRT-PCR) was prepared using ChamQTM Universal SYBR qPCR Master Mix (Vazyme Biotech Co., Ltd.). The qPCR results were normalized to the reference gene β-actin. For miRNAs, reverse transcription and qPCR reagents used were Mir-X miRNA First-Strand Synthesis Kit (Takara Bio Inc., Shiga, Japan) and Mir-X miRNA qRT-PCR TB Green® Kit (Takara Bio Inc.). U6 functioned as the reference for miRNAs. All primers are listed in Table 1. Gene relative expression levels were then calculated using the 2−∆∆Ct method.

RNase R enzyme digestion

The assay conditions were as follows: HPASMCs were incubated with RNAase R (Geneseed Biotech Co., Ltd., Guangzhou, China) digestion, for 10 min at 37 ℃, then maintained at 70 ℃ to inactivate the enzyme. This was immediately followed by the reverse transcription reaction and qRT-PCR.

Cellular localization

HPASMCs were cultured as described above. Cell precipitates were collected by centrifugation at 3,000 g for 5 min after reaching 80–90%, cell fusion and cell fractionation and cell lysis were conducted using Nuclear and Cytoplasmic Extraction Reagent (Thermofisher Scientific, Waltham, USA) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. After 30–50% of the cells were fused, the cells were fixed, permeabilized, and probe hybridization was performed. The circ_0005372, 18S, U6 probes were prepared, and the fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH) test kit (RiBo Biotech Co., Ltd., Guangzhou, China) was used to verify cellular localization of circ_0005372. Fluorescence was detected by a confocal microscope.

Cell transfection

Circ_0005372 interfering lentivirus was designed and synthesized by Genechem (Shanghai, China); three RNA interference target sequences were designed for the cyclization site of circ_0005372 and sequences are listed in Table 2. Cells were placed into 6-well plates for lentivirus transfection. When the optimal multiplicity of infection was 50, the transfection efficiency was improved, and the effect on cell status was small. MiR-877-3p mimics and inhibitors were designed and synthesized by RiBo Biotech. Lipofectamine 3000 reagent (Invitrogen, Carlsbad, USA) was used as transfection medium according to the manufacturer’s protocol. HPASMCs transfected with miR-877-3p mimics/inhibitors and NC or lentiviral interference vector were exposed to hypoxia for 24 h, followed by subsequent expression detection and functional experiment.

Cell proliferation assay

Cell proliferation was detected by the Cell Counting Kit-8 (CCK-8) method and 5-ethynyl-2’-deoxyuridine (EdU) cell proliferation assay. Cells were placed into 96-well plates, 10 µL CCK-8 detection reagent was added into each well, and incubated for 1 h at 37 ℃, before measuring the absorbance at 450 nm with a full wavelength microplate reader. The number of cells in each group remained basically the same, so that there was no statistical difference in their absorbance values at 24 h, and then compared the changes in absorbance values at 48, 72 and 96 h. The EdU cell proliferation assay was performed using the Cell-Light EdU Apollo567 In Vitro Kit (RiBo Biotech Co., Ltd.). Cells were placed into 96-well plates for 24 h, then 100 µL 50 µM EdU medium was added to each well and incubated for 2 h. Next, cell fixation and permeability staining were performed, and visualized and captured using the Olympus fluorescence microscope, with a magnification were 100×.

RNA antisense purification (RAP)

The circ_0005372 biotin-labelled probe was constructed using BersinBio Bioscience (Guangzhou, China), and then hybridized with the cell extract for incubation using the RAP Kit (BersinBio Bioscience Co., Ltd.). In total, 4×107 293T cells were collected by precipitation for lysis, DNA removal, hybridization, and capture. The RNAs and proteins that interacted with it were attached to the magnetic beads. The RNA-binding products were detected by qPCR to further verify the downstream target genes regulated by the RNA transcription. The protein products were collected for silver staining analysis, and the unknown proteins were identified by liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry (LC-MS).

Dual luciferase reporter assay

The circ_0005372 and AK4-3’UTR double luciferase reporter vectors and mutants were constructed by RiBo Biotech. Briefly, 293T cells in the logarithmic growth stage were inoculated into 24-well plates. The miR-877-3p mimics/inhibitor or non-target controls, the circ_0005372 and AK4 3’-UTR wild-type (WT) or mutant (MUT) reporter vectors were combined and diluted to the target concentration using Opti-MEM medium (Grand Island, New York, USA). The mixture was then transfected into cells using Lipofectamine 3000 reagent (Invitrogen). After 24 h of transfection and incubation, remove the cell culture plate from the incubator, cells were washed and lysed using the Dual-Luciferase® Reporter Assay Kit (Promega, Madison, USA). Firefly and Renilla luciferase activities were measured with a GloMax® Navigator microplate luminometer (Promega), and their ratios were calculated accordingly.

Western blot assay

Total protein was extracted from cultured HPASMCs with RIPA lysate (Solarbio Science & Technology Co., Ltd., Beijing, China), and the concentration of protein to be measured was calculated by the bicinchoninic acid assay (BCA) protein quantitative method. After sodium dodecyl sulfate-polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis (SDS-PAGE) electrophoresis, wet membrane transfer and skimmed milk sealing, anti-AK4 (1:1,000; Abmart Co., Ltd., Shanghai, China), DNMT1 (1:1,000; Abmart Co., Ltd.), and anti-β-actin were incubated overnight at 4 ℃, and then the secondary antibody was incubated at room temperature for 1 h. Tris Buffered Saline with Tween 20 (TBST) elution, membrane scanning, image analysis.

Statistical analysis

SPSS 26.0 and GraphPad Prism 9.0 were used for statistical analysis. The data are expressed as X ± standard deviation (SD). The independent sample t-test is used for the comparison between the two groups, and the one-way analysis of variance (ANOVA) of completely random design is used for the comparison between multiple groups. In addition, two-way ANOVA is used for data analysis in the CCK-8 assays. P<0.05 indicates that the difference is statistically significant.

Expression of circ_0005372 in HPASMCs of each group

We conducted hypoxia experiments on the cells and found that the expression of circ_0005372 in HPASMCs in the 24 h hypoxia group and 48 h hypoxia group was significantly increased, suggesting that circ_0005372 was upregulated in hypoxic HPASMCs. Cells from the 24 h hypoxia group with the most significant difference in circ_0005372 expression were selected for subsequent experiments (Figure 1C).

Characterization and clinical features of circ_0005372

Circ_0005372 is located on a fragment of the RASA2 characteristic gene chromosome 3 at position 141272698-141278837, with a total length of 336bp. It is formed by the reverse shearing of the host gene RASA2 exon 6–9, and the cyclization site of the splicing is CTGCAT. The qRT-PCR products of circ_0005372 were verified by Sanger sequencing (Figure 1D). To verify the structural stability of circ_0005372, enzyme digestion experiments were performed using RNase R. There was no significant difference in circ_0005372 expression in the RNase R+ group, but the expression levels of RASA2 mRNA and β-actin were significantly decreased (Figure 1E). Agarose gel electrophoresis results showed that the cDNA reverse transcribed from total RNA and the genomic DNA directly extracted could be amplified by circ_0005372 convergent primers, while only cDNA could be amplified by the circ_0005372 divergent primers (Figure 1F).

Cellular localization of circ_0005372

The nucleocytoplasmic fractionation assay and qRT-PCR confirmed that circ_0005372 was mainly located in the cytoplasm. At the same time, FISH experiments were conducted with HPASMCs, and the results suggested that the reference probes of U6 and 18S were located in the nucleus and cytoplasm, respectively. Furthermore, the hybridization results of the circ_0005372 probe suggested that the reference probes were mainly located in the cytoplasm, which was consistent with the results of nucleocytoplasmic separation (Figure 1G,1H).

Circ_0005372 suppresses HPASMCs’ proliferation

After HPASMCs were transfected with three groups of lentiviral interference vectors and negative control vectors designed for cyclization sites, the interference efficiency of each lentiviral interference vector was detected by qRT-PCR. The expression of circ_0005372 in each HPASMC group was significantly decreased, but RASA2 mRNA expression was not affected. This suggests that the lentiviral interference vector works by interfering with circRNA rather than linear RASA2. LV-RNAi-42 was selected as the best lentivirus interference vector for subsequent experiments (Figure 2A). CCK-8 and EdU cell proliferation assays were used to detect HPASMCs’ proliferation activity after transfection with a lentiviral interference vector. The results suggested that HPASMCs showed excessive proliferation under hypoxic conditions, and the proliferation activity of HPASMCs was inhibited after the expression of circ_0005372 was downregulated (Figure 2B-2D). This suggests that circ_0005372 promotes the proliferation of HPASMCs.

Identification of circ_0005372 interacting miRNAs

The biotin probe group was designed, and miRNAs bound to circ_0005372 were screened by an RAP technique. It was first confirmed that endogenous circ_0005372 was significantly enriched in the circ_0005372 probe group (Figure 2E,2F). Bioinformatics methods were used to predict miRNAs regulated by circ_0005372 targeting using TargetScan and miRanda, and the following 5 candidate miRNAs were finally determined through a literature review. Electrophoretic images showed that the electrophoretic bands of miR-877-3p, miR-365a-5p, and miR-216a-5p in the circ_0005372 probe group were more obvious than those in the LacZ control probe group. Meanwhile, the %Input and enrichment ratio of miR-877-3p, miR-365a-5p, and miR-216a-5p in the circ_0005372 probe group were significantly increased (Figure 2G-2K). After the interference of circ_0005372 expression by the lentiviral vector, the expression of miR-873-5p and miR-877-3p was significantly increased (Figure 3A). Combined with the RAP results, this suggests that circ_0005372 had a reverse regulatory relationship with miR-877-3p. Dual Luciferase reporter assay results confirmed the specific binding of circ_0005372 to miR-877-3p (Figure 3B).

MiR-877-3p promotes HPASMCs’ proliferation

The effects of miR-877-3p and its regulatory interaction with circ_0005372 were assessed, with the full results presented in Figure 3C-3I. miR-877-3p mimics and miR-877-3p inhibitor were transfected into cells. CCK-8 and EdU cell proliferation assays were used to detect HPASMCs’ proliferation activity after transfection. Overexpression of miR-877-3p inhibited HPASMCs’ proliferation activity, and inhibition of miR-877-3p expression promoted HPASMCs’ proliferation activity (Figure 3C,3D,3F,3G).

Circ_0005372 serves as a miRNA sponge of miR-877-3p to regulate AK4 expression

The rescue experiment suggested that interference with circ_0005372 and overexpression of miR-877-3p inhibited HPASMCs’ proliferation activity, while inhibition of miR-877-3p expression promoted HPASMCs’ proliferation activity and reversed the inhibition of cell proliferation caused by interference with circ_0005372 (Figure 3E,3H,3I). The expression of miR-877-3p was downregulated, and AK4 protein was upregulated after 24 hours of hypoxia (Figure 4A,4B). The expression of AK4 mRNA was significantly inhibited by lentivirus interference with the circ_0005372 vector (Figure 4C). Overexpression of miR-877-3p significantly reduced the relative expression of AK4 mRNA, while inhibition of miR-877-3p significantly increased the relative expression of AK4 mRNA (Figure 4D). Inhibition of miR-877-3p significantly upregulated AK4 protein expression and reversed the AK4 protein decline caused by circ_0005372 lentivirus interference (Figure 4E). Dual Luciferase reporter assay results confirmed the specific binding of miR-877-3p to AK4 (Figure 4F).

Discussion

PASMCs have been widely confirmed to play an important role in the occurrence and development of various types of PAH. PASMCs’ proliferation and pulmonary vascular remodeling are important to the pathophysiological basis of pulmonary hypertension. Different mechanisms eventually lead to uncontrolled proliferation of PASMCs through apoptotic resistance, activation of hypoxia-inducing factor, histone deacetylase modification, and the inflammatory response, leading to pulmonary hypertension (9).

The balance between PASMCs’ proliferation and apoptosis is important for maintaining the integrity of the normal pulmonary vascular structure and function. However, in severe vascular proliferative PAH, this balance seems to be broken, with increased PASMCs’ proliferation and decreased apoptosis, leading to vascular wall thickening and vascular remodeling (10). At the molecular level, PAH PASMCs exhibit many characteristics similar to those of cancer cells, with increased proliferation and decreased apoptosis, leading to the assumption that pulmonary hypertension is related to tumors (11). Therefore, many therapies used to treat cancer may provide references for exploring potential treatments for PAH. However, the regulatory mechanism of PASMCs’ proliferation in PAH is still to be fully elucidated and needs to be further explored. Studies have shown that the combination of several triggering factors with genetic, and particularly epigenetic mechanisms, can alter the proliferation and migration of PASMCs (12). NcRNAs play an important role in epigenetics. CircRNAs are a class of ncRNAs that widely exist in various biological cells and have the function of regulating gene expression. The mechanism of their participation in the proliferation and migration of VSMCs is attracting increasing attention (13). Therefore, further study into the molecular mechanisms of circ_0005372 regulating PASMCs’ proliferation is needed to explore new targets for PAH control.

Current research shows that only 2% of the genome is actually translated into proteins, most of which are composed of non-coding genetic information, and it is found that circRNAs can regulate the occurrence and progression of diseases (14). Most circRNAs are localized in the cytoplasm, which prompted researchers to study the function of circRNAs in post-transcriptional regulation (15). CircRNAs have diverse biological functions and can be used as a sponge or bait for miRNAs to inhibit their effects. It can also bind to RNA-binding proteins and can be used as a sponge or bait for these proteins to indirectly regulate their functions. Furthermore, it can also interact with specific proteins to enhance their functions (16). At present, research has found that hsa_circ_0002062 acts as a competing endogenous RNA (ceRNA) and a sponge for hsa-miR-942-5p, thereby activating CDK6 and promoting pulmonary vascular remodeling (17). M5C-modified circCCNL2 can slow the development of PH and inhibit the proliferation of PASMCs by binding to FXR2 (18). These studies elucidated the functional mechanism of circRNAs from the mechanism of the miRNA sponge and the mechanism of interaction with proteins. And one of them added m5C modification, to further study the regulatory mechanism of gene transcription and translation.

To explore the specific molecular mechanism of circ_0005372 regulating PASMCs’ proliferation in this study, RAP experiments were used to screen miRNAs bound to circ_0005372, and the classical ceRNA regulatory mechanism of circRNAs was first discussed. The results showed that miR-877-3p had a specific binding relationship with circ_0005372. Previous studies have shown that miR-877-3p is differentially expressed in a variety of diseases and plays a key role in the occurrence and development of diseases (19). Meanwhile, circRNAs and lncRNAs bind to miR-877-3p as ceRNA-targeted mRNA, and play an important role in the pathogenesis, proliferation, apoptosis, metastasis, and chemotherapy resistance of other diseases, such as tumors. Studies have found that LINC00482 may inhibit osteoblast proliferation and osteogenic differentiation by targeting miR-877-3p, thereby impeding fracture healing (20). Circ-RanGAP1-mediated miR-877-3p/VEGFA axis promotes gastric cancer progression, suggesting that circ-RanGAP1 may serve as a potential prognostic biomarker and therapeutic target for gastric cancer treatment (21). However, the role of miR-877-3p in PAH is poorly understood. Our study found that miR-877-3p can regulate PASMCs’ proliferation and is also regulated by circ_0005372, suggesting that circ_0005372 regulates PASMCs’ proliferation by mediating miR-877-3p, providing a new basis for the mechanism of PAH research.

AK4 is the downstream target gene of miR-877-3p. In this study, it was found that AK4 has an expression correlation and a targeted regulation relationship with circ_0005372 and miR-877-3p. AK4 is a potent oncogene involved in the malignant progression of a variety of tumors (22), many studies on AK4 have focused on tumor cells (23,24). Recent evidence highlights AK4 as a key regulator of energy metabolism and mitochondrial homeostasis. Studies have demonstrated that the AK4/Parkin/PKM axis protects against cerebral ischemic injury by modulating neuronal metabolic flux and mitochondrial autophagy (25). In the context of vascular remodeling, several investigations further identify AK4 as a novel metabolic regulator in PASMCs, where it interacts with HIF-1α and Akt signaling to promote pro-proliferative and glycolytic phenotypes characteristic of pulmonary hypertension (26). Moreover, AK4 has been shown to bind to protein kinase AMP-activated catalytic subunit alpha (AMPKα), thereby enhancing mitochondrial function and positively regulating VSMC dedifferentiation, proliferation, and migration (27).

In addition to the ceRNA mechanism, circRNAs has another function to interact with proteins to regulate gene transcription. In this study, DNMT1 specifically bound to circ_0005372. DNMT1 was a member of the DNA methyltransferase family, plays an important role in DNA methylation and can maintain the methylation of newly replicated DNA. DNA methylation is one of the most widely studied epigenetic changes, and abnormal DNA methylation is associated with a variety of biological processes, including PASMCs’ proliferation, immunity, programmed cell death (28), and cardiac fibrosis (29). Studies have shown that circRNA can bind DNMT1 and then affecting gene transcription level and regulating tumor cell metastasis (30,31). In addition, it has been confirmed that DNMT1 regulates PASMCs’ proliferation in hypoxia (32). In the present study, we propose that circ_0005372 likely regulates PASMCs’ proliferation via direct interaction with DNMT1. However, whether this regulation involves additional signaling cascades, such as the MAPK/ERK pathway, or results from DNMT1-mediated methylation of downstream target genes remains to be fully elucidated. In subsequent studies, we plan to systematically dissect the associated signaling networks and epigenetic regulatory layers underlying this interaction.

Some limitations should be acknowledged in this study. Firstly, the findings are based entirely on in vitro experiments using human PASMCs, and the sample size of specimens analyzed was relatively small. Future studies should therefore expand the cohort and further investigate relevant animal models of PAH to validate the role of circ_0005372 in pulmonary vascular remodeling under more complex physiological conditions.

Conclusions

In summary, our study explored two mechanisms of ceRNA and circRNAs interacting with proteins and found that circ_0005372 promotes PASMCs’ proliferation by targeting the miR-877-3/AK4 axis and may directly bind DNMT1 to regulate PASMCs’ proliferation.
