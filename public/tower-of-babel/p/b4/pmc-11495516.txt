Comment on “Bibliometric analysis of dendritic cell-based vaccines over the past 15 years”
Authors: Hua Zhao, Jiayue Xu, Kun Xu, Yuejun Zhou
Published: 2024

Dear editor,

We read with great interest the paper published in Human Vaccines & Immunotherapeutics entitled “Bibliometric analysis of dendritic cell-based vaccines over the past 15 years” by Zhi Long et al.1 This article uses advanced bibliometric methods to conduct a comprehensive visual analysis of publications, countries, institutions, journals, authors and keyword information of dendritic cell-based vaccines within the WoSCC database, and summarizes the research hotspots and frontier trends in this research field. We highly support and appreciate the researchers’ work, and thank them for their contributions in the field of dendritic cell-based vaccines. However, we have doubts about the two data in the article, which will be commented critically as follows.

In the analysis of main authors, this article first describes “Figdor, CG emerges as the most prolific author with 66 publications, followed by de Vries IJM (n = 50) and Ishii, KJ (n = 49),” but according to Table 1, de Vries IJM published 64 articles totally, which is an obvious error.Table 1.Top 11 prolific authors and co-cited authors on dendritic cell-based vaccine research from 2009 to 2023.RankAuthorPublicationsCitationsCo-cited authorCo-citations1Figdor, CG664551Banchereau, J23732de Vries IJM643497Steinman, RM22253Ishii, KJ492825Rosenberg, SA14904Pulendran, B488029Palucka, K7185Kiyono, H462184Sallusto, F9976Ossendorp, F442724Pulendran, B9667Berneman, ZN431994Kawai, T8948Kang, SM421457Sahin, U8359Kunisawa, J411278Reddy, ST80610Wang, B41592Iwasaki, A79711de Gruijl, TD401024Kantoff, PW760

In analysis of top co-cited references, this article describes “citation burst dynamics (Figure 1b), where the top 18 references with the most robust citation bursts, ranging from 2.75 to 4.81 in burst intensity, were highlighted”，It is clear that Figure 1b means “Top 18 most frequently cited keywords”，Instead of “Top 18 most frequently cited references.” Moreover, the strength ranging from 2.71 to 5.68. Similarly, in the part of “Analysis of high-frequency keywords,” this article describes “CiteSpace analysis facilitated the identification of keywords associated with the top 20 citation bursts, with red segments denoting the duration of these bursts (Figure 2b).”Obviously, Figure 2b means “Top 18 most frequently cited references,” instead of “Top 18 most frequently cited keywords,” therefore, the positions of Figures 1b and 2b should be reversed. The research work of Zhi Long et al. represents the inaugural comprehensive analysis of global trends in dendritic cell-based vaccine research, helps us understand the research hotspots, evolution paths, emerging topics, and future research trends in the field of dendritic cell-based vaccines. However, it is worth noting that the data of the article should be accurately to ensure that researchers obtain correct information.
