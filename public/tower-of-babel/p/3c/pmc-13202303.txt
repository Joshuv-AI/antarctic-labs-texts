Assessment of the feed additive consisting of 6‐phytase (4a23) (produced with Komagataella phaffii DSM 25375) (ENZY PHOSTAR ® P/L) for all poultry species, weaned piglets, pigs for fattening, sows and minor porcine species for the renewal of its authorisation (Kaesler Nutrition GmbH)
Authors: EFSA Panel on Additives and Products or Substances used in Animal Feed (FEEDAP), Roberto Edoardo Villa, Giovanna Azimonti, Eleftherios Bonos, Henrik Christensen, Mojca Durjava, Birgit Dusemund, Ronette Gehring, Boet Glandorf, Maryline Kouba, Marta López‐Alonso, Francesca Marcon, Giovanna Martelli, Carlo Nebbia, Alena Pechová, Miguel Prieto‐Maradona, Katerina Theodoridou, Stéphane Bretagne, Lieve Herman, Montserrat Anguita, Nicole Bozzi Cionci, Matteo Lorenzo Innocenti, Jordi Tarrés‐Call, Elisa Pettenati
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractFollowing a request from the European Commission, EFSA was asked to deliver a scientific opinion on the assessment of the application for the renewal of the authorisation of 6‐phytase (produced with Komagataella phaffii DSM 25375) (ENZY PHOSTAR® P/L) as a zootechnical additive (digestibility enhancer) for use in feed for all poultry species, weaned piglets, pigs for fattening, sows and minor porcine species (4a23). The applicant provided evidence that the additive currently in the market complies with the existing conditions of the authorisation. The FEEDAP Panel concluded that the use of the feed additive ENZY PHOSTAR® P/L in animal nutrition remains safe for the target animals, consumer and for the environment under the approved conditions of the authorisation. Regarding the safety for the user, ENZY PHOSTAR® P is not irritant to skin or eyes but is a dermal and respiratory sensitiser. ENZY PHOSTAR® L is considered irritant to skin and eyes and a respiratory sensitiser. Exposure of users to both forms of the additive by any route is considered a risk. The present application for renewal of the authorisation does not include any modification proposal that would have an impact on the efficacy of the additive, and therefore, there is no need for re‐assessing the efficacy.

INTRODUCTION

Background and Terms of Reference

Regulation (EC) No 1831/2003 1 establishes the rules governing the Community authorisation of additives for use in animal nutrition. In particular, Article 14(1) of that Regulation lays down that an application for renewal shall be sent to the Commission at the latest 1 year before the expiry date of the authorisation.

The European Commission received a request from Kaesler Nutrition GmbH 2 for the renewal of the authorisation of the additive consisting of 6‐phytase (produced with Komagataella phaffii 3 DSM 25375) (ENZY PHOSTAR® P/L), when used as a feed additive for chickens for fattening and reared for laying, all avian species for fattening and reared for laying other than turkeys for fattening and reared for breeding, all avian species for laying, turkeys for fattening and reared for breeding, piglets (weaned), pigs for fattening, sows and minor porcine species (category: zootechnical additives; functional group: digestibility enhancers).

According to Article 7(1) of Regulation (EC) No 1831/2003, the Commission forwarded the application to the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) as an application under Article 14(1) (renewal of the authorisation). The dossier was received on 30 April 2025 and the general information and supporting documentation are available at https://open.efsa.europa.eu/questions/EFSA‐Q‐2025‐00287. The particulars and documents in support of the application were considered valid by EFSA as of 10 September 2025.

According to Article 8 of Regulation (EC) No 1831/2003, EFSA, after verifying the particulars and documents submitted by the applicant, shall undertake an assessment in order to determine whether the feed additive complies with the conditions laid down in Article 5. EFSA shall deliver an opinion on the safety for the target animals, consumer, user and the environment and on the efficacy of the feed additive consisting of 6‐phytase (produced with K. phaffii DSM 25375) (ENZY PHOSTAR® P/L) when used under the proposed conditions of use (see Section 3.1.2).

Additional information

The additive is currently authorised for use in feed for all avian species and for weaned piglets, pigs for fattening, sows and minor porcine species (4a23). 4

The additive is a preparation containing 6‐phytase produced with a genetically modified strain of K. phaffii (DSM 25375). EFSA issued an opinion on the safety and efficacy of this product (EFSA FEEDAP Panel, 2015).

DATA AND METHODOLOGIES

Data

The present assessment is based on data submitted by the applicant in the form of a technical dossier 5 in support of the authorisation request for the use of 6‐phytase (produced with K. phaffii DSM 25375) (ENZY PHOSTAR® P/L) as a feed additive.

In accordance with Article 38 of the Regulation (EC) No 178/2002 6 and taking into account the protection of confidential information and of personal data in accordance with Articles 39 to 39e of the same Regulation and of the Decision of EFSA's Executive Director laying down practical arrangements concerning transparency and confidentiality, 7 a non‐confidential version of the dossier has been published on Open.EFSA.

According to Article 32c(2) of Regulation (EC) No 178/2002 and to the Decision of EFSA's Executive Director laying down the practical arrangements on pre‐submission phase and public consultations, EFSA carried out a public consultation on the non‐confidential version of the technical dossier from 3 December to 24 December 2025 for which no comments were received.

The confidential version of the technical dossier was subject to a target consultation of the interested Member States from 15 September to 15 December 2025; the comments received were considered for the assessment.

The FEEDAP Panel used the data provided by the applicant together with data from other sources, such as previous risk assessments by EFSA or other expert bodies, peer‐reviewed scientific papers, other scientific reports and experts' knowledge to deliver the present output.

The European Union Reference Laboratory (EURL) considered that the conclusions and recommendations reached in the previous assessment regarding the methods used for the control of the 6‐phytase in animal feed are valid and applicable for the current application. 8

Methodologies

The approach followed by the FEEDAP Panel to assess the safety and the efficacy of 6‐phytase (produced with K. phaffii DSM 25375) (ENZY PHOSTAR® P/L) is in line with the principles laid down in Regulation (EC) No 429/2008 9 and the relevant guidance documents: Guidance on the renewal of the authorisation of feed additives (EFSA FEEDAP Panel, 2021).

Safety

The safety of ENZY PHOSTAR® P/L for the target species, consumers, users and the environment, including the safety of the production strain, has been evaluated in a previous opinion (EFSA FEEDAP Panel, 2015). The Panel concluded that the genetic modification of the production strain is of no concern, the additive is safe for the target species and the use of the product as a feed additive would be of no concern for the consumers of products derived from animals fed with the additive or for the environment. Regarding the safety for the user, the FEEDAP Panel concluded that the additive is considered to be hazardous by inhalation. ENZY PHOSTAR® P was shown to be a skin sensitiser but not a dermal or eye irritant. No data were provided for ENZY PHOSTAR® L; therefore, the additive was considered a potential irritant to the skin and eyes and a potential skin sensitiser.

The production strain DSM 25375 was previously allocated to the species K. pastoris (EFSA FEEDAP Panel, 2015). In the context of the present application, the production strain was identified by WGS as belonging to the species K. phaffii, which also qualifies for the qualified presumption of safety (QPS) approach to safety assessment when used for production purposes (EFSA BIOHAZ Panel, 2026). The applicant provided information showing that the genetic modifications do not raise safety concerns. No viable cells nor DNA of the production strain were detected in the final formulations of the additive. Therefore, the additive poses no safety concern regarding the genetically modified production strain.

For the present dossier, the applicant stated that no adverse events in any of the target animal species, consumers, users and the environment have been reported since the original authorisation 23 and performed a literature search (covering the period up to 2025) that is described below. 24

The applicant used three databases (Scopus, Web of Science and the meta database of Wageningen University) and the search terms and search strategy were provided. The main search terms regarded the active substance and terms concerning the safety for the target species, consumers, users and the environment. Inclusion and exclusion criteria were specified. The literature search did not identify relevant new information on the safety of the additive for the target species, consumers and the environment but retrieved three publications which further supported the potential of 6‐phytase to be a respiratory sensitiser (Baur et al., 2002; Doekes et al., 1999; O'Connor et al., 2001).

Regarding user safety, the applicant also provided a safety data sheet stating that ENZY PHOSTAR® L is a skin irritant. 25

Conclusions on safety for the target species, consumers, users and environment

Based on the above and the fact that the manufacturing and composition of ENZY PHOSTAR® P/L have not been substantially modified, the FEEDAP Panel concludes that there is no evidence to reconsider the conclusions reached in the previous opinion on the safety of the additive when used as a feed additive in all poultry species, weaned piglets, pigs for fattening, sows and minor porcine species. Therefore, the Panel concludes that the additive remains safe under the approved conditions for these target species, consumers and the environment.

ENZY PHOSTAR® P is not irritant to skin or eyes but is a dermal and respiratory sensitiser. ENZY PHOSTAR® L is considered irritant to skin and eyes and a respiratory sensitiser. Exposure of users to both forms of the additive by any route is considered a risk.

Efficacy

The present application for renewal of the authorisation does not include a proposal for amending or supplementing the conditions of the original authorisation that would have an impact on the efficacy of the additive. Therefore, there is no need for assessing the efficacy of the additive in the context of the renewal of the authorisation.

Post‐market monitoring

The FEEDAP Panel considers that there is no need for specific requirements for a post‐market monitoring plan other than those established in the Feed Hygiene Regulation 26 and good manufacturing practice.

CONCLUSIONS

The applicant provided evidence that the additive currently in the market complies with the existing conditions of the authorisation.

The production strain K. phaffii DSM 25375 does not raise safety concerns regarding the genetic modifications. No viable cells nor DNA of the production strain were detected in the final formulations of the product. Therefore, the FEEDAP Panel concludes that the additive does not pose any safety concern regarding the production strain.

The FEEDAP Panel concludes that the use of the feed additive ENZY PHOSTAR® P/L in animal nutrition remains safe for the target animals (all poultry species, weaned piglets, pigs for fattening, sows and minor porcine species), consumers and for the environment under the approved conditions of the authorisation.

ENZY PHOSTAR® P is not irritant to skin or eyes but is a dermal and respiratory sensitiser. ENZY PHOSTAR® L is considered irritant to skin and eyes and a respiratory sensitiser. Exposure of users to both forms of the additive by any route is considered a risk
