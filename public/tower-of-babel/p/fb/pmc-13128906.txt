Blockchain-driven trust management and AI computing for sensor networks optimization
Authors: Mekhled Alharbi, Khalid Haseeb, N. Z. Jhanjhi, Atif Khan, Mamoona Humayun, Muhammad Attique Khan
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
The Internet of Things (IoT) and emerging technologies have converged to drive the remarkable development of intelligent systems. The interconnection of physical objects, sensors, and tiny communication devices enables data aggregation, which is then forwarded to edge computing for local processing and analysis. Such a system improves response time and enhances network capabilities while managing the massive amount of collected data. On the other hand, existing approaches include cloud-based schemes that leverage edge-level offloading to control and manage demanding traffic. Furthermore, data security and network integrity are ensured by integrating blockchain technology with device identity authentication. However, in a dynamic environment, most approaches still incur interception and data eavesdropping, thereby affecting the reliability of connected communication channels. Therefore, developing trustworthiness and an authenticated system is a significant research challenge for the growth of smart systems. In this research, we introduce a lightweight, trusted AI-driven model to enhance security in complex systems and to ensure a more robust data-forwarding path over the long term. First, optimized methods are introduced that use an adaptive technique to explore network conditions and generate efficient data-transfer decision policies. Secondly, distributed and collaborative interactions are enabled across devices with minimal computing resources, thereby improving the system’s response time through load balancing. Ultimately, trust is continuously updated by leveraging real-time parameters and records of neighbours’ communication, thereby providing fault tolerance and trusted channels. The proposed model is verified and validated for efficacy through a wide range of simulations, and performance results demonstrate its superiority over existing approaches on realistic scenarios and metrics.

Introduction

Smart systems and IoT networks have significantly advanced the innovation paradigms for interacting with physical objects and computing devices1,2. Sensing objects collect data from the environment and forward it to processing stations to provide the required services for interconnected devices3,4. Unlike traditional networks, IoT-based communication systems require load-balanced, consistent forwarding paths to maintain reliable data transfer in uncertain fields5,6. They interact in a distributed manner to form an autonomous network and enable the transmission of real-time data in more crucial environments such as healthcare, smart cities, and transportation systems. In recent decades, smart applications that combine artificial intelligence have gathered data from external sources, thereby enhancing the predictive power of heterogeneous services7,8. The deployed devices at lower layers monitor the network environment and analyse the parameters of interest for remote networks. Due to the use of diverse digital devices, many IoT systems sense and process collected data by leveraging high-speed mobile services9,10. These systems require a significant contribution to establish trust in relaying services and offer the most robust multi-path capabilities to enhance system performance in resource management11,12. The smart applications integrated with a local processing station at the edge analyse environmental data and enable timely decision-making. Numerous approaches to constraint systems have been introduced to limit the processing power of battery-powered devices and enhance system sustainability over longer periods13,14. Despite this, many researchers are still trying to attain effective and uniform task balancing to establish stable routing and prevent routing holes at the network boundaries15,16. Furthermore, as smart devices interact with cloud systems via intermediate, insecure relay nodes17,18, ensuring security management for data confidentiality and integrity is another significant research challenge for IoT-based systems19–21. Because smart technologies are battery-powered and have limited processing and storage capabilities, most existing schemes require robust security and reliable infrastructure to support high-performance systems and timely decision-making. Furthermore, as interconnected devices grow, smart applications need trustworthiness and secure transmission channels to support private and authentic interaction in the unpredictable nature of the connected environment22,23. The primary objective of our research is to propose an AI-driven approach to achieving trustworthiness and to explore efficient load-distribution policies for managing industrial IoT resources. In addition, the network edges are integrated to support task offloading while forwarding IoT data from capturing devices to the cloud platform. It provides a distributed system by integrating load balancing and edge-level data processing tasks with effective management of network scalability. Fault tolerance is achieved through decision-making strategies. During data forwarding, if a network edge fails, the nearby, more reliable edge assumes responsibility for rerouting, reducing disruption to network connectivity.

Related work

The development of intelligent systems using IoT networks and edge technologies has gained rapid popularity for maintaining automated communication24,25. Healthcare, smart vehicles, and surveillance applications are examples of smart communication networks that reduce human effort in uncertain environments while collecting data, which is later processed and analyzed at the network boundaries for timely decision-making and to fulfill the demands of connected devices26,27. Decentralized approaches are used in many real-time applications, leveraging network edges to reduce data latency and manage data closer to the source devices. It efficiently allocates constrained resources while maintaining scalability, particularly in smart cities that rely on cloud platforms28,29. Such techniques are frequently helpful in time-sensitive systems to maintain data transmission and processing while leveraging effective load balancing and resilience through edge computing30,31. The authors of32 proposed a routing solution that addresses congestion and security concerns in IoT applications. It provides an efficient approach to handling network load while transmitting data packets from multiple devices over IoT connections. It selects the cluster heads based on multiple factors, including congestion level and signal-to-interference ratio (SINR). Data packets are securely encrypted using adaptive quantum logic-based packet coding during data transmission. Moreover, using the Adaptive Krill Herd (AKH) optimization technique, the least congested corridor is found for optimizing the data routing. Authors in33 resolve the research issues in constraint application in terms of energy consumption and adopt an optimal method for the selection of cluster heads by proposing a Grey Wolf Optimisation-based Cluster Algorithm (GWOCA). Its aim to generate the set of clusters for devices and attain efficient load balancing along stable inter-cluster communications. The experimental results are carried out using simulations and statistic analysis demonstrated the efficacy of the proposed algorithm than existing approaches. The authors of34 introduce a scalable, robust reinforcement learning approach that integrates an efficient task-scheduling mechanism driven by epsilon-greedy Q-learning. The novelty of the proposed method lies in its exceptional performance: the agent takes action only when a noticeable decline in network performance is detected, specifically in packet delivery ratio, throughput, and delay. Experimental results, based on both simulations and real-world datasets, demonstrate that the proposed approach outperforms existing security solutions.

Authors35 integrate Equilibrium Optimization and Artificial Hummingbird Optimization techniques to introduce a clustering scheme, EQ-AHA, aiming to enhance the performance of the resource devices in terms of energy efficiency for IoT-based Software-Defined Wireless Sensor Networks (IoT-SDWSN). It offers the most reliable and optimal routing paths from cluster heads to the base station. By optimizing a fitness function that comprises distances between CHs, distances between nodes and CHs, and nodes’ energy levels, the optimal nodes are selected as cluster heads. The performance results have shown the significance of the proposed EQ-AHA for diverse network-wide communication. The authors of36 proposed a DAG-Blockchain architecture for MANET-IoT security, leveraging Multi-Factor PUF identification. The network is clustered into allowed nodes, and a Secure, Trust-Based Dijkstra’s Method ensures secure data transfer. A Bidirectional GRU performs deep packet analysis for intrusion detection, and statistical results improve the performance of the proposed approach across various network metrics. To enhance link bandwidth while minimizing latency, a novel heuristic multi-objective trust routing approach, the Levy Chaos Adaptive Snake Optimisation-based Multi-Trust Routing Method (LCASO-MTRM), is proposed37. Within LCASO framework, it integrates chaotic and adaptive operators and improves population diversity, convergence, and expands the solution space with a fault-tolerant communication system. Furthermore, a multi-objective QoS routing model integrated with a link-trust mechanism is proposed to achieve reliable communication. Based on related discussions, it was noted that many approaches struggle to address research challenges, particularly in distributed communication and in proposing efficient methods to enhance resource usage while ensuring security. However, most of them are unable to cope with the demands of relaying services using lightweight AI computing, thereby degrading the performance of the smart system as devices scale under varying network conditions. In addition, they overlooked the benefits of real-time decision-making, thereby increasing communication overhead on IoT devices. In addition, the proposed model offers a novel approach by integrating blockchain and AI to enhance network performance through resource optimisation and more effective, trusted data transmission. It prevents network congestion as the number of network devices increases and leads to developing a sustainable system to ensure more dynamic and reliable communication for intelligent systems. Methodology

In this section, we explain our proposed model, along with the developed algorithms, for optimizing decision-making strategies at the edges and in the system’s trustworthiness interactions. The proposed methodology comprises two main phases. One is AI Computing for optimisation of smart IoT networks, and the other is trust management using a Blockchain-Driven approach. Both phases contribute to the IoT network’s sustainability by enabling real-time, time-constrained applications through secure, lightweight data processing.

AI computing for smart networks optimization

In this phase, AI Computing is employed for smart communication and real-time data analysis, with data analysis conditioned on network conditions. This phase enables intelligent decision-making to optimise resource utilization and traffic flow in a dynamic IoT network. The devices are deployed randomly with limited constraints in terms of processing, storage, and analyzing, except for the sink and network edges. Initially, using their transmission power, nodes create local files that store initial parameter information and facilitate the formation of routing paths. All tables are continuously updated to route IoT data on optimized forwarders. NS-3 network simulator and TensorFlow were utilized for network simulation and AI-based prediction for learning and network optimization. The simulated framework generates synthetic data regarding device activities and records it in the log files for consideration as an input feature to train the SVM model, and IoT devices are marked as malicious or trustworthy by taking into account predefined thresholds. The devices are randomly distributed and have homogeneous characteristics. Initially, the nodes’ energy levels are fixed at 5000mJ. The evaluation of the proposed model with related schemes is conducted for network throughput, energy consumption, packet drop rate, and connection time. In experiments, an adversarial model is considered for the involvement of faulty/malicious devices in the proposed SPAR routing protocol. The inclusion of malicious devices increases the data drop rate by failing to forward data packets, thereby causing data disruption and degrading the system’s trustworthiness. Moreover, such devices flood the false packets and affect the reliability of the network by depletion of unnecessary energy resources of legitimate nodes. Conclusion

The smart IoT systems integrated with emerging technologies enables timely and real-time communication services to end users. It leads to the development of sustainable environment and facilitates the various domains in terms of connectivity, information collection and monitoring system operations. Wireless equipment and edge computing interact with physical objects to collect environmental data and process it at the network boundary, enabling timely responses and effective decision-making policies that conserve network resources. These systems not only reduce the likelihood of communication failure in uncertain conditions but also provide dynamic functionality for time-sensitive and critical applications. Despite advances in intelligent systems, most approaches still struggle to achieve secure, energy-efficient, and trustworthy communication across heterogeneous infrastructure. Our research proposes an intelligent resource management approach for a scalable, high-performance, and secure IoT network. The prediction process is achieved using SVM, which intelligently improves system responsiveness by optimizing the transmission of network data to analyzing servers. Moreover, the integrity of intelligent systems is maintained through trust computation, enhancing security against threats of malicious behaviour. In future work, we intend to test the proposed model in a dynamic, distributed threat environment, along with a real test-bed evaluation to verify its scalability and robust fault tolerance. Furthermore, historical data can be explored by deep learning models further to enhance network optimization and trust management under adaptive conditions.
