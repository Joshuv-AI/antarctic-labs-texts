Correction: Intraspecific variation in sex-biased dispersal of a threatened ecotone butterfly between forest patches in a mosaic landscape
Authors: Marcin Sielezniew, Izabela Sielezniew, Krzysztof Deoniziak, Cezary Bystrowski, Piotr Nowicki
Published: 2025

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-11817-x, published online 18 July 2025

The original version of this Article contained an error in Figure 6, where an incorrect version of the graph in panel C was used during the figure assembly.

The original Figure 6 and accompanying legend appears below.

Variation in dispersal parameters: emigration propensity (A), average dispersal distance (B), dispersal mortality scaling with connectivity (C), emigration scaling with patch size (D) and immigration scaling with patch size (E) of P. mnemosyne among three different classes of wing size in males and females. The estimated values are presented with their 95% confidence intervals; statistically significant differences (at P < 0.05) are marked with different letters.

The original Article has been corrected.
