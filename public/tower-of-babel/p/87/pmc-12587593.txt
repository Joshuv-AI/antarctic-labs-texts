Retraction Note: Up-regulation of CRKL by microRNA-335 methylation is associated with poor prognosis in gastric cancer
Authors: Jia-kui Zhang, Yong-shuang Li, Chun-dong Zhang, Dong-qiu Dai
Published: 2025

Retraction Note: Cancer Cell International (2017) 17:28

10.1186/s12935-017-0387-9

The Editors-in-Chief have retracted this article. Following the publication of this article, concerns were raised regarding similarities in Fig. 5. Additionally, Fig. 7a (SGC-7901-NC, 20 h) appears to be similar to Fig. 7a (Ctrl, 24 h) of [1]. The authors were unable to provide raw images for the concerned figures and stated that they were unable to obtain the same results as described in the study.

The authors and Editors-in-Chief therefore no longer have confidence in the presented data.

Jia-Kui Zhang has stated on behalf of all authors that all authors agree with this retraction.
