P088 Screening for Paediatric Sleep Disordered Breathing in Dental Settings: A scoping review
Authors: R Baskar, M Silva, M Vandeleur
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractIntroductionSleep-disordered breathing (SDB) can have a significant impact on a child’s growth and neurocognitive development. The gold standard for the diagnosis of SDB is an overnight polysomnography (PSG). However, challenges such as the limited availability, accessibility and cost of diagnostic services along with limited parent/caregiver awareness of symptoms can contribute to diagnostic delays. Screening questionnaires offer a simple and cost-effective method to identify at-risk paediatric patients and can guide referrals to sleep specialists. Dental appointments represent an opportunity for screening in the community. This scoping review aims to explore the existing literature on paediatric SDB screening in dental settings and identify knowledge gaps.MethodsUsing a scoping review framework, a comprehensive search was undertaken using Medline, Embase, and PubMed. The search strategy included broad search terms relating to questionnaires, sleep-disordered breathing, dentists and children.Progress to DateThe scoping review protocol was registered on Open Science forum on 15th April 2024. Of the total eligible 482 peer-reviewed publications included in the original search, 40 studies have been included in the final analysis. The author is currently in the process of data extraction.Intended Outcome and ImpactThe intended outcomes are to identify current screening tools for SDB in dental settings, describe their components, advantages, disadvantages, and pinpoint gaps in the literature.
