Correction: Transcription factor CEBPA suppresses sepsis induced lung injury by activating SIRT4 transcription
Authors: Zheqian Wu, Kunxiang Gou, Yong Wang, Lihua Dai
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-25975-5, published online 26 November 2025

The original version of the Article contained errors in Figures 2 and 4.

During the figure assembly of Figure 2, temporary placeholder images were inserted into panel 2I for formatting purposes and were inadvertently left unchanged in the final version. The authors have now provided the correct representative images for Figure 2I. This was strictly a figure-assembly error; the quantitative data associated with this figure were analysed using the correct raw data.

The original Figure 2 and its accompanying legend appears below.
