Retraction Note: Laparoscopic ventral rectopexy plus sacral colpopexy: continuous locked suture for mesh fixation. A randomized clinical trial
Authors: Andrea Morciano, Dario Caliandro, Giuseppe Campagna, Giovanni Panico, Alessia Giaquinto, Giorgio Fachechi, Marzio Angelo Zullo, Andrea Tinelli, Alfredo Ercoli, Giovanni Scambia, Mauro Cervigni, Giuseppe Marzo
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: Archives of Gynecology and Obstetrics (2022) 306:1573–1579 10.1007/s00404-022-06682-2

The Editor has retracted this article because the authors have been unable to provide provide evidence that ethics approval was received before the commencement of this study. Andrea Morciano has stated on behalf of all authors that they disagree to this retraction.
