Author Correction: Genome-wide fine-mapping improves identification of causal variants
Authors: Yang Wu, Zhili Zheng, Loic Thibaut, Tian Lin, Qian Feng, Hao Cheng, Loic Yengo, Michael E Goddard, Naomi R Wray, Peter M Visscher, Jian Zeng
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Genetics 10.1038/s41588-026-02549-3, published online 30 March 2026.

In the version of this article initially published, there was a typographical error in the third paragraph of the “Incorporating functional annotations improves fine-mapping” section, where the second SNP mentioned, now shown as rs34333163, was an inadvertent duplicate of the first SNP discussed in the paragraph. The text is now amended in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
