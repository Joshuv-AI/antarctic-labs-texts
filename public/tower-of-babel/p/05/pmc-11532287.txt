Protocol to measure cell avidity between cord blood-derived NK cells and leukemia cell line KG-1a
Authors: Yan Wang, Mingming Wu, Xian Song, Xinru Liu, Changcheng Zheng, Fang Ni
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
SummaryThe avidity between immune cells and target cells is crucial for effective immune responses. Here, we present a protocol to measure single-cell avidity between cord blood-derived natural killer (NK) cells and the human acute myelocytic leukemia cell line KG-1a. We describe the steps for isolating cord blood mononuclear cells, purifying NK cells, and culturing KG-1a cells. We then detail the procedures for detecting cell avidity between NK cells and KG-1a cells using the acoustic force-based z-Movi cell avidity analyzer.For complete details on the use and execution of this protocol, please refer to Wang et al.1

Before you begin

The efficient elimination of diseased cells, such as cancer cells, by cytotoxic NK cells depends on a specialized cellular interaction known as the immunological synapse (IS).2,3 The formation and function of the IS encompass several sequential processes, including recognition and adhesion between NK cells and target cells, lytic granule (e.g., granzyme B and perforin) and F-actin polarization at the IS, and the release of cytotoxic granules by NK cells, ultimately causing the death of the target cells.4

The z-Movi cell avidity analyzer accurately measures the binding interactions between immune cells and target cells, aiding in the selection of effector cells with high cytotoxic functionality.5,6

In this protocol, we describe the detailed steps for detecting the binding efficacy between NK cells and target cancer cells using the z-Movi cell avidity analyzer, along with the corresponding data analysis. Although our protocol focuses on analyzing cell avidity between NK cells and suspended leukemic cells, it can theoretically be applied to other types of cell-cell interactions, such as those involving T cells or solid tumor cells.

Institutional permissions

This protocol involves the use of human tissue. For research focused on studying the cell avidity of NK cells derived from cord blood, which are anonymized and categorized as medical waste, institutional review board approval is generally not necessary. However, requirements may vary between institutions, and researchers should seek approval according to their respective guidelines. In the present study, the cord blood samples were collected from the Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology of the First Affiliated Hospital of University of Science and Technology of China (USTC). The Review Committee of the Ethics Institution of the First Affiliated Hospital of USTC approved the collection and use of human tissues for this study (license numbers 2022-KY-022).

NK cell isolation

Timing: 2 h

Timing: 2 h

Here, we describe a procedure to isolate NK cells from CBMCs employing Biotinylated antibodies, magnetic microbeads, and a customizable selection column. The isolation process utilizes negative selection, where cells of interest are left unbound to the antibody-conjugated magnetic nanobeads. This method effectively preserves the resting state of NK cells during isolation, ensuring the magnetically labeled cells are retained within the column without elution. Limitations

The protocol outlined here offers a means to assess the avidity of immune cells with suspended target cells using the z-Movi analyzer. The detection of avidity primarily hinges on cellular interactions, including cell-to-cell adhesion, shared receptors/ ligands, and other possible interaction mechanisms. While it effectively detects the overall interactions, it does not specify the exact type of interaction. Additionally, the use of CellTrace Far Red dye for labeling NK cells may limit the application to genetically edited immune NK cells.
