Expression of concern: “Systematic review of Erasmus+ projects labelled as good practice and related to e-learning and ICT: Some case studies” [Heliyon 9 (2023) e22331]
Published: 2025

An investigation conducted on behalf of the journal by Elsevier's Research Integrity & Publishing Ethics team found a significant portion of the total number of citations were to papers published by the author Francisco Jos Garca-Pealvo. In summary, 12 papers by the author were cited in the article that were found to be redundant to the text including three that were cited in the acknowledgements section that serve no scientific purpose. The Editors have determined that the references in the paper do not provide readers with a balanced reflection of the literature. Readers should take this into consideration when evaluating this paper.
