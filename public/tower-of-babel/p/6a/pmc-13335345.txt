Letter to the Editor for submission to journal of translational medicine contextual programming of CAR‑enhanced TIL
Authors: Joana Rapaz Lérias, Jéssica Kamiki, Rodrigo Eduardo, Vitaly Balan, Markus Joseph Maeurer
Published: 2026

Dear Editor,

Engineered T-cells can eradicate hematologic malignancies, yet CAR efficacy against solid tumors remains limited by antigen heterogeneity, immunosuppressive microenvironments or T-cell dysfunction. Tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TIL) already possess features enabling tumor navigation, are enriched for anti-tumor reactivity, and exhibit epigenetic imprint for persistence in hostile niches [1–3]. We asked whether grafting a HER-2-specific CAR into TIL could enhance effector function against autologous tumor tissue without sacrificing endogenous TCR breadth (Fig. 1a). While CAR T-cells deliver rapid cytotoxicity, they can be vulnerable to high antigen loads [3]. Materials and methods

TIL expansion and analysis. TIL expansion (CL, Cardiolipin-enhanced TIL), was performed as previously described [4] up to 10e9 cells using T-Vivo (Lonza, Basel, Switzerland) supplemented with IL-7 (1000 IU/mL), IL-15 (150 IU/mL), IL-21 (1 IU/mL; all Bio-Techne, Minneapolis, MN, USA), and 275 nM CL (Echelon Biosciences, Salt Lake City, UT, USA), followed by IL-2 (1000 IU/mL), IL-7 (1000 IU/mL), and IL-15 (150 IU/ml).

Lentiviral constructs and Transduction. The benchmark vector encoded a conventional HER-2-CAR utilizing trastuzumab-derived 4D5 scFv, CD28/CD3ζ signalling domains. TIL were transduced using 10 µg/mL Polybrene (Tocris, Bristol, UK).

CAR-Mediated Functional Assays and Cytokine Profiling. 1 × 10e5 TIL were incubated with HER2- or BSA-coated beads (AcroBiosystems, Newark, DE, USA) at a 1:1 ratio in T-Vivo media (Lonza) supplemented with IL-7 and IL-15 (100 IU/mL each; Bio-Techne). After 24 hours, supernatants were analyzed for cytokine production using LEGENDplex™ Human CD8/NK Panel (BioLegend, San Diego, CA, USA).

Intracellular cytokine staining (ICS). 1 × 10e6 TIL were were either incubated with medium alone, positive wells received 25 ng/mL PMA (Sigma-Aldrich, St. Louis, MO, USA) and 1 µg/mL ionomycin (Invitrogen, Thermo Fisher Scientific, Waltham, MA, USA), monensin (BD GolgiStop™, BD Biosciences, Franklin Lakes, NJ, USA) and 20 µg/mL Brefeldin A (BioLegend) for 5 hours. Cells were fixed using the PerFix-nc kit (Beckman Coulter, Brea, CA, USA).

Autologous Tumor Recognition. Autologous tumor fragments from patients with metastatic melanoma, pancreatic adenocarcinoma (PDAC) or prostate cancer were co-cultured with TIL in T-Vivo media (Lonza) supplemented with IL-7 and IL-15 (100 IU/mL each; Bio-Techne,), as described [4]. CAR activation was done with HER-2- or BSA-coated beads (AcroBiosystems).

Results and discussion

We hypothesized that TIL can be used as a foundation for HER-2-CAR to create a dual-functional therapeutic: the CAR provides immediate potency [5], while preserved TCR diversity maintains breadth. We first asked whether grafting a ‘CAR onto TIL’ could enhance effector function against autologous tumor tissue without sacrificing endogenous TCR breadth (Fig. 1a).To test the functional integration of this “chassis,” we evaluated whether HER-2-CAR signalling could elicit a robust effector output in cases where TIL recognition of autologous tumor is absent. Following HER-2-CAR transduction (Fig. 1b), specific activation requirements were assessed.Non-infected (N.I.) TIL required OKT3-mediated cross-linking to produce IFN-γ, whereas HER-2-CAR TIL responded specifically to antigen engagement (Fig. 1c). This confirmed that cytokine release was CAR-dependent (Fig. 1d) . Beyond IFN-γ, HER-2 engagement triggered IL-2 and TNF-α production and up-regulated granzyme B and granulysin (Fig. 1d). In the case of tumor recognition in MHC class I-restricted manner, the addition of HER-2 beads further amplified IFN-γ output (Fig. 1e). These findings suggest that this modular design provides a de novo response when endogenous reactivity is absent and amplifies an existing TCR- mediated response. Simultaneous CAR-target engagement and TCR recognition is not detrimental and leads to increased cytokine production. CL-enhanced TIL demonstrated a robust cytotoxic phenotype, even in the absence of additional stimulation, with high frequencies of cells producing granulysin, granzyme A, and perforin. This triple-positive profile indicates that the TIL chassis remains functionally “armed” following expansion and transduction for tumor cell lysis upon CAR or TCR engagement (Fig. 1f).The CAR trigger can be adapted to target any specific antigen of choice while preserving the diverse, patient-specific TIL repertoire for tumor detection. Despite limitations such as small sample size (n = 4) and the lack of in vivo validation, these results support a “division of labor” model: the TCR provides broad, MHC-restricted recognition, while the CAR provides a programmable trigger for localized activation. This dual-signalling approach may mitigate the “antigen-load fragility” observed in classical CAR-T cells while maintaining the trafficking and tumor-homing capabilities inherent to TIL. This concept would take advantage of the CAR-target being present in cancer lesions, considering MHC-epitope heterogeneity or MHC loss variants to provide a first inflammatory signal to attract other immune cells facilitating epitope spreading and to increase a pro-inflammatory anti-cancer directed immune response. TIL can be programmed in a tissue-context dependent fashion [3] with the convenience of an inbuild (CAR) marker to qualify transgenic TIL for release in a clinical trial setting.
