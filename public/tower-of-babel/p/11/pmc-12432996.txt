In the original publication of this article [1], the authors identified typographical errors in the Graphical Abstract and the Method sections. The incorrect and correct version of Graphical Abstract and textual error in Method section are displayed below.

In the third line of graphical abstract summary, “More spent in TIR of 100-180 mg/dL and TITRs in the CGM group” should be changed to “More spent in TIR of 70-180 mg/dL and TITRs in the CGM group”.

Incorrect graphical abstract

Correct graphical abstract

In Method section, the sentence “Patients with high surgical severity, defined as Society of Thoracic Surgeons (STS) score ≥ 8 or European System for Cardiac Operative Risk Evaluation (EuroSCORE) ≥ 15”, were excluded” should be changed to “Patients with high surgical severity, defined as Society of Thoracic Surgeons (STS) score ≥ 8 or European System for Cardiac Operative Risk Evaluation (EuroSCORE) ≥ 5, were excluded”.
