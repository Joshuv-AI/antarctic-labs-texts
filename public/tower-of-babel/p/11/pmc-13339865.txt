Retraction Note: Enhancing employee job satisfaction through organizational climate and employee happiness at work: a mediated–moderated model
Authors: Yu Jianchun
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: BMC Psychology 12, 744 (2024)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-02269-5

The Editors have retracted this article after concerns were raised about its validity. A post-publication review revealed major methodological and analytical flaws, including inadequate statistical model validation, missing model fit indices, inconsistencies between described and reported analyses, and unclear longitudinal procedures. The author was given the opportunity to respond to these concerns and provided clarification. However, the response did not satisfactorily resolve the issues identified during the review. Consequently, the Editors have lost confidence in the results and conclusions presented in this article.

Author Yu Jianchun has not responded to correspondence regarding this retraction.
