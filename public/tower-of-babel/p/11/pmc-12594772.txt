Correction: Significance of changes in cavity after treatment in Mycobacterium avium complex pulmonary disease
Authors: Joong‑Yub Kim, Juye Bae, Kwonhyung Hyung, Inhan Lee, Hyun‑Jun Park, So Yeon Kim, Kyung‑Eui Lee, Yoon Hae Ahn, Si Mong Yoon, Nakwon Kwak, Jae‑Joon Yim
Published: 2025

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-71971-6, published online 10 September 2024

The original version of this Article contained an error in the IRB approval number in the Ethics statement, in the Methods section,

“The study was approved and the requirement for informed consent was waived by the Institutional Review Board of Seoul National University Hospital (approval number: H-2311-030-14836) due to the retrospective nature of the study design. The study was performed in accordance with the principles of the Declaration of Helsinki.”

now reads:

“The study was approved and the requirement for informed consent was waived by the Institutional Review Board of Seoul National University Hospital (approval number: H-2311-030-1483) due to the retrospective nature of the study design. The study was performed in accordance with the principles of the Declaration of Helsinki.”

The original Article has been corrected.
