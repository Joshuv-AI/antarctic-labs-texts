Investigating the health and safety condition of the care environment of primary schools based on crisis prevention approach
Authors: Sadegh Ahmadi-Mazhin, Javad Vatani, Mohammad Rahimkhani, Foruzan Hajiabadi, Mehdi Nouri, Parvin Sepehr, Hosna Karami, Ali Khatib, Fatameh Avijgan, Maliheh Eshaghzadeh, Reza Ghasemi, Alireza Khammar
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
BACKGROUND:Safety and health issues need to be prioritized by schools. A safe environment in schools is one of the primary goals of the education system. Evaluation of educational environments with an approach to crisis prevention and coping is of particular importance. The purpose of this study is to evaluate the state of the care environment of government primary schools with a health and safety approach in Zabol, Iran.MATERIALS AND METHODS:The present study was descriptive cross-sectional. This study was conducted in government primary schools in Zabol, Iran (2023). The sampling method was available and there were a total of 35 schools that were selected. The tool used to be the school-age care environment rating scale (SACERS) checklist. These checklists were completed by the researchers after observing the current situation in the schools. The SPSS v25 software was used for data statistical analysis (Kolmogorov–Smirnov and t-tests).RESULTS:The highest score relates to health policies, and the safety measures obtained the lowest score. Health measures, safety measures, attendance, departure, and meals studied are far from the required standards. There was a significant difference between the average safety and health of schools and the required standards (t = -8.19, P < 0.001). Therefore, the safety and health of schools did not meet the necessary standards.CONCLUSION:The evaluation of schools in eight components of safety and health shows that schools need more attention to be ready to deal with emergencies and crises. The lack of comprehensive, valid tools and inadequate monitoring can be effective factors in the nonstandard condition of schools. Schools need intervention to improve and eliminate deficiencies in terms of safety and health.

Introduction

The school is where children and adults are engaged in activities, education, and training. Children are the main capital of the country, and their upbringing is one of the main goals of social and economic development programs. In order to achieve development goals, ensuring the highest level of physical, mental, social, and spiritual health for children should be considered a necessity and priority. After the family, the school plays the most important role in a child’s health.[1] Experience shows that these places play a major role in the occurrence of accidents and diseases in developing countries. In the studies of Safdari et al.,[2] it was stated that many accidents leading to bone fractures in schools can be prevented by changing the environmental conditions. The school should provide safety for staff and students. Unforeseen events should be predicted so that, in the event of an accident, their consequences will be minimal.[3] Promoting the well-being and health of students and teachers should be the goal of health programs in schools.[45] Paying attention to health issues along with safety issues can help prevent diseases in children; for example, a comfortable and suitable chair is one of these things.[6] In an educational environment, it is necessary to have instructions related to safety and emergency situations in place. The presence of students can greatly complicate crisis management. Safety should be well managed, feeding time should be considered learning time, and personal hygiene should be part of the curriculum.[7] Having a comprehensive crisis management plan prepares principals to deal with the crises facing their schools. The comprehensive crisis management program, while preventing the impact of crises, causes the least damage to schools from crises.[8] Evaluating the current situation of schools is the first step in preventing crises in schools, and on the other hand, evaluating and measuring the efficiency of the systems. School environment measurement scales are actually a tool for evaluating the quality of the systems and organization of students’ education and care.[9] The school environment includes physical space, an educational environment, services, and physical–psychological support for students.[10] In the study of Maitra et al.,[11] it was found that children’s injuries in schools are one of the main concerns, and in order to prevent accidents, special attention should be paid to specific points and areas in schools. Also, in the study conducted by Kumar and his colleagues, it was found that there is a direct relationship between the characteristics of the physical space of schools and the behavioral problems of students.[12] According to the statistics published in the United States, about 43% of children’s accidents are related to schools, of which 20% relate to school buildings.[11] Geravandi et al.[13] The study showed that the environmental health and safety of the schools play an effective role in improving the efficiency and level of education. The study of Salehpour and his colleagues was conducted regarding the health and safety situation of the physical environment of Shahrekord government primary schools. A total of 37 schools were investigated. The results showed that the health status of school buildings in 100% of girls’ and boys’ schools is good. Even though the health conditions of school buildings are suitable, there are still accidents in these schools.[14] Childhood experiences have long-term effects on a person’s future. The primary years of school can have valuable experiences of growth and self-sufficiency along with educational progress, so paying attention to safety and health issues in primary schools is important.[15] The health and safety status of the school environment plays an important role in preventing accidents and protecting the health of students.[13] The purpose of this study is to investigate the various dimensions of health and safety and to evaluate government primary schools in Zabol, Iran. Since Zabol is one of the border cities and one of the most deprived areas in Iran, attention to crisis management in schools and the role of assessing the current situation in crisis prevention and control can be one of the most important cases. The innovation of this study is the study of safety and health in schools, because previous studies regarding the study of health situations in schools have mostly been from the perspective of environmental health. The health and safety study was limited. In border cities in Iran such as Zabol, which are far from the provincial capital and also from the capital (Tehran), the importance of health and safety is low. The ability of people to take care of themselves in elementary schools, especially in public schools, can improve their level of health, and safety in this area will help.

Materials and Methods

Study design and setting

Various articles and studies were searched in databases, journals, library resources, and scientific sites. Elementary schools in Zabol, Iran, were selected as study sites. The health and safety programs and measures of employees and students were examined. Then the data were collected and statistically analyzed. The steps are detailed below.

Study participants and sampling

The statistical population under study was all government primary schools in Zabol, Iran, in 2023, and an available sampling method was used. The statistical sample of 35 schools was selected from non-privileged, semi-privileged, and privileged areas of Zabol. This study’s data sampling was done by visiting schools, observing the current situation, and completing the scale checklist.

Data collection tool and technique

This research includes all health and safety programs and measures to maintain the health of employees and students. The SACERS checklist, which includes the health and safety index in eight components (health policies, health measures, safety and emergency, safety measures, attendance, departure, meals/snacks, and personal hygiene) was investigated.[16] The SACERS was published by Mrs. Thelma Harms (2014) and is designed in the form of 47 questions with a Likert scoring scale of seven points.[16] The health and safety index is investigated in SACERS. The content validity of the tool and the reliability of the tool in components of the health and safety index based on the study of Imanian et al. are 0.7.[17] Data analysis was done with SPSS v23. Frequency distribution, percentage, mean, and standard deviation were calculated, and descriptive analysis was presented. The Kolmogorov–Smirnov test was used to check the normal distribution of the data. To analyze the normal distribution of data on the safety and health index, the parametric t-test of one sample was used. With this tool, the health and safety index has eight components, as follows:

Health policies: Include policies and instructions related to measures when a student is sick. Also, the rules to prevent the spread of disease, the health requirements of employees, and the health records of students.[16]

Health measures include measures, supervision, and education of students about health issues, including how to contact, communicate with, and interact with parents when sick. These measures are the implementation of the guidelines in real life.[16]

Safety policies and emergency situations: Policies and instructions related to the necessary measures in emergency situations and related measures are the road map for maintaining safety.[16]

Safety measures include measures related to the elimination and control of hazards, easy access to first aid equipment, informing parents about events, and educating students about safety.[16]

Attendance: Timely follow-up when the child is late or absent, registering the student’s attendance, and sharing it with parents to maximize the student’s attendance.[16]

Departure: Clarity of the process for students leaving the school, controlling the safe departure of the student from the school, helping the student to be ready to leave, and educating the student about safe behavior when leaving the school until they reach home.[16]

Meals and snacks: The appropriateness of the timing for meals, the acceptable food value based on the needs of students, storage facilities, and hygienic consumption of food.[16]

Personal hygiene: Paying attention to the personal hygiene of students and employees, the existence of appropriate and accessible health facilities, teaching students about personal hygiene, hand hygiene, brushing and cleaning, and regular inspection of sanitary facilities.[16]

After determining the statistical population, the researchers used the SACERS checklist for data collection. The checklist was completed by observing the current situation of the schools and talking to the school principals. Completing the questionnaire in each school took one day (8 am to 12 pm in the morning). After data collection, they were entered into SPSS v25 software. The statistical analysis was done through heuristic analysis and cumulative percentage analysis.

Ethical consideration

All steps of the above study were reviewed and approved by the code of ethics (IR.ZBMU.REC.1402.041 from Zabol University of Medical Sciences).

Ethical considerations of the present study included ensuring the confidentiality of school data, not disrupting the school and teachers’ activities, observing school working hours, and having professional ethics in filling out the questionnaire.

Discussion

The status of schools in the components of health policies, safety policies, emergency situations, and personal hygiene meets the necessary standards. However, health measures, safety measures, attendance, departure, and meals and snacks are far from the basic standard and their quality level is lower. The schools in the study are in good condition in the field of health and safety instructions and measures in emergency situations, but they are not in good condition in the practical field related to health measures. The cause of this defect can be seen as a result of the lack of strict supervision of the implementation of instructions in schools by the officials, as well as the lack of planning for the possibility of parents visiting as unexpected guests, which can improve supervision.

Imanian et al. (2019)[17] studied non-governmental primary schools in Tehran, selected 65 schools, and used the School-Age Care Environment Rating Scale (SACERS). The results showed that schools meet the necessary standards in the components of health policies, safety policies, emergency conditions, and personal hygiene. However, they lacked these standards in components such as health measures, safety measures, attendance, departure, and meals and snacks. A lack of parental demand to promote emergency preparedness measures and provide school education could be a determining factor in schools. Our results, study methods, and tools are consistent with Imanian’s study. The number of schools in the Imanian study is almost double ours, which can be attributed to the differences in the study’s place.

The study of Ganji et al. (2013)[18] showed that the environmental health status of government primary schools for girls in the Khomeyni Shahr of Isfahan is not favorable. These results are consistent with the present study. The reason can be attributed to the fact that Zabol is one of the small cities in Iran that is geographically located in a border and deprived area, and due to the lack of health experts in these areas, health measures are below standard. The results showed that the schools did not have a favorable situation in the safety measures and emergency conditions, which is consistent with the research of Shaaban Khani et al. (2004).[19] In that the study of the safety status of primary schools in Mazandaran province and comparison with the standard indexes showed that the school safety in some cases (location and condition of gas piping, heaters and heating, and fire extinguishing facilities) is nonstandard. According to the results of the present study, Nekoei et al.[20] (2007) found that windows protective fences of the upper floors, cooling devices, heating devices, stoves, and fire extinguishing had a relatively favorable environmental health and safety condition. According to the results of this study Zabol has a lower level of health and safety measures than Kerman. Improving safety levels should be included in the plans. Hobobati et al. (2021)[21] investigated the health and safety situation of primary schools in Yazd city and collected data by completing a questionnaire and using the observational method. In terms of safety, 73.8% of schools do not have fire extinguishers, and the management of students’ attendance and departure is unsuitable. The reason could be that administrators and parents have different views regarding school safety and health. These cases are related to the crisis in the school and family, and their attention and management will reduce the possible crisis. In a study by Pirzadeh et al. (2011)[22] regarding the comparison of the environmental health status of government primary schools in different areas of Isfahan city, 77 schools were selected and investigated by the regular random method. The results showed that 76.6% of the schools were in compliance with the safety and health standard, and 49% of the schools had sanitary buffets in compliance with the standard; however, it was also shown that the meals were not suitable, which could be due to the weak economy, culture, food culture, and lifestyle of the people. This study showed that schools did not have favorable conditions for providing healthy snacks and meals, and this is consistent with the results of the study by Haghighat et al.[15] Most schools have certain health standards. The results showed that the status of healthy meals is unfavorable. Also in the study by Khodayari-Zarnaq et al. (2017),[23] evaluated the activities and health programs of schools regarding diet and healthy nutrition in Tabriz city, it was found that the majority of schools provided healthy foods and snacks in appropriate spaces. It is important to investigate the knowledge of students about the safety and health of the environment at school and in parks.[2425] The individual health component had a favorable condition, which can be attributed to the support of the school and family in teaching and practicing skills. The study by Geravandi et al. (2021)[13] showed that only 4.44% of urban schools had sanitary conditions. In this study, 40 urban and rural schools were assessed using a checklist based on environmental health regulations. In total, the results show that the safety and health index in schools is not at an optimal level.

In another study conducted by Zazuoli et al. (2019)[26] regarding the state of environmental health and school safety, 45 primary schools were examined. The results showed that non-compliance with environmental health indicators is due to a lack of awareness or attention of managers or responsible people toward the standards. In the current study, the results showed that schools are weak in terms of safety, which can be attributed to the attitudes of managers and stakeholders toward safety. The condition of school buildings and their equipment should be checked in terms of building and structure safety, resistance, etc., In this field, expert opinion and checklists can be used[27]; it is also necessary to measure the professional competence of managers in connection with crisis management. Education, awareness, and attitude among students regarding health in accidents and disasters complete the chain of maintaining health and safety.[3] A systematic review by Poursadeqiyan et al. (2020)[28] regarding the health, safety, and environmental conditions of Iranian schools showed that the health and safety situation in most of the urban schools in Iran is good and relatively favorable. However, there have also been some problems; hence, the implementation of integrated HSE management and personal health and safety education is imperative to improve the status of schools. Students’ health and safety in schools are considered very critical. The first step in developing such programs is to carefully and comprehensively assess schools.

Among the limitations of this study are insufficient funds to study the knowledge level of parents about school safety and health and the investigation of all the schools in the city of Zabol. In future studies, it is appropriate to examine the effects of education, executive measures, establishing a partnership system between parents and school officials, and the use of safety and health experts in schools in order to improve the level of school safety and health. It is also suggested to check the health and safety situation in all cities in Iran. In future studies, it is appropriate to study the impact of education and executive actions in order to improve school safety and health.[2930]

Limitation and recommendation

The lack of knowledge among administrators, school staff, and parents of students regarding the importance of evaluating the health and safety of schools is one of the limitations of this study.

For future studies, it is suggested to investigate the effect of training and executive measures in order to improve the level of school safety and health in the form of a before-and-after study.

Conclusion

The safety and health of students directly relate to the crisis in schools, the first step in developing such programs is a comprehensive and accurate evaluation of schools. The government primary schools in the city of Zabol are far from the optimal level in the field of health and safety in various dimensions. School managers should take into account the various dimensions of health and safety when identifying and controlling the weak points and crisis generators of the school. The evaluation of schools in eight areas of safety and health shows that schools need more attention in order to be ready to deal with emergency situations and crises. According to the crisis management approach, it is suggested that all school principals and educational district principals use such tools to periodically assess the state of schools and pay attention to weak points so that an acceptable care environment can be provided for all students in schools. Finally, it is possible to prevent any possible harm to the student when facing a crisis in school. The lack of comprehensive tools for evaluation and inadequate monitoring of schools can be possible factors in the nonstandard care environment of schools. The school’s safety and health need to improve and eliminate the shortcomings of evaluations. The participation of health policymakers and health service providers in schools should be aimed at creating commitment to the program and effective intervention in the policymaking process. The low priority of health programs for a large part of managers, the lack of budget, and the lack of health and safety experts in crisis management are major obstacles to implementing policies aimed at improving health promotion in schools.
