Published: 2024

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-024-45355-3, published online 22 February 2024

The original version of this Article omitted from the author list the 2nd and 3rd authors, Carrie D. Llewellyn and Richard O. de Visser, respectively. Carrie D. Llewellyn and Richard O. de Visser are from the Department of Primary Care and Public Health, Brighton and Sussex Medical School, UK. Consequently, the following was added to the Author Contributions: ‘CDL, ROdV: supervision, writing (original draft, review & editing)’; and ‘LFR, RG, GSC, DM, MM, LOR, SCR, MC, CK, CDL, CY, AWC, PK, AB: writing (review & editing)’. In addition, the following was added to the Acknowledgements: ‘We would like to thank Michael D. Howell for his role in reviewing our manuscript and Viknesh Sounderajah for his input in the conception of this review’.

This has been corrected in both the PDF and HTML versions of the Article.
