Using functional genomics to explore biological networks: a call for papers
Published: 2026

The journal GENETICS is calling for submission of papers for a Special Issue on “Using functional genomics to explore biological networks.”

Functional genomics has transformed our ability to investigate biological systems at scale. By enabling systematic perturbation, measurement, and analysis of genes, transcripts, proteins, and regulatory elements, functional genomics offers powerful ways to uncover the structure, dynamics, and logic of biological networks. Understanding how network dysregulation causes disease is critical for improving human health. This Special Issue will highlight studies that use functional genomics to reveal how networks are organized, how they respond to perturbation, and how network architecture shapes cellular and organismal phenotypes.

We welcome experimental and computational contributions that use functional genomics approaches to explore biological networks across scales and organisms. Examples of research topics include:

Mapping of genetic, regulatory, protein, signaling, metabolic, and other biological networks using functional genomics data.

Mapping of genetic, regulatory, protein, signaling, metabolic, and other biological networks using functional genomics data.

Integrative analyses that combine different functional genomics datasets to map biological networks.

Integrative analyses that combine different functional genomics datasets to map biological networks.

Functional genomics studies that illuminate network responses to environmental or genetic perturbations.

Functional genomics studies that illuminate network responses to environmental or genetic perturbations.

New experimental or computational methods for measuring, modeling, or interpreting biological networks.

New experimental or computational methods for measuring, modeling, or interpreting biological networks.
