The original version of this Article contained an error in Fig. 6A, where an incorrect histopathological image panel was included during figure preparation. The incorrect Figure 6 along with its captions is provided below.Fig. 6Photomicrographs of H & E-stained liver sections from control, GN, and different phenol extracts and histopathological scoring of hepatic injury. Thick black arrows: portal inflammation. Thin black arrows: extramedullary hematopoiesis in dilated sinusoids. Low magnification X: 100 bar 100 and high magnification X: 400 bar 50. Hepatic injury scores are statistically analyzed by Kruskal-Walli’s test followed by Dunn’s test to compare all means. Data are represented as mean ± SEM. Different alphabetical letters mean significant at p > 0.05. C: Control, Gn: Gentamycin, M: moringa, n = 6/group.
