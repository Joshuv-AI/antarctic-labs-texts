Correction for Jin et al., Autocrine signaling by an Aplysia neurotrophin forms a presynaptic positive feedback loop

Correction for “Autocrine signaling by an Aplysia neurotrophin forms a presynaptic positive feedback loop,” by Iksung Jin, Hiroshi Udo, Russell Nicholls, Huixiang Zhu, Eric R. Kandel, and Robert D. Hawkins, which published November 5, 2018; 10.1073/pnas.1810649115 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 11, E11168–E11177).
