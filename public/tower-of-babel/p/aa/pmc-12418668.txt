Correction: Wristful thinking: exploring the effects of robotic rehabilitation and cross-education for persons with multiple sclerosis
Authors: Kailynn Mannella, Giulia A. Albanese, Valentina Massone, Kevin E. Power, Duane C. Button, Jonathan P. Farthing, Shawn M. Beaudette, Dave S. Ditor, Craig D. Tokuno, Jacopo Zenzeri, Michael W. R. Holmes
Published: 2025

Correction: Journal of NeuroEngineering and Rehabilitation (2025) 22:152 10.1186/s12984-025-01684-y

In the sentence beginning “These average percent change …… maximal contraction [6].” in the Clinical assessment heading under Discussion section of this article [1], the values “12 to 14 weeks” should read as “4 to 12 weeks”.

The corrected sentence is “These average percent change scores for the untrained limb are greater than previously reported in a meta-analysis that concluded an average change score of 52% in the trained limb and 7.6% in the untrained limb following resistive exercise ranging from 4 to 12 weeks in duration and an intensity of 55–100% of maximal contraction [6].”

The original article has been corrected.
