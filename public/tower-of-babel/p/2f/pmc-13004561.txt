ABSTRACT
OBJECTIVE:Coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) which brings the epidemic situation to the public has spread rapidly and produce multiple variations. At present, Western medicine still lacks the specific medicine or vaccines for coronavirus. However, amount of evidence shows that traditional Chinese medicine (TCM) has advantages in releasing the symptoms of mild-to-moderate COVID patients. Those treatments are not only improving the course of the primary disease but also curb progress to severe pneumonia or acute respiratory distress syndrome. Therefore, taking TCM intervention or combined treatments appropriately to prevent worsening illness is of vital significance. This study mainly focuses on the data analysis on the effects of TCM in restoring the immune balance of COVID patients. By collecting clinical data from mild to moderate patients, we expected to figure out if TCM only plays the role of curbing inflammation or having a two-way influence in balancing the immune microenvironment.METHODS:Seven digital databases including PubMed, EMBASE, Cochrane Library, China National Knowledge Infrastructure, China Science and Technology Journal Database (VIP), Wanfang Database, and China Biology Medicine were searched from December 2019 to August 2022 nothingness of language restrictions. The studies retrieved from the database were selected and the data extracted to assess the methodological quality of the included randomized controlled trials (RCTs). Statistical analysis was completed. Pulmonary computed tomography, clinical cure rate, rate of conversion to severe cases, length of hospital stay, and scores of TCM syndrome were defined as the primary outcomes, the secondary outcomes were white blood cell count, lymphocyte (LYM) count, and C-reactive protein (CRP). This study was registered with PROSPERO (CRD42022341482).RESULTS:Nine eligible RCTs including 1159 participants were included in this meta-analysis. Compared with Western medicine treatment alone, our meta-analyses found that traditional Chinese combined Western medicine treatment has a higher clinical cure rate, better absorption of lung inflammation, and significantly shorter hospital stay. In terms of inflammatory factors, TCM can significantly reduce the CRP content compared with Western medicine methods, but the leukocyte and LYM content was not significantly different between the two treatments. In some research, TCM even has a trend accelerating the inflammation process on some specific stages of the disease.CONCLUSION:Chinese herbal medicine combined with conventional therapy is significantly effective and invulnerable in the treatment of mild-to-moderate COVID-19. In terms of control inflammation, TCM does not only block the disease onset by simply inhibiting inflammation but balancing the human environment through bidirectional regulation of inflammatory cells. However, considering of the lack of research into how TCM could activate the natural immune response, the discussion of the mechanism cannot be stretched, more high-quality RCTs are still needed in the future.

Introduction

As more clinical experiments proved that TCM reflects unique advantages in the prevention, treatment, and rehabilitation in COVID disease, the effects of combined treatment are definite and positive.[3] Besides, the amount of evidence showed TCM has significant effects on patients with mild-to-moderate COVID disease. As a large number of epidemiological survey data show[4] that mild-to-moderate COVID-19 accounts for the largest proportion of cases, this suggests that using TCM to treat patients with mild-to-moderate diseases is an important method to improve the cure rate and reduce the mortality rate.

At the same time, we noted that the degree of inflammatory response was closely determined to the TCM, enough attention should also be paid to the changes in various indicators of inflammation, for the intensity of the inflammatory response is essential for mild-to-moderate patients. Besides, there is a short of systematic studies on the specific mechanism in TCM. As the effects of TCM on inflammatory response are not clear, a series of questions need to be answered urgently. In this regard, we carried out a series of studies on the effects of TCM in regulating the inflammatory response in patients with mild-to-moderate COVID disease.

Discussion

The importance of restoring immune balance in preventing severe situation in patients with coronavirus disease 2019 disease

COVID-19 is caused by novel coronavirus, named as novel coronavirus 2019, which belongs to β Genus crown and has already generated multiple variant types, bringing long-period and complex challenges to our medical system.[17] According to clinical manifestations and auxiliary examinations, COVID-19 can be divided into light, ordinary, heavy, and severe types.[18] While under the background of the coronavirus pandemic, the mortality rate of severe/critical patients has been greatly reduced for the population has been vaccinated on a large scale. The vast majority of COVID-19-infected people are asymptomatic or having mild symptoms. However, there will still be more than 10% of patients with worsening conditions,[19] even develop to acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS) or multiple organ dysfunction syndrome (MODS).[20] Therefore, it is of great significance to take effective treatment measures for light and medium-type COVID-19 patients as soon as possible to release the symptoms and prevent the transformation to critical illness.

Inflammatory disorder[21] refers to the imbalance of the pro-inflammatory/anti-inflammatory immune regulatory network when the body is attacked by pathogens resulting in the positive feedback mechanism between immune cytokines and immune cells. Thus, the excessive increase of pro-inflammatory cytokines will instantly trigger the release of MODS or ARDS, leading to the death of patients. At present, a large number of basic experimental studies have proved that inflammatory disorder is the core pathological factor leading to the aggravation and even death of patients with COVID-19,[22] including physiological changes and disorders related to inflammation and impairment of immune function.[2324] As a result, inhibiting excessive inflammation symptom response is one of the most significant methods to prevent inflammatory storms and regulating the body’s inflammatory balance for mild-to-moderate COVID-19 patients.

The results of this study

For the above reasons, we summarized the medical trials of TCM therapy to restore the inflammatory balance. To the quality of our knowledge, this is the most completely systematic overview and meta-analysis which evaluating the efficacy of TCM and predictors of mortality in COVID-19 patients, in which we determined it is carefully associated to inflammatory markers.

In this experiment, we have collected nine double-arm control experiments in eight databases. It was found that patients who have been treated with Chinese medicine have better clinical efficacy and prognosis. It can be seen from the results that the combined treatment has a higher clinical cure rate, better absorption of lung inflammation, and significantly shorter hospital stay. In terms of inflammatory factors, both traditional Chinese medicine or Western medicine means can significantly reduce the level of inflammatory factors in specific stages such as the middle or late stages of the disease. Besides, TCM can significantly reduce the CRP content compared with Western medicine methods, while the leukocyte and LYM content were not significantly different between the two treatments. In some research, TCM can even slightly increase the content of inflammatory cells which is more likely to be detected at the beginning of the disease. Considering of the result that TCM can greatly reduce the more possibility of turning mild symptoms to severe disease and restore the completed immune function compared with Western medicine alone. Therefore, this study suggests that TCM does not only block the disease onset by simply inhibiting inflammation but balancing the immune microenviroment through bidirectional regulation of inflammatory cells.

The repeating ingredients and effects of traditional Chinese medicine in the prescription of coronavirus disease 2019

The active components of TCM have beneficial effects in the treatment of COVID-19 through various molecular mechanisms, including affecting signaling pathways, inhibiting the release of proinflammatory cytokines, reduction of oxidative stress, and inhibition of viral replication. After the analysis of the main components and related effects in the nine prescriptions, they were found to be highly associated.

For example, glycyrrhizin contained by LHQW granules and Xuanfei Dayu decoction, and quercetin contained with LHQW granules and Qingfei Paidu decoction, all have the effects of targeted inhibiting 3CLpro to inhibit novel coronavirus replication and regulate immune function. While the emodin contained in Qingfei Paidu decoction and LHQW granules can inhibit the MAPK pathway, reducing oxidative stress and inflammation. Besides, the ephedrine in Qingfei Paidu decoction and Huashi Jiedu decoction can inhibit the virus binding to angiotensin-converting enzyme 2 (ACE 2), regulating the immune function by preventing the virus from entering the body. Moreover, baicalein in LHQW granules, Qingfei Paidu decoction can directly inhibit inflammatory responses by signal pathways such as NF-kB. There are many other specific ingredients and herbs in the included RCTs, and these repeating ingredients can be used in the treatment of COVID disease. The specific mechanisms are explained below.

Mechanism of traditional Chinese medicine to inhibit coronavirus disease 2019 inflammation

Through a large number of related experimental studies on TCM treatment of COVID-19, the specialists have summarized the possible mechanisms of TCM prescriptions to inhibit the severe conversion of mild/moderate COVID patients involved in the included literature. We mainly discuss it from three aspects: anti-inflammatory factor release, anti-mucus production, and antiviral replication.

In terms of anti-inflammatory factors, TCM was found to reduce the production and activation of inflammatory factors. For instance, researchers found that the extract of honeysuckle and gardenia in Xinguan No. 1 decoction can reduce alveolar wall congestion and inflammatory cell infiltration in ALI model mice.[25] To be more specific, it can reduce the levels of various cytokines, including interleukin (IL)-4, IL-6, IL-8, IL-10, and ICAM-1 in the lung tissue, thus playing a positive role against lung infection; In addition, the experimental group treated with gardenia extract[26] have been found that the expression of IL-6 and tumor necrosis factor-α (TNF-α) was significantly reduced. Due to the expression of TLR3 and the TIR, cohesion protein (TIRF) was significantly inhibited, decreasing complications at the same time; Multiple components in LHQW such as glycyrrhizin, baicalein, forsythia syin, and emodin[8] could significantly decrease the levels of pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-6, TNF-α, and macrophage chemokine protein-1) to reduce oxidative stress and inflammatory responses. They can also effectively reduce IL-1 β, IL-2, and IL-13, and alleviate lung injury which associated with inflammatory cell infiltration; Quercetin, baicalein, and emodin in Qingfei Paidu decoction[27] may play an important role in treating COVID-19. The key mechanism could relate to the interfere with toll-like receptors and modulate downstream signaling-mediated inflammatory cytokine expression. Xu et al.[28] analyze these ingredients using the network pharmacological method, discovering that quercetin, baicalein, naringenin, luteolin, and kaempferol may be the main active component with different degrees of anti-inflammatory efficacy. In addition, it can also regulate the anti-inflammatory response of the cells and reduce immune damage by regulating the related signaling pathways such as IL-17, TNF, and MAPK.

In terms of anti-mucus secretion, poria cocos, atractylodes, and other ingredients contained in Qingfei Paidu decoction[29] can inhibited the mRNA expression of IL-1 β, IL-13, epidermal cell growth factor (EGF), and EGF receptor (EGFR) in rats with high secretion of airway mucus. Improving respiratory endothelial hyperplasia and mucus secretion is possibly related to the regulation of mucus hypersecretion and stimulating factor levels. Other studies have found that[30] heat-clearing and phlegm drugs such as Scutellaria baicalensis, gardenia, and pinellia can interfere with the airway mucus hypersecretion by blocking the “mucin MUC5AC secretion” signaling pathway. The specific mechanisms[31] include reducing the stimulation of goblet cells and mucous glands by inhibiting the ERK and p38 signaling pathways, which in the downstream of the NE and EGFR signaling pathways. Some TCM active ingredients such as baicalin can regulate the expression of phospho-Erk, phospho-p38, and phospho-JNK in the MAPK signaling pathway, to inhibit the expression of MUC5ac mRNA and the secretion of MUC5ac in human airway epithelial cells caused by Lipopolysaccharide (LPS), thus playing the effects of inhibiting cell mucus hypersecretion.

In terms of antiviral replication and transcription, protein-active enzymes are important effector targets, including 3CLpro hydrolase, four structural proteins (E, S, N, and M), and so on. Among most of the currently known coronaviruses, 3CLpro is called as dominant protease due to its ability to hydrolytically cleave the virus, which is a key step in inhibiting replication. Various TCM active ingredients can produce antiviral effects by activating 3CLpro. For example, baicalein contained in the S. baicalensis can interact with two catalytic residues of 3CLpro (the key S1/S2 subsite and the oxyanion ring) to inhibit viral replication;[32] The plant protein glycyrrhizin A extracted from licorice also plays an important role in inhibiting viral replication; In addition, forsythia, ephedrine, and luteolin – the active components in the Qingfei Paidu decoction, can combine with 3CLpro to inhibit novel coronavirus replication and transcription.

On the other hand, as coronavirus has four structural proteins, including envelope (E), spike (S), nucleocapsid (N), and membrane (M).[33] Inhibiting the binding of these proteins to ACE2 prevents coronavirus entry into host cells. TCM pharmacology studies found that ephedrine can effectively prevent the S protein on the coronavirus from binding to ACE2, to reduce the damage of the virus.[34] Meanwhile, glycyrrhizin in licorice also interferes the binding[35] of COVID virus with ACE2 protein receptors. Besides, network pharmacological analysis shows that the active ingredients in Jiedu decoction and Qingfei Paidu decoction, such as quercetin, kaempferol, luteolin, flavonoids, and plant sterols, can effectively bind to ACE2 in the COVID treatment as well. At the stage of blocking the binding of spike protein to the host cells, it was found that if the virus tends to fuse with the host cell membrane after attachment, at least one protease derived from the host cell is needed to help dissolve the spike into S1 and S2 subunits, to promote the fusion of the viral capsid protein and the host cell membrane.[36] Studies have shown that when TMPRSS2 is expressed in the host cell, it could activate the receptor-binding domain of the S protein, helping coronavirus attach to the host cell surface receptor. Limitations

The current find out about additionally had numerous barriers and ought to be interpreted cautiously. (1) Although a complete of nine research have been blanketed in the meta-analysis, the wide variety of research handy for the evaluation of every predictor was once inadequate to permit significant subgroup analyses. (2) There are few literature that reports adverse reactions and long-term follow-up, so it is impossible to accurately compare the safety and long-term efficacy among various TCMs. (3) The heterogeneity of this study is large, which may be related to the risk of bias in the included studies, and the inconsistency of disease diagnosis standards, treatment courses, measurement methods, and units of outcome indicators. (4) The quality of the literature included in the study is generally low, and some studies on the concealment of random allocation schemes and blinding methods are not mentioned, so there may be bias in selection, implementation, and measurement, which affects the authenticity of the results. (5) Although this study has carried out a comprehensive search in the four major domestic databases and the three major foreign databases, there is still a possibility of missing detection.

Conclusion

Chinese herbal medicine combined with conventional therapy is significantly effective and invulnerable in the treatment of mild-to-moderate COVID-19. In terms of control inflammation, TCM does not only block the disease onset by simply inhibiting inflammation but balancing the human environment through bidirectional regulation of inflammatory cells. However, considering of the lack of research into how TCM could activate the natural immune response, the discussion of the mechanism cannot be stretched, more high-quality RCTs are still needed in the future.
