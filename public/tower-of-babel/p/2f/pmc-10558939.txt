A novel major accidents evolution model and its application in Chinese industrial accident
Authors: Guirong Zhang, Shuai Wang, Wei Feng, Wanfu Ouyang
Published: 2023

ABSTRACT
The accident mortality rate of major accidents (MAs) show that China is still in the bottleneck period of accident prevention and control. To further promote the MAs prevention and control, this paper presents a novel major accidents evolution model from the theoretical perspective of information processing (IP). Firstly, based on the safety science paradigm of accident prevention and the emergency management paradigm of accident control, a safety information processing (SIP) process is proposed. Secondly, established the SIP model for different stages of accident prevention and control, which involves danger information processing (DIP), potential hazard information processing (PHIP), risk information processing (RIP), and emergency information processing (EIP). Thirdly, revealed the SIP of various management subject and the failure principle of accident prevention and control, that is, MAs occur under the premise of continuous failures of DIP, PHIP, RIP, and EIP under the social-technical system. Finally, the DPRE-IP model is proposed from the whole evolution path of “danger-potential hazard-risk-accident”. To demonstrate the viability of the model, this model is applied to the “6·13” Wenling major explosion accident. The results show that the proposed DPRE-IP model can provide new ideas for the formulation of accident prevention and control measures and accident analysis.

Introduction

With the rapid development of the productive and economy, many serious accidents have happened, which brought great damage to social production and life. Studies have shown that large-scale accidents have pervaded many parts of the world, especially developing countries. Most of accidents occurred in transportation [1], chemicals [2], and coal mines [3]. As a result, major accidents (MAs) became the focus of the theoretical and practical areas. In the field of safety science, scholars put forward accident prevention countermeasures by summarizing the law of risk and accident occurrence [4,5]. Although this paradigm cannot predict the occurrence of unknown accidents, it is still an efficient, highly targeted, and indispensable method for accident prevention. Scholars conducted targeted studies on the prevention and control of MAs, the methods mainly included risk analysis, human reliability analysis, the improvement of accident investigation mechanism, the optimization of emergency management, the prevention, control technology of major accidents, and so on. However, there is still a lack of studies on failures of prevention and control of MAs that include management subjects and management objects under the unified analysis framework, and it is necessary to establish a model for the complex process of its failure to guide practice. Studies on the prevention and control of MAs.

Many behavioral intervention theories, such as the information—motive—behavior skill model [19] and information—knowledge—belief—behavior theory [20], show that information is the element that controls people's behavior. Information, as something to eliminate uncertainty [21], conveys the safety state of the system and becomes the basis of safety decision-making. Its asymmetry is the main cause leading to serious accidents and environmental pollution [22]. This also indirectly shows the importance of information processing (IP), which is the carrier of safety behavior. At the micro-level, safety behavior was regarded as a circular process of “information cognition—action—information cognition” [10]. At the meso-level, information storage and processing were considered to be the most important problem facing organizations today [23]. At the macro-level, the process of national governance was regarded as the process of IP [24]. Between ambiguity and clarity, the symmetry of IP becomes fundamental to the stable functioning of all aspects of society and the state. With the improvement of the complexity and coupling of the social-technical system, especially after entering the era of industrial 4.0, new technologies, new energy, new materials, and new transportation are constantly emerging. New risks, new injuries, new accidents, and new disasters will also be accompanied one after another, these changes are based on information-driven. As a result, systems become more and more dependent on information.

As a part of safety science research, the accident causation model can not only reflect the occurrence of certain accidents, but also provide a scientific basis for accident prevention, accident prediction, and the improvement of safety management. According to statistics, more than 50 different accident causation models have been constructed, most of which are based on the compatibility and coordination between human characteristics, machine performance, and environmental conditions [10]. Currently, more scholars started to carry out relevant studies from the perspective of information. This is the result of the close relationship between information and behavior, as shown in Table 2. The above research shows that IP is the carrier of individual and organizational safety behavior, and human psychological behavior and external IP behavior are the roots of explicit safety behavior. However, the above studies lacked a thorough and comprehensive analysis of the causes of accidents from the perspective of information, and ignored the impact of IP on the management of various stages of accidents. Theoretical foundations

Model construction tries to integrate the accident prevention theory of multidisciplinary paradigm, and forms a unified model with SIP as the chain, which combines the general law of management object and the behavior pattern of management subject. The evolution of accidents has experienced the path of danger-potential hazard—risk—accident under the safety science paradigm [28], which requires targeted safety decisions and behaviors at each stage, but it is slightly different from the actual situation of management subjects. In practice, Chinese management bodies attach importance to the whole process of prevention, mitigation, response, and recovery of accidents, and adopt corresponding management methods and resource allocation in different stages, to identify the characteristics of management behavior in each stage. As a chain, the macro-, and micro- SIP run through the interactions between managers and accident at all stages.

Safety science paradigm for accident prevention

In China, safety science is a structural knowledge system for the correct understanding and prevention of accidents and their development laws, which is a new and marginal science, involving multiple disciplines of social science and natural science, and all aspects of human production and life [29]. Undoubtedly, safety science applies to accident prevention in various fields. Scholars have clarified that the development of accidents will go through three stages: gestation, growth, and loss.•In the gestation stage, the subsystem is safe, but people can feel the possible danger. To prevent danger from turning into harm, safety decision-making has been carried out according to the safety prediction (SF) results to achieve intrinsic safety. The essence of SF is to collect and analyze relevant safety data and information within the scope of governance, to implement safety policies, safety standards, safety methods, safety precautions, and other countermeasures in the initial stage of accident development [30].•In the growth stage, unsafe behavior, unsafe state of matter and environment, and management defects appear, the above are potential hazards of accidents in production and life [31]. At this time, people can specifically point out its existence. In this stage, the accident should be prevented through the investigation and elimination of potential hazards (IEPH) in the micro-system (micro-system refers to the “human-machine-environment” interaction system directly involved in production and life).•The loss stage can also be called the stage that safety risks are out of control. With the participation of people-based disaster-affected bodies, the potential hazard is converted into risk. At this time, the magnitude of risk (R=f(P,C)) will determine whether the accident occurs, involving the possibility of accident (P) and the severity of accident consequences (C). The countermeasure at this stage is the prevention and control of risk (PCR), namely, reducing the possibility of accidents and the severity of accident consequences.

Emergency management paradigm for accident control

Since the outbreak of SARS in 2003, to cope with the threat of emergencies, China has constructed an emergency management system with “One Planning plus Three Systems” (the planning and legislative/institutional/regulatory systems) as the core, and strengthened the emergency management capacity. In the emergency response law of China, the object of emergency management is emergencies, including public health events, accident disasters, natural disasters, and social security events. At the level of emergency occurrence cycle, the object in the whole process of emergency management was summarized as “risk-disaster-crisis” [32]. In the process of industrial production, emergency management pays more attention to accident control, which can also be understood as crisis management.

In the emergency response law of China, the countermeasures of crisis management are developed as emergency response and rescue, which can also be called emergency response (ER1). When the accident risk breaks out, ER1 will be carried out immediately and effective measures will be taken quickly to control the situation as soon as possible. On the one hand, taking major hazard sources as the object to take control measures to prevent the occurrence of secondary and derivative events; on the other hand, to release information on the development of emergencies and ER1 work in a unified, accurate, and timely manner to prevent social panic and other consequences caused by the dissemination of false information. Many cases in the past, such as the BP oil spill in the Gulf of Mexico and Tianjin Port explosion in China, showed that the occurrence of emergencies will have an impact on the social economy, people's lives, the natural environment, human psychology, and so on after the ER1. To eliminate derivative hazard induced by these effects, emergency management enters the recovery stage (emergency recovery, ER2), taking into account physical recovery, social recovery, and psychological recovery [33].

Therefore, in the process of accident control, ER1 and ER2 will play an important role. On the one hand, ER1 will prevent secondary accidents and some derivative accidents; on the other hand, ER2 will prevent other possible derivative accidents. Safety information processing process

Information exists in everything of any time, the state, content, and change of things are the embodiment of which. As a branch of information, safety information refers to the collection of safety-related information. Wang defines it as the self-display of the future safety state of the system, and its value is to serve for predicting, optimizing, and controlling the future safety state of the system [26]. During the operation of the system, humans, machines, materials, law, environment, and management in the system have become the main components of the safety operation of the social-technical system, all of which transmit safety information and safety state information of the system all the time during the operation of the system. In the field of safety, information asymmetry is the main reason for accidents [22], and information asymmetry is mainly caused by the failure of IP.

In the past, there have been IP processes from both micro-perspective and macro-perspective. Among them, the IP from the micro-perspective refers to the process of individual perception, cognition, and execution of information [34]. The IP from the macro-perspective refers to the process of the acquisition, analysis, and utilization of information by all individuals and organizations in the social-technical system [11]. The difference between the two is that the former is the internal processing mechanism of human perception and cognition of information, belongs to the static safety information processing mode, it is the basic mechanism of human behavior. But the latter highlights both organizational and individual IP mechanisms from the third-person perspective. Therefore, this paper selects the information processing process from the macro-perspective to carry out the subsequent research. SIP during “prevention” & “control”.

Danger information processing (DIP)

Danger information processing is summarized as a progressive process of information collection, information screening, and information mining. Information collection is based on the principle of comprehensive, correct, and applicable information and the relevant information and data of the target system are recorded and counted according to the safety requirements. Based on information collection, information screening is to sort in order of possibility and harmfulness, such as the number, frequency, and causes of a major accident, which will play a positive guiding role in the targeted prevention of major accidents. Information mining is the transformation of data and information to knowledge and wisdom. For example, the mining of the characteristics, rules, and trends of a major accident is an important process of transforming information and data into safety intelligence (useful safety information) [37], thus formulating and implementing specific prevention programs and measures.

Potential hazard information processing (PHIP)

The potential hazard information in the micro-system exists objectively, and the perception, cognition, and execution of which is a chain process of IP for the identification and elimination of potential hazards (IEPH). Information perception is a process in which the safety participant receives the carrier of safety information internally through the sensory organ, which is the premise of further internal processing. Information cognition refers to the process of generating results related to memory, thinking, judgment, reasoning, problem-solving, learning, imagination, and concept formation in the human brain after analyzing the generated perceptual information [11,38]. Rational cognition is the premise of safety decision-making. Information execution is to make decisions and perform response actions to eliminate potential hazards after the perception and cognition of potential hazard information.

Risk information processing (RIP)

Major risk refers to risks that have a significant negative impact on the stable operation and sustained and healthy development of the country's economy and society [39]. To reduce risks of work, analysis, evaluation, and management of risk information are carried out. Information analysis is the process of clarifying major risk points, which refers to parts of the system where major accidents may occur, such as major hazardous facilities and major hazards. Information evaluation is the evaluation of real-time risk information of major risk points, whose results will provide a basis for whether to take measures to reduce risks for the assessed object. Information management is the process of risk control, risk monitoring, and risk early warning of major risk information. Major hazards do not necessarily have major risks, but during the dynamic operation of the system, they may be transformed into major risks at any time, leading to major accidents.

Emergency information processing (EIP)

To prevent the occurrence of secondary accidents after the occurrence of a major accident, the implementation of ER involves information integration, information communication, and information sharing. Information integration is to unify relevant information with the major accident and system, to clarify the on-site situation, thus making correct emergency decisions. Information communication refers to the transfer and exchange of key information by multiple parties for emergency response, emergency rescue, and post-disaster reconstruction. Information sharing, on the one hand, discloses the real situation of accident occurrence and disposal to the society; on the other hand, it clarifies rumors to prevent social panic caused by various rumors; and finally provides effective information for citizens to do a good job in dealing with the situation.

The failure essence of accident causation based on SIP

Based on the chain process of SIP, this paper constructs the accident causation essence model as shown in Fig. 5, which contains two basic hierarchical structures—the left is the basic principle of the failure of IP, and the right is the state path of the accident. According to the event chain principle, the premise of the failure of SIP is the domino effect caused by the failure of a certain link of the acquisition of safety information, the analysis of safety information, and the utilization of safety information. This process includes 8 possibilities, but only all links are correct can ensure the safety of the behavior, otherwise, it will lead to unsafe behavior. Among them, the acquisition failure of safety information includes untimely, incorrect, inappropriate, or inadequate access to safety information; the analysis failure of safety information can be understood as the reasoning error of the obtained information; the utilization failure of safety information is the misuse of cognitive information, and it is also the failure of individuals or organizations to respond to expectations. Failure of PHIP

Potential hazards cannot be eliminated under complicated conditions. For example, when urban subway lines pass through large rivers, the large rivers themselves have huge potential hazards, but they cannot be eliminated. It reflects the importance of PCR. However, when the safety state of people, machines, environment, or management can be changed, the IEPH is required. Regulatory authorities implement safety laws, regulations, and standards to supervise and review companies to achieve restraint on corporate behavior; enterprise implements safety laws, regulations, and standards, communicate safety policies to lower levels to increase safety resources and safety investment and strengthen safety management; safety operators need to have professional skills to perform safe operations under the constraints of the established safety laws, regulations, standards, and norms. The above three levels have an obvious superior-subordinate relationship—the superior supervises and manages the safety behavior of the subordinate, and the behavior of the subordinate directly determines the appearance and elimination of potential hazards. Failure of RIP

The PCMR is carried out aimed at major risk points. When potential hazards cannot be eliminated or are not investigated and eliminated in time, it will play an important role. The process includes the identification, evaluation, and control of major risk points, which corresponds to the process of the analysis, evaluation, and management of risk information. Under the policies, laws and regulations, industry regulations, and supervision and management of the higher-level government, companies should clarify the major risk points in the relevant system or the major risk objects to be established. The major risk objects must maintain a safe distance from the human following the regulations, and at the same time carry out risk management during the operation of risk points. In addition, the risk assessment of safety agencies has played a positive role in guiding the response to major risk points. However, in the major accidents that occurred in the past, there have been cases of safety agencies neglecting their duties. For example, in the case of the Tianjin Port fire and explosion accident, several safety agencies resorted to fraud and conducted illegal safety reviews, evaluations, and acceptance checks. ER is jointly completed by individuals, enterprises, and governments based on emergency preparedness after an accident occurs through information integration, information communication, and information sharing. To verify the scientificity and practicability of the model, the DPRE-IP model is applied to the analysis of “6·13” Wenling major explosion accident.

Accident profile

On June 13, 2020, a hanging tanker filled with 25.36 tons of liquefied petroleum gas entered the Wenling West exit ramp of the Wenzhou direction of Shenhai Expressway at 16:40:54. When the driver drove the vehicle from the section of the speed limit of 60 km/h to the curve section of the speed limit of 30 km/h, no timely deceleration measures were taken, which caused the rear of the trailer to tilt to the right and rollover. Then the tank body collided with the end of the concrete guardrail of the ramp overpass, and the tank body ruptured and disintegrated. The liquefied petroleum gas in the tank quickly vented, vaporized, diffused, and spread, and then exploded. The accident caused heavy casualties, and nearby vehicles, roads, and some houses and factories in the surrounding Liangshan Village were damaged to varying degrees, which finally caused 20 deaths, 175 people were hospitalized (24 of whom were seriously injured), and the direct economic loss was 94.77815 million RMB. Discussion

The model is a method to reveal the shape, characteristics, and essence of the prototype, so it can not only have a positive significance at the theoretical level but also indirectly play a guiding role at the practical level. According to the results of case analysis, the DPRE-IP model proposed in this paper can structure the occurrence process of major accidents clearly and systematically, which will be more targeted, systematic, rooted, and effective for the prevention and control of MAs. All the proposed information variables have been verified in application.

The novel model of major accident causation proposed in this paper has made many improvements on the existing model. First of all, the system scope set in this study is the social-technical system because government behavior is the fundamental influence on grassroots organizations and individual behavior, which has often been ignored in the past. Information can connect all levels and structures, highlighting the coupling and interactive relationship between them on the impact of accidents. Literature [11] is the same as the system scope of this paper, but the difference is that this paper only classifies the influencing factors of information on unsafe behavior into three variables: information acquisition, information analysis, and information utilization, and lacks consideration of the differences between different subjects’ information behaviors. Other relevant literatures analyze the factors that influence individual and organizational behavior from a micro-perspective [10,[25], [26], [27]], but also lack consideration of the differences between information behaviors of different subjects. Therefore, to reflect this difference, this paper combines the accident stage, subject behavior, and information processing, and systematically represents various scenarios of major accidents with information processing behavior under the information paradigm. This overcomes the defects of the system scope and fuzzy variables in the above studies.

The value of the DPRE-IP model is as follows: (1) Enrich the accident causation theory. Further expand the accident causation model from the perspective of information, and refine the information variables while refining the accident development stage, which is of great significance for practitioners to understand the accident occurrence law more comprehensively; (2) provide a new method for accident investigation. Compared with the accident investigation method proposed by Wu [11], this method will have a more comprehensive scope of accident investigation and more specific content; (3) It provides new guidance for the optimization of national laws and regulations as well as institutional mechanisms and departmental regulations. In the future, based on this model, in-depth research will be carried out to promote the improvement of system safety, such as new safety education method, improvement of emergency management capacity, and so on; (4) the accident causation model constructed in this paper can provide a basis for accurate safety management and intelligent emergency response. For example, accurate safety management can be divided into accurate safety prediction, accurate potential hazard elimination, and accurate risk prevention and control. Intelligent emergency can be divided into intelligent emergency information collection platform, intelligent emergency information analysis platform, intelligent emergency information communication platform, and intelligent emergency information sharing platform. On this basis, design and construction are carried out for each subject. (5) The accident analysis will be further promoted. By combining Bayesian network [[40], [41], [42], [43]] etc., the possibility and consequences of accidents caused by IP errors and key factors in various situations will be analyzed through data acquisition, providing an important basis for accident prevention and control.

Of course, this article also has some shortcomings. For example, whether the model proposed in this paper is applicable to other accident situations remains to be verified; the information variable proposed in this paper also needs to be improved. Therefore, the follow-up research should first have more verification work to improve. On this basis, starting from a multidisciplinary perspective (such as artificial intelligence, safety science and engineering, public management, etc.), the influence mechanism and improvement path of information-driven major accident prevention and control effect improvement should be studied, which requires the participation of a large number of scholars.

Conclusion

Taking MAs as the research object, this paper constructs a prevention and control failure evolution model based on IP—the DPRE-IP model, to guide Chinese practice. At the same time, as a new research paradigm, it will provide ideas for related research in other countries. The main conclusions are as follows: (1) SIP is a chain process of the acquisition of safety information, the analysis of safety information, and the utilization of safety information. The failure of a link will lead to unsafe behavior. As a result, the occurrence of continuous unsafe behavior caused by continuous failure of SIP is the essence of accident causation from the perspective of IP. (2) In the development process of major accidents, corresponding safety information is transmitted at different stages, including danger information, potential hazard information, risk information, and emergency information. The continuous failure of the above SIP means the failure of the prevention and control of MAs. (3) The SIP in the social-technical system is divided into macro- SIP, meso- SIP, and micro-SIP. Only when they participate and communicate with each other can they realize the “prevention” and “control” in a certain link of SIP. The failure of SIP in a link is the result of the interaction of the three and the domino effect.

According to the above research, the occurrence of major accidents comes from the failure of prevention and control, and the core is the continuous failure of SIP. This failure spans macro and micro levels and runs through the four stages of DIP, PHIP, RIP and EIP, which is a domino chain reaction. In the context of China, this chain reaction is mainly due to the failure of the multi-level bureaucratic system of local governments and other subjects in the process of monitoring and early warning, including the public administration efficiency of grass-roots governments, the weak participation of emergency enterprises, the limited emergency literacy of citizens, etc. These problems have both social structure and technical factors.
