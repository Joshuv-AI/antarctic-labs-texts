Clinical diagnostic value of methylated SEPT9 combined with NLR, PLR and LMR in colorectal cancer
Authors: Meiyuan Huang, Shuang Deng, Ming Li, Zhenyu Yang, Jiaxing Guo, Yi Deng, Dongliang Chen, Bokang Yan
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractPurposeThis study aimed to investigate clinical diagnostic values of mSEPT9 combined with NLR, PLR and LMR in CRC.Methods329 subjects composed of 120 CRC patients, 105 polyps patients and 104 healthy participants were prospectively recruited. Clinicopathologic features were collected and analyzed. Plasma samples were collected for mSEPT9, NLR, PLR and LMR test. The sensitivity, specificity and AUC of each biomarker separately or in combination were estimated by the ROC curve.ResultsThe levels of NLR, PLR and the PDR of mSEPT9 in CRC patients were significantly higher than those in non-CRC subjects, while LMR was the opposite. The PDR of mSEPT9 in CRC patients was significantly correlated with age, tumor size, tumor stage and M stage. ROC curve analysis demonstrated moderate diagnostic values of mSEPT9, NLR, PLR and LMR in CRC patients with AUC of 0.78 (Se = 0.68, and Sp = 0.89), 0.78 (Se = 0.68, and Sp = 0.83), 0.80 (Se = 0.68, and Sp = 0.81), and 0.77 (Se = 0.72, and Sp = 0.73), respectively. Moreover, combination of these four biomarkers dramatically enhanced the diagnostic accuracy of CRC (AUC = 0.92, Se = 0.90, and Sp = 0.87), especially for CRC patients with large tumors (AUC = 0.95) or distal metastasis (AUC = 0.95).ConclusionmSEPT9, NLR, PLR and LMR showed the potential to be reliable biomarkers for the diagnosis of CRC. And the combined application of these biomarkers further improved the diagnostic accuracy of CRC significantly.Supplementary InformationThe online version contains supplementary material available at 10.1186/s12876-024-03332-6.Keywords: Colorectal cancer, Methylated SEPT9, Neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio, Platelet-to-lymphocyte ratio, Lymphocyte-to-monocyte ratio, Diagnosis

Introduction

Colorectal cancer (CRC) is one of the most common malignancies, ranking third in incidence and second in mortality worldwide [1]. In China, CRC is the second most commonly diagnosed cancer and the fourth leading cause of cancer-related death [2]. With the development of treatment strategies, the prognosis of early CRC has improved significantly. However, a large number of patients at advanced stage still lack effective treatment. Therefore, it is of great significance to diagnosis CRC at early stage for improving the prognosis of patients.

At present, some conventional methods for CRC detection, such as colonoscopy (the gold standard for CRC diagnosis), fecal occult blood test (FOBT) or fecal immunochemical test (FIT) (the most widely used screening biomarkers for CRC diagnosis) and carcinoembryonic antigen (CEA) or carbohydrate antigen-199 (CA199) all contain their own limitations, such as uncomfortable experience, high costs, susceptible to interference, low sensitivity and low specificity [3–7]. Thus, identifying a patient-friendly approach with high diagnostic accuracy is crucial to improve the clinical outcome of CRC patients.

DNA methylation is one of the most common epigenetic modifications, which plays a crucial role in the occurrence and development of various cancers, including CRC [8, 9]. Currently, several DNA methylation of genes, including nerve growth factor receptor (NGFR), short stature homeobox 2 (SHOX2) and septin9 (SEPT9), have been shown to be valuable in the early plasma-based detection of CRC [10, 11]. Of these methylation biomarkers, SEPT9, located at chromosome 17q25.3, which encodes GTP-binding proteins (G-proteins) is emerging to be a promising screening biomarker for CRC [12]. Many researches have explored the relationship between the positive detection rate (PDR) of methylated septin9 (mSEPT9) in CRC patients and different clinicopathological characteristics. Overall, they demonstrate that mSEPT9 is positively correlated with the degree of CRC malignancy, but there are also some differences between these analyses [13–15]. In addition, the reported diagnostic accuracy of plasma mSEPT9 for CRC varies greatly among different studies, with sensitivity (Se) and specificity (Sp) ranging from 36.6 to 95.6% and 62.2–98.9%, respectively [15–21]. The large difference in the diagnostic value of mSEPT9 may be caused by many factors including small sample size. Thus, it remains to be further investigated whether mSEPT9 is a reliable biomarker for CRC diagnosis.

Inflammation has been demonstrated to play essential role in the process of carcinogenesis [22]. Several biomarkers for measuring systemic inflammation such as, peripheral blood neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio (NLR), platelet-to-lymphocyte ratio (PLR) and lymphocyte-to-monocyte ratio (LMR) have been proved to be critical progression indicators of many cancers, such as, lung cancer, ovarian cancer and CRC [23–26]. And these biomarkers could be easily measured through the routine blood tests of cancer patients. Previous studies have shown NLR, PLR and LMR in peripheral blood have moderate diagnosis value of CRC independently or in combination, however, the diagnostic value of these biomarkers in CRC also remains variable among these studies [27–30]. What’s more, up to date, the combined diagnostic accuracy of inflammatory markers with other serum tumor biomarkers in CRC has been scarcely investigated.

This is the first study to simultaneously detect the levels of plasma mSEPT9, NLR, PLR and LMR in CRC patients. Additionally, we studied the correlation between the positive rate of mSEPT9 and different clinicopathological features in CRC patients. Moreover, we analyzed the separate and combined diagnostic values of mSEPT9, NLR, PLR and LMR in CRC patients compared with non-CRC subjects for the first time. Our study may provide a new noninvasive method with high diagnostic accuracy for the diagnosis of CRC, which is of great potential value for improving the prognosis of CRC patients.

Materials and methods

Participants and samples

All samples including 120 CRC patients, 105 polyps patients and 104 normal subjects (no evidence of disease, NED) were recruited from Zhuzhou Central Hospital between Sep 1, 2020 and Sep 30, 2022. The inclusion criteria were: adults with complete clinicopathological information diagnosed with CRC by endoscopy were given mSEPT9 and routine blood tests; colorectal polyps based on endoscopy and pathological diagnosis; normal subjects including age-matched subjects screened negative for CRC previously who had annual health check-up. The main exclusion criteria were: pregnant women; previous chemotherapy and/or radiotherapy; history of any cancer; evidence of immune diseases or inflammatory diseases.

Sample collection and storage

10 ml and 2 ml of peripheral venous blood of each participants were collected in EDTA-K2 tube, respectively. The 10 ml of blood was processed immediately via double centrifugation at 1500 g for 12 min, then the plasma was stored at -80 °C for subsequent testing. The 2 ml of blood was used for routine blood tests within 4 h.

Methylated SEPT9 detection

The mSEPT9 was detected using Human septin9 Gene Methylation Detection Kit (Weizhenbio Bioengineering Technology Co., Ltd). First, plasma cell-free DNA was extracted from plasma samples, then the DNA was incubated with bisulfte. Subsequently, real-time quantitative PCR (RT-PCR) was used to amplify the methylated target sequences in the bisulfite-converted DNA template. Actin Beta (ACTB) served as the internal control. PCR was performed in a single 30 µl reaction system. The thermal cycling program was as follows: activation at 94 ℃ for 20 min; 45 cycles at 62℃ for 5 s, 55.5 ℃ for 35 s, and 93 ℃ for 30 s; and cooling at 40 ℃ for 5 s. The methylation of SEPT9 was assayed on an ABI 7500 Real-Time PCR Instrument (Life Technologies Holdings Pte Ltd). The cut-off value of mSEPT9 was 41 established in this assay. If Ct < 41, the result was positive, while Ct ≥ 41 or an undetermined Ct was considered a negative result.

Peripheral blood indicators detection

The laboratory data including neutrophil, lymphocyte, monocyte and platelet counts were collected from routine blood tests of each participants using XN20A1 automatic blood analyzer (Sysmex Shanghai Ltd).

Statistical analysis

GraphPad Prism 8.3.0 software was used to perform statistical analyses. The correlations between clinical information and the PDR of mSEPT9 were assessed using Chi-square or Fisher exact test. Student’s t-test was used to determine the significance of differences between two groups. Receiver operating characteristic (ROC) curves were used to evaluate the diagnostic value of mSEPT9, NLR, PLR and LMR. The best cuf-off value were calculated using Youden index. And binary logistic regression was used for combination analysis. P-value < 0.05 was considered to be statistically significant.

Results

Participants characteristics

In this study, a total of 329 subjects being treated in Zhuzhou Central Hospital were enrolled. Among them, there were 184 males and 145 females with a mean age of 58.7 years. The CRC group comprised 70 colon cancer patients and 50 rectal cancer patients, including 63 cases at early stage (stage I and II) and and 57 cases at advance stage (stage III and IV), respectively. And the non-CRC group included 105 patients with polyps and 104 normal subjects (Table 1).

Correlation between mSEPT9 status and pathological characteristics of CRC

To determine the clinical significance of mSEPT9 in CRC, detailed clinicopathological features of 120 CRC patients were collected and summarized in Table 3. The results showed that the mSEPT9 positive rate increased significantly in patients aged 65 years old or older (P < 0.05)(Fig. 2A). Within two tumor size groups, we found a higher PDR of mSEPT9 in patients with tumor maximum diameter ≥ 5 cm (P < 0.01)(Fig. 2B). And the PDR of mSEPT9 was also significantly associated with TNM stage (P < 0.001)(Fig. 2C). In addition, patients with distal metastasis exhibited statistically significant higher PDR than those without distal metastasis (P < 0.05)(Fig. 2D). With respect to the peripheral inflammatory markers, NLR (P < 0.05) and PLR (P < 0.01) showed higher value in mSEPT9 positive patients compared with those negative patients, while LMR was the opposite (P < 0.001) (Table 3). However, no correlation was found between mSEPT9 and gender, tumor location, gross classification, tumor differentiation degree and lymph node metastasis status (P > 0.05)(Table 3). In conclusion, our results showed that the the status of plasma mSEPT9 was associated with more advanced clinicopathologic status of CRC patients.

Diagnostic value of mSEPT9, NLR, PLR and LMR separately or in combination in CRC

To explore the the diagnostic value of mSEPT9, NLR, PLR and LMR in CRC, we plotted the receiver operating curve, with defining the non-CRC subjects as control group. The ROC curve analysis of mSEPT9 showed the area under the ROC curve (AUC) of 0.78 (Best cut-off value = 41.0, 95% confidence interval (CI) 0.72–0.83, Se = 0.68, and Sp = 0.89)(Fig. 3A, Table S1), suggesting mSEPT9 has a moderate value in distinguishing CRC from non-CRC subjects. As for peripheral inflammatory markers, the ROC curve analysis of NLR, PLR and LMR individually showed the AUC of 0.78 (Best cut-off value = 2.5, 95% CI 0.72–0.83, Se = 0.68, and Sp = 0.83), 0.80 (Best cut-off value = 145.7, 95% CI 0.75–0.85, Se = 0.68, and Sp = 0.81), and 0.77 (Best cut-off value = 4.2, 95% CI 0.71–0.82, Se = 0.72, and Sp = 0.73), respectively (Fig. 3A, Table S1), which indicated that all of them had certain and similar diagnostic value in CRC.

Diagnostic value of mSEPT9, NLR, PLR and LMR separately or in combination in CRC. (A) Diagnostic value of mSEPT9, NLR, PLR or LMR in CRC. (B) The combined diagnostic value of mSEPT9 with NLR, PLR or/and LMR in CRC

To further investigate the diagnostic performance of the combination of mSEPT9 with NLR, PLR or/and LMR, four kinds of combination was established. And the results showed that the combination of mSEPT9 with NLR (AUC = 0.88, 95% CI 0.84–0.92, Se = 0.86, and Sp = 0.84), PLR (AUC = 0.83, 95% CI 0.78–0.88, Se = 0.73, and Sp = 0.81) or LMR (AUC = 0.84, 95% CI 0.80–0.89, Se = 0.68, and Sp = 0.90) all exhibited improved diagnostic accuracy of CRC (Fig. 3B, Table S2). Moreover, when we combined all four markers, the highest diagnostic performance (AUC = 0.92, 95% CI 0.90–0.95, Se = 0.90, and Sp = 0.87) occurred (Fig. 3B, Table S2). Taken together, these results clearly suggested that mSEPT9, NLR, PLR and LMR may become promising biomarkers for CRC, and simultaneous detection of all four markers provided an excellent diagnostic accuracy in discriminating patients with CRC from non-CRC subjects, with high sensitivity and specificity.

Diagnostic value of the combination of mSEPT9 with NLR PLR and LMR in CRC categorized by different pathological characteristics

Finally, we analyzed the diagnostic value of the combination of mSEPT9 with NLR, PLR and LMR in CRC categorized by age, tumor size, tumor stage and M stage. The ROC curve analysis showed that the AUC, Se and Sp value of the combination of the four markers were similar in patients aged < 65 years old (AUC = 0.92, Se = 0.83, and Sp = 0.94) or ≧ 65 years old (AUC = 0.92, Se = 0.84, and Sp = 0.94), and the similar diagnostic accuracy of the combination of the four markers also occurred between patients at early stage (AUC = 0.92, Se = 0.83, and Sp = 0.92) and advance stage (AUC = 0.93, Se = 0.87, and Sp = 0.93) (Table S3). With regard to tumor size, the combination of mSEPT9 with NLR, PLR and LMR had a better diagnostic performance in tumor size ≧ 5 cm group (AUC = 0.95, Se = 0.90, and Sp = 0.98) compared with tumor size < 5 cm (AUC = 0.90, Se = 0.83, and Sp = 0.90) (Fig. 4A, Table S3). In addition, compared with patients without distal metastasis (AUC = 0.92, Se = 0.83, and Sp = 0.93), the combination of the four markers also had a relatively higher diagnostic accuracy in patients with distal metastasis (AUC = 0.95, Se = 0.87, and Sp = 1) (Fig. 4B, Table S3). Collectively, we found that the combination of mSEPT9 with NLR, PLR and LMR had a better diagnostic performance in CRC patients with advanced pathological status.

Discussion

CRC is one of the leading causes of cancer-related death globally, and the 5-year survival rate of patients with advanced CRC is extremely low [31]. Therefore, it is urgent to carry out early screening of CRC in order to improve the prognosis of CRC patients. In the present study, we found that CRC patients had significantly higher values of the PDR of mSEPT9 (P < 0.0001) and the levels of NLR (P < 0.001), PLR (P < 0.001) and MLR (monocyte-to-lymphocyte ratio) (P < 0.001) than polyps patients and healthy subjects for 329 participants enrolled in this study. In addition, our results demonstrated a relationship between the PDR of mSEPT9 and age, tumor size, tumor stage and M stage. Moreover, we analyzed the diagnostic value of mSEPT9, NLR, PLR and LMR on CRC, and the result showed that patients with CRC were discriminated from non-CRC subjects with a sensitivity of 0.68, specificity of 0.89, and AUC of 0.78 using mSEPT9, which performed similarly with NLR (AUC = 0.78), PLR (AUC = 0.80) and LMR (AUC = 0.77). Then we further demonstrated that the combination of mSEPT9 with NLR, PLR or/and LMR could significantly improve the diagnostic accuracy for CRC. Finally, we found that the combination of these four markers had a better diagnostic performance in advanced pathological status including tumor size and M stage.

Accumulated evidences have demonstrated that plasma mSEPT9 is a sensitive biomarker reflecting the tumor burden of CRC with good accuracy [32, 33]. This study also proved that the PDR of mSEPT9 in CRC patients was a significantly higher value of 67.5% compared with 11.4% in polyps patients and 9.6% in NED subjects. What’s more, we detected the levels of NLR, PLR and MLR in different groups, and the result showed that they all exhibited higher values in CRC group than two non-CRC groups, which was consistent with the previous studies [27, 34, 35]. Taken together, the PDR of mSEPT9 and levels of NLR, PLR and MLR in plasma were positively associated with the tumor burden of CRC.

Exploring the correlation between the PDR of mSEPT9 and clinicopathological features of CRC patients is of great significance for improving the diagnosis and prognosis of CRC patients, however, studies in this field is still lacking. In the present study, our results showed that the PDR of mSEPT9 in cases aged ≥ 65 years old was higher than those aged < 65 years old, which was in accordance with a previous study [33]. Furthermore, our findings demonstrated that the PDR of mSEPT9 increased dramatically in patients with a tumor size ≥ 5 cm, and this conclusion was consistent with the results of Lu et al. and Fu et al [13, 15]. As for the relationship between the status of mSEPT9 and tumor stage, there are different conclusions among several studies, perhaps due to differences in sample size and sources [13–15], and our data indicated that mSEPT9 was positively correlated with TNM stage in Chinese CRC patients. We also proved that mSEPT9 was increased significantly in patients with distal metastasis, which was similar to the conclusion of Sun et al [14]. And it’s worth noting that we found the NLR, PLR and MLR showed higher values in mSEPT9 positive CRC patients, which had never been reported before. In summary, the PDR of mSEPT9 was increased in CRC patients with more advanced clinicopathologic status, which meant that mSEPT9 may promote the proliferation and metastasis of CRC cells.

Conventional methods for the diagnosis of CRC have their own obvious shortcomings, such as poor patient compliance rate in colonoscopy and low diagnostic accuracy in FOBT, FIT and other serum tumor biomarkers [3–7]. Consequently, developing a noninvasive and reliable method for detecting CRC is critical. mSEPT9 is detected with high frequency in CRC patients [13]. And some studies demonstrate that mSEPT9 has certain diagnostic value of CRC, but the diagnostic value of mSEPT9 in these studies varies greatly [15–21]. So further exploration on this field is urgently needed. Inflammation plays an important role in the development of various cancers, including CRC [22]. And many inflammatory biomarkers reflecting the systemic inflammatory state such as NLR, PLR and LMR have been shown as promising prognostic biomarkers in CRC [36, 37]. However, the diagnostic value of the three markers still remained uncertain for CRC [27–30]. In this study, we demonstrated that mSEPT9, NLR, PLR and LMR had moderate diagnostic performance for CRC patients, with AUC of 0.78, 0,78, 0.80 and 0.77 respectively, which was basically consistent with previous reports [27, 28, 38]. Additionally, our results first indicated that the diagnostic performance of mSEPT9 in differentiating CRC patients, especially patients with advanced pathological status from non-CRC individuals was enhanced by the combined detection of these four biomarkers. Conclusively, our research showed the combination of mSEPT9, NLR, PLR and LMR has enormous potential to benefit CRC patients’ prognosis, due to the excellent diagnostic specificity and noninvasiveness.

In general, our study is a prospective study with relatively large sample size and largely complete clinicopathologic information, which lessens the selection bias and ensures the accuracy of the diagnosis. However, there are also several limitations in this study, such as all samples from a single-center and lack of the investigation of the correlation between mSEPT9 and the CRC tumor recurrence/metastasis or the survival rate of CRC patients. Therefore, in order to confirm the clinical significance of mSEPT9, NLR, PLR and LMR in CRC, multicenter studies with complete data on the recurrence/metastasis of tumor and the survival status of CRC patients are needed in our following study.

Conclusion

The present study indicated that the levels of mSEPT9, NLR, PLR and MLR were increased in CRC patients. And the status of mSEPT9 was associated with more advanced clinicopathologic status of CRC patients. What’s more, we also showed that mSEPT9, NLR, PLR and LMR all have certain diagnostic value in CRC diagnosis, and the combination of these four markers could significantly improved the diagnostic accuracy of CRC patients, particularly those with advanced pathological status. All in all, we have opened the door for future applications of mSEPT9 combined with inflammatory markers in the early diagnosis of CRC.
