Editorial Expression of Concern: Comparative study of titrated oral misoprostol solution and vaginal dinoprostone for labor induction at term pregnancy
Authors: Xiu Wang, Aijun Yang, Qingyong Ma, Xuelan Li, Li Qin, Tongqiang He
Published: 2025

Expression of Concern: Arch Gynecol Obstet (2016) 294:495–503 10.1007/s00404-015-4000-y

The Editor-in-Chief is issuing an Editorial Expression of Concern for this article. Concerns have been raised about the P values reported in Tables 2–5. The authors were contacted but have not provided a response. Readers are therefore advised to interpret the results and conclusions with caution.

The authors have not responded to correspondence regarding this Editorial Expression of Concern.
