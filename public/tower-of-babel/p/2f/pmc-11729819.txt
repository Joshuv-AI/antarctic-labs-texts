Retraction: Medulloblastoma malignant biological behaviors are associated with HOTAIR/miR-483-3p/CDK4 axis
Authors: Long Zhao, Tao Chen, Xiaoping Tang, Shun Li, Ruofei Liang, Yuanchuan Wang
Published: 2024

The paper “Medulloblastoma malignant biological behaviors are associated with HOTAIR/miR-483-3p/CDK4 axis” (doi: 10.21037/atm-20-5006) published in Annals of Translational Medicine in Vol 8, No 14 (July 23, 2020) (1) has been retracted at the request of the authors. Upon further review and consideration, we have identified significant errors and inaccuracies in the paper. These issues pertain to the accuracy of facts presented and the thoroughness of analysis, which compromise the credibility and quality of the article. Therefore, all the authors request to retract this paper. All authors agree with the retraction of the paper. We are sorry for the inconvenience caused.
