Retraction Note: Association between low density lipoprotein cholesterol levels and prostate cancer risk in non-hypertensive middle-aged and older American men
Authors: Zhen Zhou, Zhicong Huang, Yang Zhao, Yong Wang, Yuanjie Niu
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-80190-y, published online 24 November 2024.

The Editors have retracted this Article.

After publication, concerns were raised that the conclusions presented in the Article that LDL-C levels influence the risk of prostate cancer are not supported by the data. Due to the study design of NHANES, the data does not allow to establish such causal relationships. The Authors responded to the concerns, but their reply was unable to resolve the concerns. The Editors have therefore lost confidence in the results presented in this Article.

The Authors did not respond to the Editor’s correspondence about this retraction.
