Research on the health lighting scheme of university library reading room
Authors: Meng Fanpu, Wang Shou yi, Feng Hua
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
The quality of the indoor lighting environment in the reading space of the university library is essential for students' visual perception, emotional evaluation, and cognitive efficiency. The design of the lighting environment considering the change of natural light is one of the important development directions to improve the light environment quality of the reading room and meet the health requirements of students. This paper from the perspective of reading vision, emotion, and cognitive probes into the natural light environment lighting design, builds a reading lighting environment laboratory, using subjective evaluation, task performance, and physiological indicators of the measurement method, first analyses the weight of different indicators under different time, establish lighting environment comprehensive evaluation index I and artificial illumination and desktop illumination prediction model. The experimental results show that experimental results show that visual discomfort and clarity are the key factors in the early and later stages. In the transition phase, emotional indicators become particularly important. The model of dynamic lighting design scheme during 17:30–20:30 is: y = 227753.1746–900120.63492x+ 1179428.57142x2-512000x3, R2 = 0.99702; the fitting formula of desktop illumination in 17:30–20:30 period is: y = 566.666667–197.08995x + 158.73016x2-37.03704x3, R2 = 0.95238. The results aim to provide a theoretical basis for the design and optimization of dynamic lighting under natural light in the library reading room.

Introduction

The development of electronic technology has promoted the change of reading mode. The 20th National Reading Survey Report shows that the contact rate of digital reading mode and comprehensive reading mode has reached 80.1 % and 81.8 % respectively, and it is on the rise. Electronic screens and paper-mixed reading have been the most common modes of library reading. Electronic screen reading is achieved by self-illumination, while paper text reading is achieved by light reflection, and the two work differently. In mixed reading, the line of sight needs to move frequently between the electronic screen and the paper document, and the third type of sensory cells puts higher requirements for the light environment.

Light will affect students 'physiological and psychological health. An improper light environment will not only affect visual efficacy, cause visual discomfort, and produce visual fatigue and other visual problems through visual effects, but also affect circadian rhythm, alertness, cognitive behavior, and emotion of the human body through non-visual effects. Non-visual effects vary with light intensity, light spectrum, duration of light, time of day, etc. [1]. Students work more efficiently at high color temperatures than at low color temperatures, but students prefer light environments with low color temperatures [2]. Cajochen et al. [3] found that full-spectrum LED lighting had a positive effect on mood, visual comfort, daytime alertness, and sleep quality in healthy subjects compared to ordinary LED lighting, and felt happier in the morning and evening. Vitasek et al. [4] found that it is feasible to imitate the sunny days from sunrise to sunset in summer to improve people's mood. Shi Lu et al. [5] concluded that high-intensity lighting environment stimuli can enhance alertness. Yan Yonghong [6] experimentally studied the effects of a T5 fluorescent lamp on subjects 'learning efficiency, visual fatigue, and brain fatigue under different color temperatures and illumination levels. Huang Haijing found that 4 000 K fluorescent lamp is the most comfortable light environment for visual work by establishing a regression model of pupil size and recognition time of human eyes [7]. Zhang Jingyi [8] obtained through subjective evaluation experiments that when the vertical illumination of the center of the screen is 163∼300lx, the screen reading comfort is the best. Hu Zhigang [9] found that VDT background saturation affects visual performance. From the above, subjective evaluation, performance measurement, and physiological measurement are the most common methods to judge the influence of light environment on students 'physiology and psychology.

At present, the research on library lighting environments at home and abroad has found that different lighting environments have a significant impact on the comfort of reading and learning efficiency.Zhang Jingyi [10] obtained the order of importance of different influencing factors on reading comfort under general lighting conditions of a library through experiments, and the order is as follows: VDT background brightness > desktop reflection coefficient > light color temperature > desktop illumination Guo Qi [11] established a prediction model related to subjective evaluation of visual satisfaction and illumination parameters through investigation and obtained that the illumination uniformity of inclined plane has a great influence on visual satisfaction. Feng Zilong [12] proposed a multi-objective optimization method for optimizing the model by taking the library electronic reading room as a typical use space, VDT reading comfort as an objective, illumination, correlated color temperature, reflection coefficient, and VDT screen brightness as decision variables. Most of the existing studies focus on night lighting and do not consider the effects of artificial lighting during the day, which is now the most common problem. Li Tiantian [13] discovered the influence law of VDT mixed with paper reading on visual comfort and learning efficiency in a lighting environment. Compared with paper reading, mixed reading has more physiological and psychological factors and higher requirements for a light environment.

The study found that dynamic lighting is more conducive to students' cognitive and emotional performance than static lighting environments. Sun Yuqing [14] found that dynamic lighting conforming to the natural light cycle can reduce anger and depression. Wang Yiran [15] found that compared with the Low-High dynamic lighting mode intervention, the experimenters had significantly longer deep sleep duration, simpler sleep degrees, and fewer negative emotions. Li Yanjie [16] carried out dynamic light intervention on people with different mental states at different periods and studied the changing rules of individual alertness, cognitive performance, and emotion. However, most of the above studies are aimed at artificial lighting, which is a single factor, and rarely consider the impact of natural light and artificial lighting on the experimenter.

According to the [17] Building Lighting Design Standard (GB50033-2013), the standard value of indoor natural lighting under the side lighting of the general reading room of the library is 450 lx, and according to the Architectural Lighting Design Standard (GB50034-2013) [18], the lighting standard value of the general reading room of the library is 300 lx. In addition, through the preliminary investigation, students in artificial light environments and natural light environments have significant effects on visual perception, emotional evaluation, and cognitive efficiency. It is found that the best threshold of illumination value in a natural light environment is 519.47–1420.15lx, and the best threshold of illumination value in an artificial light environment is 401.89–603.34lx. The illuminance value of a natural light environment is higher than that of an artificial light environment, but the optimal illumination of two light environments including the natural light environment and artificial light environment cannot be determined at present.

Therefore, the combination of subjective evaluation, performance measurement, and physiological measurement is adopted in the southbound reading room of the Hebei University of Engineering, carrying out experiments on mixed reading visual perception, emotion perception, and cognitive evaluation. After being combined with field research and network survey, To determine the artificial illumination from the optimal natural illumination to no natural light environment, the experimental period selected the summer of 17:30–20:30, a total of 3 h in six time periods. To explore the best artificial illumination supplement under the comprehensive evaluation of vision, emotion, and cognition in different periods, and establish the best desktop illumination model in the 17:30–20:30 period, providing the basis for reading the design of the artificial lighting environment in the natural light environment.

Research methods

Analysis methods

Principal component analysis

According to the principal components, calculate the comprehensive score [19], obtain the comprehensive score of light environment evaluation value, negative emotion, positive emotion, and visual discomfort index, and compare and analyze the difference according to the score.

Significant analysis

The Chi-square test is used to judge the significance of the difference between the highest score and the second-highest score. If there is a significant difference, the highest score illumination value is selected, and if there is no significant difference, the low illumination value between the two is selected.

The index weight calculation entropy method is to calculate the weight of each index according to the information entropy reflected by the data, that is, the influence degree of each index. It is an objective weighting method, which can reduce the deviation caused by artificial reasons compared with the subjective weighting method [20]. The smaller the entropy value of an index, the higher the entropy weight, that is, the higher the influence degree of the index. The original data obtained have different dimensions and positive and negative orientations. To evaluate them by entropy method, the data should be standardized first.

When xij is a positive indicator, the formula is:(1)yij=xij−minxijmaxxij−minxijWhen x ij is a negative indicator, the formula is:(2)yij=maxxij−xijmaxxij−minxij

Formula: Yij for the standardized value of indicators, xij for some indicators of the original data.

The greater the standardized index value, the better the evaluation, which is a positive index, the smaller the value, the better the evaluation, which is a reverse index. Light environment quality, visual clarity, positive emotion, visual efficacy, and RMSSD change rate are positive indexes, and visual discomfort, negative emotion, and LF/HF change rate are negative indexes. Secondly, to avoid the uncomputable situation in the data processing, we must eliminate the zero and negative values in the data, so we need to translate the standardized data, the formula is:(3)yij=y+α

At the same time, to maximize the retention of raw data, α The value of must be as small as possible. in this article, α = 0.0001 is taken during the time periods of 17:30–18:00, 18:00–18:30, 18:30–19:00, 19:30–20:00, and 20:00–20:30, α = 0.0101 is taken between 19:00 and 19:30. The formula for the coefficient of variation for item j is:(7)gj=1−ej

The bigger the gj, the more important the indicator.

The entropy weight formula is:(8)wj=gj∑i=1mgj

Multi-index comprehensive evaluation method refers to the theory and method of multi-index comprehensive index to synthesize the selected several indicators into an index, which is expressed by I value. Through the establishment of the evaluation system, the index of forward and infinite processing and evaluation index calculation, using the infinite processing index data set and the index corresponding weight, using the method of weighted average, the multiple indexes after infinite results, integrated into a comprehensive evaluation index. Methods

Location and time

This experiment builds the visual scene and lighting environment in the south reading room of the Library of Hebei University of Engineering. The experiment will be from mid-June 2023 to mid-August 2023. The library of Hebei University of Engineering is located in Handan City, Hebei Province, with longitude: 114.603217, latitude: 36.651677. According to the building Lighting Design Standard GB50033-2013 [17], it belongs to the optical climate zone and the temperate continental monsoon climate, with four distinct seasons. The southbound reading room is relatively stable from June to August, 17:30–18:00 desktop illumination of 500–600 lx, desktop illumination at 18:30–18:30 is 400–500 lx, Desktop illumination at 18:30–19:00 is 300–400 lx, 19:00–19:30 desktop illumination with 200–300 lx, 19:30–20:00 desktop illumination of 100–200 lx, Desktop illumination at 20:00–20:30 is 0–100 lx, Meet the experimental requirements of optimal natural light environment to no natural light environment. In spring, autumn and winter festivals, the illumination of natural light environment changes rapidly, and the illumination of natural light environment in winter does not meet the demand of the best natural light environment, so the experiment is finally determined to be conducted in summer. In addition, considering the sunset time from June to August, and the change of the illumination in the survey with time, 17:30–20:30 is selected as the experiment time, and half an hour is taken as a time period, which is divided into 6 time periods.

The window sill height is 0.9 m and the window height is 2.1 m. The wall surface is a large white powder brush with a reflectance of 0.75, the ground surface is white gray-black terrazzo with a reflectance of 0.52, and the desktop material is a wooden veneer desktop with a reflectance of 0.7. The position and orientation of students 'seats are the most selected positions during the survey, i.e. 1.5m away from the window and facing west. Lighting setting

To realize the regulation of lighting parameters, an Opal T5 lamp tube is selected as a lighting fixture, and multiple lamps jointly adjust the illumination of the artificial light environment. In addition, students 'reading habits and position selection habits are comprehensively considered, and an experimental space for reading lighting environment research is established. See Fig. 2 for the elevation of the experimental scene plan.Fig. 2Experimental scene plan and elevation drawing.Fig. 2

Experimental scene plan and elevation drawing.

Reduce the influence of glare of the lighting lamps. In the experiment, the lamps are arranged outside the visual boundary of the experimenter, and the height of the lamps ensures that the light environment of the working face of the experimenter is uniform.

Considering the change of natural illumination with time, artificial illumination is selected as 0lx, 100lx, 200lx, 300lx, 400lx, and 500lx, and the difference is 100lx. By using a factorial experiment, combined with the 6 periods selected above, there are 6 experimental conditions in each period, and a total of 36 experimental conditions are obtained.

The experimenters selected 30 students to participate in the experiment, including 15 boys and 15 girls, aged between 18 and 25, and asked the experimenters to have normal vision, or normal corrected vision, no eye diseases and physical and mental health

Experimental materials

In the preliminary investigation, the correlation between the natural light environment, the artificial light environment evaluation index and the illuminance was analyzed by Spearman. According to the correlation analysis, there is no correlation between the illumination degree and the light-color satisfaction degree and color authenticity in the evaluation indexes of natural light environment and artificial light environment. The illumination value of natural light environment was inversely correlated with overall satisfaction, non-glare evaluation, brightness satisfaction, visual comfort, visual fatigue, visual clarity, cognitive speed and cognitive accuracy, and positively correlated with activity, pleasure, calm and relaxation. The illumination value of artificial light environment was inversely correlated with the non-glare evaluation, and was positively correlated with overall satisfaction, brightness satisfaction, visual comfort, visual fatigue, visual clarity, visual clarity, cognitive speed, cognitive accuracy, activity, pleasure, calmness, and relaxation.

In order to further judge the influence of lighting environment on human visual perception, emotion and cognition, physiological and psychological methods are chosen to evaluate them.The experiment set up the tasks of Anfimov letter recognition table, number proofreading table, and Randall ring checklist to measure the mental work index of the experimenters under different light environments [13]. In the calculation process, calculate the mental work index of each scale respectively, see the formula below, take the average value as the result of the mental work index of the experimenter, and then calculate the rate of change of the mental work index of the experimenter before and after illumination, see the formula below.

The Positive and Negative Emotion Scale (PANAS) has 20 adjectives, which are used to describe emotion, representing 10 negative emotion factors and 10 positive emotion factors respectively.

A visual discomfort scale is used to measure visual comfort, and eye fatigue and dry eye are evaluated.

The evaluation of the light environment is improved from the widely used office lighting survey scale combined with the characteristics of the library reading room. The classical physical parameters of the light environment such as light intensity, brightness, and glare are evaluated. The investigation of light intensity is divided into horizontal light intensity and vertical light intensity. The investigation of glare is divided into desktop glare and electronic screen glare.

The subjective alertness scale is used to assess alertness.

Heart rate variability (HRV) is recorded with a heart rate band, and RMSSD (root mean square of successive differences) and LF/HF (frequency domain) are selected as evaluation indexes. RMSSD is the square root of the mean square of the difference between adjacent heartbeat intervals, and LF/HF is the ratio of low-frequency band power to high-frequency band power. RMSSD and LF/HF can reflect the balance between the sympathetic nervous system and the parasympathetic nervous system. The lower the LF/HF and the higher the RMSSD, the more active the parasympathetic nervous system and the higher the cognitive efficiency [21], two physiological parameters are used to evaluate cognitive efficiency.

Experimental process

The experiment consists of the following two stages.1)Preparation and testing stage: before the experiment, make sure that the experimenter has a clear understanding of the experimental process and points for attention, and wear the physiological index measuring instrument correctly. The physiological index and mental work index of the natural light environment are taken as baseline.2)Experiment stage: adjust the illumination environment parameter, and make the experiment environment reach the parameter value of the corresponding working condition. According to the instructions, the experimenters carried out subjective alertness assessment discomfort assessment-light environment assessment negative emotion scale assessment work index test-heart rate variability test turn, the whole time is about 30 min. Adjust lighting parameters, and repeat the above steps, each group of experiments is set to adapt to the light environment time and rest time to avoid visual fatigue on the experimental results. Discussed

Comparative analysis of the results of the lighting scheme

In this paper, through the field lighting experiment in the 17:30–20:30 period of the library reading room, founding the best artificial lighting illumination in that period was increased, while the desktop illumination showed steady state in the early stage and decreased in the later period. The research results are in line with the dynamic lighting of Sun Yuqing [14] in the solar cycle in the laboratory is more conducive to improving the health level of students. Hansen Ellen Kathrine [22] Research has found that the dynamic light cycle has a positive impact on visual comfort, perceived atmosphere, and work engagement. Meanwhile, in the study of Ru Taotao et al. [23], we found that the daily dynamic lighting mode was more conducive to the office workers' health and night sleep during the day compared with the static lighting mode. Through the research and design of the dynamic lighting design scheme in this paper, it can not only improve the health level of students, but also achieve the effect of energy saving, helping to improve the lighting environment of the future library reading room.

Determination of the dynamic illumination model

Guan Yang [24] is according to the classroom class time 8:30–17:30 all day, lighting experiment, mainly analyzed whether there are differences in the demand for lighting environment in different periods. Zhang, Rongpeng [25] found that the effect of dynamic lighting on the night produced a significant decrease in sleep quality and sleep duration. So at night should try not to use dynamic lighting, but the use of the library time includes the day and night and night, in the above study, we can learn that the different time during the day, try not to use dynamic lighting at night, in the process of the day to night, natural light environment change is bigger, in this period we should keep static artificial lighting or dynamic artificial lighting more in line with the needs of students, we need to be explored. Therefore, this paper designs the illumination light environment experiment to explore whether there is a difference in the best artificial illumination from the natural light environment to the period of artificial light environment.

Analysis of the index weight

Previous studies on the optical environment evaluation of natural light and artificial light have found that different indicators have different influence weights in the evaluation systems. Li Xinxin [26] proposed the influencing factors of the natural light environment performance of indoor shopping, and determined the effective lighting range and light distribution level of the key influencing factors. Zhang Jingyi [8] found that in the lighting environment of reading room, the background brightness of VDT between laptop and paper, under the coupling reading of mobile phone and paper, the importance of desktop reflection coefficient is higher compared with other indicators. F. Leccese [27] evaluated the classroom lighting quality using a hierarchical analysis, assigning weights to each standard with different effects on lighting quality. The research focus in natural light environment is obviously different from that in artificial light environment. The illumination of natural light environment gradually decreases during the period of 17:30–20:30, and in the period of 17:30–20:30, whether there is a difference in the weight of the evaluation index, or if there is a difference, how it changes. In the course of this paper, the horizontal illumination of natural light environment changed greatly, so the study period was divided into 6 periods to determine the weight of indicators in different periods and further determine the research focus.

Limitations and future challenges

In this paper, only the general reading behavior is considered for experimental research, but not the special needs of students. The personalized lighting design can be considered in the future.

Conclusion

This study analyzes subjective evaluation, performance measurement, and physiological indicator detection data to determine the impact of different supplementary lighting intensities on mixed reading visual discomfort, visual efficacy, emotional evaluation, emotional arousal, and other indicators under different natural light environments. It also analyzes the optimal supplementary lighting intensity for a single indicator under different natural light environments. And use the entropy method to determine the weights of different evaluation indicators, establish a comprehensive evaluation index I for the lighting environment, compare and analyze the optimal illuminance at different time periods, and determine the dynamic lighting design scheme during the 17:30–20:30 period, providing ideas for the lighting environment design of mixed reading during the 17:30–20:30 period. The specific conclusions are as follows:

The optimal supplementary lighting intensity based on different evaluation indicators varies at different time periods, and the variation trend of supplementary lighting intensity varies with time. The quality of the light environment, the rate of change in mental work index, visual clarity, and subjective alertness mostly show a steady state during the time period after sunset. The optimal supplementary illumination varies, with 500lx and 400lx, respectively. The optimal supplementary illumination in other time periods increases with time, but the trend of change is different. The visual clarity score, LF/HF change rate, subjective alertness, and visual discomfort mostly show a steady state during the time period before sunset, with the optimal supplementary illumination of 100 and 0lx, respectively. The optimal supplementary illumination of other time periods shows an increasing trend over time, with different changing trends. Positive and negative emotions, as well as the change rate of RMSSD, showed an increasing trend in the optimal supplementary lighting intensity throughout the entire period.

Different indicators have different weights at different time periods, and when designing lighting, priority should be given to indicators with higher weights. Priority should be given to visual discomfort during the 17:30–18:00, 18:00–18:30, and 18:30–19:00 periods, alertness during the 19:00–19:30 periods, and visual clarity during the 19:30–20:00, 20:00–20:30 periods.

The variation pattern of illuminance and comprehensive evaluation index I varies at different time periods, and the optimal illuminance varies at different time periods.The optimal illumination during the 17: 30–20:30 period shows an upward trend over time.
