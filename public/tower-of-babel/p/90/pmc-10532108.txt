P03 Long term denosumab treatment in osteoporosis - a case series
Authors: Nimanthi Premathilake, Anupama Nandagudi
Published: 2023

ABSTRACT

They have not experienced significant side effects related to treatment. The second patient was treated as possible fracture as the x-rays were not convincing of definitive fracture. Patient 3, despite having very low T scores and multiple fragility fractures in 2012, has not incurred further fractures following the commencement of denosumab.Key learning pointsDenosumab has shown satisfactory improvement in these patients without significant adverse effects, following completion of 10 years of therapy. This is in par with previous studies that demonstrated efficacy and safety profile of Denosumab.
