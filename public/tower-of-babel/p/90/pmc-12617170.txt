OA20230 A Virtual Reality tool with Real-World Scenarios to Support Decision-Making and Enhance Hazard Perception in Street Environments (Placeholder)
Authors: K Vagionaki, P Valero-Mora, R Skeva, S Lioliou, V Kontogiannis, J Chliaoutakis, M Papadakaki
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundRoad traffic incidents remain one of the leading causes of death among adolescents. This vulnerability occurs from developmental characteristics, heightened susceptibility to peer influence, and a tendency to underestimate risks. To address these challenges, the SAFETeen project is developing innovative digital tools, including a Virtual Reality (VR) tool, to promote safer commuting behaviors.MethodsThe VR tool places adolescents in immersive, realistic scenarios that replicate everyday commuting challenges providing an interactive and engaging learning experience. To ensure scenarios flow authenticity and relevance, decision-making trees were developed based on mixed-methods approach. A study was carried out with 8 secondary schools across Cyprus, Finland, Greece, and Italy, in 2024. Data were collected through a series of 16 focus group discussions with 8-12 students, teachers, and parents and an online survey with a minimum of 100 secondary students per country. Research aimed at identifying the most challenging commuting situations adolescents face, both as pedestrians and vehicle occupants. Key risk areas were distractions from mobile phone use and headphones, or facing peer pressure to engage in risky behaviors such as ignoring helmets. Based on the results and supplementary scientific evidence, a virtual reality (VR) tool was developed.ResultsPilot trials will be conducted in secondary schools across Greece, Finland, Italy, and Cyprus. User feedback will be collected to assess engagement, usability, and perceived learning impact. The VR tool is expected to outperform traditional classroom methods by enhancing students’ hazard perception, and fostering safer decision-making in real-life commuting situations.ConclusionsThe VR tool aims to strengthen critical safe commuting capacities among adolescents and bridge gaps in current road safety education by creating a more interactive and engaging learning experience.Key message• VR scenarios would provide adolescents with a safe and engaging way to practice real-world commuting decisions, helping them improve hazard perception and make safer choices.Topichazard perception, digital tools, safe commuting
