Correction for ‘Comparison of elective hemicolectomy patients before and after establishment of a preoperative anaesthesia assessment clinic: a retrospective, observational study in a hospital in Norway’
Published: 2026

Kristoffersen EW, Fossum M, Tveit TO, et al Comparison of elective hemicolectomy patients before and after establishment of a preoperative anaesthesia assessment clinic: a retrospective, observational study in a hospital in Norway BMJ Open 2025;15:e100922. doi: 10.1136/bmjopen-2025-100922

This article has been corrected since it was published online.

At the end of the Discussion section, the text currently reads:

"Refuting this possibility, another study found no significant difference in the median number of days of hospital stay for patients undergoing open right hemicolectomy within an ERAS setting (4 days (3–28) compared with that in the conventional laparoscopic group (5 days (2–18); p=0.146).37"

This text reflects an incorrect interpretation of reference 37 and should be replaced with:

"However, a study of patients undergoing right hemicolectomy within an ERAS setting found that the median length of stay was slightly shorter in the open surgery group (4 days) compared with the laparoscopic group (5 days), and this difference was statistically significant. When readmissions were included, no significant difference in total length of stay was observed between the groups (6 vs 7 days).37 As the study was conducted in 2010, advances in surgical techniques and perioperative care may limit the applicability of these findings to current practice.”
