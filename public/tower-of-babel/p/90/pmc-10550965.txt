Author Correction: Stress-resistant corals may not acclimatize to ocean warming but maintain heat tolerance under cooler temperatures
Authors: Verena Schoepf, Steven A Carrion, Svenja M Pfeifer, Melissa Naugle, Laurence Dugal, Jennifer Bruyn, Malcolm T McCulloch
Published: 2023

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-019-12065-0, published online 17 September 2019

The original version of this Article contained errors in the Results section, in Figs. 4 and 6 and in the Source Data. Due to an error in the equation used to calculate the photosynthesis to respiration ratios (P/R), the P/R ratios were higher than they should have been. The relative differences between treatment groups and, thus, the outcomes of the statistical tests were not affected. The affected sentences in the Results incorrectly read “45% lower photosynthesis to respiration” and “−29 to −40%”, “up to −76% vs. up to −50%”. The correct versions state “35% lower photosynthesis to respiration”, “−23 to −30%”, “up to −62% vs. up to −36%”.
