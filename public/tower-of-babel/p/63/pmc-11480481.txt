Author Correction: A method for miRNA-disease association prediction using machine learning decoding of multi-layer heterogeneous graph Transformer encoded representations
Authors: SiJian Wen, YinBo Liu, Guang Yang, WenXi Chen, HaiTao Wu, XiaoLei Zhu, YongMei Wang
Published: 2024

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-68897-4, published online 03 September 2024

In the original version of this Article the title of the paper contained errors.

“A method for miRNA diffusion association prediction using machine learning decoding of multi-level heterogeneous graph Transformer encoded representations”

now reads:

“A method for miRNA-disease association prediction using machine learning decoding of multi-layer heterogeneous graph Transformer encoded representations”

The original Article and accompanying Supplementary Information file have been corrected.
