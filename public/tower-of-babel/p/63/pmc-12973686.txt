Retraction Note: Internal communication from a happiness management perspective: state-of-the-art and theoretical construction of a guide for its development
Authors: Luis M Romero-Rodríguez, Bárbara Castillo-Abdul
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: BMC Psychology 12, 644 (2024)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s40359-024-02140-7

The Editors have retracted this article after concerns were raised about its validity. A post-publication review identified serious flaws in the research methodology. It appears that the authors did not adequately follow the PRISMA guidelines in conducting the systematic review, despite presenting the work as a meta-analysis. In addition, the review relied on a single database (Scopus), which is insufficient for a comprehensive systematic review. The authors failed to provide satisfactory explanations in response to these concerns. Consequently, the Editors no longer have confidence in the results and conclusions presented in this article.

Authors Luis M. Romero-Rodríguez & Bárbara Castillo-Abdul disagree with this retraction.
