RETRACTION: Effect of two different modalities of hysterectomy on wound infection and wound dehiscence in obese patients
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Abstract Retraction: Long L., He X., Liu Y., Lei C., “Effect of Two Different Modalities of Hysterectomy on Wound Infection and Wound Dehiscence in Obese Patients,” International Wound Journal 21, no. 3 (2023): e14664, 10.1111/iwj.14664. The above article, published online on 04 March 2024, in Wiley Online Library (http://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/), has been retracted by agreement between the journal Editor in Chief, Professor Keith Harding; and John Wiley & Sons, Ltd. It came to the publisher's attention from a third party that a number of articles shared concerning similarities in format and structure. Following an investigation by the publisher, the retraction has been agreed on as the peer review and publishing process for this article were found to be manipulated.
