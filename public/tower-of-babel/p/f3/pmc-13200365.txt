Retraction Note: Effect of flipped classroom method on the reflection ability in nursing students in the professional ethics course; Solomon four-group design
Authors: Mohammad Rasool Khazaei, Eshagh Moradi, Azizeh Barry, Mohammad Hasan Keshavarzi, Akram Hashemi, Ghobad Ramezani, Atefeh Zabihi Zazoli, Farzaneh Farzadnia
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: BMC Medical Education 25, 56 (2025)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-024-06556-y

The Editor has retracted this article. After publication, concerns were raised regarding the statistical analysis, including the lack of clarity with regards to the randomization process, inconsistent t-test statistics and p-values (Table 4, and 5), miscalculated mean squares and p-values (Tables 7, 8 and 9) as well as incorrect mean differences and confidence intervals (Table 10). Furthermore, discrepancies were identified between in-text citations and the reference list. The authors provided some data on request from the Publisher, but this was insufficient to address the concerns raised. As a result, the scientific integrity of the article cannot be guaranteed.

Ghobad Ramezani, Atefeh Zabihi Zazoli did not explicitly state whether or not they agree with this retraction. Mohammad Rasool Khazaei, Eshagh Moradi, Azizeh Barry, Mohammad Hasan Keshavarzi, Akram Hashemi, and Farzaneh Farzadnia did not respond to correspondence from the Publisher about this retraction.
