Retraction Note: Multimodal deep learning for cephalometric landmark detection and treatment prediction
Authors: Fei Gao, Yulong Tang
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-06229-w, published online 12 July 2025

The Editors have retracted the Article.

After publication, the concerns were raised about:

• the references that are either irrelevant to the context in which they are cited, or that cannot be confirmed to exist;

• the inconsistencies in the reporting of the datasets used in the paper.

Further investigation by the Editors uncovered irregularities in the methods description and application, issues with the reporting of the results, and issues with the validity of methodology. The Editors have therefore lost confidence in the data, results, and conclusions of this Article.

Fei Gao did not explicitly state whether or not they agree with this retraction. Yulong Tang did not respond to the correspondence from the Editors about the retraction.
