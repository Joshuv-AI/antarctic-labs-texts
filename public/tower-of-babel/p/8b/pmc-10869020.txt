Resident’s Bag
Authors: Anisha Biswal, Bhabani S. T. P. Singh, Nibedita Dixit, Liza Mohapatra, Manoj Kumar Nayak
Published: 2023

A resident’s bag is one of the highly valued possessions of a junior resident. It should contain all the essential items required in the day-to-day life of a resident doctor. It should be a small, handy, compact bag with multiple compartments adequate in size to accommodate all the materials. The bag should have a sling with proper padding for hanging so as to decrease the pain on the resident’s shoulders. In this article, we have attempted to list out all the important items that should be present in a resident’s bag, along with their uses.

Dermoscope: It is a non-invasive, easy-to-handle bedside diagnostic instrument. It is commonly used to differentiate melanocytic lesions from dysplastic lesions and overt malignancies. Recently, the use of a dermoscope has become more evident in inflammatory dermatoses, particularly pigmented and vascular conditions. Non-diagnostic uses like monitoring response to therapy and deciding the site of a biopsy also require the help of a dermoscope. It should be a handheld dermoscope with both polarized and non-polarized modes providing 10x magnification. The commonly used handheld dermoscopes are provided by manufacturers like Dermlite®, Heine®, Illuco® and Luxamed®. The latest Dermlite DL5 dermoscope comes with added cross-polarised and parallel-polarised mode settings, giving a graded polarised mode view. It also comes with an additional UV mode that substitutes for wood lamp examination, making it a complete package.

A clinician’s diagnostic expertise is reflected in the examination skills they acquire throughout their years of residency. The contents of the resident’s bag can play a crucial role in helping a resident have a better clinical examination. It makes the resident well prepared, more confident at all times, and helps in better learning. This resident’s bag is also equally useful during the postgraduate exam, as it has all the contents that are required in an exam kit.

In conclusion, resident’s bag with its contents, as mentioned, is an essential component that could be in the armamentarium of a resident for better learning and understanding of dermatology.
