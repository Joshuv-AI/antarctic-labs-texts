Correction to: Endoscopic Deep Plane Facelift: A Classified Approach
Published: 2025

This is a correction to: “Endoscopic Deep Plane Facelift: A Classified Approach,” by Mirza Firat, MD, published in Aesthetic Surgery Journal, Volume 45, Issue 10, October 2025, Pages 989–1001, https://doi.org/10.1093/asj/sjaf110.

The following changes have been made to the originally published manuscript. In order to comply with obtained informed consent, the originally published Video 6 has been removed. Video 7 has been replaced with a new video of a different patient depicting the same technique, and renumbered as Video 6; Video 9 has been replaced with a new video of a different patient depicting the same technique and renumbered as Video 8. References to the videos in the text have been updated accordingly.
