“Lung function trajectories in South African children with pulmonary tuberculosis compared to those with non-TB lower respiratory tract infection: a prospective study.” D.M. Gray, L. Githinji, K. Brittain, et al. Eur Respir J 2024; 63: 2400216.
Published: 2025

This article was originally published with an omission in the support statement. The support statement should have identified that the author Leah Githinji was an EDCTP fellow (TMA2020CDF-3200) supported by funding from the Foundation Botnar, through the EDCTP2 programme.

The complete, corrected support statement is as follows:

Support statement: This work was supported by the Regional Prospective Observational Research in Tuberculosis (RePORT TB) Consortium which is co-funded by the Medical Research Council of South Africa and the Division of AIDS Research of the National Institutes of Health, USA. Additional funding was received from the European and Developing Countries Clinical Trials Partnership (EDCTP), the South African Medical Research Council for the Tuberculosis Collaborating Centre for Child Health and the SA-MRC Unit on Child and Adolescent Health, and the National Institutes of Health, USA (RO1HD058971). H.J. Zar is supported by the SA-MRC. L. Githinji is an EDCTP fellow (TMA2020CDF-3200) supported by funding from the Foundation Botnar, through the EDCTP2 programme. Funding information for this article has been deposited with the Crossref Funder Registry.

The article has been corrected and republished online.
