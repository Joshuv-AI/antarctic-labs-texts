As a result of an error during figure assembly, the FDPS and PCNA samples in Fig. 2B were misplaced above the α-tubulin control. The Authors have provided raw data showing that these samples were analysed at the same time as p-AKT, AKT, p-ERK and ERK, and the correct control for FDPS and PCNA is β-actin. In addition, the Authors provided data to show that the p-STAT3 and STAT3 samples included in Fig. 2B were analysed at the same time as the CB1 and CB2 samples included in another publication (1) and share the same α-tubulin control.

The corrected Fig. 2 and accompanying legend appear below.
