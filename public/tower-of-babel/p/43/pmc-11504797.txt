Needs assessment of military medicine education curriculum in medical students and upcoming challenges
Authors: Abolfazl Khoshi, Mohammad Raeeszadeh, Jamal Akhavan Moghaddam, Hassan Goodarzi
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
ABSTRACTIntroduction:The curriculum of military medicine for medical students of universities of military medical sciences is a priority and a practical goal. Paying attention to the educational needs of students creates vitality and motivation among students for better learning and improving the learning environment. Frequent monitoring and updating of the content of military courses and modern teaching improve the quality and quantity of the military medicine curriculum.Materials and Methods:This applied, inductive, and exploratory descriptive was conducted in a single cross-sectional and qualitative method. The data were collected using the brainstorming method and individual and collective Delphi techniques. The population included the medical students of Baqiyatallah University of Medical Sciences in Tehran, Iran, who completed the course in military medicine. The data were collected by purposive sampling and classified until the saturation limit was reached. EXCEL 2019 and MAXQDA2020 software were used for data analysis.Findings:This research was carried out in collaboration with the military medicine department of the university to identify the needs of students in the military medicine curriculum. First, the findings were collected in 22 axes. In the next step, the needs were prioritized in the four main components of the combat medicine curriculum (1 goal, 2 content, 3 teaching strategy, 4 evaluation), and the results were extracted.Conclusion:Identifying the needs of students; improving the current military medicine curriculum; paying attention to individual differences, interests, and talents of students; and enhancing the motivation and level of satisfaction of students are the study results.

Introduction

The main goal of education is learning, and educational psychologists have considered the definition of learning as one of the most challenging concepts.[1] From the point of view of schools, different schools of psychology have provided different definitions. Some have considered it “the process of creating a relatively stable change in behavioral ability that is the result of experience”.[2] In Gestalt’s view, learning is the rearrangement of concepts and experiences that lead to insight.[3] Paying attention to the constituent components of the definitions of learning in different psychology schools shows that learning at different levels and areas requires special measures appropriate to the same levels and regions.

Learning may occur in cold conditions and based on conditioning, but it cannot be realized and take permanent forms without attention to learning principles.

Needs assessment is one of the most controversial concepts in education and curriculum planning. The challengingness of needs assessment is mainly due to the definition ambiguity of needs, presented with different views on the definition of needs, which is based on logic and supported by experience. Regardless of the difference of opinion regarding the nature and need, researchers have provided various definitions of the term needs assessment.

Generally, needs assessment is “a systematic process for determining goals, identifying the gap between the current situation and goals and the desired situation, and finally determining priorities for action.”

In other words, needs assessment is the process of determining what should be taught. Thus, the central area of needs assessment is determining what should be prepared for university students.

In another definition, needs assessment in education, training, and education is collecting and analyzing information that identifies the needs of individuals, groups, institutions, the local community, and society in general.[4]

It is possible to analyze the term assessment needs to get a more accurate concept. The word “assess” comes from the Latin root “assideeree,” which means “to put aside.”

Professors and facilitators sit learners next to each other and immerse themselves in their lives and points of view to help them achieve educational and educational goals and expected results. According to the above definition, needs assessment identifies critical needs and provides grounds for their realization through educational measures (curriculum) and forecasting.[5]

Paying attention to the needs of learners in the teaching–learning process strengthens their participation in educational activities and plays a role in creating effective communication between students with the teacher and educational content and ultimately sustainable learning (desired behavioral changes). In addition, the collaborative activities of students in the teaching process during teaching activities and after that lead to effective learning. These considerations also improve the cognitive (knowledge) and attitudinal (emotional) fields as essential factors in creating positive changes in students’ thoughts, beliefs, and behaviors in enhancing their skill fields. Titles related to military medicine courses were added to the general medicine course of Baqiyatallah University of Medical Sciences several years ago in line with the mission and organizational missions and according to the announcements of the Ministry of Health. These titles were carried out in the framework of needs assessments and implemented in the form of defined units of military medicine in the three phases of physiopathology, research, and internship. Frequent monitoring and updating of teaching titles and needs assessment based on students’ opinions are critical in the quantitative and qualitative improvement of the plan.

On the other hand, success in teaching and achieving the educational goals of military medicine courses is possible by considering these needs in the education process. This principle should be regarded as critical because learning is under the learner’s control. Students will only want to learn if individual needs are addressed in education. As a result, the education process and its goals are disrupted. Moreover, a lack of attention to the needs of students practically traps educational activities in the vortex of futility based on the principles governing learning.

The meaning of teaching method or training based on students’ needs is a set of educational activities and measures organized in line with students’ needs.

The students’ educational and skill needs are considered, and the ground for their growth is provided using active teaching methods and relying on educational standards in three areas before, during, and after teaching.

Materials and Methods

Qualitative research is a way of gaining insight through the discovery of meanings. This insight does not mean discovering the cause-and-effect relationship, but the result is the knowledge of the “whole.”

Qualitative research involves juxtaposing perceived components to build a whole, and “meaning” is created through this process.

Qualitative research is a process by which the researcher tries to better understand the complexities of mutual relationships between phenomena and collects information about actions and reactions using regular methods. He thinks about its meaning and concept and reaches conclusions and tries to describe, discover, and analyze some human phenomena in natural situations.[9]

As long as all aspects of a phenomenon are known or are not developed, qualitative methods are more suitable for a deeper understanding of such phenomena. This method also helps to build and extend the theories that already exist. More importantly, qualitative research is a suitable method for understanding the properties and dimensions of concepts and the relationships between them in different situations.[10]

This applied, inductive, and exploratory-descriptive study was conducted in a single cross-sectional and qualitative method. The data were collected using brainstorming methods and individual and collective Delphi techniques.

The statistical population of this study includes the medical students of Baqiyatallah University of Medical Sciences located in Tehran who completed the course of military medicine. The data were collected by purposive sampling and classified until the saturation limit was reached. EXCEL 2019 and MAXQDA2020 software were used for data analysis.

Sampling

Qualitative sampling

Purposive sampling is used in qualitative research because the goal is to select people who are a rich source of information, can actively participate in the study, and help the researcher to better understand the research variables.[9] The samples are selected based on inclusion criteria until data saturation is reached. The participants were selected based on their common experience and expertise regarding the research topic. Determining the medical students of Baqiyatullah University of Medical Sciences who have passed the military medicine course in the previous semesters was not possible in the qualitative research of the sample size in advance, but it was determined during the study. The researcher continued sampling until data saturation was reached, and the interview continued until no new data were added to the previous data.[11]

Discussion

The challenges of the military medicine curriculum in the sub-classes indicate weaknesses and inadequacies in the teaching methods, the lack of correct use of teaching texts, and the inappropriateness of the current teaching methods with the interests of students and with the desired educational goals. In addition, the weakness in using active and exemplary teaching methods is one of the other dimensions of this class.

The challenges are the weakness of professors in emphasizing skill issues and one-way communication with students, not engaging students in active learning environments and group and skill discussions, and not giving necessary feedback to students.

Non-use of opportunities by military medicine professors in the field of behavior and skills is one of the neglected factors. For example, it is possible to present concepts in an informal and hidden format, which is one of the needs.

Informing students about the latest scientific achievements of Iran and the world and informal education of differences and learning opportunities in military medicine and trauma can create double motivation in medical students.

Existing challenges and weaknesses are one of the obstacles to achieving the goals and implementing the curriculum in universities.

Conclusion

Brainstorming is a method of generating a large number of creative ideas for further evaluation. Accepting all ideas is one of the rules of brainstorming, and unusual ideas are more welcome. In other words, big ideas can often lead to more practical ideas with more valuable contributions.

On the other hand, the goal is quantity, not quality. Another feature of this method is that evaluative ideas are not allowed to appear, and everyone in the group shares the ideas’ ownership. The integration or modification of previous ideas is emphasized to be encouraged. When brainstorming is used for the first time, its philosophy should be carefully explained.

Education based on the needs of students is a set of educational and training measures based on the principles governing learning and education as the origin of all the activities of military medicine classrooms based on the needs of students in this field.
