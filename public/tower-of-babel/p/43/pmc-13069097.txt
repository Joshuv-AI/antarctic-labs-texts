Author Correction: Advances, challenges and prospects of origami and kirigami optoelectronics
Authors: Donggyun Lee, Su-Bon Kim, Junho Kim, Seunghyup Yoo
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-025-68217-y, published online 16 January 2026

In the version of this article initially published, there was an error in the Box 1 figure, “Origami” section, where “[22] Original perovskite photodetector” appeared originally as “[20] Original hemispherical photodetector,” and an error in the “Origami + Kirigami” section, where “[101, 183] Original perovskite photodetector” appeared originally as “[183, 184] Graphene-based 3D LED display.” In the “Considerations for actuation, mechanical degree of freedom (DOF), and scaling-down” section, in the text now reading “Notable examples of such mechanisms include humidity-responsive strategies that leverage swelling or contraction caused by water absorption133,134, light-driven configurations utilizing photothermal effects135–137,” the ref. citations originally read “133–135” and “136,137,” respectively. The article originally listed as ref. 177 was incorrect and has been replaced by Cho, J. et al. Nanoscale origami for 3D optics. Small 7, 1943–1948 (2011). The changes are made in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
