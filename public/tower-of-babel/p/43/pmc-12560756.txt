In memoriam: professor Philip S.L. Beh (1958–2025)
Authors: Duarte Nuno Vieira
Published: 2025

At the heart of forensic science, where the hidden secrets of the body reveal stories of life and death, Professor Philip Beh emerged as a luminous presence—a scholar who transformed silenced evidence into eloquent narratives. Born in 1958, he dedicated nearly three decades to forensic medicine in Hong Kong, China, serving as an Associate Professor in the Department of Pathology at the University of Hong Kong. His contributions to the journal Forensic Sciences Research and other scientific publications, where he served with excellence as an associate editor and member of editorial boards, opened new windows of understanding for a global community of experts.

He held several leadership positions, including President of the World Police Medical Officers and President of the Hong Kong Forensic Science Society. His chapters in works such as Essential Forensic Medicine and History of Forensic Medicine in Hong Kong bear witness to his knowledge and dedication to the education of future generations.

On every page he wrote, in every class he taught, he left an aura of integrity, precision, and humanity. He was not only a remarkable forensic professional—he was also a beacon of empathy in a field often immersed in human pain.

It was in sharing confidences and challenges that our friendship blossomed. We shared complicit silences and laughter, academic anguish and quiet triumphs. Philip was a friend who listened with the compassionate gaze of one who understands the fragility of the human condition. His voice, calm and thoughtful, lingered in our memories—a gentle sigh reminding us of the beauty of friendship and vocation.

May his legacy endure—in those who learned from his teachings, in the frailties revealed by his studies, and in the lives he helped illuminate. And may the memory of your friendship, marked by generosity and wisdom, continue to enlighten us in the days to come.

Thank you for everything, Philip. May you rest in peace.
