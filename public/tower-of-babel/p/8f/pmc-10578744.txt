Prediction of reported monthly incidence of hepatitis B in Hainan Province of China based on SARIMA-BPNN model
Authors: Kang Fang, Li Cao, Zhenwang Fu, Weixia Li
Published: 2023

ABSTRACT
In recent years, the incidence of hepatitis B has been serious in Hainan Province of China. To construct a statistical model of the monthly incidence of hepatitis B in Hainan Province of China and predict the monthly incidence of hepatitis B in 2022. Simple central moving average method and seasonal index were used to analyze the trend and seasonal effects of monthly incidence of hepatitis B. Based on the time series of reported monthly incidence of hepatitis B in Hainan Province from 2017 to 2020, a multiplicative seasonal model (SARIMA), multiplicative seasonal model combined with error back propagation neural network model (SARIMA-BPNN), and a gray prediction model were constructed to fit the incidence, and the time series of monthly incidence of hepatitis B in 2021 was used to verify the accuracy of models. The lowest and highest monthly incidence of hepatitis B in Hainan Province were in February and August, respectively, and MAPE of SARIMA, SARIMA-BPNN, and gray prediction models were 0.089, 0.087, and 0.316, respectively. The best fitting model is the SARIMA-BPNN model. The predicted monthly incidence of hepatitis B in 2022 showed a downward trend, with the steepest decline in March, which indicates that the prevention and control of hepatitis B in Hainan Province is effective, and the study can provide scientific and reasonable suggestions for the prevention and control of hepatitis B in Hainan.

1. Introduction

Hepatitis B is an acute and chronic infectious disease caused by the hepatitis B virus (HBV) and is one of the most common infectious diseases. According to the World Health Organization (WHO) report, 350 to 400 million people worldwide are infected with chronic hepatitis B. China is a country with a large number of people infected with hepatitis B, and chronic hepatitis B accounts for a large proportion of the population, with more than 93 million people currently infected with the HBV, which poses a great burden to the country. Viral hepatitis is one of the infectious diseases with the highest morbidity rate among the infectious diseases of A and B in China. Hepatitis B is an acute and chronic infectious disease caused by HBV, which is the most prevalent form of viral pneumonia and one of the most common infectious diseases in China. In recent years, the promotion of hepatitis B vaccination and free vaccination policy for children has greatly reduced the incidence of hepatitis B in people under 30 years of age, but Hainan Province has the top 2 hepatitis B incidences in China from 2018 to 2020,[1] which shows that the hepatitis B incidence in Hainan Province is still very serious.

On April 13, 2018, the Member of the CPC Central Committee decided to support the construction of a pilot free trade zone on the whole island of Hainan, and in June 2020, the CPC Central Committee and the State Council of the People Republic of China issued a “general plan for the construction of Hainan free trade port.” Hainan Province is facing an increase in the floating population, which will bring new challenges to the prevention and control of hepatitis B infectious diseases. It means that more people may be infected with hepatitis B, which will increase the disease burden of the society. And appropriate prediction can provide some suggestions and references for the prevention and control of hepatitis B in Hainan Province, so exploring the regular characteristics of the current incidence of hepatitis B and predicting the future incidence of hepatitis B in Hainan Province is of great guiding significance for the prevention and control of hepatitis B.

Disease prediction usually uses historical data to model and predict future disease trends. Gray prediction model is the original research data generation processing for exponential regularity data and then modeling method, the method has the advantages of processing less regularity feature data, not require a larger sample size can be modeled, in the time series data modeling more applications. ARIMA model can be modeled according to the objective laws of time series sample data to predict the future development trend, which is widely used in time series data analysis. BPNN model is a simple and easy-to-understand neural network model, with small computation and strong learning and adaptive properties, which is more used in prediction problems. There are more studies on the characteristics and hepatitis B incidence prediction in China, Wang et al[2,3] applied gray prediction model to study the incidence pattern in various regions of China, Wang[4] and Chen et al[5] applied ARIMA model to research hepatitis B incidence in several regions of China, literature[6] used point regression model to predict the monthly incidence of hepatitis B in China, literature[7] predicted the incidence of hepatitis B based on the dynamic series average development rate to predict hepatitis B incidence, and literature[8,9] et al used combined model to predict hepatitis B incidence, where literature[9] compared the effect of ARIMA model and BPNN model and predicted hepatitis B incidence in China. However, on the one hand, there are fewer studies on the incidence of hepatitis B in Hainan Province, and on the other hand, the application of these hepatitis B prediction models is relatively homogeneous, basically using one or more models to make individual prediction comparisons, which may produce great errors in the prediction results.[10] In this study, a general statistical model and a machine learning model were combined, resulting in a new combined model to predict the incidence of hepatitis B. The results were more accurate compared to a single prediction model.

The gray model and ARIMA model are fitted for the linear part of the time series, and the BPNN model has the advantage of being able to approximate the complex nonlinear information. In this study, we consider fitting both linear and nonlinear information, and introduce a combined SARIMA-BPNN model[11] to fit the monthly incidence of hepatitis B in Hainan Province and compare it with the SARIMA model and GM(1,1) model, respectively, and the best model suitable for hepatitis B incidence in Hainan was selected and used for predictions.

2. Material and methods

2.1. Study area and data

The reported monthly incidence (1/100,000) of hepatitis B in 2017 and 2018 of Hainan Province was obtained from the China Population Health Data Center platform. The number of hepatitis B cases per year and the number of hepatitis B cases per month were obtained from the China Infectious Disease Surveillance System (CIDSS) for the years 2019 to 2021; data on the reported incidence rate per year for the years 2019 to 2020 were collected from the Hainan Statistical Yearbook.

2.2. Statistical methods

2.2.1. Data processing.

Using the annual number of cases and incidence from 2019 to 2020, the population numbers from 2019 to 2020 and the population growth rate of 2020 were calculated. Since the incidence in 2021 was not yet available, the population growth rate in 2020 was used to estimate the population size in 2021, and then the monthly incidence rate was calculated based on the population size and the monthly hepatitis B cases from 2019 to 2021.

2.2.2. Trend and seasonal effect.[6,7]

The time series xt is decomposed into long-term trend (Tt), seasonal variation (St) and stochastic fluctuation (It), and the time series are decomposed using a multiplicative model, xt=Tt×St×It. Firstly, the long-term trend of the series is extracted using the moving average method, and then the trend effect is eliminated using xtTt, which is denoted as yt. Secondly, the total mean y¯ of yt and the monthly mean yj¯ are calculated, and finally obtain the seasonal index Sj=yj¯y¯,j=1,2,3,⋯,12.

2.2.3. SARIMA model.[7]

The multiplicative seasonal model is abbreviated as ARIMA(p,d,q)×(P,D,Q)S, where d denotes the difference order for extracting trend information of the non-stationary series, D and S are the difference orders for extracting seasonal characteristics and the seasonal cycle length, respectively. p and P are both autoregressive orders, and q and Q are both moving average orders. The seasonal model is smoothed by extracting the trend and seasonal characteristics of the series, initially identifying the model type based on the characteristics of the autocorrelation and partial autocorrelation function (PACF), and determining the type and order of the model based on the minimum information criterion. Fit the best SARIMA model for the reported monthly incidence of hepatitis B from 2017 to 2020, the fitted values of the monthly incidence of hepatitis B in 2021 were obtained and the accuracy of the model was verified. Calculations were performed using R 4.2.2 software.

2.2.4. SARIMA-BPNN model.[12,13]

The inputs of BPNN in the SARIMA-BPNN model are the fitted values obtained by the SARIMA model. Let the training set D={(x11,y11),(x12,y12),⋯,(xij,yij)}, where xij is the fitted value of hepatitis B incidence of the jth month in the year i from 2017 to 2020 by using the SARIMA model, and yij is the true value of the incidence in the jth month of the year i. The BPNN model has one neuron in both the input and output layers, xij is the input and yij is the output. Let n neurons exist in the hidden layer, and the input and output values of each neuron are denoted as ah and bh,h=1,2,⋯,n. The correlation function between each parameter is ah=∑h=1nxij⋅w1h, where w1h is the connection weight of the input layer neuron and the hth neuron of the hidden layer. The input ah and the output bh of the hidden layer are connected through the activation function bh=f(ah), which satisfies

Let the input and output vectors of the output layer be c and yij, respectively, and the connection weight of the neuron in the output layer and the hth neuron of the hidden layer is w2h, satisfying c=∑h=1nbh⋅w2h, and the connection function y=f(c) of c and yij satisfies y=c.

The BPNN model is a multilayer feedforward neural network that uses error back propagation method based on gradient descent rule to continuously train the network weights so that the prediction error gradually decreases to less than a threshold value, and finally outputs the network weights. The monthly incidence of hepatitis B in 2021 to 2021 was used as the test set, and the model was continuously adjusted to obtain the optimal number of hidden neurons and learning rate values based on the principle of minimizing the average relative error between the predicted and actual incidence. 3. Results

During 2017 to 2021, the average number of Hepatitis B cases in Hainan Province was 1121 in January, 834 in February, 1259 in March, 1253 in April, 1238 in May, 1205 in June, 1344 in July, 1326 in August, 1182 in September, 1141 in October, 1208 in November, and 1187 in December. The average number of cases was 1239 in 2017, 1272 in 2018, 1275 in 2019, 1076 in 2020, and 1096 in 2021.

3.1. Trend effect and seasonal effect analysis

The long-term trend Tt of the monthly incidence series of hepatitis B xt in Hainan Province was extracted using the 2 × 12 compound moving average method, and the incidence series and the long-term trend graph are shown in Figure 2, which shows a decreasing trend in the incidence of hepatitis B of Hainan Province. xtTt is used to eliminate the trend effect, which is denoted as yt. The total mean y¯ of yt and the monthly mean y¯j are calculated, and the seasonal index Sj=y¯jy¯. The seasonal index chart is shown in Figure 2, which shows that the incidence of hepatitis B in Hainan Province has seasonal effect, and the incidence is low in February and peaked in August.

4. Discussion

With the rapid development of the construction of Hainan Province, the population continues to surge, hepatitis B infectious diseases may also develop gradually, will likely threaten the construction of free trade port public health issues in Hainan Province, will bring new challenges to the public health risk prevention system for hepatitis B infectious diseases in Hainan. Therefore, the study of the incidence of hepatitis B and the prediction of the incidence can promote more accurate prevention and control of hepatitis B, which is important for the adjustment of the policy and the control of hepatitis B infectious disease. The incidence of hepatitis B in Hainan Province in 2020 was 131.77/100,000,[1] and the incidence situation is still relatively severe. However, the analyses in this paper revealed that there is a decreasing trend in the incidence rate, as well as a clear seasonal pattern.[7] From the seasonal index figure, it can be seen that after the seasonal index decreases from January to February, it starts to rise in March and maintains stability from March to June, then starts to rise again in July and August, and starts to decrease in September and maintains stability from September to December, showing that there are obvious seasonal changes in the incidence of hepatitis B in Hainan Province. The average temperature in Hainan Province is lowest in January to February and highest from June to August, which is similar to the changes in the seasonal index plot of the incidence of hepatitis B in Hainan Province, suggesting that there may be a relationship between the incidence of hepatitis B and temperature changes in Hainan Province. Relevant departments should take appropriate, reasonable and effective measures to strengthen prevention and control at different times according to the changes in seasonal characteristics found in this paper.

The ARIMA model and GM(1,1) model have been more widely used in forecasting in recent years, but each has its own strengths.[16,17] The SARIMA model can better predict diseases with seasonal characteristics, but the requirements for data are too demanding to be met in usual data, and data preprocessing must be performed on it. And the model requires data with linear trend, and the mapping ability for nonlinear data is weak, and it is difficult to extract the nonlinear information in the data. The BPNN model has a powerful nonlinear mapping capability with good global approximation and best approximation for nonlinear and unstable data.[18] The GM(1,1) model is a method for forecasting systems with uncertainty factors and is not demanding on the data, especially for short-term forecasting, where the accuracy of the prediction is high, but for long-term forecasting with long time series, the accuracy is reduced.

In this study, by comparing the MAPE and MAE of 3 models, the optimal model was selected as the SARIMA-BPNN model, which combines the advantages of both SARIMA and BPNN using SARIMA to extract the linear information of the data and BPNN to extract the nonlinear information of the data, so the model information is more fully utilized and widely used in the prediction of infectious disease incidence. In the paper,[19] 2 models, ARIMA and ARIMA-BPNN, were used to predict the incidence of hepatitis E in Shanghai, and the results showed that the accuracy of the ARIMA-BPNN model was higher than that of the ARIMA model, which was consistent with the results of this study. The incidence of hepatitis B in Hainan Province predicted by SARIMA-BPNN model shows that compared with 2021, the monthly incidence of hepatitis B in Hainan Province in 2022 will be decreased in general, more increase in January and February, more decrease in March and April, the most decrease is in March and decreased by 2.08/100,000, the lowest decrease is in June and is basically the same as last year. The lowest decrease was in June and was basically stable with last year, which means that the incidence of hepatitis B in Hainan Province is gradually improving, and it also shows that the prevention and control work of hepatitis B in Hainan Province is effective. Based on the predicted incidence of hepatitis B, the relevant authorities should continue to strengthen prevention and control and rationalize prevention and control according to the characteristics of seasonal changes, and islanders should also pay attention to their self-protection by not having too close contact with strangers, avoiding blood exposure, and testing their hepatitis B antibodies regularly to avoid being infected with hepatitis B.[20] February is the time of year when the incidence of hepatitis B is at its lowest, but the authorities still need to strengthen hepatitis B prevention and control measures during that month to nip it in the bud.

There are also some limitations in this study. First, some of the data in the study were derived from the direct reporting system for infectious diseases. It is possible that in some poor areas, some patients may have been infected with HBV and not detected and reported to the health department, resulting in possible discrepancies between the reported and true values. Second, for chronic hepatitis B is not considered. Finally, the monthly Hepatitis B incidence rates for 2017 and 2018 were obtained directly from the China Population Health Data Center platform, and the monthly Hepatitis B incidence rates for 2019 to 2020 and 2021 were estimated indirectly from data collected by the China Infectious Disease Surveillance System, and there may be discrepancies between the data collected by these 2 systems.

5. Conclusion

Monthly incidence of hepatitis B in Hainan Province has a decreasing trend, with a seasonal pattern of change. Comparison of the results showed that the best prediction model was the SARIMA-BPNN combination model, and the prediction showed that the monthly incidence rate of hepatitis B in 2022 had a decreasing trend and the fastest decrease was in March. It shows that the prevention and control of hepatitis B in Hainan Province is effective, and the predictive modeling study and predictive values can provide scientific and reasonable suggestions for the prevention and control of hepatitis B in Hainan.
