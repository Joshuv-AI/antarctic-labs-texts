The authors regret an error in the published version of Figure 2. Panel (b) of Figure 2, which shows IL‐6 production in cultured peripheral blood mononuclear cells stimulated with LPS, was incorrect. The bars for the +LPS conditions were incorrect and inadvertently replicated the +LPS bars presented in Fig. 2(f).

The updated Figure 2 with the correct panel 2(b) is provided below.
