Correction: GSH-responsive polymeric micelles-based augmented photoimmunotherapy synergized with PD-1 blockade for eliciting robust antitumor immunity against colon tumor
Authors: Chenlu Huang, Xinyu Yang, Huidong Li, Li Zhang, Qing Guo, Qingyu Yu, Hai Wang, Linhua Zhang, Dunwan Zhu
Published: 2024

Correction: Journal of Nanobiotechnology (2024) 22:542

10.1186/s12951-024-02813-w

Following publication of the original article details for affiliations of all authors except the author Huidong Li were incorrectly given as:

1. State Key Laboratory of Advanced Medical Materials and Devices, Tianjin Key Laboratory of Biomedical Materials, Key Laboratory of Biomaterials and Nanotechnology for Cancer Immunotherapy, Institute of Biomedical Engineering, Tianjin Institutes of Health Science, Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences & Peking Union Medical College, Tianjin 300,192, China.

2. Tianjin Central Hospital of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Tianjin 300,100, China.

But should have been:

1. State Key Laboratory of Advanced Medical Materials and Devices, Tianjin Key Laboratory of Biomedical Materials, Key Laboratory of Biomaterials and Nanotechnology for Cancer Immunotherapy, Institute of Biomedical Engineering, Tianjin Institutes of Health Science, Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences & Peking Union Medical College, Tianjin 300,192, China.

The original article has been corrected.
