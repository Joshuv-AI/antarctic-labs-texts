Innovation Without Boundaries – The Genesis of Journal of Cardiac Failure-Intersections
Authors: Jason N Katz, Nosheen Reza
Published: 2025

Often science-based discoveries will begin with an Aha! moment – the genesis of an idea that leads to the conception of a hypothesis that is then followed by its rigorous testing. With that in mind, we want to officially welcome you to Our laboratory. We are very much evaluating a hypothesis ourselves: that even amongst a crowded landscape of biomedical journals, and at a time when some may question the viability of these journals as a sustainable means for scientific consumption, it is still possible to create and cultivate something unique enough, disruptive enough, and creative enough to attract like-minded authors who want to invest in our vision and an audience eager to engage in transformative discussion. Unlike the many other hypotheses we have systematically tested throughout our scholastic careers, however, we have never felt more confident in the importance, the promise, and the future success of this one. What we believe will set our journal apart from the others is a belief that innovation is best nurtured, not when confined by traditional boundaries, but instead via unique connections of boundary-spanning people and ideas that occur at medicine’s many intersections (Figure). The Journal of Cardiac Failure-Intersections was created to serve as a platform for discovery and a forum for discourse, where intersections allow for the breaking of monotony, the fraternizing of seemingly disparate concepts, and the facilitation of imaginative engagement.

Intersections have played an integral part in the rich history of cardiovascular medicine. Many will recall, for instance, René Laennec as the inventor of the stethoscope. He was, of course, not the first to exploit the concept of auscultation for diagnostic purposes but is credited with creating the instrument we use to enhance the clarity of the heart’s sounds still today. His prototype was a simple, hollow, wooden cylinder which leveraged woodworking principles from his time and was inspired by a children’s game he observed while on a stroll through the Louvre.1 Hence, it was at the intersection of anatomy, physiology, carpentry, and serendipity that one of the most important discoveries in modern medicine emerged. We have since seen similarly ambitious intersectional breakthroughs including those that have led to life-saving pharmacologic therapies, innovative cardiac devices, and revolutionary operative techniques. Not all ideas will prove to be as monumental, but we share these historical reminders to motivate us all to be more observant of other fields and alternative perspectives in the pursuit of greater accomplishment. We think the Journal of Cardiac Failure-Intersections is the home for this type of work. It is a place to showcase talent, where everyone can feel empowered to confront assumptions and test unconventional ideas, and a venue for helping to construct new networks.

Creating a journal like this comes with great opportunity and unique challenges. Although we have an ambitious agenda in mind, we are not naïve to the practical obstacles facing the field. We are grappling with how to streamline the publication process and avoid delays that undermine the timely dissemination of new knowledge. We are considering how we might best sustain curiosity and foster a loyal, intellectually engaged readership over time. Perhaps most importantly, we are striving to better engage non-traditional readers and learners, connect more meaningfully with patients and communities, enhance and reward the peer review process, and help translate the science we publish into actionable change.

We hope that you will enjoy our very first issue of the Journal of Cardiac Failure-Intersections. Our new editorial team has been tasked with supporting our authors and amplifying the messages of those who have entrusted us with their work. We believe you will find important contributions that embrace and harness the concept of intersections in their own distinct and powerful ways, and we look forward to embarking upon this journey with all of you.
