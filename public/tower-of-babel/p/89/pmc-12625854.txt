Correction for Young-Brun et al., Within-country inequality and the shaping of a just global climate policy
Published: 2025

Correction for “Within-country inequality and the shaping of a just global climate policy,” by Marie Young-Brun, Francis Dennig, Frank Errickson, Simon Feindt, Aurélie Méjean, and Stéphane Zuber, which published September 23, 2025; 10.1073/pnas.2505239122 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 122, e2505239122).

The authors note that an equation in the SI Appendix appeared incorrectly. On page 3, Eq. 14, 1-sit and 1-s1t were mistakenly inverted. As a result,τit=1-sit1-s1tu′c1tu′citτ1t.

should instead appear asτit=1-s1t1-situ′c1tu′citτ1t.

SI Appendix, Figs. S6–S9 have also been updated. The authors note that the inverted equation “implies that SI Appendix, Figs. S6–S9 would need to be updated to reflect correct values. The changes only affect the results quantitatively and not qualitatively and do not affect the results discussed in the main text.”

The SI Appendix has been corrected online.
