Correction: Peripheral endocannabinoids in major depressive disorder and alcohol use disorder: a systematic review
Authors: JJ Fuentes, J Mayans, M Guarro, I Canosa, JI Mestre-Pintó, F Fonseca, M Torrens
Published: 2024

Fuentes et al. BMC Psychiatry (2024) 24:551

10.1186/s12888-024-05986-8

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors would like to correct the funding statement. The changes have been highlighted in bold typeface.

The incorrect funding statement is:

This work was supported by grants from the Suport Grups de Recerca AGAUR-Gencat (2017 SGR 316, 2017 SGR 530); Fondo de Investigación Sanitaria ISCIII (PI16/00603 and PI20/00029); SCIII-Redes de Investigación Cooperativa Orientadas a Resultados en Salud (RICORS); Red de Investigación en Atención Primaria de Adicciones (RIAPAd), grant number RD21/0009/0001. The funding agencies had no role in study design, data collection, interpretation, and no influence on the writing.

The correct funding statement is:

This work was supported by grants from the Suport Grups de Recerca AGAUR-Gencat (2017 SGR 316, 2017 SGR 530); Fondo de Investigación Sanitaria Instituto de Salud Carlos III -ISCIII- and co-funded by the European Union (PI16/00603). Project “PI20/00029”, funded by Instituto de Salud Carlos III (ISCIII) and co-funded by the European Union. SCIII-Redes de Investigación Cooperativa Orientadas a Resultados en Salud (RICORS); Red de Investigación en Atención Primaria de Adicciones (RIAPAd), grant RD21/0009/0001, funded by Instituto de Salud Carlos III (ISCIII), and by the European Union NextGenerationEU, Mecanismo para la Recuperación y la Resiliencia (MRR). The funding agencies had no role in study design, data collection, interpretation, and no influence on the writing.

The original article [1] has been corrected.
