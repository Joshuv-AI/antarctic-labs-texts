Author Correction: From injury to recovery: Spatiotemporal dynamics of the visual pathway during spontaneous structural and functional regeneration after optic nerve transection in zebrafish
Authors: Bao-Guo Shen, Yuan Wen, Sheng-Jian Lu, Hong-Yuan Wei, Shu-Rui Huang, Guang-Ming Zhou, Wen-Tao Yan, Wen-Can Wu, Yi-Kui Zhang
Published: 2025

Following the publication of Shen et al. (2025), we identified that during figure assembly, the image for the control group's Müller cells was inadvertently duplicated for the 3 dpi panel. To ensure the accuracy and transparency of the published record, we have replaced the erroneous panel with the correct image. The revised figure has been incorporated within the updated Supplementary Materials. We sincerely apologize for this oversight and any confusion it may have caused.

The corrected version of Figure 1D is provided below for reference.
