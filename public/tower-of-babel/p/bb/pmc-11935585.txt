Correction to: Proteomic Correlates and Prognostic Significance of Kidney Injury in Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction
Published: 2024

In the article by Oday Salman et al, “Proteomic Correlates and Prognostic Significance of Kidney Injury in Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction,” which published on August 29, 2024 and was included in the September 3, 2024 issue (J Am Heart Assoc. 2024;13:e033660. DOI: 10.1161/JAHA.123.033660), corrections were needed.

Due to a technical error, data points were mistakenly omitted from the volcano plots in Figure 4 (parts A and B). The figures have been corrected and are now complete, containing both data points and labels.

The authors and publisher regret the error.

The online version of the article has been updated and is available here: https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/JAHA.123.033660
