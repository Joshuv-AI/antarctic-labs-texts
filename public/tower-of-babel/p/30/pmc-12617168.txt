OA2034. AI4ProSa: AI-driven training system on product safety for professionals
Authors: M Encarnación Alemañ Baeza, M Cruz Arenas Motilla, A Ramos Fernández, P Agustín Pernías Peco, J M Deltorn
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundThe enforcement of Regulation (EU) 2023/988 on general product safety represents a major shift in how consumer products are regulated in the EU, introducing new requirements for traceability across the product lifecycle, the safety of products sold online, and improved mechanisms for risk communication. These changes create an urgent need to upskill and reskill professionals—particularly SMEs—future professionals, VET students and trainers in relation to the requirements and obligations established by the European Union on product safety, and to help them acquire new skills to integrate the advantages of generative AI in their work and training environments.Co-founded by Erasmus+ Programme, AI4ProSa “AI-Driven Training System on Product Safety for Professionals” addresses this need by developing an innovative generative AI-based assistant capable of delivering personalised training on product safety.ObjectivesThe project focuses on:• To contribute to the upskilling and reskilling of professionals, future professionals, VET students and trainers, enhancing their skills to meet the new product safety requirements established by the latest European legal provisions.• To support the acquisition of new skills to integrate the benefits of generative AI into product safety-related work and training environments.Results• AI4ProSa assistant: an interactive tool that adapts training to user profiles.• Guide on how to use generative AI for safety-related decision-making.• Training activities in Spain, Germany, and the Czech Republic to use the AI assistant in real-use environments.The development of the assistant follows the ADDIE methodology (Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, and Evaluation), allowing for iterative improvement based on real user feedback.ConclusionsAI4ProSa contributes directly to improving consumer product safety skills, promoting the safe and ethical use of emerging technologies, and reducing injury risks through improved product safety training. By empowering today’s and tomorrow’s professionals with AI-based tools, the project offers a forward-looking model for vocational education aligned with EU safety, innovation and green goals.Key messages• Supports upskilling and reskilling of professionals, particularly SME workers, equipping them with the knowledge to meet new EU product safety regulations and ensure safer products in the market.• Uses generative AI to enhance vocational training for students and trainers, providing the tools to understand and apply EU product safety regulations.TopicProduct safety, artificial intelligence, legal framework.
