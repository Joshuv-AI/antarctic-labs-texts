Robust Superconductivity in Infinite‐Layer Nickelates (Adv. Sci. 37/2024)
Authors: Minghui Xu, Yan Zhao, Yu Chen, Xiang Ding, Huaqian Leng, Zheng Hu, Xiaoqiang Wu, Jiabao Yi, Xiaojiang Yu, Mark BH Breese, Shibo Xi, Mengsha Li, Liang Qiao
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Abstract Superconductors In article number 2305252, Huaqian Leng, Mengsha Li, Liang Qiao, and co‐workers demonstrate a reversible transition between the perovskite Nd0.8Sr0.2NiO3 and the infinite‐layer Nd0.8Sr0.2NiO2 thin films without losing their structural framework and topological relationships. The structural and physical properties of nickelates are systematically investigated by successively “deintercalation‐intercalation” of the apical oxygens in 6‐fold coordination of Ni ions. The superconductivity in Nd0.8Sr0.2NiO2 still exists even for 10 times cycles, indicating the robustness of infinite‐layer structural frame.
