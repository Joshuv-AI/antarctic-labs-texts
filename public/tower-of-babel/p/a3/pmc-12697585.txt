Artistic Abstract Representation of a Cross‐Section of a Blood Vessel Revascularized After Thrombectomy
Authors: Neetu Singhal
Published: 2025

Neetu Singhal is an American‐Indian artist.

She is a multidisciplinary artist, color therapist, wearable art designer, printmaker, painter, sculptor, joyfully successful educator, and meditator.

She has 16 years of professional experience as a visual artist. She started her full‐time professional artist's journey in India after 2008. She left her professional science background behind; it was one of the biggest decisions to pursue her passion of interest.

Neetu is known for “neurobiological art.” She is fascinated by a trilogy of science, art, and spirituality, particularly through her analysis of neuroscience experiments; in search of cellular and universal consciousness. She is interested in a connection of the transcendental, metaphysical, and theosophical perspective of the mind‐brain science relationship, a focused effort of balance between bodily consciousness, thoughts, and emotions. In Neetu's work, transcendental effects of colors give a strong impression to enhance human daily life experiences and uplifting life force energies. Her paintings are based on her personal experiences and her vision to make this world more joyful. Her work, identified as “transcendence with colors, helped many children and adults to improve their mental health”.

She has connected with many holistic organizations for over a decade to understand the science of creativity. She loves to express herself as a contemporary 2‐dimensional and 3‐dimensional visual artist. The recent focus of her work has been composing color perception that accompanies various mediums and materials, with the intention of supporting people in entering a space of deep emotional intelligence.

She is creating pathways to community awareness and designing dynamic human experiences with color interactions. She believes in community building, amplifying people and insights at the intersection of consciousness, mental health wellness, and social transformation.

About the Artist

Neetu's work had been represented and recognized in more than 108 group and solo exhibitions, auctions, art fairs, art biennials, government institutions, hospitals, and art residencies. Her work was documented by the Indian government's largest national TV channel. “Doordarshan” was a full‐work documentary on her work published in 2013. Her work has been published by national and international media houses across the globe. Her artworks have been collected by multiple government agencies and private collectors (permanent collections) in many countries.

Website: https://www.neetusinghal.com/

About the Medium

Acrylic, screen print ink on stretched canvas.

About the Artwork

“Artistic abstract representation of a cross‐section of a blood vessel revascularized after thrombectomy”

(Thrombectomy is used to treat conditions caused by blood clots. In stroke [cerebral thrombosis], myocardial infarction [heart attack], pulmonary embolism, deep vein thrombosis, etc.)
