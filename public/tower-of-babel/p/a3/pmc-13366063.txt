IFPHPOS0201 Women in Healthcare: Musculoskeletal Discomfort in Nurses, Evidenced from Resource-Limited Settings
Authors: Upasana Chowdhury, Tamal Das, Somnath Gangopadhyay
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundFemale nurses form the backbone of healthcare systems in low- and middle-income settings, yet their occupational health, especially musculoskeletal wellbeing, remains under-recognised. The nurses experience a dual burden of occupational strain and gendered expectations, making musculoskeletal health a critical women’s health concern. This research seeks to evaluate the impact of various physiological, psychosocial, and occupational stressors on the development of musculoskeletal symptoms in female registered nurses in West Bengal, India.MethodsEthical permission was granted from the institutional human ethical committee (IHEC). A total of 300 nurses were randomly contacted, with 261 providing complete responses to the questionnaires and 215 individuals selected based on specified inclusion and exclusion criteria. For data collection, the anthropometric measurements, Physical Activity Level Questionnaire, Copenhagen Psychosocial Questionnaire (COPSOQ), Modified Nordic Questionnaire, and Rapid Entire Body Assessment (REBA) tool were used.ResultsA remarkably high percentage (80.9%) of the participants reported experiencing musculoskeletal discomfort, with low back pain (LBP) affecting 52.1% of the population. The physiological factors and multiple COPSOQ scales were found to correlate with musculoskeletal discomfort. Upon analysing task-related hazards, various task positions showed considerable risks and the requirement for alterations.ConclusionThis study explored a unique resource-limited niche through the dual lenses of ergonomics and musculoskeletal discomfort assessment, which contributes to the global discourse on women’s occupational health by providing context-specific evidence. Small and low-cost changes in work design, changes in staffing, task loads, and rest breaks, administrative involvement, and ergonomic awareness can show substantial improvements in this scenario.
