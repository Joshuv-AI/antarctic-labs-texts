Correct

This research was supported and partially funded by GV/2020/18, the Institute of Health Carlos III (project IMPaCT-Data, IMP/00019), co-funded by ERDF, “A way to make Europe”; PID2021-124430OA-I00 funded by MICIU/AEI/10.13039/501100011033 and by FEDER, UE.

The original article [1] has been corrected.
