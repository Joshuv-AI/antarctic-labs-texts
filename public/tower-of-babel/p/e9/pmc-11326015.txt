Enhancing medical decision-making with ChatGPT and explainable AI
Authors: Aryan Chopra, Dharmendra Singh Rajput, Harshita Patel
Published: 2024

Dear Editor,

In light of the accelerating advancements in generative artificial intelligence (AI) and its applications in healthcare, I am writing to further explore the transformative potential of ChatGPT and elucidate the role of explainable AI (XAI) in augmenting its capabilities. Building upon the insightful research presented by Chakraborty et al.1, which highlights ChatGPT’s proficiency in providing treatment suggestions for critical patients, I aim to underscore the broader implications of leveraging these technologies in medical decision-making2.

Generative AI, epitomized by models like ChatGPT, represents a paradigm shift in how we harness computational power to address complex healthcare challenges. However, to fully realize the potential of ChatGPT as a decision support tool in healthcare, it is imperative to ensure transparency and interpretability in its recommendations.

XAI emerges as a pivotal enabler in this regard, offering a framework through which clinicians can comprehend and scrutinize the underlying rationale behind ChatGPT’s suggestions3. By providing intuitive explanations for its decisions, ChatGPT augmented with XAI empowers clinicians to evaluate the relevance and reliability of its recommendations in the context of patient care4. Moreover, transparent decision-making processes engender trust and facilitate collaboration between AI systems and healthcare providers, fostering a synergistic approach to medical decision-making.

Fatemeh employed few-shot and zero-shot prompting techniques with ChatGPT to evaluate its performance in comparison to highly performing interpretable machine learning models5. Concurrently, Kenta contributed to the field by rewriting medical algorithms in Python code and innovatively developed a novel approach for implementing XAI in healthcare, leveraging ChatGPT’s capabilities6. These endeavors underscore the potential of utilizing advanced natural language processing models in tandem with traditional methodologies to enhance transparency and interpretability in AI systems, particularly in critical domains such as healthcare.

Beyond its immediate applications in treatment suggestions, the integration of ChatGPT and XAI holds promise across a spectrum of healthcare domains. From diagnostic support and prognostic modeling to personalized therapy selection and patient counseling, these technologies offer unprecedented opportunities to enhance clinical workflows and improve patient outcomes. Moreover, as telemedicine and remote healthcare delivery continue to gain traction, ChatGPT augmented with XAI emerges as a valuable asset for remote clinicians, providing timely and accurate decision support irrespective of geographical constraints7.

As we navigate the evolving landscape of AI-driven healthcare, it is imperative to address the ethical, regulatory, and technical challenges associated with deploying these technologies in clinical practice. By fostering interdisciplinary collaborations and engaging stakeholders across the healthcare ecosystem, we can harness the transformative potential of ChatGPT and XAI while safeguarding patient safety, privacy, and autonomy.

Despite these challenges, successful case studies of interactive XAI implementation in healthcare contexts offer promising insights. For instance, the AI-based decision support system developed by Holzinger et al.8 incorporated interactive XAI features, enabling clinicians to query the model’s reasoning and refine its predictions. This approach led to increased trust and adoption among practitioners. Similar initiatives could inform the development of interactive XAI solutions for surgical risk prediction models like those proposed by Lee et al.9.

In conclusion, the convergence of ChatGPT and XAI marks a transformative epoch in healthcare. This synergy facilitates transparent, data-driven insights, bolstering clinical decision-making and patient outcomes. By offering interpretable recommendations through XAI mechanisms, we ensure accountability and foster trust in AI-driven healthcare solutions. Through continuous learning and feedback loops, these technologies drive innovation, efficiency, and quality improvement in healthcare delivery. Embracing ethical principles, we navigate the complexities of AI deployment, ensuring equitable access and empowering patients in their healthcare journey.
