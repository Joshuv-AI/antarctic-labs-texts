Individual tree diameter growth modelling for natural secondary forest in Changbai Mountains, Northeast China
Authors: Jun Lu
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractIndividual modelling is a foundational approach to study the natural forest growth and in this paper, we develop a serial distance-depended individual tree model for some species in natural forest which would provide prediction and characteristics for natural species. The data used to develop individual model for natural mixed forests were collected from 712 remeasured 10-year periodic permanent sample plots of in Baihe Forest Bureau of Changbai Mountains, northeast China. Based on analyzing relationship between diameter increment of individual trees with tree size, competitive status, and site condition and finding out the major independent variables, the growth models for individual trees of 15 species in the natural mixed forests, that have good predicting precision, and easily applicable, were developed using stepwise regression method. The individual growth model developed in this study can reflect the tree increment of 15 species and be generally well suited for simulating tree and stand growth for natural mixed forests in Changbai Mountains. The research results for individual trees growth model of each species showed that main variable to influence on diameter increment of individual trees for natural mixed forests were tree size (D) and then competition index. The site condition was not related with diameter increment. The natural logarithm of DBH (lnD) and square diameter (D2) were included in the predicting models of diameter increment for all 15 species. The diameter increment was directly proportional to tree diameter for each species. For the competitive indexes in growth model, the relative diameter (RD), canopy closure (P), and the ratio of diameter of subject tree with maximum diameter (DDM) were related to diameter increment and the stand density measures were not significantly influenced on diameter increment. As canopy closure increase, tree increment decreases. The site conditions were performance less of factors in increment predictions in the model.Keywords: Natural secondary forest, Individual tree growth model, Diameter increment, Stepwise regression, Forest managementSubject terms: Forestry, Forest ecology, Forestry

Introduction

There are 12.184 million hectare of natural forest resources in China, accounting for 64% of the national forest area and 83% of the stock volume, so the scientific and rational management and protection of natural secondary forests are particularly important, Changbai Mountain forest area is a typical representative of natural forest and state-owned forest area in northeast China. It plays an important role in maintaining the ecological balance and wood safety in northeast China, and protecting the wild animals and plants represented by the Siberian tiger and Taxus chinensis.

It is the principle of natural forest management to improve the self-repair ability of natural forests, supplemented by artificial management, and the premise is to master the growth process of natural forests. The growth law of forest is mainly studied by establishing models, among which the research models of artificial pure forest are divided into whole forest model, diameter class model and single tree model1–3, the choice of modelling parameters is also the focus of research4, Natural secondary forest has many tree species, many age groups and diverse structures, and the relationship between tree species is very complex. The division of tree species composition is a difficult problem in modelling. Generally speaking, one way to solve the problem is to combine tree species with similar biological characteristics in the stand into dominant tree groups or forest types; another way is to establish single tree models of each tree species, because single tree models have nothing to do with tree species composition. Many single wood models can also hide the age5, reducing the difficulty of modelling. Single tree growth model can predict the growth of various stands, and the most commonly used methods are growth amount correction method, growth analysis method and empirical equation method, and the prediction factor is diameter growth amount or fault area6. According to previous research conclusions, the size, competition and site of trees in the stand are the key factors affecting the diameter growth. Moreover, in a model for the growth of individual stand area, which not contain age and status index, it is found that the size of trees is the most important factor affecting the growth of the stand area7,8. In the modelling technology of single wood model, many new methods have improved the accuracy of the model, such as the establishment of a limited regression equation system according to the theoretical relationship between the basal area of the diameter and the diameter growth of each diameter9, It can solve the problem of inaccurate prediction of stand by single tree model. Dummy variable model10, visualization model11, mixed effect model12 and random effect model13 can also significantly improve the accuracy of the model. The parameter values of each component model in the single tree growth model system, which consists of diameter or section area growth model, tree height growth model and tree crown rate model, were fitted by the method of joint equations14,15. The single wood model is divided into the single wood model related to distance and the single wood model independent of distance, and the single wood model independent of distance is more used16–19. Some studies have shown that the distance-independent growth model can achieve good results in predicting the growth of individual trees20,21.

The growth law of natural secondary forest is complicated and diverse, so it is difficult to study the growth model. The goal of this study is to use the tree size index, site index and competition index as independent variables and gradually regression the square diameter growth of trees and establish the empirical equation of individual tree growth prediction for multiple tree species. Based on the methods of some foreign forest growth simulation software packages, such as STEMS and PROGNOSIS22–26, we constructed a relatively simple model form according to the growth law of each tree species, introduce the correlation between the variables in the regression equation and the growth of the forest tree, and use multiple stepwise regression method for fitting, a single tree growth model was established27.

Materials and methods

Overview of the study area

Baihe Forestry Bureau is located in Yanbian Korean Autonomous Prefecture, Jilin Province, on the north slope of Changbai Mountain. Its geographical coordinates are 127° 53ʹ–128° 34ʹ east longitude and 42° 01ʹ–42° 48ʹ north latitude. Baihe Forestry Bureau belongs to the temperate continental monsoon climate, long winter and short summer, low temperature and rainy, the climate is cold, the global average temperature is only 2.2 °C, the hottest July average temperature is 27.9 °C, the coldest January average temperature is − 26.6 °C. The annual frost-free period is about 110 days, and the average annual precipitation is about 700 mm, concentrated in June to August. Baihe forest area is rich in plant resources and belongs to Changbai Mountain flora. The zonal climax community of Baihe Forestry Bureau is coniferous and broad-leaved secondary forest. Typical forest types include broad-leaved red pine forest, coniferous cloud fir forest, coniferous cloud fir broad-leaved secondary forest and secondary broad-leaved forest (Quercus mongolicat, birch forest).

Data sources

The modeling and testing data came from the retesting data of fixed plots in Baihe Forestry Bureau at intervals of 10 years during the second period, including 810 natural forest restoration plots in the two forest manager surveys, and 712 permanent plots (Fig. 1) were screened out (including excessive cutting, artificial plots, and plots of artificial origin, etc.), with 25,525 effective sample trees. 75% of the samples were used for model modeling and 25% were used for model testing. Screening method of single tree growth model parameters

Equation (5) was used to fit model parameters by stepwise regression for all tree species (groups), and each variable was screened. As the number of independent variables increased, the determination coefficient of the multiple regression model increased, and after multiple variables were included in the model, the adjusted correlation coefficient of the growth model reached a certain value. With the continuous addition of variables to the model, the added value of the adjusted correlation coefficient remains at a certain level without major changes. According to the practical principle of the established growth model, more variables should be used as far as possible to reduce the workload in forest survey, and the selected variables are the parameters of the final selected model.

In Eq. (5), the number of independent variables is very large, there will be collinearity in actual modelling, and parameter overestimation will be generated during fitting. Generally, variance expansion factor (VIF) is used to determine multicollinearity and VIF < 10 is set.

The statistical software used for fitting and testing is STATISTICA. The variables to judge the stepwise regression equation including determination coefficient R2, adjusted correlation coefficient Radj2 and residual standard deviation (residual).

Discussion

Individual tree growth model

This study used the re-measured data of 712 fixed plots to establish a prediction model for the growth per square diameter of 15 tree species (groups) on the north slope of Changbai Mountain and used multiple stepwise regression method for fitting. Compared with many modeling methods for single tree growth models based on empirical models6,31, only simple mathematical equations were used to express tree growth, which would ignore the biological characteristics of tree species. Although the coefficient of determination is relatively high, reaching above 0.9 for some conifers such as Larix olgensis and Pinus massoniana Lamb6,32, this is only a statistical result.

Combined with the growth characteristics of trees, this study conducted modeling from the three additive comprehensive factors of size, competition and site. The research results of individual tree growth models of various species (groups) showed that:

Tree diameter size factors (lnD and D2) are the most important factors affecting the diameter growth of natural secondary forest, DBH represents the vitality of trees. Trees with large DBH have strong vitality, but when the diameter growth reaches a certain degree, the influence ability is limited, which is consistent with the growth and development process of trees; For Betula platyphyla with a DBH less than 13.5 cm, the larger the diameter, the larger the growth; For those with a diameter greater than 13.5 cm, the larger the diameter, the smaller the growth. When the diameter exceeds 35.2 cm, the influence of the DBH variable on the growth volume appears negative, and the change of the diameter growth rate with the size of the forest is an inverse “J” curve or negative exponential function. The diameter growth prediction models of 15 tree species (groups) all included lnD and D2 factors. Only the parameters of lnD and D2 in larch model were positive, and the others were positive and negative. This is similar to the results of other researchers33.

The diameter growth of Pinus koraiensis, Acer mono, Tilia amurensis, Betula costata, Ulmus pumila and Quercus mongolica was inversely proportional to the canopy closure (P), indicating that the growth of these trees was more sensitive to the canopy closure.

In the fitting and testing results of each tree species (group), the relative diameter (RD), stand closure (P), and the ratio of the diameter of the target tree to the diameter of the largest tree in the stand (DDM), which reflect the competition index of trees, have a certain influence on the diameter growth; The index of stand density, such as stand break area (G), stand density index (SDI) and break area of large-diameter trees (BAL), had no significant effect on the square diameter growth of all tree species. The larger the relative diameter (RD and DDM) of each tree species, the stronger the competitiveness, and the larger the diameter growth. In this study, the neutral site conditions had little influence on the growth amount, which was completely excluded in the selection process of the model, indicating that the contribution rate of the site to the model was small. This region belongs to the site subregion of Changbai Mountain and is a typical area of the middle temperate forest type. The dark brown soil layer is thicker, which may be the reason why the difference of the growth rate is not significant. When relevant scholars used similar modeling methods, the research conclusions were also consistent34–36, such as the influence of Mongolian oak forest on site conditions was very small33.

Improvement of model accuracy

Although the prediction accuracy of Larix olgensis, Quercus mongolica and Mixed species models was high, they did not pass the confidence ellipse F test. It is suggested to improve the investigation accuracy and the selection of modeling factors. There are many reasons for this situation, mainly reflected in the following aspects: (1) Measurement error in field survey, which is the main cause of data deviation. (2) The growth of individual trees in the stand is abnormal, and the growth of trees is too high or too low due to the influence of factors such as competition and site. This difference in growth amount directly leads to the inconsistency of data. (3) Except larch that failed the test, they were all broad-leaved tree species, and the biological characteristics of tree species would also produce abnormal data.

Conclusion

The single tree square diameter growth model established in this study can be used to simulate the growth of major tree species on the north slope of Changbai Mountain, providing data support for forest management and biodiversity protection in this area. However, due to the complex age structure, tree species structure and competition relationship in natural forests32,37, the correlation coefficient in this result is not too high, generally ranging from 0.6 to 0.8. In future studies, the mixed effect between plots can be considered and the mixed model can be adopted for modeling. If a larger sample size is adopted and the research scale is expanded, the results will be improved. This method of variable screening will also improve the work efficiency in the actual field data investigation.
