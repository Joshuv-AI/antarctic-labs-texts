Infection & Chemotherapy: The Experience of Journal Integration and Future Prospects
Authors: Dong-Gun Lee
Published: 2024

Infection & Chemotherapy (Infect Chemother, hereinafter IC) is an English-language journal jointly published by three prominent Korean societies dedicated to the scopes of infectious diseases: the Korean Society of Infectious Diseases (KSID), the Korean Society for Antimicrobial Therapy (KSAT), and the Korean Society for AIDS (KOSAIDS). Starting with the second issue of 2024, the Korean Society of Pediatric Infectious Diseases (KSPID) was added as one of the publishers, making it a joint publication of four societies. This editorial briefly introduces the journal integration process, discusses its significance, and explores the future prospects of journal integration among academic societies in related fields.

In January 2023, the KSPID publication committee discussed and approved the integration of Pediatric Infection & Vaccine (PIV) journal into IC. Approval was obtained from the KSPID Board of Directors, Board of Trustees, and general meeting sequentially in May 2023. Integration preparations were then made to seek approval from the three publishing societies of IC. In August 2023, the IC publication committee discussed the matter and presented it to the boards of directors of the three societies. The integration of PIV into IC was approved by KSID and KOSAIDS in October 2023, and by KSAT in April 2024, at their respective board of trustees and general meetings in order. Following the decision to integrate, the editors of both journals met to discuss the process and registered PIV reviewers as IC reviewers. Starting from March 2024, PIV stopped submission of new manuscript. Additionally, PIV editors and editorial board members became part of the IC team as deputy editors and editorial board members, respectively. The IC website was updated accordingly, and the first joint issue of the four societies was planned for Volume 56, Issue 2, late June 2024. Before publication, revisions were made to the journal’s aims and scope, submission guidelines, editorial board, cover, copyright, and website to reflect the KSPID as a new publisher. Past PIV publications were linked to the IC website for management purposes. Official notices were sent to domestic and international organizations where IC is indexed (Korean Association of Medical Journal Editors, Korea Citation Index, The Korean Federation of Science and Technology Societies, National Library of Korea, Web of Science, Scopus, Directory of Open Access Journals) to inform them about the addition of KSPID as a co-publisher of IC.

Due to the essential nature of the medical field, it is inevitable that scope of medicine and journal will become specialized over time. Academic societies and journals have followed this trend by creating new fields of study that are increasingly divided, resulting in the establishment of numerous new societies and their respective journals. In Korea, these societies and journals are not forming due to an increase in specialists but because individuals are involved in multiple specialized fields concurrently. Currently, there are about 11 academic societies in the field of infectious diseases in Korea, and I am also a member of 5-6 of them. This highlights the reality that this field does not produce hundreds of new specialists annually, emphasizing the challenge of doctors and professors engaging in research, education, and clinical work simultaneously while facing the constant evaluation by their institutions. Academic journals are also evaluated rigorously, and their survival depends on receiving, reviewing, and publishing high-quality papers that are widely cited. The decision of domestic societies in the field of infectious diseases to publish a joint journal is significant, as it offers an opportunity to achieve better results through collaborative efforts, enhancing the competitiveness of both the societies and the journal.

I would like to thank the executive boards and members of the four societies for deciding to integrate the journals. I will promise and keep working with all of the editorial board members to make IC a leading integrated journal in the field of infectious diseases. Thank you.
