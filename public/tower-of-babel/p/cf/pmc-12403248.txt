Dear Sir.

I read with great interest the systematic review article by Chen et al., which indicates relationship between colorectal cancer screening characteristics and screening coverage rates [1]. These findings of study are interesting that demonstrate the necessity of participation and expand coverage for screening, but the problem in screening and treatment remains due to its multifactorial nature and lack a comprehensive health policy.

Cancer, as a growing disease, has been influenced by sociocultural disparity in various countries. Health care system can have special importance in cancer management [2]. There are significant differences in socioeconomic and health status indicators as well as in the affordability and accessibility of care among developed countries. The effectiveness of these systems to eliminate racial and ethnic disparities in advanced health systems is controversial and complex. Mapping health disparities in 11 high-income countries shows that the United States ranks first in geographic health disparities and has the lowest rank in health outcomes and equity. Health systems in developed countries are significantly different, which can offer different perspectives on problem-solving approaches and produce different results, especially in healthcare access [3]. This status in developing countries is more complex.

The difference of developed countries and developing countries in various aspect of health care is tangible that can influence all of dimensions of primary health care. Developing countries is suffering in a proper Framework of health care system. Lack of correct health care policy and existence of Ethnic and geographical inequalities and improper distribution of resources can complicate this issue. Developed countries in health care policy assign large significant resources and economical incomes. In low-income countries, there are various barriers that can influence health care status that include high expenditure supplies, insufficient support by government institutions, and incomplete access to supplies [4, 5].

Health problems in each country are due to the interaction of various dimensions of health. The nature of health care problem in all countries is multi-disciplinary and need a holistic view. Health care manager play a vital role in health promotion and health care policy. The change of health care system and Create an efficient system, more attention to health education and socioeconomic cultural disparity in providing service in all of health level, allocate more resources, and use of various health models can alter health care conditions. But, this issue requires cooperation between different parts of a health system. It is not unexpected to be realized.
