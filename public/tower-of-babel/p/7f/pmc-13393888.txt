Correction to: Stress-induced altered expression of hippocampal nuclear and mitochondrial encoded genes in rats and cross-species genetic associations reveal molecular links to depression
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: Ellie Hulwi, Qingzhong Wang, Aleena Francis, Anuj K Verma, Yogesh Dwivedi, Stress-induced altered expression of hippocampal nuclear and mitochondrial encoded genes in rats and cross-species genetic associations reveal molecular links to depression, International Journal of Neuropsychopharmacology, Volume 28, Issue 9, September 2025, pyaf057, https://doi.org/10.1093/ijnp/pyaf057

In the originally published version of this manuscript, the title of Table 1 contained an error.

The title of Table 1 has been corrected to read: “Top 10 significant nuclear and mitochondrial encoded differentially expressed genes in the hippocampus of restraint rats” instead of: “Top 10 significant nuclear and mitochondrial encoded differentially expressed genes in the prefrontal cortex of restraint rats”.
