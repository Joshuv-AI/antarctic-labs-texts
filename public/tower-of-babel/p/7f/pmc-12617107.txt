OA2055. Safe at home: A mobile exhibition for preventing domestic injuries among seniors
Authors: T S Hveem-Vislie, E Vaagland
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractIssue/ProblemIn Norway, over 660,000 people seek medical care annually due to accidents—most of which occur at home. Older adults are particularly vulnerable, with falls in the home accounting for 60% of injury-related incidents among those aged 80+. There is a growing need for low-threshold.DescriptionDespite the known risks, effective communication and community-based solutions for home injury prevention are lacking. Traditional campaigns often fail to engage or reach the most vulnerable. To address this, the Norwegian Safety Forum developed “Safe at Home”—a mobile exhibition designed to visualize risks and trigger preventive action.ResultsPiloted in collaboration with two municipalities, the exhibition consists of movable cardboard walls replicating rooms in a typical home. Visitors walk through a familiar environment, identifying hazards and learning practical measures to reduce injury risk. Feedback shows increased awareness, behavioral change, and adoption of safety equipment. The initiative has received positive media coverage and strong interest from local stakeholders.LessonsThe project demonstrates that simple, interactive, and low-cost tools can be highly effective in injury prevention. The flexible design allows easy setup in local venues, and the concept encourages intersectoral collaboration—particularly within public health, elder care, and civil society. Innovative, mobile, and community-oriented approaches like “Safe at Home” can bridge the gap between knowledge and action. By meeting seniors where they are—both physically and cognitively—we empower them to take ownership of their safety.Key messages• A mobile, low-cost exhibition helps seniors and municipalities prevent home injuries through simple, engaging, and effective safety awareness.• Supporting ageing in place, this initiative mobilizes communities to make home safety a shared responsibility—simple tools with lasting impact.TopicSeniors, falls prevention, community based
