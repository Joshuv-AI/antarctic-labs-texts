Publisher Correction: Targeting of NAT10 enhances healthspan in a mouse model of human accelerated aging syndrome
Authors: Gabriel Balmus, Delphine Larrieu, Ana C Barros, Casey Collins, Monica Abrudan, Mukerrem Demir, Nicola J Geisler, Christopher J Lelliott, Jacqueline K White, Natasha A Karp, James Atkinson, Andrea Kirton, Matt Jacobsen, Dean Clift, Raphael Rodriguez, Sanger Mouse Genetics Project, David J Adams, Stephen P Jackson
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-018-03770-3, published online 27 April 2018

In the version of this article initially published, in the graph in Fig. 3a, the right-hand bars labelled “Nat10+/–” should have read “Nat10–/–.” Due to the age of the article, the figure cannot be updated directly. A correct version of Fig. 3a is available as Supplementary information accompanying this amendment. This notice serves to correct the article.
