Appreciation of Invisible Intellectual Contributions
Authors: Jin-Hong Yoo
Published: 2026

As all global scholarly journals do, the Journal of Korean Medical Science (JKMS) receives submissions from around the world and selects manuscripts after critical peer review. In 2025, JKMS received 3,166 submissions, 840 (26.5%) by Korean researchers and 2,326 (73.5%) by overseas researchers. About 75% of them were desk-rejected at triage, and the remaining 25% were reviewed by invited peer reviewers. The overall acceptance rate was 8.9%; research articles 10.1%, review articles 10.6%, and case reports 1.0% in 2025.

The JKMS invited 978 experts for peer review of the manuscripts in 2025, and some of them received multiple invitations. They were divided into five groups according to the number of reviews.

Together with all of the editorial staff of JKMS, I sincerely appreciate the reviewers listed below for their contributions. Among the reviewers, Dr. Sheng-Min Wang (The Catholic University of Korea, Korea), Dr. Hyejin Joo (Korea University, Korea), and Dr. Hyun Mi Kang (The Catholic University of Korea, Korea) were awarded as the best reviewers for JKMS in 2025.

Because peer review is critical for both the quality of the journal and the manuscripts, JKMS will invite more active experts from around the world who can review submitted manuscripts in 2026. I believe that voluntary contributions to peer review should be regarded as an academic honor in science. Thanks to their contribution, JKMS is progressing soundly and steadily as a global scholarly journal of medicine in general.
