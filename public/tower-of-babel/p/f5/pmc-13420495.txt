Reviewers and awards

Peer review remains one of the cornerstones of scientific publishing, and we couldn’t be more grateful to each and every one of you who so generously dedicated your time and expertise to support the European Heart Journal—Digital Health (EHJ-DH). Your thoughtful feedback has helped authors strengthen their work and ensured that our journal continues to maintain the highest standards of scientific quality and impact.

We know that finding time for peer review is not easy. Amidst increasingly busy schedules, your willingness to contribute this essential service to the scientific community is truly remarkable. Your reviews have been instrumental not only in guiding our editorial decisions but also in helping authors improve their manuscripts. Thanks to your efforts, the scientific quality and influence of our publications continue to grow.

We are also delighted to celebrate another successful year for EHJ-DH. Our impact factor (IF) improved to 5.3, keeping us firmly within the top quartile of cardiovascular journals. This achievement is, in no small part, a reflection of your dedication and commitment to excellence. Together, we continue to strengthen our community and advance our shared ambition: to be, and remain, the leading journal in cardiovascular digital health.

On behalf of the entire editorial team, thank you for your invaluable contributions. We truly could not do it without you, and we look forward to continuing this journey together.

To share a few numbers: Since our first IF, 2 years ago, the influx of submission is more than doubling each year. To cope with all these manuscripts over 700 reviewers helped us to evaluate them.
