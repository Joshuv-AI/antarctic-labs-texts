Response to letter regarding: ‘challenges confronted by paediatricians in evaluation of paediatric athletes: awareness on paediatric sports medicine’
Authors: Abdullah Akkuş, Hüseyin Aslan, Nefise Betül Ercan, Betül Cığal, Mehtap Yücel, Ahmet Osman Kılıç, Abdullah Yazar, Fatih Akın
Published: 2025

Dear Editor

We appreciate the insightful comments provided by Ture et al. regarding our article entitled ‘Challenges Confronted by Paediatricians in Managing Paediatric Athletes: Awareness on Paediatric Sports Medicine’ [1]. Their thoughtful contribution further enriches the ongoing discussion on the urgent need to establish Paediatric Sports Medicine (PSM) as a recognized subspecialty within paediatrics.

We fully agree with their emphasis on the pivotal role of paediatric emergency departments in the management of young athletes presenting with a broad spectrum of sport-related conditions [2]. As highlighted, paediatric emergency settings often represent the first point of contact for acute and sometimes life-threatening problems, underscoring the need for structured collaboration between paediatric subspecialties, including PSM.

The issue of doping and the inappropriate use of performance-enhancing substances in adolescents is also of utmost importance [3,4]. Although our study primarily aimed to explore paediatricians’ awareness and perceptions of PSM, we recognize that doping is an emerging and underexplored concern among young athletes. We appreciate the authors’ emphasis on this subject, and we agree that preventive strategies, education of athletes and families, and early detection of risky behaviours are essential. Since our study is among initial Turkish studies regarding the importance of PSM and potential problems encountered in case of its absence. Thus, we appreciate the authors’ emphasis for limited discussion about the issue of doping and believe that future studies focusing specifically on this topic will provide further valuable insights.

In conclusion, we are encouraged that our work has stimulated constructive dialogue on the multifaceted needs of paediatric athletes. With the fact that paediatric emergency specialists are primarily paediatricians into account, we share the authors’ view that formal recognition of PSM within paediatrics will strengthen multidisciplinary cooperation, promote prevention and early recognition of sport-related health issues, and ultimately contribute to the safety and well-being of children engaged in sports.
