Advancing the science of nucleic acids together
Authors: Betty Schiefelbein, Paloma H Giangrande, Zhijian Chen
Published: 2026

Main text

Paloma Giangrande, Editor-in-Chief of Molecular Therapy Nucleic Acids (MTNA), and James Chen, current President of the International Society of Nucleic Acid Immunity (NAIS, https://www.nai-society.com/), are pleased to announce a new strategic partnership dedicated to advancing excellence and innovation across the global nucleic acids research community. As part of this partnership, MTNA will become an official journal of NAIS.

The foundation of NAIS stems from the initiative of the international community of scientists fostering research in the area of innate immune sensing of nucleic acids and holding regular scientific meetings for several years, including the Nucleic Acid Immunity meetings in Edinburgh in 2022 and Dresden in 2023. NAIS was formally incorporated as a scientific society in Luxemburg in 2023, with Gunther Hartmann from Bonn, Germany, as founding president. The first meeting of the newly founded society was held in Avignon in 2024 (hosted by Nicolas Manel, Jean-Luc Imler, and Alice Lepeley). The second annual meeting was held in Munich in 2025 (hosted by Veit Hornung, Min Ae Lee Kirsch, and Gunther Hartmann), and the third annual meeting will be held on September 16–19, 2026, in Nara, Japan (hosted by Shizuo Akira).

NAIS is committed to empowering the next generation of scientists, integrating them into a vibrant research network with access to cutting-edge discoveries, and fostering collaboration with leading experts. Operating solely to promote international scientific exchange, NAIS plays a vital role in elevating the visibility and impact of this rapidly evolving field of nucleic acid immunity, which has vast implications for nucleic acid therapeutics.

Through this partnership, MTNA will expand its scope to include nucleic acid immunity, encompassing areas of focus such as:

Together, MTNA and NAIS will deepen scientific engagement, broaden opportunities for knowledge exchange, and strengthen the global foundation of nucleic acids research. This partnership aims to accelerate the translation of groundbreaking discoveries into impactful nucleic acid-based therapeutics.

We look forward to shaping the future of nucleic acid science together.
