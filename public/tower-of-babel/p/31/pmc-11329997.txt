Correction: Perturbation of periodic spot-generation balance leads to diversified pigmentation patterning of harlequin Phalaenopsis orchids: in silico prediction

Published: 2024

Correction: BMC Plant Biol 24, 681 (2024)

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors identified errors in the numbering of figures due to the movement of Materials and Methods section at the end of the article. The current Figs. 2 and 3 need to be switched to the end of the article to be Figs. 10 and 11, and the correct Figs. 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 are the current Figs. 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 11.

The original article [1] has been corrected.
