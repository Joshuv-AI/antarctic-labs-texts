Research on coal-rock boundary identification based on the morphological sobel algorithm
Authors: Guohui Chen, Yilai Wang, Shengwei Song, Wenhua Yang
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractDue to the harsh underground environment during coal mining, the quality of images collected by cameras is not sufficient, and the acquired images are greatly affected by noise, affecting visual observation; to a certain extent, subsequent intelligent mining is limited. A morphological Sobel coal-rock boundary recognition algorithm is proposed according to the different gray levels of coal-rock images to solve the problem of coal image quality. First, the details of the coal and rock images are smoothly preprocessed to improve the contrast between the feature boundaries and surrounding pixels, and the gray-level adaptive threshold is applied after processing. Morphological corrosion theory is used to process the morphological structure in an image, and the corresponding boundary in the image is extracted for recognition. Compared with the boundary points identified by each algorithm, the area error of coal and rock identification is calculated by using the boundary point fitting curve. The morphological Sobel algorithm is used to calculate the identification area error of coal and rock at different angles according to the camera range. The experimental results show that the boundaries identified by the morphological Sobel algorithm have the best degree of overlap with the boundaries of the original image. The identification error area is only about 10% of the Sobel operator and Canny operator algorithm. Monitoring coal and rock specimens can enable the effective identification of coal and rock boundaries from various angles.Keywords: Coal-rock image, Boundary recognition, Morphology, Sobel algorithmSubject terms: Biogeochemistry, Solid Earth sciences

Introduction

Coal-rock identification technology is the core technology used to realize the intelligentization of shearers1–4. The purpose of coal-rock interface identification is to use a certain method to distinguish the coal seam and rock layer, which is convenient for adjusting the height of the shearer drum, is conducive to improving the coal mining efficiency, and avoids damage when the drum teeth cut into the rock layer, which affects the dust concentration of the coal face, thus affecting the visualization of the working face. At present, there are two methods for identifying coal-rock boundaries: vibration, cutting force and other contact recognition methods for mining equipment. Another method is based on noncontact identification of coal and rock, including infrared image recognition, image texture feature recognition, acoustic detection and other methods for identifying coal and rock.The unfavorable factors of underground coal such as dark light, uneven illumination, band shadowing greatly make it difficult to recognize the coal rock at the mining workface accurately5.

Reference6,7, aiming at the low success rate of coal-rock identification by using coal-rock images, proposed changing the gray threshold to partition coal-rock by using stratified boundary characteristics to improve coal-rock identification accuracy. References8–10 proposed a method combining machine learning and image processing to identify coal-rock boundaries and constructed a new network model. After training and learning considerable data and images, the boundary recognition of the new model is not affected when the camera shakes, so it has a better recognition ability. British Coal first proposed using artificial rays to measure the thickness of coal seams. Specifically, artificial rays are placed at the detection point, the ray receiver receives the rays coming and going from the coal seam, and the specific thickness of the coal seam is calculated based on the received reflected rays11. The United States Bureau of Mines developed a radar coal seam thickness sensor, which is a noncontact stepped radar sensor used to measure the complex reflection coefficient of the coal-rock interface and solve the problem of the wavelength accuracy of coal seam thickness12. Reference13 makes use of the theoretical knowledge of artificial neural network models. Reference14 proposes a high-order difference median CLBP image feature descriptor, introduces the deep learning perception field theory, and realizes the nonlinear dimensionality reduction of data and deep feature extraction. Reference15 aiming at the problems of poor reliability and universality of sensors in existing coal-rock identification methods, a coal-rock interface recognition system based on machine vision is proposed. In reference16, a simplified method for coal and rock point cloud data based on feature point retention was designed. References17 propose an intelligent method for recognizing coal-rock by fusing multiple preprocessing techniques applied to near-infrared spectra and employing dual attention. References2 offers an efficient hardness identification approach to address the problem of poor real-time performance and accuracy in coal and rock hardness detection. In references18 an image dataset was built for training the universal network model, which using the coal-rock interface identification test-bed, active excitation infrared images of coal-rock were tested with different excitation time, distance, and intensities.

Domestic and foreign scholars mainly use features such as color, grayscale, gradient, and texture to identify the differences between two regions in image processing boundary detection, ultimately achieving regional differentiation. However, due to inconsistent image quality, the detection results are not sufficiently accurate. In this paper, according to the different quality of images collected in different image acquisition environments, an economical and practical coal-rock boundary identification method with good adaptive ability is proposed to improve the efficiency of coal mining.

In the Canny operator boundary detection algorithm, the initial step involves Gaussian filtering. The image is smoothed through Gaussian filtering, noise is eliminated to make the image smoother, and the intensity gradient is calculated. A double threshold is a technique that performs nonmaximum suppression, ensuring the detection of only protruding boundaries through lag tracking22. The Prewitt operator has a poor filtering effect on strong noise around the boundary. The Sobel operator can effectively identify boundaries; however, they are not very clear. The Canny operator is not easily affected by noise interference and can detect boundaries; however, the texture is complex and difficult to filter out. This paper uses the Sobel operator to improve the detection of boundaries in coal-rock images.

The morphological Sobel boundary detection algorithm mainly utilizes different grayscale values and approximate brightness levels, applies Gaussian blur to smooth coal-rock images, and improves the contrast between feature boundaries and surrounding pixels to reduce the impact of noise on boundary extraction during boundary recognition and adaptive thresholding of processed grayscale images. The theory of morphological corrosion is used to process the morphological structure in images, the corresponding boundaries in the images are extracted for recognition purposes, the coal seam area is selected, and the coal seam contour line is drawn on the selected area. Experimental analysis

Boundary points are first calibrated on coal-rock specimens, and three algorithms are used to identify the boundaries of the coal-rock specimens to analyze the accuracy of coal-rock boundary recognition. The resulting boundaries are compared with the original calibration points. A camera is used to capture the coal-rock identification results at different angles on one side of the coal-rock specimen.

A coal-rock recognition test bench and prepared coal-rock specimens are built based on a coal-rock image coal-rock boundary recognition experiment. The placement distance of the camera is 1–1.5 m from the coal-rock specimen. The collection time is 5 min. The structure of the coal-rock identification test bench is shown in Fig. 7.

Structure of coa-rock identification test bed.

The test bench mainly includes a computer, a camera, a camera bracket, a fixed device bracket, coal-rock specimens, and a fill light. The coal-rock specimens are placed on the fixed bracket of the specimens, and the distance between the camera and the coal-rock specimens is adjusted. The camera is connected to the computer, and the computer uses an image acquisition system to achieve online image acquisition. The camera adopts Gewu Youxin YRH600C to capture images, with a resolution of 1400 × 1050 pixels. The error area of the coal seam boundary is calculated based on the coal-rock specimen image as the coal-rock boundary recognition test image.

Based on the comparison of three algorithms for coal-rock specimens, Sobel operator, Canny operator and morphological Sobel algorithm boundary recognition test research are carried out on the original coal-rock images, and subjective visual evaluation is carried out on the identified images. Conclusion

This paper proposes a morphological Sobel boundary recognition algorithm and uses it to recognize coal seam boundaries. The process of implementing this algorithm includes image preprocessing, morphological dilation and corrosion, and boundary line drawing to achieve coal-rock boundary recognition.
