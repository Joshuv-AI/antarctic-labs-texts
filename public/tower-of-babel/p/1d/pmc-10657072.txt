Reply to the syndemic of “COVID-19 and tuberculosis”
Authors: Rajashri A. Patil, Sahjid S. Mukhida
Published: 2023

Dear Editor,

COVID-19 and tuberculosis, both respiratory system-related syndemic infectious disease, are counted as major cause of the human death worldwide. We recently went through the article wrote on their syndemic infectivity. Authors described that both diseases are related to their transmission and treatment. We also want to give our input as some points which the authors might have missed.[1]

During COVID-19 pandemic, new tuberculosis diagnosis numbers were reduced as per WHO global tuberculosis report. Reduction in those numbers is not a reduction of the disease, but it may be due to fear from the diagnosis of diseases.[2] COVID-19 is respiratory tract infection, and during the pandemic, other systemic infection symptoms were correlated with the COVID-19 pandemic because disease was new and unknown. We were also not aware about the effect of long COVID symptoms in populations. Patients were feeling fearful to visit the hospital as their symptoms can be consider into COVID and patient had to be isolated. The current diagnosed cases of tuberculosis are lower, which can be just the tip of the iceberg. Actual cases may be so high but it hides under the COVID-19 pandemic fear effect. It is also not diagnosed or reported during the pandemic.[3]

Authors also described the relation of tuberculosis with poverty. Malnutrition is a very well-known factor related to tuberculosis and immunity of the person. As per authors’ opinion tuberculosis treatment is expensive and also need nutrition rich diet to make treatment more effective. Tuberculosis drugs are provided free of cost by government and WHO in public health sectors. Also, government provides nutritional diet to tuberculosis patient under various national health program.[4] Government and WHO are trying to eliminate the tuberculosis from the world. National tuberculosis elimination program (NTEP) has target of 2025, and WHO has the target of 2035 for tuberculosis elimination. However, due to COVID-19 pandemic, NTEP may not be achieved on time but we assure that we will achieve the tuberculosis elimination one day in future.[5]
