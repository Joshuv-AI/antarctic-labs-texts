Retraction Note to: Effects of artificial intelligence aibo intervention on alleviating distress and fear in children
Authors: Kyoko Tanaka, Maoko Hayakawa, Chihiro Noda, Akio Nakamura, Chieko Akiyama
Published: 2024

Retraction Note: Child and Adolescent Psychiatry and Mental Health (2022) 16:87 10.1186/s13034-022-00519-1

The authors have retracted this article because the appropriate ethical review for the study was not conducted. Additionally, the patient consent forms used did not meet the necessary requirements for intervention research.

All authors agree to this retraction.
