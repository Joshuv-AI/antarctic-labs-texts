Trends in the Perceived Health Status among Adolescents, 2016–2025
Authors: Doyeon Kim, 도연 김
Published: 2026

The perceived health status among adolescents in 2025 was 72.6% for boys and 63.3% for girls, representing a slight increase compared to 2024. The perceived health status was higher among boys than among girls and higher among middle school students (70.4%) than among high school students (65.5%) (Figures 1, 2).
