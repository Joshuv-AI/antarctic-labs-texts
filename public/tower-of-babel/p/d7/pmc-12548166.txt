Retraction Note: RNF43 ubiquitinates and degrades phosphorylated E-cadherin by c-Src to facilitate epithelial-mesenchymal transition in lung adenocarcinoma
Authors: Yunfeng Zhang, Liangzhang Sun, Xiao Gao, Aining Guo, Yan Diao, Yang Zhao
Published: 2025

Retraction: BMC Cancer 19, 670 (2018)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12885-019-5880-1

The Editor has retracted this article. After publication, concerns were raised regarding some of the data presented in the figures, specifically:

Figure 1D E-Cad image appears highly similar to negative Snail staining in Fig. 4 of [1];

Figure 3 A p-Src lane appears blurred

Figure 3 A Actin blot appears highly similar to lanes 2–6 of the ZEB1 blot in Fig. 4 A of [2];

Figure 3D E-Cad blot appears highly similar to Fig. 6 A p-Src;

Figure 4 A control A549 cells appear highly similar to mesenchymal cells in Fig. 1 of [3];

Figure 6 C and G images appear to partially overlap with Fig. 1 of [4], and appear to contain duplicated features within the images.

The Editor therefore no longer has confidence in the presented data.

Yang Zhao has not explicitly stated whether they agree with this retraction. None of the other authors have responded to any correspondence from the editor or publisher about this retraction.
