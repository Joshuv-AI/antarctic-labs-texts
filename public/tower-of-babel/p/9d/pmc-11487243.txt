Author Correction: Time-series sewage metagenomics distinguishes seasonal, human-derived and environmental microbial communities potentially allowing source-attributed surveillance
Authors: Ágnes Becsei, Alessandro Fuschi, Saria Otani, Ravi Kant, Ilja Weinstein, Patricia Alba, József Stéger, Dávid Visontai, Christian Brinch, Miranda de Graaf, Claudia M E Schapendonk, Antonio Battisti, Alessandra De Cesare, Chiara Oliveri, Fulvia Troja, Tarja Sironen, Olli Vapalahti, Frédérique Pasquali, Krisztián Bányai, Magdolna Makó, Péter Pollner, Alessandra Merlotti, Marion Koopmans, Istvan Csabai, Daniel Remondini, Frank M Aarestrup, Patrick Munk
Published: 2024

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-024-51957-8, published online 30 August 2024

The published version of the article contained an error in the Results section ‘Abundance and diversity of recovered genomes’ in that in the fourth sentence of the fifth paragraph the bacterial genus name was misspelled (changed from ‘Aliorcobacter’ to ‘Aliarcobacter’).

The published version of the article contained an error in the Results section ‘Community detection in network graphs can assist source attributing genomes and AMR’ in that in the second sentence of the fourth paragraph an incorrect figure was referenced (changed from ‘Fig. 4b’ to ‘Fig. 3b’).

The published version of the article contained an error in the Results section ‘Community detection in network graphs can assist source attributing genomes and AMR’ in that in the second sentence of the fifth paragraph an incorrect figure was referenced (changed from ‘Fig. 4a’ to ‘Fig. 3a’).

The published version of the article contained an error in the Results section ‘Community detection in network graphs can assist source attributing genomes and AMR’ in that in the second sentence of the eighth paragraph an incorrect figure was referenced (changed from ‘Fig. 5a’ to ‘Fig. 4a’).

The published version of the article contained an error in the Results section ‘Community detection in network graphs can assist source attributing genomes and AMR’ in that in the first sentence of the tenth paragraph an incorrect figure was referenced (changed from ‘Fig. 5b’ to ‘Fig. 4b’).

This has all been corrected in the HTML and PDF versions.
