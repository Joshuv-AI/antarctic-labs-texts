Correction: Male accessory breast carcinoma: a rare case report and literature review
Authors: Cheng Luo, Songli Wu, Ying Zeng, Song Zhao
Published: 2025

The order of authors in the author list of the published paper was erroneously given as:

“Cheng Luo, Songli Wu, Song Zhao and Ying Zeng”

The correct author list reads:

“Cheng Luo, Songli Wu, Ying Zeng and Song Zhao”

The Author contributions statement was erroneously given as “CL: Writing – original draft. SW: Writing – original draft, Writing – review & editing. SZ: Writing – review & editing, Supervision. YZ: Writing –original draft”. The correct Author contributions statement is “CL: Writing – original draft. SW: Writing – original draft, Writing – review & editing. YZ: Writing – original draft. SZ: Writing – review & editing, Supervision”.

Author “CL and SW contributed equally to this work and share first authorship” was erroneously omitted as equal contributing author.

The original version of this article has been updated.
