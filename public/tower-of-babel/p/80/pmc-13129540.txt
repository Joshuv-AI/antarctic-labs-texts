Correction to “Nascent transcriptome of embryonic genome activation reveals a regulatory axis linking transcriptional priming to early lineage specification in mouse embryos”
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: Yue Hu, Yuxiang Wang, Maosheng Ye, Yuanlin He, Zhangyi Ouyang, Chao Ren, Tianyin Miao, Siqi Wang, Ou Zhong, Li Liu, Wenjie Shu, Ran Huo, Nascent transcriptome of embryonic genome activation reveals a regulatory axis linking transcriptional priming to early lineage specification in mouse embryos, Nucleic Acids Research, Volume 54, Issue 5, 24 March 2026, gkag173, https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkag173

The authors have noticed that the colours in the legend of Supplementary Fig. S4D, presenting a comparison of H3K27me3 modifications between PAGs and other genes, were inadvertently reversed. A corrected version of Supplementary Fig. S4D has been prepared with the proper colour indication and has replaced the existing file.

This change does not affect the results, discussion and conclusions presented in the article. The published article has been updated.
