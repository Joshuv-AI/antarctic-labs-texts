Obstacle crossing stability research of the swing arm deformable tracked robot in large scale sheep farm and cold climate
Authors: Feng Bai, Haifeng Yuan, Za Kan, Minglu Zhang, Xiaojun Zhang
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
The conventional tracked robot has poor obstacle crossing ability and stability when facing special terrain in north China especially in Xinjiang large scale sheep farm, and the practical application effect in winter cold climate is not considered. This paper designs a swing arm deformable tracked robot. Solves the centroid coordinates and the swing arm position, analyzes the robot obstacle crossing ability in sheep farm special terrain. Based on the stability cone theory, judges the robot obstacle crossing stability. Simulates the cold climate condition, sets the smooth surface dynamic and static frictional coefficient of steps, slopes and gullies model, carries out obstacle crossing and stability simulations. Develops the robot and swing arm demonstrated experiment in sheep farm with height about 15 cm step, tilt about 25° slope and depth about 15 cm gully under − 25 °C cold winter outdoor environment. Verifies that the robot can dynamically form multiple support points through the collaborative control of the four swing arms and the main track as well as the collaboration between the swing arms, ensuring the balance and stability of obstacle crossing in cold climate.

Introduction

With the in-depth study of robot theory, the robot is gradually applied to various industries. Due to excellent obstacle crossing capability and terrain adaptability, the tracked robot can assist even replace human to complete inspection and operation tasks in relatively complex environment1,2. The tracked robot can be applied in agricultural field and animal husbandry enterprise for collecting and analyzing the operational condition and environmental parameter, which has high research value and application prospect3–5.

In recent years, the domestic and foreign scholars carried out tracked robot and mechanical equipment technology research. For example, Mateusz et al.6 put forward the tracked robot with omnidirectional driving system, which could move to other direction without changing the robot posture, exhibited omnidirectional movement characteristic in different terrain environments. Bai et al.7 put forward the adaptive tracking tracked robot, which could adjust robot structure based on the obstacle constraint and energy conversion, had outstanding terrain passability and tracking capability. Xu et al.8 put forward the biomimetic rescue tracked robot with four channel double joystick structure. By analyzing the robot climbing process, calculated the robot obstacle crossing height, the robot could be applied to various search and rescue tasks. Li et al.9 put forward the passive swing arm tracked robot. According to the relationship between obstacle height and swing arm size, analyzed the single step obstacle crossing process, established simplified dynamic model and revealed the application potential in different fields. Liu et al.10 put forward the tracked pipeline inspection robot. By analyzing the robot adaptability about different pipeline diameters, derived the torque output, established an internal pipeline structure model, analyzed robot obstacle crossing performance, and tested maximum obstacle crossing height. Hou Xuchao et al.11 put forward the steering control theory of tracked vehicle, designed the nonlinear automatic anti-interference control device based on kinematic analysis, checked the anti-skid control effect, improved the vehicle steering stability. Zhang Yue et al.12 put forward internal inspection tracked robot of wind electricity blade. Based on the kinematic characteristic, used multi-freedom joint and tracked mobile platform to complete the simulation and experiment of turning and climbing functions. Wu Zhengmin et al.13 put forward the crawler self-propelled tea-picker. By establishing a simulation model and conducting the dynamic analysis, verified the rationality of the structural design, studied obstacle crossing and stability performance, determined the maximum slope angle and obstacle crossing height. Wang Faan et al.14 put forward the tracked vehicle track and control theory, established the vehicle model and planed the ideal target through instantaneous rotation center, adjusted the best heading at this coordinate, experimentally tested the accuracy. Huang Hua et al.15 put forward the wall climbing robot, calculated and analyzed the maximum height, critical state and optimal distribution relationship by establishing the dynamic model of obstacle crossing and adsorption compensation. Han Changjie et al.16 put forward the crawler walking system of cotton topping machine, determined the mechanical structure composition and parameters, established a kinematic model, obtained the path deviation values, and successfully tracked the path by fuzzy PID.

Domestic and foreign scholars had carried out the theoretical and applied research on swing arm or tracked robot, and achieved the certain research results17–20. But there are still improved spaces and explored areas. For example, there are few theoretical analyses or engineering applications of the swing arm tracked robot in large scale sheep farm, and there is no comprehensive analysis about the stable movement and obstacle crossing of the swing arm tracked robot under cold climate condition similar in Xinjiang large scale sheep farm. The joint structure and driving mechanism of robot swing arm are also complicated, which affect the movement or operation of robot in large scale sheep farm. Aiming at the influence of winter cold climate, facing the special terrain such as step, slope and gully in large scale sheep farm. The conventional swing arm or tracked robot has the problem of poor obstacle crossing ability and stability. This paper develops a swing arm deformable tracked robot. The robot system adopts three layer nested method, optimizes the swing arm joint structure and transmission mechanism. The arm swing and main track are separated with power, the front and rear swing arms cooperate with each other, the connecting rod and small wheel of swinging arm can stable support and assist in moving. Then solves the centroid coordinates and swing arm position, conducts a comprehensive and systematic analysis of the swing arm deformable tracked robot obstacle crossing. Based on the stability cone theory, judges the robot obstacle crossing stability. Through simulating cold climate and sheep farm environment, establishes model such as sheep farm steps, slopes and gullies, sets the smooth surface dynamic and static friction coefficient, this paper carries out obstacle crossing and stability simulation. Finally, in cold climate and sheep farm special environment, conducts the robot demonstrated experiment based on multi-arm swing collaborative movement and support.

In Section "Swing arm deformable tracked robot structural composition and transmission principle", this paper proposes an independently designed and developed swing arm deformable tracked robot, and explains the structural composition and transmission principle. In Section "Swing arm deformable tracked robot obstacle crossing movement theoretical analysis", this paper analyzes the obstacle crossing movement theory of the swing arm deformable tracked robot, specifically describes the obstacle crossing process of the robot under six road conditions including steps, slopes and gullies. In Section "Swing arm deformable tracked robot obstacle crossing stability theoretical analysis", according to the basic theory of the stability cone, this paper analyzes the obstacle crossing stability of the swing arm deformable tracked robot under various road conditions. In Section "Swing arm deformable tracked robot obstacle crossing stability simulation", this paper constructs a robot model, simulates cold climate parameters, and carries out the robot simulation such as crossing obstacle process, parameters variation and stability cone numerical calculation. In Section "Swing arm deformable tracked robot experiment in sheep farm", this paper conducts the robot demonstration experiment based on the multi-swing arm cooperation and support in large scale sheep farm and Xinjiang cold climate. Finally, the most innovative contribution of this research is the elucidation that how the robot can use robot manipulator to assist obstacle crossing, how to deal with various obstacles from what angle to achieve efficient and stable obstacle crossing.

Swing arm deformable tracked robot structural composition and transmission principle

This paper based on the swing arm deformable tracked mobile platform, can equip the upper box with rotate lifting or lowering module and detection or identification function. And develops patrol inspection robot to adapt the multi-terrain characteristics of large scale sheep farm. The specific swing arm deformable tracked robot 3d model and experimental prototype of independent design and development are shown in Figs. 1 and 2.Fig. 1Swing arm deformable tracked robot 3d model.Fig. 2Swing arm deformable tracked robot experimental prototype.

Swing arm deformable tracked robot 3d model.

Swing arm deformable tracked robot experimental prototype.

Where the robot mobile platform uses sleeve axle structure, which separates the rotation angles adjustment of four swing arms from the conventional movement of main track. Achieved collaborative control of the four swing arms and main track as well as collaboration between the swing arms, enabling that the arm support multi-posture changes. Specifically, which adapts to the various terrains of large scale sheep farm, allows swing arm to adjust the posture at any time without affecting the robot normal movement, balance and stability. The robot upper box equips with servo structure, omnidirectional rotates near narrow passage in sheep farm and shed, prevents steering difficulty caused by insufficient turning radius. A scissor lifting or lowering mechanism is integrated inside the upper box, achieves vertical lifting or lowering movement and expands the robot observation range or operating space.

Additionally, the robot adopts modular transmission system. Through the independently swing arm shafts and sleeve axles, isolates the main track drive system, swing arm track drive system and swing arm angle adjustment system, forms a transmission system both collaboration and independence. Specifically, the main track drive system connects reduction motor through hollow sleeve drive shaft, uses the dual-side independent drive and differential control to achieve steering. The swing arm track drive system outputs power through reduction motor, and utilizes bevel gear steering and transferring power, achieves the swing arm track rotating. The swing arm angle adjustment system uses angle adjustment shaft to transfer the rotation angle outputted by the servo, elevates the swing arm angle.

Stability cone model establishment

Fundamental principle involves transforming the relationship of the robot polygonal structure and centroid projection to a conical space, and decides the robot stability by the parameters such as stability angle21,22. Specifically, this paper uses the stability cone theory, taking the robot centroid as vertex and the track boundary points as angle points, connecting angle points to form the underside, and the angle points of the polygon are sequentially labeled in clockwise direction. Conclusions

The conventional tracked robot has poor obstacle crossing stability when facing special terrain in large scale sheep farm, and practical application in winter cold climate is not concerned. This paper uses the independently designed and developed robot to analyze obstacle crossing ability and stability. Sets dynamic and static friction coefficient, carries out the simulation and experiment according to the actual operation environment of sheep farm. The specific research content mainly includes:

This paper analyzes the robot centroid coordinate, obtains the robot centroid equation, establishes the theory of swing arm rotation angle and obstacle target, determines the better robot obstacle crossing method as well as the centroid coordinate and swing arm position. Develops stable model by the stability cone theory, derives the condition of robot crossing obstacle.

This paper simulates the cold climate environment, sets dynamic and static friction coefficient, carries out simulation test of steps, slopes and gullies in sheep farm. Comprehensive depicts and analyzes obstacle crossing and stability of road single step, stair multi-steps, slope forward climbing, slope lateral climbing, unilateral half-gully and full side width-gully.

In Xinjiang winter large scale sheep farm, this paper carries out the robot demonstration experiment. Selects typical obstacles such as steps, slopes and gullies in sheep farm, utilizes the support of robot swing arms to achieve balance. Verifies the accuracy of theoretical analysis and model simulation, ensures obstacle crossing stability of robot in cold climate.

Subsequently, all authors will continuously optimize the structural design and control principle of swing arm system to improve the robot obstacle crossing success rate in large scale sheep farm and winter cold climate. At the same time, this paper needs to explain that the current trend of sheep farm steps, slopes and gullies obstacle crossing is better. And in the subsequent research, it is also necessary to increase the comprehensive analysis of various other obstacles, especially the theoretical evaluation of mobile obstacles, such as inertia, impact, track sliding and dynamic load transfer.

Moreover, although this paper has carried out a relatively comprehensive analysis of the robot obstacle crossing stability in sheep farm, there are still the limitations and have some aspects that need to be improved in future research. For example, there is a lack of control group experiment for the obstacle crossing stability, it is necessary to increase the analysis of obstacle crossing combination with forward slope and lateral slope, evaluates the strategies and risks followed by the robot obstacle crossing process, quantitative explores the innovative control theory method and evaluates physical parameter influencing factors. These shortcomings are also the directions and goals that need to be explored more deeply in subsequent research.
