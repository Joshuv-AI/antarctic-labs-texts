Correction: Characterization and transcriptomic analysis provide novel insights into the sexual differentiation of the dioecious plant Bama Huoma (Cannabis sativa)
Authors: Xiaofang Liao, Jie Zheng, Wenhuan Hou, Xingfu Tang, Bangbang Huang, Jie Tan, Yanhong Zhao, Kailiang Teng
Published: 2025

Correction : BMC Plant Biol 25, 1108 (2025)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12870-025–07096-3

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors found an inconsistency between the scale bar of Figure 1 and the value stated in the figure caption. The scale bar in the figure denotes 1mm while the caption indicated 2mm. The figure is now corrected.
