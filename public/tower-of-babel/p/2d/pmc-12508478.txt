Dynamic surface control algorithm of flexible manipulator driven by position and velocity disturbance factors
Authors: Shixi Tang, Yu Wang, Hongyong Deng, Yaser A Nanehkaran
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractClassic adaptive control systems for the dynamic surface of flexible manipulators suffer from insufficient convergence accuracy for the manipulator’s link angular position parameters and rotor angular velocity parameters. To address this issue, a new dynamic surface control algorithm for flexible manipulators driven by position and velocity perturbation factors is proposed. Specifically, two linear factors, , an offset factor, , and two functional factors, , are designed. By optimizing the virtual control law for dynamic surface control, the convergence accuracy of the position and velocity parameters is effectively improved. Compared with the virtual control law algorithm for classic flexible manipulators, the convergence accuracy of the link angular position parameters is improved from 84% to 98%, and the convergence accuracy of the rotor angular velocity parameters is improved from 90% to 98%, effectively enhancing the manipulator’s stability.Keywords: Position disturbance factor, Velocity disturbance factor, Dynamic surface control, Virtual control law, Linear factor, Migration factorSubject terms: Electrical and electronic engineering, Applied mathematics

Introduction

Flexible manipulators are used in many aspects of life. The core is to achieve precise control of the manipulator through the controller. The flexible manipulators currently available include artificial muscle-type flexible manipulators with high grasping ability1, flexible manipulators used for rehabilitation training2. The controller designs for flexible manipulators include a control design for a two-finger flexible manipulator was proposed to solve the grasping task requirements for complex-shaped objects in the industrial field3, a control design for a tendon-driven flexible three-finger manipulator was proposed to solve the grasping problem of objects with different scales and grasping forces4, a nonlinear sliding mode controller based on the output redefinition method was proposed to solve the end control problem of a two-arm flexible manipulator when the model parameters are uncertain5.

Different controller designs still face the problem of low efficiency in complex task control while controlling the manipulator. Specifically, the problem is that the manipulator’s stability control is poor due to insufficient convergence accuracy of the manipulator parameters. Researchers use deep learning, reinforcement learning and other methods to study this type of problem from the perspective of model or framework and algorithm design proposed a sim2real(simulation to reality) decision model based on feature semantic space and an AMDDPG strategy optimization algorithm to solve the problems of low migration accuracy and slow learning rate when the unmanned system decision model migrates from a virtual scene to a real scene. Make the simulation effect of the manipulator close to reality6. By combining the cascade feature enhancement learning system and elastic net regression, a new robot arm motion control strategy is proposed to solve the problem of insufficient robot arm trajectory tracking control accuracy and achieve the purpose of improving the robot arm trajectory control efficiency7. By designing a robust backstepping controller, the problem of insufficient trajectory tracking accuracy of the robot manipulator under parameter uncertainty is solved, and the Lyapunov theory is used to ensure system stability8. By combining statistical linearization with optimization of data acquisition methods, the problem of determining the nonlinear joint stiffness of industrial robots is solved, thereby achieving the purpose of improving the accuracy of joint trajectory tracking9. By combining physical information modeling and robust control algorithms, the conflict between soft robot dynamics modeling and control is resolved, achieving the goal of balancing model simplicity and anti-interference capabilities against complex environmental factors10. By combining refined modeling and composite control architecture, the problems of time-varying parameters and robust control of flexible joint robots can be solved, and the anti-interference ability of the robot’s flexible joints can be improved11. By decoupling task-space force control and zero-space joint compliance, a hierarchical control framework is proposed to improve the force tracking accuracy of the end controller and the safe operation of joint motion without a dynamic model12. By using time-delay estimation, gain matrix design and LQR optimal control, real-time compensation of interference factors is carried out to solve the problem of environmental factors interfering with the robot arm, so as to improve the operation stability of the robot arm13. Through zero-point moment and fuzzy theory, GA-PSO and PID controller are combined. The problem of robot yaw in complex environments is solved and the tracking accuracy of the robot is improved14. By combining fuzzy logic and adaptive control technology, the problem of unknown actuator failure in uncertain nonlinear systems is solved to achieve the purpose of asymptotic stability control of the system15. Introduced piecewise differentiable functions to eliminate the singularity of the function after the fractional exponential function is derived, and used neural network technology to estimate the function, solving the stability control problem of non-strict feedback nonlinear systems within a certain period of time, thereby improving the stability of the system16. During the operation of the control system, various interference factors will be encountered. Although the interference factors can be detected by the observer in the control system, the observer will also produce inaccurate results due to external disturbances, so the actual interference results are difficult to detect. The estimated interference weakens the influence of external disturbances and reduces the influence of the inaccurate results produced by the detector due to disturbances, making the estimated disturbance results more representative.

The above solution to the stability control problem of the manipulator does not simultaneously solve the problem of insufficient convergence accuracy of the angular position of the manipulator connecting rod and the angular velocity of the rotor. In order to solve this problem, this paper designs the corresponding position and velocity disturbance factors for the angular position of the connecting rod and the angular velocity of the rotor, and designs the corresponding dynamic surface control algorithm and the appropriate virtual control law, so that the convergence accuracy of the two parameters of the angular position of the connecting rod and the angular velocity of the rotor is higher, and finally achieves the purpose of improving the stability of the flexible manipulator.

Dynamic surface control algorithm driven by position and velocity disturbance factors

In order to solve the problem of insufficient convergence accuracy of the angular position of the connecting rod and the angular velocity of the rotor, position and velocity disturbance factors are designed. Position and velocity dynamic disturbance factors refer to factors that affect the angular position of the connecting rod and the angular velocity of the rotor. This paper designs two linear factors and an offset factor , as well as two function factors , which effectively improve the parameter convergence accuracy and reduce the instability of the flexible manipulator. Conclusion

This paper proposes a new dynamic surface control algorithm driven by position and velocity disturbance factors. The algorithm improves the virtual control rate and integrates offset factors, linear factors and function factors to improve the convergence accuracy of the angular position of the manipulator link and the angular velocity of the manipulator rotor. It is applied to the control of a two-degree-of-freedom flexible manipulator. The simulation results show that the improved controller algorithm has excellent convergence accuracy of the angular position of the manipulator link and the angular velocity of the manipulator rotor. red This algorithm can be applied to the controller design of flexible manipulators, which can improve the picking stability of manipulators, achieve the purpose of improving manipulator control efficiency, and significantly improve the control accuracy and stability of flexible manipulators. Therefore, the algorithm has significantly improved the control accuracy and stability of the flexible manipulator. In future research, the optimization of the angular velocity parameters of the flexible manipulator link will be considered, and the stability and accuracy of the flexible manipulator control will be improved by further reducing the phenomenon of insufficient parameter convergence accuracy.
