Correction to: Forensic efficiency and population genetic construction of Guizhou Gelao minority from Southwest China revealed by a panel of 23 autosomal STR loci
Published: 2024

This is a correction to: Siyu Chai, Shuhua Li, Ruxin Zhu, Li Luo, Kaiqin Chen, Yinlei Lei, Weihong Wan, Xijie Hu, Shiquan Liu, Pengyu Chen, Forensic efficiency and population genetic construction of Guizhou Gelao minority from Southwest China revealed by a panel of 23 autosomal STR loci, Forensic Sciences Research, Volume 9, Issue 2, June 2024, https://doi.org/10.1093/fsr/owad058

The following changes have been made to the originally published article.

In section Results, under heading Allele frequency and forensic parameters of 23 STRs, the fifth sentence: “A total of 264 alleles were identified with allelic frequencies ranging from 0.001 (26, Penta D) to 0.5255 (8, TPOX).” has been emended to read: “A total of 264 alleles were identified with allelic frequencies ranging from 0.001 (26, Penta E) to 0.5255 (8, TPOX).”

Under heading Comparisons with the Chinese populations, the fourth paragraph, first sentence: “Our PCA results showed that 90.8% of the genetic variation was extracted from the first three components (PC1:67.656%; PC2:14.374%; PC3:8.459%) (Figure 3).” should be corrected to read: “Our PCA results showed that 90.5% of the genetic variation was extracted from the first three components (PC1:67.656%; PC2:14.374%; PC3:8.459%) (Figure 3).”

In the legend of Figure 2, the second and third sentences: “The two-dimensional plot of (A) PCA1 and PCA2, (B) PCA1 and PCA3, and (C) PCA2 and PCA3. (D) The three-dimensional plot of principal component analysis results.” have been deleted

These emendations have been made within the article.
