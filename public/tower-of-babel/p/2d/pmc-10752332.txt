SMART (SMartphone-Assisted frugal Retinopathy of premaTurity screening)
Authors: Prithvi Chandrakanth, Puja Maitra, Padmavathi Sivakumar, Parag K Shah, Venkatapathy Narendran
Published: 2023

Dear Editor,

In India, there is a rising disparity between the increasing number of preterm babies requiring retinopathy of prematurity (ROP) screening and the paucity of ophthalmologists engaged in ROP care.[12] SMartphone-Assisted Retinopathy of premaTurity screening (SMART) is an innovative technique aiding young ophthalmologists in ROP screening, in accurate referrals, especially in cases of doubtful type 1 ROP. SMART comprises two frugal do-it-yourself (DIY) hand-held noncontact devices – Trash To Treasure Retcam (T3R)[34] and anterior segment photography with intraocular (ASPI) lens.[5]

Two hundred and ninety-eight eyes were screened over a month with indirect ophthalmoscopy (IDO) and additionally imaged with SMART [Table 1]. The clinical picture in images taken by T3R correlated with the stage noted when babies were referred to base hospital (BH) for evaluation/treatment [Fig. 1a and b].

Babies requiring treatment may not always be fit for discharge/transfer from the neonatal intensive care unit (NICU). SMART has helped in such scenarios for planning the treatment as these images can be sent for real-time opinion from an expert at BH via WhatsApp messaging system. The field of view of T3R is determined by the lens used (46°/60° using a 20 D lens). However, it is easy to use this device in one hand, while using the other hand to rotate the globe and perform scleral indentation [Fig. 1c and d]. We have documented few anterior segment findings with ASPI, such as tunica vasculosa lentis [Fig. 1e and f]. SMART allows real-time display and documentation and has a great impact in the NICU setting in sensitizing the pediatrician, nurses, and in counseling parents [Fig. 1g and h]. It helps in comparison of images from the previous visit, and is a great teaching tool for the accompanying residents/fellows.

SMART is a unique smartphone-based system with two DIY devices finding their extended application in imaging ROP, in less than 1000 rupees investment, ideal for any ophthalmologist, especially for a resident/fellow. This way, we can add more hands in screening more eyes, filter necessary referrals, decrease the economic burden on the family, and eliminate blindness from ROP.
