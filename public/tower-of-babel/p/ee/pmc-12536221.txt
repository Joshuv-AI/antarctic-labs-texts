Retraction: The biological functions and related signaling pathways of SPON2
Authors: Frontiers Editorial Office
Published: 2025

The journal retracts the 09 January 2024 article cited above.

Following publication, concerns were raised regarding the scientific validity of the article. An investigation was conducted in accordance with Frontiers’ policies.

It was found that the complaints were valid and that the article does not meet the standards of editorial and scientific soundness for Frontiers in Oncology; therefore, the article has been retracted.

This retraction was approved by the Chief Editors of Frontiers in Oncology and the Chief Executive Editor of Frontiers. The authors do not agree to this retraction.

Frontiers would like to thank the concerned readers who contacted us regarding the published article.
