Retraction Note: The invisible architects: microbial communities and their transformative role in soil health and global climate changes
Authors: Sajid Iqbal, Farida Begum, Benedictor Alexander Nguchu, Uzabakiriho Pierre Claver, Peter Shaw
Published: 2026

Retraction Note to: Environmental Microbiome (2025) 20:36 10.1186/s40793-025-00694-6

After publication, concerns were raised with the Editor-in-Chief and the publisher regarding substantial similarities between this article and previously published work [1] authored by a different research group. The Editor-in-Chief therefore considers this work redundant.

Peter Shaw, Sajid Iqbal, Benedictor Alexander Nguchu and Uzabakiriho Pierre Claver agree to this retraction. Farida Begum did not explicitly state whether they agreed or disagreed with this retraction.
