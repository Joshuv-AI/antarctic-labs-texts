Correction: AHSP as a novel non-invasive urinary biomarker for prostate cancer
Authors: Chen Wang, Yang-yang Zhang, Hong-qun Wang, Cong Huang, Na Yu, Huang Yan, Ya-nan Zhu, De-lun Yan
Published: 2026

Correction: BMC Cancer 26, 574 (2026)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12885-026-15796-8

Following publication of the original article [1], the author reported that the incubation times reported in the “Urinary ELISA” subsection of the Materials and Methods section are incorrect. The following details should be corrected.

1. “After sealing and incubating at 37°C for 150 min”.

should be corrected to:

After sealing and incubating at 37 °C for 30 min.

2. “incubated at 37°C for another 150 minutes”.

should be corrected to:

incubated at 37 °C for another 30 min.

3. “incubated at 37°C in the dark for another 150 minutes to develop color”.

should be corrected to:

incubated at 37 °C in the dark for another 10 min to develop color.

The original article has been updated.
