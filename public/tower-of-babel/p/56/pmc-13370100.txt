Correction: HealthData@MAD-R&I: Protocol for Design and Development of a Regional Health Data Infrastructure to Enable Secondary Use of Health Data in Research and Innovation
Authors: Montserrat León-García, Sergio Álvarez-Pérez, Janire Gesto-Gómez, Clara Urbano-Molina, Sonia Soto-Díaz, Juan Cárdenas-Valladolid, Luis Rodríguez-Rodríguez, Antonio Díaz-Holgado, Isabel del Cura-González, Javier De La Cruz-Bertolo, Noelia García-Barrio, Juan Luis Cruz-Bermúdez, Cristina García-Fernández, Carlos Rodríguez-Antolín, Laila García-Aldars, Elsa María Moreda-Sánchez, Álvaro Roldán López, José María Veganzones Alonso-Cortés, Ana Isabel Gonzalez Gonzalez, Miguel A Salinero-Fort, HealthData@MAD-R&I Working Group
Published: 2026

In “HealthData@MAD-R&I: Protocol for Design and Development of a Regional Health Data Infrastructure to Enable Secondary Use of Health Data in Research and Innovation” [1], the authors made one correction. The following sentence was removed from the Funding section:

This project has received funding from Horizon Europe programme under grant agreement no. 101080711 (SPIDERR).

The correction will appear in the online version of the paper on the JMIR Publications website, together with the publication of this correction notice. Because this was made after submission to PubMed, PubMed Central, and other full-text repositories, the corrected article has also been resubmitted to those repositories.
