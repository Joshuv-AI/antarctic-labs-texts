A study on the growth and physiological traits of Leymus chinensis in artificial grasslands under exogenous hormone regulation
Authors: Jifu Chen, Qiang Chen, Xiangyang Hou
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractLeymus chinensis is a dominant perennial grass species in the Eurasian steppe, valued for its ecological and economic importance. Conducting research on the formation mechanism and regulatory approaches of artificial grassland productivity is the great significance for improving grassland productivity, and restoration and reconstruction of degraded grassland. Select three-year-old sheep grassland as the experimental field, diethyl aminoethyl hexanoate (DA-6), brassinolide (BR), and melatonin (MT) were sprayed to explore the regulatory mechanisms and differential efficacy of different phytohormones on grassland productivity. MT promotes chlorophyll synthesis, drives enhanced photosynthesis and protein synthesis, DA-6 upregulated photochemical efficiency and improves the stability of light energy conversion. BR enhances water use efficiency (WUE) through synergistic effects of pore regulation. MT demonstrated significant in the accumulation of biomass during the early and middle growth stages, and upgrade the nutritional quality of forage. DA-6 is suitable for biomass accumulation in the later growth stage. BR has outstanding potential in water conservation and stress resistance capacity. This study elucidates the differences in the regulatory effects of different growth regulators on the productivity of sheep pastures, in order to providing technical insights to optimize the technical strategy for grassland restoration and mitigate the contradiction between the degradation of natural grasslands and the development of animal husbandry.Supplementary InformationThe online version contains supplementary material available at 10.1038/s41598-025-14022-y.Keywords: Brassinolactone, Diethyl aminoethyl hexanoate, Melatonin, Photosynthetic leaf gas exchange, Chlorophyll fluorescence, Vegetative growth and biomass, Leymus chinensisSubject terms: Ecology, Physiology, Plant sciences, Ecology

Introduction

With the increasing impact of global climate change and human activities on the natural environment, the degradation of natural grassland in China has become increasingly serious, and the degraded area of natural grassland will account for more than 90% of the total grassland area by the 20201. Therefore, it is particularly important to restore and rebuild the ecological structure and function of natural grassland2, and the establishment of artificial grassland has become a hot issue in the construction of degraded grassland3. Planting artificial grassland on the degraded natural grassland is conducive to the reversal of the degraded state of natural grassland and the reconstruction of ecosystem. In this context, plant hormones, as one of the key factors regulating plant growth and development, play a particularly important role in artificial grassland.

Artificial grassland refers to the stable artificial plant community formed by systematic establishment and planting of herbage varieties with strong ecological adaptability, high yield and excellent quality on cultivated land through scientific planning and management4. As an important agricultural production mode, the production efficiency and sustainability of artificial grassland are also directly related to food security and ecological balance5. Leymus chinensis, a perennial grass species in the genus Leymus of the Poaceae family, is the dominant grass species in the temperate grassland area of China. It is perennial rhizome-type herbage that mainly relies on rhizomes to reproduce asexually under natural conditions, and is a dominant grass species with physiological characteristics, such as drought, cold and salt stress resistance efficiency6. Compared with natural grassland, soil physical and chemical properties of artificial Leymus chinensis grassland have been greatly improved7. Under the same climatic conditions, artificial Leymus chinensis grassland is easy to manage, has long growing period, strong adaptability and high grass yield, which ensures stable and high yield of grass and provides a large number of feed sources for livestock. Alleviate the contradiction between the degradation of natural grassland and the development of animal husbandry, and promote the sustainable development of pastoral areas8.

The key issues in the study of artificial sheep grassland are the formation mechanism and regulation pathway of productivity. Since Leymus chinense has strong root penetration ability through its root system, the root competition among plants will be more intense. This competition leads to uneven distribution of nutrients, which in turn affects overall photosynthetic efficiency and biomass accumulation9. At present, the productivity research of artificial grassland mainly focuses on organic fertilizer, microbial agents, soil improvement, rotational grazing system, leaf fertilizer and other strategies, while plant growth regulator as an economic and effective regulation mode in the application of artificial grassland still needs to be further explored.

Plant growth regulators are artificially synthesized or naturally extracted active substances that can accurately regulate the growth and development the process of plants10, improve the osmotic regulation ability of plants, enhance the defense mechanism of antioxidant enzymes, promote chlorophyll synthesis, and improve the utilization efficiency of photosynthetic radiation. Ultimately, it promotes plant growth and biomass accumulation11. According to its mechanism of action and chemical structure, it is mainly divided into different categories, i.e., auxin, gibberellin, cytokinins, ethylene and new complex regulators. It plays major roles at different plant growth stages. Diethyl aminoethyl hexanoate (DA-6) is a highly efficient and synthetic broad-spectrum plant growth regulator, which regulates plant endogenous hormone balance and metabolic pathways to achieve precise management of crop growth cycle. In the soybean cultivation system, this substance exhibits multi-dimensional physiological regulatory properties, such as significantly promoting soybean plant growth and above-ground dry matter accumulation, increasing leaf chlorophyll content, chlorophyll fluorescence and photosynthetic capacity, thereby accelerating the transfer rate of organic matter from leaves to grains, and improving soybean yield and quality12. Leaf spraying with DA-6 improved the photosynthetic performance, sugar accumulation and total antioxidant capacity of active oxygen scavenging, and significantly alleviated leaf senescence13.

Brassinolide (BR), as a new green and environmentally friendly plant growth regulator, can significantly promote crop growth, improve crop quality and increase crop yield by applying stem and leaf spray, and seed immersion treatment with appropriate concentration14. BR is beneficial to the synthesis of proline of tall fescue, enhances the drought resistance of plants, improve the nitrogen and phosphorus utilization efficiency of tall fescue to a certain extent, improve the biomass and stress resistance of tall fescue, adjusts the distribution strategy and stoichiometric stability of nutrients, and mitigate the adverse effects of water stress15. Appropriate concentration of BR can improve the photosynthetic capacity and reduce the damage to lipid membrane and chlorophyll content in rice leaves16. BR can increase the activity of antioxidant enzymes, improve the stability of cell membrane, inhibit the accumulation of reactive oxygen species (ROS) in maize seedlings under low temperature stress, and reduce abscisic acid levels to maintain hormone balance17.

Melatonin (MT), also known as melotonine or pineal hormone, is a hormone found in many organisms from algae to humans, and is primarily an amine hormone produced by the pineal glands of mammals and humans18. Under drought stress condition, melatonin was applied to improve the water status of wheat leaves and enhance the photosynthetic capacity to reduce the inhibitory effect of stress, significantly upregulate the photosynthetic pigment and soluble protein, and achieve the effect of increasing production and quality of wheat19. Salt stress is a major abiotic stress factor that restricts the growth and yield of maize, and exogenous melatonin can effectively alleviate the inhibition effect of salt stress on maize and promote growth and biomass accumulation by regulating metabolic pathways and activating the expression of antioxidase genes and photosynthetic pigment biosynthesis genes20. The combined treatment of sodium nitroprusside (SNP) and melatonin (MT) on corn plants improved chlorophyll content and WUE, while reducing markers of oxidative stress, and significantly increased plant biomass and productivity under drought conditions21.

The effects of MT and BR on vegetative growth, physiology and biochemistry of Leymus chinensis have been studied, but how DA-6 regulates the growth and physiological characteristics of Leymus chinensis has not been reported. Therefore, we proposed the different hypotheses, such as different growth regulators have different effects on agronomic traits, photosynthetic responses and nutritional quality of Chinese Leymus chinensis at different growth stages, and different growth regulators affects the plant productivity and biomass accumulation through different regulatory mechanisms.

In the present study, MT, DA-6 and BR were used as test hormones to explore the mechanism of artificial Leymus chinensis grassland under the action of different length regulators on agronomic traits, photosynthetic responses, nutritional quality and biomass accumulation, and to screen and compare plant growth regulators at different growth stages of Leymus chinensis, to maximize the promotion effect. This study aims to establish a high-efficiency promoting system for Leymus chinensis growth under plant growth regulators, elucidate the physiological mechanisms and patterns underlying the effects of these regulators on artificial Leymus chinensis grasslands, and thereby provide a theoretical basis for large-scale application of plant hormones to enhance productivity in artificial grasslands and support the restoration and reconstruction of degraded Leymus chinensis ecosystems.

Natural characteristics of the study area

The experimental research area was located at the National Grassland Ecosystem Field Scientific Observation and Research Station (112°19 17.6 "E, 39°59 17.0" N), Youyu County, Shanxi Province, China. It is a typical temperate continental monsoon climate with an altitude of 1348 m. The climatic variables of as average annual temperature (4.7°C), precipitation (435 mm), sunshine duration (2,929 h), and frost-free period (104 d) throughout the year.

Materials and methods

Three-year-old artificial sheep grassland, Diethyl aminoethyl hexanoate (DA-6), Brassinolide (BR), Melatonin (MT) was selected. The optimal concentration of DA-6 (0, 15, 30, 45 mg·L−1) and BR (0, 0.01, 0.1, 1 mg·L−1) were screened in the greenhouse pre-experiment condition. It was found that the plant height and biomass were significantly increased at 15 and 1 mg·L−1 for DA-6 and BR.MT referenced to the application concentration on wheat by Li et al.22. Based on this, the field trial designed four treatment groups, such as DA-6 (15 mg·L−1), BR (1 mg·L−1), MT (100 μmol·L−1), CK (water control), and each treatment was repeated four times. The field trial plots were randomly designed according to the gradient topography, and 4 plots were set up, each of which had 4 plots, a total of 16 plots. The sub-divisions were divided into 4 × 4 m zones, and the isolation zones between the plots were 1.5 m apart. Foliar spray was carried out in the evening when the weather was clear and there was no wind, and the spray treatment was carried out in the greening, tillering and jointing stage. The conventional cultivation measures were controlled in the experimental field.

According to the key stages of nutrient growth of Chinese Leymus chinensis, the indexes were determined and samples collected. It runs from July 1 to August 30, 2024. According to the characteristics of its growth process, the whole observation cycle was divided into three phenological periods, i.e., the initial stage (July 1–July 15), the middle growth stage (July 16–August 15) and the late growth stage (August 16–August 30).

Determination of agronomic traits

A total of 15 Leymus chinensis plants were randomly selected from each treatment group and the average values were measured repeatedly to obtain the plant height, stem length, leaf length, leaf width, leaf area and leaf number23.

Aboveground biomass: The 1 × 1 m2 sample box was used to cut the above-ground part in the middle of growth period, and was brought back to the laboratory to remove impurities and weigh fresh weight, then dried at 65℃ to constant weight as aboveground biomass, and the average value was repeated five times.

Photofluorometry

The CIRAS-4F Portable Photosynthesis System was used to measure key photosynthetic parameters, including net photosynthetic rate (Pn), transpiration rate (Tr), stomatal conductance (Gs), intercellular CO₂ concentration (Ci), vapor pressure deficit (VPD), and water use efficiency (WUE). The chlorophyll fluorescence measurement was performed by FMS-2 portable pulse modulated fluorometer. The parameters under light adaptation were as stable fluorescence under light (Fs), maximum fluorescence under light (Fm′), minimum fluorescence under light (Fo′), actual photochemical efficiency of PSII (Fv′/Fm′), actual photochemical quantum yield of PSII (ΦPSII), electron transport rate (ETR), maximum photochemical efficiency of PSII (Fv/Fm). Dark adaptation parameters, such as minimum fluorescence (Fo), maximum fluorescence (Fm), and maximum photochemical efficiency of PSII (Fv/Fm).

Nutrient quality determination of forage

Crude protein, fat and ash were determined by the SKD-200 Kjeldahl nitrogen analyzer, SZF-06A fat analyzer and Daxu Yanguang SX2-4-10A, box-type resistance furnace.

The crude fibre (CF), acid detergent fibre (ADF), neutral detergent fibre (NDF), acid scrubber lignin (ADL) were quantified by the normal form washing method and ANKOM2000 fiber analyzer.

Data analysis

The experimental data were compiled and summarized using Microsoft Excel 2023 (Microsoft Corporation, Redmond, WA, USA; https://www.microsoft.com). IBM SPSS Statistics 13.0 (IBM Corp., Armonk, NY, USA; https://www.ibm.com/analytics/spss-statistics-software) was employed to conduct one-way ANOVA analysis on agronomic traits, including photosynthetic responses, fluorescence parameters, and nutritional quality indices. Graphs were generated using GraphPad Prism 10.1.2 (GraphPad Software, San Diego, CA, USA; https://www.graphpad.com/prism/).

In the middle growth stage, MT, DA-6 and BR treatment were significantly higher than CK (Fig. 1A), upregulated by 12.3, 20.4 and 12.9%, respectively. At the later stage of growth, MT, DA-6 and BR were significantly higher than control (Fig. 1A), increased by 14.9, 26.4 and 11.12%, respectively.

Influence of different growth regulators on photosynthetic responses of Leymus chinensis

Under different hormone applications, VPD and WUE of Chinese Leymus chinensis initially increased and then reduced. VPD was significantly downregulated by different growth hormone treatments in the middle growth stage, and significantly reduced by DA-6 in the later growth stage (Fig. 2A, P < 0.05). In the pregrowth and middle growth, all hormones affected WUE to different degrees, and the optimal treatment was BR and MT, which increased by 20.4 and 21.8%, respectively. At the later stage of growth, there was no significant difference between different hormone treatments compared with CK (Fig. 2C, P > 0.05). Leaf water content and Tr of Leymus chinensis showed a decreasing trend. In the early and middle growth stages, MT treatment reduced leaf water content, which was significantly lower than CK (Fig. 2B, P < 0.05). In the early and middle growth stages, Tr of Leymus chinensis was significantly reduced by BR treatment (Fig. 2D, P < 0.05), with MT > CK > DA-6 > BR as the main and the best treatment was MT. Tr showed a downregulating trend under various hormone treatments, and MT and BR significantly increased Tr by 14.6% and 41.0%, respectively, at the early and late growth stages. During the middle growth period, there was no significant difference between the different hormone treatments compared with control condition (Fig. 2D, P > 0.05).

Discussion

Effects of different growth regulators on agronomic traits and plant biomass

The agronomic traits and plant biomass are important indicators for assessing the growth and development status of plants, which can help us understand the growth potential, health status and adaptability of plants in the era of climate change25. The results of this study showed that the DA-6 treatment continued to show the optimal promoting effect throughout the whole growth period. At the initial stage, the growth promotion intensity of each treatment showed significant gradient difference. At the middle growth stage, the effects of MT and BR treatments converged, while at the late growth stage, DA-6 still maintained the advantages of biomass gain, and achieved the accumulation of growth advantages through multi-stage cooperative regulation mechanism, providing a new regulatory target for the productivity improvement of grassland ecosystem.

Biomass is the product of plant photosynthesis and the basis of nutrient absorption of crops26. It is also one of the bases of plant biological characteristics and functional performance traits, as well as a reflection of plant growth material accumulation and utilization ability of natural growth environment resources27. According to the biomass research findings, there was no significant difference between the hormone treatments in the initial stage, which may be related to the targeted differences of hormone effects28. In the initial stage of Leymus chinensis, hormones mainly acted on the growth and development of sexual reproductive organs, rather than directly promoting the biomass accumulation of vegetative organs. In the middle growth stage, the biomass accumulation of Leymus chinensis was significantly upregulated under various hormone treatments, among which MT treatment had the best effect. The effects of MT and DA-6 were still obvious in the later stage of growth, but DA-6 treatment was dominant in promoting growth and development.

According to relevant studies, melatonin mainly plays a major role in the vegetative growth stage, while it does not play a role in the reproductive growth stage29, which is somewhat different from the research results in the later growth stage of this study. According to relevant literature, there are differences in the melatonin spraying concentration and spraying approaches. BR may be related to the antagonism of endogenous hormones in Chinese Leymus chinensis, and the endogenous abscisic acid (ABA) level of Chinese Leymus chinensis increased in the late growth period30. BR and ABA have antagonistic effects in stomatal regulation, and the growth promotion effect of Chinese Leymus chinensis is not obvious. It can be seen that different hormones have different effects on different growth stages of Leymus chinensis.

Effects of different growth regulators on photosynthetic responses

Photosynthesis plays a crucial role in plant growth and development. It not only associate in the synthesis process of substances and provides necessary organic matter for plants, but also provides energy support for plant growth and development31. In this study, the effects of various hormone treatments on photosynthetic parameters of Leymus chinensis showed significant stage characteristics. In the initial stage, the effects of various hormone treatments on Pn, Ci and VPD of Leymus chinensis were not obvious, while MT could effectively increase Gs and SPAD, permit more CO2 to enter into the leaves and improve the photosynthetic efficiency, which was consistent with the results of the study of Sun Chuanjiao in wheat32. BR significantly increased WUE of Leymus chinensis, indicating that BR could reduce water loss by increasing carbon sequestration per unit water consumption, and at the same time ensure effective CO2 absorption during photosynthesis33. Entering the middle growth stage, the three hormones all reduced VPD and Tr, improved plant water status, increased SPAD, affected transpiration and CO₂ absorption by regulating Gs, and enhanced photosynthetic potential.

MT can improve photosynthetic capacity (Pn, Gs, Ci), significantly improve WUE, and comprehensively optimize WUE. Although DA-6 also improves Pn and Gs, it focuses more on the stability of the photoreaction system, and the improvement of WUE is not as obvious as MT. BR mainly maintains SPAD and reduces Tr, and has little direct effect on photosynthetic rate, so it may be more suitable for delaying leaf senescence and maintaining long-term photosynthetic function34. In the later stage of growth, the three hormones all increase Pn, Ci and SPAD to promote photosynthesis, and the photosynthetic products are preferentially allocated to the reproductive organs. This resource redistribution leads to the decrease of CO₂ utilization efficiency and the increase of Ci35. BR and DA-6 increase leaf water content (LWC), delay leaf senescence and prolong photosynthetic function period, while MT reduces VPD, reduces competitive consumption of vegetative organs and ensures the development of reproductive organs.

Effects of different growth regulators on chlorophyll fluorescence

Fv/Fm reflects the potential photochemical quantum efficiency of photosynthetic apparatus, and the changes of its ratio is considered to be the most sensitive index for inhibiting photosynthesis and destroying photosynthetic apparatus under high light intensity36. NPQ, ETR, PSII and qP were not significantly affected by various hormone treatments in the initial stage. However, DA-6 significantly increased Fv/Fm and Fv′/Fm′ compared to the control, suggesting that DA-6 stimulated the photosystem II (PSII) reaction center of Leymus chinensis, enabling it to absorb more light quanta and thus improve photosynthesis efficiency37. ΦPSII is an index to measure the actual photochemical activity of photosystem II (PSII) in plant photosynthesis, which reflects the proportion of PSII that can effectively use photons for electron transport under light irradiation37.

During photosynthesis, non-photochemical quenching (NPQ) is one of the self-protection mechanisms of plants under strong light stress38. When the light energy absorbed by plants exceeds its photosynthetic capacity, plants convert excess light energy into heat energy through the protection mechanism. Thus, the photosynthetic apparatus are protected from damage39. Entering the middle growth stage, MT treatment significantly reduced qP, ETR and PSII compared with the control, which meant that the thylakoid membrane of Leymus chinensis could not effectively utilize more light quantum during photosynthesis. Thus, it protects the photosynthetic apparatus by increasing NPQ and dissipating the excess light quanta in the form of heat dissipation37.

In contrast, DA-6 treatment promoted photosynthesis by increasing Fv′/Fm′ and decreasing NPQ; Fv/Fm and Fv′/Fm′ inhibition of photosynthesis were significantly reduced by BR treatment, and Leymus chinensis mainly protected itself from excessive light energy damage through heat dissipation and other ways40. At the later stage of growth, MT treatment inhibited photosynthesis by increasing NPQ. DA-6 decreased NPQ to promote photosynthesis. The effect of BR treatment on various indexes was not obvious at this growth stage.

Influence of nutritional quality by the application of different growth regulators

The nutritional quality of forage depends on the types and contents of nutrients contained in it. Crude protein is the core nutrient index, and its content directly determines its ability to meet the protein requirements of livestock41. Crude fiber is the main component of plant cell wall. Its content was negatively correlated with digestibility42. Crude fat is the main source of heat energy, and its unique lipid aroma has become a key indicator of palatability43. Increasing crude protein content and reducing crude fiber content are the main contents of improving forage quality. In this study, MT, DA-6 and BR significantly increased crude protein content and decreased crude fiber and acid detergent lignin content of Leymus chinensis compared with control, and the optimal treatment was MT, and MT significantly increased crude fat content compared with CK, while DA-6 and BR significantly reduced crude fat content compared with CK. In conclusion, MT can effectively promote the redistribution and regulation of photosynthetic products to protein synthesis, improve the nutritional quality of forage by increasing crude fat content and degrading fiber, and achieve the synergistic benefit of “promoting growth and enhancing quality”.

Conclusions and future research directions

The results showed that the hormone treatments showed phased multi-channel synergistic effects on the productivity regulation mechanisms and pathways of Leymus chinensis. MT treatment effectively improved the efficiency of SPAD to optimize light energy capture and carbon assimilation, drove the enhancement of photosynthesis, and showed the dominant role of phased growth promotion in the early and middle growth stages of biomass accumulation; By regulating nitrogen absorption and activating lipid synthetase, regulating carbon source allocation, promoting protein and fat synthesis and accumulation, and effectively improving nutrient quality of forage. DA-6 treatment significantly upregulated Fv′/Fm′ and downregulated NPQ to enhance the stability of light energy conversion, reflecting the long-term benefits of photosystem protection and carbon allocation, and became the best treatment for biomass accumulation in the late growth period. BR can significantly increase WUE through the synergistic effect of stomatal regulation and non-stomatal pathway in the initial stage, which has outstanding potential in drought stress adaptation. In the future production practice, it is more suitable for stage sex hormone compound spraying to achieve the maximum synergy of “water-light-production”. Further research should be focused on how specific growth regulators influence key photosynthetic enzymes (Rubisco, PEP carboxylase) and electron transport chain components, and transcriptomics and proteomics to identify genes/proteins regulated by growth regulators in L. chinensis, particularly those associated to photosynthesis and biomass accumulation.
