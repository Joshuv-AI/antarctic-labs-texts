Correction: Long noncoding RNA GAS5 inhibits progression of colorectal cancer by interacting with and triggering YAP phosphorylation and degradation and is negatively regulated by the m6A reader YTHDF3
Authors: Wen Ni, Su Yao, Yunxia Zhou, Yuanyuan Liu, Piao Huang, Aijun Zhou, Jingwen Liu, Liheng Che, Jianming Li
Published: 2026

Correction: Mol Cancer 18, 143 (2019)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12943-019-1079-y

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors reported that, upon verification, they identified that three image panels were inadvertently mislabeled during the figure assembly process. Specifically, in Figure 3f, the panel representing the LOVO-NC group was incorrectly sourced from the HCT116-NC group in Figure 3e. Likewise, in Figure 5e, the panel for the LOVO-NC group was mistakenly used from the corresponding HCT116-NC group within the same figure. Additionally, the panel labeled as LOVO-sh-GAS5 in Figure 5e was erroneously selected from the RKO-sh-GAS5 group in the same figure.

Upon identifying these errors, the authors immediately retrieved the correct raw data and have now replaced all affected panels with the accurate images in the revised versions of Figure 3 and Figure 5. They would like to emphasize that this error was unintentional and does not affect the experimental results, data interpretation, or the conclusions of the study.
