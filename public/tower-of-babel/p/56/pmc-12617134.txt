ΟΑ2060. Micromobility vehicles practice and accidents: Analysis of 2021 and 2024 French Health Barometer
Authors: M P Chaffard Luçon, L M Paget, A Gautier, N Soullier, L Guldner
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundIn recent years, the expansion of micromobility vehicles (MMVs) in major French cities has been accompanied by an increase in serious road accidents, and in severe injuries. This study aims to examine the profile of MMVs users and accident victims in mainland France based on the 2021 and 2024 Health Barometer.MethodsThe French Health Barometer is a repeated cross-sectional survey. In 2021 and 2024, French respondents were asked about their MMVs practice, helmet use and accidents. The weighted percentages of people using MMVs and having an accident in the last 12 months were calculated according to various sociodemographic characteristics (results are presented for 2021, those for 2024 will be added for the conference).ResultsAmong the 22625 respondents to the 2021 survey, 1898 (10.1%) used an MMV in the last twelve months and 16% of them wore a helmet. The proportion of users was higher among men (12.7%) than women (7.6%), younger age groups (30.1% in < 25 years vs 0.9% among 65-75), in urban areas (10.2%), particularly in the Paris conurbation (14.9%) compared to rural areas (6.0%). Of the 1898 users, 163 (8.9%) declared an accident in the last twelve months. This percentage was higher in urban areas (10.2%) compared to rural areas (4.6%). Characteristics (financial situation, conurbation size, helmet use, daily alcohol consumption) associated with the occurrence of an accident differed among men and women, and according to age groups.ConclusionsWhile analyses are still underway, these initial results underline differences in the profiles of users and those involved in accidents linked to MMVs according to gender and age group, highlighting the need to introduce specific prevention measures.Key messages• Men and people under the age of 25 are the most frequent users of MMVs.• The characteristics of accident victims vary according to age and sex.TopicMicromobility vehicles, epidemiology, Health Barometer
