Retraction and Replacement of: “Effects of Maternal Vitamin D Supplementation on Childhood Health”
Published: 2025

The above-named article by Svensson NS, Volqvartz T, Vestergaard AL, Vestergaard ET, Larsen A, and Bor P (Endocrine Reviews. 2025; doi: 10.1210/endrev/bnaf001) is retracted and replaced by the journal.

A reader notified the editors that one of the references had been inappropriately included in the review and should have been excluded per the inclusion/exclusion criteria described in the Methods. Subsequently, the authors noticed errors that affected reference number sequencing and duplication of tables.

This retraction is to clarify the scholarly record and in no way suggests any misconduct on the part of the authors. The corrected version has been reviewed and approved by the editors.

The replacement article has been published online at doi: 10.1210/endrev/bnaf034.
