Correction: Biplot analysis and correlations between changes in terpenes in calf milk and immune variables, performance and rumen variables
Authors: Kanber Kara, Gönül Pirci
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-15578-5, published online 13 October 2025

The original version of this Article contained an error in the Abstract.

“A total of 32 calves were randomly assigned to four groups: a control group receiving whole milk and starter feed, and three treatment groups supplemented with thyme (150 µL/day), sage (150 µL/day), or lavender oil (1500 µL/day) in milk.”

now reads:

“A total of 32 calves were randomly assigned to four groups: a control group receiving whole milk and starter feed, and three treatment groups supplemented with thyme (150 µL/day), sage (150 µL/day), or lavender oil (150 µL/day) in milk.”

The original Article has been corrected.
