NODG-12 PSYCHIATRIC MANIFESTATIONS OF BRAIN TUMORS
Authors: Niamien Patrice Koffi, Loukou Kouakou, Konan Landry, Haidara Aderehime
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractIntroductionHTIC and neurological focal signs are the main revealing signs of brain tumors, however psychiatric manifestations of these lesions are not rare. The prevalence of major psychiatric disorder (DSM IV) in patients with brain tumors is 28% for malignant tumors in Africa, 41% of patients with brain tumors present a psychiatric disorder responsible for initial diagnostic error.The Objective of Our StudyTo describe psychiatric disorders in patients with brain tumors. METHODOLOGY/ This is a multicenter retrospective study of ten patients seen over the period from March 2022 to January 2025 for psychiatric disorders with secondary discovery of brain tumor. Their epidemiological, clinical, radiological and histological profiles were sought. The initial test performed was the Mini Mental State TEST. RESULTS AND COMMENTS/ The presence of these disorders is found in 26% (10/26), the average age of patients is 44.1 years [26-63], the sex ratio = 6M /4F. The surgical treatment time = 21.55 months [1-60]. The psychiatric signs found are euphoria (1), depression (3), hypersomnia (1), anxiety, ideo-motor slowing (2), guilt, hallucinations (1), inhibition, neglect (1), ego hypertrophy (1). These patients were referred by unit general practitioners in 6 cases, neurologists in 2 cases, and psychiatrists in 2 cases. The pathological examination was in favor of frontal meningiomas (4), giant pituitary adenoma (2), glioma (2), left fronto-temporal arteriovenous malformation (1). CONCLUSION/ Frontal meningiomas are mainly responsible for psychiatric disorders.
