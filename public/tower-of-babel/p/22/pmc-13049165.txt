Correction: Efficacy of gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel in second-line treatment of metastatic pancreatic cancer
Authors: Yasin Sezgin, Oğur Karhan, Mehmet Naci Aldemir, Muslih Ürün, Berrak Mermit Erçek, Zuhat Urakcı, Hayati Arvas, Sezai Tunç, Mehmet Erdem, Halis Yerlikaya, Serdar İleri, İbrahim Aydın, Abdurrahman Bicer, Ahmet Ufuk Kömüroğlu, Nargiz Majidova, Savaş Gökçek, Hacer Demir, Sedat Yıldız, Sinem Akbaş, Esra Özen, Burcu Ulaş Kahya, Mürsel sali, Hicran Anık, Talat Aykut, Murat Araz, Ali Alkan, Melike Özçelik, Abudllah Sakin, Musa Barış Aykan, Mirmehdi Mehtıyev, Bilgin Demir, Mehmet Nuri Başer, Müge Sönmez, İlkay Gültürk, Nilüver Avcı, Semiha Urvay, Mustafa Özgür Arıcı, Mehmet Emin Kalender, Mustafa Yıldırım, Ali Alper Solmaz
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-96157-6, published online 05 April 2025

The original version of this Article contained an error in the Abstract.

“In the second-line treatment with gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel, the median PFS was 5.1 months (95% CI, 5.6 to 7.1), and the median OS was 8.6 months (95% CI, 7.3 to 10.0).”

now reads:

“In the second-line treatment with gemcitabine plus nab-paclitaxel, the median PFS was 5.1 months (95% CI, 4.6 to 6.0), and the median OS was 8.6 months (95% CI, 7.3 to 10.0).”

The original Article has been corrected.
