Trends in the Proportion of Adolescents Engaged in Physical Activities, 2015–2024
Authors: Yangha Kim, 양하 김
Published: 2025

The proportions of adolescent boys and girls engaged in physical activities in 2024 were 25.1% and 8.9%, respectively. The proportion among boys has shown a continuous increase since 2020. In 2024, the proportion of boys engaged in physical activities was three times higher than that of girls, and the gap between middle and high school students widened compared to the previous year (Figures 1, 2).
