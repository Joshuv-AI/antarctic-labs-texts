Response to letter to the editor concerning “statistical clarification of neurological outcome estimates after pediatric suprasellar tumor surgery”
Authors: S C Hulsmann, H M van Santen
Published: 2026

Dear Editor,

We thank the correspondent for evaluating our work and for identifying the inconsistencies in Table 2 that is included our paper describing the effect of centralization of care on the management of postoperative plasma sodium concentration after pediatric suprasellar brain tumor surgery and the neurological outcomes in patients with arginine vasopressin deficiency (AVP-D) [1, 2]. We appreciate the opportunity to clarify the issues that have been identified.

We acknowledge that the odds ratios (OR) and confidence intervals (CI) as reported in Table 2 are indeed wrong and we are very sorry for this mistake. They seem to have been inadvertently carried over from an earlier multivariable model, which was done merely for explorative purposes, and do not correspond to the univariate analyses. The P values reported in Table 2 are, however, correct and were derived from separate univariate logistic regression models, each examining the association between early postoperative AVP-D and a single neurological outcome. The P values (as originally reported) are non-significant and the corrected OR values thus do not alter the overall interpretation of our findings. The conclusion that none of the short- and long-term neurological outcomes were associated with and without early postoperative AVP-D remains unchanged.

Moreover, the CI for ‘motor deficit’ in Table 2 as printed (‘0.100-6.339.100.339’) contained a typing error that was possibly introduced during the editing process, as this had been submitted correctly.

We regret these errors in Table 2 of the original publication and we thank the correspondent for bringing them to our attention. A corrected table will be submitted separately as an Erratum to the journal.

Sincerely,

On behalf of all authors,

Sanne Hulsmann and Hanneke van Santen
