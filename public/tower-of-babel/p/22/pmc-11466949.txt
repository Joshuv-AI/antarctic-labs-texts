Publisher Correction: Specific 3-O-sulfated heparan sulfate domains regulate salivary gland basement membrane metabolism and epithelial differentiation
Authors: Vaishali N. Patel, Marit H. Aure, Sophie H. Choi, James R. Ball, Ethan D. Lane, Zhangjie Wang, Yongmei Xu, Changyu Zheng, Xibao Liu, Daniel Martin, Jillian Y. Pailin, Michaela Prochazkova, Ashok B. Kulkarni, Toin H. van Kuppevelt, Indu S. Ambudkar, Jian Liu, Matthew P. Hoffman
Published: 2024

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-024-51862-0, published online 31 August 2024

The original version of this Article contained an error in Fig. 2c, in which the bar chart for the “Male DKO” condition was white in colour while it should have been grey to correspond with the legend. Likewise, the original version of this Article contained errors in Fig. 4c and 4d, in which the bar charts for the “WT” condition were coloured white while they should have been in grey to correspond with the legends. These have been corrected in both the PDF and HTML versions of the Article.
