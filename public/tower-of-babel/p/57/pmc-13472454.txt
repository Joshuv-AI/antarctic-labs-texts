ISP mid-year: Moments, milestones and momentum
Authors: Ashish Jain
Published: 2026

Dear Members,

Greetings from the Indian Society of Periodontology.

As we look back at the first half of the year, I feel a lot of pride and also genuine gratitude for the remarkable progress our Society keeps making. The path of ISP isn’t just defined by the milestones we reach, but also by the momentum we generate together through dedication, passion, and active involvement of our members across the country.

The recently completed ISP Mid-Year Convention, held at Ahmedabad in last week of April this year is a strong proof of this spirit of excellence and innovation. The event brought clinicians, academicians, researchers, and postgraduate students onto one lively platform, where learning, cooperation, and professional growth happened side by side. The scientific program had a good mix of keynote addresses, guest lectures from eminent experts, hands-on workshops, free paper presentations, and poster sessions each one adding to an energetic academic setting, and quietly reminding us that we still care a great deal about advancing periodontal science and practice.

This convention was also memorable because it introduced a few exciting first time efforts. The launch of PerioTank, an initiative designed to spot, nurture and support inventive ideas, along with entrepreneurial thinking within our specialty, took a meaningful step toward building a culture of innovation in periodontology. Just as promising was the warm and enthusiastic response to the inaugural Perio Quiz, which highlighted the knowledge, competitiveness, and academic energy of our postgraduate students. These efforts show clearly that our Society is determined to keep evolving, and to create genuine chances for learning, leadership, and new innovations. We saw strong participation across all scientific sessions and competitive events, and it reinforces the resilience of our academic community.

Beyond our scientific endeavors, ISP keeps strengthening its commitment towards fostering research and academic excellence. I am pleased to share that the applications received under the Society’s Implant Research Grant program are now, undergoing rigorous review and scrutiny. We have been rather encouraged by the quality and variety of proposals submitted, showing a growing research drive within our specialty. The outcomes from the various ISP scholarships and research support initiatives for the current year will be announced shortly, and we look forward to backing deserving candidates in their pursuit of academic, and clinical excellence.

From nurturing fresh concepts through PerioTank to supporting meaningful research via grants, and scholarships, ISP stays focused on creating openings that help every member to learn, innovate, and move forward. These types of efforts reflect our vision of a progressive and future-ready Society, one that not only champions scientific excellence but also sparks leadership, creativity and lifelong learning.

I thank all our members for their unwavering trust, participation, and support, which continue to inspire the Society’s endeavors. As we celebrate these moments and milestones, let us carry forward the momentum with renewed enthusiasm, a shared vision, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

Together, we will continue to strengthen our specialty, inspire future generations, and advance the science and practice of periodontology.
