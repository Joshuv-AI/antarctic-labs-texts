ABSTRACT
AbstractIn this article, we find an α-valuation for disjoint union of some bipartite graphs and the tensor product of paths and even cycles.MSC: 05C76, 05C78Keywords: Graceful valuation, α-valuation, Tensor product, Paths, Cycles

Introduction

Throughout this article, the graph H is finite and simple. Notations and terminologies can be found in [1], [5]. For an almost exhaustive survey of graph labeling one can refer [4]. A labeling ϕ on a graph H with p edges is a 1-1 function from the vertices of H to the set {0,1,…,p} such that the values |ϕ(r)−ϕ(t)| for the p pairs of adjacent vertices r,t are distinct, then ϕ is said to be a graceful valuation of H (β-labeling or β-valuation). If, in addition, there exists an integer γ so that for each edge rt∈E(H) one of the values ϕ(r) and ϕ(t), respectively, does not exceed γ and the other is strictly greater than γ, then the labeling ϕ is called an α-valuation of H with critical value γ and H is said to be an α-graph. Note that a graph with an α-valuation is necessarily a bipartite. As a result, such a γ must be smaller then the smaller of the two vertex labels that yield the edge labeled 1. Assume H is an α-graph of size p and critical value γ. Then {P,Q} is the stable sets (partition) of the vertices with r∈P and t∈Q, we assume, without losing generality that P={r∈V(H):ϕ(r)≤γ} and Q={t∈V(H):ϕ(t)>γ}. Clearly, every α-valuation is also a graceful labeling but not conversely. The concept of β-labeling was pioneered by Rosa, see in [22] (1967), and also he presented the concept of certain types of vertex labeling as an important tools of decompositions of complete graphs K2s+1 into a graphs with s edges. Also, they proved that

[22]A graph H with s edges has an α-valuation, then there exists a H-decomposition ofK2st+1, t is any positive integer.

Clearly, the above theorem shows a relation between graceful labeling and decomposition of graphs.

The graph labeling is widely applied such as ATM network infrastructure, Rador, astronomy, x-ray and communication systems.

The tensor product of two graphs H1 and H2, denote by H1×H2, has vertex set V(H1×H2)=V(H1)×V(H2) and the edge set E(H1×H2)={((r1,s1),(r2,s2))|r1r2∈E(H1) and s1s2∈E(H2)}. Clearly, if H1 and H2 are connected bipartite graphs, then the product H1×H2 is disconnected and it has exactly two components. Ibrahim Moussa, in [6], has proved that Ps×Pt is odd graceful for all integers s≥2 and t≥2. Sutha and Kanniga, in [23], studied the splitting graph of the complete bipartite graph Ks,t is a β-valuation and Ks,t×Pr,r≥2 is also a β-valuation. Kotzig, in [9], shown that, for s,t≥3, the union of two disjoint cycles Cs∪Ct admits an graceful valuation iff s+t≡0or3(mod4). Furthermore, he shown for s,t≥4 is even, Cs∪Ct admits an α-valuation iff s+t≡0(mod4). Hence, for s≥1, and s≡0or2(mod4), Cs×P2≅2Cs admits an α-valuation. Also, it does not admits a graceful valuation if s≥3 is an odd. Kaneria and Makadia, in [7], [8], proved that the tensor product of P3×Pt and Ps×Pt (when s,t both are odd) admits a graceful valuation. For more results on an α-valuation, refer [2], [3], refer [10], [11], [12], [13], [14] and among other results, some other labeling of product graphs, refer [15], [16], [17], [18], [19], [20], [21] and [24]. In this paper, we find an α-valuation of the tensor product of paths and cycles.

Conclusion and open problems

We conclude this paper with the following problems.

The graph Cs×Pt, s≥3 is an odd, t≥3, determine if there is an α-valuation.

For the graph Cs×Ct, s≥3 is an odd, t≥3, determine if there is an α-valuation.

The graph G×H, determine if there is an α-valuation, where G and H are arbitrary connected graphs.

We believe that, in future, this paper motivates to find an α-valuation for different product graphs such as strong product, wreath product and lexicographic product and so on.
