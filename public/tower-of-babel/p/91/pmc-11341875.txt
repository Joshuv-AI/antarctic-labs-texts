Published: 2024

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-018-31411-8, published online 29 August 2018

The Competing interests section in this Article is incorrect.

“The authors declare no competing interests.”

should read:

“Data collection utilized radar hardware from Xandar Kardian Inc., a company co-founded by Sung Ho Cho who currently serves as a full-time professor at Hanyang University in South Korea. The radar hardware was used exclusively for data collection purposes. This research received no financial support from Xandar Kardian Inc., but it was fully funded by the National Research Foundation (NRF) of South Korea. The other authors declare no conflicts of interest.”
