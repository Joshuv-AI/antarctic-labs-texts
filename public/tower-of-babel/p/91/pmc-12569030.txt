Retraction Note: Chemerin reverses neurological impairments and ameliorates neuronal apoptosis through ChemR23/CAMKK2/AMPK pathway in neonatal hypoxic–ischemic encephalopathy
Authors: Yixin Zhang, Ningbo Xu, Yan Ding, Desislava Met Doycheva, Yiting Zhang, Qian Li, Jerry Flores, Mina Haghighiabyaneh, Jiping Tang, John H. Zhang
Published: 2025

Retraction Note to: Cell Death and Disease 10.1038/s41419-019-1374-y, published online 04 February 2019

The Editors-in-Chief have retracted this article. After publication, concerns were raised regarding highly similar images within the article and with another publication from the same Authors.
