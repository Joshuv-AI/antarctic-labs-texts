Retraction Note: Algae-mediated copper nanocatalyst for aerobic oxidation and dye decolourization via sustainable wastewater treatment
Authors: Arunadevi Mani, Velmurugan Loganathan, Janani Mullaivendhan, Anish Ahamed, Ibrahim A Arif, Idhayadhulla Akbar
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-81354-6, published online 13 December 2024

The Editors have retracted this Article. After publication, concerns were raised regarding the veracity of the SEM data presented in Figure 8a of this work, as portions of panels (a) through (d) appear partially to overlap, and portions of panels (c) and (d) appear to overlap with portions of panels A and B in Figure 5 in1. In addition, the Editors consider that the XRD patterns shown in Figure 7 likely do not correspond to the types of copper and copper oxide nanoparticles synthesized. The Authors provided some of the original data to the Editors, but these were insufficient to address the concerns. The Editors therefore no longer have confidence in the research presented in this work.

The Authors did not explicitly state if they agree or disagree with the retraction.
