Sombor characteristic values of cographs
Authors: Zahid Raza, Bilal Ahmad Rather, Muhammad Imran
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractA unique class of cograph is examined, that is defined recursively as C=C(n1)=K‾n1, and C=C(n1,n2,…,ni)=C(n1,n2,…,ni−1)∪Kni‾, for 2≤i≤k. The Sombor spectrum of C is calculated, the Sombor spectral radius and establish the sharp bounds for the Sombor index, and the greatest and smallest Sombor characteristic values. We present results on the Sombor energy of cographs and identify the graphs that reach the inequalities. Also, we characterize cographs with simple Sombor characteristic values and cographs with unique characteristic values.MSC: 05C50, 05C12, 15A18Keywords: Sombor matrix, Sombor index, Cographs graphs, Characteristic values

Introduction

A simple finite graph G(V,E) (briefly G) comprises of a node set V={γ1,γ2,…,γn} and the set of unordered pairs of nodes known as edge set E. The count of elements in V is order n and the count in E is size m of G. The collection of nodes adjacent to v∈V except v, symbolized by NG(v), is known as neighborhood (open) of v. The degree denoted by dvi (or di) related to node vi is the count in NG(vi). A node of degree zero is the isolated node of G. A collection of non-adjacent nodes is the independent set and a set in which each node is adjacent to other nodes is the clique set of G. We denote adjacency relation of two nodes γi and γj by γi∼γj.

Conclusion

We found various results related to the Sombor spectral invariants for a class of cographs and identified extremal graphs for these results. In future, the theory can be elaborated to other important topological indices as well as application of these cographs in mathematical chemistry. A detailed analysis of these graphs can be forwarded for the distance based indices and distance based matrices
