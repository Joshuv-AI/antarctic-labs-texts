PDNO-17 CHILDREN’S CRANIOPHARYNGIOMA : MANAGEMENT AND FUTURE
Authors: Coulibaly M, Okamon DM, Harmonie M, Djonde G, Meuga MW, Drogba L, D Ndri Oka
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractIntroductioncraniopharyngiomas are histologically benign tumors, but formidable due to endocrine complications and their relationships with noble vascular-nervous elements. Excision surgery is often controversial. We report our experience in the management of craniopharyngiomas.Methodretrospective and descriptive study carried out in the neurosurgery department of YOPOUGON University Hospital from March 2023 to January 2025 on 7 operated patients. Epidemiological, diagnostic, therapeutic and evolutionary data were studied.Resultsthere were 2 girls and 5 boys. The average age was 6.5 years. The reason for consultation was dominated by intracranial hypertension (ICH). All patients underwent primary ventriculoperitoneal bypass. Only one patient was treated by surgical excision. 6 patients benefited from an Omaya reservoir, of which 2 received additional treatment. Radiotherapy for one and interferon alpha in situ for the second. We noted 1 case of death.Conclusionthe management of craniopharyngiomas remains a challenge in our work context.
