Publisher Correction: Intestinal Nogo-B reduces GLP1 levels by binding to proglucagon on the endoplasmic reticulum to inhibit PCSK1 cleavage
Authors: Ke Gong, Chao Xue, Zian Feng, Ruru Pan, Mengyao Wang, Shasha Chen, Yuanli Chen, Yudong Guan, Lingyun Dai, Shuang Zhang, Liwei Jiang, Ling Li, Bei Wang, Zequn Yin, Likun Ma, Yasuko Iwakiri, Junming Tang, Chenzhong Liao, Houzao Chen, Yajun Duan
Published: 2024

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-024-51352-3, published online 10 August 2024

In this article the affiliation details for Affiliation #1 were incorrectly given as ‘Division of Life Sciences and Medicine, Department of Cardiology, the First Affiliated Hospital of USTC, University of Science and Technology of China, Hefei, Anhui, China’ but should have been ‘Department of Cardiology, The First Affiliated Hospital of USTC, Division of Life Sciences and Medicine, University of Science and Technology of China, Hefei, Anhui, China’. The original article has been corrected.
