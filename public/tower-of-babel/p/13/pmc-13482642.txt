Synopsis

Correction to: The EMBO Journal (2026) 45, 2882–2904. 10.1038/s44318-026-00731-1 | Published online 17 March 2026

Manuscript Synopsis image is corrected.

In the originally published manuscript, an error was introduced in the Synopsis figure.

The label “ΔflaA1” was incorrectly shown and should read “ΔflaA2”.

This has now been corrected in the online version of the Synopsis figure.

The authors apologise for this oversight. This correction does not affect the results or conclusions of the article.
