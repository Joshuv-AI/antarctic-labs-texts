Retraction Note: Letrozole versus clomiphene citrate for superovulation in Egyptian women with unexplained infertility: a randomized controlled trial
Authors: Moustafa I Ibrahim, Rowaa A Moustafa, Ahmed A Abdel-Azeem
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: Arch Gynecol Obstet (2012) 286:1581–1587

10.1007/s00404-012-2478-0

The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article. Following publication, concerns were raised regarding the validity of the data, specifically that there appear to be errors in the Standard Deviations and p-values reported. The authors were unable to provide a satisfactory response to the concerns and, due to the age of the article, were not able to provide the raw data for the study. In addition, during the investigation of these concerns, the authors were unable to provide evidence to demonstrate that the study was conducted with formal approval from an appropriate ethics committee. The Editor has therefore lost confidence in the study.

Authors Moustafa I. Ibrahim and Rowaa A. Moustafa disagree with this retraction. The Publisher has not been able to obtain a current email address for author Ahmed A. Abdel-Azeem.
