Loganath K, Lee KK, Oikonomidou O, Hall P, Mills NL, Joshi S, Singh T, McGillivray T, Semple S, Lang NN, Dweck MR, Henriksen PA

Anthracycline Dose, Myocardial Injury, and Change in Left Ventricular Function in the Cardiac CARE Trial

JACC: CardiOoncology. 2025;7(6):725-735.

The authors are grateful to Professor Ky’s research team for identifying an error in the reporting of anthracycline dose in their paper.

In Table 1 and Figure 2, anthracycline dose is reported as epirubicin-equivalent dose, mg/m2 but the data are cumulative dose (mg). We have repeated the analysis using epirubicin-equivalent dose corrected for BSA (mg/m2). We are grateful for the opportunity to correct this error which involves data presented in the:

No statistically significant differences were observed in absolute hs-cTnI concentrations or in changes across tertiles of cumulative anthracycline dose.

This correction does not affect the result or article conclusions. In a generalized linear mixed model adjusted for age and sex, the association between epirubicin-equivalent dose (mg/m2) and hs-cTnI was not statistically significant (p = 0.24).

HS-cTnI CONCENTRATION, CUMULATIVE ANTHRACYCLINE DOSE, AND NUMBER OF ANTHRACYCLINE TREATMENT CYCLES.

The median baseline hs-cTnI concentration was 1.0 ng/L (Q1-Q3: 0.5-2.0 ng/L). Overall, the highest levels were observed 2 months after completion of anthracycline therapy, with a median of 14.0 ng/L (Q1-Q3: 9.0-30.5 ng/L). By 6 months, concentrations had declined to a median of 3.0 ng/L (Q1-Q3: 2.0-5.0 ng/L).

On-treatment hs-cTnI levels increased across all tertiles of cumulative anthracycline dose. The median change in hs-cTnI from baseline to 2 months post-chemotherapy was +11.5 ng/L (Q1-Q3: 6.0-25.0 ng/L) in tertile 1, +13.5 ng/L (Q1-Q3: 9.0-34.2 ng/L) in tertile 2, and +14.5 ng/L (Q1-Q3: 9.0-33.5 ng/L) in tertile 3. However, there was no statistically significant difference in peak hs-cTnI concentrations across tertiles (Figure 2). Median hs-cTnI levels at 2 months were 12.0 ng/L (Q1-Q3: 7.0-27.0 ng/L) in tertile 1, 14.0 ng/L (Q1-Q3: 10.0-35.5 ng/L) in tertile 2, and 15.0 ng/L (Q1-Q3: 10.0-34.0 ng/L) in tertile 3 (P = 0.45).
