Correction

The article, ‘The Open Anchoring Quest Dataset: Anchored Estimates from 96 Studies on Anchoring Effects’ (Röseler, et al., 2022) was mistakenly published with the following co-authors omitted: Elitza Ambrus, Ryan McKay, Bjoern Hartig, Eilish Allen, Adriana Rostekova, Jodie Pearton, Susanne Ponader, Roland Imhoff, Christian Seida, and Maarten Speekenbrink.

This was due to an administrative error.

All co-authors confirm that they meet the definitions of authorship and made substantial contributions to the paper.
