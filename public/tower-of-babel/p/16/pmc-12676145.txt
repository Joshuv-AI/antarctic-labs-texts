JACC: Case Reports and Quality
Authors: Mary Norine Walsh, Olivia N Gilbert
Published: 2025

Welcome to the inaugural issue of JACC: Case Reports devoted solely to quality improvement! For this special issue, the journal has partnered with the ACC Quality Summit1 to highlight the work being done in cardiovascular quality improvement across the country.

The ACC Quality Summit is the College’s second-largest conference, focused on sharing best practice and leveraging data to drive quality improvement and transforming cardiovascular care. Since its inaugural meeting in 2000 with just 75 participants, the Summit has grown to welcome over 800 attendees, including a significant increase in Fellows in Training and Early Career member engagement. Originally centered on optimizing the use of National Cardiovascular Data Registries and ACC Accreditation Services, the Summit has evolved to address broader clinical applications, foster administrative collaboration, and promote the use of registry data to inform and improve clinical decision-making.

This year, conference attendees whose abstracts were accepted for presentation at the meeting were invited to submit their work as a paper to the journal. JACC: Case Reports has long had a submission category for quality improvement projects, publishing small studies, clinical initiatives, and operational activities with the end goal of measuring and enhancing quality of care. These projects can be from a single or multiple disciplines. Submissions by early career practitioners, advanced practice clinicians, pharmacists, registered nurses, and allied health professionals are highly encouraged.

The response to the call for papers was overwhelming! Submitted manuscripts covered a wide range of initiatives, and this special issue reflects the work of multidisciplinary teams and trainees involved in multiple areas of quality improvement. Topics include ST-segment elevation myocardial infarction care, health care utilization, transcatheter valve patient care, hypertension control, radiation safety, and the use of guideline-directed medical therapy, among many others.

We hope that our readers will learn from all that the authors and their teams have accomplished and will be encouraged to rally their own teams to focus on quality improvement projects in their practices and institutions. Prepare now for ACC Quality Summit 2026! The program committee awaits your abstract submissions and JACC: Case Reports awaits your quality improvement manuscripts!
