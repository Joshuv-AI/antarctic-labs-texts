Correction: Correlation between Phase Angle and the Number of Medications in Older Inpatients: A Cross-Sectional Study
Authors: Toshiyuki Moriyama, Mizuki Tokunaga, Ryoko Hori, Akiko Hachisuka, Hideaki Itoh, Mitsuhiro Ochi, Yasuyuki Matsushima, Satoru Saeki
Published: 2026

https://doi.org/10.4235/agmr.24.0096

Ann Geriatr Med Res 2024;28:419-426.

The original publication of this article contained several typographical errors and inconsistencies.

The proposed corrections are as follows:

1) page 1, Abstract, Results

Published text: “178 patients (34.4%) were diagnosed with sarcopenia.”

Corrected text: “222 patients (42.9%) were diagnosed with sarcopenia.”

2) page 3, Results

Published text: “One hundred seventy-eight patients (34.4%) were diagnosed with sarcopenia.”

Corrected text: “Two hundred twenty-two patients (42.9%) were diagnosed with sarcopenia.”

3) page 5, Table 3, Men, Hemoglobin, Model 1

Published value: B = 0.017

Corrected value: B = 0.168

4) page 5, Table 4, Women, Hemoglobin, Model 2

Published value: 95% CI = −0.009 to 0.006

Corrected value: 95% CI = −0.009 to 0.036

These errors in no way affect the validity of the main results of the paper.

The authors sincerely apologize for these errors and any inconvenience they may have caused.
