Correction: Assessment and nematicidal activity of Cu/Fe and Zn/Fe bimetallic nanoparticles against root-knot nematode in beetroot and cabbage
Authors: O A Fabiyi, A H Labulo, A V Ogundele, T A Adesalu, H S Mella, O B Akinsipo, A O Claudius-Cole
Published: 2025

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-14384-3, published online 09 August 2025

In the original version of this Article, H.S. Mella was omitted as a co-corresponding author. Correspondence and requests for materials should also be addressed to hector.silva@ufrontera.cl.

In addition, Figures 10 and 11 contained an error where the error bars were incorrectly displayed. The original Figures 10 and 11 and accompanying legend appear below.

Effect of treatments and concentration on height of beet root plants. Error bars represent standard error of means (SEM) comparing treatments and concentration on height of plants.

Effect of treatments and concentration on leaf number of cabbage plants. Error bars represent standard error of means (SEM) comparing treatments and concentration on leaf numbers.

The original Article has been corrected.
