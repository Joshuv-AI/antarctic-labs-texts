A fuzzy parametric model for decision making involving F-OWA operator with unknown weights environment
Authors: Muhammad Touqeer, Saleh Al Sulaie, Showkat Ahmad Lone, Kiran Shaheen, Nevine M Gunaime, Mohamed Abdelghany Elkotb
Published: 2023

ABSTRACT
AbstractWeight determining of attributes is an important factor in decision support systems since it corresponds to the relative importance of each criteria which is necessary to be determined since all the attributes aren't equally important. The aim of this paper is to put forward a method for multi Criteria decision making (MCDM) problems based on three trapezoidal fuzzy numbers under completely unknown weights environment. Based on the idea that the attribute with a larger deviation value among alternatives should be assigned a larger weight, an optimization model based on maximizing deviation method is established. F-OWA is considered to be vastly superior from the existing operators which usually take into account only the relative significance of decision makers. F-OWA operator considers not only the ratings of attribute values but also their ordered position that is it not only signifies the decision makers but also values the individual assessments. We utilize fuzzy ordered weighted averaging (F-OWA) operator to compute the collective overall preference value of each alternative and select the most desirable one according to their expected score values. The presented method is more generalized since we have used TTFNs, which are more effective in capturing uncertainty than IT2FS, just like triangular fuzzy numbers have a better representational power than simple interval numbers. Moreover, an illustrative example is given for the justification of the proposed technique.Keywords: Linguistic variables, Maximizing deviation, Fuzzy ordered weighted averaging (F-OWA), Three trapezoidal fuzzy numbers (TTFNs)

Introduction

Statistics and probability theories have been the major theories and tools to model uncertainties of real life for a long period of time. These theories are based on defined axiomatic assumptions as all other formal theories, which are seldom tested when applied to reality. Fuzzy sets were introduced and described using membership functions by Zadeh [33] in 1965 and have many practical applications. T2FS being an extension of T1FS provides more degree of freedom to deal vagueness of real world problems, can describe uncertain information in 3D model and are more flexible in handling fuzzy decision making problems [11]. Mendel and John [12] gave a new representation of T2FS which can be utilized to define the basic operations of T2FS more easily.

So far, Interval T2 fuzzy sets are most familiar T2 fuzzy sets and have been utilized in survey processing [20], intuitive computing [3], voice recognition [34], satellite communication [9], [14], data procurement and other optimization techniques specified by Wu and Mendel [21], [1], [35]. In multi criterion group decision making environment, the best alternative is chosen by decision maker under set of attributes by comparing the performance of all alternatives.

Group decision making problems act in accordance with the common resolution strategy frame by two stages namely aggregation and exploitation [17], [15], [16]. The main problem in the Ordered Weighted Averaging like operators is to calculate their corresponding weights. Xu [27] assess these techniques for calculating the weights, such as the linguistic quantifier method [31] and the rational method. Xu [22], [23] further propose linguistic aggregation operators namely linguistic ordered weighted geometric averaging (LOWGA) and uncertain linguistic hybrid aggregation (ULHA) to model fuzzy linguistic group decision making. These linguistics operators are the generalization of the conventional and have wider implementation in management science and fuzzy decision making.

Weight determining of attributes is an important factor in decision support systems since it corresponds to the relative importance of each criteria which is necessary to be determined since all the attributes aren't equally important. Commonly employed techniques for finding the weights of the attributes include the subject approach, the objective approach which is based on using the attribute values and also a combination of both of subjective and objective approaches. Usually the objective technique is considered as the more price and authentic method since it uses the information in the form of assessments of alternatives on the basis of criterion. The well known objective methods include the information entropy method and the maximizing deviation method. The concept of entropy method is that an attribute with reference to which there a significant difference among the attribute values should be assigned a greater weight whereas for an attribute with higher entropy among the attribute values with reference to it, the weight should be minimum. Similarly for maximizing deviation whose very name corresponds to significant of a criteria that causes maximum deviation among the attribute values and henceforth such a criteria should be assigned a greater weight whereas assigns a less weight to the one relative to which the deviation among the attribute values in minor. This technique can be used when either there is no information on weights or there is a partial information about them. In this article we our motive is to consider the case of determining the weights of attributes in completely unknown environment in the context of three trapezoidal fuzzy numbers (TTFNs), the type of fuzzy numbers that are arithmetically challenging and capture more uncertainty when applied to multi group decision making because three type-1 trapezoidal fuzzy numbers are involved in its representation [16] as a result there are more representative as compared to their counterparts the interval type-2 fuzzy numbers.

The aggregation operator to be employed in this research is vastly superior from the existing operators which usually take into account only the relative significance of decision makers. F-OWA operator considers not only the ratings of attribute values but also their ordered position that is it not only signifies the decision makers but also values the individual assessments.

In this paper, we propose an approach for the multi-attribute decision making problems with linguistic information modeled as three trapezoidal fuzzy numbers under unknown attribute weight environment. An optimization model based on maximizing deviation method for three trapezoidal numbers, by which weights of attributes can be calculated, is established. An aggregation operator named Fuzzy Ordered Weighted Averaging (F-0WA) is introduced to aggregate the given decision information corresponding to each alternative to get the overall preference value, and then ranking the alternatives according to their expected preference value. Finally an illustrative example is given to show the usefulness and validity of the submitted approach.

Literature review

Meng et al. [34] considered incomplete additive trapezoidal fuzzy preference relations thereby completing them by using optimization models determined by employing consistency analysis in connection with self confidence of decision makers. Wang et al. [25] proposed a technique for optimal decisions under symmetric and asymmetric attribute values by making use of web crawler technology and k-means cluster analysis. Moreover, they calculated the weights of decisions makers by exerting the combination of maximizing deviation method (MDM) and Euclidean distance. Wang et al. [26] combined the q-ROFS and typical MDM to extend MDM under q-ROFSs and q-RIVOFSs. The introduced q-ROFSs and q-RIVOFSs to combine the MDM with q-rung orthopair fuzzy information and provided two new decision making models that were applied to MADM problems with q-rung orthopair fuzzy information. Akram et al. [32] proposed a decision technique by utilizing both the MDM and the TOPSIS with partially known information in addition to neutrosophic hesitant sets. Wu et al. [30] discussed a fuzzy GDM model with MTFPRs. The priority weights are determined using a least square model and compatibility measure is introduced based on the deviation between MTGFPRs and Expected FPR. Further, an iterative method is proposed to help the individuals attain the acceptable compatibility. Meng and Chen [29] focused on GDM with MTFPRs based on consistency consensus. They proposed a ranking method with acceptable consistent MTrFPRs with a fuzzy measure determined using a central-derivation model. Wang and Zhang [28] proposed a combined weighted technique in the case when the decision makers have preferences for both objective and subjective weights which utilizes the original information on priority basis. Touqeer et al. [18] developed an effective model for manipulating MCGDM situations by using a hybrid averaging operator. They used a non-linear model for determining fuzzy priority weight vectors and a signed distance based closeness coefficients approach for ranking of the alternatives which was effectively applied to a patient centered medicine system. Siminski [13] applied the three way decision making technique to neuro fuzzy classifiers by assigning a data item with non-commitment class to run this system for this item. The fuzzy rules which are being used can be made a priory. Aleksić and Tadić [2] used interval type-2 fuzzy parameters for determining the relative importance of criterion and extending the existing ranking methods in the context of these parameters. Li et al. [10] introduced a new ranking approach based on pairwise comparison of probability comparison index and the measures of preference index sets by comparing level sets and ordering based on three way decision making. Wang et al. [24] presented a decision technique based on TI-fuzzy β-neighborhood measures. Moreover, Jana et al. [8], [4], [5], [6], [7] discussed GRA method with partial information on criterion weights, power dombi operators, intuitionistic fuzzy dombi hybrid decision making method with pathagorean fuzzy parameters.

An approach to multi-attribute group decision making (MAGDM) with linguistic information based on F-OWA operator

Fuzzy decision making problems follow usual resolution programme having two stages aggregation and exploitation so that we put forward an empirical technique based on F-OWA operator for decision making problems using deviation method for unknown attribute weights. Following is the five step algorithm for the proposed scheme which has also been elaborated in the form of a flow chart in Fig. 2.

Decision making with unknown weights using F-OWA aggregation operator.

Step 1: Construct a decision matrix Mq=[aijq]s×t for multi-criteria decision problems, where aij are the lingual variables for each alternative and q represents the qth decision maker.

Step 2: Utilize equation (10) and (11) to calculate unknown attribute weights wj for j=1,2,..,s.

Step 3: Aggregate the decision information using F-OWA operator and calculate the preference value Z˘i(q) in terms of three trapezoidal fuzzy numbers for q decision makers where i=1,2,..,s.

Step 4: Calculate the collective overall preference values Z˘i.

Step 5: Finally rank all the alternatives Xi and choose the best one according to the Expected value score E˜(Z˘i).

Comparative analysis

Weight determining of attributes is an important factor in decision support systems since it corresponds to the relative importance of each criteria which is necessary to be determined since all the attributes aren't equally important. Commonly employed techniques for finding the weights of the attributes includes the subject approach, the objective approach which is based on using the attribute values and also a combination of both of subjective and objective approaches. Usually the objective technique is considered as the more price and authentic method since it uses the information in the form of assessments of alternatives on the basis of criterion. The well known objective methods include the information entropy method and the maximizing deviation method. The concept of entropy method is that an attribute with reference to which there a significant difference among the attribute values should be assigned a greater weight whereas for an attribute with higher entropy among the attribute values with reference to it, the weight should be minimum. Similarly for maximizing deviation whose very name corresponds to significant of a criteria that causes maximum deviation among the attribute values and henceforth such a criteria should be assigned a greater weight whereas assigns a less weight to the one relative to which the deviation among the attribute values in minor. This technique can be used when either there is no information on weights or there is a partial information about them. In this article we our motive is to consider the case of determining the weights of attributes in completely unknown environment in the context of three trapezoidal fuzzy numbers (TTFNs), the type of fuzzy numbers that are arithmetically challenging and capture more uncertainty when applied to multi group decision making because three type-1 trapezoidal fuzzy numbers are involved in its representation as a result there are more representative as compared to their counterparts the interval type-2 fuzzy numbers.

The aggregation operator to be employed in this research is vastly superior from the existing operators which usually take into account only the relative significance of decision makers. F-OWA operator considers not only the ratings of attribute values but also their ordered position that is it not only signifies the decision makers but also values the individual assessments. The priority rankings obtained here in case of TTFNs are simply an extension of the rankings proposed in [23]. Moreover, if we consider the same example with q-rung orthopair fuzzy parameters [26] the priority ranking of the alternatives will be the same.

Conclusion

We analyzed decision making process of multi attributes using linguistic environment where the attribute values represent fuzzy numbers and information regarding attribute weight is not specified owing to lack of data or decision maker limited proficiency about the problem. Maximizing deviation method for three trapezoidal numbers is proposed to deal such situation. This method is developed with aim that larger weight should be allocated to the attribute with larger deviation. The eminent characteristic of maximizing deviation technique is that it lessen the effect of biased behavior of the decision makers. We also utilize F-OWA operator to compute the combined preference value of each alternative to choose the best one as per their expected score value. The main benefit of this aggregation technique is to consider the position of the arguments and to use decision information more adequately which justifies the added computation due to the involvement of TTFNS, which is the only limitation of the proposed scheme.
