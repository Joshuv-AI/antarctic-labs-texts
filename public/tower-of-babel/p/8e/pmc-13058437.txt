RETRACTION: Novel Synthesis of Titanium Oxide Nanoparticles: Biological Activity and Acute Toxicity Study
Authors: Bioinorganic Chemistry and Applications
Published: 2026

RETRACTION: M. T. Hamed, B. A. Bakr, Y. H. Shahin, et al., “Novel Synthesis of Titanium Oxide Nanoparticles: Biological Activity and Acute Toxicity Study,” Bioinorganic Chemistry and Applications, 2021, 8171786, https://doi.org/10.1155/2021/8171786.

The above article, published online on 12 August 2021 in Wiley Online Library (https://wileyonlinelibrary.com), has been retracted by agreement the handling Editor, Guillermo Mendoza‐Diaz, and John Wiley & Sons Ltd. The retraction has been agreed due to multiple concerns identified with the figures. The authors initially contacted the publisher to correct errors in Figure 3, which had also been raised on PubPeer [1]. During the course of the investigation, additional concerns were identified by the publisher in Figures 4 and 5. The concerns were presented to the authors with a request for original raw data and an explanation. The authors further requested a correction to Figure 10c. A summary of the concerns is below:
