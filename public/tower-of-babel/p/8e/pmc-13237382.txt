Author Correction: COX-2/PGE₂ signaling drives Toxocara canis-induced lung pathology by enhancing plasminogen activator activity and collagen VI degradation
Authors: Shih-Chan Lai, Cheng-You Lu, Ching-Yu Chuang, Hsiu-Hsiung Lee, Meng-Chia Lee, Ke-Min Chen
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-22711-x, published online 05 November 2025

The original version of this Article contained an error in Figure 12, panel i, where an incorrect tissue sample was included during preparation.

The incorrect Figure 12 and the accompanying legend appear below.

Immunohistochemical location of COX-2 and plasminogen activators in lung tissue infected with T. canis. Immunohistochemical analysis revealed the expression of COX-2, tPA, uPA, and COL6 in control, infected, and celecoxib-treated lungs. Minimal or undetectable expression of these proteins was observed in the control group (a–d). At 7 dpi, COX-2, tPA, uPA and COL6 were markedly upregulated in infected lung tissue (e–h) compared with those in controls. Celecoxib treatment attenuated this response, restoring expression levels to those observed in the control group (i–l). Negative Control: normal serum (m–p). Anatomical structures are labeled as follows: A, alveoli; H, hemorrhage; Br, bronchiole; V, vessel. Scale bar = 100 μm.

The original Article has been corrected.
