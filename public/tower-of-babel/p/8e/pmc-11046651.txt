A rare case of opsoclonus myoclonus syndrome following COVID-19 illness
Authors: Rajish Shil, Cordelia Dunai, Adam Seed, Greta Wood, Ceryce Collie, Sophie Pendered, Michael Bonello, Liene Elsone, Benedict Michael
Published: 2023

There has been growing evidence in the literature that infection with COVID-19 is associated with neurological and psychiatric complications since the beginning of the pandemic. While the pathophysiology remains unclear in many cases, in some these complications could be due to body's natural immune response to the SARS-CoV-2 virus.

We are presenting a rare and interesting case of a 53-year-old man presenting with limb tremors and collapse following recovery from acute COVID illness. He developed confusion and seizures, with worsening limb tremors, requiring him to be intubated and admitted to critical care unit. His EEG demonstrated an encephalopathic picture, with no evidence of any epileptiform activities. Laboratory markers including cerebrospinal fluid and auto-immune panels for encephalitis were unremarkable. He also underwent whole body CT and PET-CT, which ruled out any malignancies. Following a prolonged ITU admission, he recovered gradually with conservative management and was stepped down to ward. Further clinical observations showed evident features of opsoclonus and myoclonus, which was stimulus sensitive as in keeping with a clinical presentation of opsoclonus myoclonus syndrome. The patient was given a course of IVIG and IV methylprednisolone, followed by oral steroid taper, in addition to symptomatic treatment for myoclonus and agitation. The patient's symptoms significantly improved with the treatment and he was discharged home with follow up appointment in the neurology clinic. The patient was recruited into the COVID Clinical Neuroscience Study (COVID-CNS), a UK wide multicentre study for neurological complications following COVID-19 illness.

To date very few cases of opsoclonus myoclonus has been reported in literature.1, 2, 3, 4 Early identification and management will lead to good patient outcome in terms of morbidity and mortality.
