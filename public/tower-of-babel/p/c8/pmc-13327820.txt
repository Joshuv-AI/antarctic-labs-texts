Trends in the Proportion of Korean Adolescents Engaged in Strength Training, 2016-2025
Authors: Jihee Kim, 지희 김
Published: 2026

In 2025, the proportion of adolescents engaged in strength training was 37.7% for boys and 10.3% for girls. The adolescents engaged in strength training has been an increasing trend for boys over the decade, but there has been no significant change for girls (Figure 1). The 2025 data indicated that the proportion was 3 folds higher in schoolboys (37.7%) than schoolgirls (10.3%) and middle school students (26.2%) were higher than high school students (22.4%) (Figure 2).
