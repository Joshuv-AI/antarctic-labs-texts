Correction to ‘The nuclear and mitochondrial genomes need to tango. How is their dance synchronized during development?’
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: Justin C St. John, The nuclear and mitochondrial genomes need to tango. How is their dance synchronized during development?, NAR Molecular Medicine, Volume 3, Issue 1, January 2026, ugaf042, https://doi.org/10.1093/narmme/ugaf042

The following change has been made to the originally published paper.

An addition has been made to text in the section "Non-pathogenic mtDNA mutations and deletions and their role in oocyte quality", 4th paragraph, last sentence, to read: "Indeed, the persistence of pathogenic mtDNA rearrangements in humans might be explained by the high numbers of carriers of mtDNA rearrangements and efficient medical practice to support affected individuals; and the likely elimination in livestock species since affected animals would have been selected against, based on their commercial non-viability." instead of: "Indeed, the persistence of mtDNA in humans might be explained by the high numbers of carriers of mtDNA rearrangements and efficient medical practice to support affected individuals; and the likely elimination in livestock species since affected animals would have been selected against, based on their commercial non-viability."
