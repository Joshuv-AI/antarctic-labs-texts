Correction to: Cognitive and Neuropathophysiological Outcomes of Gamma-tACS in Dementia: A Systematic Review
Authors: Valerio Manippa, Annalisa Palmisano, Michael A. Nitsche, Marco Filardi, Davide Vilella, Giancarlo Logroscino, Davide Rivolta
Published: 2026

Correction to: Neuropsychology Review (2023) 34:338–361

10.1007/s11065-023-09589-0

In the originally published version of this article, the heading for the “Results” section was inadvertently omitted. This starts with the sentence: “Ten studies (273 patients analyzed) published between 2016 and 2022 were included in this review (Table 1)” within the Methods subheading “Data Extraction and Risk of Bias”. It includes the content under the subheadings “Single-session studies” and “Multi-session studies,” and continues until the start of the “Discussion” section.

The original article has been corrected.
