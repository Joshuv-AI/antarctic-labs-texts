Correction: Olanzapine suppresses mPFC activity-norepinephrine releasing to alleviate CLOCK-enhanced cancer stemness under chronic stress
Authors: Jinxin Lu, Xiaoyu Zhang, Keyu Su, Huandong Luo, Congcong Liu, Yuqing Yang, Bin He, Cenxin Wang, Zhuoran Zhao, Xianxian Liu, Xu Wang, Peixuan Meng, Dekang Lv, Chunli Wang, Keith W Kelley, Ling Wang, Bai Cui, Quentin Liu, Fei Peng
Published: 2026

Cell Commun Signal 22, 375 (2024)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12964-024-01747-y

This article [1] contains an image misplacement in which the representative image of NE with GEM (10 nM) group was shown in Fig. 5A. The authors have amended the image misplacement. Though this correction does not have consequence on the science, data or conclusions of this article, the authors still regret this error.

The original article [1] has been corrected.
