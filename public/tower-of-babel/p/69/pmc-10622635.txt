Trileaflet Mitral Valve: An Unusual Contributor of Mitral Regurgitation in Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy
Authors: Digvijay D Nalawade, Pratik Wadhokar, Ajitkumar Jadhav, Prakash Chaudhary, Anishkumar Khan
Published: 2023

Forty-five years female known case of hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM) was referred for Transthoracic echocardiography (TTE) in view of exertional breathlessness. TTE showed asymmetrical septal hypertrophy with systolic anterior motion (SAM) of mitral valve (MV) leaflet, left ventricular outflow tract (LVOT) gradient of 60 mmHg at rest and moderate mitral regurgitation (MR).

Further analysis of MV revealed possibility of 3 MV leaflets, abnormal ‘Mercedes-star’ appearance of MV opening and 3 papillary muscles in parasternal short-axis views (Figure 1, Movie 1).1) Three-dimensional transesophageal echocardiography (3D TEE) confirmed presence of three leaflets with 3 evenly spaced commissures and central coaptation suggestive of true trileaflet MV (Figure 2, Movies 2 and 3). Differential insertion of atrioventricular valves and central jet MR on colour Doppler (Figure 3) further distinguishes this entity from isolated cleft MV.

MR is common in HCM due to distortion of MV apparatus from SAM secondary to LVOT obstruction but in most cases, intrinsic MV disease is absent.2) Assessment of cause & severity of MR is important as its surgical correction might need not only septal myectomy but also concomitant MV repair or replacement.2) Very few cases of trileaflet MV have been reported in literature with half of them associated with HCM.3) It may remain asymptomatic for years or may present with symptoms of regurgitation or LVOT obstruction.3) Barker et al.3) had described the prognostic implication of having trileaflet MV with deterioration to severe MR over 10 years. 3D TEE is useful to confirm such rare anatomy having prognostic & therapeutic implications. Our patient was optimized for medical management and advised close follow up.
