Asia-Pacific perspectives in neuroscience: Insights from the IBRO APRC mini-series
Authors: Huong Thi Thanh Ha
Published: 2025

Since its establishment in 1961, the International Brain Research Organization (IBRO) has supported global scientific efforts to address brain-related challenges. Among its initiatives, the Asia-Pacific Regional Committee (APRC) Mini-series, launched by IBRO Neuroscience Reports in 2022, serves as a platform to highlight the contributions of neuroscientists from the Asia-Pacific region associated with IBRO. By showcasing the research of IBRO alumni, the series illustrates both the breadth of neuroscience across the region and the importance of interdisciplinary collaboration.

This special issue continues the mission of the IBRO regional Mini-series by featuring diverse contributions that span biology, clinical science, and computational innovation. Collectively, these studies demonstrate the region’s dynamic progress while underscoring the value of international cooperation in advancing brain research.

The collection opens with Dhungel’s exploration of neuroeducation as a culturally contextual approach to managing mass psychogenic illness in Nepal. Lam then examines mitochondrial dysfunction in bipolar disorder, deepening insight into psychiatric disease mechanisms. Do reports on mindfulness practice and its effects on inter-brain connectivity, while Azmi highlights the neural repair potential of full-term amniotic fluid stem cells. From the computational approaches, Dang introduces a deep learning framework for MRI-based glioma classification; Komal explores immune molecules in neuromodulation; and Nguyen demonstrates the promise of ensemble learning that for complex neurobiological data.

Together, these contributions reflect the vitality of Asia-Pacific neuroscience, from culturally adapted interventions and molecular psychiatry to regenerative strategies and AI-driven methods. We extend our deepest appreciation to the authors, reviewers, and IBRO for making this issue possible. By fostering initiatives such as the APRC Mini-series, IBRO continues to elevate Asia-Pacific neuroscience and strengthen global interdisciplinary collaboration as a whole.

We expect that this collection inspires further dialogue and innovation, empowering the next generation of neuroscientists to pursue discovery and impact across borders.
