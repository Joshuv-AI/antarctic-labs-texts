Author Correction: Influence of geomagnetic disturbances on myocardial infarctions in women and men from Brazil
Authors: Luiz Felipe C Rezende, Eurico R De Paula, Marcio T A H Muella, Severino L G Dutra, Reinaldo R Rosa, Paulo H N Saldiva, Jean P H B Ometto
Published: 2026

Correction to: Communications Medicine 10.1038/s43856-025-00887-7, published online 1 July 2025

In the version of the article initially published, in the “Admissions and occurrences in relation to quiet, moderate, disturbed days and sex” section, the text “there were 2012 geomagnetically quiet days with 1000 admissions, 449 geomagnetically moderate days with 220 admissions, and 213 geomagnetically disturbed days with 120 admissions” should have read “there were 2042 geomagnetically quiet days with 1000 admissions, 452 geomagnetically moderate days with 220 admissions, and 214 geomagnetically disturbed days with 120 admissions” and has now been corrected in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
