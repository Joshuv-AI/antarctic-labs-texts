NODG-14 Sub-regional networking in neuro-oncology: towards a West African platform for collaboration
Authors: Mèhomè Wilfried Dossou, Raïssa Abibatou Yasmina Diaby, N’déro Harmonie Virginie Mangbel, Joël Emmanuel N’guessan Brou, Gabriel Gildas Nognegengi, Melaine Landry Konan, Konan Médard Kakou
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractCentral nervous system tumors pose diagnostic and therapeutic problems, and the medical decision-making takes place following a multidisciplinary approach through multi-disciplinary concertation meetings. The implementation of CPR requires above all human resources of quality, including medical specialists, which is being suffered by some regions of the world, including West Africa. This study arms to map available human resources in order to propose a sub-regional collaboration. It a prospective survey consisting of 31 questions addressed to neurosurgeons, radiotherapists, neuro-oncologists, neuro-nursesoncology, pathologists and neurosurgery trainees across West Africa from 13 April to 12 May. To date, 26 participants from 9 countries are enrolled, 7/9 countries have more than 20 neurosurgeons, 6/9 have less than 5 neuroradiologists, all countries have fewer than 5 nurses specialized in neuro-oncology. 4/9 have a neuro-oncology CPR and the meetings are weekly or monthly. The lack of staff, the difficulties of collaboration are reasons why others do not have them. The average experience of participants in neuro-oncology is approximately 3 to 5 years. The average number of neuro-oncology cases per year is about 100 and surgical excision is the most common. The time to obtain pathology results is approximately 3 weeks and 1 month for immunohistochemistry. The clinical studies are not conducted in our region. All participants wish to see a sub-regional CPR with online meetings every month or 2 months. CPR is now a reference in the care of patients in neuro-oncology. It is an essential decision-making process, hence the need for sub-regional collaboration to overcome the resource deficit in some countries of the subregion.
