Analysis and research on iron loss of multi-layer interior permanent magnet synchronous motor for electric vehicle applications
Authors: Lianbo Niu
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractIron loss of stator is one of the main losses of interior permanent magnet synchronous motor (IPMSM). Therefore, effectively reducing iron loss is of great significance for improving operating efficiency. The air gap flux density is analyzed under various operating conditions, and three models for calculating iron loss are analyzed and compared. Among these, the orthogonal decomposition model demonstrates greater accuracy and is relatively straightforward to apply. Consequently, this paper utilizes the orthogonal decomposition model for iron loss calculations. Iron loss is primarily influenced by the operating frequency, as well as the amplitude and harmonic content of the air gap flux density. Thus, at a specific frequency, enhancing the sine degree of the air gap flux density can significantly decrease iron loss. A three-layer I2V type is proposed to further improve sine degree of air gap flux density, achieving high efficiency and low iron loss. V type, IV type and I2V type are selected as the comparative objects for optimization research, and air gap flux density and harmonic contents of three different rotor structures are analyzed and compared, the results show that I2V type have the lowest total harmonic distortion (THD) rates. Compared with V type, the THD rates of I2V type decreased by 27.2%. Iron losses of three different optimized rotor structures are analyzed and compared. Compared with the other two, the results show that I2V type is the smallest, and is significantly less than that of the other two especially under deep flux weakening conditions, which can improve operation range of high efficiency. The I2V prototype is processed and manufactured, and experimental platform is built. Experimental verifications are conducted on loss and efficiency. The test results are basically consistent with FEA results, proving the superior performance of I2V type proposed in this paper, as well as correctness of analysis method and effectiveness of simulation calculations.Keywords: Interior permanent magnet synchronous motor, Air gap flux density, Rotor structure, Iron loss, EfficiencySubject terms: Energy science and technology, Engineering, Physics

Introduction

With the increasingly prominent global energy crisis and environmental pollution issues, energy-efficient and environmentally friendly electric vehicles are receiving more and more attention and favor. And the drive motor is the core technology of electric vehicles, so the requirements for motor related technologies are constantly increasing. Motors are gradually developing towards high power density, high efficiency, constant power with wide speed range, and energy saving1. Compared with surface mounted permanent magnet synchronous motors, interior permanent magnet synchronous motors (IPMSMs) have higher mechanical strength, power density, smaller size, and lighter weight, so they have attracted more attention from domestic and foreign scholars2. Improving torque density and rated speed is an effective method and means for designing IPMSMs with high-power density. At this point, the winding current density and thermal load reach the limit that the material can withstand. The greater the unit volume loss of IPMSM, the more severe the heating phenomenon, and the higher the temperature rise, which can cause a decrease in the performance of permanent magnet (PM) and even the risk of irreversible demagnetization, which not only affects the torque density, efficiency, and other performance of IPMSM, but also puts higher requirements on the insulation level of the winding. Therefore, precise calculation of losses, collaborative optimization of electromagnetic and thermal design, avoiding temperature distribution inside IPMSM exceeding the maximum temperature required for PMs and winding insulation, fully tapping into the potential of IPMSM while ensuring safe and reliable operation, and maximizing its performance. To meet the above requirements and conditions, it is very important to optimize the design of IPMSM in order to meet the above requirements and conditions3–5.

The main performance requirement for IPMSMs in vehicles is to maintain high efficiency within a wide speed range with constant power. When IPMSM runs at high speed, that is, under deep flux weakening conditions, as the speed increases, the frequency also increases. At this time, iron loss has the greatest impact on the overall efficiency of IPMSM. The iron loss of IPMSM includes eddy loss and hysteresis loss, among which eddy loss dominates. At this point, the fundamental amplitude of air gap flux density decreases, and eddy loss of stator is mainly determined by the harmonic of magnetomotive force(MMF) caused by the rotor magnetic barrier. Therefore, the iron loss of stator is greatly affected by rotor topology structure6–9. The stator harmonic loss is mainly caused by the harmonics generated by the PM in rotor. Barcaro et al.10 effectively reduced iron loss of stator by optimizing the magnetic barrier angle and shape of the double-layer rotor of IPMSM. Parasiliti et al.11 improved the torque density and flux weakening speed regulation capability by optimizing the shape and position of magnetic isolation bridge and magnetic barrier.

Chai et al.12 designed an IPMSM with V type rotor topology and optimized the pole arc angle of the V type permanent magnet. The results showed that it can effectively reduce iron core loss. Yamazaki et al.13 optimized the IPMSM IV with type rotor topology, and the results showed that the iron loss was significantly reduced, while the average torque was only slightly reduced. Tang et al.14 conducted a comparative analysis of stator core loss of IPMSMs with V type, 2 V type, 3 V type, and 4 V type under deep flux weakening conditions. The results showed that the stator core losses decreased with the increase of number layers of PM. Han et al.15 found that the higher the operating speed, the greater the eddy current loss of PM, and the more severe heating of PM, which can lead to irreversible demagnetization, and it is also one of the main problems commonly found in IPMSMs. Research has found that the higher the operating speed of IPMSM, the greater the eddy loss of the PM, and the more severe the heating of the PM, A study was conducted on the torque ripple and iron loss of IPMSMs, and the results showed that the optimized IPMSM had significantly reduced iron loss, However, the torque ripple increased significantly, making it difficult to achieve simultaneous reduction r torque ripple and iron loss16,17.

To further reduce iron loss and improve operating efficiency of IPMSMs, a three-layer rotor topology structure with I2V type is proposed. A 2D finite element parameterized model is established, and three different rotor topology optimization models are ultimately determined under the same constraint of PM quantity. Firstly, the air gap flux density under different operating conditions was analyzed separately, and three iron loss calculation models were compared and analyzed. The orthogonal decomposition calculation model was determined to calculate iron loss. Secondly, the iron loss and efficiency of IPMSM with three optimized rotor topologies are analyzed and compared. Finally, an I2V prototype was manufactured and an experimental platform was built to test loss, temperature rise and efficiency of the prototype.

Analysis of air gap flux density

By controlling and adjusting the current angle, IPMSM can be divided into constant torque control and constant power flux weakening control. The direct and quadrature axis currents of IPMSM change with the variation of current angle, furthermore, different armature reaction flux linkages are generated, and the interaction between armature reaction flux linkage and flux linkage generated by PM produces different stator synthesized flux linkage. Different stator synthesized flux linkages cause changes in amplitude and harmonic content of the load air gap flux density waveform, resulting in different iron losses. By analyzing the armature reaction flux density under separate excitation of the current source, the no-load flux density under separate excitation of PM, and the synthesized load flux density, the flux density of yoke and teeth of stator are further obtained, and then calculate iron loss of stator.

Analysis of iron loss calculation model

Iron loss is one of the main losses of IPMSMs, especially during deep flux weakening condition with high speed. Iron loss will rapidly increase with increase of speed, accounting for a large proportion of the total loss. Therefore, reducing iron loss effectively is of great significance for improving operating efficiency and reducing temperature rise19,20. Iron loss calculation models include Bertotti separation model, elliptical rotation model, and orthogonal decomposition model.

Bertotti separation model

The waveform of flux density passing through the stator core changes with time and spatial variables, and alternating magnetic flux generates iron loss in the core, which generally includes hysteresis loss, eddy loss, and additional loss. Hysteresis loss is the loss caused by repeated magnetization of iron core materials during the process of magnetic field alternation. Conductors generate induced currents, also known as eddy currents, inside an alternating magnetic field. The higher the frequency of alternating magnetic field, the greater the eddy currents. The induced eddy current energy causes the magnetic core to heat up and cause losses, known as eddy losses. Additional losses refer to all losses except for hysteresis losses and eddy losses. The influence of local hysteresis loops is usually ignored, and it is believed that hysteresis losses are independent of the waveform of flux density and are only proportional to the amplitude and frequency; Eddy losses are not only related to the alternating frequency and amplitude, but also closely related to the sine and harmonic content of the flux density waveform. Elliptical rotation model

As mentioned above, the two magnetization methods for ferromagnetic materials in motors are alternating magnetization and rotational magnetization. The direction of flux density of alternating magnetization remains unchanged, and the amplitude changes over time. Rotating magnetization can be divided into circular and elliptical rotating magnetization. The amplitude of flux density in circular rotating magnetization remains constant, and the magnetization direction rotates over time, while the amplitude and direction of flux density in elliptical rotating magnetization change over time. A large number of experimental results have shown that the hysteresis loss characteristics of ferromagnetic materials are not different under circular rotating magnetic fields and alternating magnetic fields. At the beginning, rotational hysteresis loss has the same characteristics as alternating hysteresis loss, and hysteresis loss increases with the increase of amplitude of flux density. However, when the amplitude of flux density increases to a certain extent, the magnetic circuit reaches saturation state, and the rotational hysteresis loss decreases with the increase of amplitude of flux density22.

Orthogonal decomposition model

Compared with the other two iron loss calculation models mentioned above, theoretical basis and application advantages of orthogonal decomposition calculation model are reflected in the following aspects:

Simplification of mathematical processing

Simplification of mathematical processing

Orthogonal decomposition method transforms complex physical phenomena into mathematical processing problems in iron loss calculation, simplifying calculation process by decomposing flux density into orthogonal components. This method can decompose complex iron loss calculation into a series of simple mathematical operations.

(b)Theoretical basis

Theoretical basis

The orthogonal decomposition model in iron loss calculation is usually based on characteristics of hysteresis, eddy, and additional losses of iron core material. The Bertotti model is a commonly used formula for calculating iron loss, which takes into account iron loss at different frequencies. Through orthogonal decomposition, these loss components can be independently calculated and then combined to obtain total iron loss. This decomposition method is in line with actual physical characteristics of iron core materials, that is, hysteresis loss is proportional to frequency, eddy loss is proportional to square of frequency and flux density, and additional loss depends on the combination of frequency and flux density.

(c)Accuracy and efficiency of calculation

Accuracy and efficiency of calculation

The orthogonal decomposition calculation model considers high-frequency iron loss and extracts harmonic components through Fourier decomposition, which can more accurately calculate iron loss in transient fields. This calculation method based on multi frequency Bertotti model can more accurately reflect dynamic changes of iron loss compared to traditional single frequency models, improving accuracy and efficiency of calculations.

In summary, the classic iron loss separation calculation model only considers iron loss caused by the alternating magnetization of flux density, while in actual the flux density not only affects alternating magnetization but also rotating magnetization of iron core. The elliptical rotation calculation model for iron loss has high calculation accuracy, but it is difficult to obtain the loss coefficient under circular rotation magnetization conditions. However, the orthogonal decomposition calculation model can effectively simplify mathematical processing, accurately reflect physical properties, improve calculation accuracy and efficiency in iron loss calculation, and has wide applicability and flexibility. This method simplifies complex iron loss calculation problems by orthogonally decomposing iron loss components. The orthogonal decomposition calculation model for iron loss uses two mutually orthogonal alternating magnetizations to approximate the equivalent rotational magnetization. And any elliptical magnetic field can be orthogonally decomposed into radial and tangential components, with high calculation accuracy and relatively easy implementation. Therefore, an orthogonal decomposition calculation model is used to analyze and calculate iron loss in this paper. The total iron loss of stator is obtained by adding iron loss of stator teeth and yoke. From the above analysis, it can be found that the iron loss is mainly determined by the speed (frequency), followed by the amplitude of flux density, harmonic content, and local flux density variation in one cycle. Under a constant speed (frequency), increasing the sine degree of flux density waveform and reducing harmonic content are effective means to reduce iron loss.

Optimization and performance comparison of three different rotor topology structures

Due to the high magnetic saturation of flux density at the isolation bridge of IPMSM, the harmonic content of air gap flux density is mainly determined by the geometric topology of rotor. If rotor topology is not optimized, the harmonic content of air gap flux density will increase. During high-speed operation, these harmonics cause an increase in amplitude and frequency fluctuation of flux density in stator core, leading to a rapid increase in iron loss and a significant decrease in efficiency19. The air gap flux density is mainly determined by the magnetomotive force of PM in rotor, so reducing harmonic content of air gap flux density can effectively reduce the harmonic content of PM and reduce iron loss. It can be concluded from references25 that increasing the number of PM layers in rotor can effectively improve sinusoidal degree of no-load air gap flux density and back electromotive force (EMF), reduce harmonic content, thereby reducing iron loss and improving efficiency.

The harmonic contents of air gap flux density and no-load back EMF waveform are mainly determined by geometric topology of the rotor. The smaller the total harmonic distortion rate of air gap flux density and no-load back EMF waveform, the lower the iron loss. By establishing a 2D finite element parametric model, the V type PM opening angle and pole arc angle of first layer, which have the greatest impact on electromagnetic performance of IPMSM, are optimized and analyzed to determine rotor topology structure. Through the analysis of iron loss under different operating conditions mentioned above, it was found that when the speed is low, hysteresis loss is the main cause of iron loss. Therefore, in order to effectively reduce eddy loss, silicon steel sheets are becoming thinner and thinner in motor design.

Performance testing of I2V prototype

The schematic diagram and test bench of prototype experiment are shown in Fig. 38, including a 250 kW/500 Nm/20000 rpm loading system DynoSpirit dynamometer, a 250 kW/800 V battery simulator, a dynamometer frequency converter, a speed sensor and its measuring instrument, a torque sensor, a coolant temperature control system, a power analyzer, etc. The DynoSpirit loading system is used for steady-state, transient, and high dynamic testing of high-speed electric vehicle drive motors. The PMSM of DynoTrain loading system has the characteristics of 4-quadrant operation, high speed, and high dynamics, making it particularly suitable for high-quality testing of the entire operating range of the vehicle’s drive motor from zero speed to maximum speed in a clockwise and counterclockwise direction.

Experimental testing schematic and testing bed.

The prototype to be tested is connected to the dynamometer (PMSM) through a coupling. The prototype inverter driver can automatically adjust proportional integral control parameters of speed loop and current loop in vector control.

The prototype inverter driver can pre write a parameter table based on simulation results and use a lookup table method to perform weak magnetic expansion control on the motor, thereby obtaining experimental results at different speeds and loads of the prototype. The prototype drive controller is controlled by Maximum Torque Per Ampere (MTPA), the dynamometer (PMSM) adopts speed control mode, and the prototype adopts torque control mode.

Conclusion

The effects of time harmonics, spatial harmonics, and rotational magnetization on iron loss of stator in IPMSMs are analyzed and the armature reaction magnetic field, no-load flux density, and load flux density of each part of stator are theoretically analyzed and calculated.

The effects of time harmonics, spatial harmonics, and rotational magnetization on iron loss of stator in IPMSMs are analyzed and the armature reaction magnetic field, no-load flux density, and load flux density of each part of stator are theoretically analyzed and calculated.

Using orthogonal decomposition method, the iron loss of IPMSM with optimized rotor topology structures are analyzed and compared. The analysis results show that under rated operating conditions, compared with IV type, the total iron loss of I2V type is reduced by 82.4W, a decrease of 6.5%. Under moderate flux weakening conditions, compared with V type, the total iron loss decreased by 505.1W, a decrease of 21.7%. Under deep flux weakening conditions, compared with V type, the total iron loss has been significantly reduced by 4864.0W, a decrease of 54.1%. In summary, compared with the other two rotors, the IPMSM with I2V type has the smallest iron loss under different operating conditions, especially under the deep flux weakening conditions, achieving the optimization goal of high speed and low iron loss. The prototype test also verified that actual losses of IPMSM were basically consistent with simulation results, further proving correctness of analysis method and effectiveness of simulation calculation in this paper.
