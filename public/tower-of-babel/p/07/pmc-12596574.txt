RART-05 SURGICAL EXCISION OF FIBROUS PITUITARY ADENOMAS (FPA) BY LOW ENDOSCOPY IN 3 CASES
Authors: Harmonie Virginie Mangbel’ Ndero
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractAlthough rare, TSH pituitary adenomas are distinguished by their invasive and fibrous appearance. As they are macroadenomas, their total excision is often not possible via the lower approach; the transsphenoidal approach is preferred.ObjectiveTo propose a technique for low endoscopic surgical excision of fibrous adenomas with a greater probability of complete resection.MethodsRetroprospective cross-sectional study, with descriptive approaches covering three files of patients who presented with a fibrous pituitary adenoma operated by transsphenoidal transnasal endoscopic approach over a period from 2023-2025.ResultsIncomplete removal of a fibrous macroadenoma in 2 patients with a mean age of 46.67 years; all males. The hormonal balance revealed a drop in free T3, T4 in 2 of them. These adenomas were diagnosed on MRI as macroadenomas in all 03 patients, all of whom had undergone endoscopic surgical approach via the lower left nostril under neuronavigation. In 2 patients, the tumors had a mixed macroscopic appearance (soft-fibrous), while in the third, they were almost fibrous. The quality of excision was macroscopically complete. Postoperatively, one patient presented complications.ConclusionAdenoma entity difficult to extract by low endoscopy, if macroadenomas. Our technique allows total removal. It remains to be improved, as it is still associated with negligible morbidity.
