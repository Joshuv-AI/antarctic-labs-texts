Estimating best nanomaterial for energy harvesting through reinforcement learning DQN coupled with fuzzy PROMETHEE under road-based conditions
Authors: Sekar Kidambi Raju, Ganesh Karthikeyan Varadarajan, Amal H Alharbi, Subhash Kannan, Doaa Sami Khafaga, Raj Anand Sundaramoorthy, Marwa M Eid, S K Towfek
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractEnergy harvesters based on nanomaterials are getting more and more popular, but on their way to commercial availability, some crucial issues still need to be solved. The objective of the study is to select an appropriate nanomaterial. Using features of the Reinforcement Deep Q-Network (DQN) in conjunction with Fuzzy PROMETHEE, the proposed model, we present in this work a hybrid fuzzy approach to selecting appropriate materials for a vehicle-environmental-hazardous substance (EHS) combination that operates in roadways and under traffic conditions. The DQN is able to accumulate useful experience of operating in a dynamic traffic environment, accordingly selecting materials that deliver the highest energy output but at the same time bring consideration to factors such as durability, cost, and environmental impact. Fuzzy PROMETHEE allows the participation of human experts during the decision-making process, going beyond the quantitative data typically learned by DQN through the inclusion of qualitative preferences. Instead, this hybrid method unites the strength of individual approaches, as a result providing highly resistant and adjustable material selection to real EHS. The result of the study pointed out materials that can give high energy efficiency with reference to years of service, price, and environmental effects. The proposed model provides 95% accuracy with a computational efficiency of 300 s, and the application of hypothesis and practical testing on the chosen materials showed the high efficiency of the selected materials to harvest energy under fluctuating traffic conditions and proved the concept of a hybrid approach in True Vehicle Environmental High-risk Substance scenarios.Keywords: Nanomaterial selection mechanism, Energy harvesting schemes, Reinforcement learning approaches, Deep Q-network, Fuzzy PROMETHEE, Road traffic conditionsSubject terms: Environmental sciences, Ocean sciences, Materials science, Mathematics and computing

Perfect performance is indicated by an AUC of 1, while no better performance than random guessing is shown by an AUC of 0.5. The suggested model has an excellent AUC of 0.98. This indicates that positive and negative situations may be distinguished by the model with great accuracy.

Conclusion

The creation of a comprehensive and flexible method designed to efficiently pick nanomaterials for enhancing energy harvesting systems (EHS) in dynamic road traffic conditions has led to a ground-breaking development in sustainable energy practices. In addition to emphasizing energy output, our novel approach also takes important aspects like longevity, economy, and environmental sustainability into account. Through the smooth integration of state-of-the-art methods such as Fuzzy PROMETHEE and Reinforcement Deep Q-Network, our methodology skillfully tackles the complex problems presented by real-world situations, especially the dynamic conditions present in highway traffic settings. This extensive study both emphasizes the critical significance of energy gathering devices and further advances their evolution. Additionally, our focus on resilient materials that can withstand the rigors of road traffic and ecologically friendly materials highlights our dedication to sustainable energy practices that align with both current requirements and future goals.

Future work

The work in the future involves the continuation of extant material databases with properties and performance indicators, improvements in optimizing techniques, and comprehensive comparisons with case scenarios at other sites and traffic loads to verify the theoretical models. Environmental concerns will be dealt with through a lifecycle assessment of the proposed designs, whereas integration with other automobile systems and feasibility studies to handle the system of the proposed designs will also be considered. Collaboration between them will assist in solving complex problems while keeping an eye on the regulation and safety aspects will make deployment safe. The usage of new technologies in the selection process will preserve the relevance of the methodology and enhance the selection process constantly with the help of new conditions that appear in the course of work.
