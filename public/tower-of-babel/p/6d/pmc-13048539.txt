Odronextamab in people with relapsed or refractory follicular lymphoma: plain language summary of the ELM-2 study
Authors: Jose C. Villasboas, Michelle Poon, Monica Tani, Ana Jiménez-Ubieto, Benoit Tessoulin, Lorna Warwick, Deborah Brown, Manjusha Namuduri, Chinjune Lin, Virginie Delwart, Deepa Jagadeesh
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
Plain Language Summary What is this summary about? In the ELM-2 study, a medicine called odronextamab was investigated to measure how effective it is at treating people with follicular lymphoma and to understand more about possible side effects. Follicular lymphoma is a type of blood cancer that affects white blood cells called B cells, which can build up in lymph nodes, making them swell. What were the key results? Overall, 128 participants with follicular lymphoma, whose cancer had returned after responding to previous treatment or didn’t respond after two or more previous treatments, received odronextamab in the ELM-2 study. Odronextamab treatment reduced the cancer in 80% of participants. The cancer disappeared completely (called a complete response) in 73% of participants. For half of participants with a complete response to treatment, responses were estimated to have lasted for at least 25 months. Participants with a complete response lived longest.Everyone in ELM-2 had some type of side effect. The odronextamab dose needed to be lowered for 9% of participants and was stopped permanently for 16% of participants because of side effects. The most common side effects were cytokine release syndrome (in 56% of participants, explained below), neutropenia (low levels of white blood cells called neutrophils, in 39%), and fever (38%).Participants completed surveys to help researchers learn how odronextamab affected their daily life. Before treatment, participants generally rated their daily life as good, and for most, this was unchanged or improved after 50 weeks of treatment. What were the main conclusions reported by the researchers? Odronextamab could be a new potential treatment option for some people with follicular lymphoma whose cancer didn’t respond to multiple previous treatments.Clinical trial number: NCT03888105 (ELM-2 study)
