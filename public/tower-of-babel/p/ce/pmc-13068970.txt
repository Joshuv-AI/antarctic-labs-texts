Author Correction: Label-free whole slide virtual multi-staining using dual-excitation photon absorption remote sensing microscopy
Authors: James E. D. Tweel, Benjamin R. Ecclestone, James A. Tummon Simmons, Parsin Haji Reza
Published: 2026

Correction to: npj Imaging 10.1038/s44303-026-00154-x, published online 31 March 2026

“In the original version of this article, the animal declaration statement was inadvertently omitted from the section ‘Sample Preparation’. The statement now reads “The mouse tissue used in this study was obtained from animals housed and maintained at the University of Waterloo Central Animal Facility. All procedures were conducted under an approved institutional animal care protocol (University of Waterloo Animal Care Committee Protocol ID #44703), in accordance with relevant guidelines and regulations.” The original article has been updated accordingly.”
