Author Correction: Pain prevalence, intensity, and association with neuropsychiatric symptoms of dementia in immigrant and non-immigrant aged care residents in Australia
Authors: Pelden Chejor, Mustafa Atee, Patricia Cain, Daniel Whiting, Thomas Morris, Davina Porock
Published: 2024

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-68110-6, published online 23 July 2024

The original version of this Article contains an error in the Introduction section.

“A recent scoping review examining the burden of chronic musculoskeletal pain in immigrants and non-immigrants reported female gender, lower education, economic hardships, mental health disorders (e.g., anxiety, depression, and post-traumatic stress disorder) were associated with increasing prevalence, severity of chronic pain in immigrants24.”

now reads:

“A recent scoping review examining the burden of chronic musculoskeletal pain in immigrants and non-immigrants reported female gender, lower education, economic hardships, and mental health disorders (e.g., anxiety, depression, post-traumatic stress disorder) were associated with increasing prevalence and severity of chronic pain in immigrants24.”

The original Article has been corrected.
