We Are in This Together
Authors: Kenneth J. Noonan
Published: 2026

Why publish your research and educational contributions in JPOSNA®? Because you care about children.

The mission of the Pediatric Orthopaedic Society of North America (POSNA)is to “Advance pediatric orthopaedics by promoting education, research, and quality care.” POSNA’s vision is “A world with optimal musculoskeletal health for all children.” JPOSNA® is the vehicle that will take us there.

POSNA published the inaugural issue of JPOSNA® in November 2019, featuring educational content. It began publishing peer-reviewed original research in 2022. Today, the journal is indexed in all major scientific literature databases, including PubMed and Clarivate’s Web of Science. In June 2026, the journal earned its first impact factor, marking a significant milestone in its ongoing growth.

JPOSNA® is a Gold Open Access (OA) journal that makes text, images, video, and even panel discussions available to anyone in the world with internet access. The evidence on OA’s impact on readership is striking. OA articles generate more downloads than subscription-based journal articles in the first six months after publication. More tellingly, readership does not drop off. OA articles continue to attract steady downloads long after publication, while pay-walled articles typically see an initial spike in usage and then decline. Is it not wonderful to think that your research and your approach to pediatric orthopaedic care are discoverable anywhere a child needs help, free of charge?

OA journals shift the cost of publication from readers to authors. JPOSNA® partnered with Elsevier in late 2023 to get us this far, and our article processing charges (APCs) are currently lower than those of comparable OA journals. If you are a POSNA member, your APC is reduced by 30%, and some institutions offer additional discounts (check with your institutional administration or visit Elsevier’s Open Access Agreement page located here https://www.elsevier.com/open-access/agreements).

Every Editorial Board member who publishes in JPOSNA® pays an APC, and none of the editors receive compensation for their work. Revenue generated through JPOSNA® is reinvested to advance POSNA’s mission. A portion of every APC paid by authors helps improve children’s care. JPOSNA® is successful because our society and other pediatric orthopaedists are world experts, and we produce content that improves children’s musculoskeletal health. Our expert Editorial Board believes that each submission has the potential to help children. Whenever possible, we do not simply reject papers that do not meet our publication standards. Instead, we work closely with our authors to pivot their submissions, so they provide meaningful value to our readers. Central to this process is the expertise of our approximately 200 volunteer reviewers, who ensure that JPOSNA® maintains the highest quality standards. Their dedication exemplifies POSNA volunteerism at its best because, in the end, everything we do is for the children.
