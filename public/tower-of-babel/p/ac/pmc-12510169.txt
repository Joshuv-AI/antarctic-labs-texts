Response to "Letter to the editor related to the article "Trends in mortality due to ischemic heart diseases among patients with Alzheimer's disease in the United States from 1999 to 2020. International journal of cardiology. Cardiovascular risk and prevention, 25, 200390″
Authors: Muzamil Akhtar, Rayyan Nabi, Raheel Ahmed
Published: 2025

We appreciate the thoughtful critique and the opportunity to clarify aspects of our study. Constructive feedback such as this supports ongoing research transparency and improvement.

First, we respectfully clarify that our data were accurately extracted using ICD-10 codes I20–I25, corresponding to ischemic heart diseases, as correctly stated in the abstract's methods section. A typographical error occurred in the manuscript's methodology section, where the range was inadvertently listed as I25–I29. We appreciate this being noted and have attached the raw data files to confirm the correct coding. A corrigendum has been requested to address this error.

Second, regarding the figure labels reading “AAMR per 100,000 deaths,” this was a typographical oversight. As outlined in our methods, age-adjusted mortality rates (AAMRs) were calculated per 100,000 people, in line with epidemiological standards. This correction is also included in our corrigendum request.

Lastly, on interpreting trends with confidence intervals including zero, we emphasize that such results were not overstated. While some Annual Percent Changes (APCs) did not reach statistical significance, all findings were transparently presented with 95 % confidence intervals. We avoided labelling non-significant findings as statistically significant but acknowledge the value of clearer emphasis on statistical relevance in future reports.

We thank the author again for their constructive engagement and helpful suggestions.
