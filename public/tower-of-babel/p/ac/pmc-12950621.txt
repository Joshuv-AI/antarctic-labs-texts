Published: 2026

J. Xiong, Y. Wei, X. Huang, et al., “A Meta‐Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials (RCTs) Investigating the Efficacy and Safety of Acupuncture in Treating Myocardial Ischemia/Reperfusion (I/R) Injury,” Cardiology Research and Practice, no. 2025 (2025), https://doi.org/10.1155/crp/9970541.

In the article titled “A Meta‐Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials (RCTs) Investigating the Efficacy and Safety of Acupuncture in Treating Myocardial Ischemia/Reperfusion (I/R) Injury,” there are errors in the legends of Figures 6, 9, and 17.
