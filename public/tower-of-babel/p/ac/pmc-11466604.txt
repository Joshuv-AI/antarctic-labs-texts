Application of knowledge graph in smart irrigation district management decision making
Authors: Shaonan Sun, Yuqing Ding, Guoyu Dong, Ailing Wang
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractRegarding the traditional irrigation and drainage management methods in China's irrigation districts, there are problems such as complex management processes, dispersed information systems, difficulty in resource sharing, diverse data sources, and insufficient intelligent auxiliary decision-making. In order to further realize refined intelligent management decision-making on issues related to smart irrigation districts, this article uses the basic information of 28 Yellow River diversion irrigation districts in Henan Province, as well as the overall plan reports and related issue management manuals of 7 management offices of the first and second phase projects of Zhaokou Irrigation District as data sources. Collect and organize data from irrigation areas, and use these to build a knowledge graph for management of issues in irrigation areas diverted from the Yellow River. At the same time, the BERT + BiLSTM + CRF model is used to intelligently identify entities such as irrigation projects and problem events in the Zhaokou Irrigation District inspection log text, and entity alignment technology is used to match the inspection text with entities in the knowledge graph. Finally, combined with the graph retrieval function, the intelligent generation of smart irrigation district management decision-making solutions is realized. The validation of specific irrigation district examples and analysis of the evaluation indexes of relevant models demonstrate the reliability of the problem management decision-making scheme proposed in this paper. This application effectively facilitates the integration of information data in the Yellow River diversion irrigation area and enables visual management of intelligent irrigation zones, presenting a novel concept for the informationization construction of China's irrigation areas.Keywords: Smart irrigation district, Issue management, Knowledge graph, Intelligent decision-making, Visualization

Methodology

Knowledge graph construction techniques

Knowledge graph is a graph-based data structure and a structured semantic web knowledge base [21]. By utilizing knowledge graph technology, various concepts and entities can be linked together to form a networked knowledge structure thereby achieving multi-source and multi-factor collaborative association analysis applications. The process of building a knowledge graph includes modeling [22], extracting [23], fusing and storing knowledge [24]. Fig. 2 shows the knowledge graph construction process.

Knowledge graph construction process.

Entity recognition technology methods and models

The extraction of knowledge entities, also known as Named Entity Recognition (NER), is to identify the boundaries of represented entities from the text [25],and further determine their categories. This task plays a key role in both the construction and application of knowledge graphs. There are various method models for entity recognition, which can be mainly divided into template or rule-based methods [26], traditional machine learning algorithms [27] and deep learning-based methods [28]. Defining templates or rules is one of the simplest methods, which has the advantage of accurate extraction. However, this approach necessitates substantial domain knowledge to support rule definition, rendering it more time-consuming and labor-intensive; The machine learning algorithm must first determine the label system for entity recognition. The more complex the system, the higher the cost of corpus labeling required. Hidden Markov Model [29] (HMM) is one of the more traditional machine learning models, but due to the passage of time, such models are gradually being replaced by deep learning models; With the development of deep neural networks, scholars have begun to explore entity recognition methods based on deep learning. Chen, Y [30]et al. proposed a combination of Bidirectional Long Short Term Memory Network (BiLSTM) and Conditional Random Field (CRF) model in a named entity recognition task in the medical domain. Song, L [31]et al. used the BiLSTM + CRF model in the entity extraction task related to agricultural demand text. They conducted a comparative analysis against traditional models and concluded that the BiLSTM + CRF model yielded superior effectiveness. Therefore, in this paper, based on Google's Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers(BERT)model, combined with BiLSTM + CRF model, text entities can be effectively recognized with a small amount of irrigation knowledge labeling data. This approach circumvents the requirement for extensive labeling data while still achieving high accuracy rates.

Entity alignment technology

Entity Alignment, also known as Entity Matching, is mainly used to link entities with the same meaning from different knowledge bases as a way of discovering equivalent pairs of entities [32], and this technique is one of the important steps in realizing the generation of intelligent decision-making solutions. Entity alignment techniques can be broadly categorized into two main groups: one is the traditional entity alignment methods [33], which focus on matching entities by calculating the distances between labels and characters associated with them. For example, Cohen [34]et al. utilize Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TFIDF) to calculate the distance between entity names, and if the distance falls within a predefined threshold, the two entities are considered a match. By iterating through all potential entity pairs in E1×E2, the set of candidate entity pairs can be obtained and the binary classification function can be trained to perform entity alignment. However, the majority of these methods require manual data annotation and have limitations that cannot be well addressed when confronted with specialized and complex irrigation knowledge; Another category comprises representation learning-based entity alignment methods [35], which aim to utilize machine learning techniques to generate vector representations for the relationships between entities, and to represent the semantic relationships between objects by measuring the distance between two vectors. Wang, Z [36]et al. proposed the Entity-Pair Embedding Approach (EPEA) model, which firstly uses a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) to extract the attribute features and passes the feature similarity between the entities by edge-attentive Graph Neural Network (GNN).EPEA treats the attribute uniformly pairs of two entities as strings and calculates the Jaccard similarity of the attribute values. This class of methods has become the main technique for solving the entity alignment problem nowadays and has achieved better results. Therefore, in this paper, we choose the model based on representation learning and utilize the cosine similarity between vectors to calculate the similarity between entities, and fuse other entity features in the inspection text to accomplish the entity alignment task.

Modelling

Smart irrigation district refers to the use of modern information technology, automation control technology, Internet of Things, big data analysis and artificial intelligence and other advanced technologies, the traditional irrigation district for information technology upgrades as well as intelligent management, smart irrigation district represents the direction of the development of agricultural modernisation, through information technology and intelligent means, so that the irrigation district management is more scientific, efficient and sustainable, and it is an important mode of agricultural production and water resource management in the future. Under the development of global intelligence, the level of intelligent management of irrigation district information is slightly insufficient compared with other industries as well as the construction of water conservancy projects, and the related problem management manuals are presented as semi-structured or unstructured text, thus leading to poor knowledge relevance and low efficiency of search query.

Knowledge modeling

In the process of knowledge modeling, ontology construction is required, and the ontology of knowledge graph is a tree-like knowledge structure, which enables the sharing of knowledge information and can be widely recognized and used [37]. In the knowledge graph of management of problems related to the irrigation district, the irrigation district ontology plays the role of skeleton and core, providing a clear structure and underlying construction mode for building the knowledge graph [38]. Therefore, before starting the construction of the irrigation district knowledge graph, the work of ontology construction has to be carried out first. This paper takes the basic information of 28 Yellow River diversion irrigation districts in Henan Province, as well as the overall program report of 7 management offices of Zhaokou Irrigation District Phase I and II Project and the management manual of related issues as the data source, and at the same time, it adopts the bottom-up and top-down methods to construct the ontology and the relationship between the ontologies, and the following Fig. 3 is the model of ontology of management of related issues of Yellow River diversion irrigation districts.

Knowledge extraction and integration

Knowledge extraction is the process of filtering and extracting knowledge related to a specific research area from a variety of different data sources for deeper knowledge representation and knowledge management [39]. These data sources can be categorized into structured data (e.g., databases), semi-structured data (e.g., tables, lists in web pages), and unstructured data (e.g., plain text data). In this paper, the information related to the irrigation management program is mainly in semi-structured tables, with only a small amount of basic information in unstructured textual data, and most of it in short texts. Therefore, we can use convenient and efficient manual extraction with regular templates [40] for knowledge extraction. Through knowledge extraction, a large amount of knowledge can be initially obtained, but due to the different sources of this knowledge, there may be instances of co-reference between them, so it is necessary to carry out co-reference disambiguation through knowledge fusion [41], so as to make the connection of knowledge more tightly. Knowledge storage

In Resource Description Framework (RDF), the structure of basic units (node 1, edge, node 2) is used to describe the relationships between entities [42]. After extracting and fusing the knowledge of the data sources of the Yellow River diversion irrigation area, the resulting entities and relationships are organized into information in ternary format and coded for storage. Neo4j [43]is a high-performance embeddable lightweight database that is optimized for scalability, resource sharing, and efficient search. Unlike the traditional table data storage model, Neo4j utilizes the node space and represents the data domain in the form of a graph. Therefore Neo4j is chosen for this study to store the triples. Problem-related entity identification

In the daily maintenance work of the irrigation district, the managers of the problems found during the inspection are usually recorded in the form of short text, and it is difficult to intelligently generate decision-making solutions through graph retrieval, so the first step is to recognize the entity of the recorded short text. As there are fewer dictionaries as well as annotated data in the related fields, this paper constructs BERT + BiLSTM + CRF model based on the deep learning method to recognize entities in short texts, and the implementation process is shown in Fig. 5 below. To inspect the text of a problem occurring in the text of the information “Jiasi trunk canal siltation” as an example, first of all need to input the text of the information on the coding layer of processing, so that the text is converted into figures, and then the text is converted into figures. Then the hidden feature signals in the words and sentences are mined through the BERT model and the vector matrix is generated, then the BiLSTM model is used to make the vectors combined with the contextual semantic information, based on which a decoding layer is superimposed to get the corresponding entity labels, and finally the dependency between labels is obtained through Conditional Random Field (CRF), which outputs the optimal sequence of entity labels.

Entity alignment

Word2Vec is an efficient tool open-sourced by Google in 2013 to represent words as real-valued vectors [44], which is widely used in the field of natural language processing. Considering the limitations of the relevant managers of irrigation districts in terms of specialized knowledge and terminology, there may be differences in the content of the text recorded by different staff members when inspecting the irrigation districts, and thus the relevant entities extracted from the recorded text may not be exactly the same as those in the graph. To solve this problem, this paper generates low-dimensional vector representations of problematic event-related entities in inspection texts based on Word2Vec, and at the same time fuses the cosine similarity between the vectors to compute the similarity between the words as a means of realizing the alignment of the main problematic event-related entities in the inspection texts of daily management and problematic events in the atlas. Conclusions

This paper proposes an intelligent irrigation district management decision-making application based on knowledge graph technology, and explores the intelligent generation method of the decision-making scheme for the problems related to the diversion of Yellow River irrigation district by using the knowledge graph related technology. The key points are summarized as follows.

(1)Taking the Zhaokou Irrigation District in Henan Province as a case study, this paper analyzes the engineering problems existing in the Yellow River diversion irrigation districts based on the reports on the overall programs of the seven management offices of the first and second phases of the irrigation district and the manuals of management response to the related problems, as well as the related basic data of the 28 Yellow River diversion irrigation districts in Henan Province. Additionally, the paper proposes an ontology model for a problem management knowledge graph, and the knowledge extraction and storage technology is utilized to construct the knowledge graph of problem management related to the Yellow River diversion irrigation districts. This work lays a foundation framework for the intelligent management decision-making of the Yellow River diversion irrigation districts;

This work lays a foundation framework for the intelligent management decision-making of the Yellow River diversion irrigation districts;

(2)This paper uses the entity recognition model of BERT + BiLSTM + CRF to achieve effective recognition of relevant problematic entities based on the daily inspection record log of Zhaokou irrigation district. At the same time, an effective cosine similarity algorithm based on Word2Vec fusion vectors is proposed to effectively align the entities identified in the recorded text with those in the knowledge graph, this work provides support for the intelligent generation of decision-making scheme for the management of the Yellow River diversion irrigation districts;
