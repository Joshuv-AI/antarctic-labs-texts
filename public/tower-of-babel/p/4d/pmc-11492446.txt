Research on risk management of ship maintenance projects based on multi agent swarm model simulation method
Authors: Kewen Wang, Peng Dong, Weibing Chen, Rui Ma, Longyu Cui
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
In recent years, the role of naval ship power has become increasingly prominent in regional conflicts and military operations worldwide, and the powerful capabilities demonstrated by naval forces have also been valued by countries around the world. While naval forces are frequently deployed to carry out various tasks, the intensity of equipment wear and tear on ships is also significantly increasing, posing a huge challenge to the maintenance and support capabilities of ship equipment. In the actual process of ship equipment maintenance, a series of risk factors such as cost, schedule, and quality will have a significant impact on the entire ship maintenance project, and even affect the success or failure of the entire project. This article first decomposes the project process of ship maintenance projects, further analyzes the various risk factors of ship maintenance projects based on the project decomposition situation, and then distinguishes the overall project, project process, and project risk factors to establish an intelligent agent group model for simulation analysis. Through the above simulation analysis, we will conduct in-depth research on the specific impact of project risks on ship maintenance projects, and analyze and verify the positive role and practical significance of project risk management in project risk control and project efficiency improvement. In the process of the above research content, methods and measures for risk management of ship maintenance projects are proposed, providing scientific and efficient risk management suggestions for such projects, improving the scientific management efficiency of ship maintenance projects, and contributing to the improvement of the quality and efficiency of project management.

Introduction

In recent years, regional conflicts and military operations have become increasingly intense and complex worldwide. Armed conflicts in some key sensitive areas have a large scope and high intensity of confrontation. The frequency of naval combat forces participating in such operations has gradually increased, and the role of naval forces in modern warfare and military operations is becoming increasingly prominent. Ship combat power is the backbone of naval combat power and an important force for naval forces to carry out various combat tasks. Due to the strong endurance, support, and firepower capabilities of ship combat power, most of the combat tasks carried out by ship forces are large-scale and long-distance. At the same time, the intensity of ship equipment usage also increases with the frequency and intensity of combat tasks, and the intensity of equipment loss also increases. Most ship equipment belongs to large-scale weapon platforms, and the maintenance difficulty of such equipment is relatively high. The characteristics of different ship equipment are also very obvious, so the maintenance of ship equipment is mostly implemented through project management. For ship equipment maintenance projects, a series of characteristics such as large project scope, complex project content, and high technical requirements make the difficulty and risk of ship equipment maintenance projects very high. The impact of project risks on project effectiveness is also very obvious, and some project risks may even affect the success or failure of the project. Therefore, this article takes ship maintenance projects as the research object, lists the project process of such projects, and analyzes the project risks of ship maintenance projects in detail. Based on this, an intelligent agent group model is established to simulate the practical impact of project risks on ship maintenance projects, as well as the positive role of project risk management in this project.

Literature review

Ship maintenance is a complex large-scale engineering project, and scholars from various fields around the world have conducted extensive research in this area [1]. Abdelmoula Ait Allal et al. believe that the level of ship maintenance determines the operational capacity of ships and reflects the level of competitiveness [2]. They believe that the level of ship maintenance is closely related to reliability, and they propose maintenance methods for improving ship reliability in their paper; Hua Zhang, Minhao Mu, and others studied the dismantling problem in ship maintenance and proposed a ship maintenance dismantling method that comprehensively considers the relationship between universal components and fasteners [32]. They established a constraint interference matrix considering the influence of fasteners in their paper, and based on this, proposed scientific criteria and rules for component disassembly judgment and fastener disassembly problems [33]. The effectiveness and superiority of their proposed theory were demonstrated through practical applications [9]; Chengyu, WangMei, and LinZheng Tang believe that the existing combination prediction methods based on rough set theory are not very suitable for ship maintenance problems [3]. In order to improve the efficiency of ship maintenance [12], they proposed the weight coefficient determination method of integral weighting method [34]. On the one hand, it improves the predictive stability of ship maintenance, and on the other hand [14], it also improves the accuracy level of such projects [4]; Catur Wahyu Nugroho, Trika Pitana, and others believe that ship maintenance is a complex engineering project, involving many complex operational activities [35]. In their paper, they proposed a ship maintenance risk analysis method that combines Job Safety Analysis (JSA) with Bayesian Networks (BN) and Fuzzy Inference Systems (FIS) [36]. The combination of these three methods evaluates the risk situation of ship maintenance projects [37]. They applied this method to ship maintenance and believe that the model can provide accurate information for ship maintenance projects [38]. Accurate and flexible evaluation methods [13]; Safaai Deris, Sigeru Omatu, and others proposed a constraint based reasoning method [21], which is of great significance for determining maintenance activities in ship maintenance projects [39]. They also conducted simulation analysis on the proposed method in their paper, and the results of the analysis also verified the effectiveness of their proposed method [40].

In terms of intelligent agent swarm modeling, this type of model has good simulation characteristics and has been widely studied and applied by scholars in various fields [18]. Rob Duell, Mark Hoogendoorn, and others applied intelligent agent models to control problem research; V. K [41]. Ivanov applied the method of intelligent agent model analysis to the field of information support[10],[1 and their research results have made significant contributions to the information support of enterprise planning[19], [19technology development prediction [11], and investment project professional knowledge [20]; Zun Liang Yin has established an intelligent agent model for the field of general automation [15], which can be used for operations of different types of applications [42]. From the general model, more effective product application operation models based on intelligent agents (MPA) and regression testing operation models based on intelligent agents (MRT) have been derived [22]; Donna L. Hudson and Madeline Cohen believe that an aging population will lead to a sharp increase in the number of people in need of long-term care in future society [23]. They established an intelligent agent model in their paper to study the living conditions and needs of the elderly in future society [24]. They believe that the intelligent agent model can be used to establish family systems and provide assistance for the development of future society [25]; Xiaofeng Ji and Jing Xu believe that dynamic e-commerce is a new stage in the development of e-commerce [17], which can help enterprises better adapt to the business environment [26]. In their paper, they proposed an intelligent agent model applied to dynamic e-commerce and tested the system on a tourism plan recommendation system [27]. This model can help enterprises and the economy develop [28]; Pengcheng Li, Burkay AndUV, and others applied the intelligent agent model to fault diagnosis of chillers [29]. They analyzed the working characteristics of chillers using this model [16] and demonstrated its good generalization ability by studying the fault status of chillers under different temperature and load rate conditions [30]. This model is suitable for cross condition fault diagnosis of chillers [31]; Zhu Mang conducted in-depth research on the application of intelligent agent models in concurrent constraint programming [7]. In his paper, he established a goal driven intelligent agent model with autonomous behavior ability based on the JFLUX interpreter in Java language [8]. In addition, his intelligent agent model can automatically perform action inference on any given target in an incomplete dynamic environment using limited information obtained from sensors [5]. His research provides important reference for the construction of action planning systems for intelligent agents in office scenarios [6].

The above research results have great significance in ship maintenance and the establishment of intelligent agent models. However, there are no research results that have applied intelligent agent models to risk management of ship maintenance projects. However, with its good simulation ability, intelligent agent models can provide significant help for risk management of ship maintenance projects and effectively improve the quality and efficiency of ship maintenance project management. Therefore, based on the research on the process and risk factors of ship maintenance projects, this article establishes a risk management intelligent agent group simulation model for such projects. Through the study of such problems, it is hoped to contribute to the effective improvement of the management efficiency level of ship maintenance projects.

Simulation scheme of intelligent agent group for risk management of ship maintenance engineering projects

Intelligent agents originated in the 1940s. Due to their strong simulation performance, wide applicability, high autonomy, and good responsiveness, they began to be widely used in various fields in the 1990s. Their main applications include software development, hardware equipment, robot development, and computer system development in a series of high-tech industries. A swarm of intelligent agents refers to a collection composed of multiple intelligent agents, in which each agent interacts to achieve a common goal. Therefore, in the process of establishing a swarm of intelligent agents model, the basic idea of step-by-step decomposition is used to miniaturize and modularize complex system engineering. In this process, the characteristics and attributes of each agent need to be fully considered, and communication channels between agents need to be established. Through the information transmission between intelligent agent groups, the calculation of complex system geological structure is completed, and the modeling and analysis management of large and complex systems can be achieved.

For the ship maintenance engineering studied in this article, the intelligent agent model is divided into three modules: the main project intelligent agent, the process unit intelligent agent, and the risk unit intelligent agent. The main project intelligent agent module represents the overall situation of the ship maintenance project, involving a series of project contents such as hull maintenance, power system maintenance, ship equipment system and ship power system maintenance. The specific establishment of intelligent agent modules is shown in Fig. 2:Fig. 2Overall intelligent agent model for ship maintenance engineering projects.Fig. 2

Overall intelligent agent model for ship maintenance engineering projects.

The main project intelligent agent module has a total of 3 parameters, 6 variables, and 1 port. This intelligent agent module is connected to each process intelligent agent module, mainly used to send commands to each process module and receive feedback information from the process module after operation. Due to summarizing all information of the intelligent agent group model, this intelligent agent module records the progress and cost of the simulated project for researchers to conduct data research.

The process unit intelligent agent is the main line of project risk simulation in the intelligent agent model established in this article. It is the overall work breakdown diagram of the project, which can intuitively and efficiently display the running status of the model. It is the main part of the intelligent agent group simulation model established in this article. The establishment of specific intelligent agent modules is shown in Fig. 3:Fig. 3Intelligent agent model for ship maintenance engineering project process.Fig. 3

Intelligent agent model for ship maintenance engineering project process.

The process intelligent agent module has a total of 7 parameters, 11 variables, and 4 ports. The “upper port” of the intelligent agent module is connected to the project module, which receives instruction information from the project module and feeds back the processing results of the data in the module to the project intelligent agent module; The “lower port” connects to the risk module, collecting feedback information and risk impact information transmitted by the risk module while sending judgment information to the risk module; The “left port” connects to the pre process module and receives risk impact information and judgment information on the start of module operation transmitted by the pre process module; The “right port” connects to the downstream process module and sends risk impact information and module completion judgment information to the downstream process module.

The risk unit intelligent agent is the “nerve endings” of the entire intelligent agent group model. The parameter data of this model simulates the level of impact of project risks on the project subject. The specific establishment of intelligent agent modules is shown in Fig. 4:Fig. 4Risk intelligent agent model for ship maintenance engineering projects.Fig. 4

Risk intelligent agent model for ship maintenance engineering projects.

The risk agent module has a total of 4 parameters, 1 variable, and 1 port. This port receives instruction information from the process module. After receiving the instruction information, the risk agent module will randomly generate a random number of [0,1]. If the value is greater than the probability value of risk occurrence determined in the risk module, it indicates that the risk does not occur. If the value is less than the probability value of risk occurrence determined in the risk module, it indicates that the risk will occur. After the risk occurs, it may trigger different risk consequences set in the module, such as “stop”, “cost increase”, “time delay”, and “mixing”. If the risk does not occur, the risk impact value of “0″ will be fed back to the process intelligent agent module.

The combination of the above three intelligent agent modules forms a risk management intelligent agent group model for ship maintenance engineering projects as shown in Fig. 5.Fig. 5Intelligent agent group model for risk management of ship maintenance engineering projects.Fig. 5

Intelligent agent group model for risk management of ship maintenance engineering projects.

The large circle named W represents the process module and also represents the various processes of the ship maintenance project. The small circles named A, B, C, D, E, and F represent the risk modules and various risk elements of the ship maintenance project. The connecting lines represent the relationships and information transmission channels between various intelligent agents.

Conclusion

Ship maintenance, as a wide-ranging, difficult, and complex engineering project, project risk is the key to determining the success or failure of the project and affecting the quality and efficiency level. After analyzing the process and risk situation of ship maintenance projects, combined with the characteristics and reality of ship maintenance engineering projects, this article establishes an intelligent agent group model for such projects, and uses simulation analysis to determine the impact of project risk on ship maintenance engineering projects. At the same time, through simulation comparison, the significance of risk control measures for improving project quality and efficiency level is determined.

From the simulation results, it can be seen that risk management plays a significant role in preventing and intervening project risks, and also has significant implications for improving project quality and efficiency. Therefore, for ship maintenance engineering projects, project risks can be prevented and controlled through risk mitigation, risk transfer, and other aspects. For the ship maintenance engineering project studied in this article, different risk control measures can be taken to resolve the six different types of project risks. Among them, technical risks can be improved by providing skill training to ship maintenance technicians, improving maintenance equipment, standardizing process management methods, and other methods to enhance the efficiency and refinement of project processes; In terms of schedule risk, measures such as strengthening the supervision of procurement and supply contract performance, expanding supply chain channels, scientifically formulating and adjusting project plans and work arrangements, and strengthening maintenance efficiency inspection and supervision are taken to reduce the possibility of project schedule being affected by risks; In terms of cost risk, it is necessary to strengthen real-time control of market raw material prices, scientifically demonstrate maintenance plans, and scientifically regulate project scope to prevent uncontrolled project costs from occurring; In terms of risk management, it is necessary to strengthen the training of staff's sense of responsibility and management ability, comprehensively and meticulously formulate plans, and enhance the efficiency of information transmission and communication within the project system; In terms of environmental risks, it is necessary to strengthen the tracking and mastery of social environment and policy systems, while improving the project working environment and enhancing work efficiency; In terms of quality risk, it is necessary to strengthen maintenance and repair, conduct testing and measurement of maintenance quality, strictly supervise the material level of engineering materials, strengthen the supervision of engineering acceptance, and ensure the quality level of ship maintenance.

This article takes the ship maintenance project as the research object, establishes an intelligent agent group model to simulate and analyze the risk situation of the project, and proposes specific methods for risk control based on the characteristics and risk factors of the ship maintenance project. However, as a complex and difficult equipment project, ship maintenance engineering often faces a lot of uncertainty in the implementation process of the project. The risk control measures proposed in this article have not been practically tested, and there is still uncertainty in the effectiveness between simulation results and project facts, which needs to be verified and improved in future engineering practices. In addition, in this study, simulation analysis was only conducted on the overall project risk impact and the effectiveness of risk control measures. The specific impact and effectiveness of different project risks and risk control measures need to be further analyzed and studied in future research. It is also hoped that this study can provide assistance and contribution to the development of such projects.
