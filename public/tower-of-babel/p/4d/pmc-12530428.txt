Correction: New insights into genetic comorbidity mechanisms: type 2 diabetes and primary open-angle glaucoma
Published: 2025

Wang Y, Tian Y, Quan Y, et al New insights into genetic comorbidity mechanisms: type two diabetes and primary open-angle glaucoma. BMJ Open Ophthalmol. 2025;10:e002219. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjophth-2025-002219

The journal was notified of undisclosed overlap between a schematic illustration in figure 1 of this article and figure 1 in an earlier published paper by some of the same authors.1 Figure 1 has now been corrected by the authors at the editor’s request. All Manhattan plots in figure 1 have been replaced with plots generated from the authors' GWAS data for this study; the term POTG has been corrected to POAG; and the pathway enrichment analysis in part 5 has been replaced with the data from this study.
