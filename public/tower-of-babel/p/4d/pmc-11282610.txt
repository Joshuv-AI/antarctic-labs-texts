Retraction Note: The prevalence of electronic cigarettes vaping globally: a systematic review and meta-analysis
Authors: Hadi Tehrani, Abdolhalim Rajabi, Mousa Ghelichi- Ghojogh, Mahbobeh Nejatian, Alireza Jafari
Published: 2024

Retraction Note: Archives of Public Health (2022) 80:240

10.1186/s13690-022-00998-w

The authors have retracted this article because it incorrectly reports the results of several studies included in their review. This impacts the overall results of their meta-analysis. The authors have been offered the opportunity to re-analyse their findings and to submit an updated version to the journal, which will be subjected to robust peer review.

All authors agree with this retraction.
