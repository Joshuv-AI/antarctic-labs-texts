Correction

The article “Navigating a Decade of Integrated Care Research in the International Journal of Integrated Care: How Far Have We Come?” [1] was published with an incomplete author list. Edelweiss Aldasoro contributed to the analyses in this paper but was not listed as co-author. Her contribution was overlooked due to the corresponding author’s change in affiliation and resulting loss of access to relevant communications. The author list has been corrected in the original article.
