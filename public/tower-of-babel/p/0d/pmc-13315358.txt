Correction to: Challenges in the Choice of Nonstatin Medications for Low‐Density Lipoprotein‐C Lowering for Cardiovascular Risk Reduction
Published: 2026

In the article by Myerson et al “Challenges in the Choice of Nonstatin Medications for Low‐Density Lipoprotein‐C Lowering for Cardiovascular Risk Reduction,” which published online October 9, 2025 (J Am Heart Assoc. 2025;14:e044134. DOI: 10.1161/JAHA.125.044134), corrections were needed.

Degrees for authors Bruce A. Warden and Joseph J. Saseen were mistakenly omitted. They have been corrected to read Bruce A. Warden, PharmD and Joseph J. Saseen, PharmD.

The symbols in Figure 1 did not correctly correspond to the symbols used in the figure legend. The figure has been revised to correct the symbols used.

Practical considerations for nonstatin agent selection.

†Modest (+); moderate (++); enhanced (+++); ‡Degree of LDL‐C lowering varies with background statin use; §Low cost/easy access = $; Moderate cost/moderate access=$$; High cost/challenging access = $$$; ||Medicare recipients with supplement; #Number of pills/injections per year: Low = #; Moderate = ##; High = ###; **Considered last‐line agents when other nonstatins listed are not indicated,ineffective or not tolerable; *Cardiovascular Outcome Trial data available to support use. FH indicates familial hypercholesterolemia; LDL‐C, low density lipoprotein cholesterol; mAb, monoclonal antibody; PCSK9i, proprotein convertase subtilisin/kexin type 9 inhibitor; and siRNA, small interfering RNA.

The name of author Rodis Paparodis was misspelled in the Disclosures section. The Disclosures have been corrected to read “Dr Paparodis reports honoraria, consulting, speaker, or advisory board for Sanofi, Amgen, and Novartis.”

The authors and publisher regret the errors.

The online version of the article has been updated and is available here: https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/JAHA.125.044134.
