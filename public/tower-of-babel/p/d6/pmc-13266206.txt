Screening and validation of rabbit monoclonal antibody pair against human FGF21 for human plasma FGF21 high sensitivity bioanalysis
Authors: Shancheng Zhao, Ziyun Ji, Yaxun Wang, Jie Li, Yanling He, Jing Shen, Chang Liu, Lina Wang, Xiaoqing Ma, Shengnan Wang, Jiguang He, Feng Xu
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
SummaryFGF21 has been reported to have a high correlation with various dysmetabolism-associated diseases. However, a portion of FGF21 in human blood samples remains below the detection limit of the state-of-the-art commercial ELISA kit. Quantitative determination of FGF21 with high sensitivity will benefit FGF21 functional research and drug development. Here, a pair of monoclonal antibodies was screened and prepared through immunizing a rabbit with recombinant human FGF21, flow cytometry single B cell sorting, and ELISA screening. The lowest limit of quantification of the sandwich ELISA method was 1.526 pg/mL, which possessed the highest sensitivity ever reported. Moreover, the carboxyl-terminus 10 amino acids of FGF21 were found to be unnecessary for the binding of this pair of antibodies. The sandwich ELISA method exhibited good robustness when validated using 22 human plasma samples. Together, we reported a pair of rabbit monoclonal antibodies for sensitive and robust bioanalysis of total FGF21, supporting FGF21 functional research and drug development.

Introduction

Human FGF21 is a 209 amino acid polypeptide hormone containing a 28 amino acid signal peptide, which is a potent lipid and glucose metabolic regulator.1,2 Mature FGF21 comprises 181 amino acids and tends to be unstable in circulation.3 Fibroblast activation protein (FAP), a member of the dipeptidyl peptidase-IV (DPP-IV) family, inactivates intact human FGF-21 by cleaving the carboxyl-terminal 10 amino acid residues, which are critical for its interaction with the receptor β-Klotho, and this cleavage is also the reason for the short half-life of FGF21.3,4 In rodents, hepatic FGF21 expression can be induced by the activation of the peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor α (PPARα).5,6 FGF21 binds to three FGF receptor (FGFR) isotypes (FGFR1c, FGFR2c, and FGFR3c) and the co-receptor β-Klotho.7,8,9,10 Activation of FGFRs by FGF21 induces a stimulation of extracellular signal-regulated kinase 1/2 (ERK1/2) and serine/threonine kinase AKT.2,11 FGF21 and its analogs can alleviate metabolic disorders by promoting weight loss, enhancing energy expenditure, and improving insulin sensitivity in preclinical studies using animal models.12,13,14 The action mechanisms of FGF21 and its analogs include increasing tissue glucose uptake,15,16 enhancing fatty acid oxidation in the liver,17 promoting hepatic glycogen synthesis,18 boosting mitochondrial respiration and increasing thermogenic capacity in brown adipose tissue (BAT),16,19 as well as modulating the immune and inflammatory system by altering cytokine TNF-α production.20 Abnormal circulating FGF21 concentration has been found in many dysmetabolism-associated diseases, including type 2 diabetes, obesity, and liver diseases.21,22,23,24,25 FGF21 is effective in treating dysmetabolism-associated diseases. FGF21 analogs are currently undergoing clinical trials in various phases for the treatment of dyslipidemia, obesity, type 2 diabetes, liver disease, and obesity-related metabolic comorbidities.26 Endogenous circulating FGF21 levels rise and reduced FGF21 sensitivity or “FGF21 resistance” develops in metabolic diseases,7,27 while FGF21-related drugs overcome this limitation through pharmacological optimization and signaling pathway modulation, thereby achieving therapeutic goals.27,28 High-sensitivity and high-accuracy measurement of FGF21 concentration can provide technical support for biomarker discovery and enable the selection of specific populations with preserved FGF21 sensitivity for personalized, precise therapy based on FGF21.26,29,30

Two structural patterns of circulating FGF21 exist as described above: the intact active form and the carboxyl-terminus cleaved inactive form,3,4 thus the total FGF21 analyte target of the ELISA assay was the intact pattern plus the carboxyl-terminus cleaved pattern. The best sensitivity of the ELISA kit for total FGF21 determination in previous studies is 7 pg/mL,21 and the lowest limit of detection (LOD) of the state-of-the-art commercial ELISA kit for total FGF21 determination in the market is 4.67 pg/mL.31 The deficiency of the state-of-the-art commercial ELISA kits for total FGF21 determination still exists, particularly in that their LOD remains 4.67 pg/mL. It has been reported that some human serum samples could not be detected using these two commercial FGF21 ELISA kits.21,31 Moreover, plasma level of FGF21 had a wide interindividual variation even in the fasting state of healthy individuals, ranging from 19.8 to 1,296.0 pg/mL determined using a commercially available ELISA kit which sensitivity was 8.69 pg/mL (R&D Systems Inc., Minneapolis, MN).30 Besides, the amount of biological samples is usually very limited, especially for mice species, due to their small volume of peripheral blood. Additionally, biological samples often require further dilution because of the matrix effect caused by complex components in biological samples. Thus, the sensitivity of the determination method, such as sandwich ELISA, should be lower than 4.67 pg/mL, even lower than 2 pg/mL, with good robustness. Antibody-based bioanalysis method with good sensitivity, accuracy, precision, and robustness can save precious samples and make research cost-effective. However, very few antibodies can meet the required sensitivity and robustness described above.

In another aspect, in preclinical animal experiments, whether FGF21 and its analog action is through the liver, brain, or adipose tissue is still unclear. Their target tissue may be different due to species differences.26,28,32 The therapeutic efficacy of FGF21 analogs has been observed in most clinical studies, including the amelioration of dyslipidemia (particularly hypertriglyceridemia) and liver disease, whereas the effects on glycemic control, weight loss, glycated hemoglobin, HOMA index, blood free fatty acids, and systolic blood pressure are rather mild and highly variable.26,28 The toxicokinetic and pharmacokinetic characteristics of FGF-21 with high sensitivity in preclinical and clinical studies should be evaluated by a validated ELISA method using FGF-21 monoclonal antibodies as critical reagents.33 Thus, FGF21 monoclonal antibodies with high affinity are needed to investigate the FGF21 biological roles and promote associated drug development.

It has been reported that rabbit monoclonal antibodies have precise epitope-recognition ability and developability because of their good affinity and stability.34 Using a monoclonal antibody pair as capture and detection reagents for the analyte target can establish a classical sandwich ELISA assay format, which offers advantages in sensitivity, accuracy, precision, and robustness. We concluded that a robust, cost-effective, sensitive, and accurate sandwich ELISA method could be developed using rabbit monoclonal antibodies. In this paper, we screened and prepared a pair of monoclonal antibodies derived from a rabbit and performed method validation using this pair of monoclonal antibodies and human plasma samples. We also validated that the epitope they recognized was not dependent on the carboxyl-terminus 10 amino acids. Our results indicated the suitability and good performance of these two rabbit antibodies as critical reagents for human total FGF21 bioanalysis, and also suggested the good potential of rabbit-derived monoclonal antibodies in bioanalysis.

Results

Successful expression of human intact FGF21 and immunization in rabbits

The vector PATX2-FGF21 plasmid was amplified, extracted, and successfully transfected into host CHO cells. The cell density reached 5.06×106 cells/mL with a cell viability of 74.28% after 12 days of culture.

The recombinant FGF21 protein antigen was purified with Ni resin, and a total of 3.9 mg of human FGF21 protein was obtained. The SDS-PAGE was used for purity confirmation. As shown in Figure 1, a single band was detected with a molecular weight of about 23 kDa. According to the amount of antigen immunization for 5 rounds, 200 μg × 2 tubes were immunized for the first time, and 100 μg × 10 tubes were immunized six times. The antigen was subpackaged and then placed at −20°C. Discussion

It has been reported that rabbit antibodies have more accurate epitope recognition ability, higher affinity, and higher cost-effectiveness compared to mouse monoclonal antibodies.34,35 Specifically, rabbit antibodies can recognize small-sized epitopes that are not immunogenic in mice.36 This is because rabbits have a more diverse antibody repertoire than humans and mice, owing to more frequent gene conversion and somatic hypermutation in their antibody gene sequences.37 Meanwhile, the flow cytometry single B cell sorting strategy can preserve the natural cognate pairing of heavy and light chains and allow the exploitation of the natural process of antibody maturation.35 Thus, rabbit antibodies with a favorable affinity, specificity, and stability profile can be generated. Our results indicated that further rabbit monoclonal antibodies were suitable for sandwich ELISA assay development and had advantages in method accuracy and sensitivity. More than half of the clones had good titer, which were selected by flow cytometry single B cell sorting and ELISA screening, supporting that further single B cell sorting was an effective strategy for rabbit antibody development in bioanalysis use.34 It was less time-consuming and had better performance than traditional mouse hybridoma screening. The data also indicated that the antibody pair reported here could determine total FGF21, including intact and carboxyl-terminus cleaved patterns in human plasma samples with good accuracy, precision, and sensitivity. It was reported that total FGF-21, but not the intact form of FGF-21, was positively associated with waist circumference,21 suggesting that total FGF-21 level could serve as a potential biomarker of visceral adiposity. In this previous report, the kit sensitivity for total FGF-21 determination was 7 pg/mL.21 The sensitivity (LLOQ) of our method using the rabbit antibodies was further optimized to 1.526 pg/mL, which enabled more accurate research. To the best of our knowledge, the method utilizing this antibody pair demonstrated the highest sensitivity reported to date, with an LLOQ of 1.526 pg/mL. Moreover, it exhibited comparable accuracy and precision to state-of-the-art commercial ELISA kits in the market, making it particularly suitable for analyzing samples with low concentrations of total FGF21.21,31 They were also able to tolerate the matrix effect in human plasma, which was crucial for pharmacokinetics and toxicokinetics studies in both basic scientific research and industrial drug development. Our reports have provided a reliable routine to develop monoclonal antibodies against proteins with low stability, such as FGF21, and the resulting monoclonal antibodies can be further applied in bioanalysis.

The work concentration of capture antibody and detection antibody in a sandwich ELISA is associated with the economic cost. Theoretically, the work concentration of rabbit monoclonal antibody can be reduced due to its high affinity.35 In this study, the work concentration of capture antibody and detection antibody was 0.2 μg/mL and 0.1 μg/mL, respectively, and 100 μL volume was used in each well of the 96-well plate. The amounts of capture antibody and detection antibody were 0.02 μg and 0.01 μg, respectively, for the 96-well plate sandwich ELISA assay. To the best of our knowledge, the amount of antibody reported here is the lowest to date. Therefore, rabbit monoclonal antibodies are more cost-effective for sandwich ELISA assay development and sample determination. Besides, the application of poly-HRP as a secondary signal amplification reagent can further reduce the antibody dosage and costs.38

In our study, after screening and validating rabbit monoclonal antibodies, we carefully investigated the concentration of total FGF21 in human plasma. We found that the concentration of human plasma total FGF21 at random time ranged from 54.84 pg/mL to 538.2 pg/mL, suggesting substantial interindividual differences. It was reported that the concentration of random plasma total FGF21 in young adults was 146.42 ± 84.07 pg/mL, determined by ELISA assay, and interestingly, it was affected by cold exposure of the environment and individual BAT volume.39 Our results were consistent with the previous report and demonstrated individual heterogeneity in plasma total FGF21 levels within the population. Furthermore, in the diurnal variation experiment, plasma total FGF21 levels measured in the morning after a 12-h overnight fast were significantly higher, suggesting that FGF21 was upregulated following overnight fasting—consistent with prior findings.40 The results shown in Table 7 demonstrated that our novel technique based on rabbit antibodies could detect temporal changes within the same individual, providing a powerful tool for FGF21 biological and clinical studies. Moreover, diurnal rhythms of circulating total FGF21, with a major nocturnal rise in early morning, were observed in all ten young volunteers. In our research, the analyte target determined here was plasma total FGF21, rather than only intact FGF21 or its cleaved pattern. However, whether the interindividual difference of intact or cleaved FGF21 is significant still awaits further investigation.

The rabbit monoclonal antibodies developed herein also have other specific utility in broader biological contexts beyond bioanalysis. For instance, FGF21 has been reported to be involved in cancer immunology as an immunosuppressor.41 Moreover, physiologically, FGF21 is a versatile regulator of metabolic homeostasis and can regulate sweet and alcohol preference.7 FGF21 treatment may modulate nutrient intake and reward behavior through its central actions.26,42 FGF21 monoclonal antibodies targeting therapy may provide new avenues for cancer, metabolic, and addictive diseases. It has been reported that rabbit monoclonal antibodies have advantages in immunogenicity and affinity for therapeutic antibody development.43,44 The rabbit monoclonal antibodies against human FGF21 reported here provide new tools and molecular modalities for cancer and addiction treatment.

Limitations of the study

This study also has some limitations and areas that require further investigation. It is known that FGF21 is prone to degradation and thus has two different structural patterns.3,4 Our results suggested that the epitope for the two antibodies herein was independent of the carboxyl-terminus ten amino acids, and they could recognize both cleaved and intact FGF21. Whether the antibodies can block the biological effect of FGF21 in vivo remains to be seen and awaits further investigation. Antibody epitope mapping by X-ray crystallography or 3D-electron microscope is also necessary for their further application. Additionally, an antibody that specifically recognizes the carboxyl-terminus epitope of intact FGF21 is needed for determining the intact FGF21 pattern and for drug development. Further investigation is required in this regard.

Method details

Recombinant human intact FGF21 preparation and rabbit immunization

The human FGF21 gene codon optimization, gene synthesis and vector construction of PATX2-FGF21 vector with 6 × histidine purification tag at N-terminus were entrusted to General Biology (Anhui) Co., Ltd. Amplified and purified plasmids were transfected into host CHO cells. One day before transfection, 15 1-L shake flasks were prepared, each of which was 300 mL. The cell inoculation density was 1×106 cells/mL. The total volume was 4,500 mL. They were cultured at 37 °C, 5% CO2 and 130 rpm for one day. On the second day, 333 μL of FectoPRO/167 μg plasmid DNA mixed solution and 167 μL of another transfection reagent, FectoPRO Booster, were successively added into cell suspension, mixed gently, and then shake flasks were put into a shaker for 9 days at 37 °C, 5% CO2 and 130 rpm.

The recombinant human FGF21 protein was purified using Ni resin and the protein concentration was measured by Bradford method. Then two New Zealand white rabbits were immunized with recombinant human FGF21 protein antigen. The first immunization dose was 200 μg, and the second to fifth immunization dose was 100 μg. The volume was replenished to 300 μL with 1 × phosphate buffer solution (PBS), and then 100 μL adjuvant was added, mixed and inhaled into a syringe, and injected into the hind leg muscle for 2–3 points. The interval of each immunization was one week.

Isolation, culture and ELISA screening of antigen-specific single B cell

Antibody titer in the serum of immunized rabbits was determined by ELISA assay, and the rabbit with high serum titer was selected for subsequent flow cytometry single cell sorting experiments to isolate and culture FGF21 antigen-specific B cell clones. The rabbit spleen was taken out aseptically, and the splenic peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs) were isolated using lymphocyte separation solution according to the instructions of reagent. The cells were resuspended in PBS and stained with goat anti-rabbit lgG-FITC (dilution ratio 1 : 300) and FGF21-AF647 (FGF21 was labeled with AF647 fluorescein according to instruction of reagent, dilution ratio 1 : 50). By flow cytometry cell sorting (Beckman), single FITC and AF647 double-positive cell was plated in each well of 96-well plate, and 20 96-well plates were inoculated.

After 8 days of culture at 5% CO2, 37 °C, the culture supernatant of each B cell clone was screened by ELISA assay and the screening standard was OD value >0.3. The FGF21 antigen was coated on surface of five 384-well plates at a concentration of 2 μg/mL. Then the selected high titer cell clones with the highest OD were cultured further for 4 days.

PCR amplification of antibody variable region gene sequence

After 12 days of culture, the selected 21 cell clones were lysed using cell lysis buffer, and the reverse transcription reaction was carried out. The cDNA template was stored at −80°C, and then the variable region fragments of the light and heavy chain genes of B cell antibodies were amplified by PCR using conventional heavy and light chain primers.

Monoclonal antibodies preparation, titer determination and pair match selection using sandwich ELISA

The heavy and light chain variable region fragments of 14 clones with higher titer were subcloned into the expression vector containing rabbit IgG constant region and transformed into E.coli DH5α strain. After PCR using bacteria culture suspension, correct positive clones were selected according to the length of antibody heavy and light chain fragments, and then they were sequenced by General Biology (Anhui) Co., Ltd. The bacteria clones with correct sequences were expanded and cultured, and the respective plasmid was extracted and transfected into CHO cells. 72 mL volume of 1×106 cells/mL was cultured in 250-mL shake flasks for 11 days (16H5 clone culture for 13 days). The 10 μL supernatants of 7 days were preliminarily detected by ELISA for screening of antibodies. Then the clones with high titer (OD value was more than 2.0) were purified using MabSelect resin of GE Company. Then 100 μg of each antibody was labeled with biotin and titer was determined by ELISA.

After antibody purification, the antibody titer was determined. Antibody pair which match best for FGF21 capture and detection was screened by sandwich ELISA. The primary capture antibody was coated on surface of 96-well plates using 0.1 μg/well, and the amount of FGF21 antigen was 10 ng/well. The biotin-labelled detection antibody was 0.05 μg/well. Streptavidin-HRP and its substrate TMB were used for signal amplification. The stop solution was 2 M HCl and 50 μL/well. The plate was read at the wavelength of 450 nm and 620 nm using the microplate reader (Multiskan FC, Thermo Fisher). The final OD value was the value of 450 nm minus the value of 620 nm.

Method validation of sandwich ELISA assay standard curve and sensitivity using 5F6 and 20C3-Biotin antibody pair

Sandwich ELISA assay steps were as follows, each sample was duplicated with two wells of ELISA plate: (1) capture antibody coating on surface of 96-well plate: 5F6 antibody was diluted to 0.2 μg/mL with carbonate buffer solution (CBS), 100 μL/well, and incubated at 4 °C overnight; (2) blocking: 3% BSA-PBST, 300 μL/well, incubated at 37 °C for 90 min; (3) plate washing: PBST, 300 μL/well, and plate was washed for 3 times; (4) antigen incubation: recombinant human FGF21 was diluted to a successive gradient of 781.3 pg/mL-0.7629 pg/mL with 0.5% BSA-PBST (PBST: 0.05% Tween 20 in PBS), the blank well was filled with 0.5% BSA-PBST diluent only, 100 μL/well, incubated at 37 °C for 90 min; (5) plate washing: PBST, 300 μL/well, and plate was washed for 3 times; (6) biotin labeled antibody: 20C3-Biotin antibody was diluted to 0.1 μg/mL with PBS, 100 μL/well, incubated at 37 °C for 1 h; (7) plate washing: PBST, 300 μL/well, and plate was washed for 3 times; (8) streptavidin-HRP incubation: streptavidin-HRP was diluted with PBS 1: 32,250 in ratio, 100 μL/well, incubated at 37 °C for 30 min; (9) plate washing: PBST, 300 μL/well, and plate was washed for 3 times; (10) TMB coloration: 100 μL/well, incubation at room temperature in the dark for 4 min; (11) termination: 2 M HCl, 50 μL/well; (12) plate reading: the OD value was read at wavelength of 450 nm and 620 nm with the microplate reader, and the blank well data was subtracted when standard curve was fitted with all data.

The method was validated including correlation coefficient of standard curve fitting and detection sensitivity. According to the range of FGF21 concentration of human plasma sample, the requirements for research purposes, and also the guidelines of industry guidance, the methodological acceptance criteria of this ELISA assay using antibodies for bioanalysis were set as follows: the standard curve was fitted by logistic-four-parameter method, and within the range of the standard curve, the back calculated concentration coefficient of variation (CV) (%) of each standard point was ≤15.0%, among them the upper limit of quantitation (ULOQ) and the lower limit of quantitation (LLOQ) were ≤20.0%. The bias (%) between the calculated concentration and the theoretical concentration (bias (%) = (∣calculated concentration-theoretical concentration∣)/theoretical concentration ×100%) should be within 15.0% (among them ULOQ, LLOQ was within 20.0%). Total error = Bias% + CV%, and total error of each standard point ≤30.0% (among them ULOQ, LLOQ ≤40.0%).

The lowest limit of detection (LOD) was defined the standard point which was positive and total error ≤45.0%. The same ELISA assay was repeated more than 3 times by 2 different technicians to determine LOD and LLOQ. Results including concentrations, CV, bias and total error were reported with four significant figures.

Expression of carboxyl-terminus 10 amino acids deficient FGF21 and sandwich ELISA detection using 5F6 and 20C3-Biotin antibody pair

The carboxyl-terminus 10 amino acids deficient (also named ‘cleaved’ down below) human FGF21 gene codon optimization, gene synthesis and vector construction of pET-28a (+) vector with 6 × histidine purification tag at N-terminus were entrusted to Atantares Biology (Suzhou, China) Co., Ltd. Amplified and purified plasmids were transfected into host E.coli BL21 cells. 10 mL activated recombinant bacteria were inoculated in 1 L LB culture medium containing 50 μg/mL kanamycin and cultured at 37 °C and 220 rpm for 2.5 h, then were induced expression with 0.5 mM IPTG for 4 h. The recombinant cleaved human FGF21 protein was purified using Ni resin and the protein concentration was measured using BCA protein determination commercial kit. Sandwich ELISA detection using 5F6 and 20C3-Biotin antibody pair was performed as described above. The recombinant cleaved human FGF21 was diluted to a successive gradient of 200.0 pg/mL-0.7813 pg/mL with 0.5% BSA-PBST. Other sandwich ELISA detection steps and the methodological acceptance criteria were same as described above.

Method validation of sandwich ELISA assay accuracy and precision using 5F6 and 20C3-Biotin antibody pair for total FGF21 bioanalysis of human plasma samples

Determination of human plasma FGF21 using the method described above, the concentration of the standard sample was successively diluted and the dilution multiple was a 2-fold dilution. The concentrations of the standard points ranged from 768.0 pg/mL to 1.500 pg/mL, and the blank well was filled with 0.5% BSA-PBST diluent only. Two human plasma samples were used to determine the required dilution multiple which was suitable for human plasma samples, and each sample was set two technical replicants. Meanwhile, 0.5% BSA-PBST was used to dilute the samples, and bioanalysis accuracy and precision of the samples with 2-fold dilution and 3-fold dilution was compared (the dilution multiple was obtained according to the preliminary experimental data). To confirm further whether there was matrix effect in human plasma samples, quality control (QC) recombinant intact FGF21 antigen was spiked in the respective diluted samples with different dilution multiples, and the final recombinant intact FGF21 antigen concentration was set at three levels, HQC (high concentration quality control) = 75% × ULOQ = 576.0 pg/mL, MQC = 192.0 pg/mL and LQC = 15.00 pg/mL.

Then ten human plasma samples were determined. The dilution multiple was 3. The concentrations of the standard points ranged from 640.0 pg/mL to 1.250 pg/mL diluted with 0.5% BSA-PBST, and the blank well was filled with 0.5% BSA-PBST diluent only. QC samples were added to the respective diluted samples, and the final concentration was set at three levels, HQC = 75 % × 640 pg/mL = 576.0 pg/mL, MQC (medium concentration quality control) = 80.00 pg/mL and LQC (low concentration quality control) = 15.00 pg/mL. Each sample was set two technical replicants. The acceptable criteria of analysis batch was: the acceptable criteria of standard curve was the same as described above. The concentration recovery rate of QC samples was calculated: recovery rate = measured value of QC × 100%/theoretical value of QC. Each plate should contain at least 2 sets of 3-level (low, medium and high concentration) QC samples. Both of the QC samples and the test samples were set two replicants. At least 67% of QC samples of each plate should meet the accuracy standard of 80% ≤ recovery rate ≤120%, and at least 50% of QC samples of each concentration level should meet the above criteria.

To further validate the ELISA assay and explore the total FGF21 diurnal variation in young healthy individuals, plasma samples from another ten young healthy individuals were collected and analyzed at three time points: morning after 12 h overnight fasting, 1 h after lunch, and 2 h after lunch. Each sample was set two technical replicants and the dilution multiple was 3 as described above. The concentrations of the standard points ranged from 400.0 pg/mL to 0.1953 pg/mL diluted with 0.5% BSA-PBST, and the blank well was filled with 0.5% BSA-PBST diluent only. Other sandwich ELISA detection steps and the methodological acceptance criteria were same as described above. A third technician performed the ELISA assay to further validate the robustness of the method developed here.
