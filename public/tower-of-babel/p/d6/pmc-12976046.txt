Correction: Discovery of novel disulfide-containing PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor with in vivo influenza therapeutic efficacy
Authors: Yoshiyuki Hirata, Kyoko Hayashi, Takuma Kato, Yasuo Nagaoka, Mitsunobu Doi, Shinichi Uesato
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-17982-3, published online 26 September 2025

The original version of this Article contained errors in Figure 2. Specifically in panel B, the sensorgrams for BMS-1 and compound 2 were mistakenly reversed within the figure panel. The original Figure 2 appears below.

Characterization of the PD-1/PDL1 interaction inhibitors by SPR. (A) Representative sensorgram of PD-1 on immobilized PD-L1. (B) Representative sensorgrams of PD-1 on immobilized PD-L1 in the presence or absence of tested compounds. As a control, 1% DMSO was used. (C) Representative sensorgrams of 2 on immobilized PD-L1 or PD-1. (D) The fitting curve of the response measured at equilibrium plotted against the compound 2 concentration. All experiments were repeated more than three times to ensure reliability.

The original Article has been corrected.
