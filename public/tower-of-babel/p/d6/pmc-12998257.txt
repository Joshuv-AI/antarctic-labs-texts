Bentall Procedure: A Standard for Managing Large Aortic Root Aneurysm in Marfan Syndrome
Authors: Syed Mohsin Raza Bukhari, Muhammad Ans, Muhammad Bilal Iqbal, Jalil Abbas, Ishrat Fatima, Mohsin Raza
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
ABSTRACTThe Bentall procedure remains a reliable surgical solution for treating aortic root aneurysms in patients with Marfan syndrome. It significantly reduces the risk of catastrophic events such as rupture or dissection while offering durable and favorable long‐term results.Keywords: aortic aneurysm, Bentall procedure, David's procedure, Marfan syndrome, Yacoub's procedure

The Bentall procedure is the gold standard for treating aortic root aneurysms in high‐risk Marfan syndrome patients, replacing the dilated root with a valve conduit and coronary reimplantation for long‐term outcomes [1]. Marfan‐root aneurysms exhibit diffuse annuloaortic weakening, while bicuspid aortopathy typically presents with more localized tubular dilation [2]. Here's a case of a 42‐year‐old patient with Marfan syndrome having a very large aortic root dilation (Video 1), occupying the entire pericardial space with a prolapsed aortic root, managed by the Bentall procedure (Figure 1, Videos 2, 3, 4, 5). Given the extensive dilation and prolapse, valve‐sparing procedures like David's or Yacoub's were unsuitable [1].
