Correction to ‘Eduomics: a Nextflow pipeline to simulate -omics data for education’
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: Lorenzo Sola, Davide Bagordo, Simone Carpanzano, Mariangela Santorsola, Francesco Lescai, Eduomics: a Nextflow pipeline to simulate -omics data for education, NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics, Volume 8, Issue 1, March 2026, lqag029, https://doi.org/10.1093/nargab/lqag029

In the originally published version of this article, an error was introduced in the abstract during production. A portion of the abstract text was inadvertently omitted, and another portion was duplicated.

The abstract has now been corrected to include the full intended text and to remove the duplicated section. The publisher apologizes for this error. This correction does not affect the results, discussion and conclusions presented in the article.
