Retraction of “Integrated Electrochemical Oxidation and Biodegradation for Remediation of a Neonicotinoid Insecticide Pollutant”
Authors: Azhagarsamy Satheeshkumar, Ramanathan Duraimurugan, Punniyakotti Parthipan, Kuppusamy Sathishkumar, Mohamad S. AlSalhi, Sandhanasamy Devanesan, Rajaram Rajamohan, Aruliah Rajasekar, Tabarak Malik
Published: 2026

The Authors retract “Integrated Electrochemical Oxidation and Biodegradation for Remediation of a Neonicotinoid Insecticide Pollutant” (DOI: 10.1021/acsomega.3c09749) because Figures 3c and 3e (UV spectra) were duplicated and Figures 6–9 (GC–MS) were generated under nonideal acquisition conditions, which impacts the reliability of the compound identification and quantification in the absence of additional data to verify the results. Additional confirmatory analyses would be required to fully support the reported compound identification and quantification results. As such, the article is being retracted.

The original Article was published on March 22, 2024 and retracted on June 4, 2026.
