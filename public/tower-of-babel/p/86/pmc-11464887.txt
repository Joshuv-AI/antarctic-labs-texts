Design of agricultural product traceability system based on blockchain and RFID
Authors: Li Li, Pengbo Tian, Jiapeng Dai, Fengjuan Miao
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractEnsuring the traceability of agricultural products is essential for quality control and food safety. Recent technological advances have provided new ways to enhance traceability systems. This study aims to use blockchain technology, centralized database and RFID tags to develop a secure agricultural product traceability system, retain the detailed information of agricultural products traceability, ensure that the summary information of agricultural products on the chain cannot be modified, and optimize the SM3 algorithm to effectively summarize the traceability data and improve the efficiency of the system. The aggregated data is time-stamped, recorded on the blockchain, and written into an RFID tag. The optimization of the SM3 algorithm improved the efficiency by 30% and reduced the execution time of 192-byte messages to 210µs. The system ensures accurate linking of traceability data through secure data retention and unalterable summaries on the blockchain. The integrated use of blockchain, centralized database and RFID technology, as well as the enhanced SM3 algorithm, allows the system to meet the standards for data accuracy and performance requirements in agricultural traceability applications.Keywords: Traceability of agricultural products, RFID, Blockchain, SM3, HSM, Internet of things technologySubject terms: Computer science, Mathematics and computing

Introduction

In recent years, with the advancement of the Internet of Things (IoT) industry, the application of sensors and big data for data collection and storage has significantly enhanced China’s traceability industry. However, a series of issues persist. Traditional anti-counterfeiting traceability technologies, such as QR codes and fluorescence markers, remain relatively outdated. These methods are prone to forgery and fail to provide detailed traceability information, limiting their effectiveness in ensuring product safety and transparency1–3. Therefore, the traditional agricultural product traceability data system has barriers to interaction, limited interaction capabilities, and is prone to information islands4,5.

Currently, the majority of applications utilizing RFID-based traceability systems are primarily designed for single-node traceability, with a predominant focus on tracking information at the planting stage. Consequently, the management of comprehensive traceability data, including information from subsequent stages of the agricultural supply chain, remains underdeveloped. This lack of integration limits the effectiveness of these systems in providing a holistic view of agricultural product traceability and addressing the entire lifecycle of the product6,7. Agricultural products involve multiple links in the circulation process, and it is difficult to unify the synchronization of traceability information based solely on RFID tags, resulting in low accuracy of traceability data and errors that make it difficult to ensure the accuracy of traceability data8–10. Traceability data is stored in a central database, and if the database is subjected to tools, the data can be easily leaked and tampered with11–13. RFID-based traceability systems that rely on centralized databases face significant challenges in ensuring the authenticity and integrity of traceability data. Additionally, there is insufficient emphasis on the security risks associated with data interactions between readers and databases. The potential vulnerabilities in reader devices expose the system to security threats and attacks, compromising the overall reliability and protection of the traceability information14,15. Simultaneously, the inherent characteristics of blockchain technology—decentralization, immutability, traceability, and trust—make it exceptionally well-suited to address the challenges associated with centralized management and multi-party trust issues prevalent in current RFID anti-counterfeiting traceability systems. Blockchain’s distributed ledger capabilities offer a robust solution for enhancing data integrity, security, and transparency, effectively mitigating the limitations of traditional centralized traceability approaches16–18. However, neither personal research nor the blockchain platform can ensure the authenticity of the traceability data before it is put on the chain, and when the amount of traceability data is large, the operation efficiency of the traceability system cannot be guaranteed. Alberto Zancanaro et al. enable sensors to receive complex data through machine learning, but the data collection focuses more on specific parameters of production, processing, transportation and other links, ignoring other factors affecting food safety19. The distribution and density of data collection points in this experiment can more fully reflect the actual situation of the entire food supply chain. MIAOMIAO ZHENG et al. designed a food safety traceability system, but the stability and reliability of the mentioned RFID technology and two-dimensional code technology in complex environments (such as high humidity, high temperature cold chain environment) are poor, which will affect the accuracy and integrity of the data20. The blockchain technology used in this experiment has good data processing speed and storage capacity, and does not face performance bottlenecks when processing massive, high-dimensional data. Raouf Mehannaoui et al. designed a food traceability system based on the Internet of Things to better improve the food traceability system through the use of 5G and DLT two advanced technologies, but there was a lack of detailed description of the system architecture, as well as analysis of experimental details and performance indicators21. This experiment is improved on this basis, by applying blockchain technology, RFID tags and optimized SM3 algorithm, the traceability system has higher reliability and efficiency.

Integrating traditional agricultural product traceability systems with RFID and blockchain technologies offers a transformative enhancement in transparency and data integrity. The synergy of blockchain’s decentralized ledger and RFID’s real-time tracking capabilities significantly bolsters consumer trust and satisfaction by ensuring the authenticity and reliability of traceability data. This integration not only fosters sustainable development and scalability in industry transformation but also elevates the overall operational efficiency of commodity circulation. As a result, it enhances the social value of agricultural products and presents substantial potential for advancement and application within engineering practices.

Key technologies

Blockchain technology

Blockchain technology is a decentralized distributed digital ledger used to record transactions and data in a secure and transparent manner. It employs encryption algorithms to ensure the integrity and validity of the data stored on it, and once the data is recorded on it, it is nearly impossible to change, hack, or manipulate. Blockchain technology underpins cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin and Ethereum, but its potential applications extend beyond digital currencies. It can be Bitcoin and Ethereum, among others, but its potential applications extend beyond digital currencies. It has been used in supply chain management, identity verification, voting systems, and more.

This paper uses the storage method of combining blockchain and centralized database (MySql), which only stores the traceability summary data of agricultural products calculated by SM3, which reduces the storage space of the blockchain system and improves the query and search speed. The centralized database (MySql) is used to store the traceability details of agricultural products, and RFID tags are used to record the summary information of agricultural products at each stage.

Traceability system design

Business process analysis

The traceability process of agricultural products mainly includes planting, processing, classification, transportation, storage and sales. In order to realize the safe and efficient storage of data in the circulation process, this paper uses blockchain distributed storage summary information and centralized database (MySql) to store detailed traceability information, and combines RFID repository authentication mode to realize the safe storage and query of agricultural product traceability information. In order to record the raw data during the circulation of agricultural products while reducing the amount of upstream data, the raw traceability data of agricultural products is stored in a centralized database. This paper uses a centralized database and blockchain storage mechanism to achieve data binding verification, and the centralized database adopts MySql database, which is widely used in storage systems because of its low cost, small size and fast development speed. By designing multiple forms, they are used for data recording and authority division in the process of agricultural product circulation.

This design aims to ensure comprehensive and reliable tracking of agricultural products from harvest through to final storage, supporting data integrity and traceability.

The blockchain-based and RFID storage system model includes the following parts: data collection and writing layer, data processing storage layer, network transport layer, consensus mechanism layer, smart contract layer, data interface layer, and user participation layer. Through layering, the layers are relatively independent, and there is only a data interaction interface relationship between each layer, which has good flexibility, and does not affect the overall architecture when modifying and adjusting some information, so as to avoid the shortcomings of the business system caused by the barrel effect. When encountering network attacks or hacker damage, you can quickly locate specific location information to facilitate later operation and maintenance. The storage architecture is shown in Fig. 4.

Agricultural product traceability data storage system architecture based on blockchain.

Data acquisition read and write layer: This layer includes RFID tags, SM3 algorithms, main control chips, data transmission and passive RFID temperature sensors. An ultra-high speed and high sensitivity temperature sensor based on ZnO/MoS2/RGO nanocomposite modified microwave band-stop filter is designed and fabricated, which can collect ambient temperature information at low cost. The temperature amplitude in the environment is determined to be in the 2.73–2.76 GHz band, and the optimized microstrip patch temperature sensor obtains higher response and better optimal value, which improves the sensitivity of the temperature sensor22. Data storage layer: The links involved in the on-chain storage process of data include: SM3 (hash) data digest verification, timestamp, Merkle and blockchain chain storage structure. Network transport layer: The network transport layer uses P2P network transmission and identity authentication mechanism to communicate data between planting nodes, processing nodes, transportation nodes, storage nodes, sales nodes, and user query nodes. Consensus mechanism layer: All nodes follow the POS consensus mechanism agreed upon in the process of updating and storing blockchain data to ensure that the data reaches consensus in the storage process of their respective nodes and successfully completes the data on the chain. Smart contract layer: When the traceability of agricultural products is established, in the process of farmer planting, enterprise processing, transportation, distribution, consumer inquiry and transaction, the market supervision department will legally and morally constrain it, set script and program rules, abide by the conditions agreed in advance in the process of data on the chain, and automatically verify the nodes on the chain according to the smart contract to further strengthen the reliability of traceability data. Data interface layer: by providing API data interface, it is convenient to access the data of each node, and the interface can be used to complete the upload and query of data, which is convenient for later maintenance and operation, and facilitates the expansion of the blockchain traceability platform. User participation layer: realize human-computer interaction, use the API function of the data interface layer to obtain traceability data, consumers can realize the query of traceability information of agricultural products, supply chain departments and regulatory departments can realize the management of agricultural products, and realize data circulation in all links through the user participation layer. In view of the problems of traditional traceability platforms relying on centralized databases and unsynchronized data updates, through the above analysis of the traceability needs of agricultural products and the design of storage methods, combined with blockchain technology, RFID technology and optimization of the national secret SM3 algorithm, a complete and safe traceability platform for agricultural products is built.

Data summary information generation and validation

In the process of data acquisition and storage, the domestic SM3 algorithm is used to perform summary calculation on the original data, and the original data and summary data are further processed through the data processing layer. After completing the process of each node, the summary data of this node is summarized, and the summary data of the SM3 algorithm of the sum is calculated and written into the RFID tag. In the traceability data of agricultural products, through the pre-setting, the data of each data summary operation is much less than 264 bits, and the operation steps by feeding the original data into the SM3 algorithm are as follows: (1) Message length filling; (2) Message expansion; (3) Iterative compression; (4) Summary data outputs.

In the process of traceability of agricultural products, RFID tags are pasted inside the commodity packaging. According to the flow characteristics in the process of traceability of agricultural products, the labels selected by this system are ultra-high frequency labels with low cost, high reliability, strong anti-interference and long radiation distance.

Sm3 algorithm improvement and optimization

By theoretically analyzing the execution time of the algorithm and the running time of the ISYSTEM debugger tracking function, the time data monitored by the debugger is analyzed and it is concluded that during the execution of the SM3 algorithm, the time consumption of the compression function accounts for 58.9%, and the time consumption of message expansion reaches 25.4%, as shown in Fig. 8a, the key to improving the fast implementation of the SM3 algorithm is to reduce the execution time of the compression function and message expansion.

(a) Time proportion of SM3 algorithm implementation function; (b) comparison of execution time of SM3 algorithm and SHA256 algorithm before and after optimization; (c) comparison of execution speed of SM3 optimized compression function.

The purpose of the message expansion phase is to divide 512-bit messages into two groups. The first group is given 68 words and is denoted as w_0-w_67, and the second group is 64 characters, denoted as w_0^’-w_63^’. In the process of operating 132 words, the call and storage time of data can be reduced, and the execution time of the algorithm can be reduced indirectly. Reduce the loading of unnecessary messages by adjusting the initial value calculation of the compression function. Increase the speed of computation during execution by reducing w_0^’-w_63^’ computation and storage.

Sensing properties

Read RFID tags, use single-chip microcomputer to initialize the RF module, read RFID tag data, read the label data by operating the upper computer, test whether the module can scan the tag normally and read and write data. The main interaction process between RFID tag and reader is: when the RF module identifies the RFID tag data, completes the reading of TID code and USER area summary data, and determines whether the USER area (data summary area) data is the initial value, if it is the initial value, pop-up window reminder in the host computer to write the card. In the process of data circulation, RFID tags are pasted in the packaging of agricultural products, the reader is to identify the tag, read the EPC code, TID code, User area data of the RFID tag, upload it to the host computer through the single-chip microcomputer, the upper computer through the TID code to search for the current circulation of this commodity, query the detailed information recorded in the central database. Through the transaction address of the blockchain, read the data summary information stored by the blockchain, and judge the authenticity of the data in the process of commodity circulation by comparing the record data of the RFID tag with the information recorded by the blockchain and the centralized database. If the data is consistent in the next step, the label data is read and written at each stage of circulation to ensure the accuracy of the whole process. Use C# programming to make host computer software, and connect to the vector network analyzer (TVNA) via USB. Call VISA’s function library to realize the control of TVNA, and the steps to read passive RFID temperature sensor data are as follows: (1) Complete system initialization through the C# programming language and establish communication with TVNA; (2) After the host computer establishes communication with TVNA, read out the information string of TVNA and complete data analysis; (3) Set the measurement parameters through the host computer; (4) Vector network analysis instrument starts data measurement; (5) The upper computer reads the measurement data, converts it into Celsius according to the fitting curve and displays and records the data.

In order to ensure the safe and stable operation of the entire software, static code and dynamic code testing of the engineering code are required. Static code testing mainly tests MISRA C, CERT C check, dynamic testing is divided into unit testing and integration testing. In the process of testing, you need to write test cases, record the status of bugs, modify bugs, and reissue versions.

In order to verify the validity and reliability of processing node information in the agricultural product traceability platform based on blockchain and RFID technology, a series of experiments were designed and implemented. Figure 15 shows the data entry interface used in the experiment, which is an important part of the agricultural product traceability platform. You can see the table of “processing node information”, which lists the product name, processing method, processing location and other key information in detail. This information is uniquely identified by RFID tags or barcodes and is associated with the data on the blockchain to ensure the authenticity and immutability of the data. It also presents summary data related to processing nodes (such as “66e7f04 62eeedd9”, etc.), which are obtained by hashing the information in RFID tags or barcodes using specific algorithms to increase data security and traceability. The experimental results show that the agricultural product traceability platform based on blockchain and RFID technology can effectively prevent data tampering and falsification, and ensure the authenticity and traceability of agricultural product information.
