Challenges of regenerative medicine: New strategies from traditional Chinese medicine
Authors: Jiuhong Kang
Published: 2025

The clinical translation of stem cell therapy has long faced challenges such as establishing a good differentiation system and obtaining high-quality functional cells. In the context of the triple constraints of organ shortage, irreversible degenerative pathologies, and uncontrolled differentiation in cell therapies, conventional allotransplantation, chemical growth factors, or viral reprogramming face fundamental limitations. These include donor scarcity, high toxicity, and genetic mutation risks, which preclude definitive cures. In contrast, natural products derived from traditional Chinese medicine (TCM) leverage their multi-targeted, low-toxicity microenvironment-modulating properties. These compounds not only establish a foundation for human applications through high safety margins and millennia-spanning clinical documentation, but also enable metabolic reprogramming, epigenetic remodeling, and coordinated signaling network modulation. This unique capacity provides an ecologically sustainable, cost-effective, and rapidly derivable lead compound library for large-scale stem cell expansion, precise lineage-specific differentiation, and enhanced in vivo survival. Consequently, natural products from TCMs represent a pivotal breakthrough avenue for clinical translation in regenerative medicine.

Recent studies have shown that the active components of TCM regulate the proliferation, differentiation, and aging processes of stem cells through mechanisms such as metabolic reprogramming, epigenetic remodeling, and intercellular communication, thereby opening up new avenues for regenerative medicine. However, there is a paucity of systematic understanding or comprehensive summary regarding the mechanisms of action and structure–activity relationships of these natural active ingredients.

A review article published in CHM systematically summarizes the natural products identified as regulators of stem cell fate (Zhao et al., 2025), including discussions on the classification, mechanisms of action, and current prospects of these natural products as lead compounds. This provides valuable insights for advancing the modernization and internationalization of TCM, precision regenerative medicine using natural products and stem cells, the discovery of green lead compounds, and the development of new drugs.

Bottlenecks in stem cell therapy and novel TCM-based regulatory strategies

The clinical application of stem cell therapy has long been constrained by three major challenges: (1) Uncontrollable differentiation: transplanted cells frequently undergo aberrant differentiation or tumorigenesis; (2) Limited cell sources: primary stem cells exhibit restricted expansion capacity and accelerated senescence; (3) Microenvironmental instability: pathological conditions impair stem cell survival and functionality.

Natural products hold significant potential as either exceptional natural modulators or eco-friendly lead compounds. They provide breakthrough solutions to these challenges through multi-target/multi-pathway synergistic effects and can be used to develop new therapeutic agents.

Mechanisms of TCM in modulating stem cell fate and discovery of novel compounds

Natural small molecules from TCM serve as pivotal regulators of stem cell fate, demonstrating revolutionary potential in regenerative medicine, anti-aging research, and disease treatment. Their mechanisms span multi-level regulation, including metabolic reprogramming, epigenetic remodeling, and intercellular communication, thereby providing novel approaches to overcome bottlenecks such as uncontrollable differentiation, cellular senescence, and source limitations in stem cell therapy.

Despite their promise, limitations persist. Most small molecules lack target specificity, with single compounds often acting on multiple targets (poor selectivity), while low bioavailability compromises in vivo absorption. Moreover, undefined safety profiles restrict clinical translation. Structural modification of natural products to derive synthetic analogs substantially reduces synthetic complexity compared to de novo drug design. Such modifications enhance intestinal absorption and alter absorption sites in vivo. Structurally similar derivatives can also potentiate stem cell induction in vitro. Furthermore, structure-based design yields potent ABCG2 inhibitors to combat cancer multidrug resistance. Target engagement critically depends on structural complementarity. As superior green lead compounds, TCM-derived molecules synergize with computer-aided design and molecular docking for target prediction, validation, and subsequent compound synthesis—accelerating pharmaceutical development.

Although numerous natural products modulate stem cell fate, elucidating their mechanisms and discovering novel analogs remain imperative. Current discovery strategies include: (1) screening compounds based on known stem cell-related signaling pathways; (2) high-throughput screening (HTS) of kinase target libraries and natural small-molecule repositories.

Constructing HTS-targeted identification methods around key kinase targets in stem cell regulatory pathways represents an efficient strategy. Complementary approaches involve in silico database screening followed by in vitro/in vivo validation.

Innovative integration strategies and clinical translation prospects

Natural TCM ingredients bridge TCM theory and stem cell therapy. Their capacity to modulate stem cell proliferation and inhibit apoptosis offering solutions to low survival rates and suboptimal culture efficiency in clinical applications. Certain products further enhance therapeutic specificity by promoting directed differentiation. Synergistically, these compounds function as biocompatible materials co-delivered with functional small molecules to potentiate stem cell efficacy. Consequently, integrating TCM active ingredients, stem cells, and biomaterials overcomes monotherapy limitations. For example, in osteoarthritis, HAMA hydrogel can be used to deliver natural products pretreated with MSCs to repair cartilage (An et al., 2025), and in Alzheimer's disease, natural product nanoparticles can be used in conjunction with iPSC neurons to reverse cognitive impairment, thereby breaking through the efficacy bottleneck of single therapies (Park, Kim, & Ryou, 2023).

Both TCM theory and regenerative medicine emphasize clinical practice. Interactions between natural TCM products and stem cells provide scientific validation for TCM—e.g., blood-nourishing American ginseng saponins promote hematopoietic stem cell proliferation. This synergy enables precision medicine for diverse individuals and diseases. Critically, TCM-derived extracts offer advantages over synthetic drugs in addressing endogenous stem cell insufficiency and dysfunctional differentiation, flexibly modulating stem cells for therapeutic applications.

Leverage modern technologies to optimize natural products from TCMs for advancing cell therapy development

Natural products from TCMs constitute a rich source of green lead compounds for stem cell fate regulation. Their multi-target properties synergistically resolve bottlenecks including uncontrolled differentiation and low survival rates. Structurally diverse natural products modulate stem cell fate through conserved pathways; identifying these targets facilitates discovery of novel agents. Structural optimization of existing compounds remains pivotal for precise fate control. Regenerative medicine has historically been hindered by organ shortages, inadequate cell sources, and uncontrolled differentiation. It is evident that natural products from TCMs have demonstrated a marked capacity to enhance stem cell retention and directed differentiation efficiency in various pathological conditions, including osteoarthritis and Alzheimer's disease, through the mechanism of multi-target microenvironment regulation. This process has been observed to occur with a consistent safety profile.

To accelerate clinical translation, future research should focus on structural optimization, computer-aided design, high-throughput screening, and the integration of artificial intelligence to systematically identify and modify green lead compounds, enabling scalable production and precise delivery. The deep integration of traditional herbal medicine experience with modern technology will drive the development of a new paradigm for safe, efficient, and affordable cell therapy.
