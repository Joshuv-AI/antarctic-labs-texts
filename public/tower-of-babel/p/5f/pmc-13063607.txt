Correction to: Chikusetsu saponin IVa liposomes modified with a retro-enantio peptide penetrating the blood-brain barrier to suppress pyroptosis in acute ischemic stroke rats
Authors: Yitong Liang, Tingting Fan, Min Bai, Na Cui, Wangting Li, Jingwen Wang, Yue Guan
Published: 2026

In this article, during figure preparation, the author identified that in Fig. 6G, the cryosectioning image representing the LPs-THRre group was incorrectly displayed. The correct cryosectioning image for the LPs-THRre group in Fig. 6G has now been corrected in the original publication. For completeness and transparency, the correct and incorrect versions are displayed below.
