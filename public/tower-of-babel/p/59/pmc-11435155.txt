Text Correction

In the original publication [1], the data for the Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM) analysis were missing. A correction has been made to Section 2.2. Characterization of TiO2.

“A tapping-mode AFM probe with a cantilever tip, HQ: NSC-16 (Mikromasch), was used.”

Error in Figure

In the original publication [1], there was a mistake in Figure 9 as published. Figure 9A–F were erroneously included from the same raw data source. The corrected Figure 9A–F appear below.

The authors state that the scientific conclusions are not affected because of this correction. This correction was approved by the Academic Editor. The original publication has also been updated.
