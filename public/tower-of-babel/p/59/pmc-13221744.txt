The 77th Eaap Annual Meeting Will Be Held in Hamburg (Germany) from 7th to 11th September 2026

On behalf of the German Organising Committee, we are very delighted to invite you to attend the 77th EAAP Annual Meeting 2026 to be held in Hamburg (Germany), from 7th to 11th September 2026. The title of the conference is “Shaping resilient livestock systems, an essential element of circular agriculture”.

The sessions will be organized and jointly organized between the eleven EAAP Study Commissions to cover various areas of knowledge related to animal science, such as genetics, nutrition, physiology, animal health and welfare, precision livestock farming, horses, pigs, insects, livestock farming systems, cattle, sheep and goat. The American Society of Animal Science fosters the discovery, sharing and application of scientific knowledge concerning the care and responsible use of animals to enhance animal and human health and well-being.

Membership is open to individuals, organizations, or firms interested in research and application, instruction, or extension in animal science or associated with the production, processing, marketing, or distribution of livestock and livestock products.

Membership is based on the calendar year (January 1 through December 31)

ASAS is dedicated to promoting animal science research and supporting your career. The World Federation of Animal Science Societies and Associations, WAAP, is an International Non-Governmental Organisation with special consultative status with FAO since 1974.

The WAAP was established in 1965 after the success of the 1st World Conference on Animal Production held in Rome in 1963 organised by EAAP under the patronage of FAO.

WAAP aims to encourage closer collaboration between the animal production organisations and to bring together scientists, educators, technicians and administrators with the objective of reviewing development concerns on the five continents.

The Australian Association of Animal Sciences (AAAS) invites animal scientists from around the globe to to join us in Melbourne, Australia for the 13th WAAP World Conference on Animal Production (WCAP) and the 38th biennial AAAS Conference. WCAP2028 will run from 2 to 5 July 2028

The WCAP2028 program will feature plenary sessions from keynote speakers from around the globe designed to inspire and interest all participants along with concurrent sessions aligned with more specialised interests. The latest research relating to animal genetics, breeding, nutrition, health, behaviour and welfare across a variety of species from production animals to wildlife will be on offer.
