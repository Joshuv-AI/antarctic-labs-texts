Integration of Functional Human Auditory Neural Circuits Based on a 3D Carbon Nanotube System (Adv. Sci. 32/2024)
Authors: Yiyun Lou, Jiaoyao Ma, Yangnan Hu, Xiaoying Yao, Yaoqian Liu, Mingxuan Wu, Gaogan Jia, Yan Chen, Renjie Chai, Mingyu Xia, Wenyan Li
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Abstract Human Auditory Neural Circuits The perception of acoustic information relies on physical stimulus converted into electrical signals by the inner ear, transmitted through acoustic neural circuits, and received by auditory cortical neurons. In article number 2309617, Wenyan Li, Mingyu Xia, Renjie Chai, and their co‐workers present a novel human auditory circuit model, the peripheral‐central neural organoids with functional synaptic transmission facilitate the exploration of therapeutic strategies for hearing loss.
