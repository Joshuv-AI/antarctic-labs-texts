Correction to “Highly Efficient Supramolecular Photocatalyst for CO2 Reduction with Eight Carbon–Carbon Bonds between Ru(II) Photosensitizer and Re(I) Catalyst Unit”
Authors: Kei Kamogawa, Antonio Santoro, Ambra M Cancelliere, Yuushi Shimoda, Kiyoshi Miyata, Ken Onda, Fausto Puntoriero, Sebastiano Campagna, Yusuke Tamaki, Osamu Ishitani
Published: 2023

Page 9029 and Table 1: In the published article, the units for the emission lifetimes of Ru complexes (μs) are not correct, which should be ns. These corrections do not affect the conclusions of the paper at all.

Page 9029:

Before correction: Because the emission lifetimes of RuC2PhC2Re (761 μs) and RuC2Re (762 μs) were close to that of Ru (748 μs) (Table 1), the intramolecular electron transfer from the excited Ru unit to the Re unit (i.e., oxidative quenching) was negligible for both RuC2PhC2Re and RuC2Re.

After correction: Because the emission lifetimes of RuC2PhC2Re (761 ns) and RuC2Re (762 ns) were close to that of Ru (748 ns) (Table 1), the intramolecular electron transfer from the excited Ru unit to the Re unit (i.e., oxidative quenching) was negligible for both RuC2PhC2Re and RuC2Re.
