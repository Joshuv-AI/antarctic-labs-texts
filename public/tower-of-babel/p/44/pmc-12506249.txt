Correction: BMC Health Services Research (2025) 25: 1179.

In this article, Figs. 1 and 2 appeared as duplicates of Figs. 3 and 4 respectively, due to a typesetting mistake. The incorrect and correct version of Figs. 1 and 2 are displayed below and the original article has been corrected.

In addition, the note on equal contributions was captured incorrectly due to a typesetting mistake as “Dexing Kong, Zihe Kong and Jiangming Kong contributed equally to this work.”, but should have been captured as “Zihe Kong, Dexing Kong and Jiangming Kong contributed equally to this work and should be considered as co-first authors.”. The correct note is included in this Correction and the original article has been corrected.

The publisher apologizes to the authors and readers for the inconvenience caused by these errors.
