Expression of Concern on ‘XRCC1 is phosphorylated by DNA-dependent protein kinase in response to DNA damage’
Published: 2026

This is an Expression of Concern regarding: Nicolas Lévy, Adeline Martz, Anne Bresson, Catherine Spenlehauer, Gilbert de Murcia, Josiane Ménissier-de Murcia, XRCC1 is phosphorylated by DNA-dependent protein kinase in response to DNA damage, Nucleic Acids Research, Volume 34, Issue 1, 1 January 2006, Pages 32–41, https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkj409

In November 2025, the Editors were made aware of concerns regarding potential image irregularities in Figure 1B of the above article, which were also raised on PubPeer (https://pubpeer.com/publications/CE8559963472A406E479A8477BA600#1). These concerns relate to the apparent duplication of input western–blot bands for Ku70 and Ku80.

The Editors have contacted the authors and are investigating all the issues raised in line with COPE guidance. In the interim, the Editors are publishing this Expression of Concern to alert readers while the outcome of the investigation is pending and advise readers to examine the details of this study with particular care.

Julian E. Sale and Barry L. Stoddard

Senior Executive Editors
