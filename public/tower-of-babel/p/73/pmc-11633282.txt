Dear Sir,

Engaging patients in their own healthcare is a crucial component of patient safety. On September 17, 2023, World Patient Safety Day was commemorated with a focus on the theme “Involving patients in the pursuit of healthcare safety,” emphasizing the essential contribution of patients, families, and caregivers to healthcare safety.[1] When patients are actively involved in their care, they become partners in the healthcare process, which can lead to better outcomes and a reduction in medical errors. Here are some strategies and practices for engaging patients in the context of patient safety:

3CS – COMMUNICATION, CULTURAL COMPETENCE, AND CONSENT

LEAD – LITERACY, EDUCATION, ADVOCACY, AND (SHARED) DECISION-MAKING

Improve patients’ health literacy by providing resources and support.

Provide patients with clear and understandable information about their conditions, treatment options, and potential risks. Use plain language, visual aids, and multimedia resources to ensure comprehension. Encourage questions and discussions about potential risks and benefits. Ensure that patients have a thorough understanding of their treatment plans and are actively involved in the decision-making process. Offer patient education materials to inform patients about common safety risks and how to avoid them. This can include infection prevention, fall prevention, and other safety measures. Establish mechanisms for patients to provide feedback and report safety concerns. Act on this feedback to continually improve patient safety practices.[3]

MEDICATION HISTORY

Always elicit a proper medication history. Educate patients about their medications, including their names, dosages, and potential side effects.[4] Encourage patients to maintain an up-to-date list of their medications and share it with all healthcare providers.[5]
