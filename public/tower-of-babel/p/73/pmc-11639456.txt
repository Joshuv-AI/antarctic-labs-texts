Investigating the effect of teaching assistants (TA) on the level of satisfaction of undergraduate anesthesia students with training in the internship unit
Authors: Shahnam Sedigh Maroufi, Alireza Babajani, Shaqayeq Taghizadeh, Mohammad Gholamzadeh, Seyyedeh Haniyeh Mousavibaghi, Mohammad Mehdi Azizi Darbandi
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
BACKGROUND:Clinical training in the internship unit is the heart of professional training, and teaching assistants can be considered an essential component in the clinical training program. Therefore the present study was conducted to investigate the effect of teaching assistants (TA) on the degree of satisfaction of undergraduate students of Anesthesia with training in the internship unit.MATERIALS AND METHODS:This descriptive-cross-sectional study was conducted in 2023. The sample of the research was the Anesthesia undergraduate students who were included by census method. First, a questionnaire was designed and psychometric to check the level of satisfaction (ICC = 0.992). then it was given to students who had completed at least 1 unit of internship with TAs. The results were analyzed with SPSS software Version 22 and one-way-ANOVA and independent t-statistical tests (P Value <0.05).RESULTS:A total of 60 questionnaires were completed and analyzed. Among them, 39 were female students and 21 were male students who were studying in the 4th, 6th, and 8th semesters. the level of satisfaction of Anesthesia students with training by teaching assistants is at a favorable level with an average of 61.06. There was no statistically significant difference between the variables of gender and academic semester with the degree of satisfaction. 6th-semester students had the highest level of satisfaction with an average satisfaction of 61.63.CONCLUSION:According to the results, Anesthesia undergraduate students are satisfied with the clinical training by TAs, at a favorable and high level. Therefore it is recommended that teaching assistants in education be used to improve students’ satisfaction and learning levels.

Introduction

Clinical training in the internship unit is known as the heart of professional training. This type of training is considered an active and dynamic learning process because the knowledge and skills acquired in internship units are implemented in real situations and at the patient’s bedside.[12] Acquiring the most effective clinical skills requires providing the best clinical training in the internship unit. The main purpose of providing these units is to prepare as many students as possible to perform clinical skills, and the stronger the training, the better the success and results. Therefore clinical education and its quality are essential components of medical education.[23]

One of the influential factors in learning in medical sciences is the teacher or trainer.[4] Teaching assistants, along with professors and clinical trainers, are responsible for teaching clinical skills in educational situations. Most of them are graduate students at the master’s level. Clinical assistant teaching is a common approach in most countries of the world, and graduate education is an opportune time to develop advanced knowledge and professional skills, graduate students are valuable assets to academic institutions that, with proper training and guidance, can make a great contribution to teaching and coaching. By transferring their beneficial experiences to the undergraduate students, the assistant clinical instructors provide the basis for their learning and thinking, and the students will get the maximum benefit from learning.[567] The existence of master’s students as TAs will increase learning, ability, and satisfaction in bachelor’s students of Anesthesia, and by achieving these goals, the mission of developing this graduate education course will be fulfilled.[589]

Due to the nature of the field of Anesthesia, which is considered a clinical field, and half of the course units of this field are related to the internship unit; Therefore it is necessary to pay special attention to the clinical education of this field so that students acquire and implement the necessary skills to perform their future job responsibilities as best as possible.[1011] Despite the usefulness of the TA as a trainer in clinical education, few studies have been conducted in this regard in the field of Anesthesia and this issue can cause concern; Therefore any shortage of qualified trainers is considered a problem in clinical training. Therefore it is necessary to take a special look at the role of trainers in this field.[31213]

Considering the important and influential role of the TA on the level of student learning in the internship unit, and since currently master’s degree students in Anesthesia are teaching undergraduate students in the clinical environment under the title of TAs, and the main goal of their education is to play the role of a coach in the clinical environment, and since there has been no study on the effect of TAs (master’s degree students in Anesthesia education) on the degree of satisfaction of bachelor’s degree students in training in the internship unit, so we decided to investigate the level of satisfaction of students in this regard.

Materials and Methods

Study design and setting

This study was descriptive-analytical cross-sectional research that was conducted in 2023 after receiving the code of ethics.

Study participants and sampling

Sampling was done by census method. The samples of the research included Anesthesia undergraduate students who had completed at least one full internship course under the guidance of Anesthesia education master students under the title of TA. Students who did not spend any of their internship units with teaching assistants were excluded from the study.

Data collection tool and technique

First, to carry out the research, the description of the tasks and expectations of the graduates of the master’s degree in Anesthesia education according to the curriculum approved by the Ministry of Health and Medical Education in 2017, and other sources and texts were studied and examined, and using the mentioned sources, a questionnaire was created. It was designed under the title of evaluating the performance and educational qualification of clinical trainers in the internship unit. Then its validity and reliability were done. The face validity results are listed in Table 1. All questionnaire items scored higher than 0.78 to measure the content validity ratio (CVR) and more than 0.79 to measure the content validity index (CVI). Reliability was performed by test-retest method. The questionnaire was given to 20 undergraduate students of Anesthesia at Gilan University of Medical Sciences and they were asked to fill it. Then, after two weeks, the questionnaire was given to the same students again and they were asked to complete it again. Then, the results of completing the questionnaires were checked and analyzed using the intraclass correlation coefficient index (ICC = 0.992). then explaining the objectives of the research to the students, sampling was started and the evaluation form was given to undergraduate Anesthesia students who had completed at least one internship unit under the supervision of master’s students (students of academic semesters 4, 6, and 8), and they were assured that all their information and opinions will remain confidential and the results of this test will not have any effect on their internship grades. This questionnaire had demographic characteristics (including the academic semester and gender) and 15 items, and each item (question) had a 5-point Likert scale, the response package included excellent, good, middle, poor, and very poor, which were respectively, the numbers 5, 4, 3, 2, and 1 were awarded to them. As a result, the maximum score of each questionnaire is 75, the minimum score is 15, and the average score is 45. The analysis was done based on the score of the questionnaire. Based on this method of analysis, the scores obtained were collected and then analyzed based on the following points:

Ethical consideration

This research was approved by the ethical code IR.IUMS.REC.1401.729 in the ethics committee of Iran University of Medical Sciences. All ethical considerations, including respect for students’ rights and honesty in the process of conducting research and research standards, were observed in this study.

Data analysis

The information obtained from the questionnaires that were filled was analyzed with SPSS software version 22. A total of 60 questionnaires were collected and analyzed. Due to the normality of the data, the parametric method was used. Using descriptive statistics methods and providing descriptive indices the data were described. Independent t-test (summing questionnaire scores and comparing those scores according to gender) and one-way ANOVA test (summing questionnaire scores and comparing those scores according to academic semester) were used.

Results

The results of collecting 60 questionnaires were analyzed. Of these, 21 were male students and 39 were female students, 25 of whom were studying in the 4th semester, 21 in the 6th semester, and 14 in the 8th semester. The average satisfaction of Bachelor of Anesthesia students from TA with training in the internship unit was 61.06 with a standard deviation of 10.48, which was evaluated according to the questionnaire score analysis table in the optimal range (if the scores were above 60, the amount of the desired variable was evaluated). The highest average level of satisfaction was 75 and the lowest average level of satisfaction was 35.

According to the results of Table 2 and using the independent t-test, the equality of the average satisfaction of students with TAs in two gender groups was compared with one another. According to the obtained probability of P = 0.886 and the significance level of P <0.05, the two gender groups were not significantly different in terms of average satisfaction, although male students had higher average satisfaction than female students. The average level of satisfaction of male students according to the obtained average of 61.33 and taking into account the analysis of the questionnaire (if the scores are above 60, the amount of the variable is evaluated as favorable), was evaluated at a favorable and satisfactory level, and according to the average satisfaction obtained in females, which is 60.92, and taking into account the questionnaire analysis method, female students were also satisfied with the performance of TAs at a favorable level. According to the one-way ANOVA test, the average satisfaction of students with training in the internship unit in different academic semesters was investigated. The level of satisfaction of 4th-semester students with an average satisfaction of 60.84 and 6th-semester students with an average of 63.61 and taking into account the analysis method of questionnaire scores was evaluated at a favorable and satisfactory level. It should be noted that the 6th-semester students had the highest level of satisfaction. The students of the 8th semester were evaluated with an average satisfaction of 57.64 and considering the analysis method of the scores of the questionnaire at a relatively favorable and relatively satisfied level. According to the obtained P = 0.257 and the significance level of P <0.05, the students of different academic semesters did not differ significantly in terms of their level of satisfaction.

Discussion

In this research, the level of satisfaction of undergraduate Anesthesia students with training in the internship unit by master’s students as TAs was investigated. There are various studies regarding the effect of TAs in teaching and training students in various fields of medical sciences, but very few studies have been done in the field of Anesthesia. In the current research, the item “Appropriate behavior with students” had a high percentage of satisfaction with an average of 4.22 out of 5 and % 84.4satisfaction, but the results of this research are are different from Rezaei’s research in 2014, which was conducted to examine the quality of clinical education. In the mentioned research, only % 34 of the students were satisfied with the behavior of the instructors, which shows a low percentage compared to the current research.[15]

In a study conducted by Omid zadi et al., in 2017 to determine the factors affecting the quality of clinical education from the point of view of the operating room and Anesthesia students, the results showed that the level of satisfaction of the Anesthesia and operating room students with the performance of the trainers at the bedside was at a moderate level. It is contrary to the results of the present study[10] because, in the present study, the average level of satisfaction was evaluated at a favorable and high level.

In a study conducted in 2017 by Farghadani et al., to investigate the state of clinical education from the point of view of Anesthesia students and the operating room, the results showed that Anesthesia students expressed the level of performance of their instructors at a relatively favorable level and there was no high satisfaction. One of the most important reasons for this dissatisfaction was the lack of instructors with a master’s degree in Anesthesia in clinical education. Therefore the existence of these trainers is considered necessary to improve the level of education of students.[12] These results are not consistent with the current research, because, in the current research, the presence of master’s students as TAs has played a significant role in the level of satisfaction of undergraduate students with training in the internship unit.

In a study conducted in the United States in 2014–2015 by Jacob P. Smith and colleagues to improve medical students’ understanding of ultrasound using an anatomy teaching assistant and the physician teaching model, the results showed that the level of satisfaction and learning Students in the clinical physician and anatomy teaching assistant group were significantly higher than the clinical physician group and more than% 84 of the students stated that the presence of the anatomy teaching assistant increased their level of learning, understanding, and interest in the anatomy course. They believed that anatomy teaching assistant is considered a valuable part of education in the country.[16] These results are consistent with the current research. The current research, “Creating motivation and interest in learning in students” was evaluated by the teaching assistants with an average of 3.60 out of 5 at a high level of satisfaction.

In a study conducted in 2015 by Summer F. Odom and et al. to determine the impact of teaching assistants in education, the majority of students stated that teaching by teaching assistants leads to active learning increases their involvement and participation in the classroom, and improves the development of students in leadership[17] and these results are consistent with the present study that%68.4 of the students rated the teaching assistant’s “division of work and creating a situation to increase the clinical skills of the student” at an excellent and good level, and with an average of 3.87 out of 5, they were satisfied with the teaching assistant’s performance in this case. Dividing work and creating opportunities to increase clinical skills by the teaching assistant can cause active learning and increase the participation and involvement of students in the internship unit, and students will get the most educational benefit during the hours they are in the internship.

In a study conducted in 2018 by Sasinan Prajongjai and et al. in Thailand to investigate students’ satisfaction with teaching assistants (TA) in general education, the results showed that most students, with an average of 4.30 out of 5, from supervision and Teaching by teaching assistants had a high level of satisfaction.[14] The results of this study are in line with the present study, where the satisfaction average of the teaching assistants was obtained according to the average response to the questions of 4.03 out of 5. In Sasinan Prajongjai’s study, the level of student satisfaction with the communication and public relations of teaching assistants with students was evaluated at a high level with an average of 4.04, which is in line with the present study with an average satisfaction of 4.22 from the item “appropriate interaction of teaching assistants with students”. Both were evaluated at a high level. One of the reasons for this is that graduate students, especially master’s degrees, are usually used as teaching assistants, and because of the small age difference between undergraduate students and assistants and the experience of assistants being closer to students, there is better communication. Also, in the mentioned study, the level of satisfaction with the scientific information of assistants was obtained at a high level with an average of 4.08, which is in line with the present study. In the present study, the level of student satisfaction with the skills and mastery of the assistants in performing clinical tasks and theoretical topics was evaluated at 4.05. In Sasinan Prajongjai’s study, an average of 47.47 students’ satisfaction with the responsiveness of teaching assistants to students’ questions was obtained, which is consistent with the result of the present study, where the average response to students’ questions was 4.30.[14]

In a study conducted by Asmaa Abdel Nasser et al., in 2021 comparing faculty members vs. teaching assistants as facilitators of problem-based learning in the understanding of medical students, the results showed that%79.6 of students were satisfied with teaching assistants as facilitators in education, which was more compared to faculty members. The reason for that can be the closeness of the teaching assistants’ age to the students and their better understanding of the student’s needs.[18]

The results of the study are useful for the planners and members of the Anesthesia department, undergraduate students of Anesthesia, teaching assistants, and policymakers of the master’s degree in Anesthesia education. Among the benefits of this research for students, we can mention that they will directly participate in evaluating and improving the performance of their instructors. The planners and members of the Anesthesia group are informed about the performance of the clinical trainers, and according to the responses of the students to the questions, they are trying to find the best solution to improve the level of satisfaction of the students with the education. Assistants’ awareness of the survey results increases their interest and motivation in education, they get to know their strengths and weaknesses and try to improve them. The fact that the degree of satisfaction of undergraduate students of Anesthesia with the performance of teaching assistants in the internship unit was obtained at a high level, can be a promise for the policymakers and founders of the master of Anesthesia education to fulfill the mission of this degree.[14]

Limitation and recommendation

It is suggested that other universities and centers of medical sciences use the questionnaire compiled in this research to evaluate the performance and educational qualification of clinical instructors and teaching assistants in the internship unit. One of the limitations of this research is the non-cooperation of some students to complete the questionnaire due to the fear of a negative impact on the grade of the internship unit. Therefore all students were assured that the information obtained from the questionnaire would be published without mentioning their names and personal information and would not have any negative effect on their internship scores.

Conclusion

Considering that the majority of students were highly satisfied with the performance of teaching assistants in training, it is recommended that universities and centers of medical sciences use teaching assistants in this unit to make the training as useful as possible and for the students to benefit the most from the training in the internship unit.
