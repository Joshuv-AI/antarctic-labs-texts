The daily intake of saturated fatty acids (SFA) was 17.0 in 2022. In terms of the sex of the participants, intake of SFA in male (18.9 g) was higher than that of female (15.0 g) (Figure 1), and by age groups, the intake was the highest approximately 20.0 g among age groups of 10s and 20s (Figure 2).
