Comprehensive evaluation of rural entrepreneurial environment based on Pressure-State-Response model -evidence from China
Authors: Bin Gao, Naiwen Zhang
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Background and aimsThe study of rural entrepreneurial environment is one of the key issues in rural entrepreneurship research.The rural entrepreneurship environment plays a key role in increasing the success rate of farmers' entrepreneurship and promoting their employment. Scientific evaluation of the rural entrepreneurial environment is important for governments to formulate relevant policies and optimize the rural entrepreneurial environment.Methods and resultsThis paper constructs a rural entrepreneurship environment evaluation index system based on PSR (Pressure-State-Response) model.The entropy Weight-TOPSIS method was used to measure the rural entrepreneurship environment in 30 provinces in China, the Gini coefficient was used to explore the regional differences, and the barrier degree model was used to diagnose the barrier factors. The results of the study show that: China's rural entrepreneurship environment has a ladder, the rural entrepreneurship environment is divided into four tiers, Guizhou is in the leading tiers, Shandong and other 13 provinces are in the challenging tiers, Inner Mongolia and other 11 provinces are in the catching-up tiers, and Hainan and other 5 provinces are in the improving tiers. Provincial differences are large, and the regional rural entrepreneurship environment is obviously unbalanced.ConclusionThis paper applies the PSR ("Pressure State Response") model to rural entrepreneurship environment evaluation, analyzes the relationship between the indicators according to the relationship structure of the sub-models in the model, and provides a theoretical basis for the rural entrepreneurship environment evaluation system and scientific evaluation. The rural entrepreneurship evaluation index system is regarded as an ecosystem, and the nonlinear effect between the elements is considered.

Introduction

Rural entrepreneurship is an important part of the overall strategy of "mass entrepreneurship and innovation". Rural entrepreneurship is an important way to prosper rural industries. Rural entrepreneurship has an irreplaceable role to play in promoting the prosperity of agriculture, the beauty of the countryside and the wealth of farmers, thereby realizing the revitalization of the countryside [1].Promoting farmers' income is the key to solving the "agricultural, rural and peasant problems". However, promoting the orderly development of farmers' entrepreneurial activities is an important initiative to increase farmers' income [2].Farmers' entrepreneurship helps to optimize the structure of rural employment, reduce the income gap between urban and rural areas, and help to accelerate the level of agricultural modernization [3,4].Entrepreneurial activity is a complex systematic project, and the degree of activity is constrained and influenced by the internal and external environment. Rural entrepreneurial environment is a key factor influencing farmers' entrepreneurial willingness [5].A good rural entrepreneurial environment is a guarantee for the smooth implementation of entrepreneurial activities of farm households, which is directly related to entrepreneurial performance [6]. Rural entrepreneurial environment plays a key role in improving the success rate of entrepreneurship and promoting the employment of farm households [7,8].In the implementation of rural revitalization strategy, how to effectively create an entrepreneurial environment, strengthen the support of entrepreneurial environment to farmers' entrepreneurship, so that farmers really become the protagonist of rural revitalization, is a major theoretical and practical proposition that needs to be studied in depth [9,10]. The evaluation of rural entrepreneurial environment is the basis and premise of entrepreneurial activity research. How to evaluate the differences in rural entrepreneurial environment? What are the indicators for evaluating rural entrepreneurial environment? How to optimize the rural entrepreneurial environment? These questions are worth exploring and thinking about.

Therefore, this paper aims to construct a rural entrepreneurship environment evaluation index system under the rural revitalization strategy. It compares the rural entrepreneurial environment in different regions of China, and puts forward countermeasure suggestions for optimizing the rural entrepreneurial environment from the perspective of long-term development. The purpose of this paper is to promote entrepreneurship and employment of farmers in their localities, accelerate the cultivation of new dynamic energy for rural development, continuously expand the employment space and income channels for farmers, and provide stronger support for the promotion of agricultural and rural modernization.

The expected contributions of this study are as follows: first, theoretical motivation. Usually, PSR (Pressure-State-Response) model is mainly used to evaluate the quality of ecological environment. This paper applies the PSR (Pressure-State-Response) model to evaluate the rural entrepreneurial environment. On the basis of existing research on evaluation of entrepreneurial environment, combined with the unique attributes of farmers' entrepreneurship, the PSR model evaluation index system of rural entrepreneurial environment is constructed. Second, measurement practice. The entropy weight-Topsis method is used to comprehensively evaluate the rural entrepreneurial environment in 30 provinces in China, and to comprehensively analyze the current situation and problems of the rural entrepreneurial environment. The Gini coefficient was used to explore regional differences in the rural entrepreneurial environment, and the barrier degree model was used to diagnose the factors that hinder the rural entrepreneurial environment.Combined with the characteristics of rural entrepreneurial environment with large differences and obvious spatial imbalance, it puts forward countermeasures and suggestions for targeted optimization of rural entrepreneurial environment. This study can provide a decision-making reference for the regional formulation of rural entrepreneurship environment policies.

Literature review on entrepreneurial environment

Entrepreneurial environment research is one of the key issues in entrepreneurship research. Entrepreneurial environment is a collection of a series of concepts, is the result of the synthesis of a variety of factors, the correct understanding and understanding of the entrepreneurial environment is the premise of the evaluation of the entrepreneurial environment [7]. The evaluation of entrepreneurial environment has been explored from multidisciplinary perspectives such as sociology, economics, policy, public management, etc., and there have been a number of explorations and demonstrations with fruitful value, realistic guiding significance and pioneering. Mainly from the connotation of entrepreneurial environment, entrepreneurial environment evaluation index system, entrepreneurial environment evaluation model, entrepreneurial environment evaluation method four aspects of combing entrepreneurial environment literature.

Connotation of entrepreneurial environment

As a type of entrepreneurial environment, entrepreneurial environment is a concept that is constantly changing and evolving, and its understanding is growing. The concept of entrepreneurial environment is mainly generalized from systemic and ecological perspectives [11].Firstly, the system perspective. The entrepreneurial environment is essentially a network of systems, which can be understood as a community within a specific region, which includes numerous interacting entrepreneurial subjects, and the benign operation of the whole system aims to maximize economic and social value [[12], [13], [14], [15], [16]]. Secondly, the ecological perspective. The entrepreneurial environment is essentially an ecosystem, where different sectors have different goals, traditions and impacts, but this ecosystem endows them with a symbiotic relationship that allows them to develop synergistic and thus maximize social wealth [[17], [18], [19], [20]].

The rural entrepreneurship environment proposed in this paper mainly refers to the entrepreneurship-related hard environment, such as infrastructure, natural geographical conditions, economic conditions and so on.

Study on the Evaluation Index System of entrepreneurial environment

Scholars have constructed the evaluation index system of entrepreneurial environment based on different regions and disciplines. Some scholars have constructed the evaluation index system of entrepreneurial environment based on different regions. Establish provincial-level entrepreneurial environment evaluation index system [[21], [22], [23], [24], [25]] and urban entrepreneurial environment evaluation index system [[26], [27], [28], [29], [30]][[26], [27], [28], [29], [30], [31]]. Some scholars have constructed the evaluation index system of entrepreneurial environment based on different disciplines. The Evaluation Index System of college students' entrepreneurship environment [[32], [33], [34]] and the Evaluation Index System of Farmers' entrepreneurship environment [[35], [36], [37], [38], [39]] were constructed.

Entrepreneurial environment evaluation model

The entrepreneurial environment evaluation models mainly include five-dimension model, GEM model, MOS model and PSR model [40]. Firstly, the five-dimensional model. Construct the entrepreneurial environment evaluation model from government policies and working procedures, socio-economic conditions, entrepreneurial and management skills, financial support for entrepreneurship, and non-financial support for entrepreneurship. Secondly, the GEM model. Constructs the entrepreneurial environment evaluation model from financial support, government policies, government programs, education and training, research transfer, business environment and professional infrastructure, degree of openness of the domestic market, availability of physical infrastructure, and cultural and social norms. Thirdly, the MOS model. Constructs entrepreneurial environment evaluation from entrepreneurship education, entrepreneurship promotion, reduction of barriers to entry, start-up support, start-up financing, and target group politics [[41], [42], [43], [44]]. Fourthly, PSR model. PSR ("Pressure-State-Response") model was applied to resource environment evaluation in the early stage, and later applied to ecological environment, security environment, audit environment and innovation and entrepreneurship environment evaluation. From the pressure (Pressure)-state (State)-response (Response) to build the entrepreneurial environment evaluation model [25,30].

Evaluation methods of entrepreneurial environment

The main use of hierarchical analysis, factor analysis, entropy weight method, etc., measurement and evaluation is divided into a single method of rough measurement of entrepreneurial environment and the use of two or more combinations of methods to carry out a comprehensive measure [45]. This paper adopts entropy weight-Topsis method to measure rural entrepreneurial environment, entropy value method is an objective empowerment method, aiming at empowering the indicator system, and TOPSIS method is to evaluate the advantages and disadvantages of rural entrepreneurial environment by approximating the degree of ideal solution. Combining the advantages of both entropy value method and TOPSIS method has the advantages of simple calculation and reasonable results, which can make the results of rural entrepreneurial environment measurement more reasonable [46].

Research outlook: the existing research has laid a good foundation for this topic, but there are still some limitations and room for expansion: firstly, party scholars have constructed an evaluation index system of entrepreneurial environment based on different regions and different subjects, and rural entrepreneurial environment belongs to a kind of regional entrepreneurial environment, and there is a relative lack of research on the evaluation of rural entrepreneurial environment, and the research strength and depth are not enough [47]. Secondly, the evaluation of rural entrepreneurial environment is mostly based on qualitative research, quantitative research is not rich enough, and there is a relative lack of empirical research on the evaluation of rural entrepreneurial environment to comprehensively reveal the regional differences in rural entrepreneurial environment. Finally, most of the existing evaluation methods are based on traditional statistical analysis, which has some limitations and can have certain errors, which to a certain extent have an impact on the analysis and judgment.

Construction of evaluation index system for rural entrepreneurial environment

Pressure-State-Response model applicability analysis

The PSR model is an evaluation model commonly used in the sub-discipline of ecosystem health assessment within the discipline of environmental quality assessment, originally proposed by Canadian statisticians David J. Rapport and Tony Friend (1979), and later used as a framework system for studying environmental problems [25].

At present, PSR model, five-dimension model, GEM model and MOS model have different focuses, PSR model focuses on evaluating innovation and entrepreneurship environment from system perspective, while five-dimension model, GEM model and MOS model focus on entrepreneurship opportunity to evaluate entrepreneurship environment. However, rural entrepreneurship environment is an ecosystem, the role of each element is non-linear, based on the entrepreneurial ecosystem perspective to evaluate the rural entrepreneurship environment is more scientific and effective, so the PSR model is more appropriate to evaluate the rural entrepreneurship environment.

The PSR model can reflect the mutual influence relationship among the elements of pressure indicators, state indicators and response indicators in the rural entrepreneurial environment. Among them, pressure indicators refer to the impact of economic and social development on rural entrepreneurial activities, and the impact is both positive and negative. The state indicator is the state of rural entrepreneurship environment. Response indicators are the actions taken by the government, social organizations and other subjects to change the rural entrepreneurial environment. Rural entrepreneurial environment is an ecosystem, PSR model can reflect the balance of pressure indicators, state indicators and response indicators in rural entrepreneurial environment.PSR model is suitable for rural entrepreneurial environment evaluation, the mechanism of PSR model of rural entrepreneurial environment is shown in Fig. 1.Fig. 1Psr model mechanism of rural entrepreneurship environment.Fig. 1

Pressure indicators

There are eight secondary indicators under the pressure indicator. Gross regional product is one of the most important indicators reflecting the economic situation of a region; the higher the gross regional product(X1), the more developed the regional economy. There is a new trend of rural population migration, and a new demographic dividend characterized by the return of migrant workers to their hometowns to start businesses is emerging in the countryside. The larger the rural population(X2) is, the more potential rural entrepreneurs there are. Countries with high rural child dependency ratios(X3) and high rural elderly dependency ratios(X4) have weaker rural entrepreneurial dynamics. The more disposable income per rural farmer(X5), the more disposable funds for innovation and entrepreneurship. The higher the per capita consumption expenditure of rural residents(X6), the lower the disposable funds for innovation and entrepreneurship. The gross value of agriculture, forestry, animal husbandry and fishery is one of the important indicators reflecting the level of rural economic development. Difficulty in financing is one of the main obstacles facing farmers' entrepreneurship; the higher the balance of local and foreign currency loans from financial institutions(X7), the higher the financial health of rural areas.

Status indicators

There are six secondary indicators under the status indicator. The total power of agricultural machinery(X9) is one of the important indicators reflecting the degree of rural mechanization and a key indicator of the level of agricultural modernization; the greater the total power of agricultural machinery, the higher the level of rural mechanization. Rural electricity consumption, agricultural fertilizer application agricultural diesel applicationand pesticide application are one of the key indicators reflecting the state of resilience and vitality of the rural entrepreneurial environment. The greater the rural electricity consumption(X10), the greater the application of agricultural fertilizer(X11), the greater the application of agricultural diesel(X12), the greater the application of pesticides(X13), and the greater the total sown area of crops(X14), the more resilient and dynamic the rural entrepreneurial environment is.

Response indicators

There are seven indicators under the response indicator. The higher the expenditure on agriculture, forestry and water(X15), the amount of investment in fixed assets by rural households(X16), the investment in agriculture, forestry, animal husbandry and fisheries by rural households(X17), rural health rooms(X18), cultural stations in townships(X19), rural broadband access subscribers(X20), and expenditure on the rural minimum subsistence guarantee(X21), the more it reflects the more actions taken by the Government, social organizations and other actors to change the rural entrepreneurial environment.

Measurement method

Entropy weight - TOPSIS

Through combing the entrepreneurial environment evaluation literature, it is found that the current measurement methods mainly include data envelopment analysis, hierarchical analysis, entropy value method, fuzzy comprehensive evaluation and factor analysis. The idea of entropy weight-TOPSIS method is to use the entropy weight method to determine the weight of each index to avoid subjectivity, and then use the technique of approximating the ideal solution to determine the advantages and disadvantages of the evaluation objects in order, which has the advantages of intuitive geometric significance, less loss of information as well as flexible arithmetic and so on. Therefore, the entropy weight-TOPSIS method is used to comprehensively measure and evaluate the rural entrepreneurial environment [47].

Let there be m evaluated subjects, n specific evaluation indicators, xij is the original numerical data of the jth indicator in the ith evaluated subject, then the original evaluation indicator matrix X is:(1)X=[X11X12⋯X1nX21X22⋯X2n⋮⋮⋱⋮Xm1Xm2⋯Xmn]

The first step is data standardization. Since the outline of each indicator is different, the data of each indicator is first standardized. Dagum's Gini coefficient and its decomposition method by subgroups

This paper analyzes the regional disparities in China's rural entrepreneurial environment based on the Dagum Gini coefficient and its decomposition by subgroups [45]. Compared with the traditional Gini coefficient, coefficient of variation and Taylor's coefficient, Dagum Gini coefficient can take into account the distribution of sub-samples and cross overlap between the samples, which has a great advantage in analyzing the spatial imbalance, and can answer the question of the source of regional disparity. Equation (17) is the calculation formula of Dagum's Gini coefficient.(17)G=∑j=1K∑h=1k∑i=1nj∑r=1nh|yji−yhr|2n2y‾Where G is the overall Gini coefficient, n is the number of provinces, k is the number of regions, i and r denote the number of provinces within a region, nj (nh) is the number of provinces within the j(h) region, yji (yhr) denotes the value of rural entrepreneurial environment in any province within the j(h) region, and y denotes the average value of rural entrepreneurial environment.

According to Dagum's Gini coefficient decomposition method, the Gini coefficient can be decomposed into three components: the contribution of intra-area disparity Gw, the contribution of inter-area disparity Gnb, and the contribution of hypervariable density Gt, and it satisfies the relationship of G = Gw + Gnb + Gt. Conclusions and Recommendations

Conclusions

In this study, a rural entrepreneurship environment evaluation index system based on the PSR model was constructed considering the realistic attributes of the rural entrepreneurship environment. Unlike previous studies, the rural entrepreneurship environment evaluation index system is considered as an ecosystem and the nonlinear effects among factors are taken into account.

This study confirms that the pressure, national and response indicators in the rural entrepreneurial environment under the rural revitalization strategy form a nested, intertwined and dynamically developing entrepreneurial ecosystem. The rural revitalization strategy under the optimization of the rural entrepreneurial environment actually presents a spiral, where only the optimization of the entrepreneurial environment can provide farmers with a wider space to start or return to their own business and contribute to quantitative and qualitative changes in the rural entrepreneurial environment.

The results of the study show that: China's rural entrepreneurship environment has a ladder, the rural entrepreneurship environment is divided into four tiers, Guizhou is in the leading tiers, Shandong and other 13 provinces are in the challenging tiers, Inner Mongolia and other 11 provinces are in the catching-up tiers, and Hainan and other 5 provinces are in the improving tiers. Provincial differences are large, and the regional rural entrepreneurship environment is obviously unbalanced.The environment presents the characteristics of "the central region is developing momentum, the eastern region is catching up, the western region and the northeast region is developing slowly".

Recommendations

Provinces can implement differentiated policy supply according to the rural entrepreneurial environment and the characteristics of different regions, especially for rural entrepreneurial projects with characteristics and advantages. We will increase support for entrepreneurship, and actively guide farmers, returning farmers or college students to create family farms [48,49]. We will increase support for agricultural subsidies, and give appropriate preference to new agricultural business entities such as family farms [50]. We will increase support for rural entrepreneurship and promote intensive processing of agricultural products. Encourage financial institutions to support credit and establish a credit evaluation system. We will increase the training program for new types of vocational farmers in due time, improve the comprehensive quality of vocational farmers, and cultivate "green collars" who "love agriculture, understand technology, and be good at management". Increase the connection of rural entrepreneurship market and expand new growth points of rural entrepreneurship income [[51], [52], [53]].
