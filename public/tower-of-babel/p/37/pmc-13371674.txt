Editorial expression of concern: Adelmidrol, in combination with hyaluronic acid, displays increased anti-inflammatory and analgesic effects against monosodium iodoacetate-induced osteoarthritis in rats
Authors: Rosanna Di Paola, Roberta Fusco, Daniela Impellizzeri, Marika Cordaro, Domenico Britti, Valeria Maria Morittu, Maurizio Evangelista, Salvatore Cuzzocrea
Published: 2026

Editorial Expression of Concern: Arthritis Research & Therapy 18, 291 (2016)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s13075-016-1189-5

The Editors-in-Chief would like to alert the readers that concerns have been raised regarding Fig. 3 in this article, where panels c and d appear to overlap.

The authors have shared an alternative image for panel d and requested a correction, but have not provided the underlying raw data upon request. Readers are therefore advised to interpret these data with caution.

The authors did not respond to correspondence from the Publisher about this Editorial Expression of Concern.
