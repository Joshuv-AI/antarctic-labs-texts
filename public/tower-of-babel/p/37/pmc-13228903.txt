Trends in Deaths and Socioeconomic Costs Attributable to Direct Smoking in the Republic of Korea, 2019-2023
Authors: Seonghee Park, 성희 박
Published: 2026

The estimated number of deaths attributable to direct smoking among adults aged 30 years and older was 68,536 (60,216 men and 8,320 women) in 2023 (Figure 1). The socioeconomic costs attributable to direct smoking increased to 14.9517 trillion KRW in 2023 as compared to 2.7604 trillion KRW in 2019, reflecting a steady increase over the past 5 years (Figure 2).
