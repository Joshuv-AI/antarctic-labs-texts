PA2096. The effect of COVID-19 on emergency department visits: Real-world data from Greece, 2016-2024
Authors: J Fanourgiakis, E Papadakis, I Dimotikalis, E Papadakis, E Kanoupakis
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundThe Greek National Health System (NHS) provides healthcare services through a network of 127 public hospitals, and they are administratively organized under 7 Health Regions (HRs). The objective of this study is to demonstrate the annually Emergency Departments (EDs) visits across all over public hospitals in Greece during the period 2016-2024, before and after Covid 19 period of 2020-2022.MethodsData regarding the EDs visits were obtained from the Greek Ministry of Health and Principal Components Analysis (PCA) and Hierarchical Clustering Analysis (HCA) was conducted.ResultsIn the period 2016-2019, the EDs visits generally showed an increasing trend. In 2016, EDs visits were approximately 4,700,000, and by 2019, they had increased to 4,900,000. EDs visits for the years 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, and 2024 are compared to the reference year 2019. Over the next five years, the number of ED visits declined compared to 2019. Specifically, the decreases were more than 32.00% in 2020, 27.00% in 2021, 12.00% in 2022, 5.00% in 2023, and 1.00% in 2024. Overall, for the years 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, and 2024, there appear to be approximately 3,900,000 lost visits to the EDs compared to the visits in 2019.ConclusionsThese findings are crucial for decision makers and health policymakers in Greece, as well as for comparative analysis with other health systems. Given the observed decline in activity throughout the study period, further research is necessary to evaluate whether the population’s healthcare needs were adequately met, and to identify any impacts on mortality, life expectancy, unmet healthcare needs, private health spending, or other relevant areas.Key messages• The data indicates a significant reduction in ED visits during the period from 2020 to 2024• The decline in ED visits raises questions about whether the population’s healthcare needs were adequately met during this period.TopicManagement, Health policy, Emergency Departments (EDs) visits.
