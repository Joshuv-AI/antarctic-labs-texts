Correction to: Hydro- and Xerohalophyte Species Drive Compositional and Functional Divergence in Bacterial Leaf Endosphere
Authors: Beatriz Roncero-Ramos, Elena Romano-Rodríguez, Enrique Mateos-Naranjo, Pedro Valle-Romero, Susana Redondo-Gómez
Published: 2026

Correction to: Microbial Ecology (2026) 89:39

10.1007/s00248-025-02679-x

In the original published version of this manuscript, the analyses were done with sequencing data from two sampling seasons (spring and autumn), which were actually from the same season (autumn). Therefore, the use of season as a factor has been removed from the whole article and the analyses and all data belonging to the spring season.

The following corrections have been made:

Introduction: Removal of the use of season as a factor to compare endosphere communities (lines 101-102).

Materials and Methods: Modification of the sampling protocol (seasons sampled and number of samples; lines 148-150) and references to using season as a factor for the statistical analyses (line 190).
