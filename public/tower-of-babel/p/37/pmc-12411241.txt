Correction to “Successful Haploidentical Hematopoietic Stem Cell Transplantation for Paroxysmal Nocturnal Hemoglobinuria With Severe Pancytopenia Developed After Long‐Term Aplastic Anemia Treatment”
Published: 2025

Sakurai K Saito K Hatta S Katsuoka Y Meguro K Yokoyama H Izumi T Successful haploidentical hematopoietic stem cell transplantation for paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria with severe pancytopenia developed after long‐term aplastic anemia treatment Clinical Case Reports 2024. 12:e8832 10.1002/ccr3.8832.

In the sentence “the conditioning regimen…” under the case presentation section (page 2, right column, line 5), the text “cyclophosphamide (14.5 mg/m2 for 2 days)” was incorrect. This should read as “cyclophosphamide (14.5 mg/kg for 2 days).”

We apologize for this error.
