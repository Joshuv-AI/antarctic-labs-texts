Correction: The intrinsically disordered protein SPE-56 is required for acrosomal-like exocytosis and fertility in Caenorhabditis elegans
Authors: Dieter-Christian Gottschling, Sarah Eiser, Frank Döring
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-026-47896-7, published online 11 April 2026

The original version of this Article contained errors.

Firstly, in the Results section, under the subheading ‘Deletion of the whole IDR disrupts SPE-56 function at all temperatures’, the term ‘amino acids’ was inadvertently replaced with “base pairs”.

As a result, where:

“To evaluate the functional relevance of the C-terminal SPE-56 IDR, we created stepwise CRISPR/Cas9-mediated deletions (C-Δ) of 54, 89, 94, and 149 base pairs within the IDR sequence (Fig. 7A).”

now reads:

“To evaluate the functional relevance of the C-terminal SPE-56 IDR, we created stepwise CRISPR/Cas9-mediated deletions (C-Δ) of 54, 89, 94, and 149 amino acids within the IDR sequence (Fig. 7A).”

In addition, Figure 1 contained a display error in panel C, where the micrometre scale bar was missing.Fig. 1Structure and fertility phenotypes of F56D5.2 mutant alleles.
