Research on the method of constructing a knowledge map of relay protection device faults oriented to textual features
Authors: Wang Kui, Song Kun, Xue Xiaochao, Zhao Yiming
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
In recent years, science and technology have been developing at a high speed, and the new generation of information technology featuring digitization, networking and intelligence has been increasingly innovative and breakthrough, pushing the whole society into the digital era. The paper, on the basis of knowledge graph technology, determines the fault classification according to the standard fault knowledge base of relay protection device, adopts the method of mapping knowledge graph ontology fields to deal with the structured data related to the faults of the relay protection device involved, extracts the text feature data information from the unstructured data related to the faults of the relay protection device through the deep learning model, focuses on the research of the vector conversion of the text feature statements, and finally extracts the information extraction model through the double neural network joint extraction of statement features. The information extraction model is constructed through the method of joint extraction of statement features by dual neural networks, and the construction of the knowledge map of relay protection device faults is finally completed through model training and result optimization. The research on the construction of relay protection device fault knowledge map for text features can improve the efficiency of abnormal data management of relay protection device, and will further promote the digital transformation of the power system.

Introduction

In recent years, the rapid development of science and technology, we have ushered in the digital information age, with digital, networked, intelligent as the characteristics of a new generation of information technology increasingly innovative breakthroughs1.At present, China is in a period of deep integration of digital technology and economic society, digital transformation and heightening of traditional industries. Driven by the background of the new era, innovation and reform in one of China’s traditional industries in the power industry is also an inevitable development situation2.With the release of State Grid’s ‘Digital New Infrastructure’ ten key construction tasks, the company’s business scale continues to expand, especially under the new market economic conditions and environment, the power industry should seize new opportunities to accelerate the digital transformation of the company and the power grid, and to serve the ‘dual-carbon’ goal. The goal of ‘double carbon’ is realized. Power enterprises traditional manual management equipment defects mode there are many unavoidable and can not be solved drawbacks: the operation process procedures are cumbersome, inefficient, error rate is high, can not provide timely feedback on the status of equipment defects and processing3.

Although knowledge graph technology offers great advantages for domains dealing with large amounts of semi-structured and unstructured data, its application in the power grid industry remains limited due to the complexity involved in constructing such graphs4. This limitation highlights the current gap in effectively leveraging knowledge representation techniques within the electric power field.

A large amount of structured and unstructured data is generated during the operation of relay protection devices. Unstructured data is not easy to store but often contains a lot of valuable information5.In order to better mine the data value information in it, it is necessary to establish a data processing scheme for the unstructured data heterogeneous data in equipment defects. Focus on solving the information extraction of unstructured data, achieving the construction of relay protection device fault knowledge map, and solving the problem of knowledge modeling and knowledge fusion6.

As the knowledge graph technology is still in the research stage in the electric power field, there is no mature knowledge graph in the electric power field, and moreover, there is a lack of knowledge graph for equipment defect management. Existing methods primarily rely on relationship extraction and strategy analysis approaches. For instance, literature7 proposes a condition-based maintenance strategy for secondary electrical equipment based on a hierarchical analysis model, which considers various types of information and online monitoring data characteristics of secondary equipment. While this approach introduces a systematic framework for maintenance planning, it lacks the capability to dynamically adapt to real-time data changes and does not fully exploit the semantic relationships among different equipment states.

Similarly, literature8 employs RNN neural networks for textual information relation extraction, generating text vectors through continuous training and looping, thus utilizing the structural information of text sequences. Although this method yields relatively favorable results, it may struggle with capturing long-range dependencies and semantic nuances in complex technical documents related to power systems. Furthermore, the computational overhead associated with training deep RNN models can limit its scalability in real-world industrial applications.

Moreover, literature9 has made significant strides by establishing a knowledge graph for diverse types of power equipment data, which notably improves the efficiency of equipment query processes. However, this approach does not fully address the complexities involved in entity relationship extraction, especially when compared with our proposed method. One major limitation lies in its reliance on rule-based or shallow machine learning models, which may fail to capture subtle correlations between entities in unstructured textual descriptions. Additionally, the knowledge graph built in9 lacks fine-grained semantic understanding, limiting its ability to support advanced reasoning tasks.

Literature10 adopts ALBERT as the core of its entity-relationship extraction scheme, focusing on optimizing the pre-training process of text information models. Through continuous adjustments and optimizations, the study achieves more efficient and accurate classification of relationship categories. However, the work in 10 primarily focuses on general-domain datasets and does not specifically tailor the model to the unique linguistic patterns found in power system fault descriptions. As a result, its performance may degrade when applied directly to highly technical and domain-specific texts.

In contrast, literature11 employs a relatively straightforward approach to relationship extraction, which may lead to limited conclusions due to its simplistic modeling assumptions. Specifically, the method does not consider contextual dependencies and semantic variations across different types of equipment faults, resulting in suboptimal performance in real-world scenarios.

Finally, literature12 attempts to unify the representation and integration of multimodal data at the high-level semantic level, laying the foundation for fusion technology in the construction of multimodal knowledge graphs. Nevertheless, this method pays less attention to nonlinear variables, leading to a lack of authenticity in the constructed models. Moreover, the absence of effective mechanisms for aligning heterogeneous data sources further hinders its applicability in practical settings.

In contrast, our approach not only emphasizes the optimization of deep learning models like BERT and ALBERT but also explores their potential for further enhancements, particularly in handling the complex task of extracting statement features from unstructured data related to relay protection device faults. By adopting dual neural networks for joint feature extraction, our method aims to construct a robust information extraction model capable of converting text statements into vectors effectively, ultimately facilitating the construction of a comprehensive relay protection device fault knowledge graph.

To summarize, while existing studies have made important contributions to the fields of equipment management and knowledge graph construction, they still face several limitations, including inadequate handling of unstructured data, insufficient semantic modeling capabilities, and limited adaptability to real-time industrial environments. These gaps highlight the need for a more sophisticated and domain-specific approach to information extraction and knowledge representation in the context of power system equipment management. Our proposed method addresses these shortcomings by integrating advanced natural language processing techniques with tailored model architectures designed for the unique demands of the power industry.

The research on the construction of relay protection device fault knowledge map in this paper is an important work in the field of equipment management of power grid enterprises, which has strong application and promotion value. Through scientific and effective methods of relay protection device fault management, the management cornerstone is firmly laid, making the management more standardized and intelligent. It improves the core competitiveness of power grid enterprises, ensures orderly development of enterprises; integrates intelligent technology, promotes intelligent operation of enterprises, further promotes the construction of a new power system with new energy as the main body, serves the goal of ‘double carbon’, and serves the new development pattern13.

Unstructured data analysis

During the operation of the equipment the system generates a large number of unstructured defective output files. These files do not have a consistent structure, cannot be represented in a unified way, and cannot be stored in a database. In practical applications, people need to read, retrieve, modify and update unstructured information, which wastes a lot of manpower and leads to extremely low efficiency in transaction processing. There is an urgent need to convert unstructured into the form of structured data as a means of completing the integration of heterogeneous data sources, realizing data consistency, and eliminating the heterogeneity of data. It is necessary to process and integrate the dispersed data in heterogeneous data sources to shield the logical and physical differences between various original data, and ultimately complete the unified representation, storage and management of data18.

The unstructured data related to equipment defects mainly comes from the maintenance record text data generated by the daily maintenance of relay protection devices. Take a certain overhaul record as an example, the content is as follows: “On 4 December 2019, the Electric Power Research Institute went to Binhai Company’s 110kV High-Tech Park line to carry out transformer cable terminal local discharge detection, the test found that there is a high-frequency suspected discharge signal in the cable terminal of phase A of the No. 2 main transformer, based on the existing test situation shows that, judging initially is caused by external factors of the retaining ring and conductor Poor contact, which is a general defect, and the subsequent discovery that the equipment was produced by Jiangsu Huapeng Transformer Co. on 1 August 2013 and put into operation on 5 March 2014.”

In order to extract the required entities, relationships from these unstructured data, natural language processing (NLP) technology can be used for processing. Currently information extraction methods are divided into three main categories: rule templates, machine learning and deep learning.

Information extraction based on rule templates

Match the text to be recognized with the rule template constructed through a priori knowledge, so as to complete the information extraction. And with the improvement of the semantic feature template of the expressed text, its extraction effect will be better than other algorithms.

However, this approach relies heavily on language, domain knowledge, etc., which makes it difficult to write rule templates and less applicable, resulting in the need to build different rule templates for different domains. All in all, this method has high labour cost, the extraction effect depends on whether the rule template is well written or not, and the generality is weak due to its strong targeting.

Machine learning based information extraction

Machine learning information extraction algorithms include HMM model, MEMM model, and CRF model. Among them, the HMM model is suitable for predicting the state process of the system, which can predict the annotation results of the subsequent text according to the current text sequence annotation, so as to complete the text information extraction. However, it should be noted that there are two precondition assumptions:i.The current state of the Hidden Markov Chain is only related to the previous state;ii.The current observation of the Hidden Markov Chain is only related to the current state.

The MEMM model, on the other hand, uses a discriminative model to build classifiers for each momentary state, and eventually multiplies all classifier probability values together. Since MEMM only considers obtaining the local optimal solution, it cannot obtain the global optimal solution. Therefore, although the model solves the HMM output independence assumption, there is a labeling bias problem.

The CRF model completes the data annotation by calculating the joint probability. Although CRF solves the problem of annotation bias in MEMM, and can make use of contextual text features to make the current moment output affect the previous moment output, but also due to the introduction of too many features to make the CRF model become more complex, reducing the training efficiency.

Deep learning based information extraction

As the development of deep learning technology becomes more and more mature, experts and scholars have found that it can achieve better results in the field of natural language processing, and therefore it is gradually being used in a variety of types of text tasks, such as sentiment analysis, machine translation and entity extraction19.

Recurrent neural networks are the earlier proposed and most used. However, as experts and scholars continue to deepen their research, it is found that recurrent neural networks have the problem of gradient disappearance or gradient explosion in the process of practical application. In order to solve the above problems, the long and short-term memory neural network uses ‘gates’ to control the memory information on the basis of recurrent neural network. Although this neural network solves the gradient disappearance or gradient explosion problem of recurrent neural networks, it still cannot solve the problem that recurrent neural networks cannot carry out parallel computation, which makes the model computationally intensive and time-consuming when it needs to deal with the task of large data volume.

Later, with the proposal of Attention, researchers found that compared with recurrent neural networks and their improved models, Attention-based neural networks can save time and space costs by selectively focusing on part of the information, thus reducing the computation amount of the model. Therefore, more and more scholars and enterprises began to study and apply Attention-based neural networks, taking Google’s Bert model (based on Attention) as an example, through experiments, it was found that in the field of natural language processing, the Bert model achieved very good results in 11 tasks.

The emergence and rise of deep learning can have better generalization while solving the problem that traditional machine learning cannot handle long sequences of text. This is because deep learning models contain embedded layers that can convert text in the form of word vectors into word vector matrices, which is helpful for subsequent calculations, and the models are also able to adjust the network structure and parameters in order to achieve better experimental results.

Through the research and analysis of the three information extraction techniques mentioned above, this paper will use the deep learning model for text data information extraction, and the main extraction object is the entity in the text.

CRF constraint layer

The CRF constraint layer is used to adjust the text label sequence by calculating the joint probability, which can avoid sequence dependency and thus get the optimal result. In selecting parameters for CRF joint probability calculations, it is crucial to consider the transition features and their associated weights, which define the relationships between labels and help ensure that the predicted sequences are coherent. The learning rate should be carefully chosen, often starting around 0.001, and adjusted based on training performance to optimize convergence. Dropout rates can also be applied to further enhance generalization, commonly set within the range of 0.2 to 0.5. These parameters need to be fine-tuned to fit the specific characteristics of the dataset and task requirements. Intellectual reasoning with textual information

Knowledge fusion mainly involves conflict detection and consistency checking of the acquired data to verify the accuracy of the knowledge and eliminate duplicate and fallacious concepts. On this basis, correspondence association and merging operations are performed on the verified error-free knowledge. The process mainly includes two aspects: knowledge integration and entity mapping. Knowledge integration refers to combining the existing knowledge base with other sources such as third-party knowledge base or structured information; entity mapping refers to the process of identifying and associating entities in natural language text to the original entities in the knowledge base, which is specifically manifested in the elimination of entity disambiguation and the resolution of the co-reference problem. Deep learning models have become the dominant approach for extracting information from textual data due to their strong capability in automatically capturing semantic and syntactic features. Unlike traditional methods that rely heavily on manually designed features and domain-specific rules, deep learning models can learn high-level representations directly from raw text, significantly reducing the need for feature engineering and improving generalization across different domains and languages.

Moreover, these models excel at handling complex linguistic structures and contextual dependencies, which are crucial for tasks like named entity recognition, relation extraction, and entity disambiguation—key components of knowledge extraction and mapping. With architectures such as recurrent neural networks (RNNs), convolutional neural networks (CNNs), and especially transformer-based models like BERT, it becomes possible to model long-range dependencies and dynamically adjust word representations based on context, thereby enhancing the precision and robustness of extracted knowledge. After effectively solving the knowledge conflict problem, knowledge storage and visualization can be carried out.

Knowledge reasoning process refers to the initial construction of the knowledge graph, the use of known entities and relationships carried by the information, through the corresponding algorithms to deduce the entities or relationships not yet included in the knowledge graph, which is essentially the process of deducing the performance of unknown or new knowledge on the basis of existing knowledge. Its core focus is on the prediction of triples, and the types of reasoning can be broadly classified into simple structural reasoning and complex structural reasoning.

Simple structural reasoning treats the triples in the knowledge graph as isolated knowledge units, focusing on modeling the structure of individual triples itself, without considering the network connectivity they form through relationships. In this type of reasoning, each triad is treated as an independent fact, and the basis of its reasoning is limited to the internal information of the triad. For simple structural reasoning, based on the design principle of the objective function, the models can be classified into the categories of semantic matching models, transformation models, and neural network models, etc., which mainly aim at how to induce the head entities, relations, and tail entities to interact with each other in order to form a reasonable statement of fact.

Since single-structure reasoning ignores the connections between entities through relationships, it fails to make full use of the intrinsic structural information of the knowledge graph at the input level of the model, which means that even if the model is subsequently optimized with more sophisticated processing, it may still be difficult to make up for the lack of information brought about by ignoring the overall relevance of the model. In contrast, the complex structural reasoning approach effectively meets this challenge by exploring the graphical structure of the knowledge graph at different levels, and then subdividing it into two categories: local subgraph structural reasoning models and global path structural reasoning models. The advantage of this type of model is that it can fully consider and utilize the complex connections between entities to achieve more comprehensive and accurate knowledge reasoning.

The k-step neighbour query is a good measure of graph traversal performance, traversing all points at the starting k steps. In order to make the speed of graph traversal and the output time more obvious, the results only take as output the size of the set of k-step neighbours.

A weakly connected subgraph is the largest set of vertices and their connected edges that can touch each other. Unlike the k no-neighbour query method, the query for weakly connected subgraphs requires traversing every vertex and every edge. First, an unlabeled point is found, a breadth-first search is performed using it as a vertex and a new weakly-connected subgraph ID is assigned to it, and all reachable points are labeled using the same ID; second, each vertex is used as a starting point and is expanded from each vertex to find the weakly-connected subgraph in a parallel manner.

For k-step queries, experiments were repeated with k values ranging from 1 to 5, ensuring coverage of short and long-range traversal scenarios. For weakly connected subgraph queries, the evaluation focused on execution time and scalability across different graph sizes, with emphasis on identifying bottlenecks and optimization opportunities. Parallel processing capabilities were leveraged to expedite the computation, demonstrating significant improvements in efficiency when compared to sequential approaches. All implementations were coded in Python, utilizing the NetworkX library for graph operations and TensorFlow for any necessary GPU acceleration.

The scalability of the response time is also an indispensable criterion for the graph traversal performance. It can be seen that the constructed model has a positive query response time.

This validation analysis is based on the constructed knowledge graph and adopts the sliding window mechanism in order to test the input effect of the input layer of the fault diagnosis model. Sliding window plays an important role in computer network communication as a commonly used network traffic control tool. Based on the sequence of data to be transmitted, a pre-set window capacity is used as a limiting condition to allow the sender to send only the number of data blocks that do not exceed the current window size, starting from the starting position of the data sequence. Once the first data block has been sent, the window moves forward, sequentially, until all the data has been successfully sent. Conclusion

By understanding the characteristics of the power grid industry, analyzing the types of faults of relay protection devices in different states, and targeting the problems of the current fault knowledge map technology for relay protection devices, we have constructed and optimized the knowledge map of relay protection devices by researching the methods of information extraction and deep learning, so as to make the fault management of the equipment more adaptable to the practical needs23.

Firstly, this paper describes the research background and current status of knowledge graph technology, discusses the current problems in the grid industry regarding the application of knowledge graph technology, summarizes the classification of maintenance record text data generated by daily maintenance of relay protection devices, and forms the equipment fault ontology relationship architecture diagram. Secondly, by studying the different processing methods for unstructured data generated from the text data of maintenance records produced by daily maintenance of relay protection devices, a suitable and efficient deep learning model is selected for text data information extraction. Finally, the experiment of constructing the fault knowledge graph of relay protection device was simulated and designed, with the construction of knowledge graph as the ultimate goal, the test effect of information annotation was recorded, the deep learning algorithm was used to construct the information extraction model, and then the model training was carried out, and finally, the results were analyzed and optimized. This experiment, which has the characteristics of strong relevance and high practicality in the maintenance record text data of the relay protection device, plays a key role in the research on the construction of the knowledge graph of relay protection device faults. However, the research work in this paper has the limitations of a small range of scenarios and little processing data, and needs to do more in-depth research on the combined application of knowledge mapping and relay protection devices.

With the advent of the digital information era characterized by digitization, networking and intelligence, the safety performance of relay protection devices has been increasingly improved, and the fault management of relay protection devices is particularly important. The construction of knowledge mapping of relay protection device faults not only has strong application and promotion value, but also can better face the upcoming challenges, improve the core competitiveness of the power network enterprises, and serve the ‘dual carbon’ and ‘dual carbon’ industries.
