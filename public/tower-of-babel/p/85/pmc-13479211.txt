Correction: Blockade of interleukin 10 potentiates antitumour immune function in human colorectal cancer liver metastases
Published: 2026

Sullivan KM, Jiang X, Guha P, et al. Blockade of interleukin 10 potentiates antitumour immune function in human colorectal cancer liver metastases. Gut 2023;72:325-337.

Following publication of this article on additional review of prior data, the following errors were noted in figure 5 and in the Results section:

In figure 5A, the images used are from the wrong experimental conditions. The images have been updated to a confirmed specimen used in the included experiments.

For the experimental condition control CAR-T and anti-IL-10 antibody, two of the experimental conditions are unavailable and rather than a sample size of 4, the sample size is n=2 for this condition.
