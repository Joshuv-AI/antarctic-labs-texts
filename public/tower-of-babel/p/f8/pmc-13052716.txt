eGastroenterology: a new chapter with exciting times ahead!
Authors: Emad M El-Omar, Guoyue Lyu
Published: 2026

In the nascent life of a scientific journal, 3 years may seem like a short period, but, for the eGastroenterology family, this represents a remarkable journey of vision, perseverance and collective achievement. From our inaugural issue to our present standing as a fully indexed journal, we have crossed critical milestones that affirm our purpose, dedication and potential. In January this year, the senior leadership of eGastroenterology has changed with Emad M El-Omar joining Guoyue Lyu as co-Editors in Chief. Emad M El-Omar, who is the former Editor in Chief of Gut and current Senior Advisor to Gut Portfolio Journals, is tremendously proud and excited to share the leadership of this amazing journal with Guoyue Lyu. In so doing, both recognise the visionary leadership and wisdom of the departing Editors in Chief, Professors Ericzon and Harmon. Their dedication and stewardship of eGastroenterology have ensured the success we enjoy today. As we stand on the threshold of receiving our first impact factor, anticipated in June 2026, we do so as the beginning of a New Chapter.

Over the past 3 years, we have built more than a publication platform; we have cultivated and nurtured a community. Authors, reviewers, editors and readers have come together with a shared commitment to advancing the science and practice of gastroenterology and allied specialties. We have prioritised scientific rigour, embraced innovation and championed interdisciplinary thinking. In due course, our true impact will be measured by the influence of the work we publish on clinical care, translational discovery and global health.

As we look forward, the question before us is not merely how to sustain success, but how to redefine excellence in gastroenterology publishing. The field itself is evolving at unprecedented speed, driven by advances in microbiome science, precision medicine, artificial intelligence (AI), multi-omics and systems biology. The diseases we study are increasingly complex, intersecting with metabolic, environmental and societal factors. To remain at the forefront, our journal must not only reflect these changes but actively shape them.

A vision for the next phase: The new chapter

Our strategic vision is grounded in a simple but powerful principle: to become the home of the most influential and forward-looking work in gastroenterology and hepatology worldwide.

To achieve this, we will focus on four key pillars.

Attracting transformative original research

Outstanding journals are defined by the originality, depth and impact of their primary research. We aim to position ourselves as the destination for studies that challenge paradigms and open new frontiers.

Large-scale, well-powered clinical trials with global relevance.

High-resolution omics studies that illuminate disease pathways.

Innovative technologies, including AI-driven diagnostics and therapeutics.

Research that addresses unmet needs in underserved populations.

To support this ambition, we are enhancing our editorial processes to ensure rapid yet rigorous peer review, with clear decision-making and constructive feedback. Time to first decision and time to publication are not merely operational metrics. They are signals of respect for authors’ work and urgency for scientific dissemination.

We will also introduce ‘fast-track pathways’ for high-impact submissions, particularly those with immediate clinical or public health implications. The new editorial family are experts in such pathways having championed their use in world-class journals.

Commissioning state-of-the-art reviews that define the field

In an era of information overload, authoritative synthesis is as valuable as discovery. We are committed to curating reviews that do more than summarise. They must interpret, integrate and inspire. They must provide a roadmap addressing key unanswered questions.

Our editorial board will proactively commission:

Cutting-edge reviews on rapidly evolving areas such as microbiome-host interactions, immunotherapy in gastrointestinal cancers and metabolic liver disease.

Translational roadmaps that connect bench discoveries to bedside applications.

Consensus and guideline-oriented articles that inform clinical practice globally.

Cross-disciplinary perspectives that bring insights from immunology, oncology, bioengineering and data science into gastroenterology.

We will prioritise authors who are not only leaders in their fields but also visionary thinkers capable of articulating where the science is heading next. We want to lead the way in asking the right questions and offering a template for how they could be answered.

Building a global and inclusive scientific community

Excellence in science is inherently global. Like all human diseases, the challenges of gastrointestinal diseases, whether inflammatory bowel disease, liver cirrhosis or gastrointestinal cancers, transcend geographical boundaries. The best and most impactful research is collaborative, geographically diverse, large scale with built-in validation and with a serious attempt to answer the research questions. This requires all of us to collaborate and work towards the same aim, namely, pooling our resources to provide progress for the whole world. eGastroenterology will champion this philosophy.

We will expand our international reach by:

Engaging emerging and under-represented research hubs across Asia, Africa, Latin America and the Middle East.

Encouraging submissions that reflect diverse patient populations and healthcare systems.

Supporting early-career investigators through mentorship initiatives, editorial fellowships and recognition awards.

Our goal is not only to publish global science, but to ensure that the journal itself reflects the diversity of the community it serves.

A partnership with our contributors

None of these ambitions can be realised without the trust and collaboration of our contributors. To our authors: we invite you to bring us your most ambitious work, the studies that you believe will define your field. We are committed to providing a fair, efficient and intellectually engaged review process that strengthens your research and amplifies its impact.

To our reviewers: your expertise is the backbone of scientific integrity. We will continue to recognise and support your contributions, ensuring that peer review remains both rigorous and rewarding.

To our readers: your engagement drives our purpose. We will strive to deliver content that informs, challenges and inspires, content that ultimately improves patient care and advances human health.
