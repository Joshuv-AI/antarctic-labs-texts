Author statement:

In Fig. 7T of the originally published article, the image of the striatum from the control mouse was identical to the one in Fig. 8N. This was an error. We have corrected it by replacing the duplicate image with the correct one.

In Fig. 9D of the originally published article, an incorrect region was inadvertently included in the inset due to a labeling error. This issue has been resolved by preparing a revised figure with the proper inset.

This correction does not affect the conclusions of the manuscript.

All authors agree to this author correction.
