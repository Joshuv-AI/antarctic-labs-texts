OA2050. Practical application of risk assessments in children’s bikes
Authors: A Kühnelt-Leddihn
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractIssue/problemWhen manufacturing bikes for children, it is important to ensure the safety of the young riders. Balance bikes for toddlers and small children require different measures than bicycles for younger teens. This requires the consideration of the developmental stage of children as well as the foreseeable use in a risk assessment.Description of the problemThe question remains: What is “forseeable use” in children’s products? Even if some risks can be identified, it is not always possible to prevent some of them entirely. Especially with products which should only be used in safe spaces, for example toys, it is important to inform the caregivers that they understand the importance of not overestimating their children’s abilities. If there are no technical solutions available, only user instructions remain as a mode to inform caregivers about the correct behavior. Yet manuals are often dismissed and the warnings are too long and not provided in an easily understandable language (as required in some standards). Furthermore, they can become pretty bulky as they need to be provided in all languages. Some manufacturers also provide instructions via video (especially assembly instructions). While videos are widely appreciated by customers, they are not considered sufficient by technical standards or national legal requirements. Even though they may address topics better than written text.Key messages• Risk analysis and risk assessment can be a challenge, especially when it comes to children’s products.TopicRisk assessment, children’s product, product safety
