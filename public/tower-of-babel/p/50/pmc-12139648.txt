Attending Art is Not Medicine
Authors: Christos Voucharas, Anysia Koukouthaki
Published: 2024

Sir,

We must commend the genius behind the recent publication by Dr. Rachel Hajar that has seamlessly intertwined the realms of art and medicine, bringing forth a level of understanding that was previously elusive (Hajar R. Is art medicine? Heart Views 2023; 24:229). We extend our compliments to the author for shedding light on this profound correlation.

Nevertheless, it is crucial to distinguish between attending and acting when it comes to the appreciation of art. Engaging with art can serve as a form of education or entertainment. On the other hand, the act of performing, creating, or participating in art may have therapeutic qualities, akin to medication.

The question arises: Can everyone engage in artistic endeavors, especially those who are ill or handicapped, considering factors such as talent, experience, spare time, mood, means, and resources? The answer lies in improvization (with no rules, no right or wrong) coupled with the guidance of a proficient teacher, the one who provides encouragement and direction.
