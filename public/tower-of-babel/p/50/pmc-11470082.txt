Author Correction: Thymus algeriensis and Thymus fontanesii exert neuroprotective effect against chronic constriction injury-induced neuropathic pain in rats
Authors: Samar Rezq, Amira E. Alsemeh, Luigi D’Elia, Assem M. El‑Shazly, Daria Maria Monti, Mansour Sobeh, Mona F. Mahmoud
Published: 2024

Correction to: Scientific Reports10.1038/s41598-020-77424-0, published online 25 November 2020

The original Article contains errors. Due to an error during figure assembly, in Figure 7A the central section of panel c partially overlaps with the top part of panel f after rotation of the image. Multiple images have been acquired for each condition and an image from the “sham” group has been selected for panel f, instead of an image belonging to the “TF (p.o., 400 mg/kg). TA (p.o., 200 mg/kg)” condition.

A portion of panel b in Figure 7A overlaps with a portion of panel f in Figure 15 of a previously published article [1] from some of the same authors. The figures for both articles were prepared at the same time and due to a mistake during figure assembly a panel belonging to the group of “H. campechianum flower extract (p.o., 400 mg/kg)” from [1] has been included as panel b in Figure 7A of this Article.

In addition, in Figure 7A panel c displays the same image as panel c in Figure 15 of [1]. Both images represent the same experimental condition.
