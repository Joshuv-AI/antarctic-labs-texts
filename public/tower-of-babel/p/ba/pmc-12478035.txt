Correction for Berenbaum, Political and biological reality checks
Published: 2025

Correction for “Political and biological reality checks,” by May R. Berenbaum, which published July 9, 2025; 10.1073/pnas.2515272122 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 122, e2515272122).

The author appreciates receiving expert commentary on details about wheat vernalization and notes the text appearing at the end of the second paragraph on page one of the article should be updated to “To enable winter wheat planted later in the spring to flower at the proper time for a high crop yield, Lysenko promoted the practice of exposing imbibed (i.e., metabolically active) seeds to extended cold in late winter before the seeds were planted in the field. However, Lysenko argued that the changes induced by cold exposure that enabled flowering (a process known as vernalization) were heritable such that future generations of winter wheat would no longer require cold exposure for proper flowering—a hypothesis that experimental data refuted (3).”
