Correction: Mol Cancer 23 187 (2024)

Following publication of the original article [1], the authors noticed a minor but significant error in the representation of the author name Tarek Ben Ahmed as “Ben” was captured as given name instead of a family name. Thus, in the online version, the name Tarek Ben Ahmed was mistakenly listed as Ahmed TB instead of Ben Ahmed T. The original article has been corrected.
