Characterisation of the Novel HLA‐DPB1*1810:01 Allele by Short‐Read Next Generation Sequencing
Authors: Orla Ryan, Cara Hickey, Emma Brennan, Diarmaid Ó Donghaile, Brian Graham
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
ABSTRACT HLA‐DPB1*1810:01 differs from HLA‐DPB1*04:01:01:01 in exon 2 by one non‐synonymous change (CTG → CCG).Keywords: HLA‐DPB1*04:01:01:01 variant, HLA‐DPB1*1810:01, NGS, novel allele

Located within the human Major Histocompatibility Complex (MHC) on the short arm of chromosome 6p21.3, the HLA locus is the most gene dense and polymorphic region in the human genome with over 420 genes and 43,758 alleles described in the 7.6 Mb region. The significant diversity of alleles is due to amino acid substitutions in the peptide binding grooves. Polymorphism in HLA genes leads to non‐synonymous changes in amino acids indicating a strong selection pressure throughout evolution and the importance of HLA—peptide interactions [1].

Here we report a novel HLA‐DPB1 allele identified in an Irish donor recruited by the Irish Unrelated bone marrow registry (IUBMR).

In brief, genomic DNA was extracted from whole blood using the Qiagen EZ2 DNA Blood kit on the Qiagen EZ2 Connect automated platform (Qiagen, Alameda, CA) as per the manufacturer's protocol. Library preparation was performed using the One Lambda AllType FASTplex 11 Loci kit (One Lambda, USA) on the Illumina MiSeq instrument (Illumina, USA) typing the loci HLA‐A, ‐B, ‐C, ‐DRB1, ‐DRB3/4/5, ‐DQA1, ‐DQB1, ‐DPA1, ‐DPB1. HLA alleles were assigned using HLA typing software, One Lambda TypeStream Visual 3.0 (One Lambda, USA) with the IPD‐IMGT/HLA Database [2] (version 3.59 January 2025) as a reference sequence database. Sequencing was repeated to verify the novel position.

HLA‐DPB1*1810:01 differs from its closet allele DPB1*04:01:01:01 by one non‐synonymous change in exon 2, T > C, codon 66 (CTG → CCG) leading to an amino acid change from Leucine to Proline at residue 66 (Figure 1).

The HLA‐DPB1*1810:01 allele differs from HLA‐DPB1*04:01:01:01 by a single non‐synonymous nucleotide substitution in exon 2 (T > C) codon 66, corresponding to an amino acid change of Leucine to Proline at residue 66.

The complete HLA genotype for the individual with this novel allele was: HLA‐A*01:01:01, 11:01:01, ‐B*08:01:01, 14:01:01, ‐C*07:01:01, 08:02:01, ‐DRB1*03:01:01, 07:01:01, ‐DRB3*01:01:02, ‐DRB4*01:03:01N, ‐DQA1*02:01:01, 05:01:01, ‐DQB1*02:01:01, 03:03:02, ‐DPA1*01:03:01, ‐DPB1*03:GFNJ, 1810:01.

The nucleotide sequence of the novel HLA‐DPB1 allele was submitted to GenBank (accession number PV797331) and IPD‐IMGT/HLA Database (accession number HLA43661). The name HLA‐DPB1*1810:01 has been officially assigned by the World Health Organisation (WHO) Nomenclature Committee for Factors of the HLA System in July 2025 and released as part of the IPD‐IMGT/HLA Database, version 3.62. The novel allele was published in the WHO Nomenclature report for July, August and September 2025 [3].
