Retraction Note: Evaluation of ovarian function and metabolic factors in women affected by polycystic ovary syndrome after treatment with d-Chiro-Inositol
Authors: Antonio Simone Laganà, Luisa Barbaro, Alfonsa Pizzo
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: Arch Gynecol Obstet (2015) 291:1181–1186 10.1007/s00404-014-3552-6

The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article. Following publication, concerns were raised regarding similarities in the data reported in this article and another article by the same authors [1], specifically that some of the values in Table 1 for the pre- and post-treatment d-Chiro-inositol groups appear to be highly similar. In addition, further concerns have been raised that the statistics reported in Table 1 appear incorrect. The authors have not provide a satisfactory response to the concerns raised or supplied their raw data. The Editor-in-Chief has therefore lost confidence in the results and conclusions of this study. The authors did not respond to correspondence from the Editor about this retraction.
