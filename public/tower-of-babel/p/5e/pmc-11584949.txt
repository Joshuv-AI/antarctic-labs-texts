Digital technology and Chinese-style industrial modernization: Dynamic threshold effect based on R&D Human resources
Authors: Yingying Ding, Xiaojing Song, Yue Zhu, Ruichao Xi, Ziyi Shi
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractBased on the construction of digital technology evaluation index system, this paper builds a dynamic threshold regression model to explore the complex impact of digital technology on Chinese-style industrial modernization under the threshold of R&D human resources, taking 30 provinces as research objects. It has been found that China's digital technology index is continuously improving, but there is a digital gap among regions. The beneficial impact of digital technology on Chinese-style industrial modernization has been thoroughly validated. Considering the threshold effect of R&D human resources, the influence of digital technology on Chinese-style industrial modernization exhibits nonlinear characteristics. With R&D human resources crossing the first threshold, it has shown a significant positive effect on Chinese-style industrial modernization, and the middle range of R&D human resources presents the optimal interval of the relationship between the two. The research findings offer a theoretical framework for advancing the construction of Chinese-style modernization.Keywords: Digital technology, Chinese-style industrial modernization, R&D human resources, Dynamic threshold effect

Introduction

Given the swiftly changing global environment and the significant hurdles in domestic reform, development, and stability, the 20th National Congress of the Communist Party of China emphasizes the importance of focusing on the tangible economy, promoting new industrialization, and hastening the establishment of a robust manufacturing sector. General Secretary Xi Jinping has emphasized time and again that “we must enhance the digital economy bigger and stronger, implement the new development concept, encourage the thorough integration of the Internet, big data, artificial intelligence, and the real economy, and establish a modern economic system" [1]. During the shift from conventional to contemporary industry, digital technology has been an indispensable catalyst for the Chinese-style industrial modernization. The rapid and extensive integration of digital technologies, including intelligent manufacturing, industrial Internet, big data analysis, and artificial intelligence applications, is permeating various sectors of the Chinese industry, leading to a significant industrial revolution.

The burgeoning digital technology is progressively driving the reconfiguration of the human socio-economic structure, transforming production relations, and evolving social production methods. It is emerging as a novel catalyst for economic growth [2]. Under the environment of the Fourth Industrial Revolution, digital twins and artificial intelligence have emerged in large numbers to empower Chinese-style industrial modernization. Adopting the digital economy is a strategic decision aimed at seizing the emerging opportunities presented by the latest phase of scientific and technological advancements and industrial restructuring. This shift is favorable to the establishment of a new development pattern and a contemporary economic system. The convergence of the digital economy and the real economy has been instrumental in advancing the modernization of Chinese industry [3]. The distinctive attributes of digital technology, including disruptive technological innovation, information transparency, and knowledge intensity, have the potential to foster a conducive technological environment for industrial advancement [4]. Hence, it is necessary to promote digital technology applications that will become critical measures in Chinese-style industrial modernization under new developmental patterns. Given the current global economy slowdown and escalating international competition, it is crucial to capitalize on the significant advantages presented by digital transformation to inject continuous energy into Chinese-style industrial modernization [5], thereby overcoming challenges related to sluggish quality enhancement resulting from scale expansion. The extensive utilization of digital technology in this process may emerge as the new driving force for advancing Chinese-style industrial modernization. Consequently, a pertinent query arises as to whether digital technology has effectively facilitated the modernization of the Chinese-style industry. If so, considering the characteristics of digital technology and R&D human resources (The following is replaced by Human), along with heterogeneous issues among different regions, does digital technology demonstrate distinct rules or characteristics when influencing the Chinese-style industrial modernization in different regions? By thoroughly examining how digital technologies' influence on Chinese-style industrial modernization, and clarifying what roles are played by Human heterogeneity during this process, suitable development policies can be formulated accordingly which will have both theoretical values and practical relevance when it comes to enhancing China's manufacturing strength and international competitiveness as well as constructing a socialist modernized powerful nation.

Literature review

Relevant concept and research on digital technology

Within the digital economy, digital technology plays a crucial role [6]. With the current information network serving as an essential carrier, it has become an essential component of manufacturing in the era of the digital economy to convert all types of information into binary digits that electronic computers can recognize for efficient processing. Digitalization constitutes a broad and multi-level development trend, which significantly improves the reliability, effectiveness and breadth of information dissemination [7]. Its overall goal is to enable users to more fully and effectively share the benefits of economic development. The research on digital technology encompasses a diverse array of fields, such as artificial intelligence, big data, cloud computing, the Internet of Things, blockchain, and 5G communications, among others. The new-generation information technology revolution, driven primarily by digital technology, is advancing global transformations in production methods and profoundly altering various aspects of society [8]. It is propelling comprehensive innovation and transformation across industries, the economy, society, and culture. Traditional industries may be transformed and improved as a result of the deep integration of digital technology with the actual economy [9]. This integration can also result in the emergence of new industries, business forms, and new models [10], bolstering the new economic development engine and fostering the convergence of informatization and industrialization. These strategies are crucial for China in achieving the transition to a new economic development paradigm [11].

Research on the concept and influencing factors of Chinese-style industrial modernization

The concept of “Chinese-Style Modernization" denotes a form of socialist modernization that is directed by the CPC. Its specific connotation first appeared in the 20th National Congress of the CPC and is mainly related to five aspects–population size, the modernization of a vast population, the modernization of shared prosperity for all the people, the modernization of harmony between material and spiritual civilization, the modernization of harmonious coexistence between man and nature, and the modernization of peaceful development. In consideration of this matter, academics have suggested the idea of narrowing the disparity in advancement within the digital economy, building an advanced industrial system, promoting shared prosperity, and advancing the enhancement of the digital innovation system will help to provide endogenous power for economic development and infusing boundless energy into the efforts to foster high-quality development and the Chinese path to modernization [12,13]. Industrial modernization encompasses a multifaceted process involving innovation, selection, and dissemination of industrial components integrated within the industrialization process. It also involves the establishment, development and evolution of modern industry [14]. Throughout the evolution of the global economic superpowers, industry plays an important supporting role. Therefore, promoting Chinese-style industrial modernization is the fundamental prerequisite and material basis for realizing Chinese-style modernization [14]. Cao (2018) proposed that within the contemporary framework of advancing economic growth through digital technology development, it is imperative to thoroughly understand the fundamental trends of industrial modernization with Chinese characteristics, enhance the establishment of an industrial information security assurance system, strengthen the backing of talent teams [15], and it is imperative to emphasize the coordinated advancement of the industry and digital economy and to motivate the government, enterprises and other stakeholders to jointly promote China's industrial modernization by the central directives [16].

Relevant research on the influence of Human on digital technology

Human pertains to the labor force with professional expertise in science and technology. By accumulating knowledge, skills, and real-world experience, they may advance and foster scientific and technological innovation [17]. With the advancement of the Internet + and big data technology, modern enterprise human resource management is undergoing an innovative change, which has influenced the concept and method of human resource management [18] and has a profound impact on the research of Human.

The reserve of Human is the essential condition for promoting the progress of digital technology, and scholars believe that Human are essential to the creation, dissemination, and application of digital technology [19]. On the one hand, the expertise and innovative thinking of technological talents have spurred rapid advancements in subjects like artificial intelligence, computer science, and big data [20], thereby powering digital transformation. On the other hand, the rate and effectiveness of digital technology innovation are directly impacted by the quantity and caliber of such innovations [21]. High-quality technological talents design smarter and more sophisticated hardware and software, thereby enhancing the performance and scope of digital technology applications.

Cultivating and attracting Human is the focus of governments and enterprises in developing new technologies. At the governmental level, there is a necessity for innovation and reform within the education system, support for scientific research projects and skills training, and strengthening the introduction and guarantee of talent, to cultivate a new generation of scientific and technological talents with innovative capabilities [22]; At the enterprise level, to retain technological talents and ignite their innovative potential, it is imperative to offer incentives and foster an open and collaborative work environment, which can ensure the synchronization between the career advancement of talents and the innovation and development of enterprises [23]. In addition, the distribution and flow of Human also affect regional innovation and development [24], and innovation clusters often form in regions where talents are concentrated [25], such as Silicon Valley and Shenzhen in China. The high density of Human can encourage the continuous emergence of innovation and the rapid dissemination of knowledge, which has significantly promoted the development of regional digitalization.

Literature summary and formation of a research framework

Industry is the foundation of the country, and industrialization is the top priority of national modernization. At present, the advancement of digital technology has opened up new chances for the progression of Chinese-style industrialization, which has attracted extensive attention from the academic community. This paper mainly analyzes the correlation between the two entities through the exploration of three distinct perspectives. First, from the standpoint of resource allocation, the extensive incorporation of digital technology in the actual economy speeds up the optimization and reconstruction of the production factor system, enhances resource utilization efficiency, facilitates the progression of industrial structure, and fosters the modernization of Chinese-style industrialization [26]. Second, from the perspective of resource space spillover, on the one hand, the limitations of physical distance and administrative impediments on the flow of components are significantly reduced by digital technology. This facilitates the swift exchange and circulation of capital and workforce within a broader geographic area [27]; On the other hand, digital technology promotes the continuous deepening of exchanges and cooperation between entities in different regions, including technological exchanges between enterprises, business cooperation, and cross-regional interaction between enterprises and other innovation entities [28]. Digital technology promotes the cross-regional reorganization of elements, optimizes the allocation of elements in other regions, promotes technology integration and knowledge spillover, and promotes Chinese-style industrial modernization. Third, considering the impact of technology, digital technology, including the use of data production factors, as a new technology of production input, can improve the level of energy utilization efficiency of enterprises, innovate production technology and equipment [29], save resources [30], optimize enterprise workflow [31], improve production efficiency, and encourage enterprises to achieve green transformation [32], thus promoting Chinese-style industrial modernization.

In addition, environmental regulation (hereinafter referred to as Regulation), technology level (hereinafter referred to as Education), market size (hereinafter referred to as Market) and technology market maturity (hereinafter referred to as Innovation) also have a certain impact on Chinese-style industrial modernization. Regulation is an important tool to promote industrial transformation, and it affects or changes the cost structure and resource allocation mode of enterprises, forcing enterprises to improve existing processes and resource allocation adjustments [33], and promotes Chinese-style industrial modernization. Education is the foundation of industrial transformation, and Education promotes China's industrial modernization by boosting technological progress and industrial structure transformation [34]. The Market provides a strong impetus for industrial modernization, and the large Market can stimulate technological innovation [35], improve product quality, and promote the realization of Chinese-style industrial modernization. As a market-oriented trading platform for scientific and technological achievements, Innovation serves a pivotal role in driving technological efficiency improvements and technological progress. The development of Innovation can facilitate the transformation and enhancement of industrial structures by guiding and supporting regional independent innovation capabilities [36], thereby fostering Chinese-style industrial modernization.

For the transition economies under the new development pattern, the digital technology gap is noticeable, region heterogeneity is significant, and digital technology impact on Chinese-style industrial modernization may not be a single dimension. Digital technology has typical knowledge-intensive and technology-intensive characteristics. When the technical conditions are advanced and the level of Human is high, its role may be more prominent. Moreover, the improvement of digital technology is a dynamic change process, which will produce scale effects with changes in external conditions such as digital infrastructure, the input of digital elements, and inclusive financial index. At the same time, model (1) implies the homogeneity assumption, that is, the assumption that the influence of digital technology on regional Chinese-style industrial modernization is the same regardless of region and period, that is, each additional unit of Digitalit will increase Modernit by α units. To look at the non-linear correlation between digital technology and regional Chinese-style industrial modernization, this paper employs a threshold regression model for empirical analysis.

Hansen initially introduced the concept of the panel threshold regression model [37], which incorporates threshold variables to identify the point of structural transition and subsequently utilizes observations to estimate the actual threshold, to address the structural changes of nonlinear problems more objectively and accurately. The model is widely used due to its ability to determine the “critical value" depending on the attributes of the sample data, thereby mitigating the potential bias of subjective categorization and cross-term estimation. Nevertheless, this threshold method is only suitable for the non-dynamic panel model, as it does not account for the dynamic evolution or hysteresis impact of the sample entity, and neglects the handling of endogenous variables. Since the evolution of digital technology is characterized by a continuous and dynamic process of transformation, to examine its dynamic heterogeneous effect on Chinese-style industrial modernization, this paper utilizes an enhanced dynamic panel threshold regression approach that incorporates the lagged term of the dependent variable. It is used to govern the continuity, inertia, and model endogeneity of the Chinese industrial modernization process, and it includes dynamic factors based on dynamic panel estimation methods. In addition, the threshold value was determined using the Hansen model, and the whole sample was divided into different intervals. In this paper, the system generalized moment estimation (System GMM) estimation approach proposed in the literature was applied to dynamically estimate the slope coefficient between the partitions [38]. The system GMM estimation integrates Diff-GMM and horizontal GMM, effectively addressing endogeneity issues and capturing the early dependence and dynamic evolution features of Chinese industrial modernization. Indicator description and data processing

Due to limited data availability and reliability, this paper opted to exclude regions with insufficient data, such as Tibet, Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan. Ultimately, a total of 30 provinces and cities were chosen as the research sample for analysis. Meanwhile, regarding the selection of the sample window period, this paper mainly considers are as follows: In 2012, four years after the 2008 financial crisis, China's macroeconomic development gradually recovered, and the stability of panel data was effectively controlled. At the same time, in 2012, China's digital economy commenced its emergence as the number of Internet users surpassed 500 million and the Internet penetration rate reached 38.3 %, marking that the digital economy's evolution into a new economic paradigm that propels China's economic growth following the information economy era. Therefore, this paper chooses 2012–2021 as the study sample period. Every original data item was obtained from the National Bureau of Statistics of China's official website. To make the data statistically computable, we have carried out necessary standardization, inventory, or reduction of the original data.

Digital technology index analysis

According to the RAGA-PP, this paper measures the digital technical indicators of 30 inland provinces in China (excluding Tibet) from 2012 to 2021. Simultaneously, the digital technology index for each region is charted across three time periods: 2012, 2017, and 2021, with a focus on observing the spatiotemporal evolution of the transformation dynamics. Digital technology index by region.

In general, with the continuous promotion of the digital China strategy, up to July 2022, China e has made significant advancements in its digital infrastructure, evidenced by the extensive network of optical cable lines spanning 54.81 million kilometers, 1.968 million 5G base stations are currently in use, and the presence of 153 national green data centers across the country. Concurrently, the digital industry's capacity for innovation has increased, resulting in an acceleration of the digital revolution of industry. It is becoming more and more clear what benefits the digital economy offers, thereby facilitating the ongoing enhancement of digital technology metrics.

During the sample period, digital technology in all regions of China showed an increasing trend year, but there were also significant spatiotemporal heterogeneity and disequilibrium. The nation's general state of digital technology advancement exhibits spatial distribution patterns that align with the “leading in the east", phenomenon identified by Yang et al. [43]. Numerous provinces and cities with advanced digital technology are situated along the shore. These regions exhibit a high level of economic development, early and extensive opening hours, a well-developed high-tech industry, a concentration of talent, a solid digital infrastructure, and a prevalence of robust enterprises and industrial parks. These areas not only have enough attraction for scientific and technological talents but also have a good scientific and technological foundation and policy and technological exchange conditions, which makes the level of digital technology higher. The majority of provinces exhibiting low levels of digital development are situated in inland regions. For example, Qinghai and Ningxia are situated in the west of the Chinese Mainland, with relatively backward transportation and economic conditions. The infrastructure, technology, and application of digital technology are relatively weak, and they are less attractive to talents. However, Hainan is situated in coastal areas, with late development, backward infrastructure such as digital technology and education, and fewer universities and scientific research institutes. Moreover, there exists a significant disparity in the degree of advancement across the cities of Hainan, and the development focus is mainly in Haikou and Sanya. Hence, the overall development is relatively backward.

Leveraging the advantages of advancements in the digital era, Jiangxi is actively advancing digital technology, overcoming challenges in regional development, and establishing new poles of growth in the digital economy. Over the past five years, Jiangxi province has experienced rapid economic growth, with a growth rate of 0.21 and 10.8 % (See Fig. 2). Jiangxi's growth rate in the first three-quarters of 2022 places it among the top three provinces in China. Furthermore, its economic aggregate has ascended to the 15th position in China. The reason for Jiangxi's rapid development is closely related to the advancement of digital technology. Firstly, Jiangxi implements the “No.1 Development Project" to vigorously promote the excellence and strength of the digital economy. Relevant government departments reduce taxes and fees for digital economy enterprises, set up special funds, reduce basic costs, and implement logistics support for high-level digital talents to optimize the development environment for digital skills. An excellent digital technology development environment attracts more high-quality enterprises and digital talents to settle in Jiangxi. It effectively promotes the advancement of digital enterprises and enterprise digital transformation and improves the digital technology development index of Jiangxi. Secondly, Jiangxi actively seeks growth space and transforms the “Jiangxi economic Circle" economic development status into a development advantage that can move north, south, east, and west. Jiangxi is conveniently located to learn from the experience of digital technology and industrial transformation and upgrading from neighboring cities. Meanwhile, Ganzhou has become one of the most economically powerful cities in the four surrounding provinces to undertake the industrial transfer of neighboring provinces. Thirdly, Jiangxi boasts a rich endowment of lithium minerals. Through the tripartite collaboration of the national “carbon peak and carbon neutralization" policy, local governments, and scientific research institutions, Jiangxi's lithium new energy industry has entered a golden period of opportunity, establishing it as one of the provinces with the most comprehensive lithium new energy industry chains in China. Nowadays, Jiangxi has convenient transportation and supportive policies and attaches great importance to scientific research. Emerging industries are gradually formed, talent return is accelerated, digital technology development level is constantly improved, and the economy is more developed.

This paper focuses on analyzing the advancements in digital technology over recent years by examining three specific time points: 2012, 2017, and 2021. The progression of digital technology has exhibited a steady enhancement across diverse regions, with a noticeable trend of faster development in western regions in contrast to eastern regions. This observation implies that the effective execution of national strategies is diminishing the gap in digital technology. Digital technology might be very important for development, according to the report of the CPC's 20th National Congress. It is a novel impetus for Chinese-style modernization. Due to the eastern region's superior digital technologies, the technology level in the three-time sections is significantly higher than in other regions. Among them, Guangdong Province has maintained a good advantage and continued to take the lead. Despite its weak foundation and late start, the western region has demonstrated a remarkable and consistent growth rate in recent years, surpassing that of other regions. Sichuan and other developed areas in the west and center may take advantage of national policies to enhance innovation and application of digital technology, thereby attaining a competitive edge in the digital realm.

Analysis of endogenous problems

This research draws its data from the National Bureau of Statistics' official statistics records. To the greatest extent feasible, steer clear of the endogeneity issues brought on by measurement mistakes, Regulation, Education, Market, and Innovation are added as control variables to solve the endogeneity problem of missing variables. Nevertheless, from the viewpoint of the subject of the study, there is a certain causal relationship between digital technology and Chinese-style industrial modernization. Additionally, due to the numerous factors influencing Chinese-style industrial modernization, there may be issues with missing variables, which makes it challenging to include all factors in the regression model, considering the model's overall robustness.

The Hausman test is employed in endogeneity testing to assess the presence of endogeneity issues. The P value of 0.000 suggests that the Digital indicator in the model has an endogeneity (see Table 5). Heterogeneity analysis

To support Chinese-style industrial modernization, the heterogeneity test is applied in this paper from two angles: regional location and innovation level.

(1)Regional heterogeneity. Given differences in resource availability, historical governmental strategies, and geographic positioning, the influence of digital technology on the industrial modernization process in China's eastern, central, and western regions may differ. Hence, this paper categorizes the samples into three distinct groups based on their geographical locations: eastern, central, and western, for subsequent analysis and discussion. The findings are presented in columns (1)–(3) of Table 11. In all three locations, digital technology has significantly benefited Chinese-style industrial modernization development. Still, according to the digital technology regression coefficient, the effect of digital technology on Chinese-style industrial modernization presents the characteristics of “east > central > west." The possible reason is that the eastern region is economically developed, and digital technology is at the forefront. Hence, the driving role of digital technology in Chinese-style industrial modernization is more significant. Compared to the more developed eastern area, the central and western regions of China have a weaker economic foundation, which has limited the influence of digital technology for Chinese-style industrial modernization.

Conclusion

Research conclusions

Digital technology based on the high-tech level plays a significant part in developing Chinese-style industrial modernization This paper utilizes data from 30 provinces in China spanning the years 2012–2021, takes Human as the threshold variable, constructs a dynamic threshold model, carries out the spatiotemporal heterogeneity test, and empirically examines the complex impact of digital technology on Chinese-style industrial modernization.

Firstly, the paper found that the digital technology gap is obvious, and there are significant spatiotemporal heterogeneity characteristics. The dominance of digital technology is predominantly observed in the eastern regions, whereas the growth is primarily prominent in the western regions.

Secondly, this paper found that under the threshold role of Human, the influence of digital technology on the level of Chinese-style industrialization is characterized by a dynamic heterogeneity threshold. When the level of Human breaks through the double threshold of 2.2539, the positive impact of digital technology on Chinese-style industrial modernization will strengthen; the level of Human has crossed the double threshold of 2.3039, and the beneficial effects of digital technology on Chinese-style industrial modernization has weakened.

Thirdly, the distribution of high Human, medium Human, and low Human resources in different regions of China shows a particular space-time evolution law. In terms of time, China's medium and high Human are on the rise, while low Human are on the decline, with some fluctuations. In terms of space, China is in medium and high Human provinces almost half. Due to the geographical and economic advantages, the medium and high Human provinces are mainly the eastern region.

Managerial Implication

Drawing from the aforementioned findings, this article offers the following managerial insights: regional balance in development, enhancing digital technology innovation capacity, and promoting the advancement of R&D human resources.

Firstly, we need to promote balanced regional development. Prior research indicated that China's digital technology development is characterized by a “leading in the East, lagging in the west" distribution. Given this, the government should intensify its investment in digital infrastructure and establish a development mechanism that leverages regional strengths to complement each other. This can be achieved through initiatives such as “East-West collaboration" to enhance digital technology cooperation and integration between different regions. Additionally, we encourage businesses across regions to intensify their exchanges and collaborations in technology, talent, and resources, thereby fostering mutual advancement and contributing to the setting up of a harmonious pattern of regional digital economic advancement.

Secondly, improving the ability of digital technology innovation, especially in areas with low levels of digital technology. Previous research has suggested that digital technology use has the potential to significantly speed up Chinese-style modernization. For the government, it is essential to enhance policy support, foster inclusive financial growth, boost investment in digital assets, foster the development of digital enterprises, and elevate digital technology standards. Furthermore, the government is obligated to intensify regulatory oversight, progressively enhance emission and quality monitoring standards, and facilitate a shift towards green transformation and industrial upgrading through taxation mechanisms. These measures will further regional industrial modernization in China. Enterprises are advised to enhance their investment in research and development of digital technologies and innovation to establish fresh competitive edges amidst the current wave of scientific and technological revolution and industrial transformation This strategic approach will facilitate the achievement of Chinese-style industrial modernization.

Thirdly, give significance to the level of Human development. The previous study found that when the Human is higher than the first threshold value, the promotion effect of digital technology on Chinese-style industrial modernization reaches a peak. The Chinese government ought to increase its emphasis on cultivating scientific and technological human resources and implement specific strategies, including but not limited to strengthening the infrastructure and capacity development of scientific research institutions, optimizing the talent training system, enhancing the innovation environment of industrial parks, thereby attracting and gathering high-level scientific and technological talents, and actively introducing outstanding external talents. Enterprises should raise their Human capital standards and strengthen the competitive advantage of talents from the two aspects of attracting and training. Enterprises should attract scientific and technological talents through technology or high salaries, cultivate and introduce talents with both management and high-tech so that enterprises can target the market demand, and formulate reasonable strategic planning based on their reality. Promote enterprise digital management, technology upgrading, green transformation, and industrial structure upgrading, improve the level of enterprise digital technology, and enhance the realization of Chinese-style industrial modernization.

Academic value

This paper delves into investigating the intricate process by which digital technology enables Chinese-style industrial modernization, given the heterogeneity of Human. The primary academic value is demonstrated in three dimensions:

Firstly, regarding research techniques, this paper improves the traditional static threshold regression model and makes use of the dynamic panel threshold regression method to look into the correlation between Human and digital technology and Chinese-style industrial modernization. This method not only tackles the endogenous issues but also effectively illustrates the early phases of Chinese-style industrial modernization, characterized by its reliance and dynamism.

Secondly, in terms of variable measurement, this paper improves the projection pursuit model using an accelerated genetic algorithm based on real number coding. It also conducts a more objective and scientific evaluation of the digital technology index of different Chinese regions by looking at it from four different angles: digital infrastructure, digital trading market, digital factor input, and digital industry scale. This contributes to boosting the validity of the research's outcomes.

Thirdly, in terms of research conclusions, based on the regional heterogeneity test, it has been determined that there are variations in digital technology and Human across different regions. Under different levels of Human, digital technology has different promotion of Chinese-style industrial modernization, which greatly enhances the comprehensiveness of the research and thus draws more realistic research conclusions.

Research limitations

Given the constraints of currently available and applicable statistics, this study primarily examines the macro level of the region over the sample period. As data continues to evolve, future research could consider a broader sample range. Furthermore, beyond a H uman perspective, other factors such as knowledge levels, the maturity of the technology market, and environmental regulations may be incorporated to delve deeper into and analyze the heterogeneous mechanisms influencing micro-level differences in enterprises, which will be the focus of future research.
