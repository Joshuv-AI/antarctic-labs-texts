Retraction Note: Forest pest monitoring and early warning using UAV remote sensing and computer vision techniques
Authors: Xiaoyu Li, AChuan Wang
Published: 2025

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-84464-3, published online 02 January 2025

The Editors have retracted this Article.

Following publication the Authors identified errors in the dataset, experimental protocol, and analyses.

The pine wilt disease dataset contained misclassified images between healthy and early infected trees, altitude and lighting conditions were not consistent across the dataset, and the test/training datasets split included duplicated images. These in turn introduced uncontrolled variability and artificially inflated performance metrics for the proposed soft-thresholding adaptive filtering module and cascaded group attention mechanism Real-time Detection Transformer (SC-RTDETR) framework.

The perturbation coefficients were incorrectly applied during Fast Gradient Sign Method (FGSM) attack experiments, and that ablation experiments contained calculation errors. As a result, FGSM robustness scores and contributions of deep residual shrinkage network (DRSN) and the cascaded group attention (CGA) mechanisms were overestimated.

Finally, the CGA mechanism did not properly filter adversarial perturbations, and that due to incorrect code implementation the SC-RTDETR framework performed simple thresholding filtering, instead of the reported soft-thresholding adaptive filtering.

The conclusions regarding the enhanced robustness and applicability of the proposed SC-RTDETR framework for forest pest monitoring are no longer supported.

The Editors therefore no longer have confidence in the results reported in the Article.

None of the Authors responded to the correspondence from the Editors about this retraction.
