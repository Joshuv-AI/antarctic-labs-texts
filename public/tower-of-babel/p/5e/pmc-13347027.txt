Correction: Salvianolic acid A modulates α-Ketoisovaleric acid metabolism to inhibit human cartilage endplate cells apoptosis under mechanical tensile stress
Authors: Yuxuan Du, Ke Zhao, Chong Liu, Gewen Wang, Yupeng Liang, Chuhao Cai, Weiye Zhang, Wu Sun, Zhefeng Jin, Jia Ma, Tao Han, Hongyu Yang, Liguo Zhu, Jiawen Zhan, Minshan Feng
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-23875-2, published online 12 November 2025

The original version of this Article contained errors in Figure 6, where the ‘BMP2’, ‘Control + SAA’ DAPI staining was a duplication of ‘Wnt-3a’, ‘Stress loading’ DAPI staining. Furthermore, all images for ‘MMP3’, ‘Stress loading’ were duplicated from the images for ‘MMP3’, ‘SL + SAA’. Additionally, all images for ‘GSK-3β’, ‘SL + SAA’ were derived from the data of ‘GSK-3β’, ‘Stress loading’ and therefore these images partially overlapped with the ‘Stress loading’ images.

The original Figure 6 and accompanying legend appear below.

The result of SAA on collagenⅠ, Wnt 3α, MMP3, BMP2, collagenⅡ, GSK-3β expression detected by immunofluorescence staining. Red color was the positive expression of Collagen 1, Wnt3α, GSK-3β. Green color was the positive expression of collagen 2, MMP3, BMP2. Blue color was the DAPI expression. Data was expressed as mean ± SD (n = 3). *p < 0.05 vs. control and control + SAA group, #p < 0.05 vs. stress loading group.

The original Article has been corrected.
