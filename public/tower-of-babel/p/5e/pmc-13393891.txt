Expression of Concern: Advances and challenges in single-cell RNA sequencing data analysis: a comprehensive review
Published: 2026

This is an expression of concern regarding Ali Mohammad Nesari, Habib MotieGhader, Saeid Ghorbian. Advances and challenges in single-cell RNA sequencing data analysis: a comprehensive review, Briefings in Bioinformatics, Volume 27, Issue 1, January 2026, bbaf723, https://doi.org/10.1093/bib/bbaf723

In July 2026, a reader notified the journal of several concerns regarding potential issues with Figures 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, and 10, as well as potential issues with numerous citations and potentially fabricated data in the above article. The journal is investigating these concerns in line with COPE guidance. In the interim, the Editors are publishing this Expression of Concern to alert readers while the outcome of the investigation is pending and advise readers to examine the details of this study with particular care.
