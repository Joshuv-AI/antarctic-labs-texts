OA20226. E-mentoring as a tool to enhance mental well-being and employability among vulnerable groups: A path
Authors: A Spyridaki, M Papadakaki, K Vassilakis, G Markakis
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundImproving working conditions and fostering professional development are essential for reducing workplace accidents and occupational diseases. In the domain of mental health, opportunities for career guidance and growth can significantly mitigate psychological risks such as stress, insecurity, and low self-esteem—especially among vulnerable social groups.ObjectiveThis study explores the impact of a dedicated e-mentoring platform on the psychological resilience and professional empowerment of individuals from vulnerable social backgrounds seeking guidance and employment.MethodologyA total of 110 participants (61% women) completed a satisfaction and outcome survey after engaging with the e-mentoring platform. Nearly half were high school graduates (47.7%), with balanced marital status representation. The survey included 34 items assessing user experience, focusing on knowledge acquisition, digital familiarity, emotional security, and overall satisfaction.ResultsAs regards to emotional resilience, 52.3% of respondents reported emotional improvement through the guidance received, 51.4% noted enhanced self-esteem, and 46.8% experienced reduced stress. As for employment outlook and satisfaction, 57.8% felt more optimistic about their job prospects and 55.1% identified the platform as trustworthy and supportive in their employment journey and felt strongly engaged with the mentoring process.ConclusionThe findings demonstrate that e-mentoring is not only an effective tool for improving employability among marginalized populations but also a contributor to psychological well-being. By promoting self-confidence, emotional security, and readiness for employment, such digital platforms offer a scalable solution to occupational health and inclusion, particularly in supporting vulnerable groups in their transition to safe and sustainable employment.Key messages• E-mentoring enhances mental health, reducing stress-related risks in vulnerable workers• Inclusive workplace policies are necessary to promote mental health and prevent work-related harmTopicE-mentoring, occupational health, mental well-being, employment, vulnerable groups.
