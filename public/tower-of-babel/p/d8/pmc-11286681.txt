Correction to: Using Social Media as a Source of Real‑World Data for Pharmaceutical Drug Development and Regulatory Decision Making
Authors: Didrik Wessel, Nicolai Pogrebnyakov
Published: 2024

Correction to: Drug Safety (2024) 47:495–511 10.1007/s40264-024-01409-5

In this article, the second affiliation for Didrik Wessel was incorrectly given as “Nørrebrogade 18A 3TH, 2200 Copenhagen N, Denmark” and should have been omitted. The affiliation for both authors, Didrik Wessel and Nicolai Pogrebnyakov, should have read “Copenhagen Business School, Solbjerg Plads 3, 2000 Frederiksberg, Denmark”.

Also, the Conflict of Interest section should have read:

“Didrik Wessel was a full-time graduate student and Nicolai Pogrebnyakov a full-time faculty member at Copenhagen Business School at the time this research was conducted. At the time of the research, which was performed towards the fulfilment of his graduate thesis requirements, Didrik Wessel also undertook a part-time internship at Novo Nordisk. After the research was completed, Didrik Wessel joined Novo Nordisk as a full-time employee. Nicolai Pogrebnyakov worked full-time at Twitter before the manuscript was published and is now a full-time employee at Sparrow Bioacoustics, a medical software company.”
