Correction to “Factors associated with suicidal ideation in junior high school students with autism spectrum disorder in Japan: A cross‐sectional observational study”
Published: 2026

Matsumoto Y, Kawabe K, Horiuchi F, Jogamoto T, Hosokawa R, Nakachi K, et al. Factors associated with suicidal ideation in junior high school students with autism spectrum disorder in Japan: a cross‐sectional observational study. Psychiatry Clin Neurosci Rep. 2026;5(1):e70272.

In the first sentence of the second paragraph of the Introduction, the text “The leading cause of death among teenagers in Japan, with a 7.0% suicide rate.” was incorrect.

This should have read:

“According to the WHO database of causes of death in 2021, suicide is the leading cause of death among teenagers in Japan, with a rate of 7 per 100,000 population.”

We apologize for this error.
