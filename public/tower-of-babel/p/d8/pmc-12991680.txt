Custom vs Stock Healing Abutments on Peri-Implant Tissue Integrity in Immediate Implant: A Randomized Controlled Study
Authors: Mehjubah Mohammad, Bhuvanesh Yanamala, Rupasree Gundala, Vishudh Nagpal, Naga Jyothi Chadalavada, Narendra Dev Jampani
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
ABSTRACTBackground:Maintaining soft tissue contour in immediate dental implant is a challenging task. A combination of immediate implant therapy with customized healing abutment would be effective in simulating ideal soft tissue contour.Methodology:Patients with unrestorable teeth were randomly allocated into two groups, that is, immediate implants with customized healing abutment in study group and stock healing abutment in control group. At the end of the third month, healing abutments were removed, soft tissue evaluation was done by modified plaque index (MPI), modified sulcular bleeding index (MSBI), midfacial width of keratinized tissue and functional implant prosthodontic score (FIPS), and crestal bone levels at postoperative intervals of baseline, three months, and nine months.Results:The mean difference between the test group and control group in MPI was -0.14. The mean value of FIPS was slightly higher in the test group than in the control group with a mean difference of 0.71. The mean ± SD values of MSBI scores in the test group at baseline, three months, and nine months were 0.00 ± 0.00, 0.43 ± 0.535, and 0.29 ± 0.488, respectively, and in the control group were 0.00 ± 0.00, 0.57 ± 0.53, and 0.43 ± 0.53, respectively. Midfacial widths of keratinized tissue in the test group was maintained constantly at 3.8 mm from baseline, three months, and nine months. On evaluating mesial and distal crestal bone loss, the mean of -0.2 and -0.34 and P values 0.05 and 0.01 were seen.Conclusion:Immediate implants with a customized healing abutment group had a positive effect on maintaining peri-implant health.

INTRODUCTION

Immediate implant placement is a therapeutic approach introduced in 1976 by Schulte and Heimke as an alternative to the classic delayed implant placement described by Branemark.[1]

Implant placement immediately after tooth extraction reduces the overall treatment time (six to eight months) and the number of surgical interventions.[23] However, high rates of esthetic complications, such as midfacial mucosal recession and papillary height loss, result from direct exposure of the graft to the oral environment, inability to accomplish a primary closure, or resorption of the facial and interproximal bone.[4]

The above complications can be reduced dramatically by employing a provisional restoration.[4]

When immediate loading is not possible,[5] customized healing abutments substitute the role of a provisional restoration to overcome the complications of immediate implant therapy and also offer the possibility of creating a correct emergence profile by maintaining the gingival contours.

METHODOLOGY

A randomized control study was conducted on 14 patients who had reported with unrestorable teeth due to caries or trauma to the Department of Periodontics, with an age between 18 and 50 years, having adequate quality and quantity of bone and good oral hygiene status. Patients with uncontrolled diabetes, pregnancy, poor oral hygiene, alcoholic patients, patients with active infection, or severe inflammation at the proposed implant site were excluded. Customized healing abutments were used for seven patients categorized under group A and stock healing abutments for group B.

Surgical procedure

A pre-procedural dose of 1 gm oral amoxicillin was given to the patients, 0.2% chlorhexidine rinse (1 min) was given to each patient.

Following immediate implant placement, jumping distance was grafted using sticky bone (Autogenous Fibrin Glue and SYBO GRAFT-Plus) to compensate for ridge resorption. In group A, custom socket healing abutments are fabricated and torqued in position. In group B, stock abutments were tightened or torqued into position according to the manufacturer’s recommendation.

Customized healing abutment fabrication

Temporary abutments were connected to the implant, and Teflon tape was placed over the graft around the titanium cylinder to prevent direct contact with flowable composite. Flowable composite resin was applied around the healing abutment in small portions sequentially and light-cured to prevent the invasion of the tooth socket until the extracted tooth contour was completed. Next, the fabricated healing abutment was removed, contours and smooth transition were created extra orally with flowable composite, followed by finishing and polishing. After finishing, Teflon tape was removed from the surgical site, and the abutments were tightened or torqued into position.

Statistical analysis

Data was analyzed using IBM SPSS version 20 software (IBM SPSS, IBM Corp., Armonk, NY, USA).

In plaque index scoring, modified sulcular bleeding index was done using the criteria given by Mombelli A et al.[4] Intragroup and intergroup comparisons revealed no statistically significant differences in all times of evaluation. DISCUSSION

Implant success criteria have shifted from implant survival to creating a life-like implant restoration with natural-looking peri-implant soft tissues. To create such a natural appearance in immediate implants, several studies (Chu SJ et al. (2012),[7] Tarnow, DP et al. (2014)[8]) have proposed the use of a provisional restoration or a healing abutment customized to the existing socket contours following the immediate implant placement. thus making provisional restorations a viable option However in absence of primary implant stability (less than 25 N/cm2) or in the posterior areas with large occlusal table, customized healing abutments can be used.[9]

Chairside fabrication of customized abutment reduces the number of visits;[10] however, it increases the intraoperative time. Therefore, this study is an attempt to evaluate the effect of custom abutment fabrication when weighed against the outcomes of stock healing abutments on peri-implant tissue phenotype.

In the present study, the mean difference between the test group and control group of modified plaque index was -0.14, which is not statistically significant. The mean value of the sulcular bleeding index score in the test group reduced from 0.43 (3 months) to 0.29 (9 months). At all three time points considered in the study, the mean width of keratinized tissue was higher in the test group compared to the control group, but was maintained significantly till nine months in both the groups.

CONCLUSION

Immediate implants with a customized healing abutment group had a positive effect on maintaining peri-implant health when compared with immediate implants with a stock healing abutment group.
