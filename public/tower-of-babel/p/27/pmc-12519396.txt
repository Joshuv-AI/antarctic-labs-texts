Correction for ‘implementing an intrahospital transport preparation (IHT) and management project for critically ill patients - Identification of barriers and facilitators using consolidated framework for implementation research (CFIR): A qualitative study’
Published: 2025

Wang J, Zhang X, Li G, et al. Implementing an intrahospital transport preparation (IHT) and management project for critically ill patients: identification of barriers and facilitators using consolidated framework for implementation research (CFIR)– a qualitative study. BMJ Open 2025;15:e096290. doi:10.1136/bmjopen-2024–0 96 290

This article has been corrected since it was published online. In the published article, due to an error in the synchronisation of multi-author information when submitting the final manuscript via the Scholar One Manuscripts system, Lanfang Du was not labelled as the corresponding author. After rechecking the contributions and reaching a consensus through consultation among all authors, the correct author order should be: Jing Wang, Xiaotian Zhang, Guifen Li, Ying Lu, Rui Xie, Man Cui, Baohua Li, Lanfang Du & Li Ma. Jing Wang & Xiaotian Zhang are the co-first authors of this article, and Lanfang Du & Li Ma are the co-corresponding authors.
