Correction: Novice nurses’ experience with an ‘Attending Rounds’ clinical case mentoring model: a phenomenological study
Published: 2026

This article is corrected by the journal.

After publication, the journal was contacted about an issue regarding a published article at another journal which used the same approval number from the institutional review board as this study.

The authors stated that the approval number in question applied to both studies, and that the study presented in the BMJ Open article is a sub-study that falls within the protocol approved by the institutional review board.

The Ethics and Funding statements have been expanded to include clarification on how the study in this article relates to the other sub-studies conducted under the same overarching project approval number.

The Author Contributions statement and the Methods section of the article listed the initials of one author incorrectly due to transliteration from the local language. Author RW is now listed correctly.

The Data Availability Statement has been amended to more clearly describe whether/how data from this study may be obtained.

The main messages of the work remain the same, and the content has therefore been corrected.
