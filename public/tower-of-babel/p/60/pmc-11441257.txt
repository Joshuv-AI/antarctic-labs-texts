Correction: SMARCA4 controls state plasticity in small cell lung cancer through regulation of neuroendocrine transcription factors and REST splicing
Authors: Esther Redin, Harsha Sridhar, Yingqian A. Zhan, Barbara Pereira Mello, Hong Zhong, Vidushi Durani, Amin Sabet, Parvathy Manoj, Irina Linkov, Juan Qiu, Richard P. Koche, Elisa de Stanchina, Maider Astorkia, Doron Betel, Álvaro Quintanal-Villalonga, Charles M. Rudin
Published: 2024

Correction: Journal of Hematology & Oncology (2024) 17:58 10.1186/s13045-024-01572-3

The original article mistakenly omitted numerous elements from the article figures due to an error in transferring the files at the proofing stage. The figures have since been updated to restore all missing elements of each affected figure (Figs. 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6).Fig. 1SMARCA4 expression correlates with NE features in SCLC. C Spearman correlation of SYP, CHGA, INSM1, YAP1 and REST with SMARCA4 mRNA levels in Rudin et al. and George et al. databases and CCLE[25, 26].
