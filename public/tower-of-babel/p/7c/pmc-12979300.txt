Editorial Expression of Concern: Maternal serum leptin as a marker of preeclampsia
Authors: Amal Mohamed El shahat, Abeer Bahaa Ahmed, Magdy Refaat Ahmed, Heba Saber Mohamed
Published: 2026

Editorial Expression of Concern: Arch Gynecol Obstet (2013) 288:1317–1322 10.1007/s00404-013-2915-8

The Editor-in-Chief is issuing an Editorial Expression of Concern for this article. Following publication, concerns were raised regarding the data reported in this article, specifically the receiver operating characteristic (ROC) curves provided in Figs. 1 and 2:

• Figure 1 (ROC curve analysis for serum leptin as a marker of preeclampsia) and Fig. 2 (ROC curve analysis for serum leptin as a marker of severity of preeclampsia) appear to be identical, despite the values reported in Table 3 for serum leptin being different between participants with mild and severe pre-eclampsia;

• The ROC curves in Figs. 1 and 2 both have only two steps despite the study including 36 cases of pre-eclampsia.

The authors provided a response to these concerns, but the Editor has been unable to fully evaluate this as the authors have been unable to provide their raw data due to the age of the study. Readers are therefore advised to interpret the results and conclusions with caution.

Author Magdy Refaat Ahmed disagrees with this Editorial Expression of Concern. The Publisher has been unable to obtain a current email address for authors Amal Mohamed El shahat, Abeer Bahaa Ahmed and Heba Saber Mohamed.
