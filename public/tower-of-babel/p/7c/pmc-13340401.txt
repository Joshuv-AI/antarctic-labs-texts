Retraction Note: Trends in chronic kidney disease mortality among patients with systemic lupus erythematosus: a U.S. population-based study (1999–2020)
Authors: Maria Qadri, Shafiq Ur Rahman, Hammad Javaid, Kritick Bhandari, Rahman Syed, Ameer Afzal Khan, Zaryab Bacha, Rizwana Noor, Maryem Filal
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: BMC Nephrology (2025) 26:462

10.1186/s12882-025-04394-8

The Editors have retracted this article following post-publication concerns regarding the validity and reproducibility of the reported findings. A post-publication review identified inconsistencies between the methodology described and the underlying CDC WONDER data, including non-reproducible datasets, mismatched study populations, unsupported age and race/ethnicity classifications, and numerical inconsistencies in the reported results. The authors were invited to respond to these concerns, but the issues identified during the investigation could not be resolved. The Editors, therefore, have lost confidence in the results reported in this article.

All authors agree with this retraction.
