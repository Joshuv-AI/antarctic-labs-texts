Error in Table

In the original publication [1], some errors were identified in Table 1. The etiological agents of Hemorrhagic Fever with Renal Syndrome and Hantavirus Cardiopulmonary Syndrome were interchanged. Additionally, there were some inconsistencies in some of the reservoirs and Luxi virus was removed for not being associated with human infection yet. Finally, the reference cited as [18] in the table should be updated to [24]. The corrected Table 1 is presented below.
