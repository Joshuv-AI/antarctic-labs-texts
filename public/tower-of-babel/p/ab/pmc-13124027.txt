Cover Feature: AI‐Augmented Iterative Screening of Libraries Against RNA Targets (AISLAR) Boosts Discovery of SAR‐Tractable RNA Binders and Rational Analog Design (Small Sci. 4/2026)
Authors: Haruhiko Hattori, Maina Otsu, Koji Imai, Mebuki Narahara, Jiro Kondo, Amiu Shino, Ella Czarina Morishita
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
Abstract AI‐Augmented Iterative Screening In article number e202600007, Ella Czarina Morishita and co‐workers present AI‐augmented iterative screening of libraries against RNA targets (AISLAR), an approach that integrates machine learning–driven prediction with experimental validation to identify small molecules that bind structured RNA. Iterative selection and enrichment from chemical libraries enable the discovery of SAR‐tractable binders and accelerate RNA‐targeted drug discovery.
