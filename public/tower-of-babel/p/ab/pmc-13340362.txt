Retraction Note: Roles of KAI1 and nm23 in lymphangiogenesis and lymph metastasis of laryngeal squamous cell carcinoma
Authors: Zongzhu Zheng, Ruihua Tian, Ping Wang
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: World J Surg Onc 15, 211 (2017)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12957-017-1279-0

The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article.

Concerns were raised regarding Fig. 1, where the two panels appear to overlap with the nm23 panels of Fig. 1 in [1]. The authors did not reply to requests from the Publisher for the source data and ethics approval documentation for this research. The Editor-in-Chief therefore no longer has confidence in the reliability of the data reported in the article.

The authors did not respond to any correspondence from the Publisher regarding this retraction.

Footnotes
