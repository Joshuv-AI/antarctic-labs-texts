Effect of liquid level monitor gas injection point size on information source amplitude-frequency characteristics
Authors: Pu Liu, Kailun Quan, Kaixuan Li, Xinyuan Wu, Zhongzhi Hu
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
In order to analyse the effect of the injection point size of the CBM (Coalbed Methane) well level monitor on the amplitude and frequency of pressure pulsations in the wellhead manifold, numerical simulations and experiments were carried out to investigate the effect of different injection point sizes on the amplitude and frequency of pressure pulsations downstream of the sudden expansion structure. Using compressed air as the fluid and the size of the injection point as the variable, the amplitude and frequency of pressure pulsations at different locations downstream of the sudden expansion structure were tested. The results show that the pressure pulsation amplitude is affected by the size of the injection point, and the larger the injection point is, the larger the pressure pulsation amplitude is; the size of the injection point has less influence on the pressure pulsation frequency downstream of the protruding and expanding structure, and the pressure pulsation frequency at 0.5 m and 1 m downstream of the protruding and expanding structure is in the vicinity of 76 Hz. Therefore, the echo signal processing should be filtered around this frequency to obtain accurate liquid level echo signals, so as to improve the accuracy of liquid level monitoring and realise the efficient development of coalbed methane wells.

Introduction

In the process of CBM production, there is a certain functional relationship between the rate of decline of the dynamic liquid level and the CBM production, and the overall performance of the "low–high-low" production trend, only to ensure a reasonable rate of decline of the dynamic liquid level in order to make the gas wells stable production1,2.Therefore, it is crucial to monitor the liquid level in the wellbore during the production process, and the closed-loop control system for coalbed methane well discharge is shown in Fig. 1.Figure 1Closed-loop control system for coalbed methane well drainage and extraction.

Closed-loop control system for coalbed methane well drainage and extraction.

In the field of oil and gas well development, common liquid level monitoring techniques include pontoon method3, schematic diagram method4,5, fibre optic temperature and pressure method6–8, and echo method9–12. Among them, the echo method is easy to install and use and has high accuracy, therefore, the echo method is the main method for monitoring the dynamic fluid level in oil and gas wells. The essence of monitoring is to launch a pulsed high-pressure gas stream to the wellhead, which forms a pulsed pressure wave in the original flow field and propagates downhole, and when the pressure wave encounters different cross-sections (tubing clamps, liquid surface, etc.), it produces reflected waves with different intensities, so that the depth of the liquid surface can be calculated13. However, when the echo method is used to measure the downhole dynamic level data, the acquired signal will be affected by the complex background noise, which makes it difficult to identify the liquid surface echo wave submerged in the noise14. In order to filter the echo signal in a targeted way to improve the monitoring accuracy, it is necessary to find out the source frequency. In this paper, the pressure wave generates distortion in the propagation process without considering the pressure wave, based on which the influence on the amplitude and frequency characteristics of the pressure wave source is investigated in the case of different gas injection point sizes.

It has been studied that different structural forms will form different characteristics of airflow pressure pulsation. Rydlewicz et al.15 investigated the pressure pulsation level downstream of an orifice plate under pulsating flow conditions in a straight pipe and found that the pressure pulsation level is proportional to the cross-sectional area of the orifice plate. Palacios et al.16 investigated the pressure wave generated by the sudden closure of a valve in a straight pipe by using a finite volume method and found the most serious cavitation phenomenon at the valve. The pressure wave generated by the sudden closure of a valve in a straight pipe and the most severe cavitation was found at the valve. Zhang et al.17 investigated the flared cavity inlet process and found that the total pressure at the flared opening is affected by the inlet velocity, the density of the air in the cavity and the water pressure. Lato et al.18 experimentally investigated the pressure pulsation suppression effect of the Herschel-Quincke device in resonant piping systems and found that a larger main pipe is more effective than a larger main pipe. suppression effect, and found that a larger main pipe diameter can be divided into several smaller devices while keeping the total cross-sectional area constant, and almost the same attenuation value can be obtained. Wang et al.19 investigated the release characteristics of pressurised CO2 with different nozzle diameters, and found that the nozzle diameter is directly proportional to the length of the Mach disc. Xu et al.20 based on the micro-channel and rectangular channel Based on the structure of microchannel and rectangular channel, they explored the relationship between flow pattern and pressure fluctuation, and found that the flow period after expanding the nozzle corresponds to the pressure fluctuation period, but they did not explore the effect of different expansion ratios on the pressure fluctuation. Yang et al.21,22 investigated the impact flow field of the small hole structure of the jet and the process of the jet impact with different jet velocities in the negative-pressure environment through numerical simulation, and found that the pressure in the centre of the jet impact is increased with the increase of the orifice aspect ratio. It was concluded that with the increase of orifice length and width ratio, the pressure at the centre of jet impact also increases.

The above studies have shown that local structures can cause fluid pressure pulsation response in pipelines. At present, in the field of downhole liquid level monitoring of oil and gas wells, there are few studies on the amplitude-frequency characteristics of pressure pulsation caused by pipeline structure. Therefore, in order to further improve the accuracy of liquid level monitoring by echo method and reduce the difficulty of echo signal processing, this study will take the sudden expansion structure at the gas injection point of the liquid level monitor as the research object to analyse the amplitude-frequency characteristics of pipeline fluid pressure pulsation. Through the combination of numerical simulation and experiment, the amplitude-frequency characteristics of wellhead pressure pulsation will be analysed, and the optimization suggestion of local structure of echo method liquid level monitor will be given, which is of great significance to improve the monitoring accuracy of liquid level monitor.

Model building and selection

Through the field research of Shanxi Liulin CBM mining block, it was found that the liquid level monitor exists about 1 m from the wellhead to the wellhead pipe sink, as shown in Fig. 2.Figure 2Liquid level monitoring site.

Therefore, a geometric model of the gas cylinder and wellhead manifold was developed based on the liquid level monitor field structure 1:1, along with a mathematical model of the flow of air through the pipe.

Geometric modelling

The created geometric model cylinder pressure is 0.6 MPa, and the pressure inside the test pipe is standard atmospheric pressure. The inner diameter of the test pipe is D = 47 mm; the connection length between the cylinder and the pipe is L1 = 0.1 m, and the length of the test pipe is L2 = 1 m, and the geometrical model is created as shown in Fig. 3.Figure 3Jet geometry model.

Jet geometry model.

By changing the inner diameter of the connecting pipe, the size of the air injection point can be changed to form 4 different types of protruding structures. The inner diameters of the injection points (the inner diameter of the connecting pipes) are set to d1 = 4 mm, d2 = 5 mm, d3 = 8 mm, and d4 = 10 mm.

Analysis of simulation results

Due to the diffusion of high-pressure gas to the low-pressure region, the fluid in the pipeline to produce the force is not uniform, in the local position to produce a short period of time larger or more concentrated and have a certain period of action of the force, that is, the pressure pulsation. Statistical analysis of the observed pressure data processing, different gas injection point size pressure pulsation curve shown in Fig. 6.Figure 6Pressure pulsation curve.

Pressure pulsation curve.

From Fig. 6, it can be seen that the pressure curve of monitoring point 1 is more obvious, the larger the gas injection point is, the larger the amplitude of pressure pulsation, and the faster the pressure decay. It can be seen from the two monitoring points that the corresponding time position of the peaks and valleys of the pressure curve with different gas injection points almost coincide, especially at monitoring point 2. It can be inferred that the size of gas injection points has little influence on the downstream pressure pulsation frequency of the sudden expansion structure. In order to further analyze the influence of the size of the gas injection point on the amplitude and frequency characteristics of the pressure pulsation downstream of the sudden expansion structure, the Fourier transform of the pressure curve at the monitoring point 1 is done, as shown in Fig. 7.Figure 7Amplitude-frequency characteristics of pressure pulsation at monitoring point 1.

Amplitude-frequency characteristics of pressure pulsation at monitoring point 1.

In Fig. 7, although the size of the gas injection point is different, the intensity of the pressure pulsation frequency downstream of the sudden expansion structure all has a maximum value near 77 Hz, which indicates that the size of the gas injection point has a small effect on the main frequency of the pressure pulsation downstream of the sudden expansion structure.

Since the air pump is fixed on a metal plate together with the cylinder, it is found through the pre-test that if the rigid pipe is used to connect the test pipe and the cylinder, the vibration will be transmitted along the rigid pipe to the test pipe L2. On the one hand, the vibration may damage the airtightness of the connection, on the other hand, the pressure sensor has already begun to collect the data at the same time as the pump is compressing the air, so in order to ensure as much as possible that the pressure sensor is in a stable state during the injection of the pressure sensor, therefore In order to ensure that the pressure sensor is as stable as possible during the air injection, all the connecting pipes are connected by hoses, which play the role of absorbing the vibration in order to minimize or eliminate the influence of the vibration of the air pump on the experimental results.

Analysis of experimental results

The pressure data were collected by data acquisition card for different sudden expansion structure, and the pressure fluctuation under different gas injection point sizes was monitored at monitoring point 1 as an example, and the relationship between the size of the gas injection point and the amplitude of pressure pulsation downstream of the sudden expansion structure was given, as shown in Figs. 11 and 12, respectively. The numerical simulation and experimental results show that the pressure pulsation amplitude downstream of the sudden expansion structure increases significantly with the increase of the gas injection point, and the maximum relative error between the numerical simulation results and the experimental results is 5.9%.Figure 11Monitoring point 1 pressure pulsation.Figure 12Comparison of pressure pulsation amplitude.

The Fourier transform of the experimental data of the monitoring point 1 of the different sudden expansion structure was used to obtain the spectral map of the pressure pulsation curve, which is shown in Fig. 13. The pressure pulsation intensity of each protruding and expanding structure appears the maximum value near 76 Hz, which is basically consistent with the numerical simulation results.Figure 13Fourier transform of experimental pressure pulsation curve.

The comparative analysis of numerical simulation and experimental results for the main frequency of pressure pulsation downstream of different protruding and expanding structures is shown in Fig. 14, and it is found that the average relative error between numerical simulation results and experimental results is 2%. The relative error between numerical simulation and experimental results may be due to the lack of accuracy of the pressure sensor itself or the computational error brought by numerical simulation.Figure 14Comparison of numerical simulation and experimental results.

In summary, the numerical simulation results and experimental results of the amplitude and frequency of pressure pulsations downstream of different sudden expansion structures were compared, and it was found that the experimental results were in high agreement with the numerical simulation results, which further demonstrated the reliability of the results of this study. The above results show that the size of the gas injection point basically does not affect the main frequency downstream of the sudden expansion structure, but has a greater effect on the pressure pulsation amplitude.

Conclusion

In this paper, numerical simulation method and experimental analysis method are used to carry out a comprehensive study on the amplitude-frequency characteristics of pressure pulsation at the wellhead of coalbed methane well liquid level monitor. Under the action of jet, the downstream pressure pulsation amplitude-frequency characteristics of different sudden expansion structures formed by different gas injection point sizes are studied and analyzed, and the following conclusions are obtained:A comprehensive study of the pressure pulsation frequency response downstream of sudden expansion structure was carried out using numerical simulation methods and experimental analysis. The average relative error of pressure pulsation main frequency between numerical simulation and experimental data is 2%, and the maximum relative error of pressure pulsation amplitude is 5.9%. The numerical simulation results and experimental results corroborate each other, indicating the reliability of the study results.After changing the size of the gas injection point, the downstream pressure pulsation frequency of the sudden expansion structure is near 76 Hz, therefore, in the process of liquid level monitoring, the filtering treatment of the echo signal should also be selected near this frequency, so as to obtain a more accurate echo signal and realise the accurate monitoring of the downhole liquid level.Although the size of the gas injection point on the main frequency of the pressure pulsation does not have much impact, but with the increase of the gas injection point, the amplitude of the pressure pulsation downstream of the sudden expansion of the structure increases significantly, which will produce a stronger signal source, the liquid level echo signal will also be enhanced accordingly, and more favourable to the reception and processing of the echo signal. Therefore, in the development of the liquid level monitor, under the same conditions, a larger injection point should be preferred, but a larger injection point puts higher requirements on the volume and pressure of the gas cylinder. The next step is to determine the appropriate size of the injection point and the cylinder volume and pressure according to the propagation and attenuation characteristics of the pressure wave in the well.
