From digital sparks to performance gains: organizational resilience thrives under ESG pressure
Authors: Hui Wang, Lei Xi
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
This study investigates digital technology innovation(DTI) in a global response to the ESG management model to drive corporate performance to implement sustainable development strategies. The current analysis collected data from high-tech corporations in Shandong, Jiangsu, and Shanghai, China through a questionnaire research method. Multiple regression analysis and Bootstrap analysis were used to reveal the evolutionary characteristics of digital technology innovation under ESG management. The main findings include (1) digital technology innovation has a positive contribution to short-term financial performance(STFP) and long-term competitive advantage(LTCA) of corporations. (2) Digital technology innovation has a positive effect on organizational resilience. (3) Organizational resilience has a significant role in promoting short-term financial performance and long-term competitive advantage of corporations. (4) Organizational resilience has a mediating role between digital technology innovation and corporate performance. (5) ESG management positively regulates the relationship between digital technology innovation and a company’s long-term competitive advantage. This research examines the consequences mechanism of digital technological innovation on corporate performance in the context of ESG management, and provides theoretical and practical support for fostering low-carbon growth and recycling economy and technological innovation in Chinese enterprises.

Introduction

Under the increasingly severe situation of global environment and resources, the promotion of green and low-carbon economic development has become an urgent need in the present era1–3. As the focus on sustainable growth intensifies and the enhancement of people’s awareness of environmental protection, enterprises, while pursuing economic performance, are paying more and more attention to the efficient combination of environment, society and governance so as to achieve long-term sustainable development4,5. Enterprise transformation has become an inevitable choice for them to realize high-quality development, by increasing the research and development of low-carbon technologies, continuously enhancing the level of sustainable operation, and then promoting achieving the dual-carbon objectives6,7. Sustainability is the essential feature of superior - standard development of firms, and in order to realize the goal of sustainable development, enterprises should adhere to the implementation of the new development concept, and strive to balance the relationship between economic growth, synergistic development of society, and environmental protection8,9. However, the sustainable development of enterprises still faces problems in technological innovation, management level, strategic choice and other aspects, which leads to the obvious lack of growth momentum of enterprise performance, and how to improve the level of enterprise performance through the sustainable development model has become a hot issue of concern in the academic and practical fields.

In the strategy of building a Digital China, achieving deep integration between digital technologies and the real economy is a crucial pathway for promoting high-quality economic development. This requires enterprises to possess enhanced digital technology innovation capabilities10. To build a sustainable development model for enterprises, digital technological innovation has become an inevitable choice for most enterprises. Digital technological innovation changes the traditional organizational system, breaks the traditional information barriers, builds a market innovation model with multiple subjects and an open system, and enhances the innovation drive and development potential of businesses11,12. Digital technological innovation, through advanced technologies for instance cloud-based services and quantum computing, provides effective technical support for environmental monitoring, data analysis, and decision-making, optimizing effectiveness and exactness of environmental governance13. The policy of new energy demonstration cities has endowed digital technological innovation with more vitality. The close integration of new energy and digital technology has eliminated high-energy-consuming and high-pollution industries, driven the overall innovation of the digital technology in the upstream and downstream industrial chains, and built an ecosystem of new energy and digital technology14.

In the face of the complexity, variability and uncertainty of the characteristics of the development of the times, the turbulent market environment poses a serious challenge to the flexibility of the organization, which urgently requires the organization to enhance its own resilience and improve the ability of sustainable development15,16. Organizational toughness itself has the competence to monitor, predict, learn, respond, etc., reflecting the dynamic adaptive process of the organization, highlighting the ability of the organization to face the disturbances of the internal and external environments, relying on its own wholeness to transform difficulties and challenges into opportunities for innovation and learning17. To efficaciously reduce external shocks and environmental pressures, enterprises gradually create value by connecting stakeholders through digital technology platforms, adjusting traditional path dependence, and efficiently allocating resources in order to strengthen the level of organizational resilience18.

With the increasingly serious environmental and climate issues, the public has paid more attention to the issue of sustainable development. As the main body of economic development, enterprises need to actively practice the concepts of environment, society and governance (ESG) while seeking maximum economic benefits19,20. China’s ESG regulation is transitioning from encouraging corporate disclosure to standardizing and enhancing quality, with the strategic goal of guiding enterprises to integrate ESG management into their operations and governance. The tripartite responsibilities of environment, society, and corporate governance in ESG management are deeply in line with the core requirements of high-quality development of conglomerates, such as green and low-carbon, innovation-driven and people-oriented, and have become an important choice for enterprise development21,22. With the proposal of China’s dual carbon goals, ESG is gradually becoming an important reference standard for the holistic and enduring progress of businesses and is influencing the investment decisions of investors, banks and other related interest groups23.

Some studies confirm that digital technology can enhance the absorptive capacity, innovative capacity, and adaptive capacity of enterprises24, provide a breakthrough mechanism for industrial low-carbon transformation25, effectively identify the market environment26, and accelerate the transformation and upgrading of manufacturing enterprises27. In other words, most of the literature on enterprise digital technology focuses mainly on the reform impacts brought by the digital technology model and pays less attention to the financial performance and long-term competitive advantages of enterprises. Even if there is a correlation, only digital innovation performance28, financial performance29, corporate resilience30, supply chain efficiency31 or other single dimensions are studied.

In summary, despite the substantial achievements in existing literature on corporate digital technology innovation, theoretical gaps persist regarding the synergistic mechanisms and dynamic evolution pathways linking digital innovation to corporate performance. First, against the backdrop of the digital economy and dual carbon goals, the interactive relationship between digital technologies and environmental dynamics remains incompletely deconstructed, resulting in insufficient guidance on how firms balance short-term financial performance with long-term competitive advantage. Second, the causal relationship between digital technology innovation and corporate performance has not been integrated into a unified analytical framework, making it difficult to reveal the impact pathways of digital technology innovation on both short-term financial performance and long-term competitive advantage under complex conditions. Finally, existing research has failed to fully integrate the synergistic effects of organizational resilience and ESG management on corporate performance, limiting the practical application scope of corporate digital technology innovation.

This study makes novel contributions to the literature on digital technology innovation and corporate performance. First, grounded in the Resource-Based View and Dynamic Capabilities Theory, it delves into the underlying mechanisms through which digital technology innovation influences corporate performance. It explicitly situates its theoretical contributions within frameworks such as Dynamic Capabilities Theory and Sustainability Theory, shifting from descriptive analysis to theoretical extension. Second, our research uncovers distinctive findings: organizational resilience not only directly drives corporate performance but also mediates the relationship between digital technology innovation and corporate performance. Facing intense market competition, enterprises enhance internal management capabilities and resource allocation levels by strengthening organizational resilience, thereby overcoming the limitations of previous single-path models linking digital innovation to performance. Third, our research proposes that ESG management exerts a positive moderating effect between digital technology innovation and corporate performance. While technological and product innovation are typically regarded as key drivers of performance improvement, and increased ESG management may raise corporate input costs, our findings indicate that ESG management enables flexible adaptation to market changes, secures investor and consumer support, and advances sustainable development principles. ESG-driven business models thus represent a significant pathway for digital technology innovation to enhance corporate performance. Our research not only expands the multidimensional factors influencing corporate performance and broadens the application scope of digital technology innovation but also provides valuable references and practical implications for government agencies and corporate managers in formulating sustainable development measures.

Literature review and research hypothesis

Digital technology innovation and corporate performance

Digital technology innovation refers to the process of recreating products, processes, organizational structures and business models based on the support of digital technology32,33. Digital technology innovation is not simply equivalent to enterprise digital transformation, it is the core support of digital transformation, providing efficient technical guarantee for enterprise digital transformation process34,35. Digital technology innovation usually involves complex technology and algorithm system, refined analysis and systematic processing of huge data sets, and in contrast to the traditional innovation mode, digital technology innovation requires enterprises to have a rich team of professional knowledge and relevant practitioner experience36.

The Dynamic Capability Theory posits that ventures are always operating in a constantly changing market environment and need to cultivate the ability to cope with environmental changes. Digital technological innovation achieves this by leveraging efficient software computing methods, automated processes, and real-time data sharing, enabling enterprises to swiftly adjust the market adaptability of their products and services. Through efficient resource allocation, it creates conditions for enhancing enterprise performance and thereby elevates the financial performance level of the enterprise37. The digital business model centered on digital technological innovation strengthens the connection between enterprise production and sales, creating value for customers around content, platforms, and shopping experiences, thereby enhancing the economic benefits of the enterprise38. By leveraging the efficient advantages of digital technology in resource construction and utilization, enterprises can improve the traditional production factor system, and the improvement in research and development efficiency and production efficiency helps enterprises form unique product advantages, thereby ensuring their long-term competitive advantage in market competition39,40. In light of the foregoing analysis, the ensuing hypotheses are formulated:

H1a Digital technology innovation exerts a considerable beneficial influence on short-term financial performance of firms.

H1b Digital technology innovation generates a marked favorable effect on firms’ long-term competitive advantage.

Digital technology innovation and organizational resilience

Enterprises with stronger organizational resilience are able to swiftly modify their original methodologies confronted with high pressure and multiple uncertainties in the external environment in order to conform to transformations, maintain smooth operation and enhance long-term development41. Organizational resilience is reflected in the ability to regain and build all capabilities, including capacity, efficiency, and performance, on the eve of, during, and after distress, to ensure that the enterprise achieves long-term sustainable development42,43. Increased organizational resilience means that this agile responsiveness can significantly improve operational distress caused by environmental changes and enhance the operational efficiency of the firm through efficient strategic adjustments in the face of large market fluctuations44.

The widespread utilization of digital technology has revolutionized the conventional business operation model. Digital technological innovation has driven the development of new consumption forms, comprising online education, online shopping, and cyberspace leisure activities. Enterprises have changed the process of value creation through digital technological innovation, enhancing their adaptability to uncertain shocks and thereby improving the organizational resilience level30. The close integration of digital technology with the innovation process endows enterprises with the agility to efficiently integrate resources and discover opportunities. Through digital information technology means, they can precisely identify market development needs, break the traditional innovation path dependence by reducing the uncertainties at the front end of innovation, enhance the organization’s future situational perception ability, and thereby improve the organizational resilience level45,46. Enterprises leverage digital technologies to conduct forward-looking early warnings of market risks, optimize decision-making through big data analytics, and achieve data sharing and resource integration. This helps organizations build a cyclical system of early warning, analysis, and learning, effectively enhancing the dynamism and flexibility of organizational resilience. Based on the above analysis, the following hypotheses are proposed:

H2a Digital technology innovation has a significant positive impact on organizational resilience.

Organizational resilience and corporate performance

Enterprises with strong organizational resilience are able to create a cultural atmosphere of positive practice and encouragement of innovation, stimulate the vitality of employee creativity, promote positive feedback and continuous updating of products and services, so as to sustain a leading edge in the fierce market rivalry pattern and realize the enhancement of enterprise performance47. Enterprises with organizational resilience qualities help to help enterprises quickly restore stability in the face of failure and crisis, so that when enterprises again experience adversity, decline, and failure, they have a stronger load-bearing capacity and immune protection, and by enhancing the ability to make strategic decisions in a dynamic environment, thus improving the efficiency and quality of the recovery of corporate performance48,49.

During emergencies, organizational resilience of enterprises can actively respond to and actively adapt to the mechanism of environmental dynamics, organizational resilience also requires enterprises to institutionalize and normalize the crisis awareness, summarize the shortcomings of enterprise management and development in a timely manner, and seek for new innovation and entrepreneurship market opportunities50, to promote the enterprise’s sustainable business operations, and to improve the enterprise’s financial performance level. Enterprises with strong organizational resilience are able to flexibly improve their talent echelon structure, organizational hierarchy and departmental settings, and product mix composition to form their own development system according to the needs of business development and changes in the actual environment, so as to promote new breakthroughs and enhancements in production efficiency, product competitiveness, service quality, etc., thus building long-term competitive advantage51. Synthesizing the above analysis, the ensuing hypotheses are formulated:

H3a Organizational resilience has a substantial constructive consequence on short-term financial performance of corporations.

H3b Organizational resilience exhibits a notable positive effect upon firms’ long-term competitive advantage.

Mediating effects of organizational resilience

Based on the resource-based theory, digital technology innovation serves as the critical pathway for optimizing and reconfiguring organizational resource structures, thereby providing robust support for enhancing organizational resilience capabilities. Compared with traditional technological innovation, digital technological innovation reduces enterprise information asymmetry by virtue of massive datasets, artificial intelligence, etc., improves enterprise logistics and supply network administration efficiency, and value creativity in market competition, improves organizational resilience through high level of market competitiveness and adaptability52, and then effectively improves the level of enterprise financial performance. Digital technology innovation enables enterprises to extend digital technology to the whole process of products, marketing and management, and through governance model innovation, close collaboration between supply and demand of upstream and downstream enterprises, enhance partner trust and stable cooperative relationship, and improve the level of organizational toughness53, and enterprises with strong toughness have the potential for further development, and the profitability of the enterprise will be significantly improved.

The theory of dynamic capabilities posits that an organization’s capacity for resource identification and allocation is the core element enabling enterprises to develop sustainable competitive advantages in rapidly changing environments. As a key driver of organizational efficiency, operational effectiveness, and high-quality corporate development, digital technology innovation continues to generate new value. Relying on digital technology innovation, the digital capabilities of enterprises will help enhance their awareness of crisis scenarios, identify market opportunities or increase the investment interest of stakeholders, promote information exchange within and between the enterprise and the outside world, strengthen the enterprise’s prowess in resource acquisition, strengthen the systemic capabilities and early warning identification of the enterprise, and effectively enhance the organizational resilience level54. Strongly resilient enterprises can calmly cope with the uncertainties of the external environment, maintain the stability and protracted development ability of the startup in the turbulent market competition, and foster an enabling environment for the accumulation of knowledge resources and the shaping of a learning atmosphere55, thereby better shaping the long-term competitive advantages of the enterprise. In accordance with the preceding analytical results, the subsequent hypotheses are advanced:

H4a Organizational resilience mediates the relationship between digital technology innovation and firms’ short-term financial performance.

H4b Organizational resilience mediates the association between digital technology innovation and firms’ long-term competitive advantage.

Moderating effects of ESG management

Stakeholder theory posits that a company’s proactive ESG performance can establish shared value objectives among employees, consumers, shareholders, and other stakeholders, propelling the enterprise from pursuing singular value toward balancing economic and social benefits56. The ESG concept was first developed and evolved from ethical and socially responsible investing, and is an evaluation standard for comprehensively measuring corporate environmental, social, and governance performance under new development trends57. ESG management offers new solutions to financial challenges and market competitiveness that enterprises may encounter during their development. Enterprises actively practicing ESG performance show the transparency of corporate information to the outside world, which makes the corporate information transmission effect significantly enhanced, increases investor and consumer confidence in the enterprise, and reduces the uncertainty of corporate financing projects58,59.

Stakeholder theory and corporate social responsibility theory share a close relationship of continuity and progression. Both view the enterprise as an organization that connects and supports various stakeholders, whose strategic decisions often require attention to the interests of multiple stakeholders to maintain and achieve shared value objectives. When enterprises carry out proactive ESG practices, it will generate market incentive effects, convey positive transformation signals to the market and stakeholders, and promote changes in the field of enterprise digital technology innovation by identifying digital innovation opportunities, integrating asset efficient allocation rates, and reforming the structure of human capital49. The transparent ESG performance level of enterprises will reduce unnecessary cost expenditures for the enterprises, thereby improving their financial performance levels. Digital technology innovation can enhance the efficiency of enterprise management through intelligent reform of organizational framework, and also ensure the continuous growth of enterprise market value. As the ESG performance of enterprises continues to improve, the intensity of enterprise digital technology innovation will increase, leveraging big data and machine intelligence solutions to strengthen the long-term competitive advantages of enterprises60. In conclusion, the following hypotheses are proposed:

H5a ESG management positively moderates the relationship between digital technology innovation and firms’ short-term financial performance.

H5b ESG management positively moderates the relationship between digital technology innovation and firms’ long-term competitive advantage.

Research methodology

Research participants and data gathering

To ascertain the research hypotheses proposed in the preceding section, the present research collects questionnaires to examine the data related to high-tech enterprises in Shandong, Jiangsu and Shanghai. Considering that the management team members and technical experts of high-tech enterprises are familiar with the variables of digital technology innovation, organizational resilience, ESG management and enterprise performance, this study selects the management team members and technical experts of the enterprises as the target of the questionnaire research. The sample enterprises are mainly involved in electronic information, machinery manufacturing, new energy, new materials and other industries, and the sample enterprises are able to better utilize the digital technology innovation model to improve the level of enterprise performance, which is representative. The questionnaire was formally researched from early April 2025 to late June 2025, which lasted nearly three months. The questionnaires were collected mainly in the form of field interviews (paper questionnaires) and network channels (electronic questionnaires). In Shandong, Jiangsu, Shanghai and other regions, 300 inventories were distributed, and altogether 275 questionnaires were returned, excluding 39 questionnaires with too short a fill-in time and obvious errors in filling in the content, and the final count of questionnaires totaled 236 questionnaires. Since unreturned questionnaires compromise result accuracy, the method of T-test is applied to check that there is no practically relevant difference between the returned questionnaires (275) and the unreturned questionnaires (25) in terms of the control variables (enterprise longevity and organizational scale), and the T-test indicates that the questionnaire data do not have unresponsive bias. In this study, the questionnaire items were measured using established scales at home and abroad, and rated on a 5-point Likert scale, use 1 to denote “completely disagree” and 5 to denote “completely agree”. To assess the interference of common method bias in this study, a Harman single-factor test was conducted. The single-factor analysis revealed that the first factor explained 42.7% of the variance (less than 50%). Therefore, this study does not exhibit significant common method bias. Variables and their measurement question items.

DTI: Digital Technology Innovation, STFP: Short-Term Financial Performance, LTCA: Long-Term Competitive Advantage, OR: Organizational Resilience.

Core explanatory variable: digital technology innovation. Digital technology innovation refers to a more innovative business model that creates high-quality products and improves the quality and variety of services by matching and combining information, computing, transmission and related physical components72. Digital Technology Innovation Drawing upon the research of Pan Hongliang61 and Zhu & Kraemer 62, and incorporating insights from renowned industry experts, scholars, and corporate executives, the following five items were designed based on practical business development needs: Your company has significantly increased patent output by boosting R&D investment in digital technologies. Your company continuously enhances production efficiency and product quality by optimizing production processes through digital technology innovation. Your company has built an efficient supply chain system and reduced logistics costs by leveraging digital technology innovation. Your company has established a big data analytics platform to accurately identify market trends and consumer demands through digital technology innovation. Your company has driven adjustments to its management system and improved management efficiency by advancing digital technology innovation.

The explained variable: corporation performance. Corporation performance signifies the comprehensive manifestation of an enterprise’s profitability, asset operation and management level, debt-paying ability, and long-term development capacity73. Enterprise performance primarily comprises short-term financial performance and long-term competitive advantages74. For short-term financial performance, referring to the research of Paillé et al.63and Zhang et al.64, three items are selected for measurement: the return on investment of your company has significantly increased, the growth of your company’s sales profit margin has significantly increased, and the growth of your company’s market share has significantly increased. For long-term competitive advantages, referring to the research of Sigalas et al.65 and Yu and Yan66, three items are selected for measurement: compared with competing firms in the same industry, the profit margin of our company is higher, compared with peer organizations in the same industry, the market share of our company’s products has grown faster, and compared with other enterprises in the same industry, our company has a better reputation and image.

Mediating variable: organizational resilience. Organizational resilience refers to the dynamic ability of a firm to withstand shocks, recover quickly, and be able to implement flexibility strategies in the face of a crisis75. Organizational Resilience Drawing upon the research of Zhang et al.67and integrating practical considerations of corporate development, the following four items were designed: When facing the departure of key employees, your company can swiftly recruit new staff and restore previous production levels. When encountering management decision crises, your company can rapidly adjust and return to normal operational status. When confronting malicious competition from industry rivals, your company can quickly recover or surpass previous operational levels. When facing new technological disruptions, your company can adapt swiftly and exceed prior operational performance.

Moderating variable: ESG management. ESG management mainly includes three aspects: environment, society, and firm administration, which can scientifically measure the degree of business social accountability contribution and the level of sustainable development76. This study draws upon the research of Xi and Wang77, employing equal weights or weights derived from factor analysis. Referencing the work of Maleticˇ et al.68, Xing et al.69, Cai et al.70, and Cao et al.71, while considering the company’s current development status and future objectives, adjustments and refinements were made to the measurement items after consulting with corporate executives and academic experts. Environmental factors include: Over the past three years, your company has significantly improved raw material utilization efficiency; Over the past three years, your company has continuously reduced consumption of water, electricity, and thermal energy; Over the past three years, your company has consistently decreased waste generated per unit of product produced. Social factors include: Your company efficiently provides consumers with high-quality products and services; Your company promptly delivers accurate and comprehensive product/service information to consumers; Your company offers robust safeguards for employee welfare and benefits. Corporate governance items include: Your company has pre-established contingency plans with partner enterprises to resolve disputes; Your company and partner enterprises provide mutual care and support; Your company proactively acquires new knowledge to refine existing processes and practices.

Control variables: firm age and firm size. In previous studies, firm age can hold a crucial position in firms’ resource collaboration ability, innovation ability, and risk management ability78. Larger firms have more redundant resources and a stronger risk appetite for exploratory innovation compared to smaller firms79. Therefore, firm age and firm size were included in the study, with corporate lifespan measured by the time since the firm’s inception and firm size expressed by the staff count of the company.

Endogeneity test

Given the potential reverse causality between digital technology innovation and corporate performance, companies with higher performance levels may be more adept at anticipating market opportunities driven by digital technologies. Consequently, they are more inclined to actively pursue digital innovation to enhance their growth potential. To address this potential endogeneity, this study adopts the method proposed by Liang et al.80, employing the cubic difference of the mean digital technology innovation scores across provinces and industries as an instrumental variable. Under the influence of the instrumental variables, digital technology innovation continues to exert a significant positive impact on corporate performance.

Discussion

The positive correlation between digital technology innovation on STFP and LTCA of organizations has been confirmed. With respect to the digital economy, the digital capabilities formed on the basis of digital technology innovation help to improve the responsiveness of enterprises, adapt to changes in market demand, and improve their operating profits81,82. Digital technology can adjust and improve the existing structure and processes of enterprises, prompting enterprises to accurately locate external market and customer information, and promote the absorption and transformation of external knowledge through cross-industry and cross-organizational exchanges and learning, thus enhancing the long-term competitive advantage of enterprises83. Therefore, enterprises need to incorporate digital technology innovation into their strategic planning, improve their organization and structure, and enhance their performance level.

The positive correlation between digital technology innovation and organizational resilience has been verified. Enterprises that widely utilize digital technologies may possess stronger development characteristics. By applying big data analysis, the business processes of enterprises will be improved, the efficiency of automated production will significantly increase, and the resilience of the enterprises will be enhanced84,85. Within the framework of the global value chain, digital technology will enable enterprises to more efficiently integrate global resources, enhance their value creation capabilities and reputation in the global market, reduce losses caused by external shocks, and thereby increase the export resilience level of the enterprises52.

The positive correlation between organizational resilience on STFP and LTCA has been well established. Organizations with a high level of resilience are better at capturing long-term, intangible, and multidimensional risks, and are able to reasonably solve problems from the environment and society, reduce the operating costs of the enterprise, and thus ensure the development of the organization in the direction of the common good55. Organizations with strong organizational resilience are more responsive to the market, have a more flexible organizational management structure, have stronger management of their workforce, and take the initiative to develop new business models, which produce profound effects on the competitive advantage of the enterprise86.

The mediating role of organizational resilience between digital technological innovation and enterprises’ STFP and LTCA has been verified. Digital technology, by leveraging advanced technologies like cloud computing and big data, can enhance the transparency of market information, effectively reduce the search costs and innovation costs of enterprises’ information, enhance the innovation vitality and efficiency of enterprises, and significantly improve the resilience level of the organization. Organizations with strong resilience can achieve better business performance30,87. Through digital technological innovation and digital transformation, relying on digital technology to strengthen the supervision and management mechanism of businesses, inhibiting managers from blindly pursuing maximum profits88, improving the internal operational efficiency of startups, and enhancing the resilience ability of organizations, thereby forming the long-term competitive advantage of firms.

The moderating role of ESG management between digital technology innovation and long-term competitive advantage has been confirmed. Digital technology innovation enhances corporate market value by improving product quality, service standards, and operational efficiency. As companies strengthen ESG management, digital innovation better meets the needs of consumers, investors, and partners, thereby shaping positive corporate reputation and image to foster long-term competitive advantage60. The moderating effect of ESG management between digital technology innovation and short-term financial performance remains unconfirmed. This may stem from the fact that simultaneously pursuing digital innovation and ESG development in the short term consumes substantial corporate resources. Excessive financial investment may fail to stimulate immediate profitability. Additionally, the complexity of quantifying digital investments and ESG value makes it challenging to attract investor and consumer attention quickly, thereby impacting short-term financial outcomes.

Conclusion

This study analyzes the relationship between DTI and enterprise performance by investigating data related to high-tech enterprises in Shandong, Jiangsu and Shanghai. Our study shows that DTI helps to improve the performance level of enterprises. DTI has a significant positive impact on STFP and LTCA of enterprises. DTI has a significant positive effect on organizational toughness. Organizational toughness exerts a profound positive sway on STFP and LTCA of enterprises. Organizational toughness mediates the relationship between DTI and firms’ STFP and LTCA. ESG management positively moderates the bond between DTI and LTCA. The findings of this study highlight the ramifications of DTI on firm performance and emphasize the positive role of organizational resilience and ESG management in promoting firm performance, providing lessons for firms to achieve sustainable development.

Theoretical significance of the study

With the industrial transformation driven by digital technology and the emergence of digital emerging markets, enterprises are required to possess higher levels of digital capabilities. Digital technological innovation capabilities are gradually becoming the core competitiveness of enterprises. Against this backdrop, this study empirically analyzed the research results on digital technological innovation and enterprise performance. Furthermore, organizational resilience as a mediating variable and ESG management as a moderating variable deepen the impact of digital technology innovation on a company’s long-term competitive advantage. This study aimed to rely on digital technological innovation to drive industrial transformation, strengthen the construction of enterprise organizational resilience capabilities, and enhance the intensity of enterprise ESG management. In doing so, it promotes the further development of enterprises while setting long-term goals for sustainable development, thereby achieving an improvement in enterprise performance levels. The continuous innovation in the digital technology field is driving the upgrading and adjustment of enterprise industrial structures, and the management concept of innovation-driven is becoming an urgent need for the modernization development of enterprises.

Practical implications of the study

Corporate management must fully recognize the profound impact of digital innovation, organizational resilience, and ESG management on business performance. First, management should continuously deepen their understanding of digital technologies, adopt cloud computing and IoT solutions to support digital transformation, leverage big data analytics to accurately gauge market demand and consumer behavior, formulate precise marketing strategies, enhance production efficiency and product quality, build strong social reputation and word-of-mouth, and ultimately improve corporate performance. Second, management must continuously enhance organizational resilience by optimizing corporate structures, establishing robust risk management systems, developing contingency plans for diverse risks, and elevating leadership decision-making capabilities and risk response competencies. Simultaneously, fostering an inclusive corporate culture cultivates an innovation-driven atmosphere. Refining talent development systems through training and team-building activities strengthens cohesion, enhances collaboration and adaptability among employees, and ensures smooth business operations—all of which safeguard steady performance growth. Finally, management must reinforce ESG management development models. The strategic objectives of ESG management require management to spearhead the construction of circular industrial chain systems, transforming waste into raw materials for new products. By utilizing IoT sensors to optimize energy consumption and equipment efficiency, and leveraging digital platforms to enhance supply chain transparency, digital technology innovation can empower enterprises to improve production processes and efficiency. This supports long-term corporate development strategies and achieves continuous enhancement of corporate performance.

Government departments need to increase support for corporate digital technology innovation. First, they should formulate scientifically sound industrial policies to bolster this support, using tax incentives and fiscal subsidies to encourage enterprises to overcome bottlenecks in core technologies and increase financial investment in digital infrastructure. Second, they should strengthen collaboration between enterprises, universities, and research institutions by establishing industry-academia-research cooperation platforms led by government departments. This facilitates efficient resource and project matching, thereby enhancing the conversion rate of corporate research achievements. Finally, relevant legal provisions should be refined to regulate the digital economy market. Systematic integration of data resources should be strengthened to promote public data sharing while ensuring user data security and privacy.

Government departments need to strengthen their oversight and guidance role in corporate ESG management. First, government departments need to actively guide enterprises to establish the strategic concept of sustainable development, regularly organize and hold seminars on enterprise ESG management, invite outstanding enterprises and experts and scholars to share their practical experience in ESG management, promote the establishment of industry benchmarks for enterprise ESG management, and stimulate the enthusiasm and initiative of enterprises. Secondly, government departments need to establish a scientific and perfect ESG supervision mechanism, regularly conduct ESG performance assessment of enterprises, and use the assessment conclusions as the basis for enterprises to broaden financing channels and improve credit rating, so as to form an efficient incentive and constraint mechanism for enterprise ESG management. Finally, in terms of environmental protection, revise and improve environmental protection regulations, and increase penalties for enterprises’ indiscriminate emission of pollution. On the social front, it clarifies corporate social responsibilities and obligations, regulates the rights and interests of corporate employees in terms of salary and wages, rest and vacation, and labor safety and health, and proactively listens to the voices of consumers and protects their legitimate rights and interests. In the area of corporate governance, enterprises are required to improve their governance system, improve their corporate governance structure, increase the transparency of corporate information disclosure, and place the process of corporate governance under the supervision of the general public.

Refine the practical implications arising from industry-specific and scale-differentiated factors. First, capital-intensive enterprises in sectors such as energy and chemicals face more pronounced environmental protection demands, necessitating increased direct ESG investments to allocate greater resources—funding, technology, and management—toward low-carbon technologies and emissions monitoring systems. Second, technology-intensive enterprises in electronics, information technology, and machinery manufacturing must strengthen communication and trust with partners, optimize data-driven supply chains, and leverage digital technologies to forecast customer demand. Additionally, SMEs must integrate workplace safety and carbon reduction into core management priorities, supported by digital monitoring platforms. ESG objectives should inform digital innovation decisions to build resilient production systems. Modularized processes enable rapid adaptation to market shifts. Finally, large enterprises should integrate ESG achievement metrics into executive compensation systems to heighten management’s awareness of green development and accelerate corporate greening. Establish cross-departmental, cross-functional ESG management teams to empower organizational resilience and digitalization. By actively participating in industry ESG standard-setting, transform ESG performance into sustainable competitive advantage.

Constraints of the study and avenues for future exploration

This investigation explored the interrelationships among digital technology innovation, organizational resilience, ESG management, and enterprise performance. However, this study still has certain constraints. First, this study conducted questionnaire surveys exclusively among high-tech enterprises in Shandong, Jiangsu, and Shanghai. Given the pronounced regional disparities among China’s high-tech enterprises, this limitation may constrain the generalizability and external validity of the findings. Future research should expand the sample scope to include companies in central and western regions, as well as those with different ownership structures—such as state-owned enterprises and private SMEs—to enhance the external validity and generalizability of the results. Second, this study employs a mainstream empirical management approach using questionnaires to collect data. The selection of enterprise management team members and technical experts as survey respondents introduces a degree of subjectivity. Future research will consider utilizing third-party ESG ratings or sustainability reports to assess ESG management practices. Third, this study only included firm age and firm size as control variables. Future research will consider incorporating industry competition, government subsidies, senior management team characteristics, and regional digital infrastructure to mitigate the influence of these factors on the adoption and performance of digital technology innovation. Fourth, further research may include ownership structure, industry competitiveness, and digital infrastructure as control variables to enhance the explanatory power of the model. Fifth, the intuitive presentation of the moderation effect diagram is somewhat lacking. From an industry-specific perspective, this may stem from the fact that in sectors closely tied to environmental issues—such as energy and manufacturing—ESG management plays a significant moderating role. Conversely, in other sectors like technology and services, environmental concerns may not be prioritized, leading to insufficient investment in ESG management and consequently weaker moderation effects. Combining different types of firms in regression analysis can result in a visually diminished effect. Future research could group companies by industry to develop moderation effect diagrams, enhancing clarity and persuasiveness. Sixth, since the data for this study were obtained through questionnaire surveys, although necessary checks for reliability, validity, and common method bias were conducted to ensure the data fall within reasonable ranges, the selection of questionnaire items relies on subjective measurements by respondents, which may introduce some interference into the results. In future research, we will adopt a combined approach of panel data and case studies to ensure the scientific rigor and accuracy of our conclusions. Seventh, the comprehensive and thorough investigation of these variables (digital technology innovation, organizational resilience, ESG management, and enterprise performance) may not be sufficient. In future research, the specific technical applications of digital technology innovation, the specific manifestations of organizational resilience, and the specific practices of ESG management can be expanded to further analyze the profound impact of these variables on enterprise performance. Eighth, this study only considered the influence of digital technology innovation and organizational resilience on enterprise performance. Looking ahead, strategic flexibility can be included as an intermediary variable in the research scope, and green innovation and digital transformation can be included as moderating variables in the scholarly work.
