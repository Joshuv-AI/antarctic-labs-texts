IFPHOR0059 Transition in the North West: The Future of Gender Identity Services
Authors: Mark Westwood, Luke Wookey
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractGender identity services in England are undergoing significant transformation in response to rising demand, evolving clinical practice and increasing policy scrutiny. Within the North West of England, innovative models of care have emerged, in response to both national direction and local innovation.This session will provide a comprehensive overview of the development of gender identity services across the region, from both commissioning and clinical perspectives. Led by an NHS specialised commissioner and the clinical director of the Indigo Gender Identity Clinic for Greater Manchester, the session will explore the evolution of services, current delivery models and the challenges associated with capacity, access and variation.The presentation will include an overview of recent national policy developments within the Levy Review and how these are shaping regional service design and priorities. A particular focus will be on emerging models of care, including enhanced primary care involvement, multidisciplinary approaches and quality improvement initiatives aimed at improving patient pathways and outcomes.The session will set out an aspirational vision for future service delivery, including opportunities for research, innovation, integration with wider health systems and the development of more sustainable and patient-centred models of care.This session will be of interest to academics, public health practitioners, commissioners and clinicians seeking to understand how national policy is translated into practice and how services can better meet the needs of a vulnerable and underserved population within a complex and evolving area of healthcare.
