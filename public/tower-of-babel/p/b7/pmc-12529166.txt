Influence of the Coal Spontaneous Combustion Process on the Hazardous Area of Gas Explosion in the Goaf under High-Level Borehole Gas Extraction
Authors: Yuannan Zheng, Yujie Shi, Sheng Xue, Bo Ren
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
Due to the oxidation and heating effect of coal, the gas environment in the goaf will be affected to a certain extent, which will affect the distribution of gas explosion hazardous areas in the goaf. In this study, based on the multifield evolution law and the dynamic explosion limit of multicomponent flammable gas mixtures in the process of coal spontaneous combustion in the goaf, the determination method of the coupling disaster-causing area is established, and the influence of different conditions on the evolution law of coupling disaster is obtained. The results show that explosion limits of flammable gas mixtures in the high-temperature zone of coal spontaneous combustion increase to varying degrees. There will be a certain degree of oxygen-poor circle and methane accumulation in the high-temperature area, which affects the coupling disaster process. The negative pressure of high-level borehole gas extraction determines the range and evolution direction of the coupling disaster-causing area. With the increase in the negative pressure of a high-level borehole, the risk degree of coupling disaster in the goaf increases synchronously, and the hazardous area will migrate to the deep part of the goaf. However, due to the limitation of porosity in the deep area of goaf on the transmission of gas explosion, the hazardous area of gas explosion in the goaf is mainly distributed near the air inlet and return sides of goaf. The influence of the coal spontaneous combustion process on the multifield distribution of goaf will make some areas at the center of coal spontaneous combustion lose the risk of coupling disaster.

Introduction

At present, about 90% of coal in China is mainly underground mining, and the threat of coal spontaneous combustion and gas coupling disaster is faced in the process of underground coal mining. , With the gradual depletion of shallow coal resources in China, the depth of coal mining is increasing, and the complex deep coal geological conditions lead to frequent coal spontaneous combustion and gas explosion accidents. , In the process of underground coal mining, the coal and rock in the roof collapse to the floor under the action of gravity, forming a deposit with a certain pore space. The fresh air flow of the working face enters the goaf through the pore space. , The fresh air flow of the working face enters the goaf through the pore space. At this time, the loose coal body undergoes an oxidation reaction with oxygen, and the ambient temperature keeps rising. If the gas concentration in the high-temperature area at this time meets the conditions of gas explosion, it will lead to the occurrence of a coupling disaster.

In terms of the mechanism of coal spontaneous combustion and gas coupling disaster in the goaf, it has been found that the superposition of the four fields of fracture field, gas concentration field, oxygen concentration field, and temperature field is a necessary and sufficient condition for the coupling disaster of coal spontaneous combustion and gas in the goaf, and the explosion mechanism and hazardous area of gas explosion in the goaf are analyzed. , Previous studies have proposed that the goaf is a three-dimensional space formed by the superposition of the air leakage intensity field, methane migration field, oxygen concentration field, and temperature field through theoretical analysis and believed that high temperatures will expand the hazardous space of gas explosion. By using MATLAB software, the gas concentration distribution and temperature distribution in the goaf are calculated, and the two-dimensional distribution of the dangerous area of the composite disaster in the goaf is obtained by superposition. The coupling disaster-causing mechanism of coal spontaneous combustion and gas in the goaf was studied through experiments, theoretical analysis, and field measurement, and the influence of different extraction conditions on coal spontaneous combustion and gas coupling disaster in the goaf of high-gas and easily self-igniting coal seams is analyzed. It was found that the extraction effect will increase the three-zone range of spontaneous combustion, and the gas concentration in the hazardous area of coal spontaneous combustion in the goaf behind the air inlet side of the working face is within the explosion limit, which is the hazardous area of gas explosion. − Some studies have measured the gas concentration field in the goaf, and based on the reconstruction technology of the coupling disaster hazardous area caused by coal spontaneous combustion and gas in the goaf, the influence of other combustible gases on the methane explosion limit is considered when the coupling disaster hazardous area is superimposed, and the spatial distribution of the coupling disaster hazardous area is obtained.

At present, most of the research on the distribution law of the hazardous areas of coal spontaneous combustion and gas coupling disasters in the goaf is based on the distribution law of each gas concentration field (oxygen concentration field and combustible gas concentration field) in the goaf. Based on the gas seepage equation, oxygen migration equation, and heat transfer equation in the goaf, the three-dimensional numerical calculation model of goaf and the coupling mathematical model of coal spontaneous combustion and gas are established, and the distribution law of multifield in the goaf is obtained. It is found that with the increase in vertical height, the range of the coal spontaneous combustion danger zone in the goaf increases gradually. The multifield distribution of goaf in the process of working face advancing is also analyzed, and it is found that the range of the spontaneous combustion zone decreases with the continuous advancement of the working face. − In addition, some scholars have also studied the relationship between coal spontaneous combustion and air leakage in the goaf and believed that although the increase in ventilation wind speed will reduce the gas concentration in the goaf to a certain extent, it has little effect on the area with low porosity in the goaf. At the same time, the fire wind pressure generated by coal spontaneous combustion in the goaf strengthens the air leakage in the working face. , Based on the distribution law of the oxygen concentration field, carbon monoxide concentration field, temperature field, and pressure field in the goaf under different working conditions, it is found that the three zones and oxygen concentration field in the goaf are greatly affected by ground air leakage. The pressure in the air return corner of the goaf is the smallest, and the pressure in the air inlet corner is the largest. , On the basis of comparing the changes of oxygen and gas before and after gas extraction by buried pipes in the goaf, it is found that with the increase in the extraction flow, the gas concentration in the goaf gradually decreases and the width of the oxidation zone increases.

The current practice shows that the arrangement of high-level boreholes can effectively solve the problem of gas overrun in the upper corner of goaf. In the study of the influence of high-level boreholes on the gas concentration field in goaf, some scholars have studied the gas migration in the goaf under the action of high-level borehole extraction and found that when the negative pressure of high-level borehole extraction was 8000 Pa, the gas extraction effect was the best. At the same time, combined with the numerical simulation method, it was found that the extraction of high-level boreholes will increase the range of the oxidation zone in the goaf and the distribution of oxygen and methane concentrations in the goaf under different extraction flow and extraction negative pressure conditions is analyzed. It is found that with the increase in extraction flow, the gas concentration in the goaf decreases as a whole, and the width of the oxidation zone decreases on the side of the belt roadway and increases on the side of the track roadway. With the increase in extraction negative pressure, the gas concentration in the goaf decreases, but the width of the oxidation zone increases. − Some studies have also compared the distribution of air leakage wind speed and oxygen concentration in the goaf with or without high-level boreholes and different extraction negative pressure conditions. It is found that when high-level boreholes are arranged, then the air leakage intensity near the air return side in the goaf increases significantly, and the distribution range of oxygen increases by 8–18%. When the negative pressure of high-level borehole extraction increases, the change of the air leakage flow field is proportional to the change of extraction negative pressure, and the range of the oxidation heating zone increases. The predecessors also studied the parameters of pressure relief gas extraction in the goaf and obtained the influence of different extraction parameters on the distribution of pressure relief gas concentration in the goaf. The influence of different extraction parameters on the extraction effect from large to small is as follows: borehole diameter, borehole horizontal distance, and extraction negative pressure.

In this study, the goaf of Dafosi Coal Mine was used as the modeling prototype, and the numerical simulation software was used to simulate the oxygen field, methane field, and temperature field in a long-wall goaf. The distribution of oxygen and methane concentration fields in the goaf under high-level borehole extraction and different ventilation conditions was quantitatively determined. On this regard, the influence of multicomponent gas produced in the process of spontaneous combustion of residual coal in the goaf on the explosion limit was considered. At the same time, the influence of the temperature field was considered, and the distribution and variation laws of the gas explosion hazardous area in the goaf under the condition of coal spontaneous combustion were obtained, which provided guidance for preventing the occurrence of gas explosion accidents.

Mathematical Model

Gas Flow Equation

The air flow in the working face is turbulent, which can be regarded as pipeline flow. It is described by the Navier–Stokes equation, and the mathematical model of the solution is as follows: ρ∂u∂t+ρ(u·∇)u=∇[−pI+(∇u+(∇u)T)] 1 where ρ is the gas density, kg/m3; u is wind velocity of the working face, m/s; p is airflow pressure of the working face, Pa.

Due to the heterogeneity of the caving coal and rock mass in the goaf, the flow state of the fluid leaking into the goaf is also different at different positions. In the area near the working face in the goaf, the accumulation form of the coal and rock block is relatively loose and the regional porosity is large. The flow form of the fluid in this area is a turbulent state. In the deep part of the goaf, because the accumulated coal and rock block are gradually compacted under the action of stress, the regional porosity is small, and the flow form of the fluid in this area is a laminar flow state. The fluid flow state between these two regions is a transitional flow state, which is no longer applicable to the traditional Darcy’s law. Therefore, considering the nonlinearity of fluid flow in the goaf, the control equation of fluid flow in the goaf is , ρ∂u2∂t=∇·[−p2I+με(∇u2+(∇u2)T)−23με(∇u2)I]−(μk−1+βF|u2|+Qmε2)u2+F 2 where u2 is the velocity vector of gas migration in the goaf, m/s; μ is the dynamic viscosity coefficient of gas in the goaf, kg/(m s); βF is the Forchheimer coefficient, kg/m4; p2 is airflow pressure in the goaf, Pa; k is permeability in the goaf, m2; Q m is the mass source term of gas mass conservation in the goaf, kg/(m3 s); F is the volume force, N/m3.

Here, the Forchheimer coefficient is expressed by the following: βF=ρgεCfk 3 where ρg is airflow density, kg/m3; C f is the friction coefficient of airflow, Cf=1.75150ε3 .

Gas Diffusion Equation

The convection–diffusion of each component gas in the goaf needs to satisfy the following formula: , dc(Θ)dτ+ν·∇c(Θ)=∇[D∇c(Θ)]+c−c(Θ)c·εW(Θ) 4 where Θ is the component of each gas in the goaf, such as O2, CH4, CO, etc.; W(Θ) is the source term of gas composition Θ, mol/(m3 s); c is the multicomponent gas concentration, mol/m3; D is the gas diffusion coefficient tensor, m2/s; t is time, s.

The gas diffusion coefficient tensor in the goaf is expressed by the following: D=(αT|u2|+τ1·Da)δij+(αL−αT)u2,xu2,y|u2| 5 where δ ij is the Kronecker symbol; u2,x and u2,y are the velocity components of the gas in terms of tendency and direction, m/s; D a is the diffusion coefficient of gas molecules, m2/s; αL and αT are the transverse and longitudinal diffusivity, m; τ1 is the tortuosity of the porous medium, taken as 0.8.

The oxygen consumption source term W(O2) of residual coal oxidation in the goaf can be obtained by the Arrhenius formula: W(O2)=−HaHAC(O2)e−Ea/RTs 6 where W(O2) is the oxygen consumption of residual coal oxidation in the goaf, mol/(m3 s); H a is the height of residual coal in the goaf, m; R is the gas constant, J/(mol K); A is the oxidation precursor factor of coal and rock masses in the goaf, s–1; E a is the activation energy, kJ/mol; T s is the temperature of coal and rock masses in the goaf, °C.

As the working face keeps advancing forward, the coal and rock mass in the roof deform and crack under the action of stress until they collapse into the goaf. Gas is constantly desorbed outward from the coal and rock masses that have collapsed into the goaf. The gas emission in the goaf is simplified as W(CH4)=w(CH4)H 7 where W(CH4) is the gas emission volume per unit time and per unit volume in the goaf, mol/(m3 s); w(CH4) is the gas emission volume per unit time and per unit area in the goaf, mol/(m2 s).

Energy Conservation Equation

The reason for coal spontaneous combustion in the goaf is that the reaction between residual coal and oxygen releases heat. At the same time, when the air leakage flow contacts the residual coal, heat exchange will occur between them. According to the law of energy conservation, the control equation of heat transfer between gas and solid in the goaf is (subscript s represents the solid phase, and g represents the gas phase): , (1−ε)ρscps∂Ts∂t−(1−ε)∇·(κs∇Ts)=(1−ε)Qs−tsg(Ts−Tg) 8 ερgcpg∂Tg∂t+ρgcpgvg·∇Tg−ε∇·(κg∇Tg)=tsg(Ts−Tg) 9 where c p is the specific heat capacity, J/(kg °C); T g is the gas temperature in the goaf, °C; κ is thermal conductivity, J/(m s K); t sg is the gap convection heat transfer coefficient, W/(m3 K); Q s is the source term of the temperature field in goaf, W/m3.

The source term of the temperature field in the goaf can be expressed by the following: Qs=Q1W(O2)−2(Ts−Tw)ksρscpsπ 10 where Q1 is the reaction heat of coal, J/mol; T w is the temperature of the top and bottom plates in the goaf, °C.

The gap convection heat transfer coefficient t sg can be expressed by the following: tsg=6(1−ε)kg[2+1.1(μcpgkg)1/3(ρg|u2|dpμ)0.6]/dp2 11

Explosion Limit of Multicomponent Gases

When coal spontaneous combustion occurs in the goaf, a certain amount of flammable gases (such as carbon monoxide, ethane, ethylene, etc.) is produced due to the reaction between the residual coal and oxygen in the high-temperature area. These flammable gases are mixed into the gas emitted from the roof and floor of the goaf, which will change the explosion limit of the original gas. At this time, the explosion limit of the flammable mixed gas is no longer 5–16% but expressed by the following: Lg=100VCH4LCH4+VCOLCO+VC2H6LC2H6+VC2H4LC2H4×100 12 where Q1 is the explosion limit of the flammable mixed gas, %; LCH4 , L CO, LC2H6 , and LC2H4 are the explosion limits of each gas component, %; VCH4 , V CO, VC2H6 , and VC2H4 are the volume fraction of each component gas in the mixed gas, %.

Determination of the Gas Explosion Hazardous Area

After coal spontaneous combustion occurs in a goaf area, if the concentration of flammable gas in this area happens to be within the explosion limit, then there is a risk of a gas explosion disaster. At this point, the area should satisfy the following equation: Re(τ,x,y,z)=Rc(τ,x,y,z)∩RT(τ,x,y,z)∩Rf(τ,x,y,z)>0 13 where R e(τ, x, y, z) is the hazardous area of coal spontaneous combustion and gas coupling disaster in the goaf; R c(τ, x, y, z) is the oxygen field area that meets the gas explosion conditions in the goaf; R T(τ, x, y, z) is the temperature field area where the goaf meets the gas explosion, and the minimum temperature is 650 °C; R f(τ, x, y, z) is the area where the flammable gas in the goaf is within the explosion limit.

However, after the gas explosion disaster occurs in the goaf, the shock wave generated by the gas explosion in a certain pore space cannot propagate. At this time, the gas explosion disaster cannot cause harm to the personnel and equipment of the working face and the mine. On the contrary, when the shock wave generated by the gas explosion propagates to the working face through a certain pore space, there will be casualties and property losses, resulting in incalculable consequences. At this point, the area should satisfy the following equation: Rp(τ,x,y,z)=Re(τ,x,y,z)∩R12(τ,x,y,z)>0 14 where R p(τ, x, y, z) is the hazardous area of gas explosion propagation in the goaf; R12(τ,x,y,z) is the pore area that satisfies the gas explosion propagation conditions in the goaf (the critical porosity is determined to be 0.48 by the explosion transmission characteristics and critical condition experiments of the gas-containing mixed gas in the goaf).

Three-Dimensional Seepage Parameters of Goaf

Permeability is an important parameter to indicate the ability of coal and rock masses to conduct fluids in the goaf. The change of permeability directly reflects the heterogeneity of the flow field in the goaf. According to the Blake–Kozeny equation, the permeability is expressed as follows: K=dc2ε3150(1−ε)2 15 where K is the permeability of goaf, m2; d c is the diameter of coal and rock mass in the goaf, m.

With the advancement of the working face, the coal and rock masses on the roof continuously collapse into the goaf. Due to the different stresses at each position, the heterogeneity of the accumulated coal and rock blocks in the goaf is caused. Some areas are compacted under the action of greater stress, resulting in a smaller bulking coefficient of coal and rock mass. In the early stage of coal and rock mass caving into the goaf, the bulking coefficient decreases greatly. In the process of coal and rock mass gradually being compacted, the decrease in the bulking coefficient gradually becomes smaller. At this time, it is called the residual bulking coefficient and the value is about 1. The relationship between the bulking coefficient and the stress satisfies the following equation: Kp=Ae−σ+B 16 A=eσ0(K0−K1)eσ0−1 17 B=K1eσ0−K0eσ0−1 18 where σ is the stress on coal and rock mass, MPa; σ0 is the original stress, MPa; K0 is the natural bulking coefficient, taken as 1.45; K1 is the residual bulking coefficient, taken as 1.15.

The relationship between porosity and the bulking coefficient is expressed by the following: ε=1−1Kp 19

At the vertical height of the goaf, as the height increases, the gas concentration distribution in the goaf is less affected by the high-temperature area, and the range of the coupling disaster-causing hazardous area is larger.

Conclusions

In order to study the influence of the coal spontaneous combustion process on the distribution of the coupling disaster-causing hazardous area in the goaf under the high-level borehole extraction condition, the multifield coupling mathematical model of coal spontaneous combustion and gas in the goaf was established, and the multifield distribution in the goaf under the coal spontaneous combustion was simulated. Based on the above multifield distribution, the distribution law of coupling disaster-causing hazardous areas in the goaf was obtained, and the risk of coupling disaster-causing hazardous areas was quantitatively analyzed. The following conclusions are obtained:1)After the coal spontaneous combustion occurs, there will be an oxygen-poor circle and a certain degree of gas accumulation near the high-temperature area. The temperature in the high-temperature area determines the degree of oxygen drop and gas accumulation. When coal spontaneous combustion occurs at different locations and reaches the same number of days after coal mining, due to the inhomogeneity of oxygen distribution in the goaf, the temperature rise rate is the fastest at the center of the high-temperature area near the air inlet side when coal spontaneous combustion occurs. At this time, the decrease in oxygen concentration and the increase in methane concentration in the high-temperature area are the largest.2)After the coal spontaneous combustion occurs, the influence of the coal spontaneous combustion process on the multifield distribution in the goaf will cause some areas at the center area of coal spontaneous combustion to lose the risk of coupling disaster. When coal spontaneous combustion occurs at the air inlet side and the central area of the goaf, the temperature in the goaf is relatively high, the gas concentration distribution changes greatly, and the range of the coupling disaster-causing hazardous area is significantly reduced. When coal spontaneous combustion occurs at the air return side of the goaf, the range of the coupling disaster-causing hazardous area is the largest.
