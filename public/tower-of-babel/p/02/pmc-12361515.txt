In the version of the article initially published, in Fig. 5h, the “Senp1SMCKO, PDGF-BB + AZD6244”, “CNN1/DAPI” image was incorrect and has now been amended as seen in Fig. 1, below. Additionally, affiliation 2 was incorrect and should have read “State Key Laboratory of Ophthalmology, Zhongshan Ophthalmic Center, Sun Yat-sen University, Guangdong Provincial Key Laboratory of Ophthalmology and Visual Science, Guangzhou, 510060, China”. These corrections have been made to the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
