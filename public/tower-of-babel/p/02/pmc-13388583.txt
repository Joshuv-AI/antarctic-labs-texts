Empowering nanoscale heat transfer using ecofriendly quint nanomaterial powered by response surface technique (RST) and ANOVA scheme coupled with radiation effects
Authors: Adnan, Dase Fayera Hule, S. U. Khan, Mustafa Abdullah, Samia Elattar, Muhammad Nasir Bashir, Yasir Khan, Muhammad Bilal
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
PurposeThe key purpose of this research is to analyze the heat transfer in dovetail device wetted with quint nanofluid subject to different influential parameters like radiations, porosity, convective, conductive, heat generation and their contribution in the heat transfer. Further, to investigate the joint effects of linear and nonlinear heating sources.Design/methodology/approachThe model is designed for dovetail device associated to promising heat transfer parameters. The physical model is governed by second order problem with the influence of quint concentration. To investigate the heat transfer, an innovative idea of Response Surface Technique (RST) and ANOVA successfully implemented and achieved better responses of the model against the parameters and furnished the results.FindingsIt is examined that the presence of linear and nonlinear heating sources are crucial for heat transfer applications. The nonlinear source is observed excellent for enhanced heat transfer while linear source is good to achieve the applications at low heat transport. Further, concentrations, radiations and heat generation enhanced the dovetail device performance while permeability and conduction drop the heat mechanism.Originality/valueThis investigation provides promising heat transfer in dovetail device wetted with quint nanofluid which is unique combination not reported so far. By implementing the Response Surface Technique (RST) and ANOVA, the analysis exceptionally measures the interactive and individual role of the parameters. This unified approach offers a novel predictive paradigm for optimizing and designing of the device in advanced heat transfer applications.

Introduction

The significance of Dovetail type fin device regarding heat transfer cannot be ignored in engineering and industrial applications. Fascinating design of Dovetail fin enhances mechanical constancy and playing critical role in the device efficiency by transporting heat. Such configuration enhances the effectiveness of device because it offers excellent heat conduction process from the base and make them reliable to acquire more efficient heat transfer in engineering applications. Coupling of nanomaterials with fins is an innovative concept to augment its heat efficiency further. In recent time, researchers focused towards the study in this direction by considering single and multi components nanomaterials. Hatami [1] determined the contribution of natural convection and nanofluids for enhanced heat applications in cavity associated to fins. The prominent augmentation in the heat rate in the presence of nanofluid was examined and also observed that managing concentration levels promotes the heat mechanism. Analysis of microchannel heat transfer with longitudinal fin has been reported by Abdollahi et al. [2]. For better output, four different types of nanomaterials (ZnO, CuO, Al2O3, and SiO2) were tested with diameter 30–60 nm. Further, the study provided excellent increase in heat using nanofluids. Haq et al. [3] explored the thermal transmission in square cavity with parallel fins. Matrix type of 2D nanomaterial (SWCNTs) added in the fluid to acquire desired heat transfer.

Comparative heat efficiency between common and nanofluids discussed by Strandberg and Das [4]. The study predicts that the system becomes 11.6% efficient with 60% EG and 4.0% Al2O3 while, the simple fluid observed less effectual. Ambreen and Kim [5] studied the influence of fin dimensions on the heat using nanofluid. They noticed that fin’s structure is also of paramount interest for better applications. Different fin’s shape experienced and based on their output efficiency; circular shape is observed much efficient than other. Copper water applications for thermal augmentation in annulus with zero slope is conducted by Waqas et al. [6]. It is determined that higher level of NPs reduces the boundary layer growth and thermal transport can be enhanced at the same level. Thus, use of NPs with specific concentration help to acquire rapid heat transfer. Recently, Zaboli et al. [7] reported experimental investigation of heat exchanging device using axial fins. They examined that increasing number of fins enhances the heat efficacy and hybrid nanofluid are more effective. The significance of Iron and Ag for rectangular configuration subject to Lorentz and buoyancy effects has been explored by Sowmya et al. [8]. From the output, it is explored that the Rayleigh number reduces the intensity of stream and velocity profiles. Further, fluctuation in the concentration also disturb the performance. The potential heat assessment in tangent hyperbolic type of fluid have been examined in the studies [9], and [10].

Mixed convective phenomena coupled with heat sink for engineering applications using circular fin is discussed by Bakhti et al. [11]. Theoretical experiment executed for TiO2/H2O, Cu/H2O, and Al2O3/H2O and found superior efficiency for Cu/H2O. Concentration of these NPs chosen in the range of 2% to 10% while the Re varies 100 to 400. Fins have also concrete applications in solar collectors. In 2016, Amina et al. [12] extended the use of longitudinal fin parabolic collector performance using nanofluids. It is suggested that the hydraulic performance can be improved by setting the parameters and NPs amount. The comparative study of three fins and their energy efficacy with varying quantities is examined by Subray et al. [13]. Computational investigation of the model performed with the influence of convection and variant thermal conductivity. Among the fins, exponential type is observed much effective than other two types for which ANSYS software as computing tool used. Acharya et al. [14] reveals that Hartmann number resists the heat transmission in hexagonal cavity while convection and Ra numbers boost it. For this, GFEM implemented with better accuracy. The potential and comprehensive investigations on the performance of multiple hybrid nanofluids and their thermal performance have been reported in the Refs. [15–18], and [19]. These studies highlighted major contribution of variety of nanomaterials for enhanced thermal applications while varying the parameters over physical domain.

Coupled influence of convection and radiations has great impact on the functional device. Thus, Pavithra et al. [20] treated these effects along with hybrid nanoliquids using Wavelet method. The results estimated for multiple NPs shapes and validate the study with previous once. The accelerating fin under consistent magnetic field influence wetted with ternary nanofluid through ultraspherical Wavelet scheme was done by Shiralashetti et al. [21]. They evaluated that Peclet number faster the cooling process while permeability and Hartmann number accelerates the heat at the base surface. The LTNE and LTE model investigation and comparison of heat transfer with nanoparticles has been analyzed by Manaswini et al. [22]. It is noticed that LTNE model performs better for engineering thermal transport applications than LTE model. Further, the contribution of TiO2 and Al2O3 is observed very critical regarding the transport of heat. Wang et al. [23] preferred pin shaped fin for increasing the microchannel efficacy and found that changing functional fluids and fins structures highly alter the heat mechanism. Further, the study suggested 20 nm size and 0.04% concentration as better choices to achieve the optimum heat transport. Thermal estimation of fin under uniform movement by endorsing radial basis scheme was reported by Najafabadi [24]. The simulation shown 33.3% increase in Nr and Nc reduces the heat in the range of 4% to 10.5%.

The reviewing literature reveals that the studies have been presented regarding heat transfer in mechanical devices using multiple fin types with ternary, of simple nanofluids. These studies demonstrated that adding NPs enhances thermal conductivity and cooling or heating performance than traditional base fluids. However, many studies as discussed in the above literature are limited to ternary, or simple NPs system whose thermal capability remains limited due to low accumulative thermal conductivity. Moreover, the use of quint NPs to fin configuration, specifically the dovetail type fin has received little attention despite its potential applications. Further, the previous attempts have focused on computational or numerical investigations, providing restricted statistical insights into the single of combined effects of the model parameters. Thus, the optimized performance of the system remains inadequately explored. Thus, to address these limitations, the current study introduces a quint type nanomaterial comprising five different NPs to enhance thermal transport across dovetail fin. In addition, a deep statistical investigation is integrated with numerical scheme to quantify significance of the parameters, optimization properties, interaction effects, so providing a practically reliable assessment of heat transport performance. Hence, this attempt combined methodology not bridges the research gap but also helpful for designing better thermal management systems for engineering applications. Response surface technique

The RSM is an optimization scheme that handles the relations between difeferent model parameters to examine the ranges to acquire the desired optimum or minimum responses. It utilizes schemes like ANOVA or factorial procedure to enhnace the predictive capacity and responses thorugh plots. For the current problem, the RSM is implemented by adopting the subsequent steps.

Practical Applications The heat mechanism in dovetail fin under wetting, radiation, magnetic field, and quint nanofluid effects have promising applications in latest energy devices, nuclear reactor, heat exchangers, electronics and in solar thermal systems where efficient and rapid heat dissipation is important. The use of RSM empowers the heat optimization of the parameters, identify the interactive effects and provides the best operating conditions for improved heat transport and minimize the energy consumption and thermal losses.

Future directions Future work on the heat mechanism using dovetail fin configuration could be emphasized on multi components nanofluids using CNTs, oxides, ferrite, magnetite, bio and phase change nanomaterials for further enhanced thermal applications. Further, use of intelligent schemes, machine learning and computational studies with conjugate heat mechanism investigation can offer the high efficiency models. The transient heat transfer by incorporating variable properties, magnetic field, porous substrates will provide practical uses of quint nanofluids through dovetail fins for cooling technologies.
