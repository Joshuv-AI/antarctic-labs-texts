Correction to ‘USP37 counteracts HLTF to protect damaged replication forks and promote survival of BRCA1-deficient cells and PARP inhibitor resistance’
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: Mengfan Tang, Siting Li, Ziqiang Zhu, Chao Wang, Shuai Ding, Huimin Zhang, Min Huang, Sarah Keast, Zhen Chen, Ling Yin, Litong Nie, Dan Su, Xu Feng, Junjie Chen, USP37 counteracts HLTF to protect damaged replication forks and promote survival of BRCA1-deficient cells and PARP inhibitor resistance, Nucleic Acids Research, Volume 53, Issue 12, 8 July 2025, gkaf544, https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaf544

The originally published version of this manuscript contained errors under the ‘AID-tagging and dTAG-tagging of endogenous BRCA1’ heading.

In two instances, “FKBP1236V” has been corrected to read “FKBP12F36V”

In addition, the BRCA1 NKI gRNA sequence has been corrected to read: “5′- TTCATTGGAACAGAAAGAAA-3′” instead of: “5′-ATATATCTAAGCATTTGCAA-3′”.
