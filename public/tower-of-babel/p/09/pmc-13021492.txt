Correction to: Nature Food 10.1038/s43016-024-01109-4, published online 14 January 2025.

In the version of this article initially published, the affiliation shown for Isabel Valero Morales was incorrect. The affiliation should have appeared as “Instituto Nacional de Ciencias Médicas y Nutrición Salvador Zubirán, Mexico City, Mexico.” The error has been corrected in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
