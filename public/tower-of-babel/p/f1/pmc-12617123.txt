PA20226. Ergonomic assessment of work activities that pose a risk of occupational diseases
Authors: W Czernecka, M Butlewski, R Mierzwiak
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractPurposeThe aim of this abstract is to present an approach to ergonomic analyses at workstations where there is a risk of occupational disease, with particular attention paid to undertaking such activities in the context of corporate social responsibility and sustainable development of the organization.Design/Methodology/ApproachThe analysis was presented using the example of work stations where there is a risk of developing an occupational disease related to excessive workload on the wrists. Ergonomic methods of work assessment OCRA and Strain Index were used, and the assessment was supplemented with a work analysis using Neuron Mocap, which allowed for a more accurate assessment of the risk of occupational disease.FindingsThe results of the conducted studies led to the determination of the risk of occupational disease at the examined workplaces. It was also possible to propose corrective actions to limit the employee’s exposure. The data obtained can also be related to the context of taking into account the prevention of occupational diseases within the sustainable development strategy.Practical ImplicationsThe obtained research results and conclusions can serve as an example for companies on how to conduct ergonomic assessments at the most exposed work stations (with a high risk of occupational disease). Thus, it indicates a possible course of action in order to strive to ensure safe working conditions for employees (which is the goal of sustainable development of the organization).Originality/ValueThe article focuses on the aspect of corporate social responsibility in the context of searching for a way to reduce the occurrence of occupational diseases, which have long-term, negative professional and non-professional effects on employees. It presents the need to implement additional methods of job assessment (Neuron MoCap) and link the results of these analyses with real actions that should be taken at work stations.Key messages• Ergonomics can significantly support the management of occupational diseases, especially in the case of manual and highly repetitive work.• Ergonomic analyses of workstations can be an element supporting the prevention of occupational diseases and ensuring optimal working conditions for employees.TopicOccupational health and safety, occupational diseases, occupational health and safety management system.
