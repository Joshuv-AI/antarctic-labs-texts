Correction to: Impacts of diverse undersown cover crops on seasonal soil microbial properties
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
Using temporal data to demonstrate that toxin-encoding totivirus–satellite coinfections are unstable in natural populations of budding yeast from vineyards. Infections are frequently lost and gained.

This is a correction to: Rashmi Shrestha, Karoliina Huusko, Outi-Maaria Sietiö, Bernhard Schmid, Seraina Lisa Cappelli, Paula Thitz, Stephanie Gerin, Anna-Liisa Laine, Annalea Lohila, Jussi Heinonsalo, Impacts of diverse undersown cover crops on seasonal soil microbial properties, FEMS Microbiology Ecology, Volume 101, Issue 7, July 2025, https://doi.org/10.1093/femsec/fiaf068.

The following changes have been made to the originally published manuscript. The last name of the fifth author has been emended now to read: "Seraina Lisa Cappelli".
