Correction to: Epidemiology and Risk Factors of Norovirus Infections Among Diarrhea Patients Admitted to Tertiary Care Hospitals in Bangladesh
Published: 2026

In the original article “Epidemiology and Risk Factors of Norovirus Infections Among Diarrhea Patients Admitted to Tertiary Care Hospitals in Bangladesh” by Satter et al. [The Journal of Infectious Diseases, Volume 228, Issue 7, 1 October 2023, Pages 818-828, https://doi.org/10.1093/infdis/jiad274] the authors report an unintentional error in table 2 (Page 820). In the table, there has been an error in the frequency in the variable “Coinfection with norovirus + ≥1 other enteric pathogen” currently reading as

Coinfection with norovirus + ≥1 other enteric pathogenb96/111 (86)135/182 (74).001

which should change as,

Coinfection with norovirus + ≥1 other enteric pathogenb82/111 ( 74)123/182 ( 68)0.25

The corresponding result section in page 821 needs correction as well. The statement that begins as “Among norovirus-positive case patients and controls, both the relative distribution of GI and GII noroviruses were similar (Table 2); coinfections were more commonly identified among norovirus-positive case patients compared with norovirus-positive controls (86% vs 74%; P .001).” should read as “Among norovirus-positive case patients and controls, both the relative distribution of GI and GII noroviruses were similar (Table 2); coinfections were modestly and non-significantly increased in norovirus positive case patients compared with norovirus positive controls (74% vs 68%; P 0.25).”

This variable was significant previously, which after correction is now insignificant. This does not affect the conclusions of this article.
