IFPHOR0203 Associations between Urban Green and Blue Spaces (UGBS)’ Quality and Public Health in Greater Manchester
Authors: Jingru Yang, Christine Greenhalgh, Greg Williams, Arpana Verma
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractUrban Green and Blue Spaces (UGBS), as an important component of the natural environment, are increasingly recognised as a potential solution to health challenges associated with urban sprawl and global urbanisation. While the health benefits of UGBS are shaped by both quantity and quality, evidence on the specific quality attributes of UGBS and their relationship to public health outcomes remains limited.This study adopts a mixed-methods approach to develop a conceptual framework for defining and characterising UGBS quality, with a particular focus on public health relevance. The framework is applied to map UGBS quality across Greater Manchester and to examine its distribution in areas with varying levels of socioeconomic deprivation. It also explores the mechanisms through which UGBS quality influences individuals’ ability to derive health benefits.The study makes a novel contribution to the evidence base by advancing understanding of health-related dimensions of UGBS quality and identifying associated health-promoting pathways. It highlights spatial inequalities in UGBS quality and identifies areas where improvements may yield the greatest public health gains across and within boroughs. The findings provide evidence to inform healthier urban planning and more effective management of natural resources, supporting place-based strategies to reduce health inequalities.
