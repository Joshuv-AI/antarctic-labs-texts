Published: 2025

Retraction Note: Arch Gynecol Obstet (2013) 287:1067–1073 10.1007/s00404-012-2694-7

The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article. Following publication, concerns were raised regarding the data, specifically:

Concerns were raised regarding the validity of the data on maternal age, gestational age and duration of 1st and 2nd stage labour reported in Table 1;

Concerns were raised regarding the validity of the data on maternal age, gestational age and duration of 1st and 2nd stage labour reported in Table 1;

The weighted means of blood lactate reported in Table 3 appear not to match the total blood lactate reported in Table 2.

The weighted means of blood lactate reported in Table 3 appear not to match the total blood lactate reported in Table 2.

The author has not responded to requests from the Editor for a response to these concerns and has not provided their raw data for the study. The Editor has therefore lost confidence in the results and conclusions of this article.

The author did not respond to correspondence from the Publisher about this retraction.
