Correction: Single‑cell multi‑omics analysis reveals the mechanism of action of a novel antioxidant polyphenol nanoparticle loaded with STAT3 agonist in mediating cardiomyocyte ferroptosis to ameliorate age‑related heart failure
Authors: Haoyuan Zheng, Yuan Tian, Dongyu Li, Yanxiao Liang
Published: 2025

Correction: Journal of Nanobiotechnology (2025) 23:258

10.1186/s12951-025-03317-x

During figure preparation, the author identified that in Fig. 6B, the transmission electron microscopy (TEM) image representing the PN@Col group was incorrectly displayed. Specifically, due to a layout error, the 100 nm TEM image of the PN group was mistakenly placed in the PN@Col panel. This mistake occurred during figure assembly and does not affect the authenticity of the experimental results or the scientific conclusions of the study.

The correct TEM image for the PN@Col group in Fig. 6B has now been corrected in the original publication. For completeness and transparency, the correct and incorrect versions are displayed below. The original article has been corrected.
