Correction to ‘Development of a competitive immunoassay platform for quantifying the oligonucleotide products of nucleotide excision repair in UV-irradiated cells’
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: Geun Hoe Kim, Ha-Jeong Kwon, Youngmin Kim, Michael G Kemp, Jun-Hyuk Choi, Development of a competitive immunoassay platform for quantifying the oligonucleotide products of nucleotide excision repair in UV-irradiated cells, Nucleic Acids Research, Volume 54, Issue 5, 24 March 2026, gkag180, https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkag180

In the originally published version of this manuscript, there was an error in the affiliation information for the second co-author.

Ha Jeong-Kwon's affiliation has been corrected to read “Division of Chemical and Material Metrology, Korea Research Institute of Standards and Science, Daejeon 305-340, Republic of Korea” instead of “Department of Bio-Analytical Science, University of Science & Technology, Daejeon 305-340, Republic of Korea”.

The other affiliations have been re-numbered accordingly.

In addition, a funding source was mistakenly omitted. The funding section has been corrected to read:

“This work was supported in part by grants from the National Institute of General Medical Sciences [GM130583 to M.G.K.] and the Veterans Administration [I01CX002241 to M.G.K.], This work was also supported by the National Research Council of Science & Technology (NST) grant and the National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF) grant funded by the Korea government (MSIT) [No. CAP22041-100 and NRF-2020R1A2C1101575 to J.-H.C], and the Korea Research Institute of Standards and Science (KRISS) [GP2026-0020 and GP2025- 0007-1 to J-H.C.]. Funding to pay the Open Access publication charges for this article was provided by Wright State University funds”.

Instead of:

“This work was supported in part by grants from the National Institute of General Medical Sciences (GM130583 to M.G.K.) and the Veterans Administration (I01CX002241 to M.G.K.). This work was also supported by the National Research Council of Science & Technology (NST) grant and the National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF) grant funded by the Korea government (MSIT) (No. CAP22041-100 and NRF-2020R1A2C1101575 to J.-H.C), and the Korea Research Institute of Standards and Science (KRISS) (GP2025-0007-1 to J-H.C.). Funding to pay the Open Access publication charges for this article was provided by Wright State University funds”.
