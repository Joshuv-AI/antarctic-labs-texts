Editorial Expression of Concern: Cyclic versus continuous medroxyprogesterone acetate for treatment of endometrial hyperplasia without atypia: a 2-year observational study
Authors: Mohamed Emarh
Published: 2025

Editorial Expression of Concern: Arch Gynecol Obstet (2015) 292:1339–1343 10.1007/s00404-015-3749-3

The Editor-in-Chief is issuing an editorial Expression of Concern for this article. Concerns have been raised about the reporting of the study and the statistical analysis of the data to which the author has not provided a response. Readers are therefore advised to interpret the results and conclusions with caution. Mohamed Emarh has not responded to correspondence from the Publisher about this statement.
