The landscape of somatic mutations in lymphoblastoid cell lines
Authors: Madison Caballero, Amnon Koren
Published: 2023

(Cell Genomics 3, 100305; June 14, 2023)

In the originally published paper, the “Early” and “Late” labels underneath the replication timing histograms of Figures 1 and 2 were reversed relative to the x axes. During figure generation, the x axes were presented from late to early replication, but the labels, which were added at a later step, indicated the opposite by mistake. The data and text presented were otherwise accurate and remain unchanged. The article has been updated online so that the figures contain the correct axes orientation to match the labels. The authors apologize for the error.
