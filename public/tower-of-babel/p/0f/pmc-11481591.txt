Following publication of the original article, it came to the attention of the authors that there were errors in some units in Table 2, Table 3, and Figure 2 (some units were missing whiles others were incorrect). The tables and figure have since been corrected in the published article and the journal would like to refer readers to these new, correct versions of the tables and figure. The authors thank you for reading this erratum and apologize for any inconvenience caused.
