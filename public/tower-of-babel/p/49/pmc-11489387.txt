Strong B-T inverse
Authors: Sanzhang Xu, Yuyue Huang, Jinyong Wu, Ber-Lin Yu
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractNew-typed matrix inverses based on the Hartwig-Spindelböck decomposition were investigated, which is called the strong B-T inverse, as a generalization of the B-T inverse. The relationships between the above inverse and other matrix inverses were established. Several sufficient and necessary conditions of the strong B-T inverse are obtained via the column and null spaces.MSC: 15A09Keywords: Generalized inverse, B-T inverse, Hartwig-Spindelböck decomposition
