Authors: Abhay P. Sagare, Robert D. Bell, Zhen Zhao, Qingyi Ma, Ethan A. Winkler, Anita Ramanathan, Berislav V. Zlokovic
Published: 2023

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/ncomms3932, published online 13 December 2013

The original version of the Supplementary Information associated with this Article contained errors in Supplementary Figs. S1a, S3a, and S3b.

In Supplementary Fig. S1a (left panel), the representative image showing amyloid beta immunostaining in control APP ws/0 Pdgfrβ +/+ mouse was incorrect, owing to an image from a mouse with a different genotype being used in error during the preparation of the manuscript.
