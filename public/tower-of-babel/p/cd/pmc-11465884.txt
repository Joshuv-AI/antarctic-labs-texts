Correction: Oral Magnesium reduces levels of pathogenic autoantibodies and skin disease in murine lupus
Authors: Alberto Verlato, Teresina Laragione, Sofia Bin, Randie H Kim, Fadi Salem, Percio S Gulko, Paolo Cravedi
Published: 2024

Correction: BMC Immunology (2024) 25:58

10.1186/s12865-024-00650-y

Following publication of the original article [1], the title was incorrectly given as ‘Revised version with tracked changes oral Magnesium reduces levels of pathogenic autoantibodies and skin disease in murine lupus’ but should have been ‘Oral Magnesium reduces levels of pathogenic autoantibodies and skin disease in murine lupus’.

The original article has been corrected.
