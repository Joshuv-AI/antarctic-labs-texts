A machine-learning method for predicting the 1-year risk of death in maintenance hemodialysis patients based on continuous compliance with dialysis quality indicators
Authors: Jianhua Dong, Mingwei Zhang, Xi Yang, Chuan Li, Bian Wu, Yongchun Ge
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
ObjectiveTo establish a 1-year mortality risk prediction model for maintenance hemodialysis (HD) patients using machine learning method based on the continuous assessment methods of dialysis quality indicators.MethodsA single-center, retrospective cohort study. A total of 240 patients who received HD treatment at the JinLing Hospital in January 2015 were screened, and dialysis quality was assessed more than three times a year. The follow-up period ends in October 2022, and the endpoint is all-cause death. The indicator time-to-standard ratio and indicator fluctuation value were used as the evaluation methods for the continuous achievement of nine dialysis quality indicators.Dialysis quality indicators include interdialytic weight gain、pre-dialysis systolic blood pressure、hemoglobin、albumin、total carbon dioxide、calcium、phosphorus、parathyroid hormone and spKt/V.A prediction model for survival or death of HD patients after 1 year was constructed based on a machine learning algorithm, and the optimal probability threshold of the model was obtained.Shapley additive explanation (SHAP) values were used to measure the marginal contribution of each feature to the models.ResultsAfter 94 months of follow-up, 60 patients (25.0%) died. Six machine learning methods, KNN, RandomForest, ExtraTrees, XGBoost, AdaBoost and DecisionTree, are used to build prediction models based on the indicator time-to-standard ratio and the indicator fluctuation value. The ExtraTrees model based on the indicator time-to-standard ratio has the best prediction effect, with its accuracy, precision, recall, F1 score and area under the receiver operating curve reaching 0.92, 0.86, 0.96, 0.91 and 0.93 respectively, while confirming 0.65 as the optimal probability threshold for the model. Visualization of the TreeSHAP interpretation results of the prediction model helps physicians understand the global prediction mechanism of the model and explain the importance of a patient’s characteristic parameters in influencing the outcome of the patient.ConclusionThe indicators time-to-standard ratio and fluctuation value may serve as potential methods for the continuous reaching of dialysis quality. Preliminary evidence suggests that machine learning model based on the indicator time-to-standard ratio of dialysis quality could have a reasonably good predictive effect on the prognosis of HD patients.Supplementary InformationThe online version contains supplementary material available at 10.1186/s12882-025-04488-3.

The number of hemodialysis (HD) patients in China is growing rapidly. The total prevalence rate of HD is 597.7 per million people. By 2022, there are 844,265 HD patients and 156,645 new HD patients. Thanks to the continuous improvement of HD treatment rate and quality, as well as the improvement of medical security policies, the survival time of HD patients has significantly prolonged, with the average dialysis age reaching 4.5 years, but there is still a gap between China and high-quality dialysis countries. It is urgent to establish a prognosis prediction model for HD patients to provide reference for clinicians to formulate treatment plans, further reduce the risk of death of HD patients, and improve clinical prognosis. However, at present, there is a lack of evaluation methods for continuous reaching of dialysis quality indicators in clinical practice. It is rare to analyze the significance of continuous reaching of multiple dialysis quality indicators on the prognosis of HD patients, and a prediction model for the impact of continuous reaching of multiple dialysis quality indicators on the prognosis of HD patients has not been established. The machine learning method has more advantages in multidimensional and nonlinear analysis, which can improve the prediction performance of the model. It has been widely used in kidney disease research, and has also emerged in the field of HD [1–3]. Therefore, our study will introduce two evaluation methods for continuous reaching of dialysis quality indicators, and use machine learning method to establish the best prediction model for the impact of continuous reaching of dialysis quality indicators on prognosis.

Methods

Patient selection

A retrospective, single center cohort study. 276 end-stage renal disease patients who received regular HD treatment in the blood purification center of the JinLing Hospital in January 2015 were screened.

Inclusion criteria: (1) Age>18 years; (2) HD treatment for more than 3 months; (3) HD treatment was performed 3 times a week; (4) At least 3 dialysis quality assessments shall be completed every year.

Exclusion criteria: (1) Suffered from cancer (such as lung cancer, stomach cancer, colon cancer, bladder cancer, etc.); (2) Kidney transplantation during dialysis; (3) Transferred out of the blood purification center to other hospitals for HD treatment. 240 HD patients were finally included.

Related definitions

According to the Guidelines for Clinical Practice of Hemodialysis Adequacy in China, interdialytic weight gain (IDWG), Pre-dialysis systolic blood pressure (SBP), hemoglobin, serum albumin, blood total carbon dioxide (TCO2), serum calcium, serum phosphorus, serum parathyroid hormone (PTH), and single compartment urea clearance rate (spKt/v) as dialysis quality indicators, and the clinical therapeutic targets are shown in Table 1 [4]. The baseline values of patients were determined using the initial measurements obtained in 2015.Dialysis quality assessment was conducted quarterly (4 times annually) during the short interdialytic period.

Evaluation method of continuous compliance of dialysis quality indicators

The indicator time-to-standard ratio

A polynomial function was fitted to each dialysis quality index (Eq. 1), and the intersection of the function with the threshold of the compliance value was found.The horizontal coordinate of the intersection point is the starting point or the end point of the standardization, and the time interval between the end point and the starting point of the standardization is calculated, so as to obtain the effective length of the standardization of each physiological index.The time-to-standard ratio was obtained by calculating the length of achieved hours/length of recorded time period (Eq. 2, Fig. 1).

The indicator fluctuation value

The standard deviation of the dialysis quality indicator above or below the normal value interval of the indicator during the observation time period, i.e., the square of the difference between the abnormal value and the normal value was calculated, then summed and divided by the number of abnormal values, and then the arithmetic square root was calculated (Eq. 3, Fig. 1).

Clinical evaluation indexes

The patients’ age, gender, underlying diseases (hypertension, diabetes, coronary heart disease, etc.), dialysis age, vascular access, hemodialysis treatment mode, as well as IDWG, Pre-dialysis SBP, hemoglobin, serum albumin, blood TCO2, serum calcium, serum phosphorus, serum PTH, spKt/v, were retrieved from the medical record information system.

Follow-up and prognosis

The follow-up observation cutoff was until October 2022, and the endpoint event was all-cause mortality.

Statistical analysis

All data were statistically analyzed and processed using IBM SPSS 22 software (IBM SPSS Inc.,Chicago, USA). Normally distributed quantitative data are expressed as the mean ± standard deviation, and the independent-sample t test was used for comparisons between the two groups. Non-normally distributed quantitative data are expressed by the interquartile range, and the rank sum test (Mann-Whitney U Test) was used for comparisons between the two groups. Categorical data are expressed as percentages, and the chi-square test was used for comparisons between the two groups. p < 0.05 was considered statistically significant.

Results

Among 240 patients, 90 were female and 150 were male, with a median age of 57 (47,68) years. 156 cases were complicated with hypertension, 29 cases with diabetes and 19 cases with coronary heart disease. The median dialysis age was 75 (43,112) months. 206 patients were treated with HD and 34 patients with hemodiafiltration (HDF). AVF was used as vascular access in 227 cases and TCC in 13 cases.

Up to October 2022, 60 patients (25.0%) died. The age of the dead patients, the prevalence of diabetes and coronary heart disease were higher than those of the surviving patients (P < 0.05). There was no difference between survival and death patients in reaching the standard of baseline dialysis quality indicators (Table 2).

Establishment and performance evaluation of machine learning models

The age of patients, diabetes, coronary heart disease, time-to-standard ratio and fluctuation value of dialysis quality indicators were used as predictive indicators for predicting the prognosis of HD patients 1 year later.The data sets of survival and death patients were divided into two groups according to 4:1 by stratified sampling method, 192 cases were used as training sets (144 cases of survival and 48 cases of death) and 48 cases were used as test sets (36 cases of survival and 12 cases of death) (Table S1).

Six machine learning methods, KNN, RandomForest, ExtraTrees, XGBoost, AdaBoost and DecisionTree, were used to build prediction models respectively. In the prediction model of the time-to-standard ratio and fluctuation value of dialysis quality indicators, ExtraTrees model is superior to Logistic Regression and other models in accuracy, precision, recall and F1 score, and is lower than RandomForest and/or KNN model only in recall rate. The recall reflection model misjudges patients with good prognosis as death. The higher the recall, the lower the misjudgment rate. The precision reflects that the patients who died after the prognosis are not judged as alive. The higher the precision, the lower the rate of missed diagnosis. From the perspective of clinical application of the model, the consequences of missing judgment will be more serious than those of misjudgment. ExtraTrees model has a higher false positive rate than RandomForest model and KNN model, but ExtraTrees model performs best in accuracy, precision and F1 score. Therefore, ExtraTrees model was selected to predict the prognosis of HD patients (Tables 4 and 5).

Optimal probability values of prediction model

ExtraTrees machine learning model based on the time-to-standard ratio of dialysis quality indicators reaching the standard, the output prognosis prediction results of HD patients are probability values, and clinical research generally uses 0.5 as the probability threshold. But in clinical practice, 0.5 is not necessarily the best prediction probability threshold. Therefore, based on the variable threshold method in machine learning model, our research optimizes the model performance to improve the accuracy and stability of the prediction model. Figure 3 shows the accuracy of ExtraTrees model prediction between 0 and 1.0 probability thresholds, with the highest accuracy between 0.5 and 0.7. Through multi-dimensional verification of accuracy, precision, recall and F1 score, 0.65 can be used as the best probability threshold of the model (Table 6). If the output probability value of the prognosis prediction model based on the time-to-standard ratio of dialysis quality indicators reaching the standard is ≥ 0.65, that is, the clinical endpoint of HD patients is death one year later, and the probability value is < 0.65, that is, the clinical endpoint of HD patients is survival one year later.

Discussion

In 2015, based on the results of evidence-based medicine and referring to international guidelines, China put forward the Clinical Practice Guidelines for Hemodialysis Adequacy in China, and gave clinical practice guidance for nine dialysis quality indicators [4, 6]. According to the guidelines and the Standard Operating Procedures for Blood Purification, the National Clinical Medical Research Center for Renal Diseases conducts dialysis medical quality evaluation for HD patients every three months, and timely adjusts dialysis prescriptions and treatment plans. The baseline median dialysis age of HD patients has reached 75 months. Dialysis quality indicators of HD patients will change dynamically over time, but most clinical studies use cross-sectional data to evaluate the dialysis quality of dialysis centers and patients, but rarely longitudinal evaluation. Because there is a lack of quantitative evaluation means to quantify the continuous compliance of dialysis quality indicators in clinical practice. In our study, it is the first time to put forward the time-to-standard ratio and fluctuation value as the evaluation methods for dialysis quality to reach the standard continuously. The time-to-standard ratio of hemoglobin and blood phosphorus in HD patients in the center is low, so doctors should give priority to anemia correction and blood phosphorus control.The fluctuation value of blood parathyroid hormone and hemoglobin reaching the standard is high, which suggests that doctors should pay attention to the adjustment range of treatment drug dosage to avoid obvious fluctuation of parathyroid hormone and hemoglobin levels. The low time-to-standard ratio of hemoglobin may also be related to it. The time-to-standard ratio of dialysis quality indicators reaching the standard can be used as a method to evaluate the overall dialysis quality of the dialysis center, and the fluctuation value can indicate the stability of the treatment scheme.

In clinical practice, the baseline level of a single dialysis quality indicator is used to analyze and judge its impact on the prognosis of HD patients based on a linear model (Logistic regression model or Cox regression model) [7]. The dialysis quality indicators of HD patients will change with the improvement and adjustment of treatment methods, and the relationship between data will also change dynamically. Traditional statistical methods have limitations in highly complex multi factor analysis, while machine learning allows updating models to incorporate new data and capture changes in the relationship between features. The existing prognosis prediction model of HD patients constructed by machine learning method, the research population is mostly initial HD patients, and the research data are mostly from the baseline data before or after HD [8–13]. At present, there is no research to establish a predictive model of the impact of multiple indicators of continuous attainment on the prognosis of HD patients.

In our study, the indicators time-to-standard ratio and fluctuation value of dialysis quality were used as predictors of 1-year prognosis of HD patients, and a prognosis prediction model of HD patients based on machine learning was established. ExtraTrees model based on the time-to-standard ratio has the best prediction effect, with its accuracy, precision, recall, F1 score and AUC reaching 0.92, 0.86, 0.96, 0.91 and 0.93 respectively. Secondly, our study calculated the optimal probability threshold of the prediction model for HD patients, and confirmed that 0.65 can be used as the optimal probability threshold of the prediction model from multiple indicator dimensions. Finally, our study uses TreeSHAP algorithm to interpret the prediction model globally and make a visual display to help clinicians understand the prediction mechanism of the model and explain the importance of a patient’s characteristic parameters on the prognosis. The model is based on dialysis quality indicators that are easily available in clinic, which can help doctors classify high-risk patients, and help doctors to formulate targeted intervention strategies in time to improve the prognosis of patients.

Although this study confirmed the application prospect of dialysis quality indicators to predict the prognosis of HD patients, there are some limitations. First of all, the training data is based on retrospective data from a single center. The number of included samples is small, and there is a lack of external validation. Central effects cannot be excluded. Secondly, the study only evaluated the 1-year mortality rate of patients, and the long-term mortality risk is also important for HD patients. Finally, the study did not use treatment intervention data. Therefore, the research team plans to carry out a multicenter study to verify the reliability and generalization of the model, and further build a 2-year and 5-year mortality risk prediction model for HD patients.

Conclusion

The indicators time-to-standard ratio and fluctuation value may serve as potential methods for the continuous reaching of dialysis quality. Preliminary evidence suggests that machine learning model based on the indicator time-to-standard ratio of dialysis quality could have a reasonably good predictive effect on the prognosis of HD patients.
