AIIT-04 VIRTUAL AND AUGMENTED REALITY FOR NEUROSURGERY TRAINEES AND SURGICAL PROCEDURES IN GHANA
Authors: Samuel Kaba Akoriyea, Mariela Infante Paez, Emmanuel Yao Voado, Jonathan Oppong, Al-hassan Dasana Andani
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractIntroduction Virtual reality (VR) is a simulated experience that employs pose tracking and 3D near-eye displays to give the user an immersive feel of a virtual world. Augmented reality (AR) is an interactive experience in which a real-world environment is enhanced with computer-generated visual elements, sounds, and other stimuli. AR has been researched since 90’s and raises a great interest in the health sector.ObjectivesTo describe the benefits of VR and RA in patients with the diagnosis of brain and spine lesions.MethodA review of the medical literature on virtual reality and reality augmented applied to neurological surgery was made. The application of VR and AR in patients with a diagnosis of a brain tumor and severe spine degenerative disease are described.FindingsPatients safety and quality of care are improved as the lesions are well visualized before, during and after surgery. Patients and relatives are more confident of the procedure and participate actively with keen interest and understanding. Trainees have a 3D understanding of the lesions and able to comprehend the surgical procedures beyond the normal 2D radiology images. The surgeon is able to preplan and surgically operate on patient in the virtual world. Surgeons can equally work together from different geographical locations in the preplanning, trans operative and postoperative phases. Postoperative residual lesions are also better appreciated.ConclusionVR and AR technology in neurosurgery can boost the confidence of neurosurgeons in performing delicate and complex neurosurgical procedures. Surgeons perform better preplanning and transoperative procedures. Virtual and augmented reality reduces surgical accidents and increases patients and family’s confidence and understanding of the disease.
