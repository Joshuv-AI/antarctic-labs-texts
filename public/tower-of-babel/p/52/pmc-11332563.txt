Response to comment on “A bibliometric analysis of vaccination against atherosclerosis”
Authors: Bochao Jia, Rui Wei, Chenlu Yuan, Tao Cheng, Shuai Shi, Yuguang Chu, Yuanhui Hu
Published: 2024

Dear Editor,

We have reviewed the comments on our recently published manuscript “A bibliometric analysis of vaccination against atherosclerosis”1 provided by Xu et al. We greatly appreciate their critical reading and comments and have made revisions based on their suggestions to enhance the rigor and scientific accuracy of the paper.

Firstly, to ensure the expression of the article is more accurate and to avoid misunderstandings, we have revised a sentence under the subheading “Publication and Citation Summary.” The phrase “a total of 919 keywords are involved” has been corrected to “a total of 919 Author keywords are involved.”

Secondly, there was a data error under the subheading “Analysis of Journals and Most Cited Publications.” The sentence “The top 10 journals collectively contributed 91 papers, representing approximately 20% of the total publications” should be corrected to “The top 10 journals collectively contributed 114 papers, representing approximately 25% of the total publications.”

We apologize for the careless wording and data errors in the paper and thank them again for their valuable comments.
