Correction: Multiple objectives escaping bird search optimization and its application in stock market prediction based on transformer model
Authors: Dedai Wei, Zimo Wang, Minyu Qiu, Juntao Yu, Jiaquan Yu, Yurun Jin, Xinye Sha, Kaichen Ouyang
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-88883-8, published online 17 February 2025

The original version of this Article contained an error in the Reference list. Reference 35 was omitted and is listed below:

Shahrouzi, M. & Kaveh, A. An efficient derivative-free optimization algorithm inspired by avian life-saving manoeuvres. Journal of Computational Science 57, 101483 (2022).

As a result, in the Introduction,

“First, the Escaping Bird Search (EBS) is a unique heuristic optimization method built upon the behavior of bird flocks evading predators in nature6,7.”

Now reads:

“First, the Escaping Bird Search (EBS), originally proposed by Shahrouzi35, is a unique heuristic optimization method built upon the behavior of bird flocks evading predators in nature6,7.”

In the legend of Figure 4,

“A swift (a) utilizing wing beat-rate during straight flight, (b) in a turning maneuver.”

now reads:

“A swift (a) utilizing wing beat-rate during straight flight, (b) in a turning maneuver, reproduced from Ref 35 with permission from Elsevier.”

In the legend of Figure 5,

“Aerial escape maneuvers in a prey-predator bird system (The dashed line represents the predator’s path immediately after missing the prey).”

now reads:

“Aerial escape maneuvers in a prey-predator bird system (The dashed line represents the predator’s path immediately after missing the prey), reproduced from Ref 35 with permission from Elsevier.”

The original Article has been corrected.
