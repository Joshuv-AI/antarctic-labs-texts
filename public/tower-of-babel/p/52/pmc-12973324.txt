In Figure S2C, the DAPI image of the cochlear Apical in the cisplatin‐treated group does not correspond to the DAPI channel (blue) in the final merged image. This error occurred due to a mistake during image layout arrangement. The correct layout is shown below. We would like to emphasize that this error does not affect any of the study's findings or final conclusions, and it allows for any reproducibility experiments to confirm the results.
