Based on electronic nose and multi-omics, investigate the dynamic changes of volatile and non-volatile organic compounds in waxy wheat Baijiu from different years
Authors: Ping Zhang, Jun-na Liu, Han-xue Li, Yi Ma, Zhi-en Pu, Li Li, Liu-bin Huang, Shan Zhang, Xu-qin Wang, Guo-fei Jiang, Ling-yuan Zhang, Yu-tao Bai, Peng Qin
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractChinese baijiu is highly regarded for its unique flavor, and a variety of crops can be utilized as raw materials in its production. Waxy crops are essential ingredients in the brewing of high-quality baijiu; however, there is currently no comprehensive identification of volatile organic compounds (VOCs) and non-volatile compounds (N-VOCs) in waxy wheat baijiu (WWB). This study aims to investigate the dynamic changes of VOCs and N-VOCs in WWB during several important time periods from new to aged. A total of 25 amino acids underwent changes in the samples, with numerous physiologically active beneficial amino acids showing significant accumulation after aging. Additionally, 517 VOCs changed after aging, predominantly comprising esters and terpenoids, with 72 major VOCs being identified. A total of 718 metabolites were identified in the metabolome, primarily comprising alterations in lipids, amino acids, phenolic acids, organic acids, and alkaloids. These metabolites significantly influenced the levels of amino acids and VOCs. Our study is the first to provide a comprehensive examination of these aspects of WWB, highlighting its unique advantages over other crops. We believe that this research will establish a theoretical foundation for the application of waxy wheat in the baijiu industry, improve baijiu quality, and promote the development of functional baijius.Keywords: Waxy wheat baijiu, Electronic nose, Amino acids, Volatile organic compounds, Metabolomics

Introduction

Chinese baijiu is one of the world's most famous distilled spirits, known for its unique flavor profile. The production process involves fermentation, distillation, aging, and blending. Typically, its ethanol content ranges between 40 % and 55 % (Jin et al., 2017). Baijiu is unique due to its ingredients, production process, and style characteristics, which contribute to a rich aroma and a taste that is smooth, sweet, mellow and refreshing (Xu et al., 2022). The manufacturing techniques from different regions vary slightly, but there are usually three processes: fermentation, distillation, and aging (Zhu et al., 2016). Compared to other alcoholic beverages, the production characteristics of baijiu allow for the use of sorghum, rice, wheat, millet, or even tubers as raw materials (Liu & Sun, 2018). The special manufacturing process produces the unique rich flavor of baijiu, and the taste (sourness, bitterness, saltiness, sweetness) is an important factor in determining the quality of baijiu (Jin et al., 2017).

The taste of the new baijiu is spicy and stimulating; an aging process is required to mellow the flavor (Cheng et al., 2022; Niu et al., 2022). Therefore, new baijiu typically requires aging for several months to several years to eliminate unpleasant odors. Throughout the aging process, baijiu undergoes a series of intricate physicochemical reactions in the container, including oxidation, esterification, and hydrolysis. In addition to storage duration, the quality of liquor is significantly influenced by the choice of raw materials. Different raw materials can yield distinct flavor compounds, making it essential to investigate the quality of baijiu produced from various crops. While rice, wheat, soybeans, corn, tubers, and potatoes are commonly used in baijiu production, research on WWB is notably scarce. Specifically, the unique properties that distinguish WWB apart from other brewing ingredients have yet to be thoroughly explored. Furthermore, although the critical timeframes for transitioning from new baijiu to aged baijiu and for long-term preservation have been addressed, the optimal storage duration for WWB remains unexamined (Xiao et al., 2015).

Currently, the strategies used to identify and evaluate baijiu from different years can be divided into two categories: sensory evaluation and instrumental analysis. The results of sensory evaluation lack objectivity and reproducibility to some extent, as they can easily be influenced by external environmental conditions and the physical and mental state of the analysts (Huo et al., 2014). In terms of instrumental analysis, gas chromatography II or gas chromatography coupled with mass spectrometry (GC–MS), high-performance liquid chromatography, atomic absorption spectroscopy, and various spectroscopic techniques have been extensively utilized to analyze the characteristics of baijiu (Capone et al., 2015; Galgano et al., 2008; Han et al., 2016; Wang et al., 2015). Traditional GC–MS has become a widely used analytical tool for monitoring changes in VOCs during storage. It is employed to monitor the changes in VOCs during baijiu aging to determine the baijiu's age (Xiao et al., 2015). Metabolomics, as a powerful method, is suitable for analyzing the complete chemical composition of baijiu. It utilizes multivariate statistical analysis methods and efficient metabolite detection techniques to explore new features or describe differences between samples (Li et al., 2020). Therefore, it is widely used in the analysis of food and beverage ingredients (Herrero et al., 2012). Studying amino acid metabolites quantitatively and qualitatively is crucial for comprehending the amino acids and their metabolic status in beverages. The analysis of amino acids and their metabolites using the LC-MS/MS platform has been validated as an efficient method for analyzing unmodified amino acids in food samples (Guo et al., 2013). In recent years, the development of electronic noses has received increasing attention due to being a non-destructive and rapid method. In addition to being efficient and cost-effective, electronic noses have also been proven to perform well in the analysis of VOCs in various foods (Rizzolo et al., 2013; Peng et al., 2015). Previous research on baijiu storage has predominantly concentrated on volatile and flavor compounds, leaving a notable gap in the investigation of fundamental metabolic substances and functional amino acid components, particularly in relation to their alterations in WWB.

This study integrates an electronic nose (E-nose) system for the comprehensive identification and analysis of VOCs and N-VOCs following the storage of WWB, which, to our knowledge, is the first of its kind. Metabolomics revealed significantly upregulated accumulated metabolites and identified VOCs that play crucial roles in three key sensory aspects. Additionally, several amino acids beneficial for health and enhancing the quality of baijiu were discovered. This study aims to elucidate the dynamic changes of VOCs and N-VOCs during the aging process of WWB and to understand the mechanisms of aging and material change patterns in baijiu. It provides a reference for the optimal storage duration of WWB baijiu and opens new avenues for future in-depth research. Furthermore, establishing a benchmark for the optimal storage duration of WWB and exploring the substantial contributions of waxy wheat to baijiu production are critical areas for further research and development. While this study has enriched our understanding of the flavor chemistry of WWB, it is imperative to place greater emphasis on the regulation and synthesis of flavor quality by functional microbiota during brewing and fermentation processes. Despite certain limitations inherent in this study, it is essential to pursue additional research to fully harness the brewing potential of waxy wheat.

Materials and methods

Materials and samples

Four different storage year baijius were analyzed, including N (0 years), S1 (1 year), S2 (3 years), and S3 (5 years). All the baijiu samples were produced using the widely cultivated Dianmai 2 variety and followed the same traditional brewing process. Dianmai 2 is a variety of waxy wheat used in the production of baijiu. It was crossbred with Jan-818 and WxFan 9 in 2018. This variety features a white husk and hard white grains. The protein content is 13.44 %, while the wet gluten content is 28.2 %. The sedimentation value measures 13.5 mL, and the water absorption rate is 74.2 %. The stability time is 0.7 min, with a maximum tensile resistance of 0 EU, a stretching area of 0.0 square centimeters, and an elongation of 0.0 mm. The hardness index is 65.0, and amylose constitutes 1.94 % of the dry weight of the sample. Before further analysis, the baijiu should be stored in a sealed ceramic container away from light at room temperature.

Chemicals, reagents and omics data analysis

HPLC-grade acetonitrile (ACN) and methanol (MeOH) were purchased from Merck (Darmstadt, Germany). MilliQ water (Millipore, Bradford, USA) was used in all experiments. All of the standards were purchased from Sigma-Aldrich (St. Louis, MO, USA). Ammonium acetate and formic acid were bought from Sigma-Aldrich (St. Louis, MO, USA). Analytical grade sodium chloride were purchased from Sinopharm Chemical Reagent Co., Ltd. n-hexane (chromatographic pure) were obtained from MERCK (Shanghai, China). The stock solutions of standards were prepared at the concentration of 1 mg/mL in MeOH. All stock solutions were stored at −20 °C. The stock solutions were diluted with MeOH to working solutions before analysis. Unsupervised PCA (principal component analysis) was performed by statistics function prcomp within R (www.r-project.org). The data was unit variance scaled before unsupervised PCA. The HCA (hierarchical cluster analysis) results of samples and metabolites were presented as heatmaps with dendrograms, while pearson correlation coefficients (PCC) between samples were caculated by the cor function in R and presented as only heatmaps. Both HCA and PCC were carried out by R package pheatmap. For HCA, normalized signal intensities of metabolites (unit variance scaling) are visualized as a color spectrum. Significantly regulated metabolites between groups were determined by absolute Log2FC (fold change). For two-group analysis, differential metabolites were determined by VIP (VIP > 1) and absolute Log2FC (|Log2Fold Change| ≥ 1.0). VIP values were extracted from OPLS-DA result, which also contain score plots and permutation plots, was generated using R package MetaboAnalystR. The data was log transform (log2) andmean centering before OPLS-DA. In order to avoid overfitting, a permutation test (200 permutations) was performed. The predictive parameters of the OPLS-DA evaluation model include R2X, R2Y, and Q2. R2X and R2Y represent the proportion of variance explained by the model for the X and Y matrices, respectively, while Q2 indicates the model's predictive ability. The closer these three indicators are to 1, the more stable and reliable the model becomes. A Q2 value greater than 0.5 is considered indicative of an effective model, while a Q2 value exceeding 0.9 signifies an excellent model.

Detection of metabolites based on UPLC-MS/MS platform

According to the method developed by Chen et al., metabolites in 12 samples were detected using the UPLC-MS/MS platform (Chen et al., 2013). Remove the samples from the −80 °C refrigerator, thaw, and vortex for 30 S to mix. Take 9 mL of the sample, place it in the corresponding numbered 50 mL centrifuge tube, freeze it in the −80 °C refrigerator overnight, and vacuum freeze dry. After freeze-drying, add 70 % methanolic internal standard extract at a ratio of 30 times the concentration, e.g. add 300 μL extractant to 9 mL samples after freeze-drying and 200 μL extractant to 6 mL samples after freeze-drying. Vortex for 15 min and sonicate in an ice water bath (KQ5200E) for 10 min. 12,000 r/min and centrifuge at 4 °C (5424R, Eppendorf) for 3 min. The supernatant was removed, filtered through a microporous membrane (0.22 μm pore size), and stored in the injection vial for LC-MS/MS detection. For two-group analysis, differential accumulated metabolites (DAMs) were identified based on VIP (VIP > 1) and absolute Log2FC (|Log2FC| ≥ 1.0). Detailed information can be found in the Supplementary Materials 1 (Metabolomics analysis).

Detection of amino acids and their metabolites

According to the method developed by Le et al., amino acids and their metabolites were detected in 12 samples using the LC-MS/MS platform (Le et al., 2014). An amount of 0.5 mL of the sample was freeze-dried, and then mixed with 250 μL of 20 % acetonitrile/methanol.The sample was vortexed for 3 min under the condition of 2500 r/min and centrifuged at 12,000 r/min for 10 min at 4 °C.Take the supernatant into a new centrifuge tube and place the supernatant in −20 °C refrigerator for 30 min, Then the supernatant was centrifuged again at 12,000 r/min for 10 min at 4 °C. After centrifugation, transfer 180 μL of supernatan for further LC-MS analysis. Other detailed steps are described in the Supplementary Materials 1 (Detection of amino acids and their derivatives).

Analysis of volatile metabolic profiles

According to the method developed by Yuan et al., the volatile metabolic profiles of 12 samples were analyzed using a GC–MS/MS detection platform and a custom-built database (Yuan et al., 2022). Samples were ground to a powder in liquid nitrogen. 500 mg (1 mL) of the powder was transferred immediately to a 20 mL head-space vial (Agilent, Palo Alto, CA, USA), containing NaCl saturated solution, to inhibit any enzyme reaction. The vials were sealed using crimp-top caps with TFE‑silicone headspace septa (Agilent). At the time of SPME analysis, each vial was placed in 60 °C for 5 min, then a 120 μm DVB/CWR/PDMS fibre (Agilent) was exposed to the headspace of the sample for 15 min at 60 °C. After sampling, desorption of the VOCs from the fibre coating was carried out in the injection port of the GC apparatus (Model 8890; Agilent) at 250 °C for 5 min in the splitless mode. The identification and quantification of VOCs was carried out using an Agilent Model 8890 GC and a 7000E mass spectrometer (Agilent), equipped with a 30 m × 0.25 mm × 0.25 μm DB-5MS (5 % phenyl-polymethylsiloxane) capillary column. Helium was used as the carrier gas at a linear velocity of 1.2 mL/min. The injector temperature was kept at 250 °C. The oven temperature was programmed from 40 °C (3.5 min), increasing at 10 °C/min to 100 °C, at 7 °C/min to 180 °C, at 25 °C/min to 280 °C, hold for 5 min. Mass spectra was recorded in electron impact (EI) ionisation mode at 70 eV. The quadrupole mass detector, ion source and transfer line temperatures were set, respectively, at 150, 230 and 280 °C. The MS was selected ion monitoring (SIM) mode was used for the identification and quantification of analytes. Detailed information can be found in the Supplementary Materials 1 (Volatile metabolic profiles).

E-nose instrument and data acquisition

The E-nose system used in this study is the PEN3 portable electronic nose produced by the German company Airsense (Airsenseanalytics, GmbH, Schwerin, Germany). This system mainly consists of functional modules such as a sensor array, signal processing module, and pattern recognition system. The performance of the 10 metal oxide gas-sensitive sensors is shown in Table 1, with different detection limits for gases of various properties. Its working principle simulates biological olfaction, where odor molecules are adsorbed by the sensors, and the generated signals are processed and transmitted, ultimately leading to a judgment made by the pattern recognition system (Wei et al., 2015). Detailed information can be found in the Supplementary Materials 1 (E-Nose data acquisition).

Statistical analysis

The experiments were repeated at least three times. The data representation was completed using Microsoft software from 2010, which presents the average and its corresponding standard deviation (SD). The significant differences between the samples were analyzed using an analysis of variance (ANOVA), followed by Duncan's multiple range tests, utilizing SPSS Statistics 21 (SPSS Inc., Chicago, Illinois, USA). Results with P < 0.05 were considered statistically significant.

Results

E-nose response of WWB

A supervised OPLS-DA model was used to identify the variables responsible for classification. As shown in Fig. S1a, the contribution rates of PC1 and PC2 are 70.25 % and 28.68 %, respectively, with a cumulative contribution rate of 98.93 %, indicating that the data can effectively reflect the overall information of the samples. There is a distinction between the new baijiu and the old baijiu, and the variation among the 3-year-old baijius is notable, particularly the baijiu from the first year, which is markedly different from the other two types. Notably, the baijiu samples stored for three and five years are distinctly different from the new and one-year-old baijiu, and they are much more similar to each other. It can be seen from Fig. S1b that the classification parameters are Q2 = 0.989 and R2Y = 0.996. The P values for Q2 and R2Y are both 0.005, indicating the reliability of the model. After 200 permutations, the intercept value of Q2Y = −1.053 (Fig. S1c). The negative intercept of Q2Y indicates the robustness of the model, showing a low risk of overfitting. From Fig. S2, it can be seen that the responses of the five sensors W5S, W1W, W2S, W1S, and W2W to the baijiu samples are relatively high, while the responses of the other five sensors are lower, with no significant differences among the baijiu samples; the new baijiu has the highest response on the W5S sensor. The same results are also presented in Table S1.

Amino acid metabolic profiling of WWB

To understand the changes in amino acids in differently aged baijiu, amino acid metabolic profiling was conducted on the samples (Table S2). As shown in Fig. 1, the amino acids in baijiu stored for 1 to 5 years changed significantly compared to new baijiu. Moreover, the accumulation of these amino acids is primarily upregulated after storage. PC1 reached 80.4 %, clearly distinguishing between new and aged baijiu, and also separating the new baijiu samples from each other, indicating good reproducibility of our data and reliable results (Fig. S3). However, similar to the results from the E-nose, the samples aged for three and five years were much more closely aligned. It can also be noted that, except for l-arginine and l-glutamic acid, all other amino acids were upregulated and accumulated after aging.

A total of 28 amino acids were detected in the samples, among which 25 showed significant changes (Table S3 and Table S4). In the comparison of S1 vs N, there were 22 amino acids (21 upregulated, 1 downregulated); in S2 vs N, there were 22 amino acids (21 upregulated, 1 downregulated); and in S1 vs N, there were 24 amino acids (21 upregulated, 3 downregulated). The amino acids l-tyrosine, l-ornithine, l-pipecolic acid, l-lysine, succinic acid, l-asparagine anhydrous, l-threonine, l-alanine, d-alanyl-d-alanine, l-histidine, 3-aminoisobutanoic acid, l-citrulline, γ-aminobutyric acid, kynurenic acid, and n6-acetyl-l-lysine were not present in the new baijiu but significantly increased after storage. Urea, on the other hand, was present in the new baijiu but disappeared after storage. The Venn analysis (Fig. S4) also reveals that there are 21 common amino acids between different aged baijiu and new baijiu, with no shared amino acids among different storage years. When comparing 3 years and 5 years to 1 year, there are 2 metabolites (n6-acetyl-l-lysine and kynurenic acid) and 3 metabolites (l-cystathionine, n6-acetyl-l-lysine, and kynurenic acid) respectively. However, when comparing 3 years and 5 years, there is only 1 metabolite (l-cystathionine).

K-means analysis (Table S5) reveals that all amino acids are categorized into 10 clusters, with cluster 7 containing the most, comprising 10 amino acids. Clusters 1, 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10 generally exhibit varying degrees of increase after storage, while cluster 1 shows the opposite trend. In other words, l-cystathionine, urea, and l-glutamine decrease or become negligible after being stored for at least a year, whereas l-histidine, l-glutamic acid, 3-aminoisobutanoic acid, γ-aminobutyric acid, l-tyrosine, l-ornithine, l-lysine, l-asparagine anhydrous, l-valine, l-proline, l-phenylalanine, l-citrulline, l-isoleucine, l-leucine, l-pipecolic acid, l-alanine, d-alanyl-d-alanine, methionine sulfoxide, and l-threonine increase after being stored for at least a year (Fig. S5). Fig. S6 displays the amino acids that have changed significantly compared to the new baijiu. it is evident that l-proline, l-leucine, l-phenylalanine, l-valine, l-isoleucine, l-glutamic acid, and methionine sulfoxide all significantly increased after storage compared to the new baijiu, especially l-proline and l-leucine. However, after 5 years of storage, l-glutamine has noticeably decreased. In summary, many beneficial amino acids, such as l-alanine, γ-aminobutyric acid, and l-threonine, will also accumulate during storage (Table S6).

Dynamic changes of VOCs after WWB storage

In order to understand the changes in VOCs over different years, volatile metabolomics analysis was conducted on the samples (Table S7). As shown in Fig. S7, there are significant differences in theVOCs between new baijiu and old baijiu. After storage, the VOCs content generally exhibited an upward trend. The PCA score plot (Fig. S8) also indicates that PCA1 explains 55.84 % of the variance in the dataset, while PCA2 explains 12.13 %. It is clear that new baijiu and old baijiu are well distinguished from each other, and old baijius are also differentiated from one another, showing good reproducibility. This indicates that our data performs well, and the subsequent analysis results are stable and reliable.

As shown in Table S8, a total of 517 differentially accumulated VOCs were identified, with the top three categories being terpenes (131), esters (97), and heterocyclic compounds (78). Furthermore, the S1 vs N group exhibited 420 compounds (112 downregulated, 308 upregulated); the S2 vs N group had 410 compounds (149 downregulated, 261 upregulated); and the S3 vs N group had 412 compounds (135 downregulated, 277 upregulated). It is evident that in each group, VOCs in old baijiu are predominantly upregulated compared to new baijiu. Particularly noteworthy are aromatic compounds (styrene), heterocyclic compounds (8-azabicyclo[3.2.1]octane, 3-chloro-8-methyl-, 2,2′-bifuran, octahydro-), and esters (3-phenyl-1-propanol, acetate). Following storage, the upregulation multiples are all greater than 5. The downregulation multiples for ketone (5-methyl-(e)-2-hepten-4-one) and aldehyde (benzeneacetaldehyde, alpha.-ethylidene-) are both greater than 4.

K-means analysis (Table S9) reveals that all the differential metabolites are categorized into 6 clusters. Among them, cluster 3 (a total of 161 metabolites, including 60 terpenes, 20 esters, 18 heterocyclic compounds, and 17 ketones) shows a gradual decline over time, eventually stabilizing; clusters 4 and 6, with 243 and 56 metabolites respectively, exhibit a pattern of initial increase followed by decrease, with the former mainly comprising esters and the latter primarily composed of esters and terpenoids; cluster 5 (a total of 23 metabolites, including 6 esters, 4 terpenes, and 4 heterocyclic compounds) demonstrates a gradual increase over time (Fig. S9).

Venn analysis also indicates that there are a total of 330 VOCs after storage, among which the top ten include 76 terpenoids, 68 esters, 49 heterocyclic compounds, 27 ketones, 24 aldehydes, 21 aromatics, 18 alcohols, 10 amines, 8 phenols, and 8 acids (Table S10). Compared to the new baijiu, there are 38 unique to the first year, 26 to the second year, and 22 to the third year. There are no shared metabolites among the three different storage durations. However, there are 23 VOCs that are shared between one and three years of storage(Fig. 2).

The bar chart illustrating the variations in VOCs among different groups indicates that the predominant compounds in aged baijiu are esters, terpenes, heterocycles, alcohols, aldehydes, and aromatics (Fig. S10). The dissipated compounds are primarily concentrated in esters, terpenes, ketones, and heterocyclic compounds. Comparing 1-year and 3-year old baijiu, the significant changes occur in terpenes, ketones, esters, and aldehydes. When comparing 1-year and 5-year old baijiu, the key changes are in terpenes, ketones, and heterocyclic compounds. Lastly, when comparing 3-year and 5-year old baijiu, the main changes are in terpenes and heterocyclic compounds (Table S11). The flavor wheel illustrating the differences between groups is shown in Fig. S11. The flavor substances identified after various storage periods include fruity, green, sweet, woody, herbal, floral, waxy, spicy, rose, fatty, and tropical. Among these, fruity, green, and sweet are the top three sensory flavors with the highest number of identified VOCs. After storage, each group contained more than 40 VOCs. It is notable that tropical was identified after one year of storage (Fig. S11a) but not in the third and fifth years, while fatty was identified in the third and fifth years but not in the first year (Fig. S11bc). Similarly, the predominant flavors among the aged baijiu stored for different durations are sweet, fruity, and fatty.

As depicted in Table 2, the top ten compounds identified as fruity after one year of storage are all esters. Among the top 20, butanoic acid, 3-methyl-, propyl ester, propanoic acid, hexyl ester, and propanoic acid, hexyl ester demonstrate a decrease in accumulation during the staged process. The green flavor exhibits the highest concentration of esters, followed by aldehydes and terpenoids, albeit in smaller quantities, with some of them also showing decreased levels. In contrast, while esters in the sweet flavor are also abundant, they are not as dominant as the first two. Additionally, there is an increased accumulation of ketones. It is evident that among these three flavors, esters have the most significant impact. It is evident that esters play a significant role in shaping the flavor profile of baijiu.

Metabolomic analysis of WWB

To identify the differences in metabolites over different years, a metabolomics analysis was conducted on the samples, resulting in the detection of 718 differential metabolites (Table S12). The clustering heatmap of these metabolites shows a significant accumulation of many metabolites after storage (Fig. S12a). To gain an overview of the detected metabolites, we conducted PCA. In the PCA score plot, the four sample groups are situated in distinct regions, making it easy to differentiate between them. The three- and five-year samples were more similar. There are evident variations in metabolites among different treatments and years (Fig. S12b), primarily showing upregulation after storage. The metabolite abundance cloud plot (Fig. S13) further illustrates that older baijius exhibit a higher abundance of metabolites compared to newer baijius, with the average abundance also being greater than that of newer baijius.

We define metabolites with VIP > 1 as significantly different, and identified a total of 491 DAMs (Table S13). These mainly include several categories: lipids (98), amino acids and their derivatives (92), others (74), phenolic acids (55), organic acids (54), and alkaloids (53) (Fig. 3). The lipid category primarily consists of free fatty acids and glycerol esters, while the others mainly include saccharides. Flavonoids are primarily flavones. In the comparison of S1 vs N, there are 417 metabolites (85 downregulated, 332 upregulated); S2 vs N has 408 metabolites (72 downregulated, 336 upregulated); and S3 vs N has 394 metabolites (7 downregulated, 317 upregulated) (Fig. S14). It is noteworthy that compared to the new baijiu, all the old baijius show varying degrees of changes in amino acids and their derivatives, lipids, and alkaloids. In particular, amino acids and their derivatives (l-lysine-butanoic acid and n-methyl-α-aminoisobutyric acid) and lipids (choline alfoscerate) have been upregulated and accumulated over different years of storage, reaching up to 10 times. Conversely, the free amino acids (12S-HHT) and nucleotides (cordycepin (3′-deoxyadenosine)*) in lipids have significantly decreased by up to 7 times.

Discussions

WWB's E-nose analysis system

In our research, the new baijiu showed the highest response on the W5S sensor, indicating a higher content of compounds such as nitrogen oxides, sulfur compounds, and alcohols. It has been demonstrated (Guan et al., 2022). There has been research into the production of these substances. Jin et al. posited that various microorganisms, including molds, yeasts, and bacteria, as well as different enzymes present in Jiuqu, could hydrolyze raw materials and convert them (Jin et al., 2017). Also, the Saccharomyces cerevisiae plays a central role in the production of volatile sulfur compounds. Through the sulfate reduction pathway, hydrogen sulfide (HS−) is formed, which can lead to the production of hydrogen sulfide and various mercaptan compounds (Swiegers & Pretorius, 2007). It has been reported that the S2 (W5S), S6 (W1S), S7 (W1W), S8 (W2S), and S9 (W2W) sensors have a high response to nitrogen oxides, methane, sulfides, alcohols, aldehydes, ketones, and aromatic organic sulfides, respectively (Wei & Gu, 2020). In our experiment, the five sensors W5S, W1W, W2S, W1S, and W2W exhibited a strong response to the baijiu samples, aligning with previous research results. Notably, the W5S sensor showed the highest response to the new baijiu, suggesting that the compounds had not yet achieved a stable equilibrium, leading to a less favorable taste and aroma in the newly produced baijiu. After storage, the concentration of these compounds in the baijiu samples decreased, resulting in lower response values. This trend was particularly noticeable after five years of storage, with the response values reaching their lowest point. This observation implies that storage can expedite the volatilization and chemical reactions of these compounds, ultimately enhancing the baijiu's flavor profile. It is important to highlight that in our experiment, the responses to sulfides, alcohols, aldehydes, ketones, methane, and aromatic organic sulfides were nearly negligible after one year of storage. This indicates that the response values of each sensor varied depending on the specific VOCs present. To obtain more accurate classification results, the supervised discriminant method PLS-DA is commonly used for sample classification (Song et al., 2020). The OPLS-DA model was used for fitting. As shown in the results, the model's goodness of fit is 66.5 % (R2 Y (cum) = 0.665), and the predictive ability is 57 % (Q2 (cum) = 0.57). The values of R2Y and Q2 from the permutation test indicate that the model has good adaptability. From a predictive perspective, the model achieved Q2 > 0.5, indicating that it has good predictive ability (Bylesjö et al., 2006).

Dynamic changes of amino acids in WWB

In addition to VOCs, baijiu also contains N-VOCs such as organic acids, amino acids, various polyols, and peptides. Due to the relatively low content of N-VOCs, their importance is often overlooked. Moreover, N-VOCs frequently interact with aromatic compounds, which has a significant impact on the release of aromatic compounds in the matrix (Wang et al., 2016). In our study, 25 amino acids exhibited significant changes, particularly after storage for 1, 3, and 5 years, with 21 amino acids still showing significant alterations. Among these, l-leucine, l-proline, l-tyrosine, l-isoleucine, and l-glutamic acid exhibit specific effects on volatile compounds, which aligns with the findings of Ferreira et al. Moreover, leucine is converted into isoamyl alcohol, contributing to a “banana” flavor (Ferreira & Guido, 2018).

Additionally, apart from l-glutamine, which notably decreased after 5 years, l-proline, l-leucine, l-phenylalanine, l-valine, l-isoleucine, l-glutamic acid, and methionine sulfoxide all significantly increased after storage. The research indicates that alanine, proline, and leucine can mitigate the damage caused by ethanol metabolism after excessive drinking, which is beneficial (Xie et al., 2018). In our research, these beneficial amino acids clearly increased significantly after storage. Specifically, alanine was undetectable in the new baijiu, but it was present at a very high level in both the first and fifth years after storage. L-alanine, being an amphoteric electrolyte, can reduce acidity by influencing the alkalinity and sweetness in alcoholic beverages. Additionally, l-alanine, as a flavor enhancer, is one of the amino acids constituting human proteins and is 1.2 times sweeter than sucrose. In alcoholic beverages, it not only softens the taste of the baijiu but also promotes alcohol metabolism and diminishes the off-flavors of sulfur compounds (Claus et al., 2017). These findings suggest that WWB benefits from amino acids that enhance the baijiu's quality and are advantageous to human health after storage. This may be a key advantage of using WWB. Consequently, we posit that amino acids, as N-VOCs, not only play a significant role in the release of VOCs in baijiu but also enhance the baijiu's quality, giving WWB its distinctive character. Aromatic amino acids mainly include three types: phenylalanine, tyrosine, and tryptophan. The good news is that the levels of these amino acids are significantly increased after storage. Additionally, important or beneficial amino acids for the human body, such as γ-aminobutyric acid (Deo et al., 2018), l-asparagine anhydrous (Batool et al., 2016), the precursor l-citrulline of arginine (Bahri et al., 2013), and l-threonine, one of the three major amino acids globally (Dong et al., 2012), are also significantly detected after the storage of WWB. This highlights the benefits of our baijiu and underscores the importance of additional research and attention.

Accumulation and dissipation of VOCs in WWB

Chinese baijiu is a complex alcoholic beverage known for containing a wide range of VOCs, including esters, acids, alcohols, ketones, aldehydes, phenolic compounds, sulfur-containing compounds, and others. Among these, esters, alcohols, and acids are the predominant compounds found in most Chinese baijiu, particularly in strong-aroma baijiu (He et al., 2020; Liu & Sun, 2018). During the aging process, some highly volatile and irritating trace components in the baijiu, such as volatile sulfides and aldehydes, naturally evaporate, leading to a decrease in concentration.

Among these compounds, sulfides are highly volatile due to their low boiling points (Ma et al., 2013; Wang et al., 2019). Additionally, there is evidence suggesting that sulfides can transform into other substances; however, this phenomenon has not been fully confirmed (Rodrigues et al., 2017). The action of dissolved oxygen oxidizes alcohol and aldehydes, converting them into acids and reducing their concentrations (Li et al., 2021). It is evident that the changes in the physical and chemical properties during the aging process result from the combined effects of the volatilization of low-boiling substances, the oxidation by dissolved oxygen, the balance between esterification and hydrolysis, intermolecular forces, and the involvement of metal elements leached from storage vessels (Ma et al., 2013).

The aging process of baijiu is typically conducted in darkness, and the storage period can range from 2 to 5 years (Tan et al., 2022; Tu et al., 2022). After storing our new baijiu, 517 VOCs changed, with the highest numbers being terpenes, esters, and heterocyclic compounds. Overall, compared to new baijiu, the VOCs in old baijiu primarily increased. Related experiments found that terpenes are retained in the mouth longer than other compounds (Pérez-Jiménez et al., 2021). Sun and others identified esters, aldehydes, and acids as the primary components contributing to the aroma of an empty cup (Sun et al., 2021). In our experience, the changes in terpenes and esters are very striking. After being stored for different lengths of time, compared to new baijiu, the three types of compounds that show the most noticeable changes each year are esters, terpenes, and heterocycles. There is a significant accumulation of upregulation, with only a slight downregulation accumulation. Meanwhile, the top three compounds with downregulation accumulation each year are ketones. Moreover, it has been confirmed that terpenes make a significant contribution to the aroma of fruit wine, baijiu, and beer (Kishimoto et al., 2005).

Generally speaking, new baijiu contains some harmful and irritating compounds with lower boiling points, such as hydrogen sulfide, thiols, and some aldehydes. In our VOCs analysis, we did not detect hydrogen sulfide and thiols, and only a small amount of aldehydes was found. We identified a total of 72 major VOCs, with esters, aldehydes, and heterocyclic compounds being the main VOCs. Ester compounds play a crucial role in the aroma of baijiu, primarily formed through the interaction of acids and alcohols under the catalysis of esterification enzymes. Acids serve as precursors for ester synthesis. Hexanoic acid, acetic acid, and butyric acid are three significant organic acids found in baijiu, acting as precursors for the synthesis of volatile flavor esters. Additionally, alcohol compounds can interact with esterification enzymes produced by microorganisms, resulting in the formation of ester compounds in conjunction with acids. Ethyl caproate, ethyl acetate, ethyl lactate, and ethyl butyrate are the four primary ethyl esters present in baijiu, constituting approximately 90–95 % of the total aroma (Kruis et al., 2017; Kuribayashi et al., 2023; Ma et al., 2020; Shi et al., 2021; Zhao et al., 2024). Unfortunately, this study found no significant change in ethyl lactate levels after the storage of baijiu. Ethyl acetate, hexanol, and butanol have long been recognized as essential aromatic compounds that define the aroma of baijiu. Notably, the honey and rose scents of phenethyl acetate have been found to significantly enhance the aftertaste of baijiu (Zhao et al., 2018). In our research, these compounds accumulated at higher levels over various storage durations. The “fruity” compounds that enhance aging aromas, such as ethyl butyrate, ethyl caproate, ethyl caprylate, ethyl pentanoate, ethyl 3-methylbutyrate, and ethyl acetate (Zhu et al., 2020), were identified, and only ethyl pentanoate was not identified in our study. It has been suggested that some of them, such as ethyl caproate and ethyl acetate, may be related to S. cerevisiae and I. orientalis, and that differences in the activity of W. anomalus and S. cerevisiae may also have an effect (Wu et al., 2015). Luzhou Laojiao Co., Ltd. uses waxy sorghum as raw materials, while Wuliangye Co., Ltd. uses sorghum, rice, wheat and corn as raw materials, and this difference will also affect the microbial structure (Wang, 2022; Wang, Kong, et al., 2022). Also, the flavor compounds, namely, fatty acids, esters, terpenes, and aromatic compounds produced by Saccharomyces cerevisiae were correlated with the mixing ratio of Bacillus licheniformis in Chinese maotai-flavor baijiu fermentation (Meng et al., 2015). The results presented above, when compared to the findings of this study, offer significant insights into the types and communities of microorganisms in this research. The flavor wheel results indicate that aged baijiu has the top three sensory flavors of fruity, green, and sweet, which are the most abundant volatile metabolites compared to new baijiu. In aged baijiu, the predominant flavors are sweet, fruity, and fatty. Additionally, among these three flavors, esters have the most significant influence. The quantity of esters increases significantly after storage, but their accumulation and dissipation also vary greatly, which accurately reflects the instability of ester compounds in baijiu (Xu et al., 2022). Among all these aromatic compounds, ethyl hexanoate is the most important compound due to its highest aroma intensity. In the present study, ethyl 5-methylhexanoate and 3-hexenoic acid ethyl ester showed a significant increase in accumulation after storage and were identified as fatty acid esters. Research has found that fatty acid esters in volatiles contribute the most to the overall sensor response of baijiu (Li et al., 2023). It is worth noting that after being stored for different lengths of time, ketones gradually dissipate, while alcohols, aldehydes, and aromatics do not. Low-carbon chain aldehydes and ketones are the primary carbon-based compounds in baijiu, possessing mild aromatic properties and a pungent odor (Wu, Wang, et al., 2006; Wu, Zhao, et al., 2006). Aromatic compounds, such as benzaldehyde and 4-ethylguaiacol, also accumulate after storage. It is noteworthy that among the special flavor components that contribute to the unique flavor of sauce-flavored baijiu, only furan was detected, while pyrazine was not. The aroma of phenethyl alcohol resembles that of roses and is a beneficial flavor in baijiu, which accumulates after storage. Esters and acids are the primary components of flavor and play a crucial coordinating role, while alcohols act as auxiliary flavors. Besides showcasing their distinct flavors, they mainly regulate the release and quality of the baijiu's aroma. In our study, terpenes, esters, and heterocyclic compounds are the main VOCs, and their variations are also the most significant, reflecting the characteristics of WWB. It is important to emphasize once more. Past research has suggested that the Nongxiangxing-flavor Baijiu is characterized by the mixed aroma of ethyl acetate and ethyl caproate. Jiangxiangxing-flavor Baijiu is characterized by the mixed aroma of ethyl acetate and ethyl butyrate (Liu et al., 2017). The results of this study indicate that these substances accumulate during storage, highlighting the advantages of waxy wheat baijiu.

Dynamic changes of metabolites

Currently, research on baijiu lacks sufficient understanding of N-VOCs metabolites compared to VOCs. Studies indicate that appropriately increasing the content of organic acids can not only improve the flavor of baijiu but also enhance the levels of beneficial components in baijiu (Long et al., 2022). In our experiment, we identified a total of 54 different organic acids with moderate content levels. Additionally, Wang et al. discovered that non-volatile organic acids significantly contribute to the aroma of baijiu, influencing the overall flavor of the spirit (Wang, Jing, et al., 2022). We know that the non-volatile components in baijiu mainly include organic acids, amino acids, phenolic compounds, and higher alcohols, which form the structural components of baijiu's flavor. In our analysis, we primarily focus on lipids, amino acids and their derivatives, as well as several other categories such as phenolic acids, organic acids, and alkaloids. Moreover, compared to new baijiu, aged baijius show varying degrees of increase in amino acids and their derivatives, lipids, and alkaloids. The chemical nature of lipids consists of esters and their derivatives formed from fatty acids and alcohols. Lipids, through oxidative degradation and hydrolysis, produce various compounds that are one of the sources of flavor substances in the baijiu. The lipids studied in this research mainly consist of free fatty acids (FFA), which are not only important nutrients but can also act as signaling molecules. They regulate key biological functions by actively participating in various energy metabolism pathways (Loona et al., 2023). This study also involves a large number of saccharides, which play an important role in various physiological and pathological processes (Wang, Jing, et al., 2022; Wang, Kong, et al., 2022). Trend analysis also found that amino acids and their derivatives, carbohydrates, and alkaloids are primarily accumulated, while lipids, phenolic acids, and organic acids are mainly dissipated. In this study, organic acids were identified in the metabolome; however, these levels decreased significantly after storage. It is likely due to the conversion of these acids into esters during storage (Li et al., 2015). A study found that organic acids are transformed into esters by microbes, such as Clostridium and Pichia via esterification between alcohol and organic acids (Torija et al., 2003). Alkaloids, on the other hand, are not only a source of bitterness but also key components of traditional Chinese medicine. Most alkaloids exhibit significant physiological activity, making them effective components of many medicinal plants. In general, analyzing the metabolome offers new insights into the dynamic changes of N-VOCs in baijiu.

WWB and other crop baijiu

In the Chinese baijiu industry, there is a saying: “Sorghum baijiu is fragrant, corn is sweet, rice is clean, and barley is pungent.” This makes wheat bajiu or WWB brewing seem more interesting. Sorghum baijiu has a high ester flavor profile, particularly with high levels of ethyl acetate, and low levels of aldehydes and alcohol. Rice has low ester content but high levels of aldehydes and alcohol, making it an unsuitable choice as the sole brewing ingredient. There is an unusual taste in baijiu made from corn or barley. Among other grains, wheat has a better taste than corn, rice, and barley (Xing et al., 2017). Starch is changed into glucose by saccharification with Clostridium butyricum or amylases in fermented grains. It is degraded and then through a series of complex reactions to produce ethanol and esters and other flavor substances, which will affect the baijiu. At the same time, the protein in the raw material is hydrolyzed into polypeptides and amino acids through protease catalysis. Starches, carbohydrates, proteins, and lipids are converted into ethanol and various flavor components via a series of biochemical reactions. This indicates that the starches and proteins in the raw materials play a crucial role in the development of liquor flavor (Liu & Sun, 2018). It is important to note that the protein content and structure can change the flavor of the baijiu, as well as improve the utilization of brewing materials (Liu et al., 2021). Moreover, they also believe that the quality of sorghum baijiu improves when the tannin content is between 1 % and 2 %. The ideal tannin content of sorghum for producing baijiu should be between 1.0 % and 2.0 %. In this regard, WWB is insufficient, and our research did not find any significant changes in tannin levels. Studies on purple waxy wheat used for brewing have shown that it contains a wider range and higher quantity of volatile flavor compounds compared to ordinary wheat. Additionally, its overall brewing characteristics are superior. Among these, esters are the primary component of volatile flavor compounds, and their levels are significantly affected by varietal factors (Zhao et al., 2018). WWB offers additional performance benefits such as faster fermentation rate, reduced nitrogen crop demand, and higher conversion efficiency. Glutinous varieties ferment more quickly compared to non-glutinous ones. Among sorghum varieties, the alcohol yield of glutinous sorghum was significantly higher than that of common sorghum (Thomas et al., 2022), as well as that of wheat (Zhao et al., 2009). The primary reason for this difference is that waxy wheat contains a higher concentration of free sugars, resulting in greater conversion efficiency compared to non-waxy wheat (Zhao et al., 2009). Also, starchy crops varieties with high waxy starch, low starch-lipid complex formed, and low protein cross-linking after cooking are more productive for ethanol production (Li et al., 2022). Waxy starches are hydrolyzed more rapidly, resulting in increased glucose production and accumulation during simultaneous saccharification and fermentation. In contrast, amylose is challenging to hydrolyze due to the formation of strong hydrogen bonds among linear chain polymers when they are leached in solution. Enzymes are unable to interact with amylose in this state because specific chain lengths of amylose/amylopectin polymers are required to attach to and cleave these bonds to release sugars (Gago et al., 2014). They are advantageous for waxy wheat because they enable cost control without compromising productivity, and the overall conversion efficiency surpasses that of non-glutinous varieties (Zhao et al., 2009). Moreover, waxy wheat possesses characteristics that align well with traditional baijiu brewing techniques. In comparison to japonica sorghum and regular wheat, waxy wheat yields a higher quantity of baijiu with superior aroma and taste. This suggests its potential for growth and utilization in the production of traditional Chinese baijiu. This can also be traced back (Wu, Wang, et al., 2006; Wu, Zhao, et al., 2006).

During the drinking process, the release of compounds into the nasal cavity is a dynamic equilibrium of the compounds volatilizing in the ethanol-water solution and the surrounding air. Therefore, they are released quickly during the drinking process and decrease rapidly during the aftertaste phase. The longer the carbon chain, the stronger the hydrophobicity of these compounds. They are likely to be retained in the mouth during the aftertaste phase, allowing them to participate in the aftertaste of the baijiu for a longer time (Ployon et al., 2020). In this study, 71 out of 97 esters have a carbon chain length greater than 10, which may be one of the reasons why WWB leaves a lingering aroma after drinking.

One of the notable characteristics of baijiu is that its quality improves with extended storage time. However, this is not absolute. Most researchers tend to favor storing baijiu for more years to obtain and understand the changes in many unknown compositional characteristics of baijiu. But this approach is not entirely ideal. In the baijiu industry, commonly found products are not all stored for ten or twenty years; more often, they are stored just until an important point. If the storage time is too short, the aroma of the baijiu has not truly been activated, while if it is too long, it increases costs and risks. Therefore, to shorten the aging cycle of baijiu, it is essential to study the aging mechanisms, analyze the chemical properties of flavor substances during the aging process, and find the optimal critical point for the accumulation state of specific types of baijiu compounds. In this study, we conducted an analysis of E-nose, VOCs, amino acids, and metabolites, which revealed significant differences after one year of storage. However, at three and five years, the two groups of samples exhibited similarities, indirectly suggesting that many substances tend to stabilize during this time period. Consequently, the storage cycle is significantly shortened, allowing the baijiu to maintain its quality and achieve the effects associated with other types of long-term baijiu storage. It is generally believed that 5 years is an important point for new baijiu to become aged baijiu, and 10 years is an important point for long-term storage (Xiao et al., 2015). The duration of storage in this study aligns with this condition. The patterns of these changes have become a way to address this issue. Just like our results, some important substances begin to decline after three or five years of storage. But overall, storage enhances quality, and it may reach its best point around the five-year mark. In summary, our research results indicate that compared to the most competitive japonica sorghum and common wheat, waxy wheat has a good specificity that distinguishes it from them in both VOCs and N-VOCs. For example, it has a high alcohol yield, moderate levels of acids and esters, and lower levels of aldehydes, particularly excelling in aroma and taste compared to other grain-based baijius. Therefore, waxy wheat possesses excellent brewing characteristics, which can enhance alcohol yield and improve the quality of baijiu, showing potential for brewing development. Additionally, it is worth trying to mix health-functional ingredients (such as buckwheat, quinoa, etc.) with other raw materials in brewing to impart health benefits to the baijiu.

Conclusions

According to our knowledge, this study represents the first comprehensive investigation of WWB, detailing the dynamic changes in VOCs and N-VOCs during storage. The metabolomic analysis identified 491 metabolites that exhibited significant changes, with many being upregulated post-storage, particularly notable increases in lipids and amino acids. After storage, there were 517 changes in VOCs, with the most significant alterations observed in esters and terpenes. Esters were the primary contributors to the sensory attributes of fruity, green, and sweet. Additionally, after storage, amino acids such as alanine, proline, leucine, tyrosine, and tryptophan accumulated, which are beneficial to health and enhance the quality of the baijiu. A substantial accumulation of a key ester volatile compound was also noted, contributing to the overall quality of the baijiu, which is relatively superior to that of other crops. These substances all contribute to the overall quality of WWB and may also represent an advantage of WWB. This study aims to enhance the understanding of the flavor chemistry of WWB and to conduct a more comprehensive analysis of its flavor characteristics. Additionally, it will provide valuable insights into the role of waxy wheat in improving the quality of baijiu, as well as new perspectives on the aging mechanisms and principles of baijiu. It will pave the way for exploring the research on blending baijiu with various raw materials and incorporating functional ingredients into baijiu. In the future, greater emphasis should be placed on regulating and synthesizing flavor quality through the use of functional microbiota in brewing methods and fermentation processes.
