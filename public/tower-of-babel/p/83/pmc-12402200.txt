Correction: Regulator of G protein signaling-1 facilitates ovarian cancer development by modulating NF-kB signal pathway
Authors: Liu Shengnan, Xi Jiayan, Sun Meng, Li Li, Cai Shengyun, Xu Mingjuan
Published: 2025

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-85071-y, published online 05 January 2025

The original version of the Article contained an error in Figure 7g. Due to an operational error, an image A2780/RGS+JSH-23/ki76 was a duplication of an image SKOV3/NC/ki67.

The original Figure 7 and accompanying legend appear below.Fig. 7NF-kB signaling pathway is involved in RGS1 promoting OC growth in vivo (a) Female nude mice were inoculated subcutaneously with A2780 control cells (vector) and RGS1 overexpressing cells (RGS1) and treated with JSH-23. The photographs of dissected tumors are presented. (b) Tumor growth curve and (c) terminal tumor weights. (d) Female nude mice were inoculated subcutaneously with SKOV3 cells without (NC) or with RGS1 knockdown and overexpression of p65. The photographs of dissected tumors are presented. (e) Tumor growth curve and (f) terminal tumor weights. (g) Representative images of HE and IHC staining with anti-RGS1, anti-p65 and anti-Ki67 antibodies in tumor tissues (×100, magnification).
