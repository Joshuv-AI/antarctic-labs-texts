Towards the sustainable financial management in green economic growth through big data technology innovation; Case study in China
Authors: Zhu Lina
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
This study addresses the critical challenge of integrating sustainable financial management within the framework of green economic growth in China from 1990 to 2022, underscored by rapid industrialization and environmental concerns. The primary aim is to analyze how big data technology innovations have facilitated sustainable financial practices that align with green economic objectives. The objectives include assessing the impact of these technologies on environmental sustainability, financial inclusion, and economic resilience. Using China as a case study, the research employs a longitudinal analysis to trace the evolution and integration of sustainable financial management and green economic growth, underpinned by technological advancements. Results indicate that big data technology has significantly enhanced the capacity for real-time analysis, risk assessment, and decision-making in financial institutions, leading to improved environmental and economic outcomes. Policy implications suggest that the government should foster an innovation-friendly environment that encourages the development and adoption of big data technologies, to further integrate sustainable financial management within the green growth agenda.

Introduction

This study revolves around the challenge of integrating sustainable financial management practices within the rapidly evolving landscape of green economic growth, particularly in the context of emerging big data technologies. Despite the recognized potential of big data to transform various sectors, its specific impact on sustainable financial management remains underexplored, especially in how it can facilitate or enhance green economic initiatives. The financial sector's role in fostering sustainable development is critical, yet there is a gap in understanding how technological innovations in data analytics can be harnessed to promote financial practices that not only yield economic returns but also contribute to environmental sustainability. This study aims to fill this gap by examining the interplay between big data technology and sustainable financial management, assessing whether and how the adoption of big data analytics can improve financial performance while advancing the goals of green economic growth. The problem is further compounded by the need to balance economic growth with environmental sustainability, requiring financial institutions to adopt new technologies and practices that align with these dual objectives. The study seeks to unravel these complexities and provide a comprehensive analysis of the potential synergies between big data technology and sustainable financial management, offering insights into how this alignment can be achieved in practice.

Recently, there has been a growing emphasis on sustainability in financial management, which has led firms to include environmental and social factors in their financial strategy [1]. The shift from a conventional profit-maximization strategy to a comprehensive framework that prioritizes the production of long-term value demonstrates a wider recognition that environmental and social obligations are essential for business success [2,3]. This change highlights the growing acknowledgment of environmentally conscious businesses as frontrunners in corporate sustainability. Companies that show a dedication to reducing their environmental footprint are being recognized for their inventive methods of gaining a competitive advantage [4]. The increasing focus on environmental sustainability underscores the recognition that profitability and ecological responsibility may coexist, hence improving competitive advantage and fostering innovation.

Moreover, the increasing financial expenses linked to the deterioration of the environment have intensified the need for enterprises to give importance to sustainability, therefore strengthening their role in promoting a stable and sustainable economy [5]. The use of big data technologies has greatly enhanced the capacity of businesses in the field of sustainable financial management. The capacity of big data to store, process, and analyze immense quantities of information presents revolutionary possibilities in several industries [6]. Big data is highly regarded in economic administration as a powerful force for driving innovation. It offers organizations exceptional insights into market trends, customer behavior, and operational efficiency. Exploring the integration of big data technologies with sustainable financial management has great potential. Companies may use big data analytics to analyze their operations thoroughly, therefore making progress toward implementing more sustainable practices [7]. conducted studies indicating that big data analytics may assist companies in minimizing energy use, assessing the sustainability of supply chains, and using predictive models to anticipate the environmental impact of business choices. Furthermore, the use of big data improves transparency, allowing companies to efficiently communicate their sustainability efforts to stakeholders and align economic goals with social expectations. The intersection of big data and sustainability has significant economic ramifications that need a comprehensive evaluation, taking into account both current efficiency and future strategic objectives, as highlighted [8](H [9]).

This study makes a significant contribution by detailing the evolution and impact of big data technology on sustainable financial management in China's green economic growth from 1990 to 2022. Through an extensive examination of the period, the research employs a mixed-methods approach, combining quantitative data analysis with qualitative case studies to elucidate the transformation in financial practices influenced by big data innovations. The model utilized in the study incorporates variables such as technological adoption rates, financial performance metrics, and environmental sustainability indicators, allowing for a comprehensive analysis of the interplay between big data technology and sustainable finance. The results reveal a positive correlation between the adoption of big data analytics and improvements in financial sustainability and environmental performance. Specifically, the study highlights how big data technology enabled more effective risk management, predictive analytics, and strategic decision-making, leading to enhanced financial inclusion, reduced environmental impact, and fostered sustainable economic growth. This research not only traces the trajectory of financial and environmental policy in China but also demonstrates the pivotal role of technological innovation in driving sustainable development within the financial sector.

Theoretical framework

The study's theoretical approach examined the potential of big data technology to assist environmentally conscious organizations in implementing sustainable financial management practices that prioritize environmental responsibility. With the growing recognition of the importance of environmental stewardship, organizations are increasingly looking for innovative approaches to enhance their sustainable finance management practices [13]. Using big data technology and analytics is an approach that could transform decision-making processes and result in sustainable results. Big data technologies include the advancements in data collection, storage, processing, and analysis that enable enterprises to effectively handle vast and complex datasets [14]. By using data analytics methods such as descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive analytics, companies may get a deeper understanding of their environmental impact, identify opportunities for cost savings, optimize resource allocation, and make informed investment choices (C [15]).

Three basic hypotheses are associated with the inquiry being studied.

This perspective analyzes financial management solutions with regard to governance, social, and environmental (ESG) factors. It assesses the execution of sustainable strategies, including optimizing resource use, adopting renewable energy sources, managing waste, and reducing environmental impact. The article examines the use of big data analytics, machine learning, and artificial intelligence in several sectors, such as banking and sustainability, with the aim of fostering innovation, enhancing decision-making processes, and extracting important insights [16,17]. These perspectives are likely to be included in the study's theoretical framework to assess the potential of big data technology innovation in enhancing sustainable financial management practices in environmentally friendly enterprises. In order to encourage investments and financial strategies that prioritize the environment, there is a possibility of exploring the use of big data analytics to improve the collection of ESG data, analyze risks, develop financial models, and make informed decisions. This integration facilitates the use of factual information to make informed decisions and provides a thorough understanding of the Company's sustainability practices. The research aims to promote the use of sustainable financial administration approaches and accelerate the transition to a more environmentally friendly economy.

Empirical research in the field of sustainable finance has shown that big data can significantly enhance decision-making processes, improve financial performance, and support environmentally sustainable business practices. Studies have highlighted the potential of big data analytics in providing detailed insights into market trends, customer behavior, and risk management, which are essential for the development of sustainable financial strategies. Despite these insights, the literature reveals notable gaps. There is a scarcity of detailed analyses focusing specifically on how big data technologies influence sustainable financial management practices and contribute to green economic growth. Many studies generalize the impact of technology on the financial sector without dissecting the unique contributions of big data to sustainability goals [18]. Additionally, the long-term effects of big data-driven financial management on environmental sustainability remain underexplored, and the sector-specific benefits and challenges of big data adoption in finance are not sufficiently detailed. Moreover, the existing literature often overlooks the potential negative externalities associated with the use of big data technology, such as data privacy concerns, cybersecurity risks, and the environmental impacts of data storage and processing infrastructure (Y [19]) (Y [15]).

The contribution of our study to the literature is significant in addressing these gaps. It provides a detailed analysis of the specific ways in which big data technology can enhance sustainable financial management practices and foster green economic growth, with a particular focus on the Chinese context. By employing a robust methodological framework that integrates various metrics of technology adoption, financial performance, and environmental sustainability, our study offers nuanced insights into the complex interplay between big data and sustainable financial management. Furthermore, it extends the temporal and geographical scope of existing research, providing a longitudinal analysis that captures the evolving impact of big data technology over time and across different regions within China. Our study not only bridges the identified gaps in the literature but also contributes new empirical evidence and theoretical insights, enhancing the understanding of the role of big data technology in driving sustainable financial management and green economic growth.

Discussions

The Pesaran test is a widely used method for assessing this dependence while analyzing panel data. Furthermore, it is crucial to confirm the presence of cross-sectional dependency in order to guarantee the validity of any conclusions made by Ref. [33]. By using unit root tests such as CIPS and CADF, one may determine the level of integration of the variables. The notion of order of integration is essential in establishing the number of differencing steps needed to make a time series stationary. Economic parameters often display first-order integration, denoted as I (1), indicating that they are not stationary at their original values but become stable after differencing. This attribute is crucial for doing a cointegration assessment, which investigates the enduring equilibrium connections among parameters. Although individual variables may not be stationary, the concept of cointegration implies that a linear combination of these variables may be stationary, showing a consistent connection throughout time. Tools like the Westerlund cointegration test are often used to confirm the presence of cointegration among variables. Cointegration indicates that the variables have a durable and stable long-term relationship. The Error Correction Model (ECM) encapsulates the dynamic of how variables recover to their equilibrium state after a disturbance, and analyzing this process is crucial. This approach allows for the modification of the connection between variables based on a threshold variable. Given that connections are not inherently linear and may be influenced by many factors, this is especially relevant in the context of economics.

Managerial implications

The managerial policy recommendations derived from these findings are comprehensive and multi-faceted. To fully leverage the benefits of big data technology for sustainable financial management, it is crucial for policymakers and industry leaders to:

Invest in Big Data Infrastructure: Develop and enhance the technological infrastructure necessary for collecting, storing, and analyzing large data sets, ensuring that financial institutions have the tools they need to make informed decisions.

Foster Talent Development: Encourage educational and training programs focused on big data analytics and sustainable financial practices to build a skilled workforce capable of navigating the complexities of modern finance and environmental stewardship.

Promote Collaborative Standards: Work towards establishing national and international standards for big data use in finance, focusing on data quality, transparency, and ethical considerations, to facilitate cross-border collaboration and innovation.

Encourage Sustainable Investments: Incentivize financial products and services that support environmental sustainability, encouraging the sector to shift towards green investments and financing projects that contribute to ecological balance and long-term economic stability.

Enhance Regulatory Frameworks: Implement and continuously update regulatory frameworks that ensure the responsible use of big data technologies, safeguarding against potential risks and promoting a culture of accountability and sustainability.

Conclusion

The study, covering the period from 1990 to 2022, conclusively demonstrates that big data technology has significantly influenced sustainable financial management within China's green economic growth. The model, integrating Technology Adoption Rate (TAR), Financial Performance Metrics (FPM), and Environmental Sustainability Indicators (ESI), elucidates how big data technology enhances financial and environmental performance Financial institutions leveraging big data showed marked improvements in operational efficiency, risk management, and decision-making processes. These improvements translated into enhanced financial performance, with higher profitability and asset quality, and a stronger alignment with sustainability goals, such as reduced environmental footprints and better resource management. The study's results underscored the transformative power of big data in facilitating a more sustainable and resilient financial ecosystem. Institutions that integrated big data analytics into their operations not only achieved better financial outcomes but also contributed significantly to environmental sustainability by optimizing resource use and reducing waste and emissions. The temporal analysis further highlighted the evolving nature of this impact, showing an increasing trend in the effectiveness of big data applications over the studied period.

The study, while comprehensive, acknowledges several limitations that pave the way for future research directions. One of the main limitations is the reliance on publicly available data, which may not capture the full scope of big data technology's impact on sustainable financial management, particularly in private sector operations. Additionally, the model primarily focuses on macro-level indicators, potentially overlooking micro-level dynamics and sector-specific variations in the adoption and impact of big data technologies.

Future research should aim to address these limitations by incorporating a broader range of data sources, including proprietary and sector-specific datasets, to provide a more nuanced understanding of how big data technology influences financial and environmental performance at different levels of the economy. Qualitative research methods, such as case studies and interviews with industry experts, could complement quantitative analyses to explore the contextual factors and mechanisms underlying the observed trends. Moreover, longitudinal studies extending beyond 2022 would be valuable to track the evolving impact of big data technology on sustainable financial management, considering the rapid pace of technological advancement and changing regulatory landscapes. Future research could also explore comparative studies across different countries and regions to understand the global implications and transferability of the findings. Another promising direction is the examination of the interplay between big data technology and emerging trends in finance, such as blockchain, artificial intelligence, and the integration of environmental, social, and governance (ESG) criteria in investment decision-making. Investigating these areas can provide deeper insights into the future of sustainable financial management and the role of technology in driving green economic growth.
