Authors: Hatan Mortada
Published: 2023

INTRODUCTION

Ganga Hospital, located in Coimbatore, a bustling tier-2 city in South India, has garnered global recognition for its exceptional expertise in plastic and orthopedic surgery. Serving a population of three million residents and often referred to as the “Manchester of South India” due to its robust textile industry, Coimbatore has transformed into a hub for outstanding tertiary medical services and education. Ganga Hospital stands as a testament to this transformation, excelling as a leading center for trauma care, orthopedics, and the intricate disciplines of plastic surgery, hand surgery, and microsurgery.1

Ganga Hospital’s acclaimed healthcare also offers an abundance of learning opportunities due to its high volume of cases and superior operative techniques.2–4 This overview, based on my personal experience, will highlight the educational prospects, including the operative hand course.

VISITOR PROGRAMS AND CLINICAL OBSERVATIONS

An opportunity for a clinical observership exists either as a component of the operative hand course or as a distinct experience. This involves participating in morning teaching sessions led by consultants, ward rounds, outpatient clinics, and operating theater procedures. With 16 specially dedicated operating rooms, the plastic unit can potentially function round-the-clock, if required. This setup allows observation of numerous surgical procedures daily, encompassing significant upper and lower limb trauma reconstructions, replantations, and brachial plexus operations. Noteworthy is the patient-centric approach adopted, wherein operations, regardless of whether elective or emergency, are conducted on the day of presentation.

OPERATIVE COURSE IN HAND AND MICROSURGERY AT GANGA

Annually, this operative course facilitates the assembly of 400 delegates, with this iteration extending over a period of 3 days. The used platform permitted attendees to spectate over a dozen hours of live surgical procedures daily, dispersed across a minimum of two operating theaters functioning concurrently (Table 1). Furthermore, the platform enabled real-time discourse with the surgeons midoperation. Accommodating global attendees, the course was designed to grant the flexibility of initiating the course at one’s convenience, with the option of pausing and rewinding the streamed content for up to a week postcourse.

Over the course of this event, an array of over 30 surgical procedures were executed, ranging from relatively straightforward cases (such as trigger release) to significantly complex operations (including an assortment of tendon transfers addressing brachial plexus injuries). Each surgical case was introduced by the operating surgeon, providing a succinct patient history, a prerecorded patient examination video, and an explication of the surgical steps. The surgeon also detailed the rationale behind the chosen operative technique and identified pertinent anatomical landmarks and instruments while conducting the operation. PERSONAL REFLECTIONS AND INSIGHTS

Despite lasting only 2 weeks, my personal experience at Ganga Hospital was immensely enriching. Beyond witnessing surgical procedures, I gained insight into the hospital’s operations, where patient care is paramount. The welcoming team imparted valuable knowledge through morning discussions, consultations, and clinics. Observing numerous cases during the 3-day operative hand course was especially beneficial. I express my deep gratitude to Ganga Hospital for this invaluable opportunity.
