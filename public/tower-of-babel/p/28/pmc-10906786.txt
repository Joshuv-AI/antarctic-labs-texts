Indian Society of Periodontology Perio Parichay 2023

Indian Society of Periodontology (ISP) PERIO PARICHAY 2023 was conducted by ISP study group Johar at Hotel Triton Raipur C.G on August 12th and 13th, 2023. A galaxy of speakers consisting of Dr. Abhay Kolte, Dr. H.S Grover, Dr. Ashish Jain, Dr. Subash Raj, Dr. Rohit Mishra, Dr. Anil Melath, Dr. Raja Sridhar, Dr. Sachin K Malagi, Dr.Kaushtub S Thakre, Dr. Rajat Sehgal, and Dr. Suchita Vanjari graced the program as resource persons and shared their abundance of experience and knowledge with the students and practitioners present at the conference. The local team included a number of renowned periodontists of the region, i.e., Dr. S.S Chaturvedi, Dr. Saurabh Bhandari, Dr. Jitendra Saraf, Dr. Vaibhav Tiwari, Dr. Kuldeep Sangha, and many more who worked diligently toward the meticulous planning, smooth conduct and the grand success of the program. From all over the country, a total of 63 members attended the event and updated their knowledge and skills from seasoned mentors and experts from the field of Periodontology [Figures 1-4].

INDIAN SOCIETY OF PERIODONTOLOGY INTEGRATE 2023

The event was conducted at three places, i.e., Chennai, Delhi NCR, and Mumbai, in August and September 2023 on enhancing awareness regarding the field of Implantology, across the nation. The first program of the series was conducted by ISP Chennai Study Club in association with Saveetha Dental College (Department of Periodontics), Chennai, on August 22–23, 2023. Approximately, 100 postgraduate students from 20 dental colleges in South India participated in the event. The inauguration of the conference marked the presence of ISP office bearers, including Secretary Dr. H.S. Grover, President-Elect Dr. Gopalakrishnan, Past Presidents Dr. Ashish Kumar Jain, Dr. Anil Melath, along with the Registrar of Saveetha University, Dr. Sheeja Varghese, and the Dean of Saveetha Dental College, Dr. Aravind Kumar. The Inaugural event was truly a spectacular extravaganza. Following the inauguration, a panel discussion on the topic “Evidence-Based Approach in Peri-Implantitis Management” was conducted by Dr. H.S. Grover, Dr. Anil Melath, and Dr. Ashish Kumar Jain. The discussion was moderated by Dr. Gopalakrishnan and Dr. N.D. Jayakumar, and proved to be highly engaging and educational. Live demonstrations were held on “Peri-Implantitis Management with Er, Cr: YSGG Laser” and “Digital I mplant Planning Using Navident.” A workshop focusing on “Soft Tissue Augmentation Using Animal Models and Various Newer Biomaterials” recorded enthusiastic participation from the postgraduates. As part of the conference, a quiz on implant and laser dentistry was organized for both postgraduate and undergraduate students. All the students expressed their delight and satisfaction with the knowledge and insights they gained from the lectures, demonstrations, and workshops. In summary, the program achieved remarkable success [Figures 5-8].

ISP and ISP Study Group Delhi NCR specifically designed and organized the second of the series, “Implantology and Laser Consortium-“ ISP Integrate 2023, which was held at the esteemed Sudha Rustagi College of Dental Sciences and Research, Faridabad, on September 1st and 2nd, 2023. Renowned experts in the field of implants and lasers were invited to share their expertise and offer their insight into the journey of professional growth. The program was organized and conducted under the guidance of Dr. C.S Baiju as organizing chairperson, Dr. Meenu Taneja Bhasin as organizing secretary, and Dr. Anshu Blaggana as co-organizing secretary and treasurer, along with a collation of faculty members from colleges across Delhi NCR as the organizing committee members. With the huge participation of 150 delegates, along with 41 scientific poster presentations, the event was a great success. An E-poster competition was also organized for the postgraduates and the best e-posters were awarded on the 2nd day of the consortium to boost the spirit of innovation and learning among students. Overall, the program proved to be a huge success and left no stone unturned in doing its bit towards shaping the future of dentistry through innovation, knowledge, and collaboration [Figures 9-12].

The third event in the same row, the Indian Society of Periodontology – Mumbai study group organized the INTEGRATE – Implant and Laser Conclave on September 8th and 9th, 2023. The event was held on a large scale at the IDA Head office, Prabhadevi, Mumbai. With the huge participation of 120 delegates along with 60 scientific poster presentations, the event was a great success. The guests of honor, Dr. Wasundhara Bhad and Dr. Ashok Dhoble, along with Dr. H. S. Grover, inaugurated the event. The ocean of knowledge about implants and lasers was made available for the budding periodontists by the stalwarts of the field [Figures 13-16].
