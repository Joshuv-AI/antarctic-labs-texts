Correction for Hazime et al., Nanoscale restructuring of the immune synapse with an engager enhances NK cell function
Published: 2025

Correction for “Nanoscale restructuring of the immune synapse with an engager enhances NK cell function,” by Khodor S. Hazime, Sam Sheppard, Olatz Niembro-Vivanco, Cathal Hosty, Heather Thomas, Christopher Loosbroock, Leanne Peiser, and Daniel M. Davis, which published September 18, 2025; 10.1073/pnas.2507336122 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 122, e2507336122).

The authors note that, due to a production error, an earlier version of the SI Appendix was published, and SI Appendix, Figs. S1 and S2 appeared incorrectly. SI Appendix, Fig. S1E should be incorporated into SI Appendix, Fig. S2 as panel 2B. The SI Appendix has been corrected online.
