The construction of the index system for nurses' innovation competence evaluation in China: A Delphi study
Authors: Li-li Ma, Ya-juan Zhang, Shan Rong, Jin-xiu Yao, Hui-ren Zhuang, Wei-ying Zhang
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundScientific and technological innovation has become the "key variable" of national social and economic development. Nurses' innovation ability can improve the quality of patient care, thus shorten the hospital stay, reduce the medical costs, and promote the rapid development of the nursing industry. Nowadays nurses have gradually involved in various innovation, but less concerns are focus on the evaluation of nurse’s innovation competence.ObjectiveTo develop evaluation index system for nurse's innovation competence.ParticipantsEleven experts with innovation experience from different regions in China.MethodsLiterature review and qualitative interview were used to establish first version of index system. Then, a two-round Delphi expert consultation was conducted to collect opinions from 11 experts. Then the final version of nurses' innovation competence evaluation index was used in one tertiary hospital for preliminary application.ResultsThe response rate of the two rounds was more than 90%, the expert familiarity coefficient was 0.818, the judgment coefficient was 0.918, and the authority coefficient was 0.868. The two rounds of expert coordination coefficient test showed P < 0.05. The evaluation index system of nurses' innovation competence includes 4 first-level indicators, 9 second-level indicators and 36 third-level indicators. The scores of innovation production and innovation subject were the lowest among all the first-level indicators when use this index for preliminary application.ConclusionThe constructed evaluation index system of nurses' innovation competence are scientific, reliable and practical, and can be used to promote nurse's innovation competence and innovation conversion competence in clinical practice. Hospital managers should pay more attention on cultivate nurse’s innovation competence and provide resources and multi-form platforms to support the nurses with innovative ideas and willingness.Keywords: Innovation, Delphi study, Index, Evaluation, Nurse

Introduction

With the accelerated evolution of the new round of scientific and technological revolution and industrial transformation, scientific and technological innovation has become a crucial factor in national social and economic development. The Proposal of the CPC Central Committee on Formulating the 14th Five-Year Plan for National Economic and Social Development and the Long-term Goals of 2035 clearly emphasizes the central role of innovation in China's overall modernization drive. Self-reliance in science and technology is identified as the strategic support for China's development [1]. Innovation competence is a manifestation of human abilities, encompassing the competence to doubt, criticize, and investigate. It involves the utilization of knowledge and theories to constantly generate new ideas, skills, and inventions [2]. This competence primarily includes innovation consciousness, observation competence, imagination competence, analysis ability, and completion ability. Nurse innovation behavior, on the other hand, refers to the process of generating new ideas in a nursing environment and having the confidence to strive for their realization. It involves overcoming potential challenges to develop new processes, care strategies, or related policies that aim to restore or promote patient health [3]. Research has demonstrated that enhancing nurses' innovation competence can improve the quality of patient care, resulting in reduced hospital stays, lower medical costs, and even a decrease in stillbirth rates [4]. As nursing service contents continue to expand, clinical nurses hold a pivotal position in providing creative and innovative solutions. Their innovative behavior in the workplace can truly enhance the quality of nursing, challenge the existing norms in nursing, and drive the rapid development of the nursing industry.

Background

Innovation competence encompasses the personality characteristics of the innovation person, the mental process, the innovative products, and the interaction between the innovation person and the environment [5]. The new 4P (person, product, process, place or press) theoretical model [6] incorporates the innovation person (self-innovation reserve, emphasizing the important role of personality traits, attitude, and intelligence in innovation behavior,etc.), the innovation process (innovation-related experience), the innovation environment and press (the environment that promotes or hinders innovative work), and the innovation product (including viewpoint, invention, discovery, theory, etc.)([7,8]). The 4P theory encompasses the innovation process and results, and considers the influence of both internal and external factors, making it suitable for the comprehensive evaluation of nurse's innovation competence.

Currently, research on nurses' innovation behavior in China primarily centers on various investigations related to innovation. These studies aim to analyze nurses' innovation behavior, innovation ability, current situation, and influencing factors. Additionally, some research explores the correlation between nurses' innovation behavior, organizational innovation atmosphere, and psychological empowerment [9]. Yan et al. [10] investigated the innovation ability of nurses nationwide using the nurses' innovation scale and found that the total score of innovation ability of clinical nurses in China was at a moderate level. They observed that the higher the technical title, administrative position, and seniority of nurses, the more patents and papers they published, indicating a stronger innovation ability. Li et al. [11] reported that the total score of clinical nurse's innovation ability was 151.00 ± 22.73, suggesting a good level of innovation ability. Factors such as working years, educational background, technical titles, department, and hospital levels were found to significantly influence nurses' innovation ability. Additionally, a positive correlation was identified between nurses' psychological capital, organizational innovation atmosphere, and innovation ability. Sun et al. [12] noted that the innovation ability of nurses was at a low level, with the highest education, working years, professional title, position, and marital status being key factors affecting clinical nurses' innovation ability. While there have been limited studies on nursing innovation, there is a lack of research on the evaluation index of nurses' innovation competence. Therefore, it is crucial to scientifically evaluate nursing innovation ability and establish a comprehensive evaluation system to promote continuous and effective development. This study, based on the 4P innovation theory (person, process, environment and press, product), constructed an index system for evaluating nurses' innovation competence. This framework serves as a foundation for the scientific evaluation of nurses' innovation competence and facilitates the enhancement of nurses' innovation ability.

Methods

Establishing of the research group

The research team was composed of 5 persons, including 2 directors of the hospital-based nursing innovation working team (1 director of nursing department, 1 associate director of nursing department), 2 head nurse and 1 graduate student. The research team is mainly responsible for literature review, expert's inquiry letter and questionnaire construction, sponsoring of expert consultation and discussion, and modification of the index system.

Study design

Literature review and qualitative interviews were utilized to develop a draft version of an evaluation index system for assessing nurses' innovation ability. Building upon the findings of the literature review, this study comprehensively examined the current status of nurses' innovation competence and the relevant evaluation indicators. By integrating insights from semi-structured interviews with nurses and doctors who possess significant innovation experience, the initial version of the evaluation index system for nurse innovation competence was established based on the 4P innovation model (person, process, environment and press, product). This initial version includes 4 primary indexes, 9 secondary indexes, and 32 tertiary indexes. The study adhered to the Guidelines for Conducting and Reporting Delphi Studies. (CREDES)([13,14]).

The Delphi consultation inquiry questionnaire contains three parts.(a) a letter of questionnaire description: introduce the research background, purpose of study and filling instructions, etc. (b) the first version of evaluation index system of nurse's innovation competence, including 4 first-level indexes, 9 s-level indexes and 32 third-level indexes. Likert 5 scoring method was used to ask experts to assign the importance of each index (5 = very important, 4 = important, 3 = general, 2 = less unimportant, 1 = unimportant) [15] and the experts were also asked about the suitability of the indicators which they can add, delete, combine and supplement.

The research team sorted out the results of the consultation, and assigned the evaluation index according to the importance of each index [10], the full score ratio> 0.2, the coefficient of variation <0.25, and the indicators were screened and modified combined with the expert modification opinions. (c) expert information questionnaire: including the basic information of experts, experts' familiarity with the evaluation content questionnaire, expert's judgment basis on the evaluation content. Content familiarity is divided into five levels: very familiar (1.0), familiar (0.8), generally familiar (0.6), unfamiliar (0.4), very unfamiliar (0.2). The judgment basis of experts is quantified according to the three levels: strong, moderate, and weak [16]. Scores were assigned to each level:Theoretical analysis (0.3, 0.2, 0.1), practical experience (0.5, 0.4, 0.3), refers to domestic and foreign literature (0.1, 0.1, 0.1) and intuitive judgment (0.1, 0.1, 0.1).

Participants

Purposive sampling was taken in this study for Delphi process.The inclusion criteria for Delphi experts were as follows: (a) doctors or nurses who had much experienced in the field of medical/nursing innovation; (b) technical title is intermediate or above title and has bachelor degree or above; (c) have high enthusiasm for this study and is able to provide comprehensive opinions and suggestions from different judgment perspectives; (d) willing to participate in this study, and actively participate in the inquiry. A total of 11 experts participated the study.

Delphi process

Delphi consultation was conducted by distributing questionnaires by Wechat and e-mail between June and August 2023, and experts are required to complete and return it within 2 weeks. The results of the expert consultation were summarized, analyzed and discussed by the research team. Taking the mean importance score >3.50 and the coefficient score of variation <0.25 as index screening criteria, when the expert opinions tended to agree, then the consultation was ended [17]. Two rounds of Delphi inquiries were completed in this study. Finally, the final version of nurses' innovation competence index system was formed including 4 first-level indicators, 9 s-level indicators and 36 third-level indicators.

Preliminary application of nurses' innovation competence evaluation index system

The nurses' innovation competence evaluation index was utilized in a clinical setting to assess the efficacy and practicality of the tools starting from August to September in 2023. A total of 51 nursing units from a tertiary hospital in Shanghai were involved in the study to analyze nurses' innovation competence. Inclusion criteria required participants to be clinical nurses who were willing to take part in the study. The evaluation index was transformed into a questionnaire and distributed to the head nurse of each department via Wechat in the form of a questionnaire link. The head nurse completed the questionnaire accordingly.

Statistical analysis

Statistical analyses were analyzed with IBM SPSS 23.0(Statistical Package for Social Sciences). Descriptive analysis was used to analyze the demographics of experts and enthusiasm status of experts, the mean, standard deviation and indicator weight were applied to analyze the concentration of expert opinions, and Kendall's W coordination coefficient was used to judge the coordination coefficient between experts. Statistical significance was set as P < 0.05.

Experts' enthusiasm

The experts' enthusiasm was assessed according to their responses to the questionnaire. In the first round, 12 questionnaires were dispatched and 11 were returned, and in the second round, 11 questionnaires were given out and 11 were returned, with response rates of 91.67 % and 100 % respectively.

Expert authority

The degree of authority of experts is expressed by the expert authority coefficient (Cr), which comes from the self-evaluation of experts. The authority coefficient of experts is determined by the expert's judgment (Ca)and the expert's familiarity (Cs). The expert authority coefficient Cr = (Ca + Cs)/2. It is generally believed that the expert authority coefficient Cr ＞0.7 means that the research results are reliable.Results of the first round of expert consultation: expert familiarity coefficient were 0.782, judgment coefficient was 0.909, and authoritative coefficient was 0.846. In the second round, expert familiarity coefficient was 0.818, judgment coefficient was 0.918, and authoritative coefficient was 0.868.

Coordination degree of expert opinions

The coefficient of variation (CV) and the Kendall coordination coefficient (W) indicate the agreement of the experts. In this study, experts scored the importance of each index from 4.36 to 4.82, and the coefficient of variation (CV) ranged from 0.08 to 0.21, and the X2 test was significant (P < 0.05). The overall Kendall W was 0.145 and 0.163, respectively, that indicating that the coordination was high, the opinions were consistent, and the results were reliable (Table 2).

Consensus regarding expert opinions

While the concept of consensus is the basis of many Delphi studies, the definition of what constitutes consensus is less clear ([18,19]). During this 2 round Delphi procedure, each indicator was evaluated by Delphi experts with a scale of 1–5 points (5 = very important, 4 = important, 3 = general, 2 = less unimportant, 1 = unimportant). A mean score larger than or equal to 4 indicated that experts considered the indicator to be important [20]. In 2 round Delphi process, all the indicator scores are more than 4. In the first round of Delphi, experts gave advice to add 7 indicators, modify 1 indicator and combine 2 indicators into 1 indicator, and in second round of Delphi, these new indicators also got scores equal or more than 4. Thus experts got consensus over this nurse's innovation competence evaluation index system.

Five indicators, ‘numbers of nursing clinics’,‘structure of teaching staff’’, ‘scientific research and exploration competence’, ‘evaluation/competition related to hospital organization innovation’ and ‘numbers of patents generating benefits’, were added to third level of indicators. Discussion

The scientificalness of nurse's innovation competence evaluation index system

The selection of experts plays a critical role in the Delphi method implementation process, as the scientific validity of the outcomes heavily relies on the representativeness and expertise of the consulting experts [21]. In this study, experts were meticulously chosen based on their innovation background and specific inclusion criteria, taking into account their professional domains, educational qualifications, job titles, and years of experience. The expert panel comprised a diverse group of individuals, including doctors, nurses, and nursing management professionals, ensuring a well-rounded perspective. Around 81.82 % of the experts held associate senior or senior positions, while 72.73 % possessed a master's degree or higher, showcasing their extensive clinical and innovation knowledge and ensuring the research's scientific rigor. The response rate for the two rounds of questionnaires surpassed 90 %, with an effective response rate exceeding 70 %, indicating a high level of enthusiasm among the experts [15]. This highlights their strong engagement with the study and underscores the current importance of innovation-related research. The experts in the study demonstrated high authority coefficients (0.868), familiarity degrees (0.818), judgment basis on the research content (0.918), and expert authority levels (0.847), all exceeding 0.70, affirming the experts' credibility and the study's reliability [22]. These results validate the expertise and trustworthiness of the experts involved. The coordination coefficient serves as a metric to evaluate the credibility of consultation outcomes and reflect the harmony among different experts [5]. In this study, experts rated the importance of each index between 4.36 and 4.82, with a coefficient of variation ranging from 0.08 to 0.21. The X2 test yielded a significant result (P < 0.05), indicating strong coordination, consistent opinions, and reliable findings. The research is grounded in the 4P innovation theory, which covers all aspects of innovation, making it well-suited for the comprehensive assessment of clinical nurses' innovation competence. Therefore, this study utilizes the 4P theory as the theoretical framework to ensure the scientific rigor of the index system.

The reliability of nurse's innovation competence evaluation index system

Index weight is a digital index that indicates the importance and function of an index. The proper setting of weights plays a crucial role in the development of an index system ([8,23]). In this study, the weights assigned to the four first-level indicators were 0.247, 0.251, 0.251, and 0.251, respectively, all 4 indicators were equally distributed. Therefore, when constructing the index system for evaluating nurses' innovation competence, it is essential to consider the four dimensions of the 4P innovation theory. The assessment of nurses' innovation ability should not only focus on the innovation person, but also take into account the innovation process, innovation environment, pressure, and innovation output in a comprehensive manner. The cognition and practice of innovation represent key steps in the innovation process. Factors such as the innovation atmosphere and support promote innovation output and conversion, reflecting positive outcomes of the innovation environment and pressure. Innovation output, including considerations of patent quantity and patent transformation, serves as an assessment index for innovation work. By incorporating the principles of the 4P innovation theory, this study not only considers the influencing factors of nurses' innovation but also the factors that promote innovation behavior, the innovation process, and the support needed for innovation promotion. This provides a reference and basis for promoting nurses' innovation and comprehensively assessing innovation indicators.

The practicability of nurse's innovation competence evaluation index system

Scientific and technological innovation has gained attention from various industries [24]. Nurses, as important innovators, have the potential to create new challenges in the nursing environment and overcome them to develop new processes, nursing strategies, and related policies. This can ultimately lead to improved nursing quality, a change in the current state of nursing, and the rapid development of the nursing industry. This study is based on the 4P innovation theory and focuses on evaluating nurses' innovative competence. We have constructed an index system for evaluating nurses' innovation competence through literature review, qualitative interviews, Delphi expert consultations, and hierarchical analysis. The resulting evaluation index system includes 4 first-level indicators, 9 s-level indicators, and 36 third-level indicators. This comprehensive index system closely aligns with the innovative practices of clinical nurses. Considering the current state of nursing innovation research in China, we propose a comprehensive and multi-angle evaluation approach. By promoting construction through evaluation, we aim to provide a theoretical basis for nurturing nurses' innovative ability and to enhance support and access to nurses' innovation initiatives.

The significance of the index system of nurse's innovation competence evaluation to clinical practice

In this study, the first-level items include innovation person, innovation process, innovation press, and innovation product. Regarding the dimension of 'innovation subject', we consider factors such as the nurse's technical title, education background, nurse innovation studio, teaching architecture, and nurse's base. This reflects the importance of hospital managers providing diverse platforms to support the specialized nursing development of clinical nurses. Additionally, it is crucial to offer more opportunities for clinical nurses to engage with various forms of innovation. In the dimension of 'innovation process', we take into consideration factors such as the acceptance of new methods, new things, and new ideas, as well as the application of new or original theories to new scenarios.We hope that nurses can actively demonstrate their subjective initiative by identifying and analyzing clinical nursing problems, and fostering ideas and motivation for innovative activities. Since innovation is a complex process filled with uncertainties and challenges, nurses require supportive resources and guidance for innovative practices to facilitate the realization of innovative behavior. Research has also indicated that leaders' abilities, knowledge levels, knowledge sharing, creativity, and a conducive atmosphere for innovation are the primary factors influencing nurses' innovative behavior ([[25], [26], [27]]). Therefore, in the dimension of 'Innovation environment and pressure', factors such as the hospital and department atmosphere and support are considered to promote the development of nursing scientific research activities and enhance nurses' research capabilities. When nurses encounter clinical nursing problems, they can utilize relevant resources and effective platforms to analyze and address these issues with an evidence-based and scientific approach. In terms of 'innovation product', the evaluation of nurses' innovation competence encompasses a range of forms including papers, patents, industry standards, and more.

Preliminary application of nurse's innovation competence evaluation index system

In this study, we preliminary validated the practicability of nurse's innovation competence evaluation index. We found that hospital should pay more attention on nurse's innovation competence cultivation and supports. Firstly, the innovation output of clinical nurses and the training of innovation persons needs further supported. In the first level indicators, ‘innovation product’ score (3.20 ± 1.01) and ‘innovation subject’ score were lower (3.33 ± 1.47). It shows that in the process of nursing innovation activities, nurses can actively find and analyze clinical nursing problems, generate the ideas and willingness of innovative activities, and conduct patent application and clinical application. However, innovation itself is a complex process which full of doubts and obstacles, and it is usually difficult for nurses to obtain favorable resource support and innovative practice guidance, so that nurses rarely further realize innovative ideas, thus hindering the realization of innovation behavior. The research highlights the importance of nurses' innovative behavior in advancing the nursing discipline, enhancing nursing quality, and realizing the value of individual nurses. Hospital managers should allocate resources to support nurses with innovative ideas, assess the value of their programs, and foster a culture of ongoing scientific research and innovation. While clinical nurses demonstrate a strong willingness to innovate, they may require additional support to enhance their innovation and transformation capabilities. Key indicators of the innovation process include the ability to embrace new methods, technologies, and ideas, as well as conducting new practices and engaging in scientific research. However, indicators related to innovation products, aside from patents, receive lower scores, suggesting that while clinical nurses are eager to innovate, they may need more support and scientific training to strengthen their skills. By engaging in nursing scientific research and enhancing their research abilities, nurses can effectively analyze and address clinical nursing challenges with evidence-based and scientific approaches. Hospital managers should establish diverse platforms to nurture nurses' innovation capabilities.

Nurses rank high in title and academic structure, but score low in 'Number of bases', 'Number of innovation studios', and 'Number of key disciplines'. Hospital managers should establish diverse platforms to support clinical nurses in developing specialized nursing skills. Additionally, they should provide clinical nurses with more opportunities to participate in discipline development, join various innovation studios, and engage in environments focused on innovation and transformation. This will help foster an innovative atmosphere within the nursing profession.

Limitations

This study has several limitations. Firstly, the experts came from 1 cities and 2 municipalities, which can not represent all nurses of different regions. Secondly, the established index system for nurse's innovation competence evaluation is not verified, so the representativeness still depends. Thirdly, the involved expert's number is not big, which may affect the outcome of Delphi results. Fourthly, this index system is only used in 1 hospital, which should be validated and certified in big samples in future studies.

Conclusion

The evaluation index system for nurses' innovation competence was constructed through two rounds of expert consultations, including 4 first-level indicators, 9 s-level indicators and 36 third-level indicators, which were scientific, reasonable and practicable. After preliminary application, the index system proved to be practical and can be used in clinical setting. The construction of this index provides references for the scientific evaluation of nurses' innovative ability and the way of promoting nurse's innovation competence.
