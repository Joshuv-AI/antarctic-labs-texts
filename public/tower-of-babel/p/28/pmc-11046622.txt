Reliability of BNP to exclude heart failure
Authors: Myat Lwin, Kyar Chi Kyaw Win, Ben Sieniewicz
Published: 2024

Background

Before 2022, University Hospitals Plymouth NHS Trust (UHP) and Devon clinical commissioning group were using BNP (B-type natriuretic peptide) to screen patients for heart failure. BNP is cheaper than NTproBNP (N-terminal B-type natriuretic peptide) but is less stable. When the blood sample is left for a long period of time (ie at the surgery after being taken by a GP while awaiting transfer to hospital labs), the result can be artificially lower. We wanted to identify any patients who were initially thought not to have a heart failure diagnosis on the basis of a low BNP (<100) who were then subsequently diagnosed with heart failure at a later date. Following the audit, NTproBNP has been used at UHP and we are planning to re-audit.

Objective

To identify patients with normal BNP with subsequent diagnosis of heart failure.

Methods

In total, 104 patients with normal BNP who had borderline blood sample taking duration (6–8 hours from blood sampling to running test in laboratory) were reviewed to identify patients with subsequent diagnosis of heart failure.

Results

BNP <100 correctly identified 96% of patients as having a low likelihood of heart failure with LVSD (left ventricular systolic dysfunction) and that was confirmed on subsequent echocardiogram. However, in 4% of patients with BNP <100 (five patients), LV ejection fraction of <50% was identified on the subsequent echocardiogram (Table 1).

Conclusion

BNP is a useful screening test but given the geography of area with samples frequently being taken in rural or semi-rural GP practices and waiting for collection before being analysed at UHP, using more stable biomarkers such as NTproBNP would have advantages. According to National Institute for Health and Care Excellence guidelines, using a stable biomarker like NTproBNP to detect heart failure is recommended. Following this audit, starting from January 2022, UHP has been using NTproBNP and we are planning for another cycle of audit.
