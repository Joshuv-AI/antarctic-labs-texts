Editorial for September/October Issue of AACE Clinical Case Reports
Authors: Sina Jasim
Published: 2024

Dear Colleagues,

Welcome to another issue of AACE Clinical Case Reports (ACCR). ACCR continues to grow in both manuscript submissions and readership; this growth would not have been possible without the dedication of our associate editors, editorial board members, and editorial/publication staff. Special thank you to our excellent reviewers that provided meaningful and constructive reviews to help the educational value of the published cases.

The current issue includes interesting and educational cases to share. We will provide a summary of some of those cases below. For more details, please access ACCR online journal available at https://www.aaceclinicalcasereports.com/

Under the pituitary-gonadal-adrenal access in this issue, a case discussed an adolescent male with Noonan syndrome and celiac disease attained normal adult height with growth hormone treatment and gluten-free diet.1

A patient with isolated pituitary involvement with IgG4-related disease mimicking pituitary apoplexy was presented,2 another case reviewed changes in cortisol levels with the use of oral contraceptives with low to moderate estrogen activity;3 finally, a case of acute pancreatitis due to estrogen therapy in a transgender patient care was reviewed.4

On Diabetes, Lipids, and Metabolism, authors reported on new-onset type 1 diabetes mellitus after COVID-19 messenger RNA (mRNA) vaccination.5

The issue also included a case of non islet cell tumor hypoglycemia due to metastatic salivary myoepithelial carcinoma,6 and similar presentation secondary to malignant phyllodes tumor of the breast,7 a report on cases with stiff person syndrome and brittle type 1 Diabetes8 two visual vignettes are shared, one about insulin-induced severe Lipohypertrophy9 and the association of skin rash with gestational Diabetes Mellitus.10

In the field of thyroid disease, a case reviewed non-metastatic radioactive iodine uptake in skeletal tissue on whole-body scan in patient with differentiated thyroid carcinoma and how this might affect images interpretation.11

In the area of Bone and calcium disorders, a case discussed treating bisphosphonate-associated atypical femur fracture with a combination of Teriparatide and novel surgical techniques.12

As always, we truly appreciate all contributing authors, reviewers, editors, and staff that help improve our journal and create an educational platform to our readers to help best manage our patients.

Thank you again for your interest in ACCR. We welcome all feedback, questions, and comments from our readers. Please feel free to reach us at publications@aace.com.
