Correction to “Microencapsulated α‐Tocopherol and Moringa Extract for Improved Skin Protection: Insights From Human Skin Assessment in Cosmetic Formulations”
Published: 2025

J. C. Kessler, I. M. Martins, Y. A. Manrique, et al., “Microencapsulated α‐Tocopherol and Moringa Extract for Improved Skin Protection: Insights From Human Skin Assessment in Cosmetic Formulations,” Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology 24, no. 10 (2025): e70486, https://doi.org/10.1111/jocd.70486.
