Author Correction: A streamlined base editor engineering strategy to reduce bystander editing
Authors: Izabella Valdez, Ian O’Connor, Divesh Patel, Katherine Gierer, Jan Harrington, Ethan Ellis, Stephen A. Caponetti, Robert P. Sebra, Hillary C. Valley, Kevin Coote, Martin Mense, Samuele G. Marro, Tingting Jiang
Published: 2025

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-025-63609-6, published online 30 August 2025

In the version of the article initially published, in the second paragraph of the Results, the sentence “Some of the amino acid changes, such as N108Q and V28C, have been previously reported to attenuate bystander editing” was missing and has now been added alongside a new reference: “Perrotta, R. M. et al. Machine learning and directed evolution of base editing enzymes. bioRxiv 10.1101/2024.05.17.594556 (2024)”. This correction has been made to the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
