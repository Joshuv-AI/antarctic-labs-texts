Correction: Rewiring Recovery: Cognitive and Motor Gains Through Personalized Neurofeedback after Tumor Resection—A Case Series from Neurorehabilitation Practice
Authors: Gianvito Lagravinese, Valentina Nicolardi, Simona Aresta, Marialuisa Guglielmo, Serena Tagliente, Fabiana Montenegro, Petronilla Battista, Brendan Parsons, Stefania De Trane
Published: 2025

Correction to: Applied Psychophysiology and Biofeedback 10.1007/s10484-025-09743-9

In this article, Figs. 4 and 5 were wrongly numbered; Fig. 4 should have been Fig. 5 and vice versa as shown below.

Fig. 4Heatmap of ΔZ Scores in EEG Relative Power Across Frequency Bands Following NF TrainingEach row represents a patient; columns correspond to Delta, Theta, Alpha, Beta, and High Beta frequency bands. Warm colors (red) indicate increased deviation from the normative mean (positive ΔZ), while cool colors (blue) reflect reduced deviation (negative ΔZ). Values of |ΔZ| ≥ 2 are conventionally interpreted as clinically significant in qEEG analysis (Finnigan & van Putten, 2007; Thatcher et al., 2003)
