SPCT-08 TITLE: ALTERNATIVE PARTIAL COSTOTRANSVERSECTOMY FOR THORACIC DUMBBELL SCHWANNOMA VIA POSTERIORPARAMEDIAN APPROACH.
Authors: Souare Ibrahima sory jr
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackground and ImportanceExtramedullary intradural tumors constitute up about 25% of spinal tumors, in which schwannoma and neurofibromas are two most common tumors. Schwannomas are benign tumors, that arise from the sheaths of the nerves in the peripheral nervous system or from spinal nerve roots and therefore extra medullary in nature.Clinical PresentationA 55-year-old male patient who suffered from mechanical back pain for 6 months. MRI revealed a dumbbell tumor located on the T4-T5 levels, communicated between intra and extra thoracic spinal canal of left side with cystic change about 46 x 36 x 31 mm3 volume. Surgical resection was supplemented with a microsurgical instrument on a posterior paramedian approach. The alternative partial costotransversectomy (T4) was achieved 3cm long using high speed drill including some parts of T4 transverse process, medial aspect of the rib and pedicles (T4-5) on either side of the intervertebral foramen, to fully expose. The costal transverse process joint of T4 was removed. The tumor outside the spinal canal was removed first, then along the communicated part of the tumor, the part inside spinal canal via intervertebral foramen was dissected and completely resected, thus total resection of the tumor was achieved.Surgical Intervention StepsPosterior paramedian approach was performed, then exposure of spinal cord partial costotransversectomy and tumor resection were performed using microsurgical operation. After tumor resection, pleura cavity was repaired under microscope.ConclusionThoracic spinal canal tumors should be treated by diverse approaches to access complicated anatomical areas.
