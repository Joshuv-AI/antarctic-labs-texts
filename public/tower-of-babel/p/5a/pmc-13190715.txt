Retraction Note: Mathematical analysis of isothermal study of reverse roll coating using Micropolar fluid
Authors: Saquib Ul Zaman, Azad Hussain, Kaleem Ashraf, Lubna Sarwar, Faizan Hussain, Ali Altalbe, Ahmet Bekir, Taseer Muhammad
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-70808-6, published online 24 August 2024

The Editors have retracted this Article.

After publication, concerns were raised regarding the use of non‑standard vocabulary in the Article. Further editorial investigation identified authorship irregularities. The Authors’ responses to these concerns were not satisfactory, and they did not provide the requested underlying code. The Editors therefore no longer have confidence in the Article.

Saquib Ul Zaman did not explicitly state whether or not they agree with this retraction. Azad Hussain, Lubna Sarwar, Faizan Hussain, Ahmet Bekir and Taseer Muhammad did not respond to correspondence from the Editors about this retraction. The Editors were not able to confirm the current contact details for Kaleem Ashraf and Ali Altalbe.
