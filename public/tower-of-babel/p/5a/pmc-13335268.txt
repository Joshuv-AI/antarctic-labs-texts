Following the publication of the original article [1], the authors reported that the submitted and accepted for publication manuscript had two corresponding authors namely, Joanna Klećkowska-Nawrot (email address: joanna.kleckowska-nawrot@upwr.edu.pl), and Aleksander Chrószcz(email address: aleksander.chroszcz@upwr.edu.pl), but unfortunately, the second corresponding author is missing in final and published version. It must be corrected, due to the fact that their rules of financial support demands Aleksander Chrószcz as a corresponding author.

The original article has been corrected.
