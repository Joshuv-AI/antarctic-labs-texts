Retraction Note: NLRC5 exerts anti-endometriosis effects through inhibiting ERβ-mediated inflammatory response
Authors: Bao Guo, Haiqing Zhu, Chengwei Xiao, Jing Zhang, Xiaojing Liu, Yuan Fang, Bing Wei, Junhui Zhang, Yunxia Cao, Lei Zhan
Published: 2025

Retraction Note: BMC Medicine 22, 351 (2024)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-024-03571-0

The Chief Editor has retracted this article. A number of image integrity issues were raised by readers in a letter to the Editor. The Authors were contacted for an explanation of concerns and raw images; however, they failed to provide a satisfactory explanation and original raw images for all concerned figures. The issues are, specifically:

• In Fig. 2 K, Migration, panels NC and NC + LPS appear to contain partial overlaps.

• In Fig. 2 K, Invasion, panels NC and NC + LPS as well as ERβ and Erβ + LPS appear to contain partial overlaps.

• In Fig. 5I, Migration, panels NC and NC + LPS appear to contain partial overlaps.

• In Fig. 5I, Invasion, panels NC and NC + LPS appear to contain partial overlaps.

The Chief Editor therefore has lost confidence in these results and conclusions.

None of the authors have responded to the correspondence from the Publisher about this retraction.
