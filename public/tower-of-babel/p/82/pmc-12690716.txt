CLRD-01 RADIOTHERAPY FOR A 4-YEAR-OLD PATIENT DIAGNOSED WITH CLASSIC MEDULLOBLASTOMA IN ZIMBABWE
Authors: Laverne Hamadziripi-Sober
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackground and AimIn July 2023 a four-year-old female patient presented with a posterior fossa tumour for radiotherapy. The patient had a one-year history of postprandial vomiting, headaches and an inability to walk. A biopsy revealed the tumour to be classic medulloblastoma. A VP shunt was inserted, and mass excision was performed to debulk the tumour prior to radiation therapy.MethodsPost craniotomy, both an MRI and CT scan were performed and revealed a residual tumour (1.6x 3.2 x 2.0) cm with no leptomeningeal or spinal drop lesions. The patient was then sent for radiotherapy and chemotherapy. CT simulation was done with the patient in a supine position on a vacuum bag with a beam directional shell and headrest used for immobilisation. Whole-body images were obtained with 5mm slices from the vault of the skull to the bottom of the sacrum, with 3mm slices throughout the primary tumour. Treatment was delivered using 3D Conformal radiation therapy a month post-surgery.ResultsThe patient was prescribed 54Gy delivered in two phases. Craniospinal irradiation of 23.4Gy in 13 fractions was delivered using 2 parallel opposed cranial fields and one direct posterior field to the spine. The second phase, a boost to the posterior fossa delivered 30.6Gy in 17 fractions using 2 parallel opposed cranial fields. Multi leaf collimators were used to shield extracranial structures and field feathering was done weekly. Dexamethasone was used for vasogenic edema, and 8 cycles of chemotherapy were given prior to radiotherapy.ConclusionThe patient was able to complete treatment without severe late effects and with a single interruption. Reactions noted were erythema, vasogenic edema and fatigue, these all resolved post treatment and the patient has since regained the ability to walk. The case was well managed although advanced modalities would have provided an added advantage in its management.
