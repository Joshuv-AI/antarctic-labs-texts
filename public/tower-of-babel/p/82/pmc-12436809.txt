Assessing CIAS in clinical routine
Authors: A. Erfurth, G. Sachs
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractCognitive impairment associated with schizophrenia (CIAS) is a relevant problem and can be well assessed in different ways, as described in an EPA guidance paper (1). In routine clinical practice, the clinician usually relies on targeted questions to elicit psychopathological findings, as described in the AMDP system (2), for example.
