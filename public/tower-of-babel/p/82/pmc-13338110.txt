Entropy generation analysis of radiative magnetohydrodynamic Maxwell hybrid ternary nanofluid flow over an inclined porous sheet
Authors: Amir Abbas, Abid Hussanan, Muhammad Farman, Evren Hincal, Mohamed Ahmed Hafez, Betty Wan Niu Voon, Wubale Demis Alamirew, Kaouther Ghachem, Lioua Kolsi
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
The energy produced by the non-renewable resources is facing challenges due to high energy consumption. So, to compensate this depletion in energy production, scientists are focusing on the production of energy from renewable sources like solar energy, etc. The current study is concentrated to the solar energy enhancement which can be achieved by improving the efficiency of the energy producing devices like solar thermal collectors and photovoltaic-thermal systems by passing the ternary nanofluid in these energy producing systems. The present work deals with the Maxwell ternary nanofluid flow and heat transfer past inclined linearly permeable stretching sheet embedded in porous media. Effects of Lorentz force, solar radiation and suction of the surface are incorporated into the current mechanism. The entropy generation analysis is carried out to optimize the cooling process inside the thermal systems. The solutions of the transformed ordinary differential equations are computed using boundary value problem 4th order collocation technique-based solver. The results indicate the increasing nanoparticles volume fraction enhances temperature of fluid and controls velocity of fluid. Growing Maxwell fluid parameter and magnetic field parameter reduces the velocity of the fluid and raises the temperature of the fluid as well solar radiation improves the temperature of the fluid flow domain. The suction parameter controls the boundary layer thickness. The increasing Brinkman number enhances entropy generation and decreases Bejan number. The numerical computations were performed using boundary value problem 4th order collocation technique-based solver for different ranges of the dimensionless parameters, namely, 0.6 ≤ Br ≤ 1.0, 0.1 ≤ S ≤ 1.5, 1.1 ≤ M ≤ 6.1, 0.1 ≤ λ ≤ 0.9, 0.01 ≤ ϕ1, ϕ2, ϕ3 ≤ 0.05, 0.1 ≤ λ1 ≤ 3.1, 1.0 ≤ Pr ≤ 7.0, 1.1 ≤ Rd ≤ 6.1, 0.1 ≤ K ≤ 5.1, 0.1 ≤ α1 ≤ 0.5 and α = π/6. Sensitivity analysis to determine the variations in output by changing the parameters as input. The grid independent test has been carried out to guarantee grid independent convergence of the numerical solutions. Recent results are equated with already existing outcomes for the validation of the present modeled code.Graphical abstract

Introduction

Usual fluids like water, ethylene glycol, etc., contain low thermal conductivities which do not serve the purpose in efficient way in the cooling systems. Therefore, scientists paid focus on the new type of fluids called nanofluids. These fluids are engineered suspending nanoparticles in water, ethylene glycol, etc. These fluids are extensively used in the heat exchangers, thermal management systems, solar collectors, and electronic devices. So, the researchers started to work on such class of fluids. The primary goal of developing nanofluids is to achieve maximum thermal conductivity at low nanoparticle concentrations through uniform nanoparticle distribution and stable nanoparticle suspension in the base fluids. Choi [1] initially used the term "nanofluid" to describe a novel class of heat transfer fluids based on nanotechnology that had higher thermal conductivities than common fluids. The findings of this study can be viewed as the foundation for employing nanoparticles to enhance the thermophysical characteristics of fluids. According to an experimental study [2], nanofluids only require a volumetric fraction of five percent of nanoparticles to effectively improve heat transmission. A Brownian motion and thermophoresis effect-based analytical model for convective transport in nanofluids was created by Buongiorno [3]. Buongiorno proposed a two-phase nanofluid model in which control of Brownian motion and thermophoresis were identified as the two dominant mechanisms governing nanoparticle transport within the base fluid. Khan and Pop [4] discussed the boundary layer analysis of the nanofluid on the linearly stretching sheet. In contrast to Buongiorno model of nanofluid model Tiwari and Das [5] developed a single-phase model of nanofluid that attracted considerable attention from scientists and researchers a s lot. In the present study, the single phase of nanofluid model is considered.

Later on, it was found that if two types of nanoparticles are mixed in the based fluids, then these becomes more efficient than simple fluids and nanofluids because these fluids have more thermal conductivities than previously mentioned. Therefore, the research community picked up this class of nanofluids as important in efficient cooling systems. Turcu et al. [6] studied hybrid nanofluid by adding multi wall carbon nanotubes and magnetic \documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$F{e}_{3}{O}_{4}$$\end{document} as nanoparticles in water. These fluids have diverse applications in thermal solar collectors, cooling systems and heat exchangers, etc. Devi and Devi [7] investigated the copper and aluminum oxide and water-based hybrid nonfluids flow and heat transfer on porous linear sheet under magnetic field control. In [8] generalized heat and mass transfer laws on Williamson hybrid nanofluid with magnetic force effects were discussed. The considered the flow in squeezing channel fixed into porous space along viscous dissipating force and chemical reactions. The study on the importance of the single walls carbon nanotubes and multi walls carbon nanotubes in the flow of hybrid nanofluids along with microorganisms and surface tension impacts has been carried out in [9]. Gahoor et al. [10] time dependent flow of Casson hybrid nanofluid with Caputo-Fabrizio fractional derivatives model under the inclined magnetic field influence along the surface of the infinite plate. Zulqarnain et al. [11] scrutinized entropy generation in non-Newtonian second-grade fuzzy hybrid nanofluid along stretching and shrinking sheet exponentially porous sheet. Mushahary and Ontela [12] proposed the research observation on the radiative hybrid nanofluid magnetohydrodynamic flow through porous space with thermal conditions along with hall and ion-slip influences. Aich et al. [13] explored the influencing trend of the inclined magnetic field on chemically reacting hybrid nanofluid in Maxwell model in Darci-Forchheimer media. The researcher kept the pace of the improvements in efficiency of thermal energy systems and ultimately, they found a new class of fluids that is more efficient than simple fluid, nanofluids and hybrid nanofluids called ternary nanofluids. These have much thermal conductivities than previously outlined ones. They have various application various applications, including energy storge, heat exchangers, and cooling systems. Their use in the aerospace and automotive industries can significantly reduce emissions and improve fuel efficiency. Moreover, ternary hybrid nanofluids hold great potential in biomedical and electronic fields, where their unique properties enable the development of advanced thermal management solutions. Overall, (nanofluids, mono-nanofluids, hybrid nanofluids, and ternary nanofluids), have extensive range of applications. Due to potential applications of the ternary nanofluids in engineering and industrial fields, research scientists took this topic as of significance and contributed a lot in the literature. Variations in behavior of Newtonian fluid to non-Newtonian occurs when nanoparticles are mixed into base fluids. So, to understand better the heat transfer process, the consideration of non-Newtonian model is an appropriate. Maxwell fluid model is most important non-Newtonian model. Maxwell fluids have applications in polymer solutions, suspensions, emulsions, and biological fluids such as blood and synovial fluid. Consequently, extensive research has been conducted on Maxwell fluid because of applications in fluid dynamics and engineering. Maxwell fluid model with thermal radiation and magnetohydrodynamic in the porous media is worth significant in engineering and industrial fields on the inclined stretching sheets and other stretching sheets. Rauf et al. [22] focused on Maxwell hybrid nanofluid model on permeable linearly stretching surface with magnetic field effects. The thermal radiation, thermal diffusion and diffusion thermo effects were also included in their study. In [23] analyzed the mechanism of heat and mass transportation in Maxwell fluid with chemical reaction, solar radiation and heat source and sink effects. Simulation process of problem of Maxwell nanofluid flow across sheet stretching in linear manner with magnetic field dipoles was carried out in [24]. Due to applications of Maxwell fluid across inclined sheet in engineering and industries, Jeelani and her co-researchers studied Maxwell hybrid nanofluid models across inclined sheer under impact of solar radiation and magnetic field in porous media are shown in [25–27]. The relevant non-Newtonian fluids models with distinct characteristics are analyzed in studies [28, 29]. The artificial neural network analysis on Maxwell fluid model with non-linear solar radiation and electro-osmotic conditions is reflected in [30].

Entropy generation analysis is broadly utilized to assess irreversibly, loss of the energy, and thermodynamics effectiveness in engineering and industrial systems. Its primary applications include, the heat transfer system optimization, thermal efficiency of the heat exchangers, thermal cooling of the electronic devices, heat transfer enhancement and to improve the renewable energy technology. These real-world applications triggered the researcher minded community engage with this process with different flow phenomenon. Nawaz and Arif [31] considered analysis of entropy generation in Maxwell hybrid nanofluid under the impact of magnetic force. Optimization of the entropy generation in Maxwell nanofluid flow and heat transfer process along stretching sheet exponentially in control of velocity slip condition has been shown in [32]. Jameel et al. [33] encountered the entropy generation analysis in the non-Darcy porous media coupled with Maxwell nanofluid under the generalized the transfer law and Joule heating outcomes. The study on entropy generation analysis in magnetohydrodynamic effect on ternary nanofluid with variable properties in channel considered vertically is highlighted in Ref. [34]. Murugan et al. [35] elaborated the entropy generation analysis in Maxwell fluid along the inclined sheet with dissipation influence.

Motivation

Due to the significant application of the ternary nanofluid and Maxwell fluids, several research studies carried out to the magnetohydrodynamic Maxwell ternary nanofluid flow and heat transfer mechanism along the stretching sheet in different parametric conditions. Also, in the sperate studies, a lot of focus is paid on flow mechanisms of the ternary Newtonian nanofluid the inclined sheet with suction impacts. The entropy generation analysis with Maxwell fluid model in hybrid nanofluid model and ternary nanofluids models has been carried out. But there are lot of gaps in in the examination of fluids flow along the porous inclined sheet with different conditions. In the bridging these gaps, the current study is carried out in one frame of research work. This research endeavor is expected to enhance our thoughtfulness of this complex field and provide valuable insights that encourage and inspire further investigation in the present study.

Current study examines combined impact of angle of inclination, magnetic field, and solar radiation influences on the Maxwell ternary nanofluid flow along the linearly stretching sheet. Results and discussion on entropy generation analysis

Entropy generation is the creation of entropy inside a system due to irreversible physical processes like viscous dissipation, diffusion, magnetic effects, fluid resistance, heat transportation, and viscous friction, etc. In the Figs. 24 and 25 shows the parametric influence of Brinkman number on entropy generation and Bejan number \documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$Br$$\end{document}, respectively. The results shows that when \documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$Br$$\end{document} rises then \documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$Ng$$\end{document} increases and Bejan number decreases. By increasing the Brinkman number, the viscous dissipation rises in fluid flow domain. The increased friction of fluid converts kinetic energy more into the thermal energy, leading to enhancement in thermodynamic irreversibility and hence entropy generation \documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$Ng$$\end{document}. At the same time, fluid friction irreversibility over the thermal irreversibility declines the contribution of heat transfer induced entropy generation, which leads to the increment in Bejan number. Conclusion

Current research work carries out entropy generation analysis in ternary nanofluid flow modeled with Maxwell fluid along permeable stretching inclined sheet embedded through porous media. The physical influences of thermal radiation, magnetic field and suction on flow and heat transfer mechanism have been performed. The main outcomes are outlines as fellow:

The increasing volume fraction of the nanoparticles lead to enhance temperature of fluid and reduces fluid velocity. The increasing concentration of nanoparticles increases the viscosity of the fluid and hence increase the resistance with in the fluid and increases the thermal energy.Intensifying Maxwell fluid parameter declines the velocity distribution due to increasing dominance of the elastic nature of the fluid over the viscous nature.The increasing magnetic field leaves the decreasing influence on velocity field and increasing impact on the temperature of the fluid.Therma radiation parameter enhancement surges fluid’s temperature according to the physic of the inclusion of thermal radiation.The rise in the suction parameter cuts fluid velocity.The grid stability test is carried out for validation of the numerical scheme that it is independent of the number of grid intendent.The sensitivity analysis for the physical properties is performed to observe that how much stronger effects the pertinent parameter on the output variables.Results show that growing Prandtl number leaves swelling skin friction coefficient and Nusselt number.Results show while Brinkman number increases then entropy generation increases and Bejan number reduces.Comparison of results with published solutions has been shown indicating excellent closeness is seen ensuring accuracy of current solutions.
