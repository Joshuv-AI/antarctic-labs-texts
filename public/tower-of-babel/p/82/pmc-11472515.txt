Correction: The importance of claudin-7 palmitoylation on membrane subdomain localization and metastasis-promoting activities
Authors: Sarah Heiler, Wei Mu, Margot Zöller, Florian Thuma
Published: 2024

Correction: Cell Commun Signal 13, 29 (2015)

10.1186/s12964-015-0105-y

Following the publication of the original article [1], the authors noticed errors in Fig. 2b/9c and 4a. The authors apologize for the errors and would like to emphasize that these do not change the scientific conclusions of the article in any way.

Errors in Fig. 2b/9c.

In the published article the same images appear incorrect and have different magnification, in Fig. 2b (HEK-EpC-cld7(mP), cld7 red and cld3 green staining) and 9c (HEK-cld7, cld7 red and CD147 green). This was an error. The authors checked their archive and established that the images of the HEK-cld7 cells were correct. The incorrect and correct Fig. 2b appears below.

Incorrect Fig. 2b.

Correct Fig. 2b.

Error in Fig. 4a.

The authors regret that in Fig. 4a, the same images mistakenly appear in the row for time point 48 h of the wound healing assay of HEK-EpC-cld7 and HEK-EpC(mAG)-cld7 cells. The authors checked their archive and established that the image of the HEK-EpC(mAG)-cld7 cells was correct. A incorrect and corrected version of Fig. 4a are given in this correction article.

Correct Fig. 4a.
