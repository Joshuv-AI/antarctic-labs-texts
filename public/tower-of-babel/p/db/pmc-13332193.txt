Author Correction: Economical biogas direct methanation to pipeline grade natural gas via structured Ni based inverse catalyst
Authors: Xin Tang, Xincheng Zhang, Ziyu Song, Chuqiao Song, Yao Xu, Xuguang Yang, Yuanchang Wang, Hanfeng Lu, Siyu Yao, Zuwei Liao, Xiaonian Li, Ding Ma, Lili Lin
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-025-68119-z, published online 07 January 2026

In the version of this article initially published, due to a preparation mistake, a placeholder image and Source Data for Fig. 4c were published in place of the exact experimental stability data. The figure and Source Data are now replaced. For comparison, the original and revised Fig. 4c images are available as Supplementary Information accompanying this amendment. This correction does not change any results, discussion or conclusions of the study. The changes are made in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
