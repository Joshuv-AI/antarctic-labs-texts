Correction for Bonetti et al., Decoding the elite soccer player’s psychological profile
Published: 2025

Correction for “Decoding the elite soccer player’s psychological profile,” by Leonardo Bonetti, Torbjōrn Vestberg, Reza Jafari, Debora Seghezzi, Martin Ingvar, Morten L. Kringelbach, Alberto Filgueiras, and Predrag Petrovic, which published January 14, 2025; 10.1073/pnas.2415126122 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 122, e2415126122).

The authors note that they incorrectly stated in the following sections that two-sample t tests were computed: in the legend for Fig. 2; in Results, the first sentence under Cognitive and Personality Differences between Soccer Players and Controls; in the legend for Table 1; and in Materials and Methods, the first and second paragraphs under Cognitive and Personality Profile of Soccer Players Against Controls. The tests were instead one-sample t tests against a fixed mean. The article has been corrected.
