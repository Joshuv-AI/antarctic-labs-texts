Following the publication of the original article [1], it is reported that in Fig. 4, the β-actin band in Fig. 4C was mistakenly used as the FIS1 protein band in Fig. 4B. It was merely an error in the use of the band image, and the corresponding statistical values remain correct. Importantly, this error does not affect the results and conclusion of the study.

Additionally, in section “MA alleviated the oxidative damage”, “decreased” was mistakenly spelled as “increased” in the sentence: “MDA and 8-OHdG content significantly increased in the MA group compared with the CON group (P < 0.05, Fig. 7G and H)”, which should be corrected to: “MDA and 8-OHdG content significantly decreased in the MA group compared with the CON group (P < 0.05, Fig. 7G and H)”. The relevant statistical results and descriptions in the discussion section are correct, and this error does not affect the results and conclusion of the study.
