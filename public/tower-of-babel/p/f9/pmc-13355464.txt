Advancing Gastro Hep Advances: Building on Success, Looking Forward!
Authors: Anisa Shaker, Associate Editors, Mohamed I Elsaid, Mauricio S Garcia, Alexandra Gutierrez, Kathryn Peterson, Anand S Jain, Michael A Schumacher, Takeshi Saito, Aparajita Singh, Amol Sharma
Published: 2026

We are excited to share the next chapter of Gastro Hep Advances (GHA), a vital part of the American Gastrointestinal Association (AGA) journal family. Since its inaugural issue in January 2022, GHA has established itself as a broad scope, online, open access home for high-quality scientific and clinical knowledge in gastroenterology and hepatology for a diverse and international audience. We owe a tremendous debt of gratitude to the founding coeditors-in-chief, Drs Shanthi Srinivasan and Vinod Rustgi and the inaugural editorial board. Their leadership and vision were the driving force behind the successful launch of GHA, increasing the number of issues to 12 per year, indexing of the journal in PubMed, and securing the journal’s first impact factor. They have truly established a strong foundation, and we look forward to building on this.

As we enter this next phase, it is our privilege to work alongside an exceptional team of associate editors and an international editorial board whose collective experience spans the breadth of gastroenterology and hepatology. The scientific depth of this team encompasses basic, translational, and clinical research. As such, GHA will continue to provide a high-quality home for methodologically rigorous and impactful research across the scientific spectrum. In addition to original research, invited reviews, and the very highly received series Pearls from the Pros, we aim to further expand the journal’s scope to include emerging and interdisciplinary areas of increasing importance. These include artificial intelligence, the microbiome, bioinformatics, implementation science, and the business of medicine. We are equally committed to fostering a platform for multidisciplinary perspectives and to highlighting areas that may be underrepresented in traditional publishing venues.

We remain particularly enthusiastic about engaging early career investigators, trainees, and contributors from diverse disciplines, as well as strengthening connections across the AGA community. GHA is uniquely positioned to serve as a bridge between investigators and clinicians, across gastroenterology and hepatology subspecialties, and among the AGA journals, promoting collaboration, transparency, and the efficient dissemination of knowledge that advances science in the ultimate pursuit of improving patient care.

As we move forward, our commitment remains steadfast as follows: to uphold the highest standards of scientific rigor, fairness, and editorial excellence while ensuring timely and accessible publication. We will continue to adapt to the evolving landscape of scientific publishing, leveraging innovation while maintaining the trust of our authors, reviewers, and readers.

We are excited for this next 5-year chapter of GHA and look forward to working collaboratively with our authors, reviewers, and readers to further advance the science and practice of gastroenterology and hepatology. We welcome your feedback, your engagement, and your submissions as we continue this journey together. We hope you will share in our excitement!

Anisa Shaker

Editor-in-Chief
