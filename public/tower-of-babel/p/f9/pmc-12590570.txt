Comprehensive assessment of 5 interleukin inhibitors for the treatment of psoriasis: scientific guidance based on drug selection recommendations for Chinese medical institutions
Authors: Jia-Xin Zhang, Wen-Wei Li, Zhi-Kun Qiu, Sha Lai
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
ABSTRACTBackgroundBased on the best available evidence, rapid health technology was used to assess 5 interleukin inhibitors approved for marketing in China. This assessment aims to promote the rational use of drugs in the clinic and provide a reference basis for the selection of drugs by medical institutions in China.MethodsDepending on the Rapid Guidelines for Drug Evaluation and Selection in Chinese Medical Institutions (the Second Edition), we evaluated the pharmacological properties, efficacy, safety, economy, and other attributes of interleukin inhibitors by assigning scores.ResultsThe rankings of composite scores, from highest to lowest, were as follows: ixekizumab with 68.8 points, secukinumab with 65.47 points, ustekinumab with 65.41 points, tildrakizumab with 62.6 points, and guselkumab with 61.64 points, taking into account the results from the 5 dimensions.ConclusionUntil the guideline recommendations, the National Essential Drug List, clinical studies, and many other dimensions of this assessment are updated, ixekizumab, secukinumab, and ustekinumab, which have the top 3 scores, can be used as a priority recommendation for Chinese medical institutions to select interleukin inhibitors and optimise the use of the drug catalog based on the scoring results of this assessment.KEYWORDS: Interleukin inhibitors, psoriasis, drug selection, quantitative scoring, ixekizumab

Background

Psoriasis is an immune-mediated, chronic, relapsing, inflammatory, and systemic disease characterised by scaly erythematous plaques, which are caused by a combination of genetic and environmental factors. According to epidemiological statistics, the overall prevalence rate was gradually increasing, with the north being higher than the south in China. It was estimated that the number of psoriasis patients in my country is more than 7 million (Committee on Psoriasis, 2023). Plaque psoriasis is the most common type, accounting for more than 80% of psoriasis cases. Men and women were affected by psoriasis equally, but adults more than children were affected by it (Chinese Society of Dermatology & Medicine, 2021).

Approximately 3% of the US population was affected by psoriasis, and an estimated 125 million people worldwide were affected by it (Armstrong & Read, 2020). At present, ustekinumab, secukinumab, ixekizumab, guselkumab, and tildrakizumab are the common interleukin inhibitors to treat psoriasis. The five interleukin inhibitors can effectively alleviate the disease and regulate the immune system specifically. Therefore, treatment recommendations were made in the 2021 version of the ‘Guidelines for the treatment of psoriasis with biologic agents in China’ (Chinese Society of Dermatology & Medicine, 2021).

Health technology assessment (HTA) is a field of scientific policy research that adopts multidisciplinary approaches to conduct a systematic evaluation of health technologies and provide multi-dimensional evidence comprising effectiveness, safety, economic implications, ethical, social, cultural, and legal issues, in which economic evaluation is an important and unique part for optimising decision-making. Many health authorities and hospitals in the world have applied rHTA in the decision-making of drug access and payment (Chen et al., 2018; M. Li et al., 2025). To promote the rational use of medicines in clinical practice, we utilise authoritative technologies and mes from both domestic and international sources to scientifically evaluate and select medications. We aim to guide medical institutions in drug evaluation and selection and enhance the public's sense of gain, security, and happiness.2 Based on the second edition of ‘A Quick Guideline for Drug Evaluation and Selection in Chinese Medical Institutions (the Second Edition)’ (hereinafter referred to as the ‘Quick Guide’), a rapid health assessment of the five interleukin inhibitors in the first-line treatment of psoriasis will be conducted in this article (Zhao Z et al., 2023).

Methods

Evaluation basis

Based on the ‘Rapid Guidelines for Drug Evaluation and Selection in Chinese Medical Institutions (Second Edition)’ published in 2023, and applying a percentage assessment model to evaluate 5 Interleukin inhibitors. In terms of pharmacological properties, the main evaluation of the drug's pharmacological effects, in vivo processes, whether the pharmacology and methods of use are clear, the length of the drug's expiration date, and storage requirements. In terms of drug effectiveness, the primary evaluation was based on clinical efficacy data and the strength of recommendations from authoritative sources, such as clinical guidelines and expert consensus. Specifically, efficacy was assessed using PASI 90 response rates reported at weeks 12–16, which are commonly adopted as endpoints in pivotal clinical trials and are consistently available across most agents. This approach allowed for standardised comparison across interleukin inhibitors. Although agents such as guselkumab and ixekizumab may demonstrate similar PASI90 rates at longer time points (e.g. six months), differences in early response – such as faster onset of efficacy observed with ixekizumab – were taken into account in the scoring process, in line with the emphasis on early treatment benefit in clinical practice (Blauvelt et al., 2021). Regarding safety, the evaluation mainly focuses on adverse events, drug use in special populations, and drug interactions. In this study, we defined ‘serious adverse events’ (SAEs) according to ICH E2A guidelines (ICH, 1994) as events resulting in death, life-threatening conditions, inpatient hospitalisation or its prolongation, persistent or significant disability/incapacity. ‘Severe adverse events’ refer to the intensity of the adverse event, which does not necessarily indicate seriousness in outcome. In terms of economics, the drug's average daily therapeutic cost is being evaluated. Information on the availability of drugs is evaluated.

Data source

We searched Chinese databases including VIP, CNKI, Wanfang, and CBM, as well as English databases such as PubMed, Embase, Up To Date, and official websites including FDA, WHO, NMPA, National Medical Products Administration, and enterprise websites to access relevant literature, drug instructions, and drug registration data. To provide data on the guideline recommendations, indications, pharmacological effects, in vivo processes, clinical efficacy, prices, and adverse reactions of the five interleukin inhibitors. Based on the scoring criteria of the quick guide, the pharmacological characteristics, efficacy, safety, economy, and other attributes of interleukin inhibitors have been objectively quantified to facilitate their selection and rational use in the treatment of psoriasis.

Evaluated drug and contents

Drugs Based on commonly used drugs in clinical practice, the five interleukin inhibitors approved for marketing in China have been selected for evaluation. These include ustekinumab (45 mg/0.5 ml/vial and 90 mg/1 ml/vial, Janssen Cilag International NV 2025a), secukinumab (150 mg/1 ml/vial, Novartis Pharma Schweiz AG 2025), ixekizumab (80 mg/1 ml/vial, Eli Lilly and Company), guselkumab (100 mg/1 ml/vial, Janssen Cilag International NV 2025b), and tildrakizumab (100 mg/1 ml/vial, Sun Pharmaceutical Industries) (Table 1). The evaluation of the 5 Interleukin inhibitors included an assessment of their pharmacologic properties (28%), efficacy (27%), safety (25%), economy (10%), and other attributes (10%) (Figure 1).

Results

Pharmacological properties score (28%)

Pharmacological effects

Interleukin (IL)-12, IL-17A, and IL-23 are cytokines naturally present in the body, playing crucial roles in inflammation and immune responses. Studies have shown that the levels of IL-12, IL-17A, and IL-23 in patients with psoriasis are increased, which is the cause of rash, desquamation, and induration in patients with psoriasis (Bai et al., 2019). Interleukin inhibitors can specifically bind to the corresponding IL to inhibit the release of pro-inflammatory cytokines and chemokines, thereby achieving therapeutic effects. The five interleukin inhibitors were studied, in which ixekizumab and secukinumab inhibit IL-17A by specifically binding to it, thereby suppressing downstream inflammatory and immune responses. Guselkumab and tildrakizumab target the IL-23p19 subunit to inhibit the release of proinflammatory cytokines and chemokines. Notably, recent studies have suggested that IL-23p19-targeting agents may also influence IL-39, a novel cytokine that shares the same p19 subunit (Singh et al., 2021); however, the clinical relevance of this potential interaction remains unclear. Ustekinumab binds to both IL-12 and IL-23, thereby broadly blocking their involvement in inflammatory pathways (Simopoulou et al., 2023). Given these excellent pharmacological effects, interleukin inhibitors were recommended as biological agents for the treatment of psoriasis. In summary, the clinical efficacy, mechanism of action, and targets of the five interleukin inhibitors were all relatively clear, scoring 5 points each.

Drug in vivo process

The drug instructions (Markham, 2016, 2017, 2018; Philipp et al., 2020; Sanford & McKeage, 2015) indicated that the in vivo processes of the five interleukin inhibitors were clear except for the biotransformation pharmacokinetic parameters of the drugs. The degradation of the five interleukin inhibitors into small peptides and amino acids through the same catabolic pathway as endogenous immunoglobulins was predicted to be the metabolic pathway of the five interleukin inhibitors; therefore, all received 3 points.

Pharmacy and usage

The drug instructions pointed out that the main ingredients and excipients of the five interleukin inhibitors were clear, and they all scored 2 points. In the treatment of moderate to severe plaque psoriasis, ustekinumab was administered as a subcutaneous injection of 45 mg in the first week, followed by a 45 mg subcutaneous injection after 4 weeks and every 12 weeks thereafter. Its specifications were 45 mg/0.5 ml/bottle and 90 mg/1 ml/bottle. Secukinumab was injected subcutaneously at 300 mg at weeks 0, 1, 2, 3, and 4 and every 4 weeks thereafter, and had only one specification of 150 mg/1 ml/bottle. Ixekizumab had a specification of 80 mg/1 ml/bottle, which was injected subcutaneously at 160 mg at week 0, followed by 80 mg subcutaneously at 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 weeks, and every 4 weeks after 12 weeks. Guselkumab was injected subcutaneously at 100 mg once every 8 weeks at weeks 0, 4, and after 4 weeks, and had a specification of 100 mg/1 ml/bottle. Tildrakizumab had a specification of 100 mg/1 ml/bottle, which was administered subcutaneously at 100 mg at weeks 0, 4, and every 12 weeks thereafter. The five interleukin inhibitors were all subcutaneous injections, so all scored 1.5 points. The specifications and packaging of the drugs were suitable for clinical requirements and dosage adjustment, so they all scored 2 points. The dosage of the five drugs was fixed, except for situations such as adverse drug reactions and personalised medication that required adjustment of dosage, so they all scored 2 points. Although the frequency of administration was different, they were all less than once a day and scored 2 points. Besides, they could be self-administered after being trained by others and scored 1.5 points.

In summary, the five interleukin inhibitors received the same score in the pharmacy and usage section.

Storage conditions score

The drug instructions indicated that the five interleukin inhibitors need to be stored at 2-8°C. Additionally, the drug instructions mention the need for light protection during storage and prohibit freezing and shaking. Therefore, all five interleukin inhibitors only scored 1 point.

Drug expiration date score

The validity period of ustekinumab and tildrakizumab was 36 months, and scored 1.5 points; the validity period of secukinumab, ixekizumab, and guselkumab was 24 months, and all scored 1 point. In summary, the pharmacological properties scores of ustekinumab, secukinumab, ixekizumab, guselkumab, and tildrakizumab were 21.5, 21, 21, 21, and 21.5 points, respectively (Table 2). Efficacy score (27%)

Indications

According to evidence-based research, clinical data analysis, and authoritative sources such as the 2021 ‘Guidelines for the Treatment of Psoriasis with Biologic Agents in China,’ multiple treatment options are available for psoriasis, including glucocorticoids, phosphodiesterase inhibitors, and vitamin D analogs. Therefore, interleukin inhibitors are not the only option; all agents in this category were assigned 1 point.

Guideline recommendation

The grading of drug recommendations is variable across guidelines. According to the American Academy of Dermatology (AAD-NPF) guidelines, the combined use of topical corticosteroids with biologics for the treatment of moderate to severe psoriasis is assigned a Grade III C recommendation, indicating that some supporting evidence exists, though further validation is required (Elmets et al., 2021). The 2023 guidelines from the Dermatological Society of Singapore recommend the use of Secukinumab, Ixekizumab, Guselkumab, and Ustekinumab, with Grade II C evidence, showing their effectiveness in treating both pediatric and adult patients with severe chronic plaque psoriasis (Oon et al., 2024). Regarding vaccination, the consensus guidelines of the National Psoriasis Foundation indicate that good vaccine responses are elicited by Secukinumab and Ixekizumab, which are rated as high (High), while Guselkumab and Tildrakizumab, due to the lack of vaccination studies, are rated as moderate (Moderate) (Chat et al., 2024). The German S3 guidelines for psoriasis treatment do not provide specific evidence levels for the use of IL-17 and IL-23 inhibitors but recommend that patients be considered for enrolment in a psoriasis registry to monitor treatment outcomes (Nast et al., 2021a, 2021b) (Table 3). Therefore, all interleukin inhibitors received 7 points.

Clinical efficacy

According to the systematic review and network meta-analysis of these five interleukin inhibitors, the psoriasis area and severity index (PASI) 90 was selected as the primary efficacy indicator, and the PASI 75 was selected as the secondary efficacy indicator. Therefore, the primary efficacy indicator scores of ustekinumab, secukinumab, ixekizumab, guselkumab, and tildrakizumab were 3.65, 5.09, 6, 5.48, and 3.63 points, respectively. The secondary efficacy indicator scores of ustekinumab, secukinumab, ixekizumab, guselkumab, and tildrakizumab were 3.29, 3.89, 4, 3.43, and 3.2 points, respectively. In conclusion, the efficacy scores of ustekinumab, secukinumab, ixekizumab, guselkumab, and tildrakizumab were 14.94, 16.98, 18, 16.91, and 14.83 points, respectively (Table 4, Supplemental Table S2).

Safety score (25%)

Adverse effect

The clinical trial (NCT00267969) pointed out that the incidence of adverse reactions in patients treated with ustekinumab was 54.5%, of which the incidence of serious adverse reactions was 1.2% (Langley et al. 2018; Leonardi et al., 2008). According to the long-term safety study of secukinumab, adverse events, serious adverse events, and adverse events of special interest were analysed using exposure-adjusted incidence rates (EAIRs; patient incidence rate/100 patient-years (PY)), calculated as the number of events divided by total exposure time. Thus, the results for 8819 psoriasis patients receiving treatment showed that the cumulative EAIRs of serious adverse events were only 7% per 100 PY. The drug instructions indicated that the most common adverse events were infections (47.5%), with serious adverse events occurring at a rate of 1.2% (Gottlieb et al., 2022). In a head-to-head comparison of ixekizumab versus guselkumab in patients with moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis, the incidence rates of adverse reactions in the ixekizumab group and guselkumab group were 67% and 57%, respectively. Most of the adverse reactions were mild to moderate, and the incidence of severe adverse reactions was 3% (Blauvelt et al., 2020). The drug instructions for tildrakizumab indicated that the incidence of adverse reactions during the clinical trials of tildrakizumab was 48.2%, of which the incidence of serious adverse reactions was about 1.4% (Blauvelt et al., 2018). From the above, it could be concluded that all interleukin inhibitors scored 1 and 2 points for moderate adverse reactions and severe adverse reactions, respectively.

Special groups

In terms of pediatric medication, the drug instructions pointed out that children who are over 6 years old could use ustekinumab, scoring 1.1 points. The instructions for the other four drugs didn't mention any relevant pediatric studies. Literature reports indicate that secukinumab has been used in the treatment of 3-year-old children, with a corresponding score of 1.4 points (Zhu et al., 2021). Ixekizumab and guselkumab had only been studied in 12-year-old children (Gottlieb et al., 2022; Kim et al., 2019; Orsini et al., 2023). Tildrakizumab has received FDA approval exclusively for use in adult patients, whereas its efficacy and safety in pediatric populations remain to be established (Yi et al., 2024). Ixekizumab, guselkumab, and tildrakizumab all scored 0.5 points.

The difference in effectiveness and safety of the five interleukin inhibitors was not found between the elderly and young participants, and they could be used without dose adjustment. Hence, all scored 1 point. Pregnant women and lactating women were not allowed to use interleukin inhibitors, resulting in all receiving 0 point.

The five interleukin inhibitors were mainly cleared through intracellular catabolism, so the clearance of interleukin inhibitors would not be affected by liver injury, and all scored 3 points in terms of liver function availability.

Although these five interleukin inhibitors have not yet undergone large-scale renal-related safety clinical trials, some cases mentioned the use of ustekinumab, secukinumab, and ixekizumab in patients with severe renal dysfunction, all of them scored 3 points (Liu & Wang, 2022; Llamas-Velasco et al., 2015; Michelerio et al., 2021; Nimmannitya et al., 2016). There were no reports in the literature about guselkumab and tildrakizumab. Their renal clearance rates were estimated to be low, and the kidneys were not significant clearance pathways for interleukin inhibitors. Therefore, each of them scored 1 point.

Drug interactions and other aspects

When using interleukin inhibitors, live vaccines should not be given at the same time, so all interleukin inhibitors received 1 point. This scoring was based not on direct evidence of increased adverse events after live vaccination, but on consistent precautionary recommendations in the prescribing information of all five agents, which advise completing age-appropriate immunizations before initiation and avoiding live vaccines during treatment. These recommendations are also supported by expert consensus (Chat et al., 2024), despite the absence of published clinical studies on live vaccines for some agents. The common adverse effects of the five interleukin inhibitors were reversible and non-teratogenic or carcinogenic, and each scored 1 point. There were no special medication warnings for the five interleukin inhibitors, which scored 1 point. In conclusion, the safety scores of ustekinumab, secukinumab, ixekizumab, guselkumab, and tildrakizumab were 15.1, 15.4, 14.5, 12.5, and 12.5 points, respectively (Table 5, Supplemental Table S3).

Economy score (10%)

Drug with the same generic name

The five interleukin inhibitors to be selected were all original research drugs, and they were not yet available as drugs with the same generic name. Therefore, they all received 3 points.

Substitutable drugs for the main indications

The daily treatment costs of five interleukin inhibitors were calculated through inquiries in the national and provincial centralised procurement catalogs, and then the dosage and usage of the drugs were determined. The average daily treatment costs of ustekinumab, secukinumab, ixekizumab, guselkumab, and tildrakizumab were CNY47.81, CNY84.86, CNY43.5, CNY81.62, and CNY51.9, respectively. According to the formula ‘assessed drug score = lowest average daily cost of treatment/average daily cost of treatment of the assessed drug × 7,’ the scores of ustekinumab, secukinumab, guselkumab, and tildrakizumab were 6.37, 3.59, 3.73, and 5.87 points, respectively. The lowest average daily cost was for ixekizumab, at CNY43.5, which scored 7 points. The remaining four interleukin inhibitors were ranked by average daily treatment costs from lowest to highest as follows: ustekinumab, tildrakizumab, guselkumab, and secukinumab.

In conclusion, the economic scores of ustekinumab, secukinumab, ixekizumab, guselkumab, and tildrakizumab were 9.37, 6.59, 10, 6.73 and 8.87 points, respectively (Table 6, Supplemental Table S4).

Other attributes score (10%)

National health insurance

Ustekinumab, secukinumab, ixekizumab, guselkumab, and tildrakizumab were all listed in Category B of the National Health Insurance Catalog for reimbursement, with payment conditions restrictions, and each received a score of 1.5 points.

National essential drugs

The five interleukin inhibitors were not part of the National Essential Drugs List of drugs, and all scored 1 point.

National centralised procurement drug catalog

The five interleukin inhibitors were not part of the national centralised procurement of drugs, so they were not scored.

Original research/reference/consistency evaluation

The five interleukin inhibitors were original research drugs, and all scored 1 point.

Production enterprise status

According to the world sales rank in 2023, Novartis Pharma Schweiz AG, the manufacturer of secukinumab, was ranked 4th. Eli Lilly and Company, the manufacturer of ixekizumab, was ranked 13th. Sun Pharmaceutical Industries, the manufacturer of tildrakizumab, was ranked 33rd. Pharmaceutical companies related to ustekinumab and guselkumab were not included in the list, so they were not scored. Therefore, the scores of secukinumab, ixekizumab, and tildrakizumab were 1, 0.8, and 0.4 points, respectively.

Global utilisation

Ustekinumab, secukinumab, ixekizumab, guselkumab, and tildrakizumab had been approved for sale in China, the US, Europe, and Japan, each scoring 1 point.

In summary, the other attribute scores of ustekinumab, secukinumab, ixekizumab, guselkumab, and tildrakizumab were 4.5, 5.5, 5.3, 4.5, and 4.9 points, respectively (Table 7).

Discussion

The comprehensive score results showed that among the 5 interleukin inhibitors evaluated, the score of ixekizumab was the highest, mainly because it had both good effectiveness and cost-effectiveness. In clinical efficacy evaluation of effectiveness, the primary and secondary efficacy indicators of ixekizumab were better than the other 4 interleukin inhibitors. In terms of economic evaluation, ixekizumab had better cost-effectiveness due to its lowest average daily treatment cost. By reviewing the drug description and literature, we found that secukinumab had the youngest age of use among the five interleukin inhibitors, and its manufacturer Novartis ranked highest. According to the drug labelling and published literature, secukinumab has the youngest approved pediatric age of use among the five interleukin inhibitors, and its manufacturer Novartis ranks highest. Therefore, it obtained the highest safety assessment score in pediatric cases. In our scoring framework, these attributes are part of the safety and ‘other attributes’ domains, contributing to its relatively higher overall score despite the known risks associated with IL-17 inhibitors. Due to favourable safety and other attribute profiles, secukinumab scored second and was only marginally higher than ustekinumab. To ensure the sustainability of treatment, cost-effectiveness was crucial in drug evaluation. However, the usage of secukinumab in the treatment of psoriasis was expensive because of the relatively high price of secukinumab. So, the economy of secukinumab didn't have an advantage. Besides, other drugs ranked in order: tildrakizumab, guselkumab. The lowest scoring was guselkumab, mainly due to its lack of advantages in safety, economy, and other attributes. Details see Table 8 for information.

In addition, under the current national conditions and medical policies in China, besides cost-effectiveness, the pharmacological properties, health insurance attributes, and procurement scope were important criteria for medical institutions to choose medications. In the clinical, by quickly evaluating the pharmaceutical characteristics of interleukin inhibitors, reference was provided for specific patient populations, enabling the rapid selection of appropriate and precise drugs during the treatment process; for hospital pharmacies, considering the effectiveness, affordability, and clinical usage rates of medications provided a basis for rapid decision-making in the hospital's procurement; for national health insurance systems, offering data references during updates could have been beneficial. This health technology assessment not only contributed to the scientific and standardised evaluation of drugs, but also could quickly select safe, effective, and economical drugs, providing important evidence-based support for medical and health decision-making (M. M. Li et al., 2022). The multi-dimensional scoring of rapid drug health technology assessment still needs to be adjusted in real-time based on updates to guidelines, the National Health Insurance Catalogs, and the National Essential Drug list, to quickly provide reliable evidence support for decision makers.

However, there were some limitations in this study: (1) Timeliness limitations: As evidence from guidelines were updated, clinical studies of drugs continued to grow, the prices and the production enterprise ranking continued to change, they needed to be continuously updated to provide evidence-based support for clinical decision-making, and there were certain timeliness limitations; (2) The evaluation criteria lacked refinement: several outcome indicators analysed displayed significant heterogeneity and inconsistency. Additionally, due to the limited number of paired comparative studies, particularly in the case of novel treatment regimens or a small number of cases, conducting comparative analyses presented challenges and could not provide sufficient data support; (3) Risk assessment was inadequate: due to time constraints, rapid assessment might not have fully evaluated the potential risks of using interleukin inhibitors for psoriasis treatment, such as long-term safety; (4) Cross-study comparison bias: This study did not systematically adjust or match the clinical studies of different interleukin inhibitors for differences in population characteristics, study designs, or treatment durations, which may introduce bias in the efficacy and safety scores. Future research should incorporate more real-world data and head-to-head or matched clinical trial comparisons to better control such heterogeneity and improve the accuracy and applicability of comparative drug assessments; (5) Lack of real-world data and dosage flexibility: This study did not incorporate real-world data or patient-reported outcomes, which may better reflect long-term effectiveness and patient satisfaction. Moreover, although standard dosages were used in our evaluation, recent real-world studies by Gargiulo et al. suggest that a 200 mg dose of tildrakizumab may be more effective in patients with high body weight or severe disease burden (Gargiulo et al., 2024). However, due to limited comparative evidence across agents and dosing regimens, our current scoring remains based on approved standard doses.

Therefore, it was necessary to carefully interpret the results and further deepen the rationality discussion of the proportion of each dimension to make the evaluation results more representative and convincing (Dong et al., 2021).

Conclusion

In conclusion, this assessment can serve as a reference for Chinese medical institutions in choosing interleukin inhibitors for the treatment of psoriasis and optimising the drug catalog. According to the scoring results, ixekizumab, secukinumab, and ustekinumab were recommended as priority options. The other two interleukin inhibitors can be chosen based on the characteristics of the medical institution's medication groups.
