RETRACTED ARTICLE: Analysis of the correlation between umbilical cord blood Human neutrophil lipocalin levels and perinatal infections in newborns delivered by mothers with premature rupture of membranes
Authors: Gulipiyamu Aihaiti, He Jinfeng
Published: 2026

The authors have retracted this article due to statistical concerns, particularly the circularity in predictor-outcome definition, overfitting in ROC analysis, lack of adjustment for multiple comparisons, and undisclosed bias in model development. The authors no longer have confidence in the reported results, including the odds ratios, AUC values, and diagnostic cut-offs. All authors agree with this retraction.
