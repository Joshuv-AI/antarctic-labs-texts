Evaluation system of vocational education construction in China based on linked TOPSIS analysis
Authors: Huiping Li
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractIn response to the task of vocational education reform in the new era, in order to cultivate innovative skilled talents, it is necessary to improve traditional evaluation methods to enhance their efficiency. This study proposes an evaluation model for vocational education construction that combines grey relational analysis and approximate ideal point method (TOPSIS). By selecting indicators and combining subjective analytic hierarchy process and objective entropy weight method for weight allocation, the weights of the indicators are determined using subjective analytic hierarchy process and objective entropy weight method. Through experimental analysis, the results showed that among the 11 analysis indicators, the weight of policy environment indicators was the highest at 0.124, while the weight of employment rate and contract signing rate indicators was the lowest at 0.050. The above results indicate that the TOPSIS comprehensive evaluation system based on grey correlation analysis has better application effects in practical scenarios and is more suitable for the development requirements of vocational education.Keywords: Vocational education construction, Hierarchical analysis, Entropy weighting method, GRA, Approximation of ideal point method, Comprehensive evaluation

Introduction

Vocational education plays a crucial role in the current social development and human resource allocation. It is not only an important component of the social education system, but also one of the key pillars of China's education system. With the rapid development of the economy, vocational education is closely linked to the progress of the social economy and the demand for technical talent cultivation [1]. Since the release of the construction plan for the modern vocational education system (VES), national and local investment in vocational education has flourished, and institutional conditions have greatly improved. However, some regional institutions still face problems such as insufficient innovation and imbalanced investment in software and hardware, including inadequate education management and inadequate evaluation of teaching content [2]. In order to evaluate the construction of vocational education, scholars have studied the relevant indicators of vocational education construction, constructed various evaluation systems, and evaluated them from two aspects: project construction management level and fund utilization standards. However, due to the internal complexity of VES, it is difficult to improve the efficiency of the VET construction evaluation system, and the evaluation accuracy of the VET system construction effect, service capacity, and other aspects cannot achieve the expected results [3]. In view of this, it is necessary to improve the existing evaluation methods and adopt more scientific and reasonable evaluation models and technologies to ensure the effectiveness and operability of evaluation results, promote the comprehensive improvement of vocational education system construction, and promote better integration between vocational education and the needs of national social development. In order to solve the above problems, a VEC evaluation system based on TOPSIS (Technique for Order Preference by Similarity to Ideal Solution) was constructed. The TOPSIS method can determine the optimal choice and find the solution that best meets the target requirements by calculating the distance from the ideal solution and negative ideal solution.

The study mainly analyses the system of VET construction from four points. The first part reviews and discusses the relevant literature on the evaluation methods of VET construction, the second part proposes the method of selecting indicators, assigning weights and constructing a comprehensive evaluation model grounded on the GRA and the TOPSIS method, and the third part examines and analyses the evaluation effect of VET system under the model, and the last part is an overview summary of the whole paper.

Literature review

Promoting the construction of a modern VES and the high-quality development of vocational education is fundamental to consolidating a strong scientific and technological country. To evaluate the construction of modern VES, it is urgent to construct a scientific and efficient evaluation model of VEC. Scholars in this field have carried out various researches on the evaluation of the construction of VES. Bolli et al. investigated the impact of high school education programs on the youth labor market to enhance the evaluation of educational work. The study used panel data to analyze data from 36 countries from 2004 to 2014, using the first difference generalized moment regression method to handle unobserved heterogeneity and causal relationships. Research has found that school based vocational education and dual vocational education have different impacts on the labor market. The impact of school-based vocational education depends on specific outcome indicators and countries, while dual vocational education overall improves the integration of the labor market and the quality of work. The study also found evidence of linear and nonlinear effects, which means that policy makers in education reform should consider the possible nonlinear and heterogeneous effects of vocational education technology. These findings provide reference for policy makers in education reform, and it is necessary to comprehensively consider the nonlinear and heterogeneous effects of vocational education technology [4]. Gubina et al., in order to build the VES more effectively, analyzed the data by evaluating the subjective reasons of the students' choice of further education and entry into the profession, and collected questions outlining the direction of the student's choice of specialisation and motivation for training, using correlation analysis and regression analysis methods. The results confirm the certainty of the reasons for the choice of secondary vocational education and profession on the results of learning and provide directions for the work of teachers and specialists engaged in vocational education [5].

Karminskaya and Islamutdinov, in order to study the impact of vocational education on the development of the economy, the reasons for the construction of the VES and the development of the economy by combining the methods of multiple correlation, regression analysis and abstract logic, analysing the number of students, sectoral affiliation, application preferences, minimum tuition fees, unemployment rate and other indicators on the construction of vocational education [6]. Kokovikhin et al. contacted internal factors such as enterprises, government agencies, students and the education system to evaluate the model of the local dual VEC, and determined the reference points of the main objectives of education through modelling and comparative analysis, and the results showed that the attraction of employment to students corporate employers the fundamental reason for the supervision of the students, and that through this evaluation system, it is possible to simplify the work of career guidance [7]. Dvoryadkina and Efimova studied the problems of developing the regional VES, combining the theory of spatial development, the humanist approach and socio-economics, to build a correlation and regression analysis of the econometric model, to determine the priorities for the development of the region, and the results show that the regional economic labour force in constructing the VES has a greater impact [8]. Lesnikova et al. studied the role of education management information system in the evaluation of VET construction system, and confirm its important role in the construction of VET system by analysing the education management information system in Maryland, Ohio, Pakistan, Afghanistan and Brazil, which provides a reference basis for the evaluation of VET system construction [9].

Grey correlation analysis is a method of quantitative description and comparative analysis of the development trend of things, using grey correlation to measure the degree of correlation between factors, which is widely used in many fields [10]. On the basis of evaluating drilling design schemes, Wang et al. established an improved multi-objective combination optimization evaluation model based on GRA, technical maturity, economic feasibility, environmental coordination, and 13 subordinate secondary indicators, and further used Analytic Hierarchy Process to assign weights to each indicator [11]. Wang used GRA as the basis and combine the cloud model small-sample data credibility evaluation method to generate cloud data and calculate the confidence level, and get the results of the feasibility of small-sample data. It proves the feasibility and effectiveness of this method in multiple small-sample data sets [12]. Shan et al., in the study of multi-objective lightweight optimization of vehicle seat skeleton, introduced GRA to evaluate and optimise the seat material thickness scheme, and combined with the optimization coefficient of variation to determine the index weights. The experiment examined that the total cost of the optimised seat material was reduced by 17 % and the total mass was reduced by more than 12 % [13].

Approximate ideal solution ranking method, also known as approximate ideal point method, was first proposed by two scholars, Hwang C and Yoon K, in 1981, which is a method of ranking according to the degree of proximity of limited evaluation indexes to the idealisation, and occupies an important position in multi-objective decision-making analysis [14]. Liu constructed a new physical education teaching quality evaluation system based on the traditional and intuitionistic fuzzy sets based on the intuitive distance [15]. In order to establish a multidimensional higher education evaluation system, Liu selected a number of indicators such as infrastructure, teaching quality, research level and other indicators to construct an evaluation index system, and calculated the comprehensive score according to the TOPSIS and entropy methods, and the results showed that the evaluation system has the applicability to a number of higher education construction [16]. In addition, Susliansyah et al. used TOPSIS method as the basis of teachers' performance evaluation, combined with teachers' teaching quality to evaluate teachers' teaching performance, and the outcomes validate that the evaluation results of this method are more in line with the ideal standards and have higher evaluation efficiency [17].

Pang et al. found a close correlation between regional education and regional economy, and analyzed the impact of the relationship between regional economy and education development on regional economy. Firstly, conduct research on the education level of the region, extract main impact indicators based on economic characteristics and teaching relationships, and construct an evaluation system based on the main indicators. The constructed evaluation system is applied to specific scenarios, and the results show that the proposed evaluation technology has good application effects. At the same time, the regional economy and regional education are positively correlated, and the economy can ensure the development of regional education [18]. Chen et al. conducted research on existing education and found that educational safety issues are frequent, and effective evaluation methods are crucial for educational development. They analyzed educational accidents in recent years and used grey system theory to extract important characteristic factors. At the same time, they sorted out the correlation degree of factors affecting laboratory safety behavior and constructed a safety evaluation system. Applying the proposed technology to specific scenarios can refine the educational safety management process and improve the effectiveness of school safety management [19].

Based on the above relevant research, it can be found that the selection of indicators in the VEC evaluation system has a decisive impact on the evaluation results. Previous scholars have evaluated the construction of VES from aspects such as talent prediction, education management, and teacher training, but there are problems with incomplete evaluation indicators and low accuracy of evaluation results. Meanwhile, grey correlation analysis technology has good application advantages in educational and other scenarios. Therefore, this study selects indicators from multiple aspects such as educational environment, teachers, and students, assigns weights to indicators through Analytic Hierarchy Process and Entropy Weight Method, constructs a comprehensive evaluation system for VEC by combining GRA and TOPSIS, uses professional methods to obtain quality information of the VEC system, and further improves the problems existing in the VEC system. The research content will effectively improve the problems faced by the current VEC evaluation system and promote the healthy and scientific development of education.

In summary, it can be seen that different scholars have conducted in-depth research on the evaluation system of vocational education construction (VEC) from different perspectives. These studies mainly focus on the evaluation of the quality of higher vocational education, the integration of labor market and vocational education, the role of education management information systems, and the exploration of the impact of vocational education on regional economic development. Scholars have chosen various indicators such as teaching quality, student employment rate, regional economic development, etc. to construct evaluation systems, and determined the weights of these indicators through relevant methods such as Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) and Entropy Weight Method. In addition, some scholars have focused on the impact of specific indicators on overall educational outcomes and analyzed how these indicators promote the quality and efficiency of vocational education system construction. For example, the application of methods such as Grey Relationship Analysis (GRA) and Technique for Order of Preferences by Similarity to Ideal Solution (TOPSIS) in educational evaluation. Grey relational analysis is used to quantitatively describe and compare the development trends of things, while the ideal point rule ranks objects by evaluating their proximity to idealized goals. When these methods are used in the field of educational evaluation, they demonstrate good application advantages and can provide powerful analytical tools to quantitatively evaluate multidimensional issues such as educational security, regional education, and economic development. Through these methods, researchers can professionally obtain quality information of the VEC system and identify problems that exist within the system.

Compared with previous studies, the innovation of this study lies in not only considering multiple influencing factors of vocational education construction, but also elevating the analysis method to a new level. This study constructs a comprehensive evaluation system by combining grey relational analysis and TOPSIS method, and uses Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) and Entropy Weight Method to assign weights to indicators. This method can comprehensively and accurately evaluate the quality of vocational education construction, and point out the problems that exist in the system.

Construction of VEC evaluation system built in linked TOPSIS

In the field of vocational education construction evaluation in China, traditional evaluation methods have limitations such as excessive reliance on a large amount of data, low evaluation efficiency, and difficulty in meeting the new requirements of educational development. Therefore, under the constraints of the internal complexity of the vocational education system, the existing evaluation methods for engineering construction management level and fund utilization standards cannot accurately evaluate the construction level and service capacity of the vocational education system. So, how to build a comprehensive and efficient evaluation system to evaluate the status of vocational education construction has become the main problem for the future development of this field, and it is also the main problem to be solved in this study.

Based on the above issues, the goal of the study is to design a vocational education construction evaluation system based on the joint TOPSIS method, which can comprehensively consider the inherent complexity and diverse evaluation factors. On this basis, a structured evaluation framework is formed to evaluate the quality and effectiveness of vocational education construction.

The study mainly adopts four methods, the first of which is Analytic Hierarchy Process (HAM), which combines qualitative and quantitative evaluation to determine the relative importance of different indicators and allocate their weights. The second method is the Information Entropy Weighting Method (EWM), which is used to characterize the distribution of indicators and their contribution to the evaluation results, and to abstract indicator weights from the characteristics of data distribution. The third method is Grey Relational Analysis (GRA), which is used to handle data uncertainty and fuzziness, and can improve the accuracy of evaluation. The fourth method is TOPSIS, which can determine the best choice for evaluation and find the most suitable solution by calculating the distance from the ideal solution and negative ideal solution.

To solve the research problem, the above methods are comprehensively analyzed. The application principle is as follows: Firstly, the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) is combined with the Information Entropy Weight Method. HAM can provide expert knowledge and judgment, while EWM can quantify the distribution characteristics of each indicator to assign weights. This combination reconciles the differences between subjective judgment and objective data analysis, resulting in a more balanced and comprehensive weight system. Afterwards, the grey correlation analysis method is used to evaluate the correlation between sequences by measuring their similarity. The use of GRA in evaluation can effectively handle uncertain and fuzzy data, extract key information, improve evaluation accuracy, and thus make the evaluation system more comprehensive and scientific. Finally, the TOPSIS method is used to determine the superiority or inferiority of each option by comparing their distances from ideal and negative ideal solutions. This method can effectively find the most suitable solution, making the vocational education construction evaluation system have clear discrimination and selection ability, improving the accuracy and application effect of evaluation. Based on the above methods, this study utilizes the TOPSIS framework and incorporates HAM and EWM to provide weights. Finally, with the help of GRA, the realism and accuracy of the results are improved, and a comprehensive model for evaluating the quality of vocational education system construction is constructed. By setting up experiments and collecting data for verification, the model not only improves evaluation efficiency but also meets the new requirements of vocational education development, providing reasonable guidance and reference for the development direction of China's vocational education construction in the new era.

This study aims to construct a VEC (Vocational Education and Training) evaluation system based on the joint TOPSIS method, which combines grey relational analysis with TOPSIS method to comprehensively evaluate the quality status of VET. Firstly, based on the theoretical and practical foundations of modern VET, a series of evaluation indicators were selected, including five primary indicators and twenty-three secondary indicators. Through literature review and empirical research, we aim to understand these indicators, and then use Analytic Hierarchy Process (HAM) and Information Entropy Weighting Method (EWM) to determine their weights. This can reduce the potential subjectivity or dependency drawbacks of individual methods. Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) solves the problem by constructing a judgment matrix and using Saaty's 9-level proportional method, while conducting consistency checks while maintaining logical relationships and importance levels. The information entropy rule determines weights based on the data distribution of indicators, with the aim of reflecting the importance of indicators. In order to integrate these two weight allocation methods, the study adopts a linear weighting method and assumes a preference coefficient of 0.5. After obtaining the weights of the indicators, the study continued to use an evaluation scheme that combines grey correlation analysis and TOPSIS to construct a comprehensive evaluation system. Grey correlation analysis measures the similarity of various indicators by determining the degree of correlation between them and the ideal reference sequence (parent sequence); The TOPSIS method determines the distance between each indicator and the ideal positive and negative solutions, and then evaluates the relative advantages and disadvantages of the scheme. Finally, by calculating the grey correlation coefficient and standardized weighted index values, a comprehensive evaluation is formed by combining the two, and the quality of VET construction is ranked. Through this comprehensive evaluation method, the complexity of modern VEC can be more comprehensively considered, providing an in-depth evaluation and guiding the formulation and optimization of VET construction strategies.

Analysis of the results of the associated TOPSIS VET construction evaluation system

In the principle of analyzing the results, this study ensured the representativeness and reliability of the samples when analyzing the data. During the scoring process, the reliability of the score is also ensured. By conducting weight calculation and comprehensive analysis through joint TOPSIS evaluation, the credibility of the evaluation results can be further improved. In the evaluation of the selected vocational colleges, this study not only emphasized the scientific and accurate nature of the evaluation model, but also ensured the reliability and ease of implementation of the methods used.

Based on the above principles, this study established a vocational education construction evaluation system based on the joint TOPSIS, and conducted empirical evaluation analysis in six vocational colleges. The collected 15265 pieces of education management data include teaching management, teaching evaluation, and student personal information, which reflect the current situation of school education management. The main indicator data was determined using data analysis methods, and 200 evaluators from institutional managers, teachers, students, and employers were invited to rate the selected indicators, thus scientifically determining the importance of the indicators. In the first level indicators, the teaching environment scored the highest (90.6), while the teaching staff scored the lowest (75.8). In the secondary indicators, the policy environment scored the highest (93.8), while the employment rate and contract signing rate scored the lowest (71.6). Through weight calculation and comprehensive analysis of the score results of 23 selected indicators, the indicators that affect the quality evaluation system of vocational education construction were obtained. In order to avoid the failure of the consistency test of the judgment matrix due to the excessive number of indicators, this study also implemented the principle of reducing the number of indicators. By running the algorithm using MATLAB software, the evaluation rate and proximity values of the VEC comprehensive evaluation system for six vocational colleges were obtained. The 11 new comprehensive evaluation system indicators obtained after screening are policy environment, moral education talent cultivation, number of teachers, education level, curriculum design, school enterprise cooperation, student moral and technical education, skills competition, professional matching rate, salary level, employment rate, and contract signing rate. The judgment matrix and importance calculation results obtained through HAM show that the policy environment indicator has the highest weight (0.124), while the employment rate and contract signing rate indicators have the lowest weight (0.050).

The results of this research have received dual support from academic theories and methodologies in the corresponding field, Lu Lun. From a theoretical framework perspective, the research is mainly supported by human capital theory and Maslow's hierarchy of needs theory. The human capital theory emphasizes the role of education in enhancing individual abilities and economic development, while the comprehensive human development theory supports the view that education should promote comprehensive individual development. Maslow's hierarchy of needs theory and learning organization theory emphasize the satisfaction of individual needs and the importance of teacher development. This provides solid theoretical support for selecting educational environment, teacher abilities, and student skills as evaluation indicators in research. In addition, at the methodological level, the comprehensive application of Analytic Hierarchy Process (HAM) and Information Entropy Weighting Method (EWM) provides an innovative solution for the weight allocation of evaluation indicators. This solution combines the advantages of qualitative judgment and quantitative analysis, thereby overcoming the biases and limitations that may arise from relying on a single evaluation method. Grey Relational Analysis (GRA) provides a tool for processing fuzzy and uncertain data, and its successful historical applications have proven that this method can effectively improve evaluation accuracy. Finally, the introduction of TOPSIS provides a way for evaluation to find the best educational solution, and its widespread application in decision analysis confirms its applicability and effectiveness in educational evaluation. Combining these theories and methods, the analytical model constructed in this study not only provides theoretical and empirical support for the evaluation of vocational education construction, but also provides scientific guidance and rational suggestions for related practices.

Discussion

In order to understand the national vocational education construction situation and plan the next development direction of vocational education, it is necessary to establish a scientific and reasonable comprehensive evaluation system for vocational education construction. In order to construct this comprehensive evaluation system, this study selected 6 vocational colleges in a certain region as the research objects and collected 15265 pieces of educational management data from these schools, including teaching management, teaching evaluation, and student personal information. These data can accurately reflect the current situation of school educational management.

Through data analysis, this study identified the main indicator data and invited a total of 200 people from institutional managers, teachers, students, and employers to rate these indicators to scientifically determine their importance. Based on the scoring results, this study selected the first 11 indicators as the preliminary indicators for the new comprehensive evaluation system and conducted consistency testing. The research results show that the teaching environment has the highest score and the teaching staff has the lowest score in the first level indicators. Among the secondary indicators, the policy environment scored the highest, while the employment rate and contract signing rate scored the lowest. The results showed that the main and secondary indicators passed the consistency test. In order to verify the application effect of the comprehensive evaluation model, the average academic level scores and employment rates of students were collected in the experiment, and the correlation was verified. The results show that the level of students' learning ability is on the rise, in line with the VEC plan released by the country. The average academic score of students has increased from 71.2 points in 2015 to 92.5 points in 2022, an increase of 21 percentage points. The employment rate showed an upward trend from 2015 to 2019, but decreased due to the impact of the epidemic in 2019.

Comparing this study with other studies in the relevant research field, it can be found that this study has certain advantages. The Jiang H team explored project-based classroom teaching in higher vocational education in China and proposed the CIPP (Context, Input, Process, Product) evaluation model to evaluate the comprehensive teaching quality, including implementation background, conditions, process, and results. The team used Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) for weight allocation, and finally discussed the application effect of the model using the teaching quality of cost accounting and practical projects in higher vocational colleges as an example [28]. This study proposes a comprehensive evaluation model based on grey relational analysis and TOPSIS method, and the designed model also uses Analytic Hierarchy Process and Entropy Weight Method to determine indicator weights. The difference is that the Jiang H team focuses more on basic courses, while this study conducts a more extensive evaluation of vocational education construction. From the results, the data of this study shows that policy environment indicators have the highest weight (0.124), indicating that the impact of policy environment on vocational education construction is more significant. In addition, from 2013 to 2022, the learning ability of students has shown an upward trend, which is reflected in the improvement of academic performance and employment rate.

The Yan L team has created a smart cloud platform based on big data, aiming to solve the problems of mathematics teaching in higher vocational education. The research results indicate that using smart cloud platforms for mathematics teaching can significantly enhance students' learning enthusiasm and improve the level of mathematics teaching in schools. The results show that the improvement rate of student learning status can reach 99.89 % [29]. In contrast, this study did not focus on improving teaching in a single discipline, but instead considered the overall quality of vocational education construction by constructing a VET comprehensive evaluation model, using a broader indicator system. In this study, the weight allocation of factors such as policy environment, academic performance improvement, and employment rate was more scientific, and the research results also proved the effectiveness of the established comprehensive evaluation system. At the same time, this study is more extensive. The Pan W team conducted a study on vocational education and training for construction workers in Guangdong Province, China. The study explored the political and economic environment, stakeholders, and quality assurance mechanisms of training in the construction industry in Guangdong through document analysis, on-site observation, meetings, and semi-structured interviews. The research results found that the training of construction workers in Guangdong is deeply influenced by local history and culture, and under the strong leadership of the country, its training system is continuously evolving to respond to the constantly changing market [30]. Compared to this, this study proposes a more comprehensive evaluation model that is not limited to training in the construction industry or specific regions. This study comprehensively used HAM and EWM methods to assign weights to evaluation indicators, and combined GRA and TOPSIS methods to establish an evaluation model, highlighting comprehensiveness and scientificity. From the research results, it can be seen that the learning ability and employment rate of vocational education students have significantly improved during the observation period, which confirms the practicality of the comprehensive evaluation model and the key role played by the relevant policy environment.

Overall, this study provides a comprehensive evaluation method for vocational education construction. The results also indicate that by creating a comprehensive, scientific, and quantitative evaluation system, this study not only provides an effective evaluation tool for regional vocational education reform, but also plays a key role in improving student abilities.

In summary, the VEC evaluation system proposed in this study has good application effects in vocational education construction. Through a comprehensive evaluation model, the educational management level of schools can be accurately evaluated and suggestions for improvement can be provided. This is of great significance for improving the teaching quality and students' employability in vocational colleges.

Conclusion

To understand the national VET construction situation and plan the next development direction of vocational education, it is urgent to establish a scientific and reasonable comprehensive evaluation system for VET construction. For this reason, the study focuses on the problem that the traditional evaluation methods are not comprehensive enough, combines the HAM and EWM to assign weights to the indicators, and further combines the GRA and TOPSIS methods to construct a comprehensive evaluation model for the construction of VES. The data above showed that when the amount of indicators exceeds 15, the calculation time of the comprehensive evaluation model is greatly increased, and with the increase of the number of indicators, the closeness value of the comprehensive evaluation results is increased. The 11 s-level indicators are selected as the final evaluation basis of the comprehensive evaluation system after weight sorting. the maximum weight of the 11 indicators is 0.124, corresponding to the indicator of the policy environment, and the minimum weight is 0.050, corresponding to the indicator of the employment rate and the contract signing rate. When carrying out the correlation TOPSIS comprehensive evaluation, the original data matrix and the weight matrix results jointly obtained the relative closeness of the evaluation object is 0.852, which is closer to 1, indicating that the evaluation effect of the TOPSIS comprehensive evaluation system based on grey correlation analysis is good. The comprehensive evaluation method proposed by the study is between the comprehensive scores of grey correlation analysis and TOPSIS method, with the highest score corresponding to indicator F1, 0.91, and the lowest score corresponding to indicator F2, 0.83, which combines the advantages of grey correlation analysis and approximation of ideal point method. From 2013 to 2022, the learning ability level of students in the six vocational colleges showed an upward trend. in 2015, the average score of students' academic level was 71.2, and by 2022, the average score of students' academic level grew to 92.5, which was a 21 percentage point increase. In the student employment rate, the employment rate from 2015 to 2019 shows an upward trend, which is in line with the actual situation and proves the effectiveness of the comprehensive evaluation system of VEC proposed by the study. Although a comprehensive evaluation index system has been developed, there is a lack of deeper analysis for personalized educational content and skill training in certain specific regions. Therefore, future research should consider including a wider range of samples, and then conduct personalized application research based on existing models.
