Correction: Work participation and disability pension after work-related PTSD: trajectories and impact of occupation
Published: 2026

Persson ML, Willert MV, Kyndi M, et al Work participation and disability pension after work-related PTSD: trajectories and impact of occupation. Occupational and Environmental Medicine 2025;82:589-596.

This article was previously published with an error.

The percentage of patients with a low Work Participation Score (WPS) at the 3-year follow-up was incorrectly reported as 58% in the abstract and main text. The correct percentage is 68%. The error does not change anything in the analysis, tables or estimates in results or in the conclusions of the article.

The online version of the paper has been updated to contain the corrected sentences below.

Abstract (p 589)

Among 1187 patients, 68% had low WPS and 43% entered DP after 3 years.

Results (p 591)

Among the population, 68% had low WPS (≤75%) at the end of the follow-up and 43% received DP during the follow-up period.

Discussion (p 593)

In a cohort of PTSD patients assessed at Danish Departments of Occupational Medicine, 68% had low WP (WPS) and 43% received DP at the end of the 3-year follow-up.
