RETRACTED ARTICLE: Longitudinal changes in national incidence of Parkinson’s disease and dementia in Korea: insights from the national health insurance database, 2003–2023
Authors: Shiva Raj Acharya, So Yeon Kim, Min Ji Koo, Seoung Yeon Heo, Iseul Yu, Jaiyong Kim, Kyoung Sook Jeong
Published: 2026

The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article.

Following acceptance of the article, the authors identified that “other disease history within the last five years” had been inadvertently omitted from the exclusion criteria in Table 4. When this criterion was reconsidered and applied, it affected the overall results and conclusions of the study.

All authors agree to this retraction.
