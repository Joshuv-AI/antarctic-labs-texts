Investigation of physical education classroom teaching using AHP with IV-CIFS-based aggregation operators
Authors: Wei Zhang
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
Assessing physical education (PE) classroom teaching enhancement through modern technologies remains a difficult task in the present era. The evaluation system contains four fundamental dimensions: student engagement, skill development effectiveness, cognitive impact, and feedback and assessment capability. Multi-attribute decision-making (MADM) is one of the most trending systems for ranking alternatives based on their attributes. The interval-valued circular intuitionistic fuzzy set is an advanced approach for assessing MADM problems, rather than the existing simple circular intuitionistic fuzzy set. The ordinary circular intuitionistic fuzzy set lacks a concept of intervals in membership degree (MD), non-membership degree (NMD), and circular degree (CD), resulting in a significant amount of information being lost. Dombi operations are a valuable approach to improving the precision of aggregated results. The interval-valued circular intuitionistic fuzzy set-based analytic hierarchy process (AHP) provides a structured and objective framework for evaluating various innovative teaching approaches, which can be complex. The evaluation process utilizes interval-valued circular intuitionistic fuzzy set-based AHP to assess three innovative PE teaching methods that help educators and policymakers maximize the effectiveness of their instruction. In the past, various approaches were defined within different fuzzy set-based frameworks; however, they lacked a proper method for evaluating the weightage of alternatives. There is a need to define new concepts using interval-valued circular intuitionistic fuzzy set-based information under AHP for the assessment of vague and uncertain MADM problems. By applying the concept of AHP, Dombi operations, and interval-valued circular intuitionistic fuzzy set, this study develops new theories, including interval-valued circular intuitionistic fuzzy Dombi weighted averaging (IV-CIFDWA) and interval-valued circular intuitionistic fuzzy Dombi weighted geometric (IV-CIFDWG) aggregation operators (AOs). The presence of AHP in the proposed approach makes it unique from other existing MADM approaches. We also investigate some desirable axioms of AOs. We offer an MADM algorithm based on a theory developed for precisely investigating fuzzy information. Solve the numerical problem of selecting the best PE platform using the MADM approach. We are ranking the set of considered alternatives like use of wearable fitness technology; game-based learning technology; video Feedback and performance analysis; task-based cooperative Learning. We noticed that use of wearable fitness technology is the best alternative is achieved by using the IV-CIFDWA and IV-CIFDWG operators. We compare our proposed methods with existing methods to verify their authenticity and accuracy. Lastly, we offer a conclusion.Supplementary InformationThe online version contains supplementary material available at 10.1038/s41598-025-18080-0.

Introduction

Fuzzy set theory (FST), established by Zadeh1, enables decision-makers to utilize MD values ranging from 0 to 1 to manage uncertainty in their processes. The concept of circular intuitionistic fuzzy set (CIFS) defined by Atanassov2 includes MD, NMD and CD values from [0,√2] respectively. The systems handle advanced and uncertain data structures due to the presence of CD, which is a valuable tool to aggregate cyclic and periodic information. On the other hand, many ordinary CIFS and AHP-based concepts are unable to handle interval-valued data. The interval-valued circular intuitionistic fuzzy set (IV-CIFS) provides decision makers with the freedom to investigate the values of MD, NMD, and CD over specific intervals, thereby reducing the likelihood of data loss during the aggregation process. In this regard, many mathematicians have provided different approaches, such as Otay et al.3 developed the new concept of IV-CIFS for solving decision-making problems.

From the above discussions, many approaches have been defined by mathematicians for aggregating MADM problems.

The model proposed in this paper yields better results than current models of MADM due to the combination of AHP with the IV-CIFS framework. This combination makes the model more effective in dealing with cyclic and periodic uncertainty, which is frequently ignored in conventional fuzzy-based decision-making algorithms. AHP is critical in the analysis process, providing a systematized and coherent structure for determining attribute weights based on expert judgments. It simplifies complex decision problems into a structured framework and ensures that the relative significance of criteria is precisely measured, leading to more valid and open-minded judgments.

Significance of TNM and TCNM

The data aggregation then depends on the triangular norm (TNM) and triangular co-norm (TCNM) as fundamental components to ensure flexibility and realism in handling uncertainties. Some mathematicians propose several TNMs and TCNMs for various fuzzy frameworks. Menger18 introduced the idea of probabilistic metric spaces. Deschrijver et al.19 introduce TCNM and Lukasiewicz TNM. Frank20 proposed the TNM and TCNM systems based on Frank’s operations. Hussain and Ullah21 evaluated Sugeno Weber’s current approach to TCNM and TNM operations within fuzzy surroundings.

Dombi22 We introduce the concepts of Dombi TNM (DTNM) and Dombi TCNM (DTCNM). DTNM and DTCNM are standard templates for TNM and TCNM; the idea is popular due to the accuracy of the results. Many mathematicians have studied Dombi AOs for various unclear frameworks, such as those involving PyFS information. Seikh and Mandal23 used Dombi AOs, and Jana et al.24 developed Dombi AOs based on AOs in a t-rung orthopair FS (t-ROFS) framework. Zhang et al.25 analyzed Heronian mean operators using the picture fuzzy set framework and introduced the Dombi TNM and TCNM operations.

The choice of the Dombi operator for aggregation was due to its higher flexibility and adaptability compared to other classical operations, such as Hamacher, Frank, and Archimedean-based triangular norms and conorm. It can transition seamlessly between the conjunctive and disjunctive behavior of parameters among parameterized forms, as long as the position of the IV-CIFVs suffers significantly when working under an inconsistent world. The Dombi AOs are more responsive to changes in the input than the operators of fixed-behavior forms, leading to more discriminating choice decisions throughout the results. This results in more information being extracted from the overall results compared to the use of fixed-behavior operators. Moreover, they may express complex data structures (e.g., circular information and interval-valued data), which will be beneficial in the course of the present study. To clarify the matter, the introduction now discusses a comparison in which Dombi is compared with other existing operators.

Significance of AHP

MADM frameworks are used to evaluate decision options that involve competing criteria, helping users make informed selections. Decision sciences heavily rely on MADM because it offers essential, structured solutions for analyzing complex issues involving the simultaneous evaluation of multiple factors. Decision-makers receive essential capabilities from this framework to perform alternative comparisons and execute criterion-priority decisions, leading to well-informed choices. AHP is the key tool of MADM, providing systematic procedures for evaluating and ranking multiple elements that affect complex choices. The AHP framework supports decision-making by dividing complex decisions into organized sub-problems, for which users determine criterion weighting through direct comparison with one another. AHP includes a consistency test that improves the precision of judgment selection and prevents biases from affecting the evaluation process.

By combining qualitative data, which enables transparent decision-making processes, the AHP demonstrates its effectiveness in handling complex MADM problems across multiple domains. Many mathematicians have employed AHP to solve problems in multiple fuzzy applications. For example, AHP was integrated into IV-CIFS methods, as presented by26. Kahraman27 applied AHP analysis to select wind turbines within the IV-CIFS framework. The analysis requires integrating an AHP model with IFS data for MADM difficulties, as explained by Sun et al.28. AOs represent top-ranking tools for MACM because they transform independent criterion results into unified score values. Decision-makers use these operators to combine multiple conflicting criteria into a single evaluation result that determines alternative performance assessment. Mathematicians have developed numerous AOs across different types of fuzzy environments. For example, Wasim et al.29 suggested using AOs derived from IFS methods to evaluate the technology industry sector. The AHP-based TOPSIS method, utilizing IV-IFS information. The concept of a 4-quasiring fuzzy set is discussed by Seikh and Mandal30. The theory of quasirung orthopair fuzzy set offered by Seikh and Mandal7.

Dombi operations and IV-CIFS

The Dombi TNM and TCNM are essential for aggregating and comparing IV-CIFS, which represent cyclic uncertainty and imprecise accounts of membership. They provide more freedom and control over the manner of modelling intersection TNM and union TCNM tasks, making modelling more sensitive and realistic. This makes them particularly useful in applications that need the subtle treatment of conflicting or periodic fuzzy data. In the past literature, there have been many mathematicians who have worked extensively on Dombi TNM and TCNM-based operations. For example, the concept of MABAC method under Dombi operations and an interval-valued spherical fuzzy set environment, provided by Mandal and Seikh13. Mandal and Seikh36 proposed the theory of confidence level and Dombi operational laws based on AOs for the assessment of decision-making problems.

In the past era, numerous mathematicians have worked extensively to define multiple MADM models under various fuzzy frameworks. Most MADM models do not provide precise information about evaluating the weight vectors of attributes. In our proposed model, we have investigated the weightage of alternatives using AHP under the IV-CIFS-based data. Some advantages are discussed as follows:

The proposed IV-CIFDWA and IV-CIFDWG operators are the best tools for the investigation of fuzzy and uncertain information. The significant feature of the developed theory is AHP for IV-CIFVs. We have investigated the weightage of alternatives using the AHP. The AHP compares the included criteria and alternatives pairwise to calculate the weight assigned to each criterion. In the first stage, the goal is laid out. Step 1: Create AHP decision matrix models to identify MADA problems and their associated PFVs.

Step 4: Find the weight of the alternatives, then calculate the weighted sum of the alternatives, and finally, calculate the total weight of the alternatives. Practical implications of the proposed theory

In the age of technology, MADM is one of the most effective tools for assessing uncertain and fuzzy information with precision and accuracy. In this regard, our proposed theory of IV-CIFDWA and IV-CIFDWG operators is a suitable technique where human opinion is involved. For example, a person wants to select a car for home use; they have different cars in the market as alternatives. Suppose he selected four cars as alternatives and defined a set of attributes, such as fuel average, price, durability, and resale value, for evaluating the cars. The AHP is the best tool for assessing the weight of attributes. Then aggregate the information using the diagnosed theory of IV-CIFDWA and IV-CIFDWG operators. Hence, our proposed theory applies to all real-life seniors who are involved in decision-making.

Conclusion

MADM is a suitable technique for analyzing multiple choices among a list of options. The novel thought of IV-CIFS is one of the well-known concepts in FS theory for assessing uncertain and ambiguous information. The results achieved by DTNM and DTCNM operations are better and more reliable than those delivered by existing TNM and TCNM operations. AHP is a helpful tool for finding the weightage of attributes by combining them. In this article, we have investigated the best PE platform using the MADM approach. We are ranking the set of considered alternatives, which include the use of wearable fitness technology, game-based learning technology, video feedback, performance analysis, and task-based cooperative learning methods. We found that wearable fitness technology offers the best alternative, as demonstrated by the IV-CIFDWA and IV-CIFDWG operators. The developed theory is applicable to all real-life situations involving decision-making, where the decision maker seeks to rank multiple alternatives based on several attributes. The developed theory surpasses other existing MADM models due to its incorporation of AHP. The AHP is a suitable and trustworthy tool for precise investigation of the weightage of attributes.

Limitations of the proposed theory

The proposed theory of IV-CIFDWA and IV-CIFDWG operators is a useful tool for aggregating cyclic and periodic information. However, the proposed theory is only applicable to IV-IFVs-based details. It cannot handle other environments, such as IV-PyFS, interval-valued q-rung orthopair FS, interval-valued picture FS, interval-valued spherical FS, interval-valued t-spherical FS-based information, and hesitant FS-based information. Because its structure is not allowed to handle this type of the information.
