The prevalence of high-risk alcohol drinking among those aged ≥19 years has slightly increased among men from 19.7% to 21.3% during 2021–2022, has not changed much among women (Figure 1). Men in their 40s and women in their 30s had a higher prevalence of high-risk drinking than the other age groups (Figure 2).
