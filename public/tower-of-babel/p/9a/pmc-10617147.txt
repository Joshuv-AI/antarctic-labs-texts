Correction: Chromosome-level genome assemblies of Cutaneotrichosporon spp. (Trichosporonales, Basidiomycota) reveal imbalanced evolution between nucleotide sequences and chromosome synteny
Authors: Yuuki Kobayashi, Ayane Kayamori, Keita Aoki, Yuh Shiwa, Minenosuke Matsutani, Nobuyuki Fujita, Takashi Sugita, Wataru Iwasaki, Naoto Tanaka, Masako Takashima
Published: 2023

Correction: BMC Genomics 24, 609 (2023)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12864-023-09718-2

Following publication of the original article [1], it was reported that part of the figure captions for Figs. 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 were mistakenly inserted into the article body.

Part of the caption of Fig. 1 appeared in the sub-section “Sequencing and assembly results” and was processed as the paragraph directly preceding the beginning with “The self-synteny plot of the Cutaneotrichosporon genome showed no obvious centromeric repeats …”

Part of the caption of Fig. 2 appeared as the final paragraph of the sub-section “Comparison of nuclear genomes.”

Part of the caption of Fig. 3 appeared as the final paragraph of the sub-section “Quantification of differences in genomes using different criteria.”

The captions of Fig. 4 and 5 appeared as the final two paragraphs of the sub-section “Genes and synteny of mitochondrial genomes.”

The correct Figs. 1-5 with their caption﻿s are given in this Correction article and the original article [1] has been updated.
