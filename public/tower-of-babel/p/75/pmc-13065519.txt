Retraction Note: Processing of Counterfactual Conditional Sentences with Differential Propositional Truth-Value in Mandarin Chinese: Evidence from ERP
Authors: Hulin Ren, Xuesong Li, Ying Li, Xianglan Chen
Published: 2026

Retraction Note to: Journal of Psycholinguistic Research (2024) 53:64

10.1007/s10936-024-10107-3

The Editors in Chief have retracted this article. Initial concerns were raised about the submission of this article. In the course of further investigation, the author failed to provide evidence that ethics approval had been received prior to the commencement of this study. The authors did not respond to correspondence from the Editor about this retraction.
