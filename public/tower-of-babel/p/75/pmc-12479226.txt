Announcement of New Pediatric Section Editor
Authors: Seong Ho Park
Published: 2025

It is my great pleasure to introduce Dr. Mi-Jung Lee as the new Pediatric Section Editor for our journal, effective October 2025, succeeding Dr. Young Hun Choi.

Dr. Lee graduated from Yonsei University College of Medicine in Seoul, Korea, in 2002. She completed her residency and fellowship training in the Department of Radiology at Severance Hospital in Seoul. Since 2010, she has been serving as a faculty member in Pediatric Radiology at Severance Hospital. She is currently a tenured Professor in the Department of Radiology at Yonsei University College of Medicine and serves as the Chief of Pediatric Radiology at Severance Children's Hospital.

Dr. Lee is a leading expert in pediatric body imaging. She has authored more than 100 articles in SCI(E)-indexed journals, including over 80 as first or corresponding author. Her research interests focus on the imaging diagnosis of a wide range of pediatric diseases, with particular emphasis on the diagnosis, follow-up, and prognostic evaluation of patients with biliary atresia. In addition, she has conducted studies on quantitative imaging using ultrasound and MRI, as well as research aimed at reducing radiation exposure in CT examinations.

I look forward to her valuable contributions to the continued advancement of our journal.
