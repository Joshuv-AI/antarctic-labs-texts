In Memory of Professor Hee Young Chung, A Pioneer and Visionary of Infectious Diseases in Korea
Authors: The Korean Society of Infectious Diseases
Published: 2026

Professor Hee Young Chung (Fig. 1), Honorary President of the Korean Society of Infectious Diseases (KSID) and a venerable leader who laid the very foundation of the field in Korea, passed away on May 18, 2025, at the age of 101.

As one of the founding members of the KSID in 1961, Professor Chung personally drafted the KSID’s founding manifesto and served as the first editor-in-chief of ‘Journal of Korean Infectious Diseases’ (the predecessor Infection & Chemotherapy) in 1969. From the Society's inception in 1961 until 1987, he dedicated himself to its growth, holding various leadership roles including General Secretary, Vice President, and President. In particular, his tenure as the 9th to 13th President from 1978 to 1987 was a period of profound devotion that solidified the Society's standing and development.

His academic contributions were equally monumental. In 1963, he co-authored ‘Infectious Diseases’, the nation’s first textbook on the subject, alongside the late Professor Jong-Hwi Chun. In 1983, he single-handedly authored ‘Textbook of Guideposts to Antimicrobials’, providing an essential roadmap for clinical practice in Korea. The principles of proper antimicrobial use established through his dedication became the cornerstone upon which Korean infectious disease research reached its current world-class stature. His pioneering achievements paved the way for the development of the Society's official English journal, “Infection & Chemotherapy”, and live on through the continued publication of “Infectious Diseases’ and ‘Textbook of Guideposts to Antimicrobials’.

A true mentor of our time, Professor Chung devoted his life to rigorous research, the nurturing of future generations, and the compassionate practice of medicine. The noble academic legacy and spirit he leaves behind will forever be cherished in the hearts of every member of the KSID. We will silently yet steadfastly follow the path he so bravely blazed. With deepest condolences, we pray for his eternal and peaceful rest.
