Author Correction: Phage therapy with nebulized cocktail BX004-A for chronic Pseudomonas aeruginosa infections in cystic fibrosis: a randomized first-in-human trial
Authors: Iddo Weiner, Maya Kahan-Hanum, Nufar Buchstab, Lior Zelcbuch, Sharon Navok, Irit Sherman, Julian Nicenboim, Tim Axelrod, Dikla Berko-Ashur, Maya Olshina, Ron Mordoch, Jagoda Jablonska, Tamar Gera-Inbar, Ilya Vainberg-Slutskin, Britny Blumenfeld, Hila Sberro-Livnat, Ayelet Moses, Hadas Nevenzel, Inbal Levy-Saar, Jenia Gold, Meital Goldberg, Nina Tchernorudsky, Noa Ben-Yishay, Tal Cohen, Edith Kario, Inesa Levovich, Roman Safonov, Yaron Tzur, Yulia Zarchin, Vered Lev, Yifat Elharar, Einav Safyon-Gartman, Inbar Gahali-Sass, Sailaja Puttagunta, Xilla Ussery, Regis Vilchez, Urania Rappo, Naomi Zak, Myriam Golembo, Ariel Cohen
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-025-60598-4, published online 1 July 2025

In the version of the article initially published, a number of samples were incorrectly included in the analysis that led to Fig. 2. In the Fig. 2b–d legend, synthetic CF medium (SCFM) sample sizes were changed from “SCFM–27” to “SCFM–6.” Coordinates in Fig. 2b–d have been removed and the image replaced. In Supplementary Fig. 1, some coordinates were removed. In Supplementary Data 4, the irrelevant samples were removed, and Source Data for Fig. 2 and Supplementary Fig. 1 have been updated. The figures, Supplementary and Source Data have been amended in the HTML and PDF versions of the article. The changes do not affect the results or conclusions of the study.
