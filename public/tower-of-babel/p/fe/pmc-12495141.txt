Authors: Robert L Taylor
Published: 2025

To the Editor:

I am quite humbled and profoundly grateful to have received the first Poultry Science Association Pathfinder Award at the 2025 PSA Awards Celebration. My acceptance remarks included comments directed at those people just starting their poultry science adventure, whether as students, postdoctoral fellows, or new employees. The list below is expanded beyond the original presentation. These principles derive from no special wisdom, except that which has accumulated over my forty-seven-year career in poultry science. President Martin Zuidhof asked me to share my thoughts via a Poultry Science Letter to the Editor.

1Hard work is not a crime.

Hard work is not a crime.

2Have integrity. “If you have integrity, nothing else matters; if you don’t have integrity, nothing else matters”. U. S. Senator Alan Simpson

3Take pride in what you do as if you have signed all your work.

4Accept personal responsibility. Failure, although frustrating, means you need a bigger boat.

5My great-grandmother used to say, “Those who expect little shall not be disappointed.” High expectations are worth some disappointment.

My great-grandmother used to say, “Those who expect little shall not be disappointed.” High expectations are worth some disappointment.

6Mentors reveal themselves in unexpected ways and places. Regardless of how or where you find them, build on the foundation they provide for your personal and professional development.

7Success, achieved honestly, nurtures no guilt and requires no apology.

8Theodor Geisel, who we know as Dr. Seuss said ”Be who you are and say what you feel because those who matter don’t mind and those who mind, don’t matter”.

9Good things happen when people work together. Develop relationships with individuals having varied expertise. Good people are found everywhere; some places you just have to look harder to find them. PSA is a place to find good people.

12Be optimistic. “In the end, everything will be ok. If everything is not ok, it is not the end”. John Lennon

13Exercise kindness, show empathy, and find joy in what you do.

14Some tasks must be done for the good of the order without added incentives.

17Recognize that evil exists. Define yourself by overcoming that obstacle rather than yielding to it.

18Be part of PSA. The organization needs you and you need the organization.
