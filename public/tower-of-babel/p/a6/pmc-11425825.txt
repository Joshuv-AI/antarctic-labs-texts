Correction to “Carbomer Hydrogel Composed of Cu2O and Hematoporphyrin Monomethyl Ether Promotes the Healing of Infected Wounds”
Authors: Dongyu Wang, Jiale Jin, Chengran Zhang, Chengxin Ruan, Yifang qin, Dongdong Li, Ming Guan, Pengfei Lei
Published: 2024

Error Description:

In Figure 5B MRSA group, the wrong images of CBMG+Cu2O group were incorrectly inserted because the file names are similar in the same folder.

(A) MRSA CFUs on plates after treatment with various hydrogels with laser irradiation or without any treatment using the spread plate method. (B) Representative SEM images of MRSA after suffering various treatments. (C) Quantitative analysis of Escherichia coli and MRSA treated with different hydrogels with or without laser lighting (n = 3). * (P < 0.05), ** (p < 0.01), *** (p < 0.001), and **** (p < 0.0001).

In Figure 7A, the wrong images of CBMG+Cu2O group were incorrectly inserted to CBMG+HMME group because the file names are similar in the same folder.

(A) Representative immunofluorescence images of CD31 and α-SMA with different hydrogels after 7 days (n = 3). (B) Representative immunofluorescence images of CD31 and α-SMA treated with different hydrogels after 14 days (n = 3).

Correction:

Figure 5B: The images of CBMG+Cu2O group should be replaced with the correct picture.

Figure 7A: The images of CBMG+HMME group should be replaced with the correct picture.

These corrections made to the paper do not significantly alter the overarching findings and conclusions, maintaining the integrity and validity of the research. These adjustments do not impact the interpretations drawn from the study. In an effort to uphold precision, we aspire to make corresponding amendments to the images as well. We sincerely apologize for the oversight on our part and deeply regret any inconvenience or disruption this may have caused.
