Correction

The original Figure 1 in “A Case of Cala Trio-Induced Glossopharyngeal Neuralgia in a Patient with Essential Tremor and Idiopathic Parkinson’s Disease” by Arnold and Hiller unintentionally included an illustration of a hand with six digits [1]. The figure should have featured a hand with five digits.
