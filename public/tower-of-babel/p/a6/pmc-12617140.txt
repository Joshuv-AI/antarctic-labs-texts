OA2047. Medical data of road traffic victims, what do they teach us?
Authors: S Nijman
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundEmergency Departments and Ambulance Services register significantly more road traffic victims than police registers. In the province of Utrecht, a regional project involves collaboration between the province, the municipality, Emergency Departments, Ambulance Services, and the Dutch Consumer Safety Institute (VeiligheidNL) to prevent road traffic accidents.MethodsMachine learning algorithms can identify and extract data from road traffic victims in health care systems and transform open text data into coded information on road traffic accidents. We compared these health care data with police data and analysed what health care data can contribute on locations, causes and consequences of road traffic accidents.ResultsIn 2023, we collected data on 7.962 victims, 6.456 victims were registered in the information system of the Ambulance Service, 2.698 victims were treated at an Emergency Department and 1.192 victims were registered in both health care systems. Of the victims treated at the Emergency Department, 58% had serious injuries (MAIS 2+). Data from the Ambulance Service show seven times more road traffic victims than police registers for cyclists, car users and pedestrians. In 49% of the cases the victim was a cyclist (at least 16% riding an e-bike). Cyclist aged 12-17 years and cyclists 55 years or older were most at risk. Data from Ambulance Services also revealed accident locations. Additionally, data from Emergency Departments provided insights into how accidents happened, what kind of injuries victims sustained and on severity of the injuries. Although data cannot be combined on patient-level, as a whole the results from health care data provide a complete overview of actual numbers of traffic accidents and their locations, causes and circumstances.ConclusionsHealth care data contain valuable information on road traffic victims, which can help to prevent such accidents from happening and can be used for the evaluation of prevention measures.Key messages• Emergency Departments and Ambulance Services record significantly more road traffic victims than the police, providing valuable data for accident prevention of road traffic accidents.• Although data cannot be combined on patient-level, as a whole the results from health care data provide a complete overview of actual numbers of traffic accidents and their locations, causes and circumstances.TopicRoad Traffic Victims, Emergency Departments, Ambulance Services.
