Retraction Note: Evaluation of safety margins of chenopodium album seed decoction: 14-day subacute toxicity and microbicidal activity studies
Authors: Shrabanti Kumar, Shampa Biswas, Sukdeb Banerjee, Nirup B Mondal
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: Reprod Biol Endocrinol 9, 102 (2011)

https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1477-7827-9-102

The Editors-in-Chief have retracted this article.

After publication, concerns were raised regarding some of the images presented in this article, which are highly similar to images representing different treatments in a previously published article [1] by a partly overlapping group of authors, specifically:

Figure 2a appears highly similar to Fig. 5A in [1];

Figure 2a appears highly similar to Fig. 5A in [1];

Figure 2b appears highly similar to Fig. 5B in [1].

Figure 2b appears highly similar to Fig. 5B in [1].

The Editors-in-Chief therefore no longer have confidence in the presented data and the results.

Authors have not responded to correspondence regarding this retraction.
