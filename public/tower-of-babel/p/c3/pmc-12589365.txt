Retraction Note: Comparison of GnRH agonist and antagonist protocols in normoresponder patients who had IVF-ICSI
Authors: Mustafa Kara, Turgut Aydin, Turhan Aran, Nurettin Turktekin, Betul Ozdemir
Published: 2025

Retraction Note: Arch Gynecol Obstet (2013) 288:1413–1416 10.1007/s00404-013-2903-z

The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article. Following publication, concerns were raised regarding the data, specifically that several of the p-values reported in tables 1 and 2 appear to be incorrect.

The author has been unable to provide an adequate response to the concerns raised or their raw data. The Editor has therefore lost confidence in the results and conclusions of this article.

Author Mustafa Kara did not respond to correspondence from the Publisher about this retraction. The Publisher was not able to obtain an up to date email address for authors Turgut Aydin, Turhan Aran, Nurettin Turktekin or Betul Ozdemir.
