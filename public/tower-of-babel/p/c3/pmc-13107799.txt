Research highlight: Calling for allied efforts to strengthen digital health literacy in Sweden: perspectives of policy makers
Published: 2026

Digitalization is profoundly transforming how we live, work, and access health-related information. In this context, digital health literacy, which refers to the ability to seek, understand, evaluate, and apply health information from digital sources, has become a key determinant of health. It can significantly enhance individual and population well-being by enabling timely access to digital health services, tools, and information. However, the rapid pace of digital integration in healthcare also raises concerns about equitable access and the ability of individuals to effectively use them.

This research study is part of the Improving Digital Empowerment for Active Healthy Living (IDEAHL) project, which aims to enhance digital health literacy across Europe. The authors focus on understanding how Swedish policymakers perceive the opportunities and challenges associated with developing a European strategy for digital health literacy.

A total of 10 Swedish policymakers, with relevant professional experience in digital health or health literacy, participated in a series of online discussion meetings. These meetings explored the role of policymakers in supporting digital health literacy, particularly for marginalized groups.

The findings highlight several priority areas for advancing digital health literacy strategies. Policymakers emphasize the urgent need for a national strategy in Sweden aligned with broader policies on digitalization, health equity, and sustainability in healthcare. They highlight the importance of addressing ethical and social considerations, such as ensuring accessibility for vulnerable groups like individuals with intellectual disabilities or those at risk of digital exclusion. Participants also stress the need for tailored interventions that consider individual circumstances rather than categorizing people into rigid groups.

The study reinforces the critical role of digital health literacy as a determinant of health in reducing health inequalities and ensuring that the benefits of digital transformation are distributed fairly and sustainably in healthcare. By engaging directly with policymakers, this research offers actionable insights that can support the development of inclusive, resilient, and future-oriented strategies at both national and European levels.
