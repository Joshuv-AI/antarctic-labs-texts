To select the optimal immunohistochemical staining method for pigmented melanoma
Authors: Juan Lin, Zhijie You, Xin Chen
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractMelanin granules may be difficult to distinguish from the brown reaction product of 3,3’-diaminobenzidine (DAB) in immunostaining. To identify a reliable immunostaining protocol for pigmented melanoma that preserves tissue integrity while enabling clear observation of antigen-antibody binding. Ki67 and the human melanoma black 45 (HMB45) immunostaining was performed on 30 pigmented melanoma specimens using three methods: (1) Unbleached control, (2) Bleached with 5% H₂O₂, (3) Alkaline Phosphatase–3-Amino-9-Ethylcarbazole (AP-AEC). Compared with the unbleached control, the coincidence rates of Ki67 and HMB45 expression in bleached with 5% H2O2 group were 90.9% and 86.4%, respectively, and the one in AP-AEC group were 81.8% and 90.9%, respectively. No significant differences (all p > 0.05) in Ki67 expression and HMB45 expression parameters (percentage, staining intensity and H-score) between bleached group vs. unbleached control and AP-AEC group vs. control across all comparative measured parameters. A 25% (4/16) tissue damage rate occurred in heavily pigmented specimens subjected to the bleaching group, and non-specific staining were observed in AP-AEC processed samples. For lightly pigmented melanoma, bleached with 5% H2O2 method optimally balanced tissue preservation and staining reliability. Non-specific immunostaining was observed in the AP-AEC group. For heavily pigmented melanoma, AP-AEC method minimized tissue damage despite minor non-specificity.Keywords: Melanin bleaching, Immunostaining, Pigmented melanoma, AEC, DAB, H2O2Subject terms: Cancer, Oncology

Introduction

Melanoma is a malignant tumor originating from melanocytes. patients diagnosed with stage IV melanoma, the 3-year relative survival rate was 23% during 2010–2012. This rate improved to 35% during 2015–2017, which was benefit from systemic immunotherapy. In stark contrast, the 5-year relative survival rate for melanoma diagnosed at stage I approaches 100%1,2. Early and correct diagnosis is beneficial to the prognosis of patients with melanoma. The accurate diagnosis of melanoma depends on the evaluation of tissue sections of hematoxylin and eosin (HE) staining and immunohistochemical detection. Although the presence of melanin granules in HE sections is very important evidence for histopathological diagnosis, excessive amounts of melanin often mask the details of tumor cells and interfere with the observation. Immunostaining is the most important auxiliary method for the diagnosis of melanoma. Melanin granules in tissue section usually do not appear black, but brownish yellow or tan, which is very similar to the color of 3,3’-diaminobenzidine (DAB), a routine chromogen for visualizing antigen–antibody reactions in immunostaining. This affects the interpretation of immunohistochemical results and may even lead to misjudgment. Although a small subset of melanomas were histopathologically amelanotic in the process of melanoma progression, but approximately 80–98% retain melanin3.

Melanin is an endogenous pigment produced by melanocytes. It is a naturally occurring stable substance that does not disappear or decrease during HE staining and immunostaining. It is insoluble in water and organic solvents, but can be dissolved in strong alkalis and can also be bleached by strong oxidants. Melanin granules negatively impact immunostaining analysis of melanocytic lesions by masking the sites of antigen-antibody interactions. Some previous studies did not take into account the effect of melanin on immunostaining when using DAB chromogen4–7. Currently, melanin bleaching prior to immunostaining is not routinely performed on melanoma tissue sections in most laboratories. The presence of abundant melanin granules poses a significant challenge to the interpretation of immunostaining results, raising concerns regarding their reliability8,9. To circumvent this issue, some previous studies have employed the chromogen 3-amino-9-ethylcarbazole (AEC) instead of DAB, generating red reaction products visually distinct from brown melanin granules10,11. However, a systematic evaluation comparing the reliability of bleaching and using AEC chromogen, specifically in melanoma tissues exhibiting varying degrees of melanin granules, remains lacking. The primary objectives are to determine the optimal IHC method that minimizes melanin interference while preserving tissue morphology and antigenicity for accurate result interpretation.

Results

Melanin bleaching

Among the 60 sections underwent bleaching, it required 1 to 5 h to complete the melanin bleaching process. Among them, 4 sections had completely bleached within 1 h, 12 sections within 2 h, 20 sections within 3 h, 8 sections within 4 h, and 8 sections within 5 h. The last 16 sections were those with heavy melanin, and 4 of them suffered tissue damage during the immunostaining process (all of them occurred at the end of antigen retrieval). The tissue sections with melanin bleaching completed within 4 h did not experience tissue damage.

Immunohistochemical evaluation

Ki67 and HMB45 positive stains were located in the nucleus and cytoplasm respectively. In the microscopic view, the nucleus of the cells in unbleaching group and bleached with 5% H2O2 group were blue, and the positive expression showed brownish yellow or brown color. The melanin granules in unbleaching group were easily confused with the positive results, while in the bleached with 5% H2O2 group, there was no confusion with melanin. The positive localization of antibodies was clear and prominent. In AP-AEC group, the nuclei were blue, while the positive tumor cells were stained red. Heavily pigmented melanoma

Among the 8 cases of heavily pigmented melanoma that significantly interfered with histological interpretation, both melanin bleaching with 5% H2O2 method and the AP-AEC method demonstrated specific advantages and limitations. Application of 5% H2O2 effectively removed melanin granules, resulting in distinct immunostaining localization for both Ki67 and HMB45. However, mild background staining was observed in a subset of sections. Critically, tissue damage occurred in 4 (25%) out of 16 sections subjected to bleaching. Notably, all 4 affected sections were derived from eyeball specimens and underwent bleaching durations exceeding 4 h, correlating with an increased risk of tissue detachment (Fig. 2, C, D).

For AP-AEC Detection, the distinct red coulor facilitated clear differentiation from melanin granules. A confounding artifact was observed in some Ki67-stained sections: tumor cell cytoplasm exhibited red staining while nuclei remained negative, manifesting as circular red dots (Fig. 2, E,F). This method also existed the problem of masking the antigen-antibody binding sites in the severely pigmented areas.

The immunostaining results of these 8 heavily pigmented melanomas were compared for evaluating concordance. For Ki67 expression in AP-AEC group,1 out of 8 cases (12.5%) failed to meet the concordance criteria. For HMB45 expression, 4 out of 8 cases (50.0%) met the criteria for full concordance, while 4 out of 8 cases (50.0%) achieved only basic concordance. Discussion

This study aimed to select the optimal method that will not cause the loss of organizational integrity and will not affect the observation of antigen-antibody binding. Our findings demonstrated that neither bleached with 5% H202 nor AP-AEC method significantly affects the detection of Ki67 or HMB45, as evidenced by consistent results across all quantitative parameters. However, a 25% tissue damage rate occurred exclusively in heavily pigmented specimens subjected to the bleaching group, and non-specific staining were observed in AP-AEC processed samples. The method of bleached with 5%H2O2 is most suitable for immunostaining of melanoma without heavy melanin, while the AP-AEC method performs well in cases of heavily pigmented melanoma.

Melanin is a crystalline pigment that varies in color from brownish-yellow to tan and synthesized and stored in melanosomes. Melanogenesis is initiated by enzyme tyrosinase which hydroxylates L-tyrosine to L-dihydroxyphenylalanine and further to L-Dopaquinone in melanosomes12. Melanosomes are special organelles in melanocytes that are responsible for the synthesis and storage of melanin. Melanosomes are translocated along microtubules from the cell center to the dendrites under the action of driver proteins and dynamin, and then transferred to neighbouring keratinocytes of epidermal. The dendrites of melanocytes and keratinocytes of the epidermis interact with each other through cadherin, and then the melanin that migrates to the dendrites is transferred to the keratinocytes13–15. The drawbacks of excessive intracellular melanin granules in tissue sections are that they lead to the physical masking of histocyte details and antigen-antibody interactions in immunostaining. Particularly, their presence makes it difficult to distinguish melanin granules from the brown product of DAB that acts as an immunohistochemical chromogenic agent. At present, there were disputes over various immunostaining methods for pigmented melanoma. We evaluated the reliability of the staining results by comparing the immunostaining expression results of unbleached melanoma tissues with those of melanin bleached with 5% H2O2 and AP-AEC methods respectively. And the problems in the immunostaining process were analyzed, so as to screen a reliable immunostaining method for pigmented melanoma.

There are many ways to bleaching, the commonly used are potassium permanganate solution and H2O2 solution oxidation, which are two traditional means of removing melanin granules in the laboratory16–19. Bleaching with potassium permanganate solution prior to immunostaining has been mentioned in previous articles, but the disadvantage of potassium permanganate solution bleaching included relatively obvious tissue damage and loss of cytological details9,20,21. Orchard GE demonstrated that some antigens produced enhanced staining, some antigens were abolished in contrast, and some antigens demonstrated considerable nonspecific background staining after melanin bleaching with potassium permanganate solution22. However, H2O2 solutions have been confirmed that it could effectively bleach melanin in pigmented melanocytic lesions without significantly affecting the expression of immunostaining. It needed to be emphasized that the use of H2O2 solution for bleaching can reduce or even avoid the loss of tissue integrity and cellular morphology17,21,23. Joon-Yong Chung suggested that immunostaining for Ki-67 and HMB45 was enhanced by bleaching with 0.5% H2O2 in Tris/Tricine/SDS and Tris-HCl17. But our experimental results have a different opinion from that. Our study showed that immunostaining using the bleached with 5%H2O2 method showed excellent quality comparable to that of unbleached tissues and the immunoreactivity of both Ki67 and HMB45 antigen was preserved. And all tissue sections of non-heavily pigmented melanoma showed good quality of histomorphology. It has been suggested that the optimal condition for melanin bleaching is bleaching with 3% H2O2 in PBS at 55 °C for 2 h, whereas bleaching with 10% H2O2 in PBS at 55–60 °C for 1 h, which results in tissue damage and loss of cytological details18. However, our study disagreed these views and found that melanin bleaching with 5% H2O2 solution at 60 °C for 2 h, many melanin-rich tissues could not completely bleach, and it took more than 1–5 h for different melanin-rich tissues to achieve complete bleaching. There was basically no organizational damage within 4 h of melanin bleaching with 5% H2O2 at 60 °C. However, for tissue contained heavy melanin, the probability of tissue damage reached 25% when the bleaching time reached 4 to 5 h. Therefore, we suggested that 5% H2O2 at 60 °C may be a suitable condition for melanin bleaching of pigmented melanoma without heavy melanin.

DAB is routinely used as a chromogen for immunostaining, as it produces brown, intense, insoluble, stable deposits that remain for a long time. However, the presence of melanin in tissues, which also imparts a brown colour, nearly indistinguishable from the DAB-antigen complex. In our experiment, although there was no significant difference in antibodies expression (Ki67%: p = 0.283, Ki67 intensity: p = 0.742, Ki67 H-score: p = 0.777, HMB45%: p = 0.778, HMB45 intensity: p = 0.303 and HMB45 H-score: p = 0.617, Ki67 H-score: P = 0.860; HMB45 H-score: p = 0.387) between the bleached with 5%H2O2 group and unbleached group, both the staining intensity and the positive rate of Ki67 and HMB45 were slightly decreased in tissue sections after melanin bleacheding with 5% H2O2 at 60℃. The possible reason was that the time for bleaching in 5% H2O2 at 60 °C was too long (1–5 h), accelerating antigen oxidation and causing some antigen loss. The weakened antigen signal led to a decrease in staining intensity and even a reduction in the positive rate. For bleached with 5% H2O2 group in melanoma tissues without heavy melanin, the expression of Ki67 and HMB45 positive tumor cells were clearly located and stable. The antigenicity of immunohistochemical markers and histiocytic morphology were well preserved, and the staining background was relatively clean.

The use of AEC chromogen for immunostaining detection is an alternative approach for dealing with melanin-rich and unbleached tissues as AEC has an obviously red colour. However, due to the presence of excess melanin granules, the problem of physical masking remains and the AEC chromogen can be faded away in the time, making the tissue sections of immunostaining cannot be stored for a long time20. In addition, our experiments also found that the use of AP-AEC detection also has the problem of inaccurate positive rate of immunostaining. For Ki67 staining in AP-AEC group, we found that some tumor cells had their cytoplasm stained red by the AEC, forming round-like red dots, while the nuclei remained negative. In two cases of melanoma riched in heavy melanin, there were a large number of red dots. This caused confusion and inaccuracy in the interpretation of immunostaining. The red staining in the cytoplasm of Ki67 was a result of non-specific immunostaining. As is known to all, alkaline phosphatase is widely present in most tissue cells of the human. We speculated that the possible reason for this non-specific staining was that the partial alkaline phosphatase in the cytoplasm of melanoma cells has not been destroyed in paraffin tissue, and there was non-specific binding with the anti-alkaline phosphatase complex, thus forming red staining in the cytoplasm. Worse still, this pattern may also exist in HMB45 expression and be mixed with genuine HMB45 positivity, leading to misjudgment. We considered that this positive rate was mixed with non-specific staining and was difficult to distinguish from the true positive expression. The detailed difference was manifested as that the mean percentage of HMB45 positive (62.95 ± 24.28%) in AP-AEC group increased relatively compared to the unbleaching group (59.55 ± 28.64%). Although the coincidence rate of HMB45 expression in AP-AEC group was as high as 90.9% compared with unbleaching group, 12/22 (54.55%) cases only met the criteria of basically match. Therefore, for lightly pigmented melanoma tissues, we recommend the bleached with 5%H2O2 method.

Melanin bleached with 5% H2O2 method facilitated the himorphologic observation and analysis of antigen and antibody binding reaction in pigmented melanoma tissues, and was beneficial to more conducive to the interpretation of immunostaining. The expression of Ki67 and HMB45 were clearly located, and the positive intensity and positive rate were relatively ideal. The AP-AEC method is well manifested in immunostaining of heavily pigmented melanoma for reducing tissue damage.

Our research also has some limitations. (1) The sample size of our study was relatively small (n = 22), which may affect the efficacy of the statistical test. It also limited the generalizability of the immunostaining results across the pigmented melanoma tissues. (2) Our findings required different laboratories to further verify the reliability of the immunostaining results. (3) The non-specific immunostaining in the cytoplasm by AP-AEC method required thorough elucidation and further verification.

In conclusion, bleached with 5% H2O2 method is suitable for immunostaining of melanoma without heavy melanin. This approach results in relatively ideal antibody expression. However, for heavily pigmented melanoma, we suggested using the AP-AEC method to minimize tissue damage.

Materials and methods

Data collection

A retrospective cohort of 32 malignant melanoma specimens diagnosed at Fuzhou University Affiliated Provincial Hospital between January 2015 and August 2024 was initially enrolled. Following exclusion of 2 cases lacking melanin granules on hematoxylin-eosin (HE) staining, 30 primary melanoma cases were ultimately included. The cohort comprised 18 males (60.0%) and 12 females (40.0%) aged 35–87 years (median: 62.5 years). Anatomical distributions included: nasal cavity (n = 13, 43.3%), foot skin (n = 8, 26.7%), ocular region (n = 4, 13.3%), oral cavity (n = 3, 10.0%), stomach (n = 1, 3.3%), and sphenoid sinus (n = 1, 3.3%). All specimens were formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded (FFPE) and sectioned at 4 μm thickness, with six consecutive sections per case prepared for immunohistochemical analysis.

All methods and procedures in this study were conducted in accordance with the principles of the Declaration of Helsinki. This study was approved by the Medical Ethics Committee of Fujian University Affiliated Provincial Hospital. Due to the retrospective nature of our study, the need to obtain the informed consent was waived by the ethical committee of Fuzhou University Affiliated Provincial Hospital.

Melanin bleaching procedure

Required solutions: 5% aqueous hydrogen peroxide solution (H2O2) solution: 10 ml 30% H2O2 solution in 50 ml of distilled water.

Melanin bleaching with 5% H2O2 solution.

Deparaffinized sections were placed in 5% H2O2 solution in a Coplin jar and then heated in a water bath at 60 °C for 1 ~ 5 h until the brown colour on the sections completely disappears. After rinse in running water, sections were immersed in PBS. The melanin bleaching method using the current method requires a maximum of 5 h to complete the bleaching.

Immunohistochemical examination and evaluation

Thirty cases of pigmented melanoma were performed by three methods of immunohistochemical examination. A schematic diagram of the experimental study design of the presented study is shown in Fig. 3.

The Ki67 (clone names: MXR002), HMB45 (clone names: HMB45) mouse monoclonal antibodies against human and the immunohistochemistry kit were purchased from Fuzhou MaiXin Biotechnology Development Co, Ltd. The alkaline phosphatase and AEC regent were purchased from Roche.

For Ki67 and HMB45 assessment, the percentage of positive cells was scored as 0-100%, respectively. the intensity of Ki67 and HMB45 for each section was used for quantification, and was categorized as follows: 0 = negative, 1 = weak, 2 = moderate, and 3 = strong. The overall histochemistry score (H-score) was calculated by the percentage of positive cells multiplied by their staining intensity (possible range 0–300). Two qualified pathologists independently assessed the immunoreactivity of Ki67 and HMB45.

Judgment criterias for the consistency of immunostaining

Immunostaining Concordance Criteria: Positive staining results were classified into three concordance levels based on quantitative thresholds: (1) Full concordance: Identical staining intensity and < 10% difference in percentage of positive cell. (2) Basic concordance: Comparable staining intensity (≤ 1-level difference) and < 10% difference in positive cell percentage; or < 20% difference in percentage of positive cell. (3) Inconcordance: Significant intensity variation (> 1-level difference) or > 30% difference in positive cell percentage regardless of intensity consistency. We defined both full concordance and basic concordance as concordance with the standards.

Statistical evaluation

Statistical analyses were performed using SPSS version 27. Means ± standard deviation of the percentages and H-scores of Ki67 and HMB45 were reported, and group t-test or Mann-Whitney U test was used based on normal distribution for continuous variables. Pearson’s chi-square test or Fisher’s exact test was applied for categorical variables. P < 0.05 was considered as significant.
