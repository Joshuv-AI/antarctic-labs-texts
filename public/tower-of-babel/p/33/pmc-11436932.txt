A novel hybrid deep learning IChOA-CNN-LSTM model for modality-enriched and multilingual emotion recognition in social media
Authors: R Geethanjali, A Valarmathi
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractIn the rapidly evolving field of artificial intelligence, the importance of multimodal sentiment analysis has never been more evident, especially amid the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic. Our research addresses the critical need to understand public sentiment across various dimensions of this crisis by integrating data from multiple modalities, such as text, images, audio, and videos sourced from platforms like Twitter. Conventional methods, which primarily focus on text analysis, often fall short in capturing the nuanced intricacies of emotional states, necessitating a more comprehensive approach. To tackle this challenge, our proposed framework introduces a novel hybrid model, IChOA-CNN-LSTM, which leverages Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) for precise image feature extraction, Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks for sequential data analysis, and an Improved Chimp Optimization Algorithm for effective feature fusion. Remarkably, our model achieves an impressive accuracy rate of 97.8%, outperforming existing approaches in the field. Additionally, by integrating the GeoCoV19 dataset, we facilitate a comprehensive analysis that spans linguistic and geographical boundaries, enriching our understanding of global pandemic discourse and providing critical insights for informed decision-making in public health crises. Through this holistic approach and innovative techniques, our research significantly advances multimodal sentiment analysis, offering a robust framework for deciphering the complex interplay of emotions during unprecedented global challenges like the COVID-19 pandemic.Keywords: Multimodal sentiment analysis, Multilingual sentiment analysis, Twitter, COVID-19, Proposed IChOA-CNN-LSTMSubject terms: Health care, Medical research, Risk factors

Comparison of decision-level fusion with feature-level and model-level fusion strategies

In multimodal sentiment analysis, fusion strategies play a critical role in how different data modalities (e.g., text, image, audio, video) are combined to produce a final sentiment prediction. The IChOA-CNN-LSTM model utilizes decision-level fusion, which is compared here to other common strategies such as feature-level and model-level fusion. Each of these strategies comes with its own computational trade-offs and impacts on the accuracy and speed of sentiment analysis.

Feature-level fusion

Process Overview: Feature-level fusion involves extracting features from each modality and then combining them into a single feature vector before feeding it into the sentiment analysis model. This approach typically occurs early in the processing pipeline.

The process of aligning and normalizing features from different modalities adds to the complexity, potentially slowing down the overall analysis.

Multilingual sentiment analysis

The GeoCoV19 dataset represents a significant resource for understanding the global discourse surrounding the COVID-19 pandemic. Comprising hundreds of millions of tweets, it offers a rich source of multilingual content, reflecting diverse perspectives and responses to the ongoing health crisis. What sets GeoCoV19 apart is its incorporation of location information, providing researchers with the opportunity to analyze how discussions about COVID-19 vary across different regions and cultures. This spatial component enables investigations into regional patterns of information dissemination, sentiment analysis, and the impact of local factors on public perceptions and behaviors related to the pandemic. Furthermore, the scale of GeoCoV19 offers unprecedented opportunities for large-scale data analysis and machine learning applications. Researchers can explore temporal trends in COVID-19 discussions, identify emerging topics of concern, and track the evolution of public sentiment over time. Moreover, by integrating location data, analysts can delve into geospatial patterns, examining how discourse and responses to the pandemic differ between urban and rural areas, or across countries with varying public health policies and cultural norms. Overall, GeoCoV19 presents a valuable resource for understanding the multifaceted nature of COVID-19 discourse on social media and its implications for public health communication and policy-making worldwide. Future research directions

For future work in sentiment analysis, it is advisable to explore the creation of a single comprehensive dataset that encompasses a wide range of variations, including diverse emotions, languages, and cultural contexts. This dataset would enable more robust and inclusive sentiment analysis models. Additionally, extending research efforts towards multi-lingual sentiment analysis and real-time sentiment monitoring should be considered, as they address the growing need for sentiment analysis in a global and dynamic context, offering broader practical applications and insights into real-world sentiments across different languages and as they evolve over time.

Conclusion and significance

In conclusion, fusing text, audio, and video data for sentiment analysis is a multidimensional task that demands a thoughtful and systematic approach. We have collected input of COVID-19 data in form of text, audio, video and image. They are then processed individually and features are extracted. Each feature is generated in form of weighted mean. The outcome of each classifier needs to be present in the same format so that fusion process can be carried out. The extracted features are then fused together by using latter fusion method. The average of classes are taken and formed as positive, negative and neutral as whole. The final extracted sentiment scores are analyzed with proposed model. The Improved Chimp Optimization Algorithm with CNN LSTM gives out better results and accuracy when compared to other deep learning models. In addition, the GeoCoV19 dataset, which compiles hundreds of millions of multilingual COVID-19 tweets alongside precise location data, facilitates comprehensive analysis of global pandemic discourse. This invaluable resource enhances our understanding of public sentiment during health emergencies, offering insights that can inform effective communication strategies and policy responses.
