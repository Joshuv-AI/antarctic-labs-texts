Dear Dr. Qiu and Associated Editors,

We would like to submit our narrated case report and surgical video, entitled “Sewing machine and Agarwal four-throw techniques to repair a large traumatic iridodialysis in a pediatric patient” for consideration to be published in The American Journal of Ophthalmology Case Reports Surgical Videos Special Issue. Our case illustrates the management of a traumatic iridodialysis in a pediatric patient. We hope to reach the well-informed audience of The American Journal of Ophthalmology Case Reports to highlight two surgical techniques that surgeons can employ to manage these complex patients.

No conflict of interest, financial or otherwise, exists for any of the authors. This video is not currently under consideration for presentation elsewhere.
