Human Vaccines & Immunotherapeutics values the dedicated work of Reviewers and Editors who contribute to the high quality of published work, a fair review process and rapid turnaround times.

We thank all of our Reviewers for their time, commitment, and hard work.

In the current HV&I Reviewer Highlight we are pleased to recognize Pasquale Stefanizzi and his service to the scientific community.

Dr. Stefanizzi is a physician and an expert in the fields of public health, epidemiology, infectious disease control and prevention, and vaccinology. He earned his degree in Medicine and Surgery from the University of Parma in 2014, completed a residency program in Hygiene and Preventive Medicine at the University of Bari in 2019, and holds the prestigious National Scientific Qualification for Full Professorship in Hygiene and Public Health.

He currently serves as an Assistant Professor at the University of Bari, where he teaches in various degree programs within the School of Medicine and several medical residency programs. He also works as a Senior Medical Officer at the Bari Policlinico General Hospital, where he coordinates infection risk prevention and control activities.

Dr. Stefanizzi has published over 130 research articles in indexed international journals, with a strong focus on seroepidemiology and vaccine safety among high-risk populations. Recognized for his expertise in the field, he holds key pharmacovigilance roles with both regional and national health authorities. Furthermore, he actively coordinates national and international research projects focused on scientific communication, epidemiology, and infectious disease control and prevention.
