Authors: Kathryn A Phillips
Published: 2026

Introduction

Three years ago, we launched Health Affairs Scholar: Emerging and Global Health Policy (“Scholar”) to meet a clear need: a health policy journal that is open access and distinguished by speed, reach, and relevance. Today, that vision is being realized. Scholar has rapidly grown into an impactful and widely read platform for health policy research, distinguished by high-quality but efficient peer review, the ability to pivot quickly to meet the needs of authors and decision-makers, and the opportunity to further disseminate science via Health Affairs’ extensive networks.

We have continued to grow, expand our impact, and meet the needs of our authors and readers. We highlight five defining strengths of the journal:

Rapid growth: Submissions increased by 66% in 2025 and continue to grow at a rapid pace.
