Correction: Impact of acute exercise on performance and physiological stress during simulated firefighter occupational tasks
Authors: Philip J. Agostinelli, Nicholas C. Bordonie, Ann M. Robbins, Parker L. Jones, Lee F. Reagan, C. Brooks Mobley, Matthew W. Miller, William M. Murrah, JoEllen M. Sefton
Published: 2025

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-81015-8, published online 30 November 2024

The original version of this Article contained errors.

Firstly, under the Methods section, the numbers for female and male participants were switched. As a result, where:

“Forty-one individuals were enrolled in the study. Thirty-two (15 males, 17 females) participants completed all study requirements (25.19 ± 4.12 yrs., 173.78 ± 9.84 cm, 75.57 ± 13.22 kg, 23.28 ± 7.43% body fat).”

now reads,

“Forty-one individuals were enrolled in the study. Thirty-two (17 males, 15 females) participants completed all study requirements (25.19 ± 4.12 yrs., 173.78 ± 9.84 cm, 75.57 ± 13.22 kg, 23.28 ± 7.43% body fat).”

In addition, in Table 2, the results of the ‘Load’ values were incorrectly given in pounds instead of kilograms. The correct and incorrect data appear below.

Resistance sessionSquatBenchDeadliftBent rowLoad (kg)191.2 ± 67.0135.2 ± 63.6206.7 ± 73.7109.5 ± 44.7

Correct Table 2:Resistance sessionSquatBenchDeadliftBent rowLoad (kg)86.89 ± 30.4561.46 ± 28.9093.96 ± 33.4849.76 ± 20.32

The original Article has been corrected.
