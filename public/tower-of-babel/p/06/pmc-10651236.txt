GPT-4 in breast cancer combat: a dazzling leap forward or merely a whim?
Authors: Linfang Deng, Yang Zhang, Shaoting Luo, Jinjiang Xu
Published: 2023

Dear Editor,

Breast cancer poses a significant health risk to women worldwide. Breast cancer specialities grapple with managing all aspects of this disease, from early screening to diagnosis, complex treatment decisions, and postoperative care. A notable concern that emerges is the prevalence of psychological problems such as anxiety, depression, and experiential avoidance in patients and their families after surgery. These issues, unfortunately, are often overlooked in many healthcare systems due to a lack of resources and specialized mental health services1. As a result, it becomes essential to devise innovative strategies that ensure the delivery of necessary psychological support in conjunction with biomedical treatment.

One prospective solution comes from OpenAI (Open Artificial Intelligence) with its cutting-edge natural language processing model, ChatGPT. The latest iteration, GPT-4, boasts significant advancements in natural language processing capabilities, broadening its applications across various sectors, including healthcare2. Although GPT-4 has been widely acclaimed in several medical disciplines, including neurology3, pediatrics4, oncology5, and orthopedics6, its potential in delivering postoperative psychosocial support remains largely untapped, especially concerning breast cancer and other critical medical procedures.

GPT-4 can offer a wealth of supportive measures for breast cancer patients and their families post-surgery. Firstly, it can provide psychological support, assisting them in managing postoperative emotional stress. GPT-4, by addressing emotional and psychological challenges associated with postoperative recovery, can aid in better management of the illness’s impact on patients’ lives. Furthermore, GPT-4 offers valuable insights about postoperative recovery and self-care, extending accurate and practical advice to understand and cope with postoperative changes.

Moreover, GPT-4 can be an indispensable tool for healthcare providers, offering real-time, accurate, and up-to-date information based on the latest medical research. This can equip physicians to make more evidence-based decisions. It can also play an instrumental role in the education and training of medical students and junior doctors, enhancing their understanding of breast cancer diagnosis and treatment through case-based learning and simulated patient interactions.

To further explore the potential of GPT-4 in breast cancer treatment, our research team is initiating a comprehensive online study. GPT-4 will be incorporated into every critical phase of the treatment process, encompassing early diagnosis, treatment decision-making, postoperative care, psychological support, prognosis, and rehabilitation (Fig. 1). A panel of breast cancer experts will evaluate GPT-4’s decision-making accuracy and benchmark these results against human doctors in breast cancer treatment.

In conclusion, our study seeks to unveil the practical implications and benefits of employing GPT-4 in breast cancer treatment, focusing on its potential to provide postoperative psychological support. We aim to provide comprehensive and personalized support to breast cancer patients and their families throughout the treatment process. Harnessing GPT-4’s capabilities can significantly enhance the treatment experience and quality of life for breast cancer patients and their families.

How can GPT-4 help in early breast cancer diagnosis?

GPT-4, a robust artificial intelligence model, has demonstrated remarkable potential in various medical fields, one of which is early breast cancer diagnosis. It is utilized across a wide range of application scenarios, ranging from the compilation of patient medical histories and symptom evaluation to aiding physicians in interpreting patient conditions and forming preliminary diagnostic suggestions.

As the most prevalent cancer among women globally, early detection of breast cancer significantly enhances the likelihood of successful treatment. GPT-4, through its deep learning and natural language processing capabilities, can estimate potential breast cancer risk from symptom descriptions, assist physicians in analyzing symptoms and medical histories, and offer preliminary assessment recommendations (Supplement Fig. 1, Supplemental Digital Content 1, http://links.lww.com/JS9/A949). However, its current application reveals certain limitations, particularly in the domain of comprehensive assessment. While GPT-4 excels at analyzing patient history data in a comprehensive manner, it may need to improve in identifying and assigning weight to specific symptoms compared to experienced physicians. Medical professionals often assign more weight to certain symptoms, drawing from their clinical experience when reviewing medical records. Conversely, artificial intelligence (AI), in its judgment, tends to be more encompassing, possibly lacking specific focus. These contrasting methods each bear their unique strengths and weaknesses. On the one hand, AI’s comprehensive approach may help avoid misdiagnosis stemming from overlooked symptoms. On the other hand, its inability to form subjective judgments could potentially compromise diagnostic precision. Additionally, GPT-4’s proficiency in processing and interpreting vast amounts of clinical data helps lessen the cumbersome task of reviewing patient medical histories for physicians. By rapidly extracting crucial information from a patient’s medical history that may affect diagnosis, GPT-4 enhances the precision and efficiency of diagnostic procedures (Supplement Fig. 2, Supplemental Digital Content 1, http://links.lww.com/JS9/A949).

How does GPT-4 assist physicians in the breast cancer treatment decision-making process?

GPT-4 provides significant assistance to physicians in the decision-making process for breast cancer treatment. It performs many essential tasks, from interpreting pathology reports, recommending personalized treatment plans, and even aiding in presurgical evaluations and planning.

A key feature of GPT-4 lies in its ability to interpret pathology reports, a critical element in formulating breast cancer treatment strategies. It evaluates critical details, such as cancer type, stage, estrogen receptor (ER) status, progesterone receptor (PR) status, and human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2) status, as well as the disease progression. This enables physicians to quickly and accurately comprehend the specifics of the disease (Supplement Fig. 3, Supplemental Digital Content 1, http://links.lww.com/JS9/A949), paving the way for a seamless transition to subsequent considerations.

Building on its capabilities, GPT-4 provides insightful contributions to the creation of personalized patient treatment strategies. Although the final decisions ultimately rest on the physician’s expertise and understanding of the patient’s unique situation, GPT-4 provides additional data for a more well-rounded decision-making process. Rather than dictating the treatment plan, it presents potential options and their implications, aiding physicians in evaluating each possibility comprehensively (Supplement Fig. 4, Supplemental Digital Content 1, http://links.lww.com/JS9/A949).

Additionally, GPT-4 plays a crucial role in presurgical evaluations and planning procedures, recommending necessary medical equipment, anticipating potential complications, and devising strategies for preoperative patient preparation.

How can GPT-4 help physicians provide quality postoperative care and follow-up?

GPT-4 stands as a pivotal tool, poised to enhance the efforts of physicians and nurses in postoperative breast cancer care and monitoring. Its adeptness at handling complex medical information aids in devising personalized postoperative care strategies and generating pertinent follow-up recommendations.

As an essential ally to healthcare professionals, GPT-4 plays a transformative role in post-surgical breast cancer care. By synthesizing factors such as the patient’s medical history, surgery type, age, and overall health, it can assist in formulating a unique care regimen. It is designed to analyze and offer insights into nursing practices ranging from pain management to physical therapy, effectively covering the initial critical stage in care planning. Initially, GPT-4 may not fully identify the potential risk factors present in a post-surgical breast cancer patient. However, with further inputs tailored to the patient’s unique case, GPT-4 demonstrates the capability to provide more specific suggestions for care (Supplement Fig. 5, Supplemental Digital Content 1, http://links.lww.com/JS9/A949). In its supplementary role, GPT-4 assists medical practitioners in interpreting complex medical data. It can distill recent research findings and treatment strategies from a vast corpus of medical literature, providing the latest insights on preventing and managing postoperative complications.

Follow-ups represent a crucial component in managing postoperative breast cancer. Accordingly, GPT-4 generates customized follow-up recommendations considering the patient’s surgery type, treatment plan, and recovery trajectory. These guidelines, covering review schedules, pertinent tests, and early warning signs of specific symptoms and complications (Supplement Fig. 6, Supplemental Digital Content 1, http://links.lww.com/JS9/A949), equip physicians and nurses with the tools to monitor recovery, identify potential complications swiftly, and implement necessary interventions.

Moreover, when integrated with specialized medical software, GPT-4 could streamline the postoperative follow-up process for breast cancer patients. It can help generate responses to queries, provide information to assist in creating personalized follow-up plans, and offer guidance on maintaining a healthy lifestyle, all based on its extensive training in language processing. This not only has the potential to support patients in managing their health more effectively but can also function as a supportive tool, helping to mitigate the workload of medical professionals to a certain extent.

How can the GPT-4 assist physicians and nurses in providing effective psychosocial support to breast cancer patients and their spouses after surgery?

GPT-4 is capable of aiding in the identification of potential psychological repercussions of breast cancer and offers relevant guidelines to tackle them effectively. This encompasses addressing experiential avoidance behaviors and adjusting to shifts in relationship dynamics. For example, numerous breast cancer patients may struggle with accepting changes to their bodies after a mastectomy, an ordeal that could precipitate low self-esteem and potentially trigger experiential avoidance behaviors. GPT-4 can provide information on psychological strategies to facilitate self-acceptance and self-esteem building alongside professional psychological guidance (Supplement Fig. 7, Supplemental Digital Content 1, http://links.lww.com/JS9/A949).

Consider the spouses of patients, often faced with the challenge of adapting to the changes in their partner’s body and the responsibility of offering appropriate support. GPT-4 is well-equipped to provide a plethora of resources and strategies, thus enabling them to extend effective psychological support on how to communicate effectively with the patient in a way that helps to maintain the relationship, how to understand and accept the patient’s emotional changes, and how to take care of themselves to prevent caregiver exhaustion.

Although GPT-4 is instrumental in providing valuable information and guidelines when tackling the psychological stress of breast cancer, it is imperative to remember that it should supplement, not replace, professional medical advice and psychological support. It bolsters patients in managing emotions and coping with stress, enhancing their comprehension of the disease and their sense of control. Consequently, this could help in alleviating some of the anxiety and fear through comprehensive education and information dissemination. In essence, GPT-4 can serve as a valuable adjunct, offering detailed and targeted resources and strategies, thus assisting both breast cancer patients and their spouses in navigating the psychological aspects associated with the disease.

How does the GPT-4 help physicians and nurses with rehabilitation?

GPT-4 plays a pivotal role in breast cancer rehabilitation management and prognosis assessment. Leveraging advanced artificial intelligence technologies like deep learning and natural language processing, GPT-4 efficiently mines vast quantities of clinical data to extract critical information. This enables healthcare professionals to design personalized rehabilitation plans and provide accurate prognostic assessments for patients.

One significant application of GPT-4 is assisting healthcare teams in creating personalized, detailed rehabilitation plans. Given a patient’s unique condition, GPT-4 crafts an individualized regimen that spans from dietary advice to exercise programs. An example could be recommending elbow activities on the second postoperative day, guiding patients along a scientific path toward physical recovery. Crucially, GPT-4 can adapt the rehabilitation scheme in real-time according to the patient’s progression, ensuring ongoing comfort and safety throughout the entire rehabilitation process (Supplement Fig. 8, Supplemental Digital Content 1, http://links.lww.com/JS9/A949).

Moreover, GPT-4 presents doctors with a more comprehensive tool for prognosis assessment. It amalgamates diverse factors – including disease stage, tumor biology, and overall patient health – to offer a holistic prediction of patient outcomes. Consequently, healthcare professionals are equipped to make informed, accurate forecasts about the patient’s future health trajectory (Supplement Fig. 9, Supplemental Digital Content 1, http://links.lww.com/JS9/A949).

How to balance the innovative and ethical issues that GPT-4 brings to the field of breast cancer?

The application of the GPT-4 model in breast cancer treatment has opened up numerous groundbreaking possibilities. However, with these advancements come a host of ethical considerations. As we navigate this cutting-edge realm of AI technologies, striking a delicate balance between forward-thinking progress and upholding ethical standards becomes imperative.

The role of GPT-4 is not to replace the decision-making capacities of doctors or nurses but rather to serve as a supportive tool. It aims to provide more detailed and accurate information, enhancing their ability to make informed decisions. Safeguarding patient autonomy and privacy is essential when using this AI model. In practical terms, this requires securing informed consent from patients before employing GPT-4 as a decision-making aid and ensuring that options to decline AI-assisted care are readily available.

Transitioning to the next major concern, transparency is paramount in the design, utilization, and governance of GPT-4. This calls for legislative measures that encourage AI developers to enhance the transparency and interpretability of their algorithms. Additionally, it demands a clear disclosure regarding data sources, which promotes trust among doctors, nurses, and patients. In the context of breast cancer treatment, patient data security and privacy present significant ethical considerations. Healthcare professionals must strictly adhere to data privacy regulations to protect patients from potential misuse of their sensitive medical data.

Outlook

GPT-4, a groundbreaking artificial intelligence technology, provides a tremendous opportunity to revolutionize our understanding and approach to breast cancer treatment. It can elucidate intricate medical concepts, offers emotional support, and aids in self-management, rendering it a potential pillar in the treatment journey of breast cancer patients and their kin. Moreover, this technology significantly influences healthcare providers, prompting a multitude of changes, from improving decision-making processes to enriching medical education.

However, it is essential to remember that even the most sophisticated technology cannot entirely replace the expertise of human doctors and the compassion inherent in patient care. Although AI can act as a powerful adjunct, breast cancer treatment necessitates a physician’s expert judgment and a comprehensive understanding of each patient’s unique needs. Furthermore, it is critical to stay vigilant toward potential moral and ethical issues arising from AI’s integration into healthcare, striving to ensure fairness and transparency in its application.

Nevertheless, there is significant optimism and anticipation for the application of GPT-4 in breast cancer treatment despite these cautionary notes. It is not merely a technological innovation but potentially a significant stride in offering more holistic and personalized support to breast cancer patients and their families. We eagerly await forthcoming studies that hopefully shed more light on the utility and benefits of GPT-4 in breast cancer treatment, thereby aiding us in better comprehending and leveraging this sophisticated technology.

In conclusion, while the precise impact of GPT-4 on breast cancer treatment remains to be fully understood, it represents a remarkable opportunity for continuous learning and growth in the field. As we navigate toward a future defined by the synergy between human ingenuity and AI, we anticipate a significant enhancement in patient outcomes.
