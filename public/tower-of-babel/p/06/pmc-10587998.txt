Correction: Fundamentals of the Development of Connectivity in the Human Fetal Brain in Late Gestation: From 24 Weeks Gestational Age to Term
Published: 2023

This is a correction to: Ivica Kostović, MD, PhD, Milan Radoš, MD, PhD, Mirna Kostović-Srzentić, PhD, Željka Krsnik, PhD, Fundamentals of the Development of Connectivity in the Human Fetal Brain in Late Gestation: From 24 Weeks Gestational Age to Term, Journal of Neuropathology & Experimental Neurology, Volume 80, Issue 5, May 2021, Pages 393–414, https://doi.org/10.1093/jnen/nlab024

The following changes have been made to the originally published paper. In the Abstract section, 4th sentence should read: “In the EPT and VPT babies, thalamocortical axons enter into the cortical plate creating the electrically-active, functional synapses.” instead of: “In the EPT and VPT babies, thalamocortical axons enter into the cortical plate creating the electrical synapses.”

In the DEVELOPMENTAL PHASES AND SPATIOTEMPORAL PARAMETERS OF CONNECTIVITY DEVELOPMENT IN THE LATE FETAL HUMAN BRAIN section, third paragraph, 13th sentence should read: “Thalamocortical axons in EPT babies enter the cortical plate creating functional synapses (35-38).” instead of: “Thalamocortical axons in EPT babies enter the cortical plate creating electrical synapses (35-38).”

These emendations have been carried out in the article.
