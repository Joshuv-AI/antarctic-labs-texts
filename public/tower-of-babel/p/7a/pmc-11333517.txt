Yinxie I Formula attenuates imiquimod-induced psoriasis-like skin inflammation via IL-23/IL-17 axis
Authors: He Yanping, Gao Ting, Zhou Xinzhu, Lei Yaya, Zhao Yuna, Liu Qing, Ma Xueli, Chen Jing
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractPsoriasis is considered a chronic inflammatory skin disorder characterized by keratinocytes hyperproliferation. The IL-23/IL-17 immune pathway has been substantiated in numerous studies to be closely associated with psoriasis progression. Yinxie I Formula is a traditional Chinese medicine made from 9 herbal medicines, which has excellent clinical efficacy in psoriasis. However, to date, the mechanism of action of Yinxie I Formula against psoriasis remains unknown. In this perspective, we discuss the efficacy of Yinxie I Formula in mice with imiquimod (IMQ) induced psoriasis. Yinxie I Formula significantly reduced the area of skin lesions and the inflammatory response in mice with psoriasis. Furthermore, Yinxie I Formula alleviated the expression levels of inflammation-related genes IL-6, IL-17 A, IL-22, IL-23, TNF-α and IL-23, IL-18, IL-6 and IL-1β-related proteins and alleviated the abnormal surge of dendritic cells, macrophages and T cells in the skin and spleen. Meanwhile we found that Yinxie I Formula reduced the release of NO, TNF-α, IL-1β and IL-23 in lipopolysaccharide-induced mouse macrophage RAW264.7 cell line. The results suggest that the therapeutic mechanism of Yinxie I Formula may also be correlated with the STAT signaling pathway. We further analyzed the active ingredient of Yinxie I Formula, Buddleoside, which may be the main substance that exerts the therapeutic effect. In conclusion, we have investigated that Yinxie I Formula attenuates the IMQ-induced inflammatory response in psoriasis by inhibiting the IL-23/IL-17 axis, which lays the foundation for the antipsoriasis mechanism and provides a theoretical basis for the clinical promotion of Yinxie I Formula.Keywords: IL-23, IL-17, Imiquimod, Psoriasis, Yinxie I Formula

Introduction

Psoriasis is one of the most common chronic inflammatory skin diseases characterized by scaly erythematous plaques [1]. The onset of psoriasis is often accompanied by pain and unbearable itching, causing enormous economic stress and mental burden to patients [2]. Psoriasis can be clinically classified into several subtypes including psoriasis vulgaris, erythrodermic psoriasis, arthrodermic psoriasis and pustular psoriasis, among which psoriasis vulgaris is the most frequent [3]. Although the pathogenesis of psoriasis is not well understood, numerous studies have shown that the IL-23/IL-17 axis is involved in the progression of psoriasis [4]. Mechanistically, psoriatic lesions are the result of epidermal keratinocyte overproliferation and differentiation disorders stimulated by immune mediators of the IL-23 and IL-17 pathways [5]. IL-23 and IL-17 intracellular signaling inhibitors, such as TYK2 or RORγt, are effective in reducing the aberrant surge of inflammatory factors, such as IL-23 and IL-17, while attenuating psoriasis-related symptoms [6, 7]. Moreover, some inflammatory molecules and such as IL-6 and TNF and inflammatory signals such as STAT are also involved in the development of psoriasis [8–10].

The various degrees of adverse risks and high economic costs of immunosuppressants limit the widespread clinical application. In contrast, Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) has obvious advantages in the treatment of psoriasis, and it is important to explore psoriasis-related treatment strategies with TCM features and advantages [5, 11, 12]. As a topical medicine, the Yinxie I Formula is a commonly applied TCM in the Department of Dermatology of Ningxia Medical University for the treatment of psoriasis, which has remarkable therapeutic properties for its excellent effect of dispelling dampness and relieving itching, as well as removing spots and exfoliating flaking. However, Since Yinxie I Formula is made of 9 herbs including Sophora flavescens Aiton, Wild chrysanthemum, Common Cnidium Fruit, Angelica dahurica Benth, Rhei Radix et rhizome, Phellodendri Cortex, Fructus Kochiae, Acorus gramineus and Alumen, the molecular mechanism of its treatment of psoriasis has so far been obscure. Available studies have shown that TCM treatment of psoriasis works mainly by regulating important inflammatory factors and signaling pathways in the pathological process, anti-keratinocyte proliferative activity, inhibiting the activity of inflammatory factor release, and promoting keratin-forming cell activation and overproliferation [13–15].

Consequently, the present study elucidated the molecular mechanism of Yinxie I Formula treatment of IMQ-induced psoriasis in mice in the above aspects. Our research has shown that Yinxie I Formula can reduce skin inflammation in a mouse model of psoriasis-like symptoms induced by IMQ. This effect may be attributed to its ability to decrease the levels of dendritic cells, macrophages, and T cells, as well as cytokines such as IL-6, IL-17 A, IL-22, IL-23, and TNF. Furthermore, it is likely associated with the STAT signaling pathway. These findings explain the reasons for the excellent therapeutic effect of Yinxie I Formula and provide a theoretical basis for its further clinical promotion.

Materials and methods

Ethics statement

The experimental animals involved in this study were approved by the Ethics Committee of the General Hospital of Ningxia Medical University and national standards for laboratory animal welfare guidelines (GB/T 35892 − 2018), and the data were obtained in accordance with ethical requirements.

Animals

Male BALB/C mice at 8-week-old weighing about 25 g were prepared for the study. Before the formal experiment, mice were fed in separate cages for 5 days at room temperature of 22–25 °C and humidity of 45-65%. The mice were randomly divided into 6 groups of 6 mice each. The groups included the control, model, Calcipotriene (LEO Pharmaceutical Products Ltd, 70 mg/day), Yinxie I Formula (17 mg/day), Yinxie I Formula (35 mg/day), Yinxie I Formula (70 mg/day). Calcipotriene is approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for the treatment of psoriasis and was used as a positive control in this study. The hair on the back of all mice was scraped to make an exposed area of approximately 2 cm x 3 cm in size. For the model group, 5% IMQ cream (Solarbio Science & Technology Co., Ltd, 62.5 mg/day) was applied daily to the bare backs of mice for 7 consecutive days. Equal amounts of Vaseline (Sangon Biotech (Shanghai) Co., Ltd) were given to the control group. For the other groups, the corresponding reagents were smeared 4 h after IMQ construction of the psoriasis model. For the convenience of description, Yinxie I Formula (17 mg/day), Yinxie I Formula (35 mg/day) and Yinxie I Formula (70 mg/day) were denoted as Yinxie I Formula (L), Yinxie I Formula (M) and Yinxie I Formula (H) respectively.

High Performance Liquid Chromatograph/Mass Spectrometer analysis of Yinxie I Formula for the chemically active ingredient Buddleoside.

The Chinese herbs of Sophora flavescens Aiton, Wild chrysanthemum, Common Cnidium Fruit, Angelica dahurica Benth, Rhei Radix et rhizome, Phellodendri Cortex, Fructus Kochiae, Acorus gramineus and Alumen are decocted twice, the first time for 1 h and the second time for 0.5 h, filtered and concentrated, and the Yinxie I Formula is obtained after passing the analysis of PH and relative density. In order to determine the concentration of Buddleoside, 20.6 µg/mL of Buddleoside control solution, Yinxie I Formula concentrate and Buddleoside negative solution were prepared. The herbs used in this study are already in clinical use and do not require additional approval.

Psoriasis area and severity index (PASI)

The scoring system was based on PASI criteria for appearance of skin in the IMQ-induced mice in terms of erythema, infiltration and desquamation. Scores were separately as 0 (asymptomatic), 1 (mild), 2 (moderate), 3 (severe), and 4 (extremely severe). The back skin thickness was measured by electronic calipers. The cumulative scores were aggregated from the three groups’ points.

Immunohistochemistry

Mouse skin tissue samples were manipulated according to conventional HE staining procedures and the pathological status of the tissue was recorded under panoramic section scanner (3DHISTECH Ltd). IL-23, IL-18, IL-6 and IL-1β protein assays were performed according to the operating instructions.

Western blotting analysis

Extraction of total proteins was performed by RIPA buffer (Labgic Technology Co., Ltd) supplemented with PMSF (Labgic Technology Co., Ltd). After protein quantification with the BCA protein concentration assay kit (Labgic Technology Co., Ltd), western blot was performed to determine protein expression levels between groups. GAPDH was used as an endogenous reference. The primary antibodies were Ab against P-STAT3, STAT3 and GAPDH (Affinity Biosciences). The secondary antibody was a goat anti-mouse antibody with HRP (Multi Sciences (Lianke) Biotech, Co., Ltd).

Real-time quantitative PCR (RT-qPCR)

The total RNA was extracted from skin with Animal Total RNA Isolation Kit (Foregene Co., Ltd). The RNA was reverse transcribed with All-In-One MasterMix (Biological Materials Inc). The correlated gene expression was detected by EvaGreen Express 2×qPCR (Biological Materials Inc) with a fully automated medical PCR analysis system (Shanghai Hongshi Medical Technology Co., Ltd). GAPDH was considered as an endogenous reference, and the results were determined by 2−ΔΔCT. The experiments involved were repeated at least 3 times. Primer sequence details are shown in Table 1.

Staining and flow cytometry

In order to assay the effect of Yinxie I Formula on dendritic cells, macrophages and T cells, cells from mouse skin and spleen were surface stained with PE Anti-Mouse CD11c Antibody (Elabscience Biotechnology Co., Ltd), FITC Anti-Mouse F4/80 Antibody (Elabscience Biotechnology Co., Ltd) and FITC Anti-Mouse CD3 Antibody (Elabscience Biotechnology Co., Ltd). The proportion of dendritic cells, macrophages and T cells in the treated cells were measured by flow cytometry (Beckman Coulter Inc). The gating strategy for flow cytometry was as follows, obtaining the major cell populations first by FSC-A and SSC-A, followed by exclusion of adherent cells by FSC-A and FSC-H, with CD11c-positive populations defined as dendritic cells, F4/80-positive populations as macrophages, and CD3-positive populations as T cells. Spleen was taken to obtain cells as follows: fresh mouse spleen was placed into a culture dish containing PBS, and the spleens were ground with a 3 ml syringe plunger in a gentle circular motion, and the mixture was added to a 70 μm cell filter to obtain a single-cell suspension. Skin was taken to obtain cells as follows: The skin was cut into pieces of about 1 mm with scissors, and 1 mL of enzyme digest (RPMI-1640: 3.5 mg/ml Collagenase II, 0.02 mg/mL DNase I, 0.002 mg/mL hyaluronidase) was added, and the samples were incubated for 1 h at 37 °C in a shaker. The mixture was then centrifuged at 450 g for 10 min, the supernatant was discarded and resuspended in PBS, and finally the resuspension was passed through a 70 µM cell filter to obtain a single cell suspension.

Culture of cells

RAW264.7 cells and Human Keratinocytes Cells (Hacat) were cultured separately in DMEM medium (Wuhan Servicebio Technology Co., Ltd) containing 10% serum (Procell Life Science&Technology Co.,Ltd) and 1% penicillin–streptomycin (Wuhan Servicebio Technology Co., Ltd). Cells were expanded and cultured at 37 °C with 5% CO2 saturated humidity. All cell lines were purchased from Beyotime Biotechnology.

MTT analysis

HACAT cells and RAW264.7 cells were inoculated in 96 wells with various concentrations of Yinxie I Formula (0.25 mg/mL, 0.5 mg/mL, 1 mg/mL, 1.5 mg/mL, 2 mg/ml), respectively. After co-culture for 48 h, MTT (Beyotime Biotechnology) as and Formazan lysate were added and the absorbance value of each well was measured at the wavelength of 570 nm to examine the effect of Yinxie I Formula on the proliferation or virulence of the cells.

ELISA

Blood was collected from anesthetized mice through the orbital venous plexus, centrifuged at 2000 rpm for 10 min, and the supernatant was taken to obtain serum. The secreted cytokines such as IL-1β, IL-6, IL-17 A, IL-22, IL-23 and TNF-α were detected in the samples with the corresponding ELISA kits (Elabscience Biotechnology Co., Ltd) according to the protocol of the manufacturer. For each test object, a blank control well and a standard sample well were set up. The results are analyzed as follows: Calculate the average OD value of the standard and sample replicate wells and subtract the OD value of the blank wells as the calibration value; take the concentration of the standard as the horizontal coordinate and the corresponding OD calibration value as the vertical coordinate, fit a standard curve, and then, according to the standard curve, calculate the concentration of the corresponding samples by substituting the calibrated OD value. Calculate the concentration of the sample to be tested by multiplying it by the corresponding dilution. The experiments involved were repeated at least 3 times.

Nitric oxide (NO) colorimetric assay

The detection of NO secretion was performed according to the Nitric Oxide (NO) Colorimetric Assay Kit (Elabscience Biotechnology Co., Ltd). The concentration of NO can be calculated indirectly by measuring the OD value at 550 nm.

Statistical analysis

All data were expressed as “mean ± SD” with GraphPad Prism v 7.0 (GraphPad Software). The Student’s t-test was employed to compare the means between two groups, while the one-way ANOVA was utilized to compare variances across the means of two or more groups. Statistical significance was determined by assessing the p-value, with values below 0.05 indicating significant differences in group means for the t-test and significant differences in variances for ANOVA.

Results

Yinxie I Formula ameliorates psoriasis-like lesions in mice

Following IMQ-induced psoriasis, mice developed significant erythema, infiltration and scales at 8th day. As a positive control, these symptoms of psoriasis were significantly alleviated in mice treated with Calcipotriene. Yinxie I Formula ameliorated the symptoms of psoriasiform inflammation in mice in a dose-dependent manner, and the higher the dose, the better the treatment effect (Fig. 1A). The skin lesions in model group mice were aggravated on every day after IMQ administration based on daily PASI scores. Notably, the mice in the Yinxie I Formula (H) group had a decreased cumulative PASI score and were most similar to the Calcipotriene group. In contrast, the effect of Yinxie I Formula (M and L) group showed a lower degree of decline than the other groups, but also significantly improved the IMQ-induced skin lesions (Fig. 1B). To further investigate the efficacy of Yinxie I Formula in mouse model of IMQ-induced psoriasis, we performed histopathological analysis by HE staining of skin samples from the dorsal surface of mice. In the model group, the epidermal stratum corneum was more eosinophilic, dense and tightly adherent to the surface, with proliferation of spiny layer cells, significant thickening of the epidermal layer, irregular proliferation and projection of the dermis toward the epidermis, and infiltration of inflammatory cells visible in the dermis. The histological features were similar to the symptoms of psoriasis. In the Yinxie I Formula treatment group, there was significant drop in epidermal thickness at the lesion site, reduction in inflammatory infiltrating cells, and thinner of the spiny cell layer (Fig. 1C and D). Collectively, Yinxie I Formula can alleviate the skin lesions of IMQ-induced psoriasis.

Yinxie I Formula reduces pro-inflammatory cytokines in IMQ-induced psoriasis-like mice

IMQ-induced psoriasis-like lesions facilitate the expression of pro-inflammatory cytokines. Similar to the effect with Calcipotriene, the expression levels of mRNA for IFN-γ, IL-17 A, IL-22 and IL-23 cytokines were significantly decreased by the treatment with Yinxie I Formula (Fig. 2A). Likewise, the ELISA results showed that Yinxie I Formula reduced the levels of pro-inflammatory cytokines IL-6, IL-17 A, IL-22, IL-23 and TNF-α in the serum (Fig. 2B). In accordance with the trend of these results, immunohistochemical results also showed that Yinxie I Formula also decreased the expression levels of IL-23, IL-6, IL-1β and IL-18 (Fig. 2C). Although less effective than Calcipotriene, Yinxie I Formula dose-dependently reduced the expression levels of pro-inflammatory factors.

Yinxie I formula decreases dendritic cells, macrophages and T cells of mice with psoriasis

Previous studies have shown that psoriasis enhances the secretion of associated pro-inflammatory cytokines, which are produced by dendritic cells, macrophages and T cells [16, 17]. We herein evaluated the effects of Yinxie I Formula on dendritic cells, macrophages and T cells in the lesional skin and spleen of mice with psoriasis. Yinxie I Formula dose-dependently alleviated the abnormal surge in the number of these cells induced by IMQ, both in the lesioned skin and in the spleen (Fig. 3A and B). As well, the Yinxie I Formula (H) group had almost the same effect as the Calcipotriene group. This suggests that Yinxie I Formula may reduce the expression of pro-inflammatory cytokines by decreasing the number of dendritic cells, macrophages and T cells to alleviate the symptoms of IMQ-induced psoriasis.

Yinxie I Formula inhibits phosphorylation of STAT3 protein in IMQ-induced psoriasis-like skin lesions

Numerous studies have confirmed that the STAT signaling pathway is closely associated with the process of psoriasis [18–21]. The blockade of intracellular signaling pathways mediated by STAT3 proteins inhibits the gene transcription of pro-inflammatory cytokines and attenuates the infiltration of inflammatory cells in psoriasis.

To further explore the molecular mechanism by which Yinxie I Formula exerts its therapeutic effect on psoriasis, we investigated the protein expression levels of STAT3 and p-STAT3 in the lesional skin of psoriasis mice. Noteworthy, IMQ-induced abnormal elevation of p-STAT3 protein levels in lesional skin of mice with psoriasis was alleviated by Yinxie I Formula treatment. And this suppression was dose-dependent, the higher the dose, the closer the protein level of p-STAT3 was towards normal (Fig. 4). Yinxie I Formula (H) reduced p-STAT3 slightly more effectively than Calcipotriene.

Yinxie I Formula inhibits cell proliferation activity and anti-inflammatory activity

In psoriasis-like skin lesions, keratin-forming cells proliferate abnormally and the stratum corneum thickens. In order to further verify the effect of Yinxie I Formula, HaCat cells were treated with different concentrations of Yinxie I Formula concentrate for 48 h. Similar to animal experiments, Yinxie I Formula concentrate showed a concentration-dependent inhibition of HaCat cells viability, with the maximum inhibition effect (cell survival rate of 28.46%) at a concentration of 2 mg/mL (Fig. 5A). We also investigated the potential anti-inflammatory effect of Yinxie I Formula in lipopolysaccharide (LPS)-induced RAW264.7 macrophages. The results showed that Yinxie I Formula concentrate not only significantly diminished the proliferative activity of RAW264.7 macrophages in a concentration-dependent manner (Fig. 5B), but also decreased the release of IL-23, IL-6, IL-1β, TNF-α and NO (Fig. 5C).

Buddleoside is the active ingredient in Yinxie I Formula

The complex composition of TCM has limited its wide application in clinical practice, and the identification of the active ingredients of Yinxie I Formula can help to clarify the underlying substances that exert therapeutic effects. We identified Buddleoside as the active substance of Yinxie I Formula by high performance liquid chromatograph/mass spectrometer method and its detected concentration was 16.84 µg/mL (Fig. 6). Buddleoside, also known as Linarin, was first identified as an isoamyl ester in V. wallichii by Thies in 1968 [22]. This suggests that Buddleoside may be the main substance that attenuates IMQ-induced psoriasis-like skin inflammation.

Discussion

A considerable number of previous works have proven that TCM has been traditionally prescribed for the treatment of psoriasis in China for thousands of years [23]. Long-term clinical practice has demonstrated the positive efficacy of TCM in the treatment of psoriasis [24, 25]. Given the diversity of traditional Chinese medicine components, the treatment mechanism of psoriasis varies. Therefore, the treatment methods for psoriasis are also different [15, 26, 27]. Chinese herbal compound Sancao relieves symptoms of IMQ-induced psoriasis model by downregulating the expression of Cyr61 [28]. Our present findings revealed that Yinxie I Formula alleviates IMQ-induced psoriasis-like symptoms in various ways. Additionally, the therapeutic activity of psoriasis mice in Yinxie I Formula was comparable to that of Calcipotriene. However, Calcipotriene has a single target of therapeutic action, is easy to be resistant to drugs, and has obvious toxic side effects with long-term use [29]. In contrast, Yinxie I Formula, a traditional Chinese medicine, showed lower side effects with the same efficacy, suggesting that it can be used as an alternative to Calcipotriene or in combination. We further discovered that the active ingredient in Yinxie I Formula is Buddleoside, also known as linarin. The anti-inflammatory properties of Buddleoside have been extensively studied. Buddleoside may prevent osteoporosis by reducing inflammation [30]. Buddleoside has been reported to ameliorate colitis by inhibiting the inflammatory response [31]. In addition, Buddleoside may ameliorate diabetic liver injury by inhibiting the inflammatory response [32]. This studied findings suggests that Buddleoside is a potential treatment for psoriasis.

As a chronic inflammatory dermatosis, psoriasis can lead to aberrant production of inflammatory cytokines. IL-6 is a pleiotropic pro-inflammatory cytokine with a broad range of biological functions in the immune system [33, 34]. In psoriasis, IL-6 promotes the proliferation of keratinocytes and regulates T-cell differentiation and activation6. IL-6 is a critical mediator of IL-23/IL-17 driven skin inflammation that exacerbates skin lesions in psoriasis [35]. The expression of IL-6 has been reported as a serum marker for psoriasis [36]. Numerous studies have shown that IL-23, produced by macrophages and dendritic cells, is a major regulator of psoriasis, promoting differentiation and activation of Th17 cells and stimulating IL-17 secretion [37–39]. The combination of the two cytokines synergistically plays an important regulatory role in the course of psoriasis [40]. As a cytokine blocking agent, ustekinumab can treat psoriasis by reducing inflammatory cytokines such as IL-6 and IL-8 [41]. In serum, IL-6, IL-17 A, IL-22, IL-23 and TNF-α expression was significantly elevated in patients with psoriasis and the levels of IL-17 A, IL-23 and IL-22 were higher in patients with active disease compared to the group of patients with stable psoriasis [42]. Similarly, Yinxie I Formula also treats psoriasis by reducing inflammatory factors, and as a TCM, Yinxie I Formula is less toxic and cheaper.

The STAT signaling pathway has been proven to be involved in the progression of the inflammatory response in psoriasis, regulating the expression of inflammatory factor genes [19, 43, 44]. Targeted inhibition of STAT signaling pathways may be a treatment for common skin disorders such as psoriasis19. Our results also revealed that, in addition to decreased expression of inflammatory factors, Yinxie I Formula also inhibited STAT signaling pathways. This suggests that Yinxie I Formula, as herbal mixture, plays a diverse role in the treatment of psoriasis. One of the mechanisms by which Yinxie I Formula improves psoriasis symptoms is the STAT signaling pathway.

In conclusion, we explored the potential mechanism of Yinxie I Formula for the treatment of psoriasis, which exerts anti-inflammatory effects both by reducing inflammatory cells and decreasing inflammatory factors, as well as through STAT signaling. This demonstrates the multifaceted effects of Yinxie I Formula for the treatment of psoriasis, suggesting its bright potential as a promoted clinical application.

However, our research is also limited. We have elucidated that Yinxie I Formula treats psoriasis by reducing the expression of validated factors such as IL-6, IL-23 and IL-17 A and the STAT signaling pathway. Only the preliminary effects of Yinxie I Formula in improving psoriasis have been explored, and no further in-depth studies or multi-action studies have been conducted. We also did not have further experimental verification of the effects of Buddleoside. In this way, it is not possible to understand the comprehensive mechanism of Yinxie I Formula for the treatment of psoriasis.
