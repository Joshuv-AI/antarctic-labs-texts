Pipeline multi-type high consequence area identification based on mask R-CNN with fused attention mechanisms
Authors: Dai Xiaojun, Huang Weiyi, Xiao Jianghong, Wei Peng, Ming Xi
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Identification of high consequence areas is an important task in pipeline integrity management. However, traditional identification methods are generally characterized by low efficiency, high cost and low accuracy. For this reason, this paper proposes a recognition method based on the improved algorithm Mask Region-based Convolutional Neural Network. Coordinate attention mechanism module is introduced into the traditional Mask R-CNN algorithm to improve the recognition accuracy and reduce the training time. For the identification results, GIS tools are utilized to establish high consequence zones along both sides of the pipeline, and the grade and scope of the high consequence zones are determined according to relevant specifications.In this paper, this method is used to identify the high-consequence area of a pipeline section in Guangdong Province, the results show that: 1, the improved algorithm in the identification of densely populated, geologic hazards, flammable and explosive high consequence zones of the average accuracy of the identification of 1.7%, 3.4%, 3.9%. 2, The method in this paper identifies 8 more building elements and 0.311 more kilometers of pipeline mileage compared to traditional identification methods. The method of this paper can provide a reference for the early identification of high consequence areas of pipelines.

Introduction

Pipeline high-consequence area refers to the area where oil and gas pipelines may cause serious consequences to the public's personal and property safety in the event of accidents such as leakage or failure, and its grading method is shown in Table 1 Ma et al.1.Accurate and efficient identification of pipeline high-consequence zones in terms of their location, type, and scope is the key step and the first link in realizing the management of high-consequence zones, and in maintaining the pipeline safety operation and maintenance. In recent years, as the scale and mileage of the long-distance oil and gas pipeline network continue to increase, the number and scale of high consequence zones along the corresponding pipelines are also changing. The traditional method of manual identification of high consequence areas is too dependent on subjective experience and is unstable, which will lead to difficulty in guaranteeing the accuracy of each identification result. And the rising cost of labor is also one of the disadvantages of the traditional method. Other automatic identification algorithms researched by previous researchers also have the problems of difficulty in recognizing small targets and low accuracy, which need to be solved urgently. To address these issues, scholars at home and abroad have carried out related research. Domestically, Liu et al.2 proposed a pipeline high-consequence area identification method based on high-resolution remote sensing images, using convolutional neural network and conditional random field combination of two models to extract the elements. Zhang Shuxin et al.3. proposed the pipeline high consequence area intelligent identification and management software design method, realize the software platform to identify and manage the high consequence area. Gou et al.4. proposed a high-consequence area risk assessment based on the improved risk matrix method, which provides a basis for the risk evaluation of high-consequence zones.Ghasem et al.5. proposed a high-consequence area identification method based on machine learning technology, which can help to identify potentially hazardous zones in pipeline risk assessment. Dai et al.6. proposed a mechanism for re-identification and early warning of high-consequence areas, and formed a high-consequence area identification scheme for shale gas gathering and transportation pipelines.

According to the relevant literature and previous studies,There are fewer studies on the use of instance segmentation algorithms for high consequence area recognition, and there is insufficient research on the automatic extraction of high consequence area category elements for other types of high consequence areas besides densely populated high consequence areas, and there are problems such as small target elements in the recognition of consequence areas and difficulties in the recognition of easily obscured by vegetation.

In this paper, we propose to utilize the instance segmentation algorithm Mask R-CNN for multi-category element recognition and extraction. Recognizing multi-class high consequence areas at one time can reduce the workload. On this basis, CA attention mechanism is introduced in the algorithm structure design, which further shortens the algorithm training time and improves the recognition accuracy by reducing the number of global convolution channels.In addition, this paper uses GIS tools to eliminate some of the interfering factors in the test data in order to facilitate the algorithm to make accurate recognition and improve the recognition accuracy. The algorithm in this paper requires only a small amount of manpower to complete the long-distance and wide-range identification work, which greatly reduces the cost of the algorithm.

Ementation algorithm

Algorithm principle and network structure

Instance segmentation algorithms are one of the important types of algorithms in the field of computer vision, whose goal is to accurately identify and localize the pixel-level segmentation region of each object in an image. In recent years, deep learning algorithms based on instance segmentation have made tremendous progress Wei et al.7.

Mask R-CNN is a deep learning algorithm for target detection and instance segmentation tasks Jiang et al.8. The algorithm is based on Faster R-CNN and introduces an additional segmentation head for pixel-level segmentation. It consists of two main components: a feature extraction network and a target detection network. The feature extraction network uses a pre-trained convolutional neural network for extracting high-level abstract features from the input image for subsequent target detection and segmentation tasks. The target detection network consists of RPN (Region Proposal Network) and ROI (Region of Interest) pooling layer Peng et al.9.The RPN is responsible for generating candidate target frames and performing classification and bounding box regression on them.The ROI pooling layer is used to crop and unify the size of the detected target regions for subsequent segmentation operations. Aiming at the problems of small target elements, easy to be obscured by vegetation and insufficient sample sets in some categories in high consequence area recognition, the feature extraction network of the Mask R-CNN algorithm in this paper adopts the architecture of the combination of ResNet50 residual network and FPN pyramid feature network Li et al.10, As shown in Figs. 1. This combination has the advantages of multi-scale feature fusion, high-resolution feature reconstruction and rich semantic information in target detection and instance segmentation tasks, which makes the model can be trained with a small number of samples and also have a good recognition function rate. In this paper, CA attention mechanism is used to improve Mask R-CNN algorithm. Firstly ResNet50 network performs feature extraction on the input data and obtains the feature maps, which are notated as C1, C2, C3, C4, and C5 Yi et al.11. Secondly, the CA attention mechanism applies the corresponding attention weights to each feature channel, learns the importance of the different channels, and improves the channel and spatial focusing ability of the Mask R- CNN model for small targets .

In the algorithm structure of this paper, The ResNet50 residual network encompasses 49 convolutional layers and one fully connected layer. The model is composed of three components: Identity Block, Conv Block, and residual. The function of the Identity Block is to deepen the network, and its input dimension is identical to the output dimension. The task of Conv blocks is to modify the dimensions of the network Zhang et al.12. The FPN (Feature Pyramid Network) pyramid feature network connects the high-level features with low resolution and high semantic information to the low-level features with high resolution and low semantic information from top to bottom, thereby enabling features at all scales to possess rich semantic information. As shown in Fig. 1, FPN passes C1, C2… C6 Extracts feature maps sequentially from the bottom up and makes predictions at each layer.

Attention mechanisms

Principles of general attention mechanisms

Attention mechanism is a technique commonly used in the field of deep learning to simulate the degree of human attention to different information. Introducing an appropriate attention mechanism module into the network structure of deep learning algorithms can enable the algorithms to process the input data in a dynamic and flexible way, enhance the model to improve the feature representation ability, and then improve the model performance and robustness He et al.13. In general, the attention mechanism contains three main parts: query, key, and value. Where: αi denotes the weight corresponding to the ith input data, which can be calculated based on the query vector q and the key vector Ki , and score(q, Ki ) denotes the similarity calculation function between q and Ki . That is, the similarity between q and each key vector Ki is computed and then transformed into a probability distribution using the Softmax function in order to obtain the weight coefficient αi corresponding to each key vector Mirzaei et al.14. CA attention mechanism module

CA (Channel Attention) is a channel attention mechanism module commonly used in convolutional neural networks for dynamically adjusting and weighting the attention of different channels in the feature map to converge Zeyu et al.15. The module can adaptively learn the importance of each channel and weightedly fuse the feature information of different channels. This helps the model capture key features more accurately and improves the performance and robustness of the model. Where: F denotes the input feature map, GAP(F) denotes the vector obtained from the global average pooling operation on the feature map F, MLP denotes the multilayer perceptual machine composed of fully connected layers and activation functions, and σ denotes the activation function. In this paper, Mask R-CNN is fused with CA attention mechanism module to further improve the recognition accuracy and generalization ability of the algorithm to accomplish the task of extracting category elements in high consequence areas.

Since the CA attention module is directly embedded into the Mask R-CNN model and participates in the iterative updating of the network parameters of the entire model, the CA attention weight matrix will be continuously optimized and gradually converge to the optimal weight matrix with the continuous training of the model.

Experimental design

Research methodology

In order to verify the reliability of the algorithm improvement in this paper, the research process is divided into three parts. Firstly review the accuracy of the training dataset, eliminate the problematic or inaccurate parts of it, and adopt image processing methods, such as rotating, scaling, etc., to expand the number of samples for the insufficient number of essential element category data, so that the number of samples for each category of elements is basically the same. Secondly, the original Mas R-CNN backbone network is modified, and the CA attention mechanism is added to the convergence layer, and the ResNet50 + FRN structure is adopted. And the learning parameters of the algorithm are adjusted several times before the training of the algorithm to ensure that its structure is optimal. Finally, the trained weight information is loaded into the algorithm, and the elements are recognized in the sample set of slices in the study area and the high consequence areas are divided in the GIS. In terms of validation, this paper adopts comparison experiments and ablation experiments to verify the superiority and reliability of the algorithm improvement. Experimental data

Training set and validation set

Combined with the identification of high-consequence areas and the actual image recognition, this paper identifies high-consequence areas as densely populated high-consequence areas, high-consequence areas of geologic hazards, and high-consequence areas of flammable and explosive types, which determines that the training set of element categories for residential buildings, geologic hazards (disaster), and flammable and explosive places such as oil and gas storage sites (oil) Haichuan et al.16.

As shown in Figs. 2,the model training set is improved based on the open-source remote sensing buildings database (referred to as WHU database) established by Shunping Ji Viegas et al.17, and the remaining two category elements are added for algorithm training. First, the labeled information and raw images of buildings using this dataset are used to generate example segmented images for algorithm training. Secondly, the publicly available dataset of geohazardous feature areas and flammable and explosive features is small, and data enhancement using flipping, scaling, and vector cropping is used to increase the training sample base Yi et al.18. The Labelme tool is also used to annotate instances of the original remote sensing images containing geohazardous zones and flammable and explosive features, and to generate instance segmentation images. According to the WHU dataset requirements, the ratio of training set to validation set in this dataset is set to 5:1. Conclusion

Firstly, in order to accomplish the task of multi-type high consequence area recognition, this paper improves on the basis of the public data of WHU buildings, and increases the training data of example segmentation of flammable and explosive features and geologic hazard areas. Second, in order to explore the effect of algorithm improvement, this paper uses the original Mask R-CNN algorithm and the Mask R-CNN algorithm improved by introducing the CA module to carry out the task of detecting the target of high-consequence area category elements under the same parameter conditions, and uses multiple indicators for model evaluation. Finally, high consequence zones are generated based on the target detection results in the experimental area according to the relevant specifications, and their ranges and numbers are counted and compared with the identification results of high consequence zones obtained by traditional methods to verify the accuracy. The results are as follows:

Firstly, model accuracy, in this use of the experimental area sample dataset, Mask R-CNN algorithm after the introduction of the CA module to improve, its in the densely populated, geologic hazards, flammable and explosive high-consequence area category elements extracted the average accuracy of 1.7%, 3.4%, 3.9%, respectively, the average accuracy of the average category of the average accuracy of the mean value of the improvement of 3%1

Secondly, on the identification of high-consequence areas, compared with the traditional put, the category elements extracted using the method of this paper are more detailed, the building elements are 8 more than the traditional method, and the mileage of the pipeline in the high-consequence area is increased by 0.311KM.

Shortcomings and prospects

Based on the previous research, this study uses an improved algorithm to further increase the recognition accuracy of multi-type element extraction. The results can be applied in pipeline management work, especially for pipeline operation areas with complex terrain and more sensitive features, to realize fast and reliable identification of high consequence areas, no longer need to invest a lot of manpower along the line of investigation, and the improvement of the accuracy makes the identification results more reliable Liu Yi et al.19.

At the same time, the results of this paper also has certain limitations, some areas of the training samples are not sufficient, it is difficult to reach the minimum learning volume required by the deep learning algorithm, which also restricts its wider popularization and application, and the subsequent evolution of the algorithm can be based on the use of a small sample dataset algorithms to achieve automatic identification, in order to further improve the method system Wei Yarong et al.20.
