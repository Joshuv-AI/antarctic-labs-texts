In the section “Adapting the Stepped Care Model in Singapore: The Tiered Care Model”

The Singapore Ministry of Health, Institute of Mental Health, and various community partners have implemented a range of mental health initiatives, including the National Mental Health Blueprint (2007-2012) and the subsequent Community Mental Health Masterplan (2012-2021), aimed at providing comprehensive, accessible, and community-based mental health services

In the section “Tier 2: Early Intervention and Low-Intensity Support”

COMIT complements CREST by delivering more structured support through clinical assessment, psychotherapy, and psychosocial interventions, including cognitive behavioural therapy and psychoeducation for both individuals and their caregivers

In the section “Tier 3: Moderate-Intensity Support and Psychotherapy”

The Mental Health General Practitioner (GP) Partnership and Primary Care Network-Mental train GPs and polyclinic doctors to diagnose and manage mental health conditions within the community

Further clarifications have been made to Fig. 1, under the “Services” column of the pyramid, to accurately reflect the appropriate Tier under which each service falls

- CREST has been moved from a Tier 1 service to a Tier 2 service

- COMIT has been moved from a Tier 2 service to a tier 3 service

- IMH and ASCAT has been moved from a tier 3 service to a tier 4 service

- Removal of MHCTA as a Tier 4 service

- The arrow on the left stating “right-siting” has been removed
