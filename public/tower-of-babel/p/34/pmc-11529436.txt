Functional Outcome in Primary Anterior Cruciate Ligament Repair versus Anterior Cruciate Ligament Reconstruction: A Meta-Analysis
Authors: Ketut Gede Arya Surya Pranata, Gusti Ngurah Wien Aryana
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackground:In recent years, interest in modern anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) suture repair procedures has increased. Although some clinical studies have produced positive short-term outcomes, there is a lack of high-quality information about the efficacy of this treatment in comparison to ACL reconstruction. We aim to compare the therapeutic effect of primary repair ACL injury with reconstruction and provide the reliable evidence for its clinical application.Methods:PubMed, EMBASE, Scopus, Web of Science and Google Scholar were searched for relevant studies from January 2019 to August 2023. This research will include English-language, randomized, controlled studies that compare functional outcome (IKDC subjective score, Lysholm Score, and Tegner score) in primary ACL repair versus ACL reconstruction.Result:Five studies were used ranging from 2019 to 2023 which discussed the comparison of the treatment of ACL rupture with primary ACL repair and ACL Reconstruction. Both group of Primary ACL Repair and ACL Reconstruction have a similar in outcome in Lysholme score and Tegner score. Meanwhile they have a significant differences in IKDC score which higher in ACL Reconstruction group compare to primary ACL repair group (9,63 points, 95% CI=7.54-11,72, p<0.00001) and a higher rate of complication on primary ACL repair than the ACL Reconstruction group.Conclusion:Primary ACL Repair and ACL Reconstruction have a difference functional outcome for IKDC score. While the choice for treatment is still depend to individual patient for some factors including severity of injury, patient age, activity level, graft choice, and long-term goals.Keywords: Anterior cruciate ligament, Functional outcome, Primary repair, Reconstruction.
