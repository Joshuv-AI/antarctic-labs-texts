Vascular Tree
Authors: Batool Rizvi
Published: 2023

Artist: Batool Rizvi is a doctoral student in the University of California Irvine's Department of Neurobiology and Behavior, studying brain imaging markers of vascular pathology and their relationship to Alzheimer's disease and cognitive decline with aging.

About the art: This art piece was digitally painted using Procreate, a digital illustration app, on an iPad and with an Apple Pencil.

Artist perspective: This art piece visualizes a tree with its branches and roots reaching far out to the sky and the earth, providing a sense of life amidst a nighttime backdrop. The branching tree and far‐reaching roots symbolize the arterial branching and blood supply to the brain, which similarly provides life to the human brain. The anatomy of the tree, including its branches and roots in differing sizes, directions, and angles, indicates the complexity, variety, and intricacy implicated in the perfusion of blood to various parts of the brain. Furthermore, the tree is enclosed in an axial view of the brain. The exposed tree arising above ground resembles the anterior circulation, and the root system below ground follows the posterior circulation. The colors used provide both richness and a sense of serenity in the painting, in varying shades of blue, green, and magenta, carrying a unifying theme of flowing energy, comparable to vascular health.
