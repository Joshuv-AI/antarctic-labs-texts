EIF4A3-mediated oncogenic circRNA hsa_circ_0001165 advances esophageal squamous cell carcinoma progression through the miR-381-3p/TNS3 pathway
Authors: Xun Zhang, Yan Bian, Qiuxin Li, Chuting Yu, Ye Gao, Bo Tian, Wenqiang Xia, Wei Wang, Lei Xin, Han Lin, Luowei Wang
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractEsophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC) remains a major clinical challenge due to its poor prognosis and the scarcity effective therapeutic targets. Circular RNAs (circRNAs) are crucial in cancer progression. In this study, high-throughput sequencing was employed to profile ESCC tissues, revealing that hsa_circ_0001165 is notably elevated in both ESCC tumor samples and cell lines, with its expression is positively associated with patients' TNM staging. Knockdown of hsa_circ_0001165 resulted in reduced malignant biological behavior of ESCC cells in vitro and also inhibited tumor growth in vivo. Mechanism experimental analysis found that hsa_circ_0001165 expression is positively enhanced by eukaryotic translation initiation factor 4A3 (EIF4A3). Hsa_circ_0001165 acts as a miRNA sponge for miR-381-3p, increasing the expression of tensin-3 (TNS3) through a series of related mechanism assays include dual-luciferase reporter gene, RNA Immunoprecipitation and RNA-pulldown. The downregulation in miR-381-3p expression was observed in ESCC tissues, and the cell proliferation, invasion, and migration of ESCC were suppressed. The upregulated expression of hsa_circ_0001165 modulates the miR-381-3p/TNS3 axis and promotes aggressive phenotypes of ESCC. Hsa_circ_0001165 is regarded as a encouraging biomarker and potential therapeutic target for ESCC, presenting innovative options for both diagnostic and treatment approaches.Supplementary InformationThe online version contains supplementary material available at 10.1007/s10565-024-09927-9.Keywords: Esophageal Squamous Cell Carcinoma, Hsa_circ_0001165, MiR-381-3p, TNS3

Introduction

A global survey revealed that esophageal cancer ranks as sixth commonly diagnosed cancer type the seventh major reason for cancer-related fatalities (Sung et al. 2021). The predominant subtype observed in regions such as East Asia and South Africa is esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC) (Abnet et al. 2018; Mazidimoradi et al. 2023). Although there have been advancements in diagnostic and therapeutic approaches, the survival rate for ESCC patients continues to be disappointing (Huang et al. 2021). This highlights the necessity for enhanced insights into the molecular mechanisms that contribute to ESCC in order to enhance patient outcomes.

Circular RNAs (circRNAs) are noncoding RNAs that are generated via back-splicing. They are distinguished by the lack of a 5' cap and poly A tail. The inherent stability of circRNAs and their resistance to degradation by nucleases enable their detection in body fluids, underscoring their potential as reliable biomarkers(Yang et al. 2022). Further research into the dysregulated expression and regulatory pathways of circRNAs in esophageal cancer is essential, emphasizing the need for comprehensive studies that could revolutionize diagnostic and therapeutic strategies in ESCC(Wei et al. 2021). CircRNAs are known for their roles as miRNA sponges, encoding small peptides, and interacting with proteins during tumor progression, demonstrating their diverse biological functions(He and Zhu 2023). Current studies have identified numerous differentially expressed circRNAs in esophageal cancer(Zhang et al. 2020b). however, the aberrant expression and underlying regulatory mechanisms of many circRNAs remain incompletely understood.

This study identified hsa_circ_0001165 as a novel circRNA with elevated expression in ESCC and positively correlated with TNM staging. Hsa_circ_0001165 functions in ESCC progression by elucidating its molecular features, biological characteristics, and functional role. According to our findings, hsa_circ_0001165 has considerable potential as a new biomarker for ESCC, offering new research avenues and offering promising targets for clinical diagnostics and therapeutic interventions.

Materials and methods

ESCC tissue specimen and high-throughput sequencing

ESCC tissues and paired adjacent non-tumor samples were preserved at -80 °C at Changhai Hospital, Navy Medical University, following approval by the Medical Ethics Committee(approval no.CHEC2023-018). The Declaration of Helsinki was followed for informed consent. Circular RNA expression profiles were analyzed by RNA sequencing in four pairs of ESCC and adjacent non-cancerous tissue samples. Total RNA was extracted using TRIzol reagent, and its integrity and purity were verified. An Illumina HiSeq 4000 platform was used to sequence the RNA-seq libraries at Shanghai OE Biotech Co. Ltd. With bioinformatics analyses, including circRNA prediction via CIRI2R software peforemd subsequently. Additionally, 32 pairs of ESCC tissue samples were collected to analyze clinical features and validate tissue expression levels. A detailed description of clinical characteristics presented Supplementary Table S1.

Cell culture

Cell lines obtained from esophageal cancer, including KYSE30, KYSE150, KYSE510, TE-1, and Eca109, as well as the HEK 293 cell line, were provided by Procell Life Science & Technology Co. Ltd(Wuhan,China). Meanwhile, normal esophageal epithelial cells (HET-1A) were sourced from Immocell Biotechnology Co.Ltd. KYSE30, KYSE150, KYSE510, TE-1, and Eca109 cultured in RPMI 1640 medium; DMEM for HEK293T; and a BEGM kit for HET-1A. Each medium type was supplemented with 10% fetal bovine serum to maintain cellular growth.

Real-time quantitative PCR

We isolated RNA samples from ESCC cell lines and tissues utilizing TRIzol reagent(Sigma Aldrich). The cDNA was synthesized from 1000 ng of RNA and analyzed via qRT-PCR with SYBR Green Master Mix. Using the QIAGEN miRNA First-Strand cDNA Kit and miRNA qPCR Kits, miRNA was analyzed through qRT-PCR analysis. GAPDH was used as the normalization value for circRNAs and mRNA expression and U6 for miRNA expression. qRT-PCR was conducted using the LightCycler® 480 Instrument following the manufacturer's guidelines. Gene expression levels were quantified using A two-fold Ct method, with sequences of primer detailed in Supplementary Table S2.

Lentiviral transduction and plasmid construction

The shRNA targeting hsa_circ_0001165 was suppressed via lentiviral transduction, with a non-targeting shRNA as the control, both synthesized by OBiO Technology (Shanghai, China). KYSE30 and KYSE150 cells at 50% confluence were infected at an MOI of 20 and then selected with 2 mg/mL of puromycin (Yeason, China).Genechem Co. Ltd (Shanghai, China) synthesized the hsa_circ_0001165 and the TNS3 overexpression plasmid, which were transfected into KYSE30 and KYSE150 using Lipofectamine 3000 (Invitrogen). In addition to mimics, inhibitors, and negative control sequences for MiR-381-3p, Lipofectamine RNAiMAX (Invitrogen) was used for transfection. qRT-PCR confirmed the success of the transfections.

Western blotting

For the analysis of proteins, cells were initially lysed using a pre-cooled RIPA buffer for 15 min. Following this, the samples were centrifuged for ten minutes at 12,000 rpm, and the protein concentration was measured using the BCA. Following denaturement for ten minutes, proteins were separated onto 10% SDS-PAGE gel. A polyvinylidene fluoride membrane (Millipore, USA) was used to isolate proteins after electrophoresis. The membranes were treated with non-fat milk for one hour to block them, followed by an overnight incubation at 4 °C with primary antibodies: anti-EIF4A3 (1:1200), anti-TNS3 (1:1000), and anti-GAPDH (1:50,000), all obtained from Proteintech, USA. After the incubation, the membrane visualization was carried out using an ECL reagent and detected with an Amersham Mager 600 amplification detector following incubation with a 1:5000 dilution of a secondary antibody (Proteintech, USA).

Nucleocytoplasmic separation experiment

Invitrogen's Ambion® PARISTM Kit was used to isolate RNA samples from nuclear and cytoplasmic fractions. qRT-PCR was performed to detect circular RNA in cDNA from nuclear and cytoplasmic samples with U6 and GAPDH serving as controls.

CCK-8 and clone formation assay

After seeding 3,000 cells in each 96-well plate, we introduced 10 µL of CCK-8 solution at 24, 48, 72, and 96 h following cell adherence. Following a two-hour incubation, the absorbance of the samples was evaluated at 450 nm using a SpectraMax i3 microplate reader. Each experimental condition was assessed in triplicate wells.

To conduct colony assays, introduce 1500 cells into each well of a 6-well plate containing pre-warmed complete medium. Maintain incubation at 37 °C in an atmosphere of 5% CO₂ for a duration of 10 to 14 days. Afterward, rinse the wells using PBS, fix the samples with 4% paraformaldehyde for 10 to 15 min, and subsequently stain with 0.5% crystal violet diluted in methanol for 15 min. Finally, utilize a microscope to count colonies consisting of more than 50 cells and carry out the proliferation rate with ImageJ software (version 1.8.0).

5-Ethynyl-2’-deoxyuridine (EdU) Assay

The process involved incubating a total of 500 L of medium with 10 μmol EdU in a 24-well plate to evaluate the incorporation of EdU. Cells that had been transfected were added to the plates and incubated for two hours in a medium enriched with 10 μmol EdU, utilizing the Cell Proliferation Kit (Beyotime, China). In order to stabilize the cells, paraformaldehyde was utilized for a duration of 30 min. This was succeeded by permeabilization using 0.5% Triton-X-100 for 20 min, followed by a wash with 3% BSA. After performing staining with Alexa Fluor 555, DAPI was utilized for Hoechst staining. Fluorescence images were acquired with a fluorescence microscope (Leica, Germany) and subsequently analyzed with ImageJ software (version 1.8.0).

Assays for cell migration and invasion

For the assessment of invasion or migration, a 1:8 dilution of Matrigel was prepared in medium with serum-free and applied at a volume of 100 μL per well into a 24-well plate that housed inserts with pore sizes of 8 mm. A serum-free medium contained with 5 × 10^4 per well was in the upper chamber of each insert, while the lower compartment was supplemented with 20% fetal bovine serum, serving as a chemoattractant for the growth of ESCC cells. For the upper chamber, 48 h at 37 °C were incubated, followed by 20 min of fixative and staining with crystal violet. ImageJ software (v1.8.0) was used to analyze microscope images from Olympus (Japan) to determine cell migration and invasion rates.

RNA fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH)

Employing the Fluorescent In Situ Hybridization Kit from RiboBio (Guang Zhou), probes labeled with Cy3 were synthesized for hsa_circ_0001165, while FAM-labeled probes were designed for miR-381-3p. Cells underwent fixation using paraformaldehyde for two hours at a temperature of 4 °C. For cell permeabilization, a 0.5% Triton X-100 solution was utilized for five minutes. After a prehybridization step at 37 °C for 30 min, 5 M probes were hybridized overnight, and imaging was carried out using a Leica confocal microscope.

RNA pulldown assays

Probes that were biotinylated and aimed at hsa_circ_0001165, along with corresponding controls, were generated by CloudSeq Biotech (Shanghai, China), with the specific probe sequences provided in Supplementary Table S3. For this assay, Thermo Fisher Scientific's RNA–Protein Pull-Down Kit was employed to attach biotinylated RNA to streptavidin-coated magnetic beads, and the whole-cell lysates were allowed to incubate overnight at 4 °C with the biotinylated RNA. After the incubation period, the RNA samples were extracted for the assessment of hsa_circ_0001165 and miR-381-3p levels.

RNA immunoprecipitation (RIP) experiment

In this experiment, KYSE30 and KYSE150 cells (2.0 × 10^7) coated with magnetic beads both anti-IgG (1000 μg/ml, 5 μg, Proteintech, USA) or anti-Ago2 (2000 μg/ml, 5 μg, Proteintech, USA) antibodies Millipore's MagnaRIP Kit (Millipore, 17–700 Sigma-Aldrich, Germany). For miR-381-3p and hsa_circ_0001165 enrichment analyses, RNA was extracted overnight and purified.

Double luciferase reporting experiment

The promoter vector for both wild-type and mutant variants of hsa_circ_0001165, along with the 3' UTR of TNS3 (Genecreat, Wuhan, China), features binding sites for miR-381-3p located adjacent to the luciferase coding region. The experiments were executed 24 h after transfection utilizing the Dual-Luciferase Reporter Assay Kit sourced from Vazyme Biotech Co. Ltd, with normalization of Renilla luciferase activity being carried out in GraphPad Prism version 9.0 to calculate luciferase activity.

Nude mouse xenograft tumor model

Throughout the experiments, the Animal Ethics Committee of Naval Medical University approved the use of BALB/c nude mice aged 4–6 weeks purchased from Gempharmatech Co. Ltd. (Suzhou, China) and housed in the animal experiment facility of Changhai Hospital. Experimental and control group using ten mice were randomly assigned to establish xenograft tumor models for ESCC. Each mouse received subcutaneous injections approximately 5 × 10^6 cells of KYSE30 with sh-circ_0001165 or sh-NC. Tumor volumes were measured every five days over a period of 30 days, length × (width/2)^2 was used to as the formula to calculate tumor volume. An assessment of tumor size and weight was made after euthanasia of the mice on day 30.

Public data acquisition and statistical analysis

The GSE53622 dataset contains gene expression profiles from (ESCC) samples(https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE53622). The dataset includes both tumor and adjacent normal tissue samples, providing paired data for analysis. Quality control measures were implemented to ensure reliable data, including normalization of the microarray data and verification of sample integrity. GSE53622 consists of 119 paired samples, offering a substantial dataset for identifying differentially expressed genes and developing predictive models for ESCC prognosis.

GraphPad Prism 9.0 software was used to analyze the data, with results from repeated experiments expressed as means ± standard deviation (SD). Student's t-tests were used to evaluate differences between groups, and one-way ANOVAs were used for multiple group comparisons. Fisher's exact test emphasized the clinical significance of molecular expression, and while Pearson correlation coefficients were utilized to analyzed the correlation among hsa_circ_0001165, miR-381-3p, and TNS3 expressions. Statistical tests significant when P-values below 0.05.

Results

Expression profile of circular RNAs and molecular characteristics of hsa_circ_0001165 in ESCC

Differential expression of circular RNAs (circRNAs) in ESCC tissues and paired non-cancerous samples was identified through high-throughput sequencing (The threshold set as Log2FC threshold ≥ 1 and p-value < 0.05). These differences were visualized using bar plots and a heatmap (Supplemental Figure S1A and B). An analysis of the top 20 upregulated and downregulated circRNAs is presented as a clustered heatmap (Fig. 1A). In collaboration with bioinformatics analyses, literature reviews, and preliminary experiments, hsa_circ_0001165 emerged as a novel candidate for investigation. This circular RNA, originating from exons 4–9 of the NCOA3 gene (located at chr20: 46,252,654–46,262,380), spans 881 base pairs and is formed through head-to-tail splicing. Sanger sequencing of hsa_circ_0001165 confirmed its splice junction, as documented in the circBase database (http://www.circbase.org/) (Figure. 1B). Using divergent and convergent primers in agarose gel electrophoresis assays, we demonstrated cDNA amplification but not gDNA, indicating that hsa_circ_0001165 does not result from gene rearrangement (Fig. 1C). A RNase R treatment showed comparable stability for hsa_circ_0001165 as compared with linear NCOA3 mRNA (Fig. 1D). Additionally, we performed a nuclear-cytoplasmic fractionation assay and a FISH experiment to determine hsa_circ_0001165's subcellular location.(Figs. 1E-F).According to these studies, hsa_circ_0001165 localizes primarily in the cytoplasm. RT-qPCR analysis confirmed that tumors expressed significantly higher levels of hsa_circ_0001165 than their matched normal counterparts(Fig. 1G). Moreover, hsa_cric_0001165 expression was notably higher in several ESCC cell lines (KYSE30, KYSE150, KYSE510, Ec109,TE-1) as compared to Het-1A cell line (Fig. 1H). In conclusion, our studies demonstrate that hsa_circ_0001165 constitutes a closed-loop circular RNA, characterized by enzymatic stability and localization within the cytoplasm in ESCC.

Hsa_circ_0001165 expression levels in ESCC showed a positive correlation with TNM staging and lymph node metastasis, as presented in Supplementary Table S1.

Hsa_circ_0001165 enhances ESCC cell proliferation, migration, and invasion.

The role of hsa_circ_0001165 in ESCC was investigated by transfecting KYSE30 and KYSE150 cells with lentiviral shRNA and an overexpression plasmid targeting its splice junction. qRT-PCR confirmed the efficiency of both downregulation and overexpression. NCOA3 expression remained unchanged with shRNA and plasmid conduction (Fig. 2A). Results from CCK-8, EdU and colony formation demonstrated that lowering the levels of hsa_circ_0001165 led to a reduction in cell viability and proliferation(Fig. 2B-D). The migration and invasion capabilities of ESCC cells were demonstrated diminished in both wound healing and transwell assays when the expression of hsa_circ_0001165 was downregulated. (Fig. 2E,F). Conversely, KYSE30 and KYSE150 cells overexpressing hsa_circ_0001165 showed increased proliferation, migration, and invasion capabilities(Supplemental Figure S2).Above all, these results indicate that hsa_circ_0001165 functions as an oncogenic factor in ESCC.

MiR-381-3p is targeted by hsa_circ_0001165 to promote proliferation, migration, and invasion of ESCC.

CircRNAs are thought to act like miRNA sponges, affecting mRNA stability and transcription after transcription(Hansen et al. 2013; Misir et al. 2022). Given the cytoplasmic localization of hsa_circ_0001165 observed in FISH experiments, we hypothesize that ESCC may be driven by a similar mechanism. Potential miRNA targets for hsa_circ_0001165 were determined using the CSCD2.0 (http://gb.whu.edu.cn/CSCD2/), circBank( (http://www.microrna.org) databasesand databases and miRanda (http://www.microrna.org/microrna/home.do), screening out seven predicted miRNAs (Supplemental Figure S3).RNA pull-down assays with hsa_circ_0001165 and control probes, followed by qRT-PCR, revealed a significantly higher binding fraction of miR-381-3p to hsa_circ_0001165 than the negative control(Fig. 3A).The functional role of circRNA as a miRNA sponge typically involves its association with AGO2 to form AGO2-miRNA complexes(Chen et al. 2019).In addition, anti-AGO2 RNA immunoprecipitation (RIP) confirmed hsa_circ_0001165 and miR-381-3p bind to AGO2, further substantiating the concept that hsa_circ_0001165 functions as a miRNA sponge(Fig. 3B). Utilizing the RNA hybrid database, predictions indicated that miR-381-3p and hsa_circ_0001165 possess complementary binding sites along with mutations(Fig. 3C). Results from dual-luciferase reporter assays demonstrated a significant reduction in luciferase activity for the circ_0001165-wt reporter upon application of the miR-381-3p mimic, relative to the miR-NC mimics. By contrast, the circ_0001165-mt reporter exhibited no significant difference in luciferase activity between the miR-NC and miR-381-3p mimic groups (Fig. 3D). The findings indicate that hsa_circ_0001165 has a specific binding affinity to miR-381-3p within the CTTGTATG region. FISH experiments validated the location of both hsa_circ_0001165 and miR-381-3p in the cytoplasm(Fig. 3E). Following the knockdown of hsa_circ_0001165, an elevation in the expression of miR-381-3p was assessed by qRT-PCR (Fig. 3F). In tissues from ESCC, a notable reduction in miR-381-3p levels was detected, showing a negative correlation with the expression of hsa_circ_0001165. (Fig. 3G,H). To further support the outcomes of the functional rescue experiments, a miR-381-3p inhibitor was introduced to KYSE30 and KYSE150 cell lines (Fig. 3I). Notably, the reduction of miR-381-3p led to the restoration of cell proliferation (Figs. 3J and K), migration (Fig. 3L), and invasion (Fig. 3M) of ESCC cells that were transfected with shRNA targeting hsa_circ_0001165. Serving as a molecular sponge, hsa_circ_0001165 facilitates the advancement of ESCC by sequestering miR-381-3p.

MiR-381-3p suppresses ESCC progression in vitro

The results from qRT-PCR assays indicated that both mimics and inhibitors had elevating and lowering effects on miR-381-3p expression (Fig. 4A). Moreover, results from the CCK-8 assay indicated that miR-381-3p mimics suppressed the growth of KYSE30 and KYSE150 cells, while the inhibitors exhibited the opposite effect. (Fig. 4B, C). Furthermore, EdU assays and colony formation studies validated that miR-381-3p mimics suppressed cellular proliferation (Fig. 4D, E), in contrast to its inhibitors which foster cell growth (Fig. 4F, G). Transwell assays additionally indicated that the addition of miR-381-3p mimics led to a reduced capacity for migration and invasion in the cells, whereas the inhibition of miR-381-3p yielded the opposite effect. (Fig. 4H, I). Overall, these findings highlight the suppressive function role of miR-381-3p in ESCC.

MiR-381-3p targets TNS3 as a downstream target

To determine potential downstream targets, a transcriptomic analysis was conducted on KYSE150 cells after the knockdown of hsa_circ_0001165(Fig. 5A and Supplemental Figure S5A-D).This analysis revealed 141 genes that were significantly upregulated and 823 genes that were downregulated(log2 fold change ≥ 2 and a q-value < 0.05). Generally, miRNAs inhibit gene expression through interactions with 3'UTRs(Shang et al. 2023). We integrated our RNA-seq data with the miRDB (https://mirdb.org/) and TargetScan (https://www.targetscan.org/vert_80/) databases, enabling us to discover 15 genes that are regulated downstream of both hsa_circ_0001165 and miR-381-3p. A comprehensive analysis conducted on the GEPIA database (http://gepia.cancer-pku.cn/) identified five oncogenes characterized by significantly elevated expression levels: WNT5A, TNS3, SLC25A24, KIF11, and ERF. (Supplemental Figure S4). Knockdown of hsa_circ_0001165 significantly reduced TNS3 levels, as confirmed by qRT-PCR assays(Fig. 5B).To verify miR-381-3p's direct regulation of TNS3, we created luciferase reporter plasmids for both wild-type and mutant TNS3 with predicted miR-381-3p binding sites(Fig. 5C). Luminferase activity decreased in the TNS3-wt construct with miR-381-3p mimics co-transfection, but remained unchanged in TNS3-mut plasmids(Fig. 5D). Results from qRT-PCR and Western blot assays showed that using miR-381-3p mimics caused a decrease in TNS3 expression, whereas employing miR-381-3p inhibitors led to a rise in TNS3 levels (Fig. 5E,F). Additionally, the downregulation of hsa_circ_001165 resulted in a decrease in the expression level of TNS3; however, the further inhibition of miR-381-3p restored its expression (Fig. 5G). Compared to adjacent non-cancerous tissues, TNS3 expression levels was significantly higher in 32 matched pairs of esophageal squamous carcinoma tissues (Fig. 5H). TNS3 expression showed a positive correlation with hsa_circ_0001165 and a negative correlation with miR-381-3p (Fig. 5I, J). Analysis of the public dataset GSE53622 also indicated overexpression of TNS3 in cancer tissues and an association with a poor prognosis in ESCC patients. (Fig. 5 K, L).

The hsa_circ_0001165/miR-381-3p/TNS3 regulatory axis facilitates the in vitro progression of ESCC

To determine whether hsa_circ_0001165 facilitates ESCC progression in vitro via the miR-381-3p/TNS3 pathway, we constructed a TNS3 overexpression plasmid and confirmed transfection efficiency using qRT-PCR in KYSE30 and KYSE150 cells (Fig. 6A). In CCK-8 assays(Figs. 6B, C) and EdU assays(Figs. 6D, E), silencing hsa_circ_0001165 reduced proliferation, but overexpression of TNS3 reversed the effect. Transwell assays evaluating cell migration and invasion (Figs. 6F and G) offered further proof that the overexpression of TNS3 mitigated the suppressive effects caused by the knockdown of hsa_circ_0001165 on the functionality of ESCC cells. These findings suggest that hsa_circ_0001165 promotes the aggressiveness of ESCC through the miR-381-3p/TNS3 signaling pathway.

EIF4A3 is the upstream regulator involved in the biosynthesis of hsa_circ_0001165

RNA-binding proteins (RBPs) are pivotal in the regulation of circular RNA (circRNA) synthesis(Zang et al. 2020).To elucidate the mechanisms that enhance the expression of hsa_circ_0001165 in ESCC, the database of Circular Interactome (https://circinteractome.nia.nih.gov/) was utilized to identify possible binding sites for RBPs in the flanking regions of hsa_circ_0001165. EIF4A3 was identified as the RBP with the highest number of predicted binding sites (Supplementary Figure S6A-C). Six segments (a, b, c, d, e, and f) were defined based on these predicted regions (Fig. 7A). RIP experiments conducted with an EIF4A3 antibody revealed that the RNA co-precipitated with EIF4A3 contained elevated levels of fragments a, b, c, and d (Fig. 7B), thereby confirming the binding of EIF4A3 to the predicted regions of NCOA3 pre-mRNA. We proposed that changes in EIF4A3 levels would influence the expression of hsa_circ_0001165. The evaluation of EIF4A3 knockdown effectiveness was conducted through qPCR and Western blot analysis, where interference fragments 1 and 2 demonstrated the highest efficacy (Fig. 7C, D). qRT-PCR results indicated that EIF4A3 suppression led to reduced hsa_circ_0001165 expression(Fig. 7E). Additionally, EIF4A3 expression is elevated in ESCC tissues, showing a significant positive correlation with hsa_circ_0001165 levels (Fig. 7F, G). Our data suggest that EIF4A3 acts as a regulatory protein promotes biogenesis of hsa_circ_0001165.

Silencing hsa_circ_0001165 suppresses in vivo tumor growth

Nude mice were subcutaneously injected with KYSE30 cells transfected with sh-circ_0001165 or sh-NC to test whether hsa_circ_0001165 knockdown could inhibit tumor growth in vivo(Fig. 8A). In comparison to the control group, the knockdown group showed a significant decrease in tumor volume and weight (Fig. 8B and C). Findings from the immunohistochemical assessment indicated Ki-67, PCNA, and TNS3 expression levels were reduced within the xenograft tumors derived from the hsa_circ_0001165 knockdown cohort (Fig. 8D). Discussion

As a malignancy of the digestive tract, esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC) is characterized by a five-year survival rate that below 20% (Huang et al.). Advances in bioinformatics and high-throughput sequencing have revealed many deregulated and functional circular RNAs in various tumors (Kristensen et al. 2022; Vo et al. 2019).Xi found that circBCAR3 can act as an oncogenic factor by adsorbing miR-27a-3p, thereby alleviating the inhibition of the upregulation of transportin-1 (TNPO1)(Xi et al. 2022).Another study demonstrated that a hypoxia-induced circRNA, circPUM1, facilitates the progression of esophageal squamous carcinoma by preserving mitochondrial stability and promoting oxidative phosphorylation via interactions between UQCRC1 and UQCRC2(Gong et al. 2022).Nevertheless, the functional roles of many circRNAs in ESCC are still not well understood. This study utilized high-throughput sequencing to profile differentially expressed circular RNAs between ESCC tissues and their corresponding non-cancerous adjacent tissues.We identified that hsa_circ_0001165, originating from NCOA3 pre-mRNA, is markerly elevated in ESCC tissues and cell lines, showing a positive correlation with tumor N stage and TNM stage in patients with ESCC.Hsa_circ_0001165 facilitates ESCC progression through the modulation of the miR-381-3p/TNS3 regulatory axis. Additionally, our results confirm that the EIF4A3 as the RNA-binding protein mediates the biogenesis of circ_0001165 in ESCC.

The biogenesis of circRNAs is influenced by specific trans-acting factors (Chen 2020), with RNA-binding proteins like QKI, FUS, and EIF4A3 reported to regulate their circularization and expression(Chioccarelli et al. 2021; Conn et al. 2015; Kong et al. 2023; Yang et al. 2022).Using the CircInteractome database, we predicted potential binding sites for multiple RNA-binding proteins and identified ten EIF4A3 binding sites flanking the circularized NCOA3 sequence within NCOA3 pre-mRNA. EIF4A3 constitutes a critical component of the exon junction complex, playing an indispensable role in RNA splicing, mRNA export, and decay(Xue et al. 2021).Moreover, EIF4A3 is an oncogene in various malignancies(Sridharan et al. 2019; Zhang et al. 2020a).Beyond its regulatory roles in the cell cycle and apoptosis, EIF4A3 can enhance circRNA expression by binding to pre-mRNA(Lu et al. 2023; Wei et al. 2024).Our findings confirmed that EIF4A3 facilitates the circular expression of hsa_circ_0001165 through its interaction with NCOA3 pre-mRNA and a positive correlation exists between EIF4A3 and hsa_circ_0001165.However, whether EIF4A3 is involved in transporting NCOA3 pre-mRNA and the detailed mechanisms by which EIF4A3 mediates pre-mRNA back-splicing require further investigation.

The endogenous competing RNA (ceRNA) hypothesis posits that circRNAs can sponge miRNAs to regulate gene expression(Yang et al. 2022). Our research, using bioinformatics, RNA pull-down, RIP, and dual-luciferase reporter assays, confirmed that hsa_circ_0001165 interacts directly with miR-381-3p. The results suggest that hsa_circ_0001165 operate as a competitive endogenous RNA in facilitating ESCC tumorigenesis.miR-381-3p is recongnized as the tumor suppressor (Sha et al. 2022)plays an important role in cancer-related signaling pathways such as Wnt/-catenin, AKT, and p53(Zeng et al. 2020). Moreover, miR-381-3p has been linked to various types of cancer, including gastric cancer(Xie et al. 2019), breast cancer(Yu et al. 2021), and head and neck squamous cell carcinoma(Kong et al. 2020). Our study reinforces the conclusions of Zhou et al. (Zhou et al. 2015)by demonstrating that miR-381-3p inhibits the growth of esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC) tumors. In addition, miR-381-3p levels are reduced in ESCC cells that resist radiotherapy. TNS3 (Tensin3), part of the tensin protein family, has been associated with the progression and clinical importance of various cancers, such as colorectal carcinoma (Thorpe et al. 2015), pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (Klein et al. 2018), and renal carcinoma(Carter et al. 2013). Recent findings indicate that elevated TNS3 expression correlates with increased malignancy and worse prognosis in patient with ESCC. Inhibition of TNS3 reduces ESCC cell proliferation and enhances sensitivity to the histone deacetylase inhibitor LMK-235 (Shi et al. 2021). However, the specific signaling pathways involving TNS3 in ESCC remain to be elucidated. This study reveals that hsa_circ_0001165 interacts with miR-381-3p to modulate TNS3 expression, thereby driving ESCC progression.

Conclusion

The current research presents findings that the increased expression and clinical correlation of the circular RNA hsa_circ_0001165 in esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC). Investigations centered on its function and underlying mechanisms reveal that hsa_circ_0001165 facilitates the advancement of ESCC through the modulation of the miR-381-3p/TNS3 pathway. Such discoveries could aid in pinpointing diagnostic biomarkers and advancing targeted treatment strategies for ESCC.
