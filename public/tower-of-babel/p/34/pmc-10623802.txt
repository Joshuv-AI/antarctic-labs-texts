Correction: Ethnobotanical study on medicinal plants used by Bulang people in Yunnan, China
Authors: Hao Zhou, Jiaqi Zhang, Brian S Kirbis, Zi Mula, Wei Zhang, Yinzhi Kuang, Qing Huang, Lun Yin
Published: 2023

Correction: Journal of Ethnobiology and Ethnomedicine (2023) 19:38 https://doi.org/10.1186/s13002-023-00609-0

Following publication of the original article, the authors flagged two formatting errors: In the body of Fig. 10, ‘plants’ had been misspelled; throughout the manuscript, certain scientific names had been put in italics while, according to accepted conventions in botanical nomenclature, they should not have been. The published article has since been updated to correct these errors. The authors thank you for reading this erratum and apologize for any inconvenience caused.
