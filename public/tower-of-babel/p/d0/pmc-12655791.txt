Message from Scientific Chairman
Published: 2025

Dear Dr. K.K. Sharma & Dr. Nitin Jain,

It is with profound joy and immense pride that I extend a warm welcome to all delegates, faculty members, and distinguished guests to the Pink City of Jaipur for the 27th National Conference on Pulmonary Diseases – NAPCON 2025 under the distinguished aegis of the Indian Chest Society (ICS). The prestigious conference will take place from 13th to 16th November 2025 at the renowned Birla Auditorium, near Statue Circle, Jaipur.

It is a matter of great pleasure to be Chairman of the Scientific Committee, NAPCON – 2025, Jaipur.

I express my sincere gratitude and thanks to office bearers & members of the Governing Council of ICS, members of the Scientific Committee & Organizing Team for having confidence in me and their cooperation in preparing the scientific programme.

On behalf of the entire Scientific Committee, I extend heartfelt appreciation to all contributors for their valuable time, effort, and intellectual inputs. I am confident that this publication will not only serve as an informative compendium but also as a treasured keepsake, rekindling fond memories of NAPCON 2025 each time it is revisited.

I congratulate the whole organizing team for bringing out souvenirs and publishing the selected high quality abstracts based on original research in the esteemed journal Lung India and wish a great success for NAPCON – 2025, Jaipur.

With regards,

Dr. S. K. Luhadia,

Chairman, Scientific Committee,

Professor Emeritus, Dept. of TB & Respiratory Medicine & Former Vice Chancellor,

Geetanjali Medical College & Geetanjali University, Udaipur, Rajasthan
