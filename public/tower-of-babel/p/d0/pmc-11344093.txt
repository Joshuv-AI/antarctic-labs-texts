Regional differences, dynamic evolution and trend prediction of green manufacturing development levels in China
Authors: Yaqian Lin, Ying Li
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Green manufacturing has become a necessary way to promote new industrialization and realize the high-quality development of China’s manufacturing industry. Based on the panel data of 30 provinces in China from 2012 to 2022, this paper constructs a comprehensive evaluation index system for the green manufacturing development level and introduces the TOPSIS- Gray correlation method to comprehensively measure the green manufacturing development level of China as a whole and the four major regions in the eastern, central, western, and northeastern parts of the country. The regional differences, distribution dynamics and evolutionary trends of China's green manufacturing development level are also explored with the help of the Gini coefficient, kernel density estimation and Markov chain methods. Research Findings: (1) The green manufacturing development level in China is on an upward trend, with an overall spatial distribution pattern of “East is superior and West is inferior”. (2) There are regional differences in the green manufacturing development level in China, and the differences are widening, with interregional differences being the main reason for this overall difference. (3) The country as a whole, the central region and the western region are polarized to varying degrees, with the rest of the country showing an improvement in polarization. (4) Without considering spatial factors, the development of green manufacturing in each province experiences “club convergence” in the short term, and it is difficult to realize rapid development. Considering spatial factors, China's green manufacturing development level is generally characterized by “elevated in proximity to high levels and suppressed in proximity to low levels”, and in the long run, it shows a distribution trend toward the concentration of high values. The findings of this study can provide new ideas for promoting synergistic efficient development of green manufacturing in China.

Introduction

The report to the 20th CPC National Congress stressed that the focus of economic development should be placed on the real economy. As the main body of the real economy, the manufacturing industry is likewise the basic plate for building an industrial modernization system and the admission ticket for building a great modern socialist country. In recent years, the manufacturing industry has entered a critical period of development in which it is transforming its mode of development and shifting the dynamics of growth. Deep green manufacturing has become the starting point for promoting new industrialization and realizing the high-quality development of the manufacturing industry. However, China’s traditional crude production model still accounts for a high proportion of manufacturing production, and the problems of resource and environmental constraints are becoming increasingly severe1. At the same time, the differences in resource endowment, geographic location, and economic conditions of different regions have led to the persistent problem of imbalance in the development of green manufacturing among regions, hindering coordinated regional development. Therefore, there is an urgent need for an in-depth exploration of the development path of green manufacturing in China. The State Council's white paper “China’s Green Development in a New Era”, issued in 2023, emphasized that China’s manufacturing industry should aim to develop in a way that strikes a balance between production and ecology, development and the environment. The scientific identification of China’s green manufacturing development painpoints, and the promotion of the overall coordinated development of green manufacturing in China is an important breakthrough in achieving the development of Chinese modernization.

Research on green manufacturing can be traced back to the 1990s, when the concept of green manufacturing was first specifically explained by SMEs2. Currently, green manufacturing is regarded as a modernized manufacturing model that comprehensively considers environmental impact and resource consumption; aims to achieve low consumption, low emissions, high efficiency and high benefits; and integrates the concept of green development throughout the whole life cycle of products3. The focus of research on green manufacturing continues to change, from the content orientation of green production, green supply chains, and green management4 to the goal orientation of seeking transformation and upgrading and green benefits5. The connotation of green manufacturing is constantly enriched, which shows the importance of green manufacturing for the whole industrial system. In addition, studies on different levels of green manufacturing cover micro enterprises6, manufacturing industries7, and meso regions such as provinces, municipalities, and urban agglomerations8, which greatly enriches the theory and practice of green manufacturing. Early scholars started with the connotation of green manufacturing and considered the whole life cycle of product design, production, consumption, and recycling within the evaluation system9. With the expansion of the scope of green manufacturing, elements such as energy, environment, innovation, and capital have emerged widely in evaluation studies10. After the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology of China issued the Guidelines for the Construction of Green Manufacturing Standard System in 2016, green factories, green parks, green products, and green supply chains are gradually being taken into consideration11. Regarding linear relationships, the focus of scholars in different fields varies, and the main influencing factors include industrial agglomeration, market structure, technological innovation, and green raw materials, all of which provide ideas for the development of green manufacturing from different perspectives12. In terms of index measurement tools, dynamic factor analysis13, structural equation modeling14, fuzzy rough sets15, and the TOPSIS method16 have been widely used, and some scholars have proposed a special “green manufacturing index” technique to measure the effectiveness of green manufacturing17. To more accurately measure the development differences and dynamic evolution characteristics of an indicator in different regions, statistical methods such as Theil’s index18, Moran’s I19, Gini coefficients and kernel density estimation20 have been widely used to analyze the magnitude of the differences and the trend of changes.

To summarize, scholars have studied the connotations of green manufacturing, index systems, influencing factors, measurement tools and other aspects at different levels, which provides a strong theoretical basis for this paper. However, most of the existing studies focus on the measurement and evaluation of the green manufacturing level, and some of them only study one side of the spatial distribution of green manufacturing, without further exploration of the assessment methods of the green manufacturing level and the regional differences and dynamic evolution trends of green manufacturing under the new development concept and the new industrialization era, and lack a multidimensional and multiangle comprehensive examination of the green manufacturing development level.

The possible novel contributions of this paper can be summarized as follows. First, this paper constructs a comprehensive evaluation index system of the green manufacturing development level by combining the new development stage and existing research and introduces the TOPSIS- Gray correlation method to systematically quantify it and focuses on the whole country and the four major regions of East, Central, West and Northeast China to obtain a correct perception of the green manufacturing development level in China under the new situation and to compensate for the shortcomings of the existing studies, which usually focus on a single region or city or multiple specific industries or enterprises. Second, this paper identifies the regional differences in China's green manufacturing development level and their sources through the Dagum Gini coefficient to grasp the development characteristics and coordinated promotion path of China's green manufacturing and to provide some empirical support for regional integrated development. Third, this paper explores the distributional dynamic evolution trend of China’s green manufacturing development level based on kernel density estimation. On this basis, the long-term evolution trend of green manufacturing development is predicted through traditional and spatial Markov chain methods to compensate for the shortcomings of existing studies in terms of the lack of research on the characteristics and trends of the evolution of China’s green manufacturing level and to provide an empirical basis for high-level regions to play a radiation-driven role. It is conducive for relevant authorities to tailor and accurately implement policies according to local conditions to promote the overall improvement of China’s green manufacturing development level.

Model building and indicator design

Model building

TOPSIS-Gray correlation method

The TOPSIS- Gray correlation method is a combined comprehensive evaluation method based on the TOPSIS method and the Gray correlation method. The evaluation of the TOPSIS method is based on the Euclidean distance between the evaluation cell and the positive and negative ideal solutions. Its requirements for samples are low and easy to apply, but the distance as a measure cannot reflect the dynamic changes in the data series. The evaluation basis of the Gray correlation method, on the other hand, is the geometric similarity between the two, which makes the study of the correlation relationship between the data more objective and can well reflect the changing dynamics of the system. These two methods are different in terms of decision-making principles, but they complement each other21. Therefore, this paper combines the advantages of the TOPSIS method, which can reflect the overall green manufacturing level of the region, and the gray correlation method, which is suitable for analyzing the index system constructed in this paper as well as the changing trend of the system, and introduces the TOPSIS-gray correlation method to establish an evaluation model of the green manufacturing index to conduct a more objective, comprehensive, scientific, and authoritative evaluation of the green manufacturing development level in China. The main steps are as follows:

(1) Determine the positive and negative ideal solutions.

The positive and negative indicator data are standardized separately, and then the entropy weight method is used to calculate the weight of each evaluation indicator. The entropy weight method, as an objective empowerment method, overcomes the human factor drawbacks of the subjective empowerment method; for the specific calculation formula, refer to Wu et al.21. Dagum Gini coefficient

Dagum (1997) noted that the Dagum Gini coefficient can overcome the difficulty of decomposing a cohort through the traditional Gini coefficient and Theil’s index. This method takes into account the distribution of subsamples and the crossoverlap between samples and can be used to effectively analyze the sources of regional differences. This paper draws on this study to measure the degree of spatial distributional differences in the level of green manufacturing development in China, with reference to Zhang et al.22 for the detailed calculation formula.

Indicator design

Green manufacturing is a manufacturing method that aims to reduce environmental pollution, reduce energy consumption, and realize the unity of economic, social, and environmental benefits, so it is also known as clean manufacturing and environmentally conscious manufacturing12. Although existing studies have established different evaluation index systems based on different perspectives, the new development stage has put forward new requirements for the development of green manufacturing, which advocates starting from the direction of cleaner production processes and efficient energy use and promoting green transformation and upgrading of the manufacturing industry with scientific and technological innovation to improve economic efficiency and people’s well-being25. Data sources and data preprocessing

This paper selects 30 provinces (including provinces, municipalities and autonomous regions, hereinafter collectively referred to as provinces) in China from 2012 to 2022 as the research object. Considering the availability and completeness of the data, Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan and Tibet were not included in the study. The raw data for the relevant indicators were obtained from the National Bureau of Statistics, China Statistical Yearbook, China Statistical Yearbook on Environment, China Industry Statistical Yearbook, and provincial statistical yearbooks and bulletins on the state of the ecology and environment. Meanwhile, considering the impact of missing data on the reliability of the research results, for some of the missing data, if the variable has a clear growth trend, the average annual growth rate method is adopted to fill in the missing values, and the remaining missing values are filled in by linear interpolation.

Analysis of the empirical results

Analysis of the green manufacturing development level measurement results

In this paper, based on the indicator system constructed above, the TOPSIS- Gray correlation method is used to measure the green manufacturing index. Figure 1 presents the average values of the green manufacturing index for the country as a whole and for the four major regions of the East, Central, West, and Northeast Regions from 2012 to 2022. As shown in Fig. 1, the overall level of China's green manufacturing development is not high but generally shows an upward trend, with the average value of the index growing from 0.3048 in 2012 to 0.3374 in 2022, with an average annual growth rate of 0.93%. In particular, the green manufacturing development level first showed a short-term increase between 2012 and 2014 and then a decline before showing steady growth after 2016. This may be because in 2016, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology formally proposed a series of policies, such as the Green Manufacturing Project Implementation Guidelines and Industrial Green Development Plan. Under the encouragement and promotion of green manufacturing by national policy, the green manufacturing level in China gradually developed in a good direction.Fig. 1Trends in the evolution of the green manufacturing level index from 2012 to 2022.

Trends in the evolution of the green manufacturing level index from 2012 to 2022.

On a regional scale, the average values of the green manufacturing index for the country as a whole and for the four major regions of the East, Central, West and Northeast Regions are 0.3190, 0.3663, 0.3159, 0.2862 and 0.2876, respectively. The average value in the eastern region was always much greater than the national average during the observation period, while that in the western and northeastern regions was lower than the national average, and that in the central region was only slightly greater than the national average in 2013 and 2019 and lower than the national average in the remaining years. Overall, the spatial distribution pattern of “East is superior and West is inferior” is shown. At the same time, comparing the green manufacturing development levels of different provinces, it is found that the top ten provinces in the overall ranking mainly include seven eastern provinces and three central provinces, while the bottom ten provinces also include one eastern province in addition to two northeastern provinces and seven western provinces. Interregional and intraregional green manufacturing development is characterized by a significant imbalance. This may be because the eastern region is the first region to concentrate green manufacturing development potential due to its outstanding achievements in terms of economic growth and green innovation compared to other regions, which is in line with the national strategy for the eastern region to take the lead in the development of the region. The central region's industrial model of “reliance on energy” has hindered the development of green manufacturing, but it has been catching up with the eastern region by virtue of its strategic position as a bridge between the east and the west. There are location disadvantages and a relative lack of resources, technology, talent and other factors of production in the western region, and energy-intensive industries are concentrated in the northeastern region. Although the level of green manufacturing development has improved in recent years under the country's strong support, the growth rate is still slow.

Analysis of regional differences and their sources at the green manufacturing development level

As seen from the previous section, China's green manufacturing development level exhibits certain spatial nonequilibrium characteristics. To further explore the size and sources of regional differences, this paper uses the Dagum Gini coefficient decomposition method to decompose the level of green manufacturing development in China, including intraregional differences, interregional differences, and sources of differences across the country and in the four major regions of the East, Central, West, and Northeast Regions.

Overall and intraregional differences in the green manufacturing level

As shown in Fig. 2, the Gini coefficient of China’s green manufacturing level increases from 0.0655 in 2012 to 0.0971 in 2022, with a mean value of 0.0849, indicating that the overall difference in China's green manufacturing development level is relatively small. Despite a small decline in the overall variance in 2016 and 2020, there was an overall fluctuating upward trend. This indicates that the differences in the green manufacturing level in China are widening, which poses a serious challenge for China to realize green transformation and the construction of an integrated and coordinated manufacturing powerhouse. The Gini coefficients of green manufacturing levels in different regions differed greatly, with the mean values of the differences in the Gini coefficients in the east, central, west and northeast being 0.1036, 0.0265, 0.0410 and 0.0130, respectively. The difference in the green manufacturing development level in the eastern region is much greater than that in other regions and shows a significant upward trend, with an average annual growth rate of 5.98%. The difference in the green manufacturing development level in the central region is nonstationary with repeated fluctuations, with the difference narrowing considerably in 2016, with a decrease of 28.59%, but showing an upward trend again after 2019. The Gini coefficient of the green manufacturing level in the western region, on the other hand, shows a significant downward trend, with an average annual decreasing rate of 6.20%. The Gini coefficient of the green manufacturing level in the northeast region is generally smaller than that in other regions, indicating that the green manufacturing development level gap within the northeastern provinces is relatively small, generally showing a “rising-falling—steady rise” trend, with an average annual growth rate of 0.24%.Fig. 2Overall and intraregional differences in China’s green manufacturing development level.

Overall and intraregional differences in China’s green manufacturing development level.

Differences and decomposition in the green manufacturing level

Figure 3 shows the results of Dagum Gini coefficient calculations of green manufacturing development levels in four major regions: East, Central, West and Northeast China. A comparison of the differences across regions reveals that the interregional Gini coefficients between the eastern region and the other three regions show a significant fluctuating upward trend. The mean values of the Gini coefficients for the east-central, east‒west and east‒northeast regions are 0.0941, 0.1277 and 0.1201, respectively, which are larger than the Gini coefficients of the other interregional regions, indicating that there is a large difference in the green manufacturing development level between the east and the other regions and that the difference shows a trend of continuous expansion. The interregional Gini coefficient between the central and northeastern regions slowly increased until 2019 and stabilized after 2019, while the difference between the central and western regions fluctuated little. The difference between the western and northeastern regions, on the other hand, shows a steady downward trend, with a mean Gini coefficient of 0.0330, which is smaller than the Gini coefficients of other interregional regions. Therefore, the gap between the level of green manufacturing in the eastern region and that in other regions is still the main challenge facing the development of green manufacturing and the challenge facing China’s strategy of realizing regional coordinated development, especially given the need to focus on the coordinated development of the eastern region and the western region. Specifically, the eastern and central regions have certain industrial-scale foundations and advantages. Compared with those in the western and northeastern regions, the green transformation of the manufacturing industry in these regions is faster and easier to pull away from other regions.Fig. 3Interregional differences in China’s green manufacturing development level.

Analysis of the dynamic evolution of the distribution at the green manufacturing development level

Although the Dagum Gini coefficient can reflect the differences and their sources in the green manufacturing development level in China, it is unable to describe the dynamic evolution process of the green manufacturing development level among regions. Therefore, this paper adopts the kernel density estimation method to examine the distribution dynamics and evolution process of the green manufacturing development level across the country and different regions.

Figure 5 depicts the distribution of the kernel density curves of China’s green manufacturing development level in 2012, 2014, 2016, 2018, 2020 and 2022 as a whole. From the distribution position, the center position and change interval of the kernel density curve moved to the right by a small margin in the rest of the years, except for a slight leftward shift in 2016, indicating that China's green manufacturing development level generally showed an upward trend. From the distribution pattern, the peak of the kernel density curve is decreasing, and the bandwidth is increasing, indicating that the interregional differences in the green manufacturing development level in China show a widening trend. In terms of distributional extensibility, the kernel density curve of the green manufacturing development level shows a distinct trend over time, indicating that the green manufacturing development level of some provinces is significantly greater than that of other provinces, and the regional differences are gradually expanding. For example, Jiangsu and other eastern coastal provinces, due to China's continuous promotion of environmental protection and high-quality development in the eastern coastal areas, have obvious advantages in the development of manufacturing clusters and the layout of strategic emerging industries. In terms of distributional polarization, the overall national kernel density profile evolves from a “double-peak” shape in 2012 to a weak “triple-peak” shape in 2022, with the main peaks always on the left side. This indicates that the multipolar development of green manufacturing in China is severe and characterized by low-value aggregation.Fig. 5Distribution of kernel density curves for China’s green manufacturing development level.

Distribution of kernel density curves for China’s green manufacturing development level.

According to the test results, the Q statistic is 120.27, which passes the 1% significance level test, so spatial factors have a significant impact on the dynamic evolution of China’s green manufacturing development.

Suggestions

Based on the above findings, this paper has the following policy implications:

(1) Increase vertical governance and promote regional linkages and complementarities.

The results of the study indicate that the overall level of China's green manufacturing development is not high, and the governance idea of “vertical development and horizontal synergy” should be adopted. On the one hand, all-out efforts are needed to promote the deep development of green manufacturing. The green manufacturing level evaluation index system is used to determine the intrinsic correlation between industrial growth and environmental carrying capacity changes. The crude development mode should be gradually eliminated under the existing industrial base and resource endowment, the structure of energy production and consumption should be adjusted, reasonable environmental regulations should be supported, and a perfect resource compensation mechanism should be established. This will promote the construction of a new pattern of development for the manufacturing industry that is intensive and efficient, has cleaner production, is green and low-carbon, and is rational in structure. On the other hand, the “linkage and complementarity” model promotes horizontal and mutually beneficial development. Given the large gap between the level of green manufacturing development in the eastern region and that in other regions, the tilting of infrastructure, industrial resources and production factors toward the western and northeastern regions should increase, and the spatial pattern of green manufacturing development should be optimized. Thus, the spatial spillover from the eastern region to the central and western regions will increase, and the development gap between the eastern region and other regions will narrow, especially the need to control the growth rate of the development gap between the eastern and western regions. Research has shown that the probability of upward transfer in medium- to low-level areas increases with the level of the domain area, so it is necessary to appropriately breakdown administrative barriers and spatial barriers to enhance cross-space exchanges between low-level areas of green manufacturing. At the same time, it should actively guide the establishment of a mechanism for cooperation between high-level and low-level regions in terms of capital, technology, talent and other aspects and promote a system of regional coordination and complementarity. The high-level region should also give full play to its core strengths and radiate the overall level of green manufacturing in China.

(2) Focus on the shortcomings of regional development and implement policies according to local conditions.

The research results show that green manufacturing development in different regions of China has different characteristics, so the coordinated development of green manufacturing in China should be realized according to local conditions by combining the resource endowments and industrial characteristics of different regions. The eastern region has obvious advantages in terms of economic development, technological innovation and human capital, but it also faces the dilemmas of ecological constraints, significant intraregional differences and the obvious phenomenon of right trailing. Therefore, the region should accelerate the upgrading of production modes and production factors, accelerate the elimination of highly polluting low-end technological equipment, and at the same time fully release the dividend effect of the scale and industrialization of the manufacturing industry, strengthen the exchange mechanism of the provinces in the region, and drive the development of backward provinces. The central region has a certain industrial scale foundation, and its green manufacturing level is close to the national overall level, but it has a concentration of heavily polluting industries, a lack of green manufacturing technology innovation capacity, and a significant imbalance in the development of the region's provinces. Therefore, by consolidating the current scale of the manufacturing industry, the region should explore new development modes, improve its industrial structure, enhance the management of pollutants, and establish a unique green technology innovation system, such as building a green research and development platform, to take advantage of its location in the east and west. The western region is rich in natural resources and has great potential for the development of green manufacturing, but there is a large gap between the green manufacturing level and that of the east. The country should fully explore its latecomer advantages and resource conditions with the support of national policy dividends and adjust its energy structure in favor of clean new energy. The ecological compensation mechanism should be improved based on a reasonable assessment of the carrying level of the manufacturing industry, with a focus on strengthening the pollution prevention and technological transformation of the equipment manufacturing and metallurgical industries and striving to enhance the green manufacturing level in the eastern region. The northeastern region, which is rich in mineral resources and has a good industrial base, should control high-energy-consuming industries; eliminate processes, technologies, equipment and products that waste resources and pollute the environment in a timely manner; accelerate the development of strategic emerging industries with high technological content and low energy consumption; and accelerate the growth rate of the development level of green manufacturing.

Limitations

There are several shortcomings in this study. First, considering the data availability and the purpose of the study, only 18 representative and generalized indicators were selected as the object of the study, and whether the evaluation system is applicable to other regions needs further reflection. Subsequent studies may try to select the corresponding indicators in conjunction with the four aspects indicated in the industrial green development plan or adjust the indicator system to adapt to the actual situation in the region. Second, given the availability of data, the sample range selected for this paper stops at 2022. With the publication of relevant statistical data, subsequent studies can incorporate more up-to-date data to supplement the study of the spatial and temporal evolution characteristics of the latest green manufacturing level in a timely manner, but the overall trend of the green manufacturing level is not susceptible to significant changes in the short term. Finally, this paper combines four major regions to analyze the overall level of green manufacturing, but provinces in the same economic circle are prone to forming a network among themselves to produce a stronger radiation effect. Thus, subsequent studies can consider both China's famous Yangtze River Delta and Pearl River Delta and other economic centers to analyze the regional differences in China's green manufacturing development level in more detail.
