Incorrect display of author names:

Correct display of author names:

Olga Kordonouri1*, Mareike Niemeyer1, Erika Marquardt1, Thekla von dem Berge1, Clemens Freiberg2, Ulrike Menzel3, Rudolf Oeverink4, Laura Galuschka1, Bärbel Aschemeier-Fuchs1 and Karin Lange5.

The original article has been corrected.
