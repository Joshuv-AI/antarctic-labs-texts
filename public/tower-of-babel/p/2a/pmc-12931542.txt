RETRACTION: Wealth-based inequality and dropout rate in the completion of the continuum of maternal healthcare in Ethiopia: a secondary data analysis of the Mini Demographic and Health Survey of 2019
Published: 2026

This article1 is retracted by the journal.

What happened after publication

Concerns were raised with BMJ about the accuracy of the authorship of the work including that the listed authors had not conducted the work.

The work is based on a thesis by Samrawit Berihun,2 completed at Haramaya University. Berihun submitted this work to a non-BMJ journal for publication.

Melsew Setegn Alie was invited to peer review Berihun’s article. After providing a reviewer’s report, Alie then made minor edits to Berihun’s manuscript and submitted this to BMJ Open without Berihun’s permission. The BMJ Open submission included a new author list. Berihun was unaware of the submission to BMJ Open, and was not listed as an author. The individuals who are listed as authors on the paper published in BMJ Open could not provide evidence that they contributed to the work.

Reasons for retraction

The article in BMJ Open is retracted because its authorship was misrepresented by the individuals who were listed as authors. The work was misappropriated by Melsew Setegn Alie and was published without the owner’s permission.

Further action

The journal will notify the institutions of the individuals who were listed as authors of the BMJ Open paper of this retraction. The journal will also notify the non-BMJ journal to which Berihun submitted their work.
