Erratum: Effect of Different Antitumor Regimens on Incidence and Severity of Corona Virus Disease 2019 Pneumonia in Lung Cancer Patients: A Single-center Retrospective Study

Lu WJ, Lv JW, Wang Q, et al.

Zhongguo Feiai Zazhi, 2023, 26(6): 429-438.

In the version of this article initially published, error appeared on page 429 for reason of the authors. The authors expect to make corrections and apologize for the error.
