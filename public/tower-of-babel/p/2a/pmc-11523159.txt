The Association between Socio-demographics and Post-Reconditioning Physical Fitness of Athletes Undergoing ACL Reconstruction
Authors: Siti S Suriadiredja, Andri MT Lubis, Jull Kurniarobbi, Taufan F Reyhan
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractIntroduction:Meniscus and anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) are the most common knee sports injuries. During the injury and its management, there may be a decrease in the level of physical fitness. Athletes need good physical fitness to be able to return to sports optimally. Objective. The aim of this study is to determine the association between socio-demographics and post-reconditioning physical fitness of athletes undergoing ACL reconstruction in Indonesia.Materials & Methods:This is a cross-sectional study with analytic descriptive approach using secondary data from one hospital for two years. This study include athletes with ACL injury who underwent ACL reconstruction followed by reconditioning three times a week in six months. At the end of the reconditioning, they were tested for their level of physical fitness.Results:Forty-six athletes were obtained. Socio-demographics show that most of the subjects were male (87%) aged over 25 years (58.7%), have basic-middle level of education (65.2%), amateur athletes (54.3%) who play contact sports (73.9%), got injured during training (73.9%) with a non-contact mechanism (89.1%), had a single knee injury (60.9%), and good exercise compliance (73.9%). Most of the subjects achieved good post-reconditioning physical fitness, including cardiorespiratory endurance (65.2%), muscle strength (73.9%), muscle endurance (80.4%), and flexibility (87%). Multivariate analysis shows that exercise compliance was significantly associated with muscle strength (AOR=25.1; 95% CI:1.812-348.761) and muscle endurance (AOR=12.8; 95% CI:1.695-97.193).Conclusion:The reconditioning program following ACL reconstruction in athletes provide a good level of physical fitness and the most contributing factor is the good exercise compliance.Keywords: anterior cruciate ligament, athlete, compliance, reconditioning, physical fitness, return to sport.
