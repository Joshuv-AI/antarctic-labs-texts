Author Correction: A generalizable approach for programming protease-responsive conformationally inhibited artificial transcriptional factors
Authors: Yinxia Liu, Lingyun Zhao, Jinshan Long, Zhenye Huang, Ying Long, Jianjun He, Jian-Hui Jiang
Published: 2026

Correction to: Nature Communications 10.1038/s41467-025-59828-6, published online 17 May 2025

In the version of this article initially published, due to a data transfer error, values for cell nos. 13–18 in the Source Data for Supplementary Fig. 12b were duplicate values for cell nos. 7–12. The Source Data and Supplementary Fig. 12b have been updated. Associated text in the fourth paragraph has been updated, where in the text now reading “In contrast, TEVp-induced cleavage triggered significant nuclear translocation of eGAL+/+, evidenced by a 2.1-fold increase in the nucleus-to-cytosol fluorescence ratio (0.38 ± 0.11 vs 0.18 ± 0.12; p < 0.0001, Supplementary Fig. 12b),” “2.1-fold” replaces the original “2.2-fold” and “0.38 ± 0.11” replaces the original “0.39 ± 0.11.” The changes have been made in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
