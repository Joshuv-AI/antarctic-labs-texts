Carter, K.C.; Ata, D.T.; Aruleba, R.T.; Hurdayal, R. Preclinical Testing of Vaccine Candidates in Animal Models. In System Vaccinology; Prajapati, V., Ed.; Academic Press: Cambridge, MA, USA, 2022; pp. 257–280.

Carter, K.C.; Ata, D.T.; Aruleba, R.T.; Hurdayal, R. Preclinical Testing of Vaccine Candidates in Animal Models. In System Vaccinology; Prajapati, V., Ed.; Academic Press: Cambridge, MA, USA, 2022; pp. 257–280.
