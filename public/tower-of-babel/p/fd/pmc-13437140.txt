Correction: Cortical Support in Unstable Intertrochanteric Fracture without Fixation of Posteromedial Fragment
Authors: Ki-Tae Park, Dong-Hoon Lee, Kyung-Hoi Koo, Jung-Wee Park, Young-Kyun Lee
Published: 2026

Clinics in Orthopedic Surgery 2026;18:413-419

https://doi.org/10.4055/cios25291

In the article entitled “Cortical Support in Unstable Intertrochanteric Fracture without Fixation of Posteromedial Fragment” published in Clinics in Orthopedic Surgery, the following corrections should be made.

1. The final paragraph of the Discussion should be revised as follows:

“Despite these limitations, we demonstrated favorable surgical results of unstable INT fractures with large PM fragments even without fixation of the PM fragment. In unstable INT fractures without fixation of the PM fragment, the incidence of fixation failure was 3.2% and significantly associated with inadequate reduction, especially when CS was absent. The surgeon should pay attention to achieving optimal CS with appropriate reduction status of the fracture, which is critical in determining the surgical outcome.”

2. The affiliation of co-first author Dong-Hoon Lee should be corrected from:

Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Chung-Ang University Gwangmyeong Hospital, Seoul, Korea

to:

Department of Orthopedic Surgery, Chung-Ang University Gwangmyeong Hospital, Gwangmyeong, Korea
