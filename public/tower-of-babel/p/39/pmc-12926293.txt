ASTRO’s Advances in Radiation Oncology Outstanding Reviewers for 2024
Authors: Rachel B Jimenez
Published: 2025

I am filled with gratitude as we honor some of the outstanding radiation oncologists, radiation physicists, radiation biologists, and statisticians that have graciously reviewed for our journal this year. Year-over-year, Advances in Radiation Oncology continues to grow, with increasing numbers of submissions and academic rigor. As it does, so many of you have generously donated your expertise and scientific acumen to further our mission, helping to refine and elevate the important research being generated by your colleagues across the specialty. This year’s outstanding reviewers represent a collective wisdom spanning continents, practice settings, and specialties and I am confident that this diversity is the key to ensuring the quality of our peer review process.

In 2024, I am particularly mindful of the clinical and administrative demands that continue to challenge many of us. Therefore, it is all the more meaningful to recognize those outstanding reviewers who have been particularly magnanimous with their time (Table 1). While we can acknowledge only a small subset of our excellent reviewers by name, please know how valuable each of your contributions are for sustaining and invigorating the mission of Advances. I will seek to find new ways to say thank you while continuing to recognize your valued service through the reviewer incentive program: Reviewer Involvement and Recognition - American Society for Radiation Oncology (ASTRO).
