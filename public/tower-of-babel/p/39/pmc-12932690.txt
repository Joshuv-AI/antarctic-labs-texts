The original version of this Article contained an error in Figures 5 and 7, where the title “Multi-frequency Electric Field Enhancement (Tolerance-based Z-positions)” was erroneously added during Production. The original Figures 5 and 7 and their accompanying legends appear below.

Normalized electric-field amplitude distributions |E| on the xy-plane at the representative frequencies 0.26 THz, 1.38 THz, and 2.47 THz. Left column (a, d, g): top-layer windmill pattern; middle column (b, e, h): intermediate micro-structure; right column (c, f, i): bottom-layer slotted disk.
