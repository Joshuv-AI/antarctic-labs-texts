Sensitivity Evaluation and Stimulation Optimization of Ultralow Permeability Sandstone Reservoir
Authors: Fan Yang
Published: 2023

ABSTRACT
AbstractAcidizing operation is normally applied in low permeability reservoirs, but if the acid system is not utilized properly, it will cause sensitive damage to the reservoir. As for the Sanjianfang Formation of the sandstone reservoir in the Shanshan oilfield, the problems existing in acidizing are especially serious. For these problems, the basic characteristics, sensitivity characteristics, and damage mechanisms were obtained by means of X-ray diffraction (XRD) analysis and scanning electron microscopy. Meanwhile, gas porosity and permeability were measured, and reservoir sensitivity characteristics were evaluated. The results showed that the reservoir rocks are mainly feldspar lithic sandstone and the clay minerals are mainly kaolinite and montmorillonite, which belong to the high clay mineral reservoir. It is an unsaturated reservoir with normal formation pressure that has low porosity and ultra-low permeability, heavy crude oil, and low porosity. The reservoir is highly stress sensitive, moderately weak velocity sensitive, alkali sensitive, water sensitive, salt sensitive, and weak acid sensitive. Based on these results, a multinanoscale stimulating liquid system was developed in the laboratory, and field application of this fluid system combined with mud acid was carried out. The combination system of multinanoscale highly efficient stimulation fluid and mud acid can meet the optimal dissolution rate of rock during the acidizing process and can increase the permeability of the targeted reservoir by 2.68 times, with a remarkable field application effect. The research results can provide a scientific basis for acidizing and stimulation of ultralow permeability sandstone reservoir.

Introduction

Low permeability reservoir is one of the most widely distributed oil and gas resources in China. In recent years, the proved oil reserves of low permeability and extra low permeability reservoirs account for more than 65%, among which the proved reserves of extra low permeability reservoirs account for more than half.1 Sanjianfang Formation sandstone reservoir in the Shanshan oilfield of the Turpan-Hami basin is a typical ultralow permeability reservoir.2 However, due to the characteristics of poor reservoir physical property, high clay mineral contents, and serious heterogeneity, it is very easy to cause reservoir sensitivity damage in the process of acidizing and stimulation for the interaction between foreign fluids and the reservoir. Reservoir sensitivity is related to the transformability of ultralow permeability sandstone reservoirs, and sensitivity evaluation is the basis for maintaining stable and high production of ultralow permeability sandstone reservoirs. Through the research of a large number of relevant literature, it is found that most domestic and foreign scholars focus on the study of reservoir sensitivity evaluation on medium and high permeability reservoirs and conventional low permeability reservoirs.3−8 There are relatively few studies on the sensitivity of ultralow permeability sandstone reservoirs. Compared with conventional reservoirs, ultralow permeability sandstone reservoirs are more seriously damaged by reservoir sensitivity. Previous laboratory tests and evaluations have been carried out on the damage mechanism of reservoir sensitivity and reservoir protection measures.9−16 However, there are few studies directly combined with the acidizing practice of ultralow permeability reservoir. If the acid system is not properly treated during the acidizing of the ultralow permeability reservoir, the effect of reservoir stimulation will be seriously affected.

The sandstone reservoir in the Shanshan oilfield in the Turpan-Hami basin is taken as the research object; the reservoir characteristics are obtained by using a series of core analysis methods, and the damage mechanism of the reservoir is analyzed by combining the reservoir characteristics and sensitivity test system. The lab research and field application of acidizing stimulation fluid are carried out in order to provide geological guarantee and scientific guidance for acidizing and drainage management in the Shanshan oilfield. These results can provide a reference for the stimulation of similar oilfields.

Shanshan oilfield is located in the middle of the western area of the Shanshan arc-shaped structural belt in Taibei Sag, Turpan-Hami basin. The sandstone reservoirs in the Shanshan oilfield are interlayered, and three sets of oil-bearing strata are produced from bottom to top in the Middle Jurassic strata, namely, Xishanyao Formation, Sanjianfang Formation, and Qiketai Formation, among which Sanjianfang Formation is the main oil-bearing strata of the reservoir. The thickness of the Sanjianfang Formation in the Middle Jurassic is 280–300 m, with a maximum effective thickness of 64 m, a minimum effective thickness of 12.4 m, an average effective thickness of 37 m, and an oil-bearing area of 26.9 km2, geological reserves of 3377.44 × 104 t, recoverable reserves of 878.13 × 104 t. The lithology is gray mudstone, silty mudstone, gray fine sandstone, and argillaceous siltstone, and the sedimentary system is mainly fan delta and braided river delta, which is in integrated contact with the Xishanyao Formation.

Basic Characteristics of Reservoir

Reservoir Lithology Characteristics

The rock type is mainly feldspar lithic sandstone (75%), followed by mixed sandstone (15%) and lithic sandstone (10%). In the detrital composition, the content of lithic is high (49.3%) and that of quartz is second (29.8%). Feldspar content was low (20.8%). The lithology of the reservoir is mainly medium-fine to medium-coarse sandstone, the grain sorting is medium-poor, and the roundness is mainly angularity and then subangularity to subroundness, indicating low structural maturity. The content of reservoir cement is medium, with an average content of 15.1%, mainly argillaceous cement (13.3%) and the second carbonate cement (1.8%). The distribution of carbonate cement is not common, and only local calcium enrichment can be found, with the highest content up to 18%.

The most sensitive minerals in the reservoir are clay minerals. The type, content, and occurrence of clay minerals determine the sensitivity of the reservoir. The characteristics of common clay minerals are shown in Table 1.17 The XRD results of sandstones in the Shanshan oilfield show that the content of quartz is 72.66%, calcite is 2.48%, albita is 6.52%, kaolinite is 15.98%, and montmorillonite is 2.36%. The composition of clay minerals in the oil reservoir is mainly kaolinite and montmorillonite, the absolute content of which is up to 18.34%, belonging to the high clay mineral reservoir.18 These two types of clay form the main body of sensitive minerals, and corresponding potential damage should be prevented in the process of oilfield exploitation.

It is found that the porosity and permeability of reservoir rock samples are mainly distributed in 12–14% and (5.7–6.2) × 10–3 μm2. Therefore, the Sanjianfang Formation in the Shanshan oilfield belongs to a low porosity and ultralow permeability reservoir.

Pore Structure Characteristics

The pore structure characteristics of the reservoir can reflect the geometry, size, distribution, and connectivity of pores and can be observed from microscopic results. The pore types of the sandstone reservoir in the Shanshan oilfield include primary pores, secondary pores, and microfractures, of which the secondary pores are the main ones, accounting for 60–80% of the reservoir space. They are located in the A substage of late diagenesis. As a whole, the rock samples are compact with a fine grain size. Albite (Ab) particles are coated with felsic particles, and a small number of dissolution pores are developed, as shown in Figure 1(a–d). Pore throats are mainly small pore throats. Pore throats smaller than 1 μm account for 44.9–72.4%, with an average value of 58.2%, while pore throats larger than 3 μm account for only 14.3%.

Reservoir Temperature and Pressure Characteristics

The internal environment of reservoir damage includes reservoir temperature, pressure, in situ stress, and natural driving energy. Based on the oil test analysis and logging interpretation results, it can be concluded that the geothermal gradient of the Sanjianfang Formation sandstone reservoir in the Shanshan oilfield is 2.5 °C/100 m, with a reservoir temperature of 86 °C, and belongs to a negative anomalous low-temperature reservoir. The original formation pressure is 28.84 MPa, the pressure coefficient is 0.97, and the saturation pressure is 18.22 MPa, which belong to the unsaturated reservoir with a normal formation pressure.

Reservoir Sensitivity Experiment and Mechanism Analysis

Reservoir sensitivity refers to the phenomenon that the physical or chemical reaction between the reservoir and the foreign fluid changes the original permeability of the reservoir. The damage degree of reservoir sensitivity is determined by the physical characteristics of the reservoir, pore structure, sensitive minerals, and properties of the foreign fluid. Combined with the study of the basic characteristics of the reservoir, the quantitative evaluation of reservoir sensitivity by the core flow test can accurately identify the potential damage factors of the reservoir and protect the productivity of the reservoir. The test samples were taken from six sandstone cores (cylindrical rock samples with a sampling depth of 2734–2739 m, diameter of 2.5 cm, and length of 5.0 cm) of Well L-12 in the West second zone of the Shanshan oilfield. The multifunctional displacement physical simulation system developed by Haian Petroleum Scientific Research Instrument Co., Ltd. was adopted. The test process is based on SY/T 5336–2006 Core Analysis Method19 and SY/T 5358–2010 “Experimental Evaluation Method of Reservoir Sensitive Flow.”20 To systematically evaluate reservoir sensitivity, six types of experiments, such as velocity sensitivity, water sensitivity, salt sensitivity, acid sensitivity, alkali sensitivity, and stress sensitivity, are carried out. The experimental equipment is shown in Figure 2, and the evaluation indexes of damage degree are shown in Table 4. The critical values of each experiment are the values of the corresponding previous points on the horizontal axis when the permeability change rate of the rock sample is greater than 20%. Velocity Sensitivity Evaluation

Velocity sensitivity refers to the phenomenon that the change of fluid flow velocity causes the migration of particles in the reservoir rock to block the tunnel, leading to a change in permeability. According to the relationship between the size of particles and pore-throat, the velocity sensitivity can be divided into decreasing permeability and increasing permeability: when the particle diameter is larger than or close to the pore-throat diameter, a single particle will block the pore-throat or a number of particles gather at the pore-throat to form bridge plugging, resulting in the decrease of reservoir permeability. This phenomenon occurs more often in the reservoir with a small pore-throat, which is the most common form of damage in the construction links of oilfield development. But, when the particle diameter is far less than the pore-throat diameter, the particles will be washed away under the action of fluid, resulting in an increase in reservoir permeability; this phenomenon is often born in weak cementation and through strong dissolution of the reservoir. The purpose of the evaluation is to determine the damage degree of reservoir velocity sensitivity and the critical flow rate to provide a basis for determining the reasonable production rate and fluid injection rate of the well. In this test, simulated formation water was used to place the fully saturated core into a gripper, slowly adjust the confining pressure to 2.0 MPa, and maintain the confining pressure greater than the core inlet pressure of 1.5–2 MPa throughout the test. For the next experiments, similar procedures are executed as follows: different flow velocities are adjusted, then the fluid pressure is tested after the flow velocity remains stable, and finally, the corresponding permeability is calculated. The test results are shown in Figure 3.

Evaluation results of velocity sensitivity.

The velocity sensitivity analysis of sandstone in the Shanshan oilfield shows that with the increase of displacement velocity, the permeability of the rock sample gradually decreases, the velocity sensitivity damage rate is 33.14%, the sensitivity damage degree is moderate and weak, and the critical velocity is 5.009 m3/d. Sanjianfang Formation of the Shanshan oilfield belongs to the low porosity, ultralow permeability, and small hole throat reservoirs. With the increase of injection fluid displacement velocity, the original free particles, the particles of nonexpansive clay shed by erosion, and the loosely consolidated particles of expansive clay after water contact in the reservoir are easy to accumulate in the small hole after transport, thus causing blockage and resulting in low-permeability velocity sensitivity. The expansive clay minerals in the reservoir are montmorillonite and the nonexpansive clay minerals are kaolinite. The contents of both are low, 2.36 and 15.98%, respectively, showing a moderate and weak velocity sensitivity phenomenon. Therefore, for the subsequent development of the Shanshan oilfield, proper production pressure difference should be maintained and the injection velocity should be controlled not to exceed the critical flow velocity so as to prevent the velocity-sensitive damage caused by the migration of reservoir particles.

Evaluation of Water Sensitivity

Water sensitivity refers to the phenomenon that when clay minerals in the reservoir encounter incompatible fluids, particles expand, fall off, and migrate to block pore throats, resulting in reduced permeability. The purpose of the evaluation is to understand the degree of damage to the reservoir caused by the low salinity of injected fluid. In this test fluid, simulated formation water, half of simulated formation water and distilled water were respectively used for core displacement. The corresponding permeability was calculated and the relationship between salinity and permeability ratio was plotted, as shown in Figure 4.

Results of water sensitivity evaluation.

The permeability of the rock sample decreased with the decrease in the test fluid salinity, and the water sensitivity damage rate was 48.49%, indicating that the water sensitivity damage degree was moderate and weak. The degree of water-sensitive damage is mainly affected by the clay minerals in the reservoir, pore structure, and injected fluid characteristics. The main clay minerals in the sandstone reservoir of the Shanshan oilfield are montmorillonite and kaolinite. The content of the expansive clay mineral montmorillonite is 2.36%, which expands when it is exposed to water, dispersions, and transports, occupying part of the pore space. The content of nonexpansive clay mineral kaolinite is 15.98%. After the encounter with water, some particles fall off and migrate to block pore throats. In addition, the poorer the porosity connectivity of the reservoir, the more serious the degree of water sensitivity damage, the development of relatively fine microfractures and dissolution pores in the reservoir, montmorillonite only needs a small expansion to cut off the entire porosity, resulting in poor porosity connectivity and permeability. Therefore, when acidizing a low permeability sandstone reservoir, an appropriate amount of antiswelling agent can be added to the acidizing stimulation fluid to prevent the expansion of clay minerals resulting in water sensitivity.

Assessment of Salinity Sensitivity

The damage mechanisms of salinity sensitivity and water sensitivity are similar. The purpose of the evaluation is to determine the salinity sensitivity damage degree and the critical salinity degree of the reservoir to provide a reasonable salinity for the working fluid used in field construction. In this test, fluid with different salinities was used to displace the core, and the corresponding permeability was calculated. The test results are shown in Figure 5.

Evaluation results of salt sensitivity.

As can be seen from Figure 5, with the decrease of the salinity of the test fluid, the permeability of the rock sample gradually decreases, the salt-sensitive damage rate is 37.30%, the critical salinity is 14015 mg/L, and the salt-sensitive damage degree of the sandstone reservoir in the Shanshan oilfield presents moderate and weak. In addition to the above water-sensitive factors, the principle of salt-sensitive damage is mainly affected by the salinity of injected fluid. In order to prevent the reservoir from being damaged by salt-sensitive damage, a working fluid with a salinity lower than the critical salinity of the reservoir should be avoided in the process of development.

Acid Sensitivity Evaluation

Acid stimulation is often used to increase well production in low permeability sandstone reservoirs. Acid sensitivity can occur if the acid stimulation fluid is not used properly. There are two forms of acid sensitivity damage: one is the direct reaction of an acid solution with acid-sensitive minerals to form precipitates or colloids. The other is that the acid destroys the original reservoir structure and releases particles to indirectly accelerate the sensitization damage. The purpose of the evaluation is to know whether there is acid sensitivity in the reservoir and the degree of acid sensitivity damage so as to facilitate the preparation and selection of acid stimulation fluid. In this test, 15% of HCL was used to simulate the injection process of acidic fluid into the reservoir. The acid rock reaction lasted for 1 h, and the permeability before and after acidizing treatment was measured, respectively. The test results are shown in Figure 6.

Evaluation results of acid sensitivity.

The acid sensitivity test results show that after acid injection into the sandstone reservoir in the Shanshan oilfield, the permeability of the rock sample fluctuates below the initial permeability, and the fluctuation range is small. On the whole, the acid sensitivity damage rate of the rock sample is 14.05%, indicating that the acid injection has low damage to the reservoir, which belongs to weak acid sensitivity. The damage degree of acid sensitivity is mainly affected by iron-bearing minerals, carbonate minerals, and indirect velocity sensitivity. Hydrochloric acid can precipitate with iron-bearing minerals to block pores and dissolve with carbonate minerals to improve reservoir physical properties. In addition, acidizing fluid can also indirectly cause velocity-sensitive damage in reservoirs with a high content of velocity-sensitive minerals. The contents of calcite mineral and carbonate cement in this reservoir are 2.48 and 1.8%, respectively, both of which are low and not widely distributed. However, the high content of kaolinite in the reservoir has potential rapid-sensitive damage. Therefore, it is considered that the indirect rapid-sensitive damage is greater than the dissolution action and the reservoir presents weak acid sensitivity. The comprehensive analysis shows that the low permeability sandstone reservoir in the Shanshan oilfield can be reformed by acidizing with appropriate acidizing stimulation fluid, optimization of acidizing fluid discharge parameters, and anticorrosion measures.

Alkali Sensitivity Evaluation

As shown in Figure 7, the ratio curve of pH value to permeability showed that the permeability of rock samples decreased gradually as the pH value of injected fluid increased from 7 to 13. The critical pH value was 11, and the alkali sensitivity damage rate was 33.65%. The alkali sensitivity degree of damage was moderate and weak. The alkaline-sensitive damage degree is mainly affected by quartz, feldspar, carbonate, and clay minerals in the reservoir. Alkaline working fluid with a high pH value can react with quartz, feldspar, and clay minerals to form precipitation, colloid, or solid particles. These minerals are deposited in the reservoir, resulting in permeability damage. In addition, under alkaline conditions, OH is adsorbed on the end face of clay crystals–, SiO32– and other inorganic anions make the end face negatively charged, which increase the interlayer repulsion of mineral crystals, trigger the hydration and dispersion of clay minerals. The contents of quartz, feldspar, and kaolinite in the sandstone reservoir in the Shanshan oilfield are 72.66, 6.52, and 15.98%, respectively. The reactivity of lye with kaolinite is high and that of fine quartz sand and feldspar is medium. At the same time, affected by the diagenetic environment, the alkali sensitivity of the reservoir is medium and weak on the whole. For the subsequent development of the reservoir, the pH value of the working fluid should be strictly controlled to be less than 11, in order to prevent the occurrence of alkali sensitivity and to reduce the reservoir permeability.

Stress Sensitivity Evaluation

Stress sensitivity refers to the phenomenon that the fluid in the reservoir is extracted and the original stress equilibrium state of the reservoir is broken, resulting in the redistribution of in situ stress and the recompaction of the reservoir, resulting in the decrease of permeability. The purpose of the evaluation is to determine the damage degree of reservoir stress sensitivity and the critical stress so that a reasonable production pressure difference can be determined in the field. The pressure boost experiment and pressure relief experiment were carried out successively in the way of confining the pressure change. The pressure boost experiment increased the initial net confining pressure from 2MPa to the maximum net confining pressure 23MPa, and the corresponding permeability was measured at each pressure point. Then, the pressure relief experiment was carried out, and the experiment was ended when the initial net confining pressure was reduced to 2 MPa. The permeability of each pressure point was measured 1 h later, and the test results are shown in Figure 8.

Evaluation results of stress sensitivity.

It was found that the permeability decreased gradually with the increase of net confining pressure during the pressure boost process, and the decreasing rate in the early stage was much higher than that in the late stage. The maximum permeability damage rate reached 91.97%, the damage degree was strongly stress sensitive, and the critical stress was 2 MPa. In the depressurization process, with the decrease of net confining pressure, the permeability increased slightly and could not recover to the original permeability value, and the irreversible permeability damage rate reached 48.59%. The damage degree of stress sensitivity is related to reservoir lithology, pore structure, and external pressure. Plastic minerals such as calcite and clay, brittle minerals such as quartz and feldspar in the sandstone reservoir in the Shanshan oilfield, microfractures, and severe intralayer heterogeneity are the main factors causing stress sensitivity. When the effective stress of the reservoir rock increases, plastic minerals squeeze and deform, brittle minerals crack and form particles, cracks close, and the whole reservoir compacts. When the effective stress is released, part of the deformation cannot be restored to the original state, resulting in a large irreversible permeability damage rate of the reservoir. In the development process of the low permeability sandstone reservoir, the production pressure difference and production speed should be controlled reasonably to avoid compaction and rapid depletion of the reservoir, which will lead to the irreversible deformation of the reservoir.

Results and Discussion

Acid system is the “blood” of sandstone acidification. The performance of the acid solution directly determines the success or failure of the whole acidification operation.21 At present, the research of acid systems mainly focuses on delaying the reaction rate of acid rock, and great achievements have been made.22−24 However, the dissolution rate of the rock mineral composition is also an important factor affecting the effect of acid pressing. The conductivity of acid erosion fractures is better when the easily dissolved substances account for 30–40% of rock mineral composition.25 The quartz content of the sandstone reservoir in the Shanshan oilfield is as high as 72.66%. It is difficult to reach the best dissolution rate of reservoir rock by using a conventional acid system. In addition, if the acid system is not used properly, it will cause serious sensitive damage to the reservoir. To solve this problem, based on the basic characteristics of the reservoir, combined with the evaluation of the reservoir sensitivity damage, a multiscale nanoscale highly efficient stimulation liquid system is developed. The stimulation fluid can be used in combination with the acid fluid to achieve a better transformation effect.

Research and Development of Highly Efficient Stimulation Fluid

Based on the sensitivity evaluation results of the sandstone reservoir in the Shanshan oilfield, the technical idea of acidizing stimulation fluid is formulated as follows: The compatibility of various additives is good; the salinity is higher than 14015 mg/L, it has good inhibition of clay expansion, has the function of slow speed and deep penetration, can relieve the formation blockage caused by drilling and cementing engineering, has certain dissolution ability of quartz, feldspar, etc., at the same time, the concentration is moderate, to prevent the destruction of rock skeleton, the corrosion of metal equipment, tubing, etc.

According to the above research ideas, a large number of additives such as antiswelling agents, wetting agents, and steering agents were screened to determine the composition of the stimulation fluid. The main components and effects are shown in Figure 9.

Field Application

In order to prevent the occurrence of stress sensitivity and velocity sensitivity in the sandstone reservoir in the Shanshan oilfield, simulation experiments such as injection rate, injection liquid amount, and pumping procedure are carried out in the laboratory. After determining reasonable production pressure difference, Well L-12 in West No. 2 zone of the Shanshan oilfield is selected for field test. The acidizing method used a dynamic pipe string for plugging removal. The formula of the acid solution system was 1.4% nano stimulation solution + 3.1% hydrochloric acid (31%) + 1.2% hydrofluoric acid (40%) + 1% corrosion inhibitor + 1% iron stabilizer. Before the stimulation test, the well’s liquid production was basically maintained at 0.5 m3/day, water content was 100%, and the daily oil production was 0.83 tons/day. Since it was put into operation in November 2019, the well’s liquid production was stable at 3.0 m3/day, which increased by about six times. The water content gradually decreased to 78%, and the highest daily oil production was 3.64 tons/day. Conclusions

(1)The sandstone reservoir of the Sanjianfang Formation in the Shanshan oilfield, Turpan-Hami basin, is dominated by feldspar lithic sandstone, and the clay minerals are mainly kaolinite and montmorillonite. The average porosity and permeability of the target layer are 13% and 5.78 × 10–3 μm2, belonging to a low porosity and ultralow permeability reservoir, the degree of heterogeneity in the reservoir layer is serious, and pore-throat is mainly small throat. Crude oil has the characteristics of “three low and two high,” which belongs to light crude oil. There are two types of formation water, and the reservoir is an unsaturated reservoir with normal formation pressure.
