Retraction Note: The effectiveness of routine physiotherapy with and without neuromobilization on pain and functional disability in patients with shoulder impingement syndrome; a randomized control clinical trial
Authors: Muhammad Akhtar, Hossein Karimi, Syed Amir Gilani, Ashfaq Ahmad, Asim Raza
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: BMC Musculoskelet Disord 21, 770 (2020)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12891-020-03787-0

The Editors have retracted this Article.

After publication, it was brought to the Editors’ attention that there were concerns regarding the original contribution of this work. An investigation conducted by the Editors confirmed that the Article contains material that substantially overlaps with a previous study from a partly overlapping group of authors [1], and a later study by the same group of authors [2]. In particular, all these studies appear to have been conducted with the same patient cohort, intervention, clinical trial number, location and have presented the same conclusions. The authors were unable to provide satisfactory explanations in response to these concerns. The Editors therefore consider this Article to be redundant.

Hossein Karimi, Syed Amir Gilani, and Asim Raza disagree with this retraction. Muhammad Akhtar has not responded to correspondence regarding this retraction. Ashfaq Ahmad did not explicitly state whether they agree or disagree with this retraction.
