Correction to “Lactobacillus rhamnosus LRa05 on glycemic control and gut microbiota in patients with type 2 diabetes”
Authors: Lin Geng, Ting-Ting Sun, Wen-Bo Xia, Ying Qin, Di Huo, Guang-Jin Qu
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractThis is an erratum to the published paper titled “Lactobacillus rhamnosus LRa05 on glycemic control and gut microbiota in patients with type 2 diabetes”. We have noticed that the names of the fourth author, Ms. Qin, and the corresponding author, Dr. Qu, were spelled incorrectly. The correct names should be Ying Qin and Guang-Jin Qu, respectively.Keywords: World Journal of Diabetes, Correction

Core Tip: This manuscript is to correct the authors’ name of “Lactobacillus rhamnosus LRa05 on glycemic control and gut microbiota in patients with type 2 diabetes. World J Diabetes 2025; 16(7): 106821 PMID: 40697596 DOI: 10.4239/wjd.v16.i7.106821”.

CORRECTION

We are submitting this correction regarding our recently published paper titled “Lactobacillus rhamnosus LRa05 on glycemic control and gut microbiota in patients with type 2 diabetes. World J Diabetes 2025; 16(7): 106821 PMID: 40697596 DOI: 10.4239/wjd.v16.i7.106821”.

In this article[1], we have noticed that the names of the fourth author, Ms. Qin, and the corresponding author, Dr. Qu, were spelled incorrectly. The correct names should be Ying Qin and Guang-Jin Qu, respectively. We sincerely apologize for not identifying this error earlier.
