Correction: Multiplex immunohistochemistry defines the tumor immune microenvironment and immunotherapeutic outcome in CLDN18.2-positive gastric cancer
Authors: Keren Jia, Yang Chen, Yu Sun, Yajie Hu, Lei Jiao, Jie Ma, Jiajia Yuan, Changsong Qi, Yanyan Li, Jifang Gong, Jing Gao, Xiaotian Zhang, Jian Li, Cheng Zhang, Lin Shen
Published: 2023

Correction: BMC Medicine 20, 223 (2022)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-022-02421-1

The original article [1] contains numerical errors in the following sentence regarding median irOS:

‘Additionally, CLDN18.2-positive group had shorter OS and irOS than CLDN18.2-negative group (median OS: 23.33 vs.36.6 months, P < 0.001; median irOS: 10.03 vs. 20.13 months, P = 0.044, respectively).’

The sentence should instead read as follows:

‘Additionally, CLDN18.2-positive group had shorter OS and irOS than CLDN18.2-negative group (median OS: 23.33 vs.36.6 months, P < 0.001; median irOS: 9.27 vs. 12.83 months, P = 0.044, respectively).’'
