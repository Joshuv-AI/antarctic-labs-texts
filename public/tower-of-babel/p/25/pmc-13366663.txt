In this article the wrong figure appeared as Fig. 7; the figure should have appeared as shown below. The original article has been corrected.

Differences in metabolic pathways between AKI and non-AKI groups. STAMP analysis of differential functional pathways based on the MetaCyc database ( p < 0.05). In the left panel, the horizontal bars represent the proportion of each functional pathway within each group. Longer bars indicate higher relative abundances. Pathways are ordered from bottom to top by increasing p-value (i.e., the most significant differences are shown at the top). The right panel displays the difference in mean proportions between the two groups (%). Positive values indicate a higher proportion in the treatment group compared to the control group, while negative values indicate the opposite. The error bars represent the 95% confidence interval for the difference
