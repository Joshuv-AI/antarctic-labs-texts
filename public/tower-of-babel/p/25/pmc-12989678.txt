Notice of Retraction
Authors: The Gut and Liver Editors
Published: 2026

The Editors of Gut and Liver hereby retract the following article:

“Efficacy and Safety of a Steroid-Free Immunosuppressive Regimen after Liver Transplantation for Hepatocellular Carcinoma.”

Qiang Wei, Xiao Xu, Chao Wang, Runzhou Zhuang, Li Zhuang, Lin Zhou, Haiyang Xie, Jian Wu, Min Zhang, Yan Shen, Weilin Wang, Shusen Zheng. Gut and Liver 2016;10(4):604-610. https://doi.org/10.5009/gnl15017

The journal contacted the authors requesting appropriate documentation verifying that the study had received appropriate ethical approval and that informed consent had been obtained, as applicable. The authors failed to respond, and the journal was therefore unable to verify compliance with appropriate ethical requirements. In accordance with the journal’s editorial policies and the guidelines of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE), this article has been retracted.
