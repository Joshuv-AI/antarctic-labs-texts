Editor’s Note on ‘A bi-functional siRNA construct induces RNA interference and also primes PCR amplification for its own quantification’
Published: 2025

This is an editor’s note regarding: Ming Jiang, Andrey A. Arzumanov, Michael J. Gait, Jo Milner, A bi-functional siRNA construct induces RNA interference and also primes PCR amplification for its own quantification, Nucleic Acids Research, Volume 33, Issue 18, 1 October 2005, Page e151, https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gni144

In September 2025, the Editors were alerted to concerns about the duplication of panels E7 siRNA/HCT116 and E7 crook siRNA/HCT116 in Figure 3D, despite different experimental conditions. These concerns had also been raised on PubPeer: https://pubpeer.com/publications/76B8509468F27512EEEFAED3E7BBC5#1.

The first author has since explained that the experiments depicted in Figure 3D were performed 20 years ago. The third co-author is no longer active in scientific research and, unfortunately, the last author passed away in July 2024. The first author is unable to retrieve the original raw data or consult with the other researchers who carried out the experiments.

The first author confirmed that the incorrect panel is E7 crook siRNA/HCT116 and the error occurred during image assembly. The siRNAs have no growth effect on the HCT116 line.

The issues described above do not affect the results, discussion and conclusions presented in the article. However, in the absence of original data, the Editors advise readers to examine Figure 3D with care.

Julian E. Sale and Barry L. Stoddard

Senior Executive Editors
