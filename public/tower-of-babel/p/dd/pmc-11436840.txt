Publisher Correction: Virulence factors, antibiotic susceptibility and sequence type distribution of hospital-associated Clostridioides difficile isolates in Israel, 2020–2022
Authors: Orna Schwartz, Hanan Rohana, Maya Azrad, Anna Shor, Nir Rainy, Yasmin Maor, Lior Nesher, Orli Sagi, Avi Peretz
Published: 2024

Correction to: Scientific Reports10.1038/s41598-024-71492-2, published online 04 September 2024

The original version of this Article contained errors in Affiliation 2 and 3, which were incorrectly given as ‘Affiliated With Sackler Faculty of Medicine, Tel Aviv University, Tel Aviv, Israel’ and ‘Affiliated With Azrieli Faculty of Medicine, Bar Ilan University, Safed, Israel’. The correct affiliations are listed below:

2Clinical Microbiology Laboratory, The Edith Wolfson Medical Center, Holon, Israel, affiliated with Sackler Faculty of Medicine, Tel Aviv University, Tel Aviv, Israel

3Clinical Microbiology Laboratory, Tzafon Medical Center, Poriya, Israel, affiliated with Azrieli Faculty of Medicine, Bar Ilan University, Safed, Israel

The original Article has been corrected.
