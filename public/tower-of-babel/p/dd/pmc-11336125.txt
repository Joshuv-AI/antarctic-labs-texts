Development of automatic arc sag measurement equipment for tensioned line tightening construction on overhead transmission lines
Authors: Baogang Chen, Wanci Zhang, Feng Wang, Qidi Guo, Qi Xiao, Junfeng Guan
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Due to the low degree of automation of the existing arc droop measurement device, the tension line digital twin system is not yet able to simulate the change of arc droop in the actual engineering situation in a high degree of real time. To this end, a kind of arc droop automatic measuring equipment applicable to the working condition of tension frame line tightening construction is developed. In terms of hardware, firstly, the STC microcontroller is used to control the motor to achieve free movement of the equipment on the wire. Secondly, the Beidou positioning module is used to collect the spatial coordinates of each point on the wire, thereby obtaining the spatial attitude of the wire and calculating the final sag. Finally, LoRa technology with long transmission distance and low power consumption is selected for wireless transmission of controlled data and collected data. The software system of the device is equipped with the motor's action logic, which cooperates with the hardware to respond to the control commands issued by the terminal. The developed arc droop measurement equipment has been successfully applied in the project, compared with the traditional arc droop observation construction method construction efficiency increased by 28%, the difference between the observation file arc droop and the design arc droop after erection of the line is 1.96%. Digital simulation and remote guidance of arc sag variations by the digital twin system for tension racking lines was realized while ensuring the engineering requirements.

Introduction

With the development of computer and Internet of Things technology, digital twin monitoring technology is gradually applied to tension frame construction of transmission lines1. However, due to the low degree of automation of the existing arc sag measurement device, the digital twin system for tension bridging lines cannot simulate the change of arc sag in real-time with high fidelity in the actual engineering situation, which makes it difficult to give feasible references for the arc sag adjustment in the construction process. Therefore, an automated arc sag measurement device is urgently needed to further improve the engineering adaptability of the digital twin system for tension wire2.

At present, domestic tight line construction usually adopts optical measuring instruments such as latitude and longitude instruments and total station instruments to manually observe the arc droop of the conductor3, but with the development of construction technology, some new arc droop measurement methods have also appeared. For example, reference4 uses a rope ruler to measure the height of the conductor to the ground, and then calculates the arc sag value of the conductor through the geometric relationship derivation, but the calculation accuracy of this method is not good, and the rope ruler is easy to be interfered with by the buildings, trees and other things during the process of placing. Reference5 will be installed in the insulator string and the conductor between the tension sensor, proposed to the conductor tension inference arc sag size of the measurement method, but due to the tension sensor needs to be installed in the insulator string and the conductor between the loading and unloading is more difficult, but affects the construction efficiency. With the development of image technology, video surveillance and its corresponding algorithms such as image recognition Harris6 and edge detection7 have also started to be applied in the field of arc droop measurement. However, the accuracy of the arc droop extracted by these methods depends on the imaging clarity, which is easily interfered by tree branches, fog and other objects in the field, resulting in poor arc droop measurement accuracy.

In order to further solve the problem of external interference, the method of extrapolating the arc sag by the coordinates of the spatial position of the conductor appears in the project. Reference8 was the first to present the theory related to this measurement method, and studied a real-time arc droop monitoring technique based on differential global positioning system (GPS). Based on this spatial measurement theory, the reference9 deploys multiple information acquisition devices on the conductor and simulates the arc droop variation of the conductor based on the positioning information of the devices. Reference10, on the other hand, uses satellite positioning and drone technology to obtain the coordinate information of special points of the conductor, and then extrapolates the arc sag of the conductor. Although these techniques can eliminate the influence of external object obstruction of vision, due to the collection device loading and unloading trouble, the drone manoeuvre is too cumbersome, and the construction efficiency instead of decreasing, resulting in the lack of widespread dissemination.

Therefore, in this paper, combined with the construction characteristics of wire tightening operation, the spatial arc-drop derivation formula based on any measurement point is derived11, and an arc-drop automatic measurement equipment is developed, which realises the automatic arc-drop measurement in the construction of tension wire tightening by automatically locating the spatial coordinates and calculating the arc-drop in real time through the satellite technology. This equipment has successfully completed the measurement of wire arc sag under foggy weather in the ± 800 kV Baihetan-Zhejiang project, which has the engineering prospect of wide promotion and application.

Tension wire tightening construction

Currently, the construction crews in tight-line construction still use traditional manual observation methods such as isometric and angular methods. The specific method is using binoculars, latitude and longitude devices such as repeated observation of the conductor, and then based on the observed distance, angle, through manual calculation to obtain the arc sag value. As a result, the measurement method is labor-intensive and not highly automated. At the same time, the traditional manual observation method requires a high observation environment, if the interference of tree branches, weather and other external factors are not excluded, it is difficult for the construction personnel to observe the conductor, resulting in the arc prolapse measurement cannot be carried out, which directly affects the construction efficiency and progress.

Functional requirements for automatic arc sag measuring equipment

In order to ensure that the automated measuring equipment can be competent in the line of sight disturbed arc dip measurement work, the first need to clarify the arc dip measurement method that can work properly in complex working conditions. Then, combined with the tight line construction process, to determine the functional requirements of the equipment.

In terms of the arc sag measurement method, the idea of deducing the arc sag through the spatial position of the conductor is adopted, that is, through the direct contact between the equipment and the conductor, and then the Beidou satellite differential positioning technology is adopted to obtain the spatial coordinates of the conductor at the point of the equipment, and then the maximum arc sag of the conductor is deduced through the mathematical relationship13. Obviously, the method does not use the traditional optical line-of-sight observation, thus avoiding the interference caused by tree branches, fog, and other objects in the data collection process.

Once the arc sag measurement method has been selected, the functional requirements of the equipment need to be determined. Firstly, the mathematical relationship shows that the maximum arc sag is derived from the spatial coordinates of the two suspension points and a point on the conductor, where the spatial coordinates of the suspension points can be obtained from the basic data of the line, and the spatial coordinates of the measurement point can be collected by the equipment. To do this, the equipment needs to be moved from the end of the tower to a suitable measurement location on the conductor, and the arc sag measurement equipment should be designed with a feature that allows it to be moved on the conductor to avoid cumbersome manual work. Secondly, as the equipment is always in close contact with the conductor in the arc sag measurement of the tight line construction and hangs in the air for a long time, in order to prevent safety accidents during the loading and unloading and use of the equipment, it should be ensured that the connection between the equipment and the conductor is safe and stable, and to ensure that the equipment is free to move and stagnate in the conductor. Finally, in order to facilitate construction, the equipment also needs to meet the characteristics of easy to carry, loading and unloading.

Design ideas for arc sag measurement equipment

The development of automatic arc prolapse measuring equipment can be divided into three parts: structural design, hardware design, and software programme design.

The structural design should be carried out in close conjunction with the construction characteristics of the tension racking line tightening. For this reason, the structural design of grounding pulley14 is inspired. In the construction of tension frame wire spreading, the grounding pulley is fixed by the elastic rope in front of the traction machine and tension machine, and the built-in pulley can be classified into two kinds of pulleys, upper and lower, according to the orientation classification. The conductor passes through the gap between the upper and lower pulleys of the grounded pulley and is displaced relative to the pulley. According to the functional requirements of the arc sag measuring equipment, its structural design can be obtained from the grounding slide's infrastructure design modification. In order to realise the function of moving the device on the wire, a motor that can drive the pulley to rotate is added.

The hardware design should be carried out in such a way as to satisfy all the functional requirements of arc sag measurement for tight line construction. Firstly, as the information of positioning data and control data needs to be interacted between the measuring equipment and the terminal in a timely manner, considering that the data transmission line is difficult to lap and easy to be damaged during the construction, wireless transmission is adopted for data transmission. Secondly, as the output signals of the built-in differential positioner of the device are messy, the output signals need to be sorted out so that the terminal equipment can analyse the received positioning data; furthermore, in order to allow the device to make the action of moving and stopping after receiving the control data, it is necessary to carry out the design of the driving circuit for the built-in motor of the device. Finally, the power supply circuits for the entire equipment need to be designed to safeguard the demand for electricity.

When carrying out software design, it should ensure the smooth realisation of each hardware function. Firstly, in order to ensure the smooth progress of information interaction, it is necessary to complete the terminal interface development and information interaction programme design. Secondly, it is necessary to optimise the processing of the output data of the positioning system through the program design, and set up the motor's action response logic according to the control signals, so as to drive the relevant hardware and complete the acquisition of data and information with the structural design.

External form

The main structure of the equipment consists of a combination of 4 metal pulleys and their supporting parts. Among them, the surfaces of the four pulleys are designed as grooves to fit the wires; the axes of the driving wheels A and B are connected to the driving motors, which bring the pulleys to rotate left and right when the device receives a movement command to realise the walking of the device on the wires; and the axes of the pressing wheels C and D are fixed to a special skeleton, and the main bracket of the skeleton, which is a giant screw, is in the sliding grooves and is driven by the wheel-pressing motors. The forward and reverse rotation of the motor can support the bracket to move up and down in the slide groove, driving the compression wheel C, D up and down displacement. Among them, the upward displacement of the compression wheel will form a squeeze on the wire together with the driving wheel, which will lead to equipment stuck on the wire; the downward displacement of the compression wheel, and the gap between the driving wheel will be larger than the diameter of the wire, the construction personnel can easily put the wire into or out of the equipment, which is conducive to the installation and unloading of the equipment.

Considering the effect of touch friction of the equipment on the subconductor, the equipment design width cannot exceed the subconductor spacing, which requires that the measurement equipment should be miniaturised. As the spacing between the subconductors is generally about 0.4 m15, on the basis that the width of the device outline structure is much less than 0.4 m, the height and length should be increased appropriately, so as to ensure that the device contains enough space to accommodate the necessary components, so the device overall dimensions of the overall outline is designed to be 300 × 160 × 430 mm. the structure is shown in Fig. 3.Figure 3Schematic diagram of the structure of the arc sag measurement equipment.

Material selection

As the measuring equipment is constantly shaking during travelling, this requires that the parts should be very tightly connected to each other and not fall apart during operation. Therefore, the skeleton, as an articulation of integrated rollers, housings, electronics, etc., should be drilled with screw holes to tightly fix the other components, and at the same time, it needs to have a certain degree of mechanical strength. The aluminium alloy with low density is chosen as the skeleton, which ensures that the overall weight of the equipment does not exceed the standard and makes it easy for the construction workers to carry the equipment; the strength is close to that of alloy steel, which can fully withstand the impact of the equipment shaking.

In addition, the tension wire construction site is located in outdoor, in order to avoid the damage of the external environment on the important devices of the equipment, should be used shell to isolate the main components of the equipment from the external environment. At the same time, the shell also needs to have wear-resistant, corrosion-resistant and other characteristics, to achieve the maximum degree of protection of the internal components of the equipment, so as to extend the service life of the equipment. Polyethylene material is chosen as the shell, which is able to resist the erosion of most acids and alkalis and prevent rainwater or other special liquids from penetrating into the equipment; it has excellent abrasion resistance and effectively reduces scratches on the equipment caused by tree branches, stones and other things.

Hardware design

In terms of hardware design, firstly, after receiving the control data from the terminal, the arc prolapse automatic measuring equipment needs to respond to the action; secondly, the spatial coordinates of the conductor, i.e. the positioning data, can only be obtained through the acquisition of the satellite positioning module in the equipment; lastly, the measuring equipment, as an intelligent electronic device, needs an external power supply to supply power to it. Therefore, the hardware design is divided into control module, positioning module and power supply module.

Control module

Considering that tension wire construction is mostly located in the field, the interaction of control data and positioning data between equipment and terminals is realised by means of Local Area Network (LAN) wireless transmission16. Wireless transmission characteristics.

When selecting wireless transmission methods, it should be ensured that the effective transmission distance of the wireless transmission method is greater than the communication distance between the sag measurement device and the handheld terminal. In tension stringing construction, the sag measuring equipment is suspended on the overhead line at a distance of 1–2 km from the ground handheld terminal17. However, the effective transmission distance of infrared, Bluetooth, WiFi, and ZigBee is all within 300 m, which is difficult to meet the needs of the overhead construction site. LoRa, as a long-distance wireless technology, has an effective transmission distance of up to 3 km and strong anti-interference and wireless penetration capabilities, making it suitable for complex construction environments. Therefore, LoRa technology was ultimately chosen for wireless data transmission of the device.

Considering that the positioning module, the control module and the power supply module are integrated on one motherboard, the electronic components should be as small as possible. The size of the WH-L101-L-P-H10 communication chip based on LoRa technology is 26.65 × 18.22 × 2.60 mm, which has a high degree of integration and a small size to meet the requirements for space size18. At the same time, the operating voltage is 1.8–3.6 V and the hibernation current is only 3.5uA, which has the advantage of extremely low power consumption. Therefore, WH-L101-L-P-H10 is selected for control data transmission.

After the terminal sends the control signal, the data received by the communication chip needs to be recognised and processed by the microcontroller. Considering the low power consumption, number of pins, and memory, the microcontroller is selected as STC15W4K48S4 with sleep wake-up. This model of microcontroller, as a new generation of 51 microcontrollers, operates 8–12 times faster than traditional 51 microcontrollers19. The STC microcontroller will receive the control data through the serial port, and then identify the action commands contained in the data, and then output the drive signal. These signals indirectly drive the motor via the transistors S8550 and S9031, thus achieving the purpose of controlling the measuring device with the terminal.

As the device is suspended from the conductor, its own gravity causes the drive wheel, which is above the conductor, to exert a downward pressure on the conductor, resulting in close contact between the drive wheel and the conductor at all times. Therefore, all that is required for the device to safely and reliably perform its actions on the conductor is to meticulously control and monitor in real time the level of contact between the compression sheave and the conductor. For this reason, the pressure sensor is installed at the pressure wheel. The pressure generated by the contact between the pressure wheel and the wire can be effectively fed back to the microcontroller through the pressure sensor, which in turn allows the microcontroller to grasp the contact condition between the pressure wheel and the wire in real time, and then adjust the output drive signal. Among other things, the voltage signal output from the pressure sensor must be converted to a digital signal by an analogue-to-digital converter before it can be processed and transmitted. Under the premise of ensuring the accuracy of data conversion, the highly integrated ADS1232 is selected for analogue-to-digital conversion20; in order to reduce power consumption, the STC12LE5616D microcontroller with sleep–wake-up is selected to process digital signals. Subsequently, the data signal containing the magnitude of the pressure value is fed back to the STC15W4K48S4 main control microcontroller through the serial port, so as to know the contact condition between the compression wheel and the wire in real time. In addition, after the feedback value reaches the pressure threshold set within the main control microcontroller, the microcontroller will adjust the motor action in time according to the operating status of the equipment to avoid excessive squeezing of the wire by the compression wheel, in order to prevent irreversible damage to the wire.

Positioning module

Under the control of the ground handheld terminal, the arc droop automatic measuring equipment arrives at the suitable measuring point of the conductor by skidding, and then acquires its own position-related information by using the Beidou positioning module carried by it21, and wirelessly transmits these data to the ground handheld terminal. Since the geographic information data generated by the BeiDou positioning module is a messy American Standard Code for Information Interchange (ASCII), which is not conducive to the intuitive view of the monitor, a microcontroller is required to simplify the output data. In order to save the occupied space, no additional microcontroller is added here, and the microcontroller STC15W4K48S4 is shared with the control module. The main control microcontroller selected in the control module is the STC15W4K48S4 microcontroller with many pins and large memory, which has a sufficient number of pins to realise the design of the hardware circuits related to the positioning module and the locating module, and at the same time, the memory is able to support the software programming of the two modules. It is connected to the BeiDou positioning module in the form of a serial port, receives and processes the geographic information from the positioning module in real time, and then transmits the optimised positioning data to the communication chip.

Due to the applicability of LoRa technology to tension rack construction, LoRa technology is still chosen for wireless transmission of equipment positioning data. Compared with the control data, the positioning data is more complicated, so the design should not only consider the power consumption and performance, but also pay attention to the cache capacity. In summary, the communication chip adopts E70-433NW30S, which supports low-power mode and has a cache capacity of up to 512 Byte, and can well complete the wireless transmission of positioning data22.

Power supply module

The design of the power supply module depends on the main power electronics of the other modules.

In the control module, under the consideration of power consumption, in order to allow the microcontroller to give full play to its performance, the STC microcontroller operating voltage are 3.3 V, while the wireless communication chip WH-L101-L-P-H10 operating voltage and the main control microcontroller to keep the same, also 3.3 V, to facilitate the transmission of data23. Since the resolution of the pressure sensor is proportional to the supply voltage of the analogue-to-digital converter, the analogue-to-digital converter ADS1232 uses a high level of 5 V suitable for the operation of the electronics in order to ensure the accuracy of the pressure measurement values.

In the positioning module, the communication chip E70-433NW30S supports 3.3–5.5 V power supply, and the best performance is guaranteed with 5 V power supply.In summary, the hardware of the device requires a total of two operating voltages, i.e., 3.3 V and 5 V. Since the external power supply is powered by a common and easy-to-carry 16.8 V lithium battery, it is necessary to choose a suitable voltage reduction chip to meet the design requirements.

Under the consideration of output voltage, power consumption and load current, NB680GD switching buck regulator and RT8272 buck converter are used as the buck chip23,24.NB680GD fixed output of 3.3 V is used for 3.3 V power supply of the system, and the error of the output voltage is not more than ± 4%, with a low quiescent current of 100uA, and the peak output current is up to 10A. The RT8272 buck converter has an adjustable output voltage section for system 5 V power supply with a low power consumption of 25uA standby current and an output current of up to 3A, which fully meets the load requirements as it only needs to power the pressure sensor analogue-to-digital converter and the communication chip in the positioning module.

In order to know the power status of the external power supply in real time, the external power supply is divided by a resistor and passes the voltage signal to the total control microcontroller STC15W4K48S4. The microcontroller calculates and processes the voltage signal in accordance with the voltage division ratio, which can restore the actual voltage of the external power supply, and then wirelessly transmit it to the ground data monitoring terminal through the communication chip to realise the real-time monitoring of the power supply.

Software design

Software programming of automatic arc sag measuring equipment

After completing the hardware circuit design of the device, it is necessary to drive the microcontroller through the software program to control the operation of the motor, so as to achieve the stable walking of the device on the wire; at the same time, it is also necessary to optimise the geographic information data output from the positioning module, so as to facilitate the terminal's subsequent processing of the positioning data calculation.

When designing the software programme for the movement response of the device, the first step is to identify the three state commands contained in the control data, namely, stop, move, and install, in order to achieve smooth movement of the device on the wire. Since the built-in motors run differently when the devices switch to each other in different states, the microcontroller needs to output drive signals according to the immediate state of the device and the target state in the control data. After completing the command recognition of the control data and comparing the immediate status of the device, the software programme should drive the corresponding motor for operation. Among them, the rotation of the drive motor corresponding to the drive wheel can realise the forward and backward walking of the equipment, and the rotation of the wheel pressure motor corresponding to the compression wheel can control the degree of extrusion of the roller on the wire. And, the wheel pressure motor action should be smartly stopped at the right time to avoid excessive squeezing of the wire by the rollers. For this reason, the software programme has wheel pressure thresholds for the moving and stopped states of the device, α and β, respectively, to compare the pressure values at the compression wheels in real time.

When the pressure value rises to the threshold value β, the wheel pressure motor stops acting immediately; when the pressure value rises or falls to the threshold value α, the software program will first analyse the transition of the device's action state and then control the wheel pressure motor to stop acting. The operation of the wheel pressure motor is shown in Fig. 7 for details of the procedure flow chart of the device in response to the control signal.Figure 7Flowchart of the procedure of the automatic arc sag measuring equipment responding to the control signal.

The wheel pressure threshold setting is determined by the material and thickness of the conductor used in the project, as well as the angle of inclination of the observation gear conductor. In order to obtain the threshold value, the device is manually simulated on the ground with wires in a suspended attitude before it is put into operation. Against the actual specific engineering, so that the simulation of the wire tilt degree to reach the maximum, to ensure that the equipment to move or stop are able to hold tight to the wire, at this time to record the two states of the pressure value at the tightening wheel, that is, the pressure threshold set in the software program.

In addition, for the optimised processing procedure design of positioning data, since the positioning data issued by the BeiDou positioning module is a message based on the NMEA-0183 protocol25, which contains time, latitude and longitude position, number of satellites and other related information, the specific meaning of the message needs to be checked against the table in order to know26. To simplify the positioning data, the microcontroller compiles and parses the messages and then sends the parsed geographic information to the ground data monitoring terminal by wireless transmission.

Software programming for terminal equipment

Arc prolapse automatic measuring equipment used in tension wire tightening construction, the need for terminal equipment and its real-time information interaction, so as to achieve the terminal control measuring equipment in the conductor walking, as well as the terminal on the conductor spatial coordinates and arc prolapse information interface display. Therefore, the terminal should also be carried out software programming, in order to facilitate the design of the display window, here the development environment visualisation of the VB language programming.

In order to remotely realise the control of the measuring equipment, the display interface of the terminal is equipped with options such as forward, backward, stop, etc. At the same time, it displays in real time the value of wheel pressure at the compression wheel, the power supply of the measuring equipment, and other basic information, so as to facilitate the operators to grasp the status of the connection between the measuring equipment and the conductor in time and to avoid the occurrence of safety accidents. In addition, pre-construction operators need to fill in the basic data of the construction line into the terminal and correct these engineering data according to the spatial location of the reference station. After receiving the information about the positioning data of the measuring point transmitted by the measuring equipment, the terminal will integrate all the known data to derive and display the instantaneous arc droop size of the conductor.

Application of arc sag measuring equipment

Before applying the equipment in engineering, we conducted tests on its performance such as error and stability in the laboratory. In multiple measurements, the error of the equipment is less than 2%, meeting the actual engineering requirements of no more than 2.5% deviation.

On 26 August 2022, the developed arc droop automatic measuring equipment was successfully applied in the construction of transmission line tension erection in the E9 bidding section of ± 800 kV Baihetan-Zhejiang project. As the construction site is located in a mountainous area with cloudy skies, the wires hanging in the sky are often covered with fog, causing greater interference with traditional arc-drop observation. Therefore, the pilot application put into the project can verify the functionality of the measurement equipment to some extent. In the project, the tension-resistant section of the #70-#73 tower in the area was selected as the test object, and experimental construction based on arc sag measurement automatic equipment was carried out on the left-phase subconductors of its line, of which the line of the #71-#72 tower is an observation file with a design arc sag of 33.261 m. In the case of automatic arc sag measurement equipment, excluding the time spent by construction personnel in loosening and tightening the wire to adjust the length of the conductor, a total of 25 min was spent from the establishment of the base station to the end of the arc sag correction of the conductor; in the case of latitude and longitude-based arc sag observation, the ideal reference value for the total measurement time was 30 min, and a total of 35 min was spent in addition to the time used for the installation. Conclusions

This paper considers the problems of traditional arc droop observation in the construction of tension wire tightening, combines with the characteristics of tightening construction, and designs a kind of arc droop measuring equipment with high degree of automation and not easy to be interfered by the external environment, which has the engineering prospect of widely popularising and applying.Equipment in ± 800 kV Baihetan—Zhejiang project E9 standard section successfully applied, the successful realization of foggy weather conductor arc prolapse measurement work, construction efficiency than the traditional arc prolapse observation construction method increased by 28%, the adjusted observation file arc prolapse with the design value of 1.96%, in line with the line acceptance of the engineering requirements.This equipment ensures the engineering design requirements while realising the digital simulation preview and remote guidance of the tension wire digital twin system on the change of arc droop during the construction, which further improves the dangerous controllability of the tension wire construction.
