A study on surface tension gradient flow of viscoelastic tetra hybrid nanofluid over a Riga plate with elastic deformation for improving thermal efficiency in industrial systems
Authors: Abdullah A. Faqihi, Safa Chlahbi, Saba Liaqat, Muyassar Norberdiyeva, Ali Akgül, Murad Khan Hassani
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
This work investigates the influence of heat source on the chemically reactive flow of Boger tetra hybrid nanofluid over a Riga plate using Marangoni convection and a Darcy–Forchheimer porous medium. The diffusion of tetra nanoparticles (\documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$$\mathrm{C}\mathrm{u}, \mathrm{A}\mathrm{g},\mathrm{M}\mathrm{g}\mathrm{o},$$\end{document} and \documentclass[12pt]{minimal} \usepackage{amsmath} \usepackage{wasysym} \usepackage{amsfonts} \usepackage{amssymb} \usepackage{amsbsy} \usepackage{mathrsfs} \usepackage{upgreek} \setlength{\oddsidemargin}{-69pt} \begin{document}$${\mathrm{TiO}}_{2}$$\end{document}) in sodium alginate produces the tetra hybrid nanofluid. The Marangoni convection effect has been observed to include flow, mass and heat transmission. In nuclear reactors and electronic devices, where accurate temperature control is critical, this sophisticated fluid dynamics framework holds a vital role in improving the effectiveness of cooling systems. The model guarantees higher reaction rates and product yields by aiding in the development of cutting-edge coating technologies and chemical reactor optimization. The modelled boundary layer conservation equations are transformed into non-linear coupled ODEs using an appropriate transformation. Once the model equation system has been condensed using the proper similarity variables, it is numerically resolved using the shooting method and the traditional bvp4c solver. The findings show that with greater solvent fraction parameter values, the use of Boger fluid improves the tetra hybrid nanofluid's velocity profile and reduces its thermal field.Graphical abstract

Introduction

Carlo Marangoni, an Italian physicist, conducted the first research on surface tension gradients in fluid mechanics. Concentration and temperature fluctuations influence the formation of ST (surface tension) at the air/ liquid–liquid interface. However, a fluid with a higher ST pulls more liquid from an area with a lower ST. Concentration and thermal gradients in convection geometry are involved in the Marangoni effect. It can be used practically in material science, paint, crystal growth, semiconductor processing, welding, atomic reactors, and fine art methods like ground colouring. Khan et al. [1] studied the bioconvective flow of a HNF (hybrid nanofluid) over different geometries with Marangoni convection. Boger fluids, also known as constant viscosity elastic liquids, are useful in rheological research due to specific features. The very dilute chemical constitution of these fluids makes them viscous even as surface, meaning they not have thin shear like other elastic fluids. A high-viscosity Newtonian fluid and a small quantity of polymer are mixed to create these liquids, that contain polyacrylamide and corn syrup among others. Using this procedure, researchers can easily tell the difference between shear and elastic impacts. By associating the flow rates of Newtonian liquids with BF (Boger fluids) with similar viscosities, investigators can get vital information on non-Newtonian performance. This enables them to figure out how flow rates are affected by elasticity. The special rheological properties of Boger fluid can be advantageous to several organizations. One such use is as a model fluid for studying rheology and fluid mechanics, as well as for comprehending the behavior of complex fluids. Biomedical researchers using BF, which also helps with the growth of medical implants and drug delivery systems, can model the viscosity of biological fluids. Additionally, they rummage-sale consumer products like cosmetics and food that need precise viscosity control. The BF rise the effectiveness of drilling operations in the gas and oil sector. They are also used in the growth of dampening systems ensuring the security and stability of manufacturing constructions. Conclusion

The investigation of a chemically reactive 3D Boger tetra hybrid nanofluid with Marangoni convection over a Riga plate demonstrates the mechanical engineering potential. It can be used to boost heat exchanger efficiency, enhance lubrication in micro- and nanoscale machinery, and optimise thermal management in sophisticated cooling systems. Additionally, the model helps design smart fluid systems for energy, automotive, and aerospace devices that need to precisely control mass and heat transport, leading to more reliable, high-performance, and efficient mechanical engineering solutions. The investigation yields the following results.The THNF velocity increased for both primary and secondary profiles, but as the MC parameter was increased, the solutal and thermal distribution decreased.The solutal and temperature profiles rise along with the velocity for primary and secondary profile boosts as the solvent fraction parameter value increases.Increasing the heat source parameter raises the THNF's thermal profile.When the Darcy–Forchheimer parameter is taken consideration, the Boger tetra hybrid nanofluid typically has a lower flow.Furthermore, increasing the reaction rate parameter has been shown to result in a decrease in the concentration profile.
