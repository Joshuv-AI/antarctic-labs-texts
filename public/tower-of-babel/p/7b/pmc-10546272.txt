Correction: Psychiatric Treatment Conducted via Telemedicine Versus In-Person Modality in Posttraumatic Stress Disorder, Mood Disorders, and Anxiety Disorders: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis
Authors: Ali Abbas Shaker, Stephen F Austin, Ole Jakob Storebø, Julie Perrine Schaug, Alaa Ayad, John Aasted Sørensen, Kristine Tarp, Henrik Bechmann, Erik Simonsen
Published: 2023

In “Psychiatric Treatment Conducted via Telemedicine Versus In-Person Modality in Posttraumatic Stress Disorder, Mood Disorders, and Anxiety Disorders: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis” (JMIR Ment Health 2023;10:e44790), the authors noted one error.

In Figures 5 and 6, the labels on the forest plot, “Favors telemedicine” and “Favors in-person,” were swapped. In the revised figures, these labels are presented correctly, as follows:

Forest plot (patient satisfaction).

Forest plot (working alliance).

The correction will appear in the online version of the paper on the JMIR Publications website on September 18, 2023 together with the publication of this correction notice. Because this was made after submission to PubMed, PubMed Central, and other full-text repositories, the corrected article has also been resubmitted to those repositories.
