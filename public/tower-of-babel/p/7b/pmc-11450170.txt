Author Correction: Mentha arvensis oil exhibits repellent acute toxic and antioxidant activities in Nauphoeta cinerea
Authors: Carlos Alonso Leite dos Santos, Amanda Maria Tavares Moreira, Bárbara Rayanne da Silva Teles, Jean Paul Kamdem, Abdullah F AlAsmari, Fawaz Alasmari, Momin Khan, Luiz Marivando Barros, Mohammad Ibrahim
Published: 2024

Correction to: Scientific Reports10.1038/s41598-024-72722-3, published online 16 September 2024

In the original version of this Article Carlos Alonso Leite dos Santos, Amanda Maria Tavares Moreira, Jean Paul Kamdem, Abdullah F. AlAsmari, Fawaz Alasmari, Momin Khan, Luiz Marivando Barros, Mohammad Ibrahim were incorrectly affiliated with ‘Laboratory of Entomology, Federal University of Cariri (UFCA), Crato, CE, Brazil’. Consequently, the affiliation 3 was removed from these authors.

The original Article has been corrected.
