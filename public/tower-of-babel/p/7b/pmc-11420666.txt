Correction to: Double-territory STEMI with cardiogenic shock in immune thrombocytopenic purpura with severe thrombocytopenia—a case report
Published: 2024

This is a correction to: Yerramareddy Vijayachandra, Antony Wilson, Jayalakshmi Sreeram, Aishwarya Mahesh Kumar, Double-territory STEMI with cardiogenic shock in immune thrombocytopenic purpura with severe thrombocytopenia—a case report, European Heart Journal - Case Reports, Volume 8, Issue 7, July 2024, ytae345, https://doi.org/10.1093/ehjcr/ytae345

The following changes have been made to the originally published manuscript.

The Lead author biography has been emended to read: “Dr. Y. Vijayachandra Reddy (Yerramareddy Vijayachandra), MD DM MRCP FACC FCSI FSCAI CCDS, is a Senior Consultant & Interventional Cardiologist at Apollo Main Hospitals, Chennai. He is well known in the field of Interventional Cardiology with extensive and versatile experience and range of professional work. He is a pioneer in transradial interventions and complex high-risk CHIP interventions including left-main, bifurcation and CTO PCIs. He is credited with pioneering work in acute MI interventions and his large body of PCI work in cardiogenic shock with unparalleled outcomes is well known. He is also versatile in coronary physiology and imaging, and facile with the gamut of PCI techniques involving Robotic PCI, Rotablation, IVL, Laser atherectomy etc.”

instead of:

“Dr Vijaychandra Reddy Y is a renowned Interventional Cardiologist at Apollo Hospitals Greams Road, Chennai, with more than 27 years of experience. He has completed his DNB in General Medicine and DM in Cardiology. This passionate doctor continued his endeavour and did fellowships in the American College of Cardiology and the Cardiological Society of India. Years of experience in his career have led him to achieve numerous honourable awards including TAMPA-LIMRA Professional Excellence Award for Cardiology (2016) and the Best Doctor Award for Cardiology at Dr MGR Medical University, Chennai. He is also a member of many professional medical associations around the world.”
