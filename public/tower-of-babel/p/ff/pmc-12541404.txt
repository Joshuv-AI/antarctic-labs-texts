Retraction for Safdar et al., Endurance exercise rescues progeroid aging and induces systemic mitochondrial rejuvenation in mtDNA mutator mice
Published: 2025

Retraction for “Endurance exercise rescues progeroid aging and induces systemic mitochondrial rejuvenation in mtDNA mutator mice,” by Adeel Safdar, Jacqueline M. Bourgeois, Daniel I. Ogborn, Jonathan P. Little, Bart P. Hettinga, Mahmood Akhtar, James E. Thompson, Simon Melov, Nicholas J. Mocellin, Gregory C. Kujoth, Tomas A. Prolla, and Mark A. Tarnopolsky, which was first published February 22, 2011; 10.1073/pnas.1019581108 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 108, 4135–4140).

The undersigned authors note: “An investigation conducted by McMaster University found that the loading control in Fig. 3B was duplicated in SI Appendix, Fig. S6A. We are retracting the paper to correct the scientific record. We have not been able to reach the first author.”
