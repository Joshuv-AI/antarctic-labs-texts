Optimal configuration of hydrogen storage capacity of hybrid microgrid considering peak regulation and frequency modulation requirements
Authors: Dan Yu, Yuhan Guo, Weijun Zhu
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractThis study proposes an innovative hydrogen storage capacity optimization configuration method that considers multiple demand factors, addressing the issue that traditional methods for optimizing hydrogen storage capacity in hybrid microgrids cannot simultaneously balance economic efficiency and grid connection quality. This method breaks through the traditional optimization framework and adopts a double-layer optimization model, combining the peak shaving operation cost of the hybrid microgrid with the interactive power deviation and power fluctuation penalty terms of the interconnection line during the frequency regulation stage, achieving dual optimization of economy and grid connection quality. Through precise mathematical modeling and efficient optimization algorithms, we have successfully obtained the minimum operating cost corresponding to the minimum power fluctuation, the minimum interconnection line interactive power deviation penalty term, and the optimal hydrogen storage capacity configuration scheme. Identify two typical days through cluster analysis. Option 1 (considering peak shaving, frequency regulation, and hydrogen electrolysis) has the lowest net load and a total operating cost of 3.0331 million yuan, which is better than Option 2 (3.0973 million yuan) and Option 3 (5.0398 million yuan). This plan effectively reduces wind and solar power waste, shortens the operating time of thermal power units, and demonstrates the rationality and economy of optimizing hydrogen storage capacity configuration.Keywords: Peak regulation and frequency modulation, Hybrid microgrid, Hydrogen storage capacity, Optimize configuration, Operating cost, Power volatility

Introduction

The widespread application of distributed power generation technology, especially wind and solar photovoltaic power generation, is a crucial step in achieving the global energy structure's transition to clean and low-carbon. It is a milestone in achieving the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals for climate change response and sustainable energy development. The application of energy storage systems in hybrid microgrids further promotes the sustainable development of renewable energy by reducing wind farm waste air volume and improving energy utilization efficiency. The application of this technology not only reduces energy waste, but also reduces environmental pollution, providing strong support for achieving global sustainable development goals.Ordinary electrochemical energy storage systems, such as lithium-ion batteries, lead-acid batteries, etc., are widely used in electric vehicles, smartphones, and other fields due to their high energy density, long cycle life, and fast charging and discharging capabilities. However, in hybrid microgrids, these systems may face some challenges, such as high costs, temperature sensitivity, and potential environmental risks.

In contrast, hydrogen storage systems provide a cleaner, more environmentally friendly, and sustainable way of energy storage. The hydrogen storage system stores hydrogen gas in specific materials such as metal hydrides, carbon nanotubes, etc. through chemical reactions, and releases hydrogen gas through reverse reactions when needed. In hybrid microgrids, hydrogen storage systems can not only balance energy supply and demand, but also serve as energy carriers to achieve complementarity and optimization among various renewable energy sources.

In the design and application of hydrogen storage systems, it is necessary to fully consider the basic parameters of hydrogen storage, such as hydrogen storage density, hydrogen storage capacity, hydrogen charging and discharging speed, cycle life, etc. These parameters not only affect the performance and economy of hydrogen storage systems, but also directly affect the stable operation and energy utilization efficiency of hybrid microgrids. Therefore, improving the reaction yield of hydrogen storage systems, i.e. improving the storage and release efficiency of hydrogen, is crucial for optimizing the performance of hybrid microgrids. Compared with traditional fossil fuel power generation methods, distributed power generation technology, especially wind and solar photovoltaic power generation, is gradually becoming the mainstream choice due to its efficient and environmentally friendly characteristics [1]. In order to better integrate these distributed energy resources, researchers have proposed the concept of a hybrid microgrid, which is a system that can effectively manage multiple energy sources. In hybrid microgrids, the proportion of wind energy has been increasing year by year, demonstrating its important position in the sustainable energy structure [2]. This trend not only helps to alleviate the energy crisis, but also provides new solutions for environmental protection.How to effectively use the energy storage system to reduce the abandoned air volume of wind farm [3], so as to make the energy storage device run reliably and environmentally, and further reduce the input cost, has become the focus of current research. In chemical energy storage, hydrogen production from electrolyzed water is a clean and reliable energy storage method [4]. Combined with the fluctuation and randomness of wind power, hydrogen production from electrolyzed water and fuel cell power generation in wind farms [5] can effectively reduce the abandoned wind volume, which has broad development and application prospects. When hydrogen storage stabilizes the fluctuation of wind farm output, its capacity configuration will directly affect the energy storage economy, and then affect the large-scale promotion of hydrogen storage in wind power field. Therefore, exploring the optimal configuration scheme for hydrogen energy storage capacity is crucial for achieving efficient energy management and utilization. This research is not only related to the optimization of energy systems, but also a key link in promoting the development of clean energy technology [6]. For example, Zhang et al. first established the mathematical model of hybrid microgrid. Environmental factors such as solar irradiance, photoelectric conversion efficiency and photovoltaic cell area are introduced into the output power model of hybrid microgrid, which enhances the accuracy of photovoltaic cell model. In addition, the typical battery life decay model is simplified by taking the state of charge as the main influencing factor, which reduces the complexity of the model and is convenient for the correction of energy storage capacity later. Secondly, the comprehensive configuration model of distributed photovoltaic power supply permeability and reliability and the normalized electricity cost model of photovoltaic power supply are proposed. The "light-storage-hydrogen" is comprehensively analyzed to enhance the reliability of system configuration; The cost of electricity is introduced to make the system obtain more accurate and intuitive configuration results. Thirdly, combines the speed of particle swarm optimization with the global search ability of artificial fish swarm algorithm to enhance the efficiency and depth of the optimization process, and enhances the optimization ability of the algorithm by using variable step size method to determine the final optimal configuration scheme of hydrogen storage capacity. The results show that this method can obtain strong economic benefits, and after the optimal configuration of hydrogen storage capacity, the hybrid microgrid is more in line with the actual project on the basis of ensuring the original reliability, configuration of hydrogen storage capacity of the hybrid microgrid with optical hydrogen storage integration [7]. The advantage of this method is that it introduces environmental factors, improves the accuracy of the photovoltaic cell model, simplifies the battery life decay model, and reduces model complexity. However, this method mainly focuses on the modeling and optimization of photovoltaic cells, with less consideration given to other types of renewable energy or energy storage devices. Trevisi and colleagues proposed an innovative hybrid energy storage microgrid capacity optimization configuration method, which comprehensively considers multiple objectives such as electricity cost and the increasingly mature microgrid technology. This method pays special attention to the coupling effect between electricity and hydrogen energy, and adopts an environmentally friendly and pollution-free energy storage system, which mainly consists of electrolytic cells, fuel cells, and hydrogen storage tanks. This energy storage system improves the utilization efficiency of photovoltaic power generation.In the specific implementation, researchers applied solve a specific instance, thus obtaining a capacity allocation scheme that performs best in terms of economy and power supply reliability. In addition, the study also used the RT-LAB hardware in the loop simulation platform to apply the configuration results to practical scenarios [8].The significant advantage of this method is that it not only considers the coupling effect of electricity and hydrogen energy, but also adopts technology, effectively improving the utilization rate. Through this innovative method, the overall performance of microgrid systems has been significantly improved, providing strong support for the sustainable development of future energy systems. However, this method is mainly aimed at hybrid energy storage microgrids coupled with electricity and hydrogen, and may not be applicable to other types of energy storage systems or energy configurations. Ravada et al. built a model of wind-hydrogen complementary power generation system by analyzing and improving the operation mode of electrolyzer. Taking the standard deviation of wind power and the minimum hydrogen storage capacity, the multi-objective genetic algorithm (NSGA-II) was used for optimization, and the optimal configuration scheme of hydrogen storage capacity of hybrid microgrid was obtained. Through case analysis, the effectiveness and rationality of this method are proved, and the optimized system can obtain the optimal storage capacity of hydrogen while ensuring the small fluctuation of wind power [9]. The advantage of this method is that it establishes a wind hydrogen complementary power generation system model and uses multi-objective genetic algorithm to obtain the optimal configuration scheme. However, this method mainly focuses on the complementarity between wind power and hydrogen energy, and may not be applicable to other renewable energy sources or wind energy conditions in different regions. Hajiaghasi and his collaborators constructed a hybrid microgrid hydrogen storage capacity optimization model, and solved the model using Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) and Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) algorithms to obtain the optimal configuration scheme for hydrogen storage capacity in the hybrid microgrid. The experimental results show that this method can significantly reduce the overall operating cost of hybrid microgrids.The research team adopted an innovative strategy that combines DFT and PSO algorithms to accurately calculate and optimize the size and configuration of hydrogen storage systems. DFT is used to analyze and handle power fluctuations in microgrids, while PSO is used to find the optimal solution in a multidimensional parameter space. Through this comprehensive approach, researchers can determine hydrogen storage capacity configurations that can meet energy needs while minimizing costs.This research achievement not only demonstrates how to optimize energy storage systems through advanced algorithms, but also provides an economically efficient solution for the sustainable operation of hybrid microgrids. By reducing operating costs, this method helps to promote the widespread application of microgrid technology, especially in renewable energy integration and energy management, paving the way for a more green and economical energy future [10]. The advantage of this method is that it establishes an optimization configuration model with the minimum total cost, and obtains the optimization configuration scheme. But it did not explicitly consider various constraints in actual operation, such as equipment capacity limitations, operating conditions, etc.

In previous studies, Zhang et al. improved the accuracy of photovoltaic cell models by introducing environmental factors and proposed a comprehensive configuration model for the permeability and reliability of distributed photovoltaic power sources. Although it improved the utilization efficiency of photovoltaic power sources, it mainly focused on the modeling and optimization of photovoltaic cells, with less consideration given to other renewable energy sources and energy storage devices. The capacity optimization configuration method proposed by Trevisi et al. for hybrid energy storage microgrids, although considering multiple objectives such as power cost and emphasizing the coupling effect of electricity and hydrogen energy, is mainly applicable to the coupling of hybrid energy storage microgrids with electricity and hydrogen, and may not be applicable to other types of energy storage systems or energy allocation. Ravada et al. focused on the complementarity between wind power and hydrogen energy, but this method has limitations in its applicability to wind energy conditions or other renewable energy sources in different regions.

This study aims to fill the gaps in previous work and propose an optimized hydrogen storage capacity configuration method for hybrid microgrids that considers peak shaving and frequency regulation requirements. This method takes into account both economic efficiency and grid connection quality. By establishing a double-layer optimization model, the minimum operating cost during the peak shaving stage is taken as the upper objective function, and the penalty terms for the minimum interactive power deviation and minimum power fluctuation rate of the typical daily frequency regulation stage are taken as the lower objective functions, the optimal configuration of hydrogen storage capacity is achieved. Compared with traditional optimization methods, this study not only improves the operational efficiency and economy of hybrid microgrids, but also ensures the stable operation and grid connection quality of the power grid.

The main innovation of this work lies in: firstly, proposing a dual layer optimization model that comprehensively considers economic efficiency and grid connection quality, filling the gap of previous research that only focused on a single objective; Secondly, the requirement of peak shaving and frequency regulation has been introduced to make the optimization results more in line with actual operational requirements; Thirdly, through iterative search, the optimal configuration scheme for hydrogen storage capacity corresponding to minimum operating cost, minimum interactive power deviation penalty term for interconnection lines, and minimum power fluctuation was obtained, providing strong support for the sustainable development of hybrid microgrids.

Optimal configuration

Analysis of time series operation of hybrid microgrids

The research on optimal configuration of hydrogen storage capacity oriented to meet the demand of hybrid microgrid needs to quantify the effect of its dual auxiliary services. The contribution of hydrogen storage to peak regulation and frequency modulation of hybrid microgrid is quantified by typical daily two-stage operation simulation method [[11], [12], [13]]. The specific idea is to get the typical daily curve by clustering the load, wind power and photovoltaic time series curves in the planning year; Then, based on the typical daily curve, two-stage operation simulation is carried out to obtain the operation cost of hybrid microgrid; The contribution of hydrogen storage can be obtained by calculating the difference between the operating costs before and after the optimal configuration. By adopting two-stage operation simulation, the optimized configuration of hydrogen storage capacity not only considers the demand, but also takes into account the operational limitations of the dual application scenarios of frequency regulation. This simulation method ensures that the hydrogen storage system can effectively cope with the challenge of frequency fluctuations while meeting the load balance, thereby improving the overall operational efficiency and stability of the hybrid microgrid. Through precise simulation and optimization, hydrogen storage systems can flexibly adapt to the dynamic needs of the power grid, providing key support for achieving more intelligent and reliable energy management [14].

The reason for clustering the time series curve is that if the annual time series load, wind power and photovoltaic power are optimized, the solution scale will be too large. Considering the obvious seasonality of power, the typical daily power curve can be obtained by K-means clustering method to represent the annual power curve. Clustering steps: firstly, random selection D initial cluster centroids (planning typical days); Secondly, 365 clustering units divided by the annual time series power curve in units of days are clustered; Finally, it is concluded that D typical planning days and the corresponding days of each typical day. Using the power data of typical days for subsequent optimization can greatly reduce the solution scale and shorten the solution time.

The typical daily peak shaving-frequency modulation operation simulation based on the clustering processing of annual time series data is divided into the peak shaving stage before the day and the intra-day frequency modulation stage with reference to the idea of "here-and-now & wait-and-see". The time scales of the two stages are different. The peak shaving is optimized in 1 h time scale, and the intra-day frequency modulation is optimized in 5 min time scale. Simulation of the on-off state, planned output and reserve capacity of flexible resources such as hydrogen storage equipment and units in the peak-shaving stage [15], transfer the reference power and adjustable capacity in the first stage to the power adjustment of the flexible resources, and the organic integration of peak-shaving and intra-day frequency modulation will get the operation simulation results of typical days. Two-layer model for optimal configuration

In the double-layer optimization model of hydrogen storage capacity planning-operation, the upper layer is the optimal configuration of hydrogen storage capacity, aiming at the minimum operating cost of hybrid microgrid in peak-shaving stage; The lower layer is a typical daily FM operation simulation problem, and the optimization objective is the minimum penalty term of tie-line interactive power deviation and the minimum power fluctuation. The upper model of the bi-level optimization problem generates the optimal configuration results of hydrogen storage capacity and transmits them to the lower model. The lower model generates the operation simulation results based on different typical days and transmits them back to the upper model. Then, the upper model calculates the annual total cost of planning and updates the optimal configuration results of hydrogen storage capacity, and realizes the optimal configuration of hydrogen storage capacity of hybrid microgrid through continuous interactive iteration.

The structure is shown in Fig. 2 below.

Structure diagram of the bilayer optimization model.

The upper and lower levels collaborate through continuous information transmission and feedback. The upper layer provides configuration guidance for hydrogen storage capacity, and the lower layer conducts daily frequency regulation operation simulation based on these guidance, and feeds back the simulation results to the upper layer, so that the upper layer can adjust the configuration of hydrogen storage capacity based on these results.

The hydrogen storage capacity configuration results of upper level optimization directly determine the available hydrogen storage resources in the lower level model. These resources will affect the decision-making of the lower level model in daily frequency modulation operation simulation, such as how to charge and discharge, thereby affecting the penalty terms for interactive power deviation and power fluctuation of the interconnection line. Therefore, the upper level optimization results have direct guiding significance for the lower level optimization.

Hydrogen storage devices can release stored hydrogen during peak electricity demand to generate electricity, or store excess electricity during low electricity demand. This ability helps to balance the supply and demand of the power grid and reduce the deviation of interactive power of interconnection lines. Hydrogen storage devices can respond to rapid changes in electricity demand because they can charge or discharge in a short period of time. This ability can reduce power fluctuations in hybrid microgrids, especially when the output of renewable energy sources such as wind and solar power fluctuates significantly. By using a double-layer optimization model, especially the optimization configuration and operation simulation of hydrogen storage equipment, these deviations and fluctuations can be significantly reduced.

According to Fig. 6, Option 1 considers the requirements of peak shaving and frequency regulation, and adds an electrolytic hydrogen production system to absorb wind and solar energy and reduce waste. In order to balance the interactive power deviation and power fluctuation of the interconnection line, the hydrogen storage system will release hydrogen during peak electricity demand to provide electricity, and store excess electricity during low electricity demand. Therefore, the configuration of hydrogen storage capacity will present a dynamic adjustment mode to adapt to the changes in electricity demand and new energy generation during different time periods. During peak hours, the release of hydrogen storage systems will increase, while during low periods, their charging capacity will increase. Overall, the hydrogen storage capacity configuration of Plan 1 will be more flexible to meet the peak shaving and frequency regulation needs of the power system.

Option 2 focuses on optimizing the configuration of hydrogen storage capacity under single peak shaving conditions, and also adds an electrolytic hydrogen production system to absorb wind and solar energy. In this plan, the main task of the hydrogen storage system is to balance the changes in electricity demand and new energy generation, especially during peak electricity demand periods. Therefore, the configuration of hydrogen storage capacity will focus on meeting the electricity demand during peak hours, ensuring the stable operation of the power system. During peak hours, the release of hydrogen storage systems will be relatively large, while during other periods, their charging and release will be relatively balanced.

Option 3 adopts a photovoltaic integrated hydrogen storage system for electrolytic hydrogen production, but does not participate in peak shaving and frequency regulation, only serving as a supplement to thermal power units. In this scheme, the main goal of the hydrogen storage system is to absorb wind and solar energy and reduce waste, while serving as a backup energy source for the power system. Therefore, the configuration of hydrogen storage capacity will be relatively fixed, mainly determined based on the power generation of the photovoltaic system. When there is sufficient sunlight during the day, the hydrogen storage system will mainly perform charging operations to absorb excess electricity; At night or when there is insufficient sunlight, it will release hydrogen gas to provide electricity. Since Scheme 3 does not participate in peak shaving and frequency regulation, the configuration of its hydrogen storage capacity will be relatively simple and there will be little change.

Conclusion

Using renewable energy such as wind power and photovoltaic to electrolyze water to produce hydrogen can penetrate zero carbon emission into the whole process of hydrogen production, storage and utilization, and can also solve the problem of abandoning wind and light in the development and utilization of wind power and photovoltaic to a great extent. Because electric energy can't be stored in large scale and long period as an energy carrier, hydrogen energy can effectively make up for the shortage of electric energy storage. Therefore, in the face of the randomness and intermittence of new energy output, through reasonable configuration of hydrogen capacity, the coupling of hydrogen energy and electric energy can support the integration of new energy with higher permeability, and improve the stability of hybrid microgrid operation. Therefore, the optimal configuration method of hydrogen storage capacity of hybrid microgrid considering the demand of peak shaving and frequency modulation is studied to ensure the stable operation of hybrid microgrid and improve the economy of hybrid microgrid.
