Publisher Correction: Association between hydrogen gas inhalation and cardiac output in an asphyxiated piglet model
Authors: Kosuke Sakamoto, Shinji Nakamura, Toui Tsuchiya, Tsutomu Mitsuie, Yasuhiro Nakao, Yinmon Htun, Takayuki Yokota, Kota Inoue, Eri Inoue, Takayuki Wakabayashi, Aya Morimoto, Kosuke Koyano, Yukihiko Konishi, Takashi Iwase, Taikou Horii, Takashi Kusaka
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-026-35115-2, published online 14 January 2026

The original version of this Article contained errors.

First, in Figure 1H, the y-axis was omitted.

Furthermore, in Figures 2 and 4, the shaded areas indicating the HI insult period were misaligned.

In addition, In Figure 5, the x-axis was incorrectly labelled “1” (hour) instead of the correct “3” (hours).

Finally, Author Eri Inoue was incorrectly affiliated with “Department of Cardiac surgery, Faculty of Medicine, Kagawa University, Kitagun, Kagawa, Japan”. The correct affiliation is “Department of Pediatrics, Faculty of Medicine, Kagawa University, Kitagun, Kagawa, Japan”.

The original Article has been corrected.
