In the original publication [1], there was a mistake in “Figure 13. The time-dependent root mean squared deviation (RMSD) curve of CpGUS and the conjugated bilirubin complexed system.” as published. Figure 13 was inadvertently duplicated from Figure 6. The corrected “Figure 13. The time-dependent root mean squared deviation (RMSD) curve of CpGUS and the conjugated bilirubin complexed system.” appears below. The authors state that the scientific conclusions are unaffected. This correction was approved by the Academic Editor. The original publication has also been updated.
