Published: 2024

Following publication of the original article [1], the affiliation details in the article was incorrectly given as ‘First Department of Cardiology, the affiliated Guangdong Second Provincial General Hospital, NO. 466, Xingang Middle Road, Haizhu District, Guangzhou City, China’ but should have been ‘First Department of Cardiology, The Affiliated Guangdong Second Provincial General Hospital of Jinan University, NO. 466, Xingang Middle Road, Haizhu District, Guangzhou City, China’.

The original article has been corrected.
