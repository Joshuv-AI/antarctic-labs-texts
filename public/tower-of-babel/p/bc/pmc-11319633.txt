Study on the ecological environment quality and its driving factors of the spatial transformation of production-living-ecological space in Baishan City
Authors: Jintao Huang, Yichen Zhang, Jiquan Zhang, Jiawei Qi, Peng Liu
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
AbstractIn order to strengthen the overall planning and coordination of urban construction and ecological space in Baishan City, we should formulate a scientific land type planning of Production-Living-Ecological Space (PLES). In this paper, land use dynamic attitude model, land use transfer matrix, land use function center of gravity transfer model, eco-environmental quality index(EEQI) and geographic detector model are used to discuss the spatio-temporal evolution of PLES in Baishan City from 2000 to 2020. Spatio-temporal evolution of EEQI and its influencing factors. The results show that: (1) During the study period, Baishan City showed the characteristics that the production space first increased and then decreased, the ecological space decreased, and the living space continued to increase. Among them, the ecological space is the dominant space of Baishan City, covering an area of more than 80%. From the perspective of the transformation of PLES, from 2000 to 2020, Baishan City is mainly characterized by the transformation of ecological space into production space and living space. In the second type of space, green ecological space, forestry ecological space and other ecological space have decreased, while other types of space have increased in varying degrees. (2) During the study period, the overall EEQI of Baishan City remained in good condition, and the ecological environment quality(EEQ) of the three periods were 0.6571, 0.6412 and 0.6562 respectively. The higher EEQI is distributed in Changbai County and Linjiang City, while the areas with lower EEQI are concentrated in the north-central part of Hunjiang City, the middle part of Jiangyuan District and the northwest of Fusong County. (3) According to the analysis of the influencing factors of EEQ, the influence of the factors of spatial differentiation of EEQI in Baishan City changed significantly from 2000 to 2020, and the average annual rainfall was the core factor affecting the spatial differentiation of EEQ in Baishan City, the second is the urbanization rate and the distance from the county government, and the interaction between the average annual rainfall and the distance from the county government has a strong influence on the spatial differentiation of EEQ in Baishan City. This study reveals the evolution of spatial types and EEQ of PLES in Baishan City, and provides a scientific reference for the effective management and utilization of land resources in Baishan City.Keywords: Production-living-ecological space, Spatio-temporal evolution, Ecological environmental quality, Geographic detector model, BaishanSubject terms: Ecology, Environmental impact

Introduction

As the space carrier of human production and social economic, that is, the core carrier of human-land coupling system1, land is an indispensable base for the healthy and sustainable development of ecological environment and social economy2,3. Land resources refer to the land that has been used by human beings and can be used by human beings in the foreseeable future, which contains both natural and social attributes5. Its rational allocation should proceed from many aspects of nature and society6.

The 18th National Congress report of the Communist Party of China put forward that "Adjust the spatial structure to promote intensive and efficient production space, livable and moderate living space, and beautiful ecological space"7 in the aspect of optimizing the national land space development pattern. It clarified for the first time the requirements and directions of the country for the development of PLES. The distribution of the types of PLES is a concrete demonstration of the harmonious coexistence between human and nature, and an important trigger for ecological environmental changes8. Baishan City is endowed with abundant natural resources and diverse ecosystems. However, during the process of economic development and urbanization, the transformation of the PLES has also exerted pressure on the ecological environment9. Therefore, studying the spatiotemporal pattern evolution of Baishan City's PLES and their influence on the EEQ holds great practical significance for comprehending the relationship between land use changes and the ecological environment, and providing scientific references for ecological protection and regional planning decisions.

The research on land use change and its driving mechanisms has become one of the research hotspots for contemporary scholars. Li Bo et al10 quantitatively identified the evolution characteristics of PLES function in Jiangjin District, Chongqing City in 2009 and 2017 through the ArcGIS spatial visualization method. Jiang Dong et al11systematically sorted out the research progress of the overall optimization of the PLES through literature research and summary induction, and found that most local governments have insufficient understanding of the scientific connotation of the PLES. Kong Dongyan et al12 used the transfer matrix to study the spatio-temporal evolution characteristics of the structural transformation of the PLES in China, and introduced the EEQI and the ecological contribution rate of land use transformation to analyze the ecological environment effect of the change of the PLES in China from 1990 to 2018. Dong Jianhong et al13 analyzed the EEQ of Gansu Province by using the partial least squares method. The research shows that ecological construction and technological progress are important reasons for the improvement trend of the EEQ, while population and economic development are the reasons for the deterioration trend of the EEQ. In conclusion, for specific regions, especially areas with unique geographical and ecological characteristics like Baishan City, detailed analyses of the relationship between the transformation of the PLES and the EEQ as well as the driving factors are still relatively rare, and the research methods have not been unified.

This study selected Baishan City as the research area, selected three periods of land use data, constructed a regional spatial model, and used dynamic degree, transfer matrix and other methods to explore in detail the characteristics of the three-life spatial transformation of Baishan City from 2000 to 2020 and its impact on the ecological environment. The geographic detector model was used to analyze the driving force of the spatial differentiation of the EEQ in Baishan City, and further reveal the interaction between the main influencing factors. For the study of the driving factors of EEQ, compared with traditional statistical analysis methods, the geographic detector can better analyze the relationship between land use changes and its influencing factors, and can also effectively reveal the interaction between driving factors14. This method is immune to the multicollinearity of independent variables15. This study will provide decision-making references for the sustainable development of Baishan City and other similar regions, and promote the region to move towards sustainable development.

Baishan City is located in the southeast of Jilin Province (Fig. 1), with a total area of 17,405 square kilometers, located in the core hinterland of Changbai Mountain, through the Yalu River and Songhua River. Because of the mountain name, because of the water and show, known as "Changbai Linhai", "three-dimensional resource treasure house" and other reputation, forest area accounts for more than 80% of the city.

Deepen the reform in the field of ecological environment, improve the modern eco-environmental monitoring system, promote the use of modern perceptual means and big data, improve the level of eco-environmental supervision, take the road of ecological civilization, and turn green water and green mountains into "Jinshan and Yinshan".

The accuracy of the classification results is tested (kappa coefficients are all greater than 0.88), and the land use status data of 2000, 2010 and 2020 are finally generated. On the basis of the land use classification system and drawing lessons from the relevant research results, the PLES classification system is divided. Referring to the existing research results, the EEQI of different secondary land types was established, and the weighting method was used to assign the EEQI of PLES classification (Table 1).

Geographic detector model

Geographic detector is a statistical model to study the driving factors of spatial differentiation of geographical elements29–31. In this paper, the factor detection module in the geographical detector is used to identify the influence degree of variables on the spatial differentiation of EEQI in Baishan City, and the interactive detection and analysis variables are used to enhance or weaken the interpretation degree of EEQ grade area32,33. In order to reveal the formation mechanism. The geographical detector proposed by Wang Jinfeng34 is a new spatial analysis method to detect spatial differentiation and reveal its driving factors, which is widely used in space research and other fields. This paper uses the factor detection in the geographic detector to explore the influencing factors of the EEQI, selects the driving factors from the natural environment and socio-economic aspects with reference to the relevant research results35,36, reclassifies the relevant data using the ArcGIS, and also uses the fishing net tool to resample the grid.

The production space is mainly agricultural production space, which is mainly distributed in the middle and southwest of Baishan City, where the topography is gentle, the soil is fertile, the climate is suitable, agriculture and animal husbandry are relatively developed, and the industrial and mining production space is increasing year by year. Transfer of gravity center of land types in PLES of Baishan City.

In this paper, the center of gravity transfer of three spatial land types in Baishan City from 2000 to 2020 is calculated by using the spatial statistical analysis of ArcGIS10.8, so as to analyze the center of gravity transfer direction of various spatial types. As can be seen from Fig. 6, in the past 20 years in Baishan City, the center of gravity of water ecological space, urban living space and rural living space moved most obviously, followed by other production space of agricultural production space. Forestry ecological space, green ecological space and industrial and mining production space center of gravity migration is slow. Affected by the relatively fixed location of natural resources and geographical environmental factors, the center of gravity of forestry ecological space, pasture ecological space and industrial and mining production space has changed little, and has been migrated in the south of Jiangyuan area. The agricultural production space gradually moved from the southeast of Jingyu County to the southern Jiangyuan area in 2000 and moved to the northwest of Jiangyuan District after 2010. This is due to the large population of Jiangyuan area and the development of agriculture. Other ecological spaces are mainly concentrated in Fusong County, which has been migrated in the northwest of Fusong County for 20 years. The migration trajectory of the center of gravity of urban domestic land and rural domestic land is similar, both from Jingyu County to Linjiang City in the south, which is affected by the development policy of urbanization and industrialization. The center of gravity of water ecological space moved greatly, moving from the east of Jiangyuan District to the southeast of Changbai County before 2010. after 2010, the center of gravity moved in the northwest direction of Changbai County, which was first strongly disturbed by human beings. land resources development and broken land restoration and other policy constraints.

Discussion

In order to comprehend the spatiotemporal evolution of land use and EEQ within the PLES of Baishan City, and to grasp the response relationship between EEQ and land use alterations, this study established the EEQI of Baishan City based on the land use data of the PLES of Baishan City from 2000 to 2020. Although the EEQ of Baishan City has maintained a relative equilibrium to a certain extent, the deterioration of the local ecological environment within the region should not be disregarded. As depicted in Fig. 3, from 2000 to the middle of 2010, production space and living space expanded, while ecological space was constricted41. This occurred because urbanization and industrialization have resulted in the continuous expansion of human engineering activities in the flow of urban and rural populations. Specifically, the mining of mining resources in Hunjiang District and the establishment of a district in Jiangyuan District have significantly expanded the scope of construction land in the urban area. A considerable amount of grassland and forest land have been utilized for agriculture and industry, thereby constricting the ecological space and causing its EEQI to decline41. As shown in Table 8, the primary cause for the decline in the EEQI is the transformation of forestry ecological space into agricultural production space.After 2010, when Baishan City was listed by the state as one of the first batch of resource-depleted cities, it began to adhere to the development strategy of "building the city based on ecology, strengthening the city through industry, playing the resource card, and taking the characteristic path"42. The reserves of natural resources have gradually diminished, and the original urban development mode relying on resources is no longer sustainable. Only by resolving the green transformation of pillar industries can Baishan City achieve green and sustainable development43. Meanwhile, attention has been paid to ecological governance, such as the restoration projects of abandoned mines and the implementation of policies like converting farmland back to forest, which also alleviated the EEQ. Combined with the above conclusions, the following suggestions are proposed for the protection and utilization of land space in Baishan City:

Production Space. The production space of Baishan City is mainly concentrated in the central part of Hunjiang District, the middle-eastern part of Jiangyuan District, and the northern part of Fusong County, mainly consisting of construction land and cultivated land. Due to the economic and social development demands of the central urban area of Baishan City, the large-scale expansion of construction land has led to the compression of ecological space. Therefore, scientific and reasonable planning and zoning should be carried out in this area to achieve sustainable and coordinated development of ecological environment protection and resource development expansion. How Baishan City achieves a healthy transformation of its economic structure is not only an urgent problem for the local government to solve but also a common issue faced by resource-exhausted cities in the same predicament44. To achieve an industrial development model in Baishan City that features harmonious coexistence between humans and nature, humans and society, and is environmentally friendly, the ideal green industry should be used to lead and drive regional economic development; through the design and formulation of laws, regulations, and policy guidelines, a broad cooperative foundation should be established for industrial structure transformation; through the reallocation of means of production and public resources, the restoration of ecological resources should also be well done. For example, land restoration and pollution control in mining areas should be carried out to strive to restore the ecological environment.

Conclusion

This paper takes Baishan City as an example, starting from the perspective of the dominant functions of land use in the PLES, and using methods such as the dynamic degree model, the land use transfer matrix, and the barycenter migration model of land types in the PLES to analyze the characteristics of regional land use transformation. Combining the ecological environment quality index method, it reveals the ecological environment effects of land use transformation in the PLES of Baishan City. The Geodetector model is also used to analyze the influencing factors of the EEQ of Baishan City from 2000 to 2020. The interaction of factors leads to the trade-off and game between the increase of living space and the compression of forest-green-water ecological space, generating an adaptive steady-state mechanism, thereby resulting in the constantly changing pattern of ecological environment quality. The main conclusions are:

During the study period, Baishan City exhibited the characteristics of an initial increase and then a decrease in production space, a decrease in ecological space, and a continuous increase in living space. Among them, ecological space was the dominant space in Baishan City, accounting for more than 80% of the area. From the perspective of the transformation of the PLES, from 2000 to 2020, Baishan City mainly manifested as the transformation of ecological space into production space and living space. The main reasons were resource development, an increase in population, and a growing demand for construction land and cultivated land.
