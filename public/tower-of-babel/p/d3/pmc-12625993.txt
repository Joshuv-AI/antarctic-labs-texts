Correction for Lu and Kang, Efficient acquisition of dual metastasis organotropism to bone and lung through stable spontaneous fusion between MDA-MB-231 variants
Published: 2025

Correction for “Efficient acquisition of dual metastasis organotropism to bone and lung through stable spontaneous fusion between MDA-MB-231 variants,” by Xin Lu and Yibin Kang, which published June 9, 2009; 10.1073/pnas.0900108106 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 106, 9385–9390).

The authors note that SI Appendix, Fig. S2D and Fig. S4C appeared incorrectly.

The authors explain that SI Appendix, Fig. S2D had two mouse images (lower panel, last two mice) that appeared duplicated. This unintended overlap occurred in figure assembly during manuscript preparation. The authors also note an error in the scale bar for SI Appendix, Fig. S4C. The corrected figures using original data are now presented in the online version of the SI Appendix file.

The SI Appendix has been corrected online.
