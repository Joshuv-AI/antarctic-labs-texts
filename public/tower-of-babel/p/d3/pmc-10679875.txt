Down-regulation of miR-486-5p expression inhibits the proliferation and invasion of human extravillous trophoblasts, and decreased peripheral plasma miR-486-5p level is associated with human early pregnancy loss
Authors: Yan Gu, Ning Song, Wen-Wen Gu, Linghan Chen, Xuan Zhang, Jian Wang
Published: 2023

Recurrent miscarriage (RM) is defined as two or more consecutive pregnancy losses before 24 weeks of human pregnancy [1]. The incidence of RM in pregnant women is 1%‒2% [2], and the RM causes in 50% of cases remain unknown [3]. Fetal preservation has been widely applied for the threatened miscarriage of RM patients in early pregnancy; however, the failure of fetal preservation should be treated by uterine clearance surgery, which leads to inevitable endometrial damage and ovarian function decline. Thus, identifying a reliable index of fetal preservation is important for the clinical arrangement of early pregnancy loss (EPL), including RM.

The establishment and maintenance of pregnancy are closely related to the activities of extravillous trophoblasts (EVTs), and dysfunction of EVTs may cause EPL and preeclampsia [ 4, 5] . MicroRNAs (miRNAs) participate in the regulation of maternal-fetal interactions during pregnancy [ 6, 7] , and peripheral miRNAs are potential biomarkers of EPL [8]. We previously observed significantly decreased villus miR-486-5p expression in RM patients [ 9, 10] , suggesting that miR-486-5p might be involved in the regulation of EVT activities.

To investigate the miR-486-5p expression pattern at the maternal-fetal interface of early pregnancy, we detected uterine miR-486-5p expression levels during the peri-implantation period in mice based on the 100% homology of mature miR-486-5p sequences in humans and mice. Adult female mice were mated with fertile males to achieve pregnancy (day 1=day of vaginal plug). Trypan blue dye solution (0.1% in saline (w/v), 0.1 ml per mouse; Sigma, St Louis, USA) was injected via the tail vein on day 5 to visualize the implantation sites. The entire uterine horn was collected from the pregnant mice on days 1 and 4 ( n=3 each time point). Uterine tissues at the implantation sites (IS) and non-implantation sites (NIS) were separately collected from pregnant mice on days 5, 6, 8 and 9 ( n=3 each time point). Total RNA was extracted from uterine tissues, and real-time PCR was performed using FastStart Universal SYBR Green Master Mix (Roche Diagnostics, Basel, Switzerland) according to the manufacturer’s description and analyzed on an ABI 7900 HT PCR system (Applied Biosystems, Bedford, USA). From day 5 to day 9 of pregnancy, the miR-486-5p expression level in the implantation sites was significantly increased compared to that in the non-implantation sites ( Supplementary Figure S1A). All experiments were carried out in accordance with standard laboratory animal care protocols that were approved by the Institutional Animal Care Committee of Shanghai Institute for Biomedical and Pharmaceutical Technologies (#2018-14).

Meanwhile, we observed dynamic changes in peripheral miR-486-5p levels in healthy pregnant women in the first trimester (8‒14 gestational weeks, n=16), early stage of the second trimester (15‒19 gestational weeks, n=15) and late stage of the second trimester (20‒27 gestational weeks, n=8). The clinical characteristics are shown in Supplementary Table S1, and it was found that the peripheral plasma miR-486-5p level increased from the first trimester to the early stage of the second trimester and then decreased during the late stage of the second trimester ( Supplementary Figure S1B).

Furthermore, the difference in peripheral miR-486-5p levels between RM patients (RM) ( n=15) and normal pregnant women (COL) ( n=21) was investigated. The clinical characteristics are shown in Supplementary Table S2. All experiments involving human were approved by the Medical Ethics Committees of The Second Hospital of Tianjin Medical University (KY2017K002 and KY2022036). Written consent has been obtained from each patient. Consistent with our previous data [10], we found that the peripheral plasma miR-486-5p level of RM patients was significantly lower than that of women with COL ( Figure 1A). ROC analysis results showed that, as a predictor of RM, the AUC value of miR-486-5p was 76.0% (95% confidence interval: 0.598‒0.923, P=0.009), the cut-off value was 0.9274, the predictive sensitivity was 76.2%, and the specificity was 73.3% ( Figure 1B). The abnormality of embryonic chromosomes is a major cause of EPL. Therefore, we divided peripheral blood samples collected from 22 EPL patients into two groups: EPL patients with normal embryonic chromosomes (EPL-N, n=9) or EPL patients with abnormal embryonic chromosomes (EPL-A, n=13). Peripheral blood samples from 20 well-matched normal pregnant women (COL) were collected as the control. The general clinical characteristics of all subjects are shown in Supplementary Table S3. Compared with that in the COL group, the peripheral plasma miR-486-5p level in the EPL-N group was significantly decreased ( Figure 1C). ROC analysis results showed that, as a predictor of EPL with normal embryonic chromosomes, the AUC value of miR-486-5p was 84.6% (95% confidence interval: 0.682‒1.000, P=0.007), the cut-off value was 0.8892, the sensitivity was 84.6%, and the specificity was 78.8% ( Figure 1D), indicating that the peripheral plasma miR-486-5p level presents a promising index for clinical fetal preservation therapy.

To investigate the effects of decreased miR-486-5p expression on EVTs, the miR-486-5p expression level in HTR8/SVneo cells was down- or up-regulated by transfection with its inhibitor or mimic, respectively. HTR8/SVneo cell invasion was quantified using a BD BioCoat™ Matrigel™ Invasion Chamber (Corning, New York, USA) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. A total of 1×10 5 cells were added to Matrigel-coated top chambers, and RPMI-1640 supplemented with 25% FBS was added to the lower chambers. After incubation at 37°C for 24 h, the invaded cells were fixed with 4% paraformaldehyde and stained with 0.1% crystal violet (Sangon Biotech, Co., Ltd, Shanghai, China), the number of cells was counted in 5 randomly selected fields, and the mean number of cells was calculated. This experiment was repeated at least 3 times. The results showed that the invasive ability of cells transfected with the miR-486-5p inhibitor was significantly reduced ( Figure 2A,B). HTR8/SVneo cells transfected with miR-486-5p inhibitor/mimic or NC were harvested after 24, 48, 72, and 96 h of transfection. The cells were collected and counted, and the results showed that the proliferation of miR-486-5p inhibitor-transfected cells was significantly decreased ( Figure 2C). HTR8/SVneo cells were harvested and centrifuged after transfection with miR-486-5p inhibitor/mimic or NC and then resuspended in phosphate-buffered saline (PBS) and stained for 10 min with fluorescein isothiocyanate (FITC)-annexin V and propidium iodide (PI). Apoptotic analysis was performed with a flow cytometer equipped with CellQuest software (BD Biosciences, Franklin Lakes, USA). The apoptotic activity of miR-486-5p inhibitor-transfected cells was significantly increased ( Figure 2D,E).

In conclusion, this study revealed that miR-486-5p expression is increased at the maternal-fetal interface in early pregnancy and that decreased peripheral plasma miR-486-5p level is associated with EPL with normal embryonic chromosomes, presenting a promising biomarker for the clinical arrangement of fetal preservation. Decreased miR-486-5p expression inhibits trophoblast invasion and proliferation but promoted apoptosis in EVTs, suggesting that decreased miR-486-5p expression in EVTs may result in EPL by interfering with EVT activities.
