OA20232. Substance use among university students in Greece: Emotional and behavioral responses
Authors: M Maniou, O Stefanopoulou, N Papachristou
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractIntroductionSubstance use among university students is a multifactorial issue, shaped by peer pressure, stress, social norms, and substance accessibility. Understanding usage patterns and cessation behavior is crucial for designing effective health interventions.AimThis study aimed to explore substance use behavior, emotional responses during use, cessation attempts, and reasons for stopping among university students in Greece. Method: A cross-sectional study was conducted with 174 students between April and May 2022. Data were collected using a structured questionnaire and analyzed with SPSS v.26.ResultsThe response rate was 68%, with 63.6% of participants identifying as female. Cannabis had been used at least once by 47.7% of respondents, and 11.6% reported use of other substances. Notably, 26% began using psychoactive substances during the COVID-19 pandemic. Reported emotional responses during use included pleasure (23.3%), calmness (26.7%), and discomfort (14.7%). An additional 5.2% felt nothing, and 28.3% did not respond. Regarding cessation, 37.6% had successfully stopped, while 6.8% reported failed attempts. Additionally, 12.8% felt no need to quit, and 7.7% stated they were not ready. The main reasons for cessation included resolving the initial cause (16.9%), experiencing psychological or behavioral changes (14.6%), financial difficulties (4.5%), fear of legal consequences (2.2%), and physical health concerns (4.5%). A large proportion (57.3%) did not respond.ConclusionThese findings highlight the importance of tailored prevention and cessation programs addressing the psychological, emotional, and behavioral factors influencing student substance use. Promoting awareness and early support could significantly reduce harm and improve student health outcomes.Key messages• University students report diverse emotional experiences during substance use, highlighting the need for tailored mental health and prevention strategies.• Over one-third successfully ceased substance use, while many cited emotional and behavioral changes as key reasons for quitting.TopicUniversity students, psychoactive substances, emotional experiences.
