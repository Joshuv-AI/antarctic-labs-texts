Correction: Comparison of TACE alone versus TACE combined with synchronous ablation for neuroendocrine neoplasms with liver metastases
Authors: Sun Huiyi, Wang Feihang, Yav Sothea, Zhao Danyang, Huo Zihao, Shuai Junqi, Yan Zhiping, Chen Yi, Liu Liang, Wang Wenquan, Ji Yuan, Liu Lingxiao
Published: 2025

Correction to: Journal of Cancer Research and Clinical Oncology (2025) 151:227 10.1007/s00432-025-06274-y

In Table 1 of this article:

The data in the column 1 header was incorrectly stated as ‘Characterisitics’ and should have been ‘Characteristics’.The data in the third row of the first column was incorrectly stated as ‘/不明 Primary tumor leisions’ and should have been ‘/Uncertain primary tumor lesions’.

The data in the fourth row of the first column was incorrectly stated as ‘Primory tumor section (yes/no)’ and should have been ‘Primary tumor resected (yes/no)’.

The data in the ninth row of the second column was incorrectly stated as ‘42.16 ± 74.22’ and should have been ‘42.16 ± 4.22’.

In Table 2 of this article, the data in the Column 1 header was incorrectly stated as ‘因素’ and should have been ‘Factor’.

In the sentence beginning ‘For patients with liver metastases…’ in this article, the text ‘transarterial embolization (TAE), (transarterial chemoembolization, (Transarterial chemoembolization,). TACE)’ should have read ‘transarterial chemoembolization (TACE)’.

In the sentence beginning ‘Before surgery, the patient had…’ in this article, the term ‘ECGO 0–1’ should have read ‘ECOG 0–1’.

Throughout the article the authors used the ‘G1G2’ to denote the combined group of World Health Organization (WHO) grade 1 and grade 2 tumors; this should read ‘G1&2’.

For completeness and transparency, the old incorrect versions and the corrected versions of Tables 1 and 2 are displayed below.
