Following the publication of the original article [1], the authors wish to correct errors in the original article [1]. Inadvertent duplications occurred in Figs. 4D and 9A during figure preparation. The corrections do not affect the article’s conclusions.

Correction to Fig. 9A:

In the original published Fig. 9A, the Western blot band for GSK3β in the “MCF-10A” lane was an inadvertent duplication of the band for Akt in the same lane. The correct band for GSK3β has been restored from the original blot. The densitometry values have been re-verified using the corrected band and are confirmed to be accurate.
