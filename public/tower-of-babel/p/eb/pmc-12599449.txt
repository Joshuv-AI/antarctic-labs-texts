Editorial Farewell: The Start of a New Chapter for the Korean Journal of Neurotrauma
Authors: Je Hoon Jeong
Published: 2025

Serving as Editor-in-Chief of the Korean Journal of Neurotrauma (KJNT) has been one of the most challenging rewarding chapters of my life. I am deeply grateful for the unwavering support of my esteemed seniors, colleagues, and junior members of KJNT. Following in the footsteps of my distinguished predecessors, I endeavored to further the growth of our journal. However, I remain mindful that there are areas where my efforts fell short.

Since June 2021, over the course of four years, I have been most profoundly grateful for the remarkable dedication of my colleagues who, even in the midst of the COVID-19 pandemic and domestic healthcare crisis, collaborated to publish 10 Special Issues. Working alongside them to bring each issue to completion was more than an editorial task—it was a process of infusing KJNT with their passion and helping shape its future.

Today, I am privileged to share a moment of great joy: the KJNT has been officially indexed in the Emerging Sources Citation Index (ESCI). This achievement is the culmination of the efforts of our contributing authors, editorial board members, reviewers, and readers. I hope this milestone will enhance the global impact of KJNT and open new avenues for scholarly communication with the international neurotraumatology community.

Finally, I would like to express my heartfelt appreciation to my successor, Professor Seok-Hyung Kang, and our associate editors, Professors Hyuk Jin Oh, Byung-Ju Lee, Tae-Seok Jeong, and Eun-Jin Ha, for their leadership and dedication. I am also deeply grateful to our reviewers, whose rigorous evaluations helped ensure the journal’s academic excellence. Furthermore, I extend my sincere gratitude to all who have shared this journey with me. This moment marks the beginning of a new chapter in the history of KJNT. Our path forward remains unbroken, and I am confident that KJNT will continue to grow and thrive.
