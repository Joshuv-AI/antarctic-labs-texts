Correction to: Isolation and characterization of Toxoplasma gondii isolates from human congenital toxoplasmosis cases reveal a new virulent genotype in São Paulo, Brazil
Authors: Luciana Meireles, Elizama Carneiro Machado Bezerra, Joelma Queiroz Andrade, Larissa Aparecida Cassiano, Hilda Fátima Jesus Pena, Bruna Farias Alves, Rossana Pulcineli Vieira Francisco, Heitor Franco de Andrade
Published: 2026

Correction to: Parasitology Research (2022) 121:3223-3228

https://doi.org/10.1007/s00436-022-07643-z

The authors regret that there are some errors in the original published article.

The authors wish to correct an error in the description of the PCR-RFLP genotype for the isolate TgHumIMTBr2. The correct genotype is #315, which had been previously reported by Witter et al. (2022) in a Toxoplasma gondii isolate from a free-range chicken (TgCkBrMT36) in the state of Mato Grosso, Midwestern Brazil. The genotype number #318 is not valid.

In line with this, the below corrections needs to be incorporated.

The article title needs to be corrected from:

The article title needs to be corrected from:

Isolation and characterization of Toxoplasma gondii isolates from human congenital toxoplasmosis cases reveal a new virulent genotype in São Paulo, Brazil

to:

Isolation and characterization of Toxoplasma gondii isolates from human congenital toxoplasmosis cases reveal a virulent genotype in São Paulo, Brazil

2.The below sentence in the abstract section needs to be corrected from:

The below sentence in the abstract section needs to be corrected from:

In this study, T. gondii strains from two cases of severe congenital toxoplasmosis from the São Paulo metropolitan area were isolated (TgHumIMTBr2 and TgHumIMTBr3) and biologically and molecularly characterized using polymerase chain reaction-restriction fragment length polymorphism (PCR–RFLP) and microsatellite analysis, revealing a new non-archetypal virulent genotype designated as #318.

to:

In this study, T. gondii strains from two cases of severe congenital toxoplasmosis from the São Paulo metropolitan area were isolated (TgHumIMTBr2 and TgHumIMTBr3) and biologically and molecularly characterized using polymerase chain reaction-restriction fragment length polymorphism (PCR–RFLP) and microsatellite analysis, revealing a non-archetypal virulent genotype already described as #315.
