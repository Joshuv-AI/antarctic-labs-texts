Preventive hepatology: Comprehensive preventive liver care
Authors: Pankaj Bhardwaj
Published: 2026

In an era where the global burden of liver disease continues to escalate, the release of the book “Preventive Hepatology,” edited by Dr Bhadoria and Prof Nundy, comes as a timely and critically important addition to medical literature.[1] Published by CRC Press (Taylor and Francis) and released on April 10, 2025, this volume promises to be a foundational resource dedicated to the proactive preventive interventions and mitigation of liver diseases.

From the outset, “Preventive Hepatology” distinguishes itself by embracing a holistic and forward-thinking approach to liver health. The book’s core strength lies in its comprehensive scope, bridging fundamental scientific understanding with practical clinical applications. It is clearly designed to serve a broad audience, including medical professionals, researchers, students, public health specialists, and general practitioners, all united by an interest in advancing liver health.

The editors have assembled a team of leading experts to meticulously craft chapters that delve into the multifaceted aspects of prevention. The promised content covers a wide array of liver conditions, including but not limited to metabolic-associated steatotic liver disease, alcoholic liver disease, viral hepatitis A-E, drug-induced liver injury, autoimmune liver diseases, and various genetic liver disorders. This extensive coverage ensures that readers gain a well-rounded understanding of the diverse etiological factors contributing to liver disease and the specific preventive strategies applicable to each.[2]

A key highlight of this anticipated publication is its emphasis on actionable preventive measures. The book is poised to explore critical interventions such as lifestyle modifications, the importance of vaccinations, strategies for early detection, and the effective management of known risk factors. Furthermore, it is expected to provide up-to-date information on the latest advancements in diagnostic tools and therapeutic approaches for early intervention, offering practical strategies for implementing these measures in clinical practice and public health initiatives. The inclusion of chapters on the vital role of nutrition, exercise, and broader public health interventions underscores the book’s commitment to a truly preventive paradigm.

Notably, the book also features a significant chapter penned by Dr. Raman, the esteemed President of the Academy of Family Physicians of India, focusing on the pivotal “Role of Primary Care Physicians” in preventive hepatology. This inclusion is particularly valuable as it underscores the critical frontline role that general practitioners play in spreading awareness, early detection, risk factor assessment, and guiding patients towards crucial preventive strategies. By highlighting the capabilities and responsibilities of primary care, the book effectively extends its relevance beyond specialist circles, empowering a broader segment of the healthcare workforce to contribute meaningfully to liver health.

“Preventive Hepatology” appears set to become an indispensable reference for anyone dedicated to reducing the incidence and progression of liver diseases worldwide. Its focus on proactive strategies, combined with evidence-based recommendations and practical insights, positions it as a vital guide for navigating the complexities of liver health in the 21st century. As the medical community increasingly recognizes the imperative of prevention, Bhadoria’s “Preventive Hepatology” will undoubtedly stand as a cornerstone for education, research, and clinical practice in this crucial field.
