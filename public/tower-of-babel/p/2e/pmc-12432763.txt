Retraction: Expression of centrosomal protein 55 in glioma tissue and the influence on glioma cell functions
Authors: Yifan Xu, Tianyu Lu, Wu Xu, Weitao Chen, Weibang Liang, Wei Jin
Published: 2025

The article “Expression of centrosomal protein 55 in glioma tissue and the influence on glioma cell functions” (doi: 10.21037/tcr.2019.01.33) published in the Vol 8, No 1 (February 28, 2019) issue of Translational Cancer Research (1) has been retracted at the request by all the authors due to lack of ethics committee approval for the study. We apologize for the inconvenience caused.
