Correction to: Intravenous methylprednisolone for nephrotic syndrome with minimal change lesions: a randomized controlled trial
Published: 2025

This is a correction to: Jinxia Chen, Ruting Li, Hua Guo, Tianyu Zhu, Yongzhi Xu, Cuiwei Yao, Huafeng Liu, Intravenous methylprednisolone for nephrotic syndrome with minimal change lesions: a randomized controlled trial, Nephrology Dialysis Transplantation, Volume 40, Issue 4, April 2025, Pages 731–738, https://doi.org/10.1093/ndt/gfae208

In the originally published version of this manuscript, the title incorrectly included the phrase “in adults” and was given as follows:

“Intravenous methylprednisolone for nephrotic syndrome with minimal change lesions in adults: a randomized controlled trial”.

This has been corrected. The revised title reads:

“Intravenous methylprednisolone for nephrotic syndrome with minimal change lesions: a randomized controlled trial”.
