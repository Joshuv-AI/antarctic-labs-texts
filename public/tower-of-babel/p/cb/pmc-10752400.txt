Retraction: Solitary Pelvic Ectopic Kidney and Limb Anomalies: Rare Variant of Acrorenal Syndrome
Published: 2023

The letter to editor titled “Solitary pelvic ectopic kidney and limb anomalies: Rare variant of acrorenal syndrome” published in Indian Journal of Nephrology, on pages 207-208, Volume 30, Issue 3, 2020,[1] is being retracted due to the failure at authors’ end for taking the ethical clearance and permission from consultants. Plagiarism, fabrication, unethical or redundant publication violates the editorial policy of Indian Journal of Nephrology, which follows best practice guidelines given by the International Committee of Medical Journal Editors (ICMJE) and Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) mentioned on the Information for Authors and as codified in the signed statements made by the authors regarding the copyright of their work.

This article has been retracted on request of the Authors and Editor-in-Chief of the journal.
