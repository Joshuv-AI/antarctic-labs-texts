CNSM-07 DIFFUSE LARGE B-CELL LYMPHOMA OF THE NECK WITH SECONDARY CNS INVOLVEMENT IN A NEWLY DIAGNOSED HIV POSITIVE PATIENT
Authors: Godfrey Rukwava
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackground and AimsDiffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma (DLBCL), the most common subtype of Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma, typically presents with systemic symptoms and lymphadenopathy. Central nervous system (CNS) involvement is rare and usually manifests as leptomeningeal or parenchymal disease. Subdural hematoma as a presenting or complicating feature of DLBCL is extremely uncommon. We report a rare case of a patient with DLBCL who presented with a chronic subdural hematoma, aiming to highlight diagnostic challenges and explore potential pathogenic links.MethodsThis is a single-patient case report based on a clinical presentation to a tertiary care center. We reviewed the patient’s medical history, imaging, laboratory findings, histopathology, and treatment course. A literature review was conducted to compare with previously reported cases.ResultsA 23-year-old male with retroviral infection, not on treatment, presented with progressive headache, confusion, and left-sided weakness. CT imaging revealed a large left-sided chronic subdural hematoma with midline shift. Burr-hole drainage was performed, revealing bone erosion and caseous and necrotic tissue below the scalp. Further workup including CSF analysis and immunohistochemistry confirmed the diagnosis of DLBCL with CNS involvement. The patient was started on RCHOP (rituximab, cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin, vincristine and prednisolone) combined with intrathecal methotrexate and cytarabine (not ideal but readily available) chemotherapy and showed clinical improvement.ConclusionsSubdural hematoma may be a rare but significant presentation or complication of DLBCL, especially in cases involving CNS infiltration. Early recognition and histopathological evaluation of subdural contents can be crucial for diagnosis. Clinicians should consider underlying malignancy in atypical or recurrent subdural hematomas.
