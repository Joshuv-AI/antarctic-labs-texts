Retraction Note: Peptide hydrogel platform encapsulating manganese ions and high-density lipoprotein nanoparticle-mimicking nanovaccines for the prevention and treatment of gastric cancer
Authors: Xu Huang, Lin Hong, Yufan Lv, Kejun Li, Zengxing Zhang, Junjian Deng, Lei Shen
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: Journal of Translational Medicine (2025) 23:371

10.1186/s12967-025-06088-z

The Editor-in-Chief has retracted this article. Following publication concerns were raised with the ethics approval for the animal experiments. In the article the authors state that all animal experiments were conducted with approval from Huazhong Agricultural University (ID Number: HZAUMO-2024–0078). However, none of the authors on this article appear to be affiliated with Huazhong Agricultural University. The Editor-in-Chief therefore no longer has confidence in the provenance and reliability of this article.

The authors have not responded to correspondence regarding this retraction notice.
