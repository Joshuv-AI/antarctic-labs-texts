Sub‐Nanogram Resolution Measurement of Inertial Mass and Density Using Magnetic‐Field‐Guided Bubble Microthruster (Adv. Sci. 29/2024)
Authors: Leilei Wang, Minjia Sheng, Li Chen, Fengchang Yang, Chenlu Li, Hangyu Li, Pengcheng Nie, Xinxin Lv, Zheng Guo, Jialing Cao, Xiaohuan Wang, Long Li, Anthony L. Hu, Dongshi Guan, Jing Du, Haihang Cui, Xu Zheng
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
Magnetic‐Field‐Guided Bubble Microthruster Artificial swimming micro/nanomotors hold vast potential in biomedical and microfabrication applications. In article 2403867, Dongshi Guan, Jing Du, Haihang Cui, Xu Zheng, and co‐authors develop bubble microthruster, a variation of bubble‐driven microrobot, to measure the mass and density of a target microparticle like a embryo. This technique presents a chip‐free, real‐time, highly maneuverable strategy that integrates bubble microrobot‐based manipulation with precise bioparticle mass and density detection with sub‐nanogram resolution.
