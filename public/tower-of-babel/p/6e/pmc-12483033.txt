Correction to: Estimated Number Needed to Treat to Avoid a First Hospitalization by Maintaining Instead of Reducing Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System Inhibitor (RAASi) Therapy after Hyperkalemia
Authors: Maria K. Svensson, Michael Fischereder, Paul R. Kalra, Ignacio José Sánchez Lázaro, Eva Lesén, Stefan Franzén, Alaster Allum, Thomas Cars, Nils Kossack, Philipp Breitbart, David Arroyo
Published: 2025

In the study by Svensson et al, “Estimated Number Needed to Treat to Avoid a First Hospitalization by Maintaining Instead of Reducing Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System Inhibitor (RAASi) Therapy after Hyperkalemia” (Kidney360 5(12): 1813–1823. doi: 10.34067/KID.0000000000000561), corrections are needed.
