Research on the Strong Generalization of Coal Gangue Recognition Technology Based on the Image and Convolutional Neural Network under Complex Conditions
Authors: Qikai Xun, Yang Yang, Yongbin Liu
Published: 2023

ABSTRACT
AbstractA coal gangue image recognition method based on complex conditions is proposed to address the current issue of image-based coal gangue recognition being greatly affected by complex conditions. First, complex conditions such as different shooting backgrounds, shooting distances, and lighting intensities are set to simulate the underground coal mining environment. Then, based on three convolutional neural network algorithms, the coal gangue recognition model is established, and the influence of different complex conditions on coal gangue image recognition is analyzed. At the same time, a network model with a strong generalization ability is determined. The results show that the accuracy of coal gangue image recognition has no obvious regularity under different shooting background conditions, and complex environments should be the primary factor affecting the accuracy of coal gangue image recognition. The accuracy of coal gangue image recognition is negatively correlated with the increase in shooting distance, and strong light conditions are conducive to improving the accuracy of coal gangue image recognition. The LeNet network model has the strongest generalization ability, which can meet the requirements of recognition accuracy and respond quickly. The accuracy of coal gangue image recognition under different complex conditions can reach more than 0.99, and the average single image recognition time is only 177 ms. This article studies the influence law of different complex conditions on the recognition of coal and gangue images and confirms that the LeNet network has strong generalization ability, achieving accurate and fast recognition of coal gangue images under complex conditions and providing a reference basis for the deployment of underground coal gangue sorting.

Introduction

As the main energy source of China, coal energy is the basic guarantee for the high-quality development of the national economy and society.1 After more than 50 years of development, the coal industry has also entered the era of intelligent mining from manual and mechanized mining.2,3 At present, carbon reduction in the energy industry is the key direction of China’s “double carbon” work. The double carbon target puts forward new technical requirements for the coal industry.4−6 The coal industry needs more technological innovation to achieve intelligent development.7 As an important research direction in the field of the coal industry, the improvement of coal gangue recognition is the key to realize intelligent coal mining.

The traditional underground coal gangue separation has high cost and low efficiency, and the underground toxic gas seriously endangers people’s safety. Therefore, it is urgent to realize intelligent coal gangue recognition. In recent years, intelligent coal gangue recognition technology has made some progress. The recognition technology based on X-ray and γ-rays is relatively mature. Through the imaging of coal and gangue, rapid recognition of coal and gangue can be realized. However, its radiation is difficult to control, so it is difficult to promote.8−10 Using the sound signal and the vibration signal of the tail beam of the hydraulic support as the basis for coal gangue recognition can better fit the actual coal mining industry.11,12 However, this method is greatly disturbed by other noises, which is not conducive to accurate recognition of coal and gangue. Liquid intervention is a new idea to realize coal gangue recognition. The principle of cooling the coal sample area in the infrared image after liquid intervention can effectively improve the recognition accuracy and efficiency.13−16 However, this method has higher requirements on variables such as liquid intervention amount, intervention time, and coal gangue mixing rate, making it difficult to apply in the actual coal mining industry. Using infrared detection technology to establish a coal gangue recognition model, coal gangue sorting can be achieved based on the difference in infrared images between coal and gangue.17,18 However, this method needs high-temperature control requirements and is greatly affected by temperature. Machine learning algorithms and convolutional neural network algorithms have become more and more popular. Using machine learning technology to establish a coal gangue recognition classifier, the precise classification of coal and gangue can be realized by extracting the image features of coal and gangue.19,20 However, the machine learning method needs to manually extract various coal and gangue features, and the process is complicated, which is not conducive to the rapid identification of coal and gangue. In contrast, a convolutional neural network can automatically extract high-level features of images and respond quickly. This method is least affected by external unfavorable factors and is more conducive to achieving accurate and rapid coal gangue recognition.21−23

From the above analysis, it can be seen that the image-based coal gangue recognition methods are widely used and have great development prospects. However, the difficulty that the image quality is easily affected by the complex conditions needs to be solved urgently. Therefore, this paper will focus on the influence of coal and gangue images under complex conditions on the recognition results; set up complex condition variables such as different shooting backgrounds, different shooting distances, and different light intensities; and establish coal gangue image recognition models based on different convolutional neural networks in order to find a network model with strong generalization ability and establish a theoretical basis for the actual coal mining industry to realize intelligence.

Theoretical Background

A convolutional neural network is a multilayer supervised learning neural network, which consists of a convolution layer, a pooling layer, and a fully connected layer.24 Its structure is shown in Figure 1. The convolutional layer and the pooling layer are the beginning of the convolutional neural network and the core of the feature extraction function.25 By stacking the convolution layer and pooling layer, the function of multilayer feature extraction of the input image can be realized.26 The fully connected layer is located at the end of the entire convolutional neural network structure and is responsible for converting the input two-dimensional feature map into a one-dimensional vector to achieve end-to-end learning.27,28 The first fully connected layer receives the feature image obtained by feature extraction, and the last fully connected layer is a classifier to complete the classification of the input image.

Convolutional neural network structure.

Since 2012, the convolutional neural network has made major breakthroughs in the field of image recognition, and its recognition capabilities have surpassed humans.29 Therefore, the convolutional neural network is also widely used in emotion classification,30,31 speech recognition,32,33 agricultural engineering,34,35 defect detection,36 fault diagnosis,37 and other fields and has made significant breakthroughs and improvements. Taking coal gangue recognition as an example, the convolutional neural network has been widely used and has achieved important research results. The establishment of the coal gangue recognition model based on LeNet, AlexNet, ResNet18, and other classification networks can solve the problems of difficult recognition of coal gangue mixing rate and improve the accuracy of coal gangue recognition.38−40 Therefore, based on the coal gangue image under complex conditions, the author uses LeNet, AlexNet, and ResNet18 to establish the coal gangue image recognition models. It is expected to obtain the influence law of complex conditions on coal gangue image recognition and select a network model with a strong generalization ability, which provides a theoretical basis for achieving accurate and rapid coal gangue image recognition.

(1)Under the conditions of black background, coal ash background, and broken coal background, the accuracy of coal gangue image recognition has no obvious regularity, and the environmental complexity should be the primary factor affecting the coal gangue image recognition.

Under the condition of image shooting distances of 30, 60, and 90 cm, the recognition accuracy of coal gangue image is negatively correlated with the increase of image shooting distance.

(3)Under the conditions of light intensity of 50–100 and 130–180 Lux, the strong light intensity condition is more conducive to improving the accuracy of coal gangue image recognition.

Under the conditions of light intensity of 50–100 and 130–180 Lux, the strong light intensity condition is more conducive to improving the accuracy of coal gangue image recognition.

(4)The convolutional neural network algorithm is superior to the traditional machine learning algorithm. Among the three network models of LeNet, AlexNet, and ResNet18, the LeNet network model has stronger generalization ability, and the accuracy of coal gangue image recognition under various complex conditions can reach more than 0.99. It can meet the recognition accuracy of coal and gangue images. The memory occupation is only 21,122 kb, the calculation amount is only 54 M, the average single iteration time is only 68.3 s, and the average single image recognition time is only 177 ms, which meets the requirements of rapid recognition of coal and gangue images.

The convolutional neural network algorithm is superior to the traditional machine learning algorithm. In this study, the influence law of different complex conditions on coal gangue image recognition was obtained, and the strong generalization ability of the LeNet network was determined. The accurate and rapid coal gangue image recognition under complex conditions was realized, which provided a reference for the realization of coal gangue separation.
