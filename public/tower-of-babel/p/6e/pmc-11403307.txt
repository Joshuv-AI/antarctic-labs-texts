Correction to: “The Effect of Thiazide Diuretics on Urinary Prostaglandin Estradiol Excretion and Serum Sodium in the General Population”
Published: 2024

In the above-named article by Geurts F, Rudolphi CF, Pelouto A, van der Burgh AC, Salih M, Silva PHI, Fenton RA, Chaker L, and Hoorn EJ (J Clin Endo Metab. 2024; doi: 10.1210/clinem/dgae352), there was an error in the title and in Table 1.

In the originally published article, the title read as, “The Effect of Thiazide Diuretics on Urinary Prostaglandin Estradiol Excretion and Serum Sodium in the General Population.” In the corrected article, the title reads as “The Effect of Thiazide Diuretics on Urinary Prostaglandin E2 Excretion and Serum Sodium in the General Population.”

In Table 1, in the abbreviations, “prostaglandin estradiol” has been corrected to read, “prostaglandin E2.”

The publisher acknowledges the error.

The article has been corrected online.

doi: 10.1210/clinem/dgae352
