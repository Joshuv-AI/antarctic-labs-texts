A Simple Scope‐Advancement Technique to Correct Pancreatic–Biliary Duct Axis Misalignment During Pancreatic Guidewire‐Assisted Biliary Cannulation
Authors: Yuichi Takano, Naoki Tamai, Jun Noda, Tetsushi Azami, Fumitaka Niiya, Masatsugu Nagahama
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
ABSTRACTRecognition of pancreatic–biliary duct axis misalignment is important when pancreatic guidewire‐assisted biliary cannulation is unsuccessful. A simple scope‐advancement maneuver under fluoroscopic guidance can redirect the catheter toward the bile duct axis and facilitate biliary access without additional devices, potentially avoiding the need for more advanced rescue techniques.Keywords: biliary cannulation, ductal axis misalignment, ERCP, pancreatic guidewire‐assisted cannulation

Case Presentation

An 83‐year‐old man with distal bile duct stenosis underwent ERCP (Figure 1). Pancreatic guidewire (PGW)‐assisted biliary cannulation failed because the catheter remained aligned with the pancreatic duct axis (Figure 2). Advancing the scope deeper into the duodenum with slight upward and rightward angulation redirected the catheter toward the bile duct axis under fluoroscopic guidance, enabling successful biliary cannulation (Figures 3 and 4, Video 1).

(A) Magnetic resonance cholangiopancreatography demonstrated a stricture in the middle bile duct, with a short segment of normal distal bile duct remaining between the stricture and the papilla. (B) Magnified view of the dotted square in panel A, demonstrating the axis of the common bile duct (arrow) and the main pancreatic duct (arrowhead).

Fluoroscopic image demonstrating that the catheter is aligned with the pancreatic duct axis (arrow), preventing biliary access.

After scope advancement, the catheter orientation shifts toward the bile duct axis under fluoroscopic guidance (arrow).

Successful deep biliary cannulation.

Although advanced rescue techniques such as precut sphincterotomy are effective for difficult biliary cannulation, they require greater expertise and may increase the risk of post‐ERCP pancreatitis [1, 2]. Recognition of pancreatic–biliary duct axis misalignment and simple scope‐position adjustment may facilitate biliary access before proceeding to more advanced interventions.
