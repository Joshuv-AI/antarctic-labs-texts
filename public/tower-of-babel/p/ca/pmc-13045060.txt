Long-term trends in disease burden of esophageal cancer in China and the forecast until 2050
Authors: Xiaoyu Huang, Zi Sang, Ziliang Shi, Jian Li, Mingfei Geng, Xiaoqing Cao
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundEsophageal cancer (EC) is one of the most common malignant tumors in China, with the number of new cases and deaths accounting for about half of the global total, imposing a heavy burden of disease. The latest epidemiological statistics on EC can help policymakers allocate resources for EC prevention, treatment, and care.ObjectivesThe project was designed to comprehensively and reliably evaluate the epidemiology of EC in China and predict its future trends.MethodsThe Global Burden of Disease (GBD) study 2021 is a comprehensive data platform developed in collaboration with researchers around the world to provide detailed information and analysis on diseases, injuries, and risk factors at global, regional, and national levels for the assessment of disease health trends. Based on this, we extracted data related to China’s EC disease burden from GBD 2021 for 1990–2021. and used the Joinpoint model to fit the trend of age-standardized rates of EC, and calculate the annual percentage change (APC) and average annual percentage change (AAPC). The Bayesian Age-Period-Cohort (BAPC) model was employed to analyze and predict the trend of the disease burden of EC in China from 2022 to 2050. We also analyzed the temporal trend of EC burden in China caused by specific risk factors such as chewing tobacco, a diet with low vegetable intake, high alcohol intake, and smoking.ResultsFrom 1990 to 2021, the number of cases and deaths from EC increased. The age-standardized prevalence rate (ASPR) and age-standardized mortality rate (ASMR) both decreased over the same period, with AAPCs in the general population being − 1.07 (95% CI: -1.23, -0.92) and − 1.98 (95% CI: -2.19, -1.77), respectively. From 1990 to 2021, the number of cases and deaths in most age groups significantly increased, but the crude prevalence rate (CPR) and crude mortality rate (CMR) decreased. Males had a heavier burden on EC. By 2050, the number of cases and deaths from EC was expected to increase significantly, with corresponding increases in ASPR and ASMR for the total population and males. Interestingly, the ASMR for females was projected to be decreasing. In addition, the ASMR of EC caused by four risk factors showed a decreasing trend in both males and females.ConclusionThe prevalence and mortality of EC in China are greatly influenced by age, especially in males. Therefore, the government should take targeted intervention measures, focusing on middle-aged and elderly men as the key population for EC prevention and control, strengthening EC prevention, and improving the health-related quality of life of patients.Supplementary InformationThe online version contains supplementary material available at 10.1186/s12876-025-04484-9.Keywords: Age-standardized prevalence rate, Age-standardized mortality rate, Bayesian age-period-cohort model, Esophageal cancer

Introduction

Esophageal cancer (EC) is a tumor of the upper digestive tract that occurs in the esophagus or gastroesophageal junction [1], emerging as a major global health concern like other gastrointestinal malignancies. According to the latest statistics from the Global Cancer Statistics (GLOBOCAN) database, the number of cases and deaths worldwide is 510,716 and 445,129, making it the seventh leading cause of cancer-related death worldwide and the sixth leading cause of cancer-related death in men [2]. As the world’s largest developing country, China is one of the most common countries with EC occurrence. According to the 2022 Chinese cancer statistics, an estimated 224,000 new cases of EC and 187,500 deaths were reported [3], accounting for 43.85% and 42.12% of global incidence and mortality, respectively [2, 3]. In recent years, due to the development of the social economy and improvement in diet in China, progress has been made in the prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and research of EC, leading to a drop in the incidence and mortality of EC [4]. However, due to the large population in China and the poor prognosis of EC patients, the 5-year overall survival (OS) rate of advanced patients is only 10–30% [5, 6]. Therefore, China still bears a heavy burden of EC.

Timely description of disease trends over time is essential for evaluating disease control strategies and health indices, as well as predicting future incidence, prevalence, and health planning. Although the previous 2019 Global Burden of Disease (GBD) study showed future trends in EC morbidity, mortality, and the status of their burden [7, 8], given the rapid evolution of disease trends and national control strategies, these previous studies could only be interpreted in the context of the time when the data were collected and may not reflect the current trends. In addition, exploring the impact of specific risk factors on diseases can provide a key basis for developing more targeted prevention and control strategies and making precise public health decisions [9]. Therefore, in this study, we used the latest GBD 2021 data [10] and analyzed the latest trends in EC morbidity and mortality between 1990 and 2021 by using the Joinpoint regression [11] and Bayesian age-period-cohort (BAPC) models [12] and predicted trends in 2050. The impact of specific risk factors on EC burden was analyzed. We hope that the findings in this article can provide policymakers with references for evaluating the burden of endometrial cancer, measuring the progress of specific treatments, formulating relevant policy planning, and contributing to the improvement of cancer prevention and control measures in China.

Methods

Data source

The data in this work came from the GBD 2021, a secondary data analysis based on the GBD study. The GBD 2021 database provided in-depth assessments of health loss related to 371 diseases, injuries, and impairments, as well as 88 risk factors, using the latest epidemiological data and enhanced standardized statistical models, covering 204 countries and territories [13]. The original data sources of China EC estimated by GBD mainly came from the nationally representative China Disease Surveillance Point System, the National Health Surveillance Reporting System, the vital registration data collected by the Center for Disease Control and Prevention, and the data registered by the National Cancer Center [10, 14].

In GBD 2021, EC cases were identified based on the International Classification of Diseases (ICD) codes (ICD-9: 150–150.9.9; ICD-10: C15-C15.9) [15].

All data in this article were sourced from the GBD 2021 database and were accessed on July 16, 2024. In addition, this project did not require ethical approval as it used publicly available data. The data included: (1) The number of cases and deaths of EC in China for males, females, and general people of all age groups from 1990 to 2021, along with corresponding age-standardized rate (ASRs) data: Age-standardized prevalence rate (ASPR) and age-standardized mortality rate (ASMR); (2). The number of cases and deaths of EC for each age group of males and females in China from 1990 to 2021, along with corresponding crude rates (CRs): Crude prevalence rate (CPR) and Crude mortality rate (CMR); (3) The number of deaths and ASMR of EC caused by risk factors in each age group of males and females in China from 1990 to 2021, and the number of deaths and CMR of each age group of men and women. All available Level 3 risk factors related to EC in GBD 2021 were included, including chewing tobacco, a diet low in vegetables, high alcohol use, and smoking [16]. The screening strategy= “Display: Risk” And “Rank: Risk” And “Cause: Esophageal cancer” And “Category: All risk factors”.

Statistical analysis

Descriptive study

The estimated annual percentage change (EAPC) was calculated to describe ASRs (including ASPR and ASMR) of the EC burden in China from 1990 to 2021. If the EAPC value and the lower bound of the 95% CI were greater than 0, the age-standardized indicators showed upward trends. The calculation of EAPC was based on the natural logarithm rate of the regression model, with time as the variable. The natural logarithm of each observation was fitted into a straight line, and EAPC was calculated based on the slope of the line. The regression model formula is as follows: . The calculation formula for EAPC is as follows: , where y is the natural logarithm of the rate, α is the intercept, β is the slope of the line, x is the year, and ϵ is the error term [17].

EAPC can assess the dynamic changes in disease burden. If EAPC and 95%CI lower limit >0, it implies an increasing trend of age standardized indicator; if EAPC < 0, it implies a decreasing trend, and if EAPC = 0, it implies a constant trend [18]. Public health departments can accurately assess the situation based on this and then develop or adjust corresponding screening plans, health education, and intervention measures to effectively respond.

The time trend of EC burden caused by risk factors in China from 1990 to 2021, as well as changes in age and gender, was analyzed.

Joinpoint regression analysis

Using Joinpoint software (version 4.9.1.0; National Cancer Institute, Rockville, Maryland, USA) and the Joinpoint regression model (logarithmic linear model: ln(y)= β0་β1x), we calculated the annual percentage change (APC) and its 95% confidence interval (CI). Then, based on the trend of APC, we calculated the average annual percentage change (AAPC) to determine the trend of disease burden [19–21]. Joinpoint regression analysis assumed that the overall disease burden during the study period showed a linear trend, and it represented the change in this trend by introducing connecting points. We used a permutation test to compare the goodness of fit between the inflection point model and the zero model, and we used Bonferroni correction to adjust for multiple comparisons. To avoid overfitting, a maximum of 5 inflection points was allowed to be set.

The formula is as follows: ; −1) Where y is the natural logarithm of the rate, β1 is the regression coefficient, x is the year, wi represents the width of each piecewise function interval (i.e. the number of years contained in each interval), and βi represents the regression coefficient of each interval [22].

By comparing AAPC/APC with 0, we investigated whether the fluctuation trend of different parts was statistically significant. APC/AAPC > 0 implied an increasing trend, while APC/AAPC < 0 implied a decreasing trend. APC/AAPC = 0 implied a stable trend. P <0.05 implied statistical significance.

BAPC analysis

By utilizing the integrated nested Laplacian approximations (INLA), we created a BAPC model to predict the prevalence and mortality of EC and the trends of ASPR and ASMR in China from 2022 to 2050 [12, 23, 24]. This model introduced a Bayesian structure based on the classic logarithmic linear APC framework and obtained posterior prediction results of future trends by applying prior distributions to time components. To ensure the recognizability of the model and the smoothness of time effects, a second-order random walk (RW2) prior was used for age, period, and cohort components. This prior assumption assumed that second-order differences followed a normal distribution centered around zero, thereby achieving a smooth transition of time trends. The corresponding accuracy parameter (inverse variance) is assigned a weak information Gamma hyper prior (Gamma (1,0.0005)). A balance was achieved between model flexibility and regularization, allowing data to dominate smoothness while preventing overfitting [25]. Standardized (actual non-GBD) morbidity, mortality, and future 30-year projections for ASPR and ASMR were presented. All data analysis was carried out in software R (version 4.3.3) and R studio. The BAPC prediction model was set up by utilizing the packages BAPC (version 0.0.36) and INLA (version 23.04.24).

Discussion

This study analyzed the temporal trends of prevalence and mortality rates of EC in China from 1990 to 2021. The overall trend changes are similar to the results of previous research based on GBD 2019 data [8]. Differently, the GBD 2021 dataset we used extended the observation time to 2021, allowing us to capture more recent disease dynamics and changes in risk factors, and to preliminarily assess the potential impact of the early stages of the COVID-19 pandemic on EC disease burden, though definitive assessment of pandemic effects will require longer follow-up data. The update of GBD 2021 can build a more timely and comprehensive evaluation framework, providing new evidence for understanding the recent evolution of China’s EC. From 1990 to 2021, both ASPR and ASMR of EC in China decreased. Age served as an important factor affecting the burden of EC, and it increased with age. Moreover, the burden of EC in males was significantly higher than that in females. By 2050, the ASPR and ASMR of the total population and males in China were projected to continue to increase. For females, only the ASPR was predicted to increase, and ASMR was projected to show a potential downward trend. In addition, the ASMR of EC burden caused by four specific risk factors showed a downward trend.

From 1990 to 2021, the age standardization rate of EC in China showed a gradually decreasing trend. This is closely related to the efforts of China. Since 2006, China has strengthened measures to promote early screening, diagnosis, and treatment of EC, which are considered the key to preventing and treating EC [26]. Since then, many regions have implemented and promoted screening measures [27–29]. The implementation of these EC prevention and control policies has identified a considerable number of precancerous lesions and EC patients, inhibiting the progression of their disease to advanced EC, thereby reducing the mortality and disability rates of EC. At the same time, with the improvement of the living standards of the Chinese people, the country has begun to pay attention to the optimization of residents’ dietary structure and the popularization of nutritional knowledge. Since 1989, China has been formulating dietary and nutritional guidelines for residents [30] to encourage an increase in vegetable intake. Data shows that from 2010 to 2018, the daily consumption of vegetables and fruits by the Chinese labor force significantly increased, increasing from 418.6 g/d to 491.8 g/d. The prevalence of low intake of fruits and vegetables decreased from 51.1% to 43.5% [31], which has a beneficial effect on reducing EC burden [32]. In addition, tobacco and alcohol consumption are also important factors affecting the increase in EC in China and globally. The Chinese government has implemented a targeted “Healthy China 2030” tobacco control and alcohol restriction strategy to reduce smoking and alcohol abuse rates among the population [33–35]. These results are consistent with our risk factor analysis, which showed a decreasing trend in the ASMR of EC burden caused by low vegetable intake, chewing tobacco, smoking, and high alcohol intake. Therefore, we speculate that these measures may be an important reason for the decrease in China’s EC burden since 2004.

Moreover, with the sustained development of China’s social economy and the comprehensive improvement of accessibility to medical and health services, the effectiveness of EC prevention and control has been further consolidated. Economic growth has injected strong impetus into the public health system, enabling more people in different regions to access timely and standardized diagnosis and treatment services [36]. The development of the social economy has also brought about an increase in treatment accessibility. The government has dynamically adjusted the medical insurance drug catalog mechanism to include a large number of clinically necessary anti-tumor drugs in the reimbursement scope [37]. The measure enables EC patients to obtain therapeutic drugs (cetuximab, bevacizumab, etc.) at a lower cost, significantly reducing their economic burden and improving treatment compliance [38]. At the same time, the advancement of medical technology—the popularization of endoscopic technology, the application of minimally invasive surgery (such as minimally invasive esophagectomy), and the development of immunotherapy drugs (such as nivolumab)—has significantly improved the quality of life and prolonged the survival period of EC patients [39–41]. These factors may alleviate the burden of EC disease from the treatment stage and are an important supplement to the prevention-oriented public health strategy.

However, there may be significant regional differences behind these developments. A previous study has shown that in China, provinces near the Taihang Mountains, such as Henan, have the most severe burden of EC [42, 43]. The EC burden in rural areas is significantly higher than that in cities, with the mortality rate in rural areas exceeding twice that of urban areas in 2009 alone [44]. The reason for the high incidence in Henan Province may be related to dietary habits, as hot and pickled foods can increase the risk of EC [45]. However, EC patients in rural areas have been experiencing delayed diagnosis and treatment, and the high burden may be related to poor economic capacity and insufficient medical resources [46]. Therefore, in order to continuously and effectively reduce the burden of EC diseases nationwide in the future, it is urgent to strengthen resource allocation and policy support for high-risk provinces and rural areas, and focus on narrowing the gap in medical service quality between regions.

We need to note that the Joinpoint results showed that the APC of ASPR for both sexes from 2015 to 2021 was higher than 0, indicating an increasing trend. More importantly, compared to studies up to 2019, the updated data confirmed that the upward trend continued in the latest period of 2019–2021, providing crucial new evidence for assessing the current disease burden. The change may be driven by multiple factors. Firstly, the increasing aging of the Chinese population is a potentially significant influencing factor. According to the seventh national census data in 2020, the population aged 60 and above in China has reached 264 million, accounting for 18.70% of the total population [47]. Moreover, measures to strengthen cancer prevention and control at the national level may also lead to an increase in the statistical value of ASPR to some extent. Since 2019, China has successively implemented the Healthy China Action Plan (2019–2030) [48] and the Cancer Prevention and Control Implementation Plan (2019–2022) [49], accelerating the early screening and diagnosis of key cancers, including EC. The coverage has gradually expanded, and the improvement of screening capabilities can help detect patients earlier and more frequently, which may be statistically manifested as an increase in ASPR.

Our study also further found that the high prevalence of illness and death from EC was in people over the age of 50, which peaked in the 70–74 age group. The CPR and CMR also elevated gradually with age, peaking in the 70–74 and 90–94 age groups, respectively. This result is similar to the findings of a previous study on the incidence and mortality trends of EC in China, which indicated that the incidence and mortality rates of EC elevate gradually with age, peaking in the 75-year-old age group [50]. The elevated mortality in the elderly may be related to the lack of obvious and specific clinical symptoms of early EC, and most patients are in the middle and late stages at the time of diagnosis, resulting in a lower 5-year survival rate for patients [5]. Elderly people often have multiple chronic diseases, weakened immune system function, and weakened body resistance to diseases, making them more likely to be subjected to EC invasion and faster disease progression [51, 52]. In addition, the participation rate of EC endoscopic screening may also contribute to the increased prevalence. The results of two studies investigating EC screening participation rates demonstrated that, compared with young people, the elderly are more concerned about their health and are more willing to participate in disease screening. The participation rate, detection rate, and screening effect of people over 50 are higher than those under 50 [53, 54].

Men had a heavier EC burden in our country. In 2021, over 70% of cases and deaths from EC were concentrated in males. For males, the ASPR and ASMR of EC were 3–4 times higher than those of females. Smoking and alcohol consumption are the main risk factors for EC [55]. In China, the prevalence of smoking and drinking among men is more than 10 times that of women [54]. The results of risk factor analysis also indicated that the burden of EC in males was consistently higher than that in females due to smoking and alcohol consumption. Moreover, estrogen in women is also believed to have a role in influencing the development of EC. An in vitro study uncovered that estrogen can mediate EC cell apoptosis through interaction with the endoplasmic reticulum [56]. Another study demonstrated that estrogen response can prolong the clinical outcome of EC by facilitating the p53 pathway and inducing apoptosis [57]. Therefore, we speculated that estrogen may suppress the progression of EC by maintaining and activating p53 function by influencing the relevant status of endoplasmic reticulum stress.

However, the forecast of the future trend of EC using the BAPC prediction model is not optimistic. The results showed that the total number of EC cases and ASRs is steadily increasing, especially the number of male patients and ASPR, indicating that there will still be pressure on the prevention and control of EC in China in the future. Recently, significant progress has been made in the methods of palliative treatment for EC. The latest research shows that esophageal stent implantation has significant clinical value as a key palliative treatment for relieving swallowing difficulties in patients with advanced EC. Three types of stents (self-expanding metal stent, self-expanding plastic stent, and fully covered self-expanding metal stent) can quickly and effectively relieve swallowing obstruction (87.4% immediate relief), with high technical success rates (96.4%, 95.1%, and 97.4%, respectively) [58]. Moreover, the quality of life of most patients improves rapidly after surgery. The study provides a new important option for EC patients who cannot receive curative treatment, which is expected to reduce the overall disease burden caused by EC in the future. Therefore, in the future, China still needs to implement active disease prevention and control policies, strengthen epidemiological research and examination, improve the pertinence and efficiency of clinical diagnosis and treatment, and attach importance to the development and application of innovative therapies and personalized drug regimens, in order to curb the upward trend of EC disease burden. While sensitivity analyses of BAPC model parameters were not performed, the Bayesian framework inherently accounted for parameter uncertainty through posterior distributions, and our use of standard priors followed established methodology in burden projection studies.

We acknowledge that certain limitations persist in our work. Firstly, our study is a secondary data analysis for GBD research, which has inevitable shortcomings, such as data quality assurance. Secondly, our research on EC does not have specific cancer subtypes. The two main histological subtypes of EC, esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC) and esophageal adenocarcinoma (EAC), have different risk factors and incidence trends. ESCC is more common in Asia and accounts for 90% in China, mainly related to factors such as smoking and alcohol consumption [42]. The incidence rate of EAC in Western countries is rising, which is closely related to obesity, gastroesophageal reflux disease, etc [59]., but there is no separate data for these two subtypes in GBD database at present, which may limit the formulation of targeted policies. Thirdly, predictive models can only roughly describe future trends based on existing data and cannot provide accurate predictions.

Conclusion

Our study provides evidence of a decline in ASPR and ASMR of EC over the past 30 years. However, due to the large population base and a high degree of population aging in China, the number of EC cases and deaths has continued to increase from 1990 to 2021, especially among middle-aged and elderly men. Based on the model prediction, the number of male and female patients with EC and deaths will continue to increase over the next 25 years.
