Estimating autism prevalence among children in KSA. A comprehensive multisource benchmarking approach

Kong et al assessed differences in healthcare infrastructure, diagnostic frameworks, and screening intensity, and reviewed recent KSA-based studies. Methodological limitations such as underreporting, cultural stigma, and diagnostic inconsistencies were also considered. Integrating these findings, they estimate that the realistic prevalence in KSA lies between 1.7% and 1.8%. The prevalence of ASD among children in KSA is substantially higher than the official estimate of 0.6%.

Proton beam therapy indications in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. A SARO clinical practice guideline

Aljabab et al establish proton therapy services in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. The recommendation level was based on the overall evidence using a 4-grade scale following the World Health Organization quality of evidence recommendations. The Panel reported that the body of evidence favored proton therapy for specific indications, including ocular tumors, skull and spine tumors, oropharyngeal cancer, hepatocellular carcinoma, specific genetic syndromes and mutations, pediatric rhabdomyosarcoma, pediatric central nervous system malignancies, tumors of the paranasal sinuses/nasal cavity, and for re-irradiation of all sites when curative treatment was intended. The Panel did not recommend the routine use of proton therapy for all cancers. Each case should be assessed individually following a multidisciplinary review and expert consultation while accounting for financial, logistical, and patient-specific factors.

The relationship of ST6GAL1 with clinicopathological and prognostic factors in breast carcinomas

Ömeroğlu et al investigate 85 patients with the association of β-galactoside α2-6 sialyltransferase 1 (ST6GAL1) expression with hormone receptor (HR), HER2, and other clinicopathological parameters, including histological grade, stage, lymphovascular invasion, perineural invasion, and survival in breast carcinomas (BCs). High and low ST6GAL1 expressions were observed in 45.9% and 54.1% cases, respectively. This study revealed highly significant associations of ST6GAL1 expression with HR, HER2, and molecular subtyping.

Empowering Saudi pharmacy students. A nudge-based intervention for improved health behaviors

Alzahrani et al promote healthy habits among pharmacy students through curriculum-embedded challenges. A reflective survey assessed participation, motivations, baseline behaviors, and post-intervention intentions. A large majority (89.7%–98.7%) reported intentions to sustain the positive behaviors post-intervention, particularly among those who had not previously adopted them. Intrinsic motivation, such as the desire for personal well-being, showed a significant positive correlation with the intention to continue. Embedding nudge-based challenges into pharmacy curricula is a feasible and culturally responsive strategy for enhancing student well-being.

The incidence of unplanned reoperation and the related risk factors in pediatric surgery at a tertiary care center in Saudi Arabia

Almutairi et al evaluate clinical endpoints and factors associated with unplanned reoperations, which are defined as any secondary surgery occurring within 30 days of the initial procedure due to complications. From February 2015 to August 2023, a total of 2551 patients underwent 2963 abdominal surgeries. Among these, 75 (2.94%) patients required re-surgeries, resulting in a total of 179 surgeries. Monitoring URO rates is essential for enhancing pediatric surgical practices, improving preoperative counseling, and optimizing resource allocation.

Autism spectrum disorder in Saudi Arabia. Clinical dynamics from a multi-center study

Alfakeh et al explore the factors influencing the clinical presentation of children with ASD in Saudi Arabia, focusing on demographic, medical, and environmental determinants. Therapy participation varied with 49% receiving speech therapy, 44.2% engaged in behavioral therapy and 5.8% currently undergoing physiotherapy. Approximately, 34.6% had received occupational therapy for less than one year. he findings underscore the importance of improving therapy access and parental support to address persistent challenges, such as hyperactivity, learning difficulties, and limited self-care skills.
