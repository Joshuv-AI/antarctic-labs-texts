Retraction and replacement of: Electronic connectivity between hospital pairs: impact on emergency department-related utilization
Published: 2025

This is a retraction and replacement of: Julia Adler-Milstein, Ariel Linden, Renee Y Hsia, Jordan Everson, Electronic connectivity between hospital pairs: impact on emergency department-related utilization, Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association, Volume 31, Issue 1, January 2024, Pages 15–23, https://doi.org/10.1093/jamia/ocad204.

The manuscript was accepted for publication on 2nd October 2023 and the typeset version published online on 16th October 2023, before compilation into the January 2024 issue of the journal. The authors contacted the editorial office in August 2024 after discovering an error in their code that resulted in dropping some observations for the third outcome measure—Admit from ED following Hospital Discharge. Correcting the error resulted in alterations to the manuscript that were deemed beyond the scope of a correction. As such, the authors and editors agreed to a retraction and republication to correct the record.

The authors discovered that the majority of observations were dropped for the Same Hospital group, resulting in higher levels for both the 30- and 7-day Admit from ED following Hospital Discharge measure. The effects of this change are primarily observed in Figure 3 in which the Same Hospital group used to be lower than the two Different Hospital groups and is now higher. Figure 3 has been updated along with the corresponding text. Given that the focus of the paper is on comparing the two Different Hospital groups (with and without HIE), these changes do not impact the primary results from the study. Several other numbers were updated by a small amount as well as the online appendix. For example, the average age of beneficiaries included in the third outcome measure (30-day) increased slightly from 75.98 (SD=7.85) to 76.08 (SD=7.83).

This retraction and replacement is to correct and clarify the scholarly record and in no way suggests any misconduct on the part of the authors. The editors commend the authors for bringing the error to the journal’s attention and for their desire to present an accurate account of their results. The new revised version of the manuscript was reviewed and accepted by the Editor-in-Chief and is available at https://doi.org/10.1093/jamia/ocaf159.
