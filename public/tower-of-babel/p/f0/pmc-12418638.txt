Published: 2025

BMC Pulmonary Medicine (2021) 21:68

Following publication of the original article, it came to the authors’ attention that there was an error in Table 1: values for duration of anesthesia were erroneously detailed as being shorter than those of procedure duration. The table has since been corrected in the published article; please be referred to the corrected version of Table 1. The authors thank you for reading this erratum and apologize for any inconvenience caused.
