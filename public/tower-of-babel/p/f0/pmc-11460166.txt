The industry demand oriented construction of intelligence medicine specialty in traditional Chinese medicine university: teachers’ perspective
Authors: Wei Lin, Xiaoxue Chen, Yujie Zhang, Yuxiang Fu, Tao Jiang, Lin Xu
Published: 2024

ABSTRACT
BackgroundThe aim of this study was to explore the relationships between teaching competence, career development, industrial orientation, and specialty construction in the case of intelligent medicine specialties in universities of traditional Chinese medicine, as well as the influencing factors of specialty construction.MethodsTwo-stage sampling with unequal probability was conducted to distribute self-administered questionnaires in the fieldwork. A structural equation model was built to investigate the influencing factors of specialty construction. Forty-two teachers were recruited and completed the questionnaire.ResultsThe study found that teaching competence, career development, and industrial orientation positively affect specialty construction at a significant level. Career development and industrial orientation play chain mediating roles in the effect of teaching competence on specialty construction.ConclusionsIn the construction of intelligent medicine specialties, a professional curriculum system should be built that caters to industrial demand and is combined with industrial development, the traditional mode of teaching should be transformed, and the transformation of theoretical knowledge into practical ability should be promoted. Innovations in teaching modes should be achieved by introducing information technology, and the teacher evaluation system should be optimized.

Background

Intelligent medicine is a natural science based on modern natural scientific theories that applies advanced artificial intelligence technology and combines methods used in biology and other natural sciences to explore the nature of, and laws governing, human life and disease phenomena. It is an important emerging branch of medicine [1]. With the increasingly close links between healthcare and new technology, including artificial intelligence, robotics, and big data, the traditional medical model, which emphasizes clinical experience with the use of medical devices as a subsidiary, has entered the stage of big-data-driven intelligent medicine with human–computer collaboration. It can thus solve some problems in medical treatment via smarter algorithms and explore laws by linking up faculties, information, devices, and resources associated with medical services to improve experience in the areas of prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and rehabilitation.

As a bridge connecting intelligent engineering and medicine, intelligent medicine is a future developmental trend in medicine. Owing to its significant advantages in prevention and healthcare, the intelligent medicine industry with characteristics of Chinese medicine will continue to advance. This will inevitably generate a large number of job opportunities and needs for talent, and intelligent medicine specialties with distinctive characteristics of Chinese medicine will enter a stage of great achievements. As an important part of China’s medical system, the high-quality development of traditional Chinese medicine (TCM) needs the help of advanced sciences, such as artificial intelligence and intelligent manufacturing, and the establishment of intelligent medicine specialties will help train suitable talents for modern research and clinical practice in TCM.

The American government has continuously promoted the implementation of professional standards and evaluation for teachers at the educational level and has attached importance to the role of teachers’ ability in the construction of specialties since the 1980s [2]. Recently, both theoretical and practical studies have confirmed that there is a strong correlation between teaching competence and specialty construction. As the main pillar of school development, teachers bear the responsibility for cultivating creative talents [3]. Therefore, it is necessary to unite teachers and improve their teaching level and competence for specialty construction [4]. The professional quality, knowledge, and skills of teachers directly affect the efficiency and level of the teaching of major subjects. Moreover, in order to meet the developmental needs of higher education to improve quality and efficiency, it is essential to build high-standard teams of professional teachers and recruit more professional teachers of TCM [3]. As the directors of specialties, the competence of the professional leaders is of great significance for the improvement of the level of construction of higher vocational specialties and the promotion of the training of high-quality technical talents. In addition, competency-based theoretical models of professional leaders in colleges and universities emphasize professional ability with regard to benchmarks, professional ability in the respective field, general ability with regard to industry, and excellent behavior [5].

The deep integration of artificial intelligence in the field of TCM is leading a new revolution in traditional medicine. Intelligent diagnostic systems utilize big data and machine learning to accurately analyze patient information and assist in rapid diagnosis and treatment in TCM [6]. The analysis of ancient literature utilizes natural language processing techniques to uncover insights from ancient medical cases and thus provide valuable references for modern diagnosis and treatment [7]. Personalized treatment recommendations should be tailored to the patient’s physical condition to create a personalized plan and improve treatment outcomes. Efficacy evaluation and prediction utilize AI models to monitor the progress of treatment in real time and predict rehabilitation trends [8]. Remote medical services overcome geographical limitations and enable high-quality TCM resources to benefit a wider range of people. Intelligent pharmaceutical management ensures the quality of medicinal materials and optimizes production processes. Research assistance platforms accelerate theoretical research in TCM and promote scientific verification. In addition, health education is popularized through AI technology to vividly disseminate knowledge of TCM and enhance public health literacy [9]. In summary, the integration of artificial intelligence in TCM is of great significance in promoting its modernization and precision.

It is also one of the focuses of discussions of the level of specialty construction from the aspect of industrial orientation. The form and development of intelligent Chinese medicine, as an important component of intelligent medicine, which has improved the modes of diagnosis and treatment in TCM, are conducive to a new phase that is characterized by integration among TCM, intelligence, and information. Building a scientific and rational professional curriculum system, ensuring that professional courses match the needs of industry, and laying emphasis on the intersection of theory and practice can promote the development of specialty construction and career-oriented education [4]. Furthermore, for the sake of the improvement of construction in the information science and engineering specialty, building an industry-oriented teaching curriculum system and focusing on the training of engineering and technical talents to meet the needs of industry at the application level should receive more attention [10].

Intelligent medicine is an upgrade of the “information technology + medicine” model, which uses medical information as the medium to carry out data mining and effective utilization of big data. Courses of intelligent medicine integrate the unique theoretical system of TCM with cutting-edge information technology, covering multiple fields, such as medicine, computer science, big data, and artificial intelligence [11], and forming a distinctive curriculum system aimed at enhancing students’ comprehensive quality and professional skills. They closely connect with the needs of the healthcare industry, establish extensive cooperation with medical institutions, technology enterprises, etc., promote deep integration between industry, academia, and research, and promote the modernization and application of intelligent medical technology [12]. However, few studies have explored the relationships between career development and teaching competence, industrial orientation, and specialty construction. Some researchers have found that in courses of intelligent medicine the career development contents are taught less well [13], the students do not understand the scope and opportunities for professional career development, and the practical use of, and training in, vocational skills are not in place. As an emerging profession, intelligent medicine lacks corresponding research on how to strengthen professional construction from the perspective of grassroots teachers. Therefore, in the study of the promotion of specialty construction, it is necessary to explore the relationships between career development and specialty construction, teaching competence, and industrial orientation. This study investigated teachers from five different universities of Chinese medicine who are responsible for teaching and management in intelligent medicine. It constructed a structural equation model with four factors – teaching competence, career development, industrial orientation, and specialty construction – and examined whether career development and industrial orientation play chain mediating roles in the effect of teaching competence on specialty construction on the basis of discussion of their correlation. It is beneficial for teachers to continuously pay attention to and deeply understand the latest developments in the TCM industry and intelligent medical technology and update their knowledge structure and teaching philosophy. Teachers should be encouraged to integrate the actual needs of industry into teaching content, design more practical and forward-looking courses, and improve the quality and practicality of teaching.

Hypotheses

As the core element of the specialty, the teacher is the key factor in talent training in universities and colleges, whose teaching level directly affects the quality of the training [14–16]. The degree of construction of the teaching staff affects the overall level of specialty development [17, 18]. In the sustainable development of specialty construction in colleges and universities, famous professors and outstanding young teachers are among the decisive factors [19, 20]. The requirements for the construction of national first-class undergraduate major courses suggest that the construction of world-class major courses should be based on the construction of the teaching team [21]. In top-level system documents, specialty construction usually involves six aspects: the professional objectives and positioning, course teaching, teaching staff, supporting conditions, quality assurance, and student development [20, 22]. As domestic first-class undergraduate major courses emphasize student-centered concepts, industrial orientation, continuous reform, and a strong faculty have become requirements that domestic universities need to meet when undertaking the construction of first-class undergraduate major courses [15, 19, 23]. Therefore, in this study the following hypothesis is put forward:

H1

Teaching competence positively affects specialty construction.

The specialty is the specialized field of knowledge based on the subject and adapted to the social profession [22]. If the specialty does not set the employment direction entirely in accordance with the social and vocational needs and highlight the focus of teaching, the subject curriculum will be unreasonable and result in students with relatively weak professional abilities [23–26]. Studies have shown that lecturers are more likely to introduce content related to research work, and information about other fields such as career development is less common [13]. Specialty construction should not only include theoretical education and scientific research but also design courses to cultivate career ability, reform the mode of teaching, and increase training in job competencies [27]. Moreover, strengthening practical teaching in order to enhance students’ vocational skills via multiple routes is greatly necessary [19, 28]. In specialty construction for undergraduates, it is also emphasized that the specialty should be oriented toward the needs of industry [29], deep cooperation between schools, government, and enterprises should be implemented, and cross-platform systems should be developed to improve students’ vocational skills [30–32]. According to the abovementioned theories and research, in this study the following hypothesis is put forward:

H2

Career development plays a mediating role in the effect of teaching competence on specialty construction.

Specialty construction is inseparable from the needs of industrial development. Industry should be closely involved in the process of training talents [33]. Some studies have found that changes in social occupation and social demand for talents have a profound impact on the scale and structure of major courses in colleges and universities [34, 35]. Specialties should meet the external needs of industrial transformation [36], deepen the reform of specialty construction according to social demand, and promote compatibility between talent training and the social economy [37, 38]. They should also improve students’ career development ability in terms of vocational skills and vocational information, offer information on industrial transformation, and provide cooperation platforms and practical projects. Therefore, in this study the following hypothesis is put forward:

H3

Career development and industrial orientation play chain mediating roles in the effect of teaching competence on specialty construction.

Methods

Sampling methods

Two-stage sampling with unequal probability was conducted to determine the scope of the investigation and distribute self-administered questionnaires in the fieldwork. The specific sampling process was as follows: colleges and universities of Chinese medicine with major courses in intelligent medicine were selected as the sample population. In the first stage, a stratified sampling method was used. The sample population was divided into four layers according to the types of colleges and universities. Five colleges and universities were finally selected in accordance with the proportion in each layer. In the second stage, population proportional sampling was used. A range of random numbers was determined according to the total number of teachers in each layer of colleges and universities. The random numbers were generated by a computer. When more than two random numbers corresponded to the same college or university, a random number was generated again. The probabilities of being selected were not equal but were proportional to the numbers of students enrolled. A total of 43 questionnaires were issued and collected in this survey. One invalid questionnaire was excluded, and 42 valid replies were collected, which represented an effectiveness rate of 97.7%.

Theoretical framework

Grounded theory is a qualitative research methodology that establishes theories from empirical data and emphasizes the gradual development of theoretical frameworks via induction in the process of systematic data collection and analysis [39]. This methodology is particularly suitable for exploring emerging fields or complex phenomena, such as the professional construction of intelligent medicine. Its core concept is the construction of theories from bottom to top; that is, letting theories emerge naturally from rich practical data.

Under the guidance of grounded theory, various methods such as literature review and field investigation were used to collect data, and the core categories in the construction of major courses in intelligent medicine in colleges and universities of TCM were preliminarily extracted. These included, but were not limited to, intelligent medical technology, medical knowledge systems, modes of talent training, clinical application scenarios, ethical and legal frameworks, and industry–university research cooperation mechanisms. These categories constituted the basic framework for the subsequent analysis. When constructing a theoretical model of the construction of major courses in intelligent medicine in colleges of TCM guided by industrial demand, the selection of four variables, namely, teacher ability, career development, industry positioning, and professional construction, is based on their importance and interdependence in promoting the integration of education in TCM and intelligent medicine and meeting industrial demand.

We believe that these four variables are interdependent and complementary elements. Among these, teacher ability is the foundation, career development is the driving force, industry positioning is the direction, and professional construction is the core. By strengthening the building of teachers’ capacity, promoting their professional development, achieving close integration with the needs of industry, deepening the construction of specialty, and constructing a framework for the construction of major courses in intelligent medicine in colleges of TCM guided by the needs of industry, this framework will provide strong guarantees and support for the sustainable development of intelligent medicine in TCM.

Four latent variables, namely, teaching competence (TC), career development (CD), industrial orientation (IO), and specialty construction (SC), were extracted. In this survey a questionnaire based on these latent variables was designed. Each observable variable was measured on a seven-level Likert scale ranging from “1” (strongly disagree) to “7” (strongly agree). The specific model indicators and variables are listed in Table 1. Statistical analysis

SPSS 26.0 was used for reliability analysis and descriptive statistics. Python was used for visualization of results. SmartPLS 3.0 was used to measure indirect effects between the latent variables and model path coefficients by applying the PLS algorithm. The bootstrapping method was used to repeat the sampling 5000 times to measure the significance of each value.

Standardized path coefficients between latent variables

Partial least squares structural equation modeling (PSL-SEM), which requires fewer samples than covariance-based structural equation modeling, can deal with multifaceted complex structural models, reflective indicators, and formative indicators simultaneously. Its advantages are reflected not only in its adaptability to small-sample data but also in its performance in dealing with complex models, and it exhibits more robustness in the cleaning of data with non-normal distributions and assumptions of nonlinearity. Moreover, PLS-SEM is suitable for theoretical exploration and prediction.

In this study, the PLS algorithm was used to estimate the standardized path coefficients and indirect effects between latent variables. The bootstrapping method was used to test the significance of the estimates by repeating the sampling 5000 times [40]. Discussion

Determine the objectives of talent training on the basis of industrial demand

Intelligent medicine projects are becoming a leading force in the development of innovation-driven sanitation and wellness businesses. The construction of intelligent medicine specialties in colleges and universities of Chinese medicine should be fully combined with the dominant disciplines of Chinese medicine and reflect the characteristics and advantages of Chinese medicine so as to encourage students to compete in different areas and highlight their competitive edge under the increasingly severe pressures of employment. Industrial orientation positively cuts across the construction of specialties. It is of great significance to establish objectives for talent training based on industrial needs to improve the effect of the training of professional talents and promote the development of social industries [41]. For talent training, it is necessary to combine the current statuses of artificial intelligence and medicine development to set up objectives for talent training. In the first place, talent training should focus on industrial development and review the industry chain of intelligent medicine in TCM across the board to build a professional talent training system for intelligent medicine that integrates tracking, serving, and promoting industrial development. Therefore, the leaders of colleges and universities, managers, and teachers in the field of intelligent medicine should actively participate in enterprise-related research, clarify the developmental statuses and future trends of enterprises, understand the requirements for talents, and set goals for talent training based on the abovementioned information. Secondly, the features of intelligent medicine enterprises should be combined to determine the objectives of talent training. The goal of talent training is to serve local economic development. It is greatly necessary to integrate the advantages of talent training and the developmental characteristics of intelligent medicine enterprises in the region to determine the key points among the objectives of talent training and ensure that trained talents can optimally meet the developmental demands of industry.

Establish a professional curriculum system in combination with industrial development

Career development and industrial orientation play chain mediating roles in the effect of teaching competence on specialty construction. The establishment of a professional curriculum system should be combined with industrial development to ensure the conformity of the course content to industrial development. Firstly, it is essential to clearly understand the tasks and jobs in the industry chain of intelligent medicine to better determine the content of professional courses in intelligent medicine in universities of TCM. For instance, the generalization of multimodal AI has become a future trend since the era of large models began. The industry chain of intelligent medicine comprises multiple links, such as data resources, artificial intelligence platforms, and medical applications. Therefore, the curriculum of an intelligent medicine specialty should include many aspects such as algorithm design with features of TCM and medical data mining, which will help students to have a deeper understanding of the operation of the entire industry [42] and to be better involved in work in the future. Secondly, in the process of construction of a professional curriculum, we should not only focus on cultivating students’ professional ability but also improve their cooperation ability, communication ability, professional ethics, etc., which are of great significance for better enhancing students’ career competitiveness [43]. Intelligent medicine enterprises pay more attention to excellent interdisciplinary talents. They not only require employees to master professional skills but also attach importance to business and management skills. Therefore, colleges and universities should focus on constructing a comprehensive curriculum system for all-round training.

Introduce innovations in teaching modes and cultivate students’ advanced abilities

Teaching competence positively affects specialty construction. Focusing on innovation in terms of teaching modes and cultivating students’ advanced abilities are of great importance in the process of talent training in intelligent medicine specialties. In the first place, teachers should introduce information technology into courses in intelligent medicine specialties and provide teaching guidance by means of task-driven teaching, flipped classroom teaching, and online–offline mixed teaching so as to enhance students’ learning efficiency and transform theoretical knowledge into practical capability [5]. In the second place, the construction of practical courses should be strengthened. Intelligent medicine specialties in colleges and universities of TCM should actively cooperate with relevant enterprises. Moreover, teachers should lead students to participate in product design and processing in enterprises to improve their professional theoretical learning and practical experience. In the third place, a diversified and collaborative model of talent training should be set up. Intelligent medicine specialties should collaborate with relevant enterprises, build research and development teams, offer platforms, and promote the transformation of theoretical knowledge into practical ability [44]. This can not only help to improve students’ practical experience, innovation skills, analytical ability, and academic capability but also contribute to promoting continuous improvements in the technical level of enterprises and economic benefits. The traditional single-talent training mode should be reformed to exercise students’ advanced abilities by implementing the abovementioned measures, which are of great significance for the objectives of talent training in intelligent medicine specialties.

Improve the evaluation system and produce outstanding talents

It is crucial to build a systematic evaluation mechanism. Firstly, the objectives of talent training should be evaluated. On the one hand, professional leaders and enterprise experts should be actively organized to evaluate the training objectives, and the results of the evaluation should be collected and sorted as an important basis for subsequent revision of the objectives and plans of talent training by means of questionnaire surveys [45]. On the other hand, teachers should evaluate all students by means of questionnaire surveys, exam results, practice assessment, etc., to understand the improvement in their theoretical knowledge learning and practical ability and identify gaps and fill them in in a timely manner. Secondly, the curriculum in intelligent medicine specialties should be evaluated. As the core of all evaluation work, curriculum evaluation can be mainly performed from the contents of exams, student achievements, and the rate at which standards are reached, giving each assessment appropriate weight and finally obtaining a more objective and reasonable result of the evaluation. Moreover, other evaluation activities, such as teacher–student assessments, can be carried out. In communication between teachers and students, it is essential to understand the achievements and shortcomings of teaching, which will promote improvements in teaching competence.

Conclusions

This study conducted research on teachers engaged in teaching intelligent medicine, constructed a partial least squares structural equation model of the construction of major courses in intelligent medicine in colleges and universities of TCM guided by industrial demand, and proposed the optimization of the curriculum and teaching content of major courses in intelligent medicine in colleges and universities of TCM. It also advocates orientation toward industrial demand and the strengthening of cooperation and communication between schools and enterprises and provides specific and realistic guidance for the construction of major courses in intelligent medicine in colleges and universities of TCM. It will help to achieve seamless integration between talent cultivation and industrial demand, improve students’ employment competitiveness and adaptability, enrich and improve the disciplinary system of intelligent medicine in TCM, and promote interdisciplinary integration and innovative development. In the future, we can consider the introduction of more courses and practical activities related to intelligent technology. At the same time, we will strengthen cooperation with enterprises, establish a model of talent training that combines industry, academia, research, and application, and cultivate interdisciplinary talents who understand both TCM and intelligent technology.
