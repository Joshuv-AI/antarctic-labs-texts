Publisher Correction: Exosome-mediated Piezo1 activation in 3D-printed titanium scaffolds promotes repair of femoral head osteonecrosis
Authors: Yang Yu, Zhongyin Ji, Hongjun Xu, Mingyang Ma, Zhaojing Yin, Yiyang Du, Tao Zhang, Guiguan Wang, Feng Tian, Sen Liu, Jiajia Xue, Wenwei Qian
Published: 2026

Publisher Correction: Journal of Nanobiotechnology (2026) 24:472

In this article Fig. 9 appeared incorrectly as a duplicate of Fig. 8, due to a typesetting mistake. For completeness and transparency, the old incorrect and correct versions are displayed below.

Incorrect Fig. 9.

Correct Fig. 9.

Behavioral assessment of functional recovery following P-Exos@HAMA/Ti treatment in rat ONFH model. (a–b) Schematics of gait analysis and grip-strength test setup used for motor function evaluation. (c) Representative hindlimb gait trajectories at 8 weeks, showing restored stride coordination in the P-Exos@HAMA/Ti group. (d–f) Quantitative analysis of stride length, stance width, and forelimb weight support demonstrating significant improvement in locomotor symmetry and balance, consistent with structural bone repair (n = 6). (g) Hindlimb grip strength markedly increased in P-Exos@HAMA/ Ti-treated rats, indicating enhanced neuromuscular recovery at 8 weeks post-treatment (n = 3). (h) Time-resolved paw contact area profiles for left (LH) and right (RH) hind paws at 8 weeks, showing reduced bilateral asymmetry and smoother weight transfer in the P-Exos@HAMA/Ti group. I: Control; II: Ti;III: Exos@HAMA/Ti; IV: P-Exos@HAMA/Ti; V: Normal. Data are presented as mean ± SD; statistical significance was calculated using a two-tailed t-test; ns, not significant; *p < 0.05, **p < 0.01, ***p < 0.001, ****p < 0.0001

The original article has been corrected.
