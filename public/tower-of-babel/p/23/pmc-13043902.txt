Correction to: Gestation length both shapes and is shaped by other life history traits in terrestrial eutherian mammals
Published: 2026

This is a correction to: Thodoris Danis, Dana Lin, Daniel S. Caetano, Gregory F. Funston, Antonis Rokas, Gestation length both shapes and is shaped by other life history traits in terrestrial eutherian mammals, Evolution Letters, Volume 9, Issue 6, December 2025, Pages 628–636, https://doi.org/10.1093/evlett/qraf028.

In the originally published paper, a non-existent reference was erroneously included, and two references were cited with incorrect author information. The following changes have been made to the article.

Reference and in-text citation to: “Fitzpatrick, J.L. 2015 citation (Marine mammal adaptations to an aquatic lifestyle and its impact on life history traits. Marine Ecology Progress Series, 533, 191–203)” have been deleted.

Two references and their corresponding in-text citations are corrected.

Reference: “Kenagy J., Feldhamer L. C., Drickamer S. H., Vessey J. F, Merritt C. (2015). Mammalogy: Adaptation, diversity, ecology (4th ed.). Johns Hopkins University Press” is replaced with: “Feldhamer, G. A., Drickamer, L. C., Vessey, S. H., Merritt, J. F., Krajewski, C. (2015). Mammalogy: Adaptation, diversity, ecology (4th ed.). Johns Hopkins University Press.”

Reference: “Kenagy J., George A., Feldhamer L. C., Drickamer S. H., Vessey J. F., Merritt C. (1973). Reproductive strategies of mammals. Annual Review of Ecology and Systematics, 4, 139–163” has been replaced with: “Weir, B. J., & Rowlands, I. W. (1973). Reproductive strategies of mammals. Annual Review of Ecology, Evolution, and Systematics, 4, 139–163.”
