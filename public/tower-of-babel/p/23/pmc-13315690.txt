Retraction Note: Study on the spatial and temporal evolution characteristics and spatial influencing factors of carbon emission intensity in commercial land
Authors: Nuolan Tian, Lingzhi Tan
Published: 2026

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-11210-8, published online 07 October 2025

Authors have retracted this Article.

Following publication, the Authors have identified that the method used in the study for estimating carbon emissions from commercial land, multiplying regional GDP by unit energy consumption intensity, is incorrect. It approximates total regional carbon emissions and not, commercial land-specific carbon emissions, as intended by the Authors. This error invalidates the subsequent empirical analyses and the claims of this Article.

All Authors agree to this retraction.
