Retraction of: VEGF as a Survival Factor in Ex Vivo Models of Early Diabetic Retinopathy
Published: 2025

Retraction of: “VEGF as a Survival Factor in Ex Vivo Models of Early Diabetic Retinopathy” by Rosario Amato, Martina Biagioni, Maurizio Cammalleri, Massimo Dal Monte, and Giovanni Casini (Invest Ophthalmol Vis Sci. 2016;57(7):3066–3076), https://doi.org/10.1167/iovs.16-19285.

Upon receiving a complaint of image manipulation, the editors reviewed Figures 1 and 7 in this paper. The authors reused the DAPI images from Figure 1 to represent a different condition in Figure 7 (in one case mirror imaging), while overlaying different caspase-3 labeling on top of those same images. This represents clear image manipulation, in violation of the ARVO Policy on Publication Ethics, and undermines the credibility of the conclusions in the article. Consequently, the article is hereby retracted. It should further be noted that one of the images in this paper was later included (in part) in another publication (Amato R, Giannaccini M, Dal Monte M, et al. Association of the somatostatin analog octreotide with magnetic nanoparticles for intraocular delivery: a possible approach for the treatment of diabetic retinopathy. Front Bioeng Biotechnol. 2020;8:144) and purported to represent a different condition. Although decisions about that publication fall under the purview of the editorial board of that journal, the fact that it includes manipulated material from within this IOVS article is important to mention.
