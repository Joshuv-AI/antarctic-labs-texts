Author Correction: Cellular senescence in white matter microglia is induced during ageing in mice and exacerbates the neuroinflammatory phenotype
Authors: Tatsuyuki Matsudaira, Sosuke Nakano, Yusuke Konishi, Shimpei Kawamoto, Ken Uemura, Tamae Kondo, Koki Sakurai, Takaaki Ozawa, Takatoshi Hikida, Okiru Komine, Koji Yamanaka, Yuki Fujita, Toshihide Yamashita, Tomonori Matsumoto, Eiji Hara
Published: 2025

Correction to: Communications Biology 10.1038/s42003-023-05027-2, published online 23 June 2023.

In the version of the article initially published, in Fig. 2h, the “Iba1” images for “Old” and “Young” were switched and have now been corrected in the HTML and PDF versions of the article. In Supplementary Data 1, the wrong source data was provided for Fig. 4a. The amended Supplementary Data 1 is now available alongside the online version of the article.
