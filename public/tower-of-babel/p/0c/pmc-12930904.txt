Retraction Note: Non-tuberculous mycobacterial infections among pulmonary tuberculosis suspected and confirmed patients in Ethiopia - A systematic review and meta analyses
Authors: Deresse Daka, Belay Tessema, Awelani Mutshembele, Amir Alelign, Wubert Birhan, Baye Gelaw
Published: 2026

Retraction Note: BMC Infectious Diseases (2025) 25:1078

10.1186/s12879-025-11497-y

The Editors have retracted this article.

Following publication, it came to the Editor’s attention that this article shared significant similarity with another article [1] published by the same authors. This article is therefore redundant.

Deresse Daka stated that all authors agree with this retraction.
