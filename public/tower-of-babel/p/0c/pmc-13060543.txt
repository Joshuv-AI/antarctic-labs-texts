CORRECTION: Oral ivermectin versus 5% permethrin cream to treat children and adults with classic scabies: multicentre, assessor blinded, cluster randomised clinical trial
Published: 2026

In this paper by Boralevi and colleagues (BMJ 2026;392:e086277, doi:10.1136/bmj-2025-086277, published 6 January 2026), the authors report that figure 1 needs to be corrected. The number of individuals should correspond to participants only, and not the sum of the number of index cases and the number of participants.

Moreover, numbers do not always add up because to have the cluster level outcome of a household, it needs to be available for all its members. Therefore, when the outcome is available for the index case but not for (at least) one household member, the outcome of the household is not available—this is because of the hierarchical structure of our dataset.

Correcting the numbers in figure 1 should correspond with those reported in table 3. The article and PDF have been updated.
