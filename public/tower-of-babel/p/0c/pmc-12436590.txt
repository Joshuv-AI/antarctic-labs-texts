Retraction Note: Fe3O4@void@C-Schiff-base/Pd yolk-shell nanostructures as an effective and reusable nanocatalyst for Suzuki coupling reaction
Authors: Aliyeh Barzkar, Alireza Salimi Beni, Shahab Parang, Farhang Salahshour
Published: 2025

Retraction of: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-023-46839-w, published online 15 November 2023

The Editors have retracted this Article.

After publication, concerns were raised about the validity of the traces in Figure 3. Upon further investigation, the data was found to be reused in a previously published Article by different Authors, partially describing different conditions1. The data provided by Authors did not sufficiently address the concerns raised. The Editor has lost confidence in the data and conclusions of this Article.

Farhang Salahshour, Alireza Salimi, Shahab Parang and Aliye Barzekar disagree with this retraction.
