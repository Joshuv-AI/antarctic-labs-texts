From the desk of the editor
Authors: Shishir Singh
Published: 2026

With the start of July 2026, we mark the halfway point of the year. With over 700 submissions to the Journal of Conservative Dentistry and Endodontics (JCDE) so far, the editorial desk is full. The editorial board now has the challenging task of navigating an impressive range of scientific papers, which brings both opportunity and immense editorial responsibility.

What stands out in these submissions is the marked emphasis on evidence-based research. As for the reviews, we have received both systematic and narrative reviews that have strengthened the documentation. They also extend across original research articles, clinical trials, case reports, animal studies, and in vitro work. The large volume of high-quality research, both national and international, is appreciated by the editorial team.

This raises a valid question: how many of these submissions actually make it to publication? There are many challenges with intense competition and high stakes. For scientists, the pressure to publish in PubMed- or Scopus-indexed journals is huge, as it assimilates to visibility, institutional recognition, and professional growth.

This phenomenal rise in submissions has prompted the editorial board to launch a new initiative. In forthcoming issues of the JCDE, we will feature “A Guide to Publishing Your Research.” This issue begins with original research, focusing on how to structure a manuscript. With detailed pointers and a simplified flowchart, we aim to simplify the process and empower authors.

As we move forward, we also encourage our readers and contributors to join us through social media channels and upcoming workshops. We believe that collaboration and knowledge sharing are key to advancing the field of conservative dentistry and endodontics.

With this, we thank our contributors, reviewers, and readers for their unwavering support. We look forward to your continued contributions as we navigate the exciting landscape of conservative dentistry and endodontics. Together, let us make JCDE a premier platform for scientific research and innovation.
