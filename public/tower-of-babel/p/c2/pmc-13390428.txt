Correction: Transcriptome-wide dynamics of m6A methylation in ISKNV and Sinopec chuatsi cells infected with ISKNV
Authors: Qijin Miao, Jing Jiang, Siyou Huang, Jie Gao, Qingqing Liu, Rui Zheng, Yiling Kang, Changjun Guo, Jianguo He, Junfeng Xie
Published: 2026

Correction: BMC Genomics 26, 22 (2025)

https://doi.org/ 10.1186/s12864-025-11211-x

Following publication of the original article two errors were reported in the Results section and in Table 3.

In the “Differential m6A modifications between the positive and negative groups” section within the Results the second sentence was corrected.

The original sentence was: The mRNAs of both the positive and negative groups shared 22,587 m6A peaks.

The corrected sentence is: The mRNAs of both the positive and negative groups shared 22,487 m6A peaks.

In Table 3, the content of the Location cell for the mis18a row was ‘LG14’ and has been corrected to ‘14507685.14514041’.

The original article has been updated.
