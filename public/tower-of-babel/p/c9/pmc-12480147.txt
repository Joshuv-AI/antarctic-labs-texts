Trends in the Prevalence Gap of High-risk Drinking between Cities or Provinces, during 2014-2023
Published: 2024

In 2023, the age-standardized prevalence of high-risk drinking among individuals aged ≥19 years was the lowest in Sejong-si (9.3%) and the highest in Gangwon-do (16.9%). The prevalence gap in high-risk drinking between the highest and lowest groups was 7.6%p, which decreased as compared to 10.0%p in 2022 (Figure 1).
