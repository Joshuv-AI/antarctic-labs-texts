Perception of dentist as a patient towards restorative dental treatment; A caries risk assessment study—Case report
Authors: Khalid L. Alrashedi
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
A patient aged around 52 years visited Faculty dental centre, Al Baha University with pain and discomfort in chewing. He was dentist by profession with multiple restoration with a perception of deep caries and further to root canal treatment. He had a complaint of pain or discomfort while chewing, as he was dentist in profession, he had teeth restored due to caries from many years back. On assessment of caries risk, it was observed there was no new incidence of caries which was supportive with his lifestyle changes with disciplined eating and balanced diet with micro and macro nutrients. The patient’s perception was changed later with self-evaluation of caries status. There was cessation of caries or further progress in many teeth which is a good indication for oral and general health.

Introduction

Dental knowledge as a dentist is a significant factor in providing dental care, but when the patient himself as a dentist, sometimes it is challenging or difficult to take the decision in dental treatment. As a dentist it becomes very difficult, as a knowledge plays a barrier role as fear overturns the need for dental treatment.[1] Patients have barriers regarding various factors which can be periodically resolved with prompt counselling with strategic measures with resolving their concerns.[2] The perception of the dentist as a patient regarding the treatment plays a vital role in dental practice, sometime knowledge and fear will have different perception on the treatment plan. The present case report of a dentist as a patient gives a wide range of factors which affects the clinical practice. The anxiety level based on the perception perceived through knowledge and cognitive analysis of various treatments will be challenging in dental practice.

Case Report

A 52-year-old individual dentist male patient visited dental center, faculty of dentistry with a chief complaint with pain in his left lower back region from past two days while chewing. The oral hygiene practice was twice daily with appropriate oral hygiene aids which supported his oral health. Past dental history revealed high caries experience associated with multiple caries restored teeth with two tooth loss. The DMFT score was 12: Decayed (D) = 1, Missing (M) = 1, and Filled (F) = 10 [Figure 1]. He also gave detailed family history of high caries experience and systemic disease associated with diabetes. His diet history was high sugar intake during childhood to adulthood, associated with high caries experience, when the diet history was analyzed, the patient had more frequent consumption of sweets and high carbohydrate consumption daily during his childhood. When one day diet was analyzed, he was with 18 hours food forasting with balanced diet (More portion of protein like egg, chicken, meat and fat like avocado, fish, olive oil-Keto Diet) with no sugar intake with fibrous protein food one meal a day. He briefed his change of diet from past 20 years with incorporation of fibrous, high protein less carbohydrate with no sugar intake. This reflected his systemic health with improved sugar level of maintenance HbA1C of 5mmol/L [Table 1]. The patient is not diabetic, with a height of 165 cm with 64 kgs having an BMI of 23.5 and BMR of 1399 calories per day. Intraoral pH was accessed using pH meter which was alkaline.[34]

Intraoral examination revealed the caries restored 12 years back in molars of all sextants, restored with amalgam and composite restoration. The right lower sextant 46 and 47 were restored with implants. The lower right third molar 48 was restored with root canal treated. The main chief complaint was based on 36, which gave discomfort on chewing which was restored twelve years back [Figure 2]. The X-ray revealed caries under the restored tooth which was a need to replace and with possible clinical observation for further treatment. As the procedure was initiated, to the observation the restoration was fractured vertically, which might be the possible cause for sensitivity or mild pain while chewing. The restoration was removed along with soft caries and further application with calcium oxide, sandwiched with glass ionomer cement. The patient was instructed to take either 500 mg of Paracetamol or 600 mg of Ibuprofen every 6 hours as needed for pain relief. The patient was given appointment for revival checkup for three months. After three months the patient came with no discomfort and with evaluation of X-ray revealed secondary or reparative dentin formation that prevented the deep caries [Figure 3]. This case report signifies the importance of conservative treatment with change of perception of treatment procedures.

Discussion

Dental caries has been a modern world disease which has been prevalent in much of the population this might be due to changes in lifestyle, eating habits, frequent snacking, and instant food.[5] Many literatures support the role of carbohydrates and its role in caries process. The effect of caries process depends on the frequent snacking and duration of these habits with no preventive intervention.[5] The caries process is so rapid, and it is not impediment until there is intervention especially with children. The adults and elderly population are not spared with the spread of caries process,[6] as the cariogenesis spread rapidly from enamel to dentin and further into pulp, but if the cariogenesis is intervened with good environment with good diet with less sugar will influence the cessation of cariogenesis progress from restorative treatment to root canal treatment.

Patients practice intermittent fasting and one meal a day has shown significant sugar control in the blood with a diet history of supportive diet with fibers and protein food has showed a good oral hygiene with no incidence of new dental caries which is supportive by the study has been gentle with oral health.[7] This case report evidences the above statement which is observed significantly with caries cessation. The role of balanced food with supportive oral hygiene practice has signified the importance of lifestyle changes of the patient, the patient as a profession as a dentist had perceived the progression of dental caries with recommended to root canal treatment. He had made lifestyle changes in fear of systemic diseases like diabetes, but it also supported his oral health. His HbA1c was significant which evidenced his sugar control which benefits the mucosal health and cessation of cariogenesis which is significant factors supporting less sugar or glucose level reflecting cessation of cariogenesis.[8] The lifestyle with continuous monitoring of sugar level by the patient, alerts the effectiveness of disciplined eating pattern.[3] It is evidence that diabetes does act as a comorbidity in affecting oral health. Some of the influential factors that stopped the progress to caries might be role of less frequent food intake with intermitted fasting, no sugar intake including maltose with regular water intake.[7] The pH indicates the significant factors for active caries activity and the patient had alkaline,[34] which is not supportive for caries activity.[4] The case supports the concept of prevention is better than cure with disciplined eating pattern with good oral hygiene practices for high caries exposure will cease the caries process.[9]

Patient perception towards dental treatment varies with his knowledge of oral health. The modern world gives a various flatform to gain knowledge from smart phones to AI tools that are supportive to take a specific decision, but it does as some drawbacks and in-depth prospective feedback should be obtained from the specialist. These are the key factors influencing the perception; however, in this case report patient had perception of deep caries since it was restored more than nine years and he was not aware of caries cessation. The reason for cessation might be because of self-disciplined lifestyle changes which was significant factor for good oral health.[10]

Conclusion

The patient who was a dentist had his dental treatment procedures done 12 years back with restoration complained of pain. With perception of root canal treatment. As the present restoration with three months follow up with caries cessation. This might be as explained in this report of his lifestyle changes with healthy disciplined eating. Further research to find the specific evidence for cessation of caries process. As the findings are an awareness message for family dental health for prevention and cessation for caries process which gives emphasis in primary care.

Limitations

As this study was a case and it had too many variables, there is a need to focus on the risk factors with larger sample that helps in studying the qualitative data with quantative data synthesis.
