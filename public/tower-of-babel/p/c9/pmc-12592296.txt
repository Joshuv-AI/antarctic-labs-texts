Editorial Expression of Concern: Chitosan/silk fibroin-based tissue-engineered graft seeded with adipose-derived stem cells enhances nerve regeneration
Authors: Yujun Wei, Kai Gong, Zhenghuan Zheng, Aijun Wang, Qiang Ao, Yandao Gong, Xiufang Zhang
Published: 2025

Editorial Expression of Concern to: Journal of Materials Science: Materials in Medicine (2011) 22:1947–1964 10.1007/s10856-011-4370-z

The Editor-in-Chief is issuing an Editorial Expression of Concern to alert readers that, after the publication of this article, it was brought to the attention of the Publisher that two images in Figure 13 appear to partially overlap, but describe different conditions. The authors could not be contacted by the Publisher for a response to these concerns. Readers are urged to take caution when interpreting the content and conclusions of this article.

The online version of the original article can be found at 10.1007/s10856-011-4370-z.
