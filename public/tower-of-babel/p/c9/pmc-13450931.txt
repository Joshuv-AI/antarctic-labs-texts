From the editor’s desk
Authors: P. Jayanthi
Published: 2026

The field of Oral and Maxillofacial Pathology continues to evolve at an unprecedented pace, driven by remarkable advances in molecular diagnostics, digital pathology, artificial intelligence, precision medicine, and translational research. These innovations are redefining our understanding of oral diseases and enhancing diagnostic accuracy, prognostic assessment, and patient care. In this rapidly advancing scientific landscape, the dissemination of high-quality, evidence-based research remains fundamental to the growth of our specialty. Publishing robust and ethically conducted research not only enriches scientific knowledge but also bridges the gap between laboratory discoveries and clinical applications, ultimately benefiting patients and healthcare professionals worldwide.

The Journal of Oral and Maxillofacial Pathology remains committed to serving as a premier platform for the publication of impactful research, systematic reviews, case studies, and scholarly discussions that advance the discipline. The scope of the journal extends across the broad spectrum of oral and maxillofacial pathology, including oral medicine, head and neck pathology, forensic odontology, molecular pathology, oral microbiology, oral oncology, diagnostic imaging, and emerging interdisciplinary areas. We encourage submissions that demonstrate scientific rigor, innovation, clinical relevance, and methodological excellence, thereby contributing to the continuous advancement of knowledge and fostering academic collaboration across institutions and countries.

As we present this issue, I extend my sincere gratitude to all our authors for entrusting us with their valuable work and for their dedication to scientific inquiry. I also acknowledge the unwavering commitment of our reviewers, whose expertise, constructive critiques, and meticulous evaluations ensure the quality and integrity of the published content. My heartfelt appreciation goes to the members of the Editorial Board for their guidance, support, and steadfast efforts in maintaining the journal’s high standards. Together, your contributions continue to strengthen the journal’s mission of promoting excellence in research, education, and clinical practice within the field of Oral and Maxillofacial Pathology.
