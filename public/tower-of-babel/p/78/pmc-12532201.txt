Author Response: Comment on “Spectrum and Outcome of Acute Kidney Injury in Nonsurgical Cardiac Intensive Care Unit Patients: A Prospective Observational Study”
Authors: Neeraj Saini, Koushik Bhattacharjee
Published: 2025

Dear Editor,

We have carefully read the comments about the article “Spectrum and Outcome of Acute Kidney Injury in Nonsurgical Cardiac Intensive Care Unit Patients: A Prospective Observational Study”.

First, we would like to express our thanks and regards for showing interest in our study and for words of appreciation.

Please accept our comments in response to the following observations:

We admit that the selection of a control group, which is lacking in this study may hamper generalization into clinical practice. But in the real world, it is very difficult to select a control group in cardiac ICU setup of a Tertiary Care Hospital, with high patient turnover and heterogeneous patient population.

We acknowledge the fact about varied patient demographics, comorbidity profiles, and management strategies across different centers and regions in India. We have conducted this single-center study with a relatively short follow-up period of 3 months to understand the snapshot of this unaddressed problem of acute kidney injury (AKI) in this selected population. We have selected a 3-month follow-up to detect progression to CKD, for which persistent renal dysfunction for 3 months is mandatory. Moreover, we are glad to inform that we are planning to conduct a multicentric study across different centers in India with longer duration of follow-up, for a wide knowledge and data analysis of AKI in nonsurgical cardiac ICU settings. We hope that we will be able to publish this multicentric data in the future in this Journal.
