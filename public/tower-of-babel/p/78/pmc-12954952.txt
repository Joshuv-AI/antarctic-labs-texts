Correction: Single-atom Ca nanozyme induces glioma death through Ca2+-overload-enhanced catalytic tumor nanotherapy, ferroptosis and synergistic remodeling of the immune microenvironment
Authors: Zhizhong Jin, Xinqiao Li, Xiaoyu Hou, Jinpeng Hu, Yue Zhuo, Jianghua Shi, Tao Xu, Qi Zhao, Zhitao Jing
Published: 2026

Correction: Journal of Nanobiotechnology (2025) 23:716

10.1186/s12951-025-03798-w.

During a routine review and organization of the raw data, the authors identified an error introduced during figure preparation in Fig. 3C: an additional fluorescence image from the CaCN-treated GSC23 group that was used for quantitative analysis was mistakenly pasted into the LN229 panel. To ensure experimental rigor, the authors repeated the relevant experiments and accordingly updated Fig. 8C. The authors also noted inconsistent labeling in Figs. 1 and 2. Owing to differences in figure-assembly practices among early team members, the LN229 cell line was incorrectly labeled as “LN229-GSC” in certain panels (specifically, Fig. 1E and G, 1 H, and 2B, 2 C). The figures have now been corrected in the original publication. The authors sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused to the readers and the Editorial Office.
