OA20109. The attitudes of parents with preschool and primary school children towards child injury and safety
Authors: B Strukcinskiene, Jonas Jurgaitis, Rasa Grigoliene
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractBackgroundPreschool and primary school children’s injuries to society are extremely significant. This age is a critical stage in children’s development, which forms intellectual, emotional, and social skills. Safety promotion at this age is essential, because child safety is a priority issue for society, and their health and safety issues directly affect the future of society. Recently, injury has been the main cause of death for children in Lithuania. Therefore, preventive initiatives and safety promotion strategies at this age become crucial to reduce mortality and ensure the safety of children. The study aimed to analyze the attitudes of parents of preschool and primary school-aged children towards injury prevention and safety promotion strategies in Lithuania.MethodsThe survey was performed in 2023-2024, and parents/caregivers of preschool and primary school children were investigated in Lithuania. A total of 309 respondents participated in the study. These were parents/caregivers of children aged 1 to 10 years. The Chi square criterion was applied. The difference in data was considered statistically significant when p < 0.05.ResultsThe study revealed that about 75% of parents/caregivers believe that accidental injuries have a significant impact on children. Only half of the respondents believe that injuries are preventable. Parents/caregivers mentioned that the most common type of accidental injury in children was falls. They believe that the most common causes of death in children in Lithuania are diseases, and boys and girls are equally likely to get injured while participating in the same activity. Parents/caregivers agree that they need to educate children about injury prevention and safety. However, only one-third of adults are very concerned about their children’s accidental injuries.ConclusionsParents do not have enough knowledge about childhood injuries. More attention should be paid to their education about child safety promotion.Key messages• Parents do not have sufficient knowledge about child injuries.• More educational activities about child injuries, injury prevention, and safety promotion need to be organized for both parents and children.TopicChildren, Injury, Safety.
