Published: 2026

Correction to: Cell & Bioscience (2026) 16:47 10.1186/s13578-026-01556-5

These corrections are limited to figure labeling and formatting issues and do not affect the results, interpretation, or conclusions of the study.

The supplementary file has been updated.

The original article has been corrected.

A Subcellular localization of SRSF3 and UPF1 in HeLa cells. α-tubulin and Histone H3 are markers for the cytoplasmic and chromatin-associated fractions, respectively. D Coimmunofluorescence (co-IF) of UPF1 and SRSF3 after treatment with transcription or translation inhibitors, which are actinomycin D (ActD) and flavopiridol (Flavo), or cycloheximide (CHX), respectively. Biogenesis of p53 mRNAs and functions of p53 protein isoforms. The scheme represents the role of a newly identified intron-retained p53 transcript (p53-IR transcript) in preventing the generation of the C-terminal truncated and pro-metastatic p53β protein.
