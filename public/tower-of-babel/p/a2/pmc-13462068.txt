Pre-ICU Navigator: Streamlining Patient Assessment for Optimal ICU Care -Digital Health App
Authors: Ramkumar, HS Mohan, Laya Mahadevan
Published: 2026

ABSTRACT
AbstractIntroductionEfficient and thorough pre-ICU assessment is crucial for timely and appropriate critical care delivery. Manual data collection methods are often inconsistent and time-consuming, potentially causing delays in ICU admission decisions.Aim and objectivesTo evaluate the effectiveness of the Pre-ICU Navigator mobile application in improving workflow efficiency, enhancing the accuracy of pre-ICU assessments, and predicting ICU admission outcomes using validated statistical methods.MethodsA prospective observational study was conducted from October 2024 to March 2025 in a tertiary ICU involving 100 consecutive patients assessed for ICU admission. The Pre-ICU Navigator app was utilized to document patient status, comorbidities, physiological parameters, and decision making notes by ICU admitting doctors and nurses. Workflow times were recorded for a subset of 30 patients using both manual and app-based methods. Statistical analyses included paired t tests for workflow time, regression analyses for predictive variables, and ROC curve analysis for diagnostic accuracy. Data analysis was performed using SPSS v25 and Python's SciKit-learn.ResultsThe Pre-ICU Navigator significantly reduced assessment time, standardizing data collection while improving clinical decision-making. The paired t-test showed a statistically significant improvement in workflow efficiency. Regression analysis identified key clinical parameters influencing ICU outcomes. ROC curve analysis demonstrated the diagnostic accuracy of app integrated variables for predicting ICU intervention needs.Summary and conclusionThe implementation of the Pre-ICU Navigator app leads to a significant reduction in workflow time and enhances predictive accuracy for ICU requirements. It serves as a valuable adjunct in pre-ICU assessments, improving efficiency and supporting evidence-based decision-making. Further large-scale validation is warranted to confirm its efficacy across broader clinical settings.Keywords: Pre-ICU Assessment, Mobile Application (or Digital Health), ICU Triage, Workflow Efficiency, Critical Care
