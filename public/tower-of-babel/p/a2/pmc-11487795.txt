Following publication of the original article [1], the authors would like to correct the ethics approval and consent to participate statement and the ethical consideration. The changes have been highlighted in bold typeface

The incorrect ethics approval and consent to participate statement is:

Ethical approval of this study was granted by the Institutional Review Board Faculty of Nursing, Mahidol University (IRB-NS2020/42.3010), and the Institutional Review Board Faculty of Medicine, Chulalongkorn University, Thailand (IRB No. 099/64). Written informed consent was obtained from all study participants. All procedures were performed in accordance with ethical guidelines and regulations.

The correct ethics approval and consent to participate statement is:

Ethical approval of this study was granted by the Institutional Review Board Faculty of Nursing, Mahidol University (COA No. IRB-NS2020/583.1812), and the Institutional Review Board Faculty of Medicine, Chulalongkorn University, Thailand (COA No. 310/2021). Written informed consent was obtained from all study participants. All procedures were performed in accordance with ethical guidelines and regulations.

The incorrect ethical consideration is:

Ethical approval was granted by the Institutional Review Board Faculty of Nursing, Mahidol University (IRB-NS2020/42.3010), and the Institutional Review Board Faculty of Medicine, Chulalongkorn University, Thailand (IRB No. 099/64).

The correct ethical consideration is:

Ethical approval of this study was granted by the Institutional Review Board Faculty of Nursing, Mahidol University (COA No. IRB-NS2020/583.1812), and the Institutional Review Board Faculty of Medicine, Chulalongkorn University, Thailand (COA No. 310/2021). Written informed consent was obtained from all study participants. All procedures were performed in accordance with ethical guidelines and regulations.

The original article [1] has been corrected.
