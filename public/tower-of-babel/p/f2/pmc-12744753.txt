Background and aims

Primary obesity surgery endoluminal (POSE) is an incisionless endoscopic weight loss procedure.1 It involves placing full-thickness plications primarily in the fundus of the stomach.1,2 Different modifications have emerged since then, such as POSE 2.0, where plications are placed in the gastric body instead.3,4

Mega-POSE is a novel modification to the POSE procedure. Mega-POSE plicates the gastric body by using double-helical graspers in order to plicate both the posterior and anterior walls of the stomach. In Video 1 (available online at www.videogie.org), we illustrate the steps of the mega-POSE modification for treating obesity.

The aim of this procedure is to restrict the stomach by narrowing the anteroposterior diameter and shortening the vertical length. It also aims to reduce the number of plications needed, which makes the procedure safer, more efficient, and economically and environmentally friendly compared with previous techniques.

Methods

The incisionless operating platform (Endomanga; USGI Medical, San Clemente, Calif, USA) is used in mega-POSE. The device is made of 4 main components:1)An 18-mm flexible transport (TransPort; USGI Medical) with 2 wheels allowing it to deflect the tip in 4 directions. It also contains 3 working channels with locks that are used for accessories as well as an extra side port to insert an ultraslim gastroscope (EG-740N; Fujifilm, Tokyo, Japan).2)A full-thickness rotatable tissue proximation device (g-Prox EZ; USGI Medical). Each jaw is 3.3 cm in length, which allows it to grasp large amounts of tissue.3)Two flexible helical graspers (g-Lix; USGI Medical) used to anchor and position tissue inside the tissue proximation device.4)A hollow needle with snowshoe tissue anchors (g-Cath EZ; USGI Medical).

The procedure is performed with the patient in supine position under general anesthesia. A diagnostic gastroscopy is performed to assess the stomach and exclude any pathology. Prophylactic antibiotics are given prior to the procedure.

The transport will be inserted into the mouth and advanced to the stomach with an ultraslim gastroscope loaded in it. A laparoscopic CO2 insufflator (EVA15; Palliare, Oceanside, Calif, USA) is then connected to the transport to distend the stomach. The CO2 insufflator is set at 10 to 12 mm Hg to avoid excessive distention and pneumoperitoneum. The accessories are then advanced through the working channels, with the tissue proximation device in the middle and the 2 helical graspers in the right and left channels.

Using the grasper loaded in the left channel, we rotate it onto the tissue on the posterior side of the stomach, and with the scope operator aspirating from the scope, we carefully pull the tissue into the posterior jaw of the tissue proximation device, leaving the device jaws open. When the jaws are fully retracted, we flip the tool translation lock on the transport handle to maintain the position of the first grasper; then, using the grasper loaded in the right channel, we rotate it onto the tissue on the anterior side of the stomach and carefully pull the tissue with the second grasper into the jaws of the proximation device. We then fully close the tissue proximation device handle to grasp both folds of tissue with the jaws. After the first plication is placed, we initiate placement of the second anchor pair by repeating the steps described, working proximally along the greater curvature until the level of the distal fundus is reached.

Moreover, additional vertical plications can be placed in the posterior and anterior sides if more restriction of the stomach is needed. These plications can be placed using either a double- or single-grasping tool.

Conclusion

Mega-POSE is a novel modification for the gastric remodeling procedure, POSE 2.0, leading to significant weight loss while being more efficient, reproducible, and environmentally friendly.
