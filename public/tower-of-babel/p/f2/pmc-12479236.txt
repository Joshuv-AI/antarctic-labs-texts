KJR Honors Distinguished Reviewers for 2025
Authors: Seong Ho Park
Published: 2025

A longstanding tradition at the annual Korean Congress of Radiology (KCR) is the presentation of the Editor’s Recognition Award for Distinguished Reviewers. This award is given to select reviewers of the Korean Journal of Radiology (KJR) who have demonstrated exceptional performance in their reviews.

The Editor’s Recognition Award for Distinguished Reviewers is reserved for an elite group, representing approximately 10% of all reviewers. These individuals are recognized for their high-quality and timely scholarly reviews, as well as the significant number of manuscripts they have assessed. This year, 46 reviewers (including those who are on the editorial board) have been selected as award recipients based on their activities between August 2024 and July 2025. Their names and corresponding ORCIDs are listed below.

In addition to honoring these distinguished reviewers, the editors of KJR extend their heartfelt gratitude to all of our reviewers and deeply appreciate their invaluable contributions.
