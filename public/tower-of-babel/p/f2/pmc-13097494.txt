ABSTRACT
Abstract Li‐Ion Batteries Si‐based anodes can increase the energy density of Li ion batteries but require re‐formation of solid electrolyte interphase (SEI) via reduction reactions, which consumes active Li, thus capacity. Interestingly, some apparently "dead" SEI species can cross over to the cathode and recover the apparently lost active Li via oxidation reactions.
