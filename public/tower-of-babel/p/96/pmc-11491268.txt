WFITN Activities for Diversity and Inclusion in Neuroendovascular Treatment
Authors: Michihiro Tanaka
Published: 2024

Diversity and inclusion are pivotal in interventional neuroradiology (INR), and the World Federation of Interventional and Therapeutic Neuroradiology (WFITN) leads this initiative. With a focus on educational programs, scholarships, mentorship, and representation in leadership, WFITN actively enhances women’s participation and diversity in INR. The Mentorship Program for Women in Neurointervention, which connects trainees with seasoned INR practitioners, and the Women’s Observership Grant, which offers financial support for educational experiences, are prime examples of WFITN’s dedication to fostering inclusivity.

The following article by Suzuki reports her overseas experience of neurointervention supported by the WFITN Women’s Observership Grant in detail. It may deepen the reader’s understanding of the worth of those activities.

WFITN’s future aims include broadening representation, enriching initiatives, cultivating an inclusive culture, forging collaborations, and championing research and advocacy. These aims seek to further harness diversity as a strength, better patient care, and advance global health.

The significance of these diversity efforts will be a central topic at the WFITN Congress scheduled for 6–10 October 2024 at the Hilton Midtown in New York. This congress promises to be a conduit for change and progress in INR. Attendees are invited to register for an opportunity to contribute to the evolution of the field, with a promise of an enriched professional experience. Details and registration can be found on the WFITN official website.
