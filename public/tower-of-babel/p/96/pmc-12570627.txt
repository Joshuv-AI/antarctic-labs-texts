Correction: The critical role of methylenetetrahydrofolate dehydrogenases in immune regulation and auto-immunity
Authors: Peter Bunyard, Marina Makrecka-Kuka, Laura McMillan
Published: 2025

Correction to: Bunyard et al. Journal of Inflammation (2025) 22:44

10.1186/s12950-025-00469-3

Following the publication of the original article, the authors reported that the name of the third author, Laura McMillan, was missing and the order of the authors shall be corrected. The authorship as reflected in this Correction is now accurate.

Accordingly, the Authors’ Contributions section has been updated as follows:

Peter Bunyard, Marina Makrecka-Kuka and Laura McMillan contributed equally to the writing and preparation of this manuscript.

The Competing Interests statement has also been revised to ensure accuracy. It now reads:

Peter Bunyard is a senior editor of the Journal of inflammation. He and Marina Makrecka-Kuka were co-guest-editors of the topical collection ‘From theory to therapy; understanding and targeting immunometabolism for the treatment of inflammation and cancer’ published in the Journal of Inflammation. The peer reviewing of this paper was therefore overseen by an independent member of the editorial board while the submission was subject to the exact same review process as any other manuscript submitted to the journal.

Moreover, some inappropriate capital letters introduced during typesetting have been corrected.

The original article has been updated accordingly.
