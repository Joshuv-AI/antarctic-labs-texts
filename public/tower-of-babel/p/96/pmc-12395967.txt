In the originally published version of this article, the authors did not include the required registration in ZooBank as stipulated by Article 8.5.3 of the International Code of Zoological Nomenclature (ICZN, 2012) and therefore did not provide the necessary life science identifiers (LSIDs). The “materials availability” section has now been updated online, and with this correction, Duonychus tsogtbaatari is now available and published in accordance with the ICZN code, effective from the date of this notice.

The authors apologize for the inconvenience and assure that this correction note does not alter the main findings and conclusions of the manuscript.
