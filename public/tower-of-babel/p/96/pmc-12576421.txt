Retraction to: Rehmannioside D mitigates disease progression in rats with experimental-induced diminished ovarian reserve via Forkhead Box O1/KLOTHO axis
Authors: Yan Liang, Huimin Wang, Jin Chen, Lingyan Chen, Xiaoyong Chen
Published: 2025

Retraction to:

Korean J Physiol Pharmacol 2023;27(2):167-176.

Published online March 1, 2023 https://doi.org/10.4196/kjpp.2023.27.2.167

This article has been retracted at the request of the corresponding author.

Following an internal review by the authors, it was determined that the article may contain issues related to academic norms and does not fully comply with the integrity standards upheld by the Korean Journal of Physiology & Pharmacology and the International Committee of Medical Journal Editors (ICMJE) or the International Committee of Publication Ethics (COPE).

The authors have therefore requested the withdrawal of the article due to concerns regarding research integrity and academic seriousness.

The editorial board has reviewed the request and approved the retraction.

This retraction is issued to preserve the accuracy and integrity of the scientific record.

The authors apologize for any inconvenience this may have caused to the journal, editors, and readers.
