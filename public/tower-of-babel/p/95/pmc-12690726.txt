NODG-13 PREDICTIVE VALUE OF MULTIMODAL MULTIPARAMETRIC MRI IN THE HISTOPATHOLOGICAL DIAGNOSIS OF BRAIN TUMORS IN IVORY COAST
Authors: Christian Steve Mahugnon Padonou, Landry Drogba
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
AbstractIntroductionMultimodal MRI coupling with morphological sequences, diffusion, perfusion and spectroscopy, represents an almost essential tool in the management of surgical pathology. Used well, it allows a diagnostic approach of choice, surgical planning and therapeutic monitoring.ObjectiveEstablish the rate of correct diagnosis with the histopathology of radiologists and neurosurgeons based on MRIMaterials and MethodsThis was a cross-sectional and analytical, multicenter retrospective study from February 2017 to October 2024 and prospective from November 2024 to June 2025. Included were all patients operated on for an intracranial expansive process of nature tumor, The first retrospective part consisted of comparing the imaging results with the anatomopathology data and secondly the neurosurgeon’s hypotheses with the conclusions of the anatomopathologyResultsDuring the study we had a study population of 257 patients. The average age was 33.5 +/- 17.5 years, the sex ratio was M/F : 1.78. The supratensorial topography of the lesions was the most common. Intracranial hypertension syndrome, pyramidal syndrome, comitial syndrome, and visual disturbances were the most common. The tumor lesions identified during the study period included 25 gliomas, 19 Astrocytomas, 11 hemangioblastomas, 17 medulloblastomas, 3 Craniopharyngiomas, 156 meningiomas, 13 metastases, and 13 rare tumors. The retrospective study reported an overall positive predictive value of 72.73%, a negative predictive value of 94.12%, a sensitivity of 94% and a specificity of 72%ConclusionMultimodal MRI is effective for brain tumors and should be requested as first intention in the face of any suspicion of an expansive tumor process.IntroductionL’IRM multimodale couplant aux séquences morphologiques, la diffusion, la perfusion et la spectroscopie, représente un outil presqu’incontournable dans la prise en charge de la pathologie chirurgicale.
