Correction: A vibration compensation approach for shipborne atomic gravimeter based on particle swarm optimization
Authors: Zhong-kun Qiao, Zheng-hao Shen, Ruo Hu, Lin-ling Li, Peng Yuan, Guo-chao Wu, Yuan Yuan, Yin Zhou, Bin Wu, Qiang Lin
Published: 2025

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-92544-1, published online 14 March 2025

The original version of this Article contained a repeated error where the core coefficient optimization method “particle swarm optimization” was incorrectly written as “particle swarm organization” in the title,

“A vibration compensation approach for shipborne atomic gravimeter based on particle swarm organization”

now reads:

“A vibration compensation approach for shipborne atomic gravimeter based on particle swarm optimization”

In the Abstract,

“Then, the particle swarm organization is introduced to identify the optimal compensation coefficients.”

now reads:

“Then, the particle swarm optimization is introduced to identify the optimal compensation coefficients.”

The error has been corrected throughout the text in the keywords and Introduction, Discussion and Methods section.

The original Article has been corrected.
