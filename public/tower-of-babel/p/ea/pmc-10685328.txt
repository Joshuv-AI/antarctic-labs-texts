A Novel Improvised Laryngectomy Button Designed From A Milk Bottle Teat
Authors: Yen Min Madeline Chee, Elizabeth Roche, Ci An Gerald Tay
Published: 2023

In a total laryngectomy, a permanent opening (tracheostoma) is created at the base of the neck through which the individual breathes (Supplemental Material S1, available online). Breathing, speech, and swallowing are significantly changed.

Since this is the only passageway for breathing, it is important to maintain its patency. However, tracheostomal stenosis is not uncommon. Maintaining patency is aided using a laryngectomy tube (Supplemental Material S2, available online) or button (Supplemental Material S3, available online). Modern commercially produced laryngectomy tubes or buttons are usually designed from silicon which adjusts well to the shape of the trachea while ensuring comfort when worn. The tip should also be atraumatic to prevent irritation of the mucous membranes in the trachea and facilitate insertion. The laryngectomy tube or button can be held in place with a Velcro strap or clips (Supplemental Material S4, available online).

A Heat‐and‐Moisture‐Exchange (HME) cassette is a plastic device with a foam center fitted over the opening of the laryngectomy tube or button or directly over the tracheostoma (Supplemental Material S5, available online). It filters airborne particles, increases resistance to airflow to improve lung function and increases the temperature and humidity of inspired air.

However, commercially produced tubes are costly and may not always be available to patients. In Singapore, the average retail prices of a laryngectomy tube and laryngectomy button are Singapore Dollar (SGD) 360 (~USD 270) and SGD 400 (~USD 300), respectively. We propose a cost‐effective, easily accessible, and fashioned laryngectomy button alternative designed from a milk bottle teat.

Discussion

Tracheostomal stenosis is not uncommon. 1 Management options include surgical revision or laryngectomy tubes or buttons which are costly and may not always be available to patients. In Singapore, the average retail price of a laryngectomy tube and button are SGD 360 (~USD 270) and SGD 400 (~USD 300), respectively.

This is the first and only description of an improvised laryngectomy button designed from a milk bottle teat. Milk bottle teats are readily available in most countries and are fairly inexpensive (average cost SGD 3 ≈ USD 2.20) especially when compared against commercially available laryngectomy tubes or buttons. Our design is cost‐effective and easily reproducible, inserted and well‐tolerated. Moreover, it can even support the use of an HME cassette without further modifications to the design.

There has only been 1 previous report describing an improvised tracheostoma button from disposable plastic syringes. 2 Although the authors described its use in 2 patients with no complications, the hard polypropylene material of plastic disposable syringes can lead to stomal edge abrasions and granulation tissue in the long term, and cause tracheal or mucous membrane irritation. It is also hard to cut and mold. Silicone or PPSU are much softer pliable materials that are easier to cut and design, and are less likely to cause stomal edge abrasions or tracheal or mucous membrane irritation. These materials are also more durable compared to plastic, waterproof with low chemical reactivity including common cleaning and sterilization agents, and highly heat‐ and damage‐resistant. Silicon is also more environmentally friendly compared to polypropylene as it reverts back into its harmless components (amorphous silica, carbon dioxide, and water vapor) when burned, unlike plastic which releases harmful toxins.

Conclusion

In a developing country or for patients who have cost concerns, an improvised laryngectomy button made from a milk bottle teat is a viable inexpensive, and easily fabricated alternative to commercially available laryngectomy tubes or buttons. Further studies on more subjects can be conducted to assess parameters for use and long‐term efficacy and safety.
