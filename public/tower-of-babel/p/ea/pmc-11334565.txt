Correction: Creating a health informatics data resource for hearing health research
Authors: Nishchay Mehta, Baptiste Briot Ribeyre, Lilia Dimitrov, Louise J English, Colleen Ewart, Antje Heinrich, Nikhil Joshi, Kevin J Munro, Gail Roadknight, Luis Romao, Anne Gm Schilder, Ruth V Spriggs, Ruth Norris, Talisa Ross, George Tilston
Published: 2024

Correction to: Mehta et al. BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making (2024) 24:209

10.1186/s12911-024-02589-x.

Following the publication of the Original Article, the authors reported that some headings of the article were not processed correctly.

On Page 2 of the PDF version, the “National Institute of Health and Social Care Research Health Informatics Collaborative” should have been processed as a heading.

On Page 5 of the PDF version, the “Data extraction, linkage and standardisation, within contributing centres” should have been processed as a heading.

Moreover, on page 6, The GitHub link should read https://uclh-criu.github.io/hic-hearing-health-docs/ rather than https://github.com/uclh-criu/hic-hearing-health-docs.

The Original Article has been corrected.
