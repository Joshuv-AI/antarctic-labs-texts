(The American Journal of Human Genetics 113, 794–808; April 2, 2026)

We recently became aware of an error reporting sample sizes in the methods section, reporting an outdated sample size for CKB and omitting the maximum sample for trans-ethnic meta-analysis. We have fixed these errors by adding the correct values to the methods section. The results and conclusions demonstrated in this work are unaffected. The authors apologize for the error and any inconvenience it may have caused.
