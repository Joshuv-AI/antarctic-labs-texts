Correction: CPX-351 vs. conventional chemotherapy cardiotoxicity in high-risk AML: a post hoc phase III trial analysis
Authors: Joshua D Mitchell, Michael Pfeiffer, John Boehmer, John Gorcsan, Shunsuke Eguchi, Yoshiyuki Orihara, Nalina Dronamraju, Sonja Dhani, Stefan Faderl, Tara L Lin, Geoffrey L Uy, Jeffrey E Lancet, Jorge E Cortes
Published: 2026

Correction: Cardio-Oncology 12, 6 (2026)

https://doi.org/10.1186/s40959-025-00421-7

Following the publication of the original article [1], the terms “CPX-351” and “follow-up” are incorrectly displayed as “CPX351” and “follow up” online in the legends for Figs. 1 and 2. Additionally, the “<“ symbol for “<18%” in footnote d of the Fig. 1 legend is missing. The corrected legends are presented below.

Figure 1 Cardiac impairment at follow-up.

Percentage of patients with (A) LVEF < 53%a, individual LVEF values of patients treated with (B) CPX-351b and (C) 7 + 3c and (D) percentage of patients with GLS < 18%d.

The yellow and red lines represent the average.

a102 patients had baseline LVEF ≥ 53% and were included in the post hoc analysis (CPX-351, n = 57; 7 + 3, n = 45).

bPatients treated with CPX-351 with available echocardiographic assessments at follow-up 1 only (n = 53) or follow-up 1 and 2 (n = 26).

cPatients treated with 7 + 3 with available echocardiographic assessments at follow-up 1 only (n = 43) or follow-up 1 and 2 (n = 20).

dPatients with LVEF ≥ 53% and excluding those with GLS < 18% at baseline (CPX-351, n = 11; 7 + 3, n = 12) from the CPX-351 (n = 46) and 7 + 3 (n = 33) arms.

GLS global longitudinal strain, LVEF left ventricular ejection fraction, n number of patients.

Figure 2 Proportion of patients with baseline LVEF ≥ 53% and ≥ 1 follow-up (CPX-351, n = 57; 7 + 3, n = 45) and with clinically significant changes in LVEF or GLS at follow-up.

Percentage of patients with clinically significant change in (A) LVEFa or (B) GLSb at follow-up 1, follow-up 2, and follow-up 1 and/or 2.

Error bars indicate the 95% CI.

aDefined as > 10% absolute decrease from baseline with LVEF < 53%.

bDefined as > 12% relative decrease from baseline with GLS < 18%.

CI confidence interval, GLS global longitudinal strain, LVEF left ventricular ejection fraction, n number of patients.

The original article [1] has been updated.
