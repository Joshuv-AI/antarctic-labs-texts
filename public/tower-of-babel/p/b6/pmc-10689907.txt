Correction to: Use of machine learning algorithms to predict life-threatening ventricular arrhythmia in sepsis
Published: 2023

This is a correction to: Le Li, Zhuxin Zhang, Likun Zhou, Zhenhao Zhang, Yulong Xiong, Zhao Hu, Yan Yao, Use of machine learning algorithms to predict life-threatening ventricular arrhythmia in sepsis, European Heart Journal - Digital Health, Volume 4, Issue 3, May 2023, Pages 245–253, https://doi.org/10.1093/ehjdh/ztad025

In the originally published version of this manuscript, there was a significant error in Image S1 within the Supplementary Appendix. Specifically, in the flowchart presented in Image S1, the total sample size has been erroneously stated as 13,525 cases, whereas the correct total sample size should be 256,878 cases. The author deeply regrets that inaccurate data was included in the final version of the article. This mistake could potentially confuse readers and compromise the accurate interpretation of the figure's findings.

This error has been corrected online.
