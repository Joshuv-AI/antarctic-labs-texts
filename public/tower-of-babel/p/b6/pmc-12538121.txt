miR-455-3p is Involved in the Pathological Mechanism of Pulpitis by Modulating ACTG1
Authors: Yuejing Xu, Yihan Fu, Lei Zhang
Published: 2025

ABSTRACT
BackgroundPulpitis is an inflammatory condition primarily induced by bacteria. The onset of pulpitis is typically accompanied by severe pain, which seriously affects the life quality of patients. miR-455-3p was identified to be abnormally expressed in multiple conditions and participated in disease progression. Nevertheless, the action of miR-455-3p on pulpitis is unknown.AimTo dig out a novel biomarker for the diagnosis of pulpitis and to elaborate the related molecular mechanisms.MethodsqRT-PCR was employed to examine the levels of miR-455-3p and ACTG1 in serum, tissues and cells. Dual-luciferase reporter assay was utilized to validate the targeting relationship. CCK-8 assay was applied to check cell proliferation. Flow cytometry analysis was designed to monitor apoptosis.ResultsThe miR-455-3p levels were significantly reduced in serum and tissue of pulpitis patients. ROC curve analysis revealed that miR-455-3p had good diagnostic performance for pulpitis. In vitro, miR-455-3p levels were inversely proportional to LPS concentration and treatment time. And LPS treatment suppressed the proliferation. Moreover, up-regulated miR-455-3p facilitated proliferation and repressed apoptosis. Down-regulated miR-455-3p depressed proliferation and induced apoptosis. Mechanistically, ACTG1 is a target gene of miR-455-3p and negatively regulated by miR-455-3p. Upregulating miR-455-3p attenuated the suppression of proliferation and promotion of apoptosis by LPS, which could be further reversed by overexpressing ACTG1.ConclusionmiR-455-3p may serve as a novel diagnostic biomarker for pulpitis. And miR-455-3p could enhance proliferation and restrain apoptosis by negatively regulating ACTG1.

Introduction

The pulp is a loose connective tissue of ectomesenchymal origin, surrounded by mineralized hard tissue and connected to the external space via the apical foramen.1,2 The pulp is organized by various cell types, consisting of mesenchymal cells, immune cells, endothelial cells and stem cells.3 When the pulp tissue is infected by bacteria, it can lead to pulpitis.4 Nevertheless, bacterial infection is not the only causative factor that leads to pulpitis. Other considerations include a chemical insult and traumatic or iatrogenic factors, such as crown or root fractures and trauma caused by dental surgery.4 As its pathogenesis has not been fully elucidated, the management of pulpitis has been a hot research topic. Serum indicators could be applied to monitor the early stages of irreversible pulpitis and aid in clinical diagnosis and treatment.5,6 Currently, extensive research centers on the molecular mechanisms underlying the progression and seeking therapeutic targets of pulpitis in order to reduce morbidity and improve treatment outcomes.7,8

MicroRNAs (miRNAs) are essential regulators of gene expression in numerous biological processes, including epithelial-mesenchymal transition, osteoblast differentiation, immune and inflammatory regulation etc.9, 10, 11 MiRNAs are ∼22 nt endogenous RNAs that could target and cleave mRNAs or repress translation in plants and animals. In pulpitis, vast numbers of miRNAs were discovered to be dysregulated.12 These miRNAs have been proven to be participating in cellular activity, inflammation and oxidative stress in pulpitis, hence they may serve as potential biomarkers for pulpitis.8,13,14 Down-regulated miR-455-3p was detectable in various diseases and facilitated multiple cancer progression involving hepatocellular carcinoma and thyroid cancer.15, 16, 17, 18 Moreover, miR-455-3p participated in modulating osteoblast differentiation, lymphangiogenesis, apoptosis and autophagy by impacting downstream target genes.16,19,20 It is worth noting that miR-455-3p was downregulated in patients with rheumatoid arthritis and may serve as a clinical diagnostic marker for it.15 Hence, we consider that miR-455-3p may be related to inflammatory diseases. More importantly, a bioinformatics analysis showed that miR-455-3p was downregulated in both pulpitis and apical periodontitis.12 However, the specific regulatory mechanism has not yet been studied.

Thus, miR-455-3p was picked as our research subject. The miR-455-3p levels were monitored in pulpitis patients clinically, and its molecular mechanism was investigated in vitro.

Materials and methods

Study object

We enrolled 110 patients who were diagnosed with pulpitis and underwent extraction surgery as the experimental group, and 80 patients who underwent extraction without pulpitis were picked as a control group during 2024-2025. The healthy teeth in the control group were mainly sourced from extracted third molars, as well as teeth extracted for orthodontic treatment or due to trauma. These teeth responded normally to pulp vitality tests, indicating healthy pulp status. Average age of the experimental group was 47.13 ± 6.88 years, which comprised 49 males and 61 females. For the control group, the average age was 46.24 ± 6.73 years, including 33 males and 47 females. Participants in the 2 groups were matched for age and gender. It is notable that we selected our patients by eliminating those who consumed alcohol, smoked and took nonsteroidal drugs or antibiotics. The pulp tissue was removed under sterile conditions and stored in liquid nitrogen. Moreover, fasting peripheral blood was harvested on the morning of the extraction day, centrifuged to separate the serum and kept at -80°C. The trial was validated by the Ethics Committee of The Affiliated Yantai Stomatological Hospital and informed consent was gained from the patients.

Modeling and cell transfections

Human dermal papilla cells (hDPCs) were purchased from Lonza (Lonza Bioscience, Switzerland). Cells were grown in DMEM (ThermoFisher, USA) medium with 10% fetal bovine serum, penicillin-G (100 units/mL), streptomycin (100 µg/mL) at 37°C in 5% CO2. Subsequently, cells were processed with varied concentrations of lipopolysaccharide (LPS) to establish a cell model of pulpitis. The concentrations of LPS were 0, 0.5, 1, and 2 µg/mL. Lipo8000™ Transfection Reagent (Beyotime, China) was employed to transfect miR-455-3p mimic or miR-455-3p inhibitor into the model to up- or down-regulation of miR-455-3p. mimic NC and inhibitor NC were their negative controls, respectively. pcDNA 3.1 (ThermoFisher, USA) was obtained for constructing pcDNA 3.1-ACTG1 (oe-ACTG1), which could up-regulate ACTG1 in cells. oe-NC was its negative control.

qRT-PCR

The total RNA from samples was extracted with TRIzol reagent (ThermoFisher, USA). BeyoFast™ SYBR Green 1-Step qRT-PCR Kit (Beyotime, China) was employed for reverse transcription and fluorescence quantitative PCR, and the reactions were run on a 7500 Fast Real-Time PCR System. U6 and GAPDH are the internal reference genes for miR-455-3p and ACTG1, respectively. The acquired data have been processed by the 2-ΔΔCt.

Dual-luciferase reporter assay

The hDPCs were co-transfected with pmirGLO-ACTG1-wt or pmirGLO-ACTG1-mut (Promega, USA) with miR-455-3p mimic or miR-455-3p inhibitor by Lipo8000™ transfection reagent (Beyotime, China). After 48h, fluorescence activity was checked by Dual-Luciferase Reporter Gene Assay Kit (Beyotime, China) as recommended by manufacturer.

CCK-8 assay

Cell Counting Kit-8 (Beyotime, China) was designed to evaluate cell proliferation. 5×103 cells were seeded into a 96-well plate. Incubate at 37°C for 24, 48 and 72h. Subsequently added 10 µl of CCK-8 solution. After 2h, the absorbance at 450nm was measured using an enzyme meter.

Flow cytometry analysis

The hDPCs were processed with Annexin V-FITC Apoptosis Detection Kit (Solarbio, China) as recommended by manufacturer. FACS Caliber flow cytometer (BD Bioscience) was employed to examine apoptosis.

Statistical analysis

Our datasets were processed using GraphPad Prism 9. Data are displayed as mean ± SD. The ROC curve analysis for evaluating the diagnostic performance of miR-455-3p. Pearson correlation analysis was adopted to estimate the correlation. Variations among 2 sets were detected with Student t-test. Variations within multiple sets were estimated with ANOVA. P < .05 was considered significant.

Results

The expression and clinical diagnostic value of miR-455-3p

Our outcomes discovered that miR-455-3p levels in serum and pulp tissue were distinctly reduced versus control (P < .001, Figure 1A, C). And the results of ROC curve analysis were displayed an AUC of 0.903, Sensitivity of 0.809 and Specificity of 0.825 for serum miR-455-3p (Figure 1B). miR-455-3p of pulp tissue exhibited an AUC of 0.948, Sensitivity of 0.827, and Specificity of 0.963 (Figure 1D). miR-455-3p dysregulation affects proliferation and apoptosis

The findings uncovered that the miR-455-3p levels were negatively related to LPS concentration and induction time (P < .05, P < .01, P < .001; Figure 2A-B). And the cell proliferation was markedly diminished after LPS treatment (P < .001; Figure 2C). Moreover, miR-455-3p mimic significantly elevated the levels of miR-455-3p in LPS-treated hDPCs, whereas miR-455-3p inhibitor had the opposite effect (P < .01, P < .001; Figure 2D). Discussion

Pulpitis is a prevalent inflammatory disease mainly due to bacterial infection, usually accompanied by severe pain, sleep disturbances and anxiety-like symptoms.21,22 Pulpitis can be categorized into reversible and irreversible pulpitis, which involves nerve, vascular and immune responses.1,23 The initial localized inflammation may progress to pulp necrosis without timely therapy.24 Currently, the diagnosis of pulpitis primarily relies on clinical symptoms. Serum indicators could supplement clinical diagnosis and treatment.8 Hence, searching for diagnostic biomarkers is especially vital.

Growing numbers of miRNAs were discovered to be abnormally expressed within pulpitis. For instance, miR-340-5p, miR-30a-5p and miR-128-3p were down-regulated whereas miR-155, miR-21 and miR-142 were up-regulated in pulpitis.13,25,26 Studies have implicated that these miRNAs are engaged in cellular activity, inflammation and oxidative stress.8 Since miR-455-3p was down-regulated in rheumatoid arthritis, we speculated that it was related to inflammatory diseases.15 Moreover, it has demonstrated good diagnostic value for various diseases, including prostate cancer and Alzheimer's disease.27,28 Thus, we hypothesized that it also has diagnostic performance for pulpitis. More importantly, a previous study has shown that miR-455-3p was down-regulated within both apical periodontitis and pulpal inflammation.12 This evidence further confirms our speculation. In this study, we recruited 110 patients with pulpitis and 80 patients undergoing routine tooth extraction. RT-qPCR results showed that the content of miR-455-3p was reduced in the pulp tissue and serum of patients. Furthermore, it demonstrated good efficacy in distinguishing between healthy pulp and inflamed pulp. Hence, we considered that miR-455-3p may serve as a novel diagnostic biomarker for pulpitis. miR-455-3p acts as a key regulator engaged in the progression of multiple diseases.16,17 For example, down-regulated miR-455-3p in osteoporosis could repress osteoblast differentiation and induce pro-osteoblast apoptosis through negative regulation of SMAD2.19 It exacerbated apoptosis and autophagy in acute myeloid leukemia cells by UBN2-mediated PPARα protein expression.16 Additionally, miR-455-3p boosted proliferation and extracellular matrix accumulation by regulating Hdac2 in diabetic nephropathy.29 These results are consistent with our conclusions. We found that miR-455-3p mimic facilitated proliferation and repressed apoptosis while miR-455-3p inhibitor restrained proliferation and induced apoptosis. Accordingly, we speculated that low levels of miR-455-3p may accelerate the progression of pulpitis by promoting apoptosis and inhibiting proliferation.

To explore the molecular mechanism of miR-455-3p, we screened for its target gene ACTG1. ACTG1, also known as γ-actin, is a key component of the cytoskeleton.30 At present, the studies on ACTG1 are mainly concentrated on cancer and hearing loss.31, 32, 33 Previous findings illustrated that ACTG1 is up-regulated in various diseases involving breast cancer and sepsis.34,35 Likewise, we detected overexpressed ACTG1 in the patient's pulp tissues and serum. Additionally, we discovered that upregulation of ACTG1 could partially reverse the enhancement of proliferation and depression of apoptosis resulted from miR-455-3p mimic. Our results are compatible with the suppression of apoptosis by inhibiting ACTG1 in a sepsis model in vitro.35 The outcomes highlighted the potential of miR-455-3p and ACTG1 as future targets for pulpitis treatment. However, there is still a long way to go from basic experiments to clinical practice. Currently, exosomes are attracting widespread attention as delivery vehicles for bioactive molecules, including miRNAs.36,37 We considered that combining miRNA and exosomes for the treatment of pulpitis was a key step in clinical translation. There are certainly some drawbacks in our work like the sample size is not large enough. The next step will be to pursue the study in more patients with pulpitis. Moreover, the in vitro findings will be further validated in vivo.

Overall, our outcomes indicated that miR-455-3p has good diagnostic performance for pulpitis. It is expected to become a non-invasive biomarker for pulpitis diagnosis in future clinical practice. Mechanistically, miR-455-3p facilitates proliferation and suppresses apoptosis of LPS-exposed cells by negatively regulating ACTG1. It provides the therapeutic targets for future treatment of pulpitis.
