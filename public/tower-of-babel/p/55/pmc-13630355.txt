24th Indian Society of Periodontology and Implantology Mid-Year Convention

The 24th Indian Society of Periodontology and Implantology (ISPI) Mid-Year Convention 2026 stood as a remarkable testament to the academic excellence, unity, and progressive vision of the Indian Society of Periodontology. This prestigious gathering brought together the brightest minds in the field, fostering an environment of learning, collaboration, and professional growth. The convention was graced by the esteemed presence of all office bearers of the ISPI Head Office, whose leadership and commitment continue to elevate the standards of periodontology across the nation. Their participation added immense value to the event, inspiring both seasoned clinicians and budding postgraduates alike.

The convention witnessed an impressive participation of nearly 1300 delegates from all parts of the country. The diversity of attendees contributed to rich discussions, networking opportunities, and the exchange of ideas that will undoubtedly benefit the profession as a whole. Renowned national experts and distinguished speakers delivered insightful lectures and scientific sessions, covering a wide spectrum of contemporary topics in periodontology and implantology. These sessions not only showcased the latest advancements in the field but also emphasized evidence-based clinical practices, innovation, and interdisciplinary approaches.

The convention also provided an excellent platform for academic exchange through paper and poster presentations. Delegates from across the country actively participated, presenting their research, clinical experiences, and innovative ideas. Adding further depth to the academic experience were the preconvention courses, which received an overwhelming response. These courses focused on emerging and advanced topics in periodontology and implantology, offering participants both theoretical insights and hands-on training. The combination of lectures and practical sessions ensured that attendees could translate knowledge into clinical expertise, enhancing their skills and confidence [Figures 1-5].

PARICHAY 2026

The Department of Periodontology and Oral Implantology, Himachal Dental College and Hospital, Sundernagar successfully organized, PARICHAY 2026 on May 29–30, 2026. The 2-day academic program featured insightful sessions by distinguished speakers. Dr. Vikas Jindal delivered an insightful lecture on Stress Management During Postgraduation, Dr. Vishakha Grover on Beyond Google - Effective Literature Search for M.D.S. Students, Dr. Devinder Singh Kalsi on 2018 Classification of Periodontal Diseases, Dr. Manish Khatri on Parichay to Maintenance of Clinical Dossier, Dr. Gurprakash Singh Chahal on An Introduction to Periodontal Armamentarium, and Dr. Aakash Agnihotri on An Introduction to Dental PhotographyThe 2nd day included enlightening lectures by Dr. Baljeet Singh on Art and Practice of Manuscript Writing, Dr. Aradhana on Pharmacology of Emergency Drugs (Applied Aspects), Dr. Divye Malhotra and Dr. Sunny Gupta on Life-Saving Skills in Dentistry: Emergency Management and Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation and Dr. Bandana on Chair-side Skills in Dentistry: Injectable Techniques and Blood Collection Procedures (The hallmark of the program was live demonstrations on Basic Life Support and various injectable techniques). The program was attended by postgraduate students from 12 dental colleges of North India. Through its comprehensive blend of academic, clinical, and research-oriented sessions, PARICHAY 2026 offered participants valuable insights and practical knowledge, making it a highly enriching and rewarding learning experience [Figures 6-8].The Department of Periodontology and Oral Implantology, Government Dental College and Hospital, Nagpur, in association with the ISPI Nagpur Study Group, successfully organized Hand Holding Program-PARICHAY 2026 on May 7–8, 2026. SAMEEKSHA 2026

The Department of Periodontology, The Oxford Dental College, Bengaluru, successfully organized, SAMEEKSHA a 3 day Perio Rapid Revision Programme for 3rd year MDS students of the South Zone under the aegis of the ISPI. The program witnessed enthusiastic participation from approximately 103 postgraduate students representing 31 dental colleges across South India. Designed as an intensive examination-oriented revision course, the program featured 15 expert lectures and 2 panel discussions, covering a comprehensive range of topics pertinent to the university examination curriculum in Periodontology. A total of 21 eminent academicians and clinicians from Karnataka contributed as speakers and panelists, sharing their expertise and valuable insights with the postgraduate students. The academic sessions were highly engaging and interactive, witnessing enthusiastic participation from the postgraduate students throughout the event. The lectures and panel discussions provided a focused revision of key topics, examination strategies, and practical approaches to answering university examination questions. The program served as an excellent platform for knowledge enhancement, academic interaction, and professional development among postgraduate students and faculty members [Figure 11]The Department of Periodontology and Oral-Implantology, J.N. Kapoor DAV (C) Dental College and Hospital, Yamuna Nagar, Haryana, successfully conducted ISP SAMEEKSHA 7.0-Yamunanagar, a Rapid Revision Programme for 3rd year postgraduate students from June 11 to 13, 2026 under the aegis of the Indian Society of Implantology. The program was successfully conducted with three academically enriching days, and lectures were focused on topics such as antimicrobial photodynamic NSPT, peri-implantitis, nonsurgical periodontal therapy, periodontal regeneration, occlusion, periodontal health and periodontitis, periodontal considerations in implants, periodontal plastic surgery, evidence-based dentistry, periodontal disease management, systemic health connections, periodontal disease, surgery during examination, and periodontal instruments and instrumentation. This 3-day program served as a focused and valuable academic exercise for students, reinforcing essential concepts in periodontology and encouraging systematic preparation for examinations [Figures 12 and 13].
