Authors: Alice Vanneste, Peter Sinnaeve, Isabelle Huys, Tom Adriaenssens, Christophe Garweg
Published: 2025

Correction: The Patient - Patient-Centered Outcomes Research 10.1007/s40271-025-00767-1

In Table 3 of this article, entries for Ranks 4 and 5 were listed incorrectly. The row ‘Rank 4’ should have read ‘No scar or lump on chest after implantation of the device’ and ‘Concentration’ as both have a score of 107 and ‘Rank 5’ should have read ‘Short hospitalization after the implantation’ as it has a score of 111.

In the sentence beginning ‘On the basis of the insights from the qualitative…’ in this article, the cross reference ‘(Table 4)’ should have read ‘(Table 4, Supplementary Material 7).’ The correct sentence reads as: ‘On the basis of the insights from the qualitative interviews and the results of the scoring and ranking exercises, six attributes and applicable levels have been proposed for inclusion in the design of a quantitative preference study (Table 4, Supplementary Material 7).’

The original article has been corrected.
