Text Correction

There was an error in the original publication. In Section 4.11, Statistical Analysis, the notation “p < 0.5” should be revised to “p < 0.05”.

A correction has been made to 4. Materials and Methods, 4.11. Statistical Analysis, Paragraph 1:

The significance of the drug treatments was determined by a Student’s t-test or one- or two-way analysis of variance. Post hoc comparisons between means were conducted using the Student-Newman-Keuls procedure. Statistical significance was set at p < 0.05.

The authors state that the scientific conclusions are unaffected. This correction was approved by the Academic Editor. The original publication has also been updated.
