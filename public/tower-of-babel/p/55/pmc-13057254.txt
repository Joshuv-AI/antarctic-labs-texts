Correction: Evaluating the sustainability and productivity of conventional, organic, and regenerative agriculture in maize-soybean rotations: a modelling LCA study
Authors: Alberto Cavallito, Iacopo Bianchi, Tommaso Mancia, Davide Catania, Marta Rossi, Barbara Marchetti
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-026-38291-3, published online 10 February 2026

The original version of this Article contained errors in the names of the authors Alberto Cavallito, Iacopo Bianchi, Tommaso Mancia, Davide Catania, Marta Rossi & Barbara Marchetti, which were incorrectly given as Cavallito Alberto, Bianchi Iacopo, Mancia Tommaso, Catania Davide, Rossi Marta & Marchetti Barbara.

Furthermore, the Supplementary Material files 1 and 2 were duplicated from Supplementary Material 3, the duplicated files have been removed.

The original Article and accompanying Supplementary Information files have been corrected.
