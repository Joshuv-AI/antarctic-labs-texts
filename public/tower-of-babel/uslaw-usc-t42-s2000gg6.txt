42 U.S.C. § 2000gg–6 — Severability
Citation: us:usc:42:2000gg–6
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2000gg–6
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 21G (Pregnant Worker Fairness). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this chapter or the application of that provision to particular persons or circumstances is held invalid or found to be unconstitutional, the remainder of this chapter and the application of that provision to other persons or circumstances shall not be affected.
