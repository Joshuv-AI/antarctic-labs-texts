43 C.F.R. § 50.20 — How may a request be submitted?
Citation: us:cfr:43:50.20
Creator: Office of the Federal Register
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-50.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 50 (Procedures For Reestablishing A Formal Government-To-Government Relationship With The Native Hawaiian Community). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If the Native Hawaiian community seeks to reestablish a formal government-to-government relationship with the United States, the request under this part must be submitted to the Secretary, Department of the Interior, 1849 C Street NW., Washington, DC 20240.
