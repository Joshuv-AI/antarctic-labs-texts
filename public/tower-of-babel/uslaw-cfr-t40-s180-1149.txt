40 C.F.R. § 180.1149 — Inclusion bodies of the multi-nuclear polyhedrosis virus of Anagrapha falcifera; exemption from the requirement of a tolerance.
Citation: us:cfr:40:180.1149
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-180.1149
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 180 (Tolerances And Exemptions For Pesticide Chemical Residues In Food). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The microbial pest control agent inclusion bodies of the multi-nuclear polyhedrosis virus of Anagrapha falcifera is exempted from the requirement of a tolerance in or on all raw agricultural commodities when used to control certain lepidopteran pest species.
