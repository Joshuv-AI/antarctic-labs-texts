7 C.F.R. § 1220.218 — Officers of the Committee.
Citation: us:cfr:7:1220.218
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Marketing Agreements And Orders; Miscellaneous Commodities), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-1220.218
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 1220 (Soybean Promotion, Research, And Consumer Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The following persons shall serve as officers of the Committee:
(a) The Chairperson of the Board shall be Chairperson of the Committee.
(b) The Committee shall elect or appoint such other officers as it may deem necessary.
