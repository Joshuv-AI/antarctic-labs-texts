46 C.F.R. § 112.45-1 — -1 Visible indicators.
Citation: us:cfr:46:112.45-1
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-112.45-1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 112 (Emergency Lighting And Power Systems). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

There must be visible indicators in the machinery space to show;
(a) When an emergency battery is discharging; and
(b) When the automatically controlled emergency power source is supplying the emergency loads.
