40 C.F.R. § 131.13 — General policies.
Citation: us:cfr:40:131.13
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-131.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 131 (Water Quality Standards). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

States may, at their discretion, include in their State standards, policies generally affecting their application and implementation, such as mixing zones, low flows and variances. Such policies are subject to EPA review and approval.
