48 C.F.R. § 18.116 — 18.116 Service-disabled Veteran-owned Small Business (SDVOSB) sole source awards.
Citation: us:cfr:48:18.116
Creator: Chapter 1—Federal Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-18.116
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 18 (Emergency Acquisitions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Contracts may be awarded to Service-disabled Veteran-owned Small Business (SDVOSB) concerns eligible under the SDVOSB Program on a sole source basis. (See 19.1406.)
