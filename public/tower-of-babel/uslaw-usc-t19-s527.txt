19 U.S.C. § 527 — Sums received from fines and other receipts covered into Treasury
Citation: us:usc:19:527
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title19-section527
Context: United States Code, Title 19 (Customs Duties); Chapter 3 (The Tariff And Related Provisions); Part 5 (Enforcement Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Except as otherwise provided by law, all sums received from fines, penalties, and forfeitures, connected with the customs, and from fees paid into the Treasury by customs officers, and from storage, cartage, drayage, labor, and services, shall be covered into the Treasury as are other miscellaneous receipts.
