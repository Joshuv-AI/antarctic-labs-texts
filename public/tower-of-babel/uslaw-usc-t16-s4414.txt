16 U.S.C. § 4414 — Assessment of progress in wetlands conservation
Citation: us:usc:16:4414
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title16-section4414
Context: United States Code, Title 16 (Conservation); Chapter 64 (North American Wetlands Conservation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Not later than January 31, 1996, the Secretary, in cooperation with the Council, to further the purposes of the chapter shall—
(1)
develop and implement a strategy to assist in the implementation of this Act in conserving the full complement of North American wetlands systems and species dependent on those systems, that incorporates information existing on the date of the issuance of the strategy in final form on types of wetlands habitats and species dependent on the habitats; and
(2)
develop and implement procedures to monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of wetlands conservation projects completed under this chapter.
