7 U.S.C. § 136x — Severability
Citation: us:usc:7:136x
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title7-section136x
Context: United States Code, Title 7 (Agriculture); Chapter 6 (Insecticides And Environmental Pesticide Control); Subchapter II (Environmental Pesticide Control). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

If any provision of this subchapter or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications of this subchapter which can be given effect without regard to the invalid provision or application, and to this end the provisions of this subchapter are severable.
