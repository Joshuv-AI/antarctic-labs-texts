40 U.S.C. § 905 — Waiver
Citation: us:usc:40:905
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title40-section905
Context: United States Code, Title 40 (Public Buildings, Property, And Works); Chapter 9 (Urban Land Use). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The procedures prescribed in sections 903 and 904 of this title may be waived during a period of national emergency proclaimed by the President.
