25 C.F.R. § 247.21 — Can I appeal an administrative action?
Citation: us:cfr:25:247.21
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-247.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 247 (Use Of Columbia River Treaty Fishing Access Sites). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

You may appeal any decision made by the Area Director under this part to the Commissioner of Indian Affairs. You may appeal any decision of the Commissioner of Indian Affairs to the Secretary of the Interior in accordance with part 2 of this chapter.
