10 U.S.C. § 7563 — Clothing: replacement when destroyed to prevent contagion
Citation: us:usc:10:7563
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section7563
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part IV (Service, Supply, And Procurement); Chapter 765 (Issue Of Serviceable Material To Armed Forces). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Upon the recommendation of the Surgeon General, the Secretary of the Army may order a gratuitous issue of clothing to any enlisted member of the Army who has had a contagious disease, and to any hospital attendant who attended him while he had that disease, to replace clothing destroyed by order of an officer of the Medical Corps to prevent contagion.
