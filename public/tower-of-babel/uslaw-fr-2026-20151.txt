DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

National Institutes of Health


Notice of NIH Tribal Consultations and Listening Session

AGENCY: National Institutes of Health, HHS.

ACTION: Notice.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: In accordance with the National Institutes of Health (NIH), 
Tribal Consultation Policy, the NIH will hold two Tribal Consultations 
and one Tribal listening session at the National Congress of American 
Indians (NCAI) Annual Convention. The first consultation will discuss 
two new proposed initiatives of the NIH All of Us Research Program. The 
second consultation will discuss the National Institute of Minority 
Health and Health Disparities (NIMHD) proposed renewal initiative for 
the Resource Center for Tribal Epidemiological Centers (RC-TEC). The 
Tribal Health Research Office will also conduct a listening session to 
seek Tribal perspectives on the NIH Tribal Data Sovereignty Policy 
currently under development.

DATES: The events will be held at the following times, all in Pacific 
time:
    Monday, October 19, 2026, from 12:00 p.m. to 2:00 p.m.: Tribal 
consultation on expanding the data access model and the inclusion of 
self-identified AI/AN participants' biospecimens in PRS within the NIH 
All of Us Research Program.
    Tuesday, October 20, 2026, from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m.: NIH Tribal 
Data Sovereignty Policy listening session.
    Tuesday, October 20, 2026, from 4:30 p.m. to 6:00 p.m.: Tribal 
consultation on the renewal of the NIMHD Resource Center for Tribal 
Epidemiology Centers.
    Written Tribal testimony or comments regarding either Tribal 
consultation can be submitted to [email protected] and must 
be received by end of day on November 20, 2026.

ADDRESSES: The two Tribal Consultations and one listening session will 
be held at the Palm Springs Convention Center, 277 N Avenida 
Caballeros, Palm Springs, CA 92262, during the NCAI 83rd Annual 
Convention & Marketplace. Specific room assignments are not yet 
available. Updates will be posted on the NIH THRO Tribal Consultations 
web page at https://dpcpsi.nih.gov/thro/tribal-consultations.
    A virtual participation option may be available for one or more 
sessions. Individuals interested in participating virtually should 
contact NIH at [email protected]. Information regarding 
virtual participation, if available, will also be posted on the NIH 
THRO Tribal Consultations web page.
    Written Tribal testimony or comments should be submitted by email 
to [email protected] by end of day on November 20, 2026. 
Submissions should be on Tribal letterhead or otherwise clearly 
identify the submitting Tribe or Tribal organization.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

I. Background and Participation

    NIH recognizes the government-to-government relationship between 
the United States and federally recognized Indian Tribes and conducts 
Tribal consultation in accordance with the NIH Tribal Consultation 
Policy (https://go.nih.gov/C01PhB8). The Policy provides a process for 
meaningful and timely input from Indian Tribes on NIH policies, 
programs, and research activities that significantly affect Tribes.
    NIH invites Tribal Officials and Tribal Representatives, as those 
terms are defined in the Policy, to provide input during the October 19 
and October 20 Tribal consultations. Under the Policy, a Tribal 
Representative may include an elected Tribal Official acting in an 
official capacity, or an employee or other representative designated in 
writing by an elected Tribal Official to represent the Tribe in 
discussions with the Federal Government.
    The October 20 Tribal Data Sovereignty Policy event is a listening 
session rather than a Tribal consultation. It is intended to inform 
development of the policy before NIH conducts a separate Tribal 
consultation.

II. NIH All of Us Research Program Tribal Consultation

    On Monday, October 19, 2026, from 12:00 p.m. to 2:00 p.m. Pacific 
Time, NIH will conduct a Tribal consultation concerning two new 
initiatives within the All of Us Research Program. The All of Us 
Research Program aims to accelerate health research and medical 
breakthroughs to enable an era of precision medicine for all. All of Us 
is expanding its existing data access model to contain an added level 
of sensitive participant data that will require mandatory review by an 
Institutional Review Board. It is also planning to include self-
identified AI/AN participants in the PRS initiative involving a large-
scale biospecimen analysis effort. This effort will allow researchers 
external to NIH to apply for access to current participants' samples 
(such as blood, saliva or urine), including those from self-identified 
AI/AN participants.
    Feedback from Tribal Nations is crucial to shaping the program's 
strategies to ensure the use of biospecimens and that access to data 
aligns with priorities of Tribal and Urban Indian communities. All of 
Us would like to discuss how to (1) responsibly manage self-identified 
AI/AN participant bio samples in partnered research studies and ensure 
the research yields meaningful community benefits and (2) manage 
sensitive data access that honors both an individual's and Tribes' 
rights.

III. NIH Tribal Data Sovereignty Policy Listening Session

    On Tuesday, October 20, 2026, from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. Pacific 
Time, NIH THRO will host a listening session on the NIH Tribal Data 
Sovereignty Policy currently under development. The session will 
provide a forum for Tribal Leaders and designees to offer guidance and 
share perspectives, concerns, and recommendations on how NIH funding 
announcements, grant mechanisms, and research activities should 
recognize and uphold the inherent sovereignty of Tribal Nations over 
data generated by, about, and within their communities.
    THRO leadership will hear directly from Tribal Leaders and 
designees and consider their input as NIH develops the policy, 
including potential funding opportunity announcement language and 
grants administration practices. Discussion is expected to address how 
NIH can better align data collection, management and sharing processes 
with Tribal laws, codes, and ordinances governing data collection, 
ownership, access, use, and disposition, including Tribal Institutional 
Review Board requirements, data-sharing agreements, and Tribal Research 
Codes.
    This listening session is not a Tribal consultation and will not 
substitute for Tribal consultation on the NIH Tribal Data Sovereignty 
Policy. NIH will conduct a separate Tribal consultation before the 
policy is finalized. Information about that Tribal consultation will be 
announced separately.

IV. NIMHD Resource Center for Tribal Epidemiology Centers Tribal 
Consultation

    On Tuesday, October 20, 2026, from 4:30 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. Pacific 
time, NIH will conduct a Tribal consultation on proposed revisions to 
the renewal of the NIMHD Resource Center for Tribal Epidemiological 
Centers (RC-TEC) initiative. The session will provide an overview of 
the proposed revisions, their potential implications for Tribal 
Epidemiology Centers (TECs) and the Tribes they serve, and an 
opportunity for Tribal input.
    The consultation will help ensure that the revised initiative 
reflects Tribal priorities and the essential role of TECs in 
strengthening Tribal public health capacity, surveillance, data 
systems, and health sovereignty. NIH will provide an overview of the 
proposed revisions to the renewal of the RC-TEC initiative and describe 
potential key changes, their potential implications for TECs and the 
Tribes they serve in order to support transparency and strengthen 
government-to-government engagement.

V. Written Tribal Testimony and Comments

    NIH welcomes written Tribal testimony or comments concerning each 
Tribal consultation. Written submissions should be submitted to 
[email protected] and must be received by end of day on 
November 20, 2026. Submissions should be on Tribal letterhead or 
otherwise clearly identify the submitting Tribe or Tribal organization.
    Written input for the NIH Tribal Data Sovereignty Policy may be 
submitted to the [email protected]. Solicitation for Tribal 
testimony for the NIH Tribal Data Sovereignty Policy will occur when 
the Tribal consultation for the Policy takes place. This Notice is in 
accordance with Executive Order 13175 Authority to implement and comply 
with Federal and Departmental policies, procedures, and requirements as 
it relates to Tribal consultations.
