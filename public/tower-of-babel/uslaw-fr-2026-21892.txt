SUMMARY: This notice contains a summary of a petition seeking relief 
from specified requirements of Federal Aviation Regulations. The 
purpose of this notice is to improve the public's awareness of, and 
participation in, the FAA's exemption process. Neither publication of 
this notice nor the inclusion nor omission of information in the 
summary is intended to affect the legal status of the petition or its 
final disposition.

DATES: Comments on this petition must identify the petition docket 
number and must be received on or before November 16, 2026.

Petition for Exemption

    Docket No.: FAA-2023-0883.
    Petitioner: Placer Mosquito and Vector Control District.
    Sections of 14 CFR Affected: Sec. Sec.  61.3(a)(1)(i), 61.3(c)(1), 
61.23(a)(2), 91.7(a), 91.119(c), 91.121, 91.151(a), 91.151(b), 
91.209(a)(1), 91.403(b), 91.405(a), 91.407(a)(1), 91.409(a)(1), 
91.409(a)(2), 91.417(a), 91.417(b), 137.19(c), 137.19(d), 
137.19(e)(2)(ii), 137.19(e)(2)(iii), 137.19(e)(2)(v), 137.31(a), 
137.31(b), 137.33(a), 137.33(b), 137.41(c), and 137.42.
    Description of Relief Sought: The petitioner requests relief to 
operate unmanned aircraft systems weighing 55 pounds or greater for 
mosquito- and vector-abatement missions, utilizing reduced standoff 
distances from non-participating people and property, derived from the 
petitioner's Operations Manual buffer zone methodology.
