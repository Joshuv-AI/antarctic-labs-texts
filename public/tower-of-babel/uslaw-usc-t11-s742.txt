11 U.S.C. § 742 — Effect of section 362 of this title in this subchapter
Citation: us:usc:11:742
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title11-section742
Context: United States Code, Title 11 (Bankruptcy); Chapter 7 (Liquidation); Subchapter III (Stockbroker Liquidation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Notwithstanding section 362 of this title, SIPC may file an application for a protective decree under the Securities Investor Protection Act of 1970. The filing of such application stays all proceedings in the case under this title unless and until such application is dismissed. If SIPC completes the liquidation of the debtor, then the court shall dismiss the case.
