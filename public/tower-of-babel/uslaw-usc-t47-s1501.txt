47 U.S.C. § 1501 — Definitions
Citation: us:usc:47:1501
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title47-section1501
Context: United States Code, Title 47 (Telecommunications); Chapter 14 (Making Opportunities For Broadband Investment And Limiting Excessive And Needless Obstacles To Wireless). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this chapter:
(1) Appropriate committees of Congress
The term “appropriate committees of Congress” means—
(A)
the Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation of the Senate;
(B)
the Committee on Energy and Commerce of the House of Representatives; and
(C)
each committee of the Senate or of the House of Representatives with jurisdiction over a Federal entity affected by the applicable section in which the term appears.
(2) Commission
The term “Commission” means the Federal Communications Commission.
(3) Federal entity
The term “Federal entity” has the meaning given the term in section 923(l) of this title.
(4) NTIA
The term “NTIA” means the National Telecommunications and Information Administration of the Department of Commerce.
(5) OMB
The term “OMB” means the Office of Management and Budget.
(6) Secretary
The term “Secretary” means the Secretary of Commerce.
