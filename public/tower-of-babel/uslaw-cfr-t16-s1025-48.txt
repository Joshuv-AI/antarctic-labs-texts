16 C.F.R. § 1025.48 — Official docket.
Citation: us:cfr:16:1025.48
Creator: Consumer Product Safety Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1025.48
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 16 (Commercial Practices), Part 1025 (Rules Of Practice For Adjudicative Proceedings). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The official docket in any adjudicatory proceedings shall be maintained in the Office of the Secretary and be available for public inspection during normal business hours of the Commission.
