42 C.F.R. § 62.5 — How is application made for a scholarship program award?
Citation: us:cfr:42:62.5
Creator: Public Health Service, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-62.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 42 (Public Health), Part 62 (National Health Service Corps Scholarship And Loan Repayment Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each individual desiring a scholarship under this part must submit an application (including a signed contract as required under section 751(f) of the Act) in the form and at the time prescribed by the Secretary.
