48 C.F.R. § 1631.200 — 1631.200 Scope of subpart.
Citation: us:cfr:48:1631.200
Creator: Chapter 16—Office Of Personnel Management Federal Employees Health Benefits Acquisition Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-48/section-1631.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 48 (Federal Acquisition Regulations System), Part 1631 (Contract Cost Principles And Procedures). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The cost principles under this subpart apply only to contracts in which premiums and subscription income are determined on the basis of experience rating, in which cost analysis is performed, or in which price is determined on the basis of actual costs incurred.
