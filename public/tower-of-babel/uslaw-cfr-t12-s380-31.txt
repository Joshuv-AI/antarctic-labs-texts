12 C.F.R. § 380.31 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:12:380.31
Creator: Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-380.31
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 380 (Orderly Liquidation Authority). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subpart C shall apply to any liability or obligation of a bridge financial company or its assets or liabilities, or to any extension of credit from a Federal reserve bank or the Corporation to a covered financial company.
