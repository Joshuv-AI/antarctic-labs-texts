Correction: Biomechanical coordination and variability alters following repetitive movement fatigue in overhead athletes with painful shoulder
Authors: Rahman Sheikhhoseini, Sajjad Abdollahi, Mohammad Salsali, Mehrdad Anbarian
Published: 2026

Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-025-85226-5, published online 3 January 2025

The original version of this Article contained errors.

In the Methods section, under the subheading ‘Participants’, Reference 30 refers to a methodological source on EMG electrode placement (Basmajian & Blumenstein, 1980) and was not used to justify the sample size. The sample size was determined by an a priori power analysis conducted with G*Power version 3.1, supported by fatigue-related neuromuscular literature on overhead athletes, originally cited as Reference 37 in the article. Reference 37 has now been renumbered to Reference 28. As a result,

“The results of a study that examined the effect of fatigue on the electromyographic function of the lower trapezius muscle were used to estimate the required sample size in this study [30].”

now reads:

“The results of a study that examined the effect of fatigue on the electromyographic function of the lower trapezius muscle were used to estimate the required sample size in this study [28].”

Moreover, Fig. 1 was reused from an existing conceptual framework without attribution. The authors have now substituted it with a self-generated graphic and updated the caption to give appropriate credit. Consequently,

“Fig. 1 Repetitive Pointing Task equipment.”

now reads:

“Fig. 1 Repetitive Pointing Task equipment (adapted from Reference 30).”

The new Reference 30 is listed below:

30. Fuller, J.R., J. Fung, and J.N. Côté, Posture-movement responses to stance perturbations and upper limb fatigue during a repetitive pointing task. Human Movement Science, 32(4), 618–632 (2013).

As a result of the changes, the References have been renumbered.

The original Fig. 1 and accompanying legend appear below.

Repetitive Pointing Task equipment.

The original Article has been corrected.
