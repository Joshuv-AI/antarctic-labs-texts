10 U.S.C. § 20002 — Members: duty status
Citation: us:usc:10:20002
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section20002
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part I (Space Force); Chapter 2001 (Space Force Personnel System). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary of the Air Force, each member of the Space Force shall be placed in one of the following duty statuses:
(1)
Space Force active status.
(2)
Space Force inactive status.
(3)
Space Force retired status.
