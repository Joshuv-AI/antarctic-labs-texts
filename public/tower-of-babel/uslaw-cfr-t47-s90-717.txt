47 C.F.R. § 90.717 — Channels available for nationwide systems in the 220-222 MHz band.
Citation: us:cfr:47:90.717
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-90.717
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 90 (Private Land Mobile Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Channels 51-60, 81-90, and 141-150 are 10-channel blocks available to non-Government applicants only for nationwide Phase II systems.
(b) Channels 21-25, 26-30, 151-155, and 156-160 are 5-channel blocks available to non-Government applicants only for nationwide, commercial Phase I systems.
(c) Channels 111-115 and 116-120 are 5-channel blocks available for Government nationwide use only.
