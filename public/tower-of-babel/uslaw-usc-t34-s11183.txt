34 U.S.C. § 11183 — Withholding
Citation: us:usc:34:11183
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section11183
Context: United States Code, Title 34 (Crime Control And Law Enforcement); Chapter 111 (Juvenile Justice And Delinquency Prevention); Subchapter II (Programs And Offices); Part F (General And Administrative Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Whenever the Administrator, after giving reasonable notice and opportunity for hearing to a recipient of financial assistance under this subchapter, finds that—
(1)
the program or activity for which the grant or contract involved was made has been so changed that it no longer complies with this subchapter; or
(2)
in the operation of such program or activity there is failure to comply substantially with any provision of this subchapter;
the Administrator shall initiate such proceedings as are appropriate.
