15 U.S.C. § 9305 — Prioritization
Citation: us:usc:15:9305
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section9305
Context: United States Code, Title 15 (Commerce And Trade); Chapter 118 (Sustainable Chemistry). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In carrying out this chapter, the Entity shall focus its support for sustainable chemistry activities on those that achieve, to the highest extent practicable, the goals outlined in the chapter.
