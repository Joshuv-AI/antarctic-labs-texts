41 C.F.R. § 102-37.60 — -37.60 SASP responsibilities.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-37.60
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-37.60
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-37 (37—Donation Of Surplus Personal Property). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) Determine if an entity seeking to obtain surplus property is an eligible entity per § 102-37.275 or other approved entity.
(b) Distribute surplus property fairly, equitably, and promptly to eligible donees in your State based on their relative needs and resources, ability to use the property, and as provided in your State plan of operation.
(c) Enforce compliance with the terms and conditions imposed on donated property.
