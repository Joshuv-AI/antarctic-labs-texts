2 U.S.C. § 2145 — Restriction on use of appropriation for Botanic Garden
Citation: us:usc:2:2145
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section2145
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 30 (Operation And Maintenance Of Capitol Complex); Subchapter VI (Botanic Garden And National Garden). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

On and after July 31, 1958, no part of any appropriation for the Botanic Garden shall be used for the distribution, by congressional allotment, of trees, plants, shrubs, or other nursery stock.
