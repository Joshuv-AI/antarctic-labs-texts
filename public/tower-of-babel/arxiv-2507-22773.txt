General quantum computation on photons assisted with double single-sided cavity system
Jiu-Ming Li 1, Jun-Yan Liu 1, Yuan-Yuan Liu 1, Xiao-Ming Xiu 1, Shao-Ming Fei 2

Abstract
We propose a physical system consisting of two optical cavities and a two-level system (TLS), which can be viewed as a double single-sided cavity system. The two cavities are crossed each other in a mutually perpendicular way and are both single-sided. The TLS is coupled to the two cavities. The universal input-output relation of the system, the reflection and transmission coefficients are derived by exploiting the probability amplitude method. Then by using the nitrogen-vacancy center instead of the TLS, we generate the controlled-phase gate and the controlled-controlled-phase gate on the photon qubits, with simple protocols that can be accomplished in both weak and strong coupling regimes. The protocols are shown to give rise to high fidelities and gate efficiencies.

In this work, we first consider a system consisting of two optical cavities and a two-level system (TLS). Compared with other works, we obtain the universal input-output relation of the double single-sided cavity system, the reflection and transmission coefficients, as well as the c-phase gate and the cc-phase gate with much simpler protocols.

THE UNIVERSAL INPUT-OUTPUT RELATION OF THE DOUBLE SINGLE-SIDED CAVITY SYSTEM

Consider a system consisting of two optical cavities and a TLS, as shown in Fig.

The two cavities, labeled as cavity 1 and cavity 2, are both single-sided and coupled to the port 1 and 2 through the coupling mirror, respectively.

\displaystyle|\psi(t)\rangle\!\!

\displaystyle=

\displaystyle\!\!\int\mathrm{d}\omega\,A(\omega,t)|\varepsilon\rangle_{1}+\int\mathrm{d}\omega\,B(\omega,t)|\varepsilon\rangle_{2}

(2)

\displaystyle\!\!+C(t)|\varepsilon\rangle_{3}+D(t)|\varepsilon\rangle_{4}+E(t)|\varepsilon\rangle_{5}

,

,

According to the Schrödinger equation, five differential equations are attained as followed

\displaystyle\dot{A}(\omega,t)=-\mathrm{i}\omega A(\omega,t)+\sqrt{\frac{\kappa_{1}}{2\pi}}C(t),

\displaystyle\dot{B}(\omega,t)=-\mathrm{i}\omega B(\omega,t)+\sqrt{\frac{\kappa_{2}}{2\pi}}D(t),

\displaystyle\dot{C}(t)=-\mathrm{i}\omega_{1}C(t)-\sqrt{\frac{\kappa_{1}}{2\pi}}\int\mathrm{d}\omega\,A(\omega,t)-\lambda_{1}E(t),

\displaystyle\dot{D}(t)=-\mathrm{i}\omega_{2}D(t)-\sqrt{\frac{\kappa_{2}}{2\pi}}\int\mathrm{d}\omega\,B(\omega,t)-\lambda_{2}E(t),

\displaystyle\dot{E}(t)=(-\mathrm{i}\omega_{ge}-\frac{\gamma}{2})E(t)+\lambda_{1}C(t)+\lambda_{2}D(t).

(3)

The input amplitudes of the two ports are defined by

\displaystyle A_{\mathrm{in}}(t)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int\mathrm{d}\omega\,A(\omega,0)\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}\omega t},

\displaystyle B_{\mathrm{in}}(t)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int\mathrm{d}\omega\,B(\omega,0)\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}\omega t}.

\displaystyle C(\omega)=\frac{-\sqrt{\kappa_{1}}A_{\mathrm{in}}(\omega)-\lambda_{1}E(\omega)}{\frac{1}{2}\kappa_{1}-\mathrm{i}\delta_{1}},

\displaystyle D(\omega)=\frac{-\sqrt{\kappa_{2}}B_{\mathrm{in}}(\omega)-\lambda_{2}E(\omega)}{\frac{1}{2}\kappa_{2}-\mathrm{i}\delta_{2}},

3

V
CONCLUSION

We have proposed a physical system consisting of two optical cavities coupled to a two-level system, which can be viewed as a double single-sided cavity system. The corresponding universal input-output relation, the reflection and transmission coefficients have been attained. As important applications, the controlled-phase gate and controlled-controlled-phase gate are constructed on photons with simple protocols by using the NV-center. Both protocols can be accomplished in the weak and strong coupling regimes. They give rise to much higher fidelities and efficiencies. Our approaches may highlight further investigations on physical realizations of quantum gates in quantum computation.
