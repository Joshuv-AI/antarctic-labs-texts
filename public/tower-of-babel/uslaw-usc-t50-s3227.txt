50 U.S.C. § 3227 — Transfer date
Citation: us:usc:50:3227
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title50-section3227
Context: United States Code, Title 50 (War And National Defense); Chapter 44 (National Security); Subchapter VIII (Education In Support Of National Intelligence); Part D (National Intelligence University). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

In this part, the term “transfer date” means the date on which the National Intelligence University is transferred from the Defense Intelligence Agency to the Director of National Intelligence under section 3334a(a) of this title.
