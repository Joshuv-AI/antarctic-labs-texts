38 U.S.C. § 5124 — Acceptance of claimant’s statement as proof of relationship
Citation: us:usc:38:5124
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title38-section5124
Context: United States Code, Title 38 (Veterans’ Benefits); Part IV (General Administrative Provisions); Chapter 51 (Claims, Effective Dates, And Payments); Subchapter III (Payment Of Benefits). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a)
For purposes of benefits under laws administered by the Secretary, the Secretary may accept the written statement of a claimant as proof of the existence of any relationship specified in subsection (b) for the purpose of acting on such individual’s claim for benefits.
(b)
Subsection (a) applies to proof of the existence of any of the following relationships between a claimant and another person:
(1)
Marriage.
(2)
Dissolution of a marriage.
(3)
Birth of a child.
(4)
Death of any family member.
(c)
The Secretary may require the submission of documentation in support of the claimant’s statement if—
(1)
the claimant does not reside within a State;
(2)
the statement on its face raises a question as to its validity;
(3)
there is conflicting information of record; or
(4)
there is reasonable indication, in the statement or otherwise, of fraud or misrepresentation.
