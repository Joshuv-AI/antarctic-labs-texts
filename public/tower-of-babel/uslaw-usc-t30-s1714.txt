30 U.S.C. § 1714 — Deposit of royalty funds to Indian accounts
Citation: us:usc:30:1714
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title30-section1714
Context: United States Code, Title 30 (Mineral Lands And Mining); Chapter 29 (Oil And Gas Royalty Management); Subchapter I (Federal Royalty Management And Enforcement). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Deposits of any royalty funds derived from the production of oil or gas from, or allocated to, Indian lands shall be made by the Secretary to the appropriate Indian account at the earliest practicable date after such funds are received by the Secretary but in no case later than the last business day of the month in which such funds are received.
