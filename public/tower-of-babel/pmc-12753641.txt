Author Correction: Impact of commonly administered drugs on the progression of spinal cord injury: a systematic review
Authors: Lucie Bourguignon, Louis P Lukas, Bethany R Kondiles, Bobo Tong, Jaimie J Lee, Tomás Gomes, Wolfram Tetzlaff, John L K Kramer, Matthias Walter, Catherine R Jutzeler
Published: 2025

Correction to: Communications Medicine 10.1038/s43856-024-00638-0, published online 24 October 2024

In the version of the article initially published, there were labelling errors in Fig. 7d, where the order of studies on the left-hand side was incorrect, with Leech et al. (2014) appearing in the top row rather than second from bottom, while the year listed for Aminmansour et al. (2016) was incorrectly listed as 2012. The figure is now amended in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
