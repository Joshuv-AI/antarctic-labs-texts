20 U.S.C. § 80d — Authorization of appropriations
Citation: us:usc:20:80d
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section80d
Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter X (National Armed Forces Museum Advisory Board). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to the Smithsonian Institution such sums as may be necessary for the purposes of this subchapter.
