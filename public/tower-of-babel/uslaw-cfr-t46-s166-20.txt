46 C.F.R. § 166.20 — Applicants for certificates; when eligible for examination.
Citation: us:cfr:46:166.20
Creator: Coast Guard, Department Of Homeland Security
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-166.20
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 166 (Designation And Approval Of Nautical School Ships). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Applicants for certificates as able seamen will be eligible for examination after they have completed a course of study as outlined in §§ 166.05, 166.15, and applicants for certificates as qualified members of the engine department after they have completed a course of study as outlined in §§ 166.10, 166.15.
