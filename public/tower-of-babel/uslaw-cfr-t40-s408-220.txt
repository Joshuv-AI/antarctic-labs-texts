40 C.F.R. § 408.220 — Applicability; description of the non-Alaskan mechanized bottom fish processing subcategory.
Citation: us:cfr:40:408.220
Creator: Environmental Protection Agency
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/section-408.220
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 40 (Protection of Environment), Part 408 (Canned And Preserved Seafood Processing Point Source Category). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The provisions of this subpart are applicable to discharges resulting from the processing of bottom fish outside of Alaska in which the unit operations (particularly the butchering and/or filleting operations) are carried out predominately through mechanized methods. The provisions of this subpart apply to the processing of bottom fish such as whiting and croaker.
