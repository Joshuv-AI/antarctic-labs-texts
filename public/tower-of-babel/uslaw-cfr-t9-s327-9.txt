9 C.F.R. § 327.9 — Burlap wrapping for foreign meat.
Citation: us:cfr:9:327.9
Creator: Food Safety And Inspection Service, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-9/section-327.9
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 9 (Animals and Animal Products), Part 327 (Imported Products). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Burlap shall not be used as a wrapping for foreign meat unless the meat is first wrapped with a good grade of paper or cloth of a kind which will prevent contamination with lint or other foreign material.
