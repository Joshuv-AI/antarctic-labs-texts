30 C.F.R. § 937.823 — Special performance standards—operations on prime farmland.
Citation: us:cfr:30:937.823
Creator: Office Of Surface Mining Reclamation And Enforcement, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-30/section-937.823
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 30 (Mineral Resources), Part 937 (Oregon). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Part 823 of this chapter, Special Permanent Program Performance Standards—Operations on Prime Farmland, shall apply to any person who conducts surface coal mining and reclamation operations on prime farmlands.
