43 C.F.R. § 3715.4-3 — -3 What if BLM does not concur in my existing use or occupancy?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3715.4-3
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3715.4-3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3710 (Public Law 167; Act Of July 23, 1955). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If BLM determines that all or any part of your existing use or occupancy is not reasonably incident:
(a) BLM may order a suspension or cessation of all or part of the use or occupancy under § 3715.7-1;
(b) BLM may order the land to be reclaimed to its satisfaction and specify a reasonable time for completion of reclamation under 43 CFR part 3800; and
(c) BLM may order you to apply within 30 days after the date of notice from BLM for appropriate authorization under the regulations in 43 CFR Group 2900.
