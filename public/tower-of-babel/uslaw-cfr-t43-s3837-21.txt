43 C.F.R. § 3837.21 — How do I notify the delinquent co-claimant that I want to acquire his or her interests?
Citation: us:cfr:43:3837.21
Creator: Bureau Of Land Management, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-3837.21
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 43 (Public Lands: Interior), Part 3837 (Acquiring A Delinquent Co-Claimant'S Interests In A Mining Claim Or Site). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) You must give the delinquent co-claimant written notice by mail using registered or certified mail, return receipt requested, or by personal service; or
(b) If, after diligent search, you cannot locate the delinquent co-claimant, you must publish notification in a newspaper nearest the location of the claims or sites at least once a week for 90 days.
