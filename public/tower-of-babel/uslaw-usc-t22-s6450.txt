22 U.S.C. § 6450 — Preclusion of judicial review
Citation: us:usc:22:6450
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title22-section6450
Context: United States Code, Title 22 (Foreign Relations And Intercourse); Chapter 73 (International Religious Freedom); Subchapter III (Presidential Actions); Part A (Targeted Responses To Violations Of Religious Freedom Abroad). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

No court shall have jurisdiction to review any Presidential determination or agency action under this chapter or any amendment made by this chapter.
