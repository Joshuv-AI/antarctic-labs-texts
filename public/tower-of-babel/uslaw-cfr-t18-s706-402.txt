18 C.F.R. § 706.402 — Employee's complaint on filing requirements.
Citation: us:cfr:18:706.402
Creator: Water Resources Council
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-18/section-706.402
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 18 (Conservation of Power and Water Resources), Part 706 (Employee Responsibilities And Conduct). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An employee who feels that his position has been improperly included in the list in § 706.401 as one requiring the submission of a statement of employment and financial interests may obtain a review of his complaint under the Council's internal grievance procedure.
