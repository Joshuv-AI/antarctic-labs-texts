42 U.S.C. § 3056j — Treatment of assistance
Citation: us:usc:42:3056j
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section3056j
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 35 (Programs For Older Americans); Subchapter IX (Community Service Senior Opportunities). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Assistance provided under this subchapter shall not be considered to be financial assistance described in section 1255a(h)(1)(A) of title 8.
