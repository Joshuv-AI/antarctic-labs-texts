46 U.S.C. § 6306 — Penalty
Citation: us:usc:46:6306
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section6306
Context: United States Code, Title 46 (Shipping); Part D (Marine Casualties); Chapter 63 (Investigating Marine Casualties). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

A person attempting to coerce a witness, or to induce a witness, to testify falsely in connection with a marine casualty, or to induce a witness to leave the jurisdiction of the United States, shall be fined $5,000, imprisoned for one year, or both.
