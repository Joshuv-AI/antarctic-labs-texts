49 C.F.R. § 1114.5 — Records in other Board proceedings.
Citation: us:cfr:49:1114.5
Creator: Surface Transportation Board
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-1114.5
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 49 (Transportation), Part 1114 (Evidence; Discovery). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

If any portion of the record before the Board in any proceeding other than the proceeding at issue is offered in evidence, a true copy will be presented for the record.
