29 U.S.C. § 529 — Prohibition on certain discipline by labor organization
Citation: us:usc:29:529
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title29-section529
Context: United States Code, Title 29 (Labor); Chapter 11 (Labor-Management Reporting And Disclosure Procedure); Subchapter VII (Miscellaneous Provisions). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall be unlawful for any labor organization, or any officer, agent, shop steward, or other representative of a labor organization, or any employee thereof to fine, suspend, expel, or otherwise discipline any of its members for exercising any right to which he is entitled under the provisions of this chapter. The provisions of section 412 of this title shall be applicable in the enforcement of this section.
