49 U.S.C. § 48106 — Airway science curriculum grants
Citation: us:usc:49:48106
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title49-section48106
Context: United States Code, Title 49 (Transportation); Part C (Financing); Chapter 481 (Airport And Airway Trust Fund Authorizations). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

Amounts are available from the Airport and Airway Trust Fund established under section 9502 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 (26 U.S.C. 9502) to carry out section 44510 1 of this title. The amounts remain available until expended.
