20 U.S.C. § 80s–3 — Authorization of appropriations

Creator: United States Congress

Context: United States Code, Title 20 (Education); Chapter 3 (Smithsonian Institution, National Museums And Art Galleries); Subchapter XV (Civil Rights History Project). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

There are authorized to be appropriated to carry out this subchapter—
(1)
$500,000 for fiscal year 2010; and
(2)
such sums as may be necessary for each of the fiscal years 2011 through 2014.
