52 U.S.C. § 21052 — Report
Citation: us:usc:52:21052
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title52-section21052
Context: United States Code, Title 52 (Voting And Elections); Chapter 209 (Election Administration Improvement); Subchapter II (Commission); Part D (Election Assistance); Subpart 4 (Pilot Program For Testing Of Equipment And Technology). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In general
Each entity which receives a grant under this subpart shall submit to the Commission a report describing the activities carried out with the funds provided under the grant.
(b) Deadline
An entity shall submit a report required under subsection (a) not later than 60 days after the end of the fiscal year for which the entity received the grant which is the subject of the report.
