46 C.F.R. § 249.4 — Eligibility.
Citation: us:cfr:46:249.4
Creator: Maritime Administration, Department Of Transportation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-46/section-249.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 46 (Shipping), Part 249 (Approval Of Underwriters For Marine Hull Insurance). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

In General. All required marine hull insurance must be placed with:
(a) Underwriters licensed to do business in one or more of the United States;
(b) Underwriters at Lloyds;
(c) Member companies of the Institute of London Underwriters; or
(d) Other underwriters specifically approved in advance by the Maritime Administration.
