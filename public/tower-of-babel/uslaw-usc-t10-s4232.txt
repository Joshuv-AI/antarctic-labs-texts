10 U.S.C. § 4232 — Prohibition on use of lowest price technically acceptable source selection process
Citation: us:usc:10:4232
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section4232
Context: United States Code, Title 10 (Armed Forces); Part V (Acquisition); Subpart F (Major Systems, Major Defense Acquisition Programs, And Weapon Systems Development); Chapter 322 (Major Systems And Major Defense Acquisition Programs Generally); Subchapter II (Contracting). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

(a) In General.—
The Department of Defense shall not use a lowest price technically acceptable source selection process for the engineering and manufacturing development contract of a major defense acquisition program.
(b) Definitions.—
In this section:
(1) Lowest price technically acceptable source selection process.—
The term “lowest price technically acceptable source selection process” has the meaning given that term in part 15 of the Federal Acquisition Regulation.
(2) Engineering and manufacturing development contract.—
The term “engineering and manufacturing development contract” means a prime contract for the engineering and manufacturing development of a major defense acquisition program.
