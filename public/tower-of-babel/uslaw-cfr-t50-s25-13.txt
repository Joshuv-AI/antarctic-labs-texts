50 C.F.R. § 25.13 — Other applicable laws.
Citation: us:cfr:50:25.13
Creator: United States Fish And Wildlife Service, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-50/section-25.13
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 50 (Wildlife and Fisheries), Part 25 (Administrative Provisions). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed to relieve a person from any other applicable requirements imposed by a local ordinance or by a statute or regulation of any State or of the United States.
