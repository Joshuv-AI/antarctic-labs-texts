25 C.F.R. § 20.508 — What must the social services agency do when a child is placed in foster care, residential care or guardianship home?
Citation: us:cfr:25:20.508
Creator: Bureau Of Indian Affairs, Department Of The Interior
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-25/section-20.508
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 25 (Indians), Part 20 (Financial Assistance And Social Services Programs). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The social services agency must make efforts to secure child support for the child in foster care or residential care through a court of competent jurisdiction.
