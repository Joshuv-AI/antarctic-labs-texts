2 U.S.C. § 4558 — Withholding of charitable contributions; definitions
Citation: us:usc:2:4558
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section4558
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 45 (Congressional Pay And Benefits); Subchapter II (House Of Representatives); Part B (Administration). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

For purposes of section 4557 of this title—
(1)
the term “charitable organizations” means national voluntary health and welfare agencies designated by the Director of the Office of Personnel Management pursuant to Executive Order 10927, dated March 19, 1961; and
(2)
the term “employee” means any employee of the House of Representatives whose compensation is disbursed by the Chief Administrative Officer of the House of Representatives.
