33 U.S.C. § 1969 — Termination of authority
Citation: us:usc:33:1969
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title33-section1969
Context: United States Code, Title 33 (Navigation And Navigable Waters); Chapter 33A (Marine Debris Research, Prevention, And Reduction); Subchapter II (Marine Debris Foundation). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The authority of the Foundation under this subchapter shall terminate on the date that is 10 years after the establishment of the Foundation, unless the Foundation is reauthorized by an Act of Congress.
