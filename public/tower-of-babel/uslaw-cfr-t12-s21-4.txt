12 C.F.R. § 21.4 — Report.
Citation: us:cfr:12:21.4
Creator: Comptroller Of The Currency, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/section-21.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 12 (Banks and Banking), Part 21 (Minimum Security Devices And Procedures, Reports Of Suspicious Activities, And Bank Secrecy Act Compliance Program). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The security officer for a national bank shall report at least annually to the bank's board of directors on the effectiveness of the security program. The substance of such report shall be reflected in the minutes of the Board meeting in which it is given.
