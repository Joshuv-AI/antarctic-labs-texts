2 U.S.C. § 6516 — Sale of waste paper and condemned furniture
Citation: us:usc:2:6516
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title2-section6516
Context: United States Code, Title 2 (The Congress); Chapter 65 (Senate Officers And Administration); Subchapter I (General). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

It shall be the duty of the Secretary and Sergeant at Arms of the Senate to cause to be sold all waste paper and useless documents and condemned furniture that may accumulate, in their respective departments or offices, under the direction of the Committee on Rules and Administration of the Senate and cover the proceeds thereof into the Treasury.
