5 C.F.R. § 251.302 — All other organizations.
Citation: us:cfr:5:251.302
Creator: Office Of Personnel Management
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-5/section-251.302
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 5 (Administrative Personnel), Part 251 (Agency Relationships With Organizations Representing Federal Employees And Other Organizations). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Under 5 CFR 550.311(b), an agency may permit an employee to make an allotment for any legal purpose deemed appropriate by the head of the agency. Agencies may provide for the allotment of dues for organizations representing Federal employees under that section.
