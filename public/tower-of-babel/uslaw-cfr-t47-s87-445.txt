47 C.F.R. § 87.445 — Scope of service.
Citation: us:cfr:47:87.445
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-87.445
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 87 (Aviation Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

An operational fixed station provides control, repeater or relay functions for its associated aeronautical station.
