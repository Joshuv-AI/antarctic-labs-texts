21 C.F.R. § 338.3 — Definition.
Citation: us:cfr:21:338.3
Creator: Food And Drug Administration, Department Of Health And Human Services
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/section-338.3
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 21 (Food and Drugs), Part 338 (Nighttime Sleep-Aid Drug Products For Over-The-Counter Human Use). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

As used in this part:
Nighttime sleep-aid. A drug that is useful for the relief of occasional sleeplessness by individuals who have difficulty falling asleep.
