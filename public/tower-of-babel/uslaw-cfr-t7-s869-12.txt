7 C.F.R. § 869.12 — Safe keeping of records.
Citation: us:cfr:7:869.12
Creator: Agricultural Marketing Service (Federal Grain Inspection Service, Fair Trade Practices Program), Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-869.12
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 869 (Regulations For The United States Warehouse Act). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Each warehouse operator or provider must take necessary precautions to safeguard all records, either paper or electronic format, from destruction.
