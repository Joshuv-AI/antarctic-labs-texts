47 C.F.R. § 95.1749 — GMRS network connection.
Citation: us:cfr:47:95.1749
Creator: Federal Communications Commission
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/section-95.1749
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 47 (Telecommunication), Part 95 (Personal Radio Services). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Operation of a GMRS station with a telephone connection is prohibited, as in § 95.349. GMRS repeater, base and fixed stations, however, may be connected to the public switched network or other networks for the sole purpose of operation by remote control pursuant to § 95.1745.
