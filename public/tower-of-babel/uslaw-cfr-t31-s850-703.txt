31 C.F.R. § 850.703 — Divestment.
Citation: us:cfr:31:850.703
Creator: Office Of Investment Security, Department Of The Treasury
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/section-850.703
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 31 (Money and Finance: Treasury), Part 850 (Provisions Pertaining To U.S. Investments In Certain National Security Technologies And Products In Countries Of Concern). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

(a) The Secretary, in consultation with the heads of relevant agencies, as appropriate, may take any action authorized under IEEPA to nullify, void, or otherwise compel the divestment of any prohibited transaction entered into after the effective date of this part.
(b) The Secretary may refer any action taken under paragraph (a) of this section to the Attorney General to seek appropriate relief to enforce such action.
