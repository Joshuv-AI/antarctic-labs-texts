15 C.F.R. § 280.200 — Scope.
Citation: us:cfr:15:280.200
Creator: National Institute Of Standards And Technology, Department Of Commerce
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/section-280.200
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 15 (Commerce and Foreign Trade), Part 280 (Fastener Quality). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Section 280.201 of this part specifies that failure to take any action required by or taking any action prohibited by this part constitutes a violation of this part. Section 280.202 describes the penalties that may be imposed for violations of this part. Sections 280.204 through 280.222 establish the procedures for imposing administrative penalties for violations of this part.
