42 U.S.C. § 4854 — Research on lead exposure from other sources
Citation: us:usc:42:4854
Creator: United States Congress
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section4854
Context: United States Code, Title 42 (The Public Health And Welfare); Chapter 63A (Residential Lead-Based Paint Hazard Reduction); Subchapter III (Research And Development); Part 1 (Hud Research). Current through Public Law 119-134, as published by the Office of the Law Revision Counsel.

The Secretary, in cooperation with other Federal agencies, shall conduct research on strategies to reduce the risk of lead exposure from other sources, including exterior soil and interior lead dust in carpets, furniture, and forced air ducts.
