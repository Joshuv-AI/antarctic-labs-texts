36 C.F.R. § 1260.4 — What NARA holdings are covered by this part?
Citation: us:cfr:36:1260.4
Creator: National Archives And Records Administration
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-36/section-1260.4
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 36 (Parks, Forests, and Public Property), Part 1260 (Declassification Of National Security Information). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

The NARA holdings covered by this part are records legally transferred to NARA, including Federal records, 44 U.S.C. 2107; Presidential records, 44 U.S.C. 2201-2207; Nixon Presidential materials, 44 U.S.C. 2111 note; and donated historical materials, 44 U.S.C. 2111.
