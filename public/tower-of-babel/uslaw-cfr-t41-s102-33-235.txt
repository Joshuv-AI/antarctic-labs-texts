41 C.F.R. § 102-33.235 — -33.235 Required reporting on Government aircraft.
Citation: us:cfr:41:102-33.235
Creator: Chapter 102—Federal Management Regulation
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-41/section-102-33.235
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 41 (Public Contracts and Property Management), Part 102-33 (33—Management Of Government Aircraft). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

Report the following to GSA via FAIRS:
(a) Inventory data for Federal aircraft, including UAS;
(b) Cost and utilization data for Federal aircraft, including UAS;
(c) Cost and utilization data for CAS aircraft and aviation services;
(d) Accident and incident data; and
(e) Results of competition studies per OMB Circular A-76 for aircraft and related services acquisition.
