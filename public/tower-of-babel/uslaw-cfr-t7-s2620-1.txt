7 C.F.R. § 2620.1 — General statement.
Citation: us:cfr:7:2620.1
Creator: Office Of Inspector General, Department Of Agriculture
Year: 2026
Source-URL: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-7/section-2620.1
Context: Code of Federal Regulations, Title 7 (Agriculture), Part 2620 (Availability Of Information To The Public). Text from the eCFR as of 2026-10-26 (unofficial editorial compilation; the official edition is the annual CFR).

This part supplements the regulations of the Secretary of Agriculture implementing the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. 552 (FOIA) (subpart A of part 1 of this title, including the appendix), and governs the availability of records of the Office of Inspector General (OIG) to the public upon request.
